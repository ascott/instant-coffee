Amazing Gallery
Emily Carr University
South Building, 4th Floor

Amazing Gallery is delighted to present in 2014's inaugural exhibition works 
of five artists: Alexandra Bischoff, Nicole Dumas, Jenn Pearson, Sonja 
Ratkay, and Allison Watters. 

A constellation of sculptural, painted, and photographic imagery as well as 
the written word, Postures For Lessness brings together a selection of 
works of a modest scale: an inquiry into the expansive capacities possessed 
by economic compositions to act as vectors for intimate experiences.

The artists are observers, navigators of a territory where the relationship 
between seeing to making-sense-of is located in it's most primordial, intimate 
state. Having traveled with increasing frequency to and from this place on the 
threshold of awareness, the artists have developed a certain posture 
towards determining what is and what is not in each of their practices. 

Postures For Lessness is in a sense documentation of a logic used to deal 
with the discovery and translation of intuitions, feelings and appearances that 
exist on the periphery of our perception as human beings.

Shift of Perspective

November 20 to 26, 2014

Emily Carr Library

Photography in the past century, used prolifically in advertising and
photojournalism, was bound in conversation with the document. Because of
this, photography was considered to function outside of fine art discourses
and economies. Photography, seen as free from the art context with its
inherent fetishistic and material values, was in turn taken up by
Conceptual artists.

This adoption altered the use of photography in book design. While the
conventional photo-essay did not disappear, artists’ books that
internalized the coded space of the book as the structure for the creation
of meaning began to emerge. This revelation is evident in the variety of
approaches used in composing photo-based artists’ books as artists forged a
new relationship between photography and the document.

Shift of Perspective features a range of artists’ books that explore the
use of photography and text. The show contains work by N. E. Thing Company,
Andrey Tarkovsky, Bernadette Corporation, Ken Lum, Martha Rosler, Elad
Lassry, Sophie Calle and Duane Michals. The work on exhibition demonstrates
the different approaches employed by the artists while also pointing to the
multiple relationships and possibilities between photography and text.

Shift of Focus follows the development of photography and text in relation
to the document, memory, and archiving, in a wide variety of approaches
from the photographic-essay to the search for the poetic.

Shift of Perspective is curated by Alina Senchenko, Peter Ullstrom, Alena
Webber and Hailee Pitkethly

***

Co-Lab

November 20 to 26, 2014

Emily Carr Library

Co-Lab is an exploration of the collaborative nature of the artist book.
The exhibition features books from the collection held at Emily Carr
University of Art + Design, a collection that contains experimental,
non-linear, often enigmatic pieces of printed matter all of which push
boundaries in reading, viewing and interacting with printed form.

Considering the wide definition of an artist book, Co-Lab brings together a
variety of works to create a complex display of the collaborative
approaches artists and designers have employed in making a publication. The
artist book as a medium of innovation intersects with and expands away from
the conventions of the traditional book form. Reading here requires the
audience to critically engage with an open mind in order to experience the
potential of its multi-layered form.

The exhibition will showcase works by Sydney Hermant, Julian Berthier and
Christoph Keller, Sol LeWitt, Mahla Shapiro and June Vesvarut, Yoko Ono,
and FUKT & D: Drunk on Dreams.

Co-Lab is curated by Brent Haddow, Karen Shangguan, Yoojin Rhee, and
Junxiang Shao.

***

Shift of Perspective and Co-Lab are the final two in a series of four
exhibitions of work from the Emily Carr Library’s Artists’ Books Collection
and are presented as part of HUMN 311: The Practice of Artists’ Publishing.

Art, offers an intimate glimpse into the world of the unknown craftspeople
of Japan.

tells a tale. When you deal with the more functional wares you see how
simplicity speaks with an elegance that many far more elaborate works never
attain. I see the humble maker within these pieces that are all far from
mute. Mingei speaks low but insistently. Each of us is changed by Mingei in
our own ways."
Amaury Saint-Gilles

everyday objects kept close and held in the hand. Selected from almost

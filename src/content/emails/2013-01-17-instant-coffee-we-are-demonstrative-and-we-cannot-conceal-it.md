Stuffed and Hung features new sculptural works by Kuh Del Rosario. 
Constructed from a variety of found objects and salvaged materials, Del 
Rosario explores the limits of these urban sediments through the process of 
deconstruction and reconfiguration. They are fossilized under layers of 
synthetic crusts of acrylic medium and latex paint. Only within the imposed 
assemblages enforced upon them, do they create a visual narrative, 
fabricating a contrived anthropology.

Kuh Del Rosario is a Vancouver-based sculptor. She graduated from ACAD in 
2003 with a BFA in Painting. She recently exhibited at XPACE (Toronto, ON), 
Cinevolution (Richmond, BC) and NMSU (Las Cruces, NM). Del Rosario was 
also named One to Watch 2013 (The Vancouver Sun).

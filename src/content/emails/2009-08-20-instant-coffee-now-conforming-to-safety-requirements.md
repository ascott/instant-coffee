TALKS
HUB is holding a series of talks highlighting creative applications of technology. These talks will 
feature artists and technologists from the Vancouver area and will focus on their recent work.
Upcoming Talks:

* Josh Hite & Justine Chambers - August 20, 7:00 PM (UBC Robson Square, Room C680)
* Dennis Rosenfeld - August 27, 7:00 PM (UBC Robson Square, Room C485)

These talks will be held at UBC Robson Square, 800 Robson Street, Vancouver.

About HUB: Centre for Art & Technology
HUB is an experimental art initiative focused on collaboration, the sharing of ideas and knowledge, 
and the creative exploration of information technologies. Built around the principles of the Open
Source movement, HUB aims to create a collaborative, interdisciplinary environment that provides 
free and open access to current and emerging technologies. To learn more, please visit 
http://www.hubcentre.org/

HUB currently operates a computer lab that provides a workspace and access to software and 
technologies free of charge. The lab is open Mondays and Wednesdays from 5PM to 8PM and 
Saturdays from 10AM to 4PM. For detailed hours and address information, visit 
http://http://www.hubcentre.org/hublab/

Movable Sculpture
Window Installation by Nathalee Paolinelli
Opens Saturday August 22nd - September 12th

LES Gallery
1879 Powell St. 
Vancouver BC

Artist Statement: 
My practice is studio based and is focused on abstract drawing painting and sculpture. I typically 
work with invented formal systems that on the surface might appear to operate with in a typical 
abstract, modernist vocabulary. As I work to strip things down by reanalyzing color, surface, and
structure I am simultaneously attempting to discover, and make public, complex private emotions 
in a systematized way. I find it interesting how complicated the harmonics of art work necessarily 
becomes when paired with introspective emotion. Regardless, I strive for the results to be 
hopefully intelligent, considered, humorous, and at best inspirational.

www.nathaleepaolinelli.com

will be no formal opening reception, however the installation can be viewed 24 hours, 7 days a 
week.*

Fillip proudly announces the opening of its library and reading room on Wednesday, August 26, 
2009.

The Fillip Library is an expanding collection of new and rare contemporary art books, magazines, 
critical anthologies, and pamphlets. Beginning September 2009, the collection will be open 
weekly to the public, or by appointment at 305 Cambie Street, Vancouver.

The books in the Library have been donated by individuals and organizations from North 
America, Europe, and Asia since Fillip began in 2004. The collection also incorporates material 
from the Judgment and Contemporary Art Criticism Reading Room developed in conjunction with 
Artspeak last spring. 

Inquiries regarding donations and publication exchanges can be directed to library@fillip.ca. 
Contributions can be sent to Fillip Library, 305 Cambie Street, Vancouver, BC. V6B 2N4.

Launch & Talk
Fillip is kicking off the launch of the Library with a talk and presentation at 7pm by Felicity Taylor, 
Information Specialist for Artexte, Montreal. 

Artexte engages in research, interpretation, and dissemination initiatives in order to broaden the 
influence and appreciation of contemporary visual art. These activities are informed by their 
significant collection of art documentation and authoritative resources, as well as a network of 
multidisciplinary partners. Their activities touch on all aspects of contemporary visual arts from 
1965 on, with special emphasis placed on QuÃ©bec and Canada.

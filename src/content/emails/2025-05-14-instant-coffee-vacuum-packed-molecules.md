1st Draft

Qian Cheng

May 1 - June 1, 2025

Qian Cheng is an artist and organizer whose practice delves into personal narratives

<a href="/instant-coffee/listings/2025-05-14-02-kelowna-art-gallery-jos-luis-torres-handmade-may-10">02. EXHIBITION | Kelowna Art Gallery | José Luis Torres: HANDMADE | MAY 10</a>
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Artist creates extraordinary works reimagined from the everyday

Jos Luis Torres: HANDMADE

May 10, 2025 to May 2026

Kelowna, BC: Okanagan audiences will soon have the chance to see a towering
new art installation at the Kelowna Art Gallery that reimagines ordinary materials and
transforms them into something extraordinary.

The artist behind the work is Quebec City-based Argentine sculptor Jos Luis Torres,
whose work has been shown and celebrated in public spaces and art institutions
across Canada. The art installation, titled HANDMADE, can be found in the open-air
Courtyard space located at the heart of the Gallery building. It opens to the public on
May 10, 2025.

Through playful, evolving environments, Jos Luis Torres enjoys challenging
conventional exhibition standards and the idealized image of the artist. HANDMADE
continues this exploration between location, materials, and the viewer in a
delightfully engaging way, which is sure to spark the curiosity of Gallery-goers.

Visitors will encounter a structure that rises on stilts above a foundation built from old
wooden pallets. The small domicile-like building is constructed almost entirely from
window frames, doors, and a substantial amount of glass. Peering in from the
perimeter it looks almost maze-like in its complexity within. Torres artistic practice
involves appropriating space and materials from everyday life"like all the doors,
windows, pallets, and other items that were locally sourced from individuals"and
then given the freedom to evolve and take form through spontaneous hands-on
actions. His work reflects themes of survival, workforce, and the value of objects as
both fundamental and incidental.

Jos Luis Torres is an Argentine artist who has been based in Qubec City since
2003. He holds degrees in visual arts, sculpture, and architecture. His work has
been featured in numerous exhibitions, including at Muse national des beaux-arts
du Qubec, Museum London, and the UCCS Gallery of Contemporary Art.

An Opening Reception will be held on Friday, May 9, 2025, from 6 to 8 pm at the
Kelowna Art Gallery. The artist will be in attendance, and the public is welcome to
attend.

Jos Luis Torres: HANDMADE runs from May 10, 2025, to May of 2026.

The Kelowna Art Gallery is located at 1315 Water Street in the heart of Kelownas
Culture District. For more information visit kelownaartgallery.com or call 250-762-
2226.

CINEWORKS INDEPENDENT FILMMAKERS SOCIETY JOB POSTING

FACILITIES & EQUIPMENT MANAGER

Status: Permanent position, 30 hours per week

Schedule: Monday to Friday, 11:30am - 6pm; with flexibility for outreach & events

The Organization

Founded in 1980, in Vancouver, on the unceded territories of the xmky"m
(Musqueam), Swxw7mesh (Squamish), and Sel"l"witulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations,
Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society is a non-profit artist-run centre,
established to support independent filmmakers, media artists, and arts audiences
through initiatives that assist in the production, distribution, and exhibition of non-
commercial and independent films with educational and cultural benefit for the
community. Our programs include art performances, film screenings, exhibitions as
well publicly accessible workshops aimed at increasing filmmaking skills, knowledge,
analysis, and awareness. We maintain rare analog filmmaking equipment and
knowledge with the goal of facilitating skill-sharing and intergenerational knowledge
related to analog film practices. Our services reach members of the public and
independent artists of all ages including adults, youth, seniors, and elementary
school children. We foster experimentation with motion-picture arts and engage our
membership and wider communities in the investigative, expressive, and
transformative powers of the moving image. Cineworks is a federally registered
charity and, as such, our work is of a charitable nature.

Applications Welcome

Cineworks is committed to establishing and upholding values of equity, diversity, and
inclusion. We strongly encourage applications from peoples of underrepresented
groups and those who have faced systemic barriers at institutions, including cis,
trans, two-spirit and non-binary, and BIPOC. We also recognize that diverse lived
experience and knowledge can support a candidates eligibility outside of formal
educational and/or employment classifications.

The Position

Are you interested in working in the non-profit realm with a community organisation
dedicated to providing supports for independent filmmakers? Cineworks
Independent Filmmakers Society seeks a Facilities + Equipment Manager (FEM) to
join our small but enthusiastic team of arts professionals working with the non-profit
sector. The FEM serves as a first point of contact between the organization and our
membership, providing assistance for facilities & equipment rental, member services,
and support for production & post-production needs. The FEM is an integral part of
the Cineworks team, providing both technical support and guidance to our members,
as well as conducting outreach to advance the community that our membership
serves.

The position supports Cineworks core mandate to provide facilities to produce
independent, non-commercial film, and to foster experimentation with motion-picture
arts. The FEM supports the creative needs of our membership, relying on an in-
depth knowledge of our equipment holdings, facilities, and programs. The role
involves hands-on maintenance and upkeep of our digital and analog film equipment
inventory, including engagement with analog processes (e.g. film scanning and
processing) and administrative upkeep of our rental & membership database &
statistics.

Working closely with the Cineworks Program Coordinator, Analog Film Technician
and Executive Director, the FEM helps deliver our year-round activities, programs,
and services to our members and public.

Responsibilities

- Maintain and administer film production & post-production equipment, facilities

- Maintain practice based on a commitment to removing obstacles to

- Assist with the coordination of equipment for workshops and programming

- Deliver bi-weekly orientation sessions for prospective Cineworks members

- Schedule rentals to maintain effective inventory control and facilities bookings

- Develop accurate research and analysis to inform decisions on maintenance,
usage, purchase, and renewal of equipment

- Manage financial duties regarding equipment and facility rentals, including
invoicing, budgeting and reporting

- Provide content, input, and accurate research to enable successful grant and
fund development opportunities

- Serve as an ambassador for Cineworks in building community and through
occasional outreach to secondary and post-secondary education institutions, partner
film organizations, and local film festivals

Required Skills

- A post-secondary degree or equivalent experience in film, preferably with a
hands-on film production focus; or equivalent combination of education and
experience

- Holistic understanding of film production requirements with regards to the
departments of cameras, sound, production lighting and grip gear

- Understanding of film post-production requirements with regards to the
departments of editing, processing, and projection

- Adept in digital post-production software such as Adobe Creative Cloud,
DaVinci Resolve, Pro Tools, and Final Cut Pro

- Knowledge of Macintosh platform, office software, and some familiarity with

- Technical experience with filmmaking, in both digital and analog realms

- Excellent organizational and multitasking skills

- Excellent written and oral communication skills, with the ability to listen and

- Ability to work as part of a team

- Commitment to nurturing community

- Comfortable with public speaking and delivering information to large groups

- Self-sufficient and comfortable working independently

This position controls assets and financial transactions, and therefore the selected

Embodied Generations: Drawing & Movement Workshop with Odera Igbokwe

Join The Polygon Gallery for a live figure drawing and movement workshop led by
artist Odera Igbokwe, presented as part of the programming series To Wash

art forms rooted in Nigerian, Black, and Afrodiasporic cultures

by Kevin Day

the medium is the environment is a powerful multimedia installation by Kevin Day
that challenges the myth of the cloud and reveals the material realities behind our
digital lives. Through a blend of fog downward video projection and data-driven
systems the work reflects on the hidden ecological and social costs of digital
technology.

While a former iteration of the project utilized the artists personal data of Internet
usage the iteration at VIVO Media Arts utilizes the Internet usage data of the
institution itself. Inviting the viewers to engage in the act of content-consumption the
presence of the video is intended to activate the viewers digital labour and expose
their participation in the attention economy. Implicating the artist the gallery and the
viewers within the digital economy these two iterations of the project underscore the
environmental impact of our digitized lives highlighting the materiality of something
supposedly immaterial.

Audience Advisory and Accessibility Notes:

Fog in Exhibition Area: A controlled fog is part of the exhibition installation that might
affect individuals with respiratory conditions allergies or other health concerns.
May 20 will be a fog-free day to accommodate those with sensitivities.
Low Lighting: The studio space has low lighting which may be challenging for those
with low vision.
About Kevin Day:
Kevin Days practice and research encompassing sound video graph web and
interactive media installations examine contemporary arts critical capacity in
response to the current socio-political issues of digital culture negating the encoding
extraction and exploitation by data colonialism and information capitalism. Informed
by philosophy of technology critical theory media studies and digital materialism his
research and practice question the ubiquitous logic of framing the world through
information indicative of an information-based way of knowing. The works resist the
extraction and abstraction of algorithmic processes through an insistence on the
presence of noise in the information-capital complex.

Analog

Jane Grocott, Paige Quinn, Connor Skillen

Opening Friday, May 16th, 6-10pm

August Studios

1320 E Pender St, Vancouver

Analog is an exhibition of new work by Jane Grocott, Connor Skillen and Paige
Quinn at August Studios. Large oil paintings are interrupted by smaller explorations
to form a collection of distinct yet interconnected pictures. These three artists have
been working alongside one other in the same studio building in Vancouver for
exactly two years, absorbing similar influences from their mutual surroundings yet

<a href="/instant-coffee/listings/2019-12-19-04-news-grunt-gallery-the-blue-cabin-angela-george-weaving-gove">04. NEWS | grunt gallery | The Blue Cabin | Angela George "Weaving Governance" | JAN 6</a>
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Blue Cabin Floating Artist Residency Welcomes Multidisciplinary Tsleil Waututh
Artist Angela George as Artist-in-Residence

Angela George to create foundational woven document of governance for Tsleil Waututh
Nation during residency at The Blue Cabin.

VANCOUVER, BC " The Blue Cabin Floating Artist Residency is thrilled to welcome
Tsleil Waututh artist Angela George as the second artist-in-residence in the yearlong
inaugural program Skeins: Weaving on the
Foreshore, examining Salish weaving
practices. During a two-month residency at The Blue Cabin in False Creek, George will
create a monumental panel-weaving, Weaving Governance, which will reflect a
foundational governance model for the Tsleil Waututh Nation that draws from ancient
knowledge and connection to the lands, waters and ancestors and will continue to guide
sustainability in modern times. Georges intricate woven document will be created in its
entirety during her residency, and will be a lasting legacy for the Tseil Waututh Nation.

With the resurgence of weaving among the Coast Salish peoples, there is a growing
desire to connect to and understand the value of the intricate weaving patterns and
methods their ancestors used. Weaving was an essential practice throughout the region
that reflected the weaver, the respective family origins, and who and what the weaving
was designed for. There have been significant losses suffered through the disruption of
oral traditions and
generational record keeping due to residential schools and
colonization. These losses include languages, history and customs related to traditional
weavings. As a weaver, Angela George has come to understand the significance of the
revitalization of these practices, along with language and cultural revival. The Tsleil
Waututh people have never given up their aboriginal rights and title, nor their ancient
jurisdiction to protect the lands, waters and traditional territory in and around slilwt
(now known as Burrard Inlet and Indian Arm). Tsleil Waututh ways of knowing and doing
are key factors in an overall revitalization of self-governing customs. The weaving
design George is drafting is a compilation of the essential traditional laws, values and
vital references for the modern Tsleil Waututh Nation to consider when moving forward
with constitutional processes and systems of governance.

The public is invited to open studio sessions with Angela George from 11am-3pm on
December 2nd and
16th, 2019, and January 6th, 2020, where they are welcome to help
weave or bring small projects to weave in the Blue Cabin.

Where: The Blue Cabin Floating Artist Residency, at the Plaza of Nations beside the
Aquabus/False Creek Ferries stop.
Dates: December 2nd and 16th, 2019, and January 6th, 2020 11am " 3pm
Website: thebluecabin.ca

For further media information, contact
Katrina Orlowski | T. 604.875.9516 | communications@thebluecabin.ca

Retail and Publications Coordinator

About
The Contemporary Art Gallery (CAG) is an independent, non-profit public art gallery and
one of Canadas leading venues for contemporary art. Offering free access to everyone,
we work with artists from Canada and around the world to produce exhibitions, off-site
projects, residencies, learning, outreach, community engagement programs, events and
publications. Each of these interdependent strands of programming varies in scale,
duration and location to challenge how and where we might engage with contemporary
visual art.

Job Summary

CAG is seeking a skilled and driven Retail and Publications Coordinator to join our
dynamic institution. Reporting to the Operations Administrator and working closely with
the Curatorial and Communications departments, the Retail and Publications
Coordinator will be situated in Front of House and responsible for driving sales and
outreach of CAG publications, artist editions and merchandise.

Key Responsibilities

Reports to the Operations Administrator and responsible for shop sales,
publication distribution strategies, edition production and library coordination

Oversees and coordinates all retail aspects of the CAG Shop, including in-store
and online sales, displays and visual merchandising, discounts and special promotions,
e-commerce, packaging and shipping

Coordinates and facilitates publication exchanges, distribution, consignments and
outreach

Coordinates the
production of CAGs artist editions

Oversees front of house operations on weekends and is the weekend contact and
staff support person for volunteers

Keeps accurate inventory counts and data management and oversees annual
publication inventory procedures

Coordinates the Abraham Rogatnick Library, including management of logbooks,
cataloguing and overseeing the work of the library volunteers

Coordinates and oversees CAGs sales and returns policy

Skills and abilities

Excellent written and verbal communications skills
Outstanding customer service and working with the general public
Understanding of visual merchandising and driving sales
Confidence with Wordpress and e-commerce platforms
Confidence handling artist editions, packing and shipping
Experience working with inventory and auditors
Comprehensive computer and technology skills
Experience with library operations
Confidence leading and supervising front of house operations
Ability to prioritize and problem solve in high
pressure situations

Qualifications and attributes

University degree in the arts, publishing or related discipline. Minimum one to three
years working in retail, front of house operations and customer service. Front of house
experience within a museum or gallery setting, knowledge of contemporary art, and
experience in library science will be a considerable asset. Excellent customer service
and people relations skills are essential to the role.

Deadline to apply

Monday, January 20, 2020 at 5pm

Please send your resume and cover letter in one PDF to Shayla Perreault at
employment@contemporaryartgallery.ca

Remuneration

$17.00 per hour

This is a permanent, part-time position of 24 hours per week that includes extended
healthcare benefits. Typical work days will be Friday to Sunday, 10am to 6pm and
occasional evening work will be required.

Interviews and start date

Interviews will commence the week of January 27 with the aim to fill the position as soon
as possible.

All
applicants will receive an email acknowledgement upon receipt of their application.
We thank all who are interested in the position, however only short-listed candidates will
be contacted.

CARFAC Ontario is seeking applications for the position of Managing Director.
Position Type: Part-time (28 hours per week)
Salary Range: Compensation to be negotiated based on experience (salary range:
hourly rate equivalent to $45K-$50K per annum)
Application Deadline: Friday, December 20, 2019

Job Description:

CARFAC Ontario is seeking a dynamic, creative, and organized individual for the role of
the Managing Director.

The Managing
Director is a key leadership role for running all aspects of the
organization in collaboration with a deeply-engaged Board of Directors. The Board
offers suggestions and ideas on how to improve organization actions, while the
Managing Director decides how to implement these ideas to the best of their ability.
They will assume hands-on responsibilities for the design, development, and
implementation of all strategic plans for the organization in a manner that is both cost-
efficient and timely, and provide regular reports to the Board. They are also responsible
for all day-to-day operations including administration and communications; managing a
small team of staff, committees, and volunteers; fundraising and grant writing; as well as
financial and general operations of the organization. The Managing Director plays a
leadership and advocacy role for the organization, its members, and the arts community.
They often fulfill a motivational capacity in addition to office-based work to lead
the
organization in all development areas.

Reading and Book Launch:
Art Criticism & Other Short Stories

With readings by:

Hazel Meyer
Carmen Papalia
Vanessa Kwan
Helen Reed

Friday, December 20, 7pm
grunt gallery, #116 - 350 East 2nd Avenue, Vancouver

Blank Cheque Press is pleased to announce the launch of its latest title! Originally
produced and distributed by artist Helen Reed as a series of fanfiction zines, Art
Criticism & Other Short Stories collects together fifteen of the very best of these texts --
from deliriously concocted alternate realities and erotic fantasies, to cutting
appraisals of
gender and power. Featuring writing by a diverse lineup of artists, writers, curators and
critics working in and alongside contemporary art, this anthology of oddball writing
shows us just how critical and gushing true fandom can be, proving once and for all that
these qualities are not mutually exclusive.

With contributions by Rose Bouthillier, Amber Dawn, Jen Delos Reyes, Amy Fung, Anna
Gray & Ryan Wilson Paulsen, Onya Hogan-Finlay, Ariana Jacob, Sam Korman,
Vanessa Kwan, Hazel Meyer, Dan Nelson, Darren O'Donnell, Carmen Papalia, Helen
Reed, and Maya Suess.

More info: http://www.blankchequepress.com/product/art-criticism-other-short-stories

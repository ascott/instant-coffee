Beaumont Gallery, 315 West 5th Avenue, Vancouver, BC
Friday, July 24th, 2009, 8-12 PM

Ryan Morris' debut novel "MOLT" is being presented and celebrated through a wild collection of 
artistic interventions. The fictional book is a weaving narrative following our protagonist: an 
ornithology professor who falls into a spiral of passion, adventure, and chaos only to emerge entirely 
transformed. 

A group of artists are presenting their interpretations of novel selections through sculpture, painting, 
film, live performance, comic and graphic strips. It will be an exploration of the subjectivity of reading, 
celebration of personal expression, and an exhibition around joy of creating artwork and literature. 

Admitted by donation.
Bar and drinks available.

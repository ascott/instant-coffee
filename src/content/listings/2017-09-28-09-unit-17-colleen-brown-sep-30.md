COLLEEN BROWN
Hang up, bend and slump, low pressure Ohio.

1 - 31 October 2017
opening reception: Saturday 30 September 7 - 9pm

Unit 17 is pleased to present the first solo exhibition at the gallery by Colleen Brown " 
Hang up, bend and slump, low pressure Ohio. For this exhibition, held at the gallery's 
downtown space, Brown has brought together recent shaped paintings and sculptural 
work produced over the last three years. 

Colleen Brown (b. 1965) explores the relationships of objects and materials as a means 
of thinking through abstraction and social encounters. Brown lives in Vancouver and 
holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts from Emily Carr University of Art + Design (Vancouver) 
and a Master of Fine Arts from the Milton Avery Graduate School of the Arts, Bard 
College, Annandale-on-Hudson (New York). Recent group exhibitions and events 
include Vancouver Special: Ambivalent Pleasures, Vancouver Art Gallery; Form Play, 
Western Gallery, Bellingham; Good Neighbor, Hedreen Gallery, Seattle; Xenia, Airbnb, 
Seattle. Brown is the recipient of the 2016 Vancouver Portfolio Prize.

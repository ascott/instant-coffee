Creative Spark Vancouver Grants for Emerging Artists - Apply by April 21, 2017

Get the support you need to turn your ideas into action! Emerging artists across all 
disciplines who have an interest in building their careers and working with young 
people in an arts-based capacity are invited to apply for Creative Spark Vancouver. 
Grants of up to $2,000 are available. Eligible artists must be living in the City of 
Vancouver or residents and members of the Musqueam, Squamish or Tsleil-Waututh 
First Nations. Learn more and apply by April 21, 2017 at 

http://artstarts.com/creativesparkvancouver

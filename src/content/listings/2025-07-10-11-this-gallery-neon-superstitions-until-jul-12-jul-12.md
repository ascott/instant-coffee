Neon Superstitions, a vibrant new series of Chinatown-inspired paintings by
Vancouver artist Liang Wang, closes July 12.

Glowing neon signs. Beckoning cats. Guan Gong statues standing watch beneath
CCTV cameras. In Wangs richly coloured works, the streetscapes of Vancouvers
Chinatown become a fascinating blend of faith, folklore, and commerce. Symbols that
often slip by unnoticed"shopfront displays, mascots, cracked windows"are
reimagined as quiet protectors, reminders of unseen forces, and emblems of resilience
within diasporic communities.

At once sacred and secular, familiar yet mysterious, Neon Superstitions explores how
traditions endure and adapt in the rhythms of urban life. Its a striking homage to the
culture, tenacity, and visual language of a neighbourhood that continues to shape
Vancouvers identity.

Experience a Chinatown painted in brilliant colour and layered meaning.

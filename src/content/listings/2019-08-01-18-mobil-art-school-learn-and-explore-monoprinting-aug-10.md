Learn and explore monoprinting

Aug. 10, 10:00 a.m. - 3:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Join Val Loewen to explore the painterly process of monoprinting. This workshop will 
cover the basics of hand printed monoprints and lift prints, students will have the 
opportunity to experiment with a variety of mark making objects and materials using 
water-based inks.

Basic colours and paper will be provided. If you have preferences to use high quality 
paper please bring this to the class with you. Contact us for any questions regarding 
materials. $70
Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our FAQ.

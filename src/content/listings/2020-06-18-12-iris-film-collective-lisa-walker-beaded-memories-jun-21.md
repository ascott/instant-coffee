Special Presentation
lisa walker presents beaded memories

heartfelt beaded art pieces about childhood memories

National Indigenous Peoples Day
ONLINE!*
[https://www.lisabeading.com/](https://www.lisabeading.com/)
[https://www.instagram.com/lisa.beading/](https://www.instagram.com/lisa.beading/)
Sunday June 21, 2020

beaded memories is a heartfelt and personal series of beaded expressive art
pieces. each piece is about
memories from my childhood, including imagery from
my home, experiencing trauma, discovering my mixed race status, feeling like
an outsider, and other experiences of growing up in a small town as a mixed
indigenous person.

the art pieces are titled with haislakala, my indigenous language, and act as a
way for me to continue the meditative process of learning my language.

project will include super 8 video footage by lisa g.

this project was made possible by funds from the first peoples cultural council.

*from the artist: i will be sharing photos of my pieces online, as well as the super
8 footage that lisa g kindly created. they will be shared on the same day as the
original viewing was scheduled, Sunday June 21 National Indigenous Peoples
Day in the afternoon.

Please check my website [https://www.lisabeading.com/](https://www.lisabeading.com/) and my social media
[https://www.instagram.com/lisa.beading/](https://www.instagram.com/lisa.beading/)
thank you and my apologies for any inconvenience!

lisa walker is a mixed first nations (haisla/british)
artist from kitimat, BC currently
living as an an uninvited guest on the territories of the xmky"m
(Musqueam), Swxw7mesh (Squamish), and sel"l"witulh (Tsleil-Waututh)
Nations. she is a contemporary indigenous beader, and sees beading as
medicine. visit lisabeading.com or find her on social media @lisa.beading to see
her work.

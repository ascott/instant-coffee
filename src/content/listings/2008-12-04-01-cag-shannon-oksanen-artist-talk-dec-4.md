The Contemporary Art Gallery and the Contemporary Art Society of Vancouver
co-present:

Shannon Oksanen

Artist's Talk: Thursday, December 4, 2008, 7pm

In conjunction with Summerland, her solo exhibition at the Contemporary Art
Gallery, Shannon Oksanen will give an artist talk on December 4th that will
draw a loose chronology of her practice. She will discuss a selection of
her film and video works as well as her paintings and sculptures, showing
how, over time, these works come together as formal representations that
capture the ideals of an era as it is being broken or defined.

Summerland runs until Sunday, January 18, 2008. The exhibition is
accompanied by an illustrated catalogue.

Oksanen has a growing international reputation and has participated in
group exhibitions at the CCA Wattis Institute for Contemporary Art, San
Francisco (2004); VTO Gallery, London, England (2004); 303 Gallery, New
York (2005) and The Charles. H. Scott Gallery, Vancouver (2006). She is
represented by Lawrence Eng Gallery in Vancouver where she recently
exhibited a little boat adrift on the sea of life and other works including
some papier mache balloons.* Oksanen participated in There are Those:
Drawing by Six Artists at the CAG in 2006. Reviews of her work have
appeared in Artforum, New York and the Globe and Mail, Toronto.

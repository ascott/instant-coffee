The Atelier Gallery is pleased to present a selection of new works by artist Brad Woodfin. This Night, which is the artist's first solo exhibition, is a modern approach to historical genre painting.

Woodfin’s menagerie includes Hares, Deer, Sheep, and Dodos. Each work captures subtle aspects of what one suspects is an inner life and personality. This exhibition will be on display from January 12 to 26, 2008, with the artist’s reception to be held on Saturday, January 12 from 2-4 p.m.

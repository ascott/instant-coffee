SWEATY BONES (a project by Heidi Nagtegaal) | Artbank (1897 Powell) |
December 4, 2010 | 9PM to 1 AM

Sweaty Bones is a year long curatorial project run by Heidi Nagtegaal.
Sweaty Bones celebrates a chaotic collage of dancing, body sweats, and
charged atmosphere. It is here that a new "white cube" has been opened up,
one with colored lighting, strobes, and loud music: a place for artists to
reinvent themselves within a social context, outside of the traditional
gallery setup.

The theme is 'Mystic Prism', which is a take on survivalism, spirituality,
and salvaging. Salvaging in this case refers to a transformation of energy
from one thing to another, usually an unusable material to one which is
usable, mirroring the Christian language of 'salvation' - which in greek,
means to salvage. In this context, salvation takes on a dialogue of
restoration, process, and transformation outside of the didacticism of
dogma.

INSTALLATION
HEIDI NAGTEGAAL // God's Eyes // Over 100 God's Eyes made out of salvages
VHS tapes and Home Depot Stir Sticks, meshing craft traditions of the Middle
and South Americas with the trade, and commodity cultures of North America.
God's Eyes traditionally mean a combination of "namaste" and the Middle
Eastern "Evil Eye", in which they remind the viewer to respect the other
person as another soul, and protect them against ill intentions.

PORTAL SOCIAL ART PROJECT
HEIDI NAGTEGAAL // God's Eye Fax Mural // social practice project by Heidi
Nagtegaal // I've drawn and pasted up a 5' x 6' drawing of a God's Eye, at
Hastings and Dunlevy, in Vancouver BC. I will fax, mail, or email (zip file)
you a God's Eye, so that you can put it up in your neighbourhood or city,
creating Portals between us.

The Public Art Program and Sustainability Group invite you to join us to celebrate the 
installation of Should I Be Worried?, a neon text artwork that will light-up a disused 
industrial wooden beam structure at the south edge of False Creek (east of the Cambie 
Bridge).

This new public artwork was produced by local artist Justin Langlois 
(http://justinlanglois.com) as part of the Citys first Artist-in-Residence program. Since 
mid-2016, Justin has been working with the Sustainability Group, learning about the 
various initiatives and themes that shape the Greenest City Action Plan and contributing 
to ideas and opportunities for greater engagement. The work produced aims to open up 
an ongoing dialogue about social, economic, and environmental sustainability.

We hope that you will join us to celebrate the launch of the artwork, meet the artist and 
hear from City staff:

Date: Wednesday, December 6th 
Time: 3pm " 4:30 pm
Location: City Studio, 1800 Spyglass Place, Vancouver
(walk north under the bridge from the Olympic Village Canada Line station.)

There will be a short presentation at 4:00 p.m., after which we will flip the switch to turn 
on the artwork.

Questions or comments: publicart@vancouver.ca. Press interviews or images: 
media@vancouver.ca.
Artist Information
Justin Langlois was selected on the basis of his creative ability to synthesize complex 
systems and information into public facing, highly visible and engaging work. His art and 
research explores locality, infrastructure, and participation in relation to civic 
engagement and social change. He is an Associate Professor of Social Practice in the 
Faculty of Culture + Community at Emily Carr University of Art and Design.

This project is supported by the City of Vancouver through the Public Art Program and 
the Sustainability Group with assistance from Engineering Services, Utilities, Streets 
and Electrical Design and Operations. Thanks to City Studio for hosting the reception.

Curator's Tour / Grant Arnold / in the Gallery
Jerry Pethick: Shooting the Sun/Splitting the Pie
Saturday, Oct 24, 3pm

Curator Grant Arnold tours the retrospective exhibition of artist Jerry
Pethick. Pethick's work spanned mediums-from holograms and photo arrays to
glass-and subjects, looking at science, art history and life on Hornby Island.

Free for Members or with Gallery admission.

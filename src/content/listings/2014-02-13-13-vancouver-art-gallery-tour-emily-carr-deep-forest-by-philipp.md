Vancouver Art Gallery | Tour of Emily Carr: Deep Forest with Philippe 
Raphanel | FEB 18

Tuesday, February 18, 7pm
Emily Carr: Deep Forest
Exhibition Tour with Philippe Raphanel
in the Gallery

Emily Carr has made an impression on countless individuals with her unique 
perception of the wilderness of British Columbia. The exhibition Emily Carr: 
Deep Forest presents a selection of her works depicting the forest 
environments that surrounded her home in Victoria, BC.

Reflecting on the global reach of her work, painter Philippe Raphanel will 
present a personal tour, discussing the influence of Carr on his journey as a 
painter, how it inspired him to travel to Vancouver, and what remains 
inspiring about it today.

Emily Carr: Deep Forest, on view from December 21, 2013 to March 9, 2014

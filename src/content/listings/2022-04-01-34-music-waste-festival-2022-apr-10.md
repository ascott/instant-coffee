Music Waste Festival is seeking musicians, visual artists, and workshop/seminar hosts
for our
first in-person festival in 2 years!!

The 2022 festival will include live indoor and outdoor shows, and some of the live
content will be streamed virtually. This format is subject to change per public health
orders because honestly who the heck knows.

About Music Waste:
Music Waste is an independent, non-profit music + art festival held annually on the
unceded traditional territories of the xmkwy" m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh
(Squamish), and sl "lwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations. We are totally
volunteer organized and run.

Festival Dates: June 2-5 in "Vancouver."

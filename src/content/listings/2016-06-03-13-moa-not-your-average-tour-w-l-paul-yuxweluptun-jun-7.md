Not Your Average Tour with artist Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun: June 7th
2016 at 7pm

In response to the exhibition Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun: Unceded
Territories MOA is taking a new approach to the standard exhibition tour.
We have invited First Nations academics, activists, community members, and
politicians to use Yuxweluptun's work as a springboard for discussion. Let
these guides direct your attention to critical issues confronting
Indigenous communities locally, nationally, and internationally.

The next tourJune 7 at 7pmwill be guided by the artist himself! FREE
with museum admission...

Other upcoming Not Your Average Tours:

June 14 at 7pm - Dory Nason (UBC: Indigenous Feminism; Idle No More)
and Andrea Walsh (UVic: Visual Anthropology)

June 21 at 6pm - Daniel Justice (Chair, UBC First Nations and
Indigenous Studies Program)

For more information, please see the following link:
[http://moa.ubc.ca/portfolio_page/not-your-average-tour/](http://moa.ubc.ca/portfolio_page/not-your-average-tour/)

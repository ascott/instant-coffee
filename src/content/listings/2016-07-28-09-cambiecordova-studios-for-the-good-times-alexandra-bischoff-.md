#305 Cambie St, Vancouver BC, V6B 2N4
Sunday, July 31st
12-6pm

A performance of absurd warmth towards an arguably ambivalent 
space, Alexandra Bischoff welcomes visitors to observe her prolonged 
farewell to the Studio on Sunday, July 31st. Both a sentimental and 
practical occasion, the artist will feel the emptied space one more time, 
for the good times. The performance will conclude by returning keys to 
the landlord.

Drop in between 12-6pm, and knock on the door of #305 Cambie St to 
be let in by a studio attendant.

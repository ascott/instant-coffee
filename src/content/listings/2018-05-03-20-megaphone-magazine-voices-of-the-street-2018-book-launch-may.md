May 16, 2018. 6:30 - 9:00 PM
Tickets: $10 at megaphonemagazine.com
Lost + Found Cafe, 33 W. Hastings St.

Join Megaphone May 16th for a night of readings to celebrate our 8th Annual
Literary Edition, Voices of the Street.

The night will feature heartfelt stories, readings from Voices of the
Street writers, music and delicious refreshments courtesy of SFU's Vancity
Office of Community Engagement.

Join us for snacks and refreshments from 6:30 to 7:00pm; program starts
promptly at 7:00pm. Voices of the Street will be available for purchase
from Megaphone vendors for $10 (either cash or with the Megaphone cashless
payment app available for iPhone and Android) before and after the show.

Co-presented by Megaphone and SFU's Vancity Office of Community Engagement.

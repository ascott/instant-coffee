The On Edge Reading Series presents:

Doretta Lau + Chelsea Rooney 
7 pm, Thursday, March 24
in the ECUAD Library

The public is welcome.

Doretta Lau's debut short story collection, How Does a Single Blade of 
Grass Thank the Sun? (Nightwood Editions, 2014), was shortlisted for 
the City of Vancouver Book Award, longlisted for the Frank O'Connor 
International Short Story Award, and was named by The Atlantic as 
one of the best books of 2014. CBC selected her as a Writer to Watch. 
In 2013, she was a finalist for the Writers' Trust of Canada / McClelland 
& Stewart Journey Prize. She lives in Hong Kong, where she is an 
editor for a visual culture museum, and is working on a novel and a 
screenplay.

Chelsea Rooney grew up in the Annapolis Valley, Nova Scotia and 
lives in Vancouver, British Columbia. She is a host of the women's 
program The Storytelling Show on Vancouver Co-op Radio, and a 
teacher of writing and mindfulness to teenagers. Her debut novel Pedal 
launched in 2014 with Caitlin Press. It was nominated for the 
Amazon.ca First Novel Award and the ReLit Award for Fiction.

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the 
Arts and the Coast Salish peoples whose traditional unceded territories 
we are on.

Angela Meyers presents NO Bed Bugs. The name is a tongue-and-cheek tip of the
hat to East Vancouver and the lines that blur between high and low brow arts. It also
is an acknowledgement of the hurdles and learning curves an artist must undergo in
order to learn a new art form and it's process whilst staying humble to the pitfalls of a
novice embarking on a new experience. This screen print curation, which is the
result of various screen printing techniques Angela
developed in residency as an
inhouse print-media artist with Malaspina Printmakers, takes a look into the past of
pop culture and its trends, inspired from the 1890s & 1990s art house.

Angela is a Vancouver based artist, born and raised in the midwest of the United
States. She is a multi-disciplinary mixed media artist, with a primary focus on
animation, film, and screen printing. She received her Bachelor of Arts in theatre and
film from the University of Kansas, and she holds a diploma of Classical Animation
from Vancouver Film School.

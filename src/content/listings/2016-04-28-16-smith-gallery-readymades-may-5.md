Readymades
Artists: Arabella Campbell, Douglas Coupland, Stan Douglas, Gathie
Falk, Geoffrey Farmer, Rodney Graham, Brian Jungen, Damian
Moppett, Mina Totino, Ian Wallace, David Weir

Opening reception Thursday May 5 from 7 to 9pm
Exhibition: May 6 to August 27

The Smith Gallery presents Readymades, artists selected by Gordon
Smith

Readymades is a diverse exhibition unified by explorations of many of
the various categories of readymade-ism, including found objects,
found images, assisted readymades and assemblage. Celebrating the
approximate 100th anniversary of the readymade, this exhibition was
catalyzed by Gordon Smith's viewing of a Duchamp exhibition at
Gagosian (NYC) in 2014. The exhibition will provide an opportunity for
viewers to reconsider the state of the readymade today, assess its
increasingly large role in much of contemporary art, as well as
assaying the particular methodologies eleven British Columbia artists
have 'chosen' to use in their practice.

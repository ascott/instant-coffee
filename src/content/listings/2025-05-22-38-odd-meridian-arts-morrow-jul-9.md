Bring Your Own Body performance series, only child + 3/4 echo
Performances by Ziyian Kwan, Calder White + Ziggy Zaya

Wednesday July 9 - Sunday July 13, 7PM
Sliding scale tickets

Odd Meridian Arts presents Bring Your Own Body, four distinct shows choreographed
by local artists and special guests from Quebec and Ontario. From poetic and lyric to
kooky and irreverent, all shows will be at Odd Meridians cultural space, Morrow, where
the vibe is chill and intimate. Join us for contemporary dance that summons prowess
and play!

In 3/4 echo by Ziyian Kwan and Calder White, Ziyian and DJ Ziggy Zaya spin sound,
mix movement and comb space. In Calders solo only child, fantasy becomes reality in
vignettes that draw from queer intimacy and kink practices.

School
Friday October 28st, 2016, 6-9pm
Concourse Art Gallery
1399 Johnston St, Vancouver, BC

Working and practicing under the context of the institution offers a top
down methodology in education. In this exhibition contributors will utilize
the Concourse Gallery to encourage and promote alternative methods of
pedagogy, employing peer and self-education as models. The facilitation of
critical discourse, curiosity, and the investigation of topics often set
aside in institutional settings for example, the ballroom culture of the
LGBTQ Community, will create an exploration into esoteric facets of student
based knowledge and bring to the forefront relevant and critical discussion
of established educational structures. The works on site will examine
learning strategies through how-to guides, discussions, tutorials,
demonstrations, lectures, readings, crits and talks, interpreting them
through events, happenings, instructional works, painting, sculpture,
video, installations and performance.

Physical reminiscence of previous happenings will incorporate with the
exhibition as it develops. Modes of documentation, photographs, recordings
and writing, will accumulate over the duration of the exhibition and will
then be reworked and incorporated into the space.

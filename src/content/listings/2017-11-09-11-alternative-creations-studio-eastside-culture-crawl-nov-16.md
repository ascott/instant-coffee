Alternative Creations Studio will be open for the 4-day visual arts festival Eastside 
Culture Crawl. All are invited to peruse our artwork, meet our artists/art facilitators and 
enjoy some hot cocoa.

Studio Statement
Alternative Creations Studio provides the space and opportunity for people with 
developmental disabilities to showcase their artistic skills and to express themselves 
creatively. Our studio established a cooperative in 2013, which supports 18 artists who 
excel at painting, cartooning, sketching and mixed media work. We pride ourselves in 
collaborating with others through sharing studio space; developing friendships and 
building resources. The vibrant projects and workshops created enrich the lives of 
artists, their families and community members. Alternative Creations Studio is an art 
studio, supported by posAbilities.

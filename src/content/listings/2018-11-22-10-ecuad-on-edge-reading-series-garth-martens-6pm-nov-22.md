The On Edge Reading Series invites you to hear:

Garth Martens
Thursday Nov 22 - 6 pm
at the library, Emily Carr University of Art & Design,
520 East 1st Avenue

Everyone is welcome.

Bio:

Garth Martens won the Bronwen Wallace Award for Emerging Writers in 2011, and his 
first book Prologue for the Age of Consequence (House of Anansi Press) was a Finalist 
for the 2014 Governor General's Literary Award in Poetry. His chapbook Remediation 
was published by JackPine Press in 2018 in collaboration with visual artist Chelsea 
Rushton. A nine-year student of flamenco, Martens is a member of Palabra Flamenco, 
an ensemble that combines traditional flamenco dance, guitar, and song with English-
language poetry. They performed thirteen performances of their one-hour show, La 
Palabra en el Tiempo, at theatre and literary festivals in Victoria and Vancouver.

We gratefully acknowledge that these readings take place on unceded Coast Salish 
lands and thank the Canada Council for the Arts for its support.

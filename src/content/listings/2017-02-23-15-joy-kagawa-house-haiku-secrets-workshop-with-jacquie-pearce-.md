Haiku Secrets: beyond the basics of writing haiku - workshop with Jacquie Pearce
1-2:30pm at Joy Kogawa House, 1450 W 64th Ave (3 blocks east of Granville St)

The best haiku can express a riveting moment of awe or insight and contain a surprising 
depth of implied story. Yet haiku is one of the shortest and simplest forms of poetry. This 
workshop will explore some of the characteristics and techniques that give these tiny 
poems their big impact. We'll look at examples of modern haiku from around the world, 
including winning haiku from the Vancouver Cherry Blossom Festival international haiku 
contest.

3:00 -4:30pm - Haiku Koinobori Painting workshop. There will be an opportunity to paint 
haiku on a giant koi fish scale for the Koinobori sculpture installation, displayed at 
Sakura Days Japan Fair in April.

Register for one or both of these activities. Children are welcome to join the Koinobori 
activity if accompanied by an adult. RSVP to info@kogawahouse.com (admission by 
donation) 

Jacquie Pearce is the author of several novels for children, as well as poetry for adults. 
Her haiku have been published in a variety of journals and anthologies and received 
recognition in several years of the Vancouver Cherry Blossom Festival haiku contest, 
including "Best Vancouver Haiku" in 2015.

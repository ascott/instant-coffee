Call for Artists
South Hill Public Art Plan Committee
Budget: $13,000 (including $5,000 artist fee)
Deadline: Friday, March 25, 2011 @ 5pm.

The South Hill Public Art Plan Committee seeks to contract the
services of a Lead Artist to oversee a grant received from the Community
and Neighbourhood Arts Development Program to produce a public art
project for the business district of South Hill on Fraser Street.

The artwork commissioned will be part of a growing collection of
art in the public space as envisioned in and guided by the South Hill
Public Art Plan. This project will transform nondescript streetscape
surfaces into platforms for creative and distinct artwork reflecting the
community's unique character.
This is a part time position for a 6-8 month contract with
approximate project start-up in April, 2011. The working budget for this
project is $13,000. This includes an artist design fee of $5,000 and
accounts for production costs, project management and a mentoring
component. The position is intended for a candidate who has a thorough
understanding of contemporary art practice, is an excellent
communicator, creative thinker and possesses the necessary
administrative and managerial skills to continue the South Hill Public
Art Program. The Lead Artist will report to the South Hill BIA Public
Art Committee and the South Hill BIA Executive Board.

To view Lead Artist Terms of Reference and be introduced to the
South Hill Art Plan please visit: www.southhillbia.ca.

For more specific inquiries contact:
info@southhillbia.ca.

2009 Call for proposals / Deadline January 31, 2009

1. ARTIST-IN-RESIDENCE, RESEARCH AND CREATION
Digital image manipulation in contemporary art and large-format digital
printing.

2. EXHIBITION PROJECTS (artists and curators)
SAGAMIE will produce a publication accompanying each exhibition project.
CARFAC/RAAV fee will be paid to the artists.

3. PUBLISHING RESIDENCIES
Creation projects using the book as the medium and art object.

4. LONG-TERM PROJECTS
The SAGAMIE Centre is interested in working with artists to develop
long-term research and creation projects that last one or two years.
During this period, the artist will have several successive residency
periods. The corpus produced will be presented in a solo exhibition, and
the organization will produce a publication describing the project's
development.

The proposal must include :
1. Clearly indicate the program for which you are applying (Program : 1,
2, 3, or 4).
2. Indicate if you are under or over 35 years old.
3. Description of the project (residency, exhibition, publication)
(maximum: 1 page).
4. Artistic approach (maximum: 1 page).
5. CV (maximum: 3 pages).
6. Visual documentation : small images (about 5x7" at 72 dpi) on a CD;
slides; prints; or other.
7. Publishing: Description of collaborators, Curricula vitae of
collaborators (author or other) if needed.
8. A self-addressed stamped envelope if you wish the proposal to be
returned.

22. Fillip | seeks interns | JAN 31
Fillip seeks interns in Administration, Marketing, and Design

Fillip is currently seeking student interns for 2009 to assist in the
day-to-day operations of our contemporary arts magazine. This unpaid
internship requires 8 to 10 hours per week (5 hours onsite / 5 hours
offsite). Academic credit is available for ECU students. All candidates
should be self motivated, clean, methodical, and able to commit for at
least 9 months.

Specifically, Fillip seeks interns in the following areas:

Administration & Library
This internship will focus on general office administration and
coordination of Fillip's extensive publications library. The ideal
candidate should have strong organizational skills and a keen interest in
contemporary art publications and artists' books. They will be responsible
for processing incoming and outgoing mail on a regular basis, and therefore
should be able to commit to a stable weekly schedule.

Marketing & Advertising
This internship will assist Fillip's Publisher expand marketing and
advertising projects in the print and online versions of the magazine. The
ideal candidate will have or seek to develop creative problem solving
skills, strong email and phone communication, and a strong working
knowledge of the local (and international) art community.

Design & Production
This internship will provide support to Fillip's Art Director in designing
and laying out the publication, website, and collateral materials. The
ideal candidate will have experience using the Adobe Creative Suite, a keen
sense of typography, and strong production skills. The main task of this
internship is to create new print materials for the publication. Candidates
for this internship must submit a portfolio of work for review.

Candidates should send a letter of interest and CV by email or post to
fillip@fillip.ca or to #305 Cambie Street Vancouver, BC V6B 2N4.

23. Video Party | Call for Submissions: Consumer Spirit | Montreal | JAN 31

Call for submissions:

We’re looking for new content for our next round of screenings...

Consumerism
Short films and videos about western materialism, developing economies and
the manufacturing sector, (un)sustainable development, the media that
creates the consumer, or your favorite shoes. Video Party is ideologically
neutral, so our goal is to showcase films that encourage critical thinking
about our current global economy as much as films that will make our
audiences drool over images of excessive bling.

Spirituality
God may have died, but we’re still here, so what remains? We’re looking for
poetic films about connecting with your spiritual side, critical docs about
organized religion, intimate portraits of your Priest, Rabbi or Imam and
hidden camera exposés from your last scientology meeting.

Details online at: http://www.videoparty.info/submissions.html

Mail your DVDs to:
Video Party
5720 Ferrier
Montreal , Qc
H4P 1M7

(Please include a brief synopsis of your work, runtime and your contact
information).

If you have a film or video that does not relate to the current themes, you
can still submit your work for a future event.

Best regards,
Josh Usheroff
Video Party Coordinator
www.videoparty.info

Video Party showcases original independent short films and videos in a
party setting. Our goal is to provide an exciting and engaging environment
for audience members to view films, interact with media makers and get a
little wild. Every event is curated based on a given theme. Only the
finest, most innovative content makes it to the big screen. For more
information and a list of selected films please visit www.videoparty.info
or check out the Video Party Facebook group.

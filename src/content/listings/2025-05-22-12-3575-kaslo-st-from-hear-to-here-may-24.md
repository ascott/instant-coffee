We are excited to launch our From Hear to Here group exhibition with former alumni
and Curator, Shanna Cheng, in partnership with Childrens Hearing and Speech
Centre of BC.

From Hear to Here celebrates our mentor-mentee cohort from their peer
engagement program supporting BIPOC, d/Deaf and Hard of Hearing, emerging
artists and youth in a co-learning workshop series from October 2024 to March 2025,
culminating in an exhibition.

What began as an educational workshop on the Disability Justice framework,
leadership advocacy, and alternative artmaking, developed into an exchange of
creativity, exploration, and connection between our cohort members. From Hear to
Here has evolved into a virtual space to gain professional development skills in
pursuing a career in the arts and to encourage vitality within our own intersectional
disability identities.

Join us with our mentor, Mellissa He with mentees, Aedyn and AJ and mentor
Courage Bacchus and mentee, Thomas, in this exhibition which celebrates our
cohort in their achievements throughout the 6-month long program. We invite you to
join curator, Shanna and educators of CHSC in an interactive workshop series
throughout. What does inclusive art look like to you?

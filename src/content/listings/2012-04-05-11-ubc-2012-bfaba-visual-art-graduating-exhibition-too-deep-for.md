TOO DEEP FOR YOU | UBC 2012 BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating 
Exhibition | April 19 2012

This year, UBC's annual BFA/BA Visual Art graduating exhibition is 
TOO DEEP FOR YOU. The exhibition showcases the ambitious final 
projects of graduating Visual Art students. Materially and 
methodologically diverse, artworks in the exhibition are 
representative of students' heterogeneous areas of inquiry, and of 
their ongoing negotiation of critical thinking and material artistic 
practice. 

Come see the work of emerging young talent in Vancouver! 

Opening Reception: April 19 2012, 6-9 PM

Exhibition runs April 19 - May 5th
Monday to Friday, 12-5 PM

AHVA Library Gallery (rm 112, koerner library, 1958 main mall),
Dorothy Somerset Studios (6361 university blvd),
BC Binning Studios (6373 university blvd)

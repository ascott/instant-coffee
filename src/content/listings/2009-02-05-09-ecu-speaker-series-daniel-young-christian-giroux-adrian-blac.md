Emily Carr University Speaker Series presents:

Film does not equal Sculpture: Two Toronto sculpture practices experiment
with film.

A lecture and presentation by: Daniel Young, Christian Giroux and Adrian
Blackwell

Wednesday February 11, 7 pm. FREE

ECU South building # 301

Daniel Young and Christian Giroux are visual artists based in Toronto and
Guelph, respectively. Since 2001 they have pursued investigations of form
and materiality in sculpture within the expanded field of architecture and
landscape design. They employ industrial materials and prototyping
methodolgies to produce works that reflect upon the history of abstaction,
modernist generative models and the imbrication of social meaning in
phenomenal experience. Although primarily object makers, they also deploy
maquettes, drawings and film to explore the structural logic of objects and
spaces. Their work has been exhibited at the Power Plant in Toronto,
Aceartinc In Winnipeg and through the Public Arts Projects Initiative of the
Scope Miami Art Fair. Their first major public commission, Reticulated
Gambol, will be unveiled at Lee Centre Park in the fall of 2008, and their
first film, Every Building, or Site, that a Building Permit was Issued for a
New Building, premiered this fall, at the Cinematque Ontario. They are
represented by Diaz Contemporary in Toronto.

Adrian Blackwell is a visual artist whose work on forces of uneven
development produced by Postfordist urbanization. His sculpture and
photography use a rigorously minimal vocabulary of forms to propose
locations for potential activity and to examine separations in urban space.
His work has been exhibited at artist run centres and museums across Canada
including, Modern Fuel, The New Gallery, Mercer Union, The Art Gallery of
Hamilton, The Power Plant and The Mackenzie Art Gallery, at Torontoâ&#65533;&#65533;s 2006
Nuit Blanche, the 2005 Shenzhen Biennale of Urbanism / Architecture, and
LACE Gallery in Los Angeles. Blackwell co-edited Unboxed: Engagements in
Social Space in 2005 with Jen Budney. In 2008 he had a solo exhibition at
the Art Gallery of Mississauga and his film Night equals day premiered at
the Cinematheque Ontario in a program entitled Cinema and Disjunction.

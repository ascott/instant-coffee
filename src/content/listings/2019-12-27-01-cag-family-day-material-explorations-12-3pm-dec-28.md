Family Day | Material Explorations

Saturday, December 28, 12-3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street,
V6B 6R5

On the last Saturday of every month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition
tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Join us for this multi-station Family Day to experiment with natural materials pulled from
the local environment and industrial supplies to create your own unique artwork.

All ages and abilities welcome!

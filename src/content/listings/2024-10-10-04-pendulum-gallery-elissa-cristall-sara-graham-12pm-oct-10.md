ARTIST TALK | Elissa Cristall Projects | Sara Graham THE SPACES THAT WERE
MISSED HAVE BEEN REARRANGED
Artist Talk & Tour: Thursday October 10, 12 - 12:30pm
Exhibition dates: October 7 - November 1, 2024
Location: Pendulum Gallery, 885 West Georgia Street, Vancouver

Join us on a journey through Port Moody-based artist Sara Graham's work as she
takes us on a tour of her exhibition "The Spaces That Were Missed Have Been
Rearranged". Graham talks about the intent, the stories, and the processes behind
her work.

Sara Graham's work is primarily concerned with issues and ideas that surround and
make up the contemporary city. Her current practice is predominately rooted in
drawing and one of her central activities is exploring different ways and means to
transform a
viewers perception of place using forms of architecture, forms of
storytelling, forms of mapping or some or all the above. "The Spaces That Were
Missed Have Been Rearranged" brings together 10 years of Graham's studio
practice and her observations of the city. She writes, "The artwork in this exhibition is
informed by my daily observations of the urban form, and how spaces and places
interpolate as one moves through them and where points of views are constantly
shifting and revealing new perspectives".

Fata Morgana is a new body of work about Vancouver created for this exhibition.
Fata Morgana is a type of mirage that produces multiple overlapping images that
actively change and distort creating the deceptive appearance of distant objects. The
work in the exhibition includes photography, drawing, collage and maquettes.

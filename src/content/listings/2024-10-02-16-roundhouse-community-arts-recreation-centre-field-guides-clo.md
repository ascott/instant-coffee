Field Guides Closer Conversations
Sun Oct 6, 1pm to 3:30pm.
Roundhouse Exhibition Hall

181 Roundhouse Mews

FREE no registration required.

Listen to stories from artists and culture facilitators of encounters and connections with
people in their neighborhoods. Introduction by Gitxsan and Nisgaa cultural facilitator
Leona Brown, Indigenous Advocate for the Vancouver Urban Food Forest Foundation
with cameo appearances by Opera Lirica Community Choir and young writer Emery
Point.

This talk is part of FIELD GUIDES: Celebrating creative hubs for arts, food security and
environmental stewardship. The exhibition runs September 27 " October 6 and is
presented by Vancouver Park
Board " Arts, Reconciliation and Culture (ARC) in
partnership with the Roundhouse Society, hosted on the unceded, ancestral territories of
the xmky"m (Musqueam), Swxw7mesh (Squamish) and slilwta (Tsleil-
Waututh) Nations.

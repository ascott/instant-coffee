For the third year in a row, the PinchukArtCentre starts the online 
application procedure of the Future Generation Art Prize 2014.

As you may know, Future Generation Art Prize is the highest rated prize 
open to all artists up to the age of 35 with the aim of acknowledging and 
giving long-term support to a future generation of artists from all over the 
world.

We would like to ask for your support in distributing information on the 
Prize and in convincing those young artists you represent to apply. If 
you could send us their names, we will pay special attention to review 
their applications.

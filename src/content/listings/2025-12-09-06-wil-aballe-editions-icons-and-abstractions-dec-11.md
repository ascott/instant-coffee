The gallery is pleased to present Editions, Icons and Abstractions, a curated selection of
works from $100 to under $4,000 that brings together intimate pieces by a wide range of
exhibiting artists and secondary market works. The exhibition highlights the power of
small-scale works to hold complex histories, gestures, and ideas. Editions, Icons and
Abstractions offers an accessible entry point for new and seasoned collectors alike,
emphasizing the pleasure of living with art and the generosity inherent in sharing it.

FUNDRAISING

As part of our mezzanine presentation, Editions, Icons and Abstractions, we are pleased to
include the five remaining available prints of Jin-me Yoon's edition, available at the
inexpensive price of $500, framed.

The sale of these prints will 100% benefit VIVO; neither the artist, nor the gallery will take a
portion of the proceeds. VIVO is raising urgent funds to protect their space, preserve the
Crista Dahl Media Library and Archive, and keep essential services available to artists.
Every artwork purchased helps them stay open, support staff, and maintain accessible
equipment and studio resources. Your support ensures VIVO remains a home for creation,
experimentation, and community in Vancouver.

Editions, Icons and Abstractions will also feature HANK BULL's new photographic portolfio
of 12 prints, Sequoia Symbiosis, commissioned by the City of Vancouver Public Art
Program. It will be on display until March 2026.

The images in Sequoia Symbiosis are based on a stained-glass window design for the
King Edward Transit Station in Vancouver. The work shows various landmarks from Coast
Salish territories -- mountain peaks, the Two Sisters, sequoia trees on Cambie Boulevard,
a pine tree in Lighthouse Park, a large rock in Sechelt Inlet, covered in lichen.

Call for Submissions: Sled Island 2018

The Sled Island Music & Arts Festival (June 20 - 24, 2018 in Calgary, Alberta) is 
currently accepting project proposals for the festival's 2018 visual art programming.

Sled Island welcomes submissions of artistic and performative projects in the form of 
visual, sculptural and new media installations; performances; and critical art writing. 
Projects do not need to contain a direct link to music to be included, but applicants are 
encouraged to take the sites, sounds and experiences of Sled Island into consideration 
when crafting proposals. We want to hear your ideas about how visual art can transform 
spaces, challenge disciplinary boundaries and initiate new ways of engaging with our 
festival and our city!

The deadline to apply is February 16, 2018 at 11:59pm MST. 

Learn more and apply at SledIsland.com/Apply.

Volumes: Works in Paper
November 29, 2013 – January 19, 2014
 
The Burnaby Art Gallery is pleased to announce its upcoming exhibition Volumes: Works in Paper. This
special exhibition will feature installation works by emerging artists like Graham Landin, Maggie Boyd,
Jennifer Rose Sciarrino and 2012 Contemporary Art Society Emerging Artist’s Prize winner Nicole Ondre.
 
We welcome you to join us at our opening reception on Thursday, November 28, 7-9pm.
 
Please see the attached press release for artist biographies and programming details (available in English,
Korean and Chinese). Images and photo credits can be downloaded here.
 
Please feel free to contact me if you have further questions or request interviews with the curator and/or
artists.

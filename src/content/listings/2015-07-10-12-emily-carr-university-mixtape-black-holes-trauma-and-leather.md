Mixtape: black holes, trauma and leather
Emily Carr of Art And Design MAA Interim Exhibition

July 16 at 6:30pm - 10pm
Concourse and Media Gallery
Emily Carr University Art + Design

Mixtape: Black Holes, Trauma and Leather is the interim exhibition from
students in the Masters of Applied Art at Emily Carr University of Art and
Design. Join seven artists using media that traverse film, soundscape,
sculpture and photography for the opening reception on July 16 at 6:30pm
until 10pm in the Concourse and Media Gallery of Emily Carr University Art
+ Design. The show runs through July 26, 2015.

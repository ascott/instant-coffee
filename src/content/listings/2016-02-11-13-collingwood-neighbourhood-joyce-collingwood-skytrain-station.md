Call for Artists - Joyce Collingwood Skytrain Station Mural Project
Collingwood Neighbourhood House
Vancouver, BC
Artist Fee: $4,750
Submission Deadline: Sunday, February 14, 2016

Collingwood Neighbourhood House is seeking submissions from artists to lead
a temporary mural project that will be placed on hoarding during
construction at Joyce-Collingwood Skytrain Station until December 2017.
Preference will be given to those who live or work in the
Renfrew-Collingwood neighbourhood.

Link to Call: http://www.cnh.bc.ca/event/

For additional information or questions please contact Crecien Bencio at
604-435-0323 or at cbencio@cnh.bc.ca

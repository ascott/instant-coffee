Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist talk with Featured
Karl Mata Hipol in the Art Rental & Sales showroom on Friday, April 11th at 6PM.

Karl Mata Hipol delves into the realm of archival photography, drawing from museum
collections to unearth the gaps and erasures of Filipino representation within
mainstream art institutions. Archival Abloom cultivates a dialogue on the invisibility of
Filipinos despite their deep-rooted contributions to Canadas cultural, social, and
economic landscape. By cross-pollinating archival materials with personal and
cultural memory, Hipol engages in a broader conversation on decolonizing museums
and diversifying historical narratives to allow marginalized voices to flourish.

His series, "Weaving Heritage " Atang," germinates new archival narratives by
intertwining Ilocano cultural heritage with historical fragments. Motivated by two
photographic slides from the Whyte Museum of the Canadian Rockies"depicting
cemetery scenes in Manila"Hipol reimagines these images and expands the fonds
by collecting native flowers from British Columbia. Interwoven with Ilocano Inabel
textile motifs, these compositions bloom as acts of reclamation, transforming static
records into dynamic, living histories.

Through this visual intervention, Hipol invites viewers to confront and celebrate the
rich tapestry of Filipino heritage within the Canadian landscape"one that has long
existed yet remains largely unacknowledged. Archival Abloom serves as both a
tribute and a challenge: a call to recognize, honour, and reframe the narratives of
those who have been left on the other side of historys fence.

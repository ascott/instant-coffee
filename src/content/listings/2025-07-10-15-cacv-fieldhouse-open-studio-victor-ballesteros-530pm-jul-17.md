The Community Arts Council of Vancouver is pleased to invite you to an open studio
with our first Fieldhouse artist-in-residence, Victor Ballesteros.

Thursday, July 17th
Time: 5:30 PM - 8:30 PM
Location: McBride Park Fieldhouse
2049 Waterloo Street, Vancouver, BC V6R 1N6
(inside McBride Park, off West 4th Avenue)

Victor Ballesteros is an artist and filmmaker based in Vancouver, Canada. During his
time at the McBride Fieldhouse artist residency, Ballesteros has further developed his
project Arauco Plywall (2023"present), which inquires into the production and trade of
plywood and its use in the architecture of galleries and museum display. The use of
this material in art exhibitions, through backing in regular and modular walls, shelves,
and projection screens, is of particular interest to his practice, as it provides a critical
lens through which to examine the geopolitics and extractive economies of the
Americas in relation to the history of art display and exhibition architecture.

Ballesteros began his residency at McBride Fieldhouse on May 7, 2025, and will
continue through to the end of July.

Call for Applications: Kasahara Gabriola Trust Artist Residency 2026
Explore your Art on beautiful Gabriola Island
The Kasahara Gabriola Trust (KGT) is calling
for applications for the 2026 Artist-in-
Residence program on Gabriola Island, BC.

Open to all artistic disciplines.

Residency runs

January 16 to April 12, 2026

April 17 to July 03, 2026

September 12 to December 05, 2026

Application deadline is July 16, 2025

Apply online at artsgabriola.ca



31. SELLing/WANTing/RENTing/SUBLETing/ANNOUNCINGing
1.
221A | Artist Studios available
221A is offering new rental incentives for artists collectives and collaboratives for
several upcoming vacant units. The incentives may include up to three (3) months of
free rent to enable gathering, collaboration, and cross-pollination. Get in touch via
spaces@221a.ca to view collective-friendly vacancies and discuss rental rates.

221A operates non-residential artist studios set on a cost-recovery basis, which means
the unit rental rates cover the
proportionate costs of each facility. Rates for artist
studios range from $1.30 per square foot to $2.82 per square foot, and include utilities,
internet, waste & recycling, as well as regular cleaning and maintenance of common
areas.

View all current vacancies at https://221a.ca/housing-studios-and-work-spaces/all-
vacancies/

2.
825 Pacific | Coworking at 221A
Coworking at 221A

221A's Coworking space at 825 Pacific is inspired by thoughtfully designed public
libraries and the experience of sharing space with friends at school. Writers, editors,
graphic and interaction designers, industrial designers, architects and engineers, arts
administrators, students and researchers: we welcome you to join us!

Book a tour with our Membership & Events Coordinator via https://221a.ca/housing-
studios-and-work-spaces/coworking/, or reach out via email at coworking@221a.ca.

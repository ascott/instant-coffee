16. Drifter Video | call for proposals | deadline | SEP 1
Drifter Video is asking for submissions for a group video
exhibition planned for early September. We are looking for a broad range 
of submissions from students, emerging artists, established artists and 
videographers who work in single-channel video. 
Deadline for submission is Monday September 1, 2008.
We accept MiniDV and non-copyright DVD, please include a CV and brief artist statement.
Please send exhibition copies only.
Only materials that include a self-addressed, stamped envelope 
(Canadian postage) will be returned.
Unfortunately , Artist Fees cannot be paid.

On Saturday, January 21st from 8pm-11pm, VIVO Media Arts Centre will host a 
Vancouver installment of Bring Your Own Beamer (BYOB). The event, conceived by 
Dutch-Brazilian artist Rafal Rozendaal, is a one-night screening where artists display 
their video or film work using their own projector. Formats of all varieties will be 
presented simultaneously, across the walls of VIVO's 2,000 square foot studio space, 
backed by a crowd-sourced soundtrack.

$5 at the door (no one turned away)
Free for participants
Cash bar

If you would like to present as a participating artist, please email your name and 
film/video format of choice to info@vivomediaarts.com by Thursday, January 19th 2017 
by noon. Participating artists are asked to install their projector, media player, and video 
file between 6:00pm - 7:30pm on the night of the event. All submissions will be 
accepted.

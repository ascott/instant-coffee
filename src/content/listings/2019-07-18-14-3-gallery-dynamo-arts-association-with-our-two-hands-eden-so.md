with our two hands

Eden Solomon 

Saturday July 20, 2019, 7pm 

Number 3 Gallery @ Dynamo Arts Association 

103-30 East 6th Avenue 

a work-in-progress exploring resilience through the passing down of knowledge, and the 
similarity between peoples ways of doing things. making bread with our two hands. 
passing down what we know. 

with our two hands is a one night installation and performative event, with the 
performance beginning at 8 pm. 

With gratitude we acknowledge that this event will take place on the unceded, traditional 
and ancestral Indigenous Coast Salish territory of the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-
Waututh First Nations.

Canadian-Eritrean artist Eden Solomon grew up in Montreal and now mostly lives in 
Vancouver. She is interested in the interconnectedness of things. 

Number 3 Gallery is a mobile curatorial project initiating collaboration with emerging 
contemporary artists, thinkers and spaces and is curated by Julie D. Mills and Julia 
Lamare.

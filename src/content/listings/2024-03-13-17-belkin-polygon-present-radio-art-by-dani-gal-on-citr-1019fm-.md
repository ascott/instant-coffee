Radio Art by Dani Gal on CiTR 101.9FM
Wednesdays at 11 am beginning March 20

The Belkin and the Polygon Gallery present Radio Art by Dani Gal on CiTR 101.9FM
radio, an
eleven-week program of the Gal's art for radio. Through his in-depth
research and technical experimentation, Gal draws our attention to a vast,
distributed archive of sound documents and their role in the workings of ideology and
the production of cultural memory. Gal understands radio as an acousmatic
instrument after the Greek accousma or a "thing heard." And these transmissions
on CiTR 101.9FM invite us " as embodied listeners " to consider the relationship of
political events to acoustic events.

Throughout the eleven weeks of transmissions, we hear a full spectrum of Gals
approaches to art for radio: from selected episodes of Different Time Different Place
Different Pitch, which was composed live by Gal (in collaboration with Achim
Lengerer) as a series of radio programs originally broadcast by Documenta 14 /
Savvy Funk in the summer of 2017; to the two-part radio play entitled Vertiginous,
which was recorded in 2023 for the podcast On Certain Groundlessness, produced
by Ruth
Anderwald, Leonhard Grond and Sergio Edelsztein.

Dani Gal's work Failed to Bind (2013) is on view now in Aporia (Notes to a Medium)
(11 January-14 April 2024) and in the exhibition Historical Records at the Polygon
Gallery (9 March-14 July 2024).

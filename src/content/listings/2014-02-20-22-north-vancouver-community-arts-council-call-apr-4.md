Call for Exhibition Proposals

The North Vancouver Community Arts Council is currently accepting 
submissions for our Winter/Spring 2015 exhibition schedule at CityScape 
Community Art Space.

CityScape Community Art Space is a project of the North Vancouver 
Community Arts Council whose mission it is to celebrate and promote art and 
artists through quality experiences in a unique and inviting gallery space, and 
to create an inclusive environment, which enriches and encourages 
interaction in the community.

We accept proposals from all members of the public. There are no geographic 
restrictions. To encourage and celebrate emerging and established artists 
CityScape maintains an inclusive environment, and therefore always exhibits 
group shows with 3 or more artists. Artists are welcome to submit 
individually or as part of a group. Should an individual themed proposal be 
accepted, the North Vancouver Community Arts Council (NVCAC) will group 
him/her with additional artists through an open call or invite to other artists 
that fit in with their accepted themed proposal. Non-artists are also invited to 
submit ideas and concepts for exhibitions they would like to see in the gallery. 
CityScape is a unique and dynamic space, and we encourage diverse and 
creative themed proposals to inspire and engage the community.

Please see submission guidelines for CityScape Community Art Space below.

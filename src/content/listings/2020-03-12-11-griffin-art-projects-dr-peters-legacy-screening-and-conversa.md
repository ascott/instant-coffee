Griffin Art Projects

1174 Welch St, North Vancouver, BC V7P 1B2

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Join us for an engaging and thought-provoking afternoon in the gallery as we explore
the exhibitions main themes of activism, care, and HIV / AIDS through the lens of public
health. Griffin is pleased to partner with the Dr.Peter AIDS Foundation for a screening of
The Broadcast Tapes of Dr. Peter, followed by a conversation with local experts and
educators working in the
fields of public health and community engagement. Discussion
will reflect on Dr. Peters legacy and examine contemporary discourse surrounding HIV /
AIDS. Griffin is thrilled to welcome the films director, Vancouver-based Academy Award-
nominated documentary filmmaker David Paperny, who will be in attendance as a
special guest.

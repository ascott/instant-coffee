Samuel Roy-Bois

ARTIST TALK: Thursday, July 24th at 7pm

In conjunction with Let us, then, be up and doing; With a heart for any fate; Still achieving, Still 
pursuing; Learn to labor and to wait, his new installation for the Contemporary Art Gallery, 
Samuel Roy-Bois will present a visual history of his sculptural work, focusing on issues of 
engagement and materiality. In his talk, he will draw a map that leads us through this new work 
by relating it to his earlier practice. In all of his sculptural installations, Roy-Bois sets the viewer in 
motion or, more accurately, makes the viewer aware of their movements.

Roy-Bois is originally from Quebec City and is currently living in Vancouver. His installations have 
been shown across Canada and internationally, most recently in Divertissements at Point 
ephémère in Paris, 2007; Improbable and ridiculous at Musée d’art contemporain de Montréal in 
2005; and Faire l’independence at Quartier ephémère in Montréal, 2005.

The exhibition runs until Sunday, August 24, 2008.

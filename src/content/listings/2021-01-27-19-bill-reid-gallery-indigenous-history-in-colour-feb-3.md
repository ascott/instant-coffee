Bill Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art presents the Western Canadian premiere of
Indigenous History in Colour, February 3 " May 9, 2021. This solo exhibition by Luke
Parnell is a powerful exploration of the relationship between Northwest Coast Indigenous
oral histories, conceptual art, and traditional formline design. The exhibitions
multidisciplinary analysis of the shifting perspectives of Northwest Coast art in modern
history challenges contemporary discourse on notions of reconciliation and representation
today. A virtual opening celebration, featuring Parnell and Carter in conversation, will

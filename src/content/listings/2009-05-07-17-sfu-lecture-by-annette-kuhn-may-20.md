A public lecture by Professor Annette Kuhn (Professor of Film, Queen Mary, University of London)

"What to do with Cinema Memory"

Abstract:

Drawing on findings of my own research on cinema-going in Britain in the 1930s, as well as on other 
work on and examples of ‘cinema memory’ (including Victor Burgin’s The Remembered Film), I shall 
reflect on the characteristics of different types of cinema memory and how these variously engage
and combine the personal, the collective and the social; consider their conceptual and 
methodological implications; and possibly offer some suggestions for future research on media 
experience and cultural memory.

May 20, 6-8pm
SFU Vancouver Campus, HC 1700

All Welcome!

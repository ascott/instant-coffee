Neil Campbell responds to the work of Guo Fengyi	

Pubic Discussion

Neil Campbell on Guo Fengyi
Saturday February 11, 3pm
Vancouver based artist Neil Campbell talks about drawing and process 
in the context of the first North American solo exhibition of drawings by 
Chinese artist Guo Fengyi (1942‐2010). 
A self trained artist Fengyi belongs to an older generation whose 
embracing of Chinese folk culture combines with traditions of wisdom 
and myth. Her large‐scale works comprise intricate details and 
obsessive mark‐making which articulate ideas of spiritual and 
metaphysical significance. Like Fengyi, Campbell's work also takes into 
account the perceived energy of the human body.
Neil Campbell, a native of Saskatchewan, currently lives and works in 
Vancouver. His work has appeared in national and international 
galleries and institutions including the Contemporary Art Gallery, 
Vancouver; the Vancouver Art Gallery, Vancouver; Centre d'art 
Contemporain, Montreal; Andrea Rosen Gallery, New York City; Art and 
Public, Geneva; VeneKlassen Werner, Berlin; Magasin, Grenoble, 
France and Marianne Boesky Gallery, New York City. In 2011 he had 
solo exhibitions at Blanket Contemporary Art, Vancouver, and Galeria 
Franco Noero, Turin, Italy.

Absent-Minded Window Gazing

Exhibition 3: Vanessa Disler October 5 - 21, closing event on Oct 14, 3-
6pm

Absent-Minded Window-Gazing presents three emerging artists in a 
series over the span of 6 weeks. The three-part exhibition will take place 
in our front window. As you pass the window during this time there will be 
an installation and painting by Ian Edmonds, new painting by Israel Lund, 
and a series of prints by Vanessa Disler. Hardly absent-minded, the work 
of the artists results in a contemplative dialogue. Working by way of 
material abstraction the artists share a poetic quality that arrives through 
repositioning of common material, repetition and overall painterly 
tendencies.

Sunday October 14, 3 - 6pm
The Commons
119B East Pender

LIGHT SPHERES ARE LUMNESCENT BUT NUTRITION QUALIFIES WITHIN 
MAGNIFIED SOUNDS THAT VIBRATE PITCH & HOLLOW WEIGHT DARK 
TOWERS SHINE BULK FALSLY WHILE PEOPLE SLEEP DREAMING 
PROJECTIONS BENEATH CATASTROPHIC BEAMS CONFISCATED 
THOUGHTS THROUGH TIME SPIRALS SACRED SANCTIONS BORDER 
CRYPTIC KNOWLEDGE PRINTED SIGNED EDITIONED AND SOLD. 
SATELLITES CHANGE REPTILIAN PATTERNS LUMINOUS STORMS 
NAVIGATE BY PASSIVE STEALTH SINCE THE BIRTH OF EDEN

Featuring performances by Luciana D'Anunciacao, Lauren Marsden and Liz 
Knox

Opening reception January 24, 8:00PM

Marsden received her BFA from the University of Victoria, BC and her MFA in 
Social Practice from the California College of the Arts (CCA) in San 
Francisco. She has worked for Stanford University, CCA, The Wattis Institute, 
Emily Carr University of Art + Design, and Langara College.She now lives in 
Vancouver, BC and is the Editor in Chief of Decoy Magazine, an online 
platform for art events and critical writing

Liz Knox studied Studio Art at the University of Guelph and lived and worked 
in Toronto before moving to Vancouver to pursue her Masters Degree in Visual 
Arts at Emily Carr University. Her work has been shown in exhibitions across 
Canada and internationally. Recent exhibitions include G Gallery, Toronto; 
Eyelevel Gallery, Halifax; Lawton Gallery, University of Wisonsin; Vtape, 
Toronto; the Justina M. Barnicke Gallery, Toronto; and Ephemeroptera, 
Brooklyn. 

Luciana D'Anunciacao is a Brazilian interdisciplinary artist based in 
Vancouver, Canada, since 2007. Her works draw from performance, dance, 
video and installation and has exhibited/performed in Canada, US, Brazil, 
Germany, UK and Poland. She is currently a Master of Fine Arts candidate at 
Simon Fraser University

RE: (The Response) is an exhibition featuring the work of Rebecca LaMarre,
Emiliano Sepulveda, Christopher Olson, and Laura Hatfield. It is a show
about the
nature of dialogue, the space&#8208;time continuum created within that exchange,
and the
game&#8208;like quality of conversation.

Subliminally underlying any dialogue is a hierarchy. This is something that is
inherent within the exchange of a statement and its response. Occupying the
topmost position of this ladder is the statement, with any following
responses being
immediately subservient while also having the ability to subvert the original
utterance. Statements make up the playing field while responses warp and
distort
the dimensional fabric of the field. The playing field within chess is
constantly
transformed by the actions of participants as they respond to each.

Rebecca LaMarre, and Emiliano Sepulveda have been working together to create a
series of photographic prints that is the result of an exquisite
corpse&#8208;like game.
They each devised a set of conditions for themselves to follow as they
independently
altered the images passed back and forth between themselves.

Christopher Olson's photo&#8208;based work explores the materiality of the
photographic
object in relation to the disappearance/obsolescence of analog photographic
tools in
the wake of digital image&#8208;making technologies. For this show he has refused to
allow curatorial intervention by responding to the call for work in secret. His
response to the theme of the show will be strategically revealed at the
opening.

In two performance pieces by Laura Hatfield gallery patrons will be invited to
participate in the creation of structural music exploring what she calls
'rhythmic
pack mentality'. The first piece will be a pulse in the gallery, not seen
but heard.
The second will be Call and Response For Two Crowds and will be performed at
9:30pm.

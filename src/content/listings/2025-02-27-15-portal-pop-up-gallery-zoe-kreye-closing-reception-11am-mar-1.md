PORTAL POP-UP GALLERY

Closing reception Saturday, March 1, 11am - 1pm

Artwork on display in
window gallery:
February 4 - March 1, 2025

Portal presents a series of evocative and radiant paintings by Vancouver-based Zoe
Kreye.

The paintings are made through a process that
includes an energetic and somatic
practice that heightens sensation in order to capture and read the deeper energy
present at the moment of painting. Kreyes studio has evolved into a private stage for
creating and performing rituals. These rituals emphasize body sensation, energy
reading, and spiritual connection. Often with eyes closed to engage with her inner
self, Kreye traces these experiences with ink on textiles, producing artworks that
invite us to feel more deeply. The ethereal patterns she creates echo embodied
movements and gestures, manifesting apparitions that invite viewers into a shared
space of exploration and emotional resonance.

Zoe Kreyes interdisciplinary art projects explore transformation, collective
experience, and the disembodiment of western culture through immersive
installation,
performance and tactile sculptures. Combining expansive gestural lines,
sensorial materials and somatic ritual processes, Kreyes work invites viewers into a
depth of feeling that
signals our mutual transformative capacity. Kreyes work has
been exhibited at the Vancouver Art Gallery, Southbank Centre London, Kamloops
Art Gallery; she has produced a number of social practice projects in Vancouver and
Berlin and was listed for the 2024 Sobey Art Award.

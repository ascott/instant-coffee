Ten Thousand Things
Hamish Ballantyne, S F Ho, Tiziana La Melia.
Thursday, Nov 23, 2023, 7:30 - 9:00 p.m.
Free admission, registration recommended.

Western Front is pleased to present Ten Thousand Things, a reading by Hamish
Ballantyne, S F Ho, and Tiziana La Melia. In Daoist cosmology, The Ten Thousand
Things () signifies a vastness that metaphorizes into an uncountable infinity.
During the reading, Ballantyne, Ho, and La Melia will expand into the hybrid territory of
drone/poetry with accompaniment by the Wounded Healer house band composed of
artists-in-residence Julian Hou, Prince Nifty, Eddy Wang, and Fan Wu.

Presented in partnership with Second Spring.

To learn more, visit our website: [https://westernfront.ca/events/ten-thousand-things](https://westernfront.ca/events/ten-thousand-things)

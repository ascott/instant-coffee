Call for Submissions | Through MAY 3
Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery
www.artrentalandsales.com
www.instagram.com/vanartrental

Once a year, the Art Rental & Sales program at the Vancouver Art Gallery hosts an
open call for Canadian artists based in British Columbia to consign artwork to our
program.
All submissions are reviewed by a selection committee and applicants are notified as
to the status of their submission within 2 months of our submission deadline.

This year's Call for Submissions is now open. Applications will be accepted through
end of day May 3rd, 2017.
To apply, visit www.artrentalandsales.com/submissions.php

Due to the volume of submissions we receive, we kindly request no phone calls or in-
person visits regarding the submission process.

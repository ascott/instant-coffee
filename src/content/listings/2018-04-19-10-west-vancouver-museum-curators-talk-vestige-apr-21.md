Vestige: Selection from the Kazlaw Collection

Until May 5, 2018

Curators Talk: April 21, 2 p.m.

Please join Curator, Darrin Morrison for a tour and discussion of Vestige: Selections 
from the KazLaw Collection. Artists Roy Arden, Karin Buba, Owen Kydd, Evan Lee, 
Scott McFarland, Victor John Penner, Howard Ursuliak and Stephen Waddell focus their 
lenses on seemingly ordinary objects, things and places that mark human activity. 
Explore how these images relate to the work of earlier photographers and see how 
images of the banal reveal compelling truths and expose underlying societal issues that 
both inform and shape our world.

Vestige: Selections from the KazLaw Collection is on view until May 5 and is presented 
in conjunction with the Capture Photography Festival.

For more information please visit the museum's website.

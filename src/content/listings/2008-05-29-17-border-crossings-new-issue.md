Border Crossings New Issue - Volume 27 Number 2 Issue No. 106

Media Release - May 28, 2008

(apologies for cross-postings)

The new issue of Border Crossings features an extensive interview with internationally renowned Los Angeles-based artist ED RUSCHA who represented the United States at the 2005 Venice Biennale. Speaking of his ongoing investigation of language, he said, “When I put something down on canvas with words then I’m somehow chiselling it in stone, it really makes it official. I’m elevating the importance of unimportant words.”

The issue also features an interview with FRANÇOISE SULLIVAN -- dancer, choreographer, sculptor, performance artist, conceptual artist, painter and member of Québec’s groundbreaking Refus Global. An article by Lee Henderson on Vancouver-based artist GEOFFREY FARMER discusses the rhyme and reason of Farmer’s performative and transformative installations.

Artist John Kissick examines relationships between art and rock and roll through an analysis of the recent MCA Chicago exhibition, "SYMPATHY FOR THE DEVIL: ART AND ROCK AND ROLL SINCE 1967”.

For the Art Pages, Winnipeg artist RICHARD WILLIAMS presents a series of drawings taking on the politics and aesthetics of gender, sex and power.

The review section, as always, is broad and far-ranging and includes: HAROLD KLUNDER, LIDA ABDUL, MATTHEW BROWN, JASPER JOHNS, BRENDAN FERNANDES, ADRIENNE SPIER,
1973: SORRY OUT OF GAS, SUBCONSCIOUS CITY, and more.

Join us in celebrating the range of artistic approaches that graduating students have
taken in our two-year hands-on, studio-based foundation program.

Our on-campus exhibition and accompanying website feature artwork made in our
drawing, design, Indigenous carving, painting, performance, print media, public art,
media and textiles courses. We are extremely proud of the dedication, curiosity and
experimentation of these emerging artists and look forward to celebrating their
significant achievement.

We gratefully acknowledge that our learning takes place on traditional, ancestral and

given Musqueam name, snw"ey lelm", house of teachings.

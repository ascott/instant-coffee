The West End Chamber Choir has been performing classical and contemporary music 
under the inspirational direction of Natalia Manolov since 2009. WECCs enthusiastic 
members are committed to sharing their joy of singing with the community, performing 
three to five concerts yearly. The primary focus of the ensemble is learning major works, 
performing in more than 10 languages over the course of their 10 year history. 
Performance highlights include: Bach " Magnificat, Britten " Ceremony of Carols, Carl 
Orff " Carmina Burana, Vivaldi " Gloria, Pucini - Messa di Gloria, Verdi " Requiem, 
Mozart " Requiem, Stravinsky " Symphony of Psalms, Poulenc "Quatre Motets pour le 
temps de Noel. The choir rehearses every Monday evening at West End Community 
Centre. New singers are always welcome. westendchamberchoir.com 

Rhythm n Roots is a joyful, adventurous and soulful ensemble - fearlessly performing 
powerful songs drawn from diverse vocal traditions throughout the world. The small 
East Van choir EDNA was Rhythm n Roots genealogy, which began in 2008, enjoying 5 
years of fun, artistic growth and musical development. The larger 50+ voice choir was 
born in the fall of 2013 . Rhythm n Roots is an auditioned community choir under the 
direction of Karla Mundy. The ensemble learns their music both by ear and from written 
scores; they sing in many languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, Georgian, Yoruban, 
Swedish, Latvian). Many of its members travel to the source of their music such as the 
country of Georgia, Cuba, and New Orleans. The choir is looking forward to a musical 
adventure in Ireland in the spring of 2020.

Call to Artists - Two Projects: Donor Recognition Commissions
Royal Alexandra Hospital Foundation
Edmonton, Alberta, Canada
Deadline for Submissions: October 21, 2009

On behalf of the Royal Alexandra Hospital Foundation, Cohos
Evamy integratedesign & S. Pointe & Co. Inc. are pleased to invite
artists, craft artists, designers of all media and creative industry
vendors to respond to a Request for Submissions for two public
artwork-donor recognition commissions. These artistic installations will
not only recognize hospital donors but they will also provide a visually
striking and artistically compelling focus to the exterior South Light
Well and the interior Heart Centre Foyer of the new Robbins Pavilion on
the Royal Alexandra Hospital campus in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada.

* Competition 1
South Lightwell - Robbins Pavilion (Exterior Installation) Commission
Range: $100,000 - $300,000
Please note that the South Lightwell should not be confused with a
previous Call location - the East Lightwell.

* Competition 2
CK Hui Heart Centre Foyer - Robbins Pavilion (Interior Wall) Commission
Range: $30,000 - $90,000

Respondents can be composed of creative teams from a variety of
disciplines, and are welcome to provide submissions for one or both
locations. International respondents are welcome. Successful short-
listed respondents from Stage One will be invited into Stage Two.
Stage Two respondents will be invited to provide a Final Quote
and Design with scale drawings and/or maquette. A design fee will be
provided for Stage Two respondents.

DEADLINE FOR SUBMISSIONS: October 21, 2009

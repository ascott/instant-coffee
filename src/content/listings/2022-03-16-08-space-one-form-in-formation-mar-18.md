Liminal Gallery Projects Presents:

Space One

A Pop-Up Gallery in Marpole, Vancouver.

8930 Oak St.

Vancouver, BC

FORM / IN FORMATION
Bryan Cathcart and Holden McIntosh

Opening Reception - Mar 18, 2022 - 6-10pm

Mar 19-20, 2022 - 12-5pm Or By Appointment

This exhibition explores the connection and juxtaposition between
works by Bryan
Cathcart, including a series of new paintings on canvas and a number of works on
paper, and works by Holden McIntosh, which includes a number of sculptures and
gestural ink paintings.

Here is an excerpt from the write-up for the exhibition:

"Discovered through the dialectics of tension and harmony, the formation of these
works engages with the cerebral balance between consciousness, the subconscious,
and the unconscious. Connections between human and nature; the eternal strivings
between Yin and Yang. By perturbing the balance and seeking a way to engage with
the deepest core of experience known to the artist, the compositions are grown
through process."

The Ferry Building Gallery presents:

Frozen in Time

Nejood Al-Sehrewerdi, Crystal Rassi, Silvia Tagusagawa

May 7-June1, 2025

Opening Reception: Thursday, May 8, 6-8 p.m.

Meet the Artists: Saturday, May 10, 2-3 p.m.

Farsi Tour: Saturday, May 24, 2-3 p.m.

The Ferry Building Gallery is delighted to present Frozen in Time, an exhibition
featuring the work of Nejood Al-Sehrewerdi, Crystal Rassi, and Silvia Tagusagawa"
three local artists whose practices explore the passing of time. Through themes such
as the impermanence of beauty, the reconstruction of architectural spaces from
memory, and the role of architecture as a marker of time, their work offers a compelling
reflection on transience and memory.

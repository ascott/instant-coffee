XXV: Access Gallery's Auction Fundraiser
Saturday, November 19, 2016, 7:00 PM
222 East Georgia Street
Vancouver, BC

Please join us for our most important fundraising event of the year. When you 
support this small but vital artist run centre, you play a direct role in ensuring the 
ongoing vigour of critically engaged emergent visual art practices in Vancouver.

If you can't make the auction, join us on our PREVIEW NIGHT, Thursday, November 
17, 2016, 7:00 PM for a glass of win, a quiet walk-though of the exhibition, and a 
chance to pre-bid on a work you fall in love with.

If you know you'll miss the event itself, you can PLACE AN ABSENTEE BID! Email 
Director/Curator Kimberly Phillips directly at k.phillips@accessgallery.ca.

Admission to the auction is $25, and includes membership at Access Gallery for the 
calendar year. Entry is free for members in good standing. Memberships can be 
purchased for $25 at the event or online here.

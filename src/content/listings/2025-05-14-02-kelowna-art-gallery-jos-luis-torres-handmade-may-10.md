Artist creates extraordinary works reimagined from the everyday

Jos Luis Torres: HANDMADE

May 10, 2025 to May 2026

Kelowna, BC: Okanagan audiences will soon have the chance to see a towering
new art installation at the Kelowna Art Gallery that reimagines ordinary materials and
transforms them into something extraordinary.

The artist behind the work is Quebec City-based Argentine sculptor Jos Luis Torres,
whose work has been shown and celebrated in public spaces and art institutions
across Canada. The art installation, titled HANDMADE, can be found in the open-air
Courtyard space located at the heart of the Gallery building. It opens to the public on
May 10, 2025.

Through playful, evolving environments, Jos Luis Torres enjoys challenging
conventional exhibition standards and the idealized image of the artist. HANDMADE
continues this exploration between location, materials, and the viewer in a
delightfully engaging way, which is sure to spark the curiosity of Gallery-goers.

Visitors will encounter a structure that rises on stilts above a foundation built from old
wooden pallets. The small domicile-like building is constructed almost entirely from
window frames, doors, and a substantial amount of glass. Peering in from the
perimeter it looks almost maze-like in its complexity within. Torres artistic practice
involves appropriating space and materials from everyday life"like all the doors,
windows, pallets, and other items that were locally sourced from individuals"and
then given the freedom to evolve and take form through spontaneous hands-on
actions. His work reflects themes of survival, workforce, and the value of objects as
both fundamental and incidental.

Jos Luis Torres is an Argentine artist who has been based in Qubec City since
2003. He holds degrees in visual arts, sculpture, and architecture. His work has
been featured in numerous exhibitions, including at Muse national des beaux-arts
du Qubec, Museum London, and the UCCS Gallery of Contemporary Art.

An Opening Reception will be held on Friday, May 9, 2025, from 6 to 8 pm at the
Kelowna Art Gallery. The artist will be in attendance, and the public is welcome to
attend.

Jos Luis Torres: HANDMADE runs from May 10, 2025, to May of 2026.

The Kelowna Art Gallery is located at 1315 Water Street in the heart of Kelownas
Culture District. For more information visit kelownaartgallery.com or call 250-762-
2226.

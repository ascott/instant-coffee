This winter, Two Rivers Gallery is hosting two new and exciting exhibitions
that promise to engage and challenge the viewer. North :: An Exhibition for
the 2015 Canada Winter Games is our annual juried exhibition that we have
planned in partnership with the 2015 Canada Winter Games and showcases
amazing talent from across the province. Elapsed is a group exhibition of
work by artists from Northern BC, the Yukon all the way to Newfoundland and
Labrador. Both exhibitions open to the public on February 12th with a
reception and after party to follow on Saturday February 14th at 2pm.

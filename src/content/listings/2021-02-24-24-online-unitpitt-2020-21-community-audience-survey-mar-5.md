UNIT/PITT has just launched their 2020-21 Community & Audience survey:
https://forms.gle/Qgu1GtJdHTzMW4of8

Purpose:
This survey is intended for artists, writers, labourers, and the broader arts community-at-
large to provide feedback on UNIT/PITT's programs and outreach. The information given in
this survey may be anonymised and used to support funding applications. No potentially-
identifiable information will be shared outside of UNIT/PITT, and your answers will not be
made public.

Context:
UNIT/PITT is a charitable non-profit artist-run organization that supports art and critical
awareness by offering the tools,
space, resources, and mentorship for emerging artists and
members of our neighbouring communities to engage in experimental creative work.
UNIT/PITT has operated in the neighbourhoods of Chinatown, the Downtown Eastside and
Gastown since 1975, on unceded lands belonging to the xmkwy"m (Musqueam),
Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), and slil"wta" (Tsleil-Waututh) First Nations. UNIT/PITT has
temporarily ceased operating a public gallery, and has instead been offering mail art and
virtual programs in 2020-21. In January 2021, UNIT/PITT in partnership with VIVO Media
Arts Centre launched ReIssue.pub, an interdisciplinary platform for critical art writing.

Enter-to-win:
You may choose to complete this survey anonymously. However, if you wish to be entered
in a draw to win one of 3 UNIT/PITT book bundles and $50 gift cards from Support Local
BC, please include your name and contact near the bottom of the form. You must answer
all the "required" questions and be over the age of 19 to qualify. The draw
will happen on
March 5, 2021 at 4pm PST.

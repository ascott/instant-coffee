Tuesday, June 14, 7pm
In the Gallery

A Geographer's Reading of Ken Lum
Paul Kingsbury

Geographer Paul Kingsbury surveys Ken Lum's mapping of the city and
examines the artist's public art projects, the use of commercial signage
and references to tourism and consumption.

Dr. Paul Kingsbury is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geography
at Simon Fraser University. He received a MA and PhD in Geography, as well
as a Graduate Certificate in Social Theory from the University of Kentucky.
Specializing in social and cultural geography, his research draws on the
theories of Jacques Lacan and Friedrich Nietzsche to examine
multiculturalism, consumption, tourism, power, and aesthetics.

Free with Gallery admission

14. UNIT/PITT Projects @ waldorf | Rereading The Riot Act II | JUN 15
Rereading The Riot Act II
Artists' Performance Cabaret
Presented by UNIT/PITT at The Waldorf Hotel, 1489 E Hastings St, Vancouver
BC Canada
8 - 11 pm Wednesday, June 15 - Free admission

In an ongoing interrogation of the historical events of April 23, 1935 --
when Mayor Gerry McGeer read the Riot Act to the relief camp protesters,
their families and supporters gathered at Victory Square -- Rereading the
Riot Act II - Performance Art Cabaret offers contemporary responses to
Vancouver's turbulent labour history.

Four performance and visual artists -- leannej, My Name Is Scot, Carol
Sawyer and Lori Weidenhammer -- will present new works. The works are
expanded responses to both the events of April 23, 1935 and the reenactment
event that took place in Vancouver on April 23rd 2011.

The Cabaret will feature film, performances, music, video projections and a
sculptural installation by Jeremy Isao Speier.

Rereading the Riot Act intends to provoke an emotional, politicized
engagement with civic space and how or whether we want to narrate (back) to it.

Rereading the Riot Act is a series of actions curated by Anakana Schofield
through a UNIT/PITT curatorial residency project.

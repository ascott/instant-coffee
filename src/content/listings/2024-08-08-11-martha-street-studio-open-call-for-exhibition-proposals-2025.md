Marthas 2025/26 Call for Exhibition Proposals is now open!

Proposals can be submitted through Google Forms " Click here to go to the
submission form or visit our website at
https://www.printmakers.mb.ca/news/submissions/

Deadline for submissions: 11:59pm August 31, 2024

Martha Street Studio invites exhibition proposals by local, national, and international
artists working in printmaking for our 2025/26 exhibition schedule.

Martha Street Studio is an artist-run printmaking centre that exhibits a wide range of
print-based work, from traditional to contemporary, including work that pushes the
limits of what is considered printmaking.

Located in Treaty 1 Territory in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Martha Street Studio
encourages explorations of what printmaking can be, delighting in works that push
the boundaries of print in unexpected directions. Martha Street Studio strives to
showcase a wide range of perspectives and
approaches, exhibiting a mix of both
traditional and experimental printmaking in each year's programming.

Martha encourages submissions by artists from underrepresented communities, and
in particular from people who identify as gender diverse, Queer, 2-Spirit, Indigenous,
Black, People of Colour, Persons with Disabilities, and Official Language Minorities.

If for any reason you are unable to complete the submission form, contact
gallery@printmakers.mb.ca for assistance.

All applicants will be notified of results within 3 months of the deadline.

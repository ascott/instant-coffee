Wild Life by Mandy-Lyn
Thursday July 5th, 2018
The Playground | 434 Columbia St., Chinatown, Vancouver
mandy-lyn.com / @mandylyn

5-10PM

Join us Thursday July 5th for a special one-night-only photo retrospective of local 
photographer Mandy-Lyn's analog work. This selection, curated by Lincoln Clarkes, 
covers the last 10 years of Mandy-Lyn's erotic portraiture and showcases a powerful 
collection of influential local Women. Mandy-Lyn's bold, empowering and controversial 
photographs have been featured in Playboy, Penthouse, Vogue, Juxtapoz and Vice.

Intimate Landscapes of the Mind: Drawing Meditations by Katherine Wilson

At the start of 2019 Katherine committed to making one drawing per week for an entire 
year. This is a solo show of the 26 drawings completed thus far. 

These twenty-six unique drawings explore the internal landscape of her mind as she 
moves through, processes and transmutes emotions into drawn form. Katherine thinks 
of the process as a meditation practice which allows her to quiet her mind and simply 
notice the thoughts and feelings flowing through her. Intentionally unedited, this show is 
very much a first for the artist, giving voice to Katherine's fullest expression to date.

Fine Art Drawings
Giclee Prints
Handmade Jewelry
and Handmade Essential Oil Blends all lovingly created by Katherine will be available 
for purchase.

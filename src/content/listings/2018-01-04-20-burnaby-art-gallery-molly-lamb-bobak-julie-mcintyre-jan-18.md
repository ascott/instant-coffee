MOLLY LAMB BOBAK: TALK OF THE TOWN
January 19-April 8, 2018
Opening Reception: Thursday, January 18, 7-9pm

This exhibition explores street views and city panoramas by Burnaby-raised Molly Lamb 
Bobak (1920-2014), primarily from the 1940s to the 1960s.

JULIE MCINTYRE: TRAVEL STORIES
January 19-April 8, 2018
Opening Reception: Thursday, January 18, 7-9pm
Vancouver print and fibre artist Julie McIntyre incorporates a variety of domestic objects 
as the ground for a series of work that captures the experiences of her grandmothers 
world travels in the 1960 and 1970s.

Please visit burnabyartgallery.ca for more information.

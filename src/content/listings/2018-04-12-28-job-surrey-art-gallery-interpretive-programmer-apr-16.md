Interpretive Programmer
Surrey Art Gallery

Are you passionate about art education for families, youth, and adults? Can you 
demonstrate knowledge of contemporary art?

Surrey Art Gallery is hiring an Interpretive Programmer (regular part-time position) 
tasked with carrying out a range of educational and administrative responsibilities 
for developing, planning, and implementing exhibition-related interpretive resources, 
programs, and events. Founded in 1975, and located in Metro Vancouver, the 
Gallery is a Class A public contemporary art museum that exhibits and supports 
access to emerging through to established regional, national, and international 
artists. With the largest school district and second largest municipal population in the 
province, the Gallery provides diverse learning and engagement opportunities to 
serve art, artists, and audiences.

RESPONSIBILITIES & REQUIRED SKILLS
Join the Gallery staff team, and contribute to the innovation and growth of our 
institution through your contribution to recommending, researching, developing, 
scheduling, implementing, and evaluating exhibition-related initiatives including: 
Family Programs (Family Sundays, art parties, offsite programs); School Programs 
(K-12 tours, workshops, guides, resources, and professional development 
opportunities for teachers at the Gallery and in classrooms and at conferences); 
casual visitor initiatives (resources, tours, workshops); and adult programs (artist 
talks, panels, symposia, events, and residencies). You will work directly with the 
Curator of Education and Engagement, Volunteer Program Coordinator, Visual Arts 
Programmer, art educators and instructors, volunteers, Surrey Arts Centre staff, and 
community committees, as well as the Gallery Director/Manager of Visual and 
Community Arts, Curator of Exhibitions and Collections and curatorial staff, 
Marketing Coordinator, City staff, and contract artists, speakers, and designers. You 
will additionally assist with grant writing and facility operations as it relates to your 
area of work.

We are seeking a creative individual who enjoys working in a dynamic environment 
and thrives in situations requiring meeting deadlines and adapting to change. 
Required skills include: strong knowledge and experience in art education theories 
and practices; broad knowledge of contemporary art and art history; excellent oral 
and written communication; solid administrative, organizational, interpersonal, and 
research skills; effective problem-solving skills; and the ability to work both 
independently and collaboratively within a team.

QUALIFICATIONS
The successful candidate will have a degree/diploma in the area of art museum 
education, art education, or a related field (and additional teaching certification is an 
asset), plus 2 years progressively responsible experience in developing, 
administrating, and presenting initiatives in this area of specialty, including 
supervision, or an equivalent acceptable combination of training and experience. 
Emergency First Aid with CPR certification and the ability to successfully pass a 
Police Information Check/Vulnerable Sector Check are required. A valid BC drivers 
license and ability to lift up to 30 lbs of equipment are preferred.

This is a regular part-time, union position (Community Services Assistant 5 
classification) with a starting rate of $27.23/hour plus benefits. Hours are typically 
Monday to Friday up to 25 hours/week, including some hours scheduled in the 
evening and weekend.

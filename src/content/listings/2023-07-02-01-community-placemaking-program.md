You are invited to apply for up to $3,000 to fund an event-based placemaking project to
bring people together in a Vancouver public space. We are looking to fund community-
led projects across the city. Your project could be something youve done before, or an
entirely new idea.

Your proposal should:

Be interesting, fun, and engaging

Encourage social connections & strengthen community

Be accessible, inclusive, safe and welcoming to all

Be easy to set-up and move

Be deliverable within the budget and timeframe

Examples of past suitable event-based placemaking projects

Community gatherings and dialogues on reimagining a space

o Chinatown Mahjong Social, Backyard BBQ, Trout Lake Triangle Block Party,
Park(ing) Day, Bridge Warming, Rain City Block Party

Art, music, and dance events

o Public Disco, Community Canvas Jam, Summer Music Vancouver, Keys to the
Streets, Open Season, Street Beats

Workshops and activities

o Skate Jams, Thingery Sustainability Workshops, Dance in Transit, Public Yoga, Play
Vancouver

Further details:

Successful applicants will receive up to $3,000 for their projects (with a possible
boost of $2,000);

Projects will go live between July and end of November 2023, and will activate
one or more public spaces on at least one day;

All logistics (set up, take down, moving, storage, and cleanup) will be the
responsibility of the applicant

Invoices are due upon completion of the project;

IBPOC and 2SLGBTQ+ residents, communities and organisations are
encouraged to apply.

For more details on the program and how to apply, visit vancouver.ca/community-
placemaking. Applications are due by Monday July 17, 2023, at midnight. We
encourage you to apply even if you
dont have all the details figured out before the
deadline. If approved, staff will work with you to finalize it.

We look forward to reviewing your proposal!Please feel free to contact us if you have
any questions.

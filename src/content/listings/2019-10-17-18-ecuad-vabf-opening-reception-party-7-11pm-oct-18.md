VABF Opening Reception Party

Friday, Oct. 18th, 7PM-11PM
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Free

Come to the Preview Night and Opening Reception
of the Vancouver Art Book Fair! After
you check out the 2019 VABF Preview, drop by READ Bookstore (located by the main
entrance of ECUAD) and join us for a VABF signature drink sponsored by Stealth
Distilleries, groove to a DJ set from rising local music superstar Biawanna, and show your
love and support for VABF by signing up to be a Member. Come mix and mingle with fellow
art book fans and VABF exhibitors!

Link for more info: https://www.facebook.com/events/458420968111411/

ABOUT

Free and open to the public, Vancouver Art Book Fair is the longest-running international art
book fair in Canada. VABF is a multi-day festival of artists publishing featuring local,
national and international publishers, as well as a diverse line-up of programs, performances
and installations. Featured artists travel to Vancouver from across Canada and the globe,
and produce everything from books, magazines, zines and printed ephemera to digital,
performative or other experimental forms of publication.

Special thanks to our presenting partners: Emily Carr University of Art + Design, Ron
Burnett Library + Learning Commons and READ Books.

Deadline for Submission of Application: February 21st 2016

SOUND IN CONTEXT: FIELD RECORDING is focused on exploring the area of field
recording and contextual sound art work. MAC is a production based
organization, and as such, the production and process is a highly valued
aspect of your proposal. For SOUND IN CONTEXT, we are encouraging artists
to concentrate on the recording and field aspect of producing their works
rather than post-production.

There are two opportunities available for this call:

AIR - The Sound Artist in Residence program is open to emerging artists. It
offers equipment, support, and training for the artist. This is an
intensive production program that requires a regular log be maintained and
that the artists report regularly to the program co-ordinator.

COMMISSION - The Commission program is open to established sound artists
who have their own equipment, studio, and do not require training or
support. There is no reporting or logs required.

We give preference to Canadian Sound Artists. Please note that late
applications will not be accepted. Due to the high volume of applicants, we
may not respond to all applicants but you are welcome to request
information about your application status after the deadline date.

Applications can be submitted via our webpage at
http://mediaartscommittee.org/artist-calls

City of Vancouver
Call for Submissions
Mount Pleasant Artist Production Space | Capital Infrastructure Projects

Opportunity
The City of Vancouver is seeking submissions for capital funding for infrastructure projects that will secure and develop multi-artist production spaces in Mount Pleasant.
Proponents must be established Vancouver-based non-profit arts and cultural organizations that have been working towards securing, enhancing, or developing sustainable artist production facilities in - or adjacent to -  Mount Pleasant.

Thursday, December 19, 4:00pm	Submission deadline

If you are interested please go to:  (note link has been updated!)
Call for Submissions: Mount Pleasant Artist Production Spaces

You must contact Derek Simons, Cultural Planner, to discuss your project prior to applying:
Derek.simons@vancouver.ca or (604) 673-8283
Derek can provide applications to eligible organizations.


Background
Examples of possible projects could include:
*	Purchasing or upgrading existing artist production spaces
*	Purchasing and developing new properties
*	Implementing a reserve fund to cover a portion of facility operating costs

The City is looking for projects that:
*	Provide a significant community benefit and best meet the need for accessible, affordable multi-artist production spaces in or serving Mount Pleasant
*	Demonstrate support and demand from artists and the community that the facility intends to serve
*	Secure facilities for long term use as artist production spaces and operate them on a not-for-profit basis
*	Are led by organizations with the capacity, skills, and experience to successfully fundraise, plan for, develop, and operate the facility
*	Have realistic and sustainable plans and budgets
*	Propose robust financing strategies and mechanisms that leverage funding from other sources
*	Propose innovative collaborations and partnerships
*	Are,  or plan to be, a secured long-term public asset for use as an artist production space

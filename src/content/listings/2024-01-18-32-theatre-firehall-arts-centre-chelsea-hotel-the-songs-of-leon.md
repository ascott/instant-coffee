OPENING | grunt gallery | Sue Dong Eng and Mercedes Eng | 3PM | FEB 3
EXHIBITION TITLE: Inside/Outside: the art show my dad never had by Sue Dong
Eng and Mercedes Eng

ARTISTS: Sue Dong Eng, Mercedes Eng

OPENING: February 3, 2024. 3-5pm PST.

EXHIBITION DATES: February 3 - March 16, 2024

Guest curated by Keimi Nakashima-Ochoa and Mercedes Eng.

Composed of archival family images, copper etchings in handmade frames, and
literary work brought together by familial ties and an impulse to document and
collect, this exhibition marks the first art presentation for both Sue Dong Eng and
Mercedes Eng.

Inside/Outside: the art show my dad never had by Sue Dong Eng and Mercedes Eng
takes a close and specific look at the life and work of the late Sue Dong, showing
images of his family and upbringing focused around vancouvers Chinatown, while
connecting to broader themes of cultural visibility, institutional violence, and
community building that are still in flux in this city today.

Sue Dongs copper works were created in the carceral facilities he spent most of his
adult life going in and out of. Mercedes has gathered and built this collection of
archival images and her work " which is inextricably linked to Sue Dongs as his
daughter " intentionally responds to the prison industrial complex she and her family
frequently brushed up against, while pushing back at the idea that the colonial
nation-state of canada is a multicultural utopia.

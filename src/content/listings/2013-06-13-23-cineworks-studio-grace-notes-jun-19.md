GRACE Notes

After traveling across the country from Montreal to Halifax, Grace Notes
comes to Vancouver on

Wednesday, June 19, 2013

Cineworks Studio 1131 Howe St (back lane entrance)

Doors at 7 p.m. Open Screening + Curator Talk back

PROGRAM DESCRIPTION

Curated by Stacey Ho and initiated by Montreal's VidÃ©ographe, GRACE NOTES
is a program of videos and films in which graceful cinema is understood as
a means of awakening new and joyful ways of acting. In this configuration,
grace is a notion that is open to adaptation. Showcasing the work of Sarah
Fuller, Tamara Henderson, Helen Hill, Steve Hubert, Nikolai Gauer, Seth
Smith, and Alize Zorlutuna, the program encompasses themes of movement,
dreams, the body, water, sound, and subjectivity. In keeping with the
plurality of means apparent in this collection, you are warmly invited to
bring your favourite video art, up to ten minutes in length on a DVD or
thumbdrive, to be shown in conjunction with the Vancouver presentation of
this program.

Curatorial text:

The sunken beast is trembling. Nuts and bolts fly through the air then
crumble into crumbs. We imagine a whole ocean sucked into this little
pocket of nothing of space and pressure. The machine is cracking open. It
is made of paper-mÃ¢chÃ©. Blinking, we are outside surrounded by its shell.
There is light and there are trees. It's 6:30am EST on the street where you
live. There's a car alarm sounding over and over and with each bleat big
orange squares form in front of your eyes, smacking you over the head like
a lazy windshield wiper. Scrape your feet on the sidewalk. The clouds taste
like orange squares and the wind looks like a car alarm running until it
can't quit. Up the steps to your apartment and opening the front door is
the noise of the ocean filling your eardrums drowning. Once again, we

A film is a mirage that pacifies or awakens the imagination. In dreams,
heterogeneous instances come together to form complex entities. Similarly,
no single idea succinctly describes the interplay between film and
monument, writing and dreaming that comprise a work. Each part hints at a
larger whole.

The notion of grace is a platform that is open to adaptation. Rather than
presenting a definitive explanation, GRACE NOTES composes a singular
presentation, one of many possible versions. The plurality of means
apparent in this collection of films and videos enrich this loose platform.
Looking out from all angles, each of these works hint at a graceful way of
approaching movement and action.

CURATOR'S BIO

Stacey Ho is an artist, writer, and occasional curator. She has toured
GRACE NOTES across Canada and recently co-directed WWTWO, a project space
in Montreal focused on innovative, emergent art and artists. An awareness
of time and a spirit of pluralism informs her interests in printed matter,
photography, performance, and music. Lately, she's been doing a lot of
walking and weaving.

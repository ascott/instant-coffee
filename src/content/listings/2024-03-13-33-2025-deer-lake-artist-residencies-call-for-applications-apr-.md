Call for Applications - 2025 Deer Lake Artist Residencies

City of Burnaby provides residency opportunities for emerging and established
artists.

Professional artists can immerse themselves in the tranquil and inspiring natural
setting of Deer Lake Park, where they have the opportunity to create in two
beautifully restored heritage homes as well as facilities at the Shadbolt Centre for the
Arts and Burnaby Art Gallery, as part of the City of Burnabys Deer Lake Artist
Residencies program.

Artists from all disciplines are invited to apply online for this program. Residencies,
typically ranging from 2 to 6 weeks, may include housing and/or studio use. The
deadline to apply is April 8, 2024 at 5 pm (PST).

The intention of these residencies is to provide time and
space for professional
artists with emerging to established practices to create new work. Resident artists
may work with the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts and Burnaby Art Gallery staff to
support artist-led community engagement projects aligned with the requirement of
each residency. Alongside housing, resident artists will have access to rehearsal
spaces and studios as well as other resources to support their creative endeavors.

Application Deadline: April 8, 2024 (covering residency requests between March 1,
2025 to December 10, 2025)

Learn more and apply: Burnaby.ca/air

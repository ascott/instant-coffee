Call for Applications to New Aesthetics 2015

New Aesthetics is a two-week summer intensive for mid-career artists. Each
year a pair of international artists are invited to share their practice
with a group of twenty working artists from around the world. Past
instructors are Pol Hyvaert, Crystal Pite, Mariano Pensotti and Toshiki Okada.

New Aesthetics 2015 runs July 20-31 at SFU Woodward's and is being
instructed by Gob Squad members Sarah Thom and Mat Hand. Gob Squad are a
UK/Germany-based arts collective working with performance and video.

More about Gob Squad - www.gobsquad.com
More about New Aesthetics - www.newaesthetics.ca

To apply, please email a CV and letter of intent to
sarah(at)theatrereplacement.org.

In the letter (no more than 2 pages) let us know:
-a little bit about the work you’ve made or helped to make;
-two or three questions you’re considering within your practice;
-why are you interested in New Aesthetics at this point in your career;
-if you’d like to be considered for financial support.

Deadline for applications is Sunday, February 15.

The Dance Centre presents
Arno Schuitemaker
If You Could See Me Now
Thursday-Saturday March 14-16, 2024 | 8pm
Post-show artist talkback March 15
Scotiabank Dance Centre, 677 Davie St, Vancouver
Info and tickets: [https://thedancecentre.ca/event/arno-schuitemaker/](https://thedancecentre.ca/event/arno-schuitemaker/)

Award-winning Dutch choreographer Arno Schuitemaker brings his mesmerizing work to
Vancouver for the first time with If You Could See Me Now. A highly physical, rigorous
integration of movement, music and light, the work features three performers who take a
chilled-out club dance and reinvent it through
incessant movement, rhythm and
endurance. From turmoil to ecstasy, from a meticulous build-up to its fracturing, the
performance goes right to the core of the body and its response to change, and asks: how
do we keep going and move forward?

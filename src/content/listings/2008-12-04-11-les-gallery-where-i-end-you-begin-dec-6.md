Where I End, You Begin

New Photos By Tyler Lepore and Seth Fluker
Opening Reception Saturday December 6th, 7pm - 11pm
December 6th - January 6th, 2009

Where I End, You begin, is a conceptual collaboration between artists Tyler
Lepore and Seth Fluker. Through the lens, they comment on the limitations
and endless possibilities of our daily perspectives. From down to up and
ground to sky, they will challenge our scope and push us to see what isn't
always there.

Seth Fluker was born in rural Ontario and raised in Vancouver, British
Columbia.
Primarily a self-taught photographer, Seth's work has been shown in
Vancouver, Mexico, Japan, London and New York and was featured in the
recently published, Various Photographs by TV books and in magazines, the
Journal, Colour and Lodown. He is currently living and working in London,
England and will be having a solo exhibition at Exit Gallery (London) in
spring 2009.

Tyler Lepore was born in Gibsons, BC and raised in Templeton California. He
currently lives and works in Vancouver BC.
Tyler is an interdisciplinary artist whose practise involves painting,
collage, sculpture, installation, and photography. He was a member of the
Vancouver collective Humanfive, whose colourful and imaginative
installations were shown in Vancouver, Calgary, LA, San Diego, Barcelona,
New York and Portland and participated in BraveArt as well in
LifetimeCollective exhibitions. As an independent artist, Tyler has
participated in various group and solo shows in New York, Utah and Vermont
and his work has been featured in magazines, the Journal and Mass Appeal.
From 1999 - 2004, Tyler independently published the art magazine Full
Spectrum, which was dedicated to featuring the work of local and
international artists.

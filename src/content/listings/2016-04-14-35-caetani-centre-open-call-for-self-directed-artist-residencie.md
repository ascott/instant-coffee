2016 Caetani Centre's Open Call for Self-Directed Artist Residencies in the
Beautiful North Okanagan, BC

The Caetani Cultural Centre offers artists and writers, curators, cultural
researchers and performers, creative living and working spaces in an
independent community atmosphere for self-directed residencies, providing
time, support and room for creativity, at affordable rates. Open to
emerging and established artists in any medium. Self-directed residencies
are of varying lengths of time, from 2 weeks to 6 months, to suit the
individual needs of the applicant. Please specify the preferred time period
for the residency and length of stay for project in your application form.
All applications are adjudicated by a professional adjudication panel. The
Self-Directed Residencies are not sponsored or themed, and fees are based
and pro-rated on length of stay. Artists are responsible for their own
travel, health insurance, food requirements, materials, equipment, and
should have their own transportation.

Availabilities:
June 12 - 25
July 12 - 22
Aug 19 - Sept 30
Nov 1 - Flexible end date

05. Helen Pitt | Weekend Leisure | PUBLIC ACCESS: 1999 & Beyond | MAR 25
PUBLIC ACCESS: 1999 & Beyond - LAUNCHES FRIDAY, MARCH 25

PUBLIC ACCESS: 1999 And Beyond

PUBLIC ACCESS: 1999 AND BEYOND, an artists' television project 
curated by Weekend Leisure, launches with a public screening on 
Friday, March 25 2011 at Centre A, 2 W. Hastings St., Vancouver. 
Admission to the event is free; doors open at 8pm, screening 
begins at 9.

PUBLIC ACCESS includes video contributions by Kaitlin Fontana 
and Nicole Passmore, Emmett Hall, Nicolas Sassoon and Angus 
Ferguson. Using an overarching theme of "science fiction", the five 
participants were asked to produce works that consider the do-it-
yourself aesthetic of public access television, responding to its 
limitations as a medium of democratic creative expression. The 
resulting video content ranges from community theatre, fan fiction, 
local news broadcasting and primitive computer animation and 
brings together individuals from backgrounds both in and out of the 
contemporary art world, extending the individuals' potential beyond 
their respective fields. More information, including stills and clips 
from the videos, can be found at 
http://publicaccess.helenpittgallery.org .

PUBLIC ACCESS will be accompanied by a book, available in late 
April, including commentary by the HP Radio Show (Hank Bull and 
Patrick Ready). The program will be broadcast on Novus TV 
Channel 4 from March 26 to April 30 and will be shown in the 
window of the soon-to-open UNIT/PITT Projects space at 15 E. 
Pender from April 8 to 23.

UNIT/PITT Projects (Helen Pitt Gallery) gratefully acknowledges the 
support of the British Columbia Arts Council, the Canada Council 
and the City Of Vancouver. Special thanks to Centre A for their 
assistance with the launch of PUBLIC ACCESS.

UNIT/PITT Projects | 100 - 221 E. Georgia St. | Vancouver BC 
Canada | 604 681 6740 | info@helenpittgallery.org | www.unitpitt.ca




06. Shudder Gallery | The LMPC | | In Conversation | MAR 24 & Happy Hour | MAR 25
Shudder Gallery and the Lower Mainland Painting Co. present:
In Conversation: Communities of Necessity / Communities of Desire
Thursday, March 24 | 7:00pm | Free
The Lower Mainland Painting Co. will be hosting and moderating a 
series of public presentations by Artworkers every Thursday night at 
7pm for the duration of their residency at Shudder Gallery. These will 
be informal conversations that are open to the public.
Thursday, March 24th's topic will be "Communities of Necessity / 
Communities of Desire"

How does the relationship between our aesthetic labour and our 
wage labour effect our processes as artists? Is there any such 
distinction between our life as a laborer and our life as an artist? 
Perhaps more importantly, of all the communities that form around 
work in our lives, which are the communities formed of necessity 
and which are the communities formed of desire? The LMPC is 
inviting artists and the public to join in a conversation about the 
emotional and cognitive harmonies and dissonances that emerge 
when Artists transition between a labor of dreams to a labor of 
survival and back again. Please note that the Artworker’s Hall will be 
open during and immediately following the conversation.

This event is in conjunction with What Is Not A Part Of The Art at 
Shudder Gallery, March 11 - April 2, 2011

Shudder Gallery
433 Columbia St.
Vancouver, BC
shuddergallery.com

The Lower Mainland Painting Co.(LMPC) is an initiative around art, 
labour and art labourers. The LMPC is in fact a professional 
independent painting company that is pursuing paid work in the 
lower mainland, offering painting and low-skilled carpentry services. 
It is also a conceptual artwork / performance / residency / and 
research initiative that investigates the differing modes of labour and 
negotiation through which artists come together and share 
dialogue. Their work acts as a vehicle for community and knowledge 
production, while at the same time engaging in questions of
productivity, definitions, value production, object-hood and economy.

For more information about the Lower Mainland Painting Co. visit

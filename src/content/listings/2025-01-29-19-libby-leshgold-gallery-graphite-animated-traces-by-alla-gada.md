Graphite: Animated Traces by Alla Gadassik
Book Launch with Drawing session led by Vjeko Sager
February 5, 2025 4-6 pm
Libby Leshgold Gallery

Join us on Wednesday February 5th at 4pm for the READ Books launch of Alla
Gadassiks new book
Graphite: Animated Traces (Govett-Brewster Art Gallery), including an open drawing
session led by artist and educator Vjeko Sager. Materials and refreshments
provided.

Graphite: Animated Traces (published by the Govett-Brewster Art Gallery, 2024)
profiles the material and cultural history of graphite as a creative medium, with close
attention to its important role in contemporary art and animation. What ways
of
knowing does graphite enable as a drawing medium? How does animating with
graphite " making moving images through extended and repetitive acts of drawing "
allow us to understand the world differently? Responding with vivid analysis of pencil
drawings and animated films, this book offers a richly detailed portrait of an everyday
material we often take for granted.

Alla Gadassik is a film scholar and curator of animated media. She is Associate
Professor of Media History + Theory at Emily Carr University, where she founded the
Animate Materials Workshop. The Animate Materials Workshop is dedicated to
developing animation as a method of interdisciplinary material exploration that
bridges arts and sciences. As International Film Curator in Residence at the Govett-
Brewster Art Gallery and Len Lye Centre (2022-2025), Alla developed Interlaced:
Animation and Textiles, a large-scale group exhibition that weaves connections
between these two artforms.

Vjeko Sager is an artist and educator,
whose philosophy and creativity push against
the boundaries of knowability. His artwork challenges viewers to discover what lies in
the space beyond reason. Vjeko facilitates the Drawing Room, a drawing club
dedicated to nurturing and promoting drawing culture at Emily Carr University.

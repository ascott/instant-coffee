The tide is higher or lower, she couldnt tell you which
Ketty Haolin Zhang

Opening: Thursday May 9th, 6 - 8pm
Location: Deer Lake Gallery, 6584 Deer Lake Avenue, Burnaby, BC, V5G 3T7
Exhibition period: May 4th - June 1st, 2024

"The tide is higher or lower, she couldnt tell you which" pinpoints a central sentiment
in Ketty Haolin Zhangs current practice: embodying both closeness and distance, a
desire for belonging while resisting definition and a yearning for a utopian landscape
amidst feelings of placelessness.

Originally sparked by her recent artist residency at Casa L in Mexico City, this
collection of black velvet paintings and mixed-media resin works reflects upon and
interrogates the human desire to make sense of existence through folklore,
mythology, spirituality and religion, and considers the relevance and role of these
traditions in contemporary society.

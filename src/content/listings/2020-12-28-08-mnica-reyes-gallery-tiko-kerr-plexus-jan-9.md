Vancouver, BC Mnica Reyes Gallery is pleased to announce to the first solo exhibition of
works by Tiko Kerr schedule to take place January 9 - February 6, 2021 in the capacity that
our health officials may allow us.

About the Exhibition (text by the artist):

Within the painting process exist opportunities to both illuminate and to
obfuscate.

Ive chosen to work on the modern medium of plexiglass at a time when it has taken on a
new and critical role as a transparent, protective barrier that can shield us from an insidious
virus but, isolates us from one another as well. It is also at this contemporary moment when
we all are suffering under the omnipresent burden of distorted socio-political issues where
transparency and truth can no longer be taken for granted.

These plexiglass works provide an opportunity to look closer and deeper, to explore
networks in paint suspended mid-stroke and to challenge the authenticity of what we see.

In the tradition of Twombly and Pollack these explorations may also offer a small window
onto an indescribable cosmos.

Unit 17 is thrilled to announce the group exhibition SUPER, NATURAL, a significant 
presentation of over sixteen Canadian and international artists, writers and publishers to 
begin our 2019 program. The exhibition focuses on new and recent painting, sculpture, 
photography, prints as well as publications. SUPER, NATURAL launches a publishing 
arm of the gallery, with a physical and online reading room of artist/reference books, 
magazines & editions. Unit 17 books & editions supports the creation and dissemination 
of printed matter by represented and supported artists.

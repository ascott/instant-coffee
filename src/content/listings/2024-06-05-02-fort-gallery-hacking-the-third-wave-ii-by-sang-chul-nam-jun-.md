Fort Gallery is pleased to exhibit Sang Chul Nams exhibition Hacking the Third Wave
II which presents kinetic sculpture, projections, and abstract paintings in an immersive
installation at the historic Fort Langley Community Hall. Investigating our multifaceted
relationship to nature in an increasingly manufactured and technological age, Sang
Chul creates a space to sensitively grapple with the many forms of change facing our
climate and local ecologies. Contrasting artificial and organic forms, Hacking The Third
Wave II envisions possible futures, layering critical and
hopeful perspectives on what
may be transformed with time.

Exhibition Runs June 1-9, 2024 at the Fort Langley Community Hall, 9167 Glover Rd,
Langley Twp

Limited Term Sessional Vacancies

NSCAD University

Subject to budgetary and final approval by the Board of Governor's, NSCAD
University invites applications for the following Limited Term Sessional
Appointments at the rank of Lecturer or Assistant Professor. The successful
candidate will teach a variety of courses in the subject area.

September 2009 - April 2010

Competition No. S101.09: Assistant Professor: Division of Design
(Interdisciplinary Design)

Competition No. S102.09: Assistant Professor: Division of Foundation (Intro
to Visual Culture)

Competition No. S103.09: Assistant Professor: Division of Historical and
Critical Studies

Competition No. S104.09: Assistant Profession: Division of Media Arts (Film)

Competition No. S105.09: Assistant Professor: Division of Craft (Jewellery)

September 2009 - December 2009 (Fall 2009)

Competition No. S106.09: Assistant Professor: Division of Historical and
Critical Studies

Competition No. S107.09: Assistant Professor: Division of Media Arts
(Photography)

January 2010 - April 2010 (Winter 2010)

Competition No. S101.10: Assistant Professor: Division of Design
(Interdisciplinary Design)

Competition No. S102.10: Assistant Professor: Division of Media Arts (Media
Arts)

Competition No. S103.10: Assistant Professor: Division of Craft (Ceramics)

Applicants for all positions will hold a MFA, Ph.D, or equivalent
professional experience, have an active exhibition and/or publication
record, and teaching experience at the university level. Salary will be
dependent upon qualifications and paid according to the Faculty Salary Grid.

All qualified candidates are encouraged to apply; however, Canadians and
permanent residents will be given priority. Recruitment and hiring
practices are in accordance with Article 6 of the Collective Agreement
between the Faculty Union and the Board of Governors which is committed to
non-discrimination. NSCAD University thanks all applicants for their
interest. Only those selected for an interview will be contacted.

Applications should include a current curriculum vitae, a maximum of 20
images (CD or DVD, Mac and PC compatible) or other documentation of current
work, statement of teaching philosophy, and the names and addresses of
three referees. Please quote the competition number in your application.

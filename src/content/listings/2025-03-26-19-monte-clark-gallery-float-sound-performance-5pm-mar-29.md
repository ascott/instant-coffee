Float
Sound performance
by Evan Hardy &
Alex Tedlie-Stursberg

Saturday, March 29th

5pm to 7:30pm

Sound performance at 5:30pm

Join us for a live sound performance and activation of our current exhibition One
Thousand Years, followed by an artist talk with Alex Tedlie-Stursberg. Using contact
microphones, a modular synth, and cassette tape loops, Evan Hardy and Alex Tedlie-
Stursberg embody the life cycle of Stursberg's Float series through sound " capturing
corroded surfaces in an exploration of the passage of time.

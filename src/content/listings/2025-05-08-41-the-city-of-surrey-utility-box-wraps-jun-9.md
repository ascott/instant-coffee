The City of Surrey invites expressions of interest for artwork designs to wrap utility
boxes along the new Surrey-Langley Skytrain corridor (Fraser Highway from Whalley
Blvd to 196 Street). Three artists will be commissioned for a total of 27 locations.

Details
Please read Call to Artists, Utility Box Wraps, for more information about the project
and how to apply.

Artist Fees:
- Whalley " 4 locations " $6,000
- Fleetwood " 11 locations " $16,500
- Clayton " 5 locations " $7,500

Deadline: June 9, 2025

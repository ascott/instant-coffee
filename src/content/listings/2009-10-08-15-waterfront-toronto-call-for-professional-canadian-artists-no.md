Call for Professional Canadian Artists
Richmond Adelaide Overpass at River Street Public Art Opportunity
Waterfront Toronto, Toronto ON
Deadline for Submissions: November 5, 2009 at 2:00 pm
Budget: $525,000 CAN

Waterfront Toronto (legally named Toronto Waterfront Revitalization Corporation or "TWRC") 
is in the process of transforming the former industrial lands along the city's waterfront into a series 
of sustainable urban communities. The West Don Lands is the first of these communities to be 
developed and will serve as the example and inspiration for future developments that follow.

As part of the public realm design for the new West Don Lands community, a comprehensive 
public art strategy was developed that identified a diverse range of art opportunities that will 
enrich the new neighbourhood. The art strategy anticipates artworks installed in the public realm
as a way of creating a sense of cohesiveness and identity for the West Don Lands by drawing 
attention to Waterfront Toronto's investment in environmental sustainability as well as reflecting 
the past, present and future transformation of the area.

The vicinity under the Richmond Adelaide Overpass has been recognized as a high priority 
public art opportunity and will be the first independent artist selection process to be run by 
Waterfront Toronto. The selected artist will work with the multidisciplinary public realm design team 
to ensure the integration of their work within the design of the underpass park that is currently
underway.

ARTWORK BUDGET
The budget for the art contribution to this project is $525,000 CAN, plus GST.

SUBMISSIONS
Waterfront Toronto is seeking to commission an Artist or Artist Team to become part of and 
to work collaboratively with the Public Realm Design Team on the development of the Richmond 
Adelaide Underpass Park.

This opportunity is open to professional Canadian Artists. The Terms of Reference can be

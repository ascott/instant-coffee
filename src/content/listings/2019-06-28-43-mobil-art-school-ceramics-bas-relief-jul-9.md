Ceramics: Bas-relief (EIY- Egypt-It-Yourself) Clay tile wall hangings
Jul. 09 and Jul. 16, 6:30-9:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

In this course you will learn this process using clay to make a hanging wall tile that will 
fake people into thinking youre an art historian and ancient art collector. Bas-relief (or 
low-relief) is a type of sculpture technique using additive and reductive processes in 
which creates an image that is in-between being 2D and 3D. 

To this course bring a drawing/image of your choice (that you can get dirty) - no larger 
than 10in x 10in- to turn into a bas-relief. Level: Beginner; Age 10+, $98 for 2 sessions.

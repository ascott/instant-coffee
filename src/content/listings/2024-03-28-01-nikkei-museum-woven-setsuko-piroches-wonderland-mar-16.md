Exhibition

WOVEN: Setsuko Piroches Wonderland

We invite you to enter the unique world of Setsuko Piroche (ne Hane), a colourful
and diverse cross-cultural artist whose career spans more than 70 years. This
exhibition brings together over 40 pieces of her unique artistic worldview, including
three-dimensional textile works that were central to the Arts & Crafts Movement in
the 1970s, woven suspensions, paintings in Nihonga technique that she mastered
before immigrating to Canada, and illustrations for children books published in both
Japanese and English. Please enjoy Setsuko's curious and mysterious world. Co-
Curated by Makiko Hara and Sherri Shinobu Kajiwara.

WOVEN: Setsuko Piroches Wonderland

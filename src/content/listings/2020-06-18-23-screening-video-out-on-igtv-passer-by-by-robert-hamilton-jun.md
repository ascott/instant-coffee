VIDEO OUT SCREENING SERIES: Video Out on IGTV Episode 12
ONLINE SCREENING
Passer-by by Robert Hamilton
June 26 12PM-July 3 11:59AM
INSTAGRAM @videooutcollection
FREE

Taped on Saturday nights during an economic downturn in 1991, Passer-by
captures the lively ecosystem of a convenience store (and their competitors across
the street) in Calgary, Alberta. This work was restored in 2017 from its VHS master,
and premiered at The Cinematheque (Vancouver, BC) in student guest curator
Cassandra Bourchier's program, Non-lived
Nostalgia in December 2017.

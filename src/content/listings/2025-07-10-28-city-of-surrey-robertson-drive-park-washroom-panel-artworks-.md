The City of Surrey is inviting Expressions of Interest for a creative gate and wall
panel for a new washroom facility in Robertson Drive Park. The artist designed gate
will replace the standard washroom gate at the front, and the wall panel will be
installed at the back of the facility. Both panels will be fabricated from laser-cut,
powder coated aluminum.

Robertson Drive Park is located near 128th Street at 92nd Avenue in Whalley. The
park offers a combination of large open spaces and recreational spaces to
encourage varying opportunities for outdoor activity. Amenities include a playground,
basketball and games court, a picnic shelter, open space, and walking trails. There is
also a small urban forest at the northwest end, planted with native trees and shrubs.
The park attracts a range of events and activities such as community tree plantings,
frisbee, and picnics. The artwork should speak to the value of connecting to people,
place, and a sense of play.

Artist Fee: $6,500

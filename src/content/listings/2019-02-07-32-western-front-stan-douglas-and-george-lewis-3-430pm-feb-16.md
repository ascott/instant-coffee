ARTIST TALK
Feb 16, 3pm
FREE

In conjunction with the current exhibition "Hors-champs" at Western Front, we are 
excited to host artist Stan Douglas and composer/trombonist George Lewis for an 
intimate conversation about the genesis of this work as well reflect on its present day 
relevance. 

On view in Western Front's gallery from January 18 through February 23, Stan Douglas' 
video Hors-champs (1992 features a quartet of musicians - Kent Carter, Douglas Ewart, 
Oliver Johnson, and George Lewis"performing Albert Aylers Spirits Rejoice. Projected 
simultaneously on opposite sides of the same screen, Douglas installation considers the 
Black musical traditions (blues, gospel, etc.) embedded into Aylers composition, the 
movement of musicians from the US to France (where many musicians associated with 
the 1960s Free Jazz movement found greater success), and the political and social 
context out of which this music was born.

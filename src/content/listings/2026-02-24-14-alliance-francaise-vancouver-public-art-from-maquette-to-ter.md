Public Art: From Maquette to Territory by Marie Khouri

From February 28th to March 23rd 2026

Location: 6161 Cambie Street, Vancouver, BC V5Z 3B2
Artworks texts available in French and in English.

Discover and explore the creative process of public art with Marie Khouri, a Franco-Canadian
sculptor! This exhibition showcases scale models of her monumental sculptures across
British Columbia, inviting visitors to see how intention, form, and meaning emerge in dialogue
with the city and its community.

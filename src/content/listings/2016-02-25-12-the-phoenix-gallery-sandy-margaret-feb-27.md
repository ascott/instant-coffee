Opening Feb 27 2016 8-11pm
Surface Tension at The Phoenix. New work by artist Sandy Margaret. 

The Phoenix presents a series of new mixed media work by Sandy 
Margaret which explores what painting and ecological calamity have in 
common. The work in Surface Tension examines the intersection of the 
fluidity and physical nature of painting and that of environmental 
consequence. Sandy Margaret's mixed media work on paper explores 
the way paint behaves upon a substrate- mirroring potential lyrical and 
littoral disaster.

Sandy Margaret is an emerging artist in Vancouver originally from 
Atlantic Canada. She is a graduate of Emily Carr University with a BFA 
in Visual Arts and is also a scenic designer having recently attended 
Douglas College, specializing in theatre design and scenic painting. 
Sandy has exhibited at various galleries in Vancouver, most recently at 
the Audain Gallery at SFU Woodwards. This is her first solo show.

Established in June of 2015 by a small group of artists seeking working 
and gallery space, The Phoenix is located in the lower level of the 
historic Phoenix building on west Hastings street. It is an emerging 
gallery and studio space having hosted several exhibitions by local 
artists, performers, and makers.

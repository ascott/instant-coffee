Sad Mag, Issue 3 Launch Party, Friday, March 19

Sad Mag proudly launches its 3rd issue, the bold and beautiful Black &
White issue, Friday, March 19 (8pm-2am) at the Legendary ANZA Club (3 West
8th Avenue).

Get a first look at the new issue and meet the emerging artists beyond the
pages of the magazine. Enjoy performances by Vancouver's "Queen of Sass,"
our beautiful covergirl, Ms. Crystal Precious, the music of ok vancouver
ok, DJs Lazerbomb! and DJ Tina's Husband.

Tickets are $10 (at the door). Grab a copy of the magazine and celebrate
with us! Cheap drinks!

Sad Mag is a local, non-profit arts and culture magazine that is published
quarterly, with a mandate to support Vancouver's diverse culture and young
artists. Sad Mag feature the lives and work of Vancouver's residents as
seen through the eyes of burgeoning writers and visual artists based in the
Vancouver area.

Queer Zone: a drawing performance
Location: Leg-in-boot Square, Vancouver, BC
Day & Time: Sunday, August 25th, 2024. 10AM-2PM
Contact: Kate Barry, queerzone2024@gmail.com

You are invited to the Queer Zone!

The Queer Zone is a one-day community art event for 2SLGBTQ+ people and their
friends & allies that will activate the seawall with a collaborative drawing performance.
2SLGBTQ+ community members are invited to reclaim their relationship to public space
by responding to the theme of queer peace. Folks interested in drawing will help to
create a large drawing on canvas and may also use environmentally friendly sidewalk
chalk to queer the area around the square. Please drop-by to say, Hello, and chill with
us or to do some drawing! All drawing supplies provided. Please bring your own water
and snacks. The Queer Zone has seating prioritized for people with disabilities or
mobility issues as well as ASL and
non-verbal support.

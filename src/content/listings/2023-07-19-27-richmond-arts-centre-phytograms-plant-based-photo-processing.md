Phytograms: Plant-Based Photo-Processing

Create images on photo paper in this hands-on workshop that utilizes plants and
organic materials in place of darkroom chemicals. Artist Jeffery Chong will lead
participants through the process and share experiments from his own practice.

Participants will receive supplies to produce one print on photography paper.
Participants may bring their own supply of photography paper and film for further
experimentation. Expired paper and film can be used in this process.

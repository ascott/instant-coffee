Please join us for ilvs strauss'
MFA Project, Peter and the Wolf, presented in
collaboration with Colin Williscroft, June
Hsu, Laura Coons, and Megan Lane.

Peter and the Wolf is a retelling of the classic symphonic fairy tale for children, queerly
remade through a lens of digital and circumstantial scenography. Incorporating
elements of film, live music, and imposed communication technologies, the
performance boldly imagines what has transpired in the theatre in the six months since
the artists were forced to abandon the theatre, leaving the characters to their own
devices. Peter and the Wolf explores the performance and agency of identity through a
layered, mediated lens, inviting viewers to reflect on new definitions of otherness, of
spectatorship, of representation. Told largely from the perspective of the Wolf, the
performance challenges diametric oppositions of the self through an infinite journey of
simultaneity, moving away from Either and Or towards an inclusive Neither alive with
possibilities.

Presented
in partial fulfillment of the requirements of the Degree of Master of Fine Arts
at Simon
Fraser University.

This work will be presented and has been developed on the unceded traditional
territories of the Coast Salish peoples of the Musqueam,
Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh Nations.

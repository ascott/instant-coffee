INSTALLATION BY DEFAULT

INSTALLATION BY DEFAULT IS AN EXHIBITION OF ARCHITECTURAL 
INTERVENTIONS

Three artists have been invited to address the essential, but ultimately 
underwhelming details of a room - vacant corners, the flare of fluorescence, 
and the thickness of door frames do not undergo further disregard in this 
exhibition. These innocuous, readily consumable visual entities are a 
departure point for an examination of art and architecture in cohabitation. 
These works have been installed to erode the underrated quality of these quiet 
but fundamental facets - the seams that uphold the experience of layering art 
upon architecture. If DYNAMO is a sweater, we will be wearing it inside out. 
Thank you. 

Featuring works by Amanda Jehring, Stefan Sollenius and Zebulon Zang
Installation by Default is a Future Proof Press Production and is guest curated 
by Steffanie Ling

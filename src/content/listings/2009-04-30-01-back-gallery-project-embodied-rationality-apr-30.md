The Back Gallery Project has resurfaced at a new location with renewed commitment to 
continuing its support to the arts and the artists that the gallery represents both from Vancouver 
and Santiago.

BGP is pleased to invite you to the opening of "Embodied Rationality" this coming Thursday April 
30th, 2009 from 6 - 8 pm to mark this new phase.

Back Gallery Project is now located at 306 Abbott Street in Vancouver's Gastown.

Among the artists included in this exhibit are:

Embodied Rationality seeks to overcome a subject-centered understanding of rationality,and thus 
transcend the dichotomy between contemplation and action.

The process of creativity is a combination of both. This exhibit is alook into a generation of artists 
with no national borders, whose practicesare as varied as the mediums that they have chosen. 
With disciplines that include sculpture made from marble,yarn, and recycled 
skateboards,cellphone camera photographs or acrylic on canvas, and drawings, photography, 
and collage, the artists are fearless of the different materials available to them, yet the 
worksdialogue among each other in a natural way.

The Back Gallery Project'sfirst exhibits were on South Granville Street,behind theMonte Clark 
Gallery,and from there moved to 109 W Cordova Street. Abbott Street is now the currentgallery 
location.

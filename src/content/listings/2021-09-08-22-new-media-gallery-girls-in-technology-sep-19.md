Each week this unique workshop series will focus on a different woman who has changed
the history of technology. Kids will learn about her amazing achievements, then create
imaginative activities that celebrate her contributions. Girls who take this workshop will be
given support & mentorship from our amazing instructors. They will explore cutting-edge
technologies in a supportive environment. We emphasize new ways of connecting with
technology including traditional & non-traditional hand skills, new media skills and
unconventional outlets for creative & imaginative expression. Girls and non-binary students
are welcome.

The Department of Art History Visual Art and Theory (AHVA) and AHVA Gallery are
pleased to present the Visual Art Undergraduate Exhibition 2022, featuring new student
work from second- and third-year Visual Art courses at the University of British
Columbia. Over sixty printmaking, painting, textile, drawing, sculptural, and digital
works are showcased in this exhibition"our first Visual Art undergraduate show since
2020!

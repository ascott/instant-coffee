Analogue Basics: Introduction to Hand Processing 16mm Film

Sunday, Jul 22; 2 PM - 6 PM
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander St (driveway entrance),
Vancouver, British Columbia V6Z 2L7
$40"$60 + taxes

REGISTER: [https://bit.ly/2MMZG4p](https://bit.ly/2MMZG4p)

A 4 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles
and practice of processing 16mm black and white film by hand. Principles
also apply to Super 8 black and white film.

$40 + taxes | General Admission
$50 + taxes | Concession
$60 + taxes | Member Admission

Elements include:

Principles of chemical developing
Darkroom safety
Bucket processing
Tank processing

Film shot at the Analogue Basics: Introduction to the Bolex Camera workshop
(Sunday Jul 15) will be processed in this class. If interested in both
components (optional), please register for the Bolex class via the
following link:

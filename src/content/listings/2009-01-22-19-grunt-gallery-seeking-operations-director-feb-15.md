Operations Director
grunt gallery

grunt gallery is a non-profit artist-run centre formed in 1984 in
Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. We are committed to providing a forum
for a wide spectrum of critical discourse and multidisciplinary art
practices. Our mission is to engage communities in creating environments
that support collaboration, innovation and challenge contemporary art in
Canada.

We are seeking a highly motivated, organized and creative individual to
fill the full-time position of Operations Director, to commence March 15, 2009.

Reporting directly to the Board of Directors the Operations Director (OD)
is one of three permanent staff positions and will work in partnership
alongside the gallery's founding Artistic Director to facilitate the
day-to-day financial, administrative, external communications and human
resource functions of the gallery. The OD will also be responsible for
leading fundraising with the board through membership, annual and endowment
campaigns.

Accountable for guiding and participating in the collaborative day to day
operations conducted by a team of contract employees, the OD will be
responsible for facilitating daily operations. These include but are not
limited to budgeting and financial management, staffing coordination, and
the creation, documentation and implementation of information systems and
operational procedures. Marketing and fundraising experience will be
essential as the OD will also be responsible for developing systems for
communications and fund development.

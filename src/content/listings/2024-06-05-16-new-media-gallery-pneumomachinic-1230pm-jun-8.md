ABOUT THE EXHIBITION
Xon Xil (Spain), Ali Miharbi (Turkey), Cristhian vila Cipriani
PneumoMachinic is a sound exhibition, presenting the work of three, award-winning
artists who explore the creative and cultural significance of breath. From the earliest,
human speech, to installations evoking the ancient music of Peru and Spain, their
work suggests how breath has influenced the direction of the human condition. The
artists in this exhibition have created air-driven, sound installations following
exceptional research and using complex technologies. Their work expands and
compresses breath; evoking, mechanising and instrumentalizing it in meaningful
flows, beats, currents and codes. We invite you to join us in celebrating the work of
three, exceptional artists.

Event: Toque! Christmas Craft Sale
Date: Friday, November 27, 6-9pm & Saturday November 28, 11am - 5pm
Listing: Western Front is pleased to present Toque, our annual fine art
and craft sale, featuring a curated selection of original, local arts and
fine crafts that make great unique holiday gifts. Look for exclusive
Western Front merchandise including a specially commissioned original
artwork tea towel by Cindy Mochizuki. Proceeds from Toque support the
Western Front Society.

Press Release:

Best buys for the holiday rush at Toque, 2009

Western Front Society is pleased to present their annual fund-raising
Christmas craft sale. Find the perfect holiday gifts for all of the
important people on your list at Toque, the Western Front's annual fine art
and craft sale on Friday, November 27 and Saturday, November 28.

Toque features a curated selection of original artwork, jewellery,
handcrafted home décor items, and other unique gifts, all designed and
manufactured locally. Over 20 artists, designers and artisans come together
for this signature sale, including familiar Toque participants Simply Read
Books, Gailan Ngan Ceramics and Contexture Designs along with Toque
newcomers A Farmers Daughter and Wendy Oakman.Look for exclusive Western
Front merchandise including a specially commissioned original artwork tea
towel by Cindy Mochizuki, Western Front cupcakes and more!

Proceeds from Toque support the Western Front Society, an artist run centre
and non-profit registered charity. Held in the warm heritage setting of the
Western Front in Vancouver, the sale is the perfect feelgood holiday
shopping experience.

ALL THE COOL GIRLS WERE GEMSTONES BUT I WAS JUST A SEDIMENTARY ROCK
Lianne Zannier

Online Exhibition
Web Launch: September 12, 2018
Visit: www.farafield.ca

Curated by Caitlin Chaisson

Lianne Zanniers "all the cool girls were gemstones but i was just a sedimentary rock" is an 
online exhibition that will be featured on Far Afield's web space. The exhibition explores 
socially constructed expectations and interpretations of value, and how we designate 
something as precious. Zannier takes the geological as a proposition of malleability and 
movement, using mineral abstractions as a way to invite a closer and more attentive look. In 
her hybrid experiments combining hand-drawn and digital compositions, the lithic performs 
in surprising and unusual ways.

This exhibition is part of 'Touchstones: Deep Time in the Near Future', a three-part curatorial 
series featuring the work of artists Lexie Owen, Carrielynn Victor, and Lianne Zannier. 
'Touchstones' originated as an investigation into a time capsule that was buried on a farm in 
Agassiz thirty years ago. Throughout the month of September, these three artist projects will 
address ideas of hidden or embedded knowledge, nature's intelligence, and systems of 
value.

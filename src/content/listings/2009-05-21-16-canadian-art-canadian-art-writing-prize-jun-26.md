Designed to encourage and discover new writers on contemporary art

Submission deadline is June 26

The Canadian Art Writing Prize launches in conjunction with Canadian Art's 25th anniversary

First prize is $3,000

Two honourable mentions will receive $1,000 each.

The jury for the prize includes Richard Rhodes, editor of Canadian Art, Ray
Cronin, director of the Art Gallery of Nova Scotia, and Nancy Tousley, art
critic at the Calgary Herald.

The winning submission will be published in Canadian Art magazine.
Go to http://www.canadianart.ca/foundation/programs/writingprize/ for full
submission guidelines

RAG Collage Club: August Session

Thursday, August 7, 2025

6:30 PM - 8:30 PM

Richmond Art Gallery, 7700 Minoru Gate

$ 5.00 includes ALL art materials and tools.
Limited seating, register here: https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/rag-collage-club-august-
session-tickets-1407893590629

Get together with friends to make some art at our social Collage Club for adults!

We will provide a large assortment of paper ephemera and imagery for you to cut,
paste, and explore mixed-media collage. This month we will take up the art gallery
exhibition space to spread out and be surrounded by the art. The exhibition's
background ambience will feature dreamy music to help spark some visual ideas.
Inspired by the works in the exhibition, we will introduce some basic painting,
layering and medium transfer techniques that can be incorporated for mixed media
collages.

We will also connect to international collage groups for you to share your collage
works online by providing printed copies of collage challenges from various sites like
the Paris Collage Collective, Kolaj Magazine, and more.

All levels of artistic skill are welcome! Limited spaces, registration is required.
Recommended for ages 16+

$5 per person includes all art supplies and use of our equipment.

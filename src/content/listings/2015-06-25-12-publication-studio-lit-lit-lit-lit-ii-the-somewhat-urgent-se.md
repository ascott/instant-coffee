LIT LIT LIT LIT II & The Somewhat Urgent Series Official Chapbook Launch

Sunday, June 28 2015
8:00 PM
Publication Studio, 222 E Georgia St

Featuring new readings, written and given by Bradley Iles, Zuzia
Juszkiewicz, Ronan Nanning-Watson & Ingrid Olauson.

LIT LIT LIT LIT is a series of reading events organized by Steffanie Ling
and Emma Metcalfe Hurst that simply invites artists, writers and poets to
read their own work to interested parties. Please note a brief intermission
will follow the first two readings and concession will be available.

Bradley Iles is reading from Derby: A Poem in Six Parts, a recent
collaboration with Katrina Niebergal. Reincarnation, celebrity, the nature
of the self, the selfhood of nature, Mustang-transcendentalism, a poem
about a movie. Bradley lives on Melville street in Vancouver.

Zuzia Juszkiewicz is a fragment in some kind of multi-faceted hybrid zone
or something. She was born in Gdansk, and raised in South Africa, New
Zealand, and Canada. She holds a BA in Visual Arts (Vancouver), Human
Geography (Vancouver) and Urbanism at AHK (Amsterdam). She collaborates
with writers, musicians, filmmakers and dancers. She often works under the
name Modern Catalyst, with a focus on designing cross-disciplined artists.
http://modercatalyst.ca/

Ronan K. Nanning-Watson is a filmmaker, writer and artist from British
Columbia. His work ranges in scope, medium, and aesthetic, but is
consistently uncompromising. Recently he finished his first feature film
“Crusade”  about a group of young hipsters who start a holy war. The next
two parts in the trilogy are in preproduction.

Ingrid Olauson is a writer and trawler of people’s lives. She recently
wrote, produced and acted in the play and collaborative art piece Fort Eff,
which debuted at Deep Blue this past May.


The Somewhat Urgent Series is a series of chapbooks which document select
texts from previous LIT LIT LIT LIT readings.The following texts will be
available for the official launch:

“I’D HURT A FLY, I DON’T CARE IF I KILL A FLY, I’D KILL A FLY”  by Kara Hansen

&

“Clean Indoor Air Act 1, 2 of 3”  by Steffanie Ling

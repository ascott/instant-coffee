The Website of the Montreal contemporary art gallery "Les Territoires" is
now online! www.lesterritoires.org
Each season, Les Territoires curates a juried group show for which artists
are invited to submit works of art in respond to a specific theme. Our
first project will feature short videos. You can find all the details for
this call for videos here:
http://www.lesterritoires.org/english/submissions/special.htm
Deadline: Septembre 19
Please note that the deadline to submit your exhibition proposal for
Winter 2009 is October 1st.
For more informations:
http://www.lesterritoires.org/english/submissions/exhibitions.htm
Lastly, Les Territoires will open its doors on September 5th with the
launch of Manon De Pauw and Michel Laforest's art DVD "La petite fabrique
de temps". Please come and celebrate with us!

Best regards and see you soon!

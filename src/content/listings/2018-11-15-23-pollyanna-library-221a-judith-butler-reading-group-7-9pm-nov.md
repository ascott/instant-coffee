Pollyanna Library @ 221A
221 E. Georgia St, Vancouver, BC 
Tuesday, November 20, 2018
7:00 pm to 9:00 pm

This Fall at Pollyanna - Library, 221A, the University of British Columbias Morris and 
Helen Belkin Art Gallery, and the Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality and Social Justice are 
hosting a study group on the work of Judith Butler. This six-session study group is convened 
in anticipation of her academic visit and public lecture at UBC in the Winter of 2019.

No registration is necessary to participate. All sessions of the study group are open to all 
and free to attend. Each session will be facilitated by study leaders who have selected texts 
from Butlers work as primary readings paired alongside supplementary materials. 

Session 4 is facilitated by Marianne Nicolson.

Readings:
Gayatri Spivak and Judith Butler, excerpt, Who Sings the Nation-state? Language, Politics, 
Belonging (2007)

For access to study materials please email librarian@polly-anna.ca.

http://polly-anna.ca/activity/judith-butler-4

Portal Pop-Up is a small storefront window gallery space in Gastown for Vancouver
artists and designers, a highly visible location on pedestrian-friendly W. Cordova St.

We are seeking eye catching art and design easily presented in the Portal window
space.

***** We are now seeking submissions for 2025 exhibitions*****

@portalpopupgallery

Please submit! We are considering solo, duo and/or group projects. If you have any
preference please let us know.

Window Dimensions: upright window-facing wall: 10 w x 8 h, with a 48x20 shelf
for small objects. Both the right and left side of the window display will suit ~36"W x
50"H or smaller. Artists are encouraged to come take a look at the available window
display at 407 W. Cordova to consider how their own artwork could inhabit the
space.

Submit 50-100 words of text, your contact info, and up to 5 images of your work.
Please indicate dimensions and medium.

Submit through email: portalpopupgallery@gmail.com (see also contact info
@portalpopupgallery)

Submission deadline: end of day 24, 2024

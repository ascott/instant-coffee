EVENT
Bus Tour for Artists: DIY Art Spaces in Vancouver

Facilitated by Richmond Art Gallery, CARFAC BC and City of Richmond Public
Art Program
Saturday, June 11, 2016 from 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM

Starting at Richmond Cultural Centre, 7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond

Visit several "do-it-yourself" artist-run spaces in Vancouver for
inspiration and networking on how to develop an arts community for
exhibition and studio opportunities. Fee includes bus transportation from
Richmond to four different art spaces in Vancouver and back. The tour, led
by RAG and CARFAC BC staff, includes visits to Arts off Main, The Beaumont
Studios, Dynamo Arts Association, and the Gam Gallery for a brief tour and
discussion at each space.

$20 RAGA or CARFAC Members / $30 Non-Members
Pre-registration is required.
Limited spaces, register now to avoid disappointment.

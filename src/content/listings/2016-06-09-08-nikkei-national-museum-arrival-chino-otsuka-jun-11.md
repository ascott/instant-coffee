Come to the opening of Arrival at the Nikkei National Museum on Saturday,
June 11th to see the results of AIMIA/AGO finalist Chino Otsuka's residency
from 2014!
Arrival is a new media installation based on historical photographs from
the NNM collection interfaced with new original photography from Otsuka,
woven together with narration taken from historical transcripts of picture
brides who came to Canada from Japan from the late 1800s through to the
early 1900s on the promise of a photograph. Historical evidence cites that
most of their stories were less than happy and sometimes tragic, but
Arrival captures those early moments in these young girls lives when they
were filled with hope and anticipation.

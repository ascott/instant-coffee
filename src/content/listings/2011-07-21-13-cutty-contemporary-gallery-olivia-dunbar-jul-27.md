The Cutty Contemporary Gallery would like to submit a listing for the opening of 
Olivia Dunbar's show "HE SHOOTS HE SHOT" - we'd be honoured if you could 
include us on your mail-out! 

Opening Wednesday, July 27, 7pm 
Artist Talk Sunday, August 21, 2pm 

HE SHOOTS HE SHOT is a series of sculptures informed by The Experience of 
Freedom, an extended essay written by French philosopher Jean-Luc Nancy. 
Here Nancy suggests that the essential question of being is no longer "Why is 
there something rather than nothing?" but is instead "Why these very questions 
by which existence affirms itself and abandons itself in a single gesture?"

Cutty Contemporary Art 
564 Beatty St. 

Weekend 12-6

Dave Allen

Performance
7 October 2008 at 9pm
Charles H. Scott Gallery
Artist Talk
8 October 2008 at 7pm
ECU Theatre

The Charles H. Scott Gallery is pleased to present a performance and a talk
by Scottish artist Dave Allen. Allen has become known for making drawings,
videos, installations, and performance works in which he deals with the
relationship between visual art and rock music.

Allen is in Vancouver to participate in the Charles H. Scott Gallery
exhibition titled "The Sound I'm Looking For". He will perform "Inverted
Oh-Ton" at the opening of the exhibition on October 7th at 9pm and will
speak about his practice the following night, October 8th in the ECU Theatre.

with artists such as Jonathan Monk, Ross Sinclair, and Douglas Gordon.

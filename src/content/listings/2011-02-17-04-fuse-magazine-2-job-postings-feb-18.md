Fuse Magazine – 2 job postings
www.fusemagazine.org

Seeking an Editorial Director
Fuse seeks a highly motivated individual to solicit, develop, and 
coordinate our editorial content. The Editorial Director will have a 
broad artistic vision. You are a creative and strategic thinker, a 
facilitator who can articulate an editorial vision and further the 
organizational mandate, and a leader who can ensure 
communication and coordination. You have a strong knowledge of 
contemporary art, a proven record of publication production, editing 
and proofreading skills, excellent written and verbal communication 
skills, and experience with grant applications and budgeting.

The Editorial Director reports directly to the Board of Directors, and 
works with the Managing Director and Designer to develop material 
for publication, maintain and develop relations with contributors and 
contacts, monitor publication production schedules and 
expenditures and assist in the preparation of publication-related 
grant applications.

Seeking a Managing Director
The Managing Director is responsible for the overall financial health 
of the organization; directs circulation campaigns, creates and 
maintains cashflows, budgets and quarterly reports, supervises 
advertising staff and interns, seeks out funding and sponsorship 
opportunities, writes all grant applications in collaboration with the 
Editorial Director.

Major areas of responsibility are Administration, Financial 
Management, Circulation, Advertising, Promotion and Fundraising. 
The Managing Director reports to and is responsible to the authority 
of the Board of Directors.

To view the complete list of qualifications for both positions and 
details on how to apply, please visit 
http://fusemagazine.org/opportunities.

Looking Forward
In upcoming years, our goal is to reach increasing numbers of 
people through our website, increase our online presence and 
make it more interactive, commission more articles and artist 
projects and work collaboratively with a broader range of 
organizations — both through programming and in our online 
capacity. We are looking for individuals who not only understand 
Fuse's historical trajectory but also have a vision for taking the 
magazine into the future.

About Fuse
Fuse magazine provides innovative coverage of diverse visual and 
media arts communities and practices in Canada and 
internationally. Our editorial strategy is to offer readers in-depth 
assessment and critical analysis of the relationship of visual art to 
the social, cultural and economic contexts in which it is produced, 
supported, exhibited and disseminated.

Please send a cover letter and CV attention: Hiring Committee 
(hire@fusemagazine.org). Only applicants selected for an interview 
will be contacted. Thank you to all those who apply.

Deadline: February 18, 2011

Fuse Magazine is committed to achieving a representative 
workforce. Qualified diversity group members are encouraged to 
self identify on their applications.

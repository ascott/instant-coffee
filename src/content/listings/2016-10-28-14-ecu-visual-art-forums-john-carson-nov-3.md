Join us for a night of story and song with Pittsburgh based artist John Carson

Thursday November 3 | 5pm
MoCap Studio | Rm 285e, North Building
Emily Carr University of Art + Design

John Carson has presented live performances, soundworks, installations as
both and has been involved in many public art projects. He has exhibited
drawings, photographs, prints and sculpture in such venues as The Douglas
Hyde Gallery in Dublin, The ICA in London, CCA in Glasgow, IKON Gallery in
Birmingham, PS1 in New York, New Langton Arts in San Francisco and The
Mattress Factory in Pittsburgh.

He received his BFA from The University of Ulster in Belfast in 1976 and
his MFA from Cal Arts in 1983. From 1986 to 1991 he was Production Director
of Artangel, a London-based organization which presented temporary art
works in public locations. He has been a visiting lecturer at various
schools in Ireland, UK, Europe, the United States, Australia and New
Zealand. He taught at the National College of Art and Design in Dublin and
at Central Saint Martins College of Art and Design in London. where he was
Course Director of the BFA program from 1999 to 2006. From 2006 to 2016 he
was Head of the School of Art at Carnegie Mellon University.

This is a free event, open to everyone.

The Visual Art Forums are presented by the Audain Faculty of Visual Art and
Material Practice. This event is co-presented with the Charles H. Scott
Gallery and curated by Sean Lynch, Audain Distinguished Artist-in-Residence
and Michele Horrigan.

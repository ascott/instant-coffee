In celebration of Project Space's 10-year history producing one cool word
magazine, OCW Magazine, the 222 bookshop/gallery and the Vancouver Art/Book
Fair, we are hosting a multifaceted event featuring literary readings, art,
comedy, live music and a night market featuring local artists' book
publishers and artisans. The event also serves as a fundraiser for the 2016
Vancouver Art/Book Fair.
http://projectspace.ca/

Comedy by Jaz Halloran and Matterhorn

Music by Pink Licorice, Stefana Fratila, Les Chaussettes, Mark Mills, and
Cherchez

Art and Visuals curated by Sylvana d'Angelo and Alison Sinkewicz
Limited-edition prints by: Maya Natalie Gulin and Anezka Minarikova
Illustrations by: Juli Majer, Nathan Jones + Jeff Lee, Heidi Nagtegaal,
Alex Stursberg, Brennan Kelly, Sylvie Ringer, Aaron Read, Sylvana d'Angelo
and Chelsea O'Byrne

& MUCH MORE TBA

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to present Make It Strange, an
exhibition of works that
de-familiarize our conventional relationship to print media. Through
the fabrication or manipulation of signs, posters, official legal
documents, rubber stamps and altered printed ephemera, the works in

lives, the power it has to structure our experience and at the same
time present propositions for interaction with the medium.

Split Ends
Friday, August 16
6 " 8 PM PT

Join us for an intimate evening of readings by participants in Centre As 2024 Art Writing
Mentorship Program. Celebrating the close of our 12-week summer program,
participants will share works of fiction, creative nonfiction, poetry, and performance that
think through the possibilities of art writing. Spurred by shared readings that take up
writing as a process of not knowing (of wondering and thinking through), participants
employ place, body, and memory to write toward uncertainty and moments of transition.

Light snacks and refreshments will be provided.

Readings by:
Oceania Chee
Sena Cleave
Lauren Han
Malivia Khondaker
Parumveer Walia

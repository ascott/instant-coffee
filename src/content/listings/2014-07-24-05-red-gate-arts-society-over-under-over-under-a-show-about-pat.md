Over Under Over Under: A Show About Patterns
At: Red Gate Arts Society, 855 East Hastings
Thursday July 24 from 7 pm
Patterns are a bridge between the seeming chaos of nature and the logic 
of human-made production. Taking images found in nature, synonymous 
with untamed wilderness and danger, and turning them into predictable 
and controllable patterns allows humans to create the illusion of control 
over the natural world.

Multidisciplinary show including works relating to pattern through sculpture, 
painting, performance, print-making, drawing and installation.

Artists include:
Olga Abeleva
Alexandra Bischoff
Kai Choufour

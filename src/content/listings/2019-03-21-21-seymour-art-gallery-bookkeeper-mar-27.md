The Seymour Art Gallery was founded in 1985 as the first community operated art gallery on 
the North Shore. It moved into its current premises in the municipally-owned Deep Cove 
Cultural Centre in 1992, and continues to be the only public art gallery in the District of North 
Vancouver.

As stated in the Seymour Art Gallery Societys Constitution, its purposes are (a) to establish 
and maintain an active and financially sustainable Art Gallery that will foster excellence and 
promote interest in the arts with emphasis in the District of North Vancouver; (b) to provide 
exhibitions in all media in the Seymour Art Gallery by establishing a high standard and 
balanced program of visual and performing arts; and (c) to play an educational role in 
development of arts appreciation for the public and schools by organizing educational and 
outreach activities in conjunction with exhibitions.

Job Description: Bookkeeper

Responsibilities: Working with limited oversight from the Curator/Director and using sales 
data assembled by the Gallery Assistant, the Bookkeeper will be responsible for entering all 
financial information and maintaining the Gallerys financial records in Simply Accounting, as 
well as preparing all cheques for signature and making required CRA and other government 
filings.

Qualifications:

-Strong background in bookkeeping and use of Simply Accounting software
-Skills in other Microsoft Office programs, especially MS Excel
-Detail-oriented and precise
-Ability to work independently
-High level of professionalism and ability to communicate and work well with other staff, 
volunteers, and other members of the gallery community
-Experience in an arts-based and/or non-profit organization an asset

Specific Tasks:

-Keep a complete and up-to-date record of Gallery and Shop income and expenses, and 
undertake related book-keeping and banking activities including bank deposits, cash float 
and bank reconciliations, etc.
-Prepare an income/expense summary for the prior month(s) in advance of each Board 
meeting (11 meetings/year)
-Maintain the Gallerys accounts with various financial agencies and service providers, 
including banking, credit card and insurance.
-Prepare the reporting and remittances to government agencies.
-Provide financial input to grant applications
-Maintain records of staff hours and benefits and prepare monthly payroll.
-Coordinate with other Gallery staff as necessary to ensure smooth operation and 
information flow.

Hours: The Bookkeeper will work approximately a 3-4 hour day, one day a week " on 
Tuesdays, to facilitate coordination with the Curator/Director.

Remuneration: Starting wage is $20/hour, to be reviewed after 6 months.

info@seymourartgallery.com (Please write Bookkeeper Position in the subject line.)

Deadline for applications: Applications must be received by Wednesday, March 27, 2019.

We thank all who express interest in this position, however, only those selected for an

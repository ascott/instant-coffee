NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS form public constellations of 
engaged interdisciplinary practice, unmediated by the realpolitik 
conditions of contemporary art as a competitive professional sphere. 
The interrelatedness of art, society and everyday life is critically 
explored amongst a diverse plurality of artists and publics. Jeremy 
Todd instigates each event as an extension of his ongoing Not Sent 
Letters Project, an entanglement with the production of meaning, self 
and cultural memory, involving image/text epistolary detours online 
(since 2005), digital shorts, interdisciplinary performance works and 
cooperatively realized public events: [http://notsentlettersproject.com](http://notsentlettersproject.com) 
This is the nineteenth NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS event.

DAMLA TAMER (an artist interested in the artistic-political stakes of 
encountering singular events, forgetting them, and then letting them 
reconfigure in relation to other forms of representation) provides 
narration for a legless, carved-out table (once university property) that 
has gone through various modifications vis-à-vis successive 
institutional and/or artistic re-presentations. 

EDWARD SHARP (a Portland-based performance artist) and SIDONY 
O”NEAL (a Portland-based poet, writer, and language specialist with an 
emphasis in translation) make their first contribution to NSL&G as 
FUTURE DEATH AGENCY, a throbbing mess of noise that eatz 
technology and shitz performance art. 

SARAH SHAMASH (a Vancouver-based artist and interdisciplinary 
doctoral student) returns to NSL&G to debut Slideshow, an ode to 
Janet Cardiff, Gloria, creativity, life, and everything in-between.

ADAM ROSE (a Chicago-based artist and artistic director of Antibody 
Corporation) presents Neue Welt, incorporating movement, sound and 
text to dance around the idea of a, or the, “New World.”

NIKOLAI GAUER (a Vancouver-based artist and musician focused on 
the delivery and composition of causeries) returns to NSL&G with a 
new work sharing fictive experiences from his own life through the 
voices of inanimate objects.

BRIT BACHMANN (a Vancouver-based visual artist and writer, 
influenced by pedestrian culture and voyeurism) performs Sound of 
Centre, a work developed in technical collaboration with BRENDAN 
YANDT (an artist working in word, sound and image) to explore 
performative ritual as medium while creating a liminal state in which to 
contemplate the themes of the Not Sent Letters Project. 

SHAUNA JEAN DOHERTY (a curator and writer currently based in 
Vancouver) debuts Beach, a dream-like document of Toronto Island. 
The hazy in-camera edited Super8 footage reflects the fleeting 
memories it attempts to record (with music by BEN WILSON).

MARGARET DRAGU (a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary 
performance artist) and FRANCISCO-FERNANDO GRANADOS (a 
Toronto-based artist working in performance, drawing, and 
multidisciplinary critical practices) screen Spatial Profiling, derived from 
a 3 hour durational performance with live tv web-streaming between 
Toronto and Vancouver to celebrate May Day 2013.

ELMIRA HABIBULLAH (a Vancouver-based artist with an increasing 
interest in conceptual art) returns to NSL&G with a new video work 
exploring various art making processes amongst established 
contemporary artists.

GRAHAM MEISNER (a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary artist and 
musician originally from Halifax) returns to NSL&G with a music video 
that explores ineffectual responses to accumulated loss.

RAFAEL ALBERTO ARGUELLO RUIZ (a Vancouver-based filmmaker 
and video producer) screens About An Acrobat, a conversation with 
Darren Bersuk about maintaining his career while negotiating the 
limitations of his aging body.

JEREMY TODD presents a new NOT SENT DIGITAL SHORT featuring 
voiceover by ELISA FERRARI (reading her selection of a letter to 
Willing Conflators Of Jamaica Kincaid And Jeremy Todd from the 
project archive).

JEREMY TODD presents a new NOT SENT LETTERS SET in 
collaboration w/ BEN WILSON & NIKOLAI GAUER. The work 
incorporates performance, projections, live scoring, reading and play, 
with letters to The Creative Classes, Willing Conflators Of Oliver Twist 
And Jeremy Todd, and All Other Accidental Bohemians, selected from 
the project archive.

considering constraints | zandi dandizette

Conduit Gallery | 165 East Hastings

February 7-21st

Opening: February 7th 7pm

Open Saturdays 1-3pm and by appointment (email conduitgallery165@gmail.com to 
book)

When discussing tropes we can sometimes reinforce their structures while dissecting 
the binary aspects. considering constraints seeks to address this conundrum by 
breaking the binary with organic anomalies and a rotating loop of many faces without 
bodily intervention. Zandi frames the conversation with the material relationship to 
childhood utilizing puzzle foam mats. This time period is the foundation of our life as we 
are given names and identities to order the chaos in our existence. The absurdity being 
that weve created unrealistic expectations within these constraints.

Zandi Dandizette invites you to ascribe meaning and context to their body in a 
performance at the closing of the show on February 21st at 7pm. They will be wearing a 
blank canvas in which you may make marks over their entire being for an hour duration. 
They will continue to wear the created piece for the rest of the evening.


Zandi Dandizette is a interdisciplinary New Media installation artist and curator living 
and practicing in Vancouver, BC who originates from Portland, OR. They graduated from 
Emily Carr University of Art + Design with a Bachelor of Media Arts (2014). 
Demonstrating unwavering support for the arts in British Columbia, Zandi is the Founder 
and Director of the James Black Gallery (2014), Programs and Membership Coordinator 
for CARFAC, BC (2018) and member of the Board of Directors for VIVO Media Arts 
Centre (2018 " 2020).Zandi Dandizettes artwork has shown internationally in Australia, 
Canada, the United States, Iran, and the United Kingdom.

Zandi Dandizette likens their medium as space whether 2D or 3D. They use colour, 
queerness,gender, shape, and line to explore discourse around identity, through 
immersive interactions via the binary, absurdism, liminality, and fractal existence. They 
view it as a base code, and every medium that they learn is a tool to explore this 
identity-centric world building. Zandi most notably creates projection paintings and 
immersive installations, which have been displayed at VIVO Media Arts Centre, 
Richmond Digital Carnival WATER, Vancouver Arts and Leisure, Mount Pleasant 
Business Improvement Association, Satellite Art Show, and YVR Public Disco. They 
work with ideological tropes that are built in, rather than referencing contemporary 
Intellectual Properties and empowering them. Zandi utilizes colors like blue, pink, and 
purple, and shapes like circles, squares, and triangles for their binary and hierarchical 
aspects.


Conduit Gallery is an artist-run project founded in 2018 and directed by Allison Watters. 
It provides a platform for early career and local artists whose work initiates abstract 
experiences through performance and installation.

Conduit Gallery is located on the unceded, ancestral and traditional territories of the 
xmky"m (Musqueam), Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), and sl"ilwta (Tsleil-
Waututh) Nations.

Accessibility: Wheelchair accessible, entrance is on street, main level with no steps.

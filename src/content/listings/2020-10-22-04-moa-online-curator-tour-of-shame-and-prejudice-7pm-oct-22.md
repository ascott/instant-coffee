Online Curator Tour of Shame and Prejudice

This week MOA is offering you the chance to visit Kent Monkman's Shame and
Prejudice: A Story of Resilience from the comfort
of your own home.

Whether you've seen this thought-provoking exhibition, or can't come in person, join this
online tour with Jennifer Kramer, MOA Curator, Pacific Northwest, as she takes you
through the nine chapters of the exhibition, beginning in 18th century New France: The
Reign of the Beaver to the Urban Rez of Winnipegs North End.

October 22 | 7 - 8:30 pm
Online via Zoom
Free, registration required

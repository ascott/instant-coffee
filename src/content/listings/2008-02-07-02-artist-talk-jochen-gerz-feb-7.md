Artist’s Talk
 
Jochen Gerz 
Creating a Cultural City
 
Thursday, February 7 at 5:30pm
at the Vancouver Art Gallery in Room 302
 
In conjunction with the Gallery’s newest exhibition The Tree: From the Sublime to the Social, join exhibiting artist Jochen Gerz as he presents his current research on the role and function of public art in a cultural society.
 
Jochen Gerz is an artist whose public commissions deal with issues of memory, visibility and collaboration with the public. Recent works include The Place of the European Promise (Bochum) and Salviamo La Luna (Milan). Since the 1980s, he has created a number of anti-monuments in Germany, France, England and Ireland, subverting the idea of commemoration, involving the public and media in the process of creating and defining artwork: Monument against Fascism, Harburg, (with Esther Shalev-Gerz) 1986; Bremen Questionnaire, Bremen, 1990; 2,146 stones - Monument Against Racism (The Invisible Monument), Sarrebrück, 1990-93; The Living Monument, Biron, 1996; The Witnesses of Cahors, 1998; The Public Bench and The Future Monument, Coventry, 2004; amaptocare, Dublin, 2006.
 
Free with Gallery admission
For information phone 604-662-4717

Vancouver Art Gallery 
February 2 – April 20, 2008

04. colourschool | february schedule
*Brown Bag Lunch with Brendan Fernandes | Fri Feb 8 | 12:30 to 2 pm
colourschool’s Brown Bag events comprise a series of lunch time discussions focused on a given subject or range of subjects. Participants may bring their lunch or take a brown bag provided by colourschool.

For this session, New York and Toronto-based artist Brendan Fernandes speaks about his recent work, which deals with notions of identity through post colonial discourse. Born in Kenya of Indian heritage, Brendan Fernandes immigrated to Canada in the
1990s. He earned his MFA (2005) from The University of Western Ontario and his BFA (2002) from York University in Canada. He has exhibited across Canada and in the Western New York Biennial (Albright-Knox Art Gallery, Buffalo, NY). Fernandes completed the Whitney Independent Study Program (2007), and is presently participating in The Lower Manhattan Cultural Council’s Work Space Residency program, and holds the position of Artist in Residence at The School of Visual Arts, NY, in
the graduate program for computer arts.

•Listening Lab: Indians, Cavemen, and Ancient Civilizations with Raymond Boisjoly | Tue Feb 12 | 7 pm

A look at various ways artists have dealt with the past and primordial origins within popular music. The effect is both satirical and earnest in turn, from the Cramps silly fun to the anger of Eugene McDaniels.  Guests are invited to bring song and sound files, titles, and videos for collective aural enjoyment.

*White Reading Group with Eryne Donahue | Sat Feb 16 | 1 pm

Taking Richard Dyer's *White* as its object of study, the white reading group continues its discussion of the book, which traces representations of whiteness in Western visual culture.  In this session, the group continues discussing excerpts from Chapter 2: Coloured white, not coloured. 

*Colour Exchanges:  Johan Lundh interviews Nathalie Melikian | Mon Feb 25 | 7 pm

In place of the artist talk, colourschool presents an ongoing series of artist interviews conducted by Johan Lundh.  Johann interviews Nathalie Melikian for this session.  Since the late 1990s, Melikian has been creating videos in which she questions and analyzes the narrative structures of various film genres. Her work has been exhibited in solo shows at the MuHKA, in Antwerp, Belgium, the Malmö Konstmuseum, Sweden, the Frankfurter Kunstverein, Germany, the Centre pour l’image contemporaine Saint-Gervais Genève, Switzerland, and the IASPIS, Sweden. She has also participated in a number of group shows at the Vancouver Art Gallery, the Edmonton Art Gallery, and the Centro José Guerrero and MARCO in Spain among other venues. 

*An Open Studio in Two Parts: Part One: Artist's Presentation with Heather Keung | Tue Feb 26 | 7 pm
Co-presented by Western Front Media Arts

Heather Keung will be taking a two-pronged approach to her research in Vancouver. First, she will be developing concepts and execution plans for two new video series and will show her body of work and these new projects in an in-progress presentation/artist talk.

*An Open Studio in Two Parts: Part Two: Visitors' Presentations with Heather Keung | Tue Feb 26 | 7 pm
Co-presented by Western Front Media Arts

In Part Two, Keung will be conducting studio visits with Vancouver-based artists for media/installation presentations at the Reel Asian International Film Festival (Toronto, November 2008). Interested parties are encouraged to arrange times in advance by contacting Heather Keung directly at programming@reelasian.com.

*About:
colourschool is located @ [IDS] ECIAD,1399 Johnston Street, Vancouver, BC, V6H 3R9 or online at www.colourschool.org
Email: info@colourschool.org

colourschool is a school within a school dedicated to the speculative research and exploration of five colours: black, white, brown, yellow, and red.  Providing a free and open space for critical investigations of colour, identity, artmaking, and knowledge production, colourschool attempts to develop a collaborative colour consciousness through a variety of events including reading groups, film screenings, listening labs, interviews, roundtable discussions, brown bag lunches, performances, and installations among other activities.  All are welcome.

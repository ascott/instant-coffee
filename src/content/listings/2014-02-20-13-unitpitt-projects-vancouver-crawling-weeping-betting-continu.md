Vancouver, Crawling, Weeping, Betting

continuing until March 1, 2014
Battery Opera PerformanceWalking Projects : “Vancouver, crawling, 
weeping, betting”  is a co-presentation by UNIT/PITT and battery opera 
performance, comprising six weeks of public actions, clandestine 
screenings, chance meetings, and intoxicated psychogeographies.

Starting on January 16, the artist-run gallery UNIT/PITT Projects has been the 
focal point for an ambitious series of midnight performances, late-evening 
salons, and improvised city tours by visual artists, dancers, musicians, and 
writers. Now in its fifth week, it has already featured stories told by Margo 
Kane and Michael Turner; a virtuoso live painting performance by Bracken 
Hanuse Corlett; improvised dances by Paras Terezakis, Brian Solomon, and 
others; all drawing on the installation, writing, and wine pairings provided by 
co-curators David McIntosh and Chris Bose.

The accompanying book, Vancouver, Crawling, Weeping, Betting, consists of 
twelve illustrated stories by Bose and McIntosh, and is co-published by 
UNIT/PITT and Publication Studio Vancouver.

Week 5 of the project features a story by Cease Wyss, with dance by 
Justine Chambers, and music by Ben Brown at Friday evening's Salon. Week 
6 begins with a Thursday midnight performance by Daina Ashbee; during the 
Friday evening Salon, a story by Henry Tsang, and music by Max Murphy, 
Ben Brown, and Russell Scholburg. The final week of the project features a 
midnight performance by Chris Bose, followed by a Friday Salon with a story 
by Veda Hille, dance by Arash Khakpour, and music by Max Murphy, Joel 
Lower, Gary Wildeman, and Bruce Freedman. Every Saturday afternoon 
through March 1, unpredictable and beguiling walking tours of the city 
originate from UNIT/PITT. (Complete schedule information is below.) Admission 
to all events is free.

Thursdays: 11:30pm – 12:30 am Witching hour solos (viewable from the 
street through live projections on windows)
Fridays: 10pm – midnight Bob’s Salon (interactive art salon for the curious 
imbiber, featuring guest artists)
Saturdays: 12 – 5 pm Guided improvised tours of the city by six performers, 
based on Bose and McIntosh’s stories and maps. (first come, first tour).

Co-curators Chris Bose and David McIntosh retrace their separate 
trajectories through the streets of the city. Their memories, reanimated 
wraiths and the spirits imbibed are recorded in a series of twelve stories and 
accompanying maps. Installed in the gallery, these reports and boundaries 
document their embodied experiences of Vancouver. Afternoon guided city 
tours, late night improvised art salons and witching hour solos, will enable 
guest artists, citizens and visitors to respond, react or ignore the reflected 
world of Bose and McIntosh. Bring your ghosts, bring your body, have a drink 
and take your chances. Join the conversation about this city, your place, our 
past and its spirits – both the ones that stubbornly linger, and the ones that 
enable forgetting.

Chris Bose is a war party baby of the N’Laka’Pamux and the Secwepmec 
Nations (they hooked-up at St. George’s residential school in Lytton). He is a 
writer, artist, musician, filmmaker and a Scoundrel.

David McIntosh is a settler party baby of a White-Man and a White-Lady (they 
hooked-up at the Waldorf bar in Vancouver). He is a writer, singer, state-
funded-artist, and a Sommelier.

battery opera performance is a performing arts company based in 
Vancouver. The work of battery opera interrogates the contemporary body 
as a site of intersecting and displaced cultures, histories and habits. battery 
opera performance investigates this body, this place, within a contemporary-
colonial matrix, cognizant of the interplay between art, power, politics and 
history.

UNIT/PITT is an artist-run organization founded in 1975, with a history of vital

More than a gallery, we are a platform for vibrant experimentation.

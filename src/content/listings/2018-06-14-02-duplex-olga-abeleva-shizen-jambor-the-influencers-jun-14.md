The Influencers
Olga Abeleva and Shizen Jambor
Exhibition Dates: June 14 to July 12, 2018

Opening: June 14, 2018 at 7pm

Duplex is pleased to present The Influencers, a film by artists Olga
Abeleva and Shizen Jambor. Produced as an advertisement for the fictive
shadowy conglomerate Kunstgriff, the film offers the potential for
lifestyle updates, synergetic networking, and career metamorphosis. Through
the intersecting lives of three art archetypes: a curator, an artist, and
an academic, the film follows each characters existential journey from
stagnation, to failure, and eventual rebirth.

Resembling something between a commercial, music video, web series, and
television sitcom, The Influencers reveals a suspicious, self-reflective,
and cathartic parody of its own discipline. Combining theatrical staging,
amateur acting, and campy makeup, the artists invent an absurd image of
pretensions and desires in a world that specializes in aesthetic inflation.

Olga Abeleva and Shizen Jambor are artists, writers, curators, and
entrepreneurs based on the unceded Coast Salish ancestral territories of
the Musqueam, Squamish, Tsleil-Waututh peoples. Their collaborative
relationship has endured for three years and they have exhibited here and
in Berlin, where their combined creative practice began. Their combined
practice explores the semiotics of branding and the roles of posturing and
emulation in individual myth-making. They employ theatre methodologies for
satirical engagement with the systems of interchange between social,
cultural, and economic capital. Their work spans video, conceptual,
business, print, sculpture and installation. Their recent entrepreneurial
effort, Kunstgriff, endeavours to form an adaptive business model tailored
to the needs of contemporary creatives.

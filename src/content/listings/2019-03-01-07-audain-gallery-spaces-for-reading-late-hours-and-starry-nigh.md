Spaces for Reading: Late Hours and Starry Nights
FRI, MAR 1 / 6 " 9PM
SFU Gallery and Trottier Observatory

For one night only, SFU Gallery will be extending its hours for Ann 
Beam and Carl Beam: Spaces for Reading on Friday, March 1 from 6 " 
9pm. Visitors to the exhibition are welcome to attend Starry Nights at 
Trottier Observatory, steps away from the gallery, for their star gazing 
event that takes place 7 " 10pm.

Images of the cosmos occur alongside a myriad of references in both 
Ann Beams and Carl Beams work throughout Spaces for Reading, 
bringing into question systems of representation and the construction 
of history.

----

*Note that the Observatory portion is weather dependent. An 
announcement will be made on March 1st to confirm. If you have your 
own binoculars or a telescope, bring it with you.

Directions from SFU bus loop (last stop): Due to construction, enter the 
main doors at Blusson Hall, just to the north, and follow the hallway 
straight to the far end and down the stairs into the AQ. Turn left down 
the Eastern corridor, then turn right down the South corridor. SFU 
Gallery is located in the middle of the South corridor.

Directions from East Parking: Walk straight (west) into the Applied 
Sciences Building. Walk through the building to where it joins the AQ 
(Academic Quadrangle). Walk down five steps and turn left. The gallery 
is 100 meters down the hall.

If in need of directions call 778-782-4266.

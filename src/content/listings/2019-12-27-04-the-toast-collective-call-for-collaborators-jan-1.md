The Toast Collective is looking for collaborators!

We are a volunteer group of organizers and event runners based out of a small-yet-flexible
space at 648 Kingsway. We host a number of ad-hoc music / art / culture events each
month, as well as some regular series
such as Fake Jazz, Quiet City, and Poetry Is Bad For
You. We're committed to doing the right stuff as best we can: most of our events foreground
underrepresented voices, and the communities that we foster at events are super respectful
and posi and important. We've been around in one form or another for over 10 years!

We are looking for another collective of artists who need space to do their thing. A creative
cabal or community circle, an art assemblage or inventive bunch with fresh ideas to put the
space into use and make novel ambitious happenings.

Dancer-poets, sculptor-activists, or whatever you are: you are invited.

We are a group looking to relation-exchange with another group. We want to adapt to one
another and make a cool new thing.

Here's the gritty: part of this potential collaboration would mean helping us cover costs, and
we're hoping to secure a six-month minimum commitment. Our rent is growing every year!
But it's all and always a conversation, and we're willing to
work out the money and time
issues in a way that we all agree is fair. Also: the space is very available during the daytime
and on weekdays for almost any kind of activity, but we have most sweet and generous
neighbours upstairs so we
cannot unfortunately collab with bands or intense loudnesses
looking for a rehearsal space or the like.

What's your crew up to? How do you wanna harmonize with the Toast?

Let us know at the.toast.collective@gmail.com

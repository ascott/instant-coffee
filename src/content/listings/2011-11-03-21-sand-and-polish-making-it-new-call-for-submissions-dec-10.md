Call for Submissions
Deadline: Dec 10, 2011
Sand and Polish: Making It New 
Images of Restoration

Artisan Church is accepting submissions for an upcoming show to be held at 
"The Moat", a gallery space located on the lower level of the Vancouver Public 
Library, Central Branch. We are inviting visual artists working two-
dimensionally in drawing, painting, photography, printmaking or mixed media, to 
investigate the theme of renewal and restoration. The idea is not to mask or 
cover over something that is inherently flawed or useless, but a sort of 
discovery and excavation of that which was good but has been lost. We are 
looking for fresh approaches in articulating our common desire and hope for 
newness through diversity of interpretation and metaphor.

Submissions must be two-dimensional and no larger than 24x30 inches. Please 
submit your contact information and up to three jpeg images along with a brief 
three to five sentence explanation of how your work relates to the theme. The 
submission deadline is December 10th and artists will be notified no later than 
December 20th regarding which of their works have been selected for the show. 
Exhibition ready work will need to be delivered to the lower level of the VPL, 
Central Branch on January 3rd between 4 and 6pm and picked up again on Jan 
31, 2012 between 10 and 12pm. 

To submit or for more information please contact Laura Breukelman at 
laurabreukelman@gmail.com or Shari-Anne Vis at sharianne.vis@gmail.com.

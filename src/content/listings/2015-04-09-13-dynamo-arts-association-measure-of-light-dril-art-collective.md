Measure of Light
DRIL Art Collective

Opening reception on Saturday, April 11, 7-11 pm
April 11th - 19th, 2015

Dynamo Arts Association
Suite 103 - 30 East 6th Ave, Vancouver

Dynamo is pleased to present Measure of Light, a new photo-based exhibit by
the art collective DRIL. Resulting from their documentation of a vacant
post-war house, this project captures moments of transition, the passing of
time and traces of human activity.

Using extreme lighting to create dramatic compositions, DRIL considers Late
Renaissance and Baroque paintings for their relationship between light and
darkness. These paintings, noted for their dramatic scenes set in
chiaroscuro, hold connotations of the divine and mortality, and allude to
experiences beyond the physical world. Employing chiaroscuro as a stylistic
trope to inform domestic subject matter, DRIL mythologizes and dramatizes
the metaphysical aspects of this home.

Merging photography with the formal and allegorical elements of
chiaroscuro, Measure of Light explores ideas of change and absence,
bringing awareness to our latent relationship to the unknown.

Artist Statement:

DRIL constructs installation-based artwork and social events that combine a
broad range of disciplines and media, especially photography, video and
sculpture. DRIL gravitates towards these mediums to use and disrupt the
language of the spectacle and to immerse the viewer in a surrogate environment.

Nov 23 & 30

Podcasting: Finding the Story workshop
presented by VIVO Media Arts Centre, Media Democracy Day 2017, and CiTR 101.9FM

Learn radio reporting and audio storytelling in this podcasting primer with producer-
journalists Madeline Taylor & Alex de Boer of UBC radio CiTR 101.9 FM!

Sign up and more info here: https://podcasting-the-story.eventbrite.ca

2 sessions, 6 hours total

Session 1: Thursday, Nov 23, 6pm-9pm
Session 2: Thursday, Nov 30, 6pm-9pm

$56.25, or $45 with VIVO Extended Producer Membership (+GST)

An assortment of snacks will be provided

This workshop will enable you to start your own podcast series by introducing the 
fundamentals of writing, interviewing, listening, recording, and editing for radio story 
production. Through hands on exercises, each student will work toward creating the first 
episode of their new podcast series.

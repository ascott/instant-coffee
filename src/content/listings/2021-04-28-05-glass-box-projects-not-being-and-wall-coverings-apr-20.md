Poya Razi, exhibition at Glass Box Projects
April 20th to May 31st
book your visit via https://www.setarehyasan.net/event-details/pooys-razis-exhibition

Glass Box Projects is pleased to present Not Being and WALL COVERINGS a short
film and a series of paintings by Tehran based artist Pooya Razi.

Pooya Razi (b. 1984) holds a BA in Painting from the Art University of Tehran. Razi's
paintings have been exhibited in Tehran, Paris, New York, and Dubai. He started
making movies in 2012 and his first animated short film was nominated for the Grand
Prize at Oberhausen International Short Film Festival. Razi's movies have been
officially selected for some major international short film festivals. in particular,
Clermont-Ferrand Short Film Festival 2015, Festival du nouveau cinma, Montreal,
Canada. Glasgow Film Festival, Glasgow, Scotland, 2015. He also won the award for
the best experimental film from the 35th Tehran
International Short Film Festival and
the 7th Khorshid film festival.

The works presented at Glass Box Projects by Razi focuses on the subject of
immigration in hopes of a better life. The first time presented in the gallery space,
"WALL COVERINGS" highlights a very specific time in Iranian political history. In 2009,
after the presidential election and the mass protests in Iran, many young Iranians
decided to leave their country. In the eyes of a great number of Iranians, the result of
presidential election manifested the loss of hope. In "Not Being," Razi is looking at the
same subject through the lens of camera. In recent years, sociopolitical circumstances
have increased the rate of emigration, making separation a routine in daily lives. This
film attempts to examine the experience from the viewpoint of the person who stays
behind. Being perceptive of life around him, Razis work magnifies the intricate
everyday events that have been normalized in contemporary Iranian society.
"Not
Being" has screened at Festival du nouveau cinma, Montreal, Canada in 2019.

The Glass Box is an alternative presentation space for national and international
artists. Located in a residence on the unceded territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam),
Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), St:l and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh nations, Glass Box project(s)
presents experimental artworks with the aim of generating new ideas and
conversations.

Capulet Art Gallery is thrilled to announce its upcoming exhibition, "Roots & Routes:
Journeys of Resilience and Identity," set to open in May at our esteemed space in
Richmond, BC. This unique exhibition brings to the forefront the nuanced narratives
of heritage, migration, and the evolving identities of underrepresented
artists, with a
special focus on Asian heritage in alignment with the vibrant celebrations in May.

"Roots & Routes" delves into the rich tapestry of personal and communal histories,
exploring the origins from which we hail and the diverse life paths we undertake. It is
a celebration of the resilience in the face of adversity, the adaptation to new cultures,
and the continuous shaping of our identities through these intricate journeys.

Join us at Capulet Art Gallery this May for "Roots & Routes: Journeys of Resilience
and Identity." Experience the profound narratives of artists who navigate the complex
interplay of tradition and transformation, and celebrate the enduring spirit of
resilience that defines our shared human experience.

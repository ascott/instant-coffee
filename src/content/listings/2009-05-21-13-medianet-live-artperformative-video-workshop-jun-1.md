MediaNet announces The Engaged Body: Live Art/Performative Video workshop
June 21-28, 2009
Cost: $600 CDN
Registration deadline: June 1, 2009

The Engaged Body is a week-long intensive workshop focusing on performative art and 
performance video.

Participants receive mentoring while exploring the various types of performance art - for example: 
durational, interventional, task based and ritual. They also learn the difference between 
performance documentation, and performative video. Each participant will then create his or her 
own performative video work. The works are screened for the public on the last day of the 
program.
The workshop is taught by performance artist and arts educator, Judith Price.

For more information please go to the MediaNet website www.media-net.bc.ca
or contact Catlin at info@media-net.bc.ca

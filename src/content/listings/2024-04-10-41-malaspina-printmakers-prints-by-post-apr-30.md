Malaspina Printmakers Society
Call For Submissions I Prints By Post: A Miniature Print Exchange and Fundraiser

Malaspina Printmakers Society invites artists
to participate in Prints By Post: A
Miniature Print Exchange and Fundraiser. Artists are invited to donate three or more
prints that fit the following criteria:

Print Details
Number of prints: three or more
Maximum paper size: 5"x 7"
Medium: Must include at least one printmaking medium
Deadline: April 30th 2024

For more information, and to submit your prints, see our website at:
https://www.malaspinaprintmakers.com/prints-by-post/

How does this print exchange and fundraiser work?

The Print Exchange: Participating artists will receive one miniature print from the
collection in thanks for their donation. The prints will be mailed to participating artists
in June 2024. The prints will be randomly chosen based on a numbered draw.

The Fundraiser: The remaining donated prints will be sold to raise funds to support
Malaspina's programming. There will be an opening reception on June 20th at 1555
Duranleau Street. The funds raised will help us continue offering discounted rates to
low-income artists over the next year.

Email Val Loewen at val@malaspinaprintmakers.com with any questions.

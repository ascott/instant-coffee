Family Day | Block Printing & Pattern
Saturday, February 23, 12-3pm, free
Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

On the last Saturday of each month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition 
tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Inspired by Anne Lows explorations of surface, pattern and material, create your own 
repeat pattern using block printing methods and found materials.

No reservation required, simply just turn up!

Find out more about Anne Lows exhibition at cag.contemporaryartgallery.ca/annelow

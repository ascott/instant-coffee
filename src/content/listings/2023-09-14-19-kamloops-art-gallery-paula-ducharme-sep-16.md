Paula Ducharme

Tob Tob Kin

September 16 to December 30, 2023

The Cube, Kamloops Art Gallery

Thompson Rivers University Fine Arts graduate Paula Ducharme explores her
Indigenous ancestry through mixed media artwork inspired by Lakota stories.

The Kamloops Art Gallery presents Tob Tob Kin, a new body of work by Paula
Ducharme, a recent Thompson Rivers University Bachelor of Fine Arts graduate .
Ducharmes abstract wall hangings are inspired by her mixed cultural heritage as a
Birdtail Sioux First Nations and settler artist. This new work draws upon Lakota
stories and natural materials sourced from Lakota territory, Ducharmes homelands
in Manitoba. Leather, feathers, fur, and traditional beadwork techniques come
together in wall hangings that tell the Lakota story of creation and its ongoing
evolution. Admission to view Tob Tob Kin in The Cube is free for all visitors.

Tob Tob Kin is curated by Craig Willms.

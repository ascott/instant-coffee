Skin, inscribed
Tuesday, June 3 at 8pm
XINEMA Fieldhouse (5050 Wales St.)

PRESALE TICKETS: eventbrite.com/e/skin-inscribed-tickets-1374899444249

Please join us on Tuesday, June 3 for Skin, inscribed, an outdoor film screening and
gathering at XINEMAs new Fieldhouse in Norquay Park. As a painter applies
pigment to a canvas, a filmmaker applies light and matter to a film strip. This
program, hand-picked by Tetsuya Maruyama, features works produced at artist-run
film labs scattered throughout the vast and diverse geopolitical territories of Brazil.
Through the relation of physical sensation to the ecological and the cultural, film-skin
is profaned as a tangible material that becomes ephemeral when passed through a
projector. This group of works emerge from the personal interests of each artist
without expecting anything in return.

We are excited to present experimental works on 16mm from Francisco B. Gusso,
Moira Lacowicz, Helder Martinovsky, Tetsuya Maruyama, Maria Mion, Joo
Reynaldo, Duo Strangloscope, L-gia M. Teixeira, and -"and to have filmmaker and
guest curator Tetsuya Maruyama join us in-person for a post-screening Q&A.

Tickets are $15 suggested donation, NOTAFLOF, (no one turned away for lack of
funds). Doors at 7:30pm, screening at 8pm.

XINEMA is an ongoing series that showcases emerging and established filmmakers
within and beyond "Vancouver", BC to strengthen and preserve the experimental film
scene. xinema.ca

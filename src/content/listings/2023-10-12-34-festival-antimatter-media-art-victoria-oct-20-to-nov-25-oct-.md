A Powerhouse of International Media Art & Experimental Cinema

Antimatter [media art] | Oct 20 to Nov 25, 2023

Screenings Installations Exhibition
Various Locations in Victoria, BC & Online

antimatter.ca

While Antimatter [media art] is a powerhouse of experimental moving image work, its
programming remains personal, intimate and thoughtful. The 26th annual festival
starting this October 20 will present 111 films in-person and online, as well as free
public installations and a special group exhibition through
November featuring artists
moving image work. Hailing from 30 countries, the majority of festival offerings are
world, North American or Canadian premieres.

Weve never supported the false dichotomy between experimental film and visual
art, says Antimatter Curator Deborah de Boer. Deluge Contemporary Art, the venue
for Antimatter screenings, has a track record of organizing international exhibitions of
artists working within and beyond the medium, one of the few galleries in North
America with this particular focus. Media installation is an interesting approach to
exhibition making, with organizers reliant on hardware and modifications of space to
provide options for presentation, while digital files can be shared around the world in
seconds. The festival has a unique place in the ecology of media art. Often the films
shown at Antimatter are world or Canadian premieres and as a result they find
homes with North American distributors. Beyond the festival circuit, many of these
artists
want their work to be seen in the larger context of galleries, institutions,
biennales and art fairs and Antimatter helps facilitate this. Participating artists often
teach, program screenings and organize exhibitions, so connections made via the
festival pave the way for future collaborations."

The past three years affected travel and engagement events around Antimatter,"
says festival director Todd Eacrett. "While this spurred some creative virtual
solutions, artists and audiences are still eager for in-person experiences and
connections. Were excited about the return of filmmaker attendees and in-person
audiences while maintaining global connectivity through online programming. The
number of participating artists planning to attend this year"from the region, across
Canada or internationally"is gratifying and as always promises a wealth of
exchange and interaction.

Special feature-length presentations at Antimatter 2023 include Hello Dankness, by
Australian collage-film legends
Soda Jerk, using remixing and reappropriation to
ponder the end of consensus reality"the idea that dramatic events of recent years
have not just changed the course of human history but destroyed general agreement
about what is real and what is not. Jim Finns The Apocalyptic is the Mother of All
Christian Theology is a psychedelic portrait of the founding theorist of Christianity.
The story of Paul the Apostles life, ideology and influence is told by through 16mm
and cassette propaganda, board games, animation, reenactments, doom metal and
covers of Catholic liturgical music. back home follows Vancouver filmmaker Nisha
Platzers pursuit to know her older brother 20 years after he took his own life.
Through intimate recollections re-imagined on Super 8 and 16mm film, back home
floats between past and present in a fragmented meditation on identity, grief and
loss.

Public installations by Zhuoyun (Yun) Chen (China), Lakshya Upadhyay (India),
Nicole Rayburn (Canada) and Dina Yanni
(Austria) will be on view at Legacy Gallery,
Ministry of Casual Living in Odeon Alley and the transom window and entry foyer of
Deluge Contemporary Art.

In a new initiative extending the length and scope of the festival through November,
Deluge presents a group exhibition of international artists moving image work from
November 1 to 25. Territorial Imperative explores issues around ecology,
colonialism, commodified landscapes and interspecies communication with media
installations by Sara Ballard, Yusuf Demirors, Big Top Collective, Kimberly Forero-
Arnas, Janelle VanderKelen and Sebastian Wiedemann.

Advance tickets for screenings can be purchased on the Antimatter website.
Installations and online programs are free or by donation. The complete schedule

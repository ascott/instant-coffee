CALL / City of Surrey Public Art Program / Creative Wall and Gate Design for Chimney
Heights Park Washroom

Call for Concept Proposals for Creative Wall and Gate Design for Chimney Heights Park
Washroom

- Download all details at https://www.surrey.ca/culture-recreation/30780.aspx

- Deadline to Apply: Monday, March 30, 2020 at 4pm

- Compensation: $2,500/panel

- Eligibility: Open to all artists currently residing in BC

Concept proposals are requested for the City of Surreys Creative Wall and Gate Design
for Chimney Heights Park Washroom. This call is open to all visual artists and designers
currently living in British Columbia. Students of fine art, design, and architecture schools
are encouraged to apply. You can propose up to three different designs. Public art staff
have developed an easy application process and will supply technical support and
guidance throughout the project. Click below to download your
proposal application.
Please complete and submit to publicart@surrey.ca by Monday, March 30, 2020 at 4pm.
Dont miss this exciting opportunity to share your art with the community in Surrey!

Western Front
303 8 AVE E, Vancouver, BC

SEPT 19 @ 7:30 pm
Roving Sound Performance: Elisa Ferrari
lilithlithlithlithlithlith: through spiral upon spiral of the shell
with: Gabi Dao, Stacey Ho, Michelle Helene Mackenzie
Doors at 7.30pm / Performance starts at dusk

Elisa Ferrari has been deep listening to sounds we do not hear. Transmissions of 
electromagnetic frequency form the basis of her roving experimental sound performance. 
For one evening she will make audible the often overlooked transmissions that connect civic 
structure in the arrangements of land on which we live. With sci-fi pageantry, her 
collaborators broaden these sonic immersions into a responsive loop. More on the project 
here: www.lilithlithlithlithlith.com/

Elisa Ferrari works with text, image, and sound. To consider acts and implications of 
retrieval, she produces projects that manifest as installations, sound walks, artist books, and 
performance. 

Accessibility:

Please note that this event will take place either wholly or partially in the Luxe Hall, which is 
up a double flight of stairs. If anyone with limited mobility would like to attend, please contact 
Western Front Media Arts at media@front.bc.ca and we will offer individual mobility options 
based on our available resources.

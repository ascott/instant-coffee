Submission Deadline: JANUARY 21, 2012
Launch: APRIL 21, 2012

IN MY BED Magazine Submission Guidelines:
If you are an emerging or established writer, artist or photographer... 
there is always room for you IN MY BED.
Please adhere to our little guide if you want to submit and surrender 
your original and independent creations…

* Poetry: up to 3 poems (in Word format)
* Short fiction, prose, or excerpts from a novel: no more than 5 
pages or 1,250 words (in Word format)
* Paintings/illustrations/photography: no more than 5 images (we 
prefer to get image files in 300dpi) Please also provide a short artistic 
statement…how your work relates to the theme as well what 
technique/application you used.
* Bio: tell us a little bit about yourself in 75-150 words. 

NOTE:
We also welcome a link to your website (if you have one).
You must indicate in your submission the theme and issue.
We will only review submissions for the themes listed above.

We want you to submit by email (JPEG, PDF or Word format) to:
editor@inmybedmagazine.com

For further guidelines or information, just send an email to:
editor@inmybedmagazine.com

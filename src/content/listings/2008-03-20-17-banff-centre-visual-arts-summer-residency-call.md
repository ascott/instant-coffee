The Banff Centre – Visual Arts Summer Residency 
Call for Applications 

Figure in a Mountain Landscape with Silke Otto-Knapp and Jan Verwoert 

Program dates: July 7 - August 22, 2008 

Application deadline: April 11, 2008 

The Banff Centre's Figure in a Mountain Landscape Residency offers artists the opportunity to work with London-based painter Silke Otto-Knapp while exploring the potential of the plein air approach. 

Otto-Knapp will lead residency participants into the landscape around Banff National Park. On arrival at various scenic locations the group will ready themselves, like generations of artists before them and begin to make art. 

During these expeditions, Otto-Knapp will propose a move away from a purely experiential approach to a conceptual one. Together the group will explore methods and media ranging from watercolour, to performance and text-based practices. 

Financial assistance is available.

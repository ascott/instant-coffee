More About Digital Carnival
Since its inception in 2012, Digital Carnival has been a platform for promoting public 
awareness and appreciation of media arts, supporting the development of BCs 
emerging media artists, fostering diversity within BCs media arts community, and 
creating opportunities for creative dialogue and exchange within the media arts 
community in BC and beyond. 

One of the largest community-engaged media arts festivals in the Lower Mainland, 
Digital Carnival showcases work from a wide variety of digital media including but not 
limited to, film and video, video mapping, integrated media installations, interactive art, 
generative art, music and sound art, software and computer gaming, dance and 
performance art, gif and meme art, and community-engaged art. Through this annual 
event, we seek to nurture the convergence of media art with public art through 
accessible programming (e.g. offering free programs in accessible, public spaces, 
creating multilingual marketing collateral, and providing multilingual tour guides to assist 
the public in navigating the art work), as well as partnerships with art collectives, artist 
run centres, and educational institutions.

About Cinevolution
Founded in 2007, Cinevolution is a grassroots, women- and migrant-driven non-profit 
arts organization based in Richmond BC. Cinevolution is committed to fostering 
intercultural dialogue and critical engagement through film and media arts. For a 
decade, Cinevolution has been committed to bringing cutting edge media art into 
marginalized communities. Sitting at the nexus of art, academia and community, 
Cinevolution seeks to decentralize art while connecting people across cultural, political, 
geographic and linguistic boundaries in order to foster media literacy, stimulate critical 
discourse, and cultivate creativity.

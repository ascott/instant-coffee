FAADE FESTIVAL 2019

Dates: September 8 - September 14, 2019

Time: Sunset - Midnight each evening
Location: x"nq Xwtle7nk Square, Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby St, Vancouver, BC

Faade Festival 2019 is a week-long public art project and cultural event taking place from
September 8th to September 14th, 2019 on the Georgia Street faade of the Vancouver Art
Gallery, above the newly constructed x"nq Xwtle7nk Square in the heart of downtown
Vancouver.

A selection of BC contemporary artists, both established and early-career, have been
commissioned to create ten stunning, site-specific works of art. Using the technology known
as projection mapping, the works have been tailored specifically for the Vancouver Art
Gallerys iconic architecture, and will be projected over its Georgia Street faade, covering
the building with moving, dynamic artwork. Faade Festival is an immersive, large-scale
artistic experience that will be shared by tens of thousands of attendees.

Faade Festival 2019 presents ten new and strikingly innovative site-specific artworks. With
its biggest lineup yet, Faade Festival 2019 showcases artists Dana Claxton, Sandeep
Johal, Natalie Purschwitz, Howie Tsui, Bracken Hanuse Corlett, Justine Chambers, Josh
Hite, Hyung-Min Yoon, Drew Young, Khan Lee, and Lindsay McIntyre.

Projections will take place from sunset to midnight between September 8th and September
14th for thousands of viewers free of charge. Works by two different artists will be premiered
each night from September 8th to 12th, while on September 13th and 14th, the public will
have the chance to discover all of the featured artists works in encore screenings. A free
artist panel discussion will also be held at 3pm on September 14 at the Vancouver.

In addition to the final encore evening of the festival will feature a special projection mapping
experience from Go2Productions. Titled 'Ocean Ghosts', which deals with plastic pollution
and its devastating effects on marine wildlife. Playing with the buildings structural outline,
the work will push the limits of digital art, technology and soundscape.

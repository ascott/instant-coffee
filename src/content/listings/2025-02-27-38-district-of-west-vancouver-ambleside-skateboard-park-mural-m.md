The District of West Vancouver invites artists from the Lower Mainland and Sea to Sky
Corridor to submit a proposal for a painted mural at the
Ambleside Skateboard Park in
West Vancouver. The District is seeking designs that reflect the culture of the
Skateboard Park and its beautiful natural setting in Vancouver's North Shore. Artist fee:
Approx. $7,500.00.

Please read full application before applying: Community Murals Program | District of
West Vancouver
Send to application to arts@westvancouver.ca

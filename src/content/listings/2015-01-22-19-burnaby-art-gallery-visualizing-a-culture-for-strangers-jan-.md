Visualizing a Culture for Strangers
Burnaby Art Gallery
opening reception January 29, from 7-9pm

BURNABY, BC – The Burnaby Art Gallery is pleased to present a new
exhibition on tour from the Art Gallery of Greater Victoria. Join us on
Thursday, January 29, from 7-9pm for the opening reception of Visualizing a
Culture for Strangers: Chinese Export Paintings of the Nineteenth Century.
The evening will feature tea tasting, Gong Fu Cha tea demonstration by
Adrian Sinclair and tea leaf readings by artist Cindy Mochizuki.

Duncan Campbell, Bernadette - Screenings and Reception 
 Western Front
 303 E 8th Avenue, Vancouver
 Reception: Thursday, June 26, 7-10pm

 Screenings: Friday, June 27 - Sunday, June 29, 12 -5pm

 The Contemporary Art Gallery, Western Front and DIM cinema presents a 
 weekend-long screening in the Grand Luxe Hall of Duncan Campbell’s 
 film Bernadette, as part of the exhibition The Act of Seeing with One's 
 Own Eyes. Bernadette presents an open and indirect account of Irish 
 dissident and political activist Bernadette Devlin, constructed out of 
 archival footage from the 1960s and early 1970s. Campbell approaches 
 documentary as form of fiction, revealing the complex relationship 
 between author, subject and audience. The Act of Seeing with One’s Own 
 Eyes is a group exhibition of recent film and video that seeks to 
 interrogate notions of uncertainty within the documentary format.

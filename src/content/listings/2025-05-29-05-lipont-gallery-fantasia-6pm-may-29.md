Lipont Gallery presents " FANTASIA " Paintings by Maggie Dong and Grace Tao

Opening Reception: May 29, 2025, 6-8pm
[https://openingfantasia.eventbrite.ca](https://openingfantasia.eventbrite.ca)

Coffee Talk and Meeting the Artists: June 11, 2025 11am-12pm
[https://meetfantasia.eventbrite.ca](https://meetfantasia.eventbrite.ca)

Exhibition: May 29 - June 19, 2025 by appointment at www.lipontgallery.ca

Richmond, BC - Lipont Gallery is excited to present FANTASIA, a two-person
exhibition featuring the works of North Vancouver-based artist Maggie Dong and
Surrey-based artist Grace Tao. Both artists explore themes of nature, humanity, and
the emotional depth of life through their individual practices, showcasing their work in
this collaborative exhibition.

With over a decade of experience in painting, both Dong and Tao have established
themselves in Vancouver's art community. Their journey as artists mirrors their
experiences as immigrants, constantly evolving in response to both their personal
lives and the surrounding environment. FANTASIA reflects their ongoing exploration
of art, nature, and lifes philosophical inquiries. Despite their different backgrounds
and artistic practices, both artists convey a deep connection to the world around
them through their respective mediums.

Maggie Dongs work is defined by delicate emotions, vibrant colours, and precise
brushwork. Her People and Pets series, including her thought-provoking painting Is a
Cat a Pet?, invites viewers to reflect on the philosophical questions of joy, human
connection, and self-awareness. Through these paintings, Dong examines the
intricate relationships between people, pets, and the natural world, transforming
everyday moments into profound narratives.

Grace Tao brings a unique perspective to the exhibition, combining her background
in painting, design, and business. Her work bridges abstract oil painting with imagery
that juxtaposes machinery and nature, exploring the intersections of design, natural
laws, and human ingenuity. Taos works, which include captivating colour fields and
representations of natures laws intertwined with human-made structures, reveal her
fascination with the mechanics of life and the inherent beauty of both.

Like the unpredictable and spontaneous nature of a fantasia"a free-form melody"
FANTASIA presents a harmonious blend of both artists interpretations of the
Canadian landscape and their reflections on life and humanity. Their works echo
each others exploration of nature, culture, and philosophy, culminating in an
exhibition that captures the fluidity and complexity of lifes emotional spectrum.

The exhibition will be on view at Lipont Gallery from May 29 to June 19, 2025, with a
public opening reception on Thursday May 29, from 6"8 PM. A Coffee Talk and Artist
Meet & Greet event will take place on Wednesday, June 11, from 11 AM to 12 PM,
offering visitors an opportunity to meet the artists and delve into their creative
processes.

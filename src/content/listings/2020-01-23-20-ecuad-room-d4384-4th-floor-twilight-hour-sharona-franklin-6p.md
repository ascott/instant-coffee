Please join us for our second Twilight Hour talk of the spring 2020 series with Vancouver artist
Sharona Franklin on Tuesday, January 28th at 6:00 pm in Painting and Drawing room D4384
(4th floor) at Emily Carr University of Art + Design.

Sharona Franklin b.1987 is a Vancouver-based multidisciplinary artist, writer, and activist.
Franklins diverse practice, including but limited to sculptures, edibles, and books, examine the
bio-ethic and social responsibility of treating, educating, and living with disabilities. Much of her
practice is disseminated and documented through social media platforms that have
reached
thousands online, which include @paid.technologies, @star_seeded, @hot.crip, and
@disabled_personals. Through these varied platforms and material interventions, Franklins
work disseminates their personal mythology of disability, gender, class, bio-citizenship, and
botany. Recent exhibitions include those at Unit 17, Vancouver, Canada, G44 Center for
Contemporary Photography, Toronto, Canada; New Image Art Gallery Los Angeles, CA, and
Flux Factory, New York, NY. Recent publications include Injustice in Biopharm, 2019,
Cassandra Press and Rental Bod, 2016, Peace Library Publications.

All talks are open to the public. Seating is limited - please arrive early to reserve your spot!

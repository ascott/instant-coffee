Project Space is proud to announce TEN, a fundraiser celebrating the
organization's 10-year anniversary on Saturday, July 9 at VIVO Media Arts
Centre (2625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver) that will include a night market
featuring local artists' book and zine publishers and artisans, live music,
comedy and more.
To help us ensure this event is a success Project Space is looking for
volunteers. If Volunteering and being a part of our community is of
interest please email sarah@vancouverartbookfair.com.

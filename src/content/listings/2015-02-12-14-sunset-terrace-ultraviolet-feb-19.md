Sunset Terrace presents:

Ultraviolet

A new installation by Andrew Dadson.
Opening reception: February 19, 6 - 10 pm

Andrew Dadson (b. 1980) graduated from Emily Carr Institute of Art + Design
in 2003 and currently lives and works in Vancouver. His first solo
exhibition was at the Helen Pitt Gallery, Vancouver in the year of his
graduation and he has since exhibited in solo and group exhibitions
internationally. Dadson is represented by Galleria Franco Noero, Turin,
David Kordansky Gallery, Los Angles, and RaeberVonStenglin, Zurich.

February 19 - March 12
Sunset Terrace
2028 Clark Drive

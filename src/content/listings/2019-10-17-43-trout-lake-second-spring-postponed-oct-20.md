Updates on our October 20th event:

Please note, that due to circumstances including weather, our October 20th event this
coming Sunday is being postponed. Julian Hou will be continuing Second Spring as a
portable site of performances, exhibitions, workshops, and
collaborations.

For any inquiries, please contact Events & Exhibitions Curator Sungpil Yoon at
events@vivomediaarts.com
To join the mailing list for Julian Hou's future collaborations pertaining to Second Spring
please email amphibianslurps@gmail.com.

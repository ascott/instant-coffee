ANNOUNCEMENT OF A NEW URBAN VIDEO SCREEN IN MOUNT PLEASANT

Vancouver B.C. " grunt gallery has been given the reigns to program content on a new 
non-commercial urban media screen over a five year period in Mount Pleasant. The 
urban screen is being constructed on the second floor of the RIZE building on the 
western side of Kingsway Avenue facing the street. The Mount Pleasant Urban Screen 
will provide a wide range of artwork representing different parts of the Mount Pleasant 
community. Through videos, photography, and digital artworks grunt gallery will present 
exciting and dynamic programming from and for the community. 

grunt will also be actively pushing to develop interactive capability components for the 
public screen that will give artists the opportunity to work with interactive features that 
can directly engage the viewers through multi-modal digital platform components 
alongside curated content. Were happy to announce that CREATIVE BC " 
INTERACTIVE FUND is funding the interactive features on the Screen including an 
external camera, streaming audio and SMS capabilities!

Glenn Alteen, grunt gallerys Program Director, emphasizes the importance of reflecting 
the neighborhood " grunt gallery is very proud to present this new community initiative 
that will act as a community focal point featuring artwork and archival photos from and 
about the neighborhood of Mount Pleasant. 

Location: The urban video screen is approximately 13' feet (396.24 cm) x 22' feet 
(670.56 cm) and is located at the intersection of Broadway and Kingsway, Vancouver, 
BC 

Public Launch: July 2018 

Website: grunt.ca

grunts first program, PLACE, uses material from a number of community archives as 
well as work from and about the neighborhood of Mount Pleasant and surrounding 
areas. 

SIGN UP FOR FREE URBAN SCREEN DIGITAL STORYTELLING WORKSHOPS
Become a part of the Urban Screen project. This spring, grunt gallery and EastVan

want to create short videos. To sign up for a workshop, send an email to Lorna 
Boschman at lorna@digitalstories.ca. Up to four people can register per weekly 
workshop. In the email, please include your name, email address, phone/text number,

An installation by Suzy King.

Aug 26 7-11pm + Aug 27 3-7pm.

Duplex Art Society Gallery Space " 1889 Franklin " alley entrance.

Admission: Free

Cash Bar + snacks

INNER THREAD explores the cycle of things coming together and falling apart.
Creating a sensory space through soft sculpture installation, video and sound, Suzy
draws from struggles and growth over the past few years, and shares a space to
reflect on comfort and discomfort, destruction and healing, mending and unraveling.

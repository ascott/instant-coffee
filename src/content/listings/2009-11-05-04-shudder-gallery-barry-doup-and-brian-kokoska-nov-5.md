The Hand of a Stranger
Barry Doupé and Brian Kokoska
curated by Denver Lynxleg
Opening Reception Thursday November 5, 7-11pm

6 - 28 November

Shudder is pleased to present The Hand of a Stranger, an exhibition of
paintings and works on
paper by Brian Kokoska and an animation by Barry Doupé. Both artists affirm
peculiar renderings
of utopian vision. Kokoska depicts an extravagant world of power, group
oriented play and bodily
sharing. Doupé reveals an obscure world of sensual vibration and botanical
invention. Together,
these works image the queer body through seductive forms and immersive
fictions.

Brian Kokoska is presently studying towards his BFA from Emily Carr
University of Art + Design
(2010) in Vancouver. His practice is concerned with, but not limited to
painting, drawing, digital
and ephemeral works. He has been featured locally in Derya Akay�s self
published
Fundamentalist Zine (2009) and internationally in Kraków based Piana
Magazine (2009).
Barry Doupé (b. Victoria, BC) is a filmmaker living in Vancouver. He holds
a Bachelor of Media
Arts Degree from Emily Carr Institute of Art + Design. He is also a member
of The Lions
collaborative drawing group (www.lionspile.ca).

His films have been screened throughout Canada and internationally
including the Ann Arbor
Film Festival, Anthology Film Archives, Lyon Contemporary Art Museum,
Pleasure Dome and the
Tate Modern.

The Dance Centre presents
Belgium's Compagnie Thor | Thierry Smits in ReVoLt
A radical solo of rebellion
Thursday-Saturday May 5-7, 2016 at 8pm
Post-show talkback May 6
Scotiabank Dance Centre, 677 Davie Street, Vancouver

ReVoLt is a new work from the internationally-acclaimed Belgian
choreographer Thierry Smits. A stripped-down, intense solo created for
the explosive young Australian dancer Nicola Leahey, the piece is a
metaphor for the oppression of the female body, and an articulation of
the universal urge to resist constraint, to rebel. Leahey's fierce physical
and emotional commitment powers a tightly-wound essay on the
necessity of revolt, the breach to be found, and the freedom to be won.

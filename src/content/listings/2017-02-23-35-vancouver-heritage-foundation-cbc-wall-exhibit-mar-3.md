Artists are being invited to submit a proposal to the a yearly WALL exhibit at the CBC 
building in downtown Vancouver.

Here's the link to the official call:

[http://www.vancouverheritagefoundation.org/projects/the-wall/](http://www.vancouverheritagefoundation.org/projects/the-wall/)

The deadline to submit ideas for a 2D public artwork is March 3, 2017. The 
compensation to the artist is $5,000. As a theme, artists are encouraged to consider the 
legacy of history in the built environment and our experience of it. This may include 
themes and narratives around the history of indigenous communities in Vancouver's 
unceded territories. This is an open call; we are hoping to reach students and 
professionals alike from various backgrounds.

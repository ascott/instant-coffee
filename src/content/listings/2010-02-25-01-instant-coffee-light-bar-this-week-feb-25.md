Schedule of Events:

Thursday, February 25: JOEY DUBUC and ADAM GANDY will present an
uninterrupted timeline of masculinity from the Baroque to the Shackleton
exhibition, in the form of an historical narrative. The presentation will
take the shape of an intellectual debate with key historical speaking
points; un-moderated, un-scripted, un-speakable and un-repeatable. Dubuc's
research into the phenomenon of the frozen explorer will be umbilically fed
through Gandy's research into the Baroque period, leading to an exploration
into the unfiltered masculinity that motivated and financed both events.
The presentation, with accompanying visual bolsters, promises to be
informative, illuminating and ingratiating, with something for everyone
curious about what drives men to risk life and limb in pursuit of the
unachievable, 8:30pm and THE REVOLUCIONARY CYBORG WEDDING BAND MOBILE UNIT
will make a visit from Victoria, under cover of darkness, to a haven of
bright health and social joy. The Vancouver Island duo are described
variously as social popsters, hip-hopsters, electronic folksters, or World
Music...from PEOPLE'S ISLAND REPUBLIC! That's what Vancouver Island is to
be called after the REVOLUTION is over in 2019. These guys have visited the
future and are armed with a future history, recounted in performance, which
makes People's Island Republic an interesting postmodern place to be. And
it rocks! 10:30pm. House dj: Peter Gazendam.

Friday, February 26: GENTLE COUSINS are JULIAN CARNRITE of Christine and
LUCAS GRANITO of Dafluke both recording artists on label MoodGadget.
Divergent backgrounds unite, providing danceable soundscapes of minimal
house to lush synth pop. Prepare to meet Xenu. Guest bartender: M making
special nafta drinks.

Saturday, February 27: Karaoke and Authenticity: Seattle curator ERIC

[http://www.weekendleisure.ca/stuff.html](http://www.weekendleisure.ca/stuff.html). Lecture at 9pm. Guest bartender:

The artist collective Instant Coffee presents Light Bar, a full-spectrum
light bar installation and venue for light therapy, light lectures, light
shows, light reading and light rock. Instant Coffee Light Bar is an ongoing
art project that investigates the potential and power of light. The artists
come to this idea through necessity. Living in a place such as Vancouver,
Canada, where the rain can seem endless, the negative effects of the lack
of sunlight on individual psyches and the large social milieu feels at
times paramount. The use of light as a therapy or as a remedy for SAD
(Seasonal Affective Disorder) is commonly and affectively used by
individuals, but for Light Bar Instant Coffee puts it to the test in small
or large group situations.

As part of Bright Light a collaborative project that brings together
fourteen arts organizations from Vancouver's Downtown Eastside. Bright
Light is commissioned by the City of Vancouver through its Olympic and
Paralympic Public Art Program, www.bright-light.ca

Get creative this Spring Break with North Van Arts!

North Van Arts is proud to once again offer half and full-day spring break art camps 
for young people ages 6-11 at Maplewood House and Maplewood Farm in North 
Vancouver. Our camps are designed with working families in mind, featuring longer 
camps to accommodate parents across the North Shore and Metro Vancouver.

Our multi-disciplinary art camps are facilitated by the communitys leading 
professional artists. Delve into the fantastical world of creature and character design 
with animator Rachel Brett, or take inspiration from North Shore artists like Malcolm 
Lowry, Al Neil and Carole Itter and explore the process of art making through 
painting, drawing and mixed media with instructor Cameron McLellan.

Be sure to register early for a returning favourite, Art on the Farm, the camp which 
offers a unique behind-the-scenes experience of Maplewood Farm. Young artists 
learn from the farmers each day, and afterwards respond through creative art 
projects, processes and materials.

2019 Spring Break Art Camp Schedule:

Week 1: March 18-22 | Art on the Farm & Patterning Nature
8:30am-12:30pm - Art on the Farm with artist Michelle Weinstein and the 
Maplewood Farmers
1:00pm-4:30pm - Patterning Nature with Michelle Weinstein

Week 2: March 25-29 | Fantasy Creature Design & Flats, Paths and Places
9:00am-12:30pm - Fantasy Creature Design with Rachel Brett
1:00pm-4:30pm - Flats, Paths and Places with Cameron McLellan

Check out our website for complete camp details!

Maplewood House is located at 399 Seymour River Place, North Vancouver, situated 
adjacent to Maplewood Farm. Full day camps include a supervised lunch break. 
Please contact North Van Arts for information regarding accessibility. Register for our 
arts education programs online (northvanarts.ca), by telephone (604-988-6844), or 
in person at the North Van Arts office (335 Lonsdale Avenue, North Vancouver). 

With gratitude as guests, North Van Arts activities take place on the unceded 
territories of the Sl"ilwta (Tsleil-Waututh), Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), and 
xmky"m (Musqueam) Nations.

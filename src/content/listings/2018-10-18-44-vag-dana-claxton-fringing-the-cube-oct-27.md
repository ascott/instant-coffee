The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Dana Claxton: Fringing the Cube

Saturday, October 27

This first career survey exhibition of Vancouver-based, Hunkpapa Lakota (Sioux) artist 
Dana Claxton looks at the artists work from the mid-1990s to now. Through Claxtons 
practice combining Lakota traditions with Western influences, she critiques the 
representations of Indigenous people in art, literature and popular culture. Known for 
using an emotive mix, meld and mash approach, the artist investigates notions of 
Indigenous identity, beauty, gender and the body, as well as broader social and political 
issues. On view October 27, 2018 to February 3, 2019.

For tickets and info, visit: vanartgallery.bc.ca

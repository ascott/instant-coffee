Touchstone Theatre Kicks Off Landmark Season With Nationally Touring Revival of
Frances Kon-an's WOMEN OF THE FUR TRADE

September 24 - October 4, 2025 (Media Opening: Thursday, Sept 25)

Learn more about Touchstone's 2025/2026 season at touchstonetheatre.com

Touchstone launches its 50th Anniversary season with the acclaimed revival of
Women of the Fur Trade by Anishinaabe/Slovene playwright Frances Kon-an,
running September 24October 4 at the Frederic Wood Theatre, UBC. Following its
2024 premiere at the National Arts Centre Indigenous Theatre (Ottawa) and Native
Earth (Toronto), where it garnered a nomination for the Dora Mavor Moore Award for
Outstanding New Play, this national production is being remounted by Touchstone
Theatre and Western Canada Theatre Company.

A rip-roaring satire of survival and cultural inheritance, Women of the Fur Trade
shifts the spotlight from the male gaze to women's power past and present against
the backdrop of the Canadian fur trade. Set in a Red River fort in eighteen hundred
and something something, three women sip tea and debate revolution, colonization,
and the sex appeal of Louis Riel. Marie-Angelique (Kelsey Kanatan Wavey), a Mtis
Taurus with a plan, is busy writing bold love letters to Riel (Jonathan Fisher),
convinced they're fated. Ojibwe Sagittarius trapper Eugenia (Columpa Bobb) brings
word of the rebellion back to the fort but isn't impressed with the overrated Louis.
Meanwhile, European settler Cecilia (Cheri Maracle), a pregnant Virgo anxiously
awaiting her husband's return, secretly pines over Thomas Scott (Victor Hunter) -
who, unbeknownst to her, is the one actually responding to Marie-Angelique's letters.
Blending sharp wit, pop culture nods, modern slang, and incisive historical
commentary, Kon-an's time-bending play hilariously flips the script on colonial, male-
centric narratives, placing three unforgettable women at the centre.

Presented in partnership with UBC and Western Canada Theatre Company,
Kon-an's storytelling power has propelled Women of the Fur Trade - from its Toronto
Fringe debut to the Stratford Festival - into one of the most widely produced plays in
Canada.

Women of the Fur Trade is directed by Renae Morriseau and stars Kelsey Kanatan
Wavey (The Rez Sisters, Royal Manitoba Theatre), Cheri Maracle (Father Tartuffe:
an Indigenous Misadventure, Arts Club Theatre), Columpa Bobb (White Noise,
Firehall Arts Centre), Victor Hunter (Oliver!, Gateway Theatre) and Jonathan Fisher
(Copper Thunderbird, National Arts Centre).

Contemporary Art Gallery – New exhibitions

Grace Schwindt, Only A Free Individual Can Create A Free Society
Krista Belle Stewart, Motion and Moment Always
Shannon Bool, The Flight of the Medici Mamluk

Opening reception: Thursday, January 22, 7-10pm

Please join us to celebrate the launch of these major new projects:

Grace Schwindt, Only A Free Individual Can Create A Free Society
January 23 to February 15, 2015
Presented with PuSh International Performing Arts Festival
A new feature length film installation by German artist Grace Schwindt
which through choreography, script, costume and staging creates a narrative
that questions how freedom was, and is, understood, who has access to it
and what political and social structures need to be in place to create a
free society.

Krista Belle Stewart, Motion and Moment Always
January 23 to February 15, 2015
This first solo exhibition in Canada for artist Krista Belle Stewart
comprises new works alongside archival photographs and objects as the
culmination of a fall 2014 residency at the Nisga’a Museum. These combine
with others produced at her ancestral home in Douglas Lake, BC as part of a
residency supported by the Western Front.

Shannon Bool, The Flight of the Medici Mamluk
January 23 to April 19, 2015
Off-site: Yaletown-Roundhouse Station, Canada Line
Presented in partnership with the Canada Line Public Art Program – IntransitBC
This ambitious new commission by Canadian artist Shannon Bool is typical of
her practice in commenting on the role of decorative arts within art
history, as well as on the change in meaning that occurs through the
replication and alteration of significant cultural forms, here presenting
an exact scale photograph of the 16th Century Egyptian Medici Mamluk
carpet, recently rediscovered stored in the Palazzo Pitti in Florence, Italy.

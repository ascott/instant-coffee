Hubbub 
Tuesday, February 21 
8pm 

Artspeak
233 Carrall Street
Vancouver

Performing a convergence of sound in space and spontaneous composition, 
Hubbub is a foray into experimental acoustic and electronic music. Christine Duncan 
(theremin/voice), DB Boyko (glass harmonica/voice) and Andreas Kahre 
(percussion/electric celllo) will perform two sets in the exhibition space of Artspeak. 
This event will be Hubbub's first performance in Vancouver. One night only. 

Please come early to ensure a piece of floor as space is limited.

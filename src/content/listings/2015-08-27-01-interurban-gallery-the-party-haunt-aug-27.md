7pm (installation) "Infinite Digressions"
Infinite Digressions includes excerpts from a new work created by
Diego Romero and Ileanna Cheladyn. These momentary installations
are variations on subconscious attempts to infiltrate and disarm our
actions and motivations; exploring addiction as it engages with success
through completion.
8pm (performances)
"The On-going Wonder of…"

Concept & direction – Daisy Thompson
Performers and collaborators – Katie DeVries, Natalie Tin Yin Gan,
Michelle Lui & Ashley Whitehead
Sound - Alex Mah

"of experience…with an activist sense…the choice within constraining
systems to act. To notice, have reflected, observe ones actions, how
one can affect the space, interact with another to generate a situation"
Brian Massumi

The On-going Wonder of…treads through a collective and individual
experience of past, present(ce) and future. Through combined scores,
the performers are questioning each moment, negotiating the before,
the now and the after. Questions such as ‘what has been lost along the
way?’, ‘What has been retained?’, ‘ what has arisen?’, ‘what am I
choosing to keep?’, ‘where am I going?’, informs the dancer of what to
do next. As the performer works through their solo journey, there is a
possibility that they meet another’s rhythm. These moments have no
pre-determined questions, but are left to what arises in the moment.

READ Books

Jo Cook
Adore-Abhor: Writing Other Peoples Words

ABRRR Workshop at READ
Saturdays, October 22 and 29, 1:00 - 4:00 pm

This two-session workshop on appropriation as text generator takes 
Kathy Acker as its muse. Participants will combine cut-ups of 
magazine interviews and use Richard Foreman's on-line notebooks 
to produce a new form of interview zine.

Participants may bring images and texts to collage but it is not 
necessary. There will be plenty of sources to choose from. All other 
necessary materials will be provided.

Workshop is presented free of charge as part of ABRRR. Space is 
limited and placement is on a first come first serve basis. Please 
email readbooks@ecuad.ca to register or for more information.

***

This October Emily Carr University Library and READ Books at the 
Charles H. Scott Gallery present Jo Cook for our 2016 Artists' 
Books Research Residency (ABRRR).

Cook is an artist publisher living on Mayne Island, BC. In 2005, she 
founded Perro Verlag Books by Artists, a press that recognizes the 
importance of unstable thoughts and impractical, possibly visionary 
intentions. Since then she's published 120 titles ranging from 
stapled photocopied zines to full colour perfect bound books.

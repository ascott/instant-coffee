Concert with UBC Contemporary Players

Wednesday, April 10 at 2 pm

Join us for Senses of Doubt, a concert by the
UBC Contemporary Players in a program
that celebrates the exhibition Aporia (Notes to a Medium). Directed by Paolo Bortolussi
and coach Joanne S. Na, this UBC School of Music graduate and undergraduate
student ensemble will breathe life into the gallery during an afternoon program.

VCC Jewellery Art & Design Student Exhibition
Vancouver Community College
250 West Pender Street; Vancouver, BC

Opening Gala: Thursday,May 24th 6pm-9pm

Exhibition: May 24-June 14

Every year, the gala opening event is a whos who of designers, business owners, 
alumni, and jewellery business professionals. The event is open to the public, 
includes a cash bar, and a number of awards are presented to top students from the 
program. Students each get a display case to showcase their final projects - 5 
pieces in the theme of their choosing. 
Exhibition continues until morning of June 15th.

Please be advised that photographs will be taken at the event for use on the VCC 
website, social media, VCC marketing materials, and other college publications. By 
entering this event, you consent to the college photographing and using your image 
and likeness.

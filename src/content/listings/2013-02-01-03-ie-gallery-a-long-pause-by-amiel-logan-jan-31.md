ie, gallery presents:

A LONG PAUSE. by Amiel Logan

JAN 25 - FEB 6 / 2013
RECEPTION / JAN 31
18H00 - 20H00

Amiel Logan presents his first body of work since entering the Master of 
Applied Arts program at Emily Carr University of Art & Design.

Amiel has primarily worked through sculptural forms, however in his graduate 
studies he has moved towards the paintbrush and is focused on creating a 
completely new body of work. The formal properties in his pieces are of 
critical significance as they are meditations of presence in each art object.

As a painter, his interests continue to address surface and the undercurrents 
of energy that flow beneath. In the vein of vein of Richard Serra, Amiel 
experiences painting as an action. Energy is distilled on the canvas, 
activating the painted surface and radiating outward. A poem:

intrinsic materials resonate
essential surfaces vibrate
paint as paint
movement without restraint
form to flow
a current draws you out

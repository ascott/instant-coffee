NATURE-TECHNOLOGY TENSIONS IN CONTEMPORARY QUEER ART

Aphrodisiac Devices is borne out of the highly (in-)fertile collaboration
between friends and comrades Jamie Ross & Felipe Rivas. Their interest in
elaborating visions of queer community based simultaneously in digital and
land-based spaces draws them to seek to foment international cuir/queer
community, without eclipsing the local particularities of each culture.

A central goal of the residency is to bring together queer artists,
activists and cultural creators from around the world and to engage with
Chilean and ValparaÃ­so LGBTQ art and political histories: to act in
mutually beneficial ways to both existing local communities as well as
visiting artists. Throughout its activities, the residency aims to remain
critical and conscious of the power dynamics inherent in travel between the
Global North and South. Strong queer creative and cultural alliances will
be designed to foment greater struggle against neo-colonial exploitation.
The multimedia residency will offer studio space according to needs,
meeting space as well as room and board to artists for the duration of the
two-week residency.

INSTAGRAM RESIDENCY
Call for proposals - deadline : May 15, 2018
Dazibao is pleased to offer a new residency opportunity:

Where: Dazibaos Instagram account (@dazibaomtl) 
When: June 21 to August 18, 2018 (8 weeks)
What: Through a take-over of the organisations Instagram account, develop a
new time-based artwork in consideration of the platforms particularities
and constraints.
We are in search of a project that:

- aligns itself with Dazibaos mandate to further artistic experimentation and
question modes of image making and dissemination within a
contemporary social and cultural context.
- critically and innovatively addresses the Instagram platform as a means
of creation and dissemination.

- commits to a minimum of one post per day for the entire duration of the
residency.

The project will be presented as part of Dazibaos 2018-2019 programming year and 
will benefit from the financial and promotional support applicable to the centres 
regular activities.

All proposals must be submitted via the following online form:
http://en.dazibao.art/call-for- instagram-residency

The selected artist or collective will be notified by May 17, 2018.
* Given the virtual nature of the residency, artists living abroad will not be required to
travel to Montreal for the residency.

AMANDA REEVES 

NEW PAINTINGS
JUNE 1 - 29, 2013

Reception for the Artist: Saturday June 8, 2 - 4 pm

We are pleased to present an exhibition of paintings by San Francisco-based 
artist Amanda Reeves. This will be her first solo exhibition in Vancouver.

Amanda Reeves’ colourful yet subtly nuanced abstract paintings challenge 
the viewer to engage in the contemplation of perception. The desire to secure 
the paintings in focus is thwarted by their bright colours and tonal shifts; the 
floating and popping effect of meticulously painted shapes confuses their 
spatial relationships and the layers become indistinct. Reeves’ interest in this 
series of abstractions is rooted in her fascination with colour and pattern. 
Influenced by her previous studies in marine biology she integrates a rigid 
sense of shape and form with an intuitive and organic colour palette.

Since graduating with honours from the Ontario College of Art and Design 
University in 2005, Reeves has participated in solo and group exhibitions. She 
was regional finalist in the 2008 RBC Canadian Painting Competition and her 
work is in the collections of the Bank of Montreal, Royal Bank of Canada, 
Medcan and Cenovus Energy. Born in England, Reeves grew up in Canada 
and currently lives and works in San Francisco, CA.

Please join us and Amanda Reeves for the Opening of her inaugural 
Vancouver exhibition on Saturday June 8, 2 - 4 pm.

For more information please contact Elissa at 604 730-9611, 
info@CristallGallery.com

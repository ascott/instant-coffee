In partnership with Capture Photography Festival, Booooooom is hosting a worldwide open
call for an artist or artist group to have their work installed on the faade of the King Edward
Canada Line Station from April to September 2021. The selected artists work will be
featured on the
Capture website, and in the 2021 Capture Catalogue"thousands of copies of
which are distributed throughout the Lower Mainland. As well, the artist will be featured in
articles on Booooooom.com.

Submissions are open now until October 26, 2020.

Artist Talk / Hugh Kearney/ Tuesday, October 6, 7pm

Art Rental & Sales Showroom at the Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby Street, Vancouver

Hugh Kearney's mixed media practice appropriates elements of 
traditional landscape painting, building out from the artist's observation 
of the interactions between colour, light, form and atmosphere. His 
work is influenced by a combination of modernism, the urban 
landscape, and his Maliseet heritage. Kearney received his MFA from 
the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design in 1987. 

Free to attend but space is limited. For more information: 604.662.4746
or email info@artrentalandsales.com.

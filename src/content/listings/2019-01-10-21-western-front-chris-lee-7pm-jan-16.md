Talk: Immutable - From Designing Documents to Mutant Notations
Chris Lee
Grand Luxe Hall
January 16 @ 7:00 pm
Free!

303 8 AVE E, Vancouver, BC

Western Front welcomes Buffalo-based graphic designer and educator Chris Lee to 
residence for a new project taking place in 2019. Chris Lee will be in residence to 
develop LDM, a new project that reimagines a laser distance measurement device 
as a tool for manifesting spatial measurements as sound. This speculative 
instrument for interpreting space will be used to play spaces, figuring architecture 
as a form of notation. This project will continue into a second phase that involves a 
publication/manual that explores synchronic developments and theoretical 
resonances between the standardization of musical time as well as of language and 
measurement. All together these address a thematic research into notation as a 
matter of standardization, the production of normativity and the imposition of a 
managerial gaze. The residency will provide time for the technical development of 
the instrument, its conceptual and discursive positioning, the development of the 
publication, as well as exploring its performance capacities.

Lee will give a talk on January 16 that addresses the context of his research, in 
which an interest in notation emerges as an instrument of 
standardization/coordination (like currency), the sedimentation of memory, of 
normativity and discipline. Speaking of coinage, he will address the mutant, a-
chronological historiography of currency and propose an undoing of the linear 
chronology of conquest, property, and administration by mashing together 
material/technical methods of inscription.

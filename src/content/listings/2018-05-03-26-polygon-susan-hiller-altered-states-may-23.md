7pm: Conversation with Susan Hiller and Helga Pakasaar

8pm: Public Opening

Susan Hiller is an acclaimed artist based in London, England whose
pioneering artworks span almost five decades. The video installations and
photographic works in Altered States highlight Hillers concern with the
unknowable. Her works investigate dream states, the irrational and the
mysteries of our collective unconscious, reflecting on how technology
affects our perceptions of the world.

Susan Hiller: Altered States will be on view May 24 - September 2, 2018

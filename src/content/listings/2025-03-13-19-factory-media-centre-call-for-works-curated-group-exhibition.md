Factory Media Centre

Open Call for Works: Curated Group Exhibition

Deadline: March 31, 2025

Factory Media Centre (FMC) is seeking submissions of media artworks for
consideration in a curated group exhibition, To see the world in a grain of sand, to be
exhibited on site from August " September 2025.

Curated by Gladys Lou, To see the
world in a grain of sand will explore sand as a
medium of communication bridging physical and digital realms. From its role in the
construction industry and silicon technology to its symbolism in deserts, magic, and
mythology, sand embodies the tension between permanence and impermanence,
holding narratives of time, memory, and transformation. This exhibition will reimagine
sand as a conduit of ancient traditions and futuristic visions, connecting natural
landscapes with virtual spaces.

Submissions are now open for media works that examine sands cultural,
technological, and mystical meanings through a variety of media art forms. We are
seeking submissions that offer new perspectives on the ever-changing material of
sand and its dynamic relationship between the tangible and the digital.

Details and Eligibility:

We are accepting submissions from artists in all levels of their careers. In line with
our mission, we prioritize applicants from Hamilton, ON and the surrounding regions,
however we still encourage submissions from artists across Canada and abroad. We
are accepting submissions of in-progress works, however all artworks must be
completed and ready to exhibit by July 2025.

Deadline to apply: March 31st, 2025 at 11:59PM EST

For the full submission guidelines, further details, and to submit your work, visit:

www.factorymediacentre.ca/callforworks-toseetheworld/

Please contact Eli at programming@factorymediacentre.ca with any questions or
concerns. Please apply through the online submission form only. Hard copy and
email submissions will not be considered, unless special accommodations are
requested. Notification can be expected in late April.

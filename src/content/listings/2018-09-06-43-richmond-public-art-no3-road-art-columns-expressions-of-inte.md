No.3 Road Art Columns, Expressions of Interest, EOI

The City of Richmond Public Art Program seeks Expressions of Interest from Richmond-
based visual artists who are interested in creating original artwork for the 2019 No.3 Road 
Art Columns Exhibitions. The first exhibition from February"July, 2019 will offer two 
Richmond-based artists a mentoring opportunity with professional artist, Valerie D. Walker. 
Each artist will receive an artist fee of $1,500 CAD to produce four (4) print-ready artwork 
files. Production and installation costs will be the responsibility of the City of Richmond. 
Artists with practices in mixed-media, painting, illustration, photography, printmaking, 
drawing or other 2-D visual interdisciplinary practices are encouraged to apply. 

Artist Fee: $1,500 CAD
Eligibility: Artists residing in Richmond, BC
Deadline: Tuesday, September, 18, 2018, 5:00 p.m.
Mentorship: October 2018-January 2019
Installation: Exhibition 13: February-July 2018

For more information and how to apply, visit: richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities

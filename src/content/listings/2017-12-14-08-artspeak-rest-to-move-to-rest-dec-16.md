at 2 PM

Rest to Move to Rest with Justine A. Chambers

In this workshop participants will alternate between rest and simple repetitive 
movements adapted from social dance, somatic practice and western contemporary 
dance. By practicing individually while together, participants will work with a gentle 
awareness on the transmission of felt information between bodies. We will practice 
movement to rest our minds, and return to rest to feel the perpetual movements of 
our bodies.

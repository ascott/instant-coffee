Collage Salon with Elizabeth Zvonar

Jul. 30, 6:30 p.m. - 9:30 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Join our group collage session led by Elizabeth Zvonar and have a drink and some inspired 
fun with found and cut out material to make your own unique twist on this technique! - Cash 
bar - No minors

Collage is a way of art making that embraces combining a wide variety of materials and 
relies on good composition to make a finished work. The materials can be anything including 
what you can find in your recycle bin and as such, its a generous and forgiving medium with 
which to make art. This upcoming Collage Salon will be led by Elizabeth Zvonar, a local 
Vancouver artist working in both collage and sculpture. Zvonar will locate collage through art 
history with a brief set of images that are intended to give some context to how collage has 
been used in the past. The workshop will include some quick exercises to get us started. 
Come sift through magazines and materials to find some treasures to begin working. Mobil 
will supply scissors, glue and some books and magazines to look through. If youve got 
some material that you would like to use, please feel free to bring along. We will work 
collectively on a giant long table. You can take away your piece(s) at the end of the session. 
$25

AN ALTERNATIVE PRIDE
Vancouver's first Alternative Pride Festival is a grass-roots festival 
highlighting Vancouver's alternative culture. We are a 
decentralized, multi-venue, music and arts festival taking place over 
four days during Vancouver Pride Week. Our mission is to present 
to the public alternative art, alternative venues, and alternative 
vibes.

Alternative Pride Festival takes place at non-traditional venues 
throughout the city in collaboration with dozens of local artists and 
underground events. We seek to foster safe spaces outside of the 
traditional night clubs while offering the public new options and 
alternatives. Additionally, we seek to create spaces that challenge 
mainstream notions of sexuality, gender identity, and self 
expression.

Alternative Pride Festival has a commitment to artistic autonomy 
and compensation. We believe artists, performers, and staff should 
be compensated fairly for their talents. We believe artists should 
retain creative and artistic autonomy of their ideas and intellectual 
capital. We seek to create and promote a socio-economic climate 
that will advance artistic opportunities in the production of 
alternative art amoung artists in Vancouver.

Alternative Pride Festival has a commitment to accessibility. With 
every event, we have reserved some admission for members of our 
community with financial barriers this pride season. We have an 
application process for subsidized entry to each event. If you are 
unable to afford Pride this year, please message Vancouver Art and 
Leisure via Facebook before July 20. Please let us know which 
events you would like to apply for and we will try to find a way for 
you to be included.

Presented by Vancouver Art and Leisure Society -

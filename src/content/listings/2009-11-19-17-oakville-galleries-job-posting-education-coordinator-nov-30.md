Oakville Galleries
JOB POSTING: EDUCATION COORDINATOR

Terms: Full-time contract (35 hours/week)
Start Date: January 2010
Salary Level: $18/hour
Language of work: English and French

Oakville Galleries is a leading public art museum where contemporary art
is the impetus for advancing imagination and self-reflection. Through two
sites, the Galleries serves communities in Oakville, Halton Region and
wider audiences nationally and internationally. We are currently looking
for a bilingual Education Coordinator to add to our dynamic education
team.

Nature and Scope of Position
Reporting to the Director of Education and Public Programmes, the
Education Coordinator will oversee the implementation of Oakville
Galleries' art classes, camps and school programmes. Exploring art as a
tool for learning and personal development, the Education Coordinator will
engage with a number of audiences, including the local school community,
partner organizations and individual families in Oakville and the
surrounding area.

General Responsibilities and Functions
the design, delivery and evaluation of the Galleries'
educational activities
Managing educational programme schedules
Researching, establishing and maintaining a network of programme
partners and participants
Overseeing all outreach and promotion for art classes, camps and school
programmes
Ensuring the maintenance of the Galleries' studios, equipment and other
material resources used for education programmes
Periodically delivering educational programmes to a range of audiences

Qualifications
The ideal candidate has:
completed a degree in fine arts and/or art education with an
interest in
contemporary visual and media art practices
a demonstrated interest in working with youth
a proven ability to communicate effectively in both official languages
in verbal and written forms
excellent communication and outreach skills
strong organizational skills, detail-oriented focus and the ability to
manage multiple priorities
the ability to work both independently and in a team environment
a valid driver's license and access to a vehicle

Please submit applications to Catherine Sicot, Director of Education and
Public Programmes at catherine@oakvillegalleries.com

Oakville Galleries is an equal opportunity employer. We thank all
applicants for their interest, however only those selected for an
interview will be contacted.

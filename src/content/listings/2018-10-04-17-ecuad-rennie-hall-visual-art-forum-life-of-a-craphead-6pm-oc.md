TALK I Visual Art Forum
Life of a Craphead 

Thursday, October 11 I 6 pm
Rennie Hall
ECUAD
1399 Johnston St, Vancouver, BC

Toronto-based duo Life of a Craphead is the collaboration of Amy Lam and Jon 
McCurley. Their work spans performance art, film, and curation. Their name comes 
from the opening joke of the very first live comedy routine they performed together in 
2006. Their most recent body of work, titled Entertaining Every Second, deals with 
experiences and representations of Western imperialism in Asia. 

Past performance projects include King Edward VII Statue Floating Down the Don 
River (2017), where they dumped a life-size replica of a colonial statue into a 
Toronto river on a weekly basis for a month; and The Life of a Craphead Fifty Year 
Retrospective, 2006-2056 (Art Gallery of Ontario, Toronto, 2013), a fake career 
retrospective of all the work they will ever make.

They were longlisted for the 2018 Sobey Art Award and are inaugural recipients of 
the Sobey Art Award Residency at the Delfina Foundation, London U.K.

Visual Art Forums are presented by the Audain Faculty of Art.

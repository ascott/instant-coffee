Emerging Kamloops artist Matthew Tremblay examines the relationship
between things and objects as an archaeological exploration of
material culture in his exhibition Some Thing. He questions how we
identify and label things (is a broken wheel still a wheel if it can no
longer function as one?), and adds a imaginary second life through
drawings and transforms decaying, rusted, non-functional things into
useful objects once again. In two parts, one set of drawings forms a
continuous frieze around the walls of the vault gallery space while the
second set of drawings propose, at times absurdly, alternate functions
and usages.

Matthew Tremblay was born in Kamloops, BC in 1976 and currently
resides and works in the same city. He received his BFA from
Thompson Rivers University in 2005 with a focus in printmaking and
drawing. Currently he is the head preparator at the Kamloops Art
Gallery and has been there since 2007.

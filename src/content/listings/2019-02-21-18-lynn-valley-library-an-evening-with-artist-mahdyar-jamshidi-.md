Join us for an evening with our current artist-in-residence Mahdyar Jamshidi as he 
presents I Am Here at Lynn Valley Library. I Am Here is a continuous collective art 
project that concentrates on challenging the various notions of the center. The 
project took place in three stages in Iran. In Vancouver, during the artist residency in 
Griffin Art Projects, Jamshidi is exploring the concept of center from sociopolitical, 
economic, psychological, and physics perspectives, collecting data and sharing 
information through talks and meetings with local researchers and the public. Learn 
more about this project 

and others at www.griffinartprojects.ca

Mahdyar Jamshidi is a photo-based artist and researcher from Tehran, Iran. He has 
a Bachelor of Photography, a Masters in Studio Art, and a Doctorate of Business 
Administration. In his work, he simplifies and decodes as a strategy to deal with his 
own lifes circumstances, unknowns and systems. His works reflects on critical 
issues related to society, humanity, science and politics. His works vary between 
photography, film and installation, and have been presented in Florence, Iowa, 
Santa Cruz, Sofia, Sydney, and Tehran.

Wine & Screenprinting Night
Jun. 21, 6:30 p.m. " 9:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer St, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Start your own fashion trend! or just a little fun R&R on a Friday night
Add a bit of creative flair and individuality to your wardrobe by learning how to screenprint onto 
t-shirts and tote bags. In this session, you will learn how to use screenprinting to print a design 
onto fabric. You will have the chance to squeegee a design onto your own t-shirt or tote bag. So 
come grab a drink and let your creativity fly!
$63. Materials: bring your own t-shirts for printing. 1 plain tote will be provided. Additional totes 
available for purchase.

OTHER WORLDS: SFU Visual Arts’ Collaborative Experiment
Audain Gallery, Vancouver
March 27 – April 5

The School for the Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser University and 
the Audain Gallery are excited to announce OTHER WORLDS.

Curated by 3rd year Visual Arts students, OTHER WORLDS is an 
exhibition of collaboratively produced works by students throughout 
the School for the Contemporary Arts and other emerging artists that 
address the issues of collectivity, otherness, self-reflectivity, 
globalization and colonization. OTHER WORLDS aims to critcally 
explore the infinitely expanding frontier of space-travel, science 
fiction and science fact, futurism and global-self-awareness.

Artists include: Black Hole Vacuum (Adriana Lademann, Anchi Lin, 
Cydney Paddon and Jocelyn Sanchez); Q'os (Alanna Ho, Jasmine 
Huang, Ariel So, and Betsy Wen); Intergalactic Cultural Association of 
Art Critics (Jasmine Kwong, Wan Hang Tsang, Larisa Volkova and 
Cory Woodcock); Substantial Duality (Lauren Lavery and Stephanie 
Ng); Double Happiness (Brennan Kelly and Alex Strusberg); 
Interstellar Communications Consortium (Alex Hill, Graham White, 
Angela Yan and Michelle Zhang); Stephane Bernard and Ben 
Bonner; Wryly Andherson, Kelsey Jacobsen, Luiza Ramazanova and 
Sean Talarico; Katy Churcher, Coco Huang and Edward Peng; 
Dasha Boichenko and Aaron May; and Angela Lin and Nathan 
Evans.

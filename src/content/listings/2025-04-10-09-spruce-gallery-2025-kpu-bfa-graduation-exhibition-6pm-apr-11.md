Before Tomorrow
2025 KPU BFA Graduation Exhibition
April 11 - 24, 2025
Opening Reception: April 11 from 6-9pm
Spruce Gallery, Spruce Atrium & Arbutus Gallery
12666 72 Avenue, Surrey BC

Before Tomorrow features the work of twelve emerging artists from within Kwantlen
Polytechnic University's Bachelor of Fine Arts program: Jen Kempton, Kacey Hughes,
Claudia Shen, Wenda Zhang, Stephy Zhang, Tia Murray, Dan Zhang, Jocelyn Gosling,
Jansen Ramos, Kyle Chua, Thomas Chanyungco and Carter Cooke. The works
featured in this exhibition showcase the culmination of these students fourth year
advanced studio practice; exploring diverse themes through a wide variety of materials
and mediums.

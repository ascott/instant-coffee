Kira Pratt: Firstborn

@

Duplex Artist Society

1889 Franklin st.

Vancouver, B.C.

Join us on July 20th for the opening reception for Firstborn, a collection of sculptures
by Kira Pratt.

Kira Pratt is a multidisciplinary artist who primarily works in sculpture. Within her
practice, she explores the tension between internal and external world building and
how emotional worlds are influenced by, and reflected in, built environments.
Drawing from dreamscapes, keepsakes and back yard decorations, her work plays
with the ephemerality and permanence of moments and materials. Within her work,
she contorts the roles of materials and their contexts; giving longevity to the single-
use and highlighting the fragility in the robust.

She holds a BFA from Emily Carr University of Art + Design and will be pursuing an
MFA at the University of Guelph beginning in September 2025. She currently resides
in Vancouver BC; the unceded lands of the xmkwy"m, Skwxw7mesh and
Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh peoples.

Call to Canadian Indigenous Artists :: Valley Line Tawatin Bridge

Call to Artists - Tawatin Bridge Public Art Project (TBC) the Tawatin 
Bridge public art competition, is open to all professional Canadian 
Indigenous artists residing in Canada, and is held in accordance with 
the City of Edmonton policy "Percent for Art to Provide and Encourage 
Art in Public Areas" (C458C).

The Edmonton Arts Council Public Art Program, on behalf of the City of 
Edmonton, is seeking a Canadian Indigenous Artist or artist team to 
integrate site-specific public art at the Tawatina Bridge. Artist teams 
may be comprised of Canadian Indigenous lead artist(s), or Canadian 
Indigenous lead artist(s) in collaboration or mentorship relationships 
with emerging artists.

There are two potential locations for public art, both of which provide 
significant and highly visible opportunities for public art. Renderings are 
provided with images illustrating the art locations. In a manner that is 
culturally appropriate to the peoples of this region, artwork concepts 
should consider the significant cultural heritage and legacy of the river 
valley, the rich histories, stories and customs that tell of this place, and 
Indigenous perspectives of "river crossing". Artworks of diverse 
mediums as well as contemporary and customary (traditional) 
disciplines are welcome.

Applicants must identify one site location in their letters of intent, as 
well as their conceptual approach to the project. Letters of intent must 
also include a preliminary indication of a community engagement 
strategy.

Shortlisted artists will have first opportunity to interact with local 
Indigenous Elders and knowledge holders at a 1-2 day site 
visit/workshop (date TBA), facilitated by Indigenous curator Candice 
Hopkins.

Budget: 
$295,500.00 CAD (maximum, all inclusive)

Deadline for Submissions: 
4:30 pm on TUESDAY, APRIL 5, 2016

Installation: 
August - September 2018

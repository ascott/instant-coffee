The Ferry Building Gallery proudly presents "Anomaly," an exhibition of photography
by Vancouver-based artist and activist, Desire Patterson.

In 2018 Patterson embarked on a 12-day trek to Mount Everest basecamp to
photographically document glaciers. Humbled in their presence, this was an
unforgettable experience that forever transformed her own inner landscape.
From extensive research and documentation, Patterson created Anomaly in 2020.

Exhibition dates are August 4-27, 2023. For more information and detailed
programming surrounding the exhibition, please view the attached pdf or visit the Ferry
Building Gallery website.

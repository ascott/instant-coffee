Artist-in-Residence program at Ross Creek Centre for the Arts :::

-=-=-=-=-=-=-Application Deadline: January 15th, 2010 -=-=-=-=-=-=-

The Ross Creek Centre for the Arts invites submissions from artists in all
disciplines of performing, visual, literary and media arts for the Spring
and Fall of 2010 Artist Colony program.

The Centre offers residencies to professional creative artists from all
nations and backgrounds working in one or more of the following
disciplines: dance, theatre, music, visual art, fine craft, performance
art, sound, photography, film, video, literary arts and architecture.
Residencies last from two weeks to two months and include room, board, and
studio. Artists may apply individually or as members of collaborative
teams. Applications are juried according to merit-based criteria and
work-exchange is available for applicants who demonstrate financial need.

For more information or to apply, contact the Colony Director at
colony@artscentre.ca

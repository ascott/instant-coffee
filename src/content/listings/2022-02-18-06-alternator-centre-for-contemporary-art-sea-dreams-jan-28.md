Exhibition: Sea Dreams
Artist: Joanne Gervais and Shauna Oddleifson
Date: January 28 - March 12, 2022
Location: Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art [Project Gallery]

Local Artists Dream of the Sea at the Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art

Kelowna-based artists Joanne Gervais and Shauna Oddleifson have collaborated on a
new exhibition at the Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art commenting on the impact
humans have on the natural world. In the video-based exhibition, the artists create a
dream-like animation of a small, octopus-mask-wearing girl interacting with the ocean
world.

In addition to the character's interactions with sea creatures and underwater plant life,
the girl navigates the impact of human negligence. While Sea Dreams
depicts a
playful, dream-like picture, the work is subverted with a darker undertone. Gervais and
Oddleifson use this story to remind us that the health of our oceans is under threat and
underscore how the ways we interact with nature can have serious consequences.

The climate crisis is top of mind for many of us during these challenging times.
Through their work Sea Dreams, Gervais and Oddleifson offer a unique perspective on
the complexity of our relationship with the natural environment, stoking our wonder and
awakening our responsibility - sentiments integral to the conversations and actions that
are required of us now, and in the future. comments Artistic and Administrative
Director, Lorna McParland.

Sea Dreams will be on view in the Project Gallery of the Alternator Centre for
Contemporary Art from January 28 to March 12, 2022.

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is an artist-run centre located in Kelowna
at the Rotary Centre for the Arts, 421 Cawston Avenue. The
Alternator Centre for
Contemporary Art is a registered non-profit charitable organization dedicated to the
development of the creative community. Since 1989, the Alternator has shown the work
of emerging Canadian artists, focused on innovative and non-traditional mediums
engaged in social and cultural issues.

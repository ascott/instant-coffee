Call for Artists: Fall Figurative Group Exhibition

Submissions are open for our juried fall group exhibition celebrating the power and
diversity of figurative painting.

NOTE: artwork requirements have been updated! We are now accepting
submissions of two-dimensional artwork of any size under 20 inches (in both
directions).

Event Dates: September 27 - October 5, 2024
Opening Reception: Saturday, September 28, 12 PM - 4 PM

This is your chance to have your work featured
alongside other talented artists, in
both an in-person and online exhibition. We are seeking twenty small
captivating
figurative paintings that push boundaries and tell compelling stories, whether created
on canvas, wood, paper, or any other surface.

Find out more at thisgallery.org

Important Dates:

Submission Deadline: August 31 - midnight
Artists Notified By: September 6
Artwork Delivery Deadline: September 21 - 5 PM
Exhibition Dates: Friday, September 27 to Saturday, October 5
Opening Reception: Saturday, September 28, noon - 4 PM
Submit your best figurative work and take the opportunity to gain visibility, connect
with art lovers, and be part of an unforgettable artistic experience.

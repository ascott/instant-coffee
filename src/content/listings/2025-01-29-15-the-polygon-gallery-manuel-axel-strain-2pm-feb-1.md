Manuel Axel Strain: xlmx child is on view in The Polygon's ground floor gallery
from February 1 - May 11.
About the exhibition
In xlmx child, Manuel Axel Strain draws on Musqueam, Secwpemc, and Syilx
ways of knowing, and the discipline of Western psychology. Compositing theories of
mind from across their different cultures, Strain imagines the perspective of a child
who contemplates the world from beyond these existing frameworks. Through
figurative paintings, transformed into pictographs and set against photographic
murals, Strains work proposes a way of seeing that suspends judgement and
challenges divisions such as past and future, old and new, self and other.
In this context, Strain embraces the uncertain and unfamiliar. The table and chairs
situated throughout the installation evoke a domestic setting, but their odd
dimensions create a sense of strangeness, as if the viewer is very
small, or these
furnishings have purposes beyond what is expected. Here, the unknown is a
powerful presence. Speculation and wonder are productive points of inquiry for
Strain, inviting visitors to inhabit the imaginative, transpersonal vantage of xlmx
child.

About the artist
Manuel Axel Strain (b. 1990) is a 2Spirit artist from the lands and waters of the
xmkym (Musqueam), Simpcw, and Syilx peoples, based in the sacred
homelands of their q"ic"y" (Katzie) and qa:n"n" (Kwantlen) relatives. Creating
artwork in collaboration with and reference to their family, shared experiences
become a source of agency for Strain. They perceive their work to confront and
undermine the imposed realities of colonialism, proposing a new space beyond its
oppressive systems of power. Strain was recently featured in the Vancouver Special
exhibition at the Vancouver Art Gallery (2021), longlisted for the 2022 Sobey Award,
and was a 2022 recipient of the Portfolio Prize.

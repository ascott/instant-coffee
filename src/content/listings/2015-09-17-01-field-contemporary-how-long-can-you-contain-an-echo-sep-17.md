How Long Can You Contain an Echo?
Megan Hepburn, Simone Jarvis, Shelley Penfold, Tess Rafael
September 17th — October 17th, 2015
Opening September 17th, 7:00 — 10:00PM

looking into and through
shadows move

light falls from above
and I can see down

the path
a flicker of movement

birds dive in the rose patch
specks of green and gold part ways

my surroundings dance and fall apart

in the copper pot full of water
I can see myself looking at me

the house creaks
"how long can you contain an echo?"

Field Contemporary presents an exhibition of new paintings from four
painters. Each artist provides their own insight into the verses of
abstraction. Experience, memory, fiction, time, history, material
application, process, and playful methodologies are subjects in the making
of these paintings. The application of paint disguises representation and
solid structures. Spatial realms are constructed out of forms and lines
while mark making creates it's own dialogue amongst the paintings.

Megan Hepburn received her BFA from Emily Carr University and MFA from
Concordia University in 2010, and was fortunate to study with exceptional
painting faculty in both institutions. She has had exhibitions across
Canada and Europe since 2006. In 2010 she was the recipient of the Joseph
Plaskett Award for Excellence in Painting and shortlisted for the RBC
Painting Competition. This year she is a nominee for the RBC Painting
Competition again, coming up in November. Megan has participated in
numerous residencies internationally, most recently at the 2015 Salzburg
Summer Academy of Fine Art in Austria, mentored by Varda Caivano.

Simone Jarvis is an artist currently working out of Vancouver, BC.
Originally from Calgary, AB, she holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts from Emily
Carr. She works primarily in painting exploring the possibilities of medium
and surface relationships. Her paintings and drawings continue to be
organized primarily by colour and form working with thinned out oil paint.
Recent exhibitions include “Towards a Fictional History of Colour” at BAF
as well as a solo show “Macro Paintings and Sculptures” at Avenue Gallery.

Shelley Penfold is a painter based in Vancouver, BC. She completed her
B.F.A at Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design in 2003 and a M.F.A at the
University of Victoria in 2009. She has exhibited nationally and her works
have been placed in private and corporate collections.

Tess Rafael received her BA in Urban Studies from Brown University and
studied painting through the Rhode Island School of Design in 2010 and
Columbia University School of the Arts in 2011. She has exhibited in North
America and Europe. She currently lives and works in Vancouver, Canada.

14. College Art Association | 2010 Conference | Paper Proposals | MAY 9
CALL: CALL FOR PARTICIPATION: Age of Enlargement, College Art Association 2010 
Conference Chicago

I am seeking paper proposals / presentations appropriate for the following panel to be held at the 
College Art Association next February:

The Age of Enlargement:
In a London newspaper in 1845 an anonymous author used the phrase Age of Enlargement to 
satirically critique the burgeoning industry and class segregation going on around him. This 
phrase also describes contemporary social and political conditions. Decades of trade 
deregulation have resulted in greater income disparities between the rich and poor, while the 
world's slums increase in size, and global building-booms and consumer demand for cheap 
goods requires unsustainable extractions of natural resources. Closer to home, calorie-counting 
restaurant signage in New York attempts to curb the growing obesity epidemic, and dangerous 
new weather patterns destroy homes in Louisiana and California. Are these extremes qualitatively 
different from those of the past, or is such thinking only a vanity in the minds of the living? This 
panel solicits paper proposals on subjects that address obesity, enlargement, gluttony, excessive
wealth or poverty, and similar or related themes. Paper topics can be historical or contemporary,
and interdisciplinary scholarship that considers art or visual culture in the context of current 
economic and/or political theory is of particular interest.

Proposals must include:
1. Completed session participation form, located as a PDF on the CAA website at 
http://conference.collegeart.org/2010/
2. Preliminary abstract of one to two double-spaced, typed pages.
3. Letter explaining speaker's interest, expertise in the topic, and CAA membership status.(To 
participate in the conference you need to be or become a member of CAA)
4. CV with home and office mailing addresses, email address, and phone numbers.
5. If sent in postal mail in the USA, a self-addressed postcard for confirmation that proposal was 
received, if sent internationally we recommend sending certified mail, and/or with return receipt
requested.

Proposals should be sent via email to: Leah Modigliani, 151 Nelson St. Apt 2, Brooklyn, NY, 
USA, 11231, or email submissions to: lmodigliani@mindspring.com (please put CAA 2010 in 
subject line). EMAIL SUBMISSIONS PREFERRED.

The deadline for submission of abstracts is May 9. If your proposal is accepted a final abstract will 
be required by Aug. 3, 2009, and the final paper by Dec. 1, 2009.

Sincerely,
Leah Modigliani
Stony Brook University

16. FRONT magazine | Art Director | MAY 25
Art Director / Co-Editor, FRONT magazine
Western Front Society

Position Type: Part Time (17.5 hrs/week)
Date of posting: April 17, 2009
Application Deadline: May 25, 2009
Start Date: July 1, 2009
Salary: $15,500 per annum

The Western Front Society seeks an outstanding, highly motivated arts professional to take on 
the role of Art Director / Co-Editor of FRONT magazine.

The Western Front is one of Canada's pioneering artist-run centres and produces and presents 
works in five programs: Exhibitions, Performance Art, New Music, Media Arts, and Publications 
(FRONT magazine). The Western Front was founded in 1973 by a small group of interdisciplinary 
artists, and has developed into an exemplary multi-disciplinary environment for experimental art 
practice and research. With a staff of ten, plus interns and volunteers, the Society collectively
produces over fifty events a year.

FRONT magazine has a mandate to publish contemporary works for the printed page. Through 
columns, studio profiles, interviews and theme-based artist projects, FRONT seeks to engage 
readers and serve as a launch pad for a diverse range of work by emerging and established 
artists. FRONT magazine is published four times a year.

Responsibilities

The Art Director/Co-Editor reports to the Board of Directors, and is an active participant in the
Western Front Management Team. Working closely with the Managing Editor, this position 
assumes co-leadership and responsibility of the publications program and for:

• Performing all aspects of the design, layout and production of FRONT magazine including 
hands-on pre-press.
• Soliciting work and collaborating with artists and writers

• Selecting and editing content

• Preparing the publication for mailing and distribution

• Preparing grant applications

• Attending and organizing events related to the publication

• Visioning and shaping the future of the Western Front Publications Program

• Collaborating with other Western Front programs

• Sharing responsibility with the Management Team for carrying out the policies of the Western 
Front and guiding staff and/or volunteers involved with the core operations.

Qualifications
As our ideal candidate, you possess:

• Outstanding skills in print design and production

• Excellent written and verbal communications skills

• The ability to prioritize and meet deadlines in a bustling interdisciplinary environment

• Knowledge of a broad range of issues related to the arts

• A working knowledge of design and publishing for the web

• The ability to effectively delegate and supervise volunteers, interns and external contractors

• A clear understanding of the philosophy and history of the Canadian artist-run centre 
movement

• Appreciation for consensus-driven, collaborative management processes
• Knowledge of the principle funding agencies and grant writing experience

An understanding of contemporary issues in critical and creative writing, as well as visual and 
performing arts is an asset.

A competitive benefits package is available after the three months probation period is complete. 
After each production cycle, 7 days paid vacation may be taken, to a total of four weeks per year. 
An additional ten days paid holiday may be taken during the December/January holiday period. 
Opportunities for professional development are available on a grant-funded basis.

The Western Front Society is committed to the principles of Employment Equity and encourages 
applications from Aboriginal persons, members of a visible minority group or persons with a 
disability. Applications should include a cover letter, curriculum vitae, three references, one or two 
short writing samples, and a select portfolio of design work (you may refer us to an online 
portfolio, or include printed matter). Applications must be received by 4:00 pm on May 25, 2009.

Toque Craft Fair
Open Call
Deadline:Jul 31, 2025, 11:59 p.m. PDT
Website:toquecraftfair.com

Toque Craft Fair is an annual holiday market and fundraiser hosted by Western
Front. Welcoming over 4,000 visitors each year, Toque Craft Fair showcases a
curated selection of vendors representing the very best of BC"based artists,
craftspeople, and designers. This year Toque will take place from Dec 5 " 7, 2025.

Interested in participating as a vendor?

We invite applications from makers working in textiles, ceramics, jewelry, glass,
accessories, print, stationery, childrens gifts, homeware, pet gifts, body products,
and more. Artists and creators of all ages are encouraged to apply, with priority given
to those available for all three days of the fair.

Toque Craft Fair is a fundraiser for Western Front. Rather than charging a table fee,
vendors are asked to donate 30% of their sales in support of Western Fronts artistic
programs and the preservation of our historic building.

Selected vendors will be provided with a table, full transaction management, and
promotional support through Toques website, social media, and press outreach.

For application details, visit toquecraftfair.com.

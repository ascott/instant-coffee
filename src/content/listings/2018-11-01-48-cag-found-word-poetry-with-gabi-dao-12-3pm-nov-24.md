Family Day | Found Word Poetry with Gabi Dao

Saturday, November 24, 12-3pm, free
Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

On the last Saturday of each month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition 
tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Inspired by Kameelah Janan Rasheeds An Alphabetical Accumulation of Approximate 
Observations, join Vancouver-based artist Gabi Dao for experimental workshops using 
audio recordings and printed material to create your own found word poems.

No reservation required, simply just turn up!

Find out more about the exhibitions at https://www.contemporaryartgallery.ca/whats-on/

DO IT TOGETHER, BY YOURSELF:

All summer, every Wednesday and Saturday at the Burrardview Park 
Fieldhouse (545 North Slocan St) from 10-12 and everyday from July 8 
- 13th

BYO BBQ Wednesday July 13th at 12
Join burrardview artist in residence Lexie Owen, and Vancouver based 
artist Caitlin Chaisson to Do It Together, By Yourself- a self-directed 
summer reading/working/study group. Bring your own project, reading, 
book etc and work with us together by yourself.

Hammocks, blankets, and inflatable pineapples are provided.

Do It Together, By Yourself kicks off with a week long intensive from 
July 8 - 13th, and then continues every Wednesday and Saturday for 
the rest of the summer from 10 - 12 at Burrardview Park.

Ezra Gray

opening reception: 7 March 6 - 8pm

continuing to: 13 April 2024

Ezra Gray (b. 1986) lives and works in Paris. He completed his MA in painting from the
Royal College of Art (London) and BFA from Concordia University (Montreal). Solo
exhibitions include Maximum Yearn, Unit 17, Vancouver (2020); Joech God, Emalin,
London (2018); Brown Space, Almanac Inn, Turin and Infinitive, Jeffrey Stark, New
York City (both 2017). Select two person & group exhibitions include Folly, Emalin
Projects, Dunmore Pineapple, Scotland; Honolulu Triennale with Sean Nicholas
Savage, Honolulu, Zurich (both 2016) and Rundgang, Kunstakademie Dsseldorf
(2014), among others.

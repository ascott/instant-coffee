Conversation with Rebecca Brewer
The Chive and the Female Soul

"He labored and labored mightily, unable to advance beyond the chives. In
the first place, the painting remained green. As soon as he used a
different color, the disturbance that resulted was so great that he covered
it with green again. For a while he thought that the treacherous female
soul (treachery no doubt its most important element alongside emptiness)
could appear as a cubist lemon-yellow spiral among the greenâ€“a shape more
or less like one of those sofa-springs that winds crookedly upward. But,
alas, painting is color as well as form. The yellow refused to meld with
the massive green of his chive allegory. He had no choice but to remove the
winding spiral." - Hannah Hoch, The Painter (1920)

Rebecca Brewer is a Vancouver-based artist. She completed her MFA at Bard
College in 2012. Exhibitions of her work have included the Vancouver Art
Gallery (2012), Exercise, Vancouver (2012), W WTWO, Montreal (2012), Helen
Pitt, Vancouver (2011), Gallery Atsui (2009). Brewer was the 2011 national
winner of the RBC Canadian Painting Competition.

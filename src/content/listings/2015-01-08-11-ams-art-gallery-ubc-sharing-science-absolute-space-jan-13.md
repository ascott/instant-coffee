Absolute Space - CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

Deadline: January 13, 2015

“Absolute space, in its own nature, without relation to anything external,
remains always similar and immovable. Relative space is some movable
dimension or measure of the absolute spaces; which our senses determine by
its position to bodies; and which is commonly taken for immovable space;
such is the dimension of a subterraneous, an aerial, or celestial space,
determined by its position in respect of the earth.”  – Isaac Newton

UBC Sharing Science is seeking proposals for an interdisciplinary art
exhibition to be held at the University of British Columbia's AMS Art
Gallery from February 2-13, 2015.

Absolute Space asks you to explore the architecture of space and the role
science plays in the designing of space. We invite creators of all
disciplines, from art to architecture, engineering to computer science, to
share your visions of the future of space and meditate upon its nature.

In an increasingly connected world, where our electronics can learn our
behavior and adapt to our needs, are we to understand ourselves as an
extension of our spaces, or our spaces as an extension of ourselves?

Please submit your proposals along with images of your work to
ubcsharingscience@gmail.com by January 13, 2015. If submitting a work in
progress, please include a detailed description of the work as well as an
estimated date of completion. If your work is inspired by specific
scientific theories or debates, please share those with us as well.

Formats accepted:

- 2D/print

- Installation and 3-dimensional work (prototypes, devices, sculptures,
products)

- Video and sound

- Essays and writings

Sharing Science is a UBC AMS club dedicated to promoting understanding and
appreciation of science across disciplines. Learn more:
[http://ubcsharingscience.org](http://ubcsharingscience.org)

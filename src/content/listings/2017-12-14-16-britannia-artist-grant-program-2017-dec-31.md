Britannia Artist Grant Program 2017: Deadline date December 31, 2017
Three themes to apply for:
1/Literary-$1,000.
2/Visual-$1,000.
3/Performing-$1,000.

Your application is an email to the Coordinator and must include:
Contact Info
3-5 jpeg images
brief artist biography and summary (no more than 400 words)
& how you would spend the grant if awarded
Applicants must live in the Grandview-Woodland or Strathcona community
Note: for this year, projects involving community engagement are a first
priority.

For more info: Contact Coordinator Katherine Polgrain

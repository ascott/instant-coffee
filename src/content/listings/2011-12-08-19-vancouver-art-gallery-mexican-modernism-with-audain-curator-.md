TAKE A WINTER BREAK TO MEXICO WITHOUT LEAVING 
VANCOUVER!

EVENT: Tour & talk on MEXICAN MODERNISM with Audain Curator 
Grant Arnold

DATE: Wednesday, Dec. 14th

TIME: 4pm

LOCATION: 750 Hornby Street

Telephone: 604-662-4700

More information: www.vanartgallery.bc.ca

DETAILED INFO:

As a young university student, Michael Audain travelled to Mexico to 
view the art of Mexican modernist masters Diego Rivera, Rufino 
Tamayo, David Alfaro Siqueiros, and José Clemente Orozco. He was 
inspired by the vibrancy and powerful social message of their art. In 
the late nineties, the Audains began collecting their work, and today 
they own the largest known collection of Mexican modernist paintings 
in Canada. The stunning works featured in Shore, Forest and Beyond: 
Art from the Audain Collection have never been exhibited publicly 
before by the Audains, and provide an unparalleled opportunity for 
Gallery visitors to experience this influential school of painting. Grant 
Arnold, the Audain Curator of British Columbia Art, will provide insights 
into the art of Rivera, Tamayo, Siqueiros and Orozco, as well as a 
fascinating comparison to modernism’s development in Canada. Get 
away to Mexico without leaving the city! Come to the Gallery for this 
special talk on the Mexican modernists.

Free for Gallery Members and with regular admission.

MediaNet announces programs and events for the summer of 2008:

The Engaged Body: Live Art/Performative Video is a workshop intensive focusing on the history and techniques of performance art and the creation of a performative video work. Deadline for application is April 1, 2008. For more info, please see: www.engagedbody.com/

Dance For The Camera is a two-week intensive for established and emerging choreographers, filmmakers, videographers, dancers and visual artists to explore dance/movement for the camera, leading to the creation of a work of dance for video. The program runs from July 21 to August 2, 2008. Deadline for application is May 15, 2008. For more info, please see www.danceforthecamera.org/

The Reel Dance Film Fest showcases dance films from around the world.
Submission deadline is May 1, 2008. For more info, please see:
www.reeldancefilmfest.com/

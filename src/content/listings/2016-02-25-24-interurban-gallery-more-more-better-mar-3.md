More More Better 
An exhibition of work by 20 students in advanced Studio with Cathy Busby
and Garry Neill Kennedy in the Dept of Art History, Visual Art + Theory at UBC.
Interurban Gallery, 1 East Hastings St (corner of E. Hastings and Carrall St)
opening: Thursday Mar 3, 7 - 9 pm
open hrs: Mar 4th & 5th, 1 - 5 pm | Mar 9th & 10th, 1 - 5 pm
also: Tues Mar 8, 4 pm at Interurban Gallery, 'Super Cool Tuesdays' session
where Garry Neill Kennedy, Cathy Busby and members of the class will
discuss the show. All welcome.

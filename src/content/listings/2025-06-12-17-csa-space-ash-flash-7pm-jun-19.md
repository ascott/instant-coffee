Ash & Flash
June 20-July 31, 2025
Opening Thursday, June 19 at 7pm

CSA Space
#5-2414 Main Street
Vancouver, Canada
(Enquire at Pulpfiction Books for access)

Characterized by dramatic and often luminescent surface textures, atmospheric firing
encompasses several methods of ceramic firing in which air, ash, heat, volatile
minerals and flames within a kiln can be used to create varied and unpredictable
effects. The works in this exhibition represent wood, soda and sawdust firing methods.

Featuring Kristine Aguilar, Brit Bachmann, Giovanna Cameron, Wei Cheng, Julia
Chirka, Linda Doherty, Frances de Grace, Markian Kyba, Jay MacLennan, Gayle
Oyama, Fredi Rahn, Cheryl Stapleton, Robert Stickney, Linda Ward, and Tony Wilson.

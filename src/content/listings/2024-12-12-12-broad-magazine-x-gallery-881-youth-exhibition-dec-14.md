EXHIBITION STATEMENT
The Youth Exhibition by BROAD Magazine x Gallery 881 ponders the vibrancy,
resiliency,
and complexity of youth as interpreted by 68 local and international
photographers. The opening reception of the exhibition coincides with the launch of
the Youth Edition of BROAD Magazine on December 14, 2024.

Age is a peculiar thing; as we grow older, we gain more experience and insight into
the world. Our experiences are shaped by our upbringing and circumstances. While
younger generations may not have all the answers (and neither do we), they
possess endless possibilities and the luxury of time.

The Youth Exhibition and the Youth Edition of BROAD Magazine reflect on the world
from the perspective of young people everywhere. Join us as we peer into this
exploration of self, identity, and culture as reflected by a sense of growth,
development, and also transition.

The exhibition includes 40 physical works and also projected photographs.

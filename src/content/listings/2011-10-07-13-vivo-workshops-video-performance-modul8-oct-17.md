VIDEO PERFORMANCE & Modul8
workshop | production | presentation
3 session: 9 hours + live A/V performance
Mon/Wed/Tue October 17, 19 & 25, 7-10 PM
SHOW: Thu October 27, 8PM

Cost: $200 or $160 with VIVO Producer Membership (Extended)
Instructor: Julie Gendron
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This is a project-based, beginner-level workshop, concluding with a public 
performance presented at VIVO.

The workshop will teach students the ins and outs of Modul8 along with theory 
about performing video live. Modul8 is a video mixing tool. It uses video loops, 
motion and effects to allow on-the-fly cuing and improvisation to/with audio or 
theatre and dance performance. Other technical topics in the workshop will 
include the basics of using midi controllers to change video, technical set-up 
guidelines for video performance, creating video loops, sourcing materials, and 
more, based on the interests of the students.

This workshop will coincide with an Ableton Live (audio improvisation and 
composition tool) workshop. Students from this class will be asked to pair up 
with audio students to perform together at the end of the workshop. We 
encourage friends and collaborators who are interested in learning a/v 
improvisation together, to sign-up each to their corresponding workshop of 
interest, so that they can perform together.

What you need to bring: Ideally, you would attend the workshop with your own 
laptop and a recent version of Modul8. However, if this is not possible, other 
arrangements can be made. Please inquire while signing up.

ABLETON LIVE - Improvisation & Composition
workshop | production | presentation
3 session: 9 hours + live A/V performance
Mon/Wed/Tue October 17, 19 & 25, 7-10 PM
SHOW: Thu October 27, 8PM

Cost: $200 or $200 with VIVO Producer Membership (Extended)
Instructor: Ross Birdwise
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This is a three-part, beginner-level workshop for Ableton Live audio software, 
with an emphasis on using Ableton for improvising and composing music, as 
well as mixing the two approaches into what I like to call 'comprovisation'. The 
workshop is project-based and will conclude with a public performance 
presented at VIVO.

This workshop will coincide with another workshop on doing live visuals. Our two 
groups will eventually team up to produce the live A/V presentation at VIVO. We 
encourage friends and collaborators who are interested in learning a/v 
improvisation together, to sign-up each to their corresponding workshop of 
interest, so that they can perform together.

What you need to bring: Ideally participants should bring their laptops with 
Ableton Live installed. There is a free demo version available for download on 
their website: www.ableton.com. For the second part, bring any interfaces you 
may have (i.e. Midi keyboard, drum machine with usb or midi connection, home-
made contraption with some form of midi connection, joystick, other).

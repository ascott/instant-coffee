Holly Ward is currently conducting a print research residency at Malaspina's 
studio where she is learning how to produce multi-colored silkscreen prints. 
These techniques are being utilized in creating a new body of text and image-
based prints related to her ongoing interest in the utopian imaginary. Her 
exhibition will open on Thursday March 7, 2013, from 6 to 9pm.

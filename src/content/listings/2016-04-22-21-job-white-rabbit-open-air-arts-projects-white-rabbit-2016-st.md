White Rabbit 2016 Staff Call

Want an excuse to come hang out at Red Clay, hobnob with artists and 
eat great food? This year we are looking to hire staff for the following 
positions:

Residency Coordinator (1)
Student Assistant Coordinator (1)
Volunteer Coordinator (1)
Red Clay Radio Manager/Artist (1)
Residency Cook Staff (2)

The descriptions of all the positions can be seen below. If you have 
any questions please contact Whiterabbitarts@gmail.com. All 
applications will be due by MAY 1st, unless other arrangements are 
made. Applications do not follow a strict format but should convey your 
interest in the position, connection to White Rabbit and degree of 
commitment. Also, please include the title of the position that you are 
applying for in your subject line of the email (EX: Residency 
Coordinator Application). Hopefully, positions will be determined and 
applicants informed by May 15th.

ALSO, DON'T FORGET THAT ANY RESIDENCY APPLICATIONS 
ARE DUE IN TWO MORE DAYS. Visit the website for more details. 

ALSO, a call for volunteer positions for the Residency and Festival will 
go out in the next month and this will provide another way to be part of 
White Rabbit.

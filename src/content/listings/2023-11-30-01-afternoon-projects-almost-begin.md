Almost Begin
Bronson Smillie

Oct 19 - Dec 16, 2023

Afternoon
Projects is pleased to present Almost Begin, a solo exhibition by Montreal-
based artist Bronson Smillie. Featuring four drawing sets, three wall sculptures, and
one floor sculpture, Smillie balances personal narrative with the rigid structures found
within the alphabetical system, threading themes of childhood, play, and foundational
learning.

Bronson Smillie (b.1992, Calgary, Alberta) holds a BFA in Painting and Drawing from
Concordia University and currently lives in Montral, Qubec. He is a multidisciplinary
artist whose practice involves sourcing objects and creating works with materials that
no longer fulfill their purpose within late-capitalist modes of consumption. Solo
presentations include Almost Begin, Afternoon Projects, Vancouver (2023), A Place for
Everything, april april, Brooklyn, NY (2023), Tempo 85, Espace Maurice, Montral,
Canada (2022), NADA New York, with april april (2022), and Forever is Closing in,
MoMAPS311, Ottawa, Canada (2019). Group exhibitions include
Nicelle Beauchene,
NY (2023), LVL3, Chicago (2023), AXENO7, Gatineau (2022), Petrohradsk Kolektiv,
Prague (2021), among others.

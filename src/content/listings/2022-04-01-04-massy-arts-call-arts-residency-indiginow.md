November 2022 to March 2023, Massy Arts Gallery will host IndigiNow, an arts residency
program for emerging Indigenous visual artists, interdisciplinary artists, performers, and
poets.
.
Interested applicants are invited to submit residency proposals that explore contemporary
approaches to Indigenous cultural
practices. IndigiNow aims to investigate how voices
from Indigenous communities can establish critical and sensitive dialogues with our
multiple communities of readers, viewers, and followers.
.
For this project, Massy Arts will provide: 1) gallery space for three (03) original exhibitions
that experiment with visual arts, design, performing arts, cinema, or poetry; 2) assistance
with organization, communication, press releases, registration and hosting of exhibition +
related events; 3) venue for opening reception + workshop; 4) online room for virtual talk,
performance or reading event.
.
Know more about the residency program at:
[https://massyarts.com/event/arts-residency-indiginow/](https://massyarts.com/event/arts-residency-indiginow/)

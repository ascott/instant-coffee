1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam, BC
Free. Drop-in.Wheelchair accessible.

Within the spun fibres of yarn, the warp and weft of a weaving, the blocks of a quilt and
the bond of indigo dye to fabric, textiles hold boundless knowledge, transmitted over
generations. Join curatorial assistant Anna Luth for a tour of Transmissions, a group
exhibition exploring ways in which textiles connect to the body, sculptural forms and
technologies both old
and new.

This exhibition features the work of Jonathan Alfaro, Katherine Boyer, Roxanne Charles,
Emily Hermant, Damla Tamer and Valrie d. Walker.

"Transmissions" is on view at Art Gallery at Evergreen from May 13"July 23, 2023. Visit
our website for more information: [https://evergreenculturalcentre.ca/exhibit/](https://evergreenculturalcentre.ca/exhibit/)

EVENT: "Eternal Return" Artist Performances and Curator Tour
Saturday, November 4
Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond BC
Free admission

1:00pm and 3:00pm:
Performances by Lucien Durey

2:00-3:00pm:
Curator Tour by Sunshine Frere

Guest curator Sunshine Frere leads a tour of the "Eternal Return" exhibition. This 
exhibition is a partnership with the Richmond Museum and Richmond Art Gallery, 
presenting new works by five Vancouver-based artists: Barb Choit, Kevin Day, 
Lucien Durey, Alanna Ho and Anchi Lin. Each artist has selected artefacts from the 
Richmond Museum's Migration Collection and developed works in direct dialogue 
with this eclectic array of historical objects. The products of this dialogue span a 
diverse range of media including performance, video, photography, sound and 
sculpture. Sunshine will lead a tour offering her unique insight into the works of 
each artist and how the exhibition came together.

In addition, exhibiting artist Lucien Durey will be performing vocal compositions 
before and after the tour that were written for a number of his sculptures in the 
exhibition, to reveal additional narratives and meaning in this body of work.

More details available at www.richmondartgallery.org

Sunshine Frre is a curator, writer and artist based in Vancouver, Canada. She holds 
an MA in Interactive Media from Goldsmiths, and a BFA in Studio Arts and 
Electroacoustic studies from Concordia University. Frre regularly works within 
local, national and international art communities. She currently sits as Strategic 
Initiatives Committee Co-Chair on Access Gallery's Board and also spends time 
working with the New Media Gallery in New Westminster. Recent and upcoming 
projects include cache cache, a solo exhibition of her work at Seymour Art Gallery, 
Speculators of the Final Frontier, an experimental text exploring art and space for 
SADmag and Counteractive Subterfuge, an exhibition text for the 2017 Emily Carr 
University of Art + Design Low Residency MFA cohort.

Lucien Durey lives and works in Vancouver, British Columbia. Durey holds a BFA 
from Emily Carr University of Art + Design and an MFA from Simon Fraser 
University's School for the Contemporary Arts. Employing a variety of media, Durey 
produces performance, sculpture, painting, photography and installation. Recent 
exhibitions include HOT DOG CAR WASH, Campbell River Art Gallery, 2017, 
Swimming Pool Blue, Achterhaus, Hamburg, 2016 and Hamsterly Farm Water 
Tower, Capture Photography Public Art Installation, 2016.

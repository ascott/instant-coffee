New Sculpture and Cast paper works.

Opening Friday July 7, 7 - 10pm

July 7 - August 26, 2017
Hours Thursday, Friday 12 - 5 pm
Saturday | 1 - 6 pm and by appointment

FIELD Contemporary is located at 17 West Broadway, Vancouver, BC.

Warren McLachlan (born Calgary 1975) lives and works in Vancouver, B.C. He 
received an MA from Chelsea College of Art and Design, London UK in 2007 and a 
BFA from Alberta College of Art and Design. Current and Forthcoming exhibitions 
include: Structure for Observing Atypical Flight , Unit Pitt Facade Project 2017 , 
Inversus Mundi ( w/ Corbin Union) Unit Pitt 2016, Sisters ( w/ Jason de Haan) , 
Untitled Art Society, Calgary, AB, 2015 Concerning the Bodyguard, The Tetley, 
Leeds, UK (2014), Resonant Field, Truck, Calgary AB 2014,Centenarians, Bond 
House Projects, London, UK 2013; Century City, ESA Warren St , London, UK.
(2013), Propeller Island Projects@ Supermarket, Kulturhuset, Stockholm, Sweden 
(2011), Monday Monday, Cell Project Space, London, UK (2010); Remnant Island, 
Hooverville Projects, London UK (2010) The Disconcerted Concert Party, Elevator 
Gallery, London, UK (2010), How We May Be, Art Now: Manton Lightbox, Tate 
Britain, UK (2008) Warren is a founding member of Corbin Union, an Alberta / BC 
Collective operating out of Corbin, B.C, a ghost town in the East Kootenays and 
organizes exhibitions and projects at Dynamo Arts Association in Vancouver B.C.

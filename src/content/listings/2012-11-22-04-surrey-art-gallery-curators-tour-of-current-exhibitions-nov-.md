Curator’s Tour of Current Exhibitions
Surrey Art Gallery
Thursday, November 22, 7-8:30pm

How are artists increasingly using social media to redefine self-portraiture? 
How are electronic technologies being used to paint the human face? In what 
ways does elephant communication relate to dance? How does elephant 
speech relate to dance? Surrey Art Gallery is hosting an informal tour of its 
fall on Thursday, November 22 from 7pm to 8:30pm. Leading the tour is the 
gallery’s Curator of Exhibitions and Collections, Jordan Strom, who will 
provide background on the distinct approaches and techniques of the artists 
whose works are featured in Scenes of Selves, Occasions for Ruses, Echoes 
of the Artist: Works from the Permanent Collection, and Open Sound 2012 
featuring Debashis Sinha’s sound installation titled Hati.

There will be time for refreshments and conversation after the tour. Admission 
to the event is by donation. Surrey Art Gallery Association members are free.

The tour will examine how and why the particular artworks on display were 
chosen for the exhibitions, illustrate interesting stories about how the artworks 
relate to the artists’ previous work, and illuminate the many ideas explored in 
the exhibitions. Both regular tour participants and first-time gallery visitors are 
encouraged to ask questions and share their responses to the artworks.

What will those on the tour experience? The ten artists in Scenes of Selves, 
Occasions for Ruses have created works using drawing, painting, 
photography, and video that explore the nature of self-identity at the beginning 
of the 21st century. Echoes of the Artist explores images in which artists 
creatively incorporate representations of themselves or aspects of their lives. 
Made, in part, of chandeliers of ghungru bells, Debashis Sinha’s Hati presents 
a meditative environment addressing the relationship between elephant 
communication, spirituality, and dance.

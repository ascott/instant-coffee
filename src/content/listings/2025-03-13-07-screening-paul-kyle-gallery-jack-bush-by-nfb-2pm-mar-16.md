Screening: 'Jack Bush' by National Film Board of Canada
Date: Sunday, March 16th
Time: Door opens 1:00 PM | Movie starts 2:00 PM
Location: Paul Kyle Gallery, 258 E 1st Ave. Unit 4 (Second floor), V5T 1A6

Join us for an afternoon film screening featuring a second viewing of the National
Film Board of Canada's documentary on Jack Bush in honour of our current
exhibition, "Jack Bush: Flaunting the Rules".
The late Canadian painter Jack Bush said he painted "from the belly." Born in
Montreal in 1909, he earned his living as a commercial artist until his work gained
recognition in the New York art market in 1968. In an interview he gave before his
death, Bush
talks about his life, his work, and the development of art in Canada over
the past 25 years. Exhibitions of his work are shown, in particular a retrospective at
which he and his friend Clement Greenberg, noted New York art critic, talk about his
paintings.

We are pleased to welcome our special guest and producer of the documentary,
Rudy Buttignol, who will be present to share his insights and stories in the making of
this film. RSVP is required to attend, so please visit our website at
www.paulkylegallery.com

For inquiries, please contact the gallery at info@paulkylegallery.com. Our office
hours are 11:30 AM to 5:30 PM from Tuesday to Saturday.

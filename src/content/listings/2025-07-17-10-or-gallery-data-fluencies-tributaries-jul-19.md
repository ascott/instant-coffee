Last days to visit Data Fluencies: Tributaries featuring artists Lani Asuncin,
JAZSALYN, Lai Yi Ohlsen, Kristoffer rum, Caroline Sinders and Roopa Vasudevan,
with experimental research by the Night School for Data Fluencies, DATA/FFECT,
and Data Fluencies Pedagogies, guest curated by Roopa Vasudevan for the Data
Fluencies Project on view until Saturday, July 19, 2025. Visit us 12:00-5:00pm
Wednesday to Saturday.

'Data Fluencies: Tributaries' features the works of six contemporary artists, alongside
experimental research supported by the Data Fluencies Project, based out of the
Digital Democracies Institute at Simon Fraser University. The exhibition aims to
provide open public engagement with the research outputs emerging from the larger
project and place them next to cutting-edge and critical work of artists examining the
same themes and ideas. Together, the artists and researchers featured here offer us
ways to (re)consider our relationships with the data that surrounds and drives our
everyday lives"and perhaps find new routes to agency once we are able to do so.

The discourse around data, machine learning, and artificial intelligence (AI) seems
to be moving around us at light speed. It can feel almost overwhelming to try and
parse all the latest developments. Every day, we are confronted with new modes
through which our behavior is tracked and surveilled, monetized, or used to classify
and categorize us in ways we might not even be able to comprehend. And with the
advent of generative AI, these discussions have only become more urgent as we
struggle to wrap our heads around questions of intellectual property, consentful tech
practice, and the impacts of automation on human existence at large. " Roopa
Vasudevan

Collage Fundamentals with Elizabeth Zvonar - Wednesdays Nov 13-20-27
Nov. 13, 6:30 p.m. - Nov. 27, 8:30 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada
In this 3-part series you'll go from experimenting with various collage techniques to
developing a finished work. Classes will have a fun workshop-like curriculum and will
incorporate a section for folks to discuss and comment on each others work, led by
Elizabeth Zvonar. Open to all skill levels.
Collage is a way of art making that embraces combining a wide variety of materials and
relies on good composition to make a finished work. The materials can be anything
including what you can find in your recycle bin and as such, its a generous and forgiving
medium with which to make art.
In this
3-part series you'll go from experimenting with various collage techniques to
developing a finished work. Classes will have a fun workshop-like curriculum and will
incorporate a section for folks to discuss and comment on each others work, all led by
renowned artist Elizabeth Zvonar. Open to all skill levels.
Class 1 (Nov 13): creative warm ups and experimentation/exploration of imagery by
rummaging through magazines
Class 2 (Nov 20): begin defining motifs and subject matter
Class 3 (Nov 27): create finished individual or a series of works

Judy Chartrand - Givin it Back 2017
Friday March 24th from 7-9 pm
Gallery Weekend
3412 22nd Street, San Francisco

Macaulay & Co. Fine Art is pleased to present Givin it Back 2017, a collection of new 
works by Judy Chartrand at our project space, Gallery Weekend in San Francisco. As 
the Western world experiences a major identity crisis, it faces pertinent issues 
surrounding race, poverty, and privilege. This is the time to have a closer look at the 
work of Judy Chartrand.

On view will be a collection of bowls, teacups, and soup cans. These domestic objects 
are humorously packed with challenges to re-think complacent stereotypes, and our 
perception of other. A ceramic bowl, embellished with a floral motif has at its centre, 
almost discreetly, GO BACK TO YOUR OWN COUNTRY! In contrast, another bowl is 
decorated with cockroaches, and an exasperated exclamation of FUCK!. Chartrand 
pulls back the curtain, and invites us to look closely at the places we call home. 

In 2016, Chartrand had a solo exhibition, What a Wonderful World, at Vancouver's Bill 
Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art. She has also shown with the Rennie Collection, 
(Vancouver, BC), The Glenbow Museum (Calgary, AB), Tuscon Museum of Art (Tuscon, 
AZ), Anchorage Museum of History & Art (Anchorage, AK) and Naples Museum of Art, 
(Naples, FL).

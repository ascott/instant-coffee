PANEL | PRACTICE
Les Ramsay, Cheyenne LeGrande, Zandi Dandizette and others

Thursday, October 24 I 5 - 7 pm
Rennie Hall I 2nd floor

ECU
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Join us for the first edition of this year's Practice series, featuring presentations
and discussions
with artists Les Ramsay (painitng), Zandi Dandizette (media art), Cheyenne Rain LeGrande
(performance) and more to be announced. Practice is a semi-annual series of lectures for
students and emerging artists. In this session, panelists from a wide variety of artistic
disciplines
share their practices and how they first emerged into their field.

The panel presentation will be followed by a moderated Q + A and breakout sessions. This
is a
free event, everyone welcome.

Practice is presented by the Audain Faculty of Art in partnership with the Faculty of Culture
+
Community and the Career + Professional Development Office.

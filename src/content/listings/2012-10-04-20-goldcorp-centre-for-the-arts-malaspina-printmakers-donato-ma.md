Donato Mancini
Resources
Opens on October 12 at 1pm
Closes on October 14 at 5pm
Goldcorp Centre for the Arts
149 West Hastings Street
Vancouver, Canada

Commissioned by Malaspina Printmakers for Institutions by Artists, the main 
work in this set, Resources (Cenotaph), draws from collaboratively compiled lists 
of artist run spaces, bars, and book- stores that formed, flourished and 
disappeared between the 1970s and the present day, revealing the relationship 
between the fugitive presence of artistic practice and the accelerated pace of 
urban development in Vancouver. Through the attendant works, the thematic 
range is broadened to pertinent questions of economy, labour, symbolic violence, 
and discursive relations of force.

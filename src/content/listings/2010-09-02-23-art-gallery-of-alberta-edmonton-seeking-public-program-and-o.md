Public Program and Outreach Coordinator

The Art Gallery of Alberta is looking for an innovative thinker who can help
to build audiences and excitement through the development and delivery of an
annual series of public programs that capture the public's imagination and
expand the experience of art and the museum.

The Public Programs and Outreach Coordinator is responsible for the
development and coordination of AGA public programs and community outreach
activities. Reporting to the Deputy Director/Chief Curator, the Public
Programs and Outreach Coordinator will produce a schedule of public programs,
events and community outreach activities that animate, complement and enhance
AGA exhibitions and collections.

Specific duties of the Public Programs and Outreach Coordinator will include:
the conceptual development, administration and implementation of
adult-focused public programs including: artists' talks, lectures, courses,
film series, symposia, performances, lunch hour programs and late night event
programming. The Public Programs and Outreach Coordinator will administer the
AGA's public and private exhibition tour programs, including tour scheduling,
staff hiring, training, scheduling and overall coordination. The Public
Programs and Outreach Coordinator will also develop and oversee special
community-based partnership initiatives and related special projects.

QUALIFICATIONS

The successful candidate will possess a university degree in art or art
history, a thorough knowledge of Canadian art history and issues in
contemporary art; 3-5 years working experience in a museum, public art
gallery and/or artist run centre as well as demonstrated experience in
program development and delivery. Candidates will have an ability to
communicate effectively, both orally and in writing; strong organizational
and administrative skills and the ability to work under general direction and
within a team environment.

Artist Talk, grunt gallery, Syrus Marcus Ware, NOV 30, 6 - 8 pm

Join us this Thursday, November 30, for a talk with Syrus Marcus Ware as he explores 
his Activist Portrait Series and his current research in the University of Victorias Trans 
Archives and amongst historic Black communities on Salt Spring Island. 

Syrus Marcus Ware is a Toronto-based artist and activist who is visiting the West Coast 
for a residency in Victoria, BC to do work and research with the largest archive of 
transgender history in the world - the results of which will be featured in an exhibition at 
grunt gallery in 2018. 

Ware's residency and exhibition are supported through the British Columbia Arts 
Council.

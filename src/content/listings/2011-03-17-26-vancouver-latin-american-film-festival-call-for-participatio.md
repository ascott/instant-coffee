Call for participation: Vancouver Latin American Film Festival

The Vancouver Latin American Film Festival (VLAFF) is pleased to announce
its first Youth Choice Award for a Fiction Film. Students from across BC
ages 18 to 30 (inclusive) are invited to apply to be part of a five-member,
youth-run jury, which will select the winner of the Youth Fiction Choice Award.

The jury will choose one winning fiction film out of ten films which will
be screened during the ninth edition of the Latin American Film Festival,
September 1-11, 2011.

The students who are selected will be recognized in VLAFF’s annual
catalogue and will received a Full Festival Pass, including an invitation
to the Opening and Closing Gala Events. The members of the jury must make a
commitment to see each of the ten fiction films and be available to meet
for at least two hours during the festival to make the selection.

Deadline: Monday, April 25, 2011 (All day)

How to apply:

The applicant must complete the attached application form and must submit a
500-word original essay reviewing the Argentinean film "Nacido y Criado"
("Born and Bred") which will be screened at the Pacific Cinematheque on
Sunday March 27 at 2 p.m. Applicants will have free admission to this film
screening. Please contact youthjury@vlaff.org to let us know that you will
be attending the screening, we will put your name on a comp list.

If the applicant is unable to attend the film screening, alternate
arrangement will be made for a private screening. Spaces for this screening
will be limited, so please contact us as soon as possible to save your spot.

The applicant must be between 18 and 30 years of age inclusive.

Application deadline: April 25th , 2011.

The members of the Youth Jury will be announced May 2nd , 2011.

For more information or to inquire about an application form, please feel
free to reach us at youthjury@vlaff.org .

Oct 26th, 2009 - This group exhibition at Gallery Gachet gives a unique
perspective into the heart and home of the Downtown Eastside community,
inviting artists to express and critique their inner and outer worlds.

Headlines Theatre held a photography contest to find an image for their
play, after homelessness... An image that spoke to what living in unsafe or
inadequate housing looked like; an image that illustrated the consequences
of being in a system that often does not work. This exhibition features the
top 10 photography entries; the winner has been selected by Headlines
Theatre and the 9 runners up by a jury from Gallery Gachet.

In Our Backyard: Oppenheimer Park Community Art Show
The 2nd Annual Community Art Show, -In Our Backyard- features inspired
works by artists and allies from the resilient and creative community in
and around Oppenheimer Park. Works include printmaking, painting,
traditional craft, installations, drawings, video and documentation,
reflecting the rapidly changing community. Show your spirit in the parade
on November 6th at 5pm from Oppenheimer Park to Gallery Gachet with the
Homeless Band, artists, community members and neighborhood families.

Hope in Shadows: portraits of our community
This exhibition is a selection of winning photographs from the annual Hope
in Shadows contest for Downtown Eastside residents. Oppenheimer Park is
the heart of the city for many local people, and has been represented in
winning images since the Hope in Shadows contest began in 2003. A tribute
to Oppenheimer Park, this retrospective shows a vibrant community
reflecting moments of camaraderie, play and tranquility.

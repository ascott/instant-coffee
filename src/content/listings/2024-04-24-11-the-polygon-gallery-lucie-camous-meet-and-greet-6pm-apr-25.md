About Lucie Camous
In Camous artistic and curatorial practice, they adopt a political perspective and
work at the crossroads of artistic, theoretical, and militant forms. Mechanisms of
power, dynamics of resistance and situated knowledge are notions that question and
sustain all of their artistic efforts. Camous approach, rooted in intimate narratives,
evolves around norms, their borders and the sensitive issues related to their desire
to cross them. The experimentation and the elaboration of collective work practices
informed the entire process of their projects script. In 2019, Camous co-founded
Modle vivant.e, alongside Hlne Fromen, an artist and researcher and Linda
DeMorrir, a DJ and
model. Modle vivant.e is an experimental and transfeminist
collection of illustrations representing dissident bodies.

About the Paris/Vancouver Curatorial Residency
Alternating annually for four years between Paris and Vancouver, the aim of the
program is to provide opportunities to develop
curatorial projects and foster cultural
exchange in response to contemporary art contexts in France and Western Canada.
The program was established in October 2021, and this is its fourth iteration. Past
residents include: Missla Libsekal (2021), Amy Kazymerchyk (2021), Clelia
Coussonnet (2022), and Anne Bourrass (2023).

Residents are hosted at Griffin Art Projects during their tenure. The residency
provides travel, accommodation, and a stipend for three months, as well as a
production budget to launch a public program, during which Bourrass will have the
opportunity to share her findings from her research in Vancouver.

OPENING | Kelowna Art Gallery | What is Fragile? | 6PM | APR 26
What is Fragile? Victoria Verge and zev tiefenbach

April 20 to July 12, 2024

Two artists explore the question What is Fragile? as part of a new presentation at
the Kelowna Art Gallery. Kelowna-based artist Victoria Verge and Salmon Arm-based
artist zev tiefenbach showcase their recent work, created during their Master of Fine
Arts candidacy at UBC Okanagan.

Visitors will encounter interactive sculptures by Verge in the space that are motion
activated. She titles these sculptural meditations Chasing the Echoes of Home.
Accompanying these works is a large installation featuring wallpaper and vintage
furniture. Across the exhibition space, teifenbach has meticulously assembled a
collection of photographs and videos called these are fragile days. Viewers are
invited to flip through this personal archive from the artist and curate their own
narrative
while contemplating the intricacies of memory in their own lives.

Through their unique artistic explorations, Verge and tiefenbach shed light on how
fragile the human spirit can be, says Curator Christine May. Visitors to the
exhibition are encouraged to think deeply about how art can transform us, and the
important role that artists play in shaping today's social and cultural stories.

The Kelowna Art Gallery isnt the only place to see work by this years batch of MFA
Students from UBC Okanagan. At the Lake Country Art Gallery, Jessie Emilie and
Troy Teichrib are exhibiting their large-scale paintings from May 18 to July 14, 2024.

Through a range of mediums, these students are offering visitors the opportunity to
explore the next generation of contemporary art across a variety of styles, says
Wanda Lock, Curator at the Lake Country Art Gallery.

Through their work, Verge and tiefenbach explore the delicate interplay of memory,
movement, and resilience. An Opening Reception has been
planned for Friday, April
26, 2024, from 6 to 8 pm, at the Kelowna Art Gallery. The event is free and open to
the public to attend. Both artists will be on hand.

Victoria Verge is a multi-medium artist of settler and Mikmaw ancestry, currently
residing on the unceded and traditional territory of the syilx/Okanagan people. In
2016, she obtained a BFA from Memorial University of Newfoundland, specializing in
visual arts with a minor in art history. She is currently in her second year as an MFA
candidate at UBC Okanagan.

zev tiefenbach was born in Toronto as a second-generation Canadian, whose
grandparents were holocaust survivors. He is a settler artist living on unceded
secwpemc territory. tiefenbach has a BFA from Concordia University with a focus in
Photography and Creative Writing. He is currently an MFA Candidate at UBC
Okanagan.

What is Fragile? runs from April 20 to July 12, 2024.

The Kelowna Art Gallery is located at 1315 Water Street, in downtown Kelowna, B.C.

Award-winning clarinetist, bass clarinetist and composer James Danderfer has impressed
audiences around the world with his warm sound, melodic compositions and lyrical
improvising style. James
has performed and recorded with some of the finest jazz
musicians in North America including Steve Nelson, Quincy Davis, John Stetch, Brad
Turner, Joel Ross, Oliver Gannon, and Don Thompson. Besides composing music for his
own large and small ensembles, James is also a LEO award-winning composer for film
and enjoys the collaborative nature of this medium. To hear/see James' music, visit
jamesdanderfer.com

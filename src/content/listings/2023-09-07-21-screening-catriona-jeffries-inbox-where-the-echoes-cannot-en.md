Where the echoes cannot end is a screening of videos by artists Judy Radul, Andrew
Yong Hoon Lee, and Steven Cottingham, featuring work that crosses the genres of
music video and video essay. In these works, sound becomes illustrative and visuals
take on a supportive, emotive role. Each artist explores conditions of industry and
media through the motifs of echoes, entanglement, and noise.

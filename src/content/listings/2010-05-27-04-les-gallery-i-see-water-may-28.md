i see water
New works by Colleen Heslin
Opening reception - Friday May 28th, 7:00 - 11:00
May 28th - June 28th

LES Gallery
1879 Powell st.
www.lesgallery.ca

I see water is a comical take on landscape painting and contemporary
lifestyles. Colleen Heslin looks at water as an object, destination, or
means towards a destination and our attraction and romanticization of these
sites in modern society. This body of work stems from historical painting
as a documentary means to capture vernacular moments in time and space
while playing with common motifs in contemporary snapshot photography.
Whimsical and ambiguous gestures are prominent; a splash in a river, or a
concealed hump on a couch hint towards a narrative subtext while selected
objects shape and inform notions of identity and relationships within
modern interiors and exterior rural environments.

Miriam Aroeste - Etherial Embrace
Opening reception:

602 E Hastings Street

Artist in attendance

Miriam Aroeste's paintings utilize the power of colour, line and space to depict 
authentic, spontaneous emotion and experience in abstract ways. The new 
works in the exhibition have both visual appeal as well as an underlying 
conceptual basis, drawing from the inner workings of the mind as well as 
notions of impulse and defence.

Aroeste has exhibited in both solo and group exhibitions in Canada, USA, 
Mexico and France. She has been involved in the arts for over twenty years 
in many different capacities. Her work is held in collections in Canada, the 
United States, Mexico, Israel, Italy and elsewhere.

Thursday, June 27, 7:00-8:00pm 

Conversations on Collecting: Acts of Care in Collecting

Griffin Art Projects, 1174 Welch St, North Vancouver, BC V7P 2R5

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Griffin Art Projects founders Henning and Brigitte Freybe and Vancouver-based collector 
Bruce Wright, who support art in Vancouver and internationally, will discuss their 
histories of collecting and their decades-long relationships with artists in a moderated, 
round table format. Building on one of Griffins mandates to bring privately held art 
collections to public access, this new and ongoing series, presented in partnership with 
the Contemporary Art Society Vancouver, will open up the process of collecting to the 
public.

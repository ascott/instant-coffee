New Media Gallery is pleased to offer a a Drop-In Learning Lab for art-related projects 
and experimentation with art-related technologies. Collaborate with friends or bring a 
project of your own. Sundays are for ages 6 - 11. Saturdays are for ages 12+ to Adults. 
Our Qualified instructors are artists and makers with tech experience. They are 
available to answer questions, help operate or learn about equipment and modular 
technologies, or just chat about the direction of your own creative project. Our facilities 
are equipped to help you along with your projects and include basic video production, 
sound production, 3D modelling, 3D printing, Virtual reality, and coding, Little Bits, and 
more.. We do not have the facilities for soldering. Drop-in fees are $10.00 per person. 
On Saturdays Children under 12 years old must be accompanied by an adult. On 
Sundays children 6+ can be dropped off for up to 2 hours. 

Learning Labs:
Saturdays, October 27th - Nov 24th from 10am - 2pm
Sundays, October 28th - Nov 25th from 10am - 2pm

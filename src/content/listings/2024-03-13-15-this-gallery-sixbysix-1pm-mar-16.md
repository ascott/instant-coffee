Join us for SIXBYSIX, an innovative group exhibition featuring over 70 artists and
152 unique 6x6-inch artworks. Crafted from diverse materials and unrestricted
creativity, each piece adheres to one simple rule: make a work of art no larger than 6
inches wide.

Artists transformed a 6x6-inch wooden panel into traditional surfaces, shadow
boxes, sculptural bases, or even containers, embracing the challenge with some
very creative approaches!

SIXBYSIX is free and open to all. Join our opening reception in person on Saturday,
March 16, from 1-4 pm, or explore and purchase work online at thethisgallery.com.
The online collection drops on Friday, March 15.

As a unique twist, artworks can be taken home upon purchase, allowing the exhibit
to evolve throughout the week. Early visitors will experience the full exhibition, while
later arrivals will discover an ever-changing (dwindling) display.

Arrive early so don't miss your chance to own an original
piece of art for just $300!

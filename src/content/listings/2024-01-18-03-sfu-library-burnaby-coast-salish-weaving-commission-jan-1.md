W.A.C. Bennett Library,
Fourth Floor, North Side

The Indigenous Curriculum Resource Centre (ICRC) assists the SFU teaching
community with Indigenizing and decolonizing their curriculum. Existing as an online
resource since 2020, the ICRC launches its new physical space in January 2024 on
the fourth floor of the W.A.C. Bennett
Library on SFUs Burnaby campus. The ICRC
aims to incorporate Indigenous knowledge and ways of knowing into the practices of
the institution, and redress historical and current practices that have centered Western
learning and caused immense harm to Indigenous peoples.

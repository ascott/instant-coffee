Karin Buba: Garden of Shadows

September 23, 2023 " January 29, 2024

Garden of Shadows is an exploration of the artists ongoing Studies of Women in
Landscapes series, featuring enigmatic photographs of women in remote outdoor
settings, alongside her innovative photographic sculptures that layer images to depict
West Coast forests and floral arrangements. Both types of work invite viewers to
contemplate the hidden aspects of nature and human experience.

Book Signing with the Artist: September 23 from 11am to 12pm at the Audain Art
Museum.

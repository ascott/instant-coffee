Afternoon Projects is thrilled to present What I felt for you was love, a group exhibition
featuring work from Qian Cheng, Jonathan Xue, Sofia Yao and Caroline Zhang. This
group exhibition showcases a collection of painting and drawing work centred around
intimate experiences formed by feelings of understanding, hoping, and believing, not
out of conviction, but from lived experience.

Artist: Qian Cheng, Jonathan Xue, Sofia Yao and Caroline Zhang

Exhibition Title: What I felt for you was love

Exhibition Dates: July 15 - August 15

Hours: 1 - 5 PM Thursday - Saturday or by appointments

VENUE: Afternoon Projects (603 Powell St, Vancouver)
GALLERY HOURS: Thursdays, Fridays, Saturdays 1 - 5 pm & rest of the week by

Macaulay Fine Art presents:

Brad Phillips | The Rope at the End of the Rope

Opening reception: Thursday, January 23, 6-8 pm
Exhibition: January 23 - March 1st, 2014

Macaulay Fine Art presents The Rope at the End of the Rope, a new body of 
work by Brad Phillips.

Brad Phillips (b. 1974) is a Canadian artist and writer currently living and 
working in Ontario. He is best known for photorealist works concerned with 
dark, autobiographical themes as well as text-based works commenting on 
and generating dialogue with literary tropes and references. Phillips has 
shown extensively both nationally and internationally; his work has been 
shown at the Museum of Contemporary Canadian Art, the Museum of Comic 
and Cartoon Art in New York, Groeflin Maag Galerie in Zurich and La Maison 
Rouge in Paris, among others. Phillips' work is included in the art collections of 
Twentieth Century Acquisitions, Schiff Art Projects, Royal Bank of Canada 
and the Glenbow Museum, as well as others. His writing has been featured 
in Hunter and Cook and Whitehot Magazine.

Artist will be in attendance at the opening.

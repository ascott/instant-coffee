The Monte Clark Gallery (Vancouver) is pleased to announce a joint
exhibition with Clark & Faria (Toronto) entitled RESTRICTED. Based on
the film rating commonly used to disclose explicit content, RESTRICTED
will show work that examines voyeurism, the body, and sexuality. The
Vancouver exhibition will feature new work by Evan Lee and Hye Rim
Lee, as well as vintage photographs by Larry Clark and Hosoe.

Evan Lee's series of Flashers presents appropriated self-shot
erotica from the internet and highlights the inherent complexities of
these amateur looking shots. The camera flash itself is also a
prominent subject in the work in all of the images collected by Lee,
the on-camera flash is reflected in the mirror and results in a white
overexposed area which obscures the identity of the subject.

Korean born artist Hye Rim Lee is the self-proclaimed "next Jeff
Koons". For the past several years, she has explored contemporary
popular culture through computer-generated images and videos which she
classifies as the TOKI / Cyborg Project. TOKI is a mythical character
who reflects a highly-stylized image of Western beauty and sexuality,
as well draws upon the traditions of Japanese manga and Korean
animamix. In the video Crystal City Spun, Hye Rim Lee's work included
in RESTRICTED, TOKI and her dragon friend YONG explore a sensational
cityscape made up of giant spinning "dildo towers".

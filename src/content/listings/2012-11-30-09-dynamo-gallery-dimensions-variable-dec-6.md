Dynamo Gallery
opening reception December 6, 7pm

Jordan Milner, Gabi Dao, Kara Hansen & Graham Landin

Dynamo Gallery is pleased to announce Dimensions Variable. This exhibition 
gathers four local emerging artists whose works evoke material and 
conceptual cross overs. Similarly, their respective individual practices diverge 
within the interest of dissecting spatial illusions. Dimensions Variable 
presents various considerations, understandings and approaches through the 
works of Jordan Milner, Gabi Dao, Kara Hansen, and Graham Landin. Through 
considering conversations surrounding the objecthood of painting, sculpture, 
and collage, Dimensions Variable proposes that the failures of one specific 
medium is picked up by its opposite in order to maintain that art is an illusion 
of space. Akin to Lucio Fontana's Concept Spatiale in 1959, Milner, Dao, 
Hansen and Landin's gestures on material surfaces are unapologetically 
interrupted with another visible variable. The works in the exhibition tether on 
the edge of multiple dichotomic practices with seemingly intertwined artistic 
intentions. 

Collectively the works presented in Dimensions Variable span a diverse field 
of influences including advertising aesthetics, abstraction, and modernist 
traditions that are questioned within contemporary art. Milner, Dao, Hansen 
and Landin's work all stem out of a trajectory of art that understands how 
materiality can also expose the fragility of artistic deception. Like advertising 
and design, their artwork proposes itself as a lens on the society it engages 
with. Dimensions Variable embraces this relationship in order to investigate 
the autonomy contemporary art assumes within culture.

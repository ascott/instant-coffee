Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre
6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby BC

Celebrate this winter and kick off your holiday shopping season with locally-crafted 
items and baked goods during the third weekend in November with over 85 art, craft, 
baked goods, and food booths! 

Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre will host local crafts people from all 
across Metro Vancouver on Saturday, November 17th and Sunday, November 18th.

Our selection includes delightful knitted woolen items, one of a kind hand-made 
jewelry, imaginatively designed greeting cards, useful hand-made goods, nice 
smelling soap and bath bombs, fun gifts for your loving pets, fresh homemade 
Japanese sweets and Christmas treats, and so much more!

A unique characteristic of the Fair will include free workshops such as Hula Dance 
by Wailele Wai Wai, and Japanese traditional games, Igo and Shogi on Saturday, 
November 17th. There will be craft workshops offered onsite for children and adults 
on Sunday.

There will be Santa photo shoot opportunities, and a kids craft zone throughout both 
days.

The fair is from 10am to 5pm daily. Admission is $3; children 12 and under + 
NNMCC members are admitted free.

For more information, call 604.777.7000 or visit nikkeiplace.org.

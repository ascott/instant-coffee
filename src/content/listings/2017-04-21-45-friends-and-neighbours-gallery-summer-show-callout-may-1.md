*Deadline for all submissions: May 1st, 2017 at midnight

Friends & Neighbours is an artist-run gallery. It is dedicated to providing an
alternative exhibition space for artists outside of, or, on the fringes of the art
world. The gallery aims to connect and initiate dialogues with its outlying
community. Priority will be given to submissions from artists who identify as
BIPOC, LGBTQ2+, genderqueer, non-binary and/or cis women.

We are preparing for a group show in July 2017 and looking for submissions of
finished work or works-in-progress. If interested please send all digital submission
materials in one zip folder to:

friendsandneighboursgallery@gmail.com

Submission Guidelines:

Contact information
Artist Statement and exhibition proposal
A maximum of 5 jpeg images of the proposed work or examples of previous work
(or active links to video work)
A descriptive list for the visual material (title, date, dimension, medium)
Identify each image file or video with a number and a title corresponding to the
descriptive list Technical requirements for your proposed work

*If you have any questions related to the submission process, dont hesitate to
contact us via email.

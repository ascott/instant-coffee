VIVO WORKSHOPS: www.vivomediaarts.com/workshops
REGISTRATION DEADLINE: One week prior to the workshop

CIRCUIT BUILDING
2 sessions: 7 hours
Mon/Wed March 7 & 9, 6-9:30 PM

Cost: $160 or $130 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: David Leith
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

A base knowledge of electronics gives the artist the opportunity to
introduce electronic circuitry into a variety of media including sculpture,
sound, and light-based works. This course will cover some basic theory of
electronic principals that is essential for beginning to work with this
medium. From there we will look at some of the components that make up
simple circuits and that create the building blocks for more complex
circuits. Students will learn some of the basic skills needed for
electronic work such as soldering, breadboarding, and reading a schematic.
These skills will be used to build a simple audio amplifier. We will
experiment with different inputs such as contact microphones and how to
modify the sound output from the speaker.

The cost of materials that you take home with you ($10-15) is included in
the workshop fee.

CIRCUIT BENDING
2 sessions: 7 hours
Mon/Tue March 14 & 16, 6-9:30 PM

Cost: $160 or $130 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: David Leith
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

Circuit bending enables artists to work with complex circuitry without any
prior knowledge of electronics in order to create works spanning a variety
of artistic media. By adding new variables we can transform and customize
circuits that would be too complex for most of us to build from the ground
up. We will cover all aspects to get you started with circuit bending, from
prying open your first case to putting the knobs on a completed project.
Bring 1 or 2 circuits to bend and any parts or modifying elements that you
would like to use (we will have some modifiers on hand). Here are some
ideas for circuit bending starting points: keyboards, radios, answering
machines, portable televisions and tape decks. Don't let that limit you
though. It's going to be exciting.

The cost of materials that you take home with you ($10-15) is included in
the workshop fee.

AFTER EFFECTS
3 session: 9 hours
Mon/Wed/Fri March 7, 9 & 11, 6-9 PM

Cost: $200 or $160 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: Majid Bagheri
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This workshop provides a fundamental understanding of post-production
workflow in Adobe After Effects. It covers a range of subjects including
compositing in 2d and 3d, working with video and graphics, titling, visual
effects, keying, and animation. Lab handouts and exercise files will be
provided.

Prerequisite: Basic knowledge of digital video and editing software (e.g.
iMovie, Adobe Premiere, Final Cut Pro, Avid).

FINAL CUT PRO - INTRO
2 sessions: 8 hours
Tue/Thu March 8 & 10, 6-10 PM

Cost: $200 or $160 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: Mark Curry
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This workshop provides an overview of Final Cut Pro 6 from capturing video
to printing to tape. It covers all the basics including starting and
organizing a project, log and capture (DV), basic editing, trim editing,
introduction to transitions and filters, titling, audio tools, and
exporting to tape.

Prerequisite: Basic Mac literacy

CAMERA, LIGHTS & SOUND
3 sessions: 11 hours
Thu March 10, 6-9 PM + Sat March 12, 12-5 PM + Sun March 13, 12-3 PM

Cost: $260 or $210 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructors: Dinka Pignon and Adesegun Ade-Martins
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337

This three-day hands-on workshop introduces students to the Sony PD-150 and
PD-170 digital camera packages, as well as the Sony HVR-Z5U high definition
camera. We will cover technical aspects of camera operation, sound
recording, and basic lighting principles. There will be a lot of
opportunity for practical exercise with your newly acquired skills.

MaxMSP
3 sessions: 6 hours
Fri March 11, 6-8pm + Sat/Sun March 12 & 13, 1-3pm

Cost: $160 or $130 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: Brady Marks
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

MaxMSP is a visual programming language for working with digital media in
an interactive way. It is mainly used for interactive installation art or
live, performative audio-visual art forms. You can find out more about
MaxMSP at www.cycling74.com.

Session I: Data Flow as a Programming Paradigm. Comparison of Data Flow
Tools (MaxMSP, PureData & Isadora). Nuts and Bolts of Programming with MaxMSP.

Session II: Gestural Programming & Sub Patches. Sound Synthesis and Audio
Playback in MaxMSP. Video and 3D Graphics in MaxMSP.

Session III: Abstrations & External Objects. Interfacing MaxMSP with
Arduino Boards.

Note: Please bring your own laptop if you have one.

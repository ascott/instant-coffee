The Portrait Gallery of Canada will be launching a new national beinnial commissioning programme at the Contemporary Art Gallery on Tuesday, May 6th from 5:30 - 7:30 pm. All are welcome to attend. The program offers a $25,000 commission to an artist, and information on the application process will be available at the May 6th event. A general call for nominations will be held to choose the subject of the commissioned portrait.

Dr. Lilly Koltun, Director General of the Portrait Gallery of Canada will be in attendance.

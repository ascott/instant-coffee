The Contemporary Art Society is delighted to announce the opening of an exhibition featuring the 5 finalists of the Emerging Artists Prize.  They are: Jeremy Hof, Scott Billings, Nicole Ondre, Jordy Hamilton and Natalie Purschwitz. Please join us on Thursday May 31st at 8pm at Access Gallery located at 222 East Georgia Street.  The winner of the prize will be announced at 8:30pm.

LIVE2009 – Art that has to be brilliant!
www.livebiennale.ca

Join us for LIVE2009, Canada’s largest and most diverse Performance Art festival.

LIVE 2009 features over 40 performances by the best and brightest of the world’s contemporary 
artists across Canada and 11 other countries who explore new, experimental, and experiential 
ways to challenge how we see, think and act, at venues and locations throughout Vancouver.

With over two weeks of dynamic, eclectic and provocative performances we needed two events to 
launch this festival right:

1) Body Plural, Thursday, October 15, 2009 at 8pm
Hastings Steam & Sauna, 766 East Hastings St., Vancouver
A hot night with a bevy of performances by Naúfus Ramirez-Figueroa, Rocco Trigueros, 
Francisco-Fernando Granados, Paul Couillard, Todd Janes, and Velveeta Krisp.
$10 cover (free for women)

2) LIVE Gala Cabaret Benefit, Friday, October 16, 2009
Chapel Arts, 304 Dunlevy Ave., Vancouver
A cornucopia of craziness…this night will be filled with frivolous, fun, and courage performances
by Priscilla Costa, Francisco-Fernando Granados, Makiko Hara, Manolo Lugo, Payday Millionaire, 
Heidi Nategaal, Joomi Seo, Norico Sunayama, and Velvis! Plus we’ll be auctioning off 
performances or artifacts donated by local artists.
$10 cover

Call for Applications
The Leeway Studio Residency

Summer Term: May - August

The Leeway, a membership-based community studio at Emily Carr University of
Art + Design, invites visual artists and designers to apply for a
four-month residency.

Studio residents have access to a range of professional equipment and
receive support from our dedicated team of studio technicians with a range
of expertise. Studio residents work alongside our studio members and take
on an active role in the studio community, from attending public open
studios, to hosting lunch hour artist talks or taking part in workshops.
Studio residents are able to take advantage of the cross-disciplinary
amenities in The Leeway in a way that best suits their practice.

In addition to having access to The Leeway, studio residents may receive
coverage of their work in Emily Carr University of Art + Design's
communication channels, including social media and ecuad.ca. Studio
residents are also eligible to receive reimbursement for up to $200 towards
material and/or production costs.

LEEWAY EQUIPMENT

iMac with Adobe CS 6; Epson Perfection V600 Photo Scanner; Epson Stylus Pro
Wide Format Printers (up to 17" wide); Canon EOS Rebel T3i 18mp Digital
camera; 500W Hensel Integra 500 kit; Pfaff 1040 Sewing Machine; Showcard
Table Top Proofing Press with wood and lead type; Aaxa P300 Pico Projector;
Charles Brand Etching Press (20" by 40" bed).

ELIGIBILITY* + COMMITMENTS

This residency is for artists or designers with an active practice and
online presence (blog, website or social media). Residents must commit to
working in the studio for a minimum of 12 hours a week, participate in a
community outreach activity such as an artist talk, workshop or exhibition
during the residency term, as well as maintain a social media presence with
regular posts sharing information such as: work in progress, events,
workshops, etc.

Please note: the residency does not include travel, accommodation, artist
fees or access to Emily Carr facilities beyond the Leeway [ie: no shop
access (wood, metal, print)].

* Previous Leeway Studio Residency recipients are not eligible to apply.

HOW TO APPLY

Submit your application online through Slideroom. Applicants should be
prepared to submit a complete CV, 5-10 images of their work and answer
several questions about their current practice.

Deadline for Summer 2016 Term Residency: March 15, 2016

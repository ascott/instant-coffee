FADING LANDSCAPES: THE FILMS OF LINDA FENSTERMAKER
In Person!Saturday March 21
Doors 7:30, Screening 8pm (PWYC)
Vista Point Studios 1112 Franklin Street, Alley Entrance

Iris Film Collective in collaboration with Vista Point Studios are very excited to have Linda
Fenstermaker in person to present her impressionistic experimental works created on 16mm
and using many in-camera techniques. Linda
Fenstermaker is a filmmaker who works primarily
on 16mm film. Her work explores interactions and relationships between body and landscape
with a focus on representing organic food systems and empowered women. In addition to
filmmaking, Linda has curated and co-curated multiple programs, including Ovular Films and
the experimental program for Local Sightings Film Festival in Seattle, as well as been a visiting
artist at The Evergreen State College, Seattle University, The University of Washington and The
University of New Mexico, and an Artist in Residence at Basement Films in New Mexico.
Currently, Linda lives in the agricultural area of Skagit County in Washington State.

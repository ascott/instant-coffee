Vancouver Status of Women is looking for artwork to include in the 10th
edition of our Single Mother's Resource Guide 2016-17. We are looking for
one cover art and several kid's drawings. An honorarium of $200 will be
given to the cover art artist. Preference will be given to artists who are
single moms and children from Indigenous and racialized single mom
families. To contribute, please send your art and drawings with a short
description of yourself and your contact to womencentre@vsw.ca or deliver
to the VSW by March 10, 2016. For more information and to learn more about
the Guide, visit our website at www.vsw.ca or call 604-255-6554.

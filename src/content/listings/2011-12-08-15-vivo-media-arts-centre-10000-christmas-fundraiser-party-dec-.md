A Preamble on VIVO Media Arts Centre :

VIVO Media Arts Centre, originally founded in 1973 as the Satellite 
Video Exchange Society (SVES) and later known as VideoIn/VideoOut, 
is a non-profit, artist-run collective with charitable status supporting and 
responding to the needs of independent media artists. The 
organization is dedicated to exploring the potential of new technologies 
in art and examining current technological evolutions in a critical 
context. Professional production and performance facilities are offered 
at affordable rates to artists and producers as well as a wide range of 
related services and workshops.

VIVO promotes Canadian work in an international context and engages 
in a cross-cultural exchange of ideas, including creative initiatives. With 
a 38-year history in Canadian arts programming, VIVO is directly 
involved in community building, creating affordable access to video and 
digital production technology, developing new audiences and 
producing non-mainstream, fresh innovative works.

Special Curators Tour for Group Exhibition 'Woven Work From Near Here' as part of BC Culture 
Days | Fte de la culture

Where: grunt gallery #116 - 350 East 2nd Ave. Vancouver, BC
When: Saturday, September 29, @ 2 PM 

There will be a special curatorial tour with curators Emily Hermant & T'ai Smith in conjunction 
with BC Culture Days that takes place over the weekend of the September 28-30. grunt 
gallery's exhibition Woven Work From Near Here shows works from a diverse range of artists 
situated in the Pacific Northwest. Inspired by traditional practices"including Indigenous 
methods for weaving blankets and baskets alongside structures and patterns that come from 
elsewhere"the exhibition presents recent experiments by eight artists. Using materials and 
methods that stretch what it means to be a woven textile, the works in the show signal the 
potential of this practice to realign political protocols and alter cultural values.

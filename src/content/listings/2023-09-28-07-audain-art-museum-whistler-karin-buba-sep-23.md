Garden of Shadows is an exploration of Karin Buba' ongoing Studies of Women in
Landscapes series, featuring thought-provoking photographs of women in remote
outdoor settings, alongside her innovative layered photographic sculptures which
depict West Coast forests and floral arrangements. Both types of work invite viewers
to contemplate the hidden aspects of nature and human experience.

In the Studies of Women in Landscapes series, the interplay between nature and
culture comes alive through the carefully selected haute couture-inspired clothing
worn by Buba' models. These elements echo the landscapes tone and subtly
contrast surrounding flora.

Buba toles draw viewers deeper into her artistic practice, and reference the works
of 19th century French painter douard Manet.
These meticulously assembled
three-dimensional pieces bring fragility and depth to still-life arrangements, casting
shadows that blur the line between reality and illusion.

Karin Buba: Garden of Shadows offers viewers an immersive experience that
challenges perceptions and invites contemplation. See it in Whistler this weekend.

Request for Qualifications
Call for Artists - Capstan Village Park
Pinnacle International
Richmond, BC
Budget: $100,000
Eligibility: Open to Canadian professional artists
Deadline: July 15, 2015 - 12 p.m. (PST)

This national call is in search of a professional artist who can work with
the design team collaboratively to integrate work into the play area of an
urban neighbourhood park in Richmond, BC, during the design development
stage. The artist will develop a concept after being selected.

At a conceptual level, artists will be tasked with exploring how artwork
can enhance the play experience universally, highlighting the importance of
play as a mechanism for learning and strengthening social interconnection.

For more information and submission guidelines:
[http://www.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/capstanvillage41767.pdf](http://www.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/capstanvillage41767.pdf)

For questions regarding the submission requirements or process, contact
Emily Nixon via email at Emily@dkl.bc.ca<mailto:Emily@dkl.bc.ca>.

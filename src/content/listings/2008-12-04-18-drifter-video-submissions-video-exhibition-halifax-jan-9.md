Drifter Video is asking for submissions for a group video exhibition
planned for January. We are looking for submissions from students, emerging
and established artists and videographers who work in single-channel video.
Deadline for submission is Friday January 9, 2009.
The theme for January's screening is the artist's body. We are looking
for videos in which the artist's body acts as the simultaneous subject and
object of the work.
We accept MiniDV and non-copyright DVD, please include a CV and brief
artist statement.
Please send exhibition copies only.
Only materials that include a self-addressed, stamped envelope
(Canadian postage) will be returned.
Unfortunately, Artist Fees cannot be paid.
questions can be directed to driftervideo8@gmail.com

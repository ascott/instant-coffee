See Sounds Listening Series: Annie Chiavaroli
Friday October 21st, 2016, 7pm
Hadden Park Field House, 1015 Maple St, Vancouver

This is the eleventh monthly group listening session at the field 
house run by artist collective Publik Secrets. Bring something you 
would like to listen to together. Discussion will follow. We will have 
most available formats (digital, vinyl, CD, tape, radio). 

Meet at the field house at 7pm. Our guest presentation will start at 
7:30. Following that will be a short break, and then we will open the 
floor for other participants to share what they brought. It will be a 
phones off environment! ** We are moving our events to Friday 
evening, and have decided to start earlier **

As part of our See Sounds series, we are featuring a guest artist 
each listening party to curate the early portion of the evening. We 
have invited artist Annie Chiavaroli to be this month's guest. Annie 
is a transdisciplinary settler artist, reconciling artistic practice with 
obligate symbiosis.

Publik Secrets is comprised of a team of musicians, fabricators, 
and visual artists. our work creatively re-imagines public spaces as 
participatory gathering places. we strive to invoke wonder and play 
through multi-disciplinary, community-driven arts. with a focus on 
participation, we create collective experiences through projects that 
reunite art with the daily life of the community
we are located in unceded xmkwym, Swxw7mesh, and 
Sllwta territories, Vancouver, British Columbia

The Insurance Man: Kafka in the Penal Colony

Please join us! This is the final set of talks on the occasion of the SFU Gallery exhibition "The
Insurance Man: Kafka in the Penal Colony".

Tuesday May 26, 12:05, with Bill Jeffries
Wednesday May 27, 12:35, with Bill Jeffries
Thursday, May 28, 12:35, with Jerry Zaslove
Saturday June 13, 1:00, with Jerry Zaslove
Thursday, June 25, 12:35, with Jerry Zaslove
(Talks for classes/groups at other times by arrangement.)

Kafka Film Festival 
June 22 to 25 at the VanCity Theatre, 1181 Seymour Street in Vancouver.
Including Orson Welles’ The Trial , Michael Haneke’s The Castle, and Steven Soderbergh’s KAFKA, 
starring Jeremy Irons, Ian Holm and Alec Guinness, plus award-winning animated short films. See 
VanCity Theatre website for details. Jerry Zaslove will introduce the series on opening night.

The exhibition is conceived by Jerry Zaslove and and organized Jerry Zaslove & Bill Jeffries.

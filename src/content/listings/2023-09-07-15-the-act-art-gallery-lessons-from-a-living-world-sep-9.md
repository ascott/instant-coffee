Lessons from a Living World

[https://theactmapleridge.org/lessons-from-a-living-world](https://theactmapleridge.org/lessons-from-a-living-world)

September 9 " November 11, 2023

Opening Reception: Saturday, September 9, 2-4pm

The ACT Art Gallery

11944 Haney Place

Maple Ridge

Lessons from a Living World brings together five artists whose art practices center on
their relationships, responsibilities, and hope for the Earth. Artists Cherry Archer,
q"'t"ic"' / Phyllis Atkins, Clyde Richard Brittain, Shawna Davis, and Zuzana Vasko have
differing connections with the environment, yet all share a deep awareness of the living
world around them. Through mediums of beadwork, installation, photography, painting,
and printmaking, the artworks in this exhibition muse on the beauty of nature with a
stark reminder of humanitys dependence on these ecosystems. In a time of ongoing
ecological crisis, the exhibited artists suggest that lessons theyve learned from the
land have revealed information on how to better take care of it, and ourselves.

NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS form public constellations of engaged 
interdisciplinary practice, unmediated by the realpolitik conditions of contemporary art as 
a competitive professional sphere. The interrelatedness of art, society and everyday life 
is critically explored amongst a diverse plurality of artists and publics. Jeremy Todd 
instigates each event as an extension of his ongoing Not Sent Letters project, an 
entanglement with the production of meaning, self and cultural memory, involving 
image/text epistolary detours online (since 2005), digital shorts, interdisciplinary 
performance works and cooperatively realized public events: 
www.notsentlettersproject.com

This is the twenty-fourth NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS event.

ANDREW WITT, a Vancouver-based art historian, contributes to NSL&G for the first 
time with the letter I tried the door and it was unlocked ...". Written during a research 
trip to Los Angeles, Witt's letter discusses the collaborative photography of Maya Deren 
and Alexander Hammid, the art writings of Marcel Proust, the memoirs of Christa Wolf, 
the architecture of Richard Neutra, the aesthetics of friendship, the epiphanic and 
ecstatic. 

ELIZABETH MILTON is a performance and media artist who lives as a guest on the 
unceded territory of the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations in 
Vancouver, BC. Milton returns to NSL&G to debut Pink Noise, a performance work 
investigating screen space, materiality and the maximalist spectacle of video distortion. 

DANIEL CODE & SEAN ARDEN, two Vancouver-based multi-disciplinary artists & 
friends, collaborate with ANTHEM JACKSON PRODUCTIONS to debut Forever 
Spaghetti, a response to the citys shrinking cultural landscape. This interactive work 
immortalizes a moment in time at Nicks Spaghetti House Restaurant (a pasta and 
meatball institution that has bled starchy Calabrese and Neapolitan tradition for over 
sixty-three years). As sterile glass and steel buildings entirely displace histories of 
cultural practice and community, the virtual capsule of Forever Spaghetti might become 
the last vestige of a world as wholesome and comforting as your mothers old country 
cooking.

ALISHA WENG, a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary artist and photographer, returns to 
NSL&G with her sister ESTHER WENG to screen a new work inspired by a box of 
strangers' memories (gleaned from the sidewalks of a changing city). The Weng sisters 
consider commonalities between disparate peoples, wondering if and how their paths 
have crossed. Stories lost and found are magnified, obscured, and reflected through the 
glass of the camera, revealing intimate, oblique and fragmented memories taking on 
new life.

SARAH SHAMASH, a Vancouver-based artist and educator, returns to NSL&G to 
screen a new video experiment exploring a brief moment of inward reprieve amidst the 
humdrum of domesticity. 

NEAL ROCKWELL, JORDAN MINKOFF & LEE McCLURE, three Montreal-based 
interdisciplinary artists and friends, screen Want, a work of experimental comedy 
inspired by third rate reality television. Set in the near future, Want imagines a world 
defined by economic precarity, in which the only chance of making a living involves 
broadcasting one's daily struggles through the carnival lens of low budget spectacle. 

JEREMY TODD presents a new NOT SENT LETTERS SET featuring digital projection, 
scoring by BEN WILSON and letters selected and read from the project archive by 
ANDREW WITT (To Willing Conflators Of Emily Dickinson And Jeremy Todd), SARAH 
SHAMASH (To Gratefulness) & SEAN ARDEN (To The Last Person On Earth).

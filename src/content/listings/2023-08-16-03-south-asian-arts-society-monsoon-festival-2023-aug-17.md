Monsoon Festival 2023 Launches Aug. 17th!

The 8th Annual Monsoon Festival of the Performing Arts is back! Presented by the
South Asian Arts Society, the festival will take place August 17- 31, 2023, once again
celebrating South Asian culture through diverse and engaging programming.

This year we are excited to announce an exciting line up of entertainment at venues
throughout Greater Vancouver that will feature music, performance art, live podcasts,
dance lessons, workshops, an online visual marketplace, and an even larger outdoor
celebration in Vancouver's historic Punjabi Market!

Over 30 artists will be showcasing this year, including International Superstar Pav
Dharia, Gurtj aka NY Virtuoso, and Cinnamon Sandhu; content creators & siblings,
Amreen Gill aka
Bhangralicious and Swarndeep Gill; popular media personality,
Tarannum Thind; theatre artists Adele Noronha and Gavan Cheema; workshop leader
Zahida Rahemtulla; and dancers Krystal Kiran and Raja Singh (aka Bhangre Da Raja).

We hope you can join us!

Check out our schedule of events: www.monsoonartsfest.ca

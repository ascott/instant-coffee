December 16th
4:00 PM - 5:30 PM
Pollyanna Library (221 E Georgia St)

Join 221A Fellow Yu Su at Pollyanna Library for an end-of-semester conversation 
and 
listening session. Yu Su will discuss her Fall 2017 project Pollyanna Sound Archive 
Prototype 01 and introduce the suite of commissioned sound works produced from 
material gathered in the semesters two collaborative fieldwork sessions.

As part of the installation Collective Listening-research / Communal Cataloguing, Yu 
Sus sound works will be projected into Pollyanna Library between December 9 " 16.

Pollyanna Sound Archive Prototype 01 is part of Notes on Permanent Education 
(N.O.P.E. 2017), a collective research program convened by 221A Librarian Vincent 
Tao. 
N.O.P.E. 2017 inaugurates the organizations new fellowship model and its primary 
support, Pollyanna Library.

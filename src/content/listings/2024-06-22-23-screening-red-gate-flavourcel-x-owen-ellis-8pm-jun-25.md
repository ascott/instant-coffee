Red Gate TV is proud to present Flavourcel x Owen Ellis, an evening of films from
two entities
working outside of film industry norms. The screening will include a short
intermission and be followed by a Q&A discussion about DIY & anti-industry
practices with Owen Ellis and select artists from the Flavourcel collective.

Tuesday, June 25th / 7:30pm DOORS / 8:00pm SHOW

In-person: Red Gate (1965 Main Street)

Streaming Live:
redgate.tv

Tickets: $15 at the door or PWYC

Part 1: A selection of short animations and GIFS from Flavourcel (approx. 40 mins)

Part 2: "HOW TO GET AHEAD WITH A GIANT HEAD" (approx. 40 mins)

Flavourcel is an animation collective of 10 artists based in the Unceded Coast Salish
territories. They work collaboratively to make short-form experimental animations
that entertain the contemporary narrative of what animation is, and can be. Prepare
to devour their fermented ephemeral morsels with your little eyes and little hands!
Flavourcel is: Alia Hijaab, Chhaya Naran, Chris Strickler, gil goletski, Harlo Martens,
Josh Neu, Julia Song, Kat G Morris, Lana
Connors, & Laurel Pucker.

Owen Ellis is a Vancouver-based filmmaker who is currently producing & developing
outsider short films with a self-funded DIY practice in Pig Freeze Studios. His 40min
feature, HOW TO GET AHEAD WITH A GIANT HEAD follows a failing noise
musician who realizes that he is being haunted by a disembodied floating head
that
appears whenever he watches "the office" he decides to incorporate this into his live
sets which brings him immediate notoriety. The feature stars DJ On, Danika Thibault,
gil goletski, Delaney Smith, & Marnie.

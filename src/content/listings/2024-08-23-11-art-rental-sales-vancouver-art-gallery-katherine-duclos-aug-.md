Art Rental & Sales, operated by the Vancouver Art Gallery, is pleased to present new
work by Featured Artist Katherine Duclos.

Duclos is a Vancouver"based artist who maintains an active multi-media studio
practice delving into concepts of motherhood, identity, neurodivergence, materiality
and more. Her work reflects her engagement with her family, her environment, and
her experience as a gifted and disabled neurodivergent woman and mother.

In her AR&S showcase The light and colour we carry, Duclos presents new work
made over the last six months as she and her family prepared to move. Using
painted modular Lego bricks arranged one at a time, Duclos creates pieces that
reflect moments and memories of transitional periods of
time in specific places.

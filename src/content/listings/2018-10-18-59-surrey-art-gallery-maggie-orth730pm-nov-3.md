Maggie Orth Artist Talk

Saturday, November 3 | 7:30 p.m. to 9 p.m.

Surrey Art Gallery is pleased to welcome exhibiting artist Maggie Orth for an artist 
talk about her interactive, colour-changing textiles in Maggie Orth: Moving Towards 
Stillness on Saturday, November 3 from 7:30 p.m. to 9 p.m. Admission is free.

Learn how Maggie Orth fuses computer science, sensor technology, fashion, and 
design into her textile art.

Orths talk will highlight her roles as both an artist and an academic. She founded 
International Fashion Machines, one of the first companies to patent, research, and 
develop electronic textiles for public and private use. Some of her clients have 
included DuPont, The North Face, Motorola, and Boeing. Her research led to her 
signature series of interactive textile artworks.

Her exhibition at Surrey Art Gallery, Moving Towards Stillness, shows several 
artworks from the series of the same name. Each artwork consists of a small 
computer interface and a hand-woven textile. When a visitor presses a button, heat-
sensitive ink printed onto the weave of the fabric slowly changes colour. As if by 
magic, hidden patterns emerge from the darkness. With multiple patterns 
programmed into a single textile, Orth presents us with a vision of the future of art 
and technology.

But there is a catch. Over time, these patterns get burnt into the weaving because 
the ink fails to return to its original dark colour. Orth refers to her work as colour-
change textiles. She says, From the very first time a viewer pushes the button to 
watch them perform, they are dying: moving toward stillness and the end of their 
electronic life. In this way, Orth highlights how technology can hasten its own 
demise.

Surrey Art Gallery Assistant Curator Rhys Edwards says, With her expertise in 
multiple disciplines, Maggie Orth synthesizes some of the oldest and newest forms 
of technology in existence. Her art demonstrates how weaving practices are just as 
relevant now as they ever have been.

Maggie Orth: Moving Towards Stillness is on display in Surrey Art Gallerys TechLab 
until December 16.

Young creators art camp
Jul. 02, 9:00 a.m. " Jul. 05, 12:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Not your average art camp - at Mobil Art each day we'll explore a new medium and technique 
and end up with an exhibition at the end of the week!
Our innovative and extremely fun summer camp will expose participants to a variety of media 
and processes, from clay sculpture to printmaking to building projects and more. Each day the 
group will learn something new from an expert teacher in the field and make a variety of work 
throughout the week. The kids will end the week by displaying their work in an exhibition in our 
beautiful space and be able to invite parents and friends to come and appreciate the work! We 
guarantee a safe, fun environment. $224/week

PEOPLES CO-OP BOOKSTORE

1391 Commercial Drive, Vancouver

Doors at 7:30; readings at 8:00pm

Event is free. Snacks provided. Books for sale.

NOMADOS LITERARY PUBLISHERS is pleased to host the launch of Judith
Penners new chapbook, A Bed of Half Full: a landscape, and a reading with
Kim Minkus.

Judith Penner

Judith Penner had an early career as a poet, short story writer, journalist
(Fiddlehead, Best Canadian Stories, The Observer Magazine UK) and co-author
of several non-fiction books, followed by a long period of preoccupation
with other matters (family, travel, teaching, editing). In the last decade
she has edited a number of books on art, including The Colour of our
Dreams: the Surrealist Revolution in Art (Vancouver Art Gallery), and
contributed poetry, fiction and essays to catalogues (readymades, Gordon
and Marion Smith Foundation), anthologies (Sustenance, Anvil Press) and
literary magazines, including Geist, Prism International, The Capilano
Review and subTerrain.

Fred Wah on Judith Penners A Bed of Half Full: "These prose poems are
brilliant gestures into the landschaft of improvisational writing that
reminds us of the tension between our restlessness and our desire for
cohesion."

Kim Minkus

Kim Minkus has published three books of poetry: 9 Freight (LINEbooks 2007),
Thrash (Snare Books 2008) and Tuft (BookThug 2013), and has had reviews,
poetry and fiction published in The Capilano Review, FRONT Magazine, West
Coast Line, The Poetic Front and Jacket. She has also co-edited an issue of
The Capilano Review that focused on innovative narrative and contributed to
the anthology Best American Experimental Writing 2014 (Omnidawn
Publishing). Kim teaches at Capilano University. Kim will read from Tuft
and other work.

Gail Scott on Kim Minkuss Tuft: Kim Minkus in this exuberant melding of
the human, the flora and ragged fauna, the plunder of architects, offers
one of Vancouvers most glorious and naughtily nuanced homages.

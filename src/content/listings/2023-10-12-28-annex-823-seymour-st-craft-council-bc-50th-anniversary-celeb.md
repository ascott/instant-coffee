50th Anniversary Celebration

Craft Council of BC

Tickets: https://www.showpass.com/art-of-craft/ onsale September 26, 2023

CCBC Social: @craftcouncilbc (Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, TikTok)

Hashtags: #contemporarycraft #artofcraft2023 #artofcraft

CCBC info webpage: https://craftcouncilbc.ca/artofcraft

Events description:

The Craft Council of BC presents the return of the Art of Craft Speaker Series to be
held in celebration of the Councils 50th anniversary with keynote speaker Lou Lynn.

2021 Saidye Bronfman Awardee and BC glass artist Lou Lynn continuously explores
the archaeology of daily life, through form and material. Lou will be presenting a
retrospective talk as a glass artist and long-time member of the Craft Council of BC.

Additional presentations by CCBC Craft Awardees Ilana Fonariov, Carol E. Mayer
and Hope Forstenzer.

Project-C Program Details

Cineworks has launched Project-C, an initiative that celebrates short films from
our region. We are seeking submissions of 5 minute films that explore a big idea
through a small ecological footprint. We invite filmmakers to use recycled
materials, found footage, or repurposed moving images.

A shortlist of 10 films will be selected and uploaded to the Vucavu Cineworks
streaming channel for free public access from September 15 through to
September 30. Shortlisted artists will receive a screening fee with the option to
keep their work on the Vucavu platform and receive ongoing rental revenues of
70-100%. Two films will be chosen for honourable mention and awarded $250
each. One film will be
selected as the Project-C Spotlight Film and awarded
$1,000 with a $500 rental credit with Cineworks.

Applications opened June 1st and will close on August 1st. We strongly
encourage applications from People of Colour, Indigenous or First Nations
individuals, LGBTQ2S+, persons with disabilities, and landed immigrants.
Applicants do not have to be a current Canadian Citizen, but do have to reside
within the province of British Columbia.

Visit the Project-C website to find out more about eligibility, judging criteria & the
application process. Please reach out to us if you have any questions or
concerns.

DATA MINING TAKES OVER LOCAL GALLERY

Ever wondered where all the personal information that we feed into the
Internet ends up? So do Vancouver artists Nathan McNinch and Kevin Day.
Their exhibition, A Scanner Ubiquity, on show at the Alternator Centre for

Using kinetic sculptures throughout the gallery, their works examine, in

machines that retrieve physiological information (such as height and
density) from the nearby audience; in real time, the data is then

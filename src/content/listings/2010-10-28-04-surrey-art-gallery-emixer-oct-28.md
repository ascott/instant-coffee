SURREY ART GALLERY Presents
e.mixer
2010.10.28 7:00-9:30 PM
an evening of performances, exhibitions
and mixing with friends and colleagues

Celebrating digital media, art and artists in Metro Vancouver, e.mixer is
an annual
gathering and event with presentations by sound artists and emerging
digital media
artists. Established in 2007, this annual event attracts close to 150
artists, students,
faculty and industry professionals, thus bringing together local and
international
researchers and practitioners.

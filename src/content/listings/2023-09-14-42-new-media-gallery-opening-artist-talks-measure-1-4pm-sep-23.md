OPENING RECEPTION - MEASURE - SATURDAY, SEPTEMBER 23
Everyone Welcome - Free Admission

1:00 - 2:30
FOUR 20 minute ARTIST TALKS - Anvil Theatre Anvil Centre
Annette S. Lee, Matthew Biederman, Alan Storey, James Nizam

2:30 - 4:00
MEASURE Exhibition Opens to the
Public at New Media Gallery

Please join us in welcoming Four Artists who will give brief talks before we open the
MEASURE exhibition.

MEASURE reflects on the interconnections of time, light, colour, season, the
movement of celestial bodies and how we perceive the measure of these things.
Seven artists working at the intersection of science art and technology capture the
experience of a technologically mediated world while evoking wonder, imagination
and the embodied experience. Three artists in this exhibition will not be present for
the Talks; Semiconductor and Flicie dEstienne dOrves who will give a Talk later in
November, For more information on all the artists and the exhibition please visit our
website.

TALKS
1:10 - 1:30 - Annette S. Lee, Minnesota
Annette S. Lee identifies as mixed race Lakota Irish and Chinese her communities
are Ojibwe D(L)akota. She has developed a multidisciplinary approach to art as
social practice and social justice. Lee is Professor of Astronomy &
Physics at St.
Cloud State University, University of Southern Queensland Centre for Astrophysics,
University of California-Santa Cruz. Lee has been plenary speaker at the
Astronomical Society of Australia-General Assembly, Sanford Underground
Research Facility, First Nations Mtis and Inuit Education Association of Ontario,
Sophia Centre for Cosmology in Culture, Canadian Space Agency Quebec,
American Physical Society, American Astronomical Society American Association for
the Advancement of Science and is an Adler Planeterium/Webster Distinguished
Lecturer

1:30-1:50 - James Nizam, Vancouver/Berlin
James Nizam is a Lebanese Canadian artist based in Vancouver Vancouver Island
and Berlin. His practice investigates photography within an expanded field of the
sculptural and seeks to unveil the possibilities for photographic images to activate
and inhabit space. He exhibits in Canada and internationally most recently with a
residency in Berlin and at Galerie Clement and Schneider Bonn Muse
Regional de
Rimouski and Dazibao in Quebec, Kunst Im Tunnel in Dusseldorf , The Sharjah Art
Museum in United Arab Emirates, Vancouver Art Gallery, Fotogalerie in Wien
Austria, REITER Galerie in Berlin and Butler institute of American Art Ohio. Nizam is
represented by Birch Contemporary in Toronto, Reiter Galerie in Leipzig in
Berlin/London and Gallery Jones in Vancouver. He has been long-listed for the
Sobey Art Award.

1:50- 2:10 - Alan Storey, Vancouver
Alan Storey is a Vancouver-based artist. His sculptural practice lies primarily in the
realm of public art with a focus on the social dynamics of space time and perception.
His work often references industrial and mechanical processes. He has exhibited
extensively since 1982 including solo exhibitions at Western Front, Contemporary Art
Gallery (CAG), Or Gallery in Vancouver, the 49th Parallel in NYC, the Power Plant
in Toronto, Indiana State University Gallery in Bloomington. and the Centre Culturel
Canadian Paris, France. His work
has also shown internationally in group
exhibitions. Storey has completed a number of public commissions in Canada and
the US.

2:10-2:30 - Matthew Biederman, Montral
Matthew Biederman explores themes of perception media saturation and data
systems. He is the recipient of the Bay Area Artist Award in Video and has served as
artist-in-residence at a variety of institutions. He co-founded the Arctic Perspective
Initiative. Exhibitions include 7 ATA Festival Internacional (Lima), 11th Lyon
Bienniale, Quebec Trienniale, Montreal Bienniale (Musee des Arts Contemporain),
Bienniale of Digital Art (BIAN, Montreal), Artissima (Turin, IT), Moscow Biennale Art
and Alternative Visions (Tokyo) and Sonic Acts (NASA, Amsterdam) among others.
He has collaborated with musicians as a visual artist performing at the historic
Theatre du Chatelet in Paris as well as Ars Electronica AV Festival Elektra Mutek
Futuresonic FILE and many more. His works are represented in collections in North
America.
Biederman is currently represented by Art45 and lives and works in
Montreal Quebec.

Call for Exhibition Proposals

The North Vancouver Community Arts Council is currently accepting 
submissions for our summer/fall 2012 exhibition schedule at CityScape 
Community Art Space.

CityScape Community Art Space is a project of the North Vancouver 
Community Arts Council whose mission it is to celebrate and promote 
art and artists through quality experiences in a unique and inviting 
gallery space and to create an inclusive environment, which enriches 
and encourages interaction in the community.

We accept proposals from all members of the community. To 
encourage and celebrate emerging and established artists CityScape 
maintains an inclusive environment therefore always exhibits group 
shows with 3 or more artists. Artists are welcome to submit individually 
or as part of a group. Should an individual themed proposal be 
accepted, the North Vancouver Community Arts Council (NVCAC) will 
group him/her with additional artists through an open call or invite to 
other artists that fit in with their accepted themed proposal. Non-artists 
are also invited to submit ideas and concepts for exhibitions they would 
like to see in the gallery. CityScape is a unique and dynamic space, so 
we encourage diverse and creative themed proposals.

Please see submission guidelines for CityScape Community Art Space 
below.

To download submission guidelines please go to this link on our 
website.

http://www.nvartscouncil.ca/home/artist-calls

Chinese Cultural Center | Blim Monthly Community Market | NOV 20
Blim Monthly Community Market

Saturday November 20 | 12 – 5 PM | Free
@ Chinese Cultural Center (W. Pender St. × Carrall St.)

Blim will be presenting a monthly Market this month to take place back at the 
Chinese Cultural Center for the second year.

This Months market will include 60 vendors, music, hot food, beverages, and 
entertainment. Vendors will include food, fashion, accessories, supplies, fine art, 
vintage items and records, and not be limited to crafts, and there will be a focus 
on nurturing and promoting creative production in our community. We want to 
appeal to young, old, hip, plain, babies, animals, men, women, everyone.

We are very excited about this event and what it can contribute to Mount 
Pleasant’s artistic vitality, and how it can help support and encourage the local 
creative community.

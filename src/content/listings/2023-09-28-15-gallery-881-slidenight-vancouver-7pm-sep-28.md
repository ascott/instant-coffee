7-10PM

$25 / $35 at the Door

In the spirit of the days when a Kodachrome slide projector was the focal point for
family gatherings, SlideNight features projected work by a handful of accomplished
artists each sharing new or in-process artwork. Artists for this edition include: Alexine
McLeod, who is represented by Monte Clark Gallery; the internationally exhibited
mid-career artist Kristin Man, who recently showed at Canton Sardine Gallery; Alex
Gibson, a queer Barbadian artist who has exhibited in NYC and across Canada who
is repped by Wil Aballe Art Projects; and Brian Howell, an
accomplished mid-career
artist who was recently picked up by Gallery Jones for representation.

Gallery 881 operates on the unceded traditional territories of the Coast Salish
xmky"m, Swxw7mesh, and Sel"l"witulh Nations (Vancouver, BC) and is a
production of PrintMaker Studio " a fine art print and finishing studio located at 881
East Hastings, Vancouver.

Curated by Roger Larry, Julie Lee, and John Goldsmith.

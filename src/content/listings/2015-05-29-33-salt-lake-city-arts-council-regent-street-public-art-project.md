Request for Qualifications
Regent Street Public Art Project
Salt Lake City Arts Council
Salt Lake City, UT
Budget: $1,400,000 USD (all inclusive)
Eligibility: Open to all professional artists and/or artist-led teams based
in Utah, nationally and/or internationally
Deadline: Monday, June 22, 2015

The Salt Lake Art Design Board announces a new public art opportunity,
funded by the Redevelopment Agency of Salt Lake City, to be located on
Regent Street in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah.

Artwork Criteria:
The artwork is considered a part of the architectural whole and requires
cooperation with architects, project partners and others. The artwork
should exemplify strong, imaginative design and content and should
contribute to a visually stimulating environment that lends itself to
thought and adds character to the site. The artwork should be prominent at
all hours and proportionate to its surroundings. The artwork could be
suspended and/or attached to existing, permissible buildings. Permanently
installed or integrated elements on the street may be considered as long as
access to and the function of the street is not compromised. The artist
will likely work with the Design Board and project design team to further
conceptualize the project including determining preparation of the site.

For more information and submission guidelines:
https://www.callforentry.org/festivals_unique_info.php?ID=2658&sortby=fair_name&apply=yes

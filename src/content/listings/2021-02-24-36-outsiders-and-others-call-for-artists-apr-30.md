Outsiders and Others wants to celebrate International UFO Day with an exhibition featuring
works that celebrate UFOs, aliens, other worldly beings, and outer space. July 2nd is
recognized as International UFO Day and the exhibition will be up for the entire month of
July.

The only
way we can do this is with you, the artists that are working to celebrate such ideas
and images, and we want to see your work! No restrictions as far as medium. We are open
to painting, sculpture, photos, songs, performance, and whatever else you may be creating.

Because we are a small gallery, the exhibition will be curated to create a manageable and
magical experience for all our patrons and visitors.

How to apply:

1. Email up to 6 images of artwork to be considered for the exhibition.
2. The name of the image files should include your name and the title of the work, such as
1. Sam Stone" Outer Space, 2. Sam Stone " Alien Family.
3. Include a list that describes the images you are sending in. This list should identify your
images as you titled them.
4. Include a statement about yourself that includes your education, exhibition experience,
and your interest in the exhibit theme and how it relates to your artwork.

- Email all your information to: outsidersandothers@gmail.com and
please put UFO in the
subject line.
- Deadline for submissions is end of business day April 30, 2021.
- Artists will be notified by May 5th about the selection process results.

Other information:

- Self taught artists will be given priority consideration for the exhibition because that is the
focus of our organization.
- The gallery retains a 25% commission on the sale of all artworks.
- Artists are not paid an exhibition fee.
- Outsiders and Others will do all the marketing for the exhibition.
- Artists are responsible for getting the artwork to and from the gallery.

Any questions?
Feel free to contact us at outsidersandothers@gmail.com or visit us at
www.outsidersandothers.com

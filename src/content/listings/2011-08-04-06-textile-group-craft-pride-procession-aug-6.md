CRAFT PRIDE PROCESSION - AUG 6

A collective of local textile/craft artists, including Patrick Blaeser,
Guadalupe Martinez, Garland Lam, Satyan Gohil, Heather Martin, Aja Rose
Bond, Hanahlie Beise, KT Kilgour and Alex Achtem, have come together to
create the first Annual Craft Pride Procession.

The procession was inspired in part by Lacey Jane Roberts, who has
connected queer theory and craft in a very intelligent and creative way.
The result of this will be a parade (which has been chosen to be called
procession for practical and conceptual reasons). The procession is an
affirmative act of association, re-identification and celebration. The
group were happy to ally craft to the queer community, as crafters are
often seen as outsiders to the mainstream art world. Often crafters have
grumbled and been disappointed that they have been seen as outside
mainstream art, the Vancouver Textile Collective has decided instead of
worrying and debating about what the art world thinks, they want to emulate
the queer community and celebrate. The intention is to create a cross
cultural bridge between young artists, older more traditional crafters
(your granny), the queer community and the Vancouver Community in general.
The intention is to celebrate craft, claim space, make crafters noticeable,
create and have fun.

The procession is a community event and would love anyone who wishes to
join come the day of, whether you are a crafter or a non-crafter, a
participant or a bystander, we want YOU to come join us!
TO PARTICIPATE IN THE PROCESSION, COME TO THE PARK ON MAIN @ TERMINAL ON
AUGUST 6TH 2011 AT 12PM.
BRING YOUR FLAGS, BANNERS, CRAFTS AND WALK WITH US TO CELEBRATE CRAFT!

Following the procession will be a post-parade-picnic at the park (byop),
pinata breaking and an on-site weaving (bring a strip of fabric to leave
your mark on this special day)

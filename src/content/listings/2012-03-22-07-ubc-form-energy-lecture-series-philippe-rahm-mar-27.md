SPRING 2012 FORM & ENERGY LECTURE SERIES University of British 
Columbia, School of Architecture + Landscape Architecture

WHO: PHILIPPE RAHM (Paris, Philippe Rahm architectes / Princeton 
University)

WHEN: March 27, 2012, 6.30pm

WHERE: Yaletown Roundhouse

Lecture is free and open to the public.

ABOUT: Philippe Rahm is architect and principal in the office of Philippe Rahm 
architectes, based in Paris, France. His work, which extends the field of 
architecture from the physiological to the meteorological, has received an 
international audience in the context of sustainability. He has represented 
Switzerland at the Architecture Biennale in Venice, and won numerous awards. 
He currently holds the Jean Labatut Professorship at Princeton University. He 
has lectured widely, including at Harvard School of Design, Cooper Union, 
UCLA and the ETH Zurich.

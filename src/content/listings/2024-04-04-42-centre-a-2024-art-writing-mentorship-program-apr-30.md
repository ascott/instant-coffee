Centre A is delighted to announce our 2024 Art Writing Mentorship Program, Writing is a
Practice, a Vapour, a Many-Toed Thing. The deadline to apply is 11:59 PM PT, April 30.

Facilitated by mentor Jacquelyn Zong-Li Ross, this 12-week summer intensive program
aims to introduce art writing and criticism to five chosen Vancouver-based Asian youth
through weekly writing workshops, peer reviews, guest lectures, one-on-one
consultations, field trips to local galleries, and studio visits
with artists.

Participants will leave the program with one short-form experimental review (1,000 words)
and one longer piece (2,500 words) developed out of the ideas and methodologies
explored in the workshops, and a $1000 honorarium.

For more information, please go to https://centrea.org/opportunities/.

This program is supported by the Sector Innovation Grant from the Canada Council for
the Arts and the Regional Cultural Project Grant from Metro Vancouver.

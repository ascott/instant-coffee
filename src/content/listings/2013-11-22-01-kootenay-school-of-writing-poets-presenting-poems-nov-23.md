PROMISE

Alexander Hutchison

 

Don't be nervous.

I'm not going to bite you.

 

You're not going

to bite each other either.

 

We'll take it slowly.

Nice and easy.

 

Long traces; smooth

and wide circuit.

 

Break you in as

gently as kiss the air.

 

 

PROMESSA

Trans. Alessandro Valenzisi

 

Non vi preoccupate.

Non vi mordo.

 

Non vi morderete

neppure l’un l’altro.

 

Facciamo piano piano.

Con calma.

 

Corde lunghe; giri

ampi e morbidi.

 

Domandovi gentilmente

come baciare l’aria.

 

 

BONES AND BREATH

Alexander Hutchison

 

Here's me

barely out

of the nest,

my mouth

 

rinsed: shadow

and shape

astir on dusty

ground.

 

Heart brims

and spills.

 

Words try

eyes and wings;

try air.

 

The bones light,

my breath light.

 

 

OSSA E RESPIRO
Trans. Alessandro Valenzisi


Eccomi qui
appena uscito
dal nido,
la bocca

risciacquata:
forma e ombra
ridestate sul
suolo polveroso.

Il cuore saturo
trabocca.

Le parole sondano
occhi e ali;
sondano l’aria.

le ossa lievi
il fiato lieve.

													    Locs exists,  Locs, no:

 

sts, bractlet, no, leaflet exists, verticil, no, operculum exist

 

 

Locs, by walking in nature, could flatten it.  Locs, when the

dew point provokes, posits the idea on nature. Loc balks

at bark, carcasses, amassing on the blooms. Crumbs, lobss,

plant bed, bractlet, flake.  Locs doesn’t recapture the bee nor

his fallen hands – the day is also falling. Locs, among the

clover, in air where carbon crumbles, isn’t pressed for time.

Locs, sporadically, sees the landscape rhyme. Locs backs

down. Locs advances. Locs hesitates. Locs says what he

thinks. Locs doesn’t mind me.

 

 

 

 

s, calycule exists, bulb exists, phynocarp, no. Perianth exi

 

 

I play on the possible crest.  Peak of dawn, flash point – I

insist.  In my empty hands, juggling doesn’t exist. The sun

also bursts. Breaks out. In the forest, my voice capsules

the space of a tree. Occupies it. Light occults the spoken.  If

I had wanted eyes, I would have extracted the law from a

late-drunk fruit. If I root, darken the sun, the dishevelled air

becomes a poem. A poet. Cracks, agvert, paper, phynocarp,

tear. Counting down, it’s the only way to get there. I don’t

mind it.

Job Opportunity: Editor
Mentoring Artists for Women’s Art Anniversary Book Project
Application deadline: January 22, 2014

MAWA is seeking an Editor for an upcoming book project that 
will commemorate MAWA’s 30th anniversary. This book will not 
be about MAWA, but rather a 200-300 page illustrated text 
regarding an aspect of art and feminism in Canada. The Editor 
will be responsible for setting the thesis and parameters of the 
project, soliciting content from contributors, and shepherding 
the images and text from inception to publication.

The book will fill a valuable niche, will be useful as a textbook 
for both university women’s studies and art departments, and 
will foster spirited dialogue, argument and debate regarding 
women in the arts today. Questions raised could include: Has 
gender equality in the arts been achieved? Why not? How have 
images by women evolved? Images of women? Is the male 
gaze still omnipresent? Is there a corresponding female gaze? 
What is the impact on art of a shifting object/subject position? 
How does the visual representation of women reflect (and 
construct) the position of women in society? How is art history as 
a discipline transformed by feminist theory? Can women artists 
of the past be ascribed a feminism that didn’t exist during their 
time? Is there a place for feminism in modernism, or is feminist 
art intrinsically post-modern? Has feminist art been co-opted 
into old structures of race and class hierarchies? How have 
different cultural perspectives shifted first world, white feminism 
and artmaking generally? What is the relationship between neo-
liberalism, feminism and the art object?

The Editor will work closely with the Managing Editor in 
approaching potential co-publishers, developing the final 
budget, in liaising with potential contributors, preparing writer 
contracts, obtaining image rights, maintaining deadlines, 
contracting a copy editor, selecting a designer, and trafficking 
and inspecting proofs for accuracy.

The successful candidate must propose an exciting unifying 
principle for the publication, have strong writing and critical 
thinking skills, and have experience with publishing, art and 
feminism. Excellent organization, time-management and 
communication skills are also required. Ideally, the successful 
applicant will reside in Winnipeg. However, most tasks will be 
performed off-site (not at MAWA) and independently, so out-of-
province applicants will also be considered.

This is a twelve-month contract commencing in February 2014. 
Fee: up to $9,000. Application deadline: January 22, 2014.

Please apply by sending:
-a 1-2 page proposal regarding the book, including overarching 
themes, rationale, and potential writers
-up to 3 samples of your past publications or writings
-references
-a resume or CV

Applications can be submitted by email in doc or pdf files to 
mawabook@gmail.com, or in hard copy to 611 Main St. 
Winnipeg, MB R3B 1E1. For further information, contact Nicole 
Burisch at 514-515-1350.

About MAWA:
MAWA is an artist-run not-for-profit organization dedicated to 
encouraging and supporting the intellectual and creative 
development of women in the visual arts by providing an 
ongoing forum for education and critical dialogue.

The Works Art & Design Festival seeks proposals for 1 & 2 artist shows, performances,
workshops, community events and programs responding to the 2020 theme, Yield. For
more information and to apply, visit theworks.ab.ca/calls-to-enter

THEME: YIELD
Those who yield might surrender, relinquish, or give right of way. In conversation or
debate, yielding makes space for alternate viewpoints, ideas, and voices.

The conditions that cause one to yield might include respect, humility, fear, or awe.

Yield also refers to production, cultivation, fertility, harvest, abundance, or lack thereof. It
can be a quantitative measure of success, failure, or potential.

Proposals may consider: new technologies and voices, things larger than us, the
inevitable as well as the immovable,
resilient, reticent, or uncompromising.

The Works is dedicated to accessibility, inclusion, interactivity, and socially engaged
practice.

Perspectives on a Vertical Plane: Hito Steyerl’s videos and .mov files 
Wednesday, November 27, 6pm
Simon Fraser University
Djavad Mowfaghian Cinema
Please join us for a free screening of three video works by Hito Steyerl, in support of the exhibition Hito Steyerl: Adorno’s Grey, with runs from October 3 to December 14, 2013, at Audain Gallery.
Lovely Andrea. 2007, 30min. 

Commissioned for documenta 12, Lovely Andrea documents Steyerl’s detective-like search for a lost photograph of herself from the 1980s, taken while she was working as a bondage model under the assumed name “Andrea”.
In Free Fall. 2010, 32min. 

Used as a foil for a critical investigation of global capitalism, In Free Fall follows the surprising history of a Boeing 707-700, which was first owned by Howard Hughes and, after time with the Israeli air force, ultimately exploded for the Hollywood blockbuster Speed.
HOW NOT TO BE SEEN A Fucking Didactic Educational .Mov File. 2013, 15min.
Presented at the 55th Venice Biennale, HOW NOT TO BE SEEN A Fucking Didactic Educational .Mov File is an “educational” video that irreverently details strategies for hiding in a time marked by constant surveillance and the endless proliferation of images.
----

Running from October 3 to December 14, 2013, Hito Steyerl: Adorno’s Grey is curated by Melanie O’Brian with Amy Kazymerchyk in collaboration with the School for the Contemporary Arts’ Audain Visual Artist in Residence Program.

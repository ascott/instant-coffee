Republic Gallery is pleased to announce the upcoming solo exhibition:

Andrea Pinheiro

SAFN

Opening reception: Friday, February 10, 6 - 8 pm

Showing: February 10 - March 10, 2012
Wednesday to Saturday, 11am - 5pm, and by appointment

Andrea Pinheiro's Safn presents a series gouache paintings over 
photographs taken from a 2005 visit to the Safn Gallery in Reykjavik, 
Iceland, and related sculptural works combining paint with photographic 
material and/or objects. Located in an early 20th Century wood frame 
house, the Safn Gallery housed, at the time of the artist's visit, the 
private collection of Petur Arason and Ragna Robertsdottir. Scanned 
and printed at a large scale, the works emphasize the material qualities 
of both the paint and the photographic surface.

Andrea Pinheiro is a curator and visual artist working in print, mixed 
media, and installation. She began her studies at White Mountain 
Academy of the Arts in Elliot Lake, Ontario, and received her BFA and 
MFA from the University of Alberta. She has exhibited across Canada 
and internationally.

Safn will be showing February 10 through March 10 with an artist 
reception on Friday, February 10, from 6 - 8pm. Regular gallery hours 
are 11am to 5pm, Wednesday through Saturday, and by appointment.

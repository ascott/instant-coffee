SFU Gallery 

Please join us for an artist talk with Edgar Heap of Birds

Urban Public Art with the First Nations Voice

Saturday April 30 at 7pm
Simon Fraser University Vancouver 
515 West Hastings St. Room 1600

Artworks from Denver, the Venice Biennale and maybe even 
Vancouver will be shown. This talk will include Digital Natives of 
Vancouver, Wheel the fifty foot signature outdoor Medicine Wheel 
sculpture at the Denver Art Museum and Most Serene Republics, a 
series of public art interventions for the 2007 Venice Biennale and 
Smithsonian Institution in Venice, Italy.

Edgar Heap of Birds has a BFA from the University of Kansas and 
an MFA from the Tyler School of Art at Temple University in 
Philadelphia. Since 1989 he has been teaching at the University of 
Oklahoma, where he is currently a professor in the visual arts 
department.

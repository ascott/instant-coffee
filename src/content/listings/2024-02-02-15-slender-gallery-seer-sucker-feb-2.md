Slender Gallery is delighted to present SEER/ SUCKER, new works by Alberta based
artists Kyle Beal (Edmonton) and Kris Lindskoog (Calgary). Beals drawings and
Lindskoogs paintings both work within a set of self-imposed constraints. Each have
established guidelines that determine the game play within their works. These in turn
give form, present options, and offer beginnings and ends to the works. In between
they bend, they get misread, and are occasionally ignored.

The works invite you to make a move, intuit a counter, discover an opening, search for
patterns or poke a hole through a screen and peer through.

Seer/ Sucker. Opens Friday February 2nd at Slender gallery 3030 Commercial Drive
Vancouver, BC.

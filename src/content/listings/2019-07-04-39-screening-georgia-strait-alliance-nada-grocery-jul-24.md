Nada Grocery x Georgia Strait Alliance Movie Night: Fractured Land

Tickets:https://go.hoovie.movie/screenings/118/nada-grocery-x-georgia-strait-alliance-
movie-night-fractured-land

Location: 675 E Broadway, Vancouver, BC V5T 1X7, Canada

Date: July 24th

Time: Doors open 7pm with film starting at 7:45pm

Details: Join Nada Grocery and the Georgia Strait Alliance (GSA) for a screening of 
Fractured Land, a coming-of-age story about a young Indigenous leader and lawyer, 
Caleb Behn, whose territory is at the epicenter of some of the largest fracking 
operations on earth.

Doors at 7pm, film starts at 7:45pm. Conversation and mingling to follow.

There will be delicious package-free snacks available for purchase, and attendees will 
have the chance to enter a prize draw.

We hope to see you on July 24th for a powerful film on Indigenous climate action.

Ticket proceeds go to support the Georgia Strait Alliance's work to protect and restore 
the marine environment and promote the sustainability of Georgia Strait, it's adjoining 
waters, and communities.

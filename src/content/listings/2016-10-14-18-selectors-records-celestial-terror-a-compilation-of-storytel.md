CELESTIAL TERROR: A Compilation of Storytelling through Sound Art

Wednesday, October 19 at 7:30 PM - 10 PM
Selectors' Records
8 East Pender Street, Vancouver, V6A 1T1

Presented by MAC (Media Arts Committee) and Vancouver Co-op Radio with the
support of British Columbia Arts Council & Selectors' Records.

CELESTIAL TERROR is a compilation of sound art pieces created by 6 BC Sound
Artists who are sharing a story by means of sound alone. The compilation
features:

VICTOR BALLESTEROS
SARA GOLD
ANDREA LUKIC
CHANDRA MELTING TALLOW
GEORGE RAHI
KATE RISSIEK

This night will include the artists' introductions to their pieces,
followed by a full play of their pieces as a listening session. The night
will end with an opportunity to participate in a question period with the
artists.

Entry is by donation.

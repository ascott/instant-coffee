Artist Talk: Saturday October 18, 1pm

Gallery Hours: 12-5pm, Tuesday to Saturday. Closed on holidays

This exhibition is a project initiated by Mathieu Fraser-Dagenais that is
comprised of the responses of 3 artists to a 1:5 scale model and the phrase
â&#65533;&#65533;The complexity of the model, from the prospective space to the absolute formâ&#65533;&#65533;.

The model is subject to series of definition and images. One can categorize
it in two ways: on one hand as a formal model based on the definition of
container space, fixed and autonomous, and on the other, the relative,
evolutionary model with coexistence of times and spaces. These two forms
are not necessarily in opposition: sometimes, they complement one another
or return one to the other. As a symbolic space, the model questions our
relation with reality. It projects us in a hypothetical space where time is
fixed. For the artists, it makes it possible to push back the material or
financial constraints of a project. It is also a platform for testing new
ideas just like a different way to present a work.

In this exhibition, Fraser-Dagenais has three artists explore the
possibilities of the model in a series of actions between himself and the
participating artists. Initially Fraser-Dagenais created 3 models that are
given to the artists along with the above mentioned phrase. These models
will give information on the form, the design, the colours and the
materials of the exhibition models. These models will then be reproduced in
1:5 scale. After having received and assembled their space, each artist
will conceive an intervention which will be installed before the exhibition
in the 1:5 model. For the exhibition itself, the models will be presented

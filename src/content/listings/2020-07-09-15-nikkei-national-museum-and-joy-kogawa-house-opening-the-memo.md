Opening the Memory Chest: How to Research and Write Your Family Story
With Sally Ito and Linda Kawamoto Reid
Presented by Nikkei National Museum and Joy Kogawa Historic House

During the past months under lockdown, many of us have found ourselves more often
on the phone or on Zoom with family members. Stories from the past have emerged to
help us interpret
events today. If you have a family story you would like to research, this
is the webinar for you.

Sally Itos spring 2020 author residency at Historic Joy Kogawa Historic House has
turned into a virtual one due to Covid-19. The Nikkei National Museum is pleased to
partner with the Kogawa House to present this webinar, which we hope will motivate
some people to discover your own family history. Listen to Sally Ito read from her novel,
The Emperor's Children, and share how she researched her family history to write the
book.

Linda Kawamoto Reid, Research Archivist, will talk about how she can help you
research your family history.

Saturday, July 25, 2020
11am Pacific Time

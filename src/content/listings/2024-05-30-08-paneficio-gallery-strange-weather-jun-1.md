Yuri Arajs is a Canadian artist who works and lives in Vancouver BC. Yuri considers
himself to be a minimalist landscape artist who has a focus on the sky and what
happens above us.

"I love spending time getting lost in the sky. There are so many places to go and
things to see.

This new body of artwork was created over the last year which has been a difficult
time in the world. No blue skies here.

These latest works focus on details that the artist wants us to see and spend time
with. This could be a single spot in the sky, the outline of a building, or a mass of
rain. All of these images have been depicted with very specific found materials,
obsessive pencil drawing, and acrylic paint. The visual balance of materials is at the
core of these pieces. Everything is
done with intention.

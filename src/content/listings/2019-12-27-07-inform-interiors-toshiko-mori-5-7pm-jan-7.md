Inform Interiors Inc
50 Water St, Vancouver, British Columbia

Toshiko Mori joins us to speak about her practice. Presented in cooperation with UBC SALA

Toshiko Mori is founding principal of Toshiko Mori Architect (TMA), established in 1981 in
New York City. The practice is known for over thirty years of innovative and influential work
in a diverse body of projects that have received numerous design awards. The work of TMA
combines a strong conceptual and theoretical approach with a thorough study of
programmatic needs and practical conditions to achieve a design that is both spatially
compelling and pragmatically responsive. TMA has worked on a broad range of programs
including urban, civic, institutional, cultural, residential, museum and exhibition design.
Recent work includes the New York City theater, library, and museum projects, the Hudson
Yard Park and Boulevard, and the Center for Maine Contemporary Art in Rockland, Maine.
The firm has also designed institutional projects for Brown University and Syracuse
University, and was included on the design team for New York Universitys strategic master
plan. TMA was recently chosen as
the architect for the Watson Institute for International &
Public Affairs at Brown University. Among the many awards the firm has garnered, this year
Mori received Architectural Records 2019 Women in Architecture Design Leader Award.

Toshiko Mori is the Robert P. Hubbard Professor in the Practice of Architecture at Harvard
University Graduate School of Design; she joined the Harvard GSD faculty with tenure in
1995.

Centre A is accepting submissions to add to the unique selection of works by local
artists and creators offered in our gallery bookstore and gift shop, the Centre A

well sell it!

Have your creations displayed and sold alongside works by emerging and established
artists from all over the Lower Mainland while supporting gallery operations and

plus a Centre A membership and online exposure when you sign a consignment
agreement with us.

Submissions are accepted on a rolling basis.

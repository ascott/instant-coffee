Saturday December 2nd, 1-3pm

Final performance by Andrew Bartee
From Ballet BC, we are delighted to welcome dancer Andrew Bartee to the 
Contemporary Art Gallery, who over successive visits has been developing a series 
of 
individual performances in response to ideas and themes present within the 
exhibitions. 
Saturday December 2nd marks Andrews final performance and the culmination of 
his 
series at CAG.

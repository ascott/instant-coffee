CSA Space presents:

Window Bended Harmony
Julian Hou

Opening reception Thursday 4 September 2014, 6-9pm
4 September 2014 through 05 October 2014

Curated by Steven Tong

Julian Hou is an artist from Vancouver, BC. He holds a BFA from Simon
Fraser University and an M. Arch from the University of British Columbia.
He is currently a curatorial resident at 221A and has been published
recently in The Capilano Review, and the artist writing compilation Fantasy
Art Now (2014). Ongoing musical collaborations include the art music
project The Stick.

See Pulpfiction Books (2422 Main St.) for admission during regular business
hours: Mon-Wed 10am-8pm, Thurs-Sat 10am-9pm, Sundays and holidays 11am-7pm

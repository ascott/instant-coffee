Dear friend,

We are sending you this email because we would love for you to join us and our 
other peers Saturday December 16 at 1pm for an action at Westbanks Fight for 
Beauty exhibition.

The action will be held as an alternative tour, the purpose of which is to agitate, 
disrupt and underline the displacement masquerading as cultural production 
caused by Ian Gillespie, Westbank, et al. We will be presenting alternative 
presentations of particular works in the exhibition alongside speeches from people 
within the community. This is the final weekend of the Fight for Beauty exhibition, 
and our idea is for those who oppose Westbanks distorted idea of cultural 
production to present a counter-narrative that can push back against art-washing as 
a gentrification tactic, to get the last word.

Please email WWAS.nope[at]gmail.com if you are interested in coming out.

With spirit,
Woodwards Anti Development Society via Notes on Permanent

Education (WWAS NOPE)

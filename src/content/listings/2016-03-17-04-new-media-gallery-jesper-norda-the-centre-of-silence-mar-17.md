JESPER NORDA

An empty, white room. Few visual details are there to engage the
viewer; for some it is an unsettling experience. A woman's voice or a
prolonged sound fill the room; voice, white noise, pure tone, sine tone;
parsed moments. The source of each sound is unclear. One could
characterise the voice as lacking in intonation and emotion. The quality
of the voice, and the experience in the room invokes various reactions.
This is Jesper Norda's 'The Centre of Silence', one of four sound works
in the OTIC exhibition.

This Thursday evening Jesper will speak on his practice and his work
in the OTIC exhibition. We are delighted to welcome him to Canada
and to the New Media Gallery. This is a public talk, free of charge and
# 12 in the New Media Gallery ARTIST TALK SERIES.

Jesper Norda is a musician, composer and visual artist. He holds an
MA from the Valand School of Fine Arts, Gothenburg. He began
studying musical composition but switched to the visual arts halfway
through his training. Now his interdisciplinary practice constantly seeks
connections and examines the boundaries of music and the visual arts:
of sound, silence and emptiness.

'The Centre of Silence' resides in the collection of the Kalmar
Kunstmuseum, Sweden. NMG is grateful to Kalmar Kunstmuseum +
the Artist for agreeing to re-record this exhibition version for the New
Media Gallery exhibition OTIC.

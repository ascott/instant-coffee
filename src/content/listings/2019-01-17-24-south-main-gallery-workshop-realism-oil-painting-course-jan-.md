Realism Oil Painting Course 
Be instructed by artist Kellie Orr to create a contemporary still life oil painting using 
realism painting techniques. Kellie will share her process, tips, and tricks, and will 
coach you to build skills in a relaxed and encouraging environment. You'll have 
creative license and will take your own reference photo, with assistance as required. 
You'll be coached as you paint, building skills in colour, tone, brushwork, blending, 
and detail. All skill levels welcome. 
Kellie is an award-winning contemporary artist from Australia, with recent press 
features including The Jealous Curator, Booooooom, Beautiful Bizarre Magazine, 
and Supersonic Art, and her work is held in numerous private collections. See her 
work at www.kellieorr.com.

Start date: January 24, 2019 
Schedule: Every Thursday, January 24 - March 21, 2019, with a mid-course break 
on 21 February (8 weeks of tuition in total) 
Time: 6 pm to 9 pm 
Location: South Main Gallery (279 E 6th Ave) 
Supplies: The course includes a pre-prepared canvas and a materials list will be 
provided. 
Cost: $520 (including stretched canvas) 
For more information, and to sign up, contact Kellie at kellieorr@live.com.au

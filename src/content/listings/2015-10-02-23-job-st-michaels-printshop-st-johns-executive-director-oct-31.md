Job Opportunity – Executive Director 

St. Michael’s Printshop, St. John’s, NL is seeking a highly motivated 
and dynamic candidate for the position of EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR.

Reporting to the Board of Directors, the Executive Director is 
responsible for the organization’s consistent achievement of its 
mandate and financial objectives. St. Michael’s Printshop is reaching a 
pivotal point in its 40 year history, and the Executive Director will see 
the organization through these developments.

Closing Date: Oct. 31, 2015

Position Start Date: Dec. 7, 2015

Salary: commensurate with education and experience; benefits 
included

Primary Duties

Provide leadership in developing programming, organizational and 
financial plans with the Board of Directors, and carry out plans and 
policies authorized by the board;

Communicate with the Board, membership and general public, both 
orally and in writing;

Promote the organization’s activities through the SMP website, social 
media, monthly e-newsletter, and local media outlets;

Develop an annual budget and manage day to day finances;

Maintain inventory of prints and retail items in SMP Gallery space, as 
well as Print Archives.

Qualifications

To be obtained through some combination of post-secondary education 
and experience.

Essential: highly motivated; exceptional communication skills, both oral 
and written; strong organizational skills; experience working in a non-
profit environment; willingness to relocate to St. John’s, Newfoundland, 
if not already located there.

Preferred: experience in a leadership role within a non-profit, 
experience within an Artist-Run-Centre, some knowledge of the Visual 
Arts and of Printmaking.

For a more detailed description of the position, visit: 
www.stmichaelsprintshop.com/were-hiring

To apply, please send resume and cover letter to 
erin@stmichaelsprintshop.com

Please note, only successful applicants will be contacted for an 
interview.

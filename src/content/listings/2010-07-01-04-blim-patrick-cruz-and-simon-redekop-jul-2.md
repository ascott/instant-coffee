While intuition is independent of any reasoning process due to
its direct perception and action. Both Patrick Cruz and Simon
Redekops one year collaboration resulted in an accumulation
of works that explored and investigated the role of intuitivity
and play in the process of making and planning art. Consisting
of video, performance, painting and sculpture the installation is
manifested in a collage`esque manner where the idea of layers
are deliberately and literally used as a tool to simultaneously
change the perspective of a static state. TEH proposes the
possibility of an uncertain future while allowing the viewer to
freely associate meaning within its scatological manifestation
which produces a non-linear reading of the work.

Teh is an Internet slang neologism most frequently used as an
English article, based on a common typographical error of the.
Teh has subsequently developed grammatical usages distinct
from the.[1] It is not common in spoken or written English
outside technical or leetspeak circles, but when spoken, it is
pronounced /tɛ/ or /tə/.[2]

Teh is one of the words in the auto-correct lists of spellcheckers
in word processing applications such as Microsoft Word,
OpenOffice.org Writer, Pages, and Corel WordPerfect. T and E
are typed by the left hand on adjacent fingers in Qwerty, while
the H is typed by the right, and in rapid typing, the T and E are
often typed by the left hand in a drumming motion before the
right can get the H in between the two. Overcompensating with
the right hand can result in the misspelling hte, which is also
found in auto-correct lists.

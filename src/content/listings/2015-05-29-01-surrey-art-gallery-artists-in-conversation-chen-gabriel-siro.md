About the Speakers

Lisa Tzu Ling Chen was born and raised in Taiwan. After receiving her
Bachelor of Fine Art degree from Milwaukee Institute of Art and Design,
where she double majored in sculpture and integrated studio arts, she moved
to Surrey where she currently resides. Lisa works in a broad range of media
including painting, sculpture, film, video and book arts. When not making
art Lisa teaches art and mathematics to children.

Brandon Gabriel is a painter and sculptor from the Kwantlen First Nation.
After studying art at Kwantlen Polytechnic University he went on to further
studies at Emily Carr University fine arts programs, where he was awarded
Bachelor of Fine Arts in 2006. Instructor of Indigenous Arts at the
University of the Fraser Valley. Brandon is based in Fort Langley B.C.

Jeannette Sirois makes art about the human face. Her images are extremely
detailed and most often rendered in coloured pencil. She has a BFA from
Concordia with distinction, a graduate diploma in art and a MEd from the
University of British Columbia. Jeannette has taught art for many years in
high schools. She is currently based in South Surrey.

Cineworks presents:

Gala Screening: Play It Forward - Non-Profit Campaign Video Contest

Saturday March 19; Doors: 1.40pm; Screening: 2.00pm
Pacific Cinematheque; 1131 Howe Street, Vancouver;
FREE

Now in its third year, the Play it Forward campaign video contest is
designed to bring together the region's creative and non-profit communities
to help make Metro Vancouver a better place to live. The Gala Screening
features eight short campaign videos commissioned for this vibrant
community-engaged project.

Cineworks invited Metro Vancouver's filmmakers and non-profit organizations
to a meet-and-greet information session in December 2015. Participating
organizations were each invited to propose a pairing with a filmmaker, to
collaborate on producing a subsidized campaign video of one to three
minutes long, professionally and engagingly capturing the organization's
spirit and mission.

The following pairings were selected and began production in January 2016:

Federation of BC Youth in Care Networks | Jen Lee
Made in BC | Luke Skillen
McLaren Housing Society of BC | Kate Green
Dixon Transition Society | Joella Cabalu
PHA Canada | Tyler F Gamsby
The Gathering Place Community Centre | Neil Volkey
Vancouver Asian Film Festival | Bruno Cruz
City of Bhangra Festival (VIBC) | Samantha Mayne

The Gala Screening will feature an Audience Choice Award and the winning
filmmaker will receive:

$1500 Equipment Credit at Cineworks
Passes to DOXA 2016
$250 DOC BC Membership (1 year)
Pen & Touch Graphics Tablet

Cineworks extends special thanks to The Roundhouse Community Arts and
Recreation Centre, DOC BC, DOXA Film Festival and Pacific Cinematheque for
their generous collaboration.

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society (est.1980) is an artist-run
production and exhibition centre that supports independent filmmakers and
media artists working in digital video, celluloid film media and in
relation to cinema in expansive ways. We engage our membership and the
broader community through initiatives and services that foster dialogue and
experimentation with the moving image.

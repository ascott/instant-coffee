16mm Basics: Introduction to the Bolex Camera

12 February 20172:00pm-5:00pm
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander Street (Ironworks basement)

$40 + taxes | General Admission
$35 + taxes | Concession
$30 + taxes | Member Admission

REGISTER: [http://bit.ly/2lmcfpC](http://bit.ly/2lmcfpC)

A 3 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and practice of 
exposing film with a Bolex 16mm film camera.

Elements include:

The mechanics and chemistry of exposing film
Understanding lenses
Handling film
Loading and unloading film in light and dark conditions
Shooting in natural light

Participants in this workshop who also register for the Hand Processing workshop on 19 
Feb will get to process footage they have shot in this class.

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society, The Documentary Organization of Canada–BC 
Chapter, Praxis Centre for Screenwriters and Women in Film + Television Vancouver, in 
conjunction with the Vancouver International Film Festival, present the 15th annual Meet the 
Filmmakers.

These insightful and informal discussions with artists attending the Vancouver International Film
festival provide a rare opportunity to engage in meaningful dialogue with the creative visionaries 
of modern cinema. 

All salons are FREE and take place at the CINEWORKS STUDIO with street entrance through 
the Pacific Cinémathèque at 1131 HOWE.

Friday, 09 October

GERALD PEARY of For the Love of Movies: The Story of American Film Criticism in conversation 
with Sharon Kahanoff, 3pm

TRIMPIN + PETER ESMONDE of Trimpin: The Sound of Invention in conversation with Jean 
Routhier, 5pm

Saturday, 10 October

VIC SARIN of A Shine of Rainbows in conversation with Michael Boucher, 2:30pm

PETR LOM of Letters to the President in conversation with Mohammad Salemy, 4pm

Tuesday, 13 October

GENRE BUSTING DOCS, 3pm
Panelists: Gregori Viens + Henry Phillips of Punching the Clown
Moderator: Sally Aitken

SHERRY WHITE of Crackie in conversation with MARY BISSELL, 6pm

Wednesday, 14 October

MARILYN THOMAS + KATE KROLL of Shi-Shi Etko in conversation with Jacqueline Samuda, 
5:30pm

Thursday, 15 October

CALLING THE SHOTS, 5:30pm
Panelists: Danishka Esterhazy of Black Field and Caroline Bottaro of Queen to Play
Moderator: Kat Montagu

Visit www.cineworks.ca for daily updates and panel additions.

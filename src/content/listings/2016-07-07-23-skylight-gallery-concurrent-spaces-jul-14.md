Concurrent Spaces is the first exhibition by long time collaborators, 
Leah McInnis and Danielle Roberts, that will present large scale 
paintings made simultaneously. This process was initially developed 
during set durations of time spent at Roc Y Studio (Burnaby, BC) and 
further cultivated in their shared spaces at Skylight Studios. This mode 
of creation embraces speed, spontaneity and communication as each 
artist must forgo their ego and accept each other's decisions in 
creating a cohesive aesthetic. This body of work is self reflexive and 
takes a satirical stance to the history of painting, pop culture, and the 
contemporary art world. Both artists agree on creating illusions of 
depth translated visually through transparent layers and uncomfortable 
forms. This exhibition is curated by MOPE collective, and includes new 
works created individually by each artist over the past 4 months.

Concurrent Spaces is proudly brought to you by Empathy Water.

Join us on July 14th, 2016, at Skylight Gallery. 163 east Pender St. 
from 6pm-11pm.

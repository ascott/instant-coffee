Videodrunk Summer Online Challenge

Deadline: June 1

We at Videodrunk are running a mini-online festival this summer that
will be exclusive to our Facebook page. We're issuing a challenge to
film makers and artists to make a film under 10 minutes based on a
single sentence we provide that also follows 3 guidelines we set for
each film maker. The film can be any genre or style but must be based
around the sentence and follow the guidelines.

To accept the challenge please email us at videodrunkfest@gmail.com
for your sentence and guidelines. If you have any questions don't be
afraid to email us as well.

Entries close on June 1st and films must be submitted by July 1st.

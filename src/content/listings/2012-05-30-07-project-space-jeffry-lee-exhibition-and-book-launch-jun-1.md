Music Album Meets Photo Album in Hard Drugs and Jeffry Lee's Party 
Foreverer 
Project Space Press launches first book and produces an 
accompanying exhibition also by Jeffry Lee

Party Foreverer Book Launch & Opening Reception
Friday, June 1, 2012 at 8pm
Exhibition: June 1 to July 7, 2012
Project Space, 222 E Georgia St (at Main)
No cover

VANCOUVER, BC – Project Space Press launches its first book, Party 
Foreverer by Jeffry Lee and Hard Drugs, co-published by Karmazots 
Press and The Storyboard Label. Part illustrated photo album and part 
music album (via a digital download), the book captures a snapshot of 
the temporary New York chapter of the band Hard Drugs. Acting as 
artifacts, the songs and illustrations provide an intimate glimpse into the 
life of Lee and his bandmates, while also conveying the complicated 
interplay between documentation and memory. 

Excerpted from the book's Foreword by Project Space Press publisher 
Tracy Stefanucci:

"When Jeffry set out to record this album, he wanted a souvenir of his 
time in New York City. Party Foreverer, in book format, offers not only a 
more elaborate memento to connect him with his past, but also an 
opportunity to share this coming-of-age-like narrative with his friends, 
fans and strangers: Artist moves from Vancouver to New York City to 
cut his teeth in the world of art or—as it turned out—music. Makes life-
long friends; is initiated into something bigger than himself through 
collaboration with a new best friend, his wife, an idol from his youth, and 
a friend from home who yo-yos between Vancouver and New York. 
Working hard to record an album, taking time to amble down country 
roads and cuddle with cats. Waking in the middle of the night bursting 
with melodies, drinking beer, sipping Prosecco, weekending in the 
home of a man named Mama and laughing at a giant dog that runs the 
place. Sporadic pastoral living juxtaposed against the saturated 
urbanity of Brooklyn living. 

With his story, Jeffry breaks down the barrier between rock star and 
reader. He invites us to join him, to be transformed with him. To fall in 
love with the sparkle of the city. To crave the perceived simplicity of the 
country. To miss the damp blue-green-grey and quirky humour of 
Vancouver. The book affords him another way into our hearts, another 
channel to deliver his experience and his nostalgia: a multi-
dimensional, multisensory conveyance of this narrative. But above all, 
Party Foreverer declares its title as both a personal commitment and a 
call to arms for everyone who has felt the rush of chasing their dreams 
with reckless abandon." 

In addition, Project Space will host an exhibition of artwork by Jeffry Lee 
from June 1st to July 7.

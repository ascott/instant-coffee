Attention: Art History, Visual Art and Theory students at colleges and
universities in the greater Vancouver area

The Art History Student's Association of the University of British Columbia
is currently seeking submissions for the 5th annual Art History
Undergraduate Symposium. The call for papers deadline is Monday February
23rd at 12pm and the date of the Symposium is March 27th at 3 pm.
Submissions are open to all current art history, visual art and theory
students at UBC and current college and university students in the greater
Vancouver area.

Students are asked to submit an abstract of no more than 300 words.
Further, students should clearly identify in their abstract how their paper
is different from existing literature and have the option of including in
addition to the abstract a list of three-four points on how their paper
achieves this. Six papers will be selected by a panel made up of
undergraduate students at UBC. Each selected student will have 20 minutes
to present their paper in a formal setting followed by a question period.
There will also be a food and wine reception at the end of all six
presentations. All abstracts should be submitted electronically to
ubcahsa@gmail.com and should include the student's contact information and
the class title for which the paper was originally assigned. The personal
information of each submission will be confidential and will not be
released to the panel.

The purpose of the Art History Undergraduate Symposium is to share, present
and discuss work by current undergraduate students in art history, visual
art and theory. As such, we highly encourage students from all colleges and
universities in the greater Vancouver area to apply in order to have a
highly diverse exchange between institutions.

Any questions regarding the symposium can be made through email inquiry to
ubcahsa@gmail.com

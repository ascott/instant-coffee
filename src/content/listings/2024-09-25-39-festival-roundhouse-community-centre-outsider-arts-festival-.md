Vancouver Outsider Arts Festival

October 11-13, 2024

Presented by the Community Arts Council of Vancouver, the Vancouver Outsider
Arts Festival (VOAF) returns for its eighth edition at the Roundhouse Community
Arts & Recreation Centre from Oct. 11-13, 2024. The first and only one of its kind in
the country, the festival showcases and supports outsider artists coming from a wide
range of backgrounds who challenge the conventions of mainstream art. In order to
better commit to the values of representation and equity, for the first time ever this
year an inclusive curatorial committee came together to select the 56 visual and
performing artists that will be featured. The festivals program includes: Poems from
the Kitchen culinary workshops presented in partnership with BC Culture Days, a
podcast series in collaboration with SAD Mag, as well as various workshops hosted
by VOAF alumni. In continuing VOAFs partnership with the Outsider Arts Festival of
Helsinki, a selection of films
curated from the Finnish festivals lineup will also be
available for viewing.

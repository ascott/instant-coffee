Reading: Clint Burnham, Mark Laba/ Cop Puppett " 3pm

Saturday, March 23 - 3pm

Griffin Art Projects

1174 Welch St, North Vancouver, BC V7P 2R5

[http://griffinartprojects.ca/](http://griffinartprojects.ca/)

Clint Burnham is the author of Pound @ Guantanamo (poetry, Talon, 2016), Smoke Show 
(novel, Arsenal Pulp, 2005), and Airborne Photo (short stories, Anvil, 1999). His 
photographs were exhibited at CSA Space in 2016, and from 1996 to 1999, with Mark Laba, 
he performed puppet shows in galleries, bars, and storefronts around Vancouver. He 
teaches at SFU.

Mark Laba is a writer and artist. His first book of poetry, Movies in the Insect Temple (Proper 
Tales Press) was published in 1981. In 1985 his chapbook, The Mack Bolan Poems 
(Gesture Press) won the bpNichol Chapbook Award. The Pig Sleeps, a collaborative pork-
noir novel with Stuart Ross was published by Contra Mundo Press, 1993. He went into 
hiding until 2002 when Mercury Press released another book of poetry, Dummy Spit. Much 
of his published work is ephemeral in nature and exists only in attics and between the 
cushions of discarded sofa beds. From 2000 until 2009 he wrote a weekly restaurant and 
food review page for the Province Newspaper. A new book of poetry, The Inflatable Life is 
due out this spring from Anvil Press/A Feed Dog Imprint.

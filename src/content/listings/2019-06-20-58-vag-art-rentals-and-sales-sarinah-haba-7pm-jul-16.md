Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist with Featured Artist Sarinah 
Haba in the Art Rental & Sales showroom on Tuesday, July 16 at 7PM.

Sarinah Haba explores the places where memory lives, the space between recollection and 
certainty. As a painter, she is looking for clues to examine and express a sense of belonging. 
Haba combines transparent washes with color blocking to build energy and depth and to open 
up space. Gestural lines create a sense of movement and develop dynamic communication. 
This fluidity seeks resolution or definition, whether in figurative, structural or pattern imagery.

Featured in the Art Rental & Sales Showroom from June 10 to August 23, 2019 
Open weekdays, 10am - 5pm

750 Hornby Street, Vancouver

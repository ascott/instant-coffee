Centre at Emily Carr | Call for Mentors: Art Apprenticeship Network | NOV 20
We invite artists, curators, or workers within the cultural non-profit sector to submit
project proposals (taking place February - December 2021) for student apprentices as
part of Shumka Centre's Art Apprenticeship Network program.

Established in 2020, the Art Apprenticeship Network is a program that funds student
apprenticeships with established artists, curators and cultural workers. Through this
program, Emily Carr students will assist with studio work and project production.
Developed to build and strengthen the local art ecosystem through a part-time
apprenticeship model, the program aims to create opportunities for paid work, social
connection, and hands-on experience for emerging practitioners. There are no
participation costs for mentors.

We are invested in creating opportunities that celebrate diversity and intersectionality
in
sexuality, gender, culture, race, ethnicity, religion, and ability. We extend a special
invitation to practitioners from diverse backgrounds, including individuals of Indigenous
descent.

Please find the full call here: https://shumka.ecuad.ca/call-for-mentors-art-
apprenticeship-network-2021/
Deadline: November 20, 2021

The Art Apprenticeship Network is generously supported by the RBC Emerging Artists
Project.

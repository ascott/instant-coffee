The Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory (AHVA) at the University of
British
Columbia (UBC) is pleased to present Moving Response/s, our very first digital
media and performance exhibition featuring student work from second- and third-
year Visual Art courses.

Moving Response/s is an exhibition in flow, an open-ended experience, an
experiment in collective research and creation. A living web of artworks evolving
throughout the time of the exhibition; a journey shared by participants and invitees
across e-merging physical, digital, imaginary worlds.

February 12-March 7, 2025

Closing Reception: Friday, March 7, 5-7 p.m.

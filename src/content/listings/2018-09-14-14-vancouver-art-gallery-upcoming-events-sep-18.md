TALK:

David Wisdom Slideshow
Tuesday, September 18 at 7PM
Room 4East in the Gallery

Former CBC radio personality and photographer David Wisdom is back to host
his legendary communal slideshow at the Gallery. Featuring local artists
and arts practitioners, the evening is a warm gathering pairing selected
images with music, with introductions by Wisdom and participating artists.
This years slideshow will feature the work of artists Britta Bacchus, Seth
Berkowitz, Christopher Brayshaw, Connie Kuhns, Rodney Graham, the Adad
Hannah Family, Robert Kleyn, Brad Phillips, Carol Sawyer, Neil Wedman and
more. This event will be followed by a reception and musical set, where
Wisdom will present his album Music For Art Galleries"1,000 Recordings In
Alphabetical Order For Enhanced Viewing.

Tickets: $12 Members | $15 General Admission | $10 Students available
at https://etickets.vanartgallery.bc.ca/Info.aspx?EventID=12

OPENING:

The Vancouver Art Gallery presents A Curators View: Ian Thom Selects

Saturday, September 22

Discover the Vancouver Art Gallerys rich permanent collection through an
exhibition of nearly ninety highlights including paintings, drawings,
photographs, and sculptures in A Curators View: Ian Thom Selects, on view
September 22, 2018 to March 17, 2019. Considered one of the most
comprehensive resources for visual culture in British Columbia, the
Gallerys collection comprises of close to 12,000 artworks, hundreds of
which were stewarded by former Senior Curator"Historical Ian Thom during
his thirty-three year tenure. In this special exhibition of Pop,
abstraction, portraiture and landscape, Thom provides a glimpse into the
Gallerys process for pursuing works for acquisition. Spanning more than
two centuries, the exhibition features such artists as Henri Beau, Emily
Carr, Robert Davidson, Gathie Falk, Leon Golub, David Hockney, Ann Kipling,
Beatrice Lennie, David Milne, Paul Peel, George Segal, Graham Sutherland,
Andy Warhol, John Vanderpant and Zacherie Vincent, and more.

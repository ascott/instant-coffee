Job Posting:

Production Assistant - TORONTO INTERNATIONAL ART FAIR (TIAF)

Join the team that creates and develops Canada's most prestigious modern
and contemporary annual art event. The Toronto International Art Fair
currently in its tenth annual production showcases leading galleries from
around the world along with national and international publications, art
institutions and exciting art projects.

Working in the downtown Vancouver office the Production Assistant will work
as an integral member of the fair's small production team reporting
directly to the Vice President /Show Director. The successful candidate
will provide key administrative support and production assistance. It will
involve liaising with national and International galleries, national art
institutions and an array of creative people to assist in the production,
growth and marketing of this fine art event. This is an exciting
opportunity for a creative and innovative individual looking to work in all
aspects of the show from start to finish. Compensation includes salary and
an Employee Benefits Package.

Qualifications:

* Minimum 2 years experience working on events and/or as a
coordinator/assistant role in a fast paced environment.
* An understanding of the fine art market.
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
* Excellent organization skills.
* Self Starter/ability to take ownership of projects and see through
completion.
* Excellent computer/technical skills using outlook, database
management, adobe and excel.

Responsibilities:

* Assist Show Director on all aspect of show production.
* Liaise with exhibitors, partners, show service providers and suppliers.
* Coordinate the printing and distribution of collateral materials
* Research industry and market related news/announcements.
* Manages exhibitor's accounts including services, invoicing and
production deadlines.
* Maintain various databases including: Sales Database, (EBMS format),
web-database and Better mail email communications.
* General Admin support including contracting, sales and marketing support.

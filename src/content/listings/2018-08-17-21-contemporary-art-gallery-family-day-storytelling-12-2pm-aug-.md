Family Day | Storytelling with Emily Dundas Oke
Saturday, August 25, 12pm and 2pm, free
Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5

On the last Saturday of every month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition tours 
and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Join CAG CMA-RBC Indigenous Curatorial Assistant Emily Dundas Oke for an afternoon of 
storytelling inspired by the work of Jeneen Frei Njootli. After hearing stories told by Dundas 
Oke, families are invited to express their own stories through material in a printmaking 
workshop.

No reservation required. Simply just turn up!

Find out more about Jeneen Frei Njootli's exhibition at 
www.contemporaryartgallery.ca/exhibitions/jeneen-frei-njootli-my-auntie-bought-all-her-skidoos-
with-bead-money/

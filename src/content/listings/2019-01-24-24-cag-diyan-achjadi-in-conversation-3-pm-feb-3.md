Artist Talk | Diyan Achjadi in conversation aboard the moving bus

Sunday, February 3, 3-4pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

In this unique artist talk, 'How do you travel?' exhibiting artist Diyan Achjadi will speak 
about the concerns that propel her practice aboard her wrapped bus as it tours through 
the city.

No reservation required! Meet at CAG at 3pm. The bus will depart shortly after.

Find out more about the public art initiative at howfardoyou.travel

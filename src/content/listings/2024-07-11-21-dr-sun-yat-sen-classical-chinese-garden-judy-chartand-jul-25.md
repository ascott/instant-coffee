DR. SUN YAT-SEN CLASSICAL CHINESE GARDEN ANNOUNCES NEW SOLO
EXHIBITION WITH JUDY CHARTAND

VANCOUVER, BC, JULY 8, 2024 " The Dr.
Sun Yat-sen Classical Chinese Garden
is thrilled to present a solo exhibition by Cree artist Judy Chartrand. This exhibition
celebrates Judys artistic achievements and showcases her stunning ceramic works,
including slip-cast and hand-built plates, sculptures, installations, and pieces utilising
underglaze, glaze, lustre and mixed-media. Judy Chartrands exhibition, On the
Margins, will run from July 26th to November 31, 2024, at the Gardens Hall of
Hundred Rivers.

The opening of the exhibition is free to the public and will take place on Thursday,
July 25, from 5 pm to 7 pm, with remarks at 5:30 pm. Please RSVP here to attend.

Judys art is a powerful testament to her experiences in Vancouver, particularly her
time in the Downtown Eastside and Chinatown neighbourhoods. Through this
exhibition, the Garden celebrates her extraordinary talent and invites a broader
dialogue about the themes and stories her art conveys through her candid and
thought-provoking creations.

With a career
spanning over 30 years, this exhibition highlights some of Judys most
iconic works, including her Campbell soup cans, political lard pails, The Politics of
Racism ceramic plates, Mimbres-inspired bowls, cockroach bowls, and ceramic
crows series. Judy Chartrand continues to create extraordinary works that draw on
her heritage, personal experience, and history. Always innovating, she seeks new
ways to celebrate Indigenous culture and promote diversity and acceptance,
remaining hopeful and positive.

We are delighted to announce that Judy Chartrand has been shortlisted for the
prestigious Sobey Art Award this year. Works by the six finalists will be on view at the
National Gallery of Canada from October 4, 2024, to March 16, 2025.

About Judy Chartrand:

Judy Chartrand (born 1959) is a Cree artist from Manitoba, Canada. Judy grew up in
the Downtown Eastside neighbourhood of Vancouver, British Columbia. Her works
frequently confronts issues of postcolonialism, Indigenous feminism, socio-economic
inequity and Indigenous knowledge.

Judy Chartrand studied for her Diploma in the Fine Arts Program at Langara College
and then at Emily Carr University of Art and Design where she graduated with her
BFA in 1998. She continued on to finish her master's degree in Fine Arts in Ceramics
at the University of Regina (2003).

Her works have been collected by Rennie Collection, Glenbow Museum,
Saskatchewan Arts Board and the Smithsonian National Museum of the
American
Indian. Her work has also been included in anthologies on arts and crafts including,
Utopic Impulses: Contemporary Ceramics Practice.

About Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden

Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden is a registered museum and one of
Vancouvers top tourist attractions. It is a unique venue for cultural programming and
events, including guided tours, concerts, festivals, exhibitions, receptions, and
educational programs. This Ming Dynasty-style garden-home is the first among its
kind to have been built outside of China, and continues to be unique among the
world as the result of the joint collaborative effort of community members, the
Canadian Government, and the Peoples Republic of China. As a registered not-for-
profit, all Garden revenue goes toward the fulfilment of our mission to bring together
cultures and communities through educational and public programs.

In celebration of Emily Carr University of Art and Designs 2019 graduating students, members 
of the public are invited to experience The Show at Emily Carr (520 E 1st Avenue), on from May 
4-19, 2019 from 10am to 8pm on weekdays and from 10am to 6pm on Saturdays and Sundays. 
The Show features artworks by more than 300 graduating Design, Visual Arts and Media Arts 
students, and installations will be exhibited throughout the entirety of ECUs campus, offering 
unprecedented access to the buildings interior spaces. Visit ecuad.ca for a full schedule of 
events and listing of participating artists.

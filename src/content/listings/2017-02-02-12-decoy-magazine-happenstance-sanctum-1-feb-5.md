HAPPENSTANCE // a series of temporary artworks in Vancouver, presented by 
Decoy Magazine. SHELTERS theme, Winter 2017 (February 5 - 12)

The theme of this series, SHELTERS, has inspired artists to consider the lived 
experiences we associate with shelter, safety and inclusion, housing and 
gentrification. In these temporary installations, the artists ask: Is shelter 'home'? 
Is shelter 'safe'? What constitutes a shelter in a city that promotes an image of 
urban density and sustainability, while fostering a thriving industry of speculative 
development and artificially constructed film locations?

Artist Penelope Hetherington and the performance group Psychotic Butler 
present SANCTUM 1 & 2, a pair of performance installations inspired by films that 
depict the construction and occupation of sanctuary.

Artists Leah Weinstein and prOphecy sun will present Public livingROOM, a one-
day social sculpture created for Happenstance. Taking place inside of the 
SiteFactory bus parked in Stanley Park, Public livingROOM will bring together a 
collection of cozy seating spaces, tables and board games inside of the bus, 
welcoming people to enter and participate. The installation will also include a 
looping single-channel video and sound projection by performance artist 
prOphecy sun.

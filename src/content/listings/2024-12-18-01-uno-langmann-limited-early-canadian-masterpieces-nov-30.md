EARLY CANADIAN MASTERPIECES
Now till the end of January 2025
Location: Uno Langmann Limited, 2117 Granville Street, Vancouver

The consignment of a major collection of early watercolours spurred the decision to
mount a major retrospective exhibition of Early Canadian Masterpieces, with the
addition of works collected by us over many decades. Spanning the century between
1825 and 1925, this exhibition represents major early artists committed to capturing
the lifestyle and landscape of early Indigenous and pioneer
life across Canada.
Many of these early artists immigrated from Europe and received artistic training,
some received little training. Many artists travelled across the country before the
completion of the Canadian Pacific Railway, later at the invitation of Van
Horne as part of the Railway Artists documenting the opening of the Canadian West.
These artists developed a unique style of Canadian landscape painting.
Join us on a journey through early Canadian art history. The exhibition will be posted
online and available to view in the gallery on Tuesday, November 26. Please join us
for the opening reception on Saturday, November 30 from 1-4 pm.

The exhibition is also viewable online: www.langmann.com

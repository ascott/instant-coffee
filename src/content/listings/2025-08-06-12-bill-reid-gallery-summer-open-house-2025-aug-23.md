Summer Open House 2025

August 23rd, 12 to 4 pm

Bill Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art

639 Hornby Street, Vancouver, BC

Experience an inspiring afternoon of culture, creativity, and connection in the heart of
downtown Vancouver. This free community event invites visitors of all ages to
celebrate the richness of Indigenous Northwest Coast art and storytelling.

Explore our landmark exhibition Kihl Yahda Christian White: Master Haida Artist, a
stunning retrospective showcasing the extraordinary carvings, masks, jewelry, and
regalia of acclaimed Haida artist Christian White.

Enjoy live performances and demonstrations by Aleen Sparrow (Musqueam), who
will share traditional weaving techniques and teachings, and Kwiis Hamilton
(Hupa-asath/Leqa:mel). Special guest Haida singers will also be joining us to share
traditional songs.

Come with your family and get creative together with hands-on art-making activities,
explore our curated children's library, and spend time in the Hummingbird Gathering
Space, a welcoming place to learn, create, and connect.

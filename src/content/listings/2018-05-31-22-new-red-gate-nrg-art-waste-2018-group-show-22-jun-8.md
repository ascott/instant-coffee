"First held in 1994, the Music, and Art Waste festival purports itself as a
multi-venue, affordable and accessible stage for variations of different
artistic mediums and expressions. Originally borne out of retaliation
against the heavily commercialized and corporate-backed New Music West
festival, Music and Art Waste provided a safe, non-profit, alternative
space for music and art lovers of Vancouver. Predominantly volunteer run,
this amalgamation of music, visual art, comedy, and local culture quickly
expanded into an integral part of the Vancouver subculture scene.
Twenty-two years later, Music and Art Waste has proved itself here to stay."

Written by Tara Bigdeli
Quote from SAD MAG
June 08, 2016

Art Waste is a collaborative art festival that has run alongside
Vancouver's annual Music Waste festival for 24 years. Art Waste aims to
create a means for emerging artists to participate in a community oriented
art exhibition by holding submission based art events within participating
galleries and venues around Vancouver. Each year, Art Waste holds a Group
Show with a corresponding theme. This year our theme is HONOUR:

Honour: a drive towards unquestionable faith, a guiding nationalism,
yearning for valour, seeking conquest ... But aside from its dominant
implication of a medieval scourge, honour may also be a source of
rejuvenation and rebuilding, of community, and of the banality of it all.
Explore your own honour, be it personal, public, current, historical, in
this years Art Waste Group Show.

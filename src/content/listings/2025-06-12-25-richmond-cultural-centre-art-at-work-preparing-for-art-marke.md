Art at Work: Preparing for Art Markets

Facilitated by artist Ilena Cramer

Saturday, June 28

10:00"11:30am

Richmond Cultural Centre, Theatre Under the Stairs

Are you ready to enter the world of art markets and craft sales? Join artist Ilena
Cramer for an informative session covering different types of markets, understanding
the right ones for your business, planning your table, methods of payment, engaging
customers and other top tips for first-timers.

Art at Work is a series of professional development workshops designed for artists
and arts organizations practicing in any artistic medium. The program is presented in
partnership by the City of Richmond, Richmond Public Art and Richmond Art Gallery.
The program is intended to provide artists and arts organizations with knowledge
and skills relevant to pursuing a professional practice in the field of public art,
performance, visual art and community arts.

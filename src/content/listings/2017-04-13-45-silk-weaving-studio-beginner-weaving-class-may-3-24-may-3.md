Spend four weeks learning basic weaving in one of the most inspiring and
beautiful studios in the city. Working in a small group over the course of
four weeks we'll use Ashford Knitters Looms to develop samples that explore
materials and techniques. At the end of the session you'll have the knowledge
and skills to create your own hand woven designs. Class fee includes most
materials and use of a loom. This class is great for those new to weaving or
those who have woven tapestry on a frame loom and want to make cloth.

Please phone the Silk Weaving Studio at (604) 687-7455 to register.

Dates: May 3, 10, 17, 24, 7-9 pm

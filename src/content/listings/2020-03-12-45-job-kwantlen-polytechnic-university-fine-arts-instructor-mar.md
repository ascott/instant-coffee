FINE ARTS INSTRUCTOR - Competition Number 20-31

The Fine Arts Department at KPU offers certificate, diploma, and Bachelor programs
with a strong curriculum focusing on the ideas and techniques of producing
contemporary art within a diverse and supportive liberal arts environment. This dynamic
program focuses on the integration of studio work with theory and research. Our
graduates are prepared with the necessary skills to pursue advanced Fine Arts studies,
enter related fields, or pursue a contemporary professional practice. For more about our
department and programs, please visit our website at: https://www.kpu.ca/arts/fine-arts

We are seeking individuals who meet the following qualifications:

The Department of Fine Arts at Kwantlen Polytechnic University invites applications for
a Non-Regular (NR2) 100% position commencing September 1, 2020. This position is
subject to budgetary availability. The applicant should be able to teach the following
courses:
FINA 1142 "
Introduction to Print Media
FINA 1242 " Print Media II
FINA 2342 " Print Media III
FINA 2342 " Print Media IV
FINA 1145 " Monotype

The ability to teach introductory to advanced Print Media courses will be considered an
asset. Courses may be scheduled in three terms during the contract year, at the Surrey
campus.

Those applicants interested in applying can find further information on our department
and courses at: https://www.kpu.ca/arts/fine-arts.

The successful candidate will have an MFA with a concentration in Print Media, a
current exhibition record and experience in post-secondary teaching. Applicants will
have the interest and ability to teach foundation and advanced courses at the
undergraduate level. They will also have technical and safety knowledge of materials,
processes, and equipment, including studio lighting, digital and darkroom practices.

Ready to Apply?
A complete application includes a cover letter, a curriculum vitae, copies of graduate
transcripts, teaching
evaluations, 10-15 examples of recent work (provided via a website
link or PDF) and a list of three professional references able to comment on your
teaching ability and academic accomplishments.

To be considered for this exciting opportunity at one of BC's Top Employers, please e-
mail your full application, quoting competition number 20-31 by March 31, 2020 to:
employ@kpu.ca.

Important:

To help us process your application as quickly as possible, ensure your email subject
line is written using the following format: Last Name-First Name, Competition Number
XXXX. For example, Doe-John, Competition Number XXXX

Please submit a separate email and resum for each competition to which you are
applying. Ensure you have clearly marked each email and resum with your last name,
first name, and the appropriate competition number.

The University welcomes and encourages applications from women, members of
racialized groups/visible minorities, Indigenous persons, persons with disabilities,
persons of any sexual orientation, and persons of any gender identity or gender
expression. If you require an accommodation through our hiring process, please include
your request in the application email.

We thank all applicants for their interest in employment with KPU. Only those selected
for an interview will be contacted.

Kwantlen Polytechnic University is committed to protecting the safety and welfare of
employees, students, and the general public, and upholding the reputation and integrity

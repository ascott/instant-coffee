Join us for a participatory performance of Musicircus. 

Musicircus was written in 1967 by legendary composer John Cage. The piece brings 
numerous musicians and performers together within the Gallery over the course of 
one afternoon. Listeners also participate in the creation of the composition by 
moving around the venue. Musicircus is family-friendly and will feature music and 
sound performances, live readings, and compositions by Cage, including a 
performance on a specially prepared piano.

Musicircus is performed in conjunction with Hannah Rickards installation "One can 
make out the surface only by placing any dark-coloured object on the ground", and 
in collaboration with the Blueridge Chamber Music Festival.

LAST CHANCE – Vancouver’s Progress Lab (progresslab.ca) network of 
indie theatre-makers comes together to test the theory that creativity 
feeds on limits with the wacky and wonderful Obstructions theatre series. 
See what these theatre companies come up with when told to make a 
piece based on three “obstructions” in two back-to-back boundary-
busting shows: Theatre Replacement’s A Certain Kind of Darkness on 
Saturday Oct. 19, 2013 and Neworld Theatre’s This Butoh is Not Political 
on Friday, Oct. 25, 2013. PL1422 (1422 William Street, Vancouver). 
Tickets: $16. http://Obstructionsoctober.eventbrite.ca

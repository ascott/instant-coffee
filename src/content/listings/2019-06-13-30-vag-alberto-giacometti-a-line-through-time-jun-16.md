The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Alberto Giacometti: A Line Through Time from June 16 to 
September 29, 2019

The first major presentation of Giacomettis art in Vancouver, this wide-ranging exhibition of 
paintings, sculptures, drawings and lithographs showcases Giacomettis works alongside the 
ancient sculptures that served his imagination and works by famed artists with whom he was in 
dialogue. Featuring more than 130 artworks including those by Giacomettis contemporaries in 
Paris, Jean Dubuffet, Germaine Richier, and Csar, it also includes works by UK-based artists 
with whom he had a long-standing connection such as Isabel Rawsthorne, Francis Bacon, Lynn 
Chadwick and Eduardo Paolozzi. In 2016, the Sainsbury Centre for the Visual Arts in Norwich 
commemorated the 50th anniversary of Giacomettis death with A Line Through Time, which 
was named by The Telegraph as one of the top exhibitions of that year.

For tickets and info, visit: vanartgallery.bc.ca

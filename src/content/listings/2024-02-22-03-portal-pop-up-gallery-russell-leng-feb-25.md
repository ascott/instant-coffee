Portal Pop Up Presents

Russell Leng

February 5 - 25, 2024

Closing reception: Sunday, February 25, 1-3pm

Portal Pop-up continues this February in the window of 407 W Cordova with artist
Russell Leng. Join us for a closing reception, Sunday February 25, 1-3pm.

Leng presents two paintings Nourishing Spring through Cracked Earth and Soft
Huddle Stretch that are part of an ongoing series of work depicting the body as a
collective unit. Figures quietly move between foreground and background, merging in
and out of tender relationships with each other. At
times, the indistinct anatomy finds a
way to hold each other in a supportive gesture. In other moments, the figurative
reference is dissolved back into the density of the painted surface, which has gone
through its own transition of being built up, then reduced back down with a power
sander. By pushing bodies together in close physical proximity, the work offers an
emotional reading of interconnectedness, and questions where we begin and end in
relation to another.

Bio:

Russell Leng is an artist living and making in the Fraser Valley of B.C, Canada, on the
traditional ancestral unceded territory of the St:l people. In his recent return to
painting on canvas, he visualizes the felt experience of interconnectedness and the
complex web that binds individuals together. His studio process establishes the
generative conditions for amalgamated forms to transform into bodies within imagined
spaces. Lengs work has been exhibited and collected worldwide.

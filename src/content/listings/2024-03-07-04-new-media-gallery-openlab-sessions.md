Do you have a new media project but no workspace? Maybe you've taken a few
workshops and want to revisit the techniques you learned at your own pace?

New Media Gallery is now offering OpenLAB sessions for self-directed work. Every
Thursday evening (March - June 2024), we open our Art + Technology LAB with access to
technology within the LAB.

Art + Technology LAB is open for creative exploration and making. Expert technicians
support creation with different technologies. Explore video, music, 3D modelling and
printing, robotics, coding and animation. OpenLAB is designed for those who have some
media arts experience. Supplies not included.

Visit our website or call to register or learn more!
newmediagallery.ca/openlab

House Gallery | SOMETHING BIG AND INVISIBLE
SOMETHING BIG AND INVISIBLE
An online exhibition of miniature works in a dollhouse gallery

February - March 2024
Small House Gallery
London, UK
www.smallhousegallery.uk

Solo exhibition by Vancouver artist & writer Seema Shah at Small House Gallery, a
dollhouse gallery in the UK. The exhibition features miniature 2D & 3D works, including
collaged matchboxes and works with cut-up / erasure poetry.

The exhibition is being unveiled gradually over February and March on the gallery's
Instagram page (www.instagram.com/small_house_gallery), slowly revealing the
conversation between individual works, between rooms, and between floors. From
curator Eldi Dundee: "The works (& their placements) in this show will generate all
sorts of associations in the viewer. No two people will see the same show."

Seema's work has been exhibited in The House of Smalls, another dollhouse
gallery in
the UK. She was a recipient of the The Beaumont Studios' Artists To Watch Award
2022 and a highly commended artist in the 2022 & 2023 Contemporary Collage
Magazine Awards.

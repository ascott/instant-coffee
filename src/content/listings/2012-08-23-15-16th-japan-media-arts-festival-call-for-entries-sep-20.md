Call for Entries
Entries Deadline: September 20, 2012

The 16th Japan Media Arts Festival is calling for entries for its four
divisions:
Art, Entertainment, Animation and Manga(comic).

The Japan Media Arts Festival is a comprehensive festival of Media Arts
(media art,
manga, animation and games) that honors outstanding works of Media Arts
as well
as providing a platform for appreciation of the works that have won awards.
The last festival received a record 2,714 works from 57 countries and
regions around
the world, demonstrating its continuing evolution as an established
annual international
festival.

For the 16th Festival, entries are sought in various disciplines of
Media Arts including
interactive art, video, websites, games, animation and comics, and from
professional,
amateur, independent and commercial sources.
The award-winning works will be announced in mid-December. The
Exhibition of
Award-winning Works, with approximately 150 award-winning works and jury
selections, will be held at
The National Art Center, Tokyo from February 13 to 24, 2013.

Please check the entry details on the official website below:

[http://j-mediaarts.jp/?locale=en](http://j-mediaarts.jp/?locale=en)

*Eligibility: Works must be completed or released between September 23,
2011 and
September 20, 2012.
Works that were renewed, changed, completed, presented or released during
the above time period are eligible for submission.

The festival welcomes notable works that open up new frontiers in Media
Arts.
Looking forward to your entry!

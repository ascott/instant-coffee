HOLGER KALBERG - PARALLAX
Monte Clark Gallery
Oct 16 - Nov 15
Opening Reception: Thursday October 16, 6-8pm

The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to announce the solo exhibition
PARALLAX from Vancouver-based painter HOLGER KALBERG.

In Kalberg's latest work, fragments of imagery are assembled to create
unstable tableaus, forms unplaced and dislodged from their original
context. Using abstract vocabulary as well as representational
segments, the artist constructs a complex world of symbols and signs
which border on the edge of recognition, purposely offering the viewer
a variety of interpretations. Parallax, a measurement representing the
discrepant appearance of an object when viewed from two different
points, characterizes Kalberg's present paintings which explore the
shifting of space and perception.

Dynamic use of colour ranging from subtle pastels to intense hues as
well as sharp, angled structures which seemingly float on soft
backgrounds come together to create an unfixed and ambiguous
aesthetic. Through his referential practice of constructing these
indefinite spaces on canvas, Kalberg creates paintings that deal with
space both within the works as well as the relationship between the
work and the outside world.

While Kalberg is aware of historical precedence, the work shifts
beyond Modernist reductionist traditions. Constructing a contemporary
mode of this aesthetic, the artist creates his own unique system of
representing space.

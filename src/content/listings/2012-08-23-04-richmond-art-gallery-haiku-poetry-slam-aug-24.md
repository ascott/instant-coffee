HAIKU POETRY SLAM
Friday, August 24 7pm-9pm
Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond

Richmond Art Gallery's Emerging Artist Committee presents the first event in 
their Closing Night series.

Don't miss your last night to attend Sophie Jodoin's exhibition, Close Your 
Eyes. We invite you to meet with fellow young artists while responding to 
Jodoin's work by writing haikus. The emcee will read the poems throughout 
the night.

FREE ADMISSION. Food & drinks. Door prizes.
With musical performances by Jeong-Won Shin & Paul Chan
and emcee Jane Sojin Kim.

The Emerging Artist Committee (EAC) is a group dedicated to building a 
strong community where young artists can network, socialize, and express 
creative ideas.

Special thanks to our sponsors:
Starbucks, Opus, H-Mart, Phoenix Art Workshop, Long & McQuade and 
Jumbo Sushi.

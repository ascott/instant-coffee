Eastside Culture Crawl

Green

Mon Nov 4 - Fri Nov 29

Reception Thurs Nov 7, 6-8pm

Works in this years' ESCC curated exhibition have been selected to reflect and
interpret the many meanings of the term 'Green'. From ideas related to environmental
issues and climate change, to those connected to wealth, luck, envy and greed, the
exhibition gives artists the opportunity to contemplate what Green means to them,
explore personal experiences, and look back on their own and others' history.

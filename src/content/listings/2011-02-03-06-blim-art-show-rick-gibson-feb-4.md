BLIM ART SHOW (FEBRUARY 2011)
Blim presents:
Holy Men
by Rick Gibson
For his Masters degree at Simon Fraser University, Rick Gibson developed a
technique for making large format 3D lenticular prints. He now uses this
medium to extend his previous works in holography, performance art and
sculpture.
In this current exhibition, he uses a new digital imaging technique to
permanently project a two-dimensional image onto a three-dimensional shape.
The final picture shows a 3D form that appears to float in space. Visitors
to the gallery will not need to wear special glasses to appreciate his art
works.
Thematically, Gibson has used this technology to pay homage to six
prominent religious leaders.

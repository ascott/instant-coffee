Saturday Session: Latashkinem on Sesemiya
12 AUG 12:00 PM

One Saturday each month,
CAG invites a guest host to lead walkthroughs of our
current exhibitions, offering their insights and response to the works on view. In
August, we welcome Latashkinem to speak on the work of Sesemiya.

ASL interpretation is available on request. Requests can be accommodated up to 5
days in advance. Please contact learning@cagvancouver.org to book or for more
information.

Nilh kwens Latashkinem kweshamen aka Robert Williams tina chen tla Humulchestn
Uxwumixw ti Swxw7mesh Temixw. My ancestral name is Latashkinem aka Robert
Williams and I currently reside in the village of Humulchestn in the Swxw7mesh
Territory. I worked with our tribe as a cultural worker providing support to our youth with
the guidance of our elders and chiefs. My new career as a digital artist and storyteller
has allowed me to adapt my knowledge and experience in a modern context. My future
goals are to write new stories for children and create art inspired by the legends and
history of the Swxw7mesh. En wenaxten
skwalewen. I have love for our people.

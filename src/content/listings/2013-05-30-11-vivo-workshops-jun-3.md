WORKSHOPS

June 3rd and 5th 6:30PM-9:30PM
Max for Live : Unlimited creation in Ableton Live ($108, or $86 with Extended 
membership)
Participants will learn how to build devices in Ableton: custom synthesizers, 
samplers, sequencers, audio effects and more. Change how Ableton Live 
works by manipulating properties of tracks, clips, and native Live devices.

The workshop will cover reconfiguring how controllers and other hardware 
interact with Live. Common MIDI controllers, multi-touch surfaces and even 
“non-musical” tools like video cameras can all be repurposed to meet specific 
needs. Pad controllers can be turned into step sequencers, trigger video from 
drum pads, and much more.
June 4th and 6th 6:00PM-9:30PM
Circuit Building ($125, or $100 with Extended membership)

A base knowledge of electronics gives the artist the opportunity to introduce 
electronic circuitry into a variety of media including sculpture, sound, and 
light-based works. This course will cover some basic theory of electronic 
principals that is essential for beginning to work with this medium. From there 
we will look at some of the components that make up simple circuits and that 
create the building blocks for more complex circuits. Participants will learn 
some of the basic skills needed for electronic work such as soldering, 
breadboarding, and reading a schematic. These skills will be used to build 
from a selection of projects: a small audio amplifier with speaker, a 555 
timer/oscillator, a punk atari sound synth or a 1970's distortion circuit.
June 8th and 9th 12:00PM - 6:00PM
Camera, Lights, Sound! ($288, or $230 with Extended membership)

Understanding the camera through learning the manual settings to help you 
get the best shot. 
You will learn about exposure, gain, shutterspeed and white balance through 
a number of practical exercises.
The principals of three-point lighting and sound recording will be covered and 
practiced, these help get you ready and confident to set up a shoot under the 
skilled guidence of the instructors.

June 11th, 13th, 18th, 20th, and showcase on the 21st 6:30PM - 9:30PM
Intro to Digital DJing for Women ($288, or $230 with Extended membership)

This short course is a basic introduction to using Traktor Pro 2. Emphasis will 
be placed on the concepts of DJing in addition to learning how to use the 
software. At the end of this course students will be able to:

- Navigate the basic functionality of Traktor

- Know the basic principles of DJing and mixing

- Put together a 30min set and perform it

For practicing and preparing personal DJ sets, partcipants are encouraged to 
have their own copy of Traktor on their laptop or home computer and their own 
controller, by the second or third class, however, this is not required. This 
workshop is suitable for anyone who does not have experience using Traktor 
but would like to learn its basic functionality and foundations of DJing.

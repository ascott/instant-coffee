Job posting:

Or Gallery, Summer Gallery Assistant

Start Date: June 3rd.
Position lasts for approximately 12 weeks
12$/hour
30 hrs/week

Description:
Helping with day-to-day operations of the gallery, including installation of artworks, sitting the gallery, administration, helping with gallery publicity and public outreach efforts, organizing Or Gallery office, storage, and archives. Other projects may include a publication project. The Or Gallery focuses on contemporary and conceptually based practices.

Requirements:

Must be a student:

Students who are eligible to participate must:

   * be between 15 and 30 years of age;
   * have been registered as a full-time student in the previous
     academic year, and intend to return to school on a full-time
     basis in the next academic year;
   * be Canadian citizens, permanent residents, or have official
     refugee protection status under the Immigration and Refugee
     Protection Act; and
   * be legally entitled to work in Canada, according to relevant
     provincial/territorial legislation and regulations.

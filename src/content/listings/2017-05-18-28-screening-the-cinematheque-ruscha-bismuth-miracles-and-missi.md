RUSCHA + BISMUTH: MIRACLES AND MISSING PIECES

May 31, 2017 
7 PM The Films of Ed Ruscha

Los Angeles artist Ed Ruscha made only three, rarely shown films near the start 
of his career. Recently unearthed and restored by the UCLA Film and Television 
Archive, The Books of Ed Ruscha features his childhood friend, musician and 
comedy writer Mason Williams, knocking back sundowners on a patio as he 
solemnly incants and irreverently manhandles these limited edition art objects. 
Ruschas second film, Premium, a self-parody of the artist as a young man, 
starring L.A. artist Larry Bell, is a filmic adaptation of his photobook Crackers, 
based on a short story by Williams. The last film, Miracle, has artist Jim Ganzer 
playing an auto-mechanic who undergoes a metamorphosis while repairing the 
carburator of a 65 Ford Mustang.

The Books of Ed Ruscha, 1968-69, 16mm, colour, sound, 38 mins
Premium, 1971, video, colour, sound, 24 mins
Miracle, 1975, video, b&w, sound, 28 mins

9 PM Where is Rocky II?
France/Germany/Belgium/Italy Dir: Pierre Bismuth, DCP, 93 mins

In 1979, Ruscha allegedly made a fake rock out of resin and hid it among real 
ones somewhere in California's vast Mojave Desert. Named Rocky II after the 
Sylvester Stallone film, the work is never spoken about publicly nor is it listed in 
any catalogue of his work. Obsessed with this mystery piece, French artist Pierre 
Bismuth, in his directorial debut, hired a private detective and set out to find it.

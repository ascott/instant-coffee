Judy Chartrand : Graceful Blooms
Opening Saturday January 18th
2-4 pm

We are thrilled to present a new body of hand built ceramic works by Vancouver
based
Judy Chartrand, marking our fifth presentation of her work. Dancers, florals, insects,
berries, dogs, and birds fill the gallery in an occasion of wild revelry. The use of
humour, and whimsical imagery brings an element of surprise and playfulness, while
also conveying deeper cultural meanings, specifically the celebration of "Native joy".

Judy Chartrand is a contemporary artist of Manitoba Cree heritage, based in
Vancouver. Her ceramic and installation-based work features rich elements of ready-
made appropriations and repurposed materials. Most recently Judy was a finalist in
the
Sobey 2024 Award, and her work is currently on view at the National Gallery of
Canada.

Artist in attendance, please join us!

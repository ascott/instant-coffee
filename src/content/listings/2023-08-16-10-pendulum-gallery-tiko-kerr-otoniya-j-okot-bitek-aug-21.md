Tiko Kerr | Otoniya J. Okot Bitek

If becomes when

August 21 - September 22, 2023

Tiko Kerr is a Vancouver artist who since the 1970s, has been an important figure in
the Vancouver cultural scene through his artmaking and community activism. Kerrs
work is fundamentally informed by how he personally sees the world and the ways
human vision functions to interpret the world. This multi-part installation derives from
Kerrs long-time interest in collage informed by his practice as a painter and includes a
number of new pieces, including collaborations with poet Otoniya J. Okot Bitek, that
merge the work of the two artists and reflect on the nature of collaboration across
artistic disciplines.

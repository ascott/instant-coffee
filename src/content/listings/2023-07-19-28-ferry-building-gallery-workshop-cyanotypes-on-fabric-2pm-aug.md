Ferry Building Gallery presents

Workshop: Art and Stewardship Creating Cyanotypes on Fabric

Embark on a journey exploring concepts of stewardship and reflections on our
relationship to nature through art making with exhibiting artist and environmental
advocate Desire Patterson.

Using a 200-year-old method of cameraless photography, you will create three
cyanotype prints on cotton to take home.

Join us on August 9, 2-4:30pm

At Artspeaks Tent next to the Ferry Building Gallery

1414 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver, BC V7T 1C2

here

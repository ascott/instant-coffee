TransLink has released an RFP for a Curator to lead a public art process in
partnership with the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations. The
artwork will wrap the exterior of the new SeaBus vehicle and may also include
pieces in the vehicle interior. The Curator will work with the three First Nations
and TransLink to select the artists and work with selected artists to develop site-
specific designs. If interested, the Curator may also facilitate the artwork
fabrication and installation, as well as coordinating project blessing ceremonies
and/or launch events.

For more information, please visit:
[http://discovery.ariba.com/rfx/8765307](http://discovery.ariba.com/rfx/8765307)

Closing date: September 11, 2020

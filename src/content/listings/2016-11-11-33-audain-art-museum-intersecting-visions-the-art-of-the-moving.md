In collaboration with the Whistler Film Festival, the Audain Art Museum is pleased
to host an interactive and engaging evening focused on the work by leading
contemporary artists who choose to express their ideas through film and video. Join
visual artists Fiona Tan, Patrick Bernatchez, Matilda Aslizadeh, Lisa Jackson, Althea
Thauberger and Pascal Grandmaison for a round table dialogue that explores the
ideas, motivation and content found within video art today. The dialogue format of
this event will provide the artists and the audience with a different way in which to
explore and experience how ideas and concepts intersect one another. Time, space,
place and larger global social narratives related to the environment and the migration
of peoples and ideas will be explored.

DATE: Friday, December 2, 2016
TIME: 7pm
COST: Free for Museum Members and with admission

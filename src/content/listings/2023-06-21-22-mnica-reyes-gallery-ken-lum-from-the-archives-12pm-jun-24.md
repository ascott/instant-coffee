SATURDAY, JUNE 24, 12-3pm

Ken Lum: from the archives portrait repeated text works 1993/2023

Royale Projects

Opening Reception: Saturday, June 24, 12-3pm
Exhibition continues until July 30, 2023

602 E. Hastings Street
Vancouver, BC V6A 1R1

Mnica Reyes Gallery is delighted to host Royale Projects (Los Angeles, USA) and
together they invite you to celebrate the 10th anniversary of MRG in Strathcona and
the opening exhibition of
Ken Lum: from the archives portrait repeated text works
1993/2023.

The artist recently rediscovered original negatives and notes for six previously
unrealized works from his acclaimed Portrait-Repeated Text series. This iconic
series of works have been viewed the world over, catapulting Lums aesthetic and
ideas into popular conciseness in the mid 1990s. The saturated-colour photographs
of meticulously staged human scenarios are juxtaposed with bright minimal colour
fields and bold contrasting text that narrate the image placed next to it. Using the
strategies of advertising " this pairing creates a dramatic encounter for the viewer
and shares wry but striking social commentary.

Ken Lum is known for his conceptual and representational art in a number of media,
including painting, sculpture and photography. A longtime professor, he currently is
the Chair of Fine Arts at the University of Pennsylvanias School of Design in
Philadelphia. He was formerly Professor of Art at the University
of British Columbia,
Vancouver where he was also Head of the Graduate Program in Studio Art; Bard
College, Annendale on Hudson, New York, and the lEcole des Beaux Arts, Paris.

As an artist, he has a long and active art exhibition record of over 30 years, including
major exhibitions such as Documenta 11, the Venice Biennale, Sao Paolo Bienal,
Shanghai Biennale, Carnegie Triennial, Sydney Biennale, Busan Biennale, Liverpool
Biennial, Gwangju Biennale, Moscow Biennial, Whitney Biennial, among others.

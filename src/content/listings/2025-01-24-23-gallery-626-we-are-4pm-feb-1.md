Gallery 626 is excited to present We Are, a group exhibition introducing the seven
resident artists who are the creative team behind Gallery 626. Operated by a cohort of
emerging artists, Gallery
626 is located in the heart of Chinatown at 626 Main Street. As
a new creative space, Gallery 626 aims to exist as a platform for experimentation,
collaboration, and critical dialogue. This show brings together a diverse range of
paintings and sculptural works, exploring the individual and collective identities within
Gallery 626. Through this exhibition and its ongoing initiatives, Gallery 626 aims to
challenge the dominant dialogue within contemporary art culture and examine the
evolving conditions of artistic emergence.

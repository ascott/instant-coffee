Call to Artists - Existing & Site-Specific Artwork
Redesign of Commercial Drive Branch
Vancity Credit Union
Vancouver, BC
Eligibility: Open to artists or artist teams who are Canadian citizens or have 
permanent resident status, and reside in Vancouver, BC
Budget for the Acquisition of an existing artwork: $1,500
Budget for the commission of a site-specific artwork: $4,500 plus a materials 
budget
Deadline for digital applications: Wednesday, December 4, 2013 (postmarked)
Deadline for mailed applications: Friday, December 6, 2013 - 11:59 am

The Vancity Credit Union invites artists and artist teams living in the city of 
Vancouver to submit applications for two artist calls as part of a redesign of 
its Commercial Drive Community Branch. The two calls are for the acquisition 
of an existing artwork as well as for a commission of a site-specific artwork. 
Artists and artist teams can apply separately for both. The selected artwork 
and artwork by commission will be for two separate walls inside the 
Commercial Drive Community Branch.

Detailed information about the two calls can be found at 
[http://www.vancity.com/calltoartists2013](http://www.vancity.com/calltoartists2013)

The Moveable Feast: Food Workshops
August 2012

This summer the Burnaby Art Gallery presents The Moveable Feast, a 
public project by Vancouver artist Holly Schmidt. The Moveable Feast is 
a series of workshops, edible events, free youth programs, and a 
vegetable garden in Deer Lake Park. The Moveable Feast responds to 
the current global mobility of food in the context of rapidly dwindling 
food varieties.

For the month of August there will be a series of workshops focused on 
preparing and sharing food.

In this three-hour workshop, Brian Howell, well-known Vancouver photographer, will reveal how he 
tells stories with his camera. Howell will use examples from a wide range of his work to show how an 
idea can evolve into a photo essay, an exhibition or even a book. The discussion will be lively, and 
participants should feel free to bring along examples of their own work (prints or digital files).

The workshop will be held at Studio 42, Howell's fabulous new gallery in New Westminster, BC, in a 
historic Edwardian-era building just minutes from the Skytrain station.

Saturday, June 27, 2009
1:30 p.m. — 4:30 p.m.
Studio 42
42 Begbie Street, New Westminster, BC

(one block east of the Skytrain station)

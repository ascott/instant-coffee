Debi's (Teenage) Theosophical Enlightenment
Deborah Edmeades

January 13 - February 17, 2024

Afternoon Projects is pleased to present ()
, a solo exhibition by Vancouver-based artist Deborah Edmeades with
a text by Lauren Lavery. Edmeades invites the viewer to perceive a history of
cosmological ideas through six acrylic panels shaped like 18th century signage.
Dreamily installed throughout the gallery, the chapters beckon the viewer to move
through both physical and metaphorical time and space, sharing the perspective of
teenage Debi as she time-travels through six historical scenarios
from the
Enlightenment era.

Deborah Edmeades was born in London, England and now lives and works on the
unceded territories of the slilwta, Swxw 7mesh and xwmkwy" m. She roots
her practice in performance, which has continued at times in the form of therapeutic
and spiritual inquiry. More recently she has been investigating the historical
underpinnings of New Age religion, especially in its relationship to the feminine
struggle for spiritual authority.

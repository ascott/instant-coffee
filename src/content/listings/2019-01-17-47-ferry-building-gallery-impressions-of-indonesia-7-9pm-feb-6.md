Ultimate Traveller: Impressions of Indonesia

Wednesday, February 6, 2019 7"9 p.m.

Ferry Building Gallery
1414 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver, BC V7T 1C2

Join us for a series of imaginative travel talks on faraway places by geographer and 
travel photographer Peter Langer. Discover the wealth of human history, the natural 
beauty of our planet and explore a diversity of cultures. Experience the magic of 
these fascinating worlds through a beautiful audio-visual journey.

You will also hear Peter's impressions, curious anecdotes, frustrations and some of 
the little secrets these places have to offer. Peter Langer has travelled to over 175 
countries.

In this fascinating audio-visual journey, we will explore India from a historic and 
cultural point of view, discovering the subcontinent's art, architecture, geography and 
magnificent cultural heritage, including the religions of various civilizations that have 
occupied the land. You will view images that evoke the natural and cultural diversity 
of the country and gain a deeper understanding of India's subtleties, which is 
especially valuable if you're a potential traveller there.

We start in Delhi, the capital and continue to Agra, Khajuraho, Varanasi, Haridwar 
and Rishikesh. We then continue to explore the states of Punjab, home to the 
Golden Temple, the desert cities and hill forts of Rajasthan, and the outstanding 
cultural heritage of Gujarat.

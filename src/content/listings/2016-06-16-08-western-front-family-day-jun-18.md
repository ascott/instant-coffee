Family Day at Western Front
303 E. 8th Avenue
Saturday June 18 from 12-5pm
Free

Join us for the inaugural Family Day at Western Front, a day full of 
artist-led activities for kids. The special event features a hat-making 
workshop with artists Kathleen Taylor and Kara Hansen, drawing 
scores with pianists Lisa Cay Miller and Marguerite Witvoet, an outdoor 
obstacle course by artist Erica Stocking, and lots more. The day 
concludes with a showcase concert of creative music compositions by 
students of Burnaby North High Music & Technology Program. 
Admission is free and open to all. 

More information and full schedule at front.bc.ca.

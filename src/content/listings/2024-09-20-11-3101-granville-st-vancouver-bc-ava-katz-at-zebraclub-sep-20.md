Closing Reception: Ava Katz at Zebraclub
Fri September 20th from 6"8pm
Artist will be in attendance
Light refreshments will be served
3101 Granville St, Vancouver BC

Through textile manipulation and pigment application, I create vibrant playful objects
that speak to my experience of gender. Historically, fiber art has been deemed a
womans craft and has been devalued in comparison to more masculinized forms of
production. My work celebrates these overlooked forms of labor and subtly resists
projections of a fixed binary identity. By adorning soft malleable forms with spikey
protrusions, I am offering them a form of protection and bodily autonomy. Drawing on
the resilience and adaptability found in the queer community, I envision my
sculptures
as living beings,
evolving in response to their environment.

Ava Katz was born in 1997 in Chiin-u, Moldova and they are currently living and
working in Vancouver, BC, Canada. They received their BFA from Emily Carr in 2024
and were awarded the Bill Reid Visual Arts Scholarship. Their work has been
exhibited
in The James Black Gallery, The Michael OBrian Exhibitions Commons, and the
Well
Studios. They have been featured in Akimbo magazine, The Georgia Straight, and
interviewed for Vancouver Co-op Radio.

Drawn Our Way
July 16 - August 8 2009

Opening Reception:
Saturday July 18th 12-3
With guest speaker Ann Kipling, beginning at 12:30

As part of the first Vancouver's Drawn Festival, the Douglas Udell Gallery is excited to announce
a group exhibition Drawn Our Way.

Drawn Our Way brings together a group of artists who use drawing as their main medium to 
create dynamic and thought provoking works. The exhibit will consist of new work by gallery 
artists, including: Michael Batty, Graeme Berglund, Ann Kipling, Justin Ogilvie and Ehren Salazar.

Please join us Saturday, July 18th at 12:30 to hear Ann Kipling speak about her recent work and 
her thoughts on drawing.

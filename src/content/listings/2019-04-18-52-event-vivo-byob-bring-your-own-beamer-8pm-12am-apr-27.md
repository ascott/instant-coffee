BYOB (Bring Your Own Beamer) 
Saturday, April 27, 2019
8pm-12am
VIVO Media Arts Centre
2625 Kaslo St, Vancouver, BC
Free for participating Artists
Admission:
$5 for Members
$10 for Non Members

VIVO Media Arts Centre presents our annual fundraiser Bring Your Own Beamer 
(BYOB)! The event, conceived by Dutch-Brazilian artist Rafal Rozendaal, is a one-
night screening where artists display their video or film work using their own projector. 
Formats of all varieties will be presented simultaneously, across the walls of VIVO's 
2,000 square foot studio space.

Film, video, interactive video performances, overhead projectors, large format slides, 
looped and live! 

WANT TO SIGN UP AS A PARTICIPATING ARTIST?
There is still time! If you would like to present as a participating artist, please email 
info@vivomediaarts.com with your file/video format of choice and tell us what you'd like 
to project. 

We are looking for interested participants to sign up and present their work. 
This could include visual expirments, short or long animations, home videos, DIY 
experiments, cutting edge video art, interactive installations, web-based projects, film 
projections, or a video game you've created! If it needs a projector, and you want to 
show it, sign up! Sign-up deadline is 5pm on April 23rd.

*This is a fundraiser. Door and bar revenue goes towards preserving VIVO's technology 
archive*

A Mysterious Attraction: A new spin on gravitational art

Edzy Edzed | Pierre Leichner | Bill Westwell

Meet the artists, Edzy Edzed, Pierre Leichner and Bill Westwell, at a Virtual Opening
Reception and tour of A Mysterious Attraction: A new spin on gravitational art.

Gravity is a fundamental force for our existence. Yet its very essence remains a mystery. In
this body of work, the artists explore the force of gravity artistically, using it to create and
activate pieces in this mixed media installation. The exhibition aims to engage viewers
visually and physically in the experience.

The exhibition runs February 25 to April 1, 2021. Enjoy this show by visiting the Gallery or
viewing the online exhibition at PoMoArts.ca. PoMoArts Gallery is open daily (except
statutory holidays). Check the website
for hours and our COVID 19 Safety Plan.

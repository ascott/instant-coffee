Family Day | Hidden Structures

Saturday, October 27, 12-3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

On the last Saturday of each month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition 
tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Explore Dove Allouches exhibition, Negative Capability through a tour with Family Day 
Coordinator Eric Benedon featuring natural specimens from the Beaty Biodiversity 
Museum. Families are invited to engage with the artwork and natural objects through 
multimedia drawing activities.

No reservation required, simply just turn up!

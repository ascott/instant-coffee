XINEMA [zin-em-a] is an artist-run experimental film series founded and based on
the traditional, ancestral and unceded territory of the Musqueam, Squamish, and
Tsleil-Waututh Nations (Vancouver, BC). They began operation in VIFF Centres 41
seat Studio Theatre, with a focus on BC-based and connected media artists,
stringing emerging and established artists together into monthly thematic programs.
Their main priorities are to remain low-barrier, accepting free ongoing and
unlimited
submissions of any year or premiere status; prioritize underrepresented artists and
media forms; and connect filmmakers and film-lovers of various backgrounds,
disciplines and career levels.

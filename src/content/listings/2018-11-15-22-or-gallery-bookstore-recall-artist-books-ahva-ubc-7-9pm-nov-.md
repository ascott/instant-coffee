Cathy Busby's Printed Matter class invites you to join them for a One-night Show at Or 
Bookstore on Tue Nov 20, 7 - 9 pm.

Or Gallery
555 Hamilton St, Vancouver, BC

From Geocam footage to predictive text poetry; from self-portraiture in all the names she's ever 
been called to Asian stereotypes in Hollywood film; from lost language reclamation to fast 
fashion and sweatshop labour, this term's students have been tackling tough subjects and 
finding voice through printed matter. Come and see the results. Some work for sale.

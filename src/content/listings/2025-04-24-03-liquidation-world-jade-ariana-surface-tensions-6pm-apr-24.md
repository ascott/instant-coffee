Jade Ariana: Surface Tensions
Opening April 24th 6-9pm
Liquidation World
1965 Main St. Vancouver BC
liquidworld.vision
@liquidationw0rld

April 24 - May 8, 2025

Liquidation World is an art gallery again (for now)! Please join us for the first of our
Spring/Summer series of exhibitions. Our first show features new paintings by
interdisciplinary artist Jade Ariana.

Jade Ariana paints toward a poetics of possibility, where abstraction and
representation are not opposites but echoes. Through this slippage, she opens
space for a lexicon of meaning that is both deeply personal and expansively political
"insisting that Blackness, queerness, and art itself are terrains to be navigated with
curiosity, care, and complexity.

In Surface Tension each canvas navigates a continuum between figuration and
abstraction, where bodies emerge and recede like memories recalled in fragments.
At one glance, a face; at another, a gesture suspended in chromatic haze. These
paintings are not simply representations " they are sites of inquiry, holding space
for multiple readings, layered identities, and unresolved histories.

Surface is never just surface. Color is not only seen but felt; texture is a mode of
remembering emotional and cultural signifiers, tracing connections to Black life,
resistance,
celebration, and interiority. Each canvas is a topography of touch. There is a tactile
density to these pieces, as if every mark made is in conversation with what came
before it, what surrounds it, and what it resists. This material richness becomes a
metaphor for the complexity of Black subjectivity, which can never be fully captured,
only gestured toward.

Bio: Jade Ariana is a multidisciplinary artist living in Vancouver, BC. She works
across the genre of painting, performance, sound, and installation. Jade paints what
she sees in visions, dreams, and from visceral responses to her study of personal
and generational histories.

Art Sundays: Remnant and Retake with Niloofar Miry
October 27
2-3 p.m.

Explore the technical and conceptual aspects of Miry's work in her series Remnant & Retake,
with a focus on its philosophical and historical references to culture, land and the juxtaposition
of the ancient and the contemporary.

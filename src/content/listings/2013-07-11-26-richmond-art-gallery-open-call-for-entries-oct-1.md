Open Call for Entries Richmond Art Gallery

Memory: International Mail Art Exhibition and Swap

Mail Art (aka Postal Art) is a long-standing art practice where artists
collaborate and exchange artworks through the postal service. Artists from
all over the world have been invited to mail in original postcards based on
the theme of memory for this 2nd biennial exhibition. This broad theme
may include individual memories, collective memory, a favourite memory,
memory loss, memorization, in memoriam, and any other interpretations of
the word memory.

This exhibit is not juried and anyone is welcome to participate to maintain
the egalitarian tradition of Mail Art. The exhibition is intended to be a
cultural exchange for both the artists and the gallery audience. Artists
are able exhibit their work in the Art Gallery and online, and then receive
a new artwork at the end of the exhibit as part of the swap. For the
viewer, the exhibition presents a glimpse into a considerable number of
artists works from all over the world, many of whom may have never before
exhibited in Canada.

All artworks received that fulfill the guidelines will be exhibited in the
Richmond Art Gallery and online.

All artworks will be traded with another artists works at the end of the
exhibition.

Exhibition: November 17, 2013 to January 6, 2014

Opening Reception: November 16, 2013 from 3:00-5:00pm

Submission Deadline: October 1, 2013 (postmark deadline)

Theme: Memory

Size: 4 x 6 in (10.2 x 15.3 cm) postcard (only works of this size will be
included in the exhibition)

Medium: Any medium that can be sent through the postal system, original

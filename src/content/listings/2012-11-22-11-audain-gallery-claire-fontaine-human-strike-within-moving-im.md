Claire Fontaine: Human Strike Within Moving Images

Lecture and Screening

Saturday November 24 at 7:00 PM 

Djavad Mowafaghian Cinema, Simon Fraser University, Goldcorp Centre for 
the Arts, 149 West Hastings Street, Vancouver BC

As the Audain Artist in Residence for the fall of 2012, Claire Fontaine worked 
with students from the MFA Program at the School for the Contemporary Arts 
at Simon Fraser University and the MFA in Visual Art Program at the 
University of British Columbia, screening a selection of films and leading a 
series of seminars exploring the notion of the “human strike.” Claire 
Fontaine’s engagement with the students culminates in a final presentation 
and dialogue, featuring a talk and a screening of excerpts from the films she 
selected.

Please join us for this free public event.

About the human strike:

The notion of the human strike emerged out of the context of Italian feminism 
in the 1970s. It is a radical, all-encompassing form of action challenging the 
fundamental contours of everyday life, past the politics of labour to the point of 
human social reproduction. An explication and further theorization of this 
notion can be found in these texts, available online, by Claire Fontaine:

Human Strike Within the Field of Libidinal Economy:
[http://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/claire-fontaine-human-strike-within-the-](http://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/claire-fontaine-human-strike-within-the-)
field-of-libidinal-economy.pdf
Ready-Made Artist and Human Strike: A few Clarifications:
[http://www.clairefontaine.ws/pdf/readymade_eng.pdf](http://www.clairefontaine.ws/pdf/readymade_eng.pdf)
About the exhibition:
Running from October 12 to December 22, 2012, Carelessness Causes Fire 
is the first solo exhibition in Canada by the Paris-based “collective artist” 
Claire Fontaine (aided by her “assistants” Fulvia Carnevale and James 
Thornhill), the Audain Artist in Residence for the fall of 2012.
Presenting old and new works to examine the psychological and political 
consequences of the Arab Spring on the western perception of reality, the 
exhibition uses sculpture, writing, video, and painting as “vectors of a specific 
intensity” to critically explore what Claire Fontaine identifies as the “crisis of 
singularity” and the political impotency she sees in contemporary society.
The ambiguity of the exhibition’s title, lifted from a security warning found by 
the artist under an office chair, evokes both the metaphorical fire of the 
uprising as it invaded streets and buildings and the actual fire of weapons that 
often accompanied it.
Carelessness Causes Fire is realized with the support of the Consulat général 
de France in Vancouver, and presented in partnership with the Institutions by 
Artists conference, which ran from October 12 to 14, 2012, at Simon Fraser 
University at the Goldcorp Centre for the Arts.

Woo After-School Special: Community and Participation
Tuesday, March 24, 2009
Emily Carr University (open to the public)
Rm 410B (South Building)

A conversation about local contemporary participatory practices with
special guests:

Ray Hsu is a Post-Doctoral fellow at UBC that uses art as a tool for social
transformation, working on projects with schools in the Downtown Eastside.

Lee Lakeman is described as a "daring and dedicated feminist pioneer,
activist, and visionary" who works with Rape Relief Vancouver.

Carlo Sayo is part of the Sinag Bayan Cultural Arts Collective and works
with the Kalayaan Centre (a community centre for filipino immigrants). His
work and that of the collective explores immigrant challenges and
identities through performances and fashion shows.

For more information, please contact: woo@ecuad.ca

I am writing from Luma Quarterly, an online publication about film 
and media art, published by the Calgary Society Of Independent 
Filmmakers and EMMEDIA Gallery & Production Society. 

Luma includes critical essays, news, reviews, event previews, 
interviews, reflections, and photo/video essays about culturally 
relevant productions, events and ideas. We aim to expand critical 
dialogue about media art and film from a Western Canadian 
perspective on an international platform.

As we approach the end of our first year, Luma hopes to develop its 
regional perspective by reaching out to organizations, institutions, 
and individuals outside of Calgary. Any suggestions for upcoming 
film and media art events, artists, exhibitions, writers, publications 
etc. in your city/region throughout the coming year would be very 
much appreciated. 

Our call for submissions is now open for issue 006 - deadline 
August 22, which will be launched in early November. Please take a 
moment to share our call with your colleagues and membership in 
your newsletter/social media: [http://lumaquarterly.com/submissions/](http://lumaquarterly.com/submissions/)

Thanks so much for your help with this- we are very much looking 
forward to connecting with you to expand Luma's reach, and to 
more fully represent film and media artists and writers in Western 
Canada.

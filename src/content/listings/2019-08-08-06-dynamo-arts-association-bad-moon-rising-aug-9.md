BAD MOON RISING

Viewing by appointment from AUGUST 9

CLOSING EVENT AUG 16

Dynamo Arts Association

30 E 6th st

Justin Patterson works across mediums in sculpture, drawing, sound, and video, and 
has been involved in a number of collaborations over the past ten years. 

As much a consideration of oral histories and obscure familial remembrance as an 
emotive meditation, Bad Moon Rising includes a group of graphite works on canvas. It 
will be his first exhibition of solo work in Vancouver. The installation can be viewed by 
appointment beginning August 9th and will culminate in a closing event on August 16th. 

Patterson's work has been shown and performed locally with Michelle Helene 
Mackenzie, John Brennan, Elisa Ferrari, and others, and exhibited with Alison Yip, Ron 
Tran, and Kelly Lycan at Monte Clark, Burrard Arts Foundation, and Charles H. Scott. 
He has also exhibited and performed internationally and nationally with Arbour Lake 
Sghool, Stacey Watson and Justin Gradin.

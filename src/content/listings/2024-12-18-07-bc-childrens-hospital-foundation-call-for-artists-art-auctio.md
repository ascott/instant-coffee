Call for Artists: Art Auction Fundraiser

Event Overview
This art auction fundraiser, held with the support of BC Childrens Hospital
Foundation, aims to provide a unique opportunity for BC-based artists to showcase
their work while supporting the future health and well-being of BCs children. A final
selection of 25 artists will be chosen, with two artworks each featured in the auction.

Artwork Criteria

Size Limit: All artworks must be a maximum of 12 x 12.
Media Accepted: We accept 2D and 3D works, including paintings, sculptures, and
mixed media. The artwork should be
appropriate for all ages.
Theme: Artwork inspired by preservation, legacy, or nature is encouraged but not
required.
Submission Limit: Artists may submit up to 5 pieces; a maximum of 2 pieces per
artist will be accepted.
Entry Fee & Compensation

Entry Fee: $5 per artwork submitted.
Profit Sharing: Artists will receive 40% of the profit from the sale of their artwork.
Artist Benefits

Recognition: Participating artists will be featured in our event catalog, on our
website, and on social media.
Exposure: Art will be displayed to a wide audience, providing visibility and
connection opportunities with collectors, donors, and art enthusiasts.
Certificate: Each artist will receive a certificate of participation.
Important Dates

Submission Deadline: December 22, 2024
Notification of Acceptance: December 24, 2024
Auction Date: 26 Jan 2025
How to Submit
Please submit through Google form :

Artwork Images: High-resolution images (300 DPI) of the artwork(s).
Artwork Details: Title, dimensions,
medium, and any special notes about your piece.
Artist Bio: A short bio (up to 100 words) for inclusion in auction materials.
GOOGLE FORM: https://forms.gle/CBmRtb9ckxnvKR3BA
Event Terms

Artists grant permission for their artwork images to be used in event promotion.
Artworks are non-returnable; all pieces selected will support the fundraising mission.
Artists are responsible for shipping or delivery costs.

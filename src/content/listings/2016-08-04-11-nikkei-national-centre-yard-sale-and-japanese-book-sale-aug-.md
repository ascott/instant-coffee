Indoor Summer Yard Sale and Japanese Book Sale

Yard Sale: Saturday, August 13, 2016, 10am-4pm

Book Sale: Saturday, August 13, 2016, 10am-4pm
&
Sunday, August 14, 2016, 12pm-4pm

Nikkei Centre
6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby (Kingsway & Sperling)
604.777.7000|www.nikkeiplace.org

Thousands of second-hand items will be on sale on Saturday,
August 13 at the Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre. Most
of the items sold will be on bargain prices, so take this time to find
something that fits you! In addition, food vendors will serve you a
delightful selection of popular Japanese festival snacks, food and
drinks.
Furthermore, we will be holding a book sale on both Saturday,
August 13 and Sunday, August 14. With over 30,000 great
conditions used books, comics and magazines for a discounted
price, the book sale is renowned to be the largest Japanese book
sale in town! Find something for your new hobby you are picking
up, or become adventurous and pick up a new comic series!

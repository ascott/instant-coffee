SOUR VS SOUR VS SANTA and new text by Zo Chan

Other Sights for Artists Projects and artists Hannah Jickling and Helen Reed 
present the SOUR VS SOUR chocolate bar, a collaborative confection produced 
with grade 3/4 students from Queen Alexandra Elementary School and East Van 
Roasters, Vancouver's only bean-to-bar chocolatier. SOUR VS SOUR combines 
organic 70% Madagascar chocolate with a one-two punch of synthetic and 
natural flavours: Lemonade Wild Cherry Nerds battle it out with candied lemon 
rind and dried sour cherries on a bed of bitter chocolate. Who takes the prize? 
You decide. SOUR VS SOUR is available to purchase for $8/bar at: READ 
Books, the Or Gallery bookstore, East Van Roasters and online via the Big Rock 
Candy Mountain website. 

In conjunction with the public release of the chocolate bars, independent curator 
Zo Chan's commissioned essay "Big Rock Candy Mountain: Travels in 
Chocolate,Candy Culture and Kid Creativity" is published on the project website.

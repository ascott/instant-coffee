ROUNDTABLE | Online | (Re)building the Art World Together: Which opportunities with
NFTs?| JAN 27, 7PM

The new "phenomenon" of NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens), inviolable property titles via
the blockchain, can be
applied to any digital object: photo, video, music, video game
element (and even a tweet or an excerpt from a sporting event).

To own the title of these "crypto arts" millions of dollars can be at stake. In addition to
the new speculative bubble aspect, NFTs also promise a new experience in online
video games or museums, and to revolutionize the creation-distribution model of
works.

During this roundtable organised by Alliance Franaise Vancouver and the Embassy of
France in Vancouver, join Dorothy Woodend, Jesse McKee, Philippe Pasquier and
Rhea Myers, artists and professionals from the artistic, technological, and academic
world to understand what opportunities NFTs offer in terms of creation, diffusion, and
cultural consumption.

The discussion will be held in English.

Richmond Art Gallery

Free Workshop for Adults
MIXED MEDIA COLLAGE WITH LYSE LEMIEUX
Saturday, June 18, 2016
1:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Explore the art of mixed media collage by creating text and image 
works using all kinds of materials. View the gallery exhibition A GIRL'S 
GOTTA DO WHAT A GIRL'S GOTTA DO, then join the artist Lyse 
Lemieux for some "cut and paste" fun. All materials provided, all levels 
of art skills welcome. 

Recommended for ages 16+. 
No registration required, admission is by donation.

More details at www.richmondartgallery.org

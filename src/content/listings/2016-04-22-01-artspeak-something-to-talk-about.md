The Board of Directors is pleased to announce that BOPHA CHHAY 
has been appointed the new Director/Curator of Artspeak. Chhay is the 
co-founder of livedspace, a research and publishing organization, and 
has worked as Publications Manager at Enjoy Public Art Gallery (New 
Zealand); Editorial Assistant at Afterall, Central Saint Martins College 
of Arts & Design (UK); Projects Coordinator at 221A (Vancouver); and 
most recently, Curatorial Assistant at Charles H. Scott Gallery 
(Vancouver).

Chhay completed her MA in Art History at the University of Auckland, 
focusing her graduate work on Simryn Gill. In addition to her 
employments, she has worked on curatorial projects at The Gus Fisher 
Gallery, Objectspace, and 221A; written for Frieze, Decoy, Bartleby 
Review, and Public; and produced commissioned texts for Alterations 
Project Space, Enjoy Public Art Gallery, Dunedin Public Art Gallery, 
and Artspeak. Since moving to Vancouver in 2012, Chhay has been 
active in the community as a writer, curator, organizer, and publisher. 
She is a board member of Projectile Publishing Society, part of the 
editorial team of Bartleby Review, and co-founder and editor of 
Charcuterie, a new publishing initiative. Her most recent independent 
project is For Whom Can We Speak?, a shadow puppet play looking at 
the conditions of women workers in garment factories in Southeast 
Asia.

Chhay will assume the position of Director/Curator in May 2016. The 
Board is thrilled to welcome Chhay and anticipates an exciting new 
chapter for Artspeak.

The Board would also like to thank Kim Nguyen for an incredible 5-year 
term as Director/Curator of Artspeak. During this time Kim developed a 
dynamic and exciting curatorial program, initiated far-reaching 
partnerships, consolidated the archives and library and successfully 
advanced the organization's facility plans, through to the recent 
acquisition of a second space for Artspeak. Her efforts, vision and 
formidable wit further established Artspeak as an energetic site for 
contemporary arts activity in Vancouver. Thank you Kim for your 
excellent work. We wish you the best in your future endeavours.

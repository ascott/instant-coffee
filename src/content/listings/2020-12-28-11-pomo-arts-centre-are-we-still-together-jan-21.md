Are We Still Together?

Ghislain Brown-Kossi

Join us for a Virtual Artist Talk and Tour with Ghislain Brown-Kossi, January 21, 2021 at
7:15 pm, on Facebook Live.

A French artist based in Vancouver, Ghislain Brown-Kossi has been always surrounded by
Art and Fashion since his childhood in France. His work aims to understand how individuals
interact with each other and how social relationships are transformed. As time passes he
wonders if, as a society, we will break the walls and barriers between our diverse
communities. When we look at each other we dont really take time to talk or to have a real
conversations and
this can cause a separation. People often fear new comers and change,
but at the same time they would like to know more about them. In Are We Still Together,
Ghislain wanted to create a body of work that addresses this feeling of disconnection, and
spur a dialogue in our communities.

Enjoy this exhibition with an in person visit to the Gallery or by viewing the online gallery on
PoMoArts.ca. Open 7 days a week, check the website for hours and our COVID 19 Safety
Plan.

Exhibition runs January 14 " February 15, 2021

Are you a comic book artist, geographer, graphic designer, professional adventurer, rigger, engineer, 
sculptor, pianist, potter, or pastry chef? Are you interested in a challenge outside of your usual 
discipline? If so, we want to get to know you better. FM Machine Shop, a new Seattle design firm,
has a fun project for an independent contractor to create a work made for hire. We’ve come up with 
a new way of looking at and adventuring inside of cities and need your help to translate this vision 
into printed product that people can hold in their hands. 

You should be have access to and be well versed with the latest design tools, specifically Adobe 
Creative Design Suite. We’ll offer payment for the initial work, but there is a possibility of additional 
design work for the right person. Payment is negotiable depending on experience. 
Interested? Send a copy of your resume, samples of your work (can be image files or simply URLs),
and anything else you think we should know to info@fmmachineshop.com. If we think you could be 
a great fit, we'll get back to you. All submissions will be held in the strictest confidence.

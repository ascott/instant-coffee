The Banff New Media Institute (BNMI) is an internationally respected art and digital research 
innovation centre. Our programs are designed to support creative pluralism, different modes of 
inquiry, the production of new work, and the engagement of artists, producers, technologists, and
researchers with digital culture. BNMI commissions research, provides training, and offers creative 
production support in a multidisciplinary development environment. Practitioners are able to 
access a range of expertise, peers, facilities, and environments - all in the Centre’s inspiring 
mountain campus.

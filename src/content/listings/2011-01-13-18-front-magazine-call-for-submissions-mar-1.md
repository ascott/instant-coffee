Front Magazine // call for submissions // open-themed spring issue

Send us the best of whatever you’re working on for our just this once 
and never to be repeated open-themed issue.

Front Magazine is a journal of cultural work with a mandate to 
publish new things by emerging and established Canadian (& 
international) artists and cultural workers. Front will consider 
submissions in all forms appropriate to the printed page, including 
text-based work, image-based work, and works combining the two. 
We are interested in true stories. We are especially interested in 
true stories about things happening in Vancouver. 

Submission details at 
http://www.front.bc.ca/frontmagazine/submissions
Email submissions to frontmagazine@front.bc.ca
Read Front Magazine free at zinio.com

The street date for the Spring issue is March 1, 2011

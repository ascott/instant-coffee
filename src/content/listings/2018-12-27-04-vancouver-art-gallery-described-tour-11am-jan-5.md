Vancouver Art Gallery presents a Described Tour of A Curators View: Ian Thom Selects

January 5, 2019 @ 11 AM at the Gallery
750 Hornby St, Vancouver, BC

The Vancouver Art Gallery presents monthly Described Tours for its blind and partially 
sighted visitors starting with a tour of A Curators View: Ian Thom Selects. Trained 
Gallery Educators will use innovative visual description methods to reveal highlights of 
the nearly ninety works including paintings, drawings, photographs, and sculptures 
drawn of from the Gallerys collection as part of this exhibition. Partnering with 
VocalEye, the Gallery offers Sighted Guides to meet adults with sight loss (18 years of 
age and over) at the Burrard Sky Train station and accompany them to and from the 
Gallery. To arrange, contact VocalEye Volunteer Coordinator, Donna, at least 72 hours 
in advance of the tour date via email: buddies@vocaleye.ca

For tickets and additional info, visit vanartgallery.bc.ca

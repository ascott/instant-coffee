Beginner level Alabaster stone carving Workshop

Enjoy shaping translucent alabaster using an assortment of tools to create your own
Table light that will be illuminated with LEDs.

Teaching artist Oliver Harwood.

See his artist spotlight at [https://nwssa.org/sculpture-northwest/artist-spotlight/1265-](https://nwssa.org/sculpture-northwest/artist-spotlight/1265-)
artist-spotlight-oliver-harwood

Saturday and Sunday from 9:00-12:00, April 6th and 7th

Form Association, 1739 Venables St. laneway entrance

More details and registration at [https://formassociation.com/](https://formassociation.com/)

Complete the project on two weekend mornings in a fun group setting.

Class size limited

ABOUT THE CLASS

Working with a
beautiful Spanish white/translucent alabaster, learn how to shape the
stone with hand and some power tools. You will be able to engrave or colour your
stone.

Each participant will receive a LED light stand to incorporate within their stone project.

Enjoy group discussions and seeing various projects evolve over the weekend.

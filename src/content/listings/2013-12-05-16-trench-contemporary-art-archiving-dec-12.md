TRENCH Contemporary Art
148 Alexander Street 
Dec 12 to Jan 18
Opening Reception Thursday, December 12th 6-9 pm

Trench Contemporary Art is pleased to announce Archiving, a group show of Trench artists and historical work. This survey show including Glenn Lewis, Vincent Trasov, Ron Stonier, Enn Erisalu, David Marshall, David Mayrs and Michael Morris draws work from nearly four decades. From a 1965 study for Michael Morris’ 1966 The Problem of Nothing, to Glenn Lewis’ recent 2013 photography, Archiving collects and connects Vancouver’s art historical past to the contemporary. The scope of artists, mediums and decades represents the process and progress of archiving while simultaneously allowing for re-viewing.  Vancouver’s recent art historical past continues to develop in relation to our current environment. Trench invites you to revisit us on December 12th for an opening reception with a festive feeling.

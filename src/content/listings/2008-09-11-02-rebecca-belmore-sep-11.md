03. Monte Clark Gallery | Alison Yip | SEP 11
Monte Clark Gallery Vancouver : Alison Yip, "In the Street" - September
11th to October 11th, 2008

Opening reception Thursday, September 11, 6-9pm at the Monte Clark Gallery
Vancouver, 2339 Granville Street.

The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to announce a solo exhibition entitled
In the Street from Vancouver-based painter Alison Yip.

For her second solo exhibition at the Monte Clark Gallery in Vancouver, Yip
exhibits paintings of her observations of street life in Vancouver, Hong
Kong and Macau.

The new paintings are intimate in scale and continue Yip's developing
personal language of colour and light. The motives derive from the artist's
photographs and sketches made in the street. Many of the figures are
isolated, caught in a moment of reflection or an everyday action such as
waiting for a bus or pausing to clean one's glasses.

Every Sunday in Hong Kong, central downtown zones are blocked off to
accommodate thousands of female Filipino guest workers who picnic in the
streets. This is not a festival or special event, but simply an opportunity
for rest and communion where the women can play cards, read newspapers and
administer manicures to each other. Yip's paintings of these women explore
the forms or typology of repose. Her interest in this classical motif
connects her project to art history, and especially to Impressionism's
interest in the new leisure of modern times.

Like Fairfield Porter, Yip generalizes light and omits fine detail. Spatial
relationships are constructed with carefully tuned colours and both figure
and ground command equal presence. Observational rather than voyeuristic,
her paintings depict the everyday but their primary subject is perception
itself.

Alison Yip was born in Calgary, Alberta and currently lives and works in
Vancouver, BC. She graduated with a BFA from the Alberta College of Art and
Design and has completed a Summer Studio Residency at the School of Visual
Arts, New York, NY.

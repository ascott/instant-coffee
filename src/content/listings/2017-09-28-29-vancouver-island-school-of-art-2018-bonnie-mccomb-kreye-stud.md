The Bonnie McComb Kreye Studio Residency.originated in 2016 and is a partnership of 
the Kreye family and Vancouver Island School of Art (VISA). Artists have the use of a 
private 30 sq. m. garden studio for a residency of 2 months (studio only) or 1 month 
(studio with accommodation). The residency is open to artists working in all media. 
Opportunities are available for artists to give talks and workshops at VISA and works will 
be included in a January 2019 exhibition in the Slide Room Gallery, a professional not-
for-profit independent gallery space located on the lower level of VISA.

2018 Residencies Dates (Deadline: Nov 1, 2017)
January - February (studio)

March - April (studio)
May (studio and accommodation)
June (studio and accommodation)
Sept - Oct (studio)
Nov - Dec (studio)
For application information:
bonniemccombkreye.com

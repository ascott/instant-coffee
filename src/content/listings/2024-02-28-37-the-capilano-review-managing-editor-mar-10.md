marketing, digital strategy, and project management.

encourage applications from members of equity-seeking groups and
those
possessing the skills and understanding to work collaboratively within our diverse
community.

$42,471.37 for part-time status (26 hours/week) with 4 weeks of paid vacation time
and a monthly benefits allowance of $65. An annual cost-of-living salary increase of
4.35% will take effect on January 1st of each year, subject to Board budgetary
approval.

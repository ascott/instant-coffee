You are cordially invited to the last Special Sunday Supper of the
season.

The Special Sunday Supper is an community potluck dinner followed
by an artist talk by a local Vancouver artist. Our season so far has been
a great success, and I want to thank everyone who has participated so
far. I am really looking forward to commemorating the end of summer
with one last Sunday Supper hurrah! Please note that this event begins
at 7:30, not 8 like our previous events.

Kate Henderson is a Vancouver-based artist who received her MFA in
Visual Art from the University of British Columbia in 2013 and her BFA
with a Major in Photography from Emily Carr University of Art and
Design in 2007.

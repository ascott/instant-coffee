Churchmouse
Philip Hinge
Nov 13 - Dec 20, 2025

Except from the exhibition text:

"Cats have nine lives. Thats nine times to grow. Nine times to love and be loved. Nine times
to find faith and nine times to lose it. Nine times to die. The promise kept by recurrence, a
generational selfhood. Rebirth, regrow, relearn, the plight of a perpetual being. These lives
are only worth the weight of their memories. How easy is it to keep track of lives lived and
passed? Maybe try scratch-marks on a wall. Slow drags of a sharp claw against soft stone.
One, two, three, fourAfter the multitude of minutia, the endless routines and rituals, losing
sight of your tally would be all but inevitable. Can you even remember how to count?" -
Philip Hinge

Philip Hinge (b. Seattle WA, 1988) is an artist, curator and project space operator based
in Ridgewood, NY. Hinge holds an MFA in painting from the Virginia Commonwealth
University. His work has been exhibited at a variety of venues, including International
Waters (Brooklyn, NY), Martos Gallery (NY,NY) Brennan & Griffin (NY, NY), 427 (Riga,
Latvia), Final Hot Desert (Utah), Melange (Cologne, DE), mcg21xoxo (Chiba, Japan),
106 Green (NY, NY), Plague Space (Krasnador, RU), Freddy, (Harrison, NY), & Lower
Cavity (Holyoke, MA). In addition to his studio work, Hinge runs and curates two
separate projects, Catbox Contemporary (Ridgewood, NY), and darkzone (NJ).

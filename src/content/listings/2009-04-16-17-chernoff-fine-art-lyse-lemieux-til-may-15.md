Lyse Lemieux: SOLDIERS and VESPERERS
March 26th to May 15th 2009

HOURS
Week Days 9:00 to 5:00
Sunday April 26th 1:00 to 4:30

Soldiers, a series of watercolours on paper, are part of a more private practice that I have been
engaged in for over thirty years. Vesperers are digital paintings and the second of three series 
about faces and portraiture started in 2006. In both bodies of work small gestures reflect craterial 
shifts; retractions and additions unearthing ambiguous states.

SOLDIERS (Watercolour on paper, 2008)
Broken and shamed, the soldiers draw us in. Wounded but standing they remain vulnerable and 
on guard, facing the same enemy, the same fear and the same direction. They salute and they 
dance.

The VESPERER series (Lightjet digital paintings mounted on sintra, 2008) 
Vespererer, inspired from the words Vespers in English and Vêpres in French, is both a noun and 
a verb. In English it becomes a noun as in, a Vesperer, and in French it becomes a verb, 
Vespérerer.
In both languages it implies a state of light and of mind, late afternoon when it is neither day nor 
night - a liminal moment.

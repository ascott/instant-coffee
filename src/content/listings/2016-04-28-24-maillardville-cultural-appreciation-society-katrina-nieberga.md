The Maillardville Cultural Appreciation Society is pleased to present an
exhibition of new work by Katrina Niebergal. Confronting the nature of
interpretation and the limits abstraction, Niebergal's work maintains a
critical relationship with language, highlighting its shortcomings and
examining it as a potential residence for affect.The dialectic of nature
and culture figure within these works yet, the conversation surpasses
simplistic polarization and focuses instead on the space that binary
divisions incite within the subjective human experience. The works in
the exhibition behave like commonplace objects - clothing, pottery,
postcards - with subtly adjusted function and implication.The exhibition
provides a surface of familiarity which, in time,dissipates like a
repeating a word until it exorcizes its banality.

Please note that Lisa Robertson will be joining us for this event 
in place of Kate Eichhorn.

disjunct! performance series is fond of fall, and excited to kick 
off the fall season with two wonderful poets:

Nathalie Stephens & Lisa Robertson
Friday, September 19, 2008
7:00 p.m.
Spartacus Books
684 East Hastings (at Heatley)
Vancouver, BC

$5 donation at the door

Nathalie Stephens (Nathanaël): At Alberta (2008); The Sorrow 
and the Fast of It (2007); L'absence au lieu (Claude Cahun et 
le livre inouvert) (2007); Je Nathanaël (2003 / 2006); L'injure 
(2004); Paper City (2003); etc.

Lisa Robertson now lives in Oakland, where she is Visiting Artist 
at California College of the Arts. She has new books of poems 
coming out with Coach House Books in Spring 09, and in 2010 
from University of California Press. Past titles include The 
Weather, The Men, and Rousseau's Boat. Her current work 
includes a collaborative video, with Allyson Clay and Nathalie 
Stephens, and an ongoing digital sound project with Stacy 
Doris, as The Perfume Recordist.

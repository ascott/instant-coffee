NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS

interdisciplinary works by
KATE BARRY
LOIS KLASSEN
CARLOS COLN MORENO
TRACEY VATH

a not sent letters set by
JEREMY TODD in collaboration w/
TRACEY VATH, PIETRO SAMMARCO, LOIS KLASSEN & KATE BARRY

Saturday Night, November 4, 2017
VIVO Media Arts Centre, 3625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver
$5-$20 suggested sliding scale donation (toward event production costs)
No one will be turned away due to lack of funds.
Doors open at 7pm. The programme begins at 7:30pm SHARP.

NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS form public constellations of engaged
interdisciplinary practice, unmediated by the realpolitik conditions of contemporary 
art as a competitive professional sphere. The interrelatedness of art, society and 
everyday life is critically explored amongst a diverse plurality of artists and publics. 
Jeremy Todd instigates each event as an extension of his ongoing Not Sent Letters 
project, an entanglement with the production of meaning, self and cultural memory, 
involving image/text epistolary detours online (since 2005), digital shorts, 
interdisciplinary performance works and cooperatively realized public events:
http://notsentlettersproject.com

This is the twenty-third NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS event.

KATE BARRY contributes to NSL&G for the first time with the debut of Vagina 
Painting(After Kubota), an homage to artist and Fluxus member Shigeko Kubota. 
Barry investigates the performative capacities of the human body through drawing, 
painting and performance art, considering the phenomenological experiences of 
queer, sexualized and gendered bodies in particular. She is interested in the body as 
both object and subject, as well as the tension in this duality.

LOIS KLASSEN makes her first contribution to NSL&G by illustrating and narrating
Key_ART_Terms, a useful and usable lexicon of industry and culture". Klassen is an
artist and writer whose practice has taken up the nature of social, political and 
economic processes in art. Her artworks have most recently been hosted by 
YACTAC, Anvil Centre, Santa Fe Art Institute, Mentoring Artists for Womens Art and 
more. Her writing has appeared in Fillip Magazine, Border Crossings, Public Journal 
and more. She is currently completing doctoral research in the Cultural Studies 
Program at Queens University on the topic of ethics and art about human migration.

CARLOS COLN MORENO returns to NSL&G to screen video documentation of
Trinkets (a 2012 work that rhetorically questions what/who defines art and benefits 
from it) and discusses the continued relevance of the project to his current practice. 
Coln is an interdisciplinary artist from Mexico City living and working in Vancouver, 
BC, since 2011. He is represented by Fazakas Gallery, and is a PhD candidate in 
Interdisciplinary Studies at UBC. As a Latin American artist, his research and 
practice allow him to investigate and inquire about how our everyday life, mostly in 
Latin America, is reflected and affected by elements and patterns of political, 
religious, social, and cultural activities since colonial times. Of primary focus for 
Colin are Baroque concepts in the Latin American region and how contemporary art 
manifests their inconvenient heritages.

TRACEY VATH contributes to NSL&G for the first time, presenting a new work that 
uses memory, humour, and religious hymn to call forth a feminist utopia. Vath is an 
artist and musician who recently rode the #20 bus in Vancouver for 10 days in a row, 
12 hours each day.

JEREMY TODD presents a new NOT SENT LETTERS SET featuring digital 
projection, scoring by PIETRO SAMMARCO and letters selected and read from the 
project archive by TRACEY VATH (To Instant Ramen Noodles), LOIS KLASSEN (To 
The Creative Classes) & KATE BARRY (To The Invisible Woman).

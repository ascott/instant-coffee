Horizon 21 Public Art Project

Call to Artists

In care of the project owner, Kerkhoff Construction invites artists or artist teams to 
submit concept proposals and samples of past work in consideration for a permanent 
sculpture located near the caf plaza of Horizon 21 development located within the 
Maillardville Neighbourhood at 218 Blue Mountain Street Coquitlam, BC.

The artist/artist team selection will be chosen through a one-stage selection process. 

Total budget: $100,000. 

Eligibility: Open to Canadian Residents 

Submission Deadline: December 1, 2018

For more information and submission requirements please refer to the detailed 
Submission Package.

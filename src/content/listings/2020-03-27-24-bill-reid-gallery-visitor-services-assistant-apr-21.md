JOB / Visitor Services Assistant (Canada Summer Jobs) / Bill Reid Gallery of
Northwest Coast Art

The mandate of the Bill Reid Gallery (BRG) is to reveal the vital and diverse
Indigenous cultures of the Northwest Coast through the extraordinary work of Bill
Reid and inspirational contemporary art. In 2020, the Bill Reid Gallery is excited to
celebrate the 100th anniversary of the birth of Bill Reid, and the 20th anniversary
since the creation of the Bill Reid Foundation, which oversees the operations of the
gallery. We respectfully acknowledge the Coast Salish Peoples on whose traditional
territories we live and work, and all
the First Nations of the Pacific Coast.

The Visitor Service Assistant will work closely with the Visitor Service Manager for
the employment period. The Visitor Service Assistant will focus on customer service
such as greeting visitors to the museum; provide orientation and information about
the exhibitions, and about our organization. The student will work with gallery staff to
research, write and present a daily tour of the exhibitions. The student will also
assist with special gallery program and activities.

This is a unique opportunity for a student interested in Cultural Resources, Tourism,
Public Programming, Museum studies to gain important experience on the front lines
of a non-profit organization. The student will also be immersed in the wonderful
artwork of Bill Reid, and be given the opportunity to gain a deeper understanding of
Indigenous cultures and values. The Visitor Service Assistant must be organized,
able to multi task and perform other related duties as required.

The Gallery Assistant will acquire skills in working with the public, customer service,
handling payments and point-of- sales systems, researching, writing and presenting
public tours. They will gain strategic planning, problem solving and leadership skills
under direction of gallery staff. The setting will provide experience in cultural
diversity, working as a team, and working collaboratively.

- This position is suited for students in the field of Tourism, Cultural Resource
Management, Communications, Indigenous studies, Fine Arts, Museum studies, or
Curatorial studies;
- Must be a full time student between the ages of 15 and 30 and intend to return to
your studies in the fall;
- Indigenous students are encouraged to apply;
- Good level of computer literacy in Office, email;
- Fluent in English, Indigenous language would be an asset;
- Knowledge of Northwest Coast art and Indigenous cultural history an asset;
- Candidates will be chosen on the basis of their skill level and
ability, therefore we
welcome all applicants regardless of race, gender or disability.

Period of employment: May 25 - August 16, 2020 (12 weeks)
Hours: 9:30 a.m. " 5:00 p.m. Wednesday to Sunday (7 hours per day with 1/2-hour
lunch)
Salary: $15.00 per hour

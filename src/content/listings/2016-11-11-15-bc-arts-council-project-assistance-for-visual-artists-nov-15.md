This program assists BC professional visual artists, craft artists and independent 
critics and curators with the creation of specific projects. Awards of up to $6000 
(Category I) or up to $12000 (Category II) are available to support the creation of 
new visual art work. Applications may be made by individuals or a collaboration of 
two or more practitioners who share creative control of a project & who are all 
professional practitioners. Eligibility includes one year of residency in BC prior to 
application (and being a Canadian Citizen or Permanent Resident of Canada aka 
Landed Immigrant) AND having had at least two professionally-curated shows in the 
past (where you have been paid CARFAC fees or equivalent).

If you have not applied before, having a chat with Program Officer Walter Quan 
might be useful to confirm you or your project's eligibility before spending any time 
on preparing an application. 250-356-1728 (preferred) or walter.quan@gov.bc.ca 
(please include your phone number)

New for 2016/2017 for this grant program:

1) Assessment/Reference letters are no longer required for applicants to this 
program.
2) ALL Application materials MUST be submitted on 8.5 x 11 inch paper, single-
sided ONLY, portrait format ONLY. If your application does not fit these 
specifications, it will be disqualified from consideration for funding.
3) Applicants MUST have submitted ANY outstanding final reports for previous 
grants before applying - if you show an overdue final report when your new 
application arrives, your new application will be automatically disqualified from 
consideration for funding.

Postmark Deadline November 15 (but you don't have to wait!)... Results out in 
March 2017.

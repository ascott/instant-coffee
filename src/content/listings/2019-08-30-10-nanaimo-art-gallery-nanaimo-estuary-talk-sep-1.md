Estuary Talk with Tania Willard and Steven Thomas Davies

September 1, 10 am
Meeting Place: Nanaimo River Estuary (West Side), parking lot at 6 Maki Rd., before the 
entrance to Living Forest Campground.
This site has limited accessibility for those with mobility devices or strollers. Call the 
Gallery for details.

Free | Registration required.
Register online or call 250.754.1750

Artist and curator, Tania Willard (Secwepemc Nation) and, artist and filmmaker, Steven 
Thomas Davies (Snuneymuxw Nation) collaborated on an installation that explores the 
submerged perspectives of eelgrass beds in estuarine ecosystems for the exhibition 
Estuary at Nanaimo Art Gallery. On the last day of the exhibition, join them for a special 
dialogue on site, where they will speak about their respective practices, and their 
collaboration.

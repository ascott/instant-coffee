APLAY LAB
Collaborative project between visual artists Angela Smailes and Allison 
Watters.
October, Fri - 9, Sat - 10, Sun - 11 at ThisOpenSpace’s Playground, 
434 Columbia Street, Chinatown.

This week-end show is a layered, extended response to our inventory 
of social interactions, physical experiences and material collections of 
the The Playground neighbourhood. It acts as the showcase of a mini-
walking residency performed by APLAY in the months prior to the 
exhibition. Taking this show as an opportunity to really get to know 
Chinatown from an artistic perspective we kept our eyes open for 
unusual architecture, chance encounters, and detritus both on the 
street and inside of businesses. For the show, we are interested in 
extending our response into movement, sculpture, dance and social 
interactions in a gallery setting. All are invited to join us to interact with 
these materials and each other in our multi modality, multi-day improv 
extravaganza. We, and the ongoing rearrangement of our findings, will 
be available during the whole weekend, at the Friday evening opening, 
and particularly on Saturday and Sunday when APLAY will host improv 
workshops.

Opening Response
The official opening for APLAY LAB is on Friday from 7 to 11 pm. 
Refreshments will be available, and guests will be invited to take part in 
several improv sessions/performances to interact with our materials. 
We will be providing context and specific themes and suggestions to 
inspire people to take part. We also have an open door from 10 am to 5 
pm for anyone interested in being part of set-up and initial warm-up 
improv sessions.

WeAllPlay Workshops
On Saturday, (2pm - 4pm and 6pm - 8pm), 
The workshops will be a relationship between storytelling and 
response. During the residency APLAY collected a series of stories of 
chance encounters with people and material. We have gathered these 
anecdotes into a small publication which will be available for purchase 
at the events. These stories will act as impetus for the workshop.

ThisBelongsToYou Closing 
On Sunday (3pm - 6pm) 
We will continue an improvisational investigation of the detritus 
sculptures which will culminate in an act of exchange. We encourage 
the inspiration of movement to let the sculptures find a person with 
which they belong. Part of the focus on this day will be in letting the 
sculptures in the space leave with the participators, we ask that the 
participants in turn leave an amount of money that they feel represents 
the value of the object and their experience with APLAY.

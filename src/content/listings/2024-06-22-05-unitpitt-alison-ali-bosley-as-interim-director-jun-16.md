UNIT/PITT is pleased to announce the appointment of Alison (Ali) Bosley as our
Interim Director. Ali will be stepping into this role temporarily during the summer
months to oversee programming and operations.

Ali brings a wealth of experience and passion for artist-run culture, as the
Administrator for studio collective Duplex Artists Society since 2021, and founding
member of Vancouver Artists Labour Union (VALU) Co-op. She is also an artist in
her own right, maintaining a painting, drawing, and poetry
practice alongside her
theoretical and academic work"having earned a Masters in Contemporary Art
Theory from Goldsmiths College, University of London, in 2023.

Ali will be working closely with the Board and Director Catherine de Montreuil to
oversee UNIT/PITTs operations, and to support the dynamic community of artists
who are a part of our summer programs. We are grateful to Ali for bringing her
expertise to the table, and are confident that UNIT/PITT will continue to grow and
thrive under her care.

Ali wishes to convey her gratitude for the position, and welcomes any comments,
questions, or feedback as she adjusts to the role. You can reach her at
info@unitpitt.ca.

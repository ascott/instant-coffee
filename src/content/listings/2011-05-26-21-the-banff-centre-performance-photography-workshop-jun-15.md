Call for Applications: Performance Photography Workshop
Program dates: July 17, 2011 - July 23, 2011
Application deadline: June 15, 2011

Faculty: Donald Lee, Trudie Lee, David Cooper

Learn from the experts. Join us for intense and interactive fieldwork,
seminars, and portfolio critique with the guidance of performance photo
experts and award-winning performance photographers. Designed for
photographers with an existing knowledge of the fundamentals of
photography, Performance Photography extends participants a
once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to immerse themselves in the exclusive world
of photographing the performing arts. Over the course of this seven-day
program, faculty will introduce methods of photography for creative
exploration, and participants will delight in The Banff Centre’s
multi-faceted field work opportunities with our Dance, Theatre, Opera, and
Music performers.

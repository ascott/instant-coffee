Curators Tour of N. Vancouver

Saturday, 6 January, 1 PM

Please join us on a tour of The Polygon Gallerys inaugural exhibition, N. Vancouver, led 
by Director and Curator Reid Shier. 

The exhibition features existing and specially commissioned works in the mediums of 
photography, video, sculpture and weaving by artists from the region engaged on the 
specific theme of the north shore. Launching The Polygon Gallery, after a forty-year 
history as Presentation House Gallery, N. Vancouver is conceived as an opportunity to 
witness the imaginative possibilities of visual artworks in discourse with the immediate 
surroundings of the Gallerys new home.

Free with admission by donation

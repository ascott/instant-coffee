LAUNCH PARTY: Capture Photography Festival
Friday, April 1 at 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Roundhouse Community Arts & Recreation Centre
181 Roundhouse Mews, Vancouver,

Join us in celebrating the launch of the third annual Capture Photography
Festival. To kick off the occasion, Presentation House Gallery will
announce the winning artist of the Philip B. Lind Emerging Artist Prize,
whose work will be exhibited at the new Polygon Gallery in 2017, in
conjunction with Capture.

Admission to this event is free.

Make a Stop Motion Film Night with Sean Karemaker
Sep. 19, 6:30 p.m. " 9:30 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada
What better way to have a film night by making them ourselves before sitting back and 
enjoying the show? In this session led by Sean Karemaker we'll split into small groups 
and go from concept to creation in 3 hours, recreating our favourite movie scenes or 
making our own original creations!
We'll have plenty of materials but feel free to bring anything you like. If you have a 
smartphone/iPhone that would be great. Otherwise we'll have digital cameras for use as 
well.
Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our FAQ.
Cash Bar, 19+ only, No Minors, No Outside Drinks.
$45

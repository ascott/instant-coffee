Performance and XR Symposium (virtual reality art conference)

This October, Single Thread and Electric Company Theatre invite you to a series of
interactive presentations and discussions by Canadian artists and digital content

share discoveries and build partnerships for future collaborations.

This event will take place entirely in Virtual Reality on the AltSpaceVR platform.

Event is on October 2, 3, 9,
10

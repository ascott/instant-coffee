Artists Call to Host Nations

Call for application " Fire Station 8 Public Art

Deadline to apply: March 3, 2025

The City of Burnaby seeks a commission by an artist or artists from the Host Nations
on whose territories the City is now located, including including the xmkym
(Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh), and kikm
(Kwikwetlem) Peoples, to create a two-dimensional graphic design to be
incorporated into the faade of Fire Station 8. The design will be fabricated as a vinyl
application onto a 16 x 8 ft aluminum panel, with external lighting. The City of
Burnaby (the City) and Simon Fraser University wish honour the role of hnqminm
and Skwxw7mesh Snchim speaking peoples as the original stewards of these
lands. The goal of this
project is to support the visibility and integration of Coast
Salish design in civic life and to acknowledge the presence of the Host Nations on
their unceded territories for all residents and visitors. The City of Burnaby is
committed to fostering relationships, building knowledge, and acknowledging the
presence of Host Nations on their unceded territories.

About the Opportunity

Adorning Fire Station 8, this opportunity will offer a visual landmark to the greater
community and to Simon Fraser University. Serving the City since 1911, the
Burnaby Fire Department provides fire-related emergency services to save lives,
property and the environment. The facility will provide effective emergency response
operations of firefighting, Wildland Urban Interface, rescue and medical aid and
support the proactive engagement of fire prevention, inspection, code enforcement
and safety education. Located at: 9900 University Drive East

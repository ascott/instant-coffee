Light It Up 

Brigitte Lochhead, Vancouver BC
Carlos Coln, Vancouver BC
Klaus Pinter, Vienna Austria
Mathias MacPhee, Dawson City Yukon
Stuart Ward, Vancouver BC
Trevor Van den Eijnden, Vancouver BC

06 February 2016 - 19 March 2016

The artworks in Light It Up at Arnica Artist Run Centre February 5 to 
March 19, offer a direction in contemporary artistic practice that 
displays subject matter and cultural references. The works are discrete 
views into other worlds and provide a complex relationship between the 
kind of light used and the story being told. For example, Trevor van den 
Eijnden's Lawn, uses surveillance glass to multiply the tiny LED lit pink 
flamingos into a gridded suburbia that seems to extend forever. Stuart 
Ward's works are reminiscent of stained glass windows that feature a 
kaleidoscope of video views of natural phenomena. Other works: 
reflect their surroundings while they emit light (Lockheed); cradle fiber 
optic northern lights between the antlers of an actual caribou 
(MacPhee); use the playful quality of glow sticks to draw the viewer 
through dance steps (Pinter); and spell out ethnic rejection no lugar (no 
place) in the classic invitation of neon signage (Coln).

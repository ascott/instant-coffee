Saturday, July 25 from 7-9pm, Open Space is hosting an online Zoom workshop
Messages of Resistance: A Visual Art Workshop with Kimmortal as a part of the
ongoing Vogue project and series of events.

Kimmortal will be leading a visual art making session on Zoom where participants
will be invited to create an art piece of hand drawn lettering that expresses a
message of solidarity and resistance. In the two hours, Kimmortal will talk about
their current practise and creative curiosities, the group participants will be invited to
share their own thoughts. Then Kim will share some lettering techniques and
art/design suggestions, there will be a
group brainstorm of a list of messages of
resistance and there will be ample time to creatively work. Participants can share on
social media if comfortable!

Please note: advance registration is required to attend! Email office@openspace.ca.

Find out more about the Vogue project and Kimmortals workshop here:
https://openspace.ca/programming/messages-resistance-visual-art-workshop

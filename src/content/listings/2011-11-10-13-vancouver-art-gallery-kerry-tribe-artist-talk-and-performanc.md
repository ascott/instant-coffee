Kerry Tribe - Artist Talk and Critical Mass Performance
Vancouver Art Gallery
Tuesday, November 15
Talk 6:30pm, Performance 8:00pm

Los-Angeles based Kerry Tribe delivers an artist's talk at 6:30pm, 
covering her key film and video works and focusing on the 
relationships among memory, subjectivity and representation.

At 8pm, Kerry Tribe stages a live performance based on Hollis 
Frampton's groundbreaking experimental film Critical Mass (1971), 
performed by LA-based actors Reed Windle and Jasmine Woods. One 
night only.

In conjunction with the exhibition The Distance Between You and Me at 
the Vancouver Art Gallery

Seating is limited. Ticketing is required, first-come, first-serve. Tickets 
available at the Membership Desk starting 5:30pm Tuesday, 
November 15. Limit one ticket per person. Reservations not accepted.

Free for Members or with Gallery admission

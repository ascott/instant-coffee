BLIM ART SHOW (November 2010)

Blim presents:
Mythical Creatures
by Lief Hall

Mystical Creatures is an exhibition of shadow pictures and animation by
Vancouver based audio-visual artist Lief Hall. The illumi- nated cut-outs
portray ethereal landscapes filled with demonic wildlife and cartoon
totems: sharing the space with projections of gently rocking widows in
parallel dimensions.
Lief Hall graduated from the Emily Carr University of Art and Design in
2005 with a Bachelors Degree in Integrated Media and has exhibited and
performed at such places as The Chan Centre, VIVO, Access ARC, The Western
Front and Truck Gallery. Her current collaborative musical projects include
MYTHS and Glaciers.

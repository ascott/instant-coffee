4AM Collective - Somewhere in Particular

Chernoff Fine Art
September 12 - October 17
OPENING RECEPTION: Saturday, September 12, 1-4pm
We will be following COVID-19 guidelines and safety protocols for our opening 
and limiting the number of people in the
gallery, we ask that you wear a mask 
while inside. There will be a parking lot reception, please keep to the 
recommended 6 foot guidelines in the outdoor space.

It is important to consider how the worlds we represent act upon and are 
transformed by the medium in which they are represented, by the maker of that 
representation, and by the viewer of the representation. Our interpretations of 
landscape are relative and reveal just as much about ourselves as they do about 
the landscape we examine. Somewhere in Particular examines the relationship 
between place, artist, and viewer without the specific context or condition in 
which the image was produced. The photos become cultural constructs which 
create and are part of an intricate network of narratives which are both created 
and lived by the viewers who help to construct them.

4AM Collective are a group of Vancouver based photographers. They are 
concerned with the tradition of formalism within photography as well as various 
political and social issues, notions of representation, memory, and experiential 
learning. Their goal is to develop a local photographic community through their 
love of the medium. The collective is made up of four members: Clarissa 
Fragoso Pinheiro, Dane Murner, Matthew Wong and Scott Rossi

Sunday, March 10th, 2024 (1:00PM-3:00PM)
The Gallery at Queens Park, New Westminster, BC
RSVP Here: https://westcoastcreativessalon.com/salons/

Join
contemporary music composer and WCCS Board President Brandon Chow for a
multidisciplinary discussion on the maverick 20th-century French composer Olivier
Messiaen (1908-1992). Throughout the discussion, Chow will highlight the influence of
Messiaens music and musical thinking on his own compositional practice, referring to
Messiaens groundbreaking 1974 orchestra piece Des Canyons Aux toiles as an
example.

The broader applicability of Messiaens preoccupation with harmonic colour, his diverse
musical influences, and his unique musical language will enrich the dialogue. All artists
and community interested in the arts are warmly invited.

For more information, visit https://westcoastcreativessalon.com/.

Redefining Drawing is an opportunity to think outside the box of the
traditional definition of drawing, to see that artists continue to reuse
and invent old and new techniques and methodologies to make their marks, to
spend time looking at the detail and craftsmanship of the artworks, and to
appreciate the aesthetic and intellectual rigor of six uniquely talented
artists who employ drawing as their main medium.

Curated by Rachel Rosenfield Lafo, independent curator and former Director
of Curatorial Affairs at the DeCordova Sculpture Park and Museum in
Massachusetts, the artists, all but one of whom are guest artists to the
Jennifer Kostuik Gallery, stretch the traditional definition of drawing by
using highly unusual techniques and unconventional approaches to create
their artwork.

In recent years the medium of drawing has gained in visibility and
popularity. There are many reasons for this refocus. For one, drawings are

dominated by technology and media overload. Also, from a practical
standpoint, drawings can be a more affordable and diverse choice for
collectors.

Redefining Drawing reveals work characterized by extreme detail and
labour-intensive and time-consuming processes. Playing on the idea of a

whisky bottles with smoke to create drawings of places that are amazingly

see the image, it takes shape at the edges of our memory.

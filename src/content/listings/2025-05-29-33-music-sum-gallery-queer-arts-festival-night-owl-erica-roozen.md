The Queer Arts Festival is thrilled to present the QAF debut of Dutch accordionist
Erica Roozendaal. Visiting us from the Hague, Roozendaal shares an intimate
program at SUM gallery that includes music by the iconic Pauline Olivieros, plus
Ericas own autobiographical showpiece, Night Owl. This beautiful and moving
performance subtly addresses the theme of abuse and growing up in an unsafe
environment. Erica Roozendaal"performer, visual artist, and accordionist"created
the script and performs the monologue in a restrained, captivating manner while
alternating with playing the accordion. Presented in partnership with Vancouver
InterCultural Orchestra.

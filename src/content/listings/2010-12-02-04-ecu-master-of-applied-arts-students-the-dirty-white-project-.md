The Dirty White Project

When: Friday December 3rd 2010
Where: Origins Organic Coffee Roastery. 1245 Cartwright Street. Granville
Island. Vancouver. V6H 4B7
Time: 6pm to 10pm
[sponsored by Origins Organic Coffee and Storm Brewing Company]

The Dirty White Project is an art exhibition fundraiser by the first year
Master of Applied Arts students at Emily Carr University. The exhibition
will feature (18) eighteen graduate students from the Visual Art, Design
and Media Arts streams.

The one evening, one event art show invites the audience to sensorily
engage with the exhibition on display. Examining art outside of the
traditional "white cube" gallery space. The students are interested in
observing what happens when the art is taken out of the white cube. The
intent is to see if the artwork will undergo a transformation over the
course of the evening through interaction with the general public. The

About the Exhibition :

''-' wskwpitkan sacred bundle by Adele ''"' Arseneau honors the
children and survivors of the Residential School system. A total of 161 cradleboard
and mossbag pieces represent every school named in Canadas Indian Residential
School Settlement Agreement. The work was developed by Arseneau and was
supported and assisted by local Indigenous Communities. These works combine
hidetanning, beadwork, carving and community, with the moss-line cradleboards
nestled in the fur of traditional home-tanned animal hides - bear, elk , deer, moose
and buffalo - symbolically returning
the children to comfort of the culture from which
they were taken.

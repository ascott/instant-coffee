Presentation House Gallery, Vancouver, is pleased to present the broadest and most revealing survey of its kind, Lisette Model and Her Successors brings together for the first time a selection of vintage works by Lisette Model—one of the last century’s most significant photographers—and thirteen of her students who went on to leave their own marks on American photographic history: Diane Arbus, Bruce Cratsley, Lynn Davis, Elaine Ellman, Larry Fink, Peter Hujar, Raymond Jacobs, Ruth Kaplan, Leon Levinstein, Eva Rubinstein, Gary Schneider, Rosalind Solomon, and Bruce Weber. This exhibition has been curated by Diana Edkins, Aperture’s director of exhibitions and limited-edition photographs, and Larry Fink, photographer.

ON MAIN launches with an exhibition of	new  art works by Vancouver artist Brian
Boulton, Drawing The Anonymous Subject . A Continuing Body of Work - Phase 2.

Boulton will unveil 6 drawings from a current series in progress.  Phase 1 was “10 Drawings” a solo exhibition at the Or Gallery in 1999. His process combines	interests in  people watching, photography and drawing, specifically that of the  male body. “My subjects in this series are anonymous.  I am drawing a subject some see as completely mundane and for some unkempt, clothes males wear, street wear.

The figures of	contemporary young men in casual attire are framed moving away from our gaze. They are studied glimpses, these rendered moments are evocative and intentionally vague in suggestion.  The images are supple, understated yet captivating. The fresh and classic drawings are rich in detail and exquisitely executed.

Born in England (1955) , raised and educated in BC,his studies include Architectural Drawing + Rendering, Film History	and Art History.  He has collaborated and assisted numerous artists in	the production of exhibitions, installation, video, music, theatre, web sites, graphics and visual staging.  www.brianboulton.com

21. Shudder Gallery | Psychological Truth | MAR 14
Shudder Gallery opening March 14th at 7:00 pm - 11:00pm
Psychological Truth 
Jeremy Hof

Meghann Hubert

Steven Hubert 
Realizing that psychology is seldom a good starting point for explaining aesthetic concerns, but choosing nevertheless to eschew a discussion of formal considerations anyways, the artists in "Psychological Truth" have made works which could be construed as having psychological weight, leaving up to interpretation whether this weight is borne out in the work itself, or whether a psychological value has merely been assigned to the feelings and thoughts of the artists as they went about expressing themselves in preparation for the show.  Psychologisms aside, because it would be a bold move to suggest a connection between artwork and intentionality—or even personality—the paintings, sculpture, drawing and video included in "Psychological Truth" are meant to serve in part as a primer for debate.  To this end, and to confirm any suspicions as may arise, it will be seen as serendipitous that the artists will be in attendance on opening night. 
Jeremy Hof graduated from Emily Carr Institute in 2007.  His work can be understood to deal with sculpture and painting from within a post-minimalist, labour-intensive framework.  He has exhibited at the Museum of Contemporary Canadian Art (Toronto), Helen Pitt Gallery (Vancouver), Emergency Room (Vancouver), Gaff Gallery (Vancouver), Eye-Level Gallery (Halifax), and also was a participant in the 2007 CAG auction, Rapt, and the 2007 Helen Pitt Annual Fundraiser Auction. 

Meghann Hubert graduated from Emily Carr Institute in 2005.  Her serial landscape paintings have recently given way to pictures which seem to deal more with mental constructions than physical facts.	She has exhibited at the AMS Gallery (Vancouver), Emergency Room (Vancouver), Concourse Gallery (Vancouver), and also was a participant in the 2007 Helen Pitt Annual Fundraiser Auction. 

Steven Hubert graduated from Emily Carr in 2007.  A recent project done en plein air will be exhibited in drawing and video for "Psychological Truth".	He has shown at the Helen Pitt Gallery (Vancouver), Emergency Room (Vancouver,) LES Gallery (Vancouver), Video In (Vancouver), Eye-Level Gallery (Halifax), Concourse Gallery (Vancouver), Gaff Gallery (Vancouver), and also was a participant in the 2007 CAG Auction, Rapt, and the 2007 Helen Pitt Annual Fundraiser Auction.  He recently retired as curator of the Helen Pitt Back Gallery (2006-2007).



Show will run from March15th until April 5th. Gallery hours are Wednesday, Friday, and Saturday 12:00 - 5:30. Shudder Gallery is located on 433 Columbia St between Pender St and East Hastings. The telephone number is 778 737 3728 and the web site is www.shuddergallery.com

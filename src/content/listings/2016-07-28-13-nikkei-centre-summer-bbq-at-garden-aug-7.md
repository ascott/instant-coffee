Summer BBQ at Garden
Sunday, August 7, 2016
1pm-4pm
Nikkei Centre
6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby (Kingsway & Sperling)
604.777.7000 | www.nikkeiplace.org

A Nikkei Centre fundraising event, All-You-Can-Eat BBQ will take 
place on Sunday, August 7 at the Nikkei National Museum & 
Cultural Centre! Grilled meats, vegetables, Japanese onigiri rice 
balls, and udon will be served as much as you can eat! Tea and 
coffee will be served for free. Other refreshments and alcohol drinks 
such as beer and wine will be served with additional fees. 

Pre-order tickets are $20 and will be sold until August 3. Under 
twelve years old is $12, under five years old is $5, and under one 
year old is free. Advance tickets only, so make sure to get yours 
early!

For more information on the festival or other programs and events 
held at Nikkei Centre, please visit www.nikkeiplace.org or call the 
centre at 604.777.7000

Attention all knitters and would-be knitters! Breeyn McCarney is a fashion designer and textile artist. 
Her next collection, entitled Hard-Boiled Wonderland will reflect themes of community, 
resourcefulness and hand-made textiles. In this spirit, she is inviting knitters of all skill levels to 
contribute to the project. Excellent knowledge of knitting is NOT a pre-requisite. Beginner, 
intermediate and expert are all welcome. Any colour, any stitch size, any tension - any fibre! Even if 
it's made of old shoe-laces, she wants it. Pieces should be no smaller than three inches square, and 
no larger than eight. All who contribute will be credited on the website and in photos of the finished 
garment. Please send patches to:
Breeyn McCarney
636A Dundas West
Toronto, Ontario
M5T 1H8
Any additional questions can be sent to info@breeyn.com

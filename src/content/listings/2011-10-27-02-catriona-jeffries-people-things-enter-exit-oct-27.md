Guy de Cointet (b. 1934, Paris, d. 1983, Los Angeles) was a French artist based
in Los Angeles in the late sixties and seventies. His enigmatic and until very
recently under-appreciated body of work uses language, performances,
paintings, sculpture and prints to inhabit the poetic space of the written word and
theatre. A major monograph on his work written by Marie de Brugerolle was
published by JRP Ringier in 2011.

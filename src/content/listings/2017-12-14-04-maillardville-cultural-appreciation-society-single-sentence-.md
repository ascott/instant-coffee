Single Sentence Publishing 
Kristine Suddaby
Dec 15 - Jan 20

Single Sentence Publishing is pleased to present a new work by Kristine Suddaby. 
Suddabys practice mines both personal and public histories, reworking their material 
formation through photography sculpture and text. For SSP Suddaby has conducted 
research into Fraser Mills, a key proponent in the founding of Maillardville and the strike 
which occurred in early parts of the 1930s depression. Using census data pertaining to 
the mill, its workforce and the lumber industry as a whole at this time she gives a picture 
of untold racial, social and economic histories within the community. These are then 
played out against some of contemporary data concerning the citys real estate prices. 
Through the interplay of various data Suddabys work illustrates the evolution of the city 
of Maillardville from its beginnings as a mill town, into its current existence as a post 
industrial suburb.

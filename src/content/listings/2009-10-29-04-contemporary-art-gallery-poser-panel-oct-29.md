POSER PANEL: Thursday October 29, 7 pm

At Emily Carr University of Art + Design

Room 301 (South Building), 1399 Johnston St, Granville Island

Poser Panel is a public programming initiative co-presented by the Contemporary Art Gallery and 
Emily Carr University of Art + Design. The panelists include prominent cultural producers from the 
visual arts, the performing arts and academia. Together the evening promises to enlighten all 
those attending to what’s really going on when performers change reality. The panelists will 
discuss the persona of the artist in reference to the CAG’s current exhibition Playing Homage.

Rodney Graham lives and works in Vancouver. One of Canada’s leading artists, Graham has 
represented Canada at such major events as the 1992 Kassel Documenta, the 1997 Venice 
Biennale and the 2002 Biennale of Sydney. A retrospective of his work Rodney Graham: A Little 
Thought, (2004) was organised by the Art Gallery of Ontario, Toronto, and traveled to: The 
Museum of Contemporary Art, Los Angeles, Vancouver Art Gallery and Institute of Contemporary 
Art, Philadelphia. Among other things his work deals occasionally with film, and film history, and 
often includes himself as part of the performance.

Jerry Wasserman received a doctorate in English literature at Cornell University. His acting credits 
include over 150 film roles. Wasserman is currently working on a role in Alien Trespass and 
recently acted in Watchmen, released in March of 2009. He is Professor and Head of the 
Department of Theatre and Film at the University of British Columbia. His influence in cultural 
production has spanned many disciplines, from music history, to popular radio broadcasting.

Lisa Coulthard holds a Ph. D. from the Univercity of Toronto. Her main research falls into the 
areas of film theory and violence. She is particularly interested in film sound and violence, 
contemporary American and European cinemas, continental philosophy and Lacanian theory. 
Currently writing a book titled The Super Sounds of Quentin Tarantino, she has presented her 
work at dozens of conferences, has many articles published or forthcoming in major journals and 
edited collections and is on the editorial board for The International Journal of Zizek Studies.

Damian Moppett lives and works in Vancouver. He is one of Canada’s foremost international 
emerging artists. His art work is analytical, conceptual, and highly referential in both form and
content. The scope of his production varies considerably, often with a consideration of the 
heightened role of the artist as performer. His solo exhibitions include Yvon Lambert Gallery, New 
York, (2009); Temple University Gallery, Philadelphia (2009); Catriona Jeffries Gallery, Vancouver 
(2007); and Carleton University Art Gallery, Ottawa, (2006).

The panel discussion will be moderated by CAG curator and Jenifer Papararo.

Application deadline: 4PM September 28th 2018

Position: Curator

Location: Terminal Creek Contemporary, Bowen Island BC

Remuneration: This is an unpaid position

Description: Terminal Creek Contemporary seeks an energetic self-starter to curate an 
exhibit to be included as part of the Capture Photography Festival in spring 2019. You will be 
given access to a 600sqft gallery space located on Bowen Island BC, all preparatory needs, 
and some costs associated with mounting and presenting an exhibition. We have a modest 
advertising budget to be discussed. We can also offer accommodation on Bowen (1Bdrm 
self-contained suite with full kitchen) during the install process and/or opening weekend at 
no charge. Exhibition will run for approximately three weeks (four weekends) with an 
opening reception on the first Saturday at the commencement of the exhibition. This is a 
great opportunity for new curators or recent curatorial grads who are looking for a unique 
space to realize an exciting idea, or seasoned curators who are keen to reach a broader 
audience outside of the city.

About Terminal Creek Contemporary: TCC is a new periodic alternative art space focusing 
primarily on experimental and critically engaged art practices. Our guest curators actualize 
projects and exhibitions that would not otherwise have a regular audience. We are 
especially concerned with artistic experimentation and lean towards interdisciplinary and 
concept-oriented approaches in a variety of media. For more information please visit our 
website at www.tccontemporary.com

To apply: Please send your brief proposal and current CV by email to 
info@tccontemporary.com by 4PM September 28th 2018.

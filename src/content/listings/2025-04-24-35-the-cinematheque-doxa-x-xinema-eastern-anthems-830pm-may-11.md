Screening:

Eastern Anthems (Canada, 2024)

Matthew Wolkow and Jean-Jacques Martinod

78 min

For years, Canadian filmmaker Matthew Wolkow has been determined to capture
the rare emergence of Brood X cicadas"a phenomenon that only occurs once every
17 years in the eastern United States. However, when the COVID-19 pandemic
forces Wolkow to hand over his project to friend Jean-Jacques Martinod, the films
journey transforms into a deeper exploration of a creative collaboration under
duress. Featuring intimate recordings of the cicadas song, the film delves into
themes of friendship, the creative process, and the passage of time. Expanding the
boundaries of a typical nature documentary, Eastern Anthems offers a poetic portrait
of America on the brink of change.

Content warning: Flashing images, patterns and colours.

DOXA is presented by The Documentary Media Society, a Vancouver based non-

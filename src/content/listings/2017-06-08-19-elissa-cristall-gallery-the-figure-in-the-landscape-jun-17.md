"The Figure in the Landscape"
Liz Toohey-Wiese, Sara Caracristi, Jessica Korderas, Jessie McNeil
June 17 - July 15, 2017

OPENING RECEPTION: Saturday June 17, 1 - 4pm

ARTIST TALK with Liz Toohey-Wiese: Saturday June 17th, 2pm

The Figure in the Landscape takes a look at the narrative in landscape painting and 
the contemporary relationships between identity and space. The narrative has been an 
integral part of Canadian landscape painting whether portrayed in direct representation, 
in historical reference or in memory of real and imaginary places. We seem to have a 
keen desire to connect with nature and the landscape and to connect with other than 
oneself. The works in the exhibition comprise painting, collage and cast resin 
sculptures. Jessica Korderas sculptures are intricate dioramas depicting social 
situations in cabins and cottages - an icon of the Canadian landscape. In Caracristis 
small acrylic and resin paintings, the figures exist in a non-landscape giving us no clue 
to where they may be. But, perhaps, a clue to where they could be - leaving it entirely to 
the viewers imagination.

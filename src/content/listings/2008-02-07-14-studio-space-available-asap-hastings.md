15. 312 Online presents Nomi Talisman & a call for submissions
312 Online makes the following announcements:

CURRENT 312 ONLINE VIDEO
Feb. 2 - 29, 2008 / Nomi Talisman, excerpt from “The Last Thing before the
Last”
View at: http://www.312.ca/312online.htm
(A high-speed connection is recommended for viewing 312 online videos.)

“The Last Thing before the Last” (2006- present), explores connections to
place, history, power and violence, through memories and observations of
everyday moments. The sound combines random scanning from the radio,
speeches and archival audio files with original soundtrack.

Nomi Talisman works with photography, video, and other art forms. She
likes to use the colors orange, blue and has a special fondness for hard
drives, (which probably means she's a geek). She has exhibited her work in
Israel, where she was born, and in England, France, Germany, Switzerland,
Canada and the U.S. Her video work often explores the relationships
between home movies, amateur films and mainstream cinema, and has been
screened internationally. She likes to experiment with sound in her
movies, although she would not call herself a musician. Neither would her
landlord, dog, or her partner. Talisman currently lives in San Francisco,
CA.

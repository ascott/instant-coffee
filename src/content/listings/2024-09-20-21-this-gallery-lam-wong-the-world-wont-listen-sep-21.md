Vancouver, BC, September 6 - 21, 2024 " Lam Wong, a Chinese-Canadian artist
renowned for his evocative and contemplative works, continues to delve into
profound themes with his upcoming solo exhibition. In his new exhibition Lam Wong:
The World Wont Listen, Wong draws inspiration from The Smiths' song Ask (1986)
with the poignant lyric, "If it is not love, then its a bomb, a bomb, a bomb, that will
bring us together" This exhibition assembles a selection of paintings that
encapsulate Wong's reflections on the state of the world, both historically and in its
present tumultuous condition. The exhibition resonates with urgency and a somber
contemplation of our collective fate: "If we dont learn how to love, then we perhaps
dont deserve to live. The stakes are high."

This exhibition is not an isolated inquiry but rather an extension of Wong's consistent
exploration of existential themes and the human condition. Throughout his career,
Wong has been committed to using his art as a medium for meditation on life,
interdependence, and the pressing issues of our times. His previous works, such as
the installation MA No.1 - The Space Between Objects (Griffin Art Projects, 2019),
have often navigated the delicate balance between hope and despair, love and
conflict, inviting viewers to engage in introspection and dialogue.

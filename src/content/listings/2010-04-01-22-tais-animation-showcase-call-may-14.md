TAIS Animation Showcase | Call for Submissions
Have you made an animated short film in the last two years? Want it to
screen to a packed house as part of the fantastic TAIS Animation Showcase
this June in Toronto? Then submit!

All Genres, mediums, themes!
Under 15 minutes in length

Screeners should be submitted as either MiniDV or Quick Time Movie files on
DVD/CD.

Send your submission to the TAIS Office:
Toronto Animated Image Society
TAIS Showcase 2010
60 Atlantic Avenue, Suite 102
Toronto, Ontario
M6K 1X9
Canada

Please include the submission form, a brief description of the film, an
artist bio and contact information, as well as two film stills (tiff or jpg
@ 300 dpi 2 x 3). Description, bio, contact info and film stills may be
emailed to tais@bellnet.ca or included in submission package. Submission
form can be found here:
http://www.tais.ca/tais_showcase_submission_form_2010.pdf

Deadline for submissions:
Friday May 14, 2010, 5:00 pm

For more info visit www.tais.ca

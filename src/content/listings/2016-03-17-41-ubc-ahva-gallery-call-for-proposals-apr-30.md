AHVA Gallery UBC
Call for Submissions 2016/2017 Exhibition Program

The AHVA Gallery (gallery.ahva.ubc.ca) in the Audain Art Centre, UBC is a
2000 square foot purpose-built, contemporary exhibition space serving the
Visual Art, Art History and Curatorial Studies programs of the Department
of Art History, Visual Art and Theory (AHVA) at the University of British
Columbia (UBC). Providing a site for student and faculty artistic and
curatorial collaborations from within the department, the university and
with artists and art students outside the university community; the Gallery
allows for interactions on issues relating to art, culture and creative
research.

The AHVA Gallery encourages Exhibition Proposals throughout the year from
UBC Faculty, Students and other interested parties within and outside the
University Community.

The available exhibition slots are from mid-October to mid-November 2016
and late November 2016 to late January 2017. The gallery will be closed
from mid-December to early January during the holiday break.

The AHVA Gallery operates on a very limited budget intended to support only
basic install, takedown and limited public hours during exhibition periods.
The AHVA Gallery does not pay shipping costs, artist fees or have full time
support staff.

Please refer to the Proposal for Exhibition - Submission Guidelines
2016/2017 for further details or at
http://www.ahva.ubc.ca/formsDisplay.cfm. Please review the required
information and submit your complete Exhibition Proposal to the AHVA
Gallery Committee electronically at ahva.gallery@ubc.ca by April 30, 2016.

All proposals will be reviewed by the AHVA Gallery Committee and will
receive thoughtful consideration. Applicants should be notified of their
proposals standing by the end of May.

If you have any questions please contact the Gallery Coordinator, Nick
Smolinski and Gallery Director, Barrie Jones at ahva.gallery@ubc.ca.

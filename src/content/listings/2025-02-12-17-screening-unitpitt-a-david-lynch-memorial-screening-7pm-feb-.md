"I don't know what it is, but I know
what it feels like": A David Lynch Memorial
Screening

February 17, 2025
7-10PM

UNIT/PITT
2954 West 4th Ave

*Please note, we will be fundraising for UNIT/PITT through a sliding scale ticket
option. Some tickets will be reserved at the door. No one will be turned away for lack
of funds. RSVP Here: https://www.zeffy.com/en-CA/ticketing/i-dont-know-what-it-is-
but-i-know-what-it-feels-like-a-david-lynch-memorial-screening

As we mourn the recent loss of beloved filmmaker David Lynch, join us for a
community gathering and screening. We will showcase a selection of film clips,
interviews, and documentation of Lynch's non-filmic performative work that reflects
the depth and breadth of his singular contribution to art and filmmaking. The
screening will be accompanied by a discussion featuring local artists from the
community sharing personal reflections on Lynch's influence on their own creative
practices. We welcome you to bring an object, letter, drawing, or other ephemera to
contribute
to a physical memorial that will be on display for the duration of the event.

Organized and curated by Sunny Nestler and Sasha J. Langford

Sunny Nestler is a multidisciplinary artist and former board chair at UNIT/PITT
Society. They are a co-founder of Bike Saviours Bicycle Collective, Tempe Zine Fest,
the Vancouver Community Bike Shop Network, and VR Club, which are all spaces
that work to make complicated tools and resources more accessible. Sunny lives on
unceded Coast Salish territories belonging to the xwmkwy"m (Musqueam),
Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), and slilwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) First Nations,
where they are Assistant Professor in the Faculty of Culture and Community at Emily
Carr University of Art and Design.

Sasha J. Langford is an interdisciplinary artist who works at the intersection of
sound, writing, performance, and social practice. Sasha's solo and collaborative
work has been presented at Ehk-Kutomo (Turku, Finland), FestivALT (Krakw,
Poland), Lines of Flight
Festival of Experimental Music (Dunedin, New Zealand),
Ende Tymes (Brooklyn, NY), Suoni Per Il Popolo (Montreal), and the International
Noise Conference (Miami, FL). They are Faculty at Columbia College, where they
teach media and cultural studies on occupied xmky"m, Swxw7mesh, and
sl"lwta" territories known as Vancouver, BC.

Karen Tams Moveable Feast: A Tasting Tour of North Vancouvers Most Interesting Chinese
Restaurants

Join artist-in-residence Karen Tam for a tasting tour of North Vancouvers most interesting
restaurants! Participants will meet at Griffin Art Projects for an introductory talk by the artist,
followed by a hopping restaurant tour chauffeured by shuttle van, returning back to the gallery at
the end. Karen Tam is a Montreal-based artist whose research focuses on the various forms of
constructions and imaginations of
cultures and communities, through her installation work in
which she recreates spaces of Chinese restaurants, karaoke lounges, opium dens, curio shops
and other sites of cultural encounters. Please RSVP for this event by emailing
jasmine@griffinartprojects.ca before March 14th, 2020.

Xingyue Feng & Yuan Liu
Invite you to a celebration of their Divorce / wedding! 

Opening will be on 24th Mar. 6pm-9pm at Plaza Projects
Aberdeen Square #2370-4000, Richmond

The Divorce is a collaborative art project by Feng Xingyue & Liu Yuan. The artist couple 
hosted a lavish wedding ceremony as part of their project. This project is for their MFA 
program at Emily Carr University. 

Bio
Feng Xingyue & Liu Yuan are partners in both art and life from China, work with video, 
performance and installation. They are in Emily Carr University of Art and Design MFA 
program now.

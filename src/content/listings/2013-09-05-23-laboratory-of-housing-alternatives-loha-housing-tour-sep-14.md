Laboratory of Housing Alternatives (LOHA) is a non-profit focused on providing 
housing for creative and emerging professionals in Vancouver. 

LOHA is organizing a housing tour on September 14th. The objective of this 
event is to showcase diverse housing models in the neighbourhoods of 
Stratchona and Railtown and to spark a critical conversation around the design 
and development models of these spaces.

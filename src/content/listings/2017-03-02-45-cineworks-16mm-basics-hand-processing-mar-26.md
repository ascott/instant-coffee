16mm Basics: Introduction to Hand Processing

Sunday 26 March 20172:00pm-5:00pm
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander Street (Ironworks basement)

$30 + taxes | Cineworks Members
$35 + taxes | Concessions and Community Partner Members
$40 + taxes | General Admission

REGISTER: 
https://16mm-basics-hand-processing-mar.eventbrite.ca

A 3 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and 
practice of processing 16mm black and white film by hand.

Elements include:

Principles of chemical developing
Darkroom safety
Bucket processing
Tank processing

Film shot in the 16mm Basics: Intro to Bolex workshop on Sunday 12 Mar will be 
processed in this workshop. If interested in both components, please register for 
the Bolex workshop via the following link: 

https://16mm-basics-bolex-mar.eventbrite.ca

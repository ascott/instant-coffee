TALK I Twilight Hour
Lucie Chan

Wednesday, October 24 | 6 pm
Sculpture Studio, D1357
Join us for a talk with Emily Carr University faculty member LuChan, who will present 
her drawing practice.

Guyana-born Lucie Chan lives in Vancouver. She spent 10 years in Halifax developing 
trans-disciplinary drawings, and recently introduced written/performative elements to her 
practice. She has participated in group exhibitions at the National Gallery of Canada, 
Carleton Art Gallery, MOCCA, Richmond Art Gallery, TRUCK Gallery, & Oboro among 
others. Her work has also been featured in solo exhibitions at the Art Gallery of Nova 
Scotia, the MAI (Montral Arts Interculturels), and Mount Saint Vincent University Art 
Gallery. She was long-listed for the Sobey Art Award in 2005 and 2010. She has 
completed local and international residencies while continuing to develop her art on a 
regular basis in the Canada. She holds a BFA from ACAD and a MFA from NSCAD. 

Twilight Hour is a series of intimate talks for students and the public with guest artists 
hosted by Painting and Sculpture in the Audain Faculty of Art.

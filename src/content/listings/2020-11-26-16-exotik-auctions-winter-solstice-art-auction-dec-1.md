Exotik Auctions presents

*~~* Winter Solstice Art Auction *~~*

Instagram: @exotikauctions

Sale begins on Tuesday, December 1, 2020, 9AM PST

Bidding closes on Friday, December 4, 2020, 11:59PM PST

ExotiK Auctions is an online Instagram auction that will feature work from local artists within
Metro-Vancouver, the traditional, ancestral, and unceded lands of Hwlitsum, Katzie,
Kwantlen, Kwikwetlem, Matsqui, Musqueam, Qayqayt, Semiahmoo, Squamish,
Tsawwassen, and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations.

We acknowledge that this has been a difficult year for artists as many have faced a large
impact in stability and income. This auction aims to encourage, support, and promote the
work of artists during this pandemic era.

Bids
can be placed in the comment section at Instagram: @exotikauctions

If you would like to place a bid but do not have access to Instagram please contact:
exotikcustomframing@gmail.com

ExotiK Auctions is laboured by Asia Jong, Yasmine Haiboub, Dana Qaddah, Simon Grefiel,
Stephanie Wu, Rina Lyshaug, and Jack Kenna and is a project of ExotiK Custom Framing
and Exhibition Services.

ExotiK Custom Framing and Exhibition Services assists artists through framing services,
fabrications, art handling, and collaborations for fruition of artistic projects.

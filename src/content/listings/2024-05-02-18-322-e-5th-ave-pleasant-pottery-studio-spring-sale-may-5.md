WHAT: Pleasant Pottery Studio Spring Sale

WHERE: 322 E. 5th Ave, about 1 block from Main Street.

WHEN:
May 5th 12-5pm

Pleasant Pottery Studio (sister studio to Hand Eye Design Ceramics) will be hosting
a spring pottery sale exactly one week before Mothers Day"a perfect occasion to
grab a gift for Mom! We have over 20 vendors from our ceramic community who are
excited to show and share their work.

We will also be hosting a raffle, with chances to win pottery made by our studio
members, and a gift card for two spots in our coveted Mothers Day wheel throwing
workshop! All proceeds will be donated to UNRWA towards Palestinian relief in
Gaza. (1 entry for $5, 3 entries for $10)

CASH AND E-TRANSFER PREFERRED.

Most vendors will be equipped to handle cash and e-transfer transactions. Some
vendors may be equipped to handle credit card transactions, but again, cash or e-
transfer is preferred.

This is a great opportunity to meet local artists in the community and take home
some locally made art. We hope to see you all there!

Opening & Artist Talk April 5, 2 to 4 pm. Artist in attendance.

Location: Gibsons Public Art Gallery
431 Marine Drive, Gibsons, BC, VON 1V0
P.O. Box 1576

About the artist:
Dorothy Doherty has spent the past few decades grappling with ways to convey her
concerns about global warming and climate change. In this exhibition, she works
with discarded paper and paint to create contemporary works of art on panel using
materials produced in an industry that contributes significantly to our changing
climate.

Dorothy received her formal education from the Vancouver School of Art (now Emily
Carr University of Art & Design) and Capilano University. She holds an MA in History
in Art from the University of Victoria, and a PDP (professional education degree)
from Simon Fraser University.

Artist website: https://www.dorothydoherty.net/
Instagram: @dorothydohertyart
Gibsons Art Gallery website: https://gpag.ca/

This is a two person exhibition. The gallery is shared with artist Carolina Franzen.

The Gibsons Public Art Gallery is located on the traditional, ancestral and unceded
territory of the Skwxw7mesh xwumixw (Squamish Nation), neighbouring the
traditional, ancestral and unceded territory of the shshlh (Sechelt Nation), British
Columbia.

Call for Submissions - Records (and tapes) by Artists

Hotam Press Bookshop/Gallery is now
accepting entries for our upcoming exhibition
Records by Artists.

This exhibition is scheduled to open mid-November and will run to the end January 2021.
We would like to bring together art and music made by contemporary artists related to the
theme of music and sound of all genres.

For the exhibition, we are interested in showing work by artists including vinyl records,
cassette tapes, and other paraphernalia such as fanzines, prints, clothing and multiples.

Please visit us and drop off your work during our store hours (Sat/Sun 12 to 5 PM) until
Oct 31. All accepted works will be available for sale on consignment (70/30). For inquiries,
our contact email is hotampress @ gmail.com

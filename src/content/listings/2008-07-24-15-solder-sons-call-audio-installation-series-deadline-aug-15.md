Audio Installation Series.

Solder and Sons is organizing a series of in-store audio installation works.

Those interested should send along a site specific proposal.
As well a willingness to discuss recent practise in audio
installation and exploration. 

A recorded sound sample is welcome at this point.

Successful applicants will be willing to submit a recording of the finished piece and
participate in a year-end
panel once the installation series has concluded.

There will be technical assistance provided during the installation process.

Deadline for first installation will be August 15th.

Looking forward to hearing about your work! Store hours are 8:30-5:00, Monday-Friday.

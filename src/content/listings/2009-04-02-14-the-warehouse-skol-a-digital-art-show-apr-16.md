Skol A digital art show
Reception/Opening Free For All/One night only - April 16, 5 - 8pm

The Warehouse
577 Great Northern Way
www.skrol.org

The Warehouse at the Centre for Digital Media, part of the Great Northern
Way Campus, is hosting a one-night only reception/group show of
digital-native works by students, faculty and staff of the Masters of
Digital Media program. Curated by faculty member Ian Verchere, the work is
an expression of digital fine art practice within the context of this
innovative industry project-driven graduate program. The Warehouse is an
incredible space, one of the last great open industrial warehouses, and
recently hosted Midforms 09, a 3-day digital cultural festival.

Two modes of digital fine art practice define the work: digital as means of
production, and digital as native form. The work includes installation,
projections, 2D and 3D work. Artists represented are Ryan Nadel, Aerlyn
Weissman, Salvia Dhall, Mark Freeman, Kristina Fiedrich, Kenneth Prat,
Matthew Schroeter, Michelle Parent, Elmar dela Cruz and Ian Verchere.

Saturday Session: Chris Chong Chan Fui on Lindsay McIntyre & CFGNY
26 JUL 12:00 PM

One Saturday each month, CAG invites a guest host to lead walkthroughs of our
current exhibitions, offering their insights and response to the works on view. In July, we
welcome Chris Chong Chan Fui to speak on the work of Lindsay McIntyre and CFGNY.

Chris Chong Chan Fui works with moving images and objects that reveal unfamiliar
narratives through the natural sciences, transnational circuits of globalization, and
manufactured cultures and landscapes. Chong has premiered his films at the Cannes
Film Festival's Directors Fortnight, BFI London, and TIFFs Wavelengths, and exhibited
at the Yokohama Museum of Art, Hirshhorn Museum & Sculpture Garden, Palais de
Tokyo, EYE Film Institute Netherlands, and the Gwangju Biennale. He is a Smithsonian
Institute fellow (National Museum of Natural History), a Ford Foundation fellow, a
Rockefeller Foundation's Bellagio Arts Fellow, and an Assistant Professor, Film at the
SFU School for the Contemporary Arts.

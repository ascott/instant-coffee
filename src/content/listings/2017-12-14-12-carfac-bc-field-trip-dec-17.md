Sunday, December 17, 2017 from 1:30 to 4:30pm
Meet at CARFAC BC: Suite 100 - 938 Howe Street, Vancouver BC

Join CARFAC BC on Sunday, December 17th for an afternoon social as part of FIELD 
TRIP"a series of events promoting dialogue between artists and connecting artistic 
communities across the province.

Meet your peers over coffee and snacks, followed by a short walk to the Contemporary 
Art Gallery for a complimentary tour of current exhibitions, Andrew Dadson: Site for Still 
Life and Lyse Lemieux: FULL FRONTAL, led by Assistant Curator Jas Lally. 

The event is free, but please RSVP. Everyone is welcome! For more information and 
registration details, visit carfacbc.org

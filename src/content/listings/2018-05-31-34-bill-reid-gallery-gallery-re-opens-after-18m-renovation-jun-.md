Bill Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art re-opens its doors following a
$1.8 million, six-month renovation to celebrate its 10th anniversary with
an abundant weekend line-up of family friendly events, June 16 - 17, 2018,
including storytelling hour, tea and bannock, live performances, and free
admission.

This first major renovation to the decade-old museum showcases a new
educational and social gathering space, and expanded programming capacity.
Home Away From Home is the inaugural exhibition of the Bryan and Audrey
Williams Gallery " housed in the newly transformed mezzanine " and is on
display through Sept. 9, 2018.

The exhibition highlights artists from the Carnegie Centre Cultural Sharing
program, and is guest-curated by Samantha Nock (Cree Mtis) and Nicole Bird
(Haida). An interactive commission by Kelsey Sparrow (Musqueam) will be
unveiled over the weekend, as will the launch of two artist residencies
featuring Allison Burns-Joseph (Squamish) and Samuel McKay (Nisgaa).

Activities and schedule at: billreidgallery.ca

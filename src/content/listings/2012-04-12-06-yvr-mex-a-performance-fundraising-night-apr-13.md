YVR- MEX 
A performance (fundraising night) 
April 13th 
7-11 pm. 
VIVO Media Arts Centre
1965 Main Street. 

We have been invited to participate TRANSMUTED International Performance 
Festival at the Museum Ex Teresa Arte Actual, curated by Pancho Lopez, in 
Mexico City from April 26-27th. This is our fundraising event to cover some our 
travel costs and share some of the work that we will be doing in Mexico. There 
will be food provided by NUBA. 

Each artist will donate a piece towards a raffle. 

Three Original Performances by:

David Khang 
Naufus Ramirez Figueroa 
Emilio Rojas 
and with some justice from Margaret Dragu

Entrance by donation $5-10 sliding scale. 
No one will be turned away but your donations will help support our work in 
Mexico City. 
There will be food provided by NUBA. 

With some Djs in the front room and bar. 
Dj Day Glow from Boston with Ian Deleon from Miami
DJ Monkey Wrench from Vancouver

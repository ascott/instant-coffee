The Contemporary Art Gallery and Emily Carr University of Art + Design
co-present

I Am A Laser/Scream Like a Baby

A lecture by Jeremy Shaw

Wednesday, December 1st, 2010, 8pm

At Emily Carr Lecture Theatre (SB Room 301), Emily Carr University of Art +
Design
1399 Johnston Street, Granville Island, Vancouver

In his lecture/performance I Am A Laser/Scream Like a Baby, Jeremy Shaw
discusses state-of-the-art laser technologies using comprehensive diagrams
and schematics to illustrate the rigour and speed at which they are shaping
current surgeries, weaponry, theory, and arguably even thought. This will
serve as a stepping-off point for a dramatic proposition that links the
lasers advanced scientific capabilities to that of its aid in the
transcendence-seeking activities of popular culture from the 1970s until
the present. This duality is highlighted by a narrative written in 1973
that recurred in 1980 - the second being labeled future nostalgic by its
two-time author, David Bowie.

Jeremy Shaw is a Canadian artist based in Berlin and Vancouver. He
graduated from Emily Carr Instiutute in 1999, and works in a variety of
media including video installation, photography, and sculpture. His
artistic practice involves representations of the altered states - often
illustrating a grey area of validity between scientific and populist
notions. Shaw has exhibited extensively in North America and Europe with
recent and upcoming solo exhibits that include Storming Heaven (From Home),
Fondation Gutzwiller, Zurich (2011), Single Channel Higher States, Samsa,
Berlin (2010), Lot #422, Every Letter in the Alphabet, Vancouver (2010),
DMT, Maribel Lopez Gallery, Berlin (2010), This Transition Will Never End
#6, Toronto International Film Festival (2009), and Somethings Happening
Here! Presentation House Gallery, Vancouver (2009). Shaw is also an
internationally-renowned electronic musician recording under the name
Circlesquare, and has been released on Londons Output Recordings and
Berlins !K7. He is represented by Blanket Contemporary Art Inc. in Vancouver.

SUPER BASIC
~~
Skylight Gallery 163 E Pender St.
Friday, June 5th
7PM

In Conjuction with ArtWaste 2015, Skylight Gallery Presents:

Exploring the physical boundaries of material and the possibilities of art
beyond representation, this show features new, independent, and
collaborative works from six artists. Questioning the ontological divide
between objects and beings, the works re-contextualize objects outside of
their vernacular environments. Focusing on issues of materiality, form and
the aesthetic experience the artists attempt to distance their art practice
from connotative messages and the notion that art must mean anything.

New works by:

Melanie Thibodeau
Chelsea Yuill
Kai Choufour
Jack Morris
Natalie Schmidt
Amanda Crain

Come see the finished super8 films made over the weekend in the Super8 Shoot-to-
Screen workshop led by Nisha Platzer

This hands-on workshop, co-presented by Iris Film Collective and facilitated by Nisha 
Platzer, invites participants to explore the world of super8 film, creating a short black and 
white film which they will hand-process in photo-chemistry. A brief introduction to the 
format on friday evening will be followed by shooting films on saturday and processing 
on sunday. 

Public Screening Sunday Jan 27 at 8pm

Nisha Platzer is a Vancouver based filmmaker often working in analogue and handmade 
formats. She has presented workshops throughout Latin America and Canada and her 
short films have been screened at festivals worldwide. 

144 E 6th Ave, Vancouver, BC

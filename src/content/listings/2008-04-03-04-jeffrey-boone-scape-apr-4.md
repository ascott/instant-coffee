Please join Monique Genton, Sylvia Grace Borda and Jeff Depner for the opening reception of their group show Scape on Friday April 4th from 5-8PM.

This show represents a convergence in the disparate practices of three unconnected artists – most notably in the relationship between automobile infrastructure and the modernist architecture that is often their common subject matter.  Depner uses photography to make abstract formalist compositions. Borda has created 8000 image strong photo documentation of a Scottish Modernist suburb. Genton’s photo-based graphics in LED lightboxes take a critical look at the tension between the architecture and the automobile infrastructure Vancouver’s in West End and West Side.

The show will run April 2 – 27th, Wed-Sun 12-6PM.

The Vancouver Economic Commission and the City of Vancouver introduce the Activate 
Program. 

The Activate DTES & The Flats project seeks to address availability and affordability 
issues related to production space in Vancouvers Downtown Eastside, False Creek 
Flats and immediate surrounding areas. We are looking to generate effective change in 
the near term for groups that need light industrial space.

The Activate Program identifies underutilized spaces that can be transformed to provide 
social enterprises, artists and entrepreneurs with affordable spaces to launch and 
nurture projects or businesses that contribute to the Citys economic, social, cultural and 
environmental goals. This program helps landowners, tenants, and City staff work 
collaboratively to retrofit, permit, and fund affordable space projects. 

More info at: [https://www.vancouvereconomic.com/industrial-concierge-service/](https://www.vancouvereconomic.com/industrial-concierge-service/)

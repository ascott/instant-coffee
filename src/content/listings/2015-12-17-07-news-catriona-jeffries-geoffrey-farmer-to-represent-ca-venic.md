The water understands
Civilization well;
It wets my foot, but prettily,
It chills my life, but wittily,
It is not disconcerted,
It is not broken-hearted:
Well used, it decketh joy,
Adorneth, doubleth joy:
Ill used, it will destroy,
In perfect time and measure
With a face of golden pleasure
Elegantly destroy.

Catriona Jeffries is pleased to announce Geoffrey Farmer as the official
Canadian representative for the 57th International Art Exhibition - La
Biennale di Venezia, in Italy, 2017.

The Canadian Pavilion will be curated by Kitty Scott, Curator of Modern and
Contempoary Art at the Art Gallery of Ontario, alongside Project Director,
Jose Drouin-Brisebois, Senior Curator of Contemporary Art at the National
Gallery of Canada.

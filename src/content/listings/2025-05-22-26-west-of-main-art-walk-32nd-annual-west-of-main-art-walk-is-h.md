32nd Annual West Of Main Art Walk is here!

Vancouver, BC " For one weekend only, May 31-June 1 2025, artists across
Vancouver's west side will open their studio doors and gather at satellite venues to
welcome the public into their creative spaces. From internationally acclaimed artists
to emerging local talent, the West of Main Art Walk offers a rare opportunity to
explore the vibrant visual arts community through this free, all-ages, self-guided tour
and sale.

Details & Event Map: www.artistsinourmidst.com

Where: Across Vancouver's west side " from Point Grey to Main Street and Granville
Island to 41st Avenue
When: May 31-June 1. Open daily 11-5pm
What: Self-guided tour. Open studios, art sale, live demos, and more
Cost: Free. All ages event.
Plan your event:
View the artist lineup, preview artwork, and access both the printable and interactive
Google map at

Call for Submissions: 
The White Rabbit Open Air Arts Project is now accepting 
submissions from artists. 

White Rabbit is an opportunity for artists of all disciplines to live, 
work, and learn in community. The week-long residency culminates 
in a public celebration, performance and exhibition of individual and 
collaborative works that emerge during the week.

When: August 12th-19th, 2012.
Where: Red Clay (Upper Economy), Nova Scotia. 

White Rabbit Open Air Arts Project is an immersive outdoor 
experience. The residency encourages artists to push the 
boundaries of their practice collaboratively and individually in 
response to the unique landscape. Red Clay is a land of organic 
gardens, meadows, ponds and forests looking out over the extreme 
tides of the Bay of Fundy. 

During the week, two senior mentors will guide resident artists 
through workshops, presentations and one-on-one engagement. 
2012 mentoring artists will include Alberta-based installation artist 
and sculptor Peter Von Tiesenhausen and Newfoundland 
multidisciplinary artist Kay Burns. Michael Waterman, creator and 
director of Red Clay radio, will return for his fourth year with a micro-
radio station that participants run from the greenhouse. 

The cost for participants is $285, which covers locally-sourced 
gourmet meals, accommodations for 8 days (camping along stream 
plus a limited number of loft spaces), workshops and presentations 
by artists, field trips, and use of site facilities and available tools. 

Interested artists should submit the following before May 1st

1. A brief description (max 1 page) of your proposed project while at 
White Rabbit – please include ideas/concepts you will be exploring, 
materials and any tools that you will need. We encourage projects 
that are harmonious with the environment, therefore please specify 
and justify the proposed use of any unnatural materials.

2. A brief artist biography including your current place of residence, 
previous work and media (1 page max).

Open international call for submissions for the January, 2019, issue of Dek Unu

submit to Dek Unu.

Open to adventurous, accomplished work in all photo techniques and genres. Each

includes an in-depth English-language interview. Monthly featured artists are promoted 
extensively on web and social media.

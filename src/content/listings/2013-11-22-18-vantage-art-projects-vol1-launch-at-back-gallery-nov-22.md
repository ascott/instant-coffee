Vantage Art Projects
Vol. 1 The Launch 

Friday, November 22nd, 2013
From 6 - 9 pm

Back Gallery Project 
602 E Hastings Street
Vancouver, BC V6A 1R1
T. 604.339.2096


Back Gallery project is pleased to partner with Vantage Art Projects to show for the first time the range of limited edition prints by 24 contemporary Canadian artists working in different mediums. Vol 1 is a compilation of emerging and mid career Canadian artists, who display a continuance of aesthetic development, intellectual pursuit, and professional commitment.

Katie Bond Pretti, Rebecca Chaperon, Mark De Long, Jeff Depner, Fred Fraser, Yechel Gagnon, Angela Grossman, Ruth Jones, Lopuch + Edmiston, Dana Holst, Mark Mushet, David Pirrie, Anna Plesset, Leah Rosenberg, Mike Swaney, Matthew Sweig, Keer Tanchak, Carrie Walker, James Whitman, Margaux Williamson, Biliana Velkova, Anna Wood, Sascha Yamashita, Karen Yurkovich.

Jeremy Shaw in conversation with Caitlin Jones
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
Room 301, 1399 Johnston Street, Vancouver
Monday, March 2, 6pm

Jeremy Shaw will discuss his work and his current exhibition Medium-Based
Time in conversation with Caitlin Jones, Executive Director of Western Front.

Jeremy Shaw was born in 1977, Vancouver, BC and currently lives and works
in Berlin. He participated in the Canada Council for the Arts ISCP
Residency in New York (2011) and the Caribic Residency in Hamburg, Denmark
(2010). His solo exhibitions include Storming Heaven (From Home), Everest -
Fondation Gutzwiller, Zurich; Best Minds, MoMA PS1, New York, NY (2011) and
has been selected for group exhibitions including Based In Berlin, KW
Institute for Contemporary Art, Berlin, Montreal Biennale; A Throw of the
Dice will Never Abolish Chance, Montreal (2011), and Cities of Gold and
Mirrors, Julia Stoschek Collection, Dusseldorf (2011). He is currently
represented by Macaulay & Co. Fine Art, Vancouver and Blanket, Cologne,
Germany. He graduated in 2000 with a BFA from Emily Carr Institute of Art
and Design.

This talk is part of the Visual Art Forums, presented by the Faculty of
Visual Art + Material Practice and the Faculty of Graduate Studies at Emily
Carr University of Art + Design, in collaboration with the Contemporary Art
Gallery. It is free and open to the public.

Now on View

Geoffrey Farmer: Phantom Scripts

October 2, 2025 - February 2, 2026

Phantom Scripts revisits three works by Geoffrey Farmer from the Audain Art Museums
Permanent Collection, reframing them through newly composed scripts, annotations, and
didactic texts authored by the artist. The texts function as interjections " speculative,
contextual, poetic " that re-examine and complicate the earlier works. In doing so,
Farmer explores how art can be returned to, re-read, and re-situated under shifting
historical and ethical awarenesses.

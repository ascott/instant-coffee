Co-Learning Engagement | MAR 14
Tactile Co-Learning Engagement at grunt gallery
Program Dates: April 8 - May 16, 2025
Application Deadline: March 14, 2025

Artist Fee: $2,000
Info Session: March 5th, 2025 (ASL interpreters available)
Contact: aep@grunt.ca

grunt invites Non-Visual, Blind, DeafBlind, Low Vision, or Partially Sighted artists
with a tactile practice from across colonially named Metro Vancouver/Lower
Mainland to apply for our upcoming Non-Visual Co-Learning Engagement. This six-
week program centers on tactile creative practices and shared learning.

About the Program
This program focuses on tactile presentation such as tactile drawings, textile
sculptures, interactive or performances experienced through touch, as well as
exploring tactile communication practices such as braille, raised written lettering,
protactile, and accessible information sharing through screen readers or audio
presentations to
help artist-run centres and contemporary artist serve non-visual
artists and audiences. Our goal is to explore tactile forms of artistic engagement and
presentation, prioritize accessibility, and challenge traditional, occular-centric
approaches to art.

Gallery 295 is a new project space created to support emerging artists 
who engage in contemporary photography and related practices. This 
new gallery, located in an open warehouse area behind The Lab - 
Professional Digital Image Works on East 2nd Ave near Main street, is 
able to offer artists a much needed display area and the facilities 
required to realize their work.

The first show at Gallery 295 is scheduled for September 28. For this 
exhibition there will be a display of work connected to The Lab ́s history 
within Vancouver ́s arts community in addition to recognition for some 
of the many artists they have engaged with throughout the years. The 
gallery inauguration will be a celebration of fifteen years of artists 
working with The Lab.

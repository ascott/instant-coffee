Nostalgia
December 5"21, 2025

THIS Gallery " 108 East Broadway (alley entrance), Vancouver, BC

THIS Gallery is pleased to present Nostalgia, a group exhibition featuring paintings, fibre
works, and sculpture by nine Canadian artists: Jess Portfleet, Olga Rybalko, Paige Quinn,
Heidi Gerrits, Katy Biele, Allie Bartlett, Daryn Wright, Ketty Zhang, and Alison Tang.

Nostalgia explores memory as something active and shape-shifting " a force that edits,
unsettles, and reshapes the stories we carry. Across diverse materials and approaches,
the artists reveal how the past softens, sharpens, or rearranges itself in the present.

Gallery hours:

Sundays 12-4

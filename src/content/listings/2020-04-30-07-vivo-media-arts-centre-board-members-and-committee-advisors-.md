VIVO Media Arts Centre is looking for passionate, dedicated board members and
committee advisors!

Incorporated as Satellite Video Exchange Society in 1973, VIVO nurtures past, present
and future media arts discourses and communities through equitable and public access
to resources for preservation, production and dissemination. We value experimental,
artist-run, responsive, decolonial, and inclusive practices as well as equitable and
responsible stewardship.

VIVO has an active working board that meets quarterly. Board directors participate on
various committees including governance, human resources, facilities, fundraising,
archives, and finance. These committees, which typically meet quarterly, are also
comprised of staff and advisors. While we are looking
to fill a limited number of
vacancies on the Board at this time, serving as an external committee advisor is both
helpful to the organization and a good way to learn whether future Board involvement is
desirable. We are especially seeking artists, lawyers, designers, engineers, and those
with fundraising skills to get involved in the organization. We prioritize applications from
self-identified members of historically marginalized communities.

Please send a CV and letter of intent as a single PDF to admin@vivomediaarts.com by
April 30th.

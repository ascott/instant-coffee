#hiphapahooray! photobooth | August 2/3 Nikkei National Museum at PSF

The #hiphapahooray! photobooth at the Powell Street Festival is an
interactive way to be a part of our upcoming exhibit, kip fulbeck : part
asian, 100%hapa which will open at the Nikkei National Museum (NNM) in
September.

Answer the question that Kip poses – What Are You? - on one of our selfie
white boards, take a smartphone photo or smile for the NNM camera and be a
part of the NNM’s photo project. We invite you to tweet, or post to
instagram, pinterest, or facebook.

The #hiphapahooray! photobooth will be open in the Vancouver Buddhist
Temple at 220 Jackson Avenue, Vancouver, on Aug 2nd and 3rd from 11:30am –
7pm. www.nikkeiplace.org

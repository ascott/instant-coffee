Negative Pages will feature a selection of Vancouver-based artists 
experimenting with the form of the photographic artist's book. Highlighting 
photographic imagery's connections with experimental book forms, the 
exhibition will present a diverse range of contemporary approaches over a 
three-day book exhibition and sale, which will be accompanied by an 
exhibition of selected photographic works from the publications.

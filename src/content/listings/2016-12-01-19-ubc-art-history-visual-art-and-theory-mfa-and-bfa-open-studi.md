MFA and BFA Open Studios 

Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory (AHVA)
University of British Columbia (UBC)

Tuesday, December 6, 2016 | 4PM-7PM

MFA | 4th floor of the Audain Art Centre 
BFA | 3rd floor of the Audain Art Centre
6398 University Blvd

You are invited to the inaugural, combined Master of Fine Art (MFA) and 
Bachelor of Fine Art Visual Art (BFA) Open Studios. This event is an opportunity 
to view the work in progress of both MFA and BFA candidates and to engage 
with them on their current studio practice. MFA candidates on the 4th floor and 
BFA students on the 3rd floor will be present in their workspaces to welcome 
visitors and to share their work. This is a public event, all are welcome.

MFA Artists: Aileen Bahmanipour, Matt Browning, Steven Cottingham, Jessica 
Evans, Christopher Lacroix, Brian Lye, Cameron McLellan, Candice Okada, 
Madiha Pandhiani, Parvin Peivandi, Carolyn Stockbridge

http://ahva.ubc.ca/

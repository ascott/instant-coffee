Chernoff Fine Art
Kim Kennedy Austin: Boy Band Buddha
February 7

Opening Reception Friday February 7, 7-10pm

Consisting of a number of new ink drawings on paper, Boy Band 
Buddha takes as its subject matter Abz Love, member of U.K. boy 
band 5ive, and in particular his appearance on the television reality 
game show Celebrity Big Brother during the summer of 2013. Boy 
Band Buddha imagines Abz as a Buddha figure and Celebrity Big 
Brother as a series of televised tests aimed at finding the potential 
reincarnation of a Tibetan Lama or Tulku. 

Tweets culled from the twitter page of Abz Love are presented as Zen 
Koans for contemplation. Utilizing as research material for her 
knowledge of Buddhism such enlightened sources as the 1986 Eddie 
Murphy movie The Golden Child, Hermann Hesse’s novel Siddhartha, 
Vicki MacKenzie’s book Reincarnation: The Boy Lama, a BBC4 
program The Reluctant Lama along with a smattering of Wikipedia 
pages, this exhibition posits a surely fundamental misreading and 
misrepresentation of Tibetan Buddhism.

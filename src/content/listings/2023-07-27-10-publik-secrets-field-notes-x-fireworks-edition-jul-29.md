Taking place in Hadden Park under a canopy of
trees, this edition of the field notes
concert series showcases local artists working across experimental music, field
recordings and soundscape practices. The evening will feature performances by Sasha J.
Langford, Stefan Maier & Alexi Baris, Marina Hasselberg, and Matthew Ariaratnam &
Adrian Avendao. Come for the music, stay for the fireworks!

The event begins at 8:00pm next to the Hadden Park fieldhouse (1015 Maple Street).

RSVP in advance via Eventbrite. Donations welcome to support the artists!

You're encouraged to bring your own seats/blankets sit on. Public washrooms can be
accessed in this park. As the fireworks will begin at 10pm, there will be road closures in
effect in the area.

Presented by Publik Secrets and co-curated by Jean Brazeau

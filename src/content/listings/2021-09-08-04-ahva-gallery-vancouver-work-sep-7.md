We are very pleased to invite you to Vancouver Work an exhibition of long time AHVA
Faculty member Barrie Jones, at the AHVA Gallery in the Audain Art Centre.

Spanning over two decades from the late nineties to the present, the exhibition provides a
selection of old, new and ongoing projects and a window into earlier work that informs
Barrie's continuing practice. Please join us in celebrating Barrie's contributions to AHVA and
photography during the exhibition
run; Barrie will be present on Saturdays if you would like
to connect. As we cannot offer an opening reception at this time, stay tuned for virtual artist
tour dates/times.

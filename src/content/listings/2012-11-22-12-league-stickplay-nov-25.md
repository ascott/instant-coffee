League play this Sunday: stickplay 
Sunday 25 November, noon to 3pm 
Elm Park, 41 Ave @ Elm, Kerrisdale

League is an open gathering to play games and sports invented by community 
members. Each game, its playing field and its strategies will evolve through trial 
and improvisation, and new equipment may be invented. It’s problem-solving as 
play.

For this edition, bring a stick of some kind: branch, broom, club, etc. Its purpose 
will be revealed in the course of play.

Recently launched by artist Germaine Koh, League is based in the field house in 
Elm Park, as part of the Vancouver Park Board Field House Residency Project. 

More info: http://league-league.org or http://facebook.com/LeagueVan

Beginner Rigid Heddle Weaving Workshop

Weave a scarf or two to three towels on a simple rigid heddle loom in this beginner
workshop with teaching artist Amanda Wood. No previous weaving experience
needed.

Sept 22 & 23, Friday 6-8pm and Saturday 10am
2 Eastside Atelier, 1310 William Street
More details and register through eventbrite: [https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/learn-to-](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/learn-to-)
weave-cloth-for-beginners-tickets-681706140417?aff=oddtdtcreator

ABOUT THE CLASS

Working with a neutral palette of silks, imported cotton and linen in a warm studio
atmosphere you will weave beautiful, textural cloth in a choose your own adventure
style.

The looms will be set up and prepared for you. You can choose to weave a longer
scarf or a collection of 2-3 towels. Amanda will help you choose textures, design
ideas and colour choices to make weaving simple and satisfying.

On Friday evening we'll begin with an introduction to the loom and materials, and a
conversation about what qualities you enjoy in cloth before getting started with
weaving. You'll be offered design suggestions and material recommendations and be
able to use Amanda's sample library to see how your cloth might turn out.

Saturday will be a dedicated and supportive weaving day. In all you will weave up to
2 yards of cloth which you can divide into 2-3 towels or keep whole as a scarf.
Finishing techniques will be discussed with options for sewn hems and non-sewn
hems options.

Amanda's approach to weaving is grounded in technique but invites experimentation
and marks of the
maker. This practice based approach is gentle and inclusive of all
levels of experience.

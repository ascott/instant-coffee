This month's Creative Prompt : FASHION

Get together with friends and make some art! Open studio time led by Richmond Art
Gallery staff for adult participants to cut and paste with a variety of materials and
tools while you chat, listen to music, and sip some tea at our Collage Club social.
RAG staff will open their vast collection of cool collage materials and tools to lead a
hands-on art-making night for adults.

This month's art-making prompt explores FASHION, inspired by the current
exhibition at the gallery, Mary Sui Yee Wong: Restless by Nature.

We will provide a large assortment of patterned paper and fabric collage materials
for you to cut, stitch, and paste fashion-inspired collages. Fashion inspired music
and hands-on demonstrations will be provided for experiments with clothing, pattern,
and style.

We will also connect to international collage groups for you to share your collage
works online by providing printed copies of collage challenges from various sites like
the Paris Collage Collective, Kolaj Magazine, and more.

All levels of artistic skill are welcome! Limited spaces, registration is required.
Recommended for ages 16+

$5 per person includes all art supplies and use of our equipment.
Please note - no refunds. Funds raised go towards supporting this program.

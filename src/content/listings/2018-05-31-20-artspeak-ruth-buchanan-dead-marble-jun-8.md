Ruth Buchanan
Dead Marble
June 9 - July 28, 2018

Opening: Friday, June 8, 8pm

Talk: Saturday, June 9 2pm. Ruth Buchanan in conversation with Tai Smith.

Please join us for the opening of Ruth Buchanans exhibition Dead Marble.
This exhibition will be Buchanans first solo presentation in North America.

RUTH BUCHANAN (Te -ti Awa/Taranaki) is a New Zealand artist living in
Berlin. Buchanans work is primarily concerned with the various systems at
play in the production of culture. Often beginning in the archive, where
case studies, artefacts, or works by other artists act as departure points
for an interrogation of art as a discourse of organising the world in which
we live. She works across performance, sculpture, text, audio, film,
textiles, and graphics. She has developed projects at the Adam Art Gallery,
Wellington; Hamburger Bahnhof, Berlin; The Showroom, London, and the
Badischer Kunstverein, Karlsruhe.

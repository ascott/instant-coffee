Oct 24, 2025
Nine Cakes for Nine Decades
3:00 - 6:00 p.m.
Grand Luxe Hall, Western Front
Free / Donations welcome

Join us for a community afternoon tea in celebration of artist Glenn Lewiss 90th
birthday and his extraordinary contributions to our creative community. Well mark
this milestone with nine cakes for nine decades alongside tea, coffee, and a pay bar.
Drop in anytime between 3:00 and 6:00 p.m. to share in the festivities. In lieu of gifts,
Glenn kindly invites you to consider a donation to Western Front, the artist-run
centre he helped create. Come raise a cup, and a forkful of cake, in honour of
Glenns remarkable life and legacy!

About Glenn Lewis:
Glenn Lewis is an acclaimed artist and educator based in Vancouver, Canada, and a
recipient of the Governor Generals Award in Visual and Media Arts. With an artistic
background rooted in traditional pottery and sculpture, Lewis also works with
photography, film, video, and performance to explore the tensions between
conventional objects and art, social obligation and natural instinct, and function and
wonder. His wide-ranging practice engages concept, fiction, myth, and community. A
pivotal figure in Canadian contemporary art, Lewis was a co-founder of Western
Front and an early member of Intermedia.

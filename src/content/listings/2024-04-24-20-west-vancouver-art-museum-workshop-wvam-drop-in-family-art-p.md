April 27, 2024, 12-4 p.m.

Registration: FREE, DROP-IN

On the last Saturday of every month, the WVAM Family Art Project invites families to
drop in to the museum for a free art workshop based on the topic of the West
Vancouver Art Museums current exhibition. Join us and enjoy an afternoon with your
kids and family making art inspired by our ongoing exhibition Home and Away: Ian
Wallace. All art supplies are provided.

This workshop is generously supported by the West Vancouver Foundation.

Stride Gallery is a non-for-profit artist run centre that values experimental, risk-taking art 
work, and fosters community conversation and participation. We are currently accepting 
exhibition proposals for the Main Space and +15 Window Space. Proposals received by 
5pm (MST) on Monday, January 15, 2018 will be considered for 2019 and 2020 
programming.

Artists are encouraged to follow the online submission guidelines and to e-mail their 
submission package in .zip format to info@ stride.ab.ca with the subject line ATTN: 
CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS JANUARY 2018.

FAXED, MAILED, OR INCOMPLETE SUBMISSIONS WILL NOT BE REVIEWED.

For more information, please visit [http://www.stride.ab.ca/opportunities/](http://www.stride.ab.ca/opportunities/)

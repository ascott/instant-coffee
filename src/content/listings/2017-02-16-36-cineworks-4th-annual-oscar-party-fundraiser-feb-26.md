Cineworks' 4th Annual Oscar Party Fundraiser

26 February 20174:00pm-late
The Imperial, 319 Main Street, Vancouver

General Admission: $80 + service fee
Member admission: $50 + service fee 

Tickets: [http://cineworks2017.brownpapertickets.com/](http://cineworks2017.brownpapertickets.com/)

Cineworks requests the pleasure of your company at The 4th Annual Oscar Party 
Fundraiser.

Enjoy an evening of glitz and grime, in support of Vancouver's Independent 
Filmmakers Society, est 1980. The event features: live alternative comedy MCing 
between big-screen Oscars announcements; deluxe buffet (included in ticket 
price); silent auction; on-site 16mm shooting; processing and projections; and a 
seedy back-room screening of selected Cineworks members' short-form projects.

Your attendance will help us support Canadian independent filmmakers and 
media artists, near and far. Come indulge and enjoy!

Cineworks gratefully acknowledges and pays respect to the Musqueam, 
Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations on whose unceded traditional 
territories our organization and our work is based. We also gratefully 
acknowledge the support of Canada Council for the Arts, British Columbia Arts 
Council, The Province of British Columbia and the City of Vancouver.

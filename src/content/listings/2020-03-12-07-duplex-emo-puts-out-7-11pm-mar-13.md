"EMO Puts Out on Friday the 13th"
Location: Duplex (NEW LOCATION) 1889 Franklin Street, entrance thru the alley
Date: Friday, March 13th 7-11 pm

Join us for new analogue film works and electronics by EMO collective. Featuring
workshop creations as well as new member works made throughout EMO collective's
month long residency at Duplex. Loops will be shown throughout the night, zines will be
available and film works will be screened at 8PM and 10PM.

EMO (Experimental Media Outsiders) is an emerging new media and analog collective.
They provide workshops and art-related resources for transgender and two-spirit
identified individuals, women identified individuals, individuals who identify as BIPOC,
2SLGBTQA+, folks with disabilities and anyone else facing
institutional/educational/accessibility barriers to learning analogue filmmaking, computer
programming, electronics and interactive media making. EMO was founded and
practices on the unceded territories of the Coast Salish peoples"Squamish, Stolo and
Tsleil-Waututh and Musqueam Nations, otherwise known as Vancouver, BC.

Richmond Museum presents Doors Open Richmond

June 7-8, 2025

The Richmond Museum presents the 18th annual Doors Open Richmond on June
7"8, 2025. Visitors can explore over 50 sites highlighting the citys diverse culture
and heritage, including art studios, historic locations, farms, and religious
organizations. Highlights this year include: learning about growing sustainable local
crops at KPU Farms, exploring paper making and bookbinding at Seawrack Press
Studios, and enjoying the vineyard and guided tasting at LuLu Island Winery.
Additionally, there will also be two thematic bus tours: Sacred Spaces explores
places of worship and Discover Doors Open showcases three different curated
partner sites each day. Participants can also view videos about Steveston Kendo
Club and Gateway Theatre at the Richmond Cultural Centre Annex Lounge or
online.

Free

Richmond Museum

7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond, BC V6Y 1R9

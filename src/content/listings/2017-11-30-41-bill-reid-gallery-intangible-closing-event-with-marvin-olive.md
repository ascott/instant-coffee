Intangible Closing Event with Marvin Oliver

Come to the Bill Reid Gallery on Sunday, December 10th for your last chance to 
view Intangible: Memory and Innovation in Coast Salish Art. Enjoy an Artist Talk with 
Marvin Oliver as well as a tour of the works featured in this exhibition. Light 
refreshments will be served. 

More About Marvin Oliver: 

My works are formulated by merging the spirit of past traditions with those of the 
present... to create new horizons for the future 

Marvin Oliver is one of the Northwest Coasts foremost contemporary sculptors and 
printmakers, who has made strides in the art world for the development and 
recognition of Native American Contemporary Fine Art.

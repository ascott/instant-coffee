Unearthed Artifacts: Post-Cyberpunk Relics from a Forgotten Future closes Sunday, January
18 at THIS Gallery.

Ceramic artist Wilson Makgawinata imagines relics from a future civilisation"glossy black
vessels wired with cobalt fittings and modular ports, part technology, part archaeology.

Wilson Makgawinata is a Vancouver-based multidisciplinary artist working primarily in
ceramics. His practice explores speculative archaeology and post-cyberpunk aesthetics,
producing sculptural artifacts that resemble relics from imagined futures. Working through
both wheel throwing and handbuilding, he creates pressure-shaped forms accented with
experimental glazes and modular components. His work blends industrial references with
organic structures to suggest systems that no longer function yet remain symbolically and
materially charged.

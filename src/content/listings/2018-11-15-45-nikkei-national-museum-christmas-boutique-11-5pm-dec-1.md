The Nikkei National Museum welcomes shoppers to the Nikkei Christmas Boutique on 
December 1, 2018, 11am-5pm.

This is a one-stop shopping experience for Japanese items and items made by Nikkei artisans. 
Find unique jewellery, gift cards, sewing, clothing and scarves, vintage haori jackets, handmade 
porcelain, woodwork, Japanese scented soaps and bath bombs, Japanese inspired treats, teas 
from Kyoto, and much more at this juried market. Shop for gifts while supporting your local 
artisans and small businesses.

Free admission.

6688 Southaoks Crescent, Burnaby, BC 
(corner of Kingsway & Sperling Ave)
www.nikkeiplace.org

Call for Applications, Emerging Digital Artists Award
JUNE 1 - SEPT 15

The Emerging Digital Artists Award (EDAA), presented in partnership 
by Equitable Bank and EQ Bank, has launched its second call for 
applications. The EDAA is dedicated to early-career, Canadian artists 
who are working on-screen, and experimenting with the possibilities of 
digitally rendered imagery. Applicants are encouraged to play with 
moving image formats such as video, animation, and GIF art.

The call will stay open through the summer - deadline to apply is 
September 15, 2016.

Learn more about the award and how to apply on our new website, 
which features GIF work by Vancouver-based artist Nicolas Sassoon: 
http://edaa.equitablebank.ca/.

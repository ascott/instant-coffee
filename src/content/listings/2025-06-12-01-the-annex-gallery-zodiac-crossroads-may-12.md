We warmly invite you to experience Zodiac Crossroads, a vibrant, multicultural tactile
art exhibition by Taiwanese-Canadian artist Johnny (Tiger) Tai, now open at The Annex
Gallery in Richmond.

Exhibition Info:
https://www.richmondartgallery.org/zodiac-crossroads

This groundbreaking show reimagines the twelve zodiac signs through a unique
hands-on art style invented by the artist himself, designed to be felt as well as seen.
With deep roots in both Eastern and Western traditions, and created with accessibility
at its heart, Zodiac Crossroads explores how we understand myth, identity, and
storytelling"through the power of touch.

Tai, who is blind and partially deaf, transforms everyday materials into sculptural
illustrations. His work is an open invitation to all visitors"especially those often
excluded from traditional gallery spaces"to experience art in an inclusive and
sensory-rich way.

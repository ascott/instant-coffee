The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC invites you to celebrate the arrival of an 
Archeology Basket by Suquamish Elder and Master Basketmaker Ed Carriere into the 
museum collection on Thursday, January 18 at 7pm. This basket embodies memories 
and identities from four time periods spanning 4,500 years of Coast Salishan basketry. 
Carriere will give a richly illustrated talk alongside colleague Dr. Dale R. Cross, wet site 
archaeologist and ancient basketry researcher. Carriere and Croes will also launch their 
new publication, Reawakening Ancient Salish Sea Basketry.

Free with Museum Admission. For more information visit: 
[http://moa.ubc.ca/portfolio_page/salish-seabasketry](http://moa.ubc.ca/portfolio_page/salish-seabasketry)

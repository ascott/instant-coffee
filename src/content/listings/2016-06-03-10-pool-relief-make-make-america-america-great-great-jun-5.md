Book Launch, Screenings, Reading, BBQ | Colouring Book Series & Somewhat
Urgent Series Presents: RELIEF & "Make Make America America Great Great
Again Again"

Sunday, June 5th, 7:00 PM
4257 Fraser Street (back alley entrance)
7:00 PM-10:00 PM

RELIEF is the first publication in Colouring Book Series that features
twenty-three artists whose work has crossed into the three-dimensional in
the form of sculpture, installation and/or performance. Each included
artist has generously offered their own artwork in the form of a
documentary photograph, translated into line drawings.

Please join us for the official launch of RELIEF, a colouring book
featuring works by:

Dustin Brons, Vanessa Brown, John Burgess, Patrick Cruz, Gabi Dao, Kara
Hansen, Michael Lachman, Brigitte Lochhead, Catherine de Montreuil, Soledad
Muoz, Katrina Niebergal, Lyndsay Pomerantz, Tom Richardson, Rachel & Sarah
Seburn, Adam Shaw, Dan Starling, Kathleen Taylor, Melanie Thibodeau,
Christian Vistan, Stephan Wright, Jacobo Zambrano and Zebulon Zang

The evening of eat, drink, laugh and launch will be accompanied by short
screenings that use colour, form and improvisation in whimsy and gestural
ways, followed by a short reading by Sung Pil Yoon, whose Somewhat Urgent
Series chapbook of "Make Make America America Great Great Again Again" will
also celebrate its debut.

Colouring Book Series is designed and compiled by Emma Metcalfe Hurst &
Scott Kemp

*Screening and reading event will begin at 8:30 PM!*

*There will be a limited supply of BBQ, please feel free to bring any
meat/veg you would like to grill*

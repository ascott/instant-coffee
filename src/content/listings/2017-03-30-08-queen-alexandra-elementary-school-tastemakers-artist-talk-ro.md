TASTEMAKERS Artist Talk: Ron Tran

Friday, March 31, 1:00 pm, Queen Alexandra Elementary School. 

There are a limited number of guest spots available for each TASTEMAKERS 
Artist Talk, please email info@bigrockcandymountan.ca if you are interested in 
attending and further direction will be provided.

Big Rock Candy Mountain presents TASTEMAKERS, a series of artist talks 
takingplace at Queen Alexandra Elementary School this spring. These talks bring 
a diverse array of artists and cultural producers into conversation with the Grade
4/5 students. The short talks are followed by workshops where the artists' ideas
are enacted, tested, and unraveled by the student population.

This week, TASTEMAKERS presents Ron Tran who employs a wide range of 
media, including sculpture, photography, video, performance and installation, as 
a means to blur the boundaries between public and private space and 
authorship and identity. Tran was born in Saigon and moved to Vancouver in 
1987. He holds a BFA from Emily Carr University of Art + Design, Vancouver. Ron 
Tran has participated in group and solo exhibitions in Canada, Europe and Asia. 
He was recently awarded a Mayors Arts Award (Vancouver, 2015) and the 
Knstlerhaus Bethanien residency (Berlin, 2014). His work is featured in Art Cities 
of the Future: 21st Century Avant-Gardes, published by Phaidon Press, and is 
currently on view at Charles H. Scott gallery Goodbye Charles and The 
Vancouver Art Gallery Ambivalent Pleasures. His forthcoming collaborative 
project with Mathew Sawyer will be installed for Capture Festival.

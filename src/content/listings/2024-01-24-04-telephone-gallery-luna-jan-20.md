LUNA
Allison Dawn Johnson
Opening January 20, 4-7pm
Jan 10 - Feb 10th, 2024
Opening at KASKO, 560 Clark
Hosted by Telephone Gallery

TELEPHONE GALLERY is pleased to showcase its thirteenth exhibit at KASKO
Gallery featuring artist Allison Dawn Johnson, presented by David Ullock.

Luna is light " in a world of shadow and darkness. It is a painting series guided by
intuition, illusion and imagination. Referencing the artists memories and inquiring
about subconscious and psychological aspects, Luna asks one to remember their path
and trust their footing. It
is the creative potential of the unconscious mind and reminds
us that the moon is always changing, and so are we.

This series starts with the artist and culminates in oil paintings, leading viewers through
the tarot watercolor series associated with each astrology sign. Each tarot card
prompts contemplation of myths and stories spanning past, present, and future. Luna
opens the door to a promise that what we imagine can come true and will help guide
us even through uncertainties. If one feels lost, its a sign of the right path that will
direct you to clarity and purpose. Luna is guided by surrender to darkness and trust in
the light.

Allison Dawn Johnson is an artist and visual arts teacher residing on the unceded
territory of the xmky"m (Musqueam), Swxw7mesh (Squamish), and s"llwta"
(Tsleil-Waututh). Johnson delves into the materiality of paint, crafting ethereal
landscapes that evoke the essence of the Pacific Northwest. Influenced by dreams,
faded memories, and
psycho-spirituality, she navigates the canvas with a gentle hand,

topdownbottomup.ca
Introducing!!!! To you and the greater Vancouver arts community,
topdownbottomup! We are an emerging artist-run space located at
#212-2280 E Hastings Street.
topdownbottomup is a site for emerging contemporary art. We are four
persons, all UBC visual art graduates. We are inspired by spaces like
The Free University of Copenhagen, and any and all practices actively
engaged with political, cultural and aesthetic theory. We also have an
interest in artwork, with physical presence, affect, intensities and
enchanting thingy-ness.
There are three tasks to be achieved in this one InstantCoffee posting.
1) Introduce our space to community.
2) Call for Submission of an artist for solo show in the end of March
or early April. We will throw an opening/party for the selected artist
and together we will plan an artist led event. This is an opportunity
to exhibit your work in a professional quality yet alternative site
for contemporary art.
Call to Submission-
We are looking for an artist to have a solo show this spring, sooner
hopefully then later. We are interested in emerging artists with
professional practices. We are not a commercial space. We intend the
spring show to be by someone who identifies as female but there will
be other future shows so we encourage all interested to apply. If you
are interested please send an exhibition proposal (one page, this can
be images of works, diagrams or prose) to info@topdownbottomup.ca 
with
some background information egad. Education, art related experience,
past exhibitions etc.

3) Call for potential members to our collective. We are also looking
for contemporary artists interested in a studio space that also
functions as a gallery, school, library, social space, party space
etc. Our goal is to host exhibitions quarterly but we also use the
space as studio/office. Please if you are interested send a bit about
yourself to info@topdownbottomup.ca, or through the contact us page 
on
our lovely website topdownbottomup.ca and we will fill you in on all
the details. This is a unique opportunity to grow as an artist,
researcher, curator, or to invent and then exercise your own arts
related subjectivity. We are artist-run and have a fidelity to the
experimental; there is no limit to what can be done in this space.
For any inquiries please refer to info@topdownbottomup.ca

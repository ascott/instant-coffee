Join New Media Gallery and Jonathan Monaghan as he gives an in-depth look into his
art practice that includes narrative, symbology, mythology and computer animated
video. Jonathan will share his creative and technical process that he uses to create his
highly detailed and layered works with a special attention to his video triptych, Out of
the Abyss (2016). This workshop is beneficial to art students and emerging artists
interested in the methodology of a multidisciplinary artist who has exhibited in a wide
array of institutions from the Sundance Film Festival to the Hermitage Museum in St.
Petersburg. This Workshop will be held virtually via Zoom. For more information and
Zoom link contact: education@newmediagallery.ca

CURRENT CALLS:

1. World UFO Day / July 2 - 25
This exhibition will feature works by self-taught artists that explore outer space, UFO's,
and alien life.

2. Here and Queer / July 30 - August 29
Featuring works by self-taught artists that identify as LGBTQIA2S+

3. Nothing but Flowers / Sept 3 - 26
We are looking for self-taught floral and landscape painters for an exhibition all about
nature and it's beauty.

HOW TO
APPLY:

1. Email up to 6 images of artwork to be considered for the exhibition.

2. The name of the image files should include your name and the title of the work, such
as 1. Sam Stone" Outer Space, 2. Sam Stone " Alien Family.

3. Include a list that describes the images you are sending in. This list should identify
your images as you titled them.

4. Include a statement about yourself that includes your education, exhibition
experience, and your interest in the exhibit theme and how it relates to your artwork.

Email all your information to: outsidersandothers@gmail.com and please put the
relevant exhibition title in the subject line (i.e., UFO, Here and Queer, or Nothing but
Flowers). If submitting to more than one show, please submit applications separately.

OTHER INFORMATION:

- Self taught artists will be given priority consideration for the exhibition because that is
the focus of our organization.
- The gallery retains a 25% commission on the sale of all artworks.
- Artists
are not paid an exhibition fee.
- Outsiders and Others will do all the marketing for the exhibition.
- Artists are responsible for getting the artwork to and from the gallery.

Any questions? Feel free to contact us at outsidersandothers@gmail.com or visit us at
www.outsidersandothers.com

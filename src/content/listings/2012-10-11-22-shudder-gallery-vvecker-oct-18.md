I BELIEVE I CAN FLY is a collection of historical paintings remixed by 
V.Vecker as to situate R Kelly as the subject matter. As an artist whose 
career has thrived from the invention of the remix, it is only apt that R 
Kelly be placed within the visual canon in such a manner. From the 
ego boasting to his supposed sexual supremacy that exists in his lyrical 
prowess, one could only imagine where he would attempt to place 
himself in the visual arts, if given the chance. This collection of work is 
an attempt to view what might have been had R Kelly been of mind to 
insert himself into the works of others that defined many moments and 
histories. With his self aggrandizing swagger, one could only imagine 
what his attempts at remixing classic paintings would be.

I BELIEVE I CAN FLY marks the 5 year anniversary of SHUDDER 
GALLERY and will also be the last gallery show for the foreseeable 
future. Please join us for this exciting last event and thank you for all 
your support over the past five years.

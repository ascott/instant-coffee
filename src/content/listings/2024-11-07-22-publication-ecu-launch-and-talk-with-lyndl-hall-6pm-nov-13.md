Hildegard Press
Launch and talk with Lyndl Hall
November 13, 3:30pm
Art Forums Talk
Rennie Hall
November 13, 6pm
READ Books/Libby Leshgold Gallery

Please join us for a launch event with Lyndl Hall of Hildegard Press. Hildegard Press
is for the collection and distribution of printed matter concerned with the periphery,
the marginal, and the outside, in order to make the edge the centre of focus. The
press aims to publish reflections on outsider or mystical practices, archival texts, as
well as providing space for artists and writers to produce
publications that fall outside
the scope of their professional practice.
Hall will also be presenting an artist talk at 3:30pm presented by the Audain Faculty
of Art, hosted by FRMS 300: Art Forums Course.
Lyndl Hall is a visual artist who has had solo exhibitions at the Burrard Arts
Foundation, Vancouver; the Burnaby Art Gallery; the Reach Gallery Museum,
Abbotsford; CSA Space, Vancouver; and in a greenhouse, Calgary. Her work has
been published in The Capilano Review, West Coast Line, and by Publication Studio
Vancouver and Moniker Press. Hall has a BFA from Concordia University, Montreal
and a MAA in Visual Arts from Emily Carr University of Art + Design, Vancouver. She
is a Sessional Instructor at the Alberta University of the Arts in Calgary, and her work
focuses on the relationship between mark-making and language. She also runs
Hildegard Press, an artist publishing project with a focus on the esoteric and the
overlooked.

ALLISON HRABLUIK

Meat Loaf : Presented by DAA 
& 
This Is They Way They Make Us Bend : Cineworks: Special Projects launch party

November 19 - 26, 2016
Closing Reception & Launch: Saturday November 26 at 7 pm
Dynamo Arts Association (DAA)
Suite 103 - 30 East 6th Ave
Open Saturday & Sunday, and by appointment

Join us for the closing reception of Meat Loaf a animated work by Allison Hrabluik 
at DAA, in conjunction with the launch of Cineworks: Special Projects. 

Meat Loaf is a short portrait of an animal trying to come into its new body. The 
work is made from a series of animated watercolor drawings that in sequence 
give breath to the strange form.

Cineworks: Special Projects is an online exhibition facility for select commissions 
supporting excellence and experimentation in artistic practice. Cineworks is 
launching this platform with This Is The Way They Make Us Bend, an animated 
video by Allison Hrabluik with commissioned essays by Kim Nguyen and Pan 
Wendt. This Is The Way They Make Us Bend was made at Cineworks' Annex 
Production Facility in Vancouver, with funding assistance from The City of 
Vancouver for the Cineworks' Local Artist in Residence Program

Allison Hrabluik lives and works in Vancouver. Her videos have recently been 
exhibited at The Confederation Arts Centre, Charlottetown; SFU Gallery, 
Burnaby; and The Vancouver Art Gallery, with screenings at Images Festival, 
Toronto; Kassel Dokfest; and Chicago Underground Film Festival.

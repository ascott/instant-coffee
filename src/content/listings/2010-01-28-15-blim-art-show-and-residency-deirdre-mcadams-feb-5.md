New Works by Deirdre McAdams

These paintings are the latest manifestation of my interest in minimal
abstraction and optical art. Most recently this investigation has been
about a cut-and-paste approach to pattern and minimal form. The paintings
usually begin with an all-over pattern or colour composition which then
acts to influence the introduction of a disparate element: patterns are
interrupted by incongruous forms and minimal colour-fields are disrupted by
the superimposition of graphic shapes. Materials are a principal source of
inspiration and in my most recent series the use of spray paint allowed for
an approach that could either emphasize or remove gesture. These works
represent an investigation of that dynamic as well as an interest in
painting that works to engage the eye of the viewer in the tradition of
optical art.

Deirdre McAdams is a visual artist living and working in Vancouver. She is
a recent graduate of Emily Carr University of Art and Design (2008) where
she studied painting.

Sylvan Hamburger: Once We Flew Through Open Windows
The Art Shop (TAS) ~ 2111 Main Street (lobby), Vancouver BC

Opening: Saturday April 12, 3-6pm.

View by appointments from April 12- May 25.
Email Mariana Rivera ~ mariana@theartshop.ca

Sylvan Hamburger (b. 1994, Vancouver BC) is a visual artist working in printmaking,
textiles and public installation. His imagery is often puzzle-like, drawing inspiration
from architecture, literature, daydreams and other found materials to create new
compositions of form, texture and colour. In making, Sylvan combined found materials
with unconventional printmaking approaches to piece together his colourful images
into site-specific installations. Throughout this practice, Sylvans work is directly
shaped by the spaces and places in which it is made, notably his community of East
Vancouver within the traditional and unceded homelands of the Musqueam, Squamish,
and Tsleil-Waututh Nations.

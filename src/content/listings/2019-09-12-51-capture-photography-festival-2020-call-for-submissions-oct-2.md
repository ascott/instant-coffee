Submissions for the 2020 Capture Photography Festival are officially open!

From September 9 to October 21, 2019, Capture Photography Festival is accepting 
Exhibition and Event proposals for inclusion in the juried Selected Exhibitions Program 
and non-juried Event program.

Capture 2020 runs April 2"29.

All proposals will be processed through our submission platform Submittable, visit 
capturephotofest.submittable.com/submit. There is a submission fee of $25 for 
Exhibitions, which includes a 1-year Capture Photography Festival Society membership. 
There is no Event submission fee. Please note that Submittable requires account 
registrations (its free!)

Printing Grants will be available again this year! We were thrilled to award over $15,000 
worth of Printing Grants to 2019 Festival participants.

Before submitting, please visit the Submissions FAQ at capturephotofest.com/faq. For 
any other queries, please email coordinator@capturephotofest.com.

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Thursday, SEP 26

Join us for a presentation and discussion with Sara-Jeanne Bourget, current artist-in-
residence. Bourget is the inaugural recipient of the Studio Residency Award, a new 
partnership between Griffin Art Projects and Emily Carr University. Continuing from her 
recent thesis project at Emily Carr University of Art and Design, Bourgets large scale 
works explore physical and conceptual interconnections between Rocks, Paper, and 
Charcoal, based on [her] geological explorations and the inherent materiality of both 
paper and the artists own handmade charcoal. 

Sara-Jeanne Bourget was born in Lvis, Qubec in 1988. She obtained her BFA in 
Studio Arts at Concordia University (2015) and her MFA from Emily Carr University 
(2019). In 2016, she took part in residency programs in Finland, Quebec and Vermont 
where she also exhibited her work. She is the recipient of two grants (2017 & 2019) from 
the Elizabeth Greenshields Foundation in support of her work and studies. 

This talk is the first in a series of Tuesday evening talks with artists on their work and 
process throughout the fall of 2019, at North Vancouver District Public Library, Lynn 
Valley Branch, featuring artists currently in residence at Griffin Art Projects, North 
Vancouver.

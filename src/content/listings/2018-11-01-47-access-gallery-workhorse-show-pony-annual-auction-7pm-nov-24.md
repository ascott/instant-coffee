FUNDRAISER

workhorse, show pony
Saturday 24 November 2018 at 7pm
Access Gallery's annual auction fundraiser

AUCTION EVENING Saturday 24 November 7pm
PREVIEW Wednesday 14 November at 6pm
ON VIEW 15 to 23 November from 12 to 5pm

WORKHORSE, SHOW PONY is an energetic celebration of hardworking and 
dynamic artist-run culture! This years fundraising auction features a range of 
fantastic work generously donated by over 50 emerging and established artists 
including Vanessa Brown, David Khang, Guadalupe Martinez, Ramey Newell, and 
Christian Nicolay. Jonathan Middleton and drag queen Anne Xiety take the reins as 
MC and auctioneer while DJs Ron Tran and Rebecca Brewer drive the dancing. As 
is tradition, expect a mystery artist whose identity will only be revealed through 
bidding. The generosity of members, donors, and friends like you continue to make it 
possible for the gallery to support compelling and critically engaged emergent 
practices. Show up in your ribbons and tack to Access most important fundraiser of 
the year!

$25 entrance includes an annual membership
current members FREE

Preview the artworks at
auction.accessgallery.ca

Place absentee bids directly with Director/Curator Katie Belcher at 
k.belcher@accessgallery.ca until 5pm on Saturday 24 November 2018.

This event is generously sponsored by Fine Art Framing, Steel & Oak Brewing Co., 
Summerhill Pyramid Winery, and Odd Society Spirits.

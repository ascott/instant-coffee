The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Guo Pei: Couture Beyond
Saturday, October 13

The Vancouver Art Gallery presents the Canadian premiere of Guo Pei:
Couture Beyond, the first-ever fashion exhibition in the Gallerys history
October 13, 2018 to January 20, 2019. Chinas foremost couturire Guo Pei
is known for her avant-garde silhouettes seamlessly fusing contemporary
aesthetics with pre-republic opulence. Tracing the evolution of Guo Peis
design sensibility over ten years, this exhibition features more than forty
breathtaking designs. This includes the imperial yellow cape"constructed
from fifty-five pounds of embroidered silk and featuring a five-meter
train"famously worn by recording artist Rihanna at the 2015 Met Gala.
Visitors will see up-close the unmistakable craftsmanship of Guo Peis
jaw-dropping fashion masterpieces that have captured the Western imagination.

For tickets and info, visit: vanartgallery.bc.ca

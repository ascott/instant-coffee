Peter Rehberg : Artist Talk on Recollection GRM

In collaboration with Deep Blue, Duplex will host an artist talk with 
Peter Rehberg from the music label Editions Mego. The conversation 
will focus on the label's ongoing collaboration with the French institution 
(INA)GRM, an organization originally founded by musique concrte 
pioneer Pierre Schaeffer, and currently under the direction of Franois 
Bonnet. Using the designation: Recollection GRM, Editions Mego has 
re-released various audio recordings from the archives of (INA)GRM. 
The discussion will explore some of the questions surrounding the re-
publication of artistic works when the artist is no longer present, and 
the cultural implications of a collaboration between an independent 
music label and a French national institution.

Recollection GRM : http://editionsmego.com/releases/recollection-grm/

Peter Rehberg, who performs under the pseudonym Pita, is the 
founder of Editions Mego. His music borders between noise, industrial, 
electro-acoustic and techno. He has collaborated extensively with 
artists such as Jim O'Rourke, Marcus Schmickler, and Stephan 
O'Malley. As the head of Mego, Rehberg has released the works of 
artists such as Heather Leigh, Klara Lewis, Kevin Drumm, Thomas 
Brinkmann, Florian Hecker, Bernard Parmegiani, Russell Haswell, 
Iannis Xenakis, Oren Ambarchi, Bill Orcutt, Mark Fell and many more.

About The Clock
Recognised as one of the most important contemporary artworks of our time, The Clock is 
an audiovisual tour-de-force. Presented in a custom-built cinema within the gallery, the work 
montages film and television footage from the last 70 years. The time represented in each 
clip is synchronised with actual time, allowing The Clock to function as a 24-hour timepiece. 
Christian Marclay won the coveted Gold Lion Award at the Venice Biennale in 2011 for this 
now-famous artwork, recently presented at the Tate Modern, London.

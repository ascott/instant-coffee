Western Front | Patrycja Kamska | 8pm | April 12

I only know you in 6 green letter words
Grand Luxe Hall, 303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver
Opening Reception: Thursday, April 12, 8pm-10pm

I only know you in 6 green letter words is a meditation on film as photography and how it
relates to shared perceptions of time's rhythms.

The film unravels times invisible flow into a succession of still and visible moments,
tracing the habits of our inner processes of recollection, which condense, displace, and
plunge abruptly into forgotten recesses of our past.

This exhibition is presented as part of Capture Photography Festival.
[http://capturephotofest.com/exhibitions/i-only-know-you-in-6-green-letter-words/](http://capturephotofest.com/exhibitions/i-only-know-you-in-6-green-letter-words/)

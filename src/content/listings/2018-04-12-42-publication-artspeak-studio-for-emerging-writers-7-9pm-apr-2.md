Artspeak
233 Carrall St, Vancouver, BC
Tuesday, April 24 at 7 pm

Over the past ten months, the Studio for Emerging Writers has been running 
weekly/bi-weekly workshops, with occasional site visits, discussions, meals, walks, 
edits, bus and boat rides, readings, and hang-outs. During this time together, we 
brewed a cider along side a set of five chapbooks, each written by a participant of 
the Studio. We would like to invite you to join us on Tuesday, April 24 at 7 pm to 
celebrate the launch of these publications with READINGS and CIDER by:

Christian Vistan
Cristina Holman
Claire Geddes Bailey
Emma Metcalfe Hurst
Kiel Torres

Publications (for sale) and snacks (free) will be available - bring cash.

Special thanks to Sheryda Warrener, Stephan Garneau, Erica Wilk and Artspeak.

We acknowledge that this event takes place on ancestral and unceded Coast Salish 
territory of the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil Waututh First Nations.

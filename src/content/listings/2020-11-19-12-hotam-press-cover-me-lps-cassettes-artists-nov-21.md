Cover
Me: LPs, Cassettes & Artists
Nov 21, 2020 - Jan 30, 2021
Open hours: Saturday 12 - 5 PM, or by appointment

This exhibition brings together more than 50 artists who work with music and sound.
For the duration of this exhibition, our gallery will be transformed into a record shop /
discotheque / listening space. Visitors are welcome to browse and sample through this
rich collection of artists works.

Here are some of the artists and contributors:
Alan Belcher, Bill Burns, Cathy Busby, Juan Cisneros Neumann, Laura Gilder, Makiko
Hara, Holy Hum, Hotham Sound, Laiwan, Daniel Laskarin, Zachari Logan, Doug
Melynk, my name is scot, Kristin Nelson, Carol Sawyer, Kathy Slade, Chrysanne
Stathacos, Paul Walde, Lam Wong . . . and many more.

Most artworks are available for purchase.

Also, LPs and Cassettes by artists are accepted on an ongoing basis until January 30.
Submissions will be added to the exhibition. Please drop off or mail us your work to be
part of the show.

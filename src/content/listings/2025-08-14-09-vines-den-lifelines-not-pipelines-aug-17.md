1 to 3 pm on August 17 at the Vines Den, 825 E. Hastings

When Gitxsan and Gitanyow leaders protect their Laxyip, and Wetsuweten leaders
protect their Yintah, everyone benefits from the work of these frontline land
protectors. How can people in Vancouver organize to uplift their absolutely valid
concerns and warnings? This session is a teach-in.

We are in a five-alarm moment, as the Prince Rupert Gas Transmission (PRGT)
pipeline and the Coastal GasLink (CGL) pipeline expand fracked methane gas at
massive levels we cannot afford if we want to prevent climate disaster from
accelerating.

These pipelines threaten both Indigenous and Canadian sovereignty; the PRGT
would be built by Bechtel, a US military contractor. And the proposed Ksi Lisims
gas terminal is owned by Texas-based Western LNG, whose investors are major
MAGA and Trump supporters.

Land back is a lifeline for everyone.

This Freedom School event is held in conjunction with the exhibition Driving in
Palestine https://drivinginpalestine.cargo.site/. All Freedom School events are free
and by donation. Freedom School engages liberation praxis and community building
in support of a free Palestine and all who advocate for justice in the face of settler
colonialism, militarism, state violence and oppression in our shared and
interconnected worlds.

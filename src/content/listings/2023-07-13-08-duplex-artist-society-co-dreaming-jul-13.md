Allison Johnson and Liz Toohey-Wiese present their two-person show Co-Dreaming at
Duplex Artist Society. The work in this exhibition takes seriously the emergence of
imagery from dreams, imagination, and the unconscious. Symbols of animals and
figures
emerge from and dissolve into landscapes, connecting the human and non-human world,
and inviting the viewer into the creation of narratives and associations in this series of
drawings and paintings. Their work speaks to each other through the inevitable psychic
links made in a friendship: sharing of experiences, stories, books, dreams, ideas, and all
the other ways in which people influence the people they love.

"Co-Dreaming" opens at Duplex Artist Society at 1889 Franklin st. on Thursday July 13th,
7-9 pm. The exhibition runs for 1 week, and appointments can be made for weekend
viewings (July 15/16) by emailing Liz at ltooheywiese@gmail.com

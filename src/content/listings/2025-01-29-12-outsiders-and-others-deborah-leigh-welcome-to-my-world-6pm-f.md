Exhibition: Deborah Leigh: Welcome to My World
Dates: February 1 - 22, 2025
Location: Outsiders and Others, #100 - 938 Howe
Street, Vancouver

Associated events:
February 1: 2-3pm.
Artist Discussion with Deborah Leigh. Free & open to the public.

February 1: 6-8pm.
Opening Reception. Free & open to the public.

Please join us in celebrating the new solo exhibition by self taught artist Deborah
Leigh.

Deborah is one of a few artists that we have worked with on a long term basis
because we believe she represents some of the best outsider art being created
today in BC.

Deborah was born in Prince George in the early 1950s. She didnt start to draw until
she was in her 50s and is now in her 70s, and more creative than ever.

During a period of recovery from an illness, Deborah started to doodle to help her
relax. These doodles quickly evolved in size and in subject matter, from black and
white to full colour.

Deborah now works and lives in Victoria on Vancouver Island.

"I create as a form of escapism, and I just love the process of creating. Each piece is
never pre-planned, I simply work from my imagination,
intuition, and the
subconscious.
My hope is that viewers could be drawn into my images with a sense of curiosity,
wonderment, and questioning of what the images are trying to portray. I like to
include pathways, roads, and byways that represent the journeys we all undertake
and where these paths may be leading us to in our lives.
My art is the pathway to the imaginary place in our minds. It is escapism " a place I
can journey to, where all of our pain, struggles and worries can be left behind. Within
my work I find a sense of peace, even if its just for a matter of moments.
Deborah Leigh

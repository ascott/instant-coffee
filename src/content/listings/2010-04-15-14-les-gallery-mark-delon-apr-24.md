Known for his comic book aesthetic and disjointed humorous narratives, Mark Delong's new 
ceramic work uses a primitive visual language to explore sculptural realizations of childlike 
animations. Along with anew earthenware egg, Delong includes a sculpture made of ceramic, 
wood and a sea urchin in a minimalist meets Basquiat style. Also on view are small collage 
works and large scale airbrush drawings.

Born in 1978 in Fredericton New Brunswick, self taught artist Delong has had numerous solo and 
group exhibitions including Ed Varie Gallery in New York, Hunter and Cook Gallery in Toronto 
and Attache Gallery internationally. Delong currently lives and works in Vancouver, B.C.

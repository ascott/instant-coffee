Part 1: February 27, 10am - 12pm PST
Part 2: March 14, 3pm - 5pm PST

We are excited to share our upcoming digital roundtable series on intersectional
accessibility in the arts! This program came out of Cinevolutions initiative to create and
advocate for more accessibility, particularly through online events.

The first roundtable on Sunday, February 27 at 10am PST will focus on intersectional
community-building. In conversation with a group of artists and advocates, we will
examine the way arts organizations build community with individuals of intersecting
identities. We will look at the role reciprocality, relevancy and accountability play in
these relationships. The conversation
will feature artists and art administrators Amy
Amantea, Sean Lee, Vasuki Shanmuganathan, and Whess Harman. Regan Shrumm
will be the facilitator.

The second roundtable on Monday, March 14 at 3 pm PST will gather a group of artists
from historically underrepresented communities to discuss the barriers they face within
art institutions. The artists will question art organizations approaches and dream of
new methods and processes that art organizations could take. The conversation will
feature artists Natasha "Courage" Bacchus, Michel Dumont, Shannon Finnegan,
Ebony Gooden, and Hanan Hazime, and Regan Shrumm as the facilitator.

Accessibility
Both events will offer ASL interpreters, captioning, a land acknowledgement, visual
descriptions, and a 15 minute break. If you have any access needs, please contact
Accessibility Coordinator Regan Shrumm at regan@cinevolutionmedia.com or 778-
803-7150 x 5.

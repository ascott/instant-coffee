The Nature of Gold

Flory Huang & Tiffany Blaise, 2020.
Photographic film prints and watercolour paintings.

The Nature of Gold by Flory Huang and Tiffany Blaise is a collection of work born of the
artists reverence for uncovering beauty, reflecting on our surroundings and the inner
contemplation that takes place to influence perceptions of the world.

Flory Huang is a natural light photographer attracted to high-contrast and
sentimental,
usually nostalgic, moments in time. Tiffany Blaise is a landscape painter enamoured
with capturing her natural environments in watercolor. Her practice brings meditative
processes into her everyday while lovingly celebrating the Pacific West Coast terrain.

The artists intentions in displaying these bodies of work are bridged by their shared
visual expression of optimism: Flory Huang seeks to provoke loving curiosity in every
moment and Tiffany Blaise seeks to provide viewers a quiet and contemplative
environment for themselves, much like they would in the physical natural terrain.

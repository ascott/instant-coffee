Structures of the unsaid

Yongzhen li

Artist Reception with Q & A
Thursday March 5th 6 - 8pm

Yongzhen li's work examines the psychological and symbolic violence embedded within
Chinese patriarchal culture, focusing on its influence on identity formation, gendered
expectations, and modes of emotional expression. Rooted in lived experience and shaped by
a neurodivergent sensitivity to sensory detail, the practice draws on traditional Chinese
painting materials"ink, rice paper, brush, and mugwort dye"to create fragmentary,
emotionally charged visual narratives.

For li, painting functions as a direct confrontation. Rejecting habit and repetition, each mark
is deliberate, painful, and necessary. Ink on xuan paper allows no erasure, mirroring the
irreversible imprints of social conditioning. The brushs flexibility enables the articulation of
quiet tensions"silent resistance, internalized shame, and moments of absurd collapse.

Everyone is welcome!

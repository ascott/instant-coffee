About the Lucas R. Soi Private Collection

I got the idea for a private collection when I visited the Museé Rodin in Paris. Rodin's sculptures are all over the grounds of this huge mansion. Inside the house is some of his work but most of the rooms are furnished with pieces from his personal art collection. Paintings by Munch and Van Gogh, Greek sculpture, hard-carved furniture -- these works are arranged throughout the house.

Now think of your home. We all have art collections: DVD collections, books, records, photographs, magazine pages taped to the refridgerator.

The Lucas R. Soi Private Collection is a unique combination of personal ephemera and genuine works of art by young Canadian and international contemporary artists. Scattered among the original art are pieces collected during Soi's personal journey through life.

They include:

1) A handwritten letter from his uncle
2) A Stanley Kubrick box set
3) A receipt for his Swiss bank account

All great collections reveal a glimpse into the life of the owner. Come and see this unique assemblage today.

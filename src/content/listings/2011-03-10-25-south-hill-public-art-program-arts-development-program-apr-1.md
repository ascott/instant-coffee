The South Hill Public Art Plan Committee seeks to contract the services of
a Lead Artist to oversee a grant
received from the Community and Neighbourhood Arts Development Program to
produce a public art project
for the business district of South Hill on Fraser Street.

The artwork commissioned will be part of a growing collection of art in the
public space as envisioned in and
guided by the South Hill Public Art Plan.This project will transform
nondescript streetscape surfaces into
platforms for creative and distinct artwork reﬂecting the communityâ€™s
unique character.

This is a part time position for a 6-8 month contract with approximate
project start-up in April, 2011. The
working budget for this project is $13,000.This includes an artist design
fee of $5,000 and accounts for
production costs, project management and a mentoring component.The position
is intended for a candidate
who has a thorough understanding of contemporary art practice, is an
excellent communicator, creative thinker
and possesses the necessary administrative and managerial skills to
continue the South Hill Public Art Program.
The Lead Artist will report to the South Hill BIA Public Art Committee and
the South Hill BIA Executive Board.

For further information contact info@southhillbia.ca and request Lead
Artist terms of reference and The South
Hill Art Plan.

Deadline for applications is Friday March 25,2011 @ 5pm.

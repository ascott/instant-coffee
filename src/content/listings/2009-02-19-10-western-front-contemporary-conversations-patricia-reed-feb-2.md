Contemporary Conversations: Patricia Reed

Monday February 23, 2009
Western Front Grand Luxe Theatre, 7 PM

Western Front Exhibitions in collaboration with the Fillip Review is
pleased to present Contemporary Conversations. Several times a year, this
public program will engage the intersections of aesthetics and radical
thinking in contemporary art. Reflecting a broad range of perspectives and
practices, these conversations will build upon each other to generate
discussion about art and ideas.

This edition features Berlin- and Hanover-based artist and writer Patricia
Reed. Since receiving her BFA from Concordia University, MontrÃ©al, Reed has
participated in the research and residency programs of the Centre for
Contemporary Art Kitakyushu, Japan (2001-02); CCA Jeleni, Prague, Czech
Republic (2003); Akademie Schloss Solitude, Stuttgart Germany (2003-04,
2005) and The Banff Centre for the Arts, Banff, Canada (2009). Recent
exhibitions/projects include M6.1 Contract of Discord with IDEA magazine,
Cluj, Romania; M6.2 Off-season, Nicosia, Cyprus; Just Space(s), Los Angeles
Contemporary Exhibitions (with Markus Miessen); Airs de Paris, Centre
Pompidou (with Bruno Latour), Manual CC, Uqbar project space, Berlin, White
House Redux, Storefront Art and Architecture, NYC. As a writer, she has
contributed feature articles, interviews and reviews for Art Papers, C
Magazine, Fillip and Neue Review.

Lundh and Reed take the term "aesthetic management" as a point of departure
for a conversation about interdisciplinary, collaborative practices.

Contemporary Conversations is organized and hosted by independent curator
and writer Johan Lundh.

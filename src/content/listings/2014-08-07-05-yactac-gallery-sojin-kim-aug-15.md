Sunset Home

Sojin Kim

Opening reception: Friday, August 15 ~ 7-11pm

Show runs August 16 - 22. Open by appointment.

Yactac Gallery
7206 Ontario Street (@ E.6th St)
Vancouver BC
V5X 3B7

Yactac has been imagined and interpreted by artists in various different
ways for the past seven years. The use of a house as an exhibition space--
the gallery and the domestic-- has come to an end as Yactac is moving into
a new place on Main and Broadway. Now the function of the gallery-home no
longer takes on its legacy and the only thing that remains in the house is
the memory of the arts. Sunset Home presents a wall painting along with
Sojin's wooden pieces which are driven from her interest in space and
transformation of space in relation to the elements that inhabit it.

-Kim

Sunset Home is the last exhibition presented at Yactac Gallery before its
move to a new place.

Sojin Kim has graduated from Emily Carr University of Arts and Design in
2013. Kim's practice focuses on transnational identity and formation of
identity in relation to spaces.

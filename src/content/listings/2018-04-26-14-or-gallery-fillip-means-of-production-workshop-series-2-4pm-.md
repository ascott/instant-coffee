Join Fillip and Steffanie Ling for a Means of Production workshop on criticism.

Saturday, April 28th
2:00 PM - 4:00 PM
Or Gallery, 555 Hamilton Street
Free

Means of Production is an occasional series of free, theoretical workshops 
investigating the material conditions of contemporary artistic practice. Organized 
alongside our Reading Group series, these ongoing workshops address issues of 
labour, infrastructure, and production in a small, seminar-style enviornment.

Entrance to this event is free but space is limited and an RSVP is required.

ABOUT THE PRESENTER
Steffanie Ling is a curator at VIVO Media Arts and co-founder of Charcuterie, a 
journal for experimental art writing. Her work has appeared in C Magazine, San 
Francisco Art Quarterly, and Brooklyn Rail. Her book Nascar: Stories was published 
in 2016 by Blank Cheque and Cuts of Thin Meat, a book of minimal poems, was 
released by Spare Room in 2015.

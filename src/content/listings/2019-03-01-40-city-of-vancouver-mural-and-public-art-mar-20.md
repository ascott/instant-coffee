The City of Vancouvers Mural and Public Art Programs would like to 
share with you three exciting opportunities.

The City is inviting proposals for temporary painted murals and digital 
printed artwork to be installed in the public realm. As part of 
reconciliation and cultural redress, these three artist calls aim to 
celebrate the creativity of our rich and varied cultural communities.

Artists will be commissioned by an external 3-5 member panel of local 
artists and art professionals with a connection to each respective 
community.

There are available civic sites listed in the guidelines; as well, we will 
accept artist-initiated sites on private and public property

Artists can apply through an online web-form here. A website will be 
released soon under www.vancouver.ca/artistcalls or here. Deadline to 
apply is March 20th, 2019 at 4pm.

See the attached guidelines with full details and posters to promote. 
Please share this with artists you may know!

Feel free to send us a message at murals@vancouver.ca if you have 
any questions.

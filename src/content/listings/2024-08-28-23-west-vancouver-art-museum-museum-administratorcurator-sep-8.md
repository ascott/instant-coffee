The District of West Vancouver is seeking a permanent, full-time Administrator/Curator
for the West Vancouver Art Museum. The deadline to apply is September 8, 2024.

The position is responsible for planning, developing, supervising and implementing
exhibitions, programs and services; working with community partners to implement
offsite exhibitions and programs; supporting the Art Museum Advisory Panel as the
Staff Liaison; managing staffing needs, performance management and volunteers;
developing and maintaining relationships with District staff, the public, arts community,
business sector and all levels of government;
representing the Art Museum at
community initiatives, other local, regional and national professional meetings,
conferences; developing and administering approved budgets and grants; grant
writing.

For the full job description, qualifications, salary, and to apply, please visit
westvancouver.ca/careers.

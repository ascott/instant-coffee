Congratulations to Jeremey Shaw for winning the 2016 Sobey Art Award.

SOBEY ART AWARD JURY'S STATEMENT

About Jeremy Shaw
Jeremy Shaw's work speaks to a fundamental longing for transcendence. He creates 
and reflects extraordinary experiences and shows us how art can translate what is 
challenging to articulate. We are pleased to announce Jeremy Shaw as the winner 
of the 2016 Sobey Art Award.

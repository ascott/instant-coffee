WORKS ON VIEW November 14 - 18, 2017, 12 - 5PM
PREVIEW NIGHT Wednesday, November 15, 2017, 6PM
AUCTION EVENING Saturday, November 18, 2017, 7PM

Access Gallery
222 E Georgia St, Vancouver, BC

Access Gallery invites you to our most important fundraiser of the year! Pivots and
Spins celebrates recent shifts and changes at Access: a major gallery expansion 
and renovation and the welcoming of our new Director/Curator Katie Belcher. 
Recently arrived from the East Coast, Katie is eager to meet the Access community! 
Promising to be our most exciting auction yet, Pivots and Spins features spectacular 
work generously donated by over 50 local and international emerging and 
established artists including Kathleen Ritter, Sandra Semchuk, Derya Akay, Cindy 
Mochizuki and others. Continuing a tradition first established at our 2015 auction, we 
will also include a mystery artist whose identity will only be revealed through bidding. 
The support of members, donors, and participants like you at this important 
fundraiser continue to make it possible for the gallery to support compelling and 
critically engaged emergent practices. Join us as we warm the new Access Gallery 
space and look to the coming year!

Join us to preview the artworks and meet Director/Curator Katie Belcher on 
Wednesday, November 15, 6PM. Enjoy inspiring canaps by Juno Kim Catering and 
organic BC wines from Summerhill Pyramid Winery, and pilsner from Steam Whistle 
Brewing.

We cordially invite you to the Access Gallery Annual Auction Fundraiser, Pivots and
Spins, November 18, 7PM, with handcrafted cocktails, organic BC wines from
Summerhill Pyramid Winery, pilsner from Steam Whistle, and music by CiTR 
101.9FM.

Admission is $25, and includes membership at Access Gallery for the calendar year.
Entry is free for current members. Memberships can be purchased for $25 at the 
event or online.
Preview the works here. Absentee bids will be accepted until 5PM, Saturday, 
November 18, 2017. To place an absentee bid or make another enquiry email 
Director/Curator Katie Belcher at k.belcher@accessgallery.ca.

We're looking for Volunteers! Help us make this event a success!

*

Access Gallery gratefully acknowledges the generous support of Fine Art Framing,
Summerhill Pyramid Winery, Steam Whistle Brewery, Juno Kim Catering in making
Pivots and Spins possible, as well as the ongoing support of The Canada Council for 
the Arts, the Province of BC, the BC Arts Council and BC Gaming Commission, the 
City of Vancouver, and our committed donors, members, and volunteers.

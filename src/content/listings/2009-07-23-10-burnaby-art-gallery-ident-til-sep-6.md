I:Dent
Constructing and Deconstructing
Personal and Social Identities

Burnaby Art Gallery
July 28 - September 6, 2009

Studies reveal that physical appearance, including clothing, remains the factor
used most often in developing a first impression of someone and has an even greater
effect on personality. The importance of uniforms is made even more evident when
the importance of the group identity grows. The function of group identity - distinctiveness,
demarcation of social status or membership - is especially important in regards to the 
societal or social norms. This exhibition uses these points as a way to address the
particularities of how modes of dress and the concept of uniforms shape identity,
relationships, and the ways in which identity can be performed.

I-Dent is a group exhibition, featuring a mix of established and emerging Canadian
artists including Derek Dunlop, Toni Latour, William Eakin, Jeremiah Birnbaum, and 
Kristina Lee Podesva.

Reception: Sunday, August 2, 1-3pm

Identity Symposium: Thursday, August 6, 7pm

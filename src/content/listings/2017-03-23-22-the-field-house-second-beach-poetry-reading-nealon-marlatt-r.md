Poetry Reading:

Christopher Nealon
with
Daphne Marlatt and Shazia Hafiz Ramji

Wednesday, March 29th, 2017
7:00pm

The Field House at Second Beach @ Stanley Park, Vancouver
Hosted by Instant Coffee
In conjunction with Jeff Derksens graduate poetics seminar at SFU.

Christopher Nealon is a poet and a scholar who lives in Washington D.C. and works at 
Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore. His books of poetry include The Joyous Age 
(Black Square Editions, 2004), Plummet (Edge Books, 2009) and Heteronomy (Edge 
Books, 2014). As one of the most evocative readers of poetry and culture today, his 
critical publications include The Matter of Capital: Poetry and Crisis in The American 
Century (Harvard, 2011), Foundlings: Lesbian and Gay Historical Emotion before 
Stonewall (Duke, 2001), and numerous essays in journals such as Critical Inquiry, 
Mediations, and Representations.

Of Nealons poetry, Brian Blanchfield has written:

these longish, idiosyncratic, sharply conscious, polyhedral essay-poems that Chris 
Nealon has been writing for the last few years. (The first five constitute the entirety of 
one of the standout poetry collections of the century so far, Heteronomy, published in 
2014.) but they are also fluid, drawn forward with graceful carriage, continuous, 
developing. Like his others, this poem is an instrument for thinking, about sexuality, 
about interiority, about difference, about solidarity. I mean, you can use it, to think.

Joining Chris are the Vancouver poets Daphne Marlatt and Shazia Hafiz Ramji.

This reading takes place in conjunction with Instant Coffees residency in the Beach 
House at Second Beach in Spapayeq, (the way the water flows around), or Stanley 
Park on the unceded and traditional land of the Musqueam, Tsleil-Waututh, and 
Squamish people.

This reading is also in recognition of the poet Jamie Reid, who, along with his life-long 
partner Carol Reid, organized the initial Human Be-In in the park on March 26, 1967 " 
almost 50 years to the day of this reading.

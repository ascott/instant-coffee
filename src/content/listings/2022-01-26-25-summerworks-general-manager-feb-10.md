JOB: SummerWorks General Manager

Title: GENERAL MANAGER
Start Date: April 2022 (flexible)
Salary: $60,000 - $70,000 per annum plus benefits
Engagement Conditions: Full Time
Employment
CLOSING DATE: February 11, 2022 (11:59pm EST)

Join the SummerWorks team! We are seeking a full-time General Manager to provide
operational and strategic management of our year-round operations with an eye on
future development. We warmly encourage
applications from folks from equity-seeking
groups and individuals with experience both inside and outside the performing arts
sector.

SummerWorks is a leader, collaborator, and community builder at the forefront of
contemporary performance. Our year-round programming is anchored by the
SummerWorks Performance Festival in August. SummerWorks is looking for a bold,
generous, and diligent individual as our new General Manager; someone who can
bring their organizational systems, tools, and attention to detail to our live arts
organization.

While SummerWorks is based in Toronto, we are open to candidates who are willing to
live in Toronto with the assistance of a relocation fee. Salary range is $60,000 -
$70,000, plus benefits, and we are offering funded, on-job, specialized coaching and
self-directed training to support the candidate we hire.

Applications are due February 11, 2022.

For full applications details visit our website: summerworks.ca/job-call-general-
manager-position/

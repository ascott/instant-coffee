SENTIMENTAL JOURNEY

Michael Drebert, Owen Kydd, Mike McLean & Laura Trunkey, Gareth Moore, Kevin
Schmidt and Kara Uzelman

June 19 - August 16, 2009

OPENING RECEPTION: Thursday, June 18th, 6-9pm

Sentimental Journey continues our interest in contemporary representations
of the landscape. For this group exhibition we bring together local artists
who use the journey as a starting point, approaching the expedition, or the
landscape traversed, with a sincerity made evident by their fascination with
documentation and the way documentation is presented. The works in
Sentimental Journey are both real and fictive representations of
often-undetermined treks. Many of the works have an optimism that is tied to
the landscape, the desire to be outside and the character of the
artist/wanderer. The works in this context bring forward the tight relation
between process and presentation, embracing the romance of the journey while
also recontextualizing and privileging the terms of display in order to
question sentimentality in the experiences of their respective trips.

In conjunction with the opening of Sentimental Journey the CAG will launch
Landscape, the first publication in a series of three that use the petit
genres of landscape, portrait and still life to reflect the CAG's recent
programming, framing current tendencies in contemporary art. Included in
this publication is "Landscape and I" by Vancouver writer Michael Turner and
"Sentimental Journey" by CAG curator Jenifer Papararo with interviews
between Tim Gardner and Vancouver photographer Stephen Waddell, and bgl and
Toronto artist and architect Adrian Blackwell.

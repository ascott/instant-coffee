Lasalu Syue Si Grant: A mini grant of 100 CAD for early-emerging artists or creatives in
metro vancouver to teach a workshop on a creative knowledge or competency that was
missed out in school.

For more information, visit @lasaluartlibrary on Instagram. Apply at
https://forms.gle/w8e7cKXyYMBT3p8aA before Sept 24, 23:55.

Lasalu Art Library is a building classroom art resource thats breathing a breath of air,
shaking like the earth, speaking as a friend. This library will develop into a collection of
artworks, books, teaching materials, workshops, and events that seek to decentralize
Eurocentric art history, practice, teaching, and learning to explore more possibilities in
meaning making and world creation.

LYNDSAY POMERANTZ:
You cant stop the train youre riding when you come across a scene that will make a
great picture
Exhibition Dates:
On view until May 4, 2021
Bob Prittie Library Metrotown, 6100 Willingdon Avenue

You cant stop the train youre riding when you come across a scene that will make a
great picture is a new series of images by Lyndsay Pomerantz. This work considers
family histories and how they may inform our own identities.

She was not yet born when these photographs were taken by her maternal
grandfather, nor has she been to the places where he took them. Through a process of
deconstructing and assembling, Pomerantz works through a part of her ancestors
past, creating a narrative and
relationship of her own to the photographic sources used
in her collages.

Her process is slow and intuitive, and the outcome is unclear" the antithesis to how
we gain information in our current time.

Lyndsay Pomerantz, she/her (b. 1987 Hamilton, Ontario) currently lives on the
unceded, ancestral territories of the Coast Salish Peoples including the territories of
the xmkwy" m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), and
Sllw"ta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations. She is an artist, curator and writer.

TALK

Conversation
around the book "metttasphere. Narrating identity across borders and media"
with author, MICHAEL FACIUS and artist and educator, CHRISTINE D'ONOFRIO

Date: Sunday, March 8, 2 PM

Wil Aballe
1375 Railspur Alley
Vancouver (Granville Island)

In this book talk, Christine DOnofrio (visual artist and Associate Professor at UBC) will be in
conversation with Michael Facius (Associate Professor at Tokyo College, The University of
Tokyo) about his new book metttasphere. Narrating identities across borders and media,
which he co-edited with curator aliwen and Hannah Janz, former head of digital information
at Goethe Institut Tokyo. The mettta project began with the question Who are we on
social media? and has evolved to examine issues of identity surrounding media, culture,
and gender. The metttafestival, held on October 1-2, 2022 as a collaboration between
Goethe Institute Tokyo and Tokyo College, featured art exhibitions, performances,
workshops, talks, and a philosophy caf, creating a space for dialogue and reflection on
identities. The book compiles these efforts of many years as well as newly invited
contributions into three sections: Mediated identities, Terroir: Migrations/ translations,
and Queer & disabled post-identities. Published in late December 2025 by independent
Japanese art book publisher torch press and designed by Daisuke Kano, the book features
text printed in risograph and photographs and artworks in offset. Spanning 424 bilingual
pages with contributions by 21 writers, it explores keywords including social media,
transnationality, language, queerness, and accessibility.

Bios:

MICHAEL FACIUS is an associate professor at Tokyo College, an institute for advanced
studies at The University of Tokyo, where he is spearheading the research cluster The
Humanities, Society, and the Future of Academic Knowledge. Trained in Linguistics,
Japanese Studies and Global History, he works at the theory-practice interface researching
the past, present and future of knowledge systems. Previous stops include Freie Universitt
Berlin, where he completed a PhD in Global History and Japanese Studies, and University
College London.

CHRISTINE D'ONOFRIO is a visual artist based in Vancouver, British Columbia. She
attended York University in Toronto for her BFA, and completed her MFA at the University of
British Columbia. DOnofrio has held positions at Emily Carr University of Art and Design,
University of Toledo, and the University of Windsor. She has exhibited her work extensively
across Canada. DOnofrio has also given artist talks and served on panels in various
institutions, including the Vancouver Art Gallery, and the prestigious Art Now lectures at
the University of Lethbridge. DOnofrio works in photography, video, digital media,
interactive media, printmaking, sculpture, book works, and installation.

Tameka Norris

Ivy League Ratchet
Wednesday, January 17, 2018
5:30 pm
Room 104, Frederic Lasserre Building
6333 Memorial Road, UBC

The UBC Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory is pleased to present an artist 
talk by Tameka Norris. Norris began her career in the Los Angeles hip-hop scene before 
migrating to the fine arts. She supplemented along the way with a range of odd jobs, 
from call-centre customer service representative to sex worker. Her artwork is informed 
by her experience of how exploitation is built into these systems, particularly for women 
of colour and queer communities. She often combines intensely personal experience 
with overtly performative personas to critique the ways that identities are appropriated 
and exploited by high and low culture alike. Norris is currently Assistant Professor in the 
Department of Art and Art History at the University of Iowa. Norriss visit is presented by 
the Rennie Collection Distinguished Visiting Artist Program.

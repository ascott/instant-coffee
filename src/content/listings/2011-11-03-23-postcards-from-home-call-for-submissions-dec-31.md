Call for Submissions- Postcards from Home Deadline January 10th/ 2012

How does your home affect who you are? Is your home where you were 
born, where you live now, or somewhere in between?A home is not only 
a physical location but also a construct of the mind. How can you 
exchange a piece of your home with another? This exhibition asks you to 
consider how your home affects your identity, and how you can capture 
the nature of home in a postcard.

For more information please go to 
http://postcardsfromhomeexchange.wordpress.com/ or contact Stacey 
Cann at home.postcard.exchange@gmail.com

JAMES OMARA - ENCOUNTERS
Elan Fine Art

4-258 East 1st Ave (Second Floor), Vancouver
Exhibition Dates: June 15th " July 13th, 2019
Reception: Saturday, June 15th, 1 " 5 PM

Elan Fine Art is pleased to present an exhibition of works by the esteemed international 
photographer James OMara. OMara developed a career in the documentary, editorial, 
and commercial fields, with a diverse spectrum of high-profile clients, frequently featured 
in Rolling Stone, Vogue, the New York Times, GQ, Vanity Fair, and Mojo. Encounters, 
titled after his recently published book, features black and white photography that spans 
his career over the past 50 years.

The camera, which estranges the photographer, the photographed, and the audience is 
absent, it is rather an extension of James OMara and his tender presence. His works 
are robust, honest, unscripted shots of the people he meets, individuals with passion, 
love, doubt, sadness. These are encounters, gentle transparent portrayals, no stolen 
shots and no staged moments with desire to impress.

It is for his initial lack of photographic training, that he observed every moment in life not 
as a photographer, but as an artist. It is the play on light and composition, the essence 
of his subjects, the colour, texture, movement of elements within a frame that James 
OMara truly captures. I try to bring things far away close to me, as James OMara 
writes, he paints portraits of fragility, arousal, and contemplation, this is his way of 
painting, his language.

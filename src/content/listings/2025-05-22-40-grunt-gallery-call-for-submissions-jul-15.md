grunt gallerys Annual Call for Submissions is open until July 15th!

Visit submissions.grunt.ca for details and submission options.

Questions? Visit our FAQ at submissions.grunt.ca/faq or email
submissions@grunt.ca

New this year: We are prioritizing submissions from local artists and curators based
in the Metro Vancouver area and Indigenous reserves/territories within or connected
to this area. We are particularly interested in proposals exploring the communities,
lands, relationships and/or other specific contexts of this region.

grunt gallerys programming includes visual and media art exhibitions, installations,
performances, events and projects, which are often accompanied by print or digital
publications. A curatorial committee of 5 to 9 people consisting of curators,
programming staff, board members and artists meets for 5 days every August to
review submissions. You can expect to hear back from us in the fall. We are typically
selecting projects to be presented 1 to 2 years in the future (for example, in August
2025 we are selecting projects to be presented mid-2026 through mid-2027).

We encourage applicants to review the past few years of programming by visiting
grunt.ca or coming by the gallery to learn about how we have prioritized accessibility
for artists, arts workers and audiences. Artists are invited to name any priorities they
may have in developing an accessible practice. We do not require artists to be
familiar with accessible practices, but we hope that you come with an interest to
learn, explore, and develop with us when you consider showing at grunt. Visit
grunt.ca/accessibility-statement to read our accessibility statement.

To learn more about grunts programming, visit grunt.ca or our online archive at
archives.grunt.ca

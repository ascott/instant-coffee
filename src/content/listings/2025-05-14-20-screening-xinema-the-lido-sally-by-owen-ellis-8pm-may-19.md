Sally
Monday, May 19 (Victoria Day)
Doors 7pm, Screening 8pm

The Lido (518 E Broadway)

On Monday, May 19, XINEMA will be co-presenting a premiere screening of Owen
Ellis new film Sally, presented in collaboration with Pig Freeze Studios! Join us at
our favourite video music bar, The Lido for a night of love, death and karaoke. The
film will prelude with poems with Doug (Wideen), music by Snoozer (Justin
Patterson).

Tickets are $10 PWYC at the door. No presales available.

Screening:

Sally (Canada, 2025)

Owen Ellis

HD video

28 min

Sally is a short film that follows Mark, a man in an 8 year relationship with his partner
Sally which is on its last legs. Mark wakes up one morning realizing that Sally has
died from boredom, Mark does everything in his power to make the relationship
work, even bringing her back to life.

Content warning: Indications of death. Some scenes may be disturbing for some
viewers.

XINEMA is an ongoing series that showcases emerging and established filmmakers
within and beyond "Vancouver", BC to strengthen and preserve the experimental
film scene. xinema.ca

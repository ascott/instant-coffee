SO CRAZY IT JUST MIGHT WORK is a SFU School for the Contemporary 
Arts student show. This year, the exhibition showcases work ranging from 
1st year students though to MFAs. Although the show centers around 
notions of utopia, the participating artists were encouraged to approach 
the theme peripherally from a critical and unconventional perspective, 
resulting in an exhibition that explores the idea of utopia, dystopia and 
whatever lies between.

At the AUDAIN Gallery
149 W Hastings St.

Opening: March 7th, 7PM - 10PM
March 8th - 24th

interplay_2021 call for live online performances

Since 2016, interplay_ presents artworks that promote, challenge and experiment with
collaborative and/or multidisciplinary performance.

This call is open to independent artists residing in so-called Canada, from all
disciplines, at any career stage, who are creating live online performances of 1-15
mins / $1200 fee.

Deadline Apr 15, 11:59pm. Notifications May 2.
apply: bit.ly/call_for_interplay_2021

interplay_2021 is Sep 23 & 24, 6"7:30pm PDT.
Shows are broadcast live via Webex from the artist's location.
Works can be between 1"15 minutes in length. If youre submitting for a durational
project, please describe how your proposal can fit into a livestream 'festival' context.

$1,200
fee per artist/group.

Co-curators Tasha Faye Evans, khattieQ, Sammy Chien and Deanna Peters/Mutable
Subject will program 4"5 artists/groups.

Please message @interplay_arts or email deanna@mutablesubject.ca with any
questions or input.

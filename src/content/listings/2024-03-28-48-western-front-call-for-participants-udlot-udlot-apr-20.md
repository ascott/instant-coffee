Western Front and the Roundhouse Community Arts & Recreation Centre invites
community members to learn and perform Jos Macedas composition "Udlot Udlot."
The call is aimed at anyone interested in music and does not require knowledge of
music theory. Those who register will have the option to choose between vocals and
percussion, and will receive instruments and instruction.

The process will involve two rehearsals across two consecutive Saturdays (Apr 27 &
May 4, 2024), culminating in a public performance of 100 " 150
participants at George
Wainborn Park, Vancouver.

Registration is open until April 20, 2024, or until all positions are filled.

For more information, visit our website: https://westernfront.ca/events/call-for-
participants-udlot-udlot

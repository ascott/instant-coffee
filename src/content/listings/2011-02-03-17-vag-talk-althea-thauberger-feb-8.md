TALKS at the Vancouver Art Gallery

Tuesday February 8th, 7pm

In the Gallery, 4th floor

Althea Thauberger

THE CULTURE SERIES:

The Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to present an artist talk by 
Althea Thauberger as part of the Culture Series.

Thauberger’s internationally produced and exhibited work typically 
concerns documenting group or community dynamics that result in 
events, performances, films, videos, audio recordings and books. 
Such interactions have involved groups in such varied contexts as 
adolescent girls in North Vancouver and Canadian women soldiers 
in Afghanistan. Thauberger has exhibited at the 17th Biennale of 
Sydney; National Gallery of Canada, Ottawa; The Andy Warhol 
Museum, Pittsburgh; Guangzhou Triennial; Manifesta 7, Trento; 
Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, Vancouver; Vancouver Art 
Gallery; BAK, Utrecht; Künstlerhaus Bethanien, Berlin; Kunstverein 
Wolfsburg; Art Gallery of Nova Scotia, Halifax; Singapore History 
Museum; Presentation House Gallery, Vancouver; Museum van 
Hedendaagse Kunst, Antwerp; Berkeley Art Museum; Insite, San 
Diego/Tijuana; White Columns, New York.

The Culture Series at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents artists, 
writers, critics, historians, performers and other cultural practitioners 
from all disciplines who are changing the way we think about art, 
creativity, culture, contemporary theory and more around the world.

Marking the launch of the series were talks by musician and artist 
Brian Eno and groundbreaking experimental American filmmaker 
Barbara Hammer in January 2011. Upcoming talks include Mark 
Allen of the Los Angeles-based arts group Machine Project, and 
Peter Samis, associate curator of interpretation at the San Francisco 
Museum of Modern Art (SFMOMA), a leader in developing interactive 
multimedia for museums.

Look, listen, talk, consider and debate together with these 
internationally renowned speakers.

Free with Gallery admission.

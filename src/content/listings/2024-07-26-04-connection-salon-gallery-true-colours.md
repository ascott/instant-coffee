Connection Salon Gallery at the Gathering Place Exhibitions Program
We are continuing our series of exhibitions on the theme of Recovery.
This one is about Identity: Regaining a positive sens of self and identity and overcoming
stigma.Finding, accepting and nurturing our True Colours.
Having received a grant from the Canada Council of the Arts,
we will be able to offer an
honorarium of $160 to each exhibiting artist.
There will be a maximum of 12 artists each showing 4 pieces.
Artists are invited to submit 4 two-dimensional (2D) art for the
Group show (August 30th - October 13th) on the theme: True Colours.
Deadline for submission is August 9th.
Show will be held at The Gathering Place " 609 Helmcken St., Vancouver
There will be a Meet the Artists event hosted September 11th 5:30 to 7 PM
There are no size limitations and each piece submitted
must be ready to hang.
Submission form is available on Connection Salon website Call for Artists
http://connectionsalon.ca/

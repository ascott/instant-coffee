Kunsthal Charlottenborg

Vacancy for Kunsthal Charlottenborg Director
http://kunsthalcharlottenborg.dk

We are seeking an ambitious, visionary Director with the will and courage
to strengthen Kunsthal Charlottenborg's position in the international art
world, and, in cooperation with our proactive Governing Board, to develop
and implement a powerful artistic vision.

The Director will be allowed maximum artistic and personal freedom to
ensure Kunsthal Charlottenborg's reach as an agenda-setting organisation,
recognised nationally and internationally for its exhibition programmes,
international cooperation, innovative interdisciplinary initiatives and
specialist publications.

The successful candidate will have: excellent interpersonal skills; an
international perspective and network; a solid focus on modern and
contemporary art; the ability, aspiration and courage to discourse with all
stakeholders in the art world; experience in innovative communication and
media praxis; a talent for organisation; personal drive; the will to
succeed; an acute understanding of the correlation between finances and
politics; and, naturally, attach importance to close dialogue with Kunsthal
Charlottenborg's Governing Board.

The Director will have thorough knowledge and proven experience of
financial control, financing and fund raising.

The Director is appointed by Kunsthal Charlottenborg's Governing Board, to
which he or she will report directly. This appointment is fixed term.

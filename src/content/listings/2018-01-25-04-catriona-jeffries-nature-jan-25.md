Nature

Rebecca Brewer
Rochelle Goldberg
Charmian Johnson
Christina Mackie

The opening reception is
Thursday, January 25, 7 - 9pm
and the exhibition is
January 26 - March 3, 2018

Nature has its problems. Humans in particular treat nature as a cultural subject leading 
to the continual production of problematic clichs that ebb, flow and repeat through 
history. In times of political and cultural instability, the ideal of nature has become a 
conceptual and physical refuge from collective challenges, often with mixed results. 
While historically variable in its definition, nature has generally been understood in 
western culture as the material aspects of existence"but not the products of human 
activity. This distinction blurs quickly as the depth and breadth of human impact, 
interaction and interference into the natural world accelerates. It is this distinction that 
the artists in this exhibition contest, consider and trouble.

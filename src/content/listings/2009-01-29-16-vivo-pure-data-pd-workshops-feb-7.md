PURE DATA (PD) WORKSHOPS AT VIVO

Instructor: Brady Marks
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337

PD BASICS
1 session x 2 hours: $50 or $40 with Producer Membership
Saturday February 7, 4-6 pm

Lays the basis for working with digital media in an interactive way. We
start with an overview of this field. Pure-Data (PD) is introduced as a
visual programming language used for the creation of interactive
installation and live performative audio-visual art forms. Please bring
your own laptop if possible.

PD SONIC
2 sessions x 3 hours: $120 or $100 with Producer Membership
Saturdays February 14 & 21, 4-7 pm

Following on from PD BASICS, we will use Pure Data to manipulate live and
recorded sound. Together we will explore generative compostion and
techniques for novel sound synthesis. Please bring your own laptop if
possible.

PD VISUAL
2 sessions x 3 hours: $120 or $100 with Producer Membership
Sundays February 15 & 22, 4-7 pm

Following on from PD BASICS, we will learn how to generate and manipulate
images in real time. We can show you how to play with your images in a
dynamic 3D environment. Please bring your own laptop if possible.

PD MEDIA INTEGRATION & INTERFACING
2 sessions x 4 hours: $120 or $100 with Producer Membership
Saturday & Sunday February 28 & March 1, 4-7 pm

Following on from PD BASICS, in this workshop we will learn how to link
our visual and sonic media to the physical world using sensors,
microcontrollers and simple electronic circuits. Please bring your own
laptop if possible.

PD FOLLOW-UP
Custom Workshop, one on one: $40 per hour
Please call 604.872.8337 or email education@vivomediaarts.com to schedule

A participant-driven workshop which provides follow-up support. This
workshop is for people who have previously taken other PD workshops or
want help with digitial media projects.

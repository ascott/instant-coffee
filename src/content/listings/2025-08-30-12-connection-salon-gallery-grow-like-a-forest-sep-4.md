Opening: Grow like a Forest- An Alternative Creations Studio Artists Exhibition. An art
show about access, diversity and co-creating communities where everyone can
flourish. Meet the artists event September 4th

Place: Connection Salon Gallery, 609 Helmcken

Connection Salon Artists Collectives mission is to provide low barrier opportunities to
artists that are underrepresented and have lived experience with mental health. This is
a collaboration with Alternative Creations Studio.

12 visual artists will be on show August 29 to October 5. Join us for the Meet the Artists
event September 5, 2025, from 5:30 to 7:00 PM with DJ Ross, Colin and Soulabilities.
The exhibition is supported by the BC Arts Council, the Connection Salon Collective
and the Gathering Place Community Center.

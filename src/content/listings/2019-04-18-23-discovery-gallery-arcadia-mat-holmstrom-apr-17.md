Arcadia
Mat Holmstrom

Date: April 17 - May 26, 2019

Time: 9:00AM - 6:00PM Daily

Place: VanDusen Botanical Garden Discovery Gallery
5251 Oak Street, Vancouver, BC

Arcadia is a glimpse into the ever-expanding creative world of Vancouver based artist Mat 
Holmstrom. 

The theme of Arcadia refers to a vision of bountiful natural splendor and harmony. This 
unique collection presents works on canvas, paper, panel, pottery, and sculpture. A rich 
color palette and the integration of metallic paint stimulates a unique viewing experience in a 
variety of lighting situations. 

Holmstrom was born in Banff and grew up in Beaumont, Alberta. He studied at the Alberta 
College of Art & Design and MacEwan University. His work is in private collections across 
Canada.

For more information visit: https://www.mat-theartist.com or

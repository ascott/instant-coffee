Fillip is pleased to announce the availability of its 10th issue!

Launched last month in Berlin and Vancouver, Fillip #10 is a special limited edition featuring 
written correspondences by contributors from past issues of the magazine on key concerns facing 
the contemporary arts including boredom, participation, democracy, the economy, work, and 
more.

Bound as a collection of letters and emails printed on A4 letterhead, the issue features writing by 
Maria Fusco, Jayce Salloum, Eric Fredericksen, Fionn Meade, and Larry Rinder, amongst others. 
The issue also includes new work and conversations with Dexter Sinister, Christian Hillesø, and 
Johan Tirén, as well as a special collaboration with Dazibao, centre de photographies actuelles, 
Montréal, on the occasion of the exhibition Working Images, curated by Condé and Beveridge 
this past spring.

124 pages, 2 colour
21 × 29.7cm
Edition of 1000

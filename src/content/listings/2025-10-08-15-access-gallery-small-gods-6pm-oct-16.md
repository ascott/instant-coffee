Small Gods
Forensic Architecture, Steven Cottingham, Serena Lee, Weaver, and Kevin Sudeith
Curated by Kitt Peacock
16 October " 14 December 2025
Opening reception 16 October 2025, 6 PM
Small Gods considers the drone"as violent machine, mythic being, eye of god,
ancestral technology, and resident of our speculative futures.

I. Veiled by the thickness of the air, the drone appears from the ground to be a
smudge, a void, or nothing at all. This is a story about seeing as violence.

II. Before the drone was a drone, it was a sound. From medieval mourning music to the
quiet slide of the guqin, the meditative hum of the drone asks the listener to separate
mind and body, surrendering their senses to the direction of a divine operator.

III. In the counter-gaze, the drone leaves its mark on the land. Vernacular textiles
capture their passage overhead; simulations fracture and multiply their sightlines. The
aerial gaze is frozen, torn apart, reconstituted as a tool of resistance.

Small Gods features works by Forensic Architecture, Steven Cottingham, Serena Lee,
Weaver, and Kevin Sudeith.

Decoder 2017
February 4, 2016, 8pm
The Fox Cabaret, 2321 Main St., V5T 3C9
pushfestival.ca

Presented by Club PuSh with Theatre Conspiracy. In an alternate
visual reality, characters in a fictional world are real-time systems
operators, infiltrating each other's bodies in the psychic battle of man
and machine.

Decoder (1984) is a German cyberpunk counter-culture film, roughly
based on the writings of William S. Burroughs, about the people's
revolution against the government, particularly an evil Muzak
corporation, using the power of industrial music. Written by Muscha,
Klaus Maeck, Volker Schfer, and Trini Trimpop, and largely illuminated
in lurid reds and TV-tube blues,Decoder stars Einstrzende Neubauten's
then-percussionist F.M. Einheit as a sonic experimenter who discovers
that playing back recordings of disturbances in public spaces can
create actual disturbances among the public, a concept taken directly
from Burroughs' Electronic Revolution.Decoder features actor, artist
and scholar Bill Rice; musician and artistGenesis P-Orridge, and
famous heroin addict and musician Christiane F, with cameos by
Burroughs, plus music by Soft Cell, Psychic TV, Blue Rondo a la Turk,
Einstrzende Neubauten, and The The.

Theater artist Mallory Catlett's Decoder 2017 uses the prophetic writing
of William Burroughs as the basis for a performance installation. The
work sets out to test his theory that "when you cut up the present the
future leaks in." Catlett's title makes reference to the 1984 West
German film Decoderspecifically Burroughs' instructions on how to use
tape recorders to spark social uprising, found in the Electronic
Revolution. Performer Jim Findlayand noise artist and analog tape
deejay G Lucas Crane use live sound and text cut-ups to generate a
simultaneous visual reality. Acting both as characters within a fictional
world and real-life systems operators, they infiltrate each other's bodies
in the psychic battle of man and machine.

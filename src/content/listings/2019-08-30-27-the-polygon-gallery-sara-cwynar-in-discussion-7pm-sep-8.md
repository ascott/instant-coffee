On Friday September 6, join us for an artist talk and discussion of the exhibition Sara 
Cwynar: Gilded Age II with three esteemed presenters. Brooklyn-based artist Sara 
Cwynar will introduce her work, followed by commentary by exhibition curator Jenn 
Jackson and National Gallery of Canada Associate Curator Jonathan Shaughnessy. 
Following these remarks, the artist and curators will engage in conversation about the 
timely themes and context of Gilded Age II, and what these images tell us about the 
unique cultural moment in which we find ourselves today.

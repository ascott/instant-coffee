FREE Event!

Doors 4:00 pm
Performance 6:00 pm

Aeriosa Dance Society and HCMA Architecture + Design present this arts talk and 
community dialogue tilted "Where Performance Meets Architecture: Shifting perspectives on 
Vancouver's vertical stages followed by a live vertical dance performance.

This FREE community event will feature presentations by vertical dance choreographers 
from around the world who are in town for the first ever Vancouver International Vertical 
Dance Summit. To follow will be a conversation moderated by Georgia Straight dance 
journalist Janet Smith. Attendees will leave with a deeper understanding of vertical dance 
and its potential to transform urban public/social spaces in ways that better serve 
communities.

After the talk and Q&A, the audience wil be invited to watch a live vertical dance 
performance on the Guinness Building!

Snacks and refreshments will be provided. There is limited seating, so if tickets are sold out 
please add your name to the waitlist and we will release additional tickets near the event 
date.

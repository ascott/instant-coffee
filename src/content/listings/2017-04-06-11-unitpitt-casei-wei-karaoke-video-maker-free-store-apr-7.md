April 7 to May 5, 2017
Public Hours: Every Friday & Saturday noon to 6pm
Closing Karaoke Party Saturday May 5, 7pm

On every every Friday & Saturday in April, UNIT/PITT Projects will be transformed into a 
video store where you can make your own fabulous music video of a karaoke song (or 
your own material) in front of a green screen.
ANYTHING IS POSSIBLE!

To book your free hour long session, contact Casey at:
freekaraokemusicvideo@gmail.com

Karaoke Video Maker Free Store is part of a month-long production residency at 
UNIT/PITT, which will include the production of new videos by Late Spring, Strawberry, 
JSN, and Aileen Bryant.

Casey Wei is an interdisciplinary artist, filmmaker, and musician based in Vancouver. 
She graduated with an MFA from SFU in 2012. Her practice has evolved from 
filmmaking (Murky Colors in 2012, Vater und Sohn / Father and Son / in 2013), 
into works that cross over between art, music, and the community at large (Kingsgate 
Mall Happenings in 2014, Chinatown Happenings in 2015, and the art rock? series that 
began in 2015). In 2016, she began Agony Klub, a music and printed matter label that 
releases material under the framework of the popular esoteric. She also plays in the 
musical projects Late Spring and hazy.

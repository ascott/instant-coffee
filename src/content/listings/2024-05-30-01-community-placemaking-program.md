You are invited to apply for
up to $3,000 to deliver a small activation, temporary feature or beautification project
to
bring people together in a Vancouver public space. We are looking to support
projects across the city and are keen to receive ideas that animate or reimagine a
public space and build community. Your project could be something youve done
before,
something you are currently working on, or an entirely new idea.

Your proposal should:

Encourage social connections & strengthen community

Be free, accessible, inclusive, safe and welcoming to all

Be interesting, fun, and engaging

Be easy to set-up and move

Be deliverable within the budget and timeframe

Be creative suggesting new ways to use or reimagine a space

Examples of past suitable event-based placemaking projects

Community
gatherings and dialogues on reimagining a space

o
Chinatown Mahjong Social,
Backyard BBQ,
Trout Lake Triangle Block Party,
Park(ing) Day,
Bridge Warming,
Rain City Block Party

Art, music, and dance events

o
Public Disco,
Community Canvas Jam,
Summer Music Vancouver,
Keys to the Streets,
Open Season,
Street Beats

Workshops and activities

o
Skate Jams,
Thingery Sustainability Workshops,
Dance in Transit,
Public Yoga,
Play Vancouver

Further details:

Successful applicants will receive up to $3,000 for their projects.

Projects will go live between July and October 2024, and will activate one or more
public spaces on at least one day.

Proposed activations can be a series (e.g., multiple days in a row or on a certain day
each week)

Project can be a small activation, a temporary installation or small beautification
project

All logistics (set up, getting relevant permits, take down, moving, storage, insurance
and cleanup) will be the responsibility of the applicant.

IBPOC, 2SLGBTQ+
and other under-represented residents, communities and
organisations are encouraged
to apply and are eligible for an additional funding up to $2000.

For more details on the program and how to apply, visit
vancouver.ca/community-placemaking.
Applications are due by Sunday June 23, 2024. We encourage you to apply even if
you dont have all the details figured out before the deadline. If approved, staff will
work with you to finalize it.

We look forward to reviewing your proposal!Please feel free to contact us if you
have any questions.

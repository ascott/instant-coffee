In this new series of sculptures by Heidi Johansen and paintings by
Charlie Roberts, the artists explore themes of darkness, Norwegian winter
and weed-smoke aesthetic. The work is accompanied by psychedelic dark rap
music created by both artists as well as other collaborators in Norway.

Charlie Roberts has had solo shows at Kravets/Wehby Gallery, New York,
Rice Gallery, Texas and Richard Heller, Los Angeles. He has shown his work
in the Netherlands and Sweden and was part of the group show Paint at the
Vancouver Art Gallery. He is currently living in Norway.

Heidi Johansen has had solo shows at UKS, Oslo, Richard Heller Gallery,
Los Angeles and Blanket Gallery, Vancouver. She has been in group shows at
the Or Gallery, Vancouver , Galleri Niklas Belenius, Stockholm and Irvine
in NYC, New York.

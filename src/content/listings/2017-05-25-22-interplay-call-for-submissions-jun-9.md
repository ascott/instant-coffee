#Interplay 2017 call: [http://bit.ly/Interplay2017Call](http://bit.ly/Interplay2017Call)

Interplay focusses on producing works that promote, challenge and experiment with 
collaborative and/or multidisciplinary creation and/or performance: 
[http://bit.ly/Interplay2017Call](http://bit.ly/Interplay2017Call)

also eligible. Durational projects will be considered.

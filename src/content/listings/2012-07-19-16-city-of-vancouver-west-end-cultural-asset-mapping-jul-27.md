Of special interest to West End residents...

Cultural Asset Mapping
Friday, July 27, 9 am - noon
Barclay Room, West End Community Centre (870 Denman Street)

Be a part of the West End Community Planning process. Join staff from Cultural
Services and the West End Planning Team for a West End cultural asset
mapping session.

Participants will be asked to identify and map cultural assets in the West End and
explain their significance. For this session we want to take a wider and inclusive
definition of culture that would include businesses, organizations and
programming that goes beyond the dominant definition of culture.

The results from this session will help identify key cultural assets in the
community as well as ideas, opportunities and values related to these assets. In
turn, this will help shape the West End community planning process over the
next 15 to 18 months.

Participants can drop in at any time between 9 am and noon.

For more information, call 604.871.6007

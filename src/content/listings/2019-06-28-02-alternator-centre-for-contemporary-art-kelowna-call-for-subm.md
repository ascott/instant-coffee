The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is a non-for profit Artist run centre in 
Kelowna BC. We are currently accepting submissions for our Main Gallery space for our 
programming in 2021/2022. Please visit www.alternatorcentre.com and follow the "call 
for submissions" link for more information and to access our online submission form. 

Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art
#103-421 Cawston Ave Kelowna, BC (inside the Rotary Centre for the Arts)

Since 1989 the Alternator has shown the work of emerging Canadian and international 
artists with an experimental approach, particularly those engaged in social and cultural 
issues. We foster energetic, creative, critical discussion on art & culture. We believe in 
the greater positive potential of our creative community and value our role as key 
contributors to the cultural life of Kelowna and beyond.

We envision our artist run-centre as a welcoming site for radical art-making and 
community-building. We have two primary goals:
First, we seek to provide support and resources to emerging and alternative artists 
in the Okanagan region;
Second, we seek to inspire our members as well as the broader community by 
showcasing local, national, and international work that is engaged in its social context 
and that disturbs and transcends dominant constructions of art, identity, and value.

We are committed to fostering artists professional autonomy and are proud to pay 
successful applicants CAR/FAC fees. The Alternator does have limited funds available 
to assist with exhibition production expenses. If you require assistance with production 
expenses, please include a detailed description and budget of production expenses as 
part of your programming submission as well as expected installation schedule/timeline.

Please visit www.alternatorcentre.com and follow the "call for submissions" link for our 
artist mandate, floor plan, and access to our online submission form.

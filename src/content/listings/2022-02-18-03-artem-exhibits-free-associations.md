ARTEM EXHIBITS is pleased to announce the opening of their latest Art Exhibit. The
Main Gallery shows works by Susan Loudon over the period of 1974-2014. Titled Free
AssociatIons, these varied works spark food for thought on topics such as Dreams,
Utopias, Truths, Realities, Couples, Landscapes, Revelations, Poetry and Adoption.
Cloth, paint, wood, glass, wire, and nail polish ! are some of the mediums used.

Doug Rowed has his display in the White Rabbit Room. Titled In
other Woods, these
wooden creations are organic, other-worldly and ordered. They are simply sublime, with
visceral authenticity.

The gallery continues to welcome guests on Saturday afternoons, 1 pm -4:30 pm.

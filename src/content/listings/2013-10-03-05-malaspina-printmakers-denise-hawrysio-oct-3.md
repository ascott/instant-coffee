Malaspina Printmakers 

The Beaver Still Needs a Log: Denise Hawrysio 

OCT 3

At a time when art can no longer be defined through a single dominant 
movement or discipline, “The Beaver Still Needs a Log” expresses an 
attitude of experimentation and humour, a lightness of touch 
increasingly visible in contemporary art making. It embraces an 
economy of means and encompasses an inventive approach to a 
range of concerns in both form and content, yet the playfulness of the 
works does not compromise their seriousness and impact, which are 
balanced by criticality and, at times, subversiveness. Opens Thursday 
October 3 from 6 to 9pm.

Denise Hawrysio was born in Toronto, received her BFA from Queen’s 
University, Kingston, and her MFA from the San Francisco Art Institute. 
She works in a variety of media including site-specific installation, 
bookworks and printmedia. She has received fellowships for 
residencies at the Banff Centre for the Arts, the prestigious MacDowell 
Colony in the United States, University of British Columbia and 
University of Alberta. Her bookworks are in collections at the Victoria 
and Albert Museum in London, the Chelsea Art Library, Yale 
University, the Bruce Peel Collection in Edmonton and the National 
Gallery of Canada. She has taught at the University of British 
Columbia, the Architectural Association of Great Britain, the University 
of Southampton and is currently teaching at The University of the Arts 
London. She has exhibited at Kunstlerhaus Bethanien (Berlin), Uppsala 
Konstmuseum (Sweden), The Showroom (London), Mercer Union 
(Toronto), Dare Dare Gallery (Montréal), Overgaden Institute of 
Contemporary Art (Copenhagen), Simon Fraser University Gallery 
(Vancouver) and Dundee Contemporary Art, (Scotland).

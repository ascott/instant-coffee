ART FOR GAZA

An auction fundraiser for the Sameer Project and Grassroots Gaza

An online and in-person art auction that supports the efforts of two Palestinian-led
non-profits working towards humanitarian aid and rebuilding in Gaza. With an
incredible array of works from both emerging and established artists located on the
west coast of Canada/Turtle Island, we work to collectively bring relief to those who
are living in very difficult circumstances.

Open for Online Bidding: April 1-26, 2025 www.32auctions.com/artforgza

In-Person Bidding and Auction Closing: Saturday April 26, 2-6 pm

Kasko Gallery, 560 Clark Dr, Vancouver, BC

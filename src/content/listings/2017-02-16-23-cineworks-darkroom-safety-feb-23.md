This class is designed to introduce participants to the roles of chemicals in 
photographic work: their risks, their benefits and the procedures required to use 
them safely. It will provide a useful refresher for occasional darkroom users as 
well as a clear introduction for beginners.

To the novice, many of the precautions on photographic chemicals can sound 
like exaggeration designed to protect the manufacturer rather than inform the 
photographer, but the dangers are serious. Chemicals commonly encountered in 
the darkroom range from the harmless to violent poisons and it is important for 
the darkroom enthusiast to understand the hazards faced in the pursuit of art.

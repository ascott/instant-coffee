Untitled & Friends

Friday, July 6, 2018
8pm till late

Remington Gallery - 108 E Hastings (store front/gallery + back studio/lounge area)

Featured Artists:

Tierney Milne (Van) - @tierneymilne
Chris Dammeyer (Van) - @cdammr 
Mikaela Kautzky (Van/MTL) - @mik00k
Sam Fidlin (Van) - @samfidlinphoto
Thomas Trnka (MTL) - @thomastrnka
Tori Swanson (Van) - @torisswanson

Curated by: Lauren D Zbarsky (@ldzbar) + Frank Lemoy (@franklemoy)

Untitled & Friends is based around ideas of collectivism, togetherness and friends. The 
theme is open-ended to encourage artists to interpret in whatever way they choose, 
focusing on their own personal style and ideas they'd like to explore. 

The show will be a one night event. Artwork will be displayed in the front gallery space and 
there will be a backroom/lounge area with music, drinks and space to chat and chill.

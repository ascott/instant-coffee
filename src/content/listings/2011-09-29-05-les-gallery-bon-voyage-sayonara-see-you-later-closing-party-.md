Bon Voyage! Sayonara! See You Later!
The LES Gallery calls it a day

Closing Reception: Friday September 30th, 7 - 11pm
LES Gallery, 1879 Powell St.

After 7 years of operations, the LES Gallery is closing its doors on Oct 1st.
To celebrate the years the LES has put into the arts community, we're hosting a 
one night, express your love for the LES event, where you can take away 
ephemeral objects which you'll always be able to remember the LES Gallery by. 
There will also be confetti and drinks.

www.lesgallery.ca

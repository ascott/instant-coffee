GRAND OPENING - HOTAM PRESS @ MOUNT PLEASANT
Saturday, June 15, 2019
2 - 5 PM

In May 2019, Hotam Press has moved into a storefront space in the Mount Pleasant 
neighbourhood of Vancouver. The new location functions as both a production studio and a 
bookshop/gallery. 

While continuing to publish work by artists, Hotam Pressnow also offer public hours from 
Thursday to Saturday. Opening our doors, we would like to generate more interests in artist 
publications. 

The bookshop/gallery will exhibit book related artwork by artists of diverse backgrounds. Our 
inaugural exhibition The Greatest Stories Ever Toldis a project on art and translation. The 
original English book, with its 13 other language versions, will be on display along with 
artwork from the stories.

Besides showcasing our publications, Hotam Pressalso welcomes the public to our resource 
library, which houses a collection of books on topics including contemporary art, DIY book 
design, independent publishing, etc.

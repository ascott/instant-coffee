Jairo Salazar: Chance Encounters, Automatic Drawings, and Shuffle Thoughts: A Short
History of the Exquisite Corpse in Art.

Where does creativity come from? 20th century artists Jean Arp, Hannah Hoch, and
Max Ernst -to name a few- found answers to that question on the unconscious and the
fortuitous. They defied convention and praised these principles for revealing and
unlocking the true and conflicting realities of the everyday. With that in mind, it seems as
though similar principles applied by the Dadaists have gained a renewed interest in our
contemporary times. From the current overflow of visual information in our social media
news feeds, to the emergence of graphic filters in platforms such as Instagram or Tik-
Tok, this quirky and interactive webinar will explore the origins of the Exquisite Corpse
as an strikingly relevant and liberating art technique that marked an artistic shift in the
dawn of the
20th century.

This will be a 60 minute Zoom event (talk 30 minutes with 30 minute
participation/discussion).
September 18, 6:00 - 7:00 pm, Pay what you can.

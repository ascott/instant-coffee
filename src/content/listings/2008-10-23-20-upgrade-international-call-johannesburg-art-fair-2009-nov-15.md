Call for Submissions: Upgrade International at Johannesburg Art Fair 2009

Upgrade Vancouver, participating with Upgrade International, will show a
Special Project pavillion at the Johannesburg Art Fair in Johannesburg,
South Africa in April 2009.

The pavillion will feature a selection of work made for online platforms,
with a specific curatorial theme: The Global South.

The Global South
The Global South describes what the West is not. The "West" is understood
to define the powerful "Modern" commerce-driven continents - Europe and
North America. If you were to identify places that are part of the Global
South, you would include South America, Africa, China, India, Korea and
other parts of South East Asia.

"With Northern economies beginning to face the realities of increasingly
stagnant growth potential, emerging markets have been earmarked as havens
for the continuation of economic growth. Indeed, economic growth in India,
Brazil and South Africa has in recent years eclipsed that of developed
economies. To define these markets, the term "Global South" has emerged,
loosely designating economies in Central and Latin America, Africa and Asia
with high growth-potential and shared social histories.

Artlogic sees the emergence of the Global South as an opportunity to forge
trans-national networks through reciprocal partnerships. Through the
identification, definition and fortification of these partnerships, regions
within the Global South can benefit from increased cultural visibility and
communication of ideas."

-Art Logic, 2008

The Johannesburg Art Fair (http://www.joburgartfair.co.za) is an annual art
fair operated by Art Logic (http://www.artlogic.co.za )
The First Joburg Art Fair took place in the heart of the Sandton Central
Management District (SCMD) in April 2008. 22 Major galleries sold work in a
traditional art fair set up covering 5000 square meters of the Sandton
Convention Center. Special Projects for 2008: FUNDA Community College,
Tonic design and artist Robin Rhode. Extending the representation of
African contemporary artists at the art fair is As You Like It, a curated
show by Simon Njami. A Talks program focused on collecting African
contemporary art and the place of the art fair in the city. The Art Fair
and the Talks form part of Artlogic's Joburg Art Week.

Upgrade Vancouver launched in 2003 with a successful program of discussions
and presentations at the intersection of art and technology, happening
monthly and supported by social gatherings. The series is part of Upgrade!
International (http://theupgrade.net ), and collaborates to produce
publications, exhibitions and processes of exchange between 30 cities.

The pavillion is a project of Upgrade Johannesburg in partnership with
Upgrade International.

To apply, please send a URL, a bio, and a short description of the work to
Kate Armstrong: kate@katearmstrong.com by November 15, 2008.

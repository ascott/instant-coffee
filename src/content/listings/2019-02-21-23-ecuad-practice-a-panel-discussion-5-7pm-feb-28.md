Please join us for this edition of Practice, featuring presentations and group discussions on the 
practices of guest artists Alanna Edwards (interdisciplinary art), Connie Sabo (curation and 
visual art), Lexi Soukoreff (visual art and clothing design), Terra Poirier (photography, 
publishing, community activism). 

In this session, panelists from a wide variety of artistic disciplines share how they first emerged 
into their field. 

The panel presentation will be followed by a moderated Q + A and breakout sessions. Free 
event. Everyone welcome.

Presented by Emily Carr University of Art + Designs Audain Faculty of Art, the Faculty of 
Culture and Community, and the Career and Professional Development Office.

We are pleased to inform you that the Broadway Subway Project Public Art Open Call is
now live.

The Province is seeking to commission five (5) professional artists or artist teams to
create unique, highly integrated public artworks at each of the following stations: Great
Northern Way, Mount Pleasant, Broadway-City Hall, Fairview-VGH and South Granville
Station.

Following the
Request For Qualifications (RFQ), up to 15 shortlisted artists or artist
teams will be invited to participate in a Concept Proposal Presentation Stage. The final
selection will be made by the end of 2020.

Information on the public art call is available on the Broadway Subway Project website
https://engage.gov.bc.ca/broadwaysubway/procurement/. You can also find the
opportunity on BC Bid at http://www.bcbid.gov.bc.ca/open.dll/submitLogin?
language=En&disID=45347246. To download the document from BC Bid, click on
"Supplier Attachments Exist" in the top right-hand corner of the screen.

Interested parties are invited to apply via email to TICorpProposalContact@ticorp.ca or
via BC Bid Electronic Submission: www.bcbid.ca (Only pre-authorized electronic bidders
registered on the BC Bid system can submit an electronic proposal using the BC Bid
system.)

We encourage you to share this exciting opportunity with your networks.

Should you have any questions about this opportunity please
contact
TICorpProposalContact@ticorp.ca.

For more information on the Broadway Subway Project visit
http://engage.gov.bc.ca/broadwaysubway/.

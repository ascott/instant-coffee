Inter Tidal
September 25th, 2014

-

Opening reception at 8pm on Thursday September 25th.

@ INDEX
1305 Powell St.

-

Inter Tidal is an installation by Ryan Mathieson that exists within the
in-between space between mediums, spatial orientations, and experiences.
Furthermore, the work walks between the lines of the organic and the
super-structural - as well as the scientific and the metaphysical. As
viewers we are called to contemplate the actual in relation to methods of
display and formal functions. A quiet collection of objects, structures,
sounds, and smells, the installation acts as an immersive experience to be
used as a platform for corporeal consideration.

-

Ryan Mathieson is an based in Vancouver, whose practice uses photography as
a tool to consider an interdisciplinary practice. He received his Bachelors
of Design with a photography major from the Alberta College of Art + Design
in 2010, and his Masters of Fine Arts in interdisciplinary studies from
Simon Fraser University in 2014. Having attended residencies such as
'Dizziness of Correspondences' at the Banff Centre in Alberta (2013), and
the 'Summer Academy For Artist Researchers' in Helsinki, Finland (2011),
his practice is highly research based. Mathieson has exhibited throughout
Canada, including contributing to the Western Front 40th Annual Art Auction.

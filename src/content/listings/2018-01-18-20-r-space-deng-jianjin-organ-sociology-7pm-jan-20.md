At the beginning of 2018, R Space Art Development Foundation is honored to invite
Chinese famous contemporary artist Deng Jianjin, professor of Oil painting 
department from Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts, to hold his first solo exhibition in 
Vancouver, Canada. This exhibition will feature his latest series of oil painting, 
installation and sculpture specially made for this exhibition. All our friends and 
neighbors in Vancouver are welcome to attend the opening party on the evening of 
January 20. Local famous DJ, One Winged Angel (Matt Tecson) will give us a live 
performance.

From Subjective Eroticism to Organ Sociology

Although it may not be as purposeful today to explore about the erotic world of an 
artist, in the 1980s and 1990s of the twentieth century China, to address a subject 
matter as such was still fairly sensitive. China had just ended an era of sexual 
suppression, and with inhibited steps, it shyly pulled itself toward liberation and 
cautiously probed into the curious and palpitating world. During that particular point 
in time, Deng Jianjin was indeed an avant-garde artist who employed his keen and 
distinctive visual experiences in the attempt to transgress the perimeters of sexual 
restriction, which distinguishably characterized his work to emerge among the 
numerous contemporary artists of his generation.

At that time, seeing the work of Deng in an art museum was a psychological punch,
especially to the Chinese audience. Twenty years later, Deng is still working on the
same subject matter as he perpetuates his ceaseless personal reflections and
explorations. Deng's work could be categorized into the following stages: the self as 
the central representation, the surrounded collective idol, the uneventful city series, 
the ultimate state of suspension (Yang Xiaoyan, Live Between Sentiment and Sex), 
the dismemberment of carnal desire, and finally, the scenes of debaucherous orgy.

For his exhibition at R Space, Deng Jianjin reveals his most recent thoughts on the
subject matter. Although he seems to have returned to his dismemberment of carnal
desire stage, Deng explores the theme from an approach of internal organs and 
bodily functions. From the human body, Deng extends his subject further to animals 
and hybrid half-man half-beasts and presents a more comprehensive and composed 
analysis with references to zoology, animal instinct, mythology, and semiology. From 
his previously confined, passionate and subjective eroticism, Deng extends his work 
to a context of generalized sociological sexology, correspondingly, progresses from 
his visual experiences of real and false subjective consciousness (Deng Jianjin, The 
Conversion of Visual Experience in Virtuality) to his science-fantasies on organ and 
bodily functions. Not only are Dengs work beyond the mere portrayal of sex and the 
erotic, even more so, they are logical, dispassionate, anatomical reflections on the 
body of sexuality and desire.

*

Deng Jianjin is Chinese contemporary artist, focus on large scale oil painting and
sculpture. he was born in 1961 in Guangdong. In 1986, he graduated from the 
Sculpture Department of Jingdezhen Ceramic Institute in Jiangxi. Now he is the 
professor of the Fourth Studio of the Oil Painting Department of Guangzhou 
Academy of Fine Arts (GAFA).

ART BOOK WEEK
October 17-24, 2019
Locations throughout Metro Vancouver

Vancouver Art Book Fair's 2019 Art Book Week is a parallel series of events hosted in spaces
across Vancouver by a variety of artists, curators, collectives and institutions who are actively
creating and presenting work in this medium. When you're not at VABF, consider checking out
ABW events for more art book fun! (Who
says you can ever have enough?)

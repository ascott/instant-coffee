In the exhibition Systems Shift, Valerie Durant presents work from several series that
explore the boundaries between photographic image-making and sculpture. These
pieces serve as an inquiry into material culture, consumption, and the
interconnectedness of living systems, addressing themes of regeneration, ecological
precarity, and the impact of human intervention on the natural world.

What, at first glance looks like a simple cross-cut of a tree, is in fact a sculpture
made from pulped paper shopping bags and photographs.

With this exhibition, Durant reflects on the tension between industrialised material
use and the fragility of living systems. Durant's practice interprets climate change as
both a personal and collective call for reflection and transformation. Embedded in
her identity as a mother and grandmother is a deep commitment to sustainability and
intergenerational care"evident in her intentional reuse of organic and inorganic
materials to minimise environmental impact for future generations.

There will be an opening reception on Saturday, May 10 from noon - 4 PM.

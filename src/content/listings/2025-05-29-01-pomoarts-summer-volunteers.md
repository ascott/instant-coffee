PoMoArts is Looking for Summer Volunteers

Interested in volunteering for the arts this summer? PoMoArts is looking for volunteers
of all ages for our summer volunteer opportunities!

During the months of July and August, well need volunteers for our Kids Summer Art
Camps. Our Summer Art Camps are the backbone of PoMoArts summer
programming, and our volunteers help make them a truly memorable experience for
kids!

Or do you want to be part of something new? We will have lots of different volunteer
roles available during our exciting new summer event: Art Out Side! Art Out Side is a
new multidisciplinary arts festival transforming Port Moody this summer, where arts will
fill the streets, from waterfronts to commercial hubs, alleyways to park benches, and
secret locations

Come volunteer with us this summer! Pop over to our website now and complete a
volunteer application here https://www.pomoarts.ca/get-involved/volunteer

Fresh Feminism
New Additions #11
June 8
7:00pm Doors
7:30pm Screening
Admission is free

VIVO Media Arts Centre
2625 Kaslo St., Vancouver

On Wednesday, June 8th Video Out will host a screening of works newly in
distribution, selected based on the thematic call for submissions, "Fresh
Feminism". 6 pioneering works will be presented by artists from the US,
Canada, and India each ranging in style from Super8 to digital documentary
to experimental animation.

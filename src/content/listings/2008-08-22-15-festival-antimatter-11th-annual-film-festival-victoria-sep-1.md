Antimatter Launches 11th Annual Film Festival

Antimatter Film Festival
September 19 to 27, 2008 * Victoria, BC, Canada

From September 19 to 27, Victoria will play host to nine days of
unparalleled experimental film screenings, expanded cinema performance and
media art installations collectively known as the Antimatter Film Festival.
Produced by Deluge Contemporary Art and now in it's 11th year, Antimatter
is dedicated to the exhibition and nurturing of film and video as art,
featuring over 200 films from 25 countries, the majority of them world or
Canadian premieres.

This internationally celebrated event will allow a Victoria audience a
first hand look at the new visual languages reinvigorating filmic narrative
structures, and to meet the artists creating it. As well as twice nightly
screenings at Open Space Arts Centre, Antimatter will present three nights
of screenings at the University of Victoria's Cinecenta in addition to
exhibitions at Deluge Contemporary Art and The Ministry of Casual Living
and performances by San Francisco's Kerry Laitala and Potter Belmar Labs of
San Antonio, Texas. The Foreign Matter Series will present a specially
curated program of work from the Netherlands, as well as award-winning
animation from Mexico. For the first time Antimatter has opened up its
selection process and programming to include feature-length projects from
the avant garde of media art.

Complete program guide available online and in print beginning Sept 1 at
the venues or any South Island Serious Coffee location.

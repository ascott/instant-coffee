Forest, Air-Conditioner | JAN 11
Our current exhibition is by the Catskills NY-based artist Gary Kachadourian, who is
known for his precise and detailed rendering of objects and landscapes. The show
contains some very affordable prints and books that he has made over the years. Gary's
books and prints has been collected by various private and public institutions
including
the National Gallery of Canada in Ottawa.

Titled "Bicycle, Forest, Air-Conditioner" the exhibition is his first in Vancouver. The
exhibition will go on till January 11, 2020.

Hotam Press Bookshop/Gallery
218 East 4th Ave. (at Main St)

Hours: Thursday to Saturday 3 to 6 PM

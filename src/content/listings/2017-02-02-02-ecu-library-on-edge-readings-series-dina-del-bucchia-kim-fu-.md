6:30 pm, Thursday, February 2, 2017

The On Edge Readings Series presents:
Dina Del Bucchia + Kim Fu

Open to the public

Library at Emily Carr University of Art & Design
1400 Johnston St.
Granville Island, Vancouver
unceded Coast Salish territories

Dina Del Bucchia is the author of Coping with Emotions and Otters (Talonbooks 
2013), Blind Items (Insomniac Press 2014) and Rom Com (Talonbooks 2015), 
written with her Can't Lit podcast co-host Daniel Zomparelli. Her short story, 
"Under the 'I'," was a finalist for the Writers' Trust RBC Bronwen Wallace Award in 
2012. She guest edited the Humour Issue of Poetry Is Dead magazine and is 
the Artistic Director of the Real Vancouver Writers' Series. An otter and dress 
enthusiast, Dina lives in Vancouver.

Kim Fu's novel For Today I Am a Boy (HarperCollins 2014) was a finalist for the 
PEN/ Hemingway Award, the Kobo Emerging Writer Prize, and a Lambda Literary 
Award, winner of the Edmund White Award for Debut Fiction, and long-listed for 
Canada Reads 2015, among other honours. Her first collection of poems How 
Festive the Ambulance will be published in 2016 with Nightwood Editions. Her 
nonfiction credits include The Atlantic, Hazlitt, and NPR. Fu was a 2015 Berton 
House writer-in-residence in Dawson City, YT and a 2016 Ucross Foundation 
Fellow.

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the Arts.

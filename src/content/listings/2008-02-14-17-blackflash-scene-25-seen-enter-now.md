We are still accepting entries to, SCENE 25 SEEN, our “still” and
“moving” image contest.

ENTER NOW!

BlackFlash is still accepting entries to our Still and Moving Image
Contents, SCENE 25 SEEN. Deadline: February 29, 2007

“Best Still” category includes all lens-based and new media images.

“Best Moving” category includes 30 second films, videos and animations.

All entrants will receive a one-year subscription to BlackFlash with their entry fee.

In celebration of BlackFlash Magazine's 25th Anniversary year, and in recognition of our history of presenting original and innovative artist projects to readers across Canada and Internationally, we are proud to present SCENE 25 SEEN, a “Moving” and “Still” Image Contest focusing on contemporary, lens-based and new media art production in
Canada.

Over the past year, the Editorial Committee has worked towards programming a trilogy of 25th anniversary issues thematically constructed around our byline: LENS (25.1); SITE (25.2); SCENE (25.3). As an extension of our mandate in supporting excellence and innovation in lens-based and new media art production, we are inviting participants to submit their original “still” and/or 30 second “moving” images that will use lens or a hybrid of lens & new media as a point of departure for presenting their
interpretations of SCENE/SEEN. All entrants will be juried by an impressive roster of Canadian "art movers and shakers" – all of whom have contributed uniquely to the production and presentation of photographic and new media arts in Canada. The winner in each category will be awarded a $500 prize as well as being featured in the magazine, SCENE 25.3 (Spring
2008) and on our website.

The two categories available for entry are:

“Best Still Image”
“Best Moving Image”

Please visit our website for entry guidelines!

Centre[3] for Print and Media Arts is an artist-run centre dedicated to promoting print 
and media arts in contemporary artistic discourse. Centre[3]'s extensive Print Arts 
facilities are housed in a 6000 square foot arts centre, located at the heart of the 
James Street North Arts District. As a production, exhibition, education centre and 
animator of community arts, Centre[3] supports both traditional and experimental 
print media and media art practices. The centre encourages research and innovation 
and provides forums for discussion and examination of critical and theoretical 
issues. Centre[3] supports artists in the development of their professional endeavors 
and acts as a resource centre for the visual and media arts community.

We are currently accepting submissions for upcoming exhibitions for 2019 through to 
2021 in our main gallery. Exhibitions in the main gallery centre around a main theme 
or concept. Proposals should highlight the ways in which the work of the artists 
presented relate to the main theme of the exhibition. You can apply as a curator, a 
group of artists, or as a single artist for a solo show. All selected artists & curators 
will be paid CARFAC fees.

Deadline: October 31, 2018. Proposals will be reviewed by a committee and 
applicants will be notified by December 2018. 

More information can be found on our website: 
http://centre3.com/exhibition2018/annual-call-for-submissions-main-gallery/

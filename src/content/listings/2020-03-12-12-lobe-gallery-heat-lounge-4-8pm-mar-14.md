March 14th, 4pm-late Experimental Waves presents HEAT LOUNGE, a free group art
show from 4-8pm, followed by a ticketed live 4D jazz performance at Lobe Gallery. 713
E Hastings St, Vancouver, BC V6A 1R3.

Come warm up your winter with creative works that conjure deep heat in the heart of our
chilly city. The 12 presenting local artists are:

Phoebe Parsons
Kalvin Valko
Madelaine Mongey
Tamara Mills
Alex Gibson
Alex Joukov
Natasha Katedralis
Meaghan Packford
Julia Cundari
Olivia Di Liberto
Althemia Marsland
Max Babuik

After 8pm will be a performance at Lobe Gallery's 4D sound event space, the first of its
kind in Canada. Line up is as follows:

- JUNO Award-winning violinist, Meredith Bates in 4D sound & guest.
- Millie Wissar, Sound Designer/Field Recordist/ & guest.

Tickets for the music event avail here:
[https://www.facebook.com/events/2423164867994348/](https://www.facebook.com/events/2423164867994348/)

*~*~*~*~*~*~*~*

This event will take place on the occupied, traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of
the Coast Salish peoples - Squamish, Stolo and Tsleil-Waututh and Musqueam Nations,
in the city known as Vancouver.

Applications to Capture Photography Festivals 2025 Selected Exhibitions Program
are open from September 9, 2024, until October 18, 2024, at 11:59 PM PST for
exhibitions presented in galleries and informal spaces.

Captures vision is to connect Vancouver to the world through lens-based art and act
as a platform to expand visual literacy. Through this platform, we strive to give voice
to traditionally underrepresented communities and to present compelling, urgent
lens-based art.

Projects will be evaluated by a jury of arts professionals who will be looking to
showcase lens-based exhibitions in the Selected Exhibitions Program that
demonstrate artistic excellence across a diverse range of subject matter.

The 2025 jury consists of:

Richard Hill, Smith Jarislowsky
Senior Curator of Canadian Art, Vancouver Art
Gallery

Gonzalo Reyes Rodriguez, Artist and Assistant Professor, Emily Carr University of
Art and Design

Eve Schillo, Associate Curator, Wallis Annenberg Photography Department, Los
Angeles County Museum of Art

Emmy Lee Wall, Executive Director and Chief Curator, Capture Photography
Festival

The submission fee is $20, which includes a 2024/2025 Capture Photography
Festival Society membership and helps cover the costs of the submission system.
Please contact info@capturephotofest.com if this fee presents a barrier to
submission or if you are already an active member for a waiver.

Please review the FAQ page on our website before submitting to become familiar
with the program's requirements.

"It is with great pleasure that I present to you Fluiten in het Donker, a publication 
conceived alongside the eponymous group exhibition held at de Appel arts 
centre, Amsterdam, from May 20th to June 26th, 2011.

This book is not stricto sensu an exhibition catalogue. You will not find any 
explanation or straightforward analysis of the artworks included in the show but

quotient of documentation materials (exhibition views, photographs and

notes that developed during the thinking process preceding the exhibition.

images related to the show and guided by my evolving internal dialogue. 
Linguistic, psychoanalytic and medical, colonial and architectural, thin, 
labyrinthine, silent and loose associations constitute the body of this book.

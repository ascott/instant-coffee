Join us for the opening of 'A Generosity of Abundance' on February 22, 6-8pm at the
SUM
gallery.

Fibres-rooted artist and Indigo Griot Valrie d. Walker has transformed SUM gallery
into a sanctuary of Queer Joy: a place where Queer reality is infused with self-love
and the power of environmental transformation.

In keeping with the spirit of Queer, joyous transformation, on March 16 the exhibition
expands to include the work of Vancouver artist Jack Page whose practice
encompasses illustration, altered book art, papermaking, printmaking, photography,
and musical performance art."

Date: Opening February 22 @ 6-8pm; Exhibition February 22 to April 5
Location: SUM gallery, Suite 425 - 268 Keefer St., Vancouver, BC V6A1X5 (4th floor of
the Sun Wah Centre)

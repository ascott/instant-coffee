Revised and restructured for the age of social distancing, the sixth installment of our Work
In Progress series features Iris member Ryder Thomas White and his ever-expanding
entourage of thought shards. Forever haunted by the notion that a finished project will have
inevitably failed to incorporate a really good idea from several years back, Ryders
residency in the Burrardview
Fieldhouse will focus on briefly sketching out some of these
ideas with film unburdened by the expectation that some coherent piece will arise from
them.

Expect film jammed in laser printers, pinned under irons, frozen in ice, and otherwise
dispossessed of its normal environs.

For everyones safety, this months activities will not feature any in-person events. Ryder will
post regular updates and observations from the Fieldhouse on his Instagram
(@ryderthomaswhite) and will also be hosting an artist talk via livestream on the Collectives
Instagram account, @theirisfilmers, on April 29 at 7pm. An improvised mobile projection
screen will also be delivering a selection of the works created to the residents of the
Burrardview neighbourhood on the evening of April 25.

Iris Film Collectives WORK IN PROGRESS series is a month-long residency at the Burrard
View Fieldhouse featuring a different Iris Film Collective member each month who will be
using the space to work on their individual
projects.

Ryder Thomas White is a Canadian-American filmmaker and multimedia artist currently
working in Vancouver, British Columbia.

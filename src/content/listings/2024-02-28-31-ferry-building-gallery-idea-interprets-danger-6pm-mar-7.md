The Ferry Building Gallery is pleased to celebrate the opening of IDEA Interprets:
Danger. After a 5-year hiatus, the 2024 graduates at Capilano University IDEA School
of Design are bringing IDEA Interprets back to the Ferry Building Gallery. This year, the
annual poster show will explore the theme of Danger through typography and
illustration. The exhibition features the poster designs of second-year students
pursuing a four-year Bachelor of Design in Visual Communications, with
concentrations in branding, illustration, and interactive design.

The exhibition will open on March 7th, 6 p.m. - 8 p.m. and be on display until April 7.

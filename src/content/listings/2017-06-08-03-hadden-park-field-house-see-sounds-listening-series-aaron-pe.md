See Sounds Listening Series: Aaron Pettigrew
Thursday June 8, 2017, 8pm Talk at 8:30pm
Hadden Park Field House, 1015 Maple St, Vancouver

This will be our seventeenth monthly listening party at the field house. Bring 
something you would like to listen to with a group. Discussion will follow. We will 
have most available formats (digital, vinyl, CD, tape, radio). 

Meet at the field house at 8pm. Our guest presentation will start at 8:30. There will 
be a short intermission and then the floor will be opened to other people who have 
brought sounds to share.

As part of our See Sounds series, we are featuring a guest artist each listening party 
to curate the early portion of the evening. We have invited Aaron Pettigrew to be this 
month's guest. Aaron is a PhD student in Ethnomusicology at the University of 
British Columbia. In 2015 he traveled to Timor-Leste (East Timor) with Philip 
Yampolsky to record and document traditional music on the south coast. Aaron will 
share some of the material they recorded and talk about the music of Timor, field 
recording, and his upcoming research plans.

suggested donation $5-10 All donations go to the guest artist.

Employment Opportunity | OUTREACH + DISTRIBUTION MANAGER

VIDEO OUT: OUTREACH + DISTRIBUTION MANAGER

The Satellite Video Exchange Society (www.vivomediaarts.com) is a
Vancouver-based, charitable, non-profit, artist-run media arts centre that
encourages and directly supports the creation, production, education,
exhibition, distribution, and appreciation of media works by independent
artists and alternative, community- based producers.

Position Description: This is a full-time management position, working with
the management team of VIVO Media Arts Centre while overseeing Video Out
Distribution operations, outreach, special projects and the activities of
the Video Out Technical Coordinator.

Overview: The Outreach + Distribution Manager is primarily responsible for
the artist-focused distribution and support of new and historic video and
new media work, playing a key role in both the support and development of
new media-related contemporary art practices and the preservation of
historical video productions and the digitization of tapes from the Crista
Dahl Media Library and Archives. The Outreach and Distribution Manager is
responsible for all aspects of the day-to-day operations of Video Out
Distribution, including developing and executing distribution sales,
rentals and plans, the acquisition and dissemination of new and historical
work, assisting curators and programmers, maintaining producer files and
records, budgeting and accounting, grant writing and reporting, and
conducting outreach activities for the promotion of work in active
distribution.

Duties and Responsibilities include:

Coordinating Artist Services w/ Outreach Initiatives, Acquisitions, Artist
Relations
Marketing of Collection & Active Distribution Titles
Collection Management
Licensing
Special Projects Design & Management
Facilitation of Collaborative Community Partnerships
Curatorial & Programming Support/Promotion
Grant Writing
Human Resources Management
Policy & Procedures Development
Financial Management, Planning, Budgeting & Oversight
Qualifications:

An established knowledge of video art history and curatorial / educational
experience with independent media arts production
Marketing and/or media distribution experience
Experience working with artist-run centres, collectives or community-based
organizations
Excellent written and verbal communications skills, ability to plan and
manage basic budgets and financial operations
Ability to work constructively with diverse communities and individuals
Broad based computer knowledge (Mac and PC), in particular familiarity with
Photoshop, Word Processing, Excel, Filemaker Pro
Be self-motivated with an ability to organize and execute day to day
operations, set and meet deadlines, plan and carry out long range projects,
set priorities.

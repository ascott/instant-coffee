YCW Summer Student Contract

Marketing & Communications Assistant

Deadline: Friday, April 30, 5pm

Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver

In 2021, CAG will celebrate its 50th anniversary. To mark this milestone, CAG will launch
this fall a new visual identity, new web presence and a
marketing campaign that engages
key moments in the organizations histories.

To assist with the timely execution and rollout of these initiatives, CAG seeks a Marketing
& Communications Assistant. Collaborating with the gallerys Marketing &
Communications Officer, the Marketing & Communications Assistant will work during a
14-week period at 37.5 hours per week, from May 31, 2021 to September 3, 2021. All
work will be conducted in English.

Find out more and download the application pack at

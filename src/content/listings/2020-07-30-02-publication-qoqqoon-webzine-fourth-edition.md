QOQQOON.COM is pleased to announce the launch of its fourth edition. This edition
contains the writing of eight emerging artists on the question of artistic compromise. The
edition features, "Performer/Spectator Relationships: A Dynamic of Equilibrium" by Marilyn
Volkman, "On Absences" by Alannah Klamp, "Clinique (Of Less. Compromise)" by Leigh
Tennant, "Broadcasting as Compromise: Radio atelier and research art on air" by Benjamin
J. Allard, "Season 1" by Emma Hicks and sophia bartholomew, "Blended Marks" by Aileen
Bahmanipour and "Artistic Compromise" by Steven Cottingham.

QOQQOON (cocoon) is a webzine for writing by artists. We also republish hard-to-find pdfs
online. In our current issue (#4), we are thinking about compromise. Submissions are
welcome. Edited by Leigh Tennant and Steven Cottingham. Published on unceded territories
(Vancouver, Canada) since 2018

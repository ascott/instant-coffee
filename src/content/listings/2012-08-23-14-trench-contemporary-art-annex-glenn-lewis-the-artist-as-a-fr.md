Trench is very excited to be opening its fall exhibition season with senior artist 
Glenn Lewis, at two locations: Trench (proper) and the Trench Annex pop-up 
gallery. Lewis is a senior Canadian conceptual artist whose multidisciplinary 
practice includes ceramics, performance, video, photography and sculpture. 
The exhibition at Trench proper will focus on documentary photographs 
capturing Lewis's portrayal of characters within his performances dating back 
to 1968. These often funny and ironic photographs capture Lewis's pioneering 
performance works and introduces audiences to the ways in which Lewis 
investigates our tendency to adopt differing personas, as a means to mediate 
the space between ourselves and society. The show also includes a series of 
assemblage sculptures which address issues of social expression and 
constraint, historical reference, authenticity and personal mortality. Lewis 
believes found materials, as Heidegger put it, create “a kind of knowing-how 
rather than knowing-that…what is most important and meaningful in our lives

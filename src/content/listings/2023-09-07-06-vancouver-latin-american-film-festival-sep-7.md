The 21st Vancouver Latin American Film Festival (@vlaff) runs from September 7 - 17,
2023 in cinemas downtown! Showcasing over 75 films from 16 countries in over 12
languages (always with English subtitles) by cutting-edge, contemporary and
innovative Latin and Latin-Canadian filmmakers!

VLAFF also includes a by-donation concert series every Friday through Sunday from
September 8-17 at The Cinematheques Courtyard at 7:45pm. Each festival night
highlights different sections from our festival such as New Directors, Activismo!,
Indigenous
Cinema, Queer Latinx, Canada Looks South, and much more.

Join us for our Opening Night Film and Party starting at SFU Woodwards at 7pm with
a screening of Rule 34 (Regra 34) and dance the night away at the Fox Cabaret with
some live salsa and Brazilian music at 9pm by Wasakaka all Stars and the Sara Magal
Trio.

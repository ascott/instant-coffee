VIVO Media Arts Centre Archives
Top Ten: 1976

Wednesday, March 30 - 8:00pm

In the early years of the Satellite Video Exchange Society, now VIVO Media
Arts, the centre functioned as a community space for artists and the general
public to drop in and watch videos from the library. Tape viewing statistics
were tracked, and a list of the top ten most viewed titles were kept for
each year from 1974 to 1990. On the last Wednesday of every month, we'll
present an informal screening of a selection of these works.

This month we present the top ten hits of 1976. Free popcorn!

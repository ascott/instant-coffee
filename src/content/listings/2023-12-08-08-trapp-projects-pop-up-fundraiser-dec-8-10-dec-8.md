SALE: December 9-10, 2023
274 East 1st Avenue, Vancouver (enter via back alley)

Trapp Projects are pleased to announce our 4rd Annual Pop Up Art
Exhibition/Fundraiser.

Friday through Sunday Dec 8-10th with 50% of proceeds going to the artists and
25% donated to the Vancouver Food Bank.

Friday Dec. 8th.

In-person preview: 5-8pm Friday Dec 8th at Trapp Projects (274 East 1st Avenue,
Vancouver " entrance back alley).

to trappprojects@gmail.com (starting at 10am
Saturday December 9th. The first person to request a work will be sent an invoice to
pay.

Purchased art work can be picked up Sunday 3-5 pm or by appointment

share this email!

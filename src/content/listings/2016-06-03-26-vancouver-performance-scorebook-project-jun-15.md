The Vancouver Performance Scorebook Project (Maren Lisac and Alanna Ho) is
looking for musical and performance scores by people currently based in
Greater Vancouver, for publication in a collective book project.

***We are ONLY accepting submissions from anyone residing in the GREATER
VANCOUVER DISTRICT currently.

The book will consist of music scores in non-conventional types of
notation, e.g. graphic and verbal, as well as instructions for non-musical
performance pieces, for all kinds of people to explore and perform. When
the book is complete, we intend to put on a combination concert/exhibition
and book release party featuring selected pieces from the book.

Deadline for submitting scores is JUNE 15. Revisions (if necessary) will be
due at a later date TBA.

If you would like to submit something or have any further questions about
the project, please contact marenlisac@gmail.com or alanna382@gmail.com.

GUIDELINES:

You must be a resident of the Greater Vancouver Regional District.

The pieces can be scores for musicians, or instructions for non-musical
performance.

The pieces should be easily accessible for performers at all levels of
expertise. That means, for example, avoiding notation requiring a high
degree of technical skill.

Aim for simplicity and clarity, and at the same time, depth and substance.

You can submit a new piece, or an existing one if you have one that you
think fits the criteria for this project.

You can submit as many pieces as you want.

Submissions can be submitted on paper (email us for instructions), or
electronically, through email, file sharing services, etc.

Scores can be black and white or colour.

Format: The book will be printed in landscape orientation, in approximately
letter size. Files do not have to be scaled exactly to letter size, but
please keep this format in mind for your submission. Minimum resolution for
electronic images is 8.5x11 at 300 ppi. (larger or higher resolution files
are fine, as they can be sized down).

Please attach a short (150 words or less) bio with your submission.

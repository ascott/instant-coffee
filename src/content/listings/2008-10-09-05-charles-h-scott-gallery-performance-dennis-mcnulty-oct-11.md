Dennis McNulty
the view from now/downtown parkade
Saturday October 11, 2008 from 4:00 to 4:30

The downtown parkade, Front Street, New Westminster.
(total journey time from downtown Vancouver - 30 mins by Skytrain)

As part of the exhibition "The Sound I'm Looking For" at the Charles H.
Scott Gallery, Irish artist Dennis McNulty will create a soundtrack for the
downtown parkade.

Built in 1959, New Westminster's downtown parkade represents an idea of
city life as imagined from a particular time. These days it is used as a
set for film and TV - cop shows, horror and science-fiction. A recent
report in the New Westminster Record suggests that the structure's days are
numbered. Various groups are currently lobbying to remove the parkade to
allow the further development of the area.

More information at www.sndmsc.com and chscott.eciad.ca

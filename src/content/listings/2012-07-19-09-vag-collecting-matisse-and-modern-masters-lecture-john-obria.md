Collecting Matisse and Modern Masters:
Matisse and His Private American Collectors

DATE: Saturday, July 21
TIME: 2pm

John O'Brian, art historian and author of Ruthless Hedonism: The American
Reception of Matisse, expands on the context of Matisse's entry into the North
American art world elite and the significance of his relationship with Etta and
Claribel Cone.

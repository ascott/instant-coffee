THIRD [Opening Reception | MAY 9 - 6PM to 12AM || Live Performance of
Boymoder | MAY 9 - 8PM]
@NoParking Gallery - 4906 Main Street, Vancouver BC

ABSTRACT:
The debut Solo Exhibition of multidisciplinary artist Clara Conrado, featuring an
expansive body of work curated and organized by the artist. This exhibition is
comprised of unique, site-specific, installation, sculpture or
media-based
deformations of found/discarded monitor displays, alongside a small curation of the
artist's experimental engagements with generative artificial intelligence image as
material flow.

NoParking Gallery has two main areas: 2 gallery rooms and a large courtyard,
guests will enter through the gallery and will be able to exit through the courtyard
during the opening reception. The artist will be in attendance.

ABV. STATEMENT:
THIRD is the inaugural solo exhibition of Clara Conrado, afro-Brazilian and trans-
femme multidisciplinary artist, currently based in so-called Vancouver. Exhibiting at
No Parking Gallery, attendants are invited to encounter each piece as
{participatory||translated||displaced} themes of inexistence, virtuality, identification,
and time " as they similarly encounter the viewer.

Melding ontological, topological, and mediatic interpolations of painting,
photography, sculpture, and installation art, THIRD features unseen deformations of
image-based mediums:
9 {black&&smooth||oily||fractured||desiring} (LCD) display
modules " altered by the artist through gestural and tactile heat- or chemical-based
transfigurations of material, " && 3 experimental interventions of generative artificial
intelligence " as {a material flow of image||image as material in and of itself}.

The opening will feature the live musical performance of Boymoder at 8PM. This 4-
piece band combines emo and math-pop sounds with live-visual projections, which
they describe as a mourning of marginality and a celebration of liminality.
Boymoder's music is an expressive blend of rich guitar harmonies, dissonant bass
lines, fiery drums, lo-fi live video mixings and the lead vocalist's own thoughtfully
composed poetics that delve into the complex relationships of the artists with
displacement, identity and language.

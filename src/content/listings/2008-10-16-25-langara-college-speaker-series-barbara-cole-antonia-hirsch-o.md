Barbara Cole

The Melbourne Urban Screens 08 & Mobile Publics Conference
Executive Director of Other Sights for Artists Projects and principal of
Cole Projects, Barbara Cole, will discuss the potential of large outdoor
advertising screens as viable platforms for artists projects in Vancouver.
Having recently returned from the Urban Screens International Conference in
Melbourne, Australia, Cole will present a selection of works that
investigated new ways of engaging audiences in public spaces. Urban Screens
Melbourne 08 (USM08) is the
third international conference and exhibition of Urban Screens, following
on from the inaugural conference in Amsterdam in 2005 and Manchester in
2007. Urban Screens investigates how the commercial use of outdoor screens
can be broadened with cultural content, addressing such cultural fields as
digital media culture, urbanism, architecture and art. Geared to the use of
digital infrastructure for contributing to a lively urban society, Urban
Screens aims to engage local communities through this digital layer of the
city. Barbara Cole is a practicing artist, independent curator, educator,
project manager, and curatorial consultant in public art. She is the
founder and Executive Director of Other Sights for Artists Projects, a
non-profit society that provides support to artists, writers and curators
interested in creating temporary, critically rigorous work for highly
visible locations. Barbara
taught sessionally at Emily Carr University from 1983 to 1999, served as a
trustee for the Vancouver Art Gallery and the Contemporary Art Gallery.

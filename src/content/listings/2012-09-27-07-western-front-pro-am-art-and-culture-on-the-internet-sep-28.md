Pro-Am: Art and Culture on the Internet 
Sept. 28 - Sept. 30

Opening Reception and Keynote Performance
Friday, September 28 @ 8pm, Western Front Grand Luxe Hall

Pro-Am is a three day conference about internet art practice, bringing together a 
range of thinkers and practitioners to investigate the implications of the 
changing role of artists as 'users' online. Pro-Am aims to explore the history and 
future of artistic creation on the internet. The conference will open with a remote 
keynote performance by Cory Arcangel. Arcangel will debut a new multi-site 
performance, investigating disembodied communication and the spectacle of 
technology and performance. Working with consumer networking tools, the 
performance will engage with the Western Front's history of broadcasting and 
networked art initiatives. A performance by experimental sensory project MSHR 
(Brenna Murphy and Birch Cooper) will follow. For more information about 
MSHR visit - www.mshr.info 

On Saturday and Sunday the conference will feature a series of presentations 
from a range of local, national and international practitioners, including: Dragan 
Espenscheid (Stuttgart, Germany), Lorna Mills (Toronto, ON), Jon Rafman 
(Montreal, PQ), Rick Silva (Calgary, Alberta), Brenna Murphy (Portland, 
Oregon), Amber Frid-Jimenez (Vancouver, BC), Cheryl L'Hirondelle (Toronto, 
ON), Matthew Williamson (Syracuse, NY), Sylvain Sailly (Vancouver, BC), 
Raphael Bastide (Paris, France), Sara Ludy (Vancouver, BC). A full schedule is 
available on the Western Front website at www.front.bc.ca

Ping! A Speed Show ([http://speedshow.net/ping/](http://speedshow.net/ping/)) curated by Aaron Chan and 
Auerliano Seguando will take place at the Nicolas Internet cafe on Saturday 
Sept 29 from 7pm -11pm.

For more information about Pro-Am: Art and Culture on the Internet, please

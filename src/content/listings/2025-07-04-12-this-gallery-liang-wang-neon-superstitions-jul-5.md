THIS Gallery is thrilled to be hosting a colour infused solo exhibition by Vancouver-
based artist Liang Wang.

Neon Superstitions is a series of paintings that explore the persistence of belief, ritual,
and symbolism in contemporary urban life. Drawing from scenes in Vancouvers
Chinatown, the works feature neon signage, beckoning cats, Guan Gong statues, and
shopfront displays " symbols that blur the lines between commerce, faith, and folklore.
These icons, often found in everyday spaces, become visual cues for protection,
fortune, and justice, especially within diasporic communities.

THIS is excited to exhibit this series of works which pay homage to the gallerys
original home, Chinatown, says THIS Gallery owner, Shannon Pawliw. Chinatown is a
vibrant and exciting neighbourhood full of culture, kitsch and art. The work in Neon
Superstitions emphasizes the tenacity and perseverance of the residents of
Chinatown.

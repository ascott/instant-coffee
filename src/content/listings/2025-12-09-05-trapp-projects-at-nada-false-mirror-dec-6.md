False Mirror

Opening: 1-4 pm Saturday December 6th

Exhibition dates: December 6th through December 8th

Opening Hours: Sat & Sun 1-5pm (or by appointment)

Location: NADA 3553 Main Street (entrance via back alley)

We are pleased to announce the opening of False Mirror, an exhibition featuring the work of
Marian Penner Bancroft, Cameron Kerr, Evan Lee, Bernadette Phan and Chief Henry Speck
Sr.

The exhibition title, False Mirror, refers to a 1929 painting by the Surrealist Ren Magritte that
Man Ray once described as a painting that sees as much as it itself is seen. This exhibition
has not only been curated with this description in mind, but with a nod to the benefactor of
Trapp Projects and CFA, Nada Vuksic, owner of the optometry store Bruce Eyewear located
above our gallery. The three-day pop up exhibition brings together four artists whose uniquely
different aesthetics produce objects and pictures that have the uncanny effect of addressing
the viewer in ways that reveal meanings beyond conscious perception.

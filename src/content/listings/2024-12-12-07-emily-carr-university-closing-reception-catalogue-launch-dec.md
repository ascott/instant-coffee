Rete: Published Objects as Network features 20 original books created by
communication design students at Emily Carr University of Art + Design. Inspired by
the keyword "rete, the Latin root word for "net and "network," these works range
from the sculptural, to
the experimental, to the traditional. Collectively, these books
explore the question of what a printed books role can be in the 21st century.

We also invite you to our closing reception on December 11th, 2024, at 1PM. There

Artists' Talk and Tour
Eric Metcalfe, Gathie Falk and Carole Itter with Daina Augaitis
Tuesday November 3, 7pm
In the Gallery, 2nd floor

Vancouver artists Eric Metcalfe, Gathie Falk and Carole Itter join Chief
Curator Daina Augaitis for an in-gallery discussion on their
boundary-breaking practices that introduced new categories of art, such as
installation and performance, to Vancouver in the 1960s and 70s.

Free for Members or with Gallery admission

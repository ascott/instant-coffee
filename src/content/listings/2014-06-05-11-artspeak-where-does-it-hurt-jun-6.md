“Where Does it Hurt?” 

CATHY BUSBY, DAVID MACWILLIAM, RACHELLE SAWATSKY,
KRISTA BELLE STEWART 
Curated by JONAH GRAY

June 7–July 26, 2014
Opening: Friday, June 6, 8pm

Boris Groys recently claimed that artworks are inherently sick and require a 
curator to heal them by including them in an exhibition. While this was likely 
more of a rhetorical flourish than a programmatic statement, it nevertheless 
highlights the power imbalances that permeate both art and therapy as ideas 
and practices. The works in this exhibition reconsider some of the many 
historical and contemporary intersections of art and therapy, seeking, in the 
process, to question those very power imbalances. Contrary to at least one 
recent take on this theme, however, each of the works in this exhibition 
demonstrate that not all art is therapeutic, nor that its aim should be to make 
everyone happy.

Submissions are now open for Capture Photography Festival's 2024 juried Selected
Exhibitions Program! From September 18 to October 16, 2023 at 11:59 PM POST,
Capture is accepting exhibition proposals for the 2024 Capture Photography Festival
which runs from April 1"30, 2024.

Submissions are evaluated by a jury of arts professionals who will be looking to
showcase projects in the Selected Exhibition Program that demonstrate artistic
excellence and present a diverse range of subject matter.

The 2024 jury
consists of:
Emilie Croning, Independent curator, artist, and art historian
Monika Szewczyk, Audain Chief Curator, The Polygon Gallery
Emmy Lee Wall, Executive Director, Capture Photography Festival
Jin-Me Yoon, Professor, School for the Contemporary Arts, Simon Fraser University

Captures vision is to connect Vancouver to the world through lens-based art. The
Festival acts as a platform to expand visual literacy through lens-based art; strives to
give voice to traditionally underrepresented communities and to present compelling,
urgent lens-based art.

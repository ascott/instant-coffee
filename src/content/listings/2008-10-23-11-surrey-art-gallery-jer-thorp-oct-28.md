Surrey Art Gallery

13750-88th Avenue, Surrey, BC

Artist: Jer Thorp

What if an artwork was made to mutate and evolve, depending on the whim of
its environment?

What would an artwork look like, if it was programmed to generate image
maps of activity on the internet?

See what's happening in the world of digital art, with award winning
software artist, Jer Thorp, Tuesday, October 28, 7:30-9pm at the Surrey Art
Gallery

Whether you know a little or a lot about digital art, this talk will be
entertaining and enlightening. Jer Thorp is part of a new generation of
artists, who creates art with software. Educated as a geneticist, Thorp's
digital art practice bridges science and art, and has been welcomed in
exhibitions on almost all continents around the world. He's known locally
as an educator, teaching in the electronic design department of Langara
College, but at Flash software conferences around the world, he is
recognized as a leading expert. In his previous life as a Flash developer
and designer, Thorp produced work for a broad base of clients including
Honda, Canadian Broadcasting Corporation, FOX, and the LA Kings. Thorp's
unique collection of organic Flash experiments and generative artworks has
won numerous awards, and has been featured in many design magazines both
online and in print.

Jer Thorp will introduce some of his recent projects, and with his usual
humour and enthusiasm , share the extraordinary world of software art
practice. His talk will include some of his contributions to the
artist-in-residence team project â&#65533;&#65533;Glocalâ&#65533;&#65533; currently in production in the
Surrey Art Gallery's TechLab.

Glocal is a Cultural Capital of Canada initiative in Surrey, led by an
artist team that includes M. Simon Levin and Sylvia Grace Borda.

ON AT BRITANNIA ART GALLERY 1661 Napier Street, Vancouver, BC
EXHIBITIONS: DOMESTIC SAVAGES 
Collages by Jane Stanier and Dominique Norville

December 6 - 29, Opening Reception: Wednesday Dec 6 1, 6:30 - 8:30 pm

The genesis for these pieces was a collection of German film magazines from the 
late
fifties and early sixties, generously donated by Dominiques mother. Jane and
Dominique worked with these rich images in a parallel collaborative process.
Jane was thrilled with the quality of the colours, the saturation of the reds in
particular, and the quirky depictions of women. In putting together the collages,
Jane was impelled to restore power, dignity and a certain savagery to these women.
Dominiques focus was more towards the magazines advertisements, portraying an
affluent and glamorous lifestyle just ten years after the country had been involved in
a devastating war. The women were beautiful, and clearly, survivors.
Both Jane and Dominique were eager to work with these images as they were from
their mothers generation, and provocative from a modern feminist perspective.
Unlike North American magazines of that era, that depicted women solely in a
homemaker role, Film Revue appeared to acknowledged and even promote the fact
that women were independent and part of the work force.
The 3D installation of animal/human hybrids amidst a beautiful but disconcerting
natural environment seemed a logical extension of the 2D work. The world within
the case depicts what happens when women escape from their servitude. The
resultant animal/human hybrids are influenced by multiple sources, such as Greek
mythology (centaurs, fauns, chimera etc.), Dadaism, and dystopian visions such as
that portrayed in Margaret Atwood's novel The Year of the Flood.

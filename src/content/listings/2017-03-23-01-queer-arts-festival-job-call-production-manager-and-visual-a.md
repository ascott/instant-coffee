The Pride in Art Society (PiA) is hiring a Production Manager and a Visual Arts 
Preparator for our 2017 Queer Arts Festival. This year's festival UnSettled is Two-Spirit 
curated and developed in collaboration with Indigenous arts organizations and artists. 
UnSettled runs June 17"29, 2017.

PiA produces the Queer Arts Festival (QAF), an annual artist-run multidisciplinary 
festival at the Roundhouse in Vancouver, BC. Recognized as one of the top 5 festivals 
of its kind worldwide (Melbourne Herald Sun), QAF produces, presents and exhibits 
work with a curatorial vision favouring challenging, thought-provoking work that pushes 
boundaries and initiates dialogue. 

PRODUCTION MANAGER 
(contract position: part-time April-May, full-time June)
The Production Manager reports to the Artistic Director and as directed, provides 
leadership, coordination, and production expertise between artists, technicians, venue 
and staff. The Production Manager works cooperatively with the General Manager and 
Artistic Director to ensure that the multidisciplinary Queer Arts Festival programming 
and productions are produced and presented as planned and in a professional manner. 
The Production Manager is someone who has a passion for the arts and awareness of 
the queer community and has excellent production and project management skills.

VISUAL ART PREPARATOR
(contract position: part-time April-May, installation mid-late June)
The Visual Art Preparator reports to the Artistic Director and as directed, performs 
skilled work involving art handling and movement, and installation and de-installation of 
the curated visual art exhibition. Preparators perform work to various degrees of 
specialization in the following areas: art handling; condition reporting; mount making; 
packing and crating; matting and framing; installing two and three dimensional art works 
and exhibitions; performing other related tasks as needed.

Please apply by sending your cover letter and resume/CV to info@prideinart.ca

PiA offers competitive compensation, flexible hours, and a creative, respectful working 
environment.

PiA is an equal opportunity employer and we invite all qualified applicants to apply. 
While we thank all applicants for their interest, we apologize that we may be unable to 
respond to inquiries and will only be able to respond to those applicants who are 
selected for an interview.

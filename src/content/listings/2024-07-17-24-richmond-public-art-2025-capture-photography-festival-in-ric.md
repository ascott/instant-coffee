The Richmond Public Art Program and Richmond Art Gallery invite artists and artist
collectives/groups to submit lens-based proposals for temporary public art installations
at one of two locations on the Canada Line: Aberdeen Station and Brighouse Terminus
Station, as part of Capture Photography Festival 2025.

Artist Fee: $1,500.00 CAD

Submission Deadline: August 5, 2024

Eligibility: Open to Professional Artists

Exhibition Duration: April 2025 - March 2026

For more information and to
apply please see here:

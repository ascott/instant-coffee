UPCOMING WORKSHOPS AND SCREENINGS AT CINEWORKS

THE MOVEMENT IS THE MESSAGE
DANCE FILM PROCESS AND TECHNIQUE WORKSHOP

This one-day workshop led by acclaimed dance filmmaker Daniel Conrad is
intended to give participants an in-depth overview of dance film
fundamentals. Through exposure to various concepts (via screened films)
and practice (via live dance demos with lighting), highly unconventional
and experimental possibilities for dance will emerge. Participants will
leave the session fueled with ideas to catapult their own projects into
production. The opportunity for input from a professional dance filmmaker
is just a fringe benefit of this class come prepared to watch some
awesome dance and be inspired!

TREE PLANTING FILM TO SCREEN AT CINEWORKS ANNEX

On Nov. 18, 2010, Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society will host a free
screening of Franz Otto Ultimate Highballer, Adam Humphreys' documentary
film about tree planting culture. The film was shot in 2009 while Humphreys
worked as a planter in Vernon BC. It is currently being screened in small
venues across Canada. Screenings are being organized independently by
planters familiar with the film's Facebook page and Humphreys' blog
www.adamhump.com. The film was inspired by the online tree planter hall of
fame, where planters can nominate peers and read about legendary figures of
the industry. Humphreys was particularly awed by the nominations for a
planter named Franz Otto - some of which doubted his existence - and his
only available photo (attached). The film features several notable
planters: writer Charlotte Gill, industry pioneer Dirk Brinkman, world
record holding planter Ken Chaplin, artist Sarah Anne Johnson, and ice
climber Guy Lacelle, who died last winter in a climbing accident. 
Filmmaker Adam Humphreys studied sociology at UVIC before moving to New
York and pursuing a Master's degree in documentary film. He spent four
seasons as a planter. He lives in Brooklyn with his wife and cat.

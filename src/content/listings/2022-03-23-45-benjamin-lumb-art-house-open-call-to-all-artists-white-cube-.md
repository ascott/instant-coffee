Open
call to all Artists
Benjamin Lumb Art House|BLAH

"White Cube"
March 15 - May 5

White Cube is a "Free For All" salon that offers an open platform to all artists at all
stages of their careers. The exhibition will be pieced together based upon the artworks
submitted. Modeled on an egalitarian submission style, original artworks of all sizes,
mediums and subject matter, created over the past year may be submitted to the
gallery and will hang in a salon-style mosaic.

In principle, White Cube is an exhibition showcasing true inclusivity, diversity and
cross-pollination, allowing artists and collectors to find one another. Weekly salons will
encourage and support new connections and conversations. Artworks may be
submitted throughout the specified dates but when the gallery walls are full artworks
may be pulled by the curator to accomodate new work arriving at the gallery. Only sold
and selected works will stay on the walls until closing night May 14th.
Participating artists: Bring up to
three recently completed artworks to BLAH between
March 15th & May 5th, Monday to Thursday, 7-830pm.

Artworks will be accepted from all artists of any medium, size and subject matter.
Sculpture and installation must be submitted via email at gallery@benjaminlumb.art.
The submitting artist must be present upon dropoff. Artists can only submit once.
Artwork must have been created within the last 2 years. Works on paper must be
framed. Canvases may be unframed. Work must be ready to hang with appropriate
hardware. Artists will receive 50% of the sale price of their work paid at the close of the
exhibition. Artists may submit anonymously. If choosing to sell include fairmarket value.
If artworks are uninstalled artists will be notified and must pick up their work within two
days.

Benjamin Lumb Art House,1370 Marine Drive, West Vancouver
gallery@benjaminlumb.art

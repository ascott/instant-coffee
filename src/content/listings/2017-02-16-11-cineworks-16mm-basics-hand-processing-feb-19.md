16mm Basics: Introduction to Hand Processing

19 February 20172:00pm-5:00pm
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander Street (Ironworks basement)

$40 + taxes | General Admission
$35 + taxes | Concession
$30 + taxes | Member Admission

REGISTER: http://bit.ly/2kFTHBI

A 3 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and 
practice of processing 16mm black and white film by hand.

Elements include:

Principles of chemical developing
Darkroom safety
Bucket processing
Tank processing

Participants also registered for the Introduction to Bolex workshop on 12 Feb will 
get to process their own footage in this class.

DOWN ON THE CORNER
Presents: JONATHAN SYME

Joins us for a conversation on Sunday January 19th at 5pm.

Retaining The Energy But Losing The Image

Reflection is the change in direction of a wavefront at an interface between 
two different media so that the wavefront returns into the medium from which 
it originated. 
By utilizing the expulsion of breath as an adhesive medium to secure gold leaf 
to a glass surface I have effectively left an imprint of a personal resource 
and given weight in a tangible monetary sense to the reflective echo of being. 
Constant in my own understanding this resource unique to my person, 
breath; bountiful and unconscious while available, is ultimately finite, 
displayed here as delicate and impermanent as a visual manifestation 
pertaining to time and the experiences shared in this locale. Soon will be 
gone.

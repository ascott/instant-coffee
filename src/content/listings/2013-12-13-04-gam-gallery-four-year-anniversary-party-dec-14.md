Gam Gallery Four Year Anniversary Party
Saturday, December 14, 8 pm - 1 am

The Gam Gallery invites you to join us in celebrating our 4 YEAR 
ANNIVERSARY. We are pulling out all the Gam classics for this one: art, 
games, live music, vinyl, and festive drinks. 

It will also be the last day to pick up gifts from our Holiday Pop-Up Shop. 

$6 @ the door (for live music)

We will be accepting donations of dry or canned goods for the Greater 
Vancouver Food Bank Society.

Musical Guests:

Twin Bandit - https://www.facebook.com/twinbandit
Twin Bandit is Hannah Walker and Jamie Elliott. Together they bring you an 
intimate folk/roots performance with strong harmonies & original songs. 

Missy Donaldson - Missy will delighting your ears with folksy tunes sweetly 
sung and ripped on the electric guitar.

Mercy Walker - Soulful songstress and her guitar.

Alex Waber - Photographer and DJ extraordinaire, Alex will be spinning vinyl 
to the tune of funk, indie and other classics.

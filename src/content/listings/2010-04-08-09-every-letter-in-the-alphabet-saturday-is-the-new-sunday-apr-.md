Saturday Is The New Sunday

A once-a-month event at Every Letter
Saturday April 10th, from 4 to 8pm-ish
1875 Powell Street Vancouver BC

Please join us to help awaken Every Letter... to a new beginning, a new sound system and new 
curatorial thesis. 
Twenty-six artists in alphabetically allocated, assumed, ascribed or adopted areas of Every 
Letter... That is, an A to Z, always altering, all-encompassing, accessible arrangement and is 
absolutely audience activated, an altogether across-the-board alphabetically aligned allegory.

This project is commissioned by the City of Vancouver Public Art Program as part of Mapping and 
Marking, artist-initiated projects for Vancouver 2010.

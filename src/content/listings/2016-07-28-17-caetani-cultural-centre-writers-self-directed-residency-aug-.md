Perfect for a Writer - A self-directed residency in the beautiful North 
Okanagan, BC this August!

Private Suite - Fully Furnished 1 Bedroom + Office (Apply online 
only)
Available: August 19 - 29, 2016
Writer's Live/Work space: $50/night

This is a one-time sublet of one of our permanent residents' suite. 
This is a fully furnished one-bedroom + office suite in the Caetani 
House overlooking the gardens. This suite would be most suitable 
for a writer as there is a dedicated writing space. Part of the terms 
of this rental would include watering the plants for the duration of 
the tenancy.

This availability coincides with the Spoke Literary Festival, whom 
are partnering with the Caetani Centre, Okanagan College and the 
Okanagan Regional Library to present a three day festival of literary 
arts - Please check their website at spokefestival.com for more 
information

HOLLY WARD
Here & There

Opening reception: Thursday, 10 September, 6 - 8pm
Showing: 11 September to 8 October 2009

"Here and There" continues Ward's explorations into the speculative discourse of utopia, and the 
necessary compromise of material and aesthetic representations of this theoretical 'no-place'. 
"Here & There" is comprised of large-scale drawing and intimate sculpture which function as both 
diagrams and objects. Based on the long tradition of visionary architecture, Ward has created an 
installation that examines the dialectic of fantasy and pragmatism, mining the gap between 
possibility and limitation in a recuperative gesture.

Holly Ward is a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary artist working with sculpture, multi-media 
installation and drawing to examine intersections between aesthetics and speculative thinking. 
She has had solo exhibitions across Canada: at the Morris and Helen Belkin and the Or Gallery 
(Vancouver), YYZ Artists' Outlet (Toronto), Oeil Du Poisson (Quebec), and Struts Artist-Run 
Centre (Sackville), amongst others. She has been included in numerous group exhibitions in 
Canada, London (UK), Mexico City, New York, Bergen (Norway) and Seoul (South Korea).

Holly is the current Artist in Residence at Langara College. This is her debut exhibition at 
Republic Gallery.

Reality | OCT 11-18TH | OCT 11
Northern Sights - A 360 Virtual Reality Project
Exhibition
October 11-18
VIVO Media Arts Centre
2625 Kaslo St, Vancouver, BC
FREE

Northern Sights is a virtual reality project aimed at helping Northern Artists broaden the
reach of
their art forms, in the face of geographical isolation from audiences, this project aims to use
new
technology to bring their work to new audiences.

People all around the world are fascinated with the North, but very few of them ever get a
chance to visit. Through Northern Sights, a varied selection of works from musicians,
storytellers, sculptors, painters, and media artists alike are being captured using virtual
reality
technology, creating immersive environments for audiences, giving them unprecedented
realistic experiences of todays North.

This touring exhibition closes this Friday, October 18th; last chance to see it in Vancouver.

More information
on this project can be found here.
https://wamp.ca/northern-sights-360/

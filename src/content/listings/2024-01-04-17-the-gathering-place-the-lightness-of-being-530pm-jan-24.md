The Lightness of Being Exhibition

Place: The Gathering Place Community Center Gallery, 609 Helmcken

The lightness of being, an increasingly elusive feeling, is the theme for this Connection
Salon Members show from January 19th to March 3rd.
Stand up Comedians from Stand up for Mental Health will also be performing at our
meet the artists event January 24th from 5:30 to 7 PM at the Gallery.
The Lightness of Being is organized by the Connection Salon artists group and
supported by the Community Arts Council of Vancouver, the City of Vancouver, the
Canada Council for the Arts and the Gathering Place Community
Center.

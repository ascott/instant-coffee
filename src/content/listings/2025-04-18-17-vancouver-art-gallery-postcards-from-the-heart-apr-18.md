Postcards from the Heart: Selections from the Brigitte and Henning Freybe Collection
celebrates the Freybes extraordinary contributions as enthusiastic modern and
contemporary art patrons, featuring over 30 works by renowned local and international
artists such as Robert Rauschenberg, Julie Mehretu, and Jeff Wall. This exhibition
offers a glimpse into five decades of modern and contemporary art, highlighting the
Freybes deep engagement with the Vancouver art scene.

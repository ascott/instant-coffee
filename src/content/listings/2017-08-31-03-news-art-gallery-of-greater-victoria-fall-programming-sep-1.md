Explore art and ideas at the AGGV this September

Victoria, BC - The Art Gallery of Greater Victorias ever-popular House Tour of unique 
and architecturally stunning Victoria homes is just around the corner. Tickets for the sell-
out event go on sale Friday, September 1. Tickets for the September 24 event are $35 
and will be available at the AGGV front desk, GardenWorks, Dig This, Ivy's Bookshop, 
Munro's Books and at aggv.ca.

If youre in Victoria on the last long weekend of the summer, the Gallery is open all 
weekend including Labour Day Monday, September 4, from noon until 5:00 p.m.

The first Tuesday of the month is always Admission by Donation Day. Mark September 
5 on your calendar and make this your excuse to spend a morning (or afternoon) 
exploring the galleries.

The AGGVs new Water Work Space is part exhibition and part workshop, and opens 
with a public open house (supported by the Gallery Associates) and admission by 
donation on Saturday, September 16. The space explores issues related to water"
water as a resource, a site of trade and exchange, and water in relation to climate 
change.

Save the date for Party for our People on Thursday, September 21, from 6:00p.m. to 
9:00 p.m. and celebrate with local and regional artists featured in our current exhibitions: 
Picturing the Giants: The Changing Landscapes of Emily Carr, Moving Forward by 
Looking Back: The First 30 Years of Collecting Art at the AGGV, Close to Home: Local 
and Regional Artists in the AGGV Collection and Water Work Space. Please join us for 
music, refreshments and interactive exhibition activities. Everybody welcome!

The third annual fundraiser in support of the AGGV, ART + FARE 3 is a celebration of all 
things local. Held at and organized by the Union Club of British Columbia, the event 
takes place September 23, from 7:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m., and tickets are available at 
artandfare.com.

Two popular exhibitions close in September. Karen Tam: With wings like clouds hung 
from the sky closes on Sept. 4. Artist Karen Tam explores an artist named Lee Nam, a 
Chinese immigrant to British Columbia at the turn of the 20th century"known solely 
through the journals of Emily Carr. Tam re-imagines Lee Nam's painting studio, on 
Cormorant Street in Victoria's Chinatown to evoke the presence of an unknown artist.

September 24 is the last day to see Close to Home, which features a variety of work by 
artists from Victoria and the surrounding region tells many fascinating stories of the 
cultural life and legacies of this place.

There are three exhibition tours offered in September, the cost is included with 
admission:
September 2, 2:00 p.m., Moving Forward by Looking Back
September 3, 2:00 p.m., Close to Home
September 5, 2:00 p.m., Close to Home"Curators Tour

For more information, calendar of events and tickets, visit www.aggv.ca or call 250-384-
4171.

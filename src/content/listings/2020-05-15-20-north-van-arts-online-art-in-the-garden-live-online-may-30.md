Art in the Garden Live Online
Saturday & Sunday, May 30, 31
7pm
FREE
Art in the Garden has been re-imagined online, for the whole month of May. North Van
Arts wants to inspire the art enthusiast in all of us through a fusion of local music, art
and nature. Follow them during the month of May on Facebook, Instagram and Youtube,
and on their website as they release videos of local musicians and artists in a series of
'Art in the Garden Shorts'. Subscribe to their YouTube channel for weekly videos until
May 30th/31st. The last weekend of May, tune in at 7PM both Saturday and Sunday,
because each night a different musician and visual artist are paired up. The artists will
live-stream from their gardens and collaborate on Instagram.

Were celebrating every kind of garden (that includes balcony gardens and house plant
jungles)!

'Artist in the Garden Shorts' Released to date

Monday, May 4 - music video with Fraser
Henderson

Tuesday, May 5 - artist talk/interview with Nicola Morgan

Friday, May 8 - video from visual artist Tiffany Blaise

Monday, May 11 - music video with Chris Dietrich
Tuesday, May 12 - artist talk/interview with Pierre Leichner

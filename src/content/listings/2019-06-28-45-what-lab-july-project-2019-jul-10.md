July Project is a creative and cooperative workshop series hosted by What Lab, a new 
artist-run space in East Vancouver. We invite artists to participate in a month-long series 
for people to create, share, edit, and present any artistic project of their choice. Any 
payment is by donation. 

Participants meet weekly to share in their individual progress and hold each other 
accountable for the work done through inspiration and critique. The process ends with a 
showcase, open to the public, celebrating the work created over the previous month.

Meetings will be held Wednesdays at 7pm from July 10th through August 7th.

To find out more or to register, visit whatlab.ca/july-project-2019/

MOVIN ON UP:

Staircase Theatre presents the 4th annual installment of Movin On Up, which will feature two 
brand new one act plays by local, emerging playwrights, Cara Norrish (Sad Mommies Group) 
and Melody Anderson (Ditto). 

The two plays have been dramaturgd by Sherry MacDonald and will be directed by local 
Melissa Oei will feature the acting talents of Erla Faye Forsyth, Corina Akeson, Jess Amy 
Shead, Markian Tarasiuk, and Veenu Sandhu. The night will also feature a large silent auction, 
as well as a bar, food and fun music! 

Venue: CBC Studio 700, 
700 Hamilton Street

DRAWING EXP'08 CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

This year's Drawing Expo will take place in mid April at Little Mountain studios! Last year was a great success at The Gaff and I am very pleased to be continuing this event! Here's the deal:

Drawing Exp'08 seeks artists who work in the field of drawing. This is to be a large and diverse group showing, incorporating many styles, and approaches to drawing. While Drawing Exp'08 will mainly be a showing of works on paper, artists incorporating drawing into other mediums are encouraged to submit as well. 

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES:

Please sent images w/ notes on medium and dimensions in jpg format (no larger than 200k) to gaffgallery@gmail.com

cv or statement about work is optional. 

replies will only be send to artists accepted into the show, please don't take it personally~

DEADLINE: MARCH 25th

Curated by Gabriel Deerman

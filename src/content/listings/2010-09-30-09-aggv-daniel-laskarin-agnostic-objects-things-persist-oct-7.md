Doubt, Intrigue and the Undefinable: AGGV Showcases Local Victoria Sculptor

September 27, 2010 (Victoria, BC) - The Art Gallery of Greater Victoria
will present the work of Victoria sculptor Daniel Laskarin in his first
survey exhibition, Daniel Laskarin: Agnostic Objects (things persist).

Daniel Laskarin: Agnostic Objects (things persist) opens Oct. 8 for a four
month run at the AGGV. The show features 20 of Laskarin’s sculptures,
created over the past decade, including several new works from 2010.
Laskarin, a former helicopter pilot and engineer uses the skills from his
former occupations to find new ways of manipulating and using industrial
materials in his work.

URBANITE: Inspired by Daniel Laskarin’s Agnostic Objects (things persist).

With artists Eden Veaudry and Justin Love.

Eden Veaudry and Justin Love will engage in a live drawing & painting
performance that transforms a canvas into an emergent interface for
manipulating sound and video. Sensors under the canvas record the sounds
and gestures of Love and Veaudry while the traces of graphite and paint
they leave on the canvas become part of a "living" circuit that is
incorporated into a hand-made electronic instrument. A live video feed of
the performance will be projected onto a large screen in a special
performance during the evening.

Daniel Laskarin will also be touring his exhibit with the public.

Saturday, July 6

Person/ne Forum: Ethics of Care

Griffin Art Projects, 1174 Welch St, North Vancouver, BC V7P 2R5

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Griffin Residency Program will host as part of its residency program a group of 
international writers, researchers, curators and artists, who explore topics of political 
agency, personhood and care.

Featured participants:

Emily Rosamond (Artist and Researcher, Visual Cultures Department, Goldsmiths 
College London, UK) considers character in the age of Big Data

Lorenzo Fusi (Artistic Director, Fondation Prince Pierre de Monaco, Independent 
Curator, London, UK) considers care in relation to the treatment of HIV/AIDS in North 
America

Helena Reckitt (Curator and Researcher, Art Department, Goldsmiths College, London, 
UK) explores methods of collaboration and collectivity from her work on the Feminist 
Durations project at SPACE, London, UK.

Zoe Kreye (Artist, Vancouver) designs a relational & collaborative event for Forum 
participants

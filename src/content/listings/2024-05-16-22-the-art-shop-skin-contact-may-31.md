City Center Artist Lodge

2111 Main St, Lobby

The Art Shop presents, SKIN CONTACT, a group show that explores the tangible yet
transient ways we interact with each other and the world. Finding inspiration in the
process of skin contact wine, where winemakers allow the grapes skin to macerate
with its juices for a prolonged period of time, and the bonding act of skin to skin
contact - the show reflects the many possible relationships between the body and
the artwork.

Contemplating the physicality of the body, works in the exhibition look to the sense of
touch to find the subtle fleeting ways we experience change, exploring how physical
touch can alter us in microscopic, emotional, and
chemical ways. Artists explore how
physical connection can create profoundly meaningful moments and its influence
shaping our view of ourselves and our environment through the tangible process of
art making (weaving, sculpting, tattooing) or reflect on the body as the subject in the
artwork itself (drawing, painting, photographing). SKIN CONTACT aims to find
connections in different perspectives of how we physically interact with the world
around us through art

Works in SKIN CONTACT also resonate with the process of becoming, as our
bodies are a tangible and physical representation of the passing of time. Offering
different perspectives on how we understand ourselves and our bodies, the exhibit
aims to find parallels with processes of fermentation where the body is ever
changing and growing with the environment it is surrounded by.

Participating artists: Ana Diab, Hillary Webb, Sara Khan, Ava Ross, Kira Buro, Rydel
Cerezo, Wendy Hanlon, and Danya Gorodetsky

Ezra Gray
"Maximum Yearn"
18 June - 9 August 2020

Ezra Gray (b. 1986) lives and works in Vancouver. He completed his MA in painting
from the Royal College of Art (London) and BFA from Concordia University
(Montreal). Select solo exhibitions include
"Joech God," Emalin, London (2018);
"Brown Space," Almanac Inn, Turin & "Infinitive," Jeffrey Stark, New York City (both
2017). Select two person & group exhibitions include "Folly," Emalin Projects,
Dunmore Pineapple, Scotland; "Honolulu Triennale with Sean Nicholas Savage,"
Honolulu, Zurich (both 2016) & "Rundgang," Kunstakademie Dsseldorf (2014).

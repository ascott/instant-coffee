LEIGH TENNANT

CURATED BY tarl

JUNE 4-JULY 23, 2016
Opening June 3, 8 PM

LEIGH TENNANT'S practice is fugitive, ceaselessly metabolizing and
re-metabolizing trans, feminist, and economic discourses. They seek to
enunciate autonomous feminine signs and consider how trans might mark the
return of the excessively feminine under a different name. Speaking with
the history and materiality of painting, they explore what transgender
embodiments do to the sexual archaeology of signification. Tennant was a
founding member of the Vancouver project space TopDown BottomUp, active
from 2011-13. They hold a BA in art history and an MFA in visual art from
The University of British Columbia. Tennant lives and works in Vancouver.

tarl
tarl is an unprofessional collaborative group that facilitates solo
exhibitions. We have no schedule. We believe the social-discursive field of
art precedes institutionalization. Our programming pursues less familiar
execution paths through the systems and structures that we encounter.

77th Steveston Salmon Festival

July 1, 2024

The City of Richmond, Steveston Community Society and Richmond Agricultural and
Industrial Society present the 77th annual Steveston Salmon Festival on July 1,
2024. Taking place across Steveston Village, Canadas biggest little birthday party
celebrates the community with a show-stopping parade, live music, family-friendly
activities, and much more. Foodies will want to try the iconic Salmon Bake " a
savoury, barbecued salmon filet that has become a Steveston favourite " a bowl of
tasty chow mein, or fluffy stacks
of pancakes for early rising parade-goers. Stages
set across the village will feature music all-day, headlined by the six-piece dance
band Dr. Strangelove and Midnight Cruiser Band, which offers 70s and 80s rock
with a modern twist. Roving entertainers like jugglers and stilt walkers will keep
crowds amused, as festival goers explore the Art Show, Japanese Culture Show,
and activities at Britannia Shipyards National Historic Site.

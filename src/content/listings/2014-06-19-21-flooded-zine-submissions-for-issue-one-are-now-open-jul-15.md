Flooded zine is an ode to the process, presentation and preservation of 
colour photography. There is no theme for the issue. The zine will feature 
20 - 25 emerging photographers from anywhere around the world. It will be 
printed in full colour for September 2014. Flooded is curated and published 
in Victoria, British Columbia. 

Submissions for Issue one are now open!

The deadline to submit is July 15th, 2014.

Submission Guidelines:

All images must be colour film.

Email me up to 12 images or a link to your website. Please include links 
directly to bodies of work/images that are both film and colour.

No digital photography will be accepted.

(Please note that each issue has approximately 20-25 photographers in it. 
Due to the quantity of contributors, and cost of printing in colour, I cannot 
offer a free zine to those included, but will offer the issues at cost.)

Please write "Issue One Submission" in the subject line of your email.

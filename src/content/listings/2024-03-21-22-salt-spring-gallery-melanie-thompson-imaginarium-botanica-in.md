There will be an opening reception Apr. 12, 5-7, artist in attendance
Salt Spring Gallery, Salt Spring Island, 135 Mc Phillips Ave.
The exhibition will run from Apr. 12-May 1, Tues.-Sat. 10-4.

Using a collection of botanical and found materials Melanie has made an installation
creating an imaginary space, an Imaginarium Botanica. This imaginarium uses found and
collected botanical materials such as seed pods, leaves, sticks and bark plus many other
materials. A wide variety of techniques from basketry, stitch, 3d sculpture, printmaking to
paint and cyanotype were used to create the work. This environment encourages viewers
to view botanical detritus with new eyes.
This project is necessary
now because we are in dire need of work that encourages us to
appreciate our natural environment with a view to saving it. To see that not only is there
beauty in the living natural habitat but also in the decay and detritus of this environment.

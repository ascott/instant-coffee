Scott Kemp
Master and Apprentice, Lobster and Leaf
October 21, 2016 to November 18, 2017
Opening: Friday, October 21, 7-11pm

Information appears rigid. Like shoulder pads in a business suit it 
implies strength by its form. Like shoulder pads in a business suit it 
can't really guarantee anything. Information is a flip-flopping 
wannabe that has no right to the position of authority it so often 
presumes.

Master and Apprentice, Lobster and Leaf is an exhibition of two and 
three dimensional work by Scott Kemp.

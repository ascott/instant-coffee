SCREENING | THURS MARCH 30 | 7PM 

Elephants: 70s Video from the International Exchange Network

Elephants features excerpts of video from the collection at the Crista Dahl Media 
Library & Archive, specifically those collected at the 1973 Matrix Conference and 
global works circulated through the Satellite Video Exchange Societys first Video 
Exchange Tape Catalogue.

The videos span genres and subject matter: activist dispatches including a 
message from the Black Panthers compound in Algeria explaining their bust of 
Timothy Leary ; the counter-culture's most renowned anti-fascist conspiracy 
researcher, Mae Brussell, weighs in on the visible and invisible governments, 
CIA, and Watergate; the status of Leonard Peltier's trial and expedition; 
conceptual, real-time video by Brian MacNevin (NSCAD) and Fujiko Nakaya 
(founder of Japan's first video centre, Video Hiroba); manipulation of video 
technologies and exploration of real time computer systems and video 
synthesizers by artists, and more to be announced.

ARCHIVAL EXHIBIT | THURS MARCH 30, 31; APRIL 4-8; 11-6PM
of cabbages & catalogues: Matrix and the Satellite Video Exchange Society

This exhibition looks at the 1971 Video Exchange Network initiative of Michael 
Goldberg under the auspices of Image Bank; the first international conference of 
non-commercial videomakers, MATRIX (1973); and the subsequent formation of 
Satellite Video Exchange Society and the establishment of the Video Inn Library. 
Featuring original textual materials, audio recordings, photographs by Kazumi 
Tanaka and Murray Skuce, and videos from 1970-1977.

Ultimate Traveller: Impressions of India Part 2: The Himalayas, India's Northeast & 
Eastern India

Wednesday, February 13, 2019 7"9 p.m.

Ferry Building Gallery

1414 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver, BC V7T 1C2

Join us for a series of imaginative travel talks on faraway places by geographer and 
travel photographer Peter Langer. Discover the wealth of human history, the natural 
beauty of our planet and explore a diversity of cultures. Experience the magic of 
these fascinating worlds through a beautiful audio-visual journey.

You will also hear Peter's impressions, curious anecdotes, frustrations and some of 
the little secrets these places have to offer. Peter Langer has travelled to over 175 
countries.

Join internationally renowned photographer Peter Langer on a journey through the 
Himalayas, the Northeast and Eastern India. We will explore India from a historic 
and cultural point of view, discovering the subcontinent's art, architecture, geography 
and magnificent cultural heritage, including the religions of the various civilizations 
that have occupied the land. In the second of this three part series, we explore 
Himachal Pradesh, Dharamsala, Lahaul-Spiti, Ladakh, Kashmir, Darjeeling, Sikkim, 
and Arunachal Pradesh in the Indian Himalayas. We then head to the remarkable 
Northeast of India, visiting the states of Assam, Meghalaya and Nagaland, and travel 
in the footsteps of Buddha in Bihar, visit Kolkata and admire exceptional temples in 
Odisha. You will gain a deeper understanding of India's subtleties, especially 
valuable if you're a potential traveller there.

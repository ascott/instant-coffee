The Bakery is pleased to present "Metempsychosis" a series of eighteen allegorical 
paintings by artist Jessica Yeandle-Hignell, exploring the feelings of longing and 
agitation as opportunities to transcend the self. The paintings further develop "The Story 
of Mira M.*" an imagined storyworld shaped by influences as diverse as Rumi, Julia 
Kristeva, Philip K. Dick and David Lynch. It is a world of embodied paradoxes whose 
relationships, attractions and repulsions tell the story of an urge toward a unified self, 
albeit one that is composed of seemingly irreconcilable fragments.

Jessica Yeandle-Hignell is a Vancouver-based artist with a background as a contractual 
sculptor, painter and illustrator. With diverse media she works serially and embraces a 
nonlinear style of story-telling. Taking inspiration from heterodox religious sources and 
accounts of paranormal experience, the subject of her work is often an exploration of 
tension within personal, erotic and psycho-social experience. She earned her BFA from 
the Alberta College of Art and Design in 2008 and has exhibited most recently at 
Dynamo Arts Association, Unit/Pitt and Black & Yellow.

THE BAKERY is an artist run gallery located in East Vancouver. Programming without 
intention of profit or any fixed curatorial ideology. We aim to showcase exclusively the 
work of emerging artists, both local and international.

HINTERLANDS
Ann Ashton, Randall Finnerty, Paul Morstad, Craig Welch

GALLERY JONES is pleased to announce Hinterlands, a special exhibition of
work by The Groundhogs collective. In 2007, Anne Ashton, Randall Finnerty,
Paul Morstad and Craig Welch came together as artists to exhibit under the
moniker The Groundhogs. Eleven years later, they reunite for an exhibition
that grows ever more relevant as time passes: an examination of the natural
realm as it falls under the influence of human encroachment.

Opening reception: Thursday, September 13, 5 - 8 p.m.
Exhibition dates: September 6 - October 6, 2018

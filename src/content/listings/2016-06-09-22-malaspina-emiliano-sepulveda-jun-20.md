Artist talk: Emiliano Sepulveda

Monday, June 20, at 3:34pm

Malaspina Printmakers, 1555 Duranleau Street, Vancouver

On the occasion of the summer solstice, please join invited artist Emiliano
Sepulveda for an artist talk considering the relationship of printmaking to
the world and the body, and the moment and the past.
Emiliano Sepulveda is an artist, who is from many places, that are
connected to many histories that are connected to many places and people
who are from or connected to many places as well, even when they're not.
His identity, entangled with places near and far, and the identity of
places near and far, is what motivates his artistic practice. Deeply
distrustful of artist bios, which he believes offer up nothing of use or
substance, Sepulveda instead offers these words:

Time moves one - Huitzilopochtli looks over my shoulder
Quetzalcoatl looks at his watch
"habla"
a scratch parallax
an awl a ray
a scrawl a star
an act that marks a walk
AZTLAN attracts
an anagram
la raza maps

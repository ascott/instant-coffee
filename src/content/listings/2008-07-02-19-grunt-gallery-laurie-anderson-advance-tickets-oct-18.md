Laurie Anderson's Homeland is a series of songs and stories that create a poetic and political 
portrait of contemporary American culture. Conceived as one long piece of music, the stories and 
songs that make up Homeland are marked by a political urgency. They address the current 
climate of fear, obsession with information and security. The music, built on a foundation of 
groove electronics and featuring many new melodic forms with which Laurie has been 
experimenting, is truly revelatory. They are also - as with all of her work - personal and utterly 
unique.

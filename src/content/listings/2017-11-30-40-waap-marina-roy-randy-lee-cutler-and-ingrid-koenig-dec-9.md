MARINA ROY
TALK: Sat, Dec 9, 2 PM

artist in conversation with
RANDY LEE CUTLER and
INGRID KOENIG

Wil Aballe Art Projects | WAAP
688 East Hastings St. (lower level)
Vancouver, BC V6A 1R1
waapart.com
+1 604 800 2035

in conjunction with
Dirty Clouds
Exhibition: Nov 16 - Dec 16, 2017
Hours: Tue-Sat, noon to 5 PM

Dirty Clouds is an exhibition by Marina Roy that came about from the collaborative 
research project (SSHRC) that Randy Lee Cutler and Ingrid Koenig spearheaded, 
entitled Leaning out of Windows. The research project interfaced artists with 
physicists at TRIUMF UBC. On December 9th at 2 PM at WAAP, these three artist 
collaborators will discuss the evolution of this particularly unique project.

RANDY LEE CUTLER is a Vancouver based writer, artist and educator. In the 
intersections of gender, art, science, and technology, her practice takes up themes of 
materiality and sustenance. Randy is an associate professor at Emily Carr University 
in the Faculty of Art on the unceded Coast Salish territories also known as 
Vancouver, Canada.

INGRID KOENIG is Artist in Residence at TRIUMF, Canadas particle accelerator 
centre. In her Vancouver based studio practice she traverses the fields of theoretical 
physics, social history, and narrative of science through visual art and relational 
projects. She is an associate professor at Emily Carr University.

MARINA ROY is a Vancouver-based artist. She is associate professor in visual art at 
the University of British Columbia.

Phil Woolf: For How Long Have I Been Asleep
Record Launch and Performance

February 21 at 7 pm

READ is pleased to present a performance by Phil Woolf on the occasion of 
the launch of his debut vinyl record album For How Long Have I Been Asleep.

Woolf is a musician and sound designer who lived in Australia and China 
before settling in Vancouver. He is currently a fourth year student in the film 
program at Emily Carr. Phil received training in alto saxophone for years 
before picking up guitar. Now he plays as a solo musician and records from 
his bedroom most of the time. Recently Woolf received the Jake C. Kerr 
Chancellor Award for Excellence for For How Long Have I Been Asleep. 

Please join us. Admission is free of charge. For How Long Have I Been 
Asleep will be available at a special launch price!

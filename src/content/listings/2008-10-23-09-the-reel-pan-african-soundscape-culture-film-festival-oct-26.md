The Reel Pan-AfriCAN SoundsCAPE
Interactive Experience with Canada's
Vancouver Pan-African Arts, Culture & Film Festival (www.vpaff.org
<http://www.vpaff.org> )
Date: Sunday, Oct. 26, 2008
Time: 10:00 pm - 12:00 am Cape Town &
1:00 - 3:00 pm Vancouver
Synopsis:
Using internet video technology to connect 'sister audiences' in Cape Town,
South Africa, and Vancouver, Canada, this 2-hour extravaganza of
interactive film, music, dance and dialogue will feature 5 short films and
live musical performances showcasing world-class singer/songwriters from
across the African continent who have all made Cape Town their home (Adeson
Nchimbi-Tanzania, Sylvestre Kabassidi-Congo-Brazzaville, Xixel
Langa-Mozambique, Dingiswayo Juma-Zimbabwe and Adamu-Angola).

These films were directed & produced by Cape Town-based Canadian filmmaker,
Sarah Van Borek, and include the film, Xenophobia Unplugged: Featuring
Adeson Nchimbi, which recently screened in the Montreal World Film
Festival, the Calgary International Film Festival and has been officially
selected by the Canadian Broadcasting Corporation's Radio Canada
International for its online Migrations competition.

Each musician addresses pressing issues of immigration, xenophobia,
multiculturalism and the role of the artist in promoting cross-cultural
understanding...issues important to our diverse local and international
communities. Each film will serve as catalyst to lively discussion between
the audiences in Canada and South Africa, facilitated on each side by a
vibrant MC. As each dialogue session wraps up, get ready to sing and dance
in a global, interactive expression of African Unity and International
Cooperation, lead from the Vancouver venue by South African
dancer/choreographer, Bheki Ndlovu (productions include Bafana the movie,
Sarafina! and Amakhosi).

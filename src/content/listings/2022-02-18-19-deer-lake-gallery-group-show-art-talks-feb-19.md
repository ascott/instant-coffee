Art Talks

February 19-March 27

Group Show

Featured Artists: Carolyn Sullivan, Dorothy Doherty, Ellen Pelto, Faye Gordon, Julie
Pappajohn, Olga Campbell, Sande Waters, Terry Rammell, Vicki Scudamore

The work in Art Talks' reflects how the Covid experience resulted in artists meeting on
line through a Responsive Neighbourhood Small Grant. The group evolved out of a
desire to stay connected with other artists, the art world and the world in general during
the pandemic.

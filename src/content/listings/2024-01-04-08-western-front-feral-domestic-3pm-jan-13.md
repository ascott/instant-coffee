Feral Domestic
Dani and Sheilah ReStack
Jan 13 - Apr 6, 2024
Opening Reception: Jan 13, 2024, 3:00 - 5:00 p.m.

Western Front is pleased to present a solo exhibition by Dani and Sheilah ReStack,
who use video, drawing, and photography to contemplate queer desire, family, and
collaboration in a time of planetary crisis. The exhibition features a multi-channel
installation of the video trilogy "Feral Domestic" (2017"22) and three artist books of
drawings, writings, and images related to each
work, which together document the
artists ongoing interest in the domestic as a space of creative possibility.

For more information, visit westernfront.ca/

Richmond Culture Days

Sept. 20-Oct. 13, 2024

The 15th annual edition of Culture Days returns to Richmond from Sept. 20"Oct. 13,
2024. Richmond is consistently recognized as one of the top 10 most engaged cities
in the country during Culture Days, an event that provides barrier-free opportunities
for the public to participate in arts and culture experiences. This years celebration
features more than 80 free activities, including community quilt-making, clay wheel-
throwing demonstrations, Chinese calligraphy workshops, dance classes, open
studios, film screenings, and much more. The opening weekend of Sept. 21"22 will
kick off with a series of interactive
artist-led activities at the Richmond Cultural
Centre and Richmond Cultural Centre Annex.

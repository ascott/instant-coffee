JORDY HAMILTON
A BIT DAFT; SORRY BUT
CLOSING DAY RECEPTION
Saturday, July 12, 2014, 5 pm
in conjunction with The Flats Block Party

Wil Aballe Art Projects
528-2050 Scotia St.
Buzz 189
Text 778 229 3458

Before heading to Gallery 295 for the Scott Billings opening starting at 6
p.m., please stop by WAAP to see Jordy Hamilton's show on its last day. A
small selection from a larger body of new paintings, this show stems from a
single image cartoon of Popeye The Sailor Man. The cartoon’s relation to
the depiction of time, repetition, violence, and love inspired the series.
Light refreshments will be served.

Jordy Hamilton completed an MFA at the University of British Columbia in
2011. In 2012, he was a finalist in the RBC Painting Competition, as well
as in the Contemporary Art Society of Vancouver’s inaugural emerging
artist’s prize. Jordy currently lives and works in Vancouver. Within his
art practice, painting has functioned as the primary mode in a multifaceted
approach to making art. Sculpture, film, photography and installation have
been produced at times alongside a more traditional approach. Recent
bodies of work have developed around themes such as language, time, work,
craft, desire and escape.

TALKS at the Vancouver Art Gallery

Tuesday February 22

6-9pm

LAUNCH of The Capilano Review MANIFESTOS NOW!

7-8pm

CURATORS’ TOUR of WE: Vancouver

The Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to present two events in 
conjunction with WE: Vancouver – 12 Manifestos for the City.

1.

The launch of a special edition of the Capilano Review, 
MANIFESTOS NOW!

In celebration of WE: Vancouver and the latest issue of the Capilano 
Review, selected writers will energize the gallery in a series of 
performative readings of manifestos.

The Capilano Review is a magazine that has published innovative 
literary and artistic works for more than thirty years. Alongside 
international writers and artists, the Capilano Review provides an 
arena for new and established Canadian writers and artists 
experimenting with or expanding the boundaries of conventional 
forms and contexts. The publication continues to confront readers 
with the risky, the provocative, the innovative, and the dissident.

Readings by Colin Browne, Reg Johanson, Nikki Reimer, Kim 
Minkus, Sheila Ross, participants from UBC’s HUM Writing course, 
and more. 

2.

Curators’ Tour of WE: Vancouver

Join curators Bruce Grenville and Kathleen Ritter for an in-depth tour 
of WE: Vancouver as they highlight some of the different voices that 
make up the exhibition.

WE: Vancouver –12 Manifestos for the City examines Vancouver 
through the extraordinary range of practices, actions and ideas that 
shape and activate it.

Architects offer diverse ways for us to occupy our built environment. 
Designers invite us to consider how we consume and interact with 
the world around us. Artists reconfigure the city, revealing new ways 
to see and be seen. Activists unsettle our patterns of engagement, 
indicating new pathways. Planners shift the very ground we walk on, 
proposing new models for communities. This exhibition gives form 
and voice to these many actions.

The exhibition includes the work of more than 40 Vancouver-based 
cultural producers, including Althea Thauberger, SHAPE, Red Flag 
Design, Keith Higgins, MGB Architecture, the Office for Soft 
Architecture, Michael Turner, Natalie Purschwitz, Robert Kleyn, Office 
Supplies Incorporated, Christian Kliegel, Sabine Bitter + Helmut 
Weber, and many others.

Free with Gallery admission.

WE: Vancouver - 12 Manifestos for the City / February 12 to May 1,

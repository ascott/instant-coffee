Lyse Lemieux studied at the University of Ottawa (1973"1975) and the University of British-
Columbia (1976"1978) where she received a BFA (1978). She has exhibited nationally and
internationally including the Canadian Cultural Centre in Rome, the Vancouver Art Gallery,
and Oakville Galleries in Toronto. Recent solo exhibitions include Painted Drawings; Wil
Aballe Art Projects, Vancouver (2019); FULL FRONTAL, No Fixed Abode, SFU Galleries
Burnaby Campus (2019-2020), Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver (2017-18); A Girls
Gotta Do What A Girls Gotta Do, Richmond Art Gallery (2016); Black Is The Size Of My
New Skirt, Republic Gallery, Vancouver (2015); In-Between-In-Between: Lyse Lemieux &
Meryl McMaster, Katzman Contemporary, Toronto (2015). Participation in recent group
exhibitions include Some Kind Of Behaviour: Robin Arseneault, Allison Hrabluik, Lyse
Lemieux, Terminal Creek Contemporary (2019); Vancouver Special: Ambivalent Pleasures,
Vancouver
Art Gallery (2016"2017); Aujourdhui Encore, Trpanier Baer Gallery (2016);
Out of Line, Oakville Galleries (2015) and Cut & Paste, Equinox Gallery (2012). Based in
Vancouver Lemieux is involved in developing public art projects, one of which was recently
completed in Vancouver and another which is scheduled for completion later this spring in
Vancouver. She has received numerous awards from the BC Arts Council and the Canada
Council and is the 2017 recipient of the prestigious VIVA Award for Outstanding
Achievement in the Visual Arts awarded annually by the Doris and Jack Shadbolt
Foundation for the Visual Arts. Upcoming exhibitions include an exhibition surveying
Lemieuxs comprehensive drawing practice at the Burnaby Art Gallery in June of this year.

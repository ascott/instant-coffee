CALL FOR SUBMISSION
Deadline for online application: March 29, 2024

Dundarave Print Workshop invites artists to participate in its first open call to artists
across Canada for a group exhibition this Spring. We will be displaying work from
selected Canadian-based artists with the intention of building relations among the
printmaking community across the country.

The theme for this years open call is The Journey. We invite artists to share their
unique perspectives on the diverse narratives that the concept of journeying embodies.
Whether capturing physical travels, exploring personal growth, or delving into
imaginative realms, "The Journey" invites you to celebrate the transformative
nature of
life's paths.

The selected artworks will be featured and on display at the Dundarave Print
Workshop gallery on Granville Island, Vancouver, BC, from April 11, 2024 to May 5,
2024. The opening party will be held on April 11, 6"9pm. Mark your calendars!

Apply here: https://www.dundaraveprintworkshop.com/opencall

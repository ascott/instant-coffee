Opening reception: Thursday, May 2, 7-9 pm

May 3-June 2, 2024

Arts Alive: (re)FOLD/(re)FIT

(Lower Gallery)

The Burnaby Art Gallery specializes in collecting and exhibiting works on paper"
sometimes going beyond prints, painting or drawing. Taking inspiration from
sculptural paper artworks within the City of Burnabys Permanent Art Collection,
senior secondary students from Grades 8-12 used paper manipulation to (re)fold or
(re)fit wearable art. In its 42nd year of partnership with School District 41, Arts Alive
celebrates the achievements of students by showcasing their diverse interpretations
of this theme.

Artist Apprenticeship: Paper Allegories

(Upper Gallery)

Artist Apprenticeship supports SD41 Grade 11 and 12 students in their creative
ambitions through mentorship from artists and arts professionals, resulting in an
exhibition of their own at the Burnaby Art
Gallery. Now in its 16th year, this years
program saw students push their artistic practices beyond the paper, creating
innovative artworks in response to selections from the City of Burnaby Permanent
Art Collection.

The Burnaby Art Gallery would like to acknowledge the time and dedication of all
participating students, teachers, and career counsellors. These exhibitions are made
possible through the generous support of the City of Burnaby, BC Arts Council and
the Burnaby School District.

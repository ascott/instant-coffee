Exhibition Tour: Grant Arnold and Artists
Pictures From Here
Saturday May 27, 3pm
In the Gallery, 2nd floor

Curator Grant Arnold tours Pictures From Here with some of the artists in the exhibition. 
Arnold will talk about approaches to photography that developed in British Columbia 
over the last several decades, while the artists elaborate on their specific artworks.

Participating artists: Ian Wallace, Arni Haraldsson, Christos Dikeakos, Marian Penner 
Bancroft, Henri Robideau, Cornelia Wyngaarden, Barrie Jones, Paul Wong & Sandra 
Semchuk.

Free for Members or with Gallery Admission.

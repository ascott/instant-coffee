LOCK UP YOUR SONS & DAUGHTERS

In 2001, curator Bill Taylor created a hilarious and chilling short film
series of anti-gay propaganda films from the 1950’s to the 1990’s. 
Perversion for Profit, Boys Beware! and Red Light, Green Light and many
more were an instant hit and Bill took the series around North America and
was a hit at the Just For Laughs Festival!

The provocative short films series continued on a whirlwind tour until July
3, 2003 when Bill, happy but burnt out, returned home.

Bill said, “If I had to pick three highlights of the roughly two years on
the road, they would be:

1) Playing in Rochester, New York, as part of their Gay and Lesbian Film
Festival there. Big highlight, as one of the world's biggest film archives
is stored there, and I got a private tour. It was so breathtakingly
beautiful to be in that room with all that film history;

2) On a related note, when the Association of Moving Image Archivists had
their annual convention in Vancouver, I was asked to put the show on for
them as a screening night for them. I was so incredibly honoured (and
nervous, as many of my heroes were in that screening room that night); and,

3) The Ottawa Gay and Lesbian Film Festival invited me to do a fundraiser
for them, and I agreed. The organizers of the screening made the screening
an OUTDOOR screening at a common mall area. So that was amazing to
witness, the shorts playing for everyone passing by to see.â€ 

The time has come experience the hilarity again. 

On Tuesday, June 7, 2011 at Pacific Cinematheque (1131 Howe St.) at 7:30pm,
Lock Up Your Sons & Daughters, curated by Bill Taylor, returns! There will
be one show only as a special fundraiser for the Bob Loblaw Queer Arts
Society. Tickets are $15 and can be reserved through email at

OTHER SIGHTS IS HIRING A COOP STUDENT 

Other Sights is seeking a fall Coop Placement Student to assume the responsibilities of 
an Assistant to support curatorial projects that address the politics of public space and 
assume responsibilities that further the administrative goals of the Other Sights 
Producer Team. The Assistant will be given a thorough orientation to Other Sights 
operations, shadow the work of the General Manager, and work closely with project 
curators, artists and participants through phases of research, planning, coordination, 
presentation, communication, public outreach and documentation. The Assistant will 
have the opportunity to work on three Other Sights Projects during their placement. The 
Assistant will also participate in biweekly producer meetings and workshops where they 
will develop a strong understanding of the dynamics and collaborative nature of this 
collective organization.

Curatorial and Administrative Assistant for Other Sights for Artists Projects - COOP 
POSITION - Fall 2019

Applicants should:

Have a strong interest in contemporary art and curatorial practices and in artist-run-
centre culture
Be curious, independent, efficient, and organized
Be keen to explore inventive approaches to public programming
Have excellent verbal and written communication skills
Demonstrate interests in working directly with diverse publics and in working 
collaboratively
Have proficiency with photo documentation, Word, Excel, Photoshop
Have experience with blogs, Facebook, Twitter, and website updating
Duties and responsibilities will include:

Administrative Support

Develop, create and consolidate the Other Sights press and print ephemera archive 
both online and in print
Develop and execute social media campaigns for The Future is Floating Project and the 
FLOOD Project
Assist with ongoing communications and media promotion for the Blue Cabin Floating 
Artist Residency
Document and share Other Sights events and activities online, and update project 
pages on the OS website
Update and maintain project management timelines for projects and take meeting 
minutes as needed
Oversee MailChimp announcements and online promotion of OS fall programming
Curatorial Support

Work with General Manager to oversee project production schedules for FLOOD and 
The Future is Floating
Work with General Manager and Other Sights Producers to develop project related 
event programming
Assist with final reporting to funders
The Assistants main point of contact will be Other Sights General Manager who will 
oversee, evaluate and report on the Students term of employment. **Please note, it is 
requested that the successful applicant continues to work reduced hours (10-12hrs/wk) 
from December 14 until January 28th, 2019.

Hourly rate: $18

Start & End Dates: September 9 - January 24, 2018

30hrs/wk from Sep 9 - Dec 13, 12rs/wk for Dec 16 - Jan 24 - Total Placement Hours: 
480

**Only students in COOP Programs are eligible for this posting.

To apply, send a CV and Cover Letter to sunshine@othersights.ca Application deadline:

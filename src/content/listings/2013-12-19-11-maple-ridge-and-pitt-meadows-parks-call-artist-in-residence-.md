Call for Proposals
Artist in Residence Program - Haney Residence
Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows Parks and Leisure Services
Maple Ridge, BC
Eligibility: Open to Canadian artists in all disciplines
Deadline: January 13, 2014

The Artist in Residence Program in Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows invites 
Canadian artists (individuals or creative teams) in all disciplines including 
writers, composers, visual artists, musicians, poets, choreographers, theatre 
artists, video, multi-disciplinary and performance artists to submit proposals 
for the 2014-2016 Haney Residence. The program supports artists and the 
community on topics of interest or concern and provides the artist(s) with 
subsidized access to studio and/or live in space for a period of time.

Detailed information about the call can be found at
http://www.mapleridge.ca/EN/main/visitors/1682/public_art.html

Please direct any questions to Yvonne Chui at

Winter Solstice Festival at the Museum
Saturday, December 21, 11 A.M. - 3 P.M. PT

The Winter Solstice Festival, also known as Dongzhi , falls on the
longest night
of the year and celebrates the arrival of winter. Celebrated on December 21 this
year, many families will gather to enjoy dumplings, tangyuan, and hot pot; and also
honour ancestors.

Welcome the winter season together with us at the museum! Drop in for a family-
friendly craft activity where you can create your own snow globe featuring a
miniature boba tea or rice cooker to take home. Plus, enjoy a taste of tangyuan, a
warm treat perfect for the cold season and a staple of the Winter Solstice Festival.
Crafts and tastings are included with museum admission and available from 11 A.M.
to 3 P.M., while supplies last. You dont want to miss out on this one!

Event Details:
Date: Saturday, December 21, 2024
Location: Chinese Canadian Museum (51 E Pender St)
Time: 11 A.M. - 3 P.M.
Cost: Included with admission

Sunday, January 12, 10:00 A.M. PT

Didnt get a chance to visit our landmark exhibition in Vancouver? Take a
special,
online tour of the exhibition with curator, Catherine Clement.

In this final event, we will also sum up what happened during the 18 months that the

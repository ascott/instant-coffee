Joan-Carlisle Irving Lecture Series 2008-9

The Politics of Materiality and Matters of the Biopolitical

Part of the Joan Carlisle Irving Lecture Series "The Politics of Materiality and Matters of the 
Biopolitical". Building on recent critical interest in subject/object relations, this lecture series brings 
together speakers who address the question from two main vantage points: how the material 
world assembles the social, and how objects become things that matter; and how, on the other 
side, art and culture respond to the forces that control the bio-political, and ultimately deal with 
that Thing which defies objective signification.

JCI Lecture: "On Spirit Writing: Materiality, Words and their Magic" by Webb Keane, Prof e ssor,

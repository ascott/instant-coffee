Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate
Richmond, BC

Pierre Vassura
New Work 1983 - 2018
December 1, 2018 - January 20, 2019
Curator: Jonathan Middleton & Eli Bornowsky

Richmond Art Gallery presents the first solo exhibition by Richmond-based artist Pierre 
Vassura. The exhibition offers an eclectic mix of sculptures, paintings and drawings highlighting 
his strong graphic style and vibrant use of colour, spanning more than 40 years of this prolific 
artists material practice.

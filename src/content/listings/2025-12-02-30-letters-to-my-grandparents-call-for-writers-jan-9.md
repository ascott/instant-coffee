Call for Writers | Letters to My (Grand)Parents | Jan 19th - May 31st, 2026
Deadline: Friday, January 9th, 2026 before 11:59pm PST

Letters to My (Grand)Parents is a 6-month intergenerational exchange between Chinese
Canadian seniors and young adults through a publication project within translated letters,
workshops, and open-mic.

We are seeking:
8 young adult writers/artists/creatives to participate in a letter exchange for folks who want to
connect with their grandparents and parents.

*Priority goes to Chinese Canadian writers and artists, LGBTQIA2S+ creatives, and writers
living with disabilities. Accepting young adults between ages 25-45, based in Metro
Vancouver.

Max. 250 words answering the question:
'What do you wish to know about your (grand)parents
' What would you share with them now?
*Written formats can include a diary entry, poem, pen pal letter, short essay, comic, etc.

$125 honorarium fee

Apply through GoogleForm at https://tr.ee/tW1-0VQfpt.

Sunday, Dec. 4th at 2:00pm

Masking Operations

A Conversation with Jordan Wilson and Raymond Boisjoly

Join us for the finissage of our exhibition Screens and Thresholds for a 
conversation with Curator/Writer Jordan Wilson and Curator/Artist Raymond 
Boisjoly. They will discuss encounters with, and understandings of, 
contemporary Indigenous cultural practices as framed by institutional 
circumstances.

Curated by Vancouver artist Raymond Boisjoly, Screens and Thresholds 
considers the impact of mediation on our understanding of history and 
experience. Diverse works in photography, video, and installation are brought 
together to examine the anxieties and possibilities in visualizing cultural 
knowledge. Screens and Thresholds features the work of Scott 
Benesiinaabandan, Tricia Livingston, Mike MacDonald, Karthik Pandian, Krista 
Belle Stewart, and Postcommodity.

Followed by seasonal treats and refreshments.

Raymond Boisjoly has exhibited widely across Canada and internationally, 
including recent solo shows at Catriona Jeffries, Vancouver; Koffler Centre, 
Toronto; and VOX, Montreal. This year Boisjoly received the VIVA Award and 
was also shortlisted for the Sobey Art Prize. He is an Assistant Professor at Emily 
Carr University of Art and Design.

Jordan Wilson is a Vancouver-based emerging curator and writer. He is of mixed 
European-Indigenous ancestry and is a member of the Musqueam First Nation. 
He holds a Masters of Arts in Anthropology and a Bachelor of Arts in First 
Nations Studies, both from the University of British Columbia. He has conducted 
research at the Smithsonian in Washington, DC, the University of Troms in the 
Spmi region of arctic Norway, and at the Indian Arts Research Center at the 
School for Advanced Research in Santa Fe, New Mexico. More recently, he was 
a co-curator of the community-based exhibit csnam, the city before the city, at 
the UBC Museum of Anthropology. Wilson is currently a Canada Council for the 
Arts Aboriginal Curator-in-Residence at MOA.

Screens and Thresholds is generously supported by The Audain Foundation.

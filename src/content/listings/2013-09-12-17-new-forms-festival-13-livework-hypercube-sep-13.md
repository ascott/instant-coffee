As part of NFF 13

Live/Work: Hypercube

World Premiere and Installation Opening
Performance/ Installation by 1024 Architecture, Reece Terris, Innes
Yates and Cedric Bomford
Friday, September 13, 2013
Great Northern Way Campus (behind the Centre for Digital Media building)
Performance begins at 10pm
FREE

Live/Work is a collaborative media performance event with French
artists Pierre Schneider & Francois Wenchel (1024 Architecture),
Vancouver artists Cedric Bomford, Innes Yates, Reece Terris, and
musician Fernando Favier. Hosted by New Forms Festival and curated by
Kate Armstrong and Malcolm Levy for Revised Projects, Live/Work is a
manifestation of interdisciplinarity, collaboration and an exploration
of the contemporary landscape in relation to changing cultural and
economic conditions.

The work begins with the form of a 10 metre cube made from scaffolding
and construction materials. In the context of contemporary Vancouver,
which is being rapidly transformed into a clashing, difficult pattern
of tiny condominiums, the shape of the cube becomes a site for
interplay and critique. Situated on the Great Northern Way Campus, the
installation becomes a site for live video projections and a musical
score that maps and responds to the site.

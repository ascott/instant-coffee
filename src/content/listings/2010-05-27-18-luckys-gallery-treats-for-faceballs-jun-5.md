ABOUT DUSTY PEAS:
James Kirkpatrick and Jamie Q are Dusty Peas, a London Ontario-based
collaborative team that
makes art in a variety of media. Their drawings, paintings, prints, and
sculptures are both calculated
and haphazard, and celebrate the generative possibilities of constructive
play and good oldfashioned
imagination. They have distributed their solo and collaborative
self-published art zines at
small press fairs in Montreal, Sackville, Toronto and San Francisco, and
via Halifax INK and the
RCAAQ at Printed Matter's New York Art Book Fair. Both artists are included
in The Cheaper Show
No. 9 at VancouverÊ¼s LES Gallery, June 26, 2010.
Jamie Q received her BFA in sculpture from ACAD in 2002. She was a member
of the Long Haul artists' collective in
Montreal for four years, and is currently completing her MFA at The
University of Western Ontario. James Kirkpatrick received
his BFA from NSCAD in 2002. His work has been shown internationally and was
included in the traveling group exhibition
Pulp Fiction, presented at the Museum of Contemporary Canadian Art in
summer 2009. He is also well known as the
graffiti artist and rapper Thesis Sahib. He has created artwork for several
album covers, and his collaborative art book with
Peter Thompson, Brain Trust, is published by Anteism.

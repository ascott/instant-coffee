Kriss Munsyas artistic practice encompasses a broad thesis of cultural identity in the face
of oppression and colonization. It is also a deeply personal query into the nature of the
Self, and the macro- and microcosmic worlds that struggle under the same influence of
history,
memory and experience. Munsyas photographs have a fantastical and
comfortably absurd tone to them and act as reconstructions of personal memory.

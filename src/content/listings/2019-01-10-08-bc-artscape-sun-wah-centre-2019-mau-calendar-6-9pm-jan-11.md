2019 Makers Artists United (MAU) Pop-Up Exhibition

The 2019 MAU Calendar is an artist and community driven publication showcasing 
the work of local artists Elisa Yon, Clare Yow, Erica Wilk, Hillary Webb, Janet Wang 
and Marlene Yuen. It features community contributed food recipes, neighbourhood 
major event listings, tea pairings from Treasure Green Tea Company and highlights 
local histories and individuals whose contributions have helped shape Chinatowns 
rich history. A limited-edition of 200 calendars were printed by Moniker Press, a 
local publishing studio specializing in Risograph printing. For this pop-up exhibition, 
artists Janet Wang, Hillary Webb, Clare Yow and Marlene Yuen will show their 
original artworks produced for the calendar project. A special edition of the calendars 
will also be available for purchase.

Makers Artists United (MAU) is a long-term artist-initiated project by Elisa Yon. 
Started in 2018, the project aims to engage local craft makers, artists and 
community members in re-imagining the way souvenirs can function as art objects 
and as vehicles to examine lesser-known local histories, cultural identities and 
issues affecting the future of Vancouvers Chinatown.

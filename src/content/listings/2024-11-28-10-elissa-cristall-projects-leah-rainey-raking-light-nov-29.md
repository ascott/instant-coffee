Location: Propellor Studio, Granville Island, Vancouver

Elissa Cristall Gallery /projects is pleased to present "Raking Light" by Toronto artist
Leah Rainey, her first exhibition in Vancouver. The collection of 12 abstract paintings
combine and develop paintings from a recent body of work, and reflect a refreshing
simplicity of line and colour. Raineys handling of the brush is delicate and precise,
allowing the thin layers of vivid colour to show through the canvas. Each line and
curve is chiseled and rendered with a
deliberate flatness. Its a distinct stylistic
choice. The forms and shapes present the illusion of cut-outs assembled into a
floating composition.

Leah Rainey is a Toronto based painter who works within a combined tradition of
process painting and direct painting. Often experimenting with found or
unconventional tools and using a range of techniques, she is interested in how
elements within painting interconnect through coincidence, suggestion and the
accidental. Leah Rainey grew up in rural Ontario. She attended Loughborough
College of Art and Design and received her BA Honours in Fine Art from Chelsea
College of Art and Design in London. Her work is in the public collections of Christ
College, Cambridge and the Sheffield City Council UK, and private collections in
North America. Rainey lives and works in Toronto, Canada.

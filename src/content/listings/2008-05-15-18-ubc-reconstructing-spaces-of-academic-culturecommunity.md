Visual Arts Gallery @ Koerner Library, UBC Point Grey| UBC by Design:
 (Re)Constructing Spaces of Academic Culture and Community| May 22-June 6


UBC by Design seeks to show -- and analyze -- how the campus at Point Grey came to be in its present form: an array of buildings still carrying the imprint of the original monumental vision but now tasked with becoming an University Town.	The larger stage of public and institutional policy is further qualified by consideration of the evolving experience of studying in the buildings and landscape of the University of British Columbia. Similarly represented is both the material impact and more ephemeral rhetorical	trace of the university administration, faculty, students, staff and alumni  upon the local scene and wider world. The institutional history as recorded through site plans, architectural renderings and archived documents is supplemented with subversions of the built environment: everyday tracings, protest projects, creative spaces and salvaged ephemera of the institutional just past. This exhibition challenges you to consider these--and other--questions: What is the
university?  Why is the design important?  Who decides its purpose and fabric?	How is it experienced?

 At 12:15 on June 2, Prof. Peter McNally will present "McGill's Role in the Development of UBC:  Altruism or Opportunism?"  (Location: Buchanan A204)


 The content of the didactic exhibition space is challenged by a series of installations, happenings and conversations to occur in Lumspace, a	student-run site of free creativity.  Von Bingen performs May 29 at  7:30.   Ian Wallace will revisit "The Idea of the University" on June 5 at 5:30.

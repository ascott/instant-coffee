International Lecture Series: Conversations on Collecting

 

Howard Rachofsky in Conversation with Bill and Ruth True

with Ian Wallace, moderator

 

Monday, February 11 at 7pm

at the Vancouver Art Gallery

 

Explore the dynamic synergy between individual collectors and public collections in this exclusive conversation with Howard Rachofsky and Bill and Ruth True, moderated by internationally acclaimed artist Ian Wallace. These collectors, philanthropists and cultural leaders exhibit their private collections in innovative ways, working with public institutions to create thriving cultural communities.

 

Howard Rachofsky’s collecting is distinguished by its concentration on arte povera and minimalist art. His acclaimed international collection of modern and contemporary art includes works by Italian artists Piero Manzoni, Alighiero Boetti and Michelangelo Pistoletto, as well as significant holdings of works by Janine Antoni, Felix Gonzalez-Torres, Doris Salcedo and Robert Gober, among others. The Rachofsky House in Dallas, Texas, completed in 1996, was designed by Richard Meier to showcase this extensive art collection. The multilevel,  glass-and-steel building extends 11,000 square feet and also includes an expansive garden for sculpture and site-specific works. In 2005, Howard Rachofsky donated the Rachofsky Collection and The Rachofsky House to the Dallas Museum of Art, accompanied by an operating endowment that continues to provide for public access to The Rachofsky House.

 

Bill and Ruth True opened Western Bridge, a warehouse in South Seattle’s industrial district renovated by architect Roy McMakin in 2004. This non-profit public space for contemporary art has become renowned for its exhibitions showcasing the current state of art making around the world. The True Collection contains works in video, photography, and other media by an international roster of established and emerging artists, including Doug Aitken, Tracey Moffatt, Tony Oursler, Christian Marclay and Thomas Schütte. Western Bridge is a connector between private collecting and public exhibition. It also serves as a venue to exhibit, commission and donate art. Long time supporters of the Seattle Art Museum and the Henry Art Gallery, the Trues pledge one piece from every show to the University of Washington’s Henry Art Gallery and other museums.

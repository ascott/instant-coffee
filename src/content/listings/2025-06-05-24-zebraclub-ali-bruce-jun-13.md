Opening Reception: Ali Bruce

Fri June 13th from 6"8pm

Artist will be in attendance

Light refreshments will be served

3101 Granville St, Vancouver

As the Artist-in-Residence at Zebraclub, Ali Bruces latest work explores recuperation
and growth. The window display features a suite of paintings and bright forms cut
from plywood that are playful and vary in scale, showcasing the breadth of her visual
language.

Inspired by the transformative process of cutting, sanding, and painting wood, Bruce
is continually intrigued by the materials strength and tangibility. In her practice, she
seeks to find a sense of lightness in contrast to weight, blending painting and
sculpture.

Artist Bio:

Ali Bruce is a visual artist based in Vancouver. In 2014, she earned a Bachelor of
Fine Arts from Emily Carr University and shortly after received a certificate in
Communication Design from BCIT. Bruce has worked independently as a graphic
illustrator, tattoo and mural artist. Currently, she is focusing more on her painting and
studio practice. By juxtaposing playfulness with perfectionism, she explores themes
of youthful curiosity, purpose, pain, and beauty.

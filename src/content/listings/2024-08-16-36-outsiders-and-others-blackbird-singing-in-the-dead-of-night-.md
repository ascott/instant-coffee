Blackbird Singing in the Dead of Night
Featuring 12 artists from South Korea, France, The Netherlands, US, and Canada.
September 7 - 28
Opening reception: September 7 from 2-4pm (free and open to the public!)

Artworks by Gregory Bannister/BC, Anita Le Bo/France, Mary Bowman-Cline/US,
Jasonda Desmond/BC, Clay Dixon/BC, Liset van Dommelen/Netherlands, Ian
Freemantle/BC, Lisa Huth/BC, Haejae Debbie Oh/South Korea, Keegan
Wenkman/US, Beth Wilks/BC, and Laleh Zandi/BC

Birds have been loved by people forever. And Blackbirds are even more so as they
are represented in so many cultures and with so many different meaning. Some say
intuition and protection or even death and bad luck. It all depends on the viewer.

We put a call for artists out to see what the
world would bring us and what came
forward was nothing less than intriguing and beautiful. The exhibition represents
these majestic creatures in forged metal, paint, ink, pencils, ball point pens, and
found materials.

IMPORTANT NEWS
This will be our last exhibition at 716 East Hastings Street.
We are moving to #100 - 938 Howe Street in October.

For high resolution images or to schedule an interview with the artist, contact Yuri
Arajs at outsidersandothers@gmail.com

Outsiders and Others is a non-profit art Society who has a mission to bring non-
traditional artists to the forefront. This includes artists that identify as outsider, self-
taught, and artists living with disabilities.

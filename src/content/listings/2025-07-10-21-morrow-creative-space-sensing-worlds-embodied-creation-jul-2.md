SENSING WORLDS: Embodied Creation

Join artist and educator Guadalupe Martinez for a unique 4-week creative workshop
series that invites participants to explore movement, memory, and poetic inquiry
through a sensorial, somatic approach. Though embodied, drawing, and writing
exercises we ask "What does it mean to truly sense the world? Not just with the eyes
but with the skin, the breath, the gut? To create from what you feel before you name
it?" Join these playful, warm, intimate group sessions starting July 22!

Perfect for artists, creatives, and anyone curious to reconnect with their body and
imagination.

No previous experience needed.

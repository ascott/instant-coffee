Mina Totino's "Twisting" Opens Saturday June 27
Noon - 4pm
With A Parking Lot Reception

Mina Totino is a Canadian painter currently based in Vancouver, British Columbia.
Totino's work has appeared in solo and group exhibitions in Montreal, Toronto and
Berlin. She first came to prominence in the 1985 Young Romantics exhibition at the
Vancouver Art Gallery. "Twisting" marks the first solo exhibition of the artist at MRG.

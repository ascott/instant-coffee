Ditch celebrates a slice of Richmonds history and a deeply rooted attachment to place.
Drawing from personal memories, collected stories and archival materials, artist Julie
Pappajohn revisits childhood growing up with Richmonds exposed drainage ditches.
Drawings, paintings, mixed media works and video/slideshow..

July 4 until Sep 19

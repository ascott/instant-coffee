PeaceTalks #6: Eight Years of Critical Engagement in Northern Uganda: 
Learning the Hard Way

Speaker: Lara Rosenoff Gauvin

Date & Time: December 5, 2012 at 6:00pm 

Event Venue:
W2 Media Cafe
111 West Hastings Street, Vancouver
Register at; www.peacetalks.eventbrite.com

Lara Rosenoff Gauvin will present a photographic journal of her work and time 
in Northern Uganda since 2004. Through the photos, she will provide a history 
of the conflict, cease-fire, International engagement in the area, as well as 
personal ethical dilemmas and engagement choices in advocacy initiatives 
over the years.

About Lara

Lara has collaborated on numerous projects in and about Northern Uganda as 
artist, advocate, activist and lecturer since 2004. She just returned from a 6-
month stay with a family in a rural village in Lamwo District as part of her work 
as a PhD Candidate in Anthropology at The University of British Columbia. 
Her dissertation project examines community processes of social repair and 
social reconstruction after the long-term conflict. In 2009 she completed her 
MFA in Documentary Media with a project that follows one girl over 2 years in 
an internal displacement camp, all the while questioning her role of witness. 
For more information about Lara, visit www.lararosenoff.com. You can also 
check out one of her most recent projects at 
http://www.hernameisbeatrice.com.

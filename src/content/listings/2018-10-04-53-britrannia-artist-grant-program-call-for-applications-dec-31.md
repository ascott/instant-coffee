StudioLand | Studios Available Immediately
Available immediately at StudioLand, brand new studio 
spaces on East Pender Street between Columbia and Main Street.

The original space has been completely renovated and divided into 10 individual 
studios with exhibition opportunity in the common area. 

Located on the second floor of a protected heritage building, the remaining studios 
are available starting at $400 per month. 

There is also a small kitchen area, bathroom and shower. 

Bright, clean, safe, private, 24 hour access, free wifi and utilities, slop sink, bike 
parking, and an outstanding central location. Shared studios are welcome.

Were looking for quiet, serious, long-term studio mates, and the spaces are ideal for 
painters, graphic artists, designers, illustrators, writers, animators, fabric artists,
filmmakers, or any other creative pursuits!

A five year lease is in place until June 2023 with an option to renew.

StudioLand is in Chinatown, near Strathcona, and is within the Culture crawl 
boundaries, steps away from galleries, cafes, restaurants, bus lines, and bike paths.

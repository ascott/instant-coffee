RICHMOND ART GALLERY
PERFORMANCES BY HAZEL MEYER
THE MARBLE IN THE BASEMENT
7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond BC

May 9 and 10, 7:00 pm
May 12, 2:00 pm

Tickets priced at sliding scale: $5-$15

Hazel Meyer and her cast of two performers and a bug-eyed puppet called Marble
act out The Marble in the Basement, a site-specific performance that uses her
installation of the same title as a set. The marble found posthumously in Canadian
artist Joyce Wieland's home becomes a point of departure to think about
"inheritance, class, queerness.and what you pass on."

Limited seating, registration required.
All proceeds to be provided directly to performers.

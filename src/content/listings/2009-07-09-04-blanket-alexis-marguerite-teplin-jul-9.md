Blanket Contemporary Art is pleased to present Â“Can you hear the sea?Â”, an 
exhibition of new painting, collage, and sculpture by London based American 
artist Alexis Marguerite Teplin.

Teplin gives us a peep show, with all its seductive promises, to the 
history of painting. She uses the language of painting as colour, and colour 
as form with out linear representation. In doing so, Teplin arrives at a 
space that brings together the vibration, the artificiality, the flatness of 
space/depth of field, and the observation of movement of colour in nature. 
There is no one system in place, but a conflation of moments, brought 
together to signify the feminine, the fantasy, and the subterfuges of the 
contemporary modern.

Alexis is currently teaching in the MFA program at the Art Centre College of 
Design in Pasadena. In September, she will be presenting an artists project 
at the Serpentine Public Pavilion in London. She currently shows with Hotel, 
London; Gavlak, Palm Beach; and Car Projects, Bologna.

Call For Entry
BURNABY 1' X 1' Art Project

Burnaby Arts Council (BAC) seeks visual artists
to participate in a 'BC 150' art exhibition and sale.
Artists receive a blank 1' x 1' canvas to work on and a
challenge to create art about the beautiful, the everyday
or the edgy side of Burnaby. Free to BAC members;
$10 for non members. Registration: Feb. 12 - Apr. 30 th.
Deadline to submit art to BAC by May 16 th.

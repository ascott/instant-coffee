Breaking Waves

Opening June 8th | 8PM-11PM
Merge Gallery (NE corner of Clark and Powell)

Jen Branagan has been practicing painting for the last 10 years. Originally from 
Dublin, Ireland, she now continues her art practice in Vancouver. This new series is 
the first time working on a large scale for the artist, though the subject matter of 
waves and surfers has been a ongoing theme. Branagan is interested in the inert 
power of waves and how surfers take on the challenge of conquering one of natures 
greatest forces. 

In this series, she adds a second influence of street culture and graffiti combining 
two of her passions and experimenting in a new composition style. "Adding graffiti 
on top serves as a reminder to take risks and be more free just like the surfers 
represented in my paintings."

The opening will feature new paintings by Jen Branagan as well as music 
performances by Lutzard, All of the Above, John Fairman Band, and others. CASH

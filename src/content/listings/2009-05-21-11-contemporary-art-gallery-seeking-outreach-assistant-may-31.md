The Contemporary Art Gallery is accepting applications for the position of Outreach Assistant. 
This is a full-time, twelve week summer student position funded by Canada Summer Jobs. 
The ideal candidate will be familiar with research practices in the field of communications and 
will 
have excellent skills and experience in preparing reports and planning documents. The position 
reports to the Program Administrator and works closely with the Education Programmer to 
develop promotion strategies for education and public programs. 

Duties will include:

Researching potential target groups (community, senior, day-camp and tour groups);
Reporting findings to Education Programmer and devising outreach and communications 
strategy 
for each group;
Creating promotional materials (including print and online) and organizing distribution;
Interacting with target groups through a variety of methods;
Compiling contact list for database;
Developing media contact list;
Preparing advance publicity for 2010 Cultural Olympiad projects;
Providing status reports to staff members;
Administrative tasks such as photocopying and filing;
Keeping detailed and accurate data regarding interactions with target groups.

Qualifications include:

Excellent research, written and communication skills;
Working knowledge of Microsoft systems (Word, Excel, Access, Outlook);
Knowledge of graphic software (Illustrator and Photoshop) an asset;
Ability to work within a fast-paced, diverse work environment;
Ability to prioritize workload and meet deadlines;
Excellent organizational skills;
Ability to work independently and as part of a team;
Excellent telephone, writing and interpersonal skills.

Eligible students:

Are between 15 and 30 years of age;
Have been registered as a full-time student in the previous academic year and intend to return to
school on a full-time basis in the next academic year;
Are Canadian citizens, permanent residents, or have official refugee protection status under the 
Immigration and Refugee Protection Act;
Are legally entitled to work in Canada, according to relevant provincial legislation.

Wage: $14 / hour 

Hours are Monday to Friday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. 

Application deadline: May 31, 2009

Start date: June 8, 2009 
Please email your resume and cover letter to info@contemporaryartgallery.ca

or send it by mail to 555 Nelson Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada V6B 6R5 

We would like to thank all candidates for their applications but only those selected for an 
interview will be contacted.

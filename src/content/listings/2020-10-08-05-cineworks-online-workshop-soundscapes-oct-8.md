Soundscapes: A four-part virtual workshop

Cineworks present Soundscapes: a four-week exploration of sound, facilitated virtually.
The program will be a combination of searching, listening, sharing, and creating hands-on
using accessible tools. This workshop requires no previous experience with the production
of soundscapes. However, it has been designed for people who are passionate about
sound, and are interested in and comfortable with basic sound recording and editing.

Participants will be mentored by Marianne Thodas and Ryan Clough-Carroll.

Learn how to record, edit, and compose a soundscape of your own creation. Whether you
want to understand more about sound in relation to film, or explore soundscapes for their
own
sake, this workshop aims to create sonic connections and aural discoveries. A variety
of basic and experimental recording techniques will be considered. A Care Package will
be provided to each participant including awesome items to explore sound with!

1- Sign up: registration is opened and closes on October 8th or until spots are filled.

2- Receive an email confirming your spot with a link to eventbrite.

3- Get your supplies: pick up your care package at Cineworks.*

4- Hop on our virtual sessions: from October 16th to November 6th, join us virtually on

registration welcome email.

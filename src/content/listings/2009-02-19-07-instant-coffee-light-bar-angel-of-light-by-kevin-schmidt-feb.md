Instant Coffee presents

ANGEL OF LIGHT by KEVIN SCHMIDT

FEBRUARY 21, 2009, 10pm

Vancouver artist Kevin Schmidt will give a light show as artist talk.
Reaching deep into the Christian rock archive, Schmidt, now beyond blinding
faith, will blind audiences with Angel of Light, a swirling bright
choreography of light. This will be the best light show ever!

Kevin Schmidt works in diverse media, but primarily in photography and
video. He has a strong interest in landscape, music, and popular culture.
His work has shown internationally, across N. America and Europe. He is
represented by Catriona Jefferies Gallery, Vancouver.

As part of the exhibition Assume Nothing: New Social Practice presented by
the Art Gallery of greater Victoria, www.aggv.bc.ca

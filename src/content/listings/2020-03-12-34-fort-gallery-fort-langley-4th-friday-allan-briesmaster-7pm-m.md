On March 27th at 7pm, the Fort Gallery is pleased to host Allan Briesmaster, a widely
respected and remarkably versatile Ontario-based poet. Allan will be reading selections
from his latest book, The Long Bond, which is a gathering of his finest work from six
books over four decades, published by Guernica Editions.

The Long Bond spans a massive range of subjects and styles, encompassing the
Canadian landscape, music and art, love and family,
science, technology, and the
manifold challenges to a questioning mind on our anxious planet.

Allan Briesmaster has been a workshop leader, readings organizer, freelance editor,
literary consultant, and a founding partner in Quattro Books. He currently runs his own
literary press, Aeolus House, specializing in limited-edition poetry books. The author of
eight books of poetry, he has read his work, given talks, and hosted literary events
across Canada. He is a Life Member of the League of Canadian Poets. He lives in
Thornhill, Ontario.

The Fort Gallery thanks the Township of Langley, Metro Vancouver, and the BC Arts
Council for sponsorship of 4th Friday community arts events. This particular 4th Friday
event is also supported by the League of Canadian Poets and Canada Council for the
Arts.

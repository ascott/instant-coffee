CRAFT PRIDE PROCESSION

Come join us and celebrate CRAFT!

...The CRAFT PRIDE PROCESSION is an affirmative act of association, re-
identification and celebration. We are proposing that those who feel called come 
together to create a procession of craft pride with a diversity of tactics. 
Costumes, music, knit-bombing, banners, flags, bicycle floats, giant sculptures, 
interventions and workshops are all in the works.

Everyone is welcome to participate and create!

If you are interested, please contact us and/or attend a meeting to share your 
ideas and connect with others who want to help the visions come to life...

...or meet with us at Main and Terminal @ noon on AUGUST 6th and join in on 
the FUN!

CRAFT PRIDE PROCESSION is created by a small group of people newly 
brought together by our use of textiles in contemporary art practices. At out first 
ever meeting it was apparent that we all shared a passion and concern for the 
ways in which we each perceive and experience the marginalization of the 
mediums and techniques we love and depend on for so much of our creative 
expression.

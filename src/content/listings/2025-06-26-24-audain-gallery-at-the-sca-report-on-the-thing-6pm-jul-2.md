Report on the Thing

Chelsea MacKay, Caroline Chernega, Carlo Marchet, Taha Saraei, Kaleb Thiessen,
Constance Arden Duffy

July 3 to 26, 2025

Opening reception July 2 at 6 PM - 8 PM

Audain Gallery at the SCA

The Audain Gallery at the School for the Contemporary Arts is pleased to announce
Report on the Thing, an interdisciplinary exhibition of work by undergraduate and
graduate students Chelsea MacKay, Caroline Chernega, Carlo Marchet, Taha
Saraei, Constance Arden Duffy, and Kaleb Thiessen. The artists in the exhibition
produced new work in response to a consideration of Clarice Lispectors short text,
Report on the Thing (1974). Lispectors story concerns ideas of categorization,
offering seemingly contradictory observations about how we perceive and know
phenomena of the world; everything is like something, just as everything is unlike
something else.

Artworks in the exhibition include a painting composed through process-based
collage, a work complicating the functioning of historical consumer electronics, a
sculptural photo installation, and sculptural assemblages.

Report on the Thing is the first in the Perel series, a series of exhibitions by students
chosen by students. The Perel series includes thematic group exhibitions and open
calls that will be scheduled throughout the year alongside our annual BFA and MFA
exhibitions, and shows by professional artists.

Ill Meet You in the Space Between
BFA Graduating Exhibition 2025
April 17 " May 3, 2025
Audain Gallery
School for the Contemporary Arts
149 W. Hastings St., Vancouver
Opening reception: Wednesday, April 16 at 6 PM
Emma Campbell, Shirley Chen, Caroline Chernega, Amy Dai, Abroop Dhami, Gracy
Singh Gandhi, Sana Goel, Luvz de Guzman, Thanyarat (Ocean) Hongphankul,
Julian Injeti, Lily Sng L, Daniel Molnar, Harvir Kaur Nahal, Harmon Nakajima,
Sarah Robinson, Renan Shao, Annie Yu.
Ill Meet You in the Space Between presents the work of visual art students
graduating with a Bachelor of Fine Arts from the School for the Contemporary Arts at
Simon Fraser University.
The artists in the exhibition engage with the notion of space across multiple
registers, exploring its depth, fluidity, rhythm, and relational possibilities. Space is
considered via surface, material, or body; as dreamscape, landscape, environment,
and outer space; and as the place of connection, interconnection, and difference as
can be seen by way of cultural identity and popular culture. This notion of space also
embodies memory " memory that has life in the present and that is not relegated to
a past that is over and done with but that is recontextualized in a lived present.
Like memory, this space is ephemeral and shifting, and it can include notions of the
emotional, mental, and spiritual. It is an energy that exists outside and in-between
the parameters of artwork and acts as an interval for collaboration, conversation and
connection. Though the artworks in the exhibition take various forms including
painting, sculpture, photography, textile, and video to present a wide range of
subjects, there are rich spaces of relation and correlation of ideas, histories, and
identities.
The artists in Ill Meet You in the Space Between respectfully acknowledge the
unceded traditional territories including, the Swxw7mesh xwumixw (Squamish),
slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) and xmky"m (Musqueam) Nations, on which SFU
Vancouver is located.

PORTAL POP-UP GALLERY

407 W. Cordova St., Vancouver BC

presents

PAIGE QUINN
Reception on Saturday, May 4th. 2-4pm

Artwork on display in window gallery:
April 12 - May 5, 2024

Paige Quinn's art practice negotiates ideas of relationship to the land and spirituality,
self and other. Through abstraction, these paintings embody ideas of reverence and
attention to our environment and open space for dialogue of the earth as a mentor
and guide. These paintings encourage the viewer to reflect upon their own
relationship to nature.

The two oil paintings selected for this exhibition seem to peer through dense foliage
into an evocative and immersive landscape. Her paintings, rich with colour and
movement, are an exploration of her own personal experiences and memories in
nature. She draws inspiration from music, poetry, and her plein-air sketches which
are imbued with symbols of animals as guides. As a self-taught oil painter, she
paints with an intuitive hand, often working in layers and painting over finished
paintings until a composition is
revealed.

In her painting Bird-Lungs (2023), a pale violet covers an almost floral composition
of softly blended colours. It is Paige's own joy of painting and colour that is evident
within this piece, with sweeping brush strokes and a playful yet adept approach to
colour composition. In Through (2023) a thick sap green layer obscures a charged
scene of branches pummeled by wind. The soft, almost feminine palette of creams
and pinks are brushed into cerulean blues with a painting rag. The branches are
punctuated by sharp navy silhouettes and violent black brushstrokes, contrasting the
otherwise idyllic scene.

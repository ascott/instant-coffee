Lauren Marsden, a Vancouver-based artist with Trinidadian roots, will be
presenting an artist talk and a new series of media artworks called
ECSTATIC TIME. This project, made entirely during a recent residency at
Alice Yard in Port of Spain, Trinidad, responds to a sense of place through
cinematic experiments in slow-motion and repetition. Expanding on filmmaker
Hollis Frampton’s notion of “ecstatic time”, her project includes a set of
short looping films and animated GIFs that document a series of
performative gestures, shot in various locations around the country.
Performed by family members, friends, acquaintances, and dancers, these
brief movements (some staged, some spontaneous) portray redundancy,
futility, and slowness in a local, cultural context. While interacting with
the natural and built environments of Trinidad, her characters defy
gravity, dive in and out, hide in plain sight, push forward, retreat…(and
repeat).

Lauren Marsden received her BFA in Visual Arts from the University of
Victoria and an MFA in Social Practice from the California College of the
Arts. She presents her work internationally in galleries and film festivals
and has had recent exhibitions and screenings in Port of Spain, Mexico
City, San Francisco, Vancouver, Victoria, Chicago, and Rome. She currently
teaches at the School for Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser University, and
edits for Decoy Magazine and SFAQ.

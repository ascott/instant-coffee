PHENOMENA – New Sculptural works by MARIANNE LOVINK 
MAY 07 – JUNE 07 
Opening Reception: May 7, 6-9 pm : artist in attendance
Artist Q+A = May 09 @ 2 pm

This exhibition brings together selections from three related but distinct bodies of recent work.
These works draw from interests ranging from the organic structure of human body on a cellular 
level to the graphic language of scientific structures and systems as a growing fascination with 
the ephemeral aspects of memory and perception.

This most recent investigation has led to the series creation of cut steel silhouettes with highly 
pigmented reverse sides that serve to reflect coloured light onto the adjacent wall bathing the 
work in a subtle glow or aura which hints at a latent energy or life force. The afterimage effect of 
this impression underscores a new desire to explore the sensual rather than empirical aspects of 
our surroundings – something experienced as opposed to something observed.
The imagery employed, ranging from the microscopic (vascular systems) to the cataclysmic 
(atmospheric electricity) are related in both form and function as complex and active pathways or
conduits.

Obsidian Artist Residency " Mayne Island
Duration: 2 weeks ($500) or 1 month ($1000) " starting September 2025
Location: Private two-bedroom cabin on Mayne Island, BC
Open to: Writers, Visual Artists, Sound Artists, and Musicians

Overview:
The Obsidian Artist Residency offers a self-directed creative retreat in a quiet,
natural setting on one of British Columbias most beautiful Gulf Islands. Open to
artists from a range of disciplines, the residency provides uninterrupted time and
space for exploration, reflection, and creation. Artists stay in a private two-bedroom
cabin surrounded by forest and ocean, ideal for both solitude and inspiration.

Fees:
$500 for a two-week residency
$1000 for a one-month residency
(Fees cover private accommodation and utilities. Artists are responsible for their own
travel and meals.)

Whats Included:

A private, fully equipped two-bedroom cabin with kitchen, workspace, and Wi-Fi
Access to forested trails, beaches, and a peaceful rural environment
An opportunity to engage with Mayne Islands welcoming and curious community
Support and feedback (if desired) from the residency committee, composed of
individuals with diverse creative backgrounds

Expectations:
Residents are asked to:

Dedicate time to their creative work
Share their process or work with the local community at the end of their residency
through a presentation, performance, reading, workshop, open studio, or exhibition
(format is flexible and collaborative)
Respect the land, the space, and the community

Application Guidelines:
Please submit:

A brief artist statement (max 300 words)
A project proposal outlining your focus during the residency (max 500 words)
A portfolio or samples of recent work
Short bio or CV
Preferred dates and duration (please indicate your top three preferred dates)

Deadline: June 30, 2025
Apply to: obsidianartistresidency@gmail.com

About the Committee:
Our selection committee includes writers, visual artists, musicians, and
interdisciplinary creators who are committed to nurturing creative practice. During
your residency, they are available (remotely) to provide support, guidance, and
feedback if requested.

We acknowledge that this residency takes place on the Unceded traditional
territories of the WSNE- and Coast Salish peoples, and we invite applicants to
reflect on their relationship to place, land, and community as part of their creative
process.

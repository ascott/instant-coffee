Please join us for a free, one-day summit that will explore art as a
catalyst for sparking community imagination around belonging, trust, and
social resiliency in urban centres.

Mobilizing the Arts for Community Connection and Change will feature
presentations by renowned artists, designers, and community organizers:
Ayotzi 68, Corey Bulpitt, Alex Grnenfelder, Zoe Kreye, Carmen Papalia and
Holly Schmidt.

Following the presenters' reflections on their diverse and dynamic
practices, audience members will take the lead in imagining how the arts
can be used in interactive ways to consider themes of connection and change
in Metro Vancouver. The presenters will work closely with small groups
through a series of workshops, concluding with a peer-to-peer granting
session. Inspiring new conversations around community in Vancouver, the
summit will be a collaborative and participatory experience.

Mobilizing the Arts for Community Connection and Change is organized by
Justin Langlois, and supported by the Vancouver Foundation, posAbilities
and the Museum of Vancouver.

Tea Ceremony Performance with Lam Wong: MA No.2 - The Stillness Between 
Movements

Saturday, June 15, 1:00-2:00pm 

Griffin Art Projects Residency

1180 Welch St, North Vancouver

BC, V7P 2R5

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Wong is a Vancouver-based contemporary artist whose family background has revolved 
around the art of tea for over 15 generations. Situating traditions of Chinese style tea 
ceremony (gongfu cha) within his practice as a visual artist, Wong will offer tea to 
visitors as an opportunity for connection and contemplation within his work space in 
Griffins residency studio.

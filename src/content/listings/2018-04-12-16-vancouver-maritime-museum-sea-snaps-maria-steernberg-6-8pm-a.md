he Vancouver Maritime Museum presents
Sea Snaps: Marine Photography by Maria Steernberg
April 13 to October 7, 2018

Open to public Friday, April 13, 2018 10am
Public Reception Friday, April 13, 2018 6-8pm

Capture Photography Festival is the annual not-for-profit Festival that strives to 
nurture emerging talent, engage community, and spark public dialogue about 
photography as an art form and a vessel for communication.

As part of Vancouvers Capture Photography Festival the Vancouver Maritime 
Museum presents work by Maria Steernberg, owner of Sea Snaps Marine 
Photography Ltd. The exhibition highlights the beauty of Vancouvers maritime 
setting and the provinces coast line through a series of themes; the working 
waterfront, cruising, nature and disaster.

Sea Snaps has been a fixture on the Pacific West Coast for the past 35 years. From 
her bright pink boat, Maria has photographed captains, crews and their vessels, both 
commercial and recreational. Documenting marine activity within the marine world is 
her passion. Sea Snaps photography has graced many marine publications covers 
and marine articles. Maria has made her mark on the marine industry through her 
unique use of medium format cameras and her uncompromising standards. 

The VMM is a world-leading maritime museum and a centre for dialogue, education 
and research regarding the maritime heritage of the Pacific and Arctic Oceans.
This travelling exhibition was made possible by a grant from the Department of 
Canadian Heritage.

Vilhelm Sundin
Elsewhere

September 6 to October 6, 2018
Opening reception: Thursday September 6, 2018, 6pm to 8pm

Inspired by painting, literature and street photography, Vilhelm
Sundins work situates mundane events in a fictional universe.
Elsewhere presents a series of video-installations that invite the
viewer to be an observer of scenes that unfold over a passage of time;
a lit-up street corner is divided, like an island, from a city during
a power outage; the transition from day to night empties a park;
seagulls struggle to fly through a snowstorm. The scenes move between
realism and the imaginary as they struggle to maintain an authentic
representation of the documented spaces. Elevated by cinematic tropes,
Elsewhere introduces everyday moments we were never present for.

Sundin holds an MFA from Simon Fraser University, Vancouver, and he
completed his Bachelor of Media Arts at Emily Carr University.
Sundins work was recently shown at the Polygon Gallerys inaugural
exhibition N. Vancouver, and he won the Philip B. Lind Emerging Artist
Award in 2016. After moving from his native Sweden in 2006, Vilhelm
Sundin now lives and works in Vancouver, BC.

REPUBLIC GALLERY

Exhibition Opening:

ANTONIA HIRSCH 

light tender 

Exhibition dates: February 1 - March 2, 2013
Opening reception: Saturday, February 2, 2-4 pm

Antonia Hirsch’s installation incorporates objects and video to continue her 
inquiry into the interconnection between affect and economy. The show brings 
together the abstract phenomenon of colour and the very concrete trade in cut 
flowers. light tender mobilizes these elements of trade, affect, and colour and 
proposes that an economy be understood as not merely a system of fiscal 
transactions, but as a system of representation—expressive of a way of 
perceiving and interacting with the world and each other. While affect is both a 
generator and a consequence of economic exchanges, it is, conversely, also 
the modality of those economic exchanges that structure affect, resulting in 
topographies of the sensible that underpin everyday life. Positioning colour as 
an instantiation of affect and flowers as a luxury good without use value, light 
tender flagrantly appeals to the senses.


Working between Vancouver and Berlin, Antonia Hirsch has developed a 
broad artistic practice, initiating and sustaining an investigation into the visual 
and quantitative systems that give order to our world. She questions the 
invisible hierarchies of these epistemological structures in her installation and 
print works, offering embodied experience as a bearing for alternative mapping 
strategies. Her work has been exhibited throughout Canada and Western 
Europe, most recently at the Or Gallery Berlin and in a solo exhibition at 
Tramway in Glasgow, Scotland. In March 2013, Hirsch will be participating in 
the residency program at CCA Andratx, Spain.


light tender runs from February 1 through March 2. Gallery hours are 10am to 
5pm, Wednesday through Saturday, and by appointment.

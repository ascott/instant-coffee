Whispers Project: Invitation to Writers | JUNE 8 & 9

Whispers is putting extraordinary voices onto our city walls. As part of
Celebrate Vancouver 125, passages from local writers will be installed as
typographic murals around Vancouver.

Writers from all backgrounds living in Vancouver are invited to participate
in upcoming workshops organized to bring community voices together and
generate project-specific content.

Workshops will take place over a two day period with the intention of
generating a body of written work from which small excerpts can be selected
for mural application. All participants will have a chance to submit
writing generated during these workshops for an accompanying online and/or
print publication in which authors will be fully credited and documented.

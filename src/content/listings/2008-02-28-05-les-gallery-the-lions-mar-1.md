LES Gallery is pleased to present an exhibition of works on paper by Vancouver artists, The Lions. A forthcoming catalogue featuring an essay by Vancouver writer Lance Blomgren and short story by Lee Henderson will be published for the exhibition.

Committed to a process based approach to drawing, one that puts stock in drawing’s ability to allow for emergences of meaning over its pictorial conclusion, The Lion's amicable practice has resulted in a slightly disconcerting body of work, a series of odd mish-mashes of genre, style, narrative experiment, fantasy, and spectacle.  While The Lion's work through familiar archetypes, colloquial pictorial forms, jokes and gags, these are never left as they are but are inevitably subverted and mutated into something unlikely.	Detournement to detournement, forms, characters, and pictorial conventions are fragmented, disfigured, and eventually rendered absurdly incongruent.  This juxtaposition of incongruent elements allows a critical distance to be formed, through which the Lion's project extends to a formal investigation of image making.

The Lions (Tasha Brotherton, Matthew Brown, Barry Doupe, Collin Johanson, James Whitman) are based in Vancouver, BC.   The Lions have exhibited throughout Canada, the USA and Europe.	 Recent exhibitions include, Dripped, Dropped, Split, Upended, Exploded, Tracey Lawrence Gallery, Vancouver; Tinyvices, (curated by Tim Barber, NY)  NY, Paris, London, Tokyo, Oshkosh (Wisconsin) and Mexico City; and Extensions of Our Hands, Khyber Arts Centre, Halifax, NS.  Since 2003 The Lions have maintained www.lionspile.ca, an online gallery that extends to 450 works.

CALL FOR WORKS

Repeat as Necessary
Deluge Contemporary Art seeks artists moving image works for an online exhibition in spring
of 2026.

Insanity is doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results. This quote,
usually misattributed, has been overused to the point of cliche. What if repetition is the end,
not the means?

Reproducibility is a cornerstone of the scientific method, which is still the basis for rationality
in most parts of the world. The cyclical process that validates a hypothesis is also
fundamental to experimental forms of artistic production, from music and literature to visual
and media art.

Repeat as Necessary invites short artists film and video that engage with concepts of
repetition, whether through process, form and/or content; works that revel in doing the same
the same thing over and over with no defined expectation of results.

Artists fees will be paid. Please send links and info by March 13 to curator@deluge.ca.

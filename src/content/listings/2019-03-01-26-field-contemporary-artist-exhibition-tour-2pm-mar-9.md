A Mass Residue

Field Contemporary presents new sculptures and wall pieces by 
Alexander Tedlie-Stursberg. In his second Vancouver solo exhibition 
Stursberg employs found and salvaged polystyrene discards to 
produce a variety of abstracted and semi-organic forms. Constructed 
utilizing fiberglass, concrete, and metal, formerly fragile and 
purposeless objects are given structural integrity, weight, and a 
reinvigorated permanence as formal sculpture. Reoriented this way, 
Stursberg proposes that our relationship to these forms might begin to 
shift, whereby recognizable knowns could become unknowns.

(b.1980 UK) Lives and works in Vancouver, BC

Alex Tedlie-Stursberg is a multidisciplinary artist working across various 
mediums including sculpture, assemblage and video. His work has 
been exhibited in numerous galleries across North America and 
Europe; recent and upcoming exhibitions include Holy Wave as part of 
Glasgow International, Scotland, Everything Flows, with Burrard Art 
Foundation, Vancouver, BC (2018), Mass Residue, with Field 
Contemporary, and Super, Natural, a group exhibition at Unit 17 
Gallery, Vancouver, BC (2019). In 2018 Stursberg was an artist in 
residence at the Glasgow Sculpture Studios. He is currently employed 
as a term lecturer at Simon Fraser University School for the 
Contemporary Arts. 

Working with assemblage, cultural iconography and material 
appropriation, Stursbergs practice looks at systems of value 
throughout society, demonstrating their interconnectivity and mutual 
dependency. Concerned with the nature of undervalued materials and 
the political economy of consumer resources his practice regularly 
utilises found objects of low material value, often acquired through 
thrift, salvage, or trade.

Stursberg continues to produce a range of group exhibitions, for 
organisations including Glasgow Open House Festival, Vancouver Art 
Book Fair, and Vancouver Art Gallery. The most recent of these was 
Micro-Wave, a group exhibition of artists working with found materials 
presented in repurposed garage space in East Vancouver, BC

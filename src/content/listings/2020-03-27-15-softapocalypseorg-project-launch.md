Welcome to the Soft Apocalypse Skills Share, a volunteer artist-run hub for sharing skills
to get us through this challenging time, with a particular focus of soft skills, and hard-
skills for self-care. The project aims to connect existing networks of care, share, host
and signal boost events focusing on building capacity for caring about ourselves and
others as well as connecting people who need skills with people who have them.

visit softapocalypse.org for more information

or participate on facebook at facebook.com/groups/softapocalypse/

who is running this project?

Lexie Owen, a visual artist from Canada currently living (in home quarantine) in Oslo,
Norway. She make participatory projects that often aim to develop or recognize networks
of care, and investigates how communities form and produce culture.

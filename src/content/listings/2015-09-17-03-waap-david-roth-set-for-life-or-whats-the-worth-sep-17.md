PERFORMANCE
DAVID ROTH
SET FOR LIFE, OR WHAT'S THE WORTH OF A WHILE
THURS, SEPT 17, 7 PM

Wil Aballe Art Projects | WAAP
105-1356 Frances St.
Vancouver

waapart.com
+1 778 229 3458

DAVID ROTH's "art" is the product of his economic surroundings.
Raised in an expensive city and surrounded by financial excess with
limited personal means, David decided to study the relationships
between private wealth, artistic worth and social value. David produces
installations that act as stages where he performs comic versions of
achieving artistic and financial success. As an economic comic, David
wholeheartedly attempts achievement to the point of absurdity,
encouraging audiences to re-think how they understand worth and
value.

Set for Life or, Whats the Worth of a While is a performance where
David Roth will play 110 "Set for Life" lottery tickets. Working as a bus
boy,David invested one month of his hourly wage into buying tickets,
similar to how most emerging artists invest their wages into materials,
spaces and production. While researching how art markets operate,
David collaborated with former math students and realized that the
odds of an emerging artist in a major city achieving success were
comparable to winning the grand prize of "Set for Life". By playing
these tickets, David will perform the odds of most emerging artists. He
promises that this is a comedy. While playing the odds and tempting
success, David will be joined by musical accompaniment, fine
beverages, play-to-win odds boards and an assistant who will record
his wins throughout the night.

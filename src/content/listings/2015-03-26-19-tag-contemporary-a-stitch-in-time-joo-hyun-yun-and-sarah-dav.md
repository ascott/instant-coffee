A Stitch in Time
Joo Hyun Yun and Sarah Davidson
April 1-8
Opening April 1, 7pm
tag Contemporary
Emily Carr University, North Building painting studios (4th floor)

Can drawing be sewing?

Can stitching be mapping?

Joo Hyun Yun and Sarah Davidson both make a series of analogies at the
cross-section of personal experience, craft tradition, and abstraction.

In her Jeong series, Joo Hyun Yun works with patterns and palette that
refer back to the quilts of her Korean grandmother. The works are
structured by triangular patterns that create multiple geometries where
they join together.

In her mixed-media drawings, Sarah Davidson uses the patterns of her
great-aunts' crazy quilts as the starting point for silkscreened prints
which are camouflaged into elaborate drawings. Her images liken drawing to
stitching, and textiles to comics, making the line-work of binding the
centre of drawings about space.

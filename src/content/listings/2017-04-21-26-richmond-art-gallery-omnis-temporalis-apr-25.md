Free Performance of Omnis Temporalis
Tuesday, April 25
7:00 - 9:00 pm

Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond BC

Join RAGA Board Members and Gallery staff to enjoy wine, refreshments and a 
chance to meet the performers of Omnis Temporalis. This members event 
happens after gallery hours for RAGA Members and friends.

Composer Mark Haney leads a performance of OMNIS TEMPORALIS, telling the 
story of cartoonist Seth's novella "George Sprott: 1894-1975" through live 
dialogue and music with an ensemble of actors and a trio of alto/bass flute, cello 
and double bass.

Performance followed by a wine and cheese reception for all in attendance.

Free admission, but due to limited seating RSVP required to Kathy at 
ktycholis@richmond.ca or 604-247-8313.

More details available at www.richmondartgallery.org

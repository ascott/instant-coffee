We are delighted to announce Walking in his Footsteps (August 1 - 31, 2025) at the
Ferry Building Gallery. This exhibition features the work of Peter Kiss, Zoltan Kiss, and
Tania Gleave, and celebrates the enduring legacy of the late Zoltan Kiss, a West
Vancouver artist whose influence continues to resonate deeply.

Peter Kiss and his partner and artist, Tania Gleave, live and create surrounded by
Zoltans work. Walking in the Footsteps is both a tribute to Zoltan Kiss and an
exploration of how his influence continues to shape and inform the creative lives of
those closest to him.

Peter Kiss is this years Harmony Arts Festivals feature artist.

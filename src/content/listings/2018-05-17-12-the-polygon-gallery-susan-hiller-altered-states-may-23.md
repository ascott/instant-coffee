Susan Hiller: Altered States
Wednesday, May 23
7pm: A Conversation with Susan Hiller and Helga Pakasaar

Please join us at The Polygon Gallery Wednesday May 23, 2018, at 7 pm for a
conversation with artist Susan Hiller.

Internationally acclaimed artist Susan Hiller will join the curator of
Susan Hiller: Altered States, Helga Pakasaar, for a conversation about the
artworks in The Polygon Gallerys feature exhibition, and the artist's
interests in language, dream states and our collective unconscious. Based
in London England, Susan Hiller is one of the most influential artists of
her generation and has exhibited widely. Following the conversation, we
invite you to preview the exhibition.

Susan Hiller: Altered States is on view May 24 - September 2, 2018.

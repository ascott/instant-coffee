NSCAD University Faculty Vacancies

Assistant Professor: Division of Foundation Studies (Craft)

COMPETITION NO. F108.08

REVIEWS BEGIN: February 16
APPOINTMENT BEGINS: July 1, 2009

The Foundation program is a dynamic, first year experience that prepares
students for further study in art, craft, design, and art history. To
complement the current roster of faculty in the Division of Foundation
Studies, NSCAD University is seeking an outstanding candidate with a
background in Craft. This may include book arts, ceramics, fashion studies,
jewellery design and metalsmithing, textiles, and other material arts.

The successful candidate will hold a terminal degree in his/her field and
will be expected to be conversant with historical, contemporary and
critical craft discourses within visual culture. A strong commitment to
teaching at the foundation level is required. Post-secondary teaching
experience and professional recognition in the form of exhibitions,
publications and presentations are essential.

In addition to teaching and research responsibilities, the selected
applicant will be expected to provide leadership in the ongoing development
and administration of the Foundation program, and to participate in other
activities in support of the broader mandate and administration of the
University.

Applicants should include a current curriculum vitae, a maximum of 20
digital images of professional work (on a CD or DVD-ROM), and the names and
contact information for three references. The University will seek
permission from the applicant before contacting the referees. A statement
of teaching philosophy as well as an artist statement would be considered
an asset. Examples of work from past students are also encouraged.

Salary will be dependent upon qualifications and in accordance with the
Faculty Salary Grid. Hiring will be in accordance with the policy on No
Discrimination. All qualified applicants are encouraged to apply; however,
Canadians and permanent residents will be given priority. Please quote
competition number F108.08 in your application.

AND

Assistant Professor: Division of Craft (Fashion)

COMPETITION NO. F102.08

Subject to budgetary and final approval by the Board of Governors, this is
an initial three-year appointment, renewable for continuing appointment.

DEADLINE EXTENDED
REVIEWS BEGIN February 16, 2009
APPOINTMENT July 1, 2009

NSCAD University offers a unique, emerging Fashion program that is closely
linked to our Textiles program. The University is seeking an outstanding
candidate with an innovative vision for fashion, to teach students at both
the undergraduate and graduate level and provide leadership in curriculum
development and program expansion.

It is expected that the candidate will be capable of teaching fashion
drawing and design, construction, pattern drafting and/or draping, as well
as the experimental exploration of materials and processes.

The selected candidate will have a terminal degree in fashion or a related
area and be conversant in contemporary and historical discourses in
clothing design. Teaching experience and professional recognition such as
exhibitions, publications, and public presentation record is essential.

Applicants should include a current curriculum vitae, a maximum of 20
digital images (on a single CD-ROM or DVD-ROM) of professional work, and
the names and contact information for three references. The University will
seek permission from the applicant before contacting the referees. A
statement of teaching philosophy and an artist statement would be
considered an asset.

Salary will be dependent upon qualifications and in accordance with the
Faculty Salary Grid. Hiring will be in accordance with the policy on No
Discrimination. All qualified applicants are encouraged to apply; however,
Canadians and permanent residents will be given priority. Please quote
competition number F102.08 in your application.

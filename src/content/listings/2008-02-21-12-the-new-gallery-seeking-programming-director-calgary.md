The New Gallery Job Posting – PROGRAMMING DIRECTOR

The New Gallery (TNG), an artist-run centre, is a non-profit charitable society established in 1975. It is committed to providing a forum for a wide spectrum of critical discourse and multidisciplinary practices within the contemporary visual arts. TNG presents and promotes locally, nationally and internationally produced contemporary art while cultivating diversity, advancing critical discourse and creating opportunities for networking in the art community.

We are seeking a motivated, organized and creative individual to fill the full-time position of Programming Director, to commence immediately.

General duties of the Programming Director include:

§		      Overseeing all programming activities at the Gallery.
§		      Overseeing all aspects of exhibition planning, including correspondence, installation logistics, and documentation.
§		      Developing curatorial direction, including chairing the Programming Committee
§		      Researching and pursuing funding opportunities for all programs
§		      Liaising and maintaining relations with exhibiting artists
§		      Supervising and recruiting volunteers
§		      Promoting and advancing The New Gallery’s reputation as a progressive and established Artist Run Centre.
 
The successful candidate will:

-	   Have a solid understanding of contemporary Canadian visual art and related disciplines.
-	   Have knowledge of and interest in artist-run centres and non-profit organizations generally.
-	   Possess a minimum of a Bachelor of Fine arts, Art History, or equivalent post-secondary arts education
 
The ideal candidate is self-directed, is capable of managing multiple projects in a busy environment, possesses strong interpersonal skills and can demonstrate above-average writing abilities.
 
This position has a 35-hour work week.	A competitive compensation package, including extended health benefits, is offered.

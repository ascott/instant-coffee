Dehdeh Films is casting for a documentary called Move Like a Woman.

Funded by the Canada Council for the Arts, directed by Sepideh Yadegar and
Produced by Jaclyn Guse.

Seeking BIPOC, female & non-binary, first-generation Canadians who want to share
their stories about gender-based oppression and discrimination in their home
country.

Move Like a Woman is a long-format experimental documentary highlighting stories
about the human rights movement, and how females have found free expression
through their own movement.

All ages:
mothers, grandmothers, teenagers, and whoever is interested in sharing
their stories with us.

There is a small honorarium if you are selected to participate in the project. You must
be comfortable telling your story on camera. No acting experience needed.

For more information, please contact:

Jaclyn Guse at jaclynpguse@gmail.com

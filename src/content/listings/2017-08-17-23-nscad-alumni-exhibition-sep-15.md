CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS:
Underpinnings: Celebrating 130 Years of NSCAD Alumni

Exhibition: November 21st " December 2nd 2017 at the Anna Leonowens Gallery.
Opening Reception November 20th at 5:30pm.

ELECTRONIC SUBMISSIONS DUE: Friday, September 15th 2017

This year marks 130 years since Anna Leonowens first opened the Victoria School 
of Art in 1887. Always in a state of flux, NSCAD has maintained its reputation as one 
of the last centurys most influential art schools under many names, locations and 
presidents. NSCAD has provided the educational underpinnings for the studio 
practice of numerous significant Canadian artists, cultural workers, and creative 
thinkers.

For the 2017 Alumni Biennial the NSCAD Alumni Association invites alumni to reflect 
on the core values and pedagogy that have created the legacy for which NSCAD is 
known. We invite alumni to submit works that embody the physical and conceptual 
underpinnings of their practice that NSCAD gave them to build upon.

Alumni may submit up to two works for consideration but only one will be selected 
for the exhibition. Artists may choose to sell their work within the exhibition. 90% of 
the profits will be returned to the artist and the remaining 10% will go towards future 
alumni exhibitions. Physical works must be smaller than 13x 13x 13 and no more 
than 20 lbs. Video, performance, audio or other non-physical mediums may be of 
any duration.

Submission Requirements:

Artist CV (3 page max, including the years you attended NSCAD)
Fill out the form here.
3-5 images of the submitted work or a url to a clip for media arts pieces.
DEADLINE: September 15th, 2017.

Send submission electronically to: alumni@nscad.ca
For any questions regarding the exhibition or submission process, contact Tori
Fleming, Exhibition Coordinator at tori.m.fleming@gmail.com.

Participants will receive a confirmation email once their work has been selected 
along with shipping instructions. All works must be dropped off or shipped to the 
Anna Leonowens Gallery (5163 Duke St, Halifax, NS B3J 3J6) no later than Monday, 
November 3rd , 2017. The exhibition will pay for the shipping of the work up to a 
certain amounts.

The exhibition coordinator has the right to reject submissions if they do not fall within 
the criteria listed above or the shipping costs exceed our budget.

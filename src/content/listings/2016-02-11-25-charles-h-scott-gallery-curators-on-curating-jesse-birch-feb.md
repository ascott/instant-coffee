February 22, 2015 at 11:30 AM
Charles H. Scott Gallery

Please join us for the latest installment of Curators on Curating with a
talk by Jesse Birch, Curator of the Nanaimo Art Gallery. Birch was formerly
Co-Director/Curator of Access Gallery and Exhibitions Curator at The
Western Front. He holds degrees from Emily Carr and the University of
British Columbia, and was a curatorial fellow at de Appel Arts Centre in
Amsterdam. Birch is a member of the artist collective Project Rainbow.

Curators on Curating is an ongoing series where leading curators discuss
their work and the work of others.

WEIRD WORLDS
By David Ogilvie

April 1st - 30th
Opening Reception: April 1st, 5:30pm - 7pm
Exhibition hours: Mon-Sun, 9am - 10pm
Carnegie Art Gallery, 3rd Floor at Carnegie Community Centre (401 
Main Street)

Surrealist drawings and paintings and collages by David Ogilvie.

ARTIST BIO:
David was born in Moncton, New Brunswick. He has lived and studied 
in New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, England and Toronto.

From 1968 to 2012, he worked an assortment of jobs from Nova Scotia 
to British Columbia to the Northwest Territories including dishwasher, 
short order cook, farmhand, millworker, mailman, infantryman, 
warehouseman, cartoonist, freelance writer, press worker and janitor.

David has always had a knack for art and have always had a 
fascination for classical and surrealist art and have decided to 
surrender himself to it now that he is no longer able to work. He has 
merged it with the 'etching' styles of 19th Century book illustrations. He 
hopes you enjoy viewing them as much as he did doing them.

His illustrations were featured in the Capilano Review in Fall 2015 
issue.

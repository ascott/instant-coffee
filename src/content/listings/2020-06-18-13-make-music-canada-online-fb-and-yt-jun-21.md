Make Music Canada is an event coordinated by the 9 Alliances Franaises of
Canada, to gather from the East to the West coast and beyond!

On June 21st, the audience can stroll for free through 4 virtual stages, showcasing
more than 20 artists including 2 BC based artists (Farnaz Ohadi Cuadro and
JJ&MM) during almost 8 hours of concerts!

Make Music Canada aims to promote music in its diversity of genres, to support
amateur and professional artists, and to
display the ethnic and linguistic diversity of
Canada.

Exhibition Statement
Join us on Saturday, March 1, 2025 from 2 - 5pm for the opening reception of the
Strathcona BIA x Gallery 881 Fall for Strathcona Group Exhibition. The free and
accessible event celebrates artistic expression and diversity in our neighbourhood.
The exhibition includes 11 finished works by local photographers.

The Fall for Strathcona exhibition is in partnership with the Strathcona BIA
celebrates the distinctive character and beauty of Vancouvers Strathcona
neighborhood. This exhibition showcases the top 11 images by local photographers,
capturing moments that highlight the neighbourhoods unique blend of industrial and
urban.

Through the lenses of local photographers, we are invited to explore Strathconas
vibrant streets, architecture, people, quiet alleys, and dynamic urban landscapes.
Each photograph offers a personal interpretation of what makes Strathcona special
through the artists lens.

This exhibition not only shines a spotlight on the talent within our community but also
serves as a visual love letter to Strathcona itself"its people, its history, and its
evolving story. We hope these images spark a deeper connection to the
neighbourhood and inspire others to see it through new perspectives. Join us as we
celebrate the creativity, spirit, and beauty of Strathcona.

Gallery 881 is grateful to our partner Strathcona BIA, and sponsors including
PrintMaker Studio, Low Tide Properties, and the Vancouver Art Walk. We're also
very
excited to share a curation of meticulously placed furniture in collaboration with
Retro Modern Design. The artwork was printed and framed by PrintMaker Studio, a
Canson Infinity Certified Print Lab and custom picture framer located in Strathcona.

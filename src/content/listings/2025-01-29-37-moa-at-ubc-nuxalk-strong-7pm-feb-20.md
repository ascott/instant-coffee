Nuxalk Strong Opens
February 20 at 7 pm

Join MOA on the evening of Thursday, February 20 for the Opening Celebrations of
Nuxalk Strong: Dancing Down the Eyelashes of the Sun. In the worlds first-ever
dedicated exhibition of the Nuxalk, co-curated by
Snxakila"Clyde Tallio (Nuxalk Nation,
Director of Culture and Language) and Jennifer Kramer (MOA Curator, Pacific
Northwest), Nuxalk ways of knowing will be shared with the world. Nuxalk Strong
brings together over 60 Nuxalk historic treasures drawn from the MOA collections,
other museums, private and family collections.

Thursday February 20 | 7 - 9 pm
Free museum admission starting at 6 pm

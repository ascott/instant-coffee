OPEN CALL FOR SCI-FI FAN-FICTION & FAN-FILMS

In connection to the exhibition "Keith Langergraber: Theatre of the Exploding 
Sun", the Richmond Art Gallery and Cinevolution Media Arts Society are 
looking for sci-fi fans to present their short films and fiction for a Sci-Fi Fan-
fiction Screening and Open Mic Event to be held at the Richmond Cultural 
Centre on April 4, 2014. Selected films will be presented on the big screen in 
our new state-of-the-art performance space. Selected authors will be given 
the opportunity to read their stories to a receptive crowd of sci-fi enthusiasts.

Submission deadline extended to MARCH 3, 2014
Details on how to apply: 
[http://www.richmondartgallery.org/pdfs/RAG_Fanfiction_film_screening_call](http://www.richmondartgallery.org/pdfs/RAG_Fanfiction_film_screening_call)
_and_regulations.pdf

Thursday, February 27 - 6:30 to 9:30pm
FREE - FILM SCREENING - SOLARIS 
Richmond Cultural Centre Performance Hall
Ages 14+

Richmond Art Gallery and Cinevolution Media Arts Society are hosting a free 
film screening of the 1972 cult-classic "Solaris" by Russian film-maker Andrei 
Tarkovsky. Come see the film that has inspired multiple film-makers, writers 
and artists over the years. Exhibiting artist Keith Langergraber will be in 
attendance to introduce the film and discuss how the film has inspired his 
works in the Gallery's current exhibition. Doors open at 5:30pm, and visitors 
are welcome to preview the exhibit prior to the screening.

More info: ktycholis@richmond.ca

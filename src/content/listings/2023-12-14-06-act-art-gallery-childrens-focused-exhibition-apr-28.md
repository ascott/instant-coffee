CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

Childrens Focused Exhibition

The ACT Art Gallery

11944 Haney Place, Maple Ridge, BC

Deadline: Sunday, April
28, 2024 at 11:59pm

Exhibition dates: July to August, 2024 (Official dates TBD)

The ACT Art Gallery is accepting artwork submissions/proposals for an exhibition
focused on childrens engagement, taking place in July to August 2024. Were
developing a playful and experimental exhibition that appeals to children and families
during the time of year when school is out for summer. When children have the
opportunity to engage with art through curiosity and play, the gallery becomes a place
of joyful discovery. We encourage a wide range of mediums, ideas, and approaches to
this artist call.

We are seeking artworks/projects that feature the following characteristics:

Has a hands-on or interactive element, engaging visual, tactile and/or auditory senses.
Appealing subject matter that references select elements (line, shape, colour, value,
texture, space) and principles (balance, emphasis, movement, pattern, repetition,
proportion, rhythm, unity) of art.
Within a wide range of mediums that
include painting, drawing, illustration,
photography, ceramics, textiles, sculpture, woodwork, installation, and mixed media.
Acceptance of new media work (video, AI) will be dependent on equipment availability.
Context and meaning of art can be explained to and understood by children ages 3-11.
Sturdy and able to withstand frequent handling/interaction over the course of 6-8
weeks.
Promotes artistic and creative engagement through play and experimentation.
Work can be previously created or made specifically for this call.

For more information, and to apply: [https://theactmapleridge.org/open-call](https://theactmapleridge.org/open-call)

Renumeration: Artists will be paid a fee in line with CARFAC Category I Group
Exhibition. The final amount of the fee will be dependent upon the number of selected
artists.

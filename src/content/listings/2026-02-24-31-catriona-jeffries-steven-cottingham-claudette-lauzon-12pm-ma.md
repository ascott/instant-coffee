On Saturday, March 7, 12pm, Steven Cottingham and Claudette Lauzon will be in
conversation on the occasion of Arrows, the exhibition currently on view at the gallery until
March 14, 2026.

Steven Cottingham's practice critically examines the influence of image surveillance
technologies through case studies including bodycams, military simulation software, and the
use of AI in prisons. Cottingham's films and artworks have been recently presented at
Dazibao, Montreal (2025); Access Gallery, Vancouver (2025); Center for Performance
Research, New York (2024); The 8th Floor, New York (2023); Catriona Jeffries, Vancouver
(2023, 2021); ENTRE, Vienna (2023); and Artists Space, New York (2022). He has
undertaken residencies with the Canadian Forces Artist Program (2024-2025) and the
Whitney Independent Study Program, New York (2021-2022).

Claudette Lauzon is an Associate Professor of contemporary art history in the School for the
Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser University. She has authored and edited numerous
publications including, most recently, Too Much Light is Blinding (Publication Studio, 2025).
Lauzon is currently writing about the metaphors that both sustain and antagonize techno-
military cultures in the world today.

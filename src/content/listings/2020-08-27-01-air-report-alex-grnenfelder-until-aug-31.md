The atmosphere is a commons that connects us. In his 1967 essay Breathing the Future
and the Past" astronomer Harlow Shapely demonstrates that the atoms you are now
exhaling will spread across the country within a week, and within one year they will have
traveled around the entire earth. In this sense our intimate bodily engagement with the air
bridges all terrestrial distance. But today the air has become the site of a double crisis.
The slow emergency of global climate warming has collapsed into the immediate personal
danger of viral propagation. In
this time when the medium that supports our life and hosts
everything we do has become the site of pervasive invisible dangers, the Air Report
proposes that a deeper investigation of our daily personal engagement with the air is more
important than ever.

Throughout the month of August Alex Grnenfelder is conducting a daily series of hyper-
local air reports from his balcony in the West End of Vancouver Canada, on unceded
Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh territories.

The reports are tweeted in realtime at twitter.com/airtasting, later on facebook at
#ThisWorldOfAir https://www.facebook.com/hashtag/ThisWorldOfAir and archived on
airtasting.com.

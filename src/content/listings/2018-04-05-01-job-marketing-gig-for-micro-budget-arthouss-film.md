Marketing Gig for Micro-budget Arthouse film

This gig would involve working with the filmmaker to co-design, schedule and execute 
marketing initiatives to promote a Vancouver based arthouse film. Maximizing an uber 
tight budget, the campaign will involve the use of experimental and creative marketing 
tactics to generate public interest. Many of these marketing initiatives are already in their 
conceptual stages, but you would help flesh out their logistical details, and organize 
them into a cohesive timeline.

This gig would be suitable for a beginner looking to build experience in film or fine art 
marketing. Would be a fit for someone who may not have much experience or is a 
student in a post secondary (degree / certificate / diploma) program, and is interested in 
starting work on a micro-budget production to begin building a portfolio for a small 
honorarium ($400).

For more information about the gig, or to apply, please send a cover letter, resume or 
expression of interest to psychicconstructfilms@gmail.com.

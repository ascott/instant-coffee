ORGANIZATION:
Langley Arts Council

DEADLINE:
Jan. 19, 2024

WEBSITE:
[http://langleyarts.ca](http://langleyarts.ca)

JOB DESCRIPTION:
The Galley Assistant will work alongside the Langley Arts Councils Executive
Director to implement and expand the Art in Found Spaces, Gallery Exhibition
Program.

The Art in Found Spaces Program includes three exhibition spaces:

The Walkway Gallery is located at the Timms Community Centre.
The Civic Gallery is located at the Township of Langley Civic Facility.
The Main Hall Gallery is located at the Aldergrove Kinsmen Centre.

Role Requires:

Candidate must be able to commit to part-time hours, 6-hour shifts, generally
Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday from 10:00 am " 4:00pm
Able to work independently and collaboratively as a team.
A keen eye for detail.
Be organized and responsible; you must get work done and problem solve without
constant supervision
Excellent correspondence (internally and externally).
Must be a team player. The LAC is a collaborative environment, and we are looking
to find an addition to our hard-working team.
Someone who takes both initiative and direction.
Knowledge of the array of art practices and art history.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
Tasks and responsibilities associated with this role are;

Answer phone calls, and direct inquiries.
Provide general support to visitors, members, and volunteers.
Communicate with clients, customers, members, staff, and supervisors.
Curate and organize exhibitions and or display(s) in the Arts Councils four gallery
spaces.
Develop exhibition concepts, select artworks or artifacts, determine their placement
and arrangement, and work to create engaging and informative displays.
Support artists or contributors through the exhibition process.
Write exhibition texts or labels to provide context and interpretation for visitors.
Maintain the Programming and Exhibitions filling system, and update when
necessary.
Assist in the coordination of
opening receptions and artist talks.
Manage the display(s), including restocking the gift shop, pricing, and handling
sales transactions.
Responsible for the care, preservation, and management of the collection.
Assist in the documentation and cataloging of exhibited items, including label
creation and attaching identifying information when necessary.
Work alongside the Events Coordinator and Arts Coordinator to develop
educational programs and initiatives to engage the public with the collection and
exhibitions.
Take part in the preparation and distribution of promotional materials for the
exhibition, such as flyers and posters.
Maintain a clean and organized exhibition space, ensuring it remains presentable
throughout the exhibition.
Collect visitor feedback and compile reports on attendance, interactions, and
overall success of the exhibition.
Collaborate with other team members to brainstorm ideas for future exhibitions and
contribute to the development of the exhibition program.
Attend relevant meetings, training sessions, and workshops to further enhance
knowledge and skills related to exhibition operations.
Adhere to health and safety guidelines, ensuring the well-being of both visitors and
colleagues during the exhibition.

QUALIFICATIONS:
Previous experience will be considered an asset.

Please include a Cover Letter and let us know why you are applying for the position.

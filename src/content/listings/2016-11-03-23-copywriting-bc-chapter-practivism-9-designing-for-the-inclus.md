Practivism 9: Designing for the Inclusive Future
Thursday, November 17, 2016. 7 pm (doors at 6:30)
Telus World of Science
Tickets: $45 general / $35 GDC members and students at sfuwoodwards.ca 

The Society of Graphic Designers of BC (GDCBC) presents an evening of talks by 
three designers working in the fields of inclusive design.

Speakers include Antionette Carroll (Creative Reaction Lab Group St. Louis, AIGA's 
Diversity and Inclusion Task Force); Parker McLean (In the Rye Creative, Vancouver) 
on crafting human-centered design systems online; and Sam Holleran (The Design 
Trust, NYC) on creating social justice, design education and environmentally 
sustainable public space

To find out more, visit practivism.ca
http://practivism.ca

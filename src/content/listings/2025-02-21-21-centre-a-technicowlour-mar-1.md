Created by elika mojtabaei + Aryo Khakpour and in collaboration with Alanna Ho, ellis
cheadle, Jaewoo Kang and SF Ho, this exhibition is an installation and performance
exploring the intersections of mythology, memory, and sensory experience. Inspired by a
process that began as a response to the Iranian film The Cow (1969), TechniCowlour
transforms nostalgia into a portal to presence and asks: What does it mean to live
between worlds that carry us across times?

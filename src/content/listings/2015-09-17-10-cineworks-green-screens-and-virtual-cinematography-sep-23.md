Cineworks presents:

Green Screens and Virtual Cinematography: A Two-Part Workshop in Visual
Effects for All

September 23 – 24
6.00pm – 10.00pm
$110 Cineworks Members (+ taxes)
$140 General Admission (+ taxes)
Instructors: David Feuillatre and Naim Sutherland

REGISTRATION: https://green-screens-workshop.eventbrite.ca

Looking to learn more about green screen filmmaking and virtual
cinematography? Our upcoming hands-on workshop will give you the tools and
advanced knowledge you will need to create your next digital masterpiece as
a creator, or to understand the implications of the process inside-out, for
working in production roles.
Learn from award winning VFX director David Feuillatre (Avatar, The Dark
Knight) and accomplished local DOP Naim Sutherland (Anxious Oswald Green,
Charlotte's Song) in this great workshop, taking place over two nights,
from 6.00pm to 10.00pm next week.

This workshop is ideal for:
Independent Filmmakers
Youtubers
Marketing creatives
Hobbyists

In the industry, for:
Directors
Cinematographers / DPs
Producers
Writers
Production Crew

Participants may want to use this workshop as a pre-­production planning
session for a project already in development.

Participants are welcome to bring their own laptop for additional hands-on
time with software, but this is not essential. The workshop will use Adobe
After Effects, so it is recommended that participants who wish to bring a
laptop without the software install the 30-Day Trial, closer to the event:
http://adobe.ly/1SYIrf2

INSTRUCTORS
David Feuillatre, Visual Effects Supervisor

David is an award winning Visual Effects Supervisor, Director, and
Documentary Photographer. In advance of running a series of VFX workshops
for indie filmmakers and media artists at Cineworks this Fall, David joins
us to give a presentation on his professional practice and diverse
accomplishments. Combining fluent knowledge in storytelling, documentary
and visual effects techniques, David trained at ESRA Film School in Paris
and the International Center of Photography in NYC and applies his skills
to big­budget blockbusters and personal documentary adventures.
As a VFX Supervisor and Senior Cloth Technical Director, David has worked
for companies such as Sony Pictures Imageworks (Los Angeles and Vancouver),
Framestore (London), Weta Digital (New Zealand) and MPC (Vancouver), with a
filmography including three Best Visual Effects Academy Award winners and
one in selection: James Cameron’s Avatar, Christopher Nolan’s The Dark
Knight, Life of Pi, and Superman: Man of Steel. He has taught his skills
amongst schools in Canada, France and Nepal, currently holding a faculty
position at Think Tank Training Centre in North Vancouver, elected best
school in computer graphics in Canada and third worldwide in 2015.

Naim Sutherland, Director of Photography
Acclaimed for his stunning optical style, Naim is a visual narrator who has
a passion for fantasy and period genres. His lens choices, astute use of
the art design, colour pallet and frame movement to speak in emotions using
tone and imagery throughout, has won him several awards including best
cinematography at the Vancouver Short Film Festival and a Leo Award both in
2014.
From conception to completion, Naim offers integral cinematic consultation
on achieving precise looks and feelings to each piece that he solidifies in
the final stages through articulated use of shading and colour control.
Through extensive commercial work, Naim has created content for such
clients as the Special Olympics of Canada, the Auditor General of BC,
Parkinson’s Society, BC Mining Museum and Foot Locker Europe Apparel.

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society (est.1980) is an artist-run
production and exhibition centre that supports independent filmmakers and
media artists. We engage our membership and the broader community through
initiatives and services that foster dialogue and experimentation with the
moving image.

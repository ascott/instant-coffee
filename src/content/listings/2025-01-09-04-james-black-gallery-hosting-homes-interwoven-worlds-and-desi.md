Hosting Home(s):
Interwoven Worlds and Desires explores collective and fractured
ideas of what home is through our personal and collective hopes, histories and
futures. Through mixed-media art, photography, film, family archives, and collective
poetry, Jesse Medrano-Ramos, Moe Yang, Naomi Leung, Neven Marelj, and
Sagorika Naomi Azad Haques exhibition co-creates a space grounding us in the
homes possible beyond binaries and borders.

We explore the intimacies of distance, imposition of borders, and embodiment of
futures that invite more vibrant and tender conceptions of home and place across so-
called Canada, the Philippines, Taiwan, Bangladesh, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Japan,
and Croatia.

Amid a time of multiple genocides, revolutions, and climate crises intimately shaping
our personal and artistic lives, our heritages and futures, we are collectively
examining questions like " How do we see and understand home? What languages
and lands are we at home in? How do you grieve and celebrate home? How can
gaps be generative spaces for understanding, negotiating, and (re)creating histories
and senses of home? What is homes role for mobilizing solidarities? How can we
locate senses of place, space, and futures whilst both amid the core and margins of
the empire? As archivists, artists, researchers, teachers, and students amid the
presences of fascism, colonialism, civilizational collapse, what are our
responsibilities in the face of past and future erasures?

Shudder Gallery Presents:

F for Fetish

Opening reception Swarm Sept 9th 7-11pm

Exhibition runs Sept 10th-17th

Works by:

Marlaina Buch
Patrick Cruz
F.E.B.A.R. 
Joseph Jacques
Ross Angus Macaulay
Lauren Marsden
Melissa Paget

Curated by Alan Kollins

The title for this exhibition takes its name, in part, from the Orson

Wells film F for Fake. Wells' film contemplates forgery as a means to

adopt purpose, status and potential salvation. F for Fetish posits this

equation in the realm of cultural hording: compiling artifacts in an

attempt to heal, mend and provide agency for its user. A site of object

replacement or a [hyper real] material fetishism that extends to pop icons,

sub culture style, citizenship, women in uniform, dumpster diving, internet

blogging and beyond. The extent to which the act of hording completes

subjectivity is debatable and may in fact leave a counterfeit notion of

self in its wake. F for Fetish grapples with this paradox: the

signs employed are valued for completing a lack, plugging holes with

ephemera that at times appear vacuous and bankrupt from their original use.

at VIVO Media Arts (2625 Kaslo St., Vancouver)
vivomediaarts.com/programming/shiki-ku

Vancouver-based soundscape artist Yota Kobayashi transforms VIVO Media Arts
Centre with Shiki & Ku, an immersive audiovisual installation featuring two
thematically paired works: Shinshiki and Ksou. Rooted in the Japanese
philosophical concepts of shiki (form, tangible reality) and k (emptiness,
impermanence), the exhibition interweaves spatial sound, generative visuals, and
cultural symbolism. Shinshiki was created with visual artist Ryo Kanda (Tokyo),
string ensemble Quartetto Maurice (Turin), and historian Rurihiko Hara (Shizuoka);
Ksou in collaboration with Kanda, Japanese calligrapher Aiko Hatanaka (Tokyo),
and flutist Mark Takeshi McGregor (Vancouver). Together, the works offer a
contemplative journey through the interplay of material presence and intangible
essence.

Shiki & Ku is co-presented by VIVO Media Arts Centre and Formscape Arts, in
partnership with Vancouver New Music and IM4 Media Lab, with the support of the
Canada Council for the Arts, UBC School of Music, UBC ICICS, and Epson, and
developed with the participation of the British Columbia Arts Council, Creative BC
and the Province of BC.

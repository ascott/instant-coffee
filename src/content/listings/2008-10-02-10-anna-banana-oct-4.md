*Anna Banana, AKA Dr. Anna Freud Banana *
"Dr. Banana"is an entrepreneur who creates interactive events and
performances that parody contemporary life and poke fun at consumer
values. She weaves humor through her work, softening the edges of her
critique. In 1968, her students at the New School gave her her moniker,
and in 1970, after falling into a box of bananas, she used it herself
and in 1985, made it "real" with a legal name change. Her name always
raises the question "what is real?" - an ongoing theme in her creative
work. A member of the "Specific Research Institute," Anna parodies
scientific research in such works as Bananas in Distress, broadcast on
CBU's On the Road Again in Sept. 1998.
Through her newsletter, the Banana Rag, begun in 1971 as part of her
town Fool project, she discovered the International Mail-Art Network,
and was an instant convert. This network provided an ongoing supply of
banana news and art, a sense of community, and affirmation of her
conceptual approach to art. Banana supports her art endeavours by
producing custom, full-colour stamp editions, design & production jobs,
magazine articles, workshops, performances and public events.

On Saturday October 4, Dr. Banana will share some of the history of the
International Mail Art movement and her early involvement with ATCs.
She will also lead a hands-on art workshop on creating backgrounds and
patterns for ATCs and other small formats using frottage and blown-ink
processes.

All materials are provided, and all participants will also receive
samples of Strathmore ATC Paper generously donated by Opus Framing and
Art Supplies.

We are pleased to announce that the presentation of the 2016 Portfolio 
Prize Award will be held at the Audain Gallery at SFU, Vancouver, 
Monday, April 18 at 6:30pm. A reception will be held immediately 
following the event. The Board of Directors of the prize invite you to join 
them and the artists in a celebratory ceremony. 

Conceived by artists for artists, The Portfolio Prize aims to encourage 
the development of young artists through early career support. 

The winners of this years awards are: 

Colleen Brown - Emerging Artist Award ($10 000)
Derya Akay - Emerging Artist Award ($5000)

Del Hillier - BFA Prize ($5000)
Gabi Dao - BFA Distinction ($1000)

"We were thrilled to receive such a high level of applications and 
nominations for this inaugural Portfolio Prize. The strength of artists 
emerging in Vancouver is a credit to the schools, artists and 
community. I would like to thank our jury and nominators for their hard 
and thoughtful work. Through their discussions it became apparent that 
artistic practice today is multifarious and as such complicated to judge 
vertically instead of celebrating equal, if different, accomplishments. 
Wanting to both maintain the impact of a single large award and 
respond to the juries deliberations, the Board agreed to reorganize the 
awards resulting in the realization of a second place BFA award of 
$1000 in addition to the $5000 award planned, and restructuring the 
Emerging Artists prizes in two increments of $5000 and $10 000. With 
this decision we will be now distributing $21 000 in prize money and 
feel assured of the incredible impact this injection will make on the lives 
of these young artists"
-Lee Plested, Prize Director

The Jury has stated that they "were happy to give the foundation's 
inaugural awards to some very deserving artists who exhibit dedication, 
ambition, bravery and skill.The emerging artist awards go to Colleen 
Brown and Derya Akay, who both work in the range of expanded 
sculpture, engaging both aesthetic and social concerns. While Del 
Hillier and Gabi Dao are recent BFA graduates who have bold 
multidisciplinary practices and are part of a new generation of artists 
contributing to the culture of our city."

Please join us in presenting these much deserved awards and 
celebrate these artists distinction.

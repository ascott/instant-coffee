Clues

Exhibition Dates: May 18th-June 7th, 2018

Opening: Friday, May 18th, 7-11pm

Clues is a group exhibition featuring new work by six emerging artists and
organizers from Winnipeg, Manitoba: Kristina Banera, Hannah Doucet, Katrina
Mendoza, Michael Mogatas, John Patterson and Rachael Thorleifson. The
exhibition will examine how perception and memory are managed by objects
which occupy them. The images and objects prepared for the exhibition each
play a part in touring real or fictional narratives to retrieve information
from personal, found or invented artifacts. All artists will be present at
the opening, please come by and say hello !

Kristina Banera is an emerging interdisciplinary artist from Lockport,
Manitoba, currently based in Winnipeg, Manitoba. Her work often integrates
sculpture and digital medial to explore psychology of space, the home, and
the interreleations that constitute it. She received her Bachelor of Fine
Art honours degree at the University of Manitoba, with a concentration in
studio art. Banera has been exhibited nationally in galleries, artist run
centres and alternative spaces.

Hannah Doucet is an artist and arts educator from Winnipeg, Canada. She
received her BFA Honours from the University of Manitoba in 2015. Doucet
has exhibited in alternative spaces and artist-run centers across Canada.
Recent solo exhibitions include I wondered when my body would deflate at
PLATFORM Centre for photographic + digital arts (Winnipeg, MB) in 2018 and
I Never Recognized Her Except in Fragments at The New Gallery (Calgary, AB)
in 2016. In 2017 Doucet was the inaugural winner of the PLATFORM
Photography award, participated in the Visual + Digital Emerging BAiR at
the Banff Centre and was Mikw Chiyms artist in residence at Wiinibekuu
high school in Waskaganish, QC. She facilitates art workshops throughout
the city of Winnipeg through Art City, Misericordia Health Centre, Ndinawe
Youth Resource Centre and Plugin Institute of Contemporary Art. She is a
co-founder of Flux Gallery and co-founder and co-director of Blinkers Art
and Project Space.

Katrina Marie Mendoza is a Winnipeg artist who combines schematic digital
drawings with found material in continual reassembly. Her interests lie in
drawings as projections of space, the possibility of their becoming objects
and the limits of their isolated vocabulary.

Michael Mogatas is an artist often working between the realms of painting,
screen printing and photography. Recent exhibitions of his work include
"Recent Works" at Flux Gallery "puisser la Batterie/ Draining the Battery"
at Le Studio in 2016. Michael was co-director of C-Space Gallery which
operated from 2013-2015.

John Patterson is a visual artist and writer living in Winnipeg, Manitoba.
Often combining intuitive narrative explorations with visual or text assets
taken from the commercial sector, quick phone pics, interviews or critical
texts, Patterson uses a methodology which aims to reform the
understandings, memories, or translations of personal experiences. He holds
his BFA from the University of Manitoba and has shown work in galleries and
artist-run centres across Canada, most recently Turismo [2017, Le Studio at
La Maison des Artistes, Winnipeg], in which he developed short-term
histories of the geographical area surrounding the gallery through the
construction of objects, texts and videos. He is a co-founder of Blinkers
Art and Project Space, has written for Canadian Art Magazine and SCAN
publication, and is the co-editor of Monotext, a tri-annual publication of
writing and images.

Rachael Thorleifson is a multi-disciplinary visual artist based in
Winnipeg, Manitoba. Her practice often uses painting, sculpture, and
installation as a means of exploring subjective understandings and
emotions. She received a Bachelor of Fine Arts (Honours) from the
University of Manitoba in 2015. Rachael has exhibited in galleries,
artist-run centres and alternative spaces throughout the city of Winnipeg
and across Canada. Her most recent solo exhibition I dreamt [last night] my
teeth were crumbling (Flux Gallery, Winnipeg) featured a collection of new
paintings and text-based installations exploring love, death, religion,
romance and heartbreak. Rachael is currently working on a new project that
will examine how the sacred quality of objects and places relate to death
and her own familial identity. She is the co-founder and co-director of
Blinkers Art and Project Space located on Hargrave Street in Downtown Winnipeg.

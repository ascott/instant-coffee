Third Friday reading series
Friday, March 22
1391 Commercial Dr, Vancouver
730pm

Join us at People's Co-op Bookstore for a reading.

Jennifer Zilm is the author of two books of poetry, Waiting Room and The Missing Field. She 
recently wrote Proust's in search of lost time -- in 80 pages and set in Surrey. She presently in 
the midst of early research into animal grief and studies on colour. She also throws out books 
for a living. 

Candie Tanaka is currently writing a first novel with the working title of Tanaka and Co. and 
penning a suite of poems about employment on the waterfront. Side hustles include wrangling 
Drag Queens and being the ED at the International Centre of Arts and Technology, a literary 
focused community makerspace in Vancouver.

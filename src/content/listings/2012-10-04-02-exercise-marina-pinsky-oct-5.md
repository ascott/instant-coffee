Exercise is pleased to announce:

Marina Pinsky
Solitaire

October 5 - November 11

Please join us for the opening reception Friday October 5th, 7-10 pm

Marina Pinsky (b. Moscow, Russia 1986) received her BFA from the
School of the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston in 2008 and her MFA from the
University of California, Los Angeles in 2012. Her work has been
exhibited at Venus over Manhattan, NY, Cleopatra's, Berlin as well as
Workspace and Night Gallery in Los Angeles, among others. Her work has
been featured in Art Review Magazine, Photograph Magazine and on
Artforum.com. She currently has an installation on view at the Los
Angeles County Museum of Art. Marina Pinsky lives and works in Los
Angeles.

An edition of prints will be published with the exhibition.

Gallery hours 12-5 Friday and Saturday (ring buzzer)

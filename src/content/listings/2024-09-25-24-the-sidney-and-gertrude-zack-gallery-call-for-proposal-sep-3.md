2025 Exhibitions
Call for Proposals
The Sidney and Gertrude Zack Gallery

The Sidney and Gertrude Zack Gallery, at the JCC of Greater Vancouver, invites artists
of all ages, religions, cultures, career stages, and backgrounds, to submit proposals for
exhibitions in 2025.
Proposals for art in any medium ( mixed media, performance, 2D and 3D visual arts,
video/film, will be accepted. Individual and group shows will be considered.
Submissions will be accepted until

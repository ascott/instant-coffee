Application Deadline: March 1, 2012

Position available: Gallery Coordinator

Job Description:

This is a part time position that includes media and public relations 
through Mailchimp, Wordpress web site, Twitter, and Facebook. 
Experience with these platforms and with use of Photoshop for design 
of materials relating to the exhibits is essential. The gallery coordinator 
works to maintain and build the visibility of the gallery within the arts 
community of Vancouver and beyond. Planning and execution of 
gallery events, and basic office administration will also be part of the 
job.

Desired/Required Skills:

• Understanding of Social Media platforms for marketing purposes

• Proficiency with Photoshop for design of marketing kits

• Knowledge of contemporary art

(preferably some educational or professional experience)

• Strong written and interpersonal skills

• Fluency in written and spoken English

• Some French an asset

• Positive and energetic personality with enthusiasm for the gallery and 
its potential

How to Apply:

Please email your resume with a brief cover letter to 
info@barongallery.ca, subject line: Gallery Coordinator position.

The
Call for Artists is open until March 7, 2025. Artists can choose how to participate:

In-person Fair at Nathan Phillips Square + Online Fair, or

Exclusively online at TOAF.ca

Through our newly implemented Travel Bursary Program, out of province artists are
eligible to apply for a Travel Award of up to $1,500 to support the costs associated
with participating at the in-person Fair at Nathan Phillips Square in Toronto.

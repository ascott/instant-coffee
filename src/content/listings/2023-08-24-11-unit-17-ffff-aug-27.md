F.F.F.F (2023, 2 channel HD animation, 26m15s). marks the final exhibition for Unit 17
at 2954 West 4th Avenue & is the first collaborative installation by Paul Kajander &
Isabelle Pauwels. As of 1 September, 2023 UNIT/PITT will operate from 2954
West 4th
Avenue.

Unit 17 will re-open in 2024, initially as a micro cinema, with a solo exhibition by Gabi
Dao. Additional programming will take place in Vancouver, Toronto, Turin & Shanghai in
the coming months.

Paul Kajanders practice adopts layering and adjacency through compositional, spatial,
durational and asso- ciative strategies across drawing, installation, sound, video and
collaborative activities. In addition to solo proj- ects, he presents work as part of artist
duo HaeAhn Paul Kwon Kajander. Previous exhibition contexts include Franz Kaka;
Toronto, Trilobite et le Pneu; Montral, the Small Arms Inspection Building;
Mississauga, Forest City Gallery; London, Modern Fuel; Kingston, the New Media
Society; Vancouver, the Hammer Museum; Los Angeles, The SFU Audain Gallery;
Vancouver, Daniel Faria Gallery; Toronto, the Seoul Museum of Art; Seoul, The Real
DMZ Project; Cheorwon-gun, Art Sonje Center; Seoul and the Western Front;
Vancouver. He is rep- resented by Franz Kaka in
Toronto.

Isabelle Pauwels is currently based in Montral. She received a BFA from Emily Carr
Institute of Art and Design (Vancouver) in 2001, and an MFA from the School of the Art
Institute of Chicago in 2006. In 2009 she was the inaugural winner of the Brink Award,
granted to an early-career artist working in Washington, Oregon, or British Columbia. In
2013 she was a finalist for the Sobey Award. Recently her work has been exhibited at
Dazibao and the Muse dart contemporain, Montral; Unit 17, Vancouver, and at the
Experimental Media and Performing Arts Centre at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute,
Troy, New York.

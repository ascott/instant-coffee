Analog

Jane Grocott, Paige Quinn, Connor Skillen

Opening Friday, May 16th, 6-10pm

August Studios

1320 E Pender St, Vancouver

Analog is an exhibition of new work by Jane Grocott, Connor Skillen and Paige
Quinn at August Studios. Large oil paintings are interrupted by smaller explorations
to form a collection of distinct yet interconnected pictures. These three artists have
been working alongside one other in the same studio building in Vancouver for
exactly two years, absorbing similar influences from their mutual surroundings yet
taking divergent paths in their work. Though their practices run independently
paralleled, moments of crossover"by way of impromptu critiques of each other's
work and quick life updates as one artist arrives to begin painting and another leaves
"are revealed on the surfaces of each canvas. These fleeting encounters influence
the trajectory of each painting and initiate a framework of conversation between the
works.

Analog is on view at August Studios until May 24th.

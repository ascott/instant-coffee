Richmond Art Gallery Artist Salon Series:
ArtRich 2025 Exhibition Preparations for Artists
Wednesday, July 30, 2025
7:00 PM - 8:00 PM

Register: https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/rag-artist-salon-artrich-2025-exhibition-
preparations-for-artists-tickets-1432303330819

The Richmond Arts Coalition (RAC), in partnership with the Richmond Art Gallery,
hosts the biennial exhibition ArtRich this winter 2025. This juried exhibition features
emerging and established artists from Richmond, the Lower Mainland, and nearby
communities to exhibit together and celebrate local talent.

Meet with RAC for insights into the application and jurying process and receive
assistance documenting your artwork for exhibition application.
We will have a digital camera and lighting set up for those who need assistance
documenting their artwork for this open-call exhibition.
*Limited spaces. For ages 18+.

*As with many free events, we experience a LOT of no-shows from those who
register. Please join the waitlist and we will release more tickets as they come
available or if someone cancels.

ARTRICH Exhibition Information:
The Richmond Arts Coalition (RAC), with support from the Richmond Art Gallery and
financial support from the City of Richmond and the BC Arts Council, is pleased to
present ArtRich 2025, a juried exhibition for local artists at the Richmond Art Gallery.
This exhibition will take place from Gallery from November 29th, 2025, to December
31st, 2025, with an opening reception on November 28th, 2025. ArtRich 2025
provides an opportunity for emerging and established artists from Richmond, the
Lower Mainland, and nearby communities to exhibit together and celebrate local
talent. Entries are not expected to fit a specific theme - we want to see the work you
are most excited to share!

For more details on how to apply, visit the
https://richmondartscoalition.com/artrich2025/.

About Richmond Arts Coalition:

Richmond.

advocates for the arts and artists of Richmond.

About the Artist Salon Series:
The Artist Salon features art professionals leading inspirational artist talks and
professional development for visual artists each month from February to November.
Programs are either live-streamed via the Zoom platform or hosted in person. Past
livestreams may be viewed as videos on the https://www.richmondartgallery.org/rag-
at-home/ragathomevideo page.

We are partnering with UV to invite Khotin, who will be performing at CICA Vancouver,
as part of our live performance programming of the upcoming exhibition, Ksawery
Komputery: RRGGBB.

Join us on March 8, 2024 at CICA Vancouver for a PHYgital experience that merges
Khotin's live performance with Ksawery's interactive light art. As a collaboration
between CICA Vancouver and UV, this one-of-a-kind event blends the physical and
digital realms, creating an immersive atmosphere that transcends traditional
performance boundaries. Get ready to be mesmerized by the sonic journey of Khotin,
the brainchild of Edmonton-based electronic artist Dylan Khotin-Foote. With a decade
of genre-bending experimentation under his belt, Khotin's music is a unique blend of
warped synth, breakbeats, and piano fused with wistful samples and abstract snippets.

Likewise, prepare for an unforgettable experience as you delve into the
imaginative
world of Ksawery Komputery's artworks while getting lost in the extraordinary sounds
of Khotin. This fusion of unique visual art and musical performance promises to be
extraordinary!

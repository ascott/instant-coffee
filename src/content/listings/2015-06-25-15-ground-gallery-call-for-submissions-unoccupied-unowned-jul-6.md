Ground is currently accepting submissions for its first exhibition: a
constellation of installations, performances, and events throughout
Vancouver and the surrounding area. In order to reflect upon the nature of
not occupying a permanent location, the parts that constitute this
exhibition will be situated in several unoccupied, unowned, or squatted
spaces. Contributors should bear in mind that there will be very little
supervision over the space, and that security of the artworks cannot be
offered. Submissions in any media may relate to issues of property,
shelter, housing, precarity, contingency, or impermanence.

Submission Procedure:

1. Proposal: Briefly describe your proposal (maximum length of one page).
Provide as many details as possible. To find out more details about the
potential sites of the exhibition, please contact the curators at the email
below before the deadline.

2. Artist Statement: Discuss how your proposed project is situated in
relation to your current artistic practice and the broader context of your
work and interests.

3. Support: Please include up to a maximum of 10 images (of past or
proposed work), sketches, outlines, scores, or project documentation.

4. Curriculum Vitae: Maximum length of two pages. Please include all
relevant contact information.

Please send all documents in a single file (either zipped or in a single
pdf) to: info@groundgallery.ca by Monday, July 6.

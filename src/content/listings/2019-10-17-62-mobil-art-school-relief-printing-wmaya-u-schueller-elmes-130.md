Introduction to Relief Printing with Maya U Schueller-Elmes
Oct.26, 1:30 - 4:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada
Learn the
basics of relief printing - a versatile and cost effective type of printmaking that you
can use to make anything from printed cards and t-shirts to fine art prints. In this taster class
you will learn how to choose the right gouge on your lino cutter and use the tool safely, how to
transfer your design onto your printing block, how to carve out your design and how to pull a
successful print without the need for a printing press!

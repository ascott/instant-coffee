Richmond Museum presents Doors Open Richmond

June 8"9, 2024

The Richmond Museum presents the 17th annual Doors Open Richmond this spring
from June 8"9. This years edition features more than 40 unique sites representing
the citys cultural diversity and rich heritage. Visitors can go behind-the-scenes at
each organization or business, through tours and hands-on activities. Select
highlights from this years program include: touring a working ambulance station at
BC Ambulance Services, trying Punjabi food and learning about Sikhism at
Nanaksar Gurdwara Gursikh Temple, writing your name in the Cyrillic alphabet at the
Ukrainian Community Society of Ivan Franko, and tasting wines at Sanduz Estate
Wines. Two new partner sites can also be discovered through videos that will
premiere online during the events weekend: Richmond Public Works and the
Sudnya Dance
Academy.

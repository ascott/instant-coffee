Invisibility, But At What Cost?

Recent works by Noah Spivak

Opening Reception: 8 pm Thursday January 15 2015

AVENUE, 165 E. Hastings, Vancouver BC

January 16: Public viewing from 1-9 pm

Jan 17 & Jan 18: Viewings by appointment only. Please contact nspivak@ecuad.ca

With this collection of work, artist Noah Spivak asks us to consider
photography through its process and relationship to the material world. His
fascination with the human senses, the ambiguity of the everyday and the
space in which the art experience occurs is culminated here in a series of
works that explore the ways in which we experience the small phenomena of
the reality we inhabit. By employing minimal aesthetics and techniques of
abstraction the artist works to showcase our subjectivity in experience and
draw attention to the process of making. Relying not on image but material,
these works serve as a reminder of the physical and chemical processes of
photography allowing the viewer space to consider the value of image in our
visually saturated culture.

#OMGSMAZ (On Main Gallery Social Media Art Zones) @Khatsahlano! Music +
Art Festival. July 13, 2013.

On Saturday, July 13, On Main Gallery will be at the Khatsahlano! Music +
Art Festival. This is Vancouvers biggest, free outdoor music and art event
curated by the Arrival Agency.

This years festival will feature 12 stages showcasing more than 50 of
Vancouvers top musical performers plus eclectic pods of distinctive local
artisans and street performers. Local merchants turn Kitsilanos legendary
West 4th Avenue into a gigantic, 10-block street fair with a wide variety
of food options, bustling patios, giveaways, and special activities.
11:00am to 9:00 pm. West 4th Ave (between Burrard & MacDonald).

On Main Gallery will present #OMGSMAZ, a super-cool social media art zone,
part of This Happened Here artist pod village in the 2600 block of West
4th Ave, (between Trafalagar St and Stephens St).

The #OMGSMAZ team are the official media artists documenting this years
festival. We are on hand to help you create lasting community moments and
collective memories by posting This Happened Here images online to social
media platforms.

Using Twitter, Instagram, GIFboom, Vine, Facebook and other networks, we
will provide the platform for community-led image creation and celebration!
Join us with your smart phones for a wide range of photo opportunities and
activities for your image making and viewing pleasures. There will be
interactive video booths, emoticon photo backdrops, QR code games, and
installations. All colours, all ages and all recording devices welcome.
Come find us on 4th Ave or follow us online.

Not just another info booth - we are bringing something totally new and
fresh to street festivals. #OMGSMAZ is a vibrant public digital space led
by visionary Artistic Director Paul Wong and the SMAZ team Tommy Chain:
Snapchatographer, Trevor Discoe: Living Online Personality, Patrick
Daggitt: Code Breaker, Olivia Fauland: Professional Screenshotter, Matt
Troy: Instagram Text Artist, and Kenneth Yuen: Meme Historian.

#OMGSMAZ is a public art project made possible by the City of Vancouver
Community & Neighbourhood Arts Development Program and the Kitsilano 4th
Avenue Business Association.

Artist Talk
Rebecca Chaperon 
Peripheral Visions

Tuesday, September 13, 7pm

Art Rental & Sales Showroom
1st Floor Annex, Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby Street, Vancouver

Join Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery for an artist 
talk with Featured Artist Rebecca Chaperon on Tuesday, 
September 13, 2016 at 7pm.
Rebecca Chaperon's process often begins with the idea of place. 
Her paintings act as a means of storytelling, as landscapes meet 
flat geometry and emotive undercurrents. Chaperon's current show, 
Peripheral Visions, encompasses pieces from three distinctive 
bodies of work: Imprint, Tesseract and Antarticus. A peripheral 
vision is something seen out of the corner of the eye, on the outer 
edge of your visual perception. The work in Peripheral Visions 
revolves around how we perceive the mysterious world around us 
and our connection to it. Metaphysical spaces are presented as 
possible alternate dimensions, symbolic interpretations or enigmatic 
escape hatches, with the understanding that the space we exist in 
is more mysterious than the limits of our perception.
Chaperon's work will be featured in the showroom through 
September 23, 2016

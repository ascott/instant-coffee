Glint Publication Launch with Natasha Katedralis and Tiziana La Melia
Friday, February 21, 2025, 7:00-9:00pm

People Gallery
4277 Fraser Street
Vancouver BC

Or Gallery is delighted to announce the launch of
our most recent publication Glint.
Please join us for a celebration with readings from Emily Fedoruk, Natasha Katedralis
and Dana Qaddah and a DJ set by Thomas Weideman at People Gallery from 7:00-
9:00pm on Friday, February 21.

This publication is presented in conjunction with Tiziana La Melias exhibition Country
Mouse City Mouse Hamster, on view at Or Gallery from February 6 to May 10, 2025. A
performative reading of the magazine with artists Kiel Torres, Christian Vistan, and
James Albers will take place at Or Gallery on Saturday, February 22.

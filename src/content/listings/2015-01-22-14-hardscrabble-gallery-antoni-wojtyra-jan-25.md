Antoni Wojtyra
The Above Proposition is Occasionally Useful
January 25 - February 22, 2015

Opening reception Sunday, January 25, 2-5 PM.

Hardscrabble Gallery is delighted to exhibit a new series of unique book
works and works on paper by Antoni Wojtyra.

For his exhibition at Hardscrabble, Wojtyra has continued his experiments
with folded, two sided, painted paper. These monochromatic forms will be
echoed into a new series of books each week. These examples look to extend
the spatial sensitivities of the works on the wall by exploding them into
your hand via the publications.

Initial feelings of Munari and Matisse, Wojtyra's folding simultaneously
creates and implodes dimensional space, elasticizing the rectangle. A
combination of painting and folded paper cutouts exploit the double sided
nature of both the fold and the role of the surface in painting.

Antoni Wojtyra is a Polish born, Vancouver based artist. Recent exhibition
include Burrard Arts Foundation, Vancouver; Bed-Stuy Love Affair, NYC; and
CSA Space, Vancouver.

Artist talk with Cedric, Nathan and Jim Bomford
Thursday, August 7, 6 pm

Vancouver Maritime Museum
1905 Ogden Ave
The talk will be held in the museum

Join us for an illustrated talk with Cedric, Nathan and Jim Bomford who
will share the backstory of the 3+ years leading to the launch of Deadhead,
a floating sculptural installation mounted to a WWII barge and towed by tug
to different locations. The Bomfords will discuss their unique
collaborative process and how distinct phases of research, salvaging
materials, studio production, and assembling on the Fraser River influenced
Deadhead’s final form. This informal presentation will provide
opportunities for conversation and discussion about the artwork’s
references to British Columbia’s west coast and its unique ecologies,
economies, myths and legends.

Deadhead is Presented by Other Sights for Artists’ Projects
Hosted by the Vancouver Maritime Museum
In Heritage Harbour,
1905 Ogden Avenue, Vancouver, BC
June 14 – September 2, 2014

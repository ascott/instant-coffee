It's time for the annual summer book sale at READ Books
From July 12-19 all books are on sale from 20 - 70%

We carry artists' monographs, exhibition catalogues, art, music and film theory, philosophy, 
poetry, fiction, comics, artists' books and zines. 

READ is an ongoing public program of the Libby Leshgold Gallery. It is a social space for the 
fostering, cultivation, and promotion of publication as an artistic practice and aims to further the 
discourse around and support the production of contemporary art and design. READ also 
serves as the bookstore for Emily Carr University and the wider community.

READ Books acknowledges that it is located on unceded territory of the Coast Salish Peoples, 
including the territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), St:l 
and Sl"ilwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

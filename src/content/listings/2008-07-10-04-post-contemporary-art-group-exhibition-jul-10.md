An exhibit featuring contemporary works from Lester Ramsay, Brian G
White, Jeremy Crowle, John Burgess, and Sarah Hoemberg.

Sarah Hoemberg and John Burgess are two 08 Emily Carr Visual Arts
grads. Sarah works primarily with large scale abstract oil painting.

John is exploring local architecture and will be installing a work
referencing the recent demolition of the coastal Arthur Erickson home.

Brian White is an abstract painter working in series. He has been the
manager of all of the Opus art supply stores in Vancouver for many
years and has shown at the VAG a while back.

Jeremy Crowle is a self taught oil painter. He works mostly on large
scale portrait works.

And my work is a combination of painting and sculpture made from
found/new objects which ceremonially reference the unstoppable
renovation of turn of the century homes in Vancouver and the lower
mainland.

There are two spots available for our annual spring residency May 12-20 on the
Toronto Islands.

ALCHEMY is an artist-led international residency offered to make time and space for
participants to explore the intersection between creative and social practices in a
unique community setting.

Founded in 2015 on the Toronto Islands, Alchemys foundational
pillars are food, art, garden and table -- all inspired by the original gardens and
community at the Gibraltar Point Centre for the Arts.
This Residency is designed for artists to focus on their practice in a respectful and
supportive studio environment. Participating artists are encouraged to cultivate
new directions in their work, experiment with process and
materiality techniques, and
share their research, activities, and time with other residency participants and a
thriving community of on site artists.
Shared meals, studio visits, and dialogue with community members and guest artists
offer an opportunity for new perspectives.

Gibraltar Point Centre for the Arts is a peaceful artist retreat nestled against the
magnificent backdrop of Toronto Island - the largest car-free community in North
America and 15 minutes by ferry to Toronto's downtown core.

Cost:$1350.00 plus applicable taxes for accommodations, studio space,
programming, guest artist talks, six shared suppers and the opportunity to participate
in a closing open studio. Interested? Apply before March 1 for an early decision

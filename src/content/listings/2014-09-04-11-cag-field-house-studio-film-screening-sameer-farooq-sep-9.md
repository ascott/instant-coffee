Sameer Farooq and Mirjam Linschooten
September 2014

Canadian artist Sameer Farooq and French artist Mirjam Linschooten began
their artistic collaboration while studying at the Gerrit Rietveld Academie
in Amsterdam. They consider their joint practice as an archeology of the
present. The Museum of Found Objects, with iterations in Cairo, Johnston,
Rhode Island, Toronto and Istanbul, used everyday objects to fuel
alternative ways of engagement across a broad range of physical and
cultural contexts. Something stolen, something new, something borrowed and
something blue (2014) responded directly to the looting of the Egyptian
Museum at Tahrir Square during the Arab Spring. They built a temporary
photo studio in Cairo and worked with a local calligrapher to make

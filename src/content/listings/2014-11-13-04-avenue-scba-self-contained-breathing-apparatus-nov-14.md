(SCBA) Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus

Opening reception
19:00 NOV 14 2014
AVENUE
165 e. Hastings

(SCBA) Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus is an installation of works by
Sung Pil Yoon utilizing air circulation and ventilation to create an
environment with the objective of shaping air flow catered to the
spatiality of a given space.

Works being showcased consists of functional and aesthetic signifiers of
air and purification. Objects such as the electric fan, charcoal filtering
remedies and wet laundry are presented in a way that creates the
encompassing of space by methods other than the physical material. Everyday
objects serve to situate the environment as a place of non-specialized
entity that agitates the air to stimulate the environment by way of
continuous flow in movement.

The works being showcased are circumstantial in their physical presence
which serves only to relay air within its space; and by way of
acknowledging the ephemeral nature of air conditioning, (SCBA) attempts to
treat air molecules much akin to objects that contribute to the altering
and (re)constructing of an environment.

Intro to Music Production & Ableton Live
SIGN UP & MORE INFO: https://musicprod.eventbrite.ca

Expand your music performance and composition methods with this in-depth introduction to 
the multifaceted software, Ableton Live, taught by composer-producer Alexandre Klinke!

9 hours total over 3 sessions // $115, or $85 with any VIVO Producer Membership (+GST)

Session 1: Sat, May 4, 1pm - 4pm
Session 2: Sat, May 11, 1pm - 4pm 
Session 3: Sat, May 18, 1pm - 4pm

Prerequisite: General computer literacy (e.g., saving files, facility with a mouse and 
keyboard). No prior experience with Ableton necessary!

Ableton Live is one of the top music creation and performance platforms available. In this 
workshop students will be introduced to the fundamentals of audio recording, MIDI 
sequencing, mixing, and sound synthesis. They will learn how to use the tools in Ableton 
Live to produce music, experimental sound pieces and create their own synth patches.

One extended subsidy is available for this workshop. More info on registration page.

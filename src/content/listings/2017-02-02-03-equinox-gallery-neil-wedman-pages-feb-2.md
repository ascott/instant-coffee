Neil Wedman: Pages
Opening Reception and Book Launch: Thursday, February 2, 2017
6-8pm
Equinox Gallery 

Equinox Gallery is pleased to present Pages, an exhibition of monochromatic 
works by Neil Wedman. The exhibition will begin on Thursday February 2nd and 
will continue until Saturday February 25th, 2017. An opening reception and book 
launch for the artist will be held at Equinox Gallery on Thursday February 2nd 
from 6 to 8pm.

Over the past ten years, Neil Wedman has been working on a series of ingenious 
monochromatic paintings, watercolours, and drawings that use imagery of the 
spectacle, such as desert rainbows and exploding firework factories, while 
challenging the viewer to see past the sensationalism by advocating for these 
scenes strictly in tones of grey.

Neil Wedman is a Vancouver-based artist with a 35-year studio practice that 
includes painting, drawing, watercolour, and multi-media works. Wedman has 
exhibited extensively across Canada and his works are held in the collections of 
the Vancouver Art Gallery, Microsoft, Surrey Art Gallery, among others. Wedman 
teaches at both Emily Carr University and Simon Fraser University in Vancouver, 
where he lives.

This exhibition is being held in conjunction with the launch of a new monograph 
on the artist entitled Neil Wedman: Selected Monochromatic Paintings and Works 
on Paper. Published by ECU Press and Charles H. Scott Gallery, the book 
features texts by Patrik Andersson, Brady Cranfield and Kate Noble. A two-
person exhibition featuring works by Neil Wedman and Ann Lislegaard will also 
open Friday, February 3rd at the Or Gallery, Vancouver. 

Equinox Gallery is open Tuesday through Saturday, from 10am to 5pm.

Something Big and Invisible

An online exhibition of miniature works in a dollhouse gallery

February - March 2024
Small House Gallery
London, UK
www.smallhousegallery.uk

Solo exhibition by Vancouver artist Seema Shah at Small House Gallery, a dollhouse
gallery in the UK. The exhibition will be unveiled gradually over the next two months on
the gallery's Instagram page (@small_house_gallery), slowly revealing the
conversation between individual works, between rooms, and between floors. From
curator Eldi Dundee: "The works (& their placements) in this show will generate all
sorts of associations in the viewer. No two people will see the same show."

Seema Shah is a self-taught collage artist and writer. She pieces together narratives
that are dark and questioning.
Hinting at something deeper, meaning is expressed
obliquely in her work through the use of metaphor " and, at times, even humour "
creating distance and allowing viewers to find their own connection to it.

This exhibition features miniature 2D & 3D works, including collaged matchboxes and
works with cut-up / erasure poetry. Seema's work has been exhibited in The House of
Smalls, another dollhouse gallery in the UK. She was a recipient of the The Beaumont
Studios' Artists To Watch Award 2022 and a highly commended artist in the 2022 &
2023 Contemporary Collage Magazine Awards.

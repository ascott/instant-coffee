Arts Education
Cedar Bark Basket with Cattail
at CityScape Community ArtSpace
Sunday, July 21, 10am-4pm
with Jessica Silvey

Experience the relaxing and satisfying method of weaving a basket with red cedar bark 
and cattail with master weaver Jessica Silvey. With industry taking over many methods 
of creation, it is reassuring to know that most baskets are still made by hand. Traditions 
of basket weaving can be found all over the world throughout human history. Jessica will 
guide you in creating a basket in the Salish weaving tradition, using local materials.

About Jessica Silvey
Jessica is a self-taught weaver of Coast Salish and Portugese descent, from Egmont, 
BC. She spent many childhood hours with her grandmother in the forest, her favourite 
place to be. She harvests and prepares her own materials, which is the most time-
consuming work. Jessica describes basket weaving as 'my passion as well as therapy 
for my soul'.

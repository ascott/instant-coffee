CALL | City of Surrey | Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre Expansion " Plaza Artwork |
OCT 20

The City of Surrey invites Expressions of Interest (EOI) from professional artists or
artist teams to create a free-standing artwork for the entry plaza of the future Chuck
Bailey Recreation Centre Expansion.

An iconic artwork is envisioned for the plaza that welcomes residents to the facility
and marks the plaza as a communal gathering place. As the neighbourhood
densifies, free and open public space will grow in importance for residents. Surrey
City Centre is already one of the most diverse areas of the lower mainland and that
diversity will increase as people move to Surrey from across the province, the
country, and the world. It is important that all residents, new and old, feel welcome at
the Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre.

Project Budget: $250,000

Details

Please read the Artist Call for complete details: [https://www.surrey.ca/arts-](https://www.surrey.ca/arts-)
culture/public-art/artist-calls-project-updates

How to Apply
Submit your Expression of Interest to Cris Mora at:
Email: publicart@surrey.ca
Subject line: Chuck Bailey Plaza " (Your Name)

Your Expression of Interest should contain the following information:

Contact Details: Name, address, email, and phone number

Letter of Intent (max 300 words): Describe your proposed concept and why it is
suitable for this site.
Artist Statement (max 300 words): Share your current artistic interests and creative
process.

Artist CV: A one-page resume describing your academic, artistic, professional and
other relevant experience.
* group applications should submit one resume for each group member.
Portfolio: Up to 10 images of previous work.
* please include title, date, description (including location, if applicable), and
materials.
2x Slides (8.5 x 11 landscape orientation):
Slide One " Provide a summary of your concept (max 200 words)
Slide Two " Share an excerpt from your portfolio (3-5 images)
To Submit: Combine all pages into a single PDF document (10mb max).

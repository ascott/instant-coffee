WAAP is pleased to present an exhibition of new paintings by JEFFREY HALLBAUER. 
Hallbauers new works are like variations on a theme. The patternistic paintings are 
based on specific portraiture source material; by defamiliarizing them and emphasizing 
their material presence " a vivid dot, a line, a swoop, an evocative plane of colour - and 
by placing these elements up against each other, the paintings are evocative of similar 
experiments in the deconstructing and parsing of language in modernist poetry.

The paintings reveal the patterns of rhythm and syntactical structure that can emerge in 
paintings. By constantly inflecting and estranging the composition, the glimpse the artist 
is providing of the original portrait sources can often feel uroboric, spiralling into itself. 
The paintings, with their fragments of identities becoming unfixed, can feel like an apt 
metaphor for the complexity of contemporary identity. That the repetitions are ordered 
and bound up speaks to the sense we make of the world by articulation. These abstract 
compositions confront our temptation to extract specific meaning from which the painting 
is situated.

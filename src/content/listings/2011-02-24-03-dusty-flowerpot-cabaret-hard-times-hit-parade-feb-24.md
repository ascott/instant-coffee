Dusty Flowerpot Cabaret presents

Hard Times Hit Parade!

February 24th until March 18th, 2011

In case you haven't spotted the the feature in the Westender, read all
about it here! Great article on the Dusty Flowerpot Cabaret by Andrea Warner!

Just released today, a fabulous article in the the Province this morning.

And.. . watch for it... . we'll appear in the Georgia Straight on our
opening day, Thursday!

I think they like us, Vancouver.


Meanwhile, this is it, friends!

Most of you have been with us since the our humble beginnings. And it's
only three more days now until we release another legacy of East Vancouver
performance into the heart of Strathcona. Well over fifty cast and crew
are working hard right now on the final touches. We've extended the run of
this immersive 1930s world of dance delirium for 14 full length shows.
Let's expand the gift, the imagination and the community by spreading the
word together. Please invite your friends!

Hard Times Hit Parade!

At the Russian Hall (600 Campbell Ave in Strathcona)

Feb 24 until March 18, 2011

Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays -- doors at 7pm, shows at 8pm

Sunday matinees -- doors at 2pm, shows at 3pm

(Sun, March 13 will be a Pay-What-You-Can-Performance)

Check out behind the scenes photos of bleacher building and rehearsals on
Facebook.

Follow us @DustyFlowerpot on Twitter

(We'll be announcing surprise cabaret acts throughout the run!)

We've created a a story of cooperation, determination and survival in hard
times with art, music, dance and togetherness as the unifying, uplifting
food for our spirits. You will experience 6 months of dance marathon in one
night -- with dance parties to follow! Costumes encouraged! (Get some
inspiration from our event page -- and be sure to RSVP while you're there!)

You can pick up advance tickets online, www.DustyFlowerpot.org, or at
Highlife, Zulu or RedCat Records.

$20 advance, $25 door.

And do come early to grab a good seat! Cash bar and goodies available.
Just holler for a Wanda! (Ok.. .you'll get it when you see the show ;)

Yowza! See y'all real soon!

Andrea & the DFC Crew!

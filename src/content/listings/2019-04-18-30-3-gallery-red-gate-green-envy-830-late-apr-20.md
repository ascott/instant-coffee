Number 3 Gallery gratefully and respectfully acknowledges that this event will take place 
on the unceeded, traditional and ancestral indigenous Coast Salish territory of the 
xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish) and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh 
(Tsleil-Waututh) First Nations. 

Number 3 Gallery is a mobile curatorial project focused on initiating collaborations with 
emerging contemporary artists and spaces. As a satirical response to the lack of 
feasible real-estate spaces in Vancouvers economic landscape, the small, portable 
scale of #3 gallery allows for exhibitions to travel and take place in a variety of contexts. 

#3 is curated by practicing artists Julie D. Mills and Julia Lamare. The project was 
conceived and designed in partnership with Logan Mohr and Pongsakorn Yananissorn.

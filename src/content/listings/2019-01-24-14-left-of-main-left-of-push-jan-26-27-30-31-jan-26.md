PLASTIC ORCHID FACTORY - IN TRADUCTION [LE MANIFESTE]

Concept and performance: Evann Siebens and Natalie LeFebvre Gnam
Media: Evann Siebens
Choreography: Natalie LeFebvre Gnam
Technical and Sound Direction + Outside Eye: James Gnam 

Media and dance coexist to frame an exploration on translation. A live and archival 
performance about images that change into other images and about images that change 
meaning when they stay the same.

TWOBIGSTEPS COLLECTIVE - RAVEL

Choreography: David Harvey
Performance: Katie Cassady, Stphanie Cyr, Sarah Formosa, Andrew Haydock, Eden 
Soloman, Marissa Wong, Marisa Christogeorge (original cast), Matisse Maitland 
(apprentice)
Sound Composition: Andrew JS
Rehearsal Direction: Amber Funk Barton

Ravel is a durational piece in which the dancers attempt to deconstruct and unravel a 
limited movement vocabulary. highly physical and detailed choreography act as a 
foundation for the dancers to dismantle the movement material, as well as self-imposed 
confinements of identity.

JANUARY 30 & 31 AT 10:30AM 

VALERIE CALAM + KATE FRANKLIN - GOTTA GO CHURCH

Choreography: Company Vice Versa/Valerie Calam in collaboration with Kate Franklin
Sound: Paul Shepherd
Original Lighting: Simon Rossiter

What lies beneath the surface affects the surface"no fantasy"embodiment. in this 
award winning solo, church is a verb! 

TARA CHEYENNE - I CANT REMEMBER THE WORD FOR I CANT REMEMBER

Creation and performance: Tara Cheyenne Friedenberg
Direction: John Murphy
Movement direction: Justine A Chambers 
Music: Marc Stewart
Lighting design: James Proudfoot 

A woman and her pre-sapiens self, ask an audience to help her remember what she 
was talking about, and where she put those things she cant remember.

Exhibition Title: Entropic Fields of Displacement.
Created by Pegah Tabassinejad and curator Olumoroti Soji-George, presented by
VIVO Media Arts Centre.
Free entry

Exhibition Dates:October 30
- November 20, 2024
Hours: Tuesday through Friday, 12-6 pm, and November 9, 10 and 16, 17, 12-6 pm.
Artist Talk:November 9, 2024 | 2:30 pmFeaturing Pegah Tabassinejad and
Olumoroti Soji-George
Opening Reception: October 30, 2024, 6 pm| Join us at VIVO Media Arts Centre to
celebrate the opening of Entropic Fields of Displacement. Meet creator Pegah
Tabassinejad and curator Olumoroti Soji-George and enjoy good food and company.

"And so she goes looking, searching without asking, sifting through whatever the city
and its bodies reveal, not wanting to excavate or exhume, not wanting to make a
noise or draw attention to herself. trans(re)lating house one - Poupeh Missaghi.

Entropic Fields Of Displacement features an amalgamation of sound and images
that act as an ecosystem for an eight-channel video installation assembled by
Tabassinejad. These works establish themselves as windows or, perhaps, portals
that trace the movement of eight West Asian women as they are guided by the artist
and
serendipity in an exploration of the cityscapes of Iran, Afghanistan, Turkey,
Lebanon, Germany and Canada.

For more information, visit https://www.vivomediaarts.com/programming/entropic-
fields-of-displacement.

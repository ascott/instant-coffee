Discorder is looking for an Under Review Editor, deadline to apply April 30.
It is a volunteer position with a monthly honorarium. 

About:
Discorder Magazine, founded in 1983, is CiTR 101.9FMs independent music, art 
and current affairs publication, covering local artists and community organizers, and 
providing valuable publishing experience to contributing writers, illustrators and 
photographers. Under Review is one of Discorders most popular sections, and acts 
as a training ground for new writers. As a pillar of the magazine since its creation, 
Under Review has always been radical and subversive in the content it chooses to 
review, which is curated by the Under Review Editor.

Position Description:
As Discorder Magazines Under Review Editor, your position involves coordinating 
and assigning reviews of select new music, podcasts, books and films for the print 
magazine and citr.ca/discorder.ca. You will liaise with fellow masthead (Editor-In-
Chief, Art Director, Real Live Action Editor, Web Editor), CiTRs Music Department, 
and music labels and publishers to source review material that highlights local and 
Canadian content outside of the mainstream. As a section editor, you will develop 
pitch lists for writers, assign reviews, set deadlines, and provide edits, comments 
and suggestions to bring each piece of writing to a print-ready state. At the end of 
each month, you will submit a selection of reviews to be published as the Under 
Review section in the following issue of Discorder. The Under Review Editor will be 
given a monthly honorarium, amount to be determined.

Responsibilities:
-Maintain relationships with musicians, labels, publicists and publishers to gain 
access to upcoming releases
-Check the Under Review mailbox in the Media Lab / Discorder Office at CiTR on a 
semi-frequent basis
-Create and update lists of albums, podcasts, books and films eligible for review in 
print and online, and share those lists to Discorder contributors throughout the 
month. Ensure that the list reflects the interests and mandate of CiTR / Discorder
-Assign reviews and set deadlines. Ensure that Discorders Writing Style Guide is 
shared with all new writers
-Provide edits, comments and suggestions on submitted reviews, and provide 
feedback to writers
-Assist the fellow Discorder masthead with production duties as needed for the 
magazine and website, which includes attending two Discorder contributor meetings 
per month
-Curate a list of completed reviews for publication in Discorder Magazine and submit 
to the Editor-In-Chief near the end of every month
-Publish finished reviews on citr.ca / discorder.ca throughout the month
-Participate in discussions around the strategy of the magazine, and support 
Discorder events and initiatives

To see the full job description and for information on how to apply, visit 
http://www.citr.ca/2018/04/19/be-the-next-under-review-editor-84804/

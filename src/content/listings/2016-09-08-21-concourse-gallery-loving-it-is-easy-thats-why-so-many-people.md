Exhibition: Loving It Is Easy, That's Why So Many People Do

September 13 - September 26, 2016. 

Opening reception: September 13 from 6pm -8pm

Concourse Gallery, Emily Carr University, Granville Island
1399 Johnston Street
Vancouver, BC

Loving it is Easy, That's Why So Many People Do is an exhibition 
wherein artists explore their conscious participation in commodity 
fetishism. 

Featuring works and texts by Riley Cotter, Catherine de Montreuil, 
Diana Hanitzsch, Marisa Holmes, Shizen Jambor, Brigitte Patenaude, 
and Tori Schepel. 

Organized by Lyndsay Pomerantz.

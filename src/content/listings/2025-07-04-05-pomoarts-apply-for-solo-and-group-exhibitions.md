Call for Submissions PoMoArts - Apply for Solo and Group Exhibitions

PoMoArts is now accepting exhibition proposals for the 2026 season! We are
seeking compelling solo or group exhibitions across all disciplines, including
painting, sculpture, textiles, photography, digital and performance art, ceramics, and
more. Proposals with an immersive or participatory element are encouraged.

This is an opportunity to showcase your work within a vibrant, community-connected
arts organization that values bold ideas and meaningful artistic experiences to reflect
and engage our diverse community.

Artists interested in the 2026 exhibition season are encouraged to submit a proposal
by July 20, 2025 for consideration. Proposals submitted after this date may be
considered for future exhibition opportunities.

For more information, visit: [http://www.pomoarts.ca/artist-calls](http://www.pomoarts.ca/artist-calls)

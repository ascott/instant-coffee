Position Available

Instructor for the Summer Institute for Teens July 2 – 26, 2013:
Visual Culture

Continuing Studies at Emily Carr University is looking for an instructor to lead 
the Visual Culture component of the annual Summer Institute for Teens 
program. The instructor will teach on Tuesdays and Thursdays for four weeks 
in July, from 9:00 am - 12:00 pm, and 1:00 pm - 4:00 pm. Instruction will 
happen at Emily Carr University on Granville Island. 

The ideal candidate will have the following:
· A Master’s Degree in Art History or similar field
· Interest in working in a collaborative and interdisciplinary environment
· Experience and passion for working with youth and/or teens; and 
· Teaching experience.

The Summer Institute for Teens Program
Each summer Emily Carr University of Art + Design offers a challenging and 
intensive art experience for students who have completed grade 10, 11 or 12. 
The Summer Institute for Teens provides students with an introduction to the 
rigors of full-time study of art, design and media in a four-week, full-time 
studio-based program.

Visual Culture – Course Description
This course introduces students to histories and theories of Visual Culture in 
order to critically examine how culture is constructed and distributed through 
various forms of media such as television, fashion, movies, advertising, the 
web, fine art, and architecture.

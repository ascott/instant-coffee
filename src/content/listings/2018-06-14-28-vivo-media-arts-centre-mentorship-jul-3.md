Still Creek Salmon Sounds youth mentorship project

Call for participation, ages 14-24

Deadline to Apply: July 3, 2018

SIGN UP & MORE INFO: http://vivomediaarts.com/stillcreeksalmonsounds

Still Creek Salmon Sounds is a mentorship project providing technical and
conceptual support to create a sound-based installation artwork that
combines field recording and data sonification to explore the salmon
spawning season in a local creek.

VIVO encourages applications from self-identified members of under-served
communities as well as visible and invisible minority groups. We thank all
who express interest in participating.

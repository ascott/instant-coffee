DOMES, SPIRALS... SENSING FORM IN HUMAN MOVEMENT 
WORKSHOP BY HANNAH ACTON

This workshop will offer an introduction to Laban/Bartenieff Movement Analysis through a 
guided exploration of one's own lived anatomy. It is interested in how a development of 
kinesthetic awareness promotes literacy in Non-Symbolic languages and thus increase the 
precision with which we may exchange with our environment.

Rudolph Laban's theory of human movement (Early 20th c., Europe) acknowledges that 
forms found in our environment are perceptible within us, and vice versa. In this way, 
abstractions can be located within the body and addressed not only by the mind, but also by 
a chorus of integrated body systems.

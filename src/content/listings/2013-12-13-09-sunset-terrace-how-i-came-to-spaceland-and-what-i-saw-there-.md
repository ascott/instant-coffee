How I Came to Spaceland, and What I Saw There

Sunset Terrace

2028 Clark Dr.

8-11pm

——————-

An unspeakable horror seized me. There was a darkness; then a dizzy, 
sickening sensation of sight that was not like seeing; I saw a Line that was 
no Line; Space that was not Space: I was myself, and not myself. When I 
could find voice, I shrieked loud in agony, “Either this is madness or it is Hell.” 
“It is neither,” calmly replied the voice of the Sphere, “it is Knowledge; it is 
Three Dimensions: open your eye once again and try to look steadily.

E. A. Abbott

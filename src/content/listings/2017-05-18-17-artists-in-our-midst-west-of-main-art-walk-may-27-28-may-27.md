Artists in our Midst (AIOM) celebrates 25 years of open-door artistic explorations with 
the West of Main Art Walk, May 27 & 28, 2017. In its largest-ever year, the festivities 
stretch from Point Grey to Main Street and from Granville Island to 41st Avenue, as 
a vibrant community of more than 50 artists share their works in garden studios, 
cafs, shops, and community centres. Anticipating the weekends revelations, The 
Roundhouse Exhibit will offer early glimpses of the discoveries to-be-made on May 
18 from 10am to 9pm at the Roundhouse Community Arts and Recreation Centre. A 
silent auction will also be held during the Art Walk with all proceeds going to the 
West of Main Art Walk Legacy Scholarship Fund in support of young and emerging 
artists from Arts Umbrella and Lord Byng Secondary School.

For more information, visit artistsinourmidst.com

Wil Aballe Art Projects is proud to present a solo exhibition of current work by 
Toronto-based painter Matthew Brown.

Matthew Brown works with painting and drawing. He completed his BFA at 
the University of Victoria, BC and MFA at Concordia University, Montreal. 
Brown was a finalist in the RBC Painting Competition twice and won for 
Western Canada in 2006. Brown has exhibited throughout Canada and 
internationally. In Canada at The Vancouver Art Gallery, MOCCA, Toronto, 
and Access Gallery, Vancouver. He has had solo shows at Clint Roenisch 
Gallery, Toronto, and Tracey Lawrence Gallery, Vancouver. He has been 
reviewed in Border Crossings, Canadian Art, Contemporary and Frieze 
magazines.
In addition to "Sargasso Space", his work can currently be seen in the group 
exhibition, "Relax a little; one of your most celebrated nervous tics will be 
your undoing" at Narwhal Art Projects, Toronto curated by Jon Davies and 
Kristin Weckworth.

NEVER STEADY, NEVER STILL, by Kathleen Hepburn (2016)
SCREENING and FILMMAKER TALK
7pm | Tues Jan 22, 2019
Reliance Theatre, Emily Carr University of Art + Design 
520 East 1st Avenue, Vancouver BC V5T 0H2

Drawing from personal life, filmmaker Kathleen Hepburn's dramatic feature creates a 
striking and sensitive portrayal of a mother and son coping with the onset of Parkinsons. 
This screening is followed by a talk with the filmmaker. Free and open to the public.
This event is part of the Time, Light + Sound Series at Emily Carr University.

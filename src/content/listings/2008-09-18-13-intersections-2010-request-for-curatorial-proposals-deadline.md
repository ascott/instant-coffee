Request for Curatorial Proposals
INTERSECTIONS 2010

REMINDER: Deadline is October 8, 2008

Vancouver's Public Art Program is celebrating the 2010 
Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games by inviting curators to 
develop and produce a program of media and light artworks in 
the Downtown Eastside over the course of the Games. 
Independent curators, curatorial teams, or nonprofit visual and 
media arts organizations with a curatorial approach will be 
considered. The project will build on the 2007 Intersection 
event, in which four arts organizations resident at Carrall and 
Hastings streets collaborated on workshops, exhibitions, 
residencies and an evening of large scale projections on 
windows and building exteriors.

Budget: The total project budget is $300,000.

Selection Process: This project is open to independent curators 
and non ]profit visual art societies with a curatorial approach 
and an interest in public art. Proposals will be reviewed by a 
peer panel. One curator/curatorial team may be selected or the 
panel may elect to interview up to three applicants.

Submission Deadline: 4:00 pm, Wednesday, October 8, 2008.

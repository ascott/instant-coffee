Nocturnal Delicious Exhibition, January 30 - February 8, 2026

Opening and Performance: January 30, 2026, 7-10pm

Duplex Artist Society (back alley entrance)
1889 Franklin Street, Vancouver, BC

Gallery Hours: Saturdays and Sundays 1-5pm and by appointment

Join the artist collective Nocturnal Delicious for their first exhibition, Nocturnal Delicious.
Experience perverse institutionality, the desiring body beneath the uniform, the all-spewing
mouth, and the meat just lying there.

Nocturnal Delicious is an artist collective, a concept, and a brand by artists Gisle Suzor-
Morin and Rowan Melling. Through performance, painting, text and textiles, Nocturnal
Delicious explores the contradictions of the institutionalization of queer politics, which
achieved the goal of normalizing social justice causes even as it sterilized this politics through
corporate culture. The artists lament a loss of libidinal joy in left-wing politics; they reflect on
how this institutionalization coincided with the rise of libidinal politics on the far right, which
mobilizes affects of excess, celebration, and pleasure previously associated with queerness.

Future Concrete | readings & future soup

Thursday, March 3 at 7 PM

Arts Factory Society
281 Industrial Avenue, Vancouver

Join the poets involved in Unit/Pitt Projects' exhibition Future Concrete:
Poetry After This for an evening of readings at TCR's
event/office/kitchen space. Readers include US poets Sidony O'neal,
Ivy Johnson and Kate Robinson, as well as Vancouver-based poets
Anahita Jamali Rad, Donato Mancini, Danielle LaFrance and Jordan
Abel. Free soup will be available and donations for the visiting writers
are welcomed.

Future Concrete: Poetry After This is curated by Gabriel Saloman
([http://diademdiscos.com/gms](http://diademdiscos.com/gms))

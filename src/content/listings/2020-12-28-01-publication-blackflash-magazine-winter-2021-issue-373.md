We are thrilled to announce that BlackFlash Magazines Winter 2021 issue is now hitting
mailboxes and newsstands. Guest-designed by Saskatoon-based graphic designer and
illustrator Chris Morin, the issue offers features, spotlights, portfolios, and interviews by
artists and writers from across Canada.

For our winter issue, I had the great pleasure of speaking to Saskatoon-based multi-
disciplinary artist Amalie Atkins about her practice. After over a decade of writing, sewing,
shooting, and editing, Akins presented
her trilogy The Diamond Eye Assembly to audiences
at the Remai Modern in the spring of 2019. Atkins and I communicated for months, sharing
our thoughts on the project and its underlying message concerning mourning, preservation,
and restoration. As this issue comes to fruition, Atkins concluded our conversation by
saying, the characters in my film are women of action. They move through their grief,
and find their way. Our capacity to move through lifes exhaustive pain and loss is
examined throughout this issue. Each artist discussed manifests their own map to solace
and strength during their shadow hour.

" Maxine Proctor, Managing Editor

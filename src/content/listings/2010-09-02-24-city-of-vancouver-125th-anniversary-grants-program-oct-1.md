125th Anniversary Grants Program - Public Information Sessions

Deadline: October 1, 2010

Cultural Services is pleased to announce several public information
sessions about the City's 125th anniversary plans in 2011 and its 125th
Anniversary Grants Program.

The 125th Anniversary Grants Program supports the growing
excitement, pride and enthusiasm for Vancouver's upcoming 125th anniversary
in 2011. This program is a time-limited opportunity intended to foster
awareness and understanding of Vancouver's identities, first nations'
heritage and cultural diversity. This program will support activities that
enrich our society through civic engagement and the celebration of art,
culture and heritage.

The second (and final) intake of the program will be in
January/February 2011.

Community members are invited to attend any one of the four
sessions that will be held at the following locations, dates and times:

1. Tuesday September 7, 2010 from 6:00 - 7:30pm
Sunset Community Centre, 6810 Main Street

2. Thursday September 9, 2010 from 6:00 - 7:30pm
Vancouver Aboriginal Friendship Centre
1607 East Hastings Street

3. Monday September 13, 2010 from 6:00 - 7:30pm
Progress Lab, 1422 William Street

4. Wednesday September 15, 2010 from 6:00 - 7:30pm
St Paul's Anglican Church, 1130 Jervis Street

PORTAL POP-UP GALLERY

Vancouver BC

Sena Cleave + Cherry Archer

Reception: Sunday, January 21, 1-3pm

January 5 - 28, 2024

Portal Pop-up continues its program this January in the window of 407 W Cordova
with Vancouver-based artists Sena Cleave and Cherry Archer. Each utilizes found
organic elements to produce evocative and contemplative works.

Sena Cleaves handwoven textile, While It Sits resembles a net, a porous vessel
where things gather temporarily before sifting through. The objects attached to the
textile play with the customs of Japanese Buddhist sonaemono, which are offerings
made to ancestors. These offerings reciprocate care to loved ones who
have died
and now look after the living from a distance. Some of the sonaemono inWhile It Sits
are scraps, echoing the unofficial practice of eating foods once they have been
offered for a time, so as not to let them waste.

Cherry Archers Summer Preamble 415 is composed mostly of wild plants foraged
during meditative walks throughout East Van. It is an interpretation of emotions and
sensations experienced during interaction with nature. The botanicals were arranged
then incrementally frozen in water to form what the artist calls a Botanical Ice Tile.
The tile is illuminated with coloured light and photographed. In using ice as a
medium, Archer explores tensions between transience and preservation, and
randomness versus control. Summer Preamble 415 is an invitation to the viewer to
become absorbed in the fine details, to examine what emotions arise, and to
consider their relationship with nature.

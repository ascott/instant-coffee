Volunteer Vancouver regarding space available within our premises.
Office Space for Rent
       
Volunteer Vancouver has office space available for
not-for-profits to rent and is currently accepting applications. The available space varies from an individual workspace of a 10 x 14 work cubicle to a 285 square foot office. The spaces all include furniture, a computer, a phone and access to a kitchen. There are additional services including reception, parking, and meeting space available as well. The Volunteer Vancouver office is located in downtown Vancouver at 1183 Melville Street. If you would like further information, please contact
Merna Johnston, Office Manager at 604-637-8292 or
mjohnston@volunteervancouver.ca

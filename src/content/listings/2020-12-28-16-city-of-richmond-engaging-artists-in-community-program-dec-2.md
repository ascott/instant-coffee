Engaging Artists in Community Program

Open Artist Call
Requests for Proposals, RFP

The City of Richmond Public Art Program seeks artists with socially-oriented practices to
engage culturally diverse and multigenerational audiences in three unique artist
opportunities. Emerging and professional artists are invited to imagine innovative ways in
which shared art experiences can act as a catalyst to
foster individual creative expression,
build community and nurture mental health and well-being during this time of uncertainty.

Richmond Public Library: $10,000

Richmond School District 38: $10,000

Richmond Multicultural Community Services: $10,000

Eligibility: Artists residing in British Columbia

Deadline: January 29, 2021

Duration: Spring/Summer 2021 " Spring/Summer 2022

Join us at the Cineworks Annex Darkroom and Studio! 
We are holding a weekly open darkroom night on Tuesdays from 7-
10pm. The next open night is March 26th. If you want to come by and 
check out the facilities you're welcome to drop in free of charge. If you 
want to process film or make prints the price is $5 for members, and $8 
for non-members ( to cover the cost of chemicals) or a 5 time pass can 
also be purchased for a reduction in cost.
We also have a Community Night/ Orientation Night that is held monthly, 
the next one will be March 27th. You can read more about it on the 
Cineworks blog here 
http://cineworkspost.wordpress.com/2013/02/08/news-at-the-annex/

We hope to see you there! If you have any questions please feel free to 
email us. The space is in the basement of the ironworks building at 235 
Alexander near Main street. 

email annex@cineworks.ca for further information.

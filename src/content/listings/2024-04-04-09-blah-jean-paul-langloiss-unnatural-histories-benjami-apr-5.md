Jean Paul Langlois

Unnatural Histories is composed of paintings from two series. One is the story of
Langlois Mtis Fathers rise from a poverty-stricken, working-class background and
marrying into his Mothers affluent and educated family and the problems related with
switching class. Langlois explores various anecdotes, both funny and tragic, that
illustrate his quest for success and fear of mortality. The remainder features recurring
animal characters in environs that have been altered, adulterated or even ruined by
mankind's greed and blindness.

Benjamin Lumb

Driftwood - Ongoing Series

A Collection of 7 images capturing the progression of incineration of a
DriftWood fort
built in the once forest, now clear-cut, referencing the burn piles formed and lit during
the logging process. The Driftwood logs were transported from the beach to the
mountains and constructed in the same location as the burned tree remnants used in
the installation on exhibit, Remnant Trees, were sourced.

Benjamin Lumb Art House is a gallery based in Vancouver, Canada primarily working
with emerging and mid career artists with a focus on photography, installation and
paintings. Current spaces include a store front, and underground club, an old
warehouse and various other pop up locations. As a well-established and practicing
artist, Lumb inherently understands the work of the artists he represents and exhibits
and is able to curate and support the narrative, which they seek to communicate

Hobby Hangouts: Unit Oddity
Sun, January 19, 2025 6PM-8PM @ The JBG 144 E 6th
Avenue

This Hobby Hangouts Session will provide a creative discussion on dreams and
artistic vision. The aim is for a artistic discussion on our relationship to community,
dreams, and making art. Studio member, Caden hopes to encourage vulnerability
and resiliency in our creative undertakings. People will also be welcome to also free-
sketch and draw during the session as a form of art therapy through the process.

Suggested donation $15 for cost of materials. PWYC tickets! RSVP through this link:
https://www.zeffy.com/en-CA/ticketing/hobby-hangouts-unit-oddity-intensive-on-
artist-directive

Hobby Hangouts sessions aim to promote studio members skillsharing and
connecting with each other and in community.

The James Black Gallery is located on the stolen unceded and ancestral territory of
the xmky"m (Musqueam), Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), and sl"ilwta (Tsleil-
Waututh) Nations. These lands have been stewarded by them since time
immemorial.

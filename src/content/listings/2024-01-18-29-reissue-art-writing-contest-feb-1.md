Were excited to announce the first annual ReIssue Art Writing Contest!

Open to emerging art writers enrolled in our partnering institutions: SFU, UBC, ECU,
and Langara College. Eligible students are invited to submit their writing for a cash
prize of $1500, and publication of the winning piece online and in our upcoming Year 3
Anthology.

The call is open to students in undergraduate and graduate programs,
in and beyond
the art department. Submissions should be 3000 words maximum, and fit within
ReIssues categories of Review, Interview, Moving Image, Essay, or Creative. Upon
selection, the successful applicant will work with ReIssue Editor Casey Wei to polish
up the piece for publication following Reissues style guidelines (available as a PDF on
our About page).

The deadline to submit is February 1, 2024, with the winner selected by March 1.

NOTHING HAPPENING is where the language of the construction 
site and the film set converge. NOTHING HAPPENING is organized 
chaos; a crowd of construction workers shares the stage with a 
concrete truck, steamroller, excavator, barricades, orange cones, 
and traffic control tape. NOTHING HAPPENING addresses the 
savage economic development that is steamrolling cultural spaces, 
bohemia and marginality to create a condo wonderland. It is a mirror 
reflecting our cityâ€™s relationship to territory, land, and frontiers of 
economic and political power. NOTHING HAPPENING is a video 
loop of hand shakes between City Hall, land speculators and private 
enterprises. NOTHING HAPPENING is an artist preaching upside 
down from a scissor lift over the deserted notion of revolution. 
NOTHING HAPPENING is a lack of resistance, the laisser-faire, a 
paralyzed will against a soundscape of generators, jackhammers 
and an invisible authority marching on our present and 
manufacturing our future by deleting our past. NOTHING 
HAPPENING is an opportunity to make space for something to 
happen.

NICOLAS BOONE (1974) is a visual artist based in Paris. He 
graduated from Ecole Nationale des Beaux-Arts de Paris in 2001. 
His earlier work used the language of filmmaking to produce 
ephemeral performances without film stock. Eventually, his 
meticulously improvised parades were registered and edited into 
film loops to create a living discourse around the death of cinema.

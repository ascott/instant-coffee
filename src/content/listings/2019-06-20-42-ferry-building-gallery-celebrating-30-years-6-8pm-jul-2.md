July 2 - 28

Mixed media invitational featuring artists who have previously exhibited in the gallery

Opening Reception: Tuesday, July 2, 6-8 p.m.

Meet the Artists: Saturday, July 2, 13 and 20, 2-3 p.m.

The Ferry Building Gallery is celebrating its 30th anniversary operating as a community 
public art gallery in July 2019. To celebrate this significant milestone, we are pleased to 
showcase a special, commemorative invitational mixed media group exhibition featuring 
artists who have previously exhibited in the gallery:

Carole Arnston, Claire Babcock, Elspeth Bradbury, Ray Bradbury, Bobbie Burgers, Colette 
Chilcott, Lil Chrzan, Jane Clark, Pierre Coupey, Elizabeth Cox, Cori Creed, Joanne Frewer, 
Tania Gleave, Ingunn Kemble, Jytte Kiss, Peter Kiss, Zoltan Kiss, Ruth Leithal, Ron Love, 
Lesley MacGregor, Jill McRae, Sally Michener, Aaron Nelson-Moody, Freda Pagani, Ross 
Penhall, Isabelle Procter, Melanie Rivers, Barry Shelton, Monica Shelton, Sylvia Tait, 
Margaret Thoma, Tannis Turner, Shirley Williams and Xwalacktun.

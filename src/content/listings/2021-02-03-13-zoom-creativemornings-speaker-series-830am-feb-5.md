FREE lecture series on creativity
Featuring Rachel McKinkely, Darryl Condon, and Juno Kim
With special guests Craig Addy (piano) and Carson Ting (live drawing)
February 5 at 8:30 am PST
ZOOM

Register now for our Feb 5 online event which brings back not one, two, or even three,
but FIVE previous CMVan speakers to address the global theme 'divergent' with
updates on what has shifted for them since we last met.

We'll hear from architect Darryl Condon last stood on the CMVan stage way back in
2012 where he
shared his talk Moving Beyond Sustainability & Building Community
With Inclusive Spaces.

Also joining us will be chocolatier, entrepreneur, and educator Rachel McKinleywhose
2018 presentation Craft (chocolate) and Creativity resulted in her team at Purdy's
bringing bespoke chocolates to every in person event since!

Lastly, we'll welcome back chef, entrepreneur, consultant, and freelance creative Juno
Kim who inspired the CMVan community with his talk The Flow of a Creative Path in
2019.

BONUS! As if this amazing lineup wasn't enough, non-other than award-winning art
director and illustrator Carson Ting, who inspired us with his 2014 talk Being a Creative
Rebel, will be capturing the stories and ideas via live illustration!

This month's musical guest is pianist, composer & improviser Craig Addy, who first
shared his story The Mystery of Improvising and performed for us back in 2017.

This will be a creative morning indeed"don't miss it!

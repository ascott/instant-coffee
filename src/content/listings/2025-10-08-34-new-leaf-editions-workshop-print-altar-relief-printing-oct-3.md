Print Altar Relief Printing Workshop: Celebrating the Day of the Dead | NEW LEAF
EDITIONS | October 31st, 6 - 9 PM, November 1st, 10 to 4 PM, 2025

A Creative Exploration of Da de los Muertos Traditions through Printmaking

Join us for a hands-on relief printing workshop inspired by the traditions of the Day of
the Dead (Da de los Muertos). In this workshop, we will lead you through the basics of
relief printmaking while creating unique artworks that reflect the symbolism and spirit of
the Day of the Dead.

During the workshop, participant will
Carve 2, 6 by 6-inch block prints

Create individual prints inspired by Day of the Dead

learn the fundamentals of relief printmaking

Make a prints-alter made with the prints during the workshop

Refreshment provided at the end of the workshop
Home-baked Pan de Muerto

Hot chocolate by Kasama

Additional Notes
All necessary tools and safety equipment will be provided.

Wear comfortable clothing that you dont mind getting a bit of ink on!

No prior printmaking experience is necessary"just bring your creativity and a
willingness to learn!

Location: Vancouver/Remote
Term: 10 months
Hours: Approx. 14 hours/week
Contract Value: $20k
Application deadline: June 30, 2023

Chapel Sound Art Foundation (CSAF) is looking for someone experienced in working
with artists, administratively skilled, and excited about organizational and community
capacity-building to guide the organization into the next stage of its evolution. This is
an opportunity for someone who is excited about building an administrative
backbone that will support the growth of a grassroots, interdisciplinary, experimental
electronic music and art community that
includes all artists regardless of their race,
class, gender or orientation.

Reporting to the Board of Directors, the Executive Director will be responsible for
community engagement and organizational management including grant writing,
financial management, operational planning, record-keeping and filing. The
Executive Director will also support and facilitate the organizations upcoming
archival and succession planning initiatives.

email Jenniffer Tai at recruitment@cinevolutionmedia.com.

The Mayor's Art Awards 2016
Participate in celebrating Vancouver's creative community!

Each year the Mayor's Arts Award recipients reflect the depth and diversity
of the talent in Vancouver's creative sector, representing disciplines
ranging from performing and visual arts to literary and culinary arts.
Awards for support, philanthropy and lifetime achievement are also presented.

Nominating an individual or group who have made significant contributions
to the creative life in our city is easy.
Find a nominating partner and submit your suggestion by Monday April 25th
at noon.

Find the information you need at:
[http://www.allianceforarts.com/blog/2016/3/16/nominations-2016-mayors-arts-awards](http://www.allianceforarts.com/blog/2016/3/16/nominations-2016-mayors-arts-awards)

Our creative future is in our collective hands. From music and dance to
visual arts and new media, Vancouver is enriched, enlivened, and
economically and socially enhanced by arts and culture. Based on the
talented, inspiring people we've honoured since
2008, through
the Mayor's Arts Awards, our future looks bright.

Experience Art Through a New Perspective

Join Jinnie Saran for FREE blind-led tours of grunt gallerys current exhibition, A
volar entre rocas, this October! Jinnie, a blind Indo-Canadian emerging artist and
writer, will guide visitors through the current exhibition by artist Mariana Muoz
Gomez.

Wednesdays: October 1, October 8, October 15, October 22 and October 29

Tours start at 4 PM and 6 PM.

Duration: 30 minutes + Q&A

Location: grunt gallery (#116-350 E 2nd Ave)

All are welcome-sighted, low-vision, and blind visitors!

No registration required, just drop-in when you can!

Questions? Email access@grunt.ca

grunt gallery is excited to announce a series of free, drop-in volunteer-led tours this
fall, facilitated by Jinnie Saran. These unique tours allow visitors to experience
contemporary art through visual and non-visual perspectives, challenging traditional
ways of engaging with gallery spaces.

The 30-minute tours will be held on Wednesdays at 4pm and 6pm. Each tour will be
followed by a question-and-answer session, creating space for dialogue about
diverse ways of experiencing and interpreting art.

Visit A volar entre rocas exhibition page at grunt.ca/exhibitions/a-volar-entre-rocas/

Check out Jinnies website at jinniesaran.com/

Learn more about exhibiting artist, Mariana, by visiting their website at

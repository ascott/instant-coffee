Date: Thursday, April 14, 2016
Time: 5-9 PM
Exhibition and Hours: April 15-27, 2016 | Monday-Saturday, 12-4 PM
Location: AHVA Gallery 1001 and Audain Studio 3000 | Audain Art 
Centre, 6398 University Blvd, Vancouver, BC

The Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory is pleased to 
present Re: Exhibition, this year's University of British Columbia 
BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating Exhibition at the Audain Art Centre.

Please join us for refreshments and fun as we celebrate the 
achievements and emerging talent of this year's graduating class.

The exhibition showcases the contributions of more than forty 
emerging artists working in an extensive range of mediums, including 
printmaking, installation, photography, textile, video, drawing, painting, 
artist books, digital media, and sculpture.

We look forward to seeing you there!

For more information, please visit the AHVA website www.ahva.ubc.ca 
or contact us at ubcgradshow@gmail.com

Follow us on Instagram @ubcgradshow

This exhibition was made possible with support from the UBC 
Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory and generous 
contributions from our donors.

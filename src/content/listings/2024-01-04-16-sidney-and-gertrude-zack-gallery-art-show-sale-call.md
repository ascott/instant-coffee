for Submissions | JAN 24
We are pleased to announce that the JCC Inclusion Department's Art Show & Sale,
"Community, Longing and Belonging", has opened their artist registration!

We are looking for artists of mixed levels and abilities to share a visual, digital or
auditory record of what community longing or belonging looks like to them.

Art will be exhibited at the Sidney and Gertrude Zack Gallery from February 21st -
April 2nd, 2024.

To apply, please send shelly@jccgv.bc.ca the following information:

1. An image or file of your artwork.

2. A description of media and size.

3. A brief artist statement via email.

Application deadline: January 24th

Admission is free.
Artists may price their work.
80% of the proceeds from the art sales will go to the artist, and the other 20% will be
donated to support the JCCGVs Inclusion Departments low-barrier and free art-
based programming.
Before you begin your
application, please ensure you read well the information
described in the CALL FOR ARTIST and the TERMS OF PARTICIPATION PDF
https://www.jccgv.com/inclusion/

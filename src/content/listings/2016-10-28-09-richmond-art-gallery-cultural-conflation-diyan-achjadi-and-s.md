October 29, 2016
2-3 pm
Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond, BC V6Y 1R9

The artists from the Cultural Conflation exhibition, Diyan Achjadi and
Shawn Hunt, discuss their work and invite the public to join in the
conversation about cultural identity and appropriation in contemporary
art.

Free admission. Everyone welcome to attend.

Achjadi received a BFA from the Cooper Union School of Art (1993) and
an MFA from Concordia University (2002). She has exhibited nationally
and internationally. Born in Jakarta, Indonesia, Achjadi currently
lives and works in Vancouver, BC where she is faculty at the Emily
Carr University.

Hunt has a diploma in studio art from Capilano College and a BFA from
the University of British Columbia. He has exhibited nationally and
internationally.

Exhibition is on view October 15 - December 31, 2016

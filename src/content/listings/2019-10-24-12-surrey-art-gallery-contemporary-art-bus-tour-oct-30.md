SURREY ART GALLERY
Contemporary Art Bus Tour
Mount Pleasant, Vancouver

Wednesday, October 30, 9 a.m."3 p.m.

Surrey Art Gallery and Surrey Art Gallery Association invite art enthusiasts of all
knowledge and experience levels to join them for a bus tour of galleries and artist-run
centres in Vancouvers Mount Pleasant area on Wednesday, October 30 from 9 a.m. to
3 p.m. Bring a packed lunch or grab a bite from a local eatery. Ages 16+. Cost is $39.
Participants can register online at surrey.ca/artgallery under Gallery Events.

The Contemporary Art Bus Tour will make four stops
to explore various art spaces in
Vancouver. Participants will receive guided tours of current exhibitions and learn about
the significance and history of Mount Pleasants art scene. Stops include the Burrard
Arts Foundation for a group show, including works by Kate Metten, Ryan Quast, and
Mollie Burke; Western Front for the Experimental Time Order exhibition put on by
Philadelphia-based artists Black Quantum Futurism; and a sneak peek of a sentimental
dissidence by Gabi Dao at the grunt gallery. The tour will also conclude with a visit to the
recently built Emily Carr University campus to see works by Myfanwy MacLeod at the
Libby Leshgold Gallery.

The group will meet at Surrey Art Gallery for coffee before heading to Vancouver. Please
note that participants must meet and begin the tour at Surrey art Gallery. For more
information please visit Surrey Art Gallerys website or give us a call.

*this tour will be wheelchair accessible.

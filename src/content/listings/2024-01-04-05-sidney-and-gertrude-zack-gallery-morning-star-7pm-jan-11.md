Inspired by mysticism, magic, surrealism, astrology, and tarot, Rosamunde Bordo
presents Morning Star, the fourth installment in her ongoing project The Denise File.
The enigmatic
Denise becomes increasingly more real through artistic interventions,
but by the same process of invention is paradoxically more fictitious in this work of
serial detective fiction written through physical space and material.

Drawing from the line and circle iconography of Aramaic amulets that were used for
healing and protection, Bordo has created a series of hardwood sculptures that
recall elements of furniture and domestic architectural features, such as banisters,
doorknobs, or table legs. Resembling molecular structures, including that of salt
(NaCl), these Constellation works mediate the cosmic and the infra-molecular
through the everyday.

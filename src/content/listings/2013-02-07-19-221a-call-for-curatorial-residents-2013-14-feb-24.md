221A is an artist-run centre located in Vancouver, Canada with a mandate to 
support the advancement of contemporary art that explores design as a 
subject. Projects presented at 221A address design in its widest sense, 
which may encompass graphics, products, architecture, or, more broadly, 
social codes and conventions.

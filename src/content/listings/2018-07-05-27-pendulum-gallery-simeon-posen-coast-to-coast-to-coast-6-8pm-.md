Simeon Posen
Coast to Coast to Coast
June 25 - July 27, 2018
Reception July 12, 6-8pm

Pendulum Gallery
885 West Georgia St, Vancouver BC

In Coast to Coast to Coast, Toronto-based photographer Simeon Posen presents 
two series of works taken across Canada over the last few decades. In the first we 
can see Posens meticulous technique combined with a poetic examination of 
natural elements " earth air water " in a range of images from close-cropped details 
to sweeping panoramas.

In the Midway Lights series, he utilizes the funfair games and ride-based 
entertainments found in urban and sub-urban areas across Canada. These 
photographs capture both the unique architectural form contained in the structures 
and the dynamic energy of the rides, recording their patterns of movement as they 
weave lines across the night sky. Shot using a Pentax camera with 400 film, Posen 
allows us to experience the sensation of speed and motion in a still image.

This series of works provide the viewer with contrasting images of movement and 
stillness, urban and natural and combined with his exceptional handling of textures 
and tonal range push some images into near abstraction. His work reminds us that 
how we look at things is a function of how we frame them, and that the camera is a 
unique tool for uncovering the fundamental and personal nature of our world.


Simeon Posen is a Canadian landscape and architectural photographer whose work 
spans more than four decades. His studies in architecture at Taliesin, the Frank 
Lloyd Wright School, and stage design at the National Theatre School in Montreal 
fundamentally inform his photography practice. The technique, style and 
composition of his photographs are influenced by classic B&W formalist 
photographers Wynn Bullock, Brett Weston and Ansel Adams with whom he studied 
in California in the early 1970s. He works with large format negatives - 8x10 and 
4x5 - hand developing the film materials and printing on silver gelatin fibre-based 
papers as well as utilizing Giclee printing methods.

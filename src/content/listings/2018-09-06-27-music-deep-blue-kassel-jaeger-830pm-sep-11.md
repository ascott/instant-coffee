Kassel Jaeger

Deep Blue
255 E. 2nd Ave 
(alley entrance)

September 11th, 8:30pm

Kassel Jaeger is the pseudonym for composer, theoretician, and artistic director of INA-GRM in 
Paris, Franois J. Bonnet. He has performed internationally and released albums on labels such 
as Editions Mego, Shelter Press, and Senufo Editions. His musical compositions are a balance 
between concrte experimentalism, ambient noise, and electroacoustic improv. Bonnet's 
writings, The Order of Sounds (2016) and The Infra-World (2017) were published by 
Urbanomic.

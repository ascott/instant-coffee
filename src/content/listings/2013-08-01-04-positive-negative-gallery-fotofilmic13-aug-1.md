Thursday, August 1
Positive Negative Gallery
7–11 PM

We are pleased to announce the opening of the FotoFilmic’13 
International Juried Film Photography Exhibition on August 1st!

The 1st edition of the FotoFilmic global film photography competition 
has been a real success, drawing hundreds of submissions from 
around the world in assembling its massive 6-month ShortList of 90 
film photographers and 270 selected works! The resulting exhibition, 
juried by no less than 9 established artists from 8 countries, 
represents a world celebration of the film medium’s undeniable 
presence, leading practices and persisting influence in contemporary 
photography at large.

All FotoFilmic’13 Exhibition Awards will be announced during the 
opening on August 1, 2013.

Winsor Gallery announces the upcoming exhibition entitled The State of Infinite Division 
curated by Brooke Wise. 

The exhibition runs from February 1st - February 24th, 2018. An opening will be held on 
the evening of February 1st, from 6-8pm; Brooke Wise and selected artists will be in 
attendance. The exhibition features work from artists Jen Stark, Thrush Holmes, Drake 
Carr, Katie Stout, and Ariana Papademetrpoulos.

Registration is now open for the 15th Annual Graduate Student Symposium: Empathy /
Empathie, organized by the Art History Graduate Student Association (AHGSA) at
Concordia University, which will take place virtually over Zoom on February 5-6th,
2021!

This symposium will address questions around empathy as it relates to art practice,
theory, and history. We are interested in exploring the role of the arts in bridging the
gap between feelings and actions, especially regarding issues of gender, race, class,
and ability. We are also concerned with the limits of empathy, and what empathy
can
and cannot do with regards to reconciling discrimination and privilege.

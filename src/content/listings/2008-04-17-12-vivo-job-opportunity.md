Interim Administration Support Position
2 days / 14 hours per week				      $14 per hour

Position Overview:
The Interim Administration Support Position is responsible for providing administrative and financial support to the VIVO Management Collective, the Board Treasurer and the Finance Committee.

Contract Dates and hours:  ASAP through July 15, 2008; 2 regular days on site (Monday-Thursday); 11am - 6pm 
 
Reports to: 
The VIVO Management Collective

Responsibilities:
Provide day- to- day financial and administrative support for the VIVO Management Collective. Maintain VIVO's financial systems, including accounting / bookkeeping and departmental budgets, in coordination with the bookkeeper and VIVO management collective members. Prepare documents with the Treasurer for Management’s quarterly review. Monitor departmental budgets and overall cash flow on a monthly basis in cooperation with the VIVO management team. Prepare, with the Treasurer, a financial report for the Board’s monthly meeting. Support grant writing through grant? scheduling and reporting to the VIVO Management Collective. Attend VIVO Management Collective monthly meetings and the Finance Committee meetings.
Co-ordinate annual report for June AGM and organize the Annual General Meeting.
Provide other administrative support as required.

Qualifications:
Experience in and knowledge of not-for-profit, charitable organizations and/or art centres / galleries including:  budget preparation, financial reporting, and day- to -day administration. Excellent time management, organizational, verbal, written and interpersonal communication skills.
Commitment to sharing work space with diverse cultural communities and social practices. Computer knowledge including the use of word processing applications (Word), spreadsheets (Excel) and Quick Books; HTML an asset.
Experience working with artist-run centres, collectives or community-based organizations an asset.

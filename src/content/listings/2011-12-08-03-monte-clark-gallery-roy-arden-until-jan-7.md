ROY ARDEN
VOX

November 24, 2011 to January 7, 2012

Opening reception:
Friday November 25, 7:30pm to 9:30pm

Roy Arden is widely known for his photographic work produced over 
the
past three decades. However, in the past few years he has been
vigorously producing works in other media. His recent exhibition,
UNDERTHESUN at the Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver, featured 
over
one hundred drawings, paintings, collages, and sculptures in a
wunderkammer installation.

Vox, Arden's new exhibition for Monte Clark Gallery, Vancouver, will
present recent work in a variety of media including collage, oil
painting and sculpture. All of these works refer to the idea of the
'voice', whether understood as the speaking voice, musical voice,
poetic voice or social voice. Taken as a whole, the exhibition could
be seen as a poem rich in reference to an eclectic array of art
history and popular culture including Loretta Lynn, Jack Spicer and
Kurt Schwitters.

Gallery hours:
Tuesday through Saturday, 10am to 6pm

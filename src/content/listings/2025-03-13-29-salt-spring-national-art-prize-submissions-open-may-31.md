Submissions open: January 3, 2025
Deadline for entry: 8PM PDT, May 31, 2025

$52,000 in Prizes Available.

The Salt Spring National Art Prize (SSNAP) is a biennial juried open competition
focused on Canadian contemporary visual arts. Finalists are selected for the
exhibition by an independent national jury. We invite all Canadian artists whose work
exemplifies originality, quality, integrity, and creativity to submit work for
consideration.

Conditions of Entry: www.saltspringartprize.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/SSNAP-
2025-Conditions-of-Entry-30-Oct-2024.pdf

KEY DATES
Submissions open: January 3, 2025
Last day for assistance with submissions: 8PM PDT, May 28, 2025
Deadline for entry: 8PM PDT, May 31, 2025
Jury decisions and SSNAP finalists announced week of July 14, 2025
Finalists Exhibition dates: o Saturday, September 27, 2025, to Sunday, October 19,
2025 o Gala Awards announcements: Saturday, October 18, 2025

ELIGIBLE WORK
Prior to making a submission, entrants should carefully review the Conditions of
Entry, to confirm eligibility. In summary:

Entrants must be Canadian citizens or have permanent residency status.
Entrants must be aged 18 years or older as of January 1, 2024.
Work may be 2-dimensional wall-mounted, 3-dimensional wall-mounted or 3-
dimensional free-standing, executed in any medium.
Submissions must be new and original work, completed on or after January 1,
2024. /5 30 October 2024 SSNAP Submission
Guidelines HOW

TO ENTER
Submissions must be made using the online entry form through the website.
Entrants may submit up to three works for adjudication; however, only one work will
be selected for the exhibition.
A separate entry must be completed for each work submitted.
The cost per entry is $35, payable by PayPal, credit card or e-transfer and is non-
refundable.

For more information on how to submit please visit www.saltspringartprize.ca or
contact info@saltspringartprize.ca

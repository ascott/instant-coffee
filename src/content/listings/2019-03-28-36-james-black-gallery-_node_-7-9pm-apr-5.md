_NODE_
wynne palmer - solo exhibition
The James Black Gallery
144 E6th Ave. Vancouver, BC

opening april_05_2019 @ 7 - 9pm
show run april_05 - 14_2019
hours 1 - 4pm thursday - sunday or by appointment
closed monday - wednesday

While pervasive in our culture, from comic books to movies and 
television, the term parallel universe is just one facet of the 
astronomical theory of the multiverse. _NODE_ is a photo-based 
project that explores interpretations of portals, time travel and parallel 
worlds.

Wynne Palmer is a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary artist, 
independent curator, producer and mentor working predominantly with 
digital media. Her personal research, art practice and collaborations 
often focus on thresholds and liminal spaces, where the natural and 
technological worlds converge. She brings into question issues of 
identity, concepts of location and the philosophy of language within the 
collective consciousness.

The James Black Gallery is an artist-run centre operating within the 
Main Street arts district of Vancouver, BC located on the unceded, 
ancestral and traditional territories of the xmky"m (Musqueam), 
Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), and sl"ilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

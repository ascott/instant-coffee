Please join us for a public lecture brought to you by the Interdisciplinary
Forums course at Emily Carr University.
Thursday January 27 @ 3:50 p.m. in the lecture theatre

James Rout, Mutable Memory and Digital Heritage

James Rout is the University Librarian at Emily Carr University. In a
former life, James was the Head of Library and Archives Services at The
Banff Centre,

one of North America's leading institutions for professional development in
the Arts. James has worked closely with artists and writers throughout his
career,

helping them navigate through the ever-expanding universe of information.

Everyone is welcome!

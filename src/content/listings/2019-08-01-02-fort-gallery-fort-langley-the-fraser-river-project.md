The Fort Gallery is seeking proposals for solo and group exhibitions and exhibitions 
organized by independent curators or collectives relating to the theme of the Fraser 
River. We will select three proposals for six to eight-week exhibitions to be presented 
from January-June 2020. Priority will be given to artists who can suggest community 
engagement activities or are willing to facilitate workshops.

Proposals are invited from all artists whose work reflects on the Fraser River. We 
welcome submissions of ideas/proposals, completed works, and works in progress. We 
encourage a wide range of responses to the theme, including but not limited to 
environmental, cultural, social, economic, historical, indigenous perspectives on the 
Fraser River.

For complete information: http://www.fortgallery.ca/the-fraser-river-project.html

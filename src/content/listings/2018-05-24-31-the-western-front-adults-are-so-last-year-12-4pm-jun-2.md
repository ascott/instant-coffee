Adults Are So Last Year! Saturday June 2 @12-4pm
303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver
Free Admission

We are pleased to present Adults Are So Last Year on Saturday, June 2 from
12-4pm at the Western Front. Artists and musicians have been commissioned
to create works and participatory projects in all mediums and for all ages.

Visitors of all ages are invited to participate in collaborative,
hand-drawn animated workshops with Lianne Zannier and Heather Smith and
create 19th century animation toys to take home. Sound artist Alanna Ho
will lead an exploration of sounds made by everyday objects and provide an
opportunity for kids to create their own graphic score. The Green Horn
youth community marching band will be roving around Dude Chilling Park and
outside the Western Front during the event with three performances. Artists
Derya Akay and Vivienne Bessette lead a workshop showing kids how to make
colourful patterns and imprints on fabric using flowers and natural dyes.

Plus, we'll be serving up grilled bratwurst from D-Original Sausage Co. and
scoops of Earnest Ice Cream

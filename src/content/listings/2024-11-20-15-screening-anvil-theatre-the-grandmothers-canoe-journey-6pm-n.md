Film Screening and Discussion: The Grandmothers Canoe Journey

Sunday, November 24, 2024 | 6:00 - 8:30 pm

Anvil Theatre, 3rd Floor at Anvil Centre

777 Columbia Street, New Westminster,
BC, V3M 1B6

The event is Free but please RSVP to museum@newwestcity.ca or call
604.527.4640

Join us for the premiere screening of the short film, The Grandmothers Canoe
Journey by Nadine Spence and Love Intersections. The film follows a special cedar
chest on an interconnected journey of wild salmon and Indigenous peoples from
Cheam to Sapperton Landing and honours Indigenous women, who despite colonial
harms, persist in healing with the lands and waters. A discussion to follow with the
filmmakers, artists and supporters: Nadine Spence, David and Jen of Love
Intersections, Atheana Picha, Rita Wong, Ronnie Dean Harris and David McCullum.
The event will open with a welcome from Mary Point.

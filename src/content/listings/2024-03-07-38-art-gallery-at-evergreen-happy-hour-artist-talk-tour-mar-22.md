Happy Hour Artist Talk & Tour
Silas Ng: "Music in My Eyes" and "Paths"
March 22, 2024
Doors 5:00 p.m.
Art Gallery at Evergreen
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam BC

Join us for a Happy Hour artist talk with Silas Ng and curatorial tour of "Paths" on
Friday, March 22 at Art Gallery at Evergreen. Doors open at 5:00pm. This event will be
delivered in ASL. Ng will introduce his practice and new public art installation "Music in
My Eyes" (2020-) on the Evergreen Cultural Centre lobby windows. Next, tour the
gallery exhibition Paths with assistant curator Anna Luth.

ASL interpretation will be available for the talk and tour. Cash bar will be open, and the
art gallery will be open until 6:30 p.m. Free to attend. Drop-in. This event is wheelchair
accessible.

About Silas Ng: "Music in My Eyes:"
Vancouver-based artist Silas Ng has been fascinated with classical music since
childhood. He explores movement, vibration and the
visual experience of music
through his series "Music in My Eyes" (2020-). Working from his experience as a Deaf
artist, Ng photographs performances of quick, energetic gestures and the long draw of
the bow across the strings to demonstrate how "volume can be shown through action."

This installation is part of the 2024 Capture Photography Festival Selected Exhibition
Program.

About "Paths:"
"Paths" features artwork by Canadian artists exploring paths, both real and imagined,
through the mediums of painting, drawing, sculpture, photography, collage and
printmaking. "Paths" is organized and circulated by Artists for Kids & the Gordon Smith
Gallery and curated by Amelia Epp.

Open Studio & Pottery Sale
Featuring Brit Bachmann & Cheryl Stapleton (Clay Addiction)
Saturday, June 7 from 11am-6pm
965 Vernon Drive, Vancouver

Bios:
Brit Bachmann is an artist and cultural worker based on the unceded territories of the
hn"q"min"m" and Swxw7mesh Snchim speaking peoples in Vancouver and
Burnaby. She throws, pinches and alters clay forms to represent movement, erosion
and the uncanny. Brit is the Ceramic Arts Programmer for the City of Burnaby at the
Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, an organizer of the 2026 Canadian Clay Symposium,
and a member of the Programming Committee for DOXA Documentary Film Festival.

Cheryl Stapleton (Clay Addiction) is an artist, educator, pottery studio technician and
manager based in Vancouver. She holds a BFA (1999) from Emily Carr University of
Art + Design. She has managed the Roundhouse Pottery Studio since 1999 and the
studio at West Point Grey from 2002 " 2021. She has taught ceramics classes to
both children and adults since 1996 at Kwantlen University, the Surrey Arts Centre,
the Vancouver School Board, the Vancouver Museum of Anthropology, and West
Point Grey Community Centre.

Accessibility:
Brit and Cheryl's studios are located side-by-side on the lower ground level of the
Mergatroid Building (down 2 steps) within the clay co-op. The entrance is located on
the east side of the building at 965 Vernon Drive. Masks are optional. Call/text for

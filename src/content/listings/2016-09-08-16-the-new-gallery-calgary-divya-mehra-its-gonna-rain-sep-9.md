Divya Mehra's new body of work at The New Gallery rains down this Friday

Calgary - On Friday, September 9, The New Gallery will premiere It's Gonna
Rain, an exhibition of new works by Canadian artist Divya Mehra. It's Gonna
Rain explores the universal relationship to the unexpected losses we all
face, focusing on the artist's own experience with the loss of her father,
Kamal, just over one year ago.

In the exhibition text, Kegan McFadden, longtime collaborator with Mehra,
writes, "in Mehra's unconventional shrine to her own loss, she asks, how do
we come to know violence?"

The three, new, sculptural works come together as a portrait of Kamal, and
also illustrate her grief after the unexpected loss, and the accumulation
of hurt as prominent symbols she associated with him were subsequently
damaged or lost. The exhibition consists of three fragments of her father's
life: first, the remnants of a statue of the deity Ganesh, who, shortly
after Kamal's passing, was sawed from His base and stolen from the place
where Kamal had installed Him. Second, the trunk of the tree where Kamal
used to pray, which was cut down without notice, also in the year following
his passing. Third, water collected from the Ganga River in India and the
Bow River in Calgary trickle down a plaster and lath wall, which falls to
ruin over the course of the exhibition from the slow but constant 'tears'
running down it's faade.

The New Gallery is pleased to present: It's Gonna Rain
Exhibition Dates: September 10 to October 22, 2016
Opening Reception: September 9 at 8PM
Location: 208 Centre Street SE, Calgary, AB

Divya Mehra splits her time between Winnipeg, New York, and New Delhi. Her
work has been included in a number of exhibitions and screenings, notably
with Creative Time, MoMA PS1, MTV, and the Queens Museum of Art (New York),
MASS MoCA (North Adams), Artspeak (Vancouver), The Images Festival
(Toronto), The Beijing 798 Biennale (Beijing) and more. She holds an MFA
from Columbia University and is represented in Toronto by Georgia Scherman
Projects.

The New Gallery (TNG) is an artist-run centre located in Calgary, Alberta.
This charitable centre for contemporary art was established in 1975 as the
Clouds & Water Gallery and Visual Production Society. Today, the Society
continues its vision through the operation of two venues - the Main Space,
a storefront in Calgary's Chinatown neighbourhood, and the +15 Window, a
shallow vitrine located at the Arts Commons building on a high-traffic
pedestrian walkway. These spaces support the research, creation, and
exhibition of social and political creative practices from artists at all
junctures of their careers, while enabling a public engagement with
contemporary art. Our programming comprises a broad range of art and
educational activities, including exhibitions, residencies, lectures, and
community collaborations that serve to invigorate our audience's experience
of contemporary art and culture.

The New Gallery gratefully acknowledges the ongoing support of Canada

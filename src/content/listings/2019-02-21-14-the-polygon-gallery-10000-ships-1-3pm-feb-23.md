10,000 Ships: An Afternoon Reception | 1-3 pm | February 23

Join us for an afternoon reception in celebration of 10,000 Ships, an exhibition which 
showcases a truly unique photographic archive created by local ship-spotter and photographer 
Rod Logan who has documented the comings and going of every manner of ship, tug, barge 
and vessel entering and leaving Vancouver's harbour over the past 30 years.

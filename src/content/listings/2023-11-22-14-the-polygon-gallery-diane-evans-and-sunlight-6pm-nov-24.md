About the Exhibition
Hailed as an expert in the field, Diane Evans (1954-2023) was an influential teacher
and an accomplished photographer. She captured wide-ranging subject matter
through a variety of cameras, including pinhole cameras that she constructed from
cardboard and black electrical tape, a Diana camera, and a Clix Deluxe gifted by her
grandfather. Diane Evans and Sunlight brings together a survey of Evanss
remarkable works that, in the artists own words, make tangible delicacy of light,
heaviness of shadow and the elusive quality of time without end.

Curated by Karen Love and Linda Chinfen

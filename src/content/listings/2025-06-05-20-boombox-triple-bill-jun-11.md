A room for holding
Avery Smith

A room for holding is an attempt to unpack my relationship to being responsible for
the literal survival of an actual human person. Through this dance, Im exploring my
ambivalent and joyous surrender to carrying, waiting, watching. Im considering
these acts of care as a continuous thread, where small actions, repeated over time,
quietly accumulate and contribute to a larger pattern of attention.

Averys current creative interests reflect her evolving identity as a full-time mother
and a sometimes dancer/maker of things. Threading these stories, she is
questioning her own ideas of rigour and balance in the search of a more embodied
artistic practice.

With collaborators Andrea Cownden and Emily Clarke
___

Swing, Knots, and Premonitions (working title)
Mia Pelayo & Madeleine Cruz

In their duet, Maddy and Mia explore themes of swing, knots, and premonitions as
vehicles of transformation through various characters, moods, and behaviours.

Maddy and Mia met a decade ago at their local dance studio and bonded over their
excitement about the possibilities of contemporary dance. In the summer of 2020
they created their dance films Risk and Where To? which premiered at the Modus
Operandi Sunset Screening. This summer of creating was the catalyst for their rich
collaborative partnership. Their most recent work, Two Girls Collide has been
presented at the 2022 MO Focus Group Showing and the 2024 Chalk it Up!
Emerging Artist Showcase. Their practice is driven by illustrative and precise
choreography, a love for pop culture and music, lightheartedness, friendship, text,
and storytelling. Theyre excited by dance as a way to animate living spirit within the
body, creating raw and unfiltered performance.
___

supergroup
Erika Mitsuhashi + One Single One

supergroup is a score based work that celebrates the improvisational prowess of
One Single One, repetition, telepathic spatial constellating and dancing together (to
music) as medicine.

Erika Mitsuhashi is an artist making performances, installations, video art and
sometimes objects that end up in performances. She is exploring her dramaturgical
practice in earnest and feels like the luckiest guy in the world to collaborate in life
and art with her beloved friends/peers/lovers.

One Single One is a collective of six dance artists based on the unceded territories
of the Squamish (Swxw7mesh), Tsleil-Waututh (sl"ilw"ta") and Musqueam
(xmky"m) nations. One Single One exists to support a shared vision for a
robust dance future of dancing together: one that includes space for radical
experimentation, vulnerability that is cradled in care, and a dedication to physical
practice that is inextricable from both their love for their body-selves and their love
for each other.

One Single One dancers:

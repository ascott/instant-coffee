Call to Local Artists :: South Haven Cemetery Service Building

The South Haven Cemetery Service Building public art competition is 
open to professional artists residing in the greater Edmonton area, and 
is held in accordance with the City of Edmonton policy "Percent for Art 
to Provide and Encourage Art in Public Areas" (C458C).

The Edmonton Arts Council Public Art Program on behalf of the City of 
Edmonton is seeking an artist or artist team to create an artwork in one 
of two locations at the South Haven Cemetery Service Building.

Artwork Option #1 Entrance Plaza
The exterior entrance plaza of the Service Building will be clad in steel 
panels. Artwork installed at this location will be highly visible to users of 
the building. The steel panels at the entrance of the service building will 
be installed to ensure proper fit with fastener holes and countersinking 
intact, and then removed to facilitate art creation in a studio setting. 
The panels will be transported back to the site and reinstalled once the 
artwork is complete. The hot rolled steel panels will be supplied and 
installed by the prime contractor at no cost to the artist.

Artwork Option #2 Courtyard Glazing
The interior lobby of the South Haven Cemetery Service Building will 
feature a curtain wall facing out onto a public courtyard. Consisting of 
six insulated glass units, the curtain wall is available for glazing with a 
ceramic frit coating. At this location the selected artist will work with the 
project team to coordinate delivery and pickup of the glass units to the 
artist's chosen glass frit manufacturer.

South Haven Cemetery is a multi-faith cemetery, therefore, overt 
"religious" themes are discouraged. Artists are asked to carefully 
consider their artwork choices at both locations. Artworks can include 
themes such as:

Seasons and time
Permanence and ephemerality (the common human experience)
The individual
Transformation
Material and immaterial
Budget:

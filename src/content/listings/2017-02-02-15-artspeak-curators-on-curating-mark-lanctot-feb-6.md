Curators on Curating: Mark Lanctot
Monday, Feb 6, 11:30am
Charles H. Scott Gallery

Please join us in the Charles H. Scott Gallery for a lunchtime talk by Mark 
Lanctot, curator at the Musee d'art contemporain de Montreal (MACM). As part of 
the Curators on Curating series, Lanctot will discuss an exhibition that has 
influenced him alongside an exhibition he has curated.

Born in Montreal, Lanctot completed his Master's in art history at the Universite 
de Montreal in 2002. He has published articles in Canadian Art and Esse: Art + 
Opinions, among other magazines. Before joining the MACM in October 2006 as 
a curator, he worked as an independent curator and was director of the 
Contemporary Art Galleries Association in Montreal. At the MACM, he 
coordinated the exhibitions Guy Ben-Ner (2007), Arnaud Maggs: Nomenclature 
(2008) and Beat Nation: Art, Hip Hop and Aboriginal Culture (October 2013). He 
curated solo exhibitions of Yannick Pouliot (2008), Tacita Dean (2009), Marcel 
Dzama (2010), Runa Islam (2010), Daniel Young & Christian Giroux (2011), 
Pierre Dorion (2012), Michel de Broin (2013), Ryan Gander (2016) and Olafur 
Eliasson (upcoming 2017). He also co-curated the first two editions of The 
Quebec Triennial (2008 and 2011) and the Claude Tousignant retrospective 
(2009).

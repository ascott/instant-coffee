In conjunction with the recent release of the Hotel Theory Reader, Fillip is pleased to 
present a Listening Group focused on the music of the Red Krayola at Selectors 
Records on Thursday, July 20, at 7 pm followed by an afterparty at the Projection Room.

Join curator Jordan Strom and musician Casey Wei for an evening looking at the radical 
output of the experimental band the Red Krayola (originally the Red Crayola). Active on 
and off since 1966, the groups output defies easy classification. As a Pitchfork review of 
their 1967 album The Parable of Arable Land surmised, It's a band that has no idea 
how to play its instruments. In fact, they don't even know what instruments are. The 
Red Krayola was founded in Texas by visual artist Mayo Thompson, writer Frederick 
Barthelme, and musician Steve Cunningham and is a frequent collaborator of British 
conceptual art group Art & Language. 

Drinks and light snacks will be served.

Free afterparty at the Projection Room (upstairs at the Fox Cabaret), beginning at 9 pm 
with music by Darwin Meyers. Everyone welcome!

The Hotel Theory Reader is the fourth title in Fillips ongoing Folio Series presenting 
writing by critics, artists, and curators that engages specific and recurring questions on 
international contemporary art. This anthology explores the possibilities of theory as an 
art form, bringing together ideas initially explored in an exhibition organized in 2015 at 
REDCAT | CalArts Downtown Center for Contemporary Arts. The book assembles a 
collection of texts by David Antin, Art & Language, Ruth Estvez, Bruce Hainley, Wayne 
Koestenbaum, Chris Kraus, Snejanka Mihaylova, Sohrab Mohebbi, Cally Spooner, V-
Girls, Danna Vajda, and Tirdad Zolghadr.

Jordan Strom is Curator of Exhibitions and Collections at Surrey Art Gallery and a 
former editor at Fillip.

Casey Wei is a Vancouver-based artist, musician, and the curator of art rock?, a monthly 
performance night held at the Astoria.

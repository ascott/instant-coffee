Toque Craft Fair

Dec 1 (6:00 - 10:00 p.m)

Dec 2 & 3 (11:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m.)

Admission: By Donation

This holiday season, Toque Craft Fair returns to Western Front bigger and better
than ever! Featuring over forty BC-based artists, craftspeople, and designers, join us
to find unique textiles, ceramics, jewellery, stationery, and home and body products
to cherish or to gift to a loved one.

Friday night festivities include a cash bar with drinks by Main St. Brewing, The Strait
& Narrow, and St
Hubertus & Oak Bay Estate Winery, and food by Planted Love.

As a community event and fundraiser, Toque is all about goodwill and fostering
sustainability for artists and artist-run culture in Vancouver. The participating artists,
craftspeople, and designers generously donate 30% of all sales to support Western
Fronts artistic program.

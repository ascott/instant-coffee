Hollow Anchor consists of folded paper sculptures, often in multiples, 
created from screen-printed patterns. The sculptures are realistic 
representations of everyday objects and create a trompe-l'oeil on first glance. 
Everyday objects get reduced to forms and shapes with their functions 
subverted. The sculptures become both the object and the representation of 
the object at the same time.

Suzie Smith is a print based artist from Winnipeg, Canada. She graduated 
with a Bachelor of Fine Arts Degree from Concordia University in 2004 and a 
Master of Fine Arts from The Glasgow School of Art in 2011. She has shown 
nationally and internationally and has had solo exhibitions at Atelier Circulaire 
(Montreal), Open Studio (Toronto), and Ace Art (Winnipeg).

Applications open for Portfolio Prize | 2016 BFA Prize

2016 Portfolio Prize

Applications Due: February 15th 2016

Conceived by artists for artists, The Portfolio Prize aims to encourage
the development of young artists through early career support. The
2016 Portfolio Prize funds will be directed toward the recognition of two
emerging artists. The Foundation will present these prizes in 2016 with
over $17,000 in granted money. Recipients of the juried prizes will be
announced in the spring of 2016.

This year's portfolio features the work of distinguished artists Graham
Gillmore, Angela Grossmann, Attila Richard Lukacs, Derek Root,
Gordon Smith, and Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun. Launched in April
2015, the subscription sales of this portfolio of six prints established the
funds for the 2016 awards with future sale proceeds going to the
development of the next portfolio and prize.

Applications are welcome for the BFA Prize.

Nominations will be made for the 2016 Emerging Artist Prize, and will
be chosen from nominations only.

Application Guidelines for BFA Prize:

Eligible students within 5 years of graduation from a BFA programme in
visual arts at a Lower Mainland University (or reasonable equivalent)

1 :: CV
Upload a PDF with your updated CV, including your BFA program.

2 :: Text
Write an artist text of up to 100 words.

3 :: Portfolio
You may submit either one pdf with a captioned work per page or up to
five separate image files. If submitting a video work, submit a screen
capture from the work with a link in the caption to a vimeo video or
another reliable video streaming service of your choice. The video may
be unlisted or a private link as long as it is not necessary to log in to
view the video.

The maximum number of works that may be submitted are any ONE of
the following options:

Up to 5 image works
Up to 3 image works and 1 video work
Up to 3 video works

Video works submitted must be at most 3 minutes in length.

4 ::Submission

Applications will be made online by February 15th 2016, please do so
here: http://www.portfolioprize.com/apply

Artist: Jonathan Syme

ExhibitionTitle: Up and High, Down and Low

Where: Monte Clark Gallery

Opening Reception Details: Saturday October 14th @ 12- 5 pm

Exhibition Run: Oct 14 - Nov 11

The title suggests an exploration of opposites or dualities. The artworks delve into
the contrast between light and shadow, chaos and tranquility, or expansion and
confinement,
creating a visual dialogue between these opposing elements."Up and High" might
allude to moments of transcendence, inspiration, or spirituality, while "Down and
Low" could
signify grounding, rootedness, and connection to the earth. The paintings explore the
balance between these states.

The artworks follow a narrative of exploration, with subjects venturing into both
elevated and subdued landscapes containing allusions to figuration. The medium of
paint is employed
not only as a means of crafting illusory images but, also as a sculptural material that
extends beyond the surface of the canvas and into the physical space of the viewer,
manifesting as substantial gestures of pigment layered upon preceding strata of
visual stimuli. These strata are cultivated through techniques of staining, scraping
and the application
of expressive brushwork. As the compositions undergo transformation, the layers
engage in choreographed interplay, coalescing into a depiction of dynamic spatial
dimensions that alternately
recede and project in a fluid intermingling of visual phenomena creating a optical
journey for the audience.

The sculptural quality of the paint is echoed and reinforced by the architectural
expressions of the frames. By matching the visual weight of the paintings with the
substantial mouldings used to frame them, the works become unified objects and
can be read as three dimensional works rather than being read face on thus being
confined to a flat picture plain. Further to the role of the frames is a visual reference
to Art
historical periods. The traditions of placing paintings in frames has historically
been a way to imply status or completion of a work, though those same
considerations are utilized in these works they are also meant to convey a
humorous take on those traditions, often through unexpected colour choices adding
some needed levity to counteract what could otherwise be construed as overly
serious in utilizing such traditional aesthetics. Through nearly twenty years of
experience fabricating bespoke picture frames, Syme has developed a sensitivity to
the effects of frame forms and colour choices, both of which, can influence the
reading of an artwork and he employs this skill in the coupling of the paintings and
the frames that house them. The result is a presence that is greater than the sum of
its parts.

Submissions to OPEN e v+ a 2010, a further 2010 Guideline

To: All Artists, and to the attention of Gallerists, and Arts Officers

From: The Committee and Curator of e v+ a 2010

Re: Submissions to OPEN e v+ a 2010, a further 2010 Guideline.

Dear Artist:

The committee and administration of e v+ a trust that you have already
received by email and/or in hard copy by post the Official Application Form
and the Submission Guidelines for OPEN e v+ a 2010. If not, please contact
www.eva.ie <http://www.eva.ie/> or info@eva.ie or +353 (0)87 9477042.

The 2010 Curator, Elizabeth Hatz, whose CV and statement accompanies the
Application Form, has asked us to pass on some further information about
the approach and emphases that she will use in the adjudication of
submissions, a process that begins directly after the submission deadline,
Friday, 27 11 09, and continues into early December.

In the adjudication process of e v+ a 2010 no specific, pre-conceived theme
or concept will determine the selections made from the artworks submitted.
Each artist and artwork will be considered on her/his/its own terms.
Therefore an important initiative will remain with the artists and their
submissions.
The foremost standard guiding the adjudication process will be that of
"quality".
As clearly stated in the Guidelines, all artists are encouraged to submit
their work whatever the materials, medium, design approach, style, genre,
theme or concept used.
It is important that painters, sculptors, and printmakers realise that
their best work is very much desired as submissions. All such work will
receive full and equal consideration on its own terms as will any and all
other artwork.
This explicit emphasis is made now in response to the serious concern
voiced by many artists and audiences that recent curatorial emphasis in e
v+ a exhibitions seems to have definitely favoured multi- and lens-screen
based media as exhibited in dark cubes and installations with the effect of
marginalizing or eliminating other media and approaches.
As an exhibition founded by artists and always aimed at bringing into close
encounter artists and audiences, the Committee and Curator assure you
that the selection process of OPEN e v+ a 2010 will clearly be open to any
and all artists’ approaches. All submissions will be chosen by the Curator
by the quality they offer; the ultimate theme or concept of e v+ a 2010
will arise out of that quality.
For any further clarification of these or any other matters please reach e
v+ a at the above contact addresses and number.

The Safehaus Project is looking for artist for it's second 
"pop - up" gorilla gallery / group show taking place in Victoria mid-
November. 

Interested artists please send an e-mail with Name / Contact Info & 
examples of Current work ( up to 5 images) to 
safehaus@hotmail.com 
* please have all submissions in by Oct. 23 2015*

The CSB Art Collective's Safehaus Project is pop-up art exhibit 
showcasing new work that pushes the boundaries for the artist and the 
public. With the space and artist line up consistently changing, the 
theme of risk-taking remains constant. What does an artist create when 
freed from a need to think about audience and market? This project 
wants to explore that question..

Having successfully done the first one this past August, the CSB is 
aiming to start Safehaus as a quarterly event. Committed to bringing in 
new artists and new ideas, while most are local, the call is open to any 
artist anywhere to submit. 

Run as a co-op each artist pays a fee: this goes to pay for space, 
insurance, and the countless other small parts that go into putting on a 
three day show

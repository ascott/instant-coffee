Lecture / Philip Tinari: ON/OFF / Asia Contemporary Speaker Series
VIFF / Vancity Theatre - 1181 Seymour Street
Monday, March 11, 7pm
The rise of Asia on the international scene is one of the most compelling 
stories in contemporary art. Today, Hong Kong, Singapore, Dubai, Tokyo and 
Beijing are major hubs for contemporary art, competing directly with London 
and New York. In order to understand this phenomenon and its connection to 
economic and political power, the Asia Contemporary Speaker Series 
presents On/Off: The Double Consciousness of China's Newest Generation of 
Artists with Philip Tinari, Director of the Ullens Centre for Contemporary Art, 
Beijing.

Presented by the Canadian Art Foundation International Speaker Series in 
collaboration with the Asia Pacific Foundation of Canada's National 
Conversation on Asia and the Vancouver Art Gallery. Free for Vancouver Art 
Gallery Members and students (with ID), $10 Non-Members. Tickets at the 
door.

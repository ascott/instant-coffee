FUNDING OPPORTUNITY
Neighbourhood Matching Fund
The City of Vancouver's Neighbourhood Matching Fund supports
neighbourhood-based groups that want to make creative improvements to local
public lands. Eligible projects must actively involve the community and
build neighbourhood connections.
The Neighbourhood Matching Fund is not a grant; community groups are
reimbursed for project expenses based on equivalent contributions from
volunteer labour, other funding sources, and in-kind donations.
Funding requests are needs-based and can receive up to $10,000. The average
project is funded for about $3,000 to $5,000.

Application deadlines: April 15 and October 15

Find program guidelines and the online application in the Apply for funding
by visiting:
http://vancouver.ca/people-programs/neighbourhood-matching-fund.aspx

Cut and Please
Works by Peggy and Karen Ngan
Opening Friday, February.1  8-11PM
February.1- February.29


Lucky's Gallery
3972 Main Street
Vancouver, B.C.


Peggy and Karen Ngan
 This new series of collage and drawing is created with the still images from the video piece, “Dynamite” which was finished in 2007.  In “Dynamite”, they explore the identity of being twin sisters through a performative game between individuals.	While the idea of role-playing is being explored in the video, it is also performed through drawing and collage in this exhibition.
 The process of creating the collage or drawing recalls Ngan’s processes of video editing and shooting of “Dynamite”.  When bringing the two mediums together in the show, they not only mirror each other but also create different means within the medium itself.  
Peggy and Karen Ngan live and work in Vancouver.  They graduated from Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design in 2005.  They work on drawing, video and performance collaboratively.  They are also members of the Yac-Tac artists collective.

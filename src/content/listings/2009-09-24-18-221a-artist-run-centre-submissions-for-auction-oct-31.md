221A Artist Run Centre is hosting its first Silent Auction. We are looking for local artists to submit 
work by October 31st, 2009. Proceeds will be split 50/50 between artist and gallery. 
This event will take place from December 11th to the 13th

To submit work please email us at hello@221a.ca with the following information; your name, email 
and telephone number as well as a description of your work as detailed below.A mix of small and 
large work will be accepted and multiple submissions are encouraged. 

Email images of your work in JPEG or PDF format. Please keep individual images under 1MB and a 
maximum of 10MB in total. Include a list describing number of works, size and price. For sets of 
images (e.g. photo, prints) please include these as one submission.

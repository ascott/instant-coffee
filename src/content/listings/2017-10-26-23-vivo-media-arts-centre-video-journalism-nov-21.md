Nov 21 & 24

Video Journalism workshop
presented by VIVO Media Arts Centre & Media Democracy Day 2017

Learn to produce Real News with community engaged video artist Flick Harrison!

Sign up and more info here: https://video-journalism-with-flick.eventbrite.ca

2 sessions, 6 hours total
Session 1: Tuesday, Nov 21, 6pm-9pm
Session 2: Friday, Nov 24, 6pm-9pm
$56.25, or $45 with VIVO Extended Producer Membership
An assortment of snacks will be provided

Learn documentary shooting techniques, on-camera reporting, off-camera interview 
skills, journalistic writing, and documentary editing. Develop a documentary concept, or 
bring one, and shoot footage to be analyzed and edited over two workshop sessions. 
Participants may use VIVO's video cameras, or bring their own.

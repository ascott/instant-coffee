The Webinar: In this
webinar I will teach you my tips and tricks when it comes to
manifesting the things you want into your creative life / creative business.

By attending this webinar you will already be giving yourself permission to dream
and work towards the things you want to manifest. Maybe you want to work with
a new client, maybe you want to learn to be a photographer, maybe you want to
paint your first ever mural? Whatever it is that your heart desires for your creative
life / creative business, I'll share my personal experiences, and tips and tricks on
how to work towards manifesting these things.

About me: Hello, I'm Lauren. I'm an artist, poet and creative business consultant.
I have been creative all my life but it wasn't until I moved from the U.K to
Vancouver that I took my creativity seriously. In the space of a year I went from
painting in my kitchen to having a shared studio and now a studio of my own. I
have started my own creative business and have worked with a range of creative
organizations here in Vancouver. Not only that, I have made many new friends in
the creative industry that in turn helps me manifests my creative dreams.
How did I personally do this? Through a range of both practical and "woo woo"
manifestation techniques which I will share with you in this webinar.

Find out more about me via my website (laurenwelland.com ) or Instagram
(@laurenwelland)

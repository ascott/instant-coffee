Call for Submissions:
FACE / WASTE Group Exhibition
Submission Deadline: August 25, 2024, 11:59 pm EDT
Exhibition Dates: October 19 - December 15, 2024
Industrial Arts, Markham, ON

Steelcase Art Projects (SAP) is pleased to invite emerging, mid-career and
established artists to submit their work that explores the theme of Waste. The
exhibition will be held in a semi-demolished space at Industrial Arts in Markham from
October to December.

Waste, regarded as worthless material, is a human-defined and controlled concept.
When we designate something as waste and discard it into the trash bin, it
undergoes a nullification of its monetary value and its symbolic meaning changes.
The symbolism of waste extends beyond its origin and final destination, delving
into
the deeper relationship between humans, the material world, and the land. This
involves the social and ecological imagination, practices, and open narratives
associated with waste.

FACE / WASTE invites artists to face the waste created through their art practice
and daily life, with honesty.

Please submit the following materials via email steelcaseartprojects@gmail.com attn
to Curator Yuluo Wei with the subject FACE / WASTE Open Call [First Name Last
Name]
Up to 5 images (jpeg format, max 5MB each image)
CV
Artist bio (PDF, max 250 words)
Artwork dimensions and description (PDF, max 250 words)
Free submission.

CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS
DEADLINE MARCH 17

The Lido (518 East Broadway, Vancouver) and Warm Notice are accepting submissions 
for *paid* residencies for musicians and artists working with sound. 

The month-long residency will include a minimum $400 artist fee guarantee; 2-4 
evenings of stage time at the Lido and 1 optional day for a workshop/artist talk; a sound 
tech; visuals.

There will be 4 residencies/year, with the first beginning in April 2019. Deadline to apply 
is March 17. See the Google form for more info:

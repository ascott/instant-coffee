On September 20, for the first time, Canadian Art will be webcasting one of
its panels with the goal of making it available live to artists, students,
critics, educators, curators and collectors across the country, rather than
just a small audience in one city.

The panel we're webcasting is (we hope!) of national interest, as it
addresses changes to art writing that have happened over the past 30 years
since Canadian Art first began publishing in September 1984.

Panel speakers include Gabrielle Moser, Sarah Milroy and Sky Goodden. As
you likely already know, these critics have published in the Globe and
Mail, Artforum, Artinfo, Fillip, the National Post, Border Crossings, Esse,
C Magazine, Modern Paintersâ€¦ the list goes on.

All three of these critics have also worked in editorial capacitiesâ€”Milroy
at Canadian Art, Moser on the board of C and other publications, and
Goodden at Artinfo Canada. So they can offer perspectives on this issue as
editors as well as writers.

The discussion will be moderated by Richard Rhodes, editor of Canadian Art
and founder of C Magazine, which is also celebrating its 30th anniversary
this year.

Anyone interested is welcome to tune in to canadianart.ca/writingpanel from
12 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time (I think that's 9 a.m. to 10:30
a.m. Pacific) on September 20 to partake of the free webcast.

Curator Chat on Rita McKeough: dig as deep as the darkness
RAG Curator Nan Capogna with special guests:
Horticulturalist Egan Davis
Artist & Curator Holly Schmidt

Sunday, September 15
2:00 - 3:00 pm
Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate

On the final day of the exhibition Rita McKeough: dig as deep as the darkness, we are
closing the show with an informal discussion on the overarching themes in the exhibition.

McKeough's latest installation is an immersive experience exploring the vulnerability of the
environment, giving voice to the plants and animals that are fighting back against
development, pollution, and other human activity.
Hear from the curator how the exhibition developed and some of the conversations she had
with the artist. Davis and Schmidt will respond to the exhibition from their own perspectives
as educators who work with and about nature.

Free admission. Everyone welcome to join in the conversation.

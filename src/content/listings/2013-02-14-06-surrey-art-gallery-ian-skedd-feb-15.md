Ian Skedd: Whatever Is Contained Within Is Art, And Everything Else is Life
Surrey Art Gallery
February 15 to April 21

What purpose does ambient music and sound serve in the public spaces of 
the city? How is sound used to make announcements and direct human 
behaviour through these environments? How have artists, who work 
predominantly with sound, engaged with these forms of broadcast? Stationed 
in the Surrey Art Centre’s three-storey elevator, Ian Skedd’s Whatever Is 
Contained Within Is Art, And Everything Else is Life is a sound art installation 
that appropriates two forms of public broadcasting systems: Muzak and 
public service announcement. Skedd’s project is part of Open Sound 2013: 
Sound/tract, a three-part exhibition of audio-based art works presented at 
intervals throughout 2013. Guest curated by Debra Zhou, Sound/tract 
presents different approaches to sound that explore and translate the interplay 
between text and information within the interior spaces of the Surrey Art 
Centre and Surrey Art Gallery.

Ian Skedd’s Whatever Is Contained Within Is Art, And Everything Else is Life 
presents a sound installation inspired by ambient shopping music—
soundtracks that are often a translation of popular songs—along with spoken 
phrases and aphorisms borrowed from various literary, philosophical, political, 
and historical sources. By focusing on the psychological process of decoding 
aural information, the artist invites the audience to consider the trigger that 
identifies the relationship between language and thought, and how words and 
sounds slip into our media-saturated, cultural environments. By subtly altering 
the normal contents of these ambient sounds, Skedd draws attention to the 
types of spaces that these soundtracks often inhabit: spaces of waiting, rest, 
or transition, such as corridors, elevator cars, and lobbies.The title of the work 
is taken from a manifesto by artist Allan Kaprow on the blurring art and life. 
This sound installation continues to April 21.

Ian Skedd is a multi-disciplinary artist living and working in Vancouver, BC. He 
studied at Emily Carr University of Art + Design, Vancouver, and received his 
MFA from Chelsea College of Art and Design, London, UK in 2009. His 
practice focuses on psychological and conceptual relationships to space, 
incorporating a wide reaching interest in performative actions, as well as the 
psychological process of decoding information. His work has been shown in 
solo and group exhibitions including: Gallery Fukai, Vancouver; Triangle 
Space, London UK; Western Front, Vancouver; Charles H. Scott Gallery, 
Vancouver; Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver; Artspeak, Vancouver; and 
Galerie Werner Whitman, Montreal, among others.

Debra Zhou is an arts and culture facilitator and independent curatorbased in 
Vancouver. She worked at Centre A (Vancouver International Centre for 
Contemporary Asian Art) from 2006 to 2011, and the Vancouver Art Gallery in 
2012. She has curated exhibitions for LIVE Biennale (Politics of Romance, 
2010), 221A Artist Run Centre (My Big Family, 2011), and Asia Pacific 
Foundation (Instant Coffee, 2012). Past sound-focused curatorial projects 
include Strawberry Jam concert, a telematic sound art performance using 
Skype (2010), and CO-LAB, a workshop and exhibition project in collaboration 
with Vancouver New Music (2011). She is currently the Editor for YISHU: 
Journal of Contemporary Chinese Art’s Chinese language version.

Open Sound is a Surrey Art Gallery exhibition program that was developed in 
2008 to support the production and presentation of audio art forms as part of 
contemporary art practice. Open Sound seeks to feature the leading edge of 
audio art, and has included site specific installation, radio experiments, 
ecological practices, and other investigations into the limits of sound today.

Concurrent to Ian Skedd’s Whatever Is Contained Within Is Art, And 
Everything Else is Life, Surrey Art Gallery is presenting three ceramics 
exhibitions, which continue to March 24.

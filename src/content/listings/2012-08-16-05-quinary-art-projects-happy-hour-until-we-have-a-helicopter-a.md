Quinary Art Projects presents HAPPY HOUR: An exhibition by Until We 
Have A Helicopter
Wednesday August 15 - Monday August 20, 2012
popup gallery @ 1387 Richards Street and Pacific Blvd

UWHAH will be serving a special drink for free each day for one hour 
only. Come enjoy our company at our custom-built bar and view new 
works we've created for the occasion. To attend Happy Hour please 
follow @UWHAH1 on Twitter or email matt@uwhah.com with the subject 
"Happy Hour" and we will notify you every morning with the time the 
hour will commence. 

Until We Have A Helicopter is the collaborative entity of Vancouver 
based artists Wes Cameron and Matthew Robertson. Working together 
since 2005 they have developed a practice that includes large-scale 
sculptural production, curatorial projects, gallery direction and 
functional-object making. Invoking themes of sport, leisure, travel and 
adventure to humanize a conceptual approach, UWHAH perpetuate the 
overlap of life and art with a serious and critical wit. 

Quinary Art Projects by Wesley Yuen is presenting a 14 week popup 
gallery @ 1387 Richards Street where invited guests organize weekly 
exhibitions.
This is week 12.

Thinking about Habitat and Community | Artist Talk with Carmen Rosen

DEER LAKE ARTIST RESIDENCIES

Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Studio
103

October 10, 2023 at 7pm

And October 24, 2023 at 7pm

Join us for an evening with Carmen Rosen as she discusses her practice in the Still
Creek and Brunette River watershed over the past two decades, and her current
explorations as Deer Lake Artist in Residence at the Baldwin House. Tea and Q&A to
follow.

Carmen Rosen is an interdisciplinary artist initiating creative community integrated
projects that enrich and provide ongoing creative work for many artists and community
members. She has an Art History degree from UBC, and a diploma from Emily Carr
College of Art and Design. Carmen has received the Vancouver Mayors Arts Award,
the Queens Jubilee Award, and the BC Achievement Award for her art with the
community. She is founding Artistic Director of Still Moon Arts Society. Through Still
Moon and in collaboration with many community partners, Carmen founded the
Renfrew Ravine Moon Festival, and numerous Still Creek Art and Stewardship
projects.

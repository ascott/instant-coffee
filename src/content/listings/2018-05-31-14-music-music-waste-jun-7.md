DIY Fest Continues On in Harsh Cultural Climate

May 24, 2018 -- Vancouver, BC

The climate for DIY Arts in Vancouver may be getting dire, but Music Waste
trudges on into its 24th year! From June 7-10 at various venues throughout
Vancouver, the festival will feature over 18 music shows, 12 comedy events,
two spoken word showcases, and three art exhibitions. Emerging and
innovative talent will be front and center, as Music Waste has a mandate to
highlight new and underground artists in the city. Music Waste also strives
to stay truly affordable in a city where the definition of affordability
is becoming increasingly absurd. All access passes are $20, or individual
shows are $7 at the door.

This years theme is space -- as in outer space -- but speaking of space:
were running out of it. Venues continue to close down as developers add to
their already expansive domain. Finding available venues was tougher this
year than any other in recent history. With the closure of many Vancouver
music institutions including Red Gate Hastings, The Cobalt, Hidden City,
and Selectors Records, affordable venues are becoming a scarce resource. If
trends continue, outer space may be the only space the local art scene has
left.

Nonetheless, the festivals music selection is as diverse and innovative as
ever, ranging from punk to electronica, indie pop to hip-hop, and a dash of
the avant-garde " all at the 12-band opening night bill at Fortune Sound
Club. The festival also features many brand new bands such as Kamikaze
Nurse, LeMaster, Garbage Dreams, My Wife, and Sigh.

Art Waste also continues its 24-year run with art shows at Fortune Sound
Club, Dynamo Arts Collective, and NRG Zone. All the shows meditate on the
concept Honour, which the Art Waste Committee describes as a drive
towards unquestionable faith, a guiding nationalism, yearning for valour,
seeking conquest ... aside from its dominant implication of a medieval
scourge, honour may also be a source of rejuvenation and rebuilding, of
community, and of the banality of it all.

Comedy Waste is bigger than ever with 12 shows and a newspaper launch. It
features two brand new interdisciplinary shows at the China Cloud, curated
by award-winning comedian and musician Aaron Read. Friday nights
Comics//Comics collides the world of visual art and comedy by featuring
both stand-up comedians and comic artists, while Saturday nights
Chrisariffic! Storytelling! Improv! Show! lives up to its name as
comedians improvise scenes based around the music of local legend Chrisariffic.

Brand new this year, Music Waste features a spoken word series to reflect
the changing landscape of the Vancouver art scene. The spoken word showcase
at Luckys Comics features local writers reading their funny, insightful,
and moving works of fiction and poetry. The Toast Collective will host a
special Music Waste edition of Poetry is Bad for You, a casual night of
non-pretentious poetry (and drinks) which prioritizes centering Indigenous,
Black, and other writers of colour and Queer, Trans, and Two-spirit writers.

Also new this year, in response to community feedback, Music Waste has
expanded programming that celebrates queer artists. On Friday night, some
of the citys best-known drag things (and some fresh faces, too!) including
Amy Grindhouse and Tommi Horror will star in BUST: Drag Waste! Following
the show is a late-night dance party hosted by DJ M-M and softieshan.
Saturday night will feature a show of all bands with queer-identifying
members including local favourites Anybodys, Kellarissa, and Pudding.

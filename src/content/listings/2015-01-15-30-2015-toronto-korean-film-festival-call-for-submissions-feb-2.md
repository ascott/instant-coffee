TKFF 2015 Korean Shorts SUBMISSION ANNOUNCEMENT

Toronto Korean Film Festival is now calling for submissions from filmmakers!

TKFF is a cultural organization that aims to present authentic Korean
cinema to the audience of Toronto, as well as to support the Korean-
Canadian film community.

Eligibility for TKFF 2015 Korean Shorts Program
Films must meet one of the following requirements:

- Made by Korean or shot in Korea
- Made by Korean living in Canada
- About Korean culture or theme (e.g. Actors speaking Korean language)

** All submissions in languages other than English must provide ENglish
subtitles.
SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

DEADLINE FOR ALL SUBMISSIONS IS FEBRUARY 28th, 2015, 11:59 P.M. EST

- Link to your film (e.g. youtube/vimeo)

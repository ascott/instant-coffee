The Contemporary Art Gallery presents the first solo exhibition in 
Vancouver in more than four years by artist Kevin Schmidt, now 
based in both Vancouver and Berlin. Comprising a survey of recent 
works, it includes two major new pieces, EDM House and High 
Altitude Balloon Harmless Amateur Radio Equipment. 

Schmidt is best known for addressing notions of displaced spectacle 
where remarkable events containing elements of urban culture are 
staged against the backdrop of the natural world. Often situated in 
remote locations, these juxtapositions of seemingly disparate 
elements question both the seductive elements of contemporary 
cultural production and the constructions that surround the idea of 
nature. 

The exhibition is made in collaboration with Kunstverein 
Braunschweig, Germany.

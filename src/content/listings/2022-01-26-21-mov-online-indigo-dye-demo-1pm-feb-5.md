Online Indigo Dye Demo
1 PM - 3 PM, Saturday, February 5, 2022

You're invited to experience the fascinating world of dyeing with natural Indigo!

Although it is not a traditional colour, it is a very interesting one! Join Rita Kompst,
Musqueam Artist and Zoe McDonell, Natural Dye Expert, for an Indigo dye workshop
where we will briefly discuss the history of Indigo while dyeing! Each participant can pick
up their dye sample card after the workshop.

They will explore the incredible history of Indigo while participants learn how to set up an
indigo vat, as well as techniques on dyeing their own animal and plant fibers. Learn how
you can zero in on the perfect shade of blue and how indigo can be used for many more
colours.

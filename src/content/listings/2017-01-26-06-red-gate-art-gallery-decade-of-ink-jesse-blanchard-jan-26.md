A panoptic display of illustrations and zines covering ten years of Jesse's continual 
explorations in ink. Utilising styles of automatic drawing and underground comics, 
Jesse's narratives frequently escape the panel into frenetic environments. Characters 
free from their sequence are left to explore these surroundings, or dissolve into a sea of 
faces, geometry, gay sex, anxiety, and macaroni.

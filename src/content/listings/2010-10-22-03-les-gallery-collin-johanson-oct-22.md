Get Lucky
New works by Collin Johanson
Opening Reception Friday Oct. 22nd 7 - 11 pm
October 22- December 5

LES Gallery
1879 Powell St. Vancouver BC

In his new body of paintings and drawings, Get Lucky, Collin Johanson
investigates a middle ground between representation and abstraction. Using
everyday objects such as foil, denim and rope he approaches his works
sculpturally, focusing on the surface textures of these objects and the
types of marks that they are able to leave. Johanson explores the
associations in these marks, allowing them to act as a bridge between
abstract figuration and straight representation. The physical trace of the
object suggests a tangible scale, as well as a sense of a readable subject
while also casting the object in an unfamiliar way. It is this area between
readability and unfamiliarity which is the main area of interest in
Johanson's process in this series.

Collin Johanson was a semifinalist in the RBC painting competition in 2008.
He has shown at the Red Bull 381 Projects (Toronto), The Museum of
Contemporary Canadian Art (Toronto), Access Gallery (Vancouver) and Helen
Pitt Gallery (Vancouver.) He was the recipient of the VADA (Visual Arts
Development Award) in 2007 and is currently completing his MFA at Concordia
University in Montreal.

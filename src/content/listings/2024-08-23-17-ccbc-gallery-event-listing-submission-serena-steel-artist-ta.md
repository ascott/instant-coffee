Hours & Locations: 5:45 " 8p, CCBC Gallery & Carousel Theatre

Description:

Join us for a special evening with Secwpemc artist Serena Steel, featuring an artist
talk and private viewing of her exhibition, q"emxwqsem (to tie a knot at the end of
your thread).

private viewing: 5:45 - 6:45p Experience q"emxwqsem up close before the artist talk
begins. This private viewing offers an intimate atmosphere
to appreciate each
artwork's intricate details and contemplative narratives.

artist talk: 7 - 8p Delve deeper into Steels artistic journey and the inspirations behind
q"emxwqsem. Hear firsthand about the techniques, stories, and cultural influences
that shape each piece in this thought-provoking exhibition.

Webpage: [https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/qemxweqsem-artist-talk-private-viewing-](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/qemxweqsem-artist-talk-private-viewing-)
tickets-936911275847


q"emxwqsem is a collection of work that is centered around the act of hand-
stitching. From beadwork to basketry, each artwork requires time and consideration
from the maker, set in its unwillingness to be rushed. The labour becomes a
reflection of the care that is taken with each stitch, and the repetition becomes a
ceremony in itself.

Steels work is centered around belonging and the ways that it is rooted in the land
and the people who she comes from. Each object is imbued with the memories and
stories that ground her in her home, and remind her of the responsibility
that she has
to it.

In a time of rapid production and increased demand, q"emxwqsem is a love letter to
the importance of slow and careful work.

Call for Submissions: The Hatch Art Gallery

Deadline: June 27th 2019

The Hatch Art Gallery is UBCs only student-run exhibition space. Situated on the unceded, 
ancestral and traditional lands of the xwm0"kwym (Musqueam), the Hatch aims to 
stimulate the exchange of art and ideas between artists, students and the public. The Hatch 
seeks to present innovative projects on critical subjects relevant to larger discourses in 
contemporary art, visual culture and theory. In addition to biweekly exhibitions, the Hatch 
also maintains and displays the AMS Permanent Collection, an impressive assortment of 
Canadian art that provides a cross section of artistic growth and development in Canada 
since 1945.

The Hatch Art Gallery is now accepting submissions for the upcoming 2019-2020 exhibition 
season.

We invite you to submit proposals for artwork, exhibitions and performances to take place in 
the Hatchs main gallery. Proposals for projects containing artwork of all media will be 
considered. Priority will be granted to submissions from UBC students and alumni.

How to apply:

Download our application at: ams.ubc.ca/hatchapplication and send it to 
hatch@ams.ubc.ca, in addition to all relevant support materials by June 27th, 2019 at 
11:59PM.

Questions? Please email: hatch@ams.ubc.ca

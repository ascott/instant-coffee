Friday, December 1 at 2 pm and 7 pm

Once again, we are pleased to welcome the UBC Contemporary Players to the Belkin
Art Gallery for an afternoon concert inspired by the exhibition The Beautiful Brain: The
Drawings of Santiago Ramn y Cajal . Led by Directors Corey Hamm and Paolo
Bortolussi, this graduate and undergraduate student ensemble from the UBC School of
Music will animate the Gallery with a program that includes piano and vibraphones and
features George Crumb's masterpiece Vox Balaenae.

And at 7 pm, Corey Hamm's piano class invites you to enjoy THE FOUR ELEMENTS:
Piano Music Inspired by Earth, Fire, Water, Airfeaturing works performed by Noah Ha,
Andrew Hung, Isaac Jiang, Jinny Kim, Peter Krejcar, Matthew Leung, Mark Masaites,
Sylvia Nam, Emily Picard, Edward Tang, Adrian To, Tracy Wu and Susan Xia.

The concerts are free and everyone is welcome.

Annual Celebration of Moving Image Art Presents a Plenitude of International
Experimental Cinema

Antimatter [media art] | Oct 17 to 27, 2024

Screenings Installations Online Programs
Various Locations in Victoria, BC

antimatter.ca

A bastion of experimental moving image work, Antimatter [media art] will present
120+ films in-person and online, beginning on October 17, as well as free public
installations and the return of Automat, an ongoing series of unique, commissioned
self portraits of/by participating filmmakers. Originating from more than 30 countries,
the majority of festival offerings are world, North American or Canadian premieres.

Weve never supported the false dichotomy between experimental film and the
visual art world says Antimatter Curator Deborah de Boer. Deluge Contemporary
Art, the venue for Antimatter screenings, has a track record of organizing
international exhibitions of artists working within and beyond the medium. Media
installation is an interesting approach to exhibition making, with organizers reliant on
hardware and modifications of space to provide options for presentation, while digital
files can be shared around the world in seconds. The festival has a unique place in
the ecology of media art. Often the films shown at Antimatter are world or Canadian
premieres
and as a result they find homes with North American distributors. Beyond
the festival circuit, many of these artists want their work to be seen in the larger
context of galleries, institutions, biennales and art fairs and Antimatter helps facilitate
this. Participating artists often teach, program screenings and organize exhibitions,
so connections made via the festival pave the way for future collaborations."

Automat allows audiences worldwide entre into the creative process and personal
lives of selected festival artists via their own words and images, providing nuance
and context for their creative output. This year Automat at Antimatter will offer films
by Magdalena Bermudez, Laura Ohio, Chanasorn Chakitiporn, Charles Coderre,
Arjuna Keshvani-Ham, Marisa Hoika, Rachel Nakawatase and Charlie Tweed.

While the post pandemic landscape is still shifting says festival director Todd
Eacrett were excited about the return of filmmaker attendees and in-person
audiences while maintaining
global connectivity and continually building new
audiences through our online programming. The number of participating artists
planning to attend this year"from the region, across Canada or internationally"is
gratifying and as always promises a wealth of exchange and interaction.

The public is invited to engage with attending filmmakers throughout the festival at
introductions, post-screening Q&As and informal gatherings in a thoughtful, intimate
and supportive festival environment.

Public installations by Ghislan Sutherland-Timms, Laura Kraning, Sarah Ballard and
Felix Dierich will be on view at Legacy Gallery, the Window Gallery in Millies Lane
and the transom window and entrance foyer of Deluge Contemporary Art.

Advance tickets for screenings can be purchased on the Antimatter website.
Installations and online programs are free or by donation. The complete schedule
and festival program guide is available in print and online now:

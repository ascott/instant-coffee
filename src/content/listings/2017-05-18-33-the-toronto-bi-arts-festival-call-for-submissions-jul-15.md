Call for submissions for a Bi Arts Zine, to be published in conjunction with the first 
annual Toronto Bi Arts Fesitval, September 22-24, 2017.

Submit original artwork, illustrations, comics, photography, write ups, poetry, essays, 
interviews, personal stories, or any other ideas you might have. Deadline is July 15. 
Send Submissions to biartszine@gmail.com.

The Toronto Bi Arts Festival is a new grassroots, volunteer run, community arts 
festival celebrating bisexual life and culture.

MARKETING YOUR ARTWORK
Saturday, April 30, 2016
1:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Receive advice and tips from artist and marketing consultant Bryce Evans on
making the most of digital media, social media and other promotional tools
to get your work noticed.

$20 RAGA or CARFAC Members / $30 Non-Members

Bryce Evans is an artist, marketing consultant and founder of The One
Project. He specializes in using photography and interactive art to share
insights, create conversation and build community to bring positive social
change to the world. Bryce has a Business Administration diploma, majoring
in Marketing, with over five years experience creating brands and
developing communities on social media. He has been featured as a suggested
user on Instagram twice, gathering a community of over 52,000 people, and
his work has been exhibited across Canada, the United States, as well as
internationally winning multiple awards.

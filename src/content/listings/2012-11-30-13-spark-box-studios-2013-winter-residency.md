Spark Box Studio's 2013 Winter Residency Open Call is now accepting 
applications from national and international artists at all stages of their career. 
Each year we are excited to invite a new group of artists into our space. Here 
artists will be given the time and space to network, research, experiment and 
built their artist practices. Focusing on your art career at a quiet, self-directed 
residency is a great experience and what better place to do it than surrounded 
by the rural Ontario countryside.

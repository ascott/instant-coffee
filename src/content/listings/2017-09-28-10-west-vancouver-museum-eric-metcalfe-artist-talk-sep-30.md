ERIC METCALFE
Gargoyles and Improvisations
Sep 20 - Oct 28
Artist Talk: September 30 at 2 p.m.

Eric Metcalfe is an influential visual and performance artist who has practiced for over 
five decades in a variety of media including performance, film, video, mural painting and 
light projection. Over the last decade he has produced numerous gouache sketches 
with titles such as Trio, Furthermore and Winged Rhino corresponding to his lifelong 
interest in jazz, popular art forms (e.g. movies, comics) and art history. These works are 
defined by graphic narrative, film story boards and sustained improvisational jazz 
rhythms. Resembling dazzle camouflage deployed on ships during the First World War, 
Metcalfes use of pattern impart movement and animation to otherwise static forms. 
Gargoyles and Improvisations draws reference to Metcalfes his early practice when he 
performed with leopard spotted saxophones as part of the Leopard Realty project for 
which he is well known.

In 2006 Metcalfe was awarded the Audain Prize for Lifetime Achievement in the Visual 
Arts, in 2008 he received the Governor Generals Award in Visual and Media Arts and in 
2015 an Honorary Doctorate from Emily Carr University of Art + Design. His works have 
been exhibited and collected at numerous local, national and international institutions. 
He is co-founder of the artist run centre the Western Front.

Eric Metcalfe's presentation, Materials and Domesticity, Saturday, September 30 will 
provide context for his perceptive/intuitive art making practice.

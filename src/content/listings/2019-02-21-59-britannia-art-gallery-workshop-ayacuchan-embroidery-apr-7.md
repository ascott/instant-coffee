Workshop: Sunday, April 7, 1-4 p.m.
Ayacuchan Embroidery for Beginners

Fee: $10/per participant
Registration Code: 207871 register at Britannia Information Services
Building in person or by phone at: 604.718.5800.
address: 1661 Napier St. Vancouver
Age Limit:14+ .
Max participants: 10
Location: Rink Mezzanine Room
Facilitator: Valeria Valdeiglesia

This type of embroidery comes from a specific part of Peru, called Ayacucho.
Its a millenary craft that has been done between women, passing the knowledge
along across generations. It uses wool or thread which creates bright
and colorfuldesigns in mostly floral shapes, it is best used in cushion covers,
but it can be applied to almost any surface (denim, dresses, purses, sneakers, etc). It is
easy to learn and to make it a hobby, usually creating new shapes and mixing different
colours while you learn more patterns. The main objective is to relax the user while we 
spread the different traditional techniques of Ayacuchean embroidery, empowering
and uniting women along the way.

The class will last 3 hours and all supplies will be included.
Participants do not require
previous experience.

Facilitator will bring patterns, equipment and materials so
participants can embroider one,
Participants need to bring note taking material.

QOQQOON
PUBLICATION LAUNCH

July 19, 7:00 PM
Wil Aballe Art Projects
1129 E Hastings St
Vancouver

We are pleased to celebrate the launch of QOQQOON"an online research project 
aimed at supporting artists writing in Vancouver. This project aspires to shift artists 
(our) efforts away from what we have termed the supplicative discourse (a system of 
textual production oriented toward institutions, curators, and granting bodies as primary 
readers), and into an argumentative or peer-oriented, discursive community.

This event will include a short introduction to the project by editors Leigh Tennant and 
Steven Cottingham, a selection of free-to-take zines, and a celebration marking efforts 
made by all contributors.

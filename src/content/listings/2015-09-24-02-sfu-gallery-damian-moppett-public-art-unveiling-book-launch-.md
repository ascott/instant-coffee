Damian Moppett: Public Art Unveiling, Book Launch and Artist Talk

SAT, SEP 26 2015 / 3PM
SFU Gallery, Burnaby

Join us in celebrating the permanent installation of Damian Moppett's 
Large Painting and Caryatid Maquette in Studio at Night (Sculpture 
Version), 2012 at SFU's Burnaby campus and the launch of the 
publication The Bells: Damian Moppett. The first in the new SFU 
Galleries Critical Reader Series, the publication includes texts by the 
artist, Hollis Frampton, Sharon Kahanoff and Lisa Robertson. 

The artist will speak to both projects at SFU Gallery before leading 
viewers to the sculpture, located at the SFU Residences Dining Hall.

Damian Moppett is a Vancouver based artist. His work has been 
exhibited at Carleton University Art Gallery, Ottawa; Temple Gallery at 
Tyler School of Art, Philadelphia; Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver; 
National Gallery of Canada, Ottawa; Witte de With, Rotterdam; 
Catriona Jeffries Gallery, Vancouver; Vancouver Art Gallery; The Power 
Plant, Toronto; Rennie Collection, Vancouver; and the Morris and 
Helen Belkin Art Gallery, Vancouver, among others. He has taught at 
Concordia University and Emily Carr University of Art and Design, and 
has been awarded residences through Glenfiddich in Scotland and 
International Residencies Program in London.

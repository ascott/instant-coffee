In conjunction with Publication Studio Vancouver
as part of Vancouver Art Book Fair
Saturday October 15, 2-3pm at Vancouver art Gallery

We are excited to announce the launch of For Machine Use Only produced in
conjunction with Mohammad Salemy's installation at the 11th Gwangju
Biennale 2016 at Vancouver Art Book Fair (hosted at Vancouver Art Gallery
Annex, 750 Hornby Street)

For Machine Use Only expands on the idea of machinic vision, featuring
short texts by a range of thinkers, philosophers and scholars who were
asked to contemplate about the possibilities and limitation of a world
understood and interpreted by algorithmically-driven forms of artificial
intelligence. Our increasing reliance on computation requires us to come up
with new ways of thinking about automation and its relationship to the
politics of knowledge and our knowledge of politics.

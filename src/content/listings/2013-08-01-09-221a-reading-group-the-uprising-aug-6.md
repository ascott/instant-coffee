Reading Group 
The Uprising: On Poetry and Finance 
by Franco "Bifo" Berardi 
August 6, 13, 20, 27 7:30pm 

Beginning in August, 221A will host a weekly reading group 
focused on Franco "Bifo" Berardi's The Uprising: On Poetry and 
Finance. Berardi's book, written in the wake of Occupy Wall 
Street and the Arab Spring, seeks to offer methods for thinking 
outside of what Bifo calls semiocapitalism, using poetry as a 
metaphor for resistance.

A limited number of copies of Bifo's book will be available for free 
to those who sign up for the group. Please sign up by email to 
Bopha Chhay at bchhay@221a.ca. Meetings will take place 
Tuesday evenings, beginning August 6th at 7:30. The reading 
group comprises part of Brady Cranfield and Jamie Hilder's 
project, Due To Injuries..., opening at 221A on September 13th, 
which includes a public lecture by Bifo on September 14th co-
presented by the Vancity Office for Community Engagement at 
Simon Fraser University's Woodwards Campus.

A member of the Autonomia political movement in Italy in the 
1970s and one of the founders of the notorious pirate station 
Radio Alice, Berardi has for decades maintained an active 
engagement with the concept of work under global capitalism, 
focusing on the fraught interrelationship between affect and 
media technologies. His recent books include The Uprising: On 
Poetry and Finance(Semiotext(e) 2012), After The Future (AK 
Press, 2011), and The Soul at Work: From Alienation to 
Autonomy (Semiotext(e) 2009).

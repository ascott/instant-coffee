The Beaumont Studios is pleased to introduce emerging contemporary artist 
Patricia Sparks.
"DUENDE" presented by The Beaumont Studios | Curated by Kirsten Larsen
Exhibition Space: The Grand Hall at the Beaumont Studios 
Show: September 4th - 25th
Opening Reception: September 4th | 6 - 10pm
Duende::
n. The mysterious power of art to deeply move a person.
Join us as we escape into the magical world of Patricia Sparks, a self taught 
artist based in Vancouver, on the un ceded ancestral lands of the the Squamish, 
Musqueum and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations.
Duende is a vibrant body of mixed media art that celebrates the magic of 
creativity, the sensuality of women and the joy of life.
PATRICIA: Patricia Sparks was born and raised in Halifax, where she started her 
painting journey at age 11, inspired by the beautiful Nova Scotia landscape.
As an adult she has always been drawn to colour and art in many forms, leading 
to successful careers as a make up artist, photographer as well as in nail artistry.
As a mother to three beautiful children of colour, Patricia has always had strong 
ties to the black community and its culture which has acted as a catalyst for her 
work.
Patricia finds her muse in the strength, beauty, power and resilience of the 
women and hopes this comes through
in her work.

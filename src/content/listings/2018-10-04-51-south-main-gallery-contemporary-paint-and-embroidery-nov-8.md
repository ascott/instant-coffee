Contemporary Paint and Embroidery with Katy Biele

Join Chilean artist Katy Biele on Thursday, November 8th, for a Contemporary Paint and 
Embroidery Workshop. You will learn how to paint with acrylics and watercolor to make a 
background for your canvas fabric " and then stitch in it, with Katy's freestyle way. You 
don't need to have any experience, and materials are included. You will create a 
complete mixed media art piece. 

Start date: Thursday, November 8, 2018, from 6:00 pm to 9:00 pm 
Location: South Main Gallery
Supplies: All materials included
Cost: $105
Contact: For more information, and to sign-up, contact studio@katybiele.com

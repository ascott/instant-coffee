Dedicated Drawing Club

Nicola Tibbetts and Toni Latour

DDC was formed in 2011 as an East Van Collective.
Meeting on a regular basis over a three-year period,
the artists came together to produce drawings
that act as homages and dedications to the people
in their lives. This exhibiton features the original
drawings along with a book documenting the work,
prints and DDC products for sale.

The Premiere Exhibition will take place at the
Capilano University Library
2055 Purcell Way, North Vancouver

April 12-June 12, 2014
Opening Reception: Friday April 11, 6:30

DDC would like to thank Capilano U for their 
support of this project.

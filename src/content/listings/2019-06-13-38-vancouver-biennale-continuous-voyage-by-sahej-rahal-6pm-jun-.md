Vancouver Biennale 
Continuous Voyage by Sahej Rahal
Presented as part of Vancouver Biennale, Re-IMAGE-ning Public Space 2018-2020

Opening and performance on June 20th 2019, at 6pm

2nd floor, Sun Wah Centre (268 Keefer St, Vancouver, BC)
Exhibition runs June 20th until June 29th 2019

Presented by the Vancouver Biennale, Continuous Voyage is an exhibition by international artist 
Sahej Rahal. Combining sculpture and AI video, this new body of work culls through the citys 
flotsam and jetsam to see what mythologies rise to the surface. A collaborative performance 
with Arts Umbrella's PReP program and the School for the Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser 
University on June 20th at 6pm will expand these mythologies through embodied enactment. 

Sahej Rahal (b. 1988, Mumbai) is currently an artist-in-residence at the Vancouver Biennale and 
a recipient of multiple awards, including Forbes Award for Debut Solo Show (2014), Henry 
Moore Fellowship (2017), and Sher-gil Sundaram Arts Award (2019). He is also a recipient of 
the prestigious Akadamie Schloss Solitude fellowship and has shown in major international 
exhibitions such as the Liverpool Biennial (2016) and Kochi Muziris Biennial (2014).

For the first time in five years, we would like to invite you to our Ceramic Auction and 
Gala at the cafe area of Emily Carr University of Art & Design (ECU) on Granville Island, 
on February 24th from 7 pm to 9:30 pm. Join us for a great evening of food, music and 
of course exciting bidding wars. Emily Carr alumni and artist David Robinson will be the 
host of a live auction beginning at 8 pm sharp. 

The auction features ceramic work by local artists, alumni and current students from the 
ECUAD ceramics program. It will be a great opportunity to access art works from 
established and emerging Vancouver artists. All proceeds raised will go towards 
educational fieldtrips for ECUAD Ceramics based students as well as public lectures by 
visiting artists. 

Tickets are $18, or $15 with a valid student ID, and will include a free mug upon 
admission, as supplies last. 

For more information, or if you are interested in donating work, please email 
ncecabound@gmail.com.

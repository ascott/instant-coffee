The call is open, open, open for interplay_2024: https://bit.ly/call_interplay_2024

interplay_ presents live online artworx that promote, challenge and experiment with
collaborative and/or multidisciplinary performance. We're one of the only festivals in the
world dedicated to live online performance and we aim to reach audiences globally.

The call is open to independent artists of all abilities residing in so-called Canada, from
all disciplines, at any career stage, who are creating for a live online performance
context:
https://bit.ly/call_interplay_2024

interplay_2024 takes place online Dec 12, 13, 14 + 15. **New this year**, we're taking
proposals to present via the digital platform of your choosing and works can be of any
duration.

Co-curators khattieQ, Sammy Chien and Deanna Peters will program 4"6
artists/groups. Selected artists receive an artist fee of $1,200. Other resources include
promotion, production support, documentation, letters of support and FREE access to
the Moberly Arts and Cultural Centre for rehearsals.

Please submit your application using this link: https://bit.ly/call_interplay_2024

Deadline Mar 15, 11:59pm. Notifications Mar 25.

For more context, please see a video recap of interplay_2023:
https://vimeo.com/878502092 and visit our website and scroll down for past artists and
the forms they were working in: https://mutablesubject.ca/interplay_/

We're grateful for support from the City of Vancouver via the plastic orchid factory and
the Moberly Arts and Cultural Centre.

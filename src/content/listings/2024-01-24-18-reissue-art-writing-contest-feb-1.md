The ReIssue Art Writing Contest is an annual art writing contest open to emerging art
writers enrolled in our partnering institutions: SFU, UBC, ECU, and Langara College "
eligible students are invited to submit their writing for a cash prize of $1500, and
publication of the winning piece online and in our upcoming Year 3 Anthology.

The call is open to students in undergraduate
and graduate programs, in and beyond
the art department. Submissions should be 3000 words maximum, and fit within
ReIssues categories of Review, Interview, Moving Image, Essay, or Creative. Upon
selection, the successful applicant will work with ReIssue Editor Casey Wei to polish up
the piece for publication following Reissues style guidelines (available as a PDF on our
About page).

Submit to: editor@reissue.pub

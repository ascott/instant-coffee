Friday Dec 5th from 6-10pm, with cocktails and winter bbq
Saturday Dec 6th from 11am-5pm
toque2014.tumblr.com

TOQUE is back! Please join us for Western Front's annual fundraiser and
craft fair on Dec 5th and 6th. The event showcases a carefully curated
selection of 24 artists, designers and artisans with everything from books,
ceramics and jewelry, to artisanal treats, skincare and fashion. Also
available is the annual limited-edition tea towel designed this year by
artist Casey Wei.

TOQUE coincides with our Holiday Membership Drive, so join/renew at the
event for perks and prizes and help us reach our goal of 200 members.

Admission is by donation. Proceeds support Western Front’s year-round art
and new music programs.

Drawing Attributes
Dec 15th - Jan 15th 2017 
Opening Reception: Thursday Dec 15th, 6-9pm

"Drawing Attributes"
CSA Space presents "Drawing Attributes" from December 15, 2016 until January 
15, 2017.

There will be an opening reception December 15, 2016 from 6 - 9pm

At the invitation of CSA, the "See you next Thursday" collective behind the 
Schneiderei exhibition space in Vienna Austria will create a collaborative 
exhibition with works that deliberately provoke the security of their individual 
practices. Artists in the project include Abdul Sharif Baruwa, Johann Groebner, 
Lia Karl, Bina Klingler, Vasco Costa, Fernando Mesquita, Wolfgang Obermair, 
Lisa Ruyter, and a number of other collaborators.

CSA Space, 2414 Main St, Vancouver, BC
See Pulpfiction Books (2422 Main St.) for admission during regular business

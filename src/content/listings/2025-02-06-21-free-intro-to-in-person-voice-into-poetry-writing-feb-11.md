"Its true. My voice is " Ive been told on numerous occasions " unlikely. Childlike,
almost. Not seductive. Informal The older I get, the more my voice seems to
disagree with what people perceive of me." " Durga Chew-Bose, from Too Much
Not the Mood

In this 7-week in-person series you'll go on a compassionate exploration of your
spoken and written voice, using poetry as your tool and companion. There are 6
spaces available.

Every week, we'll read poetry from a diverse collection of authors writing on the
same themes as us (different theme related to voice each week) and notice how the
author's relationship to their voice sheds light on our unique relationship to our own.
Next, we'll read these poems out loud. As you read out loud, how comfortable are
you with taking up space? How comfortable are you with being playful, or speaking
with boldness and confidence without shyly retreating into yourself ? As we speak
these poems out loud, we'll gently challenge ourselves to expand the expressive
capacity of our voices in a way that feels good. These explorations of the spoken
voice will be guided.

After that, it's time to take everything you've been reflecting on throughout the
session, and write a poem that gives voice to your experience. This segment is really
about learning to approach the blank page with bravery and curiousity, and about
learning to treat your creativity with respect by giving it space and attention. No
experience with poetry writing is necessary to participate.

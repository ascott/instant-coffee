Nominations Open for the 2014 City of Vancouver Mayor's Arts 
Awards
The City of Vancouver Mayor's Arts Awards recognize established 
and emerging artists in a wide array of disciplines that make up our 
creative sector - from literary to culinary and visual to performing arts, 
as well as five unique awards for contributions to our community.
Honourees in the ten artistic categories are chosen by a jury of peers, 
usually consisting of the three previous years' recipients, for having 
made a significant contribution to their respective disciplines, 
achieving or contributing to artistic excellence, and for showing 
leadership and support for Vancouver's cultural community over the 
course of their careers.
Each arts category Honouree selects an Emerging Artist in their 
discipline who represents the promise of the next generation.
Honourees for Business Support, Philanthropy, and Volunteerism are 
chosen by juries consisting of business and cultural community 
members active in those fields.
Honourees in the Arts Board Member of the Year and Lifetime 
Achievement categories are selected by the Board of Directors of the 
Society to Bridge Arts & Community.
The selection in each category is made from nominations submitted 
by individual jurors and solicited from the community at large through 
an online nominations process.
Artist and Emerging Artist Honourees and Lifetime Achievement 
recipients receive a cash award. Special non-cash awards are made 
for contributions through Volunteerism, Philanthropy, Business 
Support, and the Arts Board Member of the Year.
The 2014 Mayor's Arts Awards will be presented the evening of 
Tuesday, October 7 at the Roundhouse Community Arts & 
Recreation Centre.
Deadline for Nominations: 12 noon on Friday, May 9.
Submit a Nomination: Details and the nomination form can be found 
online at: 
http://fluidsurveys.com/s/2014mayorsartsawardsnominations/
Please note that each nomination requires two nominators.

Please join me Jan 30, 4pm PST. REGISTER: bit.ly/WILATCILAY-register

"When I look at the camera, I'm looking at you" is a live online performance created
specifically for Zoom meeting. Choreographed and performed by Deanna
Peters/Mutable Subject with music by Ahmed Khalil, #WILATCILAY places sensation at
the forefront of virtual exchange. As meta-narrative feeds into moving images, and skin
becomes pixels, our boundaries extend.

Part dance performance, part quarantine journal, I use Zoom's chat
function to share
written context. Meeting participants are invited to type responses and participate in
short choreographic proposals/games. Please join me in this continued exploration, of
grasping towards unknown co-futures:

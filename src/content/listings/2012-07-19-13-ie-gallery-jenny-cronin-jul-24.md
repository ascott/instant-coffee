July Resident � Jenny Cronin

Jenny Cronin
July 1st - July 27th
Reception: Tuesday, July 24th, 6-9pm

Soaring images showcase an idealized and romantic representation of
landscape. Through her playful approach, Jenny Cronin revisits geographic
exploration and adventure as a science, questioning contemporary systems in
defining place.

Jenny Cronin is an exhibiting artist and graduate student at Emily Carr University
in British Columbia. She completed a B.A in Geography from the University of
Victoria in 2004, and a B.F.A in Photography from Ryerson University in 2011.

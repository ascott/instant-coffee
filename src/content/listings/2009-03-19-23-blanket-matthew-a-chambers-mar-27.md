...Chambers' exhibitions confront the viewer not only with individual
works relating to a grand project of self-knowledge within the context
of 21st century American cultural corruption and tradition, but with a
general renewed sense of the possibilities of the self's articulation
in and by art in this world. There is no high-art pedestal in
Chambers. His art recharges art's ability to cross out of the
hyper-real of theoretical discourse and land directly in the blood,
sweat and tears of the human real.

Matthew Chambers is exemplary of a generation that will reclaim the structures
of the art world in the interest of a cultural future outside of the
old power dialectics of high/low, insider/outsider that generate only
generic repetition at the expense of improvisation and discovery.
Chambers' work reminds us that the future and hope come out of our
trials via art and its real-world ability to contain and transform our
dreams and nightmares.

This is the second exhibition of Chambers at Blanket.

Re-Photographing My Mother | JAN 31

WORK IN PROGRESS: RE-PHOTOGRAPHING MY MOTHER
Sydney Southam

OPEN HOUSE - 6-8 pm Jan 31
545 N Slocan, Burrardview Fieldhouse

For the third month of our WORKS IN PROGRESS series, Iris Film Collective member
Sydney Southam will be experimenting with re-photographing the film and television
archive of her mother Pia Shandel, a local actress, television and radio personality who
was active from the 1970s until the mid 2000s.

By experimenting with techniques of re-photography and manipulating the images
during the developing process, Sydney hopes to gain a new understanding of this public
archive, which holds great weight for the artist personally. This month of technical
exploration is the very beginning of a longer experimental documentary work that has
been percolating in the mind of the artist for some time. By attending this free event, the
audience will get a unique
opportunity to witness the beginning stages of creation!

Saturday January 31 6-8 pm, Sydney will host an informal open house where viewers
can see what she has been working on and ask any questions they might have about
the work.

Iris Film Collectives WORK IN PROGRESS series is a month-long residency at the
Burrard View Fieldhouse featuring a different Iris Film Collective member each month
who will be using the space to work on their individual projects. The public is invited to
pop by while they are working; as well, some days/evenings in the month will be
devoted to public events. This is a great opportunity to meet the artists and see what
they are up to, as well as to visit the Fieldhouse and check out our setup!

Sydney Southam is a visual artist, filmmaker, performance artist, and professional pole
dancer. She often works with archival 16mm film, exploring themes of nostalgia, death,
memory, and identity. Sydney is one of the founding members of Vancouver-based Iris
Film
Collective, and the curator of the potluck dinner and artist talk series Special
Sunday Supper. Her films and artwork have shown across Canada, Europe and Asia.
She graduated from Central Saint Martins with a BA Fine Art First Class Honours in
2011 and from the University of Toronto with a BA in English, Philosophy and Cinema
Studies in 2007.

Iris Film Collective respectfully acknowledge that we sit on unceded, traditional and
ancestral Musqueam, Squamish,and Tsleil-Waututh territories.

2024 Engaging Artists in Community Program, Request for Proposals (RFP)

The Richmond Public Art Program seeks an artist or artist team with a socially-oriented
and civic-minded practice for a unique community-engaged artist opportunity working
with Dixon Elementary School in partnership with City of Richmond Engineering.
Professional artists are invited to imagine innovative ways in which art can act as a
catalyst to foster individual creative expression, build community and
address topics of
relevance working within communities.

Opportunity: $14,000 | Dixon Elementary / CoR Engineering

Eligibility: Artists residing in British Columbia

Deadline: October 3, 2023

Duration: January " December 2024

For more information and to apply:
https://www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities.htm

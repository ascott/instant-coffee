Public Private Cubic Couple
On being two places at once and simultaneously revealing both public and private persona

All are invited to attend the unveiling of a revolutionary photo-cubic public artwork transforming 
Gastown Vancouver on Saturday, June 27, 3:00 to 6:00 PM at Cambie Annex Front, Cordova 
Street, east of Cambie Street.

Gale force winds of change are threatening to blast away the oppressively stale air of Vancouver 
culture and are generating a massive new wave tsunami to sweep away the intertidal detritus of 
Vancouver School stuffed-shirt academy, and the stagnation of curatorial tunnel vision. 
Multiplicity, hyper-dimensionality, cultural hybridization, achingly vivid chromatics, in-your-face 
irreverence, and even truth and beauty have returned with teeth bared, and love has finally 
conquered all.

Public Private Cubic Couple is a revolutionary photo-based conceptual art program; a large-scale,
kinetic photo-cubic, outdoor public artwork, smashing through the confines of private studio 
practice, institutionalized art, ivory tower academy and bureaucratic civic architorture, thereby
taking direct action to provide immediate reflection, gorgeous beauty and humorous joy for the 
culturally impoverished masses. A dual site, larger-than-life photo-cubic double portrait tableaux 
vivant at a scale of 1 to 1 extending to 4, 5 and 6 dimensional hyperspace. Two people, guises, 
iconic locations, shifting time frames, cameras, photographers, and print formats combined into 
one hyper-dimensional entirety.

“The very definition of the real has become: that of which it is possible to give an equivalent 
reproduction…The real is not only what can be reproduced, but that which is always already 
reproduced: that is, the hyperreal…which is entirely in simulation.” “It’s a disruption in the space-
time continuum.”

All are invited to attend public unveiling of this revolutionary public art project. Refreshments will 
be served. Guns, knives, needles, pipes and tazers forbidden on-site, and must be checked-in 
with security. Bring your chequebooks. Mastercard accepted. ATM on site.

Who: Vancouver-based visual artist and public art producer Andrew Owen, aka: Andrew01

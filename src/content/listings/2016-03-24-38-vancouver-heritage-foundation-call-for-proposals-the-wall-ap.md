Vancouver Heritage Foundation is accepting proposals for The WALL 
public art initiative. The selected artist will relate their work to the theme 
of Vancouver's built environment. The work will be installed for 
approximately one year at the CBC Plaza. More information can be 
found at vancouverheritagefoundation.org/The-Wall.

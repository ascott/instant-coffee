Artist Opportunity
Community Digital Art Project
Vancouver Public Library - ncamat ct Strathcona branch
Budget: $8,000 (combined artist fee and materials expense)
Submission Deadline: March 29, 2016

The Vancouver Public Library is seeking an artist to work with 
community members to create a digital artwork for the ncamat ct 
Strathcona branch. The selected community artist or artists will use the 
process of creating art as a means of connecting community members 
to the new branch, and will use digital and artistic skills and tools to 
encourage creativity and imagination of community members. The final 
art piece will be displayed prominently by being shown on the digital 
display screen planned for the ncamat ct Strathcona branch and will 
inspire other community members to use the ncamat ct Strathcona 
digital creation space to create their own art.

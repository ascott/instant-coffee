Artist-in-Residence Virtual Launch | 7PM | FEB 11
Branscombe House 2021 Artist-in-Residence Virtual Launch

Thursday, February 11
7:00-8:30pm
Online via Zoom

Environmentalists and art-lovers alike are invited to meet Rachel Rozanski, the sixth
annual Branscombe House Artist-in-Residence at this virtual event. Rozanski will
describe her creative practice and plans for her upcoming residency, which will include
an exploration of Richmond's islands by water. The artist will examine the evolution of
these landforms, reshaped many times over, including the unique ecosystems that now
exist on them. Rozanskis research-based practice focuses on land change, including
recent projects documenting permafrost degradation in the Arctic using video, drawing
and contact imaging. The artist is seeking collaboration from community members that
have personal connections or knowledge of the area. The virtual talk will be followed
by a Q&A session. RSVP in advance to culture@richmond.ca.

The Branscombe House Artist Residency takes place in a restored Edwardian-style
house in the residential area of historic Steveston. Branscombe House is located at
4900 Steveston Highway and is one of the earliest homes built in the area. Details
about the current residency can be found at

www.richmond.ca/branscomberesidency

Richmond Art Gallery

Date: May 1 - Jun 13, 2010

Title: In Transition: New Art from India
Artists: Shilpa Gupta, Reena Kallat, TV Santhosh, Sudarshan Shetty, Thukral
& Tagra and Hema Upadhyay
Opening Reception: Saturday, May 1 from 1:00 to 3:00pm. Hema Upadhyay will
be in attendance.

In collaboration with the Vancouver Biennale the Richmond Art Gallery will
present In Transition: New Art from India. India is experiencing a period
of remarkable growth and transformation and its artists (like contemporary
artists everywhere) are responding to these changes. With an eye on its
past and a view to the future, these artists are examining the social,
political, economic and religious implications of becoming a major world
economy. The exhibition will feature installation-based work by six of
India's most recognized contemporary artists: Shilpa Gupta, Reena Kallat,
TV Santhosh, Sudarshan Shetty, artist collective Thukral & Tagra, and Hema
Upadhyay.The opening reception will take place Saturday, May 1 from 1:00 to
3:00pm.

Talk on Thursday June 3, 2010 at 7:30pm
Contemporary Art in India: At Home and in the World
Keith Wallace discusses contemporary art in India today

Related Events; a month long performance work at the Aberdeen Centre by
Hema Upadhyay which will be unveiled the week of May 24 to June 2nd; the
Lulu Series lectures in Richmond with Biennale sponsored artist, Hema
Upadhyay from Mumbai, India Dream a Wish, Wish a Dream on May 20 at 7pm in
Council Chambers.

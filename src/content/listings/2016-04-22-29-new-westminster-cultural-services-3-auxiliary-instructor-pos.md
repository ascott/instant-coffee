The City of New Westminster Cultural Services has recently posted 
three auxiliary instructor positions for Summer 2016 and Fall/Winter 
2016. These positions are currently open for application, with a starting 
rate of $31.48/hr (2015 CUPE local 387 rates) + 12% in lieu of all 
benefits.

Anvil Centre is the showpiece civic facility located in the heart of the 
downtown New Westminster. Anvil Centre encompasses 84,000-
square feet of interior space and includes: conference and meeting 
facilities, theatre, multi-purpose rooms, multi-purpose art studios, art 
gallery, the City's Museum and Archives, and much more. Details and 
links on this exciting new facility can be found at www.anvilcentre.ca

The Anvil Centre cultural studios are top of the line professional studio 
spaces that offer quality setting for visual, performing, applied, sound 
and new media arts. The Centre also offers professionally outfitted, 
acoustically insulated music practice rooms.

The courses will take place in the cultural studios and instructors will 
have the ability to connect their classes to the overall programming of 
the Centre (New Media Gallery, Community Art Space, Museums and 
Archives). See listings below. For more information, please contact:

Kristina Fiedrich, Arts Programmer

Position Vacancy: Dance Instructor (Summer 2016) - Anvil Centre
We are looking for a dynamic dance instructor to join our faculty and 
help develop our performing arts program. For the Summer 2016 
Program session, you will be responsible for creating the curriculum 
and implementing dance classes for preschool, children and youth 
within our current programs Contemporary Dance and Movers & 
Shakers. Some specific duties will include developing curriculum in line 
with the ongoing programming and upholding the high quality and 
professionalism of the Anvil Centre. This position may lead to further 
opportunities to design and develop dance and movement-based 
programming for the Fall/Winter 2016 programs.
Please apply by emailing your resume and cover letter to 
kfiedrich@newwestcity.ca quoting competition #16-36, by May 13, 
2016.

Position Vacancy: Drawing Instructor - Anvil Centre
We are looking for an experienced drawing instructor to join our faculty 
and help develop a dynamic life drawing program. Subjects could 
include anatomical drawing as well as life drawing with draped and 
undraped models. You will design and implement drawing classes 
appropriate for teens, young adults, adults and seniors. Some specific 
duties will include developing core curriculum in line with the ongoing 
programming and upholding the high quality and professionalism of the 
Anvil Centre.
Please apply by emailing your resume and cover letter to 
kfiedrich@newwestcity.ca quoting competition #16-37, by May 27, 
2016.

Position Vacancy: Dance Instructor (Fall / Winter 2016) - Anvil Centre
We are looking for a dynamic dance instructor to join our faculty and 
help develop our performing arts program. You will design and 
implement dance classes for preschool, children and youth, and/or 
teens, adults and seniors, as well as modify and/or maintain current 
programs such as Contemporary Dance and Movers & Shakers. Some 
specific duties will include developing curriculum in line with the 
ongoing programming and upholding the high quality and 
professionalism of the Anvil Centre. In your resume, please highlight 
your training, teaching methods and any classes you have developed.
Please apply by emailing your resume and cover letter to 
kfiedrich@newwestcity.ca quoting competition #16-38, by June 10, 
2016.

Requirements for all instructor positions include:
Completion of a post secondary degree, diploma or equivalent in the 
relevant media/practice.
Minimum 3 years of previous teaching experience.
Considerable knowledge of the principles, practices and objectives of 
contemporary art education in community recreation facilities.
Sound knowledge of non-formal teaching methods and group 
leadership techniques.
Excellent supervisory and organizational skills.
Must be able to successfully pass and maintain a criminal and police 
record check.
Please note that the hours of work are dependent on program 
registration and the successful candidate will have the ability to work 
weekdays, evenings and/or weekends.

We thank all applicants in advance; however, only those selected for 
an interview will be contacted.
The City of New Westminster is committed to employment equity.
We welcome diversity and encourage applications from all qualified 
individuals.

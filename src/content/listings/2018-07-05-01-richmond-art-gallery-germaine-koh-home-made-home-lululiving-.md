Germaine Koh: Home Made Home
June 17, 2018 - August 26, 2018

Visit Lululiving, by Germaine Koh 
Tuesdays and Saturdays from 12:00 - 4:00 p.m. until August 25.

As part of the exhibition, Home Made Home currently on display at Richmond Art Gallery, 
Germaine Kohs newest construction, Home Made Home: Lululiving, a 160 square foot tiny 
house is open to the public. The house is located on the lawn between the Richmond 
Cultural Centre and the Seniors Centre. A fully functioning small home, it becomes a 
platform for public dialogue on the locally and globally relevant issues of housing and 
accessibility.

Lululiving will be open every Tuesday and Saturday from 12:00 - 4:00 p.m. until August 25. 
(Closed on Holidays)

The Richmond Art Gallery received a Canada Council project grant in support of the 
exhibition. The Richmond Art Gallery gratefully acknowledges the on-going support of the 
City of Richmond, Richmond Art Gallery Association, the BC Arts Council, and the Province 
of British Columbia. Home Made Home: Lululiving is presented in partnership with 
Richmond Public Art Program.

THIS Gallery Presents Total Residuum, a solo exhibition of works on paper by Marc
Bell.

Vancouver, BC, May 23 - May 31, 2025 " THIS Gallery is thrilled to be hosting a
solo exhibition by Vancouver-based author and cartoonist, Marc Bell.

THIS________ presents an exhibition of works by Marc Bell, Total Residuum, from
May 23rd through June 7th, 2025. For this solo exhibition, Bell has created over
twenty new works that vary in medium and dimensions, from several tiny 4 x 3
mixed media collages to a large, detailed 30 x 40 ink and watercolour drawing.
These works contain a fully formed visual world of abstracted characters,
landscapes, and wordplay that imply narrative as quickly as they distract from it.

One of the works in the exhibition, Up With Art!!!/ Organized Cacophony (Systems),
2025, has a compartmentalized composition teeming with Bell's tightly arranged
blocks of colour and figures/shapes amidst text rife with free associations such as
Marc, I love these jpegs you sent me! (attributed to a Mr. David Zwirner here), and
one cant help but leave the planet like a worn ham. To create this (and several
other works in the show) Bell worked with many small scraps of paper and loose
drawings from his collections and distilled them into compositions, adding to and
negating from these fragments in a process he likes to call The War With Paper.

There will be an opening reception on Saturday, May 24 from noon - 4 PM. Note:
This is the final exhibition at the gallery's Chinatown location.

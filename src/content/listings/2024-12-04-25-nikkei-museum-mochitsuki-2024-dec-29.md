Nikkei Mochitsuki 2024
Celebrate a Year-End Japanese Tradition

Come feel Japan at Nikkei Mochitsuki 2024! This annual event invites
the
community to enjoy Japanese traditions, flavours, and entertainment of
mochitsuki (rice pounding).

Date: Sunday, December 29, 2024
Time: 11:00 AM - 3:00 PM
Location: Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre, 6688 Southoaks Crescent,
Burnaby.
Admission: Free

Event Highlights:

Fresh and Frozen Mochi for Sale: Indulge in the soft, chewy goodness of freshly
made mochi or take home a bag of frozen mochi to enjoy
later.

Mochi Pounding Demonstration: Witness the rhythmic pounding of steamed rice into
mochi, a captivating traditional process.

Pound Your Own Mochi: Roll up your sleeves and try your hand at mochi pounding
under the guidance of experienced practitioners, members of the Vancouver
Japanese Gardeners Association. Popular for both adults and children.

Stage Performances: Enjoy a variety of cultural performances, including Japanese
taiko drumming, dance, and martial arts demonstrations.

Mochi Sales:

Fresh Mochi (3 pieces): $5

Frozen Mochi (1 lb.): $10

Fresh mochi can be
enjoyed with:

Soy sauce and seaweed

Grated daikon radish and soy sauce

Kinako (roasted soybean powder) and sugar

Frozen mochi can be grilled, microwaved, or added to soups.

Entertainment Schedule

11:00 AM: Chibi Taiko

12:30 PM: Satsuki-Kai Dance

1:30 PM: Judo Demonstration

2:30 PM: Vancouver Okinawa Taiko

This event is presented in collaboration with NNMCC, the Vancouver Japanese
Gardeners Association
and NNMCC Auxiliary, with the generous support of the
Province of British Columbia.

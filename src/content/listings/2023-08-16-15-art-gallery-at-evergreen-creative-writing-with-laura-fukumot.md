Call and Response: Creative Writing with Laura Fukumoto
Art Gallery at Evergreen
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam, BC
Saturday, August 26 | 2:30 - 4:30pm

Have you ever wanted to write a poem? Learn the writers craft in this beginner-
friendly workshop. Join poet and multidisciplinary artist Laura Fukumotofor a
creative writing workshop at the Art Gallery at Evergreen. We will respond to
theEmerging Creators Incubator, a showcase of works bysix local emerging artists.
Students of creative writing and seasoned authors alike are encouraged to explore
the gallery pieces in a
new and dynamic way.

Free and wheelchair accessible event. Registration recommended. 30 spots
available.
Register here: https://evergreenculturalcentre.my.salesforce-
sites.com/ticket/#/events/a0SHp00000ewbQVMAY

About the artist:
Laura Fukumoto settled in so-called Vancouver more than a decade ago from the
Toronto area, and is committed to loving and living in this place. She gets paid to talk
a lot, and you may have heard her hosting the annual Powell Street Festivals annual
Sumo Wrestling tournament, or moderating a panel discussion about Japanese
Canadian Heritage at the Museum of Vancouver earlier this year. Laura performs
poetry (Vines Festival, Verses Festival, the Evergreen Rehearsal Sessions), has
published articles with the Federation of BC Writers Wordworks Magazine the
Vancouver Pride Mag. BFA Theatre Production from UBC and The Writers Studio,
SFU.

About the showcase:
"Echoes of a Circle'" is a showcase featuring artwork by six emerging
multidisciplinary artists
participating in Evergreen Cultural Centres (ECC) Emerging
Creators Incubator, featuring Pegah Ahmadi, Jordanna George, Alia Hijaab, Juan
Imperial, Jullianna Oke and Samantha Walters.From May to July 2023, the artists
came together to learn, dedicate time to their creative practices and form community.
Bridging performing and visual arts, the works in "Echoes of a Circle" reflect the
artists journey through preservation and evolution.

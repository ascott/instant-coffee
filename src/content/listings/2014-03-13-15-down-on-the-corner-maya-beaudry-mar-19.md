Free Style Vision Boarding

Visualization is an important tool for achieving your dreams. 

Though the process begins in the mind, creating a physical presence for your 
ideas can be an empowering and uplifting experience. 

Begin by searching for things you find inspiring. Images are important, but 
often simply refer to someone else's vision. Scraps or samples of real objects 
and materials can serve as concrete fragments of the intangible thing for 
which you're searching.

Assemble these things in whatever way feels right to you. Place an object 
next to an image, take note of how it makes you feel, and try a different 
combination. You will know when the combination is right. 

Pay attention to the relationship between objects and their positions. Think 
about the psychology at play in your decisions. 

Embrace metaphor, and don't worry about cliches. Remember, this Free Style 
Vision Board is for you! Though there's nothing wrong with showing it to your 
friends or family, don't expect them to understand or appreciate the 
complexity of what you've created. 

Once you've invented your own language, you'll be able to reveal secrets to 
yourself you may not have known you were keeping.

Happy Boarding!

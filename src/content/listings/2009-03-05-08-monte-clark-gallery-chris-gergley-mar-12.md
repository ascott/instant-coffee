MONTE CLARK GALLERY

Chris Gergley
"Color Work"

March 12 - April 4th 2009

Opening reception
Thursday, March 12th, 6-8 pm

Images with a colored past / Black bodies and color models / Imaginary
color for standard observers / Large format dry plates and medium
sized dry goods

The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to announce the solo exhibition
Color Work by Vancouver-based artist Chris Gergley.

To follow the success of his exhibition "Copy Work" at the
Contemporary Art Gallery Vancouver (2007), Gergley's new exhibition
Color Work finds the artist pitting the irrational knowledge of color
against the colorless world of work and reason, the result, a wholly
unique and remarkable display emblematic of Gergley's insightful
technique.

Chris Gergley was born in Regina, Saskatchewan. He graduated from the
Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design in 1996.

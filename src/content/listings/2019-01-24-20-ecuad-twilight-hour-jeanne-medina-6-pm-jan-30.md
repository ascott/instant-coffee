TALK I Twilight Hour 
Jeanne Medina

Wednesday, January 30 I 6 pm
Sculpture Studio, D1357 
Jeanne Medina is an artist working between textile, fashion, and performance. She 
received her BFA in Fiber and Material Studies and Post-Baccalaureate in Fashion, 
Body and Garment from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (SAIC), and her MFA 
in Fiber from Cranbrook Academy of Art. She is currently Visiting Assistant Professor in 
Fibers at Oregon College of Art and Craft (OCAC). Her most recent exhibitions include 
solo exhibitions at The Alice gallery in Seattle, WA and the Ancestral Offeringsexhibition 
at Reynolds Gallery in Richmond, VA; group shows include the Remnant 
Accumulationexhibition at Sediment Arts in Richmond, VA; the Discursiveexhibition at 
the Jordan Schnitzer Museum in Eugene, OR; University of Oregon Whitebox Gallery in 
Portland, OR; Form & Concept in Santa Fe, NM; and the Fiber Face 4exhibition in 
Yogyakarta, Indonesia. 

Seating is limited; first come, first served. 
Twilight Hour is a lecture series hosted by the Painting and Sculpture + Expanded 
Practices programs in the Audain Faculty of Art.

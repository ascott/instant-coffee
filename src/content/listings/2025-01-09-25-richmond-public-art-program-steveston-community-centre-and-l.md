The Richmond Public Art Program is seeking an artist or artist team to propose an
integrated mosaic tile artwork design for the new Steveston Community Centre and
Library in Richmond, BC. The public artwork will be located in the lobby of the facility
and will aim to create a sense of arrival and welcome to the facility. All information
about the project is described in the link below.

Budget: $15,000 CAD (design only fee)

Eligibility Requirements: Open to professional artists and artist teams residing in
British Columbia

Deadline for Submissions: January 27, 2025

Installation: 2026

For more information and to apply: Steveston Community Centre and Library Interior
Artwork

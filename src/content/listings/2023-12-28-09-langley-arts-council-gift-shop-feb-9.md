Call Name: Gift Shop

Organization: Langley Arts Council

Details:
Gift Shop Location
Aldergrove Kinsmen Community Centre (26770 29 Ave, Aldergrove, BC V4W 3B8)

Dates

February 9th - June 27th, 2024

Submission Deadline

January 30th, 2024

Criteria
We are looking for makers of all sorts, textile artists, ceramicists, soapers, card
makers, and more. Applications will be considered on a case-by-case basis. We are
looking for work that is creative, innovative, cohesive, and respectful of other cultures,
and copyright laws.

Each Piece Must Be

An 'Original Artwork' (refer to the Arts Council's definition below)

Original artwork is created at the hand of the artist who is submitting the work. The
creation process must be the effort of a single individual to convey their interpretation
of an image, multiple images, or a subject. Submitting artists are allowed to use
reference photos to inspire their work, but artists must use their design and
composition for the final artwork to be considered original.

Submitted works must not have been created at an art class, lesson, or paint night.

Submission Details

Each Artist/Artisan is invited to submit up to 8 works for consideration for the Gift Shop
through the digital application below. Please note, that the LAC may not accept all 8
works for the Gift Shop.

To apply visit https://www.langleyarts.ca/proposals/gift-shop

Talks at the Vancouver Art Gallery this week

Thursday, September 30, 7pm

Offsite: Heather and Ivan Morison

Location: Vancouver Art Gallery Offsite is located on West Georgia
Street between Thurlow and Bute streets.

Join artist Ivan Morison for a talk and a walk through of their installation.

Heather and Ivan Morison explore architecture as it relates to ideas
of fantasy, escape, shelter and refuge. For Offsite, they have created
a pavilion which hovers between sculpture and architecture. The walls
lean outward towards the street, as if they are peeling away from the
site. The interior structure is raised above the water, propped up by
crossing diagonal supports, and the roof is open to the sky. The walls
are made of heavy timber beams, burnt to a dark charcoal using a
Japanese technique for preserving and protecting wood from the
elements called shou-sugi-ban. The blackened structure evokes a
distopic narrative as if it is built upon the ruins of a desolated
city charred by the scorching sun of an apocalyptic future.

Free.

Saturday, October 2, 1pm

Robert Adams: The Place We Live, A Retrospective Selection of Photographs

Curator's Tour / Joshua Chuang / In the Gallery

Guest curator Joshua Chuang from Yale University Art Gallery lead a
special tour of the exhibition, illuminating Adams' four decades of
photographic work. Eric Paddock, Curator of Photography from Denver
Art Museum, and artist Mark Ruwedel join in for an in-depth
discussion.

Free with Gallery admission.

Saturday, October 2, 3pm

TD NEXT: Song Dong's Waste Not

Artist Talk / Song Dong with Daina Augaitis / In the Gallery

Beijing-based artist Song Dong engages in a conversation with
exhibition curator Daina Augaitis about the memories of the artist's
late mother and her role in the creation of the monumental
installation Waste Not.

Free with Gallery Admission

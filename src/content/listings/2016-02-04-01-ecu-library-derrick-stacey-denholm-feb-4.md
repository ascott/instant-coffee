Derrick Stacey Denholm

7 pm, Feb 4, in the ECU Library

The public is welcome

Derrick Stacey Denholm has spent most of the last 25 years living and
working in the remote forestlands of northern and coastal BC. His
literary work concerns respect for the wild, social and environmental
justice and the ongoing industrialization of Indigenous lands. Two
books, Dead Salmon Dialectics and Ground-Truthing: Reimagining the
Indigenous Rainforests of BC's North Coast were published by Caitlin
Press in 2014 and 2015. Denholm's writing and visual art have also
been published in Canadian Literature, The Capilano Review, CV2,
Dreamland, Drunken Boat, fillingStation, The Goose and ::stonestone::

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the
Arts and the Coast Salish peoples whose traditional unceded territories
we are on.

Brown, Elizabeth Zvonar and Jamie Hilder | MAR 9
Conversation with Colleen Brown, Elizabeth Zvonar and Jamie Hilder
Saturday, March 9 at 2 pm

As part of the exhibition Aporia (Notes to a Medium), exhibiting artists Colleen Brown and
Elizabeth Zvonar are in conversation with artist and writer Jamie Hilder. Having previously
written intimately about Brown and Zvonar's work, Hilder will moderate a discussion on
the themes of uncertainty and doubt in their practices, as well as how memory and future
predictions are interwoven throughout their work.

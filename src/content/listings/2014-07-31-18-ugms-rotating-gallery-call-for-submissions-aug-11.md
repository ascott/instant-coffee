UGM's Rotating Gallery |

CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS |

AUG 11

People are more than just a physical need. That’s why Union Gospel
Mission’s innovative continuum of care—which includes Emergency Shelter,
Alcohol & Drug Recovery, Employment & Education Services, Art Therapy, a
unique Photo 101 course, and more—is strategically designed to meet the
needs of the whole person.

In order to meet holistic need, UGM cultivates a life-giving environment
with intention and care. We strive to build a warm, creative, and healing
space where our guests, residents, and tenants can re-build their lives.

When considering our guests’ social, emotional, or spiritual needs we like
to daydream about the different ways that we can spark, inspire, and
cultivate their imagination. That’s why, two years ago, we transformed our
fifth floor into UGM’s Rotating Art Gallery. By curating local, hope-filled
art on our walls, we believe that we can create an environment that brings
dignity and life to marginalized people’s lives—and we’d love to have your
partnership and participation.

We are searching for artists interested in exhibiting a piece on our fifth
floor’s Rotating Gallery for one year. Please submit your proposal by
Monday, August 11th.

Your artwork could play a vital role in our residents’ lives—all of who are
working hard to overcome poverty, homelessness, or addiction. In addition
to being seen daily by staff and residents, your piece will be viewed by
countless donors and visitors on weekly tours. This is a chance to provide
signposts of hope for our guests while gaining valuable exposure for your work.

We’re looking for art that’s local and creates a sense of unity or
belonging to bring our walls to life.

If you have any questions about the criteria, please contact UGM’s Art
Committee Chair at 604.215.5445 ext 332 or
artcommittee@ugm.ca<mailto:artcommittee@ugm.ca>. We look forward to
reviewing your submission.

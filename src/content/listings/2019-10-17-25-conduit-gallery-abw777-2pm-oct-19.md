ABW: 777
SEVEN books from SEVEN bookstores for SEVEN days.
Curated by James K-M

Conduit Gallery, 165 e Hastings.

Panel Discussion: October 18th, 7-8pm
Opening: October 18th, 8-10pm
Talk: October 19th, 2-4pm
Gallery Hours: October 20-24, 5-7pm

Vancouver bookstores; Massy Books, Macleods Books, Paper Hound Books, READ
Bookstore, and Spartacus Books, among others, will lend Seven books that express their
ideology, point of view or literary aesthetic. Each bookstores selection of books will be
stacked horizontally so that only the spines with the books titles are visible. The list of titles
can be read as a poem, or like a column of numbers to be added up, but instead a series of
possibly disparate or abstract ideas
collected to
have a poetic relationship or resonance.
The book titles collectively gesture to a non-literal reading of what the books contain,
creating a perceptual experience rather than merely conceptual. There will be seven
different tables and table-styles that hold the stacks.

Part of Vancouver Art Book Fair's Art Book Week. Art Book Week is a parallel series of
events hosted in spaces across Vancouver by a variety of artists, curators, collectives and
institutions who are actively creating and presenting work in this medium.
https://www.facebook.com/events/367449477235325/
https://www.facebook.com/events/336581730360629/

Panel Discussion Moderated by Jairo Salazar:

Table with Allison Watters (Gallery Director), James K-M (Curator), Kim Koch (Paper Hound
Bookstore), Patricia Massy (Massy Books), with others TBD. Moderator Jairo Salazar will
spark discussion on critical concepts included in the exhibition, particularly those regarding
the intricate and multiple relations
concerning
art and literature as means of human
expression.

Talk with Guest, Jairo Salazar:

Please join us for a journey through art history to explore some examples of artists and
collectives of the past four decades who found -through the inclusion of words and written
language- political and socially committed ways to defy aesthetic and social conventions in
their artistic practices.

Jairo Salazar (Bogot, Colombia) earned his M.A. in Art History from the University of North
Texas in 2008. His academic background includes teaching and research experience in the
United States and Colombia. His areas of expertise are modern and contemporary art. He
currently teaches art history at Coquitlam College and collaborates as guest speaker for
Mobil Art School.

https://www.linkedin.com/in/jairosalazar/


About James K-M:

An accomplished presenter, K-M has given artist talks and panels at Simon Fraser
University, the Baron Gallery, the Museum of Anthropology (Vancouver), Camagey
Council
of Fine Arts (Cuba), and the International Symposium on Electronic Art (Montreal and

Vancouver Civic Theatres is proud to present a free Visual Arts Open House in the
lobbies of the Queen Elizabeth Theatre on May 1, 2024 from 5-8pm. The current
visual arts exhibition features work by local artists sharai mustatia and Biliana
Velkova.

About the Exhibitions

sharai mustatias KEEP GOING

sharai mustatia is a Mtis/Romanian person coming to terms with intergenerational
trauma. Through collage, analogue photography and in-camera editing, sharai's
work is an exploration of survival as a Mtis mother who was coerced into giving
their two children up for adoption. The images in KEEP GOING are filled with layers
of meaning, emotion, and one persons journey of transformational healing.

Biliana Velkovas The Language of Beasts

Fragmented bodies, protean spaces and mercurial atmospheres, The Language of
Beasts is a quicksilver suite of paintings that elicits lucid dream states and conjures
infinite transformation. Through fantastical renderings of familiar animals - from
dogfish-entrail hybrids to wolves who breathe fire - Biliana to creates a world of
possibility and potential, one without humans and anthropocenic dominance, inviting
us to ponder: What would life look like without us?

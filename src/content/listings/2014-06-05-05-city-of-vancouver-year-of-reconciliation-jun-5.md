New artwork for the Year of Reconciliation debuts in June

Vancouver artist Bracken Hanuse Corlett will show two new transit shelter 
posters that explore the role of monsters and giants from Northwest 
Coast 
indigenous oral history. The posters will be in sites around the city 
through June. 
New projects will be introduced monthly through August 2014.

WHITE, Dec. 3, 5-7 PM, 320 Carrall Street

We are all different much like the snowflakes that fall from the sky during the holiday 
season. Each snowflake has a story - a message that is here for a moment and gone 
the next. This holiday season we would like to bring the community together to share a 
piece of their snowflake selves.

On December 3, 5-7 PM, we are inviting neighbours to bring a white object to adorn the 
Cheeky Proletariat Gallery storefront like snowflakes, at 320 Carrall Street, Vancouver. 
Objects can be big, small, odd, absurd, beautiful, or kitschy. Bring anything - it just has 
to be white. Examples - white plastic fork, porcelain figurine, photos, everyday items, 
anything. We will also have snacks to celebrate the community holiday window.

The exhibition continues to January 8.

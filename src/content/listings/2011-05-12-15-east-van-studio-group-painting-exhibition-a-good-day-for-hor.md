East Van Studio / A Good Day For Horse Races / a group painting
exhibition / May 20

A Good Day For Horse Races, is a group show of new work by 12 emerging
artists. Through paint as a shared medium, the show explores deconstruction
and reconstruction of pattern, shape, and pictorial space.

Opening Friday May 20th 7pm - 11pm, Show runs Saturday May 21 & Sunday
May 22 noon - 6pm
East Van Studio, 870 East Cordova Street

New work by:

Benjamin Raymer

Colleen Heslin
Collin Johanson
Chrystal Fisher
Jessica Delorme

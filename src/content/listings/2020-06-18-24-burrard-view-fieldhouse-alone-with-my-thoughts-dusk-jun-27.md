alone with my thoughts: Saturday June 27th at dusk
Burrard View Fieldhouse 545 N Slocan Street

The eighth instalment of our Work in Progress series features Iris member lisa g.
Over the month of June in collaboration with artist/sculptor Cheryl Hamilton, lisa g
will explore worry, sleeplessness, and anxiety through mixed media, culminating in
an experimental installation & performance on the lawn in front of the fieldhouse.
Due to the unique circumstances of the novel coronavirus this performance is
designed to be viewed from afar. Using excerpts from previously created works in
16mm & super 8 combined with project-specific new material, this experiment will
animate the Burrard View Park with projection and sound testing until the
final
performance on Saturday June 27th at dusk.

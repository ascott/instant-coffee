VANCOUVER ART GALLERY ASSOCIATION
CASUAL POSITION AVAILABLE
Graphics Installer

DEADLINE: Monday, April 30, 2018

DUTIES:
Reporting to the Audio Visual Technician III, the incumbent assists the Designer for 
Exhibits, Graphics and Production in the preparation and installation of graphic and 
signage materials for gallery exhibitions, programs and wayfinding.
Responsibilities include all physical handling of graphic materials to be used for 
display or installation; installation of large-scale printed, adhesive-backed mural 
graphics; installation of large and small-scale die-cut vinyl lettering. Also includes 
preparation of all graphic output materials including vinyl weeding and masking; 
mounting, trimming and organizing small-scale signs and artwork labels; cutting and 
preparation of various substrates; following technical drawings and floorplans; 
devise and implement custom signage systems; working with other
technicians engaged in exhibition preparation and installation.

QUALIFICATIONS:
Post Secondary courses in graphic production, media or visual arts and/or a 
minimum of one year of experience in graphic/signage installation and preparation is 
required. Demonstrated experience with mural graphics installation, cut vinyl 
mounting, and work in the visual arts field are assets. As a graphics installer, you will 
be knowledgable of a variety of graphics materials and substrates including 
adhesive-backed vinyls and paper, dibond, sintra, foam core etc. Skill and 
knowledge required in the handling of manual and power tools for measuring, cutting 
and mounting materials. Experience and safety in working on ladders, scaffolding 
and mechanical lifts is required. Scissor lift certification is an asset. Applicants must 
demonstrate an attention to detail and ability to follow directions ensuring high 
quality work; an efficiency to work within a compressed timeline in a fast
paced environment; excellent communication skills with the ability to effectively work 
independently and as part of a team. The successful applicant will have proficient 
knowledge and skills in basic office software. Woodworking and construction 
experience is also an asset.

SALARY: Pay Grade 18 Step 1 $23.02 per hour

HOURS: This is casual on-call work during normal business hours. Some weekend 
and evening hours may be required.

Please submit your resume by Monday, April 30, 2018 to Debra Nesbitt, Director of 
Human Resources, Vancouver Art Gallery, 750 Hornby Street, Vancouver, B.C.
V6Z 2H7, Fax: 604-682- 1086 or email hr@vanartgallery.bc.ca.

North Van Arts is currently accepting submissions for our Art in the Community

Program for 2026. Art in the Community consists of two exhibition venues:
- District Library Gallery / Located at the Lynn Valley Main Library (2D submissions)
- District Foyer Gallery/ Located at North Vancouver District Hall (2D and 3D
submissions)

The Art in the Community Program, run by North Van Arts aims to celebrate and
enable emerging and professional artists through solo exhibition opportunities in
public spaces.

We invite visual artists working in 2D mediums or small 3D mediums (including
painting, photography, ceramics and mixed media) to apply. Successful candidates
will be offered an eight-to-twelve-week solo exhibition time slot within the 2026
calendar year.

Artists must be able to fill the entire exhibition space, and are required to submit a
minimum of 10 artworks to exhibit. Exact number of artworks exhibits will be
dependent on artwork sizing.

Deadline to Submit: October 30, 2025
Submit here: https://form-can.keela.co/art-in-the-community-call-for-artists

Saturday, December 9 at 7:30 PM - 9 PM
Griffin Art Projects, 1174 Welch St, North Vancouver

Please join us for this performance event in conjunction with our current exhibition, Paul 
P. Civilization (inverted). 

For the last forty years, Vancouver artist Oraf Orafsson has worked in film, video, 
performance, and installation. Performing across Canada and the US, Orafssons 
projects have addressed postwar politics, oppositional cultural movements, and AIDS 
activism. 

He will present a new performance, Burnt Offering, which is a variation on Bonfire of the 
Vanities. Doors will open at 7:30pm and performance will commence at 8pm.

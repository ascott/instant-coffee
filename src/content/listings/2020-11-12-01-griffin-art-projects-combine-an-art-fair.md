COMBINE: AN ART FAIR (The closure of national and international art fairs
globally due
to Covid-19, provides an opportunity to reconsider art economies and solidarities.)

CONVERSATION | COMMUNITY | CONVIVIALITY

A collaboration between Franc Gallery " Griffin Art Projects - Monica Reyes Gallery -
Unit 17 " Wil Aballe Art Projects

At Griffin Art Projects Residency, 1180 Welch Street, North Vancouver, BC, V7P 1B2

The closure of national and international art fairs globally due to Covid-19, provides an
opportunity to reconsider art economies and solidarities. This project features four
young Vancouver galleries and finds within a collaborative model, ways to think together
to imagine how such impacts can result in exploring the fissures and sites of opportunity
that this unprecedented moment represents, to ask: How are we now working? What do
proximities mean at this time? How do we express and experiment with the idea of
collaboration as opposed to being in competition?

COMBINE art fair also features an online public program that explores new ecologies in
gallery, artist and art fair cultures. Accompanied by an onsite exhibition at Griffin
residency, Ecologies of Resilience.

Overview

At this particular moment of COVID-19, within the Vancouver art ecosystem, there are
many interdependent parts: artists, collectors, galleries, non-profits and institutions. All
of them are relevant. We need each other to thrive to keep the ecosystem healthy.
Galleries are important in the nurturing phase for artists, as well as the nurturing of
young collectors. The four galleries featured in COMBINE art fair provide an access
point for great new art that is being made on the West Coast while keeping the
conversation going with the more seasoned collectors who want to continue learning
about new voices in the local scene (and beyond).

COMBINE, held at Griffin Art Projects"an important collector-driven institution on the
West Coast"is an event where galleries combine in support of collectors of
contemporary art by coming together in the same space at
the same time. At a moment
when travelling to separate spaces to see work may be challenging, we offer a new
collaborative experiment and model.

The long-term aim of this initiative and our work as collaborators, is to foster the
development of confident collectors in the city, who use their own instincts and insights
as they navigate the art works they encounter and acquire, and are aware of the
importance of their patronage in supporting artists (and their galleries) in Vancouver.
Although all galleries represent artists at every stage of their career, both artists and
gallerists also benefit from such support at the early stages in their career. Ultimately,
we are all here, collectors and galleries, to support artists who serve our community.

Griffin will host four progressive Vancouver gallerists onsite throughout the residency
spaces in early December, (December 5 & 6 and December 9 " 13, 2020) for a
collaborative venture. Wil Aballe Art Projects, Unit 17, Monica Reyes Gallery
and Franc
Gallery will work with Griffin to create an Art Fair, developed to support younger galleries
who have been impacted by Covid-19 through the suspension of art fairs globally. The
project also provides a cross-fertilization with new audiences for all. Griffin will work
with the galleries to promote the event and develop a safe onsite plan that follows
COVID-19 protocols. The galleries will be located in the two studio areas while Griffin
will curate an exhibition of gallery artists in the bedroom space that will be transformed
into a small gallery for this occasion. The overall project, which will be held in the
residency area, is intended to create an exciting new alternative event that supports our
local art community during these challenging times.

Exhibition: Ecologies of Resilience. Based on the idea of the Decameron, the famous
medieval novel written during the time of a global pandemic, the exhibition posits
conviviality and storytelling as a main locus. Each of the
artists will show how
solidarities, empathy and conviviality can result in the sharing, and the necessary
reshaping of, narratives and of histories.

VIVO is now taking bookings for this popular two day Video Journalism 
workshop.

==Saturday April 28& Saturday May 5==

Learn on-camera reporting, off-camera interview skills, documentary shooting 
techniques and polished journalistic writing.

Participants will develop a documentary concept (or bring one you have in mind) 
and shoot footage that will be analyzed and edited over two workshop sessions.

Session 1: Saturday April 28 12-5pm Learn how to set up a good interview with 
lights, camera, and good questions, plus documentary shooting and sound 
skills.

Session 2: Saturday May 5 12-5pm With footage you've shot as homework from 
session one, learn to cut together a short news story with voiceover, on-camera 
introduction, and solid structure.

This workshop is suitable for: Those who have completion VIVO's Camera 
Lights& Sound and Final Cut Pro Intro, or those familiar with the basics of video 
production and post-production.

This is a great opportunity to learn first hand from workshop leader Flick 
Harrison, one of Canada's first television videographers. Flick has degrees in 
both film production and journalism.

Make docs that ring true.

Cost: $200 or $160 with VIVO Producer Membership (Extended) Instructor: 
Flick Harrison

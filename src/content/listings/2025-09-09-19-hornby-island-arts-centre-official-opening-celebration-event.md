The Hornby Island Arts Council is celebrating the official opening of the new Hornby
Island Arts Centre on Friday, September 12, 2025, with guest speakers including Gord
Johns, Josie Osborne, Daniel Arbour, and architect DArcy Jones.

The $3.1-million, award-winning building was made possible through the support of
governments, funders, donors, and community members, marking a major milestone
for the island.

Opening festivities include poetry with Lorna Crozier and Cornelia Hoogland (Sept 12),
a concert with Fred Penner (Sept 18), and the launch of the t ti sem Elder Barb Whyte
Healing Pathway (Sept 27).

The Portfolio Show

CLOSING EVENT

SUNDAY JUNE 9
2-5pm
at BLAH
848 E. Hastings St.
Vancouver, BC

We are pleased to invite you to the closing event of the inagural exhibition of the
Portfolio Prize!

Please join us in the afternoon to celebrate the exhibition with the artists present, for
an opportunity to learn more about the Portfolio edition and support its mandate.

The Portfolio Show, the inaugural exhibition of the Portfolio Prize is a celebration of
the 20-year history of the foundation and its mandate
Artists for Artists. The show
includes works from its prize recipients: Colleen Brown, Derya Akay, Del Hillier,
Manuel Axel Strain, Durrah Alsaif, Bracken Hanuse Corlett, Rebecca Bair and Gabi
Dao; supporters of the foundation: John David James and Benjamin Lumb,
alongside works from its celebrated guest contributors: Rebecca Belmore, Dana
Claxton, Vikky Alexander and its founding members, Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun,
Attila Richard Lukacs, Derek Root, Douglas Coupland, Angela Grossmann and
Graham Gillmore and honorary Chair Gordon Smith.

The Portfolio Prize extends its gratitude to all guest artists, jurors, collectors and all
who have contributed to the success of this initiative.

The exhibition runs from May 18 - June 15, 2024 at BLAH.

Gallery opening hours are Wednesday - Saturday 12-5pm.

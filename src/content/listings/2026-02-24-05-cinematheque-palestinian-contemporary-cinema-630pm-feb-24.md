You are invited to a screening of a foundational feature film of Palestinian contemporary
cinema When I Saw You, directed by Annemarie Jacir (2012, 1:38:00), and the classic film
Far From the Homeland (1969), directed by Qais Al-Zubaidi (1969, 11:00)

Tues. Feb. 24th, 6:30PM at the Cinematheque: 1131 Howe St, Vancouver

Suggested donation $10 to Palestine relief.

A program featuring two significant works of Arab cinema that explore themes of
displacement, identity, and resistance. They offer child-centered portrayals of Palestinian
dispossession after the 1967 Naqsa. Al-Zubaidis film is a work of radical cinema where
children are commenting on their own filmed images, a reflection of who controls
representation, self and other. Jacir, the acclaimed director of Palestine36, is also critically
engaging the past, with an intimate narrative, restoring hope for the ongoing struggle.

Opening remarks by Jeeda Musleh, Palestinian organizer from Apartheid Free Communities,
and a pre-recorded introduction to the films by director Annemarie Jacir.

Films to be followed by a Q&A and a prerecorded discussion with director Annemarie Jacir.

Presented by from the river to the sea collective in partnership with The Centre For
Comparative Muslim Studies-SFU, Apartheid Free Communities-BC, and Cinema Workers
for Palestine. With the support of, and many thanks to the Cinematheque (Vancouver),
PhilistineFilms (Ramallah), The Match Factory (Cologne) and Nadi LeKol Nas (Beirut). Hala
Gaza vendor table on site.

*Childcare to be provided.

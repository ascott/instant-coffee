North Van Arts is hiring 3 positions through the Canada Summer Jobs Program

1) Events and Exhibitions Assistant (Event Coordinator)

The assistant will support the planning, organization, and execution of community
events such as Arts in the Garden, and the North Shore Art Crawl as well as 3-5
visual arts exhibitions which are varied in subject, medium, and themes representing
artists from various cultures, genders, and ages, at various points in their careers
ranging from emerging to professional.

REMUNERATION: $20.00 per hour, 35 hour per week for 16 weeks

Start date: Monday, May 12, 2025

2) Cultural Mapping & Marketing Assistant (Cultural Geographer)

The assistant will support the development, tracking, & evaluation of multiple digital
& print marketing for multiple North Van Arts programs as well as assisting in regular
map maintenance, the addition of new cultural assets to the map, content creation
for community outreach campaigns, & the creation & development of treasure hunt
activities for the North Shore Culture Compass.

REMUNERATION: $20.00 per hour, 35 hour per week for 16 weeks

Start date: Monday, May 12, 2025

3) Camp Creative Assistant (Art Coordinator)

The Camp Creative Assistant (Art Coordinator " Education) will support the
artists/instructors in managing and preparing supplies and equipment, assisting kids
in the art activities, cleaning up and overseeing outside time and lunch/snack
breaks, and supporting administrative tasks related to registration, communication,
and logistics, gaining valuable experience in these areas. The Camp Creative
Assistant will be working closely with the lead Instructor and the Arts Education
Manager as Supervisor and Mentor, to support the operation of each of these camps
in July and August.

REMUNERATION: $20.00 per hour, 35 hour per week for 10 weeks
Start date: Monday, June 21, 2025

For full job posting details visit: [https://northvanarts.ca/about/career-opportunities/](https://northvanarts.ca/about/career-opportunities/)

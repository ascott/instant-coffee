5-6pm on 02/18/2017 at Vanier Park, by the Maritime Museum.

Two prairie-born performance artists meet by the ocean for an impromptu 
collaboration.

Terrance Houle (Calgary) and Alexandra Bischoff (Edmonton) exorcise ghosts using 
music, the land, and intimacy as medium. For the love of salt and singing, come 
witness Bonnie's seance from 5-6pm on 02/18/2017 at Vanier Park, by the Maritime 
Museum.

Join us this year in putting on the 9th annual Signal & Noise Festival;
three days of provocative, innovative and challenging contemporary interdisciplinary art. Showcasing a spectrum of audio, live action performances and immersive installations, Signal and Noise is Vancouver's only alternative multi-disciplinary art event. There are many opportunities to volunteer for this year’s festival, which runs from April 17-19th. Volunteer roles include:

•	Postering (March 18-25th)
•	Pre-Festival Spring Cleaning (March 29th – 30th)
•	Painting
•	Carpentry
•	Installation assistance for the art works
•	Assisting with Audio/Video equipment
•	Bar/Concession
•	Coat Check
•	Security/Door
•	Admissions
•	Post Festival Tear Down
•	Lots more!!!

We need volunteers urgently for postering and our pre-event clean-up!

Volunteers will be provided with access to the festival events and
installations, based on the hours that they donate, and all VIVO members
will receive video bucks.

Please contact Liz at volunteer@signalandnoise.ca or call 604.786.6249.
Visit www.signalandnoise.ca for more information.

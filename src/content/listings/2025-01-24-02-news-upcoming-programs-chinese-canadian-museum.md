celebrated across the globe this year on January 29. In preparation for the new year,

lanterns, door banners, and paper cuttings.

About Caf
Daughter
A Cree-Chinese girl struggles to keep her dreams from being extinguished in this
affecting period drama from celebrated Kanyenkeh:ka (Mohawk) multimedia artist
Shelley Niro, subject of a major touring retrospective currently on view at the
Vancouver Art Gallery. Adapted from Cree playwright Kenneth T. Williamss one-
woman play, itself based on the life of retired Canadian senator Lillian Eva Quan
Dyck, Caf Daughter follows young Yvette Wong, a whip-smart student coming of
age in small-town 1960s Saskatchewan, who has been taught to conceal her
Indigeneity in a bid to pass as fully Chinese, the lesser persecuted minority. A death
in the family compels Yvette to embark on a discrimination-laden path to medical
school, while a taboo friendship with a Mikmaq classmate sows the seeds of
Indigenous pride. Niro, whose art practice routinely involves film, crafts a soul-stirring
picture that honours the extraordinary resolve of its inspiration"a neurosurgeon
before her appointment to
the Senate.
Audience Choice Feature Award
imagineNATIVE Film + Media Arts Festival 2023
This screening is co-presented with the Cinematheque and the Vancouver Art
Gallery in conjunction with their exhibition Shelley Niro: 500 Year Itch, on view until
February 17, 2025.

3. Year of the Snake at the Museum
February 1st & 2nd, 2025

January 29, 2025 marks the start of the Year of the Snake, representing a year of
wisdom, intuition, and transformation. Welcome the new lunar calendar at the
museum with us on February 1st and 2nd for a weekend full of fun activities,
storytelling, crafts, treats, and more with your family and friends!

On both days, drop-in for free Chinese New Year treat sampling, Year of the Snake-
themed crafts, and Chinese board games. On Saturday, February 1st, join us for a
special Lunar New Year Cantonese storytime; and on Sunday, February 2nd, after
the Chinatown Parade, celebrate at the museum with some artisanal ice cream and
balloon art!

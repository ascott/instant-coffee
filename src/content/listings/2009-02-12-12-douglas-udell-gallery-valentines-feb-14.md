Valentine's @ Douglas Udell Gallery

We are excited to present Valentine's. An exhibition featuring new
valentine inspired work by our gallery artists - be it heart felt or
heart wrenching. Including works by Vancouver artists Michael Batty, Graeme
Berglund, John Capitano and others.

Please join us for our Opening Reception Saturday, February 14th, 2009.
Exhibition continues through the end of February, 2009.

Opening Reception: Saturday, February 14th

Time: 2pm-4pm

Location: 1558 West 6th Avenue. Vancouver, BC.

At the foot of Gallery Row, just off of South Granville.

Yactac Craft AfFair
Opening: Dec 17, 2011 - 11am - 5pm
Yactac Gallery

Yactac is pleased to present "Yactac Craft AfFair" on Dec 17th. We 
are calling for artists submission for the fair, if you or anyone you know 
is interested in selling some hand-made somethings, please contact us 
at yactacgallery@gmail.com. Bring your crafts and cheat on'em~!

Please come and enjoy some hand-made somethings on Saturday, 
December 17th from 11-5pm at Yactac Gallery. The Craft AfFair 
features local artisans ranging from bakers, felters, painters, and 
shakers. We will be displaying our works for your buying pleasure free 
of charge, free of charge, free of charge! There will a crafting table 
where you are welcome to make some fun ornaments. Tea, Coffee, 
and snacks will be available. Come out, have fun, and enjoy 
yourselves! Because it's great, and we are Yactac!

Everyone welcome!

Yactac are Janice and Justine Cheung, Peggy and Karen Ngan.

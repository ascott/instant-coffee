Pressure Point is a reduced-barriers space for local artists to sell their art work in a
managed, curated, and high-foot-traffic environment. This project is not-for-profit,
meaning that commissions from art sales go back into keeping the space open. We
aim to reduce barriers for emerging artists and share resources in solidarity with those
targeted by systemic barriers. Pressure Point prioritizes support for local emerging
2SLGBTQIA+ and BIPoC artists in so-called Vancouver. To find out more about
Pressure Point, visit our website:

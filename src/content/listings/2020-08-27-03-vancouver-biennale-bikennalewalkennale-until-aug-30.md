The Vancouver Biennale re-imagines their annual arts and culture cycling event into 18
self-paced cycling and walking tours that offer Covid-safe, creatively inspired, outdoor
urban fun. With a one-time $5 registration for the 2020 BIKEenale/WALKennale,
participants will receive weekly self-paced, annotated bike and walking tours that highlight
great public art, architecture and points of cultural and historical interest. We guarantee
that even the most avid cyclist and urban hiker will discover something new! As an added
bonus, each registered participant will be automatically entered to win a Norco Indie 2
hybrid bike worth $900.

Designed for people of all ages and athletic
abilities, and for those wanting laid-back short
tours as well as those looking for longer challenges, participants will be guided through 24
Metro Vancouver neighbourhoods using easy to follow GPS navigation on their cell phone.
The perfect staycation activity, this re-imagined BIKEenale/WALKennale makes it easy to
adhere to Covid social distancing while enjoying some much-needed outdoor recreation.

New tours are released every Sunday until August 30th and remain available on the cell
phone app through 2020. Ride as often as you like and take as many different tours as
you like for your one-time $5 registration and feel good knowing that funds raised support
the Vancouver Biennale public art exhibitions, artist residencies and BIG Ideas education
program.

Learn more at www.vbbike.ca

ISEA2015, the 21st International Symposium on Electronic Art.

August 14th -19th.

SFU Woodwards.

ART PROGRAM

Join us from August 14 – 19 for the 21st International Symposium on 
Electronic Art (ISEA2015). 

Over the course of six days, over 450 speakers will present workshops, 
tutorials, panels, papers and institutional presentations and exhibitions 
featuring work by international and local artists working at the 
intersection of art and technology. Simon Fraser University is hosting 
the symposium, which will be focused at SFU Woodwards. 
Installations, videos and soundworks on view at SFU Woodwards 
throughout the duration of ISEA.149 West Hastings Street, Vancouver

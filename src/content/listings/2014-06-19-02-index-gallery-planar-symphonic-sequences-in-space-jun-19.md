PLANAR - Symphonic Sequences in Space

sound performances to enliven your present lived experience.
find yourself spatially within the relative immortality of sounds.
immerse yourselves in a symphonic implicatedness within a rhizomatic 
vision.

-
​
The in-between space of interconnectivity is where an affectual experience
of sound lies. It is another dimension of assemblage, driven by desire.
The use of sound in space exonerates the use of linear thinking and
inherently links itself to a rhizomatic and affectual space.

The rhizome is inherently connected to an artist’s practice, as each 
series,each study, is connected through a thought process that seeks to 
grasp onto a certain interconnectivity of existence.

If concepts are lines - number systems attached to multiplicities - then 
sound is the space that permeates around multiplicities.
In terms of its presence in space, sound may appear centralized, but it is in
fact evenly distributed as the particles make their way across planes (of
people and places).

The spatial relationship between sound and space exists in a state of
totalized, corporeal permeation.

Sound becomes an intangible space that affects both the conceivable and
material world but works through the interior world.

The architecture of sound curates the body to be within the NOW. ​

Tim Okamura
BROOKLYN WALLS: hopechangedreams 
6 June -20 June, 2009 

Artist reception: 6 June 4pm-6pm
(artist will be in attendance)

The paintings that comprise the exhibition, “Brooklyn Walls: hopechangedreams”, continue the 
investigation of many of the same themes of identity, environment, and iconography visited 
previously in the Urban Portrait and Brooklyn Mythology series, though the driving force behind 
these new works is an attempt to ‘capture optimism’.

The sanguine expressions in the subjects are both overt and subtle – in some paintings, the idea 
is to portray a kind of heroism, especially when the figures have been scaled larger than life, 
while in other canvases the intention is to capture a sense of anticipation or a process of 
introspection.

The social and political environment of America is seen through a lens of positivity – though 
admittedly not always immediately apparent - in this exploration of a microcosm of the American 
experience: the neighborhood of Bushwick in Brooklyn, New York. The subjects inhabit the 
streets of an innately contradictory urban setting – one that is appears dilapidated and 
neglected, yet simultaneously seems dynamic and vibrant.

They are a diverse group of Brooklynites who re-define stereotypes of the ‘average American’ 
and who personify stoicism and strength and solidarity in the face of adversity. They look to the
future while grounded in a present day urban ‘reality’ – a reality that sometimes is infiltrated by 
graffiti, word fragments, dream symbols, and ‘visions’.

While the individualism of the subjects is celebrated, the hope is to also discover through them 
metaphors for aspects of the greater human experience; a sense of approaching dividends of 
patience and perseverance; possibilities for progress of the individual and societal change, and a 
palpable expression of the buzzword of a new regime: Hope.

Please join the artist for his Opening Reception Saturday, June 6th from 4-6pm. Exhibition 
continues through June 20th , 2009.

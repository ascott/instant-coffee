Colin Smith
Marie Annharte Baker

Saturday, June 15, 2013 @ 1:00 pm

Kootenay School of Writing 2012
237 Keefer Street, Unit 245

Colin Smith, author of CARBONATED BIPPIES! (Nomados), 8 x 8 x 7 (KRUPSKAYA)
and Multiple Poses (Tsunami), is a spore from the Kootenay School of
Writing now living in Winnipeg.

Annharte aka Marie Baker is an Anishnabe poet and author, a cultural critic
and activist, and a performance artist/contemporary storyteller. Her books
are Being on the Moon (Polestar, 1990 / Raincoast 2000), Coyote Columbus
Cafe (Moonprint, 1994), Exercises in Lip Pointing, (New Star Books 2003)
Indigena Awry (New Star Books 2013).

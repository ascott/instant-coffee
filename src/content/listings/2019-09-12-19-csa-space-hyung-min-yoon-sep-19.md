Black Book 

Hyung-Min Yoon 

Sept 19 - Nov 1, 2019 (opening hours Wed - Sun 11-4pm, or by appointment) 
Opening reception Sept 19 6-9 pm

CSA Space presents Black Book, an exhibition by Vancouver-based artist Hyung-Min 
Yoon. The installation uses analog film projectors controlled with microprocessors for a 
large screen viewing experience of the original artists book. Black Book joins two 
distinct threads of disparate cultures and histories - Confucian virtues and modern black 
humour - inquiring into contemporary ideologies such as patriarchy, political coercion, 
and technological determinism. The artist's book of the same title was published by 
Information Office in 2018, and can be purchased at Pulpfiction Books during the 
exhibition. 

Hyung-Min Yoon is a visual artist working between Vancouver, Canada and Seoul, 
Korea. Her work utilizes a variety of media and forms, with an interest in language, 
translation, and the elemental roots of meaning. Yoon has held solo exhibitions in 
Vancouver (Gam Gallery, 2018; grunt gallery, 2014) and Seoul (Hyundai Gallery, 2015; 
Project Space at Wumin Art Centre, 2014).

Phoenix Art Collective - 11 years and 1 Day

The Phoenix Art Collective is excited to invite you to our upcoming show featuring the talents
of our working artists.

For 11 years, since taking possession on April 15th, 2015, The Phoenix has served as a
creative space where Vancouver artists come together to exhibit their work and refine their
craft. Our collective includes artists who have been part of this space for 11 years, others for
10, 5, or 3 years...

This showcase highlights the diverse talents of painters, fabric artists, and ceramicists. We're
presenting several dozen works that demonstrate the exceptional skill and creativity of these
talented individuals.

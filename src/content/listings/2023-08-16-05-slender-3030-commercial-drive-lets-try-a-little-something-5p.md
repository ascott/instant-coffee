Let's try a little something
Andre Ethier & Adrian Norvid

Slender gallery 3030 Commercial driveis delighted to present in its second
exhibition, a selection of works by Toronto artist and painter Andre Ethier, and UK
born, Montreal based drawer and paper fanatic Adrian Norvid. Both Norvid and
Ethier have exhibited widely, though rarely in Vancouver, till now (shout out to the
astounding peanuts gallery) Satire and levity girded by a healthy dose of fantastic
absurdity are on the menu. Main
course, the perils that plague us as humans. The remedy, why not come see for
yourself?

Lets try a little something opens Friday August 18th at Slender gallery

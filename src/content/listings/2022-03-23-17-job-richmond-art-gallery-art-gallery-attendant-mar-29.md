RICHMOND ART GALLERY
JOB POSTING: Art Gallery Attendant
Apply by: March 29, 2022

Position Overview
Current employment opportunities exist at the Richmond Art Gallery for part-time and
on-call Gallery Attendants starting April 2022. Gallery Attendants help to create an
accessible and enjoyable experience in the Art Gallery assisting visitors with a general
understanding of current art exhibitions, while also focusing on protecting the art works
and monitoring the gallery spaces.
This is a part-time position, working 1
shift per week on Saturdays or Sundays starting
in April, 2022.
On-call Attendants work various 4-5 hour shifts throughout the week,
including weekends.

Duties and Responsibilities:
- Receiving the public: Greeting all visitors and offering information on exhibitions,
programs, and memberships; Answering a wide range of general questions from
visitors, in person, by phone and email in a courteous and respectful manner while
upholding Gallery policies.
- Securing the gallery and its contents: Circulating frequently through the gallery to
deter acts of vandalism, theft or negligence and ensure the safety of guests;
Accounting for works of art in exhibitions, equipment and furniture upon opening and
closing procedures and during regular rounds.
- Maintaining reception and gallery spaces: Working with all staff on various
administrative duties such as membership database, attendance record updating,
marketing mail outs, and online research; Ensuring reception, public areas and
literature
displays are organized and tidy.

Working Conditions:
-
Part-time hours of a minimum of 1 day per week. Sunday shift is from 12:00 - 5:00
pm.
- On-call positions to cover staff sickness and vacation times, may lead to a permanent
shift.
- New Attendants are required to attend two orientation sessions prior to the start of
duties. Once completed, all Attendants attend a 2-hour orientation session prior to the
opening of each new exhibition.

Required Qualifications and Skills:
- Minimum 2 years of experience dealing with the public in a customer service or
hospitality environment. Hospitality-oriented individual with strong people skills is
preferred.
- Friendly and outgoing, possessing strong communication skills to interact with diverse
visitors of all ages.
- The ability to clearly speak, write and understand the English language. (Speaking
Mandarin is an asset).
- Familiarity with art galleries/museums; strong interest in contemporary art.
- Proficient in MS Outlook, Word and
Excel.
- Strong sense of responsibility, enthusiasm,
diplomacy with the public, and
comfortable working independently.
- Ability to manage multiple tasks during gallery events, as well as remain focused and
attentive for long periods of time in a very quiet environment.
- On-call position - Must have flexible schedule and able to accept assignments on
short notice.

Wage: $15.20 an hour

To Apply:
Send resume and cover letter via email to:
Kathy Tycholis, Education & Public Programs Coordinator - ktycholis@richmond.ca
No phone calls please. Only shortlisted applicants will be notified.

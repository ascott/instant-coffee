NOW THE TREES HAVE ALL GROWN UP

New Drawings and Paintings by Shari-Anne Gibson at Little Mountain Gallery
October 29th - November 18th, 2009 
Opening: October 29, 7-10pm

Little Mountain Gallery 195 East 26th Avenue at Main Street
Hours: Wednesday-Friday: 1-5pm
Saturday-Sunday: 11am - 5pm
Monday-Tuesday: Closed

In this new series on canvas and mylar, Gibson re-invents the traditional landscape by creating 
interrupted and dislocated scenes. Historically, the natural world is praised for offering the viewer 
rest and fullness, a respite from the frenetic activity of daily life. And yet, today, our experience of 
landscape is constantly interrupted: by power lines, newly paved roads, cell phones. Being drawn 
in to a quiet center is truly rare. 

Gibson's new work investigates how movement between illusionistic depth and the painted 
surface express our fragmented experience of nature and invite the viewer to see the sky not as 
endless but as a skin of paint. We cannot fully climb into the image, but are forced to consider 
the flat surface that surrounds and invades the scenes. These inconsistencies generate 
meaning and challenge our experience of natural beauty.

Lumapit Sa Akin, Paraiso (Come to Me, Paradise), 2016, 25min 46sec

Yesterday, In The Years 1886 and 2017 is a two-channel video projection 
installation. The two protagonists Jos Rizal and Lourdes Lareza Mller occupy a 
channel each; projected adjacent to one another, they inhabit the same space while 
remaining distinctly separate.

Music by NaEE RoBErts and Elysia Crampton

Stephanie Comilang is an artist living and working in Toronto and Berlin. She 
received her BFA from Ontario College of Art & Design. Her documentary based 
works create narratives that look at how our understandings of mobility, capital and 
labour on a global scale are shaped through various cultural and social factors. Her 
most recent film Lumapit Sa Akin, Paraiso (2016) is a science fiction documentary 
about Filipina migrant workers in Hong Kong. It has been screened at Asia Art 
Archive in America, New York; S.A.L.T.S., Basel; UCLA, Los Angeles; Images 
Festival, Toronto; and Art Athina in Athens.

The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Artists on Artists: Sara Cwynar on Vikky Alexander and
Robert Rauschenberg

Saturday, September 7 @ 3 PM on the Second Floor of the Gallery
750 Hornby St, Vancouver, BC

Regular admission applies: $24 General | $18 Student + Senior | Free for Members!

Join artist Sara Cwynar and independent curator Jenn Jackson as they discuss intersecting
ideas and ways of making that speak across artistic generations. In response to the current
exhibitions Vikky Alexander: Extreme Beauty and Robert Rauschenberg 1965-1980 (on view
through to January 26, 2020), Cwynar and Jackson consider the power of media in
contemporary life and the role of art in subverting familiar images and subject matter. Cwynar
will also discuss the work of Alexander and Rauschenberg in relation to her own photographs
and films, now on view in Sara Cwynar: Gilded Age II at The Polygon Gallery.

For tickets and info, visit: vanartgallery.bc.ca

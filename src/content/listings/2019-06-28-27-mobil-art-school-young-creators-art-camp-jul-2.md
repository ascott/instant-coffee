Young creators art camp July 2-5, 2019
Jul. 02 " Jul. 05, 9:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Not your average art camp! Our innovative and extremely fun summer camp will expose 
participants to a variety of media and processes, from clay sculpture to printmaking to 
building projects and more. Each day the group will learn something new from an expert 
teacher in the field and make a variety of work throughout the week. The kids will end 
the week by displaying their work in an exhibition in our beautiful space and be able to 
invite parents and friends to come and appreciate the work! We guarantee a safe, fun 
environment.
Ages: 10-14, Dates: Jul through August, $280/week

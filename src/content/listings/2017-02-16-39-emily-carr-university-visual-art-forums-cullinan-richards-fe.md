VISUAL ART FORUMS | Cullinan Richards
Monday February 27th, 2017 | 6pm
MoCap Studio | Rm 285e NB

Please join us for a talk by Cullinan Richards.

The collaborative duo, made up of British artists Charlotte Cullinan and Jeanine 
Richards, have been working together for 20 years first under the moniker Artlab 
and more recently as Cullinan Richards. They have exhibited their work widely, 
including exhibitions at Cooper Gallery, Dundee; Whitechapel Gallery, London; 
South London Gallery; Museum of Contemporary Art, Seville, Spain; Daniel 
Spoerri Foundation, Seggiano, Italy; Whitstable Biennale, Kent, UK; and 
Kunstmuseum Lucerne, Switzerland. In 2006 they established the Savage 
School Window Gallery using the window of ...their studio on Vyner Street in East 
London to display texts by writers, artists and curators. In 2013, in collaboration 
with curator Andrea Sassi, this became 4 Cose, an Italian grocery shop-as-
artwork open for artists interventions. Cullinan is Professor of Fine Art and Course 
Director, MA Fine Art Programme at Kingston University. Richards is Principal 
Lecturer MA Fine Art Programme at Kingston University.

Cullinan Richards is in Vancouver as part of the exhibition Goodbye Charles on 
at the Charles H. Scott Gallery from March 1 to April 23, 2017.

The Visual Art Forums are presented by the Audain Faculty of Visual Art + 
Material Practice at Emily Carr University. This talk is co-presented with the 
Charles H. Scott Gallery.

Terms Of Use is a newspaper that focuses on the relationship between artists and the 
institutions they inhabit, serve, promote and critique. The first issue gives voice to 
people loosely affiliated with the Emily Carr community and discusses the repercussions 
of the school's relocation to the new campus. The name of the publication references 
the customer-product agreement and examines the fluidity with which one must 
alternate between the roles of consumer, producer and facilitator. The readers of the 
paper are encouraged to participate in the dissemination of the publication: when ripped 
down the middle, each page functions as a poster (tape not provided).

Terms Of Use is free but the supply is limited. Pick up a copy at READ bookstore or 
221a or Spare Room or message termsofuseanonymous@gmail.com and we will 
deliver the publication to you. Issue I features writing or "text art" by five local 
superstars, S. J., P. A., J. D., A. K. and R. C. Grab a copy to find out who they are and 
what the fuss is all about!"

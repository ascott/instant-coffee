Call To Professional Emerging Artists! LAUNCH! Festival 2016

SFU Woodward's Cultural Programs and 149 Arts Society proudly announced the
fourth annual call to artists for the 2016 LAUNCH! Festival. Seeking
innovative, new performance works by emerging artists, the festival aims to
mentor new talent that dares to push conventional boundaries. Selected
applicants are paid to perform in a cabaret-style showcase in front of
professional, industry mentors. Performers will receive live insightful
feedback from a panel of top industry experts. All applicants are welcome
to audit mentorship workshops. Applications close May 24th. 

All applicants (even if not selected for the festival) will also be invited
to attend a day-long career building workshop the next day, led by industry
mentors, on the topics of: how to survive in the arts, developing a "plan
B' strategy, financial planning, marketing, and building your online
profile. Past mentors have included choreographer Vanessa Goodman, Jim
Smith, producer of Dancehouse; award-winning filmmakers Mina Shum and Kevin
Eastwood; and award-winning musician Veda Hille. 

Artists from across the Lower Mainland in the areas of performance (dance,
music, spoken word, theatre, or new forms), and media/film (fiction,
documentary, experimental) are encouraged to apply with their original
submissions of 8 min or less. The submission guidelines and application
form for applicants can be found at sfuwoodwards.ca

Application Deadline: May 24, 2016, 5pm. Festival Dates: June 18th & 19th, 2016
Where: SFU's Goldcorp Centre for the Arts, 149 W. Hastings Street. 
Application is free!
For further information contact Festival Coordinator Simon at

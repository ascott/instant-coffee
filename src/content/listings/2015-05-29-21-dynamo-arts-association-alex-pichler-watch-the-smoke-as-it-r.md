Watch The Smoke As It Rises From The Pipes
New work by Alex Pichler
Organized by Vanessa Brown

Opening June 4th, 8-11pm
June 5th-14th by appointment

Dynamo Arts Association
Suite 103 - 30 East 6th Ave
Vancouver, BC, Coast Salish Territories

Shapes and lines guide you along the surface. Volumes of color, what
happens when it's spread thin, or left opaque and bold?

I've become increasingly interested in an abrupt, finite method of mark
making. There is something exciting in that immediacy; the structural
potential of a certain fragility. Open spaces can have an architecture, a
weight is poised there.

-AP

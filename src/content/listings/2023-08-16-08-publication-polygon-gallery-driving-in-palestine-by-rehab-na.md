BOOK LAUNCH: DRIVING IN PALESTINE BY REHAB NAZZAL
AUGUST 20, 2023 AT 6:00 PM TO 7:30 PM

The Polygon Gallery, in partnership with Or Gallery and the Morris and Helen Belkin
Art Gallery, is pleased to present the West Coast launch of Driving in Palestine, a new
photo book by artist Rehab Nazzal. Join us
on Sunday, August 20th at 6pm for a
conversation on this timely project between Nazzal and artist Jayce Salloum, featuring
never-before-seen photographs from Nazzal's ongoing research, followed by a book
signing.

Doors at 5:30pm
Conversation at 6pm
Book signing at 7pm

Polygon Gallery
101 Carrie Cates Court
North Vancouver, BC V7M 3J4

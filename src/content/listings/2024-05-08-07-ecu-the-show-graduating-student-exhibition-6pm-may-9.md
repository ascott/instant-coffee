The Show at Emily Carr University

WHAT
Emily Carr University of Art + Design (ECU) will be holding its annual, highly
anticipated student art, media and design exhibition, known as The Show.

The Show is a hybrid in-person and interactive virtual exhibition of work from over
350 graduating ECU students.

More information, an online gallery, and dynamic media screen schedule can be
found at www.ecuad.ca/events/the-show-2024.

WHEN
May 9 | Opening Night | 6-11pm
Friday, May
10-23, 2024 - 8 am to 11 pm
Closed on Victoria Day (May 20)

WHERE
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
520 East 1st Avenue
Vancouver

Visitors can access the exhibition from the south-west entrance of the ECU campus
building, facing Great Northern Way. The exhibition area includes the Libby Leshgold
Gallery and runs along the full length of the main hallway as well as inside the
Library + Learning Commons.

COST
Free admission

ONLINE
People unable to attend in person can explore the online gallery at
theshow.ecuad.ca
beginning May 9.

Total Eclipse of the Art
Fundraiser Auction:
Total Eclipse of the Art
Friday, February 18, 2011 8PM
$15 entry/ Free for members (Become a member!)

You are invited to the 2011 Access Art Auction fundraiser! Please 
join us on Friday, February 18th for Total Eclipse of the Art, a 
memorable fundraising event that promises to be full of 80s style 
glamour. The auction this year features over 36 works of art from 
local and internationally renowned artists. A limited edition of thirty 
signed posters by Sobey-nominated artist Brendan Fernandes will 
also be available at the event. Total Eclipse of the Art will feature live 
and silent auctions and raffle prizes, and the evening will culminate 
in the auctioning off of a 1976 Chevy Malibu Classic with special 
artist add-ons! Following the auction festivities will be a retro dance 
party with all of your favourite 80s hits.

Proceeds from the event will help support Access' operations and 
programming. Access is uniquely committed to fostering critical and 
emerging creative practices, and the support of members and 
donors over the past twenty years have made it possible for the 
gallery to present a diverse range of locally and internationally 
renowned exhibitions and programming. Help us to continue to 
provide the Vancouver arts community with an inclusive space for 
the presentation of some of the most compelling and engaging 
practices this city has to offer!

The auction this year will feature works by: Diyan Achjadi, Joshua 
Bartholomew and Cedric Meister, Jesse Birch and Jesse Gray, 
Raymond Boisjoly, Fabiola Carranza, Roy Caussy, Christine 
D’Onofrio, Lucien Durey, Lisa Fedorak, Brendan Fernandes, Jacob 
Gleeson, Lee Henderson, Julian Hou, Paul Kajander, David Khang, 
Lois Klassen, Vanessa Kwan, Laiwan, Gwenessa Lam, Evan Lee, 
Tiziana LeMelia, Marilou Lemmens & Richard Ibghy, Daylen 
Luchsinger, Elizabeth Milton, Tegan Moore, Michael Morris, Heidi 
Nagtegaal, Ryan Peter, Ed Pien, Samuel Roy-Bois, Dan Siney, Ben 
Skinner, Lucas Soi, Ron Tran, Shirley Wiebe, Gu Xiong, and Sascha 
Yamashita.

Highlight Auction Item
The highlight auction item for this year's event will be Amy Nugent's 
1976 Chevy Malibu Classic! 

Details: 83,000 miles. Second owner. Four-door. White with blue 
interior. 350, V-8 engine. Stacked quad headlights and 
crosshatched grille. Automatic transmission. Original window glass 
in excellent condition. New belts, battery, back brakes, indicator and 
alternator. Aircared. Runs great.

The car features a new driver's manual designed by Michael 
Cadamia with an accompanying mixed tape to the manual by Jeff 
Khonsary to be played on the SONY "Radio Cassette-Corder CFM-
10" that comes with the car, and two God's Eyes on the rearview 
mirror and a Tektite Power Crystal in the ashtray by Heidi Nagtegaal. 
Helen Reed, Hannah Jickling and Jen Kovach have designed a 
pizza crust keychain. The Malibu will be auctioned off during the live 
auction at 10:30pm.

Live auctioneer: Curtis Grahauer

Absentee/Phone bids accepted.

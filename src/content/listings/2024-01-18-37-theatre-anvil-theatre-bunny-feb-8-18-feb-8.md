Bunny
Written by Hannah Moscovitch
Produced by The Search Party

February 8-18, 2024 | Anvil Theatre | 777 Columbia Street, New Westminster

Opening Night: Friday, February 9 at 7:30pm

Preview: Thursday, February 8 at 7:30pm

Performance Times: February 10, 11, 15, 16, & 17 at 7:30pm

Matinees: February 10, 17, & 18 at 2:00pm

Tickets: $36.50 - $46.50 at ticketsnw.ca or 604.521.5050

Anvil Theatre is thrilled to present Hannah Moscovitchs
Bunny from Thursday, February 8 to Sunday, February 18, 2024.

Moving from object towards agent, Sorrel (Emma Slipp) discovers the power of her
own desire. Armed only with Victorian Literature and an unconventional upbringing,
she struggles to find her way. With Bunny, Moscovitch dives into societal
inhibitions,
desire, and sex.

In most love stories, a man is pursuing a woman who is hesitating, says Moscovitch.
In that structure, the man is the protagonist and the woman is the antagonist. We
have two thousand years of that. Bunny is a whole rewriting of the structure.

First produced at The Cultch in 2022 by Mindy Parfitts The Search Party, Bunny
received eight Jessie Richardson Theatre Award nominations and won four,
including Best Production and Best Performance by a Leading Actor.

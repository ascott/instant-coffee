Film Screening: from the river to the sea
Co-presented with Cinema Workers for Palestine

Tuesday, January 23, 7:30PM - 10:30PM
The Cinematheque - 1131 Howe St
Reserve tickets through Zeffy
Suggested donation $10, no one turned away for lack of funds.

Featuring:

R21 aka Restoring Solidarity (2022)
directed by Mohanad Yaqubi
run time: 71 min.

Hreash House (2004)
directed by Rosalind Nashashibi
run time: 20 min.

UNIT/PITT is pleased to support Cinema Workers for Palestine (CWP) in hosting a
screening of Palestinian films, under the banner of from the river to the sea, a global
collective initiative established by a group of displaced media artists and activists all
in solidarity with Palestine This screening will take place
at The Cinematheque on
January 23 at 7:30pm.

All proceeds from ticket sales and subsequent donations will go to the artists, as well
as the Palestinian Youth Movement.

Films are in Arabic, English, and Japanese with English subtitles. For more
information contact cinemaworkersforpalestine@gmail.com.

Cinema Workers for Palestine (CWP) is a collective of cinema workers based on the
unceded territory of the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh Nations. We aim
to address a gap in programming in our cinemas by exhibiting moving image works
in solidarity with Palestine. In the climate of censorship within the arts, academia,
and media landscapes regarding the ongoing genocide and apartheid system
enacted by the state of Israel, CWP believes it is crucial to foreground Palestinian
perspectives through cinema while emphasizing international solidarity. As art is a
crucial facet of social movements, we commit to supporting all artists involved in our
events in order to widen the reach of
Palestinian art and culture, showcasing
resistance, joy, and creativity. Working from a foundation of anti-imperialism, anti-
colonialism and anti-zionism, we endeavor to inspire critical thought and further
action through film screenings, lectures and discussion.

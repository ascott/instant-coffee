Education: Luxurious Oil Pastels, Fridays, May 30 - June 27, from 12:30 pm - 2:30 pm,
at Roundhouse Community Arts and Recreation Centre
ID Code: 551690
Call: (604) 713-1800
Learn to enrich oil pastel application techniques and surfaces for creating luxurious
silky oil pastel drawings at this hands-on social activity. Immerse yourself in this
vibrant, luscious and tactile world of oil pastels. This is open to all skill levels, from oil
pastel beginners exploring the medium for the first time to seasoned artists seeking to
refine their oil pastel techniques. Using a variety of approaches to create artworks
while exploring color, mark-making, still life, landscape, portraiture and abstraction.
And most importantly we will have FUN! Open to all skill levels. Supply list will be
provided at registration. Immerse your creative capabilities and trust the creative
process with Faye Fayerman

A new gut psycho-biome

a sonic performance piece by Maliv Khondaker and Jae Lew.

Vancouver, B.C. February 19th, 2022

a new gut psycho-biome is a sonic performance piece alongside a collection of mixed
media works that will be on display following the end of Maliv Khondaker and Jae Lews
month-long Pod Residency at The James Black Gallery. Through their work in the
residency, they explore the connection between technology, the gut, and the mind. They
will use quotidian and culturally significant materials such as ancestral food and nostalgic
objects as resistors in building square wave synthesizers for a sound performance. They
will document the process and keep a reflection journal that will be used to create an
artist book. The publication will serve as a meditation on the themes that will surface
throughout the creative process, through exploring the interconnectedness in
tech/mind/gut. What culture is bred
in the petri dish? What is sterile? What is alive?

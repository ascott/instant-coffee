Making art with friends exhibition opening
Thanks
Opening: Making Art With Friends - An Oppenheimer Park Artists Exhibition May 1rst
5:30 to 7 PM

Place: Connection Salon Gallery, 609 Helmcken

Connection Salon Artists Collectives mission is to provide low barrier opportunities to
artists that are underrepresented and have lived experience with mental health. This is
a collaboration with Oppenheimer Park Fieldhouse.

12 visual artists will be on show April 25th to June 23rd Join us for the Meet the Artists
event May 1st, 2025, from 5:30 to 7:00 PM with Music with Matt Kennedy.

The exhibition is supported by the City of Vancouver, the Connection Salon Collective
and the Gathering Place Community Center.

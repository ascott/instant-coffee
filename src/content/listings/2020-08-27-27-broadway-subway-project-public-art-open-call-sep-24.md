Organization
Transportation Investment Corporation (BC Ministry of Transportation and Infrastructure)

Extended Deadline
September 24, 2020

Call for Artists Description
Transportation
Investment Corporation, on behalf of the Ministry of Transportation and
Infrastructure, is seeking to commission five professional artists or artist teams to create
five site-specific, highly integrated public artworks; one per station for five SkyTrain
stations for the Broadway Subway Project. Each station offers a unique public art
opportunity. The public artworks will be integrated with the new transit stations and
respond to the surrounding public realm design and station contexts.

There are opportunities for community-engaged art, integrated 2D artwork, suspended 3D
artwork, applied 2D/3D artwork and light and projection artwork.

This opportunity is open to all artists and/or artist teams currently residing in Canada.
Indigenous artists, emerging artists, and other artist groups who are under-represented
are all encouraged to apply.

To view the call, interested artists should visit the posting on BC Bid here:
http://www.bcbid.gov.bc.ca/open.dll/submitLogin?language=En&disID=45347246

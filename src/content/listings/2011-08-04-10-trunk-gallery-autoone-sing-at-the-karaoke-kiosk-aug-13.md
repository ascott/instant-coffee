Sing! at the Karaoke Kiosk
Urban Republic invites you to sing and share a tune at 700-block Granville
Street on Saturday, August 13, 20 and 27 from 4 - 8pm. Sing! celebrates and
shares the pop cultures of Vancouver's diverse population. Come sing and
hear what grooves in Cantonese, English, Filipino, French, Japanese,
Mandarin, Hindi and Spanish. The Karaoke Kiosk is free and open to all. 
www.urbanrepublic.ca/sing/

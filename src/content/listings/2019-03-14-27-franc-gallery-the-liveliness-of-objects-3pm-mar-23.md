ARTIST TALK: Saturday, March 23rd, 3 pm The liveliness of objects 

A Conversation with Kate Hennessy, Associate Professor, School of Interactive Art and 
Technology, Simon Fraser University and Dina Gonzlez Mascar about the liveliness of 
objects and their place in marking the passage of life.
March 23rd, 2019 3pm

Dina Gonzalez Mascaro: Vessels are Never Empty 

From the mountain structures she "entered" to meditate, to the vessels she molded to 
contain the transformative energy, Gonzlez Mascar has built a delimited space that 
acts not only as a container but is also a way to put in place the ontological relationship 
between life death and the body, and finally to let go

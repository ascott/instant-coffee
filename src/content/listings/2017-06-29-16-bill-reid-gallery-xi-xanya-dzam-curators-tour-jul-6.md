Xi xanya dzam Curators Tour
Thursday, July 6 | 3pm
Free with Bill Reid Gallery Admission 

Co-curators Beth Carter and Lou-ann Neel will guide you through this remarkable 
collection of work by the 11 recipients of the BC Lifetime Creative Achievement Award 
for First Nations Art. This exhibition offers both an intimate look at the diversity of 
Indigenous cultures in the Northwest Coast and explores how achievement and 
excellence are viewed in traditional and contemporary First Nations art.

Picturing Arctic Modernity - North Baffin Drawings from 1964

Opening Reception: Thursday, November 15, 7-9pm 

Join the Burnaby Art Gallery for the opening reception of Picturing Arctic Modernity " 
North Baffin Drawings from 1964. The evening will feature opening remarks, 
followed by 
a reception.

This exhibition features 50 original drawings created in 1964 by Inuit men, women 
and youth of North Baffin Island. These drawings offer rare first-person perspectives 
of Inuit communities transitioning from traditional nomadic hunting camps to 
permanent settlements. Picturing Arctic Modernity is a travelling exhibition 
developed by the Agnes Etherington Art Centre at Queens University and the 
Canadian Museum of History, with the assistance of the Pond Inlet Archives, 
Ilisaqsivik, Ittaq and Piqqusilirivvik, and funded in part by the Museums Assistance 
Program of the Government of Canada. This exhibition will be on at the Burnaby Art 
Gallery until January 13, 2019.

Instant Coffee presents

Speak and Sing: Contemporary Karaoke Practice

Hosted by ERIC FREDERICKSEN

Saturday, FEBURARY 28, 2009, 10pm

Seattle curator Eric Fredericksen will host an evening of karaoke, using
breaks between songs to suggest parallels between karaoke practice and art
practice. Creating a social space that collapses the distinction between
production and reception, karaoke is a site for working out the promise and
problems of participatory practices."

Please come listen a little and sing a lot. This will be the best Karaoke
lecture ever!

Eric Frederickson is the former art critic for the Seattle Stranger and is
currently the director of Western Bridge, Seattle. He has organized
exhibitions in Canada, the US. and Europe, and has written for Architecture
magazine, the Organ, Nest, Metropolis, Hermenaut, Studio 360, and Art on
Paper.

As part of the exhibition Assume Nothing: New Social Practice presented by
the Art Gallery of greater Victoria, www.aggv.bc.ca <www.aggv.bc.ca>

'Pin It To Me' at Black and Yellow Gallery, Opening Thursday Dec 6th, 7-11pm.

Description:

The launch exhibition of new Director/Curator Allison Mander-Wionzek in the 
form of a group show that explores the role of performance in the development of 
virtual identity, and the projection of taste in connection with pop culture's 
appropriation of a 'curated' space. Featuring work by Arseni Khamzin, Erica 
Wilk, Kristina Fiedrich and Tom Hsu. 
Rm 106 at The Waldorf Hotel.

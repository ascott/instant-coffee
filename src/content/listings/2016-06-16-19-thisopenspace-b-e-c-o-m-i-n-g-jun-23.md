Presented by Vancouver Artists Charlotte Spafford and Flory 
Huang, B E C O M I N G is a one-night showcase for their latest 
visual art collections. 

About Charlotte Spafford:
Charlotte is an abstract painter and collage artist whose work 
explores spontanaeity, play, and imagination as it relates to her 
subjective experiences of happiness. She uses a mixed-media 
approach to give her paintings a sculptural and organic quality, and 
loves using juicy colours and glitter liberally. B E C O M I N G is a 
showcase of Charlotte's latest creative explorations, including 
fragmentation and collage of her paintings. 

About Flory Huang:
Flory is currently working primarily in 2-dimensional media with ink 
and paint. After last year's collection, 'Time to Bloom', B E C O M I 
N G is the next progression forward and as a celebration with her 
childhood best friend and fellow local artist. While many pieces still 
involve botany-based musings, chosen on floral symbolism and 
highly detailed texture, this next body of work incorporates 
surrealist portraiture of the human anatomy as well as more graphic 
inspired works featuring handlettering. 

Thisopenspace, 434 Columbia st, Vancouver BC
Thursday, June 23rd, 6-11pm

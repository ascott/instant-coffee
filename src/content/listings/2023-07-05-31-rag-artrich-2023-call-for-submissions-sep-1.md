ARTRICH 2023

EXHIBITION INFORMATION:
The Richmond Arts Coalition, with support from the Richmond Art Gallery and
financial support from the City of Richmond and the BC Arts Council, is pleased to
present ArtRich 2023, our fifth juried exhibition for local artists at the Richmond Art
Gallery. This exhibition will take place from November 24 to December 31, 2023.
ArtRich 2023 provides an opportunity for emerging and established artists from
Richmond, the Lower Mainland, and nearby communities
to exhibit together and
celebrate local talent. Entries are not expected to fit a specific theme"we want to see
the work you are most excited to share!

ENTRY INFORMATION:
The Richmond Arts Coalition (RAC) invites artists with a connection to the Lower
Mainland to enter a maximum of two works per artist (regardless of medium) for
adjudication. We welcome original artwork in a variety of mediums, including
painting, drawing, 3D works, textile/fibre arts, photography, video, and digital media.
Artworks must be made between 2021 and 2023.

Only one work per artist will be selected for the exhibition. Each artist submitting
work must be or become a member of the Richmond Arts Coalition. The membership
fee is $10 per year (or $45 if you choose a five-year membership). Richmond-
resident youth artists (under the age of 18) who apply for ArtRich 2023 will receive a
year-long free membership with the Richmond Arts Coalition. Please note: you must
be an individual member of RAC, not just a
member of a group that is a RAC
member.

ENTRY COST:
All applicants must be members with the Richmond Arts Coalition to apply. Thanks to
grants from the Province of British Columbia via the BC Arts Council, there are no
other entry fees to apply. Entry forms will not be processed from entrants until they
have an active membership with RAC. Memberships can be acquired at
richmondartscoalition.com/membership .

ARTIST EXHIBITION FEES:
Thanks to a kind and generous one time Impact grant from the BC Arts Council,
each selected ArtRich 2023 artist will receive an honorarium.

AWARDS:
Once again, there will be Peoples Choice awards for the event, with winners chosen
by the public and announced at the end of the exhibition. There will be three $150
awards for three artists with the most votes.

CRITERIA FOR ADJUDICATION:
Jurors will make selections based on the following:

Originality, technical execution, and creativity of work, exploration, and approach
Balanced presentation of a range of styles, subject matter, and media

SUBMISSION OF WORK FOR ADJUDICATION:
Only online submissions will be accepted for adjudication. Artists are permitted to
submit up to two works of art for adjudication. Before emailing your images, please
save them as JPEG files. Only the image of your artwork should show (no frames or
extraneous backgrounds). Image files should be sized at large enough dimensions
to be properly adjudicated (at least 1000px wide or tall), and with a file size smaller
than 6 MB. Digital media works should be no longer than 10 minutes and saved as a
standard video file type. Digital media works (such as videos) can be provided via
online submission form, weblink, or email attachment. Direct any
questions to
richmondartscoalition@gmail.com . All artists will be notified via email about whether
their work has been selected by September 20, 2023.

SELECTED WORKS:
Richmond Art Gallery will take the utmost care in handling the work. Artwork must be
dry and ready to be displayed, with (for 2D work) a hanging device (such as a
hanging sleeve, cleat, or loops/rings on the back of the work). Interactive computer-
based artwork must be presented on a supplied computer. Artwork must be able to
be carried easily by one person and fit through a standard door. Works exceeding
four feet in width (including the frame), or of excessive weight or height, will be
refused entry by Richmond Art Gallery staff at the time of delivery. Multi panel or
multi component pieces (i.e. diptychs, triptychs) will be accepted; however the whole
work cannot exceed the four-foot width limitation as outlined above.

DROP OFF OF WORKS SELECTED:
Selected works must be dropped off at the Richmond Art Gallery on
November 20,
between 3pm " 7pm.

EXHIBITION:
The exhibition runs from November 24 to December 31, 2023. All artists who submit
pieces for the exhibition (not merely those whose pieces were accepted) will be
invited via email to join us at the ArtRich 2023 Opening Reception at the Richmond
Art Gallery on November 24 from 7 pm to 9 pm.

SALE OF WORK:
Artists will be asked if they would like their contact information to be given to
potential purchasers, should a purchasing inquiry be made. If the artist agrees, the
Richmond Arts Coalition will forward their contact information to the potential
purchaser.

INSURANCE:
Richmond Art Gallery will insure all artworks while on site.

PICKUP OF WORK FOLLOWING EXHIBITION:
Work must be collected from the Richmond Art Gallery between 2 pm " 7pm on
January 4, 2024. Work must be picked up on this date, unless other arrangements
have been made with the RAG. Otherwise, the artist will be charged a storage fee of
$5.00/day/item. Work not picked up
within 2 weeks will be discarded. When picking
up work, please bring the necessary materials to wrap and pack works " the Gallery
does not supply this.

PUBLICITY:
By entering this call, permission is given to the Richmond Arts Coalition and the
Richmond Art Gallery to photograph accepted works for publicity and documentary
purposes, including promotion of the exhibition on their websites and social media,
as well as other media outlets.

EXCLUSIVE NO. 3 ROAD ART COLUMN OPPORTUNITY FOR ARTRICH
APPLICANTS:
In a partnership with Richmond Public Art, Richmond-resident artists who apply for
ArtRich may also apply for a paid artist opportunity to present artwork in the No.3
Road Art Column Exhibition from February 1, 2024 to July 31, 2024. Three artists will
be selected and provided with a $1,500 artist fee. Printing and installation costs will
be the responsibility of the City of Richmond. Artists will be asked to apply by
October 5th, 2023, with images of their proposed four (4) artworks
for the exhibition
and a Statement of Interest (incorporating artist bio and artwork statement). For
more information about the No.3 Road Art Columns, visit:
www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/whatsnew . The Richmond Arts Coalition and the
Richmond Art Gallery agree to help selected artists digitize their work, if needed.

16mm Basics: Introduction to the Bolex Camera

Sunday 12 March 20172:00pm-5:00pm
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander Street (Ironworks basement)

$40 + taxes | General Admission
$35 + taxes | Concession
$30 + taxes | Member Admission

REGISTER:
[https://16mm-basics-bolex-mar.eventbrite.ca](https://16mm-basics-bolex-mar.eventbrite.ca)

A 3 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and 
practice of exposing film with a Bolex 16mm film camera

Elements include:

The mechanics and chemistry of exposing film
Understanding lenses
Handling film
Loading and unloading film in light and dark conditions
Shooting in natural light

Film shot in this workshop will be processed at the 16mm Basics: Intro to Hand
Processing workshop on Sunday 26 Mar. If interested in both components, 
please register for the Hand Processing workshop via the following link:

[https://16mm-basics-hand-processing-mar.eventbrite.ca](https://16mm-basics-hand-processing-mar.eventbrite.ca)

2010 VANCOUVER PRIDE PARADE

Call to Artists

Creation of a Grand Prize Trophy for the Vancouver Pride
Parade

The Vancouver Pride Society (VPS) is seeking a qualified artist
to create a trophy to be awarded to the best entry in the annual
Pride Parade.

Proposal Guidelines

Theme:
Artists are asked to incorporate imagery reflecting the Pride
Parade story (protest, diversity of LGBT people and
celebration) and its current theme to ï¿½ï¿½Educate, Liberate,
Celebrateï¿½ï¿½.

Materials and design:
The trophy must be of durable, shatter-proof material. Metal is
preferred but other solutions may be considered. The design
should imply the trophy is a grand prize and have the scale
and colour appropriate to this status. The design should
include a solution for showing the name of the trophy on its
body, and names of the annual winners on plaques mounted
on a wooden or similar base.

Timing:
Proposal are to be received at the Pride Society Offices on or
before Wednesday June 23, 2010. A selection panel, including
artists, will choose a finalist on Friday, June 25th , 2010. The
finished work is to be completed, ready for presentation, by July
26th, 2010

Proposals may delivered in person, my mail or e-mailed in PDF
format, and should include the following:
1. A one-page resume of the artistï¿½s qualification and up to two
pages of images of previous work relevant to this Call.
2. Initial concept sketch, up to two pages..
3. Written description of the concept.
4. Names of fabricators if the proposal requires their services.
5. Sample or models are welcome, and will be returned to the
artist.

All submission will beheld in confidence, and artists retain the
copyright to their work. The selected artist will receive 15% of
the contract as a design fee to develop the concept to a final
design, which will require approval before commencing
fabrication.

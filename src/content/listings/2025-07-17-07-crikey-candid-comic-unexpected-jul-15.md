Crikey! Candid, Comic, Unexpected"Street-Inspired Photos & Art

Clare Palmer and Anne McCarthy use photography as a starting point for a fresh
perspective on everyday life. This unusual mix of street photography and digital art
encourages viewers to reconsider what they see through humor, irony, and unexpected
details. Crikey! opens Tuesday July 15 at the Gage Gallery Arts Collective at 19
Bastion Square, Victoria, BC. The Gallery is open Tuesday through Sunday from 11 am
to 5 pm. Crikey! runs until Sunday August 3. Clare and Anne will host a casual non-
alcoholic opening reception on Saturday July 19 from 3-5 pm. For further information
please visit the Gage Gallery website at https://gagegallery.ca/ or contact Clare Palmer
at clarepalmerarts@gmail.com.

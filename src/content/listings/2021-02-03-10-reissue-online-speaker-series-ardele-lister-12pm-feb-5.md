Bio:
Born in Calgary, Ardele Lister came to filmmaking in 1973 when she co-founded the
womens film collective, ReelFeelings, in
Vancouver. ReelFeelings' first film, So
Wheres My Prince Already?, a tragicomedy about love and marriage, directed by
Ardele, was selected for the International Festival of Womens Films in NYC in 76.
Later that year, Lister moved to NYC where she still resides.

From her days in ReelFeelings through the 80s Listers work focused primarily on
stories of women, historically under-represented as subjects in media. These works
include Sugar Daddy (1980) and Split (1981) with a sequel to Split entitled It Happens
to the Best of Us (1989)-both featuring Vancouvers Susy James. In 1986 she made
Zoes Car (shot at the Western Front), a humorous look at the American Dream of
receiving something for nothing.

Defining identities in our globalized culture and the role of media in shaping those
identities characterize other dimensions in her research. From her point of view as a
Canadian who has lived in the United States for many years, Behold the Promised
Land (1991) and Conditional Love (1997)
focus on nationalisms, provincialisms, at how
borders affect identities, at how we come to know who we are through the
media/propaganda of our places and histories.

After writing for The Province and for Vanguard, Lister founded and edited Criteria, a
critical review of the arts in Vancouver, which published from 1975 until 1978. In 1977,
while working for the Foundation for Independent Video and Film in New York City, she
founded and edited The Independent, the first monthly publication for independent
video and filmmakers, still in publication online.

At the School of Visual Arts, NYC, and at Rutgers, the State University of New Jersey,
Lister taught media for many years. She is currently Professor Emerita at Rutgers
University.

Her works are in the permanent collections of the Museum of Modern Art (NY), the
Centre Pompidou (Paris), the National Gallery of Canada (Ottawa), the Stedelijk
Museum (Amsterdam) and the Kunsthalle (Berlin). [http://ardelelister.com/](http://ardelelister.com/)

About:
ReIssue is an
interdisciplinary art writing platform focused on shaping and sustaining a
contemporary west coast discourse rooted in critical engagement with experimental art
practices. ReIssue is a partnership between UNIT PITT Society for Art and Critical
Awareness and VIVO Media Arts Centre, funded through a Vancouver Foundation
Systems Change Grant. [https://reissue.pub/](https://reissue.pub/)

ReIssue operates on unceded Musqueam, Tsleil-Waututh, and Squamish homelands
known as Vancouver, British Columbia.

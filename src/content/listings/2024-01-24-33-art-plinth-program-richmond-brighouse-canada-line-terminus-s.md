The City of Richmond Public Art Program seeks artists or artist teams to create a
temporary public art installation at the
terminus Canada Line station: Richmond-
Brighouse, located at No.3 Road and Cook Road in Richmond, British Columbia.

Richmond-based artists, including those from equity-seeking communities, are
encouraged to apply.

Artist Budget: $50,000 CAD

Eligibility: Artists residing in British Columbia

Deadline: February 29, 2024

Completion: Spring 2025

For more information and to apply please visit:
www.richmond.ca/culture/howartworks/publicart/artist-calls

The Artist Salon Series features art professionals leading inspirational artist talks
and professional development for visual artists.

Our guest presenter this month is illustrator and mural artist Priscilla Yu. Learn what
it takes to be a mural artist and ask your questions about working in this medium
during the live webinar.

Yu will share some of her past mural experiences and offer tips on how to get started
working in this field, things to consider when planning a mural project, and how to
transform your studio painting practice into working in public spaces.

About the
Presenter:
Priscilla Yu is a Vancouver-based visual artist, illustrator and muralist, painting over
twenty murals locally and internationally over the past few years. With a personal
style involving abstracted perspective and a geometric language, she creates worlds
that dwell in a strange gravity.

grunt gallery is inviting submissions to our open calls for community engagement! In
response to the Covid-19 pandemic, we launched the Mount Pleasant Community Art
Screen Community Diary. Each week we send out a Community Diary call related to the
new normal via social media, Instant Coffee, and our newsletter. We invite community
members to send in their responses to communications[at]grunt[dot]ca or tag us in their
social media posts - selected
submissions will be featured on the MPCAS!

Details and submission guidelines can be found at https://www.mpcas.ca/programming/

Our Latest Community Diary Calls:
Homeschool confidential - Confessions, highs and lows, cute moments, rants, raves for
parents, kids, students, educators working at home.

My new friends - Pets, imaginary friends, sidekicks. Shout outs, very short stories. Make
a figurine of your new imaginary friend. Tell us about them.

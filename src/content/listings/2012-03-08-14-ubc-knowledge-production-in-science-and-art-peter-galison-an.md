The Joan Carlisle-Irving Lecture Series 2011-2012

Department of Art History, Visual Art, & Theory

"Distributors: Knowledge Production in Science and Art,"
by Peter Galison and Caroline A. Jones.

Peter Galison is the Pellegrino University Professor in History of 
Science and Physics at Harvard University. Caroline Jones is Professor 
of Modern and Contemporary Art at MIT.

Date: Monday, March 12th 2012
Time: 5:30pm
Location: Lasserre Building, Room 102, 6333 Memorial Road, UBC 
Vancouver Campus
Directions: http://www.maps.ubc.ca/PROD/index_detail.php?

factory to the lab/studio and how knowledge production has become 
redistributed in the digital age.

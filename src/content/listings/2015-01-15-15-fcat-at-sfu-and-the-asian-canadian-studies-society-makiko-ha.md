Public lecture by Makiko Hara
Fictive Communities Asia: Konganecho Bazaar 2014 and Air Yonago 2014

Monday, January 19
7:00 to 9:00 pm
Room HC 1600
Harbour Centre
Simon Fraser University
515 W Hastings Street

Introduced by Naomi Sawada
(Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery)

All Welcome.

Makiko Hara will discuss two projects she curated in Japan in 2014. While
very different from one another, both were intimately linked to the
everyday life of the exhibition sites, engaging with street life and local
people. The 7th Edition of "Koganecho Bazaar" was held in conjunction with
the Yokohama Triennale. For her project, Hara went on an intensive
research trip to 4 cities in South East Asia (Manila, Ho Chi Minh City,
Chiang Mai, and Yogyakarta). She invited 38 groups/collectives of
contemporary artists from Asia and Canada to Yokohama's 2 month
artist-in-residence program where they explored personal and collective
voices rooted in long and complex colonial histories while reflecting on
the rapidly transforming social and political dynamics of Asian Pacific states.

"AIR Yonago" was an artist-in-residence project that took place in Yonago
City, Tottori Prefecture located on Japan's south Atlantic coast. Hara
invited 3 artists including Vancouver-based artists Khan Lee and Cindy
Mochizuki to create site-specific projects. The works revealed surprising
geographical similarities as well as immigration histories between Yonago
and Canada's Pacific North West.

bio:
Makiko Hara is an independent curator, artist and writer from Tokyo. She
has worked as a curator and project coordinator for numerous international
exhibitions, including the Yokohama Triennale (2001/2005), Echigo Tsumari
Art Triennale (2003), Koganecho Bazaar (2014), and AIR Yonago (2014).
Based in Vancouver since 2007, Hara was the curator of Centre A - Vancouver
International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art from 2007 to 2013. In 2013
and 2014, Hara led the Canada Council Asia Pacific Visual Arts Delegation.
She is the curator for "Crossed" by Ahmad Tabrizi at grunt gallery (January
15 to February 21, 2015).

This talk is sponsored by the Faculty of Communication, Art and

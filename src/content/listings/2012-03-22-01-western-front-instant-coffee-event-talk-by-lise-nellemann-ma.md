Lise Nellemann is an artist and one of the founders of SparwasserHQ 
a non-commercial artist run space in Berlin. She has been co-running 
its operation since 2000. Sparwasser is characterized by an open 
process of participation from a select group of artists and theorists, 
influencing the program as well as the day to day running of the space. 
Theory and organization result from this artistic collaboration. New 
projects grow out of an intense exchange of ideas, leading to constant 
questioning and reformulating of Sparwasser's strategies. The project 
aims to examine contemporary society and the conditions of artistic 
production by confronting various artistic and curatorial practices. 
Through Sparwasser Lise has initiated projects such as I Know the 
World, Dying to Make a Living and hosted the Curating Degree Zero 
Archive. In 2005, Lise invited Instant Coffee to take over Sparwasser 
for six weeks, for which the Collective set up a temporary studio and 
held over 20 events and gatherings.

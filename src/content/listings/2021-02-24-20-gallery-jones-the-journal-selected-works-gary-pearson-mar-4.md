Artist: Gary Pearson
Gallery: Gallery Jones
Exhibition title: "The Journal: Selected Works"
Exhibition dates: March 4, 2021- April 3, 2021
Artist Statement:

Gary Pearsons forthcoming exhibition in March 2021 titled The Journal: Selected Works,
will primarily feature a selection of paintings and works on paper from 2020. During this
pandemic year Pearson continued his studio production as always, but has admitted the
impact of the broader discussions and circumstances of the coronavirus did infiltrate his
conceptual and creative process. For example, Portrait Sketch (The Bubble), The
Piano,
and The Balcony, all from 2020, underwent pictorial transitions that, in part, were influenced
by the social consequences of COVID-19.
The artist has said that in general, the tenor of the 2020 art works became noticeably
inflected by a sub-text of quietude and contemplation, however, as those familiar with
Pearsons work will recognize, this characteristic is consistent with his art throughout his
career. Three paintings from 2019, included in this exhibition, are cases in point: The
Journal, from which the exhibition title is derived, Woman on a Park Bench, and The
Pineapple Lamp, exhibit an aura of introspection and calm, and all were produced pre-
pandemic.

While the majority of the works in The Journal: Selected Works are mid-size canvasses,
and feature the human subject as the principal motif, Gallery Jones is also delighted to
include the large landscape painting The Bird Fountain, 2015, one of the four canvasses
and multiple paper works from Pearsons Fountain Series. In
this painting the narrative of
landscape is configured as a (partially) constructed environment, just as in the figurative
works, where a park bench, a chair, or a street serve to, again partially, construct the
human narrative.

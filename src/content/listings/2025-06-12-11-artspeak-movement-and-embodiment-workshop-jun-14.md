Movement and Embodiment Workshop

Join Jotika Chaudhary Samant for a workshop to connect into what roots & grounds
you in this overwhelming and often exhausting world. Lets breathe together, let's sing
together & move our bodies while we reflect on what collective care and wellness feel
like. As we help to activate the space for the exhibition of expansive textile works in
10,000 Flowers Bloom at Artspeak that explore alternative forms of self-representation
and self-portraiture by Black and Brown artists. How can we move with intention and
care for our communities while also holding space for ourselves? How can rest be a
deep care practice, how can we honour and make space for our grief? Join me to
explore these themes through our voices and through movement. All bodies & voices
welcome.

Date: June 14th, 2025, 3:00pm-5:00pm, Location: 233 Carrall St. To join RSVP to
info@artspeak.ca

Instant Coffee presents

ARABESQUE

by NATALIE PURSCHWITZ

Saturday, March 07, 2009, 10pm

Arabesque is a comedic, but beautifully sincere look at the desire for
inspiration through performance, process and play. Vancouver artist and
fashion designer Natalie Purschwitz will be mixing video of her latest
fashion designs made for a special selection of handpicked west coast
sticks. This will be the best stick fashion show ever!

Natalie Purschwitz is the designer for Hunt and Gather, which she
established in 2004. She comes to fashion through the visual arts, having
studied at Emily Carr in Vancouver. Her art works have been exhibited
internationally in Japan and New York, and Hunt and Gather has been
featured in the New York Times, The Wall Street Journal, and on CBC Radio
1. In 2008, she received an honorable mention in Travel and Leisure's
Best In The World Design Awards.

Light Bar is part of the exhibition Assume Nothing: New Social Practice
presented by the Art Gallery of greater Victoria, www.aggv.bc.ca

Playhouse
Curated by Annie Canto and Amelia Epp

Opening Reception: Friday, September 20, 6-8pm
Curator's Tour: Thursday, September 26, 3:30pm
Exhibition Dates: September 20, 2024 - February 27, 2025

Playhouse features artworks from over 20 Canadian artists and presents the Gordon
Smith Gallery as a place to hear stories, meet characters, and transform the
spaces
we inhabit in order to unveil unseen narratives. The exhibition sets the stage for us
to consider ways in which the physical, social, cultural, and imagined qualities of a
place are intertwined.

Textiles and zines from elementary and secondary students intermingle with artwork
from the Artists for Kids Permanent Collection and featured works by artists Whess
Harman, Hannah Jickling and Reed H. Reed, Guna Jenson, and Cindy Mochizuki.

Artists for Kids acknowledges the support of the Canada Council for the Arts.

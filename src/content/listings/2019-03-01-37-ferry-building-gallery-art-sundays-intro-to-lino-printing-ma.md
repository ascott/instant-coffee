Art Sundays: Workshops & Artist Demos at the Music Box, 1564 Argyle 
Ave, West Vancouver

Introduction to Lino Printing with Sandrine Pelissier

Lino printing is a fun, easy and accessible way to get started with 
printing techniques. No previous experience is required for this hands-
on workshop where Sandrine will show you the basics of designing, 
carving and printing by hand. The Art Sundays series is generously 
sponsored by Opus Art Supplies.

Sandrine Pelissier is a North Vancouver-based artist whose work has 
been collected and exhibited extensively in Canada and internationally. 
She is inspired by her West Coast surroundings and paints the BC 
landscape, figures and still life, mixing elements of drawing and 
patterning into her paintings. She is a published author, blog writer, 
instructor and co-founder of the North Shore Art Crawl.

Sunday, March 17, 2"4 p.m. at the Music Box, 1564 Argyle Ave, West 
Vancouver

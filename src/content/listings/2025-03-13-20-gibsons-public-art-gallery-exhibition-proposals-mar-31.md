We are currently accepting proposals to
exhibit at Gibsons Public Art Gallery (GPAG)
in 2026 in the Main Gallery and Eve Smart Gallery. We accept submissions from both
emerging and established artists working in all mediums and styles*. The deadline to
submit your proposal is 4PM on March 31st, 2025. Before applying, please read our
Exhibition Policy and Submission Guidelines.

[https://gpag.ca/submissions/](https://gpag.ca/submissions/)

You may also view a sample Artist-Gallery contract for reference. We strongly
recommend that you submit your application using our online form.

[https://gpag.ca/exhibition-application/](https://gpag.ca/exhibition-application/)

Alternatively, you may apply via Google Drive or USB thumb drive; to do so, you will
need to include a PDF Application Form with your support material. Please note: we
do not accept submissions via email.

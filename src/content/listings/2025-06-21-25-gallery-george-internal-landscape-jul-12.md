Internal Landscape

A Solo Art Exhibition by Grays Luzande

Soft Opening July 9, 2025

Showing from July 9 - 13, 2025

Reception July 12, 2025

About the Exhibition

Internal Landscape is a kaleidoscope look into the world inside me and the world I
am physically in. As these worlds are complex, primary colours and shapes are
deliberately used to portray clarity.

About the Artist

My newest collection, Internal Landscape, will be displayed at The Gallery George
in Vancouver. With new works and pieces spanning the last 15 years, the collection
is a lens into the internal interpretations of life.

Using primary colours and shapes, I organize the chaos of my life onto canvas, with
the hopes of being inspired and inspiring others.

My belief is that everyone is an artist, that art is everything and everywhere. Creating
art is the one thing I do for myself where I dont think about others and their opinions;
it is for me to express and impress myself. I have been an artist all my life, finding
ways to create while growing up in the provinces of the Philippines and now in my
home studio in Surrey.

Come and see my works by visiting the Gallery George at 140 W Hastings Street, in
Vancouver from July 9-13 12:00 pm to 6:00 pm, or at the Reception on July 12th
from 6:00 pm to 9:00 pm.

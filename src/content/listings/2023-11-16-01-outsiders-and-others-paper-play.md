Paper Play: A Collage Exhibition Featuring Zanahoy, John Catron, Sarah Grant
Duff, Torea Frey, Jack Procter, Evie Osborn, Seema Shah, Chris Short, and Geoff
Wonnacott

November 4 - 25, 2023
Outsiders and Others, 716 East Hastings Street, Vancouver

Artist Discussion: November 18 at 2pm online on Zoom
To register, email outsidersandothers@gmail.com and we reply with the Zoom link for
the discussion.

This exhibition features artists from both Canada and the US which came from a call
for artists we posted 6 months ago. We do this on occasion so we could bring some
new artists to our gallery and to share the perspective of these artists in our City.

And even though we live in an age of advanced technology and AI, all the artists
featured in this exhibition cut paper and use glue to make their
artwork.

About Outsiders and Others:
Outsiders and Others is a non-profit art Society who has a mission to bring non-
traditional artists to the forefront. This includes artists that identify as outsider, self-
taught, and artists living with disabilities. www.outsidersandothers.com

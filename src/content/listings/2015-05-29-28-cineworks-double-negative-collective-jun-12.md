Founded in 2004, the Double Negative Collective has become a major presence
on the experimental cinema scene in Montreal and had an undeniable impact
on the shifting landscape of moving-image art and the notion of artist
collectives. Self-financed, the Collective has successfully managed for
more over five years to maintain its own studio with a variety of analogue
filmmaking equipment and to organize experimental film screenings,
performances and artist talks that have inspired artistic communities both
at home and abroad. This collection of films and videos demonstrates the
depth and scope of the diverse approaches taken by members of the
collective, whose varied backgrounds include other artistic practices such
as music, poetry and visual art.

The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Offsite: Polit-Sheer-Form Office

Chinese contemporary art collective Polit-Sheer-Form Office (PSFO) will have its first 
Canadian exhibition Fitness for All at Vancouver Art Gallerys Offsite public art space 
located at 1100 West Georgia Street from November 10, 2018 to March 31, 2019. 
Comprising of artists Hong Hao, Xiao Yu, Song Dong, Liu Jianhua, and curator/critic 
Leng Lin, PFSOs multi-disciplinary projects address the idea of we in a me world. 
The collective will produce a five-piece set of exercise equipment specially designed for 
Vancouver. This work appropriates the publicly funded workout equipment found in city 
squares and residential compounds throughout China. Treating the work as sculpture for 
the betterment of visitors and passersby, PSFO explores the possibilities of shared 
experience through group activities.

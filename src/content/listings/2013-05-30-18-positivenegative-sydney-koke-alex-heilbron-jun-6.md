Sleepover
Sydney Koke & Alex Heilbron
June 6-22nd, Opens June 6, 7-11 pm
Guided by a shared interest in exploring intuitive processes, Koke 
and Heilbron work to uncover and communicate new ideas through 
intimate and bold experimentation with their chosen mediums. 
Nonverbal and abstracted, these works convey information through 
gesture, colour, form, and material, and serve as a portal to a 
complex internal world characterised by fragmentation, absurdity, 
vulnerability, excitement, and frustration. Sleepover features 
paintings, drawings, sculptures, and a collaborative installation.Artists:
Sydney Koke
Sydney Koke is a MFA candidate in the interdisciplinary program at 
the School for the Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser University. 
Having previously worked and studied as a neuroscientist, Koke is 
interested in addressing and challenging perceptions of subjectivity 
and objectivity in thinking about and describing the world. Working 
primarily in vinyl, resin, and airbrushed enamel, her visual language
arises from an emphasis on the embodied and intuitive aspects of 
creative action and perception. Currently based in Vancouver BC, 
Koke has exhibited her artwork in Vancouver, Montreal, and New 
York, and has performed internationally as an experimental/rock 
musician.
Alex Heilbron
Currently based in Vancouver, Alex Heilbron graduated from the San 
Francisco Art Institute with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in painting in 2009
and completed a Post-Baccalaureate program at the Pont Aven 
School of Contemporary Art in France in 2012. Heilbron’s work 
contains a visual dichotomy between the figurative and the abstract, 
which is intended to create a playful and explorative exchange 
between the viewer and the artwork. Familiar graphic shapes, bright 
colours, pattern, repetition, and sharp contrasts become narrative 
devices, often featuring familiar symbols taken from a visual language 
that is unique to Heilbron’s work. Heilbron has exhibited her artwork 
nationally and internationally, including shows in Vancouver, 
Germany, France, San Francisco and Los Angeles.

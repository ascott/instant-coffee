FLEET PROGRAMMING LEAD

Other Sights is looking for an experienced programmer to develop and implement
the inaugural year of programming for FLEET Mobile Studios in 2024.

ABOUT FLEET AND OTHER SIGHTS

Other Sights for Artists Projects operates as a collective of Vancouver-based
individuals with expertise in curation, project management, presentation, delivery
and promotion of temporary art projects in public spaces.

FLEET working group is composed of seven individuals: two Other Sights
Producers, Other Sights General Manager, as well as artists, architects, and a
planner. The group also works with a 3 person Indigenous advisory group, and with
additional support and advisory from Other Sights board and producer team.

FLEET is a creative approach to complement the communitys ongoing
efforts to
build more studio opportunities for artists. Responding to the loss of arts and culture
hubs across Vancouver, FLEET is a multi-year project that aspires to plan, design
and, pilot a FLEET of moveable studios for working artists. The project will place
these studios on a temporary basis throughout the city and manage their ongoing
use. FLEETs visible presence will draw attention to Vancouvers artistic communitys
space deficit while foregrounding the need to share the public spaces of the city
more equitably.

ABOUT THE ROLE

This is a hands-on project that involves coordinating with FLEET working group,
Other Sights, and potential site and programming partners (municipalities, non-
profits, Indigenous Host Nations, community organizations, or private developers) in
order to develop, oversee, and implement FLEETs inaugural year of programming
for two studios (2023-2024).

The FLEET Programming Lead is a planner, implementer, and relationship builder
who is responsible for
developing this new initiative in conjunction with multiple and
diverse communities across the Lower Mainland. The position will require the
incumbent to familiarize themselves with the many different stakeholders in
Vancouver, Burnaby and potentially elsewhere, including community groups, non-
profits, municipalities, universities, and artist run centres. The Programming Lead will
work with these groups to develop programming for two FLEET Studios in the form
of artist residencies and jury selection, lectures, panels, open houses, exhibits and
discussions that are part of the projects broader scope of providing accessible
studio space and bringing awareness to the ever diminishing amount of studio space
available in cities such as Vancouver.

The Programming Lead will work with the FLEET working group, Other Sights, and
the Indigenous Advisory Committee to maintain relationships within the three
Indigenous host nations; as FLEET Studios will move throughout the lower
mainland, we are
committed to strong relationships with Musqueam, Squamish,
Tsleil-Waututh (MST) and other Host nation communities where the studios will be
sited. The incumbent will play a fundamental role in maintaining our connections with
MST and Urban Indigenous communities.

The FLEET Programming Lead is expected to work independently, while maintaining
a strong connection to the overall project and curatorial planning.

Reception: April 23, 7pm

Taking place in our exhibition room at 221 E Georgia Street, The Kitchen
Garden at Home/Store centres on the purchasing, modification and
commensurate re-sale of products sourced exclusively from businesses within
Vancouver's Chinatown. Developed in the weeks leading up to the opening as
well as throughout the project, Vancouver-based artist Ron Tran will use
the exhibition as a vehicle for financial and social exchange, exploring
the shifting consumer culture in the neighbourhood, not as a declaration of
cultural obsolescence but by participating in the vitality of an existing
community.

Curated by Brian McBay.

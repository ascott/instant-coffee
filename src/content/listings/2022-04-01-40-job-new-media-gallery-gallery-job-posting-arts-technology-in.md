Apply by: April 22, 2022

POSITION OVERVIEW
New Media Gallery is looking for one or two dynamic instructors to join our faculty and
develop
unique public workshops which incorporate technology while teaching art
fundamentals.
You will begin by committing to developing and delivering a set of summer programs
off-site.
All our Instructors have a contemporary arts and/or design background, with
identifiable technical
skills, experience and aptitude. Our initial focus for this
hiring is on finding individuals
with solid
experience in filmmaking and videography for childrens programming. Our secondary
focus is
on Coding. For 2022 summer camps, you will propose, develop and deliver high quality
public
Arts + Technology workshops and day camps for children that will focus on the Art of
Film-making.
Later, depending on ability and scheduling, you may wish to propose programs for
children, adults
and professional artists.

Specific duties include developing and submitting workshop proposals, demonstrating
a variety of
teaching methods, delivering imaginative art & technology workshops. You are
experienced and flexible
in your teaching approach, have experience teaching children and the ability to
document and promote
your programmes. Your focus is quality, creativity, imagination and professionalism.
You have the ability
to pass and maintain a satisfactory Police Information Check.

This project will require 20+ hours in June (flexible) and 70 hours (FT), July
11- 22,
Mon-Fri.
You must be able to commit to this summer schedule.

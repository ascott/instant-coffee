Katayoon Yousefbigloo is The Lido's artist-in-residency for September 2019. Through 
video and music, Katayoons residency seeks to evoke the nostalgia and aesthetic of 
the bootleg VHS market in Iran during the late 90s-mid 00s, which resulted from a 
network of low budget studios in Los Angeles operated by members of the Iranian 
diaspora broadcasting music videos, live talk shows, news and special programming 
back to Iran via satellite. Every Sunday evening in September will feature a free public 
performance:

Sep 8 | SCREENING | Telstar 12 | Video collage dedicated to Telstar 12, the name of 
the satellite used to broadcast illegal shows from the United States and Canada into 
Iran.

Sep 15 | MUSIC VIDEO FILMING | Katayoon will be filming a live music video in 
keeping with the distinct style of bootleg VHS. All welcome to participate!

Sep 22 | ARTIST TALK & VIDEO | Katayoon will speak to this project and her research, 
and will be showing music videos from the peak of the bootleg satellite show era.

Sep 29 | SCREENING & SPECIAL PERFORMANCE | The final event will be a 
screening of the live music video filmed on Sep 15, followed by a performance with 
special guests. 

The Lido Residency is an initiative presented by Warm Notice, a non-profit collective 
created to support women, non-binary, and marginalized musicians in British Columbia. 
Applications are still open for Fall & Winter, see @yourwarmnotice on Instagram for 
details. 

The Lido (518 East Broadway) is a neighbourhood pub located on the unceded, 
occupied, and ancestral homelands of the xmky"m (Musqueam), Swxw7mesh 
(Squamish), and sl"ilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations in East Vancouver. The venue is 
19+. For full accessibility information, please email yourwarmnotice@gmail.com

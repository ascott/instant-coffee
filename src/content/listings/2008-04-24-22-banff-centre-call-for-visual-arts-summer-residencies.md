The Banff Centre – Visual Arts Summer Residencies 
Call for Applications 


Figure in a Mountain Landscape with Silke Otto-Knapp and Jan Verwoert 

Program dates: July 7 - August 22, 2008 
Application deadline: Extended to May 2, 2008 
Financial assistance is available. 

The Figure in a Mountain Landscape Residency offers artists the opportunity to work with London-based painter Silke Otto-Knapp while exploring the potential of the plein air approach. Together the group will explore methods and media ranging from watercolour, to performance and text-based practices. 

Self-Directed Creative Residency 

Program dates: July 21 - August 22, 2008 
Application deadline: Extended to May 2, 2008 
Residency length: Minimum of five days 

The Self-Directed Creative Residency provides time and space for individuals and groups to create new works, research innovative ideas, and experiment with different techniques and modes of production.

ALEPH NULL, GRAPHIC SYNTHESIZER
Prints and computer screen
Jim Andrews
Massy Books, 229 E Georgia St, Vancouver
Oct 25 - Nov 20, 2019

Opening: Friday Oct 25, 6-8pm

More info: [http://vispo.com/massy](http://vispo.com/massy)

Jim Andrews created the images in this show with Aleph Null, online
software he wrote that he calls a "graphic synthesizer"; it's part tool,
part generative art machine, and part work of art itself. Some of the
images were
created in collaboration with the legendary
poet/painter/performer/musician bill bissett, and some with the visual
poet Jim Leftwich, originator of the term 'asemic writing'. The show
will feature archival, high-res, facemounted prints plus one computer
screen that will display a slideshow of 1500 images created with Aleph Null.

For the artist statement and a catalog of available prints--and for
links to Aleph Null itself--please visit [http://vispo.com/massy](http://vispo.com/massy)

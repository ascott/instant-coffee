Ewan McNeil / RE + RE

March 30th to April 15th. 
The artist will be in attendance on Thursday, April 12th 2012. 

Capilano University Studio Gallery 
2055 Purcell Way North Vancouver 

This exhibition is Curated by Marcus Bowcott . 

“RE + RE”  is a builderʼs term about “reviewing and replacing”  or 
“reviewing and 
renovating” . Itʼs an apt title for this exhibition. More importantly the 
spirit of “RE + RE”  is 
at the heart of McNeilʼs practice of photography, painting, sculpture 
and collage. His 
aesthetic process is a constant search of reconsideration and 
adaption, he is always 
looking for the simple and functional resolution to visual and design 
considerations. 
His latex paintings of our urban and manufactured environments are 
highly articulated 
renderings that are directly stated. They are compositionally balanced 
to the point of 
visual elegance and embody a spare beauty that is utterly 
unsentimental. Several 
observers have commented on the ﬁlm noir associations these images 
evoke. 
The unsentimental nature of this work is not only embodied in form, it 
is embraced in 
process. McNeilʼs self critical practice involves reconﬁguring highly 
articulated images in 
relation to a table saw. After all, as he states... “the saw is a drawing 
instrument, a tool 
for editing.”

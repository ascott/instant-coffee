PRIDE IN CHINATOWN MMXX
Curated by Paul Wong
August 8 - September 7, 2020

Pride in Chinatown 2020 is on! For the third year in a row, On Main Gallery, Dr. Sun Yat-
Sen Classical Chinese Garden and Vancouver Art and Leisure will present new art by
queer pan-Asian artists in Vancouvers historic Chinatown. This year, 8 artists will
activate sites in Chinatown with a series of artworks and interventions that can be
experienced 24/7! With a custom walking map of Chinatown, we invite you to explore
our unique, rapidly shifting neighbourhood and discover these exciting new site-specific
artworks.

Pride in Chinatown began in 2018 as a
modest one-night event in the Dr.
Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden featuring hybrid drag and Cantonese opera
performed to a sold-out audience of 400. Last year, we took over the Garden, Park and
Plaza for a momentous celebration featuring over 40 artists and a packed audience of
over 800 guests. We have shifted away from the centralized festival format this year due
to COVID-19 and have expanded our vision to a month-long street exhibition. Pride in
Chinatown is out loud and proud in the streets of Chinatown more than ever before.

2020 has been a challenging and provocative year where communities have come
together to support each other while rifts and inequities have broken wide open for the
whole world to see. This years Pride in Chinatown explores the absence of LGBTQ2+
histories in our Chinatowns with a focus on queer Asian identities and experiences at
this pivotal and precarious moment. This year will include a site specific installation on
the Lim Association Building by
David Ng, based on a 1906 newspaper
article about the bathhouse that existed in this building on Carrall St; intersectional non-
binary feminist drag performance artist Maiden Chinas Quarantine Series of self portraits,
created during 40 days of COVID-19 isolation, blending drag, portraiture, mythology,
prosthetics, and narrative; an illustrative series of minimalist vinyl artwork by multiracial
artist and writer Candie Tanaka; Diyan Achjadi, Jay Cabalu, Ho Tam, and Kara Juku &
Christian Jones. This years artists identify as being part of the LGBTQ2+ community with
origins from Indonesia, Vietnam, China, Japan, the Philippines and other hyphens,
curated by Paul Wong who identifies as a he/him Chinese-Canadian. These exciting new
works will be installed in various locations throughout Chinatown including storefronts,
windows, walls and hoardings for small groups to experience anytime between August 8 -
September 7, 2020.

Pride in Chinatown MMXX will launch on August 8th at
prideinchinatown.com with
documentation from our 2018 and 2019 events and a guide to this years street
exhibition. We invite visitors to support the local shops, restaurants, community
organizations and services proudly displaying our Pride in Chinatown welcome decals.

Pride in Chinatown MMXX features a host of community partners including Queer Arts
Festival, Fortune Sound Club, Access Gallery, Or Gallery, Propaganda Coffee and
more! We received generous support from the Canada Council for the Arts and the City
of Vancouver.

Pride in Chinatown takes place on the unceded lands of the Musqueam, Squamish and
Tsleil- Waututh Nations. We recognize our responsibility as uninvited guests on this land
to work actively in support of Indigenous sovereignty.

BFA Graduating Exhibition 2024:
Look Both Ways

APR 17 - MAY 4, 2024
AUDAIN GALLERY

OPENING RECEPTION
APR 17 / 6 - 8PM

Noor A. Ghazal, Amelia Burggraf, Jazmin Erice, Agnes Fan, Andra Galbin-Pristavu,
Caro Garofalo, Sana Kapoor, Cathy Li, Elizabeth Li, Melody Li, Amrit Kaur Nagra,
Christopher Outten, Quinton Qiu, Kinar Saragih, Samantha Steven, Kaleb Thiessen,
Minty Zhang.

Look Both Ways presents the work of visual art students graduating with a Bachelor
of Fine Arts from the School for the Contemporary Arts at Simon
Fraser University.

The artwork presented in the exhibition offers a glimpse into the practice of
seventeen artists who have been part of a cohort for four years. Over this time, they
have worked together and supported one another in their learning and artmaking.
The exhibition marks a critical transit point in the lives of these students and in this
time of transition. It serves as an anchor, a moment to reflect, assess, and analyze
one last time before venturing beyond. It is wise to look both ways before taking
such a step, and while the overarching themes are diverse, the concept of looking
both ways binds the artwork together.

Working in mediums ranging from sculpture, textiles, ceramics, drawing, painting,
video, photography, and performance, the artists physically manifest everyday
encounters and raise metaphysical questions about our collective consciousness,
the meaning of existence, archival authority, and loss. Through these works,
sensations, ideas, and materialities, the
artists come together.

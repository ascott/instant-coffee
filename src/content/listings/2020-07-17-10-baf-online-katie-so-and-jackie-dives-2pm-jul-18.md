BAF Virtual Artist Talk

Jackie Dives
Katie So

2pm, Saturday July 18th
Instagram Live and Facebook Live @burrardarts

Join us online at 2pm on Saturday, July 18th for a free, virtual artist talk featuring both of
our currently exhibiting artists!

Jackie Dives will guide us through her show 'Becoming Not a Mother', while Katie So will
discuss her exhibition 'Take Care'. Both shows were created in BAF's artist residency
program.

Like all BAF events, this one is free and open to the public - but this time, it's online!

Tune in via Instagram Live or Facebook Live at 2pm on Saturday, July 18th. Find us on
both platforms @burrardarts or join the Facebook event for updates at:
https://www.facebook.com/events/282648652804038/

We hope to see you there!

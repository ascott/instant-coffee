Cineworks presents:

Animation Noir: The Paint-On-Glass Animations of Patrick Jenkins

Thu 26 November, 7.00pm
Cineworks Studio, 1131 Howe Street (lane entrance)
By donation

[http://bit.ly/1QvS3gO](http://bit.ly/1QvS3gO)

Join us for an evening of screenings and a talk by Patrick Jenkins - a
Toronto based artist/filmmaker who creates unique animated films using the
Paint on Glass Animation technique.

Way out West to impart some of the skills behind his remarkable style in a
series of workshops and special presentations, Jenkins is welcomed by
Cineworks to present a selection of compelling works created over the past
decade including:

Labyrinth - a surrealistic detective story;
Amoeba - an abstract animated film based on a composition by John Zorn;
Phantom City - Jenkins most recent work, completed in 2015.

Jenkins will provide an introduction to each film, talking about the
techniques he uses to create his films and the ideas behind them, as well
as answering questions from the audience.

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society (est.1980) is an artist-run
production and exhibition centre that supports independent filmmakers and
media artists working in digital video and celluloid film media. We engage
our membership and the broader community through initiatives and services
that foster dialogue and experimentation with the moving image.

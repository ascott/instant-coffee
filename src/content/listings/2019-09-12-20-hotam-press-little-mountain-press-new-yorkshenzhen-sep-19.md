POWDER ROOM
RISO prints by Little Mountain Press
September 19 to October 26, 2019

Little Mountain Press is an art and illustration independent publisher from New York, 
USA and Shenzhen, China. The press was established in the summer of 2018 by the 
illustrator duo Xiao Mei and Mountain Dog. Little Mountain Press has published most 
zines and prints in risograph. As a duo, they love bright colors and are not afraid to 
express their sexual and ethnic identity. Their mission is to transform boredom into joy 
and challenge people's perception through print media. 

Powder Room is the first gallery exhibition of the print work by Little Mountain Press. 
Many of Little Mountains customers have told Little Mountain that the prints make great 
decoration in the bathroom. The powder also refers to the colourful toner used in 
Risograph printing. 

To coincide with the upcoming Art Book Week and the Vancouver Art Book Fair which 
Little Mountain Press is to participate, Hotam Press Bookshop/Gallery will have a 
welcome reception for the dynamic duo on Thursday, October 17, from 5 tp 7 PM.

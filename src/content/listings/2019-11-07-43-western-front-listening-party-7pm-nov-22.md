Listening Party: Aram Bajakian,
Raven Chacon and Gabi Dao
FRI, NOV 22, 2019 | 7PM
Western Front, 303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver

An ongoing series hosted by the Western Front's New Music program, listening parties invite
artists, composers and guests to share recent musical and sound works that exert emotional
pull. The series also explores the Western Front's own archives, unearthing recordings of artists
in their early years through raw, unedited sound files. In dialogue with Relations of
Responsibility, this listening party will feature selections made by artists Raven Chacon and
Gabi Dao, alongside New Music curator Aram Bajakian.

This event is co-presented by the Western Front and SFU's School for Contemporary Arts'
Audain Visual Artist in Residence
Program.

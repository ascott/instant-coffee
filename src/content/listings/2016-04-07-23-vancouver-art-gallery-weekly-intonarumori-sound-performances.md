Weekly Intonarumori Sound Performances
Martin Gotfrit & guest artists 
Tuesday April 12, 19 & 26
7pm & 8pm 
In the Gallery

Join Martin Gotfrit, Professor at Simon Fraser University, and guest 
musicians for a weekly activation of the intonarumori every Tuesday 
evening in April. The intonarumori are a group of experimental musical 
instruments created by the Italian futurist Luigi Russolo in the early 
1900s. Local musicians bring these instruments to life with casual, 
improvisational performances. 

Free for Members or with Gallery admission.

ARTIST TALKS | AHVA Gallery [ONLINE] | APR 28-29

Please join us online April 28-29 from 4:00 to 5:00 each day for The Fold Artist Talk
series, where six graduating student artists from UBCs visual art program will discuss
their works in the graduating exhibition as well as the media, materials, influences, and
theoretical concerns attendant to their individual practices.

April 28: Erinne Huston, Miya Kosowick, Vicky Mo

April 29: Sai Di, Connie Li, Sissie He

To receive the Zoom link for the talks, RSVP with your full name to
ubcundergradshow2021@gmail.com

All attendees will receive the link on April 27 via email.

The Fold, the 2021 BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating Exhibition (@ubcundergradshow2021),
continues online and at the AHVA Gallery until April 29. The physical exhibition is open
Tuesday to Thursday, 12:00 to 4:00 pm at the AHVA Gallery, 6398 University Boulevard,
Vancouver.

Attention all media art lovers, film buffs, culture geeks, tech-savvy wizards, and people 
who just love to be part of something inspiring and fun - Cinevolutions Digital Carnival is 
back and we want your help! On August 30 & August 31, we'll need you to help us throw 
the coolest media arts event at the Richmond World Festival this summer.

Join award-winning Cinevolution Media Arts Society and the City of Richmond for a fun 
summer learning experience with some of the most passionate team members you'll 
ever meet.

// ABOUT US

Cinevolution is a non-profit film and media arts society based out of Richmond, BC that 
seeks to increase media literacy, social awareness, and strengthen community and 
cross-cultural understanding through film and media art. Winner of the 2012 Richmond 
Artistic Innovation Award and 2013 Nova Star Award of Volunteer Richmond.

// DIGITAL CARNIVAL (CO-PRESENTED with Richmond World Festival and the City of 
Richmond)

Digital Carnival 2019: FIRE is the final installment in a 4-part series seeking to unpack 
the complex relationship between art, nature, and technology. This year's exhibition 
features over a dozen emerging national and international artists with exciting works 
including video projections, interactive installations, virtual reality projects and more!

Part of the Richmond World Festival, this exciting summer event is attended by more 
than 3000 people from all over the Lower Mainland each night. If you love art, culture, 
diversity and summer fun, then this is the volunteer opportunity for you!

// VOLUNTEER JOB DESCRIPTIONS
Event Ambassadors
Event ambassadors will help to ensure the success of the event by setting up, 
welcoming artists and guests, attracting patrons, answering questions, maintaining 
order, and providing further support where needed. Ambassadors will work as a team to 
staff the entry and exit to the tents, help people move through the exhibit, inform them 
about upcoming performances, encourage them to interact with the artists and their 
work, and share information about digital carnival and Cinevolution. We would like to 
see energetic and outgoing personality!
Photographer & Videographer
Photographers and/or videographers will help us record footages from the festival. Are 
you a professional photographer looking to expand your portfolio? A budding filmmaker? 
A film student? If you are a photography/filmmaking enthusiast, wed like to hear from 
you. You will be responsible for capturing moments throughout the festival, taking 
pictures/videos of the crowd, artists, performances, and various exhibits over the two 
days. Creativity and a passion for visual media is a must!

Please Note: All volunteers will be required to familiarize themselves with the event 
program, schedule, and layout. A short orientation will be provided at the beginning of 
your shift to address any of your questions or concerns.

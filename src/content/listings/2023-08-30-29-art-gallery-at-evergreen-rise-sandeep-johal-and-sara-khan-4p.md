OPENING AND TOUR | "RISE: Sandeep Johal and Sara Khan" at Art Gallery at
Evergreen
Saturday, September 16 from 4-7pm
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam, BC

Join us on Saturday September 16th, 4:00pm-7:00pm for an artist-led
tour and the
opening reception of "RISE: Sandeep Johal and Sara Khan"at Art Gallery at
Evergreen. A tour with the artists, Sandeep Johal and Sara Khan, and curator,
Katherine Dennis, will begin at 4 p.m., followed by the opening reception for the
exhibition from 5"7 p.m.

Sandeep Johal and Sara Khans artistic collaboration gives rise to a playful and
fantastical environment. The artists merge their distinct creatures, dynamic patterns
and rich colour palettes into new hybrid forms. Their artworks celebrate the joy,
power and complexities of womanhood from their perspectives as two South Asian
women artists who are mothers to young children.

Light refreshments will be served. The tour will be approximately 30 minutes;
remarks will take place at 6 p.m. This event is wheelchair accessible. Pay parking
available on-site, easy access from Lafarge Lake-Douglas College Skytrain Station.

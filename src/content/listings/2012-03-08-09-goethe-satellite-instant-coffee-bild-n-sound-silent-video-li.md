==Bild N Sound==

Goethe Satellite @ Vancouver
Saturday March 10th, 2012 at 8pm at Western Front, 303 East 8th
Avenue, Vancouver

Instant Coffee and Bernd Milla program a selection of silent videos by
German artists inviting Canadian musicians to play a live score.
Silents videos by Andreas Lorenschat, Mirko Martin, Pia Maria Martin,
Gabriela Oberkofler, Patrick Fabian Panetta and Sebastian Stumpf.

Live music by Jeremy Schmidt (Black Mountain and Sinoia Caves),
Stephen Lyons, Shanto Bhattacharya, Skye Brooks (Fond of Tigers),
Marta McKeever, Tess Kitchen & Diona Davies (E.S.L.)

A project by the Goethe-Institut in cooperation with Instant Coffee and
Revised Projects

For more information about upcoming events visit www.front.bc.ca

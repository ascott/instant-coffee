The Vancouver Art Gallery's beloved late-night art party returns as FUSE: Object 
Lessons on Friday, November 23 at 8 PM until late. Inspired by themes of the Gallerys 
Fall 2018 season, this signature evening merging visual art, music and performance will 
awaken guests to a new consciousness around bodies and objects as a way to transmit 
meaning, lessons, ideas and beliefs. Featured artists will respond to the intricate works 
of Guo Pei: Couture Beyond as well as photography, video and installation works of 
Dana Claxton: Fringing the Cube. DJ Todd-Rod Skimmins Simmons will curate the 
nights sounds, while Downtown Eastside independent radio station No Fun Radio 
animates an artist bar. 

Tickets $29 General/ $15 for Members available online at vanartgallery.bc.ca

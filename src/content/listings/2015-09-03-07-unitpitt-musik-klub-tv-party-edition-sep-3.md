Musik Klub is happy to present TV Party Edition. Join us in screening
an hour-long program of live (or sometimes badly lip-synced)
performances from a variety of international television music programs
broadcast between 1965 & 1990. Keeping with the tone of the night,
Elliot Heintzman will be playing records before, between and after
video sets. Come out, drink refreshing beverages, hang with friends
and listen to (and watch) some great garage / punk / freedom / weirdo
rock music. Ends early so you can keep the night going; its like happy
hour for music & video fan-fiends!

MUSIK KLUB is a screening series organized and hosted by Jamie
Ward and is intended to highlight ongoing research into exciting or
challenging music available on YouTube. Videos are chosen for both
their innovative or sublime video content as well as their unique
musical quality, with an emphasis on material produced Pre-

Contemporary Embroidery Textures on Black with Katy Biele

Join Chilean artist Katy Biele on Wednesday, February 6, for a Contemporary 
Embroidery Textures on Black Workshop. You will learn how to create different textures 
on black fabric, from simple stitches to more advanced 3D stitches. You don't need to 
have any previous experience, and all the materials are included. 
You will bring home all the materials to finish your piece, as well as threads and a new 
pattern for a second piece.
Start date: Wednesday, February 6, 2019
Schedule: 6 pm to 9 pm 
Location: South Main Gallery
Supplies: All materials included in the price, you don't have to bring anything. 
Cost: $110
For more information, and to sign up, contact Katy at studio@katybiele.com

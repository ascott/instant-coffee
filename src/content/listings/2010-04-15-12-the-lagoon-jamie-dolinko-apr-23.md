THE LAGOON l Jamie Dolinko l APR 23 - AUG 31

Opening Friday, April 23, 2010
'The Lagoon' by Jamie Dolinko, is a digital installation of 108 photographs selected from a 
decade of walks around Lost Lagoon, a visual meditation on the intersection of people, animals, 
and nature. 
Please join us for the opening reception from 6 - 8 p.m., Friday, April 23, Earth Day Weekend, or
during regular hours: Saturday & Sunday, 11 a.m. - 4 p.m. Summer hours in July and August are 
11 a.m. - 4 p.m. Tuesday through Sunday. The show runs until August 31, 2010, and is located at 
the foot of Alberni Street, below the viewing deck, at the Nature House at Lost Lagoon. 

The Stanley Park Nature House is a gateway for nature interpretation and park exploration, and 
is Vancouver’s only nature interpretation centre, showcasing interpretive displays on various 
habitats and their flora and fauna. A re-imagining of the Nature House has created more space 
for interactive displays that will change with the seasons, and enable the Nature House to pilot 
our vision to host other installations that combine ecological and environmental themes with 
artistic vision. Public Programs is always looking to use the Nature House in innovative and 
creative ways in our endeavour to connect people with nature.

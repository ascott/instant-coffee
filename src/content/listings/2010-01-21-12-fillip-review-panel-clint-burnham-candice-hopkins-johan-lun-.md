About the Speakers

Clint Burnham teaches at Simon Fraser University in Vancouver. His most
recent book is The Benjamin Sonnets (BookThug, 2009); his writing on art
has appeared in Boo, fillip, artforum and Canadian Art, as well as
Vancouver Art and Economies and many catalogues both locally and
internationally. He can be followed on twitter @Prof_Clinty

Candice Hopkins is the Sobey Curatorial Resident, Indigenous Art, at the
National Gallery of Canada in Ottawa, and the former director and curator
of exhibitions at the Western Front in Vancouver. Her writing has been
published by MIT Press, BlackDog Publishing, New York University, Catriona
Jeffries Gallery, Banff Centre Press, and the National Museum of the
American Indian, among others and she has given talks at venues including
the Tate Modern, Tate Britain, the Dakar Biennale, and the University of
British Columbia.

Johan Lundh is an independent curator and writer, dividing his time between
New York and Stockholm. He is the organizer and the host of The Fillip
Review Panel.

Amy Zion is the Assistant Editor of Fillip.

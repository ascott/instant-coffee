SHIFT Curators Tour with Lisa Baldissera

Griffin Art Projects

1174 Welch St, North Vancouver, V7P 1B2

Join Lisa Baldissera, Griffin Art Projects director and curator of SHIFT: Ecologies of
Fashion, Form + Textile, for a hybrid (in-person and online) curators tour followed by
audience Q&A.

SHIFT: Ecologies of Fashion, Form
and Textile addresses the intersection of visual
art, fashion and textiles, to consider these practices as worldmaking forms of
knowledge and a human encounter that comprises every aspect of daily life.
Including a residency, symposium, and works by China Adams, Jason Dodge, Olaf
Holzapfel, Brian Jungen, Annette Kelm, Christiane Lohr, Medrie MacPhee, Manuel
Mathieu, Meret Oppenheim, Wolfgang Tillmans, and Janet Werner, among others,
SHIFT considers the ways in which power, colonialism and consumerism are
expressed within fashion and proposes forms for an emancipating futurity.

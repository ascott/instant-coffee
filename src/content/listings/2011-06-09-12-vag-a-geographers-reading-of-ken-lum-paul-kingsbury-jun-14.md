TALKS at the Vancouver Art Gallery
Tuesday, June 14, 7pm
In the Gallery

A Geographer's Reading of Ken Lum
Paul Kingsbury

Geographer Paul Kingsbury surveys Ken Lum's mapping of the city and
examines the artist's public art projects, the use of commercial signage
and references to tourism and consumption.

Dr. Paul Kingsbury is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geography
at Simon Fraser University. He received a MA and PhD in Geography, as well
as a Graduate Certificate in Social Theory from the University of Kentucky.
Specializing in social and cultural geography, his research draws on the
theories of Jacques Lacan and Friedrich Nietzsche to examine
multiculturalism, consumption, tourism, power, and aesthetics.

Free with Gallery admission

Join us this Saturday, June 2nd between at 2:00 PM for experimental
readings, SSML text-to-speech listening, and the launch of the (very
limited edition) OH x SPIT publication.

Refreshments will be served.

*

In a strategy to engage with artist-led writing and publishing from Los
Angeles and Vancouver, Access Gallery invited SPIT to lead a series of
experimental readings and events responding to the Office Hours
publications and installation installed at Access Gallerys PLOT throughout
the month of May, 2018. On May 12 and 19, SPIT, together with
Vancouver-based artist and poet Sonja Ratkay, led two intimate workshops
designed to interpret the publications in the Office Hours omnibus in
unexpected and experimental ways.

The workshops centered language as both image and sound; engaging the body
through corporeal mark-making and unreadable writing; proposing image as
text; and utilizing Text To Speech readings, synthesized voices, and
sounding forms. From staining pages with fruit juices to formatting Speech
Synthesis Markup Language (SSML) code, this publication, and its
accompanying audio components, contain traces of the idiosyncratic and
personal interpretations of participants in these workshops. The original
writings and images found in the Office Hours collection have been
translated into something altogether different; their materials surviving
in a cryptic new state.

This unique Vancouver edition exists as a contribution to the Office Hours
publication series, marking the closing of Office Hours tenure, as well as
Brody Alberts exhibition, Nonlocal, in the exhibition space.

SPIT is a series of reading experiments, workshops, interviews, events, and
poetic expressions based in Vancouver, and organized by Emma Metcalfe Hurst
and Christian Vistan. The online archive of their former self is located at
www.lit-lit-lit-lit.com.

-
Office Hours is installed in Access Gallery's PLOT space for the duration
of Brody Alberts exhibition Nonlocal. Operated by Brody Albert and
Nathaniel Klein, Office Hours is a nonprofit gallery and publisher
initiated in 2013 in LA. Dedicated to the intersection of visual art and
creative writing, their mission is to build an active dialogue between
literary scenes and arts communities. After being forced out of their Arts
District space in 2016, Office Hours took an itinerant position. Office
Hours has mounted 9 exhibitions and produced 8 publications featuring the
work of 30 LA artists and 19 writers.

Office Hours has brought an omnibus of their publications, a new video work
titled Open Ocean by Klein, and a new publication made specifically for
PLOT by Albert in collaboration with Rachel Paprocki, Miguel Espinoza and
Michael Kennedy Costa.

-
Sonja Ratkay is an artist, poet and researcher. Through reliefs filled with
poems, diagram drawings and immersive installations she researches topics
of language and the body, forming of thoughts through known and unknown
symbols, intuition, haunting and the haunted, states of being, and
environmental triggers which lead to psychological transformation. She
lives and works in Vancouver, BC.

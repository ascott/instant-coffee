Please join us on Thursday December 10th, 2009 from 7 - 10pm for a
reception to welcome our new Executive Director, Cailtin Jones, to the
Western Front.

Caitlin joined the Western Front in November of this year, returning to
Vancouver from New York City.

Jones has been intimately involved with contemporary art for the past
decade as a curator and administrator at major institutions in the United
States. From 2001-06 she worked in the curatorial and conservation
departments of the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum. There she co-curated the
groundbreaking exhibition -Seeing Double: Emulation in Theory and Practice-
and coordinated the Deutsche Guggenheim exhibition Ã¢-Nam June Paik: Global
Groove 2004- in Berlin. She was also the coordinator of the Variable Media
Network, an international consortium of art institutions dedicated to the
preservation and documentation of video, performance and installation art.
Subsequently, Jones was the Director of Programming at the Bryce Wolkowitz
Gallery and a freelance curator, coordinating a series of exhibitions for
Madison Square Park's Mad.Square.Art program.

We are thrilled to have Caitlin in the role of Executive Director and hope
that you can join us to meet her at this informal reception and to welcome
her to the community.

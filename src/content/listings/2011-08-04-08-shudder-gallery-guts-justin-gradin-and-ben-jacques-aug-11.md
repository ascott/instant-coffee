Shudder Gallery presents:

Guts
Justin Gradin Ben Jacques
August 13th - 27th
Opening Reception Thursday, Aug 11th, 7:00 -11:00 pm

GUTS is a new collaborative show between Vancouver artists Ben 
Jacques and Justin Gradin.
GUTS is....
Abstract punk outfits, dystopian bowel movements, eye sores and 
rainbow colors, melting B-movie posters,inside out geometrical 
drum sets,
new unpopular line work, beer induced hallucinations, loud feelings 
and destructive senses.

The artists will also premier a new performance style on the 
opening night. collector art mugs are also for sale.
come for the magic of it all.

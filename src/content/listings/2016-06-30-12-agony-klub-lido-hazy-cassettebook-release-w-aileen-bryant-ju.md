JUL 7 THUR
MUSIC: hazy cassette/book release w/ Aileen Bryant

Agony Klub presents:

hazy is the solo project of KC Wei (Late Spring.) Live, her sets weave
together intimate songwriting with most-beloved covers through a throb of
guitar-noise. hazy sounds naive, complex, vulnerable, mysterious, crushy,
and crushing.

x.o. Virgo Ox is her debut cassette/book album written over the last 5
years. Along with the cassette comes a chapbook of writing and images.
Together, the two components complete an odyssey of love and destruction
through which the artist invites you to take pleasure, solace, and indulgence.

Opening for hazy is Aileen Bryant, vocal enchantress known to make audience
members faint with her trance inducing soundscapes.

9pm The Lido
Free Show

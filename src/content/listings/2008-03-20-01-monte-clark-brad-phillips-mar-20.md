NO COMEBACKS 
March 20th to April 12th 
The Monte Clark Gallery Vancouver is pleased to announce the solo exhibition of new paintings by Vancouver-based artist Brad Phillips.

Brad Phillips is not hiding the fact that for the time being, he feels like a failed writer. Actually, this very idea informs his present work. Interested in using this concept – his desire to write – as the general theme for his exhibition No P.S., Phillips applies a meta-narrative construct to create paintings with “a certain sort of self-referential fiction about neurosis, lack of material and writers block”. Although his recent works initially appear disconnected, the common thread that runs throughout the small and midsized oil on canvas works is their autobiographical theme. Keen on using his biography and day-to-day life as subject matter, Phillips introduces characters, thoughts and places without narrative. "It's sort of like a panicked autobiography, a cyclone of frustrated ideas and an attempt to put a life into images …".

Brad Phillips lives and works in Vancouver. He studied at the Toronto School of Art and the Ontario College of Art and Design. Phillips has shown in New York, LA, Toronto and Switzerland. His most recent exhibitions include Wallspace, New York, Groedlin Maag Galerie, Basel, Switzerland, Allston Skirt Gallery, Boston and the Stolenspace Gallery, London.

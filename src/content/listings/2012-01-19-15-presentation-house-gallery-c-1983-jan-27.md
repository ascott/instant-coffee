This first of a two-part exhibition looks at how artists in Vancouver worked with 
camera images during the important period of the 1980s. The exhibition brings 
together photographic and media art from two generations, and many works will 
be seen for the first time since they were originally exhibited. It highlights 
conceptualist and experimental approaches to the construction of images 
through collage and appropriation techniques, and sequences of moving and still 
pictures. There is an emphasis on the material qualities of photographs and the 
interactions of light captured on surface that draws attention to modes of 
perception and visuality itself. The artists reference pictorial traditions from the 
histories of painting, cinema and mass media, and photographic images are 
often treated as a language and system of signs with an acute awareness of the 
social impact of public images in a mediated world.

Presented in honour of Kitty Heller, with generous support from her estate.

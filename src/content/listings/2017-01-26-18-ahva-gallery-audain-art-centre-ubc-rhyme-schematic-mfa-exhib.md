Opening reception: Wednesday, February 1 from 6 - 8 pm
Exhibition dates: February 2 - 25, 2017
AHVA Gallery
Room 1001
Audain Art Centre, UBC
6398 University Boulevard
gallery.ahva.ubc.ca

Please join the UBC Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory (AHVA) at the AHVA 
Gallery in the Audain Art Centre on Wednesday, February 1 at 6 pm for the opening 
reception of Rhyme Schematic. University of British Columbia MFA candidates in their 
first year present an exciting selection of their current research and artwork spanning a 
range of media.
ARTISTS: Aileen Bahmanipour, Christopher Lacroix, Cameron McLellan, Candice 
Okada, Parvin Peivandi, Madiha Sikander

The opening reception follows on from a concurrent event in the Audain Art Centre: 
"Producing without Profit: Art, Labour and Postcapitalism," an artist talk by Dave Beech, 
which starts at 5:30 pm in Room 1002. More details elsewhere in this Instant Coffee 
listing.

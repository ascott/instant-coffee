Kelly Lycan: The Fireplace

June 21-August 25, 2024

Opening Reception and Book Launch: June 20, 6:30-9 pm

Performance by My Name is Del: 8:30 pm

Kelly Lycan is a photo-based installation artist whose work investigates how objects
and images are placed and displayed in the
world, and the resulting cycle of value
they experience. With a longstanding inquiry of repurposed and recontextualized
interiors, objects and materials, her exhibition The Fireplace looks at a host of
occupants in the once-residential space of the Burnaby Art Gallery, mediating
between the world of objects and the absence of its former residents.

Lycans works reference past furniture, decorations and styles that have passed
through the house along with its people. Offering new glimpses of the gallery/home
through reconfiguration and drawing on her long history as a set decorator, The
Fireplace is the staging of a home through the mythical conflation of time periods
and stories, set in the present day.

The opening reception will also celebrate the launch of the publication Kelly Lycan |
The Fireplace, an artists book featuring texts by Anne Low and Jennifer Cane,
created on the occasion of the exhibition. Copies of the publication will be available
for purchase.

WORKSHOP | Burnaby Art Gallery | Botanical Printmaking Workshop | JUNE 8 & 9


Botanical Printmaking with Bonnie Devine and Raven Davis: June 8th & 9th, 10am-
3pm

We are thrilled to invite you to our Botanical Printmaking workshop with Deer Lake
Artists in Residence, Raven Davis and Bonnie Devine on June 8 and 9! The Deer
Lake Artist Residencies run year round and provide opportunities for artists from
across Canada and beyond to stay and create in Burnaby. This spring, we are
pleased to work with Serpent River Ojibwa artist Bonnie Devine and Anishinaabe
two-spirit artist and educator Raven Davis as part of their residency. Their two-day
workshop on Botanical Printmaking uses traditional alum transfer methods and inks
made from locally gathered flowers, barks, and seeds. Davis and Devine will also
share the outcome of this workshop at a public talk about their practices on June 22
at 2pm, at FLEET Studio in Edmonds Park.

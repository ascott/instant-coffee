Please join Mike Grill at the opening reception for PARROT, his second solo show at Jeffrey Boone
Gallery, April 9th, 6-9PM.

"There is a deeply founded underlying structure to this selection of works which have been drawn 
from a large body of recent work. All of the works I've chosen mirror and strengthen a similar process 
against one another. There is a theme of abundance and visual mimicry to these works, that plays,
semantically, off one another when seen together." 
MIKE GRILL

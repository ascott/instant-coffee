Nourishing our Nature: Minoru Park Sensory Walk & Tour
Wednesday, March 9, 2022
10:00 AM - 11:00 AM
Location: Richmond Art Gallery & Minoru Park

Take time out to slow down and connect with the nourishing benefits of nature. This
special session of Walk Richmond will start with a short guided tour of the Richmond Art
Gallerys exhibition NOURISH, followed by an interactive walk through nearby Minoru
Park using our five senses to rediscover and connect with nature.
This free guided walk and tour is for
all ages and fitness levels, and will be led by a Walk
Richmond leader and Richmond Art Gallery staff.

Meet at 10 AM: Plaza outside the Art Gallery, 7700 Minoru Gate

Free Drop in

This is an on-site, indoor and outdoor event. Covid-19 safety protocols will be observed.
Masks must be worn while inside the art gallery.

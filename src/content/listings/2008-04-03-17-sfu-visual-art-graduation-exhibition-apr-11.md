Simon Fraser University 
School for the Contemporary Arts
Visual Art Graduation Exhibition

fifteen thousand square feet

Shilo Jones, Karina Larocque, Erika Petro, Miriam Ng, Dan Newbrook, Vanessa Kelly, Vivienne Bessette, Julie Voeuk, Deborah Roukema

Opening: Friday April 11, 7:30pm
Alexander Centre Studios 
330-611 Alexander St, Vancouver

Dates: April 11 to April 20, 2008
Hours: 11am to 7:30pm

Simon Fraser University’s School for the Contemporary Arts presents fifteen thousand square feet, the annual BFA Visual Art graduation exhibition, opening at Alexander Centre Studios (611 Alexander) on Friday, April 11. The shows title references the square footage of the school's studio space and features a range of contemporary art in media such as photography, sculpture, performance, and video. These young artists critically explore concepts and formal strategies through the lens of contemporary dialogue. Using methods such as gallery display, performative sculpture, and multimedia installations, each student's work engages a different facet of discourse both personally and culturally. Through innovation, creative experimentation, and humour, these artists question a complicated history of conceptualism and formalism. The exhibition oscillates between formal engagement and conceptual strategies.

Andrew Dadson and Jonathan Syme exhibit new works that explore the

Andrew Dadson lives and works in Vancouver. He is represented by
Galleria Franco Neoro, Torino and has exhibited at the Museum of
Contemporary Art Chicago, Art | Basel (Switzerland), the Vancouver Art
Gallery, the Seattle Art Museum, the Power Plant Gallery, and Museion
Bolzano (Italy). He received a BFA from ECUAD in 2003.

Jonathan Syme lives and works in Vancouver. He has shown recently at
CSA space, in an exhibition curated by Adam Harrison, Jeffrey Boone
Gallery, Hotel Gallery, and the White House Gallery. He received a BFA
from ACAD in 2003.

Artist Talk
Whats Copyright Got to Do with It? Maryam Jafri with Martha Rans

Saturday, July 6, 3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

Maryam Jafri joins Martha Rans, Arts Lawyer and Co-Founder and Director of Artists 
Legal Outreach in Vancouver, to discuss the meaning and significance of copyright 
issues as they surface in the visual arts. This event forms part of Artists Legal 
Outreachs Yours, Mine and Ours series of conversations about copyright, made 
possible by the generosity of the City of Vancouver Cultural Services and CreativeBC 
with additional support provided by McMillan LLP, Vancouver.

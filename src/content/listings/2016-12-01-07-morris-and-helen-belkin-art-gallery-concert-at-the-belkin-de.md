Concert at the Belkin

with UBC Contemporary Players

Friday, December 2, 2016 at 2 pm

Once again, we are pleased to welcome the UBC Contemporary Players to the 
Belkin Art Gallery for a concert inspired by the exhibition Julia Feyrer and Tamara 
Henderson: The Last Waves. Led by Directors Corey Hamm and Paolo 
Bortolussi, this graduate and undergraduate student ensemble from the UBC 
School of Music will animate the Gallery for an afternoon program celebrating 
themes from the exhibition.
All welcome. Admission is free.

PROGRAM
Allen Vizzutti, Cascades, 1981
Tommy Vu, euphonium
Nickitas Demos, Tonoi IX, 2011
Tommy Vu, euphonium
Toru Takemitsu, Paths, 1994
Kathleen Clarke, trumpet
Antoine Tisn, Emotion, 1992 with poems by David Niemann
Kathleen Clarke, trumpet
Gyrgy Ligeti, Viola Sonata: Hora Lunga (mvt 1), 1991-94
Enoch Ng, viola
Henryk Grecki, Genesis I: Elementi, 1962
Nellicia Klop, violin; Avery Tsang, viola; Laine Longton, cello

Job Summary

About the position

In support of the NGCs strategic pillars to strengthen community connections through
transformative art experiences; build a collection and program that inspire human
connection; and to empower, support and build a diverse and collaborative team, the
Gallery is hiring in the position of Senior Curator, Contemporary Art. The qualified
candidate is a leading resource in applying a global contextual framework to the
Gallerys collection and programming in terms of achieving pluralistic dialogues over
the existing collection of Canadian and international contemporary art, and in the
development of transnational frameworks for collection-building, including the
incorporation of artworks and research of non-Western, diasporic, and/or under-
represented regions/identities/pathways of origin. A commitment to sharing and
reciprocating in an inclusive manner with the broad range of communities served by
the Gallery is a key attribute for the position.

Job Summary

Under the direction of the Director, Curatorial Initiatives, the Senior Curator,
Contemporary Art plans and oversees the priorities of the department with respect to
the development and care of the collection, permanent collection installations, the
proposal of special exhibitions, ongoing research into the collection and the potential
for collaboration on curatorial programming with internal and external stakeholders;
recommends works of art for acquisition establishing their authenticity, art historical
and aesthetic significance; recommends treatments for the conservation of works of
art; researches, writes and prepares exhibition catalogues, art historical publications
and various didactic materials for installations and exhibitions; provides leadership to
curatorial staff; develops and maintains a national and international professional
networks of peers and colleagues; actively contributes to outreach, partnerships,
development and donor relations. Works collaboratively with all departments within and
outside of curatorial, and actively engages with and supports the NGCs Indigenous
Ways and Decolonization team.

DBLSPK: (A Taste of Empire)
by Jovanni Sy, Translated by Derek Chan

July 13, 2018 8pm
@theFishbowl
(1398 Cartwright Street, Granville Island)

Boca del Lupo and rice & beans theatre proudly presents DBLSPK: (A Taste of 
Empire). DBLSPK is a quarterly reading series where we present and discuss works in 
translation. We talk about what changes linguistically, culturally, and performatively. 

We are what we eat. But where does it come from? How does it get here? (A Taste 
of Empire) is a wacky and fun culinary exploration of global food domination and the 
conquest for our appetites. 

We are so excited to bring this work back to Boca del Lupo after its presentation in our 2015 
Micro Performance Series. These works have a long list of supporters (which you can find 
on our website) and we are so thrilled that the Cantonese version was recently nominated 
for a Dora Award! 

http://bocadellupo.com/dblspk-taste-empire/

Admission is by donation.

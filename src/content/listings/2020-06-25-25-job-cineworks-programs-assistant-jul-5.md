Programs Assistant

Canada Summer Jobs

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society

30 hours per week

$19.50 per hour

Start Date: July 13th

End Date: Sept 11th

Applications Close: Midnight July 5th

This is a Canada Summer Jobs Position and the potential employee must therefore
meet the eligibility requirements as listed at: https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-
social-development/services/funding/canada-summer-jobs/screening-eligibility.html

Job Description:

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society is seeking a highly motivated, creative
individual to support the development and delivery of Cineworks education and
public programming.

The Programs Assistant will report to and be mentored by the Community and
Education Liaison (CEL), and will be a key participant in the development and
delivery of the arts organizations exciting new and existing programs.

Please note: we continue to follow the guidance of provincial health authorities and
will work with the successful candidate to create a work-plan
that includes remote
work. Working onsite at our Howe street facility is possible but not required.

Key Functions of the role include:

- Provide research assistance and administrative support to the Community &
Education Liaison (CEL) in the development of programs with a focus on analog film
media

- Assist with the development and delivery of Cineworks marketing and
communication initiatives with a focus on creating content for social media.

- Develop community programs highlighting analogue film practices.

- Support Cineworks community-building and partnered programs with Echo Park
Film Centre North including our virtual CineCamp.

- Support delivery of virtual programming via videoconferencing platforms.

- Assist in the coordination of Project-C, an open call for films that explore big ideas
in sustainable ways.

- Support the coordination and delivery of workshops when necessary.

- Assist CEL in the research and development of programs to address barriers to
participation
in film for marginalized communities.

Requirements:

- Some experience working in public programming, preferably with an arts focus.

- Ability to work some evenings and weekends to support program delivery

- Experience with and/or knowledge of filmmaking practices including analogue

film.

- Excellent writing and language skills.

- Ability to problem-solve and creatively take on challenges.

- Exceptional organization and strong interpersonal skills.

- Experience with or interest in community engagement.

- Experience with or interest in expanding media accessibility.

- A team player but also comfortable with self-direction.

- Proficiency with standard office equipment, Mac computers, web-based

applications (Google Drive), Microsoft Office, and database software.

- Working knowledge of Mailchimp, Instagram, Facebook, EventBrite and

videoconferencing platforms such as Jitsi Meet and Zoom.

- Experience using design/graphics software such as In Design, Photoshop, and
Illustrator is an asset.

Please send your cover letter and resume to education@cineworks.ca with the
subject: Programs Assistant

Call closes on Midnight July 5th PST.

Interviews will be conducted via Zoom on July 8 and 9, 2020

Within our organizational processes and structures, Cineworks acknowledges that
work needs to be done to expand our representation of and resources to peoples
from diverse backgrounds. We strongly encourage applications from Black
Indigenous People of Color, peoples living with disabilities, peoples of
underrepresented groups, and those who have faced systemic barriers at
institutions. We also recognize that diverse lived experiences and knowledge can
support a candidates eligibility outside of formal educational and/or employment
classifications listed in our Requirements.

EVENT: David Wisdom: Summer Slide Night
DATE: Sunday, July 22
TIME: 7pm
LOCATION: Vancouver Art Gallery, 750 Hornby Street

CBC radio personality, writer and artist David Wisdom back for another round of
his legendary slide shows. Have a drink on the Gallery's Rooftop Pavilion and
enjoy unique perspectives on art, music and life in the city. A musical guest
closes each night.

Free for Members or with Gallery admission.

Cash bar available

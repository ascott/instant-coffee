SelcouthCircl. presents a call for artist submissions to the Evening Gallery. The space is
an emerging platform that will be hosting a series of exhibitions and cultural events in
the heart of Vancouver. Situated off of Arbutus and Broadway, the space aims to act as
a cultural hub for artists and patrons alike. Exhibiting works by emerging and local
artists, the
Evening Gallery will offer a unique experience in the heart of Vancouver with
its extended hours and lounge-like ambience.
We invite artists to submit works under the broad canopy of Discord and Dissidence "
contributing to the art of anxiety. Inspired by international leaders such as Ai Wei Wei,
Yue Minjun, Kent Monkman, Laurence Paul Yuxweluptun, and the like, we are looking
for provoking and engaging works that can contribute to, form and shape these
dialogues.

All mediums accepted.

The exhibition will take place during the last week of November, opening reception
Friday, November 22, 2019.

Exhibition Submission Deadline: Tuesday, November 5, 2019

To apply please visit selcouthcircl.com and follow the submissions link with further
instructions.

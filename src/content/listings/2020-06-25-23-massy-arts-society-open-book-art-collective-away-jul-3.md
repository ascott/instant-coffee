Open Book Art Collective
Away
July 3 - August 4th

By the time I finish this story you will have decided to hug the land - the real earth -
the trees in the orchard, the timbers of
this house. You will have decided never to go
away.

Created as an echo to Jane Urquharts 1993 novel Away, the Open Book Art
Collective's exhibition bring together personal narratives of displacement. Away
traces the lives of four generations of women across landscapes: from the rocky
coasts of 19th century Northern Ireland to the slums of Montreal, to the present-day
shores of Lake Ontario " emotive, political and literal landscapes that both enchant
and devastate the characters. Historic connections to land resonate with
contemporary realities of refugee crisis, colonial legacy, and industrial development.

The artists of Open Book Art Collective respond to the poetic and cyclical storytelling
found in the novel, intending to honour the richness of the literature. Threads of Irish
mythology and spirituality weave throughout the corporeal, earthy, and material-
focused imagery found in the exhibit. We invite viewers to contemplate bodily
responsiveness to ones unseen inner forces,
the symbols we cling to in order to tell
our life stories, the often unknown but intimate ties to ones matrilineal heritage, and
the ever-evolving understanding of our relationship to the non-human world.

Please note, our gallery space is not accessible at this time. The gallery is on the
second floor without a lift or elevator.

We acknowledge we operate on the traditional, ancestral, unceded, and occupied
territory of the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh Nations.

Chinatown 2050, an Online Film Screening and Conversation Between Directors Linda
Zhang and Maxim Gertler-Jaffe: July 16, 12PM

How might the pandemic shape the future of Torontos Chinatown? Five scenarios
tackling this question are imagined by Asian Canadian youth, with their
speculations
brought to life by a visual collage of dreamlike LiDAR 3D modelling scans.

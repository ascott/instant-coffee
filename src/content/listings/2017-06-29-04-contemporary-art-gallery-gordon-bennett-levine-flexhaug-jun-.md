Gordon Bennett - Be Polite
June 30 to September 24, 2017
Preview and opening reception: Thursday, June 29, 6-9pm
Alvin Balkind Gallery and Events Room

During the preview there will be a tour of the exhibition by Institute of Modern Art, 
Brisbane, Co-Director, Aileen Burns at 6.15pm

Working closely with the Estate of Gordon Bennett and IMA Brisbane Be Polite 
comprises of a selection of works on paper including drawing, painting, watercolour, 
poetry, and essays from the early 1990s through to the early 2000s.

======================================================

A Sublime Vernacular: The Landscape Paintings of Levine Flexhaug
June 30 to September 24, 2017
Preview and opening reception: Thursday, June 29, 6-9pm
B.C. Binning Gallery

This first overview of the extraordinary career of Levine Flexhaug (1918 - 1974), 
born in the Treelon area near Climax, Saskatchewan, brings together approximately 
450 of the artist's paintings as well as several of his mural-sized works. 

======================================================

Curator Talk - Nancy Tousley
Friday June 30, 6pm

Join guest curator Nancy Tousley as she discusses the research undertaken with 
Peter White to produce the exhibition Levine Flexhaug, 'A Sublime Vernacular: The 
Landscape Paintings'.

POETRY READING
Sunday March 24, 2 p.m.
Admission is free of charge

In his arresting solo exhibition, Manifest | Trace, West Vancouver artist Pierre Coupey 
presents work in a variety of media that foreground the importance of language and the 
immense impact it has on shaping our world. Join Pierre Coupey and poets Matea Kuli- and 
George Stanley for an afternoon in the gallery where visual and literary arts intersect.

PIERRE COUPEY 
Pierre Coupey, a founding co-editor of The Georgia Straight and founding editor of The 
Capilano Review, moved to Vancouver from Montreal in the mid1960s. He has published 
nine books of poetry, chapbooks, and catalogues, and exhibited in Canada and abroad. His 
work is represented in private, corporate, university and public collections. He received the 
Distinguished Artist Award from FANS in 2013, and in 2018 was inducted into the Royal 
Canadian Academy of Arts.

MATEA KULIC 
Born in Belgrade, Matea Kuli- lives in Vancouver as a guest on unceded Coast Salish 
territories. Her work has been published in literary magazines across Canada, most recently 
in the Polymorphous Translation Issue of The Capilano Review and the Intersections Issue 
of Poetry is Dead. In 2017 her poem A Precise Benefit was nominated for a Pushcart Prize 
and was included in The Fourth Decade of Making Room: Forty Years of Room Magazine. 
She studied under the mentorship of poet Jen Currin at SFUs Writers Studio.

GEORGE STANLEY 
Born in San Francisco, George Stanley has lived in BC since the early 1970s. He edited and 
contributed to the intergenerational Vancouver literary journal Tads(1996-2001), through 
which he helped mentor a generation of younger Vancouver writers. He has published ten 
books, including Gentle Northern Summer (1995), A Tall, Serious Girl: Selected Poems 
1957-2000 (2003), Vancouver: A Poem (2008), After Desire(2013) and North of California 
St.: Selected Poems (2014). Widely recognized for the originality of his work, he received 
the Shelley Memorial Award for Poetry in 2006.

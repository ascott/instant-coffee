Event: Mad Pride Parade and Cabaret July 14th 2 PM to 7PM

Place: The Gathering Place Community
Center Gallery, 609 Helmcken

Starting at 2PM the Carnival Band will leads us in a parade around the block. We will
meet up at the Gathering Place Gallery at 3PM for the Cabaret featuring Iveno,
Beautiful Lizards, and Sarah Jickling, Kagan Goh and emcee Jacques Lalonde. At
5:30 to 7 PM the artists from the Gifts of Madness visual arts show will be there.
Pizzas, snacks and refreshments will be offered.

Banners/placards workshops on July 10 and 11th, 2 to 4 PM at the Gathering Place
art studio.

Mad Pride is an arts, culture, and heritage festival was created by psychiatric
survivors, consumers, mad people, and folks the world has labelled mentally ill.
Mad Pride is a movement that celebrates the human rights and spectacular culture
of people considered very different by our society.

The event is supported by the Canada Council for the Arts, the Connection Salon
Collective and the Gathering Place
Community Center.

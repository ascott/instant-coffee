Shadow Theatre Workshop with Mind of a Snail
Oct. 27, 1:00 - 4:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

This 3 hour workshop is an introduction to shadow play using the overhead projector. We will
use our own silhouettes, found objects, scrap materials, and puppets to demonstrate and
explore a variety of visual storytelling techniques and theatre games. This workshop is fun and
active with lots of laughter.
Mind of a Snail is a compost-modern theatre company based in Vancouver. Since 2003, Chlo
Ziner and Jessica Gabriel have been developing a multi-layered style of visual performances
that play at the intersection of puppetry, visual arts, clowning & music. They have toured their
award-winning shows across Canada, USA, and Taiwan. As artistic collaborators, they are
interested in exploring beyond the boundaries of traditional theatre; and creating magical
immersive experiences for their audiences and workshop participants.

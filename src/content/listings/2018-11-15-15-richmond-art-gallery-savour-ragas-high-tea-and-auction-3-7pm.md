Savour: RAGAs High Tea and Auction

Saturday, November 17, 2018
3:00 PM - 7:00 PM
Tea Service: 3:30 pm 

Location: Richmond Art Gallery, 7700 Minoru Gate

Auction Preview 

Schedule of events:
3:30pm " Welcome
3:40pm " Tea Service
4:30pm " Best Dressed Awards
4:45pm " Live performance
5:15pm " Auction Lot #1 Closed
5:20pm " Wild Sweets Chocolate Talk
5:50pm " Auction Lot #2 Closed
7:00pm " Event closes

Join us for an afternoon high tea with performances, tasty snacks, handmade cocktails and 
a silent art auction representing the gallerys past, present and future all to benefit the 
diverse programming of the Richmond Art Gallery. Dress to impress in your best high tea 
attire. Best dressed High Tea and best Fascinator will be awarded at the event.

Alongside the event, RAGA presents a unique all chocolate installation titled Abstract 
Geometry developed by the creative team of Wild Sweets. Dominique & Cindy Duby of Wild 
Sweets are internationally acclaimed award-winning pastry chefs / chocolatiers. The duo 
have been ranked one of the 25 Best chocolatiers in the World, awarded Best Chocolatiers 
in America and ranked Top 10 Pastry Chefs in the World while leading Pastry Team 
Canada. More info on Wild Sweets here.

Silent Auction includes works by: Diyan Achjadi, Carl Beam, Richard Bedwash, Chris 
Charlebois, Tony Chu, Caroline Dahlmanns, Margreth Fry, Chris Gallagher, Greg Girard, 
Jennifer Heine, Jill Henderson, Jeanette Jarville, Russna Kaur, Miao Ting Lee, Brian 
(Pashigeesic) Marion, Jonathan Middleton, Adrienne Moore, Russel M. Noganosh, Holly 
Schmidt, Ho Tam, Karen Tam, Pierre Vassura, Biliana Velkova, Lorraine Wellman, Janice 
Wu, Debra Zhou and more. Plus special items from Gateway Theatre, Richmond Oval, Arts 
Club Theatre Company, Vancouver International Film Festival, Vancouver International Wine 
Festival, Vancouver Art Gallery, Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, Richmond Center- 
Indigo Spirit Books and Gift and many more.

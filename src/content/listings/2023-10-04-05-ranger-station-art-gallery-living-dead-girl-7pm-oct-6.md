Exhibition runs October 6th to November 5th at the Ranger Station Art Gallery (98
Rockwell Drive, Harrison Hot Springs). The opening reception will be on Friday, October
6th at 7:00 pm.

Artist Stephanie Gagne's show Living Dead Girl will consider how insect wings are used
across art history and contemporary media, to simultaneously represent both beauty and
death.

Stephanie Gagne is a visual artist based in Vancouver. She received a bachelor of fine
arts degree from Emily Carr University and a master of fine arts degree from Simon
Fraser University. Her interests include popular culture and childhood nostalgia. Gagne's
interdisciplinary projects involve sculpture, photography, drawing, and video.

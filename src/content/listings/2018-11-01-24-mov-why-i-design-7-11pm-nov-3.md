Museum of Vancouver invites Vancouverites to explore and celebrate innovation in 
action. 25 designers stationed throughout the museums galleries discuss their work 
during Why I Design. Why I Design spotlights the process of invention. 

This years Why I Design pays particular attention to Social Impact through design 
strategies of upcycling and repurposed materials. Participating designers will 
engage you with stories behind the development of everyday technologies and cool 
things youve never seen before. You can ask the designers"ranging from those 
emerging their careers to innovative leaders in international corporations"what 
motivates them, how they do what they do, and why Vancouver inspires them to 
keep doing it.

Date: Saturday, November 3, 2018

We are pleased to announce the launch of our summer solstice sound project.
Sound of the Sun, Volume I is the weird, entrancing, celebratory, sometimes gloomy
yet euphoric summer mixtape of planetary travel, solar interventions, story telling,
and frog choruses you didnt know you needed for summer 2023.

As UNIT/PITTs first iteration of a multi-part project of sonic-celestial
explorations
connecting with the seasons, the Sound of the Sun project asks participants to
consider their relationship to cycles of solar movement as the days lengthen on this
half of the planet"to observe, respond, record, and retell through music, poetry, field
recordings, storytelling, and any other audio-based methods in order to speak to this
important time of the solar year. Featuring sound works by 10 artists, Sound of the
Sun, Volume I beckons the promise of sun-worship, long beach days, A/C, forest
fires, and excess levels of Vitamin D.

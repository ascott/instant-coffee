The Media Arts Committee would like to invite you to the presentation
of new works created by our current residents, Florence Debeugny and
Daniel Majer.

This call was SOUND IN CONTEXT: FIELD RECORDING and the
works are focused on exploring the area of field recording and
contextual sound art work. For the works created, we encouraged
artists to concentrate on the recording and field aspect of producing
their works rather than post-production.

Artist In Residence - Florence Debeugny is a photographic and
videographic artist in two fields: documenting old industrial sites in BC
and exploring photo and video abstract subjects. She says that her
"first exposure to the connection between visual and audio was the
exhibition I produced of a 5-projector installation with a soundscape
composed by Hildegard Westerkamp and exhibited by the Western
Front. After this and while producing subsequent projects mainly
focused on photography, I realized that the human presence was
missing in the new artistic project I was working on at that time. Audio
recordings was the way I chose to add this dimension."

Commission - Daniel Majer is an electronic musician and sound artist
based in Vancouver, Canada. A pupil of Giorgio Magnanensi, Daniel
has collaborated with visual artists and musicians alike in film,
installation and live electroacoustic work. An LP of electronic material
entitled "Intimacy Muscle" is expected to be released in the summer of
2016.

This event is free and all ages.

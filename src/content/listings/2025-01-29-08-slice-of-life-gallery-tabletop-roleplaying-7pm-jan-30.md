Curious about D&D but never had the chance to play? OLD SCHOOL TABLETOP
GAMES are quick to learn and best taught over the course of play. Whether this is
your first time hearing of Dungeons & Dragons or you're a seasoned 5e veteran,
BASIC
DUNGEONS & DRAGONS (1980) will hold plenty of surprises. NO
EXPERIENCE NECESSARY!

What Can I Expect?
- A character of your own creation.
- 2 hours of action and adventure.
- Rolling dice.
- A world of high fantasy.
- A narrative shaped by YOU!

CAN'T MAKE A SESSION? We're running an OPEN TABLE, which means each
session is its own contained adventure. Whether you want to be a main character in
a long-running campaign or are just curious enough to see what one session is like,
you are welcome to join as fits your schedule. If you can't attend a session, your
character will stay safe in the KEEP, going about their daily business, until you return
and it is time to depart on another adventure.

Scrolls are suspended exploring drawing as a traditional form and as a

street windows. As the illumination and glow of natural and artificial
light sources shift throughout the day and night so does the experience of

both the title of the exhibition and the name for this new artists' team.
This work combines stories that respond to the traditional iconic imagery
found in representations of life and personal histories growing up in rural
BC in the 70's and 80's. This includes landscape, memory, and nostalgia of
growing up in highway towns, Squamish and Westbank.

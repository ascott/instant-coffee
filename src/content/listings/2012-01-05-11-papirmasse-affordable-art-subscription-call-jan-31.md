CALL: Papirmasse Affordable Art Subscription seeks artists & writers

Papirmasse is a publication that seeks to demystify and democratize the 
art experience by sending a monthly print with art on the front and writing 
on the back to subscribers all over the world for a mere $5 a month. We 
are seeking artists and writers to help round out our 2012 print run. 

Artists and writers are invited to submit samples of their work, along with 
a biography and CV, to kirsten@papirmasse.com 

Please spend some time on our website before applying to get a sense of 
our aesthetic.

For full details, including word count, visit 
[http://papirmasse.com/art/2008/submissions](http://papirmasse.com/art/2008/submissions)

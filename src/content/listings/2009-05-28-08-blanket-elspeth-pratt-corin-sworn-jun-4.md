June 4 - July 3, 2009
Opening Reception Thursday June 4 6-9pm

Blanket is pleased to present a two person exhibition of work by Elspeth 
Pratt and Corin Sworn. 

Elspeth Pratt’s new work engages with the role for ornamentation in the 
constitution of architecture. Ornament problematizes the duality of form 
and function and allows us the possibility of thinking about their 
relationship in new ways. The spaces that she considers are spaces of 
authority ranging from the obvious spaces for incarceration to the more 
subtle instances power and control reside in furniture or spatial 
delineations implied within a space. Elspeth Pratt has had solo exhibitions 
at the Charles H Scott Gallery (Vancouver), Contemporary Art Gallery (Vancouver), Artspeak 
(Vancouver), YYZ (Toronto) and the Southern Alberta Art Gallery (Lethbridge). Pratt has 
exhibited in group exhibitions in Canada, Japan, Australia, Taiwan and Italy, including Weak 
Thought at the Vancouver Art Gallery, Architettura: Astriazione in Rome, and Contingent: 
Eva Hesse, Elspeth Pratt and Martha Townsend at the Dunlop Art Gallery. 
Elspeth Pratt lives and works in Vancouver, BC.

Corin Sworn, often combining precise references and oblique allusions, 
offers a world where ideas and symbols shift meaning of popular perceptions 
of social structures. Uncovering some of the ways in which design functions 
as language, Sworn brings into question contestations involved in readings 
of the past, drawing attention to the embedded nature of cultural constructs. 
Having received a BA in psychology and art history from the University of 
British Columbia, Sworn continued her academic path at Central Saint 
Martins College of Art and Design (London, UK), Emily Carr University of Art 
and Design (Vancouver, BC) and Glasgow School of Art, where she recently 
received her MFA. Sworn has exhibited at galleries including the Morris 
and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, Or Gallery, Artspeak, and Access Artist-Run 
Centre, Vancouver, BC; VTO Gallery, Cover Up, and Gasworks (curated by 
Goshka Makuga), London, UK; Wolfsburg, Germany; and ZieherSmith in New York, 
NY. Sworn has been selected to participate in East International at Norwich 
Gallery (Norwich, UK) this coming summer. Sworn lives and works in Glasgow.

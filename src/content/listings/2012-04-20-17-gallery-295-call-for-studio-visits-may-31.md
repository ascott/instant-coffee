Call for studio visits:

Gallery 295 is currently searching for emerging artists for programing in 
it's 2013 year. Please send us a short email with a link to your website or 
a selection of images.

Gallery 295 is a new project space in Vancouver committed to providing 
space and facilities to support the finished production of photographic-
based works. Our mandate is to engage with contemporary photography 
and related practices, focusing on emerging Canadian artists. Through 
our association with The Lab, we are able to provide much needed access 
to photographic and printing facilities in Vancouver, allowing young artists 
the means to complete and exhibit their projects.

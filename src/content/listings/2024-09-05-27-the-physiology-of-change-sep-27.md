The Physiology of Change - An 8 week somatic inquiry container facilitated by artist
and somatic educator Katy Slany

Fridays at 10am (PST) starting Sept.27th on Zoom

How can the imagination support us in the rewriting of our physiology?

Over the course of 8 weeks we will explore how engaging our imagination through
somatic practices can support the rewriting of our physiology. When we come back
to the wisdom of our sensate body we can connect again with ourselves, with the
intelligence of the world around us, with the integrated wisdom of our ancestors and
with each other.

Through this process of re-embodiment we acknowledge the systems of oppression
that live within us while also centering empowerment, pleasure, and transformation.
From this new embodied center we can create what we long for most, both in our
individual lives but in the collective as well.

This course is designed for seekers, creators and
change makers or anyone who
has a body and is longing to transmute stress, anxiety, pain or grief into something
more life giving.

Please feel free to share with anyone who might resonate.

Concert at the Belkin: Collective Acts

WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 28 AT 2 PM 

Join us for a concert by the UBC Contemporary Players at the Belkin Art Gallery. 
Ensemble Director Paolo Bortolussi presents a program that celebrates the Belkin Art 
Gallerys current exhibition Beginning With the Seventies: Collective Acts. This term, the 
concert will feature works by Gyrgy Kurtg, Harry Somers, Frederic Rzewski and UBC 
School of Musics own Stephen Chatman.

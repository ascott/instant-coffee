Conversation with Richard Cavell and Barbara Cole
Saturday, September 21 at 2 pm
As part of UBC's Homecoming 2024, join us for a talk about the conservation and re-
installation of Lionel and Patricia Thomas's 1958 mosaic mural, Symbols for Education.
This outdoor mosaic mural has been part of the UBC campus community since it was
commissioned by the graduating class in 1958. Since 2020, it has been undergoing
conservation and restoration work and will be re-installed in early October on campus.
Barbara Cole, founder of Cole Projects and the Belkin's former Curator of Outdoor Art,
and Richard Cavell, UBC Department of English and co-founder of the Bachelor of
Media Studies Program, will discuss the mural's conservation, its history on
campus,
and the idea of the mosaic as a particular art form and mode of information that reflects
the information age of the late 1950s-1960s when art, architecture and design merged
so aptly and, when Marshall McLuhan who was often at UBC during these years, uttered
his phrase, "the medium is the message." Registration is not required, but seating is on
a first come, first served basis

GRAVEYARD SHIFT

Celebrate Artspeak's launch of its OFFSITE program with a graveyard shift
at the Mountain View Cemetery.

Friday, September 5, 2008

10pm - 1am (after SWARM)

Mountain View Cemetery, Celebration Hall
5455 Fraser Street (enter at 39th Avenue, Vancouver BC)

Drinks | Food | Music | Thrills

Cello Quartet | Michael Turner Types | No Kids
Fire Work by Kevin Schmidt | DJ Ian Wyatt

Tickets $10
Get them at Artspeak or at the door

After Artspeak's opening of Learn to Read Art: A History of Printed Matter
and Christian Kliegel's ONSITE
as part of SWARM, come to the graveyard and follow the lights to
Celebration Hall.

Parking on Fraser Street
or
Take the no. 8 Fraser bus. It stops at Fraser and 39th
(catch it kitty corner from Artspeak on Cordova Street at Carrall Street)

Over the next two years, Artspeak is shifting OFFSITE, taking artist
projects outside the gallery to the street, airwaves and other alternative
sites in Vancouver and beyond. OFFSITE will include performances, public
projects, speaker series and collaborations that engage with innovative
strategies of production. At the same time, Artspeak's current gallery
space is transforming into an exciting venue for publications, printed
matter, readings and dialogue.

Mountain View Cemetery first opened in 1887. Closed to new burials for the
last two decades, it has served as the staging ground for several
contemporary art projects. It is currently undergoing a major
transformation under the direction of Birmingham & Wood, Architects and
Planners with Sandra Moore, MAIBC Principal-in-Charge and Annabel Vaughan,
Intern Architect.

DAREU!
Call for Participants

Writer-Directors 17-24
Participate in workshops with theatre & film professionals
Create an Augmented Reality site-specific experience
Our 2nd Story mobile app gives access to your content onsite
Theme: RISK
Place: Granville Island
Workshops: Wednesdays 4-8 PM, March 11 â€“ June 17, 2015
Premiere: September 10-20, 2015 at Vancouver Fringe

Using our custom mobile app, participants will collaborate in the creation
of an immersive site-specific Augmented Reality experience for audiences at
the Vancouver Fringe Festival. Working on the theme of "RISK" each
Writer-Director produces a short video-based piece that is integrated into
the app. Viewers will be able to discover these stories at the Granville
Island location during the Vancouver Fringe event. Content will also be
available online after the event. Participants will attend a series of
workshops taking them from concept to premiere. Led by Eric Rhys Miller,
Artistic Producer of The Only Animal, with Tricia Collins (Access to Media
Education Society) and Sophia Dagher (sophiadagher.com) as well as guest
instructors. Topics include:
Augmented Reality, Site-Specific Creation, Writing, Storyboards, Planning A
Shoot, Directing, Camera/Light/Lens Fundamentals, Production Design,
Animation, Sound Design, Editing & Post-Production, Installation & Event
Production.
The Only Animal will provide mentorship, instruction, workshop materials,
video & editing equipment, design support, a production materials budget,
production & event support including printed & social media publicity
materials.
The Vancouver Fringe will feature DAREU! in their printed and online
program and provide Front of House and Box Office support.
All participants will receive an Artist Rush Pass for free admission at any
available Fringe show!

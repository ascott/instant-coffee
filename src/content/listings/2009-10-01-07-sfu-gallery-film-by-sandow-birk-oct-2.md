In Smog and Thunder: The Great War of the Californias

The SFU Gallery Presents: Two Free Screenings of a Film by Sandow Birk. 
Friday October 2 & Wednesday October 14th, 7pm
SFU Vancouver Campus 515 W. Hastings, Room 1600 

The film is constructed from pans of the 100+ ‘history paintings’ that Birk created ‘documenting’ the 
‘recent’ war between San Francisco and Los Angeles. It is in the style of the documentaries that Ken 
Burns has done for PBS, with, however, mock-serious narration that is perfectly suited to such an
imaginary series of battles. 45 min.

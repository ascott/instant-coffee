JOB POSTING: Director of Operations and Development
Mercer Union, A Centre for Contemporary Art

Deadline: Friday, January 15th 2010 at 5:00PM

Mercer Union, A Centre for Contemporary Art is one of Canada's leading
artist-run centres for contemporary art with an international
reputation. Established in 1979, Mercer Union is a not-for-profit
charitable organization dedicated to the existence of contemporary
art. Conceptually and aesthetically engaging art acts as a catalyst
for a new conversation, and we insist on providing the place for its
production and exhibition. In addition to a programme of 12
exhibitions a year consisting of local and international artists,
Mercer Union pursues critical activities such as lectures, screenings,
performances, publications, events and special projects.

Working with the Director of Exhibitions and Publications, and the
Board of Directors, the successful candidate will be responsible for
implementing and managing the organization's budgets, grant
applications, fundraising initiatives, public programmes, audience
development, and special projects.

QUALIFICATIONS:
The successful candidate will possess:
- An MA, MFA, MBA, or equivalent experience in financial governance,
cultural management, marketing, or development.
- A minimum of 3 years experience working in a non-profit art gallery
or equivalent
- Strong experience managing budgets and developing financial
forecasts in a non-profit environment
- Proven track record in successful grant writing and meeting
fundraising targets
- Macintosh computer skills and facility with accounting software,
Excel, Word, Filemaker Pro, and Quickbooks
- Experience supervising part-time staff and desire to work with and
coordinate volunteers
- A solid knowledge of Canadian and international contemporary art
- Strong verbal and written communications skills
- Excellent interpersonal skills

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Setting financial priorities; developing and overseeing short and
long-term operational planning (business plan) for the organization in
collaboration with the Director of Exhibitions and Publications and
the Board
- Managing all financial activity, including bookkeeping, banking,
payroll, and preparing regular financial reports for the Board
- Overseeing the operational management of the gallery, including
negotiating and managing contracts
- Developing short and long-term strategies to meet fundraising
targets and actively seeking out new avenues of revenue including
donor prospecting and stewardship
- Researching, writing and coordinating operational, special project
and employment grant applications (governmental, foundational,
corporate, and private)
- Overseeing the membership campaign including membership renewal,
acquisition and stewardship of annual donors and prospects
- Coordination, and administration of Board fundraising events
- Providing exhibition and publication coordination support to the
Co-Director of Exhibitions and Publications
- Developing outreach and communications strategies in collaboration
with the Director of Exhibitions and Publications and the Board
- Acting as a spokesperson for the organization to government,
funding agencies, the media and the public
- Opportunity to develop public programmes: lectures, panel
discussions, and other educational events in collaboration with the
Director of Exhibitions and Publications and the Board
- Managing, recruiting and hiring support staff and volunteers
- Preparing Board of Directors meetings, including developing Board
of Directors agendas with the President of the Board and ensuring all
Board Members are abreast of current issues
- Attending all Board and Committee meetings as required
- Flexibility to work evenings and weekends as required for openings
and special events

REMUNERATION:
$37,500 per annum
Position includes benefits
36 hours per week

Interested candidates are requested to submit a resume, cover letter,
and three writing samples by 5:00PM on Friday, January 15, 2010 via
email to jobs@mercerunion.org to the attention of the Hiring
Committee. Please send application documents as PDFs.

No phone calls please. Only successful candidates will be contacted.
Mercer Union is an equal opportunity employer.

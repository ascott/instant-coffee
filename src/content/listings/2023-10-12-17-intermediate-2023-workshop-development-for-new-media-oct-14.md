INTER/MEDIATE 2023: New Media Co-Creation Workshop
COLLABORATIVE ART EDUCATION WORKSHOP DEVELOPMENT FOR NEW
MEDIA

ARTISTS.

VANCOUVER OCT 14 - 28, and NOV 18

Following the success of their previous
iterations, INTER/MEDIATE
announces a new 4-part workshop for aspiring artists and educators interested in
developing their own new media educational practices.
Supported by Emily Carr University of Art and Design and the City of Vancouvers
Cultural Grants Program, this years event will continue its aim of igniting change in the
art and technology education community through roundtables, workshops, and
mentorship. Now in its third iteration, INTER/MEDIATEs event will be held at a series
of venues throughout the city including Atelier 254 and VIVO Media Arts Center.

Join us for Odd Numbers: Performance Roulette at The Birdhouse!

WHAT:
Short performances of 1, 3, or 5 minutes by a collection of local artists performing in
any and all genres! Performers wont know whether their time limit is 1, 3, or 5
minutes til they go on stage. When their time is up lights and sound gets cut and its
on to the next player. Come out for some low stakes, high fun times!

WHEN:
Thurs, Mar 28
Doors 8PM
Performance 8:30PM onwards

WHERE:
The Birdhouse
44 W 4th Ave
(The building is right next to Boombox.)

WHY:
This event is a fundraiser for Boombox, to replace stolen equipment and cover repair
costs after a break-in in November. AND we love hanging out with our awesome
community and are excited to gather in a space that can fit more than Boomboxs
tiny 30-person capacity. Hope to see you there!

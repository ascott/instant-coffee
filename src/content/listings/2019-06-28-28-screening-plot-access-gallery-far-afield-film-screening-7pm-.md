Film Screening
Ursula Biemann, Jayce Salloum, Paul Wong
Tuesday, July 2
7 PM - 8 PM
PLOT @ Access Gallery
222 E. Georgia Street

Please join us for a screening of three films, with a short discussion to follow. The films 
include: Deep Weather (2013) by Ursula Biemann [8 min: 58 sec]; untitled part 4: terra 
(in)cognita (2005) by Jayce Salloum; and Scorched (2010) by Paul Wong [8 min: 40 
sec].

This talk takes place as part of the multifaceted research creation project, "Under the 
Beating Sun, From Summer to Summer," presented by Far Afield at Access Gallery's 
PLOT space. More about the project, and details on the weekly upcoming events can be 
found on Far Afield's website: 

www.farafield.ca

For more, contact Caitlin Chaisson: caitlin.chaisson@gmail.com

This project has been generously supported by the Canada Council for the Arts, British 
Columbia Arts Council, and Access Gallery.

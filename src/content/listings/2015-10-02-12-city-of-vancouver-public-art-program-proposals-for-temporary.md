DEADLINE EXENDED TO OCTOBER 5th - 12 NOON

Platforms 2015 - 2017: Coastal City
Call to Artists - Proposals for Temporary 2-D Outdoor Works
City of Vancouver - Public Art Program | Vancouver, BC, Canada
Deadline for submissions: Monday, October 5, 2015 - 12 Noon
Budget: $5,000

The City of Vancouver Public Art Program invites artists to propose 
new artworks for 2-D platforms on the streets and public places of 
Vancouver, BC. For this call, we ask artists to consider the general 
theme of "coastal city" - the border between land and sea, the 
designation of boundaries and life in a region near the shore, the 
changing land and ocean-scape, the influx of people and goods, the 
unique ecosystems, challenges, and opportunities - the circumstances 
that make coastal cities such as Vancouver unique.

New works will be commissioned for the following platforms:

* LED Video Screens

* Transit Shelter Ad Space

* Canada Line City Centre Station

* Queen Elizabeth Plaza

Up to 15 artists will be shortlisted and asked to develop a full concept 
plan for approval before completing the work. Selected artists will 
supply a high-quality digital file meeting the specifications of the 
commissioned platform. The City will be responsible for the cost of 
production and installation.

ELIGIBILITY

This opportunity is open to artists living and working in the Pacific 
Northwest, including British Columbia and Washington State. 
Aboriginal artists and artists of diverse communities and backgrounds 
warmly welcomed.

PRINT READY: Dynamo Zine Exhibition (Zine-a-palooza!!!) 

Dynamo Arts Association is pleased to kick off the new year with a one day 
exhibit of zine induced mania. Over 10 artists will be exhibiting their latest 
endeavours into independent artist publishing. These artists work collaboratively 
and independently, creating subversive and often allusive dialogues. Print Ready 
is coordinated and curated by Michael Lachman and Nathan Jones.

Print Ready is a showcase for self-published art zines including works by: Amiel 
Gonzales, Doug Wideen, Josef Carhoun, Justin Gradin, Linton Murphy, Michel 
Groat, Michael Lachman, Nathan Jones, Phadra Harder, Sarah Davidson, 
Stephine McDonell, Tylor MacMillan, and more.

ONE DAY ONLY! 
January 18th
4pm - 10pm
Dynamo Arts Association
30 EAST 6TH AVE VANCOUVER, BC

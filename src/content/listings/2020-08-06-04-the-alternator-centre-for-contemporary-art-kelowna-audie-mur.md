Audie Murray - As Old As The Hills
July 31 - September 12

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is pleased to have artist Audie Murray
exhibiting in the main space of the gallery. Audie Murray is an artist who also practices
handpoke and skin stitching tattooing traditions. In As Old As The Hills, Audie explores
how the adornment of our bodies through clothing, jewelry and tattoos carry with them
power and protection. She is curious about tattoo culture as a whole and how the term
traditional tattoos carry multiple definitions. In this exhibition, Audie asks many
questions, such as how do old school traditional American tattoos reflect in current
culture? How does historical slang continue to perpetuate colonial perspectives? And, at
what point does tattoo culture at large converge with Indigenous cultures?

Find the answers to these inquiries and more in the main space from July 31 to
September 12.

https://www.alternatorcentre.com/events/audie-murray-as-old-as-the-hills

Amanda Wood - Robustness To Uncertainty
July 31 - September 12

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is pleased to host Vancouver based artist in
the window space of the gallery. Amanda Wood is an interdisciplinary artist living and
working on the traditional, ancestral and unceded territory of the Coast Salish peoples.
Her practice is process and materials driven. Current work considers time, labour,
physical gesture, connectivity, digital space and self-organizing systems.

In Robustness To Uncertainty, Amanda explores how digital processes and physical
experiences
can be connected through weaving. She creates complex forms through
manipulation of materials in a systematic way.

Robustness To Uncertainty opens Friday, July 31 and runs until September 12.

https://www.alternatorcentre.com/events/amandawood

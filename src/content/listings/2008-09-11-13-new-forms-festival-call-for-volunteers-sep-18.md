CALL FOR VOLUNTEERS: NEW FORMS FESTIVAL: MOSAICS, SEPTEMBER 18 -27, 2008
+++The Convergence of Media Art, Electronic Music, and Digital Culture+++

New Forms Festival is an annual international showcase of new media,
performance, exhibitions, digital video, and electronic music. This year,
the festival's theme is Mosaics; examining relations of cross-cultural
media through video, sound, internet, print, performance and exhibition.
The festival showcases cutting edge local and international artists and is
a recognized member of the international multi-media festival movement.

We are seeking inspired people to help activate and participate in the
festival through volunteering. Volunteering with NFF08 is a unique way to
contribute to the festival while gaining intimate insight into the festival
and the artists works. Connect with like-minded people, learn new skills,
experience next level art, and contribute to Vancouver's pulsing art scene.

Volunteers are needed for the following roles:

Marketing and Sponsorship
Tech Support
Gallery builders and guides
Hospitality
Concession/Door
Photography/Videography
More

Bring your skills and offer your creation; together we can refine the lens
on New Media Art.

Go to www.newformsfestival.com. To get involved, contact Liz at
volunteer@newformsfestival.com, or call 604.786.6249.

++++NFF is hosted by the New Forms Media Society which is a non-profit
society and media arts organization founded in 2000 that nurtures and
connects local and international arts, science and grassroots communities
through the annual New Forms Festival++++

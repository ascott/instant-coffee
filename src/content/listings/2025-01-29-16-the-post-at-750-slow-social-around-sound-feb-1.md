Slow Social: Around Sound (and Music and Drag) with Gabriel Dharmoo
The Post at 750
February 1st 10am-12:00pm

Centered around an open discussion with artist Gabriel Dharmoo, and hosted in a
textile installation by Barbara Adler, this hybrid of The Only Animals Slow Socials
and Music on Mains Around Sound series will explore how slow practice can set the
stage for joyful climate futures. As you listen, The Only Animal will teach you how to
create cordage from fibres you can find close to home.
A beginners lesson will be held from 10:00-10:30am. All materials will be provided.
This event is FREE but registration is required. This event will be filmed.

Join artist Gabriel Dharmoo, The Only Animal, Music on Main and PuSh International
Performing Arts Festival for an artist talk
and craft session. Learn to create cordage
from local materials while exploring connections to drag and climate futures.

Presented with Music on Main, The Only Animal, and PuSh Festival.
Gabriel Dharmoo is the creator and performer of Bijuriya (Jan 28 & 29, 2025 at
7:30pm at the ANNEX).

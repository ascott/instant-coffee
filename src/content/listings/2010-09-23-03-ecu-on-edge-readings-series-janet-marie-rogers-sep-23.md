Janet Marie Rogers - On Edge reading Sept 23

The On Edge readings series presents:

a reading by Janet Marie Rogers
7 pm, SB 406
Emily Carr University
Granville Island, Vancouver
Coast Salish Territories

Janet Marie Rogers is a Mohawk/Tuscarora writer from the Six Nations band
in southern Ontario. She has been living on the traditional lands of the
Coast Salish people (Victoria, British Columbia) since 1994. Her first
published collection of poems is titled Splitting the Heart (Ekstasis
Editions 2007). You can hear Janet on the air waves hosting Vancouver
Island?s only native radio program on CFUV 101.9fm in Victoria called
?Native Waves Radio? and she is host of a native music column entitled
Tribal Clefs on CBC Radio One?s program, All Points West in British
Columbia every Tuesday. Her spoken word CD Firewater released in June 2009
is an independent recording featuring Janet?s words with the music of 5
native musicians.

The On Edge series gratefully acknowledges the support of the Canada
Council and Emily Carr University.

Note: There is free parking in the parkade under the ECU South Building
after 7 pm.

LIGHTHOUSEWIRE MAGAZINE IS ACCEPTING SUBMISSIONS FOR ITS 2009-2010 ISSUE.
Lighthouse Wire Magazine is a Canadian publication. The magazine is
published yearly and its mandate is to provide a platform for all artists
to get published and expose their work. National (Canadian) and
international submissions are welcomed. Entries are selected democratically
(by popular vote) by the editorial board. The cost to submit and be
considered for the 2010 issue is completely free.

Please submit your work to lighthousewire@hotmail.com BY JANUARY 15TH 2010
with:
HIGH RESOLUTION, WELL LIT IMAGES ONLY - No less than one thousand pixels in
height or width.
YOUR NAME AN TITLE OF THE WORK - Resumes, or CVs are not necessary at this
point. You may send a short description of your artistic background if you
like.

Please note:
-Don't direct us to your website.
-If your piece is two dimesional you can send one or two photos. If it is
three dimensional you can send several.
- Email your submission with this in the subject line: Your Name's
Submission (Jane Doe's Submission)
- You will receive an email confirm your submission.
Thanks!

LIGHTHOUSE WIRE MAGAZINE Dedicated to the continued exposure of visual arts

Various Business
New Works by Margaux Williamson, Laura Piasta and Julia Feyrer
February 7th - March 7th
Opening Reception Saturday February 7th, 7pm - 11pm
LES Gallery 1879 Powell Street
www.lesgallery.ca

Various Business is an exhibition of three Canadian painters of varying
styles, aesthetics and concepts.

Although sharing the same medium is the single common denominator between
the three participating artists works, we can acknowledge that through
comparison we are able to measure individuality. The simple task of
grouping artists whose initial intention to paint has brought them together
for a unique opportunity for inspiration, analysis and interpretation.

Margaux Williamson was born in Pittsburgh and studied Fine Arts at Queen's
University in Kingston, Ontario. She is widely recognized as one of
Canada's leading young painters and has exhibited her work internationally
and has had solo shows in Toronto, New York and Los Angeles. She recently
completed a feature-length video project that was part of the Future
Projections program at the 2008 Toronto International Film Festival.

Williamson began painting the series of studies to be shown at the LES in
the Fall of 2008. Her process with these sketches has been improvised and
intuitive; the last time she worked so thoroughly in this manner was nine
years ago. This show represents the beginning stages of rediscovering
painting in the wake of two years focused primarily on film.

Laura Piasta was born in 1981 and received her BFA from Emily Carr College
of Art and design in 2006. Her work has appeared at galleries and
institutions including Blanket, the Contemporary Art Gallery, the Video In,
and the Helen Pitt Back Gallery in Vancouver; Ephemeroptera Gallery in
Brooklyn, NY; El Levante in Rosario, Argentina; and Sign Gallery in Tokyo,
Japan. In 2007, Piasta was the recipient of a Vancouver Artist Development
Award. Laura currently lives and works in Berlin.

Piasta explores themes related to the German Romantic painters such as
Casper David Friedrich, Henry Fuseli, and Carl Blechen. Travel,
architecture and it's relationship to environment (landscape) are aspects
of these themes seen in her series of water colour paintings shown at the
LES Gallery.

Julia Feyrer was born in 1982 and received her BFA from Emily Carr College
of Art and Design in 2008. Her work ranges from installation, painting,
film, design and sculpture and has been exhibited and screened in galleries
and institutions across Canada and in Europe as well as appearing in
publications such as Pyramid Power, U.E. Magazine and Hell Passport.

Feyrer has created a series for the LES Gallery of awkward compositions,
where distorted snippets layout a selection of purposely obscured
references and abstracted decorative motifs, yet still revealing the
spontaneous aspects of the water colour medium.

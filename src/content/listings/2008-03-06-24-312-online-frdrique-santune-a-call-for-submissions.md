312 Online makes the following announcements:

CURRENT 312 ONLINE VIDEO
March, 2008 / Frédérique Santune, “A.U.T.O.M.N.E.”
View at: [http://www.312.ca/312online.htm](http://www.312.ca/312online.htm)
(A high-speed connection is recommended for viewing 312 online videos.)

“A.U.T.O.M.N.E.” takes place during a train journey at the end of the summer. Outside, the country is still green, but the city is already getting chilly. With a soundtrack by 33dB.

Frédérique's work includes video, photography and typography, and is primarily focused on random interactive stories; it deals with dreams, contingency, the act of speech, and gesture. She also collaborates with other artists on videos and installations. Her work has been screened in several festivals in Europe, U.S.A., China and Senegal.

Frédérique, born in France, lives and works in Cardiff and Paris. Her website is found at [http://www.saturne-feerique.net](http://www.saturne-feerique.net).

312 ONLINE - CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS
312 seeks artist videos to exhibit online. There are no thematic
restrictions, however, no commercial productions or music videos will be
considered. This is a year-round open call. We’re flexible. 312 welcomes
submissions from emerging and established artists.

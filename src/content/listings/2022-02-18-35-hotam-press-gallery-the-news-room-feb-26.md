THE NEWS ROOM - An Exhibition of Artists' Newspapers
at Hotam Press Gallery
JAN 29 - Feb 26

Hotam Press Gallery is pleased to present The News Room, an exhibition of works by
artists that are made in the form of a newspaper. All the artists' newspapers are
collected through our open call and also selected from our private archive.

For the run of the exhibition, our gallery is transformed into a reading room where
visitors can sit down and read the papers. Due to our current safety measures under
the pandemic, we are only open by appointment. Each
visit is limited to 50 minutes.
Please book your visit on our Eventbrite.ca page (search for The News Room).

During the run of the exhibition, we continue to accept public donations to add to the
exhibition. Please send work by postal service only, no couriers.

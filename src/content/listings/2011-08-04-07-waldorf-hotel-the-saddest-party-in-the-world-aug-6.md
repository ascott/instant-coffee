Waldorf Hotel to host The Saddest Party in the World

This Saturday August 6, the Waldorf Hotel will host The Saddest Party in
the World, a multiroom party with live performance, film and dancing with
tears in your eyes. In the Cabaret, this show will mark the debut of our
All-Star Waldorf House Band led by Drum Legend Kurt Dahle (The New
Pornographers) with Ryan Dahle (Age of Electric) and Shaun Verreault (Wide
Mouth Mason). This performance will feature an all-female lineup of local
chanteuses — Dandi Wind (Fan Death), Colleen Rennison (No Sinner), Abigail
Maria Fernandes (The Radii) and more — covering famous songs about crying.
The show will be followed by a Tragic Italo Dance Party with DJ Ultimate
Lover. Throughout the evening we'll be projecting the films of Guy Maddin
on the walls and there will be sad clowns wandering throughout the venue to
help keep the evening cheerless.

Official Details
The Saddest Party in the World
Saturday August 6
Doors 10pm
Admission is $10

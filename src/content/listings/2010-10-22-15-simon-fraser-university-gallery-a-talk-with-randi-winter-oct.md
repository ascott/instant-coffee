Please join us for the following talk & discussion on the last day of the
SFU Gallery BESA exhibition – BESA then moves to the Vancouver Holocaust
Education Centre, where it will be exhibited through May 2011.

BESA: Shifting Perceptions-Muslims Who Saved Jews During WWII-Albania's
Untold Stories

A talk with Randi Winter

Thursday October 28, 7-8:30pm

Room 2205, SFU Vancouver, 515 West Hastings Street

Randi Winter’s talk will include the following videos relating to Norman H.
Gershman’s BESA project:

The story of The Besa Project- Muslims Who Saved Jews During WWII through
Norman Gershman's photographs-narrated by Norman Gershman Interview with
Norman Gershman, Interview with Fritzie Weitzman (11 members of her family
were rescued by King Zog of Albania.)

Untold stories about specific historical periods like the Holocaust are
always the most fascinating when tempered with the passage of time and
interpreted through the filter of the current global issues that are
important to us individually and collectively. Norman H Gershman's
ethnographic photojournalism provides personal and historical insight into
the understanding of those issues.

Randi Winter has been involved as a Director of The Eye Contact Foundation,
founded by Norman H Gershman to promote the values of religious, political,
cultural and economic understanding and tolerance. The foundation fosters
the use of portrait photography to bring together people of radically
diverse backgrounds and beliefs. Randi Winter is Vancouver-based but has
travelled to Israel, Albania and Kosova and has assisted in research for
the upcoming movie called ‘BESA: The Promise’.

No reservations are required for this talk.

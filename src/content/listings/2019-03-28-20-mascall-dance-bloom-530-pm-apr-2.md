Bloom is Back! 

Fresh off the heels of February's showings, MascallDance is ready to 
present a fresh batch of works in progress. This April's Bloom features 
works from an exciting array of emerging choreographers, and 
presents a unique opportunity to see works in progress from a new 
generation of creators. 

MascallDances BLOOM is an informal evening of dance, wine and 
comedy.

Our choreographers in residence will be showing excerpts of their work 
at 5:30pm Tuesdays April 2nd and 9th. Each dance is paired with a 
wine by acclaimed sommelier Lisa Haley and interpreted for the 
audience by a stand-up comedian. Tickets only $10!

Come join us in a lighthearted evening of Dance, Drink, and Delight!

Our Choreographers for this year are as follows:

Ralph Escamillan
Chelsea Goddard
Rachel Maddock
Trevor Pick
Anya Saugstad

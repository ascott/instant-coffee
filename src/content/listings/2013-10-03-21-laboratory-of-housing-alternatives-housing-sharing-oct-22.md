LOHA - Housing + Sharing
Laboratory of Housing Alternatives (LOHA)
Tuesday, 22 October 2013 from 6:30 PM to 9:00 PM (PDT)
Vancouver, BC

The Laboratory of Housing Alternatives is hosting an evening event on 
Housing + Sharing.

This event focuses on exposing how sharing can allow people to live 
more affordably while engaging with their community. A variety of 
speakers will give us their insights on how to establish thriving sharing 
communities in Vancouver!

We are seeking to engage with partners and media who can help us 
spread the word on this event. A media-kit is attached for your 
information. If you required additional information on our event please 
contact me at alicia@lohavancouver.org or call me at 6049927436.

Hope you are able to join and help us spread the word!

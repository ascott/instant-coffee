Eponymously Yours

Open Space presents Cindy Baker: Eponymously Yours at random intervals and
sites in
downtown Victoria from August 29 to September 2.

Performance artist / actionist Cindy Baker will make personal appearances
in downtown Victoria as â&#65533;¦ wait for it â&#65533;¦ Cindy Baker!

Baker is interested in how mascots operate as effective business and
marketing devices. She
then applied her research to her personal identity. The result, Eponymously
Yours, functions
doubly as public relations excercise and as an actual performance. Cindy
Baker walks a
thin line between remaining true to her own personality and following
standard guidelines of
successful and appropriate mascot behaviour. Baker explains, "Building a
professional mascot
costume of myself allows me to travel to communities across the country
making personal
appearances as Cindy Baker. In this project, my persona's similarity to
other cuddly and
approachable characters will function to erase social barriers and
encourage physical contact
and play, as well as the building of emotional bonds; it will therefore
allow me further and more
complex access to study people through allowing them to study me."

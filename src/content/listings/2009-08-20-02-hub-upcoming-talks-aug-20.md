TALKS
HUB is holding a series of talks highlighting creative applications of technology. These talks will 
feature artists and technologists from the Vancouver area and will focus on their recent work.
Upcoming Talks:

* Josh Hite & Justine Chambers - August 20, 7:00 PM (UBC Robson Square, Room C680)
* Dennis Rosenfeld - August 27, 7:00 PM (UBC Robson Square, Room C485)

These talks will be held at UBC Robson Square, 800 Robson Street, Vancouver.

About HUB: Centre for Art & Technology
HUB is an experimental art initiative focused on collaboration, the sharing of ideas and knowledge, 
and the creative exploration of information technologies. Built around the principles of the Open
Source movement, HUB aims to create a collaborative, interdisciplinary environment that provides 
free and open access to current and emerging technologies. To learn more, please visit 
[http://www.hubcentre.org/](http://www.hubcentre.org/)

HUB currently operates a computer lab that provides a workspace and access to software and 
technologies free of charge. The lab is open Mondays and Wednesdays from 5PM to 8PM and 
Saturdays from 10AM to 4PM. For detailed hours and address information, visit 
[http://http://www.hubcentre.org/hublab/](http://http://www.hubcentre.org/hublab/)

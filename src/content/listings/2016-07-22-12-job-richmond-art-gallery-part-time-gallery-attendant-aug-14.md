The Richmond Art Gallery Association is taking applications for 
auxiliary part-time and on-call positions as a Gallery Attendant to 
work 3 - 5 days per week.

Position Overview: 
Currently employment opportunities exist at the Richmond Art 
Gallery for part-time and on-call Gallery Attendants starting in 
September 2016. Gallery Attendants help to create an accessible 
and enjoyable experience in the Art Gallery assisting visitors with a 
general understanding of current art exhibitions, while also focusing 
on protecting the art works. In addition to providing excellent 
customer service, Gallery Attendants play an essential role in 
gallery operations by monitoring the gallery spaces and assisting 
with administrative tasks. These are part-time positions, working a 
minimum of 3 days per week including weekday, evening and 
weekend shifts. 

Duties and Responsibilities: 

Receiving the public: Greeting all visitors and offering information 
on exhibitions, programs, and memberships; Answering a wide 
range of general questions from visitors, in person, by phone and 
email in a courteous and respectful manner while upholding Gallery 
policies. 

Securing the gallery and its contents: Circulating frequently 
through the gallery to deter acts of vandalism, theft or negligence 
and ensure the safety of guests; Accounting for works of art in 
exhibitions, equipment and furniture upon opening and closing 
procedures and during regular rounds. 

Maintaining reception and gallery spaces: Working with all staff on 
various administrative duties such as membership database, 
attendance record updating, marketing mail outs, and online 
research; Ensuring reception, public areas and literature displays 
are organized and tidy. 

Working Conditions: 
Part-time hours of a minimum of 3 days per week, including 
evenings and weekends. Shifts are 4 to 4 hours long. On-call 
positions to cover staff sickness and vacation times, may lead to a 
permanent shift.
New Attendants are required to attend two orientation sessions 
prior to the start of duties. Once completed, all Attendants attend a 
2-hour orientation session prior to the opening of each new 
exhibition. 

Required Qualifications and Skills:

Minimum 2 years of experience dealing with the public in a 
customer service or hospitality environment. Hospitality-oriented 
individual with strong people skills is preferred. 
Friendly and outgoing, possessing strong communication skills to 
interact with diverse visitors. 
The ability to clearly speak, write and understand the English 
language. (Second language of Mandarin or Cantonese is an 
asset).
Familiarity with art galleries/museums; strong interest in 
contemporary art. 
Proficient in MS Outlook, Word and Excel.
Strong sense of responsibility, enthusiasm, diplomacy with the 
public, and comfortable working independently. 
Ability to manage multiple tasks during gallery events, as well as 
remain focused and attentive for long periods of time in a very quiet 
environment. 
On-call position - Must have flexible schedule and able to accept 
assignments on short notice.

Wage: $10.45 an hour

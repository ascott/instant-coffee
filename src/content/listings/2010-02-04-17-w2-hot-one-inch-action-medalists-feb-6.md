W2 presents Hot One Inch Action MEDALISTS

Saturday February 6, 2010 · doors @ 8pm
W2 Culture + Media House - 112 W Hastings Vancouver

February 6, 2010, Vancouver, BC With its building success and 100-150
submissions each year, Hot One Inch Action curators Jim Hoehnle and Chris
Bentzen have a difficult time choosing buttons to be in the show. To kick
off the opening weekend of W2's 2010 operations, Hot One Inch Action
MEDALISTS will showcase Vancouver's amazing independent artists who have
not made it to the podium in previous competitions.

Admission is free, and bags of 5 random buttons will be available for $5.
Get a button you don't want? Trade with the people around you! With a
packed house, Hot One Inch is one of the most popular events in the
artist-run culture scene. It provides art lovers with a chance to meet with
others and trade art in a fun atmosphere. The party will be one of W2's own
medal winners as we celebrate the great subculture parties of Vancouver
during February. It takes place Saturday February 6, 2010, doors @ 8pm at
W2 Culture + Media House 112 W Hastings.

W2 is a community media arts centre in Vancouver's DTES opening in Spring
2010. W2 will feature a 150-capacity performance space, community TV
studio, FM radio station, gallery, social enterprise cafe, letterpress
studio, telepresence and mobile media programs.

Until its official opening in Spring 2010 in the Woodward's building, W2
Media Arts Centre is running programs, media arts residencies, and special
events in the W2 Culture + Media House at 112 W Hastings in Vancouver. Past
events have included Paper Tigers and Re-Wrap, Re-Gift, Re-Mix.

For more information on W2 and upcoming programming and events, visit
www.creativetechnology.org. For more information on Hot One Inch, visit
http://www.hotoneinchaction.com/About.html.

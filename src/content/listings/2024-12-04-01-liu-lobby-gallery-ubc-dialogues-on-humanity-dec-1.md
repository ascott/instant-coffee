The UBC School of Public Policy and Global Affairs is not only welcoming us to their
campus tonight, they are also hosting the Dialogues on Humanity exhibition, which
you will find outside these doors, in the Liu Lobby Gallery.

Consulate General of Switzerland in Vancouver, the Swiss Agency for Development
and Cooperation, Photo Elyse, the Museum of Photography in Lausanne, and the
International Committee of the Red Cross are presenting the contemporary photo art
exhibition Dialogues on Humanity.

We sometimes feel helpless and
overwhelmed by images of crises, violence, armed
conflicts and natural disasters. The exhibition is a reaction to these feelings. The 30
photographs from around the world and the ICRC archives illustrate themes and
situations we encounter every day, which have a link to the Humanitarian Principles.
The idea is to provide a space for discussion about humanity and how it is changing.
Art photography invites to see challenging topics from new perspectives and a Swiss
digital platform " combined with human-centered artificial intelligence " allows a
worldwide exchange. The exhibition will be on display until January 10.

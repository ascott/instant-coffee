JULY 26 - SEPTEMBER 7, 2019
50th Anniversary Opening & Celebration:
Thursday, July 25, 7 " 9pm
CityScape Community ArtSpace
335 Lonsdale Ave
North Vancouver

You are invited to join North Van Arts in celebrating 50 years of building community through 
the 
arts. We launch our 50th Anniversary season with an exhibition that takes a closer look at 
significant events, experiences, and influences that have shaped the culture of North 
Vancouver 
and beyond.

Exhibiting artists Alexandra Phillips, Amelia Guimarin, Amy Liebenberg, Bev Ellis, Biliana 
Velkova, Grace Gordon-Collins, Mehran Modarres-Sadeghi, and Taehoon Kim draw on their 
personal interactions with the North Shore and the Lower Mainland. With experience 
ranging from a degree in sociocultural anthropology to a study of the evolution of place, the 
artists are each able to share a unique perspective of the North Shore. The exhibit engages 
viewers in an exploration of the history, identity and landscape of North Vancouver, offering 
a critical window into the past and a thoughtful vision of the future.

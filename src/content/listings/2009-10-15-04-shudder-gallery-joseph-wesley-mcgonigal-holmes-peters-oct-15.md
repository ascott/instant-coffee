Joseph Wesley Mcgonigal Holmes Peters describes his inspiration for his solo show with these 
words, "There are few utilities less appealing or relevant than those of objects forgotten. In July I 
fell out of love with my hands and by the end of September I had re-appropriated them. I fixed 
them to a board; tied strings to the tips. Sanded off the destiny lines and offset the composition 
with a few well placed pieces of metal I'd found in the street. When it was all done these hands 
were some other kind of contraption. They were a machine of different intention and something 
that I might fancy once more; despite being just as static, aesthetically awkward, and as 
purposeless as they'd always been. These pieces are much like those hands."

Joesph Wesley Mcgonicgal Holmes Peters will be displaying a series of new work in his first solo 
exhibition, taking place at Shudder Gallery on October 15, 2009. The artist will be displaying work 
in the multiple avenues that he explores through his medium of wood.

The artists' use of wood brings the pieces intentions to attention without becoming the focal point 
for the viewer. In his investigations of found work, Joesphs' use of wood is masked to the point of 
obscurity. The intent of this is not to fool but show the complexity of the subject matter, as well as 
serve as the allegory of falling out of love with his hands.

When the artist shifts his view to his furniture pieces, he approaches his ideas with out the veiled 
notions of his found work. The grain fully on display, and often made from one solid piece, this is 
a more sober statement of his medium as art with in functionality.

Seasons Seasons
hosted by Access Gallery, Project Space and 221A Artist Run Centre
Saturday, December 17 | 8pm
Free | Everyone welcome
222 E Georgia St, Vancouver, BC V6A 1Z7

Access Gallery, Project Space and 221A Artist Run Centre are co-hosting 
a holiday party for members, friends, family and volunteers.

Taking inspiration from the classic office holiday party, there will be—

Semi-formal dress
Holiday Decor
Hors d'oeuvres
Cash bar
& live music by Devon Lougheed of Beekeeper

This event will also serve as the Closing Parties for Paragraph of 
Possibility by Tonik Wojtyra at Access Gallery and Goethe Satellite @ 
221A: Material Arrangement at 221A Artist Run Centre.

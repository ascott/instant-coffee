Greetings,

The Invisible Majority is now accepting submissions for its inaugural online issue. We hope that you will be able to help us spread the word and share this with any writers and visual artists you feel may be interested in contributing their work. Please add this to your "submissions" page if you can. The theme of our first issue is: Made in China.

We have attached a small announcement we hope you can post on your bulletin board, in campuses, coffee shops and community centres.  To learn more, please visit:

www.theinvisiblemajority.com

Future issues will be on topics such as Elections, Pollution, Food & Drink, Corruption, and much more

The Invisible Majority refers to the majority of the world, which is
experiencing an abuse or attempted negation of their rights. Often this majority does not have a free voice to decry the iniquity of their inequity. Without a voice, these people essentially become invisible to the more fortunate minority.

Thank you very much,

From the editors of www.theinvisiblemajority.com

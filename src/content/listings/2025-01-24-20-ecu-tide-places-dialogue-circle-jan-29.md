Please join us for a facilitated conversation featuring Tide Places artists Lou
Sheppard and Laura Pld, alongside Senaqwila Wyss and Germaine Koh. Rooted in
practices of collaboration and shared reflection, this Dialogue Circle invites the
public
to explore themes of resilience, adaptation, and ecological interconnection. Key
questions include:

How do these layers of change reflect a larger narrative about relationships in this
place?

What can we learn from the flexibility and adaptability of beings in the intertidal
zone?

What stories, language, memories, and futurities do these lands and waters hold?

What resiliencies and futures are possible here?

All Welcome!

January 29, 2025 | 5pm - 6pm

Emily Carr University of Art + Design, Integrated Motion Studio (D1400)

What is a Dialogue Circle? It is a space for shared reflection and collaboration,
fostering open conversations between artists, community members, and audiences.
Through thoughtful facilitation, Dialogue Circles create a meaningful exchange of
ideas and perspectives.

Tide Places is a residency hosted by
ECUs Shumka Centre for Creative
Entrepreneurship in partnership with the Libby Leshgold Gallery. It has been made
possible through the generous support of Peeter Wesik, with additional funding from
the Estonian Academy of Arts, Tallinn.

'Fiery February'
The Year of the Dragon & Chinese New Year
January 31st - February 25th, 2024

Opening Reception: Friday, February 2nd from 6-9pm

SUBMISSION DEADLINE: January 10th, 2023
In 2024, Dragon will be associated with the element of wood. It indicates a time for new
starts, great
beginnings, hard work, and power to Growth and prosperity. Wood
Element is also known to be the mother of Fire element. So it has the ability to create
passion , creativity, invention, and introduce new things.

'Spring Equinox'
March 6th - 31st, 2024

Opening Reception: Friday, March 8th from 6-9pm

SUBMISSION DEADLINE: February 10th, 2024
In cultures around the world, the spring equinox has represented a time of renewal,
fertility, and rebirth. In agricultural societies, the spring equinox marked the start of the
planting season, when crops are sown and farmers begin preparing for the coming
season of growth.

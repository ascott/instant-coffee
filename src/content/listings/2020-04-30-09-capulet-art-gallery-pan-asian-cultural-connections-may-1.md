Capulet Art
Gallery is pleased to present PAN-ASIAN CULTURAL CONNECTIONS in
part with exploreAsian, celebrating Asian Heritage Month. There will be eleven artists
representing China, Japan, Korea, Indonesia, Philippines and Iran showcasing works
that symbolize the cultures theyre from with the experiences theyve had as an Asian
artist.

The artists include Raymond Chow, Chrissy Cheung, Taka Sudo, William Liao,
Farahnaz Samari, Caroline Dahlmanns, Lauren Brevner, Broderick Wong, Jace Kim,
Banny Jayanata and Yukiko Adachi.

Please check -in with this link : https://explorasian.org/integration-art-culture-canada/

Glut
Shilo Jones

January 26 to February 23
Opening Reception: January 25 at 7:00 pm

The Helen Pitt Back Gallery is pleased to present Glut, a show of sculptural objects by Shilo Jones characterized by visceral softness and material paradox. A Flavinesque neon slide bridges a nest of objects that flaunt their obsolescence and ancient physicality. A puddle of axle grease across from beaded necklaces drape over gouged pieces of plaster. A single work may contain elements of wood or stone carving, plaster, dollar-store kitsch and pop-culture references. Shilo Jones' practice appeals to irreverence for 'readable' codes and signs. The dichotomy of purity and theatricality in sculpture is blurred when axle grease meets wood carvings and dollar store effluvium. The history of objects become a poetic field from which to sample and reconfigure codes and signs to produce a unique aesthetic encounter. 

Shilo Jones is a fourth year Visual Arts Major at Simon Fraser University. His sculptural work is currently driven by a simple love of things.

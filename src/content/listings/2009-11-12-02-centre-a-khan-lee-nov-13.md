Centre A presents

Khan Lee

Untitled

November 14 - December 20, 2009
Opening reception, Friday, November 13, 8:00 pm
Curator: Makiko Hara

Support Circle: David Cousins, Charles Haynes, Elsie Jang, Alice Ming Wai
Jim, Moshe Mastai, Sidney Shadbolt

Centre A is pleased to present the first solo exhibition by Vancouver
artist Khan Lee. The exhibition will include recent sculpture, a large
kinetic installation and Shot, a three-screen 35mm film and HD video
installation commissioned by Centre A.

Shot embodies metaphors that mirror the relationship between viewer and
screen. Materially it visualizes a generational bond between 35mm film
production and high definition video, acknowledging aesthetic differences
between the media, but also tying them together in their visual precision.
This desired precision is also reflected in the content, which with one
clean shot has viewers witnessing a single dramatic event that is the
physical destruction of the film's own making.

In the street front window of Centre A, Lee will install Inflation, a
kinetic work that uses a 2000 foot roll of clear poly tubing and a blower
motor. Over the course of the exhibition the clear plastic will gradually
inflate to fill the windows, eventually generating a mass of contained but
moving space.

Lee's exhibition also includes a series of sculptures that reutilize
forgotten objects from our daily life. A large part of his studio practice
is to collect objects, from scratched cds to old cell phone chargers, and
then combine and reconfigure them in a manner that draws attention to the
original object, but also applies formal and physical stress to its shape
and material. While very small in scale and tightly configured, these works
often deal with the idea of excess and repetition.

Khan Lee has a background in architecture and media arts. His natural
tendency is to experiment with and reconstitute the formal qualities of a
material or object to create complex sculptures and minimal media works. He
is a member of contemporary artist collective Instant Coffee

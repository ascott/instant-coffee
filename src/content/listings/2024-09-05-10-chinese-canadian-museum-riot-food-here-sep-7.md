RIOT FOOD HERE

Date: Saturday, September 7, 2024, 6:00-8:30 P.M.
Location: Chinese Canadian Museum (51 E Pender St)

RIOT FOOD HERE is an innovative food art project by Henry Tsang, artist and
author of "White Riot", commemorating the anti-Asian Riots that occurred in
September 1907. These Riots were organized by the Asiatic Exclusion League in
protest of rising Asian immigration during the twentieth century, sparking angry mobs
of white rioters to pillage sections of Chinese and Japanese neighborhoods in
Vancouver, including Powell Street and Pender Street, where the Chinese Canadian
Museum sits today. RIOT FOOD HERE consists of a lecture interspersed with a five-
course tasting menu. Drawing on historical research to inform the tastes of different
demographic groups that were in Vancouver during the time of the Riot (Chinese,
Japanese, Punjabi, Indigenous, and white), this event explores how food would have
represented your people, culture, preferences, and biases at the time.

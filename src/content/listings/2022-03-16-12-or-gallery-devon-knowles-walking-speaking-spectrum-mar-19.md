Devon Knowles: Walking Speaking Spectrum

Please join Or Gallery for the sidewalk launch of Walking Speaking Spectrum, a public
art installation by Devon Knowles, commissioned by Or Gallery and curated by Denise
Ryner.
Saturday March 19th at 236 Pender
Street East.

The event begins at 3pm with refreshments, followed by opening remarks at 4pm from
Skwxwu7mesh carver Tawxsin Yexwella (Aaron Nelson Moody).

If you plan to attend, please RVSP to or@orgallery.org

Walking Speaking Spectrum was founded through the City of Vancouvers Public Art
Program.

The Or Gallery acknowledges its presence on the unceded territories of the
xmky"m (Musqueam), Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish) and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh
(Tsleil-Waututh) First Nations.

Germaine Koh
Home Made Home

Artist Talk
Wednesday, October 24, 7pm

Art Gallery at Evergreen
Evergreen Cultural Centre
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam, BC

Join Vancouver-based artist Germaine Koh for a discussion of her creative 
enterprise and current exhibition Home Made Home. Featuring functional structures, 
archival photos, architectural presentation panels, and Lululiving"a 170 square foot 
house on wheels"the exhibition Home Made Home draws attention to creative, 
modest approaches to modern living. A self-proclaimed amateur, Koh proposes a 
do-it-yourself mentality to the challenges of our current housing climate that 
combines civic engagement with a critical art practice. Koh will elaborate on her 
recent creative enterprise"also called Home Made Home"which is meeting real 
needs and reimagining models for home and community through the construction of 
custom design-builds, prototypes, habitable sculptures and gallery installations. 

Visit Lululiving after the talk. Free to attend.

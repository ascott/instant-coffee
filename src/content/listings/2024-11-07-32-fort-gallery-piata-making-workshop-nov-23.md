Fort Gallery hosts a Piata making workshop led by artist Francisco Berlanga on
Saturday, November 23rd.

During this free, all ages workshop participants will create a traditional 7 point
Piata. With various supplied materials, participants will have the chance to decorate
and piece together their own Piata using a premade papier mch sphere.
Francisco will discuss how a Piata is traditionally broken and we
will then all break
a Piata together and enjoy its gifts!

Where: Fort Langley Community Hall (9167 Glover Road, Langley).

About the artist:

Francisco Berlanga is a Vancouver based textile artist whose practice reflects on his
relationship to his Mexican identity as a second-generation immigrant through the
lens of Craft. He attempts to understand how one can inhabit a culture while being
partially absent from it. He engages in discourse with his own identity through the
creation of traditional Mexican manualidades or crafts. His practice engages with
concepts of
inaccessibility attempting to bridge the gaps between his personal and
cultural identities by forcing connections between them and trying to understand the
limitations that these identities impose upon each other. Histories of repetition often
produce apparitions of motifs that haunt his works.

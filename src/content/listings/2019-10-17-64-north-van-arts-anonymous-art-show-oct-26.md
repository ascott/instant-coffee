Call for Artists: Anonymous Art Show 2019
Cityscape Community ArtSpace | November 22 - December 18
OPENING RECEPTION AND SALE: THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 28, 7 - 9:30PM
North Van Arts is excited to announce the 15th Anonymous Art Show. This group exhibition
and fundraising event will include emerging and established two-dimensional artists working
strictly on unframed 8 x 8 x 1 back stapled canvas or wood cradle panel.

This event is a significant fundraiser that contributes to our annual roster of exhibitions,
education programmes, and community events, and we encourage
you to join in on the fun!
The concept for this show is for each artist to remain anonymous until after their work is
purchased, therefore participating artists are required to sign only the back of the canvas to
keep their identity unknown. Each piece is priced at $100. Sales are divided 50/50 between
the artist and North Van Arts. Purchased paintings are immediately taken off the wall and
given to the purchaser. In its place a sold tag will hang revealing the artists name.

This year is going to be a little different! Following feedback from visitors and artists, we
have decided to have a preview week. North Van Arts has decided to include the preview
week to allow the public to see the exhibition before works are sold and to help boost sales.
The preview week will start on Friday, November 22 at 9am and continue until Wednesday,
November 27, 5pm where guests can view the exhibition in its entirety. Buyers will not be
able to purchase any of the works during this week. The Anonymous
Art Show will then
officially begin with the traditional Opening Reception and Sale Extravaganza on Thursday,
November 28 at 7pm.

The sale will continue until Wednesday, December 18, giving the community an opportunity
to purchase original art in time for Christmas.

All are welcome to submit. You do not have to be a resident of North Vancouver or a
member of North Van Arts to participate. Due to its extreme popularity, the show is now
gently juried; however, we will strive to include as many artists as possible. Artists can
submit up to three pieces of artwork.

All Artwork MUST meet the following requirements:

We ONLY ACCEPT unframed, 8 x 8 x 1 , back stapled canvas or wood cradle panel.
Canvases and panels are available to buy at your local Opus Art Supplies store.
A limit of 3 submissions per artist
Two-dimensional works only. No relief work or 3-dimensional work will be accepted.
New submissions only. Previous years AAS works will not be accepted
Full name, signature, title and
website (if available) must be written clearly on the back of the
canvas (Canvases signed on the front or sides will not be accepted.)
All works must have a title and may not be called Untitled.
Paintings must be submitted completely dry and unwired.
Mark an up arrow on the back of canvas to indicate top
Diptychs and triptychs are acceptable but will not be shown together
Please take note of these important dates:

Submission Deadline: Saturday, October 26, 4pm
Tuesday, November 19 - you will be contacted by this date if you have pieces that will not
be included
in the show for you to pick up.
Friday, November 22 - Wednesday, November 27 - Preview week. Please note, visitors
cannot purchase work during this time.
Thursday, November 28, 7 - 9:30pm - Opening Reception and Sale
Friday, November 29 - Wednesday, December 18, 2019 (sale continues)
Gallery hours: Mon-Fri 9am - 5pm / Thu 9am - 8pm / Fri 9am - 8pm / Sat 12 - 5pm
How to Submit?

Pick up a copy or download the fillable pdf of
the Artist Contract/Information Sheet to be
submitted with your artwork. All artwork must be received at CityScape Community
ArtSpace by the deadline.

Deadline for submissions: Saturday, October 26, 2019 at 4pm
Drop off artwork any time prior to the deadline at: CityScape Community Art Space (3 blocks
up from the Seabus)

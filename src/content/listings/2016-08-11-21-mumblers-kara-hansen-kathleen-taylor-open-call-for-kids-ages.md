This is an open call for kids ages 7-10 interested in art, costumes,
movement, dance and performance.

Mumblers is a contemporary art project by Kara Hansen and Kathleen Taylor
generated through a series of four workshops held during the last two weeks
of August. By creating paper costumes and props with recycled materials, we
will develop characters together through play. Engaging in hands-on
activities and games we will explore masks, disguise and storytelling.

FREE WORKSHOP DATES
Friday, August 19: 10am - 2pm
Saturday, August 20: 10am - 2pm
Monday, August 22: 10am - 2pm
Saturday August 27: 10am - 2pm

Kara Hansen and Kathleen Taylor share a studio and collaborate in an
unofficial capacity in multiple areas of their lives. They have also worked
together professionally, performing a piece entitled Lights on the River of
the Eternal Baby Moon as part of Live Biennale. Mumblers will be their
second artistic project together, in collaboration with a small group of kids.

Kara Hansen is an artist and writer based in Vancouver BC. Through
improvisation, costume, makeup, scripts, and movement scores, her work
engages collaboration to build absurd narratives around the body. Recent
exhibitions include Hypnic Jerk at Spare Room (2016). She holds a BFA from
Emily Carr University.

Kathleen Taylor is an artist based in Vancouver who completed her BFA at
Emily Carr University of Art and Design. Her work engages with drawing as
means to play with sculpture, installation and architecture. Exhibitions
include PULP SHADES at Dynamo Arts Association, and Balmoral 12 Tone at
Avenue Gallery. Currently she works as the Curatorial Assistant at Western
Front.

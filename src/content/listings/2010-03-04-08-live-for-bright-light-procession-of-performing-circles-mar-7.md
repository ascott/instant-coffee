The Procession of Performing Circles is a living artwork based around
the concept of a parade, procession, or carnivale--a festive and
inclusive public intervention to celebrate the story of Vancouverâ��s
historical Downtown EastSide community.

Conceived and curated by Glenn Lewis, the Procession of Performing
Circles features some of the cityâ��s most celebrated performers,
choreographers and dancers. Six performing groups include: Margaret
Dragu as Lady Justice with her 7 Virtues and Vices (escorted by Rose
Man); Karen Jamieson, working with her Carnegie Centre troupe; Coleen
Lanki of Tomoe Arts; Leaky Heaven Circus; the Vancouver Carnival Band,
the Vancouver Morris Men and a calliope of independent performers
following the Parade Marshall, Randy Gledhill. Participants interact
with spectators along the route, performing little vignettes,
demonstrating skewed Dada-esque signs and offering gifts to the
spectators. Historical photos of the buildings along the parade route
are projected onto the walls of the same buildings, intercut with
current live video portraits of people on the street.

Starting on the corner of Carrall and Pender St on March 7 at 7:00pm,
the Procession of Performing Circles will trace a loop through the
oldest Vancouver streets. It will advance North along Carrall, East on
Powell, South on Columbia, and finally turn West on Pender back to Carrall.

The Procession of Performing Circles is a story of the Downtown East
Side expressed through dance, music, projections, visuals, public
interaction and intervention, inhabiting the city -- our city. Artists
and spectators produce an alternate kind of knowledge, illuminating the
composite realities of the contemporary city and the forces at play
within it.

Come one and all! Witness the illuminated sights and musical cacophony
celebrating the Downtown Eastside.

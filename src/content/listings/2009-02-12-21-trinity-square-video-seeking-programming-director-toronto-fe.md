Job Posting:
PROGRAMMING DIRECTOR
(24 hours/3 days a week)

Deadline February 20, 2009 - 5:00 pm.
TSV is looking for a PROGRAMMING DIRECTOR to realize the full
potential of TSV's Gallery space and develop programming on-site and
off-site that will build on TSV's reputation as an inspiring place to
create, view and screen video.

TSV's Programming Director will activate a community of members and
non-members by programming a range of innovative and engaging
exhibitions, workshops and artist talks.

TSV is one of Canada's oldest and most respected not-for-profit
artist-run centres and provides artists and community groups with the
tools and support to make video. TSV's community is, but not limited
to, emerging and mid-career media artists and artists from other
practices who incorporate video into their work. TSV's membership is
diverse and TSV encourages members from under-represented communities
to participate in all levels of the organization. TSV encourages
individuals from these communities to apply for the position.

Responsibilities will include the following:

Developing and implementing Themed Commissions, Artist-in-Residency,
screenings and exhibitions by invited artists and curators.
Creating a programming schedule and budget in consultation with the
Executive Director.
Collecting, reviewing and selecting outside curated screening
proposals for presentation on a regular basis.
Providing programmed artists with supervision, guidance and support
in order to complete projects as scheduled and budgeted.
Writing promotional copy and press releases.
Implement the creation of postcards, flyers and posters for
programming events.
Reporting and planning tasks associated with grants.
Qualifications

Demonstrated experience with artist-run organizations.
Experience working with committees.
Well organized, with ability to meet deadlines.
Strong verbal, written and interpersonal communication skills
Ability to work in a co-operative environment committed to equity and
non-discrimination.
Demonstrated ability in programming
Ability to use design software (InDesign, Photoshop)
Familiarity with video production and postproduction basics
NOTE: While the position will be three days a week, exhibitions and
screenings may require increased hours for preparation. This will be
decided on case by case.

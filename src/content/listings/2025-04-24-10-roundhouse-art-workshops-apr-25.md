Get together with new friends and make art and have Fun chatting to each other as
you cut, tear, paste, glue various media at this hands-on social activity for all levels
of interest. Collage is a way of art making that embraces combining a wide variety of
everyday materials from photographs, magazines, fabrics, buttons, ribbons,
hardware etc. The materials can be anything including what you can find in your
recycle to assemble images on cardboard or paper. We will use glue sticks, glue,
and scissors or will tear paper to adhere to the surface. Individual attention is given
every second to encourage personal growth. Open to all skill levels. Supply list will
be provided at registration. Let go of perfection and trust the creative process with
Faye Fayerman

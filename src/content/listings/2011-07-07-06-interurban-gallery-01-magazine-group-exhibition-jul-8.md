01 Magazine is pleased to present the finale of their traveling group exhibition, 
01, at Interurban Gallery, Vancouver. Curated by Redia Soltis and Jennilee 
Marigomen, the exhibition showcases 39 inspiring artists of different disciplines 
that have been previously featured in the magazine. The exhibition in Vancouver 
is the third showing of 01, following its New York exhibition at No. 6 Gallery and 
Toronto exhibition at 107 Gallery.

01 Magazine Group Exhibition
Presented by 01 Magazine and The Storyboard Label with support from RVCA, 
Flying Pigeon, Yulu Advertising and The Parking Spot.

July 8th - August 6th, 2011.
Opening: Friday July 8, 2011
Gallery hours: Wed - Sat, 1pm to 5pm

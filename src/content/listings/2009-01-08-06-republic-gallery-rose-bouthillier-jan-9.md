Keep Faith Lonely Trapper!
Rose Bouthillier

January 9th through February 12th
Opening reception Friday, January 9th, 7-9pm

Michael Oros, a.k.a. Sheslay Free Mike, was a rogue trapper and murderer
who roamed the northern wilderness from 1972 until his death by shoot-out in
1985. Oros came to the North to find a simple way of life in tune with nature,
where he could clear his mind and share his philosophies. Instead he met harsh
conditions and isolation that produced confusion, paranoia, and violence.

Bouthillier became interested in Oros' story when she came across Vernon
Frolick's book "Descent Into Madness: The Diary of a Killer" at a museum gift
shop in Hope, BC, in 2006. Over the next two years she repeatedly read the book
and continued to make works based on it. These works illustrate a variety of
conflicts: longing and repulsion, idealism and perversion, freedom and failure.

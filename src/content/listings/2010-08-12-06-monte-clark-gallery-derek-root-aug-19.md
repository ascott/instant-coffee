DEREK ROOT
Where the Day Begins

August 19th to September 18th, 2010

OPENING RECEPTION: Thursday, August 19th, 6pm to 8pm

The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to announce a solo exhibition of
new works by Derek Root.

Where the Day Begins features new paintings composed of oil and wax.
The images allude to ideas and sensibilities beyond the limits of
formal abstraction, merging enigmatic and inventively unexpected
elements of representation—Root’s thoughtful arrangements of forms
suggest the liminal spaces between memory and reality.

Hyper-sensitive colour combinations create a psychological energy
within the works. As the imagery is unfixed and ambiguous, the unusual
and unexpected colour choices play a large part in the subject matter
of the works.

Derek Root studied at the Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design. His
works have been acquired by many permanent collections including the
Vancouver Art Gallery and the National Gallery of Canada. He has
exhibited both nationally and internationally. Derek Root lives and
works in Vancouver.

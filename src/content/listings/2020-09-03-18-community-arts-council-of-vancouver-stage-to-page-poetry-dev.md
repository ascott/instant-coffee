Stage to Page
A Poetry Development Project, November 4, 2020

Spoken Word Poets, have you ever considered what it means when someone 
else reads your words out loud?

Stage to Page is a program where spoken word poets interested in getting their 
work published are offered an opportunity to have their work read and discussed 
by a panel of industry professionals. Honorariums will be paid to participating 
poets. The goal is to help spoken word poets translate their words from the stage 
to the page.

Selected poets will participate in a live stream event, where a panel of 
publishers, authors, writers,
and performers can support their work, fine-tune 
their editing, and provide insight to all viewers on the editing and publishing 
process. The live stream event will take place on November 4, 2020. 

To submit please visit: http://www.cacv.ca/stage-to-page/
Submission deadline: Thursday, October 1, 2020

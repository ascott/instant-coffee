ON MAIN

ON MAIN Call for Artists - CAMERA/WHORE

Camera/whore is looking for visual, media, and performance artists to 
participate in a spectacular one night event July 14, 2012. Submission 
Deadline: June 21.

Camera/whore focuses on creating an open space for new media art 
involving interactive installations. Inspired by the rise in personal and 
professional photography and digital recording on and offline, 
Camera/whore borders between real and mediated time and space. 
Camera/whore provides a participatory service/space, an open 
form/forum activated by the viewers/viewed blurring the boundaries 
between artist/audience, voyeur/exhibitionist, spectator/spectacle, and 
consumer/consumed.

Camera/whore’s name is appropriated from net culture. Popularized by 
the home webcam, â€œCamera whoreâ€  or â€œcam whoreâ€ , is an internet 
slang developed by the rapid trend of digital representations. The slash 
(/) is inserted to draw attention to the separation of both the camera 
and the participant while simultaneously causing internet search 
engines to separate the terms as different items.

Today, with shared and collective appropriation of digital photography 
facilitated by electronic communications the role of the photo booth has 
radically changed. With technology we have access to create our own 
digital identities. Digital imaging has become important to our social 
interactions: we construct ourselves through off/online representations. 
We seek to see and be seen. What/who is being exploited?

We are looking for artists to submit concepts that engage performative 
spaces recording the audience (photobooths): new media, 
photography, video, installation and net-art that create interactions 
between audience and media. All content produced at the exhibition will 
be premiered on Social Media platforms. Selected artists will receive an 
artist fee. Please fill out the submission form by June 21, 2012 on 
www.camerawhore.me.

Camera/whore is curated by Erica Lapadat-Janzen/Matt Troy and 
produced by On Main. The curators are young artist/curators and 
recent graduates of Emily Carr University of Art and Design. On Main 
(est. 1985) is a professional arts organization dedicated to 
encouraging, producing and presenting new art by emerging and 
established artists that is challenging, contemporary, diverse and 
enlightening to local, national and international audiences. Artistic 
director: Paul Wong. www.onmaingallery.com

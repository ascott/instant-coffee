VIVO Media Arts Centre is looking for individuals who are interested in participating 
in our fundraiser Bring Your Own Beamer (BYOB) on January 21st. The event was 
initially conceived by Dutch-Brazilian artist Rafal Rozendaal, as a one night only 
exhibition where artists display their video or film work using their own projector. At 
VIVO, interactive light projection performances, still images, video and film formats of 
all varieties from 16mm film, digital projection, slides and overhead projections will be 
presented simultaneously, across the walls of VIVO's 2,000 square foot studio 
space. 

If you are interested in projecting your work please email info@vivomediaarts.com to 
sign-up. 

Set up will occur between 6-7:30 pm on the 21st, participates will receive 
complimentary entry and a free beer ticket.

BYOB Will take place at VIVO Media Arts Centre on Saturday January 21st. Cover is 
$5 but no one will be turned away.

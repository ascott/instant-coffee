‘The Land We Are’:

Arts, Culture, and the Politics of Reconciliation

Colloquium and Book Launch

April 7-8 2011

Vancouver, Canada

FREE AND OPEN TO THE PUBLIC

How might the development of collaborative practices between 
Indigenous,
racial minority, diasporic, and other cultural communities convey a
sense of shared historical accountability in establishing a 
meaningful
politics of reconciliation in Canada? This one-day colloquium will
coincide with the celebration and launch of the Aboriginal Healing
Foundation’s third volume entitled Cultivating Canada: 
Reconciliation
Through the Lens of Cultural Diversity, edited by Ashok Mathur, 
Jonathan
Dewar, and Mike DeGagné.

Thursday April 7 (The Western Front, 303 E 8th Ave, 7.30 – 10.30 
pm):

Launch of Cultivating Canada. The editors and many of the 
contributors
will be present to showcase the extraordinary art and scholarship
contained within this volume.

Friday, April 8 (SFU’s Harbour Centre, 515 W Hastings Ave, 9-5 pm):

Roundtable discussions, exhibitions, position papers, and readings 
by
writers, artists, and academics.

Confirmed Speakers

Diyan Achjadi, David Chariandy, Dorothy Christian, Dana Claxton, 
George
Elliott Clarke, Marie Clements, Mike DeGagné, Jonathan Dewar, 
Ayumi
Goto, Kevin Loring, Glen Lowry, Ashok Mathur, Cecily Nicholson, 
Deena
Rymhs, Sandra Semchuk, Sid Chow Tan, and more.

Free Lunch!

A complimentary lunch will be served at Kentizen Restaurant. All
participants receive a copy of Cultivating Canada.

Registration and Program

Pre-registration is required and free at
https://sites.google.com/site/thelandweare/. Full program 
information is
also available at this site.

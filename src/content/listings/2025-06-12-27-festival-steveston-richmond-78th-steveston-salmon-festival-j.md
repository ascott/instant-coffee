78th Steveston Salmon Festival

July 1, 2025

The City of Richmond, Steveston Community Society and Richmond Agricultural and
Industrial Society present the 78th annual Steveston Salmon Festival on July 1,
2025. Taking place across Steveston Village, one of the countrys largest Canada
Day celebrations will feature a show-stopping community parade, live music,
performances by local youth and professional bands, a variety of delicious food, and
much more. Dont miss this years family-friendly programming at the brand new
Salmon Run Zone located next to the refreshed Steveston Community Park
playground. Festivalgoers will have lots to explore, from the artisans Marketplace
and Art Show, to the Japanese Culture Show, and sets from local bands including
the Ten Souljers, The BluesHawks, and more. Foodies wont want to miss the freshly
barbecued Salmon Bake or the iconic Japanese-Canadian version of chow mein,
paying tribute to Stevestons diverse community and history.

Free

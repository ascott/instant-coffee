Shudder Gallery is pleased to present:

In The Forest

New work by Sylvana D'Angelo and Bryce Kristopher Paul Agecoutay

Opening Reception November 17 7pm - 11pm
Exhibition November 19th -December 3rd

A sculpture installation of light boxes, forest lit with fluorescent light and sound 
scape designed to create the feeling deep inside a forest. In the forest will 
examine the quality of light as it streams through trees and dense foliage and 
how this setting can create a sense of wonder. Matching light source, sculpture 
materials and soft noise a subtle cohesive calmness is created. Then as 
everything lays still, the motion of wonder sweeps in capturing a restraint inside, 
a moment for something beautiful to take hold in you.

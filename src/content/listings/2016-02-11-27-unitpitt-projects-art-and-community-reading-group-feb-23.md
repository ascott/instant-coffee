Art and Community Reading Group
Discussion led by Elaine Carol
Tuesday February 23, 7pm
UNIT/PITT Projects

Reading: Arts for Change: Teaching Outside the Frame by Beverly Naidus

****To obtain a copy of the text, please email Jamie at
unitpitt.manager@gmail.com

Everyone is welcome, no need to RSVP however we encourage getting familiar
with the reading ahead of the discussion.

Starting in February, UNIT/PITT will be hosting a series of monthly reading
discussions on topics paralleling the joint concepts of art and community.
This series is part of 2055 programming, and aims to think through the
following questions: What will the next 40 years look like? Looking at the
past, what kind of future do we want for UNIT/PITT? What kind of future do
we want for the city of Vancouver? What would a future that takes into
account social and cultural diversity, community history, and social and
environmental justice look like? What is the role of the arts and of the
artistic community in building this future?

The first reading group discussion will be lead by Elaine Carol. Upcoming
discussions will be moderated by: Justin Langlois, Matt Hern, and Althea
Thauberger.

Elaine Carol is an artist, writer, solo performance artist, rapper, theatre
and film director whose work has been seen and heard internationally. Born,
raised and educated in Montreal with an 18-year stopover in Toronto, she
has made her home in Vancouver for the past 21 years. She is the Artistic
Director of MISCELLANEOUS Productions, now in its sixteenth year of
production for which she has directed, produced and written/co-written
multiple live, community-engaged performances. Recent productions include:
POWER and POWER 2 (live performances in 2009-2010 and documentary); Kutz &
Dawgs (commissioned by and premiered at Vancouver International Children's
Festival in May/June 2012, BC tour in 2012 - 2013); the site-specific
performance Haunted House (performance August 2015 and documentary
in-production). She is currently developing a new, community-engaged music
theatre work with a working title of Monsters, a collaboration with
multi-barriered, culturally and socially diverse youth.

Elaine was the Artistic Producer and Co-Programmer of Raincity Rap: The
History of Hip Hop in Vancouver for the City's 125 Anniversary Celebration
and produced and programmed Raincity RapConference and Festival in October
2013. More recently, Elaine was artist-in-residence in three artist
colonies in Europe in the Fall/Winter 2015. She screened her films in
Vancouver, Barcelona, Marseille and Brussels to packed audiences and
standing ovations, and worked to develop her new performances in Belgium,
France and Spain. She returned to Vancouver in late-December to continue
work on Monsters and a short workshop tour featuring youth from Haunted
House working in collaboration with culturally and socially diverse youth
from the regions of British Columbia.

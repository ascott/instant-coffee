"What colour is this?" she asks.

In Rouge, everything moves, except the colour, explored in all its nuances.
On the stage, the accumulation of what wears the colour of blood, of shame,
of emotion and of revolution. Objects multiply, cadenced by the artist's
actions and sudden questions. As the performance space gives way under the
vermillion, she asks again and again, "What colour is this?" Because red
remains that diabolical colour in which everything is revealed.

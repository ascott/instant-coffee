ART STUDIO AWARDS DEADLINE FRIDAY

This email is a friendly reminder that applications for the Artist Studio
Awards Program are due THIS FRIDAY (September 5th) by 4:30 p.m. Our address is:

Suite 501
111 West Hastings Street
Vancouver British Columbia V6B 1H4

Applications must be received at the 5th Floor Reception Desk by the deadline.

The Artist Studio Awards Program is open to Vancouver-based artists who can
demonstrate financial need and artistic excellence. Seven studios (five
live-work and two work-only) are awarded either for free or at affordable
rents ranging from $300 to a maximum of $470 per month. Terms are for two
or three years, depending on the studio.

For more information about the program and to download an application form
please visit www.vancouver.ca/artist-studio or email Derek Simons at
derek.simons@vancouver.ca.

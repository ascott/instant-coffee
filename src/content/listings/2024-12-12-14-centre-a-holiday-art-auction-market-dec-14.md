This is your last chance to check out Centre A's Holiday Art Auction and Market!

This year's theme is Silver Jubilee, which marks the 25th anniversary of the accession
of Centre A to the rank of public art gallery, starting from our humble beginnings as an
artist-run centre. It's the perfect opportunity to support the community, discover unique
pieces, and find a special gift for the upcoming season!

Artwork from the auction will remain on display for bidding throughout the exhibition
period, and works from the craft market are available at the gallery to be purchased &
taken home. For bidding instructions and our full list of artists, visit our website.

Exhibition Sale
Period: November 30-December 14
Gallery Hours: Weds-Sat, 12-6 PM
Location: Centre A (205-268 Keefer Street)

The City of Vancouver Public Art Program is pleased to present work by Artist K.C. Hall
as part of its Platforms: Nine Places for Seeing program. K.C. has created a series
of graphic comic book covers that centre around his interpretation of four Heiltsuk
raven legends. The individual pieces, Raven Searches for Light, Raven Steals the
Light, Raven the Dying Light and Raven Becomes the Light,
document Ravens
search for community, life and meaning. The raven character is also a self-portrait
series detailing K.C.s journey in finding out who he was as an artist. If you look closely
at the transit shelter posters around town you will see the different influences of
stylized drawing, graffiti, urban noir and Northwest Coast formline. Raven Steals the
Light reveals the artists sense of humour " with the double entendre of the smoking
raven stealing someones lighter.

This work can be seen throughout the city from now until November 5th.

You can learn more about this work and the artist on the City of Vancouvers website:
https://vancouver.ca/platforms

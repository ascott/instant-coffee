STONE SOUP - A Progressive Art Installation - June 2009

Produced by Vantage Art Projects.
Curator Jennifer Mawby with Rachel Zottenberg of Grace Gallery, present STONE SOUP, co-
produced with Sherri Kajiwara. 

During the month 6 artists will be making 2 progressive, site specific artworks at Grace-Gallery in 
Vancouver (a wall mural and a sculptural installation). 

The artwork will be made in the gallery, and each subsequent artist will work overtop of the 
previous artists work . The idea behind the exhibition is the "Stone Soup" fable and artists and 
artmaking can respond to the "current economic crisis". These ideas underscore the importance 
of how a community can work together to provide for it's members in difficult times. The artists are 
also working with ideas related to the ephemeral, the present, and navigating the fact that their
work will only live for a few days before it is reworked by another artist. 

There will be 3 opening this month for each of the 3 phases of the artworks: June 12, June 19, 
and June 26th. 

The gallery will be open on Monday June 15th, 22nd and 29th to accommodate extra viewing 
days for these works. 

SUPPORT SYSTEM – A PROGRESSIVE SCULPTURAL INSTALLATION

The artists working on the sculptural installation are: Sascha Yamashita, Christopher Donnelly 
and Rebecca Donald. These three conceptual artists work with ideas related to repetition, the 
everyday and domestic environments.

STONE SOUP – A PROGRESSIVE WALL MURAL

The artists working on the wall mural are: Lisa Birke, Weakhand and Christian Nicolay. All have a
rebellious or unique interdisciplinary practice and take a street or outsider approach to their art-
making and their subject matter.

Northwest Coast Fashion: Style In a Different Light
July 30, 2 " 3:30 pm

Meet Autumn Jules, Yolonda Skelton and Meghann OBrien: three Northwest Coast 
Indigenous fashion designers who are transforming the world of contemporary couture 
with inspired works that draw on their heritage. The designers will showcase recent 
works on live models and discuss their visions of Indigenous design. They will also 
share their perspectives on historical artworks featured in our new exhibition, In a 
Different Light: Reflecting on Northwest Coast Art.This event is co-produced by MOA 
and Vancouver Indigenous Fashion Week.

JCI Lecture Series
Rob Stone - "Splash: Greg Louganis at the Threshold of Aural Architecture"

Date: October 05, 2009
Time: 5:30 PM
Location: Lassere Building, Room 104, 6333 Memorial Road

Part of the Joan Carlisle Irving Lecture, "Im/Perfect Bodies"

Dr. Rob Stone is Senior Research Fellow in the Visual Culture Research Group at Middlesex 
University. He is also closely attached to the Research Architecture, Visual Cultures and Visual Arts 
Departments at Goldsmiths College. His critical interests are in sound, architecture, fine art and the 
creative-theoretical problems of writing the social and aesthetic relationships between them. His book 
‘Auditions: Architecture and Aurality’ will be published by MIT Press later this year. He is recently 
appointed Adjunct Professor with the Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory at UBC.

Lecture commences at 5:30 pm, Lasserre Building, Room 104, 6333 Memorial Road, UBC Campus
All are welcome

PERSPECTIVES ON CHARLES EDENSHAW

Saturday, January 11, 2pm | Curator’s Tour: Robin K. Wright

Saturday, January 18, 2pm | Perspectives Tour: Kwiaahwah Jones

Saturday, January 25, 2pm | Perspectives Tour: Corey Bulpitt

Saturday, February 1, 2pm | Perspectives Tour: Raymond Boisjoly

Installation view of Charles Edenshaw, presented at the Vancouver Art 
Gallery from October 26, 2013 to February 2, 2014
Photo: Rachel Topham, Vancouver Art Gallery

Curator’s Tour: Robin K. Wright
Charles Edenshaw
Saturday January 11, 2pm
In the Gallery, 1st Floor

Co-curator Robin K. Wright gives a tour of the Charles Edenshaw 
exhibition. Wright, a specialist of Northwest Coast art, is the Bill Holm 
Center Endowed Professor of Art History, Curator of Native American 
Art and Director of the Bill Holm Centre, Burke Museum, University of 
Washington.

Perspectives Tour: Kwiaahwah Jones
Charles Edenshaw
Saturday, January 18, 2pm 
In the Gallery, 1st Floor

Kwiaahwah Jones presents a tour of the Charles Edenshaw exhibition 
that emphasizes oral histories and Edenshaw’s social rank amongst 
the Haida. Jones is co-curator of the Bill Reid Gallery’s current 
exhibition, RezErect: Native Erotica, which provides a provocative 
exploration into the sensuality and sexuality of works by twenty-seven 
First Nations artists from the Northwest Coast and central Canada.

Perspectives Tour: Corey Bulpitt
Charles Edenshaw
Saturday, January 25, 2pm 
In the Gallery, 1st Floor

Vancouver-based artist Corey Bulpitt presents a special tour of the 
Charles Edenshaw exhibition, during which he will discus his 
interpretations of Edenshaw’s works. Bulpitt, a great-great grandson of 
Edenshaw and Louis Collison, is an avid painter, jeweller, and wood 
and argillite carver known for his playful explorations in a range of 
mediums, from the carving of totem poles to large-scale paintings 
produced in spray paint.

Perspectives Tour: Raymond Boisjoly
Charles Edenshaw
Saturday, February 1, 2pm 
In the Gallery, 1st Floor

Raymond Boisjoly gives a tour of the Charles Edenshaw exhibition that 
explores ideas of transformation and continuity as they are made 
available through works by and representations of Charles and Isabella 
Edenshaw. Boisjoly is a Vancouver-based artist who has exhibited 
widely across Canada. His recent works have addressed issues of 
indigeneity in relation to processes of historical and technological 
mediation.

All tours are free for Members or with Gallery admission.

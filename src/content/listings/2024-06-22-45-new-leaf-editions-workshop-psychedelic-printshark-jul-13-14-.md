Psychedelic Printshark | Workshop | New Leaf Editions | July 13-14

Dates: July 13 - 14, 2024
Time: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM (Both Days)
Location: New Leaf Editions, Granville Island
Capacity: Limited to 10 participants

Description:

This workshop will feature individual and collaborative relief printmaking using the
reductive method. Techniques covered include block preparation, carving, inking and
registration, press printing, hand burnishing, and tool care. Each participant will
individually create and edition an abstracted image to form a collaborative,
psychedelic shark.
Additionally, participants will view and discuss prints from the
instructors extensive collection. A ceviche delight will be served for all attendees !

To know more about the workshop and reserve your spot, visit the event page on our
website https://www.newleafeditions.com/psychedelic-printshark-workshop/

About the Instructors:

Cassie Normandy White is Assistant Professor of Art at Texas A&M University-
Corpus Christi. She received her BFA in Printmaking from Texas State University
and her MFA from Tulane University. Utilizing print, drawing, and collage, her work
expresses curiosity and appreciation for the interconnectedness of the natural world.
She has exhibited in numerous national and international exhibitions, with work in
private and public collections including the US Library of Congress.

Ryan OMalley is Professor of Art and Graduate Coordinator at Texas A&M
University " Corpus Christi. He received his BFA in Printmaking from the University
of South Dakota and his MFA
from Louisiana State University. As an artist, educator
and member of the Outlaw Printmakers, his work has been featured in numerous
exhibitions, publications, and collections. His expertise is included in Printmaking: A
Complete Guide to Materials and Process, Second Edition, by Fick and Grabowski.

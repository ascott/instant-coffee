Sat, Apr 1 & 8, 10 am -2 pm
2-Day Youth Photo Workshop:
From the Darkroom to the Internet: Alternative Ways of Making and Sharing 
Photographs

Capture Photography Festival Office, 305 Cambie St, Vancouver
Free, but registration req. by March 24 (Space limited)
Email: RSVP@CAPTUREPHOTOFEST.COM 

Artist Kate Henderson leads this two-day workshop for high school"aged youth. 
In session 1, participants will explore early forms of photography. Session 2 
jumps ahead in time to consider how photography is used by artists today, 
including digital techniques. Prints made in this session can be used in the Apr 
23 "Let's Photo Zine" workshop. (Venue not wheelchair accessible)

Sun, Apr 23, 10 am - 2 pm
Youth Photo Workshop:
Lets Photo Zine
James Black Gallery, 144 E 6th Ave, Vancouver
Free but registration req. by April 14 (Space limited)
Email: RSVP@CAPTUREPHOTOFEST.COM 

This workshop for high school"aged youth introduces participants to the history 
and concept of the photo book as an art form and mode of documentation. Led 
by artist Julia Dahee Hong, participants will collaborate to design, layout, and 
produce a collectively authored photo book. The workshop, run as part of the 
inaugural Vancouver Photo Book Fair, will engender passion for photography 
and book making and equip young, emerging artists with skills that provide 
increased agency in their creative practices. 
(Venue not wheelchair accessible)

The On Edge readings series presents
Sonnet L'Abbe

Thurs, Nov 19 - 7 pm

in South Building Room 406
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
1399 Johnston St.
Granville Island, Vancouver/Coast Salish Territories

Sonnet L'Abbe is a doctoral student in the English program at UBC,
currently writing her dissertation on figures of sensitive and sentient
plants in Romantic poetry. She has won national prizes for her own poetry,
has translated Korean poetry, and is the author of two collections, A
Strange Relief and, most recently, Killarnoe, both published by McClelland
and Stewart. Sonnet has taught writing at the University of Toronto's
School of Continuing Studies, and reviews poetry and fiction for the Globe
and Mail. She has work forthcoming in the journal Dandelion and in the 2009
anthology of Best Canadian Poetry in English.

This reading is free and open to the public. All are welcome.

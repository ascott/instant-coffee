Help make 2016 Vancouver Art Book Fair awesome by joining our VABF
Leadership Team!

The VABF is looking for art book enthusiasts that want to be a part of
making our fifth edition of the fair the best one yet. This year, we'd like
to be able to do more for our volunteers and in turn build a volunteer team
that is more actively involved in the event's overall delivery.

What you get:
- A screen-printed VA/BF 2016 team member shirt (you provide the shirt,
so you know it's one you love!)
- A 2016/17 Membership, which includes a tote with artwork by Dana
Kearley, discounts on publications, entry to the VABF Members Preview,
discounts and access to other VABF events throughout the year
- Free access to all VABF 2016 events and parties, including the Opening
and After Parties
- To connect with other like-minded art book lovers and creators who want
to be a part of VA/BF
- An opportunity to build a relationship with VABF for potential future
collaborations or projects

What you do:
- Help us promote VABF by distributing flyers and other promotional
materials, spreading the word on social media and by word of mouth
- Be a part of launching our 2016/17 Membership program by helping us
make our Fall Membership Drive amazing
- Volunteer for at least one shift at VA/BF 2016
- Support our staff with various tasks in the lead-up to the fair

Interested? Send a resume and brief letter of introduction to
sarah@vancouverartbookfair.com.

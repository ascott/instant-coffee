MIRA M LIES INBETWEEN
Jessica Yeandle-Hignell
Dynamo Arts Association
Suite 103 - 30 East 6th Ave
Vancouver, British Columbia V5T 1J4
Opening Reception: Friday, August 28th, 7-11pm,

Enter the world of Mira M, a labyrinth of grasping egos and unrequited love
- where oblivion is the ultimate direction of desire.

Dynamo Arts Association Presents 'Mira M. Lies in Between' - a series of
paintings and new sculpture by Jessica Yeandle-Hignell
_

Jessica Yeandle-Hignell is a Vancouver-based artist, with an extensive
background illustrating for local publications. Her work references
medieval bestiaries, alchemy, psychoanalysis, and ancient erotica. She
takes her inspiration from all occulted forms of knowledge and experience
and is interested in exploring work that unites sexuality and architecture
in its story-telling.
She has exhibited in Vancouver and Calgary most recently at Black and
Yellow, Unit Pitt, and Red Gate gallery.
She graduated from the Alberta College of Art and Design in Calgary in 2008.

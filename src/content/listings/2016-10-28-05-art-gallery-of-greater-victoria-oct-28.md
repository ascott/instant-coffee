IT'S IN THE MAKING
Cedric, Nate and Jim Bomford, Nicholas Galanin,
Shelley Penfold, Angela Teng, Jess Willa Wheaton

Curated by Haema Sivanesan and Nicole Stanbridge

28 October, 2016 - 12 February, 2017
Founders and Drury Galleries
Art Gallery of Greater Victoria

Can the manual process of "making" be equated with the intellectual process
of "thinking"? What is the relationship between body and mind, activity and
contemplation, craft and concept in the practice of art? To what extent is
art a means of knowledge-production?

Historically, the visual arts have been considered as a field of aesthetic
inquiry, discussed in terms of beauty, taste, morality, and judged in
seemingly objective terms of composition, proportion, technique, balance
etc. But since the 1960s the notion of beauty has all but disappeared from
discussions about art, focussing instead on the idea that art, as a process
of inquiry, contributes to knowledge.

What kind of knowledge does art produce that other forms of knowledge
cannot? And what processes of inquiry contribute to the development of this
field of knowledge? How can we better understand the role of the
imagination - of process, play, humour, spontaneity, intuition,
synaesthesia - as a faculty of the mind?

"It's in the making" features new works by artists Angela Teng, Shelley
Penfold and Jess Willa Wheaton, and site-specific installations by Nicholas
Galanin, and Cedric, Nathan and Jim Bomford. These artists investigate
relationships between ideas, techniques and things, taking familiar
materials and ways of making and presenting us with new kinds of objects.
Whether it is crocheting with paint, building structures that de-stabilize
our preconceptions of space, or transforming found images to create new
ideas and possibilities, these artists unsettle set ideas of how things
should be.

ARTIST BIOGRAPHIES:

Cedric Bomford (b. 1975, Vancouver, BC; lives Victoria, BC),

Nathan Bomford (b. 1977, Kamloops, BC; lives Shawnigan Lake, BC), and

Jim Bomford (b. Duncan, BC)
Cedric and Nathan Bomford are visual artists who began working
collaboratively in 2007 along with Jim Bomford, a retired professional
engineer, to develop and realize large-scale site-specific/site-responsive
works that reconsider the architectural environment. The Bomfords' process
of construction marries pragmatic and structural concerns with speculative
thinking, experimentation, research, learning, invention, and failure.
Their projects follow a methodology they call "'thinking through building'
in which construction takes on an emergent quality rather than an
illustrative one". Cedric Bomford currently lives and works in Victoria,
BC, and is assistant professor in the Visual Arts Department at the
University of Victoria. He holds an MFA from the Malm Art Academy (2007)
and a BFA from Emily Carr University (2003). Nathan Bomford is senior
designer for a construction firm in Victoria, BC. He received an MFA from
the University of Victoria (2006) and a BFA with a major in photography
from the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design (2003). Jim Bomford received
his Bachelor of Applied Science (Civil Engineering) from the University of
British Columbia in 1971 and practiced as a civil, structural and
environmental engineer in British Columbia until his retirement from
Professional Engineering in 2010.

Nicholas Galanin (b. 1979, Sitka, Alaska; Tlingit/Unangax)
Nicholas Galanin is a multidisciplinary artist who has trained extensively
in "traditional" (or "customary") as well as "contemporary" art practices.
Galanin pursues both approaches to art as parallel paths. His work
simultaneously preserves his culture while also exploring new perceptual
territories. Galanin studied at Guildhall University, London, England,
receiving a BFA with honours in jewellery design and silversmithing, and
earned a master's degree in Indigenous Visual Arts from Massey University,
New Zealand. Valuing his culture as highly as his individuality, Galanin
has created a unique path for himself, navigating the "politics of cultural
representation" as he balances two ends of an aesthetic spectrum.

Shelley Penfold (b. 1974, Edmonton, AB; lives Vancouver, BC)
Shelley Penfold works primarily in painting and drawing. She begins by
researching natural environments en plein air, making preparatory studies
before translating the experience in the studio, where a second layer of
observational and chance processes enter the often large-scale paintings.
Penfold's practice encourages the juxtaposition of states of being and
creating, using the controlled observations of nature to provide a
framework for the experimental approaches she takes in the studio. Her
completed works combine traces of the original framework with the processes
of understanding and knowledge gained while in the studio. She holds an MFA
from the University of Victoria and a BFA from the Emily Carr Institute of
Art and Design.

Angela Teng (b. 1979, Victoria, BC; lives Vancouver, BC)
Angela Teng is a painter who employs a laborious dedication to the
processes of craft through abstraction and studiobased exploration of
materials. Teng obtained a BFA from Emily Carr University of Art and Design
in 2011 and has exhibited in Toronto, Vancouver, Victoria, Saskatoon, New
York, and Chicago. She is a finalist in the 2016 RBC Canadian Painting
Competition.

Jess Willa Wheaton (b. 1984, Sebastopol, California; lives New York, NY)
Jess Willa Wheaton has an art practice that encompasses collage, painting,
and artist's books. She creates compositions that explore the possibilities
of re-inscribing found images with new meaning. Space and its changing
pictorial expression throughout time, including its continued evolution
within digital environments, are of particular interest to the artist.
Wheaton holds an MFA from Hunter College in New York, a BFA from the
California College of the Arts in San Francisco, and a Certificate in
Visual Arts from Camosun College in Victoria. In 2014 she received a funded
three-month residency at Camosun College.

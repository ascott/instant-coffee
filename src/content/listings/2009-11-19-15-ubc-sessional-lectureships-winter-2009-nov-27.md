SESSIONAL LECTURESHIPS: Winter 2009

The Department of Art History, Visual Art, and Theory will offer part-time
sessional lectureships for the winter session 2009 as follows. For more
details on courses see www.ahva.ubc.ca. The positions are tentative and
subject to enrollment and final budgetary approval.

Preference will be given to applicants with a degree in their discipline
(PhD for Art History) and with teaching experience at the college or
university level. The period of appointment is for a single academic term;
Term 2 (Jan 1, 2010 to Apr 30, 2010) as noted below.

Effective July 1, 2009 salary range for sessional lecturers is $5,970 to
$6,477per 3-credit course. Salaries are dependent on the period of
appointment, percentage of full-time load, and years of appropriate
full-time post-secondary teaching experience.

With the letter of application, candidates should include a statement on
pedagogy, sample course outlines, teaching evaluations, a CV, and a
transcript of academic record. The CV should include a detailed list of all
post-secondary courses taught including name, length, credit value, and
teaching responsibilities. If applying from outside the department, three
confidential letters of reference, in sealed envelopes from appropriate
persons, should also be forwarded.

All positions are governed by UBC's Agreement on Conditions of Appointment
for Sessional and Part-Time Faculty members.

Please send letters of application to: Whitney Friesen, Administrator

Department of Art History,
Visual Art, and Theory

University of British Columbia

Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z2

Deadline for applications is November 27, 2009

In accordance with Canadian Immigration requirements, priority will be
given to Canadian citizens and permanent residents of Canada. The
University of British Columbia hires on the basis of merit and is committed

VIVO Media Arts Centre presents
Top Ten: 1984

Wednesday, September 28th
Doors at 7:30pm, screening at 8:00pm

In the early years of the Satellite Video Exchange Society, now VIVO Media
Arts, the centre functioned as a community space for artists and the
general public to drop in and watch videos from the library. Tape viewing
statistics were tracked, and a list of the top ten most viewed titles were
kept for each year from 1974 to 1990. On the last Wednesday of every
month, we'll present an informal screening of a selection of these works.

This month we present the top ten hits of 1984. Free popcorn!

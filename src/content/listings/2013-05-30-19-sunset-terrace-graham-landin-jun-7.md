Zurping in the Shade: New Paintings by Graham Landin
June 7-21
Opening Reception June 7, 7pm
Sunset Terrace
2028 Clark Drive

Please join us for our inaugural exhibition at Sunset Terrace, Zurping in the 
Shade, a presentation of new paintings and sculptures by Graham Landin. The 
artworks included are languid, if not provisional. The supports on which the 
paintings are made consist not of primed canvas or board, but raw pieces of 
hardboard, already marred with the residual markings of past efforts – drips and 
errant scuffings of paint, charcoal, and ink. Upon these surfaces, Landin has 
deposited amoebic blobs of paint, which slouch and loll in ambiguously pictorial 
spaces. Viewers to the exhibition will be joined by sculptures which draw said 
amoebic anthropomorphs, all pudgy and prone, into the third dimension.

Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist with Featured Artist 
Sandeep Johal in the Art Rental & Sales showroom on Tuesday, April 9 at 7PM.

Sandeep Johal is a Vancouver based visual artist whose colorful geometric forms, and 
intricate black and white line work are aesthetically and conceptually inspired by her South 
Asian heritage. Johal believes in the power of art to create awareness around issues related 
to cultural identity, gender equality, and human rights. Her art practice is an expression of 
her social and cultural concerns, particularly gender justice.

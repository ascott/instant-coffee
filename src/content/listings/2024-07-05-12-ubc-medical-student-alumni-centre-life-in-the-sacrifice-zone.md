LIFE IN THE SACRIFICE ZONE

This immersive, multimedia installation of sculpture, film, photography and the printed
word invites viewers to imagine the tensions that arise from the intersection between
the LNG industry and everyday lives in northern British Columbia-and
in turn, our roles
as bystanders. Featuring the art of Karl Mattson and photographers from across
northern BC.

Opening July 7, 1-3pm
Fracking the Peace film screening July 13, 6-8pm
Meet the artist July 20, 6-8pm
Open houses July 13, 14, 20, 12-5 pm

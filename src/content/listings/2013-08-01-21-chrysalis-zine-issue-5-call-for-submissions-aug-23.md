Call for Submissions: Chrysalis Zine Issue 5, Future

Are you a writer or artist? Chrysalis Zine wants you for our next issue! 
We’re currently taking submissions for work that has to do with, or is 
inspired by, or is about:

fu·ture [fyoo-cher]

noun

1. time that is to be or come hereafter.

2. something that will exist or happen in time to come: The future is 
rooted in the past.

3. a condition, especially of success or failure, to come: Some people 
believe a psychic can tell you your future.

Please submit work to chrysaliszine@gmail.com. We encourage 
submissions of: 

poetry

short fiction (usually ~500 words or less)

visual art

non-fiction/essays

We are also always looking for reviews of art, music and literature as 
well as general submissions of work for the Chrysalis blog.

Please include a brief bio separately from your submission 
document. DO NOT put your name on your submission document. 
Visual artists, please send your submissions in jpeg format. We will 
be publishing a limited number of colour pages. Please include the 
word "future" in your subject line.

Deadline is Friday, August 23rd.

Call for Submissions: Black Arts Series Film Screening 2024
FOFA VCR NouLa

For
this second iteration of the Black Arts Series Film Screening, the Visual
Collections Repository (VCR), the NouLa Black Student Centre and the FOFA
Gallery are seeking short films, performance videos, documentaries, or animated
works for the Black Arts Series Film Screening taking place on Concordias SGW
campus in Montral in the fall of 2024.

The deadline to apply: June 13th, 2024, at 11:59PM (EDT) (accepting submissions
until Friday).

Thematic Statement
Applicants are encouraged to apply on the theme Collective Symphonies, which
explores how sound informs our connections with the world. Key concepts include
the examination of an ensemble of voices (as in a choir or cacophony), hymns of
resistance and joy, sound frequencies and their felt effects, and our relationship with
non-human sounds. This call aims to investigate how these notions help us generate
cultural or emotional bridges, a refuge, or mnemonic devices.

The submission must be from a Concordia student or alumni who
identifies as Black.

More information about the submission requirements and the projection fee are
available here.

For any inquiries, please contact exhibition.coordinator@concordia.ca

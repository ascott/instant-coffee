ELAN FINE ART

AUTUMN ALMANAC EXHIBITION AT ELAN FINE ART FEATURING 
CONTEMPORARY PAINTING
FROM FIVE DIFFERENT AND DISTINCT AESTHETICS

Vancouver, BC - On Saturday, September 30th, Elan Fine Art 's new show, Autumn 
Almanac is opening and will feature contemporary painting of renowned local artists 
Stephanie Aitken, Neil Campbell, Allyson Clay, Landon Mackenzie and David 
MacWilliam.

With a gentle nod toward The Kinks 1967 smash hit: Autumn Almanac offers a diverse 
and provocative look at contemporary painting from five different and distinct aesthetics. 
Stephanie Aitkens dense reflections on our west coast; Neil Campbells intelligent wall 
paintings that ask the views to engage physically in their opticality; Allyson Clay refined 
colour sensibilities and irregularly shaped, colour monochromes; Landon Mackenzies 
large painted fields of colour, and David MacWilliams archetypal figure ground 
relationships.

Autumn Almanac is co-curated by David MacWilliam and ELAN Fine Art. Produced in 
conjunction with the Vancouver Art Gallery's exhibition Entangled: Two Views of 
Contemporary Canadian Painting and Emily Carr University's symposium A Crimp in the 
Fabric: Situating Painting Today.

The exhibition continues until October 28th, 2017.

ELAN FINE ART is located in the south flats area nearby the new Emily Carr University. 
ELAN specializes in Fine contemporary international art featuring artists such as: Andy 
Warhol, Damien Hirst, Gordon Smith, Joseph Kyle, David Robinson, Willem de Kooning, 
Geoff Rees, and Tony Scherman.

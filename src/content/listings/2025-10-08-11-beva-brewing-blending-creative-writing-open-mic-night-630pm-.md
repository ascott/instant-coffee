Creative Writing Open Mic Night
Come listen to Vancouver's creative writers share their captivating tales, heartfelt
poems and inner most thoughts to an encouraging crowd!

Date and time: Wednesday October 15th at 6.30pm
Location: Beva Brewing & Blending, 1405 Pemberton Avenue North Vancouver, BC
V7P 2R8

Be inspired by Vancouver's budding creative writers as they read their work to a crowd
of encouraging listeners!

We're inviting creatives to come out from behind their words to share their hard work
with a supportive audience. Whether it's heartfelt poetry, imaginative storytelling, or
simply some musings, we're excited to hear what they have to say.

Whether you're looking to discover emerging talent or test your material, this is your
chance to engage with like-minded people who have a passion for the power of words!

We'll be welcoming new and long-standing writers to share 2 - 3 minutes of their work.

Would you like to read your work?
If you'd like to sign up to read your work, please contact the organizer, Belinda at
beatricebmaude@gmail.com.

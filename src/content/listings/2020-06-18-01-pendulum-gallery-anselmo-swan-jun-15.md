Anselmo Swan

Home

June 15 - July 10, 2020

The Home series of paintings are inspired by
the
artists twilight observations of homes in his
Vancouver neighbourhood. Representing ideas of
protection, safety and serenity these works take on
additional resonance in the context of our socially
and physically isolating times.

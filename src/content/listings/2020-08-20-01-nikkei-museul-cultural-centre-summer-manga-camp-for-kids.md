Keeping your kids at home this summer doesnt have to mean missing 
out on Manga Camp! Our new at-home package includes do-it-yourself 
Manga drawing challenges, as well as fun Japanese cultural activities 
that you can do with the whole family at home. Register on Eventbrite 
and receive the downloadable package.

Camp Fee $50 + tax, per household. Materials not included.

https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/manga-camp-at-home-with-nikkei-national-
museum-tickets-111013307792 

Advance your Manga drawing techniques and experience Japanese 
culture hands-on at home! Our at-home package launches August 1, 
and includes instructions for:

5 manga drawing challenges
Baking your own yaki manju treats
Matsuri Mikoshi craft
Ikebana flower arrangement
Learning about traditional Kimono clothing through play and crafts
Teru Teru Bozu craft
A cultural reflection activity
You'll also get an access code for our interactive digital activity space, 
where you can watch demonstrations for the drawing challenges, and 
post videos to show your creations and share with other at-home 
campers!

Recommended for campers ages 9-12 and their families.

Drawing instruction by Asami Hopkins. See her work at 
http://nekoplanetarium.com/

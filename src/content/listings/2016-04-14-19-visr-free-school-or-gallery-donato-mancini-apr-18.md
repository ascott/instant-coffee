I am a UBC English PhD candidate and Emily Carr Humanities Instructor.
Am Johal and I organize the Vancouver Institute for Social Research (VISR),
a critical theory free school, which is taking place Monday nights at 7 pm,
at the Or Gallery (555 Hamilton St, Vancouver), from April 11 to May 30.
This term, an excellent array of local professors, instructors, graduate
students, and community activists will be exploring subjects related to the
term's theme, "Fantasy, Ideology, Media."

No money changes hands, the school is completely free of institutional
affiliations, and participation is free of charge. No registration is
required. The next VISR lecturer will be local poet Donato Mancini, who
will be discussing what he refers to as "Paracanonic Acitivities" in
relation to the literary canon, on Monday, April 18.

Remaining VISR Schedule

Activities"
April 25 Alessandra Santos (UBC French, Hispanic, Italian): "Dust,

Miscreants and Reprobates
Myfanwy MacLeod
William Hogarth

June 22 to August 21, 2011
Opening Tuesday June 21 at 7:30pm

The Charles H. Scott Gallery is pleased to present an exhibition that
will bring together work by Vancouver artist Myfanwy MacLeod and 18th
century prints by William Hogarth. This playful pairing highlights the
social satire that dominates the work of both artists as they document
the pitfalls of human folly from vice and wantonness to ambition and
indolence.

Fillip is pleased to announce the release of its 10th issue!

Launched last weekend in Berlin, Fillip #10 is a special limited edition featuring written 
correspondences by writers for past issues of the magazine on specific, key issues facing the 
contemporary arts including boredom, participation, democracy, the economy, work, and more. 
Bound as a collection of letters and emails printed on A4 letterhead, the issue also features artist 
projects by Dexter Sinister, Christian Hillesø, and Johan Tirén.

Vancouver Launch Party, Sept. 11th, 10pm
Join us for the Vancouver launch of Fillip #10 and an evening of pop music with To Bad Catholics 
(Nick Krgovich and Julia Chirka of No Kids: [http://bit.ly/l7ly1](http://bit.ly/l7ly1)) and Rose Melberg (of The Softies, Tiger 
Trap, and Go Sailor: [http://bit.ly/tFeko](http://bit.ly/tFeko)) at the Fillip offices, September 11th.

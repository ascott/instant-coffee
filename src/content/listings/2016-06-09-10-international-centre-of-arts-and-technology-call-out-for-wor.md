Call for Workshops and Talks now open!

This will be an ongoing call out.

We offer revenue sharing, space and marketing for your event!

At ICOAAT (International Centre of Arts and Technology) part of our mandate
is to keep programming open to the community. We are looking for people to
teach workshops in technology, writing and the arts. Which means pretty
much everything.

We can help with social media marketing as well as sharing our networks to
help you find participants for your workshop or event.

Are you here on holiday and want to meet up with the local community and do
some networking? Then consider this a great opportunity too.

Pitch us an idea(s) at icoaat@gmail.com, we'll review them and let you know
if your workshop/talk is a good fit for our space.

ICOAAT (International Centre of Arts and Technology)

A new way of learning technology starting with the Arts.

We're bringing the Arts to STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts
and Mathematics). We offer 3D Printing, Virtual Reality Workshops and more!

STEAM educators in our space want to provide a more diverse and inclusive
learning experience to students and the community.

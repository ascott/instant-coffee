Malaspina Printmakers' Spring workshops are open for registration. If you want to try
something new in a supportive community environment take one of our single-session
workshops. Or sign up for an introductory multi-session workshop if you want to learn
the basics of a printmaking process so you can continue experimenting on your own .

Single session workshops - $55 per evening, all materials are included
Come and experiment with monotype, collography, stencil-based screen printing,
book-making, or lego-letterpress!

Multi session workshops - between $262 to $409, all materials included
Come and learn cyanotype, screen printing, plate lithography, etching, or reductive
relief! Those who are new to our studio can purchase a monthly studio access pass for
$95.26 following the completion of the workshop.

For more information and to register see our website:
www.malaspinaprintmakers.com/classes-workshops/

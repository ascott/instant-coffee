Please join us for the launch of 'Blackie Schwartzkopf's ABC of Being and Time' by Laura 
Lamb at Libby Leshgold Gallery and READ Books on August 25 at 2pm. 

This launch is presented as a part of the current exhibition at the Libby Leshgold Gallery 'It's 
difficult to put a painting in the mailbox: Toward new methods of artists' publishing' and 
presented by READ Books and Publication Studio Vancouver. 

'Blackie Schwartzkopf's ABC of Being and Time' is an illustrated alphabet which has 
emerged from the artist's long-term project which chronicles a community of travelling 
players, (some human, some almost human,) and their struggle to live and create 
authentically in the face of freedom, responsibility, meaninglessness and death. Blackie 
Schwartzkopf, their cryptic impresario, has constructed this alphabet incorporating 
references to Expressionism, political pamphlets, children's book illustration, and more, 
beginning with A for Acts of Apparent Incredulity and ending with Zenith, passing through 
Strange Songs, eXistence, and Utopia on the way.

Laura Lamb is an artist who enjoys the modernist mythos. She has created bodies of work in 
video and photography, which have been exhibited and collected nationally and 
internationally. Laura lives and works in Vancouver. [http://lauralamb.ca/](http://lauralamb.ca/)

Gallery Jones, 1725 West 3rd Avenue, Vancouver

DANNY SINGER: SMALL BENEATH THE SKY
Opening reception, Saturday, October 5th, 2 - 4pm

BOOK LAUNCH & PANEL DISCUSSION for Main Street: Towns, 
Villages and Hamlets of the Great Plains, by Danny Singer, with an 
essay by Grant Arnold.
Tuesday, October 8th, 5:30pm
The panel discussion will feature Danny Singer, Grant Arnold and 
George F. Thompson, Publisher.

Gallery Jones is pleased to announce the opening of Small Beneath 
the Sky, an exhibition of new work by Danny Singer. In conjunction with 
the release of Singer's new photographs is the launch of Main Street: 
Towns, Villages and Hamlets of the Great Plains, a 140 page 
hardcover book featuring 41 colour reproductions of images from 
Singer's epic photographic project, along with an essay by Grant 
Arnold.

For the past decade, Danny Singer has been traveling and 
photographing habitations across the North American prairies and 
plains. His work exists as many things: a subtly revealed history of lives 
lived, a testament to the ethic of progress that initiated these towns 
and the modernization and urbanization that is depleting them, a 
tableau of prairie life, and a portrait of the concept of community. All of 
these characteristics exist because of, and could not resonate without, 
the aesthetic power of the photographic object. 

Ranging in widths from 7 to 10 feet and height from a foot and a half to 
4, the photographs are visually arresting and inviting. Comprised of up 
to 150 different shots, taken by Singer at regular intervals as he walks 
down the street, they are also an impossible way of viewing the locale. 
Yet, as Grant Arnold notes in his essay in the book, they participate in 
the "temporally grounded character of human experience". Singer 
spends several hours taking dozens of images and the final 
photograph unfolds in a cinematic way, complete with the sense of 
time passing.

Danny Singer has exhibited across Canada and is in the public 
collections of the National Gallery of Canada, the Glenbow Museum 
(Calgary), the Vancouver Art Gallery, and the Mendel Art Gallery 
(Saskatoon), among others. He was included in the 2010 Alberta 
Biennial and had photographs exhibited in the Vancouver Art Gallery's 
Shore, Forest and Beyond: Art from the Audain Collection.

The exhibition runs from October 5th to November 2nd, with an 
opening reception on Saturday, October 5th, 2-4pm (Artist in 
attendance).

The book launch and panel discussion will be held Tuesday, October 
8th at 5:30pm. 

The panel discussion features Danny Singer, Grant Arnold (Audain 
Curator of British Columbia Art at the Vancouver Art Gallery and author 
of the essay "Legacy Code" featured in the book) and George F. 
Thompson, book publisher. 

SFU's Philosophers' Café will be hosting an Art Salon in conjunction 
with the exhibition on Friday, November 1st, 1pm.

RED GATE & KOLAJ present PAPER CLUB
Wednesday, June 22, 7pm 
_
Kolaj is happy to collaborate with Carmen Loretta & Sarah Judge to 
bring you a special presentation of PAPER CLUB at Red Gate.
The evening will feature an exhibition of original works by local 
collagists Sarah Judge and Carmen Loretta, as well as Jordan 
Westre who was recently featured in the portfolio section of Kolaj 
Magazine #15.

As usual, feel free to bring your magazines, glue and scissors to 
create your own works on site. The event will also serve as a West 
Coast launch of Kolaj #15, which was released in Montreal in 
March 2016.
_
SARAH JUDGE is a designer from Vancouver, BC. She takes 
things apart and puts them back together with purpose and 
originality. designjudge.ca // @mizzjudge
CARMEN LORETTA is a practitioner of paper art and a mystic 
maunderer. Along with Sarah Judge, they are the organizers of 
PAPER CLUB. info@carmenloretta.com // @pacific_spiritbear
JORDAN WESTRE is an analog collage artist born in Regina, SK, 
who is currently based in Vancouver, BC. livingcouchart.com // 
@livingcouch
KOLAJ MAGAZINE (Maison Kasini: MTL) is a quarterly, printed 
magazine specializing in contemporary collage. kolajmagazine.com 
// @kolajmagazine
_
PAPER CLUB: 7PM @ Red Gate Arts Society, 855 E Hastings 
(FREE!)

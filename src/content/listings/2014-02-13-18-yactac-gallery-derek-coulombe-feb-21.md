SLOW ROOMS

Derek Coulombe

Exhibition Opening: 21 February 2014 / 7-11pm
21 - 28 February 2014

Yactac Gallery
7206 Ontario Street
Vancouver B.C
V5X3B7

Derek Coulombe (b.1987) Lives and works in Vancouver BC, he graduated 
from Emily Carr University in 2012. His collages and prints are situated within 
a dialogue on the nature of architectural space, the implications of entropy, 
and pictorial abstraction. Slow Rooms is a visual exploration of swimming 
pools and other domestically contained bodies of water.

Blood Libel is a solo exhibition by Jewish multi-(un)disciplinary artist Elijah Lake
Holstein, opening Saturday, August 23rd at 4by4 Gallery (234 E. 5th Ave), with Live
music & Havdalah Ceremony starting at 7pm. The show will run until Sept 6th.

Working in papirshnit (traditional jewish paper-cutting) and sculpture, the show
confronts the weaponization of Judaism in service of Israeli state violence, and
imagines Judaism beyond Zionism in the world to come.

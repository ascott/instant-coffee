CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS: SOUND OF THE SUN, VOLUME III

All expressions of interest must be submitted by Friday, April 5, 2024
See full
submission guidelines on our website

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Sound of the Sun, Volume III, is the third iteration in UNIT/PITTs multi-part sound
project of sonic-celestial explorations. As days lengthen in the Northern Hemisphere,
this project asks participants to consider their relationship to cycles of solar
movement: to observe, respond, record, and retell through music, poetry, field
recordings, storytelling, and any other audio-based works which speak to this
important time of the solar year. The project will culminate in the production of a
limited edition cassette tape, be broadcast through online platforms, and aired in a
radio show. Participants will also be considered to perform their work in a live event
at UNIT/PITT's gallery and garden space the end of June 2024.

This iteration of the project will be guest curated by Jefferson Alade, made possible
through the support of the Shumka Centre's Artist Apprenticeship Network 2024
Program.

This project is made possible with the
support of the Canada Council for the Arts.

WHO CAN APPLY

This program is open to artists, musicians, writers, storytellers and cultural
practitioners of all ages and stages of their creative careers who wish to engage with
the thematic of the project. While UNIT/PITT prioritizes supporting emerging artists
from within our direct community"on the unceded land belonging to the Musqueam,
Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations, also known as Vancouver, BC"we
welcome international applicants to consider applying, in order to expand our
community across geographical lines.

***Please note: If applicants require any technical assistance in assembling their
application, please contact us as early as possible before the deadline.

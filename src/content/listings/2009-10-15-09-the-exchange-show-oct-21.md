The Exchange Show EMILY CARR UNIVERSITY 

21 October - 27 October 2009

Opening Wednesday October 21 7-10 pm, Emily Carr University Concourse Gallery.

What does it mean to displace one's self? To leave one’s routine and experience the new? In 
their third year, Emily Carr students have the chance to do just that, as a select number 
participate in the Student Exchange Program, traveling to various institutions across North 
America, Europe, and Australia. Here they have the opportunity to not only diversify their 
education, but also establish new connections while encountering a foreign place in a local way. 
Invaluable in it’s infinite contribution to what these students will produce in the future, the 
Exchange experience is one of great excitement and personal expansion; there is nothing quite 
so thought-provoking as stepping neatly out of one life and into another.

Upon their return, students from all faculties have come together to share a small part of their rich 
and varied journeys. The Exchange Show is a collection of these moments. Spanning multiple 
disciplines and medias, including industrial and communication design, drawing, painting, film, 
and photography, the student-organized Exchange Show presents work both inspired by, and 
created while, studying abroad.

See more at
http://exchangeshow.blogspot.com

10. The Grey Square Relay | OCT 28
The Grey Square Relay

Wednesday Oct 28, 2009

900h – 1600h, Downtown Vancouver

A minimum of 16 people, dressed in GREY, occupy a street corner, making a SILENT grey 
square.

Someone in that group will be designated corner captain. 

The corner captain keeps track of time and leads the grey square from one corner of the city to 
another.

After 15 minutes in a corner, led by the corner captain, they walk single file to another city corner 
and make another square.

This goes on all day, all around the downtown core.

People will be funneled in and out in hourly intervals so that no one except the most zealous 
need to commit more than an hour of their day.

At least 100m away, (this is important – so that people seeing the grey square have some time 
and space to go “what the hell?” and experience a bit of mystery in their lives before they are 
given an explanation) volunteers give out pamphlets that ONLY say:

The world without art is grey.

At the back of the pamphlet there will be an address to a blog:

http://worldwithoutart.wordpress.com/

This blog will tell them in greater detail what they are seeing and why.

Statistics, information, links to Alliance and other relevant sites etc.

Also, when there might be future grey squares.

(Future grey squares can be in front of performances, theatres, libraries etc and do not have to 
go on all day.)

It will eventually have videos and photos of past grey squares.

This grey square relay will also be documented – videos and photos.

This is not a protest. This is about art and artists taking their place in the world.

How to participate:

We need volunteers to:

- stand in the squares

- hand out pamphlets

- video the event and edit your version of the relay

- take photographs

11. Eyelevel Galley | Call for Submissions | OCT 31
Call for Submissions:

Eyelevel Galley Main Space Exhibitions

Deadline: October 31st, 2009.

Eyelevel Gallery is seeking proposals for the 2010-2011 exhibition season in our main gallery 
space. We are interested in progressive forward-looking practices; the fruition of innovative ideas; 
inventive approaches to artistic production; and work based on an articulate theoretical and/or 
critical and/or poetic thesis. We encourage and facilitate the presentation of artists and artworks 
that have not been exhibited in this locale. We are enthusiastic about artists' most recent 
endeavors and art forms that propel contemporary production and incite conversation.

Eyelevel Gallery accepts proposals on an ongoing basis. Proposals are reviewed by a peer 
committee once a year and reviews for the upcoming programming year will be accepted until 
October 31, 2009. Eyelevel Gallery pays fees that meet CAR/FAC guidelines. Special projects 
may have other deadlines throughout the year. These will be updated on the website.

-10-20 images or audio/visual samples on CD-Rom or DVD (please edit appropriately for viewing).

-current CV (maximum three pages)
-artist statement

-copies of catalogue texts or reviews (if relevent)
-a self addressed stamped envelope (for return of proposal)

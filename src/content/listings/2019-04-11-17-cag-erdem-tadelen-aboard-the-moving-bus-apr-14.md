Artist Talk

Erdem Tadelen reads from The Characters aboard the moving bus

Sunday, April 14, 3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

In this unique event, Erdem Tadelen will preview a new and never-before-seen body of 
work through a series of short readings aboard his wrapped How far do you travel? bus 
as it tours through different neighbourhoods of the city.

Meet at CAG at 3pm sharp. The bus will depart shortly afterwards.

Find out more about the public art initiative at howfardoyou.travel

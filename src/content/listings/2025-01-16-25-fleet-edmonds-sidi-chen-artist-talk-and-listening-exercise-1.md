FLEET: Edmonds

Intersection of Humphries Ave and Rosewood St, Burnaby

January 26, 2025 at 1 PM

Join FLEET visiting artist Sidi Chen for an artist talk and listening exercise. Chen will
expand upon their community engaged practice and engage in performance
through sound recordings in various water vessels as an alternative way of
community storytelling. Audiences are encouraged to bring their own water vessels
to the artist talk for the listening exercise.

Sidi Chen will be activating the studio during January and April 2025. Chen's project
current project invites the public to contribute rain vessels for a sound walk and
recording project. Chen is collecting small containers (cups, bowls, mugs, vases,
pots, etc.) as a way to record the sound of raindrops.
Chen asks community
members to bring a water-collecting vessel that has personal or cultural connection.
All of the borrowed vessels will be returned back to the owner with corresponding
sound clips at the end of Chens time in the studio.

If you have vessels that you would like to contribute to this project, please bring them
to FLEET: Edmonds (located at the intersection of Humphries and Rosewood)
Tuesday - Saturday, 2-5 pm until January 27 or contact Sidi Chen to arrange a drop-
off via Instagram DM (@scherlchen) or email (schensart@gmail.com).

Sidi Chen (MFA) is a diasporic queer artist who inquires the relational aesthetics
through performance, media arts, and other interdisciplinary practices. Through their
research across arts, natural science, and community development, Chen imagines
alternative relations and perspectives with the world through mediations by bodily
mobility and empathy. Sidi Chen is currently working as a practicing artist and an
independent arts
administrator on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territories
of the hn"q"min"m" and Swxw7mesh Snchim speaking peoples, known as
Burnaby, BC.

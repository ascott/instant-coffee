Outside, the cherry blossoms are coming into bloom on Moss Street 
and inside of the Art Gallery of Greater Victoria new exhibitions are 
coming to life.

The highlight of the spring season at the AGGV is the Season Opener 
on March 9 from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. A highlight of the event will be a 
performance by Uminari Taiko, Vancouver Island's first taiko drumming 
ensemble, from 11:00 a.m. to 11:30 a.m.

The all-day by- donation Public Open House celebrates a trio of 
exhibitions, Fiona Tan: Ascent, Landscapes of Edo: Ukiyo-e Prints from 
the AGGV Collection and Quiet Nature: The Woodblock Prints of 
Walter J. Phillips . The three exhibitions all examine the enduring 
significance of the landscape in Japanese culture.

On March 3 and 10, from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., the popular Sunday 
Lecture Series with Dr. Daniel Mato continues with a look at the Arts of 
Africa presented by The Gallery Associates. Tickets for each individual 
lecture are $30 for Gallery members/students; $35 for non-members 
and are available online at aggv.ca/sunday-lecture-series and at the 
AGGV. 

The first Tuesday of each month is the AGGVs monthly Admission by 
Donation Day. This month it falls on March 5.

On Tuesday March 7, from 6:00 p.m. to 8:30 p.m. the Massey Sales 
Gallery hosts an opening celebration for the Canadian Federation of 
Artists, Victoria Chapter juried show of artists.

Celebrate St. Patricks Day with the whole family and head to the 
Gallery to enjoy an afternoon of hands-on art-making at Family Sunday 
on March 17 from 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m., included with Gallery 
admission or membership.

Want to delve a little deeper? Explore the current exhibitions and 
interact with exhibition facilitators who present opportunities for inquiry.

STRATUS, a BIPOC-run collective that works to build community through
highlighting emerging
artists, has launched a call for submissions to showcase
photography, installation work, and writing that shows different dimensions and
unique interpretations of the theme: PACIFIC in Vancouver.

PACIFIC is a production of artist interpretations of the Pacific Oceans relationship
with Vancouver, and culminates in a film photography-based publication, and gallery
showcase featuring interactive installations in Spring 2024. PACIFIC hopes to
explore dichotomies in Vancouver: nature vs. culture, individualism vs. aggregation,
memory vs. modernity, anonymity vs. identification, or others.

The publication, STRATUS ISSUE 03: PACIFIC, will feature mainly Vancouver-
based artists who will be selected based on the submissions which have been
extended until October 10/23. The submission portal is open for:
Photographers
Creative writers
Editorial writers
Installation artists
SUBMISSION DEADLINE: Tuesday, October 10th, 2023.

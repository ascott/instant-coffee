Join us for a series of artist talks. Every semester ECI hosts a free
public lecture series as part of its Interdisciplinary Forums class.
“Theory is my Co-Pilot” is the name of this term’s course which looks
at the relationship between theory and practice through ten artist
talks.	All talks begin at 3:50 (sharp) in the South Building of
Emily Carr, Rm. 301 (the main theatre.)  1399 Johnston Street on
Granville Island.

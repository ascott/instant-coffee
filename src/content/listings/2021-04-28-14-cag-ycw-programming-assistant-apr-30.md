YCW Summer Student Contract

Programming Assistant

Deadline: Friday, April 30, 5pm

Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver

In 2021, the Contemporary Art Gallery will celebrate its 50th anniversary. To mark this
milestone, CAG will deliver a range of
public-facing talks and events this fall that
engage key moments in the organizations histories, inviting a variety of
stakeholders"artists, curators, audience members and collaborators"to
reflect on the gallerys development over the years.

To assist with the planning and execution of these events, CAG seeks a Programming
Assistant who will collaborate with the curatorial team to develop and deliver a
calendar of special programs for the organizations fall 2021 season. The Programming
Assistant will work during a 14-week period at 37.5 hours per week, from May 31, 2021
to September 3, 2021. All work will be conducted in English.

Find out more and download the application pack at
www.contemporaryartgallery.ca/about/opportunities

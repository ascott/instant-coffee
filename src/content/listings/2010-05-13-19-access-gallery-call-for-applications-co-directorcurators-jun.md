ACCESS Gallery

Call for Applications: Co-Director/Curators

Access is a non-profit organization dedicated to emergent contemporary art
practices. We provide points of access for artists and audiences alike by
supporting presentations of diverse views and creative practices of local,
national and international artists, curators and cultural practitioners.
Access strives to spark critical conversations about contemporary art
through experimentation and risk-taking within the gallery space and beyond.

Access has been contributing the cultural landscape of Vancouver and Canada
since 1991 and will be celebrating its 20th anniversary in 2011. Over the
past two years, Access has made a number of progressive changes to the
organization. At a new location in the heart of downtown Vancouver, we have
upgraded and expanded our gallery facilities. Importantly, Access has
developed the position of Co-Director/Curators, whose responsibilities are
to conceive and execute coherent curatorial programming, produce
publications, facilitate public programs and administer the operation of
the gallery in consultation with the Board of Directors. We are seeking
co-applicants with passion for contemporary visual arts. Successful
applicants will have curatorial experience and vision, working knowledge of
non-profit administration, demonstrated grant writing skills, and a proven
history in financial management, team management, and fundraising.

Individual applicants may also apply.

Job Description

Director/Curators collectively work 35 hours per week. This position is for
a minimum term of 2 years, with possibility of extension, and is ideal for
curators/artists who also maintain independent practices.

Responsibilities

The Director/Curators will:

* Prepare and manage the annual budget
* Research and apply for funding opportunities
* Develop and implement coherent curatorial programming in consultation
with the Board of Directors
* Produce publications and facilitate public programming
* Develop relationships with other arts organizations, funders,
businesses, advocacy groups and the media
* Work with the Board of Directors in planning and implementing
fundraising activities
* Recruit and work with volunteers/contractors/employees

Qualifications

* Experience preparing and managing organizational budgets
* Proven grant writing experience
* Excellent knowledge and practical experience of curating exhibitions
* Non-profit administration and/or Board governance experience
* Excellent organizational and time management skills
* Familiarity with artist run centre culture and knowledge of diverse
contemporary art practices
* Volunteer coordination and staff management/evaluation skills
* Graphic design skills will be considered an asset

Salary

The Director/Curators collectively receive an annual salary of $36,000
based on a 35-hour work week with six weeks paid vacation. Health benefits
are available.

How to apply:

Send a cover letter and CV to the Hiring Committee to access@vaarc.ca

Application Deadline: 5pm 14 June 2010

For further information on Access Gallery, please visit www.vaarc.ca

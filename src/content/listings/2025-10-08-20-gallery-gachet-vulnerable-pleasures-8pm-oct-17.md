In parallel with the LaConference on Feminine Desire, 12 clinical psychoanalysts were
each given one artwork to analyze without knowing who the artist was. Their written
responses are the basis of the exhibition, Vulnerable Pleasures.

Drawing on Anne Dufourmantelle and Mari Rutis insistence that risk, tenderness, and
vulnerability are conditions of subjectivity, the exhibition reimagines analysis as a
practice of openness rather than mastery. Here, artworks appear less as objects to
master than as
enigmatic subjects whose desires unsettle, exceed, and invite us.

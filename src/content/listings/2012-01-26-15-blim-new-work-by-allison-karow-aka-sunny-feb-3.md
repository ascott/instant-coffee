New Works
New Work by Allison Karow (aka. sunny)

Sunny is an artist from Phoenix, AZ, currently in the Fine Arts stream of 
the Master of Applied Arts program at Emily Carr University of Art + 
Design, here in Vancouver. She has been displaying work in group and 
solo shows since 2004 and has participated in curating shows in 
alternative art spaces. She is the founder of a community bike shop in 
Tempe, AZ and ran a small house/venue, YOBS, for five years with a 
small group of friends before moving to Vancouver to go back to 
school.

My work looks at the difficulty of defining the natural. I use hybrids, 
taxonomy and invented landscapes to build an absurd narrative around 
a heterotopia where natural and constructed elements become 
ambiguous. Currently I am exploring drawing as a metaphor for genetic 
copying; creating creatures and environments based on mutations in 
the work itself until a biome emerges. I am rooted in the traditions of the 
handmade but my practice is interdisciplinary and includes painting, 
drawing, print-making and animation in addition to book binding.

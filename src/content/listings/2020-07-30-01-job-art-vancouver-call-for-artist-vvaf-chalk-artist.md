Title: VVAF Seeking Chalk Artist for Summer Event Series

Description:

The Vancouver Visual Art Foundation (VVAF) is currently offering a paid opportunity for a
chalk artist to share their work with the public.

We are looking for a chalk artist to join us at our summer events
program Art Downtown
on a Wednesday at Lot 19 (855 W Hastings St, Vancouver), and on a Friday at Cathedral
Square (566 Richards St, Vancouver), between 10:30am - 2pm. We would love to have
you colour the space with your chalk art.

VVAF has partnered with the Downtown Vancouver Business Improvement Association
(DVBIA) to introduce Art Downtown, an exciting new program to connect the Vancouver
community with local artists. Art Downtown is an outdoor live painting event that allows
artists to exhibit their work, to connect with the public, and share creativity.

Due to the current pandemic, social distancing will be in effect and controlled by staff at
each of our events to ensure all artists feel safe and comfortable.

The dates for Art Downtown are as follows:

Lot 19 (855 W Hastings St, Vancouver):

July: 29
Aug: 5, 12, 19, 26,
Sept: 9

Cathedral Square (566 Richards St, Vancouver):

July: 24 & 31
Aug: 7, 14, 21 & 28
Sept: 4 & 11

If you are interested and available on any of the
above dates, please contact
info@vanvaf.com with your preferred dates and we can organize this opportunity with you
further!

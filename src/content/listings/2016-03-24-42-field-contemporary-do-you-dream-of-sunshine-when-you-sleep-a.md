Do You Dream of Sunshine When You Sleep?
Patrick Campbell and Madison Killough
Curated by Avalon Mott
Friday April 1st Saturday April 30th, 2016
Opening Friday, April 1st. 7:00 10:00PM
RSVP

FIELD Contemporary
17 West Broadway, Vancouver

Campbell + Killough present a new installation Do You Dream of Sunshine
When You Sleep? at FIELD Contemporary for Capture Photography Festival
curated by Avalon Mott. The exhibition focuses on the relationship between
light, atmosphere and healing. Using both art and design, the artists
construct an experiential environment.

The placement of objects and their orientation will visually guide the
viewer into a fully curated experience. Exploring light and form through
photography and sculpture, Campbell + Killough create a meditative
atmosphere for spatial reflection. The viewers simultaneously experience
two-dimensional impressions of light with three dimensional experiences of
light.

Campbell + Killough use art and design to structure spatial experiences.
Informed by architecture, modernism, contemporary art, and atmospheres -
their immersive spaces + objects balance invisible affects with formal
qualities. Patrick Campbell and Madison Killough have been collaborating
since they met in Vancouver, Canada in 2012. They recently founded the
design studio wolke.

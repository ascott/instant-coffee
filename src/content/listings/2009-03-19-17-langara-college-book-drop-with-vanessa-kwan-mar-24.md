Vehicle: Thinking through the Box

Kristina Lee Podesva

January 13 - April 21, 2009

Hours: Monday - Thursday,4-8pm 

Langara College

100 West 49th Avenue, Vancouver

For the inaugural artist residency at the Langara College Centre for Art in
Public Spaces, 

Kristina Lee Podesva presents Vehicle. The project re-purposes the symbolic
and architectural space of shipping containers to catalyze conversations 
and collaborations with the Langara College community, neighbourhood and
beyond about our experience and understanding of globalization. Shipping
containers, which are normally used as vehicles for international commerce,
become in this project an intimate and interactive site. Located outside
the main entrance to the college, the project provides a discursive space
for participants, housing a series of reading rooms developed by Podesva in
collaboration with invited local and international guests Jeffrey Swartz,
Jeff Khonsary, and Vanessa Kwan. Inspired by Aleksandr Rodchenko's Worker's
Room from the 1925 Paris Exposition, Vehicle takes a contemporary approach,
hosting a program of performances, workshops, recitals and events that
facilitate playful, poetic, and more serious investigations that re-signify
the symbolic space of globalization. Vehicle constitutes a temporary zone
of exception where new forms of global knowledge are encouraged, produced
and articulated. 

Book Drop

with Vanessa Kwan

Tuesday, March 24 - April 14

Opening March 24, 6pm.

For Book Drop Vancouver artist and writer Vanessa Kwan offers and performs
an art service for Langara College students in which used course texts
and papers are transformed into art works according to a set of
instructions. Appropriating office In Box / Out Box systems, Kwan
invites students to provide materials and requests for art works, which she
will produce during the course of the project. As a reading room,Book Drop
refashions the container space as an art studio, a site for artistic
production, which takes both concrete and ephemeral forms. Where Thought
for Food broadly addressed consumption and Copy Room loosely investigated
property, Book Drop brings labour into Vehicle's local discussions on
globalization. Moreover, it develops a specific, heartfelt, direct and yet
anonymous relationship with each student through art work based on
information provided by the student. The project ultimately considers
educational techniques of the assignment, the test, and concepts of
instruction, direction and customization as it relates to artistic
production, labour and patronage. Weekly Programs Include:

Instructions Set: Tuesday, March 31, 7 pm

For the course of an evening, Vehicle provides a space and audience for
enacting a set of instructions. Visitors may enact instructions and or see
their instructions enacted by others during the event. For more
information, email info@thisisavehicle.com

Oblique Strategies Workshop: Tuesday, April 7, 7 pm

Using Brian Eno and Peter Schmidt's Oblique Strategies card deck,
participants are given support and advice on current projects. The session
introduces the element of chance and collaboration into the creative
process.

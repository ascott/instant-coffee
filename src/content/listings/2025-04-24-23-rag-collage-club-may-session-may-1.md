Creative Prompt : CULTURE
This month's art-making prompt explores CULTURE, inspired by the current
exhibition at the gallery, Restless By Nature: Mary Sui Yee Wong.

We will provide a large assortment of collage materials and a slide-show full of
inspirational collage artists to get you started on your cut and paste
experimentations. Musical playlist will include songs inspired by our theme, exploring
music from all over the world.

We will also connect to international collage groups for you to share your collage
works online by providing printed copies of collage challenges from various sites like
the Paris Collage Collective, Kolaj magazine, and more. Or prep for upcoming World
Collage Day events in May, to celebrate the wide world of collage!

All levels of artistic skill are welcome! Limited spaces, registration is required.
Recommended for ages 16+

$5 per person includes all art supplies and use of our equipment.
Please note - no refunds. Funds raised go towards supporting this program.

About RAG Collage Club:
First Thursday night of the month, February - December 2025
Get together with friends and make some art! Open studio time led by gallery staff
for adult participants to cut and paste with a variety of materials and tools while you
chat, listen to music, and sip some tea at our Collage Club social. RAG and Media
Lab staff will open their vast collection of cool collage materials and tools to lead a
hands-on art-making night for adults.

NOTE: While the focus is on the tenets of collage and cut-and-paste culture, we
welcome participant suggestions each month to experiment and play with various art
materials and techniques in our art classroom.

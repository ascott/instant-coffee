POSITION TITLE: Project Manager

APPLICATION TIMELINE: Open until Jan 31. Shortlisted applicants contacted by Feb 8
STATUS: Full time position, starting Feb 15, 2O21
LOCATION: Vancouver, BC
REPORTING TO: Director, Creative Projects
COMPENSATION: $48-60K per year + benefits and professional development opportunities

The Project Manager is an integral role in stewarding the lifecycle of commissioned projects
that fall outside of the annual Festival. Working with clients across the Lower Mainland --

external stakeholders, managing several projects simultaneously.

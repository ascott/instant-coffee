July Project is a creative and cooperative workshop series hosted by What Lab, an
artist-run space in East Vancouver. We
invite folks to participate in a month-long
series for people to create, share, edit, and present any artistic project of their
choice. Any payment is by donation.

Participants meet weekly to share in their individual progress and hold each other
accountable for the work done through inspiration and critique.

This year, with COVID-19 restrictions, July Project will look a little bit different.
Instead of meeting as one large group, we will create smaller groups of about 4
people. These groups will decide when and how they will interact " digitally, or
distanced at an outdoor space, or even at What Lab, depending on everyones
comfort level. Expect 4-5 meetings, once a week starting in early July.

To find out more or to register, visit http://whatlab.ca/jp-2020/

Questions? Email us at studio@whatlab.ca

| Richmond Art Gallery | Webinar with Priscilla Yu | 7PM | JUN 30
Richmond Art Gallery presents:

Artist Salon Webinar with Priscilla Yu
Tuesday, June 30, 2020
7:00 - 8:30 pm

The Richmond Art Gallery's monthly Artist Salon Series features art professionals
leading inspirational artist talks and professional development for visual artists.

Our guest presenter this month is illustrator and mural artist Priscilla Yu. Learn
what it takes to be a mural artist and ask your questions about working in this
medium during the live webinar.

Yu will share some of her past mural experiences and offer tips on how to get
started working in this field, things to consider when planning a mural project,
and how to transform your studio painting practice into working in public spaces.

About the Presenter:
Priscilla Yu is a Vancouver-based visual artist, illustrator and muralist, painting
over twenty murals locally and
internationally over the past few years. With a
personal style involving abstracted perspective and a geometric language, she
creates worlds that dwell in a strange gravity.

Agony Klub and Publication Studio Vancouver are pleased to present Whitney Houston, 
vol. 2. A continuation of Whitney Houston, et. al., editor/author Casey Wei invites six 
writers to reflect on their relationship to popular music in film, keeping in mind that 
popular music has always been as much about the desire for an image as about the 
catchiness of a song. The resulting essays on Elliot Smith, Amlie, Real Genius, The 
Pixies, Drive, and The Conversation explore themes of time, love, and evolution.

Join us for the launch of this book at Publication Studio/ Selectors' Records. Writers will 
be in attendance and read from their text, copies of Whitney Houston, vol. 2 will be for 
sale (along with other AK releases), and refreshments will be served.

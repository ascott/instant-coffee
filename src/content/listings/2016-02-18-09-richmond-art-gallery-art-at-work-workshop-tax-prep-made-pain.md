Tax season is once again upon us. Are you prepared? 

The next instalment of the Art At Work series is catered to the tax 
needs of artists and craftspeople. Facilitated by Marianna Scott of 
Quantum Accounting Services, learn the fundamentals of taxation for 
individuals, which expenses are deductible, what kind of record 
keeping is required, and how to get and stay organized. Ideal for self-
employed artists who have little or no experience preparing taxes.

This workshop is an opportunity to speak directly to an accountant and 
get tips on how to better manage your bookkeeping so that you can be 
prepared for the year ahead.

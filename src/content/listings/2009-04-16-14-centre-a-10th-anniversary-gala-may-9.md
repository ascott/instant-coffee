Centre A 10th Anniversary Gala
Saturday, May 9

Art Auction Preview Website
www.centrea.org

Centre A today launched the online exhibition its 10th anniversary collection of contemporary 
Asian art. The forty works on offer by leading local and international artists will be sold in a live 
and silent auction during a gala dinner to be held on Saturday, May 9 at the fabulous new 
Kentizen Restaurant at International Village, 88 West Pender Street.

The amazing low ticket price of $88 (tables of 8 are only $688) includes free parking, BC 
sparkling wine compliments of Mark Reid at Cityfone, dinner and dancing to live jazz by the 
legendary Doug Louie and Henry Young, backing up the sensational new vocal discovery Sarah 
Kennedy. The all-you-eat buffet dinner will feature unlimited sushi, crab legs and barbecue. The 
evening will tell the story of Centre A’s first ten years.

The centre piece of the evening will be tribute to the artists who have not only made important 
contributions to this history but have also generously donated their work for auction. This will be a 
rare opportunity to acquire contemporary art of the highest quality at great prices ranging from as 
low $10 to $5,000 or more. The live auction is guaranteed to be exciting. A free art preview and 
cocktail party will take place at Centre A on Wednesday, May 6, from 5:30 to 8:00 pm. The 
website is up now and new art works are being added every day.

A limited fine art edition has been created especially to mark the occasion by Vancouver artist 
Khan Lee of Instant Coffee.

Don’t miss this very special 10th anniversary event. The dinner is already half sold out.

Tickets may be reserved on the website, by email or charge by phone.

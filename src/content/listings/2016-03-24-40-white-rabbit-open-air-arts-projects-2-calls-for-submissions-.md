WHITE RABBIT OPEN AIR ARTS PROJECT 2016

This year we are putting out TWO CALLS! 

A call for applicants to partake as a resident in our traditional six day 
Residency and an expression of interest to be part of the new 
Metamorphosis Period where teams will be curated to produce works 
(installation, performances, etc.) specifically for the Festival. 

Due Dates for both calls are April 21st.

CALL FOR APPLICANTS:
WHITE RABBIT: OPEN AIR ARTS RESIDENCY // August 6th - 11th

The Red Clay Studio Society is pleased to invite applications for the 
8th annual White Rabbit Artist Residency as part of the 2016 White 
Rabbit Open Air Art Project.

White Rabbit is a residency and festival that encourages artists to push 
the boundaries of their practice collaboratively and individually in 
response to the unique climate and culture of the Bay of Fundy's 
shoreline. White Rabbit takes place at Red Clay, a property that has 
been cultivated for several decades, with many small intimate 
microclimates. This diversity includes orchards, streambeds, tall 
forests with steep terrains, low lying meadows and nearby ocean 
access. 

The residency itself is a 7-day immersive experience in which emerging 
and established artists are invited to set up camp and to find/lose 
themselves in practice, creation and collaboration. The residency 
includes field trips to waterfalls and tidal flats, a gathering around a 
campfire each evening, use of site facilities (sauna, pond swimming, 
solar showers, etc), and full accommodation (camping or lofts). White 
Rabbit seeks to achieve culinary excellence: our wine-glass-in-the-
woods dining features fresh food from Red Clay Farm prepared by 
experienced and dedicated chefs on-site.

We encourage artists from a broad range of disciplines to apply 
(textiles, dance, theatre, ceramics, paint, poetry, philosophy, words, 
audio, documentary, film, performance, music, you-name-it!) to push 
the boundaries of their practice, and work together to create the 
environment that is formed collaboratively and individually in response 
to the unique landscape. In addition to working on individual projects, 
residents have opportunities to participate in peer-to-peer learning, 
workshops, presentations, and collaborative projects.

This year the residency will culminate in a small, private celebration 
that focuses on the tight-knit, process-oriented strength of the 
residency. Following this celebration there will be a new 6-day 
component called Metamorphosis leading into our traditional festival on 
August 20th - 21st. Conceived of as an intensive, collaborative 
production period, Metamorphosis will see teams of artists and 
musicians/sound artists working in tandem to create work specifically 
tailored towards the festival experience. 

Artists that are interested in staying on site during this production 
period will have the opportunity to stay on as a volunteer/collaborator. 
Regardless, all artists that participate in the residency will be 
encouraged to return and exhibit their works during the festival. It is 
expected that the culmination of both the Residency and 
Metamorphosis will make for a spectacular event not to be missed.

EXPRESSION OF INTEREST:
WHITE RABBIT: METAMORPHOSIS PRODUCTION PERIOD // 
August 14th - 19th

This year White Rabbit is introducing a new component called the 
Metamorphosis Production Period. The intent of this period is to offer 
fresh space and focused energy to expand the production of our 
annual festival. Whereas the traditional residency is a very personal 
process focusing on pursuing the pursuit of independent visions and 
practice within a common landscape, Metamorphosis will explore the 
potential of works produced through intensive collaboration.

Metamorphosis will see teams of artists working with musicians/sound 
artists to explore installations and/or performances that will be 
exhibited during the festival. Like all components of White Rabbit, this 
collaboration will emphasize a sensitivity and creativity that celebrates 
the ecological richness and diversity inherent to the land. Also these 
works often explore social constructs within a rural setting or focus on 
sentimental qualities such as spectacle, disbelief, subtly, ingenuity, 
craft, folly, to name a few.

Aided by the festival curator, successful applicants will be paired with 
like-minded collaborators across various disciplines.The basis for these 
pairings will be explored through mutual interests in the months leading 
up to Metamorphosis however much of the magic will likely develop 
fully on-site in a flurry of creativity. While we imagine the pairings to be 
focused and unique, we expect the result 6-day production period to be 
a fluid and mutually-generative experience as team interests and 
observations overlap and find resonance.

If you are interested in being part of this process please submit a brief 
letter of interest (1 page max.) that addresses some of the following:

What is your connection with or interest in White Rabbit?

Explain your interest in producing environmentally evocative 
installations/productions. 

How does your practice interact with or bring attention to environmental 
conditions? How might it manifest within a festival setting?

What collaborative ideals do you value? 

What do you hope to bring to this project?

What do you hope this project may offer you?

Artists: Please include in the body of your e-mail a link to your website 
and/or relevant work, and attach a current CV in .pdf format.

Musicians: Please include in the body of your e-mail a link to your 
website or relevant media (ideally including one audio and one video 
link).

Applicants that are selected will receive an honorarium for the 
production of their work and will have the overarching support of 
technicians and seasoned staff to help with the logistics of preparing 
for the festival. All participants (staff, artists, volunteers, etc.) will live 
on-site at Red Clay during the production period and will work as a 
team to help produce not only specific installations but the festival as a 
whole. In addition to core projects this might include lending a hand 
creating a kiosk, bringing your creative energies to bear on trail lighting 
or taking a shift cutting vegetables for our two chefs that will be working 
hard in the kitchen to feed everyone.

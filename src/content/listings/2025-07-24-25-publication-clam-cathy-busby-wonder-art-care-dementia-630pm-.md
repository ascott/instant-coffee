CLAM is pleased to host Cathy Busby as she tours her recent artists book I
WONDER: Art + Care + Dementia (Art Metropole, 2025). Grounded in Busbys
personal and artistic relationship with her late husband Garry Neill Kennedy, this
book outlines their experience living and making art through Kennedys dementia
decline. Throughout the book, Busby looks at some of the practices Kennedy
returned to later in his life, speaks about the joint projects which carried them
through his time in long-term care and expands on I WONDER, a wall-text painting
started in Garrys room at Parkview care facility and was subsequently fully realized
as a memorial at Art Metropole (Toronto) after Kennedys death. This book, and the
multifaceted projects it describes, use curiosity, a long-time art practice, and lived
experience as entry points to address the potential of creativity to enhance dementia
care.

For this event, Cathy will speak on and read from I WONDER, and we will play
selected media from the Busby / Kennedy archives. I WONDER publications will be
available for purchase, along with light refreshments.

About the artist: Cathy Busby has a longtime personal and political interest in pain
and care. From her splayed SUV in the middle of the Gallery floor, to her humorous
yet poignant collections of self-help books, her energetic, multi-modal work
addresses timely and critical issues. She attended the Nova Scotia College of Art
and Design (BFA 1984), where she directed the Colleges Anna Leonowens Gallery.
Busby holds an MA in Media Studies and a PhD in Communication from Concordia
University (1999). She was a Fulbright Scholar at New York University, and a
Contemporary Art Researcher at the National Gallery of Canada (1997-98). She has
exhibited extensively in Canada and internationally in New York, Beijing. Berlin and
Melbourne. She lives in Vancouver.

The On Edge Readings Series presents:

Caroline Adderson & Kevin Spenst

6:30 pm, Thursday, February 23, 2017
Open to the public
Library at Emily Carr University of Art & Design
1400 Johnston St.
Granville Island, Vancouver
unceded Coast Salish territories

Caroline Adderson is the author of four novels (A History of Forgetting (Key Porter 
1999), Sitting Practice (Thomas Allen 2003), The Sky Is Falling (Thomas Allen 2010), 
Ellen in Pieces (Patrick Crean Editions 2014)), two collections of short stories (Bad 
Imaginings (Porcupine's Quill 1993), Pleased To Meet You (Thomas Allen 2006)) as well 
as many books for young readers. She is also the editor of Vancouver Vanishes: 
Narratives of Demolition and Revival (Anvil Press 2015). Winner of three BC Book 
Prizes and three CBC Literary Awards, Caroline was also the recipient of the 2006 
Marian Engel Award for mid-career achievement. 

Kevin Spenst, a Pushcart Poetry nominee, is the author of Jabbering with Bing Bong 
(Anvil Press 2015), Ignite (Anvil Press 2016), and ten chapbooks including Pray 
Goodbye (the Alfred Gustav Press), Retractable (the serif of nottingham), and Surrey 
Sonnets (JackPine Press). His work has won the Lush Triumphant Award for Poetry, 
been nominated for both the Alfred G. Bailey Prize and the Robert Kroetsch Award for 
Innovative Poetry, and appeared in publications such as Prairie Fire, CV2, BafterC, 
Lemon Hound, Poetry is Dead, and the anthology Best Canadian Poetry 2014. 

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the Arts.

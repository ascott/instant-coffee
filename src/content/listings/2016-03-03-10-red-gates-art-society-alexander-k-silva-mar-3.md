Intergalatic: Alexander K. Silva (Oakland, California)
Opening March 3rd at 7 PM

Red Gates Art Society
855 E Hastings St, Vancouver, BC V6A 3Y1
Appointments: lastdoor@gmail.com

The renowned astronomer Carl Sagan famously stated in 1980 that
"the cosmos is all there is, or ever was, or ever will be." This series,
"Intergalactic," goes one step further to show that the cosmos is also
within us--we are made of stars. This work is a journey through the
cosmos: the story of our own galaxy, and the stars and planets we
share it with. On the ship of imagination, we will explore the cosmos.
This ship, perfect as a snowflake and light as a feather, will take us
through the worlds of our dreams.

"Intergalactic" is a combination of photography, sculpture, video,
installation, and text based art. The visual inspiration for the work is
derived from the mid-20th century notions of space exploration, such
as explored in Barbarella and 2011: A Space Odyssey. These romantic
notions of what the future looks like play a large role in creating the
cinematography narrative for "Intergalactic." An ever-revolving theme
of
my work--the narrative centers around exploration of personal and
communal identity and self discovery.

Artist Bio:

Alexander K. Silva is an American-born artist, living and working in
Oakland, California. His primary mediums include photography, video,
and installation arts. In 2012, Alexander graduated with a BFA in Art
and Design from California Polytechnic State University, San Luis
Obispo. Since then he has received several awards for his work, most
notably the Kodak Professional Photography Scholarship and a Top 10
Portfolio ranking at the 2013 Emerging Focus Expo in Los Angeles,
California. Alexander has also shown his work in numerous group and
solo shows in cities such as Los Angeles (USA), Oakland (USA),
Chicago (USA), Vancouver (CAN), Ahmedabad (IND), and Rajasthan
(IND). His most recent show took place in Beijing (CHN) as part of a
three month artist residency with Red Gate Gallery. In 2016, Alexander
is planning his first solo museum show in Beijing (CHN) sponsored by
the Si Shang Art Museum after completing a multi-month artist
residency.

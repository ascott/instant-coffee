The Foreshore
A series of informal sessions of research and knowledge exchange

Tuesday, January 10, 2017, 7:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Session 5
Germaine Koh on working between disciplines and Carol Sawyer on counter-
narratives

The Foreshore
222 East Georgia Street
Vancouver, BC

Join us for these brief presentations followed by discussion:

Germaine Koh will open a conversation with other producers about working 
between typical disciplines. The idea of interdisciplinarity or boundary-crossing 
may be quite fashionable academically, but actually attempting to make headway 
outside one's field of specialization can be fraught with frustration and filled 
with potential. Koh will speak to the feeling of being always "in-between" and her 
impulse to move her work outside of the gallery system, in the belief that it can 
make a bigger difference there.

Carol Sawyer will talk about her ongoing project, The Natalie Brettscheider 
Archive, which she began in 1999 out of frustration with the conventions of art 
historical narratives that largely omit the contributions made by female performers 
of the early 20th century European avant-garde. In developing the body of work 
Sawyer has grown fascinated with archives and the anecdotal histories that can 
be unearthed in them. She will consider how these offer counter-narratives or 
points of resistance to more "official" histories.

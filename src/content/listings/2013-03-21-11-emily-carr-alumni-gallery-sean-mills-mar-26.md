Emily Carr Alumni Gallery QE Theatre| Sean Mills | MAR 26

March 26 – May 27, 2013
Telling Time While Travelling at the Speed of Light
Sean Mills

The Emily Carr Alumni Gallery and the Queen Elizabeth Theatre are 
pleased to present Telling Time While Traveling at the Speed of Light, 
a solo exhibition of new work by Sean Mills.

Telling Time While Traveling at the Speed of Light explores the 
properties and interactions of light, matter, gravity, motion, space, and 
time, relative to the art object. The objects and paintings in this 
exhibition are derived from a varied array of processes and materials, 
with paint playing a central role in the evolution of the work. Breaking 
from traditional applications, paint is used in unconventional ways that 
mimic other materials and processes of both synthetic and natural 
origins.

In this exploration, a translation occurs between the original idea or 
vision of the artist and the realized art object. Translating an idea into 
an art object is often an indirect act, especially when working with 
unfamiliar methods and materials. In this translation, there is a great 
potential for unintentional and unanticipated consequences. A decision 
must then be made whether to reject, or accept and integrate these 
outcomes. Integration challenges the work and will inevitably lead to 
new interpretations and meaning. The result is a dynamic and ongoing 
process that fuels the artistic practice.

Gallery Hours: during QET performances or by appointment

Please join us on Friday, February the 13th for AEXCESS: Access Gallery's
annual fundraising auction.

For almost twenty years Access Gallery has been committed to facilitating
new and critically engaging work by Canadian and international visual
artists with a strong emphasis on work by local, emerging artists. This
history is reflected in the generosity of more than forty nationally and
internationally recognized artists who have contributed works to this
year's auction. Don't miss this exciting opportunity to invest in
contemporary art while helping Access Gallery continue to support emerging
artists. A selection of works can be seen online at www.vaarc.ca, or visit
the gallery from 12 to 5pm on February 11th, 12th, or 13th for a preview of
all available works.

AEXCESS auction. Featuring work by:

Abbas Akhavan, Rebecca Belmore, Raymond Boisjoly, Matthew Brown, Matt
Bushell, Wes Cameron, Sean Coggins, Christine D'Onofrio, Derek Dunlop,
Jessica Eaton, Geoffrey Farmer, Angus Ferguson, Jason Fitzpatrick, Jacob
Gleeson, Babak Golkar, Jesse Gray, Jeremy Hatch and David Khang, Christian
Kliegel, Gwenessa Lam, Arvo Leo, Justin Ogilvie, Sara Mameni, Phillip
McCrum, Gareth Moore, Ryan Peter, Sandy Plotnikoff, Kristina Lee Podesva,
Project Rainbow, Matt Robertson, Marina Roy, Ken Singer, Mark Soo, Erica
Stocking, Ron Tran, Momoko Usami, Jen Weih, Osvaldo Yero, Elizabeth Zvonar,
and more

Admission is $10, or free for Access members, students, and contributing
artists. There will be door prizes from Solder and Sons, Pyramid Power and
more. Personalized debit cards by Russell Gordon will be available on site.

Catered by Salt Tasting Room

Works needing frames will be eligible for discounts from Chernoff Fine Art

Save the Date! AEXCESS AUCTION! Friday, February 13th, 2009 @ 8PM.

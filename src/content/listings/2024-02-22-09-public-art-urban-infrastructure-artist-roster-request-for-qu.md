The Richmond Public Art Program is seeking artists, designers and other creative
professionals for the 2024"2026 Public Art + Urban Infrastructure Artist Roster. This
roster will be comprised of pre-qualified artists who can provide original artwork for a
variety of art opportunities, including but not limited to cabinet box art wraps, temporary
construction hoarding, waste management containers and other temporary urban
design and infrastructure-based installations in the public realm.

Artist Fee: $500 to $5,000

Eligibility: Creative professionals that live and/or work in Richmond

Deadline:
March 1, 2024

Duration: April 2024 to April 2026

For more information and to apply, visit:
www.richmond.ca/culture/howartworks/publicart/artist-calls

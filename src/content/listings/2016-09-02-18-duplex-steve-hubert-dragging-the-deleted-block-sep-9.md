Steve Hubert

Dragging the Deleted Block into the Deleted Shape Input: DONT 
REMIND ME!!!

September 10th - October 6th, 2016

Opening: Friday, September 9th, 2016, 8-11pm

The exhibition is a showcase of new assemblage-based works by 
Vancouver artist Steve Hubert. Materials are concrete, canvas, 
vinyl, floor tile, Plexiglas, foam, yellow cedar, Baltic birch, spruce, 
ink, and glue. Steve invites us to consider horizontal and vertical 
styles of information gathering, and how this relates to powerful 
forms. 

With an introduction by Greg X Voltz.

Index Gallery | 1305 Powell Street. 

 Thursday June 26 | 7pm-11pm

 NEW WORKS BY EMMETT ROSE

 In the physical world, just as time gives birth to newness so does it bury 
 its dead and its living in the layers itself. Nothing remains as it begins: 
 bodies disintegrate and transform within the gaze of their beholder, 
 transmuted or decomposed dependant upon their value as it shifts 
 through time and perception. Here we see the elegance of form and flesh 
 ensconced in the multitude of itself and its surroundings, the feminine no 
 longer merely the feminine we know, but a shard peering out from under a 
 heap of all the substance that would seek to erase it.

 Curated by: Lindsay Dakin

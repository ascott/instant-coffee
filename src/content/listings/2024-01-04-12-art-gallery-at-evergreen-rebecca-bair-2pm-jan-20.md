Exhibition Reception for "Rebecca Bair: Where the Light Meets My Shoulder" at Art

1205 Pinetree Way,
Coquitlam, BC

Join us on Saturday, January 20 from 2:00-4:30 p.m. for the exhibition reception of
Rebecca Bair: Where the Light Meets My Shoulder at the Art Gallery at
Evergreen.We will also celebrate the release of the essay brochure that
accompanies the exhibition. This colour brochure includes a poetic response by
artist Erika DeFreitas that captures the essence of Bairs artistic intentions when
sharing her work within community.The brochure will be available for purchase

About the Artist
Interdisciplinary artist Rebecca Bair uses a multitude of mediums to push the
boundaries of how Blackness is represented.Her work continues a legacy of refusal
and resilience in her solo exhibition, Where the Light Meets My Shoulder.
Bair
works across installation, video, drawing and photography. Raised in Ottawa, she is
based in Vancouver on the traditional and ancestral territories of the Coast Salish
peoples. She has exhibited widely across the Lower Mainland, including group
exhibitions at the Vancouver Art Gallery and Surrey Art Gallery and a solo exhibition
at West Vancouver Art Museum. Her work continues to gain national recognition and
has been featured in Canadian Art and NUVO Magazine. As an educator and
facilitator, Bair centres mentorship in her work, as she uplifts Black, Indigenous and
racialized youth through teaching at Emily Carr University of Art + Design,
Vancouver, where she also received her Master of Fine Arts in 2020. She also works
across many not-for-profits around the Lower Mainland. Bair is a founding director of
the Black Arts Centre, a Black owned and operated artist-run centre and community
space in Surrey.

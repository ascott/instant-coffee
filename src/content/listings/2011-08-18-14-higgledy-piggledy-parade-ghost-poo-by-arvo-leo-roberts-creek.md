Ghost Poo
by Arvo Leo

September 20th, 2011
Higgledy Piggledy Parade
Roberts Creek.

The Ghost, as an undecidable, does not conform to either polarity of 
a dichotomy. For example - present/absent cure/poison and 
inside/outside.

The figure of a ghost seems to be neither present or absent, or 
alternatively, 

it is both present and absent at the same time.

*

It is customary during a festive parade for participants to usually 
provide the spectators with some kind of sugary sweet. If, rather 
than a lollipop, a token on arguably desirable excrement is offered 
by a participant, in guise and anonymity, how then does this ghostly 
offering trouble notions of obligation and reciprocation within a gift 
giving transaction?

The Helen Pitt Gallery Artist Run Centre is pleased to present:
Signs, City Wall, City Path: An informal panel discussion and site visit
with Jenipher Hur, Avery Nabata, Paul Kajander and Laura Kozak

Saturday February 20, 2010
3:00 pm - Meet at 221A East Georgia Street
3:30 pm - Walking tour of the work (Carrall Street, between Keefer and
Hastings)
4:15 pm - Return to 221A for panel discussion and reception

Signs, City Wall, City Path is a public installation by local artists
Jenipher Hur and Avery Nabata. Part of the Bright Light festival, this work
is a series of outdoor installations that humourously co-opt billboard
marketing, tourist maps and other forms of official public signage.
Jenipher Hur and Avery Nabata address the familiar features of Vancouver’s
constantly changing cityscape, subtly drawing our critical attention to the
way the city represents and negotiates its complex social reality.

Show runs from February 12 to March 21, 2010 (24 Hours)

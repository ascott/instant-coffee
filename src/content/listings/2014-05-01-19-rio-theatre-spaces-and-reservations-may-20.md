Rio Theatre

Screening: Spaces and Reservations

Spaces and Reservations is Brendan Prost's third self-financed feature 
length project. It's an intimate relationship drama that tells the story of a young 
couple being torn apart by malaise and infidelity, and the way the trauma of 
their breakup brings them together again. It's a really raw, candid, and 
naturally performed film that features the talents of a number of local actors, 
and we're all really excited to be sharing the movie this spring via a DIY 
screening tour with dates in Vancouver, Calgary, Winnipeg, Toronto, and 
Victoria.

Our screening dates in Vancouver are May 20 (7:30pm) and 27 (6:30pm and 
9:30pm) at the Rio Theatre (1660 East Broadway), and we have a full list of 
screening dates and online ticket information available on our website 
[http://spacesfilm](http://spacesfilm).

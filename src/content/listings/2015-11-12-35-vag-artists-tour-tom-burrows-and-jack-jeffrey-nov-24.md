Artists' Tour
Tom Burrows and Jack Jeffrey
Tuesday November 24, 7pm
In the Gallery, 3rd floor. Meet at the title wall.

Long-time friends and fellow artists Tom Burrows and Jack Jeffrey lead a
tour of this retrospective exhibition and speak about Jerry Pethick's use
of materials, aesthetics and understanding of art history and science.
Burrows and Jeffrey tell stories of their shared history with Pethick, from
time spent in London, Paris, Montreal and on Hornby Island.

Free for Members or with Gallery admission.

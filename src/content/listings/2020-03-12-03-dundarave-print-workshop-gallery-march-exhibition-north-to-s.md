The Creative Process: North to South
KAREN NGUYEN
PATRICIA GASPAR

March 4 - 28, 2020
Opening: March 7, 7-9 pm

Using various printmaking techniques, artists Karen Nguyen and Patricia Gaspar
explore their creative evolutions through their art processes. Nguyen (Canada) uses her
art to explore the creative process itself and Gaspar (Argentina) uses her art to exhibit
personal experiences.

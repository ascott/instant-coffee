Open and free to all artists living and working in BC. No CARFAC membership required.

CARFAC BC invites you to join us on Tuesday mornings from 10:00 - 11:00 AM for In the
Studio and Beyond: Coffee Break Conversations. We are hosting weekly get-togethers to
provide a space for artists to connect, share what we have been working on, and talk
through how we are managing
our creative work and mental health during this time.
Please feel free to share art and ideas you are working on from home or in your studio.
[http://www.carfacbc.org/](http://www.carfacbc.org/)

Sessions are facilitated by Laura Bucci: [http://laurabucci.com](http://laurabucci.com)

Questions? Contact Laura at coffeebreaks.carfacbc@gmail.com

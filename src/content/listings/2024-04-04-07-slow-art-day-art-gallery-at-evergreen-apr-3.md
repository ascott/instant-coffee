Slow Art Day at Art Gallery at Evergreen
"Paths" April 13, 2024, 12 p.m. - 5 p.m.
Art Gallery at Evergreen
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam BC

Join us for Slow Art Day on Saturday, April 13 at Art Gallery Evergreen during our
public hours from 12:00 p.m. - 5:00 p.m. Slow Art Day is a global event with a simple
mission: help more people discover for themselves the joy of looking at and loving
art.One day each year " April 13 in 2024" people all over the world visit local
museums and galleries to look at art slowly.

Participants are invited to look at selected works of art in the current exhibition "Paths"
to facilitate a slow looking experience. The activity is self-directed, with prompts
provided and the opportunity for casual discussion during available throughout the
public hours. Free to attend. Drop-in. This event is wheelchair accessible. Pay parking
available on-site, easy access from Lafarge Lake-Douglas
College Skytrain Station.

About Paths:

Paths features artwork by Canadian artists exploring paths, both real and imagined,
through the mediums of painting, drawing, sculpture, photography, collage and
printmaking. Paths is organized and circulated by Artists for Kids & the Gordon Smith
Gallery and curated by Amelia Epp.

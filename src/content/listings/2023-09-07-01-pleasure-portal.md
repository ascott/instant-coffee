Open Call:

We are re-issuing the call for Pleasure Portal.
For those who have been following along you may already know that the original show
Pleasure Portal was postponed due to a change in our original venue.

If you missed the original deadline and are interested in submitting work now is your
chance for our upcoming show at The Well Studios (#27- 2111 Main St) on September
24th from 5-7pm.

Here is the call:

Calling artists: performance artists, video artists, word artists, sound artists, movement
artists and all makers whose materials are ephemeral and who are based on unceded
and ancestral territories of the xmky"m (Musqueam), Swxw7mesh
(Squamish), and slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

Inspired by Audre Lordes Uses of the Erotic Pleasure Portal is an exploration of what
it means to reclaim our senses as resistance and come back to our bodies as
pathways to possibility and liberation.

Together we will consider notions of existence in a society that is disembodied and
positions the body only as a site of capitalist productivity.

We will reclaim pleasure within the landscape of the body and beyond, divesting from
oppressive systems in order to create space to turn towards our imagination, to access
possibility.

May we collectively and creatively dream of what it could mean to exist in a world
where pleasure is more important than productivity.

Where pleasure is the point.

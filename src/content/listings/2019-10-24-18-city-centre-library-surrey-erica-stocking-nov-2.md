ARTIST TALK / Erica Stocking / November 2, 2-3pm / City of Surrey Public Art Program

Artist Talk with Erica Stocking

- Saturday, November 2, 2019, 2-3pm

- City Centre Library, 10350 University Drive, Room 120

- easily accessible by Skytrain

- Refreshments will be served

Join us for an artist talk by Erica Stocking about her recently completed public artwork,
Blankets. Installed on the roof of the West Village Park and Energy Centre, Blankets is a
fibreglass sculpture of three blankets arranged in different configurations
(folded,
draped, and wrapped). The sculptures respond to the purpose of the building: to provide
energy and warmth to the City Centre neighbourhood.

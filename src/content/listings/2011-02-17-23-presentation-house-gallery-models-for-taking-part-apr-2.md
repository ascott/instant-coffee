Exhibition Introduction with the curator: April 2, 7:30 pm

Models for Taking Part brings together contemporary media works 
by three international artists: the Romanian and Slovakian duo 
Anetta Mona Chisa & Lucia Tkacova, Berlin-based Tobias Zielony 
and Artur Zmijewski from Poland. The exhibition takes on critical 
questions of the public sphere as an idea (or ideal) that intersects 
uneasily with private interests, and is thus constantly thrown into 
fragments. These works offer models of participation that are 
unsustainable and counter the ideal marriage of democracy and the 
public sphere.

In addition, there will be a special screening of Renzo Martens’ 
Episode III: Enjoy Poverty (2009) followed by a discussion in early 
April.

walk | AUG 15
Multitides - a site collaborative tidal walk

Location: Spanish Banks tidal flats. Enter from the beach in front of the West
Concession

Schedule:
August 15, 8:50am-10:50am
August 16, 9:40am-11:40am
August 17, 10:40am-12:40pm
August 18, 12:30pm-2:30pm

A site collaborative tidal walk at the xsyim (Musqueam) / wew7pay"
(Squamish) intertidal zone, also known as Spanish Banks.

The main choreographers of the tides are celestial bodies. The Pacific Oceans
natural period of oscillation is close to 24 hours. Just as our own circadian rhythms
are affected by influences outside ourselves, the tidal waters act in response to 400
celestial constituents, shifts in local weather patterns and ocean floor topography.

How do you open your awareness to these cycles that are in our bodies and on our
doorsteps?

The tidal walk begins at a site-responsive installation, where participants receive a
guidebook leading
them into the intertidal zone - a frontier emerging and
disappearing between the urban and the natural world.

Whether walking alone or in the company of others, you are free to choose the
duration of your stay within the timeframe of the performance installation.

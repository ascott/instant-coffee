Speakers Series 2008-09

Marianne Nicolson

Kingcome Inlet Pictograph & The House of Ghosts

Tuesday, February 17, 2009 | Langara College Library, 3rd floor 7:00 pm.

Marianne Nicolson will discuss two monumental works of art produced for
very different public spaces. A member of Dzawada'enuxw Tribe of the
Kwakwaka'wakw Nation, Nicolson painted a 28*38 foot pictograph on a
sheer rock face in Kingcome Inlet in 1998. The first pictograph to be
created in the inlet for over sixty years, the painting is viewed from
the waterway access to her ancestral village of Gwa'yi in proximity to
pictographs from a generation earlier.

Ten years later, Nicolson used a highly powered theatrical light, images
and text to transform the Georgia Street facade of the Vancouver Art
Gallery. At dusk, and lasting until dawn, a house front and totem poles
emerged upon the neo-classical architecture of the former courthouse and
jail, simultaneously illuminating the text and image panels of The House
of Ghosts, and reasserting the vitality and contemporary relevance of
Kwakwaka'wakw culture into a place where it was once banned by law.

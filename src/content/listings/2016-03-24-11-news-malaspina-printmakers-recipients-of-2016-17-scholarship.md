Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to announce the recipients of our upcoming
Scholarship Residencies. Scholars Olga Abeleva and Marita Michaelis will
start their four month residencies this May, and present on their research
and work in August. Scholars Danielle Bobier and Krystle Coughlin will
start their eight month residencies in September, with public programs to
be announced.

Olga Abeleva is a student at the Emily Carr University of Art and Design,
and was recently on exchange at the Universitat der Kunst, Berlin. She
works in printmaking and painting, and much of her work explores historical
and personal narratives, frequently blending fact and fiction.

Marita Michaelis is in her first year at Langara College, studying Library
and Information Technology. She learned printmaking at the Purple Thistle
Centre in Vancouver. Her print work includes posters, comics, and zines,
and is informed by her work as an activist and organizer.

Danielle Bobier is currently completing her final year of a Bachelor of
Fine Arts at Emily Carr University of Art + Design. She previously received
a Fine Arts Diploma from Langara College. Her proposed project will use a
variety of printmaking techniques to combine found and created imagery,
exploring themes of deep time, displacement, extraction, and surveying,
with a focus on local geo-political contexts.

Krystle Coughlin is currently completing her final year of a Bachelor of
Fine Arts at The University of British Columbia. She holds a Bachelor of
Arts from the Institute of Gender, Race, Sexuality, and Social Justice at
The University of British Columbia, and an Associate of Arts degree from
Douglas College, where she majored in Women's Studies and Gender Relations.
Her proposed project will use digital collage to create multi-layered texts
that will read with varying cultural, racial, and sexual significances,
depending on the context of their display, and the identity of their audience.

We would like to extend our thanks to all applicants. For more information
about applications to our Scholarship Residency program, please visit:
malaspinaprintmakers.com/submissions

Sketch & Chill 
Join artist Paula Barrantes on Tuesday afternoons in October for a relaxed session of 
drawing and painting on location around the Mount Pleasant area. The group will usually 
walk to a nearby park or caf and get busy painting the scenery or small details in the 
surroundings. Youll find a friendly atmosphere that encourages sharing ideas and 
experiences on art and life while you paint the afternoon away. All skill levels welcomed.

Start date: Tuesday, October 9th, 2018. 
Schedule: Every Tuesday from 4:00 pm to 6:00 pm 
(October 9 " October 30, 2018) 
Location: Meet in front of South Main Gallery. Depending on the weather, you will sketch 
outside or indoors.
Supplies: Please bring your own supplies and a small mixed media sketchbook.
Cost: $25 per class. Drop-ins welcome! 

Contact: For more information, and to sign-up, contact puralunaestudio@gmail.com or 
(778) 855-5585

Flower Petal Tongues 

Opening Reception Friday, September 14th, 7-9 PM

September 14th to Dec 15, 2018

The exhibition presents work resulting from or inducing the action of performance, either as 
text, documentation, or objects. All the selected works are loaned from private collections 
from across Canada, furthering the element of the ongoing, intimate performance of living 
with works of contemporary art. The sensual awareness of viewership, the echoing of the 
object in the body. This is the investigation, presented here through a selection of works 
which both draw from artists investigation into performance, spectatorship, and durational 
viewing. These works draw upon the intimate act of viewership, the durational dialogue that 
springs between the viewer and the object. Within these spaces for reflection, the 
opportunity for reconsideration is not only in the evaluation of that exterior object, but also 
one of re-evaluation of the self " and the experience of viewership. Questions arise of 
whether one performs in an act of looking. Is it a passive observation, a gesture, can it be 
isolated within ourselves but also carries into our bodies, our politics, and our experience - 
asking us to draw from all this in our action of understanding. Exhibiting artists include: 
Valrie Blass, Cynthia Daignault, Sharon Hayes, Maria Hupfield, Annette Kelm, Germaine 
Koh, Jenine Marsh, Liz Magor, Judy Radul, Jana Sterbak and Joyce Wieland.

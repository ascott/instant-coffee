CALL: QUICKIE DUE JUNE 30TH

THE JAMES BLACK GALLERY is dedicated to providing space for emerging artists 
to experiment, create, and show their work. This curation is by Zandi Dandizette. 
The J B G is located in a heritage building in Mount Pleasant (est. 1889). This 
exhibition will show projects of emerging local artists in a variety of formats including 
media, installation, and painting. Any sales of work goes 100% to the artist. 

QUICKIE
definition: something done or made in a hurry: such as a quickly and usually cheaply 
produced work or a hastily performed act of sexual intercourse.

In a society that's so fast, the next viral moment can already be over by the time you 
notice... artists are formally invited to engage with instant gratification in QUICKIE. 
Sketches or gifs, fast food or fast fashion, that minor glance or a quick blow and go. 
Celebrate that lapse in judgement or indulgent reprieve.

What is your take on this ability to get our needs met at the speed we do?

Explorations can be conceptual or process based. Experimentation is welcome. 

Please include:
Images |Name | Work Title/Year | Price | Short Description of Installation | Relation to 
the Theme | 2 Sentence Biography | 2 Sentence Artist Statement

Official submission or proposal due JUNE 30th, 2018. Artists will know by JULY 7th 
on acceptance. 

Submissions accepted at: TheJamesBlackGallery@gmail.com

Media artists will be required to come in August 3rd in order to plan out where the 
work should be shown in advance. Explorations should be delivered by/on August 
5th, and the official show will be on August 7th-11th. Opening 10th. Artists can pick 
up their work on Sunday, August 12th.

Bcc: Digital Art Subscription by Decoy Magazine // April 2017 Featured Artist: Li Alin

Since 1996, Montreal-based performer and provocateuse Li Alin has presented art 
works that question evolution strategies and human reproduction. She is the co-founder 
and co-director of Real Art Estate (RAE www.realartestate.de). She studied 
contemporary art at Paris VIII (France), communications at UQAM (Canada) and 
staging at the Superior School of Art and Design in Karlsruhe (Germany). Her projects 
include a notorious internet sperm bank,Eugenie, sponsored by the Canada Council for 
the Arts and the Canadian Cultural Center in Paris, the provocative sperm distribution 
Weisser Markt in the Zoologischer Stadtgarten Karlsruhe, installations created with 
Cecile Martin during MUTEK 2014, Royal Mustang presented at La Chapelle, and more. 
http://www.lialin.net/

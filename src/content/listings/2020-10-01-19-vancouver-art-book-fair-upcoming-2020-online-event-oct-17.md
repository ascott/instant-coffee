FREE & OPEN TO THE PUBLIC:

VABF is a multi-day festival of artists publishing featuring local, national and
international publishers, as well as a diverse line-up of programs, performances and
installations. Featured artists travel to Vancouver from across Canada and the globe,
and produce everything from books, magazines, zines and
printed ephemera to digital,
performative or other experimental forms of publication.

Visit VANCOUVERARTBOOKFAIR.COM for full exhibitors list, programming and other
information.

//

VABF is a non-profit organization committed to fostering access and deepening
engagement with publication as an artistic medium and social practice. The organization
operates year-round thanks to dozens of dedicated volunteers.

We gratefully acknowledge that our annual activities take place on the unceded
Territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish) and
Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

Where possible we want to make sure everyone can enjoy this event: please contact
@VancouverArtBookFair with venue accessibility inquires.

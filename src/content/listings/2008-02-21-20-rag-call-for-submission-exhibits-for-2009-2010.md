CALL FOR SUBMISSION
Richmond Art Gallery - Exhibits for 2009-2010

DESCRIPTION:

The Richmond Art Gallery is accepting proposals from individuals, collaborating artists and curators for exhibitions 2009-2010. Work in all media will be considered. Submissions for the Art Gallery must include a completed application form and be received by the Gallery for the deadline of March 1, 2008. Submissions may include no more than eight images on disc, a recent CV, a description of the project and a self-addressed stamped envelope.


HOW TO APPLY:

Applications may be sent to the Richmond Art Gallery, Attention,
Exhibition Committee, 180-7700 Minoru Gate Richmond, BC, V6Y 1R9.

Application forms are available at the front desk at the Richmond
Cultural Centre or can be downloaded from the RAG website.


APPLICATION DEADLINE: March 1, 2008

NICOLE ONDRE | PAINTINGS
CSA SPACE VANCOUVER

Curated by Graham Dalik & Adam Harrison

27 April - 27 May, 2012
Opening reception: Friday, 27 April, 18:00 – 21:00

Nicole Ondre is a Vancouver-based artist, and co-founder of Exercise 
Projects.
She has recently exhibited at Hayaka Arti, Istanbul, and Night Gallery, 
Los Angeles, as well as a solo exhibition at Hunter and Cook Projects in 
Toronto.

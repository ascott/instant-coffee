SESSIONAL LECTURESHIPS, WINTER 2009

The Department of Art History, Visual Art, and Theory will offer part-time Sessional lectureships for 
the winter session 2009 as follows. The sections, including section date and time, are tentative 
and subject to change. The positions are tentative and subject to enrollment and final 
budgetary approval.

VISA 110 Foundation Computing for Visual Arts**

VISA 180 Introductory Studio One

VISA 182 Studio One

VISA 183 Studio Two

VISA 210 Digital Arts**

VISA 220 Drawing

VISA 230 Painting

VISA 240 Introduction to Photography

VISA 250 Introduction to Printmaking

VISA 310 Intermediate Digital Arts I

VISA 320 Intermediate Drawing I

VISA 330 Intermediate Painting

VISA 340 Intermediate Photography

VISA 350 Intermediate PrintMedia

VISA 410/1 Advanced Digital Arts I

**plus oversight and or teaching in associated lab sections.

Preference will be given to applicants with a degree in their discipline (MFA for Visual Art) and
with teaching experience at the college or university level. The period of appointment is for a 
single academic term; Term 1 (Sep 01 - Dec 30, 2009) or Term 2 (Jan 01 - Apr 30, 2010).

Effective July 1, 2009 the salary range for Sessional lecturers is $5,970 to $6,477. Salaries are
dependent on the period of appointment, percentage of full-time load, and years of appropriate 
full-time post-secondary teaching experience.

With the letter of application, candidates should include a statement on pedagogy, teaching and 
research methods, sample course outlines, teaching evaluations, a CV, and a transcript of 
academic record and publications and CD portfolio. The CV should include a detailed list of all 
post-secondary courses taught including name, length, credit value, and teaching responsibilities. 
If applying from outside the department, three confidential letters of reference, in sealed 
envelopes from appropriate persons, should also be forwarded.

All positions are governed by UBC’s “Agreement on Conditions of Appointment for Sessional and 
Part-Time Faculty members.”

Please send letters of application to: Whitney Friesen, Administrator

11. CARFAC BC | Marketing for Visual Artists | MAY 21
CARFAC BC Presents:

Marketing for Visual Artists with Chris Tyrell

Thursday, May 21st, 6:30 pm 
Alliance for Arts and Culture 
Suite 100 - 938 Howe St 
Between Smithe and Nelson 

Artists must develop their small business skills if they wish to earn a living from a visual arts
practice. Chris, the author of “Artist Survival Skills: How to Make a Living as a Canadian Visual
Artist,” makes the implementation of some basic business practices comprehensible and gives 
attendees an enthusiasm for some of the practical skills that can very effectively build a visual
arts practice. Chris understands the artistic personality; he knows business building techniques 
that are well suited to the artistic temperament. Past participants are gratified by his 
presentations. 

CARFAC Members: $25 
Non Members: $35
Pre-registration is required: communications@carfac.ca or 604-681-3535

Chris Tyrell has been involved with the visual arts, visual artists and the arts in general since 1976 
when he established Presentation House Gallery of Photography in North Vancouver. He was a 
founding member of the BC Touring Council and co-founded the Alliance for Arts and Culture in 
Vancouver.

Chris is the founding editor of the Visual Arts Newsletter of Opus Framing & Arts Supplies. He 
established its format and has written every editorial since its inception in 1986. His editorials 
earned Vancouver Business and the Arts awards for the newsletter. He is the author of Artist 
Survival Skills: How to Earn a Living as a Canadian Visual Artist. 

CARFAC BC is the provincial branch of the national association of professional visual and media 
artists. CARFAC defends artists’ rights through advocacy and professional development and 
produces a schedule of artists’ fees that is widely recognized as the national standard.

JULY 21st, 2012 AT 3PM

EXHIBITION TOUR OF PHANTASMAGORIA by Jonah Gray

and

DANCE PERFORMANCE by Christine Elsey, "White Lotus in Soup", 2012

Raymond Boisjoly, Christopher Brayshaw, Andrew Dadson, Jessica Eaton, Julia
Feyrer, Allison Hrabluik, Jay Bundy Johnson, Evan Lee, Mathew McWilliams,
Rachelle Sawatsky, Kevin Schmidt, Dan Siney, Corin Sworn, Ron Tran,
Elizabeth Zvonar

Christine Elsey is an established Vancouver contemporary dance artist and
choreographer with over 25 years of experience in both contemporary dance and
performance art. This contemporary dance work has been choreographed by
Christine Elsey in response to Allison Hrabluik and Andrea Young's video
projection "Abut". Artistic direction by Ingrid Baxter.

Jonah Gray is an independent curator as well as co-editor and -publisher of
Pyramid Power, a contemporary art magazine based in Vancouver. He earned a
bachelor of media art from Emily Carr Institute in 2006 and is currently an MA
candidate at the University of British Columbia in the Critical and Curatorial
Studies department. In 2009, along with Pyramid Power's co-founders, he
received Emily Carr's Emily Award for Postgraduate Achievement.

Phantasmagoria closes Sunday, July 22, 2012.

Griffin Art Projects

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Griffin Art Projects is thrilled to present a weekend of collaborative panels and talks
facilitated live over zoom in partnership with Centre A, The New Gallery and the Asian
Canadian Writer's Workshop with collaborative support from Tea Base, Aiya"' and Youth
Collaborative for Chinatown. Join us for a weekend of conversation, connection, and
solidarity as we celebrate Chinatowns across the country and engage with topics that range
from cultural heritage and revitalization to gentrification, economy and the changes that
have swept across Canadas Chinatowns due to development, and population, prior to and
post-COVID.

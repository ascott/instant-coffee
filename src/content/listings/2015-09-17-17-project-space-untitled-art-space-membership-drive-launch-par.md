Project Space Membership Drive Launch Party
September 26 from 8 pm till 1 am
Untitled Art Space, 436 Columbia Street

Join us at the annual fundraiser for the Vancouver Art/Book Fair and other
art publishing projects.

As Project Space, Vancouver’s non-profit organization dedicated to
publication as an artistic medium, nears its 10-year anniversary, we’re
gearing up for a year of organizing more amazing events and projects like
the annual Vancouver Art/Book Fair (the only art book fair in Canada) and
Monthly Open Studios featuring a varied roster of artists and publishers.

To keep the lights on and the team working, we’re throwing a multifaceted
art-centric party on September 26th at Untitled Art Space (436 Columbia
Street) that will be an event unto itself. Featuring a silent art auction,
a silent auction of our favourite locally sourced goods, music by
Selectors' Records, a reading room by Moniker Press and local artist
Brandon Cotter, and libations from 33 Acres and The Sunday Cider Co.,
there’s lots to take in. And we’ll be selling Project Space memberships
(starting at $20), which offer lots of enviable perks—like exclusive access
to the Members Preview of the Vancouver Art/Book Fair, cinch bags,
limited-edition risograph prints, discounts at local bookstores The Paper
Hound and Lucky's Comics, and more.

Silent auction artists include: Brandon Cotter, Patrick Cruz, Sylvana
d'Angelo, Paul de Guzman, Sarah Davidson, Ben Frey, Del Hillier, Julia
Hong, Leanne Johnson, Donato Mancini, Jessie McNeil, Ryan Ming, My Name is
Scot, Heidi Nagtegaal, Aaron Read, Emile Rubino, Alex Stursberg, Melanie
Thibodeau, Christian Vistan, Carrie Walker, Erica Wilk, Antoni Wojtyra

Silent goods auction includes: Fillip, C Magazine, 1080p, Selectors'
Records, Pacific Rhythm, Strathcona Stockings, Harvest Community Foods, Or
Bookstore, The Storm Crow Tavern, READ Books, Publication Studio, Our
Community Thrift & Vintage, Make Vancouver, Hey Jude, Treasure Green Tea
Company

The Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory is pleased to present The Fold,
the University of British Columbia's 2021 BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating Exhibition. The
concept of the fold relates to the opening and closing of space, whether that is
something as simple as paper or as complex as time. This year's cohort of graduating
students engages with the fold by presenting an exhibition that manifests in both
the virtual and physical realms, and each participating artist proposes a personal
folding of time with past, present and future all being shown at one moment.

We invite you to join us in celebrating the diverse practices and accomplishments of
our 2021 graduates. We look forward to seeing you at The Fold!

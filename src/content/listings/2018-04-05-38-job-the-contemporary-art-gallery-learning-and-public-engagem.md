Learning and Public Engagement Assistant
YCW Summer Student Contract

The Organization

The Contemporary Art Gallery is Vancouvers longest standing independent public art 
gallery dedicated to the research, exhibition, education and documentation of 
contemporary visual art as it is practiced locally through to internationally. It aspires to 
generate significant audiences for its innovative and diverse programs through free 
access and a profile that is international in scope. CAG aims to be perceived as a 
producer and transmitter of new ideas and activities, as a container for artistic 
experience. Radiating out from our center in Vancouver, our program seeks to signify an 
understanding of art as something of meaning in our daily lives, asserting a continuum, 
enmeshed within and of significance to all other experiences.

For more information about the CAG, please visit www.contemporaryartgallery.ca.

Job Summary

The Learning and Public Engagement Assistant will provide assistance with Learning 
and Public Programming initiatives including supporting the planning and delivery of a 
three week Summer Intensive for youth ages 15-19 in partnership with Arts Umbrella, 
and will assist with public programs related to the current exhibitions. The Learning and 
Public Engagement Assistant will perform these duties during a 12 week period at 35 
hours per week. The position will be conducted in English and will begin June 5, 2018 
and end August 25, 2018.

This summer student position is part of Young Canada Works. Applicants must be a full-
time student in the semester immediately preceding June, returning to school full-time in 
the fall and be between 16 and 30 years old at the start of employment to apply. Eligible 
applicants must also be Canadian citizens, permanent residents or have official refugee 
protection status, be legally eligible to work in BC, not have another fulltime job 
(30+hours/week) and be registered in the YCW online candidate inventory 
(www.youngcanadaworks.gc.ca).

Duties will include

- Researching links with the exhibition program and the Provincial K-12 curriculum
- Developing lesson plans for exhibitions
- Supporting the planning and delivery of the Summer Intensive for youth
- Assisting with the delivery of public programs
- Liaising with involved parties and ensuring deadlines are met
- Updating contact databases
- Data entry and record keeping
- Other duties as required

Qualifications

A background or demonstrated interest in education and visual arts
Enrolled in a post-secondary institution in education or visual arts program
Must be able to work in a PC office environment, using all MS Office software
Excellent time management skills and demonstrated ability to manage multiple 
tasks and priorities
Careful attention to detail
Excellent communication abilities and experience working in a team environment
Creative problem solving
Teaching experience desired

Hours: Tuesday to Saturday 10am " 6pm

Salary: $15.00 per hour, plus a percentage in lieu of benefits. Occasional evenings may 
be required.

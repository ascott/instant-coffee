Modern Calligraphy for Beginners
Oct. 26, 2:00 p.m - 5:00 p.m.
Present Art Studio, 8930 Oak Street, Vancouver, BC V6P 4B7

**Hosted by
Vancouver Calligraphy (@vancouvercalligraphy) and
Present Art Studio (@presentartsvan)**

What you will learn

- Techniques on how to correctly hold and use a calligraphy pen holder
and nib
- Tips to create the perfect strokes in ink
- How to write a modern calligraphy alphabet as well as learning how to
create your own alphabet style
- How to join up letters to create beautiful looking words
- Lots of information and tips on how to practice from home
and
recommendations on paper, ink and equipment

Take home kit included in ticket (pen, nib, ink, worksheets)

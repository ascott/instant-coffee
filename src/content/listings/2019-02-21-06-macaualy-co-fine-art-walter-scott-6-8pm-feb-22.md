Walter Scott
Slipping on the Missing X
Macaualy & Co. Fine Art 

February 22nd - March 23rd, 2019
Opening Reception Friday February 22nd, 6-8 pm

Macaulay & Co. Fine Art is pleased to present a solo exhibition of new and recent work by 
contemporary artist Walter Scott. Slipping on the Missing X comprises of mixed media 
sculptures that explore disembodiment, empathy, and navigation through social and 
emotional worlds.

Walter Scott ( b. 1985) is a Kahnawake born contemporary artist, currently based in 
Montreal and Toronto. Scott's practice interdisciplinary practice includes drawing, writing, 
video, performance, and sculpture. Through his work, Scott explores contemporary 
questions of representation, cultural production, popular culture, and narrative construction. 
Walter Scott has shown in solo and group exhibitions in Japan, North American, Europe, 
and the United States. His most recent exhibitions include Betazoid in a Fog at the Remai 
Modern, and Art For A New Understanding: Native Voices 1950s to Now, Museum of 
American Art, Arizona.

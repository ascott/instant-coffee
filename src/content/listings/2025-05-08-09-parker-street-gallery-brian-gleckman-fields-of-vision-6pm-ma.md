Brian Gleckman: Fields of Vision - Selected Abstract and Colour Field Paintings

Opening Reception: May 9, 6-9pm
Exhibit: May 9-24

Parker Street Gallery

1000 Parker St #102, Vancouver, BC V6A 2H2

Gallery Hours: M-F 11am to 3pm or by appointment (www.parkerstreetgallery.com)

By way of abstract and colour field painting, Vancouver artist Brian Gleckman
explores
the interplay between compositional space, visual texture, and movement. Fields of
Vision is a sampling of the directions that Gleckman has taken in this personal visual
journey. Comprised of more than twenty paintings, this exhibit invites viewers to
immerse themselves in interpretive colour fields as contexts for their own
explorations.

Originally from Los Angeles, where he studied Baroque and Rococo art history,
Gleckman was inspired in the studio by the energy and tension of Fauvism, Abstract
Expressionism, and Minimalism. His paintings fuse these influences and focuses on
the visual and conceptual potential of composition.

House Games is a non-profit artist-run triennial. This event takes place 
in a standard Finnish apartment which maintains its function as a 
home, while serving as an alternative exhibition outlet. The exhibition is 
composed of recent artwork from a broad range of artistic practices 
and cultures. The site-specific installations, videos, paintings, drawings 
and sculptures shown in House Games range from free associations to 
literal takes on the theme of house and/or home.

This call for artists is open to artists of all disciplines including 
installation, dance, media art, performance, sound art, and all kinds of 
visual arts. The selection of artwork will be based on the thematic 
relevance to "home/house" and the ability to be site-specific. New or 
re-newed work will take priority.

If you are interested in submitting a project proposal for the next House 
Games exhibition, please send a free-form application including a 
project description, and project related images (92dpi, minimum 10 x 
15cm, maximum 5 images) to the email address below. 

Floor plans will be provided upon request. 
For more information please contact us at:
housegames.info@gmail.com

www.housegames.fi

Deadline: January 31st 2014.

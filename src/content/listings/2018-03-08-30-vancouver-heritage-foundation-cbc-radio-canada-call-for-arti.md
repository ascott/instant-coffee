CALL FOR ARTISTS

Vancouver Heritage Foundation in partnership with CBC Radio-Canada, with
support from JJ Bean Coffee Roasters and produced in partnership with the
City of Vancouver Public Art Program, is once again accepting submissions
for a large scale art installation on Hamilton Street, on the CBC Plaza. The
WALL, a 40x30 vertical concrete expanse, offers opportunity each year for
an artist to respond to the theme of Vancouvers built environment.
Independent curators and artists, including those who have previously
applied but not been selected, are invited to apply by March 23rd, at 5pm.
The successful artist and/or curator will have the opportunity to visit the
CBC Archives as a source for material and inspiration.

We invite you to share this call for artists with your network and encourage
those interested to apply. Attached is the Call for Proposals, and below is
the link to our webpage where more information, as well as details about
previous artists and their work, are available. Any questions or inquiries
can be directed to mail@vancouverheritagefoundation.org.

www.vancouverheritagefoundation.org/projects/the-wall/

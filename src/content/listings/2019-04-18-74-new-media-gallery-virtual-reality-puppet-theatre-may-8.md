About this course:
This unique, collaborative, workshop series is for kids interested in experimenting and 
learning about fascinating new technologies. Work behind the scenes in the Learning Lab to 
create an innovative Virtual Reality (VR) puppet theatre and the awesome productions you 
create inside it. 

Youll have a chance to dive into both sides of the virtual puppet world. Learn cutting edge 
digital media tools as well as traditional hand tools. Understand how Virtual Reality 
technology really works. Work on puppets, scenarios and scripts. You will become a real-
life puppeteer on a virtual stage. 

We are delighted to welcome experienced educator, artist and musician Flick Harrison who 
is developing and leading this awesome project.

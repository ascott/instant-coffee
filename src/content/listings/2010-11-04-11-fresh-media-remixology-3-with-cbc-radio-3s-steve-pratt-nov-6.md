Fresh Media presents:

Remixology 3 with CBC radio 3's Steve Pratt!
5 pm - 11 pm
Sat, Nov 6
W2 Storyeum Salon (151 West Cordova)
Vancouver, BC

In collaboration with Media Democracy Day, OpenFile.ca, W2, and the
Vancouver Community Television Association, Remixology 3 will feature a
talk from Steve Pratt, director of CBC Radio 3 and CBC Radio Digital
Programming, amazing local music, and an opportunity to socialize and
network with Vancouver's leading media innovators. This month's gathering
is the official after-party from Media Democracy Day, the launch party for
OpenFile Vancouver, and a fundraiser for VCTA.

Doors open at 5 p.m. for food, music, cash bar, and socializing.

Starting at 7 p.m., Steve Pratt will discuss the future of media; this will
be moderated by Karen Pratt, the Vancouver editor of OpenFile. Following
the discussion, the gathering will close with a collaboratory
social/networking session including local music from Buckman Coe andl DJ
Brad Winter.

South Main Gallery
Opening Reception Friday, August 9, 6 to 9 pm
279 East 6th Avenue | Vancouver BC

Straight from Los Angeles, artist Charlie Edmiston opens his second solo-show at South Main 
Gallery, during the unveil of his mural for the 2019 edition of the Vancouver Mural Festival. 

Gestalt, translating to shape" in German, is a new body of work that explores the relationship 
between color and form. Emphasizing a more sculptural approach, the works in this series play 
with depth and dimension, turning each color field into a physical object. With raw wood and 
contrasting vibrant hues, a harmony is created between natural and man-made material.

Moon Lantern Making Workshop
August 10, 5:30PM

Join the Secret Lantern Society and create your very own moon lantern! Youll be 
given all the materials and knowledge you need to build a lantern embedded with 
natural materials and celestial imagery. We also invite you to share your finished 
lantern during the lantern procession at Blackout: Night Sky Festival taking place on 
Saturday, August 12.

Workshop cost includes materials, museum admission and tax. Participants of all 
ages are welcome (children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult). 
Participants may leave the finished lanterns at the museum to dry and pick them up 
on the day of the lantern procession.

Registration is required. 

Adults: $40
Youth + Senior: $30
MOA Members + UBC Students & Staff: $20

LEARN MORE: [http://moa.ubc.ca/moon-lantern-workshop/](http://moa.ubc.ca/moon-lantern-workshop/)

Emily Carr / AHIS 333 — DING: the Art and Politics of Assembly Presents

Aaron Gach, Center for Tactical Magic

Thursday, Apr, 9, 4:00pm 
Room 301, Emily Carr University
Open to the Public

The Center for Tactical Magic engages in extensive research, development, and deployment of 
the pragmatic system known as Tactical Magic. A fusion force summoned from the ways of the 
artist, the magician, the ninja, and the private investigator, Tactical Magic is an amalgam of 
disparate arts invoked for the purpose of actively addressing Power on individual, communal, and 
transnational fronts. At the CTM we are committed to achieving the Great Work of Tactical Magic 
through community-based projects, daily interdiction, and the activation of latent energies toward 
positive social transformation.

Bio: Though associated with numerous groups including the American Red Cross, Earth First!, 
and the Black Panthers, Aaron Gach is best known for his position as co-founder of the Center 
for Tactical Magic (CTM). Established in 2000, this Berkeley-based organization is committed to 
the activation of latent energies toward positive transformation. In his work as a teacher, 
spokesman, and social leader, Gach makes a conscious effort to combine the disciplines of art, 
design, architecture, and community service. A man of many trades, Gach and has also taught at 
the San Francisco Art Institute and the University of the Pacific. His work produced through the 
CTM has appeared most recently at the Massachusetts Museum of Contemporary Art, the 
Museum of Contemporary Art in Chicago and Grand Arts in Kansas. Recently he presented in 
NYC as part of Creative Time's "Democracy in America: The National Campaign" and in Frankfurt, 
Germany at the Frankfurter Kunstverein

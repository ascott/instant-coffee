Burrard Arts Foundation invites you to join us for the opening reception
for two simultaneous exhibitions: Experience Spheres and Antoni Wojtyra:
BAF Residency on Saturday, January 10 at 6 PM.

1. In collaboration with PuSh Festival and Tangible Interaction, BAF is
proud to present Experience spheres, an interactive art installation
seeking to transport participants to new places. The public is invited to
enter the Experience Spheres by placing their heads inside the
installations where they will be fed different sensory stimuli including a
range of audio, visual and audio/visual experiences, lights, video streams
and soundscapes.

The project is an mirror of the transformative theatre experience that PuSh
Festival provides to its audiences. The Experience Spheres seek to deepen
the participants’ sense of place, belonging and temporality—to emerge with
a more fully rounded understanding and awareness of their place in the world.

2. BAF is delighted to present new paintings by BAF studio resident: Antoni
Wojtyra.

Sweaty toil has produced works on paper that Wojtyra (VOY-TI-RA) calls
"double-surface" pictures. He states:

The studio is an ABC playground. My time and energy is spent studying
painting's first principles. I learned that any flat surface has two main
sides; we could call each side the first and second surface. The face or
first-surface of a painted picture is conventionally "a painting." A
painting is usually also an object; generally flat-faced and a type of
shallow relief; it has sides and depth. A painting has a back. The back of
a painting is its second-surface. I paint the front and the back of a
surface to make my works. They are double-surface paintings.

Genevieve Robertson and Jay White
Pinhole Cameras and Pipelines: Ecological Stewardship, Art and Activated
Communities

Saturday January 28, 1-4 pm
Price: By Donation

Artists Genevieve Robertson and Jay White invite participants to work with them
in bringing attention to local resource extraction projects that have an effect on
micro and macro ecosystems, from local waterways to global climate change.
Gen and Jay will host a discussion about resource extraction and transport
projects in the Lower Mainland of BC, including the proposed Woodfibre Liquid
Fracked Gas facility, the Kinder Morgan crude oil pipeline and the proposed
McNab Creek gravel mine in Howe Sound. Drawing from the strategies used in
Robertson and White's The Watchers, we will collectively build long-exposure
pinhole cameras with the intention of visiting and drawing attention to a local
development project that workshop participants are concerned about.

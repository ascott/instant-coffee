The Curator's Sauna
Immediate Open Call !!!

_______till July 29th

Please help to spread it out

As art spaces, particularly visual art spaces such as galleries and museums are
supposed to be seen and positioned as places of public discourse and dialogue, in
which, the curator plays a key role to create the opportunity.

In thinking about the platforms for public discourse, we, the alternative art space
executors, found ourselves thinking about saunas, baths and the functions they
served across in various cultures as a platform for more
than just physical wellness,
they served as a place for the public to intersect exchange, where the
discourse/naked talk has been disregarded for long.

We are creating a sauna space within the gallery space and inviting proposals which
engage with the ideas of public discourse, dialogue, community and health,
preferably while in the sauna.

Who is the curator?
This project is curated by S, a curatorial boyz band casually formed by Steven Tong
and Steven Dragonn. Steven Tong is an independent curator and the co-director of
CSAspace, an independent Canadian project space founded by Christopher
Brayshaw, Adam Harrison and Steven Tong in Vancouver since 2007. Steven
Dragonn, founder, director and curator of Canton-sardine, which was founded with
Dr. Xiaoyan Yang in 2018. This project will take place in Canton-sardine.

Submission
This open call is world wide! Simply send your proposal and short bio via email to
stdragonn@canton-sardine.com Act now! Until July 29th, 2023.

Selection
Due
to the limitation of time and space, the proposal accepted by the curator will be
announced and will be executed weekly from June 14th to July 29th. All proposals
will also be listed on Canton-sardines website, e-Newsletter and social medias
handles, updated weekly. (Please subscribe our mailing list to receive the result via
Canton-sardines website at the bottom of the front page)

Schedule
NO OPENING!!! Instead, a closing event will be addressed on July 29th to discover
what happened with the project completed. During the project, everyone is welcome
to have a sauna in the gallery, free charge!

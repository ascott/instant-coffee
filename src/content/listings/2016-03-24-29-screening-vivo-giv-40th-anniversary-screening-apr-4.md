GIV 40th Anniversary Screening

April 4th 2016
7:00pm Screening
Admission is free

VIVO Media Arts Centre in the *NEW* Microcinema
2625 Kaslo St., Vancouver 

In celebration of Montral-based video distributor, Groupe Intervention
Vido's 40th Anniversary, VIVO will be screening a program drawn from their
illustrious archive. The program titled, "Gestures of Performativity", was
curated by Dayna Mcleod, who will be in attendance. The program is
comprised of English, French, and silent works. 

Program Notes and Video Synopses can be found on VIVO's website:

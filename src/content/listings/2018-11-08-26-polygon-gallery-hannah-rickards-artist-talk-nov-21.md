Hannah Rickards: Artist Talk 
7:30pm | November 21

Hannah Rickards is an interdisciplinary artist from London, UK, now based in 
Vancouver. Rickards' interdisciplinary practice explores the fluid, non-linear dynamic 
between site, gesture, staging and recording integrating elements of the language of 
performance, film, drawing and installation.

Panel Conversation:
Objects and Desire: the lives of artworks

With panelists Wil Aballe, LaTiesha Fazakas, Jane Irwin, and Ian Thom, Senior 
Curator-Historical, Vancouver Art Gallery

Date: Saturday, April 14, 3 PM
Location: Room 4East, Vancouver Art Gallery, 750 Hornby Street, Vancouver

Free for Members or with Gallery admission

Join us for a roundtable conversation with collectors Wil Aballe, LaTiesha Fazakas, 
Jane Irwin and Ian Thom, Senior Curator-Historical, as they discuss practices of 
collecting, the impact of the private collector, and the caring and sharing of 
collections in a variety of contexts.

Drawing inspiration from our current exhibitions A Cultivating Journey: The Herman 
Levy Legacy and Living, Building, Thinking: art and expressionism, from the 
McMaster Museum of Arts Levy Collection and Bequest from Herman Herzog Levy, 
we will examine how private collections find their way in and out of institutional 
environments and the residual effects this movement might have on the artworks 
contained therein.

With speakers representing a range of collection environments"from the public 
institution to the semi-public and private, we will discuss the humanity and 
responsibility of collecting, as well as how to determine a collections function and 
preservation over time. Panelists will also share the desires and enthusiasms that 
drive them to collect and details about an important acquisition in their own personal 
collections.

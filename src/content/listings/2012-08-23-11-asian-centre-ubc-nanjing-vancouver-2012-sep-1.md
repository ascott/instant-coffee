Nanjing - Vancouver 2012
南京 -温哥华 2012 艺术交流展
Asian Centre, UBC

Sept 1-3, 2012

Opening: Saturday, September 1st 2012 at 1:30 pm

Featuring: Jin Chang, Weihong Jin, Jun Huang, Gabor Koleszar, Zhang 
Li, Qin Shen, Howard Ursuliak, Weide Xu, Siming Yang, Qin Zhao

Curated by Wei Xia

A series of exhibitions “Space beyond the Boxes – The Choice of 
Destiny”  was launched in Shangdong Contemporary Arts Center in 
Nanjing, China in May 2011.

Presenting artists from different regions, cultural backgrounds and 
artistic training it is meant to engage the individual artists and audience 
to find a way out of rigid boxes through artistic presentation and 
discourse. They are so diverse in media and styles such as oil, acrylic, 
Chinese style ink-brush paintings and photographs, that they would 
normally be featured in separate group shows.

However they share a common characteristic: their cross-cultural, 
cross-regional and cross-professional living and working experiences 
endow them with the multi-faceted identities, which generate a cross-
cultural and cross-boundary effect in their art. It is not hard to see a 
subtle correlation between their art works. Through a stimulated 
intellectual exchange, the audience can observe how the artists see 
themselves and how the artists want to be seen.

Asian Studies Department of UBC kindly supports the second exhibition 
in this series.

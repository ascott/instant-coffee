Group exhibition, A Bright Mark Left, featuring Charlene Vickers, Jean Paul Langlois,
and Shannon Pawliw, opens at THIS Gallery August 15, 2025

A Bright Mark Left is a collaboration between three Vancouver-based visual artists -
Shannon Pawliw, Charlene Vickers, and Jean Paul Langlois. All three artists work in
a variety of media, but with a focus on large-scale colour-filled painting. A Bright
Mark Left refers to the after-image effect experienced when seeing the
complimentary colour.

Colour stays with you in your eyes and perception. It burns, flashes"it lingers as a
bright silhouette in your sight. Colour tells stories, communicates emotions, and
shapes viewers perceptions. Each artists approach"whether rooted in ancestral
connection, pop culture tropes, or quiet protest"speaks to the different ways colour
can embody histories, identities, and emotions. The resulting exhibition serves as a
chromatic language that transcends verbal explanation, inviting viewers to interpret
and connect in their own way.

A solo exhibition featuring the works of lauren everall/lola elkmother

Artist Statement:

These works employ the orifice as a site to explore and experience that which 
is abject, that which is metaphysically discordant and allows for ambiguous 
and phenomenological responses from the viewer. My experience of abjection 
during harvest and preservation of these materials is critical to the functioning 
of my expression and intuition to curate these specific materials into new 
forms and open narratives. Drawing from the psychosexual development 
theories of Julia Kristeva, Sigmund Freud, and Helene Cixous to unpack 
themes of sexuality, the abject, domesticity, and identity, I seek to collide an 
intuitive and material practice within a contextual framework of feminist critical 
theory.
I seek to reconcile the ontology of my childhood with that of my adulthood 
experience through the physical representation of the orifice wherein lies both 
formal and conceptual tensions. Illusory materials of the 1980’s are pulled, 
stretched, pierced, and sewn in, over, and through hunted, harvested, and 
preserved ‘raw’ materials of the present time; forcing a relationship between 
past and present, domestic and wild. The orifice is a place of balance and 
unbalance. A membrane represents a place of collision, a site for inspection 
in relationships before the entrance or exit into multiple narratives which are 
non-linear, perverse, violent, transcendental, and consuming.

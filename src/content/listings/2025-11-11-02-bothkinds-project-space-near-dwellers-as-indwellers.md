Near Dwellers as Indwellers

an art + research project exploring new visions for multispecies neighborhoods

Bothkinds Project Space
602 E. Hastings Street, Vancouver, BC V6A 1R1

Exhibition Dates: October 1 " December 16, 2025
Opening Reception: Friday, October 3, 2025, 5:30 - 9:00 pm

Near Dwellers as Indwellers is an art-research project: an exhibition, an expanded
workshop, an open forum, a set of thought experiments, and an invitation to
reimagine our relationship with undomesticated animals in the city. We invite
participants"artists, designers, scholars, students, members of the public"to join
us in exploring the aesthetics and politics of who exactly is visible as an animal
neighbour? What are their needs, and what does this entail for how we share space,
care for, and take responsibility for the well-being of our fellow animal neighbours?

The exhibition features works by artists and designers, alongside a guest speaker
series and artist-led workshops, to explore our attitude to wild and enigmatic more-
than-human animals that traverse, endure, or dwell within the city, and in turn, to
imagine new ways for living more conscientiously with them as members of our
communities.

As van Dooren and Bird Rose ask: What are the possibilities for a more equitable
multispecies city, a task that is particularly important for those species that are in
some way tied or drawn to specific city places, and perhaps especially, in these
perilous times, for those whose future is endangered.

Near Dwellers as Indwellers is presented in collaboration with Bothkinds Project
Space. We are grateful for the support of Emily Carr University of Art + Design and
the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council.

The Near Dwellers exhibition program, initiated in 2023, is jointly led by Street Road
Artists Space (Cochranville, Pennsylvania) and the Tree Museum (Pender Island,
BC).

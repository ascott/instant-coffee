Summer slideshows: David Wisdom
Tuesday July 26, 7pm

Tickets: $10 General admission/$5 for Members and Students with 
valid ID.

Former CBC radio personality and photographer David Wisdom hosts 
his legendary communal slideshows. Featuring local artists and arts 
practitioners, the evening is a warm gathering pairing selected images 
with music, with introductions by Wisdom and the artists. Each 
presenter creates a new slideshow to share, and the event is followed 
by musical set and chance to mingle with the artists. 

Doors open and cash bar 6:30pm 
Slideshows 7pm 
Musical performance 8:30pm

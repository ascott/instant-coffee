ISSUE MAGAZINE is accepting submissions!

ISSUE Magazine is a resurrection of a publication that came out of 
the Helen Pitt Gallery and Unit 306 Society starting in September of 
1983. The revived ISSUE is looking to encourage discussion about 
the work happening in Vancouver, while providing a venue for 
critical discourse about current happenings.

We are looking for the following:

Reviews of local exhibitions and events. Keep it short. 200 words. 
Feel free to send multiple 200 word reviews.

Articles with a critical stance, pertaining to a specific subject that 
could be a particular artist, cultural phenomena, or something else 
related to art and life in Vancouver. 1500 words MAX.

We also accept interviews, and poetry, so long as they are not 
promotional.

Please email your submissions, in .doc format, with your name, and 
the category to which you are submitting (short review or long 
article), to unitpitt.manager@gmail.com

Please also state in your email if you have also submitted your 
article anywhere else.

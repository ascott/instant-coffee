TO EMIT THOUGHT TIME OUGHT TO; works by Jordan Milner

Opening Reception: Thursday, March 28, 7-11 pm.
Show runs: March 29-April 4. Gallery opens by appointment.

Yactac Gallery is pleased to present "TO EMIT THOUGHT TIME 
OUGHT TO"; works by Jordan Milner.

"In this present work the reflexive process and material results merge 
as a body, an ensemble of organs individually performing amongst one 
another as transistors, yearning for an energized body. From the artist, 
a subtle transference of reformulated observations perform as a 
collective experiment; each piece functioning under its own system of 
qualities but still remaining in harmony with the whole.

The trans reflexive process used is about feeling and not knowing; by 
listening to ones imagination when it calls for attention even during the 
most focused or distracted moments of consciousness. Through the 
use of intuitive divergence a body of work is manifested to represent a 
cohesive whole in itself; an open body consisting of a variation of 
individualized forms, each gifted with a separate but crucial and 
transient function. Coexisting wholly to emit what fundamental 
compositions it exists as a part of and escaping its own dualistic state 
through the lens of the viewer.

Through time, intuition becomes a vessel in which all clarity is gained 
through chaos, from the conversion of the purity of your imagination 
into the physical complexities of reality and back again."

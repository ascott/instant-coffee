Open Call - only 2 weeks left

eva International
12 April - 6 July 2014
Curated by Bassam El Baroni

Open Call for Proposals
Closes: 5pm Monday 2 September 2013

The open call for proposals for eva International 2014, Ireland's Biennial, 
curated by Bassam El Baroni, closes in 2 weeks: 5pm Monday 2 September 
2013.

Applications are invited from individual artists or groups, working in any 
medium and can be for the presentation of existing works or production of new 
projects. Artists can make up to 2 proposals and there is a submission fee of 
€20 for 1 proposal or €30 for 2 proposals.

To begin the online submission process please:

Register here

If you've already registered and would like to complete or continue your 
submission please:

Log in here

Please see www.eva.ie for further information or contact opencall2014@eva.ie 
with any questions.

Many thanks to everyone who's already submitted. If we have any questions 
about your proposals we will contact you directly. eva International will 
contact all artists after the deadline to confirm receipt of submissions and all 
artists will be informed about selection before 31 October 2013.

The Vancouver Art Walk, a vibrant initiative designed to showcase the remarkable
galleries and art spaces in Vancouvers Strathcona, Chinatown and Railtown
neighbourhoods where there are 15 galleries within walking distance of each other.
Every first Saturday, we try to bring attention to the events and exhibitions hosted at
these galleries.

For example, on Saturday, April 5th, Fazakas Gallery (659 e Hastings) is hosting
Three Piece Dinner, featuring work by Marcy Friesen, Jake Kimble and Michelle
Sound. At the heart of this exhibition is the playful, yet profound, use of KFC"a
fast-food icon often tied to a longstanding stereotype of "Indigenous people like fried
chicken." The joke becomes the catalyst for a deeper exploration of the
connections between culture, food, and identity.

Gallery 881 (881 e Hastings) is hosting an opening reception from 2-5 pm for Expect
Delays by Barbara Strigel, which is part of the Capture Photography Festival.

At THIS Gallery (268 Keefer) Jess MacCormack presents, Look What the Clown
Dragged In an installation of AI generated images and videos, mixed with cardboard
and text that form unhinged narratives about self and Other.

Paneficio Gallery (800 Keefer) is open Saturday, April 5 from noon to 5 pm with
Christopher Aitkens exhibition of abstract painting Architectures of Colour.

Canton Sardine continues their Capture exhibition of large scale photographs by
Berlin-based Chinese trans media artist Miao Xiaochun. The exhibition features A
Visit from the Past to the Present, a series of photographs created by artist Miao
Xiaochun between 1999 and 2004 where the artist placed a sculpture he made of an
ancient Chinese figure into various modern urban settings around the world to create
a series of cinematic-like still images.

You can download a map of the Vancouver Art Walk showing all of the participating
locations at vancouverartwalk.com.

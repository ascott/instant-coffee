Sleep 1, 2, 3, 4

Eli Bornowsky

Artist Talk: September 29, 7pm

September 10 – October 17, 2009

Each year, Malaspina awards the Print Research Residency to a local artist who has no printing 
experience. Eli Bornowsky was a recipient of the award in 2008. Over the past 6 months he has 
worked closely with Andrea Pinheiro to learn multi-plate etching, relief, and drypoint. Sleep 1, 2, 
3, 4 presents some of the work developed of his residency.

Sleep 1, 2, 3, 4 continues Bornowsky's interest in the formal aspects of colour and non-
representational imagery seen in his painting practice. Previous works have used 1, 2, and 3 as 
variables that inform the structure or logic that guides his intuitive process of non-representation 
image generation. With this body of work, Bornowsky increases the complexity of his approach 
through the addition of ‘4’.

The acts of sleeping and dreaming informed this series of prints as Bornowsky sees a correlation 
between his visualization process and the process of printmaking. Both are comprised of 
interrelated acts that build upon each other, each visitation augmenting what has come before 
until finally an image is created, or revealed. Inherent in printmaking is the act of the reveal, for it 
is only after the sheet rolls through the press and the paper lifted off the plate that the work is 
seen. This property, when coupled with the ability to visualize a completed work in reverse 
through numerous steps, another fundamental printmaking skill, highlights the additive and 
WYSIWIG (what you see is what you get) nature of painting and drawing, Bornowsky’s usual 
manner of intutive working. This work has come out of the gap that exists between intuition, 
process, and systems.

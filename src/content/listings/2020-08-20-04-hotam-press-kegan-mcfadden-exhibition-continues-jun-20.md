Kegan McFadden
Partners in Crime
June 20 to August 22, 2020

Partners in Crime, composed of over twenty printed matter and 
multiples by Kegan McFadden, is built around the artists involvement 
with the mail art. An evolving body of work that references 
interconnected nature of art making through text and reader, distilling 
the relationship of you and me as a means of identifying/reinforcing 
the importance of the recipient in mail art/art making. This
showcase is 
a survey of Kegan's work published over the past fifteen years. 

Based in Victoria, Kegan McFadden is a graduate of the Critical and 
Curatorial Studies of UBC. As an artist, writer and curator, his output 
has been varied but often relies on interpersonal connections, usually 
through a queer lens, and incorporates a mixture of text and image as 
well as modest means of production.

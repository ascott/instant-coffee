September 13 - October 18, 2025

CityScape Community ArtSpace | 335 Lonsdale Avenue, North Vancouver

Exhibition Opening: Friday, September 12, 2025 | 6pm - 8pm

This is the 2025 iteration of North Van Arts' biannual Pushing Boundaries program.
Since its debut in 2011, each iteration of Pushing Boundaries has featured an
Indigenous curator who designs an exhibition for CityScape Gallery. This year, artist
Jake Kimble has curated The Body is a Present for the program.

Featuring the work of Jake Kimble, Maria-Margaretta Cabana Boucher, Cheyenne
Rain LeGrande, and Vance Wright, the exhibition presents a metaphysical body, one
that celebrates the importance of Indigenous joy, humour, and care. Challenging
colonial narratives that have long misrepresented or commodified Indigenous
adornment, the artists incorporate modern materials and experimental processes,
blurring the lines between tradition and innovation.

Join Vancouver Art Gallery for a reception in celebration of the first Canadian exhibition 
by Chinese contemporary art collective Polit-Sheer-Form Office (PSFO) Fitness for All 
on view until March 31, 2019 at Offsite (1100 West Georgia Street). Comprising of artists 
Hong Hao, Xiao Yu, Song Dong, Liu Jianhua, and curator/critic Leng Lin, PFSOs multi-
disciplinary projects address the idea of we in a me world. Their five-piece set of 
exercise equipment specially designed for Vancouver appropriates the publicly funded 
workout equipment found in city squares and residential compounds throughout China. 
Treating the work as sculpture for the betterment of visitors and passersby, PSFO 
explores the possibilities of shared experience through group activity.

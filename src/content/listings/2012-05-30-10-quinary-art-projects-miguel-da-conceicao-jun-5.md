Quinary Art Projects by Wesley Yuen presents an one night exhibition 
with Vancouver artist, Miguel da Conceicao "Short Mirage, 2012" 
week 2 of our 14 week popup space @ 1387 Richard Street on 
Tuesday June 5, 6-9 pm.

" the exhibition resumes the deliberation of elements specific to the 
grammatical construction of space in architecture......The elements of 
light, volume and enclosure first introduced in String Cave, 2008 for the 
exhibition, In the diagram below, line AB and line GH intersect at point D 
(Or Gallery). It proposed the possibility of a space without corners and 
edges, volume without mass. Short Mirage enters this deliberation with 
emphasis on surface, sunlight and duration. A temporal performance of 
sunlight reflecting a still life of glass and mirror. "

Miguel da Conceicao received his BFA (2001) from Emily Carr Institute, 
and has exhibited in Vancouver at the Or Gallery (solo, 2008), Helen 
Pitt Gallery 
(solo, 2002), State Gallery, Charles Scott Gallery, Contemporary Art 
Gallery, Western Front, Belkin Satellite and Atelier Gallery, Blackwood 
Gallery, (University of Toronto), Mississauga, and internationally at the 
Liu Haisu Museum, Shanghai, Canadian Embassy Gallery, Washington 
DC, London Biennale Pollinations, Berlin, and the London Bienalle, 
London UK.

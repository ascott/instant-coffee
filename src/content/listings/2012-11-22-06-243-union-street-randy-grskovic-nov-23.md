"There is an end to everything, to good things as well." - Chaucer 1374

Friday November 23rd, Randy Grskovic: The Truth is Truth isn't

For the past 3 months I have had the privilege of working with some of 
Vancouver’s most talented artists. We’ve held 10 exhibitions, each 
completely unique, and have had over 1,000 visitors come and share the 
experience. Unfortunately, our time in the space on Union Street is coming to 
an end. But we’d like to share some more work with you before we go.

This coming Friday, November 23rd, I will join the ongoing conversation at 
GOOD LUCK by presenting some of my own artwork. The gallery was 
intended to be a place for opportunity to underexposed and unrepresented 
artists. Quite frankly, we joined together to show our own work because no 
one else would. I am so proud of how each artist we’ve shown has risen to the 
occasion to produce some of the best art Vancouver has ever seen. I 
personally have been inspired by each of the artists the gallery has presented, 
and I am humbled to show my artwork along side them.

This week, the entire space at 243 Union St. will be open. We will take down 
the center wall and the gallery will expand to twice its current size. GOOD 
LUCK will end like a supernova; expand and go out with a bang!

Randy Grskovic

"The work of Grskovic tends toward the point of ridiculous. He takes viewers 
beyond irony to confront them with the improbability of representation, forcing 
them to consider the fundamental assumptions underlying utopian ideals.

By problematizing representation and making its process transparent, 
Grskovic sets up a dialogue between what we consider to be real and our 
propensity to idealize that reality."

-Stephen Foster

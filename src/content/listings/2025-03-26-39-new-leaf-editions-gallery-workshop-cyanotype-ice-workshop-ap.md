Cyanotype & Ice Workshop
April 12, Saturday noon to 4:30 PM

Location
New Leaf Editions Gallery
3-1244 Cartwright St, Vancouver, BC V6H 3R7
Workshop Description

Cyanotype, a camera-less alternative photographic process, utilizes photosensitive
chemical solutions & modifiers, such as vinegar, that are applied to, but are not
limited to paper and fabric.

Valerie Durants exhibition, Influx: Waiting on the Water is showing at the Gallery
from March 27 to May 2. The body of work melds botanical ice photographs &
sculptures with camera-less cyanotype images. Natural elements from the
sculptural installation will be utilized in the workshop to create images as a
continuation of a lens-based process. She will demonstrate the darkroom
photographic processes of preparing paper and fabric with photosensitive chemicals.
You will compose images using botanicals, ice and found objects. The
compositions will be exposed to light using a UV device and the power of the sun .
Each participant will have the opportunity to complete one or more images and
leave with the knowledge to create their own cyanotype & ice prints at home.

All materials will be provided, however, you are welcome to bring additional
botanicals and small objects which could be used to resist light in your compositions.
It is recommended that you wear
casual clothes and/or an apro. as the chemicals
could stain your clothing.

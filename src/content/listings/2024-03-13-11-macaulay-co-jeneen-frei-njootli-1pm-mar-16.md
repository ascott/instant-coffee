JENEEN FREI NJOOTLI
Throats tight with medicine, teeth that cannot chew, mountains and rivers in the gut.

Opening Saturday March 16th 1-4pm
Macaulay + Co, Vancouver

This exhibition consists of images of the artists body in their homelands printed onto
hot rolled steel using rust and epoxy. Alongside these works are resin coated
belongings worn down by relationships to land, labour and liberation. Dagoo
(ptarmigan bird) are meditations throughout.

1 pm welcome by Salia Joseph
1:30 pm performance Rising Moon Singers
3 pm artist talk
Please join us!
We respectfully acknowledge that we live and work on unceded, traditional and
ancestral xmky"m
(Musqueam), Swxw7mesh xwumixw (Squamish), and
sl"ilw"ta" (Tsleil-Waututh) territories.

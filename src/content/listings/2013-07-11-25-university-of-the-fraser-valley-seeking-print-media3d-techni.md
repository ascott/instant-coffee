Staff Opening

Posting 2013.71
Position Print Media/3D Technician
Department Visual Arts
Status Staff, 66.67%, 9 months per year (September to May) (equivalent to
50% FT)
Start Date September 2013
Salary Scale $21.57 - $26.04 per hour (Staff pay group 6)
Closing Date August 23, 2013

The University of the Fraser Valley is the school of choice for 16,000
students annually pursuing more than 100 programs, including two masters
degrees, 15 bachelors degrees, majors, minors and extended minors in more
than 30 subject areas, and more than a dozen trades and technology
programs. We are committed to teaching excellence. Our foundation for
academic excellence is small class sizes, support for active research and
scholarship, and a friendly supportive environment open to collaboration
and innovation.

Duties & Responsibilities:
The Print Media/3D Technician is responsible for providing in-class
technical support to the Print Media instructor and for managing the
day-to-day operations of the Print Media facilities and equipment. Other
responsibilities include ensuring that a safe and healthy standard of
housekeeping is maintained by monitoring student practices and habits; by
directing work-study students and by personally assuming cleaning and
organizing tasks. The Print Media Technician will also prepare and assist
with development of information resources for students in 2-D areas; assist
students and faculty with exhibit setup and removal; assist with research
into new processes and materials; monitor inventory supply levels, and
prepare chemistry solutions for curriculum use.

The Technician is responsible for supervising Open Studio sessions in the
Sculpture studio on designated days and for providing support and technical
guidance to students from all disciplines while they are working in the
Sculpture area. The Technician will also work collaboratively with the
Senior Technician to ensure that any of the maintenance, safety or student
support requirements that might present themselves department wide are met.

Qualifications:
BFA from a recognized institution or equivalent. Minimum one year related
experience. The successful candidate should be an experienced print maker
and be familiar with traditional Print Media processes including relief
printing, copper and zinc etching, screen printing, intaglio and
photo-intaglio. Understanding of Mac OSX, Adobe Photoshop, large format
digital printing, print media traditional and digital production techniques
including digital-transparency output is essential. Must have experience
with most commonly used stationary and portable wood power tools and be
able to demonstrate their safe and effective use. Experience with mould
fabrication practices and theory is also essential. Some experience with
metal fabrication processes is desirable.

Shortlisted applicants may be required to undergo a criminal record check.

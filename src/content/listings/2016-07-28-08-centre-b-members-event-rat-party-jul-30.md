MEMBERS EVENT: RAT PARTY
Saturday, July 30
7pm til late
Centre B (1981 Main St.)

Join us in welcoming our Vancouver-Seoul exchange artists-in-
residence, Moon Hayn and Jeong Chanmin! This August, Chanmin 
and Hayn will be occupying Centre A's gallery space between July 
30 and August 5 as artists-in-residence as part of Centre A and 
RAT school of ART's annual exchange and residency program. The 
gallery will be open to the public during Chanmin and Hayn's week-
long residency, culminating in an exhibition opening August 5th.

Saturday, July 30, come by our satellite space, Centre B (1981 
Main St) from 7pm till late for a member's only welcome party to 
meet Hayn and Chanmin, see some friends, dance, and have a 
beer. Kiyoshi Whitley will be providing a selection of music and 
sound, with beer provided by Red Truck Brewery. Space is limited, 
so please RSVP at dev@centrea.org

If you haven't renewed or aren't a member, you'll be able to pick 
one up at the party or click here to purchase online ; $20 regular, 
$10 students. Some membership perks include a vote at our 2016 
AGM, access to special member-only events (such as this!), 
discounted rates on ticketed programming and publications, 
borrowing rights to a selection of our special collections library, and 
more!

A Formal Idea

A Formal Idea brings together a collection of emerging Vancouver based
artists within YAC TAC's unique exhibition space, a modernist home circa
1977. The exhibition considers the living space and attempts to
aesthetically compliment the existing architecture.

Though each artist has utilized minimal, repetitive and highly formal
aesthetics they diverge drastically in conceptual nature. The works explore
topics such as objectivity, substance, meditation, material politics and
cultural mergers.

Works by:

Patrick Cruz
Kyle Duske
Michelle Fu
Chia-Chen Jane Hsu
Heather Pennell

YacTac Gallery Space is located at 7206 Ontario Street. Opening is on
Saturday March.19th, 2011 @ 8pm.

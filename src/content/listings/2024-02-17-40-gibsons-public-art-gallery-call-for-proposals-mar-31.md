GPAG is now accepting proposals to exhibit in 2025! Apply for the Main Gallery, Eve
Smart Gallery, or both. We accept submissions from both emerging and established
artists working in all mediums and styles. The
submission deadline is March 31st,
2024.

The Gibsons Public Art Gallery is a professional exhibition space for contemporary
artists located just a short 40 min. ferry ride from Vancouver, BC. We are a non-profit
public arts organization situated on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of
the Skwxw7mesh (Squamish Nation). Much of our work takes place in the
traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the shshlh (Sechelt Nation). GPAG's
vision for the past 20 years has been to foster and promote artistic creativity and
learning, while serving as a cultural centre that unites art and community.

On our website under Submissions you can find our exhibition policy, submission
guidelines and forms, and a sample artist-gallery contract. Before submitting your
proposal please check the submission guidelines and requirements carefully.
Submissions should be made digitally via Google Drive, DropBox, or USB thumb
drive. Do not email us your submission material. Artists will
typically be notified of
acceptance within three months of the submission deadline (March 31st). We look
forward to seeing your work!

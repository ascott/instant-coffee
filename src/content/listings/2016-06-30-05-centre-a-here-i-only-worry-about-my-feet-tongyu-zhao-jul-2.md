Foot Note On A Few Things (day 30)
Tongyu Zhao
July 2, 7:30pm
for Here I only worry about my feet, your feet, everybody's feet
Centre A

Foot Note On A Few Things (day 30) happens on the 30th day of its month
long participatory object accumulation from the neighborhood of Chinatown,
Vancouver. You might wonder what will happen, the artist is also wondering
the same as you. She hopes that this can turn into something chaotic but
simple, personal but communicative. Through these known and unknown
objects, with the travels that both you and the artist have made in order
for this to happen, the artist expects to counter our collective language
of understanding history and memory, but most importantly in awareness of
where the feet stand: the very present moment as creation, knowledge and
life happens. 30 notes acting as 30 blank scores will be performed
throughout the evening. For each note the artist will create an intuitive
active installation of play and display with the objects. As of now, the
artist still have no idea of what's there, what's not there and what's more
to come.

--

TONGYU ZHAO is an artist and performer. Born and raised in Hubei, China,
Zhao has also lived in Singapore, Shanghai and Beijing. Zhao is currently
based in Chicago, but mostly based in her guts and wherever she locates,
Zhao thinks about material encounters and lived experiences. In an often
chaotic expression, she intends to slow down experience and expand life's
weirdness. Zhao has performed at Rational Park, Links Hall and Mana
Contemporary and has showcased works at various venues across Chicago.
She's a recent recipient of the LeRoy Neiman Fellowship from Ox-Bow School
of Art, MI. She received her BFA from the School of the Art Institute of
Chicago in 2016. Zhao is also in a collaboration duo with artist Alyx Harch
as Ha Ha Gallery.

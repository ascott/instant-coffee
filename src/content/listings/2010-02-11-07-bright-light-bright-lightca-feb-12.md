Bright Light is one of a wide variety of public art projects that have been
commissioned to mark the occasion of the Vancouver 2010 Olympics and
Paralympic Games. In the case of Bright Light, the intention is to animate
the newly completed Carrall Street Greenway with projects that explore new

Bright Light sees public space as a political process rather than fixed
place. A big question during the Olympics: who controls public space? And
what public are we talking about? There are many different publics in the
Downtown Eastside, including visitors to Vancouver, homeless people,
business owners and a large concentration of artists, perhaps more than in
any other neighbourhood in Canada.

Take a walk on the Downtown Eastside. You will discover a vibrant, dynamic
and historic city centre, home to diverse communities, interesting
architecture, and lots of art. Let Bright Light be your guide.

Bright Light is commissioned by the City of Vancouver through its Olympic
and Paralympic Public Art Program.

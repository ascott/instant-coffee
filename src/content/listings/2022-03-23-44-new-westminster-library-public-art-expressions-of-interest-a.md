The City of New Westminster invites artists to submit expressions of interest for a
public art opportunity at the recently upgraded New Westminster Public Library, Main
Branch. This improved public facility seeks to include a public art project by a
professional artist or artist team to create an artwork in any medium with a maximum
total budget of $50,000, plus GST.

The artist selection is a one stage process and will be undertaken by a Selection Panel
comprised of artists, arts or design professionals and community members with
representation from the New Westminster
Library Board and a Library Youth Advisor.

The Selection Panel will review all eligible Expressions of Interest submissions and
three to five candidates will be shortlisted for interviews with the goal of selecting one
artist for the project. The selection process is based on qualifications and an interview
only.

The selected artist will work with the City, the Library and stakeholders to develop a
concept responding to the site and context of the Library.

Submissions to this call are due Friday, April 29, 2022 (PST)

To learn more about this opportunity, review the full Call to Artists here:
www.newwestcity.ca/artservicesopportunities

Contact Quyen Hoang, Public & Community Art Coordinator qhoang@newwestcity.ca
or at 604.636.3553 for questions.

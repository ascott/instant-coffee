The Vancouver Art Gallery and Emily Carr University of Art + Design present Publishing 
Performance

Saturday, October 20 at 1 PM at ECUAD

Join us for a lecture by David Senior, Head of Library and Archives at San Francisco 
Museum of Modern Art, as he discusses historical examples of artists' publications that 
reflect experiments with performance and the various ways that these published works 
were used to communicate and distribute new art practices.

Presented as part of the Vancouver Art Book Fair.

ECUAD
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

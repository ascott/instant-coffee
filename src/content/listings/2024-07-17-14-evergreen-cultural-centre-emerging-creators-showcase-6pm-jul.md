Emerging Creators Incubator Pop-Up Showcase
Thursday, July 25, 6:00pm-8:30 p.m.
Evergreen Cultural Centre
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam

This one-night-only event features work-in-progress by six emerging multidisciplinary
artists participating in Evergreen
Cultural Centres (ECC) Emerging Creators
Incubator, featuring Chisom Akomas, Izzy Bull Bear, Yasmine Haiboub, Julian
Legere, Sybil Xiaoyun Shen and Kelsey Steeves.

The Emerging Creators Incubator is designed to support emerging artists from all
artistic disciplines living on the Coast Salish territories colonially known as the Tri-
Cities and Lower Mainland. The participating artists
came together from May to July 2024 to share knowledge, form community and
dedicate time to their creative practices. Over three months, the artists received
mentorship from guest mentors Derek Chan , Krystal Paraboo and ECC staff who
fostered the development of their work, as well as a month-long residency during
which participants had access to the studios and facilities at ECC. The program
concludes with this Pop- Up Showcase. Bridging visual and performing arts, the
artists explore identity, empowerment and social responsibility through their research
and creative process.

This program is
co-facilitated by Anna Luth, assistant curator and Tanya Mathivanan,
performing arts assistant.Light refreshments will be served. Artist spotlights will
begin at 7p.m. This event is free and wheelchair accessible.

Follow the link to learn more about the artists:
https://evergreenculturalcentre.ca/exhibit/emerging-creators-incubator/

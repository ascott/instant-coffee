In partnership with Artspeak, Cineworks presents:

"The accursed share": Screenings and Artists' Talk
Curated by Marina Roy

Saturday April 23; Doors: 2.00pm
Cineworks Studio, 1131 Howe Street (lane entrance)
FREE

Featuring moving-image works by:
Aleesa Cohene
Amanda Dawn Christie
Deborah Edmeades

This screening and discussion provides context and further exploration for
"The accursed share", curated by Marina Roy, currently in exhibition at
Artspeak until May 21. 

'At the heart of any economy of surplus is a communal release in material
expenditure. Works by Aleesa Cohene, Derek Dunlop, and Deborah Edmeades are
brought together through intuition - only later did the idea of an
aesthetic "economy of the flesh" make itself felt. Of interest is the
vitality of inanimate matter - how, through processes of artistic
transformation, new affective forms come to resonate in us - an often
difficult, unrepresentable singularity embeds itself in thought. Brought
together, these works could even be said to point toward some manic concept
of redemption - under the guise of a tainted sacrificial offering, the
building of automata through melding old and new electromagnetic devices,
and unlocking obscure affects through colour research...'
Marina Roy

Artspeak is a non-profit artist run centre established in 1986. The mandate
of the gallery is to exhibit contemporary art and to encourage a dialogue
between visual art and writing. Artspeak actively contributes to cultural
communities through our commitment to artists producing challenging,
innovative work in diverse media, our affiliation with like-minded
organizations, and the public interest we generate in contemporary art.

www.artspeak.ca

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society (est. 1980) is an artist-run
production and exhibition centre that supports independent filmmakers and
media artists working in digital video, celluloid film media and in
relation to cinema in expansive ways. We engage our membership and the
broader community through initiatives and services that foster dialogue and
experimentation with the moving image.

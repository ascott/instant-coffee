Folio F: Services Working Group
Ed. Eric Golo Stone
English / German Edition

Fillip is pleased to announce the release of Services Working Group, a new publication
presenting complete transcripts from the ground-breaking working group on labour
relations and institutional governance in the arts organized in 1994 by Helmut Draxler
and Andrea Fraser at the Kunstraum of the University of Lneburg.

Held on January 22 and 23, 1994, the candid discussions between the organizers,
Kunstraum representatives, and invited
practitioners were video-recorded. Newly-
produced transcripts of this compelling recording are presented here in English and
German, documenting the entirety of the debut Services Working Group discussions.
These transcripts are supplemented by newly-digitized photograph documentation of
the conversations and a newly-commissioned post-script text by Draxler and Fraser
that reexamines Services in light of current discussions around the socioeconomic
conditions of art and its institutions.

Contributions by Judith Barry, Ute Meta Bauer, Jochen Becker, Ulrich Bischoff, Beatrice
von Bismarck, Iwona Blazwick, Susan Cahan, Michael Clegg, Stephan Dillemuth,
Helmut Draxler, Andrea Fraser, Rene Green, Martin Guttmann, Renate Lorenz,
Christian Philipp Mller, Fritz Rahmann, Eric Golo Stone, Fred Wilson, and Ulf
Wuggenig.

Services Working Group is edited by Eric Golo Stone. It is available for purchase for
$25 CAD, shipped internationally. Wholesale orders may be placed through Art
Metropole
(US/CA) and Motto Books (EU). The German translations of the transcripts
included in this volume were generously supported by Knstlerhaus Stuttgart and the
Kunstraum of Leuphana University Lneburg.

Stage to Page
A Poetry Development
Project, July 20, 2020 at 6 pm

Join us on Monday, July 20th, as an ensemble of curated poets have their poetry performed
and workshopped by a panel of industry professionals.

Local poets will participate in a live stream event, where a panel of publishers, authors,
writers, and performers can support their work, fine tune their editing, and provide insight to
all viewers on the editing and publishing process.

This is a free event. Please register online at [http://www.cacv.ca/stage-to-page/](http://www.cacv.ca/stage-to-page/)

RESPONSES TO THE OUTDOOR ART COLLECTION

Untitled II (The Shadow) 

Friday, February 8 at noon, rain or shine

The performance will take place at the artwork located on University Commons 
Plaza in front of the The Nest.

Friday, February 8 at noon, 2019
Responses to the Collection is an ongoing series that invites a broad range of guest 
contributors to select work from the Outdoor Art Collection as subjects for reflection 
and response. Exploring how the UBC campus can be a fruitful place for 
experimentation and learning, Temporary Collective have come together to perform 
through movement and rhythmic gestures, responses to Asiatic Head by Otto 
Fischer-Credo (1958/1977) and The Shadow by Esther Shalev-Gerz (2018). The 
collective of ten artists attempts to dialogue with these two very different artworks by 
generating an embodied engagement with site, materiality and history.

Temporary Collective evolved out of a performance course taught by Randy Lee 
Cutler in the spring of 2018 at Emily Carr University. Members of the collective 
include Tianna Barton, Fadwa Bouziane, Keely Bruce, Randy Lee Cutler, Matthew 
Dillon, Maya Gauvin, Mimi Gonzalez Guerra, Gillian Haigh, Caroline Lee and Brigitte 
Patenaude. 

Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery
1825 Main Mall, Vancouver, BC

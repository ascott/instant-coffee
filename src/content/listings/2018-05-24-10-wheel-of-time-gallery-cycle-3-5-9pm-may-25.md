Opening: Friday May 25th, at 5-9pm
Wheel of Time Gallery
2655 Renfrew Street, Vancouver, BC

The Wheel of Time Gallery is pleased to present a group exhibition: Cycle #3: "Why 
Did You Separate Me From The Earth with artist Nicolas Segura, Zachary 
Schroeder, Malou Uptmoor, Michael Undem, Lee Garlach, Grace Fournier, Matt 
Ariaratnam, Sydney Thorne and Joylyn Secunda. Curated by Raj Gill. The show will 
open Friday May 25th, at 5-9pm, with performances through out the evening, a final 
performance at 9:00pm and an artist talk-back at 8:00pm

The show will feature a variety of happenings"photography art, performance art, 
improvisational music, dance, projection, community art making, a take a home 
gardening and more...

The Wheel of Time Gallery is a unique place and time because of the importance to 
the -now-

The space is soon at one point in the future to be demolished, as will the other 
houses on the street. The impending destruction---Doom or Bloom? Cycles

We have in this moment in this time a temporary space to create a vision of an 
experience of what we want to share in the world. We have a space where streams 
of people walk by outside-- who we can engage with"people who normally would 
not engage with art or performance"but here there is collective story that surrounds 
this space that they can connect with. It means so much to be able to provide a 
space where artist can create and share and try out things in a context that can 
shape and inform our lives and the lives of our communities ---this is a conventional 
space used for unconventional purposes

and the wheel turns- 

The Why Did You Separate Me From The Earth" will run Friday May 25, 2018 at 
2655 Renfrew Street, Vancouver, BC. The show will run from 5:00 pm-9:00 pm. 
Opening reception and performance through out the evening with a final 
performance at 9:00pm. For additional information email at 
temporaryframes@gmail.com or visit www.temporaryframes.space and follow us on 
instagram: wot.gallery

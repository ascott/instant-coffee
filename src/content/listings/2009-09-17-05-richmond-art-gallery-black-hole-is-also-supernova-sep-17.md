Colleen Brown, Paul Kajander, Kara Uzelman
Title: Black Hole is Also Supernova
Curator: Vanessa Kwan

This exhibition will feature the work of Colleen Brown, Paul Kajander and Kara Uzelman. These 
artists will produce sculpture in a variety of media: video, photography, sculpture, found materials, 
and text. Their treatment of objects and thematics will be loosely oriented around the trajectory
that the title implies: a treatment of objects and cultural product that is both vacuum-like and 
explosive. The shakiness with which objects hold meaning in our world-- art and otherwise-- 
speaks to a kind of bankruptcy of signification (one thing refers to all things, everything's been 
done before, it's all relative, and so on), and this "black hole" will intersect with another kind of 
approach: that meaning, in this vacuum, might explode into generative new constellations, 
narratives and associations. The work in the exhibition will be treated as experiments in form, 
concept and embodiment.

Elizabeth Russell
Title: Migration/ Immigrant Stories

Elizabeth Russell's work explores the early transitional stages experienced by migrants and 
immigrants through the symbolism of everyday objects. Drawings, photographs and paintings 
portray the complexities of a new beginnings in Canada. The artist used personal recollections 
and oral stories from Vancouver and Richmond-based immigrants as research for the exhibition.

Elizabeth Russell is a conceptual, interdisciplinary artist whose practice involves the creation of 
site-specific works for alternative spaces and galleries. Russell is a sessional art instructor at North 
Island College in Courtenay, where she resides. The artist received her BFA in Painting from 
Emily Carr University of Art and Design, Vancouver, BC (1995) and her Masters in Combined 
Media from Chelsea College of Art and Design, London, UK (1997). Russell's art has been 
exhibited in a variety of unusual spaces from a London police station (2000) to a barge on the 
river Thames, London, UK (Drift, 2008) and in group shows at galleries such as the Evergreen 
Cultural Centre (Coquitlam, BC), the Art Gallery of The South Okanagan (Penticton, BC), Comox 
Valley Art Gallery (Courtenay, BC), word.image.sound.House (London, UK) and Galerie Herold, 
(Bremen, Germany).

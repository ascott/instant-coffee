SYMPOSIUM: CONCRETE POETRY
1 - 5 pm
Friday, February 10, 2012

All welcome. Free admission.

Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery
1825 Main Mall, University of British Columbia, Vancouver
www.belkin.ubc.ca

The Belkin Art Gallery is pleased to present, "Concrete Poetry," a 
symposium, in conjunction with our current exhibition, "Letters: Michael 
Morris and Concrete Poetry." The Concrete Poetry movement was 
perhaps the first global art movement, emerging in the Americas, 
Japan, and Europe in the 1950s with poetic experiments in form, and 
attention to new modes of communication. The concrete poem, at once 
a manifestation of language-based visual art and of poetry, is about 
words and concerned with their "verbivocovisual" nature (see the 
Brazilian group Noigandres' manifesto "Pilot Plan for Concrete Poetry"). 
The originality of Vancouver concrete and sound poets forged 
important links to the international movement.

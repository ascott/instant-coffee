Special 2-day-only event! Saturday and Sunday, August 5 and 6. A juried group
exhibition featuring select recipients of the Vancouver Foundation DTES Arts Grants.

Participating Artists:

Tallulah

Taylor Neal

Christy Frisken

James Diamond

Michael Honeyman

Victoria Marie

Natalia Elena Medellin Rabell

Rojina Farrokhnejad

Jin Wang

THIS Gallery will host a two day reception for the exhibition on Saturday and Sunday
August 5 and 6 at 485 Main Street, Vancouver.

Vancouver Foundation created the DTES Small Arts Grants Program, in
partnership
with the Carnegie Community Centre. This program is to support DTES Artists to take
their work to the next level. It is for Artists who have limited or no access to support,
such as funding, sponsorship, or regular arts and culture programs.

Vancouvers DTES is a hotbed of creativity. Many artists connected to this
neighbourhood lack the finances to advance their work. The DTES Small Arts Grants
Program is for DTES Artists who can apply for a financial award, attend workshops,
receive expert advice, and participate in a year end group show.

This juried exhibition features the best and the brightest of the works submitted.

Saturday and Sunday, August 5-6, noon to 5.

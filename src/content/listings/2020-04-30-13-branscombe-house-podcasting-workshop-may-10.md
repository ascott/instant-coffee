Branscombe House - Podcasting Workshop
Sunday, May 10, 2020
1:00pm - 2:30pm
Free online
workshop via Zoom
Facilitator: Artist-in-Residence Lou Sheppard and Pamela Hart

Join Branscombe House Artist-in-Residence Lou Sheppard and audio engineer Pamela
Hart for a free online podcasting workshop. Participants will meet on Zoom to talk about
how to develop a story and record audio files on phones and computers. This workshop
will focus on basic audio editing using free software. Participants will create a brief
"report" on some way their lives have been impacted by the current COVID-19
pandemic, which will then be compiled as an audio record of this strange time.

Technology requirements: Participants will need to have a way to record audio files (a
smartphone/tablet or laptop) and will need to have access to a computer or tablet that
they can use to edit their audio. This workshop will cover basic audio editing using
audacity (free download: [https://www.audacityteam.org/download/](https://www.audacityteam.org/download/)) or Garage Band. If
you are uncertain about your technology please get in touch with Lou prior
to the
workshop.

RSVP to lou@lousheppard.com (RSVPs are required to receive a Zoom meeting link)

n.b. Lou and Pamela plan to host a similar workshop in-person when they can, so if you
do not have access to technology for this online version please reach out to Lou to
register for the future in-person workshop.

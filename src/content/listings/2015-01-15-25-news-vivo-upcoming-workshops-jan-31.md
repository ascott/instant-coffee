Camera, Lights and Sound

January 31st, February 1st, 12-6pm. Total 12 hours. | $288 for VIVO
Members; $230 for Non-Members | Register for this event via Eventbrite.

This intensely hands-on workshop introduces students to the Blackmagic
Cinema Camera and the Sony HVR-Z5U high definition camera package (both
available to rent from VIVO following the workshop). Basic technical
aspects of camera operation, sound recording, and lighting principles are
taught through instructor demonstration and practical exercises. Our
knowledgeable instructors guide participants to gain skills best suited to
their own project needs.

What is taught?
Understanding the camera through learning the manual settings to help you
get the best shot.
You will learn about exposure, gain, shutterspeed and white balance through
a number of practical exercises.
The principals of three-point lighting and sound recording will be covered
and practiced, these help get you ready and confident to set up a shoot
under the skilled guidence of the instructors.

This workshop includes the necessary skills and practice required for
competancy when renting camera, lights and sound equipment from VIVO. On
completion of the workshop, participants can apply to rent equipment
through our Tech department. Check out VIVOâ€™s great value equipment rental
rates. (Beginner workshop)

Intro to Arduino

February 5th, 7-10 pm | $36 Non-Members ; $30 for VIVO Producer Membership
(Extended) Register for this event and read the course outline on Eventbrite.

Are you interested in playing around with Arduino but feel a bit
intimidated? This course is a 3-hour introduction to the technology: What
it is, how it works, and what you can do with it.

By the end of the class, you will know the basics of how to use your
Arduino so you can apply your knowledge to more advanced projects. This
class is designed for troubleshooting with an instructor who is available
to answer your questions. If you have questions about how to use your
device, bring them to this class.

** We have Arduinos available for use at VIVO and some for sale as well.
Please contact Emily at education@vivomediaarts.com if you would like to
use one of ours, or purchase one at VIVO.

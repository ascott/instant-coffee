FIREHALL ARTS CENTRE GALLERY
CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS
2013/2014 Season

The Firehall Gallery is a unique brick faced gallery space located in the lobby 
of the Firehall Arts Centre. The Centre is celebrating its 30th season as a 
producer and presenter of contemporary dance, theatre and multi disciplinary 
work. The Firehall has an active schedule of performances and festivals and 
the Lobby Gallery provides exposure to a diverse audience. Gallery hours are 
Wed. – Sat. 1:00 -5:00 p.m.

The Firehall is currently inviting proposals from artists for exhibitions for 
2013/2014. We are particularly interested in work that engages with 
contemporary issues of diversity and community. We welcome all 2-
dimensional work, but unfortunately we cannot exhibit video works at this 
times. Exhibitions will run approximately 6 weeks.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES:

* Submitted works must be no smaller that 8” x 10”
* Submit up to 15 numbered images, on CD, 300 dpi, with .jpg extension. 
List images as they appear on the CD. Must be Mac compatible.

PLEASE INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING:
* Brief exhibition Proposal
* CD of images
* List of images
* CV
* Artist’s Statement - 300 words max.
* Self addressed stamped envelope, if you wish to have your images 
returned. We cannot be responsible for materials without a SASE or adequate 
postage.

Please mail submission to:

Shout Out!
Call for submissions: Emerging Artists

In conjunction with the Firehall Arts Centre’s annual B.C. Buds Spring Arts 
Fair of emerging artists in May of 2013, the Firehall invites submissions to an 
exhibition of work by emerging visual artists. 

We are looking for work that pushes the envelope of our understanding of 
contemporary culture by artists who have finished formal training within the 
past 5 year. Works will be hung in the Lobby Gallery; an intimate space 
visited by festival and theatre audiences.

Due to space constraints works must be no larger than 121 x121cm. All 
works must be ready to hang from a rail system. We welcome work in any 2-
dimensional media. Unfortunately we are not able to exhibit video work.

Please submit electronically:
* 6 images with titles
* List of images, with medium, dimensions, price, 
* C.V.

To: firehall@firehallartscentre.ca

All submissions must be received by March 29, 2013.

Exhibition runs April 24 – June 8; opening reception, April 25.

WORKSHOP

On the last Saturday of every month, the WVAM Family Art Project invites families to
drop in to the museum for a free afternoon art workshop based on the topic of the
West Vancouver Art Museums current exhibition. All art supplies are provided. This
workshop is generously supported by the West Vancouver Foundation.

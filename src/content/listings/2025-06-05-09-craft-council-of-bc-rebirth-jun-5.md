"Rebirth" at the Craft Council of BC

Artist: Amy Li Chuan Chang

June 5-July 24, 2025

A pottery exhibition by Amy Li Chuan Chang from June 5-July 24th. "Rebirth" is
about a group of creatures and plants that settle down on another planet and start a
new life there. Amys creatures have adapted to the harsh environment with AI
technology and strange industrial outfits. The theme of Rebirth raises the question:
what is the meaning of rebirth? Is the creature an old one returning or a new one
arising?

1386 Cartwright St. Granville Island

(604) 687-7270

Gallery Hours: Tuesday-Sunday 10-5:30

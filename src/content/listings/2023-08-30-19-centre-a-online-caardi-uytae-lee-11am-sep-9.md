We are excited to announce our 8th speaker in the CAARDI: Counter Anti-Asian
Racism Digital Initiative series, Uytae Lee! Join us for a conversation with urban
planning video journalist Uytae Lee for his talk, Excluded By Design: How Racist City
Policies Shaped Vancouver.

Drawing from research and history, we will explore the influence of racism and
exclusion in creating Vancouver's housing policies and how we can begin to repair this
legacy today. Uytae Lee is the creative mind behind About Here, a YouTube channel
dedicated to covering the complex issues
surrounding our cities. In addition to the
channel, Uytae produces the CBC series Stories About Here.

Date: September 9, 2023
Time: 11 AM - 1 PM PDT
Location: Online (Zoom)

Sign-ups will be necessary, and will be available on Zoom. Stay tuned for more
information on our Instagram (@centre_a)!

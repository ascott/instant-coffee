Alixzandar Morle // The 20: A Site-Specific Poem and Walking Tour // Saturday April 
22, 2:30-3:30pm

For the third series of HAPPENSTANCE, Decoy Magazine's ongoing series of 
temporary artworks in Vancouver, Trinidadian artist and designer Alixzandar Morle will 
be presenting a new site-specific text piece called The 20.

The 20 is a poem based on Morle's experiences riding the 20 bus line. He will be 
fragmenting the poem and writing it in chalk on sandwich board signs outside 
participating businesses along a portion of the 20's route"from the Downtown 
Eastside to Commercial Drive. Join us this Saturday for a public walking tour where 
the artist will take us through the poem, line by line, while engaging a conversation 
that reflects on the landscapes and communities framed by the 20.

The tour begins at Gorilla Food (637 East Hastings) at 2:30pm on Sat April 22 and 
will last about an hour, ending at Pasture to Plate (1420 Commercial Drive)

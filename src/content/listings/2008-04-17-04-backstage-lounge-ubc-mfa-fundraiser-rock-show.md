On April 17, 2008, the Master of Fine Arts in Visual Art and the Master of Arts in Critical and Curatorial Studies students at the University of British Columbia are hosting a rock show. This concert is a fundraiser for their upcoming exhibition at the Organ Artist Run Centre and symposium at the Sichuan Fine Arts Institute in Chongqing, China.

Location:
Backstage Lounge
1585 Johnston Street
Vancouver, BC

Doors open at 9PM

Featuring:
-Call the City (www.myspace.com/callthecity)
-Sleep Holiday (www.myspace.com/sleepholiday)
-The Barcelona Chair (www.myspace.com/thebarcelonachair)

Tickets are $7 and will be available in advance through the MFA and CCST
students as well as at the door.

PoMoArts 25th Anniversary Exhibitions: Honouring the Past, Embracing the Future
and An Abundance of Caring, Ceramic Artists in Residence 1998-2023

As we pay homage to the past, we recognize the immense power of art to bridge gaps,
dismantle barriers, and forge connections. Over the past 25 years, the Port Moody Arts
Centre has become a vibrant hub where diverse voices intersect, fostering dialogue,
understanding, and appreciation for a myriad of cultures, experiences, and
perspectives. Our anniversary exhibitions "Honouring the Past, Embracing the Future"
and An Abundance of Caring; Ceramic Artists in Residence 1998-2023, embody this
spirit of inclusivity, inviting you to embark on a journey that transcends borders and
defies limitations.

Opening Reception July 27th 6-8pm for PoMoArts 25th Anniversary Exhibitions

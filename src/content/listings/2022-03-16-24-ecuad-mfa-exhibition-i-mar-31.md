Exhibition I

March 28 - April 3

Reception: March 31 from 6 - 9 PM

Multidisciplinary artist Jordan Baraniecki is exhibiting his multi-layer collages. He
creates gestural marks on Yupo paper with ink and isopropyl alcohol, meticulously cuts
them out and pieces them together.

Matty Flader is premiering his highly professional and expensive short film The Real
Alex Kazemi.

Found Material Sculptor, Kyla Gilbert will present assemblages of deconstructed
mundane objects (office furniture, household items etc.) combined with industrial
materials (steel, concrete, glass, wood).

Heidi Holmes sensorial installation begins with symptoms that develop within a body
and then exist outside of a body as material.

New Media Installation artist, Guillaume Saur is exhibiting an immersive three-
dimensional
animated movie that considers the ways in which hierarchical power
systems are embedded within virtual environments.

Lacey Jane Wilburns oil paintings of empty houses oscillate between gesture and
reality to create fragmented narratives of private space.

Printmaker, Patrick Wong is exhibiting multi-part silkscreen prints and experimental
printing processes that questions the perception of time, and the process of
decolonization and reclamation within the context of Hong Kong.

Exhibition II

April 11 - 19

Reception: April 14 from 6 - 9 PM

Siyi An, a graphic novel artist whose work is focused on discussions and exploration
around her relationship with her Chinese cultural background, her female body and
her female experience.

Stephanie Buer will be presenting a series of representational landscape drawings in
charcoal. Her drawings explore connection to place through delicate mark making as a
form of meditation.

Xinwei Che practices slipping from times commodification with
embodied material
gestures. Her film, Laying Ground, explores earth time and maintenance through the
cyclical processes of unfired clay.

New media and movement artist Gemma Crowe will be exhibiting an immersive sound
installation which explores the illusory potential and physical presence of sound
through embodied processes.

Tommy Li is an illustrator who works with portraiture and comic books to explore the
idea of the co-existence of the West and the East

Kaitlyn Roberts is an interdisciplinary artist absorbed in how art-making and art-viewing
can create an empathetic relationship to the vulnerability and isolation surrounding the
mental wellbeing of women. Through the use of photography, installation, and mixed
media, her practice-based study conveys certain aspects of her autobiographical
experiences.

Onikamow Tapwew (Gigi) has mostly been focusing on experimental sculptural works
of rawhide and plant pigment paintings. Gigi is an interdisciplinary artist interested in
themes
of decolonization, reclamation, anti-capitalism, and anti-industrialism from an
Indigenous Woodland Cree and Osage perspective.

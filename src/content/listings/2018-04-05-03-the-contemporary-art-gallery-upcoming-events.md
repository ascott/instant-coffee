CAG Family Day | Re-imagined Landscapes
Saturday, April 28, 12-3pm, free
Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson St

On the last Saturday of each month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition 
tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Responding to Kapwani Kiwangas 'Subduction Studies', use a projector to enlarge and 
trace found photographic slides, collaging these drawings with second hand maps to re-
imagine, re-make and collide landscapes.

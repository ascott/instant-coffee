Gwenyth Chao: fieldnotes ... after capitalist ruin

Location: Ranger Station Art Gallery (98 Rockwell Drive in Harrison Hot Springs, BC)

Opening Reception: September 3rd from 2-4 pm

Exhibit: September 2nd-October 1st

Description:
fieldnotes ... after capitalist ruin speculates on the bodies and habitats that
exist in ecosystems after capitalist ruin. Featuring Chao's biomaterial sculptures made
from food refuse alongside a recent collection of drawings, each fragment offers clues
for the becoming-with that is necessary to live on a damaged planet.

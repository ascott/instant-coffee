It's Paint Night with Ryan Sluggett at Mobil Art!
Drink and...Paint?
Sep. 13, 6:30 p.m. - 9:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada
It's a social paint night...but with a Mobil Art School twist. Join Los Angeles based 
Canadian artist Ryan Sluggett for an evening of drinks, painting and fun. Anyone can 
learn to paint! Working from a big to small approach, and learning to see shapes 
abstractly, participants will paint from a projected image that slowly shifts over time with 
surprising and satisfying results.
Suitable for all levels and beginners are welcome! Price includes basic materials but you 
can also bring your own acrylics.
Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our FAQ.
Cash Bar, 19+ only, No Minors, No Outside Drinks.

Hyphenated Sites presents four solo exhibitions by artists Caitlin
Chaisson, Lily Mead Martin, Joseph O'Brien and Lianne Zannier.
Hyphenated Sites is a newly founded short-term artist-run space
located inside a shipping container underneath a defunct dockside
crane on Granville Island. Over the course of one month, the four
artists will take turns rotating through exhibitions. The collective project
aims to encourage the production of new spatial attitudes and
alternative forms of exchange. The artists make use of the site in order
to challenge established exhibition and institutional practices, whilst
questioning the role of the container as both a tenant of industry and a
prop of the art world. Through animation, drawing, installation,
lightworks, and sculpture, the artists will position a contemplative
context from which to haul, navigate, question and trouble the
boundaries of bodies and the boundaries of space.

Please accept this invitation to the closing reception of Rereading Room: the Vancouver 
Women's Bookstore. We invite you to take this opportunity to come and read the 
project's 75 books, documents, and periodicals before they are archived into 221A's 
permanent library collection. 

In the past two months, 221A was pleased to welcome over 300 guests to Rereading 
Room's seven events. This project would not have been complete without the artists, 
writers, and readers who brought life to our re-staging. And we are of course most 
grateful for the example and support of Vancouver Women's Bookstore's members and 
community. So we'd like to thank you all one more time on Saturday, January 14th. 
Beginning at noon, we'll be serving refreshments and putting on a few Ferron vinyls lent 
to us from a friend of a friend. Come stay a while.

See you soon,
Alexandra and Vincent

Open hours and Closing Reception
On Saturday, January 14, visit 221A between 12 - 4pm to read and chat over 
refreshments; stay from 4pm, onwards for a celebration with members and friends of 
Vancouver Women's Bookstore.

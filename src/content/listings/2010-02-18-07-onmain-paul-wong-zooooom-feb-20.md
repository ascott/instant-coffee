PRESS RELEASE

PAUL WONG PROJECTS NEWS Feb.10th, 2010

OLYMPIC PROJECTS HAPPENING NOW

Saturday Feb. 20th 5pm to 10pm

OFFICAL LAUNCH of 5.2 ZOOOOOM

The second in a series of 5 weekly events, ZOOOOOM is a mobile audio-visual
journey. At press time the exact route through the urban core is being
determined. Seating is limited and participants are advised to pre-book
on-line or arrive early with time to purchase a seat for 5, 6, 7, 8, or 9pm
departure times. Visit www.5.paulwongprojects.com for details.

From 5pm to 10pm passengers will ZOOOOOM through Vancouver on a magical
mystery tour. Participants will experience distorted and altered space
created by real and edited time, mashing fiction/non-fiction and abstract
sources.

On-the-Bus Playlist:

Venenzia, Storm, Snakes On A Bus, Eat, Go Greyhound, Set Fires, 8th Avenue,
Car Crash, Round Trip by Paul Wong, Rails by Brian Kent Gotro, Dovetale by
Tony Pantages/Paul Wong.

Passengers can relax in comfort before and after each round-trip at the
ZOOOOOM lounge, located at Lifetime Space, 68 west 5th Ave., Vancouver, BC

Tickets available on-line, $10 at the door.

The Look of Team 5:

Beanie+Scarf and signed limited edition Umbrella are available at:
[http://paulwongprojects.bigcartel.com/](http://paulwongprojects.bigcartel.com/)

Lifetime Olympic Pop Up Shop, 354 Water Street, Vancouver, BC.

‘5’ is commissioned by The City of Vancouver through its Olympic and
Paralympic Public Art Program, as part of Mapping and Marking
Artist-Initiated Projects for Vancouver 2010.

CALL/2019 Bonnie McComb Kreye Studio Residency, Victoria, BC/Nov 1

The Bonnie McComb Kreye Studio Residency, which originated in 2016 in Victoria , BC, is a 
partnership of the Kreye family and Vancouver Island School of Art. Artists have the use of 
a private 30 sq. m. garden studio for a residency of 2 months (studio only) or 1 month 
(studio with accommodation). The residency is open to artists working in all media. 
Opportunities are available for artists to
give talks and workshops at VISA and works will be included in a January 2019 exhibition. 
As well, The Ministry of Casual Living will offer possibilities for exhibition of work.

2019 Residencies Dates (Deadline: Nov 1, 2018)
January - February (studio)
March - April (studio)
May (studio and accommodation)
June (studio and accommodation)
Sept - Oct (studio)
Nov - Dec (studio)

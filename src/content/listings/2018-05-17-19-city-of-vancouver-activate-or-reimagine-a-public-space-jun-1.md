You are invited to apply for up to $5,000 to fund a pop-up project to
activate and reimagine a public space.

Were looking for creative ways to activate public spaces, get people
outside, foster social connections, build community, and make the city more
fun and inclusive. Projects should be:

Interesting, fun, and engaging
Welcoming and inclusive
Easy to pop-up and take down
Deliverable within the budget and timeframe
This year were focusing on 3 locations:
Robson Square (800 Robson and the Vancouver Art Gallery North Plaza)
Arbutus Greenway (four locations)
Helena Gutteridge Plaza

Other locations, such as an existing plaza, sidewalk, parking space,
street, or laneway may be considered. Projects go live between July and
December 2018.

To apply, and for further details, please visit vancouver.ca/viva.
Applications are due by midnight on Friday June 1, 2018.

Submissions will be reviewed by the VIVA team, and successful applicants
will be notified in mid-June.

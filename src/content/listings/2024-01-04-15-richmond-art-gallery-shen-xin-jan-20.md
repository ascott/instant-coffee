Shen Xin - but this is the language we met in;

January 20 - March 31, 2024

Richmond Art Gallery is proud to present Chinese artist Shen Xins haunting debut
exhibition in Canada. At its heart is a poetic
new work called but this is the language
we met in, the first film in a forthcoming series with the overarching title of Grounds
of Coherence. With this project accompanied by four small paintings, the artist
deepens their ongoing engagement with what they describe as ways of coming to
knowing, and the ecosystems of languages.

Xin sees language as encompassing the entire body including the voice, the gaze,
gestures, facial expressions, laughter, and even dance. The video highlights how
language has evolved over time: while some aspects of it have changed, others
have remained the same, emphasizing the advantages and drawbacks of modern
communication.

The Helen Pitt Gallery is now accepting applications for a part-time
Administrative Coordinator.

The Administrative Coordinator will report to the Board of Directors, and
will work in cooperation with the Board, Resident Curators, and volunteers
in the operations and management of the Gallery. This part-time, term
position will include specific tasks relating to communications and
financial management. This position is 16 hours per week, with the
expectation that hours will fluctuate depending on work load.

Applicants should have a thorough knowledge of the non-profit sector and be
an active participant in the arts community. They should have demonstrated
experience in administration, including financial management; previous
fundraising and grant-writing experience is necessary. Strong oral and
written communication skills, computer literacy, and organization are all
essential to this position. A relevant undergraduate degree is mandatory; a
master's degree will be considered an asset.

The Helen Pitt Gallery is a non-profit artist run center dedicated to the
promotion of experimental contemporary art that addresses social,
political, cultural, and critical issues outside of the mainstream
commercial art practice. Emerging artists, critics and curators continue to
find opportunities and support at the Helen Pitt Gallery. The gallery is
committed to showcasing challenging works from local, national and
international artists in all media. The gallery provides administrative
support to artists and curators in the design and implementation of
exhibitions.

Applicants should submit a one-page letter of interest, addressing their
vision for art-run centres in the current funding climate. Please also
include a current CV (no more than three pages) and a list of references
with current contact information.

Terms: Part-time contract (16 hours per week)

Start Date: February - April 2010; possibility of renewal

Remuneration: $20/hour

Applications must be received by Monday, January 11, 2010.

Please submit applications to info@helenpittgallery.org; PDFs only please
do not send other file types. The Helen Pitt Gallery thanks all the
applicants for their interest; however, only those candidates selected for
an interview will be contacted.

Gallery Manager
Vancouver, BC, CA

Requisition ID: 1014

Salary: $30,000.00 Annually at 20 hours/week

Gallery Manager

P/T Permanent (20 hours)

The Sidney and Gertrude Zack Gallery, located within the Jewish Community Centre of
Greater Vancouver is seeking a Gallery Manager to curate exhibits, engage the
community, organize openings, manage the day to day operations and contribute to the
strategic direction of the Art Gallery.

Reporting to the Senior Director of Programs, the right candidate will have experience
as a curator as well as experience in artist management and program development.
The successful candidate will possess strong oral and written comprehension and
expression, creativity, and an ability to work collaboratively. Budget management and
donor development and stewardship are involved. Hours are flexible. Some evening
work is required.

Key responsibilities:

Manage the day-to-day operations of the gallery including the volunteer docent
program.

Solicit proposals, work with a jury to set the Gallerys exhibition schedule,
curate
exhibits and manage artists.

Organize and be involved in installations.

Work collaboratively to develop and manage the Gallerys annual budget.

Work collaboratively to Identify and develop grant proposals.

Establish and cultivate partnerships and donors.

Contribute to the strategic direction of the Gallery and its role within the greater Jewish
Community Centre.

Build and maintain relationships with community stakeholders, aligning with the
gallerys mission and goals.

Engage with the community members and JCC programs.

Qualifications:

University degree in a relevant area.

Demonstrated success with budgeting & resources management.

Grant-acquisition experience.

Experience in the non-profit sector is preferred.

Strong network-building skills with community partners and funders.

Compensation: $30K annually (for 20 hours per week). We offer a competitive benefits
package that includes extended health and dental, an employer matched pension plan
and employee assistance
program (EAP).

How to apply

If this position interests you and you have the required qualifications please provide a
resume and cover letter outlining how your experience fits with the opportunity. Submit
your documents via the career centre by February 4, 2024. Applications will be
reviewed on a rolling basis.

The successful candidate must clear a vulnerable sector criminal record check. We
thank all applicants for their interest however only qualified candidates will be
contacted.

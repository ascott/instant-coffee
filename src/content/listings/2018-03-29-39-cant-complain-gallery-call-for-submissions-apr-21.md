CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

An exhibition that will be seen by three visitors only. 
Deadline: April 21, 2018 

Can't Complain Gallery is a portable D.I.Y gallery space that focuses on 
contemporary art. Can't Complain was created in 2015 and since then it has had 
exhibitions in Guelph, Canada, Bremen, Germany, and Paris, France. 

For this exhibition only a 60 year old male, a 55 year old female and their 20 year old 
daughter will be the visitors, all of whom have no knowledge regarding contemporary 
art. This time the gallery space will be located at a residence in Mississauga, 
Canada. We accept video, audio, conceptual work, painting, print, drawing, sculpture 
and installation. RULES: General (G) Suitable for viewing by all ages. The residence 
has three monitors, two headphones, speakers, nails, screws, a drill, a hammer and 
a level. We're currently not supported by institutions, thus we cannot provide 
financial support to artists. 

Website: www.cantcomplaingallery.tumblr.com 

Please submit your proposal to:
cantcomplaingallery@gmail.com
Deadline: April 21, 2018

Proposals must include:
1. Name(s)
2. Project Description (one short paragraph is enough, but if you want to write more 
you're more than welcome) {PDF FORMAT}
3. Support materials (up to 5 images, links to videos)
**RULES: General (G) Suitable for viewing by all ages.**

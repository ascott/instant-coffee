workhorse, showpony
Access Gallery's annual auction fundraiser!
222 E Georgia St

AUCTION Saturday 24 November at 7pm

PREVIEW Wednesday 21 November at 6pm
ON VIEW 21 to 23 November from 12 to 5pm 

workhorse, show pony is an energetic celebration of hardworking and dynamic artist-
run culture! This years fundraising auction features a range of fantastic work 
generously donated by over 50 emerging and established artists including Vanessa 
Brown, David Khang, Guadalupe Martinez, Ramey Newell, and Christian Nicolay. 
Jonathan Middleton and drag queen Anne Xiety take the reins as MC and auctioneer 
while DJs Ron Tran and Rebecca Brewer bring us the tunes. As is tradition, expect a 
mystery artist whose identity will only be revealed through bidding. The generosity of 
members, donors, and friends like you continue to make it possible for the gallery to 
support compelling and critically engaged emergent practices. Show up in your 
ribbons and tack to Access most important fundraiser of the year! 

$25 entrance includes an annual membership
current members FREE

auction.accessgallery.ca

Place absentee bids directly with Director/Curator Katie Belcher at 
k.belcher@accessgallery.ca until 5pm on Saturday 24 November 2018.

Summerhill Pyramid Winery and Odd Society Spirits.

Accessibility Notes
There is an 8cm (3.5 in) lip at the entrance to the gallery. We plan to have a portable 
ramp for the exterior entrance in time for this event, and can assist with the 112cm 
(44 in) sliding door. The self-contained gender neutral washroom is not wheelchair 
accessible. Access is within 120m of major transit routes (#3, 8, 19, 22), and the 
nearest Skytrain stops are Main Street-Science World (650m) and Stadium-
Chinatown Skytrain (800m). Detailed transit information is available by request. 
There is limited metered street parking and a parking garage on East Georgia. 

If the annual membership fee is a significant barrier to your participation, and you 
still wish to attend this fundraiser, please contact Katie directly at 
k.belcher@accessgallery.ca. Other than fundraisers, all of our programming is free 
to the public.

Tuesday, November 17, 2015
7:00pm - 9:00pm
Location: UBC Museum of Anthropology

On select evenings throughout the duration of the exhibition, the
involved institutions host Musqueam community members, elders,
artists, and activists to share their knowledge and explore the themes
of The City Before the City. Come and engage in the
dialogue surrounding issues of traditional and contemporary relevance.

This week's Cultural Exchange will be a conversation between Morgan
Guerin and Wayne Point.

Morgan Guerin
Morgan Guerin was born and raised at Musqueam. His
father was George Guerin and his grandfather was Arnold Guerin. For
the past 20 years, Morgan has worked as a Fisheries Officer for
Musqueam. He is also a Musqueam councilor.

Wayne Point
Wayne Point is a Musqueam band member and an archaeologist. By
learning from Musqueam's oral histories and ancient belongings,
Wayne is learning to make tools used by his ancestors.

Free with admission (reduced admission on Tuesdays!)

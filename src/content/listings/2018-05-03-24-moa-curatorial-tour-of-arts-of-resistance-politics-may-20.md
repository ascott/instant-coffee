Curatorial Tour of Arts of Resistance: Politics and the Past in Latin America

6393 NW Marine Dr, Vancouver, BC V6T 1Z2

Event description: Join us for the first curatorial tour of the powerful
new show, Arts of Resistance: Politics and the Past in Latin America, by
its curator, Laura Osorio Sunnucks.

You will explore the many new acquisitions that were sourced from
politically tense areas in Latin America and learn how these works express
outrage and engage with injustice. Laura will also use these works to
highlight the relationship between cultural heritage and contemporary
realities in Latin America.

Cost: Free with museum admission

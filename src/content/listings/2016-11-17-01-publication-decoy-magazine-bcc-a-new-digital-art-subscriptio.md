Bcc: A New Digital Art Subscription Program by Decoy Magazine
Curated by Maria Fedorova

[http://www.decoymagazine.ca/bcc](http://www.decoymagazine.ca/bcc)

Bcc: is a monthly, digital art subscription program that is designed to support new 
media artists and their audiences by delivering newly created contemporary 
digital artworks to subscribers' inboxes every month. The subscription fees we 
collect go directly into the pockets of the diverse media artists who are 
commissioned to create new digital works that will be released exclusively to Bcc: 
subscribers. The commissioned artworks have never been exhibited before, and 
each one will only be released once! We see this project as a gesture of 
collective patronage that supports the work and livelihood of digital artists.

The more subscribers we have, the more we can pay the participating artists.
It is only $3 per month to subscribe or $30 for the whole year. 

We are happy to announce that San Francisco-based Anthony Discenza will be 
the first commissioned artist for the project. His artistic practice is centred around 
the exploration of hybridity and fragmentation of digital content and imagery 
available online. Discenza works in a wide range of media including text, video, 
and sound. He has had exhibitions at SFMOMA, MOCA Cleveland, the Getty 
Center in LA, Aspen Art Museum and more. [http://anthonydiscenza.info/](http://anthonydiscenza.info/)

We believe it is very timely to revisit the idea of digital art ownership and to re-
imagine how we might define a culture of sharing creative work. Sign up by 
November 27th and receive the first artwork for free! Sign up here: 
[http://www.decoymagazine.ca/bcc](http://www.decoymagazine.ca/bcc)

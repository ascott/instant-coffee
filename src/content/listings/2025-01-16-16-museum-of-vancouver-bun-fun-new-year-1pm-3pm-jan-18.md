Join Five Blessings for their delightful performance of Bun Fun New Year, the first
episode in their audio play series, A Year of Blessings. This charming production
introduces young audiences to traditional and contemporary celebrations of Chinese
holidays through an engaging 30-minute show featuring original songs and an
interactive dance for all to enjoy.

Following the performances, the Chinese Canadian Museum will present a short talk
on the history and traditions of Lunar New Year, highlighting how it is celebrated by
diverse communities in Vancouver today. Stick around for fun activities after the
presentation! Attendees can enjoy putting on themed temporary tattoos and coloring
pages
inspired by the play's characters.

| 7PM | FEB 21
Join us for a Palestine fundraiser screening and arts event featuring Palestinian-
made short films, prints, books, speeches and more, organized by filmmaker Natali
Karajeh.

Films for Gaza
February 21st, 7pm-late
1965 Main Street

Tickets: https://www.zeffy.com/en-CA/ticketing/cdaab1ae-b96d-4199-b317-
9cca4f9142a1

Through the works of Razan AlSalah, Suha Araj, Saeed Taji Farouky, Farah Nabulsi,
Dina Nasser, and Ahmad Saleh, the program will discuss personalized Palestinian
experiences and the harsh realities of lived occupation and genocide. The screening
will be about 115 minutes, with an intermission in between; offering the audience a
chance to connect with local supporting initiatives, artists, and community members.

Guest speech by Sohbi Al-Zobaidi, filmmaker and co-owner of Tamam Fine
Palestinian Cuisine.

Original prints by artists Diana Quandour and Peonica Fernando, and Palestine-
related books by Massy Books available for purchase.

All proceeds donated to Emergency Relief for Gaza:
https://piousprojects.org/campaign/2680

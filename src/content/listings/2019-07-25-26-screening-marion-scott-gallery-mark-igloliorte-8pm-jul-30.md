Film Screening + Talk: Mark Igloliorte
Tuesday, July 30, 8:00 pm at MSG

Join us for a summer evening screening of Eskimo Roll, Mark Igloliortes 2017 video work. 
Projected onto the gallerys front window and optimally viewed after sundown, Eskimo Roll is a 
playful exploration of personal identity, addressing the themes of cultural displacement and 
reclamation. Presented in conjunction with Point of View, the gallerys summer exhibition, the 
screening will be followed by an artist talk.

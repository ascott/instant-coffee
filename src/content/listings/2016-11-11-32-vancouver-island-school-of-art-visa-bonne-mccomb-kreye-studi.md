The Vancouver Island School of Art has worked in partnership with the Kreye family, 
to establish the Bonnie McComb Kreye Studio Residency. Residents have the use 
of a private 30 sq. m. garden studio for 3 months or 1 month (with accommodation). 
The residency is open to artists working in all media and across disciplines. The 
Bonnie McComb Kreye Studio Residency is open all emerging and mid career 
artists including VISA students and alumni. Opportunities for artists to give talks and 
workshops at VISA can be discussed. Three applicants will be selected each year. 
Their final art works will be considered for an exhibition in Slide Room Gallery. The 
Slide Room Gallery is a professional not-for-profit independent gallery space 
located on the lower level of VISA.

2017 Residency Dates (Deadline: Nov 30, 2016)

January 3 - March 31 (studio)
June 3 - 30 (studio and accommodation)
Sept 5 - Nov 30 (studio)

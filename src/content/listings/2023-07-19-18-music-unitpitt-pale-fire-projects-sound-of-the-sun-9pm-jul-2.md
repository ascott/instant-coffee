Hosted by UNIT/PITT
LOCATION: Pale Fire at 866 E Broadway, Vancouver
DATE: July 28, 2023
TIME: 9:00 PM

Join us Friday, July 28th at 9:00pm for a special performance by Jane Harms and
Ian Davies to celebrate the release of UNIT/PITT's limited-edition Sound of the Sun,
Volume I cassette tape, which will be available for purchase the night of the
event.
Jane Harms' new work The Weekend of the Year is featured on the compilation,
which she will perform alongside several of her other works. Here for the celebration
is "The King of Bedroom Country" Ian Davies, who is graciously bringing tunes for us
from Montreal. Thank you to Pale Fire for their support in hosting us"this will
additionally be an opportunity to see their current exhibit Primes, showing works by
Nicole Andre.

Sound of the Sun, Volume I is the weird, entrancing, celebratory, sometimes gloomy,
and somewhat euphoric summer mixtape of planetary travel, solar interventions,
story telling, and frog choruses you didnt know you needed for summer 2023.
Curated by Marisa Kriangwiwat Holmes and Catherine de Montreuil, the compilation
features tracks by 10 different artists.

As UNIT/PITTs first iteration of a multi-part project of sonic-celestial explorations,
the Sound of the Sun project asks participants to consider their relationship to cycles
of solar movement as the days lengthen on this half of the planet"to observe,
respond, record, and retell through music, poetry, field recordings, storytelling, and
any other audio-based methods in order to speak to this important time of the solar
year.

JANE HARMS thought she would make pop music but in the end any music would
do because all music brings us closer to heaven. She bought a harp but the kind
with buttons, because it was all she could manage, or afford. She found a way to
have its automated strings trigger synthetic tones, to then collide with a voice
straining, breaking under the weight of interpretation.

IAN DAVIES' music is written, recorded, and released on the unceded indigenous
territory of Tiohti:ke (Kanienkehka Nation). "...Davies conjures a sound at once
laid back and fatalistic,
employing the classic country amalgamation of sadness,
nostalgia and fondness that sits next to the likes of Adeline Hotel." " from Jon
Doyle, Various Small Flames

Sing! at the Karaoke Kiosk
Urban Republic invites you to sing and share a tune at 700-block 
Granville Street on Saturday, 20 and 27 from 4 - 8pm. Sing! 
celebrates and shares the pop cultures of Vancouver's diverse 
population. Come sing and hear what grooves in Cantonese, 
English, Filipino, French, Japanese, Mandarin, Hindi and Spanish. 
The Karaoke Kiosk is free and open to all. 
www.urbanrepublic.ca/sing/
Sponsors: City of Vancouver, Viva Vancouver, Downtown Vancouver 
BIA, Lux Karaoke Centre

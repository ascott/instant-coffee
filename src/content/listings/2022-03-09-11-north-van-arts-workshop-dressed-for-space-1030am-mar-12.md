Dressed for Space

Saturday, March 12, 10:30am-11:30am

CityScape Community Art Space
(in person)

As part of the exhibit, I wanted to go on a Space Walk but I had nothing to wear, artists
Ilze Bebris, Marcia Pitch and Kiku Hawkes will guide participants on how to make their
very own space travel suit. All materials and hot glue guns will be provided as well as a
10 stand. Explore space, fashion, and stretch your imagination with this out of the world
Saturday morning workshop.

Registration required

Philosophers’ Café
The F Word

Join Susan Stewart on the first Thursday of every month for a 
three part series considering counter narratives within 
mainstream feminism.

This fall the Philosophers’ Café works with the exhibition 
WACK! Art and the Feminist Revolution as a starting point to 
discuss three counter narratives within mainstream feminism. 
Above all we will focus on the tensions that rise up between 
assumptions that all women are the same, a key operating 
strategy within second wave feminism, and difference, as 
articulated around issues of race, sex and gender.

Thursday, October 2 at 7pm
Masculinity and Feminism 
This café will address masculinity and feminism. We will 
consider representations of men by second wave feminist 
artists, and the hidden and to some degree censored text of 
heterosexual desire. We will discuss the utility of contemporary 
masculinity studies and ask such questions as: What has the 
feminist movement meant for men? Do we still live in 
patriarchal times? What are dominant models of masculinity? 
How do ideas about race, class, and sexuality complicate our 
understanding of masculinity? What can we learn from the 
study of masculinities?

Joining us will be Helen Hok-Sze Leung, from the Department 
of Women Studies at Simon Fraser University.

Susan Stewart is an award winning interdisciplinary artist who 
has produced and directed videos, media installations, 
photography and performance works that have been screened 
and exhibited both nationally and internationally. Susan is on 
the faculty of Emily Carr University and resides in Vancouver, 
British Columbia.

The Vancouver Art Gallery is an ongoing partner with the 
Philosophers’ Café founders, Simon Fraser University’s 
Continuing Education.

Exhibition: Works by Jonathan Petersen

Dates: April 6 - 27, 2024

Location: Outsiders and Others, 716 East Hastings Street, Vancouver

Reception: April 6 from 2-4pm. Free and open to the public.

Artist talk: April 20 at 2pm. Free and open to the public.

This month we are excited to present the artwork of Jonathan Petersen. Jonathan is
a self-taught artist who primarily makes carvings and paintings. His recent works are
collectively defined by fine line brush work that he often paints on found wood and
metal.

The meaning behind his work ranges depending on the piece; however they are
often inspired by dreams and nature. He will sometimes create work within a set
perameters. For instance, Jonathan created a
series of carvings in the time span of
his break at work, out of wood pallets and then refined the pieces at home. He also
made carving while train hopping across Canada which have a rough quality to them
because of the movement of the train.

Jonathan has a unique ability to transform found objects into visceral landscapes
and objects with his painting style and attention to detail.

This is an exhibition everyone will love!

THREE SPOTS ADDED! 2015 VENICE-FLORENCE-ROME EXCHANGE

Developed and presented by the Ontario Association of Art Galleries (OAAG)

We have added up to three more spots to the program, depending on
preference of shared or single room. We received a high number of inquiries
after the registration deadline and have booked additional accommodations
in Venice. First come first serve basis.

This two and a half week exchange is an immersive, intensive program in
Venice, Florence, and Rome in Italy from May 4 to 20, 2015. It has been
timed to coincide with the opening of the 56th Venice Biennale.

This professional development program is developed for public art gallery
directors, curators and arts professionals. It is also valuable for
directors who have been hired into the gallery and museum sector from the
larger charitable sector, and who are also charged with developing new
museum assets in their communities.

OAAG will deliver a very economical, efficient, concentrated (and safe)
learning experience for up to 20 professionals that will strengthen
peer-to-peer resource-sharing relationships for their institutions.

One hundred per cent of 2013 participants told us their participation led
to improved professional knowledge, skills and practices.

Krystle Silverfox: Part of the Land
Opening Thursday March 6
4-7 pm
curated by Jake Kimble for Capture Photography
Festival

Part of the Land is a series of photos which use analogue collage and beading to stitch
back together images of landscapes. The title of the work, Part of the Land, is inspired
by the book Part of the Land, Part of the Water: A History of the Yukon Indians, by
Catharine McClellan. The title is also an acknowledgement of parts: blood quantum
percentages, land allotments, land stewardship, and missing parts of culture. This
series pays homage to the connections we have to the land through acts of care "
beading " to reconnect broken links.
" Krystle Silverfox

Krystle Silverfox is a member of Selkirk First Nation (Wolf Clan) interdisciplinary visual
artist. Her artistic practice explores different materials, methodologies, and symbols to
create conceptual works. Silverfox is inspired by Indigenous feminism, trans-
nationalism, de-colonialism, activism, and lived experience.

VIRTUAL REALITY EXPERIENCE | @Alliance Franaise Vancouver + online| Recto VRso
@Vancouver | Oct 2-4

From October 2-4, Alliance Franaise de Vancouver invites you to experience -online or in
person- Recto VRso @Vancouver, an Immersive Art Gallery Experienced in Virtual Reality.

Featuring 16 digital artworks from local and international artists, the virtual gallery created by
Judith GUEZ, exhibition curator and founder of the RECTO VRso festival by Laval Virtual
(FR), has been transformed into a multi-user VR experience on the
unique platform made by
local company KREIS Immersive.

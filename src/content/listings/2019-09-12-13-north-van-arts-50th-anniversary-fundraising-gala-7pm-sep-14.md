For one night only we are transforming our gallery into a pop-up bar and immersive 
visual art experience inspired by the past 50 years. Decades is a celebration where you 
can indulge in site-specific light & video installation by Mieke Jay Bray, an immersive art 
gallery showcase in VR by Museum of Other Realities, spoken word performance by 
rising star Andrew Warner, a gastronomical tour of the past five decades by our friends 
at Served, live music, as well as your favourite hits from the past fifty years.

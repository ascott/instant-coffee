Communicating from the Grave: Archives and Estates 
with Amy Nugent and Teresa Sudeyko

Date: Sunday, March 5, 2017 from 1:00-4:00pm
Location: Fireside Room, Burnaby Art Gallery, 6344 Deer Lake Avenue, Burnaby, BC.
Cost: $20 for CARFAC members; $32 for non-members
Presented by the Burnaby Art Gallery in partnership with CARFAC BC.

In the 50 years after the deaths of artists Frances Loring and Florence Wyle many 
attempts were made to fulfill their dying wish to create an acquisition fund to support 
Canadian sculpture. 

Looking through the lens of research into the lives, work and estates of Frances and 
Florence Amy Nugent presents her findings, including what was missing from the 
archives and documentation along the way, and how she arrived at the plan to realize 
The Sculpture Fund.

Teresa Sudeyko, the registrar at the Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, will walk 
through some of the lessons learned from Frances and Florence and from her 
experience working with museum collections and artist archives. Teresa will discuss 
documentation, preservation and labeling among other key elements of organizing one's 
artwork and archive. 
_

About the instructors:
Teresa Sudeyko has been the Registrar at the Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery since 
2008 and an independent art appraiser since 2013. She also works with private 
collectors to organize and document their collections. Teresa has a Master's of Library 
and Information Studies from UBC (2008) and a Bachelor's degree in Fine Arts from 
York University (2005).

Amy Nugent is Executive Director of the Inclusion BC Foundation and past-president of 
Artspeak's Board of Directors. She was the 2016 recipient of the Mayor's Arts Award for 
Arts Board Member of the Year. Amy has worked in resource development for VIVO 
Media Arts, Western Front and The Banff Centre. She just launched The Sculpture Fund 
to fulfill the Last Will & Testament of sculptors Frances Loring and Florence Wyle and is 
currently enjoying being a member of the City of Vancouver Northeast False Creek 
Stewardship Committee.

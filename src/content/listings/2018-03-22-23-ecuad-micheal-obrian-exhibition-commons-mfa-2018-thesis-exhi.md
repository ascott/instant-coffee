ECUAD's MFA 2018 Thesis Exhibition 
When: March 28 to April 6
Where: Emily Carr University of Art + Design
Reception: Michael O'Brian Exhibition Commons - 5th April 6-9pm

April from 6-9pm for their MFA Graduate Exhibition. The show and reception will take 
place on the 2nd floor of ECUAD in the Michael OBrian Exhibition Commons. Non-
alcoholic beverages and small refreshments will be served.

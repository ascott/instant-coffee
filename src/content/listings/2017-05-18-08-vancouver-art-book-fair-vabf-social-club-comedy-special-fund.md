VABF Social Club: Comedy Special Fundraiser
Saturday, May 20, 2017, 7-11:30 PM
Studio 700 (700 Hamilton St)

Join VABF at Studio 700 for an evening of fresh comedy and DJ Cherchez La
Femme.

Come support the 2017 Vancouver Art Book Fair while sharing drinks and gags 
with some of Vancouvers funniest comedians. Cameron Macleod (The Hero 
Show, House On Fire Comedy, JFL NorthWest) has put together a line-up of 
musical, standup and physical acts only book fair goers could love. Cherchez will 
take us dancing into the night. Stay tuned for info about an exclusive after party!

What kind of VABF fundraiser would it be without art books? Drop your name into 
an exclusive art book raffle or upgrade your event ticket to a $20+ donation to 
receive a limited-edition Comedy Special zine.

FEATURING

Comedy by Cameron Macleod, Jacob Samuel, Aaron Charles Read, Fraz Wiest,
Carla Mah, Kelli Ogmundson and Nima Gholamipour.

DJ set by Cherchez La Femme

as well as an Art Book Raffle and Limited-edition Zine

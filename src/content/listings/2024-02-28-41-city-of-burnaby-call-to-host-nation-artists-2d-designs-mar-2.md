The City of Burnaby invites artists from host First Nations: xmky"m (Musqueam),
Swxw7mesh (Squamish), slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh), and kik" m (Kwikwetlem)
to respond to this public art opportunity. Burnabys Events team is looking for one artist
from a host Nation to create a series of four 2-dimensional designs for wayfinding at
civic events.

Commission fee: $10,000

Deadline to Apply: March 22, 2024 at 11:59pm.
Target Date for Artwork Creation: early May 2024.

Full opportunity details available on our website at www.burnaby.ca/publicart
For Inquiries Please Contact: Allison Collins, Public Art Coordinator, City of Burnaby
604-297-4969 publicart@burnaby.ca

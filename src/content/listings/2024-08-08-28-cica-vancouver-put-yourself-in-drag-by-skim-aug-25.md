We are thrilled to invite SKIM, also known as Romi Kim, who will be
hosting Put Yourself in Drag on August 25, 2024.

This workshop provides an inclusive space for participants to delve into the world of
drag, guided by the
renowned drag artist SKIM. Celebrated for their innovative
performances and distinctive style, SKIM has captivated audiences with dynamic
and expressive shows, becoming a prominent figure in the drag community. Beyond
their artistic talents, SKIM is a dedicated advocate for 2SLGBTQIA+ rights, using
their platform to promote inclusivity, self-expression, and community engagement.

Whether youre a seasoned performer or new to the world of drag, this workshop is
open to all individuals aged 7 and older, including families. Come unleash your inner
drag persona, and experience the joy and empowerment of drag artistry at CICA
Vancouver.

We kindly ask our participants to bring their own makeup bag. Why? Because we
want to teach you how to put yourself in drag using what you already have. This
approach highlights that you don't need an extensive or expensive makeup kit to
create stunning drag looks.

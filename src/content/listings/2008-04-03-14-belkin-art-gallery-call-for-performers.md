Call for Performer

The Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery is hiring 2—3 performers to attend an artwork in our upcoming exhibition, Idyll.  

The ideal candidate is a physically fit male, with long hair, in his late teens/early twenties.  Performers will work 5 hour shifts during which they will make themselves at home inside a dome structure. 

Part time, 15-20 hours per week, shifts available Tuesday to Sunday between May 16 and August 17, 2008.
$10.00 per hour

Application deadline April 18, 2008

Please send a recent photo and c.v. to Julie Bevan at julie.bevan@ubc.ca

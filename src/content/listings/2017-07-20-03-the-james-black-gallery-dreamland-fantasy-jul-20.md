Open Hours 2-4PM July 20th and 21st
Closing Event 7PM July 22nd

Starchild Stelas Dreamland Fantasy is a multidisciplinary sensorial experience that 
invite the viewer to make themselves at home and feel nostalgic, in a sweet, ethereal 
kind of way. Building on delightful memories & instance of childhood traumas, 
Dreamland Fantasy is a whimsical bedroom where healing and nostalgia collide into 
daydreams.

Starchild Stela is from Montreal and graces The James Black Gallery in July's guest 
artist residency.

Silent Runnings is a silent film festival hosted by Cornershop
Projects in Vancouver in March 2008.  Set within a larger thematic of
Obsolete Concepts, the films included in Silent Runnings will be
shown in multiple and various sites and locations in Vancouver.
Homes entertainment systems, computers (on and offline) art
galleries, public projections, and store fronts are some of the
potential viewing locations.

Cornershop Projects is a project space located in Vancouver, British
Columbia.  Cornershop hosts,  initiates and encourages projects that
explore various modes of exchange.

These are the guidelines/requirements:
- Subject matter is up to you
- 30 seconds to 5 minutes in length (including any credits, if included)
- No sound (no music)
- 2 separate DVD copies of your film
- A brief synopsis (1 or 2 paragraphs) of your film and contact info
(email and/or telephone)
- Submissions should be received by FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 15th, 2008

debut solo exhibition by maya thiersch

viewing hours 2-5.30ish, social closing 6ish til 8pm at Liquidation World (1965 Main
St.)

" SEX LIKE THIS " anthologizes a year of daydreaming and reflections on semi
requited love, working shitty menial jobs, and uneasy relationships with the body
through references taken from personal messages, found text, and images from pop
culture.

maya's current practice weaves the bravado of
petty graffiti and absurdism of
internet humour; an ongoing body of work that includes public proclamations of " i
was here", playful documentation of daily life, infantile bids for attention, and
occasional cries for help through a range of media including painting, collage,
sculpture, and amateur taxidermy.

holding a bfa from emily carr university of art + design, maya has previously
exhibited in group shows including " 8 Frames 8 Artists " with TapewormCorp inside
the Host Gallery inside Gallery George, and " May I Please (Look Into Your Home) ?
" with Novalynn Diguistini and Josh Koole in the Michael O'Brian Exhibition
Commons.

Steven Brekelmans - New Drawings of Bottles, Cans, Hats, Cheese, Sculptures, 
Posters, Casseroles, Azaleas, Canes, Flags, Trash bins, Cacti, Glasses, Lighters, 
Mirrors...

Chernoff Fine Art
17 January - 23 February
OPENING RECEPTION: Thursday, January 17, 6-8pm

This collection of new drawings, an extension of Steven Brekelmans studio 
sculpture practice, allow for creative gestures that would otherwise be impossible. 
Casseroles, buckets, seashells and all manner of castoffs humourously augment 
and disarm the formal geometric constructions. These flattened three-dimensional 
efforts, rendered in an illustrative, comic book style, build upon his previous 
exploration of collecting, labour, formalism and worth.

Steven Brekelmans was born in Vancouver, attended the Emily Carr Institute of Art 
and Design and graduated from the University of Victorias MFA program. Working 
across a variety of mediums, he has exhibited his work both locally and 
internationally at Or Gallery, CSA Space, UNIT/PITT Projects, Western Front, The 
Museum of Longing and Failure (Norway), The Western Bridge (Seattle), Or Gallery 
Berlin, and Soi Fischer Projects (Toronto).

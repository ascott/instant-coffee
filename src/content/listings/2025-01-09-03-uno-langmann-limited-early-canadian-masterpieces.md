EARLY CANADIAN MASTERPIECES

Now till the end of January 2025

Location: Uno Langmann Limited, 2117 Granville Street, Vancouver

The consignment of a major collection of early watercolours spurred the decision to
mount a major retrospective exhibition of Early Canadian Masterpieces, with the
addition of works collected by us over many decades. Spanning the century between
1825 and 1925, this exhibition represents major early artists committed to capturing
the lifestyle and landscape of early Indigenous and pioneer life across Canada.
Many of these early artists immigrated from
Europe and received artistic training,
some received little training. Many artists travelled across the country before the
completion of the Canadian Pacific Railway, later at the invitation of Van Horne as
part of the Railway Artists documenting the opening of the Canadian West. These
artists developed a unique style of Canadian landscape painting.

Join us on a journey through early Canadian art history. The exhibition is available to
view in the gallery until January 31, 2025 and is also viewable online:
www.langmann.com

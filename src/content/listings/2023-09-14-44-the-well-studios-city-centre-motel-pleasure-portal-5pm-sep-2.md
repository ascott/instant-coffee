PLEASURE PORTAL
An immersive evening of ephemeral art
Sunday Sept.24th 5-7pm

The Well Studios @ City Centre
Motel

2111 Main Street #27 Vancouver, BC V5T3C

Inspired by Audre Lordes Uses of the Erotic Pleasure Portal is an exploration of
what it means to reclaim our senses as resistance and come back to our bodies as
pathways to possibility and liberation.

Gathering on unceded and ancestral territories of the xmky"m (Musqueam),
Swxw7mesh (Squamish), and slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations - together we will
consider notions of existence in a society that is disembodied and positions the body
only as a site of capitalist productivity.

We will reclaim pleasure within the landscape of the body and beyond, divesting
from oppressive systems in order to create space to turn towards our imagination, to
access possibility.

May we collectively and creatively dream of what it could mean to exist in a world
where pleasure is more important than productivity, where pleasure is the point.

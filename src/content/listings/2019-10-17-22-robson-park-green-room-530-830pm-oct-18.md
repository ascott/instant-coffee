GREEN ROOM

Matthew Ballantyne
Matt Browning
Maegan Hill Carroll
Alannah Clamp
Steven Cottingham
Jessica Evans
Andrea Oliver Roberts
Leigh Tennant

Friday,
October 18th, 2019 5:30 - 8:30 pm
Robson Park, Kingsway + Carolina Street, Vancouver, BC

Green Room is a group exhibition in Robson Park, on the corner of Kingsway and 15th
Avenue. The public park functions as a site free of the gallerys regulating conventions
where objects are granted value by virtue of their provenance within exchangeable
locations. Instead, this project situates artists' work against the picturesque, natural light,
and weather. Rather than adhering to an exclusively artistic logic, the works are opened
to the logic of 'the public' and are apprehended as familiar materials in unfamiliar
configurations. The park is a living space - socially, ecologically - and Green Room is an
experiment in bringing that unmanageability into the static aesthetic dimension.

*Please join us following the exhibition for drinks at the British Ex Servicemens
Association, 1143 Kingsway.
*This exhibition is outdoors, please consider proper clothing/weather protection

Glossary of Cognitive Activism by Warren Neidich
Collective Audience Activated Performative Reading

at

People's Co-Op Books
1391 Commercial Drive, Vancouver, BC
April 24, 7-9pm
Organized by Gallery Gachet

Guest Readers: Fiona Bowie, Jesse McKee, more TBA

The Glossary of Cognitive Activism is an iterative activist dictionary composed of 330
words that together describe and navigate the technological, ecological, sociogenic,
artistic and philosophical landscape of our planet today. It provides readers with the
intellectual tools to guide them through and dissent against our difficult and
precarious moment.

Berlin-based Warren Neidich and invited readers ignite the audience into a
conversation by reading a definition or part of a definition from the book, which will
be available for sale. Neidich is the director of the Saas-Fee Summer Institute of Art,
and he will be exhibiting artwork at Gallery Gachet in July.

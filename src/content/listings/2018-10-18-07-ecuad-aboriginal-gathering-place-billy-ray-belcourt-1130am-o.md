Billy-Ray Belcourt

Thursday, October 18 | 11:30 am
Aboriginal Gathering Place
ECAUD
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Billy-Ray Belcourt (he/him) is a writer and academic from the Driftpile Cree Nation. 
He is a Ph.D. student and 2018 Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholar in the 
Department of English & Film Studies at the University of Alberta. He is also a 2016 
Rhodes Scholar and holds an M.St. in Womens Studies from the University of 
Oxford and Wadham College. 

Billy-Rays debut book of poems, This Wound is a World (Frontenac House 2017), 
won the 2018 Griffin Poetry Prize and the 2018 Robert Kroetsch City of Edmonton 
Book Prize. It was also named the Most Significant Book of Poetry in English by an 
Emerging Indigenous Writer at the 2018 Indigenous Voices Awards, and best 
Canadian poetry collection of 2017 by CBC Books. His sophomore book, NDN 
Coping Mechanisms: Notes from the Field, is due out in the fall of 2019 with House 
of Anansi Press. 

Hosted by the Aboriginal Gathering Place.

LVL

Vancouver's local queer femme bass supergroup

@djbeermusic | @loouup | @stephanie-tsunami

LVL (elle-vee-elle) is known for their dynamic sets featuring a range of high-energy
sounds and catchy vocal stabs that electrify the crowd. Collectively, they have
played some of Vancouvers largest clubs such as Red Room, Celebrities, and
Levels, and toured across BC playing clubs and festivals including Contact,
Rifflandia, Wicked Woods, and Valhalla. Flexing an
expansive collection from breaks
& 140 to techno, jungle, and drum & bass, this high-octane trio is primed to bring
their unique flavour of bass

the-passenger

Over the past 20 years the-passenger, aka Jesse Creed, Dimir Standard, CreedMD,
DJ Val Pillmer, has been playing and or making everything from ambient, drone,
acid, IDM, euro dance, bmore, to whatever. Having a 12 year, weekly residency at
one of Vancouver's only legal after hours clubs honed his skill for late night dance
marathons.

On this fine evening he will be playing some of the finest examples of dank dance

Event / What Do We Owe To Each Other? 

This is an experimental and experiential space cultivated for dialogue, exchange and 
reflection. By contemplating notions of care, the aim of this space is to form a collective 
understanding of how care functions - in its past, present and futures. 

Come inhabit space, collaborate and form new connections by engaging with a range of 
senses to express notions of care towards oneself and others. 

*space includes sound, smell, taste, touch, sight and potential insight.

Radical Care @ The Ground Floor Art Centre 
735 Gore Ave, Vancouver BC VC V6A 2Z9 

November 26th to 28th / 3:00 - 9:00 pm daily.

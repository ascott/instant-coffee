We invite you to join us for the opening of our summer exhibition, preceded by two short 
Artist Talks. We are thrilled to present WINDS; four multi-media installations by four 
international artists. Each takes as their material process, the representation, recreation and 
manifestation of wind through physical space and time. Each suggests how our perception 
and understanding of this material-process can first be created through pressure, sound, 
data, pattern, music and motion and then further appreciated in more subjective and poetic 
ways that influence our understanding of how language functions, how we perceive historic 
events and the subjective nature of time.

Sound is a key element here ; emphasizing or recreating sonic experiences and effects that 
trigger memory or emotion, or prompting the viewer to consider significant philosophical 
questions. The works prompt us to consider how ephemeral information can be perceived + 
imagined, collected, controlled and interpreted; how this is done through objective 
observation and measurables; pressure, sound, pattern, motion and collected data and 
through naming ; and how we further interpret through implied narrative, music and the 
suggestions of past times and distant places. There is an attempt, in all the works, to show 
how a specific place or moment in time can (or cant ) be captured, bringing inevitable 
connections to loss, memory, memento mori.

ARTIST TALK
David Bowen
Born in the US in 1975, his multi-award-winning work revolves around the relationship 
between technology and nature. His work has been featured in numerous group and and 
solo exhibitions and publications around the world. With a BFA from Herron School of Art, 
1999 and an MFA, University of Minnesota, Minneapolis, 2004. He is currently Associate 
Professor of Sculpture and Physical Computing at the University of Minnesota, Duluth. 

Chris Welsby, landscape artist and pioneer of moving-image installation in Britain, whose 
subtle meditations are exhibited and collected by museums and galleries around the world. 
Welsby has been making and exhibiting work since 1969. He completed undergraduate 
diplomas at Central School of Art and is a graduate of the early Experimental Media 
Department at Slade School of Fine Art, London. He received a BA from Chelsea School of 
Art. Since 1989 he has been Professor of Fine Art at Simon Fraser University - School for 
the Contemporary Arts, now retired. 

ARTalk Workshop: David Bowen
June 22, 11:00am-1:00pm
New Media Gallery is proud to present another in our ARTalk series for students & emerging 
artists. Join celebrated artist David Bowen as he delves into the creative and technological 
process behind multi-media work tele-present wind (2018). David will offer demonstrations 
and hands-on access to his robotic devices. He'll share his knowledge of robotics, 
accelerometers, realtime data and discuss how his work has transmitted the wind between 
very disparate locations. This special workshop has been made available to emerging 
artists, by invitation, and will be streamed live for broad access.

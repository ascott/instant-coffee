LIVE Biennale - Patrick Cruz "Kitchen Codex"

Patrick Cruz will be grilling succulent comestibles on July 29, Saturday from 3-7 pm at
John Hendry Park in Trout Lake and you are invited to eat and partake by trading your
favorite recipe.

Kitchen Codex is an ongoing site-dependent food-based project. Since 2015 It has
manifested, shared, and collected recipes in various locations and contexts such as
Guelph, Sudbury & Toronto, Ontario; Berlin, Germany; Malm, Sweden; Surrey, British
Columbia and Mexico City, Mexico. Most of the recipes have been archived and printed
onto a tablecloth where future Kitchen Codex performances happen.

This performance will be kicking off the launch of the newly minted LIVE Biennale.

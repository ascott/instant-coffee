OCAD U announces new Emerging Artist Summer Intensive Program

Want
to expand your creative practice? Apply to be part of a new artist residency at
OCAD U next summer!

THIRDSPACE: Emerging Artist Summer Intensive Program is a new month-long
artist residency at OCAD University, launching next June 2025. Designed for
interdisciplinary emerging artists, this immersive summer program welcomes
individuals at the early stages of their careers or those seeking to explore new
directions in their creative practices.

Program Highlights:

Work with a renowned Artist-in-Residence: Be guided by an established Toronto-
based, internationally recognized artist as you deepen your creative practice within a
thematic framework.
Get studio access: Enjoy a full month of open studio access, as well as use of OCAD
Us specialized facilities.
Explore art and culture in Toronto: Immerse yourself in the vibrant artistic community
of Toronto while engaging with its cultural scene.
Show your work: Participate in a final exhibition showcasing your work to the public.

Who
Should Apply?

This program is designed for visual artists and designers who are in the final stages
of their formal post-secondary education or training, have gained equivalent
experience through independent practice, cultural or traditional knowledge, and are
recognized by their peers as emerging talents. Eligible applicants include:

Current undergraduate or graduate students
Recent graduates
Early-career practicing artists and designers
Artists and designers seeking to explore new creative directions.

Apply Now!
Fill out the online application form at bit.ly/ocaduthirdspace and be ready to submit a
written statement of intent and a portfolio.

Early bird deadline: February 14th, 2025 (Save $200)
Final application deadline: May 13th, 2025

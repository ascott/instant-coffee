Case Study: Vancouver Art Gallery and Protocols

SUN, Feb 21 / 4 - 5:30PM
Audain Gallery, 149 W. Hastings St. 

Talking Stick Festival, Industry Series

This panel discussion looks at the roles and responsibilities of arts 
institutions today when working with Indigenous cultural material. 
Through a close examination of a recent performance at the Vancouver 
Art Gallery's FUSE, presenters bring forward questions and strategies 
regarding protocol, permission and re-enactment.

Presenters:

Daina Augaitis, Chief Curator/Associate Director, Vancouver Art Gallery
Glenn Alteen, Program Director, grunt gallery, guest curator FUSE
Mique'l Dangeli, Dance Artist-In-Residence, Scotiabank Dance Center
Lauraleigh Paul Yuxweluptunaat

Moderated by Chris Creighton-Kelly

Wrap up by Nika Collison, Curator, Haida Gwaii Museum at Kay 
Llnagaay

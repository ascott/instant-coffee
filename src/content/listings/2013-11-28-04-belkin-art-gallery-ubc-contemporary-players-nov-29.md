Concert by UBC Contemporary Players at the Belkin
Friday, November 29, 2:00 pm

Join us for a concert by the UBC Contemporary Players at the Belkin Art Gallery. Ensemble directors Corey Hamm and Paolo Bortolussi present a program inspired by the Witnesses exhibition and Indian residential schools.  

PROGRAM

Ali Askin, Some Body(‘s) Music (1989): Geronimo Mendoza, Vivian Chen, Waylon Ye, Yan Ng, Jonathan Mok, Corey Hamm

Gyorgy Kurtag, The Little Fix (1979): Joohee Kim (piccolo), Justin Chiang (trombone), Abraham Landa (guitar)

John Cage, Living Room Music (1940): Lidia HaeJu Lee, Luke Wang, Samantha Ballard, Elaine Du

Toru Takemitsu, Toward the Sea, 1st movement (1981): Joohee Kim (flute), Abraham Landa (guitar)

Michael Oesterle, Sunspot Letters (2006): Geronimo Mendoza (oboe), Edmund Chung (violin), David Xiao (viola), Peter Hwang (cello)

All welcome. Admission is free.

THE BAKERY is a new independent exhibition space in Vancouver BC. THE
BAKERY provides an intimate environment for emerging artists to exhibit
their work. We are looking to showcase artists who's works combine
investigated artistry, experimentation with informed fundamental discourse.
THE BAKERY is preparing it's 2015/2016 programming and is accepting
submissions from individual artists, collectives and curators. We will be
accepting proposals until Sept. 8th, 2015.

If you wish to submit a proposal to exhibit your work email us at
info@thebakeryonfranklin.com and include all the pertinent information like
artist statement, proposal, cv, images, image list and website link. Thank
you for your interest, only those considered will be contacted.

To view a floor plan of our gallery space go to:

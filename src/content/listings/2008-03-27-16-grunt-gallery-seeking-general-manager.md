General Manager

grunt gallery

grunt gallery is a non-profit artist-run centre formed in 1984 in Vancouver, British Columbia,Canada. We are committed to providing a forum for a wide spectrum of critical discourse andmultidisciplinary art practices. Our mission is to engage communities in creating environmentsthat support collaboration, innovation and challenging contemporary art in Canada.Due to our growth and success we are seeking a highly motivated, organized and creativeindividual to fill the full-time position of General Manager, to commence June 15, 2008.Reporting directly to the Board of Directors the General Manager is one of two permanent staffpositions and will work in partnership alongside the gallery's founding Artistic Director tofacilitate the day-to-day financial, administrative, marketing & communications and humanresource function of the gallery. Over the next two years grunt Board and staff will support theLegacy Campaign Manager in achieving the successful implementation of a 2-year
fundraisingplan that will see the establishment a $1,000,000 endowment intended to support and sustainthe healthy growth and continued long-term success of the organization.Accountable for guiding and participating in the collaborative day to day gallery operationscarried out with the support of a team of contract employees, the General Manager will beresponsible for facilitating daily operations including but not limited to: budgeting and financialmanagement, operational grant writing, staffing coordination, information systems andoperational procedures. Marketing and fundraising experience will be beneficial as the GeneralManager is also responsible for coordinating marketing and communications initiatives using thewebsite, print and other media.Ideally you enjoy and have experience building team capacity and aspire to increasing levels ofaccountability and efficiency. A leader as well as a hands-on doer you possess analytical skills,can formulate and implement plans, coordinate
details and timelines, build consensus, andsupport a collaborative spirited team of contractors, artists and volunteers. Applicants will berequired to demonstrate 3-5 years of experience in financial management, including working fornon-profit – preferably in the arts; good communication skill both written and oral; as well as aknack for negotiating shared project management.Visit www.grunt.bc.ca to learn more about grunt gallery.Salary - commensurate with experience.Qualified applicants please apply by April 11, 2008 with Resume and Cover Letter in confidenceto:Search Committee – General Manager, grunt galleryBy email only please: stayinvancouver@shaw.ca

Burnaby Arts Councils 4th Annual Summer Arts Festival Taking Place July 7th at 
Deer Lake

Burnaby will once again be dazzled by an event noted for being fun for the entire 
family! The Summer Arts Festival is an all-day event on Saturday, July 7th from 10 
am to 5 pm. Set against the beautiful Deer Lake Park, its a chance to experience 
the best of Burnabys art scene. 

Festival highlights:

Parks Edge Paint Off, the festivals annual live painting competition: Come and 
see how paintings are created and vote for your favourite! The top three artists will 
all win cash prizes, courtesy of Burnaby Tourism.

Artisan Market featuring over 15 of BCs finest artists and designers displaying 
a variety of hand crafted items including: Art Cards by Valerie, KCs Woodcraft, Euro 
Designs, Burnaby Artists Guild, and Slate Jewelry.

Inspiration Station hosted by Muckabout Gift Gallery: Share your stories, draw 
and write what inspires you at their kids colouring station!

Live Entertainment including the Burnaby Summer Theatre (dont miss their live 
performance of Dragcula, an original play written, directed by performing art 
students from SFU that is based off of Alice in Wonderland!). One Human Race " 
Afrika Collective Trio, Art Exhibition: Tricksters Laugh featuring Alanna Edward & 
Geronimo and Ben Lee aka DJ BJAM will also be at the festival!

Beer Garden in the gallerys backyard featuring local brewery Dageraad

Silent Auction of the painting from the Paint Off " a chance to take some of the 
magic home!

The Summer Art Festival is organized by the Burnaby Arts Council and takes place 
at the Deer Lake Gallery, located at 6584 Deer Lake Avenue in Burnaby.

About the Burnaby Arts Council

The Burnaby Arts Council (BAC) was established in 1967 to fund raise for a Burnaby 
based public art gallery. In its 45+ years, the BAC has continued with its mandate of 
supporting, promoting and expanding the arts throughout Burnaby. Further, the 
Council plays a pivotal role in providing leadership to emerging artists and 
established art groups alike; showcasing their creative talents and providing 
opportunities for greater involvement in community culture events and projects. For 
more information, visit: www.burnabyartscouncil.org

Title: Flat-Stitch Beading Workshop with Serena Steel

Date: September 27th, 2024

Hours & Locations: 6 " 8p, CCBC Shop & Gallery

Description:

Interdisciplinary artist Serena Steel will be sharing her knowledge and experience in
this introduction to beading workshop.

In this intimate workshop for 8, participants can expect to create their own flat-stitch
project with step-by-step instruction and support from Serena. You will each choose
your own bead colours and decide whether you want to create a pendant, pin, or
earrings. You will learn three styles of stitching to utilize as you complete your
project, and learn more about your preferred beading style. Serena will discuss what
she has
learned about beadwork from her experience, as well as share the cultural
teachings that she has surrounding the medium.

All of the materials that you will need are provided and will be yours to keep.

No experience required, but please bring your glasses, the beads are smaller than
you think!

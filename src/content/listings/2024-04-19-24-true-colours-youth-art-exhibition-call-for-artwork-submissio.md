True Colours Youth Art Exhibition

Call for Artwork Submissions " Deadline Extended to April 21, 2024

PoMoArts, the Port Moody
Arts Centre Society, is a community-based arts
organization in Port Moody, British Columbia. We create art experiences to engage,
challenge, and awaken opportunities for connection.

The True Colours exhibition is creating a safe and welcoming space for Tri-City
LGBTQIA2S+ youth, their families, and their allies, where they are respected for their
differences and their unique contributions. It is a celebration of youth artists (age 12-
19) who identify as queer, questioning or allies of the LGBTQIA2S+ community. The
goal is to encourage and promote inclusivity and diversity within the Tri-Cities arts
community among our young people.

For the True Colours exhibition, PoMoArts is inviting youth artists to submit original
artwork that explores themes of identity, family and culture. School District 43 (Port
Moody, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Anmore and Belcarra) students are encouraged
to apply.

The exhibition runs from May 16 " July 8, 2024. An invite-only True Colours Artist
Reception
will be held on June 6, 2024 for the artists to invite their friends and family.
In addition, True Colours artists are invited to attend the public Opening Reception
for all PoMoArts exhibitions in this show on May 16, 2024.

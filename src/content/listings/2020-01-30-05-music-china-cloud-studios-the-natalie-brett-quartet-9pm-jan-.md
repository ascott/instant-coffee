Inspired by the work of 20th century avant-garde singer Natalie Brettschneider, this
crack quartet of Vancouver improvisors explores non-standard ways to play jazz
standards. Their repertoire features the work of 20th century female composers and
lyricists, including Maria Grever, Irene Higginbotham, and Canadians Ruth Lowe and
Florence Fazan. Join us as we get ready to go into the recording studio to make an LP
of these great tunes!

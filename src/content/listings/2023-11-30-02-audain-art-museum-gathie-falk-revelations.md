Gathie Falk: Revelations is now open at the Audain Art Museum in Whistler. It will be on
display until May 6, 2024.

This exhibition, with over 90 works on display, underlines the incredible range of Falks
creative output in ceramic and bronze sculpture, acrylic and oil on canvas painting,
photography and video, as well as multi-media installation.

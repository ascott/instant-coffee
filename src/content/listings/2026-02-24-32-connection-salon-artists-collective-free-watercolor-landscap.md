FREE Watercolor Landscape Workshop

Join us for a series of relaxing and inspiring watercolor sessions with our instructor Jimmy Ko
on Saturday March 7, 14, 21 and 28 at 1pm-3pm at the Gathering Place Community Center
Art Studio!

In this beginner-friendly workshop, you will:
learn the basic skills and knowledge of watercolor landscape painting,
complete your first watercolor artwork,
discuss, share, and appreciate each other's finished pieces in a supportive group setting.

This event is supported by the Connection Salon Artist Collective, the Gathering Place
Community Centre, and the British Columbia Arts Council.

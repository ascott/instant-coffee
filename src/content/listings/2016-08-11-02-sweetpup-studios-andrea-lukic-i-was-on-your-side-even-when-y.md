I was on your side, even when you were losing.
Andrea Lukic
Opening: August 11th 7pm
Sweet Pup Studios
79 E Pender, Vancouver, British Columbia

With a special performance by the Cowboy City Rockers featuring Hugo
Noriega, Mitch Charron, Aileen Bryant, Daniel Pitout and Claire Newton.

Andrea Lukic is a multidisciplinary artist currently based in Vancouver,
BC. Her work focuses on surreal, fantastic, absurd, alchemical, and
grotesque visions in hell and heroes. In "I was on your side, even when you
were losing" the work travels as a forensic investigator peering into the
pockets of time of an altered world of forbidden fruit and fallen heroes.
Past the castles, knights explode, we see the WACO siege on television and
many faces which look the same. The clown in the court dances the same
strange dance, the one of knowing his place in the great chain of being,
but in this story the public executions show blue blood running in the
streets. Over everything a heroine watches with divine inspiration, she
time travels the dark ages to 1980s where she works as a dancer in a post
plague club called "Choices" spinning roses and breaking up fights between
rug merchants and broke heirs. She dreams big.

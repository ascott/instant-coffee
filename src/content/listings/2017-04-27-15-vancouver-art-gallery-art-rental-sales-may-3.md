www.artrentalandsales.com
www.instagram.com/vanartrental

Once a year, the Art Rental & Sales program at the Vancouver Art Gallery hosts an 
open call for Canadian artists based in British Columbia to consign artwork to our 
program.

All submissions are reviewed by a selection committee and applicants are notified as to 
the status of their submission within 2 months of our submission deadline.

This year's Call for Submissions is now open. Applications will be accepted through end 
of day May 3rd, 2017.
To apply, visit 
www.artrentalandsales.com/submissions.php

InFlux
Surrey Art Gallery
Friday March 14 | 8 to 11pm

Immerse yourself in a night of art action as InFlux returns to the Surrey Art 
Gallery on Friday, March 14 from 8pm to 11pm. Whether you want to soak up 
the sounds or get up and dance, be entertained by a stimulating evening of 
live music mash-ups, break dance battle demos, and performance art. Surrey 
Art Gallery’s InFlux evenings bring together art, music, dance, and DIY 
activities in a casual, fun atmosphere. No tickets needed – donation at the 
door of $5 minimum per person. Beverages will be available for purchase. No 
minors admitted.

Emcee, Tad Hozumi and DJ, Dennis Ha will move you through the evening 
with excitement and ease. Sounds will include spoken word by poet Larissa 
Lai, and the debut of musical group Holy Hum (Andrew Lee & friends) with 
visuals by Andy Dixon & Ryan Flowers. Performance artists Karen and 
Peggy Ngan have got your number in Still Counting, while dumb instrument 
Dance (Ziyian Kwan in collaboration with James Gnam) and break dance 
battle demos led by local b-boy Arthur Tiojanco provide occasions to watch 
and move. Throughout the evening, DIY art led by artists Kenten Bowick and 
Eryne Donahue serve up platforms to create and collaborate with friends.

InFlux is presented as part of the Surrey Art Gallery exhibition (Da bao)
(Takeout), curated by Toronto-based Shannon Anderson and Beijing-based 
Doug Lewis. The exhibition is organized and circulated by the Varley Art 
Gallery of Markham. The exhibition closes on March 23.

Surrey Art Gallery is located at 13750 - 88 Avenue, Surrey, BC, Canada. 604-
501-5566 | www.surrey.ca/artgallery

GALLERY HOURS (until mid-July 2014): Tues-Thur 9am-9pm | Fri 9am-5pm |

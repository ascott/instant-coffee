EIGHT x EIGHT: A Preview To Kwantlen Polytechnic Universitys Fine Arts Graduation 
Show

The EIGHT x EIGHT exhibition serves to show its viewers what artists outside the city 
centre of Vancouver, specifically Fine Arts students from Kwantlen Polytechnic 
University, are creating in their respective art practices. Featuring the work of both 
current and graduated students of Kwantlen Polytechnic Universitys Fine Arts program, 
EIGHT x EIGHT will operate as a preview in anticipation to the Fine Arts graduation 
show of 2018. Please join us for the opening on December 7th at 7pm at the Gam 
Gallery (110 E Hastings) to explore the work of these artists and their individual 
practices.

Instant Coffee and Lotte Van den Audenaeren [LVDA]
(Vancouver Brussels Exchange Project)

PART 1 Vancouver
@ Instant Coffee's Store Front Studio 441 Powell St. Vancouver, Canada.

Jan 24th 3 to 7pm 

I SENT YOU
Collected and transported by LVDA

A multiple exhibition with art works by Brussels based artists Michel 
Francois, Ann Veronica Janssens & Richard Venlet and LVDA. The artists 
were asked to deliver / donate / formulate a message to an unknown 
audience.

The project contains posters, facsimile prints, art books, video work and 
sculptures that have been published or distributed within another exhibition 
context. The work is free to move and travel; one is not able to follow the 
course of the artwork once it exits the exhibition context. The work is public 
and becomes common property. LVDA functions as the transporter / 
messenger / accomplice / executor.

Michel Francois' facsimile print shows the image of a pair of sandals used by 
a Mexican citizen attempting to cross the US border. On her journey to 
Vancouver, LVDA crossed the US and Mexican border with these sandals: an 
assignment & responsibility given to her by the artist. These sandals are
included in the exhibition.

Ann Veronica Janssens offers a piece without materiality: Phosphenes is a 
nomadic and visual proposal.

Richard Venlet, A page from his book becomes a multiple sculpture.

LVDA PROJECTS: During her stay in Vancouver, LVDA will work on small 
site specific gestures & multiples. Some pieces will arrive or will be sent back 
to Brussels by mail (no new messages).

PART 2 Brussels:
Instant Coffee, in progress.

Instant Coffee will curate an exhibition of instructions in Brussels in the fall of 
2013. The concept is to commission a select group of Vancouver artists to 
make instructions for producing an artwork. The exhibition will only feature the
instructions in which each artist will produce as aesthetic pieces in their own 
right varying in media from posters and paintings to video and performance.

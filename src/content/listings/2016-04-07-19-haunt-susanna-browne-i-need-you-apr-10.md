Sunday, April 10, 12-5pm
3729 Glen Drive
(entrance in yard)

HAUNT is pleased to present I Need You, a solo exhibition by
Vancouver-based artist Susanna Browne. The site-specific installation
features new sculpture and video work made in the interest of considering
the tropes of desire and the performance of romantic love. Predictable
representations of adorationa bouquet of flowers, the flickering light of
candles and a love songdevise a scene of yearning and infatuation that
indulges in overt sentimentality. Browne creates an appeal to devoted
attention, staging love props and cues from the spectacle of longing,
allowing moments of celebrity and material worship to collide in a
narrative of faith, delusion, and decay. From the substructural domestic
setting of HAUNT, a wood-panelled basement, the stars of I Need Youthe
actors and diamondsbrush the boundaries of escapist fantasy and nostalgic
mistranslation.

Curated by Jenn Jackson.

Susanna Browne (b. 1987) is a Canadian artist based in Vancouver. She holds
a BFA from Emily Carr University of Art + Design and an MFA from Piet Zwart
Institute. Through her mining of pop culture tropes, Browne's
multi-disciplinary practice confronts personal and public histories of
longing with both humour and sincerity. Calling on the sentimentality of
everyday emotional languages, Browne conflates subversion with infatuation,
skepticism with desire. Her work has been exhibited in Germany, the
Netherlands, Sweden, and Canada.

Griffin Art Projects, 1174 Welch St, North Vancouver, BC V7P 2R5

www.griffinartprojects.ca

2:00pm Artist talk with Sara-Jeanne Bourget

Join artist Sara-Jeanne Bourget to see the work she has made while in residence at
Griffin, and for an intimate artist talk in the studio. Bourget is the inaugural recipient of
the Studio Residency Award, a new partnership between Griffin Art Projects and Emily
Carr University. Continuing from her recent thesis project at Emily Carr University of Art
and Design, Bourgets large scale works explore physical and conceptual
interconnections between Rocks, Paper, and Charcoal, based on [her] geological
explorations and the inherent materiality of both paper and the artists own handmade
charcoal.

Sara-Jeanne
Bourget was born in Lvis, Qubec in 1988. She obtained her BFA in
Studio Arts at Concordia University (2015) and her MFA from Emily Carr University
(2019). In 2016, she took part in residency programs in Finland, Quebec and Vermont
where she also exhibited her work. She is the recipient of two grants (2017 & 2019) from
the Elizabeth Greenshields Foundation in support of her work and studies.

SQUID LAUNCH OF NEW WEB SITE AND SUMMER READING! 

www.squidproject.net  
[Please observe the new URL] 

- - - 
SQUID is an online archive of texts written by professionals from the cultural field, but also a 
context to use for live arrangements such as public events.  
- - - 

We are proud to present the new SQUID website. The layout makes it easy to navigate, various 
functions have been improved and you can now search the texts by theme, author, or in 
chronological order. 
Furthermore, we would like to announce that Martijn van Berkum (NL) has joined as project 
manager on a regular basis. Thanks to this cooperation, the weblog Point of View has become a 
vital part of the SQUID project. Please have a look at the diverse and interesting posts 
at: www.point-view.net	
For more information, please visit: www.squidproject.net/english/about.htm 

We warmly invite you to browse the new website!

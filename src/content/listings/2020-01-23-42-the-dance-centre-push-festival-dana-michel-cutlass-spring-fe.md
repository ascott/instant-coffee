The Dance
Centre and PuSh International Performing Arts Festival present
Dana Michel
CUTLASS SPRING
February 6-8, 2020 | 8pm
Scotiabank Dance Centre, 677 Davie St, Vancouver

The audacious works of Montreal-based Dana Michel have earned international
attention and prestigious awards. Her daring choreographies reject stereotypical
physicality and identity, and create a liberated world of shape-shifting metamorphosis,
incorporating elements ranging from sculpture and comedy, to psychology and social
commentary. Her latest creation, CUTLASS SPRING, delves into the mystery of the
sexual self, exploring how cultural repression and her life as a performer, a mother, and
a lover have shaped her sexual identity.

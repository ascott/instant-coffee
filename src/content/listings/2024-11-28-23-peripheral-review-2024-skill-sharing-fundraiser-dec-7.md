From Saturday November 23 " Saturday December 7, 2024, Peripheral Review will
host our third annual online fundraiser, with a new intention! Support PR by bidding
on auction items of SERVICES, SKILLS & MORE from Canadian artists,
organizations, collectives, and individuals! The full line up will be revealed here on
November 23rd.

on an item or buy a membership (or two!) to support PR!

Are you an artist with a body of work to exhibit? Or, do you have a vision for a compelling
community group installation? Wed love to hear from you!

Gibsons Public Art Gallery (GPAG), on the beautiful Sunshine Coast BC, is accepting
submissions for our 2022 exhibition season. Get your ideas in to the Gallery,
digitally, by
April 30, 2021

GPAG presents exhibitions in two gallery spaces, the Main Gallery and the Eve Smart
Gallery. Our curatorial team is committed to providing public access to the best exhibitions
we can offer, from artists both on and off the Sunshine Coast. We support and encourage
diversity and our mission is to present exhibitions for the purpose of promoting and
encouraging public interest and educational opportunities in contemporary art. Check us out
at www.gpag.ca

Deadline: April 30, 2021
Visit [http://www.gpag.ca/submissions](http://www.gpag.ca/submissions) for further information.

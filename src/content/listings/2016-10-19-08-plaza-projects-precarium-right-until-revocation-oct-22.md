Precarium - Right Until Revocation

Opening October 22nd 6 - 9 PM

October 22nd - November 25th 2016

Plaza Projects presents its inaugural exhibition curated by 
7:30assemble, Precarium - Right Until Revocation, curated by six 
emerging curators and fifteen artists. Investigating the possibilities 
for a cultural space within the arcades of a mall. The curatorial 
collective has chosen to ask the artists to respond to both the 
temporary nature of Plaza Projects, a space made available for the 
pursuit of artistic and cultural endeavours for the duration of a year, 
and their own precarity. Embracing instability, the exhibition will 
change and evolve reflecting changes to circumstances of the 
artists.The exhibition, curators, artists, and their works emerge from 
and address these conditions of uncertainty, both implicitly and 
explicitly, whether it is through political, economical, cultural, or 
social issues that contemporaneity is entangled in today.

Plaza Projects is a non-profit art space in Aberdeen Square mall 
that aims to shift a site of consumption to a site of dialogue. We are 
dedicated to the dissemination, discussion, and realization of 
contemporary art. Exhibitions, talks, events, and other projects are 
developed with artists, providing space for experimentation, 
research, and practice. Bringing about new modes of interactions in 
the context of the mall.

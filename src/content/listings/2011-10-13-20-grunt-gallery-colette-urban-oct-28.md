Grunt gallery is please to present video and digital stills of works by noted, 
Newfoundland based artist Colette Urban. The exhibition will feature a single 
channel video loop, digital still, and costume from Urban's performance video 
HOOT; as well as still images and costume from her project "Limited 
Possession".

HOOT is a performance that is video taped in a reflecting pool. The costume is 
made up of reflective foil and a mask made of cotton and metallic discs. The 
activity in the performance is the disruption of the water's surface with my 
distorted reflection in the pool. The audio used in the performance is the reading 
Spinning Song from an audio recording of Edith Sitwell from 1954.

Limited Possession is a performance-based project that was documented for an 
exhibition of still photographs in 2009-2010. The performance was documented 
in a storage facility in the community of McIvers, Newfoundland. This project 
includes the production of a 2012 signed edition calendar with images taken in 
the original photographic sessions. The gallery installation will also include a 
slightly larger than life size photographic sculptural cut out of the performer 
constructed of honeycomb aluminum.

Performance artist Colette Urban uses her own body as an almost kinetic 
sculpture to explore themes of identity and social convention. Born in Denver, 
Colorado, she immigrated to Canada in 1973 where Urban studied and 
established her career. She began spending summers in Newfoundland in 1993. 
In 2007, she moved full time to Newfoundland in 2007 and established the Full 
Tilt Creative Centre in an abandoned chicken farm.

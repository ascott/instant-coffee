SOMETHING IN THE LIVING ROOM (https://www.greencollege.ubc.ca/green-
college-players-theatrical-performance-something-living-room) 
Written by Kavelina Torres
Directed by Mathieu Aubin
(both Resident Members of Green College)

The Great Hall, Graham House, Green College, UBC

6201 Cecil Green Park Road, Vancouver BC V6T 1Z1
April 12 - 14, 8:00 pm
Admission is by donation.

When Cora Alice, a hired assassin, shows up to the wrong address for a job, things 
get messy, fast. Juggling chatty captives, her inept mob boss, and an unexpected 
visitor from her past, can Cora Alice clean house? Or will she wind up in the Big 
House? Come see Something in the Living Room to find out!

The play is written by Kavelina Torres and directed by Mathieu Aubin. The stage 
manager is Brynn Williams. The cast includes Green College Resident Members, 
Gina Hsu, Corey Liu, Lucy De Souza, Corey Cerovsek, Kyrie Vermette, Maria Keka 
Guerrero, Brynn Williams, Marc Fawcett-Atkinson, Wesley Yocom, Green College 
Society Member, Brent Holmes, and UBC student, Sharon Engbrecht.

In conjunction with the Green College Outreach Committee, donations will be 
accepted for Insite.

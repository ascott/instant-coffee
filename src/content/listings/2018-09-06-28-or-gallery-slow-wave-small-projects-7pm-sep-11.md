Slow Wave Small Projects is a one-week intensive program on Mayne Island, Coast Salish 
Territories that re-imagines approaches to learning and art-making from feminist, activist, 
and land-based perspectives. The program strives to centre the practices of artists who are 
low-income, disabled, queer, trans, two-spirit, Black, Indigenous, or racialized.

In August 2018, twelve participants camped at the Old Dogwood Society for Arts and 
Ecology, taking part in discussions, reading groups, bodywork, ceremony, dance parties, 
ocean explorations, plant walks, and other experiences facilitated by Agniezska Forfa, Alize 
Zorlutuna, Anne Riley, Aretha Aoki, Cease Wyss, John Aitken, Julia Aoki, and Marie Weeks. 
Healthy and ethically-minded meals cooked by noaa edwards and Sorrel Mommi kept 
everyone nourished through the week. Participants also created their own unique and 
autonomous projects and programming. 

Please join us at Or Gallery as we reflect upon this time together, share some of our 
experiences, and discuss how the spirit and intention of Slow Wave Small Projects may 
extend beyond Mayne Island and continue in possible future iterations.

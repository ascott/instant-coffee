Landscapes, Let Go

Landscape Paintings by Liz Toohey-Wiese

Opening Reception: Thursday December 12, 7-9 pm

Exhibition runs December 12-27, contact the artist directly for additional viewings at
ltooheywiese@gmail.com

Duplex Artist Society, 1889 Franklin St.

Opening reception will use alley entrance

In this new body of work, Landscapes, Let Go depicts a number of environments
across so-called British Columbia, playing with tensions between fantasy and reality,
stability and ephemerality. Recent work in this exhibition depict landscapes from
Northern BC, where
multiple sites get collaged into the same composition. The
transience of travelling through places where one visits temporarily, but doesnt stay,
becomes a metaphor for the types of letting go we are being asked to do in the midst
of the climate crisis.

So how do landscape paintings like Toohey-Wiese's shape, enhance, or distort our
attitudes toward nature and wilderness? Does it even really matter, can't we just
enjoy the painting?

Exhibition essays by Sean Alward and Hannah Tollefson

Artist Website: www.liztoohey-wiese.com

As part of CICA Vancouvers exhibition Clouds for Breakfast by Daisy Parris, we are
hosting a special sound bath meditation session facilitated by Corus, a Xisla &
Kitasoo Xaixais sound artist. This event will create an immersive and contemplative
space where sound and contemporary abstract art converge, offering participants a
deep sensory experience of Parris work.

This sound bath meditation is designed to expand the ways in which participants
engage with Parris paintings. Through harmonic vibrations and immersive
soundscapes, Corus will guide attendees through deep listening and reflection,
inviting them to connect with the emotional resonance of color, texture, and
movement within the works.

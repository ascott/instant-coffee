BEN RAYMER

Wobably

14 October - 4 November 2010

Opening Reception 14 October, 7pm

Curated by Jenn Jackson

///////////////////////////////////////////

Lucky's Gallery is pleased to present the forthcoming exhibition Wobably by Vancouver artist Ben Raymer. An equation of abstract annunciations, Wobably indulges in the re-presentation of feelings: those thoughts, in/tangible things and in-betweens. Thematically extracted from a memoir of everyday experience, Raymerâ??s punctuations insist on illuminating the imaginative quality of viewership. Balanced between variables of installation, sculpture and painting, the display of abstractive compositions repeal notions of absolute totality. Encouraging a playful drift away from succinct historical and acute archival desire, Raymer affords a platform for interpretative folly. French fries, fingers, fabric folds and phallic formations simultaneously occupy a linear periphery, flip-flopping from one form to another. Between wobbly definition and a probable assumption, Wobably suggests a departure from square systems and level logic, formally materializing a semblance of fictional facts.

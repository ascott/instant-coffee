DEC 6 & 7 | WESTERN FRONT HOLIDAY FUNDRAISER

TOQUE: Western Front Annual Fundraiser and Craft Sale
303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver
Friday Dec 6th, 6-10pm
Saturday Dec 7th, 11am-7pm

The annual fundraiser and craft sale returns for the Western Front’s 40th Anniversary with an exciting showcase of artists, designers and artisans. TOQUE offers a unique selection of vendors with goods ranging from publications, textiles and ceramics to jewellery and tasty treats. Vancouver-based artist Arvo Leo is designing this year’s limited-edition tea towels, available for sale at the event.

Renowned as one of Vancouver’s most well-curated craft sales, TOQUE has been cited as ‘the most artful of all the artisan fairs’ (Georgia Straight) and ‘a true artists’ fair with a solidly local feel’ (Scout Magazine). It is a craft sale unlike any other and the event to do your holiday shopping, with gifts from $5 to $500.

The event kicks off Friday evening with celebratory cocktails, a back deck barbecue and fantastic raffle prizes. The event continues Saturday with new extended hours.
As part of our annual holiday membership drive, one lucky member will take home a brand new bicycle from Whoa! Nellie Bikes. So become a member of Western Front by December 7 for your chance to win!

Proceeds from TOQUE directly support Western Front’s year-round programming in Exhibitions, New Music and Media Arts.

For more information visit toque2013.tumblr.com or contact admin@front.bc.ca.

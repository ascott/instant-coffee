W2 Creative Technology Summer Camps
W2 presents the W2 Creative Technology Summer Camps for children and teens
ages 9-13 and 14-18, led by established artists, musicians and designers at
Vancouver's historic Woodward's complex. This year W2 is offering three
camps, each with it's own unique programming stream: digital media,
traditional media, and DJ camp. The camp's primary focus is to engage
children and teens with local artists as an exploration of artistic practice.

Workshops will be facilitated by local artists, designers and musicians:
Indigo - Stenciling
DJ Betti Forde - DJ Skills
Warren Fulton - Letterpress
Jen Castro - Photoshop
Robin Dutta - Photography
Cami Rush - Fashion
Perry Miotto - Film Making

W2 camps are innovative, affordable, and priced to encourage families in
the inner-city to be involved. Camps run Monday to Friday, 9:30am - 5pm
with a healthy lunch served at noon in the W2 Media Cafe. Special dietary
requests must be made in advanced. No experience is necessary, we cater to
any young people who show an interest in learning.

To sign up email: education@creativetechnology.org

Lecture by photojournalist Farah Nosh

Monday, November 29th

9:30 am 11:30 am

Emily Carr University

Lecture Theatre 301, South Building

1399 Johnston Street, Vancouver

Farah Nosh, an award-winning photographer based in Vancouver will be
talking about her work at Emily Carr University. Farah has worked in Iraq,
Afghanistan, Lebanon, Pakistan, Syria, and Egypt. In 2007 she won the
Overseas Press Club of America 1st Place, Feature Photography for TIME
Magazine. Her work has appeared in The New York Times, Time Magazine, and
Newsweek. In 2006, Nosh was featured on CNN's Inside the Middle East
segment titled, Someone You Should Know,".

In September of 2002, Nosh moved to Iraq for 11 months where she was one of
the few western freelance photographers working in Baghdad under the regime
of Saddam Hussein. She has returned to Iraq repeatedly, covering both the
civilian side and embedding with American military forces. In 2006, she
covertly moved around Baghdad for her award-winning work on brutally
wounded Iraqis and intimate daily life in the war torn country. Later that
year, Nosh covered the war in Lebanon. Then in 2008, Nosh began coverage of
the Iraqi refugees crisis in Damascus. Her projects in British Columbia
have included a large-format photography project documenting the last of
the fluent speakers of the Haida language in Haida Gwaii and a recent
project in the Downtown Eastside.

Farah has taught in the photography department during the Summer Program at
ECU and her work has been exhibited in galleries in the USA, UK and UAE.

The lecture is free and open to the public

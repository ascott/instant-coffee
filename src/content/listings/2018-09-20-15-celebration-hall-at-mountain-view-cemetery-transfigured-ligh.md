TRANSFIGURED LIGHT: PROVERB

Thursday, September 27, 2018 at 7:30 PM
Celebration Hall at Mountain View Cemetery
5455 Fraser St., Vancouver BC
Tickets $30/$20 @ brownpapertickets.com or at the door
A co-presentation of Arkora and Redshift Music

On Thursday September 27th, Arkora returns to Mountain View Cemetery to perform 
Transfigured Light: Proverb, an evening of ancient, contemporary, and world premiere choral 
chamber works. The concert pairs motets by Protin and Vicentino with new works by 
composers Fay Wang, Tova Kardonne, Jonathan Wild, Noah Meites, and Benton Roark, 
culminating in a rare Vancouver performance of Steve Reich's Proverb for sopranos, tenors, 
vibraphones, and organs. 

Arkora is a 15-piece vocal chamber ensemble consisting of eight voices, electric guitar, 
violin, cello, keyboards, and percussion, including hand-built microtonal instruments. 

Featuring: 
Jane Long and Alicia Hansen, sopranos
Hilary Ison and Debi Wong, altos
Noah Horn and Taka Shimojima, tenors
Tyler Simpson and David McCune, basses

Jae-Won Bang, violin
Rebecca Wenham, cello
Brendon Randall-Myers, electric guitar
Miranda Wong, keyboards
Daniel Morphy and Jonathan Allen, percussion
Fay Wang, vocalist

Kathleen Allan, music director
Jeff Harrison, lighting design

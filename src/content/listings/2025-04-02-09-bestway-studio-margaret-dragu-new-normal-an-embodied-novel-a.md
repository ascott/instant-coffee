Date: April. 3rd

Time: doors 6:30, screening 7pm

Location: Bestway Studio (21 E. Pender)

Tickets: free

C.L.A.M. is pleased to host the iconic and dear Margaret Dragu for the first full
screening of her 13 part series New Normal: an embodied novel, a multi-modal
project documenting her experiences on public transit while waiting for/recovering
from two hip replacement surgeries. This series of poetic-prose stories are
articulated in the body as short videos produced by Dragu alongside her
collaborators, Justine A. Chambers (choreography) and E. Kage (score), as well as
in text in her publication by the same name.

We will be screening all 13 shorts (about an hour in duration), before a short chat
with the artist. New Normal publications will be available for purchase, along with
light snacks and drinks.

About the artist: Margaret Dragu was the recipient of the Governor Generals Award
for Visual and Media Arts in 2012, minence Grise (2012) for 7a*11d, and in 2000,
the first artist in FADO's publication series Canadian Performance Art Legends.
Dragu works in video, installation, web/analogue publication & performance.
Spanning relational, durational, interventionist and community-based practices, she
has shown in Canada, USA and Europe.

About CLAM:

C.L.A.M. (cinema, literature, art, and media) is a seasonal event which blends short
films, live performances, and experimental collaborations with local artists, writers
and chefs. It is organized and curated by Yasmine Whaley-Kalaora, with gracious
venue support from Elisha Burrows.

Accessibility:

Bestway is located on the second floor and is only accessible by a flight of stairs. All
videos being screened have audio description and Closed Captions written and
performed by Danielle Wensley.

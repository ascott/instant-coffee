Call for videos (max 3 min)

Submission deadline : January 15, 2010
Exhibition : Sang Neuf // Young
Blood, February 27, 2010
As part of the Montreal All-Nighter of the MONTREAL HIGH LIGHTS Festival

Les Territoires invites all artists under 35 to submit short videos for the exhibition Sang Neuf // 
Young Blood, which will be presented for one night only on February 27, 2010 in conjunction with 
Nuit Blanche à Montréal.

Les Territoires will select about ten to fifteen single-channel works that will be playing in the
gallery space during the winter event. Videos selected will loop and be presented through one 
large format projection. This exhibition offers young local and international artists the opportunity 
to show independent and/or experimental video works. There are no restrictions of theme or on 
the number of works that one artist can submit. Each single-channel work submitted must not 
exceed 3 minutes.

The gallery will contact the selected artists about two weeks following the deadline.

SUBMISSIONS SHOULD INCLUDE :
• an artist statement (1 page maximum)
• a curriculum vitae (3 pages maximum)
• A 720 x 480 Quicktime NTSC (high resolution) please send high resolution QuickTime files. If 
chosen, your original submission will be used when compiling a DVD for the exhibition.
• A self-addressed, postage-paid envelope for return of materials if you wish them to be returned
(Les Territoires will dispose of non-claimed materials.)

MAIL TO :
Les Territoires / Submission for exhibition
372 Ste-Catherine O. suite 323
Montreal, QC. H3B 1A2 Canada

For more information: info@lesterritoires.org

Les Territoires is a non-subsidized and non-profit gallery whose mandate is to support emerging 
artists, present professionally curated projects and festivals.

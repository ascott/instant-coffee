Concert at the Belkin
with UBC Contemporary Players
Friday, April 6, 2018 at 2 pm

MORRIS AND HELEN BELKIN ART GALLERY
belkin.ubc.ca

Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery
The University of British Columbia 
1825 Main Mall, Vancouver, BC

Once again, we are pleased to welcome the UBC Contemporary Players to the 
Belkin Art Gallery for a concert inspired by the exhibition Beginning with the 
Seventies: GLUT. Directed by UBC School of Music faculty Drs. Corey Hamm and 
Paolo Bortolussi, the UBC Contemporary Players ensemble includes graduate and 
undergraduate students focusing on music and performance of our time. Programs 
blend masterworks by internationally acclaimed composers with exciting world 
premieres of works written expressly for the ensemble by UBC composition majors.

For this concert, School of Music composer Mariah Mennie will work with the UBC 
Contemporary Players in conjunction with the final Readers Respondevent as part of 
Alexandra Bischoffs project, Rereading Room. Mennie will compose a work that 
incorporates her own reactions to the exhibition, as well as the performers reactions 
in real time, through guided improvisation passages. The program will also feature 
Lied by Vancouver New Music artistic director Giorgio Magnanensi, which similarly 
involves directed improvisatory passages combined with a richly enveloping 
electronic track.

Admission is free and all are welcome.

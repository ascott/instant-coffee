Come celebrate Access Gallery's big move!

We are pleased to announce that effective 1 April 2010, Access Gallery will
relocate to 437 West Hastings St in the heart of downtown Vancouver!

As moving is a major endeavor, we need all the help we can get. Don't
worry, we aren't going to ask you to lift boxes! Rather we would like to
sit down and enjoy a delicious meal with you and chat about the gallery's
past and future. Our current next-door neighbour, the Irish Heather, is
kindly hosting a dinner in support of Access, and we would like to see you
there!

Please join us on Thursday March 25th at 8PM for a delicious meal and a
drink, a slide show of memorable moments at 206 Carrall St, and more
detailed announcements of the move!

For only $20 per person, as part of their long table dinner series, Irish
Heather offers a drink and a gourmet meal featuring sustainably harvested
freshwater fish or a vegetarian pasta. Proceeds help Access fund its
relocation and renovation of the new space.

The menu options are:

Trout Saffron Risotto with Almond Green Beans

or

Herb Gnocchi With White Onion Puree, Butternut Squash & Shaved Parmesan

Dining at a 40ft communal table, you will enjoy great conversations and
amazing food! Guaranteed!

To reserve your place, please email access@vaarc.ca with your name, phone
#, and meal choice. Seating is limited. We recommend early registration.

For more information of the Long Table Dinner Series please see
http://ltsmenu.blogspot.com/ and

http://www.vanmag.com/Restaurants/The_Next_Course

Also note: we will have Life After Doomsday drink coasters at the dinner!

Hope to see you there!

SCall for Performance Art Videos
LIFT gallery at Greensboro College is soliciting video of performance 
art for its grand opening show LIFTOFF. Emerging Artists should send 
videos documenting performance art on any theme by October 22. 
Videos will be juried by a panel of Art, Communications and Theater 
faculty and students. The videos selected for the show will be exhibited 
at LIFT from November 9 through December 7 as well as added to our 
permanent, searchable online database for public viewing. Top three 
jury selections will receive $200 for first place, $100 for second place 
and $50 for third place. Your video should be 1 to 10 minutes in 
duration and have been completed in the past 5 years. Please send 
only one video per entry. No submission fee is required. Applicants will 
be notified of the jury's selection in an email by Wednesday, November 
7.

Video Submission
To submit videos in the LIFTOFF exhibition, please create a VIMEO 
account (www.vimeo.com) and upload your video using their services. 
Simply click on upload a video on the start screen, choose the video 
you want to submit and upload it. For best results go to compression 
guidelines on the right side of the upload screen for information. 
Upload at highest quality and size allowed by your account. On the 
next screen please title the work, include the date, location and a one-
paragraph description of the work.

Please send a link to your video in an email to theliftgallery@gmail.com 
when you finish your upload. Along with the link, provide a CV, artist's 
statement and description of the work as an attachment in pdf format. 
Please make sure that we are able to download and embed your video 
by going to me, my settings, videos on the VIMEO home page. 
Selection of a privacy setting is up to you, although if you are going to 
make the video private please provide the passcode to us in your 
email.
If the video is public, you might consider applying some sort of Creative 
Commons License to it, which you can do through the Advanced tab.

Permission
By sending LIFT gallery an email with the link to your video you are 
allowing LIFT gallery to download it and show it multiple times in the 
LIFT gallery space as well as embedding it on the LIFT gallery's 
searchable blog database for public view, and linking to it on social 
media sites as well as the Greensboro College website. LIFT gallery 
will not distribute or screen your videos in any other way. 

About LIFT gallery
LIFT gallery supports emerging artists working in performance, video 
and social practice art through exhibition and an online database of 
artists' work. The aim of the gallery is to promote scholarship of 
contemporary art through a collaborative process of engagement of 
students, faculty and the public. 
LIFT gallery is part of Greensboro College, a four-year, independent, 
coeducational liberal-arts college, also offering four master's degrees, 
located in Greensboro, North Carolina, United States, and affiliated 
with the United Methodist Church. It was founded in 1838.

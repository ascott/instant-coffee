Artist Talk with Jean Paul Langlois and Daina Warren at Fazakas Gallery

Saturday, November 29, 2025, 2-4 pm

Fazakas Gallery, 659 East Hastings Street, Vancouver BC

Fazakas Gallery is pleased to present an Artist Talk with exhibiting artist Jean Paul
Langlois and Indigenous art curator Daina Warren on Saturday, November 29, 2025,
from 2-4 pm.

The conversation will delve into Langlois solo exhibition War and Peace, and Smoky
the Cowhorse, on view at Fazakas Gallery until December 19, 2025. Based in East
Vancouver, Langlois explores the intersection of his Mtis and Scottish settler
heritages through vibrant pop-culture motifs and ultra-saturated colour schemes.
This exhibition brings together works from across his current projects, reflecting on
family history, personal mythology, and the complexity of identity.

Joining Langlois in conversation is Daina Warren, an Indigenous curator and artist
from the Montana Cree First Nation. Warren serves as the Executive Director of
Indigenous Initiatives at Emily Carr University of Art and Design and has built an
extensive career dedicated to supporting and promoting Indigenous artists across
North America.

This event offers an opportunity to hear directly from Langlois and Warren as they
discuss the narratives, histories, and creative processes that inform Langlois
practice. Fazakas Gallery invites guests to join us for an engaging and thought-
provoking conversation into how contemporary Indigenous art continues to
challenge, expand, and redefine cultural perspectives.

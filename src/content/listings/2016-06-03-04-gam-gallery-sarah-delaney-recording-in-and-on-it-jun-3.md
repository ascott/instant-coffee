Please join us for the opening reception of 'Recording In and On It' with
mixed media pieces by Sarah Delaney.

June 3 - June 25, 2016
Opening Reception: Friday, June 3, 8 pm

Sarah Delaney is a multi-disciplinary artist, working and living in
Vancouver, BC. She has her BFA in Visual Art from the University of
Victoria, and her Diploma of Interior Design from the Vancouver College of
Art and Design.

This exhibition aims to show the artist's personal relationship with
thought and action, memory and touch. Sarah Delaney uses gestures of
diverse motion, speed, scale and intent to steer the direction of the work
within the moment. Drawing in and off of the canvas surface, each mark made
becomes a sign of a tangible connection and departure. The work is made up
of a variety of small reactions that illustrate the artist's paths of
thought and process, and in turn creates a visual narrative. Sarah uses
drawing for it's fragile and temporal qualities, and equates it to our
relationship with the physical world. She is interested in the marks made
as interactions with out surroundings. This includes the tracks, prints,
and marks that we make as creatures of the human race, as well as the
natural elements that overtime alter the state of the world.

This exhibition is in conjunction with the Art Waste festival.

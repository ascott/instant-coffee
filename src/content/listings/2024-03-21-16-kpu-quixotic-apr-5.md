Opening Reception: Fri April 5 2024 6:00pm-9:00pm

University, Surrey Campus

12666 72 Avenue
Surrey, B.C. V3W 2M8

We are KPUs Bachelor of Fine Arts Graduating Class of 2024! We welcome everyone
to the opening of the Quixotic Exhibition at the KPU Surrey campus on Friday Apr 5.
Our works are installed at the Spruce Gallery, the Spruce Atrium, and the Arbutus
Gallery. Come look at art and hang out. There will be food and drinks!

Visit our Instagram page for the latest updates!

IG: @quix.ot.ic

The art show titled "Quixotic" signifies diverse and imaginative pursuits. Through their
art, the graduates challenge norms, explore visionary themes and

invite viewers to embark on imaginative
journeys, reflecting the essence of "Quixotic"
ideals in the realm of contemporary art.

The"Quixotic" art show marks the culmination of years of artistic development at KPU.
It showcases the journey of each graduating artist in creating works that reflect their
unique artistic practices. From inception to execution, these pieces

embody technical skill, conceptual depth, and personal growth.

At KPU, we live and work on traditional and unceded territories oft h e Kwantlen,
Musqueam, Katzie, Semiahmoo, Tsawwassen, Qayqayt and Kwikwetlem peoples

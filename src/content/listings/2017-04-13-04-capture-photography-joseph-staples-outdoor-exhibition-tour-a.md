Tour: 6:50pm-7:30pm
Reception: 7:30pm-8:30pm

Join a walking tour of Joseph Stapless outdoor exhibition, displayed on the 
storefront windows of businesses around the Gastown BIA. Often, collages are 
made from many different images that are used only once. For the Falun Series, 
Staples investigates if something lasting can be made by returning over and 
again to one series of images.

After the tour, there will be a post-tour reception at lululemon lab.

Tour is FREE and open to the public. RSVP required.
Please email: rsvp@capturephotofest.com

Tour particpants will meet at Capture's Office at
305 Cambie St, Vancouver.

lululemon lab is located at 50 Powell St, Vancouver.
Wine will be served at the reception courtesy of lululemon lab.

Accessibility: The tour will take place on public sidewalks. We will not be entering 
any of the businesses. However, some sidewalks in Gastown are paved with 
cobble stones and are uneven. The post-tour reception will be held at lululemon 
lab. Alcohol will be served, so the reception is 19+. We regret that due to 
ongoing maintenance work, this location will not be wheelchair accessible at the 
time of the event.

Joseph Staples' Falun Series is presented by Capture Photography Festival in 
partnership with the Gastown BIA.

About the Artist and Exhibition:

Steve Dynies Disco Lab series has evolved into an ongoing, personal exploration
that has grown in scale and meaning since its inception in 2022. Each of the series
thirteen large-scale interactive mirror structures is meticulously handcrafted. The
meditative, labour-intensive act of gluing each fragment offers a counterpoint to our
fast-paced world, inviting contemplation and presence and offering a creative outlet
for the artist to cope with PTSD experienced as a
working firefighter in the Lower
Mainland.

Steve Dynie is a multi-disciplinary artist living in Coquitlam, BC. His photographic
work has won numerous awards from several prestigious international photography
competitions. In recent years he has expanded his artistic practice to include large-
scale interactive mirror structures designed to invite viewer participation. Whether
through the lens of a camera or the reflective surfaces of his mirror structures,
Dynies work invites viewers to see the world in new, transformative ways.

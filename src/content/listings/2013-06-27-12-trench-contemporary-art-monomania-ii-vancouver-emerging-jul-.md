MONOMANIA II: Vancouver Emerging
June 20th-August 31st
Opening Night July 4th 6 pm- 9 pm

Craig Sibley and Trench Contemporary Art are pleased to announce 
MONOMANIA II: Vancouver Emerging. Presenting over 20 professional 
Vancouver-based artists from a variety of practises and mediums, the second 
bi-annual instalment of MONOMANIA is barometer of rising local talent. 
MONOMANIA, or the obsession with a single object/concept/idea, is cheekily 
adapted to mean an obsession with diversity multiplicity. What Trench is 
obsessing over is the astounding array of new talent happening in our city.

Exhibited in a salon style, the 1,000 square feet of Trench Gallery is 
transformed into a totalizing art experience. Each work and artist brings their 
own unique qualities and formulates a dialogue of inclusion and collaboration. 
MONOMANIA reaches beyond a singular theme or concept to present an 
invigorating array of artists living and creating in Vancouver.

The show begins Thursday June 20th, with the opening reception held on July 
4th.

Artists exhibited include,
Shelley Penfold, Tristan Unrau, Sean Mills, Lauren Marsden, Evan Broens, 
Tonik Wojtyra, Rebecca Donald, Janice Wu, DRIL, Mike Bourscheid, Jen 
Aitken, Sarah Gee, Gabriel Dubois, Ali Ahadi, Natalie Tan, Min Yoon, Ed 
Spence, Kuh Del Rosario, Patrick Cruz and more.

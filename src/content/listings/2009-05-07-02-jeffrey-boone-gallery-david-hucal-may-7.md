Please join David Hucal on May 7th, 6-8PM for the opening reception of his first solo show at the
gallery.

Choosing banal subject matter drawn from experience and memory of urban landscape, David Hucal 
traces the intersection between nature and its modification in relation to urban development. Devoid 
of the human figure but with its presence everywhere, Hucal's economically loose painting style 
suggests a fondness for a collocation often looked at more analytically in contemporary art. 
Fragments of nature and architecture share the frame compositionally purposeful but imply an 
aesthetic optimism.

JOB OPPORTUNITY

Gallery Manager
Centre A (Vancouver International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art) is a 
leading public art gallery with a mandate to present innovative exhibitions that 
develop the public appreciation of contemporary Asian art. Centre A promotes 
the work of Asian-Canadian artists, whilst also showcasing the work of 
leading artists from abroad. Maintaining high artistic, curatorial and scholarly 
standards, Centre A enjoys an international reputation for excellence and 
innovation. Centre A is looking to hire a highly capable Gallery Manager to 
join our team.
Reporting to the Executive Director, and working in co-ordination with the 
Curator and other gallery staff, the Gallery Manager is a key support position 
performing a range of administrative and visitor services tasks, ensuring the 
efficient functioning of the gallery.

Scope of the position:
The Gallery Manager plays a vital role in the day-to-day operations of the 
gallery. General tasks include opening and closing the gallery, providing visitor 
services, recruiting and co-ordinating volunteers, handling a range of requests 
including media enquiries, group tours and school visits. General 
administrative duties include mailing, banking, ordering supplies, and co-
ordinating gallery maintenance.
The Gallery Manager co-ordinates membership administration and 
communications, and is responsible for writing and issuing regular member 
newsletters, growing the membership and generating renewals. S/he will be 
an active participant in donor development and audience development 
initiatives. S/he will manage website content and Centre Aâ€™s social media.

Strong English language skills are essential to this role as the candidate will 
be required to undertake a variety of writing tasks including drafting press 
releases, newsletters, letters and reports to a range of stakeholders including 
government funders. Experience in grant writing would be an asset. Fluency in 
an Asian language would be an asset.
The Gallery Manager assists with the management of gallery events including 
opening receptions, donor events and venue rentals. S/he will play a key role 
in providing administrative support to Centre A's Fundraising Committee by 
managing the annual fundraising dinner and auction, and efficiently 
undertaking a range of event co-ordination tasks with a minimum of 
supervision.

Qualifications:
The ideal candidate for this position will have a degree in the visual arts, art 
administration, curatorial studies or communications, with a specific interest 
in contemporary Asian art. The candidate will have a minimum of two years 
experience in a small non-profit environment.

The candidate will be a self-starter with excellent interpersonal and 
communications skills, an ability to demonstrate initiative, but also exercise 
judgment, tact and discretion in this key support role. The candidate must be 
highly organized and detail oriented with the ability to multi-task and work in a 
fast-paced environment. S/he must be able to work independently and as part 
of a team.

The candidate must be able to work in a Microsoft Office environment with 
hands-on experience of both Mac and PC platforms. S/he must have 
familiarity with Excel and database applications. Centre A uses Giftworks, a 
database application for managing memberships and donations and Access 
for managing event ticket sales and receipting.

must be available to work Saturdays. From time to time the candidate will be 
required to work after hours to manage or assist with events. S/he will be 
compensated with time in lieu for any after hours work.

This is a full-time position, salary: $32 000 - $36 400 with benefits.

BLIM ART SHOW (October 2011)
Blim presents:
New Works
New Work by Andrea Tucker
Andrea Tucker is a self-taught Vancouver painter who has shown her work in 
Vancouver and internationally for over 10 years. Her usual medium is oil paint but 
this show at Blim will be different. Due to lack of space and stable living space 
she has decided to do a body of work of all ink and watercolour paintings. This is 
a refreshing and exciting change for her. This work still has her trademark 
creepy-cute characters and some other surprises of interesting subject matter. 
This is a show not to be missed as these pieces are sure to sell fast.
Call 604 872 8180 or info@blim.ca for more information.

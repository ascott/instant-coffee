Writers' Exchange is seeking committed and enthusiastic volunteer literacy mentors to 
help kids from inner-city neighbourhoods get excited about reading, writing, and their 
own potential. Volunteers are asked to commit 1.5 to 2 hours each week during our fall 
programs, beginning September 9th - December 20th.

Writers Exchange volunteer mentors help kids boost their literacy skills and self-esteem 
during super fun writing projects. Mentors help kids (ages 6 to 13) read picture books 
and graphic novels, encourage creativity during crafts and writing activities, and help 
kids create a story to include in their very own professionally published book. You dont 
need to plan the activities or worry about managing behaviour--a staff programmer will 
be there to support you 100% of the time. Just show up ready to have fun and cheer on 
the kids.

We love working with volunteers from all backgrounds. This is an especially great 
volunteering opportunity if youre thinking about becoming a teacher, social worker, child 
psychologist, or working with children or youth in any capacity and want to gain concrete 
skills, direct experience and hours working with kids. Its also a great way to make 
connections in your community and a meaningful opportunity to work with kids who 
need your support.

Please visit https://vancouverwe.com/volunteerfall2019 for more info and to fill out an 
application form before September 9th.

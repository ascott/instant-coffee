American Art Now
Works by Keegan Mchargue, Nicholas Pittman and Ry Fyan

May 8th - June 6th
Opening Reception Friday May 8th, 7 - 11pm
LES Gallery 
1879 Powell Street
Vancouver BC

A selection of gouache, acrylic and oil paintings of various themes, concepts and styles.

"... I guess we called it “American art now” partly as a diplomatic joke, but perhaps also because all 
three of us are very serious about making work that does not directly respond to current trends in 
American contemporary art. We are just trying to preserve our souls. Despite the ambitious title, all of 
the work in the show will be modest in scale, and though all three of us have different approaches, 
the thing we have in common is an attempt to provide an experience akin to a psychedelic one with
out referential tendencies: not an illustration of the trip but an actual trip itself.” - Nicholas Pittman

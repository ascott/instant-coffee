Call for Submissions

Artists in visual, performance, installation, new media / digital
arts, photography, video art, design,fashion and music are invited to
submit their work for an exciting curated exhibition Connecting the
Dots, presented during the Culture Days in Vancouver and 
showcased at
Five Sixty. Artists are invited to submit a work as a reflection on
their community, their city, and political, social, economical and
environmental aspects of everyday life. What defines our 
communities
and the art practice in Vancouver?

About the exhibition:

Connecting the Dots celebrates Vancouver`s rich and diverse 
cultural
and artistic character. It aims to catalyze the connection among the
public, communities, and artists with varied cultural background and
forms of artistic expression.

About the Culture Days:

Culture Days is a collaborative pan-Canadian volunteer movement to
raise the awareness, accessibility, participation and engagement by
all Canadians in the arts and cultural life of their communities.

How To Apply:

Please send us:
- Your resume/CV including your contact information: name, 
address,
telephone number and email.
- Artist statement (no more then 200 words).
- An image / visual sample, (a concept for performance arts) of your
work that you will present for the show. Please include size, medium,
year of each artwork and any specific instructions for its presentation.

Please send your submission to: connectingthe3dots@gmail.com

Artists selected for the show will be contacted during the first week
of September.

Deadline for entries : August 21, 2011

For more information and details, email

The Dance Centre presents the Global Dance Connections series
CO.ERASGA
Eternal Gestures
October 9 & 10, 2025 at 8pm
Scotiabank Dance Centre, 677 Davie St, Vancouver

Info and tickets: https://thedancecentre.ca/event/co-erasga-2025/

The Dance Centre presents a visionary world premiere from Co.ERASGA: Eternal
Gestures is a trilogy of evocative solo works commissioned from Indigenous Coast
Salish-based choreographers Starr Muranko, Michelle Olson and Margaret Grenier, and
performed by Artistic Director Alvin Erasga Tolentino. This poetic work uplifts the voices
of Indigenous women, asserts our deep connection to the land, and speaks to truth,
healing, and the decolonization of art.

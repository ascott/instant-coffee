Ceramics Handbuilding Workshop

August 10th 11am-12pm

Make your own unique pottery piece with us while supporting our space!

The James Black studio members present a hand building workshop fundraiser!

The funds from this workshop will go towards our new kiln that we have 80% of the
funds for.

The possibilities are endless with hand building -- make a mug, a ring dish, a bud
vase, a plate,

a pour over, little animals, or anything else you can imagine!

The session will be 1hr and we will provide the clay as well as guidance, tips, and
tricks!

You will be given 1lb of clay and will be able to keep up to two art pieces you make.

From there we will fire your work twice
in the kiln and you will be able to pick up your
finished

piece 3 weeks after the workshop. Pick up will be at our nonprofit art shop Pressure
Point

just across the street from the James Black Gallery. We will send an email to pick up
your

pieces to the email address you provide at registration.

HOME AND AWAY: A public artwork for Empire Fields and Plateau Park

Artist talk and Display

Thursday, July 16, 7pm
Vancouver Public Library, Central branch
Alma VanDusen/Peter Kaye Room, lower level, 350 W. Georgia
Free Admission

The Vancouver Public Library and City of Vancouver Public Art Program
present a talk by award-winning artists Annie Han and Daniel Mihalyo of
Lead Pencil Studio (Seattle). The artists will discuss their practice in
public art and architecture, including a major piece at the Canada/US
border crossing in Blaine, and talk about the inspiration for their new
artwork for Hastings Park, a unique grandstand called Home And Away.

We also invite you to meet the artists and the Public Art team at the
opening of the park on Saturday, July 18th, from 10 a.m. – 5 p.m. A model
of the artwork will be on display along with a model of Hastings Park.

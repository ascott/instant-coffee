Kate Metten: The Thinking Eye
Ryan Quast: Eleven Minutes Late
Mollie Burke: Unfolded

Opening Reception
Friday, October 18, 2019
7pm-11pm
258 East 1st Avenue

On Friday, October 18, three new art exhibitions will open at Burrard Arts Foundation
(BAF).

Two of the shows were produced by the latest participants in BAFs Residency Program,
Kate Metten and Ryan Quast. During the program, the two artists worked side-by-side in
the two studios at BAFs facility in the False Creek Flats. Also opening is new work from
Mollie Burke in BAF's Garage; this street facing exhibition window
displays art to the
public 24 hours a day and showcases early-career artists. The show opens on Friday
October 18th and runs until December 14th. The show is free to the public during BAF
Gallerys opening hours of Tuesday to Saturday, noon to five.

For these three very different exhibitions, the three artists have created works in
painting, sculpture, and installation according to their own unique practices.

Kate Metten's new body of work, 'The Thinking Eye', consists mainly of paintings,
influenced by Modernism and informed by her research into metaphysics, cognitive
science, and how the brain processes abstract images. Metten also drew on her recent
experience working with Virtual Reality in a residency at Emily Carr University of Art and
Design

For 'Eleven Minutes Late', Ryan Quast created a new series of paint sculptures. This
unique process, created by Quast, meticulously builds realistic three-dimensional
objects out of nothing but layers of dried paint. For this show, Quast
has departed from
the utilitarian objects he usually depicts and has made absurd, self-defeating objects the
subjects of the works.

'Unfolded', Mollie Burke's Garage installation, explores the role that visual perception
plays in our experience of reality by revealing the interplay between flatness and
physical space.
Using painting and textile installation, Burke will transform the Garage
into a space where the distinction between between image and object, and flatness and
dimensionality is softened.

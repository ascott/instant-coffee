for Applications | DEC 12
ALCHEMY is an artist-led international residency offered to make time and space for
participants to explore the intersection between creative and social practices in a
unique community setting.

Founded in 2015 on the Toronto
Islands, Alchemy's pillars are food, art, garden and table -- all inspired by the original
gardens and community at the Centre. This Residency is
designed for artists to focus on their practice in a respectful and supportive studio
environment. Participating artists are encouraged to cultivate new directions in their
work, experiment with process and materiality techniques, and direct their research,
activities, and time in collaboration with other visiting artists. Shared meals, studio
visits, and dialogue with community members and guest artists are all part of the
Residencys programming.

Gibraltar Point Centre for the Arts is a peaceful artist retreat nestled
against the
magnificent backdrop of Toronto Island - the largest car-free community in North
America and 15 minutes by ferry to Toronto's downtown core.

Cost:$1350.00 plus applicable taxes for accommodations, studio space,
programming, guest artist talks, six shared suppers and the opportunity to participate
in a closing open studio. Interested? Want to learn more? Visit us on instagram at
Alchemy Artists Residency. Details about this Residency, how and where to submit
your application can be found at www.makealchemy.com Questions? Please

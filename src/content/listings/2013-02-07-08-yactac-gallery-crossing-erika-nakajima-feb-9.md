Crossing - Erika Nakajima

February 9-16, 2013
Opening Reception: February 9, 3-7pm. 
Gallery Hours: February 10-15, 12-5pm; February 16 12-4pm. 
Artist in attendance.

Yactac Gallery is pleased to present "Crossing"; work by Erika Nakajima.

There is a sign of a human being around me.
I do not know who he is.
Because of its sign, I feel I am existing.
We are co-existing here and always crossing each other.
- Erika Nakajima

>>>>

Erika Nakajima (b. 1985, Gifu Pref., Japan) is a Japan based artist. She 
received her BFA from Tama Art University in Tokyo (2008). She has been 
exhibiting in several galleries in Japan, and recently participated in an Artist in 
Residence "Fronda" in Real del Monte, Mexico.

Weird Flux: BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating Exhibition

Exhibition runs: April 18-27 (closed April 19, 21, 22)

Gallery hours: 12-5PM

The Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory is pleased to present Weird Flux, the 
University of British Columbias 2019 BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating Exhibition. Embodying the 
contemporaneity of the graduating class, the exhibition, held at the Audain Art Centre, brings 
together ideas of celebration, boastfulness, change, growth and movement, while showcasing 
the diverse practices and accomplishments of this years cohort of 39 students.

This event is free and open to the public.

AHVA Gallery (rm. 1001) and Studio 3000
Audain Art Centre
6398 University Boulevard, UBC, Vancouver

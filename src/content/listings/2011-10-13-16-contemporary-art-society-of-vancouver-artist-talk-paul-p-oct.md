Public talk by Paris-based artist Paul P. co-sponsored by the Contemporary Art 
Society of Vancouver and the Simon Fraser University Gallery. The talk will take 
place Wednesday, October 19th at 7pm in room 7000, 515 West Hastings 
Street (at Richards), SFU Vancouver Campus. This event is free of charge. 
The artist's first exhibition in Vancouver will open Friday October 21st at Blanket 
Contemporary Art.

Paul P. makes sensitive painting and drawing studies, taking young men as his 
predominant subject matter. The figures in these intimate portraits appear as if 
from another time or place. Recent solo shows include Mark Selwyn Fine Arts, 
Los Angeles (2011), Daniel Reich Gallery, New York (2010), Thaddaeus Ropac, 
Salzburg (2009), Maureen Paley, London, 2008, and the Power Plant, Toronto 
(2007). Recent group shows include, The Art on Paper Biennial, Weatherspoon 
Art Museum (2010), Compass in Hand, MoMA (2009). His work was also shown 
at Compass: Drawings from the MoMA, Martin Gropius-Bau, Berlin, and the 
Freud Museum, London. Paul P.’s work is in the collections of MoMA, SFMoMA, 
the Brooklyn Museum, The Speed Museum and the Art Gallery of Ontario. Born 
in Canada in 1977, Paul P. lives and works in Paris.

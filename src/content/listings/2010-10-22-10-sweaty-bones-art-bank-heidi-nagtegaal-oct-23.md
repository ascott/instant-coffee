SWEATY BONES / ART BANK / HEIDI NAGTEGAAL / SATURDAY, OCTOBER 23, 2010
Heidi Nagtegaal launches SWEATY BONES - a 1 year project consisting of
monthly dance parties at the Artbank (run by Mandy Hardwick), where we'll
dance, explore moves, and experience art in a new way.
Social Practice Explosion! If you have a project you're dying to try out in
a non-traditional gallery space, please contact
heidinagtegaal@hotmail.com and send me a proposal.
October 23, 2010 features the last installation of Headbands and Bracelets
before November 5, 2010, where the project will end, with FUSE at the VAG.
Come get Headbanded for the first time, or the 500th time!

THE ARTBANK
1897 Powell Street
Vancouver, BC
9pm to 2am
Door by donation / Drinks in the back

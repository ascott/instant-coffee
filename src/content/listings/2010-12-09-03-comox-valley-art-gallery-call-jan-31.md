COMOX VALLEY ART GALLERY

Proposal Application Guidelines for Gallery Exhibitions

These guidelines are for artists wishing to exhibit in the Comox Valley Art
Gallery.

DEADLINE: January 31 of each year for the following exhibition year for the
Public Gallery and Arts & Craft
Gallery, which are programmed at least one year in advance. CVAG accepts
artist proposals throughout
the year and does not have a deadline for submissions for the other exhibit
spaces.

APPLICANTS ARE REQUESTED TO PROVIDE THE FOLLOWING:

2) Exhibition Proposal: (Maximum 300 words) Indicate which exhibition space
you are applying for:
Public & Window Galleries or Arts & Craft Gallery. We encourage you to read
our gallery mandates on our
website to choose the best exhibition space for your work. Please include a
title for the exhibition you are
proposing, if one is established. Please discuss the theme(s) of the
exhibit, what media the work is created
in and how you plan to use the space.

3) Artist Statement (for a group show, include one from each artist)

4) Resumé or Curriculum Vitae, including the applicants’ exhibiting history
beginning with the most recent.
(For a group show, include one from each artist)

5) 10 - 20 Images of artwork relevant to your proposal. (for a group show,
max. 20 images total)
Images must be of a professional quality to facilitate evaluation.
CD - Jpegs or Tiffs only, PC compatible.
Name each file with artist’s last name and title (or part of title).
DVD or VHS videotape - Must not exceed a running time of 15 minutes. Name
each file with artist’s
last name and title (or part of title). Label all videotapes with the
artist’s last name and title.

6) Image List of work submitted detailing Title, Year, Artists Name,
Materials, Dimensions in cm

7) S.A.S.E. - Self addressed stamped envelope, with sufficient potage for
the return of your proposal
materials. If you do not want your materials returned to you, please
indicate this clearly.

8) CD-ROM: Please include items 1-4 as .doc or .rtf files on a CD-ROM (with
your image files).

ADDRESS APPLICATIONS TO:
Curator, Comox Valley Art Gallery - 580 Duncan Avenue, Courtenay, BC, V9N 2M7

INCOMPLETE SUBMISSIONS WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED

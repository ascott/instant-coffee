Mistletoe Magic 2020 is the second online edition of this popular event. For 32 years, this
important event has raised much-needed funds for the Aird Gallery's year-round
programming (which includes 12 exhibitions per year), helped increase the gallery's
visibility provided exhibition opportunities for the participating artists and organizations, and
supported the Aird in continuing to be free to all. In 2019, the Aird Gallery relocated to
Artscape 906 Queen West from its original location in the Macdonald Block (at Bay
Wellesley) and will be returning in 2024-25. Impacting its capacity to program at the same
caliber.

This year has been challenging due to the
ongoing effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, the
Aird has not escaped financial hardship, and we are urgently seeking your support. Your
contribution through this year's fundraiser greatly supports the community of artists the Aird
continues to build. You can feel the joy of giving the most treasured gift of art by purchasing
unique pieces from our talented artists during this holiday season.

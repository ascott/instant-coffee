Open Call for Submissions for June Opening at The Phoenix Gallery, June 6 - 23, 2019

The Phoenix Gallery is an artist run centre on the DTES. We are putting together a final 
opening before the close for the summer season. We have a 1200 sq ft gallery space 
and are looking to integrate this programming as an open form; Form developed out of 
subsequent submissions to iterate this content specific to the programming derived from 
the call. 

Forms are completed; forms are submitted; forms are received. Form is the shape, 
visual appearance, or configuration of an object. In a wider sense, the form is the way 
something is or happens. New forms inspire action and cultivate new thought. 

We wish to invite any visual artists working in Vancouver to participate in our inaugural 
Open Forms exhibition. This call is open to all visual artists working in Vancouver in 
any medium that may engage with the concept of Open Form.

How to apply:

Please email send a brief introduction about your work along with five images and a 
proposal of artwork that you would like to exhibit to info@thephoenixgallery.ca by April 
15th. 

(Note any works exhibited must have been created within the last 2 years.)

Submission deadline is April 15th, 2019.

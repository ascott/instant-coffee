East FEAST Vancouver is accepting project grant proposals from artists or 
artist groups living or working in the Grandview-Woodland & Strathcona 
neighbourhoods

The EAST FEAST is a micro-funding social event that directly supports local 
artists who live or work in the neighbourhoods of Grandview-Woodland & 
Strathcona. Through ticket sales to this event, ticket holders will be served a 
beautiful meal while listening to the short listed artist project presentations. 
After the presentations the ticket holders will vote on their favourite 
presentation, and that artist group will receive the funds raised!

This FEAST is grassroots micro-funding at its best, relying on community 
engagement to be successful.

SUBMISSION DEADLINE: March 28, 2014

THE EVENT:

- 3 finalists present and compete for funding of their project at a community 
meal on June 1 2014

- Attendees vote for their favourite project

- A goal of 50 tickets will be sold at $20 per ticket (includes meal) with the 
object of collecting $1000 for the winning artist.

BENEFITS TO PARTICIPATING ARTISTS/ARTIST GROUPS:

- Up to $1000 in funding to the winner
- Where appropriate the Britannia Art Gallery's display case is available in 
June 2015 for the winning project Winning artist(s) to present at EAST FEAST 
2015 with artist update
- Honorariums of $100 to the two semi-finalists
- Promotion and networking opportunities through event presentation and on 
our project website

COMPLETE SUBMISSION INFO: [http://eastfeastvancouver.wordpress.com](http://eastfeastvancouver.wordpress.com)

East FEAST Vancouver is organized by
Britannia Community Services Centre Society and the Britannia Arts & Culture

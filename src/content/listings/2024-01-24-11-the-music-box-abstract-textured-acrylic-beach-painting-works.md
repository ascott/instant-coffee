Abstract
Textured Acrylic Beach Painting with Tracey Hayes

at the Music Box (Harmony Room), 1564 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver

Create an abstract beach painting using a palette knife, and master the art of building
texture. Participants will be using modelling paste and various viscosities of gel
medium and resin sand, layered with acrylic paint and highlighted with gold leaf and
resin to create a sunlit effect. Bring your own canvas, all other materials supplied. All
skill levels welcome, ages 14 and up. Cost: $50. Register by calling 604-925-7270
(course #158791)

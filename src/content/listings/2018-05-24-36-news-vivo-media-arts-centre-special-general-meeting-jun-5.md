Satellite Video Exchange

Society Special General Meeting
Tuesday, June 5, 2018 6pm
2625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver

Notice is hereby given that a Special General Meeting of Satellite Video Exchange 
Society will be held on Tuesday, June 5, 2018 at six oclock PM in the main studio of 
VIVO Media Arts Centre, 2625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver, BC, V5M 1Y8, Canada. 
This meeting is in advance of our Annual General Meeting, notice for which is 
forthcoming. 
The Agenda for this meeting will be as follows:

1. Approval of Agenda 
2. Vote on Special Resolution - bylaw amendment:

BE IT RESOLVED
THAT DIRECTORS & OFFICERS CLAUSE #8 WHICH STATES:
There shall be a Board of Directors (the Board) up to nine (9) Directors elected at 
the Annual General Meeting. The number of Directors sitting on the Board shall 
always be an odd number, not less than five (5).

SHALL BE AMENDED TO READ:
There shall be a Board of Directors (the Board) up to eleven (11) Directors elected at 
the Annual General Meeting. The number of Directors sitting on the Board shall 
always be an odd number, not less than five (5).

3. Adjournment

All members in good standing on the date of and 6 weeks prior to the meeting are 
authorized to vote on resolutions put forward at this SGM. Due to changes to the 
revised BC Societies Act, proxy voting is no longer permissible due to the absence 
in our existing by-laws. However, you may do so by telephone or other 
communications medium if all the persons participating in the meeting, whether by 
telephone, by other communications medium or in person, are able to communicate 
with each other. To arrange remote attendance please contact 
admin@vivomediaarts.com

Artist Salon and Open Studio with Jenie Gao

Thu, Mar 27 2025, 3 - 4:30pm

Thu, Apr 10 2025, 3 - 4:30pm

Thursdays March 27 and April 10, current Deer Lake Artist in Residence Jenie Gao
will welcome artists into their studio to share, connect, and seek feedback with each
other. This is a casual drop-in for artists specifically to have the chance to connect
with other artists. The Artist Salon and Studio is located in the Burnaby Art Gallery
Fireside Room.

Jenie Gao (they/she) is an artist specializing in printmaking, public art, social
practice and community storytelling. Gao runs an anti-gentrification arts business
and does consulting for cultural organizations and the public sector on equity and
ethics.

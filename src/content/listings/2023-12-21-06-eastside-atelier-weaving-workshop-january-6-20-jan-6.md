Eastside Atelier | Weaving Workshop | January 6-20
Beginner Rigid Heddle Weaving Workshop

Weave a scarf or two to three towels on a simple rigid heddle loom in this beginner
workshop with
teaching artist Amanda Wood. No previous weaving experience
needed.

Saturdays from 9:30-12:30, January 6-20
Eastside Atelier, 1310 William Street
More details and register through eventbrite: https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/learn-to-
weave-cloth-for-beginners-tickets-717838744007?aff=ebdsoporgprofile

Registration closes December 30.
ABOUT THE CLASS

Working with a neutral palette of silks, imported cotton and linen in a warm studio
atmosphere you will weave beautiful, textural cloth in a choose your own adventure
style.

The looms will be set up and prepared for you. You can choose to weave a longer
scarf, table runner or a few small towels. Amanda will help you choose textures,
design ideas and colour choices to make weaving simple and satisfying.

We'll begin with an introduction to the loom and materials, and a conversation about
what qualities you enjoy in cloth before getting started with weaving. You'll be offered
design suggestions and material recommendations and be able to use
Amanda's
sample library to see how your cloth might turn out.

The following two Saturdays will be dedicated and supportive weaving days. In all
you will be able to weave up to 2 yards of cloth which you can divide into towels or
keep whole as a scarf or table runner. Finishing techniques will be discussed with
sewn hem and non-sewn hem options.

Amanda's approach to weaving is grounded in technique but invites experimentation
and marks of the maker. This practice based approach is gentle and inclusive of all
levels of experience.

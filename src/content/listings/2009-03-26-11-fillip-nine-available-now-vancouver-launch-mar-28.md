FILLIP EVENTS:
Fillip Nine Available Now

Documentation of the distribution of the New York Times Special Edition.
Photograph by Conway Liao. Courtesy of Steve Lambert.

Projectile Publishing and Emily Carr University are pleased to announce the
official release of Fillip 9.

Following its recent series of events investigating art criticism and
critical judgment with Artspeak gallery, Vancouver, Fillip continues to
expand the space of contemporary art writing.

In our new issue Berlin-based writer Diedrich Diederichsen provides an in
depth discussion of Paul ValÃ&#65533;Â©ry and pop music, and critic Shepherd Steiner
considers the Martha Rosler Library project through the lens of the Boolean

The issue features long-form reviews of Chantal Akerman at the Camden Arts
Centre, Suddenly: Where We Live Now, Althea Thauberger's Carrall Street,
and WACK!, among others

Also included are conversations between Lea Feinstein and Christian L.
Frock on second wave feminism and last year's proliferation of feminist art
shows, and Boris Groys and Andro Wekua on art practice and production today
and in the former East Europe. Cliff Lauson completes the second in a
series of dialogues on the influence of 1960s and 1970s conceptual art on
Vancouver with Dan Graham, and Fillip interviews artist Steve Lambert on
the New York Times Special Edition.

We are very pleased to present a special audio project for Fillip 9, a
yellow vinyl 45 by artists Cranfield and Slade, which is included in each
copy of the magazine. The edition is produced in collaboration with the Or
Gallery, Vancouver, and in support of the artists' forthcoming album 12 Sun
Songs by the Or Gallery, Christoph Keller Editions, and JRP/Ringier.

To subscribe to Fillip, visit [http://fillip.ca/about/subscriptions](http://fillip.ca/about/subscriptions)

Vancouver Launch, March 28, 8pm
Join us for a launch of Fillip 9 at the Fillip offices in Vancouver on
March 28th at 8 pm. Please call 604 781 4417 for access. The launch follows
a final session in our ongoing Judgment and Contemporary Art Criticism
series at Artspeak at 1pm. Join us for an open discussion with critic Sven
Lutticken (Utrecht) on ways of navigating criticality within the spaces of
contemporary art writing.

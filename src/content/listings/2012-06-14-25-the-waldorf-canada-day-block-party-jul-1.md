The Waldorf, Yelp and the Street Food Vancouver Society present a 
day of Food, Live Music and Fun.

Join us on Sunday, July 1 for free and family-friendly event. Our first 
annual Canada Day Block Party will feature food from Vancouver's top 
food carts and live music programmed by After Dinner Mint's Joy 
Mullen. The event will take place outdoors in the Waldorf's Parking Lot.

Vancouver's top food carts will be on hand to roll out their summer 
menus for their largest gathering ever. This event is being produced in 
conjunction with the Street Food Vancouver Society. Participating food 
carts so far include La Brasserie, Soho Road, Tacofino, Off the Wagon, 
Re-Up, Juice Truck, Mom's Grilled Cheese, Didi's Greek, Street Meet 
and more to be announced.

The music portion of the event will be programmed by Joy Mullen, who 
organizes a weekly evening of live music and dancing at the Waldorf 
called After Dinner Mint. The entertainment will be diverse and features 
acts like Cris Derkson (Cree Nation electric Celloist), The Ballantynes 
(60s Soul and Rock n Roll), Bella Monde (Reggae), Myriam Parent 
(French Ukele), the Radii (60s Soul) and more.

This event will also mark the launch of The Waldorf Hotel's outdoor 
summer space, which includes a beer garden and a Fish Taco stand 
featuring a menu designed by the NUBA and Waldorf executive chef 
Cesar De la Parra.

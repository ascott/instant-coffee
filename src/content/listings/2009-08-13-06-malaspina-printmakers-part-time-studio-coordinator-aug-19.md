Malaspina Printmakers is Vancouver’s only artist run centre dedicated to printmaking. Located on 
Granville Island, Malaspina provides a co-operative printshop for experienced printmakers, 
courses and workshops at introductory and intermediate levels, an exhibition gallery devoted to 
print culture and a print consignment sales program for members. Currently approximately 100 
printmakers access the studio throughout the year renting space on a weekly, monthly or yearly 
basis. 

Malaspina is currently seeking a temporary part-time Studio Coordinator to maintain the studio in
a safe and healthy manner for the use of studio renters. This position is currently under review 
and as such is a temporary three-month contract at 12 hours/week that will run from the 
beginning of September to the end November 2009. The Studio Coordinator will report to the 
Executive Director.

Responsibilities:

ensure adequate levels of all Malaspina supplied materials for all printing areas
order supplies as necessary from established suppliers
maintain list of suppliers, research new suppliers and set up accounts
manage studio budget
perform routine maintenance and repairs to presses and other studio equipment
supervise the volunteer Studio Monitor
work with the Program Coordinator in managing internal sales of supplies, storage locker and 
drawer assignments, and stone rentals
assist the Executive Director and Program Coordinator in the review of portfolios of prospective 
renters to assess technical competence
provide technical guidance to renters on appropriate use of studio equipment and supplies
participate in tours for prospective renters, visitors and educational groups
recommend technical information relating to studio processes
maintain high standards of health and safety in the studio by keeping members apprised of 
health and safety issues

Qualifications:

previous experience as a studio technician
BFA or equivalent education/experience in printmaking
demonstrated knowledge of printmaking techniques
self-motivated and able to take initiative
ability to demonstrate techniques
WHIMIS, desirable but not necessary
problem solving skills and mechanical aptitude
excellent communication skills
ability to work with a wide variety of people
Salary: $15/hour for 12 hours/week

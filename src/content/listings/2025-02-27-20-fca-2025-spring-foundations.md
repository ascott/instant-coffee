Program | MAR 4

"Foundational art tools and skills are as important to a visual artist as grammar and
spelling are to writers; it's hard to write a compelling story with bad grammar, and it's
difficult to communicate visually without understanding colour theory or the principles
of design. But you dont need to go back to school to learn the skills you need.

The Federation of Canadian Artists' Foundations Program offers a structured yet
flexible approach to artistic development. Through practical, hands-on training, this
program provides a direct and enjoyable path to building essential skills. Once you
gain confidence in your foundational knowledge, the creative process becomes more
rewarding and successful.

This series consists of 10 classes, each taught by a different master instructor,
covering 10 fundamental concepts of visual art. These classes provide a high-level
overview of what every artist should
know"helping you assess your current skill set,
identify areas for growth, and strengthen your artistic foundation.

January 23 2014

Staging Economy: Adam Shaw & Logan Sturrock

Sunset Terrace

2028 Clark Dr.

8-11pm

~~~~~~

We went to the watching place
for entertainment.
we have instead
become something,
occupied,
managing house,
orchestrating.

Down on stage,
2 o'clock smoke
for untitled actor.
Meanwhile,
unbeknownst to guard,
computers cease
to be present.

Choir members
of utmost suspicion,
seen last operating dolly,
Now broken,
prop scotch tape no fix
pop can paper,
can it?

We are responsible hosts,
but for Pete's sake,
please,
entertain yourself, spectator.
Don't just stand there,
do not leave your valuables unattended,
and remember to log in.

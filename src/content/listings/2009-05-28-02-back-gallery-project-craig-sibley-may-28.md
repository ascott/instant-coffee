The Back Gallery Project is pleased to announce an upcoming solo sculpture exhibition with 
Craig Sibley.
Please join us for the artist's reception on Thursday, May 28, 2009 from 5 - 7 pm at:

306 Abbott Street buzz # 10
Third Floor
Vancouver, BC

Craig Sibley's abstract sculptures resemble nothing so much as continuous lines and forms drawn 
through space yet these works are so humble. Its material is utterly mundane as styrofoam a far 
departure as serious sculpture is often perceived with its daunting history of monumentality and 
such heavyweight materials as marble, steel and bronze. Yet Sibley has managed to make 
sculptures that command space, that's because Sibley's uses negative - or emptiness- as a 
medium with the potentiality as any other material.

Sibley does not simply make new and improved Minimalist works he also brings back some of the 
fun and freshness in this new work. 

For the past 15 years Craig Sibley has been making minimal sculptures from wood, steel and 
latex. For his first solo exhibit at Back Gallery Project featuring 5 sculptural works he has 
developed a body of work that is physical articulation. Making relevant sculptural forms from 
marginal, unlikely materials, he conveys the integrity of building in these works.

Craig Sibley received in 1995 his BA in Fine Arts from the University of Victoria at Okanagan 
University Collage, Kelowna, B.C. His works have been exhibited thought out British Columbia, 
New Zealand and most recently at the Richmond Art Gallery.

For more information please contact Monica Reyes at info@backgalleryproject.com

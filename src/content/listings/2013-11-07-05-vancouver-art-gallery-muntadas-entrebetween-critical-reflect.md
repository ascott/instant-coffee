About the exhibition

A pioneer in the fields of conceptual and media art, Antoni Muntadas' rigorous 
practice includes performance, video, photography, multi-media installations, 
publications, web-based projects and public art. Muntadas: Entre/Between, a 
survey exhibition of the artist's more than forty-year career, includes a 
selection of key works that manifest his ongoing exploration of the important 
political and social issues of our time.

Incorporating in-depth research and astute readings of cultural situations, 
Muntadas' incisive works have addressed ideas such as the relationship 
between public and private, the role of the media in transmitting ideas and 
information, and the complex dynamics of architecture and other social 
frameworks. From his early works of the 1970s that utilized the senses and

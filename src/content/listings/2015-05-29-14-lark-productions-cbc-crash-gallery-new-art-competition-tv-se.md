YOU ARE INVITED TO A LIVE TAPING OF CRASH GALLERY - A NEW ART COMPETITION
TELEVISION SERIES

Lark Productions and CBC have teamed up to bring you Crash Gallery – a
ground breaking new art competition series. Artists of all backgrounds will
step out of their comfort zone and create spontaneous art while competing
against their peers in front of a live voting audience.

We are looking for energetic art lovers and experts to fill the audience,
to cheer them on and ultimately decide who the victor of the evening will be.

This is your chance to see what creative cloth these artists are cut from
as you watch them go toe to toe in a mainstream network television event.
Watch them paint, sculpt, and perhaps even paper maché their way to the top
of this new art competition – and new TV series.

WHO:
Artists, Gallery Owners, Curators, Art Experts, Art Lovers and Aficionados!
We want your opinion. The LOUDER, THE BETTER. Must be 19 or older.

WHEN:
June 1 - June 5 - Evening
Appetizers, wine and beer will be served.

HOW:
Fill out this form to reserve your FREE spot for this new competition show:
https://docs.google.com/forms/d/15HAzUtU2LKY3E84lOcHsNPX-N0Gt4EEnljVTxoC9iMc/viewform?c=0&w=1&usp=mail_form_link
All audience members will be required to sign a release before they can get
in on the fun. You will be on camera. Large groups are encouraged to attend
and can register directly by emailing at crash.gallery@larkproductions.ca
instead of each person filling out a single form.

Once we receive your form, you'll be contacted to confirm your spot and
give you any additional information you may need. (studio location, dress
code, release forms, etc.) Your personal information will remain just that,
your personal information. We will not sell, publish, or share it in any
way with anyone.

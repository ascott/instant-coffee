CHAD MURRAY ENTERS THE SIGNATURE SERIES MARKET
WITH THE CHAD MURRAY SIGNATURE SERIES,
FEATURING NEW WORKS BY CHAD MURRAY

For Immediate Release - The Chad Murray Signature Series is available at
Sunset Terrace for one night only, 7-11PM, July 18, 2013, 2028 Clark Drive,
Vancouver, Canada. Pricing starts at $1.

VANCOUVER, July 4, 2013 In Collaboration with Chad Murray, Brick Press
announces the worldwide release of the Chad Murray Signature Series,
products that combine the best in connectivity, style and
entertainment features from Brick Press for the smaller product industry to
bring users the next level in Signature Series.

Consumers have been for more affordable and smaller Signature Series
Products, and weve responded by introducing our smallest products ever,
making Signature Series more usable and redefining todays market, said
Ryan Smith, Co-President, New Product Development, Brick Press. Throughout
our company, we have strived to give customers the best possible experience
with the smallest Signature Series on the market.

PERFORMANCE & PORTABILITY
Whether its looking at product, playing with product, drawing or erasing
with product, consumers want smaller and more portable Signature Series.
The Chad Murray Signature Series raises the bar for Signature Series. For
the first time ever, Signature Series users can enjoy high definition
clarity with sleek product design.

The Chad Murray Signature Series features powerful branding that delivers
functionality normally found in bigger Signature Series at a lower price
point and smaller size, said Dave Pullmer, Co-President, Brick Press.
This little Signature Series is perfect for drawing, playing games, and
even editing.

ART MAKING ON THE GO
The Chad Murray Signature Series was designed for consumers looking for a
super minimal, portable and affordable Signature Series. Measuring less
than an inch thick, the Signature Series leads the industry for thinness.
The lightweight Signature Series weighs very little.

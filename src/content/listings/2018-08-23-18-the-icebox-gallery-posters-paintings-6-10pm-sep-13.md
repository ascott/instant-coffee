Poster & Paintings by Brandon Stephenson
Opening reception Thursday September 13th, 6-10 PM

The Icebox Gallery
312 Railway Street
Vancouver, BC V6A 1C4

Artist Statement:

Im an artist who works as a graphic designer. At times, this feels like
two opposing ways of thinking. More recently, with "Posters and Paintings"
my art and design processes are gradually becoming complementary.

Paintings on canvas are shown beside posters. Both commercially printed and
physically painted, these posters reveal the various iterative and
complementary processes of painting and graphic design.

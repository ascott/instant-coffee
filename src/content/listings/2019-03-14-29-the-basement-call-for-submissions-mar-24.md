the basement show

Opening: April 11th Exhibition duration: April 12th - 21st

Presented by Val Loewen with support from Malaspina Printmakers Society. 

Submissions are open for the basement show. We are looking for works that were made 
or conceptualized in Vancouver recently and focus on the engagement between art 
practice and social experience. For pieces that take place outside of the gallery, 
documentation can be submitted. All mediums are welcome, including installations. 
Artists selected for this exhibition will receive artist fees at industry standard. 

The basement: 60-268 Keefer Street

A 1,206 square foot space with 18 foot high ceilings located in the lower ground level of 
BC Artscape Sun Wah. This space was once a Chinatown grocery store, and was 
recently used as a screen and textile studio. It still bears the marks of a work space. 
Artists are responsible for installation and, if required, insurance for their work. 

How to apply:

Please email your cv along with up to five images and brief description of artwork that 
you are proposing to exhibit to whoisvalloewen@gmail.com by March 24th. 

Timeline: 

Installations and set up will start the first week of April with more details to be 
determined closer to the date. All works will have to be removed by April 26th.

Whos involved:

Val Loewen is a Vancouver-based artist, printmaker, and aspiring curator. She holds a 
BFA from NSCAD University where she focused on printmaking and sculpture. She has 
worked as a collaborative printmaker and printmaking technician in various print studios 
in Vancouver since 2013, and her work has been exhibited in Vancouver, Alberta, and 
Arizona. She is interested in the intersection between creative communities and social 
experience.

Malaspina Printmakers Society is a nonprofit artist-run center that advances education, 
promotes excellence in the art form, and increases the publics understanding and 
appreciation of contemporary art and print media. 

BC Artscape Sun Wah is a community cultural hub in Vancouvers Chinatown that spans 
the Lower Ground, 3rd and 4th floors of the Sun Wah Centre at 268 Keefer Street. 
Tenants are a mix of cultural organizations, artists and community groups that share a 
commitment to being engaged and responsible to the areas communities.

Langara College is pleased to announce the formation of The Langara College Centre for Art in 
Public Spaces. 

An innovative approach to the City of Vancouver public art requirement, The Langara Centre for 
Art in Public Spaces has a mission to engage communities in the research, production and 
presentation of art in the public realm. The Centre encourages dialogue, and fosters 
opportunities to collaborate on, experience and learn about art in public spaces. 

The Centre’s activities include an on-going Artist in Residence program, the development of 
curricula specific to art in public spaces, and a public Speakers Series beginning this Fall, to 
enhance the discussion about art in the spaces we share. 

The Centre is delighted to welcome Kristina Lee Podesva, a Vancouver-based artist, curator and 
writer as the inaugural Artist in Residence. Kristina will research and produce a project in 
collaboration with Langara students and faculty, and participate in the curriculum and 
programming activities of the Centre until April, 2009. 

Kristina Lee Podesva is the founder of colourschool, a free school within a school most recently 
hosted by Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design. She is cofounder of Cornershop Projects, an 
open framework for the examination of the relationship between art and economic transactions, 
and is Assistant Editor at The Fillip Review. 

Kristina’s most recent project, Showroom, was organized with Inge Roecker in collaboration with 
Michelle Allard, Marianne Bos, Patrick Chan, Paul de Guzman, Vanessa Kwan, Gwenessa Lam, 
Heidi Nagtegaal, Alex Pensato, Ryan Peter, and Jordan Strom. Located at Centre A Gallery, 
Vancouver, Showroom was a platform for addressing the relationship between art and public 
space through the framework of a condominium showroom/construction site.

Stephanie Aitken
Breathing Machines
February 12th — March 14th, 2015
Opening February 12th, 6:00 — 10:00 PM

We are pleased to announce Stephanie Aitken as our first exhibiting artist
of 2015. Her exhibition titled “Breathing Machines”  opens on Thursday,
February 12th from 6:00 — 10:00 PM.

These paintings are about trees, tree parts, dead trees, stumps, burn
piles, driftwood, branches, cut limbs, birds, beetles, heads, torsos,
trunks, light around trees, plants and bodies.

The breath in these paintings is light, light passing in and out through
layers of colour. Fast and slow marks, liquid, thin, opaque, a regular give
and take.

Stephanie Aitken is a Vancouver based artist. Recent exhibitions include
Black Diamond Dust at the Nanaimo Art Gallery, Drama of Perception curated
by Sandra Meigs and New Land Paintings, a one-person exhibition, at Deluge
Contemporary Art, Victoria, B.C. Stephanie took part in the thematic
residency Studio Time: Work of the Living Watch with Geoffrey Farmer at The
Banff Centre in the summer of 2012. Stephanie's work has been written about
and published in The Walrus, Canadian Art, The Vancouver Review, C Magazine
and Border Crossings. She recently designed a series of three hand knotted
rugs that have been produced in collaboration with Burritt Bros. in
Vancouver and weavers in the Kathmandu Valley.

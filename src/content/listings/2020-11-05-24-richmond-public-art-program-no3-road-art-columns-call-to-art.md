No.3 Road Art Columns, Requests for Proposals, RFP

The City of Richmond Public Art Program seeks proposals from Richmond-based visual
artists who are interested in exhibiting original artwork on the No.3 Road Art Columns.
The exhibitions will run from February 1 to July 31, 2021 and September 1, 2021 to
January 31, 2022. Six commissioned artists will each receive an artist fee of $1,500 CAD
for four (4)
print-ready digital artwork files. Production and installation costs will be the
responsibility of the City of Richmond. Artists with practices in mixed-media, painting,
illustration, photography, printmaking, drawing and other mediums suitable for digital
reproduction are encouraged to apply.

Artist Fee: $1,500 CAD

Eligibility: Artists residing in Richmond, BC

Deadline: Monday, November 16, 2020, 5:00 p.m.

For more information and to apply: www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities

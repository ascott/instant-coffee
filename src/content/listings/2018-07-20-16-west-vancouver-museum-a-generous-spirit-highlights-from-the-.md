A Generous Spirit: Highlights from the Permanent Collection

July 25"September 1, 2018
Opening Reception: Tuesday, July 24, 7"9 p.m.

Please join us for the opening reception of A Generous Spirit: Highlights
from the Permanent Collection as we celebrate our new designation as the
West Vancouver Art Museum, which will serve to better reflect the museums
distinct focus on art and design, as well as the depth and breadth of its
expanding permanent collection.

A Generous Spirit will feature a selection of exceptional works drawn from
the over 300 historical and contemporary works in the permanent collection,
generously donated to the museum since 2006.

The exhibition will include work by renowned artists from across the
country, including Emily Carr, Gordon Smith, Ann Kipling, Jack Shadbolt,
Sylvia Tait, B.C. Binning, Atilla Richard Lukacs, Kim Kennedy Austin, and
Babak Golkar, among many others.

For exhibition info, please visit:
https://westvancouvermuseum.ca/

Event: Mad Coffee House April 24th 5 to 7 PM

Place: Connection Salon Gallery, 609 Helmcken

This is the inaugural event for bimonthly coffee houses featuring 3 performers and
Emcee Kagan Goh.

Richard Lett, Lea Taranto, and CR Avery will be performing on April 24th from 5:00 to
7:00 PM.

Coffee and snacks will be served.

Connection Salon Artists Collectives mission is to provide low barrier opportunities
to artists that are underrepresented and have lived experience with mental health.

The Canada Council for the Arts defines "Mad arts" as artistic works created by
individuals who identify with Madness and express Mad Pride.

The term "Mad" has been reclaimed by those living with mental illness or psychiatric
disabilities to symbolize pride, collective identity, and community building.

This event is supported by the City of Vancouver, the Connection Salon Collective
and the Gathering Place Community Center.

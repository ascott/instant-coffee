The Art Auction Fundraiser in support of BC Childrens Hospital Foundation is
currently seeking a Volunteer Social Media & Promotions Coordinator to join our
team.

This fundraiser will feature artworks from BC-based artists, with proceeds supporting
childrens health and well-being while also providing visibility and opportunities for
local artists.

We are inviting individuals with a passion for social media, community engagement,
and the arts to apply. Successful candidates will help manage and promote the
events online presence in the weeks leading up to the auction.

Volunteer Responsibilities:

Create and schedule engaging social media posts across platforms

Promote participating artists, artworks, and event details

Interact with the online community and encourage participation

Support promotional campaigns to broaden reach

Commitment: Flexible, approx. 3"5 hours per week leading up to the fundraiser

Qualifications:

Interest in social media, promotions, or arts marketing

Strong organizational and communication skills

Creativity and enthusiasm for community-building

Experience with Canva, Instagram, or similar tools (asset, not required)

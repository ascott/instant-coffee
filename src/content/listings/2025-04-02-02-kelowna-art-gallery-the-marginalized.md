The Marginalized opens at Kelowna Art Gallery

Roland Samuel: The Marginalized

March 29 to June 1, 2025

March 27, 2025 " Kelowna, BC: This weekend offers Okanagan audiences
something new to see at the Kelowna Art Gallery. The Marginalized showcases the
MFA thesis project of Roland Samuel, who is a candidate at UBC Okanagan.

Through the assemblage and repurposing of found objects, Roland Samuel creates
richly detailed sculptures that symbolize the discarded and overlooked materials,
which he says mirror the experiences of marginalized groups worldwide. The
exhibition is a personal one for the artist"it draws on his own background growing
up as an Igbo child in southeastern Nigeria. The exhibition highlights the emotional
and psychological toll of being excluded and offers a powerful commentary on
segregation and societal alienation.

Visitors will be able to explore the exhibition space that features salvaged car doors
mounted on walls where they might expect to find paintings. The car doors become
works of art when laced with the beautiful patchwork of textiles, diverse materials
including wicker, and intricately carved narratives that the artist has imbued within
the sculptures. In the middle of the exhibition stands Njirimara (Identity in Igbo), a
figurative sculpture meant to represent the artist himself. A large, colorful fabric
covers its head and torso, symbolizing the concealment of his true identity.

Roland Samuel says, This work aims to empower those who face similar struggles,
giving a voice to the silenced and shedding light on the impact of marginalization.

The Marginalized runs from March 29 to June 1, 2025.

Roland Samuel is an artist, environmentalist, and musician currently based out of
Kelowna. He earned a BFA from Olabisi Onabanjo University in Ago-Iwoye, Nigeria,
and is currently pursuing his MFA at UBC Okanagan. Samuel is the recipient of the
2024 Audain Foundation travel award.

The Kelowna Art Gallery is located at 1315 Water Street in the heart of Kelownas
Culture District. For more information visit kelownaartgallery.com or call 250-762-
2226.

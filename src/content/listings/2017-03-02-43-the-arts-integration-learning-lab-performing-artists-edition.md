The Arts Integration Learning Lab: Performing Artists Edition - Apply by March 20, 
2017

The Arts Integration Learning Lab: Performing Artists Edition is a professional 
development experience for musicians, dancers, poets, storytellers, actors and 
interdisciplinary artists in the Greater Vancouver Area. Work with experienced 
professional teaching artists to build your capacity and work alongside educators in 
schools to provide engaging arts-based learning for BC's young people. Artists 
selected to participate will be fully subsidized to tuition to attend five days of 
interactive workshops (April 24-28), receive program materials, and enjoy lunch each 
day. This opportunity is made possible through funding support from BC Arts Council 
and the Government of British Columbia's Creative Futures program. Learn more and 
apply by March 20, 2017 at [http://artstarts.com/aill](http://artstarts.com/aill)

Special Sunday Supper feat. Guadalupe Martinez

August 17, 7:30- 10

You are cordially invited to a summertime potluck dinner in a lovely
Strathcona backyard, followed by an artist talk featuring Guadalupe Martinez.

Guadalupe Martinez is an Argentine-Canadian artist based in Vancouver since
2008.
Her work fosters interactions that pay attention to the specificity of
place using found materials with the intention to transcend the (apparent)
static nature of sculpture. Embodiment is the lyrical potential to
interrogate and reorganize meaning.
Her practice combines three-dimensionality, performance, and
site-specificity creating work that mnemonically activates bodies and found
materials, reanimating their meaning into new forms of signification and
representation.

Martinez obtained her BFA at the Instituto Universitario Nacional del Arte
in Buenos Aires and is currently completing an MFA at University of British
Columbia.
In 2010 Martinez attended the Master Class Residency with Ken Lum at The
Banff Center for the Arts which significantly influenced the development of
her work. In 2013 she was invited to work at Dorchester Projects with
artist Theaster Gates, extending her practice into the realms of pedagogy
and engagement.

There will be some food provided, but please arrive with a dish to share.
Dinner will begin around 7:30, with the talk to follow at about 8:45. This
is an outdoor event, so please dress accordingly. This event is family
friendly and inclusive to all.

To RSVP, please email ssoutham@gmail.com. Once you RSVP, you will recieve
the location of the event.

Curated by Sydney Southam and co-presented by Iris Film Collective.

This event was made possible with the support of the Neighborhood Small
Grants and the City of Vancouver.

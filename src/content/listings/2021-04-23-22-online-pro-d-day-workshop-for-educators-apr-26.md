Pro-D Day Workshop for Educators: Exploring Cultural Appropriation

Monday, April 26, 2021, 10 - 11:30 am
Teachers are invited to
join educator and artist Toonasa (Dakelh/Wetsuweten) for an online
discussion on cultural appropriation. With the introduction of Indigenous content in the new
BC curriculum, many teachers have questions and concerns about using Indigenous art in
their classrooms. In this workshop, you will gain a broader and deeper understanding of
cultural appropriation, especially as it relates to Indigenous art of the Northwest Coast. This
will help you navigate conversations and projects with Indigenous content in the classroom.
Registrants will receive a link one day before the workshop.

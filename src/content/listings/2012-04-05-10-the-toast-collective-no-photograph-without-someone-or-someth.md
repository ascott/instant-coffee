"No Photograph Without Someone or Something"
New work by Georgia Graham and Mieke van Delft
Opening Reception: April 13th @ 7 pm
The Toast Collective, 648 Kingsway, Vancouver

Please join us for the opening reception of "No Photograph Without 
Someone or Something", Friday April 13th at 7 pm at the Toast 
Collective (648 Kingsway). Photographs will be shown by Georgia 
Graham and Mieke van Delft. In addition, a slideshow installation 
featuring photographs from over 40 local and international artists will 
be exhibited.

"No Photograph Without Someone or Something" is an investigation 
into the desire to validate our subjective existence through capturing 
our own behaviour in a visual format. The exhibition considers 
photography's relationship to nostalgia in the present moment, 
bringing private moments into the public view, and ideas of value in 
personal memory and authenticity.

Curated by Liz Toohey-Wiese

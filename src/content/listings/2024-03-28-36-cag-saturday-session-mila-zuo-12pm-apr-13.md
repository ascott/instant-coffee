Saturday Session: Mila Zuo on Diane Severin Nguyen

13 APR 12:00 PM

One Saturday each month, CAG invites a guest host to lead walkthroughs of our
current
exhibitions, offering their insights and response to the works on view. This April,
we welcome Mila Zuo to speak on the work of Diane Severin Nguyen. No advance
registration required.

Mila Zuo is an associate professor in the Department of Theatre and Film at UBC, and
Graduate Advisor for the Cinema and Media Studies graduate program. She is the
author of Vulgar Beauty: Acting Chinese in the Global Sensorium (Duke University
Press, 2022) and conducts research on transnational Asian cinemas; film-philosophy;
abject and enchanted epistemologies; star studies; digital and new media; and critical
theories of gender, sexuality, and race and ethnicity. In addition to her scholarly work,
Zuo writes, directs, and produces narrative films, visual essays, documentaries, and
music videos.

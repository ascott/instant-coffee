Arts in the Garden 2024
Saturday May 25 & Sunday May 26

11am - 4pm

Music | Performance | Nature | Art

This May offers the unique opportunity for the North Shore community to gather with
artists, musicians, and community in beautiful, intimate spaces across the North
Shore for a weekend of creativity, play, learning and reflection. Now returning for its
25th year, Arts in the Garden is a celebration of arts and nature, inviting the
community to reflect on their relationship to the land, to place, and to each other.

Arts in the Garden will bring together over fifty visual artists, musicians and
performers in ten outdoor spaces across the North Shore. The weekend offers
valuable opportunities for connection: visual artists in
each garden offer art activities
or demonstrations, and two locations offer workshops and information regarding the
land. The work of many participating artists, musicians and performers respond
directly to place and environment, through a wide variety of art forms, styles and
mediums.

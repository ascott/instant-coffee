WORKSHOP: Arts-Based Methods for Engagement
Date: November 25, 2011, 9 AM - 4:30 PM
Session Leader: jil p. weaving

Workshop Description:
You don't need to be an artist to take this workshop or work effectively with arts-
based engagement methods. We will explore examples that draw on a range of 
artistic media that invite diverse perspectives and bring people together to better 
understand and address public issues.

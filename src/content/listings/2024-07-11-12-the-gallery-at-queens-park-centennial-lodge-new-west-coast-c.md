Join contemporary composer Brandon Chow as he presents a recording of his
recent work, Aura soave, which was performed at the Academie Domaine Forget
in Charlevoix, Qubec, as well as a discussion on music and meaning. Weather you
are a musician, history buff, or want to share your own work, we welcome you to join
us!

Brandon writes This piece includes as its model the 1601 madrigal Aura soave di
segreti accenti (Sweet breeze of secret words) by Italian Renaissance composer
Luzzasco Luzzaschi. Luzzaschi was closely affiliated with the Ferrara court and their
virtuoso female singers, the concerto delle donne. It was for these singers that
Luzzaschi
would notate music that previously existed as improvised ornamentation.

I wanted to compose a piece which looks backwards in time to a composer whose
elegant and highly expressive works deserve more widespread recognition, but
conversely reinvents the highly ornamented melodies of the late Renaissance and
early Baroque musical traditions and extends them into the present and future.
Simultaneously, I have been frequently influenced by surrealist painters such as
Giorgio de Chirico and Paul Delvaux, who continue to have a tremendous affective
influence on my work.

I am tremendously grateful to the Quasar Saxophone Quartet for their boundless
enthusiasm for contemporary music as well as the wonderful discoveries we made
rehearsing together.

There will be an accompanying discussion on music and meaning/ expressivity as it
has been influenced by the philosopher Stephen Davies.

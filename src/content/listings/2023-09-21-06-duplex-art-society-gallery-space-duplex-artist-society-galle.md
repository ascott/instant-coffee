An excerpt of the exhibition text written by Mller:

Millions of these tinier particles ....

- - - - Dash - - - Sunspot - - -Stitch - - - - -

Smoke covered the sun and I wondered what all I was breathing in...
Incapable of leaving this seance. I drew with crystals, made dashes from dust, stitched
with neon nylon, layered layers of acrylic, and poked holes in paper so the work could
breathe.
- - - - - - - - - -

Hannah Mller situates her art practice in the expanded field of painting. Her art
making apparatus currently consists of painting, drawing, and sculpture. Propelled by
experimentation and play she is unconcerned by medium specificity and drawn to a
variety
of surfaces; drop cloth, string, paints, paper, graphite, crayons and clay.
Transfixed by the non-linear aspects of memory, the malleability of poetry and whimsey
of fiction. She often thinks of each artwork as its own character and having its own
slew of hypotheticals and prose. As she cuts, scratches, pokes and stitches, she
deconstructs and reconstructs.
Breathing new life into the material - each element having a mark/or scar left on it from
a physical presence.

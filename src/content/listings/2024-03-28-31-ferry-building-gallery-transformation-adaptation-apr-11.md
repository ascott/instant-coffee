The Ferry Building Gallery is proud to announce its upcoming exhibition,
"Transformation & Adaptation," showcasing ceramics, prints, and paintings from local
artists Carol Demers and Anthea Cameron. This exhibition presents a blend of nature-
inspired artworks that mirror the deep connections both artists have with the natural
environment of the North Shore. The exhibition will open on April 11 and run until May
5.

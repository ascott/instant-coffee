PLANK 

New Works by Allison Watters at Avenue:

PLANK - wood- fabricated - to be strong- allow a moment to be held. 

The threshold can break with the intention of the artist; Open for the 
audience to grasp. 

Precarious objects, suspended ready for a step, withhold information.

Swing - stop - transfer - touch - break - push - gravity - transfer - Swing.

Disintegrate - crumble - residue - transfer - trace - replace - jibber - Create.

Structures to hold - the body - in tension.

Mixed messages might push futures for us.

Environment is announced, reborn from the ﬁssures. 

Isolation is lost, with an accompaniment of words. 

Bellows entice inquiry of more. 

Sculptures can be seen in performance with the artist on Friday June 20th, 
7pm at 165 e.Hastings.

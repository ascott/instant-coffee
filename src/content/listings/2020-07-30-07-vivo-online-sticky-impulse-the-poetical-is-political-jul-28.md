ONLINE EXHIBIT: The Poetical is Political

Sticky Impulse
The Poetical is Political
July 28-August 11 @ vivomediaarts.com/archive
FREE

Curated by KC Wei (Video Out)

Whats more political than identifying oneself as a poet? To wax language down from
utility, to play with words and shift cognition; to rest on sound, form, and rhythm? This
months Sticky Impulse features a selection of readings, conversations, abstractions, and
physical ephemera from the Lenore Herb
Archive and other videos from the Crista Dahl
Media Library and Archives.

Videos with Stan Persky, Beth Jankola, Cathy Ford, Nicolle Brossard, and by Ernest
Gusella, Russell Wallace, Pia Massie.

Publication excerpts from Pulp, The Literary Storefront, and by Earl Courtney, bill bissett,
Bern Porter, Pat Stevens, Lenore Herb.

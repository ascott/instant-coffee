JOB: SUMMER GALLERY ASSISTANT
Deadline: 23 April 2018, by 9:00 AM PST

14 May to 3 August 2018
35 hours per week, at a rate of pay of $17.00 per hour
Working language is English

Job Description
The Summer Program Assistant will work closely with the Director/Curator, the 
Project Coordinator, the Board of Directors, visiting curators, and participating artists 
to coordinate the gallerys exhibitions and execute a series of discursive projects.

About Access
Access Gallery is an artist-run centre with a mission is to create conditions of 
emergence for provocative ideas and work in the visual arts. We enable critical 
conversations and risk-taking through new configurations of artists, audience, and 
community. The organization searches out opportunities to experiment through 
collaborations (both local and international) that encourage innovation. We aim to 
bring visibility and critical reception to artists and cultural practitioners when they 
need it most, at important moments often early in their practices. Access is located 
in Vancouvers Chinatown, on the unceded territories of the xmkwy"m 
(Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), St:l and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-
Waututh) Nations.

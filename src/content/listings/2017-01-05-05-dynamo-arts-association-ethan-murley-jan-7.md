Opening reception: Jan 7, 7-10pm
Dynamo Arts Association
suite 103 - 30 East 6th ave. Vancouver 

Dynamo is pleased to present a solo exhibition of work by Ethan Murley. Working in 
figurative painting and text-based print, Murley's practice is simultaneously 
understated and startlingly literal. 'Traces' explores the eroticized male form while 
mapping a narrative of embodied experience. 

Ethan Murley graduated from Emily Carr University in 2016. Recent group exhibitions 
have included Cruising the Dog Days of Summer at Glad Day gallery, Toronto, TBH 
at Franc Gallery, and The Male Gaze, Vancouver.

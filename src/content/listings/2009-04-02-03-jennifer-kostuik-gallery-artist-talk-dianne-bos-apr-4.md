LIGHT OLDER THAN WINE New Images from Italy by DIANNE BOS
02 April - 26 April 2009

Opening with artist in attendance, Thursday 02 April 6-9 pm
Artist talk, Saturday 04 April 2-4 pm

The Jennifer Kostuik Gallery is pleased to present new black and white and
color pinhole photographs of Italian based perspectives by Dianne Bos, her
fifth solo exhibition with the gallery. For over 25 years, Dianne Bos has
practiced pinhole photography as a contemporary art form. Also featured in
the gallery will be a survey of prominent photographs by the artist from
2000 to the present.

Pinhole projections and the camera obscura - Italian for dark room -
changed Western thinking of the Earth's relationship to the sun, and in
turn, assisted in transforming the face of art during the Renaissance
period. In her new series, Bos explores pinhole photography in the same
environment where Leonardo da Vinci once experimented with similar image
projection techniques. Her ongoing interest to capture the passing of time
and light is seen in particular images as the hauntingly beautiful White
Chair, Montecastello di Vibio, where light still pours in to large
cavernous cathedrals through a tiny hole in the ceiling, projecting the
image of the sun itself on the floor. Bos explains, although initially
designed to tell time, this sun projection helped changed earlier beliefs
that the earth was the centre of the Universe.

As Derek Walcott describes in his poem In Italy, this is "light older than
wine" whose "shadows that are the dial of time." (quotation from Derek
Walcott, In Italy, The New Yorker Magazine, April 21, 2008)

Biography

Originally from Hamilton Ontario, Dianne Bos is a Calgary, Alberta based
photographer and teacher at the Alberta College of Art and Design.
Multi-talented, her art practice and interests extend into a variety of
creative fields, from being a lead singer and keyboardist for several
musical bands, having her photography designed for several vinyl, cassette,
CD and video releases in Canada, USA, UK and Europe, is a writer and
photographer for contemporary garden and design, and has consistently
exhibited in solo and group shows in public galleries and museum across
Canada from 1986 to the present, including the 2007 Garden Shed Galactica,
Outdoor installation, London Museum, Ontario and 2008 Through the Looking
Glass, Glenbow Museum, Calgary, Alberta.

Nov 25, 2023 - Jan 20, 2024

Kimberly Fulton-Orozco pursues, in-depth, a reconnection with her Kaigani Haida
heritage and the ancestral knowledge base passed on by her Mother and
Grandmother. This deeply personal exploration of Orozco's identity touches on themes
of knowledge transfer, power dynamics, truth in self-expression, and the cultural loss at
the hands of assimilation. The exhibition features artworks in various media, including
works on paper, paintings on canvas and linen, hand-finished gicle prints, and
sculpture. Using a blend of figurative formline design and abstraction, Orozco attempts
to navigate the complexity of remaining clearly connected to her past during an
increasingly fraught and confusing present.

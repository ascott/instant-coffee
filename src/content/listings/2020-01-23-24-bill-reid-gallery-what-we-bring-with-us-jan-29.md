Bill Reid Gallery: What We Bring With Us - January 29-April 12, 2020

639 Hornby St, Vancouver, BC

The Bill Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art presents the work of six emerging Indigenous
artists in What We Bring With Us, on display from January 28-April 12, 2020.

Each artist addresses their relationship to the land and kinship they have formed through
displacement in works that range from photography to performance. Guest curated by Maria-
Margaretta (Mtis), the exhibition features Cheyenne Rain LeGrande '' (Nehiyaw), Jake
Kimble (Deninu Kue), Lacie Burning (Kanien'keh:ka), M.V. Williams (Skwx7mesh,
Wet'suwet'en), Taran Kootenhayoo (Denesu-n, Nakoda Sioux), and Whess Harman (Carrier
Witat), as they navigate space and negotiate their identities as visitors on unceded
territories.

Admission details and information at: billreidgallery.ca

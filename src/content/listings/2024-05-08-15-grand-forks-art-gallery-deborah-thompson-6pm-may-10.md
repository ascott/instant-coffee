Grand Forks Art Gallery presents DEBORAH THOMPSON
BOAT WITHOUT A BOAT
Reception Friday, May 10. 6-9pm
Performance at 7pm
Artist talk Saturday, May 11 at 1pm
May 10 to August 03, 2024

Exhibition Boat without a Boat launches Deborah Thompson's art practice in two
new directions; Stop Motion Animation and Constellations of drawn and cut paper.
Through figurative based narratives the artist explores metaphysical questions
around instinctual consciousness and creation mythology that continue to inspire her
practice. Female protagonists are agents of change in acts of hatching, doubling,
splitting, shapeshifting and hybridity.

The title, Boat without a Boat, named after one of the pieces in the show, references
the artist's creative process of working from a felt
sense, and serves as a
metaphorical umbrella for the work. The Boat as a vessel for passage from one
realm of consciousness to another. It is also a nod to these times of mass global
migration of humans and the transformations that accompany those individuals on
their journeys.

The exhibition will occupy two gallery spaces, with eight new works on paper and
one of the videos in the Reid Gallery and the other video in the West Gallery now a
black box theatre.

Both stop motion animation works, Night Ritual and Midden bring the implied
narratives of the still works into surprising narrative arcs of animal - human
relationships with morphing figures and acts of transcendence. Musical scores for
both videos are by composer Graham Tracey. The project was supported by a grant
from the Canada Council for the Arts.

This is an open call for kids ages 6-10 interested in art, costumes,
movement, dance and performance.

Mumblers is a workshop series lead by artists Kara Hansen and Kathleen
Taylor in October. By creating paper costumes and props with recycled
materials, we will develop characters together through play. Through
hands-on activities and games we will explore inventive disguises and
imaginative ways of storytelling. Participants are free to attend any or
all of the workshop dates listed below.

FREE WORKSHOP DATES
Saturday, October 15: 11am - 2pm
Sunday, October 16: 12pm - 2pm
Saturday, October 22: 11am - 2pm
Sunday, October 23: 12pm - 2pm

Kara Hansen and Kathleen Taylor share a studio at Duplex, an artist studio
and gallery space located in Vancouver Mountain View (4257 Fraser St). We
collaborate in an unofficial capacity in multiple areas of our lives, and
we have also worked together professionally, performing Lights on the River
of the Eternal Baby Moon as part of Live Biennale 2016. For Family Day at
artist-run centre Western Front we lead an informal paper-hat making
workshop and photo booth. In collaboration with a small group of kids,
Mumblers will be a more extensive development of this previous workshop.

Kara Hansen is an artist and writer based in Vancouver BC. Through
improvisation, costume, makeup, scripts, and movement scores, her work
engages collaboration to build absurd situations around the body. Recent
exhibitions include Hypnic Jerk at Spare Room (2016). She holds a BFA from
Emily Carr University.

Kathleen Taylor is an artist based in Vancouver who completed her BFA at
Emily Carr University of Art and Design. Her work engages with drawing as
means to play with sculpture, installation and architecture. Exhibitions
include PULP SHADES at Dynamo Arts Association, and Balmoral 12 Tone at
Avenue Gallery. Currently she works as the Curatorial Assistant at Western
Front.

This project is generously supported by ArtStarts.

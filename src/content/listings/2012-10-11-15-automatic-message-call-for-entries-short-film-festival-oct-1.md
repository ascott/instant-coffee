Call for Entries
Entries Deadline: EXTENDED! October 15th, 2012

The Automatic Message, supported by the Vancouver Foundation, 
VIVO Media Arts and the Mount Pleasant Neighbourhood Small Grants 
Project, is pleased to announce IN MEDIAS RES, a Short Film Festival 
for artists living and working in Mount Pleasant.

IN MEDIAS RES is a showcase of short film arts, including media art, 
animation, music videos, narrative and experimental film.

Entries are sought in various disciplines of Media Arts including video, 
film, animation and from professional, amateur, or independent 
sources. The Festival will take place on Friday, November 9th at VIVO 
Media Arts.

Eligibility: Artists must be living or working in Mount Pleasant at the 
time of submission or at the time they created their work. Works must 
not be longer than 30 minutes. Digital works only. The catchment area 
for Mount Pleasant is from 2nd to 41st and from Cambie to 
Commercial. 

Please send us links to view your work online - if your work is not 
available online, please email us the best way to view your work for 
consideration. With your entry, please let us know your name, the date 
of the work, the address of your home or studio in Mount Pleasant and 
a short description of your work.

We are looking forward to viewing your entry!

Curators: The Automatic Message (Tanya Goehring and Trevor 
Jacobson)

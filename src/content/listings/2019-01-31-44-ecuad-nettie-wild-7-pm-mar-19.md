NETTIE WILD, DOCUMENTARY FILMMAKER + MEDIA ARTIST
TUES, MARCH 19 | 7 - 8:30p
Rennie Hall, 2nd flr
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
520 East 1st Avenue, Vancouver BC V5T 0H2 

Wilds feature documentary KONEL-NE: our land beautiful is being screened March 13, 
7p, 1 week in advance of the filmmakers talk.

Nettie Wild will be giving a lecture on her career, and emphasizing her recent 
documentary, KONELINE and public media installation UNINTERRUPTED. Wild is one 
of Canadas leading documentary filmmakers. Her highly charged and critically 
acclaimed films have brought her audiences behind the frontlines and headlines of 
revolutions and social change around the world. Her more recent work has gained 
recognition for forging new frontiers in story telling on the big screen, on the web and in 
large scale public installations.

Nettie is best known for her award winning documentary features : KONELINE: our land 
beautiful (2016); FIX: The Story of an Addicted City (2002), A PLACE CALLED 
CHIAPAS (1998), BLOCKADE (1993) and A RUSTLING OF LEAVES: Inside the 
Philippine Revolution (1988). Her latest projects explore new forms of storytelling: 
UNINTERRUPTED is a public art installation first exhibited in the summer of 2017, a 25-
min digitally-mapped projection using unique underwater footage of the Adams River 
sockeye salmon migration.

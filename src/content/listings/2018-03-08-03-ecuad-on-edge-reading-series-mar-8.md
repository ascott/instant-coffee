Join us! The On Edge Reading Series features Christopher Pollon and Aaron 
Giovannone Thursday March 8 @ 5:30 p.m. in the Emily Carr University library 520 East 
1st Avenue.

Christopher Pollon is a Vancouver-based independent journalist who reports on the 
politics of natural resources, focusing on energy, mines and oceans. His work has been 
published by National Geographic Books, The Walrus, Readers Digest, The Globe and 
Mail, and The Center for Investigative Journalism. He has been a feature writer and 
contributing editor at The Tyee since 2008, and currently reports for Vancouvers 
Discourse Media. The Peace in Peril, his first book, has been nominated for the George 
Ryga Award for Social Awareness in Literature, and is also shortlisted for the 2017 
Roderick Haig-Brown Regional Prize.

Aaron Giovannones poetry collections are The Loneliness Machine (Insomniac, 2014) 
and The Nonnets (Book Thug, forthcoming 2018). His prose has appeared in The 
Walrus and Vice, and with Naomi K. Lewis, he is co-editing an anthology of poetry and 
creative non-fiction titled Money. He has a Ph.D. in English Literature and Creative 
Writing and has taught at the Universita' di Siena (Italy), Brock University, and 
Okanagan College. He currently lives in Calgary.

We acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the Arts, which last year 
invested $153 million to bring the arts to Canadians throughout the country.

Everyone is welcome!

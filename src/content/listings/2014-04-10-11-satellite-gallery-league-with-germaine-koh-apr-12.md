League with Germaine Koh
Saturday, April 12 from 2 to 4pm
Satellite Gallery
560 Seymour Street, 2nd Floor
Free admission

In conjunction with the Canadian Art Foundation Gallery Hop Vancouver, join 
us for provisional gaming at Satellite Gallery! In League, initiated by 
Vancouver-based artist Germaine Koh, participants are invited to participate 
in invented sports and games as a form of creative problem-solving, exploring 
processes of negotiation, improvisation, and strategy. Participants attempt to 
play within the suggested guidelines, equipment, space, and further develop 
the game as they play, with the expectation that it will evolve through trial and 
improvisation. Commonplace sports and play equipment, markers and 
everyday objects will be available, and new tools might be invented on the 
fly.

2020 MAU Calendar Launch

Now in its second year, the 2020 MAU Calendar is an artist and community produced
publication showcasing the work of local artists, creatives and community businesses and
social service organizations including, Ai & Om Knives, Bagua Artists Association, Bao Bei,
Dan Pon, Evan Lee, Fat Mao, Jeffery Chong, Kimberly Sayson, Kimberley Wong, Laurie M.
Landry, Melanie Kwan, Powell Street Festival Society, Rita Wong, Sarah Ling, Szu Shen,
Wayde Compton, WePress, Yarrow Intergenerational Society for Justice and Youth
Collaborative for Chinatown. The project aims to capture a contemporary portrait of a rapidly
changing community while expanding the use and purpose of a calendar -- as a tool to track
time, historical record
and artist publication. Printed by Moniker Press, a local publishing
studio specializing in Risograph printing.
Join MAU Calendar contributors, Justin and Vanessa Ho in a cooking and storytelling
workshop inspired by their fathers recipes. They will prepare The Chicken Dish, in honour of
their father who did the best with what he had and brought past and present cultures
together through his cooking. Participants will be invited to sample the dish as part of

the project launch reception. Saturday, October 19, 11am-4pm. BC Artscape, 268 Keefer
Street. 4th Flr. Maximum 15 participants. Please RSVP at 2020maulaunch.eventbrite.com.

https://www.eventbrite.com/e/2020-mau-calendar-launch-the-chicken-dish-tickets-
68642917815

This event is part of Vancouver Art Book Fair's 2019 Art Book Week. A limited-edition of
calendars will be available for purchase along with samples of featured dishes from the
project and Mahjong playing tables, hosted by Youth Collaborative for Chinatown. All are
welcome.This project is supported by a 2019 BC Arts Council grant.

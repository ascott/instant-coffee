Council | Now hiring | OCT 12
We are currently hiring for TWO positions through the Canada Summer Jobs program.

Gallery Program Coordinator

The Gallery Program Coordinator will collaborate with the BAC staff and Board of
Directors to plan for our upcoming Gallery exhibitions and related activities for 2021. The
Coordinator will participate in planning, development and delivery of arts activities that
connect the local Burnaby community to artists and their works through various channels
(online and in-person).

Objectives:

Participate in planning for Deer Lake Art Gallery exhibitions through 2021

Plan and implement other arts activities related to the programs mentioned above,
adapting them to the current pandemic and government-mandated safety regulations

Support marketing efforts by maintaining the BAC website and social media updates

On-site/live event support as needed (or participation/moderating in
the case of
livestreams and other web-based live events)

Connect with artists to coordinate exhibitions and expand our current network

Track goals, progress, and deadlines and make occasional summary reports to the
Board of Directors

Skills & Qualifications:

Post-secondary education in arts management, visual arts or related studies

Previous experience in planning art programming, festivals, events or exhibitions an
asset

Knowledge of Microsoft Office

Excellent verbal and written communication

Excellent organizational and research skills

Strong project management skills, able to prioritize tasks

Able to work independently

Project Coordinator

The Project Coordinator will focus on strengthening our connections with the community,
through communications strategy and promotion of our current programming. The role will
specifically focus on developing marketing strategies for the Burnaby Arts Council as a
community arts organization. The role will focus on developing more
meaningful
connections and create engagement opportunities that extend beyond in-person and
event-based engagement.

Objectives

Participate in developing a marketing strategy for the 2021

Work with the BAC staff and our guest curator in developing a consistent brand and
track key metrics.

Research partnerships and potential sponsorship opportunities

Enhance our current Indigenous Cultural Engagement program by strengthening
current Indigenous connections and researching new engagement opportunities

Adapt current programming to the current pandemic, and develop digital solutions.

On-site/live event support as needed (or participation/moderating in the case of
livestreams and other web-based live events)

Track and report weekly progress and set goals to supervisor

Skills & Qualifications

Post-secondary education in arts management, marketing & communications or related
studies

Previous experience in planning art programming, festivals, events or exhibitions an
asset

Knowledge
of Microsoft Office

Excellent verbal and written communication, and interpersonal skills

Excellent organizational and research skills

Strong project management skills, able to prioritize tasks

Able to work independently and collaboratively across all stakeholder groups

These roles are offered as a part of the Canada Summer Jobs 2020 program. Roles are
temporary 8 week position between October " November 2020, with 20-30 hours per
week ($16.00 per hour). The following are the requirements to participate in this federally
funded program. Both roles are open to remote/work-from-home as long as they fulfill the
following requirements.

Must be between 15 and 30 years of age at the start of the employment

Must be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or person to whom refugee protection
has been conferred under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act for the duration of
the employment

Must have a valid Social Insurance Number at the start of employment and be legally
entitled
to work in Canada in accordance with relevant provincial or territorial legislation
and regulations

Interested candidates are encouraged to forward a cover letter and resume, in confidence,
to info@burnabyartscouncil.org or apply on our website at
[https://burnabyartscouncil.org/organization/careers/](https://burnabyartscouncil.org/organization/careers/). Application closes on October 12,
2020. We thank all applicants for their interest, and those selected for an interview will be
contacted.

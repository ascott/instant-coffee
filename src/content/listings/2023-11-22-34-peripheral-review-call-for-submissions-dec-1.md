Peripheral Review is a non-profit platform for documenting and expanding the
emerging and underrepresented Canadian art scene, as well as enabling access for
emerging writers through encouraging expanded and accessible critical dialogue.
Our mandate states that we prioritize the voices of artists and writers who are
considered emerging, underrepresented, Indigenous, LGBTQ2S+, from diverse
cultural backgrounds, and those working
in alternative practices or abilities.

We are looking for completed first drafts and pitches for our upcoming Fall 2023
submission deadline on Friday December 1, 2023. Read the full call at
[https://www.peripheralreview.com/contact-submissions/](https://www.peripheralreview.com/contact-submissions/)

All submissions and pitches should be sent to: submissions@peripheralreview.com

We are looking for:

Reviews: (between 500 - 1,200 words)
Reviews should be succinct, interesting writing on an art exhibition, an artist project,
artist publication, performance (including dance, theatre, performance art), film, or
any other experimental art forms. PR is especially interested in and prioritizes
covering art, artists, and art spaces that are considered emerging,
underrepresented, outside of large city centres, or working in alternative methods or
abilities. We are interested in local or international works and artists, but prefer if the
proposed piece should have some sort of Canadian connection (ex. Canadian
artists, curators, gallery spaces,
content with significance to a Canadian art
audience, etc.)

Interviews: Should contain a minimum of 8 " 10 questions, but priority consideration
will be given to a more conversational-style rather than a strict question-answer
format.

Feature Essays: Any long form essay or review between 1,500-2,500 words. These
will also be considered for print-only publication in our annual print editions.

Experimental Pieces: No maximum word count, but the content, style, and format
should be clearly delineated and understandable in your pitch.

We offer successful submissions a fee for published pieces on our website and in
print. We encourage submissions by writers and about artists who are considered
emerging, under-represented, Indigenous, LGBTQ2S+, from diverse cultural
backgrounds or working in alternative practices and abilities, as well as prioritize
content on emerging and underrepresented artists and spaces. To familiarize
yourself with the content we publish, take a look at the
archives section of our
website: [https://peripheralreview.com](https://peripheralreview.com)

In your submission please include:

1. Short bio about yourself and your previous writing experience (no more than 100
words) and a CV with links to published work if you have not written for us before.

2. If you have a first draft, please attach it; if you are pitching, include the description
of your proposed piece, which can be in an attached document or in the body of the
email, with approximately 150-300 words in paragraph form describing the artist(s),
artwork, gallery, performance or exhibition space and/or the thematic concepts on
which you are interested in focusing on in the written piece.

Remember that a pitch/submission is introducing both yourself as a writer, as well as
the work on which you are proposing to write on to us, so make sure your ideas and
the context are as clear as possible.

If you have never pitched or published before, let us know in your email in advance
of the pitch deadline and we can
help you answer any questions you may have! and
send by the deadline to submissions@peripheralreview.com by midnight PST on
Friday December 1, 2023.

If you have further questions about your pitch or our call, send us an email in
advance of the deadline to contact@peripheralreview.com

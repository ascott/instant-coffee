The Bat-Yam Biennale is pleased to announce the open call for participation
in The 3rd Bat-Yam Biennale: Urban Action, due to take place during October
2012.

Following its two former editions, 'Hosting 2008' and 'Timing 2010', the
theme chosen for the third Biennale is 'Metamorphosis'.

We call on anyone interested or involved in urbanism to respond to the
theme of metamorphosis and suggest an act of intervention in Bat-Yam's
northern areas.

Proposals should express innovative ways of thinking and present outlines
for the development of an enhanced and beneficial living environment for
the city and its residents.

The Open Call for 'Metamorphosis 2012' can be found online, along with
detailed information on the submission process as well as sights and sounds
from the urban space were the Biennale will take place.

For more details, go to: opencall-batyam.org/batyam/english/

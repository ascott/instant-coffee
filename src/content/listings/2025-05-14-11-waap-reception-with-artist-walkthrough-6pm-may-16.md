RECEPTION

Fibre:
CAROLINE ACHAINTRE
JONATHAN ALFARO
GHISLAIN BROWN-KOSSI

Mid-exhibition reception and artist walkthrough: Fri, May 16, 6-8 PM
Artist tour begins at 6:30PM

On view: Apr 30 - May 24, 2025
Hours: Thurs - Sat, 12-5 PM
Wil Aballe
1375 Railspur Alley, Vancouver, BC

Material conversations float through space between the works of Caroline Achaintre,
Jonathan Alfaro, and Ghislain Brown-Kossi. Time, texture, and tactility ground these
artists exploration of visual language, histories, and craft, through a multiplicity of
detailed processes. Fibre becomes the medium through which they inhabit unique
visual threads to highlight an array of tangible histories.

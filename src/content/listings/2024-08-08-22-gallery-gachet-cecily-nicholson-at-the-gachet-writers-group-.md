Cecily
Nicholson at the Gachet Writers Group
August 16, 2"4pm followed by a mixer from 4"5pm
Space is limited, please rsvp to communications@gachet.org if you would like to attend
Gallery Gachet, 9 W Hastings St.

This workshop considers place and wayfinding. We will engage prompts as we practice
noticing connections and writing about them. No prior writing experience is required.
Writing materials are available for anyone taking part. The workshop will be followed by
a reading from participants (optional), facilitated by Bruce Ray and Cecily Nicholson.

Cecily Nicholson is an award-winning author of four books of poetry and a volunteer with
community impacted by carcerality. She is the 2024/2025 Holloway Lecturer in Poetry
and Poetics at UC Berkeley and an assistant professor in the School of Creative Writing,
UBC, on the unceded territory of the hn"q"min"m" speaking xmky"m (Musqueam)
people.

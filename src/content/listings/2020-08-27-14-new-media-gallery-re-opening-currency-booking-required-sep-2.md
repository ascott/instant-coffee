Eight award-winning artists consider diverse narratives, counter narratives, and symbolic
systems relating to notions of currency and value in a rapidly changing world of technology.
The use of cutting-edge technology and processes, combined with the ability to distill
complex
information has resulted in seven richly, metaphoric works that reflect the seductive,
volatile and problematic relationships between currency & value.

Using artificial intelligence, internet streaming, proximity sensors, diagnostic gas
chromatography, block chain, Bitsoil and bot systems these artists have produced intelligent,
evocative works of great complexity and depth.

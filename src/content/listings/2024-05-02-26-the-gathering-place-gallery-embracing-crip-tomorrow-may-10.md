Event: EMBRACING CRIP TOMORROW exhibition
Place: The Gathering Place Gallery, 609 Helmcken
This is a collaborative exhibition between Kickstart Arts and Culture and connection
Salon artists collective of a series of exhibitions on the theme of Recovery. This second
one is about Hope. Crip futurism is about the hope of dreaming into futures where all
disabled people are valued in society.
Crip is a term, similar to mad, that is used as a way of showing pride in being disabled.
Kickstart uses disability broadly; but this is meant to include anyone within communities
or experiences that are disabled, d/Deaf, mad, trauma impacted, experiences with
mental health).
Eight visual artists will be exhibiting from May 10 to June 23th.
Kagan Goh will also be performing at
our Meet the Artists event May 15th from 5:30 to 7
PM at the Gallery. Kagan Goh is a Vancouver-based multidisciplinary Mad Artist: award-
wining filmmaker, published author, spoken word poet, playwright, actor, mental health
advocate and activist. He will be showing a couple of his short films.
Embracing Crip Tomorrow is supported by the Community Arts Council of Vancouver, the
City of Vancouver, the Canada Council for the Arts and the Gathering Place Community
Center.

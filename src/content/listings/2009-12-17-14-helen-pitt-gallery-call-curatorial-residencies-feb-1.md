CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS: CURATORIAL RESIDENCIES
HELEN PITT GALLERY ARTIST RUN CENTRE

Submission Deadline: February 1st, 2010

The Helen Pitt Gallery ARC is commencing a new mode of programming
that will feature an ongoing series of curatorial residencies
(scheduled to commence in the Spring of 2010). These six-week
project-based residencies are intended to offer curatorial
opportunities to a wide range of professional experience(s) and
approaches. Ideas addressing social, political, cultural and critical
issues outside of commercial and/or conventional art industry
practices will be a priority, in keeping with the gallery's mandate.
This new residency program aims to increase support to local and
emerging practices by welcoming submissions from a diverse pool of
candidates that may include: practicing artists, recent graduates of
contemporary art related programmes and professionally recognized
curators.

Successful candidates will be responsible for coordinating all aspects
of an exhibition, including the development of a curatorial focus,
engagement and communications with artists and contributors, the
drafting of publicity materials and installation duties. Guest
curators will be engaged in ongoing consultations with the gallery's
Board of Directors throughout their residency. Residents will also
receive support from staff, co-op students and volunteers while
realizing their curatorial project.

The Helen Pitt Gallery is a non-profit artist run center dedicated to
the promotion of experimental contemporary art that addresses social,
political, cultural, and critical issues outside of the mainstream
commercial art practice. Emerging artists, critics and curators
continue to find opportunities and support at the Helen Pitt Gallery.
The gallery is committed to showcasing challenging works from local,
national and international artists in all media. The gallery provides
administrative support to artists and curators in the design and
implementation of exhibitions.

Submission Guidelines

Applicants must submit a digital proposal including the following components:

- a one-page project proposal, describing the curatorial project and
its relationship to the mandate of the Helen Pitt Gallery Artist Run
Centre
- a current CV (maximum 3 pages)
- related portfolio (pdf or jpegs, maximum 15 images, or up to 10
minutes of video)
- an image list to accompany portfolio

If you require further information regarding Curatorial Residencies,
please contact the Helen Pitt Gallery at info@helenpittgallery.org.

Please email all Submission Materials to the following address:
info@helenpittgallery.org

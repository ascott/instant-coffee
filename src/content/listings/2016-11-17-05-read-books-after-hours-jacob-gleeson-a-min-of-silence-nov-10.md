A min. of silence, 2016 
HD video, 1 min loop
Jacob Gleeson 

November 10 - December 7 
5 pm - 12 am

'Clearing the mind and sliding in 
to that created space, 
a web of waters steaming over rocks, 
air misty but not raining'

'Who has come up with 
these miraculous forests and springs? 
Pale ink 
on fine white silk'

- Gary Snyder from 'Endless Streams and Mountains' 

For this iteration of After Hours in the READ Books window, Vancouver based 
artist Jacob Gleeson presents an intimate portrait of a natural hot spring in an 
undisclosed Pacific Northwest location. 
Within the image, moss thrives on fallen trunks and severed boughs as cedar 
and sword ferns frame the lush scene. Geothermal steam rises to meet the 
sunlight filtering down through tall firs. The illuminated clouds hang meditatively 
over the pool below ad infinitum. 
Gleeson shares an experience of transcendent repose in an undeveloped 
pocket of BC wilderness. An unexpected diversion from the quotidian lull for 
observant tourists and passersby. 

Jacob Gleeson is a Vancouver based artist and facilitator whose work has 
appeared in numerous galleries, festivals and publications including: SFU 
Gallery, Western Front, Belkin Satellite, Catriona Jeffries, Charles H. Scott, Helen 
Pitt, VIVO, Access, Open Space, Noorderzon Film Festival (NL), Nuit Blanche, 
Contemporary Magazine, Fillip, Pyramid Power and Vancouver Art & Economies 
(Artspeak).

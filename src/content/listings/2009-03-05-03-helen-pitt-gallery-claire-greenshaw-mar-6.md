The Helen Pitt Gallery Artist Run Centre

Claire Greenshaw
Are You Getting Smart With Me?

March 7 - April 10, 2009
Opening Reception: Friday Mar 6th, 7 - 10pm

The Helen Pitt Gallery Artist Run Centre is delighted to present Are you
getting smart with me? an exhibition of new work by Canadian artist Claire
Greenshaw. This series of drawings and sculptures are consistent with the
subtle inquiries into materiality and representation that - through diverse
media - Greenshaw has investigated in her post-conceptual, studio-based
practice.

Claire Greenshaw is a Canadain artist currently based in Scotland. She has
exhibited throughout Canada, in the UK and Sweden at the Charles H. Scott
Gallery, Vancouver, The Khyber Artist Run Centre, Halifax, Mount Saint
Vincent University Gallery, Halifax and the Neon Gallery, Sweden. She
received her BFA from the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design, Halifax
and is currently completing her MFA at the Glasgow School of Art.

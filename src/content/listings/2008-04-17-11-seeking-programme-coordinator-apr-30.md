Artspeak Programme Coordinator 
Deadline: April 30, 2008 


DESCRIPTION: 

Artspeak is seeking a to fill the full time position of Programme Coordinator. Working with and reporting to the Director/Curator, the Programme Coordinator assumes responsibility in the areas of publicity, communications, exhibitions and publications, volunteer liaison, project coordination, gallery administration and public interface. 


TASKS: 
 
Programming 
• Assists the Director/Curator in programming projects, exhibitions, events and publications, including communications with artists and writers. 

Communications 
• Implementation of publicity plans developed in consultation with the Director/Curator. 
• Press/media liaison, mailings and publicity associated with programming. 
• Production of publicity materials with Director/Curator and contract designers. 
• Correspondence and other public interface regarding exhibition policy, submissions, general information and publications. 
• Maintenance of gallery systems such as publication files, archives, support materials, computer hardware, manuals, and filing systems. 
• Develops and manages publicity, marketing and communications budget. 

Volunteer and Staff Supervision 
• Schedules and supervises volunteer support for mailings, installation and maintenance work. 
• Supervises contract, seasonal and program staff as required. 

Exhibitions/Projects 
• Works with artists to install exhibitions, coordinates display elements such as signage, oversees the maintenance of the gallery and organizes gallery openings and receptions. 
• Has some technical knowledge of presentation requirements. 

Administrative 
• Book-keeping and accounting as requested by the Director/Curator. 
• Assist in grant preparation as requested by Director/Curator. 
• Fundraising development on a project basis. 
• Participation in fundraising efforts of the Board of Directors. 
• Participation in Board Meetings and annual Board Retreats. 


Vacations 
The Programme Coordinator will have one month paid vacation during the summer period when the gallery is normally closed. An additional 14 days holiday time will be granted during the December/January holiday shutdown period. Paid statutory holidays.  




Professional Practice 
Given the principles of support of the professional practice of artist-run centre staff, Artspeak shall endeavor to be flexible to allow for the continuation of the professional practice of the Programme Coordinator.  This shall be determined in consultation with the Director/Curator. 


Contracting 
This is a contracted position, renewed annually with a performance review. 


Qualifications 
• Local and international contemporary arts knowledge. 
• Strong administrative skills: organization, efficiency, problem-solving, team work, self-direction. 
• Experience working with tight deadlines and project-based activities. 
• Strong computer skills and ability to learn new skills. 
• Excellent written and verbal skills. 
• Ability to multi-task and to work in a close environment. 
• BA or higher in arts related field preferred. 
• Gallery experience an asset. 
 

SALARY: $29,000 plus benefits

CALL FOR ENTRIES

-=-=-=-=-=-=-

switch is now issuing an open call to artists working with film, lens 
based work and moving image 
to submit work responding to the theme of ‘movements’

switch is an event that promotes and shows contemporary works of 
moving image in a public context. 
switch has to date shown the artworks of 31 international artists who 
have through the project invited the public into their practice, ideas and 
viewpoints. 
switch locates itself outside of the big city and applies itself to the 
rhythm of smaller places.

The event itself will be accompanied by a publication & a website.
An artist fee of Euro 250 [incl. VAT] will be paid per selected artist .

to apply: http://www.s-w-i-t-c-h.org

deadline for entries: 7th December 2012

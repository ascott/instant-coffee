Cindy Mochizuki

Ancestral Dreams and Other Premonitions

April 20 to July 6, 2024

Central Gallery, Kamloops Art Gallery
Curated by Charo Neville

Artist and Curator Conversation: Saturday, April 20, 5:30 to 6:30 pm

Opening Reception: Saturday, April 20, 6:30 to 8:00 pm

Cindy
Mochizukis immersive multi-media exhibition holds space for the stories of
Japanese Canadians through audio fiction, performance, animation, and community
engagement.

The Kamloops Art Gallerys spring exhibition Ancestral Dreams and Other
Premonitions, curated by Charo Neville, opens April 20. In this solo exhibition,
Vancouver-based artist Cindy Mochizuki, re-tells stories drawn from the Japanese
Canadian experience. Employing audio fiction, performance, animation, and
community engagement, Mochizukis multi-media installation weaves family,
community, and memory to shed light on invisible histories and archives. Mochizukis
practice opens pathways for Japanese Canadians to share stories shaped by trauma
and reveal a larger narrative of Japanese resilience in Canada, often drawing from
her familys history within internment camps in Canada during World War II and their
post-war experiences in Japan.

Step into the real and imagined realms of Ancestral Dreams and Other Premonitions
through four key artworks. Autumn Strawberry and Tides & Moons: Herring Capital
illuminate the resilience of Japanese Canadian communities amidst adversity, The
Sakaki Tree, a Jewel, and the Mirror explores folklore through 50 enigmatic figurines
and performances, and Cave to Dream features four fictional videos with sound,
hand-drawn animation, and live-action video.

Join us for the opening reception and visit Ancestral Dreams and Other Premonitions
in the Central Gallery from April 20 to July 6, 2024.

Time-Lapse: Posthumous Conversations - A Geoff McMurchy Retrospective

October 29th - December 1st

Opening reception: Thursday, October 29, 2020 from 5pm - 8pm PST

SUM Gallery

#268 425 Keefer St

A memorial retrospective of visual art by Geoff McMurchy, a storm force fag who blew
open disability art in Canada and whose legacy includes a generation of disabled artists
who thrived under his mentorship. Curated by Yuri Arajs, SD Holman and Persimmon
Blackbridge in partnership with Kickstart and All Souls at Mountainview Cemetery.

Visit sumgallery.ca for more details on how to attend our opening reception and access
the exhibition by appointment and according to COVID-19 protocols.

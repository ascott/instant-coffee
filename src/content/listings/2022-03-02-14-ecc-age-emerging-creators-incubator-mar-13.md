Situated on the
shores of Lafarge Lake in Town Centre Park, Evergreen Cultural
Centre (ECC) and the Art Gallery at Evergreen (AGE) exist on the unceded, traditional
and ancestral core territory of the kik"m (Kwikwetlem) First Nation, which lies
within the shared territories of the sli"lwta tmx (Tsleil-Waututh), Slh Tmxw
(St:l), qiqyt (Qayqayt) and sq"c"iy"a tmx (Katzie), xmkwy"m (Musqueam)
and Swxw7mesh xwumixw (Squamish) nations in the region known today as the
Tri-Cities.

We are pleased to announce the second iteration of the Emerging Creators Incubator
from May"August, 2022. We will invite a group of six multidisciplinary emerging
creators between the ages of 18-30 to participate in a mentorship and residency
program that includes hands-on workshops and studio visits with professional artist
mentors including Rydel Cerezo and kimmortal, and a collaboration workshop with
guest artists.

Emerging creators will receive a $450 exhibition fee, ongoing support from Evergreen
staff, and access to our
facilities (including studio space, performing arts theatre, a kiln,
wood-working shop, etc.) to make work for a one-month group showcase in August.

Who is an Emerging Creator?

An emerging creator is defined as an artist who works in any discipline within the visual
and performing arts. They are between the ages of 18-30, in the early stages of their
professional career, and are committed to the development of their craft and practice.

We welcome and will prioritize applications from Black and Indigenous artists,
racialized people, disabled, neurodivergent, persons of
diverse sexual orientations, gender identities or expressions (LGBTQ2S+), migrants,
and any of these intersections. Please head to the application where you will find our
commitment to Justice, Equity, Inclusion and Anti-Racism to learn more about the
concrete action we are taking.

The Emerging Creators Incubator centers a multidisciplinary approach to the arts and
will prioritize local emerging artists from the
Tri-Cities area who engage in the
performing, written and visual arts. Applications are also open to emerging creators
from across the Vancouver Lower Mainland who are interested in engaging with the
ECC and AGE.

Application Deadline:
The written application deadline is March 13, 2022 at 11:59 p.m.; the interview deadline
is March 10, 2022 at 6:00 p.m.
All applicants will be notified about the success of their applications before April 29,
2022.

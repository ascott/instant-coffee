The National accessArts Centre is seeking applications for an exciting project this
fall!

In partnership with the Korean Foundation for International Cultural Exchange
(KOFICE), NaAC is recruiting 5 Canadian artists living with disabilities to participate
in a touring exhibition and cultural exchange program from September 23rd and
October 4th 2025.

What will artists be doing?

Selected artists will be traveling alongside 5 artists from Korea. Together, they will go
to Canmore (Alberta), Toronto (Onterio), and Ottawa (Onterio) to exhibit their
artwork, engage in cultural exchange activities, and explore each citys vibrant arts
scene.

What expenses will be covered?

All travel expenses will be covered for successful artists and one support, including
airfare and ground transportation, meals, accommodations, and per diem. All artists
will receive an exhibition fee for showing their work.

Who can apply?

Applications are open to all artists who meet the following criteria:

A Canadian resident identifying as having a developmental, physical, and/ or
acquired disability.
A practicing, emerging, or mid-level career artist working in a visual art medium.
18 years of age or older.

What do I need to include in my application?

A complete application form
A statement of interest
An artist bio
An artist statement
A CV (if available)
An artists headshot
Maximum of 10 support images of the artists artwork
A signed declaration form

When is the deadline?

Applications will be open until July 11th, 2025. After applications close, they will be
assessed by a jury. All applicants will be notified by late July, 2025.

How can I apply?

Please visit www.accessarts.ca and locate the News and Stories page for more
information or to access application forms. You may also click this link to go directly
to the webpage.

Opening the same night as the Issue 10 launch is LA artist Kirsten Stoltmann's 
exhibition, "My Therapist is a Real Wet Blanket".
Consisting of paintings on magazine pages and a single video work, this will be 
her first Canadian solo exhibition.

Kirsten Stoltman has exhibited internationally including the Saatchi Gallery,

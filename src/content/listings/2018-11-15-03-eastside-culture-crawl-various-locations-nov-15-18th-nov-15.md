The ECCS hosts an annual 4-day visual arts festival the "Culture Crawl" in November that 
involves artists on Vancouver's Eastside opening their studio to the public. The event is focused 
on the area bounded by Columbia St, 1st Ave, Victoria Drive, and the Waterfront and involves 
painters, jewelers, sculptors, furniture makers, weavers, potters, printmakers, photographers, 
glassblowers; from emerging artists to those internationally established.

http://culturecrawl.ca/program

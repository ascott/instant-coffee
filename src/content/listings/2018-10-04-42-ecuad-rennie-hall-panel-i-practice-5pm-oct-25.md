PANEL I Practice
ECUAD
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Thursday, October 25 | 5 pm
Rennie Hall

Join us for the first edition of this year's Practice series, featuring presentations and 
group discussions with artists Gailan Ngan (ceramics), Bracken Hanuse Corlett (multi-
media), Claire Sherzinger (painting), and Lucien Durey (sculpture, art writing). 

Practice is a semi-annual series of lectures and discussions for students and emerging 
artists. In this session, panelists from a wide variety of artistic disciplines share how they 
first emerged into their field. 

The panel presentation will be followed by a moderated Q + A and breakout sessions.

Practice is presented by the Audain Faculty of Art in partnership with the Faculty of 
Culture + Community and the Career + Professional Development Office.

James Jeans first solo exhibition in Canada is now open! In this exhibition, Jean
creatively utilizes visual motifs that weave together mythological and folkloric narratives
from both Western and Asian contexts, fostering a fusion that resonates with a diverse
audience. His unique combination of characters, animals, and intricate patterns forms a
tableau of cultural ambiguity, reflecting the complex identities forged by migration and
globalization. He addresses the linguistic and semantic tensions within his heritage by
depicting Chinese radicals in an unformed and abstract state"a deliberate choice that
highlights the simultaneous familiarity and estrangement, poignantly reflecting on the
challenges of language and meaning, and the pursuit of an
authentic personal history.

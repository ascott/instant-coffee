Film Night: Anselm (2023)
Date: Thursday, November 28th
Time: Door opens 7:30 PM | Movie starts 8:00 PM
Location: Paul Kyle Gallery, 258 E 1st Ave. Unit 4 (Second floor), V5T 1A6

Directed by Wim Wenders, Anselm is a mesmerizing documentary that delves into
the life and work of renowned German contemporary artist Anselm Kiefer,
recognized for his monumental paintings, installations, and sculptures. Kiefers work
engages with themes of memory, history, morality, and humanitys darkest chapters,
particularly German fascism and the Holocaust. His works draw inspiration from the
poetry of Paul Celan, weaving words into visual narratives that elicit layers of
meaning and emotion.

The film captures the scale and ambition of Kiefers practice, exploring his
connection to Germanys dark history. The film examines how art is used to confront
and reconcile historical trauma, his provocative use of fascist iconography
and the
critiques of those who question his approach.

Beyond the artistic process of Kiefer's work, Anselm is a serious and contemplative
meditaition on the purpose and impact of art. It probes the ways in which art can
illuminate and challenge cultural and historical perspectives. Wenders direction,
combined with the films captivating cinematography, constructs a narrative that
invites the audience to contemplate on the role of art in preserving collective memory
and identity, while honouring Kiefers legacy.

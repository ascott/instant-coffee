We're making a mahjong film, and we want to include you in it! Whether you're a
complete beginner or an expert, playing richii, Hong Kong or American style, we
want you to come play with us. In partnership with Odd Meridian Arts, we are hosting
a day for community, connection, and games. Come join us for a day, free play
mahjong, and we may capture your dancing hands to use in our film!

This event is a fundraiser to help us boost the artist fees of the many incredible
people we are working with on our film. Entry, door prizes tickets, drinks and snacks
all by sliding scale donation!

Thanks to Odd Meridian Arts for generously donating Morrow as a space to host and
film our project!

Time/Date: 11am-3pm; August 3, 2025
Location: Morrow (910 Richards St " Unit 204, Vancouver BC)
Admission: Sliding scale $5-20 (all proceeds will go towards the film)

Information about the film:
With the evolution of many cultural variations of mahjong, we use the game as a way
to demonstrate the bridging of cultural divides. How can people with different skill
levels and different ways of playing come together to share a game of mahjong?
"chattering of sparrows" is a movement film, commissioned by the 2025 Festival of
Recorded Movement, and co-created by Andie Lloyd, Amlia Simard, and Ronnie
Cheng.

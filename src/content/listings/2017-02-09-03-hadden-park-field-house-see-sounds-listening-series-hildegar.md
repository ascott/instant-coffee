See Sounds Listening Series: Hildegard Westerkamp
Thursday Feb 9, 2017, 8pm
Hadden Park Field House, 1015 Maple St, Vancouver

It's time for our fourteenth monthly listening party at the field house! Bring something 
you would like to listen to together. Discussion will follow. We will have most available 
formats (digital, vinyl, CD, tape, radio). 

Meet at the field house at 8pm. Our guest presentation will start at 8:30. It will be a 
phones off environment!

As part of our See Sounds series, we are featuring a guest artist each listening party to 
curate the early portion of the evening. We have invited UBC History professor 
Alejandra Bronfman to be this month's guest. Bronfman's research explores 
Broadcasting, Sound, Listening and Sonic Archives specific to the Caribbean and Latin 
America. Her current book project, Isles of Noise: Sonic Media in the Caribbean records 
the unwritten histories of radio and related sonic technologies in the early twentieth 
century.

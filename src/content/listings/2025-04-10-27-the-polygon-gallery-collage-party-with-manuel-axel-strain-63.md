Join The Polygon Gallkery for a collage party led by artist Manuel Axel Strain, whose
exhibition xlmx child is on view at The Polygon until May 11. In xlmx child,
Manuel Axel Strain draws on Musqueam, Secwpemc, and Syilx ways of knowing,
and the discipline of Western psychology. Compositing theories of mind from across
their different cultures, Strain imagines the perspective of a child who contemplates
the world from beyond these existing frameworks. Through figurative paintings,
transformed into pictographs and set against photographic murals, Strains work
proposes a way of seeing that suspends judgement and challenges divisions such
as past and future, old and new, self and other.

About Manuel Axel Strain

Manuel Axel Strain (b. 1990) is a 2Spirit artist from the lands and waters of the
xmkym (Musqueam), Simpcw, and Syilx peoples, based in the sacred
homelands of their q"ic"y" (Katzie) and qa:n"n" (Kwantlen) relatives. Creating
artwork in collaboration with and reference to their family, shared experiences
become a source of agency for Strain. They perceive their work to confront and
undermine the imposed realities of colonialism, proposing a new space beyond its
oppressive systems of power. Strain was recently featured in the Vancouver Special
exhibition at the Vancouver Art Gallery (2021), longlisted for the 2022 Sobey Award,
and was a 2022 recipient of the Portfolio Prize.

Feb 11, 2:00pm - 4:00pm
221 E Georgia

Join us at 221A for the launch of Mark Delong and Antoni Oko's yearly calendar, a result 
of their collaborative drawing practice. They will be drawing on the covers of the 
calendars that you might purchase. 1 for $30 or 2 for $50. Soup will be served. 

Facts:
The new Salamander is 11x17 closed, 22x34 open. 28 pages. Feb 2017 - Feb 2018. 
Black laser printed on 20lb paper. Black spiral bound. Unprinted white 65lb card cover. 
Each cover has an original drawing. We've got tools. 100 copies. Family show. Mark's 
dry jokes. 

Have a lucky year of the Rooster with a 2017 Salamander.

This event is co-presented with the Vancouver Art Gallery in the context of its current 
exhibition, Vancouver Special: Ambivalent Pleasures. Read more about the library and 
public program:

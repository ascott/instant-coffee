On ghosts:

The Ghost, as an undecidable, does not conform to either polarity of a
dichotomy
(eg. present/absent, cure/poison, and inside/outside). The figure of a
ghost seems
to be neither present or absent, or alternatively, it is both present and
absent at the same time.

( ) ( )

On gift-giving poo:

It is customary during a festive parade for participants to usually provide
the spectators with some sort
of sugary sweet. If, rather than a lollipop, a token of arguably desirable
excrement is offered by a participant
in guise and anonymity, how then does this ghostly offering trouble notions
of obligation and reciprocation
within a gift-giving transaction?

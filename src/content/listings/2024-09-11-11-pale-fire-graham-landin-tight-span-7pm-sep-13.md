Graham Landin (b. 1982) is a visual artist and carpenter who lives and works in
Richmond, BC. Drawing influence from classical antiquity, off-grid culture, folk art
and a variety of modernisms, Landin makes pavilions, sculptures, furniture objects
and wall reliefs. Since relocating his studio to agricultural land in 2018, he has
employed a chainsaw to work swiftly at the scale of the human body. His work has
been exhibited in Canada and the United States, and he has created over thirty
sculptures for Stssy Internationals flagship stores.

CARFAC Ontario is seeking applications for the position of Managing Director.
Position Type: Part-time (28 hours per week)
Salary Range: Compensation to be negotiated based on experience (salary range:
hourly rate equivalent to $45K-$50K per annum)
Application Deadline: Friday, December 20, 2019

Job Description:

CARFAC Ontario is seeking a dynamic, creative, and organized individual for the role of
the Managing Director.

The Managing
Director is a key leadership role for running all aspects of the
organization in collaboration with a deeply-engaged Board of Directors. The Board
offers suggestions and ideas on how to improve organization actions, while the
Managing Director decides how to implement these ideas to the best of their ability.
They will assume hands-on responsibilities for the design, development, and
implementation of all strategic plans for the organization in a manner that is both cost-
efficient and timely, and provide regular reports to the Board. They are also responsible
for all day-to-day operations including administration and communications; managing a
small team of staff, committees, and volunteers; fundraising and grant writing; as well as
financial and general operations of the organization. The Managing Director plays a
leadership and advocacy role for the organization, its members, and the arts community.
They often fulfill a motivational capacity in addition to office-based work to lead
the
organization in all development areas.

For more information and how to apply please visit https://www.carfacontario.ca/job-
managing-director

Deadline for Applications Extended to Friday, December 20, 2019.

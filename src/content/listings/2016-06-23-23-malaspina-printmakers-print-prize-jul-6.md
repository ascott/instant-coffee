Call for submissions: 101 Prints Print Prize
Deadline: Wednesday, July 6, at midnight

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to announce our first call for
submissions and biannual Print Prize, in support of our 101 Prints
Gala & Draw.

101 Prints, our biannual fundraising event, supports the year-round
charitable programs that we provide through our gallery and studio.

This year, our jury will award three prizes to support artists in the
production of new print works in our studio:

1st Prize: Six-month Visiting Residency for the production of new
work

2nd Prize: Three months free studio rent for the production of new
work

3rd Prize: One free workshop to learn a new print technique

Our jury comprises Malaspina Printmakers Executive Director
Justin Muir and Program Manager Francesca Szuszkiewicz, and
invited guests, artist Barb Choit, and Burnaby Art Gallery Assistant
Curator Jennifer Cane.

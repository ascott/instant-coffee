Hello Friends of Trench,

Please join us in celebrating Visual Art in Vancouver with the closing 
party for Monomania:an organic archive of the Terminal City avant-
garde 1949 till now! The party is set for Thursday August 25th from 
6:30 pm till 9ish and any of the artists will be in attendance !

Trench is also pleased to announce the exhibition of some very 
special early photo conceptual works by Vancouver artist Laura 
Baird in our back gallery. These works have not been exhibited 
since her1978 exhibition " A Laconic Correspondence" at the Helen 
Pitt Galleryand are exceptionally beautiful. This show is on display 
now until September 3rd.

Finally,since starting on June 23rd Monomania has become 
increasingly popular and of course now with the review by Robin 
Laurence in last weeks Georgia Straight things are getting 
downright out of hand!
http://www.straight.com/article-419557/vancouver/monomania-
celebrates-vancouver-art So to ensure everyone has a chance to 
see the show we have decided to extend it by one week. 
Monomania's new closing date is now September 10th! 

Thank you for your interest in the work going on in the Trench and 
we look forward to seeing you next week! 

Best Regards,

Craig

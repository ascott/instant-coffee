Call for fall 2023 and spring 2024 Residents

Are you a visual artist?

Writing a play?

Working on some poetry?

Apply to the Cobbler Shop Residency for a one month stay.

We offer a space for thinking, reading,
making, and sharing. You will have access to a
small studio and sleeping space in the old town of Lunenburg NS.

The Cobbler Shop is a small out building on the same property as a separate larger
house. It is located in Lunenburg NS, on the unceded territory of the Mi'kmaq people.
The building was historically used as a cobbler shop and is currently used as a studio.
Accepted applicants will have exclusive use of the upstairs space of the cobbler shop
for the duration of their stay, and the whole space after 5pm till the following day at
9am. The ground floor is a shared studio space between 9am-5pm.

The studio is equipped with electricity, and wifi, but no modern plumbing. Residents will
have access to a shared bathroom in the house beside the studio. There is no running
water in the studio space, there is a refillable water dispenser suitable for drinking
water and an outdoor faucet.

**Accessibility note: Unfortunately this is currently not an accessible space as there are
stairs
between at entrances to both buildings, and the studio is up a set of stairs.

On costs and funding:

As we are new; we can only offer CARFAC fees for one artist talk or workshop. The
Artist-in-Residence is responsible for securing their own funding for food, and travel
expenses.

Deadline for Fall 2023 residency is July 14th.

Deadline for Spring 2024 is September 10th.

To apply, please submit in a single email to cobblershopresidency@gmail.com

subject line cobbler-shop residency application

Don't hesitate to reach out if you have questions.

CV
Short Artist Statement (300 word max)
Project Proposal (500 words max) please include types of materials that you intend to
use. As some projects and materials might not be suitable for the space.
Max 10 Images of your work (image format: JPEG) Please include a corresponding
image list with titles, dates, dimensions, and material/medium

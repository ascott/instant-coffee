So Long Summer Slideshow/ David Wisdom/ in the Gallery
Tuesday September 8, 7pm
Former CBC radio personality and photographer David Wisdom hosts his
legendary communal slideshows, featuring a selection of works by local
artists with musical accompaniment.

This special farewell to summer slideshow features presentations of images
set to music by acclaimed artists Sophia Burke, Aaron Carpenter, Adad
Hannah, Doug Lander and Alex Morrison and other special guests.

Tickets: $5 for Members and Students (with valid ID)
$10 for non-members
Tickets available at the Gallery on September 8 from the Membership desk
starting at 6pm. Doors and cash bar open at 6:30pm

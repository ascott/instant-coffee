REGENERATION: Breaking Time with Indigenous Video Games | Saturday, October 19
@ 8pm. Western Front, Grand Luxe Hall

Curated by Maize Longboat (Kanien'keh:ka), REGENERATION transforms the Luxe
theatre into a video arcade for an evening. The event brings together work by Indigenous
developers that engage game design as a medium to tell stories, both traditional and not, on
their own terms.

Games and makers include:

Thunderbird Strike
Elizabeth LaPense (Anishinaabe/Mtis)

When Rivers Were Trails
Elizabeth LaPense (Anishinaabe/Mtis), Nichlas Emmons (Miami/Shawnee) and Weshoyot
Alvitre (Tongva)

PURITY&decay
Achimostawinan Games

Tipi Kaga
Carl Petersen and Oohe Numpa Lakota (Cheyenne River Sioux
Tribe)

Hold My Hand
Nathan Powless-Lynes (Mohawk)

Kakwitene VR
MoniGarr (Onkwehonwe)

Maoriland Adventures
Johnson Witehira (Maori)

Wao Kanaka (the Realm of Humans)
Ka Lei Milika'a

The event is also the official public launch for Terra Nova, a two-player cooperative
platformer created by SlipCycle, led by Maize Longboat (creative director/producer),
Mehrdad Dehdashti (technical director), Ray Caplin (artist/animator) and Beatrix Moersch
(sound designer).

REGENERATION is a partnership between Western Front, Heart Projector Arcade and
Maize Longboat.

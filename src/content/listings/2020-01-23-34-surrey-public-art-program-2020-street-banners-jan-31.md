Call for Concept Proposals for 2020 Street Banners

- Download all details at [https://www.surrey.ca/culture-recreation/30520.aspx](https://www.surrey.ca/culture-recreation/30520.aspx)
- Deadline to Apply: Friday, January 31, 2020, 4pm
- Compensation: $2,500 for selected design
- Eligibility: Open to all artists currently residing in BC

This call is for concept proposals for 2020 Public Art Banners. The theme for the call is: Surrey:
Love Where You Live. This competition is open to all visual artists and designers currently
living in British Columbia. Students of fine art, design, and architecture schools are encouraged
to
apply. Design proposals must be submitted by Friday, January 31, 2020, 4pm. You can
propose up to three different banner designs. Dont miss this exciting opportunity to share
your art with the community in Surrey!

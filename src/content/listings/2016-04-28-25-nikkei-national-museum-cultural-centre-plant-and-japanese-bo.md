Spring Bazaar, Plant Sale and Japanese book sale

Saturday, May 7, 2016
11am-3pm
Nikkei Centre
6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby (Kingsway & Sperling)
604.777.7000|www.nikkeiplace.org

Quality Japanese gifts, housewares and a broad selection of plants will
be on sale on Saturday May 7 at the Nikkei National Museum &
Cultural Centre. To add on, food vendors will also be there to showcase
a selection of popular Japanese festival snacks, food and drinks.
This year we are extremely excited to conduct our ever-in-demand
book sale as a simultaneous bonus. This is the largest Japanese book
sale in town with over 30,000 prime condition used books, comics and
magazines for a discounted price. Find something on that new hobby
you are picking up, or even get adventurous and pick up a new comic
series!

For more information on the festival or other programs and events held
at Nikkei Centre, please visit www.nikkeiplace.org or call the centre at

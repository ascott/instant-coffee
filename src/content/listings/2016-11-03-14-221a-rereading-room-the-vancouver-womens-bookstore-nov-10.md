Opening Reception: November 10 at 7pm
Full duration: November 11, 2016 - January 14, 2017
Public Programming: http://221a.ca/rereading-room

Rereading Room presents a collection of books, documents, and periodicals 
selected from the titles offered by the Vancouver Women's Bookstore (1973-1996) in 
its nascent years. Join us for the opening reception and discussion between 
Alexandra Bischoff and founding member Jeannine Mitchell. Consider this an 
invitation to the Bookstore's restaging. Come by, read, maybe talk over coffee; we 
have a to to catch up on.

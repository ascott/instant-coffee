Lief Hall announces the release of her new album Roses for Ruins, a collection of 
songs which blend electronic beats, foreboding synths and haunting vocal 
harmonies into pop-flirting melodies which explore the connection between the 
personal and the political, nature, technology and the subconscious.

Lief Hall is an interdisciplinary artist and musician whose practice incorporates 
sound, installation, video, multimedia performance and choreography. Her 
interdisciplinary installation and performance works explore themes of nature, 
technology and the body as they relate to mythology, mysticism and the posthuman 
voice.

Mourning Coup

Mourning Coup is a classic rock tribute band commonly mistaken for experimental 
pop. Spear-headed by Chandra Melting Tallow, failed amateur lip syncher and 
sculptor. The art of psychic defense as song writing becomes auditory talismans in a 
tidy little package known as rock and roll. In 2015 Mourning Coup released one full 
length LP on No Sun Recordings to the pleasure of a handful of music bloggers. 
Their mascot is a scorpion, red is the color of my true loves history.

Michelle Mackenzie

Michelle Helene Mackenzie writes music for animals, plants, and furies, against bully
hypnotists, reptilian sham glam, and world-eating industries. Assemblages are 
arranged from modular synthesizers, found sounds, digital processing and layered 
acoustic samplings. Mackenzie completed a BA at Simon Fraser University, after 
which she enrolled in the doctoral program in Literature at Duke University. While 
there, she became interested in literatures of necrophiliac-agonies, the possibilities 
of sounding the unheard hills of banshee-perturbations, and the violence and cultural 
amnesia that devours female genius. Mackenzies recent sound works have been 
performed and exhibited at The Western Front, 221A, Sunset Terrace, and Polygon 
Gallery, as well as released in conjunction with written works via The Capilano 
Review's Small Caps (Vancouver) and The Operating System (Brooklyn) . She has 
upcoming sound collaborations at Esker Foundation (Calgary) with Vanessa Brown, 
and at Malik (Hamburg) with Alison Yip. Her tape, All the Little Objectiles, will be 
released in the spring of 2018 via IsLa.

https://soundcloud.com/bombyx_mori

Gabi Dao

Gabi Dao thinks through the structures that mediate memory, history and identity 
with sculpture, installation, sound, writing. She collectively programs, organizes and
manages the studio/project space Duplex in Vancouver on unceded Coast Salish
territories, including the territories of the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh
Nations. She also produces, edits and publishes the podcast series Here Nor There.

A quadraphonic composition of soft synths, cinematic sound samples and field
recordings set a tableau for an interview between Bina48 (a humanoid AI robot
developed by Hanson Robotics) and the 'real', transhumanist Bina Rothblatt. The 
piece will play between sets.

http://gabidao.tumblr.com/

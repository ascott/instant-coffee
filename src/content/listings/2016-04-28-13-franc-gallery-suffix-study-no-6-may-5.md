Ed Spence: Soft Error
April 14th - May 14th

Performance "Suf(fix) Study No. 6"
Thursday May 5th, 2016 @ 7pm & 9pm
Duration 20min

Suf(fix) Study No. 6 is an excerpt from a full length piece created by
Julianne Chapple, current choreographer in residence at the Scotiabank
Dance Centre, in collaboration with artist Ed Spence and performer Maxime
Chadburn. Activated by the performer as she navigates the room, a
sculptural element distorts and illuminates the darkened space and acts as
a physical embodiment of the virtual. The performer's image, reflected and
multiplied, suggests a meditation on our finite lives in a conceivably
infinite universe. By drawing on the theme of body and its shifting
position in a Post-human world, this piece finds common ground with the
current exhibition, Soft Error.

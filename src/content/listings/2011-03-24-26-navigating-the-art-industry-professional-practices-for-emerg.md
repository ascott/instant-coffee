Navigating the Art Industry is a unique 3-hour seminar designed
specifically for emerging artists working in Canada. It is ideal for fine
artists of any medium pursuing careers in the commercial Vancouver art
market or those looking to apply to higher education. 

Presenter and Vancouver curator Pennylane Shen has taught full courses in
New York and Toronto. Navigating the Art Industry is a condensed
introductory version, combining theory, industry experience and practical
resources for marketing oneself in the arts.

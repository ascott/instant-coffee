Ultimate Traveller: Impressions of Madagascar
October 30
7-9 p.m.

Madagascar is often referred to as the eighth continent and the islands legendary uniqueness
is best reflected by its plethora of amazing lemurs, colourful
chameleons, many endemic bird
species and a fascinating array of endemic flora, all of which combine to make for a truly
exceptional photo safari. This travel talk provides an introduction to the nature reserves and
cultural sights of this fascinating island.

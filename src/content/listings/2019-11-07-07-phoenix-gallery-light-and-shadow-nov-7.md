Light and Shadow c/o The Phoenix Gallery

A cooperative show hosted by Jolene Simpson and James Welk and The Phoenix
Gallery.

We are turning out the lights to elicit thoughts of texture, sound & environment. As the
light hits discarded objects and things, we wish to offer a meditation of Light and
Shadow. Trash and objects are only as literal or invisible as the light playing on them.

Spark! Emerging collisions between art and science

Date/Time: Friday November 13, 6pm-10pm
Location: VIVO Media Arts Centre (2625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver, BC)
Facebook page: [https://bit.ly/SparkArtScience](https://bit.ly/SparkArtScience)
Tickets: $5 - $20 (sliding scale). Proceeds support future art-science
co-labs and events.

SPARK! is an interactive art-science exhibition co-produced by
Curiosity Collider and VIVO Media Arts. Our show spotlights 18
interdisciplinary exhibits by 15 artists & scientists that demonstrate how
art changes our experience of science. From 2- and 3-dimensional art
to animation and interactive exhibits, VIVOs warehouse sets the stage
for new expressions of science.

SPARK! features the emerging art-science collisions of local artists like
Christopher Rodrigues, Erick James, Dzee Louise and more. Exhibits
include original collaborative works with scientists and technology
experts. Some works will be offered for sale. Join us for a drink at
VIVOs cash bar, interact with the art and their creators. Satisfy your
curiosity.

Spark! is curated by Vancouver artist and Curiosity Collider Creative
Director, Char Hoyt.

Participating artists and scientists: Aileen Penner, Char Hoyt,
Christopher Rodrigues, Daniel DeGagne, Dzee Louise, Erick James,
Erik Zepka, Jaedan Leimert, Jeremiah Birnbaum, Julia Maddison, Luke
Blackstone, Michelle Weinstein, Patrick Keeling, Robi Smith, and Willa Downing.

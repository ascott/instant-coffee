LOGIC PRO - INTRO

This workshop will introduce students to Apple's Logic Pro, a
software-based audio sequencing, recording and editing package. In these
sessions, students will learn the basics of audio and MIDI recording and
sequencing, how to arrange and manipulate objects in the timeline, add and
modulate effects, use Logic's native virtual instruments, use busses to
route audio in and out of Logic, and mixing down (bouncing) projects for
publication. In addition, we will cover the setting up of audio and MIDI
interfaces which allow the user to send audio and MIDI signals in and out
of the sequencer (very important!) The aim of the session is to leave you
with the knowledge necessary to create and publish a basic project from the
compositional/audio capture stage right through to exporting to CD WAVE,
AIFF or MP3.

These sessions do not require any previous sequencing experience, but it
would of course be beneficial. Sessions will take into account the level of
knowledge of the participants and be adapted accordingly.

Participants must provide their own laptop with a version of Logic
installed (Logic Express, Logic 7, Logic Pro 8 or Logic Pro 9).
Participants are also advised to bring an audio/MIDI interface if they have
one, and headphones.

This workshop aims to address a variety of issues particular to the
individual attendees of the class, going beyond the most basic principles
of sequencing and audio recording and editing. These might include, but are
not limited to, simultaneous multitrack recording, mix and effect
automation, destructive and non-destructive audio file editing, audio bus
and submix routing, virtual instruments, project and file management,
keyboard shortcuts and techniques for increasing the speed of your
workflow. The goal of the workshop is to resolve any issues the users may
have with interfacing with Logic in order to ultimately increase their
power, speed and overall ability while using the software..

Participants are asked to prepare a list of issues in advance of the
workshop so that the instructor can adequately prepare for their needs.

Participants must provide their own laptop with a version of Logic
installed (Logic Express, Logic 7, Logic Pro 8 or Logic Pro 9).
Participants are also advised to bring an audio/MIDI interface if they have
one, and headphones.

Alicia Munro
The Sebastians

Opening: Monday November 17, 6-8pm
November 17-21, 12-4pm

In September I traveled to Kelowna, BC to visit my grandparents. At their
house, I gathered photographs from their family albums. I used these images
to make a painting every day for the month of October. The photographs span
fifty years of the lives of the Sebastians.

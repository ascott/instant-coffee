Love dance? Join us at Britannia for the month of September.

Britannia Art Gallery (1661 Napier St, Vancouver) features Choreographer and Dancer, Alvin
Erasga Tolentinto, repurposing his archival treasures as choreographic artifacts. These
materials have been part of Tolentinos dance presentation on cultural stages. These
memorable objects provide another dimension to the creative entity. All were once utilized as
props, sets, costumes, or images and include photography, books and notations.

Opening Sept 4th 6:30 to 8:30 pm. In addition, a performance and artist talk by Tolentino and
company, Co.ERASGA, will be held on Sept 13th at 7 pm at Family Canuck Education Centre
(north end of Grandview Park). This is a free public event (space is limited).

Summer at Nikkei Garden
AUGUST 7 & 8 and AUGUST 21 & 22 in the Nikkei Garden at Nikkei Centre in Burnaby,
BC 6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby BC V5E 4M7

Start times vary. Reserve a ticketed time slot through EventBrite.

The Nikkei Matsuri, Burnabys largest Japanese cultural festival at Nikkei Centre, looks
a little different this year! Due to the pandemic, we are adapting the Nikkei Matsuri
festival experience to ensure the health and safety of the community. This year, Nikkei
Matsuri presents Summer at Nikkei Garden, a Japanese cultural marketplace. Join us
in-person on select weekends in August to enjoy Japanese arts and culture. Browse a
selection of Matsuri vendors and Museum "pop-up shop" in our beautiful outdoor
garden, along with small-scale entertainment. Head inside for access to our Mikoshi
display and second-hand Japanese Bookshop. You can also join Matsuri Share virtually
for themed challenges and
fun activities. Share your Nikkei Matsuri memories with
#nikkeimatsuri from home or while you visit! Follow us on social media to stay up-to-
date.

Ticketed time slots are now available - Space is limited. Reserve your spot to avoid
disappointment! Full details available on EventBrite.

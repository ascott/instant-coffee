Open hour: Thurs-Sun 12pm-6pm

_

About the show
How does one grasp the nature of things? Articulate objects that border on concepts? 
Elements that allude direct confrontation can only be reached by dancing around it. 
Francois Rouxs practice could be considered a sideways approach to ontology. The 
nature of being either appears simple at a first glance or it is completely withdrawn, and 
to tease them out is to position yourself at various positions and perspectives. 

Rehearsal/Reversal offers a glimpse of how one might go about addressing these 
elusive moments, to walk around it, tilt your head, to pause for a long while and 
simultaneously only paying attention for a few seconds.

_

About the artist
Franois Roux likes fireworks, watches science documentaries and catches sound 
waves. Franois Roux was born in 1988 in Besanon (France) and studied at the cole 
Suprieure dArt et Design de Grenoble " Valence. He had his first solo shows at CSA 
Space (2012) and Grunt Gallery in Vancouver (2012) and has recently participated in 
group shows at CAB Grenoble (2013), Muse dArt Moderne in Saint-Etienne (2013), 
Galerie El Kasbah in Sfax (2013) and ERG Gallery in Brussels (2013). Since his early 
works, this young artist has been questioning his relationship to the world mostly by the 
association of images and sounds. His quest concerns recording and interacting with 
what surrounds him physically. His videos are ethereals, short, cheap. He wants them to 
be free from the physicality of the matter, heralds of his vision. He looks to the world, 
and decides how to frame it. Franois Roux is interested in the meaningless, the 
corrupted, and the smallness of the human being.

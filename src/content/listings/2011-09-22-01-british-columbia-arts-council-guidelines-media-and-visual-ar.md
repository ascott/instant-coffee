Professional Project Assistance for Media Arts Organizations - Guidelines and 
Application Form

The 2011/12 program guidelines and application forms to assist in completing 
applications for Professional Project Assistance for Media Arts Organizations, 
are now available to download from the BC Arts Council website at: 
www.bcartscouncil.ca .

Please note that the deadline for submitting an application for 2011/12 is October 
15, 2011.

If you have any concerns or questions, please contact Program Officer Sherry 
Ewings at 250 356-0081 in Victoria or by e-mail at: 
sherry.ewings@gov.bc.ca. 

The British Columbia Arts Council invites you to submit your application 
electronically via the Electronic Application System. We continue to develop and 
improve the electronic system to better meet the needs of our users and 
welcome your comments and feedback at bcartscouncil@gov.bc.ca. We are 
encouraging the use of the system's attachment option to eliminate the 
formatting restrictions experienced in the previous version. If you choose to 
submit your application electronically, please refer to the Technical Guide to 
ensure you have the technical environment to use the system successfully and 
to the User Guide for general information. The online application form will be 
available at www.bcacapplications.gov.bc.ca from September 23, 2011 until 
October 15, 2011. 

To access an electronic application, you require a USER ID and PASSWORD 
which is unique to your organization. If you do not have a User ID and password, 
please contact mailto:bcartscouncil@gov.bc.ca and it will be e-mailed to you. 

If you choose to submit your application electronically and encounter difficulties 
while doing so, please contact the BC Arts Council in Victoria at 250-356-1718.

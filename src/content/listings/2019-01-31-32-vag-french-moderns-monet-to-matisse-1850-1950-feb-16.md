The Vancouver Art Gallery presents French Moderns: Monet to Matisse, 1850"1950, on 
view February 16 to May 20, 2019

The Vancouver Art Gallery presents the West Coast premiere of French Moderns: 
Monet to Matisse, 1850"1950, on view February 16 to May 20, 2019. Visitors will find 
paintings, drawings and sculptures by artists such as Czanne, Chagall, Degas, Manet, 
Matisse, Morisot, Renoir, Rodin and others in this exhibition focused on France as the 
centre of international modernism. Featured artists represent a century of artistic 
innovation from which emerged Impressionism, Realism, Surrealism and other 
movements, when genres of landscape, portraiture, still life and the nude were redefined 
in radical ways. Discover these important works touring from the Brooklyn Museum 
Collection, today recognized as one of North Americas preeminent repositories of 
French modernism.

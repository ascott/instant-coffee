Beyond the Trees:
WALLPAPERS in Dialogue with Emily Carr

April 25 to September 7, 2015
Opening June 11th

Beyond the Trees considers mediated representations of nature and the shift
of perspective that occurs between our physical and virtual experiences.
Wallpapers, a collective comprised of artists Sara Ludy, Nicolas Sassoon
and Sylvain Sailly uses technology to produce an immersive environment that
both mimics and experiments with the scales and forms found in nature. In
this juxtaposition of historical and contemporary, both Wallpapers and
Emily Carr invite us to reflect on their perceptions of the West Coast
landscape, the former by means of digitally animated patterns and the
latter through nuanced brushstrokes of line and colour. In both, nature is
viewed through subjective aesthetic filters.

Wallpaper's Beyond the Trees is the fifth in a series of dialogues with
Emily Carr organized by the Vancouver Art Gallery. The exhibition is
curated by Caitlin Jones, Guest Curator and Diana Freundl, Assistant
Curator, Vancouver Art Gallery.

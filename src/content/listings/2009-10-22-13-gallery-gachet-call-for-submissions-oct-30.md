gallerygachet
ANNUAL CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS
Deadline for submissions: Oct 30 2009

We welcome proposals that challenge mental health stigmas, barriers to full social, cultural, and
economic participation. We actively look to create and maintain collaborations within the mental 
health and cultural sectors, as well as the DTES neighbourhood. Exhibitions may thematically 
coincide with other events and festivals. 

We accept proposals for solo, group, juried and curated exhibits. We accept proposals for major 
exhibition projects, screenings, workshops, performances and collaborations. We pay CARFAC 
fees, and provide support for openings, catering, marketing, mailings, A/V equipment, and 
installation.

Include in your package:
- at least 10 supporting images (slides or digital) per artist
- artwork list including title, medium, dimensions and year 
- artist’s CV for each participant (exhibition history)
- written proposal stating a well-thought out theme for the show (250-500 words)

Gallery Gachet supports artists marginalized by their mental health, trauma and/or abuse 
experience, by working for cultural and economic justice. Our programming features a range of art
practices from contemporary to outsider, and a range of mediums, from photography, video, 
performance art, drawing, 3D, installation, ceramics, textiles and more. 

Please e-mail submissions to Lara Fitzgerald, Programming Director, programming@gachet.org, 
or drop off clearly labelled packages to Gallery Gachet during gallery hours, Weds – Sun 12 – 
6pm.

Recent Acquisitions Screening

Please join us in celebrating new works in distribution while
directing public attention to the people and practices that generate
them. This is the second in an ongoing series of screenings at VIVO
Media Arts Centre to promote recent acquisitions for Video Out, with
works by Gabriela Aceves, Margaret Dragu, Oliver Hockenhull, Russell
Wallace & VestAndPage (Verena Stenke & Andrea Pagnes).

Thursday Evening, November 13, 2014, 7:30pm
@ VIVO Media Arts Centre, 2625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver

Site Accessibility and Conditions: Our new venue will be refurbished

steps past the entrance door (each is a foot deep and seven inches

provided and the films are not captioned. Please contact us if other

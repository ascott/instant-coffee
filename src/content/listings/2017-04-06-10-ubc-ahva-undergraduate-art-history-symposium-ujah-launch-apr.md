University of British Columbia (UBC)
Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory (AHVA)

13th ANNUAL UNDERGRADUATE ART HISTORY SYMPOSIUM
& UJAH ISSUE 8 LAUNCH

Friday, April 7, 2017
3:00 " 5:00 p.m. | 5:00 " 7:00 p.m.

Room 1002, Audain Art Centre
6398 University Boulevard, Vancouver BC

The Undergraduate Art History Symposium aims to create a discursive platform for 
undergraduate students at UBC to showcase and publish academic research and critical 
study conducted in the fields of art history and visual culture. Please join us for an 
afternoon of presentations by Mary Angoncillo, Simranpreet Anand, Georgette Espina, 
and Nick Loewen.

Following the symposium, we would like to invite you to celebrate the launch of UJAH 
Issue 8 in the Audain Art Centre lobby. Snacks, refreshments, and good company will 
be provided.

For more information, please visit facebook.com/ubcujah or ahva.ubc.ca.

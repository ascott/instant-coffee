Balcone Art Society is currently accepting calls for the 2009-2010 residency season. We are 
interested proposals for new works that will incorporate alternative / public spaces and are 
temporary, ephemeral, or event based. 

Please make your application relevant to the project you propose and include visuals, C.V. along 
with your succinct proposal. Applications must arrive before May 15. Email submissions to 
rina@balcone.org, or contact by email for a mailing address.

Balcone’s programming aims to improve the creative and financial well being of its artists by offering 
a supportive relationship with which to foster the emerging practice. Balcone strives to provide highly 
visible venues, CARFAC fees and public relations to a wider community / audience. Balcone’s goal 
is to create a platform where artists can produce works that might otherwise be overlooked in the
commercial and artist-run gallery systems.

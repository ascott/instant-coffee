SITE
This public art project forms part of a significant new facility
at an important intersection in central Cloverdale. Located
at the intersection of Highway 16 (176th St.) and 62nd
Avenue, the Cloverdale Recreation Centre serves the
adjacent new residential area and anchors the revitalization
of the fairgrounds district. Designed by CEI Architecture,
this state-of-the-art facility is slated for completion date in
early 2011.

The building program includes a gymnasium, fitness and
wellness centres, and multi-purpose rooms. The
architectural concept for the building and landscaping is
motion, rhythm, and flow. Inspiration for the forms is derived
from the idea of repetition as a visual identifier of movement

PUBLIC ART OPPORTUNITIES

Proposals are sought for artwork that is within the building
and highly visible from the street or foyer.
Three spaces have been identified:
1. Entrance Pavilion with exterior glass curtain wall
2. Fitness Centre with exterior glass curtain wall
3. Wellness Centre with interior double-glazed glass wall
dividing it from the foyer

The Entrance Pavilion and Fitness Centre will support
artwork that stands off the curtain walls and responds to
existing fritted/coloured panels, and lighting concepts. The
Fitness Centre with similar fritted/coloured panels in the
exterior wall also offers opportunity for nine hanging light
fixtures in the two-storey space.
The Wellness Centre, seen from the foyer, offers
possibilities for internal lighting and glass treatment of a
large dividing wall.

For architectural drawings see www.arts.surrey.ca

the shortlisted artists will be contracted to develop their
concept in detail for technical review and presentation to the
second meeting of the selection panel. The City reserves
the right to choose not to make a selection from the
submitted applications.

WELCH STREET PROJECT STUDIOS, which formerly housed the GRIFFIN Artist
Residency, is seeking to rent two adjoining Artist Project Spaces as of January 2025.

Located in the same building as GRIFFIN Art Projects in North Vancouver (just off
Pemberton, on Welch Street), the spaces are ideal for visual artists who are seeking
a temporary space for project development.

About the studio spaces:

- Roughly 350 sq ft. each, adjoining

- Both are on the upper floor (no elevator) with west-facing windows

- Access to shared kitchenette/social area and bathroom

- 12-hour access (9 am to 9 pm, open to adjustment)

- Basic studio furniture supplied

- Wifi, heat, A/C and electricity included

- Each studio has 3 good sized white walls, grey plywood floors

- Good natural and artificial light

- Free parking and access to public transit nearby

About the studio agreement:

- The rate is $900 / mo., + GST with an additional damage deposit of $300

- Studio fees to be paid monthly

-If artist pays in full at the start of the 4 month basis the fees will be discounted to
$3500 (including GST)

- The studios are available on a 4 month basis, with option for an additional 4
months

(January to April; May to August; September to December); 2 or 3 month rental is
possible, but preference is given to those who wish to stay 4 months)

- No living space allowed for project artists

- No public events or social gatherings allowed without permission

If you are interested in renting one of the two
studios available, please send an artist
bio, copy of your CV (2-page max) as well as short statement of intent to:

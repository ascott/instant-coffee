Pulse: SINS Dance, Reece Terris, 30Toes

The sixth edition of the Pulse series features three eclectic
performances by artists who trained at Simon Fraser University's School for Contemporary Arts. SINS (Sometimes in Nova Scotia) Dance brings together dancers from across the country to perform work by award-winning choreographer Sara Coffin; visual artist Reece Terris, who recently received a major commission for the Vancouver Art Gallery next season, remounts his quirky and thought-provoking untitled (my big brown box); and 30Toes performs Now by Megan Walker-Straight.

Friday-Saturday May 9-10 at 8pm at Scotiabank Dance Centre, 677 Davie St, Vancouver. Tickets $18/$15 students and seniors from www.ticketstonight.ca. Info: www.thedancecentre.ca.

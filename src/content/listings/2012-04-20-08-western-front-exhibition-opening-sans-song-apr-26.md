THURSDAY, APRIL 26th, 7:00 PM | OPENING
Exhibition Opening: Sans Song

Thursday, April 26th, 7pm
Sans Song features a combination of vocal works from the 1970s with 
contemporary sonic and kinetic sculptures. The exhibition proposes the 
sonic as artwork representative of the “in-process' between two 
tangible moments.

Introducing new sonic, kinetic sculptural works by Nathan McNinch and 
by Joshua Bonnetta, in conjunction with two pieces released in 1976 by 
Joan La Barbara, the artworks in Sans Song allow for an investigation 
of form and physicality through the exploration of resonance, breath 
and corporeality in vocal production and reception.

Sans Song is curated by Jenny Walton, a candidate to the Masters 
Degree in Critical and Curatorial Studies at The University of British 
Columbia, with support from the Killy Foundation and the Audain 
Endowment for Curatorial Studies through the Department of Art 
History, Visual Art and Theory in collaboration with the Morris and 
Helen Belkin Art Gallery at the University of British Columbia.

Sans Song runs from April 27th through June 2nd.

This exhibition will feature in the 2012 Vancouver Gallery Hop on 
Saturday, April 28th, including a talk with the curator at Western Front 
at 3:30pm.

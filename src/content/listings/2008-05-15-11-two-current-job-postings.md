MEDIA ART PROJECTS LEAD
W2/Gallery Gachet Society

(more job postings at www.gachet.org)

This staff position will assume considerable responsibility for
coordinating Fearless City Mobile. Together with the DTES Community ArtsNetwork (DTES CAN) Fearless Media Cluster (www.fearlesscity.ca), MobileMuse 3, and the National Film Board, Gallery Gachet is producing this community generated media project for mobile technology.

The Media Art Projects Lead will also assist with the development of W2: Community Media Arts. For the past four years, Gachet has been co-creating W2: Community Media Arts Centre (www.creativetechnology.org) with several other local arts groups. Gallery Gachet will move to this Woodwards site at the corner of Abbott & Hastings in September 2009. The 12,500 sq ft space will feature a cafe, labs for radio, television, and crossmedia, literary and printing resources, creative technology incubator, disability arts studios, as well as other production and presentation venues.

Gallery Gachet is a multidisciplinary artist-run centre supporting artist informed by mental health issues, and is located in the Downtown Eastside (DTES). Current Gallery Gachet media arts program includes video, digital photography, mobile media, and web-based storytelling. Other programs include ceramics, painting, woodworking, and performance art.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
* Provide hands-on coordination of Fearless City Mobile initiative working closely with Gallery Gachet and the Fearless Media Cluster of the DTES Community Arts Network (CAN).
* Deliver artist commissioning program for Fearless City Mobile and liaise with artists;
* Assist in the development of W2: Community Media Arts Centre working closely with Gallery Gachet and W2s Steering Committee;
QUALIFICATIONS:
* Minimum five years experience in arts administration and/or project management.
* Have experience in media arts or ICT (Information and Communication Technologies) sector, either as a professional or volunteer. May have an understanding of creative technology within non-profit or artist-run centre context;

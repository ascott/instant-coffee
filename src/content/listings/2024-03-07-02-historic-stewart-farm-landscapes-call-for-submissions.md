Historic Stewart Farm is welcoming local artists to exhibit works around the theme of
Landscapes, This is an opportunity to present your 2d artwork to be hanged or small 3d
works which can be displayed in cases.

The exhibition will be at Stewart Hall on Historic Stewart Farm. A heritage site in South
Surrey. Nestled on Crescent Road along the Nikomekl River. Historic Stewart Farm
presents the farm life of a Surrey family in the early 1900s. The site is visited by adults,
families, and school programs.

Deadline: March 22

Dylan Townley-Smith
Situ Comoedia

Opening Thursday, October 11th
7:00-10:00PM
October 12th - November 22nd, 2018
Field Contemporary
17 West Broadway, Vancouver, BC.

Field Contemporary presents a new body of work by Dylan Townley-Smith 
accompanied by an exhibition text by Darcy Blake printed by Moniker Press.

Dylan Townley-Smith is a multidisciplinary artist working predominantly in sculpture, 
web, video, photo, and painting art. Recently he has explored creative writing in the 
form of the novel. His practice is informed by the visual and social culture of western 
society, the semiotics of advertising, the sanctity (or fetishization) embedded in 
everyday commodities, the uncanny, the psyche, and the surreal. His work has been 
acquired into several private collections in Vancouver, Los Angeles and New York.

Please join us on the evening of Thursday, October 11th for an opening
reception. The exhibition will continue through November 22nd. Please
contact info@field-contemporary.com for more information.

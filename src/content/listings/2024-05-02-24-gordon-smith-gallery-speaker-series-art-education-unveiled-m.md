Speaker Series - Art Education Unveiled: Perspectives from Prevailing Landscapes
Thursday, May 9 | 6pm
Gordon Smith Gallery of Canadian Art
2121 Londsdale Avenue, North Vancouver, BC V7M 2K6
Facilitated by Andrew Booth, Vancouver Art Blog Founder

Join
Prevailing Landscapes exhibiting artists and educators Stan Douglas, Ian Wallace
and Karen Zalamea as they discuss the intricate relationship between art and education.
This first installment of our new 'Art Education, for Life' Speaker Series will offer a rare
glimpse into the minds of artists who are shaping the landscapes of contemporary art
and molding the future of artistic expression through their roles as educators.

'Prevailing Landscapes' is presented by The Gordon and Marion Smith Foundation for
Young Artists in Partnership with Artists For Kids with generous support from Parc
Retirement Living, the North Vancouver Recreation and Culture Commission, the North
Vancouver School District and Iain Mant. The 'Art Education, for Life' Speaker Series is
generously supported by The Audain Foundation, Polygon Homes and Anthem
Properties.

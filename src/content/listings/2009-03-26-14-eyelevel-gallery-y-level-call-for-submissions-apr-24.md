The Call:

Y-Level Call For Submissions

Eyelevel Gallery seeks submissions of new and engaging work for its annual
Y-Level emerging artists show. This exhibition is open to students in
their final year of undergraduate studies and recent undergraduates (up to
one year post-graduation) from the Atlantic provinces (New Brunswick,
Newfoundland, Nova Scotia and PEI) and New England (New Hampshire, Maine,
Rhode Island, Massachusetts, Connecticut and Vermont).

2009 Theme: Future Tense

This years show comes at a time of great uncertainty, with dynamic shifts
in international politics, a troubled economy and a rapidly changing
climate. With this uncertainty as a backdrop, we ask emerging artists to
consider the theme Future Tense. We seek art that envisions the future, be
it mundane or fantastic, apocalyptic or utopic, violent or peaceful. A
challenge to emerging artists to question our direction, considering the
innumerable problems and potentials that face us today and how they will
shape tomorrow. We encourage work that is critical, thought provoking, and
pushes beyond the obvious.

Both individual and collaborative work in any media is welcome. We look
forward to your submissions.

Submissions must be received no later than April 24, 2009, 5pm. Please include:
-a project description outlining the concept, scale and media of proposed
work (250 words max)
-CV
-artist statement (250 words max)
-10 digital images (jpgs) or applicable multimedia on CD or DVD
Please include a self addressed stamped envelope if you would like your
submission returned.

Submissions MUST be mailed or dropped off to:
Eyelevel Gallery
2128 Gottingen St.
Halifax, NS
B3K 1Z7 Canada

Email or fax submissions will NOT be considered.

The Y-Level exhibition runs from June 11 to July 11, 2009.
Selected artists will be paid an honorarium.

For more information please contact:
Chris Foster (curatorial intern) whylevel@gmail.com
www.eyelevelgallery.ca

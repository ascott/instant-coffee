The Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre invites the public to join in a
Remembrance Day ceremony to honour the Japanese Canadians who served and
sacrificed for our country.

Date: November 11, 2024

Time: 10:40 AM

Location: Japanese Canadian War Memorial, Stanley Park, Vancouver

The ceremony will include wreath-laying and speeches. Following the ceremony,
attendees are invited to a reception at the Vancouver Rowing Club in Stanley Park to
share in fellowship and remembrance.

For those unable
to attend in person, the ceremony will be livestreamed on the
NNMCC YouTube channel.

HOSTED BY

Japanese Canadian War Memorial Committee

Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre

Nikkei Place Foundation

SPONSORS

Veterans Affairs Canada

National Association of Japanese Canadians

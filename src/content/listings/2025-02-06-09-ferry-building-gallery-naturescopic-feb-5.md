The Ferry Building Gallery is thrilled to present Naturescopic, an exhibition showcasing
the work of local artists Marianne Hansen, Ralph Sears, and Stephanie Taylor. Through
their art, the artists explore the beauty and fragility of the natural world, inviting viewers
to pause, reflect, and deeply observe their surroundings.

The exhibition runs from February 5 to March 2.

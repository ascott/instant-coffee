Spring into art and join us for a week of virtual spring break programs from March 15 to
19!

Through free daily online artmaking sessions, well consider the many ways that we can
express our ideas, identities and stories through the real, imagined and dream-worthy
themes of Underwater, Underground, Playspace, Outerspace and Intergalactic.

Each workshop will be led by Family
Programs Coordinator Christina Jones and/or a
local artist, who will share inspiring artworks and demonstrate imaginative activities that
you can make from home!

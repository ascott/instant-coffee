by the well of urd
Judith Copithorne, Carole Itter, Rhoda Rosenfeld, Trudy Rubenfeld 
curated by Lee Plested

April 7th - May 11th, 2019
Opening Reception Sunday April 7th, 1-4pm

Macaulay and Co. Fine Art are pleased to present an exhibition of four 
Vancouver artists whose work reflects on the desire and turmoil of life 
in our contemporary age. 

Having made art and poetry in this city for over 50 years these artists 
accumulated methods and means, drawn from absorbing the rich 
energy of Vancouvers fecund experimentation and collaboration, 
enrich the work that they now make today. Continuing to release this 
resonance through their various media, the work carries the complexity 
of their gained experience and perception.

by the well of urd draws on an image from Norse myth, where the 
Norns sit by the well of urd and draw from its endless depths to water 
Yggdrasil, the tree of life. These are the Norns who know the past and 
so spin futures, pulling material from the dark recesses of urd and 
always, steadtight, turn it through the twists of our shared presence, as 
they thus watch it wind and resolve tomorrow. Tracing the energy and 
desires of life, the mournful watchers braid beginnings and ends along 
their ever extending flow. Nurturing desires in the shadows of death, 
then delivering forth the accomplishments and scorn of this mortal coil. 

This exhibition will present paintings, sculpture, digital drawings, 
photography and video made within the last ten years. 

Please join us for the opening reception on Sunday April 7th, from 1-
4pm. Artists will be in attendance.

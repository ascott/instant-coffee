Collective Legacies: Artist Talk & Forum 
Sat Jan 19th, 4 – 6pm

In honour of Gallery Gachet’s 20th Anniversary
Collective Habitat 1997-2012
ART-i-FACT: 88 East Cordova Project
Curated by Bernadine Fox | Dec 6th, 2012 – Feb 17th, 2013
As part of the Collective Habitat 1997 – 2012 exhibition, Gallery Gachet 
presents an artist talk and forum: Collective Legacies taking place on Sat Jan 
19th, 4 – 6pm at 88 East Cordova Street.

Moderated by curator, Bernadine Fox, the talk features six artists from the 
Collective Habitat exhibition: Rebecca Chunn, Cherise Clarke, Quin Martins, 
Bill Pope, Steve Borton and Karen Ward. The artists will speak about their 
exhibited artworks and their evolving personal relationships with Gallery 
Gachet. Following the talk, join us for an open discussion forum on issues 
relating to Gachet, its history, place in the art world, inherent challenges, and 
possibilities for the future. Join us, especially if you are a past member or 
have had any associations with Gachet, to talk about our 20-year legacy as a 
unique artist-run centre – help us imagine what moving forward will look like, 
as we integrate lessons from past challenges.

Emily Carr is pleased to present

Crossing Over
Painting a Critical Conversation - A Symposium
Thursday, October 20 + Friday, October 21

This symposium proposes a series of creative-critical exchanges about the 
practice of post-conceptual painting, as act and cultural object, and the larger 
institutional contexts with which it is framed in Vancouver. It considers painting 
as a localized set of practices and conditions set within the global art world. The 
event invites an intergenerational approach to the discussion of making pictures 
in a city that is known internationally for conceptual photography and to broach 
questions about the space between embodiment and intellectualization, artistic 
production and critical reception, culture and knowledge. -Landon Mackenzie and 
Glen Lowry

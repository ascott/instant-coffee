Catriona Jeffries is now accepting applications for the position of Preparator to start 
January 2019 or sooner. This is a part-time position, 3-4 days per week, with full-time 
potential. The Preparator is a key team member for an ambitious program and schedule, 
overseeing all handling of the gallerys inventory and exhibition installation.

Working closely with gallery staff and artists, the Preparator is responsible for exhibition 
installation and de-installation, as well as packing works for storage and shipping. They 
must work both independently and collaboratively to plan, schedule, and accomplish 
tasks, on- and off-site. They must be attentive to detail in all communications, 
demonstrate initiative in sourcing appropriate materials and equipment, and be 
resourceful in learning to pack, ship, and install contemporary art. As such, a desire to 
learn, excellent listening skills and clarity in speaking and writing, and the ability to 
problem solve and manage priorities of time, space, and staff, are key to this position.

A background in visual art or a related discipline, and an ongoing curiosity for 
contemporary art is necessary. Basic skills in the use of hand and power tools, wood or 
metal shop machinery, and lifting apparatuses are essential. Further knowledge and 
experience in woodworking, metalworking, painting and general construction is 
beneficial. Prior experience with art handling in a gallery, museum, or related setting, 
and knowledge of best practices for packing and crating artwork, is ideal. A local class 5 
drivers license is considered an asset.

Remuneration: $20-22 per hour, or commensurate with experience

Please send a cover letter detailing your qualifications and your resume in Word or PDF 
format by e-mail to gallery@catrionajeffries.com, subject ATTN: Preparator Position; 
applications will be accepted until the position is filled.

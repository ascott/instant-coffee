GUILT

August 13 – September 2, 2011

Opening Reception

Saturday August 13, 7-10 PM

A solo exhibition featuring paintings by Kirk Gower guest curated by 
Suzanne Fulbrook. A display of painting as guilty pleasure, void of 
criticality but sumptuous in its aesthetics. Gower’s art produces a 
space from which to break the rules of contemporary painting that 
are regurgitated at art school: no over working, no photo realism, no 
white paint used as highlights, avoid being decorative, no drips for 
drips sake, the more like an under painting the better. Gower will 
supply a list of rules he feels obligated to follow while painting, 
focusing on the ones that he breaks.

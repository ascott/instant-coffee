Mobile Native. Paper Tigers. Revolution Through Rhythm.
Friday, December 4, 7pm-late.
W2 Community Media Art, 112 W Hastings Street, Vancouver.
www.creativetechnology.org @W2Woodwards
W2 is excited to present a triple bill of discussion, visual art and music.
Come early, stay late. Free before 10pm; afterwards $5 sliding scale.

Listen 7-8:30pm: Artist talk with Catalan artist Antoni Abad/megafone.net.
Mobile technology and ethnographic media. (deets below)

Witness 8:30-10pm: Paper Tigers open exhibit 8:30pm-10:00pm.
Internationally renowned printmaker Favianna Rodriguez and indigenous
artists Tania Willard (Secwepemc) and Marika Swan (Tla-o-qui-aht) exhibit
in the Perel Gallery until Dec 11. (daily 12-6pm)

Sounds: 10:00-onwards TOBIAS.DJ (Whistler/Montreal). Hailing from the West
Coast technoculture, tobias has DJ'ed warehouse raves from Montreal to
Amsterdam, Barcelona to Berlin. From dubhouse to deep techno, electro to
minimal, tobias plays with a unique sonic signature crafted from over 15
years of DJing hedonist gatherings worldwide. His DJ mixes can be found at
[[http://controltochaos.ca](http://controltochaos.ca)].

W2 hosts Barcelona-based mobile artist-in-residence Antoni Abad from
November 28th - December 12th. Abad's artist talk will highlight his work
with marginalized people around the world (Geneva, Sao Paolo, Colombia,
Mexico, Spain, the Sahara Desert and elsewhere). His megafone.net program
uses technology to identify major cultural and community points that act as
barriers to self-representation. Earlier work includes sculpture, projected
video, and the 'virtual fly' project that lived in the user's computer, a
community prepared to be able to conspire. He has been a regular at the
Banff Centre for the Arts as mentoring artist.

Our Mobile*Native collaborative project connecting W2 and Megafone.net
engages local First Nations youth in digital media expression as citizen
ethnographers. In using the internet and new technology, the project aims
to give voice and visibility to groups that previously had no or little
truthful presence in the media. The residency will give tools and
experiences for youth to become critical investigators of their own
community. Coast Salish and Statimc youth interested in participating in
the workshop contact hywel@creativetechnology.org

GIFFING: From Analog to Digital with Lauren Marsden

10 hours |$120 for VIVO Members and $126+ GST for Non-Members10 hours |
Saturday June 18, 3-7pm (Falaise Fieldhouse) & Saturday June 25 (VIVO -
2625 Kaslo Street), 12-6pm - Register via Eventbrite.

In this workshop, artist Lauren Marsden will take participants on a journey
through the world of GIF-making. The Animated GIF (Graphics Interchange
Format), although not a new form of image making, has become increasingly
popular in contemporary advertising and digital media culture.

On the first workshop day, Lauren will present a history of the GIF (from
early stereoscopy to contemporary memes), and participants will create
their own photographic still images on film (yes, actual film), which will
be developed on-site at the Iris Film Collective Studio at the Falaise
Fieldhouse by a technician.

The second workshop day at VIVO will focus on transforming these film
stills into digital form and creating living, breathing, animated GIFs
through various techniques in speed, palette, sizing, and 3D effects via
Adobe Photoshop.

Students will each be provided with one roll of film.

Requirements: Some Photoshop experience is beneficial but not required.
This workshop is best suited to photographers and filmmakers who are
comfortable with a manual SLR camera.

A few still cameras will be available for use, but participants are
encouraged to bring their own cameras as well (35mm or 120mm).

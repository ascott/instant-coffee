September 9th-18th @ Dynamo Arts Association, Suite 103 - 30 
East 6th Ave, Vancouver, BC.
Opening September 9, 7-11 pm. 
Additional viewing by appointment only.

"(Super) Natural" considers five artists' understanding of separation 
and disconnection to nature, one-sided relationships to space and 
place, while attempting to reconnect to the natural world. Their work 
responds to their perceived notions about the human possibility to 
connect to place, while acknowledging the unnatural implications 
human interactions place on the environment. These attempts are 
heavy-handed at times, and embrace failure and 
miscommunication. The exhibition encompasses installation, 
painting, collage, and textiles, each work responding to our shifting 
relationships to surrounding natural spaces, and reconsidering 
traditional notions of landscape art in the contemporary moment.

Lauren Everall is a Pacific Northwest based multidisciplinary artist, 
educator, and naturalist. She obtained her BFA in Painting and 
Sculpture from Emily Carr University in 2011. She then completed 
her BEd in Art Education from the University of British Columbia in 
2012 where she focused on the intersection of Art and Ecology in 
pedagogical practices. While at UBC, Lauren was awarded the 
Sam Black Prize and the Elmore Ozard Art Education Prize. She is 
currently working on her first book with the Birds on Bikes collective 
and is preparing for a Northern expedition in 2016 to film an 
experimental documentary on solitary living in the 'wild'. 
laureneverall.com 

Liz Toohey-Wiese is a recent graduate from the MFA program at 
NSCAD. She completed her undergraduate degree in painting at 
Emily Carr University, also undertaking coursework at the University 
of Victoria and the cole Nationale des Beaux-Arts de Lyon. She has 
taken part in solo and group shows across Canada, and will be 
traveling to Scandinavia in May 2016 as a recipient of the W.B. 
Bruce Fine Art Travel Scholarship. Deeply interested in the history 
of landscape painting, her paintings explore contemporary 
relationships between identity and place. liztoohey-wiese.com 

Hailey Gooch is a visual artist and designer from Vancouver, BC. 
She is a graduate of Emily Carr University where she studied 
sculpture and painting, and has since pursued a Master of 
Architecture at UBC. Her work, currently based in mixed media 
collage and painting, uses the architectural process of layering to 
construct an image, while then deconstructing it to reveal abstract 
shapes and color fields. haileygooch.com 

Haley Hunt-Brondwin is a visual artist and designer from 
Vancouver, BC. She received her BFA in visual art from Emily Carr 
University, including an exchange with the European Exchange 
Academy in Beelitz-Heilstatten, Germany. Haley's work explores 
the qualities of human relationships and our phenomenological and 
physiological relationship to the Natural world. Using slow labour 
and 'low tech 'methods, through tapestry weaving, she utilizes 
colour, shape and texture to create abstracted landscapes that are 
surreal, vibrant and visceral. Her work offers a space to 
contemplate our mediated relationships with the Natural world and 
with our own bodies and selves. haleyhb.com 

Jo Peters is a Squamish based artist who completed her studies at 
Emily Carr University in 2011. She also studied at Chelsea College 
of Art in 2010. Her objective as an artist is to understand the natural 
world by expressing her experiences through physical mark making 
and material interactions. She uses collage, photography and 
drawing as a method to reflect on her experiences when hiking, 
canoeing, kayaking, biking, and skiing. She is especially aware of 
patterning and repetition within an abstract field; and the existing 
mimicry in nature and ones specific sensual experience of such.

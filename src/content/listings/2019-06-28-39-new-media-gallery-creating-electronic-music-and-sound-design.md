Creating Electronic Music and Sound Design A two-part series that explores the exciting 
world of coding, sound production and composition using MAX MSP and Reaper. Join 
educator, artist and musician, Kiran Bhumber for a sound theory class. This Introductory 
class will cover sound Synthesis and Processing, Envelopes and Glissandi, Additive and 
Subtractive Synthesis, Noise Generators, Filters, and Control Signals. Students will be 
introduced to listening examples and will discuss the processes used in developing 
these sounds. At the end of the session, students will present their own electronic music 
works to their peers. This workshop welcomes women and non-binary people.
* Please bring a laptop with a trial version of MAX MSP pre-installed. Contact NMG 
Programmer Chris Jung for details cjung@anvilcentre.ca

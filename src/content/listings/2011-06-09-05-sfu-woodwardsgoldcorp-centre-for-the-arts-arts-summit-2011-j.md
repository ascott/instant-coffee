Arts Summit 2011 (SFU Woodward's/Goldcorp Centre for the Arts :: 149 West
Hastings)
Friday, June 10 (9-5pm) & Saturday, June 11, (noon to 6pm)
Schmoozy at Centre A on Saturday, June 11 to follow

Arts Summit 2011 is quick approaching. The coordination team, panel
presenters, dialogue peers and facilitators are gearing up for
game-changing connections. Along with flagship players you'll be in
dialogue with an inclusive cross-section of the creative community. With
lots of focused time to speak with each other and learn from each other
help make Arts Summit 2011 a turning point of how we build together.

Artists Emilio Rojas w/Patrick Blaesar, Russell Wallace w/ Sonny Assu and
Wayde Compton will be featured during the summit. You can pop into the Arts
Umbrella open rehearsals during breaks. We will have special events and
receptions on Friday and Saturday in partnership with Centre A including a
commissioned performance and installation by Howie Tsui. His new work
Celestials of Saltwater City are stories from Vancouver's Chinese community
will be compiled, re-interpreted, and transformed.

Join the conversation to share strategies, stories, build strength,capacity
and connectivity in our creative communities.

Our program is built on five themes:
Our Value and Worth
For The Love of Art
Hearts In The House
Going Places / Creating Spaces
Soft Assets For Tough Times

Register now, prices range from $50-$235. It is our goal that everyone can
attend and contribute in their capacity.

Contact us for more information on supporting, volunteering or
accessibility of the 2011 Arts Summit, events@allianceforarts.com

FAIR SECRETS
Lexie Owen

Agricultural Hall
Agassiz Fall Fair and Corn Festival
6800 Pioneer Avenue, Agassiz, BC

Booth Open: 10:30 AM - 7:00 PM

Curated by Caitlin Chaisson

Domestic, horticultural, and agricultural production is steeped in tacit,
embedded knowledge. Success in these endeavours is often the result of
decades, or even centuries-long relationships with materials, matter, and
living organisms. Working in collaboration with the Agassiz Fall Fair and
Corn Festival, artist Lexie Owen addresses the accrual of this knowledge
amidst the midways and Exhibition Halls of the festival. Owens 'Fair
Secrets' will invite participation from the local community by soliciting
an assortment of secrets"from prized recipes to personal confessions.
Through anonymous contributions, 'Fair Secrets' will create a collective
representation of the knowledge harboured within the region. The shared
secrets will be transformed into a publication and released in a small
printed edition.

This exhibition is part of 'Touchstones: Deep Time in the Near Future', a
three-part curatorial series featuring the work of artists Lexie Owen,
Carrielynn Victor, and Lianne Zannier. 'Touchstones' originated as an
investigation into a time capsule that was buried on a farm in Agassiz
thirty years ago. Throughout the month of September, these three artist
projects will address ideas of hidden or embedded knowledge, nature's
intelligence, and systems of value.

Book Launch: September 28, 6 PM - 8 PM.
A book launch for this project will take place at the Ranger Station Art
Gallery.

Vancouver Art Gallery: Tuesday, April 13, 7pm
Cities in Flux: Christos Dikeakos in conversation with Lee Henderson
Artists & Writers in Conversation
This series of events at the Vancouver Art Gallery brings BC-based writers and artists featured in 
Visions of British Columbia: A Landscape Manual together for expanded discussions on urban 
landscapes, the construction of the “natural” world and the artist’s relationship to this place and the 
land.

Artist Christos Dikeakos will be in conversation with BC writer Lee Henderson (The Broken Record 
Technique, The Man Game).

Free with Gallery admission.

SFU FCAT and Cineworks present

THE FESTIVAL OF (IN)APPROPRIATION 8
Fri Nov 18 8.00pm-9.30pm
SFU Harbour Centre, Room 1800
FREE

Contemporary Found Footage Filmmaking 

Whether you call it collage, compilation, found footage, dtournement, or 
recycled cinema, the incorporation of previously shot materials into new artworks 
is a practice that has generated novel juxtapositions of elements which have 
produced new meanings and ideas that may not have been intended by the 
original makers, that are, in other words "inappropriate." Founded in 2009, the 
Festival of (In)appropriation is a yearly showcase of contemporary short 
audiovisual works that appropriate film or video footage and repurpose it in 
"inappropriate" and inventive ways. This year's show is curated by Jaimie Baron, 
Greg Cohen, and Lauren Berliner. Sponsored by Los Angeles Filmforum.

Programmer Jaimie Baron in attendance

festivalofinappropriation.org

FCAT is the exciting intersection of communication, art, and technology at Simon 
Fraser University, in Vancouver, BC. It is what happens when creativity meets 
new technology, when science meets art, when design encounters new media.

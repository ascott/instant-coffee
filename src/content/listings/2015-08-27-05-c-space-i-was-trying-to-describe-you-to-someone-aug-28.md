I Was Trying to Describe You to Someone

Tommy Chain
Catherine de Montreuil
Scott Kemp
Michael Lachman
Graeme Wahn

Curated by Lyndsay Pomerantz

I Was Trying to Describe You to Someone is the first installment of a
multiple city exhibition being held in Winnipeg (C Space Gallery,
August 28-September 4) and Vancouver (Avenue, September 24-30).

The works included in' I Was Trying to Describe You to Someone' are
produced from the desire to conjure the experience of distance by
responding to the prospective journey, the site of the exhibition, and an
a priori relationship between the artists. They develop their
understanding of Winnipeg, Manitoba through means of documentation
and research, correspondence, hypotheses, and storytelling.

Employing photography, sculpture and video, notions of the unknown
are confidently asserted obstructing one’™s ability to
differentiate between fact and constructed narrative. Replication is
used to trouble the recognizable and extraneous steps are taken to add
layers that further heighten one’™s awareness of what
they should or should not believe.

'I Was Trying to Describe You to Someone' will present the work of a
diverse set of artists currently developing their practices in Vancouver.

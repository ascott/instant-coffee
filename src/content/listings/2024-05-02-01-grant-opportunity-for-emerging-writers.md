The 2024 John Fleming Award in Decorative Arts Writing, which recognizes an
exceptional example of original magazine
writing on decorative arts in Canada is an
award offers emerging writers an excellent opportunity to showcase their decorative
arts writing and gain recognition in the field. Each year, the Canadian Society of
Decorative Arts accepts submissions from emerging writers across the country. The
winner receives a $1,000 cash prize and the opportunity to be published in our
award-winning magazine, Ornamentum.

Participation in the John Fleming Award provides numerous benefits, including:

Valuable Recognition: Winning this award is an impressive accomplishment
that can boost a student's confidence and academic/professional credentials.

Monetary Prize: The $1,000 cash prize can make a significant difference for
students, helping to cover tuition, living expenses, or other educational costs.

Publication Opportunity: Having their work published in Ornamentum, Canadas
leading decorative arts publication, is an excellent addition to a student's writing
portfolio and can open doors for future
opportunities.

The submission deadline for the 2024 John Fleming Award is June 2, 2024. You can
find all the details on eligibility, guidelines, and other information on our website at
csda-ccad.org/awards. I encourage you to discuss this opportunity with your student
members and support them in applying and submitting their best work. Please find
attached a promotional poster to assist you in sharing this information within your
community.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please do not hesitate to
contact me directly. Thanks in advance for your help and continued support for future
arts writers!

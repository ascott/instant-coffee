Outsiders and Others presents:

The Many Faces of Time
Featuring artworks by Golya Mirderikvand, Pari, Hyunjee Lee, Les Sears, Val
Loewen, Stefan Moeri, and Kate MacDonald

May 8 - June 30
Outsiders and Others window gallery located at #100-938 Howe St., Vancouver
Co-curated by Seema Shah and Yuri Arajs.

We are excited to present The Many Faces of Time, a group exhibition of seven
artists, each connecting to the concept of time in their own unique way. The artworks
in this exhibition convey multiple facets of time through reflections of both self and
society, aspects of personal experience and memory, and the literal depiction of time
passing

PoMoArts is Hiring Visual & Performing Arts Manager
Permanent Full-Time | 35 hours/week

Salary: $50,000-$55,000 annually, depending on experience

Excellent benefits package | 3 weeks vacation to start
Do you believe in the power of the arts to connect, inspire, and transform lives?
Are you a detail-oriented and organized arts administrator who thrives in a
collaborative environment? Do you have a passion for visual and performing arts,
and want to make a meaningful impact in your community? If so, we invite you to join
our creative and dedicated team at PoMoArts as our Visual & Performing Arts
Manager.
About PoMoArts
The Port Moody Arts Centre Society is a not-for-profit community arts organization
located in the City of the Arts. We create inclusive and meaningful art experiences
through education, exhibitions, and outreach. Run primarily by artists and supported
by a vibrant network of volunteers, donors, and community partners, we offer a wide
range of visual, performing, ceramic, and media arts programming for people of all
ages and skill levels.

Our core values are connection, integrity, inclusivity, and growth mindset. If you
share our passion for the arts and believe in its ability to shape lives, youll feel right
at home here.

The Role: Visual & Performing Arts Manager
As the Visual & Performing Arts Manager, youll lead the planning, coordination, and
evaluation of a wide array of visual and performing arts programs. Youll supervise a
diverse group of talented instructors and work closely with the Ceramics Manager
and other team members to deliver high-quality, mission-driven arts education and
experiences both in our centre and through community partnerships.

This is a role for a builder"someone who enjoys creating systems, supporting
artists, and contributing positively to a collaborative team.

A Day in the Life
You might start your day finalizing a schedule for childrens art camps, collaborate
with a local theatre group to develop a new community class, check in with music
instructors, and finish with budget planning or preparing content for an upcoming
program launch. Its a role that blends vision, detail, and people-focused leadership.

Key Responsibilities
Program Development & Administration
Lead the planning, scheduling, and documentation of visual and performing arts
programs in collaboration with the Ceramics Manager
Prepare and manage program budgets, pricing, and revenue targets
Supervise, recruit, onboard, and evaluate visual and performing arts instructors,
including private music teachers
Ensure Occupational Health & Safety compliance and best practices across
programs
Maintain program records and share updates with leadership and staff
Enter and update course offerings in our online registration system (XplorRec)
Collaborate with the Marketing Coordinator to ensure timely and accurate program
promotion
Oversee performance metrics and report on participation and program success
Lead the annual Visual Arts Scholarship for Emerging Artists selection process

Community Engagement & Partnerships
Build and sustain strong relationships with schools, local organizations, artists, and
businesses
Actively seek collaborative programming opportunities within the local arts and
culture ecosystem
Support fundraising, sponsorship, and advocacy efforts for the Arts Centre

General Management
Participate in strategic planning and cross-departmental initiatives
Propose new opportunities aligned with our mission
Occasionally support broader events and projects led by the Arts Centre

Who You Are
A natural collaborator who enjoys working with artists and building strong
relationships
Highly organized and detail-focused, with excellent time management skills
Passionate about the visual and performing arts, and their impact on individuals and
communities
A clear and thoughtful communicator with strong supervisory experience
Comfortable balancing creative planning with administrative follow-through
Qualifications
University degree in Fine Arts, Arts Administration, Business Administration, HR, or
equivalent experience
2+ years in program management or related experience
Budgeting and pricing skills; ability to analyze and report on financial and
participation data
Strong HR, team management, and leadership capabilities
Familiarity with online registration systems (XplorRec preferred)
Proficient in Microsoft Office; knowledge of Google Suite and Jotform is an asset
Understanding of MSDS, WHIMIS, and OHS protocols
Knowledge of the Tri-Cities arts and cultural landscape is a significant asset
Level 1 First Aid certification is a bonus

Why Join Us?

Opportunities to grow professionally and shape arts education in a thriving
community
A role where your creativity and leadership will have a lasting impact

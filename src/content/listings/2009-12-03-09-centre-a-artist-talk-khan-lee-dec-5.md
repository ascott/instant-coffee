Artist Talk

Khan Lee
Saturday, December 5, 2009
2:00pm - 4:00pm

Khan Lee will deliver an artist talk on Saturday, December 5, at 2pm on his
multifaceted solo exhibition Untitled at Centre A. The accompanying
catalogue with foreword by Jeff Wall and text by Hank Bull will be launched
at the event. For those who missed Shot at the opening, we are pleased to
announce that the 35mm projector is up and running. Please join us for the
artist talk and see the projection.

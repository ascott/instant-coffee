VIVO Media Arts Centre Archives
Top Ten: 1987

Wednesday, October 26th - 8:00pm

In the early years of the Satellite Video Exchange Society, now VIVO Media Arts, 
the centre functioned as a community space for artists and the general public to 
drop in and watch videos from the library. Tape viewing statistics were tracked, 
and a list of the top ten most viewed titles were kept for each year from 1974 to 
1990. On the last Wednesday of every month, we'll present an informal 
screening of a selection of these works.

This month we present the top ten hits of 1987. Free popcorn!

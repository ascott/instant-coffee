Holly Ward is a Vancouver-based artist. She received her BFA
(Interdisciplinary) from the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design in 1999
and her MFA (Studio) from the University of Guelph in 2006. She has
exhibited in solo exhibitions across Canada at the Morris and Helen Belkin
Gallery (Vancouver), the Or Gallery (Vancouver), and YYZ Gallery (Toronto),
amongst others. She has participated in group exhibitions in Canada,
England, Mexico, the United States, Norway and South Korea. Her public art
project, News of the Whole World (2011), can currently be seen on the
exterior of the CBC building in downtown Vancouver. She is represented by
Republic Gallery (Vancouver).

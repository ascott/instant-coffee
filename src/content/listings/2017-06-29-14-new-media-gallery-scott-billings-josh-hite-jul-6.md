Two Vancouver-based artists discuss their individual and collaborative practice, 
including their installation Stairwell, part of the current New Media Exhibition 
BRIDGE. 

Thursday, July 6
7:00pm - 8:00pm
New Media Gallery, Anvil Centre
Opposite New Westminster Sky train Station
newmediagallery.ca
604.875.1865

BRIDGE exhibition continues until July 16 : Scott Billings, Josh HIte, Willie Doherty, 
Bill Fontana

Vancouver-based artists Josh Hite and Scott Billings discuss both their individual 
and collaborative practice, including Stairwell, 2016, part of the BRIDGE exhibition at 
New Media Gallery. Scotts practice looks at issues of cinematic spectatorship + the 
unconscious and the ways in which the apparatus itself reveals mechanisms of 
causality. Joshs practice is concerned with tactics for documentation and human 
movement through urban spaces. 

Stairwell is a multi-year project; the result of six years of exploration and 
experimentation. The work was shot in a stairwell located within the Burrard Bridge. 
Considered unsafe, it was boarded up shortly after it opened in 1932. Shot on a 
vertical track running the 62 foot height of the stairwell, this physical track is now 
installed horizontally in the gallery. A projector now pans along the horizontal plane 
of the gallery wall, unraveling images of a lost space in a sort of vertiginous 
unfolding. 

This is a public event, free of charge. Everyone welcome.

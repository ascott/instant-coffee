The Cinematheque is proud to present HARM: A Harmony Korine
Retrospective, a complete retrospective of l'enfant terrible Harmony
Korine's corrosive, cult-adored cinema, the first of its kind in
Canada. November 8-15.

The films of Harmony Korine (b. 1973) are a polarizing lot. Gleefully
provocative, formally challenging, morally oblique, and chock-full of
transgressions most filmmakers wouldn't dare dream of putting
onscreen, Korine's one-of-a-kind oeuvre has drawn outrage and
adulation in almost equal measure.

Included are all five of the bad-boy wunderkinds feature films, a
quintet of brazenly deviant works that obfuscate the term commercial
cinema. His three most exemplary affronts Gummo (1997), julien
donkey-boy (1999), and Trash Humpers (2009) screen from rarely
exhibited 35mm prints.

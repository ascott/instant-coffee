FIELD CONTEMPORARY
launches
HIGHWAY MAGAZINE NO. 1
ENDS BY ERICA WILK
MONIKER PRESS

with sound performances curated by
SOLEDAD MUÑOZ

presented in conjunction with
VANCOUVER ART/BOOK FAIR
ARTISTS' BOOK WEEK

________________
Saturday October 4
6–10pm
Field Contemporary, 17 West Broadway

________________
Field Contemporary is excited to announce our hosting of the Highway
Magazine vol. 1 launch and the Moniker Press Ends book launch in
conjunction with the unveiling of our new in-house, art-centric bookstore
during Vancouver Art/Book Fair’s Artist Book Week.

Join us Saturday October 4th to enjoy drinks, live readings, and sound
performances curated by Genero founder Soledad Muñoz.

Highway is a publication committed to finding new ways to think about and
understand culture. Through artist collaborations, fragmentary fiction,
cultural analysis, speculative futures, food-as-memory, and experimental
sound, we strive for the universal.

Moniker Press presents its newest publication Ends; a limited edition zine
complied by Vancouver artist, Erica Wilk. Ends collages elements of
conversations, interpretations, secrets, discussions, the collective
consciousness, and infinitely more.

Moniker Press is micro publishing studio in Vancouver that works
collaboratively with artists and writers to produce small editions of
books, zines and print ephemera.

We hope you can join us.

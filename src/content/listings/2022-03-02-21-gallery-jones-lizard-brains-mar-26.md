Artist: Fiona Ackerman
Gallery: Gallery Jones
Exhibition title: Lizard Brains
Exhibition dates: February 26 to March 26, 2022

Artist Statement:

I see myself as a lone painter in a sea of musicians. My community, closest friends and
husband have all been active musicians, filling my world
with music. My relationship to
music is largely as a vested listener, though sometimes I write songs and perform.
What happens in my studio often begins with an intellectual pursuit. Painting becomes
a framework through which to explore my interests in philosophy, botanical history,
nature, colour and composition.

Beginning this new series, I felt hungry for the freedom of music. Music is visceral and
deeply personal. When it works, music comes from somewhere unselfconscious and
raw. Abandoning the search for theme and subject, I set out with rolls of blank canvas
and no plan. I painted instinctively, trusting impulse, leaning on what popular science
calls the Lizard Brain: primitive, unthinking response to stimuli.

Working this way for hours, while listening to music on my headphones, I began to
wonder what it is to appreciate painting and music. A piece of music or work of art
becomes meaningful when it reflects or summons a familiar yet indescribable feeling.
Music situates the
feeling in time, while painting lends it a place. Our oldest stories
emerge from our Lizard Brains, retold, once upon a time, in another place ... endlessly
repeated, always new. These paintings are the result of quiet listening, the record of a
time when I was immersed in music, and let the act of painting lead.

- Fiona Ackerman

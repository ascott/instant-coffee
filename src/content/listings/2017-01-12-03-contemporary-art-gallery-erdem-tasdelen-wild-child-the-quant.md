Erdem Tadelen
Wild Child 
Events room

The Quantified Self Poems
Window spaces

January 13 to March 19, 2017
Opening: Thursday, January 12, 7-9pm
Artist talk at CAG: Thursday, January 19, 6.30pm

Join us for the launch of two new works by Toronto based artist Erdem Tadelen.

Commissioned by CAG in partnership with Cineworks, Wild Child is an ambitious two-
part video installation which takes as its starting point, An Historical Account of the 
Discovery and Education of a Savage Man by French physician Jean Marc Gaspard 
Itard. Tadelen adapts the story, this time set in contemporary British Columbia and 
presented through two distinct elements.

The Quantified Self Poems, is a new series of twelve screen prints. Over three months 
Tadelen reported his moods daily on "Emotion Sense", a self-improvement app. 
Answering a series of questions the artist's feelings were numerically encoded as data 
effectively quantifying the unquantifiable and translated into words drawn from a unique 
dictionary created by Vancouver-based poet Daniel Zomparelli.

Combined each work exposes the dynamics at play through differing representations of 
human nature, the familiar made compellingly strange.

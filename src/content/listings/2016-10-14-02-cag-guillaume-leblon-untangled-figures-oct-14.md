Guillaume Leblon
UNTANGLED FIGURES
October 14, 2016 to January 1, 2017
Opening: Thursday, October 13, 7-9pm

Join us to celebrate the launch of a solo exhibition by French artist
Guillaume Leblon.
Featuring a radical transformation of our galleries populated by a
selection of new and recent works, the exhibition will create a
characteristically poetic universe, a world of its own.
We gratefully acknowledge the support of Institut Francais and the
Consulate general de France in Vancouver. The exhibition is generously
supported by Jane Irwin and Ross Hill.

You are invited to apply for up to $3,000 to deliver a small activation, temporary
feature, or beautification project to bring people together in a Vancouver public
space.

Proposals should:

Encourage social connections and strengthen community
Be free, accessible, inclusive, safe and welcoming to all
Be interesting, fun, and engaging
Be easy to set-up and move
Be deliverable within the budget and timeframe
Be unique, creative and use a space to its full potential

Further details:

Successful applicants will receive up to $3,000 for their projects. Please note that we
are only accepting one application per applicant.
IBPOC, 2SLGBTQ+ and other under-represented residents, communities and
organisations are encouraged to apply and might be eligible for an additional
funding.
Projects are required to go live between June and October 2025 and should activate
one or more public spaces on at least one day.
Proposed projects may be a single-day event or a series of two non-consecutive
days.
Your project can be a community event, a temporary installation or a small
beautification project.
Applicants will be responsible for all aspects of their project, including setting up and
taking down, getting relevant permits, moving and storing materials, securing
insurance, and cleaning up after the project.

Approved Locations:

Plazas: Our neighbourhood plazas are excellent options for public space
programming and other small placemaking activities. View our map of
neighbourhood plazas.
A space in your community: You are the expert of your community. If you know of a
space in your community suitable for a small placemaking project such as an
underutilized open space, laneway or street block that could easily be closed, please
feel free to propose it.
Note that your preferred location is not guaranteed. We will review its feasibility and
get back to you.
Parks, beaches and community gardens are not eligible for this financial support.

For more details on the program and how to apply, visit vancouver.ca/community-
placemaking.

Applications are due by May 11th, 2025, at midnight PST.

We look forward to reviewing your proposal!

Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions.

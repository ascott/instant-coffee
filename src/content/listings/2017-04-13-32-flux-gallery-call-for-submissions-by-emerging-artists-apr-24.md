Flux Gallery
2-290 Mcdermot, Winnipeg, Manitoba r3b 1k2

Flux Gallery is seeking exhibition proposals from emerging artists.

To apply please submit the following in a digital format to flux@aceart.org by April 
24, 2017.

Successful applicants will hear by mid May 2017.

To submit, enclose:

An exhibition proposal (1 page)
Support Material:
-You may include up to 5 images
Individual images must be jpegs 72dpi, 1024 x 768 pixel, 500k (.5 MB) RGB or 
SRGB only
-Video may be submitted through a link to Vimeo, YouTube or any other easily 
accessed website, a max of 5 minutes will be reviewed by selection committee

When applying, please consider:
-Works involving sound must be played through headphones.
-Your work will be for sale; artist fees are therefore not applicable.
-A map of the space can be found at: http://www.aceart.org/facilities

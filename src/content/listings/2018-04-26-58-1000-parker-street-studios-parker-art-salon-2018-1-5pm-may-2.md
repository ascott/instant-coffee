Now in its fourth year, the Parker Art Salon 2018 is one of Vancouvers premier art 
events, a unique, salon-style exhibition showcasing the work of more than sixty fine 
artists at Vancouvers iconic 1000 Parker Street Studios.

Parker Art Salon takes place May 25 - 27, 2018, at 1000 Parker Street Studios. Open 
free to the public on Saturday, May 26 and Sunday, May 27 from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM, 
the Parker Art Salon features artist talks, studio tours, live music, and more.

Friday, May 25, 2018, the Parker Art Salon opens with the exclusive Collectors Night 
event at 1000 Parker Street Studios. Guests will join other art lovers, enjoy first dibs on 
the latest art pieces, and celebrate with food and wine as our rugged, industrial space is 
transformed into an elegant gathering celebrating fine art. Tickets: $20 in advance at 
https://www.eventbrite.com/e/parker-art-salon-tickets-42884969097?aff=eac2 $25 at the 
door, $50 for a Collectors Pass.

Parker Art Salon 2018 Collectors Night includes a Silent Art Auction (online bidding now 
open at www.parkerartsalon.com) and a Small Art Exhibition, with pieces priced at $200. 
Proceeds shared with www.AnxietyBC.com.

About Laiwan:

LAIWAN is an artist, writer and educator recognized for her 
interdisciplinary practice based in poetics and philosophy. Born in 
Zimbabwe of Chinese parents, she immigrated to Canada in 1977 to 
leave the war in Rhodesia. Her practice investigates embodiment 
through performativity, audio, music, improvisation, and varieties of 
media, along with bodily and emotional intelligence, so as to unravel 
and engage presence. She teaches in the MFA Interdisciplinary Arts 
Program at Goddard College (Washington State, USA) and is current 
Chair of the Board of Directors at grunt gallery (Vancouver).

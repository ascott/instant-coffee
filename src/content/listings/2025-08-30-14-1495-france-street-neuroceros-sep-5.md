SeFriday and Saturday, September 5 and 6, 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm

A site-specific Dance-Theatre performance work inspired by the play Rhinocros,
written by Romanian-French playwright Eugne Ionesco in 1959. We will expand on
themes from this absurdist play as they relate to our present time. Rhinocros
serves as a response to and critique of the rise of Fascism and Nazism before World
War II. Our performance will unfold over three acts and exploring conformity,
responsibility, logic, morality, mass movements, mob mentality, communication,
networks, and neuroses.

Ultimately, we aim to address the question: As progress on social, moral, and
environmental issues is reversed, who are we becoming, and how do we navigate
this change?

With artists James Gnam, Tara McArthur, Charlotte Boye-Christensen, Jesper
Egelund, Nathan Webster and Will Fu.

Presented by NOW-ID | NOW International Dance Co., a not-for-profit dance and
design organization.

RAGA INTERNATIONAL MAIL ART EXHIBITION AUCTION - PART 2
November 24, 2023 - December 31, 2023
Artworks start at $5 CDN!
https://app.charityauctionstoday.com/auctions/raga-mail-art-auction-part-2-40355

Join us for PART 2 of Collecting & Reassembling: International Mail Art Exhibition
Fundraiser
In the original exhibition from September 9 - November 5, 2023, the Richmond Art
Gallery had 288 artists from all over the world contribute postcard-size artworks for
the RAGA International Mail Art Exhibition & Fundraiser. The Art Gallery hosted an
open-call inviting artists to send in a work of Mail Art in response to the theme of
"collecting and reassembling." RAGA hosted a live in-person and online auction of all
the works on November 18, 2023.

After this event,
we wanted to ensure the remaining 130+ artworks had the
opportunity to find a home, and help RAGA with its fundraising goals. The artworks
in this Auction will be available for purchase now until December 31, 2023.
Works are currently on exhibit at the Richmond Art Gallery, and online. All proceeds
help support Richmond Art Gallery Association's outreach and education programs.
Your support will help to ensure our public programs remain free to all our
audiences, and our school programs are accessible to hundreds of local children
and youth.

How to participate in the online auction:
1. Go to the website Charity Auctions Today and set Up Your Account : Start by
creating your personal account, ensuring you're all set to bid on fantastic items and
support our cause: https://app.charityauctionstoday.com/auctions/raga-mail-art-
auction-part-2-40355
2. Find artworks you want to bid on: Artworks can be discovered by searching for the
artist name, city or country. Once you find the artwork
you want to bid on, click on
the artwork image to open up a new window.
3. Place Your Winning Bids: Place bids on the item by clicking "enter bid". Select the
pre-determined bidding amount, or an amount you would like to bid, and then click
"place bid".
4. Bidding increments: Bidding increments are automatically set. They are:
Bid Range Increment Amount
$1.00 - $10.00 $3.00
$11.00 - $50.00 $5.00
$51.00 - $100.00 $10.00
$101.00 - $500.00 $25.00
$501.00 - $1,000.00 $50.00

5. If you want to set up AUTOBID up to a certain amount: The website will
automatically add increments (listed above) up to an amount you select. You will
receive an email whenever the website places an auto bid for you.
6. Track Your Bids: Keep a close eye on your bids and stay informed about the
status of your favorite items on the website.
7. Celebrate Your Victories:
When you win an auction item, the auction site will notify you by email for each item
you won. Shortly after the winning notification email, you will
receive a second email
that will contain a link to view your invoice.
In a few days after the Auction closes on December 31, gallery staff will email you to
confirm whether you would like your artwork shipped to you or to pick up in person.
If work is being shipped to you, we will tally any shipping costs and add that amount
to your credit card before shipping. You will receive a second invoice via email for
the shipping amount.

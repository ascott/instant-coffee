Reception: Thursday, October 17, from 5 to 7 PM.

POWDER ROOM
Colourful RISO
prints by Little Mountain Press
September 19 to November 2, 2019

To coincide with the upcoming Vancouver Art Book Fair which Little Mountain Press is to
participate, Hotam Press Bookshop/Gallery will have a welcome reception on Thursday,
October 17, from 5 tp 7 PM.

Little Mountain Press is an art and illustration independent publisher from New York, USA
and Shenzhen, China. The press was established in the summer of 2018 by the illustrator
duo Xiao Mei and Mountain Dog. Little Mountain Press has published most zines and prints
in risograph. As a duo, they love bright colors and are not afraid to express their sexual and
ethnic identity. Their mission is to transform boredom into joy and challenge people's
perception through print media.

Powder Room is the first gallery exhibition of the print work by Little Mountain Press. Many
of Little Mountains customers have told Little Mountain that the prints make great decoration
in the bathroom. The powder also refers to the colourful toner
used in Risograph printing.

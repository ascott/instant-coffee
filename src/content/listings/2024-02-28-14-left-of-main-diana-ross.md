***
In 2015 I had a dream of Diana Ross on Broadway, dancing in a rose gold costume
in a sea of other rose gold-clad Black dancers. Years later I discovered that there
was a remarkably similar scene in the 1978 film The Wiz (in which Diana Ross plays
Dorothy).

As a teenager I attended Eric Hamber Secondary School (near Oakridge Mall) and
in a stage production of The Wiz mounted in the spring of my grade 11 year, I was
cast as Evillene,
The Wicked Witch. Decades later, the lyrics to one of the Wizards
songs from that film"So You Wanted To Meet The Wizard"became the central
text to inspire my first full-length choreographic work, the aisha of is. I havent been
able to let that movie go! These days the scene from The Wiz I recall most strongly
is the one in which Dorothy meets the Scarecrow (Michael Jackson) and they
embark on the yellow brick road in search of the Wizard. This now brings to my heart
the sacred belonging I feel with Devon, my collaborator and dearest friend, in this
work and in life.

It is exquisitely special for me to perform DIANA ROSS DREAM in Vancouver: in the
city I grew up in, and to an audience that will include my mother, sisters and niblings
as well as chosen family.

" Aisha Sasha John

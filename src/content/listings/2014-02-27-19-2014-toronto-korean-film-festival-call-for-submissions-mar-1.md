TKFF 2014 Korean Shorts SUBMISSION ANNOUNCEMENT

The Toronto Korean Film Festival is now calling for submissions from 
filmmakers!

The Toronto Korean Film Festival is a cultural organization that aims to present 
authentic Korean cinema to the audience of Toronto, as well as to support the 
Korean-Canadian film community. Selected films will be screened at 2014 
Toronto Korean Film Festival on May 30th at Art Gallery of Ontario.

Eligibility for TKFF 2014 Korean Shorts Program
Films must meet one of the following requirements:
Made by Korean or shot in Korea
Made by Korean living in Canada
About Korean culture or theme (e.g. Actors speaking Korean language most 
of time)

** All submissions in languages other than English must provide English 
subtitles.

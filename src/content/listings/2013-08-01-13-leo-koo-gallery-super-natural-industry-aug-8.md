LEO KOO Gallery presents…

SUPER NATURAL INDUSTRY

A group exhibition featuring:

Susanna Blunt
Michelle Jean Giguere
Robert Moss
Leandro Garcia Pimentel
Severino Trinca
Jasmine Wallace

All humans interact with the natural environment. We pave over 
grass and cut down trees, manipulating it to suit our needs. 
Alternatively, we cultivate plants and recycle paper, giving back 
to what we’ve used. Naturally, the line between organic and 
manufactured becomes more distorted every day. It’s hard to tell 
where we are at with nature. Are we in battle? If so, are we 
winning or losing? Or are we capable of living in harmony with 
nature?

SUPER NATURAL INDUSTRY deals with this battle for power 
between industry and nature. From the works of various artists, 
the contrasting relationships of natural elements and 
manufactured ideas are weaved together into our 
understandings of co-existence.

Exhibition from August 6th to August 31st

Opening reception on August 8 from 6pm to 8pm.

Curated by Natalie Cammarasana, Jennifer Yuen, and Vanessa 
Krystin Wong.

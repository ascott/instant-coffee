Sad, Depressed, People
David Horvitz

New Documents is pleased to announce the availability of Sad, Depressed, 
People, an artist book by David Horvitz.

Published this fall, Sad, Depressed, People looks at a set of images 
circulating within stock photography collections. These photographs, in which 
actors are photographed holding their heads in their hands, ostensibly 
depressed, are here shown to contain a bizarre tension between their status 
as stock images and the emotional content they are supposed to embody. 

David Horvitz is a New York–based artist who works with photography, the 
Internet, performance, and watercolor. Recurring interests across these 
disciplines include attention to how information is circulated and the 
impermanence of digital artifacts.

Sad, Depressed, People includes an introduction and glossary of terms by 
curator and writer Laurel Ptak. It is available for order through DAP 
(US/Canada), Motto Distribution (Europe/Asia), and directly from New 
Documents.

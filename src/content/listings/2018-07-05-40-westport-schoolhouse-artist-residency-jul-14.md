Westport Schoolhouse 
Artist Residency

This summer the Village of Westport in Nova Scotia is offering two live-in studio classrooms. 
Located on Brier Island in the midst of the Bay of Fundy, the Atlantic Ocean and Saint 
Marys Bay, the area is a pristine land and sea paradise with nature trails, beaches and 
wildlife. 

The Village of Westport has two churches, a general store with a liquor outlet, motels, two 
restaurants, a post office, laundromat and a library with internet access. Lobster fishing and 
whale watching tourism are the mainstay employment for the 200 year round residents.

The two basic live-in studios are large and full of natural light, conducive to contemplation 
and creativity. Each studio includes two single beds, a sofa, large tables to work on, chairs, 
etc. and has access to a common fully equipped kitchen and a bathroom with shower. Artists 
must bring all of their own art supplies.

Each live-in studio is being rented out to an artist on a two week basis for $400. If the artist 
chooses the studio may be shared with friend, companion or another artist at no extra cost.

Studio periods available are 

July 14th to July 28th,
July 28th to August 11th, 
August 11th to August 25th.

Interested artists should send 5 jpegs of their work, a short bio, short resume, short artist 
statement and indicate their preferred time period to Jane at

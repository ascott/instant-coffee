Drawing inspiration from Andr Breton's poignant question, "Isn't the real
significance of a work, not the meaning we think we give it, but the meaning it is
likely to take in relation to what surrounds it? Objects of Affection explores the
interplay between objects, context, and the observer's interpretation. On view is a
captivating presentation of works by gallery artists along with rare and internationally
sourced design pieces curated by Max King. The exhibition transcends the
conventional boundaries of artistic expression, embracing the paradoxical order of
eclectic curation and the natural world.

Objects of Affection

Curated by Max King

The Garage Public Exhibition Space
Call for Proposals
Deadline: Friday, February 22

With the Burrard Arts Foundations move into new premises at 258 East 1st Avenue 
has come an exciting addition to our programming: The Garage. A former loading 
bay, The Garage has been renovated into a street-facing, glass-fronted exhibition 
space.

In keeping with the BAFs mission to support emerging artists, foster creativity in the 
public realm, and break down barriers to accessibility, we are excited to announce 
that we will be opening up The Garage to proposals from local artists. The Garage is 
an ideal opportunity for artistic experimentation, and a low-barrier way for artists to 
get involved in BAFs activities. The Garage acts as the most public access point into 
BAFs on-site programming, and we are seeking proposals that will spark public 
curiosity and engage passersby with the gallery.

We are now accepting proposals, with a deadline of Friday, February 22nd. The 
selected artist(s) will be notified on Friday, March 1st, with the first selected 
installation opening on Friday, April 5 alongside BAF Gallerys next exhibitions from 
artists Holly Schmidt and Charlene Vickers.

Detailed Application Information:

1. Call for Entry 
The Burrard Arts Foundation (BAF) is seeking local artists to submit proposals for 
installations in our new street-facing exhibition space, The Garage. The Garage acts 
as the most public access point into BAFs on-site programming, and we are looking 
for work that will spark public curiosity and engage passersby with the gallery. 

2. Scope of Project 
Artists can apply individually or as a collective. The space is approximately 144 
square feet; 12 by 12, with a 12 foot ceiling. The space is lit day and night to be 
visible from the street at all times. Please see the diagram of The Garage space 
included at the end of this document. The selected artist(s) will be notified on Friday, 
March 1. The installation period will depend on the exhibition period the work is 
selected for; if selected for the next upcoming exhibition period, the installation of the 
work will take place from Sunday, March 24th to Wednesday, April 3. Selected 
proposal(s) will open concurrently with BAF Gallerys scheduled exhibitions. The 
next scheduled opening will be on April 5th. 

3. Eligibility 
This call for proposals is open to all Vancouver-based artists. We would like to 
particularly welcome submissions from student, emerging, and early-career artists. 
More weight will be placed on the strength of your submission than your existing CV. 

4. Project Budget 
An artist fee of $700 will be provided to each selected artist or collective. This fee 
includes all materials, taxes, and artists fees. 

5. Submission Requirements 
Please submit your Proposal as one PDF or Word Document. 
Include: 
- Your artistic CV, full name, and contact information. 
- A description of the proposed installation 
- Brief artist statement 
- Dimensions of work if applicable, and mediums used.
- As many images as possible to demonstrate the effect of your proposed installation 

6. How to Apply 
Please submit your proposal via email, to info@burrardarts.org. Include Garage 
Proposal and your name in the subject line. All proposals must be submitted by 
11:59PM, Friday February 22, 2019.

7. Floor Plan
A diagram of the exhibition space can be viewed on burrardarts.org, and 
downloading the Call for Proposals PDF in the most recent blog post, "The Garage: 
Call for Proposals"

Jennifer Kostuik Gallery presents Stu Oxley and Curtis Cutshaw: The Subconscious
Voice

Exhibition Dates: April 1 - May 1, 2022

Opening Reception: Friday April 1, 5 - 8 pm & Saturday April 2, 2 - 5 pm

Kostuik Gallery is pleased to present a duo exhibition of new artworks by two Canadian
artists, Stu Oxley and Curtis Cutshaw, who have each been represented by Jennifer
Kostuik for over 20 years. Both masters of abstraction, Stu Oxley's serene paintings on
paper with their expressive markings are contrasted with Curtis Cutshaw's rough
outlines and elaborately layered wood constructions.

***************************

Stu Oxley is based in Ontario and is an outstanding Canadian artist with a 40-year
history of exhibitions in national and international, public and private galleries. Primarily
working as a printmaker and painter, his practice is defined by a distinctive form of
poetic abstraction involving evocative colour.

Curtis Cutshaw is based in Calgary, Alberta and conceptualizes art that is both
painterly and sculptural. This new body of work is made of both created and found
materials that are colourful and graphic, giving new life and purpose to items that have
lost their original use.

Jennifer Kostuik Gallery is open Tuesday to Friday 10 am - 6 pm, Saturday 11 am - 5
pm, and Sunday 12 pm - 4 pm.

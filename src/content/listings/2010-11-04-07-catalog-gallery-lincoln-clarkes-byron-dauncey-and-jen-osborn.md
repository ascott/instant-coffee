Catalog Gallery presents a collection of beautiful women captured by three
Vancouver based photographers, Lincoln Clarkes, Byron Dauncey and Jen
Osborne. The selected photographs focus on vulnerability linked to beauty,
and beauty linked with power.

This dichotomy is most clear in the stylish Shot in America series by
Lincoln Clarkes, in which Texan women of all class pose with their loaded
guns. In a country constantly at war with itself and others, this series
reveals the pity of power.

Byron Dauncey’s Granville Street Saturday Night is voyeuristic and
intrusive. Women are photographed candidly outside nightclubs in
Vancouver’s Entertainment District, exposing a pressure to be beautiful,
and flaunting the authority that it enables.

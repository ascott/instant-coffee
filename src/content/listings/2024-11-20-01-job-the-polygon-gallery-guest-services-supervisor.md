The Polygon Gallery is seeking a Guest Services Supervisor to join their team.
Reporting to the Head of Guest Experience, the Guest Services Supervisor leads the
Guest Services staff on the sales floor, ensuring an exceptional guest experience,
effective retail operations, and support for revenue goals in admissions,
memberships, and retail.

Key responsibilities
Operations Management: Oversee day-to-day Guest Services operations,
including opening, closing, staff scheduling, and training.
Staff Leadership: Lead the hiring, training, and
ongoing development of Guest
Services staff. Ensure staff are informed about current exhibitions, events, and
programs.
Guest Services: Act as the primary point of contact for Guest Services inquiries,
ensuring an excellent guest experience.
Retail Sales Support: Encourage donations and drive sales to support Admissions,
Membership, and Retail revenue goals.
Administrative Support: Support the Retail Buyer with tasks such as the
consignment program, Lightspeed inventory management, product tagging, and
organizing sales desk operations.
Social Media: Maintain and post updates to the Gift Shops Instagram account to
promote products, events, and new items.
Collaboration: Work closely with the Head of Guest Experience to discuss and
implement strategic priorities for Admissions and Retail.
Team Building: Create a positive and productive team environment through
training, support, and regular feedback.

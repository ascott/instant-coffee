Maggee Day: Pop-up Art Residency

Location: Ranger Station Art Gallery (98 Rockwell Drive in Harrison Hot Springs, BC)

Time: Tuesday, August 29-Thursday, August 31 from 11 am-3 pm each day.

Description: Maggee Day is a visual artist working predominantly in the medium of
oil
paint. Day lives and works in Vancouver, on the unceded land of the Coast Salish
peoples, including the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh nations. During her
pop-up residency, she will be working on smaller canvases that are influenced by
visuals in the area of the gallery and Harrison Hot Springs.

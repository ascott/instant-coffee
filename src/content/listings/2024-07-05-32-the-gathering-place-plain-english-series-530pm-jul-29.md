Event: Art Talks in Plain English Series - July 29th 5:30 PM to 7PM

Place: The Gathering Place Community Center. Meeting Room, 609 Helmcken

Once a month over next 6 months , 2 graduate students from Emily Carr University
of Art and Design will give a 20 minute presentation about their work
followed by
informal discussion. Snacks and refreshments will be provided.

Plain English is talk that a general audience can understand the first time they hear
it. Plain English is clear, straightforward expression.

In July the presentations will by:

Jefferson Allade
"Listening Spaces: Reimagining Music Listening Experience"

Andrea Simmonds
Avant-Gardening with Andrea Simmonds

This event is supported by the British Columbia Arts Council, the Connection Salon
Collective and the Gathering Place Community Center.

BOOK LAUNCH & READING - Off the Map: Vancouver Writers with Lived Experience
of Mental Health Issues

Date & Time: Saturday, June 7th from 1-4 pm
Venue: VPL Central Library, Alice MacKay Room
Location: 350 West Georgia St., Vancouver

Off the Map: Vancouver Writers with Lived Experience of Mental Health Issues (Bell
Press) aims to bring diverse and distinctive voices to the literary landscape. Edited by
Betsy Warland, Seema Shah, and Kate Bird, this anthology of creative nonfiction,
fiction, and poetry on a wide range of subjects showcases unique storytelling styles
and forms, and oers fresh and fascinating perspectives. The authors in this collection
write with compelling honesty and inventiveness.

The event will feature short readings by anthology contributors and offer attendees the
opportunity to buy books and have them signed. Please join the drop-in celebration!
Free to attend. Visit vpl.ca (Events Calendar) for more information and to register -
registration not required, but appreciated.

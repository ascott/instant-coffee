Saturday, September 15, 2018
10:00 AM - 4:00 PM
Reliance Theatre
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
520 East 1st Avenue
Vancouver

Join us for a symposium to share the results of the Ten Different Things
series: http://tendifferentthings.ecuad.ca/

Ten Different Things is a series of public art commissions presented
throughout Vancouver in Spring 2018. Artists were invited to create new
works in the spirit of free inquiry at the intersection of public art,
community engagement, and civic process.

Participating artists Colleen Brown, Instant Coffee, Laiwan, Khan Lee,
Holly Schmidt, Associate Professor Henry Tsang, Janet Wang, Casey Wei, Jen
Weih, and Denise Holland and Pongsakorn Yananissorn will give short
presentations about their work and findings to be followed by lunch and an
afternoon panel.

10:00 Welcome and Introductions
10:20: Artist talks
12:00: Lunch
2:00: Panel Discussion moderated by Adrian Sinclair

Curated by Kate Armstrong, the Ten Different Things series is a
collaboration between CityStudio Vancouver and Living Labs, and supported
by the City of Vancouver Public Art Program.

Questions about the Symposium? Contact Kate Armstrong: karmstrong@ecuad.ca

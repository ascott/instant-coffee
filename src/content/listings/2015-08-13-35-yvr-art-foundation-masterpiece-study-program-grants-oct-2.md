Call for Artist Applications
Masterpiece Study Program Grants
YVR Art Foundation
Eligibility: Open to BC and Yukon First Nations artists
Submission Deadline: October 2, 2015 - 4pm

The YVR Art Foundation is now accepting applications from BC and 
Yukon First Nations artists for the Masterpiece Study Program grants. 
Grants of up to $2,500 each will be awarded to BC and Yukon First 
Nations artists seeking funding to travel to one of four participating 
museums to research/study the museums' collections of First Nations 
art and artifacts.

How to Apply for a Grant:
Complete information on the YVR Art Foundation and the Masterpiece 
Study Program, including grant application guidelines, application 
forms, and how to submit a grant application can be found on the YVR 
Art Foundation website www.yvaf.com/programs

If you have any questions, please contact the YVR Art Foundation at 
info@yvaf.com or phone 604.276.6261.

Disrupted Narratives. Art Exhibition. Queen Elizabeth
Theatre, July 12 to September
18.
This free event will include a 6:30pm artist talk and live music by Louise and Greg Weir
throughout the event on August 9th.

About the Exhibition

The current visual arts exhibition at the Queen Elizabeth Theatre features work by
CARFAC BC member, Dorothy Doherty, and Olga Campbell and Jane McDougall.
Disrupted Narratives brings together the work of three artists who share a common
vision, employing similar techniques and materials in their art.

The exhibition features artwork where layers of paper are adhered to wood panels and
altered, distressed, abraded, and more. The pieces are worked and reworked until the
desired aesthetic is achieved. To this end, the artworks in this exhibition include early
works, as well as new works inspired by experimentation, time, and change. This
exhibition presents the diversity of possibilities that can arise from a non-traditional
approach with modern materials.

Everyone is welcome. The Open House is open to the
general public one day only.
Otherwise, the only way to see the exhibition is with a ticket to an event in the theatre.

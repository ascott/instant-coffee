NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS form public constellations of
engaged interdisciplinary practice, unmediated by the realpolitik
conditions of contemporary art as a competitive professional sphere.
The interrelatedness of art, society and everyday life is critically
explored amongst a diverse plurality of artists and publics. Jeremy
Todd instigates each event as an extension of his ongoing Not Sent
Letters Project, an entanglement with the production of meaning, self
and cultural memory, involving image/text epistolary detours online
(since 2005), digital shorts, interdisciplinary performance works and
cooperatively realized public events: http://notsentlettersproject.com

This is the twentieth NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS event.

STPHANE BERNARD, a Vancouver-based artist and writer, presents
Hospital Landscape. The work combines elements of drawing with a
spoken word presentation of sampled texts by the likes of Borges,
Joyce, Keats, Thoreau, Updike, and Wilde; with instrumentals by
Bowie.

TOMOYO IHAYA, a Vancouver-based artist often working
abroad, presents a letter written to a friend living in a politically
sensitive place. The letter can't be sent without compromising the
addressee's safety and will be read aloud at NSL&G by KUNSANG
CHOEKYI. The first draft of a new animated short will be screened
immediately following the reading, dedicated by the artist to everyone
caught up in similar circumstances.

AHMAD TABRIZI, a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary artist and
designer, resurrects Gullible Gutsy, a gate keeping, nepotistic,
incestual art-critic-historian-whore that nobody wants to hear from.

DAMLA TAMER & JEREMY TODD, two Vancouver-based
interdisciplinary artists, engage with the politics of encounter, friendship
and the atomization of contemporary life. Reduction, elaboration, mis-
and over-interpretation become discursive strategies within an
accumulative exchange and presentation of images and text.

LAURA BUCCI, a Vancouver-based artist exploring inter-human
relations through intervention, screens Redbook Presents: Causes &
Effects, a work juxtaposing vintage footage and contemporary screen
experiences to question the modern pursuit of meaning.

FILIP GORECKI, a Vancouver-based artist and musician focusing on
time, duality and age, screens his latest music video: Subaqueous II.

GRAHAM MEISNER a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary artist and
musician, returns to NSL&G with a music video inspired by This Picture
of You (first letter) from the NOT SENT LETTERS project archive.

RAFAEL ALBERTO ARGUELLO RUIZ, a Vancouver-based filmmaker
and video artist, screens Genesis, a work exploring hairstyles,
connectivity with nature and other related themes.

JEREMY TODD presents a new NOT SENT LETTERS SET with BEN
WILSON, ERIN SIDDALL, SARAH SHAMASH & CYNTHIA FLEUR
BRONAUGH. The work incorporates performance, projections, live
scoring, and play, with a first letter to Clotho, Lachesis and Atropos, a
second letter to My Waking Self and a third letter to Julia, selected and
read from the project archive:
http://notsentlettersproject.com/category/letters

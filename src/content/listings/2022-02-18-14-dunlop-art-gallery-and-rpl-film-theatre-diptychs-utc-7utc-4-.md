Hello, its my pleasure to announce the launch of Diptychs UTC-7/UTC-4, a new series
of videos by Johanna Householder, Judith Price, and collaborating artists seth cardinal
dodginghorse, Jeanne Randolph, Anne Bourne, Rita McKeough, Homo Monstrous and
Jeff Morton.

We will be launching the project with an online House Party, with seth cardinal
dodginghorse, Jeanne Randolph, and Anne Bourne, which will include a tour of shared
virtual and physical spaces to witness how physical spaces influence the artists work.
This event will take place on Dunlop Art Gallerys YouTube channel at 7 PM CST on
Tuesday, February 15.

Diptychs UTC-7/UTC-4, is featured in Dunlop Art Gallerys Digital Lounge at Regina
Public Library. The works are also available online, and in the RPL Film Theatre
accompanying screenings of films selected by the artists.

Looking forward to seeing you there!

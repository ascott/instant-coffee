Open Call For Artist Submissions: Sights/Sites of Spectacle
29th Annual UBC, Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory Graduate Symposium and 
Exhibition

In conjunction with the 29th Annual UBC Art History, Visual Art and Theory (AHVA) Graduate 
Symposium,conference organizers invite artists – local, national and international – to present 
projects for the exhibition Sights/Sites of Spectacle, to take place at UBC in Vancouver, B.C. from 
January 29th – February 11th, 2010.

For details please visit:
[http://www.ahva.ubc.ca/infoDisplay.cfm?Keyword=Graduate%20Symposium](http://www.ahva.ubc.ca/infoDisplay.cfm?Keyword=Graduate%20Symposium)

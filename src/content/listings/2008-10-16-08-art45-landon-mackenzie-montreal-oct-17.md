       LANDON MACKENZIE – Neurotransmitter
 
From October 17 to November 8, Art45 is pleased to present recent paintings from Canadian artist Landon Mackenzie. Neurotransmitter brings together small oil paintings as well as Mackenzie's trademark large-scale works. The exhibition focuses on the artist's fascination with temporal networks and fictitious mapping of the nervous system. Skillfully using bold colors and complex patterns, the artist continues to link the intriguing spheres of landscape, memory and metabolic systems.
 
Landon Mackenzie studied at the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design and received her Masters of Fine Arts from Concordia University in Montreal where she also began her teaching career. She won First Prize for her "Lost River Series" in the Third Quebec Biennial of Painting. Her work is included in the collections of the National Gallery of Canada, the Vancouver Art Gallery, the Art Gallery of Ontario, the Montreal Museum of Fine Arts and the Musée d'art contemporain de Montreal among others. Mackenzie is on the faculty of the Emily Carr Institute of Art + Design in Vancouver.

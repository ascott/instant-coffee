Welcome to Unbound: Activating the Artist Book, an exhibition co-curated by Katherine
Allaye-Chan, Louise Cointepas-Robin, Mike Ma, Allysa
Pierre, Aaron Rice, Defne
Taslicay, Jesse Ward, Hailey Wispinski and Ella Zheng. In collaboration with Emily Carr
Universitys Artist Book Collection, this show circulates around selected artist books of
each curators choosing. This unique showcase brings together ephemera of art history
in conversation with new and emerging artists in the Vancouver art scene. This pairing
will activate the archives and open up the individual works through relational dialogue.
Explore our haphazard visual library that recoils the narratives of our groups' selected
artist books. This show moves away from the binding, gathering and order that books
fall into and is untethered by strict parameters of cohesion. It involves extraction and
unthreading of multiple reaches and choices by the curators. Unbound poses the
questions of limitation, restraint, collective disharmony, and freedom.

Opening Friday the 24th 6-8pm at Trapp Projects 274 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC V5T
1A6. Exhibition available for viewing
1-4 November 25th.

Exhibiting Artists: Jiaxi Chen, Louise Cointepas-Robin, Andrew Dadson, Chloe Gilson,
Lucas Gomez Cid, Louis Karoniaktajeh Hall, Gwangjoon Kim, Nico McGiffin, Laura
Perry, and Wenqing Tan, Hailey Wispinski, and Christina Young Kim.

Analogue Basics: Introduction to Bolex 16mm Camera

Sunday 14 Jan 2018-2:00pm-6:00pm
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander Street (Ironworks basement),
Vancouver, V6A 1C1, entry via driveway

A 4 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and practice 
of exposing film with a Bolex 16mm film camera.

$60 + taxes | General Admission
$50 + taxes | Concessions and Community Partner Members* 
$40 + taxes | Member Admission

REGISTER: https://analogue-basics-bolex-jan-2018.eventbrite.ca

Elements include:

The mechanics and chemistry of exposing film
Understanding lenses
Handling film
Loading and unloading film in light and dark conditions
Shooting in natural light

Film shot in this workshop will be processed at the Analogue Basics: Intro to Hand 
Processing workshop on Sunday 28 Jan. If interested in both components (optional), 
please register for the Hand Processing workshop via the following link:

https://analogue-basics-processing-jan-2018.eventbrite.ca

*Community partners include VIVO, Women in Film, and Doc BC

to an orientation at our Howe Street location.

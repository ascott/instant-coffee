louder than the sum of its parts

at the fifty fifty arts collective

2516 Douglas Street, Victoria BC

Saturdays from 12-4 until May 25

featuring experiential bilateral drawing with Shauna Kaendo May 11 and 18 from 1-
2pm

This solo exhibition by Vancouver based artist, Amanda Wood, invites deeper
exploration into how we perceive our environments and relationships by translating
the overlooked into soundscapes, pattern repeats and immersive installations.
Drawing from patterns found in nature, such as bird flocking, in combination with
repeated actions, and common materials Amanda attempts to create a language for
the unspoken and to hold space for uncertainty.

Join art therapist, Shauna Kaendo for two bilateral drawing sessions on Saturday,
May 11 and
Saturday, May 18 from 1-2pm. In these free sessions, materials and
prompts will be offered for folks interested in exploring the process of bilateral
drawing (drawing with two hands). How it might relate to and intersect with Amanda's
installation - and with our own bodies and nervous systems, as we navigate what it
is to be in a body, with a nervous system...in a world. We'll have a variety of drawing
materials, and a collaborative drawing surface. No experience with art or drawing
required to participate!

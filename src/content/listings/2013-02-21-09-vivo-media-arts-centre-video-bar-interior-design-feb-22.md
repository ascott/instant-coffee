Video Bar: INTERIOR DESIGN
Friday, February 22, 8PM-12AM
1965 Main St. Vancouver
Ambient social. FREE.

http://www.vivomediaarts.com/event/video-bar-interior-design
Programmed by Shockk Mongoose

INTERIOR DESIGN is a project by guitarist/composer Shockk Mongoose in 
which ambient sounds, night photography and music combine to create a 
series of darkly lyrical tonal landscapes.
Deploying subtly detailed beats and effects with fretboard technique, bursts of 
color with rich drones and Reichian loops, Shockk's music locates a still point 
in the listener's mind; it's a headphone trip in an unknown part of the city with 
an unobtrusive companion.

Shockk has played lead guitar in many punk rock bands for over fifteen years, 
touring Canada, USA and Japan. In addition to his collaborative projects, he 
teaches, composes experimental music and captures railway tracks, trains 
and empty industrial sites at night, meandering through the city with his 
camera.

Rennie Collection at Wing Sang is the largest active contemporary art
collection in Canada. We maintain a privately funded museum that is
open to the public. We are currently seeking an experienced fine art
preparator to assist with collection and museum exhibition
preparations. This is a full time permanent position reporting directly to
the Director.

Responsibilities

work closely with gallery administrators and artists to produce and
maintain high-quality exhibitions by assisting in the planning,
preparation, security, installation and de-installation of gallery
exhibitions and other display materials, including maintaining gallery
walls and building display units for exhibitions accurate and timely
updates to inventory in database produce detailed and accurate
condition reports with all associated data and images, release forms,
installation instructions, etc. maintain galleries, art storage areas, and
preparatory spaces, and when necessary, perform some basic building
maintenance care of artworks in the permanent collection pack and
unpack incoming and outgoing works of art load and unload truck and
van deliveries, and transport artwork locally maintain shop equipment
and oversee the safe operation of security doors, fire exits, and alarm
systems when necessary, arrange for, install and operate audio-
visual equipment for exhibitions, meetings and public events assist
with the set-up and take-down of museum-related functions

Requirements

high school diploma or equivalent (BFA or other Bachelor's
degree an asset)
minimum two years in exhibit preparation and/or art handling
must have a valid BC Driver's License and clean driving record
able to work occasional irregular hours, including evenings
and weekends
ability to plan, prioritize, schedule and implement projects
simultaneously, and oversee a variety of projects
strong time management and problem-solving ability;
pragmatic organizational skills, with ability to meet deadlines efficiently
working knowledge of art handling procedures for two-and-
three-dimensional art objects in a variety of materials, sizes, and
weights, including the rigging and movement of art, mount making
principles, and lighting concepts
knowledge of art handling practices and procedures: safe art
storage, packing, crating, and transport techniques and procedures
ability to explain and carry out methods and procedures
relating to the handling of irreplaceable objects of art
ability to interpret working drawings; develop and implement
design concepts, and knowledge of the design and construction of
museum exhibits
experience in using standard tools, practices and methods
in painting and basic carpentry
some audio-visual experience an asset
skill in the safe use and care of standard hand and power
tools; and knowledge of safety regulations, practices and procedures
have a proven appreciation for high value works of art and a
solid knowledge of artists' methods, materials and techniques
excellent attention to detail, strong interpersonal skills and
ability to effectively communicate (both verbally and in writing)
Working Conditions and Physical Requirements

ability to climb ladders and stairs and work on scaffolding or
ladders
able to lift materials up to 50 pounds without assistance
ability to stoop, bend, kneel and to stand for up to eight (8)
hours per day
ability to safely operate power tools, and other equipment
and machinery that require constant attention
ability to work around paints, solvents, and other similar
products in a gallery environment
Renumeration

Salary commensurate with experience
Benefit package available

How to Apply

Please submit via EMAIL ONLY to Human Resources
(hr@renniecollection.org):

resume showing relevant experience
cover letter that specifically addresses how you meet the
qualifications
three professional references (references will be contacted,
in confidence, and credentials will be confirmed prior to hire)
A criminal records check will be conducted before final job offer.
Only candidates selected for an interview will be contacted.

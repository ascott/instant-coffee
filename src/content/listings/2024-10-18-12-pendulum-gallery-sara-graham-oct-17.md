Sara Graham

The Spaces That Were Missed Have Been Rearranged

Reception : Thursday, October 17, 6:00 " 8:00pm

Graham's work is primarily concerned with issues and ideas that surround and make
up the contemporary city. Her practice is predominately rooted in drawing with the
focus on exploring different ways and means to transform a viewers perception
of
place using forms of architecture, storytelling and mapping.

The Spaces That Were Missed Have been Rearranged brings together 10 years of
her studio practice and observations of the city. Her artwork is informed by daily
observations of the urban form, and how spaces and places interpolate as one
moves through them and points of views are constantly shifting and revealing new
perspectives.

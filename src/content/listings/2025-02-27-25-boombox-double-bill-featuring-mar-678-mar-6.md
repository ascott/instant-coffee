The universe at home
Scott Kemp + Alexi Baris

A kaleidoscope-meets-lava-lamp-style artist's rendering of the microbial landscape
that thrums inside and around our bodies all the time, just beyond the reach of
normal senses.

Scott Kemp is an interdisciplinary artist whose art practice dialogues with science,
pseudo-science, graphic design and other systems of belief.

Alexi Baris is a composer based in Vancouver, Canada " the unceded, traditional
territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), and
Sl"lwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations. Bariss methods are patient and deceptively
rigorous, trading in sonics that are at once organized and organic. His is a diligent
and painterly approach to sound design and arrangement, in which tiny events are
magnified and
brought up close, and expansive gestures are repurposed and shifted
in scale. Alexi was the artist in residence at the Western Front in the fall of 2023
where he presented his piece Firedamp. Recent album releases include Support
Surfaces (Soda Gong, 2022) and Thema (Total Stasis, 2020).

She made items appear
Zoe Kreye and Guadalupe Martinez

She made items appear - like visions, magic before my real eyes. Her hands caress,
her daughters head, softly, gently, deeply brushing her hair. A touch so loving, so
real, so true, that it travels to the centre of both their beings. My body expands. I feel
myself changing and I am scared a human fear. This is an acceptance of all the
stories that proceeded them, and that are still to come. Like true knowing, or a
protector of all beings.

Zoe Kreye and Guadalupe Martinez are interdisciplinary artists and performance
collaborators. Their work takes form through objects, drawing, writing, movement,
and friendship using the medium of
performance art as the realm through which their
imagery, sensations, and concepts are brought to life, ritualized and integrated as
part of their healing and spiritual processes. Both Kreye and Martinez live in
Vancouver and show their work across Canada and internationally.

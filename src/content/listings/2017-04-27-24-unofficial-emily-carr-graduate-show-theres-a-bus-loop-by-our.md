theres a bus loop by our house And An Art Show In Our Basement 
(unofficial) Emily Carr Graduate Show
May 6, 2017. 6pm - 9pm (ONE NIGHT ONLY)
6005 Alma st, Vancouver

A house built in 1938, last sold for $2,650,000 and is destined to be demolished like 
many of the homes in Vancouver. In its basement, preserved, artifacts of previous 
families. The objects found in a basement such as this experience little curation and 
consideration for the aesthetic experience, but many basements carry the same 
aesthetic. Raw wood, unfinished ceilings and floors, cob webs, and an abundance of 
objects left unappreciated . 

The Liquid Lunch Collective presents for ONE night only, theres a bus loop by our 
house And An Art Show In Our Basement, the debut show hosted by the Collective 
showcasing the work of a group of artists graduating from Emily Carr University. This 
body of work is created and presented in response to both the precision of the white 
cube and the haphazard of a basement. Work created by Terry-Dayne Beasley, Simon 
Hauck, Daniel Hoffman, Kate Metten, Sahand Mohajer, Jack Morris, Stefan Obusan, 
Natalie Schmidt, Louis Trautman, Dylan Townley-Smith, and Iman Virji. The show takes 
the experience of a gallery and loosely drapes it over the pre-existing history of a truly 
remarkable basement. Located conveniently by the bus loop on 41st and Dunbar; An 
Art Show in The Basement.

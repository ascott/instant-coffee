Artist Talk with Ray Ophoff

Sunday, January 30, 2022 at 2pm
Outsiders and Others - online via Zoom

Join us for an artist talk with self-taught Ray Ophoff next Sunday, the last day of his
current exhibition at the Outsiders and Others gallery. Its online and free to join in.
Gallery Director / Curator Yuri Arajs will speak with Ray about his painting, coffee,
albums, and family. Zoom link available at www.outsidersandothers.com (the Zoom link
for the talk will be posted on the gallerys website Sunday morning).

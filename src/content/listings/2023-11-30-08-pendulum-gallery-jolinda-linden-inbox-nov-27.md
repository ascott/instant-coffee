Chris Keatley

Jolinda Linden

METHODS

Nov 27 " Dec 16, 2023

An overview of artist Jolinda Lindens conceptual approaches and working methodology
developed during her career as a porcelain-based artist. Her practice is based in a
dedicated and determined exploration into the properties of porcelain, which she pushes
to its artistic and physical limits. Underlying Lindens process is an approach which limits
the formal qualities of the imagery to explore and develop a deeper relationship with raw
materials and their ultimate capabilities.

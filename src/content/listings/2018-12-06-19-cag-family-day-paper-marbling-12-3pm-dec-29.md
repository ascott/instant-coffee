Family Day | Paper Marbling
Saturday, December 29, 12-3pm, free
Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

On the last Saturday of each month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition 
tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Inspired by Dove Allouches works featuring funghi spores, join us for a workshop 
experimenting with special inks to create colourful, expanding forms on water. Well 
transfer from water to paper to fix your unique forms.

No reservation required, simply just turn up!

ARTS ASSEMBLY: Jacob Gleesoon | Tent Shop

The installation will be open from:
3pm Tuesday, August 8th, to 8pm Thursday, August 10th.

The festival is free and open to the public.

Arts Assembly is delighted to host an iteration of Jacob Gleeson's Tent Shop from
August 8-10th, 2017. In West Vancouver along the waterfront on Argyle Avenue,
from 14th Street to 16th Street.

As an intervention in this year's Harmony Arts Festival, Jacob Gleeson will be
creating an interior installation space using the modular tents usually intended for art
and craft sales.

Elaborating on and abstracting the idea of the market, Gleeson will transform the
space inside into a bizarre and speculative departure from the tradition of the arts
festival.

Jacob Gleeson is a Vancouver based artist and facilitator whose individual and
collaborative projects have appeared in numerous galleries, festivals, publications
and provisional spaces including: SFU Gallery, Western Front, Belkin Satellite,
Catriona Jeffries, Charles H. Scott, VIVO, Duplex, Access, Model, Open Space,
Noorderzon Festival, Nuit Blanche, Contemporary Magazine, Fillip and Vancouver
Art & Economies (Artspeak)

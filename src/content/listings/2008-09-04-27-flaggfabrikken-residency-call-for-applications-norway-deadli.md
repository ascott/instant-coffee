CALL FOR APPLICATIONS

Flaggfabrikken international artist residency in Bergen, Norway

Flaggfabrikken centre for photography and visual arts is offering an
opportunity for artists that work with camera based art to undertake a
three months residency in Bergen, Norway.

Next application date is October 1st 2008.

The residency is supported by the municipality of Bergen. Flaggfabrikken
offer two residencies a year, one for the spring period and one for autumn.
October 1st 2008 we accept applications both for spring (March-May) and
autumn (Sept-Nov). Please inform us of which period you are applying for
and if you are applying for both periods

Flaggfabrikken is an artist collective based in Bergen. In addition to
having our own studios and artist practice at Flaggfabrikken, we work in
different ways to promote camera based art locally and internationally.

For more information, please visit www.flaggfabrikken.net
<[http://www.flaggfabrikken.net](http://www.flaggfabrikken.net)> .

Flaggfabrikken will with the generous financial support from Bergen
Municipal offer the artist: studio, accommodation, travel expenses paid and
give a small grant towards covering other living expenses during the
artist's stay. During the residency the artist is expected to give a talk
on his/her work for the members of Flaggfabrikken and local
artists/curators. In addition Flaggfabrikken suggests that the artist will
take the opportunity of showing work in collaboration with a local institution.

PLEASE USE THE APPLICATION FORM. You can download it here:
[http://www.flaggfabrikken.net/default.asp?k=8&id=48](http://www.flaggfabrikken.net/default.asp?k=8&id=48)
<[http://www.flaggfabrikken.net/default.asp?k=8&amp;id=48](http://www.flaggfabrikken.net/default.asp?k=8&amp;id=48)>

Applications must include

- up to 20 (preferably digital, 1024768 px, 72 dpi) images of previous works

- And/or a Dvd of video-art

- Project proposal for the residency period (max 500 words)

- Artist statement (max 500 words)

- Updated cv (max 2 A4 pages)

THE APPLICATION MUST REACH FLAGGFABRIKKEN BY POST OR E-MAIL NO LATER THAN
OCTOBER 1ST 2008. The residencies will be decided before November 15th 2008.

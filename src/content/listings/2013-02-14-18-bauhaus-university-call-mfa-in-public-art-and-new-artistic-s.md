Call for Submissions: MFA in Public Art and New Artistic Strategies at the 
Bauhaus University

Dear all prospective applicants,

the new aptitude test questions 2013 are uploaded now!
www.uni-weimar.de/mfa/test

Please be aware of the information on our website regarding the
application process and requirements. Read it thoroughly.
In addition you can find a detailed description of the "Gauforurm"
(question 2) and visuals there.

Attention
Foreign applicants must submit their official certified materials and
documents in not only the original language, but also in German or
English.
Applicants from the People's Republic of China require the Certificate
of the Academic Examiner of the daad in Peking.
The application form for registration, along with the letter of
acceptance, will first be sent when the entry requirements are
completed (by the beginning of May).
Applicants that do not successfully complete the aptitude test will
receive a written notification. Their documents will be returned if a
self-addressed envelope with international postage certificate stamps
is included with their application materials. Please do not forget to
send it, thanks.

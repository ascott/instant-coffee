Fire, Steel and Flowers - Street Art for the Soul: A Surreal Adventure by Ron Simmer

Reception Thursday May 2nd 7 to 11 pm. There will be a bar, snacks and a DJ.

Beaumont Gallery 2109 Alberta St., Vancouver BC. Gallery hours Monday " Saturday 9
AM " 5 pm Show wraps Saturday May 11.

Ron Simmer is a local Vancouver sculptor from the Burning Man community and exhibits
public art in BC and several US states. As head of ReVision the Art of Recycling society
he produces a wide spectrum of art from upcycled materials and found objects, from
Pop to Steampunk. His work is interactive, questioning and direct. He contributes
fun art
to festivals and events celebrating music, spirituality and community " art for the
moment. He creates art highlighting environmental issues " plastic pollution, destruction
of the biosphere and the global climate disaster.

Author bios

Weyman Chan has written short stories and poems for over 30 years. His
first book, Before A Blue Sky Moon (Frontenac House), received the 2002
Writers Guild of Alberta Stephansson Poetry Prize. His second book, Noise
From the Laundry (Talonbooks), was a finalist for the 2008 Governor
General's Award for poetry. His fifth book of poetry, Human Tissue, was
published by Talonbooks in 2016. An intensely personal clash between
purpose and temporality, sentiment and fate, informs Weyman's writings, or
bio-histories-- which imprint and iterate the human tissue of language on
to the collective imagination.

Kayla Czaga's first book, For Your Safety Please Hold On (Nightwood
Editions 2014), a coming of age collection of poems, won the Gerald Lampert
Memorial Award and was nominated for the Governor General's Award and the
Dorothy Livesay Poetry Prize. She is also the author of Enemy of the
People, a chapbook of poems about the Soviet Union under Stalin. She lives
in Vancouver and holds an MFA from UBC.

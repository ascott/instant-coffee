(UN)PROMISED LAND

A VISUAL STORY BY KRISS MUNSYA

June 10 " July 5, 2024

Reception : Thursday, June 13, 6:30 " 8:30pm

(UN)PROMISED LAND is the combination of two bodies of work : Genetic Bomb and
Killing da Vinci. Genetic Bomb was shot in 2022 in Congo
(DRC); Killing da Vinci
was shot in 2023 in Brazil, Colombia, Puerto Rico, New Orleans and Detroit. Genetic
Bomb is based on Munsya's return to his homeland, and his experiences as a child
of the diaspora. Killing da Vinci is a story about environmental racism in the
Americas.

Although the visual codes and the locations of these two stories are different, they
are deeply rooted in the same source; colonialism. One is a personal experience,
where the other speaks about the collective aftermath.

(UN)PROMISED LAND, refers to a world wherein the systemic exploitation of people
and land leads to a situation in which colonized peoples are limited in their
unrestricted use, experience, and enjoyment of their own land. The democratic
promise of freedom has never been respected for the most marginalized.

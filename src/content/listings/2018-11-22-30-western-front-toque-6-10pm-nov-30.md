TOQUE is Western Fronts largest annual community event and fundraiser showcasing 
a curated selection of Vancouver-based designers and artists. Shop for locally made 
products ranging from bags, bowls, wallets and books, to ornaments, jewellery, skin 
care products, candles, teas and so much more. Look out for this year's limited-edition 
tea towel designed by Kim Kennedy Austin available at the event. Partial proceeds 
support Western Front's year-round art and music programs. For the latest vendor line 
up, visit our event Tumblr.

TOQUE dates/times:

Friday, November 30, 6-10pm
Saturday, December 1, 11am-5pm
Sunday, December 2, 11am-5pm

Admission is by donation. 

Our Holiday Membership Drive is on now!
Join or renew before or at Toque and you'll have the chance to win incredible prizes. 
Basic membership begins at $40 and benefits include free access to music workshops, 
discounts on Western Front concerts and publications, and an invitation to members' 
events. Visit our website for details and to join!

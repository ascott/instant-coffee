Reception with face painting and artist talk
Master and Apprentice, Lobster and Leaf
Saturday, November 12th 2:00 - 4:00pm

Join us on Saturday for a reception in Scott Kemp's exhibition, Master and 
Apprentice, Lobster and Leaf. The reception will run from 2:00 - 4:00pm, with FREE 
face painting following the subject matter of the exhibition being offered throughout. 
At 3:30pm artist Scott Kemp will give a talk discussing the work in the show. The 
exhibition will remain up until November 18th.

As part of Duplex's unofficial mandate we are asking exhibiting artists to organize an 
event (performance, screening, critique, etc.) in addition to, and separate from, an 
exhibition's opening reception. We ask this in order to encourage critical discourse
because we register its lack, because we value its presence.

We appreciate your support!

No Reading After the Internet is a monthly opportunity to gather and read a
text aloud in hopes that it might provoke theoretical illumination on
particular art works, or the broader scape within which such work exists.
This program departs from Cineworks' Thought on Film series, conceived by
Cheyanne Turions. Whilst still very interested in cinema, the focus of this
incarnation is softened to accommodate the more broad (and ever expanding)
scope of media art.

The idea of a reading group isn't new. No Reading nonetheless poses itself
as an experimental learning and discussion space. Simply put, we are
suspicious of our own reading abilities, and the extent to which our
readings are conversant with one another. No Reading means to offer a slow
space within which to retrace our steps in the hopes of discovering
individual and collective ways through the realms of language and
interpretation. The strategies we have at our disposal are twofold: through
the yoking of our discussion to a text; and inducing conversation, where
possible, between text and specific, local, contemporaneous art discussions
and happenings.

Participation in No Reading After the Internet is free and open to
everyone, regardless of his or her familiarity with a text or its author.
Texts will be handed out at the gathering. No pre-reading or research is
required. Those interested in acquiring the work in advance can contact 
traffic@videoout.ca

07. Surrey Urban Screen | Fiction Facade: Urban Visuals | SEP 30

Fiction Facade
Urban Visuals (Konstantinos Mavromichalis & Nathan Whitford)
August 26 to November 13
Opening Reception: Friday, September 30 7:30 pm

Fiction Facade is a digital animation artwork activated by your presence. Its 
generative sequences recall early arcade games with abstract geometric 
shapes and patterns responding to the architectural features of the west wall of 
the Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre. As a 100 foot wide architectonic work, it 
engages with the physical attributes of the building, drawing attention to the 
interaction of digital media and architectural form. 

A soundscape informed by console-based 8 bit video games and responsive to 
the projected virtual objects seen in the animation and the physical objects such 
as the windows and borders of the building accompanies Fiction Facade. 
Viewers can tune into FM 89.9 on their MP3 player or car stereo when in front of 
Surrey Urban Screen.

Through the principle medium of light, Urban Visuals (Konstantinos 
Mavromichalis & Nathan Whitford) specialize in creating immersive 
environments, from permanent public artworks to event-based installations, 
projected stage design and exhibition design. 

About the Event

This is a joint opening reception with Surrey Public Art unveiling of Lightmodal. 
As a zero energy, environment responsive installation, Lightmodal responds to 
the surrounding motion, vibration, and sound of pedestrian and vehicular traffic 
with light patterns inspired by naturally occurring light phenomena like auroras 
and bioluminescence. A Blue Bus will shuttle visitors from Chuck Bailey 
Recreation Centre to view Lightmodal on King George Boulevard. 

The artists will be in attendance, and there will be refreshments and popcorn.

Affordable Self-Directed Artist-in-Residence Program - perfect for visual artists, writers,
researchers and creatives. Due to Covid-19 we are currently only accepting applications
from within Canada.

In the north Okanagan Valley at the Caetani Cultural Centre (a historic heritage house) in
the small town of Vernon, BC, Canada

CURRENT + UPCOMING AVAILABILITY:
Caetani Room (Large):
Nov 30, 2020 - March 19, 2021
April 5 , 2021 - July 2, 2021
July 19, 2021 - onwards
Sermoneta Room (Medium):
November 16, 2020 - onwards into 2021
Fogliano Room (small):
December 2, 2020 - onwards into 2021
DATES: ongoing availability (minimum 2 weeks), please check on line for availability
RATES: between $10-$30 CAD/night depending on room + season
Private Studio rate @ $5 day
APPLICATION DEADLINE: ongoing

WHO IS ELIGIBLE: Open to emerging and established artists, writers and creatives (of all
disciplines)

INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY:
Live/work spaces @ reasonable fee (between $10-$30 CAD/night depending on room +
season)
Artists stay in their own private room with shared accommodations (kitchen, bathroom,
living room, entryway) at the Caetani Cultural Centre (a heritage house walking distance
of downtown Vernon.)
Live/work spaces are suitable for writers and digital artists.
Separate private studios are extra @ $6/day.
One additional occupant (partners) can stay in Caetani and Sermoneta rooms only @10
/day (Queen sized beds)
If desired, artists staying for 4 weeks or longer are offered the opportunity to teach a
workshop and/or host a reading/presentation/exhibition etc., at no cost to them.

NOT INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: travel expenses, food, health insurance, art
materials, shipping costs, exhibition costs and other personal expenses.

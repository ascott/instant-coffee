Art Show: Sky Cathedrals & Other Stories art show by Marion Webber.
Location: Visual Space Gallery, 3352 Dunbar St. (@ 17th Ave.), Vancouver, BC,
Canada
Exhibition Dates: Sept. 11 - 21, 2020, Noon - 6pm daily. Or, book an
appointment.
No formal reception. Social distancing required. Masks are optional but advised.

Art and Tenacity: Vancouver Art Show Inspires Survival
Through the Presentation of Beauty.

Vancouver, British Columbia, August 31, 2020 - Landscape artist, Marion
Webber will be hosting an
art show that portrays beauty in nature as the source
of physical and psychological survival. The show is entitled Sky Cathedrals &
Other Stories. It will be hosted at the Visual Space Gallery in the Dunbar area of
Vancouver from Sept. 11-21, 2020. Tenacity is the key to survival especially
during these challenging times of COVID-19.

Marions goal is to present beauty in art as a tool for personal catharsis and
communication to others needing solace and contentment. The show will
highlight oil paintings from various series including: Sky Cathedrals, Avenue
Walk, Into the Other Land and Traditional.

Marions research on the health benefits of art and nature are echoed in her
personal and painting experiences. One source is that of Dr. Dennis Dutton who
wrote the book, The Art Instinct on the natural inclination for humans to create
art. He also hosted a Ted talk entitled A Darwinian Theory of Beauty. He found
that artistic endeavor came long before formal spoken language and writing.
Humans are hard-wired to use physical expression to
communicate thoughts and feelings. Beauty is a survival mechanism for humans and
other species. We are attracted to those natural motifs that create a sense of calm
assurance for survival. Thats why we feel good around beauty. Recurrent imagery like
bright skies, pathways, ponds, mountains, forests and fields are used in paintings in
many cultures throughout history.

Susanna Stem, president of the Chali-Rosso Gallery comments: Marion paints
landscapes like many other artists, but hers are unique. Her paintings capture
the energy of nature and provoke wonderful feelings.
She transmutes her personal stories through the paint.

Marion paints broad vistas and intimate spaces. Her intent is to often look for
ordinary motifs that may be easily ignored and turn them into complex engaging
paintings. Motifs may be bushes, shadows, sunlit branches, trees, grasses, etc.
She notes: My interest is to notice the ordinary and make it
extraordinary. Too
often we overlook the intricacy of nature opting for stimulation only from dramatic
viewpoints. The ordinary complexity of nature sustains the network of life and
also offers exciting mysteries for artistic expression.

About the Artist:
Marion has lived and painted in Vancouver for over 30 years. She has degrees in
fine arts and graphics/advertising. She has lectured and taught art courses in
Vancouver since 2008. Her work is collected by corporations and patrons across
North America and Europe. Her website: http:www.mwebberart.com

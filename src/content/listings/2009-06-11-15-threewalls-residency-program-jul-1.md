threewalls thematic residency is a new incarnation of the residency program that shaped what 
threewalls is today. Originally designed to bring emerging professional artists to Chicago to live, 
research, make work and network in this dynamic community, threewalls redesigned the program 
for 2009 to bring 4 residents to town, curated around shared themes evident from the application 
pool. These residents are given a $1000 honorarium and living and studio space around Chicago 
by partnering with other arts organizations. Artists participate in a group exhibition and 
symposium as well as being invited to host ad hoc performances or projects during their stay. The
residency is ideal for developing new work, networking with artists from around the world and the
Midwest and participating in Chicago’s multi-faceted arts community. Residencies are 10 weeks 
long.

We are currently accepting applications for the 2010 residency, to be held in partnership with the 
Working Studio, an exhibition and studio site at the Sullivan Galleries at The School of the Art 
Institute of Chicago. Working Studio, part of the Studio Chicago series of programs, brings 
together the unique space of production (the studio) and the space of exhibition and display 
(galleries). This project will remake the vast exhibition spaces comprising SAIC’s Sullivan Galleries 
into working studios. Studios will be given to up to 20 artists representing a range of practices
and career stages. A modest scale meta-gallery built within the space with host a series of 
rotating projects related to the expanded idea of studio. A unique project and opportunity, our 
regular thematic residency program will happen at the Sullivan Gallery site, offering our residents 
an expanded circle of colleagues and exposure as part of the year long, multi-institution Studio 
Chicago project that includes the Museum of Contemporary Art, The School of the Art Institute of 
Chicago, Gallery 400, The City of Chicago Department of Cultural Affairs, DePaul University, Hyde
Park Art Center and threewalls.

Applicants will be curated by threewalls Director and Curator, Shannon Stratton in conjunction 
with Mary Jane Jacobs, Executive Director of Exhibitions, SAIC and artist and Picturing the Studio 
co-curator, Michelle Grabner

For program and application form, please visit this link:
www.three-walls.org/programs/threewallsresidencies/

All applications are due online or postmarked, July 1st, 2009.

We look forward to seeing your work!

threewalls is a nonprofit 501(c)3 organization dedicated to increasing Chicago’s cultural capital by 
cultivating contemporary art practice and discourse, and by creating a locus of exchange 
between local, national and international contemporary art communities through a range of 
programs: www.three-walls.org.

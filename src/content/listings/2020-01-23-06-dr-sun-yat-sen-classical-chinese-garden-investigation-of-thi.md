Investigation of Things: 1st Edition of Canada-China Exchange Exhibition for Young
Emerging Artists

Exhibition dates: January
23 - March 21, 2020
Opening Reception: January 22 5-7pm, 2020
Venue: Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden, Vancouver, Canada
Academic Host: Yang Xiaoyan
Curators: Lam Wong, Steven Dragonn

Collaborated with Shang Foundation for Art and Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden,
Canton-sardine invites you to celebrate the opening of Investigation of Things, the first
edition of the Canada-China Art Exchange Exhibition for Young Emerging Artists, and
second art exhibition by Artist-in-Residence, Lam Wong.

For this multimedia art exhibit Lam is joined by co-curator Steven Dragonn " independent
curator, founder of Canton-Sardine and instructor at the Guangzhou Academy of Fine Arts"
to showcase the artwork of 10 young, emerging artists from China and Canada. Nominated
by an esteemed group of experts in the art community, the artists work explores how we
understand the world around us, and aims to open cultural dialogues between the East and
the West through the evocative language of
contemporary visual art.

Hosted in collaboration with the Shang Foundation for Art, this exhibition will be showing in
the Garden Jan 23 - Mar 21, 2020

The event is open to the public by donation. Please enter via side entrance on Carrall
Street.

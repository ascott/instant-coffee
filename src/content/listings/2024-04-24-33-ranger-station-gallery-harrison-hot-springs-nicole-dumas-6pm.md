Nicole Dumas
Saturday, May 4, 2024
Doors open at 6pm for catered appetizers and mingling
Talk from 6:30-7:30 pm
First come, first seated
Free public event

The Kent-Harrison Arts Council is excited to present an artist talk with Nicole Dumas,
our current Artist-in-Residence. The talk marks the final weekend of Dumas' show
"Welcome to Lonely Lake: Best of all possible towns" and the nearing of the end of
her year-long residency. The talk will happen within her installation and focus on this
particular body of work as well as be a reflection of time as our AIR.

We acknowledge we are on the unceded territory of the Sts'ailes people. The gallery
has one accessible parking spot that is directly in front of a ramp that leads to the
front door, which is automatic. There are stairs leading from the gallery to a single-
stall washroom. To avoid the stairs, the washroom can be accessed through a side
door, directly off
the parking lot. The majority of food will be vegan, with some gluten
free options.

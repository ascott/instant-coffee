Mar 24 - Apr 2

A contemplative journey for this time of many passings and many becomings. A sacrifice
for the good of one and the good of all. Come light a candle. Migration is a collaboration
between Fight With a Stick and Tomoyo Ihaya " inspired by Tomoyo's visual art and her
ongoing series, Eyes Water Fire, which is devoted to those who have made the ultimate
sacrifice for freedom. In Migration the two-dimensional is brought to life in three-
dimensional space. One of Tomoyos main media is gampi paper (made from white
mulberry bush). In keeping with Alex
and Fight With a Sticks ongoing conscious
collaboration with other-than-human materialities, gampi paper is one of the key
performers in Migration.

Leave Room For the Holy Spirit

Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design Off-Site Graduation Exhibition
Class 0f 2008

Friday, May 9 to Saturday, May 17, 2008
Opening Reception: Thursday, May 8 at 8:00 pm

Nayeob Kim, David Mook, Robyn Croft, Scott Moore, Melanie Coles, Barry Meldrum, Christopher Olson, Tiziana La Melia, Julianne Claire, Rainbow Friberg, Anna Szaflarski, Allison Tweedie, Sarah Hoemberg, Deirdre McAdams, Johnny Burgess, Rebecca La Marre, Samuel Rudolf, Zarah Ackerman, Tegan Moore, Tondela Myles, Susan Kang, Ian Wyatt, Aja Rob


Nuns with ruler in hand, supervising teenage slow-dancers, separate their bodies with the stern reminder to “leave room for the holy spirit!” Like a teeny bopper’s slow-dance the works in this show share a quick moment of closeness before the measure between them breaks. As new grads blossom, nerves, sweat and excitement mix with post-productive shock. Upon our awkward separation the work makes room for a whole new spirit.

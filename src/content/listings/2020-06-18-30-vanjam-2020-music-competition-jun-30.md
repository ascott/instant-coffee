CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS! (DEADLINE JUNE 30)

Ready to play for the "world stage"? VANJAM is Vancouver's first virtual battle-of-
the-bands style music competition with an all-international judging panel.

INDEPENDENT MUSICIANS, GLOBAL JUDGES
Our mission is to show the rest of the world what makes Vancouver's
independent music scene so special - and have a little fun while doing it! If we
can get Vancouver on the map for its
thriving music culture, everyone in the
industry will benefit from the results.

We have partnered with industry experts from USA, France, The Netherlands,
UK, Israel, Brazil, India, and Japan to judge the event, and act as your mentors
as you progress in the competition. Find out who's on the Global Judges Panel:
https://yvarts.ca/vanjam-2020-judges

PRIZES:
1st place - $2000 value including 3 songs record/mix/master
2nd place - $1000 value including 1 song record/mix/master
3rd place - $500 value including 1 song record/mix/master
Audience Choice Award - world wide votes!

HOW TO ENTER
All you need to do is upload a video of your live performance from the past 12
months. For the complete rules & submission page visit https://yvarts.ca/vanjam

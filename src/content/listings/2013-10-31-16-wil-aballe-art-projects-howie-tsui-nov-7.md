Wil Aballe Art Projects is proud to present a solo exhibition with Vancouver-
based artist Howie Tsui. This will be the first solo show for the artist since his 
acclaimed presentation at Centre A in 2011. The centrepiece of the show is an 
epic 48x48" acrylic on canvas painting entitled Celestials of Gold Mountain.

Celestials of Gold Mountain is an attempt to destabilize prevailing historical 
portrayals of early Chinese immigrants from nondescript, victimized and hapless 
labourers into that of mythic beings, referencing ancient cosmology and 
bestiaries. Conversely, these exaggerated otherworldly depictions may further 
perpetuate the view of this ethnic group as a monstrous and mysterious other.

Please join us for the opening on Thursday, November 7th, from 7-9 p.m. The 
show runs until the last Saturday of the month.

The Vancouver Art Gallery offers a Described Tour of The Metamorphosis

Saturday, February 2, 2019 at 11am

Join a described tour for blind and partially sighted visitors of this exhibition drawing 
from the Gallerys collection that looks at transformation and change. Inspired by Franz 
Kafkas 1915 novella The Metamorphosis, the literary classic of a man who one day 
finds himself with the body of a giant insect, this contemporary exhibition looks at 
transformation through physical, spiritual and cultural realms. Discover installation 
works, film, photography and sculpture by local and international artists: Sonny Assu, 
Beau Dick, Lyse Lemieux, Marianne Nicolson, Skeena Reece, Fiona Tan, Wang 
Jianwei, among others.

Registration is required as capacity is limited. Please contact Donna with VocalEye to 
register by e-mail: buddies@vocaleye.ca

VocalEyes Sighted Guides are available to meet adults with vision loss (18 years of age 
and over) at the Burrard Sky Train station and accompany them to and from the Gallery. 
To arrange a Sighted Guide meet-up, please contact Donna at least three days in 
advance: buddies@vocaleye.ca

The Vancouver Art Gallery is wheelchair accessible. Street-level access is available 
through our Hornby and Robson street entrances, open during all public hours. 
Wheelchairs are available to visitors on a first-come first-served basis or may be 
reserved in advance of your visit by calling 604-662-4700.

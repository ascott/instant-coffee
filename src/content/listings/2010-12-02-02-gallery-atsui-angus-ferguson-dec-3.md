Gallery Atsui is proud to present We Have All The Time In the World part
IV. New works by Vancouver based artist Angus Ferguson.

Please join us for the opening this Friday December 3rd from 8pm onward.
Gallery Atsui is located at 602 Hastings Street East.

For his first solo exhibition at Gallery Atsui, Angus Ferguson presents one
chapter of his on-going project identifying the paintersâ€™ studio as a kind
of abject special effects workshop. Haphazardly pulling from inspirations
as disparate as Albrecht Durer, Carl Sagan, Mary Shelley and Jim Henson,
Ferguson equates painterly gesture with mutation, his wild strokes and
smudges of colour articulate alien tendrils, contrapposto exoskeletons and
ectoplasmic hairstyles. By indulging his impulse to accumulate, transpose
and metamorphosize a teeming abundance of source imagery, Fergusons
microcosmic studio practice becomes a venue for intergalactic ponderance
and empyreal ennui.

Born in Vancouver, Angus Ferguson received his BFA from Emily Carr
Institute of Art + Design in 2002. His work has appeared in group
exhibitions at Charles H. Scott Galley, Belkin Satellite Gallery, Bodgers
and Kludgers Co-operative Art Parlour and more. Solo shows include Blanket
Gallery, and Douglas Udell Gallery in Vancouver. In 2007 he was shortlisted
for the RBC Painting Competition.

The artist will be in attendance.

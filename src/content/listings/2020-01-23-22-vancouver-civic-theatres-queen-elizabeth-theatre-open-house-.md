Open house https://vancouvercivictheatres.com/events/vct-visual-arts-gallery-open-
house-jan-28-2020/

In the Queen Elizabeth Theatre lobbies from 5pm to 8pm

Vancouver Civic Theatres is proud to present a visual arts gallery open house with
artwork by Tatjana Mirkov-Popovicki and Pierre Vassura.

Tatjana Mirkov-Popovicki - Land
Love Unfolding
Loving a good land is easy, but finding a way to truly belong in it is a unique challenge
for every newcomer. The current phase of her journey deals with responsibility. At first
glance, the natural beauty of Western Canada is celebrated. Still in awe of what it says
to her, a new question arises these days: Can these places be saved?

Artist Talk at 6:00pm by Tatjana Mirkov-Popovicki

Pierre Vassura - Polychromatic Analogies: childhood theatrical and literary memories
Recreating and rearranging the memory, bringing it to the present and pushing it to the
future, the trunk of memories opens and brings back to life the popular fables of La
Fontaine. The ant and the cicada, the frog and the ox, the lion and the mouse perform in
a game of human comedy. Stilled in their movement, they give back to
observers the
naive freshness and strength of a practical and traditional art full of wisdom.

The gallery open house also includes work from the Vancouver Community College
Jewellery Art and Design Program.

A free all ages event from 5:00pm to 8:00pm in the lobbies of the Queen Elizabeth
Theatre with a no-host bar.

Shrine Building Project Commemorating lives shaped by borders
https://vancouvercivictheatres.com/events/shrine-building-project-jan-28-feb-1-2020/

Electric Company Theatre, Playwrights Theatre Centre and Vancouver Civic Theatres
are hosting a community-based shrine-building project to honour the undocumented
migrants of the Americas and beyond, and to remember those lost on their journey. Two
shrine creation workshops with Mountain View Cemetery Artist in Residence Marina
Szjiarto will be offered free to the public, with shrine building activities offered before
each performance of Carmen Aguirres new play Anywhere But Here.
In the Queen Elizabeth Theatre salons -
accessible - free to the public, all ages.

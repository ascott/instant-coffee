Gallery Atsui
602 East Hastings, Vancouver BC

"The Evolution of Inspiration"

"If history had worked out differently, and Michelangelo had been commissioned to paint a ceiling
for a giant Museum of Science, mightn't he have produced something at least as inspirational as 
the Sistine Chapel?"
Richard Dawkins, The God Delusion

With Dr. Dawkins' questions in mind and with this the bicentennial of Charles Darwin's birth, as 
well as the 150th anniversary of the publication of his seminal work On the Origin of Species, 
Gallery Atsui endeavors to explore and exhibit contemporary works that are "science inspired."

Submissions deemed to be or interpreted as being overtly anti-religious will not be considered. In 
keeping with Gallery Atsui's mandate, works that have been shown locally are discouraged, but 
may be considered on a piece-by-piece basis. Please include a bio, brief CV and images of works 
to be considered, in a single PDF. Direct links to online galleries are acceptable, but please 
clearly identify the works to be considered. An artist's statement is encouraged though not 
necessary.

Submissions can be emailed to tnickel@galleryatsui.com.

Any questions should be directed to Todd Nickel, Co-Director, Gallery Atsui by email.

Deadline - due to space constraints, we will select artists' work on a rolling basis, with the final 
deadline for submissions Sept 15, 2009.

Pleased to announce Abegael Jackson's first solo exhibition, ONE,
which aims to introduce the viewer to pure emotion in the form of
colour, texture and viscosity. Each painting, filled with raw powerful
feelings, can exist only in that ONE moment it was created; only in that
ONE fleeting moment; only when the artist existed in that unequivocal
arduous condition.

Preferring to interfere as little as possible with her medium, Abegael
Jackson spews intensity onto the canvas, chromatically etching that
ONE moment with permanence. Self taught, Abegael began as an
outsider artist and transformed into an intuitive painter who’s work
resembles a cross-over of action painting, lyrical abstraction, and
abstract expressionism.

This exhibition is not only a first viewing of Abegael's work, it is also a
celebration and a demonstration of what community can accomplish:
Injured, unemployed and living on social assistance Abegael began
painting whilst living on Hastings in a SRO (hotel). Through art and the
services in the DTES Abegael healed, grew stronger, and soared
above and beyond the limits she faced. This show is a thank you and a
goodbye to Vancouver, as she begins a new career in Victoria.
ArtStarts, CACV, Carnegie, and VNHA supported Abegael emotionally,
financially, and by providing art materials. It is with great pride that
Abegael thanks these institutions for all they did and at Saturday's
show, 20% of any proceeds will be donated to the Carnegie kitchen.

You are invited to join friends and family at the Gam Gallery this
Saturday to view the collection, which was partly funded by Vancouver
Foundation’s DTES Small Arts Grants. Abegael will be live painting
10am-6pm and will be conducting an artist talk at 3pm.

If you have little ones, bring their paint clothes and they are welcome to
play.

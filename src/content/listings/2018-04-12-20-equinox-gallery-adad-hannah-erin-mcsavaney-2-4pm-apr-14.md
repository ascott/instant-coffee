ADAD HANNAH: NEW ARRANGEMENTS
April 14 - May 19, 2018

The act of looking and how we make sense of the visual clues in a work of art has 
always been central to Adad Hannahs practice. This exhibition builds on Hannahs 
interest in the human form and the implementation of illusory visual systems as a 
way to simultaneously explore stillness and movement in video and photography. 
Through camouflage and optical illusion and the use of props as punctuation, 
Hannah is able to control the concealment and revelation of the body, drawing 
attention to how we perceive movement and how that perception changes as we 
move around an artwork.

Adad Hannah received his BFA from the Emily Carr University of Art + Design, and 
his MFA and PhD from Concordia University in Montreal. Hannah has exhibited 
widely across Canada and internationally, including exhibitions at the Samsung 
LEEUM Museum (Seoul), Prague Biennial, Museo de Bellas Arts (Santiago), 
National Museum of Contemporary Art (Bucharest), Whitechapel Gallery (UK), 
Zendai MoMA (Shanghai), National Gallery of Canada (Ottawa), Koffler Centre of 
the Arts (Toronto), and Ikon Gallery (Birmingham). Adad Hannah lives and works in 
Vancouver, BC.

ERIN MCSAVANEY: COUNTERPOINT
April 14 - May 19, 2018

McSavaneys new series is undergirded by a desire to bring together domains 
usually considered distinct: figuration and abstraction, photography and painting, 
reality and fiction. McSavaney starts by photographing sites around the city" 
concrete walls, garage doors, backways to nondescript businesses"creating 
compositions devoid of figures and contextualizing details. Typically, they juxtapose 
buildings with some type of plant life; these become his source material. McSavaney 
then carefully renders his subject matter in acrylic paint on canvas while adorning 
the once drab structures with bold patterns. The result is a hybrid image that boldly 
bridges the divide between photorealism and abstraction.

Erin McSavaney studied art and design at Emily Carr University of Art + Design and 
Capilano College. Since that time, he has exhibited across Canada, including solo 
exhibitions at Parts Gallery (ON), Evergreen Cultural Centre (BC), Michael Gibson 
Gallery (ON), Herringer Kiss Gallery (AB). McSavaneys work is included in the 
Senvest Collection, Colart Collection, and TD Art Collection. Erin McSavaney lives 
and works in Vancouver, BC.

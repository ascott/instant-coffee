Front Magazine is looking for visual art, design, writing and
collaborations that address silence for our winter issue, and the best of
whatever you're working on for our open themed spring issue.

Image-based work is welcome. Written works, both artistic and journalistic,
are usually shortish (500-1,000 words), but occasionally longer. Proposals
are welcome and assignments are sometimes available (please send a sample
of your writing).

Front Magazine is Vancouver's favourite 20-year-long cultural exploration.
It is published quarterly by the Western Front Society in Vancouver and
lovingly distributed free throughout the Lower Mainland, and at better
newsstands nationally. Read issues of Front Magazine for free on Zinio.com.

Silence - Winter 2010
Deadline: September 17, 2010
Display dates: December 2010 - February 2011

Open theme - Spring 2011
Deadline: December 17, 2010
Display dates: March - May 2011

Submissions can be emailed to frontmagazine@front.bc.ca. For full
submission guidelines, please see [http://front.bc.ca/frontmagazine/submissions](http://front.bc.ca/frontmagazine/submissions)

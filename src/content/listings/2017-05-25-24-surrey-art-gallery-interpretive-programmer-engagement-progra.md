Interpretive Programmer
Surrey Art Gallery

Are you passionate about art education for families, youth, and adults? Can you 
demonstrate knowledge of contemporary art?

Surrey Art Gallery is hiring an Interpretive Programmer (regular part-time position) 
tasked with carrying out a range of educational and administrative responsibilities for 
developing, planning, and implementing exhibition-related interpretive resources, 
programs, and events. Founded in 1975, and located in Metro Vancouver, the Gallery is 
a Class A public contemporary art museum that exhibits and supports access to the 
work of emerging through to established regional, national, and international artists. 
With the largest school district and second largest municipal population in the province, 
the Gallery provides diverse learning and engagement opportunities to serve art, artists, 
and audiences.

RESPONSIBILITIES & REQUIRED SKILLS
Join the Gallery staff team, and contribute to the innovation and growth of our institution 
through your contribution to recommending, researching, developing, scheduling, 
implementing, and evaluating exhibition-related initiatives including: Family Programs 
(Family Sundays, art parties, offsite programs); School Programs (K-12 tours, 
workshops, guides, resources, professional development opportunities for teachers at 
the Gallery and in classrooms and at conferences); casual visitor initiatives (resources, 
tours, workshops); and adult programs (artist talks, panels, symposia, events, 
residencies). You will work directly with the Curator of Education and Engagement, 
Volunteer Program Coordinator, Visual Arts Programmer, art educators and instructors, 
volunteers, Surrey Arts Centre staff, and community committees, as well as the Gallery 
Director/Manager of Visual and Community Arts, Curator of Exhibitions and Collections 
and curatorial staff, Communications Coordinator, City staff, and contract artists, 
speakers, and designers. You will additionally assist with grant writing and facility 
operations as it relates to your area of work.

We are seeking a creative individual who enjoys working in a dynamic environment and 
thrives in situations requiring meeting deadlines and adapting to change. Required skills 
include: strong knowledge and experience in art education theories and practices; broad 
knowledge of contemporary art and art history; excellent oral and written 
communication; solid administrative, organizational, interpersonal, and research skills; 
effective problem-solving skills; and the ability to work both independently and 
collaboratively within a team.

QUALIFICATIONS
The successful candidate will have a degree/diploma in the area of art museum 
education, art education, or a related field plus 2 years progressively responsible 
experience in developing, administrating, and presenting initiatives in this area of 
specialty, including supervision, or an equivalent acceptable combination of training and 
experience. Emergency First Aid with CPR certification and the ability to successfully 
pass a Police Information Check/Vulnerable Sector Check are required. A valid BC 
driver's license and ability for physical work with some heavy lifting are preferred.

This is a regular part-time, union position (Community Services Assistant 5 
classification) with a starting rate of $26.70/hour plus benefits. Hours are typically 
Monday to Friday up to 25 hours/week, including some hours scheduled in the evening 
and weekend.
Do you qualify and are interested?

For more information about employment with the City of Surrey visit: 
www.surrey.ca/careers.

Engagement Programmer Surrey Art Gallery

Are you passionate about connecting art, artists, and audiences? Can you demonstrate 
knowledge of contemporary art?

Surrey Art Gallery is hiring an Engagement Programmer (auxiliary part-time position) 
tasked with carrying out a range of educational and administrative responsibilities for 
developing, planning, and implementing engagement initiatives. Founded in 1975, and 
located in Metro Vancouver, the Gallery is a Class A public contemporary art museum 
that exhibits and supports access to the work of emerging through to established 
regional, national, and international artists. With the largest school district and second 
largest municipal population in the province, the Gallery provides diverse learning and 
engagement opportunities to serve art, artists, and audiences.

RESPONSIBILITIES & REQUIRED SKILLS
Join the Gallery staff team, and contribute to the innovation and growth of our institution 
through your contribution to recommending, researching, developing, scheduling, 
implementing, and evaluating engagement initiatives including: youth and young adult 
programs (Art Together and other pilot/new projects with mentoring artists, offsite art 
programs, youth digital art screening nights at UrbanScreen); Secondary School 
Programs (tours and workshops in the Gallery, special projects with mentoring artists); 
casual visitor initiatives (resources, tours, workshops); TechLab engagement projects 
(exhibition-related, special projects); and events related to community-based exhibitions 
(artmaking with artists and art educators, talks, panels, receptions, parties). You will 
work directly with the Curator of Education and Engagement, Interpretive Programmer, 
Volunteer Program Coordinator, Visual Arts Programmer, Engagement Assistant, art 
educators and instructors, volunteers, Surrey Arts Centre staff, and community 
committees, as well as the Gallery Director/Manager of Visual and Community Arts, 
Curator of Exhibitions and Collections and curatorial staff, Marketing Coordinator, City 
staff, and contract artists, speakers, and designers. You will additionally assist with grant 
writing and facility operations as it relates to your area of work.

We are seeking a creative individual who enjoys working in a dynamic environment and 
thrives in situations requiring meeting deadlines and adapting to change. Required skills 
include: strong knowledge and experience in art education theories and practices; broad 
knowledge of contemporary art and art history; excellent oral and written 
communication; solid administrative, organizational, interpersonal, and research skills; 
effective problem-solving skills; and the ability to work both independently and 
collaboratively within a team.

QUALIFICATIONS
The successful candidate will have a degree/diploma in the area of art museum 
education, art education, art history, curatorial studies, or a related field plus 2 years 
progressively responsible experience in developing, administrating, and presenting 
initiatives in this area of specialty, including supervision, or an equivalent acceptable 
combination of training and experience. Emergency First Aid with CPR certification and 
the ability to successfully pass a Police Information Check/Vulnerable Sector Check are 
required. A valid BC driver's license and ability for physical work with some heavy lifting 
are preferred.

This is an auxiliary part-time position (Community Services Assistant 5 classification) 
supported through grants with a starting rate of $26.70/hour plus 12% in lieu of benefits. 
Hours are typically Monday to Friday up to 21 hours/week, including some hours 
scheduled in the evening and weekend.

Do you qualify and are interested?

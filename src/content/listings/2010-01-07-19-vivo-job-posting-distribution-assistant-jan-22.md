JOB POSTING: DISTRIBUTION ASSISTANT
Application Deadline: January 22, 2010
Part-Time 14 hours/ week (Flexible Hrs) Wage: $14/h

Video Out Distribution, part of the VIVO (Video In/Video Out) Media Arts
Centre, is a non-profit distributor of video art and documentary works. We
maintain a collection of independently produced video artworks, which we
distribute to festivals, galleries, broadcasters and libraries
internationally. For more information on our work please visit our website
at www.videoout.ca.

The Distribution Assistant works closely with the Distribution Coordinator
and is primarily responsible for responding to general queries by artists
and exhibitors, open festival calls, administrative support for
acquisitions, title accessioning and basic financial transactions. The
Distribution Assistant carries out exhibitor and marketing research,
organizes special projects, and works extensively with email and database
software. The Distribution Assistant is also familiar with the day-to-day
running of Video Out and covers for the Distribution Coordinator when they
are off-site.

The ideal candidate will have strong verbal and written communication
skills, computer fluency and a capacity to juggle multiple tasks to
deadline using Mac OS, Word, FileMaker, and Excel. Understanding of
Photoshop, Illustrator, DVD Studio Pro and Final Cut Pro would be an asset.
This position also draws upon a strong understanding of the festival
programming process, video and media art in Canada, artist run culture and
VIVO's mandate.

Applications should outline an applicant's:

Familiarity with Video Art Distribution
Communication / Administrative Work Experience
Computer Skills and Work Experience

If you are interested in applying for this position, please email a resume
and cover letter by 5pm Friday, January 22 to:

Sharon Bradley
Coordinator - Video Out Distribution
info@videoout.ca

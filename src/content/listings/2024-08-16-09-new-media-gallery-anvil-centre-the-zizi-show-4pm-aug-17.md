For one evening only, in a North American debut, THE ZIZI SHOW. This unique,
interactive A.I. Drag Show, will be presented on a 14 x 26 foot screen, open to the
street for all to share. Choose performers from a select AI cast.

UK Artist Jake Elwes has created The Zizi Project and The Zizi Show, a deepfake,
virtual cabaret. Drag performers interpreted through AI ...perform captivating
moments of deepfake chaos and imperfection; queering the data. Zizi explores what
AI can teach us about drag, and what drag can teach us about A.I.

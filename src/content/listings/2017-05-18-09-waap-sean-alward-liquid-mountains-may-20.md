ARTIST TALK
Sat, May 20, 2017, 2 PM

SEAN ALWARD

Liquid Mountains

WAAP
688 East Hastings St. (lower level)
+1 778 229 3458
www.waapart.com
wil@waapart.com

Liquid Landscapes is a series of hybrid photograph-paintings investigating 
material metamorphosis and the fabrication of history along the Fraser River. The 
photographic components are cyanotypes (solar blueprints), which come from 
locations related to the transformation of the natural world into economic 
resources, including a former brick factory, an abandoned fur-trading post, and 
a salmon cannery. The painted components are composed of clay that comes 
from these same sites and references the forces of natural history and deep time 
in relation to human narrative.

SEAN ALWARD is a Vancouver based artist working in painting and 
photography. He received his BFA from the Nova Scotia College of Art and 
Design (exchange at Cooper Union, New York) and MFA from the University of 
British Columbia. Exhibition venues include Helen Pitt Gallery, Or Gallery, 304 
Days, Access Gallery, UBC AHVA Gallery, Richmond Art Gallery, Nanaimo Art 
Gallery, Surrey Art Gallery, and Wil Aballe Art Projects. Permanent Public Art 
installations include the Nova Scotia Forensic Hospital, New Westminster Skytrain 
Station, and Newton Recreation Centre. Writing published in Canadian Art, C 
Magazine, Border Crossings, Georgia Straight, and Art Margins.

I am interested in how painting and photography reconfigure space, how time is 
enacted within these spaces, and how history is formulated as a present 
experience: now.

Thursday, July 11
Positive Negative Gallery
7-11 PM

In "Take Me to Anywhere", Jaynus O'Donnell's paintings depict natural
phenomenon and ideas of place that have been abstracted and embellished by
recollection. This is the artist's first solo exhibition in Vancouver.

Jaynus O'Donnell is an artist and former Vancouverite now living and
working in Montreal.

Jeff Ladouceur: Pearl Path
June 15-August 19, 2018
Opening Reception: Thursday, June 14, 7-9pm featuring music by Hot Towers

With his first solo exhibition with a Canadian art museum, New York-based
Canadian artist Jeff Ladouceur exhibits his contemporary drawings featuring
old-timey comic-inspired characters: droopy elephants, big-nosed salesmen,
and bubble babies rendered with a dizzying level of detail.

Opening Reception: Thursday, June 14, 7-9pm
Join us for the opening reception at the Burnaby Art Gallery. The evening
will feature opening remarks, followed by a reception with live music
featuring Hot Towers (Lee Hutzulak, Brady Marks, Matt Skillings, Brady
Cranfield): a beat-based synth quartet improvising with psychedelic
intensity, searching for transcendence through confusion.

The artist will be in attendance.

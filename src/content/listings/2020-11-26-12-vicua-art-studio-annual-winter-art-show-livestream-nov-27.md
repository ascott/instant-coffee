Event type: VIRTUAL OPENING

Live Stream Link: www.vicunaartstudio.com/winter-art-show

Date/Time: Friday, November 27th, 1:00PM - 1:45PM

This year, Vicua Art Studio will be virtually Live Streaming their annual Winter Art Show!
Guests can safely see beautiful artworks from the comfort of their homes. You will get a tour
of the exhibition, learn about the art works, and watch artists' demonstrations and
interviews.

The show will feature more than 50 artworks by Vicua artists. Beautiful paintings, inspiring
illustrations and creative sculptures will be on display for sale. They will make unique and
meaningful gifts for loved ones this Holiday Season. We hope you can make it!

Vicua Art Studio was opened in 2008 by Maria Daley. It is a studio devoted to supporting
artists with diverse disabilities to embrace their individuality, develop their talent and create
inspiring works. Currently Dhanha Lee, the director,
is continuously growing the studio. It is
owned and operated by the Ridge Meadows Association for Community Living in Maple
Ridge.

For more information, please visit our website or contact us.

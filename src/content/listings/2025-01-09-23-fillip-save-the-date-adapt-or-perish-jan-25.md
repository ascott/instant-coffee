Adapt or Perish: Twenty Years of Fillip

Saturday, January 25th, 2025 | 7"10PM

Hycroft Manor (1489 McRae Avenue, Vancouver, BC)

In 2004, a large group of artists and curators gathered over
several evenings to
discuss the need for an independent arts publication in Vancouver, and in 2005, the
first issue of Fillip magazine was launched. Over the last twenty years, Fillip has
published nearly fifty publications, commissioned over forty new artists works,
published nearly five hundred works of writing, and produced three large-scale public
symposia. Weve also hosted over two hundred free programs, including artists
talks, lectures, workshops, screenings, reading groups, seminars, and book
launches.

You are cordially invited to attend a reception in honour of Fillips twenty-year history.
We look forward to reconnecting with old friends and collaborators and reaching out
to new ones, bringing together our far-flung network for an evening of cheer. Over
the course of the evening, assemble with Fillips community while we mingle, share
stories, and celebrate Fillips contributions to contemporary art discourse over
libations and small bites.

On the night, we will launch a
special edition publication, the Image Index: 2005"
2024, and present a Library documenting our twenty-year history, with free gifts and
surprise guests! A commissioned sound work by Mnemonic Pulse will set the tone
for an evening of celebration and reflection in the elegant setting of Hycroft Manor.

Please join us in celebrating twenty years of Fillip. Heres to twenty more!

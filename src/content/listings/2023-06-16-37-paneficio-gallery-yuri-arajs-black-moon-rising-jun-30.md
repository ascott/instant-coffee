Yuri Arajs is a formally educated Canadian artist who works and lives in Vancouver BC.
He creates minimal landscapes focused on details of land and sky.

"I love spending time getting lost in the sky. There are so many places to go and things
to see up there.

Most of my art is landscape based in its simplest form. Horizon lines and objects filling
space with intention help me create works of absolute silence with an under current of
misunderstanding.

I use lots of vintage paper and book pages as well as found metal and scraps to
introduce the discussion of history and experience within the specific work. My colour
palette is fairly limited and my appreciation for graphite is huge." - Yuri Arajs

This exhibition features a series of new
and older works that have not been shown
before.

Old Growth Michael Nicoll Yahgulanass

Contemporary artist Michael Nicoll Yahgulanaas will be launching his 
new book Old Growth Thursday February 16, 7-9pm at the Rhizome 
Cafe in Vancouver. 

Michael Nicoll Yahgulanaas is a renowned contemporary artist who, 
gradually, during a thirty-year career as a political activist, began to 
apply classic Haida visual forms and narratives to current means of 
communication, including political cartoons and comics.

Old Growth chronicles over thirty years of Yahgulanaas' graphic works 
that document the history of political negotiations and cultural conflict 
off the Northwest Coast of Canada from the 1970s to the early 2000s. 
Containing studies and experiments, finished work previously 
unpublished, and works that few have seen, this book reflects on such 
urgent topics as depletion of natural resources, global climate change 
and the fundamental question of "how to live together."

This book was published though a partnership between grunt gallery 
and Read Leaf.

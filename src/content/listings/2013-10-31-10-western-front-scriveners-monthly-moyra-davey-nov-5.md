Scrivener's Monthly is pleased to host New York based photographer, writer and 
filmmaker Moyra Davey who will be screening her film Les Goddesses (2011) on 
Tuesday, November 5th at 8:00pm in the Grand Luxe Hall at the Western Front. 
The screening will be followed by a response and a Q and A with Vancouver 
based photographer Marian Penner Bancroft.

Davey will be in Vancouver to open her exhibition Ornament and Reproach at 
Presentation House Satellite Gallery and she will also give a talk at Emily Carr 
University.

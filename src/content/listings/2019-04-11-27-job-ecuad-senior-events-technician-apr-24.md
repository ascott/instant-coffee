Emily Carr University of Art and Design:
Senior Events Technician

Temporary Part-Time (ASAP to 16 August 2019)
(Possibility of additional hours and extension) 
1:00 pm to 9:00 pm Thursday + Friday / 8:00 am to 4:00 pm Saturday
Pay: $27.48 to $29.42 per hour

Reporting to the Manager, Collaborative Technologies + Media Resources, and under 
the day-to-day direction of the Collaborative Technologies Production Lead, the Senior 
Events Technician is responsible for supporting all technical aspects of events from 
lecture series to the full execution of complex productions. The Technician oversees 
multimedia equipment and systems within theatres, learning spaces, classrooms, and 
conference rooms, ensuring spaces are operational and maintained for supporting 
curriculum and events. Additionally, the Technician provides tier 1 support to students, 
staff, and faculty on operating and troubleshooting equipment problems. The Technician 
supervises, evaluates and directs casual Event Technicians and student workers.

TYPICAL DUTIES:

1 Under the day-to-day supervision of the Collaborative Technologies Production 
Lead, provides support for various events from lecture series to the full execution of 
complex productions. Ensures continuous service and works collaboratively with staff, 
faculty, external clients, Facilities, University Events and the Teaching & Learning Centre 
to successfully execute events, lectures and conferences. Consults with the 
Collaborative Technologies Production Lead regarding complex issues. 

2 Supervises, trains and directs casual Event Technicians and Student Workers. 
May participate in the hiring of casual Event Technicians. 

3 Ensures technical staff are well trained and event activities are handled in a safe 
and respectful manner. Supports a safe working and learning environment by ensuring 
staff are aware of, and adhering to, safety regulations and procedures. 

4 Oversees multimedia equipment and systems within theatres (i.e. Reliance 
Theatre), learning spaces, classrooms, and conference rooms, ensuring equipment and 
systems are fully operational for supporting curriculum and events. Troubleshoots and 
performs repairs, or consults with the Collaborative Technologies Production Lead and 
Audio-Visual (AV) Specialist on repair suitability and options when repairs are extensive. 
Works with Facilities Services on issues as required. 
5 Works as part of a team with CTS and IT Services providing tier 1 support by 
responding with advice to CTS Help Desk tickets. Troubleshoots, diagnoses and repairs 
AV systems, recording/streaming and videoconferencing equipment, and software 
application configurations. Ensures the University community is aware of changes and 
the status of repairs, and arranges for equipment substitutions.

Education/Experience

Two-year diploma in a media related field of audio, film/video, theatre 
technician, or audio-visual installation, plus one year specialized and/or ongoing 
relevant training.

Minimum two years related experience working with a wide variety of multi-
media and presentation equipment, preferably in a post-secondary environment.

Minimum two years working knowledge of touch screen and IP based AV 
control systems, conferencing, streaming, recording, lighting and sound systems.

Minimum two years working knowledge of Mac and Windows OS.

Minimum two years working knowledge of either Bi Amp, Crestron, Extron or 
ETC.

Or an equivalent combination of education and experience.

Supervisory experience.

Skills/Abilities

Ability to clearly communicate and demonstrate the use of multi-media 
equipment to students, staff, and faculty with widely varying technical backgrounds.

Sound knowledge of standard theatrical equipment and terminology. 

Working knowledge of presentation software (i.e. PowerPoint and Keynote).

Working knowledge of video file formats and bandwidth limitations.

Excellent organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills. Ability to 
solve problems with diplomacy and tact, and to remain calm in stressful situations.

Demonstrated ability to work independently, and as a member of a team.

Physical stamina and mobility to permit lifting, carrying, and moving heavy 
objects.

Supervisory skills.

FLEET: Edmonds

Edmonds Park, Intersection of Humphries Rd and Rosewood Ave

Sidi Chen Artist Talk April 13th at 1 PM at FLEET: Edmonds

We want to give a warm welcome to Sidi Chen, who will be activating the FLEET:
Edmonds studio during January and April 2025. Chens project at FLEET has two
parts; in the winter they will invite the public to contribute rain vessels for a sound
walk and recording project, and in the spring they will hold workshops creating
home-made chalk using eggshells for a community mapping project.

Sidi Chen (MFA) is a diasporic queer artist who inquires the relational aesthetics
through performance, media arts, and other interdisciplinary practices. Through their
research across arts, natural science, and community development, Chen imagines
alternative relations and perspectives with the world through mediations by bodily
mobility and empathy. Sidi Chen is currently working as a practicing artist and an
independent arts administrator on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territories
of the hn"q"min"m" and Swxw7mesh Snchim speaking peoples, known as
Burnaby, BC.

Kegan McFadden
Fifty-six four-letter names of men I met between 1981 and 2008, remembered 
in chronological sequence (approximately)
Opens on October 11 at 6pm
Closes on November 11 at 5pm

Kegan McFadden's exhibition takes a conceptual approach to traditional 
printmaking techniques, offering an exhibition fueled less by skill than by 
culturally informed research. He is interested in using traditional silkscreen 
and new digital printmaking techniques to discuss how relationships, 
particularly between men, may be described and subsequently historicized in 
contemporary cultural contexts. The silkscreened prints in this exhibition 
contain a list of fifty-six names of men that he has known. The process of 
printmaking is akin to memory in that we print to create a record, in this case 
of individuals who he has met. However, the process is noticeably flawed in 
his unskilled technique, resulting in a varied edition that both offers and 
obscures information. The list of fifty-six names on some of the prints is 
completely legible, while other prints are missing letters or full names all 
together.

Malaspina Printmakers is a nonprofit artist-run centre that supports the 
development of printmaking as a contemporary art form and promotes and 
preserves traditional print practice. This exhibition is made possible through 
the generous support of the Canada Council for the Arts, the Province of 
British Columbia through the BC Arts Council and the Gaming Policy and 
Enforcement Branch, the City of Vancouver, and our donors.

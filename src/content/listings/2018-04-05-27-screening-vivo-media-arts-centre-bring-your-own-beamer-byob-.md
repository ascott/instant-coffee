VIVO Media Arts Centre presents the second edition of Bring Your Own Beamer 
(BYOB)! Friday April 13th from 8 - 11pm.

The event, conceived by Dutch-Brazilian artist Rafal Rozendaal, is a one-night 
screening where artists display their video or film work using their own projector. 
Formats of all varieties will be presented simultaneously, across the walls of VIVO's 
2,000 square foot studio space, backed by a crowd-sourced soundtrack.

Film, video, live visuals, projection mapping, overhead projectors, large format slides, 
looped and live! 

Tickets: $5 at the door * (no one turned away)

Individuals who are interested in projecting their work please email 
info@vivomediaarts.com to sign-up. Give us your name, and tell us about your film or 
video format of choice. Deadline is midnight on April 11th. 

**Set up will occur between 6-7:30 pm on the 13th, participates will receive 
complimentary entry and other perks.**

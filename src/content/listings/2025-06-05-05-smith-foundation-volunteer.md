achieve our mission. At the Gordon Smith Gallery, we are committed to offering
meaningful volunteer opportunities that reflect each individuals unique skills,
interests, needs, and backgrounds. Volunteers are welcomed as integral members
of our team and community.

High school students are encouraged to apply, and volunteer hours may count
toward their required service hours.

by the Smith Foundation.

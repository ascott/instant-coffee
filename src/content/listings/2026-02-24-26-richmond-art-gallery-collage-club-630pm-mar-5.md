MONTHLY PROMPT: COLOUR

Looking for a fun, inspiring night out? Join our monthly Collage Club for Adults-a relaxed,
social evening dedicated to art-making in a welcoming environment. No artistic experience
required, just bring your imagination!

This month's prompt is COLOUR, inspired by Richmond Art Gallery's The Chromophiliacs
exhibition.

We'll start with a quick slideshow of contemporary collage artists to get your ideas going, then
you'll create your own artwork using our wide selection of unique materials including a large
assortment of paper ephemera, fine art imagery, textiles, and more.

All tools for gluing or stitching are provided, along with tips for working with different
materials. A curated playlist of colour themed music videos adds atmosphere and extra
inspiration.

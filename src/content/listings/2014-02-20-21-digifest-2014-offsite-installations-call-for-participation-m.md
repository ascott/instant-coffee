Digifest 2014 Offsite Installations - Call for Participation

Digifest, Canadaâ€™s international festival celebrating digital creativity, is proud 
to announce a unique opportunity for galleries, artists and designers to 
participate in the first Digifest offsite exhibitions series. Projects that engage 
with digital culture will be featured, inspiring us to think about how technology 
shape our lives and our future.
Digifest 2014 runs May 8th to 10th, with the main festival located at the 
prestigious Corus Entertainment building on Toronto's waterfront. The festival 
will feature a series of international keynote lectures, panel discussions, 
hands-on workshops, and more. This year we are especially pleased to 
present Massimo Banzi, co-founder of the Arduino micro-controller boards 
which has revolutionized open source making. A detailed list of speakers is 
available online at www.TorontoDigifest.ca.
Offsite projects will be open concurrently with the festival, and can open 
earlier than May 8th and run longer than May 10th. Projects can occur 
anywhere in the world but require some way for the project to be 
encountered at the festival. 
Impact:

+ Have your project promoted as a part of the festival (Print/Web)
+ Participate in a broad dialogue about digital culture
+ Select projects will be part of a gallery crawl on the evening of May 9th
Theme: Digital Urbanism

Digifest 2014's keynote speakers address the theme of Digital Urbanism 
through the lenses of open source making, interaction design for 
hardware/software interfaces, and the impact of technology on the physical 
design of the city.

Offsite projects will build on the theme of Digital Urbanism, exploring how 
new technologies disrupt existing social realities. Technological innovation 
dislodges existing ways that human's fulfill their needs and wants. It makes 
familiar tools obsolete and transforms our patterns of behaviour. 
Digifest seeks to celebrate the best in digital creativity. We are seeking a 
diversity of perspectives, working methods, and projects and will present a 
range of them. 

We invite you to investigate what it means to live in 2014, where our physical 
experience of the world is perpetually blended with digital assets.

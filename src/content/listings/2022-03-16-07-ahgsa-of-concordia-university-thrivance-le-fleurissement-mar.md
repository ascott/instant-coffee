Thrivance | Le Fleurissement

The Art History Graduate Student Association (AHGSA) of Concordia University
(Montreal/Tiohti:ke/Mooniyang, unceded Kanienkeh:ka traditional territory) is
pleased to announce the 16th annual graduate symposium, which will take place
through a hybrid format on April 22-24, 2022

AHGSA is seeking proposals that critically and creatively examine the role of thrivance
as related to art history and art practice through critical, historical, and or/theoretically-
informed analysis. AHGSA welcomes proposals from artists, research-creators,
historians, writers, and emerging scholars across all disciplines. Applicants are invited
to submit a proposal for a paper, artist talk, workshop,
panel session, performance, or
alternative form of presentation that addresses this theme creatively. We are open to
proposals of any nature and you are welcome to contact us with any questions
regarding your submission.

Call for proposals due: March 18, 2022

Email submissions to: ahgsaconference@gmail.com
Details and a bibliography (in English and French) can be found in the attached call
For more information on AHGSA: https://www.facebook.com/ahgsaconcordia

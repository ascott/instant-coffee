(re)patterning - a two-person exhibition by Amanda Wood and Val Loewen
June 20 - 28, 2025

THIS Gallery | New location!
108 East Broadway (alley)
Vancouver, BC
thethisgallery.com

THIS Gallery is thrilled to announce its grand reopening at a brand new location with
(re)patterning, a collaborative exhibition by Vancouver-based artists Amanda Wood
and Val Loewen. Marking the gallerys first exhibition at its new location at the former
Peanuts Gallery, (re)patterning runs from June 20 to 28, 2025.

The gallery is open and free to the public Fridays and Saturdays, 12 - 5.

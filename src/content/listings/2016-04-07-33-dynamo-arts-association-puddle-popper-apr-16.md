Puddle Popper

Opening: April 16th 7pm - 11pm

Dynamo Arts Association
30 EAST 6TH AVE VANCOUVER, BC

Featuring works by:
Sarah Davidson
Juli Majer
Melanie Thibodeau
Sonja Ratkay

+++

Caught nibbling on edges

Through a close and constant drawing practice, each of these artists have
an interest in translating immediate markings into the physical world.
Puddle Popper is the coming together of a collection of objects created and
arranged by all four artists.

---event includes the launch of a collaborative zine---

+++

Sarah Davidson is an artist based in Vancouver, Canada. Her work explores
fragments as symbols, medium as gendered and maps as they exist in lived time.

sarahdavidson.ca

Juli Majer is an interdisciplinary artist based in Vancouver, Canada. Juli
is interested in fractured storytelling/symbolism, and studies translations
of movements, emotion, and fantasy within her work.

julimajer.tumblr.com

Melanie Thibodeau is a Vancouver-based queer artist whose practice explores
the strangeness of having/living in a body. Other ongoing themes in their
work deal with nostalgia and loss; comfort/discomfort; BDSM; and politics
surrounding gender, sexuality, and feminism.
melaniethibodeau.com

Sonja Ratkay is an interdisciplinary artist, poet and researcher born and
based in Vancouver, BC, Canada. She primarily explores topics of
intuition, mood, states of being, and environmental triggers that lead to
psychological transformation.

sonjaratkay.com

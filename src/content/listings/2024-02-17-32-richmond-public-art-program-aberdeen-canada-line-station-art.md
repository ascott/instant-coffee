The Richmond Public Art Program and Richmond Art Gallery invite artists and artist
collectives/groups to submit proposals for a temporary public art installation at
Aberdeen Canada Line Station comprised of two large-scale images flanking the main
entrance at street level.

Artist Fee: $1,500.00

Submission Deadline: March 4, 2024

Eligibility: Open to Professional Artists

Exhibition Duration: May 2024 - March 2025

The Emerging Digital Artists Award (EDAA) is currently open for
applications from early-career, Canadian artists who are working on-screen,
and experimenting with the possibilities of digitally rendered imagery.
Applicants are encouraged to play with moving image formats such as video,
animation, and GIF art.

The deadline to apply for the award is September 15, 2016.

Learn more about the EDAA and how to apply on our website:
http://edaa.equitablebank.ca/.

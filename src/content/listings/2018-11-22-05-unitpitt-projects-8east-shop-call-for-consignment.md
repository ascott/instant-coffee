UNIT/PITT is opening an artists retail area in time for the holidays in our new location at 
8 East Pender and are putting out a call for consignments.
We're looking for: shirts and other garment, totes, poster editions, (possibly) zines, and 
other ephemera. We're NOT looking for artist books; there are already plenty of lovely 
bookstores. 

To submit work for approval, and all other information regarding consignment terms, 
please contact Jamie at unitpitt.manager@gmail.com with the subject line: 
CONSIGNMENT INTEREST. All emails will be answered, although we reserve the right 
to refusal on items submitted for review.

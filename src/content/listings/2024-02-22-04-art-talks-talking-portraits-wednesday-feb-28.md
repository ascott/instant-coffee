Art Talks: Talking Portraits. Wednesday, February 21 and 28 from 7-9 pm. The Kent
Harrison Arts Council is hosting a free discussion group about portraits with artist Colleen
Brown on Zoom. No prior experience necessary! Sign up by emailing:
kentharrisonartscouncil@shaw.ca

To open the topic in the first session, Colleen Brown will summarize some perspectives
artists and art historians take toward portraits. Colleen will provide some examples from
art history to seed the discussion. In the second session, group members will find or
create their own images to bring to the discussion.

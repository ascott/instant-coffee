Marina Roy

We are delighted to welcome artist Marina Roy presenting an excerpted
reading from her upcoming book on the appearance of the mythological
Sasquatch in Northwest Coast BC, including her childhood home of Coquitlam.
This reading is intended to offer alternative connections into the multiple
meanings of hybrid creatures and languages in Germinal.

Big Foot. Ssq'ets. Sasquatch. A hominid-like hybrid; a bipedal creature
inhabiting the forests of the Pacific Northwest Coast. Myths, stories and
sightings perpetuate the appearance of the Sasquatch across languages and
cultures. Sasquatch variously conjure the grotesque, the comical and the
camp. Their presence underlines our fear of the unknown, merging the real
with the imaginary.

Marina Roy's work is cross-disciplinary. She investigates the intersection
between materials, history, language, and ideology. The foundations of her
research are in psychoanalysis, biopolitics, human-animal distinction,
feminism, natural history, and linguistics. Roy is interested in a
grotesque art: how human, animal, plant, mineral and microbial life
coalesce into new material formations.

New Media Gallery, in partnership with the City of New Westminster Cultural
Services, presents the FRESH TALK series. FRESH TALK brings together
artists, writers, poets, performers, scientists, critical thinkers and
community members for interdisciplinary discussions related to the themes
and concepts of New Media Gallery exhibitions. Fresh Talk is open to a
broad audience and is intended to reflect the diversity of contemporary new
media practices.

GERMINAL, the current exhibition from New Media Gallery, brings together 3
installations by 6 artists. "Germinal" refers to the earliest stage of
development. From every germinal moment comes the potential to grow and
become hybrid. The works in the exhibition incorporate hybrid creatures,
hybrid words & hybrid languages.

Join us for an exciting talk, tour and discussion that will connect deeply
with works by Germinal artists Nadia Hironaka + Matthew Suib, Laramascoto
(Santiago Lara + Beatriz Coto) and Suhee Kim & Oliver Smith.

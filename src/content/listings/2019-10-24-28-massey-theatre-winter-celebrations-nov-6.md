Call for Media Artists

Winter Celebrations at Anvil Centre is seeking submissions of works by Professional
Multimedia Installation Artists for presentation in December 2019. The Multimedia
Installation will be incorporated into the Winter World scenographic installation during
the Winter Celebrations, and should reflect on the event themes (winter, solstice, nature,
renewal, darkness/light) Professional fees will be paid
to the selected artist. This
programming is presented by the Anvil Centre Theatre.

Installation will be selected based on the following criteria

- Artistic merit/interest

- Suitability to venue

- Diversity of disciplines/mediums

- Technical/logistical considerations

- Appropriateness for all-ages audiences

Please submit a proposal including: description of work(s), technical requirements, bio
of lead artist(s), images (of submitted installation or similar work) to
julian@masseytheatre.com with the subject line WC Multimedia Submission by
November 6th, 2019

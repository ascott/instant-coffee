Diyan Achjadi Artist Talk

Thursday, September 20, 2018, 6 - 7pm
Rennie Hall, Emily Carr University of Art + Design
520 E 1st Ave
Vancouver, BC V5T 1A7, Canada

Join the City of Vancouvers Public Art Program for an artist talk by
artist Diyan Achjadi, who will present on her public artwork Coming Soon!

This is a year-long project taking place throughout Vancouver on the
structures that surround construction sites. Each month, a new series of
hand-printed posters are installed throughout the city, mirroring the
landscapes around new developments. Over the course of the year, the
posters will accumulate. As they are vulnerable to the effects of the
elements, they will begin to decay and transform. At the heart of this
project is an exploration of questions of value, temporality, and labour.

Diyan Achjadi works in drawing, printmaking, and animation. She received a
BFA from the Cooper Union School of Art (New York) and an MFA from
Concordia University (Montreal). She has exhibited internationally and is
the recipient of a 2017 artist-initiated public-art commission from the
City of Vancouver. Born in Jakarta, Indonesia, Diyan lives and works in
Vancouver, and is an associate professor at Emily Carr University of Art +
Design.

Coming Soon! was selected as part of the City of Vancouvers
Artist-Initiated Public Art call that invited local artists to propose new
works throughout the city, contemplating its defining features, spaces and
neighbourhoods.

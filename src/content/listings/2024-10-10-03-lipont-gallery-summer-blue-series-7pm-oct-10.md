Lipont Gallery is honored
to present the Summer Blue Series by esteemed
watercolour artist Fucai Zhao, an evocative exploration of Vancouvers urban and
natural landscapes through the medium of watercolour. We invite you to join us for
the opening reception of this exceptional exhibition.

Exhibition Opening Reception:

Thursday, October 10th, 2024

7:00 PM - 9:00 PM

Lipont Gallery, Room 218 (No elevator), 4211 No. 3 Road, Richmond

Summer Blue Series captures the essence of Vancouver, distilling its unique light
and atmosphere into fluid and expressive compositions. Fucai Zhaos mastery lies in
his ability to merge the spontaneity and ethereality of watercolour with the rigorous
formality of traditional Chinese painting. The result is a harmonious balance between
the familiar and the abstract, the observed and the imagined.

Zhaos works, painted during his midsummer sojourn in Vancouver, invite viewers
into a world where the citys fire hydrants, street signs, and urban vistas become
symbols of the quiet beauty
in everyday life. These works reflect a deep meditation
on place and memory, blending vibrant colours and relaxed forms that evoke both
the dynamism and the serenity of Vancouver.

As you walk through the gallery, Zhaos watercolours will offer moments of quiet
reflection, inviting you to see the world through the artists eyes"where every detail,
no matter how mundane, holds beauty and meaning.

For more details, visit Lipont Gallery's website. Admission is free, and parking is
available. We are also easily accessible via the Canada Line at Aberdeen Station.

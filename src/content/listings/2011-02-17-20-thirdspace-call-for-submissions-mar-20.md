Thirdspace, Call for Submissions, Deadline March 20th 2011

Thirdspace is a non-commercial zine produced out of the UVSS Women's
Centre through the Women's Publication Network. As Western Canada's
longest running university funded zine, Thirdspace aims to be a source for
alternative thought, expression, knowledge and discourse. We will not
print racist, sexist, homophobic, trans-phobic or ableist materials.
Current and future issues may soon have a digital version.

The Spring 2011 issue of Thirdspace is celebrating the 30th anniversary of
the Women's Centre. We are seeking submissions of artwork, articles,
interviews, poetry, fiction, comics, papers and photography. Submissions
about the history of Women's Centres are especially welcome.

Please email all questions, queries and submissions to Caitlin, Esha and
Jessie at thirdspace.zine@gmail.com or drop by the Women's Centre in
UVic's Student Union Building, B107.

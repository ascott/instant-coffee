Free LIVE Performances at Richmond Art Gallery
Saturday, September 30, 2017

As part of Culture Days, the Richmond Art Gallery is hosting free live performances from 
artists in the current ETERNAL RETURN exhibition.

LUCIEN DUREY PERFORMANCES
12:00 pm and 3:00pm
Exhibiting artist Lucien Durey will perform Scarecrow, Visionary, Muse, Musa, Saguaro, 
Christiana live in the Gallery space. Dureys vocal compositions have been written for 
a number of his sculptures in the exhibition, revealing additional narratives and 
meanings in this series and within each sculpture.

ALANNA HO with RAINBOW FORECAST PROJECT
2:00 " 3:00 pm
Richmond Cultural Centre Performance Hall
Join Rainbow Forecast Project in a collaborative installation / performance for all ages to 
play with sound and music-making. This performance is a culmination of a series of 
workshops led by artist Alanna Ho with children aged 4-6 years old over the month of 
September 2017 as an additional component to the Eternal Return exhibition. Students 
will participate in a live performance, and invite audience members to join in the fun of 
sound-making through interactive play. 
This performance was supported by Richmond Canada 150 and Appleseed Childrens 
Playhouse.

All events are free and open to all ages.

For more information: http://www.richmondartgallery.org/

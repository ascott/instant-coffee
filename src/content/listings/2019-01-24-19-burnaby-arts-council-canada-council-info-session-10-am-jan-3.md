Join us at the Burnaby Arts Council on January 30 for an information session about the 
Canada Council for the Arts' funding programs. This session is designed to provide an 
overview on grants available to artists working across artistic fields of practice. Canada 
Council will also be booking one-on-one sessions with artists same day starting at 1:00 
pm.

Please note that the info session will be given in English, with a Q&A period in both 
official languages.

Time: Wednesday, January 30 from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. (noon)
Location: Burnaby Arts Council
6584 Deer Lake Avenue
Burnaby, BC V5G 3T7
Cost: Free

For more information and to RSVP, please contact Laura Beks, Program Assistant, at: 
laura.beks@canadacouncil.ca

To request sign language interpretation or access related supports, please email Laura 
Beks by 12 p.m., Friday January 18.

If you would like to book a one-one-one meeting with a CCA officer you can contact 
them directly:

Steve Oliver steve.oliver@canadacouncil.ca for one-on-ones in Burnaby, BC.

For more info on funding opportunities see: [https://canadacouncil.ca/funding/grants](https://canadacouncil.ca/funding/grants)

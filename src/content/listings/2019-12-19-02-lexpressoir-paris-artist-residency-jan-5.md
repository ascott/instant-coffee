L'Expressoir allows regional, national, and international artists the opportunity to
exchange professional and emotive approaches towards new experiences and aesthetic
realities. Located in the historic village of Marnay Sur Seine (1 hour by train from Paris)
L'Expressoir emphasizes the connection between the local community and the diverse
artists this village attracts. This residency encourages projects promoting the interaction
and relationships between different cultures worldwide.
Duration of Residency:
1 to 2 months
Number of Artists at one time:
3
L'Expressoir offers each artist support for the following aspects of the residency:
-Private bedroom with private bathroom
-Shared kitchen with other artists in residence
-Private art studio
-Possibility of individual exhibition/Artist talk
-Weekly trips to the farmer's market/local establishments for groceries/art supplies, etc.
-Two group outings per residency
to research the Champagne-Ardenne region
-Information about daily logistics as well as the regional arts scene
-Interaction with the community
Residency fees do not include:
-Airfares
-Visas
-Airport pickup or return (although we will help organize this for you)
-Transportation
-General living costs such as food, health, and travel insurance (we will assist you in
finding proper groceries and staples)
-Art supplies (we will assist you in finding specific supplies if necessary)
L'Expressoir is an independent project and is currently unable to provide financial
support for visiting artists. For artists applying for grants and scholarships in their home
country L'Expressoir can provide official documents and letters of invitation as
necessary.
Application Deadlines:
Spring 2020 residencies (March, April, May) due January 5, 2020
Summer 2020 residencies (June, July, August) due March 1, 2020
Fall 2020 residencies (September, October, November) due June 1, 2020
Email keri@expressoir.fr for
detailed information and application

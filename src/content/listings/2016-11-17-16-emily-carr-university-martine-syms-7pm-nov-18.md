Join us for a talk with L.A. based artist Martine Syms.

Friday November 18 | 7:00 PM
MoCap Studio I 285e, North Building
Emily Carr University of Art and Design

Martine Syms is a Los Angeles based American artist whose practice encompasses 
video production, photography, web-based art, writing, performance lecturing and 
publishing. She works with popular image, language and text vernaculars, 
particularly ones that circulate within and about blackness in visual culture in 
America. She co-directed Golden Age, a project space focused on printed matter in 
Chicago, and is the founder of Dominica, a publishing imprint in Los Angeles. Syms 
has framed her practice as a "conceptual entrepreneur", which holds tension 
between conceptual strategies that artists have used to challenge the 
commodification of art, and the flexibility that artists of her generation (b. 1988) have 
to employ within the economic conditions in America. Her talk will focus on her 
publishing projects, and writing and editing strategies.

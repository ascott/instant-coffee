Opening reception
Thursday, March 23, 6-9pm

Exhibition runs March 23 - April 22, 2017

Franc gallery is pleased to present an exhibition of new works by Hank Bull, in 
conjunction with the artist's cross-Canada exhibition tour Connexion, now 
showing at the Burnaby Art Gallery. 

The works take the character of the cardboard box, one that is ubiquitous and 
well-travelled, and explore its language through a series of mediated 
transformations. Bull dresses the box in the forms of photography, painting, 
sculpture, and theatre. These multiple instances allow for comparison, unveiling 
the attributes that are essential to the iterative object itself. The forms play with 
the usefulness of the object, opening access to aspects in some that are barred 
from the viewer in others. This recyclable structure of transportation and storage, 
made specific by the artist's precise colour choices and the modernist form-
sensitive grids, takes on a vibrancy that is to be found in the stuff of everyday 
life. The palimpsests of their past use, seen in blankness, size and structure, pull 
at our desire with their negative capabilities, and their recalcitrance towards their 
global capitalist origins.

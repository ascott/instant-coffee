Before This and Now: New works by Madeline Smith at Blim Gallery

Opening: 8 PM, Friday January 8. Runs January 8 - 29, 2010

Before This and Now is a collection of work based on the space of Blim.
Paintings and drawings where the past and present of the building are made
evident. Details of the space are pulled out and played with. It is a space
where art is made and shown, and has now become even more integral as a
subject.

Madeline Smith was born in Mission, British Columbia in 1982. She attended
high school at Langley Fine Arts School and graduated with a Bachelor of
Fine Arts in Visual Arts from Simon Fraser University in 2007. She recently
spent a year in Ontario working at the Art Gallery of Peterborough as a
children's art educator.

Smith has worked in a variety of mediums and contexts including
installation, sculpture and video, but painting remains her primary focus.
Many of her recent oil paintings examine the details of detritus in urban
spaces and explore the relationship between people and their by-products.

Madeline Smith lives and works in Vancouver.

write up:

SATELLITE is a free, outdoor ambient music concert and stargazing event in Elm
Park. All ages are welcome. We gather in the green space between the Elm Park
Fieldhouse and the tennis courts to explore the night sky with telescopes and
binoculars against a backdrop of gentle, ambient music. Audiences are encouraged
to bring a picnic blanket, some extra layers of clothing, and food or drinks.
Performances begin at sunset (9:09pm) and end at astronomical dusk (10:48pm).

C. Olivia Valenza and Charlie Cooper, artists-in-residence at the Elm Park
Fieldhouse, will take turns guiding the night with musical performances that
intermingle with the nighttime soundscape.

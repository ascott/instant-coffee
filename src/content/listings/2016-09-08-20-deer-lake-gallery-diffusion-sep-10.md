Exhibition: Diffusion
Dates: September 10 to October 1
Artists: Corey Hardeman, Karolyn Kramer & Sean O'Flynn
Media: acrylic / mixed media
Artist Talk: Saturday, October 1st @ 12 pm

Diffusion is a group exhibition that will be held at The Deer Lake 
Gallery featuring the work of Carolyn Kramer, Corey Jennifer 
Hardeman and Sean O'Flynn. Diffusion is hosted and organized by 
The Burnaby Arts Council. The exhibition will open Saturday 
September 10th, 2016 and run until October 1st.

Diffusion represents the subconscious dispersion of impressions 
and ideas by the 3 artists who participate in this exhibition. Through 
a radically singular approach that is well known in contemporary 
debate, all use references and ideas that are so integrated into the 
process of the work that they may escape those who do not take 
the time to explore how and why these images haunt you, like a 
good film, long after you've seen them. The intention of the work is 
highly personal but don't reference recognisable form. The results 
are deconstructed to the extent that meaning is shifted and possible 
interpretation becomes multifaceted. Their mixed media artworks 
never show the complete structure. This results in the fact that the 
artist can easily imagine their own interpretation without being 
hindered by the historical reality. O'Flynn's work is an implosion of 
colour which allows the viewer intimate access to emotions 
expressed in the work. Kramer's work is a more subtle 
dispersement of colour. They originate from microscopic 
photographs or sketches that she extols into dramatic and serene 
works of singular beauty. Hardeman uses her paintings as 
documented moments in time rendered in oil paint; they are the 
remnants of her experience of bereavement. They have a unique 
beauty of an almost transcendent quality. As the viewer receives 
the full impact of their celebration of life in its purest form they will 
embraced by their colour, textures and designs.

Please join us for the opening reception of Diffusion that will be 
held September 10th, 2016 between 2-4pm at the Deer Lake 
Gallery.

We also invite you to an Artist Talk with Corey Hardeman on 
Saturday, October 1st @ 12:00 pm.

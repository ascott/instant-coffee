In recognition of Indigenous History Month, New Leaf Editions is honored to present
"Indigenous Printshow", a landmark exhibition showcasing prints by Indigenous artists
who have collaborated with master printmaker Peter Braune over the last four decades.

The exhibition features works by celebrated artists including Germaine Arnaktauyok,
Shuvinai Ashoona, Kelly Cannell, Elisapee Ishulutak, Brian Jungen, George Littlechild,
David Neel, Tim Paul, Tim Pitsiulak, Susan Point, Bill Reid, Lyle Wilson, Xwalacktun,
and Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun.

Spanning a wide array of printmaking techniques, the exhibition highlights Braunes
technical mastery and his enduring artistic partnerships. Many of these collaborations
have spanned decades and continue to this day.

Exhibition Dates: June 3rd to July 1st, 2025
Location: New Leaf Editions, 3-1244 Cartwright St., Granville Island. Vancouver
Opening Reception: June 26th, Thursday 6 to 8 PM
For more information, please visit: www.newleafeditions.com

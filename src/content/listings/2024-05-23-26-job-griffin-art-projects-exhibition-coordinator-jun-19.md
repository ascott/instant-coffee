Exhibition Coordinator

PTE: 4 days per week (Wednesday to Saturday from 9 - 5PM)

Permanent position

Start date: August 14, 2024

Location: North Vancouver, British Columbia

Deadline for Applications: June 19, 2024

Salary range : $27 - 30/hour

Established in 2015, Griffin is a non-profit art gallery and residency located in North
Vancouver. Griffin is a non collecting institution that has quickly become a vibrant
contributor to the North Vancouver cultural landscape.
Griffin promotes and
celebrates visual art by supporting artists and curators in the production of new work
through its residency program, sharing private collections with the public, and
producing new research on contemporary art from BC and around the world through
its exhibitions and public programs.

Griffin Art Projects is seeking an Exhibition Coordinator to provide support in the
areas of daily operations and facility management, exhibition coordination and
development and marketing. This role also researches, develops and implements
strategies for the effective operations of Griffin Art Projects and its activities. Under
the supervision of the Director, the Exhibition Coordinator is responsible for
administration and facility management for the organization, as well as coordinating
its development plan, including volunteer and event management.

This position requires the ability to problem solve and work on multiple time-sensitive
deadlines and detailed tasks independently
and with a high degree of accuracy. The
role requires the candidate to manage the operational and development areas of
the program on time and on budget, while also keeping track of statistics and
monitoring of the facility

The successful candidate will be an effective communicator, both orally and in
writing and possess understanding of the local and regional arts community.

This is a four day per week position with regularly scheduled work taking place from
Wednesday to Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. This position includes occasional mid-
week and evening hours, as required to fulfill responsibilities.

During exhibition changes (occurring three times per year), the position will shift to a
five day per week schedule, taking place from Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00
pm, for two weeks.

The responsibilities of the Exhibition Coordinator include but are not limited to the
areas of Facility and Operations, Development Coordination, and Registrar &
General Administration.

Qualifications and Experience

The successful candidate will possess a Bachelors degree in Art or Art History,
Museum or Curatorial studies or a related discipline. It is essential that the
candidate have a minimum of 3 years full-time work experience managing gallery
operations and exhibitions in a museum, public art gallery and/or artist run centre
context.

Key Skills and Assets:

Knowledge of best practices in gallery processes and procedures, including the
installation, storage and handling of artworks and exhibitions

Experience communicating and working directly with curators, artists, preparators,
interns, gallery stakeholders and contractors, as well as the general public

Exceptional written, presentation and interpersonal communication skills

Strong organizational and administrative skills and the ability to work under general
direction within a small team environment

Organized and detail oriented, with the ability to work independently with minimal
direct supervision,
and to deal effectively and tactfully with colleagues, community
partners and the public.

Experience in the coordination of grants and funding proposals

Intermediate design skills, social media and marketing experience

Ability to assist in preparation, planning, research and installation of exhibitions.

Ability to process administrative work and prepare correspondence and reports.

Ability to manage budgets involving arithmetical and statistical computations along
with sound project and records management abilities

Experience working with Adobe Creative Suite, Microsoft Office, Google Drive,
Squarespace, MailChimp, and Eventbrite.

Ability to successfully pass a Criminal Record check

Access to a vehicle is considered desirable

including a detailed list of responsibilities.

info@griffinartprojects.ca

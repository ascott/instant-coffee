It's that time of year again, and if you are a publisher or an author with a book or books that
are published in 2020 and that meet our criteria for entry, we're inviting you to submit work
for consideration for the 2021 BC and Yukon Book Prizes.

The deadline for submissions is Tuesday, December 1.

The BC and Yukon Book Prizes award excellence in writing by writers from BC and Yukon
across eight categories: fiction, non-fiction, poetry, regional writing, childrens literature,
illustrated childrens literature, the Jim Deva Prize for Writing That Provokes, and the Bill
Duthie Booksellers' Choice Award.

For more information about submitting to the 2021 BC and Yukon Book Prizes visit our
website.

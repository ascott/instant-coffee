Michael Taussig March 27 Lecture
"Re-enchantment of the Sun in An Age of Global Meltdown“
March 27, 7:00 – 9:00 pm
3200 Djavad Mowafaghian Cinema, Goldcorp Centre for the Arts,
149 West Hastings Street, Vancouver

Dr. Michael Taussig
Professor of Anthropology at Columbia University, New York

He is the author of 11 books including "The Devil and Commodity 
Festishism"
(1980); and "Beauty and the Beast" (2012); and many articles.

Using parts of a theater-piece called The Berlin Sun Theater: The 
Master of Non-Mastery, that he recently presented in the 
Anthropocene conference at the Haus Der Kulturen Der Welt in Berlin, 
Professor Taussig will explore the meaning and hope for the "mastery 
of non-mastery" as the basis for a new approach to our technological 
domination of nature

No registration required for this event

Sponsored by the Department of Sociology and Anthropology, 
Department of English, and the Dean of Faculty of Arts and Social 
Sciences. For more information, please contact Dara Culhane, 
Department of Sociology and Anthropology: culhane@sfu.ca

FREE AND OPEN TO THE PUBLIC

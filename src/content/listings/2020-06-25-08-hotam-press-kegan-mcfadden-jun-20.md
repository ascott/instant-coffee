Kegan McFadden
Partners in Crime

June 20 to August 30, 2020

After three months of closure due to COVID-19, Hotam Press Bookshop/Gallery will
reopen with a survey exhibition of works by Kegan McFadden.

Partners in Crime, composed of printed matter and multiples by Kegan McFadden, is
built around the artists involvement with the mail art. An evolving body of work that
references interconnected nature of art making through text and reader, distilling the
relationship of you and me as a means of identifying/reinforcing the importance of
the recipient in mail art/art making. This showcase is augmented with over twenty
printed matter and multiples Kegan has published over the past fifteen years.

Based in Victoria, Kegan McFadden is a graduate of the Critical and Curatorial
Studies of UBC. As an artist, writer and curator, his output has been varied but often
relies on interpersonal connections, usually through a
queer lens, and incorporates a
mixture of text and image as well as modest means of production.

New Group Exhibition - 'Hindsight is 2020'

In this exhibition we explore our ability to look back through time to select important
moments and give them the honours of remaining with us for the rest of our lives.
Sometimes a particular year is very overwhelming for us and it seems as though the theme
of the year has been set beyond our control, with news headlines, and group hysteria, it
may be hard to shift gears and appreciate the positives or hidden blessings in our lives
when everything and everyone demands that we obsess over the negative.

This has been one of those years, and we invite you to almost do the unthinkable and look
back on something truly positive that has transpired in your life in 2020. Some moment, or
event, or person, or story, that you would never give up or trade, that
lifted your spirit,
changed the course of your life, made you happy to be alive, gave you promise for a better
future, or simply made you relish in the present moment when you felt no matter what is
going on outside, it was this one thing in your life that was truly important, and worth giving
the spotlight over all the other headlines.

Please send all your submissions with titles in the image itself to
ariaroundtheworld@gmail.com On average we can pick maximum 3-5 images based on
how much response we get but even just one would be great. You may include a bio or any
contact info you want attached.

As art spaces, particularly visual art spaces such as galleries and museums are
supposed to be seen and positioned as places of public discourse and dialogue, in
which, the curator plays a key role to create the opportunity.

In thinking about the platforms for public discourse, we, the alternative art space
executors, found ourselves thinking about saunas, baths and the functions they served
across in various cultures as a platform for more than just physical wellness, they
served as a place for the public to intersect exchange, where the discourse/naked talk
has been disregarded for long.

We are creating a sauna space within the gallery space and inviting proposals which
engage
with the ideas of public discourse, dialogue, community and health, preferably
while in the sauna.

Who is the curator?

This project is curated by S, a curatorial boyz band casually formed by Steven Tong
and Steven Dragonn. Steven Tong is an independent curator and the co-director of
CSAspace, an independent Canadian project space founded by Christopher Brayshaw,
Adam Harrison and Steven Tong in Vancouver since 2007. Steven Dragonn, founder,
director and curator of Canton-sardine, which was founded with Dr. Xiaoyan Yang in
2018. This project will take place in Canton-sardine.

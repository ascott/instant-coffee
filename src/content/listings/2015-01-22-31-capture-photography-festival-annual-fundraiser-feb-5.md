Capture Photography Festival Annual Fundraiser

An evening in support of the 2015 Capture Photography Festival, Vancouver’s
celebration of photography and lens-based art. The night marks the launch
of the new Capture ‘Mini’ Artist Editions series, with prints also
available for purchase through the Capture website (capturephotofest.com)
after Feb 5.

Participating artists include Danny Singer, Birthe Piontek, and Scott
Massey, among others.

When: Thursday, February 5, 2015, 7:30 pm to 10:00 pm
Where: MINI Yaletown (of course), 1039 Hamilton Street, Vancouver V6B 5T4
Tickets: $175 earlybird / $200 regular

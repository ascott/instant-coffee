Pyramid Power #5 is finally here!

Friday, September 19th, at Monte Clark Gallery (2339 
Granville St.), from 7pm until 11pm. 

Entry by donation. Get the latest issue. Impress your friends!

With music by Cam Dales and Cale Bernard Sr.

Pyramid Power magazine showcases the best in Canadian and 
international contemporary art and literature. Pyramid Power’s 
brazenly eccentric perspective on the art world is visually 
embodied in the balance it strikes between form and content—
the magazine’s bold yet considered presentation accentuates 
the richness of its artwork and writing.

Issue #5 features the work of Ron Tran, Clint Burnham, Mat 
Bushell, Holly Ward, Kathy Slade, Jeff Derkson,Melanie Schiff , 
Arni Haraldsson, Stephen Waddell, Paul Chan, Candice 
Hopkins, Melanie O’Brian, Christian Kliegel, Dan Starling, 
Allyson Clay, Elspeth Pratt, Cullinan + Richards, Keith Higgins, 
Eddie Berrigan, Mike Hauser, Weldon Gardner Hunter and Neil 
Wedman.

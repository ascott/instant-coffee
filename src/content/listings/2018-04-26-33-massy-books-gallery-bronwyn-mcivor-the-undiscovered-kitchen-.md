Massy Books Gallery
Bronwyn McIvor: The Undiscovered Kitchen
May 3 - May 15
Opening Reception: Thursday, May 3, 7-10pm

Location: Massy Books Gallery, 229 E. Georgia St (second floor, stairs access only), 
Vancouver, V6A 1Z6
www.massybooks.com
604-721-4405

Throughout her practice, Bronwyn McIvor has taken unusual angles on the classical 
subject of still life, focusing mainly on the detritus of food preparation rather than the 
prized objects showcased in the Dutch still lifes that continue to inspire her. Created 
primarily in oils, the paintings in The Undiscovered Kitchen monumentalize and enrich 
this overlooked everyday debris with sumptuous, bold colours, referencing the intensity 
of Caravaggio. These interpretations transform the mundane, relishing its detail and its 
drama, and in the process animating it with unexpected vitality. Commonplace 
foodstuffs become landscape, portrait, or strangely unrecognizable presences, giving 
the viewer pause to appreciate the surreal qualities of everyday life.

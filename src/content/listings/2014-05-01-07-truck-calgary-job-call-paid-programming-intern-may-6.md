SECOND STORY ART SOCIETY, O/A TRUCK Contemporary Art in Calgary

Job Call: Paid Programming Intern

(Canada Summer jobs)

Located in Calgary, Alberta, TRUCK is a not-for-profit artist-run centre 
dedicated to the development and public presentation of contemporary art. 
Since it was founded in 1983, the Society has facilitated countless 
exhibitions, activities and events that foster cultural development and the 
dissemination of contemporary art. TRUCK is currently seeking a dynamic 
individual to assist the organization with our program activities in the role of 
Summer Programming Intern.

JOB DESCRIPTION: The Programming Intern is a 12 week summer contract 
position that reports to the Director, and has a close working relationship with 
our Programming Coordinator. The current position is for 30 hours per week 
and assists TRUCK with the presentation of all aspects of our public 
programs and engagement activities, and is responsible for numerous 
initiatives including, but not limited to: support of our Main Space programming, 
the research, development, and implementation of our Summer CAMPER 
programming; supervision of volunteers; supervision of opening receptions; 
installation; assist with artist talks, panel discussions and workshops; 
communication with the general public, gallery visitors, artists and the media.

QUALIFICATIONS/SKILLS: The Programming Intern must be a returning 
student and have experience in art history, visual arts, and/or arts 
administration, with a strong understanding of contemporary art, art history 
and artist-run culture. Skills required for this position include, an 
understanding and experience of working with power tools; experience with 
Mac OSX software; and a working knowledge of FileMaker Pro, Adobe 
Creative Suite, and Microsoft Office software. The Programming Intern will 
exhibit excellent art handling skills, research skills, and be able to work 
individually and as part a team.

PROGRAMMING INTERN'S DUTIES

- Assist with the presentation of our public programs.

- Assist with the promotion of TRUCK'S Main Space Exhibitions & CAMPER 
Projects, etc.

- Install artworks.

- Work directly with artists and volunteers.

- Organize gallery in readiness of exhibition openings (tidy and clean)

- Liaise with local cultural groups and stakeholders in support of our activities

- Represent TRUCK in a professional and courteous manner to artists, 
visitors, funding bodies, sponsors etc.

This is a 12 week summer contract position, open only to returning students. 
The rate of pay will be $15.00 per Hour for 30 hours per week, commencing 
June 1, 2014. Address application to the Hiring Committee, 2009 10th Ave 
SW, Calgary, Alberta, T3C 0K4. Applications must arrive by the application 
deadline May 15, 2014. Late applications and email submissions will not be 
accepted and only successful applicants will be notified. TRUCK is an equal 
opportunity employer.

U-Hall Community Space

U-HALL

MANDATE

U-HALL is a new community space in Calgary, located above and 
administrated by Truck Contemporary Art Gallery. Our main objective is to 
provide an affordable venue for just about anything that Calgary artists, 
cultural organizations, or art interested general public might need.

U-HALL is dedicated to providing a respectful, inclusive and non-hierarchal 
space to host your events. Proposals to rent U-HALL that are counter to our 
own mandate (please see www.truck.ca) or encourage violence, hate or 
oppression will not be considered.

THE PARTICULARS

Offering approximately 404 square feet of floor space and 73 linear feet of 
wall space U-HALL is available for whatever you might need: Lectures, 
Workshops, Classes, Sales, Gatherings, Meetings, Screenings, 
Performances, Weddings, DIY-exhibitions, Funerals, Bar Mitzvahs, Coven 
Meetings, etcetera.

U-Hall's rental rate is $250 per week or $50 per day. Financial sponsorship by 
TRUCK Contemporary Art through the waiving of rental fees is available for a 
select number of proposals per year. If you or your group is in need of 
sponsorship please fill out the optional 'sponsorship' field in the U-Hall Rental 
Request Form.

To inquire about rentals or if you have any questions regarding the U-HALL 
space or mandate, please contact engagement@truck.ca.

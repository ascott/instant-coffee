Photobase Artist Call 

The North Vancouver Community Arts Council is seeking artists for a themed 
exhibition called Photobase (working title) at CityScape Community Art Space. 
Photobase is a Selected Exhibition Program for the 2018 Capture Photography 
Festival.

The exhibition will be held at CityScape Community Art Space March 16 - April 21, 
2018, with an opening reception on Thursday, March 15, 7-9pm. We are calling 
artists who use family photographs as the groundwork for their practice. A 
photograph can be read as truth or a reflection of a truth. The artist occupies the 
space between the family photograph and the meaning it conveys to the public 
world. There are many kinds of families that can be represented in this exhibition. 
There are families we are born into, families we create, families from abroad, friends 
who are more like family, and people we choose to call family. The word family is 
inclusive to the stories of the people who mean something in your life and the stories 
you want to share!

We are looking for more than family portraits. Old photos could be used to convey an 
emotional connection with your family. Photographs can be cut, manipulated, and 
changed to create a new piece of artwork. Figures in the photograph can be the 
main focus or the background to the piece and they can be altered, silhouetted, 
distorted, and painted. We are looking for creativity working with family photographs.

Submission Deadline: Saturday, January 20, 2018 at 4pm
Online submissions HERE or drop off the submission to:
CityScape Community Art Space, 335 Lonsdale Avenue, North Vancouver

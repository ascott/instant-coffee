Summer 2009: Artspace celebrates its 35th year of programming!

And while we prepare for the celebrations coming this September, we are
opening up the main exhibition space for our ARCHIVES LAB.

ARCHIVES LAB is a project looking at the history of the centre through an
extensive collection of materials recovered after the flood five years ago,
along with materials collected from members and the community and stories
shared and told that fill in the many gaps in our history. Our aim is to
compile the most detailed and comprehensive history of exhibitions and
events since the first exhibition produced by the centre in 1974.

With this in mind we are releasing a national call to artists, curators, and
those affiliated with ARTSPACE to help us find the missing links. If you
have documents, photographs, catalogues, or any other material produced by
Artspace, we very much want to see it! If you have stories, anecdotes,
recordings or any other documents you think will be important in this
project, please share them with us!

We will be collecting all materials over the months of July and August, with
a completed restoration of our archives slated for September and a big
celebration party set for Friday, September 25th.

In conjunction with ARCHIVES LAB we are re-launching our upgraded site,
www.artspace-arc.org . Here you can find our most recent history with
extended documentation of our exhibitions posted online. Please keep an eye
out for more archival data being posted each day, as we work towards having
each and every exhibition produced by ARTSPACE posted on our site.

Blanket Contemporary Art is pleased to present New Shapes, an exhibition
of new paintings by LA based artist Monique Mouton. This is her first solo
show with Blanket.

Monique Mouton's softly iconic paintings, all on roughly hewn plywood,
sweetly insist that more than a moment is needed. A thin application of oil
paint delivers you back to the gesso; the hand cut edges, the plywood. There
is no one behind the curtain, clandestinely working to deliver truth or
transcendence swiftly, no movie magic. She stops short of delivering a
fully resolved composition confounding the viewer with such delicacy, that a
sudden awareness of time, of the very moment when something begins, or
suddenly changes, descends from these modest ellipses. The standard forms
and elements of modernist painting are there; the monochrome, the stripes,
the drips'¦but these are precedents that she uses for something slower, much
smaller and poetic recalling the astonishing experience of art today.

Mouton graduated from Emily Carr University of Art and Design, Vancouver, in
2006. Her work was most recently seen at the Vancouver Art Gallery's
Enacting Abstraction (group), 2009; the OR Gallery (Vancouver) Making Real
(group) curated by Eli Bornowsky, 2008; Bodger's and Kludgers Co-operative
Art Parlour (Vancouver) Measure (solo), 2008; and CSA Space (Vancouver), a
two person show with Michael Morris curated by Adam Harrison, 2008.

About Liquidation World
Liquidation World is an art collective formed in 2021 with the intention of producing
and showcasing experimental artworks and performances. Projects the collective
undertake reach at the unexpected and incalculable phenomena that emerge from
combining different practices, and communities in unconventional presentation
spaces. Past projects include an open-submission art consignment store, a multi-
room audiovisual Rock Opera, visual art exhibitions, a laser show, video
performances and installations, and an ongoing series of screenprinting events and
artist-led workshops. In 2022 the collective curated Wrong Wave: The Liquidation
World Guide to Maximizing Your Autonomy, a multi-venue festival of art rock,
experimental film, visual art and community projects. The collective currently
operates a multi-purpose art space at 1965 Main St, in addition to supporting off-site
projects and collaborations.

13. EDUCATION
| Art Gallery at Evergreen | Towards Truth and Reconciliation: Comic Workshop | SEP 27
Towards Truth and Reconciliation: Comic Workshop with Jordanna George at Art
Gallery at Evergreen
September 27, 2024, 6:30 p.m. - 8:30 p.m.
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam BC

How can you play a part in reconciliation? Join artist Jordanna George, an artist of
TSou-ke and Ukrainian ancestry now residing in Coquitlam, for an illustration
workshop and creative activity in honour of National Day for Truth and
Reconciliation. This public program, inspired by "Hummingbird Takes Flight" by q"ic"
y"(Katzie) First Nation artist smx, Rain Pierre, is a meaningful opportunity to
reflect on Truth and Reconciliation and create a comic artwork in response to the
ways we can take action.

"Hummingbird Takes Flight" (2021) is a digital drawing by Rain Pierre which
incorporates Coast Salish design and symbolism. The artwork is exhibited on the
windows of Evergreen Cultural Centre and is an offering of protection and a
reminder to passers-by of the possibilities present all around us.

Free and wheelchair accessible event. Family friendly, ages 12+ recommended. 45
spots available.

Stills Film Processing Workshop (Black and White, 35mm, 120mm)

Sunday April 10; 10.00am - 6.00pm
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander Street (lane entrance)

$60 + taxes (Member Admission)
$75 + taxes (General Admission)
Price includes materials and workbook
Instructor: Jesse Andrewartha

Registration: [http://ow.ly/106SDC](http://ow.ly/106SDC)
Share: [https://www.facebook.com/events/194529780920681/](https://www.facebook.com/events/194529780920681/)

A hands-on workshop in the art and craft of stills film processing, for
anyone with an interest in analogue image making. For participants who have
never tried developing their own film, this will be an in-depth primer. For
those who have processed their own film before, this course will provide
in-depth information on theory and techniques, with an array specialized
practical tips.

Ideally, participants will bring their own unexposed black and white 35mm
or 120mm film roll, but anyone without the capacity to shoot in advance
will be given a roll of film on the day and lent a camera with which to
shoot during the lunch break. They will be able to process the film in the
afternoon session.

Workshop Components:

The workshop will focus on black and white film processing only, but our
class will involve three distinct parts:

(i) Film (1 hour)

Discussion of films stocks currently available, their characteristics and
how to select a specific stock for your needs.

(ii) Exposure (1 hour)

Exposure is critical in processing your own film and understanding how
light in your scene will translate into silver is integral to success. We
will discuss several simple techniques to ensure the best exposure for most
situations.

Break for lunch

(iii) Developing ( 5 hours)

Developing opens up world of the darkroom. In addition to basic processing
theory, there are a huge number of developers available with myriad
benefits and limitations. We will cover the process of developing film, the
choice of developer, stop and fix, but the main focus of this section is
hands-on activity, where students will have the opportunity to mix their
own chemistry and process their own film.

Chemistry and processing equipment will be provided, included in the
workshop fee.

Requirements:
Participants should bring an exposed but unprocessed film on 35mm or 120mm
format. 

Interested participants who can't bring exposed film should let us know at
least one week in advance and we will provide resources for them to shoot
during lunch break on the day.

About the Instructor:

Jesse Andrewartha was introduced to the magic of light and film as a
post-graduate in scientific photography. A 16 year veteran of the motion
picture visual effects industry, his films combine his two passions: cinema
and the underwater world in all its forms. Jesse is Volunteer Technical
Coordinator for Cineworks Annex analogue lab and studio.

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society (est.1980) is an artist-run
production and exhibition centre that supports independent filmmakers and
media artists working in digital video, celluloid film media and in
relation to cinema in expansive ways. We engage our membership and the
broader community through initiatives and services that foster dialogue and
experimentation with the moving image.

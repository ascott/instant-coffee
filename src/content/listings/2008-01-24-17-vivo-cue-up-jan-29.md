Press Release -
For Immediate Release

Video In Video Out and the Cue Up Collective present HOSPITALITY SERIES

Hospitality Series is an all-new format for Cue-Up and open screening at
VIVO. As in the past, all video producers and artists are invited to bring
up to 15 minutes of video based work or work-in-progress to screen and to
discuss with the assembled audience. Each event will also include an
invited guest who will present a talk or an intervention that puts
hospitality into a critical context. The aim of this series is to learn
about hosting both new work and old ideas.

All events begin TUESDAYS at 7:30 pm with the sign-up of videos for
screening from the floor (first come first serve). Video screenings will
begin at 8:00. Interventions or comments by the guest will be interspersed
throughout the evening (beginning at 7:30 pm). The event will conclude at
9:30 pm.

Tuesday, January 29, 2008
Location:: Video In Video Out, 1965 Main St.
Submissions 7:30pm and screenings 8:00pm
Socializing to follow.

----
Cue-Up Open Screening will include a short lesson about African
Hospitality led by Jackline Ochieng.  Jackline came to Vancouver from
Kenya to complete graduate studies in Sociology at UBC.  She has practiced
as a social worker in Nairobi as well as the lower mainland.  She hosts
Kenyan dinners in an effort to enrich western hospitality.  Kenyan Mandazi
and Chai Tea will be served.  Bring some sweets to add to the dessert
table.	Everyone is welcome!

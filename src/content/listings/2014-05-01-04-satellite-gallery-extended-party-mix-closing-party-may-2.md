Join us at Satellite Gallery﻿ on Friday, May 2 from 7pm to 10pm for the closing 
of Extended Party Mix.

Satellite Gallery has invited artists Eric Angus, Jamey Braden, Anyse 
Ducharme and Jessica Gnyp from the first year Master of Fine Arts (MFA) 
program at UBC's Department of Art History, Visual Art, and Theory to create 
an experimental, site responsive collaboration in the gallery's exhibition 
spaces. The artists will continue to work and collaborate in the Gallery during 
regular hours until May 2 and late at night, culminating in this closing party. 

Using the Gallery's distinctive site above Club FiveSixty (previously the site of 
Vancouver's A&B Sound) as a point of departure, the Club has become a site 
to respond to within the Gallery, an embodied place to research, a historical 
institution about which to learn, a fantasy place on which to project thoughts, 
feelings and creative imaginings. The artists share a particular interest in 
looking at the Gallery's site using a multitude of digital and sculptural 
processes, documenting the after hours soundscape that engulfs the Gallery, 
and creating a parallel night club through physical, material play.

This project is made possible with support from the Michael O'Brian Family 
Foundation and the Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory (AHVA) 
at UBC in collaboration with Satellite Gallery. A special thank you to Marina 
Roy for her dedication and support of this project.

Rain or Shine

Join local historian Bruce MacDonald for a fascinating exploration of
Delamont Park. Delamont/Kitsilano officially began in 1905, the year the
neighbourhood was named, the streetcar service was extended to Kitsilano
beach and development started in earnest. Amazingly, amongst the blocks and
blocks of apartment buildings in east Kitsilano is a small group of houses
surviving from those earliest years and beyond. Come and explore unique
homes from Kitsilano's pioneer years, with the local historian who wrote
the heritage report on Delamont for its owners, the Parks Board.

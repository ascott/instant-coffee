We are pleased to present Something good that never happened, a solo exhibition by
Nabil Azab with a text by Emily Zuberec, as part of Capture Photography Festival 2022.
The exhibition opens on Thursday, March 31, from 4 - 8 pm.

Something good that never happened brings together new abstract photographic works
by Nabil Azab made specifically for the exhibition space at Afternoon Projects.
Azabs photographs are in conversation with his
writing, drawing, etching, and research
practices. The dialogue between these areas of interest offers a fertile ground for his
images and their realities which will be presented in this exhibition.

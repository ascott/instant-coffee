Call for Participants - Queer Zone: a drawing performance

Location: Leg-In-Boot Square, Seawall, Vancouver, BC

Day &
Time: TBA (August 2024)

I am seeking 2SLGBTQ+ artists to help create a collaborative drawing performance.
Honorarium available.

If youre interested and for detailed information please contact, Kate Barry, at
queerzone2024@gmail.com before August 13th, 2024

More about the project: The Queer Zone is a one-day community art event for
2SLGBTQ+ people that will activate Leg-In-Boot Square with a collaborative drawing
performance for 4-hrs. 2SLGBTQ community members are invited to reinvent their
relationship to public space by responding to the theme of queer joy. Queer
collaborators will create a large drawing on canvas and may also use
environmentally friendly sidewalk chalk to queer the area around the canvas.

The Queer Zone has seating prioritized for people with disabilities or mobility issues
and ASL and non-verbal support for the project.

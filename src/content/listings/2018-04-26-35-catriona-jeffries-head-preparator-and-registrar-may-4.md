Head Preparator and Registrar
Catriona Jeffries
Deadline: Friday, May 4, at midnight

Based in Vancouver, Catriona Jeffries is one of Canadas pre-eminent spaces for 
contemporary art and is recognized internationally for its ongoing, rigorous 
contribution to contemporary art discourse. Established in 1994, the gallery 
represents artists of international prominence whose practices have emerged out of 
the conceptual art histories of Vancouver. By maintaining dialogues with artists, 
writers, curators, museums and private collections, Catriona Jeffries continues to 
curate projects that contextualize the work of gallery artists within an historical 
trajectory, as well as with those of other artists working internationally through an 
expanded practice of exhibition making and research.

Job Description:
Catriona Jeffries is now accepting applications for the position of Head Preparator 
and Registrar, to start May 2018. This is a permanent, full-time position that is 
responsible for all aspects of the movement of works between gallery, studios, 
exhibitions, art fairs, and collections. The Head Preparator and Registrar oversees 
all documentation and handling of the gallerys inventory, and establishes and 
maintains strong relationships with gallery artists, directors, and collections. 
Occasional extended hours during gallery installation or for travel during art fairs 
allow for some flexibility in day-to-day hours.

Responsibilities and Qualifications:
Working closely with the Directors and the gallerys artists, the Head Preparator and 
Registrar is responsible for the storage, handling, installation, shipping, and 
documentation of the gallerys inventory, both on- and off-site. As tasks and planning 
follow the cyclical rhythm of the gallerys exhibition schedule, as well as multiple, 
simultaneous external schedules of exhibition-making and production, 
responsibilities include directing or supervising other staff and contractors as 
necessary, as well as establishing and meeting deadlines. Other gallery staff rely on 
accurate and timely information provided by the Head Preparator and Registrar to 
maintain administration, communications, and archive on an ongoing basis, and 
support and collaborate with the Head Preparator and Registrar in turn.

The ideal candidate will work independently and in collaboration with others inside 
and outside of the gallery, in order to plan, schedule, and accomplish tasks, on-site 
and off-site locally, nationally, and internationally. They must demonstrate initiative 
and attention to detail in internal and external communications, fostering strong 
relationships with artists in particular, for comprehensive and accurate record-
keeping. They must be resourceful in researching and sourcing materials and 
equipment, and learning independently to execute, teach, and/or delegate processes 
for packing, shipping, and installation of works, noting the prevalence of unusual 
materials and technologies in contemporary art. As such, kindness and clarity in 
speaking and writing, a desire to learn and teach, and ability to problem solve and 
manage priorities of time, space, staff, and budget, are key to this position.

A background in visual art and/or a related discipline is essential; prior experience in 
an art gallery or museum is an asset.

The flexibility to travel nationally and internationally is required for art fair 
participation. A local class 5 drivers license is an asset.

The successful candidate must have general knowledge of Microsoft Office, 
including Word and Excel for communications and import/export to databases; an 
understanding of data entry and linking records in a relational database is an asset.

Woodworking experience and safe operation of basic power tools, including table 
saw, chop saw, drills, is required; other tool knowledge is an asset.

How to Apply:
Please send a cover letter detailing your qualifications and experience with 
contemporary art, and your resume, in Word or PDF format by e-mail to 
gallery@catrionajeffries.com, subject ATTN: Head Preparator and Registrar, by 
Friday, May 4, at midnight.

Remuneration:
$50-55,000/year, commensurate with experience

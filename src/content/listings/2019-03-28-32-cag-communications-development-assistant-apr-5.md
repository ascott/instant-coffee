Job opening at the Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver
Communications & Development Assistant
Deadline : April 5, 2019
www.contemporaryartgallery.ca/about/opportunities

The Contemporary Art Gallery is seeking a Learning and Public 
Engagement Assistant to provide assistance with Learning and Public 
Programming initiatives including supporting the planning and delivery 
of a three week Summer Intensive for youth ages 15-19 in partnership 
with Arts Umbrella, and will assist with public programs related to the 
current exhibitions. The Learning and Public Engagement Assistant will 
perform these duties during a 14 week period at 35 hours per week. 
We offer a flexible work week with some evenings and weekends 
required. The position will be conducted in English and will begin May 
27 2019 and end August 30 2019.

This summer student position is part of Young Canada Works. 
Applicants must be a full-time student in the semester immediately 
preceding June, returning to school full-time in the fall and be between 
16 and 30 years old at the start of employment to apply. Eligible 
applicants must also be Canadian citizens, permanent residents or 
have official refugee protection status, be legally eligible to work in BC, 
not have another fulltime job (30+hours/week) and be registered in the 
YCW online candidate inventory (www.youngcanadaworks.gc.ca).

Please note that this position is subject to funding approval from Young 
Canada Works.

Download the application pack at

50: Half a Century of UNIT/PITT
and
UNIT is U Publication Launch
November 8, 2025 - February 15, 2026

Opening event:
November 7, 2025, 7-11PM
2954 West 4th Ave

!! U/P turns 50 !!

You are formally invited to the opening night of 50: Half a Century of UNIT/PITT, and
the launch of the Unit is U 50th anniversary publication on November 7, 7-11PM.

Consider this a 50th birthday bash, and the first chance to help us break in our newly
renovated Kitsilano space.

The evening will feature live audio and visual archival interventions by Kaila Bhullar
and Jefferson Alade, reimagining artist david george morgans extensive audio and
video archive of the Pitt of the Past. Then, Jefferson will carry us through the
evening with open decks"sign up form to come!

50 will be open for viewing from November 8, 2025 - February 15, 2026 with gallery
hours on Tuesday - Friday: 12 - 5pm and Saturday: 11 - 4pm. The exhibition will
provide an opportunity to witness a glut of historical records from our extensive
archive, documenting Vancouvers early artist-run culture from 1975 onwards. The
exhibition will be accompanied by a special events series, including a Visioning
Palestine Revisited gathering hosted by Noor Ghazal, a collage workshop with Sam
Olsen, a film night hosted by XINEMA, and the first of our recurring monthly jazz
night c.a.f.f (calling all friendly faces) curated by Ben Frost.

November 7 will also be the launch of our 50th Anniversary publication UNIT is U,
designed by the brilliant Shafira Vidyamaharani. With contributions from Dana
Claxton, Reece Metcalf, Katayoon Yousefbigloo, Emma Sommers, Kay Higgins, and
many more, UNIT is U presents an essential history (and future) of UNIT/PITT
through musings and mishaps spanning five decades of the organizations particular
brand of messy, oft absurd, yet fiercely community-minded artist-run culture.

Presales for UNIT is U are available now, with discounts available for U/P members

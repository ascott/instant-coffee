The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC invites you to join master weaver Jessica 
Silvey for a one-day cedar basket weaving workshop on Saturday, December 9, 2017 
from 10-4pm, and learn about the cultural significance and traditional techniques of 
Coast Salish weaving.

Using the cedar that was harvested and prepared by Jessica herself, participants will get 
to take home their own cedar basket creations. Jessica is a self-taught weaver of Coast 
Salish and Portugese descent of the Silvey family in Egmont, BC. The workshop is 
organized in conjunction with MOAs newest exhibit, The Fabric of Our Land: Salish 
Weaving.

Admission: Adults $110, Youth/Student/Senior $100, MOA Members, UBC Students or 
Faculty $90

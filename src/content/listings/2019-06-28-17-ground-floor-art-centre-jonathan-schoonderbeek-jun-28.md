Speculative Survival Scenarios: An Exhibition of Dystopian Design & Alarmist 
Architecture

By Jonathan Schoonderbeek
Curated by India Eliot Oates

Opening night: 
Friday, June 28, 6 - 10 pm

Open gallery hours:
June 29 - July 5, 12 - 5pm

***************************
The reign of capitalism as the prevailing way of life is coming to an end, either willingly 
or from a complete ecological and societal collapse. Our collective way of life as we 
know it is about to change, one way or another.

This work is designed with the intention to explore the challenges arising from self 
sufficient survival in a post capitalist landscape; one that is stripped bare of its natural 
bounty, and polluted with the detritus of our disposable culture. 

***************************
Jonathan Schoonderbeeks artistic practice reflects his desire to develop new paradigms 
regarding new, old, global and local issues. In particular, he is concerned with the global 
ecological effects of anthropocentrism, and the present and future ramifications for life 
on earth. 

In the case of Speculative Survival Scenarios, Jonathan explored sea level rise and its 
potential effects on our ability to navigate the terrain, forage, grow food, construct 
shelter, and survive. 

Im aware that there are no design solutions for every problem; most problems that truly 
need solving have no clear solutions, let alone ones that are governed by design. 
Therefore, I present this methodology of investigative and speculative design not as a 
solution, but as an indication of a set of problems. 
-Jonathan Schoonderbeek.

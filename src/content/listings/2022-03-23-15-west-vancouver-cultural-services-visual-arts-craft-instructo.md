The District of West Vancouver Cultural Services Department is accepting applications
for Visual Arts (Artisanal Craft) Instructors to teach a variety of craft classes. These
include, but
are not limited to: weaving, fabric arts, stained glass, basket weaving,
felting, leather work and jewelry instructors. The lessons will be based out of the West
Vancouver Community Centre, and/or the Gleneagles Community Centre, and/or the
Music Box, located at John Lawson Park.

The successful candidates will have professional training in the visual arts, experience
as instructors within their respective field, knowledge of community programming
philosophies and instruction techniques, and experience working with a variety of age
groups. Desirable qualifications include a degree or diploma in Visual Art, or an
equivalent combination of education and experience.

All applicants must provide a cover letter, resume, and a sample lesson plan for each
class or program to be taught. The successful candidate is required to supply a police
information check with vulnerable sector and a copy of their credentials. All District of
West Vancouver employees are required to be fully vaccinated
against COVID 19 as a
condition of hire.

Position Status: Non-Union " Casual Hours of Work: Days and hours of work may vary.

Rate of Pay: $27.00 - $30.00 per hour plus 4% in lieu of benefits, based on experience
and training

Closing Date: 11:59 p.m. on Sunday, March 27, 2022

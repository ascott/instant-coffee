Apply by: October 13, 2023.

Griffin Art Projects invites you to apply for the Emerging Indigenous Artist Studio
Residency & Mentorship with Artist Rolande Souliere, for February/March of 2024.

The Emerging Indigenous Artist Studio Award invites emerging Indigenous artists living in
(Metro Vancouver) the ancestral and unceded territories of the qi-"(Katzie), q':"
(Kwantlen), kik"m (Kwikwetlem), mthxwi (Matsqui), xmky"m (Musqueam),
qiqyt (Qayqayt), semyame (Semiahmoo), Swxw7mesh xwumixw (Squamish),
sc"wan msteyx (Tsawwassen) and sllwta" (Tsleil-Waututh) nations to apply.

This residency provides a subsidized 2 month residency in February and March of 2024
in a 350 square
foot studio space, along with mentorship from the recipient of the 2024
Established Indigenous Studio Art Award, visual artist Rolande Souliere. For this
opportunity, an emerging Indigenous artist is defined as an Indigenous artist who has not
yet exhibited in institutionally recognised art spaces.

More Info: https://www.griffinartprojects.ca/residency-opportunities

Collision
Shudder Gallery's first annual Christmas "functional art" group show

Lorenzo Giuffrè, Keith Jones, Mysa Kaczkowski, Hernan Kahs, Denver Lynxleg,
Rose-Marie Pickard, Susan Schroeder, Nick Smit, Nico Soule, Jason Statler,
Monika Valyi, Jason Ververgaert

Opening reception December 5th 7pm -11pm
Show runs December 6th-December 23rd

This show is a collection of work that stretches the boundaries between
"Fine Art" and the everyday object. A colliding of art and craft and the
places where the lines cross and meet. All the artists in the show will
also be offering up an original, one of a kind, Christmas card which will
be for sale at the opening.

Gallery hours for the December show are extended to Wednesday thru Sunday
12:30-5:30.

Shudder Gallery is located on 433 Columbia St between Pender and East
Hastings, Vancouver.

Gallery Administrative Assistant/ Receptionist job posting

February 5, 2009

Uno Langmann Limited Fine Art is an internationally recognized gallery and
is Canada's foremost specialist in the finest quality European and North
American paintings from the 18th, 19th, and early 20th century. The
gallery also showcases a careful selection of antiques. Both museums and
private collectors have come to rely on the gallery for a full range of
services relating to acquisitions, consignment, sales, appraisals,
conservation, and framing.

DESCRIPTION: Gallery Administrative Assistant/ Receptionist

We are seeking a highly motivated, organized and creative individual to
fill the full-time position of Administrative Assistant/Receptionist.

This position will work to facilitate the day-to-day administrative, retail
and research needs of the gallery. This position will also be responsible
for creating advertising, assisting with the organization of exhibitions,
special events and special projects.

The candidate must be skilled in time management and setting priorities in
a busy workplace and delivering support to other gallery staff. The hours
are Tuesday to Saturday 9:30am to 5:30pm.

QUALIFICATIONS:

- Post-secondary graduate with a major/minor in Art History, and an
interest in 17th, 18th, 19th century fine art.

- Fluent in English, both oral and written; some understanding of a second
language an asset, but not a requirement.

-Minimum 1 years experience working as a coordinator/assistant role in a
fast paced environment.

-An understanding of the fine art market.

-Strong communication and interpersonal skills.

-Excellent organization skills.

-Self Starter/ability to take ownership of projects and see through completion.

-Excellent computer/technical skills using outlook, database management,
Photoshop and excel.

- A combination of relevant education and professional experience may be
considered.

SALARY:

To be determined based on experience.

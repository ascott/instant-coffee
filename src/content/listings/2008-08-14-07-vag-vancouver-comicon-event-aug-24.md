Vancouver Comicon Event

Comix & Stories

Organized by Leonard Wong

Sunday, August 24, 11am to 5pm

at the Vancouver Art Gallery in the Annex, Room 301, and Room 302

Join us for Vancouver's annual alternative and small press comic show, featuring Kim Deitch, 
Derek Kirk Kim, Julie Morstad and over 50 other creators and publishers. The day is a showcase 
and marketplace for independent and alternative comics, graphic novels, chap books, zines, 
prints and original artwork. Please note that some of the material on display and for sale may not 
be suitable for all ages.

Local comic historian Leonard Wong is the organizer of Vancouver Comicon. Wong teaches 
English at Templeton Secondary School in Vancouver. He is one of the organizers of the Comic 
Legends Legal Defense Fund. He has been an integral part of the Vancouver comic scene for 
many years.

Free admission.

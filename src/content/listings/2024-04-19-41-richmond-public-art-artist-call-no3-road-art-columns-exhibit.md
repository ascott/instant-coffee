Richmond Public Art Program: Call to Artists, No.3 Road Art Columns

The City of Richmond Public Art Program seeks proposals from artists for the 2024-
2025 No.3 Road Art Columns Program. The exhibition will run from August 1, 2024
to January 31, 2025.

Three (3) commissioned artists will each receive an artist fee of $1,500 for four (4)
print-ready digital artwork files. Printing and installation costs will be the
responsibility of the City of Richmond. Artists with practices in 2D graphic arts,
including drawing, painting, printmaking, illustration, mixed-media collage,
photography, comics or graphic novels, to name a few are encouraged to apply.

Artist Fee: $1,500.00

Eligibility: Artists Residing or Working in Richmond

Submission
Deadline: Monday, May 6, 2024

For more information and to apply:
https://www.richmond.ca/culture/howartworks/publicart/artist-calls.htm

Opening Times - Digital Art Commissions 

www.otdac.org

Opening Times is a new not-for-profit, digital art commissioning body and 
platform for artworks whose primary means of encounter is online. 
Opening Times went live on the 2nd of June 2014 with a new commission by 
artist Ruth Proctor (UK); a research commission by artist and writer Nicholas 
O’Brien (US); a selection of work from resident artist Nicolas Sassoon (FR); and 
an online reading resource edited by curator and writer Karen Archey (US).

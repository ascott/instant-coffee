TALK & RECEPTION 
Reenacting Histories: Docufiction and the Work of Sara Diamond and the Women's 
Labour History Project

October 12, 2018 
Doors: 6:30pm. Talk: 7:00pm
Reception to follow
2625 Kaslo St, Vancouver, BC

Tickets
General Admission: $20 + fees
Students: $10 + fees
No one turned away for lack of funds. 

This event is a fundraiser in support of the Sara Diamond Archive at VIVO's Crista 
Dahl Media Library & Archive. 

In the 1980s and 1990s, performance, re-enactment, the melange of fiction and 
documentary, and "bad acting" were tactics used by video artists to unsettle notions 
of documentary realism, provide alternate readings of histories, elicit or manifest 
subjective interpretations of events, or provide a feminist sense of time. The work of 
Sara Diamond and the early work of the Women's Labour History Project used these 
tactics drawn from film theory, feminist criticism and documentary practice. Diamond 
will discuss these roots and draw from her works of personal 
documentary/autobiographical narratives and early docufiction. 

Sara Diamond is a data visualization, wearable technology and mobile media 
researcher, artist, critic, scientist, designer, and President of OCAD University. 
During her years in Vancouver, she co-founded the feminist media collective, Amelia 
Productions, founded the Womens Labour History Project, and worked as an 
educator/curator at VIVO for a decade before founding the Banff New Media Institute 
(1995).

Thursday, April 14, 2016 at 7PM
Join us at the Charles H. Scott Gallery to mark the end of Like a Rolling
Stone: An Exhibition About Rock and Rock with a live performance by
Experience This.

Experience This is a three-piece blues band, featuring Laura Piasta, an
artist participating in Like a Rolling Stone, along with members Jen Smyth
and Johan Bjrck. In addition to the performance, Vancouver-based artist,
Katrina Niebergal will DJ.

About the exhibition:
Like a Rolling Stone conflates geology and rock music, it considers the
ways in which two seemingly unrelated subjects linked by the same homograph
share associations and points of contact that are both concrete and
implied. The exhibition includes work by Kevin Romaniuk and Liam Hogan,
Slater Bradley, Sean Coggins, Nadja Frank, Myfanwy MacLeod, Ana Mendieta,
Dennis Oppenheim, Laura Piasta, Melanie Schiff.

Like a Rolling Stone: An Exhibition About Rock and Rock, which runs until
Sunday, April 17, 2014.

Sustaining Apertures | MAY 4
Sustaining Apertures: Artist Talk

Saturday, May 4, 2024

2:00-5:00PM

Rise Up Marketplace

Join artists Lys Divine Ndemeye and Colin Berg Mbugua at Rise Up Marketplace for
an artist talk and conversation on food cultivation, community organizing and
alternative architectural practices. Introduction and moderation by Or Gallery
Director, Jenn Jackson.

This artist conversation is presented in conjunction with the exhibition Sustaining
Apertures, on view at Or Gallery from March 7 to July 6, 2024.

This program is free and open to all. Registration is recommended but not required.

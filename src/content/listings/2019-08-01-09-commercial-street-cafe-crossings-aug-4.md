EXHIBITION
COMMERCIAL STREET CAFE
3599 COMMERCIAL STREET
AUGUST 4 - 25

Michael Cox: Crossings

Hours: 
0700 - 1700 weekdays and 0800 - 1700 weekends. The show runs August 3 to August 
25. While there isnt an opening per se, Ill be there SUNDAY AUGUST 4 in the morning. 

My photographs of crossings references people leaving and entering the frame, of 
voyages and elapsed time, and to our vision, biological and technological, the way we 
scan spaces. Photographs can be thought of as a techno-optical analogy to our eye's 
saccades, when we jump from one focus point to another.

The Neighbourhood Time Exchange is currently accepting 
submissions from artists based in Prince George and Northern BC 
who are interested in participating in a monthlong residency 
through a new artist residency program.

Located in downtown Prince George, the Neighbourhood Time 
Exchange operates on a simple principle: for every hour an artist 
spends in their studio, they provide an hour of volunteer service to 
the community. This residency uses the concept of time banking, in 
which artists and community members work together for mutual 
benefit through the exchange of temporal resources, as a platform 
for social, civic, and economic revitalization. In this model, artists 
will be able to dedicate half of their time to work on studio projects 
and half of their time collaborating with community partners, 
working on community projects.

Artists will have access to a studio in Prince George, be paid an 
artist fee, and provided with a budget for materials for both studio 
and community work.

For more details on the project, and for a complete list of items to 
be included in your application, please visit: 
[http://neighbourhoodtime.exchange/](http://neighbourhoodtime.exchange/)

The deadline for submissions is July 31st, 2016. Send inquiries and
completed applications by email to: 
info@neighbourhoodtime.exchange

Camera/whore has returned. The one night photoparty is back with a 
second round of 9 conceptual photobooths. You are recorded as you 
move through the installations, that will include photography, video, net 
art, performances, GIFs, pinhole cameras, webcams, green screens 
and more. Camera/whore is a one night art event that provides 
attendees with digital media of themselves, after the event, for free 
online.

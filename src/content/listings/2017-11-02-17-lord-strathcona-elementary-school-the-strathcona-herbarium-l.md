The Strathcona Herbarium Launch
Thursday November 9, 5.30-7pm
Lord Strathcona Elementary School
Classroom 203

Come down to teacher Maryann Persoons grade 6/7 classroom to join Coast Salish 
ethnobotanist, community gardener and interdisciplinary artist T'uy't'tanat Cease Wyss, 
the Lord Strathcona Elementary community, and our primary supporter, Telus 
Community Board, for the public launch and discussion of The Strathcona Herbarium.

This event is part of A New Path to the Waterfall, a CAG satellite gallery presenting 
exhibitions and events at Lord Strathcona Elementary School initiated by US artist 
Harrell Fletcher.

Adele Arseneaus piece "wskwpitkan: Sacred Bundle" consists of photographs of four
miniature cradleboards
and beaded moss bags taken from a larger series of 161
miniatures she created to acknowledge the children who were forcibly enrolled at one of
the 140 institutions that were listed in the Canadian Residential School Settlement
Agreement. The entire project took over two years to complete and in Adeles own
estimation required 5152 stitches, 35,000 beads and almost 7 animal hides. The work is
contemplative, a reclamation of traditional skills and values and a delicate offering from
Adele to her larger community still struggling with this colonial legacy. Her work will be
installed on transit shelters throughout the city until August 13th. You can learn more on
the City of Vancouvers website: https://vancouver.ca/platforms

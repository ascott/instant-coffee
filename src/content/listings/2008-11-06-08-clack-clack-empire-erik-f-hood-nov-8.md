Clack Clack Empire Presents:

Erik F. Hood

When I Sit, I Do Not Care

Opening Reception: 5-9pm Saturday November 8th
through Tuesday December 2nd, 2008

In the changing landscape of Vancouver there has been many periods of
redevelopment, growth, and transformation. Some structures have been
allowed, through an almost luck based existence, to remain and play
constant amidst the political, economic, and cultural shifts of our city.
Hood's exhibition chronicles the comings and goings of a building about to
celebrate its centennial.

Curated by Charlotte Matthews

To raise awareness and help educate the public, we are launching ReVision: the art of 
recycling - a non-profit society of artists dedicated to saving our environment who are 
partnering with the Burnaby Arts Council to present this explorative exhibition about 
recycling and art. ReVision artists create amazing artwork by upcycling waste materials 
and discarded objects.

During the exhibition, the Deer Lake Art Gallery is hosting several workshops in 
October! Here is the schedule:

Oct. 6, 12 -1:30 pm: Lori Goldberg workshop Session I on art from waste plastics

Oct. 6, 2-3:30 pm: Lori Goldberg workshop Session II on art from waste plastics

Oct. 19, 2-4 pm: Abby Greene Bull - FouWornd Sound Drum circle with found 
objects

Oct. 20, 2-4 pm: Workshop on making Eco-Fashion, artist TBA

Each Workshop Session has $5 fee, each class is limited to 25 seats.

For the workshops on Oct. 6th and 20th, all participants MUST bring their own recycled 
materials.

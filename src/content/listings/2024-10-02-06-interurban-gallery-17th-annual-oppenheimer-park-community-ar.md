17th Annual Oppenheimer Park Community Art Show
Gallery Gachet Offsite at InterUrban Gallery, 1 E Hastings St
September 26-October 19, Wed"Sat 12"4pm

The Oppenheimer Park Community Art Show
features artwork and insight of artists and
community leaders connected to or displaced from Oppenheimer Park, the green space
known as Lek'lek, the Powell Street Grounds, Paureu gai, the backyard of the
Downtown Eastside; the unceded land of the xmky"m (Musqueam),
Swxw7mesh (Squamish) and slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) peoples. For some artists,
participating in the show is an annual tradition, while others are contributing for the first
time.

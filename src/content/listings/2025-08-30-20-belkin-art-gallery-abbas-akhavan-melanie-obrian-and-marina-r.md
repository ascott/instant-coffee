Conversation with Abbas Akhavan, Melanie O'Brian and Marina Roy

Saturday, September 6 at 2 pm

Join us for a conversation with exhibiting artist Abbas Akhavan, curator Melanie
OBrian and artist Marina Roy on the current exhibition Abbas Akhavan: One
Hundred Years. The conversation will approach ways of working and relationality.
Having received his MFA from the UBC Department of Art History, Visual Art and
Theory in 2006, how does Akhavans return to a place two decades later inflect on
the exhibition? How do the works operate in relation to one another, as well as
respond to site?

Taking up notions of suspended time, Abbas Akhavans exhibition One Hundred
Years contends with temporal halting and how time is represented in narrativized
spaces. Weighing the narrative drive of storytelling against the emptying out of
content or the freezing of narrative, Akhavans work simultaneously activates a stage
and presses pause

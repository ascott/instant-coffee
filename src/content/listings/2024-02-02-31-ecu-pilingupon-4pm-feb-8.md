PILING/UPON

SARA-JEANNE BOURGET / MIRA L D'SOUZA / MARK JOHNSEN / WILL PRICE

A collaborative print-based exhibition.

February 5-16, 2024

Opening reception: Thursday, February 8th, 4-6pm

Emily Carr University of Art + Design " Zone 4 Gallery (1st floor)

PILING/UPON is a research project centering on printmaking, collaboration, and
material experimentation. Starting in the summer of 2023, four participating artists
have actively gathered and printed discarded and trashed objects - leftovers from the
many creative activities taking place on the campus of Emily Carr University. The
resulting exhibition draws inspiration from scientific methods of capturing data and
actively aims to legitimize printmaking as a visual tool to record time and
experiences. The piling represents the
accumulation of physical debris while
hinting at the [compressed] piles hidden beneath the ground.

This exhibition is the culmination of a SSHRC Institutional Grant.

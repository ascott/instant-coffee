Andreas Bunte Artist Talk
Oct 1, 6 pm
Goldcorp Centre for the Arts
149 W. Hastings Street, Vancouver
FREE

Andreas Bunte is a Berlin-based artist who has, over the past years worked
within the format of short experimental film and film installations, which
frequently incorporate a variety of other media such as collages,
architectural structures, sound, texts.

Deformation of Glass: Roundtable discussion with Andreas Bunte
Oct 8, 6:30 pm
Goldcorp Centre for the Arts
149 W. Hastings Street, Vancouver
FREE

With Christopher Pavsek and Judy Radul
Andreas Bunte will show a selection of clips from scientific research films
and related visual material. For Bunte, films and videos made for
scientific research escape certain conventional languages of cinema and
open conversations into media’s role in “empirical observation." The
discussion will consider the films in relation to their pretense of being
pure visual information.

Andreas Bunte's Audain Visual Artist in Residence is in partnership with
the Or Gallery and Republic Gallery, Vancouver and SFU Galleries.

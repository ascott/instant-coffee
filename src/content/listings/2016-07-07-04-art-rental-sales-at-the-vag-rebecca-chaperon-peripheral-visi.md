Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery
Rebecca Chaperon | Peripheral Visions
July 4 through September 23, 2016

Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents Peripheral 
Visions, with new works by Rebecca Chaperon.

Peripheral Visions is a show of works by Rebecca Chaperon that 
encompasses pieces from three distinctive bodies of work: Imprint, 
Tesseract and Antarticus. A peripheral vision is something seen out 
of the corner of the eye, on the outer edge of your visual 
perception. The work in Peripheral Visions revolves around how we 
perceive the mysterious world around us and our connection to it. 
Metaphysical spaces are presented as possible alternate 
dimensions, symbolic interpretations or enigmatic escape hatches, 
with the understanding that the space we exist in is more 
mysterious than the limits of our perception.

Rebecca Chaperon's process often begins with the idea of place. 
Her paintings act as a means of storytelling, as landscapes meet 
flat geometry and emotive undercurrents. Chaperon's work will be 
featured in the showroom July 4 - September 23, 2016.

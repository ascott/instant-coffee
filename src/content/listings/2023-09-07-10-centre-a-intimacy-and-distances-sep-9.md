Centre A welcomes Intimacy and Distances, Maiko Jinushis first solo exhibition in
Canada. Curated by Makiko Hara, this exhibition features four short video works and
drawings.

Here is an excerpt from the curatorial statement:

The relationship between self and others are unraveled through the seemingly
contradictory keywords "Intimacy and Distances, reflecting the physical and
emotional distance experienced during the Corona pandemic, to explore intimate
relationships through digital communication with others

Created throughout the time of the Coronavirus pandemic beginning in the spring of
2020, Jinushis short videos explore the desire for the possibility of exchanging energy
with others through the video camera. The invisible and layered distancing that
emerges opens diverse interpretations, through poetic fragments of the realities we
face in our daily lives. This exhibition offers, through interaction with Vancouver's
diverse art community, a forum for dialogue about how art addresses how we can
reconstruct new relationships between self and others in an uncertain and transforming
contemporary society.

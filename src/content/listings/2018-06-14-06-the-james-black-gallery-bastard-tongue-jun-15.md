EVENT: June 15th 6PM Bastard Tongue @ The James Black Gallery

Bastard Tongue is an experiment in formal language. What do all these
disparate artists from the accepted capital-A Art Canon have to say to one
another when we strip away all semblance of emotion, history, story? What
happens when we reduce the suffering/joy/pain/triumph of a painting to it's
bare formal elements, divorced from the human hand that produced it? When
the form is brought to it's barest, what does it say about it's content?

Michael Mateyko / KOMBOH is a visual artist from Singapore / Alberta and
works primarily in the space between commercial illustration and fine art.
His work often revolves around the tension between tragedy and comedy,
attempts to make sense of an absurd life, and bringing levity to otherwise
dour situations. A frequent contributor to galleries across North America,
Michael graduated from the Alberta College of Art and Design in 2009 and
resides in Vancouver, British Columbia.

http://www.komboh.com/

CALL: QUICKIE DUE JUNE 30TH

THE JAMES BLACK GALLERY is dedicated to providing space for emerging
artists to experiment, create, and show their work. This curation is by
Zandi Dandizette. The J B G is located in a heritage building in Mount
Pleasant (est. 1889). This exhibition will show projects of emerging local
artists in a variety of formats including media, installation, and
painting. Any sales of work goes 100% to the artist.

QUICKIE
definition: something done or made in a hurry: such as a quickly and
usually cheaply produced work or a hastily performed act of sexual intercourse.

In a society that's so fast, the next viral moment can already be over by
the time you notice... artists are formally invited to engage with instant
gratification in QUICKIE. Sketches or gifs, fast food or fast fashion, that
minor glance or a quick blow and go. Celebrate that lapse in judgement or
indulgent reprieve.

What is your take on this ability to get our needs met at the speed we do?

Explorations can be conceptual or process based. Experimentation is welcome.

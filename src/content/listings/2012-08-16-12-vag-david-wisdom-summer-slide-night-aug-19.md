EVENT: David Wisdom: Summer Slide Night
DATE: Sunday, August 19th
TIME: 7pm
LOCATION: Vancouver Art Gallery, 750 Hornby Street

The Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to welcome former CBC radio 
personality, writer and artist David Wisdom back for another round of 
his legendary slide shows. Have a drink on the Gallery's Rooftop 
Pavilion and enjoy unique perspectives on art, music and life in the city. 
Featuring a special performance by The Rodney Graham Band.

Free for Members or with Gallery admission.
**Limited capacity; arrive early!
Cash bar available

Featuring:

Aaron Carpenter
Ruth Skinner
Doug Lander
Deirdre Hofer
Brad Phillips
Jamie Dolinko
Barrie Jones
Connie Kuhns
David Wisdom

Live music by The Rodney Graham Band

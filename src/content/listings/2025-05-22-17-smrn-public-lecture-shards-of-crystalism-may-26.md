Shards of Crystalism: A Decolonial Media Archaeology of Deleuzes
Crystal Image

Speaker: Dr. Radek Przedpeski (Trinity College Dublin)
Moderator: Dr. Laura U. Marks
Date & Time: Monday, May 26, 10:00 AM (Vancouver time)
Location: Online via Zoom

We are pleased to invite you to SMRNs third public lecture, featuring
Dr. Radek Przedpeski. In this talk, Dr. Przedpeski will present a
decolonial media archaeology of Gilles Deleuzes crystal image from
Cinema 2: The Time Image. Revisiting Deleuzes reference to Krzysztof
Zanussis film Structure of Crystal (1969), the talk explores how
Eastern European and African cinematic and artistic traditions,
particularly Polish cine crystallisations and Sudanese Crystalist
conceptualism, construct an alternative genealogy of sustainable media
rooted in socialist contexts. This decolonial perspective reframes the
crystal image and forges new connections between Eastern European and
African practices.

The lecture will be followed by a conversation with Dr. Laura U. Marks
and a Q&A session.

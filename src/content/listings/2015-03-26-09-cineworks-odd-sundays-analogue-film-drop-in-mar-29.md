Cineworks Annex, Ironworks, 235 Alexander Street
(basement, lane entrance: please ring the bell to the right of the gate)

Open Film Lab and Analogue Studio Drop-In

Sunday Mar 29, 10.00am – 2.00pm

FREE

Odd Sundays are open studio and darkroom events at The Annex - Cineworks'
analogue film facility, focusing on hand-, tank-, and machine-based film
processing, optical and contact printing, animation, installation and
exhibition.

Come for an Annex equipment and facilities orientation, bring your own
cinefilm or stills negative to develop, come for a chat if you'd like to
know more about working with 35mm, 16mm and 8mm film, or if you’d like
advice on a project you have in mind.

(Odd Sundays take place on Sundays with odd-numbered dates).

For further details on additional Annex orientations and services please
contact Annex Coordinator Alison Burdeny:annex@cineworks.ca

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society (est.1980) is an artist-run
production and exhibition centre that supports independent filmmakers and
media artists. We engage our membership and the broader community through
initiatives and services that foster dialogue and experimentation with the
moving image.

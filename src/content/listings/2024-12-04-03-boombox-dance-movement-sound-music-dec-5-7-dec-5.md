Yasmine + Derya Whaley-Kalaora
We're thinking around the repetition and work of rehabilitation/mending after physical
and interpersonal injury. This project came out of boxtime as a reason to spend time
and relearn alongside each other through our respective movement and sound
practices.
//
A little about the artists:
Derya Whaley-Kalaora (she/her): dance/movement
Yasmine Whaley-Kalaora (she/they): sound
//
Derya Whaley-Kalaora is a singer, songwriter, scientist, and musician coming home
to movement after a long time away.
Yasmine Whaley-Kalaora is an artist/writer/archivist whose practice currently
encompasses video, experimental audio, writing, and curating.

Jarah + Isaiah Terrel- Dobbs
Presenting: Lava.
A combination improv, original pieces, and covers.
//
A bit a bout Lava:
Lava is a band. The band is Isaiah and Jarah, two brothers. A musical band that
plays really good music.

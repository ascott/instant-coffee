Spend a day at the beach with Vancouvers creative community! On Sunday, July 30 Josh
Byer will be hosting his third complimentary art event in 2023 " an art party and picnic at
Second Beach from noon to 4pm. Mr. Byer believes in the fundamental power of art to
foster community, friendship and goodwill and is dedicated to providing free-of-charge
creative events throughout the Lower Mainland and beyond.

Join us at the picnic table area under the big
trees, where everyone is invited to draw,
paint, chat and chill while meeting other artistically inclined Vancouverites. Bring a
sketchbook, a canvas, or help yourself to the complimentary paints and paper available
for anybody who wishes to sketch or paint. Josh will be available to demonstrate his Faux
Fauvist painting technique and is happy to provide free art instruction to anyone who
requests it.

Recognized as a new modern art movement by the Saatchi Gallery, MoMA San Francisco
and ArtFinder, Joshs Faux Fauvist style attempts to create a cloudgazing effect by
exploring elements of visual cognition. Over 1000 artworks have been completed to date
" appearing seven times on the front page of Reddit, exhibiting internationally alongside
the work of Matisse, Picasso, Dali, and The Group of Seven. Most recently, Joshs
artwork was featured in the Hallmark movies It Happened at Christmas, Marry Go
Round and Game of Love.

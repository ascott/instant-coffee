1.
The Sound I'm Looking For
Part 2

Brady Cranfield
Brian Joseph Davis
Ceal Floyer
Luke Fowler
Holly Ward

26 November 2008 to 18 January 2009
Opening 25 November at 7:30 pm
Charles H. Scott Gallery

The Charles H. Scott Gallery is pleased to present the second part of the
two part show titled The Sound I'm Looking For. The exhibition features
sound-based work by Canadian and international artists curated by Cate
Rimmer. Working with a variety of media, these artists use music, the urban
soundscape, and the play between the presence and absence of sound as their
subject matter.

2.
PAULINE OLIVEROS
ARTIST TALK
27 November 2008 at 7pm
ECU Theatre

Contemporary music legend Pauline Oliveros will speak about her practice.
Oliveros is an internationally renowned composer and performer. Over her
fifty-year career, she has profoundly influenced contemporary music through
her innovative work with improvisation, electronic music, meditation, myth
and ritual. She is founder and president of the Deep Listening Institute,
Ltd., and Distinguished Research Professor of Music at the Rensselaer
Polytechnic Institute in Troy, NY.

The Pauline Oliveros talk is presented in collaboration with the Western
Front New Music Programme.

3.
BRADY CRANFIELD
PERFORMANCE
The Beatles vs The Rolling Stones
3 December 2008 at 7:30pm
ECU Theatre

Brady Cranfield invites high-school students from Collingwood School to
debate the age old rock and roll question, Who's better, the Beatles or
the Rolling Stones

Brady Cranfield is a Vancouver-based artist, writer, and musician. He has
had solo exhibitions at the Contemporary Art Gallery and the Western Front.
Recently he collaborated with Jamie Hilder to produce the exhibition Island
Developments at Artspeak Gallery. Cranfield & Slade: 12 Sun Songs, a
concept album made in collaboration with Kathy Slade, is forthcoming from
Christoph Keller Editions for JRP|Ringier (Zurich) and the Or Gallery in 2009.

Cranfield is a member of several bands and is founding co-director of The
Music Appreciation Society. His work is included in The Sound I'm looking
For Part 2.

The ECU Theatre is located in the South Building, Room 301 of the Emily
Carr University of Art and Design, 1399 Johnston Street, Vancouver

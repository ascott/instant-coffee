GLORY – A video installation by Alexander Tedlie-Stursberg
Live score performance by Midden 9pm

Screening 8-11pm, Friday September 18th, 2015
Crying Room Project Space – NEW LOCATION
1202 East Pender Street, Vancouver BC

I hate letting my teammates down. I know I'm not going to make every shot.
Sometimes I try to make the right play, and if it results in a loss, I feel
awful. I don't feel awful because I have to answer questions about it. I
feel awful in that locker room because I could have done something more to
help my teammates win. - Lebron James

Alexander Tedlie-Stursberg presents a new video installation with a live
musical score by ambient artist Midden. Working with assemblage using
cultural iconography Stursberg looks at relationships of value in society,
demonstrating their interconnectivity and dependency.

Curated by Colleen Heslin

Midden is an ambient noise project by musician Evan Hardy from Vancouver, BC.

Alexander Tedlie-Stursberg is a mixed media artist based in Glasgow and
Vancouver. With a BA in Political Science and a BFA from Simon Fraser
University, Stursberg is currently an MFA candidate at Glasgow School of
Art. Stursberg has exhibited in North America and Europe.

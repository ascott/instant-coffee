APPLY BY: MAY 13th 2024 @ 11:59pm

If you are passionate about media arts and interested in helping guide
the future of
the sector, please consider joining the MAAP board of directors!

The Media Arts Alliance of the Pacific (MAAP) is a growing non-profit arts service
organization dedicated to promoting media arts across the Pacific region, particularly
the contributions of Indigenous artists. We are a community of media arts
professionals and media artists across British Columbia and The Yukon working
together to improve the conditions for media art creation, presentation, and
distribution in our region.

MAAP has a working board that collaborates closely with staff to further the
organizations vision and mission. The Boards role is to guide MAAP, advise and
support staff, and connect the organization to media arts communities. The Board
consists of individuals with diverse skills, knowledge, experience and perspectives
that contribute to MAAPs vision of a diverse, equitable, and sustainable media arts
community.

We welcome new board members to share their experiences and perspective in
a
collaborative and welcoming environment. No previous Board experience necessary.

For more information or to apply, visit [https://www.maap.ca/join-our-board](https://www.maap.ca/join-our-board).

Morning Sun Garden
Aracha Cholitgul
Thursday, June 15th, 6 - 9pm

nap is pleased to present new works by Thai artist Aracha Cholitgul in her Canadian
debut solo exhibition, Morning Sun Garden. Through considering the ecology of the
house of which nap gallery is located in and its many human and non-human
relationships, Morning Sun Garden becomes an exploration into complex desires that
arise from loneliness. Guided by
intimately honest conversations between the artist
and the resident of nap on personal dilemmas surrounding endings, pets, and
companionship, the gallery is transformed into an offering for all neighborhood cats
who regularly visit, but also an invitation to consider the value of human/non-human
relationships, the complex dynamics of ownership, and of human/non-human needs
and of being needed. Featuring a giant litter box, a selection of drawings depicting
plants preferred by cats such as Thyrsostachys siamensis (otherwise known as
Gamble Cat Bamboo), a video piece, and sculptures made with cats enjoyment in
mind, Arachas visual sensitivity comes together through Morning Sun Garden,
becoming an expression of longing across distances.

Aracha Cholitgul was born in 1988 in Bangkok, Thailand (currently living and working
on Koh Phangan.) She has been writing, drawing, painting, and telling stories relating
to obscure feelings. Cholitgul has had high myopia since she was young (her mother
took her to see an eye doctor because she always walked into things.) As a result, the
idea of blurriness and uncertainty interests her. Her project is often an attempt to
observe and explore what goes on in the mental sphere.

Cholitgul received her Bachelor of Fine Arts from the Chulalongkorn University,
Bangkok, Thailand in 2010 with a concentration in ceramics. Cholitguls selected solo
exhibitions include The Study of A Long Distance Relationship (2021) and Museum of
The Swamp Eels (2018), both at Nova Contemporary, Bangkok, Thailand. Her works
have been exhibited in a number of galleries and institutions mainly throughout Asia
such as C U R RENT, UP at Vargas Museum, Manila, Philippines (2022); PLANT TIME
at 69 Campus Art Center, Beijing, China (2021); They Have Brought Erasers With
Them at A+ Works of Art, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (2020); Long March Project: The
Deficit Faction at Long March Space, Beijing, China (2019); Tree of Life, a solo
presentation at ART021 Shanghai
Contemporary Art Fair, Shanghai, China (2019); The
Last Two Digits at graw bckler garage, Berlin, Germany (2019); and Art Jakarta with
Nova Contemporary, Jakarta, Indonesia (2019).

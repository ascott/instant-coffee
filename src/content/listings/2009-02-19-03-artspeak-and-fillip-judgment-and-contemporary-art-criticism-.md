Judgment and Contemporary Art Criticism: Reading Room
Opening reception Friday, February 20, 8pm
Reading room runs from February 14 to March 28, 2009
Artspeak, 233 Carrall Street, Vancouver, BC
Organized by Artspeak and Fillip

As a complement to the forum being held at ECU on February 27 and 28,
Artspeak is housing a reading room of texts, publications, and journals
related to issues of judgment in contemporary art criticism. The reading
room builds on Artspeak's ONSITE programming, allowing a space for study,
conversation, and information.

A workbook including detailed information about the forum and panelists, as
well as a selection of reprinted texts related to issues of valuation and
art criticism, is available free at the reading room. The workbook
functions as a primer for the forum to encourage discussion and debate.

For more information please visit www.artspeak.ca or
www.judgmentandartcriticism.com

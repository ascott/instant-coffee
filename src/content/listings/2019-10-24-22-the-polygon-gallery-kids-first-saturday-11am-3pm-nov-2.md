Through the exploration of film, performance and storytelling, Wael Shawky creates video works
that look at cultural and artistic identities in different ways. With this in mind, we will be creating
hinged shadow puppets using cardboard, paper fasteners
and wooden dowels, so that visitors
young and old will be able to put together moveable characters that can be manipulated in a
makeshift shadow puppet theatre. Parents are encouraged to bring their cell phones along, in
order to document the performances through short video clips.

The Polygon Gallery

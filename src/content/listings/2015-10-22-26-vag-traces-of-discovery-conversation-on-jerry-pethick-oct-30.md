Traces of Discovery: Conversations on Jerry Pethick
Jerry Pethick: Shooting the Sun/Splitting the Pie
With artists John Drury, Melissa Crenshaw and Buster Simpson

Friday October 30, 3 - 5:30pm
In the Gallery Annex, Courtroom 302
Free. No Gallery admission required.

Guest speakers explore the vast scope of Jerry Pethick's practice and
legacy. Artist and friend John Drury shares stories of Pethick's time at
Pilchuk Glass School, Melissa Crenshaw explains his contribution to
pioneering holography as an art medium and artist Buster Simpson speaks to
Pethick's ongoing artistic influence. Curator Grant Arnold moderates Q&A
with the presenters.

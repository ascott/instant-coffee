Parade and Cabaret
Saturday, July 14th 
5pm Parade - Route Starts at Gallery Gachet - 9 W Hastings
6pm Doors - Cabaret at Lost and Found Cafe - 33 W Hastings

We are harnessing amazing feats of madness in an evening of celebration as part of 
International Mad Pride Day. Beginning with a parade led by the fabulous Carnival Band 
and culminating with an evening of entertainment. You are invited to join the revelry. 
Well travel down Hastings to the Carnegie and back again before arriving at the Lost 
and Found Cafe where well keep the party going with our Annual Mad Pride Cabaret.

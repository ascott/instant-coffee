Date: Saturday, May 10, 2025, to Spring 2027

Event name: Cannery REcollections: Redefining Storytelling Through Objects

Time: Open Daily, 10 am to 5 pm

Address: 12138 Fourth Avenue, Richmond, BC V7E 3J1

Details: The Gulf of Georgia Cannery Society is proud to present Cannery
REcollections. An exhibit that reveals how objects serve as touchstones for storytelling
and connect us to people, places and moments that shaped Canadas West Coast
fishing industry. This exhibit will feature artefacts from the Cannerys collection that
have not been seen in over a decade! Celebrate with us at the exhibition opening on
Saturday, May 10th from 12:30pm to 2:30pm.

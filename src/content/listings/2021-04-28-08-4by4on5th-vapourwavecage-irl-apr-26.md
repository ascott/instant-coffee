Vapourwave.Cage IRL (by Vapourwave.Cage)
COVID-friendly solo window show at @4by4on5th, curated by James Koester.
4by4on5th is located near 5th and Main (234 E. Main st), across from NEC. April 26 - May 09
Technically viewable from the sidewalk 24 hours a day but you should probably go during daylight hours.

Vapourwave.Cage is a 2020 spring lockdown project that has since snowballed a little. The low-brow digital collages and gif art explore vaporwave subculture and intersect with celebrity buffoon Nicolas Cage, whose perennial meme-ability lends to the comedic surrealism of the work. True to the vaporwave aesthetic tradition, it creates a series of non-places, but also a new, more radically-aligned non-person in Vapourwave.Cage - the cop-hating, anti-capitalist, anti-imperialist version of Nicolas Cage we arent likely to get in this iteration of the universe.

Vapourwave.Cage IRL departs somewhat from its digital counterparts in its incorporation (and celebration) of a very real
place, however: The Pickle Palace. A beautiful crumbling teardown home located in Dunbar, the artist Banana Sam lived there for three years with many others (both human and otherwise). Built in the 1950s and never renovated to match the times, this blast from the past of a house was the first place of safe and comfortable refuge for the artist in 18 years. The Palace was more than the physical house, however, populated with art(ists), music(ians) and trouble(makers), local flora and fauna like mould and ants that were eating the houses timber As these things go, the house was slated for demolition and we had to recently move out, which has been a bit traumatic for our dear artist. The demolition has since been stalled, the houses life extended until who-knows-when and for who-knows-whom, but the Pickle Palace is no more.

Pieces of the Palace have been woven into Vapourwave.Cage IRL as a means of preserving its memory while creating something new, honouring its impact in my life and
that of others. - Banana Sam

Vapourwave.Cage is a project by locally-based artist Banana Sam, and Vapourwave.Cage IRL is their first solo exhibition. Their work previously appeared in group shows around so-called BC, Detroit and Kosovo. This show was curated by artist James Koester, who started the 4by4on5th window exhibition series in 2020.

King Street Alternative Film and Video Festival

On February 8th and 9th, 2013, the second Winter Edition of
the King Street Alternative Film and Video Festival will take
place at Betty's.

We are putting out an open call to any independent filmmakers
or video artists with works between 5-20 minutes to show at
these screenings. Toronto and GTA works will be given
preference during the submission process.

All-genres and subject matters are welcome.

Past screenings have been praised by such media outlets as
Blog TO and Snap Magazine.

If interest pleased send a Youtube or Vimeo link to
mjjrooney@gmail.com

Deadline: Jan 29th, 2013

For more info see

http://kingsteetaltfilmfest.blogspot.ca/

Jeff Hallbauer - Fruits

Chernoff Fine Art
13 September - 12 October
OPENING RECEPTION: Friday, September 13, 6-8pm

Hallbauers paintings are plural, shifting between abstraction and representation. This
exhibition Fruits" focuses on a body of work that straddles these lines, with biomorphic
depictions of plants insects and animals, vis vis digital technologies which are
employed to make new kinds of hybrid abstractions. The paintings, which ideologically
demonstrate within them pure colour, are supported by Thierry de Duves treatise on
painting as a readymade, using oil pigments straight out of the tube, effectively
representing bright, un-muddied, true to their material source colours in the rainbow
spectrum. The paintings are intended to read queer, positioned by early debates
between disegno (drawing) and colore (colour) in the late 1600s. Colour was defined as
threatening the dignity of painting, and was seen unessential and secondary by the
Poussinists. Drawing, on the other hand, used light and was perceived and praised for
using a divine model, creating shadows mimicking Biblical descriptions of Gods world
creation story. Alluding to the historical political undertones present within contemporary
painting.

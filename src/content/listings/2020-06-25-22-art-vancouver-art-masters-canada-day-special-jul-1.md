Title: Art Masters: Canada Day Special
Day: July 1st, 2020
Times: 12pm - 1pm (competition time)
Location: Lot 19, 855 W Hastings Street, Vancouver, V6C3N9
Description:
On Wednesday July 1st from 12pm " 1pm, the Vancouver Visual Art Foundation
(VVAF) will be merging their two events Art Downtown and Art Masters in celebration
of Canada Day, in the heart of downtown Vancouver at Lot 19.

Art Masters is an art competition in which 8 professional artists create a piece of
artwork based on a theme (which will be announced on the day), using one mystery
toolbox filled with unconventional painting tools. They will have just one hour to
create a piece of art based on this theme, requiring them to unleash their creative
talents
and imagination within an exciting environment.

Following this, the audience will be asked to vote for their favourite piece based on
its creativity and originality, and a winner will be announced. The artworks will then
be auctioned off, with proceeds going to the Vancouver Visual Art Foundation.

There will be live music after the competition, and artists will be exhibiting and selling
their artwork to the public until 2pm.

We hope you can join us in celebrating Canada Day while supporting local artists!

This event is weather dependent, please check our Facebook page for updates.

Summer ARTventure: Exhibition for Children and Families is the ACT Art Gallerys
exhibition focused on childrens engagement. This exhibition is playful, experimental,
and especially created for family and childrens enjoyment during the summer
months. The artworks in Summer ARTventure are designed by professional artists
for hands-on engagement and play, and/or selected for visual appeal for a
young
audience.

Summer ARTventure features artists Brenna Maag, Brianne Siu, Kristian Adam,
Madeleine Chaffee and Tammy Routley. Visitors will experience the wonder of the
camera obscura, interactive ceramics, a floor puzzle game, an under-the-sea
inflatable seating area, and a series of paintings featuring curious characters. Beat
the summer heat with a cool artventure!

Render Presents: The 2016 Prism Prize at Vancity Theatre

Please join us for the screening of the Prism Prize's Top 20 Best
Canadian Music Video Awards at the Vancity Theatre on April 11, 2016
at 7pm. This is presented by Render: Vancouver's 3rd Annual
Vancouver International Music Video Festival.

The Prism Prize is a national, juried award established to recognize the
artistry of the modern music video in Canada.

After viewing hundreds of music videos released in 2015, a jury of
more than 120 Canadian music, film and media arts professionals has
selected the Prism Prize Top 10 based on originality, creativity, style,
innovation and effective execution. These jurors are then charged with
the task of crowning one video as the winner of the annual Prism Prize,
which carries with it a substantial cash reward.

You the Jury: The Audience Award is voted on by the public at
prismprize.com. The award was created to highlight the favourite
Canadian music video of the year amongst music video lovers, and you
will be eligible to vote after this evening's show.

In addition to screening the Top 10 votable videos, we will be screening
the videos that made it into the Top 20.

The screening and opportunity to vote is brought to you by Render:
Vancouver's International Music Video Festival.

Consign with us!

Centre A: Vancouver International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art is now
accepting submissions to add to the unique selection of works by local
artists and
creators offered in our gallery bookstore and giftshop, the Centre A Boutique!

Have your creations displayed and sold alongside works by emerging and
established artists from all over the Lower Mainland while supporting gallery
operations and exhibitions! Artists will receive 60% of the retail price of sold items on
a quarterly basis, plus a Centre A membership and online exposure when you sign a
consignment agreement with us.

Zines, art books, ceramics, socks, totes, jewelry, postcards you make it, well sell
it! Your creations will be debuted as part of our newly renovated boutique space. Join
us for the upcoming reopening, details coming soon.

Space is limited and only select submissions will be contacted.

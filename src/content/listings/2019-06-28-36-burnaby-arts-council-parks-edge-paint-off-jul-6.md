Parks Edge cannot wait for you to join us for the 4th Annual Parks Edge Paint Off in 
Burnaby British Columbia!

3-2-1. and paint. The final words one often hears when entering the colour 
massacres of live painting competitions. Usually a short amount of time, a palette of 
colours, brush in hand and an audience of eager onlookers excited about what creations 
lay in store from the competitors. Vancouver has seen a plethora of new live painting 
events pop up in recent years; from Art Battle, Golden Brushs Battle of the Brush, 
SNAG, Vancouver Art Attack and others they have all become a unique way for art 
lovers and creators to come together and get closer to the creative process. Mingle that 
with an emphasis on speed and the chance for prizes and recognition and its a recipe 
for a fun engaging event with thrills, excitement and culture. Audience voting determine 
the winners of the events, and silent auction for the real prize of those who will get to 
walk home with a one-of-a-kind piece of art?

What is Parks Edge Paint Off?

A community live painting tournament showcasing local artists bridging the gap between 
the art lover and artist! Audience vote determines the winner & all paintings will be 
available via silent auction.

Where is Parks Edge located?

Join us outdoors in the beautiful back yard of the Deer Lake Art Gallery:

6584 Deer Lake Avenue, Burnaby

Who are the artists participating in the live painting tournament?

The call is out for the artists for the 2019 event is currently happening. . . there will be 
maximum of 20 artist competing! There will always be a few of our favorites returning 
plus new artist will to take on the Parks Edge Paint OFF!

Is the event FREE?

YES! Many people have never seen how paintings are created and it is an exciting 
spectacle to get a glimpse of what the creative process looks like first hand.

What can I expect at Parks Edge?

1:00 Artists begin painting, theme and style is open to the artists. Silent 
Auction opens.

1:00 " 3:00 Artists have 2 hours to paint and the audience can mix and mingle

2:00 Community can begin voting for their favorite artist.

3:15 Voting closes and organizers tally

1:00 " 5:00 Live Music all day long!

3:45 Winner of the Parks Edge Paint Off Announced

4:00 Silent Auction Closed

We are happy to announce that DJ BJAMS will be joining us for the 2nd year! So you 
can rock it out to tunes and enjoy the live painting tournament.

This year please join us for our Beer Garden in the back yard featuring local brewery 
Dageraad beer!

Why is there a Silent Auction?

Parks Edge is an amazing community event. Audience can walk home with a one-of-a-
kind piece of art and help the Burnaby Arts Council to raise money so we can organize 
even more community events! All artwork created at Parks Edge is available for silent 
auction with 50% of the proceeds going to the artist.

Who are the artists competing in the 2019 event?

Parks Edge includes a diverse group of emerging and professional artists!

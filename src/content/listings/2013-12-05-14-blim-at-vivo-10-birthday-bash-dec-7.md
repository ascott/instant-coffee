Blim to celebrate 10 years in Vancouver
Vancouver’s beloved arts and craft facility to hold birthday bash at VIVO on December 7th, 2013
 
November 26, 2013 - Vancouver, BC – Blim, Vancouver’s independent family-run arts and craft facility is announcing its 10 year anniversary celebration on December 7th, 2013. Beginning from its roots in the heart of historic Chinatown, then Main St and now back Chinatown, Blim is a keystone in Vancouver’s creative community. It is considered the epitome of handmade and creativity offering everything from arts and craft workshops, unique cosmic goods and apparel, curated events and experiences, and monthly community markets.
 
“We’re proud to be celebrating our tenth year in Vancouver,” says Yuriko Iga, Director and founder of Blim, “Blim started out as a reflection of who I am as an artist and creator and it has blossomed into a creative platform that aims to build community through the spirit of fun and creativity, making the arts and crafts accessible to all.”
 
Established in 2003, Blim is a unique arts facility, because despite being a resource and platform for artists and makers, it is funded solely by sales and community support without any government funding. At its current Pender St. location, Blim offers instructed workshops, drop-in space and a screen-printing studio for those curious and experienced. It also lent its space as an affordable venue for emerging visual and performing artists to display their work. Blim furthers its mission by providing new merchants with opportunities to launch and test their products and services at Blim’s monthly community markets. Over the years, Blim has also collaborated with countless organizations and events, such as the Powell Street Festival, VIVA Vancouver, Vancouver Art Gallery’s FUSE and Khatsahlano Music Festival, to create unique experiences that encourages a more creative Vancouver.
 
On December 7th, 2013, Blim is inviting Vancouverites to celebrate its 10th anniversary at VIVO. The event will be a nostalgic journey of Blim, featuring performers, DJs and artists, such as Chrissarriffic, Kellarissa Larissa Loyva, DJ Ultimate Lover, Jonathan and Sean Orr, and many others who have been a part of Blim’s community throughout its history. The festivities is from 9pm to 1am at VIVO on 1965 Main St. Entry is by donation and everyone is welcomed.
 
 
Fast Facts on Blim
Birth date: December 1, 2003
 
Founder: Yuriko Iga
 
Locations and dates: 
Dec 2003 to May 2005 @ 23 Pender St.
Sept 2005 to May 2010 @ 17th Ave and Main St.
May 2010 to present @ 115 East Pender St
 
Background of name ‘Blim’: Blim came from ‘Blimblim’, Yuriko’s imaginary animal kingdom that she conjured up when she was 4 years old.
 
Unofficial mascots: Early Blim mascot was a white owl; mid-Blim was two white rabbits; current Blim mascots are Furbys, Garfield, ET, and trolls, and cute cats.
 
Prominent artists and musicians hosted: The Hidden Cameras, Trevor Dunn and Shelley Burgon, Womankind, No Kids, The Yellow Swans, Vincent Parker
 
Prominent events hosted and curated: Cartune Express, Blim Market, Cute 2 Kill, Video-Blim
 
Types of workshops hosted: Screen-printing, tie dye, soap-making, moccasin-making, button-making
 
Number of workshops hosted: Over 500 workshops
 
Number of Blim Markets and vendors hosted: Almost 100 Blim Markets and thousands of Blim Market vendors hosted
 
Upcoming Blim Market: Annual Blim Holiday Market on Saturday, Dec 14 at the Chinese Cultural Centre from 12pm to 6pm

NEW PUBLICATION

STEPHEN WADDELL
Hunt and Gather

Published by Steidl, 2011

The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to announce the publication of Hunt and 
Gather, a new monograph on the work of Stephen Waddell. Published by Steidl 
and containing an introductory essay by Michael Fried, the collection of 
photographs from Germany, Vancouver, Italy, France, Japan and Lebanon offers 
an overview of Stephen's work from the past 20 years.

Hunt and Gather will be available through Monte Clark Gallery beginning in 
September 2011. To order your signed copy, please contact Matt McGale: 
matt@monteclarkgallery.com

Stephen Waddell (born 1968, Vancouver, Canada) received his M.F.A. from the 
University of British Columbia in 1994, and was recently honoured by the Liliane 
Bettencourt Prix de la Photographie 2010. He has exhibited in galleries and 
institutions including the Monte Clark Gallery in Vancouver and Toronto; the 
Vancouver Art Gallery, Espai d'art contemporani de Castello in Castello, Spain; 
Kunstforum Baloise in Basel, Switzerland and C/O in Berlin. Waddell's works 
belong to the Armand Hammer Collection in Los Angeles, Vancouver Art Gallery, 
the National Gallery of Canada in Ottawa, and others. The artist lives and works 
in Vancouver, Canada.

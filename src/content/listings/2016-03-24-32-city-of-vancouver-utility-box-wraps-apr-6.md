Call for Artists - Utility Box Wraps
Integrated Graffiti Management Program
City of Vancouver
Artist Fees: Artists will be paid a total of $1,000 for each approved wrap
design and its ongoing use.
Submission Deadline: Wednesday, April 6, 2016

The City of Vancouver is looking for artists to create designs to be
digitized and printed on vinyl wraps for approximately 27 utility boxes in
Vancouver. The ongoing project enhances the public realm by adding local
artists' work to surfaces that had often been targeted by vandalism. In
2015 the City of Vancouver ran a successful pilot in the West End, and this
year's projects will be rolled out City-wide, many in partnership with
Business Improvement Associations.

Table Talk Arts Collective is a Victoria-based group of six emerging
visual artists. Our common experience of studying Visual Arts at the
University of Victoria enabled us to connect. Yet it was our curiosity 
to
expand on our individual practices of art making that lead us to 
working
collaboratively.

Our upcoming exhibitions consist of two shows occurring 
simultaneously on
Wednesday April 20. 8 Hour Work Day is a performance art piece, 
which
will take place at the Ministry of Casual Living located on Haultain
Street. Our other show is titled Buck or Two hosted across the 
street by
Koffi House.,
8-Hour Work Day
This performance piece will consist of us assembling origami 
cubes in a
Fordist style factory line. Through our action of monotonous labour 
we
question our role in mass production and our detachment from the 
source of
manufacture.

Buck or Two
During this two-week exhibition small multi-media art pieces will be 
for
sale for a buck or two. By mimicking a dollar store model this 
exhibition
will engage the local community in investigating our current 
approach to
consumerism.

In this engaging two-hour session, participants will get hands-on experience creating
their own paintings with the artistic guidance of Charlene Johnny, a talented
Quwutsun artist. Charlene will guide participants as they use stencils to create their
own Salish-style paintings and he will explain the meaning behind the different
shapes and forms often found in Coast Salish art. This kid-friendly workshop will
result in a new artwork for participants to take home!

Dates: April 27, 2024

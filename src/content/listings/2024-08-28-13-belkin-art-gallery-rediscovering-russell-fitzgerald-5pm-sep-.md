An Opulence of Squander / That Directionless Light of the Future: Rediscovering
Russell FitzGerald
Opening Reception and Curatorial Tours with Weiyi Chang and Jon Davies
Thursday, September 5 from 5 to 8 pm

Join us on
Thursday, September 5 at 5 pm for curatorial tours with Weiyi Chang and
Jon Davies, followed by the opening reception from 6 to 8 pm. Offering the potential
for ongoing research and reconsideration, the Belkins collections emphasize artists
practices that challenge the status quo. Guest curators Weiyi Chang and Jon Davies
offer responses to the collection with their respective exhibitions, An Opulence of
Squander and That Directionless Light of the Future: Rediscovering Russell
FitzGerald, which will be shown concurrently in the gallery. Taking its title from an
essay by artist Kelly Wood, An Opulence of Squander brings together works that
reflect on concepts of surplus and excess to question the dual ascription of artistic
work as a form of both luxury and waste. That Directionless Light of the Future
features rarely seen works by American artist and writer Russell FitzGerald (1932-
78) and his contemporaries, largely from the Bay Area, to explore how secret and
subcultural knowledge
complicates archiving and transmission.

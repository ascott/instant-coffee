Fight With a Stick presents Revolutions
at The Warehouse, 3681 Victoria Drive
May 19-22 at 8pm (2pm matinees Sat 21 & Sun 22)
May 25-29 at 8pm (2pm matinees Sat 28 & Sun 29)

A telescopic journey through dust, human chemical relationships and
geological time.

Fight With a Stick, the company that brought you last year's sold-out
and critically acclaimed Steppenwolf at the PuSh Festival, invites you
on a new adventure - Revolutions is an immersive performance in a
7,000 square foot warehouse. Fight With a Stick is a company of
theatre-makers, video artists, composers, installation and performance
artists. Steppenwolf was described as "visually astonishing"
(Ledingham) and "mind-bending" (RealTime). Revolutions promises
more of the same.

Directed by veteran theatre artists, Steven Hill and Alex Lazaridis
Ferguson (co-direction), created in collaboration with Jay White (set
design), Josh Hite (video artist), Nancy Tam (sound design) and
devising performing artists, Sean Marshall Jr., Delia Brett and Carmine
Santavenere. Tickets $25 / $20. More info and purchase available at:
fightwithastick.ca

"Something good is happening in Vancouver contemporary
performance . . . an original vision" - RealTime Arts, Australia

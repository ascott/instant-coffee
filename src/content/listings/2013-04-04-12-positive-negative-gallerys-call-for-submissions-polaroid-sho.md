Positive Negative Gallery’s CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS for the "Beautiful 
Decay" Polaroid show.

"Beautiful Decay" sharpens its gaze on urban disorder and desolation, where 
memories are forgotten and left behind, and the laws of entropy take hold: the 
cracks and blood and decay of modern life. Everything grows older, yet is 
beautiful in its release.

How to submit:
We are looking for 30 artists. At this time, we are only accepting 
Polaroid/Impossible Project instant film pictures. Please email a picture of 
current work you would like to submit, or the concept if it is not yet taken. 
Please send all to contact@positivenegativegallery.com

Deadline:
Deadline for application is April 10, 2013.

About Positive Negative:
We love creativity. We love the thought, the expression, the execution that 
creative people pour into their passion -- their work. And we love showing, 
accepting and more so sharing; providing access and an outlet to affect. We 
believe in ideas that are meant to change the way people think, feel, and 
interact. Thankfully, we have a gallery space where we can do just that.

We are a Vancouver, Canada based artist-run gallery located at 436 Columbia 
St. You can find us at www.positivenegativegallery.com

Terris has been commissioned to install a six-storey apartment tower in the
rotunda of the Vancouver Art Gallery (VAG). This monumental installation,
entitled Ought Apartment, is to premiere at the VAG in May 2009. The
installation will consist of a tower with six full-scale apartments stacked
one on top of another. Each apartment level will represent a decade from the
1950s to our current decade. Each floor is fitted with typical consumer
choices of the era, with exterior walls removed to allow views within. In
keeping with the authenticity and architectural accuracy of the work, the
dimensions of each apartment fluctuate so that the design at each level
indicates the floor plan specific to that decade. As a result, each floor

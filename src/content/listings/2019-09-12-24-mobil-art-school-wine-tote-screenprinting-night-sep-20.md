Wine & Tote Screenprinting Night
Sep. 20, 6:30 p.m. - 9:30 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer St, Vancouver, BC V6A 1X5, Canada
Add a bit of creative flair and individuality to your wardrobe by learning how to 
screenprint onto t-shirts and tote bags. In this session, you will learn how to use 
screenprinting to print a design onto fabric. You will have the chance to squeegee a 
design onto your own t-shirt or tote bag. So come grab a drink and let your creativity fly!
Materials: bring your own t-shirts for printing. 1 plain tote will be provided. Additional 
totes available for purchase.
Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our FAQ.
Cash Bar, 19+ only, No Minors, No Outside Drinks.
$63

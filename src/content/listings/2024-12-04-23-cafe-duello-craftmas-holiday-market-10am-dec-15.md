Craftmas Holiday Market and Fundraiser with Registered Autism Service Providers
and Cafe Duello is a fundraiser for children with autism to better access Registered
Autism Service Providers.

Date: Dec 15th, 10am-3pm
Location: Cafe Duello, 426 W Hastings St.
Free entry

We will be featuring artwork made by participating children, alongside curated goods
for sale such as: toys, stationary, decorations, handmade gifts, plushies, fidgets,
clothing, and more! We are grateful to Dressew and Vancouver Christmas Market for
donating raffle prizes and there will also be a clothes donation hamper in partnership
with Revivify Textile Recycling and Big Brothers Canada.

There will be activities for all:
-Holiday Cards Station! Grab a warm drink from Cafe Duello and sit down to write
your holiday cards!
-Accessible DIY arts and crafts booth
-Sensory art display by multidisciplinary artist Suze Shore
-Lego play area
-Reading
corner. Overwhelmed by the holiday season? Take a break and curl up on
a bean bag with a book!

Craftmas is organized by Annie Hung, with the support of 3 Registered Autism
Service Providers and Cafe Duello. Recently, she curated First Generation, an
artexhibit featuring the work of immigrant seniors and children for the 2024 STAND
Festival. She is passionate about supporting marginalized demographics in her
community. When she isn't organizing community art events, Annie teaches art to
children in Burnaby Community Schools while studying for her doctorate at SFU.

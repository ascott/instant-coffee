InstA Salon: Parvin Peivandi

Saturday, September 5, at 4 PM PST, via Instagram Live @centre_a

Join us for an online conversation between our current exhibiting artist Parvin 
Peivandi and Centre As curatorial intern Hana Amani, as they discuss Peivandis 
practice, influences and work as a contemporary Iranian artist and, in relation to 
the exhibition, We cast Spells on the Mothers of our Daughters and Daughters of 
our Mothers. 

Parvin Peivandi is an Iranian Canadian artist that makes art
across disciplines 
that includes sculpture, painting, drawing, performance, installations and 
community art projects. Born and raised in Iran from Kurdish descent, Peivandi 
makes art influenced by Iranian architectural elements: ceramic tiles, mosaic 
mirrors and textiles. Peivandi studied English literature and fine arts in Iran, then 
immigrated to Canada in 2009 to pursue her career as an artist. Since then, she 
has received her BFA from Emily Carr University of Art and Design, her BEd 
from the University of British Columbia, and her MFA in interdisciplinary art from 
the Art Institute of Chicago. Peivandi has exhibited her art locally and 
internationally in significant art shows such as the Iranian Ceramic Biennale and 
Art Expo Chicago. Peivandi is currently working between her studio in Vancouver 
and Chicago, making art and doing research. She has received awards, grants 
and scholarships from the BC Arts Council, Shapiro Center for Research and 
Collaboration in the
United States and SSHRC in Canada. By deconstructing the 
architectural elements and juxtaposing conventional and unconventional 
materials together, she explores spaces of personal and public narratives, and 
memories of places and the dynamics of social/political systems.

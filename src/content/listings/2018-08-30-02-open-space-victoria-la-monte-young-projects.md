N E W S R E L E A S E


For Immediate Release
August 27, 2018

Victoria, BC " A multidisciplinary work by Vancouver-based artist David
Khang, La Monte Young Projects, will open at Victorias Open Space
artist-run centre on September 7.

In 1960, avant-garde composer La Monte Young produced Compositions 1960, a
series of ground-breaking conceptual compositions that play with ideas of
performance and audience. One piece, Composition #4, instructs the
performer to turn a butterfly loose in the performance area when the
composition is over, be sure to allow the butterfly to fly away outside.
The score for Composition #7 is two notes: B3 and F#4, with the sole
instruction: to be held for a long time.

Now, over fifty years later, artist David Khang has re-interpreted the
pieces from Compositions 1960 through installation, video, and performance
works to explore what they might mean to us today.

David Khang is a visual, performance, and biological artist whose practice
is informed by education in psychology, theology, dentistry, and law. Since
2003, Khang has used his own body, as well as other entities, such as raw
beef tongue, live monarch butterflies, and a Clydesdale horse, to
facilitate the performance of a non-verbal language and interrogate social
constructions of gender, race, and interspecies relations.

The gallery exhibition for La Monte Young Projects will run at Open Space
from Sept. 7-Oct. 20, 2018.

Join Open Space on Friday, Sept. 7 at 7:00 p.m. for the opening reception,
featuring a performance by David Khang and local musicians at 8:00 p.m.
Khang will also present an artist talk Saturday, Sept. 8 from 2:00 - 4:00 p.m.

Open Space, 510 Fort Street, 2nd floor

Free, but donations are welcome

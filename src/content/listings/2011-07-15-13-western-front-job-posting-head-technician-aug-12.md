The Western Front is an artist-run-centre founded in 1973 as a space for
the broad exploration of contemporary art. It is the mandate of the Western
Front to support the research, creation and presentation of work by
emerging and established contemporary artists in all disciplines. Over the
past thirty-five years, over 2,500 events have taken place, including
exhibitions, performances, installations, screenings and production
residencies.

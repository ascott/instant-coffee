"Hearth" is the centre square of a traditional log-cabin-style quilt block and the title of an
18-month (and counting) social-engagement project now installed in the windows of
AMP Gallery, 800 Keefer St. in the Strathcona area of the Downtown Eastside until
March 30. Part
of artist Carlyn Yandle's thesis, "Joyful Making in Perilous Times", the
site-specific installation of nearly 100 hand-stitched, improvisational log-cabin blocks
stands as an archive of regular two-hour community-building sessions around
neighbourhood kitchen and studio tables including EWMA (Enterprising Women Making
Art) Studio, developed by Atira Women's Resource Society, and via video
communications apps.

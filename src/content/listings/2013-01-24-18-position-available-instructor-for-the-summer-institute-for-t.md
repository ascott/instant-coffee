Aboriginal Arts Studio â€“ Course Description
Interested in exploring your Aboriginal heritage in a multi-disciplinary visual art 
studio? Are you serious about pursuing fine art or design education after high 
school? This course introduces students to the form and function of traditional 
and contemporary Aboriginal art practice. Introducing themes and tracing 
traditions, this studio explores form in terms of personal, cultural, and 
historical contexts without concentrating on a particular culture or style. 
Students will work on a series of assignments, starting with basic principles 
of drawing and line forms, the introduction of colour and personal image 
development. Students will also work with materials such as cedar, deer hide 
and beads to create small-scale utilitarian and aesthetic objects using the 
traditional methods of BC First Nations artists.

Chinatown Speculative Futures with Linda Zhang, Sunday, March 28, 1pm

Griffin Art Projects

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Zoom link: [https://zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_5XCRAbzNQ1OTo2txgG3rig](https://zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_5XCRAbzNQ1OTo2txgG3rig)

Join Linda Zang, professor in the School of Interior Design at Ryerson University in Toronto
ON,
live over Zoom to learn about the story and process behind her recent project
ChinaTOwn, a multi-player board game that prompts participants to put their heads
together to negotiate what is worth including in a community space. Zhang partnered with
research technology officer in Ryersons Library Collaboratory, Jimmy Tran, to fly drones
over Torontos Chinatown, photographing 99 individual buildings and subsequently turning
each one into a board-game sized model. To play the game, participants must select only
12 of the 99 buildings to include in their hand-picked Chinatown. Centred on questions of
heritage preservation within the community, ChinaTOwn asks players to have a
conversation about what is important to them and the future of their community, a topic that
is relevant now more than ever as Chinatowns across the country face unprecedented
challenges and change.

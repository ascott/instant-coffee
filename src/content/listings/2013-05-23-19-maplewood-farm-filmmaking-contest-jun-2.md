Bring film making to the farmyard May 31st - June 2nd, 2013

Announcing the first Film On The Farm 48 Hour challenge! North 
Vancouver’s Maplewood Farm is opening its doors to creative 
filmmakers who think they have what it takes to write, shoot and edit a 
10 minute movie in just one weekend!

Contestants will be challenged to use Maplewood Farm as one of their 
filming locations, as well as incorporating other farm-themed props and 
dialogue into their finished masterpiece! 

All films will be eligible for cash and industry prizes, and will be 
screened as part of the Golden Goat Awards.

Space is limited, so log on to [http://maplewoodfarm.bc.ca/filmcontest/](http://maplewoodfarm.bc.ca/filmcontest/) 
for more information, and to register now. Join the Film On The Farm 
48 Hour Contest, and be outstanding in your field!

This film project is a direct result of a Community Art Grant from the 
North Vancouver Arts Office!

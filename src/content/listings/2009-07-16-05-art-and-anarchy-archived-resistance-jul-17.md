* Anarchist Insurrection! * Indigenous Uprisings! * Prisoner Liberation! *

Captured from silence and reconfigured in the detonation of struggle.
Archived Resistance is a show of force, of anarchist newsprints and
anti-authoritarian/anti-colonial propaganda, collected from various terrains.

Artifacts on display, not to create a prison/museums, but to celebrate
the symbols inscribed within the body memory of the Movement.

Bring what you can, zines, papers, posters, dreams; donate them to the
cause, or- we will make copies of your treasures and pass those copies
on to the Anarchist Archive {a digital universe of anarchist press}.

Archived Resistance also features a cataclysmic display of documents,
letters, paraphernalia and tears collected from Political Prisoners of
War/Conscious; as gathered by 4StruggleMag; with a workstation to write letters.

Coincided with an assortment of workshops, films, presentations,
happenings. To find out when these take place check out this site or

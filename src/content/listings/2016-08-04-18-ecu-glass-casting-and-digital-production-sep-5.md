Glass Casting and Digital Production (Sept 10 - Nov 5)

Emily Carr University of Art + Design and the Terminal City Glass Co-op
have partnered to offer a new weekend course that combines 3D modeling with
traditional glass casting. Suitable for all skill levels, this course will
especially benefit those who work in related disciplines, including
architecture, ceramics, product design and sculpture. Register early -
space is very limited.

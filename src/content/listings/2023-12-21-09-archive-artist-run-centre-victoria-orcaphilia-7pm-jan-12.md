Orcaphilia is an exploration of Natasha van Nettens admiration and curiosity towards
orca. This dynamic collection of paintings, drawings and installations are influenced by
her personal experiences and by discoveries made in the field of cetology (the study of
whales). While orcas are powerful creatures, they are also vulnerable. The works in
this exhibition highlight this duality of strength and fragility.

Location: arc.hive artist run centre, 770b Hillside
Ave, Victoria

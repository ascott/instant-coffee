Frankenkenstein
(theatre)
ellis cheadle

Sept 28 at 6:30PM
Oct 4 at 8PM
Oct 5 at 6:30PM

Frankenkenstein is a theatrical collage monstrosity. It's an exhibition of results from
experiments performed on the Frankenstein story, as stitched back together (again
and again) by a
hack-surgeon. It's an indulgent autopsy of the gothic-horror genre
with a focus on the intrusiveness of the past inserting itself in the present. Its a dark
and stormy night. Its alive!

ellis cheadle is a collaborative theatre-maker living and working on the stolen
territories of the xmky"m, Swxw7mesh, and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh Nations.
She is drawn to slow creation processes, and is increasingly dedicated to creating
malleable theatrical set-lists that can responsively adapt to requirements of scale,
duration, site, and collaborator skill-sets/curiosities. She values playfulness, and
strives to make theatre that invites the audience into a shared game of finding links
between seemingly incongruous images, texts, themes, and materials

Felt
(contemporary dance / sound)
Hayley Gawthrop

Sept 26 at 8PM
Sept 27 at 8PM
Sept 28 at 8PM

Felt offers the viewer a window into one tired humans relationship to the semiotics
of femininity and productivity. Music, Dance, Drag " Felt is all that,
and a bag of
chips.

Born and reared in Semiahmoo territory (South Surrey) Hayley Gawthrop is an
independent artist residing in the unceded, ancestral territory of the Musqueam,
Squamish and Tsleil- Waututh Nations (Vancouver). Hayley makes art that is a
consequence of their life. They are passionate about gentleness, bodily autonomy
and listening with curiosity.

What's Happening?
(theatre)
Santi Henderson

Sept 27 at 6:30PM
Oct 3 at 8PM
Oct 5 at 8PM

Whats Happening is a representation of theatre and its power to change the world -
thats why the ruling classes keep trying to take a permanent hold of it and utilize it
as a tool of domination (said Augusto Boal). For this show I am going to wake up
early and exercise and stretch every day before it, and read books. Im going to stop
smoking and drink less, follow through on my commitments, and you should too. The
world has had a terrible year, but the whole universe, the cosmos, the rays, the
algorithm, all of AI, have conspired to
bring us together - now we have each other in
the trailer of a semi. Whatever this is will never exist again. But everything goes on.

Santi Henderson (he/him) is a performing artist and filmmaker based on the unceded
traditional territories of the xmky"m (Musqueam), Swxw7mesh (Squamish),
and slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

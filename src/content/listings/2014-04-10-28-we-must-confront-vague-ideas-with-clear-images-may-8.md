29. West Vancouver Museum | SD45 One | until | MAY 31
SD45 One: An Exhibition of West Vancouver Students Past and Present

April 8 to May 31, 2014
WEST VANCOUVER, 26 March 2014: What do internationally acclaimed artists 
Douglas Coupland, Geoffrey Farmer, Graham Gillmore and Ian Wallace all 
have in common? They are all past students from West Vancouver public 
schools - School District 45. An exhibition featuring their work, as well as 
nine other SD45 "alumni", alongside current kindergarten to grade 12 
students, opens April 8th at the West Vancouver Museum.

This is the first exhibition ever held that brings together art work created by 
past and present students from West Vancouver public schools. "The West 
Vancouver Museum is pleased to host this innovative exhibition and to 
recognize that West Vancouver schools offer such excellent opportunities 
for emerging young artists," says Darrin Morrison, Director of the Museum.

SD45 One is the brainchild of West Vancouver Secondary School art teacher 
Jackie Wong. "There is an incredible number of renowned artists who have 
attended West Vancouver public schools, starting in the 1960s, and I thought 
it would be inspiring to students to hold an exhibition that includes their work, 
plus the work of current students," says Wong, who organized the exhibition.

In addition to the West Vancouver Museum exhibition, an SD45 Satellite 
Exhibition will be held in shipping containers along the West Vancouver 
waterfront. Several of the SD45 "alumni" artists led classroom workshops for 
current students over the past few months, and the artwork created in these 
workshops will be exhibited in the containers from May 3-21, 2014.

"This pairing of works by professional artists alongside current students 
demonstrates the strength of art education in the community", says Patrik 
Andersson, an associate professor at Emily Carr University of Art + Design, 
who guest curated the exhibition. "What this exhibition celebrates is the 
diverse creative output that West Vancouver continues to nurture into local, 
national and international relevance and acclaim."

Opening night of the exhibition at the West Vancouver Museum is 
Wednesday, April 9th from 6pm - 8pm and is open to the public. A full-colour 
catalogue was published to accompany the exhibition.

This exhibition is being held in concurrence with the West Vancouver School 
District's annual Lighthouse Festival.

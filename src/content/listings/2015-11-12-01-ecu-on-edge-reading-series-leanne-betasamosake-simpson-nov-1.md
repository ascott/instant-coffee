The public is welcome.

Author bio:

Leanne Betasamosake Simpson is the author of three books: Dancing
on Our Turtle’s Back (Arbeiter Ring Publishing 2011), The Gift Is in the
Making (Highwater 2013) and Islands of Decolonial Love (ARP Books
2013). She is “a gifted writer who brings passion and commitment to her
storytelling and who has demonstrated an uncommon ability to manage
an impressive range of genres from traditional storytelling to critical
analysis, from poetry to the spoken word, from literary and social
activism.” In 2014, Leanne was named the inaugural RBC Charles
Taylor Emerging writer by Thomas King. She is of Michi Saagiig
Nishnaabeg ancestry and a member of Alderville First Nation.

We gratefully acknowledge the Canada Council for the Arts, the

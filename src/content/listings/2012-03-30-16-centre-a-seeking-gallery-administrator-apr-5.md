Centre A (Vancouver International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art) 
is a leading public art gallery with a mandate to present innovative 
exhibitions that develop the public appreciation of contemporary Asian 
art. Centre A promotes the work of Asian-Canadian artists, whilst also 
showcasing the work of leading artists from abroad. Maintaining high 
artistic, curatorial and scholarly standards, Centre A enjoys an 
international reputation for excellence and innovation. Centre A is 
currently looking to hire a part-time Gallery Administrator to our team.
Reporting to the Executive Director, and working closely with the 
Curator and other gallery staff, the Gallery Administrator is an integral 
member of the Centre A team. S/he will perform a range of 
administrative and visitor services tasks to support the ongoing 
functions of the gallery.

Scope of the position:
The Gallery Administrator plays a vital role in the day-to-day operations 
of the gallery. General tasks include opening and closing the gallery, 
providing visitor services, recruiting and co-ordinating 
volunteers, managing venue rental enquiries and handling a range of 
requests including media enquiries, group tours and school visits. 
General administrative duties include mailing, banking, ordering 
supplies, and co-ordinating gallery maintenance. 

The Gallery Administrator co-ordinates membership administration and 
communications, and is responsible for writing and issuing regular 
member newsletters, growing the membership and generating 
renewals. S/he will be an active participant in donor development and 
audience development initiatives. The ability to manage website 
content and social media would be regarded as a valuable asset. 
Strong English language skills are essential to this role, as the 
candidate will be required to undertake a variety of writing tasks 
including drafting press releases, newsletters, letters and reports to a 
range of stakeholders, including government funders.

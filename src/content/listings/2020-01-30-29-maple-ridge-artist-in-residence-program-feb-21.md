Artist in Residence Program - Expressions of Interest for Live-Work Studio House 2020
to 2023
Deadline February 21, 2020
Canadian artists and artist teams working in any artistic/cultural discipline are invited
tosubmit an Expression of Interest application for one new artist in residence opportunity
in Maple Ridge, BC. There is
one live-work house with studio approx. 1600 sq. ft. for a
term of 3 years starting spring 2020 in Port Haney. Please see Guidelines, budget, and
provisions documents, location specs and Expression of Interest Application form for
details.

Deadline: February 21 | 5:00 pm
Email application to submissions@mapleridge.ca.

Call to Artists " Performers, Storytellers and Entertainers for Vibrant Downtown
Programs and BC Summer Games
Performing artists from various disciplines are invited to apply to participate at outdoor
events and activations in 2020. Events may include a concert series in Memorial Peace
Park, signature events such as Canada Day and Celebrate the Night or the BC Summer
Games in July. A variety of genres and styles to engage our diverse audience, including
street performers, storytellers, actors and entertainers are encouraged to apply.

Deadline: February 16 | 5:00 pm

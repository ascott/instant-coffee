June Open Studio
Moniker Press
Saturday, June 29th, 1-6pm
268 Keefer St, Vancouver, BC

Drop by on June 29th to see inside the process' of the Moniker Press risograph print 
shop! At the open studio, you're invited to: pick up a copy of our latest colour chart, see 
the RZ and MZ printers in person, view print samples from the archive and meet others 
who are interested in risograph printing.

A selection of riso books collected from around the world will be available for browsing 
and inspiration. Moniker Press publications will also be available for purchase with 
select copies on sale! Including our newest releases Noodle Soup (Harlo Martens) and 
pensamientos en la frontera (andi icaza-largaespada).

Moniker Press is a risograph publishing studio that works collaboratively with artists and 
writers to produce small editions of books, zines and print ephemera. monikerpress.ca

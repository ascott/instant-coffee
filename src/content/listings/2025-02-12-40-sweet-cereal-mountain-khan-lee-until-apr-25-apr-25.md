***Sweet Cereal Mountain*** proudly presents:

Khan Lee

Now on view until April 25

Artist statement:

A string of this lighter collection was
passed onto me by an artist who moved away
to Amsterdam in 2004. I was living in the downtown eastside with lots of action on
the street, so finding these colourful mangled plastic handheld devices was not
difficult, hence the collection grew. And, because this generic design is almost
universal, many of the items in the collection also came from my pocket around the
globe. Twenty years later, I see people still burning flames all day, but maybe a
cheap plastic torch that melts away when you hang on to it too long might be a little
out of trend...

is still just a simple collection, and not much else. It was hidden in plain sight,
and now gets to be displayed in a gallery that was also hidden in plain sight until
now. Does fire mean something significant? Is that why I still carry one in my pocket?
Well, I really have no answers here

Sweet Cereal Mountain is a non profit public art display gallery within the confines of
222cm by 15cm by 5.73cm and located in1610 Robson Public Market

Hours of operation: Monday - Sunday 9am-9pm

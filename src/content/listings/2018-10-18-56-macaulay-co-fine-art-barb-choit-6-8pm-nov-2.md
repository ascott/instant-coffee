100 Lot Contemporary Handmade Art Drawings NEW Wall Art Home Decor
Barb Choit 
Work on Paper 2018 
Installation of 100 colored pencil drawings 
Dimensions variable

Reception Friday, November, 2nd 6-8 pm 

Statement:

100 Lot Contemporary Handmade Art Drawings NEW Wall Art Home Decor Work 
on Paper is an installation of one hundred colored pencil drawings, each depicting 
an item (or group of items) for sale on e-commerce website eBay.com. The work is a 
remake of a past project, eBay Photographic Archive Circa 2005, for which the artist 
sought to preserve a disappearing genre of vernacular photography: Amateur 
Digital Product Photography Circa 2005. For one year, she collected images posted 
online by eBay users selling personal items. She presented them as an installation 
of archival prints stored in archival boxes, organized chronologically. By downloading 
new eBay photographs, she continues her absurd project of creating an analogue 
archive of ephemeral digital images. Although, in 2018, there are newer more 
prevalent social media platforms awash with user-generated imagery, the artist 
posits that eBay is a place where people reveal themselves without performing for a 
social media audience. In contrast to the rehearsed selfie or the carefully curated 
family photograph, approximations of commercial product shots can reveal more 
about the users offline life and aesthetic sensibilities. The new work takes a closer 
look at the amateur photographer behind this imagery. The switch in medium to 
drawing reflects this. The artist references Colored Pencil Art, an amateur art genre 
with a devoted online community. She draws a parallel to the amateur eBay 
photographer, whose oftensophisticated aesthetic discloses itself through unplanned 
compositional elements: juxtaposition, repetition, color combinations, hands and 
feet. This new archive of contemporary eBay imagery is structured linguistically 
rather than chronologically - reflective of modern Internet search methods. All 
chosen images have the word lot or lots in their titles. The word references the 
auction lot. However, as most eBay items are no longer sold via auction, the term 
now broadly means more than one object, and is used by sellers to manipulate 
search results. These new drawings are presented as a lot, as a singular archive 
rather than a collection of individual works.

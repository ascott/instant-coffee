No.3 Road Art Columns Exhibition

The City of Richmond Public Art Program seeks proposals from artists for the

2025-2026 No.3 Road Art Columns Program. The exhibition will run from

August 1, 2025 to January 31, 2026.

Three (3) commissioned artists will each receive an artist fee of $1,500 for

four (4) print-ready digital artwork files. Printing and installation costs will be

the responsibility of the City of Richmond. Artists with practices in mixedmedia,

painting, illustration, photography, printmaking, drawing and other

mediums suitable for digital reproduction are encouraged to apply.

Artist Fee: $1,500.00

Eligibility: Artists residing in Richmond

Deadline: Thursday, May 22, 2025

For more information and to apply:
https://www.richmond.ca/culture/howartworks/publicart/artist-calls.htm

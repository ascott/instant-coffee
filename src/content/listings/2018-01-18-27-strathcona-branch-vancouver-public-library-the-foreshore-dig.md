Talk | The Foreshore | Dignity and Access

Tuesday, January 23, 7-8.30pm
nc"a"mat ct Strathcona Branch, Vancouver Public Library
730 East Hastings Street
Free

Carmen Papalia and Joulene Tse Parent will discuss issues of cultural accessibility and 
human rights in the city, including Tses ongoing research on the history of Indigenous 
workers on the waterfront, as well as Papalias projects leading up to and including his 
recent conceptual work Open Access, a new, relational model for accessibility that sets 
a precedent for considerations of agency and power in relation to the disabling social, 
cultural, and political conditions in a given context.

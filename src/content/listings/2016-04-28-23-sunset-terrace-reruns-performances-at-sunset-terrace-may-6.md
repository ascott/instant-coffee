Reruns: Performances at Sunset Terrace

2028 Clark Drive

Friday May 6, 2016 at 8PM

A reenactment can come from personal or collective memory and can
function as a lens through which stories of the past are shifted into
present perspective.

Join us at Sunset Terrace for RERUNS, an evening of short theatrical
performances. Organized by Kara Hansen and Chad Murray, artists,
dancers, and musicians, have been invited to reanimate the past,
reinterpret preexisting narratives, and to present the conceivable
potential of a reenactment through performance.

In order of appearance:

Pascale Theoret-Grouix & AJ the Cat (ongoing)

The For'er Players (Steven Hubert & Johnny Burgess)

Katherine Somody & Krystle Sivorot

Michel Beaudry

Juli Majer, Melanie Thibodeau, & Chandra Melting-Tallow

Monique Levesque

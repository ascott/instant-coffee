Fillip is pleased to announce the release of a new publication by grupa o.k. as part of 
Supplement, an occasional pamphlet series.

Produced in partnership with Plug In ICA, Winnipeg, Stagelessness is a visual essay on the 
history and evolution of the stage built up from a sequence of pictures and extended 
captions. The publication charts a physical space that formalizes distinctions between 
spectacle and spectators, orator and audience, performers and those-performed-for, actors 
and those-acted-upon.

Supplement 4 is the second in a series of four books produced in collaboration with Plug In 
ICA in response to their 2017 exhibition Stages: Drawing the Curtain. Forthcoming 
publications in the series will include work by Candice Hopkins and Jenifer Papararo.

Supplement 4 is available for purchase for $10 CAD, shipped internationally, or as part of a 
yearly membership subscription that also includes books published in Fillips ongoing Folio 
series. Supplement 4 is also distributed by Motto Distribution (Europe).

About the Writers

grupa o.k. is the collaborative endeavor of J. Myers and J. Szupinska. Their practice 
includes editing, curating, writing, and research, with a focus on contemporary art, the 
history and future of exhibition making, and the complexities of collective organization. Their 
name is a mischievous borrowing from grupa a.r. (artyci rewolucyjni), the Polish avant-
garde group founded in 1929 by Wadysaw Strzemiski, Katarzyna Kobro, and Henryk 
Staewski.

grupa o.k.s productions include "Goodbye, Auteur" (Art Journal, 2019), The Prehistory of 
Exhibition History (Art Journal, 2017), Proposal for a Museum (San Francisco Museum of 
Modern Art, 2013), and Robert Morris Is Unavailable (Museum of Contemporary Art, 
Chicago, 2011).

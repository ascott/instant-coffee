grunt gallery and the Native Education College (NEC) have partnered
together to create two new large-scale, full colour murals in celebration
of the NEC's 30th anniversary at their location in Mount Pleasant.

Both of these murals will be featured as part of the first annual Vancouver
Mural Festival (VMF) where local and international artists will be creating
35+ brand new permanent murals around Mount Pleasant!!

Join us on Saturday, August 20 at the launch party to celebrate the
creation of these two murals! There will be live Indigenous world music by
Kalan Wi, free bannock samples and Aboriginal cuisine for sale by Cedar
Feast House Catering, free Yerba Mate tea supplied by VMF, and of course
painting activities!

EVENT: NEC Mural Community Paint and Launch Party
DATE: Saturday, August 20
TIME: 12:00 pm - 6:00 pm
LOCATION: Native Education College, 285 East 5th Ave, Vancouver, V5T 1H2

MURAL #1 - Where the People Are Welcome led by artist Marissa Nahanee
(Squamish & Nisga'a) with Jerry Whitehead (Cree), is being painted on the
west wall of the NEC's Longhouse.

MURAL #2 - Past and Presence led by artists Corey Bulpitt (Haida), Sharifah
Marsden (Anishinaabe) and Jerry Whitehead (Cree), is being painted on the
east wall of the Tsimilano Building, an administrative building located
next door to the NEC's Longhouse.

Both murals recognize the history of the Native Education College and its
location on unceded Coast Salish territories. The educational institution
has become a meeting place where many First Nations, Inuit, and Mtis as
well as settler and immigrant students come together for sharing and
learning that is grounded in Indigenous cultural knowledges. We honour the
land that we call home, the people who have lived here for generations and
this rich diversity of contemporary Indigenous existence in Vancouver and
the Mount Pleasant neighbourhood.

Drop by the NEC weekdays until August 20th between 8:00 am - 5:00 pm to
watch the artists at work.

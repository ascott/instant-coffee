Massy Arts is calling all Indigenous women (cisgender and transgender), Two-Spirit
and
nonbinary poets to be a part of a series of workshops investigating intersectional voices in
Indigenous contemporary poetry!
.
The position will be paid - and your workshop will be a part of Massy Art's new writing
marathon in the Summer.
.
If you are a published Indigenous poet and have an idea for a 2-hours-long literary
workshop, send us an email at info@massyarts.com.
The workshops will be held online, so poets across BC are welcome to apply.
Send your bio + workshop idea via email at info@massyarts.com.
.
We will start contacting applicants soon.

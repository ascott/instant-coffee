Screenings, performances and video installations highlight 12th annual Antimatter Film Festival

Antimatter Film Festival
October 9 to 17, 2009 | Victoria, BC, Canada | www.antimatter.ws

From October 9 to 17, downtown Victoria and beyond will be be energized by screenings, 
installations and live cinema performances that make up the nine-day celebration of experimental 
media that is the Antimatter Film Festival. Featuring the newest work from film and video artists
from around the world, Antimatter has an international reputation as an incubator and testing 
ground for exciting new talent, as well as a showcase for cinematic legends.

Venues for the 12th annual edition of Antimatter include Deluge Contemporary Art (636 Yates), 
Open Space Arts Centre (510 Fort), Cinecenta (UVic), the Window Project (1407 Government) 
and the Visual Arts Building at the University of Victoria.
The festival opens on Friday, October 9th with Small World, a musical detournement of Disney 
classics interpreted by local musicians Natasha Enquist, Walrus, Rad Juli, Wes Borg, Chelsea 
Chenery, The Pine Family, Lindsay Coulton, Jody Franklin, Slut Revolver, Run Chico Run, 
Grayson Walker, J McLaughlin and more.

Other standout events include the world premiere of Sami Van Ingen's Just One Kiss: The Fall of 
Ned Kelly, a recreation of the first narrative feature constructed from appropriated footage, 
intertitles and what little remains of the original with live music by Lee Hutzulak (Dixie's Death 
Pool); Loki: Arnaldo Baptista, the award-winning biography of founder and former lead singer of 
Os Mutantes, Brazil's most influential and enduring rock band; and Visual Acoustics, celebrating 
the life and career of architectural photographerJulius Shulman, whose images brought modern 
architecture to the mainstream.

Antimatter closes with ZAIREEKA in SOUND & PICTURES: The Flaming Lips' infamous 
quadraphonic album-synched with four-screen video especially commissioned of Winnipeg 
filmmakers Leslie Supnet, Clint Enns, Neil Hoare, Hope Peterson, Jaimz Asmundson, Damien 
Ferland, Kier-La Janisse and Cam Woykin! On the same bill is Fixed: View | Sky | Rail, a triptych
in 16mm performance by Vancouver's Alex MacKenzie.

Deluge Contemporary Art will be the venue for installations Cloud Cuckoo Land by New York 
based artists Erik Moskowitz and Amanda Trager, and Daniel Martinico's KHAN. The public can 
view Fred Worden's 1859 at Window Project, and the short program Circadia on campus at the 
University of Victoria (Visual Arts Building, Main Concourse).

Antimatter features over 20 programs of short and feature length films organised by festival and 
guest curators, screening at Open Space Arts Centre and Cinecenta.
Complete festival information can be found at http://www.antimatter.ws or in program guides 
available at the venues and at all south island Serious Coffee outlets.

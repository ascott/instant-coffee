Mindful Movement Workshop with Krystal Kiran
Saturday, March 26, 2022
1:00 PM - 2:00 PM
Richmond Art Gallery - 7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond BC

The 2022 Branscombe House Artist in Residence, Krystal Kiran, will lead a free mindful
movement workshop in the gallery space surrounded by art from the NOURISH
exhibition. This workshop will explore the relationship between movement and breath
set against the backdrop of gentle music. Participants may participate from a chair.
Join for a peaceful Saturday afternoon to nourish your body and mind. Suitable for all
ages and fitness levels.

Limited capacity, registration required.
Recommended for ages 16+

About the Artist:

Krystal Kiran is a Punjabi-Canadian performing artist and educator whose practice
explores third culture identity and the arts as healing practices. She has performed on
Broadway and toured internationally as a featured vocalist with Grammy and Oscar-
winning composer A.R. Rahman and is a certified yoga, meditation and pilates teacher.
As an actor, dancer and choreographer, she has performed on a variety of stages
including the Shaw Festival and Arts Club.

Krystals free community arts programs, activities and workshops will be held at
Branscombe House and other locations in Richmond throughout the year. For more
information and to keep up to date with Krystals offerings and residency, visit her blog
at www.krystalkiran.com/branscombe and follow @branscombehouse on Instagram!

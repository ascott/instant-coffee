CAG Reads

Hannah Jickling and Helen Reed present Paul Takes the Form of a Mortal Girl (2017) by
Andrea Lawlor

Contemporary Art Gallery

Online via Zoom

Sunday, August 16, 4 - 5.30pm, free

We are pleased to announce our August installment of CAG
Reads, a book club where
artists invite us to read alongside them. Each month an artist proposes a book for our
collective reading pleasure, culminating in a virtual hangout where the artist leads a wide-
ranging discussion grounded in their chosen reading material.

This edition of CAG Reads will be hosted by artists Hannah Jickling and Helen Reed.
Since 2006, Reed and Jickling have collaborated on projects that span photography,
video, public installations and printed matter, as well as intergenerational research
projects such as the ongoing Big Rock Candy Mountain. Jickling and Reed have exhibited
and performed widely, with both individual and collaborative work appearing in venues
such as the Portland Art Museum, The Power Plant, Smack Mellon and the Vancouver Art
Gallery. In 2017 they released Multiple Elementary (YYZBOOKS), a publication that
explores the elementary school classroom as a site of invention and reception of
contemporary art practices.

For this months installment,
Reed and Jickling have chosen the novel Paul Takes the
Form of a Mortal Girl (2017) by Andrea Lawlor. Set in 1993, Paul Polydoris is a young,
queer shapeshifter who transforms his body and gender at will as he crosses the country
in an adventure filled with pleasure, connection and loss. Focusing on the musical
references that appear throughout the novel, Jickling and Reed will pair selections from
the book with a playlist of songs for an online listening party.

This virtual event is free but space is limited. RSVP and find out how to acquire a copy of
the book at contemporaryartgallery.ca/events/cag-reads-hannah-jickling-and-helen-reed/

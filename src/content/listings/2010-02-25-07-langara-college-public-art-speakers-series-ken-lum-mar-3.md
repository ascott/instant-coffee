Langara College Public Art Speakers Series presents Ken Lum

Time: Wednesday, March 3, 7pm
Location: Langara College, Rm A136a (100 W 49th Ave, Vancouver, BC)
Cost: Free to the public

The Langara College Centre for Art in Public Spaces invites you to join us
for an engaging lecture. Artist Ken Lum will speak about his public art
work, and will also present a short paper titled To Say or Not to Say:
Thoughts on the Relationship between Art and the Real.

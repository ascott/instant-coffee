DISCOTALK GUEST BIO

Evann Siebens makes media, performance and photos with movement. Her lens-based
practice negotiates the human body as an archival site and the politics of the female gaze.
She danced professionally before attending film school at NYU, shooting dancers such as
Mikhail Baryshnikov, Bill T. Jones and Lucinda Childs in NYC as a dance cinematographer.
Evann, in collaboration with Keith Doyle and 43 artists, released the book 'Pedestrian Protest'
published by the Vancouver Art Gallery in 2025. Recent exhibitions include: Trieste
Contemporanea (2025), Surrey Art Gallery (2025), Cloud Seven (2024), Vancouver Art
Gallery Offsite (2021), MACBA (2021) and Kunstinstituut Melly (2020).

Afternoon Projects is pleased to present Wrought Bundle, a solo exhibition by Garrett
Lockhart. In this exhibition, Garrett Lockhart meditates on a history of non-earthquake
seismic tremors and slow slip events identified in his hometown Vancouver Island area,
imperceptible to human
consciousness, known but not seen. The invisibility of the
underground geological movements echoes with other things not seen, the life above
the ground. A geological sense not just of the structure of our world, but of its politics.
Perhaps what we witnessed, ignored, and pushed away are the rumblings of our
reality. Perhaps different narratives are needed to move past the romanticization of the
doomsday scenario as one large apocalyptic event.

This exhibition displays emergency preparedness packs containing traces of forgotten
essentiality, our unreliable memories, things that seem so clear and so embedded in
us. They become a projection, a wish, a vision of a possible future and all that went
with them.

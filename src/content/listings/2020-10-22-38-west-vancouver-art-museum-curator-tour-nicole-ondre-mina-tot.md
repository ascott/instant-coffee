THE EYES HAVE WALLS: NICOLE ONDRE & MINA TOTINO

Current Exhibition runs from SEP 30 - DEC 12

Join us for a free Curators Tour of our new exhibition, The Eyes Have Walls: Nicole
Ondre & Mina Totino on NOV 5th.

This exhibition features paintings and ceramic works by Vancouver-based artists Nicole
Ondre and Mina Totino. Ondre and Totino challenge the mechanical processes of their
materials and, through their experimentations, cause their artistic media to elude fixed
forms. This tour requires pre-registration and attendance is capped is at 8 attendees. To
register please call 604-925-7270 and quote course #81752.

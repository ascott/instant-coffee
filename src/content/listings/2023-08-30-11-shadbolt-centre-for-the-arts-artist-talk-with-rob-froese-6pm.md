Artist Talk
with Rob Froese
Deer Lake Artist Residencies

Tuesday, September 5, 6PM

Shadbolt Centre for the Arts (6450 Deer Lake Avenue, Burnaby), Studio 103

FREE & FREE PARKING

Rob Froese makes objects of use and contemplation that range from small cups and
plates to vase forms, wall works, and site-sensitive installations. His ceramic work is
a story of cross-cultural utilitarian and sculptural forms with a special interest in
subtle effects obtained through atmospheric firing, especially salt and soda firing.
During his artist talk, Rob will share insights into his practice and current work as a
2023 Deer Lake Artist in Residence. There will also be time for questions and
discussion.

Rob Froese (b. Moose Jaw, Saskatchewan) is a ceramic artist and educator. His
formal studies include a B.A. (Art, English) from the University of Saskatchewan, a
B.F.A. (Ceramics) from the University of Regina, and an M.F.A. in Craft Media from
Alberta College of Art + Design. He maintains a studio practice
between Canada and
Japan.

More information: brit.bachmann@burnaby.ca

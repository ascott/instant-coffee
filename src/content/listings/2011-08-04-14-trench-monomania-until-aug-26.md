Trench Contemporary Art Gallery

presents

MONOMANIA

An Organic Archive of the Terminal City Avant-Garde 1949 till Now!

After weeks of gradual installation MONOMANIA is now up, on and open for
the rest of the
summer!

For the past 60 years Vancouver’s art scene has produced some of the best
visual artists in Canada.
Monomania is an adventurous and playful exhibition featuring Paintings,
Drawings, Prints, Photographs,
Sculpture and much more by some of Vancouver’s most accomplished artists.

Artists such as Vincent Trasov, Glenn Lewis, Michael Morris, Sarah Gee, Ron
Stonier, Laura Baird, Robert
Young, Carrie Walker, Shirley Wiebe, Torrie Groening, Dave Mayrs, Jeff
Depner, Enn Erisalu, Ken Lum,
Geoffrey Farmer, Roy Kiyooka, Brian Fisher…….. Just to name a few!

On until September 3rd this show is a must see this summer.

mon·o·ma·ni·a –noun

1. an inordinate or obsessive zeal for or interest in a single thing, idea,
subject, or the like.
2. An art dealers attempt to convey his passion for the city and region he
calls home.

The Crossover: Summer Mixer

Gallery 881 and Retro Modern Designs are excited to share this unique event. Join
us as we bring together creatives for a special summer mixer! Plus, RSVP for a
chance to win a Gallery 881 Annual Membership ($99 dollar value)! This is an
exciting night of cool refreshments + cheese and art + design and networking with
artists, interior designers, and creative professionals!

Start off your evening at Gallery 881 (881 East Hastings) with drinks & cheese in the
company of Andrew Latreilles Crossover Exhibition between 5 - 7pm. Then, well
walk over together just 1 block to Retro Modern Designs (727 East Hastings) for an
afterparty featuring hors doeuvres, drinks, and live DJ in their stunning furniture
showroom.
This is a relaxed and inspiring evening to meet artists, designers, and creative
professionals as we casually explore art and interiors while celebrating summer in
style.

About Gallery 881

Gallery 881 promotes the photographic arts through exhibitions, art sales, public
events, and other visual art-based programming. Committed to fostering a vibrant
and inclusive arts community, Gallery 881 provides a platform for diverse voices for
emerging, mid-career, and established artists. Through exhibitions, artist talks, and
collaborative programming, we strive to create meaningful connections between
artists and the public. As a space that values accessibility, inclusivity, education, and
creative exchange, our mission is to ensure that art remains open, engaging, and
available to all.

Gallery 881 grateful to our sponsors, including PrintMaker Studio, is a Canson
Infinity Certified Print Lab and custom finisher and picture framer. Please contact the
gallerist for acquisitions of art + design. Your patronage of PrintMaker Studio directly
supports the free and accessible programming of Gallery 881.

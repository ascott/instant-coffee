TOQUE 2011 - Western Front’s Annual Fundraising Craft Sale
Friday, December 9 from 6-10pm
Saturday, December 10 from 11am-4pm
Western Front, 303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver

TOQUE is less than a week away! Please join us on December 9th and 
10th at this unique event, featuring a curated selection of talented 
artists, designers and artisans.

Twenty-three invited vendors contribute to Toque this year, with wares 
ranging from books, cards, jewellery, textiles and ceramics, to one of a 
kind art objects. Check out our blog!

Come by for great shopping, cocktails on Friday night and snacks on 
Saturday. Also on Saturday is a kids' craft table courtesy of Collage 
Collage. There will be on-site book binding, an outside surprise by Tin 
Can Studio, and a fabulous raffle prize to be won, courtesy of Whoa! 
Nellie Bikes. Also available are limited edition commissioned tea 
towels by artist collective Instant Coffee and exclusive Western Front 
publications.

Proceeds from Toque support the Western Front Society, an artist run 
centre and non-profit registered charity. Held in the 1920s heritage 
setting of the Western Front in Vancouver, this fundraiser is a great 
way to support your artistic community.

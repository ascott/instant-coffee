Arnica Artist Run Centre
 
Call for Submissions
 
Arnica invites emerging artists to submit works for display in The Stairwell Gallery.  The Stairwell Gallery is an alternative exhibition space located in the stairwell between Arnica’s street-level entrance and the Main Gallery.  This space has prime street visibility for passers-by and visitors to Arnica.  
 
We anticipate the privilege of showing various sizes of exhibitions including single artworks, series of works and site-specific projects, with all media considered—from the classic to the experimental. Priority may be given to Artists whose work considers, enhances, or anticipates the work of the featured exhibitions in the Main Gallery. 
 
Arnica is unable to pay CARFAC fees at this time, however we do provide the gallery space for free as well as covering all expenses for the production and distribution of promotional materials.

Deadline for submissions is May 23rd, 2008
 
Please send your proposal to:
 
The Stairwell Programming Committee
Arnica Artist Run Centre
232 Victoria St.
Kamloops, BC.
V2C 2A2

Fabiola Carranza and Sydney Hermant present:
J'aim faim, J'ai froid (I'm Hungry, I'm Cold) Sing for Your Supper Open Mic
with MC Casey Wei and special guests: Monique Levesque and Nikki Never and
... you?

Thursday March 3, 7-10pm
Burrard Marina Field House
1655 Whyte Ave

The premise for this feminist open mic is the extended hat or hand, tips,
the barter, trade or collectively made object, in the form of singing for
your supper.

Meal provided for performers.
Everyone Welcome!
Libations by Unique New York Bar Service.

For those interested in taking the stage sign up here:
[http://cagsing.eventzilla.net/](http://cagsing.eventzilla.net/)

Open mic has: a piano, amplification, mics. BYO guitar, harmonica, violin,
banjo, gutbucket, washboard, etc. if some minor accompaniment is desired,
please contact openmicchek@gmail.com

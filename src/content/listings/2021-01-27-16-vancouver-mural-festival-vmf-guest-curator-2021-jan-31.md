POSITION TITLE: Guest Curator, 2 positions

APPLICATION TIMELINE: Open until Jan 31. Shortlisted applicants contacted by Feb 8

CONTRACT: Feb 15 - September 15, 2021

LOCATION: Vancouver, BC

REPORTING TO: Director, Creative Projects

COMPENSATION: P/T Contract, VMF will make an offer commensurate with experience

VMF is dedicated to evolving how our curatorial program integrates and supports the work
of BIPOC artists and cultural producers, while keeping a strong focus on creating
opportunities for members of the three local Coast Salish Nations. We are looking to fill two
positions for Guest Curators, one of whom will be a member of the Musqueam, Squamish
or Tsleil-Waututh communities. The two Curators will work closely in a
mentorship and
collaborative capacity. The term for both positions is one year, with the possibility of renewal
for an additional year.

Emerging or Early Career Curator: This position is available for someone in the early stages
of their career, and with a keen interest in programming or curating in the public sphere.
The ideal candidate is looking for opportunities to work with artists, connect with
communities, and learn about the logistics and challenges of curating in public space. We
are looking for a range of relevant skills and experience, which does not necessarily require
past work with mural artists.

Established Curator: This position is available for someone with a strong curatorial voice,
and a range of experience locally, nationally and/or internationally and/or in public space.
This person brings much to the team in experience, network, and mentorship capacity. We
are looking for a range of relevant skills and experience, which does not necessarily require
past work
with mural artists

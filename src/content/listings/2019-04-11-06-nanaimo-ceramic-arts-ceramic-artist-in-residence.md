Nanaimo Ceramic Arts has an opening for June 1st-August 30th Artist-in-residence 
program. Along with providing a sunny studio to work and fire, NCA specializes in 
business planning assistance and product development mentoring. This is a three 
month residency with an option to renew for our Sept-December slot. This is a work 
exchange program with a housing option for those who need it. Artist-in-residence are 
expected to be on site 15hrs a week during our open hours to greet customers and 
assist with maintaining the classroom. Artists have 24hr access to the studio and will 
meet with a Mentor once a week to further their business and artistic development. They 
may submit a gallery proposal to culminate their time with us and are encouraged to 
submit workshop proposals. Apply at www.nanaimoceramicarts.com or send further 
inquiries to nanaimoceramicarts@gmail.com

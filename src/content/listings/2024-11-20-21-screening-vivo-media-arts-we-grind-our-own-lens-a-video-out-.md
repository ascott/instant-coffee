VIVO Media Arts
We Grind Our Own Lens: A Video Out Screening
Thursday, November 28, 2024
Screening: 7 PM
Doors: 6:30 PM
At VIVO Media Arts (2625 Kaslo St., Vancouver)
FREE! Register here: showpass.com/we-grind-our-own-lens/
More info: vivomediaarts.com/programming/we-grind-our-own-lens/

Join us for a powerful evening of experimental storytelling through film. For our final
screening of 2024, Video Out has brought together a collection of works which aim
to make sense of ourselves, the world, and the lens through which we perceive
them. From deforestation and the persistence of vision, to elegies for aging bodies
and camcorders, to Sufi
stories discovered in cut footage, these six videos ask us to
consider not just the images we create, but the ways in which they are formed.

Genki Ferguson, Video Out Distribution Manager, has selected works by Rachel
Stuckey, Jayce Salloum, nazanin oghanian, Joseph Clark, Zachery Longboy, Pablo
Griff, and Clark Nikolai for this screening. Stay after the screening for a conversation
with the artists!

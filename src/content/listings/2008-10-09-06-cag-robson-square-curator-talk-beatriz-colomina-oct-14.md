Clip/Stamp/Fold: The Radical Architecture of Little Magazines 196X-197X, is
curated by renowned architectural theorist Beatriz Colomina and a group of
PhD students at Princeton University, School of Architecture. The
exhibition takes stock of over seventy little magazines from the sixties
and seventies. It has met great critical acclaim especially for remaining
geographically specific to each city where it is presented. Forty-four
vintage architectural magazines were collected from Vancouver architects
and artists for the 6th presentation of Clip/Stamp/Fold. The exhibition was
first presented in New York at the Storefront for Art and Architecture in 2006.

For Clip/Stamp Fold 6, the CAG has organized a comprehensive programme of
talks, inviting Colomina as well as architects, writers and publishers from
the region to respond to the exhibition.

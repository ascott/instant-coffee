Cedar weaving demonstration with Melvin Williams
March 10, 2-4pm
The Polygon Gallery

Join us on Saturday, March 10 for a live demonstration of cedar weaving with 
Indigenous artist Melvin Williams. Williams, whose cedar mat is featured in the ongoing 
exhibition N. Vancouver, is an accomplished weaver who gathers and prepares his own 
materials. Visitors will have the opportunity to watch traditional Salish weaving 
techniques, interact with the materials, and meet the artist.

An exhibition tour will begin in the lobby of The Polygon Gallery at 1pm, after which 
Melvin Williams will be weaving in the Gallery from 2-4pm. Admission is free.

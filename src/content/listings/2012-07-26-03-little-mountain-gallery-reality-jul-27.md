Reality at Little Mountain Gallery July 19- Aug 2, 2012 GALA NIGHT: 
July 27, 2012

Curated by Tanille Geib, this exhibit features three artists looking at 
reality through the scope of a lens. How do we capture the reality of 
what we see? How do we alter what we see and what we desire to 
show? What delights our eyes and minds? This showcase of diverse 
artists and subject matter creates an exciting mix of visual stimulation. 

Aly Mai takes us on a narrative journey with emotion-induced 
characters bordering consciousness and escapism. 

Charles Ellery blends structure with glitch/databending techniques to 
create a spatial awareness realized with strong colours and unique 
perspectives. 

Craig Mariano-Groza brings a fresh lease on capturing nature through 
his lens. This dialogue of viewpoint vs. pleasure paints a vivid 
naturalistic reality.

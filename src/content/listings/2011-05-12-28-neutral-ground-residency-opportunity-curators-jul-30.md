THE ART OF GIVING ART PROJECT COMES TO VANCOUVER | Call for Artists!
Call for Artists!

THE ART OF GIVING ART PROJECT COMES TO VANCOUVER
PAPERGIRL - A Redefinition Of Street Art Combining Art, 
Philanthropy And Bikes

Art and bikes are nothing new to Vancouver, but put the two together, 
powered by
passionate volunteers and the kindness of artists from all over the 
city and globe,
then you have something new to talk about!

The Art of Giving Art is the slogan of this global movement, which 
began five years
ago in Berlin. Papergirl is a non-commercial art project for all, from 
the amateur to
the professional artist. It aims at taking art out of the galleries and 
onto the streets
via direct delivery to the public in the style of the American paperboy.
The project has taken place in New York, San Francisco, Portland, 
and last year in
Kelowna, and we are excited to be bringing it to Vancouver.

Artists both locally and globally are invited to participate by donating 
their work as part of a week long
exhibition to be held at the Roundhouse Community Arts Centre 
running August
22 to 27.

On the final day of the exhibition, volunteers will come together to
bundle the artwork into rolls, to be distributed by a team of volunteer 
cyclists, to
completely random strangers!

Questions or Want to be involved? check out our blog: 
http://papergirl-vancouver.blogspot.com/

Or email us at:PapergirlVAN@roundhouse.ca

Ink-Making Workshop:
"Drawing with the Land / Diasporic Particles"

Date: Saturday July 5 and/or Sunday July 6, 4:00 - 6:00 pm

Location: Megaspora Studio (near Hastings and Nanaimo)

Free to attend! Sign up: https://forms.gle/JyRWbDXLZgtrPsEb8

Join Sunny Nestler for an ink-making workshop at Megaspora Studio. In this workshop
you will have the opportunity to contemplate your own connection to themes of
diaspora, the land, and biodiversity, while making inks using pigments from common
and surprising sources. There are two workshops; attend one or both. Come as you
are (suitable for all experience levels), ages 13 and up. We will work with substances
that may require extra care such as wearing a KN95-style mask or gloves.

Supported by Sierra Club BC's Mother Tree Local Leaders Program

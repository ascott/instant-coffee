The Maynard poetry magazine (themaynard.org) is seeking 
submissions of
cover art for our October 2016 issue.

The only requirement is that the art involves an orange or oranges in 
some way.

Send a high quality image in the format of your choice to
submission@themaynard.org with the subject heading "Cover Art".

We're also accepting poetry submissions. 

Send us your best and boldest original work to 
submissions@themaynard.org or check out our submission guidelines 
here: http://www.themaynard.org/submissions.html

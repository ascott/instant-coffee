A 4 hour workshop introducing participants to the principles and practice of exposing 
film with the Arri BL 35mm film camera.

Elements include:

The mechanics and chemistry of exposing film
Understanding lenses
Handling film
Loading and unloading film in light and dark conditions
Shooting in natural light

Colour negative film will be shot in this workshop and sent for processing. The results 
will be scanned and made available to participants in HD video format.

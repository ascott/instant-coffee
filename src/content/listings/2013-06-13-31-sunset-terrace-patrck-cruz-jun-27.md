Westcoast Maximalist

new works by Patrck Cruz

Sunset Terrace, 2028 Clark

opening reception June 27th at 7:00 - 11:00

performance at 9:30PM

Lines, dots, marks and colors meet and abandon corners only to reconvene
and chill at a point. Displaced erratic shapes circumnavigate wild and
tamed plateaus. Raw layers converse in unpleasant discourses while
prosthetic fingers arouse sympathetically capsized symbolic scenarios. A
gust of perversion remains stoic and depressed alongside tranquil shaded
strokes. Meek central figures lay bare displays similar to peacock orbs
situated in post menstrual frenzy. Blots and pieces set kaput the gutted
portraits of torn landscapes marching towards a bleak reception. Together
they chant an angelic tune to the discordant notes of influenced barnacles.
Mental blocks eschew material presence through the process of malignant
emotional body, transferred in low speed wireless etheric combustion, hence
a physical attitude. Discrete phenomenon of improvised ninjitsu applied
with coarse aspiration behind ballistic nature. Wild salmonella disguised
in guile suites dipped in caviar html. Stretched, reimbursed, sold and
compressed junctions gravitate to a lesser known paraphernalia. Cream of
consciousness negates reclined xenophobes basking in the groping sun.
Delegates hung and twisted traversed callous personifications, reincarnated
to the point of extinction.

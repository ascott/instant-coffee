" Futile Efforts by Rebecca Wang " at Massy Arts Society: April 21-May 30
Opening Reception: Saturday, April 27, 2-4pm
This is an window exhibition, and is on view 7 days a week

Featuring photography and installation from two interconnected bodies of work, this
exhibition delves into the construction and negotiation of selfhood in a contemporary
diasporic context, through partly fictional, partly autobiographical narratives. " is
a Chinese term denotes futile effort toward a predetermined goal. Using it as a
pessimistic defence mechanism to navigate disappointment, this exhibition explores
how the permeating boundaries between the cultural and the personal, the public and
the domestic, the conscious and the unconscious, impact ones sense of self and ones
understanding of what they can accomplish.

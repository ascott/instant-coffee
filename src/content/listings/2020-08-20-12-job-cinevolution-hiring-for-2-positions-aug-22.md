CINEVOLUTION IS HIRING!
Cinevolution is growing, and we are searching for like-minded team members to 
help amplify our mission of bringing contemporary migrant experiences into the 
global conversation through film and media arts, and challenging conventional 
definitions of
identity, while making media arts accessible to all!

Cinevolution is a grassroots, women-led, migrant-driven non-profit arts 
organization, with a mission to: Promote innovation and critical discourse 
through film and new media art works; Bring new ways of thinking and 
expression into cross-cultural communication; and Foster creative exchange and 
collaboration among filmmakers and media artists in Canada and around the 
world. Sitting at the nexus of art, academia and community, we seek to 
decentralize art while connecting people across cultural, political, geographic 
and linguistic boundaries in order to foster media literacy, stimulate critical 
discourse, and cultivate creativity.

www.cinevolutionmedia.com

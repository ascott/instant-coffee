Curatorial Assistant
Richmond Art Gallery, City of Richmond

This is a professional Art Gallery based position that involves working directly with 
the Curator to assist in the development and facilitation of gallery exhibitions as well 
as provide administrative and curatorial support for the Richmond Art Gallerys 
Collection in accordance with professional standards.

This role is ideal for those passionate about art, exhibition-making, and collection 
care. The ideal candidate will be adept in decision making, prioritizing, multitasking, 
working in a team and working well under minimal supervision. Join our team and 
apply to be a Curatorial Assistant today.

Qualified candidates will have the following:
Completed a University Degree in a related field of study including or 
supplemented by courses in curatorial studies, museum studies or cultural 
management/policy.
A minimum of 1 year of related experience working in a gallery setting working 
directly with artists, art works, conservation and research.

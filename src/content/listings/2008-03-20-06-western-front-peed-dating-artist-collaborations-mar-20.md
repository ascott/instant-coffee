Simon Fraser University’s Visual Arts student Union Presents:

SPEED DATING: ARTIST COLLABORATIONS, BRINGING PEOPLE TOGETHER, FIVE MINUTES AT A TIME.

When: March 20, 2008
Where: Western Front at 8:00pm

In order to benefit emerging artists in our community, students, curators and artists are invited to a social event, with the purpose of exposing art production that occurs in Vancouver Art schools. On the evening of March 20th, 2008, participants are invited to attend Speed Dating for Art Collaborations at the Western Front Gallery at 8:00pm. This social event is a networking opportunity will generate discourse on the new ideas and themes that are recognized by local and international art.

Participants are encouraged to bring pocket size images of their work or to prepare a short program note about their work, history, artistic interests and practice to share with others.
The “speed dating” element of the evening will be similar to musical chairs. Participants will have five minutes to share their work and artistic interests before being signaled to get up and move to the next seat. During these meetings, artist, students and curators are encouraged to make connections for future collaborations.  After the official “meet & greet”, participants have an opportunity to eat, drink, mingle and chat with potential collaborators. The goal is to inspire each other to engage in new projects. Projects resulting from these collaborations will be showcased and auctioned during a second evening of meeting.

Blockchains & Cultural Padlocks Research Showcase

June 21 - September 7, 2019

Participants: Julian Hou, Sharona Franklin, Tiziana La Melia, Ron Tran, Christian Vistan, 
DOMA, Maksym Rokmaniko, Francesco Sebregondi, Francis Tseng

This spring research from the Blockchains & Cultural Padlock Research initiative by Artist 
Researcher Julian Hou and Research Partner DOMA, was presented at the 
Blockchain@UBC Summer Institute and Annual Conference. Please visit Pollyanna - 
Library for an open storage display of a textile portfolio developed by Julian Hou with the 
Associate Artists, Sharona Franklin, Tiziana La Melia, Ron Tran, and Christian Vistan, as 
well as a multi-player online game currently under development by DOMA: Maksym 
Rokmaniko, Francesco Sebregondi and Francis Tseng.

Read More @ http://polly-anna.ca/activity/blockchains-cultural-padlocks-research-showcase

Supported by the Canada Council for the Arts Digital Strategy Fund

Pollyanna - Library is a cultural infrastructure operated by 221A that displays and 
collects print material, audio, videos, objects, and artworks from 221As Research Initiatives 
and Fellowship program. Pollyanna -Library also produces educational programming 
" talks, performances, screenings, reading groups, and classes " that animates and 
develops the collection.

Accessibility
The library has a 1.8m (6) wide double set street front entrance with manually operated 
outward-swinging doors. An interior 6cm (2.5) high by 60cm (24) long ramp spans the 
width of the doorway. The bathroom is not wheelchair accessible. Please contact us to make 
arrangements to best accommodate your needs.

Unceded Territory
221A acknowledges that the area called Vancouver is within the unceded Indigenous 
territories belonging to the Musqueam, Skxw7mesh-ulh xwumixw (Squamish) and Tsleil-
Watututh peoples. 221A recognizes that the colony of British Columbia was created through 
organized dispossession and colonial violence. 221A seeks to shift its organizational 
practices to work together with Indigenous people to end ongoing violence, dispossession 
and displacement.

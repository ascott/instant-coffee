We are 2 artists, both into visual arts. We live in Amsterdam, and 
sometimes in BC. Last year we spent 8 months in Victoria BC, now we 
are trying to find another place to live in Vancouver/Victoria.

We are the tenants of an apartment and artist's studio in the very heart 
of Amsterdam. And before moving to Canada permanently (we may do 
that after next year) we want to keep this Amsterdam apartment for a 
while longer. 

So, now, we wonder if there is an artist/couple from Vancouver/Victoria 
who would like to do an exchange of home and studio for 1 year. 

We are self-employed, so, as long as there is an internet connection, 
we are fine. We are mainly into drawing and photography, writing too. 
that means we do not need a huge studio, nor a very high ceilinged 
one, but a good place to work and live would be great. 

We can offer the same to an interested artist: the studio -appr 45m2- 
is on the top (4th) floor of an artists building, it has high ceilings and 
lots of light. There is no elevator, which makes the place most suitable

a living, den, bedroom (boxspring 160 x 210 cm), storage room, living,

etc etc). The apartment is in a quiet street, with all the hubbub 
(fleemarket, Opera, museums, shops, zoo) just around the corner. The

From Pinar Del Ro, Cuba, in-person at the Salt Spring Island Public Library

ART AND ANCESTRAL MEMORY

Presentation by artist and curator Juan Carlos Rodrguez Valds, with translation by
Wilson Gonzalez-Rico
Sunday, August 17, 2025, 2 pm-3 pm

Join Cuban artist and curator Juan Carlos Rodrguez as he explores how ancestral
wisdom continues to inform contemporary artistic practice. His presentation shares
the development of his lifes work through an anthropological, psychological,
philosophical, and poetic lens. Working across video, photography, installations, and
performance, he explores ways that art connects us to the roots that define our
identity.

Juan Carlos will also discuss his influential FARMACIA project, which uses art as a
healing process to reintegrate humanity with nature. Drawing on ancestral memory
and imagination, this vision has evolved through educational programs and gained
international recognition at the Havana Biennial, offering a poetic path back to our
essential connection with the earth.

ABOUT JUAN CARLOS:
Curator of the upcoming Unseen Island exhibition opening September 5th at
ArtSpring Gallery, Juan Carlos Rodrguez Valds is a Cuban visual artist, curator,
and educator. Director of the Pinar del Ro Art Museum since 2008, he uses diverse
media to explore cultural memory with metaphysical, philosophical, anthropological,
and Jungian psychological themes. His work has been featured in multiple Havana
Biennials and earned him the Cuban Prize for Visual Arts in 2007.

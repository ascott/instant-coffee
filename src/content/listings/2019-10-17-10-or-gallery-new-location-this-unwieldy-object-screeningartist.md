This Unwieldy Object Screening and Artist Talk
Thursday October 17, 6-8pm
At Or Gallery, 236 East Pender Street.
Please note this is a venue change from the Roundhouse Art & Recreation Center

This Unwieldy Object is a research drama and personal investigation into the colonial myth of
the dinosaur. On a road trip through the dusty heart of the USA, a researcher intends to make
physical contact with a phantasy, traversing a mythical non-place between East and West,
masculine and feminine, material and projection, past and future. Meetings with various
paleontologists, fossil hunters, sculptors, and scientists on both
sides of the American Frontier
trace diverse interpretations of the past. Narratives of natural history and progress intermingle
and digress as the reconstructed bodies of dinosaurs become less and less comical in the
background of the brutal history of European settlement in indigenous territories. The story ends
in the middle, in Rapid City, South Dakota, at the foot of an imported Berlin Wall fragment from
the researchers home country, but the journey continues.

Exhibition Tour of Graeme Patterson: Secret Citadel

Surrey Art Gallery

Wednesday March 2, 7:309:00pm

An enormous fabric-covered mountain, life-sized bleachers, twin bunk beds,
a player piano have taken up residence at the Surrey Art Gallery this
winter. Graeme Patterson: Secret Citadel on Wednesday, March 2 from 7:30 to
9:00pm. Surrey Art Gallery Curator Jordan Strom will lead you on a guided
tour of this fascinating narrative exhibition that is as rich and detailed
as the miniature scenes contained within each sculptural piece. The event
is free.

In Secret Citadel, New Brunswick-based artist Graeme Patterson tells a
story of friendship through four large-scale sculptural installations
complemented by close to two dozen stop-motion animations and live action
video loops. An anthropomorphized bison and cougar represent two boys in
this visual coming-of-age tale that is as playful as it is poignant.
Although based on specific memories of the artist's past, there's something
for everyone to relate to in Secret Citadel through its universal themes of
love and loss, play and competition, companionship and loneliness. You'll
journey from childhood to adulthood as you are led from piece to piece,
enriching your understanding of contemporary art in this exhibition that
triggers are own memories and experiences.

Co-curated by Melissa Bennett and Sarah Fillmore, Secret Citadel is on tour
from the Art Gallery of Hamilton and Art Gallery of Nova Scotia. Surrey Art
Gallery is the only contemporary art museum presenting this national
touring exhibition in British Columbia.

Surrey Art Gallery gratefully acknowledges the financial assistance of the
City of Surrey, BC Arts Council and the province of British Columbia, the
Canada Council for the Arts, and the Surrey Art Gallery Association.

block east of King George Blvd, in Bear Creek Park. Phone: 604-501-5566 or

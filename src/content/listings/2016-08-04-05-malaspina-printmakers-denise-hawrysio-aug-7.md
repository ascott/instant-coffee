Artist talk: Denise Hawrysio
Sunday, August 7, at 2pm to 3pm

Malaspina Printmakers, 1555 Duranleau Street, Vancouver

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to present an artist talk with Denise
Hawrysio, guest curator and exhibiting artist of the current group
exhibition A Lot of Things Have Happened. The exhibition offers a rare
opportunity to see work from the UK by artists who use a variety of media
to explore process. Using chance, structure, or system, many of these works
employ a variety of strategies to disrupt intuition and interrupt the
measured performance of art-making. This artist talk will be held on the
final day of the exhibition, and Hawrysio will speak about her own
process-based printmaking practice.

Denise Hawrysio, a Canadian-born artist living in London since the
late-1990s, was born in Toronto and received a BFA from Queen's University
and MFA from the San Francisco Art Institute. She works in a variety of
media, including site-specific installation, book works, and print media.
Hawrysio has received many fellowships for artist residencies and has
exhibited and taught widely, and her book works are held in many public
collections. She has previously exhibited at Malaspina Printmakers, with a
solo exhibition The Beaver Still Needs a Log in 2013, and in the group
exhibition Surface Tension in 2011.

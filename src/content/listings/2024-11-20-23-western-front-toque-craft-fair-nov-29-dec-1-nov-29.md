Toque Craft Fair
Nov 29 - Dec 1, 2024

Toque Craft Fair is a holiday market and
fundraiser hosted by Western Front. Explore
Western Fronts heritage building while you shop a curated selection of textiles,
ceramics, jewelry, print, accessories, apothecary and homeware by over fifty BC-based
artists, craftspeople and designers. Toque is all about coming together, meeting artists
and connecting with whats made in our community.

Admission is by donation in support of Western Front's artistic program.

Learn more at toquecraftfair.com

helen pitt gallery

The Helen Pitt Gallery is accepting applications for the position of
Director / Curator.

The Director / Curator is responsible for programming and all
administrative aspects of the gallery. This is a three-year contract
position that begins on January 15, 2009, with an annual salary range of
$30 000 - $36 000. The Director / Curator reports to the Board of
Directors, but functions independently in the administration of the gallery.

The Helen Pitt Gallery is a non-profit artist run center dedicated to the
promotion of experimental contemporary art that addresses social,
political, cultural, and critical issues outside of the mainstream
commercial art practice. Emerging artists, critics and curators continue to
find opportunities and support at the Helen Pitt Gallery. The gallery is
committed to showcasing challenging works from local, national and
international artists in all media. The gallery provides curatorial,
technical and administrative support to exhibiting artists.

Applicants should have a thorough knowledge of contemporary art and be an
active participant in the arts community. They should have demonstrated
experience in administration, including financial management; previous
fundraising and grant-writing experience is considered an asset. Strong
oral and written communication skills, computer literacy, and organization
are all essential to this position. A relevant undergraduate degree is
mandatory; a master's degree will be considered an asset.

Applicants should submit a one-page letter of interest, addressing their
curatorial focus, role in the arts community and vision for artist-run
centres. Please also include a current CV (no more than three pages) and a
list of three references with current contact information.

Applications must be received by November 1, 2008.

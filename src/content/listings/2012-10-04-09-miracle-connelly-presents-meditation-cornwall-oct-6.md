Meditation Cornwall
Curated by Jenifer Papararo

October 6, 2012 between 4 pm and midnight /East Van Studios at 870 East 
Cordova, Vancouver.

The exhibition features a selection of artworks and oddities from the homes of 
local artists, curators and collectors. Much of the work was acquired as gifts, 
offered as exhibition take-aways or first distributed as ephemera with a few of 
the works 'found' in small second hand stores. For one night these private and 
personal works, often attained through friendship or trade will be on public 
view, reflecting a barter and gifting system of exchange as well as giving a 
glimpse at some cherished and never-exhibited works. - JP

Carnaval del Sol 2020 is not cancelling, it is expanding Across the Americas! Latinos
always bring the sun to Vancouver, Canada and beyond! Check out all the surprises at
www.carnavaldelsol.ca/carnaval-del-sol/

From July 15 to August 9, we will host many wonderful activities where everybody is
very welcome! You can learn about Latin American culture by experiencing food, music,
art, and much more.

From July 15 to August 8, enjoy the online series Across the Americas
(www.carnavaldelsol.ca/across-the-americas/)

From July 18 to August 4, our Latin American Experience will present four different
events:
- July 18: www.carnavaldelsol.ca/art-blockchain/
- July 25:
www.carnavaldelsol.ca/indigenous-territories/
- July 30: www.carnavaldelsol.ca/flourishing-women/
- August 4: www.carnavaldelsol.ca/flamenco-night/

From August 7 to 9, we also bring Carnaval del Sol to Restaurants around Metro
Vancouver: www.carnavaldelsol.ca/restaurants/. Reservations open soon!

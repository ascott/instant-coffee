Alice Ming Wai Jim | MAY 12

in China
The Different Worlds of Cao Fei: Critics Talk with Alice Ming Wai Jim
May 12, 2pm
Free

Our computer age has produced many online, interactive parallel 
worlds. This talk examines RMB City Planning and Apocalypse 
Tomorrow (both by Cao Fei) in relation to the numerous artworks, 
interactive exhibitions, and events that the art of Cao Fei in the 
simulated online environment known as Second Life has spawned over 
the last three years. Officially opened to the public in 2009, RMB City 
(2008-2011) is arguably the most widely acclaimed Second Life art 
project, in both the Second Life and Real Life art worlds. From platform 
to surfing game, this project speaks to an expanding cultural brokerage 
between the art world and virtual economies as well as the role gaming 
and social media play in questioning these developments.

Both the talk and exhibition are presented as part of Yellow Signal: New 
Media in China.

About Alice Ming Wai Jim

Alice Ming Wai Jim is Associate Professor of Contemporary Art at 
Concordia University, Montreal. She was Curator of the Vancouver 
International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art (Centre A) from 2003 
to 2006. Her main fields of research are in contemporary Asian art and 
Asian Canadian art with a particular interest in recent media art, 
theories of representation, and the relationship between remix culture 
and place identity. 

About the exhibition Cao Fei: Simulus

In the exhibition Cao Fei: Simulus, visitors are able to experience an 
interactive game environment, and two videos constructed from events 
that have taken place in Second Life. In these works, the dynamic new 
urban landscapes of China are re-imagined and used to inform a future 
threatened by uncertain ecological transformation.

About Yellow Signal: New Media in China

Yellow Signal: New Media in China consists of six exhibitions and one 
film screening taking place in various venues throughout Metro 
Vancouver between March and September.

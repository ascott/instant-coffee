"unearthed: Mitra Mahmoodi & Jamal Tabasinejad"
Art Gallery at Evergreen
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam, BC
Wednesday, May 22 | 6 - 8PM

Adobe buildings-uncommon in the wet, mild climate of British Columbia-are a fixture
of Iranian desert architecture. This sophisticated and ancient architectural style uses
sun-dried clay bricks to construct a diverse array of structures, from humble domes
to vast citadels and intricate ziggurats. The magic of adobe construction has
captivated the imagination of two Iranian Canadian artists: painter Jamal
Tabasinejad and ceramic artist Mitra Mahmoodi. Their joint exhibition delves into the
artists' shared fascination with adobe architecture, with a focus on representation in
painting and
abstraction in sculpture.

Celebrate the opening of unearthed and enjoy refreshments served in our lake-side
lobby. Remarks with the artists at 7PM. This event is free to attend and wheelchair
accessible. Drop-ins welcome.

"unearthed" is on view from May 18 - July 21, 2024. Learn more about the
exhibition: https://evergreenculturalcentre.ca/upcoming-exhibition/

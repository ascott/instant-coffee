Centre A: Vancouver International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art is now accepting
submissions to add to the unique selection of works by local artists and creators
offered in our gallery bookstore and giftshop, the Centre A Boutique. Zines, art books,
ceramics, socks, totes, jewelry, postcards you make it, well sell it!

Have your creations displayed and sold alongside works by emerging and established
artists from all over the Lower Mainland while supporting gallery operations and
exhibitions! Artists will receive 60% of the retail price of sold items on a quarterly basis,
plus a Centre A membership and online exposure when you sign a consignment
agreement with us.

Submission is
accepted on a rolling basis, but if you would like to get your items
featured as part of our reopening party on March 1st (details coming soon), please
submit before 11:59pm PDT, February 11th.

Space is limited and only select submissions will be contacted.

To submit, please fill out the Boutique Consignment Submission Form:

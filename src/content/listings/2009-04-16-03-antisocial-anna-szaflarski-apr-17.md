HELEN MAJOR / A DOCUMENTARY ACCOUNT
works by Anna M. Szaflarski

Antisocial Gallery
337 Main Street
Vancouver, B.C

Opening: April 17, 2009. 8pm

Antisocial presents, "Helen Major / A Documentary Account", an exhibition and book launch from 
emerging artist Anna Szaflarski, that is framed around her explorations of non-fictional adventure.
This exhibition is part of a larger adventure narrative that focuses on the interactions between the 
artist and her illusive and mysterious subject, Helen Major. In these works Szaflarski is interested 
in developing a narrative through the documentation of real events and then combining them 
with the suspenseful tactics of paperback literature and video journalism.

Anna M. Szaflarski graduated from Emily Carr University in 2008 for which she produced her first 
book, Documents of Suspicious Non-Events; a project that introduced themes of non-fictional 
adventure accounts into the artist’s practice. Aside from narrative projects, Szaflarski works 
primarily in video, photo and drawing. She is a member of the publishing collective AKV Berlin, 
and is in the process of moving her practice from Vancouver to Toronto.

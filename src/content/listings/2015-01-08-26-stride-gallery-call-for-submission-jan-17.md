/STRIDE SUBMISSION DEADLINE: JANUARY 17, 2015

Stride is currently accepting submissions for the MAIN SPACE and PROJECT
ROOM as well as OFFSITE projects. Proposals received by 5pm (MST) on
Saturday, January 17 will be considered for programming as early as April,
2015. To read more about exhibition and offsite project opportunities, and
to view the complete submission requirements and floor plans, please visit
the website: www.stride.ab.ca/submissions.html

Stride supports artistic pursuits that combine innovative creative
expression with informed critical discourse and promotes recognition of
emerging, mid-career and established artists by providing opportunities for
community engagement with contemporary art practices.

Artists are encouraged to follow the online submission guidelines very
carefully and to e-mail their submission package in .zip format to
info@stride.ab.ca with the subject line
ATTN: CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS JANUARY 2015.

FAXED, MAILED, OR INCOMPLETE SUBMISSIONS WILL NOT BE REVIEWED.

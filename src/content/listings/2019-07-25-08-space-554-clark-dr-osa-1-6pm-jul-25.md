Open Source Arts

1st Show: July 15th to July 25th, 2019
Closing reception: Thursday, July 25th, 6PM to 10PM;

2nd Show: July 27th to August 7th, 2019
Closing reception: Wednesday, August 7th, 6PM to 10PM.

Open Source Arts (OSA) is a new experimental curatorial project by Avery Hannig and
Stephanie Wu. It has manifested during the Shumka Satellite Residency at SPACE. The
projects mandate is to cultivate relationships between artists through making and
conversing. Works featured in the show have been developed in a critical community
informed by shared critical texts, feedback from guest critics and peer review.

This is the first of two OSA back-to-back exhibitions taking place between from July 15th
- August 7th. The group exhibition features the work of emerging artists prompted to
explore the concept of transgressive languages in response to John McHales essay
Plastic Parthenon and Four Selections by Experiments in Art and Technology; From
"The Garden Party"; From 9 Evenings: Theatre & Engineering [Press Release]. This
project aims to probe transdisciplinary ways of thinking in the pursuit of uncovering and
investigating structures and intersections between hyper-specialized schools of thought
and making.

Participating artists include: Joni Cheung, Ben Evely, Stephanie Gagne, Avery Hannig,
Phoebe Huang, Sunroup Kaur, Alina Senchenko, Isaiah Wolf Somsen , Neil Young and
Stephanie Wu

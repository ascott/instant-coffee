TUESDAY, SEPTEMBER 27th, 7:00 pm | ARTIST TALK & SCREENING Neïl 
Beloufa Artist Talk & Screening 

exhibition view, As Far as we know, Neïl Beloufa, Western Front, 2011. Photo by 
kevin Schmidt

The Western Front is pleased to invite you to an artist talk and screening with 
Neïl Beloufa on Tuesday, September 27th at 7 pm. Beloufa’s exhibition As Far 
As We Know is currently on view at the Western Front until November 05, 2011. 
The exhibition features two new moving image works, which were 
commissioned and produced by the Western Front over a month long residency 
in the summer.

A selection of video works by Beloufa will be screened throughout his artist talk, 
which include:

April second (2007), 13 minutes
Kempinski (2009), 14 minutes
Sans Titre (2010), 15 minutes
Sayre and Marcus, Chapter 1 (2010), 7 minutes

Neïl Beloufa is an artist who lives and works in Paris, France. His work has 
previously been exhibited at The New Museum, (New York), Manfiesta 8 (Murcia, 
ES), Chung-King Projects (Los Angeles), White Box (New York) and the Palais 
de Tokyo (Paris). He’s represented by Francois Ghebaly Gallery (Los Angeles) 
and Balice Hertling (Paris). His film Sans Titre (2010) recently won the Grand 
Prize at the Oberhausen Film Festival in 2011 and it was included in the 
Wavelengths programme at the Toronto International Film Festival 2011.

With thanks to the Consulate General of France in Vancouver and Institut 
Francais for their generous support of this exhibition.

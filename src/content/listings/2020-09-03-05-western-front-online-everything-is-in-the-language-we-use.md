| SEP 3

While we are closed for install, a few final components of the exhibition 
Everything is in the language we use are now available online!

Throughout its run, Emma Hedditch facilitated a series of proposals that 
engaged with some of the administrative labor involved in running Western Front 
as an institution, and the exhibition itself. Hedditch is interested in working 
through, and working in, different roles in the production of artistic value, and 
working with others to whom those roles and the work they carry out are 
valuable.

Two of these proposals focus on structural and administrative questions within 
Western Front. In the first, Hedditch conducted inquiry into the various roles at 
Western Front. Sent as questions to staff, this proposal serves as a kind of 
accounting for and reassessment of labor: who does what and what it is for? 
Hedditch collected the responses she received, and
compiled them with her own 
response as an artist into a document. For the second proposal, Hedditch made 
an instructional work that connects to the artist lodging at Western Front. 
Considering this as a resource that the organization possesses, Hedditchs 
instruction to the staff is to be able to live here. These materials will be posted on 
the exhibitions web page as downloadable PDFs.

Lastly, an audio description of Lis Rhodes film Journal of Disbelief (2000-2016) 
will be shared online as a video to stream with a supplemental soundtrack by 
artists Jae Lew, Cori Coutu, Emma Hedditch, and Tiffany Muoz. This audio 
description is a precondition for inclusion of low and no vision audience 
members, and the labor and study amongst the group who came together to 
produce it. This element will remain online for streaming through Sunday, 
September 6, 2020.

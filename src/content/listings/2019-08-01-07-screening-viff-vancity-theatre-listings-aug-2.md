In 1930, Luis Buuel was the foremost surrealist filmmaker in the world, penniless, and 
ostracized. Inspired by tales of the poorest region in Spain, Las Hurdes, Buuel set out 
to film Land Without a Bread, a 27-minute documentary that would confront his 
audience with humanity in its cruelest state. He would shock his audience out of their

Amateurs
GABRIELA PICHLER, SWEDEN, 2018, 102 MIN.
SAT AUG 3 - 3:45PM SUN AUG 4 - 3:00PM
TUE AUG 6 - 6:15PM THU AUG 8 - 8:20PM
BUY TICKETS | TRAILER | VARIETY REVIEW
This delightful Swedish comedy about two teen besties making an i-phone portrait of 
their small town - initially to lure a supermarket chain to the area - is the kind of movie 
we need more of. Its a simple, sweet film that resonates with timely issues around 
globalism, diversity, immigration, and, yes, cinema. The girls film-within-the-film may be 
amateurish, but their point of view gets closer to the truth than any slick, professional big 
budget production. more

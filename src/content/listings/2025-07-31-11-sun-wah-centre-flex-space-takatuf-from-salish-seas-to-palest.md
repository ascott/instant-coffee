Takatuf: From Salish Seas to Palestine

Sun Wah Centre, flex space

August 2 " 5; 12 " 6 PM (Exhibition Open at LG Sun Wah Centre)

Join us in celebrating the opening of Tak-tuf: from Salish Seas to Palestine on
Saturday, August 2, at the BCA flex space in Sun Wah Centre from 6-9pm

Presented in partnership with Centre A, The Vancouver Black Library, and The
Burnaby Art Gallery, this group exhibition and art sale features the work of 22
emerging artists from the West Bank. Works are presented and available for
purchase as high quality inkjet prints, from $30-$75. All funds raised directly support
the artists.

After nineteen months of one of the most brutal genocides of contemporary history,
through the use of the Western weaponry and with the most death faced against the
people of Gaza, the question still remains: what is the capacity for art to express
these atrocities, from the heart of the atrocities themselves?

All the works of Tak-tuf: from Salish Seas to Palestine depict the Palestinian
experience with a Palestinian brush. Tak-tuf brings artworks across colonial borders
from the besieged West Bank to the unsurrendered, occupied territories of the
Musqueam, Squamish, Tsleil-waututh Coast Salish peoples, to express in the heart
of Western empire, the united Palestinian body. Tak-tuf contemplates the role of art
in shaping narratives drawn from lived experiences, moving from art as merely a
passive reflection, towards a vital force of change.

Takatuf, meaning shoulder-to-shoulder, offers work that is brimming with emotions
and feelings that manifest through a diversity of artistic media including weaving,
painting, photography, and sculpture, all represented in print.

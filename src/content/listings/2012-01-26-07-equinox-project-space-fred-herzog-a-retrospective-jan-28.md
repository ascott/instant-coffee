EQUINOX PROJECT SPACE - Fred Herzog: A Retrospective

Opening Reception: Saturday January 28th, 2 to 4pm

On the occasion of our 40th anniversary, Equinox Gallery is very 
pleased to announce the launch of EQUINOX PROJECT SPACE. This 
12,000 square foot building in East Vancouver will offer expanded 
possibilities for exhibitions and dialogue on contemporary art. The 
inaugural exhibition, FRED HERZOG: A RETROSPECTIVE, is a survey 
of 130 photographs made form 1952 to the present. This important 
exhibition situates Mr. Herzog as a premier figure in the development of 
colour photography.

EQUINOX PROJECT SPACE is located on the former Finning Lands at 
the Great Northern Way Campus.

Directions:
The Project Space is located at 525 Great Northern Way between Main 
Street (2nd Avenue) and Clark Street (6th Avenue). The building can 
be accessed via Great Northern Way or East 1st Avenue.

Hours:
Starting February 2nd, the Project Space will be open Thursday to 
Saturday, from 12 to 6pm.

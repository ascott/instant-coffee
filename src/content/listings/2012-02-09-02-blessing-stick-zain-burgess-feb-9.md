ZAIN BURGESS 'THE FOG' | BLESSING STICK, WINTER BEACH 
TOUR, TRAFALGAR BEACH | THURS FEB 9TH, 6-8PM

For the Winter Months of 2012, Blessing Stick will present one artist 
project per month, on the beaches along the Vancouver - Victoria 
Shorelines, connecting the communities of Blessing Stick and the 
Ministry of Casual Living (Victoria, BC). http://www.mocl.com/

For this first exhibit, Blessing Stick has invited Zain Burgess to present 
his work titled 'The Fog,' a version of John Carpenter's 1980 horror film 
"The Fog," but emptied of content, so that only 12 minutes of beautiful, 
rolling fog remains. Although Zain has many homes, he currently lives 
and works in Vancouver BC. Please come at 7pm to Trafalgar Beach 
from 6-8pm.

Zain Burgess was born and raised in Deep Cove, North Vancouver. He 
received an Associates Degree in Sociology and Philosophy at 
Capilano University and received a B.F.A. from Emily Carr University of 
Art and Design with supplementary studies in Berlin at Weissensee 
Kunsthalle, University of Art and Design. Heâ€™s been living in between 
Berlin and Vancouver since, basing himself out of Vancouver. His work 
consists of installation, performance, video and sculpture that works 
with ideas of the cinematic phenomenology and the speculative 
catharsis of experience. He was recently featured in shows: Collective 
Show Cruise, New York (2011), Dependent Art Fair, New York (2011), 
Galerie Traktor, Berlin (2011), Silvershed, Los Angeles (2011), and a 
solo show at Every Letter In The Alphabet, Vancouver (2010). 
http://www.zainburgess.com/

Previous exhibits include Marlaina Buch and Rohanna Gibson's 
'Garden Beach Hardbodies' project, shown on Cattle Point Beach, 
Victoria BC on February 4th, http://www.marlainabuch.com/ 
Upcoming exhibits include Dawn Johnston who will be working with a 
Gulf Island beach in March 2012 
http://www.johnstondawn.blogspot.com/ 

All exhibits within the WINTER BEACH TOUR will embody provisional 
methods of displaying art, using site specific materials, clotheslines, 
strings, rocks as paperweights, generators to fuel projectors run onto 
rocks or white sheets. BYOB, hot toddies in thermoses, and smiles to 
warm by. All events are one time only. Blessing Stick is Heidi Nagtegaal 
and Jenny Lee Craig, for more information, 
http://blessingstick.wordpress.com/

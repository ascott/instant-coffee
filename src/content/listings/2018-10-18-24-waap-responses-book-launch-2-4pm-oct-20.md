Responses book launch + Archive of Inspiration, an exhibition of collected artist 
books and editions.

WAAP, 1129 East Hastings St.

Book launch: Sat, Oct 20, 2018, 2-4 p.m.

Gallery hours: Wed-Sat, 12-5 p.m.

During VABF, WAAP will launch its new book, entitled Responses, a book of texts 
written in response to exhibitions at the gallery. 

Writers include Alex Quicho, Jaclyn Bruneau, Elliat Albrecht, Michael Prokopow, 
Nora O Murchu, Areum Kim, Alex Bowron, Carla Benzan, Sunshine Frere, Yokna 
Patofa, Steffanie Ling, Steven Cottingham, Marina Roy, Jeremy Arnott, Felicia E. 
Gail, Kimberly Phillips and Lauren Fournier. The book has been designed by Erica 
Wilk of Moniker Press and has a limited print run of 250. 

In conjunction to the book launch, a grouping of collected artist books and editions, 
by Michael Morris, Lee Lozano, Lucy Lippard, J. Meejin Yoon, Adam Bloomberg and 
Oliver Chanarin, Image Bank, Zeesy Powers, Gareth Long and Derek Sullivan, will 
be on display to peruse.

Interdisciplinary Forums course at Emily Carr University.
Thursday January 20 @ 3:50 p.m. in the lecture theatre 301NB

Randy Lee Cutler and Kyla Mallett, Text + Image Practice

Whether through performance art, experimental video or 
creative/critical writing, Randy Lee Cutler’s practice explores the 
aesthetic of embodiment. This includes knowledge garnered 
through the human body and its 
environment which foregrounds being and ontology. Working with 
sense experience and spatial adaptation her practice responds 
directly to social interaction. Through photographs, recipes, staged 
cooking shows, or 
interventions in gallery windows, her artwork takes up biopolitics as 
its primary frame and reference. Randy has performed and shown 
work at numerous Vancouver venues including the Vancouver Art 
Gallery, the 
Contemporary Art Gallery, Zeno Gallery, Dadabase, VIVO, the Helen 
Pitt Gallery as well as the Tate Modern (Turbine Hall) for the Western 
Front. Originally from Montreal, she lives in Vancouver where she is 
an 
associate professor at Emily Carr University.

For the past decade, Kyla Mallett has been a practicing artist, 
exhibiting both nationally and internationally. While consistently 
utilizing pseudo-sociological methods of collecting and archiving, 
her work consistently 
deals with the intersection of language and the social realm. She 
has often focused on transgressive activities in such cultural arenas 
as adolescence, girlhood, feminism, academia and art. Early works 
were based 
on investigations of social phenomena such as suburban youth 
culture, covert communication in ‘girl culture’, and gossip as a 
social practice. In recent years she has shifted away from 
adolescence as a subject, 
opening up her investigation of marginalized and idiosyncratic 
voices to a wider social pool; current projects continue to focus on 
the subversive possibilities of language and communication. She 
has received critical 
attention for her work, having exhibited at such institutions as the 
Canadian Cultural Centre in Paris, the Vancouver Art Gallery, 
Vancouver's Contemporary Art Gallery, the Art Gallery of Alberta, 
Presentation House 
Gallery, Artspeak, ThreeWalls in Chicago, and the Mount St. Vincent 
University Gallery in Halifax. Mallett is an Assistant Professor at 
Emily Carr University.

Gallery FUKAI is pleased to announce (M)use. Women As Subject. New works 
by Vancouver collective Phantoms In The Frontyard. 

Guest curated by Pennylane Shen. Feb 3 - 26th. Opening Reception Feb 3rd at 
8pm.

PHANTOMS IN THE FRONT YARD brings together the work of prominent 
Vancouver artists Chad Krowchuk, Jay Senetchko, Jeremy Birnbaum, Michael 
Abraham, Marcus Macleod and guest phantom: Jonathan Sutton.

Phantoms in the Front Yard is a unique collective of figurative artists seeking to 
revive the human subject as muse in the Canadian art scene. Each artist boldly 
counters the notion that figurative art has become the phantom of the fine art 
world, haunting Modernism with its dated and classical reputation.

Inspired by the success of the ON GREAT MEN exhibition, (M)USE or (mis)use 
explores the female form as the long standing muse within classical to 
contemporary art. A complicated and often controversial topic, particularly when 
approached by a group of male artists, (M)USE has forced each member to 
consider his position of power behind the canvas.

Job Description: Festival Communications and Events Coordinator

The Capture Photography Festival is seeking a qualified and
enthusiastic Festival Communications and Events Coordinator for the
April 2016 Festival.

This is a 5-month part-time (24 hours a week) contract from late
January to May 2016.

Application deadline: January 5, 2016

The responsibilities of this role include:
- Working directly with Program Director and in collaboration with
Festival Publication Manager to coordinate exhibitions, events, and
public art installations
- Coordinating mail outs and overseeing social media
- Assisting with developing the Capture Speaker Series and
managing its implementation
- Liaising with participating galleries and organizations to ensure up-
to-date information is on hand
- Assisting Festival Publication Manager with scheduling and
production management of the Capture Magazine
- Managing and organizing all image files for the Festival catalogue,
website, and press
- Filing corresponding caption and credit information
- Converting images for a variety of outputs (print, web, large-format
print)
- Responding to press requests from Capture's PR firm
- Assisting with PR, communications, and social media
- Reports to: Program Director; Supervises: Social Media and Admin
interns and events volunteers

Required Skills and Experience
- Postsecondary level education in Art History, Visual Art,
Communications, related discipline, or equivalent combination of
experience and education.
- Familiarity with photography in Vancouver and/or art history would
be an asset
- Experience in event coordination
- Familiarity with Wordpress content management, Mailchimp, and
online Project Management tools (Basecamp and Slack)
- Ability to work with Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, InDesign,
Illustrator)
- Familiarity with social media tools (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram)
- Experience managing volunteers
- Effective communication and interpersonal skills, being able to
exercise sound judgment, tact, and discretion when dealing with others
- Strong organizational and time management skills, being able to
manage multiple tasks and competing priorities within short timelines
- Strong analytical and problem solving skills
- Ability to work under pressure and tight deadlines
- Personal laptop an asset
- Valid driver's license and access to a vehicle is an asset

How to apply:
Send resume and cover letter by email by January 5, 2016 to:
Meredith Preuss, Program Director, Capture Photography Festival:
info@capturephotofest.com

Please state "Festival Communications and Events Coordinator" in the
subject line of your email.We thank all applicants, but only those
selected for an interview will be contacted.

Please note the Capture office will be closed from Dec 21, 2015-Jan 1,
2016 for the holiday season.

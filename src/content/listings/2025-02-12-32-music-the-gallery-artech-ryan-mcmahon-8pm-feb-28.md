Join us for this rare opportunity to
enjoy an intimate evening with award-winning
Canadian recording artist Ryan McMahon.

Whether it has been as a solo act, or with the harmony-driven trio Lion Bear Fox, Ryan
McMahon has toured the globe, giving memorable performances everywhere he's
been for over 20 years now. Having shared the stage with everyone from Burton
Cummings & Buffy Ste. Marie to Mother Mother & Aaron Pritchett, Ryan has proven
himself to be as adaptable as anyone in Canadian music. His new single, "One More
Fire was released in February 2022, and was produced alongside a fleet of top-shelf
talent inside the walls of Vancouver's venerable Bryan Adams-owned Warehouse
Studio. After two years of forced reflection and re-calibrating, Ryan is thrilled to perform
live again and reconnect with friends & fans that he holds so dear through the power of
music.

Date: Friday, February 28, 2025
Venue: The Gallery @ Artech
336 E 1st Ave Vancouver BC
Doors at 7:00pm, music at 8:00pm.

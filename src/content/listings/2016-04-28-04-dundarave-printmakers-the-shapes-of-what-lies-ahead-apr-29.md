The Shapes of What Lies Ahead
Solo Show by Lone Tratt
April 27 - May 23, 2016

Opening: Friday April 29, 4 - 8 pm

These new monotype prints, that have been created over a three year
period, represent an exploration of internal landscapes. Matte surfaces
and simply curved forms are the latest expression of Lone Tratt's long
held fascination with geological forms. Using a neutral palette shocked
with swathes of cyan, she crafts textured stone surfaces with brash,
unapologetic strokes. Her goal is to create a calm spirit that refreshes
the soul.

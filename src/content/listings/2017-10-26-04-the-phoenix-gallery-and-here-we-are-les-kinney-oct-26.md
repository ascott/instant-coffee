'And Here We Are' - a Solo Exhibition/Retrospective of Vancouver artist Les Kinney

Opening Show, Thursday Oct 26th 7pm-10pm
c/o The Phoenix Gallery
45 West Hastings St (back alley entrance)

Kinney's work is about our perception and the beauty within natural form. It is an 
exploration of the point in our brain, where the subconscious influences observation and 
its portrayal. Les considers the vibrancy of colour and the stroke of the brush can be as 
important as the image itself. 

Over time, Kinney's style has evolved in what can best be descibed as "Surrealistic 
Impressionism".

You won't want to miss this solo exhibition, showcasing both recent and retrospective 
works.

Matt Smith - BogScape
Surrey Art Gallery
March 28, 2014 to January 4, 2015

Surrey-based media artist Matt Smith creates a naturalistic sound 
installation based on information gathering systems, such as weather 
monitoring station data and traffic counters, located in or near Burns 
Bog, Delta, B.C.. Smith then translates the data, including estimated 
cellular transmissions, into sound using foley sound effects. The 
resulting soundtrack is pleasantly ambient, but clearly artificial and, at 
times abrasive. The constantly fluctuating sounds are reminiscent of 
the constantly shifting topography of the bog that the source data is 
derived. Yet, the more abrasive metallic sounds, connected by the 
artist to the cellphone traffic, makes evident the invisible 
communications produced by humans that increasingly encroach on 
the natural world. BogScape, invites the listener to reconsider the 
limits of landscape, and the relationship between information and 
nature.

This is the first of three art installations as part of Soniferous Kingdom 
- Open Sound 2014, a multi-phased sound art exhibition that 
examines the relationship between vegetation and sound. The other 
two participating artists are Stephanie Loveless (New York) and Paul 
Walde (Victoria).

About the Artist

Matt Smith has been involved in media arts since the 1990s. While 
working for the Ars Electronica Center in Linz, Austria he co-founded 
the loose collective of technicians and artists, FirstFloor Electronix in 
1995. Since arriving in Vancouver in 1998, he has collaborated in a 
wide variety of art projects as an artist and/or technician. Between 
2002 and 2007 he also co-hosted the highly experimental 
FirstFlooRadioshow on CITR in Vancouver, and produced sound-art 
and radio shows for international broadcast. Personal milestones are 
the founding and operation of the Artist Run Limousine between 
2002-2005, which spawned a series of international productions of 
Audiomobile between 2003-2009. As a member of the Second Site 
Collective, Matt created a environmental sound piece, also using his 
remote-sensing software, at Sun Yat Sen Garden this spring. Most 
recently a solo-show this summer (2013) at CSA in Vancouver 
featured photography related works from 2008 to 2013.

Open Sound 2014 - Sonorous Kingdom is a Surrey Art Gallery 
exhibition program that was developed in 2008 to support the 
production and presentation of audio art forms as part of 
contemporary art practice. Open Sound seeks to feature the leading 
edge of audio art, and has included site specific installation, radio 
experiments, ecological practices, and other investigations into the 
limits of sound today. It is made possible with the ongoing support of 
the City of Surrey, the Canada Council of the Arts and the BC Arts 
Council

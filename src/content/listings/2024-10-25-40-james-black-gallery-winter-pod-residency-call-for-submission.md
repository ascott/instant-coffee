WINTER POD RESIDENCY

CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS
Due October 24th, 2025 11:59PM

1EdJzhJkymlq3zZKysA/viewform

We are pleased to offer a 40 day residency for one group of local artists (4-6
collaborators) for interdisciplinary collaboration from December 1st 2024 through
January 10th 2025.

In the midst of the lockdown, we all learned new languages and ways of being. This
included the idea of a pod in which we committed to building new systems of support
together. Since 2020, we have seen heightened suppression of these new systems in
the form of backlash,
censorship and exploitation of marginalized communities, limiting
the potential of the future. We aim to encourage open collaboration between
underrepresented artists for whom resources have been a barrier to making work.

Pods are encouraged to generate art that delves into the problems, conflicts, sources
of collective joy and inspiration that touch our communities.

Our residency includes work space, show space, access to our tools, as well as a 2025
Group Exhibition CARFAC rate honorarium.

Priority will be given to artists who are 2SLGBTQ, Black, Indigenous, and People of
Colour with consideration towards artists impacted by intersections of inequality (such
as income, disability, chronically ill, neurodivergence, etc.).

Pre-existing collectives are welcome to apply as well as newly formed.

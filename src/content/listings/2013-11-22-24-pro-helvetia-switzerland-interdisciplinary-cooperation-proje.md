Pro Helvetia, Switzerland | DEADLINE: DECEMBER 1, 2013 
Interdisciplinary Cooperation Projects New Media  
In accordance with the message on culture for the period 2012-2015, various federal institutions have set themselves the joint target of exploring the impact of digitalisation on cultural production, outreach and reception.  
With its call for «Interdisciplinary Cooperation Projects New Media», Pro Helvetia is promoting both creative artistic work in the new media and technologies area and cooperation among cultural institutions from different disciplines.

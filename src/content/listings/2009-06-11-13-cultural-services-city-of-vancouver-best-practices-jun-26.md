Cultural Services City of Vancouver - BEST PRACTICES MOVING FORWARD
as part of: The Arts Summit - Shifting Ground :: new realities. new
opportunities. new ideas.
(Organized by 2010 Legacies Now in partnership with the Alliance for
Arts and Culture)

Friday, June 26, 2009, 11:15am - 1:15pm, Vancouver Public
Library, Alice Mackay Room
Like all members of the arts and cultural community, the
department of Cultural Services at the City of Vancouver has experienced
significant shifting ground with a new Mayor and Council, a new City
Manager, a new Culture Plan, changes in staff and a whole new economic
reality. In pursuit of the best way forward given this context, Cultural
Services staff welcomes an opportunity to share with you some recent
research on models for the delivery of cultural programs and services at
a municipal level, and how Cultural Services currently operates and
proposed changes based on implementation of the Culture Plan and
community input. Staff also invites your input on best practices moving
forward for the development of a structure that makes sense for the
delivery of cultural programs and services to the community in these
changing times.
There are a few ways to participate:
* Simply attend the session - if you ARE registered for the Arts
Summit. Or;
* RSVP directly to Cultural Services to attend the session - if
you ARE NOT registered. Please note, capacity is limited so RSVP quickly
to ensure a spot! Or;
* Complete the survey. If you attend or not, you can complete a
survey which will be disseminated after the session and will be
available through our website. Please contact Jenny Fung if you wish to
receive notification of the survey by email.

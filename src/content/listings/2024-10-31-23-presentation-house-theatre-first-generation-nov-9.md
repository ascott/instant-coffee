The Gallery at Presentation House Theatre, North Vancouver

First Generation is a group exhibition featuring the art of immigrants whose voices
are often inaccessible to the public: children and older adults. At the beginning of
their journeys, immigrant children are still in the process of developing their cultural
identities when they find themselves in a new country. This is juxtaposed with older
adults, who have a wealth of lived experience and have had the time to create a
body of work that speaks to that. By looking at the art of these two demographics,
this curated show works to bridge the generational gap that immigrants often face as
a result of being separated from their cultural origins.

Please join us for a
community art exhibit opening reception with live music, tea from
Bean Around the World, snacks, and Presentation House bar.

First Generation is curated by artist Annie Hung for the 2024 annual STAND Festival,
a
non-profit event supporting immigrant and refugee artists. Annie Hung is a

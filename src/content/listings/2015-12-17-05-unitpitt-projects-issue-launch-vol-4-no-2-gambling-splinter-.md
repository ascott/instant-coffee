DEC 17 ISSUE Launch VOL. 4 NO 2 // Gambling Splinter Closing
Thursday, December 17th at 7pm we are celebrating a trifecta of endings.

ISSUE Magazine has finished up its latest chrysallis and is ready to
re-emerge as a fresh new winter butterfly. Come see the results of what
seems like 85 months of toiling little hammers: with a new layout,
exhibiton reviews, a conversation with Tiziana La Melia, thoughts about
production and authorship, and more narrative fictions (or truths) bled
from the overwrought minds of LOCAL CULTURAL WORKERS YOU PROBABLY KNOW!

Added bonus: Also see the last days of Rachel & Sarah Seburns' Gambling
Splinter, before it is (possibly) taken underground!

As well, we will be celebating the closing of UNIT/PITT for the holidays --
we need a break!

Magazines! Poker! Bunkers! Holidays! Exclamation points!

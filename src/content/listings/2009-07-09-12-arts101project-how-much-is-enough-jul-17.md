HOW MUCH IS ENOUGH?

July 17-19, 2009

Opening July 17, 6 -9 pm

750 Pacific Blvd., Vancouver, BC

www.arts101project.ca
www.contemporaryartgallery.ca
www.watari.org

ARTS101 is a volunteer mentorship program established by the Contemporary Art Gallery and 
Watari Youth & Family Services, 
where professional artists work one-on-one with youth between the ages of 16-22. "How Much is 
Enough" is an exhibition
featuring work by members and mentors, including Safi Arnold, Melanie Bond, Scott Bowering, 
Tyler Dale, Jamie Dolinko,
Kyle Duske, Serenity Friesen, Michelle Fung, Sam Glennie, Chris Meyer, Tondela Miles, Jonah 
Ocean, Megan Taylor, 
John Vaneltsi, Tara Vickers.

The studio is upstairs at 750 Pacific Blvd., at the Plaza of Nations, between Yaletown and 
Science World.

The opening is Friday, July 17th, 6-9 p.m., and we're there Saturday the 18th and Sunday the 
19th from 12-5 p.m., so please 
come by, see the work, & meet the youth and mentors.

http://www.arts101project.ca

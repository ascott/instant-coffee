Job: Exhibit Preparators - New Media Gallery. (2 positions)
Terms: Part-time, permanent, project-based
Start Date: April 29
Hours: Three 4-5 week blocks per year plus short periods in between exhibitions.
Salary: $26.35 " $30.93 per hour + 12% in lieu of all benefits
Deadline for Applications: March 25, 2022

THE ROLE
Two auxiliary Exhibition Preparators are required to work on a part-time,
project basis to
support the fabrication, installation and de-installation of international, technology-based
art exhibitions at New Media Gallery. This is skilled technical and trades work. It involves
extensive tear-down and construction of walls, along with the installation of international
artworks, technologies, and exhibitions. You will be able to commit, in advance, to the
first four week period of full-time work from April 29 - June 4.

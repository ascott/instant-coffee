SUBMISSIONS DUE November 15, 2016 at 11:59pm

To submit please visit http://capturephotofest.com/submissions/

The 4th annual Capture Photography Festival will take place April 1-28,
2017 around Metro Vancouver and parts of the Lower Mainland.

Capture Photography Festival is now accepting submissions for exhibitions
and events for the 2017 iteration of the Festival. Proposals must be
submitted by November 15, 2016 at 11:59pm.

This call is open to all exhibitions of photography and lens-based works
addressing any subject matter. Additionally, we welcome proposals for
innovative events.

EXHIBITION PROPOSALS:

Applicants can choose either the juried "Selected Program" or the
non-juried "Open Program".

The "Selected Program" is open to exhibitions presented in professional
spaces. Capture defines a professional space as any space that operates
full-time as a gallery or exhibition space. This includes public galleries
and museums, commercial galleries, artist-run centres, artist collectives,
etcetera. Proposals submitted to the "Selected Program" will be evaluated
by a jury of artists and arts professionals.

The "Open Program" is open to exhibitions presented in any space, from
conventional gallery spaces, to community venues, to the side of a bus.
Exhibitions can be any length, including one-day pop-ups. Submissions to
this category will not be juried, and will automatically be included in
Capture 2017 if they meet the Festival's mandate.

Exhibition Participation Fees:

The submission fee is $300 (plus GST) for the "Selected Program". All
proposals are subject to a $100 fee upfront, and upon acceptance into the
Selected Program, applicants will be invoiced for the remaining $200 fee
(not-for-profits are exempt from the latter).

The submission fee for the "Open Exhibition" category is $100 (plus GST).

EVENT PROPOSALS:

Event submissions must be related to photography and can include exhibition
openings, talks, panels, tours, workshops, family events, films, and more.
Events will not be juried, and as long as they fall within the scope of the
Festival, they will automatically be included in Capture 2017.

Submission Fees for Events:

There are NO submission fees for events.

HOW TO APPLY:

For more information, please visit:

To apply, please visit: http://capturephotofest.com/submissions/

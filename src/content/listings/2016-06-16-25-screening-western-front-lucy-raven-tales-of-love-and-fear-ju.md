Screening with Artist in Attendance: Wednesday, June 29 @ 7pm
Free Admission

Grand Luxe Hall
Western Front
303 East 8th Avenue
Vancouver, BC

Western Front is pleased to present the International Premiere of 
Tales of Love and Fear, a cinema built for a single film, which 
contains a single, stereoscopic image. The project comes out of 
research Raven conducted regarding the relationship of historical 
bas-relief carvings in the US and India to Hollywood's contemporary 
obsession with 3D, and the outsourced labor used to produce the 
effect.

This film is composed of a single stereoscopic photograph in which 
the right and left eye perspective are split across two projectors 
which counter-rotate from a custom built rig around the room. 
Though the image itself is not a moving image, the projected still 
image is in constant motion, creating a cinema in the round. 

Tales of Love and Fear was commissioned by the Experimental 
Media and Performing Arts Center (EMPAC) at Rensaleer 
Polytechnic Institute, Troy, NY. 

Artist Biography: 

Lucy Raven was born in 1977 in Tucson, Arizona, and lives and 
works in New York. She studied at Bard College's Milton Avery 
Graduate School of the Arts and works with animation, installation, 
sound, and the live format of the illustrated lecture. 

Raven's films and installations have been exhibited internationally, 
including at the MoMA (New York, 2010); the Hammer Museum 
(Los Angeles, 2012); mumok (Vienna, 2013); Images Festival 
(Toronto, 2013); the Yerba Buena Center for the Arts (San 
Francisco, 2014); Portikus (Frankfurt, Germany, 2014); the 
Berlinale and Oberhausen (Germany, 2013/2015) and Wavelengths 
at the Toronto International Film Festival (2014). In 2016 Raven is 
in a two person show with Sam Lewitt at Pilar Corrias (London, 
UK), a solo exhibition at the Columbus Museum of Art (Columbus, 
Ohio) and will participate in the 2016 edition of La Biennale de 
Montral.

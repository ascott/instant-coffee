Improvisation', the first single from Aileen Bryant on 7" vinyl flexi-disc,
accompanied by a folio of pastel artworks by Nick Howe and writing by
Hannah Acton.

This is the second release from Pythagoras in this format, which was
developed to foster collaborative relationships between disciplines.

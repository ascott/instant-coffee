The Ceramic Department at Emily Carr University is hosting a 
Ceramic Auction Gala on Saturday, March 5th, 2011 from 7 to 10pm. 

The auction provides an opportunity for the students to raise funds 
for future educational experiences such as fieldtrips and guest 
lectures during the year. 

This is a wonderful opportunity to see upcoming ceramic work.

In addition to student work, there will be alumni and professionally 
established artists work available for purchase.

CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS:
Please submit:

1) Images of your completed ceramic work for the show.
2) A description of your work.
3) Your name and contact information
4) Title(s) of work submitted and starting bid value.

Work must be submitted by: February 16. 2011 (all day) to Room 
180 in the Ceramics Department

To be submitted to

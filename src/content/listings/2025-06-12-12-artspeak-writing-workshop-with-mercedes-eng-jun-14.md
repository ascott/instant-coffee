Writing Workshop with Mercedes Eng

This writing workshop asks us to contemplate place and unceded land in relation to
our creative production and will engage with the politics of colonial cartography and its
counterpoint, community-generated mapping. Durational cartographer Mercedes Eng
will activate the workshop by situating participants in the Downtown Eastside where
Artspeak gallery is located, as well as the neighbourhoods in/adjacent to the DTES,
Chinatown, Japantown, Hogan's Alley, and Strathcona. A variety of maps"visual,
memorialistic, poetic, embodied"will be deployed as conversational jumping-off points
and writing prompts to consider the colonial origins of mapping; how/when we use
maps in daily life; real-estate developers' branding strategies; mapping as surveillance;
and community-generated mapping as archive, agency, and resistance to
gentrification. Please bring a journal and your curiosity.

Date: June 14th, 2025 11am -2pm at 233 Carrall St. To join RSVP to info@artspeak.ca

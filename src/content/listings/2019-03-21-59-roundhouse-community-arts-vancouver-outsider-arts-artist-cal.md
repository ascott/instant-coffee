Call for Outsider Artists!
Open Through April 15, 2019
2019 Vancouver Outsider Arts Festival
Roundhouse Community Arts & Recreation Centre

August 9-11, 2019

Are you an Outsider Artist 19 years and older residing in Vancouver?
Want to share, learn, and connect? - Ready to perform/show/sell your work?

VOAF is dedicated to supporting self-identified outsider artists who face social exclusion and 
other

visible/invisible barriers to sharing their work.
Applications for Visual & Performing Artists Open Now
Visual Artists: How to Apply
In 500 words or less, please tell us the following:
1. Provide Your Name, Email, and phone numbers
2. Describe how do you identify as an Outsider Artist
3. Tell us about your practice: how did you get started, why do you make art, what 
medium(s) do you
work in? Are you self- taught?
4. Tell us about your ongoing commitment to making art.
5. Tell us about your exhibition history, if any. Previous exhibitions are not a requirement.
Artwork image submission details
1. 2-D and 3-D work is accepted. Maximum width/diameter of artwork is 48"
2. Video/digital media work no more than 5 minutes in duration can be submitted for 
consideration.
3. Attach photos of four (4) artworks as .jpg files attached to your email. If mailing an 
application, please
provide printed copies of the photos. We cannot return original photo prints.
4. Images should be samples of the type of work you create. They do not have to be the 
exact works you
will exhibit if you get to exhibit in the 2019 VOAF.
5. Attach a separate document listing each of the 4 images you are submitting for 
consideration in your
application as the following: See examples in ().
1. Last Name (eq. Smith)
2. Title (eq. Summer Breeze)
3. Year (eq. 2018)
4. Medium (eq. Mixed Media)
5. Dimensions (Width in Inches X Height in Inches) (eq. 24 x 30)

Performing Artists: How to Apply
In 500 words or less, please tell us the following:
1. Name, email, and phone numbers.
2. How do you identify as an Outsider Artist? What kind of work do you make, and why? 
What is your
history of performing? What barriers do you face to sharing your work?
3. Describe your performance including duration (10-30 minutes recommended)
4. Give us some sense of number of performers, needed technical support, space needed 
(indoor,
outdoor, raised stage, roving, interactive...?)
5. Please attach links to videos or be prepared to audition.
Submission e-mail and mailing address can be found at the bottom of the page.
HOW WILL WE CHOOSE?
Our primary value is inclusion of self-identified outsider artists. We look at whether you have 
a committed
creative practice and can benefit from participating in the Vancouver Outsider Arts Festival. 
We exist to
provide opportunities for exhibition, performance, sales, learning, and connection to the 
outsider artist
community in Vancouver, therefore eligible artists applying to the festival for the first time are 
given priority.
We do encourage past VOAF participants to apply.
NEED HELP APPLYING? GOT QUESTIONS?
Come to an Info Session where you will learn about the festival and complete an application.
Bring 4 photos of your artwork, or 4 pieces of art to be photographed.
To reserve your spot, please email voaf@cacv.ca confirming the specific date and your 
name.

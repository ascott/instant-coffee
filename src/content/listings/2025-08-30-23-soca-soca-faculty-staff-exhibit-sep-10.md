ART IN PRACTICE: SOCA FACULTY & STAFF EXHIBIT
September 10-29
An exhibition showcasing recent works by Visual Arts faculty and staff at UFV. Spanning
diverse media and approaches, the show offers a glimpse into the creative practices
that shape, inspire, and mentor the next generation of artists. Featuring works by: Shel
Stefan, Aimee Henny Brown, Tony Latour, Luke Parry, Zaira Ramirez, Paula Funk,
Tetsuomi Anzai, Jen Chew, Andrea vanSchubert, Liz Wurzinger, and more.

School of Creative Arts, UFV, Abbotsford Campus, B136

Hours: Mo-Fr 12-5pm

Opening Reception: September 10, 12.30pm

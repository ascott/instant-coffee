The Gallery Shop at Seymour Art Gallery offers unique handmade gifts by more than 60 
local artists. Artist submissions are accepted on an on-going basis and the Gallery Shop 
Committee will review submissions twice annually.

We STRONGLY recommend that you visit the Gallery Shop before you make a 
submission, so that you can see the space and the items we currently carry. We are 
specifically looking for work which is quite different from the pieces already on display. 
Each of the Gallery Shop artists has their own unique style and therefore if your work is 
similar to that of an artist we already carry, it is very unlikely that your work would be 
selected.

If the Gallery Shop Committee is interested in your online submission, you will be asked 
to bring finished samples for jurying. Accepted artists will start on a three-month trial to 
ensure that the relationship is a good fit for both the artist and the Gallery Shop.

RESTRICTIONS:

1. All work considered must be handmade by the artist and artists must represent their 
own work.

2. We do not accept submissions for consumable items.

3. While we welcome applications from all BC artists, each artists stock must be 
replaced every 3 months and we cannot offer shipping; if the artist will not be able to 
visit the Gallery Shop that often, we regretfully cannot offer representation.

4. The Gallery Shop is a small space. While we do not have formal restrictions on the 
size of artworks, we must consider size when deciding what artworks we will carry.

5. Works on paper must be framed and wired.

If your work is accepted, it is required that you become a member of the Seymour Art 
Gallery Society. You do not need to be a member to apply.

There is a $5 (USD) Slideroom admin fee, but no jury fee

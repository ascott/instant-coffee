Video Communication: Art & Technology Between Canada and Japan, 1967"1985
explores the rich history of influence and exchange between Japan and Canada in the
early, exploratory years of video.

The series launches October 27th with a program by curator Jesse Cumming of
interviews he recorded with Michael Goldberg, co-founder of the Satellite Video
Exchange Society (VIVO) and facilitator of some of the earliest exchanges between
Canadian and Japanese video artists: Moira Simpson and Liz Walker " both
filmmakers, Metro Media producers, and co-founders of the Vancouver feminist film
collective, ISIS Women & Film " and Mihato Taura, videomaker and co-founder (with
Ko Nakajima) of Video Earth Tokyo Collective (est. 1971). Visit:
http://vivomediaarts.com/archive/video-communication/

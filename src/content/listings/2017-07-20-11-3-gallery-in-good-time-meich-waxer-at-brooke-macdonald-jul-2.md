Thursday July 27, 2017, 7:00 PM

65 west Cordova st Appt 405 (Buzz 0405)

Welcome to #3 Gallery! In solidarity with all venues placing third, Vancouvers hottest 
new mobile micro gallery offers exhibition space to established and emerging artists 
alike. With a nod to the lack of feasible community spaces in Vancouvers economic 
jungle, #3 Gallery exists between the private and public realms, situated in artist's 
homes wherein public openings will be held for each new exhibition of work.

For our inaugural exhibition, #3 Gallery presents Meichen Waxer at Brooke McDonald. 

Meichen Waxer Is a interdisciplinary artist based in Vancouver, and recently completed 
her Masters of Fine Art at Emily Carr University of Art + Design. Her practice engages 
with culturally inherited structures of value and their relation to different modes of 
representation, often looking to architecture, ornamentation, and objects that hold 
generational, cultural and nostalgic value. Waxer's interdisciplinary approach to these 
subjects facilitates a fluidic method of engaging with the physical and metaphoric 
occupation of ornamentation, and other culturally inherited structures. 

Brooke McDonald is a visual artist whose practice is based in painting. With an interest 
in gesture, abstraction and the picture plane her work investigates the devices used 
within contemporary painting. McDonald received her BFA from Emily Carr University in 
2015.

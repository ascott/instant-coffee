UBC Curatorial Lecture Series Fall 2009

Nicolaus Schafhausen: Curating in Front of a Local Backdrop

Thursday, November 26, 2009, 6:30 to 8 pm
Room 104, Frederic Lasserre Building
6333 Memorial Road, The University of British Columbia

Nicolaus Schafhausen is Artistic and Managing Director of Witte de With
Centre for Contemporary Art, Rotterdam. He was curator of the German
Pavilion at the 52nd (2007) and 53rd Venice Biennial (2009). As Director of
Frankfurter Kunstverein from 1998 to 2005, he curated thematic exhibitions
on contemporary social issues such as the "local," migration, and a
triology on the phenomenon of globalization. To mark the 100th anniversary
of the birth of Theodor Adorno, he curated, "Adorno. The Possibility of the
Impossible" (2003).

This talk will focus on the traditional European art centre. Operating in
front of a local backdrop, the centres are internationally oriented and
address a public that is often made up of tourists. The centres face a
number of financial and demographic challenges. Though they are
increasingly being integrated into the marketing strategies of cities, they
are being left more and more to fend for themselves financially and the
centres continue to assume their core audience is their local citizenry,
though none of the demographic factors favour this relationship.
Schafhausen will also talk about his current project, "Morality" which he
has initiated as a leitmotif for Witte de With.

The Curatorial Lecture Series is organized by the Morris and Helen Belkin
Art Gallery in collaboration with the Department of Art History, Visual Art
and Theory; the Museum of Anthropology; and the Department of Anthropology;
with the support of the Audain Endowment for Curatorial Studies; and the
Faculty of Arts at The University of British Columbia. This lecture is
organized in collaboration with the Walter Phillips Gallery, Visual Arts at
The Banff Centre; and the Curatorial Practice MA Program at the California
College of the Arts.

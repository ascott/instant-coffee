RITUALS EXHIBITION with BROAD Magazine at Gallery 881
Opening Reception: Saturday, December 9, 2023 from 2 - 5pm

Gallery 881, a lens-based contemporary art gallery, invites you to the RITUALS
EXHIBITION with BROAD Magazine. Join us for the opening reception of Rituals, a
group exhibition featuring the work of 50 artists from Vancouver and around the
globe. The exhibition includes 28 physical lens-based photographic works,
sculptures, and a digital projection. The Rituals Exhibition also coincides with a
launch of the latest BROAD Magazine: Rituals Edition. Rituals was curated by Broad
Magazine Editor Greg Farkas and Gallery 881 Owner/Gallerist John Goldsmith.
Gallery 881 is free and open to the public.

IMPORTANT DATES
Opening Reception: December 9, 2023 from 2 - 5pm
Exhibition Dates: December 9, 2023 - January 12, 2024

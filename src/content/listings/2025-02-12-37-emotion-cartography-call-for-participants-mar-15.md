free creative cohort

There will be 5 weekly sessions based in the Commercial Drive area (right across
from Slice of Life) from March 15"April 12! This series, called "Emotion
Cartography," explores how we use our senses, mark-making, and creative
techniques to "map" the way we experience and understand both our inner and outer
worlds.

Connected to my forever work of investigating belonging (the ways we find it and
create it together), participants will engage in interactive,
movement-based creative
exercises, navigating both familiar and unfamiliar spaces. Week by week, well
explore themes like roots and home, nature, public spaces, and beyond, culminating
in the creation of a GIANT collaborative inner-world map.

No prior artistic experience is needed"just curiosity and a willingness to explore!
The cohort is free and open to adults 18+. All materials are provided and the space
is wheelchair accessible with a low stimulation area.

If this sounds like something your community might be interested feel free to share
this link for more info and to sign up: https://www.hellochowder.com/new-
events/emotion-cartography

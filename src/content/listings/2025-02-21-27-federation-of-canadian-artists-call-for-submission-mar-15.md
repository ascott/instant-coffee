About the exhibition:

As technology advances and environmental consciousness grows, artists are
increasingly re-evaluating the materials they use. Works on Paper challenges artists to
explore the versatility and expressive potential of paper in all its forms. This exhibition
invites innovative approaches to a medium that has long been a foundation for artistic
practice. From traditional handmade sheets to contemporary industrial materials,
anything commercially recognized as paper serves as an open-ended substrate for
creative exploration.

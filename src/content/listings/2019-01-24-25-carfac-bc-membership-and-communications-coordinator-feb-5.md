CARFAC BC Membership & Communications Coordinator Job Description 

CARFAC BC, the member-based advocacy organization representing British Columbias 
visual arts sector, seeks a part-time Membership & Communications Coordinator for its 
Vancouver office.

The Membership & Communications Coordinator will maintain the CARFAC BC office 
and serve as the primary contact for members, non-member artists, stakeholders, and 
the general public. The Membership & Communications Coordinator will be required to 
address individual queries and concerns of artist members, process and maintain 
membership records, disseminate information and resources, maintain CARFAC BCs 
communications channels (social media, website, e-newsletter), assist with 
programming such as workshops and other professional development and other tasks 
related to general operations.

Along with CARFAC BCs Board of Directors, the Membership & Communications 
Coordinator represents the public face of CARFAC BC. We are seeking an outgoing and 
conscientious individual with a background in visual arts who is looking for part time 
hours.

This is a part time position averaging 8 hours per week dependent on workload and 
funding.

Compensation: $16.00/hour with 4% vacation pay.

The position is based out of CARFAC BCs office at 100-938 Howe Street, Vancouver. 
Schedule may be flexible for the right candidate.

Please send a cover letter and applicable resume to bc@carfac.ca by February 5th 
2019

Zandi Dandizette
Membership and Programs Coordinator

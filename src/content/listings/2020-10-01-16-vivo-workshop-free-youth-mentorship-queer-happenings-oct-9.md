Queer Happenings

More info & sign up:
https://vivomediaarts.com/queer-happenings

Application deadline extended to Oct 9, 2020.

This FREE mentorship is designed to inspire Two Spirit, queer, trans & non-binary youth
and their allies, 15 to 18 years old.

Learn about local art and activism from the past 50 years and make a creative project in
response to what inspires you, with the help of mentoring artist Alysha Seriani. You'll
explore VIVO's newly released Every Queer Thing
collection.

Takes place over 5 weeks, from Oct 26 to Nov 28, with a group exhibition in December.

Know a friend who might be interested in this project? Tell them to apply too!

The Whole is the Untrue

Setareh Yasan

1129 East Hastings

Opening January 10th 1-5

Exhibition till February 14th

Thursday-Sunday 12-5 or by appointment

The Whole is the Untrue is an exhibition of recent works by Setareh Yasan. Taking as its
point of departure an ancient vessel from the 1st"3rd century AD, excavated in present-day
Iran, the exhibition seeks to destabilize the idea of wholeness and its idealization. Yasan
examines the persistent appeal of wholeness within a fragmented global condition in which
images, information, and lived experiences are continually filtered, remixed, and reframed
across borders, cultures, and oceans"rendering the image of a unified whole increasingly
deceptive. Through the vessels shifting manifestations, Yasan questions the fiction of
wholeness, both historical and contemporary, revealing how meaning fractures and
reassembles across material, medium, space, and time.

Setareh Yasan is an artist and educator who lives and works across Coast Salish territories.

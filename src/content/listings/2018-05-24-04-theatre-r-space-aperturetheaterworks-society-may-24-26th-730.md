THEATRE PLAY + EXHIBITION | R Space | Aperture Theaterworks Society - Family, 
Happiness | MAY 24, 25, 26

Address: 123 E 8th Ave. Vanvouer, BC

Time: May 24, 25, 26 7:30 pm
Tickets by donation. Dialogue in Mandarin. 

Family, Happiness is a play produced by Aperture Theaterworks Society, in cooperation 
with UBC Theater and Film. It follows the lives of several unique characters who have 
immigrated from Aisa. This play portrays some of the challenges of immigration, and 
explores many themes such as love, family, money, and loneliness. 

Screenwright and director: Jada Ding, President, Aperture Theaterworks Society

Special consultant: Siyuan Liu, Associate Professor, UBC Theatre and Film

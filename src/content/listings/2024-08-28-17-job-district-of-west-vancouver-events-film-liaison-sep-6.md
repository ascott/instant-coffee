The District of West
Vancouver is seeking a permanent, full-time Events & Film
Liaison to join the Cultural Services Dept. The deadline to apply is September 6,
2024.

The position is responsible for reviewing and evaluating special event proposals, and
issuing permits when approved; attend and monitor events to ensure compliance
with guidelines and permits; may be required to direct traffic and provide emergency
first aid; provide direction to one or more events staff and coordinate volunteer and
clerical work; develop event plans for annual events such as West Vancouver
Community Culture Fest, National Indigenous Peoples Day, Canada Day and
Harmony Arts Festival; support applications for grants, or sponsorship proposals to
funding to support District events; coordinate District and sponsor receptions
including catering, entertainment and production equipment; assist the Special
Events team in the development of marketing plans and collateral for annual District
events; prepare and develop content for
the District websites and signage for events;
maintain and prepare records, statistics, correspondence, and reports; review filming
applications; identify requirements, disruptions and risks to the public and the
environment; liaise with film organizers and communicate special requirements and
standards of conduct.

For the full job description, qualifications, salary, and to apply, please visit
westvancouver.ca/careers

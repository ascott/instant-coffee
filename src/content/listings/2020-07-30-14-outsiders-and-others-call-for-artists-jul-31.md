In September, Outsiders and Others is having an exhibition called "Power to the People",
and we are looking for artists who want to participate. This exhibition has a goal of sharing
voices that
don't get heard enough.

We primarily show visual art but for this we are open to site specific performance works
that can take place in a window space gallery.

This is an opportunity to have your voice heard and seen through art.

Send us images of the work you would like to show and a statement about yourself and
your work. Proposals for any other ideas you may have are welcome too.

Deadline is July 31.

Email your submission or any questions to

outsidersandothers@gmail.com

Thank you.

And Power to the People!

Join us online via Zoom to hear about the curatorial research of 2023
Paris/Vancouver Curatorial Residency Program Resident, Anne Bourass..

Anne Bourrass (b. 1991, lives and works in Paris) is an independent curator and art
critic at the intersection of the visual arts and the humanities. She is currently
director of artistic programming at Le Consulat Voltaire cultural centre in Paris and
works with numerous cultural institutions in France and abroad. In 2019, she co-
founded the association Contemporaines in France to fight against gender
inequalities in contemporary art. She is a graduate of the cole Nationale Suprieure
des Arts Dcoratifs de Paris, Sciences Po Paris and Cornell University, USA. She is
the winner of the ADIAF mergence Grant - Curator / Art Critic in France.

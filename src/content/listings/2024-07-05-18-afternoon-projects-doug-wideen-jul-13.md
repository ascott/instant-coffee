Ride the Pigeon
Doug Wideen
June 8 - July 13, 2024

This exhibition is a chronicle of the development of Doug Wideen's process with
paintings, sculptures and drawings from the past 40 years.

Doug Wideen is an artist currently residing in Coquitlam, BC. His characters reflect his
childhood, life's struggles, and his journey to self-discovery as an artist. After high
school, he worked in construction for decades. At 40, he decided to pursue art, taking
night classes before establishing his self-taught practice in drawing and painting at
Dynamo Arts. He continues to work in construction while creating art at Pig Freeze
Studios.

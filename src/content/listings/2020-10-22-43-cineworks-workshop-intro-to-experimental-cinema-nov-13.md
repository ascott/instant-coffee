The Bad Academy x Cineworks | Intro to Experimental Cinema

Join Cineworks and The Bad Academy for a 3-part workshop series.

Were getting into experimental cinema: what it is (yes, its weird), why you dont need
to be intimidated by it, and how you can make some of your own.

Well look at experimental film from some amazing women and femme filmmakers,
scratch, paint, ferment,
and/or otherwise destroy some 16mm film, make our own
cyanotypes and finish things off with some phytograms.

Each week, from 6-8pm, we'll come together to learn about each of these unique
techniques! NOTE: This workshop will be recorded if you cannot make the sessions!

Dates/Times

Session 1 - Working with Found Footage Friday, November 13th

Session 2 - Pre-Cinema Printing and Cyanotypes Friday, November 20th

Session 3 - Ecoprocessing and PhytogramsFriday, November 27th

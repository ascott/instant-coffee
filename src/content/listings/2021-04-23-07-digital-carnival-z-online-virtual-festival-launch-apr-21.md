Wednesday, April 21, 6:30 PM"8:30 PM

Digital Carnival Z: Virtual Festival Launch

Featuring Cecilia Point and Kofi Oduro (Illestpreacha)

For registration information, visit www.digitalcarnival.ca

Join us in celebrating the virtual launch of Digital Carnival Z with a traditional welcome from
Elder Cecilia Point and a live performance from Montreal-based creative coder, engineer,
thinker, poet, and performer Kofi Oduro (Illestpreacha). Setting the stage for the festival,
Digital Exploration of the Expansion is a presentation and performance
of speculative fiction
through live coding, creative coding, and other mediums that explore how the body can be
reimagined.

Digital Carnival Z is a hybrid media art festival headlining Featured Artists Nancy Lee
and Kiran Bhumber , co-presented with the Richmond Art Gallery. Explore
the festival at DigitalCarnival.ca.

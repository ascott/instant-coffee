The Terra Vivente Art Studio is located in southern Italy, not far from Naples, in 
the village of San Potito Sannitico. The residency program runs in July and 
August. Artists come for one month to work in the studio, explore the village and 
the region, and participate in the festivals and exhibitions. A maximum of four 
artists participate in the main residency, with accommodation in two two-bedroom 
apartments with an indoor and outdoor covered studio. In addition to the public 
art and film festivals that take place in San Potito, there are many other festivals 
in nearby villages, notably in Guardia Sanframondi every August, there is a 6-day 
wine festival, Vinalia, a wonderful opportunity to participate in cultural events and 
engage with the community. In addition, the Riti Settennali, an important religious 
ceremony that takes place only once every seven years will be happening in 
August in Guardia Sanframondi. This is a region that is off the tourist route; 
participating artists have the opportunity to immerse themselves in the local 
culture. The residency program aims to foster interaction with the community, 
dialogue and collaboration, with an eye on environmental sustainability within 
artistic practices. For details on how to apply, the residency fee, and to download 
the application form, see website,

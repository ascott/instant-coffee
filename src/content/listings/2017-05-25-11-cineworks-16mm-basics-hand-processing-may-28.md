16mm Basics: Introduction to Hand Processing

Sunday 28 May 2017"2:00pm-5:00pm
Cineworks Annex, 235 Alexander Street (Ironworks basement),
Vancouver, V6A 1C1, entry via driveway

$30 + taxes | Cineworks Members
$35 + taxes | Concessions and Community Partner Members (WIFTV, DOC BC, VIVO)
$40 + taxes | General Admission

REGISTER: 
https://16mm-basics-hand-processing-may.eventbrite.ca

A 3 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and practice of 
processing 16mm black and white film by hand.

Elements include:

Principles of chemical developing
Darkroom safety
Bucket processing
Tank processing

Film shot in the 16mm Basics: Intro to Bolex workshop on Sunday 14 May will be 
processed in this workshop. If interested in both components, please register for the 
Bolex workshop via the following link: 

https://16mm-basics-bolex-may.eventbrite.ca

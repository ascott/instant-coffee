Eternal Network
March 7, 2015

Fillip is pleased to present an evening of experimental music and video by
Monty Cantsin, Gretchen Snakes, and Robert Filliou at the Western Front,
Vancouver, in celebration of the launch of our new digital publishing
platform (fillip.ca, online March 6).

The Eternal Network, so named by Fluxus artists Robert Filliou and George
Brecht in 1965, exists through the collective and continued efforts of
artists around the world.

On March 7, Monty Cantsin (Gabriel Saloman) resurfaces for a one-off
performance of a new composition, alongside a noise/drone set by
Vancouver-based Gretchen Snakes. The night also revisits the Western
Front’s role as an important historical node in the Eternal Network,
through screenings of Robert Filliou’s 1970s artist-in-residence
performances—shown in the place of their creation, the Grand Luxe Hall.

This event is organized in conjunction with the introduction of a new
Fillip website and the online publication of Ken Becker’s essay “Not Just
Some Canadian Hippie Bullshit: The Western Front as Artists’ Practice,”
forthcoming in Fillip 20 (Spring 2015).

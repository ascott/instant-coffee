One Dot Press is calling for submissions to Vinarterta: a cake zine, a small
publication about the Canadian-Icelandic cake vinarterta. The zine is intended to be
both an introduction to vinarterta and a celebration of the hyper-regional, the many
layers of culture, intergenerational memory, and all things tasty.

While we are not looking for recipes or stories of making vinarterta at this time, we
welcome creative responses to the cake such as:
- photos of the cake making process or dishes the cake is served on
- illustrations, paintings, comics etc.
- a history of ingredients such as prunes or cardamom
- pairing recommendations (drinks, songs, frames of mind)
- epic poetry
- blind tasting notes
- or any other ideas you might have!

Submissions are welcome from people of any
age and regardless of previous
experience with the cake. Participants will be given a copy of the zine and invited to
a small launch party. Submissions will be chosen based on printing limitations and
how they fit into the larger collection.

The deadline to submit is February 9th, 2025. Submissions should be sent as a word
doc, PDF or JPEG to onedot.press@gmail.com. Please reach out if you have any
questions!

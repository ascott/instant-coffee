Record Launch
Date and time: Friday, July 12th, 7pm to 10pm
Location: Wil Aballe Art Projects
1129 E Hastings St, Vancouver

For tickets to the record launch, please visit Second Springs website:
[https://secondspring.online/Strawberry-Launch-Tix](https://secondspring.online/Strawberry-Launch-Tix)

Emotionality is a recording of a live performance by Strawberry at Or Gallerys
Dance-a-thon in Vancouver, Canada, produced and remixed by the band. Weaving
together vocal samples, improvisational sounds, and discordant perspectives, the
experimental
dance record bids listeners to move their bodies among a mass of
unruly voices. Words pierce with percussive insistence, forming
rhythms that crest
and disperse into order and disarray.

A record launch for Emotionality will take place at Wil Aballe Art
Projects at 7pm on
July 12th, with performances by Strawberry, Eddy Wang (Ira Dot), and
Kc (Kamikaze
Nurse and Late Spring).

Emotionality is the fifth record released by Second Spring, an
artist-run record label
that aims to forge an alternative to the rhythms of traditional
gallery programming.
The record is the latest in a series of projects by artists whose
practices extend
beyond traditional music production.

About Strawberry
Strawberry is a Vancouver-based band comprising Barry Doup (various
instruments) and Dennis Ha (vocals, various instruments). Formed in
2012, the band
has also featured special guests Arvo Leo (harp) and Marie-France
Berard (vocals).

Barry Doup is a musician and artist who works primarily with computer
animation,
digital painting, and sculpture, using imagery and language derived from the
subconscious. In 2024, he was selected for the prestigious Sobey Art
Award longlist.
His films have been screened internationally, including at the
Vancouver Art Gallery,
Chicago Underground Film Festival, International Film Festival Rotterdam, e-flux
screening room, Swallow Gallery, DOK Leipzig, Hors Pistes, and Deluge
Contemporary.

Dennis Ha is a musician and artist working in photography. Born in
Hong Kong SAR,
he currently lives and works in Vancouver.

About Second Spring
Second Spring is an artist imprint that produces vinyl records and
apparel. It is
devoted to supporting expanded and experimental practices, and
bridging audiences
from diverse fields.

Second Spring was formed in 2020 by artist/musician Julian Hou, with
support from
a variety of collaborators including musician Dan Colussi and curator Xenia
Benivolski. Previous releases include
records by Fan Wu, Prince Nifty, Fortunato
Durutti Marinetti, and Julian Hou.

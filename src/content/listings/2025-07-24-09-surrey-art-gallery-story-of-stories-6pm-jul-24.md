Join us on Thursday, July 24 from 6-8pm for a tour of 10 and 10: Story of Stories led
by Assistant Curator Jas Lally.

This 50th anniversary exhibition pairs permanent collection artworks acquired from the
first ten years of collecting (1975"1985) and the last ten years (2014"2024) through
themes of landscape, architecture, portraiture, and movement. Reflect on the
connections between artworks and hear the stories behind these pairings.

Surrey-based artists Kiranjot Kaur and Chito Maravilla will also be joining to share their
reflections on the two artworks they selected from the Gallerys permanent collection"
offering personal insight into their artistic choices.

Sunday, November 13, 1 pm 

PRESENTATION HOUSE GALLERY 
PANEL DISCUSSION

EXPOSED: Voyeurism and Photography. 
Perspectives on the invasive camera and voyeurism, ethical issues in 
contemporary photography, surveillance as an aesthetic strategy, and power 
relations between photographers and human subjects. With Clint Burnham, 
Catherine Soussloff, Shep Steiner, Althea Thauberger. 

In conjunction with the exhibitions: “Larry Clark: Tulsa” and “Kohei Yoshiyuki: The 
Park” on view through November 13.

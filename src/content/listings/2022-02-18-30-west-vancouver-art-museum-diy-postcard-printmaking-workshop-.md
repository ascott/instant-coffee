REBECCA BAIR: DEEP CONDITIONING, AND LEON COUPEY: MY DEAREST KATE

Join artist and educator Mark Johnsen for a free evening of DIY printmaking in which
participants will create several printed postcard size artworks. This workshop revolves
around making unique prints by using processes that can be done at home and with
limited resources. The session
will introduce methods of printmaking such as:
additive/reductive monotype, trace printing, rubbings, and paper alteration. Please
register to receive a link to this workshop.

Play Nice 

Lee Hutzulak 
August 11th-September 1st, 2018 

Opening Reception: August 11th, 2018 from 7:00-9:00pm

Join us at Cartems Donuts (534 W Pender St) for the opening reception for Play 

Nice, a series of paintings by Lee Hutzulak.

Lee Hutzulak pursues several avenues of creativity including music, video and graphic 
design, and painting/drawing. In 1993 he graduated with honours from the drawing program 
at the Alberta College of Art. He has exhibited internationally, but mostly in and around his 
home town of Vancouver, Canada. Current and ongoing fascinations include the material 
nature of paint, an exploration of techniques and the aesthetics of composition, centred 
around a singular subject matter: human relationships. In 2011 he became a father and 
again in 2015, shifting his focus to other pursuits. Recently his energy and interest in 
painting has returned with a renewed vigour and intensity.

[https://leehutzulak.com/](https://leehutzulak.com/)

Cartems Exhibition Series is a bi-monthly art show at our downtown location. The 

exhibitions provide increased opportunities for emerging artists to showcase their 

work while engaging the public with Vancouvers vibrant artistic community.

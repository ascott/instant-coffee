Richmond Art Gallery presents Inaction (February 12-April 3, 2021)

Richmond Art Gallery presents a new solo show by renowned Canadian contemporary
artist Brendan
Fernandes, Inaction, on display from February 12 to April 3, 2021. The
exhibition, co-produced with the Ezra and Cecile Zilkha Gallery at Wesleyan University,
addresses violence against queer, POC and marginalized bodies, as well as the
potential for change through collective action. Inaction features the Canadian premiere
of the two-channel video Free Fall: for Camera and a commissioned series of nine
minimalist sculptural works. The sculptures will be activated in three livestreamed
performances featuring contemporary and ballet dancers in March and April, dates to
be announced.

Visit www.richmondartgallery.org for more information.

Admission is by donation.

Hours: Mon-Fri 10am to 6pm & Sat, 10am to 4pm

June 19 - Jul 10
6:30-9:30 p.m.

Join us for a series of 4 painting workshops with renowned Canadian painter Etienne Zack as 
we explore creative ways of arranging pictorial space in acrylic painting. Each class will begin 
with a look at historic techniques of creating pictures from early Modernism to today. Explore 
techniques ranging from Cubism to the contemporary use of photography and painting with 
acrylic gel transfer.

This class is open to all skill levels.

Shudder Gallery welcomes the Aquarian Foundation: III/XII/IX 
live performance and video installation FRIDAY June 29th

The exploratory house ensemble the Aquarian Foundation, expands 
the groundwork of a 
myth world numerology through a multimedia release show.
Channeling obscured symbols of the Pacific Northwest the group will 
perform live
accompaniment to their ritual-inspired film "IX: COSMIDRAMA" along 
with light installations 
and printed matter in a synesthetic experience.

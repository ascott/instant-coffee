Gallery 626 invites you to Tiny Show, a group exhibition featuring small-scale works that fit in
the palm of your hand! Working within an intimate format, the artists explore scale as both
constraint and possibility through a diverse range of media"including sculpture, painting,
drawing, and more"presented by emerging artists. With each piece functioning as a
viewfinder, we encourage visitors to come closer during the Tiny Show and engage with the
artworks in stillness and quietness.

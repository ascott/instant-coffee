FESTIVAL ADMINISTRATOR

Application deadline: Monday August 24th, 2009 at 5pm

Projected start date: Mid September

CONTACT Toronto Photography Association, a not-for-profit organization, is
accepting applications for the full time position of Festival Administrator.
With over 1,000 local, national and international artists participating at
more than 200 venues across the GTA, CONTACT attracts over one million
visitors annually. CONTACT is devoted to celebrating and fostering an
appreciation of the art and profession of photography. This new member of
staff will contribute to the further development of initiatives to support
the growth of the festival.

RESPONSIBILITIES:

€ Assists with the planning and coordination of the annual festival,
including exhibitions, and events

€ Responsible for festival registration, registrant communication and
image submissions

€ Manages online data and image conversion for festival publications

€ Maintains and updates website and mailing lists

€ Responsible for information requests via info@contactphoto.com and
general phone inquiries

€ Maintains festival¹s e-newsletter and other communications as required

€ Responsible for general office administration

€ Manages multiple Mac and PC computers, their network and shared
database

€ Assists with grant applications and reports

€ Oversees office volunteers and interns

QUALIFICATIONS:

€ Strong communication, writing, editing and organizational skills

€ Excellent telephone and customer relation skills

€ Experience working in an arts organization and a degree in photography
or equivalent

€ Thorough attention to detail with ability to work independently and
under tight deadlines

€ Focused, motivated and capable of working well with others in a small
organization

€ Proficiency in computer applications including Word, Excel and
Photoshop

€ Knowledge of contemporary art and photography

€ Previous experience with web design is an asset

€ PR and communications experience and interest an asset

This is a full time position that reports to the Executive Director.

No phone calls or email inquiries please. We thank all applicants, but only
those selected for an interview will be contacted.

Send resume, cover letter & a minimum of two references to:

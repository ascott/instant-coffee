VABF is still looking for passionate volunteers to help out with its 7th
Annual edition, taking place Thursday October 18- Sunday October 21.

What you get:

A screen-printed VABF 2018 wearable
A 2018/19 Membership, which includes our annual tote, our 2018 special
edition enamel pin, discounts on publications, entry to the VABF Members
Preview as well as discounts and exclusive access to other VABF events
throughout the year
Free access to all VABF 2018 events and parties, including the fair's
Opening and After Parties
Mentorship from VABF staff and a chance to build leadership, event
production, fundraising, archival, curatorial and graphic design skills
A relationship with VABF for potential future collaborations or projects
Connection with other like-minded art book lovers and creators
VABF Orientation and appreciation parties

What you do:

Help out in areas that you are passionate about"such as publications,
marketing and publicity, fundraising or curation"to help do meaningful work
on projects such as the VABF Library & Archive Project, VABF 2018's
marketing and publicity campaign, VABF's first Comedy & Zine Fest and our
Monthly Open Studio events
Help us promote VABF 2018 by distributing flyers and other promotional
materials, as well as spreading the word on social media and by word of mouth
Assist us at our VABF Members Opening and Art Auction Fundraiser on
October 18th at Emily Carr University
Assist us in operating VABF 2018 over the weekend of October 19 to 21,
2018 at the Emily Carr University
Support our staff and Board with various tasks in the lead-up to the fair

If youre ready and able, please email Emma at
community@vancouverartbookfair.com to sign up!

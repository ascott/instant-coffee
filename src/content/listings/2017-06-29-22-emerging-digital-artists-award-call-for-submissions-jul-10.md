Emerging Digital Artists Award (EDAA) Call for Applications
Deadline July 10, 2017

The Emerging Digital Artists Award (EDAA) is currently accepting applications for its
2017 award program. Founded on a desire to support and celebrate digital 
innovation in the arts, the EDAA caters to early-career artists who are working on-
screen, and experimenting with the possibilities of digitally rendered imagery. 
Applicants are encouraged to play with moving image formats such as video, 
animation, and GIF art.

Equitable Bank and their digital banking operation, EQ Bank, have partnered with 
Trinity Square Video to present the 2017 EDAA. The winner of the award will receive 
a space in Trinity Square Videos 2018 Themed Commission program, and all 
finalists will receive a membership to the centre. The finalists works will be shown in 
an exhibition at Trinity Square Video September 28 " October 12, 2017. The award 
winner will be announced at the EDAAs closing event on October 5, and offered a 
$5,000 monetary award. The remaining four finalists each receive a $1,000 award.

If youre an emerging digital artist based in Canada, apply to the EDAA by 
completing an online application. The call will remain open until July 10, 2017.
http://edaa.equitablebank.ca/pages/en/application.html

Learn more about the EDAA and eligibility by visiting our website, following us on 
Twitter
@edaa_eqb, or sending a line to edaa@eqbank.ca.

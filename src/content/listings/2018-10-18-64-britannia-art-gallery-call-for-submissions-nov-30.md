CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS - BRITANNIA ART GALLERY 2019 PROGRAM
Deadline: November 30, 2018

Britannia Art Gallery invites submissions from leisure and
professional artists, artisans, school groups and collectives living

Centre. Our catchment area covers from the south shore of Burrard

Applications are available at the gallery located in the Britannia
library or online at www.britanniacentre.org go under services section
to the arts & culture and then click on the gallery.

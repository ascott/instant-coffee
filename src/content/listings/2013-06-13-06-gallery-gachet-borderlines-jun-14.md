BorderLines: an artistic examination into mental health diagnoses

Gallery Gachet 88 E Cordova st. Vancouver BC

Exhibition runs: June 14th - July 28th, 2013

Opening reception: Fri June 14th, 7 - 10pm

Mad Pride Cabaret: July 13th, 7 - 10.30pm

When does a behaviour, mood, thought become a disorder? BorderLines is a
multidisciplinary group art exhibition that challenges ideas concerning
borders around behaviour, mood and thoughts as defined by contemporary
Western medicine.

The Diagnostic and Statistical Manual (DSM IV) of Mental Disorders of the
American Association is a categorical classification system whose use is
becoming globalized. The next DSM V book is due to be launched in 2013, as
such, our proposed exhibition and the discussions it will engender, are
very timely.

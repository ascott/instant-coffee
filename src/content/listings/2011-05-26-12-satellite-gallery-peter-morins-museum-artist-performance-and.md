Peter Morins Museum (exhibit on view thru July 3, 2011) weaves together
familiar practices of museum display with a series of performances and an
evolving installation to create a space shaped by Tahltan knowledge.
Elements of the museum change and transform over time, inviting visitors
to reflect on history, objects, and places of connection. Join us at
Satellite Gallery for the second performance and launch of a hand-bound
artist book to accompany the installation. As part of witnessing the
performance, each guest will receive a copy of the book. Peter Morin, of
the Tahltan Nation of northern British Columbia, is a Victoria-based
performance artist.

May 29th at 2 PM

560 Seymour Street , 2nd Floor

Sunny Side Up
Christopher Kuhn
October Thursday 24th — November Saturday 22nd
Opening Thursday, October 23rd 6:00 — 10:00 PM
FIELD Contemporary
17 West Broadway

FIELD Contemporary is pleased to present Sunny Side Up, the first 
exhibition of Christopher Kuhn's work in Canada. The title references not 
only how the artist prefers his eggs, but also suggests a positive outlook 
that guides his process toward image making. Culled from over two years 
of work, spanning 2012 to the present, the selection of paintings reveals 
Kuhn's open-ended approach. He begins each work spontaneously, initially 
moving forward without a specific plan to steer the creation of each piece. 
Eventually, improvisation gives way to strategy, and the finished 
composition achieves a balance between conscious decision making and 
the chaos of unrestrained impulsive mark making. Risk plays an important 
role, as certain moves within a painting have the potential to enhance or 
destroy what had been built up prior. Thus a steadfast optimism underpins 
each painting, allowing the artist to treat mistakes as stepping stones in the 
journey towards resolution.

Kuhn's works revel in the inherent dualities of paint, as both object and 
artifice, and canvas, as both a surface and a window. A painting has the 
potential to create the illusion of spaces that would be impossible in the 
real world but are nonetheless acceptable to our eyes and minds despite 
their conflicting logics. The negative space of the raw fabric support 
becomes a positive element within the composition while simultaneous 
reinforcing the flatness of the plane. Brushmarks that might represent the 
earliest beginnings of the piece initially appear as though they were the 
finishing touches, laid on towards the end. By piecing together visual clues, 
traces and textures left behind in the paint, the viewer is able to reconstruct 
the moves of the painter, be they pared down or built up, and read the 
history of its creation, like a magic trick that still deceives despite showing 
off the source of its illusion.

A solo exhibition of Kuhn's work will open in Los Angeles at Sonce 
Alexander Gallery on November the 1st and run concurrently through the 
duration of this show.

Christopher Kuhn lives and paints in Los Angeles. His work has been 
exhibited at the Torrance Art Museum, the George Lawson Gallery, and 
Autonomie and reviewed in San Francisco Arts Quarterly and the LA 
Times. Publications include Fabrik magazine, laimyours.com, and 
twocoatsofpaint.com. He graduated from the American University of Paris 
with a B.A. in Art History and earned a Post Baccalaureate Certificate from 
the School of the Museum of Fine Arts Boston. He is represented in Los 
Angeles by Sonce Alexander Gallery.

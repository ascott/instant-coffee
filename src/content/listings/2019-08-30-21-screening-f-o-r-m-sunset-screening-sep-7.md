Sunset Screening: A showing of dance film projects

The students and alumni of Modus Operandi Contemporary Dance Training Program 
welcome you to our 2nd annual summer screening of short experimental dance films.

Doors open at 7:30pm

ENTRANCE BY DONATION

All proceeds go towards future Modus Operandi Dance on Screen projects.

Screenings at 8pm and 9pm. 

Each screening will contain a different program of films. 

Cash bar and snacks on the patio. 

Come and go as you need! Join us for a drink and relax at sunset.

Presented in partnership with F-O-R-M

Festival of Recorded Movement, 

@formvancouver

http://www.f-o-r-m.ca/ 

Special thanks to Josh Hite, Sophia Wolfe, David Raymond and all the collaborators 
who helped make these films. Poster artwork created by Samantha Krystal. 

We acknowledge that this event takes place as a part of an active, ongoing occupation 
on the unceded territories of the Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), Sl"lwta (Tsleil- 
Waututh), and xmkwy"m (Musqueam) nations.

Accessibility: The Marcuse studio and patio, on the 7th floor of The Dance Centre at 
Granville and Davie, is wheelchair accessible by elevator. There is a gender non-
specific accessible washroom located on the same floor.

We are striving to make this a scent-reduced event - however the building does not 
have a scent reduction policy - please refrain from wearing perfume and scented 
products.

Please contact us with any questions or concerns.

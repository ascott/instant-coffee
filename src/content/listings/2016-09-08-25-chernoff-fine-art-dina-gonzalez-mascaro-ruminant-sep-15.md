Dina Gonzalez Mascaro - Ruminant

Chernoff Fine Art
September 15 - October 19
OPENING RECEPTION: Thursday, September 15, 7-9pm

Inspired by the remote "Womb Mountains" in Asia where pilgrims 
travel to experience transformation, Dina Gonzlez Mascar 
contemplates the death of her mother and father in Ruminant an 
exhibition that also marks the Argentinean-Canadian artist's return 
to drawing. The powerful works strike a precarious balance 
between light and dark, fragility and strength, structure and 
collapse, with myriad pieces intricately entangled, both defying and 
depending upon gravity to hold together. Ruminant reflects a year 
that Gonzlez Mascar spent "inside the mountain", where she 
navigated her own darkness and tight passages, as well as light 
and calm, and emerged with some of her most breathtaking work to 
date.

Dina Gonzlez Mascar lives and works in Vancouver. Trained as a 
sculptor in Argentina, DGM works in an - open space - overlap 
space - non place - between the art and design world. It is within 
that gap where Gonzlez Mascar displays her always three-
dimensional view of things. DGM's body of work oscillates between 
scales and between disciplines - drawing- jewellery sculpture- 
furniture and painting.

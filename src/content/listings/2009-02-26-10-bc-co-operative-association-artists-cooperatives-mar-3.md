Artists & Cooperatives:
Is a co-operative structure the right choice for your arts group?

Date: Tuesday, March 3rd, 2009
Time: 7:00 p.m. - 9:30 p.m.
Location: VIVO Media Arts Centre
1965 Main Street (front entrance)
Vancouver, BC
* Refreshments Provided No registration required. Session is free.

In today's challenging environment, artists and other creative people
are looking for effective ways to join forces to get things done, to
find ongoing professional support and to build sustainable access to
resources (housing, workspace, training, administration, revenue,
materials, markets).

The BC Co-operative Association, SPARC-BC, and cultural sector partners
VIVO Media Arts Centre and the BC Cultural Sector Development Council
invite you to bring your questions and ideas to an evening of
exploration: how does the co-operative organizational form meet the
needs of today's artists?

Interactive Information Session Includes:

An overview of key information you need to know about the
co-op model and its diverse applications.

A panel of existing and developing local arts sector
co-operatives to share their experience.

o 901 Artists Co-op, The Wood Co-op, Creative Women Craftworks,
and others.

Resources that are available to get you started.

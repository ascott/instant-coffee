Join Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery for an artist talk with Featured Artist 
Liz Toohey-Wiese on Tuesday, February 14, 2017 at 7pm.

Liz Toohey-Wiese's paintings explore contemporary relationships between identity and 
place. Toohey-Wiese was born and raised in the suburbs of Vancouver, and is deeply 
interested in the history of landscape painting. Her work seeks to intervene in the 
broader notion of "landscape" in the cities and their natural surrounds. Toohey-Wiese 
has taken part in solo and group shows across Canada. She holds an MFA from the 
Nova Scotia College of Art and Design.

Toohey-Wiese's work will be featured in the showroom through
March 24, 2017

Free to attend; space is limited
Doors open at 6:45pm

Art Rental & Sales Showroom
1st Floor Annex, Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby Street, Vancouver

Art Rental & Sales is a not-for-profit program run by the Vancouver Art Gallery.

Join us June 22nd from 3 to 6 PM for Mad Pride Cabaret at the Gathering Place
Community Center theater/gallery with performers:MC Human Girl, 88WAYY, Robin and
Wren, HK and GOLDbard.

Mad Pride Cabaret is organized by the Connection Salon artists group and sponsored by
the Community Arts Council of Vancouver, the City of Vancouver, and the Gathering
Place.

Mad Pride month began in Canada in 1993. It is an arts, culture and heritage festival that
is about: participating in mad history, challenging discrimination, advocating for rights,
affirming mad identities, developing, and empowering mad communities and having fun!

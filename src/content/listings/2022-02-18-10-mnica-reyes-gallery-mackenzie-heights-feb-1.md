Mnica Reyes Gallery Mackenzie Heights
Opening February 2022

Mnica Reyes Gallery is thrilled to announce that we are expanding to Mackenzie Heights
in Vancouver, BC.

Our new outpost, located at 2895 West 33rd Avenue " at the intersection of Mackenzie
Street, will open in mid February 2022 with
This Must Be The Place, an exhibition by
Maggee Day. This place, in the very heart of Vancouver's west side, will provide a unique
opportunity for the gallerys roster of artists to connect with a new audiences, to present
new projects and to participate in curated group exhibitions.

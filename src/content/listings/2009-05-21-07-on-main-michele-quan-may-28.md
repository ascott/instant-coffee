ON MAIN

“The tree is my muse. I’ve made ceramic garlands to hang from their branches in offering to the 
earth, the community and the self. My hope is they serve as objects of contemplation
to inspire reverence and as a source of encouragement and refuge.” M.Q.

ON MAIN in conjunction with The Potters Guild of BC is pleased to present GARLANDS the 
inaugural exhibition of ceramic and mixed media works by New York City artist Michele Quan. 
Combining drawings, photography and ceramic elements this site-specific installation is designed 
to be viewed 24/7 from the street or can be experienced from inside the gallery. The principle 
pieces are intended as indoor architectural elements or outdoor garden sculptures.

Her mixed media and ceramic work continues a lifelong pursuit to visually bring form to our 
internal landscape and locate the thread that connects us with the experience of the world we 
live. Motifs for Quan's ceramic garlands are rooted in visual symbols of Eastern iconography, an 
extension of her study of Buddhism. www.mquan.com. We are excited to launch this new body 
of work in Canada. Curated by Donna Partridge & Paul Wong.

Michele is originally from Vancouver and studied at Langara College. She moved to New York in 
1984 to continue her education in graphics & photography at Parsons School of Design. For 6 
years she worked as a runway and print model in NYC, Milan and Paris and appeared in such 
publications as Vogue and Elle & on the runways of Chanel and Armani.

Twelve years prior to embarking on her current medium of clay, Quan co-founded the NYC jewelry 
company Me&Ro. This design house operated retail stores in New York, Miami and Japan and 
was instrumental in bringing attention to charitable organizations such as Doctors of the World 
and The Tibet Fund. In 2001 Me & Ro partnered with amfAR to create the AIDS ribbon for 
nominees and presenters at the Emmy Awards.

ON MAIN is a project room/gallery for visual , media and interdisciplinary art. Artistic Director: Paul 
Wong. Rebranded from On Edge www.onedge.tv to On Main in 2008. We gratefully 
acknowledge assistance of the Canada Council. The Potters Guild of BC promotes awareness, 
education and appreciation of the ceramic arts. Its membership operates the Gallery of BC 
Ceramics located on Granville Island. www.bcpotters.com

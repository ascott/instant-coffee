VIVO MEDIA ARTS CENTRE

JOB POSTING: DISTRIBUTION ASSISTANT

Application Deadline: September 23, 2011

Part-Time 12 hours/week (flexible hours) Wage: $15/h

Video Out Distribution, part of the VIVO Media Arts Centre, is a non-profit 
distributor of video art and documentary works. We maintain a collection of 
independently produced video artworks, which we distribute to festivals, 
galleries, broadcasters and libraries internationally. For more information on our 
work please visit our website at www.videoout.ca. 

The Distribution Assistant works closely with the Distribution Coordinator and is 
primarily responsible for responding to general queries by artists and exhibitors, 
open festival calls, administrative support for acquisitions, title accessioning and 
basic financial transactions. The Distribution Assistant carries out exhibitor and 
marketing research, organizes special projects, and works extensively with e-
mail and database software. The Distribution Assistant is also familiar with the 
day-to-day running of Video Out and covers for the Distribution Coordinator when 
they are off-site. 

The ideal candidate will have strong verbal and written communication skills, 
computer fluency and a capacity to juggle multiple tasks to deadline using Mac

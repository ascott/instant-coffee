Publication Launch

Jeneen Frei Njootli | my auntie bought all her skidoos with bead money

Thursday, November 29, 7 - 9pm, free

Off-site at the Aboriginal Friendship Centre, 1607 East Hastings St, Vancouver

Join us to celebrate the launch of Jeneen Frei Njootlis first monographic publication, 
which features commissioned texts by renowned Sto:lo writer Lee Maracle, Vuntut 
Gwitchin spoken word poet Nagoohiindaak / Melissa Frost and celebrated Cree poet 
Billy-Ray Belcourt, winner of the 2018 Griffin Poetry Prize. This book was produced in 
tandem with Frei Njootlis recent solo exhibition at the Contemporary Art Gallery.

At this event, Swxw7mesh language and culture advocate Salia Joseph will share a 
welcome, and Nagoohiindaak / Melissa Frost will perform the hauntingly beautiful Ehdii, 
her contribution to the book, along with other recent spoken word poems. Frei Njootli 
and Frost will share a caribou stew they have made together, and bannock will be 
provided by the Aboriginal Friendship Centre.

The first 40 copies of the publication will include a special limited-edition artist 
intervention.

This publication has been made possible through the generous support of the Audain 
Foundation, Jane Irwin and Ross Hill, Art Gallery of Burlington, Macaulay & Co. Fine Art 
and Nada Vuksic.

Find out more at https://www.contemporaryartgallery.ca/events/publication-launch-
jeneen-frei-njootli/

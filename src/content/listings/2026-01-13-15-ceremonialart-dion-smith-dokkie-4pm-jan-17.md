Dion Smith-Dokkie: ...everything was so infinite, as according to my heart and truest wish
Opening Saturday January 17th 4-7pm
Ceremonial/Art
3712 W 10th Ave
Vancouver

Ceremonial/Art is thrilled to present a solo exhibition of recent works by West Moberly First
Nations artist Dion Smith-Dokkie. Born in the Peace River region of Northern BC Smith-
Dokkie is now based in Grande Prairie Alberta where they continue to develop their
interdisciplinary practice. everything was so infinite, as according to my heart and truest
wish brings together two series of works by the artist that focus on the fractal abstraction of
land and bodily representation.

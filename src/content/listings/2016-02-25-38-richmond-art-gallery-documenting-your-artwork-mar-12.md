DOCUMENTING YOUR ARTWORK
Saturday, March 12, 2016 from 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM
Richmond Art Gallery

Learn to document your artworks with professional photographer 
Andrea Sirois, who will show you the basics of lighting and proper 
camera set-up so you can have images for exhibition, public art or 
residency proposals, for website use, and promotional materials. 
Andrea will also offer tips on what to look for when selecting your 
images and a photographer for hire.

$25 RAGA or CARFAC Members / $35 Non-Members

Andrea Sirois is a commercial and fine art photographer whose 
specialty is nature, landscape, and abstract photography. On a 
commercial basis, she shoots editorial photography for a number of 
publications and has had numerous gallery showings of her personal 
work. Her images have been commissioned by city planners for public 
art installations throughout the lower mainland, including Richmond, 
Surrey, and Vancouver. Andrea is an experienced educator who has 
worked with a number of Vancouver schools in photographic arts.

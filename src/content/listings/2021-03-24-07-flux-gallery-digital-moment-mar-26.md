FLUX Gallery Presents Digital Moment
March 26 - May 21
On our website (www.fluxmediagallery.org)
and in the Window Gallery at 1524 Pandora Ave. Victoria BC
Window Gallery hours: Tuesday to Friday, 5pm to 9pm
Curated by Alicia Lawrence

This exhibition of short films by GO2 Productions, June Yeo, Allison Moore, Judith Price
and Johanna Householder (with CG animations by Alicia Lawrence) attempts to answer the
questions, How does film mediate the online digital environment? and What is our social
relationship to digital interactivity?

Event: (I) Contact exhibition
Place: The Gathering Place Gallery, 609 Helmcken
This is the first of a series of exhibitions on the theme of Recovery. This first one is about
Connectedness.
It is about having positive relationships with other people and our environment.
Twelve visual artists will be exhibiting from March 15 to April 28 th.
Tonye Aganaba will also be performing at our Meet the Artists event March 20th from 5:30
to 7 PM at the Gallery.
(I) Contact is organized by the Connection Salon artists group and supported by the
Community Arts Council of Vancouver, the City of Vancouver, the Canada Council for the
Arts and the Gathering Place Community Center.

Exhibition / Karen Vanon / Noticed in Vancouver... / July 12th - August 17th

Viewings, with the artist present, are available on

Saturday, July 12th from 2:00 to 4:00 &

Sundays from 2:00 to 4:00 {July 20th & 27th and August 3rd, 10th & 17th}

Circles in the Square

665A Market Hill, Vancouver

Karen Vanon is an artist based in Vancouver. Shes most intrigued by documenting
her time and place. Of particular interest are subjects that are not typically imaged.
Her practice focuses on capturing these subjects spontaneously as they go about
their daily lives, depicting the events and personalities of the ordinary world.

Karens Noticed in Vancouver exhibition will feature her 9x11 map of Vancouver,
as well as select smaller drawings from her Noticed series.

Jillian McDonald: Superfan
May 1 -31, 2008
reception May 1, 6-9pm
artist talk May 3, 3pm
Digital Media

This exhibition highlights work from the past five years, which can best be described as culture-jamming.
In Mcdonald’s recent work spanning video, web art, performance, and photography, she addresses the American cult of celebrity, obsession, and the fantasy that buoys extreme fandom. The presence of her image in the work serves not as a self-portrait, but as a projection of universal emotions such as desire. She confounds the traditional roles of “star” and “fan”, to transcend cinematic conventions, and examines how popular films and their stars affect their audiences.
"Superfan" stars Jillian Mcdonald riding in vehicles with costars Billy Bob Thornton, Vincent Gallo, and Donald Sutherland. Despite their attempts at conversation, the trio of male leads cannot shake her concentration on the Superbowl game. "Staring contest, Brad Pitt" finds Mcdonald and Hollywood's leading heartthrob locked in an endless gaze of a familiar childhood game. In "To Vincent with Love" Mcdonald inserts herself digitally into scenes from Vincent Gallo's "Buffalo 66", playing the ingénue opposite his socially awkward male lead. In “Me and Billy Bob”, she digitally manipulates romantic scenes from Hollywood films starring actor Billy Bob Thornton, creating a soft critique of celebrity obsession.

TALKS at the Vancouver Art Gallery

Tuesday March 15, 7pm

In the Gallery, 4th floor

Peter Samis

THE CULTURE SERIES:

The Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to present a talk by Peter Samis as
part of the Culture Series.

Peter Samis is Associate Curator of Interpretations at the San Francisco
Museum of Modern Art. He served as art historian/content expert for the
first CD-ROM of modern art, American Visions: 20th Century from the Roy R.
Neuberger Collection, and spearheaded the development of interactive
multimedia programs for the inauguration of SFMOMAâ€™s new building in 1995.
Samis is also an adjunct professor at the University of Lugano,
Switzerland, focusing on multimedia and art-related topics, and is on the
governing councils of two museum-focused open source initiatives: Pachyderm
2.0 (www.pachyderm.org) and steve (www.steve.museum), the art museum social
tagging project.

The Culture Series at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents artists, writers,
critics, historians, performers and other cultural practitioners from all
disciplines who are changing the way we think about art, creativity,
culture, contemporary theory and more around the world.

Marking the launch of the Culture Series were talks by musician and artist
Brian Eno and groundbreaking experimental American filmmaker Barbara Hammer
in January 2011. Other events in the series include an artist talk by
artist Althea Thauberger in February, and upcoming talk by Mark Allen of
the Los Angeles-based arts group Machine Project in April.

Look, listen, talk, consider and debate together with these internationally
renowned speakers.

Free with Gallery admission.

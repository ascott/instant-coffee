Ella Gonzales
"Home Office"

Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden

Opening Reception: 23 November 4:30 - 6:30pm

Artist Talk & Exhibition Tour: 23 November 5:30pm

This new body of work parallels Gonzales' recent public art billboard commission at
Mercer
Union in Toronto titled, Sleeve. Both projects are concerned with layering
transparencies as a tool for opacity, iterating gestures such as folding, creasing,
flattening, and the use of abstraction to create tenuous surfaces. The scale has
shifted from imposing to intimate in this iteration at the Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical
Chinese Garden. Because of this, so has the material resonance. In "Home Office,"
pia and jusi silk are sewn together; compressed between their sheets are a
collection of paintings of the same materials. The doubling of silk as container and
content is a deliberate prompt to consider the tools for, and act of, preservation as
part of the work. Though compression is defined by a decrease in volume, it does
not require a reduction of content. Pressing against resistance is resourceful " a
way to store and communicate information, including our personal and collective
histories.

Ella Gonzales is a Filipina Canadian artist working between painting and Computer-
Aided
Design programs. She has recently exhibited at Mercer Union, Toronto; grunt
gallery, Vancouver; The Power Plant, Toronto; Unit 17, Vancouver; Galerie Nicolas
Robert, Toronto; and the Southern Alberta Art Gallery, Lethbridge. Gonzales holds a
Bachelor of Fine Arts from Western University and a Master of Fine Arts from the
University of Guelph.

Future Witch collective is now accepting submissions for projection-based, performance 
and installation art for a non-profit fundraiser event taking place in Vancouver, BC, this 
Spring. Fundraising for the Open Privacy Research Society, the event will be an 
interdisciplinary art experience, featuring a curation of visual art and electronic music 
performers.

The mission of the Open Privacy Research Society is to conduct scientific research 
advancing the areas of privacy and anonymity as they relate to computer science and 
computer security, and to create and publish free and open source software that realizes 
our research and makes it accessible to the public. Open Privacy focuses on Digital 
Harm Reduction for marginalized communities, and approaches Privacy as digital 
Consent.

The theme of the works exhibited will center around cyber/xenofeminism, net neutrality, 
technoccultism, digital consent, queerness, and sex worker rights in the online sphere 
" particularly in relation to issues such as social media policies. Future Witch is an 
occult xenofeminist collective, focused on creating alternative futures using today and 
tomorrows tech, art, and magick. Submissions are open until March 10th, 2019. Please 
email submissions in the form of links or attachments with title, medium and a brief 
statement describing the work(s) to curator Miki Aurora at mikiaurora.info@gmail.com.

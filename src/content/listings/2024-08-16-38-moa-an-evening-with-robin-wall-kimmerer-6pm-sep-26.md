An Evening with Robin Wall Kimmerer:
Book Your Free Tickets Today!

MOA is thrilled to announce an exciting event coming up next month! Join us on
Thursday, September 26 at 6 pm for an evening with Robin Wall Kimmerer,
Potawatomi educator, botanist and New York Times bestselling author of Braiding
Sweetgrass: Indigenous Wisdom, Scientific Knowledge and the Teachings of Plants.

For her 2024 Vancouver Institute Dal Grauer Memorial Lecture, titled "Restoration
and Reciprocity: Healing Relationships with the Natural World," Dr. Kimmerer will
draw on Indigenous and scientific wisdom to offer lessons on how we can heal our
relationships with the
natural world.

Dr. Kimmerer will discuss how ecological restorationcan be understood as an act of
reciprocity, in return for the gifts of the earth. In this thought-provoking discussion
and Q&A, she traces the evolution ofrestoration philosophy and practice, and
considers how the integration of Indigenous knowledge can expand our
understanding of restoration from the biophysical to the biocultural. Reciprocal
restoration includes not only healing the land, but our relationship to the land. In
healing the land, we heal ourselves.

Book signing to follow presentation.

This event is presented in partnership by the Chan Centre at UBC, Critical
Indigenous Studies at UBC, Museum of Anthropology at UBC and the Vancouver
Institute.

Thursday September 26 | 6 pm
Chan Centre for the Performing Arts at UBC
Free, registration required

Group Exhibition - Fjallraven on West Broadway - "Into The Wild"

Exhibition Running July 5, 2024 - August 9, 2024

Our core mission at Fjllrven has always been, and
always will be, to
enable and inspire more people to spend time in nature. This is
because when people spend time in nature, they see for themselves how
important the natural world is to protect. We don't conquer mountains,
tame rivers, or beat the weather. We believe in going slowly, treading
carefully, and always cherishing the places we visit. "Into The Wild"
is the visual representation of how the natural world inspires all of
us.

The Fjllrven store on West Broadway is proud to showcase the works
of over 20 local Vancouver-based artists from varying backgrounds and
artistic disciplines. The theme of "Into The Wild" asks one simple
question: "How does nature inspire you?"

Join us on July 5th from 5-8 pm to celebrate the opening of "Into The
Wild" and enjoy snacks, drinks, and, of course, a 20% friends and
family discount on all regularly priced products.

CALL | Vancouver Park Board | Fieldhouse Activation Program | JUL 19
Vancouver Park Board's Fieldhouse Activation Program transformed former care-
takers suites in parks or underused facilities across Vancouver into active spaces for
community engaged creative practices. In each cycle artist collectives, local food, and
environmental groups are given access to these spaces in exchange for engaging
neighbours, colleagues, and curious visitors in imaginative, collaborative work in parks.
These inspiring projects lead communities into deeper conversations about living in the
urban environment.

The program is currently accepting applications for new residencies in ten fieldhouses
for a 3 year term from Jan 2025 to Dec 2027.

Find out more information about key dates, information sessions and how to apply
here.

Deadline is Friday, July 19, 2024.

Free to attend, drop-ins welcome.

ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

On tour from the Kelowna Art Gallery, this exhibition celebrates the life and work of Sharni
Pootoogook (1922-2003), whose bold and balanced ink-on-paper drawings blur the
boundaries between human and animal, spiritual and visceral. Pootoogooks work draws
clear connections between first-generation artists in Kinngait and the current trends in Inuit
graphic art.

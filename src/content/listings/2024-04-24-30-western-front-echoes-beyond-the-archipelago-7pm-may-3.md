Portrait of Jos Maceda. Courtesy of UP Center for Ethnomusicology.

Western Front is pleased to present Jos Maceda: Echoes Beyond the Archipelago,
a multi-part project about the pioneering work of Filipino composer and
ethnomusicologist, Jos Maceda (1917"2004).

Maceda is a significant yet little known figure within the history of twentieth-century
music, both for his fieldwork on Filipino musicality and knowledge of European
avant-garde music. In particular, Macedas own musical compositions uniquely fused
cutting-edge compositional techniques such as spatialization, attention to timbre,
and musique concrte with traditional Asian instruments, rhythms, and structures.
Curated by the long-term Maceda expert Aki Onda, the exhibition provides greater
insights into Macedas life and work through a display of photographs, print
ephemera, objects, and scores from Macedas
archives, which explore his work as
an ethnomusicologist and his major compositions Pagsamba (1968), Ugnayan
(1974), Udlot Udlot (1975), and Music for Five Pianos (1993).

The exhibition will be accompanied by an opening reception on May 3, 2024
between 7:00 - 9:00 p.m.; a community performance of Udlot Udlot at George
Wainborn Park on May 4, 2024 at 4:00 p.m.; and a month-long installation of
Uganyan in Western Fronts Grand Luxe Hall from May 4 " Jun 1, 2024.

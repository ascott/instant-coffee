Solange Adam Abdala - La Conversin de la Huella | FEB 21

Vancouver, BC, February 21-March 1, 2025 " THIS Gallery is thrilled to announce a
solo exhibition of new work by Vancouver-based Peruvian photographer, Solange
Adum
Abdala.

La Conversin de la Huella (The Conversion of the Trace) explores the
transformation of natures tangible perception through the material intervention of
analog photographic images. By altering the chemical composition of the
photographic medium, the project disrupts its conventional role as a representational
tool, blurring the boundaries between photography and painting"two disciplines
historically examined in modern and contemporary art.

The process begins with chromogenic photographic prints, which are subjected to
bleach, a corrosive agent composed of alkaline salts and chlorine. This substance
erases, disinfects, and removes traces, creating voids within the image. These blank
spaces, or washes, are then reconstituted with pigment, introducing new visual
elements into the composition. The act of erasure and replacement produces
landscapes that are at once surreal, fantastical, and ominous, challenging the notion
of photographic fidelity.

Closing Soon: Lisa Reihana's in Pursuit of Venus [infected]

June 13, 2024 - January 5, 2025

These are your final weeks to experience M-ori artist Lisa Reihana's panoramic video
and sound work, in Pursuit of Venus [infected], which closes on January 5, 2025 at
MOA. Integrating hand-painted landscapes with live-action figures and a densely
layered soundtrack, the exhibition invites viewers to observe a series of restaged
historical events, real and imagined, of the first contact between British and Pacific
peoples.

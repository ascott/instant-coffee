Mixed Media "Watercolour"

Get ready for an exciting adventure in artistry. You will employ watercolour techniques
with acrylics on an unexpected canvas, and then stitch in details. Your artistic sense
will be thrilled with this new medium - it's not your traditional watercolour paper!

Experience is not required! Great for all levels.

Date: April 7th

Time: 11 a.m. - 3 p.m.

Location: 208 - 1000 Parker Street
Cost: $145 + GST
Everything is included!

21. The
Kent-Harrison Arts Council | Artist-in-Residence Show Nicole Dumas | APR 7
The Kent-Harrison Arts Council presents its annual Artist-in-Residence Show, featuring
work by our 2023-24 resident artist Nicole Dumas. The show is called "Welcome to
Lonely Lake" and is an installation of paintings and sculptures made during Dumas' time
here.

The opening reception is on Sunday, April 7th from 2-4 pm at the Ranger Station Art
Gallery (98 Rockwell Drive in Harrison Hot Springs). You can view the show from April 7
until May 5. Gallery hours are Monday-Friday from 1-4 pm and weekends from 12-4 pm.
There is gallery parking out front and the space is accessible (with an entrance ramp and
automatic door).

The Kent Harrison Arts Council would like to acknowledge we operate on the unceded
territory of the Stsailes Peoples.

switch call for entries!

switch is an event that promotes and shows contemporary works of moving 
image in a public context. It offers an alternative way to navigate familiar places, 
creating new encounters. switch has to date shown the artworks of 23 
international artists who have through the project invited the public into their 
practice, ideas and viewpoints. switch locates itself outside of the big city and 
applies itself to the rhythm of smaller places.

The 4th annual installment of switch invites you to take part. VisualArtists 
working with film/video/animation are invited to submit works responding to the 
theme of Balance.

The deadline for submission is Tuesday 1st November 2011.

The event will run from the 5th-12th February 2012 in Nenagh, Co.Tipperary.

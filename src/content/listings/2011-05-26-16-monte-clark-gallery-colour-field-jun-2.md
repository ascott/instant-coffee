KARIN BUBAS | COLOUR FIELD

June 2 to July 2, 2011
Opening reception: Thursday June 2, 6pm to 8pm

Karin Bubas's new body of work engages coloured smoke as an expressive
visual medium. The artist has literally painted on landscapes of
forests and snow with various artificial hues, documenting the results
through large-scale photographs and small oil paintings based on her
photographic captures.

These misty landscapes float somewhere near minimalism, the focus of
the viewer drawn towards the fog shapes. In certain images the hues
are dreamy, while in others the smoke infusion appears bizarre. The
contrast of the synthetic within these outdoor settings results in
unearthly landscapes, highlighting Bubas’s well-known ability to
create mysterious and captivating narratives within her images.

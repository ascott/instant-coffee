Art Dubai is looking to recruit a Press & Marketing Manager and 
Graphic & Web Designer to join its dynamic, creative team in Dubai.

Press & Marketing Manager
The Press & Marketing Manager will oversee the fair's PR, 
marketing and social networking strategy, liaise with the media and 
press agencies, and develop partnerships with local and 
international organisations and sponsors. The ideal candidate will 
have a background in contemporary art with an interest in the Middle 
East, North Africa & South Asia (MENASA) region, a minimum of 
three years Public Relations and/or marketing experience, fluent in 
written and spoken English; proficiency in Arabic preferred but not 
required.

Graphic & Web Designer
Implementing the fair's brand identity, the in-house designer will 
create templates for and populate each piece of collateral, and see 
the materials – including catalogues and booklets - through from 
concept to production. The ideal candidate will be bilingual in 
spoken and written English and Arabic, have a degree in graphic 
design, have a minimum of two years professional design and 
production experience, and be proficient in web and HTML design. 
An interest in contemporary art and in being a part of the growing 
cultural scene of the Gulf is preferred.

Application Deadline: 30 April 2011

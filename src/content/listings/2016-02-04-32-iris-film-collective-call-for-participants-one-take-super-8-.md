ONE TAKE SUPER 8!

IRIS FILM COLLECTIVE announces ONE TAKE SUPER 8

Call for Participants: REGISTER HERE!

One Roll. No Splices. Shoot and Show.

In 2000 Alex Rogalski held the first ever One Take Super 8 event in
Regina. Since then it has inspired over 1000 films in 50 venues around
the world!

Iris Film Collective is pleased to present the First Vancouver Edition of
One Take Super 8. This is an open call for you to participate: One Take
Super 8 is first come first serve!

Registration ($50) includes film, processing & a public screening.

You may choose from a limited amount of Black and White Kodak Tri-X
200D or Colour Reversal Agfa 200D cartridges. Your film will be lab-
processed and presented on the big screen. You will also receive a
screening fee!

As this is One Take, all editing must be done "in camera" as you shoot,
and the film will be presented just as it was filmed. No peeking before
the night of the screening, it's straight from the Lab to the Screen! This
is a project full of surprises and innovations, so get creative: animate,
gyrate, deviate, Super 8!

You must live in the Vancouver area to participate. (Richmond,
Burnaby, Surrey, North Van, West Van, Horseshoe Bay etc. are all
fine!)

The premiere screening of all the films will be free and open to all. No
experience is necessary and we will be holding two info sessions in
early March for those who haven't shot film before or want some
pointers or guidance.

Cameras will be made available to borrow (with a deposit) on a first-
come first-serve basis. (Cameras may of course also be rented or
purchased elsewhere at varying prices and levels of reliability.)

TIMELINE

FEB 1 - Registration begins

MARCH 1 - Film cartridges are distributed

MARCH 2 to April 14 - SHOOT!

APRIL 15 - FILM IS DUE

LATE MAY/EARLY JUNE - PREMIERE SCREENING at THE GRAND
LUXE HALL, WESTERN FRONT (date TBA)

NOTE: If your film is not submitted by April 15th AT THE LATEST it will
not be screened or processed and your screening fee will be forfeited.

Many thanks to BC Arts Council, Niagara Custom Labs and the
Western Front.

ART AFTER DARK REMIX - April Session
Thursday, April 20, 2017
7:00 PM - 9:00 PM
Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond BC
www.richmondartgallery.org

A re-mix of music and visual art, this monthly program is for aspiring artists and 
DJs to meet, socialize, and experiment with your desired art mediums or to try 
something new. This month we feature DJ Beaubien! Listen to soulful house 
music as you work on comic book drawings inspired by the works of cartoonist 
Seth, featured in the Omnis Temporalis exhibition.

FREE drop-in. All skill levels of artists and DJs welcome to join.
Basic art materials and instruction on drawing and DJ techniques provided.
Artists welcome to bring their preferred drawing materials - no wet/messy media 
permitted in the Gallery space.
Not suitable for children under 14 years of age.
Presented by Richmond Art Gallery and Richmond Media Lab, 3rd Thursday of 
each month.

Guest Artist Bio:
After over a decade as a house music lover and supporter of the local scene, 
Beaubien was born when Lila Bujold started mixing soulful and deep house 
music after her favorite club night ended and there was nowhere to hear the 
uplifting and classic style of house music she craved. Born and raised in 
Vancouver, BC this West Coast Canadian found a deep affection and love for 
East Coast House Music: soulful vocals, happy keys and rich bass lines bring 
depth and warmth to her mixes.
Beaubien's Carte Blanche radio show airs every Saturday night 7-8pm PST on 
SPiNZFM.COM and mixes the best in soulful and deep house with an uplifting 
and inspirational vibe. Over the last three and a half years, Beaubien has held a 
residency at the Distillery Bar in Vancouver where she mixes the "sounds of the 
future past" ; crooner, 60s spy movie, soul, swing and jazz classics and modern 
throwbacks.
"I get chills from a great piano lick, a bold woman singing about how it is great to 
be alive and the deep rhythm of house" - DJ Beaubien

The Sun and The Soil
May 24 - June 29, 2024
CityScape Community ArtSpace Gallery
Opening Reception Thursday, May 23, 2024, 7- 9pm
Artists: Anna Heywood-Jones, Emily Neufeld, Jennifer Brant, Mitra Mahmoodi, and
Mehran Modarres-Sadeghi
At the foundation of the work presented by the five artists in this exhibition is a sensuous
experience of land, interdependence, and wayfinding. Whether articulating the
relationship between fibre, dye, mordant, soil, and season through colour and cloth,
these artists prioritize
perception, both the act of seeing and the dynamic participation of
body with place.

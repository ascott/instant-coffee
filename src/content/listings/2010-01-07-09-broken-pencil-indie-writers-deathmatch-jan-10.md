Broken Pencil, the magazine of zine culture and the independent arts,
presents the third annual edition of its short story contest, Indie Writers
Deathmatch.

First Prize: $300, publication, and a Broken Pencil gift pack including
t-shirt, pins, the BP anthology of fiction published by ECW Press and
singed books by BP editors Hal Niedzviecki and Nathaniel G. Moore.

$20 dollar entry fee, payable ONLINE or by mail

DEADLINE January 10, 2009

3000 words max

Indie Writers Deathmatch is a super fun contest. The BP editors select the
top 8 entries. Two at a time, the selected stories are posted online and
voted on by BP readers. Last year's Deathmatch had over 20,000 visitors.
The top story moves on to the next round until there is a winner.

for complete information on how to enter, visit
http://www.brokenpencil.com/deathmatch or email richardr@brokenpencil.com

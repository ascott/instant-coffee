Reading Group: 
Rebecca La Marre in Conversation 
w/ Tiziana La Melia
June 29, 2019

Join Fillip and artists Rebecca La Marre and Tiziana La Melia on Saturday, June 29, at 
2pm at Hotam Press, Vancouver, for a performance and discussion on the slippages 
between language and bodies. 

This event takes as its starting point the publication of Love Is The Language That Sex 
Speaks, a new artists book by La Marre released by Ma Bibliothque. La Marres book 
is a collection of found texts of uncertain authorship, derived from overheard 
conversations and passages from books, articles, films, and images"all collected as if 
physical material.

For this event, La Marre will re-stage her 2015 performance at Matt's Gallery, London, 
that led to the publication of Love Is the Language that Sex Speaks and La Melia will 
read from her recent publication The Eyelash and the Monochrome and Other Poems, 
published by Talon Books. The readings will be followed by a discussion between the 
two authors.

Entrance to this event is free but space is extremely limited and an RSVP is required.

About the Presenters

Rebecca La Marre is a queer Canadian artist with a writing, research, and performance 
practice. Her work investigates how ritual, desire, and technology shape bodies, and 
what that contributes to an experience of being a person. 

Her work has been exhibited and published internationally include at the Serpentine 
Gallery, MOMA PS1, and the Darling Foundry. She is an emeritus commissioning editor 
for E.R.O.S. Journal and is currently Editor and Publications Coordinator at Remai 
Modern, Saskatoon.

Tiziana La Melia is a Vancouver based artist, writer, and filmmaker. Her work manifests 
long-term, collaborative investigations of desire, gender, architecture, the history of 
painting, ecology, place, and memory.

She is the author of The Eyelash and the Monochrome (Talonbooks) and Oral Like 
Cloaks, Dialect (Blank Cheque Press). Her writing has been published extensively 
including by C Magazine and The Capilano Review, and recent exhibitions include those 
at Mercer Union (Toronto), the Vancouver Art Gallery, Oakville Galleries, Walter Phillips 
Gallery (Banff), and Damien and the Love Guru (Brussels).

CSA SPACE
Presents: SAVE NOW
By Kelly Lycan
November 22 - January 10, 2008
Opening Reception: 7pm
Friday, November 21, 2008

The act of collecting, and the recycling of existing collections lies at
the conceptual and material core of Kelly Lycan's sculptural practice. Save
Now, Lycan's solo exhibition for CSA continues her examination of the
visuals of consumer culture, but more specifically addresses her material
pursuit and research of runaway consumption. This includes an investigation
of spaces that catch the overflow of abundant objects that are in a state
ofsuspension from appreciation to depreciation.

If the acquisition of things is both a palliative act and a stimulant,
what happens to these collections after they serve their therapeutic and
consumptive purposes? Whether it is the stuff of junk drawers, ordered
shelves or deep storage (self-storage is now a 17 billion dollar annual
industry in the North America), Lycan is interested in looking at the
configuration and negotiation of what has selectively been gathered and
saved, and not yet abandoned.

Collecting is a common social practice that reflects behavioral patterns
as it mirrors aspects of public and private life. It constructs a way in
which we relate to each other and instructs how we order the material
world. One main focus of Lycan's work has been the way that objects are
valued and devalued depending on their place of display. Save Now
materially stores, orders and reorders consumer culture and its
relationship to industrial design and contemporary art practices, creating
a link between market culture and visual culture in order to address how
aesthetic
values are created; how materials are used in the formation of identities
and ideologies; and how these forms take up space in her life.

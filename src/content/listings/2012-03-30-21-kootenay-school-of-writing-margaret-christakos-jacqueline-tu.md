Margaret Christakos' books include What Stirs (2010) and Sooner 
(2008), both nominated for the Pat Lowther Memorial Award, and 
Excessive Love Prostheses (2002), winner of the ReLit Award. More 
recently, her Welling (Scrivener, 2010) was a Globe 100 Book. 
Originally from Sudbury, ON, her writing practice since 1989 has 
included many editorial and curatorial projects. She is currently a 
Chalmers Arts Fellow, investigating abraded matrilineage and girls' self-
representation, working on a novella embodying grief called L's Wake, 
and completing a textwork exploring the poetic solo and social called 
Multitudes. A Creative Writing instructor at U of T School of Continuing 
Studies since 2006, she is also Associate Faculty with University of 
Guelph's Creative Writing MFA.

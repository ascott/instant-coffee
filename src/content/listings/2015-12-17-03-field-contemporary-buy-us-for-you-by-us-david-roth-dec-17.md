Buy Us, For You, By Us
David Roth
December 18th, 2015 January 16th, 2016
Opening Thursday December 17th, 2015, 7:00 10:00PM
FIELD Contemporary
17 West Broadway, Vancouver. BC

Buy Us, For You, By Us is a locally-inspired look at urban planning and
lifestyle marketing. The open-concept space features the debut of DRP, an
artisanally-curated unisex streetwear line featuring locally-sourced
organic materials. Buy Us, For You, By Us will also offer scheduled
barbershop services (trims only), allowing for community contributions
towards future editions of DRP designs. Visitors are also welcome to stop
by the official DRP workshop where lead designer David Roth will be
producing new works that are well-suited for both private collections and
the socially-conscious style hunter. All DRP originals will be available
for purchase both in-house and through our online shop, When I DRP, You
DRP, We DRP. Limited edition prints of DRP editorials will also be
exhibited and made available for photo and fashion enthusiasts alike. By
Us, For You, By Us is a community space, dedicated to conversations about
fashionable innovation, socially-progressive consumption and what quality
means to you.

David Roth's practice is the product of his economic surroundings. Raised
in an expensive city and surrounded by financial excess with limited
personal means, David decided to study the relationships between private
wealth, artistic worth and social value. David produces installations that
act as stages where he performs comic versions of achieving artistic and
financial success. As an economic comic, David wholeheartedly attempts
achievement to the point of absurdity, encouraging audiences to re-think
how they understand worth and value.

Talk: Art Talks in Plain English Series "January 15th 5:30 PM to 7 PM
Place: Connection Salon Gallery at the Gathering Place Community Center, 609
Helmcken
Once a month, 2 graduate students from Emily Carr University of Art and Design will
give a 20-minute presentation about their work followed by informal discussion.
Snacks and refreshments will be provided.
Plain English is talk that a general audience can understand the first time they hear
it. Plain English is clear, straightforward expression.

In January the presentations will by:

Lucine Luo
Living Materials: How Transformation Creates meaning.

Jason Young
Intuitive, Abstract, queer portraits and figures exploring human connection and the
relationship with self.

This event is supported by the British Columbia Council for the Arts, the Connection
Salon Collective and the Gathering Place Community Center.

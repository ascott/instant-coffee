laughing, crying, and dancing the night away on Thursday, October 16th at
the Anza Club.

Featuring the formidable talents of the following guests:

Roaming Magician, magic that comes to you.
Rowan Lipkovits on accordion
Charles Demers, Stand-up comedian
April O' Peel singing, dancing and comedy
The Alley Bourbon Bootleggers on banjo and ukulele
The Skinny, Sketch comedy troupe
and DJ Cam Labine

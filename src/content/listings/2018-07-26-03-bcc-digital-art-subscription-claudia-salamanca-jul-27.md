Commissioned Artist: Claudia Salamanca

Subscribe by July 27 to receive an exclusive copy of Claudia's commissioned
digital artwork: http://www.decoymagazine.ca/bcc

Claudia Salamanca is a visual artist and theorist working on the
intersection between the visual and the political. She is assistant
professor of New Media at Arkansas State University in the Department of
Arts + Design. She received her BFA from Universidad de Los Andes in Bogot
Colombia. Salamanca earned her M.L.A. from Rutgers University with a
Fulbright Scholarship with emphasis in Science, Technology and Culture and
holds a PhD in Rhetoric from University of California, Berkeley.

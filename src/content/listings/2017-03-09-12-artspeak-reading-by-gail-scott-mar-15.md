Wednesday, March 15, 2017
7:00pm
At the invitation of Jeff Derksen, SFU English Department 

Gail Scott is a Montreal-based writer, translator and editor whose innovative prose and theory 
has been influential across Canada and North America since the publication of her first book, 
Spare Parts (Coachhouse 1982/2002). Her subsequent books, Heroine (Coach House, 1987; 
Talon, 1997), Main Brides (Toronto: Coach House, 1993), My Paris (Mercury Press, 1999), and 
The Obituary (Coachhouse 2010) situate her work in the nexus of poetics, narrative, and 
literature as a reflexive engagement with the textures of the urban and the social. 

Gail's essays - which push at the form and poetics of critical thought -- are gathered in Spaces 
Like Stairs (Women's Press, 1989). As an editor, Gail's collaborative work has opened a space 
for a critical feminist investigation of language, literary form, and the social -- she is associated 
with the crucial journal Tessera and is a founding editor of the French-language cultural journal, 
Spirale, and co-edited the key anthology, Biting the Error: Writers Explore Narrative 
(Coachhouse, 2004).

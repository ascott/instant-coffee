Exhibition: Quixotic

Date: Fri Apr 5-Wed Apr 17, 2024

Where: Spruce Atrium, Spruce
Gallery, and Arbutus Gallery (Library) at Kwantlen
Polytechnic University, Surrey Campus

12666 72 Avenue
Surrey, B.C. V3W 2M8

We are KPUs Bachelor of Fine Arts Graduating Class of 2024! We welcome
everyone to the Quixotic Exhibition at the KPU Surrey campus. Our works are
installed at the Spruce Gallery, the Spruce Atrium, and the Arbutus Gallery.

Visit our Instagram page!

IG: @quix.ot.ic

The art show titled "Quixotic" signifies diverse and imaginative pursuits. Through
their art, the graduates challenge norms, explore visionary themes and invite viewers
to embark on imaginative journeys, reflecting the essence of "Quixotic" ideals in the
realm of contemporary art.

The "Quixotic" art show marks the culmination of years of artistic development at
KPU. It showcases the journey of each graduating artist in creating works that reflect
their unique artistic practices. From inception to execution, these pieces embody
technical skill, conceptual depth, and personal growth.

At KPU, we
live and work on traditional and unceded territories of the Kwantlen,
Musqueam, Katzie, Semiahmoo, Tsawwassen, Qayqayt and Kwikwetlem peoples.

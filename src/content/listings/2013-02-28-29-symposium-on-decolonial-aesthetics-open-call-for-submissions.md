Open Call For Submissions

Symposium on Decolonial Aesthetics from the Americas

Toronto, Canada
10, 11, 12 October 2013
Special issue of FUSE Magazine - September 2013

Organized by e-fagia organization in collaboration with FUSE Magazine, and 
Justina M. Barnicke Gallery / Hart House. 

Sponsored by Canada Council for the Arts.
Deadline: 7 April 2013
Decolonial Aesthetics From The Americas is a multidisciplinary and 
collaborative symposium for artists and scholars of the Americas and the 
Caribbean. Featuring papers, workshops, performances, and online 
components, the symposium will provide an unparalleled opportunity to 
engage the diversity of contemporary aesthetic practices informed by 
decolonial thought. A special issue of FUSE Magazine, to be published in 
September 2013, will serve as a reader. We invite separate proposals for the 
symposium and for the magazine, and especially welcome joint proposals 
that work across both platforms.

Submissions should engage the current state of aesthetics and the 
contemporary artistic sphere in the Americas. In addition to the traditional 
formats of papers and panel sessions, we will incorporate a wider artistic 
program.

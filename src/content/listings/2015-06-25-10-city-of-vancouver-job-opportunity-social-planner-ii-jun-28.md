Job Opportunity
Social Planner II (Signature Projects Site Planner)
City of Vancouver - Cultural Services - Public Art
Deadline to apply: June 28, 2015

The City of Vancouver Public Art Program is seeking an individual to
undertake a complex, multi-layered assignment to develop and deliver a
city-wide plan that guides the placement of Signature Projects -
international calibre public artworks - for many years to come. An
exceptional combination of public art, land-use, and/or community planning
experience at senior levels is required. The incumbent must take initiative
and be comfortable exercising independent judgment. The position will lead
and develop a plan that identifies a site suitable for a concentration of
artworks such as a sculpture park but also, locations across the city that
have site conditions suitable for stand-alone artworks. The associated
tasks require knowledge of public art placement practices, public
art/sculpture park models, and related land-use policies. Familiarity with
international models and practices will be an asset. The assignments also
require in-depth consultations with senior City and Park planners and the
public.

For more information and how to apply: Job Posting - Social Planner II

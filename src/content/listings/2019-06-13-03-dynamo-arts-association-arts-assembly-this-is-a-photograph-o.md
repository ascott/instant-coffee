This is a Photograph of Me 
Bu Benim Foto-raf-m

June 13 - 30, 2019
Opening | June 13, 2019, 6pm - 9pm
12 - 5pm, Thursdays through Mondays
DAA Projects, 30 W. 6th Ave, Unit 103
Vancouver, BC, Canada

This is a Photograph of Me | Bu Benim Foto-raf-m will take place in Vancouver, in June 
2019 as part of phase two of Common Ground | Uyuma Temeli. Curated by halka sanat 
projesi, This is a Photograph of Me | Bu Benim Foto-raf-m will exhibit Canadian based 
artists, Tanya P. Johnson and Terriann Walling to explore themes of individual restriction 
in relation to identity, expression and instability. A parallel exhibition of This is a 
Photograph of Me | Bu Benim Foto-raf-m and a documentation of both years of the 
project Common Ground | Uzlama Temeli is taking place in tandem at halka sanat 
projesi in Istanbul, Turkey. Exhibition title inspired by "This Is a Photograph of Me", 
Margaret Atwood.

Call for Applications: Assistant Curator

Applications due: Friday 1 August 2014, 5:00 PM

Term of employment: 28 Weeks

Hours: 35 hours/week

Salary: $18 per hour.

Position description:

The Art Gallery at Evergreen (AGE) at the Evergreen Cultural Centre in
Coquitlam seeks an Assistant Curator to support the

development and delivery of the AGE’s exhibition, public engagement and
education programs. With the AGE celebrating its twentieth anniversary and
the City of Coquitlam celebrating 125 years since its founding in 2016, and
the celebration of 150 years of Canadian Confederation in 2017, the
Assistant Curator will assume a leading role in the development of the
AGE’s anniversary programming and support development efforts towards the
realization of this programming and building the AGE’s collection. The
Assistant will also participate in the research and development of
exhibition, education, public engagement and marketing and communication
initiatives for the 2014/2015 programming year.

Responsibilities:

Assist with curatorial development, management, installation and
dismantling of fall and winter 2014 exhibitions.

Provide research assistance and administrative support to the Curator on
the development of upcoming exhibition and

public engagement activities.

Participate in the development of programs for the AGE’s twentieth
anniversary that will run from summer 2016 through to 2017 and Canada 150
programming and work with ECC staff and related committees to develop and

coordinate the AGE’s twentieth anniversary programming initiatives.
Support the continued development of the AGE’s digital and physical
programming information architecture including scanning of archival slides
of past AGE exhibitions with records for the year beginning 1996.

Develop and lead weekly public tours related to the exhibitions presented
over the course of employment term.

Contribute writing and editing services towards the preparation of AGE
publications, didactic materials and educational

and public engagement program materials

Provide assistance with the development and delivery of marketing and
communications initiatives for the all AGE

programs including initiatives supporting the development of funding
applications from public and private sources for the AGE’s 2014/2015,
2015/2016 programs and for anniversary year programs.

Conduct acquisition research, registration, collections management and
participate in the development of the AGE’s collections management
strategies and policies.

Support the development of the AGE’s youth and art & technology programs
and initiate program specific projects.

General administrative duties.

Qualifications:

The Assistant Curator will be a graduate in Fine Arts, Curatorial and
Critical Studies, Art History, Interactive Arts, History or related field
and will possess:

Demonstrated knowledge of current contemporary Canadian and international
artistic trends.

Exceptional communications skills

Exceptional organization skills

Strong interpersonal skills

Ability to complete tasks with minimal supervision.

Ability to take initiative and problem solve.

Computer experience with web applications, Microsoft Office and basic
database knowledge.

JIM BREUKELMAN: ALTERED STATES

MAR 20 - MAY 11

Opening Reception: MAR 19, 7-9 p.m.

Artist Talk: MAR 21 at 1:30 p.m.

Artist Talk: APR 27 at 2:00 p.m.

Join us for an artist/curator talk related to the new exhibition, Jim Breukelman: Altered 
States, which is a part of the Capture Photography Festival. In this exhibition, 
Breukelman revisits series from 1966, called Sansons Diner, where it is installed with 
more recent series of photographs of a taxidermy shop (After Life), a biosphere 
(Mesocosm) and altered landscapes (Planted Life).

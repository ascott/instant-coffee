Invitation to artists to exhibit their work in the Zack Gallery.

Call to artists for our spring Festival HaRikud in celebration of Israel at 70 on the 
theme of Israeli Music through the years.

This is an unjuried call to artists of all levels who would like to participate in a group 
show in celebration of Festival HaRikud at the Sidney and Gertrude Zack Gallery at 
the Jewish Community Centre.

Each artist will be offered a small cradled wood panel from Opus to be picked up at 
the Zack Gallery and signed out on the form provided. Panels will be available from 
January 15th onwards until we run out. These versatile panels can be used with 
almost any medium: oil, acrylic, watercolour, pastel, encaustic, photography and new 
media. 

The artists are asked to do an artwork on the theme of Israeli music as mentioned 
above however the theme can be loosely interpreted.

The finished panels are to be returned wired and ready to hang by May 8. The artists 
name and the title of the piece should be on the back of the panel.

There will be an opening for the exhibition on May 15 from 7 to 9pm and the artists 
will have the pleasure of inviting their friends and family to this event.

At the opening night each piece will have a silent auction sheet attached with an 
opening bid of $ 70.00. The bidding will be open for 10 days. 100 % of the proceeds 
from sales will go to the gallery. The artists whose work sells will receive a tax 
receipt in the amount that the work sold for. The artists whose works dont sell will be 
asked to retrieve their pieces after the show.

Your participation in this show is encouraged and appreciated.

Thank you, Linda Lando, Zack Gallery Director | gallery@jccgv.bc.ca

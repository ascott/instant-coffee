Project Space (run by OCW Arts & Publishing Foundation in 
collaboration with Project Space Studio) is looking for interns for 
Spring/Summer 2012:

Position: 
Internship

About Project Space:

Project Space is a book shop, publisher, programming space and 
studio run by OCW Arts & Publishing Foundation in collaboration with 
Project Space Studio. As the possibilities inherent to digital space 
become increasingly infinite, the roles of physical and print spaces are 
being redefined. Project Space examines, challenges and supports this 
redefinition process by presenting print and in-person projects—
including a curated selection of publications, exhibitions, workshops, 
lectures and readings—for artists, designers, writers, curators and 
publishers. Our shop features a curated selection of art, design and 
literary publications with a focus on those that are rare, independent, 
small-run, limited-edition or locally-produced. 

We are located at 222 E Georgia St, next to Access Gallery and across 
from 221A Artist Run Centre. 
Description: 
Project Space is seeking self-motivated and bright individuals for 
Spring/Summer internship positions that will run from late April until 
September 2012. Interns will work at the space 3 days a week from 
noon to 5pm to assist with:
Running the bookshop / project space
Organizing and facilitating Project Space's programming (such as 
exhibitions, openings, workshops, readings, launches and special 
events)
Administration related to the bookshop and our various projects 
Maintaining our online shop and contributing to various social media 
platforms (Facebook, Twitter, Tumblr) 
Interns will receive discounts on books and programming, as well as 
mentoring and resources from Project Space's Directors and 
Committee Members. 

Qualifications: 
The ideal candidate would be—

passionate about publication and either contemporary art, design 
and/or literature 
excited about Project Space, its programming and working in the space
reliable, resourceful, self-motivated, detail-oriented and able to work 
independently
excellent at collaboration, cheerful and outgoing, with a positive attitude 
and a willingness to learn new things
experienced in working with the public and comfortable making 
conversation with customers/visitors to the space
skilled at using Word, Excel, Facebook, Twitter, Tumblr and Wordpress 
skills such as writing, editing, photography, videography, graphic 
design, industrial design, web development or grant writing would also 
be assets
non-profit or retail experience would also be an asset 
How to apply:
Please submit a cover letter and resume to info@projectspace.ca no 
later than 5pm on Monday, April 9, 2012. Please indicate the skills you 
will bring to the internship, why you are interested in interning at Project 
Space and what you are hoping to learn/get out of the experience.

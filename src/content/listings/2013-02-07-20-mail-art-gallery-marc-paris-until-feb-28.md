Limbo - Marc Paris

Mail Art Gallery, February 1-28, 2013

>>>

Marc Paris is a Vancouver born artist with a home based studio practice. He 
is pursuing a bachelor’s degree at Emily Carr University. The shared space of 
the Mail Art Gallery with a post office excites his ongoing study of liminality.

>>>

Mail Art Gallery is a unique exhibition platform situated inside a postal retail 
outlet. The gallery merges retail space with art and design, blending poetic 
tendencies with daily life.

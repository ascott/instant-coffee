About (Let Us Believe In) The Flight
(Let Us Believe In) The Flight is a performance piece by Sevin offering an intimate
glance into a moment where body is suspended in anticipation, waiting for: words,
warmer days, waiting for meaning encased in rituals of devoted repetition. The Flight
features fragmented visual projections and a soundscape created by the artist,
accompanying this short journey.

The title and conceptual elements in this piece draw inspiration from one of Forough
Farrokhzads longest and most contemplative poems, Iman Biavarim Be Aghaz-e Fasl-
e Sard / .... ... .

About the artists
Sevin is a multi-disciplinary artist, a gear in several machines. In her creative practices,
Sevin is currently devoted to themes of
transformation, deconstruction and destruction,
as well as the sensuality/sentience of 'non-living' material structures. She navigates her
artistic explorations and documentations in a multi-disciplinary fashion, with the idea of
cross- pollination in mind. Her visions are encapsulated over the span of several
mediums including photo/video, performance, curatorial works, and poetry.

Lydia Pourmand is a swiss army knife of a designer. Specialising in Motion Design, AI
image/video, Graphic Design and disruptive technology. She is a digital visionary with a
diverse background in projection mapping, visuals for record labels, design for
marketing, fine art, photography and working in frontier tech. She is experimental
which brings an original flare to any project and role. Based in between her hometown
London, UK and Vancouver.

The University of British Columbia (UBC)
Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory (AHVA)

works on paper

Exhibition: January 25 " February 24, 2018
Opening Reception: Wednesday, January 24 (5 " 7 PM)

Please join the UBC Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory (AHVA) at the AHVA 
Gallery in the Audain Art Centre on Wednesday, January 24, from 5 to 7 PM for the 
opening reception of works on paper, an exhibition featuring recent work by AHVA visual 
art students in the undergraduate program. The exhibition presents a range of media 
which incorporate paper within their process including photography, printmaking, 
drawing, painting and mixed media.

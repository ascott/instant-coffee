The Or Gallery Society's Annual
General Meeting is being held on Tuesday, November
5 at 6.30-7.30PM at Emily Carr University of Art + Design in Room D2435 (east wing of
2nd floor).

The AGM is open to Or Gallery members in good standing and is a great opportunity to
meet the Or Gallery Board of Directors, staff and other members.

The 2019 AGM comes shortly after the Or Gallery and Bookstores move to a new
storefront location at 236 E Pender Street.

Sunny Nestler & Liz Knox, Unpacking My Bookshelf
Opening Oct 19, 4-6pm
Exhibition runs October 16 - 27, 2018

Faculty Gallery, ECUAD
520 East 1st Avenue, Vancouver

Unpacking My Bookshelf brings together works by Sunny Nestler and Liz Knox. The 
artists have long standing bookmaking practices that employ repetition and 
endurance. The exhibition title alludes to Benjamins essay Unpacking My Library, a 
text about book collecting and the relationship between a collector and their 
collections, order and disorder, use-value and fate.

Sunny regularly engages ideas around mutation and replication with a humourous 
tongue-in-cheek approach to details from desert landscapes. Unpacking My 
Bookshelf includes works from their newest book Undergrowth, Glow Worm 
Habitats, Coneworms Growing, and Glow Worm Paragraph. In these works, 
everyday objects multiply, grow off of one another and make funny object jokes. 
Dont worry, as a non-object you will still understand when a joke is occurring. Liz 
collects text from web-based platforms, often user-generated sources. Unpacking 
My Bookshelf includes her book Missed Connections, Synopses drawings and a 
poster project Bataille You Scoundrel. Using Craigslist missed connections from 
Occupy Wall Street, IMDB content advisories for classic films and a repurposed 
alphabet, these works mine found text to generate wry portraits. Sunny and Liz tend 
towards endurance based works that are built on an idiosyncratic logic. Details are 
put under the microscope and examined again and again and again.

Sunny and Liz are also exhibiting together at the Vancouver Art Book Fair, go to the 
second floor of Emily Carr to check out their table and get a complete view of their 
publishing projects. VABF runs from October 19-20, 11am - 7pm, and October 21, 
11am - 6pm. Unpacking My Bookshelf runs Oct 16 to 27 and is accessible to the 
public from 7:30 am to 11:00 pm during the week, and 8:30 am - 11:00 pm on 
weekends. This venue is wheelchair accessible - please contact 
accessibility@ecuad.ca to request additional access details. The exhibition is 
located on the unceded, traditional and ancestral xmky"m (Musqueam), Swxw7mesh 
xwumixw (Squamish), and sl"ilw"ta" (Tsleil-Waututh) territories.

Sunny Nestler received an MFA from ECUAD in 2013 and has taught here since 
2012, including drawing and science classes. Sunny has published work as an 
illustrator and book/zine maker, and exhibited in the US and Canada. Sunny's work 
is based in drawing and studies mechanisms of biological life using a process that 
mimics DNA replication and mutation.Their work also embodies connections 
between biological processes and DIY communities and sometimes takes the forms 
of video, installation, performance, or painting. Sunny is one of the founders of the 
Tempe Zine Fest (2010) and a contributor to the first Underground Publishers 
Convention (Phoenix, AZ, 2009) and regularly participates in zine and book fairs, as 
well as organizing community events.

Liz Knox uses conceptual strategies, examining systems of interpretation with an 
emphasis on irony. Language is the most common thread in her work, and a 
common starting point is found text. Much of her work exists as artists books, large 
wall-text installations, videos, and drawings. Her practice involves intensive periods 
of research which usually result in book or video projects. Her 5th book is 
forthcoming in Spring 2019, ask her to see a sample offprint if you are at VABF 
2018. Liz studied Studio Art at the University of Guelph and received her MFA at 
ECUAD in 2013. Her work has been shown in exhibitions and film festivals across 
Canada including Paved Arts, Saskatoon; Vtape, Toronto; Winnipeg Underground 
Film Festival, Winnipeg; Cold Cuts Film Festival, Dawson CIty; and the Justina M. 
Barnicke Gallery, Toronto.

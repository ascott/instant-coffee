Materiality and Mourning " A creative workshop exploring grief through artmaking

Amberlie Perkin is an interdisciplinary artist and art educator whose practice
explores how embodied engagement with the natural world can shift our
perspectives on the fragility of the body, loss, and death. She sees artmaking as a
regenerative and hopeful activity which allows us to process and visually express
experiences of grief. Making art is a means to creatively and physically
acknowledge grief by moving it through our bodies, while remembering and
honouring those we have lost.

The event will begin with Amberlie leading a short tour of her exhibition Veils &
Echoes. In the workshop to follow, participants will explore grief
through simple relief
printmaking techniques & creative writing activities using natural materials they have
collected outside.

No previous experience is needed in either of these areas- simply a willingness to
explore emotion through the lens of nature and creativity.

All art supplies will be provided.

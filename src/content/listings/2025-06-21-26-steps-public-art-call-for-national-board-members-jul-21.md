Call for National Board Members @ STEPS Public Art
Application Deadline: Monday, July 21, 2025

STEPS is a national charity and social enterprise that cultivates inclusive public spaces
and supports artists to reimagine the places where we live, work, and gather. We're
looking for new members to join our Board of Directors whose expertise will shape our
next chapter of growth!

We're interested in candidates who bring one or more of the following skills: Finance,
fundraising, human resources, communications, cybersecurity, public art, placemaking,
community engagement

Thanks for your consideration!

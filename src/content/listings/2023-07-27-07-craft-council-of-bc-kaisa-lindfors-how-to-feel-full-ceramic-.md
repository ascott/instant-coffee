Artist talk: 6:00pm July 27 @ Carousel Theatre, 1411 Cartwright Street, Granville

Info:

An exhibition and artist talk by Revelstoke ceramist Kaisa Lindfors exploring memory &
experience.

Lindfors presents a new body of work that includes a series of vessels that are painted
and written on using excerpts from her journal " visual and written " juxtaposing the
fragile yet long lasting nature of the material against the making of a usually private
practice; both permanent and
fragile, quiet and public.

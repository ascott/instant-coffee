VAG
750 Hornby St, Vancouver, BC

Date: Saturday, June 2 at 3pm

Vancouver Art Gallerys Described Tour in Honour of BC Access Awareness Day

Organized in partnership with VocalEye and Kickstart Disability Arts and Culture

Join Vancouver Art Gallery for a described tour of Emily Carr in Dialogue with Mattie 
Gunterman facilitated by Steph Kirkland, Founder & Executive Director of VocalEye 
as 
well as art educators Marie-France Berard and Lynn Chen. The Gallery tour is 
designed 
for people who are blind or partially sighted; however, all are welcome to attend.

Sighted guides are available to escort visitors with vision loss to and from the 
Gallery. 
Those requiring a sighted guide can call 604-662-4700 or email 
buddies@vocaleye.ca. 
The meet up location will be at the ticket level of Burrard Skytrain station at 2:45pm. 
Sighted guides will return visitors to this location at the end of the event, at 5pm or 
earlier, as needed.

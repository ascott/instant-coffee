Kristina Lee Podesva, Miles Collyer, Michael Morris & Vincent 
Trasov, and Rhonda Weppler & Trevor Mahovsky

LIKE SOME POOL OF FIRE

Opening: Friday, January 14, 7:30pm
Artist Talks at 8pm

Victoria — Open Space is pleased to announce Like Some Pool of 
Fire, an exhibition of artists employing formal and political concerns 
to develop new languages in colour theory.
Join us for an opening reception and artist talk on Friday, January 14 
starting at 7:30 p.m. The exhibition will continue until February 12, 
2011. 

Curator Gerry Gauthier has brought together Kristina Lee Podesva 
(Vancouver), Rhonda Weppler and Trevor Mahovsky (Vancouver), 
Michael Morris and Vincent Trasov (Victoria/Berlin) and Miles Collyer 
(Toronto) to chart strategies in colour-based practice. In this arena, 
the physicality and value of the art object are territories for discovery 
through the use of props, studies, and building blocks that explore 
the constantly changing nature of colour – from deviations of 
perspective, ways of seeing, light sources, and retinal 
discrepancies.

Like Some Pool of Fire takes wide-ranging approaches to colour, 
invoking a multigenerational response, from Fluxus to contemporary 
concerns, illuminating critically chromatic dialogues.

64 pp book available by download at openspace.ca on January 14, 
2010.
Featuring projects by Michael Drebert, Raymond Boisjoly and 
Kristina Lee Podesva
and essays by Byron Peters and Gerry Gauthier.

Thursday, April 21
Lulu Series: Art in the City

Michael Rohd
The Arts as Civic Practice: Listening is the New Revolution

Michael Rohd, founder of the Center for Performance and Civic Practice
(Evanston, IL), will offer insights into developing collaborative projects
with community and civic organizations. By engaging in a community-defined
practice, his work reveals new ways of employing artists to build healthier
communities. Michael is also the founding artistic director of Sojourn
Theatre, a 17-year old ensemble-based company and 2005 recipient of
Americans for the Arts' Animating Democracy Exemplar Award. This talk will
be preceded by a short performance by hip hop/spoken word artist,
beat-boxer and youth educator JB the First Lady.

Richmond City Hall Council Chambers
6911 No. 3 Road, Richmond
7:00 PM, FREE

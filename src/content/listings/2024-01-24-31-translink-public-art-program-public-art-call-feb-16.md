Public Art for Customer Facing Facilities

This project is an opportunity for three artists to showcase existing artwork designs in
three of TransLinks new customer facing facilities (one artist per facility), which open in
2024. Vinyl decals will be applied to large areas within three customer facing facilities
to create an immersive, mural-type art experience. Artists are invited to apply for the
project with existing (already made) 2D artworks.

All artists (residing in BC), including emerging artists and artists without public art
experience are encouraged to apply. This is a design-only contract and artists will not
be required to do project management.

Artist Fee: $2,000.00 CAD per artist/artwork

Deadline: February 16, 2024.

For more
information and to apply please visit:

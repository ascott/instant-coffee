Sunday, December 2, 2018
1:00 - 4:00 pm
Free drop-in for all ages

Richmond Art Gallery
7700 Minoru Gate
Richmond Art Gallery
www.richmondartgallery.org

Family Sunday is a free arts event that occurs once per exhibition in the Richmond Art 
Gallery. 

Join us in December for painting projects inspired by the work of Pierre Vassura. Guest 
artist Russna Kaur will lead demonstrations on how to create colourful mixed media 
paintings full of shapes, patterns and symmetry. All art supplies provided.

Activities include: Painting projects | Story time | Gallery Tours

All ages welcome to attend.
Children must be accompanied by an adult throughout the event.
Free drop-in - registration not required.

Tours and projects led by the RAG Youth Collective.

www.richmondartgallery.org

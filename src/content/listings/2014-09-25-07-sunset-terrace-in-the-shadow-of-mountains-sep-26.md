ALEXI BARIS, AILEEN BRYANT, TRISTAN UNRAU, MATTHEW ROBERTSON.

1. IN THE SHADOW OF MOUNTAINS

Farmers fields line the gravel road with long dashes of gold. A youngish
man sits atop a ladder in a quaint, tin roofed structure. His thumb guiding
a blade through the Birch with which he works. In contemplation of his
body's ability to persist, the mind leads to mortality; the expulsion of
energy serving as an expression of ones ephemeral nature. Each day can only
produce so much. The blisters are now covered by gloves. The hand reaches
in and places another log on the fire.

2. A SMALL SOUTH FACING BALCONY

A woman mimes the lines to a yet unwritten song as she cares for her
plants. Feeling, and flooding them, watching the soil as it drinks up the
water. She listens as it drips from the pot to the saucer. The moment is
shared with decisions regarding the value of contours and surface
inflections. The shape is beginning to materialize, slowly carved away from
everything else leaving a feathered edge. Once the rain begins it rarely
rests before the saucer if full. These calculations are momentarily
thwarted by practicality and the inherent desire to survive. Momentarily.
Her dog begins to bark

3. SEAT 16 A.

Gazing out the window the lights begin to appear one by one. A handful at
first and then by the tens and quickly growing to numbers beyond counting.
He had boarded the flight as the sun was setting. By now he was afloat
somewhere in the atmosphere above the mountains, lost in thoughts of what
was to be. By the time they begin to descend the lights shone in every
direction as far as the eye could see. Imagining his place among this
unfamiliar cluster of stars makes his feet sweat. As the earth approaches,
the stellar assemblage gives way to familiar forms of warehouses and
sporting complexes. Even this banal vernacular harbours an air of excitement.

4. DUSK ALONG THE INLET

As the saucer shaped edifice slowly dematerializes behind him the reverie
sets in. Contained within the milky way, containing the milky way, knowing
there are other ways, he is compelled to compose. Something catches his
eye. An unorthodox celestial body? Was it a square or a circle? Whatever it
was it seemed luminescent in green and didn't linger. Retreating as hastily
as it appeared.

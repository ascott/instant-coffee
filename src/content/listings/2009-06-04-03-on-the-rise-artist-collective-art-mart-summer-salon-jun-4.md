Emerging Vancouver Artists sell their art for $500 or less

June 4th – 28th, 2009

Preview Thursday, June 4th, 2009 from 5:00 – 8:00 p.m. 

Emerging Vancouver artists take over On The Rise Artist Collective for our Summer Salon. Thrilled
with the inaugural exhibition of ART MART, On The Rise Artist Collective is pleased to announce 
that ART MART will take over the gallery with a Salon style exhibition focused on original art at
affordable prices. 

Visit our South Granville Gallery and view diverse, innovative artworks by up and coming local 
artists. On The Rise’s Summer Salon will feature a variety of multi-disciplinary artworks ranging
from photography, painting, printmaking and mixed media. Priced at $500 or less ART MART’S 
Summer Salon provides a unique opportunity to own your very own masterpiece without breaking 
the bank. 

With all proceeds going directly to the artists, the Summer Salon is consistent with On The Rise’s 
mandate of providing emerging and established artists with unparalleled career opportunities on 
South Granville. 

Join us Thursday June 4th from 5pm -8pm for our Preview Celebration and enjoy a live acoustic 
music performance by Canadian singer/songwriter Natalie Edwards. 
Come on in and Shop! 
For additional information please email Katherine Dennis at press@ontheriseac.ca.

Brad Phillips
Somebody Write Me

At Monte Clark Gallery, April 28 to May 28, 2011
Opening reception: Thursday April 28, 6pm to 8pm

Brad Phillips' exhibition of new paintings explores, in a manner
central to confessionalist poetry, the cliche of the male artist as a
mentally ill, alcoholism prone, sexually voracious type of a person.
The work points out and pokes fun at this stereotype, and as Phillips
says, "autobiographical paintings [suggest] the possibility of this
[stereotype] being a true assessment of myself as an artist and
person."

Brad Phillips graduated from the Ontario College of Art in 1995 and
has had solo exhibitions at Monte Clark Gallery (Vancouver and
Toronto), Wallspace Gallery (New York), Groeflin Maag Galerie
(Zurich), LaMontagne Gallery (Boston), among others. His work is 
held
in numerous collections including the Royal Bank of Canada, 
Sammlung
Hauser + Wirth Collection (Switzerland), and the Glenbow Museum.

Please join us for a tree planting in conjunction with Mexican
artist Pedro Reyes, currently featured in The Tree: From the
Sublime to the Social at the Vancouver Art Gallery.

Friday April 4, 2008, 10:30 am
Rain or Shine
MacLean Park, Strathcona
710 Keefer Street (at Heatley Avenue)
TREE PLANTING
750 Hornby Street
Vancouver BC V6Z 2H7
Tel 604.662.4719
www.vanartgallery.bc.ca

For Palas por Pistolas, Pedro Reyes, an internationally-acclaimed artist, worked  with a local Mexican agency to encourage people to hand in their weapons in  exchange for food stamps. 1,700 traded guns were made into spades and each  spade is to be used to plant a tree. The Vancouver Art Gallery, in collaboration  with the Vancouver Board of Parks and Recreation, will plant a tree using one of  these special spades.

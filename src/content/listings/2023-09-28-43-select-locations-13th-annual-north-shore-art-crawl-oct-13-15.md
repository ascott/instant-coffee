13th annual North Shore Art Crawl

Over 150 artists at 69 locations

October 13, 7pm-9pm Opening Receptions at select locations

October 14-15, 11am-5pm

northvanarts.ca

Immerse yourself in imagination and the vibrancy of creativity this fall, as
150+
artists in 69 locations enliven the North Shore for the 13th annual North Shore Art
Crawl. Studios, galleries, and host businesses from Horseshoe Bay to Deep Cove
are opening their spaces to community for this free weekend event, celebrating local
artists and artisans.

Whether entering an artist studio, visiting a local gallery, or popping into an art-filled
caf or brewery, visitors will be met with a unique and vibrant array of art, design,
and craft. The Art Crawl features a dynamic range of mediums and styles, including
illustration, mixed media, oil, pastel, watercolour, jewellery, clay, metal, glass,
printmaking, design, wood, and textiles.

The North Shore Art Crawl welcomes connection, with 50 artists hosting Friday night
opening receptions, and another 50 offering demonstrations and art activities over
the course of the weekend. We are especially excited to welcome 26 artists
participating for the first time this year, and to see new, captivating works and
collections
from fabulous returning artists.

"I'm excited to show new and existing work, meet people interested in art and maybe
have a pint or two...and North Point is a perfect match - it's a cool venue and the
vibe of the brewery is well suited for my styles." - Alex Asai - a new artist
participating.

"Ive participated in the North Shore Art Crawl for 6+ years and have really enjoyed
throwing the the studio doors open and welcoming in the public. Its a great way to
meet new clients and to get feedback on new collections. It also gives you a
deadline to aim at for new pieces and forces a good studio tidy up! " - artist Danyne
Johnston

Learn more and plan your visit at northvanarts.ca, or by picking up a printed event
guide at community hubs throughout the Lower Mainland. Your North Shore Art
Crawl event guide can be kept as your lasting resource directing you to local artists
and galleries!

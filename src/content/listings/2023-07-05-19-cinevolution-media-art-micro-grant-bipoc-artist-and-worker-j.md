Are you interested in exploring media art? Do you identify as a racialized emerging
artist or arts worker with lived experience of disability?

Cinevolution is offering five flexible grants to support self-directed learning, research,
and creative experimentation in media arts September 15-December 15, 2023.

We welcome applications from emerging artists of all ages, with priority for those with
limited access to arts funding and/or based outside urban centres. You do not need
formal arts training to apply.

For program guidelines and application instructions, visit https://bit.ly/cinemg23

We offer access supports and multiple ways to apply. Contact Sarah Wang at
submissions@cinevolutionmedia.com or by phone at 778-803-7150, extension 5, or
attend our June 23 Information Session for details.

Deadline: July 14, 2023, 11:59pm Pacific Time

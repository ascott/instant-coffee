Bringing Light and Life to Vancouver Alley

Westbank and TELUS unveil new work by contemporary artist Martin Boyce at
TELUS Garden

Westbank and TELUS invite journalists to meet Martin Boyce, one of the
world's leading contemporary artists, who unveils "Beyond the Sea, Against
the Sun" at TELUS Garden.

6:00 pm
Wednesday, October 12
TELUS Garden
Back Lane
510 West Georgia Street

Why
North America's back lanes are known for deliveries and dumpsters. Inspired
by the lanes in Europe that add significant character to a neighbourhood,
Boyce's geometric lantern sculpture challenges local perceptions and draws
people into the evolving space behind the TELUS Garden mixed-use building.

What
Westbank and TELUS engaged Boyce as part of the City of Vancouver Public
Art Program to create a work for the alley of TELUS Garden.

Who
Martin Boyce, winner of the Tate's 2011 Turner Prize, has work in
collections at the Museum of Modern Art in New York, Tate in London, Museum
fr Moderne Kunst in Frankfurt, Germany, Carnegie Museum of Art in
Pittsburgh, PA, Gallery of Modern Art in Glasgow, among others. In 2009,
Boyce represented Scotland at the 53rd Venice Biennale.

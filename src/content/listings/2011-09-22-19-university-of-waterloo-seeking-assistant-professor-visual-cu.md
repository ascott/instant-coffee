Assistant Professor Visual Culture/Film and Media Studies

The Department of Fine Arts at the University of Waterloo invites applications for 
a tenure-track position at the rank of Assistant Professor in Visual Culture with a 
particular emphasis on media (film, television, emerging media). Candidates 
should have a PhD or be nearing completion of a PhD in a relevant discipline 
(visual culture studies, media studies, film studies, art history, etc.) and should 
have demonstrated research and teaching interests that examine theory, 
criticism, and historical inquiry across a range of visual media production which 
could include film and television history, documentary film, avant-garde and 
alternative film practice, video and digital media. Interest in the global context of 
media practice is an asset. Candidates must demonstrate potential for 
excellence in scholarship and in graduate and undergraduate teaching.

The successful candidate will work with the department chair to co-ordinate the 
Film Studies and Visual Culture undergraduate degree program, which draws 
upon the vibrant culture of visual studies both within the department and the 
university as a whole. The Department of Fine Arts also offers undergraduate 
degree programs in Art History and Visual Culture, and undergraduate and 
graduate degrees in Studio. Ability to supervise graduate students in studio 
research and to contribute to the pedagogy of the MFA graduate seminar will be 
considered important assets.

We are seeking a person with the energy, enthusiasm and vision to explore 
cross-disciplinary interaction with other departments and faculties. Applicants 
should be active in research of national or international significance. 
Commitment to excellence in teaching, collegiality and a willingness to contribute 
to the community life and ongoing evolution of the department are equally 
important qualities. The Department of Fine Arts offers a vibrant collegial 
atmosphere within a renowned, innovative university 
(www.finearts.uwaterloo.ca). The Waterloo Region is a culturally active 
community and home to a number of institutions including the 
Kitchener/Waterloo Art Gallery, Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics, 
Cambridge Galleries, the Canadian Clay and Glass Gallery, and the University of 
Waterloo Art Gallery.

Applicants should include in their response:

· Cover letter;

· A teaching philosophy (one page);

· A research statement (one page);

· A CV detailing educational and professional background, publication history, 
teaching experience, and demonstrated excellence in teaching;

· The names and contact information of at least three persons who may be 
contacted regarding the candidate's qualifications.

All qualified candidates are encouraged to apply; however, Canadians and 
permanent residents will be given priority. The University of Waterloo 
encourages applications from all qualified individuals, including women, 
members of visible minorities, native peoples, and persons with disabilities. The 
appointment is subject to final budgetary approval.

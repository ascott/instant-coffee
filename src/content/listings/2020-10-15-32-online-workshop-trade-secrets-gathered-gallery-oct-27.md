ONLINE WORKSHOP - TRADE SECRETS: WHAT GALLERIES EXPECT WITH
ALISSA SEXTON | October 27th, 7pm- 8:30pm EST | $30.00 | PRESENTED BY THE
GATHERED GALLERY, HOSTED ON ZOOM

Ever wonder why some artists around you seem wildly successful in sales or gallery
representation?

Join Artist Consultant and industry expert Alissa Sexton for a presentation and candid
discussion on how to work towards gallery representation, or, if it applies, how to work
together with your gallery to increase sales.

Topics
covered include:

Deciding whether gallery representation is right for you
Strategies for increasing sales without a gallery
What galleries want and how to gain representation
What galleries expect from artists
What artists should expect from their galleries
Q&A time will follow, giving participants time to ask Alissa questions relating to the topic.

Are you ready to join a supportive community of artists teaching you how to build a
profitable, sustainable, and authentic creative career?

Join the workshop now!

$30

October 27, 2020

7:00 - 8:30 PM EST (Toronto)

(We have implemented a subsidized payment option for those who self-identify as
members of the BIPOC and/or LGBTQ2S+ communities to support artists facing
statistically fewer opportunities in galleries, magazines, and institutions.)

10% of profits from this workshop will be donated to Shelter Movers.

About Alissa Sexton:

Since 2005, Alissa has facilitated representation for emerging and established artists
while acting
as co-director for Canada's longest-running contemporary gallery. Alissa
now focuses her attention on individual artist consultations, providing artists with the
assistance they need to succeed in the commercial art world. By coaching through
studio and curatorial work, and advising on online presence and market trends, her
guidance has helped hundreds of artists reach their goals. In 2020, Alissa founded the
temporarily online exhibition space Artwrk.ca, which helps artists by providing an
accessible e-commerce exhibition solution.

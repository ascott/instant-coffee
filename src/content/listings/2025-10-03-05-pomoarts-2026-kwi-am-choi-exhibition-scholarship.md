PoMoArts is excited to announce applications are now open for the 2026 Kwi Am Choi
Exhibition Scholarship for emerging artists!

Established in 2007, this annual scholarship awards $2,000 to two emerging BC artists
(ages 18"32) to support the creation and presentation of a solo exhibition at PoMoArts
from July-August 2026. This is an opportunity for recent graduates and current Fine Arts
students at recognized Canadian post-secondary institutions, offering funding for new
artwork creation, curatorial mentorship, and professional guidance to bring your
exhibition to life.

Apply by November 16, 2025 for the opportunity to showcase your work in a public
gallery, gain mentorship, and connect with other artists and arts professionals!

Apply today at pomoarts.ca/get-involved/kwi-am-choi-scholarship

A Study in Restraint - Nanlaban. Cu Unjieng is a Filipino-Canadian artist, and the work is
political. Five vertical stacks of large ceramic vessels are anthropomorphic, at human
scale or slightly larger, the tallest at 7.5 ft. They are kind of monuments to a resilient
people, ceramic bodies standing in for absent bodies.

Nikkei National Museum is pleased to present its ninth annual BLOOM party and 
silent art auction on Saturday, June 10, 2017 from 2 - 4pm. This is the museums 
main fundraiser, to support our quality exhibits and educational programs related to 
the history and culture of Japanese Canadians.
Taking inspiration from the Kimono Culture exhibition, which will be showcased at 
the Nikkei National Museum (NNM) from May-September 2017, BLOOM this year, 
will be around the theme of kimono with a bonus theme of tea! Info & tickets: 
purchase tickets on Eventbrite or call 604.777.7000 ext.109 | jcnm@nikkeiplace.org

We invite guests to attend in kimono or Japanese inspired attire to add to the 
festivities.
Kitsuke, professional kimono dress + styling by Satsuki-kai, $25 by appointment. 
Limited number of womens kimono + mens yukata available with booking in 
advance. Contact Karah at 604-777-7000 ext 110 or email 
kgoshinmon[at]nikkeiplace.org to make an appointment. 

Bid on select artists interpretations of KIMONO and Japanese Culture

Wine by Kanazawa Wines
Japanese Tea Ceremony by Maiko Behr

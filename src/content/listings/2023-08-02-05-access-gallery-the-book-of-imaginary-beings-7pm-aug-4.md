Join us at the opening reception on Friday 4 August from 7 to 9pm
The Book of Imaginary Beings, Access Gallery

5 August - 9 September 2023

Curated by Ophelia Yingqiu Zhao

If it is believed
that materials encapsulate memories in physical forms, a
constellation of stories will arise from the prism of an imaginary reality. Curated by
UBC MA Candidate in Critical and Curatorial Studies, Ophelia Yingqiu Zhao, The
Book of Imaginary Beings invites you to enter into the physical presence of art
objects as living creatures. Taking its title from a book by Jorge Luis Borges, the
exhibition space transforms into an animated sphere, where the art objects
themselves become storytellers, narrating their own tales through their material
bodies. Featuring artists Sai Di, Marcy Friesen, Woojae Kim, Josh Steinhauer, and
Katayoon Yousefbigloo, each work intimately embodies sounds, images, tastes,
consciousness, and spirits in their physical relations to us, with the hope that our co-
existence will become temporal, energetic, and successive in space.

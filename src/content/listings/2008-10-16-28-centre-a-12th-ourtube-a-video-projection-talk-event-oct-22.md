Centre A presents

The 12Th OurTube: A Video Projection & Talk Event

Wednesday, October 22, 8:00pm - 10:00pm

Host: Debra Zhou

Centre A: Vancouver International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art

2 West Hastings Street
Tel: 604-683-8326 www.centrea.org info@centrea.org

Free Admission. Everyone welcome

For the 12th edition of Our Tube, we invite you to join us with Debra Zhou
for a report of her two-week research trip to Shanghai last month.
September is an exciting month for the art scene in Shanghai. The 7th
Shanghai Biennale, SHcontemporary 2008, and Shanghai Art Fair 2008 opened
in the same week, along with a dozen of other exhibitions at Bizart,
Shanghart, MOCA, Zendai MOMA, Duolun MOMA, Ke Centre for the contemporary
arts, etc, featuring local and foreign artists. Although not replacing
Beijing as the art centre of China anytime soon, Shanghai is slowly cooking
up its own artistic flavour. Small groups of young emerging artists are
focusing on more experimental and conceptual art practices. These practices
are outside the institutional boundaries and without much attention from
commercial art dealers. Ke Centre and Fei Contemporary, two non-profit
artist-run centres, opened in the last two years to join the oldest
non-profit art centre Bizart. These organizations create a dynamic platform
for contemporary art in Shanghai.

Debra Zhou is Centre Aâ&#65533;&#65533;s gallery coordinator. She has worked for Shanghai
Duolun Museum of Modern Art as a curatorial assistant and Yishu Journal as
a project coordinator. She is preparing an exhibition at Centre A featuring
artists based in Shanghai.

OurTube will be held at Centre Aâ&#65533;&#65533;s lounge space on the last Wednesday night
of every month after gallery hours. Each program will be curated by local
and visiting curators and artists, who will also invite the curator for
next month so that it continues like a chain event. The guest curator will
select series of videos for projection, and initiate the discussion
following the screening. Drinks will be served during the screening, so the
atmosphere is pretty casual just like at home.

Our Tube is a play on YouTube. The idea behind the project is not only to
broadcast your video, but also to share the experience of watching video
with others and talking about it.

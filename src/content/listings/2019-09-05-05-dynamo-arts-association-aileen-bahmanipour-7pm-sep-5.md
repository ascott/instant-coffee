ARTIST: Aileen Bahmanipour
OPENING: September 5, 7:00 - 10:00 PM
DATES: September 6 - September 27, 2019 (BY APPOINTMENT)
LOCATION: Dynamo Arts Association, East 6th Ave, Suite 103, Vancouver
Free for public

A Performative Critique is an exhibition of installation works by Vancouver-based,
Iranian-born artist Aileen Bahmanipour that explores the dichotomy between ready-
made and artist's hand/artistic labour through cultural identity. In this installation project,
Bahmanipour expands on this question that If ready-made defeats the artists hand and
singularity, can juxtaposition or even overlap of ready-made and artistic labour create a
new form and discourse today?

The exhibition is accompanied by a text by Leigh Tennant.

Artist Bio:
Aileen Bahmanipour is an Iranian born Vancouver-based artist. She has received her
BFA in Painting from the Art University of Tehran and MFA in Visual arts at the
University of British Columbia. Bahmanipour has exhibited her work in a body of solo
and group exhibitions in Iran as well as in Canada, including her solo and group
exhibitions at Vancouvers grunt gallery, Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, Gallery
1515, Hatch Art Gallery, and Two Rivers Gallery. She is the recipient of the Social
Sciences and Humanities Research Council grant in 2017 and Gail and Stephen A.
Jarislowsky Artist Award in 2019.

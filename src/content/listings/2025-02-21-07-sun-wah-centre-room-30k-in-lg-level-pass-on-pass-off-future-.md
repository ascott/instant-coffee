pass on, pass off: Future Artists in Residence reveals a storytelling of grief and
healing through archived memories of family, community lineage, and shared
language. Through a diasporic recounting of intergenerational and cultural
knowledge, our Future Artists explore their intimate histories in a lifelong journey to
reconnect with their communities and collectively resist colonial disruption. In
processing healing through chronic illness and familial loss, reclaiming
intersectionality of gender and spirit, pass on, pass off weaves together community
belonging, collective grieving, and land-based healing. This exhibition seeks to
preserve ancestral knowledge against the erasure of language, family connection
and cultural traditions, carrying memories as a vessel into the next generation.
We invite you to experience the collaborative works of Jasper, Joy, and Sarah;
by
collectively exchanging in dialogue and engaging with our artists in shared healing,
resilience, and endurance.

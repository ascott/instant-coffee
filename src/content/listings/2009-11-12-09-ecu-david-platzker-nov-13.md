DAVID PLATZKER
Lecture
Friday November 13th at 7:30
ECU Theatre (SB301)

Please join the Charles H. Scott Gallery and The Emily Carr University
Speaker Series for a talk by New York-based curator David Platzker. In
Vancouver for the opening of his exhibition Vintage Dan Graham: Projects
for Publication 1966-2009 (opening November 17th at the Charles H. Scott
Gallery), Platzker will speak about artists' books, editions, his
curatorial practice, and his Chelsea gallery Specific Object.

Specific Object aggregates interesting objects in any artistic medium and
presents them in a contained venue, allowing these works their own place,
space and time. The material shown ranges from artists' publications,
ephemera, prints, multiples and other editions to literature, music / audio
works and unique artworks of the contemporary world. Please visit
www.specificobject.com.

From 1998 through 2004 David Platzker was the Executive Director of Printed
Matter, Inc. He is also the co-author, and co-curator with Elizabeth
Wyckoff of Hard Pressed: 600 Years of Prints and Process, and - with
Richard H. Axsom - the book and exhibition entitled "Printed Stuff: Prints,
Posters, and Ephemera by Claes Oldenburg : A Catalogue Raisonne 1958-1996".
Recently Platzker curated Lynda Benglis / Robert Morris : 1973 -974 at
Susan Inglett Gallery. He has curated exhibitions of the works of Art &
Project, John Baldessari, Marcel Broodthaers, "Documenta 5", Conceptual
Art, Marcel Duchamp, Donald Judd, Bruce Nauman, Oldenburg, Raymond
Pettibon, Dieter Roth, and Edward Ruscha in addition to commissioning or
curating exhibitions at Printed Matter of Angelblood, Larry Clark, Erin
Cosgrove, Meg Cranston, General Idea, Jenny Holzer, Reverend Jen, Allan
Kaprow, Yoko Ono, Ryan McGinness, Sonic Youth, Tom Sachs, David Tremlett,
Richard Tuttle and the Guerrilla Girls. Platzker was also the host of
WPS1.org's Recorded Matter on-line radio program. Archived programs can be
found at www.artonair.org.

Vintage Dan Graham: Projects for Publication 1966-2009
Opens November 17th at 7:30 pm in the Charles H. Scott Gallery and runs to
December 20, 2009.

Admission to the talk and exhibition is free. For further information
please visit chscott.ecuad.ca or telephone 604.844.3809.

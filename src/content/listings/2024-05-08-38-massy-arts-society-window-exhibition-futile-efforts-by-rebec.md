" Futile Efforts by Rebecca Wang " at Massy Arts Society (window
exhibition)
April 21 - May 30, Wednesday - Saturday, 12-5pm

Featuring two interconnected bodies of work, this exhibition explores the
construction and negotiation of selfhood in a contemporary diasporic context,
through partly fictional, partly autobiographical narratives. A Chinese term for efforts
in vain for a predetermined goal, " is employed as a pessimistic defence
mechanism to navigate disappointment, and to highlight how the permeating
boundaries between the cultural and the personal, the public and the domestic, and
the conscious and the unconscious, impact ones sense of self and ones
understanding of what they can accomplish.

Rebecca Wang " is an artist and curator based on the unceded territories of
the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh nations (Vancouver, Canada) and in
her hometown Hangzhou, China. Drawing from her own diasporic experiences, her
multidisciplinary and curatorial practices investigate the absurdity ingrained in the
structures that uphold the everyday, which is often characterized by capitalist
consumer culture.

ARTISTIC DIRECTOR

The Artistic Director is a dedicated, passionate, visionary and highly
motivated individual. Reporting to the Executive Director, and working with
the Board of Directors, Executive Director, Administrative Assistant,
contract staff and volunteer committees, the Artistic Director is
responsible for developing and maintaining the artistic vision of the
Powell Street Festival Society. The Artistic Director carries out the
creative projects in each season (as developed collaboratively by the
Programming Committee and approved by the board of directors), as well
assists in grant writing and some production management. The Artistic
Director will work closely with the Executive Director to develop accurate
budgeting forecasts and maintain productions on budget. This is a part-time
(15 hrs/wk) position that increases hours during late July Festival
production and Festival presentation.

Director in establishing the Powell Street Society as a renowned community
hub to engage with Japanese Canadian art and culture.

ADMINISTRATIVE ASSISTANT

Powell Street Festival Society seeks a dedicated team player for the
position of Administrative Assistant to join our staff permanently,
starting in March 2015. This is a part-time (20 hrs/wk) position that
increases hours during Festival production and Festival presentation.

Reporting to the Executive Director, the Administrative Assistant will work
with the Executive Director, the Artistic Director, seasonal staff
(Production Coordinator, Volunteer Coordinator and Communications Manager),
Board of Directors and Festival Core Committee. The Administrative
Assistant will be responsible for general administration for the Festival
and year-round programs.

Duties and Responsibilities:

Administration:
-Manages Society database and tax receipts.
-Manages and maintains phone and email correspondence.
-Drafts and prepares a range of documents related to Society business and
Festival production.
-Manages regular website updates.
-Assists with coordination of the Board Retreat.
-Assists with preparation of agendas, paperwork, etc for various meetings.
-Ensures that workplace remains hospitable and in good order.

Publicity and Community Outreach Assistance:
-Assists with regular publicity production, including but not limited to:
website and social media account updates, fall and summer newsletter
production, monthly e-newsletter production, Festival program production,
poster and rackcard/postcard production and distribution.

Programming Assistance:
-Liaises with artists and curators for bookings and program development,
grant applications and publicity.
-Books out-of-town artist flights, assists with project development for
season events.

Fundraising Assistance:
-Assists with preparations for fundraisers by soliciting donations,
securing venues, liaising with artists/caterers/technicians, coordinating
volunteers.
-Solicits Festival lottery donations, acquires Gaming licence, oversees
lottery ticket production, coordinates Festival lottery sales.
-Participates in monthly evening Fundraising meetings.
-Assists with Festival sponsorship requests.

Production Assistance:
-Oversees Festival permit acquisitions.
-Coordinates the Festival Volunteer Party, including securing a venue,
soliciting donations, arranging food orders, organizing volunteer prizes
and games.
-Updates, distributes, receives and organizes Festival artist applications.
-Confirms Festival community groups, performers, vendors and venues.
-Collaborates with Production Coordinator and Volunteer Coordinator in
production tasks as needed.
-Assists at various PSFS events, including taking coordinator role at the
Festival

QUALIFICATIONS

-Excellent interpersonal and communication skills
-Strong time management and multi-tasking skills
-Ability to work flexible hours, including occasional weekends and evenings
-Ability to work independently and as part of a team
-Microsoft Word and Excel experience required
-Database and website maintenance experience an asset
-Photoshop and Illustrator skills experience an asset
-Fundraising and event production, particularly in the non-profit cultural
sector, an asset

LEARNING OPPORTUNITIES

The Administrative Assistant will gain important on-the-job skills and
knowledge particular to cultural non-profit organizations, including
fundraising skills, project management, event production and promotion, and
grant writing skills. Through our main partnerships and productions, the
Assistant will gain knowledge and insight about Vancouver and BC artistic
and cultural communities, and be afforded the opportunity to network and
situate his or her self within those communities.

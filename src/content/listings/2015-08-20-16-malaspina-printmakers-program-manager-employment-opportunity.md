ORGANIZATION DESCRIPTION
Celebrating its 40th anniversary, Malaspina is a non-profit artist-run
centre that advances education, promotes excellence in the art form, and
increases the public’s understanding and appreciation of contemporary art
and print media. Malaspina supports and promotes the contemporary visual
arts through the critical and technical exploration of print media.
Through the excellence of our facilities and programming, we create a
vibrant, productive, and sustainable community of artists and art enthusiasts.

JOB DESCRIPTION
Malaspina is looking for a Program Manager to help us with event planning,
fundraising, office administration, managing the studio, promotional
activities, and liasing with members, volunteers, staff, and visitors. We
are looking for an energetic individual with lots of initiative and a
commitment to the arts community. This is a part-time and flexible two year
position with potential for growth.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Designing and publishing newsletters, social media posts, program guides
and website updates
Assisting the fund development committee with fundraising events
Planning and scheduling workshops
Preparing and installing exhibitions in coordination with Executive
Director and artists
Coordinating and planning exhibition openings

QUALIFICATIONS
Excellent written communication skills
General office administration skills
Background in the arts
Strong sense of initiative
Supervisory experience (asset)
Curatorial experience (asset)
Event planning / fundraising skills (asset)

HOW TO APPLY
Email cover letter and resume to info@malaspinaprintmakers.com by August
31, 2015

REMUNERATION
$24,000 plus benefits per year (part-time two year term with growth potential)

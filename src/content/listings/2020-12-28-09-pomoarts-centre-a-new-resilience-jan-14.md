A New Resilience

Sonya Iwasiuk

Join us to open PoMoArts
January exhibitions, and stay for a Virtual Artist Talk and Tour
with Sonya Iwasiuk, January 14, 2021 at 7:15 pm, on Facebook Live.

A body of work that corresponds with one of the most pressing current world issues "
people fleeing their turbulent homelands searching for peace and prosperity. Iwasiuks
multi-media installation is based on the First Wave of Ukrainian immigrants to Canada. This
project is visually based on Ukrainians and a past time, but many of the same
circumstances, issues and hardships are analogous to Canadian immigrants today.

The history of Ukrainians in Canada speaks to many human wrongs and parallels ongoing
and current world issues of displaced refugees running from war, famine, poverty,
oppression or prejudice. It also tells of a story many of us do not like to admit or face; how
our new Canadian immigrants face prejudice and racism here, in our beloved country,
especially in todays political environment.

Enjoy this exhibition with an in person visit to the
Gallery or by viewing the online gallery on

Plan.

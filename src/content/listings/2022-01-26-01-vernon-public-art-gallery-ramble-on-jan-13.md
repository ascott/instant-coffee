Ramble On, Vernon Public Art Gallery

January 13 - March 9, 2022

Vernon Public Art Gallery
3228- 31st Ave, Vernon
Open Mon - Fri 10:00 - 5:00, Saturday 11:00 - 4:00

Ramble On showcases new acquisitions of UBC Okanagan's Public Art Collection with
works by Tania Willard, Judith Schwarz, Sheldon Louis, Moozhan Ahmadzadegan, Neil
Cadger, David
Wilson Sookinakin, Clinton McDougall, Ida Shichi and Takesada
Matsutani.

The exhibition was inspired by UBC Okanagan alumnus Clinton McDougalls wooden
sculpture of a car tire Reinvention (2005). The tread on the tire forms a pattern based
on the utilitarian principle of adding grip or traction to move forward. The idea of using
patterns, repetition or grids to gain traction or derive meaning is a universal principle.
From the longitude and latitude of cartography, to charting information in graphs, to
ideas of the matrix (with or without Keanu) " there is something about hypnotic
repetition and persuasive grids that artists utilize to communicate complex ideas with
clarity, impact and irreverence. Curated by UBC Okanagan Gallery curator, Stacey
Koosel and hosted by Vernon Public Art Gallery, the exhibition showcases Okanagan
Indigenous contemporary art and new acquisitions of the UBC Okanagans Public Art
Collection.

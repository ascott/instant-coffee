The Storm and The Fall

Vanessa Kwan

Opening: April 25, 8pm

April 26 - June 7, 2008

Artist Talk: May 10, 2:00pm



Access is please to present The Storm and The Fall, an exhibition by Vanessa Kwan. 

The front gallery features a large-scale wall installation—a self portrait of sorts—of two dominant images relevant to the West Coast landscape: the tidal wave and the condo. Inspired by narratives of both economic expansion and environmental collapse, the work mimics glossy surfaces of billboards and advertisements while depicting an individual’s interaction with contemporary realities of catastrophe and urban development.

The Storm and the Fall is accompanied by a site-specific sculpture created from materials culled from demolition sites and housing developments in the area immediately surrounding Access

Condo Zine - Call for submissions

This zine may function as an obituary for Vancouver as we 
understand it today.

This zine may function as a place for individuals or collections of 
such to address the loss of their home, chill zone, cultural centre, 
organization, snack bar, beer bar, queer bar, community garden, art 
studio, park or way of being due to a 'renoviction' or the introduction 
of a real estate development.

Condo Zine is accepting contributions in the form of anything that 
can be printed and distributed in a zine format.

This includes, but is not limited to: drawing, photography, poetry, 
narrative, political rants, spelling mistakes, and so on.

Condo Zine will be printed in B&W, so please don’t grind down your 
favourite prismacolors.

Please send print quality images and text to:

vancouvercondozine@gmai.com

or mail to:

1513 Salsbury Drive
Vancouver B.C
v5L 4b7

Submission deadline July 15th 2014

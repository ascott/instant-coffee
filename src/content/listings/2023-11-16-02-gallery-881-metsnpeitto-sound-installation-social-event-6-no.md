Gallery 881, a lens-based contemporary art gallery, welcomes metsnpeitto Sound
Installation & Social Event. The event is free and open to the public.

metsnpeitto Sound Installation & Social Event
Richelle Greabeiel and de_vinchee
November 17, 2023 from 6 - 9 pm

Gallery 881 opens Friday, November 17th 6-9 pm. Experience our current metsnpeitto
exhibition as a social event set to this collaborative sound installation created by artist
richelle greabeiel and sound designer
de_vinchee.

Artist Bios:

De_vinchee is a sound designer based in Vancouver, BC whose primary objective is to
explore singular and otherworldly soundscapes and challenge preconceived notions of
what music can be. By utilizing noise and nontraditional musical arrangements, and
various programs. The group creates a sonic atmosphere that acts as a conduit
between the conscious and unconscious mind. De_vinchee takes inspiration from
experimental music genres such as ambient, drone, and noise, with an aim to expand
the definition of music and investigate uncharted sonic landscapes. In essence,
De_vinchee strives to challenge conventional music boundaries, seeking to create
something truly original.

Richelle Greabeiel (b. Alberta, lives and works in Vancouver, BC; the unceded
territories of the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Wautuh peoples), is a
multidisciplinary artist. Since 2019, Richelle keeps an art practice focused on
investigating the production of multi-sensory experiences through
conceptual and
studio-based inquiry. Her works are inspired by materiality and its connections to the
natural environment and often result in site-responsive installations.Richelles work
focuses on the reciprocal relationship between the self and its environment. Themes of
identity, heritage, and the politics of (re)presentation of the self are also prevalent in her
practice via research and art-based experimentation. In her works, Richelle utilizes
photography, video, performance, sculpture, installation & sound.

ONLINE SCREENING
Video Out New Acquisitions: Tricky Eyes
Thursday November 5, 8pm PT

https://www.vivomediaarts.com/programming/tricky-eyes-new-acquisitions-screening

FREE
For our latest call-for-submissions, Video Out, in partnership with IFF Animation,
invited works that address and play with the conventions of animation. The result is a
wonderfully diverse program of 7 shorts by artists working across a wide range of both
analogue and digital techniques.

This program is 40 minutes, with an introduction by Video Out and IFF Animation.
Tasha Brotherton & Lindsay Lorraine - Tell The Dogs, 3min, 2020, Canada.
Tigris Sakda - C Pas ton Panda, 5min, 2018, Canada.
Shon Kim - Bookanima: Martial Arts, 12min, 2019, South Korea/USA.
Kunsang Kyirong - Yarlung, 5min, 2020, Canada.
Julianne Chapple - Escape, 4min, 2020, Canada.
Matthew Williamson - Traitor, 4min. 2020, Canada.
Phoebe Parsons - Terror Fervor, 7min, 2019, Canada.
This event was programmed and introduced on the Unceded Coast
Salish Territories of
the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish) and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh
(Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

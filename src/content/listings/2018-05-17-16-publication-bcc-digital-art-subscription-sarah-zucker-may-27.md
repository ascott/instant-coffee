Sarah Zucker is a writer and artist based in Los Angeles. Her work combines
obsolete and cutting edge technologies with performance to yield visions of
a neon future that never was. She produces curatorial + consultancy
projects as The Current Sea, showcasing art + media that tugs at the veil
of reality, and/or uses grotesqueness as a counterpoint to the sterility of
online experience. She runs YoMeryl, an art + animation studio, with
partner Bronwyn Lundberg. She writes TV and feature scripts with a focus on
female-driven/queer narratives, and camp sensibilities. Her one-hour pilot,
The Sun Set, was a finalist for the 2017 Women in Film / The Black List
Episodic Lab. She has written several short-form comedy series for Super
Deluxe.

She became a Jeopardy! Champion on September 27th, 2013.

Clients and Publications include: Super Deluxe, The City of West Hollywood,
The Brooklyn Museum, MTV, Universal Music Group, Tumblr Creatrs, Toyota,
Ovo Sound, Yahoo!, The Center for Disease Control, Electric Objects, Vice
Creators, New York Magazine, NPR Music, Stereogum, Installation Magazine,
LAist, AdHoc and more. She co-produced Prism Pipe, a monthly visual music
event at pehrspace in Los Angeles from 2014-2016.

She holds a BA in Theater/Creative Writing for the Media from Northwestern,
and an MFA in Dramatic Writing from NYU. http://www.sarahzucker.com/

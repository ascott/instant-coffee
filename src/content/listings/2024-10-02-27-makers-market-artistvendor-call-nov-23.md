Do you make, bake or create something youd like to share with the world? Or at
least with the folx in the West End? Join us at St. Andrews Wesley United Church for
our first Makers Market. This beautiful venue in the heart of the West End is opening
its doors to support local and palestinian makers - Just in time for the Holiday gift
giving season.

Market will be on Saturday November 23, 10am-4pm in the Sanctuary of the Church

Table fee is $30.

Set up will begin at 9am. You must be packed up by 5pm.

Tables and 2 chairs provided. A limited number of tables have power available upon
prior request. There is limited parking in the neighborhood - but there is paid
underground parking at the church.

Please email stawmarket@gmail.com by October 15th with a short description of
what you intend to sell, and include a photograph of an example of your work. If you
are intending to sell imported or re-sale of items made
by others please include that
information in your email, and tell us about your relationship to the maker/s.

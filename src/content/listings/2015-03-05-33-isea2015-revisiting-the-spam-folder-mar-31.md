THE MAINSTREETERS
From 1977 to 1985, the Mainstreeters’ Dragballs began as intimate studio
events, developing into elaborate art parties with spectacular décor,
performances, and DJs. Shaping an important chapter in Vancouver’s history,
the Mainstreeters were an “art gang” of East Van rebels who were fearlessly
open about their work, sexualities, and lifestyles, helping to build a
Warhol Factory-like scene that still resonates today. This month’s dragball
will be a showstopping night of old and new-school gender-bending drag
queens, kings, and everything in between. There will be special
performances, homages, and awards for Best in Drag—the perfect opportunity
to finally express your “other” self and take it to the next level
takingadvantage.ca

Thursday, March 24
Lulu Series: Art in the City
Brian Wakelin
Public Works

Despite Greater Vancouver's wide recognition for urban design, we
lack significant spaces for public gathering. In this context, Brian
Wakelin, of the celebrated Vancouver-based design firm, PUBLIC
(recent recipient of the Professional Prix de Rome in Architecture) has
focused almost exclusively on designing temporary pavilions, small
public buildings and urban infrastructure. Rather than monumental
public spaces, these structures are fragments of an incomplete and
evolving puzzle that make up the city. This talk will be preceded by a
short performance by performing artist Sam Herle.

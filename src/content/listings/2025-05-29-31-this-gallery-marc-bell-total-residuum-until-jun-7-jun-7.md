THIS Gallery Presents Total Residuum, a solo exhibition of works on paper by Marc
Bell.

THIS Gallery is thrilled to present an exhibition of works by Marc Bell, Total
Residuum, runs from May 23rd to June 7th, 2025. For this solo exhibition, Bell has
created over twenty new works that vary in medium and dimensions, from several
tiny 4 x 3 mixed media collages to a large, detailed 30 x 40 ink and watercolour
drawing. These works contain a fully formed visual world of abstracted characters,
landscapes, and wordplay that imply narrative as quickly as they distract from it.

To create this exhibition, Bell worked with many small scraps of paper and loose
drawings from his collections and distilled them into compositions, adding to and
negating from these fragments in a process he likes to call The War With Paper.

Total Residuum, runs from May 23rd through June 7th, 2025, and will be the final
exhibition in the gallerys Chinatown location!

The gallery is open from noon to 5 pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

Marc Bell is the author of Raw Sewage Science Fiction, Worn Tuff Elbow, Stroppy,
Hot Potatoe [sic], Pure Pajamas, Shrimpy and Paul and Friends. He has also been
active in self-publishing and the small press for decades, producing countless other
limited edition booklets. His comics have appeared in Kramers Ergot, The Ganzfeld,
New York Times Magazine, The LA Weekly and many Canadian monthlies and
weeklies.

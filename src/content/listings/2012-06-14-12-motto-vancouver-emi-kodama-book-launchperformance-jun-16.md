Emi Kodama: Book Launch/Performance

June 16
12 to 6pm

Motto Vancouver is pleased to present a launch for Emi Kodama's new 
book, If I Were You, published by MER. Paper Kunsthalle. Join the 
author throughout the day for an interactive performance as she invites 
participants to draw, with eyes closed, as she reads stories from the 
book.

Anna is on her way to see friends when she stops at some graffiti of 
two dogs mating and draws a house for them. So begins this deft 
collection of short stories and drawings that follow Anna through the 
moments that make up a life. She is constantly looking, and what she 
sees delights, even when she sees wrongly or mistakenly. The stories 
live as much through images as through narrative. Dreams, memory, 
and imagination blur the boundary between the visible and the hidden 
in this wise and beautiful book.

Emi Kodama (Vancouver, BC, Canada) is currently living and working 
in Ghent, Belgium. She is a laureate of the Higher Institute of Fine Art 
(HISK), a post-graduate program in Ghent and completed her Master 
of Fine Art at the Frank Mohr Institute in Groningen, The Netherlands. 
Emi has exhibited in Canada, Belgium, The Netherlands, Germany, 
Turkey, and China.

If I Were You was designed by Studio Luc Derycke in conversation with 
Emi Kodama, and includes an afterword by Oscar van den Boogaard.

The On Edge Reading Series presents:

Oana Avasilichioaei
7 pm, Thursday, March 17
in the ECUAD Library

The public is welcome.

Oana Avasilichioaei explores the infinite social, political, intimate
possibilities of language through poetry, translation and sound work. She
has published five poetry collections (including We, Beasts, 2012, winner
of the A. M. Klein Prize for Poetry, and feria: a poempark, Wolsak and Wynn
2008) and six translations of poetry and prose from French and Romanian.
Her newest poetry collection is Limbinal, a hybrid, multi-genre work on
notions of borders that includes translations of Paul Celan's Romanian
poems (Spring 2015) and her most recent soundwork is THRESHOLDS (2015). She
lives in Montreal (www.oanalab.com).

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the Arts
and the Coast Salish peoples whose traditional unceded territories we are on.

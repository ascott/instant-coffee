"Around us the illusion of infinite space or of no space, ourselves and the obscure shore
which it seems we could touch, the water between an absence.
" Margaret Atwood

These paintings full of washes and layers and textures respond to the tenderness and
fragility of the natural world. Affected by the way my children create, my painting process
has become a kind of playful improvisation, always moving towards the feeling of
immersion, of what it can mean to let the landscape hold us.

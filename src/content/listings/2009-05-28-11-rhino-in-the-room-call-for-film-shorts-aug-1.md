"The purple rhino made its first appearance in December 1974. It was created by two Boston gay 
rights activists: one source names Bernie Toal and Tom Morganti, another says it was Bernie Toal 
and Daniel Thaxton. The entire campaign was intended to bring gay issues further into public 
view. The rhino started being displayed in subways in Boston, but since the creators didn't qualify 
for a public service advertising rate, the campaign soon became too expensive for the activists to 
handle. The ads disappeared, and the rhino never caught on anywhere else.

As Toal put it, "The rhino is a much maligned and misunderstood animal and, in actuality, a 
gentle creature." But when a rhinoceros is angered, it fights ferociously. At the time, this seemed 
a fitting symbol for the gay rights movement.

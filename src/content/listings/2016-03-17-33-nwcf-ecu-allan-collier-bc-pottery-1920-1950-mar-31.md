Talk by Allan Collier: BC Pottery 1920 - 1950
Thursday, March 31, 7:30 pm
Room 245, North Building
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
1399 Johnston Street, Granville Island

Free admission. Organized by the North-West Ceramics Foundation.
www.nwcf.ca

Join us for a talk by Allan Collier who will discuss his latest research on
British Columbia pottery from the 1920s to the late 1940s. Collier will
contextualize the work of individuals and commercial potteries in the lower
mainland including Vancouver's Royal Ariston, BC Ceramics, and Crown
Ceramics; New Westminster's Ravine Pottery, and the Summerland Arts League.

Allan Collier is a furniture designer-maker, teacher, collector and curator
based in Victoria, BC. He has curated, written about and lent objects from
his collection to exhibitions across the country. The project, "The Modern
Eye: Craft and Design in Canada, 1940-1980," was exhibited at the Art
Gallery of Greater Victoria in 2011. Featuring over 40 pieces of furniture,
100 examples of ceramics, plus printed fabrics, tapestries, appliances and
lighting, this exhibition and catalogue highlighted the range of modernist
expression in Canadian craft and design during this time.

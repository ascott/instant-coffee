"This festival supports independent, boundary pushing filmmaking and media art by bringing 
artists together, showing diverse work, and holding space for an exploration of short film and 
video art as an artistic and multi-dimensional medium.

Were a group of volunteer artists and film makers looking to bring together more public displays 
and engagements of film and media art in an exhibition of narrative, documentary and artistic 
video work. Exhibited on the lands of the Lekwungen speaking peoples of the Songhees and 
Esquimalt First Nations, the festival aims to celebrate a growing community of film and video 
artists through featuring pieces of work from the island and from abroad.

The festival takes place in the first week of September 2019 and Submissions close July 15th! 
Accessibility and equity are so good, let us know what you need in that regard at 
victoriashortfilmfest@gmail.com. 

Submit on Filmfreeway here: https://filmfreeway.com/VictoriaShortFilmFestival

Opens Friday September 29 at 6:30

Arnica Artist-Run Centre is pleased to present the exhibitions: Futur Simple by Aime 
Henny Brown and Melodies for Solo Voices by Roberto Santaguida. Common to these 
exhibitions are complex acts of fragmentation and reorganization. Futur Simple and 
Melodies for Solo Voices will be at Arnica Artist-Run Centre from September 29th - 
November 4.

Artist Talk with Aime Henny Brown: September 28 at 1:30 at the Thompson Rivers 
University lighting studio

Personal Documentary Film Workshop with Roberto Santaguida: September 30 + 
October 1

Opening: Saturday, June 25th @ 2pm - 5pm
June 25 - July 10
New Show/Work/People features members of CAKE, a group of 
emerging artists who believe in gathering to share food as a means 
of building community and starting conversations. 
Highlighted here is a selection of each artist's recent work, in 
consideration for the gallery space; a sample of the contemporary 
artistic research and materials coming from Vancouver. 
Participating artists: 
Shannon Deer
Vanessa Grondin
Josephine Lee
Nick Loewen
Chris Eugene Mills
Patrick O'Neill
Ridia Onzo
Tung Yi
Sherman Yik

Curated by:
Zoya Mirzaghitova

Viridian Gallery, 1570 Coal Harbour Quay, info@viridiangallery.ca

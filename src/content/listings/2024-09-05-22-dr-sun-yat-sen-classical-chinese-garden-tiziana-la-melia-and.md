PERFORMANCE:
Tiziana La Melia + Ellis Sam at the Mid-Autumn Moon Festival

'to carve without cutting' duo Tiziana La Melia and Ellis Sam will be performing a
special set during the Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden's 38th Annual Mid-
Autumn Moon Festival!

Expect romantic, ethereal poetics and soundscapes make specially to suit the
festival theme of a Celestial Dance of Yin and Yang.

Book Launch of The Jade Pond haiku anthology
Saturday, January 20, 2-4pm

Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical Chinese Garden
In The Hall of One Hundred Rivers
578 Carrall St., Vancouver

You are invited to the launch of The Jade Pond, edited by Angela Naccarato and
Jacqueline Pearce, a collection of haiku inspired by the Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Classical
Chinese Garden. There will be readings by the Vancouver Haiku Group contributing
poets, and erhu music by Nicole Ge Li. Free admission; entrance through the doors on
Carrall St. This will be interesting and fun for all ages, for garden enthusiasts and all
who like haiku.

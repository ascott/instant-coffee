Three Part Valentine, with Kristin Bjornerud, Amanda Church and Katie
Pretti opens on Thursday, Feb. 7th from 6pm to 9pm at the Bjornson
Kajiwara Gallery.
Three seemingly disparate views of humankind's relation with nature,
from the anthropomorphizing of fauna to the sexual dynamics of
personal relationships.  Exhibition continues to March 14th.
Bjornson Kajiwara Gallery, 1727 W. Third Avenue, Vancouver, BC V6J
1K7. www.bjornsonkajiwara.ca, ph: 604. 738.3500.

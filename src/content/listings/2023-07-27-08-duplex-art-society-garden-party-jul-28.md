GARDEN PARTY

~ a performance ~

//composed by and starring Jules D. Mills and Sierra Megas as twinflower //

Fri, Jul 28 at 7PM

Sat, Jul 29 at 3PM

Hosted by: Duplex Art Society

1889 Franklin St - enter from the alley at Victoria Dr

Admission: FREE

Cash Bar (w drinks and treats!)

GARDEN PARTY is the culmination of a winter, spring and summer's worth of
collaborative movement, making and contemplating neighbourhood petals, blossoming
fruit trees, and roadside weeds. Twirling through sun-cycles, slowing to plant-time and
inhabiting internal
landscapes, two twinflowers dance into the late summer heat.
Rooting, wilting. Composing, decomposing. A whimsical and tender celebration of
small wonders.

Bring a flower or wear a floral!

Venue is wheelchair accessible via a short ramp and the audience will be outside in a
shaded area. Performance is ~15 mins.

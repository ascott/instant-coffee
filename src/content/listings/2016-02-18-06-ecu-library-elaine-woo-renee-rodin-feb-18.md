The On Edge Reading Series presents:

Elaine Woo + Renee Rodin
7 pm, Thursday, Feb 18
in the ECUAD Library

Elaine Woo is a poet, librettist, and non-fiction writer. She is the author 
of Cycling with the Dragon, Nightwood Editions 2014. She is a 
graduate of the University of British Columbia's creative writing 
program. She is a contributor to Shy: An Anthology which won a silver 
medal IPPY (independent Book Publishers) award in 2014. Her art 
song collaboration with composer Daniel Marshall, Night-time 
Symphony, won a Boston Metro Opera festival prize in 2013. Elaine 
also contributed to V6A: Writing from Vancouver's Downtown Eastside, 
a finalist for the City of Vancouver's Book Award 2012. When not 
writing, Elaine is often found in front of the stove, wielding a spatula, 
cooking for friends in need.
Renee Rodin is a Vancouver writer, artist and cultural worker whose 
reality-based prose centres on community in contemporary society. Her 
books include Bread and Salt (prose poems, Talonbooks, 1996), Ready 
for Freddy (Nomados) and Subject to Change (literary non-fiction, 
Talonbooks, 2010). She is currently working on a new manuscript.

The public is welcome.

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the

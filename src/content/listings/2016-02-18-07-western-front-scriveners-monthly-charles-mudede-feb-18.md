Thursday, February 18 @ 7:00pm
Grand Luxe Hall, Western Front
Free Admission

Scrivener's Monthly is pleased to present an evening with Seattle-
based writer, critic and filmmaker Charles Mudede. Part lecture, part 
filmic journey, Mudede will take us on a narrated tour through poetry, 
politics and the environment to examine how questions of morality 
continue to riddle the human condition.

Charles Mudede's films include Police Beat, North American, and Zoo. 
His writing on politics, arts and culture has appeared in The New York 
Times, The Village Voice, LA Weekly, and elsewhere. He is currently 
the Film Editor at the Seattle-based weekly, The Stranger.

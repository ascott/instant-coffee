JUNE 16, 2013 | 12-6 PM | SPECIAL EVENT OPEN HOUSE Western Front 40th
Anniversary Celebration
Sunday June 16, 2013 @ 12-6 pm

The Western Front is 40! Join us on June 16 for a day of anniversary
celebrations.

League, a project by Germaine Koh, with Cedric Bomford, Ian Verchere, and
Bruce Emmett, will take place in and around the building and artist
collective Instant Coffee will be serving up snow cones. Scrivener's
Monthly, a 'periodical that talks', will be presented inside Tent Shop. Our
interactive music installation Sonic Playground will be on site with new
projects by Publik Secrets and Leslie Ross with Square One and there will
be a performance by Voice Over Mind Choir with Scant Intone. Fancy Dress
Party, a celebratory screening of works from the Archive and beyond, evokes
the history of costume at Western Front with works by Kate Craig, Spartacus
Chetwynd, General Idea and many others.

Come by for BBQ! Balloons! Music! Cake! Hat making with Collage Collage!
And much more!

FOR FURTHER DETAILS please contact Kristin Lim on 604-876-9343 /
development@front.bc.ca or visit www.front.bc.ca.

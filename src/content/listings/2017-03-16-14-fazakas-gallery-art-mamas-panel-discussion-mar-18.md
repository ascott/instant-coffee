Saturday, March 18th, 2 - 4 p.m.
Art Mamas Panel Discussion

Featuring: Gabriella Aceves-Sepulveda, Matilda Aslizadeh, Sarah Shamash, Heather 
Passmore, Maria Anna Parolin, Natasha McHardy, Damla Tamer, and prOphecy sun. 

This group of Vancouver-based artist mothers will be presenting a unique collective 
and heterogeneous artist talk on motherhood and art practice and the intersections 
between reproductive and artistic labour. The panel discussion, in the form of an 
informal dialogue, will elaborate a utopian model for a feminist, women-centred, 
sustainable creation process that integrates life and all of its chaos into a viable and 
valued way of being and creating without being marginalized by and excluded from 
the male-dominated art system.

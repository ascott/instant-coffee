Call for Submissions Monobande III

Les Territoires is a non-profit art gallery dedicated to exhibiting emerging 
artists and curators. Exhibitions, projects and events at Les Territoires reflect 
a multi-disciplinary approach to the dissemination of art practices and will act 
as a place where exchanges and contacts can be made.

The objective of Monobandes’ third edition is to draw a brief panorama of 
emerging video creation.

Monobandes will stand from Thursday, November 7th to Saturday, December 
14th, 2013. 
The deadline for application is Thursday, October 10th, 2013 included.

The Jury selection criteria’s are the originality, the quality and the coherence 
of the artistic vision. There is no imposed theme. The only restrain is to keep 
the length of each video to 10 minutes or less. Several videos can be 
submitted, under the limit of 4 per artist.

Your project proposal should include: 

• A short resume, a biography and your contact information
• A DVD with your video(s) in QuickTime
• A presentation text of maximum 500 words, the title of your video(s) and 
its/their length
• A self-addressed stamped envelope if you wish your documents to be 
returned

The participation fee is $70.00 CAD for each video. It can be paid from Paypal 
to Paypal (info@lesterritoires.org) or with a Canadian check.

If an artwork is sold, the artist retains 100% of the sale.

Reminder:
• Only the submissions fulfilling all of the conditions listed above will be 
considered.
• Make sure your payment is included (check addressed to Galerie Les 
Territoires or Paypal payment receipt) 

Submissions should be mailed to:
Les Territoires / Monobandes’ submissions
372 Sainte-Catherine st. West, suite 323
Montreal (Quebec) H3B 1A2, Canada 
Or e-mailed to: info@lesterritoires.org

Deadline: Thursday, October 10th, 2013 included.

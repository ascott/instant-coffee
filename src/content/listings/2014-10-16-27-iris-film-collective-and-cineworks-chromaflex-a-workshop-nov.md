Iris Film Collective in partnership with Cineworks presents 

CHROMAFLEX, a workshop with experimental/materialist filmmaker 
Richard Tuohy in person from Australia. 

A stunning new film processing technique for achieving colour, black and 
white, negative and positive all on the same 16mm or 35mm film stock. 

All materials supplied with full darkroom access. Show up with your own 
film to process or use the film supplied (see details). No experience 
necessary.

Limited space, advance sign up required. 

Dates: Saturday/Sunday Nov 15/16, 2014

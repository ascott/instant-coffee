Cineworks requests the pleasure of your company at The 4th Annual Oscar Party 
Fundraiser

Sunday 26 February 20174:00pm-late
The Imperial, 319 Main Street, Vancouver

General Admission: $80 + service fee
Member admission: $50 + service fee 
Tickets: [http://cineworks2017.brownpapertickets.com/](http://cineworks2017.brownpapertickets.com/)

Support Vancouver's Independent Filmmakers Society, est.1980, and enjoy a night of 
Silver Screen glamour, live entertainment, gourmet refreshments and a microcinema 
featuring a fine selection of members' short films. In addition to red carpet photography, 
the event will include shooting guests on 16mm film, to be processed and projected on 
the night.

Your attendance will help us support Canadian independent filmmakers and media 
artists, near and far. Come indulge and enjoy!

* Book Launch of Mostly True by Bill Daniel
Friday September 5 2008 5-6pm By Donation
Spartacus Books 684 E. Hastings St. www.spartacusbooks.org

A rollicking rail zine of freight riding stories, interviews with hoboes
and boxcar artists, historical oddities and tons of photos of modern day
boxcar tags- known as monikers- the result of a 25year obsession with hobo
and railworker folklore presented in the guise of a vintage railroad fanzine.

* Sunset Scavenger
Friday September 5 2008 8pm Donation $5-10
Maclean Park - 700 block E.Georgia
www.cinematheque.bc.ca

Sunset Scavenger is an outdoor video installation projected on the sails of
the Sailvan, a 1984 Ford veggie oil-converted diesel van with a 2-masted
gaff-rigged. Based on ideas concerning the end of the oil age, It is a
collage essay on ecological catastrophe, sustainability, improvisation and
self-reliance, featuring hippie houseboaters, punk back-to-the-landers,
rubber tramps, off-the-gridders, and desert rats.

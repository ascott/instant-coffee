Based in London, UK, David Campany is one of the most acclaimed photography 
experts of our time whose fresh perspectives on the history of the medium have had a 
profound impact on art and scholarship. He has published widely and curated a number 
of critical exhibitions. For his writing, he has received the ICP Infinity Award, the 
Kraszna-Krausz Book Award, the Alice Award, a Deutscher Fotobuchpreis, and the 
Royal Photographic Societys award for writing. Campany is co-founder and co-editor of 
PA Magazine, which has been published since 2008. He teaches photographic theory 
and practice at the University of Westminster.

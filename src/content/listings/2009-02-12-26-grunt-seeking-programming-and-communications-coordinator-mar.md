Programming and Communications Coordinator

grunt gallery

grunt gallery is a non-profit artist-run centre formed in 1984 in
Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. We are committed to providing a forum
for a wide spectrum of critical discourse and multidisciplinary art
practices. Our mission is to engage communities in creating environments
that support collaboration, innovation and challenge contemporary art in
Canada.

We are seeking a highly motivated, organized and creative individual to
fill the part-time position of Programming and Communications Coordinator,
to commence March 15, 2009.
Reporting directly to the Operations Director, the Programming and
Communications Coordinator is one of three permanent staff positions with
whom they work in partnership to facilitate the day-to-day delivery of
programming and communications. The position works directly with grunt's
program artists as well as with our audiences and supporters by managing
the organization's external communications.

The 20-hour/week position includes the following duties:

1. Providing support and assistance with all ongoing gallery projects &
events as required including managing all aspects related to the successful
delivery of exhibitions, performance and artist talks/workshop programs
(confirmation of program & artists; artist contracts and correspondence;
artist travel; transport, shipping and storage arrangements; support for
exhibition/performance requirements; assisting with arrangement of
necessary materials, tools, equipment, rentals, signage and installation).

2. Managing all grunt marketing & communications, including preparation,
coordination and distribution of various program marketing materials (press
releases, membership mailing, email, e-newsletters, event listing to media,
and bi-annual brunt magazine). Included is creating content for our website
and social network sites.

We are looking for an individual with good people skills, able to negotiate
delicate and competing interests, has experience working within a budget
and can offer creative and innovative solutions to challenges. This
individual should work well in an artist’s environment and be able to
coordinating a dynamic group of contractors to deliver and promote our
programming. Ideally the candidate should enjoy working as part of a team,
but be able to initiate and complete projects independently as required.
Applicants will be required to demonstrate 3-5 years of experience in
successful project coordination including working in the non-profit sector
(preferably in the arts), as well as excellent communication skills (both
written and oral).

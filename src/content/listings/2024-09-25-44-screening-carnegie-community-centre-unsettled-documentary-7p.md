Theatre Terrific & Lantern Films Presents:
UNSETTLED
The Documentary
Premiere November 7th 2024, then available online

Unsettled shares the story of a group of artists who came together over several
years to create a theatrical piece around the general theme of "disability." The film
shares how this process unfolded, and what it meant to the artists. Artists include
Stephen Lytton, Nlakapamux poet, writer and actor living with cerebral palsy,
residential school survivor, 2016 Governor Generals Caring Canadian Award
recipient, and
president of the British Columbia Aboriginal Network on Disability
Society; Sandra Pronteau, artist and community worker born in The Pas, Manitoba
who grew up in foster care in Winnipeg as part of the 60s Scoop, born partially
hearing impaired/Scoliosis and with some limited mobility issues; Caroline Hebert,
Deaf actor, fluent in American Sign Language, Language des signes Qubcoise,
and written French and English; and Julia Siedlanowska, former theatre practitioner
and current Registered Clinical Counsellor. The film also honours Kat Zucomulwat
Norris, who passed before the film could be completed. Kat was Coast Salish from
the Lyackson First Nation and a significant community figure, cultural educator,
artist, and activist. She served as artist and elder advisor throughout the creation
process of Unsettled.

There will be a talk-back with the artists after the documentary screening. After the
premiere of Unsettled, the documentary will be available on Theatre Terrifics
website.
Accompanying the documentary is an educational booklet that will be
distributed in BC schools. This booklet is created as an educational resource,
expanding on the themes of Unsettled, including equity, living with disabilities,
cultural practices and traditions, storytelling, and self-care.

Since 1985, Theatre Terrific has been part of the fabric of Vancouver theatre,
pioneering inclusive opportunities for artists of all abilities to develop performance
skills and collaborate in the production of theatrical works, challenging audiences to
be open to the impact of thought provoking art. Lantern Films was founded in 2015
as a company committed to making critically important and visually stunning
documentaries. Their films are driven by those who have lived the story, prioritizing
community consent and knowledge ownership, centering careful research and
transparency. Unsettled is presented as part of the Downtown Eastside Heart of the
City Festival, which, for twenty-one years has been
grounded in the Downtown
Eastside, and focused on listening and learning from the community's cultural
practices, working with, for and about the community to carry forward stories,
ancestral memory, cultural traditions, lived experiences, and artistic processes to
illuminate pathways of resistance and resilience.

Artists featured: Stephen Lytton, Caroline Hebert, Sandra Pronteau, Julia
Siedlanowska; Production Managers Natalie Jean Davidson, Julia Siedlanowska;
Dramaturgy and Interview questions Susanna Uchatius; ASL interpretation Gillian
Pettit, Samara Ferguson; Produced by Theatre Terrific and Lantern Films; Editing
and Cinematography Dave Rodden-Shortt

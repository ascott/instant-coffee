The Crit Show is accepting submissions for its next show on December 9th. 

The Crit Show is a venue for emerging artists in the Vancouver region to
show, talk about and get feed back on their art. It is the aim of The Crit
Show to give artists a place to try out new ideas and concepts where they
can get some honest constructive, supportive and critical feedback on the work.
The Crit Show opens with a reception at 8 and artist talk at 9, leading
into a discussion of the work.
Artists are encouraged to show experimental new work.

see www.thecritshow.com for more information

Application deadline is November 18th.

Arts Alive 2018
April 20-June 3, 2018 | Lower Gallery
Opening Reception: Thursday, April 19, 7-9pm
*In collaboration with School District 41

Arts Alive 2018 is an annual showcase of student works held in collaboration with 
the Burnaby School District. Highlighting works by secondary students, this years 
36th edition presents a selection of artworks from eight schools across the city. 
Please visit burnabyartgallery.ca for more information

David Ostrem: Why Wont You Be Art? 
April 20-June 3, 2018 | Upper Gallery
Opening Reception: Thursday, April 19, 7-9pm

*In collaboration with Capture Photography Festival

Why Wont You Be Art? features 50 recent photo-collage based works by Vancouver 
artist David Ostrem who incorporates art history, literature and pop culture into 
incisive statements about artistic struggle, emotion and meaning. Please visit 
burnabyartgallery.ca for more information.

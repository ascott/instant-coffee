Contemporary Art Gallery presents two exhibitions:

On Time
Elizabeth Zvonar
November 13, 2009 to January 10, 2010
Opening Reception: Thursday, November 12, 6-9pm

Vancouver artist Elizabeth Zvonar is currently interested in the
connections between Cubism, representations of the 4th Dimension and rubber
bands as metaphors for time.
'I find that at the end of a journey, which of course is neverending; that
I have found things out', This quote by British playwright Harold Pinter,
is an apt description for On Time Zvonar's exhibition at the CAG, which
will include an array of sculptures and collage that act as possible
portals into implausible places.

Artist Talk: Tuesday, November 17, 7:00 p.m. at Emily Carr University, Rm. 245.

Face Up
Lee Lozano, Bruce Nauman and Sarah Lucas

Repetition, exaggeration and the process of making are the common threads
that tie the works together in Face Up an exhibition co-curated by CAG
curator Jenifer Papararo and artist Elizabeth Zvonar.

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, Vancouver, BC, V6B 6R5, Canada

Gallery Hours: Wed. - Sun. 12 - 6 pm. Admission is free by donation. For
more information

Republic Gallery is pleased to announce the upcoming solo exhibition:

Samuel Roy-Bois | Candid

Opening reception: Friday, April 9th, 6 - 8 pm

Showing: April 9th - May 8th, 2010

Samuel Roy-Bois' Candid is an exciting "retour aux sources" for the artist.
Roy-Bois' first personal exhibition, presented at his father's gallery in
1994, was exclusively made of paintings. For the past decade the artist has
dedicated himself to a critique of the built environment and architecture,
with the production of large-scale installations that address antagonistic
concepts of everyday life and utopia. This new arm of Roy-Bois' production
nonetheless confirms an unfailing interest in the mundane and the
economical, as much as it exemplifies his wish to create work informed by
an ever-refreshed visual vocabulary.

Candid will be on show April 9th through May 8th with an artist reception
on Friday, April 9th, 6-8pm. Regular gallery hours are 11am-5pm, Wednesday
through Saturday, and by appointment. Candid is Roy-Bois' second solo
exhibition with Republic Gallery.

CALL FOR ENTRIES

Harvestworks Digital Media Center &#9553; Video Art Festival #003 

Founded by artists in 1977, Harvestworks Digital Media Center (HDMC) has helped a generation of artists create new works using technology. HDMC invites artists to submit videos of all types (experimental, animation, music video, documentary, silent, short, etc) for a guest curated video art festival. Works selected from this call, as well as by private invitation, will be featured in a series of themed screenings in New York City, September 2008.

POSTMARK DEADLINE: June 16, 2008

Details and entry form available at [http://www.harvestworks.org](http://www.harvestworks.org)
Contact: videoscreening003@harvestworks.org

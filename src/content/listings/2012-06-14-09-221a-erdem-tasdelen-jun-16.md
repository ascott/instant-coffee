221A 

16 June 2012 at 2:00pm
Talk by Erdem Tasdelen

221A is pleased to host a talk by artist Erdem Tasdelen in conjunction
with What a Drag, a solo exhibition of new work by Tasdelen that
examines the role of language in the making of subjectivity. The
exhibition continues at 221A until July 22, 2012.

Erdem Taşdelen grew up in Switzerland, Germany and Turkey. 
He 
received
his BA in Visual Arts and Communication Design from Sabanci 
University
in Istanbul in 2007, and his MAA in Visual Arts from Emily Carr
University of Art + Design in Vancouver in 2010. He continues to live
in Vancouver and teaches at Emily Carr University of Art + Design. His
artistic practice involves an interrogation of subjectivity and its
manifestations, often resulting in works on paper, sculpture and time
based media.

221A is a non-profit organization and Canadian registered charity
funded in part by the generous support of government grants, 
members

contemporary art and design practices. What a Drag is made possible

Redaction
Annual Aboriginal Student Art Exhibition
Concourse Gallery, Emily Carr University of Art & Design
March 18-25, 2014
Opening Reception, 6 pm, Tuesday, March 18, 2014
Curators: Lou-ann Ika'wega Neel and Richard Heikkila-Sawan

Redaction: 'to select or adapt (as by obscuring or removing sensitive 
information) for publication or release; to obscure or remove (text) from a 
document prior to publication or release'.

Following in the spirit of Truth and Reconciliation week held last September, 
Aboriginal students at Emily Carr University of Art & Design have selected 
'Redaction' as the theme for this year's Aboriginal Student Art Exhibition. 

We will explore the idea of redaction from two perspectives - first, the kinds 
of documents, information and practices that are redacted by governments, 
and what this ultimately means in terms of social justice, freedom of speech, 
and the right of Canadian citizens to have access to 'public records'; and 
second, ideas around how things might be different were they NOT redacted.

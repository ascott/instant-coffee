I am the guest curator for the Bookmark Project 2010: Amulet organized by
the Koffler Centre of the Arts, Toronto. The Bookmark Project is an
intervention of artist-designed bookmarks concurrent with the annual
Toronto Jewish Book Fair. Unique bookmarks and multiples created by
practicing artists and visual art students will be inserted clandestinely
into the thousands of books for sale at the 34th Annual Book Fair.

The deadline is October 4, 2010, and I was wondering if you could share

See Sounds Listening Series: Pietro Sammarco
Thursday May 4, 2017, 8pm
Hadden Park Field House, 1015 Maple St, Vancouver

This will be our sixteenth monthly listening party at the field house. Bring something you 
would like to listen to with a group. Discussion will follow. We will have most available 
formats (digital, vinyl, CD, tape, radio). 

Meet at the field house at 8pm. Our guest presentation will start at 8:30. There will be a 
short intermission and then the floor will be opened to other people who have brought 
sounds to share.

As part of our See Sounds series, we are featuring a guest artist each listening party to 
curate the early portion of the evening. We have invited Pietro Sammarco to be this 
month's guest.

suggested donation $5-10 All donations go to the guest artist.

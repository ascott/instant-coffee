Opening: Friday, January 19 at 7 pm
Dynamo Arts Association (DAA)
Suite 103 - 30 East 6th Ave

Mark Butler (Dublin, Ireland) presents new paintings, in which the floodlit landscape 
of nocturnal Suburbia is manifested as a series of human heads.

In this body of work, ubiquitous features of Suburbia such as commuter routes, 
concrete car parks and street lights, become a kind of fuel supply thats used to carry 
out experiments in picture making. As the idea of Suburbia begins to loosen and 
dissolve, unknown territory is traversed, and a series of head like structures emerge.

Butler is interested in exploring routine and repetition in everyday life, and both the
potential and absurdity that lie within. With that in mind, his work attempts to take the
most quotidian and humdrum of subject matter, and use it as a key to access a 
channel for invention and metamorphosis. It represents his search for the exotic in 
the embers of the absurd.

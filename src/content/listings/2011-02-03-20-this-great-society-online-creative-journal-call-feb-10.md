This Great Society is an online creative journal exhibiting visual,
time-based, and audio arts in all mediums.

We are currently accepting submissions in all mediums for our 17th issue.
Work considered will relate to the curatorial theme "SLEEP" (wide
interpretation is encouraged).

Send examples of your work (or a portfolio link) to
submit@thisgreatsociety.com before February 10.

Illustrating and Other Involvement:
The Society is always looking for quality illustrations to accompany the
written work we publish. If you are interested in providing illustrations
(static visual content in any media) please email submit@thisgreatsociety.com

NOW OPEN! Call for Applications for Level UP! 2025-2026!

Deadline: April 18, 2025

MOTHRA: Artist-Parent Project and Balancing Act are teaming up again to support
artist parents and caregivers!

Join the growing community of Level UP! Partners exploring the creation of policies
and practices to support parents and caregivers in the visual arts.

Open to organizations, collectives, artists, galleries, and project-based initiatives.

Apply with a pilot project today! Attend one of our Info Sessions to learn more.

Balancing Act is a national initiative to support artists and arts workers with
caregiving responsibilities, supported by Strategic Funds from Canada Council for
the Arts and Theatre Direct. Level UP! Sector Expansion is funded by Women and
Gender Equality Canada.

FAQs

Who can apply?

Artists, organizations, service organizations, companies, collectives, ad hoc
collectives, project-based initiatives, festivals, residency programs, and presenting
venues.

If you have a pilot project youd like to explore that supports artist and artsworker
parents and caregivers, wed love to hear from you!

If you have a question about the eligibility of your proposed pilot, please contact
Margaret Evans, Producer, at margaret [at] theatredirect [dot] ca

As a Level UP! Strategy Partner, what benefits would I receive?

Financial support of up to $3000 toward project/administrative costs of your pilot
Facilitated knowledge exchange with other members of the the cohort
Consultation as needed (HR arts professionals, elders, mentors)
One-on-one support from the Balancing Act team

What are the expectations of participating in Level UP! ?

Attend online meetings every 2 months (3 peer exchange meetings, 1 one-on-one
consultation)
Participate in 1 public-facing roundtable event and attend other Balancing Act events
(online and/or in-person)
Promote, communicate and champion the work being done to support caregivers in
the participants specific sector
Engage in an evaluation process with RTK Consulting (2-survey format)
Create and submit templates/resources to be shared through Balancing Acts
Resource Portal in the Spring of 2026

What can the financial support be used for?

The funds can be used toward the project costs, and/or to support the administrative
costs of executing the pilot project and participating in the program.

How long should the pilot project last?

Some projects span an entire season, such as policy creation. Others are
implemented for a specific project or production and might therefore have a specific
timeline. Whether the duration of the pilot is long or short, partners engage with
Level UP! for the duration of the program for the purposes of the learning exchange.

What types of pilot projects are welcome?

Past Level UP! projects have included child care stipends for artists and
artsworkers, compassion fund/wellness programs, touring strategies, on-site
childcare, flexible working models, caregiver policies, caregiver supportive
residencies, and resource guides.

And we are open to new ideas! If you have an idea for a pilot project that supports
care needs or creates greater access for parents and/or caregiver arts workers,let us
know! Perhaps you are already working on something that could be explored
further?

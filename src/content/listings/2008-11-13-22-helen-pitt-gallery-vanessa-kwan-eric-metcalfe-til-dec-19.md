Vanessa Kwan & Eric Metcalfe

The Set-Up

November 7 - December 19, 2008&#8232;

Closing reception, Friday December 12th, 2008 at 8:00 pm&#8232;Conversation
between artists and curators, Saturday December 13th at 3:00 pm

Guest curated by Alissa Firth-Eagland and Johan Lundh

The Helen Pitt Gallery is pleased to present a new and unprecedented work
created between artists Vanessa Kwan and Eric Metcalfe. Over the period of
five weeks, Kwan and Metcalfe will conceptualize and produce a mural work
in the gallery, culminating in a reception held December 12th, 2008. The
Set-Up will develop through and reflect upon dialogues involved in creative
exchange. Consequently, it will explore and examine the generosities and
risks involved in collaboration. The exhibition will be accompanied by a
publication available at the closing reception. 

Vanessa Kwan is a Vancouver-based artist and writer. Her solo work has been
exhibited at the Or Gallery, Access Gallery, The Richmond Art Gallery and
the Art Gallery of the South Okanagan, among others. She has published
reviews of art and culture in C Magazine, Fuse Magazine, Canadian Art
magazine, the Georgia Straight, and Pyramid Power. In addition to her solo
practice, she is a founding member of the artist group Norma whose
performance and installation works have been exhibited locally and
internationally since 2002.

Eric Metcalfe is a Vancouver-based artist. Toronto curator Peggy Gale
recently described Metcalfe’s practice as the epitomization of the
avant-garde in Canadian art. In 1973, he co-founded Western Front, a
pioneering artist-run centre in Vancouver. He has exhibited at the
Artspeak, Charles H. Scott Gallery, University of British Columbia, Art
Gallery of Greater Victoria, Vancouver Art Gallery, Whitney Museum of
American Art, among many others. In 2008, his practice was recognized with
a Governor General’s Award.

Alissa Firth-Eagland is a curator, writer and artist based in Vancouver.
She is currently the Director/Curator of Media Arts at the Western Front.

Johan Lundh is an independent curator and writer, dividing his time between
Vancouver and Stockholm, Sweden.

Jenipher Hur & Avery Nabata

FLAB

November 7 - December 19, 2008&#8232;

Closing reception, Friday December 12th, 2008 at 8:00 pm

Curated by Joshua Bartholomew and Robin Selk

Jenipher Hur and Avery Nabata's site-specific installation for the Helen
Pitt Galler Artist Run Centre comments on the production and utility that
the space of the gallery serves. They do this by extending the gallery into
storage space and storage space into the gallery. The space of the gallery
diminishes while the role of the utility closet becomes exaggerated. Their
project is an attempt at substantially questioning the composition of an
exhibition as well as the part-to-part construction of its content.

Avery Nabata: is a fourth-year GFA student at Emily Carr University.

Jenipher Hur: is a fourth-year GFA student at Emily Carr University.

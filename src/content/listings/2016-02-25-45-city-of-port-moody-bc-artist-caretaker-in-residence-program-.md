Artist Caretaker in Residence Program
City of Port Moody, BC
Residency Term: June 1, 2016 - October 31, 2017
Eligibility: This opportunity is open to all artists legally able to work
and live in Canada for the entire duration of the residency
Application Deadline: Monday, April 4, 2016 at 5pm

Port Moody is launching its first artist in residence program. This
opportunity provides an artist with a rent-free one bedroom apartment in
Rocky Point Park in exchange for artistic and caretaker services. This
program is open to all artists legally able to work and live in Canada for
the entire duration of the residency (June 1, 2016 to October 31, 2017) and
capable of performing all of the duties expected of the resident caretaker.
This program is open to artists working in any discipline that suits the
studio/residence space.

Complete details on the program, including the application process, can be
found on the City of Port Moody's website:
http://portmoody.ca/index.aspx?page=1362

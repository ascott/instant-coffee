Night School Speaker Series. Rhizome Cafe, Thursday, May 31st.
317 East Broadway
7-9pm

Night School is an evening of learning with its shirt unbuttoned and its 
tie undone, just kicking back and talking about interesting things. The 
mission of these events is to create an accessible atmosphere of 
mutual learning in order to create a culture of critical engagement 
within the city. 

It is a monthly night of interdisciplinary talks on a variety of topics 
ranging from science, humanities, art and much more. It is a forum for 
people working on a variety of projects to speak about what they are 
doing in an informal setting, to people outside of their field. Anyone is 
invited to give a talk. If you would like to speak get in touch with us at 
vancitynightschool@gmail.com or 
www.facebook.com/vancitynightschool.

Speakers include Ryan McCormick, on the process of creating a 
feasibility study for the Safe Amplification Site Society and new all ages 
venues; Jaimie Kendal-Ward on how sex trade workers in the 
Downtown Eastside are portrayed in mainstream media; and Ray Hsu 
on his new experimental poetry project, the Future is Laser Tag.

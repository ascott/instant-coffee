SKIN & BONE - Salty Sailor Tales offers a rare opportunity to see scrimshaw
not included in the current Tattoos & Scrimshaw exhibit and discuss the
idea of erotic imagery and taboo concepts in both art forms.

SPECIAL GUEST: SAILOR JERRY RUM

sailorjerry.com

About the Exhibit

Since opening in March, TATTOOS & SCRIMSHAW - the Art of the Sailor has
caught the attention of local and international media. Erotic scrimshaw,
taboo tattoos, and the history and influence of sailor art on western
culture have sparked conversations around superstition, tradition, and
"What constitutes as art"?

The exhibit explores the history and craftsmanship behind these two iconic
nautically influenced pastimes.

Reclaim + Repair: The Mahogany Project opens at the Museum of Vancouver on
July 20, 2023

Come celebrate the creativity and craft of Vancouvers design community.

The Museum of Vancouver (MOV) proudly presents Reclaim + Repair: The
Mahogany Project, a new feature exhibition opening to the public on July 20, 2023.

A diverse group of 31 emerging and seasoned local designers and makers were
selected to create 22 objects made from vintage mahogany provided by MOV. The
exhibition features a wide array of design objects from furniture, lighting and
household objects, to jewelry, and much more. This exhibition is curated by Propellor
Studio in collaboration with Museum of Vancouver.

The exhibition celebrates the creativity and craft of Vancouvers design community,
while engaging with questions central to the role of design in advancing sustainability
as well as social and environmental justice. The idea for Reclaim + Repair: The
Mahogany Project was born out of a desire to honour this material and the places
from which it originated.

The mahogany used in this exhibition came from a local business that specialized in
designing and manufacturing boating equipment. It was harvested between the
1950s and 1970s in Guatemala and Nicaragua. When the factory closed, this
leftover wood went into storage for decades. Recently, the organization responsible
for the wood felt it was important to give it a second life and donated it to the MOV's
upcycling design program.

Works created by designers for the exhibition are for sale. A
significant portion of the
sales will be donated to support Indigenous-led reforestation efforts in Nicaragua
and Guatemala, where the mahogany was extracted in the last century.

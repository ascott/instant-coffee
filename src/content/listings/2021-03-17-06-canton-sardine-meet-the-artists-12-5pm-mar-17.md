Pierre Coupey + Dion Kliner: Imaginary Portraits

Please join artists Pierre Coupey and Dion Kliner in person to learn more about their
works in our current exhibition Imaginary Portraits. Curated by Lam Wong, Imaginary
Portraits explores the mysterious relationship between memory and creativity by reducing
the figurative subject to pure forms of the imaginary, the fictional. The exhibition features
oil stick drawings and abstract paintings by Pierre Coupey, and the pure white figurative
plaster sculptures of Dion Kliner.

restriction the gallery is limited to maximum 4 visitors at a
time. Please register your visit early.

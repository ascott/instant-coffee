Free film screening: No Men Beyond This Point 

SFU Woodward's Cultural Programs, Telefilm and VIFF host a free screening of NO 
MEN BEYOND THIS POINT, will be on November 17th at 1 pm in the Djavad 
Mowafaghian Cinema, Goldcorp Centre for the Arts, 149 W. Hastings Street, 
Admission is free and everyone is welcome to attend. No RSVP required. 

Film Synopsis:
In this mockumentary shot in Vancouver, writer-director Mark Sawers imagines a 
contemporary world in which men are going extinct and women rule, procreating by 
parthenogenesis and producing only female children. Sawers scores some clever 
points against both the matriarchy (women have achieved world peace but failed to 
invent the Internet) and the patriarchy (ineffectual male revolutionaries depend on 
the female state to cook their meals). Patrick Gilmore excels as the youngest man 
on Earth, a quiet, unassuming 37-year-old nanny/housekeeper named Andrew 
Myers who finds himself at the center of a battle to keep men from going extinct.

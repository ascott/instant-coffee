VANCOUVER ART GALLERY

You Are Here: Artists in the Gallery
Part 2: Pleasure and Doom: The West Coast Sublime
How Soon Is Now
Thursday, March 5, 7pm
In the Gallery

Panelists: Sonny Assu, Luanne Martineau, Marina Roy and Rhonda Weppler &
Trevor Mahovsky;

Moderator: Heidi Reitmaier

This series of evening talks in the Gallery will bring together artists to
explore contemporary art practices focusing on works within How Soon Is
Now. From architecture to social engagement to the nature of the
interdisciplinary act, issues pertinent to local and international artists
will be investigated.
Free with Gallery admission.

Out for Lunch Music Series
Friday, March 6, 12:10pm
Annex, 3rd floor

Iman Habibi, piano and composer
Music from Brahms, Beethoven, Villa-Lobos and Habibi
Free with Gallery admission. Arrive early-this popular event has limited space.

Multimedia Performance - Jackson 2bears: Iron Tomahawks
How Soon Is Now
Tuesday, March 10, 7pm
In the Gallery

Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby Street
Vancouver, BC V6Z 2H7

Iron Tomahawks is an ongoing series of multimedia performances informed by
electronic music and digital media. Working as a VJ, Jackson 2bears remixes
sound and video to critically reflect on issues of racism, colonialism,
discrimination, Indigenous subjectivity and Native stereotypes. In
conjunction with How Soon Is Now.
Free with Gallery admission.

In Conversation - Jin-me Yoon with Ian Thom: The Question of Place
Western Landscapes
Saturday, March 14, 2pm
In the Gallery

Artist Jin-me Yoon and Senior Curator Ian Thom discuss the exhibition in
relation to notions of place and identity. Yoon is the Assistant professor
of Visual Arts at Simon Fraser University and her most recent works will be
exhibited at Canadian Cultural Centre in Paris and Doris McCarthy Gallery
at the University of Toronto until March 2009. In conjunction with Western
Landscapes: Emily Carr, E.J. Hughes, Ann Kipling, Gordon Smith.
Free with Gallery admission.

Opening Thursday September 11, 2014, from 9pm to 12am
with live music performance by Femme Zeppelin
Exhibition runs from September 12 to October 12, 2014

This exhibition will feature new work by a local artist who practices under
the moniker of Office Supplies Incorporated and Joseph Staples. The artist
produces xerox prints that are collaged and pasted on public spaces. The
upcoming exhibition deals with the musical cover band as a metaphor for the
matrix and impression within printmaking. The history of print as a means
of promoting live music will also be explored. Femme Zeppelin will be
playing live in the gallery during the opening with sets at 9pm and
10:30pm, and their performance will then be projected as an installation
for the duration of the exhibition. $2 beers and posters will be available.

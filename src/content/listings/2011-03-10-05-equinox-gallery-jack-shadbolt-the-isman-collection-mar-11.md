Equinox Gallery - Opening Reception - March 11

JACK SHADBOLT: THE ISMAN COLLECTION
March 11 to April 9, 2011

Opening Reception: Friday March 11, 6 to 8pm

Equinox Gallery is very pleased to present Jack Shadbolt: The Isman
Collection. The Ismans were longtime supporters of Jack Shadbolt and this
collection is representative of their relationship with the artist and
their admiration of his work. A letter from Jack and Doris to the Ismans
reveals the warm nature of their friendship: "You two are quite something -
always generating optimism and good spirits and we bask in your radiance."
The exhibition is a testament to this friendship and includes over fifty
extraordinary works, dating from 1939 to 1990.

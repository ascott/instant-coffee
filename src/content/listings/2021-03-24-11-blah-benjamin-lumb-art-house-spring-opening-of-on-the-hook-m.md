The event will be catered by The Boatshed Ambleside Beach restaurant
Saturday March 27, 1pm - 3pm
1370 Marine Drive, West Vancouver

This event will be showcasing the works of:

Dina Goldstien - Dina Goldstien is a Vancouver based visual artist, photojournalist and
narrative photographer. Her work is eminently conceptual as she incorporates complex
social commentary and iconography from the collective common imagination with narratives
inspired by the human condition.

Showcasing:
Fallen Princesses (2007 - 2009) - This series features humanized fairytale princesses
dealing with modern day scourges such as Cancer, addiction, obesity, war, and
environmental degradation. The series questions the "happily ever after" motif created by
Disney and Western society.

[https://www.dinagoldstein.com/dina/](https://www.dinagoldstein.com/dina/)

Attilio Fiumarella - Attilio is an
Italian photographic artist who is currently based in
Vancouver. He is interested in exploring social landscapes, identity and memory through
photography and architecture.

Showcasing:
Works of Mercy - This series is meant to reflect on the contemporary society through the
nudity of people with a tough background. Attilio's inspiration for this series came from
Carvaggio's magnificent allegory called Seven Works of Mercy, created in 1607.

The 100 Swimmers - This series displays one of the first public facilities in Birmingham, UK
- The Moseley Road Baths built in 1907. Attilio was inspired by the beautiful architectural
design of the facility as well as the resilience and cohesion of the Moseley Road Baths
community.

[https://attiliofiumarella.com/home](https://attiliofiumarella.com/home)

Benjamin Lumb - Benjamin Lumb is a Vancouver based artist who intersects past and
present. His works are conversation pieces in their most manifest form. Through tactile
storytelling, he explores memory, emotion, social issues,
utility and progress.

Showcasing:
Be Wild While You Can, 2019

Be Wild While you Can Photography Series, 2020 - Circle Man, Direction, Layers, Growth,
2 Be, Wild, While, You, Can

Sea to Sky Table, 2019

Hollyburn Ferry Propeller, 2018

Safe Explosion (light), 2019

Safe Explosion (dark), 2019

[https://benjaminlumb.art/works](https://benjaminlumb.art/works)

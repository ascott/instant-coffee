Listen, Listen Festival
Oct 6-7, 2023
At Left of Main (211 Keefer) and Toast at Doris (242 E Pender St) in Historic Chinatown

A two-day multidisciplinary festival celebrating improvised arts sparked from joyful
spontaneous play. Presented by Vancouver
Improvised Arts Society in partnership with
plastic orchid factory, the festival is a collaboration between artists of different
backgrounds, influences and styles.

Featuring FREE happy hour socials with free food + beverages AND a special
dumpling-making workshop on Oct 7.

Limited capacity, please reserve in advance.
Tickets on a sliding scale are $0-$50.

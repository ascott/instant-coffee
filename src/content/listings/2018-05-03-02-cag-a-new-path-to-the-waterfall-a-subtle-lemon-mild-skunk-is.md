CAG: A New Path to the Waterfall
Thursday, May 3, 5.30-7pm, free

Lord Strathcona Elementary School, 592 E Pender St
Classroom 203

Over the course of April, artists and collaborators Helen Reed and Hannah
Jickling have led Ms. Persoon's Grade 6/7 class on weekly explorations of
the human senses as they relate to experiences of taste. Analyzing flavours
in chocolate bars, evaluating kid-centric candy advertising and exploring
relationships between smell and memory, the students have been encouraged
to question marketing tactics to children, issues of consumption and what
it means to mark the scents around us as only 'good' or 'bad'. Through
olfactory adventures and taste-making brainstorms, the class have brought
these explorations together to develop ideas for their own soda creations.

Please join them on May 3 for a soda-tasting celebration at Strathcona
Elementary School!

This event is part of A New Path to the Waterfall. Find out more at
www.anewpathtothewaterfall.ca

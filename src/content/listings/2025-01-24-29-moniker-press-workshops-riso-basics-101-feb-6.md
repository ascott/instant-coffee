WORKSHOPS: Riso Basics (101) at Moniker Press
February 6, 630pm and February 9,
1pm

Riso Basics (101) is an introductory workshop that explores the fundamentals of
Risograph printing. Participants will learn about the inner workings of the machines
and have the opportunity to create their own 2-colour postcard through drawing and
collage, which will be printed during the session.

To register visit: www.eventbrite.ca/o/moniker-press-67162740083

Details:
Type: in-person
Capacity: 8
Cost: $60 (sliding scale 40-60-80 + tax)
Location: Moniker Press Studio
Duration: 3-3.5 hours
When: monthly-ish
Day/Time: varies
Who: Beginners who are new to riso printing, no prior knowledge required!
Colours: 2-colour
Take away: mini Riso 101 worksheet + 50 copies of a 2-colour postcard and print
exchange and goodie bag.

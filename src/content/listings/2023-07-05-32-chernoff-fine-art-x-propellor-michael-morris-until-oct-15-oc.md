F L A T F O R M | WORKS BY MICHAEL MORRIS

CHERNOFF FINE ART x PROPELLOR

Propellor Studio, 1247 Cartwright Street, Granville Island

June 15 - October 15

We have recently assembled a special collection of Michael Morris graphic works
dating from the late 1960's and are partnering with our good friends at Propellor
Studio on Granville Island to exhibit these pieces.

These silkscreened pieces embrace the mediums bold, flat colours and simple
techniques. Morris strong design sense is displayed in his precise abstract forms
and narrow bands of colour. These artworks feel fresh and contemporary with a
strong Pop aesthetic despite being an astounding five decades old!

Similar pieces were recently shown at the Vancouver Art Gallery in their Hard Edge
exhibit and many are held in their permanent collection.

Also on view are a series of printers proofs made by Michael Morris with the master
printer Peter Braune of New Leaf Editions. These are a series of etchings made in
2012 of Morris City de Luxe series, originally created as paintings in 1968. We are
excited to show these works as we feel they are a very special piece of Vancouver
art history.

Michael Morris 1942-2022 is a Canadian artist known for his significant contributions
to the world of contemporary art. Working in Vancouver, he initially gained
recognition as a painter and printmaker, exploring various abstract and conceptual
art forms. His early works often combined elements of geometric abstraction with an
emphasis on color, space, and form.

In addition to his artistic practice, Morris played a crucial role in establishing artist-
run spaces and collectives, including Western Front Society in 1973, and Image
Bank (1969) which embraced the concept of art as a social
exchange and
challenged traditional notions of authorship.

As his career progressed, Morris expanded his artistic repertoire, delving into
multimedia installations, photography, and video art. He embraced new technologies
and techniques, continuously exploring innovative ways to express his ideas.

Over the years, Michael Morris's artwork has been exhibited extensively in
prestigious institutions such as the Vancouver Art Gallery, the Art Gallery of Ontario,
the National Gallery of Canada, and the Museum of Modern Art in New York.

Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery | 2016 Call for 
Submissions

Deadline: May 6, 2016

Call for Artists Description: 

Art Rental & Sales program at the Vancouver Art Gallery's annual call 
for submissions is now open. If you are a British Columbia-based artist, 
we invite you to submit to the program. 

Submission requirements: 

A completed Art Rental & Sales Consignor Application Form (see 
attached) 
A brief (200 word max) artist statement 
A brief (one page max) artist CV 
An 8.5" x 11" thumbnail index sheet of artwork images identified by 
title, medium, size, and 
purchase value (template available on our website)
Max. 10 images of your 2-dimensional work either: emailed as 
attachments, shared via Dropbox or Wetransfer, or (for mailed 
submissions) on a USB/flash drive 
Image files must be JPEG format identified by title, medium, size, and 
purchase value to match the thumbnail index sheet (ex. 
ForestFog_oil_canvas_30x34_1000.jpg) 

Art Rental & Sales accepts one submission per artist per year. 
Submissions may include work in various media by the same artist, but 
each submission is limited to 10 images. 
If you are mailing your submission package, it must fit in a 9" x 12" 
manila envelope. Do not send bulky or over-sized packages, elaborate 
binders, or mailing boxes. If you wish to have your materials returned 
to you, please include a self-addressed stamped envelope. 

Each submission is individually reviewed by a selection committee and 
applicants will be notified within two months of the submission 
deadline. You do not need to reserve the artworks for us until you are 
notified. New artwork will be brought into the Showroom in the fall of 
2016. If you are accepted into the Art Rental & Sales program, we will 
select artwork from the pieces that are available at that time. 

How to Apply: 

Please visit www.artrentalandsales.com/submissions.php to review 
submissions guidelines and to download the application forms. 

The deadline for submission is Friday, May 6, 2016. Submissions 
received after this deadline will not be accepted. No phone calls 
please.

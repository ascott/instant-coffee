The Firehall Arts Centre is excited to present Slated for Demolition, a body of work by 
Canadian photographer Larry Wolfson. With over 40 years of engagement in 
photography, Larry has turned his lens on the most glaring examples of Vancouver's 
changing landscape.

Wolfson photographs Vancouver's unused homes in various stages of demolition. 
These domestic spaces are captured, devoid of human presence and eerily empty. 
Beyond the existential, these sites conjure the recent discussions over our changing 
landscape and fiery controversy about the housing market, affordability, aesthetics 
and meanings of community.

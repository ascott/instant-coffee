FACES

Presented at Livingspace

August 16-November 22, 2025

1706 W 1st Ave, Vancouver
Monday-Friday: 10am to 5:30pm
Saturday: 10am to 5pm

FACES brings together a cross-generational group of artists who explore the face as
subject, symbol, and site of identity. The exhibition reflects on presence and
absence, the emotional landscape of expression, and the many roles the face plays
in shaping how we see ourselves and others.

Staged within the context of Livingspace"a leader in contemporary design"the
exhibition inhabits a hybrid realm where visual art and design intersect. Here,
aesthetic experience is both spatial and conceptual; material form and emotional
resonance are held in dynamic balance. The setting itself becomes part of the
inquiry, inviting reflection on how we present, frame, and inhabit identity.

Moniker Press Studio Launch Weekend
080, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC

At last, Moniker Press is opening the doors to our new studio! Drop by anytime for a 
weekend-long launch party that includes; open studio hours, print demos, opportunities to 
view the print archive and paper samples, plus the launch of several new publications 
including the 2019 Artists Calendar, Screen Ecologies (by sophia bartholomew + Emma 
Hicks), and the soft launch for Suburbantomy (by Adi Hadzismajlovic). 

A selection of riso books collected from around the world will be available for browsing and 
inspiration. Moniker Press publications will also be available for purchase with select copies 
on sale!

MONIKERPRESS.CA

Moniker Press studio is a book production and community space, made up of an assembly 
of artists who use publishing to inform their practice. The studio currently houses, Aeiou, 
Hayley Burns, SPIT (Emma Metcalfe Hurst), and soon, Blank Cheque Press. The studio was 
created to foster inclusive education and community around artists publishing and 
printmaking, offering opportunities for observation, practice and experimentation through the 
medium of printed matter.

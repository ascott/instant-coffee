Calling all emerging and mid-career Asian Canadian theatre artists!

Applications are now open for Vancouver Asian Canadian Theatres 2025 Creation
Lab!

This is your chance to develop your theatrical work in a space dedicated to
exploration, experimentation, and storytelling. Two selected projects will receive:
$3,500 stipend
Mentorship from dramaturgs Leslie Dos Remedios & Kamila Sediego
Actor workshops (16 in-studio hours)
A tailored development process to suit your artistic needs

The program culminates in a work-in-progress presentation at the Roundhouse
Community Centre in December 2025!

vAct welcomes a broad
range of work"whether its narrative-driven, experimental,
or collaborative"centered on your unique artistic and cultural perspectives.

"... Deadline: February 21, 2025, 11:59 PM PST

Submit your application at vact.ca. Got questions? Reach out to Kamila Sediego at
kamila@vact.ca.

Dont miss this opportunity to grow your craft, connect with a vibrant creative
community, and share your voice.

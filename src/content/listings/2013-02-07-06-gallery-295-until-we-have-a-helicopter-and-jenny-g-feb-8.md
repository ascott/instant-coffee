Gallery 295 - Until We Have A Helicopter and Jenny G

Opening Reception: Friday Feb 8th 7:00 - 9:00 

Gallery 295 is pleased to announce new works in our Main Gallery and 
Lightbox project space.

Artist duo Until We Have a Helicopter presents "Lost". In a measured 
departure from their typical practice, the artists have used the services 
available through Gallery 295 and The Lab to produce their first exhibition of 
photographs. These new works are documentation of archetypal wilderness 
signals that the artists have created in attempt to communicate not only with 
each other, but with the encountering world, Anybody.

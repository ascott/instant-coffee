From Ground Zero: 22 untold short stories in film from Gaza (Part 2)
Screening & public discussion
Thurs. November 28, 6:00 pm doors open, 6:30 screening
At the Norm Theatre, UBC
6138 Student Union Blvd #130, Vancouver

No reservations, entrance by suggested donation of $10, proceeds to
Palestine and
Lebanon relief. Presented by from the river to the sea collective, with special thanks
to Critical Image Forum research cluster (vimeo.com/criticalimageforumubc), UBC
Film Society (@ubcfilmsociety, facebook.com/UBCFilmSociety), and the UBC
Department of Anthropology (@anthropology_ubc,
facebook.com/anthropologydept.ubc).

From Ground Zero is a collection of short films initiated by Palestinian filmmaker
Rashid Masharawi, in response to the genocidal events following October 7, 2023.
The Ground Zero project was designed to give voice to the people of Gaza and to
document their experiences, which often go unheard. In a context where artistic
creation is more than complex, this project has captured a diversity of perspectives
through a 112-minute film in two parts, made up of 22 short films between 3 and 6
minutes. These intimate stories explore a range of genres, from fiction to
documentary to animation, all share in the verit richness and dangers of daily life,
surviving
on the ground in Gaza.

We hope to have an intimate and engaging discussion after the screening, one to
motivate and help build the movement of Palestinian solidarity with practical actions
we can take here.

There will be short presentations before the films, the speakers include:
semetna:t / Audrey Siegl, opening and welcoming to the xmkey"m lands.
Jasbir K. Puar, writer, Distinguished Faculty of Arts Professor of Global Race Studies
Institute for Gender, Race, Sexuality, & Social Justice, UBC, will speak to the films:
jasbirkpuar.com
Hicham Safieddine, Associate Professor & Canada Research Chair in the History of
the Modern Middle East, UBC, will speak about the ongoing genocide and provide
insights on the current invasions into/onto Lebanon.
Althea Thauberger, artist, Associate Professor, Dept of Art History, Visual Art &
Theory, UBC, will introduce the Critical Image Forum research cluster, a co-sponsor
of the event.
Graduate Students 4 Palestine (GS4P), will speak to student
initiatives on campus
surrounding PACBI (Palestinian Campaign for the Academic and Cultural Boycott of
Israel) and BDS (the Boycott, Divestment, Sanctions) movement working to end
international support for Israel's oppression and occupation of Palestine:
@gs4palestine

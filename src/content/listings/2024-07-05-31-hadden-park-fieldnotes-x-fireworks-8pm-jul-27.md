Taking place in Hadden Park under a canopy of trees, this edition of the Fieldnotes
concert series features live performances by Piu, Isaac Rufus, Immix, and DeForrest
Brown, Jr.

Free! Come for the music, stay for the fireworks!

The performances begin at 8:00pm next to
the Hadden Park fieldhouse (1015 Maple
Street).

Public washrooms can be accessed in this park. As the fireworks will begin at 10pm,
there will be road closures in effect in the area.
Presented by Publik Secrets, with support from the BC Arts Council and Vancouver
Park Board's Fieldhouse Activation Program.

Hadden Park is located on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded Indigenous
territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), and
Sl"lwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

W2 Hosts Book Launch of Challenge for Change: Activist Documentary at the
National Film Board of Canada

Vancouver, BC | Thursday February 25th, 2010 - W2 is pleased to present the
book launch for Challenge for Change: Activist Documentary at the National
Film Board of Canada. Join us 5-8pm on March 22 for screenings, discussions
and light refreshments with editors Thomas Waugh, Ezra Winton and CFC/SN
documentarians. The Special Guest of Honour will be Bonnie Sherr Klein.
Co-sponsored by SFU's Department of English and School of Communication, W2
Community Media Arts Society, McGill-Queen's University Press, the National
Film Board of Canada, and Cinema Politica.

Published by McGill-Queen's University Press, Challenge for Change is a
600-page volume of essays and articles edited by Cinema Politica founder
and programmer Ezra Winton, along with Thomas Waugh and Michael Brendan
Baker. It includes a forward by Naomi Klein, famed author and activist.
Three years in the making, this collection brings together for the first
time historical and contemporary literature and research on one of the
National Film Board of Canada's most (in)famous initiatives to date.

Between 1967 and 1980, the activist documentary program Challenge for

Challenge for Change presents an examination of the radical politics and
cinema of the legendary documentary film program devoted to social change.

Challenge for Change has occasionally been referenced in regards to W2's
development as a people-powered media project. Watch the trailer created by

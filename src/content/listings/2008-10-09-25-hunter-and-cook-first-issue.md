Hunter and Cook is a new quarterly contemporary art magazine, produced in
Toronto by Jay Isaac and Tony Romano.

The first issue has features and projects by David Armstrong Six, Geoffrey
Farmer,Claire Greenshaw, Derek Sullivan, Brad Phillips, Corpusse (by Amy
Bowles and photos by Jimmy Limit), Seth Scriver, Therese Mastroiacovo,
Terence Koh, Robin Fry, Corin Sworn and Laura Aldridge.

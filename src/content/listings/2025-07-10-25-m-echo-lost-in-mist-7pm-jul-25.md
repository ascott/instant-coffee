We are hosting a group show, M-echo lost in mist, on the top floor of the Sun
Tower,128 W Pender St W, Vancouver, BC V6B 1R8, from July 26th to August 8th.
Admissions are free by appointment. https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/mi-echo-lost-in-
mist-tickets-1468760174219?aff=oddtdtcreator
The open reception night will be on July 25th, 7 pm-9 pm.

Exhibition Introduction: It all begins from a hazy starting point " like stepping into a
dream, unsure whether its a memory, a premonition, or the first time arriving.

The Meaning of M : M is a Chinese word meaning to seek "but not always
toward a clear destination. It suggests a state of drifting, of moving through unnamed
experiences with hesitation, proximity, and dislocation. Its soft, ambiguous
Romanized sound, Mi, mirrors the emotional haze present in the works of all three
artists in this exhibition. The subtitle Echo Lost in Mist points to something that once
existed, or might have " elusive yet lingering. We follow it without knowing what we
are answering.

This exhibition brings together three artists from distinct backgrounds and
disciplines. Each of them uses a different medium and process to explore how they
experience reality, memory, and emotional perception.

Lucas Gmez Cid creates sculptural characters that exist between the adorable and
the uncanny. Working with mixed media and pop-cultural references, he invites
viewers into a tactile world of stitched smiles and surreal hybrids " asking: what is
normal, and how do we define identity?

Jenny Chen paints like shes staring out from inside a burning amusement park. Her
images are bright but emotionally ruptured "abstract self-portraits that express the
tension between outward charm and inner collapse.

Noomumi Works in digital illustration to depict awareness that resists resolution. Her
compositions are nonlinear and dreamlike, evoking impermanence and shifting
perception through drifting atmospheres and fragmented relations. Her works invite
slowness, allowing space to feel rather than interpret.

The Exhibitions Intention

This exhibition does not attempt to define the dream or the real. It opens a path " a
field of emotional and perceptual echoes. There is no final meaning, no answer to be
found.

What you see may not be clear, but perhaps that is precisely the point.

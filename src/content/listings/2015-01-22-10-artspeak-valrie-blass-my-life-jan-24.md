VALÉRIE BLASS: MY LIFE

January 24–March 7, 2015
Opening Friday, January 23, 8pm

Conversation with Ariane Noël de Tilly
Saturday, January 24, 2pm

It begins in 1992, but perhaps more seriously in 1995. Blass, and her work,
represent the elongation of recognition. She devotes her twenties to doing
what most people do in their twenties, finding her way, and in 1995 she
enrolls in fine arts at the Université du Québec à Montréal. Hers is a
career that feels antithetical to the rapid pace of contemporary art but is
a path far too familiar for women. She is approaching thirty when she
decides to be serious about art making, and even then prolongs the
completion of her graduate studies. She has responsibilities to her
children and to herself to earn a living. She sees her male peers rise to
success in half the time. But she does not need to be in the business of
catching up, which erroneously implies she is behind. She is, and this is
fortunate for us, an artist out of time.

Monique Mouton, Shades, oil on panel, 2012, 40”  x 39” 

Blanket Contemporary Art is pleased to present “Sides” , an exhibition 
of new 
paintings by LA based artist Monique Mouton. This is her second solo 
show 
with Blanket.

Monique Mouton’s new paintings present a deceptive form of 
nonchalance. 
There is a determined ambivalence to her brush strokes, and her hand 
cut 
panels are a spare backdrop to the thinnest application of paint. On the 
wall, these paintings hover; an effect that draws the viewer not only to 
the 
face of the work, but to the side, to the back, and to the wall itself. 
Mouton’s paintings have always been a quiet declaration, but this new 
body 
of work brings something more intent, and multifold: the scale is 
increased; 
the various trapezoid-like shapes lend themselves to allusions of 
perspectival space and landscape, yet remain resolutely abstract. Here 
gravity is paired with an underlying sense of folly as a poetic narrative 
wavers in a space both dense and delicate.

Monique Mouton lives and works in Los Angeles, California. Recent 
exhibitions include her solo show Pitch at Pamela, Los Angeles; and the 
group show Rope of Sand, Soi Fischer, Toronto. Her work was also 
included in 
recent exhibitions at Equinox Gallery, Vancouver Art Gallery, Or 
Gallery, 
and CSA space (all Vancouver); and in Working Title curated by 
Elizabeth 
McIntosh at Diaz Contemporary, Toronto. Mouton earned her BFA from 
Emily 
Carr Institute in 2006, and is a candidate for her MFA from Bard 
College, 
Annandale-on-Hudson, NY in 2014.

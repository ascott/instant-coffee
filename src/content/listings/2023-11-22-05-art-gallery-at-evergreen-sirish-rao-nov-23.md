About the speaker:
Sirish Rao is an internationally recognized cultural innovator, curator,publisherand
writer withtwenty-fiveyears of leadership experience in arts and culture. He is
currently Director of Public Engagement and Learning at Vancouver Art Gallery. Born
and raised inBangalore, India, Rao moved to Vancouver in 2010, and co-founded
Indian Summer Festival with his partner LauraByspalko. In his role as Founding
Artistic Director, Sirish presented more than a thousand artists on the stages of the
festival, in a roster that features Nobel, Booker, Grammy and Oscar Award-winning
artists alongside exciting emerging talent. Sirish has received an Extraordinary
Achievement Award (Darpan),
been named One of 20 Most Influential South Asians
in Vancouver (Urban Asian), andreceived a Lifetime Achievement Award (Hari
Sharma Foundation) for his contributions to social and economic justice through the
arts.

About the exhibition, "RISE: Sandeep Johal and Sara Khan:"
Sandeep Johal and Sara Khans artistic collaboration gives rise to a playful and
fantastical environment. The artists merge their distinct creatures, dynamic patterns
and rich colour palettes into new hybrid forms. Their artworks celebrate the joy,
power and complexities of womanhood from their perspectives as two South Asian
women artists who are mothers to young children.

To Speak With a Golden Voice

July 16, 2020 - April 11, 2021 - Main Exhibition Hall

Bill Reid (1920"1998) was a pivotal force in Northwest Coast art who empowered and
inspired a generation of artists. In honour of his 100th birthday, To Speak with a Golden
Voice brings a fresh perspective to his legacy, through rarely seen treasures, personal
stories from friends and community, and newly
commissioned works from Cori Savard and
Kinnie Starr. Co-curated by Gwaai Edenshaw and Beth Carter.

Yahguudangang - To Pay Respect - The Repatriation Journey of the Haida Nation

July 16 - October 11, 2020 - Mezzanine Gallery
The Haida Nation has identified over 12,000 Haida belongings and ancestors held in
museums around the world, that were stolen or sold under duress in the last century. This
immersive exhibition is a powerful and transformative look at one nation's efforts to
reclaim their cultural treasures. With the core philosophy of respect, the Haida Nation
continues to pursue repatriation through perseverance, negotiation, cooperation and trust.

Organized by the Haida Gwaii Museum at Kay Llnagaay and the Haida Repatriation
Committee.

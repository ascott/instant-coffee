MISTER SISTER
Works by Anthony Record and Nadia Moss
Opening reception Saturday October 25th 7pm - 11pm
October 25th - November 22nd

A selection of drawings and paintings.

Anthony Record received his M.F.A. from the San Francisco Art Institute in
2008 and currently lives and works in Tampa, Florida. Working to achieve
absolute equivocally in all elements of visual experience, his paintings and
drawings challenge the boundaries of recognition and address the limits of
perception.

Nadia Moss is a Montreal-based visual artist. Her favoured media includes
drawing, painting and doll making. Her current subject matter includes
monsters, Canadian geese, and the beach. She has exhibited in Montreal,
Toronto, and Vancouver. Her last book of drawings Mr. Non. Pigeon was
published in 2005 by L'Oie de Cravan with the next one due this November.

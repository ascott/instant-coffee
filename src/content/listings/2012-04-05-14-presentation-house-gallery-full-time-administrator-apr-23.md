Presentation House Gallery is seeking a Full Time Administrator.

Presentation House Gallery, one of Vancouver's primary public 
spaces for media and photographic art, seeks to appoint an 
energetic, highly motivated team player with a desire to contribute to 
the gallery's next phase of development. 

Reporting to the Director and with support from the Treasurer, the 
Gallery Administrator is responsible for Administrative and Financial 
Management of the Gallery. Duties include financial and budgetary 
management, marketing and communications development, 
granting coordination, oversight and management of gallery 
volunteers and weekend staff, planning of education and outreach 
programs and oversight of personnel. Interim duties include 
fundraising plan development and execution (including fundraising 
events), development and cultivation of Gallery membership, as well 
as overseeing a membership, contacts, sponsor and donor 
database. Contingent on the Gallery's imminent plans the position 
could also involve capital campaign support and management.

Skills required:

Candidate will have excellent communication and organizational 
skills, and good working knowledge of budget and accounting 
processes. Proficiency with office software and financial 
management software is essential. While no direct experience is 
required, past work experience in the field of arts management, 
and/or engagement with art institutions and the visual arts will be a 
significant asset.

Salary range will be based on experience. Benefit Package including 
RRSP plan is available.

Please visit www.presentationhousegallery.org under "News" for a 
detailed job description.

Posting" by April 23rd, 2012 at 5:00 pm to:

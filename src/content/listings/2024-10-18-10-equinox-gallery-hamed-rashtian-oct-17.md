Hamed Rashtian: Word Sculptures
October 17 - November 16, 2024

Equinox Gallery is pleased to present Word Sculptures, an exhibition of recent
bronze sculptures by Hamed Rashtian. Word Sculptures bridges the realms of
contemporary Persian poetry and visual expression by evolving abstract literature
into visual forms. Through a multistage elimination process, Rashtian carefully
selected words that resonated deeply within various poems, often centering around
themes of time and space or loosely intertwining these concepts. Transforming each
word into 3D forms, Rashtian ventured further, creating architectural spaces within
them, drawing inspiration from the human body, and inviting viewers to immerse
themselves in these living realms.

Hamed Rashtian: Word Sculptures will be on view at Equinox Gallery until
November 16th, 2024.

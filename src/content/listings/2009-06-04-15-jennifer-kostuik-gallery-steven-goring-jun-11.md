SUROUNDING New paintings by STEVEN GORING
June 11 July 06
Opening Reception: Thursday June 11 6-9 pm :artist in attendance
Artist Talk Saturday June 13 @ 2 pm

artist statement: The work in ³Surrounding² has evolved from my experiences
of living in a rural environment. Observing the changing conditions of
light and weather systems over time in a circumscribed area has led me to
re-examine open space in contrast to contained space. For example, I have
watched the effects of a violent windstorm, which removed several trees and
hundreds of tree branches on the property. This changed the surrounding,
creating more open space and a changed horizon. The differently shaped works
reflects viewing the surrounding as individual isolated areas or as it¹s
vast wholeness. Merging a minimalist framework of reduction with romantic
overtones, results in the use and exploration of hard and soft lines.

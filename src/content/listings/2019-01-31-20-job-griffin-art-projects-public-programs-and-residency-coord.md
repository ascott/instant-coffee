Public Programs and Residency Coordinator

PTE - 2 days per week, 9 to 5 pm

Griffin Art Projects is seeking an innovative and dynamic thinker who can help to build 
audiences and excitement through the development and delivery of public programs and 
residency programs that capture the publics imagination and expand the experience of 
art and art galleries.

Griffin Art Projects (GAP) is a non-profit art gallery and residency located in North 
Vancouver. GAP is a non-collecting institution that has quickly become a vibrant 
contributor to the North Vancouver cultural landscape.

Griffin Art Projects is introducing a new position that will assist in the coordination of its 
residency and public program. The successful applicant will assist in the design of 
programs that offer unique opportunities for visitors of all ages and levels of interest to 
engage with art and culture at GAP. This position will assist both with the development 
of adult public programming and youth engagement in order to encourage new 
audiences to participate and to generate new knowledge and innovative thinking on 
contemporary art and culture with the ambition of creating dynamic learning 
opportunities for a wide range of visitors whose familiarity with contemporary art may 
differ.

This role will engage with best practices in pedagogy and for the impact of learning and 
public engagement and offer a unique opportunity to create a meaningful program that 
integrates deeper learning and participation, involving high schools, higher education 
institutions, the local, national and international arts community, and the general public, 
and liaising with artists, thinkers, and innovators from various disciplines to support GAP 
in producing innovative and impactful learning and public engagement. 

Reporting to the Director, the Public Programs and Residency Coordinator will produce 
a schedule of public programs, events and community outreach activities that animate, 
complement and enhance our exhibitions and residency program, and GAPs 
exploration of art collecting and collections practices and methodologies.

Duties include: assisting with the conceptual development, administration and 
implementation of adult and youth focused public programs including: artists talks and 
tours, lectures, workshops, film series, symposia and performances. The Public 
Programs and Residency Coordinator will also assist with the development and 
coordination of special community-based partnership and education initiatives and 
related special projects.

It is a permanent part-time two day per week position, with scheduled work from Friday 
to Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. This position includes some evening hours, as 
required to fulfill responsibilities and be on hand for gallery programs. 

Please submit a cover letter and current CV as one document in pdf format to: 
info@griffinartprojects.ca. We thank all applicants in advance. Only those selected for 
an interview will be contacted. Griffin Art Projects advocates equal opportunity for all 
employees and applicants for employment and is committed to diversity in the 
workplace.

..

QUALIFICATIONS

The successful candidate will possess a Masters degree in art or art history, museum or 
curatorial studies, thorough knowledge of Canadian and international art history and 
issues in contemporary art; 2 years working experience in a museum, public art gallery 
and/or artist run centre as well as demonstrated experience in program development 
and delivery. Exceptional written, presentation and interpersonal communication skills 
are required. Experience working successfully with internal and external partners 
including artists, educators, curators, producers and academics and the ability to 
problem solve and work independent and quickly, and manage the program on budget, 
while also keeping statistics, documentation and monitoring. Must be able to work with 
youth as well as adults, with the relevant clearances.

Candidates will have an ability to communicate effectively, both orally and in writing; 
strong organizational and administrative skills and the ability to work under general 
direction and within a small team environment. Fluency in French is considered an 
asset.

This will be a part-time position beginning in February 2019. The deadline for 
applications is February 7, at 5pm. To apply please send a cover letter, current CV to 
info@griffinartprojects.ca.

For more information on our organization, visit griffinartprojects.ca.

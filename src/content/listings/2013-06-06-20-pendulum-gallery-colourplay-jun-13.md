ColourPlay
Elizabeth Barnes : John Capitano : Rojia Dadashzadeh : Laura Skuse
June 10 – July 6, 2013

Opening Reception: June 13, 6-8

Colour Play is an exhibit that brings together four Vancouver painters, all 
interested in the use of colour through a process of improvisation and play. In 
these works colour acts as content through a variety of considerations, from 
chance performance to reflections on memory and culture, considerations of 
beauty and altered perceptions. Colour is heat. Colour is energy.
Any themed exhibition will naturally bring forward certain traits of an artists’ 
work to the exclusion of others, and what a viewer or curator feels is the 
essence of an artwork is a reading not necessarily shared by the artist. 
Putting together a show of artists based around a concept as inclusive as
colour runs the risk of misinterpreting those artists. Colour has so many ways 
to be explored; so many aspects. Barnes, Capitano, Dadashzadeh and 
Skuse have specific strategies for painting the way they do,
with approaches ranging from improvisational to conceptual, systematic to 
ordered by chance. But each of the artists in the show uses colour in a way 
that affects us viscerally. The works in the exhibition make us consider colour 
– the balance and interplay of hue, saturation and brightness, the emotional 
and sensual impact, the proximity to music in harmony, tone, and dissonance 
– as a central component of the paintings. Whether employed through a 
system or intuitive process, colour is the thread that unites the work in Colour 
Play.

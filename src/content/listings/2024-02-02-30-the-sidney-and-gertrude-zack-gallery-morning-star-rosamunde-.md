Morning Star

Rosamunde Bordo

January 5- February 7

Ideas are to objects as constellations are to stars. "Walter Benjamin, The Origin of
German Tragic Drama

Inspired by mysticism, magic, surrealism, astrology, and tarot, Rosamunde Bordo
presents Morning Star in the Sidney and Gertrude Zack Gallery. Drawing from the
line and circle iconography of Aramaic amulets that were used for healing and
protection, Bordo has created a series of hardwood sculptures that recall elements
of furniture and domestic architectural features, such as banisters, doorknobs, or
table legs. Resembling molecular structures, including that of salt (NaCl), these
Constellation works mediate the cosmic and the infra-molecular through the
everyday.

Show text by Sol Hashemi.

Curated by Hope Forstenzer and Carmen Levy-Milne

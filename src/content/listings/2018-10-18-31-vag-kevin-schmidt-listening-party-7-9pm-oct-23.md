More details:

DIY HIFI is an audiophile listening room - you can bring your own vinyl records to 
play on the record player there. The speakers I've made for the installation are 
really special, very audiophile - when I play records of material that I know super well 
on them, I can hear more or clearer detail in them. I really would love for all of you 
to experience this, and I have no idea when I will every be able to set up this system 
again... 

On the remaining Tuesday nights 7-9pm (a by-donation night) I am running listening 
events - specifically inviting friends with big record collections to come play some 
stuff. I invite you all to join me. Last week Josh Stevenson played a bunch of rare 
Vancouver art rock records. Of course you can go play your records whenever the 
gallery is open. Some folks have asked me what kind of music works well:
-stuff you know well, so you can hear the difference on this system
-live recordings of jazz
- electronic music
-recordings that are not too compressed (at my last listening event someone played 
Kid A - it was really compressed and didn't sound that good to my ears). When Josh 
played Chilliwack's second album from the '70s it sounded awesome. More dynamic 
range tends to sound better.

RGB Beg Cycle is a work that allows you to control the lighting animations on RGB 
strips installed on the outside of the VAG. Inside the gallery are keyboards that 
trigger samples from a field recording I made at Ribfest Kamloops - each sample 
has an accompanying animation. You can create musique-concrete audio visual 
compositions that play over the lights and are broadcast over FM on the outside of 
the building. You can borrow an FM receiver from the VAG coat check to walk 
outside and listen to the audio that goes with the flashing lights!
Essentially the soundtrack has the VAG masquerading as a small town barbeque 
festival, acting like the cover bands you can hear in the samples - which I think is 
really funny. Also, any composition you create is recorded by the system and when 
no-one is pressing keys, these recordings are played back. So go make some 
soundtracks for the VAG building to perform.

HUNTER AND COOK MAGAZINE ACCEPTING ARTIST PROJECT SUBMISSION FOR ISSUE 08

As an opportunity to have access to otherwise unknown artistic production,
Hunter and Cook is accepting submissions for one artist to do a four page
project in our next issue 08. Hunter and Cook is a curated art magazine,
produced in Toronto by artists Jay Isaac and Tony Romano. It is widely
distributed across Canada (including Chapters and Indigos) the US and
Europe and attends major fairs such as Art Basel, TIAF, NADA, and Printed
Matters Art Book Fair at PSI Moma. It is subscribed to by major North
American institutions such as the AGO, MOMA New York, The Musee D'art
Contemporain, Montreal etc.

This will be a great opportunity for emerging or established artists to
have a project, specifically made for Hunter and Cook, to be viewed by a
wide audience which includes numerous gallerists,writers, curators and
collectors.

The fee to enter your project is $35.00

DEADLINE IS DEC 15th, 2010

Artists can submit as many projects as possible, but each entry will cost
$35.00

You will only be contacted if your project is chosen.

Have a look on our website to see some projects that have been produced by
invited artists in the past

www.hunterandcook.com

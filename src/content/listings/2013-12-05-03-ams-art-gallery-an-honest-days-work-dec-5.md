AMS Art Gallery

AN HONEST DAY'S WORK
December 5-13
Reception: Thursday, December 5th, 5-7pm
AMS Art Gallery, UBC Student Union Building
Hours: Mon-Fri 11-5pm

An exhibition of 4th year BFA/BA student work exploring the relationship between art, labour, and leisure, and the area in between.
How do artists work? How is art work, and how does it fall outside the notion of work?

Participating artists: Warut Boonyaleephun, Julia Casol, Adrian Diaz, Christina Dixon, Leslie Fetsch, Ervin Hadzic, Rachel Jao,
Woojae Kim, Soda Leavey, Marie Lee, Julia Mills, Niousha Ronassi, Emma Wilson, and Katharine Yi.

Blue Cabin Restoration

ARTIST: Jeremy Borsos
LOCATION: Seymour Art Gallery, 4360 Gallant Avenue, North Vancouver, BC, V7G 1L2. 
DATES: October 3, 2017
TIME: 7 pm

Please join us for an artist talk on the restoration of the Blue Cabin. Jeremy Borsos will 
give a short presentation on their experience with the Blue Cabin remediation " from 
taking the cabin apart board by board, to their discovery of a series of 1927 cultural 
posters hidden under its floorboards.

The talk will touch upon the cabins broader history and its significance to the area. It will 
be followed by a short Q&A period. 

For more information visit our website posting on event and our main page for the 
project.

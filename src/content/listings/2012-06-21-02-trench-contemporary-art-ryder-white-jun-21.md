Ryder White: 'The Chase!' 3-day Film Installation June 21-23
Ryder Thomas White is a filmmaker whose choice of medium is
expansive, but the Simon Fraser University graduate loves to work with
film most of all, particularly when it comes to exhibition. And film is what
visitors will certainly get when they come to see "The Chase!" a film
installation/experience in "Infiniscope" that White has created in the
Trench. The apparatus consists of a single loop of film winding its way
through three projectors and a viewer along the way, constructing an
unreal and yet very mechanically real viewing space. More details to
come!

Camp Wavelength

We are excited to announce this year's Camp Wavelength call for submissions for 
Visual & Media Art, Performance Art, Dance & Activities! This year's festival runs 
August 18-19 at Fort York: Garrison Common. Torontos Dreamiest Music & Arts 
Festival Relocates to a Parkland Oasis in the City!

Our call for art submissions is now open.
We are looking for Performance Art, Visual/Media Artists, Installation Art, Games & 
Activities only. Unfortunately no music submissions are being requested this year.

Submission Deadline: April 30, 2018.

NO SUBMISSION FEE! 

You can find the submission form here: https://bit.ly/2Gu40WH
FUN FINDS A WAY: CAMP WAVELENGTH FINDS A NEW HOME!

Save the Date for August 18-19, 2018 as the fourth annual Camp Wavelength Music 
& Arts Festival emerges at a new location in downtown Toronto: Garrison Common 
at Fort York National Historic Site.

Camp Wavelength is an urban weekend getaway like no other. For two magical 
days, Wavelength will summon the spirit of summer camp and take festival-goers on 
a journey of discovery and escape. For 2018s festival, Camp Wavelength will move 
to a new, central, outdoor location " Fort York National Historic Sites tree-lined 
green space, Garrison Common. Easily accessible by transit, bicycle or on foot, 
Camp Wavelength 2018 will be a hidden oasis of creativity and community within the 
city.

Submissions are now open for non-musical artists to animate this years festival. 
Visual & media artists, dance & performance artists, and creators of interactive 
games & activities are free to submit proposals until Monday, April 30.

Please note: Camp Wavelength 2018 is a Day Camp only. Mark both August 18 and 
19 on your calendar to attend Camp, but plan to sleep in your own bed!

CALL FOR *ART* SUBMISSIONS:

2018 Curatorial Theme: Fun Finds A Way. Nature has reclaimed the city. 
Overgrown buildings, the adults are away, the kids have taken over the camp!

Camp Wavelength is seeking proposals for site-specific pieces from artists working 
in the following disciplines:

VISUAL & MEDIA ARTS
DANCE & PERFORMANCE
GAMES & ACTIVITIES

All visual & media arts projects must work outside and during daylight and nighttime 
hours.
All Camp Wavelength artists will be paid an honourarium and the cost of materials 
will be covered.
Apologies, but due to limited space, we cannot accept submissions from musical 
artists.
Vendors, hold tight! We will be issuing a separate Request for Proposals for food & 
beverage partners and on-site vendors.

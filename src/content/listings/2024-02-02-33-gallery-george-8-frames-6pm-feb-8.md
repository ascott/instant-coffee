OPENING NIGHT: Feb 8, 6-9pm at 140 W Hastings St inside a gallery inside a gallery.

8 Frames is a show of 8 emerging artists each asked to make a work to fit a frame
made from bike frames (recycled bike frames donated to TAPEWORM Corp by UBC
Bike Kitchen). The works are small (either 4x6 or 4x4in) and so is the gallery (4x8ft)
containing an eclectic mixture of art from Jane Grocott, Connor Skillen, Maya Thiersch,
Wharfie Boy, George Hawitt, Bianca Del Rio Kodato, Josh Koole, and Elise Gerhards.
Their art and talent will likely be emerging from the frames, from the gallery, from the
gallery, into the streets and mind, of a new generation.

Employment Opportunities

Director, Programming

Plug In Institute of Contemporary Art (ICA) is a laboratory for research and a 
centre for the presentation of art that confronts ideas and issues affecting 
contemporary society. Over the past forty years Plug In ICA has grown to 
serve local, national, and international audiences with a variety of programs 
and activities. Our activities encompass visual culture in the broadest sense 
including: film and video, architecture, new media, popular and material 
culture, design and literature. Plug In ICA organizes six to ten exhibitions each 
year, publishes through our imprint Plug In Editions, and tours contemporary 
art in all media.

Plug In ICA is seeking an energetic, organized and professional Director, 
Programming. The Director, Programming will provide organizational 
leadership and develop, maintain and implement overall missions, artistic 
mandate, programming and projects of Plug In ICA, including: artistic and 
curatorial direction, research, design, marketing, production, interpretation, 
outreach, publishing and program evaluation and follow-up. Reporting to the 
Board of Directors or its delegate, the incumbent is one of two senior staff 
positions at Plug In ICA.

The candidate will have at least three years of gallery experience in a 
leadership or managerial position, a baccalaureate degree in Art History, 
curatorial studies or a related field, and curatorial experience among other 
qualifications listed in the job description. A job description is available for 
download.

This position is permanent full-time (40 hours/week).
Remuneration for this position is dependant upon qualifications and 
experience.
Interested candidates should submit a curriculum vitae, letter of interest, and 
three work-related references (including name and contact information) by 5 
pm on Monday, June 30th, 2014.

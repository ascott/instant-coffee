Nana Home Gallery is pleased to present 7,7lb a group exhibition curated by Alice Grassi.

Join us on April 26th 2019 for the opening reception: a day of artists' talk, screenings and 
exhibition. 

In this exhibition the theme of motherhood and birth is the core of the questions the invited 
artists investigated.

The show exhibits photographic works of Belli, Cardillo, Castioni, Grassi and Spertini, as 
well as The Life of an Artists Book, Light and Sensitive, a selection of artist's books from 
the KoobookArchive, curated by Anna Guillot, which contain pieces by Blum, Duchatelet, 
Guillot, Migliora, Mir, Opie, Piotrowski, Wuthrich.

As last year, Nana Home Gallery is part of Capture Photography Festival as an accredited 
event, showcasing a screening section by artists Contino, De Cicco and Tognoli.

HUB is holding a series of talks highlighting creative applications of
technology. These talks will feature artists and technologists from the
Vancouver area and will focus on their recent work.

Upcoming Talks:

* Dennis Rosenfeld - August 27, 7:00 PM (UBC Robson Square, Room C485)

These talks will be held at UBC Robson Square, 800 Robson Street,
Vancouver.

About HUB: Centre for Art & Technology

HUB is an experimental art initiative focused on collaboration, the
sharing of ideas and knowledge, and the creative exploration of
information technologies. Built around the principles of the Open Source
movement, HUB aims to create a collaborative, interdisciplinary
environment that provides free and open access to current and emerging
technologies. To learn more, please visit 
http://http://www.hubcentre.org/hublab/

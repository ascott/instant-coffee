ASSUMING PERMANENCE

February 28th - March 9th
OPENING RECEPTION March 3rd @ 6pm

Emily Carr University of Art and Design
1399 Johnston Street, Vancouver

A group show exhibiting new works by six female artists:

Blake Critchley
Alexine McLeod
Liz Saunders
Natalie Schmidt
Amanda Crain
Marika Vandekraats

The observation that we assume permanence in our perception of objects and
things, produces equally viable questions about the act of assuming
impermanence. Rather than offering only two extremes for one to choose
between, each artist explores the dialogue between these polarities,
thereby allowing for open-ended interpretation. In these playful
interactions between artist and object, object and viewer, one perpetually
moves from one side to another, creating new modes of seeing and thinking
in the process.

STORYHIVE is dedicated to support emerging filmmakers in British 
Columbia through funding, training and connection to a thriving 
community. STORYHIVE is open to pitches for Digital Shorts: Female 
Director Edition, August 18 - September 12, 2016. Creators who 
submit will have the chance to win one of 30 $10,000 grants for a short 
film (10 minutes or less). Watch previous STORYHIVE Digital Shorts 
for inspiration here.

Everyone can apply - this edition is open to all creators in BC and 
Alberta to pitch and participate in the production of these digital shorts, 
however we're on the hunt for female directors. Creators need to 
attach a female director to their project, and female directors must 
assemble a team, then prepare a pitch and submit before September 
12, 2016. Creators own their work once complete. Email 
storyhive@telus.com for questions!

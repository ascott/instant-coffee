Contemporary Conversations: Monika Szewczyk

Saturday September 27, 2008
Western Front Grand Luxe Theatre, 7 PM

This fall, Western Front Exhibitions will host a new public program,
Contemporary Conversations. Each month this program will engage the
intersections of aesthetics and radical thinking in contemporary art.
Reflecting a broad range of perspectives and practices, these conversations
will build upon each other to generate discussion about art and ideas.

The inaugural conversation will feature writer, editor and curator Monika
Szewczyk (Berlin, DE, and Rotterdam, NL). Szewczyk is currently Head of
Publications at Witte de With Center for International Contemporary Art
(Rotterdam, NL), and a contributing editor of A Prior Magazine (Ghent, BE).
A long-time resident of Vancouver, she has worked at the Belkin Art Gallery
and the Vancouver Art Gallery and as an instructor at the Emily Carr
University of Art + Design, before relocating to Europe in 2007.

Johan Lundh and Monika Szewczyk will discuss currents projects and the "art
of conversation" as a dynamic form of intellectual exploration. Future
conversations will feature artist Fia Backström (New York, US) and a
discussion about the practice of Kate Craig.

Contemporary Conversations is organized and hosted by independent curator
and writer Johan Lundh.

For more information contact Exhibitions Assistant Sara Mameni at
exhibitions@front.bc.ca

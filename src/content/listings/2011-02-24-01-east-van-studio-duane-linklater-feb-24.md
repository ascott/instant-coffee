EAST VAN STUDIO

Duane Linklater, will be showing some of his video
work this Thursday at East Van Studio. Duane is from Moose Factory
Ontario and is currently in the MFA program at Bard College in NY
State. Duane's videos could loosely be described as short experiments
on exposing his Cree background to unconventional environments, but
that's just a starting point.

East Van Studio on Feb 24 at 7 pm, 870 East
Cordova st.

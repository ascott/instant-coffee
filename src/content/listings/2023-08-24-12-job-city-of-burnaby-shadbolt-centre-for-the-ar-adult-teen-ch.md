Ceramic Arts contract Instructor, multiple positions/shifts available and continued on a
seasonal basis. The Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, located in Deer Lake Park in the City
of Burnaby is seeking experienced Ceramic Arts instructors to plan and facilitate
ceramic arts programs starting this October. Students will vary in age based on
the
class being taught as detailed below. Ceramic Arts Instructors require a strong level of
self-sufficiency and confidence with the equipment and supplies required in a
specialized ceramic studio classroom setting.

Position #1, Thursdays, October 5-November 30:
-3:30-5:30pm, Clayworks (9-12 years). These lessons further develop skills in ceramic
arts. Handbuilding and wheel-throwing are covered as well as techniques for thrown
work, such as trimming, assembling and glazing.
-6:30-9:30pm, Ceramics I: Introduction (18+ years). Designed especially for adult
absolute beginners who learn a range of basic methods used in making clay forms by
hand and on the wheel. Learn glazing and decorating techniques.

Position #2, Fridays, October 6-November 24:
-3:30-5:30pm, Clayworks (9-12 years). These lessons further develop skills in ceramic
arts. Handbuilding and wheel-throwing are covered as well as techniques for thrown
work, such as trimming, assembling and glazing.
-6-8:30pm, Clay:
Handbuilding & Wheel Throwing (13-18 years). Learn pottery and
sculpture techniques in our professionally equipped studios. Wheel-work, handbuilding,
and glazing for our high-fire gas kiln are covered. After being introduced to various
building techniques, students are encouraged to explore their own ideas through
individual projects.

Remuneration:

$39/hour + 4% in lieu of benefits

Responsibilities:

Plan age-appropriate activities while teaching basic pottery skills. Be flexible with
varying student skill levels within each classroom setting. Be adept at classroom
management. Good customer service skills an asset when talking with parents of
children or youth.

Qualifications:

A Bachelor of Fine Arts degree with a Ceramics specialty or the equivalent in relevant
experience is required. In order to teach at the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, a minimum
of 100 hours of teaching experience as a Ceramic Arts instructor (preferably with
children/youth) is required. First aid certificate
(First and + CPR-C) is a condition of
employment for all City of Burnaby instructors.

How to Apply:

Submit a brief cover letter and resume or professional CV by email to
brit.bachmann@burnaby.ca. Please specify which position(s) you are applying for in
your cover letter. All shortlisted candidates will be contacted by Saturday, September
16.

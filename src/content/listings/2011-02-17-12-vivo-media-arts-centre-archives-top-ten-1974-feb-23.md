VIVO Media Arts Centre Archives
Top Ten: 1974

In the early years of the Satellite Video Exchange Society, now VIVO 
Media Arts, the centre functioned as a community space for artists 
and the general public to drop in and watch videos from the library. 
Tape viewing statistics were tracked, and a list of the top ten most 
viewed titles were kept for each year from 1974 to 1990. On the last 
Wednesday of every month, we'll present an informal screening of a 
selection of these works.

This month we present our inaugural screening, featuring the top 
ten hits of 1974. 

Wednesday, February 23 - 8:00pm

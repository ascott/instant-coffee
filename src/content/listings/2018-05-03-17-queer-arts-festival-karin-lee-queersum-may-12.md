On May 12, 2-4pm, join the Queer Arts Festival for the opening of SUM
inaugural exhibition:
[http://queerartsfestival.com/event/karin-lee-queersum-opening/](http://queerartsfestival.com/event/karin-lee-queersum-opening/)

Queer-sum a Chinglish translation and play on the words Queer Love,
alludes to queer attraction that people experience, even though they
believe themselves to be straight identified " or queer-sum (sum=love).

Filmmaker Karin Lee presents three of her film/media works: a 2-channel
remix of her classic 16mm film My Sweet Peony, a fantastical drama shot in
the Dr. Sun Yat Sen Classical Chinese Gardens; Portrait of a Girl, a
documentary shot in Beijing, and Small Pleasures, a period drama set in
Barkerville BC.

An artist talk will follow on May 17, 7-9pm:
[http://queerartsfestival.com/event/karin-lee-queersum-artist-talk/](http://queerartsfestival.com/event/karin-lee-queersum-artist-talk/)
and the exhibition runs till August 18:
[http://queerartsfestival.com/queersum-show-karin-lee/](http://queerartsfestival.com/queersum-show-karin-lee/)

SUM opens as a permanent space, Canadas only queer gallery, presenting
year-round multidisciplinary exhibitions and events that further the
artistic vision of the Queer Arts Festival.
[http://queerartsfestival.com/about-sum-gallery/](http://queerartsfestival.com/about-sum-gallery/)

The address is 268 Keefer St., between Main St. and Gore Ave. The SUM
gallery is located on the 4th floor, suite 425.

Transit access:
Skytrain: Main Street-Science World or Stadium-Chinatown, bus 22 on Gore,
buses 03, 08, 19 on Main, 14, 16, 20 on Hastings

Accessibility: This location has not yet had an accessibility audit.
- There is an elevator, accessible non gendered washrooms.
- Our events are scent free.
- There is a paid parkade as part of the building until 7pm.
- ASL interpretation will be provided at events.
Please let us know if you have any requests or need more information.

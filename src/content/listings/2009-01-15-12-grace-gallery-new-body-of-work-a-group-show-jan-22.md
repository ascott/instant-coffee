New Body of Work
A Group Show
Photographs and Sculpture
at Grace-Gallery

Opening:
Thursday, January 22 8pm-12

Grace-Gallery is proud to present New Body of Work, a group show of Emily
Carr student work
The body is a diverse physical entity, it's debated about, held up to the
light, yet nobody agrees and nobody's right.
It is entrenched in everything: it is how you represent yourself, how we
see other people, and how one experiences the world.
Experience is essentially grounded in the body, and it ultimately guides
our work and thought processes.
This show touches on a few of these thoughts, visual representations and
experiences.

Featuring the work of:
Krista Bailie
Miles De Courcy
Ande Kuric
David Mcguire
Mayan Vered

Show Runs January 22 - February 12th

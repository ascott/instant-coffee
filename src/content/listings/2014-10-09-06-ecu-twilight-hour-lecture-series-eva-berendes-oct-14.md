Twilight Hour Lecture Series
Painters Talking about Painting
Eva Berendes

Tuesday October 14, 2014, 5:00 PM
Room 410B South Building

Eva Berendes’ works interrogate how we establish an object as a painting,
or a painting as an object. They examine the constituent elements of
image-making and unfold painting as an expanded field of production and a
process of layering intervals of various surfaces.

Eva Berendes has studied Painting at the Academy of Fine Arts in Munich and
the University of Arts in Berlin and completed a MA in Fine Art
(distinction) at Chelsea College of Art and Design in London, UK in 2002.
She has presented her works in numerous international solo and group
exhibitions at galleries and institutions such as Mumok Wien (2010), S1
Artspace Sheffield (2011), Hara Museum Tokyo (2011), Witte de With
Rotterdam (2011), Bundeskunsthalle Bonn (2013), Grazer Kunstverein (2013)
and CRG Gallery New York (2014). Most recently she has created a sculptural
installation as a set for a contemporary dance project by German
choreographer Sebastian Matthias. The artist has received, among others, a
DAAD scholarship for postgraduates, a Pollock-Krasner Foundation Grant and
a working grant of Stiftung Kunstfonds (2014). She is represented by Sommer
& Kohl Berlin, Jacky Strenz Frankfurt, Ancient & Modern London and CRG
Gallery New York.

Tuesday October 14, 2014, 5:00PM

Room 410B South Building, Emily Carr University

so I dont lose my silly little mind is a group exhibition featuring five artists-in-
residence at Gallery 626: Alejandra Mercedez, Kaela Ho, Lucas Gomez Cid, Nedda
May, and Veronica Yang.

The exhibition brings together works in painting, drawing, and sculpture that explore
imagined, dreamlike spaces. Through the use of experimental materials and
unconventional imagery, the artists engage with themes of absurdity, curiosity, and
play.

so I dont lose my silly little mind reflects on how wonder, silliness, and play can
serve as meaningful strategies in art-making"strategies that reconnect artists with a
sense of creative freedom and resist the loss of spontaneity often shaped by broader
cultural demands.

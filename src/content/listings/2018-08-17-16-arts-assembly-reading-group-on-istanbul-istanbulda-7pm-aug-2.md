on istanbul | istanbul'da

ARTS ASSEMBLY 
Reading Group

August 23rd, 7pm

During our tenure at PLOT, ARTS ASSEMBLY will facilitate reading groups, workshops and 
artistic research as part of our upcoming collaborative exchange project, tutma | hold. 

This reading group deals with selected texts from the reading group held in Istanbul as a 
part of the exhibition Ne Bana Sen, Ne Sana Ben. Texts selected highlight symbolism and 
notions of holding. This reading group concludes the series of four reading groups, three 
held in Vancouver and one in Istanbul each culminating to exploring different facets of 
holding.

rsvp to info@artsassembly.ca, reading packs are sent as a pdf.

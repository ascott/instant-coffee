Stories from the Edge showcases internationally renowned artists as 
part of a series of events that explore performance art and its 
relationship to identity, storytelling, theatre, activism, politics, and 
archives.

Friday, October 2, 7:30pm - An evening of performance art, Metro 
Studio Theatre. $20 tickets advance/door, 250-383-8833 to reserve.

Native American visual and performance artist James Luna, in 
collaboration with Gwich’in artist Jeneen Frei Njootli, will present a 
powerful new work Ishi: The Archive Performance. 

La Pocha Nostra troupe members Guillermo Gómez-Peña, Saul Garcia 
Lopez, and Jessica Balitrónica will present two Psycho-Magic 
performance actions The Mexican God of Corn and The Shooting 
Gallery. 

Saturday, October 3, 1:30pm - Performance and Conversation, Art 
Gallery of Greater Victoria

Amy Malbeuf will perform Ravel and then participate in RED WORDS: 
Conversation on Indigenous Performance with James Luna and Amy 
Malbeuf, facilitated by Chris Creighton-Kelly. 

Wednesday, September 30 - Artist Lectures

2:30 PM - James Luna Artist Lecture, Camosun College, Room 216, 
Young Building, Landsdowne Campus

8:00 PM - Guillermo Gómez Peña, Orion Lecture, University of Victoria, 
Room A162, Visual Arts Building.

Thursday, October 8 - 1–8 p.m. - Performance Art Workshop, 
conducted by Guillermo Gómez-Peña, Saul Garcia Lopez, Open Space 
Arts Society, contact 250-383-8833 to register.

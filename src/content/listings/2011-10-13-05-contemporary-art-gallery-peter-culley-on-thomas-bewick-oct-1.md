Peter Culley on Thomas Bewick
Saturday, October 15, 3 pm

Peter Culley provides a personal guide to the Thomas Bewick exhibition. Culley 
is a writer and poet currently living in Nanaimo, British Columbia. His blog in 
pictures and text reveals his ongoing reflections on and concern with the 
environment in which he lives and works. Places are free.

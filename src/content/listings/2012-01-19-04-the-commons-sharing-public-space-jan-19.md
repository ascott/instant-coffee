Here in North Van, and perhaps everywhere, the time has come to talk 
about 
public space! This January marks 2 years since the death of the 
National 
Maritime Centre project that promised to bring a significant 
Museum/public 
amenity to a piece of publically/municipally owned piece of North 
Vancouver 
waterfront. Since then, nothing. There was brief but significant 
backroom push 
to use the site to build a casino under the guise of something insiders 
referred 
to as the Great Canadian Concert Centre, but the public would have 
nothing to 
do with that plan, so it went away. And since there has really been little 
development. 

A citizens group called North Van Urban Forum has come into being, 
and they 
are working to build a conversation in hopes that in the end something 
thoughtful 
will emerge; something that rises out of a process that thoroughly 
engages the 
public and honours the histories of the site. Tomorrow is the first event 
of a 
series aimed at building this conversation. Come one, come all!! The 
following 
week on Jan 26, we will be hosting "Lower Lonsdale Pasts and 
Presents", a 
history exhibition created with the generous collaboration and in large 
part 
curation of the North Vancouver Museum and Archives. There will be a 
reception on January 26, 7-9pm. This also features an appreciative 
inquiry 
photo project organised by Cath Anne Ambrose. I understand she's still 
accepting photos of things people love about Lower Lonsdale, so get 
clickin'! 
(lovinlolophotos@gmail.com). The cafÃ© for contemporary art is super 
proud to 
host!

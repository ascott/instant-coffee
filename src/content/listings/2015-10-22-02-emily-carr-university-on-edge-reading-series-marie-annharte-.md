The On Edge Reading Series presents:

Marie Annharte Baker
7 pm - Thurs Oct 22, 2015
Aboriginal Gathering Place, Emily Carr University of Art + Design
1399 Johnston St, Granville Island

The public is welcome.

Author bio:

Marie Annharte Baker is an Anishinabe (Little Saskatchewan First
Nation, Manitoba) poet. The author of four poetry books—Indigena
Awry (New Star 2012), Being On the Moon (1990), Columbus Coyote
Cafe (1995) and Exercises In Lip Pointing (New Star 2003)—she also
has a book of essays, AKA, published by Capilano University Editions
in 2012. The Blue Metropolis Literary Festival awarded her an
Indigenous literary prize in 2015. Marie’s work creatively challenges
conventions and offers great insight into decolonial ways of being and
doing.

We gratefully acknowledge the Canada Council for the Arts, and the
Coast Salish peoples whose traditional unceded territories we are on.

You may have heard that the Langara College Centre for Art in Public Spaces
program has issued a Request for Qualifications for the next Artist in
Residence, 2011-12.
If you know of anyone who may be interested in applying, please forward
this message on. The deadline has been extended to May 20th, 2011.

Thanks and all the best,
Katie Eliot
Division Assistant

> The Langara College Centre for Art in Public Spaces is accepting
applications for an Artist-in-Residence for Fall 2011. The Centre, an
initiative by Langara College, is designed to inspire collaboration among
students, professional artists, and the community. It generates creative
projects through an Artist-in-Residence program, guest speakers, and
continually evolving curricula. The integration of visiting professionals,
collaborative exploration, and learning opportunities make the program
unique in Canada.

For more information on the application process visit:

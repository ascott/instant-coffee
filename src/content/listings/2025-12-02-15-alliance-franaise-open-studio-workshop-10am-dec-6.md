OPEN STUDIO: Curious about what's happening on the 4th floor of Alliance Franaise
Vancouver? Come visit our brand-new studios (2025) and meet the artists working there!
Free entry on Saturday, December 6th, during special hours (10 AM - 2 PM, subject to
change). The studios are usually closed to the public, so don't miss your chance to take a
behind-the-scenes look. Additional open studios will be scheduled in 2026. The studios are
wheelchairs accessible.

Info : December 6th, 2025. 10AM-2PM. Alliance Francaise Vancouver, 6161 Cambie St,
Vancouver. Studios (4th Floor)

=

WORKSHOP DURING OPEN STUDIO: Greeting Cards & Paint with coffee and tea, with
Nancy Byrtus. Join us during the AFV Open Studios for an art workshop with artist Nancy
Byrtus, where you'll get to explore coffee and tea painting. No traditional paint tubes this
time: just a brush dipped in a little cup of coffee, and the use of tea-bags to create unique
artworks! A nice opportunity to make and take home beautiful greeting cards for the end of
the year. So make yourself comfortable in the studios and let Nancy guide you through this
workshop, while enjoying a warm drink! RSVP required to take part in this activity, please
take 1 ticket per person.
Info : December 6th, 2025. 10:30AM-12PM. Alliance Francaise Vancouver, 6161 Cambie St,
Vancouver. Studios (4th Floor)

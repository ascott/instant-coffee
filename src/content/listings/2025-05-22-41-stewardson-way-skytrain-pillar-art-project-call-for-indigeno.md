TransLink and the City of New Westminster are commissioning eight Indigenous
artists to create large-scale digital artworks for installation on SkyTrain pillars along
Stewardson Way.
Were looking for artists who:
" Are Indigenous (First Nations, Mtis, Inuit)
" Live in or have strong ties to New Westminster
" Work in digital media (Illustrator or Photoshop)
Each selected artist will design one pillar. This is a design-only project"no
installation required. Artworks will be printed on vinyl and wrapped around the pillars
(14 ft tall).
Open to emerging and established artists. Public art experience not required.
Deadline: June 15, 2025
Fee: $3,500 per artist
$100/hour for any additional work
(No submission fees)
To apply:
Send the following to IndigenousRelations@translink.ca
" 300-word letter of intent (your interest + cultural elements youd explore)
" Up to 4 images of past work
" Artist bio (including digital media experience)
" Optional: portfolio link or socials

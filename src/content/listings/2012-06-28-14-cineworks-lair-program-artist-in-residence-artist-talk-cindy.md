2012 Local Artist In Residence (LAIR) ARTIST TALK

Cinework's LAIR Program for 2012 features the work of Cindy 
Mochizuki,
an interdisciplinary artist working in installation, audio,
performance, drawing, video, webworks and animation. Cindy's works
explore relationships between memory and history, and the 
construction
of narratives that combine both the documentary and the imagined.

The public is invited to attend this free artist talk down in
Cinework's Annex Space in Gastown, in the historic Iron Works 
Building
(235 Alexander Street). An informal open house of our facilities
including our dark room will follow.

For her residency, Cindy has been working on a new project, Shiro
Yagi, which will take the only piece of sheet music from her maternal
grandfather's archives as a starting point into her new narrative.
Shiro Yagi will have an open studio during SWARM 2012 and premiere 
as
a full length installation and performance in the Fall of 2012 at
Cineworks' Annex Space.

For more information, visit Cindy Mochizuki.com

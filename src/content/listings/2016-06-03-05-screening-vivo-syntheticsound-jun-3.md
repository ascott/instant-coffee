SyntheticSound: experimental music chronicles from the Crista Dahl Media
Library & Archive, Video Out Distribution.

Friday, June 3. 7:30pm
VIVO Media Arts Center
2625 Kaslo St.

Following two much lauded screenings in Berlin, Video Out Distribution at
VIVO Media Arts is pleased to present the local edition of SyntheticSound,
a dynamic video program featuring experimental music as its source.

The program chronicles movements in music composition and performance,
multi-media / video experimentation, technological advancements and
subculture developments with works dating from the 1960s to the present.
Rarely screened works featuring Terry Riley, Throbbing Gristle, Tunnel
Canary; multiple Canadian musicians and video artists are highlighted. The
archive's oldest title, Music With Balls (1968), will be screened in all
its live video editing glory! All titles culled from The Crista Dahl Media
Library & Archive, housed, managed and loved by VIVO Media Arts Centre.

A panel discussion to follow featuring guest speakers Lief Hall, Gary
Bourgeois and Alan Kollins. Moderated by Shauna Jean Doherty.

Program curated by Alan Kollins.

Video program runs approximately 95 minutes.

>Generic Video Art, excerpt: Androgina (Laurie McDonald, 1982. USA 01:00
min.) Generics is the name applied to no-name foods and household items.
Video art has evolved into specific genre groups which are easily
categorized. One of the represented groups (PUNK) is featured. An
electro-pop infomercial of sorts and a homage to late night television's
talking heads.

>Music For Stocking-Top, excerpt (COUM Transmissions, Cosey Fanni Tutti,
1974. UK 11:06 min.) A conceptual art performance for one camera:
synthesized music, pyramid, calculating machine, staircase, swing and two
men dress as women. At the beginning, one man wearing high stockings and
using a vibrator masturbates. Highly rare footage from the Throbbing
Gristle vaults.

>Music With Balls, excerpt (Arlo Action, Terry Riley, 1968. USA 05:22 min.)
Television studio performance; video imagery in the form of giant
translucent balls swinging in pendulous motion, featuring live electronic
music performed by Terry Riley.

>999 Live at Nashville Rooms, excerpt (Fantasy Factory, 1976. UK 05:40
min.) The band 999 is featured live in concert from this famous London punk
venue. Vintage punk moments. Memorable live video sequencing.

>The Temple of The Sacred Lady Crayon goes Percussion (Clark Nikolai, 1984.
Canada 06:53 min.) Three musicians use the kitchen and living room as an
instrument. Shot at the famous Temple of the Sacred Lady Crayon in
Saskatoon, SK. during the 1980s punk scene.

>Tunnel Canary (Eric Lorenz, 2009. Canada 37:00 min.) A feature documentary
about an improvised anarchist noise/punk band from Vancouver Canada that
formed in 1978. They performed live only 26 times before disbanding in
1983. Described as neglected outcasts on the scariest, artsiest fringes of
the Vancouver punk scene. Most punks avoided them andthey were often being
shut down from the general public. Superb footage of early 80s street
performances on Granville St. and a general hybrid documentary style that
is as much art as it is narrative.

>supa girl (Michael Venus, 2001. Canada 04:18 min.) Be seduced by the
sistas of the House of Venus as they take on the roles of all kinds of
female beauties. A businesswoman, a model, a cop - who's your supa girl?
Shot behind a pre gentri fied urban Vancouver landscape.

>Sexnoys (Mathilde Geromine, 2006. Canada 01:29 min.) Sex toys / vibrators
try their damndest be a no-wave music period piece.

>Through The Holes (Elizabeth Vander Zaag, 1981. Canada 03:00 min.) An
experimental look at the content of the video screen. A freedom resulting
from the discovery that the holes in the screen have a life of their own
beyond the often depicted soap opera, is called up in the soundtrack; voice
processed through a digital delay. 1980s video processing at its very best.

>Cybernetic Love Triangle (Brendan Baudat, 2008. Canada 02:30 min.)
Although it brings up many questions about the mating habits of underwater
cybernetic organizations, the melody is based on an ancient cybernetic love
song. It is attempt to describe synesthesia to those who cannot see music.

>Mother and Babe (Lief Hall, 2011. Canada 01:14 min.) Using 3D computer
software, Mother and Babe (on A Rocking Chair) is a kind of digital
painting which explores the iconic image of Madonna and Child. The piece
manipulates perceptions of 2 and 3D space attempting to create a sense of
familiarity through texture, form and repetitive movements. The vocal
soundscape is both human and knowable engaging in this sense of intimacy.
Yet the portrait itself remains unknown as it explores the intimacy of a
mother and child within a virtual landscape.

>It's Hard to Get in My System (Duane Linklater, 2010. Canada 05:42 min.)
The negotiation of distinct binaries is a signifi cant part of Duane
Linklater's practice. It's hard to get in my system is a continuation of
these negotiations and a gesture to connect disparate learning systems,
sound forms and perspectives. During the course of one hour with Zoe, she
asked Duane if he had any sheet music, he said no. She also said to him
several times, "its hard to get in my system."

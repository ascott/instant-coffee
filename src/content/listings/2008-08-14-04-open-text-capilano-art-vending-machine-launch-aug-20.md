Capilano Art-Vending Machine Launch

Wednesday, August 20
6:30 to 8:30 p.m.
Cafe Montemarte (4362 Main Street, Vancouver)

Inspired by Disroboto in Montreal and Outsider-art-in-a-box locally, Capilano now has its own art and 
chapbook-vending machine. The machine will make a brief sojourn off campus next week, stopping 
in briefly at Cafe Montemarte for a launch. Thereafter, the clown's head vending machine will reside 
at Capilano, Fir building, room 402. Please come out and show your support for locally-grown talent! 
There will be readings by first year Capilano students and a series of chapbooks available for a 
nickel a piece. Find out more about the Creative Writing program at Capilano!

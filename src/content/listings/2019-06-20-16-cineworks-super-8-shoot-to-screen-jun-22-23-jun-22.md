A 2 day entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and practice of 
shooting and processing Super 8 film. Film shot in this workshop on Saturday will be 
processed on Sunday, and presented in a public screening at 7:30pm.

Join artist Nisha Platzer for this weekend workshop where participants will learn a brief 
history of Super 8, shoot their very own black and white films, process by hand, and have 
the final works shown in a screening with live music. The program for both days is briefly 
presented below:

Day 1: 
Sat, June 22nd: 10am-6pm
Well learn about the format of Super 8 and see some examples before going out and 
shooting our own films.

Day 2: 
Sun, June 23rd: 10am-6pm
Screening: 7:30pm Sunday
Using photochemistry, well process the films and splice them together before the screening!

Elements covered will include:
- Principles of exposing film
- Shooting options
- Operating super 8 camera
- Film processing in photo-chemicals

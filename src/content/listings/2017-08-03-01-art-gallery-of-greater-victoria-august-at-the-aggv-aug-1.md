Enjoy art this August at the AGGV

Summertime is a great time to visit the Art Gallery of Greater Victoria (AGGV).

If you havent had a chance to check out the AGGV this summer, August is the 
perfect time to catch four popular exhibitions before they close this fall, Mirror with 
Memory, Karen Tam: With wings like clouds hung from the sky, Close to Home and 
Moving Forward by Looking Back.
You can also check out the AGGVs newest examination of Emily Carr with Picturing 
the Giants: The Changing Landscapes of Emily Carr, which runs through March 31, 
2018.

August kicks off with Admission by Donation Day on August 1. BC Day is August 7 
and the AGGV will be open from noon until 5:00 p.m. On both days curator Haema 
Sivanesan will be leading exhibition engagement tours of Karen Tam: With wings like 
clouds hung from the sky from 2:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m.

AGGV will be participating in the Integrate Arts Festivals free Art Crawl on Saturday, 
August 26, from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. As participants in the festival, the Gallery 
will be waiving admission fees and offering a range of activities. Exhibition animators 
will be on hand in Karen Tams interactive exhibition and in our Massey Sales 
Gallery, local artists from the Art Rental and Sales program will offer art 
demonstrations from 12-4 p. m. For more information visit integrate.ca

The Gallery will also be hosting the 8 x 10 Photo Rally on Saturday, August 26 from 
10:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. Inspired by our current exhibition Mirror with Memory, which 
presents photographs from the Hayashi/Kitamura/Matsubuchi photo studio (1912-
1942 Cumberland, BC). Participants will be provided a disposable black and white 
film camera and a map to find 8 way points and photograph images that reflect 8 
different themes before they return to the Gallery. Let loose your inner street 
photographer, photojournalist and studio photographer all in one event that will 
celebrate Victoria's vibrant and historic downtown. The first three participants back 
have a chance to win prizes and highlights from all of the participants photographs 
will be featured in an album on the Gallerys Facebook page after the event.
This is a free event but pre registration is required by emailing name and phone 
number to visitorservices@aggv.ca

The Summer Small Works Show & Sale is on until August 31 in the Massey Sales 
and Rental Gallery.

August offers the following exhibition engagement tours, which are included with

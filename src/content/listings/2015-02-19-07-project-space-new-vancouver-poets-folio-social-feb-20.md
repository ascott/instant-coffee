New Vancouver Poets Folio Social
FEB 20 7-10PM
PROJECT SPACE

Join Project Space, in partnership with Poetry Is Dead magazine, to
celebrate the New Vancouver Poets Folio edited by Dina Del Bucchia and
Daniel Zomparelli and published on Lemon Hound. The social is an
opportunity to connect with the poets and check out their books, which will
be available for purchase. The event runs from 7-10PM at Project Space's
studio at 2 - 236 E Pender, Vancouver, and is free and open to the public.

NVPF poets in attendance include:
mia susan amir
andrea bennett

& of course the editors:
Dina Del Bucchia
Daniel Zomparelli

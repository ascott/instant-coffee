TALKS at the Vancouver Art Gallery

Tuesday May 17, 7pm
In the Gallery

Isabelle Pauwels
THE CULTURE SERIES

The Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to present an artist talk by
Isabelle Pauwels as part of the Culture Series.

Pauwels is a Vancouver-based artist with a primary thematic focus on
narrative structures and how they shape our sensory experience.
Working primarily in video installation, Pauwelsâ€™ conceptually-based
work draws from interests in hybrid cultural forms, prosumer
production, early history of television and film, and narratives of
colonial-era exploration. She is the winner of the 2009 Brink Award
presented by the Henry Art Gallery in Seattle and the 2007 VIVA Award
presented by the Jack and Doris Shadbolt Foundation, and has exhibited
in Canada, the United States and Belgium.

The Culture Series at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents artists,
writers, critics, historians, performers and other cultural
practitioners from all disciplines who are changing the way we think
about art, creativity, culture, contemporary theory and more around
the world.

Marking the launch of the series were talks by musician and artist
Brian Eno and groundbreaking experimental American filmmaker Barbara
Hammer in January 2011. Subsequent talks have been given by
Vancouver-based and internationally renowned artist Althea Thauberger,
Mark Allen of the Los Angeles-based arts group Machine Project, and
Peter Samis, associate curator of interpretation at the San Francisco
Museum of Modern Art (SFMOMA), a leader in developing interactive
multimedia for museums. Upcoming speakers include Glenn Lewis, an
artist, educator and co-founder of the Western Front, as well as David
Wisdom, a former Canadian CBC radio personality and active
photographer.

Free with Gallery admission.

XINEMA is looking for screen and media player donations or loans for our upcoming
exhibition with Eric Tkaczyk: Im planting a garden in my screen, happening on
September 13, 2025 from 6-9pm.

Working screens of any size and type accepted (old laptops, iPhones, tv or computer
monitors, etc.). Please email info@xinema.ca for pick up or drop off before
September 6 if you have any gear you are willing to share!

Im planting a garden in my screen
(2023" present), is an ongoing series of media artworks that explore the dystopic
realities our technologically-influenced futures. Dispersed among dirt and decaying
plant life, natural and artificial worlds collide to expand dimensions of the afterlife
within the confines of cyberspace. Ghostly, 3D-rendered flowers play continuously in
motion, creating an algorithm that both acknowledges a beginning and end, while
also preserving a state of immortality. Trapped between the endless black and the
glass that contains them, these screen flowers represent a desire of our present day,
to experience moments of beauty through the mediation of the screen.

XINEMA is an ongoing series that showcases emerging and established filmmakers
within and beyond "Vancouver", BC to strengthen and preserve the experimental film
scene. xinema.ca

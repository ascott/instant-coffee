Republic Gallery is pleased to announce the upcoming solo exhibition:

Yedda Morrison | ReGenesis

Opening reception: Thursday, May 12th, 6 - 8 pm

Showing: May 12th - June 19th, 2010

ReGenesis is a series of found "action shots" of the artistic reproduction
of works found in the Hermitage in Saint Petersburg, one of the oldest and
largest collections of fine art in the world. Today, for $3800, an exact
replica of any one of the paintings in the collection can be re-painted --
sans an artist's signature -- by a team of anonymous artists, represented
in the current images only by their hands.

Writer and visual artist Yedda Morrison was born and raised in the San
Francisco Bay Area. Her books include Darkness (forthcoming from Make Now
Press, 2010), Girl Scout Nation (Displaced Press, 2008) and Crop (Kelsey
StreetPress, 2003). She has performed and exhibited her work widely. She
divides her time between San Francisco and Montreal. This is Yedda's second
solo show with Republic Gallery.

ReGenesis will be on show May 12th through June 19th with an opening
reception on Thursday, May 12th, 6-8pm. Regular gallery hours are 11am-5pm,
Wednesday through Saturday, and by appointment.

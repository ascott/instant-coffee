Algoma University invites applications for:

JOB TITLE: Studio Technician, Fine Arts 
Department

TYPE: Part-time, limited-term contract 
position
Hours will be non-regular including evenings, weekends

DEPARTMENT: Department of Fine Arts

Duties & Responsibilities:

The Print Media/3D Technician is responsible for providing in-class 
technical support to the Print Media instructor and for managing the 
day-to-day operations of the Print Media facilities and equipment. 
Other responsibilities include ensuring that a safe and healthy 
standard of studio cleanliness is maintained by monitoring student 
practices and habits and by personally assuming cleaning and 
organizing tasks. The Print Media Technician will also assist students 
in Painting, Drawing, Fundamentals, Special Topics and Advanced 
Studio Classes; assist with research into new processes and 
materials; monitor inventory supply levels, and prepare chemistry 
solutions and materials for curriculum use.

The Technician is responsible for supervising Open Studio sessions 
in the main studio on designated days and for providing support and 
technical guidance to students from all disciplines while they are 
working with power tools. 

The Technician will also work collaboratively with the faculty to 
ensure that any of the maintenance, safety or student support 
requirements that might present themselves department wide are 
met. 

The Technician will also be responsible for distributing materials for 
purchase to students and for preparing billing forms for student digital 
printing and use of other materials. Additionally the Technician will 
help faculty to prepare material and supply orders.

Minimum Qualifications:

BFA from a recognized institution or equivalent. Minimum one year 
related experience. The successful candidate should be an 
experienced printmaker and be familiar with traditional Print Media 
processes including relief printing, copper and etching, monotype, 
intaglio and photo-intaglio. An understanding of Mac OSX, Adobe 
Photoshop, large format inkjet printing and digital production 
techniques including digital-transparency output is essential.

Must have experience with most commonly used art materials and 
portable wood power tools and be able to demonstrate their safe and 
effective use.

Flexibility on work schedule and the ability to work independently.

SALARY: $15.50 per hour – 15 hours per week for 15 weeks per 
each Fall and Winter semesters

Deadline for submission of applications: August 19, 2013 or until the 
position is filled.

In accordance with Canadian immigration requirements, priority will 
be given to Canadian citizens and permanent residents. Algoma 
University encourages applications from all qualified women and 
men, members of visible minorities, Aboriginal people and persons 
with disabilities.

Qualified candidates should submit, electronically, a letter of 
application, resume and the name of three referees addressed to Dr. 
Arthur Perlini, Dean, Algoma University. Email: 
deanapps@algomau.ca

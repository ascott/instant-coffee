"Room's 35th Anniversary Party
Room is in its 35th year of publication and that calls for celebration! Room 
has been providing essential opportunities for women writers and artists to 
showcase their work for 35 years, and you've been a part of that rich history. 
Help us celebrate our accomplishments past and present as we move full 
speed ahead into our 36th volume and beyond. We are very pleased to invite 
you to join us at our 35th Anniversary Party on December 9th at 3pm at the 
Roundhouse Community Centre in Vancouver.

Join us for a late afternoon gathering featuring readings and art work displays 
by many of the contributing writers and artists from Issue 35.4: Labours. 
Edited by Lorrie Miller, Labours is the final issue of our 35th Anniversary 
Volume. The issue features an interview with prominent Canadian personality 
and Room alumnus, Eleanor Wachtel. It also includes new creative non-fiction 
by performance artist, writer, and filmmaker, Amber Dawn. This issue is filled 
with art and literature that exemplify the multiplicities of labour, from the toiling 
of typical and atypical work, to birthing, and the work of the heart.

We welcome you to come out and help us celebrate this milestone. Space is 
limited, so please RSVP to the contact below.

35th Anniversary Party Event Details

Date: Sunday, December 9th 3pm–5pm
Location: Roundhouse Community Centre in Yaletown, 181 Roundhouse 
Mews (Davie & Pacific), Vancouver
Cost: Suggested $5 minimum donation to recoup costs. A Cash bar and light 
refreshments will be provided.
RSVP: Please RSVP to news@roommagazine.com to secure your spot at 
the event."

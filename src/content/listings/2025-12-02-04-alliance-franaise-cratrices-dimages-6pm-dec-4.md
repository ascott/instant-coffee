EXHIBITION: In December 2025, Alliance Franaise Vancouver invites you to discover
Cratrices d'images, the new solo exhibition by Laurence Belzile, a canadian artist and one
of the francophone creators working daily in the AFV's new studios. Inspired by three
pioneering women artists of the 20th century, Laurence Belzile presents a series of abstract
paintings and experimental archives where research, emotion, and creation converge. Each
work opens a poetic dialogue between Laurence Belzile's artistic practice and that of the
artists who inspire her. Join us on Thursday, December 4th (evening) to celebrate the launch
of the exhibition. Open Night with the artist.

Info : Dec 4, 2025 - Jan 3, 2026. Alliance Francaise Vancouver, 6161 Cambie St, Vancouver.
Gallery (4th Floor)

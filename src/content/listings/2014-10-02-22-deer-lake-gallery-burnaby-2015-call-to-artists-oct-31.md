Deer Lake Gallery

2015 Call to Artists

Application Deadline: October 31, 2014

The Deer Lake Art Gallery is now inviting proposals for exhibitions to be
shown from February 2015 to December 2015. We welcome applications for solo
shows and joint/group shows (thematic or non-thematic). We are seeking
dynamic artists to showcase a diverse selection of all mediums.
Exhibitions change approximately every 3 weeks.

For further information and application please go to our website:

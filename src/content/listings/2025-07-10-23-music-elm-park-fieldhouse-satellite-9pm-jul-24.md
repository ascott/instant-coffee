Thursday, July 24, 9pm-11:30pm
Elm Park Fieldhouse (5837 Larch St)

SATELLITE is a free, outdoor ambient music concert and stargazing series in Elm
Park. All ages are welcome. Organized by Patchbay Neighborhood Sound Lab, we
gather in the green space between the Elm Park Fieldhouse and the tennis courts to
explore the night sky with telescopes against a backdrop of gentle, ambient music.
Audiences are encouraged to bring a picnic blanket, some extra layers of clothing, and
food or drinks.

Performances begin at sunset and end at astronomical dusk.

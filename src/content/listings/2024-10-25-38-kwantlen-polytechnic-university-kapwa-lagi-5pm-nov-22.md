Kapwa Lagi! An Exhibition of KPU Filipino Artists from the BFA program curated by Vin
Cuadra
November 18-29
Opening Reception: November 22nd 5-7pm
Arbutus Building, Kwantlen Polytechnic University, 12666 72nd Avenue, Surrey, BC

Kapwa Lagi! is an exhibition curated and organized by a current Bachelor of Fine Arts
(BFA) with a Minor in Anthropology student from Kwantlen Polytechnic University and
emerging
Filipino-Canadian featured artist Vin Cuadra with other Filipino-Canadian
emerging artists from the BFA program ranging from 3rd and 4th year advanced studio
students to alumni. The exhibition name derives from the Tagalog words Kapwa and
Lagi. While there is no direct English translation of Kapwa, the term refers to the
Filipino core value of interpersonal identity despite a persons background through a
shared community of various experiences and Lagi means always. The rough
translation refers to the concept that a person is always welcome in a community despite
the experiences and differences that each one has to face. Kapwa Lagi! is the first
minority-based art exhibition and the first Filipino community project for a wider audience
at KPU.

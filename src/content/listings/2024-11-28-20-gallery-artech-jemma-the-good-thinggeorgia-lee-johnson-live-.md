A double-bill songwriting showcase coming to Vancouver:

Head to The Gallery @ Artech on Thursday, December 5, for a cozy evening of
songs featuring the original tunes and stories of Georgia Lee Johnson (Squamish)
and Jemma & the Good Thing (Cortes Island)

Cortes Island-based Jemma Hicken (Jemma & the Good Thing) weaves together
tender lyrics, funny storytelling, and catchy sing-a-longs to create a feel-good folk
show. Drawing on the
humour and sentimentality of John Prine and Dan Reeder, the
innocence of Ingrid Michaelson, and the fire of Ani DiFranco, you can find Jemma
touring around BC and AB in 2024.

Dancing between deep self-reflection and playfulness, Georgia Lee Johnson's songs
weave intimacy and belonging where there was previously conflict and unease.
Inspired by the female folk singer-songwriters of the past (Joni Mitchell and Joan
Baez) and present (Anais Mitchell and Adrianne Lenker), her style blends more
traditional folk sounds with imaginative instrumentation and arrangements.

A concert not to miss!

Date: December 5
Venue: The Gallery @ Artech
336 E 1st Ave Vancouver BC
Doors at 6:30pm, music at 7pm.

Sunday, November 13, 1 pm

PRESENTATION HOUSE GALLERY
PANEL DISCUSSION

EXPOSED: Voyeurism and Photography.
Perspectives on the invasive camera and voyeurism, ethical issues in 
contemporary photography, surveillance as an aesthetic strategy, and 
power relations between photographers and human subjects. With 
Clint Burnham, Catherine Soussloff, Shep Steiner, Althea Thauberger.

/////////////////////////////////

Clint Burnham is a writer and culture critic who teaches in the 
Department of English at Simon Fraser University.

Catherine Soussloff is Professor and Head of the Department of Art 
History, Visual Art and Theory at the University of British Columbia 
whose most recent book is about portraiture.

Shep Steiner has written extensively on photography and currently 
teaches at Emily Carr University.

Althea Thauberger is a visual artist based in Vancouver whose widely 
exhibited works often involve social interactions.

More details at www.presentationhousegallery.org

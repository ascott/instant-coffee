Yactac is pleased to present Room of Her Own, new works by Setareh Yasan at our 
new location at #5-115 Pender St in Vancouver Chinatown.

The new body of work by Setareh Yasan explores her ongoing interest in the 
fundamental existential quandaries that individuals face during different stages of their 
being. In Room of her Own, Yasan uses her sculptures as props to stage the 
psychological and emotional state of a fictional character who is struggling to adapt to 
her ever changing body. Room of her Own is specifically made for Yactacs new location 
in Chinatown.

Setareh Yasan works and lives in Vancouver. Yasan directed a residency program for 
artists and writers in Ferndale, Washington; she was one of the selected artists at 
Beyond Spectacle Residency at the Simon Fraser University. Her work has been 
exhibited at Axis Mundi Gallery, Two Rivers Art Gallery, AHVA Gallery, Satellite Gallery 
and Access Gallery in British Columbia, Canada and Siks Art Center in Siks, Sweden.

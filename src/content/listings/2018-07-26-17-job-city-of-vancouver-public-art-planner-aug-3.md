Position: Social Planner II (Public Art-Civic Program)

Application Close: August 3, 2018

Business Unit/Department: Arts, Culture and Community Services

Affiliation: CUPE 15 Non Pks

Employment Type: Reg. P/T Benefits

Position Start Date: August 20, 2018

Salary Information: Pay Grade GR-031: $47.82 to $56.57 per hour

Main Purpose and Function

This position is responsible for developing a variety of public art
projects and programs; public art planning and administrative duties,
including preparing reports, spreadsheets and documents as required for
program operations. The incumbent will plan public art processes in
consultation with other civic departments; develop public art
opportunities; initiate and manage public art projects from artist call to
documentation. Liaison with artists, civic departments, other government
and outside agencies will be required. An employee of this class may be
assigned extensive research assignments and will be influential in
developing and implementing new policies and programs. Work is performed
with considerable independence and latitude to exercise individual judgement.

Specific Duties/Responsibilities

Monitoring Civic planning processes to assess the public art potential
of new civic developments, buildings or infrastructure projects
Creating public art plans for civic planning and development projects,
ensuring departmental and community consultation and liaison
Initiating artist calls and managing artist selection processes. The
position will contract artists, oversee art project fabrication,
installation, dedication, documentation, registration and maintenance
Maintain liaison as needed with artists, consultants, civic
departmental staff and others
Assist in developing and implementing policies and programs relating to
public art
Prepare related reports and meet with officials of other city
departments such as Planning, Parks, and Engineering Services
Draft and present reports to the Public Art Committee; make
presentations and answer questions at public meetings; answer public
inquiries and correspond with the public, other civic and outside agencies
and perform related work as required

Key Contacts and Reporting Relationships

This position reports to a Senior Planner.

Minimum Position Requirements

Education and Experience:

University graduation, preferably at a Masters level in Fine Arts or
Cultural Planning sector, with considerable (5-10 years) experience, or a
combination of education and relevant experience.

Knowledge, Skills and Abilities:

Demonstrated administrative experience, preferably with public sector
public art programs
Demonstrated experience managing staff and consultants
Demonstrated experience in concluding complex planning processes
Knowledge of contemporary public art planning and development issues
Demonstrated experience working with artists to create or present
artworks in public or gallery and museum contexts is preferred
Writing and communication skills and demonstrated ability to
communicate art to broad audiences and communities
Knowledge of contemporary art and artists, and demonstrated engagement
with contemporary issues in art practice
Knowledge of public art resources, including artists, art institutions,
educational institutions, and other infrastructure supports
Knowledge of civic governmental structure, and development processes
Make presentations and answer questions at public meetings; answer
public inquiries and correspond with the public, other civic and outside
agencies and perform related work as required
Excellent ability to work within a team environment and within defined
timelines

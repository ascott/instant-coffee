Call for Applications
THE LEEWAY STUDIO RESIDENCY 
Spring Term: January - April 2015

Emily Carr Continuing Studies invites Emily Carr alumni* to submit 
applications for a 4 month Studio Residency in the Leeway.

The Leeway is a multi-disciplinary resourced studio space for artists and 
designers. It is a shared, open studio space suitable for those who are 
seeking to forge a sense of community while cultivating their creative 
practice and exploring new techniques and processes. Artists and 
designers in The Leeway work alongside other makers and learn from 
experienced technicians and instructors through demonstrations and 
workshops.

Eligibility*
This residency is for emerging artists or designers with an active practice 
and online presence (blog, website or social media). Residents must 
commit to working in the studio for a minimum of 12 hours a week, as 
well as participate in a community outreach activity such as an artist talk, 
workshop or exhibition during the residency term.

This residency includes: 

- unlimited studio access during University hours from January â€“ April 
2015
- access to a range of professional equipment, tools and basic art 
materials 
- technical support and resources for a wide range of disciplines 

Please note the residency does not include travel, accommodation, 
production costs, artist fees or access to Emily Carr facilities beyond the 
Leeway [ie: no shop access (wood, metal, print)].

How to Submit
To submit an application for the Spring 2015 Studio Residency, please 
include the following information in a single PDF file:

- your name + contact info
- CV
- a short description of your proposed project and/or activities to be 
pursued (300- 500 words)
- a link to your online portfolio or blog

Deadline: November 21, 2014
Submit to: theleeway@ecuad.ca

*Eligible alumni must hold an Undergraduate Degree, Graduate Degree 
or Essentials Certificate from Emily Carr University of Art + Design. 
University staff and full-time faculty, as well as previous Leeway Studio 
Residency recipients are not eligible to apply.

More information: http://www.ecuad.ca/programs/cs/theleeway

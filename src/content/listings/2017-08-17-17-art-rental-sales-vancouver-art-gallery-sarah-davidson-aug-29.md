Free and open to the public

Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist talk with Sarah 
Davidson in the Art Rental & Sales showroom Tuesday, August 29 at 7PM.

Sarah Davidsons investigation of the fragment aims to excavate both its art 
historical narratives as well as its quotidian symbolic functions. Making reference to 
turn of the century comics, art historical drawings and household crafts, the work 
suggests possibilities born of rearranging parallel, underrepresented cultural 
histories.

Davidson holds a BFA from Emily Carr University of Art + Design, and will begin an 
MFA at the University of Guelph this fall

Art Rental & Sales is a not-for-profit program run by the Vancouver Art Gallery. 
Supporting our local arts community since 1952.

PORTAL POP-UP GALLERY

407 W. Cordova St., Vancouver BC

presents

BILIANA VELKOVA
Paper Flowers
Opening and Performance:
Friday, March 8 at 6pm

Artwork on display in window gallery:
March 8 - 31, 2024

www.bilianavelkova.ca
@missbiliana

From surreal dreamscapes, to post-industrial spaces, Biliana Velkovas work conflates
opulence, folklore and kitsch. The glossy images are indicative of her fascination with
popular culture and storytelling, creating a spectacle from everyday encounters and
shifting realities. Through her Eastern European lens, Velkova inserts a
magical
realism narrative by presenting alternative ways of viewing and interpreting a post-
collapse reality.

Velkova will present two paintings from her series, Paper Flowers. The work
encompasses colourful, textured and magical spaces featuring imaginary plants and
mythological birds based on Slavic folklore symbolizing rebirth and renewal.

Biliana Velkova Biography
Originally from Sofia, Bulgaria, Biliana Velkova is based in Vancouver. Her practice
incorporates painting, drawing and performance to explore the significance of diaspora,
folklore and social identity. She has an MFA from the University of Saskatchewan and a
BFA from Concordia University.

Time Was Water, a collaborative performance by Martina Govednik will be presented at
the opening of Paper Flowers.

Martina Govednik Biography
The eclectic, multi-talented singer and pianist Martina Govednik studied classical piano
at the University of Belgrade, before moving to Montreal to obtain Master's in Solo
Voice at the
McGill Schulich School of Music and has since been a presence on stages
in Canada and Europe. She regularly performs locally in classical solo-recitals as well
as with the Vancouver Chamber Choir and the Vancouver Opera Chorus. Martina is
also devoted to cultivating and reviving Serbian traditional music through various style
cross-overs.

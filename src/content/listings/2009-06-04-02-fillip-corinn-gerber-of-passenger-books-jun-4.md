FILLIP EVENTS: 
Corinn Gerber of Passenger Books
Thursday, June 4, 2009 at 8pm

Linda Herzog, photograph from Birmingham Istanbul Zürich, 2005.

Fillip is very excited to present a talk with publisher and bookseller Corinn Gerber this Thursday, 
June 4, at 8pm. In addition to past stints at Verlag der Buchhandlung Walther König (Cologne), 
Pro qm (Berlin), and the CCA Bookstore (Montréal), Gerber has run Passenger Books since 2005 
— a highly eclectic publishing house based in Zürich and Berlin focussing on small-run artist 
books. Recent publications include Vier5, Best Company (2009), Danna Vajda, New Directions in 
Curatorial Practices (2008), Linda Herzog, Mihriban (2007), and David Shrigley, Worried Noodles 
(The Empty Sleeve) (2005).

Admission is free, but given space limitations, visitors are encouraged to arrive early to guarantee 
a seat. Please buzz or call 604.781.4417 for access.

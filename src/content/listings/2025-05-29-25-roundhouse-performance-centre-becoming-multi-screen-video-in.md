BECOMING
An Immersive Multi-Screen Video Installation

Roundhouse Performance Centre, June 4 +5

Becoming is a multi-screen video installation that explores the human experience of
memory and impermanence. Conceived by Luciana Freire DAnunciao, with the
assistance of Marco Esscer, and sound score by Matthew Tomkinson, the work
features the Roundhouse Community Dancers.

Through four simultaneous video projections, Becoming fragments the self, time,
and space, evoking the elusive and dream-like nature of memory: non-linear,
fractured, and interwoven with fiction. Dance improvisations and original poetry,
developed collectively with the dancers, are layered into a rich soundscape,
enveloping visitors in a multisensory, immersive environment.

The installation will be open on June 4 & 5 from 3:00-6:00 PM inviting visitors to
enter, linger, and experience the work at their own pace.

We will also be hosting two special events:

June 4 7:00 PM Opening Reception + Q&A " Join us for an opening night
gathering with snacks, refreshments, and a conversation with the artists about the
creative process behind the work.

June 5 6:30 PM " Dance Workshop " Led by Luciana and Marco, this
participatory workshop will share dance scores used in the creation of the
installation, offering insight into the collaborative process. Open to all levels and
abilities.

Roundhouse Performance Centre
181 Roundhouse Mews, Vancouver

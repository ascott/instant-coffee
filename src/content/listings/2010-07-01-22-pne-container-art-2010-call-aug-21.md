exhibition
opportunity for artists.

Where, What, and When

PNE Container Art Show is unique multi-disciplinary
venue/gallery housed in
12 - 20 foot shipping containers which will be set up in the
Spirit Plaza
area on the grounds.
What
The 17 day show will be open-themed. The jury will select work
that
reflects the diversity of Vancouver artists. Who will see your art?
It will
be up during the PNE Fair and we are anticipating to draw over
1 million
people over the 17 day fair due to our 100 year anniversary
celebration!

August 21st to September 6th, 2010

PNE CONTAINER ART 2010

SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS: We are looking to select works
based on the
Quality, the fit with the location and logistical limitations.
Before
submitting a project we strongly recommend you review other
examples of
this type of installation at www.containerart.org.
Set up date is the week of Aug 16th. The artwork will be
installed by the
artists from 9 am to 11 pm at the PNE. All sales will be directed
to
artists and any art sold during PNE will not be removed during
the run of
the show. Artwork will be insured during the entire duration of
the show
and security will be onsite.
Space for each artist selected will be a 20ft by 10 ft container
with a
duplex outlet. Post the event the Container must be returned to
its
original condition with no damages, painting, or holes on any
surface.

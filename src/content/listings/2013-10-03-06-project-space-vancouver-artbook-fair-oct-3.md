Vancouver Art/Book Fair: The only international art book fair in Canada

The only event of its kind in Canada, the Vancouver Art/Book Fair on October 
5 and 6 will bring a plethora of books, magazines, zines, printed ephemera 
and other experimental forms of publication to the Vancouver Art Gallery 
Annex.

Part of a network of art book fairs taking place around the globe—such as the 
NY Art Book Fair, Miss Read in Berlin and the Tokyo Art Book Fair—VA/BF 
simultaneously puts Vancouver artists on an international stage and brings 
artist publishing from around the world to this city. 

At a time when speculators bemoan "the death of print," artists—aided by 
digital modes of production and distribution—are reinventing the notion of "the 
book" and producing an unprecedented amount of material falling into the 
broad categorization of "artists' publishing." 

Examples range from OCW Magazine's latest "issue," which consists of a 
series of in-person engagements—the first of which will be a performance by 
award-winning Vancouver writer Amber Dawn on October 5 at 4pm—to 
traditional paper and ink publications created by publishers who 
simultaneously operate graphic design studios—such as OWT Creative 
(Manchester) and la silueta ediciones (Bogotá), who present at 3pm Saturday 
and 3pm Sunday, respectively.

Spanning all four floors of the Annex, VA/BF includes three rooms of 
publishers sharing and selling their wares, as well as performances, panels, 
workshops, artist talks, installations and more.

Event Overview

Project Space presents the second annual Vancouver Art/Book Fair, a two-
day festival of artists’ publishing that features nearly one hundred local, 
national and international publishers of books, magazines, zines, printed 
ephemera and digital or other experimental forms of publication, as well as on-
site programs, performances and installations.

Free and open to the public, VA/BF is the only international art book fair in 
Canada and one of only two on the West Coast. Launching with a Reception 
at Project Space at 8pm on Friday, October 4, VA/BF will take place from 
12pm to 5pm on October 5 and 6 at the Vancouver Art Gallery.

VA/BF will also produce Artists’ Books Weekend, a parallel series of events 
hosted by a variety of publishers, artist-run centres and independent curators 
in spaces around Vancouver.

VA/BF has been made possible through the generosity of the following 
sponsors: Vancouver Art Gallery, Georgia Straight, City of Vancouver, 
Downtown Vancouver BIA, C Magazine, Scout, Discorder, SAD Mag, 
Estampa, Publishing @ SFU, Modo, Paper Pusher and Fillip.

Please join us this Thursday (August 15th, 4-6 pm) for the opening of the exhibition
exhibition Destiny hope despair alistair by Drew Spielvogel, with an accompanying
text by Phillip Pyle.

Drew Spielvogel was born and raised in southwest Michigan. He currently lives and
works in central Pennsylvania. He graduated with a Painting BFA from RISD in 2024.
He recently participated in a group presentation at NADA New York with Afternoon
Projects and has exhibited work at the Brown University Granoff Center and RISD
Gelman Gallery. In 2023, he attended the KuBA: Kulturbanhof residency in Klein-
Warnow, Germany. Painting, for him, mirrors a personal struggle to create meaning
and
emotional resolution out of detritus; the final painting is the result of countless
failed attempts at solving problems he intentionally or unintentionally creates in the
work via risk-taking and improvisation. He describes the end paintings as poor
objects which contain hazy accumulations of felt and recollected feeling over time.

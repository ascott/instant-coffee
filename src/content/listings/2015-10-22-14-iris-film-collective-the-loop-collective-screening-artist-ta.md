The Loop Collective Screening & Artist Talk
Oct 24-25, 2015

Our latest instalment of the Collective Unconcious series brings
Toronto’s Loop Collective to Vancouver! This is a free screening and
artist talk.

Iris Film Collective and Cineworks are excited to present films by the
Loop Collective in celebration of the Toronto-based experimental film
collective’s 20th anniversary. This selection of works generates a series
of interrelated conversations between purity and hybridity, nature and
technology, matter and memory, rhythm and stillness and spans a
diversity of forms, including 35mm, 16mm, Super-8 and HD video. Full
programme here.

Also, to celebrate their forthcoming 20th anniversary, they will be
launching a new, limited edition monograph on the works of the
collective, featuring critical essays by Kelly Egan, Izabella Pruska-
Oldenhof, R. Bruce Elder, Kathryn Elder, a historical chart by John
Porter, and artist pages from Loop’s current members. Alongside a
recently published DVD, Selected Works by the Loop Collective, this
book offers a significant glimpse into Loop’s history and its place within
Canada’s experimental film and video communities.
Screening: Saturday, October 24, 7:30pm
1131 Howe - Cineworks Studio (enter via alley)

Artist Talk: Sunday, October 25, 2:30pm
Falaise Park Fieldhouse, 3434 Falaise Ave & Worthington Dr.

Idealverein
Mike Bourscheid

JANUARY 22, 23 & 24, 7PM

Idealverein is a performance by Mike Bourscheid with choreography by Justine A. Chambers.
The event unfolds
as a set of improvised movements that make up a game of gestures, actions,
relationships, commitments, questions and moods of six players. The stage is a field of play and
the rules are dictated by the costumes which determine how the players can move and with
whom they are allied, working together to form allegiances or switching sides at a whim!

Team 1: Alison Denham, Ralph Escamillan, Ed Spence
Team 2: Kate Franklin, Germaine Koh, Erika Mitsuhashi

Tickets: $15

An artist talk will be presented on January 25 at 2pm and is free to attend.

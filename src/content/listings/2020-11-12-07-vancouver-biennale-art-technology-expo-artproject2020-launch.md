Wednesday is the official launch for #ArtProject2020 - the Vancouver Biennale Art &
Technology Expo!

Tune in at 9AM Wednesday Pacific Time to hear Opening Remarks by Vancouver
Biennale Founder & Artistic Director, Barrie Mowatt, and Artist/Founder of ArtProject.io
Jessica Angel. Catch the live stream on YouTube or Facebook. Register for a free event
pass at www.artproject.io for the full interactive experience.

The expo will run from November 11th to 15th and feature over 80 international
speakers
and 40 events offering accessible information and educational resources for
digital art. Experience as many events as you like during the expo and connect with
industry leaders.

Everyone with a personal or professional interest in art and technology, including
curators, galleries, museums, artists, collectors, innovators, experience designers, and
futurists will find the expo fascinating and is invited to attend.

Register Free

Over 40 virtual events, experience as many as you like.

Panel Discussions, Artist Talks, Keynote Speakers: Innovators, curators, legal experts,
and artists working at the leading edge of digital art will cover a wide range of topics.
Speakers include Whitney Museum, Victoria & Albert Museum, Christies, Foundation
for Art and Blockchain, SuperRare, and Art in America.

Learning: Learn how to use open-source and accessible innovative tools to create,
monetize, and collect digital art. Workshops are integrated with various blockchain
projects to drive adoption
through experience.

Activities: A Crypto-Art Puzzle will drop clues every day of the event, and the Digital Art
Battle will challenge artists to draw live. Participate to win rewards! Or, take a Virtual
Gallery Tour through the Metaverse, where gallery owners will share the inspirations
behind their virtual spaces.
Tip: after registration, download the virtual event app on your phone to access special
features such as notifications to selected events and exclusive chat channels with
industry leaders.

Media

Interviews with keynote speakers can be arranged.

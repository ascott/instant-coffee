Kara-Lis Coverdale
with Immix
February 22,2025 | 8 PM
Pacific Spirit United Church (2195 West 45th Ave)
https://www.newmusic.org/events/kara-lis-coverdale

Vancouver New Music presents musician and composer Kara-Lis Coverdale.
Creating
immersive and layered compositions within unique sonic landscapes, Coverdale will be
performing new work on the Pacific Spirit United Churchs magnificent Casavant
Frres pipe organ, the largest of its kind in the city. Coverdale has worked with artists
such as David Sutton (aka LXV) on their collaborative album Sirens, and Tim Hecker
on Love Streams, Virgins and Konoyo. She has also written music with the artist and
writer Kara Crabb for several theatre productions. An opening set will be performed by
Immix, a new solo project by Robyn Jacob featuring sonic experimentations with
natural and synthesized voice through vocal harmonizer, MIDI keyboard and pipe
organ.

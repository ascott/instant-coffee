Vancouver Art Gallery | 

Special Event | David Wisdom, Summer Slideshows

July 29th, 7pm
In the Gallery, 4th Floor

Former CBC radio personality and photographer David Wisdom hosts 
another night of his legendary slideshows. A community of images with 
musical accompaniment, featuring slideshows by:

David Wisdom
Connie Kuhns
Hank Bull
Scout Upex
James O’Mara
Erica Zacharias
Dennis Ha
Sophia Burke

Beverages will be served. Music by Nick Wisdom. Doors/bar open at 
6:15pm, Event begins at 7:00pm.
Free for Vancouver Art Gallery Members and students. $10 for Non-
members. 

Tickets available online at slideshows.eventbrite.ca or at the membership 
desk on the night of the event, starting at 5:30pm.

Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby St., Vancouver BC V6Z 2H7

KARIN JONES
Artist Talk

ECUAD
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC
in the Reliance Theatre 

NOV. 1, 11:00-12:15 PM

Karin Jones' work addresses African-Canadian identity and its relationship to the 
histories of colonialism and slavery. Her work is held in the permanent collections of the 
Royal Ontario Museum and the National Ornamental Metal Museum (Memphis, TN). 
She is an instructor and Department Head of Jewellery Art & Design at Vancouver 
Community College.

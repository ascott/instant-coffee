Planner Specialist 
Architecture / Public Art 

The City of Kelowna seeks a talented and committed individual to lead a 
variety of community projects within our multi-disciplinary infrastructure 
planning team. With your substantial knowledge in the principles of 
architecture, urban design and sustainable building practices, you will have 
the opportunity to show leadership in planning public buildings and civic 
spaces that support the vision of a great and sustainable mid-sized City. 
With a university degree in Architecture or a related discipline, bring your 
considerable experience in urban design, architectural programming and 
design management to our City planning team. As an experienced 
communicator with highly developed research, project management, and 
analytical skills, you will lead project scoping workshops, charrettes, liaise 
with stakeholders and community groups, prepare reports and make 
presentations at public meetings, committees and City Council. A high level 
of tact and diplomacy is required and strong interpersonal skills are 
fundamental. Experience with public arts programs, facility programming, 
building forensics and/or GIS analysis would be beneficial. Candidates must 
be eligible for professional membership in their applicable profession such as 
the Architectural Institute of British Columbia. 
We offer career/professional development, progressive employment 
practices, and an “earned day off program” along with flexibility in hours. 
This is a CUPE bargaining unit position and offers a salary range of 
$2,316.95 to $2,660.56 bi-weekly, along with a competitive benefits 
package. These wage rates will end on December 31, 2009 and a new rate 
will be established upon completion of collective bargaining with CUPE. 

Please forward your resume quoting Competition No. 1847-09 including a 
current copy of your driver’s abstract, copies of relevant credentials and a 
1-3 cover letter demonstrating how your qualifications match the above 
criteria by May 19, 2009 
Apply to: apply@kelowna.ca or visit our website www.kelowna.ca 
Applicants not contacted within three weeks of the competition closing date 
are thanked for their interest

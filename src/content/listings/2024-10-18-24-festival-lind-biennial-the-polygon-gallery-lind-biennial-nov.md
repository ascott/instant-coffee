The Polygon Gallery: Lind Biennial

Nov. 9, 2024"Feb. 2, 2025

The Polygon Gallery presents the Lind Biennial, a collective exhibition of works from
five artists shortlisted for the newly revamped Philip B. Lind Emerging Artist Prize:
Casey Wei, Dion Smith-Dokkie, Karice Mitchell, Mena El Shazly, and Parumveer
Walia. They were selected from a longlist of more than 60 nominees by a panel of
esteemed international jurors: Art Institute of Chicago Curator Grace Deveny;
acclaimed contemporary artist Brian Jungen; and Hammer Museum Curator Aram
Moshayedi. The gallery recently announced a $1 million endowment from the Lind
family, which secures the future of the award for decades to come and will increase
the prize amount to $25,000 " making it the countrys largest accolade
dedicated to
supporting emerging visual artists. The winner of the prize will be announced on Jan.
23, 2025.

Admission to The Polygon Gallery is by donation, courtesy of BMO Financial Group.

Employment Opportunity 
Associate Director (Full Time)

Starting Wednesday February 5, 2014 
28 hours/week on a flexible schedule 
Remuneration: $22.50 gross 
Deadline for Applications: January 15, 2014

Job Description:
- Oversee financial and administrational operations and planning of the 
organisation including fundraising and development in collaboration 
with Executive Director, Board of Directors, and staff

- Co-curate and assist in development and coordination of membership 
and donor drives

- Co-act as liaison between Board of Directors and staff for all 
managerial questions regarding operative administration, budgets, and 
financial planning

- Direct development and oversee implementation of yearly budgets, 
Deficit Reduction and Business Plan in collaboration with ED, Board 
and staff and oversee their implementation and control

- Manage year-end reports and audits

- Manage insurances, tax-redemptions, benefits, etc.

- Plan, manage and oversee yearly inventory count in collaboration 
with shop manager

- Manage day-to-day financial details in collaboration with staff 
including scheduling and execution of payments of artists´ and 
publishers´ fees from inventory and projects

- Assist in the development and control of project budgets for 
programming, publishing, and travel

- Assist with grants and other funding applications and oversee their 
timely reporting

- Develop and maintain grant applications and reporting schedule, 
oversee collecting and allocation of receipts for these

- Maintain financial information and fill in CADAC financial form sheets 
for reports

- Co-coordinate calendar of off-/on-site projects and respective staff 
scheduling, including leaves in collaboration with ED and all staff

- Co-develop back-end of new website, adjustments to existing 
inventory management system and QuickBooks in collaboration with 
website project manager, ED and all staff

The candidate will have related work experience. The job requires 
strong organisational and managerial skills, initiative, and interpersonal 
skills in dealing and coordinating with other staff, affiliate persons and 
collaborators.

Friday, October 19 - Sunday, October 21 

ECU, various venues

Join us for the Vancouver Art Book Fair (VABF) October 19-21. Canada's first and 
longest running art book fair takes place on campus in the Ron Burnett Library + 
Learning Commons, the Michael OBrian Exhibition Commons and READ Books.

All events are free and open to the public. The Emily Carr community is also invited to 
the Members Preview on Thursday evening from 6 pm to 8 pm.

Additionally, from October 15 to October 21, VABF will also host Artists' Book Week, an 
open platform for artists, curators, collectives and others to present events that celebrate 
the art of publishing. 

These events are free and open to the public. Be sure to visit VABF for complete details 
on all events.

Intro to Handbuilding Functional Ware
Sep. 18, 6:30 p.m. - Oct. 09, 9:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada
In this 3-week teaser with Emma Canning (aka Dirt Spindle), you will learn traditional 
hand-building techniques in order to make functional objects such as vases, bowls, and 
mugs. Basic surface design techniques, including using decorative slips, will also be 
covered. Participants will make pieces over the first 2 classes, and apply underglazes 
and/or glaze in the final class. So come with your wacky ideas and let Emma show you 
how to make it a reality! 
Open to all levels and age 10+. Classes on Sep 18, 25, and Oct 9 from 6:30-9:00pm.
Pieces will be fired and ready for pick-up 2 weeks from the end of the course.
$150 Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our 
FAQ.

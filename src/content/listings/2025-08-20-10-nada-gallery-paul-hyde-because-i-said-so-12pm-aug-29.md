PAUL HYDE - Because I Said So

OPENING RECEPTION: FRIDAY, AUGUST 29 6-9pm

SHOW RUNS: Saturday, August 30 - Sunday, August 31, 12:00-5:00pm

NADA GALLERY - 3553 Main Street - Back alley entrance

Chernoff Fine Art is pleased to announce Because I Said So, an exhibition of work
by Vancouver artist and musician Paul Hyde. Paul, with no formal artistic training,
has been working in collage for over 20 years, perfecting his techniques with resin,
rhinestones and found imagery. He searches for collage materials in magazines and
books, collecting, then sifting through the images until inspiration strikes.

Paul Hyde was born in Yorkshire, England. He is a Juno award-winning
singer/songwriter, best known for his work as lead singer and lyricist with eighties
bands Payola$ and Rock & Hyde.
Hyde, who lives in Vancouver, is no longer touring in a band, now describes himself
as a full time visual artist who makes the occasional record. Most day you can find
him happily locked away in his studio, creating new works.

ARTIST STATEMENT:
After 20 years of collage I'm still having fun with anthropomorphism. I have recently
been exploring surrealist themes and the dark history of the church in Canada.

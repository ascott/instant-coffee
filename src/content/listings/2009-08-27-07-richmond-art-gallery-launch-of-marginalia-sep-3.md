The Richmond Art Gallery is pleased to invite you to a special event, Thursday, September 3, 
2009 from 7 - 9 pm. The Gallery is hosting a launch party to celebrate the catalogue publication 
for MARGINALIA (made possible through the generous support of the Canada Council for the 
Arts). MARGINALIA: Getting Out of the House was the wildly popular exhibition resulting from the 
four year-long, coast-to-coast correspondence between Margaret Dragu and Pamela Hall, held 
last autumn at the Richmond Art Gallery. Margaret Dragu (aka Lady Justice) will perform with 
Vancouver band Payday Millionaire in celebration of the 40th anniversary of John Lennon and 
Yoko Ono’s Bed-In for Peace.

The event will also host the Richmond Women's Centre which will be selling Dragu-designed 
stationery cards, and introduce new Gallery Board Members and Gallery Director, Lynn Beavis to 
the public.

What: MARGINALIA Catalogue launch, performance by Lady Justice (aka Margaret Dragu) and 
Payday Millionaire 
When: Thursday, September 3, 2009 from 7 - 9 pm 
Cost: Free Admission. The catalogue will be on sale for a special event price of $4 
Where: Richmond Art Gallery

THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 8th, 7:00 pm | EXHIBITION, PUBLIC 
WORK, SCREENING SWARM 2011 Join us for the opening of Neil 
Beloufa's exhibition, Glenn Lewis' public work and a screening of 
recently restored video works from our archive.

Exhibition
Neil Beloufa: As Far As We Know
September 09 – Nov 05, 2011

Western Front has commissioned a new moving image work by 
Paris based artist Neil Beloufa for his autumn solo exhibition. Shot 
in Vancouver over the summer, this new work will be embedded 
within an installation constructed from readily available and found 
materials. For this new video work, viewers are asked to think 
outside of their own initial assumptions. The images seem 
commonplace at first and then quickly become more intricate and 
ominous. Filmed completely from above a Vancouver suburb, 
Beloufa seamlessly plays between the aesthetics of several film 
styles, while the narration of various voices tries to construct 
meaning in the image. This juxtaposition permits us to free-fall 
through understanding, in a world without a horizon.

Public Work
Glenn Lewis: TAXONOMIES
September 09 – Dec 16, 2011

Glenn Lewis’ TAXONOMIES is a public work that was created for the 
exterior and interior of the Western Front building. It consists of six 
planted coniferous trees in newly thrown ceramic pots, wooden 
signs and a large format photographic study. TAXONOMIES 
incorporates several elements that are characteristic of Lewis’ 
established 50-year art practice. Cumulatively in this new work, 
these elements come together to draw attention to the displacement 
of the lumber industry as a primary industry in the region, and as a 
consequence, the decline of wooden architecture in the city of 
Vancouver. As one of the founding members of the Western Front in 
1973, Glenn Lewis’ has personally been involved in the centre and 
the dynamic surrounding of the Mount Pleasant community.

SWARM Screening
One Night Only

A selection of recently restored and digitized works from our archive, 
including Gathie Falk's Red Angel (1977) and General Idea's 
Cornucopia (1988). This programme is part of Western Front's 
renewed focus on the preservation and presentation of the Western 
Front Media Archive. Works will be screened throughout the evening 
in the Grand Luxe Hall.

SWARM
Initiated by the Pacific Association of Artist Run Centres in 1999, 
SWARM is an annual festival of Vancouver's artist-run centre 
programming season. Over two nights in early September it brings 
together events from some of Vancouver's most vital and innovative 
contemporary art spaces. For more information on other SWARM 
programming please visit http://swarm.paarc.ca/

Art Next Door is looking for new and emerging artists to contribute
to their new online catalogue and exhibition. The title of the Exhibition is NO MORE
DOING!

September 6th 2020

We are exploring what it means to stop doing. This is a particular
challenge
regarding art and an artists work, because practically speaking it's all about
doing more, criticizing more, demanding more, and creating some kind of
product. But what happens when we want to do less, or stop doing altogether?
What does not doing look like? What does a boycott, or a divestment, or a de-
funding look like? What does slowing down or striking feel like? How does
calmness and silence manifest itself? What is the absence of activity? The
retreat from normal. Self-isolating. Giving back to yourself. What does
selfishness look like? All these are variations of the theme of not doing.

Deadline is September 6th 2020

Please send all your submissions with titles in the image itself to
ariaroundtheworld@gmail.com On average we can pick maximum 3-5 images
based on how much response we get but even just one would be great. You may
include a bio or any contact info you want attached. In the spirit of the times and
the theme of the exhibition, the deadline will not be a
hard line. It will be soft, and
retreating. So you can always submit additions to our exhibition at any time.
Thanks so much and we're looking forward to seeing what's out there.

Check out Art Next Door for more information.

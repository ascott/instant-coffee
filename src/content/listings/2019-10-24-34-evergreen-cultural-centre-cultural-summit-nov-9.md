Engage with Creative Leaders at Coquitlams Cultural Summit!

Get inspired and connect with local arts leaders and creative thinkers from Coquitlam
and the Lower Mainland at
the 2019 Coquitlam Cultural Summit.

The 2019 Summit launches with an exciting new Arts Night Social showcasing dynamic
live performances, artist dialogue and delicious food and drink.

The full-day Summit will include two keynote addresses, roundtable talks and immersive
live arts experiences designed to explore current trends, ideas and themes in the arts,
culture and heritage sectors.

Speakers Highlights

- Brenda Leadlay // Executive Director for BC Alliance for Arts + Culture

- Kim Senklip Harvey // Proud Syilx, Tshilqotin, Ktunaxa and Dakelh Nations
womxn and Fire Creator (director/playwright/actor/community member)

- Kevin Huang // Executive Director of the hua foundation

- Marie Lopes // Arts, Culture & Engagement Coordinator for the Vancouver Board
of Parks & Recreation

- Jessi Fowlis // Youth Engagement and Marketing Assistance for Evergreen
Cultural Centre

Artist Highlights

- Maya Rae

- SV Dance

- Franoise Thibault

- Dawn Pemberton

Get Your Tickets

Tickets for
the event are $25 per person and include admission to both the Arts Night
Social and the Cultural Summit, refreshments on both days, as well as lunch during the
Summit.

Purchase Tickets and learn more about the Summit at www.coquitlam.ca/culturalsummit

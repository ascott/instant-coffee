Western Front Exhibitions and Media Arts is pleased to present Delicate
Issues â&#65533;&#65533; A round-table conversation on the practice of Kate Craig in
conjunction with the current exhibition The F Word.

Kate Craig was born in Victoria, BC, in 1947. She lived and worked in
Vancouver since the early 1970s until her untimely death in 2002. Her video
and performance based art has been presented at venues throughout North
America, Europe, and Asia. Together with seven other artists, she founded
the Western Front Society in 1973. She also initiated the Western Front's
artist-in-residence program in 1977. Her importance for feminist art in
Canada and beyond cannot be underestimated.

This round-table conversation will feature presentations by three speakers
who each have unique insights into her life and work: Hank Bull (Director
of Centre A), Randy Lee Cutler (artist, writer and professor at Emily Carr
University of Art and Design), and Eric Metcalfe (multidisciplinary artist
and co-founder of the Western Front Society).

The presentations will be followed by a response by Marina Roy (artist,
writer and Assistant Professor in the department of Art History, Visual Art
and Theory at University of British Columbia).

Delicate Issues â&#65533;&#65533; A roundtable conversation on the practice of Kate Craig
is part of series of conversations organized and hosted by curator and
writer Johan Lundh.

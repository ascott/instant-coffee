Please join us at Dundarave Print Workshop + Gallery on April 16th from 6-9pm for the
opening reception of Shifting Ground!

Change is constant. Landscapes erode, identities evolve, histories are rewritten, and
what once felt solid may suddenly shift beneath our feet. Dundarave Print Workshop
invites printmakers from across Canada to explore themes of transformation,
instability, and adaptation"whether personal, political, environmental, or abstract.

As printmaking itself is a process of layering, erasure, and impression, this exhibition
seeks works that reflect the fluidity of our times. How do we navigate uncertainty?
What emerges in moments of transition? Through relief, intaglio, screenprint,
lithography, and experimental techniques, we welcome interpretations that challenge,
document, or embrace change. We encourage submissions from artists of all
backgrounds and experience levels. Let your work become part of the conversation as
we examine the shifting ground beneath us.

For more information visit our website at dundaraveprintworkshop.com or our
instagram @dundaraveprintworkshop

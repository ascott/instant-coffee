I am thrilled to share with you an exciting opportunity for artists to submit artwork for
one or two art wraps on the utility boxes located outside the Ferry Building.

The call is open to all artists and designers residing on the North Shore, Sea to Sky
Corridor, Bowen Island & Sunshine Coast. We are searching for artwork that reflects
the unique history and location
of The Ferry Building Gallery. To deter graffiti, artwork
should be detailed, with few blank spaces, and we are open to considering existing
designs and artworks.

We kindly request your assistance in spreading the word about this opportunity among
your networks. The deadline for applications is July 12, 2023 at 4 p.m.

For more information, please go to: https://ferrybuildinggallery.ca/wrap

Jeff Otto O'Brien is a Vancouver-based documentary photographer. He
photographs the everyday, and often mundane interactions between humans and
the environment.

Investigating how they each influence one another, O'Brien's work comments
upon how each affects one another and the subsequent synthesis created
through the captured photograph.

Despite this photography being of a conceptual nature, O'Brien maintains a
street photography ethic: he takes photographs that are often relatively
spontaneous, and of subjects that he ultimately finds interesting.

This new collection of photographs examines the interplay between humans
and the constructed environment.

Happy Hour Tour: June 23 at 4:00 p.m.
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam, BC

End your week with a Happy Hour art tour. Join us for
a tour with curator Katherine
Dennis and exhibiting artists Jonathan Alfaro and Janet Wang on Friday, June 23, at
4pm at the Art Gallery at Evergreen. Janet Wang will introduce her practice and new
public art installation To Exit is to Enter (2023) which is currently on view on our
lobby windows. Next, tour Transmissions with curator Katherine Dennis and artist
Jonathan Alfaro who will share his work and offer reflections on the exhibition.

Stay for a drink with friends in our lake-facing lobby. We will have a cash bar, and the
art gallery will be open until 6 p.m. Free to attend. Drop-in. This event is wheelchair
accessible.

About Transmissions:
On view from May 13 " July 23, 2023
Within the spun fibres of yarn, the warp and weft of a weaving, the blocks of a quilt
and the bond of indigo dye to fabric, textiles hold boundless knowledge, transmitted
over generations. The artists in this group exhibition play with ideas and materials to
explore how textiles connect to the body,
sculptural forms and technologies both old
and new. Transmissions features the work of Jonathan Alfaro, Katherine Boyer,
Roxanne Charles, Emily Hermant,Damla Tamer andValrie d. Walker.

About To Exit is to Enter:
On view until October 2023
To Exit is to Enter (2023) is a large-scale drawing series and augmented reality
animation by Vancouver-based multidisciplinary artist Janet Wang. Using the allure
of mystery, the artist explores the notion of belonging as a Chinese Canadian amid
histories of exclusion in Canada.

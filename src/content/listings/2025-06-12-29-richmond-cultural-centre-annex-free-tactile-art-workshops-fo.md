Free Tactile Art Workshops " For All Abilities
July 5 - July 12, 2025
1:00 PM - 3:00 PM Daily
Creativity Lab, Richmond Cultural Centre Annex
Sign Up Here:
https://www.richmondartgallery.org/tactile

These free art-making workshops are thoughtfully designed for Blind, d/Deaf, and
Non-Verbal participants, as well as anyone curious about inclusive, accessible art
practices.

Together, we will:
Create tactile drawings using metal and plastic impressions
Explore mark-making through texture, touch, and narrative
Develop a visual-tactile language for self-expression

Workshops are hosted by Vancouver Placemaking and led by:
- Johnny (Tiger) Tai
- Kay Slater
- Keimi Nakashima Ochoa

Whether youre an artist, educator, or patron of the arts, this is your chance to
engage with new creative tools and inclusive perspectives.

On Saturday, September 13, from 6-9pm, XINEMA will be hosting a Im planting a
garden in my screen
: a free, one night media exhibition.

Im planting a garden in my screen
Saturday, September 13, 2025, 6-9pm
XINEMAs Fieldhouse Garden - 5050 Wales Street, Norquay Park

Im planting a garden in my screen (2023" present) is an ongoing series of media
artworks that explore the dystopic realities our technologically-influenced futures.
Dispersed among dirt and decaying plant life, natural and artificial worlds collide to
expand dimensions of the afterlife within the confines of cyberspace. Ghostly, 3D-
rendered flowers play continuously in motion, creating an algorithm that both
acknowledges a beginning and end, while also preserving a state of immortality.
Trapped between the endless black and the glass that contains them, these screen
flowers represent a desire of our present day, to experience moments of beauty
through the mediation of the screen.

This exhibition will be held outdoors. In the case of inclement weather the exhibition
will be postponed. Please keep an eye on our socials for updates!

The production of this series was graciously supported by Canada Council for the
Arts.

XINEMA is an ongoing series that showcases emerging and established filmmakers
within and beyond "Vancouver", BC to strengthen and preserve the experimental film
scene. xinema.ca

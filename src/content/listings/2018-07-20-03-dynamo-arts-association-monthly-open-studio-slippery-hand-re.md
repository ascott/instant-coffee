Monthly Open Studio: Slippery hand reaches slippery tail

VABF invites you to Slippery hand reaches slippery tail an exhibition of
paintings, installation and media work by Vancouver-based artists: Tasha
Hefford, Maria Lagimodiere and Liam Johnstone.

Remembering is like taking a shortcut through your mind. A convenient
travel method to return to old sentiments, virtual locations, the good old
days. These shortcuts can be induced by digging out a favourite video game
or picking up a much re-read novel. But shortcuts are tricky and risky.
Things are not always as you left them. Ideas bend and shatter, Events
shuffle and recombine. Virtual and digital spaces superimpose upon lived
spaces. Memories, crumbled away from chronology, re-formed away from the
source. The lines between fiction and real become absolutely obsolete.

In taking a shortcut, rememberers will find that upon returning, the past
is closer to the fantastic. Traditions and cultural imagery seem peculiar
and stagnantly silly while internet iconography is polymorphic, subject to
constant updates, alive. These lived and virtual spaces merge to produce
disjointed sentiments and experiences. Yet, we cling to these morphed
impressions as source of nostalgic comfort.

In a written exchange with Timothy Morton, a purveyor of object-oriented
thought, Singer/songwriter Bjork attempts to describe the relationship
between art and theory, producing the phrase: Slippery hand reaches the
slippery tail. This phrase also rings true for memories and the past.
Memories are ungraspable and the objective past is subject to the same
frictionlessness. Your trying hand only ever able to make contact with the
slick surface, as it slides away from you.

BRING CASH

LIAM JOHNSTONE explores perception, role-playing, immersivety, and
esotericism, through the fictional archetype of the Merchant. Johnstones
installations are embedded with narrative components that chronicle lived
experiences. He graduated from Emily Carr University of Art + Design with a
BFA in 2017.

TASHA HEFFORD is an illustrator, visual artist, and writer based in
Vancouver. A graduate of Emily Carr University she has exhibited work
throughout Vancouver and Comox Valley. Her work often addresses the
complicated relationship she maintains with technology.

MARIA LAGIMODIERE is a Vancouver based artist originally from Saskatoon,
SK. With physical and psychological sensitivity, Lagimodiere crafts
environments based on memories of her hometown. Personal histories become
staged as overgrown fragments. Material acuity and excess parallel the
problematics of processing memory, namely the obscuring quality of time's
passing.

JENNIFER DICKIESON is a writer and artist in Vancouver. She holds a BFA
from Emily Carr University of Art + Design (2017).

YIFAN JIANG is a Vancouver-based artist working in painting and
performance. She graduated from Emily Carr University of Art + Design with
a Bachelor of Fine Arts in 2017.

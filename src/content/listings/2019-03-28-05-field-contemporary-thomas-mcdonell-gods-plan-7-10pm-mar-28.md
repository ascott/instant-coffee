Field Contemporary presents an exhibition of new paintings by Thomas 
McDonell. 

McDonell's representational oil paintings depict objects that were 
primarily collected by the artist along the freeways of Los Angeles. The 
objects were then composed, painted in three dimensions, 
photographed, and subsequently rendered flat in a realist fashion with 
impasto and impressionistic details. This is McDonell's second 
exhibition with the gallery. 

For more information please contact info@field-contemporary.com and

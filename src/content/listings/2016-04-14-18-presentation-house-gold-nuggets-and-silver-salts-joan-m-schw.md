LECTURE
Presentation House Gallery
April 18, 7:30 pm

Gold Nuggets and Silver Salts: The Photographic Legacy of Frederick Dally,
lecture by Joan M. Schwartz

During his brief career behind the camera (1866-1870), Frederick Dally
produced not only a remarkable visual record of people and places from
Coast to Cariboo, but also an enduring vision of land and life in early
British Columbia. This talk will take a close look at the man behind the
many familiar and iconic images of gold-rush era British Columbia and offer
some observations on significant aspects of his photographic legacy.

Dr Joan M Schwartz is a professor at Queen's University, Kingston, with
research interests in the power of archives and photographs to shape
notions of place and identity. This lecture draws on her research for a
forthcoming book and exhibition at the Royal BC Museum on the life,
landscapes, and legacy of Frederick Dally.

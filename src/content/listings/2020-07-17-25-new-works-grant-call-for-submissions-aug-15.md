THE NEW WORKS GRANT IS A PROJECT-BASED INITIATIVE AIMED AT
SUPPORTING UNDERREPRESENTED ARTISTS IN OUR COMMUNITY

The first iteration of this initiative is a peer adjudicated grant
of $2000 for a Black
filmmaker living on the unceded lands known as British Columbia. Projects can vary from
short to feature, from scripted to doc, from experimental to animation. The funds are
meant to support artist fees while you work on your project but can be used as you see fit.

Questions can be sent to info@experimentalforest.ca

Eligible Applicants:

Identify as Black
Live and work on the unceded lands known as British Columbia
Are a filmmaker or media artist
No previous works required; applications will be assessed on their artistic merit and
general feasibility.
Eligible Projects:

Any audio-visual format including: short films, feature films, mid-length, documentary,
experimental, animation, interactive, new media, etc.
Any genre at any stage of development or production

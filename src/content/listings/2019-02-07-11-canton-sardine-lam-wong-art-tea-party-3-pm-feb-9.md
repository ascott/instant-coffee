Join artist Lam Wong for a tea ceremony and social gathering at Canton-sardine artist 
run centre in Chinatown. We will sit around the tea table having conversation about art 
and life, also exploring the works in Lams current exhibition, Mind Transiton. 

Lam is a contemporary painter, designer and scholar of Eastern philosophies whose 
family background has revolved around the art of tea for over 15 generations. 
Continuing these traditions of Chinese style tea ceremony (gongfu cha) and tea 
meditation, guests are invited to participate in aesthetic dialogues through spiritual and 
cultural communion. The journey goes from light to dark tea, over two hours, with 
periods of looking, drinking and discussing.

More Information about this event and exhibition: www.lamwong.com

THIS Gallery is excited to announce that submissions are now open for "20x10x10,"
a juried group exhibition celebrating the power and diversity of figurative painting.

Event Dates: September 27 - October 5, 2024
Opening Reception: Saturday, September 28, 12 PM - 4 PM

This is your chance to have your work featured alongside other talented artists, in
both an in-person and online exhibition. We are seeking twenty captivating 10x10
figurative 'paintings' that push boundaries and tell compelling stories, whether
created on canvas, wood, paper, or any other surface.

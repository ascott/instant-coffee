Located just across the street from the Richmond Art Gallery, City Hall
Galleria is an
offsite exhibition space overseen by Richmond Art Gallery. As part of its outreach to the
local community, City Hall Galleria primarily highlights artists from Richmond and/or
artworks with a close connection to Richmond in terms of themes and subject matter.
City Hall Galleria does annual open calls and accepts submissions throughout the
year.

Comprising two major walls, City Hall Galleria primarily showcases framed or ready-to-
hang two-dimensional artworks. There is also a 32 flat screen TV where videos can be
displayed. Dimensions of the two walls are available at:
https://www.richmondartgallery.org/wp-content/uploads/RAG-city-hall-measurements-
2011.pdf.

Deadline: September 22, 2023 for 2024 programming

Submission Checklist

* Brief bio
* One-page (maximum) artist statement
* One-page statement of proposed work
* Five to seven JPEGS (up to 300 dpi and 2 MB each) of proposed work through file-
sharing software Google Drive or Dropbox. Make sure the link is open
as multiple staff
may view it using different emails
* An image list with thumbnails of the work, artist name, title, medium, dimensions,
and year
* Website and/or any social media accounts

Please email complete submissions to
galleria@richmondartgallery.org with the
subject line City Hall Galleria Submission. If you need assistance or any
accommodations, please dont hesitate to reach out via email. Only selected artists will
be contacted. Submissions will be kept on file for a period of up to one year for
consideration.

New Offsite Space

In 2024, we are launching a new offsite space called the Art Wall, located in the newly
renovated Richmond Cultural Centre Annex, that is dedicated exclusively to Richmond-
based artists! The approximate dimensions for the double-side art wall are 89 x 188
(h x w). All submissions to City Hall Galleria by Richmond artists will also be
considered for this space.

For more information, please visit
https://www.richmondartgallery.org/exhibitions/city-
hall-galleria.

Join VIVO Media Arts Centre as our new General Manager and contribute to our
mission as stewards of critical history and champions of emergent experimental media
arts practices. In this role, you will collaborate with the Board of Directors and staff to
set and implement strategic organizational goals, ensuring the continued growth and
success of VIVO. Your responsibilities will span organizational oversight, program
management, financial stability, community engagement, and human resources. We
seek a passionate individual with experience in artist-run centres or community-based
organizations, a proven track record in nonprofit operations, and expertise in
fundraising and grant writing for arts funding agencies. If you are committed to social
justice,
possess strong interpersonal skills, and are excited about fostering
independent media arts, we invite you to apply. This is a permanent position with a
competitive salary and benefits, providing you with an opportunity to make a
meaningful impact in the vibrant landscape of media arts. Join us and be part of an
organization dedicated to amplifying marginalized voices and fostering inclusivity.
Apply by January 5th, 2024, and become a key player in VIVO's
continued success.

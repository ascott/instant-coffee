When: Friday, March 18, 7-10pm
Location: Project Space, 2 - 236 E Pender St, Vancouver
Enter through the alley and knock loudly to be let in

Join Project Space for our Monthly Open Studio: Human in My Bed, a
poetry and photography collaboration by Ukranian artist Alina
Senchenko and Estonian writer Mart-Matteus Kampus.

"Poetry is dead! Photography is dead! Nothingness, absence,
emptiness, silence. How would you fill this void? Where do you find
meaning and the answer to the questions which were never asked?
Can poetry portray image and are there poetics left in photographs?
This collaboration brings together a writer and an artist that are
interested in exploring the relationship between photography and
poetry, bringing to light the theatricality of existence."

In this collaboration, Kampus and Senchenko depict the simplicity of
their chosen mediums through their strengths and failures. By
combining photography with poetry, they fill the bareness of these
forms visually. The poetry, written on different found materials,
represents the passage of time and consequential loss of meaning.
The photographs reveal the realities, momentums which have been
lost.

*****

ALINA SENCHENKO is a Ukrainian artist living and working in
Vancouver. She has recently graduated for Emily Carr University,
majoring in photography. In the past few years, Senchenko has closely
examined cultural and social structures with an interest in the urban
spaces that we create and occupy. She works with a variety of
mediums in order to communicate her ideas.

MART-MATTEUS KAMPUS is an Estonian writer, studying in
Aberystwyth, Wales. In his poetry he discusses the unusual and
uncanny moments that we all experience in our daily life; the unique
and personal experiences that are universal and expressive of the
human condition.

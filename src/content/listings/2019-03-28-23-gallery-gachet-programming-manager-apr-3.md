Gallery Gachet is seeking an organized, compassionate and creative 
individual who will work collaboratively with the staff and collective 
team to contribute to the delivery of cultural services and operations, by 
managing the exhibition and programming components of the gallery.

Gallery Gachet is a thriving, collectively run arts production, education 
and presentation space located in the Downtown Eastside of 
Vancouver, on Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh territory. We 
support participants as artists, educators and curators. Through artistic 
means we aim to demystify and challenge issues related to mental 
health and discrimination, in order to educate the public and promote 
social and economic justice. We believe that the expression and 
practice of art and active participation in culture-building is a human 
right, and that art and culture are critical elements of a healthy society.

Programming Responsibilities:
- Coordinate and report to the Programming Committee;
- Manage and provide direction for public programs and exhibitions, 
with the support of the Programming Committee.
- Liaise with and support artists and curators for all exhibitions and 
projects, inclusive of installation and deinstallation of shows;
- Liaise with Finance Administrator around the implementation of a 
programming budget and contribute content for grant proposals and 
funding reports;
- Working with the Communications Coordinator and Communications 
Committee, direct the design and production of marketing materials 
related to exhibition and programming at the gallery;
- Support work toward the annual submission process;
- Support artists and curators with overall promotion and outreach 
strategies;
- Represent Gallery Gachet at various meetings or events on and off 
site;
- Manage relevant contracts/documentation for all programming.

Organizational Development:
- As a part of the staff and collective, taking an active role in 
implementing the gallery's mandate;
- Provide programming information to all committees;
- Based on a mentorship model, coordinate and support initiatives that 
advance collective, volunteer and community members' professional 
arts visibility.
- Promote audience development and engagement.

Knowledge, Abilities & Skills:
- Experience with exhibition curation, program coordination, and project 
management, preferably in an arts organization or non-profit;
- Knowledge and experience in the operation of an artist-run centre;
- Knowledge and experience with issues related to mental health 
diversities, trauma, and addictions;
- Experience working within community impacted by displacement, 
poverty, homelessness, racism and other disabling conditions;
- Interest and experience working in a collaborative and lateral manner;
- Commitment to a curatorial approach grounded in the promotion of 
original, experimental, and vital projects.
- Experience working in non-profit or grassroots community 
organizations, and knowledge of collective, empowerment, and 
capacity-building models;
- Sound capacity for office procedures and file management; ability to 
prepare and maintain reports and correspondence related to the work;
- Able to operate all office equipment and demonstrated capacity for 
working with Mac OS X as well as with a variety of software (Office 
programs, such Adobe CS suite, Acrobat, and other relevant 
programs);
- Graphic design aptitude and the capacity to contribute to design 
decisions;
- Ability to exercise considerable judgment and action in performing all 
aspects of the work.
- Ability to perform duties with minimal direction, while keeping the 
Programming Committee, Staff and Collective informed;
- Ability to multi-task, function effectively and to keep to deadlines;
- Compassionate, active listener who is responsive and engaged with 
community.

Authority:
Reports to Collective on a monthly basis;
Reports to Staff Collective, Human Resources and Programming 
Committees.

Remuneration and contract term:
The Programming Manager will be paid $23.48/hr, with a work week of 
28 hours. Full medical benefits are available pending a three-month 
probation period. Vacation is accumulated at 6% for a maximum of 
three weeks available per annum, plus a week off in combination with 
December stat holidays. The 28 hours per week is expected to be 
performed on site. Details of a work schedule can be discussed.

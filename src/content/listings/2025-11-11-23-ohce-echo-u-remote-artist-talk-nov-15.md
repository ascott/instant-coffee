OHCE-ECHO, 1469 Venables

Open every Saturday in November, from noon to 4pm, and by appointment through

- - - -

According to u, u is part artist, part arena, it is a transferable personal alias and a
social space for collaboration"a project, and, as u's, a project space, inviting artists
to collaborate in and on project spaces since 2019.

Starting as the letter "u" in the word "Current" on the website of 67 Steps, after that
project closed (2017 to 2018), us was a project space in Calgary (2019 to 2020),
and us hut is now under construction in Diamond Valley, Alberta, while u has
organized exhibitions in various off-site locations across Foothills County and rural
Alberta (since 2020).

u has also organized or taken part in exhibitions and artists projects in Portland,
Calgary, London, Toronto, Los Angeles, Warsaw, and Montreal, and us project
spaces have been exhibited at Centre Village Mall, Lethbridge, an off-site project for
The Southern Alberta Art Gallery / Maansiksikaitsitapiitsinikssin (project spaces,
2022 to 2023), and at Backrooms, organized by Otto Bonnen for Kunsthalle Zrich
(new project spaces, 2024), with a publication to complete the former exhibition (in
2024).

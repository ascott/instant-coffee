Call for Contributions to the Intuition Commons!

Visit the Intuition Commons at www.intuitioncommons.com

The newly-launched Intuition Commons is a space that aims to facilitate an archive of female 
influences in creative practice that lie outside of conventional citations. You are invited to 
contribute your own moments, through written or multi-media creative gestures, that detail the 
intuitive, affective or tacit links with female-identified artists that have informed your own 
creative output and ways of being in the world.

The site is accepting contributions with the hopes of building a rhizomatic web of links " visual 
connections showing nuanced and overlapping stories that demonstrate the complexity of 
relationships we have with knowing through art. The commons will be further activated this fall 
in an installation at the Belkin Art Gallery as a part of the Collective Acts exhibition, curated by 
Lorna Brown. 

Intuition Commons is supported by Canada Council for the Arts, BC Arts Council and 
CreativeBC. Please direct inquiries about the project to Christine, email: 
intuitioncommons(at)gmail.com

Workshop at Gachet - to be held and held and held

Saturday, February 28th, 12-3pm

Gallery Gachet
9 W Hastings Street.

Join us at Gallery Gachet on February 28th from 12"3pm for a workshop centred on care,
sensuality, and the intimacies of adornment, featuring henna painting, aromatherapy, sheet
masks, a film screening, tea time and treats, and curated gift bags for participants.

Limited Spots Available " RSVP at info@gachet.org (priority will be given to BIPOC femmes
and femme-presenting individuals).

This gathering considers how gestures of care and eroticism function as modes of self-regard
and community-building within our current exhibition Act 3: As Visible as Blood. Adornment is
approached not as ornament alone, but as labour, ritual, and a site where pleasure, refusal,
and self-possession converge. The workshop creates space for being with and tending to one
another, an invitation to slow down, to soften, and to gather through embodied forms of care.

Gallery Gachet is located in the Downtown Eastside of Vancouver on the unceded and
occupied territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish) and
Sl"lwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

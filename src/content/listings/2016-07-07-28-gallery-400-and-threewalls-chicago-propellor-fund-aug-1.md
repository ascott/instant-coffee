Gallery 400 at the University of Illinois at Chicago and Threewalls 
announce the seventh year of Propeller Fund granting opportunities for 
visual artists in Cook County, IL. 

Propeller Fund awards $50,000 per year to artists and groups for 
collaborative, public-oriented projects that are independent and self-
organized. 

Propeller exists to stimulate the growth of indpendent cultural 
production throughout Chicago; to encourage more varied models; to 
spread artistic activities into more diverse areas; to promote the 
public's interaction with and recognition of such activities; and to spark 
ambitions beyond current formats.

Application Deadline: August 1, 2016

Start your application today!

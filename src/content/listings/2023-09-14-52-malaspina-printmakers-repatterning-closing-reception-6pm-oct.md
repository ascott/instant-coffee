(re)patterning

October 7-14
Val Loewen and Amanda Wood

Closing reception: Thursday, October 12, 6-8pm

(re)patterning is a visual language created through close looking. Whether diving
deeper into an image or pulling far back from it, these two print media artists explore
the unseen landscapes found in the structures and systems they work with. This
conversation between their two bodies of work explores these transitional spaces. As
they work alongside and in between the unknowable they travel far from their starting
point towards something new.

Malaspina Printmakers
1555 Duranleau Street
Vancouver, BC

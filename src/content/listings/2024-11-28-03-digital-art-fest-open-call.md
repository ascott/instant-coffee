Chroma Link Collective is calling all digital artists to submit video works for a digital

January 30, 2025
Location: Various screens across downtown Vancouver
Theme: Exploration of Movement through Digital Media
Open Call: Animators, Motion Designers, and Digital Artists

transform the city into a moving canvas, exploring the theme of movement through
innovative digital media. This is an opportunity to showcase your work across

Extra-wide screens
HDTV screen resolution
Portrait mode displays

Existing work is welcome. We invite submissions of past works that align with the

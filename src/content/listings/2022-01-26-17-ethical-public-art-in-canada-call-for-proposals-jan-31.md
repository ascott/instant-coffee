Call for Proposals: Ethical Public Art in Canada | deadline: January 31st, 2022

"Ethical Public Art in Canada", consisting of Analays Alvarez Hernandez and Laurent
Vernet (Department of Art History and Film Studies, Universit de Montral)

The colloquium will take place from August 24 to 27, 2022 at Universit de Montral,
Concordia University, Dare-Dare, Vertical and the 3rd Imperial. It will prioritize
Canadian actors engaged in art in public spaces, including artist-run centres, artists,
independent curators and art collectives, who have led the way in disrupting,
modifying, revitalizing and reinventing art practices outside of the gallery and museum.

We are accepting proposals until January 31st, 2022. All the details about the
submission modalities are detailed in the call that you can find on the Department of
Art History and Film Studies of the Universit de Montrals website:
https://histart.umontreal.ca/departement/evenements/evenement/news/detail/News/un-
art-public-ethique-perspectives-canadiennes-ethical-public-art-in-canada/

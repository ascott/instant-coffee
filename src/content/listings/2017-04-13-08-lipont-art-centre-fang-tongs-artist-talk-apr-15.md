Fang Tong's Artist talk

April 15th 2:00pm
April 22nd 2:00pm (same content as April 15th)

Free admission. Seating is first come, first served.

Fang Tong will elaborate on a slideshow of 200+ of her photographic work, 
including the three series, Soap Opera, The Chen Family, and Windows, that are 
on view at the Lipont Art Centre till April 27th, and the widely known award-
winning Swimming Pool Series and Love Illusion Series.

The slideshow will be playing on a 2 metre by 4 metre LED screen. Fang Tong 
will introduce the motives, techniques, process and presentation of her work, and 
invite conversations on contemporary photography.

Vancouver Lipont Art Centre, Lipont Place
4211 No. 3 Road, Richmond, BC V6X 2C3
Canada Line Skytrain: Aberdeen Station
Free parking on south side of building.

Review of first issue---------

First Fuck is like the proverbial sheep in wolf's clothing. Yes, you read
that correctly. On the outside, it's cold and callous, and has a nasty
namesake that would make most Catholics blush. And yet inside is nothing
but page upon page of naivety and teenage wanderlust. Little more than a
laundry list of contributors recounting the first time they "got busy", FF
is chock full of innuendo and lewd language, but it's so innocent and
backwards looking at the same time. It's definitely the handiwork of young
artsy types and other such tripe but the tales on display are universal and
morever, thoroughly engaging to both the eye and the hearts strings. Lord
only knows the age of FF's editor Yuula but whatever the case, this zine
manages to capture the awkwardness and proclivity of being 16 years old, an
age spent with an undeniable urge to tickle thy filthy and nary a clue of
how to do so. Whether it was upstairs at grandma's house or in a Friday
night field in some wretched suburban wasteland, anyone who has ever done
the deed will no doubt see a bit of themselves in the stories. (Cameron Gordon)

The Ferry Building Gallery, located in West Vancouver, is excited to announce that
submissions are now open for the 2025 Great Gifts holiday exhibition and sale!

We would greatly appreciate your help in spreading the word as the Ferry Building
Gallery is seeking artists to participate in the Great Gifts Holiday exhibition and sale, to
be held in the Ferry Building Gallery from Nov 14 - Dec 14, 2025. We are looking for
unique crafts, fine artwork, and distinctive gifts at affordable prices. We are accepting
submissions until September 26, 2025, at 11:59 p.m.

Attached in this email is a PDF of our poster, and a link to our application page: Great
Gifts Call for Entry | Ferry Building Gallery

For more information, please contact the gallery at 604-925-7290 or by email at
gallery@westvancouver.ca

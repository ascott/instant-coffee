Emily Carr / AHIS 333 DING: the Art and Politics of Assembly

Presents Oliver Kellhammer and jil p.weaving: a Dialogue about Art and the
Environment

4pm Thurs, Feb 12.
Rm 301

Oliver Kellhammer is from Cortes Island, BC. "As an Artist and Practitioner
of Permaculture, Mr. Kellhammer works collaboratively to expand our
understanding of urban/civic agriculture as a force for social change. As
such, his work, "Means of Production" at Vancouver's North China Creek
Park, is a "botanical intervention" of art that stretches our understanding
of what is urban/civic agriculture and how it is important for our inner
and outer landscapes. His other botanical interventions projects around
Vancouver include Cottonwood Community Gardens and "Healing the Cut -
Restoring the Gap."

jil p. weaving is an artist who has pursued various strategies over the
past 25 years in order to explore and engage with issues, audiences and
community participants. She was a founding director with The Artists for
Noncommercial Culture, an artist run collective, has created community
based arts projects and residencies, worked as a provincial researcher with
the jointly funded Artists and Communities Pilot Funding Program, and
coordinates the city wide Artist in Residence Program with the Vancouver
Board of Parks and Recreation.

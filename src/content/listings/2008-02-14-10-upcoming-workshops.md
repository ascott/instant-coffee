Final Cut Pro–Classroom

In this 2-1/2 day workshop, spread out over the course of two weekends, students will learn capturing, basic editing, titling and effects, as well as outputting options for tape and DVD. This workshop features a mix of seminar, hands-on training and practice time, giving students plenty of opportunity to take notes and hone their new skills. Great for the first time user or the self taught FCP editor looking to improve their techniques.

22-23 February, 02 March
Instructor: Alec McNeill Richardson

Grant Writing Demystified

Join instructors Peg Campbell and Walter Quan (from the BC Arts Council) as we delve into the domain of public funding for film and video projects, and grant writing for arts councils.

01 March
Instructors: Peg Campbell + Walter Quan

Final Cut Pro for Laptops!!!

This class is especially for filmmakers who use laptops. Participants will meet at Cineworks with their FCP-equipped laptops and all work will be carried out on your own computer, editing footage in the real time.

07-09 March
Instructor: Gwendal Castellan

DVD Authoring

Learn the secrets of DVD authoring. During this one-day workshop, the main features of DVD Studio Pro will be explored, from application workflow from Final Cut Pro and Compressor, to basic and custom menu design. At the end of the workshop, participants will be able to properly output their projects to DVD, create menus and buttons, and add a little custom pizazz to their final DVD.

30 March 2008
Instructor: Alec McNeill Richardson

11. Khyber | CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS | Halifax

Khyber Institute for Contemporary Arts

AT LAST Burning Up the House Performance Series

 

Coinciding with the first Khyber ICA's Contemporary Arts Summer Festival – Khyber Building: Pro Bono Publico – proposals are currently being accepted in a range of media focusing primarily on live performance works.


Serving as base locus for site-responsive works, the Khyber's Turret Room Gallery as space, conceptual construct, and metaphorical context will embody both on and off-site performing spaces works circling around notions of the Widow's Walk, Belvedere, Watch Tower and Cupola as architectural features.  
Whether gazing out to sea, wishing beyond hope for the return of a lover; allowing easy access route to chimneys in the prevention of fire by sand pouring, or as a defensive position in the days of military fortification, AT LAST invites works that consider the cultural, political, social and historical characteristics of such an architectural feature, and how this can parallel and give voice to the continuing tenuous position of the Khyber Building as a cultural landmark in the Halifax community. 

Seeking proposals for site-responsive performance and installation works, one-act plays, performed manifestos, sound, dance, movement, spoken word and more.  

Possibility for short-term shared residency/workshop space for selected projects prior to installation/show dates.  

Submissions should include the following: 
Detailed written description of proposed work (500 words max.)

Support material can include: digital jpg images, audio, video on CD/DVD; working schematic sketches/plans and scripts, other relevant material.

Artist biography and statement (1 page max.)

CV (up to 3 pages max.)

SASE with sufficient return postage

13. Eyelevel Gallery | CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS | Halifax
CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS: Eyelevel Gallery: “If it ain’t funny, it ain’t
art”

Eyelevel Gallery is now accepting submissions of new and engaging work
for it's annual Y Level emerging artist's show.  This exhibition is
open to students in their final year of undergraduate studies and
recent graduates (up to one year post-graduation) from the Atlantic
Provinces (New Brunswick, Newfoundland, Nova Scotia and PEI) and New
England (New Hampshire, Maine, Rhode Island, Massachusetts, Connecticut
and Vermont). *

For this year’s show, Eyelevel is asking artists to consider the
statement:

“If it ain’t funny, it ain’t art.”

We are open to a variety of responses ranging from the serious, the
peculiar, and the laugh out loud hilarious.  We encourage work that is
critical, thought provoking, and goes beyond the obvious. The best
regarded submissions won’t employ humour as an end, either formally or
in terms of content, but as a means of investigating other emotions,
relationships and connections.

Both individual and collaborative work in any media is welcome. We look
forward to your submissions.

Submissions must be received by March 19, 2008. Please include:
-A project description (250 words max)
-CV
-Artist statement (250 words max)
-Slides or digital images (max. 10), video, DVD or CDROM.
Please include a self addressed stamped envelope for return of
submission contents.

Submissions MUST be mailed or dropped off to:
Y Level at Eyelevel
2063 Gottingen St.
Halifax, NS
B3K 3B2
Email or fax submissions will NOT be considered.

The Y Level exhibition runs from May 8 to June 8 2008.
Selected artists will be paid an honorarium.

For more information please contact:
Susan Wolf (curatorial intern)
902-425-6412
info@eyelevelgallery.ca (please include Y-level in the subject heading)

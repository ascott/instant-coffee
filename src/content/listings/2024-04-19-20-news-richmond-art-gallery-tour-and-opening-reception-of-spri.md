Unit Bruises: Theodore Wan & Paul Wong, 1975"1979

April 20 " June 30, 2024

Unit Bruises presents the works of two Chinese-Canadian conceptual artists,
Theodore Saskatche Wan and Paul Wong who, through the use of their bodies,
subverted notions of objectivity through hegemonic imagery. They asserted their
othered subject-positions and contemplated on illness, death, and the human
condition. More info: [https://www.richmondartgallery.org/unitbruises](https://www.richmondartgallery.org/unitbruises)

Hazel Meyer: The Marble in the Basement

April 20 " June 30, 2024

Local artist Hazel Meyers The Marble in the Basement untangles issues of power,
memory, and inheritance by anthropomorphizing a forgotten object from Canadian
artist Joyce Wielands domestic archive, looking to her legacy to work across
questions of inheritance and
lesser-supported histories of art, land, representation,
and care. More info: [https://www.richmondartgallery.org/hazelmeyer](https://www.richmondartgallery.org/hazelmeyer)

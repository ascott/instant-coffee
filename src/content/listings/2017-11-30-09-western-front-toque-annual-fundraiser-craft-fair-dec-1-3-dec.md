Toque: Western Fronts Annual Fundraiser & Craft Fair
Dec 1-3, Friday 6-10pm, Saturday/Sunday 11am-5pm
Western Front, 303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver, BC V5T 1S1
Admission: By donation
Website: front.bc.ca/toque

THIS YEARS VENDORS ARE
Anita Sikma Design, AW by Andrea Wong, Carall St. Canvas Co, Collage Collage, 
Coral + Cloud, Draw Me a Lion, Easy Tiger Spa, Erin Templeton, Explorer's Press, 
fancypop, Fieldwork, Forest & Waves, Gailan Ngan, Henderson Dry Goods, Hendrik 
Lou, Julia Chirka, Maggie Boyd Ceramics, New Look, Osmics, paperbacknote, Petits 
Vilains, Publication Studio Vancouver, Simply Read Books, Strathcona Stockings, 
Thistle Town Toys, Tony Dubroy, tuk + milo, WarmAndDrift, Woodlot and Wasted 
Effort.

Curated by Western Front, this years fair is a showcase of 30 Vancouver-based 
artists and designers with wares ranging from textiles, ceramics and jewellery, to 
books, bags and home & body products.

Proceeds support Western Fronts year-round art and music programs.

The fair spans three days - Friday to Sunday, December 1-3. Opening night is a 
party, where you can shop and enjoy a drink and a bratwurst and continues 
Saturday and Sunday with a bake sale and kids activities.

ABOUT
Freak on a Leash is a new performance developed at and for Western Front
that combines elements of prop comedy, experimental monologue, performance
art, popular culture, absurd literature, existential anxiety, and intuitive
dance. We are not sure how long it will take.

Toronto-based video and performance artist Bridget Moser is in residence at
Western Front untilJune 20. This will be her first performance in Vancouver.

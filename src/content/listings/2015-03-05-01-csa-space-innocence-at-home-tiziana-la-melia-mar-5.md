CSA Space presents:

Innocence at Home
Tiziana La Melia

Opening reception Thursday 05 March 2015, 6-9pm
5 March 2015 through 5 April 2015

Curated by Steffanie Ling

Tiziana La Melia is a Vancouver based artist and writer. Her first book of
writing will be published by Publication Studio Vancouver and appear in
late March. In 2014, she was the Writer in Residence at TPW R&D in Toronto.
Recent solo-exhibitions include "The Eyelash and The Monochrome" at Mercer
Union (Toronto). In 2014, she was the recipient of the RBC Painting Prize.
She is represented by The Apartment (Vancouver).

See Pulpfiction Books (2422 Main St.) for admission during regular business
hours:

Mon-Wed 10am-8pm, Thurs-Sat 10am-9pm, Sundays and holidays 11am-7pm

Tuesday, April 28
7:00 - 8:30 pm
Free online webinar
Richmond Art Gallery ARTIST SALON WITH JAS LALLY

The Richmond Art Gallerys Artist Salon Series is a free monthly program for artists to
connect, network, socialize and learn from each other. In response to the temporary closure
of the Richmond Art Gallery due to the current global health pandemic, RAGA has launched
our public programs online to encourage dialogue and connection in the era of physical
distancing. Everyone is invited to join through the webinar platform Zoom. This program runs
every last Tuesday of the month.

HOW TO APPLY FOR JURIED ART PROGRAMS

Guest presenter Jas Lally, Showroom Assistant at Art Rental & Sales
at the Vancouver Art
Gallery, will go over what to include in your CV, Artist Statement and social media platforms
when applying to juried art programs. In addition, we will look at different programs for
visual artists to apply for exhibitions and sales in the Lower Mainland, including Art Rental &
Sales at the VAG, and Burrard Arts Foundation Residency and The Garage. Session
includes a Q+A period for online participants to join in the discussion.

About the Presenter:
Jas Lally is the Showroom Assistant at Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery
where she works with local B.C. based artists. Her past positions includes Assistant Curator
at the Contemporary Art Gallery in Vancouver. Lally is currently serving as President for the
Board of Directors of the Contemporary Art Society of Vancouver and is Fundraising Chair
for the Board of Directors of the Richmond Art Gallery Association. She received a Masters
in History of Art from the University of Birmingham, UK, in the
curatorial program where she
was the team leader in organizing a graduate led exhibition of drawings from the National
Portrait Gallery, London.

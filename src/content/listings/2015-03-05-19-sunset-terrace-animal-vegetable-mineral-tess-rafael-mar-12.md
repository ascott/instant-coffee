Sunset Terrace Presents:

ANIMAL, VEGETABLE, MINERAL

New works by Tess Rafael
Opening reception: Thursday, March 12, 8 pm

A cultured self. A photosynthetic self. A mnemonic self. A mechanical self.

A spectral, mineral self.

The crab, who moves backwards, forwards, and sideways, at will; across the
stage, through the garden, up the back wall, and into the street.

You lie under cool enormous leaves. An apronful of green ripples, pickled
on your lap.

No, no – there. It’s an umbrella above you, see?

As though at the edge of sleep, all the rooms of a rainy day.

Animal, Vegetable, Mineral.

///

You know what they said to me? They said, "Be here, now"
As though I couldn't - as though I could somehow avoid being, there, where
I was, then.

And, sincerely, you wanted to be with the images.
Yes, I wanted to be with the images.
Where you could be many, multiple, unreconciled.
Impossible landscapes make warm homes for impossible beings.

Do you imagine it is possible, to fall, in a terrain without depth?
The mole and his myopia used to field questions of this nature, and now it
seems as though we would have to appeal to the wisdom of the snake.

-Tess Rafael and Hannah Acton in conversation

March 12, 2015 - March 25, 2015
Sunset Terrace
2028 Clark Drive

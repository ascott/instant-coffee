I'm putting together a Canadian independent filmmakers' booth for the next
Fast Forward Media Showcase, April 29-30, 2009 in North Vancouver, BC.
For those of you who are unfamiliar with Fast Forward, the showcase
attracts between 200-250 media evaluators and buyers a year, mostly from
colleges, universities, libraries, and K-12 schools. Many are keen to view
Canadian productions, especially those on topical or local issues or that
support particular curriculum or courses.

The showcase is on-demand viewing, that is, people watch what they want
when they want; there are no preset viewing times for productions. FYI,
productions must not have a distributor already, and must have public
performance rights attached. I take care of all the details of setting up,
staffing, and taking down the booth, as well as promotion for your
production on our web site & emails to our delegates.

You're welcome to check out the booth page on our web site:
http://www.langara.bc.ca/ffwd/ for more information or to register, and to
contact me if you have questions.

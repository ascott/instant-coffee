Expo 86
An Unofficial History
Reception Thursday September 8, 6-9pm

Expo86: An Unofficial History explores the personal photographic 
record of workers, visitors, performers and partiers who attended the 
1986 World's Fair in Vancouver. The over 50 images on exhibit reveal 
a kitschy, quirky and charming array of events that happened at the 
fair.

Join us for the public reception on September 8th (6-9pm) at which the 
Vancouver Public Library will be on site to capture audio memories of 
Expo86 from exhibition visitors. The recorded memories are intended 
to also be added to the VPL's This Vancouver online project. 
([http://thisvancouver.vpl.ca/](http://thisvancouver.vpl.ca/)) The project is a collective archive of 
memories, audio and images from participating Vancouverites.

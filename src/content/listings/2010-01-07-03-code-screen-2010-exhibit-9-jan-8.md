With the 2010 Winter Olympics fast approaching the Cultural Olympiad
showcases the best in Canadian Art on bi-weekly online digital art
exhibitions on CODE Screen 2010

Artist/curator Dave Dyment bookends Exhibit #9 with the extraordinary work
of art savant Michael Snow. Weighing in on the exploration of the
structure, content and possibilities of books as building blocks and our
relationship to and through these objects are contemporary artists Beth
Howe, Paul de Guzman, Christian Bok, Micha Lexier, Roula Partheniou, Hadley
and Maxwell, and Brian Davis.

Animate your screen with some of Canada's finest creators.

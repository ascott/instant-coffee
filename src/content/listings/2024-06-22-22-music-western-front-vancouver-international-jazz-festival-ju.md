June 24 - 28, 2024
Vancouver International Jazz Festival at Western Front

Field:Talks and Open Rehearsals

11:00 a.m. - 2:00 p.m. (Jun 28)
Location:Grand Luxe Hall, Western Front
Admission:Free, no registration required
A graphic that reads "Vancouver International Jazz Festival 2024 at Western Front"
in a black script font on a lilac background.

Western Front is pleased to be a partner for the Vancouver International Jazz
Festival, hosted by Coastal Jazz.

From June 24 - 27, 2024, at 3:00 p.m. daily, Western Front will be a hub for talks
between festival artists and members of the Vancouver music community. This talk
series will support audiences to learn about the creative processes of artists,
including Shahzad Ismaily; Peter Knight, Daniel Wilfred, and David Wilfred of Hand
to Earth; DJ O Show; and Sakina Abdou.

Alongside this, a new week-long educational initiative
titled The New Improvisers
Studio will support young musicians aged 17 to 24 to explore musical improvisation
through the mentorship of acclaimed multi-instrumentalist Shahzad Ismaily and
invited guests Sakina Abdou, Matthew Ariaratnam, Meredith Bates, Claire Devlin,
Aviva Endean, Mili Hong, Sunny Kim, and Peggy Lee. An open rehearsal will be
presented to the public on June 28, 2024, from 11:00 a.m. - 2:00 p.m..

All events are free.

Visit westernfront.ca for further details about the talks and open rehearsals on offer.

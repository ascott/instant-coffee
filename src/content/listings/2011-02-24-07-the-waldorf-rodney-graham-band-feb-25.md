Friday, February 25, 2011

The Waldorf is hosting The Rodney Graham Band for a rare live performance
in the Cabaret.

About The Rodney Graham Band

Abbotsford born conceptual artist Rodney Graham will be performing live
with his band on Friday, February 25th. While studying at Simon Fraser
University, Rodney played in the band UJSRK5 with fellow colleague and
artist, Jeff Wall. Rodneys work has been influenced by philosophical,
popular, and historical literary traditions. His artistic endeavors include
photography, drawing, painting and music.

The night includes performances by World Club with DJ Tyler Fedchuk.

Doors for the event are at 9pm, World Club plays at 10:30pm and the Rodney
Graham Band will perform at midnight.

$5 before 11pm, $7 after 11pm.

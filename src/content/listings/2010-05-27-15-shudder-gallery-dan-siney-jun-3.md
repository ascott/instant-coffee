Shudder Gallery presents:

Dan Siney
New Works
Opening reception: Thursday June 3rd 7-11pm
June 4th-26th
Preview May 29th for participants of the 2010 Canadian Art Gallery Hop

Shudder Gallery is pleased to present an exhibition of new works by
Vancouver artist Dan Siney. Dan Siney works with photography and video to
create images which are at once candid and considered, responsive and
meditative. Preferring the pliability of 35mm photography and portable
video equipment his images have an aesthetic and accessibility unique to
his method.

Dan Siney's work finds moments of union with the world. This union provides
an opportunity to witness ourselves through our relationships to the
objects and events surrounding us. Within the viewing experience there is a
feeling of inseparability, and an unspoken language inherent to this aspect
of our being. This language is also in tune with questions involving the
nature of light, and the intrinsic objective and abstract qualities of
photography. On display is a selection of photographs and a video
installation, which reflect the artist's continued fascination with this
process. Within this process Siney's photos develop their own narrative
symbolic language displacing the original context.

Since graduating from Emily Carr University of Art + Design in 2003 Dan
Siney has shown his work in group exhibitions internationally, including
shows in Vancouver, Toronto, New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco, Paris,
London, Barcelona, Milan, Tokyo and Mexico City. His work is currently on
display at 107 Shaw Gallery in Toronto, and on June 26th 2010 he will be
participating in The Cheaper Show No. 9 in Vancouver. Dan's editorial and
personal works have been published in magazines such as Border Crossings,
C-Magazine, Carousel, Color, Hunter And Cook, Pyramid Power, Tokion, and
Vice Magazine (US & DE), and the Vice Photo Book (2008). An article about
the artist's work entitled "Stump Skulls" appeared in The Capilano Review
3.8 in 2009, written by Canadian Author Lee Henderson and published in
cooperation with the Presentation House Gallery in North Vancouver, for the
group exhibition 'Moodyville' (2008). Recently Dan Siney participated in
the Hunter and Cook auction in Toronto, 2010, and the Canadian Art Magazine
auction in Toronto, 2009. A newly conducted interview with the artist can
be read online in 01 Magazine (www.zero1magazine.com). Dan Siney is
represented by the LES Gallery in Vancouver. This will be his first solo
exhibition.

CALL FOR PARTICIPANTS | Fearless City Mobile Presents | Studio XX Residency
with Bérengère Marin Dubuard (Beewoo)
November 18- November 28 2008
[http://www.studioxx.org](http://www.studioxx.org)
For ten days- November 18-28 2008- Montreal New Media Artist and Studio XX
Production Director Bérengère Marin Dubuard (Beewoo) with collaborate with
four women from Fearless City Mobile in the Downtown Eastside. This
intensive residency will explore feminist interventions in Open Source
technology, the differing class realities of Vancouver neighbourhoods, and
the repurposing of interactive & mobile technology. Beewoo will offer her
extensive skills in open source multimedia software, collaborative Web
tools, real-time video processing, and VJ performance for collaboration
with the Mobile Muse 3 technology platform. The residency will culminate in
a high profile live streaming performance at the Vancouver Art Gallery's
WACK! Fuse event on November 28th 6pm-1am.
____

Bérengère Marin Dubuard (aka beewoo):: Artist
Beewoo has worked in photography, video and installation while living in
Japan, traveling in Asia and living in Melbourne, Australia between 1991 1999.
She then travelled throughout Europe and North America showing her
real-time video performances with the group Battery Operated. She has been
involved in making multimedia artwork of various forms as part of
collectives such as KIT and Battery Operated and has performed with
Montréal's sound artists Alexis Bellavance, les Platinistes Numérique and
virgilage&jmx. Her work has been presented at electronic festivals such as
INVIDEO, Italy; Split 2001, Croatia; FCMM, Canada and in places like The
Anchorage, New York City and Stubnitz Rostock, Germany. OFFF, Spain; FCMM,
Elektra and Mois Multi in Canada. She is a founding member of the
multimedia label C0C0S0L1DC1T1. Based in Montréal since 2001, she has done
research involving real-time video processing and interactivity in the
Interstices research group. She was a member of the Mix_Session Committee
at SAT (Society for Arts and Technology) where she often performed as a VJ.
She has served as Production Director at Studio XX since 2006, focusing on
promoting the use of Open Source multimedia software and collaborative Web
tools with projects like Digital Autonomy and Activism and Digital Ludology
at Studio XX. She co-hosts The XX Files, a talkshow on women and technology
on McGill University's CKUT Radio Station.
www.interstices.ca | www.cocosolidciti.com | www.ckut.ca | www.studioxx.org

Applicant Guidelines

* Applicants must be women. Trans identified women are encouraged to apply.
* Women in the Downtown Eastside are encouraged to apply.
* Applicants must be able to commit to the full ten days of the
residency, approximately 4-7 hours every day. A schedule will be finalized
upon the selection of participants. (upto 10% absence in the residency maximum)
* Applicants must show a sincere interest in Open Source and Mobile
Technology, and be able to demonstrate a commitment to skill building in
these fields.
* Applicants should express an interest in feminist interventions and
manipulations of technology and media art content.
* Previous experience with the platforms and hardware introduced in
this residency is an asset, but not a requirement.
*

Participant Remuneration

* Artist Fee $500
* Childcare subsidies can be negotiated. Please identify if you would
need childcare to complete this residency.
* An Artist Fee will be paid for the production of cultural content
performed at WACK! (VAG) Fuse will be awarded to participants who
successfully complete the residency.

Application Outline

* Please submit a letter of interest that highlights relevant
experience, areas of interest within the technologies being presented, and
expression of commitment to media arts.
* Submitting an artist CV and/or digital portfolio is optional.
* Please submit your application attn: Studio XX Residency Selection
Committee c/o amy@fearlessmedia.ca

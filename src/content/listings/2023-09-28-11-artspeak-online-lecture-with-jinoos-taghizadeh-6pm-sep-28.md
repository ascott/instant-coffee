Lecture description:
The politics of representation or Why I dont like
Tarantino?

Should political and social issues be reflected in artistic works? Is art obligated to
serve as a medium for expressing up to date reactions to political and social issues?
And do artists, whether in exile or any form of diaspora, perceive this responsibility
upon themselves? If the representation of political and social matters in artistic works
is inevitable, is this inevitability driven by a sense of duty or the pursuit of an exotic
and captivating form of identification that can garner interest within the international
art scene?

This performative lecture aims to challenge artists' encounters with the politics of
representation, avoiding deep academic discussions on the topic; rather, it is based
on the observation and artistic practice of the speaker/artist. It seeks to present
familiar examples of artworks that explore representative artistic forms, specifically
those encountered by artists in the diaspora. Moreover, instead of providing
definitive answers to these
questions, it intends to engage the audience through
continuous inquiries that illuminate the complexities of the situation.

Jinoos Taghizadeh Biography:
Jinoos Taghizadeh is a multidisciplinary Iranian artist, storyteller, and activist with
extensive experience in the fine arts. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Sculpture
from the University of Tehran and also in literature drama. She has worked in various
capacities in the field of arts, including as the editor-in-chief of the fine arts section in
several art magazines. In addition to her formal education, Jinoos has gained many
experiences working in a variety of artistic mediums, including painting, sculpture,
printmaking, Performing art, video art and scriptwriting. This breadth of experience
has enabled her to approach her work with a high level of creativity and innovation,
constantly seeking new ways to express her ideas.
Jinoos is also an active member of the arts community, having served on several
jury committees, and
editorial boards in Iran and abroad. She has been awarded
numerous grants and artist residencies, including the Avolon and RHA Global Studio
Award in Ireland and the WARP Contemporary Art Center in Belgium.

In almost three decades with several solo exhibitions in all around the world, Jinoos
is a prolific artist who has showcased her work across multiple platforms and
countries. She has had the opportunity to work with several established museums
and galleries, including Chelsea Museum, Maxi Museum, White Chapel Gallery,
Wisbaden Kunsthale and etc. She is also an author and speaker, having published
books, delivered speeches, and conducted workshops on various topics related to
art, activism, and collaboration

Overall, Jinoos is a well-known and dedicated artist with a passion for pushing the
boundaries of traditional art forms and challenging political issues. Her diverse range
of experience and her ability to collaborate effectively with others make her a
valuable asset to any
artistic project or exhibition. After the recent political uprising in
Iran, eventually leaving the country was inevitable and she currently lives in Toronto.

The Cabin

June 21-July 5, 2025

The cabin is both a place and a state of mind-a retreat from the city's rhythm into a
slower, more tactile mode of being. The works in this exhibition evoke the intimacy of
shelter and the quiet poetry of the everyday, reflecting on landscape, memory, and
the rituals of solitude. Attuned to texture and tone, these pieces invite a lingering kind
of attention, like a sun-warmed room or the slow pleasure of fruit shared by the lake.

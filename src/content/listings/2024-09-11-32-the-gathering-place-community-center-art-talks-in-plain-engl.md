Art Talks in Plain English Series - September25th 5:30 PM to 7 PM

Place: The Gathering Place Community Center, Connection Salon Gallery, 609
Helmcken

Once a
month, 2 graduate students from Emily Carr University of Art and Design will
give a 20-minute presentation about their work followed by informal discussion.
Snacks and refreshments will be provided.

Plain English is talk that a general audience can understand the first time they hear
it. Plain English is clear, straightforward expression.

In September the presentations will by:

Claudia Goulet-Blais
A Collaborative Journey of Care Between Mother and Daughter.

Pip Mamo

"My Body is a Haunted House: Haunting as a Site of Artistic Inquiry".

This event is supported by the British Columbia Council for the Arts, the Connection
Salon Collective and the Gathering Place Community Center.

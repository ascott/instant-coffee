Pendulum Gallery

885 West Georgia St

Vancouver BC

Splash 2025 : Preview Exhibition

Sept 8 - Oct 2, 2025

Held annually at the Pendulum Gallery since 2003, the exhibition is a preview of the
works to be auctioned on-line and at the Live Auction and Gala on Saturday October 4
at the Fairmont Hotel in Vancouver. The Preview Exhibition provides an opportunity for
the public to both view and place bids on works from this dynamic and diverse
collection featuring over 100 prominent Canadian artists.

Champion
An immersive theatre game
Directed by Brian Postalian

Youve invited to play a game. Its a game of merit. Over an evening you will help 
determine the next champion through a strategic game of voting and competition. 
Guests will be divided into teams as the night progresses, but only one individual will 
emerge the winner. How you self-identify may help you gain or lose points. Once we 
are liberated, will meritocracy rule?

Warning: audience participation

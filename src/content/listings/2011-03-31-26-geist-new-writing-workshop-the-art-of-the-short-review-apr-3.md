New Writing Workshop: The Art of the Short Review. Sign up now!

The Art of the Short Review:
Workshop with Patty Osborne
Patty Osborne

A hands–on workshop for anyone who wants to write what they 
think—in a few strong sentences. This workshop will be valuable to 
those who want to write reviews, but the same techniques can be 
used for clear, succinct blog postings.
Patty Osborne, a regular contributor to Geist's Endnotes section, will 
lead participants through the process of focusing on what needs to 
be said and the best way to say it.

Bring a book you want to review, a pen or pencil, a pad of paper or a 
laptop. By the end of the workshop you will have written a short 
review!

"...as valuable as the courses offered at local universities, and much

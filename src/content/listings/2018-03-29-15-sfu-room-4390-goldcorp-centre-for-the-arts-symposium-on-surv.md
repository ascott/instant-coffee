Student Research Symposium on Surveillance, Art, and Digital Culture
Wednesday, April 4, 2018
2:00-8:00 PM, Keynote at 6:30 PM

Room 4390
Goldcorp Centre for the Arts
149 West Hastings Street.

Free and open to the public

Keynote "The Gaze of Cyber-War: Surveillence and Complicity," Svitlana 
Matviyenko, PhD

In this talk, Matviyenko will introduce the notion of 'cyber-war' as an intimate and 
automated mobilization and discipline of population which users are fully complicit, 
and as a manifestation of recurrent technological revolutions by which capital 
periodically renews itself. 

Svitlana Matviyenko is an assistant Professor of Critical Media Analysis in SFU's 
School of Communication. Her publications include The Imaginary App (co-edited 
with Paul D. Miller, MIT Press, 2014) and articles in (Re)Turn: A Journal of Lacanian 
Studies, Harvard Journal of Ukranian Studies, Fibreculture Journal, and Digital 
Creativity. She is co-editor (with Judith Roof) of Lacan and the Posthuman (Palgrave 
Macmillan, 2018) and co-author (with Nick Dyer-Witheford) of Cyber-War and 
Revolution (Minnesota UP, 2019).

Organized and presented by students in CA412/825 New approaches to Digital Art 
Studies, spring 2018.

For more information, contact Claudette Lauzon, lauzon@sfu.ca

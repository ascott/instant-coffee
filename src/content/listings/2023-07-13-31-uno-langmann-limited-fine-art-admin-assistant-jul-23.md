Uno Langmann Limited Fine Art is seeking an enthusiastic individual with a strong
background in Historical Fine Arts and Antiques with experience in Websites/ Social
media and Photography for the position of Admin Assistant. This is a full time 9:30 am
to 5:30 pm Tuesday to Saturday position.

The main objective of the position is to assist in developing and maintaining the
gallerys communications and marketing through the gallery websites, mailing list and
social media, along with assisting with the day-to-day administration of the gallery. This
position engages all aspects of the art gallerys activities from research and database
to website and client relations. This position will include research, writing, graphic
design, website management, database management, and public relations.

The candidate must have a diploma or degree from an arts-related field (art history,
visual arts, museum studies, cultural studies, communications, or related discipline)
with excellent verbal and written communications skills in English. Research and
organisational skills,
including various computer applications, will be required. Working
with gallery staff and clients, the candidate will assist in a range of activities relating to
the functioning of an internationally recognised gallery.

Deadline for applications is July 23, 2023.

To be eligible for this position, applicants must:

be a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident, or have refugee status in Canada (non-
Canadians holding temporary work visas or awaiting permanent status are not eligible)
be legally entitled to work in Canada;
be willing to commit to the full time Tuesday to Saturday 9:30 am to 5:30 pm
Fluent in English, both oral and written; some understanding of a second language an
asset, but not a requirement.
Minimum 1 year experience working in an assistant role in a fast-paced environment.
An understanding of the fine art market.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, attention to detail, hand-eye co-
ordination, manual dexterity, decision making, critical thinking, and
significant use of
memory.
Position requires a combination of sitting, standing or walking for long periods. Must be
able to lift 40 pounds easily. This position will require lifting and moving art and
antiques, the applicant must be able to do this safely.
Excellent organization skills.
Self starter with the ability to take ownership of projects and see through completion.
Excellent computer technical skills including Photoshop and Excel.
Experience with photography equipment.
Must be bondable and have basic security clearance.
This is a non-smoking environment.
Salary: $36,500 based on experience

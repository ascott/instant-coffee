A 7-week in person compassionate exploration of your spoken and written voice, using
poetry as your tool and companion. Every week, we'll read poetry by authors writing
about their relationship to their voice and use their poems to reflect on our own, speak
these poems out loud and bring awareness to our own vocal resonance and
playfulness, and write poetry of our own.

Writing can be a wonderful tool for empowerment. Even if youve never written or read
poetry before, join us and explore how writing can be used to better understand
yourself, and (if needed) to dream into being
a different way of showing up in the
world. Different theme related to voice each week.

6 participants max. 4 spots remaining.

Event: 'Guest Speaker: Roald Nasgaard
Time: Wednesday, September 25th, 7:30 - 9:00 PM
Location: Paul Kyle Gallery, 258 E 1st Ave. Unit 4, Vancouver, BC V5T 1A6

We are thrilled to welcome Dr. Roald Nasgaard, an award-winning author, renowned art
historian and former chief curator at the Art Gallery of Ontario, for an engaging talk on
abstract art, followed by Q&A session at Paul Kyle Gallery. Join us on Wednesday
evening, September 25th.

Dr. Nasgaard is known for his in-depth exploration of visionary artists. His
critically
acclaimed books include Abstract Painting in Canada (2008), The Mystic North:
Symbolist Landscape in Northern Europe and North America, and many others,
alongside numerous exhibition catalogues. During his 15-year tenure as chief curator at
the Art Gallery of Ontario (1978-1993), he managed the gallerys growing permanent
collection and curated notable exhibitions such as The Mystic North. He was appointed
as Officer of the Order of Canada in 2013.

Seats are limited, so please RSVP at www.paulkylegallery.com. For any questions,
please feel free to contact us at info@paulkylegallery.com.

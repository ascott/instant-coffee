Coming Soon, Kathy Slade

In the Hastings Street window of the Audain Gallery Vancouver
artist and SFU alumni Kathy Slade asks the question, "Is
Everything Going to Be Alright?" This seemingly direct
questioning tests the public sentiment at a moment of general
global uncertainty and at a moment when Vancouver is
coming out its Olympic and its ebranding and is refiguring its
relationship to nature as a green city based on sustainable
urbanism. The text-work is also in a dialogue with a neon work
by British artist Martin Creed, who, like Slade, produces text
and music-based works. Creeds affirmative work, Everything is
Going to Be Alright has been shown in various cities such as
Detroit and New York before it its installation on the Wing Sang
Building in Vancouvers Chinatown. The typeface for Slades
sign is based on the type used in Stanley Kubricks 1964 Cold-
War film, Dr. Strangelove or: How I Learned to Stop Worrying
and to Love the Bomb. This use of popular cultural references
is characteristic of Slades solo and collaborative work (with
Brady Cranfield); her textual and music-based works draw on
specific songs and slogans and recirculate them into new site-
specific contexts. These three aspects of the work its public
address, its dialogue with Creeds work, and its reference to a
dystopic film add several layers of convergence and
contradiction to this compelling public work.

Slades window project concludes the initial public art project
entitled Coming Soon for the new Audain Gallery, located in
the Woodwards development in the Downtown Eastside of
Vancouver. The works in this provocative series address the
qualities of Vancouvers Downtown East Side, the
neighbourhood and context of the new location of Simon
Fraser Universitys School for the Contemporary Arts. As well,
Coming Soon highlights the Audain Gallerys commitment to
art in the public sphere that addresses the present and history
of Vancouver.
Four visual artists who are Simon Fraser University alumni have
been commissioned to create new site-specific public art works
for a series: Ken Lums work in the window was followed by web-
projects by Lorna Brown and Jamie Hilder.
see www.audaingallery.ca/coming-soon

Sentimental Journey

Michael Drebert, Owen Kydd, Mike McLean & Laura Trunkey, Gareth Moore, Kevin Schmidt, and 
Kara Uzelman. 

June 19 – August 16, 2009
Opening and publication launch Thursday June 18 6 – 9 pm

Sentimental Journey continues our interest in contemporary representations of the landscape. 
For this group exhibition we will bring together six local artists who focus on romantic notions of 
nature, highlighting the journey as an essential aspect in the way the landscape has been 
historically represented and constructed.

Landscape is the first in a series of three publications that use the petit genres of landscape, 
portraiture and still life to reflect the CAG’s recent programming, framing current tendencies in
contemporary art. Included in this publication is “Landscape and I” by Vancouver writer Michael 
Turner and “Sentimental Journey” by CAG curator Jenifer Papararo, as well as interviews 
between Tim Gardner and Vancouver photographer Stephen Waddell, and BGL and Toronto 
artist and architect Adrian Blackwell.

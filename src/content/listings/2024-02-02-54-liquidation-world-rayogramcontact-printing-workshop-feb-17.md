Join us on Saturday, February 17th, for a Rayogram/Contact Printing workshop,
hosted by queer emulsion-based artist Sidney Gordon. Learn various exposure and
development techniques for how to copy diverse textures, shapes and images onto
b&w high-contrast 16mm film. Bring your own home-materials to print with (ex.
fabrics, transparencies, plants, optical glass, small opaque objects)! Film stock,
chemicals, found footage, and encouragement provided! The workshop will consist
of a short demonstration and
approximately 2h for experimentation, editing and
projection. No previous film or darkroom experience necessary.

Feb 17th, 1pm-4pm(ish)
Liquidation World
1965 Main Street

Price $35 (to cover cost of materials)
10 person participant limit
14+ age restriction

Workshop will include extended periods of standing under subdued light. Frequent
breaks are welcomed. Contact liquidationworld2000@gmail.com with any inquiries.

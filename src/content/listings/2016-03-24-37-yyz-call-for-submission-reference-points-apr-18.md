CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS: REFERENCE POINTS
Deadline: Monday, April 18, 2016.

What do you keep as reference points when there is no home, no familiar
cultural context, no common background and conventions? We are looking for
contemporary video works on themes of nomadism, migration and displacement
to screen at YYZ Artists' Outlet on Friday, May 13, 2016 in Toronto. 

People have always moved on; to a new town, a new country; a new
'something' in search of opportunity, experience and a place that
eventually becomes home. This movement conjures an internal dialogue of
what home actually means. Artists, in particular, tend to move many times
over, drawn by life circumstances, educational opportunities, research,
political reasons, or simply because of an underlying feeling that
contemporary life is an uncertain place. In order to survive, and perhaps
through a search for fulfillment, they must move on. 

Our lives are filled with information and shaped by digital communication
technologies that have made it easier to pick up and move on to the next
place, but in this migration do we lose our reference points, or do we
carry them with us? These references could be a substitution for one's
homeland: a reminder or a record of the past. 

We are asking artists who work with ideas of nomadism and migration in
video to share their understanding of home and belonging in the state of
nomad, which they witness and create at the same time. 

Selected works will be chosen and screened during a one night event and
reception at YYZ Artists' Outlet on Friday, May 13, 2016 in Toronto.
Artists will be notified by email by April 30.

Submissions Details:

-Works may be video art or video documentation of performance projects
-Video submissions can be sent via email with a web link (e.g. Vimeo,
YouTube) and submissions must include passwords if necessary
-Proposed work must not be more than 5 minutes in length

For each submission please include:
- Name of artist
- Full contact information (address, phone, email, website)
- Brief biography of the artist and statement (300 words max)
- Artist CV
- Title of work and date of production
- Description of technical requirements 
- Brief synopsis of the work (200 words max)

Submission Process:
-Please email submissions to call4strangers@gmail.com with 'Reference
Points Submission' as the subject line. All attached files must be named in
the following format: ReferencePoints_ARTISTLASTNAME
-Compile written documents into one PDF or Word document in an attachment
with your email
-Video links may be submitted in the body of the email

Deadline:
-Submissions must be received by April 18, 2016.

INTERNATIONAL CALL FOR PROJECTS!
McLaren Wall-to-Wall

Creators from all fields! The National Film Board of Canada (NFB) and the 
Quartier des Spectacles Partnership are calling on your creativity to celebrate 
the legacy of the filmmaker Norman McLaren on the occasion of the 100th 
anniversary of his birth.

In the spring of 2014, the video projection facades in Montreal’s Quartier des 
Spectacles will be devoted to Canada’s best-known animation filmmaker.

Creators from around the world in all disciplines – film, digital art, music, 
architecture, design – are invited to propose an original linear work that will 
engage in creative dialogue with McLaren’s work.

The four winning projects will be projected on the designated facades from 
April 11 to June 1, 2014 in Montreal’s Quartier des Spectacles.

FIRST PRIZE: $25,000 CAN
SECOND PRIZES (3): $5,000 CAN

Full details at: www.mclarenwalltowall.com

Registration deadline: February 20, 2014, noon, ET
Final submission: March 6, 2014 noon, ET

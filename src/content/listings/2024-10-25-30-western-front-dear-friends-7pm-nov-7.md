Dear Friends &: Cody Caetano, Dorothy Trujillo Lusk, Fred Wah

Thursday, Nov 7, 2024
Doors at 7:00 p.m. / Readings at 7:30 p.m.
Free admission

Western Front and The Capilano Review are pleased to present the next reading in our
monthly series, Dear Friends &. Please join us for an evening of poetry by Cody
Caetano, Dorothy Trujillo Lusk, and Fred Wah.

The evening will be hosted by Deanna Fong.

Admission is free, but registration
is required for in-person attendance. The event will
also be available to virtual audiences by livestream.

For more information and to register, please visit our website: westernfront.ca

Produced in partnership with The Capilano Review and with support from the BC Arts
Council.

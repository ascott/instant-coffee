PUBLIC ART COORDINATOR
Join our Public Art staff team and contribute to the specialized cultural 
development work related to the administration, coordination, 
promotion and implementation of public art projects. Reporting to the 
Manager, Visual and Community Art, and working in as part of a team, 
this position will see you involved in the production of public art 
opportunities throughout Surrey. This position involves: developing 
reports and terms of reference for projects, recruiting artists, 
coordinating competitions, reviewing and commenting on technical 
drawings and specifications, reviewing art project production from 
concept development through installation. Tasks also include 
monitoring budgets and creating reports, responding to the 
maintenance needs of the art collection, maintaining a contract 
database, organizing events and working with volunteers including 
members of the City's Public Art Advisory Committee.
Completion of Grade 12 supplemented by a degree/diploma in Fine 
Arts, Architecture, Planning or related discipline plus 3 years of 
progressively responsible related experience OR completion of grade 
12 supplemented by a degree or diploma from a recognized College in 
Fine Arts or related discipline plus 5 years of progressively responsible 
related experience. You must also demonstrate a thorough 
understanding of project management, artistic process and fabrication, 
as well as community cultural development. Your highly developed 
organizational and administrative skills are supplemented by excellent 
communication skills tailored for writing reports, managing 
spreadsheets and drafting marketing materials including web updates 
and PSAs, efficiently conducting meetings and documenting projects.
Working out of the Surrey Arts Centre in Bear Creek Park, you will be 
surrounded by visual and performing arts in a beautiful park setting.
We currently have an auxiliary position available, up to 21 hours per 
week. Compensation is $27.79/hour plus benefits payout.
If you flourish in an innovative atmosphere and are looking to make the 
most of this opportunity to make a positive contribution to the City of 
Surrey, please apply on-line. [http://www.surrey.ca/city-](http://www.surrey.ca/city-)

WORKS ON VIEW during regular gallery hours November 14 - 19, 2016
PREVIEW NIGHT Thursday, November 17, 2016, 7:00 PM
AUCTION EVENING Saturday, November 19, 2016, 7:00 PM

XXV features spectacular work generously donated by nearly 70 local and 
international emerging and established artists, many of whom-including Colleen 
Heslin, Antonia Hirsch, Jon Sasaki, and others-exhibited with Access early in 
their careers. Continuing a tradition established last year, XXV will also include a 
mystery artist, whose identity will only be revealed through bidding. The support 
of members, donors, and participants like you at this important fundraiser 
continue to make it possible for the gallery to support compelling and critically 
engaged emergent practices. Join us as we celebrate the last 25 years of 
Access Gallery and look towards the next 25! 

Admission is $25, and includes membership at Access Gallery for the calendar 
year. Entry is free for current members. Memberships can be purchased for $25 
at the event or online here

Absentee bids will be accepted until 5:00 PM, Saturday, November 19, 2015. To 
place an absentee bid or make another enquiry email Director/Curator Kimberly 
Philips at k.phillips@accessgallery.ca

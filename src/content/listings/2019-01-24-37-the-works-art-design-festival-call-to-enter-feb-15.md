The Works Call to Enter 2019 - Deadline February 15 

The Works Art & Design Festival is now accepting submissions for 1 or 2-artist shows, 
community programs, performances, and special events on the theme "code". 

When something is coded, it may be understood by many, or few. Code applies to 
computer programming languages, systematizing or streamlining information, restricting 
or permitting access, and intra-cultural communication. 

Code is used to simplify, sublimate, or signify. 

Proposals may consider art as communication; new technologies, implicit or explicit 
audience segmentation, symbols of belonging, and language.

Deadline: February 15, 5 PM MST, 2019

For more information, and to apply, visit theworks.ab.ca/calls-to-enter/

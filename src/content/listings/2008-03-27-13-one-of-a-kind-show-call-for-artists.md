Call for Artists:  Information Reception

One of a Kind Show & Sale is coming to Vancouver! 
This four day show and sale will feature more than 200 juried contemporary creators, artists and designers displaying and selling their works. 

Showcase your hand-made, one of a kind items to qualified buyers, collectors, and art enthusiasts!

 

Artists are invited to an information session and wine and cheese reception to learn more about this exciting event and to meet the members of the One of a Kind Vancouver team. 

The Information Session is being held at:

Location:  SOMA Café - 151 E 8th Ave, Vancouver, BC
Date:  Wednesday, April 9, 2008

Time:  5.30 – 7.30 pm

 

To reserve your spot, RSVP to susan@oneofakindshow.com no later than Friday, April 4, 2008.

 

For more information and to download an application visit www.oneofakindvancouver.com

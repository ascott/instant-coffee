Expositions
Group exhibition with selected gallery artists
July 23-August 31, 2011
Opening reception Saturday July 23 6-9pm

-- 
BLANKET CONTEMPORARY ART INC.
560 Seymour Street, 2nd Floor
Vancouver BC
V6B 3J5

http://www.blanketgallery.com

Blanket is pleased to present Expositions, a group exhibition presenting works by 
Neil Campbell, Audrey Capel Doray, Matthew Chambers, Matthias Dornfeld, 
Jeremy Hof, Babak Golkar, Jeremy Shaw, Mark Soo, Corin Sworn, Alexis Teplin 
and Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptin. Each artist will offer a curated selection of 
works iconic to his/her practices of the present and recent past. The exhibition 
aims to provide a context of the gallery's current development and future 
directions. 

For more information please contact the gallery: +604 709 6100 or 
info@blanketgallery.com

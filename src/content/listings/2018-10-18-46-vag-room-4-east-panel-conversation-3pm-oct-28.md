Gifts of Fringe Series - presented by Vancouver Art Gallery in celebration of Dana 
Claxton: Fringing the Cube

Panel Conversation: Glenn Alteen, Grant Arnold, Monika Kin Gagnon and Jaleh 
Mansoor

Sunday, October 28 at 3PM in Room 4East in the Gallery
750 Hornby Street Vancouver BC

Join panelists Glenn Alteen (Founder and Director of grunt gallery), Grant Arnold 
(Audain Curator of British Columbia Art at the Vancouver Art Gallery), Monika Kin 
Gagnon (author and Professor of Communication Studies at Concordia University), and 
Jaleh Mansoor (Associate Professor in the Department of Art History, Visual Art and 
Theory at the University of British Columbia) as they offer personal accounts of Dana 
Claxtons practice as an artist, mentor, teacher and instigator.

Free for Members or with gallery admission.

MEASURE Exhibition Opens to the Public at New Media Gallery

EXHIBITION
MEASURE reflects on the interconnections of time, light, colour, season, the
movement of celestial bodies and how we perceive the measure of these things.
Seven artists working at the intersection of science art and technology capture the
experience of a technologically mediated world while evoking wonder, imagination and
the embodied experience. For more information on all the artists, their works, and
the exhibition please visit our website for details.

ARTISTS
Annette S. Lee, Matthew Biederman, Alan Storey, James Nizam, Flicie dEstienne
dOrves, Semiconductor

TALKS
1:10 - 1:30 - Annette S. Lee, USA
Annette Lee identifies as
mixed race, Lakota, Irish and Chinese; her communities are
Ojibwe, D(L)akota. She has developed a multidisciplinary approach to art as social
practice and social justice with a focus on painting, digital and interactive art. After
nearly two decades, Lee recently stepped away from higher education (Professor of
Astronomy & Physics at St. Cloud State University, Honorary/Adjunct Professor at the
University of Southern Queensland in the Centre for Astrophysics, Associate Adjunct
Professor at the University of California-Santa Cruz, Department of Astronomy &
Astrophysics, and Professional Visual Artist Professor of Astronomy & Physics) in order
to work as a full-time, freelance artist-scientist.

1:30-1:50 - James Nizam, Canada/Germany
James Nizam is a Lebanese Canadian artist based in Vancouver Vancouver Island and
Berlin. His practice investigates photography within an expanded field of the sculptural
and seeks to unveil the possibilities for photographic images to activate and inhabit
space. He exhibits in Canada and internationally most recently with a residency in
Berlin and at Galerie Clement and Schneider Bonn Muse Regional de Rimouski and
Dazibao in Quebec, Kunst Im Tunnel in Dusseldorf , The Sharjah Art Museum in
United Arab Emirates, Vancouver Art Gallery, Fotogalerie in Wien Austria, REITER
Galerie in Berlin and Butler institute of American Art Ohio. Nizam is represented by
Birch Contemporary in Toronto, Reiter Galerie in Leipzig in Berlin/London and Gallery
Jones in Vancouver. He has been long-listed for the Sobey Art Award.

1:50- 2:10 - Alan Storey, Canada
Alan Storey is a Vancouver-based artist. His sculptural practice lies primarily in the
realm of public art with a focus on the social dynamics of space time and perception.
His work often references industrial and mechanical processes. He has exhibited
extensively since 1982 including solo exhibitions at Western Front, Contemporary Art
Gallery (CAG), Or Gallery in Vancouver, the 49th Parallel in NYC,
the Power Plant in
Toronto, Indiana State University Gallery in Bloomington. and the Centre Culturel
Canadian Paris, France. His work has also shown internationally in group exhibitions.
Storey has completed a number of public commissions in Canada and the US.

2:10-2:30 - Matthew Biederman, USA/Canada
Matthew Biederman explores themes of perception media saturation and data
systems. He is the recipient of the Bay Area Artist Award in Video and has served as
artist-in-residence at a variety of institutions. He co-founded the Arctic Perspective
Initiative. Exhibitions include 7 ATA Festival Internacional (Lima), 11th Lyon Bienniale,
Quebec Trienniale, Montreal Bienniale (Musee des Arts Contemporain), Bienniale of
Digital Art (BIAN, Montreal), Artissima (Turin, IT), Moscow Biennale Art and
Alternative Visions (Tokyo) and Sonic Acts (NASA, Amsterdam) among others. He has
collaborated with musicians as a visual artist performing at the historic Theatre du
Chatelet in Paris as well as Ars
Electronica AV Festival Elektra Mutek Futuresonic
FILE and many more. His works are represented in collections in North America.
Biederman is currently represented by Art45 and lives and works in Montreal Quebec.

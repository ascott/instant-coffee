The upcoming exhibition Morning Trophy Question by Vancouver-based artist
Steven Hubert presents recent studio works and projects in painting,
sculpture, and drawing. On view the dilettantish mix of trophies,
landscape paintings, en plein air text drawings and more may give the
impression of an 'ars combinatorial' mishap, but each work in turn circles
loosely around the idea of abstraction itself as both critique and
celebration of the inevitable flow from specific to general and back again.

The trophies, with titles such as Award for Best Triangle to Rectangle
Transition and Panopticality at the Moment of Classical Remembrance
highlight the problem of attempting to visually represent a very specific
idea as much as they embrace rhetorical hedging and mismanaged symbolism.
Continuing on the previous theme of ersatz nature, some paintings included
in the exhibition use various garden philosophies as a springboard for
appropriate marks, while one smaller painting features a lone figure
guiding an air mattress through a misty swamp towards an ancient ruin.

Reification aside, Morning Trophy Question asks the question What Morning
Trophy? The answer is: it is the one right here and now.

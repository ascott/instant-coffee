EMERGING PHOTOGRAPHERS 2011: TEN DAYS LEFT TO SUBMIT ENTRIES!

Canadian photography students, photography workshop participants, and
apprentice photographers only have ten days left to submit their entries
for the Photo Life 2011 Emerging Photographers Competition. The contest
which attracts hundreds of Canadian entries each year, gives amateur
photographers a unique opportunity to share their vision and talent with
the world.

Winning photographers will be judged on their ability to capture a true,
uncompromised photographic vision as well as demonstrate an original and
bold approach in their photography. From all submissions received, editors
will select the most outstanding up-and-coming talents to showcase in Photo
Life’s August/September issue as well as in the Photo Life Emerging
Photographer’s on-line gallery.

Photographers who wish to participate in the Photo Life Emerging
photographers Competition are invited to send their submissions exclusively
via our on-line facilities at www.photolife.com/emerging.php. Participants
may submit up to 3 images, must provide technical information and a brief
narrative/commentary for each image. Additional information and submission
instructions are available on-line at www.photolife.com. Please note that
this competition is open to Canadian residents only.

Deadline for submissions: May 20, 2011 at 11:59 pm

About Photo Life
Photo Life is Canada's premier photography arts and technique magazine. An
industry leader for 35 years, Photo Life continues to deliver the art,
culture and science of photography to enthusiasts, photographers and
creative professionals. Published six times per year, Photo Life provides
the latest in photographic techniques; inspiring interviews and profiles of
major Canadian and international photographers; relevant news and events;
destination editorials as well as an informed voice on industry issues.

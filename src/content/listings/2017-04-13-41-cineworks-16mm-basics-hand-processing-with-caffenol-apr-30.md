A 3 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and 
practice of processing 16mm black and white film by hand with Caffenol-C, a 
coffee-based film developer.

Elements include:
Principles of chemical developing
Darkroom safety
Bucket processing
Tank processing

Caffenol is a developer consisting of coffee crystals, washing soda and 
sometimes vitamin C. It was introduced in 1995 by Dr. Scott Williams and his 
class at the Rochester Institute of Technology during experiments to find a 
household developer.

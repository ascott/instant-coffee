Make a ceramic planter

Aug 13 6:30 - 9:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

This course will explore making small hanging and/or standing planters out of clay using 
slab-hand-building techniques. Custom make some planters that hopefully your plant 
babies will love! I mean plants are picky right? If you are a plant nerd, come in with an 
idea of what type of small plant you might house in it and we will meet its meticulous 
specifications. Or if your green thumb is crap and you might be making this as a gift or a 
neglected succulent: anything goes! Just be prepared to get youre hands dirty!
Age 10+ welcome! 

Pieces will be fired and glazed and can be picked approximately 2 weeks after 
completion of the class. $63
Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our FAQ.

Image Process: a Fundraiser for the Western Front Archive

Saturday October 25 at 6:30pm at the Western Front

Join us for cocktails, dinner and a screening of animated works from the
Western Front’s Archive. Proceeds support the Western Front Archive, an
important collection of works spanning a 41-year history. Be among the
first to view newly digitized works with special guests in attendance.

Works by Hank Bull + Eric Metcalfe, Barry Doupé, Ko Nakajima, Sylvain
Sailly, Liz Vander Zaag, and more

Cocktails by Sons of Vancouver, dinner by Roaming Dragon + snacks by
Western Front.

Tickets are $59 and very limited, and include dinner, drinks, and the
screening.

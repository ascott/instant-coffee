The vivid, colourful, and alluring works of Vancouver artist
Elizabeth Zvonar are
examinations of subliminal power dynamics and ideologies in visual culture. Her
exhibition, I Spy, features two new large-scale murals and recent photographic collages
that allude to how we look at and make sense of photographs. Zvonar experiments with
new techniques, working with the aesthetics of concealment and excess. She uses
analogue and digital tools, combining cut images from various sources, from magazines
to the artists own picture archive, to create evocative pictures.

Elizabeth Zvonar is a VIVA Award-winning artist whose work has been exhibited across
Canada and internationally.

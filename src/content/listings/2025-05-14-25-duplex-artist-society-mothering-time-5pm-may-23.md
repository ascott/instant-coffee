The Motherload Collective is excited to announce its inaugural group exhibition,
Kairos: Mothering Time.

In response to a world governed by chronological time, this exhibition considers the
strange nature of time as experienced by caregivers and mothers. The works in the
show feature explorations of intergenerational temporality, lost time, timelessness
and maialogical time"a unique perception of time that relates to the dyadic
relationship held within the postpartum experience.

Opening reception: May 23, 5-8pm

Gallery hours: May 24 (12-5pm), May 25 (12-5pm), May 28 (12-4pm), May 31 (12-
5pm)

Artist Talk: May 24 (2pm)

Location: Duplex Artist Society, 1889 Franklin St (access via the alley entrance)

Participating Artists and Caregivers: Helene Aspinall, Kyla Bailey, Amelia Butcher,
Diane Espiritu, Anna Heywood-Jones, Haley Hunt-Brondwin, Alice MacKenzie,
Tehya MacKenzie, Jessie McNeil, Leslie Urquhart, Leah Weinstein, Marlene Yuen,
Anna Zoria

The Motherload Collective emerged in the summer of 2023, created by Anna
Heywood-Jones and Jessie McNeil, in an effort to build community and respond to
the particular experiences and challenges of being an artist/parent. Motherload
currently consists of over twenty artists, and the children we care for range in age
from infants to pre-teens. Our practices vary widely, some centering around themes
of motherhood, birth and care-work, and some not. However, we all share the desire
for connection with other artist-caregivers and to normalize the presence of
artist/parents and children in the art world.

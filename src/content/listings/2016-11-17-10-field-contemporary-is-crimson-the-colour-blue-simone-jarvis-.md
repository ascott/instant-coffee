V5Y 1P1
contact: info@field-contemporary.com
www.field-contemporary.com

Is Crimson The Colour Blue?, a solo exhibition by Simone Jarvis, presents new
paintings and sculptures that regard the presence of form and colour. The
exhibition gets it's disposition and titles from significant moments in the artist's
life. Representative emotive subjectivities similar to that of a conversation with a
friend, a witnessed happening, or a sighting on a walk or bike ride attest in
making her work autobiographical. Forms and gestures found in the paintings
reference spaces from flora to urban structures and domestic objects. Rich colour
and monochromatic hues are exalted, guiding the viewer through the numerous
spaces within the paintings.

Simone Jarvis is an artist currently working out of Vancouver BC. Originally from
Calgary, Alberta, she received her Bachelor of Arts from Emily Carr University. Her
most recent exhibitions include Towards a Fictional History of Colour at Burrard
Arts Foundation and How Long Can You Contain an Echo? at Field
Contemporary in 2015, Vancouver BC. Jarvis's painting practice explores formal
qualities and processes of the material. Her paintings, drawings, and sculptures
continue to be organized primarily by colour and form.

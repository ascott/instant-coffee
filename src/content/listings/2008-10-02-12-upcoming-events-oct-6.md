Blim Screenprint Flocking Workshop (craft event)

featuring:
Yuriko Iga

If we can date ourselves a little, back in the 60s and early 70s, shirts
were also decorated with velvety feeling images made of small rayon
particles called flocking. In those times, college and university shirts as
well as bowling team shirts were all done with the flock process.

Flocking is once again made a come-back in a whole new fashion. In fact,
not a week goes by that someone doesn't ask about the process. This
afternoon workshop will be a primer on the process. Because it is not the
most popular process there are only a handful of companies who deal with
the process and the products. You can count on Blim to present a workshop
on this stuff, because we specialize in the "specialized".

This workshop is ideal for paper, fabric, or accessories.

info@blim.ca to register.

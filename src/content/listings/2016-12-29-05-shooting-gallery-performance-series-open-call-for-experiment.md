Shooting Gallery Performance Series

Open Call for experimental performance works, between 5 and 15 mins in length.
Shooting Gallery, is a contemporary performance series open to dance, theatre 
and performance art; works in progress and premieres. Created as a opportunity 
for new ideas, experimentation in movement and interdisciplinary performance, 
we are striving to create a platform in the Vancouver artistic community for 
adventurous and interesting work in a low tech studio theatre environment.

Where: The Dusty Flowshop, Vancouver, BC, Canada
When: March 31st/April 1st
Deadline for application: January 6th

The performance will be a curated mixed bill, three spots chosen by open call 
and two by invitation.
Selected artists will receive a cut of the tickets sales and potentially rehearsal 
space. Technical capabilities in the theatre are limited.
To apply please send a short bio, brief description of proposed piece and two 
links to recent works to shootinggalleryperformance@gmail.com with 'Shooting 
Gallery Submission' in the subject line.
Submissions are open to:
- artists at any stage of their career
- solo, duet and group pieces
- works between 5 - 15 mins
- performances with minimal technical needs
Curatorial criteria is based on: quality of previous work, unique vision, range of 
aesthetic viewpoints, and artists whose work is underrepresented within the 
Vancouver arts community.

Costs: None. Selected artists will receive a performance fee and potentially 
rehearsal space.
Contact details: shootinggalleryperformance@gmail.com
For further information please visit: 
http://shootinggalleryperformance.wordpress.com/

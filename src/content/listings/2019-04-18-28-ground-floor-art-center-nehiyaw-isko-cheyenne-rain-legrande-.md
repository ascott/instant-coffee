An Indian woman sits inside the institution.
She slowly applies red to her body
As its read to her body
One red line, after
One red line, after
One red line, after
One red line
Until her body is covered
Red as race
Red as history
Red as pain
Red as trauma
Red as blood
Red as protection
She stands and begins to rub her redness all over the white walls...

Nehiyaw Isko is a performance by Cheyenne Rain LeGrande in which she explores her 
body in relation to institutional space. This exhibition will be centered around Nehiyaw Isko 
and
display large scale photographs of the performance taken by Jake Kimble, and red body 
imprints on paper.

Cheyenne Rain LeGrande is a Nehiyaw Isko artist. She is currently living and working on the 
territories of the Musquem, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh nations. Cheyenne is a part of the
Skwachays Lodge Artist Residency program and is currently attending Emily Carr University. 
Her work often explores the interconnection between history and the body. She works
interdisciplinary; moving through instillation, video, sound, and performance.

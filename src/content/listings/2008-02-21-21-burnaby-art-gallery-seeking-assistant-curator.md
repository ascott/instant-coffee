BURNABY ART GALLERY | Seeking Assistant Curator

Competition #2008-AC 
Passion. Performance. Possibilities. Discover a Municipal career the way it should be:working side-by-side with a team of dedicated professionals. Let's get to work makingBurnaby the best it can be!Our Parks, Recreation and Cultural Services Department is looking for an auxiliaryAssistant Curator. Duties involve assisting the Burnaby Art Gallery’s curator inacquiring and identifying art works, engaging in a variety of research activities,administering the gallery’s accession and catalogue procedures, overseeing andparticipating in the handling, storage and maintenance of art works and preparation ofexhibits. Other duties include assisting in preparing budget estimates and in thedevelopment of grant applications, making recommendations regarding policies andprocedures related to collections management and the direction of the work ofvolunteers. The assistant curator exercises independent judgment and action withinestablished guidelines and performs related work as
required.Qualifications include: University graduation in a discipline related to museum work,preferably supplemented by courses related to collections management, plus soundrelated experience. Along with considerable knowledge of modern museum methodsand techniques and computerized record-keeping methods, attributes include workingknowledge of the methods and techniques of historical and scientific research andconservation principles. A valid BC Driver’s Licence is required.The starting wage is $28.42 per hour plus 12% in lieu of benefits. Applicants under consideration may be required to consent to a Criminal Record Search.

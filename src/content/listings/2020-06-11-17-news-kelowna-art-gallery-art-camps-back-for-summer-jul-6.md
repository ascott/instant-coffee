Art camps back for summer at Kelowna Art Gallery

June 9, 2020 -- Kelowna, BC: The Kelowna Art Gallery has re-opened its doors and is 
inviting children to choose their own Art Adventure this summer.

The return of their popular Art Adventures summer camps is welcome news and now 
they come in two exciting options. An at home version of their weekday camps is 
scheduled for children ages 6 to 10, beginning July 6 until July 31, 2020. For the month 
of August, half-day camps will be held at the Gallery for children ages 3 to 12. They run 
from August 4 through August 28.

No matter which option you
choose, children can expect to explore a variety of drawing, 
painting, printmaking, mixed media, and sculpture activities inspired by the current 
exhibitions on view at the Gallery. The new at home camps provide self-guided 
activities, with additional remote support, and culminate in a fun digital show and tell to 
cap off the week.

All camps will be led by two experienced and talented local university students.

Senam Tsikata is currently a student in the Bachelor of Arts program at UBC Okanagan. 
I am very excited to work with the Kelowna Art Gallery this summer and bring the 
beauty of arts to kids of all ages. I cant wait to teach them to express themselves with a 
variety of art techniques.

Abigail Wiens is a current student at UBC Okanagan pursuing a Bachelor of Arts degree 
with a minor in visual art. I am happy to be back for Art Adventures once again this 
summer, and to bring the wonder of art to the kids in our community. Even though this 
year looks different from the
last, art is what brings people together, and I am thrilled to 
be able to teach kids about art and creativity.

Class sizes for all the Gallerys camps are limited, which allows for individual instruction 
and enriches the experience for all participants. In person camps adhere to current 
provincial health guidelines and are limited to eight participants to ensure safe social 
distancing practices are followed.

Our camps provide a fun and engaging opportunity for children to learn about and 
create art, says Ryan Trafananko, the Gallerys Education Coordinator who oversees 
the camps. This summer is especially unique, and we have worked hard to create 
exciting programs that continue to encourage creativity and imagination.

The cost for a week of at home camps is $100.00, or $80.00 for members. The cost for 
a week of half day in person camps at the Gallery is $120.00, or $96.00 for members.

For more information, or to register, please visit www.kelownaartgallery.com or call the 
Gallery at 250-762-2226.

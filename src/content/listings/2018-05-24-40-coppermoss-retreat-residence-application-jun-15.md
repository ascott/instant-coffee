Coppermoss Application 2018-19
Deadline: June 15, 2018
Dates: November 15, 2018 - May 15, 2019

Applications are now being accepted for retreats and residencies for Fall 2018 and 
Spring 2019. We invite applications from individuals who are interested in coming for 
1-4 weeks to the Sunshine Coast, BC. We are interested in supporting the most 
expansive definition of culture, and encourage anyone who feels a connection to the 
values and intents of Coppermoss to apply. Through residencies, supported 
research, commissioned projects, learning experiments, curated events and tactical 
discussions, Coppermoss supports ideas and practices considering the edge of 
nature and human activity. We are driven by a commitment to decolonial futurities, 
and seek to work with collaborators who imagine through responsible action and 
radical thinking. We support retreats and residencies that consider the locality of the 
cottage as a means of considering broader restorative relationships with land, 
environment and communities.

Coppermoss is located in the village of Tuwanek (twnkw) on the Sunshine Coast 
of British Columbia. For millennia the shshlh peoples have been stewards of this 
land, interrupted in the last two centuries by the violence of colonialism. As settlers 
we have directly benefited from the colonisation of the indigenous peoples of this 
land. Coppermoss is committed to the values put forward by the shshlh nation " to 
consider a restorative and responsible relationships to the land, to preserve the 
natural balance of humans, animals and plants, and to plan for the generations 
ahead who will inherit the responsibility of stewardship of this place and all it 
provides.

Please apply to one of the following two options. 

Retreat: We support your need to retreat into slowness at Coppermoss in order to 
complete a project you may be in the midst of; or to go deeper into ideas you have 
just kept putting away. In this category, you are able to select the option of coming 
for 1-4 weeks to spend focused and committed time towards the completion of a 
project. We support individuals/collectives/groups who see their projects and 
practices connecting with the values and mission of Coppermoss.

Residency: We invite applications from individuals/collectives/groups who are 
interested in making new work through a longer and more sustained relationship 
with Coppermoss and the region. Whether you already have a project in 
development that connects to the Sunshine Coast, or you are interested in 
researching and exploring potential projects, this category would be ideal. 
Residencies are 1-2 weeks in length, and are intended to repeat over the course of 
time, depending on the nature of the research and work. This option may result in 
the development of new work, new research, new skills or a new project. If selected, 
Coppermoss will commit to collaboratively supporting you, both through returning 
and hosted visits, but also through financial support. Once here you will be 
introduced through hikes, guided tours, walks and introductions to local contacts, to 
the specific geography and cultural history of this place. We ask you to describe in 
detail how you see connecting with this intent.

Deadline: June 15, 2018
Dates: November 15, 2018 - May 15, 2019

Cost: The cost for either option is $120/week**, which covers the use of wood and 
other consumables. Please note that we dont pro-rate this per day. For example, if 
you decide to ask for a 10 day retreat/residency, the cost would still be for 2 weeks. 
**Subsidies are available to help. Please indicate this in your letter.
Dates: All retreats/residencies begin on a Friday.
Capacity: Max 4 people, the cottage has 2 queen beds + a pull-out
Space: 2 bedrooms + a studio for non-toxic materials. To our great regret, the 
cottage is not friendly to guests with mobility issues. There are 40 steps from the 
steep driveway to the cottage, and the ground is at times uneven and rocky.

HOW TO APPLY:
The application is simple. Please send the following items in a single email to 
coppermosstuwanek@gmail.com by midnight on June 15, 2018.
Cover Letter stating which option you are applying to and 2 preferred start dates.
Statement of Intent (up to 500 words) outlining why you would like to join us, and 
how what you are proposing aligns with the option you selected.
A curriculum vitae, Biography and Artist statement, 
Images of your artwork and/or a link to a website.

For more information, please contact coppermosstuwanek@gmail.com

RINA LIDDLE
We Are Watching
FEB 12 - MAR 21

Please join Rina Liddle at the opening reception of her new work, We Are
Watching, on February 12th, 7-10PM.

The project, dealing with ideas of private/public space, surveillance and
public art, features dual indoor/outdoor projections of participant
supplied video footage and live surveillance video. A publication in two
parts, with essays by Kristina Lee Podesva and Danna Vajda, will accompany
the work.

Further information and to submit video please see
http://liddlethought.blogspot.com

This project is generously supported by the City of Vancouver Public Art
Program as part of BRIGHT LIGHT.

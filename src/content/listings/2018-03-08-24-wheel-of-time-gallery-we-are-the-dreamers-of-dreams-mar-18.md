Vancouver, BC- The Wheel of Time Gallery is pleased to present a group exhibition of 
current artist-in-residence Marie Whimbey, Tito Ohep and collaboration with in-house 
artist Raj Gill. We Are the Dreamers of Dreams will open Sunday March 18, 2018 at 5-
9pm with a performance and artist talk at 7pm. The exhibition will also feature an 
outdoor installation.

The WOT Gallery is located in a tear-down home on Renfrew Street, Vancouver. Artists 
have the ability to use the house as a canvas. theatre. laboratory, studio, atelier, 
incubator, think tank, holodeck, portal, vortex, whirlpool, digester, hotbed, fermentor, 
petri dish, wind tunnel and, cloud chamber. To show other ways of living, inhabiting 
space and creating art. WOT explores what it is to embrace our ephemeral existence 
on, within, and around these walls that will soon crumble. Much like our short existences 
that will also drift off into a place other than here " a time other than now? Or will it?

Whimbey's visual art bridges dreamscapes into our reality. She deliberately plays with 
vibrant colours and mystical landscapes to experience a multitude of felt sensations. 
Oheps explores the phenomena of multi-dimensional living and shares its tangibility in 
the now moment through performance. Gills multi-disciplinary approach, explores 
human interaction through theatrical intervention, play, being and not being.

The WOT Gallery invites you to build a temporary time machine with us. To visit eternity 
together temporarily. Enteral impermanence .To find new legends, mythologies, 
|forms|, ((waves)), ###, and ___---layers---___. Or perhaps, it is a rediscovery.

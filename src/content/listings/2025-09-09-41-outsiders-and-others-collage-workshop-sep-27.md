Date: Saturday, September 27, 2025
Time: 2-4 pm

Venue: Outsiders and Others (#100-938 Howe St., Vancouver)

In this collage workshop, participants will create mini self-portraits on shipping tags.
Seema will describe the intuitive process behind her collages. By bringing metaphor
and symbolic imagery to the surface, this process can convey a felt sense and
express what is beyond words and/or conscious knowing.

Using her collaged tags as an example, Seema will show how these works can lead
to greater self-understanding. She will also share some self-taught tips she
regularly uses in her collage practice.

Scissors, glue sticks, and magazines for collage will be provided. (Feel free to bring
your own supplies, as well as additional collage materials.)

All levels of artistic experience welcome.

Lauryn Youden is an Canadian artist based in Berlin, Germany. Her practice
is focused in conceptualizing futures for humans and technology, including
meditative techniques to create a soothing atmosphere that expels and
brings awareness to the unnatural states and anxieties that technology
places on the human body and mind. She is currently in residence with
Electric Objects. Ongoing collaborations include project space OTHER
Projects in Berlin, Germany

www.laurynyouden.net

--

Alan Woo is the author of the award-winning children's book, "Maggie's
Chopsticks" out now in book stores near you and online!!

Praised by the New York Times as a pianist with assurance and vitality
Mr. Woo was also named the winner of the 2010 Juilliard Concerto
Competition. His other accomplishments include second and audience prize at
the IIYM International Piano Competition in 2009, first prize at the
Idyllwild Arts Academy Concerto Competition in 2008, and top prizes at the
Lee University National Piano Competition and the International Young
Artists Piano Competition in Washington, D.C. in 2006.

Alan Woo is now based in Berlin, freelancing and working on projects. He
currently works with HORT, Serpentine Galleries in London, UK, e-flux and
Olafur Eliasson Studio.

www.alanwoo.ca

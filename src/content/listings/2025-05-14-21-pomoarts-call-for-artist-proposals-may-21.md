Art Out Side Festival

Calling emerging and professional artists to transform Port Moody with arts! We invite
artists of all disciplines to propose works that are immersive, engaging, and push
beyond traditional formats " visual and media arts, performance, poetry, sound,
interactive installations, film, sculpture, dance, and all creative forms.

Art Out Side is a multidisciplinary arts festival transforming Port Moody this summer.
Arts will fill the streets, from waterfronts to commercial hubs, alleyways to park
benches, and secret locations.

This new city-wide arts festival brings together bold artistic voices creating
unforgettable experiences in unexpected spaces.

Curated by PoMoArts with the support of the City of Port Moody, Art Out Side will leave
creative marks across the city each year.

Submit your application by May 21, 2025

221A is delighted to host the launch of The Longest Way to Eat a Melon, a new
collection of fictions by Vancouver-based writer Jacquelyn Zong-Li Ross on
Saturday, July 19th at 6 pm.

This evening of celebration includes readings by local authors Andrea Actis,
Francesca Bennett, and S F Ho, with music by Vancouver duo Strawberry. A feature
conversation with the author led by NYC-based writer Jac Rene Bruneau will follow.

The Longest Way to Eat a Melon is out now from Sarabande Books. Copies of the
book will be available for sale, courtesy of The Paper Hound.

Light refreshments will be served.

Admission is free, RSVP to attend.

Photography may be taken at this event for use on 221As website, marketing and as
part of documentation for our archives.

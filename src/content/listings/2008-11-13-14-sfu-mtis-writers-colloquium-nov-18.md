Métis Writers’ Colloquium:
Nation and Diaspora in Métis Writing

Tuesday, November 18
2:30 to 6:00 pm
ASSC1 9095 (Arts & Sciences Building: First Nations Studies Classroom)
Simon Fraser University (Burnaby campus)

Join Métis writers Marie Clements, Sharron Proulx-Turner and Gregory
Scofield as they read from their work and discuss questions relating to
Métis nationalism, mixed-race identity, and diasporic Métis communities.
The colloquium will consist of 2 panels of readings and discussion (2:30 –
4 pm and 4:30 to 6 pm) with a reception in between.

About the Authors

Marie Clements author of The Unnatural and Accidental Women (1997), Burning
Vision (2002), finalist for Governor General’s Literary Award for Drama,
and Copper Thunderbird (2007).

Sharron Proulx-Turner, author of Where the Rivers Join (1995), finalist for
the Edna Staebler award, what the auntys say (2002), finalist for the
League of Canadian Poets’ Gerald Lampert Prize for best first book of
poetry, she is reading her blanket with her hands (2008), and she walks for
days/ inside a thousand eyes/ (a two-spirit story) (2008).

Gregory Scofield, author of The Gathering: Stones for the Medicine Wheel
(1993), winner of Dorothy Livesay Poetry Prize, Native Canadiana: Songs
from the Urban Rez (1996), I Knew Two Métis Women (1999), and Singing Home
the Bones (2005), among other works.

Opening date: October 24
Location: The Phoenix Gallery, Alley Entrance, 45 West Hastings
Artists: Untitled Art Group

The theme of focus examines the artist’s personal interpretation and
experiences with both visual connotations and actions. As art students,
we have had to “focus” in terms of studying, researching,
conceptualizing and creating. As visual artists, we have a unique
relationship with the noun/verb “focus” as it refers to both visual
clarity/sharpness (or lack thereof) along with the attentive action of
being specific.

Focus can mean 20/20 eyesight/imperfect eyesight, meaningfulness,
confusion/specificity, and blurriness/clarity. Focus can also mean
spirituality and religion – it is a state of mind. For this group show, we
explored what “focus” means to each of us individually and together as
a diverse collective.

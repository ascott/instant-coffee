The Builders

An accumulation and performance built by unlikely architects

8pm September 10, 11, 12 | SFU Woodwards | B2540

An extraordinary environment constructed by seven unlikely architects
- theatre artists, dancers, and a musician. A performance that emerges
from heaps of found and recycled materials. An accumulation that
attempts to extend and expand beyond the space that holds it.
Welcome to the world of The Builders.

In a chaotic merging of theatre and installation, The Builders
investigates transformation, territory and the practice of making
ourselves at home in the world. Inspired by the worldwide phenomenon
of art environments built by outsider artists, The Builders explores the
compulsions behind transforming spaces, objects and the self.

Created and directed by Megan Stewart, a theatre artist and MFA
candidate at SFU’s School for the Contemporary Arts. The Builders
was devised over a five-month process in collaboration with the
ensemble: Robert Azevedo, Gordon Havelaar, Eveleen Kozak and
Keely O’Brien. Featuring music by David Cowling.

Performances take place September 10-12 at 8pm in the basement of
SFU Woodwards at Goldcorp Centre for the Arts, Room B2540, 149
West Hastings Street. Tickets are $5 / $15. Advance tickets are
recommended due to limited seating and can be purchased here:
www.sfu.ca/sca/events/details/the-builders. Learn more about The
Builders at meganblythe.com/tag/the-builders/

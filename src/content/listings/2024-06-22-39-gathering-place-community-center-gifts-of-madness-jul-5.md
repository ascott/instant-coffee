Exhibition: Gifts of Madness -The Vancouver Touring Show

The Gathering Place Community Center Gallery
609 Helmcken
12 visual artists will be on show July 5th to August18th. Join us for the Meet the Artists
event July14th from 5:30 to 7:00PM.
The Mad Pride Cabaret and Parade will precede it from 2 to 5:30 PM.

Gifts of Madness was an international show guest curated by Kagan Goh at the
Penticton Art Gallery in March. Vancouver based Artists are in this Touring Show. The
exhibition is supported by the Canada Council for the Arts, the Connection Salon
Collective and the Gathering Place Community Center.

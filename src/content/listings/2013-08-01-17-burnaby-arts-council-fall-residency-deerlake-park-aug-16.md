Burnaby Arts Council Fall Residency @ DeerLake Park

Call to Submission:

Important Dates:

Application Deadline August 16th 2013 (Results within a week)

Residency Start Date September 10th – End date November 9th 
2013.

Exhibition at Deer Lake Gallery, October 18th- November 9th 2013. 

The Burnaby Arts Council Fall Residency @ Deer Lake Park is an 
opportunity for an emerging or professional artist to use The BAC 
Studio, in order to develop an exhibition that will be shown at The 
Deer Lake Gallery from October 18th – November 9th. We are 
looking for emerging or professional artist, and or groups that 
possess active studio practices that engage with the discourses, 
themes, politics and dialogues of contemporary art. The BAC House 
is located within Deer Lake Park. The park is an urban sanctuary, 
within the diverse and dynamic City of Burnaby. Our goal is that this 
Residency provides a platform for emerging or professional artists to 
develop their studio practice, exhibition history and experience 
engaging the public, while simultaneously engaging the Burnaby 
Public and the Burnaby Arts Council and its members.

Open to:

The BAC Fall Artist in Residence is open to all artist (visual, literary, 
dance, etc.), and artist groups as long as the exhibition requirements 
are fulfilled (As determined by BAC Staff) between October 18th – 
November 9th 2013.

Expectations of the Council:

-We will provide a small self contained room/studio/office for the 
duration of your residency within the BAC House.

-You will have 24/hr access to the studio space with WIFI, the BAC 
House Kitchen, Bathroom and grounds.

-You will have access to a office with computer/printer, during 
business hours.

- A solo exhibition at the Deer Lake Gallery, between October 18th- 
November 9th 2013.

-You will be featured in our communications , we will provide printing 
at cost.

- Honorary membership of the Arts Council for the 2013 year.

Expectations of the Artist/Collective:

-Maintain an active use of the studio space provided for the duration 
of the residency September 10th- November 9th.

-Provide two workshops with the public during your residency.

-Develop and install an exhibition at the end of your residency for the 
period of October 18th- November 9th 2013.

-Gallery sit on all of the Saturdays during your exhibition from 12-
4pm, (workshops could overlap).

Criteria:

-An active studio practice.

-An ability to engage the public.

-One exhibition on C.V, that can demonstrate the nature of your 
practice and past experience developing exhibitions.

-An enthusiasm for the project, and the ability to fulfill the BAC 
Residency requirements of,

- Gallery sitting on Saturdays 12-4pm for duration of show (Can 
be combined with workshops and studio work).

- 2 workshops.

- Active engagement of project and use of space.

- A finished exhibition to be installed into Deer Lake Gallery from 
October 18th- November 9th.

To Apply:

-Three images of your work, exhibition shots are an asset, jpeg files.

-Statement of Intent for the Residency, workshops and exhibition, 
Max 500 words.

-Current C.V.

-Completed application form.

-To be submitted to info@Burnabyartscouncil.org by August 16th 
2013.

Hamza Walker on Altered States
7pm
August 3

American Curator and Writer Hamza Walker will respond to the work in Susan
Hillers exhibition Altered States, in particular her five-channel
installation PSI Girls (1999). Walker will discuss the artistic milieus
amongst which this work emerged, and the contemporaneous practices of media
artists. He will also reflect on Hillers selection of source material,
including The Fury (1978), Stalker (1979), and a field recording of the
gospel choir from St. Georges Cathedral in North Carolina. Walker will
draw upon concepts and phenomenology that he has explored in his own
curatorial work, such as social zeitgeists that conjure dystopic surrealism
in Teen Paranormal Romance (2014); experimental forms of expressive
notation in Wadada Leo Smith: Ankhrasmation: The Language Scores, 1967-2015
(2015) (with John Corbett); and contemporary methods of cultural archiving
and collage in Made in L.A. 2016: a, the, though, only (2016) (with Aram
Mshayedi).

Hamza Walker is the Executive Director of LAXART in Los Angeles. Previously
he was the Director of Education of The Renaissance Society at University
of Chicago, and an adjunct professor at the School of the Art Institute of
Chicago. In 2016, Walker co-curated the Hammer Museums Made in L.A.
biennial (2016) with Aram Moshayedi. He is the recipient of the Norton
Curatorial Grant (1999), the Walter Hopps Award for Curatorial Achievement
(2004) and the Ordway Prize for contributions to the field in the form of
writing and exhibitions (2010). Walker has contributed reviews and art
criticism to New Art Examiner, Art Muscle, Dialogue, Parkett, and Artforum
in addition to numerous catalogue essays on artists such as Allora and
Calzadilla, Jno Cook, Jo Hormuth, Michael Lash, Raymond Pettibon, and Heimo
Zobernig among others.

Products include:
Books by Nieves, ceramics by Gailan Ngan, zines by the Lions and various local artists, 3D puzzles by Kim Sutherland and custom dolls from Spain by Emma Pardos, sound recordings by Lief Hall and much more to come. 

The LES Store (LESS) offers a space for artists and designers from the local and international community to display wares and works that extend beyond the typical gallery context. With a rotating window display and product-oriented works, some commissioned specifically for the space, the LESS is designed to facilitate an engagement between the art market and more everyday economies. A carefully curated selection of offerings including literature, housewares, and more esoteric items (both functional and non) ensure that the exceptional is made accessible.

Currently showing at the LES Gallery: Arrested Development by Kayla Guthrie until Feb.23

Join us for the fourth annual Vancouver Draw Down, on Saturday June 15.

The Contemporary Art Gallery is one of twenty-three different arts and
cultural organizations across Vancouver who will host hands-on drawing
workshops.

At the CAG participate in the workshop Drawing Experiments for an afternoon
of unconventional drawing. In response to the current exhibitions by Erin
Shirreff and Nancy Holt, the CAG has invited Cineworks to present
collaborative drawing-on-film and flipbook drop-in workshops for all ages.
Working with filmmakers Zoe and Ariel Kirk-Gushowaty, participants will
create their own simple animations on film, projected live in the gallery.

At the False Creek Community Centre take part in Re-inventing Drawing a
free hands-on experimental drawing workshop with CAG Field House Studio
artist-in-residence Raymond Boisjoly.

The Field House Studio Residency Program is generously supported by the
Vancouver Park Board and the City of Vancouver. The inaugural residency
with Raymond Boisjoly is supported by the Province of British Columbia
through the Ministry of Advanced Education, Innovation and Technology.

Raymond Boisjoly, As It Comes continues until June 16 and is located in the
window spaces at the CAG and off-site at Yaletown-Roundhouse Station,
Canada Line and The Burrard Marina Field House Studio Residency Program.

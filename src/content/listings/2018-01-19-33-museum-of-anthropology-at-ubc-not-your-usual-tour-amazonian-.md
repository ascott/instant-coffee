Museum of Anthropology at UBC: Not Your Usual Tour: Amazonian Languages l Jan 25
6393 NW Marine Dr, Vancouver

The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC presents a tour of the languages of 
Amazonia on Thursday, January 25, 2018 at 7pm.

Explore the linguistic diversity of the region and learn more about the past and current 
challenges to its preservation. MOA will examine current documentation and 
revitalization efforts of Amazonian languages, which colonization, slavery, resource 
exploitation and assimilationist policies have gravely endangered, in some instances to a 
point of no return.

Free with museum admission. For more information, visit moa.ubc.ca/ not-your-usual-
tour-amazonian-languages

Artist Talk: Kyla Mallett and Elizabeth Zvonar
Thursday June 6, 6pm
Audain Gallery
149 West Hastings St.
Vancouver

Please join us in the gallery for a free informal talk with the artists
Kyla Mallett and Elizabeth Zvonar, in conversation with Amy Kazymerchyk,
the curator of the Audain Gallery.

Although each artist has a distinctive practice, Mallett and Zvonar can be
seen as sharing similar methods of production. Like biographers, they
gather their thematic and material resources through a process of research
and discovery. As a result, their work is built out of combinations of
fragments and references, some popular, some oblique. Unlike conventional
biographers, however, the narratives Mallett and Zvonar thereby produce do
not address particular, individualized subjects so much as higher-order
processes of narrativization: the making of the making of a biography of
images.

Artists' Biographies

Kyla Mallett's work deals with the intersection of language and the social
realm, using anthropological strategies of research, collecting, and
archiving. She has received critical acclaim for exhibitions at such
institutions as the Canadian Cultural Centre in Paris, the Contemporary Art
Gallery in Vancouver, Vancouver Art Gallery, Artspeak, Presentation House
Gallery, the Art Gallery of Alberta, ThreeWalls in Chicago, and the Mount
St. Vincent University Gallery in Halifax. Mallett is an Assistant
Professor in Visual Art and Graduate Studies at Emily Carr University.

Based in Vancouver, Elizabeth Zvonar has exhibited work nationally and
internationally. In 2009, she received the City of Vancouver Mayor's Award
in the emerging visual arts category, and in 2011 she received the Emily
Award, Emily Carr University's alumna award in recognition of outstanding
achievement. Zvonar's exhibition, Banal Baroque, is currently on display at
the Daniel Faria Gallery in Toronto, and this summer she will lead a
thematic residency at Gibraltar Point in Toronto, titled The Future Is
Coming Everyday.

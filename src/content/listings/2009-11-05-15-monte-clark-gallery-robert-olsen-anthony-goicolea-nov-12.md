Monte Clark Gallery

FOUNDATIONS
Robert Olsen & Anthony Goicolea

November 12 - December 5, 2009

The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to present Foundations, an
exhibition of new works by Robert Olsen and Anthony Goicolea. These
works have been brought together to compare each artist's take on the
image of cement foundation blocks, the result a contemplative and
atmospheric presentation.

Anthony Goicolea's family history is the basis of his new body of work
entitled Once Removed. Having made his first pilgrimage to Cuba to
visit the sites once inhabited by his parents and grandparents,
Goicolea created a collection of photographs documenting the
architecture and landscape of the sites. He felt a strange sense of
nostalgia for something [he has] never been a part of or experienced
directly. The result of his experiences is a collection of lush
images featuring foliage, strung lights, water, earth, and cement
blocks; though eerily devoid of life. Foundations will include a
select group of these newest Goicolea photographs.

Robert Olsen's work reflects his obsession with the qualities of
light. His small, finely detailed images come to life through his
meticulous depiction of highlights and shadows. His latest paintings
included in the exhibition study cement foundation blocks contrasted
with young, fledgling greenery. These ethereal subjects seemingly
float upon their rich, black backgrounds.

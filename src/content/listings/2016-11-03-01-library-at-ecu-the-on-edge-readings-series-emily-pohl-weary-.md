Author bio

Emily Pohl-Weary's latest book is Ghost Sick (Tightrope 2014), a collection of 
poetry about the impact of violence on an inner-city community. Her six previous 
books include the teen novel Not Your Ordinary Wolf Girl (Skyscape 2013), a 
Hugo Award-winning biography of her sci-fi writing grandmother, a female 
superhero anthology, a middle-grade mystery, a second poetry collection, and a 
girl pirate comic. She served as the University of the Fraser Valley's 2015 Kuldip 
Gill Writing Fellowship Writer-in-Residence, and she was the 2014 Toronto Public 
Library system's eWriter-in-Residence for Young Voices.

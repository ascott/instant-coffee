Art Education, For Life speaker series
A Lens for Discovery: Altered States, Transcendence and Belief
Jeremy Shaw in conversation with Andrew Booth, Vancouver Art Blog founder

Monday, November 4, 2024
Reception: 5:30pm / Talk: 6:15pm

Gordon Smith Gallery of Canadian Art
2121 Lonsdale Ave, North Vancouver, BC V7M 2K6

North Vancouver-born and Berlin-based artist Jeremy Shaw sits down with Andrew
Booth to delve into the role that early exposure to quality art education had in
building alternative perspectives on visual media from a young age. A former student
of Artists for Kids, Shaw now works in a variety of media to create a post-
documentary space to dissect and assemble disparate belief systems and
counter-
histories.

Please join us at 5:30pm for a welcoming reception before the talk begins at 6:15
PM. All ages are welcome, attendance is by donation. Underground parking is
available.

Art Education, For Life is generously supported by The Audain Foundation, Polygon
Homes and Anthem Properties, with additional support provided by the North
Vancouver School District and the North Vancouver Recreation and Culture
Commission.

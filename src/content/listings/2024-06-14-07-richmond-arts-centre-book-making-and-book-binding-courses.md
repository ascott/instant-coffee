Hardcover Book-binding

Create a hardcover book or journal from start to finish by learning how to cut paper,
make covers and bind books. Also, discover the cross-cultural history of
hardcover/casebound books. Supplies
included.

Course led by Mickey L.D. Morgan (they/them), an interdisciplinary artist raised in
St:l (Sto:lo), Swxw7mesh (Squamish), Sl"lwta" (Tsleil-Waututh), and
xmky"m (Musqueam) Land in Richmond and Vancouver. Their work draws
from the Jewish idea of tikkun olam (world-mending) and other decolonial values.
Mickey uses art as a way to grow imagination and bridge gaps, believing that
building community is a creative act.

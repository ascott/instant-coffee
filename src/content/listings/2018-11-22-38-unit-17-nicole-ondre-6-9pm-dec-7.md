NICOLE ONDRE 
8 December 2018 - 13 January 2019
opening reception: 7 December 6 - 9pm

Nicole Ondre (b. 1986, Salt Spring Island, Canada) holds a BFA from Emily Carr 
University and an MFA from Hochschule fr bildende Knste, Hamburg. Her work has 
been included in solo, two person and group exhibitions at Diaz Contemporary, Toronto 
(2014); Or Gallery, Vancouver (2013); Benzulli zeigt, Dsseldorf (2012) and the Center 
for Curatorial Studies, Bard College (2011). Since 2010, she has worked under the alias 
Feminist Land Art Retreat (FLAR) in collaboration with Vanessa Disler. Most recently, 
FLAR's work has been exhibited at Nottingham Contemporary and 500 Capp Street 
Foundation / David Ireland House, San Francisco (both 2018); Kestner Gesellschaft, 
Hannover and Ginerva Gambino, Cologne (both 2017); Kunsthaus Bregenz and JTT, 
New York (both 2016).

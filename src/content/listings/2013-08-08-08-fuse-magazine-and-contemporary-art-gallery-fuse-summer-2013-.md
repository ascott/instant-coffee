Wednesday, 14 August 2013, 19:00 - 22:00

Please join FUSE magazine and the Contemporary Art Gallery to celebrate the 
launch of the FUSE Summer 2013 issue: Survivors and Survivalists in the CAG 
Field House Studio space at the Burrard Marina. The evening will feature a 
musical performance by artist and Dhrupad vocalist Harkeerat Mangat from 7-
8pm followed by the DJ stylings of artist Helen Reed. Refreshments will be 
served. Suggested donation at the door includes a copy of the latest issue of 
FUSE and what is sure to be a fun evening on shore of False Creek.

Field House Studio at Burrard Marina
1655 Whyte Avenue

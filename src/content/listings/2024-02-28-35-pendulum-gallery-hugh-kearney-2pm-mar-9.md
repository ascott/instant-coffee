Hugh Kearney

Big Boy and his Adventures at the Circus

March 4 - March 28, 2024

Reception : Saturday March 9, 2-4pm

Hugh Kearneys new body of work is influenced by a combination of historical
abstraction, West Coast modernism, the urban landscape, and his Maliseet heritage.
The strong graphic forms in his paintings are most closely tied to the organic abstract
works of Miro and Calder, combined with the curvaceous forms of Coastal BC
indigenous art, with cross-hatched elements referencing the Maliseet art of weaving.

Gallery 295 is looking for gallery interns for the coming exhibition
season. Our interns are the cornerstone of the volunteer run space that
makes Gallery 295 an accessible artist run gallery. As a volunteer we are
requiring a weekly commitment during exhibition runs (one set day per week).
We require interns to fill gallery vacancies on Monday, Tuesday, or Fridays
from 1pm - 5pm. Each intern will take on a special project during their
internship assignment, coordinated with Gallery Director & Curator.

Interns receive 50% off film processing & printing at The Lab.

Send CV & letter of intent via email info@gallery295.com marked Attn:
Patryk Stasieczek & Hieu Nguyen by Saturday January 24th, 2015.

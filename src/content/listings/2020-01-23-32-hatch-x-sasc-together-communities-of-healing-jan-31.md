The AMS Sexual Assault Support Centre (SASC) invites you to contribute any piece of art
youve created, which embodies your healing journey and experiences. As transformative
processes are often made possible through the healing and care a community can provide, we
are hoping to capture and
elevate this concept in our upcoming exhibition Together:
Communities of Healing, taking place from February 24 " March 12 2020 at the Hatch Art
Gallery.

Submit your work to sascvolunteer@ams.ubc.ca by January 31st, 2020.

Kindly provide the following details in your message:

-Name and email (noting anonymity as desired)

-Media and approximate size

-A description of the work/artist statement (optional)

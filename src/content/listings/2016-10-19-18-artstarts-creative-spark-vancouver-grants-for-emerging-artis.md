Creative Spark Vancouver grants empower emerging artists to engage 
young people (ages 5-18) in arts-based projects. Emerging artists 
across all artistic disciplines are invited to apply. Applicants must be 
residents of the City of Vancouver or residents and members of the 
Musqueam, Squamish or Tsleil-Waututh First Nations. The grants offer 
100% funding for small projects, up to $1,000. Creative Spark 
Vancouver is disbursed by ArtStarts in Schools and funded by the City 
of Vancouver. Apply by December 15, 2016 at 
http://artstarts.com/creativesparkvancouver

Artist Talk & Tour
Friday, July 18 | 1:00 pm
Join Karen Yurkovich for a talk & tour of The Herbarium Project. Learn

Family Sketching Program
Saturday, August 2 | 1:00 pm
Join The Herbarium Project artist Karen Yurkovich in sketching specimens
from the Beaty Biodiversity Museum's collection. Paper, drawing materials,

draw!

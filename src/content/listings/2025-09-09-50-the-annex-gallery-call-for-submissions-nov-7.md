The Annex Gallery

Located right across the main entrance of the Richmond Cultural Centre, and on the
ground floor of Richmond Cultural Centre Annex, The Annex Gallery is an offsite
exhibition space overseen by Richmond Art Gallery. As part of its outreach to the
local community, The Annex Gallery is dedicated exclusively to Richmond-based
artists. The Annex Gallery does annual open calls and accepts submissions from
Richmond-based artists throughout the year. Comprising three major walls, The
Annex Gallery primarily showcases framed or ready-to-hang two-dimensional
artworks. There is also a 32 flat screen TV where videos can be displayed.

Next deadline: November 7, 2025 for 2026 programming

We offer CARFAC fees for Exhibitions in Other Public Places (2026 schedule): $594
for solo exhibitions; $297 for two-person exhibitions.

Selected artists will be contacted by September 29, 2025. Due to the volume of
submissions, only selected artists will be contacted. Submissions will be kept on file
for a period of up to one year for consideration.

Submission Checklist

Brief bio
One-page (maximum) artist statement
One-page statement of proposed work
Five to seven JPEGS (up to 300 dpi and 2 MB each) of proposed work through file-
sharing software Google Drive or Dropbox. Make sure the link is open as multiple
staff may view it using different emails
An image list with thumbnails of the work, artist name, title of the work, year,
medium, and dimensions
Website and/or any social media accounts
Please email complete submissions to galleria@richmondartgallery.org with the
subject line Annex Submission 2026. If you need assistance or any
accommodations, please dont hesitate to reach out via email.

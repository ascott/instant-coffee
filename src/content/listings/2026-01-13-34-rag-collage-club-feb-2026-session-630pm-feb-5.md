Looking for a fun, inspiring night out? Join our Collage Club for Adults-a relaxed, social
evening dedicated to art-making in a welcoming environment. No experience required, just
bring your imagination!

This month we're diving into Februllage, an international online collage challenge created
by The Edinburgh Collage Collective and The Scandinavian Collage Museum. The
challenge celebrates a daily collage practice throughout February using an official prompt
list. During our session, we'll tap into this global movement and create works inspired by
one of its 28 themes.

The evening begins with a slideshow of contemporary collage artists from around the world,
offering quick tips and techniques to spark your creativity. You'll have access to a wide
variety of paper ephemera while a curated playlist of collage-inspired videos play in the
background to keep the inspiration flowing.

No experience needed-just bring your creativity!

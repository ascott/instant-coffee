Subject: Your experience of looking for work.

Kunsthalle Schade* is a new
artist-led curatorial platform focused on developing new
modes of collective discursive practices.

This is our first research call:

We are interested in talking to you about your experience of looking for work in"
broadly speaking"creative field, within the last two years. Do you have thirty minutes
to an hour to take part?

All contributions are made on a free cooperation basis and your private information
(contact, name) will not be shared with the public. The video conversation will be
anonymized (image blur, voice augmentation).

If this sounds of interest, fill out the below contact form. After reviewing incoming
entries, we will be in touch to arrange the time of our video meeting.

https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSezCX2ZwFDNwEH_io2tywb5RXpl1-
aKrswqRdODwMwpu1NKMA/viewform?usp=sf_link

We are in UTC+2 or CEST time zone and use Jitsi. The contact form will be up online
until May 31, 2021.

*Self-initiated and self-funded, we forgo biographies, location services and
artist
statements, to focus on discrete, project-defined criteria.

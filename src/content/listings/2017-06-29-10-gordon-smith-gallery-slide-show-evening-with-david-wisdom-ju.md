EVENT- The Gordon Smith Gallery of Canadian Art- A Slide Show Evening

with David Wisdom

ART MUSIC SCHOOL CARS HIGH:

A Slide Show Evening with David Wisdom

Tuesday, July 4 th

Doors at 7, show at 7:30

Former CBC radio personality and photographer David Wisdom hosts one of his
legendary communal slideshows. Featuring local artists and arts practitioners, the 
evening is a warm gathering pairing selected images on the topics of art, music, 
cars, and school with music. Introductions and remarks will be made by Wisdom and 
featured artists Rodney Graham, Neil Wedman, and Kathy Slade- all significant 
artists in our current exhibition, Art School High.

Wisdom began presenting public slideshows in the late 1960s with a group of friends 
that included Jeff Wall, Ian Wallace, and Rodney Graham, the artists who became 
the core of the so-called Vancouver School of Photography. The four later went on to 
form the music project UJ3RK5 in the late 1970s. After 30 years as a network radio 
host on CBC Radio 2, Wisdom revived the public slide show concept retooled for the 
digital age. Since then he has presented multiartist shows in dozens of venues 
including the PuSH Festival in Vancouver, the Elks Hall in Sidney, Nebraska, Mahon 
Hall on Saltspring Island, and for the past eight years at the Vancouver Art Gallery.

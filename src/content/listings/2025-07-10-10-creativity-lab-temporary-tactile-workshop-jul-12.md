Temporary Tactile Workshop
July 12, 2025
1:00 PM - 3:00 PM
Location: Creativity Lab, Richmond Cultural Centre Annex, 7660 Minoru Gate

A workshop exploring mark-making that you can touch

Free art-making workshops designed to be accessible to Blind, d/Deaf, and Non-
Verbal communities, as well as non-Disabled community members.

Participants will explore making marks and illustrations using plastic/metal to create
tactile drawings through impressions, consider how to create a tactile language to
express ideas, and consider materials and touch as narrative tools. Hosted by
Vancouver Placemaking: Johnny Tiger, Kay Slater, Keimi Nakashima Ochoa.

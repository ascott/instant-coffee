Please join Jonathan Syme at the opening of his solo exhibition of new work
on May 6 from 6-8PM. In this new body of work Jonathan engages a
sophisticated use of colour to extend his complex visual analysis of form
within the structure of stripe paintings. His work maintains a very
contemporary sensibility while continuing to demonstrate a keen knowledge
and understanding of the history of abstract painting

Jonathan Syme
New Work
May 6 - June 5

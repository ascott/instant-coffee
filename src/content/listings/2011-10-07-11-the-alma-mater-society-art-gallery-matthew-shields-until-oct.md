MATTHEW SHIELDS
Manifesto: God of the Anabaptists of Munster and of the Revolutionary Peasant 
of 1525
September 23 to October 16, 2011

Opening reception: Thursday, September 23, 6:00pm to 9:00pm
Artist in attendance.

The Alma Mater Society gallery is pleased to announce a solo exhibition of new 
works by Matthew Shields entitled Manifesto: God of the Anabaptists of Munster 
and of the Revolutionary Peasant of 1525. 

Matthew Shields is a Vancouver based artist and writer. Photographs by Shields 
were recently featured in Hunter and Cook Magazine issue 08 2011. 

Curated by Mohammad Salemy
Gallery Hours: Tuesday - Saturday, 12 - 6pm
The Alma Mater Society Art Gallery
6138 Student Union Boulevard
Vancouver, BC, V6T 1Z1

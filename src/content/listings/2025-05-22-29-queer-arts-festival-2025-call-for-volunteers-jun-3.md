Call for Volunteers: Queer Arts Festival 2025

Queer Arts Festival 2025: Portals is actively seeking volunteers from June 6 - 28.

QAF is an annual artist-run, multi-disciplinary arts festival, recognized as one of the
top 5 festivals of its kind worldwide. QAF produces, presents, and exhibits with a
curatorial vision favouring challenging, thought-provoking work that pushes
boundaries and initiates dialogue. Each year, the festival theme ties together a
curated visual art exhibition, performing arts series, workshops, artist talks, and
more! Throughout the festival, we have a wide range of volunteer opportunities
suited for all ages, experience levels, and accessibility needs.

If you havent volunteered with us before, you can sign up with us now via our Better
Impact page. If youre already signed up, youll notice a variety of festival shifts &
positions are now up for grabs. Shifts range from gallery hosts to box office support,
to bartending! We ask that new volunteers sign-up for and attend one of our virtual
volunteer orientations, scheduled for Saturday May 31st or Tuesday June 3rd.

Besides being an awesome opportunity to connect with Vancouvers arts & Queer
scene, perks of volunteering with us include: A free QAF t-shirt, a free QAF
membership, and a chance to win the coveted Queer as Fuck award at our
volunteer appreciation party on July 9th!

More information can be found on our website:
https://queerartsfestival.com/volunteer/

September 12th-26th
Opening reception September 11th 7-11pm
Shudder Gallery 433 Columbia St Vancouver

New Work by Mairin Cooley, Lindsay Elliott, Mysa Kaczkowski, and Susan Roch.
Performances by Burrow Owl and Puppet Heresy featuring Jen Cole,
Caitlin Brown, and Julie Chapple.
Performances commence at 7:30pm.

Shudder gallery is featuring an all female show for Swarm 2009. "Some Girl"
which will incorporate photography, dance, music, and mixed media.

Burrow Owl is an artist based in the downtown eastside of Vancouver
working primarily with analogue sound for live performance, recording
and installation work. Her work begins in experimentation with
obsolete electronics and the perforative presence of the artist in
engagement and conflict with the audience. Individual performances and
works develop from an improvisation expanding her studio experiments
to a finished works focused on the intensity and immediacy of the
viewers/listeners experience. In addition to her independent practice
Burrow Owl also works in numerous collaborative projects including
theinternet!, sex in the woods and formally the Her Jazz Noise
Collective.

Visual artist, film maker, and designer Mairin Cooley and photographer
Lindsay Elliott collaborate on a series of photographs entitled Kin
Prints// Negative Edge Patterns. The series of photographs looks at
the relationship between skin as a necessity and a decorative means.
Skin is thought of a coverage against exposure and the elements,
cloth is the coverage for the skin, cloth is also used to express
one's status, taste, and personality, comparatively skin is rarely on
display. The series challenges these concepts.

Puppet Heresy is a dance collective formed in 2009. Puppet Heresy
seeks to create accessible yet exploratory works that are enticing to
alternative audiences. The aim is to connect with the viewers on a
human level puts us in unconventional venues and focuses on
conversational performance material. Puppet Heresy will be performing
a piece entitled "Know What I Mean?" Communication is great - when it
works. In a social atmosphere, watch as they explore the frustration,
determination and acceptance of making themselves understood.

Mysa Kaczkowski is featuring a series of self portraits that explore
the concept of self, who are we, how we represent ourselves, and how
others perceive us. Mysa uses decaying industrial sites, and epic
landscapes' for the new work.

Susan Roch will be working with projected images as an exploration of
landscape. A collection of images taken in the neighbourhood and then
returned to the same street. The landscape presented as landscape in
situ.

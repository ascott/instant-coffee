Free admission and activities at Kelowna Art Gallery on Canada Day

What: Free Admission + Family Art Activities

When: July 1, 2025, 10 am - 4 pm

Who: Everyone Welcome

The Kelowna Art Gallery is inviting families of all ages and compositions to add a
creative spin to their celebrations on Canada Day. To make it an easy decision, they
will be offering free admission and a variety of free activities for everyone on Tuesday,
July 1, 2025, from 10 am to 4 pm.

The Kelowna Art Gallery is located at 1315 Water Street, in downtown Kelowna, B.C.

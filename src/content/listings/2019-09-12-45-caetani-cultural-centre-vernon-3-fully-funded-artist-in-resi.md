Three two-month residencies in 2020

DATES: May/June 2020, July/August 2020, September/October 2020
RATES: no fee 
APPLICATION DEADLINE: October 1st, 2019

WHO IS ELIGIBLE: open to emerging and established visual artists in most mediums: 
installation, earthworks, sculpture, painting, drawing, photography, digital media, 
printmaking, etc. 

INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: accommodations and private studio space.

The writer stays in their own spacious room with shared accommodations (kitchen, 
bathroom, living room, entryway) at the Caetani Cultural Centre (a heritage house 
walking distance of downtown Vernon.) They have access to a private studio in the 
backyard of the Caetani Centre grounds.

NOT INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: travel expenses, food, health insurance, writing 
materials, shipping costs and other personal expenses.

artist-residency/

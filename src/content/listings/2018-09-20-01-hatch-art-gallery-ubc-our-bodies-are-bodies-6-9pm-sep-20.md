Opening Reception: Hatch Art Gallery, Our Bodies Are Bodies, September 20th 
Hatch Gallery
289-2366 Main Mall, Vancouver, BC

Our Bodies Are Bodies is a multi-disciplinary art exhibition running in the Hatch Gallery from 
September 19 to October 18. This exhibition is an inquiry into the representation of bodies"
how bodies are depicted, who has depicted them, and how this affects our understanding of 
the subject as well as our relationships to our own bodies. The works in this exhibition reflect 
on issues of race, representation, sexuality, queerness, mental health, and the digital body.

Join us at The Hatch on Thursday, September 20 for the opening reception of Our Bodies 
Are Bodies, from 6 to -9pm. The reception includes a performance piece, a drag 
performance, and a musical number. Performances start around 7pm.

The exhibition features work by:

Joy Gyamfi
Kayt Willemina
Ariel Elise
Tyler Homan
Aileen Yadokoro
Alexandra Rodriguez 
Vicky Mo
Chloe Sernasie

The exhibition is curated by VISA Undergraduate student Julia Wong.

The University of British Columbia is founded on the Unceded lands of the Coast Salish, 
Musqueam, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples. The Hatch Gallery is located on the second floor of 
the Student Nest (6133 University Blvd, Vancouver, BC) and is accessible via the elevators.

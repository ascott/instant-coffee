Flicia Atkinson improvising animals as delayed audiobooks in an ambient park
Flicia Atkinson, artist, experimental musician, and founder of the independent imprint Shelter 
Press, makes work that broadly thinks about improvisation, fiction, instant composition, noise, 
abstraction and poetry. For Scriveners Monthly, Flicia will present a talk about her publishing 
practice through the lens of some of her own books such as Ambient Park, Animals, Audio 
Book, and Improvising Sculpture As Delayed Fictions.

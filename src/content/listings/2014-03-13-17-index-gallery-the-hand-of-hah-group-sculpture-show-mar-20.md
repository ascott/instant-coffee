The Hand of Hah 

Group Sculpture Show/ Emerging Artists Maggie Boyd, Brennan Kelly, 
Sydney Koke & Alex Stursberg 

Index Gallery
1305 Powell St
Opening Reception 7pm-11 March 20th
For viewings please contact thehandofhah@gmail.com 

Against the backdrop of carefully polished, sometimes alienating, Vancouver 
based sculpture, the often overlooked craft and experimentation of Maggie 
Boyd, Brennan Kelly, Sydney Koke and Alex Stursberg, reveal a surprising 
depth of content, persistence & humility. 

The Hand of Hah explores intuitive investigation and the quietly defiant nature 
of a practice of materiality.

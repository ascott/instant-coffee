Intersecting Orbits: Michael Morris and Joan Balzar
January 27 - May 5, 2024

Opening Reception: Friday, January 26, 2024, 6:00-8:00PM

Curated by Lisa Baldissera and David MacWilliam

Joan Balzar (1928-2016) and Michael Morris (1942-2022) were foundational to the
development of abstraction and conceptualism in British Columbia. To be within their
orbit was to be part of a movement which shaped and internationalized regional visual
art in the 1960s and 70s, and went on to become enduringly echoed and quoted. Co-
curated by Lisa Baldissera and David MacWilliam, Intersecting Orbits presents works
by both artists, as well as works from Morriss collection and archive.

A generation apart in age, Balzar and Morris studied with the same painters (Jack
Shadboldt, Joe Plaskett, Peter Aspell, Roy Kiyooka and Don Jarvis) at the Vancouver
School of Art. They both achieved early success in the later 1960s, with expansively
scaled, hard-edge abstract paintings and later, their conceptual explorations. Morris
oversaw an art collection that forms a personal, yet important
legacy of a number of
overlapping histories in the contemporary art of Western Canada.

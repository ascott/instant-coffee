The Ferry Building Gallery is now accepting submissions for our annual Great Gifts
exhibition and sale, which will take place from November 15th to December 15th, 2024.
This a fantastic opportunity to showcase and sell unique handmade crafts, fine artwork,
and distinctive gifts.

Artists are invited to submit their work ONLINE only by September 20, 2024. A $20
exhibition fee applies, and the gallery retains a 30% commission on
sales, with the
remaining proceeds going directly to the artists.
Selected artists will be asked to submit for a second round, an in-person adjudication to
be held on Saturday, September 28, from 9 to 11 a.m. at the Music Box, 1564 Argyle
Ave, West Vancouver.
To be considered, artwork must be original, not mass-produced or made from kits, and
should not exceed a value of $300 per piece.
Interested artists can apply here. For the jury fee, registration is available by phone at
604-925-7270 (course #171698) or online.
For more information, please visit our website here.

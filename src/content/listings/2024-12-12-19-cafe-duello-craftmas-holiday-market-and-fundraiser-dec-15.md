Craftmas Holiday Market and Fundraiser with Registered
Autism Service Providers
and Cafe Duello is a fundraiser for children with autism to better access Registered
Autism Service Providers.

Date: Dec 15th, 10am-3pm
Location: Cafe Duello, 426 W Hastings St.
Free entry

Featuring:
Artwork by participating children
Curated goods like toys, handmade gifts, and more
Raffle prizes from Dressew and Vancouver Christmas Market
Clothing Donation Hamper with Revivify Textile Recycling and Big Brothers and
Sisters Canada

Activities include:
Holiday card station
DIY arts and crafts booth
Sensory art display by Suze Shore
Lego play area
Reading corner

Craftmas is organized by Annie Hung, with the support of 3 Registered Autism
Service Providers and Cafe Duello. Recently, she curated First Generation, an
artexhibit featuring the work of immigrant seniors and children for the 2024 STAND
Festival. She is passionate about supporting marginalized demographics in her
community. When she isn't organizing community art events, Annie teaches art to
children
in Burnaby Community Schools while studying for her doctorate at SFU.

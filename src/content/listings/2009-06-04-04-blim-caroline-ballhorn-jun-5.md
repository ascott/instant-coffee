Caroline Ballhorn is a Vancouver-based artist whose practice moves between sculpture, print, 
drawing, and textile-based work. She has a major interest in paper and all things made by hand, 
which has evolved into a love of craft and DIY. Her newest work includes a giant hand-cut 
wooden doily, as well as three-dimensional cut paper drawings. Referencing the vocabulary and 
tools associated with craft, Caroline’s work draws attention to the connection between labour and
meaning as it relates to constructed notions about the feminine.

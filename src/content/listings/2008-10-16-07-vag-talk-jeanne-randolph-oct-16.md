Public Talk

Jeanne Randolph
The Thrill is Gone

Thursday, October 16 at 7pm
at the Vancouver Art Gallery

Jeanne Randolph's copiously illustrated talk, The Thrill is Gone, will
explore the ironies, utopian remnants and dilemmas of representation
relevant to 21st century feminist art.

Since the publication of Psychoanalysis & Synchronized Swimming in 1991
Dr. Jeanne Randolph has continued to write about the intertwining of art
and such major themes as technology, advertising, psychoanalytic theory,
the politics of representation, and mass-mediated phenomena. Her most
recent book Ethics of Luxury: Materialism and Imagination was launches this
month at Artspeak Gallery on October 8th.

Free with Gallery admission.

WACK! Art and the Feminist Revolution

Vancouver Art Gallery

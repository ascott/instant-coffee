Metaphors are all around us and shape how we see the world. When we create new
metaphors for our experience, we open the door to new ways of seeing, feeling, and
engaging with life.

In this free intro to an 8-week poetry writing series, you'll discover what metaphor has
to do with renewal, engage with your fears about metaphor, and give voice to any
beliefs/assumptions you may have about whether or not you have the ability to write
creatively in this way. We'll begin this intro with conversation. Afterwards, you'll engage
with a guided writing practice where you'll create an original metaphor for a personal
experience"such as creativity, uncertainty, or time"and notice how ease, rather than
effort, can support the creative process. If space allows: using your metaphor as a
seed, youll explore the beginning process of writing a poem. No prior experience with
poetry writing is necessary to participate.

Art + Technology Instructor
(Specialist)
New Media Gallery, Anvil Centre
New Westminster, British Columbia

The Art + Technology division oversees the delivery of New Media Gallery (NMG), a
contemporary public gallery, owned and operated by the City of New Westminster,
and located at Anvil Centre. The Art + Technology LAB is the education space
managed by NMG, offering the equipment, technologies, and expertise necessary to
deliver a rich array of public, school, outreach, and professional programs. We are
looking for a dynamic instructor to join our faculty and develop unique public and
school workshops which incorporate technology while teaching the fundamentals of
art and/or design. You have the ability to propose and develop original workshops
(curriculum and instruction) and organize your time. Ideally you will have experience
with the new BC school curriculum. Our initial focus for this hiring is on finding an
individual with solid experience in early childhood education, ages 3-7.

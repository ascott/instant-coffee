Unbound Gestures: Exhibition of works by Deer Lake Artists in Residence 2023

Featuring work by Brit Bachmann, Tamana S. H. Djuya, Rob Froese, Michelle
Grimm, Cat Hart, SF
Ho, Azadeh Mehryar, Crystal Noir, Nazanin Oghanian, Kennedy
Snider, and Mandy Tsung

Visual Arts Exhibition: February 17-April 18 at Centre Aisle Gallery & Encores Caf

Ceramic Arts Exhibition: February 21-March 31 at Shadbolt Atrium & Galleria

Reception & Artist Talks on Thursday, February 29 at 6pm in Studio 103

Shadbolt Centre for the Arts

6450 Deer Lake Avenue, Burnaby BC

Free Parking

This exhibition features work by the 2023 cohort of Deer Lake Artists in Residence,
representing ceramics, visual art, land-based art, creative writing, and new media.
Deer Lake Artist Residencies, based on the ancestral and unceded territories of the
hn"q"min"m" and Skwxw7mesh speaking peoples at Deer Lake Park, provides
unique opportunities for emerging and established artists from various disciplines,
cultures and locations to create and produce new work. Applications for the 2025
DLAR are open from March 8-April 8, 2024. For more information, visit
www.burnaby.ca/air

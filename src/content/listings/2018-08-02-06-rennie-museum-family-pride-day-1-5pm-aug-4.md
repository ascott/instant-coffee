Saturday, August 4, 2018 
1:00 PM - 5:00 PM
Register on www.renniemuseum.org 

Rennie Museum
51 East Pender Street
Vancouver, BC V6A 1S9

Rennie Museum welcomes families with individuals that identify as LGBTQI+ and/or Two-Spirit, 
as well as their allies to its very first Family Pride Day! Join an interactive tour of our Kerry 
James Marshall: Collected Works exhibition. Enjoy refreshments on our rooftop sculpture 
garden as your children explore gender fluidity and self-expression during Drag Queen Story 
Hour featuring Gina Tonic. Finish the day with a hands-on art workshop inspired by the 
festivities of Vancouver Pride Week.

Family Pride Day is FREE and open to families with children who are 5 years of age or older. 
Snacks and refreshments will be provided. RSVPs are first come first served and limited to two 
adults per reservation.

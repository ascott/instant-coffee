Modeling itself after Tokyo's popular music jams held at famous Yoyogi
Park, The Mile Long Jam - Vancouver, is the first ever linear audio art
installation. The Mile Long Jam will attempt to mix, blend, layer, &
overlap both human energy & sound, to form a continuous musical art piece
The Mile Long Jam takes place on Saturday July 17th, along Vancouver's
popular & trendy, Main Street.

The places to play, will be in front of or near popular cafes, restaurants,
& some of Vancouver coolest shops, where each Musician or Band will be free
to perform, meet, entertain, & solicit their original music for the day in
the company of other amateur & professional Musicians, Bands, DJs,
Ensembles, Orchestras, Quartets, Vocalists, Singers, etc.

To join The Mile Long Jam, register at: themilelongjam@gmail.com Email us
your name, a contact number, the name of your Act - if you have one,
including the number of performers, type of music, & your choice on Main
Street where you'd like to play, be heard, & be seen.

The Mile Long Jam : Vancouver
July 17th
11am - 7pm
Main St, Vancouver
from Main Street Skytrain Station to King Edward

To register: themilelongjam@gmail.com

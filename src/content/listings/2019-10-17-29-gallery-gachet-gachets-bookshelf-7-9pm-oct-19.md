Gachets Bookshelf

Launch: Saturday, October 19 7pm - 9pm
Hours: October 22 - 26 12pm - 6pm

Gallery Gachet Foyer, 9 W Hastings

Stop by the foyer of Gallery Gachet for the launch of Gachets Bookshelf. Researched
and published by artists in the DTES,
Gachets Bookshelf is an annotated bibliography
in zine format compiling life writing, fiction, critical psy and dis/ability studies, poetry, and
artist books on topics of madness, mental health, substance use, art, and creativity.
During Vancouver
Art Book Week, the zine will be adapted as an installation in Gallery
Gachets foyer, including sound recordings and selected works available for reading and
viewing. We welcome visitors to contribute titles, books, annotations, and illustrations to
this community bibliography and archive. Meanwhile in the main gallery, dont miss the
12th Annual Oppenheimer Park Community Art Show.

Gallery Jones is pleased to present new work by DANNY SINGER.

opening reception: March 3, 6pm

Danny Singer's subjects are the small towns on the prairies and 
plains of North America. The relationships between the landscape, 
the buildings, the inhabitants and, at times, even the weather, form 
the content of the images, revealed in both the minute details and 
the vast expanse of the work.

Singer's photographs are in the collections of the National Gallery of 
Canada, the Canada Council Art Bank, the Glenbow Museum, the 
Mendel Art Gallery and the Vancouver Art Gallery, to name a few.

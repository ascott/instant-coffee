Author bio:

Madeleine Thien is the author of the story collection, Simple Recipes (Emblem 
2001, Winner of the Ethel Wilson Fiction Prize and the City of Vancouver Book 
Award), and the novels Certainty (Emblem 2006) and Dogs at the Perimeter 
(McClelland and Stewart 2012, awarded the Frankfurt Book Fair's 2015 
Liberaturpreis, celebrating avant-garde literature by women writers). Her novels 
and stories have been translated into 25 languages, and her essays have 
appeared in Granta, The Guardian, The Financial Times, Five Dials, Brick, Al 
Jazeera and elsewhere. Her new novel, Do Not Say We Have Nothing, won the 
Giller Prize and the Governor-General's Literary Award in 2016.

Afternoon School: Presented as part of Safe Assembly 2010

Afternoon School consists of both planned and spontaneous seminars, with
examples of skill sharing, media activism, screenings from the Video Out
archive with its rich history of protest in Vancouver, and discussions
using critical theory and contemporary art to produce a counter-public.
Curated by cheyanne turions and Lois Klassen. Co-presented with VIVO Media
Arts.

A free school when there is no school, every weekday of the spectacle.

Detailed information about Afternoon School presentations can be found
here: www.cineworks.ca/events/afternoon_school
Full information about the larger Safe Assembly project can be found here:

UNIT/PITT Society for Art and Critical Awareness:
CALL FOR BOARD MEMBERS

Position: Board Member
Salary: Volunteer Position
Hours: 4+ Hours/Month
Closing Date: August 16, 2024

Call for Board Members!

Interested in getting involved with UNIT/PITT? We are searching for new Board
Members!

UNIT/PITT is seeking new board members in a time of exciting change as we
embark on our 50th year (!!). If you are interested in supporting emergent and
experimental artist practices within Vancouvers unique artist-run community, please
fill out an application by August 16th at midnight.

Qualities we are searching for:
Contributes new
perspectives to our governance operations
Interested in community
Encourages decolonizing practices within governance, programming, and operations
Knowledge or willingness to learn budgeting and financial management within a
charitable nonprofit context
Experience or interest in learning grant writing
Openness to process, thoughtful reflection, and collaboration
Finance, fundraising, and HR experience an asset
Eligible candidates will be contacted by August 19th.

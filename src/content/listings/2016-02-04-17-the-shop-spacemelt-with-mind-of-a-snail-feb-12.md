SpaceMelt with Mind of a Snail
9pm, Friday, February 12, 2016
The Shop (at Renegade Productions)
125 East 2nd Avenue, Vancouver, BC,

Experimental art+music collective SpaceMelt returns to Vancouver for
another evening of unbridled creation and collaboration! The group is
celebrating their 7th Anniversary with their largest and most ambitious
performance to date.

SpaceMelt has assembled some of the best musicians and visual
artists in Canada for a one night only completely improvised
performance, the kind of insanity that you've only read about in art
history textbooks that talk of Tristan Tzara and Hugo Ball performing at
Cabaret Voltaire, and the "Happenings" of Cage, Rauschenberg and
Merce Cunningham that took place in the 1950's.

Formed by visual artist Michael Markowsky (painting and sculpture)
and musicians Sam Davidson (clarinet & EWI) and Terri Hron
(recorder) at the Banff Centre in 2009, SpaceMelt aims to bring
together diverse creative talent, push the boundaries of the spaces
they inhabit and to test the very definition of performance.

SpaceMelt is excited and proud to announce this year's collaboration
with critically-acclaimed shadow puppet troupe Mind of a Snail, who
have thrilled audiences across North America! Formed in 2003, the duo
of Chlo Ziner and Jessica Gabriel have won numerous awards
including the the Best of Fest at the Edmonton Fringe Festival in 2015,
and Patron's Pick at the Vancouver, Toronto and Winnipeg Fringe
Festivals in 2014.

Additional guest performers include renowned composer and
electronics musician Giorgio Magnanensi, vocalist Camille Hesketh,
percussionist Ben Brown, and french horn player Micajah Sturgess.

The performance will take place in "The Shop," the former rehearsal
space for the Vancouver Playhouse, which is a traditional "black-box"
theatre-type venue with plush, velvet seats for the supreme comfort of
audience members!

The Polygon Gallery
101 Carrie Cates Ct, North Vancouver, BC
730-930

Join Swiss visual artist and bookmaker Batia Suter who will introduce her art practice 
followed by a conversation with curator Helga Pakasaar. This dramatic installation of 
photographic reproductions sourced from thousands of books from her personal 
collection are based on Suters latest artist book, Parallel Encyclopedia #2 (2016), for 
which she was shortlisted for the Deutsche Brse Photography Foundation Prize. Based 
in Amsterdam, Suter has published numerous artist books and has exhibited widely, 
most recently at Le Bal in Paris.

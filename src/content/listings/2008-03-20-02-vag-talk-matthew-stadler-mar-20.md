Public Lecture
Matthew Stadler
Going Nowhere: Where Was Paradise?

Thursday, March 20 at 7pm

at the Vancouver Art Gallery in Room 4East

Join writer Matthew Stadler as he discusses ideas of paradise and the work of Kutlug Ataman. In his newest video installation, Ataman offers a remarkable portrait of twenty-four southern Californians who describe their encounter with that place they call “paradise.” Stadler will discuss the ways that Ataman's paradise emerges from the orientalist traditions of the 18th and 19th centuries - searching for a fantasy "other world" far from home. The inquiry is complicated by this increasingly common fact of living globally...where is home? And if home is no longer geographically bounded, where is “paradise”, that place from which we have been expelled? In this lecture Stadler will explore the rupture of these once opposed searches: the utopian search for home, and the orientalist search for paradise.

 

Matthew Stadler is the author of four novels, including Landscape: Memory, The Sex Offender, and Allan Stein. He is a past contributor to the short-lived Dutch architecture journal Wiederhall, Frieze Magazine, Arcade, The New York Times, and The Stranger. He is the literary editor of Nest magazine and editor and co-founder of Clear Cut Press.

 

Free with gallery admission.

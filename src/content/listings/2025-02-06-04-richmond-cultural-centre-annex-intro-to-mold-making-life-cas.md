Intro to Mold-Making: Life Casting

Richmond Cultural Centre Annex
Learn how to mix, pour and create an alginate mold and how to work with plaster.
Develop the skills that sculptors and prop makers use to safely make plaster positive
casts and create a detailed cast of your hand. Participants can expect to get messy.
Beginner and intermediate mold-making students are welcome. Supplies included to
produce one mold and cast.

Instructor: Vanessa Mercedes Figueroa

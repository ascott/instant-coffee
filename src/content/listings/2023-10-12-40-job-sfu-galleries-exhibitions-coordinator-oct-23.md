JOB POSTING: Exhibitions Coordinator

Union/Affiliation: CUPE
Pay range: $29.78/hr ($54,199 per annum) plus benefits
SFU Department: SFU Galleries
Position Grade: 8
# of openings: 1
Biweekly Hours: 70

SFU Galleries are
dynamic centres for the presentation and interrogation of art
practices and ideas. There are three distinct galleries: SFU Gallery on the Burnaby
campus, Audain Gallery at the Goldcorp Centre for the Arts in Vancouver, and Teck
Gallery at Harbour Centre in Vancouver. The forthcoming Marianne and Edward
Gibson Art Museum, on the Burnaby campus,
will join this suite of spaces in early
2025.

Stewarded by SFU Galleries, the SFU Art Collection includes contemporary and
historical works of art from regional, national, and international artists. The SFU Art
Collection has grown from its inception in the late 1960s to include more than 5,800
works of art in 2021. The collection is diverse and is an important repository for
modern
and contemporary works of art regionally and nationally. Approximately 1,000 works
of
art are shown on SFU campuses in public locations year-round. The gallerys
collection
is an invaluable resource for study and exhibition.

The Exhibitions Coordinator supports the
curatorial program by coordinating
exhibitions, exhibition receptions and other related public events presented across
all
of SFU Galleries exhibition sites and platforms. Reporting to the Curator (and to the
Director, when she undertakes curatorial projects), the role coordinates external
service providers including artwork shipments and
exhibition documentation; liaises
with community partners and collaborating SFU academic units when necessary;
schedules the installation and de-installation of gallery exhibitions in collaboration
with
the Curator; responds to enquiries; coordinates the activities of volunteers and
gallery
attendants; schedules and supervises exhibition preparators and exhibition
documentation; tracks and maintains records of the Galleries expenses related to
exhibitions and related programming; word processes correspondence; and orders
supplies. The Exhibition Coordinator also provides administrative support to the
Curator by drafting artist contracts; preparing
grant applications; updating website
and
preparing HTML announcements if necessary, and directed by the Communications
Manager; and supporting the coordination of activities for the Audain Artist-in
Residence program. Additionally the Coordinator welcomes and orients visitors to
gallery when sitting the welcome desk, and offers short
introductory tours of presented
exhibitions when requested.

Candidate Profile:

The ideal candidate for this role is a respectful and inclusive team player, a clear and
effective communicator. They are collaborative and self-reflextive. They able to plan
and prioritize tasks and maintain a view of the big picture while focusing on all details
of exhibition coordination.

Top Qualifications:

- strong understanding of contemporary art practices, particularly their breadth of
materials and technical demands

- exceptional organizational and time management skills

- exceptional oral and written interpersonal communication skills

- ability to coordinate
the work of others in a fast-paced environment within strict
timelines

- excellent listener, with a commitment to working well in a small and active team

- undergraduate degree in visual art, art history or related field an asset

SFU Galleries is dedicated to building a diverse staff and advancing the rights and
inclusion of
equity-deserving groups and communities, including, Indigenous peoples,
racialized persons, persons with disabilities and 2SLGBTQI people. We are
interested
in candidates with a commitment to equity, social justice, Indigenous Truth and
Reconciliation, diversity and inclusion. Equity-deserving people are strongly
encouraged to apply.

Please include your cover letter and resume in one attachment.

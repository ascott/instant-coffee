Jessica Jang and Sadie Cha at Little Mountain
Opening Friday Aug. 15th
Reception at 8pm.

195 e 26th Ave.
Vancouver B.C

Little Mountain Studios is very Proud to present, Return the Tiger to
the Mountain, a show of new work by Jessica
Jang and Sadie Cha.
Two painter's whose practice is inspired by the masters, and whose
work fuses modern elements within a historical painting framework.
For those of you weary of half finished doodles, these two painters
offer a refreshing
commitment and dedication to the craft of painting. Don't miss this
show folks, these paintings are very beautiful and well worth checking
out.

Sadie's work is immersed within the art historical context, often
paying direct homage to painters such as Fragonard, Vermeer, and
Velasquez. Within that traditional discourse she mixes in pop cultural
elements to question post-colonial themes. There is an immediate
humorous nature that surfaces from her work as it inundates the viewer
with the elegance and over-embellished picture plane (often
incorporating sparkles and rhinestones. The titles of the pieces come
from hip hop songs.

Jessica's work deals with dystopian landscapes. The landscapes are
imagined, apocalyptic, and implicate the viewer as the singular
witness to natural disaster and loss. The paintings have an
exaggerated palette that that intends to capitalize on the confusion
of the scenario.

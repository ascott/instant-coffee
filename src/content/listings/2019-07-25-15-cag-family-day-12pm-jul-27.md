Family Day | Assemblage

Saturday, July 27, 12-3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

On one Saturday per month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for free art-making activities 
that respond to our current exhibitions.

Inspired by Maryam Jafris sculptural works, join us to create your own assemblage 
using found materials and clay forming surprising connections in three dimensions.

No reservation required, simply just turn up!

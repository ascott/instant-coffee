Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents new work by Featured Artist 
Sandeep Johal.

Sandeep Johal is a Vancouver based visual artist whose colorful geometric forms, and 
intricate black and white line work are aesthetically and conceptually inspired by her 
South Asian heritage. Johal believes in the power of art to create awareness around 
issues related to cultural identity, gender equality, and human rights. Her art practice is 
an expression of her social and cultural concerns, particularly gender justice.

Featured in the Art Rental & Sales showroom from March 25 to June 7, 2019 
Open weekdays, 10am - 5pm
First floor Annex, Vancouver Art Gallery

750 Hornby Street, Vancouver

Art Rental & Sales is a not-for-profit program run by the Vancouver Art Gallery.

About VIVO Media Arts Centre:

VIVO Media Arts Centre is seeking an individual to fill the role of Interim
General Manager.
VIVO Media Arts Centre is one of Canada's pioneering artist-run centres.
VIVO's mandate is to directly support artists and independent
community-based producers to develop, exchange, and disseminate their
skills in a supportive environment through accessible services and
programs. Our vision is a robust, diverse, and vibrant media arts sector: a
catalyst for critical and innovative engagement with the material forms and
cultural meanings of media and technology. VIVO benefits from an
international reputation built over its rich 43 year history.

To support this mandate we offer programming in five key areas:
-Technical: Access to the material necessities for quality production
through affordable equipment rental, editing facilities, software, and
production space.
-Education: A broad range of skill development opportunities that
encourage the exploration of technology and aesthetics within a critical,
artistic framework.
-Events and Exhibitions: The programming, facilitation, and execution
of events, exhibitions, residencies, co-productions, critical forums for a
variety of communities
-Distribution: International distribution, work exchange, and media
arts preservation which support the aspirations and livelihood of artists.
-Archive: Western Canada's largest public reference library and
archive of media art, independent video, and related publications,
documents, audio recordings, and photographs.

Interim General Manager Position:

VIVO Media Arts seeks a knowledgeable, skilled, and personable Interim
General Manager who can step directly into a leadership position This key
role will be of interest to arts professionals with a passion for
contemporary art, a wide knowledge of media art and new media practices, a
creative vision and team-leading abilities. It presents the opportunity to
make a significant contribution to the arts in Vancouver and beyond. The
successful candidate will be an individual who is energized by the
challenge of working with the history of this longstanding arts organization.

During the six-month contract, the Board of Directors will undertake a
search for permanent leadership of the Centre. The Interim General Manager
will be eligible to apply for the permanent position.

Responsibilities:

Reporting directly to the Board of Directors, the Interim General Manager will:
-Oversee the operations of the Centre
-Provide HR oversight: have input into hiring decisions, supervise
and mentor staff
-Oversee the initiatives set out by the education, programming,
technical and distribution department managers
-Work with staff and the bookkeeper to develop project budgets,
secure funds, and provide financial reporting for grants
-Oversee the overall Centre budget, and provide monthly reports to
the board
-Work with the staff and board to ensure financial and risk
management controls are in place and that VIVO meets all governance and
reporting requirements
-As the main staff liaison to the Board of Directors, ensure
transparent communication, work to build the staff/board connection,
provide expertise to the board, and support the board and its committees
with appropriate administration and reports
-Facilitate policy development
-Facilitate stakeholder engagement, build consensus and communicate
effectively
-Act as key spokesperson representing VIVO externally
-Foster partnerships within the local, provincial, and national arts
communities
-Develop community and member relations and promote VIVO locally,
nationally and internationally

Qualifications:

The ideal candidate will have:
-An MA, MFA or equivalent experience in a relevant discipline
-An understanding of the artist-run centre movement and its principal
concerns
-An excellent working knowledge of contemporary media art practices
-Enthusiasm for the position and the organization
-The ability to foster a healthy collegial working environment
-Excellent budget preparation, financial management and experience
working within fixed budgets
-Excellent communication, presentation, writing, and time management
skills
-Strong planning and organizational skills including the ability to
prioritize competing tasks
-Proven managerial experience, creativity and vision
-Experience working with a Board of Directors
-Experience with office and data management software, mailing lists,
and membership databases

Remuneration:

$45,000-$49,000 per annum plus 4% vacation pay.

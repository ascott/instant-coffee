Daina Augaitis on
The Tree: From the Sublime to the Social
 
 
Sunday, February 3, 2:00pm
at the Vancouver Art Gallery
 
 
Join Vancouver Art Gallery Chief Curator Daina Augaitis for a tour of The Tree: From the Sublime to the Social. 
 
Daina Augaitis has been Chief Curator/Associate Director at the Vancouver Art Gallery since 1996.  She was formerly the Director of the Visual Arts Program at The Banff Centre for the Arts, and has held curatorial positions at the Walter Phillips Gallery, Western Front, Convertible Showroom and Franklin Furnace. In the last decade she has organized numerous solo exhibitions of artists such as Stan Douglas, Ann Hamilton, Brian Jungen, Gillian Wearing, Paul Wong, Zhu Jinshi and her thematic exhibitions have often explored socially-based ideas.  She has edited anthologies, worked with artists on new commissions, curated spoken word, pirate radio and performance art projects and organized artist residencies.  
Free with Gallery admission
 
For information phone 604-662-4717

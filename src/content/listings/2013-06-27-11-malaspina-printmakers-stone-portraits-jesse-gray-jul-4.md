Jesse Gray has been participating in a print research residency at Malaspina's 
studio on Granville Island for the past six months. Her upcoming exhibition 
consists of black and white prints lifted from eight limestone slabs that make up 
a portion of Malaspina's lithography library. Each print records small geological 
impressions within the stone that could be fragmented pieces of fossilized 
organisms. Opens on Thursday July 4, 2013, from 6 to 9pm. Gray is a 
Vancouver-based multi-disciplinary artist who uses a process of collecting and 
archiving to investigate the hidden meanings and invisible histories of 
manufactured objects and systems in the natural world.

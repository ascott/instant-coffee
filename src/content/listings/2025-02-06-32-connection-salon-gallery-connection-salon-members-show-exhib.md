Opening:
Connection Salon Members Show Exhibition February 20th

Place: Connection Salon Gallery, 609 Helmcken

Connection Salon Artists Collectives mission is to provide low barrier opportunities
to artists that are underrepresented and have lived experience with mental health.
This is the annual collective members show.

10 visual artists will be on show February 14 to April 14th

Join us for the Meet the Artists event February 20th, 2025 from 5:30 to 7:00PM with
Music with collective members Mike Levin and the Astronauts Socks and Spoken
Word with Kagan Goh.

The exhibition is supported by the City of Vancouver, the Connection Salon
Collective and the Gathering Place Community Center.

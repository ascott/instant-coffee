INNER LANDSCAPES

Tread through the meadows of tall grass and bitter-sweet air
as the sky becomes shrouded in the shadows of daybreak.

Fog hugs the skin so that I don't know where it ends and you begin.

All the while, you are ankle deep in the malaise of my yesteryear
and the hopes that are bubbling up from the bog of self-worth.

My Inner Landscape

***

Inner Landscapes is the first collaboration between painter Kaz Simpson and
collage artist Jordan Westre. The show will be comprised of over 30 pieces.
Ranging from oil paintings and collage to sculpture and interactive works.
Each piece will dissect the idea of the "unspoken and unseen" within all of
us through a strong figurative narrative.

Kaz Simpson's work focuses on the ambivalent nature of communication
through facial cues. His method involves contrasting realism with
patterning and geometric shapes, which works to offer a glimpse at the
unuttered and veiled in human faces.

Kazimirsimpson.ca

Jordan Westre's work utilizes imagery sourced from various forms of print
media, often contrasting vintage landscape and advertising with modern
fashion photography. It is through this juxtaposition that Westre aims to
engage the viewer - to have them reconsider context and create new
scenarios for her protagonists.

OPEN CALL - MiNute Art Residency

-Call closes on Monday November 20th
This is the call for residencies during Jan, Feb, Mar 2025. you would
receive a response by Nov 29th.
MiNute is grateful to be placed on the traditional
and ancestral land
of Swxw7mesh and shshlh nations, town of Xwesam, known as Roberts
Creek, BC.

MiNute Artist Research and Residency, provides residency to electronic
artists, musicians, writers, artists in research phase of project,
performers and any other medium of expression that is compact, and
doesnt require a large space. We cant offer this residency to
collectives, artists and creators working with large scale mediums
such as paintings or sculpture, as the size of the space doesnt
permit these mediums. The space is self-contained and has all
amenities you would need.

Visit " https://linktr.ee/minuteresidency "or email "
MiNuteArtResidency@gmail.com " to apply.

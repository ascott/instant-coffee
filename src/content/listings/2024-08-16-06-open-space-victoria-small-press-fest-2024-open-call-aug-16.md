Small Press Fest

Open Spaces Small Press Fest is back! This year, Open Space is partnering with the
Greater Victoria Public Library to bring you a tabling fair, panel discussion, and
publication launch at the librarys central branch (735 Broughton St). Join us on
Saturday, September 21 from 11am-4pm in celebration of printed matter and
independent publishing.

We are currently accepting vendor applications from artists,
publishers, collectives,
institutions and art book distributors for the tabling fair. Deadline: August 16. Apply
here!

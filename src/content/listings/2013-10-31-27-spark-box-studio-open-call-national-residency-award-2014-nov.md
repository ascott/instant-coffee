OPEN CALL: NATIONAL RESIDENCY AWARD 2014

Spark Box Studio is offering to artists living and working in Canada: one full 
month at the Spark Box Studio Residency where you will be given a studio 
space and living space for FREE . This residency opportunity is a unique way for 
established and emerging artists to cultivate new ideas, access a rich library of 
arts resources, and focus on their career.

One artist will receive a month-long residency at our studio in Prince Edward 
County. The selected applicant will be granted a semi-private studio space and 
given access to the Spark Box Studio equipment and resources, as well, the 
winner will be provided with living accommodations for their month-long stay.
The National Residency Award will provide a four consecutive week stay to the 
winner between January 5 and April 30, 2014. 

DEADLINE: November 30, 2013

Application: http://sparkboxstudio.com/nationalaward/

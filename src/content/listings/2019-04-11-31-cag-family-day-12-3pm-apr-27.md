Family Day

Tracing Vancouver

Saturday, April 27, 12-3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

Inspired by the work of Deanna Bowen, create a mixed media collage of found images, 
maps and texts of Vancouver/unceded Coast Salish Territories. Trace movements and 
memories of yourself and family through neighbourhoods, parks and terrain, mapping 
your connections to the land and city.

Find out more about Deanna Bowens exhibition at 
cag.contemporaryartgallery.ca/deannabowen

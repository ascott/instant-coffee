Something More than Nothing

Aganetha Dyck, Colleen Heslin, Karin Jones, Michael Mandiberg, Kelly Mark, Andrew
Norman Wilson

November 16, 2019 - January
12, 2020

Opening Reception: Wednesday, November 20, 2019, 6:30"8:30pm. Tour with curator
Adrienne Fast at 6:30 pm.

Join us for a tour with curator Adrienne Fast, followed by our opening reception of
"Something More than Nothing". Free admission and no registration required. All are
welcome! Cash bar will be available.

This group exhibition brings together projects by a range of artists who all deal in some
way with notions of invisible or hidden labour: that is, work that is not seen, valued, or
adequately paid. From domestic or care work, to digital and tech labour that is
increasingly performed in the home as piece work, to the hidden labour that is inherent
in the art world itself, all the artwork in this exhibition draws attention to labour that,
although often overlooked or ignored, is nevertheless essential-work that is
fundamentally Something More than Nothing.

Organized by The Reach Gallery Museum, Abbotsford and curated by Adrienne Fast,
Curator of Art and Visual Culture.

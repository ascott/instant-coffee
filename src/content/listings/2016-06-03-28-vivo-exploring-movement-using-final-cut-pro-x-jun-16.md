Exploring Movement Using Final Cut Pro X

3 hours | Thursday, June 16th, 7-10pm

Register via Eventbrite.

An introduction to editing movement with Apple video software, in
connection with FORM (Festival of Recorded Movement). Learn how to import,
edit video, add effects and titles, and output to dvd, web, or master files
on the once-and-future king of editing software. Stop paying for Adobe
subscriptions and get back on the FCP bus!

If you have some footage you'd like to work with, please bring it with you.
Useful for movement artists (or anyone!) interested in learning more about
video editing.

We have limited seats available. Please contact us if you would like to use
VIVO computers.

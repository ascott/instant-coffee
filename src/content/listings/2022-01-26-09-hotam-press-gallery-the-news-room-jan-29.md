THE NEWS ROOM
at Hotam Press Gallery
JAN 29 - Feb 26

An Exhibition of Artists' Newspapers

Hotam Press Gallery is pleased to present The News Room, an exhibition of works by
artists that are made in the form of a newspaper, from our open call for artists and our
special collection.

From January 29 and runs to February 26, the gallery is transformed into a reading
room where visitors are free to sit down and read all the papers. However, due to our
current safety measures
under the pandemic, we are only open by appointment. Each
visit is limited to 50 minutes. Please visit our Eventbrite page to make appointments.

If there are questions regarding the exhibition and the details, please
email hotampress
@ gmail.com

During the run of the exhibition, we continue to accept public submissions and to add
to the exhibition. Please send work by postal service only, no couriers.

COVER ME - LPs, Cassettes, Artists
Extended Play Feb 1 to 13

Open hours: Saturday 12 - 5 PM, or by appointment

This exhibition brings together over 50 Canadian and international artists who work
with music and sound. For the duration of this exhibition, our gallery will be temporarily
transformed into a record shop / discotheque / listening space. Visitors are welcome to
browse and sample through this rich collection of artists
works.

Here are some of the participating artists and contributors:

Alan Belcher, Bill Burns, Cathy Busby, Juan Cisneros Neumann, Brady Cranfield,
Laura Gilder, Makiko Hara, Hotham Sound, Laiwan, Daniel Laskarin, Zachari Logan,
Doug Melynk, my name is scot, Kristin Nelson, Carol Sawyer, Kathy Slade, Chrysanne
Stathacos, Paul Walde, Lam Wong, . . . and many more.

Also, we are still accepting LPs and Cassettes by artists until January 30. New
submissions will be added to the exhibition. Please do send us your work if you are
interested to be part of the show.

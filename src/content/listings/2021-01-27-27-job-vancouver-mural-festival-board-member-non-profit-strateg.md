POSITION TITLE: Board Member, Non-Profit Arts Strategy

APPLICATION CLOSE: February 28, 2021

STATUS: Volunteer

LOCATION: Vancouver, British Columbia

Leadership and guidance from our Board of Directors is integral in achieving Vancouver
Mural Festivals strategic vision. We work together to build a strong foundation of
governance, operations, community engagement, artist engagement, and more. There are
positions opening with
a focus on a background in Legal/Policy/Risk and Non-Profit Arts
Strategy. Joining the existing board members, each will work collaboratively with the Board,
Executive Director and VMF leadership team to continue to mature the organization and
help navigate its future path.

This is a roll-up-your-sleeves team, and the right board candidate will have the same
approach. A seasoned practitioner in their field, they will have served on a previous board
of equal scope in the non-profit environment or have performed in a leadership role as an
administrator in a non-profit arts organization. The ideal applicant brings with them a broad
knowledge of arts programming throughout the Metro Vancouver region. Bonus points for
having already attended the Vancouver Mural Festival in previous years. VMF has evolved
in its six years from an ambitious seasonal arts start-up to one that now operates year
round with a growing roster of initiatives and talented team of passionate employees and
contractors. In this role, we seek a board member who can provide a depth of knowledge
and experience specific to the growth and maturity of a non-profit organization and can
guide the team with a seasoned and strategic perspective.

To apply: [https://vanmuralfest.ca/joinourteam](https://vanmuralfest.ca/joinourteam)

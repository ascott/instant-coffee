THE ART OF LISTENING

We're delighted to announce another in our series of gallery talks.
John Oliver will share his great knowledge of sound through a
discussion of works in the OTIC exhibition. Over the next two
Sundays, John will speak and demonstrate 'The Art of Listening'. You
won't want to miss this!

John is a Canadian composer and musician; an associate of the
Canadian Music Centre and a member of the Canadian Electroacoustic
community. His music has been performed throughout North America
and Europe. He attended the San Francisco Conservatory of Music
and obtained his Bachelor of Music from UBC. He was awarded a
Masters and a Doctor of Music from McGill University. He later studied
psychoacoustics at the Centre Pompidou in Paris.

John will help visitors visualize the dynamics of sound and discuss his
own response to the works in the exhibition. After the talk there will be
plenty of time to ask questions and spend time in the gallery. The
Curators will also be present for post-talk discussion. This public
presentation is free of charge. Due to gallery size attendance may be
limited so please arrive a few minutes early.

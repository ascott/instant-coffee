We are hereby announcing our call for papers for a conference to be held in 
collaboration with McGill University's Institute for Gender, Sexuality, and Feminist 
Studies (IGSF) in Montreal.

The conference is scheduled for Friday, November 7, and takes place within the 
framework of the feminist festival for media arts + digital culture, The HTMlles 11: 
ZERO FUTURE (November 7-15, 2014). We are looking for artistic and academic 
contributions around the theme ZERO FUTURE. Deadline for submitting proposals 
is April 14.

Please find the call both in English and French language in the attachment, and 
online here: [http://www.studioxx.org/newsletter/HTMlles11-](http://www.studioxx.org/newsletter/HTMlles11-)

TRANSACTANTS: Agents of Process 

 July 17 - 26
 Opening, July 17th @ 6:30-10 pm

 Charles H. Scott Gallery 1399 Johnston St, Granville Island, Vancouver

 This exhibition explores the diverse and connected practices of Emily Carr 
 University of Art + Design's 2014 low-residency MAA graduates. Through 
 performance, installation, video, sculpture and drawing, these seven 
 artists consider how everyday objects, situations and gestures mediate 
 experience.

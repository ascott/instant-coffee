Tuesday, August 15th
The Gordon Smith Gallery of Canadian Art
2121 Lonsdale Ave, North Vancouver
7:00 - 9:00 PM
Free Admission
Beverages by donation

Join Pandora's Collective for an evening of poetry inspired by the summer exhibition at 
the Smith Gallery: Art School High.

Audience members will be given time to write in direct response to the exhibition Art 
School High. Participants will then have a chance to read their fresh, unedited poems at 
an open mic. Following this, six established Canadian poets will present their own 
creative responses to the exhibition. The event is hosted by Fran Bourassa of Pandoras 
Collective and will feature original poetry by Lindsay Kwan, David Geary, Alan Girling, 
Angela Rebrec, and Timothy Shay.

Contemporary Art Gallery
Vancouver, BC 

Nancy Gillespie in response to Endless Renovation by Corin Sworn

Public Discussion

Thursday January 12, 7 pm
Alvin Balkind Gallery
Places are free

Nancy Gillespie, poet and scholar, will examine the literary aspects of 
Corin 
Sworn’s exhibition, Endless Renovation. This thought provoking 
installation by 
Sworn began with the chance discovery of a discarded collection of 
35mm 
slides, through which she has shaped a meandering narrative that 
questions the 
act of reading images and draws attention to the limitations of our 
memory. 

Gillespie works at the intersection of experimental poetics and New 
Lacanian 
theory. She is a member of the Kootenay School of Writing, and 
recently edited 
a special issue of the journal Open Letter, entitled “Negotiating the 
Social Bond 
of Poetics,â€  forthcoming in 2012. She completed her Doctorate on The 
Ecstatic 
Woman and the Grotesque: A New Lacanian Subject in the work of 
Djuna 
Barnes and Mina Loy in 2008 at the University of Sussex, UK and 
currently 
divides her time between Vancouver, NYC and Paris. Presently she is 
involved 
in completing two publications: a revision of her thesis, and an edited 
collection, 
Gothic Remains: Symptoms of the Modern, as well as researching a 
new book, 
Shifting the Discourse in Feminist Avant-garde Poetics and New 
Lacanian 
Theory.

Current exhibitions Robert Orchardson Endless Façade, Corin Sworn 
Endless 
Renovation and Federico Herrero Vibrantes continue until January 15, 
2012.

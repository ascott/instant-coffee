Destiny hope despair alistair
Drew Spielvogel
August 15 - September 21

Drew Spielvogel was born and raised in southwest Michigan. He currently lives and
works in central Pennsylvania. He graduated with a Painting BFA from RISD in 2024. He
recently participated in a group presentation at NADA New York with Afternoon Projects
and has exhibited work at the Brown University Granoff Center and RISD Gelman
Gallery. In 2023, he attended the KuBA: Kulturbanhof residency in Klein-Warnow,
Germany. Painting, for him, mirrors a personal struggle to create meaning and emotional
resolution out of detritus; the final painting is the
result of countless failed attempts at
solving problems he intentionally or unintentionally creates in the work via risk-taking
and improvisation. He describes the end paintings as poor objects which contain hazy
accumulations of felt and recollected feeling over time.

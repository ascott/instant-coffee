"Native graffiti art, indigenized ipods, Inuit break dancing,
indigenous-language hip hop and video, Indian bling and urban wear: the
roots of hip hop culture and music have been transformed by indigenous
cultures and identities into new forms of visual culture and music that
echo the realities of Aboriginal people. Beat Nation is about music, it's
about art and it's about the spirit of us as indigenous peoples and cultures"

Tania Willard: Co-curator Beat Nation

Originally conceived as an exhibit and a website, Beat Nation has evolved
as a collective of Native hip hop artists working in traditional and new
media environments. Moving from exhibition and website Beat Nation is now
creating a touring version largely based on digital technology with a
specific focus on selected global hubs.

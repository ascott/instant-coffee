JOB POSTING: DISTRIBUTION ASSISTANT
Application Deadline: January 22, 2010
Part-Time 14 hours/ week (Flexible Hrs) Wage: $14/h

Video Out Distribution, part of the VIVO (Video In/Video Out) Media Arts
Centre, is a non-profit distributor of video art and documentary works. We
maintain a collection of independently produced video artworks, which we
distribute to festivals, galleries, broadcasters and libraries
internationally. For more information on our work please visit our website
at www.videoout.ca.

The Distribution Assistant works closely with the Distribution Coordinator
and is primarily responsible for responding to general queries by artists
and exhibitors, open festival calls, administrative support for
acquisitions, title accessioning and basic financial transactions. The
Distribution Assistant carries out exhibitor and marketing research,
organizes special projects, and works extensively with email and database
software. The Distribution Assistant is also familiar with the day-to-day
running of Video Out and covers for the Distribution Coordinator when they
are off-site.

The ideal candidate will have strong verbal and written communication
skills, computer fluency and a capacity to juggle multiple tasks to
deadline using Mac OS, Word, FileMaker, and Excel. Understanding of
Photoshop, Illustrator, DVD Studio Pro and Final Cut Pro would be an asset.
This position also draws upon a strong understanding of the festival
programming process, video and media art in Canada, artist run culture and
VIVO's mandate.

Applications should outline an applicant's:

Familiarity with Video Art Distribution
Communication / Administrative Work Experience
Computer Skills and Work Experience

If you are interested in applying for this position, please email a resume
and cover letter by 5pm Friday, January 22 to:

Sharon Bradley
Coordinator - Video Out Distribution
info@videoout.ca

20. Cineworks Studio | INTRODUCTION TO THE ARRIFLEX BL4 CAMERA | JAN 31
AN INTRODUCTION TO THE ARRIFLEX BL4 CAMERA: Especially for Independent
Filmmakers

This introduction to the Arriflex 35mm BL4 is an orientation on using the
35mm camera system available at Cineworks to members and non-members.

Workshop participants will leave this introduction competent enough to use
Cineworks's 35mm film equipment to later shoot on their own, with
confidence. Topics include loading and unloading film, cleaning and
threading the gate, shutter adjustments, speed control, lens handling and
accessories available at Cineworks.

Workshop participants should have some previous experience determining
exposure, either photographic, digital or video, as lighting will not be
covered in the context of the workshop. Also, while we will touch on many
of a camera assistant's responsibilities, this orientation is intended for
those working independently, and artists who would like to use the 35mm medium.

Accessories and some studio lighting will be available, but please do bring
your own ideas and questions.

AN INTRODUCTION TO THE 35MM ARRIFLEX BL4 CAMERA
31 January 2010, 10am-5pm
Cineworks Studio [1131 Howe, back lane entrance]
Cost is $25 for members, $50 for non-members

Registration Deadline: 27 January 2010

Registration: Please contact Leanne at info@cineworks.ca or 604.685.3841.

Instructors: ROB SIMPSON is a graduate of Emily Carr and has worked behind
cameras for years.

KATHLEEN HEPBURN, founding member of the non-profit collective The
Suspension of Disbelief Film Society and chair of the Cineworks board of
directors, is an independent filmmaker and photographer from Vancouver, BC.
She is currently hard at work on numerous projects, including a dramatic
narrative based on her travels through Asia, a feature length documentary
on the world of show ponies, and a feature length dramatic script about the
great Canadian North.

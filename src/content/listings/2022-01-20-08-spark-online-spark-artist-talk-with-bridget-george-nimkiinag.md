Spark Artist Talk with Bridget George (Nimkiinagwaagankwe)
Thursday, January 20th, 12:15pm PST
Online

The latest season of Spark Talks begins Thursday, January 20th! This month's talk
features Bridget George (Nimkiinagwaagankwe). Presented online, free and open to the
public, Spark Artist Talks are casual lunch-hour conversations, presentations, and talks by
emerging Indigenous artists on the third Thursday of each month from 12:15-1pm. This
season of Spark Talks is curated by Alanna Edwards.

Bridget George (Nimkiinagwaagankwe) is an Anishinaabe illustrator, childrens book
author and graphic designer from
Kettle and Stony Point First Nation. They are the author
of the childrens book Its a Mitig!, a dual-language book written to support families like
theirs that are working to reclaim the Anishinaabeg language with their children. They
recently were presented with the Periodical Marketers of Canadas Indigenous Literature
Award for Its a Mitig!.

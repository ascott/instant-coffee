Call for Submissions - KAPSULA Magazine - Double call!

1. Exhibition Reviews - Ongoing call

Any interesting art shows taking place in your city? KAPSULA is always
looking for new & provocative art exhibition reviews written by the
critical eye of the underdog! We are seeking reviews that wander outside
the typical format and style of major art publications—critical, radical,
creative, daring—these are all descriptors of what we hope to publish at a
pace where our readers can actually still visit the exhibitions being
discussed. Our call for exhibition review submissions will be ongoing,
with no deadlines.

For more info, visit: http://kapsula.ca/submissions/index.html

2. Call for cover images - Ongoing call

KAPSULA will be looking for artists to submit cover images for our monthly
publications!
Every month, we'll be looking for one new cover image to go on the website
for our monthly archive. This image will be inspired by the current call
for feature article submissions.
So, if you've got some work you would like published into our magazine,
submit to: submissions@kapsula.ca

For additional information, visit: http://kapsula.ca

generally considered to be a good roommate. I am not a tobacco smoker, and

porch, or wherever else you choose). Moderate levels of alcohol use won't

drinking WILL prove problematic for me in the long run. Unfortunately this
is a no-pets building so that may be a problem for some (if it was my

Ideally I would like someone who (like me) does most of their partying

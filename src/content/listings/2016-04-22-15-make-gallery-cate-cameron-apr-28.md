If you missed the opening, Make is hosting an Artist Talk with Cate 
Cameron and we'd love for you to join us.

Make and Capture Photography Festival are excited to present Cate 
Cameron's original photographic series, The Flowers are Talking. 
Telling a story through imagery, Cate Cameron documents the cultural 
heritage of the Huichol community. Join us to learn more about The 
Flowers are Talking and how modernity and culture intersect in the 
Huichol way of life.

Artist Talk
Thursday, April 28th, 2016
5:00pm - 6:00pm

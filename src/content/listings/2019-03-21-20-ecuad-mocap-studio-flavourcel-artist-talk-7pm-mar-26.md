Flavourcel is a Vancouver-based animation collective formed with the aim to uplift its 
members and the communities it belongs to. Flavourcel is comprised of local talented 
animation artists brought together by their enthusiasm for the fun, the weird, but most of all 
for the art of animation. The Collective creates and hosts live events and installations, 
animation challenges and recently theyre cranking along on some cool music videos theyve 
been hired to do.

At this artist talk, the Collective will discuss their groups mission and efforts, and share 
media from their live events and installations, and music videos.

This event is part of The Time, Light + Sound Series 2019 spring schedule of guest artists. 
Curator Lorelei Pepi has crafted a special focus on the theme of Collaborative Spirit which 
suffuses much of this semesters line-up. Artists are capable of astounding acts of creativity, 
and their ideas and energies are often amplified and transformed when working in crews, 
ensembles and collectives.

Accessibility info!!
The Emily Carr University building has multiple elevators at each end and in the middle of 
the building. The Motion Capture studio is on the bottom floor and is wheel chair accessible. 
The building is not scent-free, nor reduced. There may be some bright colors/flashing lights 
during the presentation on screen. There are multi stall and single stall gender neutral 
washrooms on the floor where the studio is and on the floors above. 

As well, please let us know directly if you are in need of an ASL interpreter.

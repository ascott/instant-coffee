Kiosk, Dynamo Arts Association, and Moniker Press are hosting a one-day Print 
Fair/Market on December 17th at Dynamo Arts Association. (Suite 103 - 30 East 
6th Ave) from 1-9pm. Readings and performances start at 6pm and will go late.

The event showcases a variety of print/publishing work from emerging and 
experimental artists from Vancouver and beyond. Snacks and refreshments will 
be available throughout the day!

Participating publishers:
Kiosk, Moniker Press, Andrea Lukic, Kate Fobert, Cole James Pauls, Kirsten 
Hatfield, Perro Verlag, DDOOGG, As.iZ, Unfastened, Hayley Dawn Muir, Leftover 
Crafts, Zine Club, Paper Innards Distro, Stephanie McDonell + Phaedra Harder, 
Neoglyphic Media, Carli Erin Boisjolie, Vancouver Art Book Fair

Performances by:
Christian Vistan, CPI poetry, Cowboy City Rockers.

Snacks by:
Vanessa Kroeker,Sonja Ratkay

Organized by Juli Majer, Erica Wilk, and Sarah Davidson

Artspeak is seeking applications for Director/Curator

Organization Description:
Artspeak is a non-profit artist run centre established in 1986. Artspeak
presents contemporary practices, innovative publications, bookworks,
editions, talks and events that encourage a dialogue within a unique
interdisciplinary community of writers, poets, critics and visual artists.
The mandate of the gallery is to exhibit contemporary art and to encourage
a dialogue between visual art and writing. Of particular interest is work
that crosses the boundaries between the two disciplines, exploring their
common areas of praxis - a distinct aspect of the history of cultural
practice in this region. Throughout its history, Artspeak has played a
significant role in addressing the historical, social, and intellectual
conditions of contemporary visual and language arts production from the
West Coast and beyond.

Director/Curator Responsibilities:
-Overseeing all programming, planning, and day-to-day operations
-Curating exhibitions, talks, and publications
-Creating and administrating annual budget using accounting software to
keep accurate books and prepare monthly and yearly statements of expenditures
-Ensuring the operation of the gallery through successful grant
applications from municipal, provincial and federal government sources, as
well as foundations and other private sources
-Assisting the board in reviewing policy, operational planning,
organizational development, and fundraising campaigns
-Coordinating board meetings, AGMs, and fundraising campaigns
-Overseeing Artspeak staff and volunteers

Qualifications:
-3-5 years experience in independent administration of an art gallery or
other cultural institution
-Exemplary knowledge of contemporary art practices
-Prior experience in administering curatorial and publication projects
-Strong organizational and management skills
-Excellent verbal and written communication skills
-Proven track record in financial management and planning
-Successful grant writing experience with the ability to develop further
fundraising opportunities

Remuneration:
Salary: $40,000

How to Apply:
Applications should include a brief letter of interest, curriculum vitae
and the names and contact information for 3 references. Following review of
applications by the Board of Directors, the hiring committee will conduct
interviews in mid-August.

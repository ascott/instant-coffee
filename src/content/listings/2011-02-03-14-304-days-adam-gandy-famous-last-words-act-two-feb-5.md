304 Days | FAMOUS LAST WORDS | Act Two | Too Late For Fruit, Too Soon
for Flowers | Adam Gandy | Feb 05

Please join us on Saturday, February 5th for:

FAMOUS LAST WORDS: An Exhibition in Two Parts

Act Two:
Too Late For Fruit, Too Soon for Flowers

Adam Gandy

February 5th to February 19th 2011
Opening Reception // Saturday, February 5th // 7pm - 11pm

Adam Gandy exhibits new video, sculpture and photography that stems
from an exploration of our lived world. Gandys work examines the cult
of the masculine ideal reflected through film, philosophy and history
exploring the notion of resourcefulness as heroism and an interest in
the moment before climax.

Adam Gandy is an artist and writer living in Vancouver. He received a
BFA from ECUAD in 2006 and is currently on hiatus from pursuing a BA
in Sociology and Philosophy at UBC.

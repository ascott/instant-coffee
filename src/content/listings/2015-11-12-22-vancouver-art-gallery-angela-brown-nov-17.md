Performance
Angela Brown
Tuesday November 17, 7pm
In the Gallery

Dancer Angela Brown and her collaborators animate the art of Evelyn
Roth through a wearable art performance with musical accompaniment
taking place throughout the galleries. This performance is presented in
conjunction with the exhibition Between Object and Action:
Transforming Media in the 1960s and 70s.

Performers: Angela Brown, Michelle Hersey, Yayoi Owen, Janet Walker
and Ross Barrett

Free for Members or with Gallery Admission. Meet in the Rotunda on
the 1st floor.
A special thank you to The Dance Centre for their in-kind contribution
to this performance.

11. SFU Student Show | A Felling | FEB 29
A Felling
2nd & 3rd Year SFU Student Show
March 1 – March 20, 2008
SFU Gallery, Burnaby Campus

Opening: Friday, February 29 from 7 to 9pm, SFU Burnaby

Vanessa Andres, Crispin Bartlett, KitSum Cheng, Stephanie Granger, Benjamin Lee, Tommy Lee, Gary Mak, Heidi Meixner, Simon Murtagh, Angie Nguyen, 
Gordon Nicholas, Natalie Panovic, Laurel Richardson, Jasmine Wagner, 
Oliver McTavish Wisden, Sangmi Yoon

A Felling presents recent work from visual art students at the School for the Contemporary Arts at SFU. This art is “hot off the press” and reveals some of the ways that today’s younger people see society’s relationship to the wounded environment in which it must live. The show is a snapshot of current ideas in SFU's creative visual art community, and acts as a looking glass into the diverse relationships between humanity and the earth, and in some cases, sends a message to those who put profit over good environmental practice.

The New Gallery Job Posting – PROGRAMMING DIRECTOR

The New Gallery (TNG), an artist-run centre, is a non-profit charitable society established in 1975. It is committed to providing a forum for a wide spectrum of critical discourse and multidisciplinary practices within the contemporary visual arts. TNG presents and promotes locally, nationally and internationally produced contemporary art while cultivating diversity, advancing critical discourse and creating opportunities for networking in the art community.

We are seeking a motivated, organized and creative individual to fill the full-time position of Programming Director, to commence immediately.

§		      Researching and pursuing funding opportunities for all programs
§		      Liaising and maintaining relations with exhibiting artists

13. Pacific Cinematheque | Sharon Kahanoff | MAR 4
The Here To Gathered | Sharon Kahanoff
16mm, 20min
2008

March 4, 2008
Pacific Cinematheque
1131 Howe St. Vancouver, BC
Opening Reception and Screening 7:30 - 10:30

A reception hall, a park, an art gallery, a living room, a street, a beach and a gymnasium– The Here To Gathered plays inside these theatres of the social, performing along with the actors who move through them.  Gestural, visceral, corny and critical, meditative, artificial and often realistic, the film threatens to erupt at the seams from its own difference.  But The Here To Gathered is held in check by a rigid structuralism that, like any law excessively applied, vents itself into the absurd.  Lurking on the borders between documentary, narrative and experimental film, The Here To Gathered is a portrait, in both major and minor keys, of nothing more than passage.


Sharon Kahanoff is a filmmaker, artist and writer. Her artwork has been exhibited in galleries and cinemas locally and in the US.  Interested in critical writing, Sharon publishes in exhibition catalogues and art journals and gives occasional lectures.  This exhibition is in partial fulfillment for the degree of Master of Fine Arts at Simon Fraser University.  http://www.sfu.ca/sca

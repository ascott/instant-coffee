The Art in the Written Review
A workshop by Peripheral Review
Saturday, April 22, 10am **Pre-registration recommended by April 20th
Email: RSVP@capturephotofest.com

Presented by Capture Photography Festival, in partnership with the Vancouver 
Art Book Fair, as part of the Vancouver Photo Book Fair: Book Fair Talks & 
Workshops...

This workshop explores and tests the boundaries of the exhibition review through 
multiple literary and artistic methods, including poetry, critical analysis, and 
experimental writing. Artist and writer Kristina Fiedrich and Peripheral Review 
Editor Lauren Lavery facilitate a discussion, readings, and workshop to help 
navigate the space for abstraction in the written review. 

Peripheral Review is an online platform for documenting and expanding the 
Vancouver and Toronto art scenes while enabling access for emerging artists 
and writers. Through publishing critical exhibition reviews, interviews and artistic 
responses, the magazine strives to bring together multiple perspectives to form a 
cohesive, thorough and expansive dialogue. 

Kristina Fiedrich holds an MAA in Visual Arts (ECUAD 2012) and an MA in 
Comparative Media Arts (SFU 2015). She has exhibited nationally and 
internationally and received awards for excellence in academics, printmaking and 
photography. Alongside her practice, Fiedrich is the Arts Programmer for the City 
of New Westminster.

Peripheral Review will also be exhibiting at VPBF's Book Fair taking place from 
Friday, April 21 to Sunday, April 23 at Western Front (303 E 8th Avenue). For the 
exhibitor list and full offsite program, please visit: 
[http://capturephotofest.com/vancouver-photo-book-fair](http://capturephotofest.com/vancouver-photo-book-fair).

ABOUT THE VANCOUVER PHOTO BOOK FAIR

The VPBF is comprised to of two parts: a book fair in the Grand Luxe Hall at 
Western Front (303 E 8th Ave) and a series of offsite Talks & Workshops at 
James Black Gallery (144 E 6th Ave). 

The Vancouver Photo Book Fair surveys one of photographys earliest forms of 
dissemination"the photo book"demonstrating how this vital form continues to 
evolve, push the conventions of artists books, and carry photography into new 
contexts.

Free and open to the public, the Vancouver Photo Book Fair is Vancouvers first 
art book fair dedicated to artists whose book-making practices incorporate or 
intersect with photography and lens-based art. The multi-day event 
encompasses an engaging program and a variety of local and international 
artists, collectives, and publishers.

Admission to the VPBF at Western Front and James Black Gallery is FREE. We 
regret that neither the Grand Luxe Hall at Western Front nor James Black Gallery 
are wheelchair accessible.

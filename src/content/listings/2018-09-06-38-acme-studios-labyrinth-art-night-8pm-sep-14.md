Labyrinth Art Night
Friday, September 14
8:00 PM
112 E Hastings - ACME Studios

An immersive evening of experimental sound, projected images and art. Join us for 
Labyrinth Art Night " ACME studios open house and celebration. After the cancellation of 
Swarm, we at ACME Studios have decided to keep the dream alive!

ACME Studios holds 16 studios and close to 50 artists of all disciplines, many of whom will 
be showing art in their studios and the Labyrinth Gallery.

There will also be special exhibitions and DJ sets in the Gam Gallery and Remington Gallery 
and Studio.

Featured sound and projected image artists: Mortlake, R&D feat. Daniel Majer, and 
Postdrag.

Research + Presentation Centre

April 7 to May 5, 2012 - opening Thursday, April 5, 8pm

UNIT/PITT Projects, 15 E Pender St, Vancouver BC Canada

Open hours: Wednesday - Saturday, noon to 5pm

Research + Presentation Centre is an experimental reading room that 
locates and interprets personal libraries to position them in the public 
realm for information exchange and printed matter dialogue. 

Contributors include local artists, writers, a musician, and a curator, all 
of whom apply research in a dynamic way. Featuring the personal 
libraries of Jesse Birch, Lenox & Burrard, Stefana Fratila, Michael 
Turner, and Jerry Zaslove, and social furniture by Kellen Deighton and 
Nathan Welland. The project is curated by Amanda Jehring and 
Steffanie Ling.

With an emphasis on research materials and its relationship to critical 
and creative output, contributors will be showcasing a range of chosen 
ephemera including texts, film, image and object sources. A personal 
institution and a material preface to the presentation of a practice. In 
the hopes of continuing the conceptual loop from research to creation 
to presentation, the space will include tools and materials that incubate 
the research process, entreating visitors to consider and utilize the 
space as a tangible, generative and convivial site. Taking into account 
the structures that support the dialogue between materials and 
researcher, two artists have interpreted the relationship that sustains 
the dictum, form equals content. Within the space is a series of social 
furniture designed to accentuate the versatility of space as it changes 
between hosting a performance and facilitating intimate engagement 
between the visitors and the libraries.

Book Launch: Kindred Tracings

October 17, 2024 at 5:30 PM

Edmonds Park, Intersection of Humphries and Rosewood Ave, Burnaby

5:30 PM: Doors open, Book sale

6:00 PM: Poetry and artist talk
followed by refreshments

Join us to celebrate Michelle Sounds visit to FLEET and the launch of Kindred
Tracings, an exhibition catalogue published by the Burnaby Art Gallery featuring the
work of Muriel Ahmarani Jaouich, Minahil Bukhari, Russna Kaur and Michelle
Sound. To mark this special event, guest-reader and award-winning writer Dallas
Hunt will be reading a selection of poems. Local independent bookstore Wildfires
Books will be onsite selling a selection of enticing titles, and Michelle Sound and
Minahil Bukhari will share an artist talk exploring their practices and how motherhood
and matrilineal learning and creating informs their work and process.

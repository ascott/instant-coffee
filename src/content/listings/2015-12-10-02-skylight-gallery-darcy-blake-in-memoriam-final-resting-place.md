In Memoriam: Final Resting Place
A solo exhibition by D'arcy Blake
December 7-10, 2015
Opening Reception December 10th, 7-11pm
Skylight Gallery
163 E Pender St.

In Memoriam: Final Resting Place is the first solo exhibition by
Vancouver-based artist D'Arcy Blake. For four days, the exhibition will
transform the gallery into a mausoleum, memorial, and ceremonial
purgatory for a tree whose life was surreptitiously cut short. The
installation will present the tree's reconstructed cadaver, providing an
opportunity for reflection upon the destruction of the material that builds
the physical world around us. A series of documentary photographs,
presented around the installation, expose the questionable residential
development practices that led to the tree's untimely demise.

Following the exhibition, the tree will be delivered to its final resting
place in the forest to decompose, allowing it to be reconstituted as part
of a cyclical ecosystem.

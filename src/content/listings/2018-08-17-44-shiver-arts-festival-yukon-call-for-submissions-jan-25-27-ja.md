The (s)hiver arts society is accepting submissions for its 2019 arts festival in Dawson City, 
Yukon. The festival is a weekend long celebration during the coldest and darkest days of the 
year featuring performances, music, art, installations, freezing cold, good times, and barely any 
light. 

This year we are planning on having the majority of the festival's projects be on the frozen 
Yukon river, so if you have an idea for a project that can be durable enough for the cold, windy 
environment and also be awesome, please apply on the submissions page of our newly 
revamped website:

www.shiverartssociety.ca

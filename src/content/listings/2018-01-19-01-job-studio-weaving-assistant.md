Job Title: Studio / Weaving Assistant 

Details: Full time, 1 year position with potential to stay longer 

Wage: $18.00 per hour to start / $20.00 after training period 

Description: 

Established professional artist who operates a textile arts studio in East Vancouver 
seeks applicants to fill a Studio Assistant position starting mid-February, 2018. The 
Studio Assistant will work closely with the artist and other team members in an open-
concept workspace. Joining us means you are a creative person active in the 
contemporary arts sector and/or has experience working in a studio. The position will 
focus on weaving with a floor loom but will also involve studio management duties.

Responsibilities:

Practical knowledge of weaving with a preference for someone who has been 
trained 

Using a floor loom to execute basic textile designs created by the artist

Sewing skills (i.e. hand / machine / industrial)

Creative decisions (i.e. use of colour)

Communicate clearly and be able to follow directions 

Studio management (i.e. sending emails, updating website, ordering supplies)

Experience with photography, Photoshop and image editing an asset 

Work independently / be proactive 

Qualifications: 

An ideal candidate will have: 

A diploma/certificate in weaving or a portfolio of previous weavings 

Or a degree/diploma in studio arts / art history

Textiles skills with a keen interest to develop weaving skills

Other Qualifications:

Experience assisting other professional artists ( hands on production and/or studio 
management )

BC Drivers license

Please contact: bwaddenstudio@gmail.com for more information or to submit a brief 
coverletter and resume.

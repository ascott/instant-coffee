Videodrunk is a small experimental/indie/underground film festival that
takes place during November in Toronto in Kensington Market. The
festival is run by filmmakers and video artists and aims to present films
in an almost mixtape sort of way to audience in a non-formal easy
going environment.

We love indie, underground, experimental, docs and genre but
encourage all types of filmmakers to submit. Video art is also cool.

Entry Fees

Under 20 Minutes: $10/Over 20 Minutes: $20/Feature: $25

Deadline September 15th

Submit via FilmFreeway: https://filmfreeway.com/festival/Videodrunk
For more info visit: https://videodrunk.wordpress.com/ or email
videodrunkfest@gmail.com

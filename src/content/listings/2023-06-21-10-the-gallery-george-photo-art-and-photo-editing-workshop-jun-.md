Workshop Description:
This two-hour workshop is for iPhone & Android photographers who want to learn
how to shoot exceptional photos, and edit them to produce stunning photo art.

Participants will learn how to use composition and lighting more effectively, then edit
their photos to take their photography to a more professional level.

Participants will receive easy to follow
demonstrations and personal one-on-one
instruction on the use of the best photo editing apps. Participants can expect to
produce some spectacular photos for sharing on social media platforms and/or for
printing as wall art.

Participants only need to bring their iPhone or Android cameras.

Note: This lesson is applicable to Android users. However, the instructor will be using
an
iPhone camera for demonstration purposes.

Instructor:
Gary Squire is a qualified/certified BC teacher. His photo art has been exhibited in
numerous venues, including most recently at The Gallery George.

140 West Hastings St., Vancouver BC

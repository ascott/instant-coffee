Artist's Choice Film Screenings @ Pacific Cinematheque, 1131 Howe Street,
Downtown Vancouver

Presented in conjunction with the Presentation House Gallery exhibition
Isabelle Pauwels "B and E"
featuring two works selected by Isabelle Pauwels: Michael Snow's 1967
experimental landmark Wavelength and Robert Altman's enigmatic 1977 drama 3
Women.

The two films relate to Pauwels's interest in the artifice of narrative,
the presence of the camera, and the choreography of human actions and
camera movement. As with her own artworks, both films leave room for the
viewer to build their own narrative, and to actively engage in the
construction of meaning.

MARCH 9, Monday | 7pm Wavelength (45 min.), 8pm 3 Women (124 min.)
MARCH 11, Wednesday | 7pm 3 Women (124 min.), 920pm Wavelength (45 min.)

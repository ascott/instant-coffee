LAUREN RICE
Water Like Dirty Glass
Oct 11-Nov 11, 2018
KIND-POT
Hours: Sat & Sun 12-5pm
apt D 218 East 11th ave

Say that it is an allegory and that the sunlight was the memory of a glass of water -
David Antin
Open Mouth Already A Mistake - Zen Master Wu Kwang 

Lauren Rices practice borrows fragments from literature and history to create imaginary 
thresholds in which sculpture, drawing and found objects commingle on a level playing 
field. The exhibition Water Like Dirty Glass consists of a new body of sculptural work 
that reflects on material memory, impermanence and decomposition while also drawing 
upon the artists ongoing interest and/or research into wellness culture, experimental 
pedagogical systems (for example Rudolf Steiners Waldorf schools and philosophy), art 
therapy, anxiety, the intermission, theatrical debris and craft history.

A graphic that reads "Toque Craft Fair" on a green and orange striped background.
This holiday season, Toque Craft Fair returns to Western Front from November 29 -
December 1, 2024!

Explore Western Fronts heritage building while you shop a curated selection of
textiles, ceramics, jewelry, print, accessories, apothecary and homeware by over fifty
BC-based artists, craftspeople and designers. Toque Craft Fair is all about coming
together, meeting artists and connecting with whats made in our community.

This year our opening night party on Friday, November 29, features a cocktail bar by
The Woods Spirit Co., drinks by 33 Arces Brewing Company, Oddity Kombucha, and
St Hubertus & Oak Bay Estate
Winery, and food by Indish. Our weekend cafe on
Saturday, November 30 and Sunday, December 1, includes coffee by Estratto Coffee
and baked goods by Subo Bakes.

Admission is by donation in advance here or at the door.

Proceeds from entrance and 30% of all sales are generously donated by our
participating vendors to support Western Front's artistic program.

Discover this year's vendors at toquecraftfair.com

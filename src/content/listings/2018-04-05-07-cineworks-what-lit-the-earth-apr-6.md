Cineworks, CSIF and Iris Film Collective present:

What Lit The Earth: 
Selected 8mm and 16mm Works from 
Artifact Small Format Film Festival, 2018

Introduced by Raeesa Farooqi (CSIF)

Friday, April 6; 8.00pm - 9.30pm
Cineworks Studio, 300-1131 Howe Street (lane entrance) 
Vancouver,V6Z 2L7
FREE

In partnership with Calgary Society of Independent Filmmakers and Iris Film Collective, 
Cineworks presents a screening of nine experimental film works finished on Super 8 and 
16mm film, selected from Artifact Small Format Film Festival 2018 and introduced by 
CSIF Programming and Communications Director, Raeesa Farooqi.

Artifact Small Format Film Festival (formerly 100 Dollar Film Festival) is one of only a 
few festivals worldwide, dedicated to works finished on Super 8 and 16mm film. When 
the $100 Film Festival was born in 1992, it showcased eight short films on Super 8. The 
name sprung from the challenge to shoot a short film on four rolls of Super 8 " which 
tallied to the cost of $100. In following years, the festival dropped the budgetary limit and 
allowed 16mm film, which shifted the focus from low budget to quality small-format films. 
Thus, the name was changed to the Artifact Small Format Film Festival in 2017 so as to 
better represent the festival's role as an international celebration of creative storytelling 
on celluloid.

small POGS GALLERY
CALL FOR SUBMISSION: march 13th

An old peppermint patty machine has been converted into an artist trading
card and networking gallery. These miniature POGS or small works are sold
for 25 cents a piece for artists and collectors to swap, trade, and own
them all.
Submit your small series of traders up to fifty will be accepted at a time
and will be filtered in as the gallery expands.

HOW TO APPLY
EMAIL Kaylea Reeve at
smallpogsgallery.gmail.com

Email showing interest in being apart of small POGS GALLERY, and get
cracking on your artist trading cards! You will recieve a follow up email
with drop off or mailing instructons.

REQUIREMENTS
SIZE~ diameter 4 cm
WIDTH~ no bigger than 2cm
QUANTITY~ no less than 15 pieces
POGS small token to be talked about

These small POGS are for making conections in the art community but also
for the fun of collection and trading. These trades can be artwork based or
treated like an artist calling card. Make them your own.

~Feel free to ask questions and shoot images over about the space or the
project itself.

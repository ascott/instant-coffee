Left of Main, 211 Keefer St, Vancouver, BC V6A 2T9

Saturday October 19th, 7:00 p.m.

A sound and video installation / performance by Matthew Tomkinson and Andy Zuliani.

Hotel Fata Morgana explores the notion of music-as-mirage. The piece uses weird and
wobbly textures, spectral shimmers,
microtonal melodies, and slow-oscillating drones to
approximate, musically, the optics of refraction. A four-part video series mirrors these
transformations by reworking found YouTube footage - obscure documentaries, amateur
meteorology, and news coverage - into new, hallucinatory geometries and nested
surfaces. A study in distorted sonic realities, Hotel Fata Morgana questions the
seductiveness of false horizons and fairy-tale architectures. It asks: what might hazy
morphologies of light and sound tell us about our desires for sublimity and
entrancement?

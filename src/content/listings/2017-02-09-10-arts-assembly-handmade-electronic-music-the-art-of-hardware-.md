Soledad Munoz, Fiegehen and Tifanie Lamiel 
Saturday, February 11, 1-4 pm

6404 Wellington Avenue, Horseshoe Bay
Price: 40.00

Ages 12 +

In a time where the mass production of goods has homogenized our audio/visual 
landscape, and where hardware and software can dictate the outcome of an 
artistic exploration, it's necessary to deconstruct our relationship with electronics 
to its most simplified form. Based on the book by Nicolas Collins "Handmade 
Electronic Music: The Art of Hardware Hacking" this two part workshop will explore 
the principles of electronic art through applied learning. The first half covers 
analog principles: from electromagnetic fields and coil antennas, vibrations and 
pick-ups to synthesis through the building of an oscillator. The second half will be 
an introduction to coding with Arduino micro controllers and LEDs. 
Arduinos will also be available for purchase during the workshop.

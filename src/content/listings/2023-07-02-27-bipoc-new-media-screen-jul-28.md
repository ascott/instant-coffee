BIPOC New Media Screen

Gallery Gachet is excited to announce that we are currently accepting applications
from emerging BIPOC artists whose practices revolve around the mediums of new
media art and filmmaking. Selected artists will have the opportunity to develop their
practices with opportunities for professional and community mentorship. Applicants
are encouraged to create work that reflects the gallery's mandate and actively engage
in dialogue in the DTES area. Work will be featured on the gallery street-facing monitor
as part of the gallerys 2024/2025 programming year. The gallery heavily encourages
individuals without a formal
art education background who self-identify as BIPOC
emerging artists to submit an application for consideration.

NEW MEDIA SUBMISSION MATERIALS:

A statement of intent that connects your current practice or past bodies of work to the
gallerys mandate and the potential themes that the new media work you are proposing
would encompass. (250-500 words)

A CV or biography charting your arts education and exhibitions/publications/project
history. We value and recognize diverse forms of knowledge and cultural creations.
Applicants have the liberty to include any relevant experiences they may have acquired
outside of formal educational institutions in their CVs or biography.

Documentation of past work that could include images, audio files or videos (5-10
images, 3-5 minutes audio and video files).

Deadline for New Media Submissions: July 28, 2023.

Gallery Gachet is located in the Downtown Eastside of Vancouver on the unceded and
occupied territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh
(Squamish) and
Sl"lwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations. Gallery Gachet has a mandate to support artists
and offer art programs addressing mental health and socio-political marginalization,
while promoting art as a means for survival, cultural participation, and human rights.

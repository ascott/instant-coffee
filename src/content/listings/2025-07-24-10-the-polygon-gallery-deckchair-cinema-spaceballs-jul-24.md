HAPPENING July 24, 2025 at 7:00 PM TO 11:00 PM

Deckchair Cinema: Spaceballs
The evil leaders of Planet Spaceball, having foolishly squandered their precious
atmosphere, devise a secret plan to take every breath of air away from their peace-
loving neighbour, Planet Druidia. Spaceball's leader President Skroob (Mel Brooks)
orders the evil Dark Helmet (Rick Moranis) to kidnap Princess Vespa (Daphne
Zuniga) of Druidia and hold her hostage in exchange for air. But help arrives for the
Princess in the form of renegade space pilot Lone Starr (Bill Pullman) and his half-
man, half-dog partner, Barf (John Candy).

Spaceballs
Director: Mel Brooks
Running time: 1h 36m
Year: 1987
Rated: G

About Deckchair Cinema
You are invited to enjoy classic films under the stars on Cates Deck in front of the
Gallery. Now in its fourth year, Deckchair Cinema treats audiences to a summer of al
fresco film screenings. Hosted for nine Thursdays beginning July 3, the screenings
commence at sunset, and will be complemented by music and entertainment leading
up to curtain time. Attendees are encouraged to arrive early and make themselves
comfortable by bringing their own blankets or low chairs. A concession stand will
feature drinks from Strathcona Beer Company.

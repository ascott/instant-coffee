Antoni Wojtyra: How Little I Know
Feburary 21, 2015 / 12:00 PM
108 E Broadway

Burrard Arts Foundation was pleased to host Antoni Wojtyra as artist in
residence from October 2014 to January 2015. His time at BAF resulted in an
exhibition of paintings currently on view at the gallery until February 28.
On February 21, Wojtyra will deliver an artist talk in which he will
discuss "framing" as a basic principle of pictures.

A frame is a structure that underlies or supports a system or concept. An
artist talk - generally a lecture - is an educational framing device that
artists use to promote and disseminate their work and ideas to an audience.
In the realm of paintings and photos frames protect pictures. Frames also
delimit or contain pictorial space and so, frames create pictures. How?
Why? The difference between a picture and an object necessarily becomes a
focus. Modern painting since Malevich's Black Square of 1915 should be
understood as both picture and object. Paintings exist as a tension between
being immaterial pictorial space and material physical objects. This
tension between object and picture will rambles Wojtyra towards what he
thinks of as "The Spectrum of Painting". This is a framework to attempt to
know, map and appreciate the possibilities of paint and painting. In
speaking about pictorial framing, what is outside of a picture's frame
becomes taboo. Or becomes "not a picture" or "not an object" or "not art"
and "nothing" at all. Wojtyra's talk will discuss his past work and the
work up at BAF in order to frame it for an audience by addressing framing
itself.

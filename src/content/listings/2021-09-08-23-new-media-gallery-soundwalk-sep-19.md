Explore the world of ambient sounds outdoors and learn how to record this soundscape!
Take some walking journeys with an experienced sound artist and technology expert. You
will learn to use a handheld recorder to collect your own personal archive of sounds from
the City & Nature. Capture sound with purpose: Learn to listen from new perspectives.
Learn to capture exciting, loud sounds as well as subtle, quiet sounds. In the Studio the
group will then learn how to mix, edit and master their sounds into ambient soundscapes or
works of art. Bring your own field recorder or
contact us to reserve one
(education@newmediagallery.ca). Please bring a pair of personal headphones to the
workshop.

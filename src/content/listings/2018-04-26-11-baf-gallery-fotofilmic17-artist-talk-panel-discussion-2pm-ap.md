FOTOFILMIC17 Artist Talk + Panel Discussion

Saturday, April 28, 2PM
BAF Gallery, 108 E Broadway 

Join us to share in FotoFilmic's conversation on the state and future of analogue and 
film-based photographic practices in the digital age. First, FotoFilmic Directors 
Virginie Lamarche and Bastien Desfriches Doria will introduce the theoretical 
framework behind FotoFilmic's global vision and mandate, advocating for the 
fundamental artistic ethos material practices that the featured photographers engage 
with. Then, three current FotoFilmic exhibitors, Matthew Finley from Los Angeles 
and Marisa Chafetz from New York in person and German artist Jacqueline Roberts 
via Skype, will jump into the discussion and talk about critical aspects of their work in 
relation to photographic materiality. Virginie and Bastien will moderate and 
encourage a broader, historically inclusive picture to form for the audience, who will 
then have the opportunity to address a limited number of questions to both the 
artists and moderators.

The panel discussion as well as FOTOFILMIC17 group exhibition are presented in 
partnership with Capture Photography Festival. 

Burrard Arts Foundation (BAF)
108 E Broadway, Ground Floor
Vancouver, B.C

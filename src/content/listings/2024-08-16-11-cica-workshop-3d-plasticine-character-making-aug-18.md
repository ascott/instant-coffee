Join us for an exciting and creative 3D Plasticine Character Making Workshop
facilitated by the talented multi-disciplinary artist, Weskin (also known as Will
Hannan). In this hands-on workshop, you'll learn the basics of creating captivating
3D characters using plasticine"a soft, easy-to-manipulate medium perfect for
beginners. Inspired by the imaginative and detailed characters in James
Jean's work, participants will explore basic sculpting techniques and have the
opportunity to develop their own unique characters.

The workshop will include a tour of the exhibition!

We will be providing:

Plasticine in various colours

"Basic sculpting tools

"Drawing paper and pencils for sketching character ideas

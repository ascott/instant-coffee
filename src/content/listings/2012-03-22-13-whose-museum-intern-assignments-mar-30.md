March 10, 2012

Dear Interns (Annie, Ann, Claire and Karen),

We at Whose Museum are very pleased to have you as our newest 
members. As
interns you will be assigned the duty of putting together the show in 
Vancouver,
Canada, in the absence of the Whose Museum Associates who are 
currently in
Sweden and Germany. As the collection is presently dispersed across 
several
international borders, we have given you a list of tasks that you can
do in order to
put together the show.
We recognize that the list is rather long but we hope that you will enjoy
yourselves in the process of completing it. We trust this internship
will lead you to
interesting encounters and discussions. In addition to the task list
we would like
you to email us updates on your progress once weekly. Please 
accomplish what
you can before the opening on 30 March 2012, and then carry forward 
with the
rest of the tasks for the duration of the show.
We very much look forward to hearing from you and to seeing 
documentation of
the show.
Sincerely,

Laura Hatfield

Whose Museum
www.whosemuseum.org

topdownbottomup is a site for emerging contemporary art. We are
interested in practices actively engaged in political, cultural and
aesthetic theory. We also have an interest in artwork, with physical
presence, affect, intensities and enchanting thingy-ness.

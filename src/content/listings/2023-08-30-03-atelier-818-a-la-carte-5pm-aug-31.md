Atelier 8.18. is a gallery that showcases present-tense artwork from local and
international artists 3 times a year in a home setting. This exhibition A la carte features
artists who have diverse
practices and are very materials focused: Weaving, Painting
Paper cutting, Collage, Painting and A ceramics and weaving collaboration. All artwork
is affordable and easy to collect for any size space.

"A la Carte" Features artwork by
Hillary Webb x Ana Diab
Heidi Gerrits
Rachael Ash
Lydia Cecila
Dominique Norville
Kyla Bourgh

Opening Thursday August 31st 5pm-9pm
8 east 18th ave
Show is until mid Nov with viewing by appointment, and small events throughout the
exhibition timeline.

Reading and Book Launch:
Art Criticism & Other Short Stories

With readings by:

Hazel Meyer
Carmen Papalia
Vanessa Kwan
Helen Reed

Friday, December 20, 7pm
grunt gallery, #116 - 350 East 2nd Avenue, Vancouver

Blank Cheque Press is pleased to announce the launch of its latest title! Originally
produced and distributed by artist Helen Reed as a series of fanfiction zines, Art
Criticism & Other Short Stories collects together fifteen of the very best of these texts --
from deliriously concocted alternate realities and erotic fantasies, to cutting
appraisals of
gender and power. Featuring writing by a diverse lineup of artists, writers, curators and
critics working in and alongside contemporary art, this anthology of oddball writing
shows us just how critical and gushing true fandom can be, proving once and for all that
these qualities are not mutually exclusive.

With contributions by Rose Bouthillier, Amber Dawn, Jen Delos Reyes, Amy Fung, Anna
Gray & Ryan Wilson Paulsen, Onya Hogan-Finlay, Ariana Jacob, Sam Korman,
Vanessa Kwan, Hazel Meyer, Dan Nelson, Darren O'Donnell, Carmen Papalia, Helen
Reed, and Maya Suess.

More info: [http://www.blankchequepress.com/product/art-criticism-other-short-stories](http://www.blankchequepress.com/product/art-criticism-other-short-stories)

grunt gallery is located on the unceded and ancestral territories of the xwmkwy"m
(Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish) and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil- Waututh)
Nations, who have lived in kinship with this land, water and air for thousands of years.
We recognize and acknowledge their leadership and our own complicity in
settler
colonialism, its present occupation and its violent legacy. We acknowledge our
responsibility to work actively in support of Indigenous sovereignty, and towards a
respectful relationship with this place.

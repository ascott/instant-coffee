Art Katalyst Magazine is a monthly publication dedicated to the
diversity of the arts in the Greater Toronto Area, the rest of Canada
and the world.

In this magazine, you will see the true beauty of diversity that we,
the creators of Art Katalyst, want to expose in not only the Canadian
Art Market, but all over the globe. Although we focus primarily on
Canadian artists, we accept submissions from international talent.

This Toronto-based publication will feature artists of every medium
touching on taboo topics, writing articles that push the boundaries of
the mainstream comfort zones, and telling stories that not only
express the underlying issues in our society, but also help bring us
together.

***ACCEPTING SUBMISSIONS FOR NOV 2014: Theme "Decadence and Indulgence"**
DEADLINE: October 20th, 2014
Decadence and Indulgence pertains to the idea of consumption, excess and/or
money. We encourage artists to explore the idea of consumption whether it
refers to food, substances or wealth. Art of all mediums is welcome, we
welcome unique usage of materials (combining food and fashion) for this
issue specifically.

***ACCEPTING SUBMISSIONS FOR DEC 2014: Theme "Nostalgia"**
DEADLINE: November 20th, 2014
As the year draws to an end, we reflect on our decisions, our choices, the
friends we have made, the love that we've gained or lost and so on. In this
issue we encourage artists to look back on their last year and create works
that reflect the theme of nostalgia. Interpretation is open.

TO SUBMIT:
Fill out the online form under "Submissions" on our website at:
http://artkatalyst.com

-Include 3-8 high resolution (300 dpi, high quality, non watermarked)
photos of the art work emailed to: art.katalyst@gmail.com

-A 150-300 maximum word bio/artist statement from you and any other artists
involved
-Full photo credits to all those involved
-Links to your websites, portfolio, etc.

Bio must include the following criteria (Submitted in Google Doc format):
-Information on yourself, your art, where you’re located
-How long you've been working in this/these medium(s)
-If/where you trained/attended school
-Why you love what you do
-Where you see your work taking you
-Additionally any information pertaining to the chosen theme.

For photoshoots, please contact those involved to inform them that the
photoshoot has been submitted.

DISCLAIMER:
By sending content to Art Katalyst Magazine you consent to have your
content published in our magazine if selected. Submissions are on a
volunteer basis, at this time we cannot provide monetary compensation or
complimentary copies to contributors. We reserve the right to adjust your
images/text to fit to the size and image quality of the magazine.
Contributors will receive a digital tear sheet of the pages they are
featured in as compensation. Tear sheets will be sent out on the last day
of the month after the issue is released (ie. November release, tearsheets
will be sent out by the end of December).

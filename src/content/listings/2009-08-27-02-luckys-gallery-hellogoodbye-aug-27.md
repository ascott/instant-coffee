hello/goodbye

27th - 29th August
Opening - August 27th at 8pm
Lucky's Gallery - 3972 Main St (at 24th)

An exhibition of photographs and drawings by Beth Atkinson and Matthew Robley-Siemonsma

Over the last three years we have moved around a lot, in fact over 15 times back and forth between 
3 countries and two continents on temporary work permits.

Maintaining a consistent art practice throughout all of the upheaval and instability has been 
incredibly difficult. Whilst applying for visas, making new friends and leaving old ones behind, finding 
employment and quitting satisfying jobs, packing and unpacking endlessly; we realised that the 
nature of our work has embodied this aspect of our lives revealing a fixation on the transitory, the 
fleeting and the impermanent.

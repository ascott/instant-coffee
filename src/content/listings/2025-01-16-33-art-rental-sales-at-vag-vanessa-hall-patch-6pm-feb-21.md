Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist talk with Featured
Vanessa Hall-Patch in the Art Rental & Sales showroom on Friday, February 21st at
6PM.

Vanessa Hall-Patch's Here and There brings together past and new works,
expanding on her exploration of
the impermanence of British Columbias vernacular
architecture and the landscapes they inhabit. These works reflect on the tension
between transience and permanence, finding beauty in the inevitable cycles of loss
and renewal.

Through layered printmaking techniques"including etching, screen printing, relief,
and chine coll"Hall-Patch documents cabins and cottages that are slowly
disappearing. By cutting these structures away from their backgrounds, she brings
focus to their forms, preserving their presence while embracing impermanence as an
essential part of their beauty and meaning.

In addition to documenting the cabins, she also shifts her focus to the natural
surroundings left behind in their absence, centering the landscapes as independent
pieces. These new works capture empty spaces that speak to the disappearance of
these structures while emphasizing the resilience and permanence of the landscape.

Here and There suggests a duality"of presence and absence, preservation and
change. It speaks to the shifting nature of these spaces and the delicate balance
between what remains and what fades away.

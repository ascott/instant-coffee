March 4, 2016 at 5:30 PM
READ Books
1399 Johnston Street
Granville Island, Vancouver

For the second installment of After Hours, Justine Gabias presents
Exporte, a projection of Super 8mm film footage shot by her
grandfather during the 1950's and 1960's in Qubec. Justine remixes
her family's history into semi-fictional vignettes, adding subtitles of
dreamed up conversations between her grandparents and their 7
children.

Please join us on Friday March 4th for the first screening of Justine's
"Exportee" and enjoy a Labatt Bleue tablette* and some delicious
poutine.

*Biere tablette translates to "shelf beer", which means the beer is
served at room temperature. This is how they would drink it in Qubec.
Try it to get the full experience!

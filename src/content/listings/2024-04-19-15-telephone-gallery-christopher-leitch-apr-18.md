Christopher Leitch
BLUEPRINTS
Presented by Allison Dawn Johnson
Hosted by Gallery Jones (back Gallery)
Opening Reception: April 18th, 5-8pm

We're excited to introduce our upcoming Telephone exhibition titled BLUEPRINTS,
showcasing artworks by Vancouver-based artist Christopher Leitch and presented by
Allison Dawn Johnson. This spring's edition will take place in the back gallery at
Gallery Jones on Thursday, April 18th, from 5-8pm.

Comprised solely of graphite on canvas, Christopher Leitchs artwork is a meticulous
exploration of intricate organic shapes achieved through extensive line repetition.
Examining a cross section of these forms unveils interconnected systems of echoing
lines, forming balanced structures that dynamically interact and converge,
contributing to a harmonious composition

Using a regimentation of line making parameters, the works draw attention to the
internal, physical rigidity within a living form while dichotomously exhibiting fluidity
and elegance within that state of growth.

Christopher Leitch is a Vancouver based painter and illustrator and holds a BFA from
the Emily Carr University of Art + Design.

Please Join us!

Nikki Peck + Anita Cirillo

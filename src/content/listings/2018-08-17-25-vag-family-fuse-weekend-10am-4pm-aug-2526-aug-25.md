The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Family FUSE Weekend - August 25 & 26, 2018

The Vancouver Art Gallery hosts its fun-filled Family FUSE Weekend on August 25 & 26, 2018 
from 10 AM - 4 PM with the summer edition Listen to the Land. This much-loved, activity-
packed weekend offers kids of all ages the opportunity to indulge their creative side by way of 
visual art, music, film and performance. Workshops on sketching, weaving, do-it-yourself cabin-
making, spoken-word poetry, DJing, soundscape-creating and much more await. Also on the 
schedule: a family-friendly film screening and interactive exhibition tours. This event is 
organized in conjunction with the Gallerys summer 2018 exhibitions, which each offer a 
multitude of ways to consider our relationship to land and place.

All programming is free for Gallery Members, as well as children aged 12 & under when 
accompanied by an adult.

For more info, visit: vanartgallery.bc.ca

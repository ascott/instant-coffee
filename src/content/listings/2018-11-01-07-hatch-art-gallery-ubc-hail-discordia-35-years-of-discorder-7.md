Opening Reception: Hatch Art Gallery, Hail Discordia! 35 Years of Discorder 
Magazine

The Nest - 2nd Floor
289-2366 Main Mall, Vancouver, BC

The Hatch is hosting an opening reception on Thursday, November 1 from 7-10 pm 
for their latest exhibition Hail Discordia! 35 years of Discorder Magazine . This 
exhibition showcases the diverse artwork in Discorder Magazines 400+ issue run, 
from 1983 to present day. Discorder is the independent magazine from CiTR Radio 
101.9FM, UBCs on-campus, student-run radio station. Though initially founded as a 
music-focused magazine, Discorder now covers a range of content related to local 
arts and current affairs, publishing 10 print issues per year with a distribution of 
8,000 copies across the Lower Mainland.

The reception includes complimentary food and a cash bar (with valid ID). 
Attendance is free and open to all.

The Hatch Gallery is located on the second floor of the Student Nest and is 
accessible via the elevators. The Hatch Gallery and Discorder acknowledge that 
they are located on the University of British Columbia campus which is founded on 
the Unceded lands of the Coast Salish, Musqueam, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples.

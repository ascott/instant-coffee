Black Arts Vancouver is hosting a movie night fundraiser for February 7th and the 28th,
from 4pm to 8:30 at Artstarts (808 Richards Street Vancouver, BC) showcasing black
directors and filmmakers in order to raise awareness and funds for our upcoming events
scheduled in March and April. Our workshops are volunteer run community based and
cater to LGBTQ+ youth and kids as well as young families. We will be serving snacks,
and light-finger food. There is a $5-$10 sliding scale in effect at the door. The movies
are TBD but are going to be all ages friendly, the building is wheelchair accessible as
well as the gallery space we will be using. For more information please check out our
website: blackartsvancouver.tumblr.com or send an email to
blackartsvancouver@gmail.com

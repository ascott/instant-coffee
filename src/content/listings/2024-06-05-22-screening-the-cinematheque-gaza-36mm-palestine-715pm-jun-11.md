Gaza 36mm (Khalil al Mozian, Palestine, 2012, 47 mins.)

Tuesday June 11, 7.15PM (doors at 7:00PM) @ The Cinematheque, 1131 Howe St,
Vancouver, BC V6Z 2K8

Free

Sponsored by SFU Contemporary Arts, UBC Film Society, and Cinema Thinks The
World

With a brief introduction and post-screening discussion facilitated by SFU's Laura
Marks.

@ubcfilmsociety

Khalil Al-Mozians rarely viewed experimental documentary is a Gazan Cinema
Paradiso that looks stunning on the large screen. The Gazan filmmaker and teacher
and his visual artist partners Tarzan and Arab reminisce about Gazas movie houses,
back before the mosques started preaching against the
dangers of movie going.
Their madcap performances in the ruined movie houses recall the earliest days of
cinema. Al-Mozian says, Gaza is like any country on earth, distinguished perhaps
by the kindness of its people despite all their suffering, Gaza deserves more than
that. Cinema makes you happy, the way going to the beach does. Gaza needs
cinema.

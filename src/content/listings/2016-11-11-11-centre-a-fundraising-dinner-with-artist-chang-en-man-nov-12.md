Saturday Night 
November 12th, 2017
6:00 - 9:00 pm
at Bambudda
99 Powell Street 
Vancouver, V6A 1E9

We would like to invite you to join Centre A on Saturday, November 12th, 6:00pm for 
a delicious dinner at the renowned local modern Chinese restaurant, Bambudda, to 
celebrate Chang En Man's first North American solo exhibition. Chang is a leading 
Taiwanese indigenous artist whose works focus on the dynamic interplay between 
story, tradition, and the struggles of indigenous peoples in the face of ongoing 
experiences of colonization. This dinner will be a fun gathering of everyone close to 
Centre A, and we would like to show our appreciation to our wonderful supporters. 

Centre A is excited to partner up with Bambudda to host this dinner. Located on 99 
Powell street is Ray Loy's beautiful restaurant with a goal of honouring Asian 
cuisine. Bambudda strives for a modern and elevated take on traditional Chinese 
food, served by a passionate kitchen with highest standards. Their passion for food 
resonates with Centre A's drive to support emerging artistic practices while 
facilitating trans-pacific and inter-cultural dialogue through our exhibitions, 
performances, talks, publications, and international exchange programs. 

The evening consists of an exquisite dinner prepared by Bambudda's own chief 
Curtis Luk (an 8-courses family style dinner specially designed for Centre A by 
owner Ray Loy), red and white wine from local wineries, a meet-and-greet with 
Chang En Man, and a take-home souvenir prepared by Centre A.

Tickets are $80 each (member's rate: $60) 

All proceeds go to support Centre A programming. Space is very limited for this 
event, to reserve your spot and/or to make a further contribution, please contact 
Ruby at: dev@centrea.org.

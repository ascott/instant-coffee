October 15 @ 7PM

Please join us for Casper Leerink's MFA Project: and we continue.

Come and participate in and we continue, an online performance that tells the story of
water. Throughout this performance you can participate in the chaotic journey from a
single water particle that starts interacting with others, towards a system encompassing
every participants input. The story is told from the perspective of water, shifting
timescales expose the unnatural pace in which water
systems change as a result of
human input.

Please access this performance using a big screen (laptop or desktop) with an up to date
browser. The performance runs 35 - 40 minutes.

Presented in partial fulfillment of the requirements of the Degree of Master of Fine Arts at
Simon Fraser University.

This work will be presented and has been developed on the unceded traditional territories
of the Coast Salish peoples of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh
(Squamish), and Sl"lwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

Dear Friends and Supporters of Centre A,

There is so much exciting news to share this New Year and we will start
with just a snippet!

It is our honour and delight to announce to you that earlier today, on our
way to re-establishing a dedicated curatorial position here at Centre A, we
formally appointed Centre A's long time curator Makiko Hara to be our 2016
Curator-in-Residence!

With the generous support of a BCAC Capacity and Sustainability Grant,
Makiko is joining us this year, with the hope that with your ongoing
support we will be able to continue our collaboration for years to come. We
thank the BC Arts Council and British Columbians for their robust support
of this curatorship. Over this year Hara will be charged with curating two
of our upcoming exhibitions, collaborating on fundraising, and
participating in the development of our 2017-19 programming.

In celebration of her appointment, on Sunday February 14th, Valentine's
Day, we will be holding a Welcome Makiko, Welcome Love Dim Sum fundraising
event at the highly acclaimed Chef Tony's in Richmond. We will also be
joined by special guest, artist Ujino Montenero.

Tickets are $100 and seating is very limited. Please email info@centrea.org
to reserve!

Also, in celebration of the Lunar New Year, this Friday & Saturday
(February 5th and 6th), the most glamorous Italian fashion brand, Versace
will be welcoming the Year of the Monkey by hosting a special two day event
and sale at their new location at 747 Thurlow Street! Not only are they
offering you a 10% discount, but they are very generously donating 10% of
sale proceeds to benefit Centre A! Please note that on Saturday, February
the 6th at 2:30pm, in collaboration with the Mah Athletic Association,
there will be a Lion Dance celebration! Your attendance is appreciated and
your shopping is encouraged!

As you can imagine, we aim to raise a lot of money this year to support
these initiatives, so we'll be hosting some parties and knocking on doors.
Your tax deductible generosity is deeply appreciated and very important to us.

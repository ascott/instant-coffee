Geist Magazine presents: The Art of the Sentence Workshop

Good writing begins with good sentences and Stephen Osborne knows
where to find them and how to write them. Learn about good verbs and
bad verbs, and even those pesky adverbs. Bring along a sharp pencil
and you'll leave with some great sentences in your pocket.

Stephen Osborne is editor-in-chief of Geist magazine.

August 25th 2012, 11am to 5pm, on Granville st. between Robson and

TOGETHER, is a new work by manomano collective presented for VIVA 
Vancouver 2012. This public art installation, textile maze, temporary 
exhibition, and collaborative project, offers a platform to reflect upon 
art, craft, community and social landscapes. We invite you to come 
downtown Vancouver to be a part of this project, reflect, play, engage 
and meet the artists.
manomano is a collective based in Vancouver since 2011. We came 
together with the intention to generate a democratic and creative space 
for experimenting, investigating and sharing our passion for textile and 
process art. Our work takes form through site-specific projects that 
intend to promote a dialog between art disciplines and community. We 
are interested in creating communal spaces for social and cultural 
interaction; offering an accessible and educational approach to art and 
process that may inspire people and communities to engage creatively. 
We are interested and respect art and craft tradition and we are 
concerned with art education as a way to foster critical and creative

Weird Flux: BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating Exhibition

Opening reception: Wednesday, April 17, 5-8PM

Exhibition runs: April 18-27 (closed April 19, 21, 22)

Gallery hours: 12-5PM

The Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory is pleased to present Weird Flux, 
the University of British Columbias 2019 BFA/BA Visual Art Graduating Exhibition. 
Embodying the contemporaneity of the graduating class, the exhibition, held at the 
Audain Art Centre, brings together ideas of celebration, boastfulness, change, growth 
and movement, while showcasing the diverse practices and accomplishments of this 
years cohort of 38 students.

We invite you to join us in revelry and refreshments as we celebrate the achievements 
and emerging talent of this years graduating class. The opening reception will take 
place at the AHVA Gallery and in the artists studios on the third floor of the Audain Art 
Centre on Wednesday, April 17, from 5 to 8PM. This event is free and open to the 
public. The exhibition will remain open April 18-27, Tuesday-Saturday, 12-5PM. (The 
exhibition is closed April 19, 21, 22.)

AHVA Gallery (rm. 1001) and Studio 3000

Audain Art Centre

6398 University Boulevard, UBC, Vancouver

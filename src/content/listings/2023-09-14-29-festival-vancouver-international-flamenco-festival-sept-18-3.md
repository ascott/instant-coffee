The Vancouver International Flamenco Festival's 33rd edition proudly announces its
2023 lineup featuring some of the finest Canadian and International Flamenco artists
from September 18 30.
The 2023 Festival will feature live performances, with both
ticketed and FREE events. VIFF promises to deliver exceptional and dynamic
programs and we celebrate our local, national and international connections and
performers from Spain, Cuba, Turkey, Mexico, India and Canada. One of this year's
highlights is the co-presentation of Spain's COMPA'A ROCO MOLINA for a 4-day
run.

Bio:

Poet and critic Stephen Collis is the author of three books of poetry, Mine
(New Star 2001) and Anarchive (New Star 2005), which was nominated for the
Dorothy Livesay Poetry Prize, and The Commons (Talonbooks 2008). His essays
on contemporary poetry and poetics have appeared in many journals, he has
edited Companions & Horizons: An Anthology of Simon Fraser University
Poetry (WCL 2005), and he is the author of two book-length studies, Phyllis
Webb and the Common Good (Talonbooks 2007) and Through Words of Others:
Susan Howe and Anarcho-Scholasticism (ELS Editions 2006). He teaches
American literature, poetry, and creative writing at Simon Fraser University.

coming up next:

Dec 4 - Susan Holbrook

Susan Holbrook is a poet and fiction writer whose first book, misled (1999)
was short listed for the Pat Lowther Memorial Award and the Stephen J.
Stephensson Award. Her long poem chapbook, Good Egg, Bad Seed, was released
in the Spring of 2004. An Associate Professor in the English Department at
the University of Windsor, Holbrook specializes in 20th-century poetry and
poetics in Canada and the U.S. Her critical articles on experimental poetry
appear in such journals as American Literature, differences, Essays on
Canadian Writing, and Open Letter. Her current research projects include
the edited correspondence of Gertrude Stein and Virgil Thomson and a
critical study of Stein in prescient relation to theories of performativity
evolving over the last fifty years. Other interests include women's
autobiography, queer theory, 19th-century American literature, theories of
translation, and creative writing.

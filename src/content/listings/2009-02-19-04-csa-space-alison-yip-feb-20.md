CSA Space presents

ALISON YIP
DRAWINGS: 2002 - 2007

Curated by Christopher Brayshaw, Adam Harrison, and Steven Tong

21 February - 20 March, 2009
Opening Friday, 20 February, 6 - 9 p.m.

Alison Yip's new exhibition at CSA Space records her ongoing engagement
with the act of drawing. Culled from a much larger image archive, the
drawings on display speak to Yip's careful observation of the world
around her, the people, animals, and objects occupying it, and her
sensitivity to the subtleties of dress and gesture that define her human
subjects.

Alison Yip is represented by Monte Clark Gallery, Vancouver and Toronto.

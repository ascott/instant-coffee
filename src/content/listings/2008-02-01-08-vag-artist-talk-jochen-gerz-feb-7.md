Artist’s Talk
 
Jochen Gerz 
Creating a Cultural City
 
Thursday, February 7 at 5:30pm
at the Vancouver Art Gallery in Room 302
 
In conjunction with the Gallery’s newest exhibition The Tree: From the Sublime to the Social, join exhibiting artist Jochen Gerz as he presents his current research on the role and function of public art in a cultural society.
 
Jochen Gerz is an artist whose public commissions deal with issues of memory, visibility and collaboration with the public. Recent works include The Place of the European Promise (Bochum) and Salviamo La Luna (Milan). Since the 1980s, he has created a number of anti-monuments in Germany, France, England and Ireland, subverting the idea of commemoration, involving the public and media in the process of creating and defining artwork: Monument against Fascism, Harburg, (with Esther Shalev-Gerz) 1986; Bremen Questionnaire, Bremen, 1990; 2,146 stones - Monument Against Racism (The Invisible Monument), Sarrebrück, 1990-93; The Living Monument, Biron, 1996; The Witnesses of Cahors, 1998; The Public Bench and The Future Monument, Coventry, 2004; amaptocare, Dublin, 2006.

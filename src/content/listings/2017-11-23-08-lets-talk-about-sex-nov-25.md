LETS TALK ABOUT SEX

The show will be a sex positive on night only art exhibit featuring photography, paintings, 
and live performances and will celebrate sexuality, sexual preference and sexual 
expression. More than sensual, more than erotic " this event will welcome all genders in 
an exploration of human sexuality in its many forms. With an aim to encourage 
conversation, promote enthusiastic consent, safe sex and sexual self-determination, 
featured works will offer a voice to a wide array of personal choices within the sexual 
spectrum. None of us would be here without sex. It is an innate part of who we are, even 
for those of us who are asexual. This show will create a safe space to explore ideas 
about relationships, sex, intimacy and pleasure.

WHEN
November 25, 2017
7:00 PM - midnight

WHERE
Element Athletic - 190 a W 3RD Ave # 110, Vancouver, BC

WHY
Proceeds from the bar, admission and the designated portion of art sales will support 
QMUNITY - an LGBTQ community centre located on Bute Street in the Davie Village 
neighbourhood of the West End of Vancouver. Jeffrey Preiss, Manager of Community 
Engagement at QMUNITY, will be joining us to share some information about what our 
support means to the organization and how it helps them to achieve their mission.

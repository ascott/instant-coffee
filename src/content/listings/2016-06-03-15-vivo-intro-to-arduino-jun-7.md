Intro to Arduino

3 hours | $36 + GST Non-Members ; $30 + GST for VIVO Producer Membership
(Extended) This event happens the first Tuesday of every month.

June 7th, 7-10pm - Register via Eventbrite

Are you interested in learning more about the Arduino but feel a bit
intimidated? This course is a 3-hour introduction to the technology: What
it is, how it works, and what you can do with it.

By the end of the class, you will know the basics of how to use your
Arduino so you can apply your knowledge to more advanced projects. This
class is designed for troubleshooting with an instructor who is available
to answer your questions. If you have questions about how to use your
device, bring them to this class.

Vancouver Civic Theatres is proud to present a free
Visual Arts Open House in the
lobbies of the Queen Elizabeth Theatre Friday, November 1 from 5-8pm. The visual
arts exhibition features work by Michael Slobodian and Sally Clark. This free event will
include a 6:30pm artist talk and musical performance throughout the evening by Lenny
Pallerstein.

On display is Michael Slobodians collection of work entitled Polaroid SX-70
Manipulations. Since the early 1980s Michael Slobodian has travelled around with his
Polaroid SX-70 camera, snapping photos and waiting for them to appear " at which
point the image is manipulated before it sets. This process of manipulation has to
happen quickly and on the spot, with only 60-90 seconds of optimum time before the
emulsion hardens and can no longer be modified. The images presented in this
collection were solely manipulated by hand, and each one is an original, one-of-a-kind
image.

Sally Clarks exhibition Divine Insider is an exploration of the landscape of a human
face. Are these portraits? Yes and
no, says Clark, My friends' faces are the starting
point for an investigation into the mystery of what it is to be human. They are
themselves and they are the More of themselves. Focused on the area between the
chin and just above the eyebrows, the features in these larger-than-life works become
iconic and subjects transform and reveal depths of self not previously seen.

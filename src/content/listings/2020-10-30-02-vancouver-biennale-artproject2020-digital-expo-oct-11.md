November 11 - 15, FREE

Digital Expo: www.artproject.io

The Vancouver Biennale will host #ArtProject2020, a virtual art and technology expo
exploring digital art and the newest technologies shifting the art world. This event is free
to attend and those without previous exposure to the field will benefit most. Everyone is
welcome. #ArtProject2020 will be the Vancouver Biennales first display of tokenized art,
which is powered by blockchain technology and has redefined digital artwork ownership.
This expo will feature 80+ international art & technology experts hosting panel
discussions, workshops, and activities exploring leading innovations in art and
blockchain.

To reserve a spot and see the complete speaker list and schedule, visit
www.artproject.io. Trilingual
programming in English, Spanish, and Chinese will be
available.

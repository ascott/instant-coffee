New Media Gallery is pleased to announce an Artist Talk with Dries Depoorter. His
fascinating work Seattle Crimes Cams (2015) is currently showing in the Eyewitness
exhibition at New Media Gallery. Dries will speak on the intricacies of his complex,
technology-based practice. He is based in Ghent Belgium and exhibits and speaks
internationally.

Depoorter creates installations that touch on surveillance, privacy, social media and
machine learning. His work often considers how technology can be used for ways not
originally intended. He tackles issues such as social identity, big data
sharing,
encryption and his works consider the (lack of) protection of our online privacy.

Depoorter attended the KASK School of Arts Ghent. His work has been presented
internationally at the Barbican Centre, MUTEK Festival, Art Basel, Bozar Para Site
Hong Kong, The Glass Room San Francisco, HEK Basel, WIRED, IDFA Doclab,
Mundaneum FOMU, Ars Electronica, Athens Digital Art Festival, Art Soutterain, STRP
festival Heidelberger, Kunstverein. He has given talks for MoMA, TEDx Brussels
,SXSW Austin, Chanel Adidas, Mutek Montreal, KIKK-festival, STRP festival, ETH
Zrich, Internet Week Denmark, Web2Day, Night By Us.

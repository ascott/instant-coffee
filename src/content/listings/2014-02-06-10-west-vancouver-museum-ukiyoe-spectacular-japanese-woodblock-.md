Ukiyoe Spectacular

Two special exhibits from a private collection in Japan will amaze 
viewers to the rich and varied traditions of Japanese wood block prints 
by over thirty artists, including Utagawa Kuniyoshi, Yoshifuji and 
Hiroshige. From horrific to whimsical, these rare prints from the late 
1800s are being shown in Vancouver for the first time. Unique but 
complimentary exhibits will be hosted by the West Vancouver Museum 
(to March 22) and the Nikkei National Museum (to March 23).

Special Ukiyoe Programs at the West Vancouver Museum:

February 12, 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.
March 5, 1 p.m. to 4 p.m.
Gallery Hop -Ukiyoe Spectacular-
Kiriko Watanabe, Assistant Curator, West Vancouver Museum
Sponsored by Godfrey Lynum

Join Ukiyoe Spectacular's co-curator Kiriko Watanabe for a guided 
gallery tour at the West Vancouver Museum and the Nikkei National 
Museum. She will share her experience of collaborating with Shin'ichi 
Ingaki, who was instrumental in making this exhibition possible, and tell 
fascinating stories about a wide array of prints on display. Advance 
registration for this private bus tour is required. It includes a round trip 
bus transportation from the West Vancouver Museum to the Nikkei 
National Museum. Please call 604-925-7179 or email 
wvmuseum@westvancouver.ca to ensure your spot.

Saturday March 8 from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m.
Kuniyoshi's Comic Prints: Exploring the Roots of Manga in Edo-Period 
Ukiyoe
Maiko Behr, Japanese Art Consultant and Translator
Co-sponsored by The Canadian Society for Asian Arts

Highlighting images from the exhibition, Ukiyoe Spectacular: Japanese 
Woodblock Prints from the 1800s, this talk will explore definitions of the 
comic genre, tracing the term "manga" and characteristic features of its 
style and subject matter into the past. In this context, closer 
examination of the humorous, bizarre, and often cryptic imagery of 
19th-century woodblock print artist Utagawa Kuniyoshi and his circle 
will illuminate intriguing connections to the world of manga comics, 
which enjoy such widespread popularity today.

Saturday March 15, 2 p.m. to 4 p.m.
(additional session is now organized by the popular demand!)
Japanese Woodcut Printmaking Demonstration
Shinsuke Minegishi, Print Media Arts Instructor, Emily Carr University 
of Art and Design

Demonstration of Japanese style woodcut printing with water-based 
inks. Artist Shinsuke Minegishi demonstrates the traditional way of 
block creation using printmaking tools. He will also demonstrate 
multiple stages of woodcut production from carving to printing with both 
traditional and modern tools which are available locally.
Artist info: www.s-minegishi.com

All programs are open to the public. Limited space is available on a 
first come first served basis.

This online-only exhibition is the Federations annual exhibition of artworks that
explore the visual language of shapes and forms. Accepting both painting and
sculpture submissions.

Open to artworks of any size, subjects, and styles
Open to available, sold and not for sale artwork.

Painting submission link: https://artists.ca/submissions/view/id/1937
Sculpture submission link: https://artists.ca/submissions/view/id/1938

About the FCA:
The Federation of Canadian Artists (FCA) is a registered charity founded in 1941 by
professional Canadian working artists including Group of Seven luminaries Lawren
Harris, Arthur Lismer, and A.Y. Jackson. The first National Executive
Council was led
by Andre Bieler, and Lawren Harris was the BC regions first Chairman. Other
notable founding members included Emily Carr and the Honorable Mark Kearley.

ENTRY FEES: are non-refundable and accepted in Canadian dollars only.

FCA MEMBERS: $20.00 for your submission and first submitted artwork. Further
submissions are $20.00 per artwork.
NON-MEMBERS: $25.00 for your submission and first submitted artwork. Further
submissions are $25.00 per artwork.

Artists can submit up to five (5) artworks for consideration. If successful at jury, a
minimum of one (1) artwork per artist will be exhibited.

COMPENSATIONS
AWARDS: First Place in Painting & Sculpture $100 CA each
Second Third Place and 3 x Honourable Mention Awards in Title.

COMMISION RATE:
FCA MEMBERS: 65% to artist, 35% to Federation
NON-MEMBERS: 50% to artist, 50% to Federation

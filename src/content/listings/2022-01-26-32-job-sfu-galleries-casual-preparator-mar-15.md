SFU Galleries is looking to expand its roster of Preparators for periodic employment.
Preparators will work closely with the Exhibitions Coordinator to install and deinstall
exhibitions at all three SFU Galleries locations: SFU
Gallery (Burnaby), Audain and
Teck Gallery (Vancouver).

Please send a resume and outlining your exhibition knowledge and experience to
christopher_lacroix@sfu.ca.

Responsibilities and Tasks
Preparators will work with the
Exhibitions Coordinator to develop and execute plans for
the installation and deinstallation of exhibitions. Regular tasks include but are not
limited to repairing and painting walls, installing vinyl lettering, unpacking and packing
artworks, installing two and three dimensional art works, installing A/V works, and
exhibition lighting.

Requirements
- One year of experience working or volunteering in a museum or gallery or equivalent
experience
- Capable of climbing ladders, operating a scissor lift (on job training provided), lifting
up to 30lbs
- Working knowledge of power tools and materials commonly used for installation and
painting.
- Experience installing Audio/Visual works consisting of monitors, projectors, media
players and speakers
- Detail Oriented
-
Excellent communication skills
- Excellent creative problem-solving skills

Assets:
- Experience with woodworking machines (tablesaw, drill press, mitre saw, etc)
- A drivers license
- Completion of a Degree or Diploma in
Fine Arts, Visual Arts or related field
- Working knowledge of BrightAuthor
- First Aid certification

Frequency of work:
SFU Galleries exhibition schedule consists of three professional exhibitions a year at
Audain Gallery and SFU Gallery and one per year at Teck Gallery. Installation of SFU
Gallery and Audain Gallery exhibitions are consecutive occurring in
September/October, January, and May/June Depending on scale and difficulty,
installations typically run 4 - 12 days, 7 hours a day, for 35 hour work weeks.

Compensation:
$22.00 - $26.00 per hour, based on experience.

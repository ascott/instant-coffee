Exercise is pleased to announce:

SALLY SPATH

July 20 - August 19

Please join us for the opening reception Friday July 20, 7-10 pm

For the past 10 years Sally Spath has made paintings directly on the
wall using a combination of paper, tape and a highly reduced colour
palette and paint stroke. Subtle in nature, her work explores a number
of different boundaries between the paper and the wall and between
positive and negative space. Spath has had solo exhibitions at the
AGYU (2003), Wynick/Tuck Gallery (2006) and goodwater gallery (2007),
and participated in a number of group exhibitions including Working
Title (2008) at Diaz Contemporary. Spath lives and works in Toronto
and this is her first exhibition in Vancouver.

An edition of photographs by Spath will be published with the exhibition.

Gallery hours 12-5 Friday and Saturday (ring buzzer)

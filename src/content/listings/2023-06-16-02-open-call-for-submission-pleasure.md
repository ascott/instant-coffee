Calling performance artists, video artists, word artists, sound artists, movement artists,
body artists, social artists and those that work with ephemeral materials (sound, light,
energy) based on Unceded Coast Salish land.

This is an opportunity to share work exploring the theme of pleasure.

Inspired by Audre Lordes Uses of the Erotic ; Pleasure Portal explores what it means
to reclaim our senses as resistance and to come back to our bodies as pathways to
liberation.

You are invited to consider notions of existence in a society that is disembodied and
positions the human body only as a site of capitalist productivity.

Together we will reclaim pleasure within the landscape of the body and beyond,
divesting from oppressive systems in order to create space for what we long for most.
May we
collectively and creatively dream of what it could mean to exist in a world
where pleasure is valued over productivity.

*Priority will be given to submissions from artists of BIPOC, disabled and Queer
communities as we work to decolonize Vancouver art spaces

Deadline for Submission: July 15th
Show date: Aug. 19th
Please send submissions/inquiries to: katyslany@gmail.com

Half an Hour of Our Time
Presented by Plastic Orchid Factory, Produced by Compagnie de la Citadelle
Influenced by Jean Cocteau and Francis Poulenc's one-act opera, La voix humaine,
James Kudelkas Half an Hour of Our Time was originally commissioned by Moonhorse
Dance Theatre in 2008. Performed in silence, this duet for a woman and a man is
dense with incident. Everything that has bound this couple is now unravelling before us.

2025 Olivier Award nominated James Kudelka is one of Canadas most acclaimed
choreographers, and he doesnt let just anyone host his work. This is a rare opportunity
to experience his brilliance in one of the citys most intimate venues. Space is limited,
so get your tickets now.

Apr 17 + 18, 7pm

Left of Main (211 Keefer St, Historic Chinatown, MST Territories/Vancouver)

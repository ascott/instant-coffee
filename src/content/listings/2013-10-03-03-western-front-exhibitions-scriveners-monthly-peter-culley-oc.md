Scrivener's Monthly is pleased to host Peter Culley reading The End of 
Hammertown on Thursday, October 3rd at 8pm in the Luxe Hall at the 
Western Front.

Peter Culley will be reading from Parkway, the third and final book of 
his Hammertown trilogy and projecting images taken from the daily 
walks he took during the period of its composition.

Peter Culley was born in 1958 and grew up on RCAF bases in Ontario, 
Alberta, Saskatchewan, Scotland and finally Nanaimo, British 
Columbia, where he has (mostly) lived since 1972. His poetry and his 
writings on visual art have been appearing for three decades in a 
variety of venues.

Scrivener’s Monthly is a series of public presentations that explore the 
space between material practices and spoken words: a periodical that 
talks. Set alongside the exhibitions program at Western Front, this 
experiment in “not publishing” involves readings, performances, and 
other articulations.

The event will be co-presented with New Star Books as a launch for 
Parkway.

Western Front Exhibitions is located at 303 East 8th Avenue, 
Vancouver. Please visit our website at www.front.bc.ca

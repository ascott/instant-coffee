CALL FOR ENTRIES: 3D Visual Artworks

VIVO Media Arts Centre is inviting proposals from Video, Computer Graphics,
Generative Media Artists, Kinetic Sculptors and 3D Animators to create a 3D
Animated Visual Art Work using VIVO's experimental Persistence of Vision
Display (POV). We are offering 3 commissions of $1000 CAD each.  Video,
Computer Graphics, Generative Media Artists, Kinetic Sculptors and 3D
Animators who are interested in working with a low resolution true 3D volumetric
display should apply.
 Artists who work with the Procce55ing programming language would be ideal.
Artists familiar with other programming languages such as JavaScipt, Java,
MaxMSP, PureData etc. and Animators who use 3D Modelers such a Blender,
MODO, 3ds Max, LightWave are also welcome. Traditional/non-digital sculptors
who are interested are welcome to apply if they can secure assistance in
translating their work into 3D computer models.

The Satellite Exchange Society (VIVO Media Arts Centre) is a Vancouver-
based, charitable, non-profit, artist-run centre that encourages and directly
supports the creation, production, education, exhibition, distribution, and
appreciation of media works by independent artists and alternative, community-
based producers and activists. We provide a dynamic artistic program that
includes exhibitions, performances, lectures, workshops, residencies, historical
archives and access to contemporary research materials. We are dedicated to
exploring the potential of new technologies in art and examining current
technological evolutions in a critical context. This POV project aims to provide
artists with a brand new means for creating and displaying works in 3D space. A
prototype of this platform is available to view at http://youtu.be/Z3vaDtZJ64g

Commissions expected to commence in early September 2012.

Submissions should include an outline project proposal, biography, CV and any
relevant supporting documents.
These can be sent to

Hannah Keba
Education Coordinator 
VIVO Media Arts Centre
1965 Main Street 
Vancouver BC
V5T 3C1

Or emailed to education@vivomediaarts.com

*Deadline Extension* Applications to be received by: Monday July 30 2012

Job Posting: Sessional Faculty @ Alberta College of Art + Design, Calgary
Deadline: December 1, 2008

The Alberta College of Art + Design is one of only four publicly funded
Canadian post-secondary institutions devoted exclusively to the advanced
education of visual artists and designers. The college has recently entered
into a vital new phase in its development and embraced a new mandate and
vision that endorse a model of the institution as a laboratory for
experimentation and as a catalyst for cultural development and creative
inquiry into the arts and emergent cultural fields.

SESSIONAL FACULTY

The college welcomes applications to fill various temporary faculty
positions for the 2009-2010 academic year. Offerings may be for the summer,
fall, winter and/or spring semesters of 2009-2010. Our academic year begins
with the summer semester in July 2009, encompasses the fall and winter
semesters, and ends with the spring semester in April 2010.

Programs seeking applications include:

Ceramics, Drawing, Fibre, First Year Studies, Glass, Jewellery + Metals,
Liberal Studies, Media Arts + Digital Technology, Painting, Photography,
Print Media, Sculpture, and Visual Communications Design

Qualifications:
The Alberta College of Art + Design offers a studio based approach to
instruction.

Studio based programs and First Year Studies require an MFA /MDes or
equivalent combination of experience/qualification, an active studio
practice and/or substantial professional practice, and post-secondary
teaching experience.

Liberal Studies requires a PhD or appropriate terminal degree, a strong
research and publication record and/or creative practice, a sound knowledge
of critical theories and methodologies, an understanding of studio based
art and design education and a preferred minimum of three (3) years
post-secondary teaching experience.

Please submit applications by December 1, 2008 including: a letter of
application; current curriculum vitae; statement outlining teaching
philosophy and practice; examples of your students' work, student
evaluations, or evidence of satisfactory teaching performance; the names
and contact information for three (3) references. Submissions must include
a digital portfolio (Mac compatible). Submit applications by mail or email to:

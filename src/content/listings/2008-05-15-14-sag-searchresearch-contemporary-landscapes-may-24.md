Surrey Art Gallery | Search/Research: contemporary landscapes exhibition | Conversations in the Gallery event  MAY 24

Conversations in the Gallery 
How do artists investigate and express the landscape? Be part of an intimate conversation with three artists exhibiting in Search/Research: contemporary landscapes at the Surrey Art Gallery. Join Blaine Campbell, Scott Massey and David Pirrie in the exhibition as they discuss the different strategies and research methodologies they use to consider the complex scientific, historical, social, political and environmental issues that affect our relationship to the landscape. Their approaches are represented through paintings, photographs, drawings and sculpture. This event is facilitated by exhibition curator Brian Foreman.

Free event.

Saturday, May 24, 1 - 2:30pm

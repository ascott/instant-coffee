RECENT ACQUISITIONS

THURSDAY, APRIL 16, DOORS 7PM + SCREENING 7:30 (sharp)

Recent Acquisitions Screening features the newest additions to Video Out
Distribution. Artists include:

Jocelyn Robert (Quebec)
Paolo Pennuti (Vancouver)
Pirouz Nemati (Montreal)
Fracesca Fini (Italy)
Frederick Cummings (Vancouver)

Video Out is the distribution branch of VIVO Media Arts Centre. It provides
artist driven distribution of media art to galleries, festivals,
educational institution collections and broadcasters to promote independent
media art nationally and internationally, and earn revenue for independent
media artists. Recent Acquisitions / New Additions are monthly screenings
of newly acquired works, free and open to the public.

The ability to affect an audience by crafting experiences that can etch themselves into a 
viewers memory is a discernable strength of many an artist. Awe is a powerful form of 
this resonance. Yet, despite its currency in art both contemporary and historical, the 
nature of awe and the conditions that instigate its occurrence are elusive. This exhibition 
presents works created in response to thoughtful study and analysis of awe, produced 
by second and third year students at Emily Carr University through the studio course on 
this same topic. 

Art Practice and Awe is a visual art studio course led by Annie Briard in the Audain 
Faculty of Art at Emily Carr University of Art and Design.

For more information contact: Annie Briard at abriard@ecuad.ca

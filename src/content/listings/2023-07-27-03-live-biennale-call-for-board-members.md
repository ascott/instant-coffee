LIVE is seeking individuals to join our Board of Directors which is
composed of
individuals who represent a diverse range of backgrounds, including artists, and other
professionals with a commitment and interest in performance art. At LIVE Biennale, we
value a Board of Directors that represents the communities and demographics of our
participating artists, writers, and cultural workers, but also those who bring diverse
skills and lived experiences to the Board. As we approach our Annual General Meeting
on August 2, 2023, we are actively seeking to fill vacancies within our board.

We are particularly interested in individuals who possess professional skills and
expertise in any of the following areas: arts administration, fundraising, grant writing,
non-profit law, accounting, real estate, web development, disability arts and advocacy,
and community engagement. Your involvement in any of these fields would greatly
contribute to our mission and programming.

LIVE Biennale promotes inclusivity and diversity within our organization. In line with
this
commitment, we strive to provide opportunities for individuals who have a diverse
range of lived experiences including those with intersectional identities. LIVE is a
space where the voices and perspectives of underrepresented communities are
amplified and valued. We strongly encourage these individuals to apply for Board
Member positions within LIVE Biennale.

By July 27th, kindly send an email to director@livebiennale.ca including your name
and a brief statement expressing your interest in becoming more actively engaged as a
Board Member.

The LIVE Board of Directors acknowledges that we work respectfully within the
ancestral, traditional, and unceded territory of the Coast Salish Peoples, including the
territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), and
Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

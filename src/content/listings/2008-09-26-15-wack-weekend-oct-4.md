16. READ BOOKS | Book launch: Andrew Dadson | OCT 7
Please list the book launch up coming at the Charles H. Scott Gallery's
READ Books

Andrew Dadson
Visible Heavens from 1850 -2008
Book Launch
7 October 2008 at 7:30pm

Please join us at READ Books for the launch of Andrew Dadson's artist book
Visible Heavens from 1850 - 2008.

In this striking volume, Vancouver artist Andrew Dadson presents a
photocopy of a found star map from 1850 and then one subsequent, slowly
degrading, copy for each year leading up to 2008, documenting the changes
from an accurate nineteenth century vision of the stars to an abstracted
blackness depicting "heaven" today.

Edited by Kathy Slade. Published by the Emily Carr University Press, the
Charles H. Scott Gallery, and JRP|Ringier with generous support from
Galleria Franco Noero and the Rennie Collection

17. Things of Desire | is looking for another writer
Canada's favourite new alt-art blogzine Things of Desire is looking for
another writer to join the ToD team. As a staff writer you would suggest
stories you think should be covered, take on assignments and do one or two
stories a week. The ideal candidate would have experience conducting
interviews as well as an ear to the ground to what's going on in Canadian
galleries. You would also have to like working for free, and have some way
of doing telephone interviews. Sound like fun?! Well, let us know why you
want to be a part of the team and include a published writing sample or a
short 100 word write up about a current or upcoming exhibition you think
ToD should cover: to thingsofdesire@gmail.com.
---Mike Landry, editor of ToD blogzine

18. Charles H. Scott | The Sound I'm Looking For | OCT 8
The Sound I'm Looking For
(Part One)

8 October to 16 November 2008
Opening Tuesday October 7th at 7:30pm
Performance by Dave Allen October 7th at 9:00pm

Dave Allen
Matthew Buckingham
Annika Eriksson
Dennis McNulty
Ian Skedd

The Sound I'm Looking For is a two-part group exhibition at the Charles H.
Scott Gallery this fall. The exhibition features contemporary artists from
Vancouver, Toronto, London (UK), Dublin, Berlin, and Stockholm who work in
a variety of media to represent sound and to challenge our expectations of
sound.

Scottish artist, Dave Allen will perform his work "Inverted Oh-Ton" at the
opening. His performance will commence at 9pm.

The Sound I'm Looking For is curated by Cate Rimmer

CALL | City of Surrey | Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre Expansion " Community
Living Room Artwork | OCT 20

The City of Surrey invites Expressions of Interest (EOI) from professional artists or
artist teams to create a suspended artwork for the interior of the Chuck Bailey
Recreation Centre Expansion.

A key theme that emerged during community engagement was the need for free and
open social spaces. In response, at the heart of the new facility is the Community
Living Room, an inclusive space that provides opportunities to meet and engage in
both planned and spontaneous ways. The space contains a lightwell that rises
vertically from the ground floor through the fitness centre on the second floor then to
the roof above. An artwork is envisioned that is suspended in the lightwell to create a
welcoming atmosphere while drawing the natural light down to animate the
Community Living Room. The artwork will also be visible through viewing windows
on the second floor.

Project Budget: $125,000

Details

Please read the Artist Call for complete details: https://www.surrey.ca/arts-

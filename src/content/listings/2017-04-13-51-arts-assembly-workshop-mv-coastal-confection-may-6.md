ARTS ASSEMBLY WORKSHOP: MV Coastal Confection | Hannah Jickling & 
Helen Reed

Saturday, May 6, 2017 1-4pm

6404 Wellington Avenue, Horseshoe Bay

Price: 20.00

Artists Hannah Jickling & Helen Reed host a taste-making focus-group with kids 
in Horseshoe Bay. In the midst of ferry queues, coast mountain vistas, kites for 
sale, (and with the threat of BMW car alarms in the distance), children are invited 
to create both delicious and disgusting flavor combinations. Participants will 
learning how to make cotton candy, and collaboratively create their own flavours. 
Werthers Originals mixed with Sour Apple Jolly Ranchers and Vicks cold lozenges 
can result in Fruity Butter Vapo-floss! These unique cotton candy confections will 
then be sold to a hungry public, who pass through the village of Horseshoe Bay. 
Adults accompanied by children are welcome to join the enterprise.

Helen Reed and Hannah Jickling have been collaborating since 2006. Their 
projects take shape as public installations, social situations and events that 
circulate as photographs, videos, printed matter, and artists multiples. They are 
currently fascinated with the contact high intrinsic to collaborative work, 
especially in their recent projects with children. Giant vegetable growers, 
orienteers and therian teens also feature in their work. Helen and Hannah have 
exhibited and performed internationally, with both individual and collaborative 
work appearing in such venues as: The Portland Art Museum (OR), The Dunlop 
Art Gallery (SK), Smack Mellon (NY), The Yukon Arts Centre Gallery (YT), YYZ 
Artists Outlet (ON), Carleton University Art Gallery (ON), Dalhousie University Art 
Gallery (NS), The Vancouver Art Gallery (BC), The Power Plant (ON) and Flat 
Time Houses first issue of NOIT (UK). In Spring 2017 they will release Multiple 
Elementary, a book explores the elementary school classroom as a site of 
invention and reception of contemporary art practices, co-published by 
YYZBOOKS and Black Dog Publishing. They currently teach at the Emily Carr 
University of Art + Design in Vancouver, Canada where they received the 2016 
Ian Wallace Award for Teaching Excellence.

Chance Geometry by Olivia Bull
Book Launch and Exhibition

The Toast Collective, 648 Kingsway
December 18th, 7:30-10:30pm

Chance Geometry is photographer Olivia Bull's first self-published photo book. 
Through these photos, Olivia investigates the repetitive geometric systems 
occurring in her everyday surroundings. Arrangements of shape and colour 
link the structured, compositions, while a sense of emptiness pervades the 
minimal series.

The show will display prints from the book and other selected photographs. 
Copies of Chance Geometry will be for sale.

All photographs are shot on 35 or 120mm film.

Gallery Hours will be announced

In Conversation with Kuh Del Rosario and Scott Lewis, at FIELD Satellite

Tuesday November 18, 7pm
FIELD Satellite
29 West Broadway
Vancouver BC

FIELD Contemporary invites you to an evening of conversation with Kuh Del
Rosario and Scott Lewis, at FIELD Satellite. This evening is designed to
facilitate exchange between the artists and the attendees, in an informal
environment. Words around the artists practices and artworks in the current
exhibition N I R D V A N D V A will be co-facilitated by Avalon Mott and
Vanessa Brown. Attendees will be invited to join in this open discussion.

All welcome.

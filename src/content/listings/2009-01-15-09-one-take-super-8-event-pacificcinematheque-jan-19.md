Monday January 19 2009 | PacificCinematheque | 7:30pm | Presented by DIM |

dimcinema.ca | onetakesuper8event.blogspot.com | cinematheque.bc.ca

ONE ROLL. NO SPLICES. SHOOT AND SHOW. This is the mantra of the One Take
Super 8 Event. Now in its eighth season, One Take Super 8 has inspired the
production of over 400 Super 8 films that were shot on one roll of film and
screened without preview or editing, in Regina, Winnipeg, Ft. Lauderdale
FL, Montreal, Ottawa, Oberlin OH, and Syracuse NY. Toronto based filmmaker
Alex Rogalski, the event’s producer, will perform the projection of 18
selections from the event’s archives. Like other DIY Super 8 Projects, such
as Vancouver’s Project8, the One Take Super 8 Event is a celebration of
accessible filmmaking, empowered storytelling, and chance genius.

DIM Is a monthly evening of contemporary short from moving images and
cinematic collaborations. DIM is focused on expanding the visibility of
Canadian and international experimental artists and their practices in the
cinema; and illuminating underground moving image culture in Vancouver.
Programmed by Amy Lynn Kazymerchyk through the Pacific Cinematheque
Pacifique. For more information contact programming[at]dimcinema[dot]ca

DIM evenings take place on the 3rd Monday of every month unless otherwise
stated, and commence at 7:30pm. Screenings are held at the Pacific
Cinematheque Pacifique at 1131 Howe St. Vancouver BC Canada | Screenings
are 18yrs+ | Tix $9.50/$8students + $3membership | cinematheque.bc.ca

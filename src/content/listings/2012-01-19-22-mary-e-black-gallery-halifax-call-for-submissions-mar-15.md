The Mary E. Black Gallery is accepting proposals for solo, duo, or 
group exhibitions for 2014. Submissions are welcome from community 
groups, cultural groups, guilds, artists/craftspeople, and curators.
Deadline for submissions is March 15, 2012.
Please consult proposal guidelines available at: http://www.craft-
design.ns.ca/prop.html

Our mandate, as Nova Scotia's only public fine craft gallery, is to exhibit 
traditional and contemporary fine craft created by local, regional, 
Canadian and international artists. Year round the Centre for Craft and 
Design hosts juried, curated and travelling exhibitions in the Mary E. 
Black Gallery which educate the public about craft and design, and 
promote the pursuance of excellence in the craft community.

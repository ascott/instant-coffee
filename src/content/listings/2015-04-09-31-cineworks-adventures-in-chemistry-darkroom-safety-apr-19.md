A two-hour lab workshop designed to introduce participants to the roles of
chemicals in photographic work: their risks, their benefits and the
procedures required to use them safely.

To the novice, many of the precautions on photographic chemicals can sound
like exaggeration designed to protect the manufacturer rather than inform
the photographer, but the dangers are serious. Chemicals commonly
encountered in the darkroom range from the harmless to violent poisons and
it is important for the darkroom enthusiast to understand the hazards faced
in the pursuit of art.

This workshop will be a useful refresher for occasional darkroom users as
well as a great starting point for beginners.

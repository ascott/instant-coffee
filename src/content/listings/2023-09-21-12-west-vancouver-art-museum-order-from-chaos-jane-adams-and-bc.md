Join us for a curatorial tour of the West Vancouver Art Museums current exhibition, Order
From Chaos: B.C. Binning and Jane Adams.

FREE

This exhibition explores the art of B.C. Binning and Jane Adams, and how the former
influenced the work of the latter. Adams is the daughter of Jean Coulthard, the Canadian
composer, and Don Adams, who ran a modern Scandinavian furniture store in Vancouver.
The Adams were close friends with B.C. Binning and his wife, Jessie. As a child, Adams
visited Binnings studio and became familiar with his paintings and designs. As she
developed her
own artistic practice, she came to recognize the impact of this early
influence. This exhibition includes works by Binning from the Collection of the West
Vancouver Art Museum.

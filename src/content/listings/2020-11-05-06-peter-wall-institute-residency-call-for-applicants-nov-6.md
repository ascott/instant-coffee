The Peter Wall Institute for Advanced Studies has issued a call for the Wall Scholars
2021-2022 program at the University of British Columbia. This years thematic program
is open to Canadian and international scholars and artist-scholars from any discipline.
Successful scholars will spend a year in residence at the Peter Wall Institute for
Advanced Studies in Vancouver, Canada, focusing on the theme of Complex Systems.
The deadline for applications is
November 6, 2020. Application details are available on
our website, here. Pre-submission inquiries are welcome.

THIS Gallery is excited to present Whirl, a solo exhibition by Vancouver painter Leon
Phillips, running October 10-19, 2025.

Phillips paintings are all about movement, colour, and the body. Working with canvases
on the floor, he builds up swirling layers of paint that feel alive with gesture and energy.
His process transforms painting into a shared sensory experience"more than just
looking, its about feeling.

Whirl also pushes back on the old gendered ideas of art history, where gesture was seen
as masculine and colour as feminine. Phillips brings these together in bold, non-binary
ways, expanding how we understand abstraction today.

Opening Reception: Saturday, October 11, 12-4 pm

THIS Gallery, 108 East Broadway (alley), Vancouver, BC

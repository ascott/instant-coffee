DIED YOUNG STAYED PRETTY

A feature documentary about rock posters
by Eileen Yaghoobian

@

Vancity Theater

Dec. 10 &11 @ 7pm 2008
TWO NIGHTS ONLY

Watch the trailer :
WWW.DIEDYOUNGSTAYEDPRETTY.COM

"DIY artists of every stripe, this is your movie"- JOHN
GRIFFIN, THE GAZETTE
**** Eileen Yaghoobian's fabulous debut doc is an inclusive
look at the renaissance of poster art in the wake of punk and the rebirth
of a defiant counterculture. Utilizing many of the same in-your-face visual
techniques, wed to an obsessive desire to interview everyone remotely
associated with the movement, she talks to giants like the Ames Brothers,
Print Mafia and Rob Jones, who between them have created work for Arcade
Fire, the White Stripes, Radiohead, the Melvins and many bands you've never
heard of, but may one day. DIY artists of every stripe, this is your movie.

"DYSP is a thoroughly enjoyable film."- JON MANDELL - ADBUSTERS

"Finally somebody had the endurance and fortitude and plain old

shelters and telephone poles prêt de chez vous." - MELORA KOEPKE, HOUR

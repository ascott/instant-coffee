Downtown Shopfront Murals
#makeartwhileapart

August 17 - September 18, 2020

An exhibition about the murals painted on the
temporary hoardings put up on many
downtown Vancouver ground level stores,
restaurants and bars during the early stages
of the Pandemic. The exhibition features 
in-situ documentary photographs of the pieces
as well as portions of selected murals.

www.pendulumgallery.bc.ca

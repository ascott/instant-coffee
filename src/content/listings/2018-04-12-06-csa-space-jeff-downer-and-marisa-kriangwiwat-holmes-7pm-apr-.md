Jeff Downer and Marisa Kriangwiwat Holmes
CSA Space
2414 Main St, Vancouver, BC

Opening Thursday April 12th @ 7pm
Until May 12th

"Objects of my Affection"

At first glance, through the conventions of photographic sculptures in their broadest
sense, Downer and Holmes both play with the relationship between the photographic
image and their modes of presentation. Using primarily mass-produced, run-of-the-mill
household objects as materials, both artists are concerned with the balance between
subjects and the objects that confine them. Their works together meet at a new platform
which hopes to raise questions on photography now. Working within no strict
boundaries, participating in a current visual slang.

DARC Indigenous Residency Program

Submissions close June 30th, 2025

Submissions for the DARC Indigenous Residency are now open!

Presented with the support of The Hnatyshyn Foundation, DARCs Indigenous
Residency is a one-month intensive on-site artist residency, offered to mid-career
Indigenous (First Nations, Inuit, and Mtis) artists across Canada who are seeking to
develop their current practice, experiment with a new medium, or continue an
existing project.

The residency provides access to DARC's facilities and equipment, up to 16 hours of
advisor time, and an artist fee of $2,000 plus $500 for an artist talk or presentation,
with out-of-town recipients also being granted a housing subsidy of up to $1500 and
up to $1750 in travel expenditures. Submissions for the Residency will open on May
2nd, 2025, and the residency will run for the month of October 2025.

Visit our website to apply. Applications are due by June 30th, 2025.

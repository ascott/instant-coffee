The Polygon is delighted to offer a virtual screening of a remarkable short film by the
highly-acclaimed Thai filmmaker Apichatpong Weerasethakul. A newly-created
commission in conjunction with the Third Realm exhibition, October Rumbles is a
poignant response to this time of global crisis, and is accompanied by Apitchatpongs
writing. A podcast conversation between Apitchatpong and film and media curator, Noah
Cowan, to follow next week. October Rumbles will be available for a limited two week
viewing online, until November 12.

Watch here: https://thepolygon.ca/news/apichatpong-weerasethakul-october-rumbles/

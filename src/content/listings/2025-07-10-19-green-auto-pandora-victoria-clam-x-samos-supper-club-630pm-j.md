SAMOS x CLAM is back w/ a 15+ course menu of small plates collaboratively
developed with Kieran and Nico (of Samos) paired with an experimental audio work
by Yasmine Whaley-Kalaora.

Last year we collaborated for the first time, pairing food and film. This year were
looping back with an extended menu of small plates situated in a live audio work (an
extension of Yasmines ongoing series of audio essays, cite/site).

For this event, were taking the groggy dream audio note as a starting point to think
on the disjunct-soothing of repetition, looping and Sisyphean progress cycles.
Through sound, ingredients and flavours were winking at the uncanny to see where
we can find relief/humour/solace in a time when progress/resolution/resolve is
anything but linear.

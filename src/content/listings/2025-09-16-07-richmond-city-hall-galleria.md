Richmond City Hall Galleria
Located just across the street from the Richmond Art Gallery and on the ground floor
of Richmond City Hall, City Hall Galleria is an offsite exhibition space overseen by
Richmond Art Gallery. As part of its outreach to the local community, City Hall
Galleria primarily highlights artworks with a close connection to Richmond in terms of
themes and subject matter. City Hall Galleria does annual open calls and accepts
submissions throughout the year. All submissions by Richmond artists will also be
considered for The Annex Gallery. Comprising two major walls, City Hall Galleria
primarily showcases framed or ready-to-hang two-dimensional artworks. There is
also a 32 flat screen TV where videos can be displayed.

Next deadline: November 7, 2025 for 2026 programming. We offer CARFAC fees for
Exhibitions in Other Public Places (2026 schedule): $594 for solo exhibitions; $297
for two-person exhibitions. Selected artists will be contacted by January 30, 2026.
Due to the volume of submissions, only selected artists will be contacted.
Submissions will be kept on file for a period of up to one year for consideration.

Submission Checklist
Brief bio
One-page (maximum) artist statement
One-page statement of proposed work
Five to seven JPEGS (up to 300 dpi and 2 MB each) of proposed work through file-
sharing software Google Drive or Dropbox. Make sure the link is open as multiple
staff may view it using different emails
An image list with thumbnails of the work, artist name, title of the work, year,
medium, and dimensions
Website and/or any social media accounts
Please email complete submissions to galleria@richmondartgallery.org with the
subject line Galleria Submission 2026. If you need assistance or any
accommodations, please dont hesitate to reach out via email. For more information,
visit https://www.richmondartgallery.org/exhibitions/off-site.

The Annex Gallery
Located right across the main entrance of the Richmond Cultural Centre, and on the
ground floor of Richmond Cultural Centre Annex, The Annex Gallery is an offsite
exhibition space overseen by Richmond Art Gallery. As part of its outreach to the
local community, The Annex Gallery is dedicated exclusively to Richmond-based
artists. The Annex Gallery does annual open calls and accepts submissions from
Richmond-based artists throughout the year. Comprising three major walls, The
Annex Gallery primarily showcases framed or ready-to-hang two-dimensional
artworks. There is also a 32 flat screen TV where videos can be displayed.

Next deadline: November 7, 2025 for 2026 programming. We offer CARFAC fees for

for two-person exhibitions. Selected artists will be contacted by January 30, 2026.
Due to the volume of submissions, only selected artists will be contacted.

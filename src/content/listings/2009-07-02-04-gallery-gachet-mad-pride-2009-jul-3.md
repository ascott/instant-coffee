Mad Pride 2009 – Group ExhibitionJuly 3 – August 2,2009
Dates: Opening Reception: Friday, July 3, 7-10pm, Cabaret Night: Saturday July 18, 8 - 11pm

A non-juried group exhibition and multi-disciplinary cabaret featuring work addressing mental health 
issues, art and healing, human rights and psychiatry. For this year’s Mad Pride, local artists were 
invited to respond to one of the 6 statements included in Gallery Gachet’s Basis of Unity.

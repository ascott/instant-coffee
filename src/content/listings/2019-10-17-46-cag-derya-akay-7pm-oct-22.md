Publication Launch | Derya Akay | Manti, Borek, Baklava

Tuesday, October 22, 7pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5

Join us for the launch of our new publication culminating Derya Akays 2017 Burrard Marina
Field House Residency project Manti, Borek, Baklava. At the launch Akay will share Baklava
prepared by special guests.

Presented as part of Vancouver Art Book Week

This publication is made possible with the generous support of The Province of British
Columbia
through the Multiculturalism Grants Program. The Contemporary Art Gallery gratefully
acknowledges support for the Burrard Marina Field House Studio Residency Program from
the
Vancouver Board of Parks and Recreation, the City of Vancouver and the Vancouver
Foundation, along with many private and individual donors.

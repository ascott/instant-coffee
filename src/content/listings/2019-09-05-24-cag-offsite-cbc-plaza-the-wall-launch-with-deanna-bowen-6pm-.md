The WALL Launch with Deanna Bowen
Tuesday, September 10, 6pm, free
Off-site at CBC Plaza, 700 Hamilton St

Join us to celebrate the launch of the 2019 WALL, a monumental new commission by artist
Deanna Bowen on the wall of the CBC Plaza in Downtown Vancouver. Continuing Bowens
work in tracing Vancouvers Black entertainment community from the 1940s through the 1970s,
as seen in her recent exhibition at CAG, Night Prowl captures part of a film frame from a CBC
news story that aired in October 1959, reporting on the dramatic purging and forced closures of
many nightclubs in Vancouvers ethnically diverse east end.

Following the event, an advanced screening of CBCs In the Making will take place at VIFF,
presenting two short documentaries of artists Ken Lum and Deanna Bowen. The screening will
be followed by a Q&A between Bowen and In the Making host Sean ONeill.

The WALL is a Vancouver Heritage Foundation public art initiative presented in partnership with
CBC Radio-Canada, the City of Vancouver Public Art Program, and with additional support from
JJ Bean Coffee Roasters. The WALL features a new artist every year.

JACQUELINE TARQUINIO

Welcome to your day

June 15 - July 14

the University of Rhode Island and later at the Staedelschule,
Frankfurt. In 2010 she participated in a residency at the Center for
Contemporary Art in Kitakyushu, Japan. Her work is currently on view

We would like to invite you to join us on Tuesday, April 24 at 7 pm to celebrate the 
launch of these publications:

Glue by Claire Geddes Bailey
Swan Dives by Kiel Torres
A Lot by Christian Vistan
Liminal Ziplock by Emma Metcalfe Hurst
Stop Wincing/We're Fine by Cristina Holman

Over the past ten months, the Studio for Emerging Writers have been running weekly/bi-
weekly workshops, with occasional site visits, discussions, meals, walks, edits, bus and 
boat rides, readings, and hang-outs. During this time together, we brewed a cider 
alongside a set of five chapbooks, each written by a participant of the Studio. 

Publications and refreshments will be available for sale. 
Special thanks to Sheryda Warrener, Stephan Garneau and Erica Wilk. 
Bios for the participants can be found on our website. 

Artspeak gratefully acknowledges the BC Arts Council Youth Engagement program for 
their support of this initiative.

School of Communication's Book + Speaker Series

Description of series:
The School of Communication's Book + Speaker Series is a space in which the 
School engages with recently published books by faculty and other members of 
the School community. A selected reading will be circulated well in advance to 
allow for informed discussion between the audience and author. The event is 
moderated by an invited respondent.

The monthly series is conceived as a social event where faculty and students 
come together regularly to celebrate the achievements of our scholarly 
community, think critically, pose questions and search for new avenues for 
research and activism.

Host: SFU School of Communication @ Polyanna Library 

Time: 6pm - 9pm 
Date: Nov 15th
Location: 221 E Georgia St #100, Vancouver, BC 

Speaker: Dr. Rick Gruneau Respondent: Dr. Adel Iskandar 

Speaker bio:

Richard Gruneau is Professor of Communication at Simon Fraser University in 
Burnaby, British Columbia. His areas of research interest include leisure, sport, 
and popular cultures; political economy of communication; media studies; critical 
social theory; historical sociology; modernity studies; and the politics of mega-
events. A concern for interdisciplinary research, the critique of ideology, and the 
analysis of social inequality in Canada and elsewhere are consistent themes in 
his work. 

His recent publications include the books Sport and Modernity (Polity Press, 
2017) and Mega Events and Globalization: Capital and Spectacle in a Changing 
World Order, co-edited with John Horne (Routledge, 2016); as well as a number 
of other essays and reports, including (with James Compton) Media Events, 
Mega-Events and Social Theory: From Durkheim to Marx, in Wenner and Billings 
(eds.) Sport, Media and Mega-Events (Routledge, 2017); The Somatic/Linguistic 
Turn in Histories of Exercise and Sport. In Edelman and Wilson (eds.), The 
Oxford Handbook of Sport History (Oxford University Press, 2017); and Goodbye 
Gordie Howe: Sport and Class Inequality in the Pay for Play Society. In 
Christopher Waddell and David Taras (eds.) How Canadians Communicate V: 
Sports. (Athabaska University Press, 2016). 

In addition to his scholarly research and writing, as senior supervisor or 
supervisory committee member, Gruneau has been involved in the supervision of 
more than 120 graduate students over the course of his career. Many of these 
have gone to successful careers in Departments of Communications, Sociology, 
and Kinesiology at universities in North America, Europe, South America and the 
West Indies. In 2012 Gruneau was awarded the Simon Fraser University, Faculty 
of Graduate Studies, Deans Medal for 
Excellence in Graduate Supervision.

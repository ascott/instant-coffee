Call For Submissions

The Fountain Project asks artists to reflect on Marcel Duchamp as a muse
for the contemporary art world. Whether you reject or accept conceptual art
you are engaged in a dialogue with Duchamp. This is a web based project and
is ongoing.

To view what is been done so far visit:
http://thefountainproject.wordpress.com

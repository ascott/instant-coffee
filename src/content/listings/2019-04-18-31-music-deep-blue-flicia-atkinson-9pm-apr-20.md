Deep Blue is pleased to present a concert by French artist and musician Flicia Atkinson.

Flicia Atkinson works within the disciplines of visual art, experimental music, and poetry. 
Her performances emerge from an interest in musical and literary discourses"from Joan La 
Barbara, Robert Ashley and Delia Derbyshire, to Georges Bataille, Antonin Artaud, and 
Henri Michaux"and combine spoken narratives with ambient and musique concrte type 
soundscapes to address topics found within sci-fiction, geography, musicology, and art 
history. A graduate of Les Beaux Arts de Paris, Atkinson co-publishes the label and 
curatorial platform Shelter Press together with her partner Bartolom Sanson. Flicia is 
currently a PhD researcher in musicology at Rennes 2 University.

Atkinson has performed at institutions such as Issue Project Room in New York, Cafe Oto in 
London, Ina GRM in Paris, Fylkingen in Stockholm, Palais de Tokyo Paris, Emily Harvey 
Foundation in New York, Reed University Portland, CalArts in Valencia, Human Resources 
in Los Angeles, and Ambient Church in Brooklyn. Additionally she has exhibited works at 
Bozar in Brussels, Kunsthal Charlottenborg in Copenhagen, Komplot in Brussels, 
ChertLdde Gallery in Berlin, CAC La Galerie Noisy Le Sec, Overgaden in Copenhagen, 
CAC La Crie Centre dArt in Rennes, Last Resort Gallery in Copenhagen, MUCA ROMA in 
Mexico City, City Limits in Oakland, and Blindside Gallery in Melbourne. In 2013 she was 
awarded the Langui Prize for Painting / Young Belgian Art Prize and received fellowships 
from Institut Franais, Villa Medicis Hors Les Murs, and FNAGP. Residencies include the 
Rupert Vilnius, CAC La Galerie Noisy le Sec, Los Angeles Contemporary Archives, 
Bundanon Trust in Australia, RMIT in Melbourne, EMS Studio in Stockholm.

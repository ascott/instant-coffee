just lines
drawings by colin mclaine
music by shed

30 east 6th ave, suite 103 (Dynamo Arts Association)
October 18th, 8:30 - 11pm

just lines is a visual art installation, accompanied by live music. The 
drawings in Just lines use simple materials - sharpies and newsprint - to 
transmit a physical impulse. Via movement, repetition and connection to 
wave forms, mclaine creates a room-sized recording of the moment. Raga-
inspired improvisations are interspersed with songs, with synth by Andrew 
Short, guitar, drums and vocals by colin mclaine.

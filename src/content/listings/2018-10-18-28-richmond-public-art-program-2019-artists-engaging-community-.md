2019 Artists Engaging Community Program, Request for Qualifications (RFQ)

The City of Richmond Public Art Program seeks one artist or artist team with socially 
oriented practices to engage diverse and multigenerational audiences in a unique 
public art opportunity. The selected artist will have a performance and socially-
oriented practice and will work across two sites including Minoru Chapel, in Minoru 
Park and the nearby City Centre Community Centre. This project-based artist-in 
residence opportunity invites emerging and professional artists to imagine innovative 
ways to engage seniors, adults, youth and children in the making of performance-
based artwork, fostering individual creative expression, multigenerational cross-
cultural exchange and community building.

Budget: $10,000

Eligibility: Artists residing in British Columbia

Deadline: Monday, October 22, 2018, 5:00 p.m.

Duration: January - December 2019

For more information and how to apply, please visit 
www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities

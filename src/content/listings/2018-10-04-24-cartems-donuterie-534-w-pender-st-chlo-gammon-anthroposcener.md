Join us at Cartems Donuts (534 W Pender St) for the opening reception of 
Anthroposcenery, a collection of works by Chlo Gammon.

Reception: October 13th, 2018 from 7:00-9:00pm

We're thrilled to be partnered with the Contemporary Art Gallery's Young Patrons 
program to showcase work of Vancouver-based artists that speak to experiences 
within our city. 

Chlo Gammon is an artist and designer working within the practices of printmaking, 
painting, graphic design and illustration. She received her BFA from Emily Carr 
University in 2010.

Her work explores the concept of the Anthropocene, the proposed current geological 
epoch where humans now change the Earths systems more than all natural forces 
combined. Employing levity, she looks at the fantastical possibilities of how our 
actions will impact our geological age. By combining the pop aesthetic and 
fabricated elements of recreation parks with galactic voids, the artist projects future 
relics, floating microcosms of our legacy on a planetary scale.

Chlo lives and works in Vancouver BC on the unceeded Musqueam, Squamish, 
and Tsleil-Waututh territories.

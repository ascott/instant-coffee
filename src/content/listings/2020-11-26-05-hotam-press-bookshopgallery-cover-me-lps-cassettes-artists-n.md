Open hours: Saturday 12 - 5 PM, or by appointment

This exhibition brings together over 50 Canadian and International artists who work with
music and sound. For the duration of this exhibition, our gallery will be temporarily
transformed into a record shop / discotheque /
listening space. Visitors are welcome to
browse and sample through this rich collection of artists works.

Here are some of the participating artists and contributors:

Alan Belcher, Bill Burns, Cathy Busby, Juan Cisneros Neumann, Brady Cranfield, Laura
Gilder, Makiko Hara, Hotham Sound, Laiwan, Daniel Laskarin, Zachari Logan, Doug
Melynk, my name is scot, Kristin Nelson, Carol Sawyer, Kathy Slade, Chrysanne Stathacos,
Paul Walde, Lam Wong, . . . and many more.

Also, we are still accepting LPs and Cassettes by artists in an ongoing basis. New
submissions will be added to the exhibition. Please do drop off or send us your work to be
part of the show.

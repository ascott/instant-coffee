Founded in 2006, Fort Gallery is a mobile, artist-run center dedicated to contemporary art
through exhibitions, workshops, and public programs. It brings engaging, challenging, and
thought-provoking work to the Fraser Valley in alternative and public spaces across the
region.

In celebration of our 20th anniversary, Fort Gallery is seeking for artists to propose and lead a
variety of one-day events and workshops taking place throughout 2026!

Proposed workshops should be accessible to all skill levels and encourage thoughtful
engagement with place, while offering participants opportunities to connect with other
creatives in the community. A wide range of mediums, approaches, and themes are welcome.
Priority will be given to artists who live or work in the Fraser Valley.

Submission Link: http://bit.ly/4kGKpmM

Selected artists will be compensated in accordance with the CARFAC 2026 fee schedule. All
general materials, marketing, and venue coordination will be provided by Fort Gallery.

Fort Gallery welcomes proposals from artists at all stages of their practice. This form is
intended to gather information about your workshop idea and its feasibility. Only successful
candidates will be contacted. Proposals will be kept on record for future consideration by the
Programming Committee. If you have questions or need support completing this form, please
contact fortgalleryarc@gmail.com.

Submission Deadline: March 17th, 2026.

Not Sent Letters & Guests

interdisciplinary works by 
DAMLA TAMER
STEPHANIE SKOURTES
SAJIA SULTANA KABIR
DEL HILLIER
EMMETT HALL
PRISCILLA COSTA

a screening by ALISHA WENG & MARC GUENETTE

a not sent letters set by JEREMY TODD, GRACE SALEZ & JUDITH PRICE

@ The TOAST COLLECTIVE, 648 Kingsway, Vancouver 
Saturday Night, March 23, 2019 Doors open at 7pm (back alley entrance only). The 
programme begins at 7:30pm SHARP! $5-$20 suggested sliding scale donation. No one 
will be turned away due to lack of funds. 

All proceeds will go directly to Unist'ot'en Camp, in solidarity with Wet'suwet'en peoples 
currently occupying their traditional, unceded territories while resisting non-consensual 
pipeline development and RCMP violence. If you are not attending this event but want to 
support Unist'ot'en Camp, learn more here: http://unistoten.camp

NOT SENT LETTERS & GUESTS form public constellations of engaged 
interdisciplinary practice, unmediated by the realpolitik conditions of contemporary art as 
a competitive professional sphere. The interrelatedness of art, society and everyday life 
is critically explored amongst a diverse plurality of artists and publics. JEREMY TODD 
instigates each event as an extension of his ongoing NOT SENT LETTERS project 
(involving image/text epistolary detours online since 2005, digital shorts, interdisciplinary 
performance works and cooperatively realized public events). This is the twenty-sixth 
iteration of NSL&G: https://notsentlettersproject.com

DAMLA TAMER was born in Istanbul, Turkey and has been living, teaching, and 
producing art on this side of the world for the past ten years. Her work and research 
explore how models of agency and solidarity create particular kinds of etiological 
structures and configure our notions of space and time while exploring concepts of 
hospitality, forgiveness, sincerity, risk, and representation. Damla returns to NSL&G to 
present material in relation to the ongoing trial cases of Academics for Peace, a large 
group of academics currently being charged in Turkey for having signed the petition We 
will not be a party to this crime! to call an end to state violence in the Kurdish regions of 
the country.

STEPHANIE SKOURTES contributes to NSL&G for the first time with the one act 
monologue, Hysterical: A Feminist Killjoy in Bed with Masculinity exploring the 
intersections of hetero-patriarchy, misogyny, and intimate partner violence. Dr. Skourtes 
is a scholar, documentarian, and sociologist. She is interested in critically theorizing and 
uncovering the everyday material realities that shape daily life and using this knowledge 
in the service of transformative social change. Towards this end she creates visual, 
radio, and performance works, documentary in form or content, which showcase 
marginalized voices and ideas as a kind of social praxis.

SAJIA SULTANA KABIR is a poet-composer, dancing songwriter, creative dance artist, 
and singing actress based in Vancouver. She is passionate about classical and folk art 
forms, science fiction and fantasy, avant-garde and DIY performance, and social justice. 
Sajia contributes to NSL&G for the first time with new material for voice and keyboard, 
debuting a set of love letters which detail the complicated relationship she has with her 
queer community.

DEL HILLIER, a Vancouver-based interdisciplinary artist and musician, contributes to 
NSL&G for the first time with a multimedia performance entitled How To Make A 
Country Song. Del tells the story of his journey to Merritt, BC, Canadas Country Music 
Capital and home of the official Canadian Country Music Hall of Fame. In a romantic 
quest to discover how to write a country song, Del's visit quickly disheartens him, as the 
small interior BC town does not seem to provide the answers he is searching for, 
reminding him instead of complex and unsettled feelings from his rural and isolated 
upbringing.

EMMETT HALL is an accomplished performer, writer, musician, conceptual artist and 
comedian who has worked with affected personas and high concept characters in 
various mediums for many years. Emmett contributes to NSL&G for the first time by 
attempting an honest stand-up comedy set -- just as himself.

PRISCILLA COSTA is a renowned Vancouver-based artist with an extensive 
background in Clown Theatre & Clown du Bouffon. From Fine Art to Clown to Energy-
healing school, Priscilla finds herself fascinated by the masks we wear and what lies 
under them. In 2016 she toured her full length solo Bouffon show with the vivacious and 
outlandish Mona Mona, who won audiences over whilst playing with trickster, 
innocence, wild feminine and rape-culture. Currently, Priscilla has been called by her 
spiritual practice to pull back her own mask and to dig into more personal terrain. She is 
currently working on a new full length show called FIST and will be workshopping a 
section of it as a first contribution to NSL&G.

ALISHA WENG & MARC GUENETTE started collaborating last year, creating work for 
the Vancouver Asian Film Festival and other art projects. They will screen a new work 
for the upcoming programme informed by their joint efforts so far. Alisha is a Vancouver-
based Digital Media artist. Her practice focuses on photography and video. She has 
contributed to past NSL&G programmes and photo-documented several of them for the 
project. Marc, a Vancouver-based Graphic Designer and Videographer, is contributing to 
NSL&G for the first time. Marc has over a decade of experience performing and 
recording music, which ties into his passion for video. 

JUDITH PRICE & GRACE SALEZ are Victoria-based artists whose long-term practices 
include performance, installation, video and film. They are members of the performance 
collective Open Action while also maintaining individual art practices. They return to 
NSL&G to collaborate with JEREMY TODD in the realization of a new NOT SENT 
LETTERS SET. They have chosen four letters from Jeremys project archive to engage 
with in performance (as he reads them): To Our Absences (seventh letter), To These 
Pictures of the Departed (fourth letter), To These Pictures of You And Your Twelve 
Theses On The Economy Of The Dead (first letter) and To This Remembrance of Spring 
(first letter).

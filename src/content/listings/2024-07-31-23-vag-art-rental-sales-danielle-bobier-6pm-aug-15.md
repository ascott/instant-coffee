Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist talk with Featured
Artist Danielle Bobier in the Art Rental & Sales showroom on Thursday, August 15 at
6PM.
Danielle Bobier is a Vancouver-based artist who finds influence in the forces of
nature and the ever-changing atmosphere of the internal experience. Through the
actions of observation and transformation, her practice filters both inner and outer
landscapes through an abstracted and symbolic lens. Working primarily as a painter,
she fosters an
interest in the perceptual and emotive effects of colour as a vehicle
for meditation

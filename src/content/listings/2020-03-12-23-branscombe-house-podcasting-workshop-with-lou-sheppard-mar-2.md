Two Part Podcasting Workshop with Branscombe House Artist-In-Residence Lou
Sheppard
Workshop 1: Saturday, March 21, 2020, 10:00am-12:00pm, Branscombe House, 4900
Steveston Highway, Richmond
Workshop 2: Saturday. March 28, 2020, 10:00am-12:00pm, Richmond Media Lab,
Richmond Cultural Centre, 7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond

Join Branscombe House artist-in-residence Lou Sheppard for a two-part podcasting
workshop. Participants will learn the basics of audio
recording and skills to create
original podcasts, and together the group will create an original podcast inspired by
stories from the City of Richmond. These workshops will be led by Lou Sheppard and by
Audio Engineer Pamela Hart. We will cover the basics of story development and audio
recording. Well edit our podcasts and share them online. This is a free two-part
workshop open to all " participants are encouraged to attend both sessions.
Branscombe House and the Richmond Cultural Centre are wheelchair accessible.
Instruction will be in English but translation may be available by request. Please contact
Lou with any questions. RSVP to lou@lousheppard.com.

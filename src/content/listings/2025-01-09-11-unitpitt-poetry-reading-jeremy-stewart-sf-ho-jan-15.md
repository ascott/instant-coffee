UNIT/PITT
2954 W 4th Ave., 'Vancouver'

SF Ho is a porous object. They live on the unceded territories of the xwmkwy"
m, Swxw7mesh, and sl"lwta" peoples. Operating somewhere between words
and whatever words cant be, their work is informed by feminist methodologies, land-
based practices, and grassroots community networks. Ho has presented their art
and writing
both regionally and internationally. Theyve published a book about love
and aliens called George, the Parasite. Theyre cultivating a practice of wary
sociality, never finishing books, and being sort of boring.

Jeremy Stewart is the author of three poetry books, several chapbooks, and a
creative-critical book on Jacques Derrida. His writing has appeared in Minor
Literature[s], Lemon Hound, Open Letter, and elsewhere. A guitarist, singer,
songwriter, and improviser, Stewart has shared stages with artists like Oxbow,
Lightning Bolt, Lori Goldston, and Stanley Zappa. Stewart is the founding Artistic
Director of Casse-Tte: A Festival of Experimental Music; 2025 will be its 8th year.

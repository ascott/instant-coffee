Dear Friends, 

Please join us to celebrate the opening of our newest public installation 
in Lee Square in front of the UBC Bookstore on Tuesday, April 12 from 
4:30-6:30pm.

The installation, entitled They grow without us, is the latest iteration in a 
series of mushroom-based design objects. The project suggests a 
future in which biodegradable architectural components are grown 
rather than manufactured, adding valuable material to local 
ecosystems throughout their lifecycle. Mycelium biocomposites offer 
the prospect that silently growing buildings could one day replace 
conventional carbon-intensive construction methods. The project builds 
upon transdisciplinary research at the intersection of architecture, 
sustainable materials, building technology and mycology. 

The installation has been made possible through generous support 
from the University of British Columbia SEEDS, UBC Campus and 
Community Planning, Peter Wall Institute for Advanced Studies, UBC 
Alma Mater Society, UBC School of Architecture & Landscape 
Architecture, and the Studio for Extensive Aesthetics

They grow without us

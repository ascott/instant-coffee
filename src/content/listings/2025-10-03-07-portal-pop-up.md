Portal Pop-Up is a small storefront window gallery space in Gastown for Vancouver
artists and designers, a highly visible location on pedestrian-friendly West Cordova
St.

We are seeking eye catching art and design easily presented in the Portal window
space.

***** Now accepting submissions for 2026 exhibitions*****

@portalpopupgallery

Please submit! We are considering solo, duo and/or group projects. If you have any
preference please let us know.

Full dimensions of display apparatus: upright window-facing wall: 10 w x 8 h, with a
48x20 interior shelf at sill height on the right side (when looking in from the street).
The vertical right side will suit two-dimensional work ~36W x 50H or smaller, and
the left side will suit ~46"W x 50"H or smaller. Artists are encouraged to come take a
look at 407 W. Cordova to consider how their own artwork could inhabit the space.

Submit 50-100 words of text, your contact info, and up to 5 images of your work.
Please indicate dimensions and medium.

Submit through email: portalpopupgallery@gmail.com (see also contact info
@portalpopupgallery)

Submission deadline: end of day October 31, 2025

Concert by Immanuel Wilkins and Lutosawski Quartet

7:00 - 8:30 p.m.
Grand Luxe Hall, Western Front

To celebrate the opening night of the Vancouver International Jazz Festival, Western
Front is pleased to present a concert by American saxophonist Immanuel Wilkins and
the Polish contemporary classical string ensemble Lutosawski Quartet. This
performance will mark the Canadian debut of a piece composed by Wilkins for the
quartet, commissioned by Jazztopad Festival and the National Forum of Music in
Wrocaw, Poland.

For further information, visit westernfront.ca/events/concert-by-immanuel-wilkins-and-
lutoslawski-quartet

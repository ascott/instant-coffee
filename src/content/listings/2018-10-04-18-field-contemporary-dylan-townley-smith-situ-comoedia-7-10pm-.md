Dylan Townley-Smith
Situ Comoedia
Opening Thursday, October 11th. 7:00-10:00PM
October 12th - November 22nd, 2018
Field Contemporary
17 West Broadway, Vancouver. BC.

Exhibition text by D'Arcy Blake.

Field Contemporary presents a new body of painting, sculpture, and media works by 
Dylan Townley-Smith.

Dylan Townley-Smith is a multidisciplinary artist working predominantly in sculpture, 
web, video, photo, and painting art. Recently he has explored creative writing in the 
form of the novel. His practice is informed by the visual and social culture of western 
society; the relevance of myth as a catalyst for perceptual change; the semiotics of 
advertising; the sanctity (or fetishization) embedded in everyday commodities; the 
uncanny; the psyche; and, the surreal. His work has been acquired into several 
private collections in Vancouver, Los Angeles, New York, and Germany.

Please join us on the evening of Thursday, October 11th for an opening reception. 
The exhibition will continue through November 22nd. 

For more information please contact Info@field-contemporary.com

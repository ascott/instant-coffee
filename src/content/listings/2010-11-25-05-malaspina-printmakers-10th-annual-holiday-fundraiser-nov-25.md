Malaspina Printmakers Members Exhibition
Jingle
10th Annual Holiday Fundraiser
November 25 2010 January 16, 2011

Opening Reception: Thursday November 25, 2010 5 pm 8 pm

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to present its Annual Winter Members
Exhibition jingle.

This dynamic display of original prints is a celebration of the wide
variety of work made by artists that are Members of Malaspina Printmakers.
This salon-style presentation features a diverse range of print mediums
and is constantly evolving as works are rotated into the exhibit.

Comprised of original prints from Malaspina Printmakers membership of over
200 local, national and international print artists, jingle showcases a
wide range of original, hand-pulled prints, in media such as lithography,
intaglio, woodblock, mixed media, monotypes and digital. All works in the
exhibition are ready to hang and can be taken home at time of purchase. In
addition to the exhibition, Malaspina Printmakers has over 1500 unframed
prints available for sale.

Please join us for the Opening Reception on November 25, 2009 between 5 and
8 pm.
Many exhibiting artists will be in attendance at the opening reception.

Christine D'Onofrio, Elizabeth MacKenzie, Hannah Jickling, Helen Reed
Saturday, January 28, 2:00 - 4:00 pm

New Media Gallery, Anvil Centre, 777 Columbia Street, New Westminster

In a response to the CHILDREN exhibition, a group of diverse & accomplished women 
offer fascinating perspectives on childhood psychology, feminism, motherhood & artistic 
practice, and the topsy turvy world of collaborative, childhood creativity. 

Guest speakers Christine D'Onofrio, Elizabeth MacKenzie, Hannah Jickling and Helen 
Reed weave together their own unique perspectives and experiences on the idealized 
and sentimentalized role of mother, the complexities and contradictions of living a 
creative life with children, identity formation and encouraging meaningful creativity and 
play in children. Join us for an open conversation in response to the current New Media 
Gallery exhibition Children, that considers the role of the artist and the maternal in 
creating works about, and in collaboration with, children.

Please include time to visit the CHILDREN exhibition with works by Candice Breitz, 
Kerry Tribe, Christian Jankowski, Lenka Clayton, Chiharu Shiota and Elisa Giardina-
Papa. CHILDREN explores the spectrum of human perception, imagination, 
identification and expectation through the eyes of children. We are presented with 
intelligent, imaginative beings who long for ideas, who question the meaning of life, 
human interaction and beginnings, who imagine and become 'other' and who stretch the 
limits of familial bonds. Six video installations offer a rich trajectory of the human 
condition.

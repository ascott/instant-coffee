Lauren Marsden (Vancouver/Port-of-Spain), will be discussing the
production of The Pit: A Study in Horror and will present a series of
collaborative approaches to directing film and performance.

Lauren Marsden received her BFA in Visual Arts from the University of
Victoria and an MFA in Social Practice from the California College of
the Arts. She presents her work internationally in galleries and film
festivals and has had recent exhibitions and screenings in Port-of-
Spain, Mexico City, San Francisco, Vancouver, Victoria, Chicago, and
Rome. She teaches at the School for Contemporary Arts at Simon
Fraser University, is currently the artist-in-residence at Quest
University, and edits for the San Francisco Arts Quarterly.

Call for Board Directors
Deadline: Sunday, June 9, 2020

About Mountain Standard Time

Mountain Standard Time (M:ST) is a non-profit arts organization dedicated to the
creation, research, and presentation of contemporary performative art practices.
Foregrounding the body, we create artistic and cultural contexts where nuanced
dialogues, ethical reflexivity, and social change are animated through experimental, risk
taking programming.

M:ST was established in 2001 as a collaborative endeavor between several local arts
organizations and to this day, we connect local and global artistic
communities through
our unique partnerships. As a spaceless and flexible organization, run out of our office
at CommunityWise Resource Centre, our activities include artistic research and
production residencies, online and print publications, workshops, lectures, panel
discussions, advocacy, and our biennial festival.

We are seeking new Board Directors

M:ST is seeking Board Directors to serve a two-year volunteer term (renewable) who
will bring dedication, energy, and experience to the organization. Board Directors are
expected to attend 9 meetings a year, each meeting being approximately 1.5 hours and
scheduled on weekday evenings. They are also expected to lead and enthusiastically
volunteer for additional tasks related to outreach, equity work, hiring, etc. Our mission,
vision, and values are listed here and we encourage prospective new board members to
review them before applying.

We highly encourage applications from individuals from BIPOC communities, persons
with
disabilities, persons of minority sexual orientations and gender identities,persons
from poor and working class backgrounds, and others with the skills and knowledge to
engage equity seeking communities.

While M:ST and most of the Board Directors are located in Calgary, Canada we also
encourage national and international expressions of interest.

Board Directors must:

be committed to the work of the organization
be interested in contemporary art
be invested in community building
be committed to organizational equity
support and participate in M:STs activities

Desired skills and experience:
Creative problem solving skills
Strong written and verbal communication skills
Ability to communicate in languages other than English
Experienced in financial oversight; familiar with non-profit accounting and budget
management

Duties:

Board Directors are fully informed on organizational matters and participate in the
Boards deliberations and decisions in the matters of policy, finance,
programming,
personnel and advocacy. Additionally, they:

Determine mission and purpose
Hire, support, and evaluate executive staff
Ensure effective long-term planning
Ensure adequate financial resources and provide proper financial oversight
Build a competent board
Ensure ethical and legal integrity
Enhance the organizations public standing

How to Apply

To apply, send a statement of interest (approx. one page) to M:STs Board President
Kaylee Maciejko at board@mountainstandardtime.org. The statement should address
the unique skills and experience you are bringing to M:STs Board of Directors.

Potential applicants are invited to contact Kaylee in advance of submitting their
applications if they have any questions regarding M:ST and/or board member
requirements and duties.

The deadline for applications is Tuesday, June 9, 2020. Applications will be evaluated by
the current board and staff. Successful applicants will be nominated to the board at
M:STs upcoming Annual General Meeting
on Tuesday, June 16, 2020.

M:ST is committed to accommodating applicants with barriers to the recruitment
procedures outlined above. Please notify us if you require accommodation with respect
to the materials or procedures used at any time during this process. We will work with
you to determine how to meet your needs throughout recruitment and your potential
term as a board member.

Liminal Spaces

August 10th - September 2nd, 2023

Curated by Andrew Booth, Vancouver Art Blog

Opening reception: Thursday, August 10th, 4-7pm

Liminality lies at the confluence of several elements, where the familiar transitions into
the unknown. This exhibition presents works by several of Monte Clarks artists that
explore the psychological, physical, and spiritual dimensions of these transitional
realms.

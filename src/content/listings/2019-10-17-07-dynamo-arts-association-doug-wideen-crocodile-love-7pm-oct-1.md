Dynamo Arts Association is delighted to present recent drawings and paintings by Doug
Wideen alongside collaborative works made with a cast of characters, both real and imagined.

Doug Wideen is an artist working in painting, drawing, video, spoken word poetry, and zine
works . His zine projects include 'Oops Fantasy' with Justin Gradin and Ben Jacques, 'Isn't
that Pip? " with Justin
Gradin, and he has also contributed to 'Waffle Moo -Moo Cow' (with Marc
Bell) Doug regularly hosts drawing nights at Pig Freeze Studios and Dynamo Arts Association.
He has also self published a book of poetry titled 'Unrelated Poetry'. Zine's will be available at
the the exhibition.

A limited Edition 'Crocodile Love' t-shirt will be available on the closing night of the exhibitions
for $20 . This Edition of 26 will go fast!

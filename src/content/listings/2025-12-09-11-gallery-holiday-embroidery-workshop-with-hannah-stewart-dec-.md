Holiday Embroidery Workshop with Hannah Stewart
December 13th, 12 to 3 pm
Bill Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art
639 Hornby Street, Vancouver, BC

Join us at the Gallery for a beginner-friendly embroidery workshop led by Nisgaa artist
Hannah Stewart (they/them). Each participant will receive a small embroidery hoop and fabric
featuring a Northwest Coast"style Candy Cane designed by Hannah. Learn foundational
embroidery stitches on a small fabric patch and create a festive, hangable holiday ornament
to take home.

Hannah Stewart (they/them), also known as Litkw Creative, is a 2-Spirit Nisgaa and Settler
artist from the north coast of BC. A self-proclaimed jack-of-all-trades, Hannah loves exploring
a range of mediums, including painting, carving, printmaking, and textile work. Their practice
draws from the intersections of identity and the deep inspiration they find in the coastal
landscapes they grew up around.

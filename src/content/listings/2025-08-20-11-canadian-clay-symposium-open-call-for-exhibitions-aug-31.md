The Canadian Clay Symposium is accepting submissions of exhibitions that coincide
with the next symposium scheduled for March 21, 2026 at the Shadbolt Centre for the
Arts (Burnaby). This call is open to galleries, museums and alternative spaces in the
Metro Vancouver and Fraser Valley regions that will be showing contemporary or
historical ceramic art, pottery, or other artworks in direct conversation with clay.
Selected exhibitions will be promoted to guest presenters and attendees of the 10th
edition of the Canadian Clay Symposium, titled, Clay: How Hard Can It Be?

The exhibition submission deadline is August 31. If you would like to submit an
exhibition, or if you have access to a venue and would like to express interest in
organizing a ceramic art exhibition, please email exhibition.canadianclay@gmail.com

For more information about the Canadian Clay Symposium, please visit
www.canadianclaysymposium.com

RANGER STATION ART GALLERY 
CALL FOR 2015 EXHIBITION SUBMISSIONS

DEADLINE: Monday, December 1, 2014

Kent Harrison Arts Council is a not for profit organization, nurturing and 
supporting local arts and culture in the region for over 40 years. The arts 
council operates the Ranger Station Art Gallery in Harrison Hot Springs, 
BC and is supported by the BC Arts Council. We are currently seeking 
visual artists for our 2015 exhibition year. The art gallery is located on the 
shore of beautiful Harrison Lake, approx. 100 kms east of Vancouver. 
For more information please view our website: 
www.kentharrisonartscouncil.com

Submission Requirements:
We invite artists (individuals or groups) of all mediums to submit 
proposals for the 2015 Exhibition year. 
Your submission must include:

- A one-page proposal including brief artist statement and gallery show 
description
- A Curriculum Vitae (an artistâ€™s resume) or Biography relating to art 
practice (2 pages max).
- Visual Documentation of up to 10 images of recent work; jpeg format 
only, 300 dpi preferred, no larger than 1024 x 768 pixels. All images 
must be in a .zip file if submitting by email, or on CD if submitting in 
person or by post. Colour photo copies of original work may be 
submitted with clearly labeled with artist name and contact information.
- A list describing all your visual documentation, including title, media, 
dimension of work and year of production. Please specify whether or not 
they are examples of previous work or works to be exhibited.

Please do not send original artworks.
All material must be both PC & Mac compatible.

The Gallery Director will notify selected artists by Dec 15th 2014.

See Sounds Listening Party with Helena Krobath
Nov 26

https://www.eventbrite.com/e/127933487505

The See Sounds Listening Parties provide a look in to the musical and sonic influences
and processes of local artists.
For this version of See Sounds, which will happen over zoom, we invite local sound
artist Helena Krobath.

Helena Krobath is a sound artist and educator exploring ways that information is
created, communicated, and made meaningful. She uses listening, recording, and
composition to consider infrastructures and narratives in places like ports,
neighbourhoods and nature zones. Her sound art has appeared in festivals and radio
specials, and her audio essay on street economies during COVID-19 will be published
in the Journal of Design and Culture in spring 2021. Helena has presented many
workshops on storytelling with audio field recording. She volunteers with
the Vancouver
Tenants Union and is a co-host of the Soundscape Show on Vancouver Co-op Radio.

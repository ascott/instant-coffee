16mm Basics: Introduction to the Arri SRii Camera

Sunday 23 April 2017; 2.00pm"5.00pm

$40 + taxes | General Admission

REGISTER: http://16mm-basics-arri-apr.eventbrite.ca

A 3 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and practice of 
exposing film with an Arri SRii 16mm film camera.

Elements include:
The mechanics and chemistry of exposing film
Understanding lenses
Handling film
Loading and unloading film in light and dark conditions
Shooting in natural light

Film shot in this workshop will be processed at the 16mm Basics: Intro to Hand 
Processing workshop on Sunday 30 Apr. If interested in both components (optional), 
please register for the Hand Processing workshop via the following link:

https://16mm-basics-hand-processing-apr.eventbrite.ca/

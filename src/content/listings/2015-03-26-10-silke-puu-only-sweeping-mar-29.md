Only Sweeping
Vivienne Bessette, Jerry Zaslove, Brendan Reed

Exhibition Dates: April 1 - April 28th 2015
by appointment

Language grows more slippery as one reaches towards its boundaries, euphemism.

Situatedness could be confined from positioning through a distinction
related to time and viscosity. The situatedness of an interpretive context
ingests broader associative seepages by compressing itself more tightly
into the singular conditions of a moment.

Only Sweeping unites three works by three artists. All art works to
modulate our suspension of meanwhile, but here focus has been placed around
citations of identity within time's collapse. Placed within the borders
spaces of 877 East Hastings, these pieces address both their internal
polarities and the framing contradictions of a building itself in transition.

For how long do the gravitational relations between ash and marble remain
accountable as a vacuumed glass hurtles through the atmosphere.

Can the vacuum be made porous and what will this tell us about forgetting?
capital? drywall? florescence?

A temporary present bound in place, hallowed coughers amid the gears of
renovation.

stay darling,

- Silke Puu

Vivienne Bessette
Developed from a 2013 proposal to reprogram the abandoned Main street
Blockbuster Video into a space of discourse and production, Vivienne
Bessette's work may or may not ever materialize. Despite selection, the
projects impossibility became evident while attempting to negotiate with
landlords via skype from China. Flickering between the bureaucratic
organizing of urban license and the anecdotal posturing of premise
conceptualism, the work situates memory and failure into a circuitous
monument to unresolvable action.

Brendan Reed
Brendan Reed was raised in Colonial Williamsburg, Virginia, a stones throw
from the Confederate capital during the U.S. Civil War. He studied at the
York School of the Arts, Bard College, and the Rhode Island School of
Design, before moving to Montreal in 2001. His videos comprise broken
narratives touching themes of everyday bloodshed, horror and exemption.
Bejeweled in a pursuit of extreme beauty, Brendan commutes between Montreal
& Western Virginia.

Sabine Bitter/Helmut Weber: Since 1993, Vienna and Vancouver-based artists
Sabine Bitter and Helmut Weber have worked on projects addressing cities,
architecture, and the politics of representation and of space. Since 2004
members of the cultural collective Urban Subjects US. Their series of
photo- and video-works like Caracas Hecho en Venezuela, Living
Megastructures, and Differentiated Neighborhoods of New Belgrade engage
with specific moments and logics of the global-urban change as they take
shape in neighborhoods, architecture, and everyday life.

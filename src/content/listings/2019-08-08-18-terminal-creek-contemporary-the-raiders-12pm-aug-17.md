The Raiders
August 17 - September 8 Terminal Creek Contemporary 569 Artisan Lane Bowen 
Island, BC
www.tccontemporary.com

Opening: Saturday, August 17, 12-4 p.m.

THE RAIDERS are a revolving cast of artists, writers, and curators periodically hosted 
by Gillian McMillan and Eric Metcalfe for evenings of food, wine, chatting, and painting. 
Gillian produces a series of blank ceramics for each party as canvases for the artists to 
work with for the evening. The results are as varied as the conversations...

Exhibition curated by Kate Bellringer.

Featuring works by: Eric Metcalfe, Gillian McMillan, Eli Bornowsky, Allyson Clay, Colette 
Urban, Monique Fouquet, Paul Mathieu, Rene Van Halm, Katie Lyle, Jacquelyn Ross, 
Marian Penner Bancroft, David MacWilliam, Greg Bellerby, Elizabeth McIntosh, 
Rebecca Brewer, Rick Ross, Lyse Lemieux, Jesse Birch, Jan Wade, Ciara Phillips, 
Marlene Madison, Garry Neill Kennedy, Cathy Busby, Cindy Richmond, Philippe 
Raphanel, Pierre Coupey, Mina Totino, Michelle Normoyle, Tamara Mills, and Kate 
Metten.

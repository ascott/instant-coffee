the des hume initiative is a for-profit organization dedicated to researching utopian
social dynamics. Operating on the principles of The Founder, a musician and
philosopher whose work has touched on theories of gender, love, sexuality, grief and
technology, the DHI has produced publications, performances, installations and
commercially available recordings. Their work has been
featured on BBC Radio 1,
KEXP, the Netflix series Tiny Pretty Things and has accumulated over 3 million streams
on Spotify. Their sophomore record FM.era was released in June 2023.

Zuleyyma is an Afro-Indigenous Queer artist who fearlessly pushes the boundaries of
music and pop culture. With an unwavering passion for collaborating with artists, they
create breathtaking single/EP artwork and orchestrates mesmerizing editorial shoots,
complimentary to the various sounds and music that they are constantly spinning at
venues and performing. Zuleyma is now on a mission to discover and collaborate with
emerging talents who are poised to revolutionize the industry through their iconic
storytelling imagery & sound.

goddaughter is Kait Ramsden, a Canadian multidisciplinary dance artist, sound maker,
and hopeless romantic. Kait timidly approached her first sound experiments in 2019
using the MPC 1000 to make sound collages and pop songs from her bedroom. She
released her first single redmoon
in 2023 under her solo project goddaughter. Her
sound and movement performance take me with you when you (2022) was shown at
Duplex Gallery in Vancouver, BC. Kait has also performed as part of the Active/Passive
Fall Concert Series on Galiano Island. She is a band member of Troll Dolly.

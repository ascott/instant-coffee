Assume Nothing: New Social Practice film screening

MediaNet will be participating in the Art Gallery of Greater Victoria's
exhibition, Assume Nothing, New Social Practice.
We invite filmmakers to produce short film and video works (10 min max.)
introducing interesting people from their lives and communities. We are
looking for both documentary and experimental works. There are many ways to
tell a person's story: drawings, photographs and visual poetry are some of
many examples. The screening is inspired by the work of Harrell Fletcher,
whose website, Some People, has become an opportunity for individuals to
talk about someone important in their lives.
See www.somepeoplepeople.com/

Deadline for submission: March 15, 2009

for information, contact Catlin at info@media-net.bc.ca

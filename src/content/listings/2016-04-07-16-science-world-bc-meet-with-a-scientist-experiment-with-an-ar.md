9 artists. 9 scientists. 
Participate in their experiment!

Join Curiosity Collider and Science World BC on April 9/10 for the special
"Meet a Scientist - Experiment with an Artist" weekend! Participate in
hands-on activities with scientists, and interact with on-site artists
while they experiment with their art in collaboration with the scientists.

Date & Time: April 9 and 10, from 10am to 4pm
Location: TELUS World of Science (1455 Quebec Street, Vancouver)
Admission: General Science World admission is required to attend this
event. Visit our Facebook event page (http://bit.ly/ArtSciExperiment) to
let us know you are coming. Plus, we will be giving out some free Science
World passes on the page!

Join the MacKenzie Art Gallery for the virtual opening of Echoes from the Future:
Speculative Creatures & Post-Human Botanicals. This digital exhibition highlights
artists working across diverse
media to preserve nature, reveal environmental
devastation, and speculate on future life forms. Curator Tina Sauerlaender (peer to
space) presents artists Aviv Benn (ISR/UK), Laura Colmenares Guerra (COL/BE),
Reiner Maria Matysik (DE), Sarah Oh-Mock (DE), Bianca Shonee Arroyo-Kreimes
(CAN), Sabrina Ratt (CAN), Tamiko Thiel (US/DE) in an immersive online exhibition.

Echoes from the Future renders current environmental issues visible in virtual reality,
inviting audiences into an immersive alternative future for hybrid lifeforms.

This immersive experience invites visitors to explore an alternative future inhabited by
hybrid lifeforms, both online and through an in-person Virtual Reality (VR) component
available at the Gallery.

The event will be livestreamed to the MacKenzies Facebook, YouTube, and Twitch
accounts.

Times: 12-1PM CST (Saskatchewan)

Alternate times: 8-9 PM CEST (Germany)

Echoes from the Future is part of a series of digital exhibitions created through the
MacKenzie
Art Gallerys Digital Exhibitions Toolkit and Art Installation Launcher
(DETAIL)"a ongoing resource championing the development of art exhibitions for
digital platforms.

Learn more by visiting the Digital Exhibition page.

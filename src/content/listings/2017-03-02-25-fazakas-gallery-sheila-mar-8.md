SHEILA: Women, Art, and Production
Opening Reception: Wednesday, March 8th, 2017, 6-9 PM
Exhibition: March 8 - 25th, 2017

Fazakas Gallery is pleased to announce an all-female group exhibition to 
coincide with International Women's Day 2017, featuring work by both 
Indigenous and non-Indigenous multidisciplinary contemporary artists. Please 
join us for the opening reception on March 8th, 2017.

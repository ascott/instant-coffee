Some Day Soon
Mike Jones

Opening reception Thursday 09 April 2015, 6-9pm
09 April 2015 through 03 May 2015

Curated by Steven Tong

Michael Jones works in Vancouver. He attended the Alberta College of Art
and Design, obtaining a B.F.A in Media Arts and Digital Technologies from
1999-2003.
During 2004-2006 he attended the Slade School of Fine Art in London, U.K.
and received a M.F.A in Fine Art Media. He has shown across Canada and
internationally.

See Pulpfiction Books (2422 Main St.) for admission during regular business
hours:

The Vancouver Art Gallery presents A Curators View: Ian Thom Selects

Saturday, September 22

Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby Street Vancouver BC

Discover the Vancouver Art Gallerys rich permanent collection through an exhibition of nearly 
ninety highlights including paintings, drawings, photographs, and sculptures in A Curators 
View: Ian Thom Selects, on view September 22, 2018 to March 17, 2019.

Considered one of the most comprehensive resources for visual culture in British Columbia, the 
Gallerys collection comprises of close to 12,000 artworks, hundreds of which were stewarded 
by former Senior Curator"Historical Ian Thom during his thirty-three year tenure. In this special 
exhibition of Pop, abstraction, portraiture and landscape, Thom provides a glimpse into the 
Gallerys process for pursuing works for acquisition. Spanning more than two centuries, the 
exhibition features such artists as Henri Beau, Emily Carr, Robert Davidson, Gathie Falk, Leon 
Golub, David Hockney, Ann Kipling, Beatrice Lennie, David Milne, Paul Peel, George Segal, 
Graham Sutherland, Andy Warhol, John Vanderpant and Zacherie Vincent, and more.

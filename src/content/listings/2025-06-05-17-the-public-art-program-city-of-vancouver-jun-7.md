The Public Art Program at the City of Vancouver is celebrating the second year of its
Launch Pad initiative with a new artist call for recent graduates. Vancouver-based
artists and those from xmky"m (Musqueam), swxw7mesh (Squamish), and
slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) communities who have completed a BFA, MFA, Fine Arts
Diploma, or formal/informal apprenticeship within the last year are invited to submit
existing 2D artworks for consideration. Selected works will be displayed on five city-
owned billboards along the Arbutus Greenway, between Fir St and Burrard St near
6th Avenue"an incredible opportunity to showcase emerging talent in a public
space.

Submissions are open now through Monday, July 7th at 4:00 PM (PST).

This is a great chance for new artists to gain exposure"spread the word!

To learn more about the call and to apply, please go here:

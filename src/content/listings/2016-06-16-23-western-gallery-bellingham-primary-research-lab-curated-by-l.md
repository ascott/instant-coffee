PRIMARY RESEARCH LAB
MINIMALIST LABS AND FIELD RESEARCH 
Albers De Maria Holt Judd Martin Morris Nauman Noguchi Serra di 
Suvero and more

June 26 - Aug 31, 2016
Opening reception Sunday, June 26, 3-5pm
Research Labs every Sunday all summer long

WESTERN GALLERY is pleased to invite you to our special 
summer exhibition, Primary Research Lab, which will open Sunday, 
June 26, 3-5pm and host special events every Sunday all summer 
long.

Primary Research Lab is an exhibition project which incorporates 
talks, tours, research facilities, performances, drawings, films and 
events to frame situations for actively viewing the extraordinary 
examples of Minimalist and Post-Minimalist art in Western 
Washington University's Outdoor Sculpture Collection. This 
nationally-recognized collection was assembled largely through the 
visionary philanthropy of Virginia Wright, who realized the import of 
bringing great examples of major artists work to Washington for 
study and enjoyment.

Taking half of its title from the seminal Minimalist exhibition, Primary 
Structures, this project enlists this term to both reference the 
shared style of these works and a research oriented exhibition 
where viewers will encounter examples of Minimalist and Post-
Minimalist art first hand.

Minimalist art insisted on positioning itself physically in front of the 
viewer. The impact of this art came from artists anticipating the 
physical engagement of the viewer in relation to the artwork, 
formalizing an encounter in space. This physicality was specifically 
important to how their art was experienced by the viewer in relation 
to their own body, to their own experience. Curated by Lee Plested 
and organized to run through the summer months, the exhibition 
provides a platform to interact with these large scale works in the 
Outdoor Sculpture Collection in the open air, including work by 
Scott Burton, Mark De Suvero, Nancy Holt, Robert Morris, Bruce 
Nauman, Richard Serra and more. Parallel to this presentation and 
installed in the Western Gallery the exhibition will present related 
prints by Josef Albers, Jo Baer, Dan Flavin, Ellsworth Kelly, Walter 
di Maria, and Agnes Martin. These works will be installed to 
highlight their physicality, the near sculptural relationship the artists 
establish between the viewer and these two dimensional objects.

The exhibition will include a research center with documents, 
interviews, print material and other ephemera to provide exposure 
to these artists' thoughts, discussions of their project and the 
physical forces that went into their constructions. Weekly tours, 
performances, events, lectures and screenings will be scheduled 
for this space to create a dynamic environment to engage with this 
survey. For more information about these weekly events please 
contact the gallery or visit our website at westerngallery.wwu.edu.

Free parking is available in the C Lots on weekends.

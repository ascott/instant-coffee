Left of PuSh - presented by Plastic Orchid Factory
Left of PuSh is Plastic Orchid Factorys singular festival-within-a-festival returns for a
6th edition, showcasing experiments-in-process by local and national dance makers.
Taking place alongside the 20th PuSh Festival, Left of PuSh is one of the most
anticipated events in Vancouvers contemporary dance calendar.

On January 30, local street dance artist Joshua Jammin Cameron tests For on the
Floor in a double bill with
Ily + Billys GLAMOURING. Then on February 1, catch
Anything Whatsoever by experimental dance artist Katie Ward (Tio'tia:ke /
Mooniyang / Montral).

For on the Floor is an immersive dance/theatre experience that centres the transfer
of memory and experience through the transformative properties of vintage clothing
both inanimate orotherwise.

GLAMOURING. An interrogation of all the intimacy weve never had, a fleeting
memorial of being crushed by the fullness of everythings expression, and a score of
being disconcerted with ones own capacity for sensitivity.

In Anything Whatsoever the scores of movements and the words of the audience
evolve in parallel, intertwining, creating a constantly changing composite " a kind of
dream bath.

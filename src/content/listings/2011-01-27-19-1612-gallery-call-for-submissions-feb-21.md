CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

A FURTIVE CONUNDRUM: PRACTICING FURTIVE

1612 Gallery Vancouver April 7-16 2011
Curatorial Panel Mariane Bourcheix-Laporte & Ryan Mathieson

We are inviting artists to submit works investigating a change in 
social structures of space

through barely perceptible actions. We are looking for works that 
might be qualified as furtive,

that is, they operate on the level of camouflage and demonstrate the 
potential to seep into an

environment. We are conscious of the fact that exhibiting these 
works potentially counteracts

their ‘unframed’ nature. With this call for submissions, we seek 
creative ways of negotiating

this conundrum. *

How might a furtive practice re enforce its original environment from 
the 1612 Gallery space?

Is the trace, the document, the remnant the only means to 
showcase these works? Maybe the

way to have a furtive work enter the gallery is through furtive means.

Please forward submission proposals by Monday February 21st 
2011, 4pm to

furtive.conundrum@gmail.com.

All mediums will be considered.

SUBMISSION GUIDLINES

-A description of work (max. 200 words).

-Specific details relating to installation and dimensions of work.

-An artist statement (max. 150 words) relating the work to furtive 
practice.

-Max. 5 images (jpg format @ 72 dpi, 400-500k) of work,

OR max. 5 images related to ideas about the proposed work,

OR max. 2 minutes time-based media.

DEADLINE FOR SUBMISSIONS: MONDAY FEBRUARY 21st 2011, 
4PM.

Send any questions to furtive.conundrum@gmail.com.

Commercial Street Cafe Hosts a group exhibition by five art students from Emily Carr
University of Art and Design.

The Commercial Street Cafe is delighted to invite the customers to celebrate the vibrant joy of
Vancouver summers, "Midsummer Holiday" that evokes shared summer memories: views of
Moraine Lake, plates of ripe summer fruits, summers spent in Japan, or quiet moments with
family at home - scenes that feel gently juxtaposed against the often grayscale reality of
"Raincouver."

Featuring traditional acrylic and oil paintings, the exhibition temporaily tranforms Commercial
Street Cafe into a radiant space. At its core, "Midsummer Holiday" aims to offer a safe space
from emotional fatigue brought on by persistent rain, heavy workloads and everyday
interpersonal tensions. It is an invitation to slow down, enjoy a light and relaxed atmosphere,
and momentarily step away from the fast-paced, gloomy winter days ahead.

Exhibition Details:

What: Midsummer Holiday
When: Dec 9th to January 30th
Where: Commercial Street Cafe (3599 Commercial St, Vancouver, BC V5N 4E9)

Cost: Free

FLUX Presents new work by Libby Oliver

Dates: February 3 to April 1, 2022
Locations: on the FLUX website, and in our windows at 1524 Pandora Ave.
Times for window gallery: videos will be played in the windows from 5pm to 9pm
Tuesdays to Fridays.

For more information, or to arrange for interviews, please contact Programming
Director Cat Lewis cat@fluxmediagallery.org

PRESS RELEASE: Victoria artist Libby Oliver is expanding her photography practice to
explore moving images in this exciting exhibition of new work.

Collector is a video and photo-sculpture exhibition centred on Richard, a British hunter
and taxidermy collector. This body of work is a study in the historical and conceptual
link between hunting and photography, and the power dynamics of the gaze that
envelopes both practices. Libby takes, captures, and shoots Richard with his
collection in various extravagant staged sets.

There is always power involved with being an artist who communicates and represents
another human in their work. While the starting point of Collector was on how identity is
facilitated by ones possessions, it eventually veered into being more about the
construction of idealized images, myth-making and the relationship between artist and
their muse.

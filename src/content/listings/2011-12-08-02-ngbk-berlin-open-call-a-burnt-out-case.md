NGBK

“A BURNT-OUT CASE?”

AN OPEN CALL AT NGBK BERLIN FOR AN EXHIBITION FROM 9/1–
10/14/2012

For the show “A Burnt-Out Case?” at the NGBK from 09/01 to 
10/14/2012, we invite artists, artists’ groups, activists and interdisciplinary 
projects to cast a view from the future back to the present and create 
complex perspectives on burnout – not only as an individual but above all 
as a social symptom...

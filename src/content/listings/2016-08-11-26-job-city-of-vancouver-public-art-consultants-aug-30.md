Request For Applications: Public Art Consultants, City of Vancouver

The City of Vancouver seeks the services of multiple consultants with
expertise in contemporary art and public art management. The intent is to
select a pool of qualified professionals and teams, with expertise in art
and project management services including public art planning, project and
area plans, community consultation, artist selection, art fabrication,
construction, installation, documentation, promotion and engagement and
other related services for a variety of public art projects over the next
several years.

The City is interested in pre-qualifying multiple proponents. As specific
requirements will vary from project to project, the City wishes to select a
number of proponents that have particular capabilities and expertise to
provide services and then enter into contracts for specific projects as
needed. The number of consultants engaged and the scope of work offered
will vary depending on priorities and project timing. Consultants with
expertise in any of the services above, and especially those with
experience in municipal settings, are invited to respond to this Request
for Applications. Curators and consultants with knowledge of working with
First Nations and Indigenous communities are encouraged to apply.

Closing date and time: 3:00:00 pm (Local Vancouver Time), Tuesday, August
30, 2016

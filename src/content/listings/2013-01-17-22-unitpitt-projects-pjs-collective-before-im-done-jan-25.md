PJS Collective: Before I’m Done

January 25 to March 9, 2013.

Opening Friday, January 25, at 8pm. Public hours noon to 5pm, Wednesday - 
Saturday. Unit/Pitt, 15 E Pender St., Vancouver BC Canada.

PJS Collective is John Walkus-Greene, Skylar Stock, and Paul Lang. This 
project is curated by Cease Wyss.

Before I’m Done is an interactive conversation between three men. Through 
carving and drawing, photography, and video, the PJS Collective documents 
and creates a living art practice in an urban landscape — a type of practice, 
and living, normally unseen by most citizens.

John Walkus-Greene is a local artist who has been living and in the DTES for 
several years. John’s cultural background is Kwakwakwaxw’a, and is a 
traditional carver. John is trying to remain rooted in his culture through his art. 
He creates carvings and 2D artworks.

Skylar Stock is an artist and local business owner who has been friends with 
John for several years. His friendship with John began through dialogues in his 
store in the DTES. They have a unique friendship, and one that has grown into 
a great trust and mutual respect for one another. Skylar will be documenting 
John’s work, through photography.

Paul Lang is a local artist who has been working in and with artists groups for 
25 years. He has been the silent observer of this unique friendship for many 
years. Paul will be compiling a series of video clips that documents John’s 
process and will be contributing to this collaboration through as a â€œwitnessâ€  to 
John and Skylar’s photo conversation.

Before I'm Done is the first in a series of new works commissioned by 
Unit/Pitt Projects. The series, collectively titled What Future, is made 
possible by a grant from the Vancouver Foundation, and support from our 
other funders and donors.

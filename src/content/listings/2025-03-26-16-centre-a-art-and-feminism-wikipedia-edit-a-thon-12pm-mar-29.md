Art and Feminism Wikipedia Edit-a-thon

This March, Centre A has been joining Wikipedians in the Art + Feminism Wikipedia
Edit-a-thon. This year, we are highlighting the role of arts and culture in resistance to
genocide and occupation. Come to Centre A for our final event of the month, the
Wikipedia Edit-a-thon itself! We will be creating and editing Wikipedia articles about
artists, artworks, and cultural movements resisting genocide and occupation.

Please join us in reconsidering representation and community in open-source
knowledge platforms. We will provide help for beginner Wikipedians, reference
materials, and technical support for making your edits. And snacks!

Date: March 29, 2025 at 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM
Location: Centre A (205-268 Keefer Street)

This is a masked drop-in event. KN95s and air purifiers on site. There will be indoor
eating.

Childcare available upon request for in person events. Please email
info@centrea.org if you require childcare minimally one week before the event

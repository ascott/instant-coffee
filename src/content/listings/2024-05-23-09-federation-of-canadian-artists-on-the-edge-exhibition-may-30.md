The Federation of Canadian Artists is announcing the open call for the 2024 On The
Edge (OTE) Exhibition for both painting and sculpture intakes. Paintings may not be
larger than 96 tall or 48 wide inclusive of the framed dimensions, if framed. Works
on a cradled wood panel or canvas must be at least 1 inches deep. Thinner
substrates are eligible if framed. If your artwork is framed you must submit accurate
framed dimensions at the time of submission " they cannot be added later.
Fragile
and sharp sculptures are not permitted. Sculptures may not exceed 80 tall x 36
wide. For heavy sculpture, sculptors must be prepared to install artwork themselves

PERMISSIBLE SCULPTING MEDIA: stone, metal, glass, clay, wood, or ceramic.

EXCLUDED MEDIA: ivory, plaster, wax, reusable modeling compound, found
objects, electronics, holography and other projected media, and fragile modeling
media such as paper-mch and salt dough. Submit through this link:

https://artists.ca/submissions/index

Prizes:
First Place in Painting: $2,000 CA
Second Place in Painting: $1,000 OPUS Art Supplies Gift Card
Third Place in Painting: $1,000 OPUS Art Supplies Gift Card
Best Sculpture: $1,000 OPUS Art Supplies Gift Card

COMMISION RATE:
FCA members: 65% to artist, 35% to Federation
Non-members: 50% to artist, 50% to Federation

Please join us for a public lecture brought to you by the 
Interdisciplinary Forums course at Emily Carr University.
Thursday April 14 @ 3:50 p.m. in the lecture theatre 301NB

Seaton, Text Dunes and Twitter Mazes: Textual Experimentation and 
Social Media 

Seaton is currently teaching a course at Emily Carr on the cultural 
and philosophical effects of social media in our lives, and recently 
taught one on the graphic novel.

In the last ten years, he has taught academic and studio courses at 
various institutions in such diverse areas as queer studies, feminist 
studies, literature, cultural studies, 

sculpture, and art history. Seaton has a PhD in literature as well as 
a BFA in sculpture from Emily Carr, and his recent art practice has 
been in video installation and activist 

illustration. Recent academic conference papers have been on 
transgender and queer studies topics. He also runs a small 
business as a carpenter and gardener.

Everyone is welcome!

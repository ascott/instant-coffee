About the Museum of Vancouver

The Museum of Vancouver connects Vancouverites to each other and connects
Vancouver to the world. The museum is a gathering place that encourages
social engagement and inspires conversation about the future. MOV
exhibitions and collections invite exploration of contemporary issues and
stories from the past. MOV activities ignite a passion for Vancouver and
its people. The museum, an enthusiastic advocate for the city, is an
independent non-profit organization that depends on support from the
community. The Museum of Vancouver is located in Vancouver at 1100 Chestnut
Street (in Vanier Park).

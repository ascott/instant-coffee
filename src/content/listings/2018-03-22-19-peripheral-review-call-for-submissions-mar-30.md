Peripheral Review is a website and publication based out of Toronto
dedicated to documentation and critical reviews of Vancouver &
Toronto-area art exhibitions, performances (art, music, dance, theatre)
and events. We are currently looking for writers and artists to review
exhibitions and events in both cities to be published on our website.
Accepted and published works will also be published in our annual print
publication, which is exhibited and distributed by stores and at art book
fairs nationwide.

We are looking for:

Critical reviews of Vancouver or Toronto-area art exhibitions,
performances (art, music, dance, theatre) and events (650-3000 words
max.) Must include title of the show, venue name and full names of
artists.

Artistic responses to art exhibitions and performances:
poems/creative writing/recorded descriptions/soundscapes/other:
propose it to us!

Short reviews/descriptions of exhibitions/performances/events (200-
350 words max.) Must include title of the show, venue name and full
names of artists.

Any submission MUST include an image of the
exhibition/performance/event for reference for the published piece, with
photo credit (Artist Name, title of work/exhibition title, year.
Photo/Image credit: name.)
JPEG format, if possible submit high and low quality versions
if no photo attached please insert link for appropriate source/website,
etc.

Also include your preferred social media handles, link to website, short
bio (150 words max), and if you have previously submitted
article/response anywhere else.

Currently, as we are grassroots and unfunded, we can offer no
monetary compensation for submission. If you require tickets to review
an event we can supply complimentary passes.

DEADLINE: All submissions should be sent to:
peripheralreview@gmail.com by midnight EST on Friday March 30,
2018.

For more information visit: www.peripheralreview.com

Subscribe to our email list here: http://eepurl.com/daokq1 

With all submissions please format according to the submission
guidelines. For general inquires please note your query in the subject
line.

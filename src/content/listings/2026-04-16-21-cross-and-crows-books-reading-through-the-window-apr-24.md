Artist Q&A, Poetry Readings, and Zine-making about weird oreos! Opening event of Reading
Through the Window by Joanna Liang (exhibition open April 24-May 24)
Opening event: April 24, 7pm-9pm

Location: Cross and Crows Books; 2836 Commercial Drive

Free entry!

More info:

https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/reading-through-the-window-artist-qa-poetry-readings-and-zine-
making-tickets-1986495409244
The "Window" refers to Joanna's unique way of designing her images and text around the
windows she cuts out from her pages allows her to take advantage of a books sequentiality
to form recontextualizing repetitions with a single page turn. She will be doing readings of her
window books at the opening event on April 24th, along with an artist Q&A and an exclusive
look into the process behind her newest work-in-progress artists book, In another world / an
orchid blooms!

Bookending the event will be zine-making about oreos, inspired by one of Joanna's one-page
zines where she rates different weird oreo flavours. Come have a taste and create a zine
expressing your love-- or distaste-- of some weird oreo flavours!

Call For Submissions - 2012 Spring Artist Colonies/Residencies
Deadline: December 15, 2011

Located on 178 acres of farm and forest overlooking the Bay of Fundy, 
The
Ross Creek Centre for the Arts is the ideal artist's retreat. Our
residencies provide tranquil space and time for artists to develop their
projects and ideas. Participants receive 24-hr access to large brightly lit
studios, accommodations in our farmhouse, and fresh meals prepared 
by our
chef.

We welcome submissions from emerging and professional artists of all
disciplines, including but not limited to individual or collaborative visual
artists, curators, dancers, writers, actors, musicians and architects. In
the submission proposal, please make reference to how your project 
might fit
within the context of the Centre and our rural community. The Centre is 
a
registered not-for-profit organization dedicated to encouraging and
promoting the arts through education and arts development. Selection 
for our
residencies is a juried and competitive process.

Submissions for our spring season (May 1 - June 30, 2012) should be
postmarked on or before Dec. 15th, 2011. PLEASE NOTE: We 
encourage gallery
artists to also be colony artists. If you are interested in being 
considered
for both, please ensure you fill out both the gallery application form and
residency package.

For more information and to apply:
[http://www.artscentre.ca/artists/colonyandresidency.html](http://www.artscentre.ca/artists/colonyandresidency.html)

Mail your submissions to:
Colony Director

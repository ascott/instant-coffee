Glory Days with Jacqueline Teh & Kristin Fung

We are excited to host Juno Award-winning songwriter Jacqueline Teh from Toronto for a
warm and intimate summer evening of sultry jazz and R&B. Featuring all original music
in a beautiful setting, join a small audience for some captivating melodies and luscious
harmonies. Jacq will be joined by special guest artist, Kristin Fung, as the two will be
celebrating their collaborative song release, "Glory Days." The band will also feature
JeanSe Le Doujet on bass and Daniel Ruiz on percussion.

Doors open at 7:00 PM. Show starts at 7:30 PM sharp. Light refreshments will
offered,
but everyone is encouraged to bring something to share.

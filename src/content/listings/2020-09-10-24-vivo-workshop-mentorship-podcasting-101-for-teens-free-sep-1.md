Podcasting 101 for Teens

More info & sign up:
https://www.vivomediaarts.com/podcasting101

Apply by Sept 18, 2020.

This FREE mentorship is designed to inspire teens 15 to 18 years old. Tell your
own story about living through
COVID-19 by creating an original podcast
episode, with the help of podcaster Paige Smith.

4 sessions long, taking place on Saturdays from 1pm to 4pm:
Oct 10, Oct 17, Oct 24, and Nov 7

Learn how to record, edit, and compose an audio documentary of your own
creation that expresses what it's like to live during COVID-19.

Work individually or with a partner to create an
audio story between 5 and 15
minutes.How has the pandemic affected your life? How has your school, work,
friendships, or activities changed? How are you staying connected with others
during this time? These are some examples of questions that might guide your
production.

A variety of podcasting techniques will be considered. Will your podcast include
interviews? Journalistic investigation? Poetry? Environmental recordings? You'll
be encouraged to experiment.

Final audio documentaries will be shared with the public through VIVO's website.
Know a friend who might be interested in this project? Tell them to apply
too!

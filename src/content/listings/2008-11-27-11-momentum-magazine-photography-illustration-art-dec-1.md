Call for Submissions:

Photographers and visual artists for work to be reproduced in the Momentum
Magazine (photographs, illustrations, portraits etc) which reflect the life
and times of people who ride bikes to get around.

Momentum publishes a Vancuver-specific section and we are seeking artistic
reflections of Vancouver as a cycling city.

Momentum is a bi-monthly print magazine for self-propelled people.
Based in Vancouver BC, we are distributed in 14 North American cities.
We are bike lovers interested in helping North American bike culture evolve.

Accepting applications until March 29 at 5pm

Deer Lake Artist Residencies are located on the ancestral and unceded territories of
the hn"q"min"m" and Skwxw7mesh speaking peoples at Deer Lake Park. From
August 4 to September 12, 2026, Burnabys cultural hub, Shadbolt Centre for the
Arts, will host a cohort of potters and ceramic artists to work independently on
projects in the ceramics studios. Artists can apply for just studio space, or studio
space paired with lodging in one of the residency houses on Deer Lake.

The Ceramic Arts Residency provides access to multiple facilities on-site: the Steam
Plant is a small cottage studio
located next to the Kiln Station in Deer Lake Park,
and is suitable for 1-2 artists working collaboratively or independently; the Potter
Studio in Mathers House can accommodate 4-5 artists each with their own work
station; and the Place of Dust glaze lab can accommodate one artist focused on
chemistry and testing. All studio spaces include access to pottery wheels, tables,
shelving, and access to the Kiln Shed and glazes. Mathers House has a slab roller,
extruder, decal printer, and sand-blaster. The residency includes technician support,
a clay stipend, and some bisque and glaze firings. Additional gas and electric kiln
firings are available for rent. Artists are also offered a spot in one specialty firing
(soda, wood, raku or sawdust) in Fall 2026 after the residency has ended, at the
expense of the artist. Ceramics residents are expected to engage with the public
during their residency, which may include giving an artist talk and participating in an
Open House.

For more
information on the 2026 Ceramic Arts Residency, please contact the staff
below. Visit the Deer Lake Residency website for application details, lodging options,
and the online application: www.burnaby.ca/air

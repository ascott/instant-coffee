Celebrate the revitalization of the DLG

Commemorate the official debut of Paul Wongs Art House Swirl, and toast the energized,
new chapter of the Burnaby Arts Council with Mayor Hurley. Screen Paul Wongs first
video installation, Earthworks in Harmony, reprised for its 50th anniversary. Want to
further support our revitalization? Bid on Jay Cabalus 100% hand-cut collage, Groundhog
Day, or purchase one of 25 affordable, framed artist editions by the Burnaby-raised artist.
Through the Surface will be on exhibition and the DLG will be unveiling the work of
muralist Arty Guava as our second
parkside mural program installation. Free to attend. All
are welcome.

Just Beyond Hope, Screening & Artist Talk by Pia Massie
Friday, Aug 5, 8pm, W2 Media Cafe, 111 W Hastings, Free.

Surge 2nd Annual Festival of Urban Digital Culture presents an artist talk
& immersive 3-channel video installation by Pia Massie. Optional Japanese
long table dinner at 7pm by donation. 

Using personal archival photos, letters and newspapers, Just Beyond Hope
creates a dialogue between White and Japanese, Canadian and American,
first, second and third generation women. Each person offers a unique
perspective. As these women tell their stories from different points of
view, a fractured jigsaw puzzle of the time period builds memory fragment
by memory fragment.
A presentation of W2’s Vancouver 125 Redress Series and W2's Soul Gardens
project, with support from the City of Vancouver's 125th Anniversary Grant
Program and the participation of the Government of Canada.

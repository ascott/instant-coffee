Museum Of Jewish Montreal Call for Exhibition Proposals: 2018/19 Programming 
**Deadline Extended: Sunday January 28 at midnight (Montreal time/Eastern Time 
Zone). 

The Museum of Jewish Montreal invites artists and collectives (maximum 8 people) 
to submit their proposed exhibition projects for the Museums 2018-2019 exhibition 
series Movement & Migrations. 

Founded in 2010, the Museum of Jewish Montreal (MJM) is a not-for-profit 
organization that creates, explores and shares Montreals Jewish experience and 
culture. Our activities include tours of historic Jewish neighbourhoods and buildings, 
exhibitions, online exhibits, oral history collection, lectures, workshops, concerts, film 
screenings and food programming. 

About the theme: Movement & Migrations We invite artists to submit an exhibition 
project proposal that deals with the themes of Movement & Migrations. Confronting 
local and global mobilities --whether physical, spiritual, ideological, or emotional -- 
we encourage artists to interpret these themes as creatively as they wish. Works that 
bear a strong demonstrable connection to the Museums mandate and Montreals 
Jewish experiences are of particular interest, though not always necessary.

For complete guidelines: https://tinyurl.com/MJMExhibitionProposal

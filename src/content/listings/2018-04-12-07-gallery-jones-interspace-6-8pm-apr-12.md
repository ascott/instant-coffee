INTERSPACE
Featuring Fei Disbrow & Birthe Piontek

April 12th - May 19th, 2018
Opening Reception | April 12 | 6 - 8 PM

Gallery Jones
258 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Gallery Jones is pleased to present Interspace, an exhibition of works by Birthe 
Piontek and Fei Disbrow. While the two artists explore different subjects, their tactile 
methods of cutting, collage, and assembly produce interesting intersections between 
their works. Where essential information is hidden, omitted, or replaced, a curiosity 
in the "interspace" is invoked. Interspace is a selected exhibition of the Capture 
Photography Festival.

As we all look to innovate ideas on how to create systematic changes in our board
models, where do lawyers fit in? Everybody always say that you have to have a lawyer
on your Board of Directors. While its true that lawyers bring a breadth of skills to a board
table, and love to give back to their areas of interest, what does this really mean and is it
really a good piece of advice? On the other hand, lawyers may often offer general
counsel, or be expected to provide legal advice on behalf of the society while acting as a
board member simultaneously. What challenges and opportunities does this dual position
offer?
For thisevent as part of the National Networks Virtual Forum, well be joined by Jeanne
LeSage (LeSage Arts Management & Research Lead with Mass Culture on Future of Arts
Work Project) as moderator to discuss with panelists (TBA) the role of the lawyers on
boards, examining what works, and what doesnt.

For the month of November, Sage Paul and Erika Iserhoff of Setsun Indigenous 
Fashion Incubator will inhabit the Instagram space of the Western Front.

Setsun Indigenous Fashion Incubator is a collective of Indigenous Women artists 
who foster the creation and exhibition of new works by Indigenous artists working in 
traditional and contemporary fashion, textiles and wearable art. Setsun (set-soon-
eh) means grandmother in the Dene language, a term that is used to acknowledge 
intergenerational crossovers, blood memory and oral histories expressed through 
fashion and the arts. The group forms partnerships with industry, galleries and allies 
to promote Indigenous fashion and material-based art across diverse cultures and 
sectors. 
As an online social media platform for image exchange, Instagram is fuelled by a 
desire for connection and the immediacy of the digital image. Our second virtual 
residency adds a layer of public dissemination to our support of ReMatriate, an 
image-based Collective treating artmaking as information that can be spread and 
shared, replicated and duplicated, copied and forwarded, recorded and discarded. 
Their social media campaigns advocate for the sovereign, ethical representation of 
Indigenous women in media.

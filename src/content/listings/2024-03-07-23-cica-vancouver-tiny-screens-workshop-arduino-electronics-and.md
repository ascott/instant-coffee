We are excited to announce our upcoming program: Tiny Screens Workshop: Arduino,
Electronics, and Coding Basics, that will be led by Ksawery Komputery.

No coding experience will be necessary!

The workshop will be a hands-on coding session facilitated by the artist, who will be
traveling to Vancouver for the preparation of his first-ever solo exhibition: RRGGBB. As
an artist who works in the realm of the PHYgital (physical and digital), interactive art
is a key component in his practice. As such, Enter (2023) and Symphony in Acid
(2022)"a collaborative music video
project with Max Cooper"will serve as inspirational
pieces to participants in the workshop, who will be led by Ksawery Komputery to code
their own dynamic interactive artworks.

Date: March 10, 2024

Time: 2 " 6 PM

Date: April. 30th

Time: doors 7:30, films 8pm

Location: Bestway Studio (21 E. Pender)

Tickets: By donation, cash bar

Event info:

This months CLAM will be dedicated to the short films of the late experimental film
maker David Rimmer. An innovative figure in early structuralist/materialist cinema,
Rimmers playful, poetic, and meticulous work has had a
significant impact both in
his home city of Vancouver and abroad. This month's programming will be a sort of
little-sister follow up to the Cinematheques mid-month National Canadian Film Day
feature on Rimmer, happening on April 17th. We will be playing different deep cut
shorts of Rimmers from our personal archive as a way of continuing to celebrate the
works of this local filmmaker.

Along with the screening, we are also pleased to be collaborating again with Sol
Hashemi (@sol.hashemi) who will be serving a selection of his fresh beer works for
folks to enjoy!

About CLAM:

CLAM (cinema, literature, art, and media) is a monthly (or otherwise) event which
blends short films, archival performance documentation, live performances, and
experimental collaborations with local artists. It is collectively run by Yasmine
Whaley-Kalaora, Joshua Silickas, and Elisha Burrows.

Accessibility:

Bestway is located on the second floor and is only accessible by stairs.

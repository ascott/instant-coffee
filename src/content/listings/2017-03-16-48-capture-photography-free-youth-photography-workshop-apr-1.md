Sat, Apr 1 & 8, 10 am-2 pm
From the Darkroom to the Internet: Alternative Ways of Making and Sharing 
Photographs
Capture Photography Festival Office, 305 Cambie St, Vancouver
Free, but registration req. by March 24 (Space limited)
Email: RSVP@CAPTUREPHOTOFEST.COM 

Artist Kate Henderson leads this two-day workshop for high school-aged youth. In 
session 1, participants will explore early forms of photography. Session 2 jumps 
ahead in time to consider how photography is used by artists today, including digital 
techniques. Prints made in this session can be used in the Apr 23 "Let's Photo Zine" 
workshop. (Venue not wheelchair accessible)

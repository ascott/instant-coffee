221A Artist Run Centre is pleased to present Pact 20: Recess, a
one-night non-juried exhibition of work by artists and designers. Work
is submitted one day prior to the event then installed the day of. The
event aims to support the production and exhibition of collaborative
and transient practices as well as nurture a participatory learning
environment for artists, designers, curators, critics, volunteers, and
the public.

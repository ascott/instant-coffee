The Beaty Biodiversity Museum is proud to present an exhibition of Mtis artist, Daphne
Boyers work titled: Li Bootee Mishiwayitay " The Beauty of It All. The exhibition features
new artworks that explore the ecosystems that have sustained Indigenous Peoples and all
prairie life since time immemorial.

Several works celebrate the historic Mtis communities of Lac La Biche and Beaver Hills in
Alberta, and the exhibition also includes an eight-metre finger-woven sash that traces the
vast migratory watershed of the Mtis homeland.

DanceHouse presents REBO(U)ND

Projected nightly from April 29 to May 8, 2021

In English Bay on an East-facing tower building at Denman and Davie St.

DanceHouse presents the Vancouver premiere of Montreal choreographer Caroline
Laurin-Beaucages multimedia work REBO(U)ND, projected nightly from April 29 to May
8, 2021 in English Bay, on an East-facing tower building at Denman and Davie St. In a
year of creative pivots, this latest innovation will see DanceHouse moving art outdoors
and broadcasting the striking architectural
video projection on a loop, commencing 30
minutes past sunset until 10:30pm nightly. Launching in celebration of International
Dance Day (April 29), the free outdoor public presentation offers passers-by an
opportunity to engage with the larger-than-life artform in a manner suitable to the COVID-
19 era. Further information at: dancehouse.ca

EXHIBITION | B1 Gallery | UPCOMING EXHIBITION + OPENING PARTY
MEET ME AT THE PLAYGROUND is a solo exhibition by Ukrainian visual artist
Bravebirdie (Olena Tolstokora, b.1997), running from 9-28 August at Vancouver's B1
Gallery " part of the Beaumont Studios. All B1 shows are free to attend, and everyone is
invited to the exhibition's opening party on Friday 9 July, 7-11pm.

In this exhibition, the innocence of "then" confronts the stark reality of "now." MEET ME
AT THE PLAYGROUND considers time, mortality, and the essence of adulthood, delving
into transformative events which radically shift the experience of growing up. Here,
Bravebirdie invites us to face the versions of ourselves that no longer exist, to reflect on
personal experiences of change and loss, and to foster a collective recognition of our
shared humanity.

At our opening party on 9 July, everyone is welcome to join us for an inspiring evening of
art, music, conversation, and drinks.

Ian Johnston is an internationally exhibited architect turned sculptor
based in Nelson, BC. He studied architecture at Algonquin College, and
Carleton University in Ottawa and with the University of Toronto. Prior to
opening his Nelson studio in 1996, he spent five years working at the
Bauhaus Academy in Eastern Germany. At the Bauhaus, together with two
architects, he developed and facilitated a series of semester-long
interdisciplinary workshops around themes of urban renewal and public
intervention in a tumultuous time of cultural transformation.

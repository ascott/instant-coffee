The Visionary Art & Life of Alex Grey & Allyson Grey
Friday April 28, 8pm
Vogue Theatre, 918 Granville St. Vancouver BC

World renowned visionary artists Alex Grey and Allyson Grey make their debut 
appearance in Vancouver. 

Artists Alex Grey and Allyson Grey claim three bodies of work between them: 
Alexs art, Allysons art and their shared social sculpture, COSM Chapel of 
Sacred Mirrors. Soul and studio-mates for over forty years, Alex & Allyson will 
share their blended journey of painting, performance art and their creation of 
sacred space at the forefront of a Visionary Art Movement. 

Join them for a special evening at the The Vogue Theatre as they present an 
illustrated discussion on their personal creative paths along with music from 
special guest Phaeleh and visuals by Onbeyond Metamedia.

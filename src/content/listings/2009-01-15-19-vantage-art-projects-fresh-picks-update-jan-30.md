Vantage Art Projects is a new Vancouver based virtual arts communication
service with several project streams including Fresh Picks, Vantage Vantage
Art Press and Vantage Exhibitions. We launched in Toronto in October 2008
with a Fresh Picks release by Angela Grossmann.

Fresh Picks is Vantage Art Projects' series of affordable limited edition
fine art prints. The series allows art lovers to own work that is current
and representative of the contemporary artscape. It is an unprecedented way
for artists to share their work with collectors and art lovers across the
world. Released twice a month, the artist and work is first announced to
our mailing list and is sold exclusively through the Vantage Art Projects
website.

New Release: "Manifestation" by Montreal multi-media artist, Yechel Gagnon.
See http://vantageartprojects.com/Fresh_Picks.html for all releases to date.

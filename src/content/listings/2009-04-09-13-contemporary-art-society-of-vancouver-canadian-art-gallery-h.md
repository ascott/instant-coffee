The Contemporary Art Society of Vancouver is proud to partner with the Canadian Art 
Foundation to present the Canadian Art Gallery Hop Vancouver. 

We hope you will join us on April 18th for a day of art, conversation and film!

Begin your day with a special talk by Sarah Thornton, author of Seven Days in the Art World. 

The New York Times calls it "A smart and savvy guide... written with the amused skepticism of a 
well-informed outsider."

In the afternoon, hop to FREE gallery talks around town, where experts will offer their views on 
contemporary art and artists.

Join us in the evening for a special screening of Herb and Dorothy, Megumi Sasaki's award-
winning documentary film about a former postal worker and librarian who amassed one of the 
most important contemporary art collections in history. The Washington Post calls the film "A Love 
Affair with Art."

Following the film, join us for a cocktail reception with fellow art lovers!

For more information and to buy your tickets now please visit www.casv.ca

VIVO Media Arts
Novel Narratives
Video Out Screening Call for Submissions
Deadline: February 18, 2025

When telling a story, there are only seven basic plots. Or maybe three. Or maybe
five. It depends on who you ask. But you need conflict. And you need a protagonist.
Or maybe you
dont. Maybe you dont need any characters at all?

Video Outs latest call-for-submissions, Novel Narratives, is looking for works which
tell stories through a fresh lens. Whether youre examining the art of narrative, or
telling a classic story in new ways, we are searching for works which embrace the
full possibilities of the form. All genres and lengths accepted. Documentary/non-fic
works welcomed.

To submit, please submit a weblink of the work (WeTransfers not accepted), a short
synopsis, and an artist bio to the Google Form. Digital (including digitized film)
submissions only, maximum two submissions per artist.

For those requiring alternative formats, video or phone submissions are also
accepted for accessibility purposes. For any questions or accessibility requests,
please contact distribution@vivomediaarts.com.

Video Out accepts works for screen, installation, and web-based contexts. A
selection of works will be presented in a program in Spring 2025. Video Out is
committed to
compensating artists with a screening fee based on the IMAA
(Independent Media Arts Alliance) suggested fee schedule.

Submission Form: https://forms.gle/b2ibS9PJucYC3VsS8

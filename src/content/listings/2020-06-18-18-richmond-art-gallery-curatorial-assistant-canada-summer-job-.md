RICHMOND ART GALLERY
Canada Summer Job Position
Curatorial Assistant

$18/ hour 240 hour contract

(Part-time work July - November, 2020)

The Curatorial Assistant position is an engaging entry-level position within the
programming department of a contemporary art gallery. The
Assistant will work
with the Curator and other Gallery staff providing support in the following areas:
the Gallerys Permanent Collection, social media, and exhibition and
public programming. Ability to communicate in Mandarin or Cantonese is
considered an asset for this position.

Key work activities:

Assisting in maintaining and updating Richmond Art Gallerys collection database
and hardcopy records including accession registers, donor files, artwork loans.

Providing assistance for upcoming 2020 - 2021 exhibitions -- including research
and promotion.

Providing administrative support for exhibition programming including completion
of condition reports; organizing and maintaining artist images and files, and
publications database.

Providing administrative support in updating information on website and
generating social media content -- Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, WeChat.

Assisting in the production of didactic materials for exhibitions, including the
translation of text into
Mandarin and Cantonese.

Providing support in daily gallery operations.

EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE
Currently enrolled in an undergraduate or post-graduate program
focused on museum studies, art history, or studio arts. Experience working in a
gallery setting is an asset.

KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES

Mandarin and/or Cantonese language skills.

Interest in contemporary art.

Interest in curatorial practice.

Interest or experience in public programming or facilitation.

Knowledge of the Curatorial profession, institutions and sites in Canada.

Skill and ability to use a related database program management.

Ability to work both independently and interdependently while exercising sound
judgement and initiative.

Ability to establish and maintain effective relationships with both internal staff and
external clients/customers.

Ability to courteously and effectively deal with the public in providing information
and assistance regarding exhibitions and collections.

Ability to follow
direction and meet established deadlines.

Ability to successfully pass a Criminal Record Check.

For those interested please submit a CV and Cover Letter to
gallery@richmond.ca by
June 24th 5pm.

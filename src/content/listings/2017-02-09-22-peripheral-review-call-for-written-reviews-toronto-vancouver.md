Peripheral Review is a website based out of Vancouver and Toronto dedicated to 
documentation and critical reviews of Vancouver & Toronto-area art exhibitions, 
performances (art, music, dance, theatre) and events. We are currently looking for 
writers and artists to review exhibitions and events in both cities to be published on our 
website. 

We are looking for:

Critical reviews of Vancouver or Toronto-area art exhibitions, performances (art, music, 
dance, theatre) and events (650-3000 words max.) Must include title of the show, venue 
name and full names of artists. 

Artistic responses to art exhibitions and performances 
poems/creative writing
drawings/sketches/collages/creative photography/digital media (GIFF, collage, etc.)
recorded descriptions/soundscapes
other: propose it to us!

Short reviews/descriptions of exhibitions/performances/events (200-350 words max.) 
Must include title of the show, venue name and full names of artists. 

Any submission MUST include an image of the exhibition/performance/event for 
reference for the published piece, with photo credit (Artist Name, title of work/exhibition 
title, year. Photo/Image credit: name.) 
JPEG format, if possible submit high and low quality versions
if no photo attached please insert link for appropriate source/website, etc. 

Also include your preferred social media handles, link to website, short bio (150 words 
max), and if you have previously submitted article/response anywhere else

Currently, as we are grassroots and unfounded, we can offer no monetary compensation 
for submission. If you require tickets to review an event we can supply complimentary 
passes. 

All submissions should be sent to: peripheralreview@gmail.com by midnight on 
February 20, 2017. With all submissions please format according to the submission 
guidelines. For general inquires please note your query in the subject line.

For more information visit:

http://www.peripheralreview.com

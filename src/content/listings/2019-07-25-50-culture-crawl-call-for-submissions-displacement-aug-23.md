Displacement: Eastside Culture Crawl Special Exhibit
SUBMISSION DEADLINE: Friday, August 23, 2019

As part of ECCSs programming, we are mounting a multi-venue, salon-style curated exhibition 
titled Displacement. Our industrial lands are under enormous pressure from development. In 
the past couple of years, the Eastside Culture Crawl Society (ECCS) became aware of 
numerous artist production spaces facing doubling of rents and/or eviction. We are in a crisis as 
a community and are looking at various ways to address this pressing issue.

Open to all visual artists from Metro Vancouver who have been evicted, are currently being 
evicted, or have found ways to survive displacement. The submitting artist does not have to be 
registered with the Crawl

For full details, criteria, selection process and submission instructions please view the Call For 
Artists and visit the online Submission Form:

EVENT: ARTIST TALK: YANG FUDONG

DATE: Saturday, May 26

TIME: 3pm

LOCATION: Vancouver Art Gallery, 750 Hornby Street

Information:

Shanghai-based artist Yang Fudong speaks about the creation and 
inspiration for Fifth Night, his newest 7-channel video installation.
Free with gallery admission.

About the exhibition:
YANG FUDONG
Fifth Night
TD NEXT: A Series of Artist Projects from the Pacific Rim
May 12 to September 3, 2012

Fifth Night is a recent multi-channel video installation by Chinese artist 
Yang Fudong. One of the most important and influential artists to 
emerge in China since the late 1990s, Yang Fudong produces 
sophisticated film and video installations that engage the cinematic 
traditions of both Hollywood and experimental film while referencing 
the changing cultural conditions of contemporary China.

Organized by the Vancouver Art Gallery and curated by Daina Augaitis, 
chief curator/ associate director. In conjunction with Yellow Signal: New 
Media in China, a multi-venue presentation of contemporary Chinese 
artists initiated by Centre A with Shengtian Zheng.

Yang Fudong: Fifth Night is the thirteenth installment in TD NEXT: A 
Series of Artist Projects from the Pacific Rim, presented by TD Bank 
Group.

Support for research and public programs by Xi'an Art Museum, 
Shaanxi, China.

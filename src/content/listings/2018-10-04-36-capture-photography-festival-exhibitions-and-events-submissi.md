Call for Exhibitions and Events Submissions to Capture Photography Festival

Deadline to Apply: Oct 21, 2018

APPLY HERE: [http://capturephotofest.com/submissions/](http://capturephotofest.com/submissions/)

Capture Photography Festival is devoted to exhibiting challenging and thought-
provoking photography from local and international artists. Launched in 2013, the 
annual not-for-profit Festival strives to nurture emerging talent, engage community, 
and spark public dialogue about photography as an art form and a vessel for 
communication.

Each April, photography is presented at dozens of participating galleries and 
community spaces throughout Metro Vancouver as part of the Festivals Selected 
Exhibition Program, alongside an extensive Public Art Program and an Events 
Program that spans tours, films, artist talks, and community events.

ABOUT THE CALL
We are excited to announce that submissions to the April 2019 edition of Capture 
are now open. The call is open to all exhibitions of photography and lens-based 
works focusing on any subject matter. Additionally, we welcome proposals for 
innovative events and outdoor exhibitions"think beyond the boundaries! We cant 
wait to see what photography and lens-based exhibitions and events youve been 
cooking up.

All submissions to Captures Exhibition Program are subject to evaluation by a jury 
made up of arts professionals and artists. The jury determines which exhibitions to 
include in that years Selected Exhibition Program, based on a set of evaluative 
criteria.

HOW TO APPLY
Our online submissions portal (Submittable) is open for application to the Selected 
Exhibitions Program and the Events Program. Submissions to the Exhibition 
Program are subject to a $15 fee, associated with the costs of the submissions 
system (Submittable). This fee includes an annual membership ($10) to Capture 
Photography Festival Society. 

Submissions will close October 21.

For further questions and information, including the benefits of being a participant 
with Capture, please visit our submissions FAQ:
[http://capturephotofest.com/faq/](http://capturephotofest.com/faq/)

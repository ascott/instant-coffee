DAVID WISDOM TALK BECOMES A SLIDE SHOW FESTIVAL. 

From 1969 to 1979, David Wisdom and his friends got together once a year to look at each 
other’s latest slides. Over time it became a more organized event, with invitations sent out, and
people doing slick presentations, often set to music. 

David Wisdom will present a slide show this Friday June 5, 7PM at SFU Vancouver, room 1600 at 
515 W. Hastings Street. Followed by an Opening Reception in the Teck Gallery Space.

His June 12 slide show has been expanded to re-create one of the legendary 1970s’ slide 
evenings: now in the Fletcher Challenge Theatre, (Room 1900) at SFU Vancouver at 7PM. This 
event is free and takes the place of the June 12 talk by David Wisdom (on his own) previously 
announced. 

On June 12 David Wisdom will be joined by:
Rodney Graham showing some of his favourite album covers, images seen only once before in a 
recent presentation at he Louvre.

The team of Bernie Lyon (illustrator/artist) and Lee Bacchus (writer/photographer) showing 
vintage views of Vancouver.

Animator Marv Newland, whose work was recently seen at The VAG in the show Krazy!, showing 
some of his best car pictures.

Rick Staehling (art director/film critic) will show pictures of Hastings Street in the 1970s. 

Neil Wedman showing pictures of the sidewalks of Vancouver.

David Wisdom is showing a couple of newly assembled presentations, as well as his 1976 opus, 
Dick/Patti, featuring tableaux in series of the fall of Richard Nixon via the Watergate conspiracy 
and the kidnapping of heiress Patti Hearst by The Symbionese Liberation Army.

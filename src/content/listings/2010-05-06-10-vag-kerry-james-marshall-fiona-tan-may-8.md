Vancouver Art Gallery

New Exhibitions

opening on May 8, 2010

Kerry James Marshall

Kerry James Marshall, celebrated for his insightful work focused on the
African-American experience, will introduce attendees to his massive and
vibrant paintings, drawing on his upbringing during the Civil Rights
Movement in Birmingham, Alabama and in the politically charged atmosphere
of South Central Los Angeles.

Fiona Tan: Rise and Fall

Video artist Fiona Tan, the 2009 representative for the Netherlands at the
2009 Venice Biennale, will present her compelling recent work addressing
the formation of identity and memory in a world with an increasingly global
culture.

SPARK [FWD] 2014 - 3D, VFX, ADVANCED IMAGING

This event is brought to you by Emily Carr/S3D Centre, the Canadian 
3D and Advance Imaging Society, and Spark CG Society.

Dates:
February 5th-9th 2014

Expanded Dates:
Festival: February 5-9, 2014. Conference: February 6-8, 2014. Job 
Fair: February 7 & 8, 2014

Location:
Vancouver International Film Centre, 1181 Seymour St, Vancouver, 
BC.

Cost:
Early-Bird until JANUARY 17th $225 for 3-day conference. Conference 
dates are Thursday February 6 through Saturday February 8 from 9 
am to 6:30 pm. After January 17th, the pass is $250. Individual 
sessions and films available from $10! Job Fair registration is free, and 
opens January 6th.

Description:
SPARK [FWD] is a three-day conference and five-day film festival with 
a focus on Stereo 3D, VFX & Advanced Imaging topics in High Frame 
Rate, live action film production, 4K-8K and more. There is a two-day 
job fair with 11 hiring studios present, and attendance to the Job Fair is 
free! SPARK [FWD] is one of Canada's most affordable conferences 
and film festivals, with world-class speakers and presentations. Gain 
insight into the latest practices from industry, get exposed to leading 
artistic and technical achievements, and watch some excellent films. 
We start the event with an industry mixer, and we close the event with 
a high-energy reception. Register online at sparkfx.ca.

If you are a filmmaker, producer, VFX artist, technology or CG 
enthusiast, this isn't an event you want to miss.

For registration, please visit the website below:

Website: [http://sparkfx.ca/](http://sparkfx.ca/)

Assembly of Text | Letter Writing Club | SEP 5
Letter Writing Club
The Letter Writing Club has been gathering at our flagship store in Vancouver B.C.
to write letters to their loved ones since September of 2005 (save a few covidy
years). This free monthly event is open to everyone, especially those of us who love
writing letters as much as we love receiving them.

CLUB INFORMATION:

Date: First Thursday of every month.

Time: 2 sessions. 18 spots available at each session. First come, first served.
7:00 - 8:00pm & 8:00 - 9:00pm

If you arrive and the 7pm is full, take a number and come back at 8pm.

Location: 3934 Main Street Vancouver

Details: No Cost. Everyone welcome. Supplies provided... tea, cookies, paper,
envelopes, typewriters & encouragement.

* if you are feeling under the weather, please come next month instead.

To keep up to date, follow us on instagram: @assemblyoftext

For millennia textiles have defined human life, fulfilling basic needs like 
shelter and warmth while also functioning in culturally complex 
mannerscommunication, ritual, currency, etc. As a literal and 
figurative thread connecting prehistory to our present moment, the 
evolution of textiles parallels our innate fascinations with body 
augmentation and pattern recognition and knowledge gained from the 
advances of textile production have birthed new industries and 
subcultures in every generation. At the core of this lineage is a 
feedback loop: The more that we invest in textiles the more we come to 
understand basic forces that govern our environment: pattern, 
structure, informatics. These paradigms are present in nature, whether 
defined in terms of "data" or cellular matter, and textiles provide a 
timeless framework for their interpretation. 

SEAMLESS VISIONS, The 19th Annual Subtle Technologies Festival, 
will add to this lineage, showcasing innovations and leading research in 
the art and science of textiles. Specifically, the festival speaker series, 
Manifold, will create opportunities for the critical exchange of 
knowledge about experimental processes, cultural trends, 
socioeconomic challenges and philosophical implications. Boasting six 
lectures and two moderated discussions addressing myriad dimensions 
of textile and wearables-based research, Manifold promises to spur 
new discoveries and collaborations among all those attending.

To accompany and augment the topics highlighted in Manifold, Subtle 
Technologies seeks posters illustrating textiles and wearables-based 
research by graduate and post-graduate students in the Greater 
Toronto and Hamilton Area and beyond. Posters will be exhibited as 
part of the speaker series and poster authors will be invited to attend 
and present their work to visiting scholars, designers and artists over 
lunch during a designated networking session. 

Eligibility:

Applicants must be pursuing a masters, doctorate, or post-doctorate 
degree at an accredited university in Canada. Research included in the 
proposed poster must be unpublished and rightfully owned or licensed 
by the applicant(s).

Submission process:

Send research abstracts and project descriptions (500 words 
maximum) and 2-5 images to be included in your poster design to 
submissions@subtletechnologies.com

Abstracts/proposals should clearly state: 
Project title 
Names of primary investigators and contact information 
Names of affiliated institutions
Please place "research poster proposal" in the subject line of your e-
mail

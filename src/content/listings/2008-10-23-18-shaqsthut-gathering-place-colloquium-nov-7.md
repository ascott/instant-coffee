Shaq'sthut: Gathering Place Colloquium

November 7, 8, 2008

Sty-Wet-Tan Longhouse University of British Columbia
The Aboriginal Curatorial Collective (ACC) is pleased to announce its
forthcoming colloquium, Shaq'sthut - Gathering Place. The colloquium will
be the third of a successful series of gatherings initiated and
administrated by the board and members of the Aboriginal Curatorial
Collective and involves Aboriginal curators, art historians and artists
from across North and South America. The title, Shaq'sthut, refers to the
Coast Salish name for a site that was a gathering place in which it was
common for people to speak five or six languages because of trading,
intermarriage, and other transactions that occurred between groups. Taking
Vancouver as an equally significant space, the ACC Vancouver Caucus has
proposed this city as the site for the ACC/CCA colloquium, scheduled to
take place on November 7, & 8 (2008) at the Sty-Wet-Tan Longhouse at the
University of British Columbia.

20. Upgrade International | CALL | Johannesburg Art Fair 2009 | NOV 15
Call for Submissions: Upgrade International at Johannesburg Art Fair 2009

Upgrade Vancouver, participating with Upgrade International, will show a
Special Project pavillion at the Johannesburg Art Fair in Johannesburg,
South Africa in April 2009.

The pavillion will feature a selection of work made for online platforms,
with a specific curatorial theme: The Global South.

The Global South
The Global South describes what the West is not. The "West" is understood
to define the powerful "Modern" commerce-driven continents - Europe and
North America. If you were to identify places that are part of the Global
South, you would include South America, Africa, China, India, Korea and
other parts of South East Asia.

"With Northern economies beginning to face the realities of increasingly
stagnant growth potential, emerging markets have been earmarked as havens
for the continuation of economic growth. Indeed, economic growth in India,
Brazil and South Africa has in recent years eclipsed that of developed
economies. To define these markets, the term "Global South" has emerged,
loosely designating economies in Central and Latin America, Africa and Asia
with high growth-potential and shared social histories.

Artlogic sees the emergence of the Global South as an opportunity to forge
trans-national networks through reciprocal partnerships. Through the
identification, definition and fortification of these partnerships, regions
within the Global South can benefit from increased cultural visibility and
communication of ideas."

-Art Logic, 2008

The Johannesburg Art Fair (http://www.joburgartfair.co.za) is an annual art
fair operated by Art Logic (http://www.artlogic.co.za )
The First Joburg Art Fair took place in the heart of the Sandton Central
Management District (SCMD) in April 2008. 22 Major galleries sold work in a
traditional art fair set up covering 5000 square meters of the Sandton
Convention Center. Special Projects for 2008: FUNDA Community College,
Tonic design and artist Robin Rhode. Extending the representation of
African contemporary artists at the art fair is As You Like It, a curated
show by Simon Njami. A Talks program focused on collecting African
contemporary art and the place of the art fair in the city. The Art Fair
and the Talks form part of Artlogic's Joburg Art Week.

Upgrade Vancouver launched in 2003 with a successful program of discussions
and presentations at the intersection of art and technology, happening
monthly and supported by social gatherings. The series is part of Upgrade!
International (http://theupgrade.net ), and collaborates to produce
publications, exhibitions and processes of exchange between 30 cities.

The pavillion is a project of Upgrade Johannesburg in partnership with
Upgrade International.

To apply, please send a URL, a bio, and a short description of the work to
Kate Armstrong: kate@katearmstrong.com by November 15, 2008.

22. Charles H. Scott Gallery | The Sound I'm Looking For | til | NOV 16
The Sound I'm Looking For - Part 1
Dave Allen, Matthew Buckingham, Annika Eriksson, Dennis McNulty, Ian Skedd

October 8 to November 16, 2008
Charles H. Scott Gallery

The Charles H. Scott Gallery is pleased to present The Sound I'mLooking
For a two-part exhibition of sound-based work by Canadian and
international artists curated by Cate Rimmer. Working with a variety of
media, these artists use music, the urban soundscape, and the play between
the presence and absence of sound as their subject matter.

In Part 1 of the exhibition Scottish artist Dave Allen presents two works,
For the Dogs based on an Erik Satie composition and Everything Starts
on A, Everything Ends on B, a sketch for a turntable/live concert
composition. Berlin-based Swedish artist Annika Eriksson's video projection
The Soundtrack depicts a lone musician on a busy city street. Though
alone at his drum kit he is accompanied by the sounds of the urban
environment around him. The urban soundscape is also the subject of Irish
artist Dennis McNulty's work in which he creates improvised site-specific
soundtracks for locations within the built environment. For The Sound I'm
Looking For, McNulty has created a new work, one second of a possible
future/monospan twin ride, and performed "the view from now/downtown
parkade" at a parkade across the street from New Westminster's Columbia
Station on Saturday October 11th. Please see www.sndmsc.com for further
details.

A Brief Account of the History of Recorded Music, Re-interpreted (Au Clair
de la lune), a new silent video piece by Vancouver artist Ian Skedd is
based on the artist's research into the phonautogram, a 19th century
invention designed to record sound visually rather than aurally. In New
York artist Matthew Buckingham's audio work, Infinite Tuning, we hear an
orchestra alternately warm up and tune but never actually play a piece of
music, thus subverting expectations and our feelings of anticipation.

The exhibition is accompanied by a number of public programming events
including talks and performances.

The Sound I'm Looking For - Part 2 includes artists Brady Cranfield,
Brian Joseph Davis, Ceal Floyer, Luke Fowler, and Holly Ward. It will run
from November 26 to January 18.

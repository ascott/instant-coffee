Leo Koo Gallery presents

| | | Front Lines | | |

Opening: October 3rd at 7:30pm
Dates: October 3rd - November 2nd

Featuring works by:

Tia Casper
Wouter le Duc
Lok-Him Fung
Jaeden Chayce Leimert
Willie Seo
Mira Song

Front Lines is an exhibition intended to propel young, early-career artists 
currently living and working in British Columbia to the forefront of conversation. 
Leo Koo Gallery’s juried call for submissions is providing six unrepresented 
artists with the opportunity to present their recent work in a month-long group 
exhibition. The gallery invites members of the public to assist in the selection 
of the exhibition’s audience choice award recipient during the show’s opening 
reception.

Front Lines presents a range of works that enter into dialogue with one 
another visually and thematically. Each of the featured artists is engaged with 
ongoing investigations: they take up the retelling of sobering narratives of 
alternative worlds, the piecing together of fragmented memories and identities, 
and the examination of space- both concrete and imaginative. In Leo Koo 
Gallery’s intimate venue their different angles of thought have also overlapped 
in the exploration of boundaries- the lines between remembrance and 
forgetting, between belonging and exclusion, between real and surreal places 
are carefully considered. Conceptually dynamic works in an array of media 
including painting, photography and installation come together in an 
examination of these topics.

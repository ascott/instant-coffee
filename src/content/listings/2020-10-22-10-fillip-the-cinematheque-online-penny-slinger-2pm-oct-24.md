Penny Slinger: Exorcisms
Free Screenings and Talk
October 20"25, 2020

Fillip and The Cinematheque are pleased to announce the launch of Penny Slinger:
Exorcisms, a week-long virtual screening series focusing on the film work of artist Penny
Slinger.

Join us this week for screenings of Slingers early experimental film works as well as two
films Slinger made with Welsh
filmmaker Jane Arden (1927"82)"Vibration (1975) and
The Other Side of the Underneath (1972). The series concludes with a presentation of
Out of the Shadows (2017), a recent documentary that tracks Slingers life and practice.

As part of Penny Slinger: Exorcisms, Fillip presents Penny Slinger in conversation with
art historian and critic Jaleh Mansoor.
October 24 (Saturday) 2pm PDT

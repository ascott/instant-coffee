Make Your Own Orange Shirt on Truth & Reconciliation Day
Saturday, September 30, 2023 | 11:00am - 3:00pm

Join Laxgalts'ap multimedia artist, Shaina Stephens in making an Orange Shirt on
Saturday, September 30. Shaina will lead an open conversation about residential
schools and recount Phyllis (Jack) Webstads story of having her orange shirt taken
from her, which influenced the beginning of Orange Shirt Day.

Shaina will walk you through making your shirt using stencils, paint, and more. Shirts
and decorating materials are included in the cost of the workshop. One ticket per shirt.
Groups and families are welcome to collaborate on making a shirt together!

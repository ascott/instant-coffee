New Presentation House Gallery publications launch at The Apartment 
together with Art Cities of the Future published by Phaidon Press 
Friday, October 4, 7PM to 9PM

Isabelle Pauwels
Flakey: The Early Artworks of Glenn Lewis
& Art Cities of the Future

Presentation House Gallery is thrilled to host the launch of the first 
comprehensive catalogue on Isabelle Pauwels’ art practice, together with a 
web catalogue on Glenn Lewis’ early works, flakey.ca. Both artists will be in 
attendance.
This celebration also marks the Vancouver release of the latest Phaidon 
Press compilation, Art Cities of the Future, that features Vancouver as one of 
twelve cities to watch closely as it becomes a vital centre for contemporary 
art. Reid Shier, Presentation House Gallery’s Director/Curator, gives his take 
on Vancouver’s cultural landscape and the artists who are contributing to its 
growth, which includes Isabelle Pauwels and seven others. Artists in 
attendance. 
Isabelle Pauwels is represented by Catriona Jefferies, Vancouver. Glenn 
Lewis is represented by Trench Gallery, Vancouver.

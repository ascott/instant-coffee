Hotam Press & Friends are pleased to present this important documentary about Hong
Kong's Democratic Movement to our Vancouver community. Please come and join us
in this intimate screening in the heart of Chinatown.

Revolution of Our Times (Chinese: ') is a 2021 Hong Kong documentary film
directed
by Kiwi Chow. With interviews and footage of the frontline protest scenes, the
film covers the stories of the 2019"2020 Hong Kong protests. The documentary takes
its name from a locally well-known political slogan "liberate Hong Kong, revolution of
our times" which has been ruled as illegal by the Hong Kong High Court. The film had
its debut in the 2021 Cannes Film Festival in France and is currently banned in China
and Hong Kong.

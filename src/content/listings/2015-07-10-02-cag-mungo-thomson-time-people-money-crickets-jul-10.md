New Exhibition

Mungo Thomson
Time, People, Money, Crickets

July 10 to August 30, 2015
Opening: Friday, July 10, 7-10pm

Live Performance: Mungo Thomson – Cricket Solos for Clarinet, Piccolo,
Percussion, and Violin

Presented in partnership with Vancouver New Music
Friday, July 10, 2015; from 8:30pm
Emery Barnes Park (Richards and Davie Street)

This evening performance is concurrent with the opening of the exhibition.

The Contemporary Art Gallery presents a major solo exhibition by Los
Angeles based artist Mungo Thomson. Time, People, Money, Crickets brings
together a survey of work produced during the past five years, including
key works in film, sound, sculpture, performance complemented by an
extensive monographic publication.

Shawn Hunt: Dream Season
October 17 - November 16, 2024

Equinox Gallery is pleased to present Dream Season, an exhibition of new paintings
by Heiltsuk artist Shawn Hunt. This exhibition presents a series of surreal scenes in
which moments of the artists own life are re-imagined as present-day daydreams
infused with Indigenous traditions. Illuminated by the moonlight, Hunts figures are
suspended between the past, present and future, resisting a singular interpretation.

In Hunts compositions, we see his innovative use of traditional Northwest Coast
design principles where an unbroken outline weaves various
patterned motifs into
figures. Creatures both human and animal, and sometimes mythological, are
intertwined and in a state of constant flux, shifting between human and spirit realms.
Using traditional structures, Hunt celebrates the fundamental characteristics of
traditional design while adapting the subjects of his paintings to a contemporary
context.

Shawn Hunt: Dream Season will be on view at Equinox Gallery until November 16,
2024.

Visual Artist in Residence (Fresh! AiR Program)
Caetani Cultural Centre, Vernon, BC, Canada

DATES: 3 timeframes available: May1st-June 30th, July 4th-August 31st & September 
5th-October 31st 
APPLICATION DEADLINE: December 15th, 2018
NOTIFICATION DEADLINE: end of December 2018

WHO IS ELIGIBLE: Open to emerging and established visual artists (of all visual 
disciplines)

INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: accommodations at the Caetani Cultural Centre (a

NOT INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: travel expenses, food, health insurance, art 
materials, shipping costs, exhibition costs and other personal expenses.

Available at StudioLand, December 1, $400 per month, 1 brand new studio

Bright, clean, safe, private, 24 hour access, free wifi and utilities, slop sink,

welcome.

Were looking for quiet, serious, long-term studio mates, the spaces are
ideal for painters, graphic artists, designers, illustrators, writers, animators, 
filmmakers, or any other creative pursuits!

A five year lease is in place until June 2023 with an option to renew.

StudioLand is in Chinatown, near Strathcona, and is within the Culture Crawl
boundaries, steps away from galleries, cafes, restaurants, bus lines, and
bike paths.

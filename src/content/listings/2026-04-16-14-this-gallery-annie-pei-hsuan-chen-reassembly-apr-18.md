Annie Pei-Hsuan Chen - Reassembly

THIS Gallery presents Reassembly, a new exhibition by Annie Pei-Hsuan Chen, on view April
17"26, 2026. Through bold forms and refined colour palettes, Chen explores the fluid and
reconstructive nature of memory. Her painted compositions"suggestive of architectural
structures and puzzle-like arrangements"engage a dynamic interplay between construction
and deconstruction, where familiar forms shift and take on new meaning. Balancing structure
with spontaneity, her work reflects a personal visual language shaped by both discipline and
creative freedom.

Opening Reception: Saturday, April 18, 12"4 PM.

Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents new work by Featured 
Artist Cameron McLellan.

Cameron McLellans work concerns the built environment, architectural space, and 
materiality through drawing, painting and site-specific responses. Through form, 
texture and the meditative quality of repetition, he explores how these connections 
can be rethought or inverted, in service to the social and political aspects that result 
from and determine urban form.

McLellan holds a Master of Fine Arts from the University of British Columbia.

Digital Fracture: VOICES

By Theatre Terrific Ensemble
October 30th-November 8th 2020

Vancouver, BC "

There is a crack in everything
That's how the light gets in.
-Leonard Cohen

What is our universal language at this time? How are we a part of mother earth and
what is our responsibility to mother earth and to each other? Covid has put us in a
deeper awareness of ourselves. Through gesture, recorded audio, and visual media,
Digital Fracture: VOICES asks us to step away from the screen and to transition into a
deeper self-awareness and connection. The Fringe was one of the first festivals to
embrace our inclusive theatre company so many years ago, and we are overjoyed to
join again at this time.

The all-inclusive cast of 14 actors enter into the space one at a time, taking their social
distanced place. They give voice to their
pre-recorded thoughts on the gifts of mother
earth, their contribution using gestural expression; humanities most vocal instrument.
They seek to safely connect, to share and ultimately do so, with courage, strength and
power. Finally, they challenge the audience to safely connect and share with them;
through the universal language of music.

Running Time: 45 to 60 minutes

Theatre Terrific develops & enacts the process & performance of authentic radical
inclusion. We proudly identify as one of the few disability-mandated arts organizations in
BC whose work values differences as creative gifts of possibility & truth.

Theatre Terrifics purpose is to collaboratively create activities & events that respectfully,
rigorously & with risk, honor the gifts of experienced & emerging artists. Our work
reflects the work of artists & community members whose lived perspective includes
physical, mental health, cognitive, cultural, gender, age & any other sectionalities that
make up the mosaic of
humanity. We honour this great variety of lived experience as
immensely creative, ultimately bringing together those who many never have had the
opportunity to creatively work with those outside their established community; whatever
that may be.

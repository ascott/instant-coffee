OPENING

Neil Wedman: Selected Monochromatic Paintings and Works on Paper. 
2007-11, Part One
Opening Reception: Saturday January 7th, 2 to 4pm

Equinox Gallery is very pleased to present an exhibition of new works 
by Vancouver artist Neil Wedman. In this exhibition, Wedman takes 
typically picturesque imagery, such as desert landscapes or the 
materialization of rainbows, and renders them in a monochromatic 
palette. These seductive paintings play on our expectations of the 
pictorial, disrupting predictable visual associations of colour and form. 
The exhibition continues until February 4th.

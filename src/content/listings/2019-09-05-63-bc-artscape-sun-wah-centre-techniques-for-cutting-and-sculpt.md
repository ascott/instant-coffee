Paper Play: Techniques for Cutting and Sculpting Paper
Sunday September 29th, 10am to 3pm
BC Artscape Sun Wah Centre
268 Keefer Street, 3rd floor
Cost: $100

Register through eventbrite: https://www.eventbrite.com/e/paper-play-techniques-for-cutting-
and-sculpting-paper-tickets-68642440387

Rachael Ashe is a Vancouver-based artist working in paper-cut, sculpture, and installation work.
Through this four hour hands-on session she will introduce students more in-depth to
techniques for cutting, sculpting, folding, and transforming paper. The goal is to help
participants develop papercraft skills, discuss tools and materials, as well as inspire ideas for
integrating paper elements into their own work.

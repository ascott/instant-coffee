Vancity Theatre
1181 Seymour Street, Vancouver, British Columbia V6B 3M7

True North | Marion Scott Gallery
For the Inuit, there is Inuit Nunangat - the four northerly territories - and then there is the fifth 
region, the parcel of land to the south where most of the settlers live alongside the 49th 
parallel. Southern Canada is now home to approximately 30 percent of their own, of course, 
either through resettlement, economic necessity, or to pursue educational or career 
advancement, or by virtue of love and family. In their mid-length documentary, Gabriel 
Nuraki Koperqualuk and Aeyliya Husain follow Inuk artist Niap and Torontomiutaujugut co-
founder Joshua Stribbell, two Inuit raised in the fifth region under the shadow of the Sixties 
Scoop and the residential school system, and still working to reconcile their identity, 
heritage, upbringing and experience.

This screening, co-presented with the Marion Scott Gallery, will be preceded by the short 
film Urban Inuk (6 min) and followed by a discussion with guests Joshua Stribbel (and more 
to be announced).

Generously supported by John and Joyce Price.

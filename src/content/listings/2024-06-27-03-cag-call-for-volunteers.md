Call for Volunteers: Contemporary Art Gallery, Vancouver

Working as part of a larger team, CAG Volunteers will
have the opportunity to
engage with and expand their knowledge of contemporary art while learning about
gallery operations across various departments. Volunteers provide ongoing support
with visitor engagement, special events, and administration.

CAG volunteers are the primary contact for visitors to the gallery and are responsible
for:
welcoming visitors and offering information on the exhibitions and gallery
programming
operating the point of sale system for all bookstore transactions
occasional assistance with special events, including exhibition opening receptions,
talks, and tours
other operational and administrative duties as required
Our volunteer application forms can be found at
https://cagvancouver.org/about/opportunities.

For questions, please contact volunteer@cagvancouver.org

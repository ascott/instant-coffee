Vancouver Civic Theatres presents

Why Not Theatres What You Wont Do For Love

starring David Suzuki and Tara Cullis

at the Vancouver Playhouse from February 13 to 17,
2024.

Experience a special live evening with these renowned environmentalists as they
make their acting debut in a unique experimental play that shines new light on their
50-year relationship as partners and activists, and poses the question " can the love
we have for each other save the planet?

Actors and real-life couple Miriam Fernandes and Sturla Alvsvaag navigate a poetic
and playful dialogue on stage with David and Tara, who share stories and insights
that weave an enchanting love story and ask us to consider loves capacity to inspire
action and propel us all towards a healthier, more sustainable planet.

Following each show, audiences are invited to stay for an informal Q&A with David,
Tara, Miriam and Sturla.

What You Wont Do For Love

A Why Not Theatre Production

Feb 13, 14, 16 & 17, 2024 at 7:30pm

Vancouver Playhouse, 600 Hamilton Street, Vancouver, V6B 2P1

Run Time: 90 minutes + optional 25 minute Q&A with David, Tara, Miriam, and Sturla

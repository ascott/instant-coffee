Nicole Raufeisen & Ryan Witt

YOU'RE STILL HERE
AND SO ARE WE

April 5 - May 3, 2009

Republic Gallery is pleased to announce that 7 new artists will be joining the roster for the month 
of April courtesy of Nicole Raufeisen and Ryan Witt. This online exhibition takes its impetus from 
the artist duo’s old Vancouver studio at 882 East Hastings in which they worked from 2003 
through to 2007. The storefront studio was converted into a photo co-op following their departure
and the artists have invited several of the former and current occupants of the 882 space to 
participate in this updated version of a past exhibition.

You’re Sill Here and So Are We can be viewed online at [http://www.republicgallery.com](http://www.republicgallery.com) from April 
5th through May 3rd, 2009. Regular gallery hours are 11 am – 4 pm, Thursday through Saturday, 
and by appointment.

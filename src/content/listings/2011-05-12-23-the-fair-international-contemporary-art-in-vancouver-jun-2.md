International Contemporary Art
in Vancouver // June 2 - 5, 2011

THE FAIR allows local and international commercial galleries, non-profit
artist-run centers and independent project spaces to join together and
exhibit contemporary art.

THE FAIR is hosted by The Waldorf Hotel. After 63 years in business The
Waldorf Hotel has been re-imagined as a creative compound where
contemporary art, music, food, and culture convene under one roof.

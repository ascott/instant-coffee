Background
Trans Mountain Corporation operates Canadas only major pipeline system
transporting oil products to the West Coast. We can deliver approximately 890,000
barrels of petroleum products each day through a dual pipeline system operating
across the 1,150 kilometres from Alberta to British Columbia, and Washington state.

Our Mission: To transport Western Canada's oil safely, competitively, and reliably,
opening up international markets and unlocking Canadian resources.

Our Vision: To be North America's most respected pipeline pathway for petroleum
customers.

For close to 70 years our Westridge Marine Terminal, located in the Burrard Inlet has
facilitated safe export of crude oil to global destinations and receipt and delivery of
jet fuel to the Vancouver
International Airport.

The Mural Project

If you are a BC or AB based artist or artist collective with experience in designing
and installing large-scale artworks in site-specific locations, this opportunity may be
for you.

Trans Mountain is seeking an artist to design and install a mural at Westridge Marine
Terminal. The mural will be approximately 90 metres (length) by 2.5 metres (height)
and will be on the south side of a concrete wall beside the railway lines that transect
the terminal. The mural location is outside.

Concept

Having recently completed the Trans Mountain Expansion Project, the mural will
commemorate an important milestone in Trans Mountains history.

Besides showcasing our Mission and Vision, Trans Mountain welcomes the mural to
incorporate the following important aspects of the company:

Trans Mountain employees are deeply committed to Trans Mountains company
values of safety, respect, excellence and integrity. Their efforts are exemplified in our
practices and
procedures.
Trans Mountain is committed to ensuring its operations are conducted responsibly,
with due regard to environmental protection and sustainability.
Trans Mountain is a transboundary company operating in many traditional territories
and communities throughout Alberta, British Columbia and Washington state.
Trans Mountain is committed to working with Indigenous communities in a spirit of
cooperation and shared responsibility to build and sustain meaningful relationships
based on mutual respect.
We value the relationships we hold with Indigenous groups, neighbours and
stakeholders as well as the contributions of our employees and contractors.
Artwork incorporating representational or narrative elements is encouraged.

The mural will be visible to hundreds of passengers on the West Coast Express
(WCE) commuter rail service every weekday between Downtown Vancouver and
Mission City, BC.

Project Goals

Commemorate the completion of the Expansion Project and the legacy of Trans
Mountain.
Provide an opportunity for BC and AB-based artists.
Elevate the space at Westridge Marine Terminal by transforming a previously
unusable area into an enjoyable visual asset.
Budget

The total budget available to the artist(s), inclusive of all expenses, is up to $95,000.
The artwork budget must be inclusive of all costs including but not limited to, artist
fees, studio overhead, artwork fabrication, site preparation, weather mitigation,
travel, transportation and installation.

Eligibility & Timeline:

BC or AB artists, artist collectives and artist teams are welcome to apply for this
opportunity. Given the scale and location of this work, mid-career artists who have
experience in mural and/or large-scale site-specific works are preferred.

The installation of the work must be completed no later than December 31, 2024.

How to Apply:

Trans Mountain invites interested artists to submit an expression of interest package
no later than noon on July 5, 2024.

Artists are
requested to include:

A CV outlining their qualifications and relevant arts-related experience.
Relevant images of past artworks.
Indication of availability within proposed timeframe.
If an artist collective or a team of artists are interested, please submit one expression
of interest.

Selection Process:

A panel will review all expressions of interest and short list up to three artists. These
artists will be contacted in early-July and provided a site visit, subsequent to which,
the artists should provide a written concept, a detailed design and/or sketch, budget,
time schedule and any other pertinent information. Each of the shortlisted artist(s)
will be provided an honorarium of $1,500 to complete this phase.

Shortlisted candidates will be notified early August of the panels finalist decision.

The artist will have from August 2024 to the end of December 2024, to complete the
project.

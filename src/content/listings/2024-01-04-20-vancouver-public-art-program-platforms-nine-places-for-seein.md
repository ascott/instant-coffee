The City of Vancouver Public Art Program is pleased to present the work of Coast
Salish artist Jonas Jones as part of its Platforms: Nine Places for Seeing program.
Jonas is from the slil"wta" (Tsleil-Waututh) Nation and also has strong bloodlines
from the Swxw7mesh (Squamish) Nation. He is primarily a carver, but begins most
of his carving work with digital or hand-done drawing. The work he created for
Platforms is a digital illustration entitled Reflection. In his own words the work is about
a reflection of your inner being, a look inside of who you are. This illustration can be
seen on transit shelters throughout the city from now until January 28th.

You can learn more about this work and the works of other featured artists on our
website at https://vancouver.ca/platforms

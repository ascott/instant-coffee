Call for Submissions: Long Distance Picnic

Perpendicular Play is an ongoing collaborative zine series through What Lab
(whatlab.ca). We are now accepting submissions for our fifth volume, on the theme Long
Distance Picnic.

FORMAT:
Work on paper: vignettes, photography, waka, creative non-fiction, water color, collage,
poetry, prose, line drawingsanything capable of achieving tangible reality on a two-
dimensional printed page. Work must be submitted in digital format.

DEADLINE:
All submissions must be received by midnight on November 15th, 2020. For more
information on the submission process, visit:

whatlab.ca/ppvol5-long-distance-picnic/

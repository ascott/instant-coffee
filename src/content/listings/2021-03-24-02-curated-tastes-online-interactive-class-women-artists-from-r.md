Lost and Found: Women Artists from Renaissance to Impressionism.
Thursday, March 25th, 2021
Starting at 5 pm PDT
Register: https://mailchi.mp/1a85a560046f/fun-and-interactive-art-history-class

In honour of Womens History Month, Curated Tastes brings forward women artists that
have been lost in history and found over the past few decades.
Women artists remain to be undervalued and underrepresented in museums, galleries,
auction houses and major art collections compared to their male counterparts. This
disparity is deeply rooted in the historical denial of their education, exclusion from art
academies and omission of their achievements from history books!
No
previous knowledge of art history is required. The content of this class is given in a
concise and approachable way where attendees will get to learn about iconic classical,
academic, modern female artists and the meaning behind their artworks.
Expect anonymous polls, visual hunts, break-out rooms and lots of learning!
This class is open to everyone looking to have fun, interact with other people online while
learning about inspiring female artists from the Renaissance to Impressionism.

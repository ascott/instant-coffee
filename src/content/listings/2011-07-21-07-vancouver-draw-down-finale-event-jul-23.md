Vancouver Draw Down Finale Event : July 23, 2011 from 10am - 4pm

Join us for any or all of these two-hour come-and-go, all-ages workshops

at each of the nine participating sites across the city.

No drawing skills needed--just your imagination and sense of fun!

Here is the List of two hour workshops across the city:

10:00 am - 12:00 pm

Sunset Community Centre

6810 Main Street, Vancouver, BC

Creatures at Sunset: Collaborative Drawings

FREE and open to everyone!

Drop by and help create collaborative creature drawings in the lobby at Sunset. 
Each creature begins with a random brush mark, and depends on at least three 
different people’s contributions.

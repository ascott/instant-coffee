TO ARTISTS | SEP 5
CALL TO ARTISTS: 2024 BRANSCOMBE HOUSE ARTIST RESIDENCY

The City of Richmonds Arts, Culture and Heritage Services invites professional
artists in all disciplines (including visual artists, composers, musicians, performers,
writers and other creative professionals) to submit proposals for an 11-month live-in
residency located on Canadas west coast, in Richmond, BC, on the traditional lands
of the hn"q"min"m" speaking First Peoples.

The City is seeking applications from artists who are interested in developing new
projects and programs that promote meaningful community engagement through
artistic opportunities.

The Branscombe House Artist Residency will begin in mid-January 2024 and run
through until mid-December 2024.

Branscombe House is a beautifully restored Edwardian-style house located in the
residential area of historic Steveston in Richmond. The self-contained two-bedroom
suite
upstairs is offered as an artist residence/studio with rent 100% subsidized in
exchange for contributing a minimum of 240 hours of community-engaged work on
approved creative projects/programs developed in collaboration between staff,
artist(s) and community groups/organizations.

Deadline for Applications: September 5, 2023 at 5:00pm PDT

For more information about this opportunity, visit:
www.richmond.ca/BranscombeHouse

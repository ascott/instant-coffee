The Art of Conversation - Artist panel with Karin Jones, Manuel Axel Strain and Clare
Yow

Join curator and artist Janet Wang for a conversation with exhibiting artists Clare Yow,
Manuel Axel Strain , and Karin Jones. This panel discussion will take place on
Saturday, Nov. 25 from 2-3:30pm at the Gordon Smith Gallery, 2121
Lonsdale Ave,
North Vancouver

The Art of Conversation features artworks from our permanent collection and six
invited artists, including Manuel Axel Strain, Lexy Ho-Tai, Karin Jones, Parvin Peivandi,
Clare Yow and Janet Wang.

The Art of Conversation explores art's role as a communication platform. The Art of
Conversation draws conversational threads between the works in the gallery,
highlighting art's potential to facilitate and provoke dialogue. The perspectives and
artistic conversations of elementary and secondary school students will be featured
through original writing and art.

We acknowledge the generous support of the Canada Art Council.

POSITION TITLE: Board Member, Legal/Policy/Risk

APPLICATION CLOSE: February 28, 2021

STATUS: Volunteer

LOCATION: Vancouver, British Columbia

Leadership and guidance from our Board of Directors is integral in achieving Vancouver
Mural Festivals strategic vision. We work together to build a strong foundation of
governance, operations, community engagement, artist engagement, and more. There are
positions opening with a focus on a background in Legal/Policy/Risk and Non-Profit Arts
Strategy. Joining the existing board members, each will work collaboratively with the Board,
Executive Director and VMF leadership team to continue to mature the organization and
help navigate its future path.

This is a roll-up-your-sleeves team, and the right board candidate will have the same
approach. A seasoned practitioner in their field, they will have served on a previous board
of equal scope in the non-profit environment. The
ideal applicant has previously supported
or worked with an arts organization (as a volunteer or employee) and brings with them a
broad appreciation of arts programming throughout the Metro Vancouver region. Bonus
points for having already attended the Vancouver Mural Festival in previous years.

To apply: [https://vanmuralfest.ca/joinourteam](https://vanmuralfest.ca/joinourteam)

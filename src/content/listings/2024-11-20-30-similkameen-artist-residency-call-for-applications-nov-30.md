Call for Applications: Similkameen Artist Residency (SAR)

The Similkameen Artist Residency is now accepting applications for one-, two-, or
three-month residencies from Jan to Dec 2025. Two to three-week micro residencies
will also be considered.

Deadline:

Applications of interest must be submitted by Nov 30 to be reviewed for our first
round of application
intakes. Applications will be accepted on a rolling basis after
Nov 30, based on availability after the first round of applications.

About the Similkameen Artist Residency (SAR) :

The Similkameen Artist Residency (est. 2021) is an artist-run residency that operates
out of a renovated log house. Surrounded by sage-covered hills on the edge of
Keremeos, SARs mission is to provide a tranquil, affordable, and supportive
environment for artistic exploration. We believe that all artists"including those at
various career stages and across diverse artistic trajectories"should be given
opportunities to rest and refocus their practices. Enhanced by an ethos of
community, curiosity, and creative exchange, SARs self-directed residency structure
fosters productive solo studio time and collegial cohorts.

Qualifications:

SAR welcomes emerging, mid-career, or seasoned artists from across Canada and
internationally. As this is a self-directed residency, we encourage applicants from a
variety of
disciplines and backgrounds to apply. We seek artists with a BFA, MFA, or
equivalent experience, who demonstrate a strong and committed practice. As the
residency is a communal environment, we welcome artists who value collaborative
and community practices.

For Program Guidelines, Artist Fees, FAQs, and Application Instructions, please visit
our website at:

simartres.com

For questions or individual application support, please contact us:

info@simartres.com

The Similkameen Artist Residency acknowledges that our programming takes place
on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territories of the Syilx tmix
(Syilx/Okanagan) Nation. We ask visiting artists to be considerate to the land and to
the Smalqmix people who have tended to this place for centuries, and to treat these
territories with care and respect. SAR is honoured to share space to create here.

Intro/Continuing Watercolour with Sophia Longo

Where: Maplewood House, 399 Seymour River Pl, North
Vancouver, BC
When: Saturdays, 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM
Intro: February 1st"22nd, 2025
Continuing: March 1st"22nd, 2025
Who: Ages 15+ (youth must attend with an adult, and both need tickets)
What Its About:

Intro (February): Perfect for beginners or anyone wanting to brush up on the basics. Its
a fun and accessible way to get comfortable with watercolour techniques like washes,
glazing, and textures.
Continuing (March): Focuses on painting birch trees, taking skills to the next level. Youll
create three unique pieces and really dig into advanced techniques and composition.
Why Its Great:

Build confidence in watercolour, whether youre just starting out or refining your skills.
Learn in a supportive, hands-on environment.
Take home artwork youre proud of and a visual library of techniques.

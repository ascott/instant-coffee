Vancouver, BC, September 27 - October 5, 2024 " Still/Life is a thought-provoking
new exhibition curated by Barbara Bryn Klare, an artist and curator from Athens,
Ohio. Drawing from a recent call for entry, Klare has created an innovative and
intimate show by pairing works from different artists into nine dynamic duos. The
twist? Artists will only discover their partners when the exhibition opens, adding an
element of surprise and curiosity to this unique event.

Inspired by the historical concept of still life, Klare reinterprets the genre through
the lens of the human figure. In her curatorial statement, she notes:

The concept of still life has long been a cornerstone in art history, arranging
commonplace objects"fruit,
flowers, drapery"to evoke contemplation and themes
of fragility or mortality. In STILL/LIFE, the figure is subject, object, and vessel for
these everyday yet universal stories. The artists featured reveal layers of meaning
hidden in the mundane, urging us to see beyond the surface to connect with subtle
energies that animate us and the world around us. We are still and alive: we are still
life.

This exhibition serves as a meditation on the body as both art and storyteller, inviting
viewers to reflect on the subtle forces that shape our shared existence.

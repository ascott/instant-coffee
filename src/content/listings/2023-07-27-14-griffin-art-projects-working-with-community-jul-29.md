Working with Community: A Talk by Artist and Curator-In-Residence Su Ying Strang,
July 29, 1PM

What are the challenges and impacts of community engagement within the arts?
Alberta-based artist and curator Su Ying Strangs work in the arts is informed by an
artist-centered and community-driven ethos, prioritizing thoughtful stewardship of
artistic practices and accessibility to programming for audiences. In a conversation with
Alice Lam on her work with The New Gallery, Calgary, Strang noted that, It was
important that if we were taking up space in this community, that the space was
accessible by the
community. That the community felt welcomed in the space, that
there were programs specifically serving this community.

Join Su Ying Strang, for her online discussion of these and other related curatorial
practices and projects, in her presentation, Working with Community this Saturday, July
29 1pm - 2:30pm.

Job Posting Gallery Director

Access Gallery (Vancouver British Columbia) is seeking an experienced
Gallery Director with a passion for the visual arts. Access Gallery is a
non-profit Artist Run Centre which has contributed to the cultural
landscape of Vancouver since 1991. Access Gallery retains the traditional
model of a board-programmed Artist Run Centre. Access is committed to
presenting the work of professional artists in all stages of their practice
that are underrepresented to Vancouver audiences. While maintaining a
commitment to a board-governed Artist Run Center, we are looking for a
director who can creatively implement diverse models of curating and
programming.
As a proven administrator and team leader, our successful candidate will be
responsible for managing all activities of Access including planning,
fundraising, finance, human resources. Working with the Board of
Directors, the successful candidate will plan and implement the artistic
programming, and fundraising activities, as well as developing
collaborative programming with partnership organizations.

The successful candidate will be a proven administrator and team leader,
with recent experience in administration and board governance of non-profit
organizations.

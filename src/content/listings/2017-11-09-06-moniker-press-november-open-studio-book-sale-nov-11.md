Moniker Press' November Open Studio + Book Sale!

Saturday November 11, 1-6pm
524 Main Street, Vancouver, BC

Drop by on November 11th to learn about the inner workings of the Moniker Press 
risograph print shop! At the open studio, you're invited to: pick up a copy of the Intro to 
Riso zine, see the RZ and MZ printers in person, view print samples from the archive 
and meet others who are interested in risograph printing. 

A selection of risograph books collected at the Toronto Art Book Fair (and from the 
Reading Nook) will be on display for browsing / inspiration. Moniker Press publications 
will also be available for purchase with select copies on sale! Including the new 2018 
Artists Calendar > www.monikerpress.ca/product/2018-artists-calendar

Elizabeth Varty brings PRECIOUS STONES to Britannia Art Gallery (1661 Napier St. 
Vancouver BC) for the month of August (7 " 30th). Opening August 7th 6:30 to 8:30 
pm. PRECIOUS STONES: A Japanese saying goes, the pebble in the brook secretly 
believes itself to be a precious stone. For me, the pebble, the moving water of the 
brook, the unassuming plants nearby and any nearby rock formations are all precious 
things. My paintings and photographs are based on subjective associations, attempts to 
invite the viewer to make new personal associations and to perhaps change their 
perception of the world around us.

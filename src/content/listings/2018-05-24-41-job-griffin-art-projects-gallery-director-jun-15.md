GRIFFIN ART PROJECTS: Gallery Director
1174 Welch St, North Vancouver, BC

GRIFFIN ART PROJECTS is currently seeking a Gallery Director. Candidates
should demonstrate extensive knowledge of contemporary art and a strong
vision for a forward-thinking, dynamic program that showcases the strong
collections of art from our area for the local community. The director
should have the ability to engage with a broad and diverse audience. As
this position provides stewardship and expertise to local collections,
experience in successful collector/donor cultivation is critical. Griffin
Art Projects would like an MA in art history and at least five years
experience working in the field, but will consider qualified candidates at
all career levels.

The successful candidate will report to the President of the GRIFFIN ART
PROJECTS. The position will be approximately 20 hours per week, though you
will be expected to adopt flexible work flows. Remuneration for the first
year will be approx. $30-35 an hour, depending on experience. Your
responsibilities include:

Exhibitions - create an on-going program of exhibitions (and events, to
augment exhibition program), utilizing independent curators, largely BC
based, including yourself.

Operations - manage day-to-day operations, including hiring + training of
staff/volunteers;

Budgets - prepare and manage GRIFFIN budgets, oversee/control expenses;

Loan Agreements - work with curators to oversee signing of loan agreements
with collectors, etc.;

Installation - manage installation of shows, reception planning,
de-installation;

GRIFFIN Promotion - develop + implement effective GRIFFIN promotion program;

Disbursements - work with accountant to ensure efficient and effctive
accounting;

Fund Raising - develop fund raising strategy, oversee implementation;

Renovations - determine and oversee renos and required GRIFFIN space
improvements;

Strategic Plan - assist the Board in developing a long-term strategic plan
for the GRIFFIN;

GRIFFIN Website - enhance the website to make it more effective and kept up
to date;

Arts Community - liaise with arts community (and cultural groups);

Publishing - contribute to the budding publishing program.

To apply, please send a CV and cover letter to info@griffinartprojects.ca
Application deadline is June 15th, 2018.

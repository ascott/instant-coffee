Access Gallery is excited to announce the open call for our upcoming NOW, NEVER
artist residency. Considering themes of urgency
and community resilience, NOW,
NEVER will invite selected artists and collectives to redeploy the gallery space as a
site for rapid, intersectional forms of social care. How can our practices trouble time?
How do we seek moments of rupture to create opportunities for radical change?
What networks support us in times of crisis; how do we weave them before the
moment arrives? What cannot wait? What happens when the dust settles?

The NOW, NEVER residency will run from October 1st through October 31st, 2024,
followed by a public group exhibition in November - December, 2024.

Residents will receive:

Shared studio space in the gallery at 222 E Georgia St, Vancouver, BC V6A 1Z7

Group exhibition

Opportunities for additional public programming

Professional documentation

Open call closes: 11:59 pm PDT on Sept 10, 2024

Residency dates: Oct 1 - 31st, 2024

Exhibition dates: Nov - Dec, 2024

Compensation: Artists will receive an exhibition fee according to the 2024 CARFAC-
RAAV guidelines,
as well as compensation for a group public engagement event
(workshop, talk, etc). Total compensation is approximately $950 CAD.

More information on the NOW, NEVER residency is available in the full open call:
https://accessgallery.ca/news/now-never-residency-open-call

If for any reason you are unable to complete the submission form, please contact

programming@accessgallery.ca for assistance.

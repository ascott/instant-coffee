Please join the University of British Columbias (UBC) Department of Art History, Visual 
Art & Theory (AHVA) at the AHVA Gallery, in the Audain Art Centre on Wednesday, 
September 18 at 5 p.m. for the opening reception of the AHVA Faculty Exhibition 
2019/20.

Twelve current faculty members and visiting artists Stan Douglas and Lin Xin present 
recent works in this annual AHVA exhibition.

Artists: Barbara Zeigler, Barrie Jones, Carolyn Stockbridge, Christine DOnofrio, Dan 
Starling, Dana Claxton, Dustin Brons, Gu Xiong, Jeneen Frei Njootli, Lin Xin, Manuel 
Pia, Marina Roy, Phil McCrum, Stan Douglas

September 18, 2019 to October 4, 2019

Opening Reception: September 18 from 5-7 p.m.

All events are free and open to the public.

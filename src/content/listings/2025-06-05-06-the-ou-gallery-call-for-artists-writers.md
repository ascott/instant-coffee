Call for Artists + Writers
Winter Artist Residency + Writing Retreat
Deadline to Apply: August 30th 2025

The Ou-Telier, is a self-directed residency in a beautiful old boat-building workshop in
the heart of Vancouver Island; the unceded territory of the Quw'utsun first nation and
Hul'qumi'num treaty group. Come here to decompress, gather inspiration alongside
other artists and make space to create.

We welcome applications from emerging and established artists including writers,
photographers, textile and 2D artists. There is no fee to apply.

Our hope is that you will leave the residency feeling rested, renewed and inspired. See
photos + learn more www.theougallery.com/residencyretreat

Anna Wong: Traveller on Two Roads

Opening Reception: Thursday, August 30, 7pm

Please join the Burnaby Art Gallery for the opening reception of Anna Wong: Traveller on Two 
Roads, a retrospective exhibition of Canadian master printmaker Anna Wong (1930-2013),

featuring over 70 original artworks from a lifetime of travel and cultural influence: from 
Vancouver's Chinatown to New York City, and from Quadra Island to Beijing.

Mingle over refreshments, learn more about the artist, and preview the special edition 
hardcover art book accompanying the exhibition, available in two bilingual editions: 
English/French and Chinese.

The Toronto Smartphone Film Festival was created with the goal to encourage
filmmakers and artists to share their stories through film, without having
to equip themselves with expensive video equipment. As the largest
smartphone film festival in North America, TSFF provides aspiring and
professional filmmakers, from all backgrounds, with a platform to showcase
their talent.

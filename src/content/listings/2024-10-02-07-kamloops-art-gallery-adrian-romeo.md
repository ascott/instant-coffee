Adrian Romeo

Every Day is a Day at the Races When You're Mentally and Physically Ill

September 28 to December 28, 2024
Curated by Craig Willms

Interactive Sculptures and Witty Textile Works Create
Safe Space for Mental Health
Conversations at Kamloops Art Gallery

Beginning September 28, the Kamloops Art Gallery presents Adrian Romeo: Every
Day is a Day at the Races When You're Mentally and Physically Ill, in The Cube.
Through interactive sculptures and witty textile works, Romeo explores the human
condition and reflects on her own mental and physical struggles. Central to the
exhibition is a wooden racetrack comprised of five sculptural wind-up toys, each
symbolizing the artists personal battles: depression, fatigue, chronic pain, insomnia,
and self-doubt. Visitors are encouraged to choose a racer to send down the track.

Accompanying the racetrack are brightly coloured, hand-sewn textile banners with
tongue-in-cheek slogans including: I used to hate how big my hands are. Now Im
glad I can hold so much. These works use humour and whimsy to reframe difficult
subjects, creating a welcoming space for open conversations about mental and
physical health.

Adrian Romeo received a
Bachelor of Fine Arts from Thompson Rivers University in
2024, along with the TRU Medal in BFA. Visit Romeos exhibition in The Cube from
September 28 to December 28, 2024. Admission to The Cube is always free.

Jean Paul Langlois

Unnatural Histories is composed of paintings from two series. One is the story of
Langlois Mtis Fathers rise from a poverty-stricken, working-class background and
marrying into his Mothers affluent and educated
family and the problems related with
switching class. Langlois explores various anecdotes, both funny and tragic, that
illustrate his quest for success and fear of mortality. The remainder features
recurring animal characters in environs that have been altered, adulterated or even
ruined by mankind's greed and blindness.

Benjamin Lumb

Driftwood is an ongoing series inspired from Lumb building beachforts as a child and
currently with his 3 daughters. Overtime his consideration of this material and its
journey from the forest to the beach has inspired the current installation & 7
photographs on exhibit which depict the incineration of a beachfort lumb built in the
mountains.

Benjamin Lumb Art House is a gallery based in Vancouver, Canada primarily working
with emerging and mid career artists with a focus on photography, installation and
paintings. Current spaces include a store front, and underground club, an old
warehouse and various other pop up locations. As a well-established and
practicing
artist, Lumb inherently understands the work of the artists he represents and exhibits
and is able to curate and support the narrative, which they seek to communicate

Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist talk
with Jean Paul Langlois in the Art Rental & Sales showroom Tuesday,
September 18 at 7 PM.

Jean Paul Langlois is a Mtis artist who paints from his home studio in
East Vancouver. Influenced by Pop Art, Neo-expressionism, and Saturday
morning cartoons, he paints large canvasses in heavily saturated,
contrasting colours. His subject matter is informed by cinema, especially
Westerns, a genre owing its visual and written narrative to codes and
iconography that are universal. While themes examined include violence and
racism, the images are often bright and playful.

September 18, 2018, 7 PM; doors open at 6:45 PM

Soapbox

Submission Call:

The Khyber ICA is currently seeking proposals for an exhibition based
around public signage. We are asking individuals to design and construct a
sign that can be installed in a publicly accessible outdoor or indoor
location in the Halifax area. The material and subject matter of this sign
project is completely open to interpretation. The sign could make a protest
or just be a poetic statement that breaks up someone's routine. The
intention of the project is to develop new strategies of art making within
the public domain and to create a spectacle within everyday locations. The
gallery exhibition will feature the photographic documentation and
descriptions of all sign work submissions. So get your sign out there and
say what you want to say!

Some suggested Themes: political, social, poetic, protest, prophetic,
absurdist, historical, commentary Dis/Information, memorial, pictorial, a
series of dialogues or responses to locations.

Examples of sign types: stickers, sandwich boards, photocopies stapled to
poles, phone box flyers, wearable signs, stenciled pavement lettering,
window displays, flags, balloons, banners, badges, business cards, notes in
books, garden markers, etc.

What you need to do:

1. Construct a sign out of a material of your choice.
2. Install and leave your sign in a publicly accessible location. (* The
signs should not be of danger to the general public or damage their location.)
3. Photograph the sign in situ.
4. Make a single 10 X 8 photographic print of the sign.
5.Complete the application form overleaf and drop it off with your print to
the ICA. (*More than one sign project can be submitted per person)

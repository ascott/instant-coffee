Out of the Rain
April 9th to 26th, 2008
Gallery Gachet
Reception: Friday April 18th, 2008, 7:00pm-10:00pm.

Artists: Ken Foster, John Walkus Green, Samantha Ellis, Dolores Dallas,
Jorge Campos, Berge Tashdjian, Alec Weir.

Artists from Vancouver’s Downtown Eastside show new work produced during Gallery Gachet's third annual artist mentoring program for street and homeless artists. Video installations, carving, grafitti ink, ceramic, and paintings are featured. Artist mentors: Archer Pechawis, Guinevere Pencarrick, and Jennifer Merasty, with support from peer artist Quin Martins.

Gallery Gachet, 88 East Cordova Street, Vancouver, 604.687.2468,
www.gachet.org

Greg Girard
Sensitive Surface

Sensitive Surface is Ste Maries atmospheric response to Snack Sakura, a
photographic series by Greg Girard that documents the fading world of Japanese
snack bars.

Presented at Monte Clark alongside the launch of Girards new book Snack Sakura,
the installation translates the emotional weight and cinematic quiet of his images into
a physical experience, intended to behave more like memory than space.

Exhibited alongside this immersive experience is a selection of pictures from Greg
Girards Snack Sakura series taken in Japan between 2017 and 2025.

Presented by Ste Marie and Monte Clark in collaboration with

AD Projects, Jonny Ostrem and Jolin Ras Group

Friday, August 1st, 4pm to 7pm

Sunday, August 11 or Sunday August 18, noon to 2 pm

$65 - including all supplies, tools and materials

The aim of this workshop is to spend a fun, creative and relaxing 2 hours learning
about colour, connecting with other likeminded people, sharing our ideas and
creating an artwork or two. We will use items such as gouache, coloured pencils,
pastels, markers and collage materials to
create interesting effects.

During this session we will be using a variety of techniques and methods to achieve
exciting and colourful compositions. There will be some colour theory taught, but
mostly we will be learning to trust our intuition. The idea is that, when you leave, you
will be inspired to create with colour.

Learn how to embrace colour in a world that wants you to be grey.

About the Instructor:

Shannon Pawliw is a Vancouver-based artist, gallerist, and introvert who uses art as
a way of communicating. Shannon is a practicing visual artist, graphic designer and
the owner of THIS Gallery, a borderless art space designed for the creation of art
and ideas.

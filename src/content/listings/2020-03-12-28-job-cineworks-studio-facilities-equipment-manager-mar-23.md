Position Title: Facilities & Equipment Manager
Deadline to apply: Wednesday March 11 midnight

Wage rate: $22.50 per hour
Status: Permanent
position, 30 hours per week
Schedule: Monday to Friday, 11:30am - 6pm; with some flexibility for outreach & events

The Organization

Established in 1980, Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society is a non-profit artist-
run centre, supporting independent filmmakers, media artists, and arts audiences
through facilities and initiatives that encompass production, exhibition, consultation,
outreach, and advocacy. Through programs that foster experimentation with motion-
picture arts, in dialogue with their historic, current, and future cultural contexts, we
engage our membership and wider communities in the investigative, expressive, and
transformative powers of the moving image.

Cineworks is committed to establishing and upholding values of equity, diversity, and
inclusion. We strongly encourage applications from cis and trans women of colour, Two-
spirit and non-binary people of colour, peoples of underrepresented groups, and those
who have faced systemic barriers at institutions. We
also recognize that diverse lived
experience and knowledge can support a candidates eligibility outside of formal
educational and/or employment classifications.

The Position

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society seeks a Facilities + Equipment Manager
(FEM) to join our small but dynamic team of arts professionals.

The FEM will serve as the first point of contact between the organization and our
membership, providing assistance for facilities & equipment rental, member services,
and support for production & post-production needs. The FEM is an integral part of the
Cineworks team, providing both technical support and guidance to our members, as well
as conducting outreach to advance the community that our membership serves.

The position supports Cineworks core mandate to provide facilities for the production of
independent, non-spored film, and to foster experimentation with motion-picture arts.
The FEM will direct the public toward Cineworks capacity for supporting their creative
needs, relying on an in-depth knowledge of our equipment holdings, facilities, and
programs. The role will involve hands-on maintenance and upkeep of our digital and
analogue film equipment inventory, and administrative upkeep of our rental &
membership database.

Working closely with our Community & Education Liaison and Executive Director, the
FEM will help deliver our year-round activities, programs, and services to our members
and the general public.

Responsibilities

Maintain and administer our film production & post-production equipment, facilities &
resources
Prepare rental packages including camera packages, lighting and grip, sound, and other
resources available for rental
Assist with the coordination of equipment for workshops and programming
Deliver bi-weekly orientation sessions for prospective Cineworks members
Schedule rentals to maintain effective inventory control and facilities bookings
Develop accurate research and analysis to inform decisions on maintenance, usage,
purchase, and/or retirement of equipment
Manage financial duties regarding our equipment and facility rentals, including invoicing
and budgeting
Provide content, input, and accurate research to enable successful granting and fund
development opportunities
Serve as an ambassador to Cineworks through occasional outreach to secondary and
post-secondary education institutions, partner film organizations, and local film festivals

Required Skills

A post-secondary degree or equivalent experience in film, preferably with a hands-on
film production focus
Holistic understanding of film production requirements with regards to the departments
of cameras, sound, production lighting and grip gear
Understanding of film post-production requirements with regards to the departments of
editing, processing, and projection
Adept in digital post-production software such as Adobe Creative Cloud, DaVinci
Resolve, Pro Tools, and Final Cut Pro X
Knowledge of Macintosh platform, office software, and some
familiarity with online
browser-based database systems for inventory
Technical experience with filmmaking, in both digital and analog realms is essential
Excellent organizational and multitasking skills
Excellent written and oral communication skills, with the ability to listen and connect with
people from a variety of backgrounds
Comfortable with public speaking and delivering information to large groups
Self-sufficient and comfortable working independently

To apply please submit a CV and cover letter responding to the responsibilities via email
with the subject line FEM to director@cineworks.ca. Interviews will be conducted
between March 16th - 18th, 2020.

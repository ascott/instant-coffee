Dear Friends,

In December 2014, we announced a new travelling artist residency in
partnership with Burrard Arts Foundation and Art Contraste. Titled
Twenty-Three Days at Sea, this highly unconventional residency offers
selected emergent visual artists passage aboard cargo vessels sailing from
Vancouver to Shanghai, for the 23 days it takes to cross the Pacific Ocean.

After receiving an overwhelming response of nearly 900 submissions from
artists worldwide, it became clear that Twenty-Three Days at Sea is more
than simply an artist residency: it is a powerful framework through which
to address the complexity of our contemporary condition. Compelled by the
sheer diversity of artistsâ€™ proposals, and the breadth of creative queries,
concerns and materials that might be brought to this sea voyage, we have
expanded our initial parameters of the program so that we may now realize
it as a three-year project, enabling four international and local emerging
artists to make this extraordinary voyage each year.

We have just launched a Kickstarter Campaign to make this expanded project
a reality, and we are reaching out to you for your support, so that as
members of Access' extended family, you too can participate in this
ambitious program.

Every little bit helps! DONATE HERE and by contributing to this fundraiser,
you'll become part of something quite remarkable, and helping to give
exceptional emerging artists a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity that will
make a tremendous impact on their practice.

And in fact, we are launching this fundraiser on an auspicious day, because
this afternoon at 5:00 PM PST, our second artist-in-residence, Nour
Bishouty, departs for Shanghai on the MV Hanjin Brussels. Bon voyage, Nour!
We wish you a wonderful trip.

With big hugs,

Kate Craigslist: occurs and reoccurs

Hatch Art Gallery - Second Floor of The Nest, The University of British Columbia

Opening Reception: April 8, 5:00-7:00 pm

Exhibition: April 9-12, 12:00-4:00 pm

Responding to a collection of photographic works in the archives of the Morris and
Helen Belkin Art Gallery by Vancouver artists working between art, activism and
photography from the 1970s to the 1990s, this exhibition is articulated and arranged by
senior undergraduate students enrolled in VISA 440: Advanced Photography in the
Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory. Focussing on the intimacy and power
of photography and documentation, the artists in the exhibition draw inspiration from
works by Kate Craig and Roy Kiyooka and include additional responses to projects by
Melinda Mollineaux, and Hank Bull and Patrick Ready.

The exhibition unfolds the themes of passing time and inhabiting
place and history
through performative and process-based photographs. Each artist in the show
approaches their work by blending art and life, highlighting the mundane and the
everyday as something worthy to be captured.

The works have been developed and presented in collaboration with course instructor
Associate Professor Althea Thauberger and workshop leader Shelly Rosenblum,
Curator of Academic Programs at the Belkin. The artists would like to thank Teresa
Sudeyko, Registrar at the Belkin; Anna Tidlund, Archivist at the Belkin and Western
Front; and Susan Gibb, Executive Director of Western Front, for their additional
guidance and research support.

Artists: Jasmine Almeida, Luke Brown, Steven Chu, Dan Driedger, Emma Grove, Amy
Gu, Sirui Lai, Wenji Li, Yuewen Lyu, Oren Mathews , Yin Mei, Quynh Nguyen, Erin
Olmstead, Vivi Piao, Marcus Rahal, Elno Saung, Gill Sticks, Alex Vido, Jeidn Winkler,

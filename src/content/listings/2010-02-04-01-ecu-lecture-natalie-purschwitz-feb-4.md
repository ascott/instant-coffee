Emily Carr University of Art & Design Interdisciplinary Forums Speaker Series
Thursday @ 3:50PM, Lecture Hall 301, South Building
All talks are free and open to the public
Spring 2010 theme: APPETITES

February 4 Natalie Purschwitz, Going All The Way: making a shift to see
the light

Natalie Purschwitz has a BFA in Intermedia from ECIAD and a BA in
Archaeology from the U of Calgary. She has shown her artwork
internationally and has a clothing line called Hunt & Gather. Natalie has
designed costumes for dance companies as well as maintained a shop/studio
called Hunt & Gather on Caroll street for several years. She has since
closed that studio and is currently working on a one-year project called
Makeshift, during which time she will only wear things she has made
herself. You can
visit the project at huntandgather.ca

Jonah Samson
Every Exit is an Entrance Somewhere Else
April 8, opening from noon to 5pm.

Macaulay & Co. Fine Art is pleased to announce an exhibition of recent work
by Jonah Samson. 

The exhibition's title, Every Exit is an Entrance Somewhere Else, is taken
from Tom Stoppard's absurdist play, Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead,
which follows two minor characters from Hamlet as they contemplate the role
of fate and chance in their own tragic demise. 

In his second solo exhibition at Macaulay & Co. Fine Art, Jonah Samson
continues to infuse new life into found photographs with a large
installation of 150 vintage photographs in the main gallery. The
photographs depict people pointing to one another in an endless series of
imaginary exits and entrances. This clash between free will and destiny
also haunts the appropriated images of puppets, which have been produced
using an archaic photographic printing technique that uses the chemical
reaction between ocean salt, silver particles and the sun's radiation.

The shift of photographic production from a "wet" or chemical process to
digital code has led to the eradication of traditional black and white
silver gelatin printing. The ghostly remnants of this fading process are
visible in old family photo albums that have been subjected to chemicals,
which remove the silver particles from each photograph. We are left with
shadows and lost memories.

In reference to Robert Rauschenberg's Erased de Kooning Drawing (1953),
Samson employs a similar chemical process to dissolve the silver particles
from a photograph by fellow artist Stephen Waddell, leaving almost a blank
piece of paper behind. 

Jonah Samson is a self-taught artist who has exhibited nationally and
internationally. He presented his first solo exhibition with Macaulay & Co.
Fine art in 2015 and his exhibition Another Happy Day (2013) at
Presentation House was listed as one of the top 10 shows that season by
Canadian Art magazine. The publication that accompanied Another Happy Day
was listed as one of the top releases at the New York Art Book Fair by The
New Yorker magazine that year. Other exhibitions include Otherworldly at
the Muse Eugne Leroy, France and the Museum of Arts and Design, New York;
Unearthed at Sainsbury Centre for Visual Arts in the U.K. He currently
lives on Cape Breton Island, Nova Scotia.

Hair 

Beau Dick, Valie Export, Geertje Geertsma, Tyler Bright Hilton, Nicole Lefaivre, Jeremy 
Mendes, Steven Shearer 

Opening Reception: Friday, July 19th, 6-9pm

Macaulay & Co. Fine Art is thrilled to present to HAIR.

Hair is an object of considerable elaboration and preoccupation in almost all societies. 
Hairstyles and rituals surrounding hair care and adornment convey powerful messages 
about a persons beliefs, lifestyles, and commitments, and in some cases, social status. 
Inferences and judgments about a persons morality, sexual orientation and proclivity, 
political persuasion, and religious beliefs can sometimes be surmised by a particular 
hairstyle. Because of its versatility, hair can not only symbolize social norms but also 
changes in social ideologies. It can also signify acts of defiance, political resistance, and 
solidarity. Past uses of hair as a means of social control and dehumanization have 
influenced the meaning of hair taking by the state; however, hair cutting can also be a 
nuanced act of caring between friends and loved ones.

Artists

Beau Dick 
Kwakwakawakw Hereditary Chief Beau Dick draws on a deep knowledge of ceremonial 
Kwakwakawakw culture, giving us face-time with the mythological with a pantheon of 
masks. At the same time, Dick mediates the space between aesthetics, social utility, and 
the art for sale condition. Beau Dick, acclaimed as one of the Northwest Coasts most 
versatile and influential carvers, was born on Village Island, Kingcome Inlet, BC in 1955. 
Beau passed away in 2017, and he is greatly missed by all as a teacher, a legendary 
artist and storyteller, family man, and friend.

Valie Export
When she changed her name in 1967 to VALIE EXPORT, the Austrian artist Waltraud 
Hllinger (ne Lehner) renounced the names of her father and her former husband"
tokens of patriarchal ownership"and transformed herself into a brand identity. Almost 
immediately after this break, EXPORT, then 27, began to develop a body of the most 
important experimental feminist art of the postwar period, exploring the nexus of 
relationships among politics, experience, and personal identity. Since these early 
influential works, EXPORT continued to develop means of artistic expression that are 
both exhilaratingly direct and utterly mediated. 

Geertje Geertsma 
Geertje Geertsmas visual work takes the viewer to a world that is anthropomorphic, 
zoomorphic,herbamorphic, and fungimorphic. She is influenced by the transformational 
processes of symbiosis between man, animal, and Mother Earth. Through her work, 
she explores the feeling of what happens when we remove the barriers between 
ourselves and other life forms to unveil the strange conenction from within. Geertje 
Geertsma grew up in the countryside of Friesland, in the north of the Netherlands, 
where she felt a deep connection between herself and her environment. She graduated 
from Minerva Art Academy in Groningen, the Netherlands in 2015 and exhibits 
nationally and internationally.

Tyler Bright Hilton
Tyler Bright Hilton expresses a range of emotions illuminating the human condition in his 
narrative print
series and in figurative drawings. He makes work of great beauty, and magical power 
using both classical
and contemporary techniques. Born in 1979 in Ontario, Canada, Hilton received a BFA 
from the Ontario College of Art and Design, Toronto, and an MFA from the Chelsea 
College of Art and Design, London. His work has been exhibited within Canada and 
internationally, and is represented in the public collections of the Montreal Museum of 
Fine Arts, Montreal, and the Archive of Modern Conflict, London, as well as numerous 
private collections of note. He currently lives and works in Toronto, Ontario.

Jeremy Mendes | Nicole Lefaivre
The work, The Universal Truth was created by Jeremy mendes and Nicole Lefaivre in 
2018, as part of a collection of online shorts produced by the National Film Board of 
Canada. Nicole Lefaivre is a hair stylist and sole proprietor of Suedehead salon in 
Vancouver, BC. Jeremy Mendes is a director and producer of digital media projects and 
based in Vancouver, BC. 

Steven Shearer
Steven Shearer (b. 1968) was born in New Westminster, Canada and lives and works in 
Vancouver. He studied at Emily Carr in Vancouver (1992) and at the NASCAD New York 
Summer Studio Programme (1991). Shearer is a contemporary artist based in 
Vancouver, British Columbia. He has exhibited internationally, including the Tate Modern 
in London, The New Museum, the Barbara Gladstone Gallery in New York, the Galerie 
Eva Presenhuber in Zurich, and the Renaissance Society in Chicago. Nationally, he has 
shown at the Art Gallery of Ontario in Toronto and the Contemporary Art Gallery in 
Vancouver. He is represented in London, New York, Turin, and Zurich.

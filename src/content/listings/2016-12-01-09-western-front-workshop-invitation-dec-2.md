Aleesa Cohene and Western Front invite young artists who are currently working 
with or have an interest in video, media, film, gender, social politics and/or visual 
art to participate in a workshop series that will investigate and address 
constructions of race, class and gender in Hollywood cinema. The project 
considers these topics as part of a mutual inquiry into subject matter, 
constructions of identity, and the role of the producer as interpreter, narrator and 
intermediary. We actively seek artists who identify with the critical analysis and 
concerns of the materials at hand, and especially encourage Black, Indigenous 
and other POC who have an interest to apply.

The workshop will include three main activities: a reading and discussion group, a 
tutorial on viewing and dissecting films in Adobe Premiere, and a series of related 
working sessions to create components of a new project. This intensive workshop 
aims to generate a group dialogue about the nature of biases in popular forms of 
cinema, and is an opportunity to develop tools for intervening in the narratives 
and their attendant ideologies.

DATES and TIMES

Saturdays, January 29 and February 4, 10am - 4pm

AN HONORARIUM of $200 will be awarded to each participant in recognition of 
their contribution to the group. Space is limited to 8 individuals.

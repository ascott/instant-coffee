Spatial Poetics and the Powell Street Festival Society present:

Street Surgery

a performance by Kim Cooper and an installation by Natalie Purschwitz

on Saturday, July 12 at 7:30 pm to see the performance.

Street Surgery is a lighthearted attempt to investigate the theme of construction in Vancouver.

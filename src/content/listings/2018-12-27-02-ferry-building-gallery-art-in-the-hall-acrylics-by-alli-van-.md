Acrylics by Alli van Gruen
November 27, 2018 - February 21, 2019

Location:
Ferry Building Gallery
West Vancouver Municipal Hall - Lower Atrium
750 17th Street, West Vancouver

Alli van Gruen received her fine arts diploma from the Ontario College of Art and 
Design (OCAD) in Toronto, Ontario. Part of her studies included a year abroad at the 
college's satellite campus in Florence, Italy. It was in Italy that she was able to 
explore and get comfortable with her expressionistic style in both oil and acrylic 
paints. Since then, colour and texture figure quite prominently in her paintings. 
Painting for Alli has always been a process of exploration and discovery. Using her 
intuition and imagination, she applies layer upon layer of multiple colours to achieve 
the desired ethereal affect in her work.

After graduating from OCAD, Alli moved out west to marry and took a break from her 
painting career to settle into a new home and raise a family. She has now lived in 
Whistler for over 20 years with her husband and two children.

Alli's days are filled spending time with family and friends, exploring the outdoors 
and looking for creative inspiration. Alli works from her studio in Whistler, BC, and 
her works can be found at Art Junction Gallery, Whistler and in many private 
collections across Canada.

The exhibition is free. Viewing hours are 8 a.m."4:30 p.m. Monday to Friday

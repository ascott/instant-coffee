Exhibition Storytelling with Ed Hall
Burnaby Art Gallery
6344 Deer Lake Ave

Sunday, April 7th: 1pm, 2pm and 3pm

For the final afternoon of Exhibition Storytelling, former chief of the Kwikwetlem First
Nation Ed Hall will share stories and reflections in response to the artwork of Roy
Henry Vickers. This free, family friendly, drop in event is open to all, and allows for us
to reflect on how water, stories, and land are interconnected.

Exhibition Storytelling is one way in which visitors are invited to engage with the
current exhibition on view. The Burnaby Art Gallery is proud to be home to one of the
largest public holdings of limited edition prints by renowned artist Roy Henry Vickers.
Roy Henry Vickers is an award-winning Indigenous artist of Tsimshian, Haida, Heiltsuk,
and English ancestry. Born in the northern village of Laxgaltsap (Greenville), Vickers
has spent a lifetime drawing inspiration from the Gitxsan, Wet'suwet'en, Gitxaala, Tla-
o-qui-aht, and Lekwungen traditional territories he has called home.

For more information, please visit:
https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/arts-and-culture-facilities/burnaby-art-
gallery/exhibitions/roy-henry-vickers

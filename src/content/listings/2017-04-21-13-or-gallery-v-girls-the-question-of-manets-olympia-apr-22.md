V-Girls: The Question of Manet's Olympia
Apr. 22, 2017 at the Or Gallery 7 pm

In conjuncton with the release of the Hotel Theory Reader, Fillip, REDCAT, and 
the Or Gallery are pleased to present a free screening of The Question of 
Manet's Olympia: Posed and Skirted (1989"92) a filmed performative lecture by 
the V-Girls at the Or Gallery, Vancouver, on Saturday, April 22, at 7 pm.

Hotel Theory is the fourth title in Fillips ongoing Folio Series presenting writing by 
critics, artists, and curators that engages specific and recurring questions on 
international contemporary art. This anthology explores the possibilities of theory 
as an art form, bringing together ideas initially explored in an exhibition organized 
in 2015 at REDCAT | CalArts Downtown Center for Contemporary Arts. The book 
assembles a collection of texts by David Antin, Art & Language, Ruth Estvez, 
Bruce Hainley, Wayne Koestenbaum, Chris Kraus, Snejanka Mihaylova, Sohrab 
Mohebbi, Cally Spooner, V-Girls, Danna Vajda, and Tirdad Zolghadr.

V-Girls was a feminist performance group based in New York. Active between 
1986 and 1996, its members included Martha Baer, Jessica Chalmers, Erin 
Cramer, Andrea Fraser, and Marianne Weem. The group created satirical scenes 
of staged art discourse, organizing performances in the form of academic panels, 
fictional papers, and humorous critiques of art historical analysis.

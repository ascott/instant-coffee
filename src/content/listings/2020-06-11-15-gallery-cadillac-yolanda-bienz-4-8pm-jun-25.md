The new gallery is located at the ground floor of the Del Mar Inn Hotel at 555 Hamilton 
Street in the heart of Downtown. The owner Michael Riste has renovated and enlarged 
the former venue of the Or Gallery and created a bright, clean, contemporary, 1,500 
square feet space which becomes once again available to artists to show their works.

On Tuesday, June 23rd, the Cadillac Gallery is opening its door, showing the artwork of 
Yolanda Bienz, a surrealist painter, followed by current works of established artist Martin 
Guderna. Both artists live and work in Vancouver.

Grand Opening: Thursday, June 25th, 4 -- 8 p.m. | Yolanda Bienz

Gallery Hours:
Monday -- Sunday, 11 a.m. -- 6 p.m.

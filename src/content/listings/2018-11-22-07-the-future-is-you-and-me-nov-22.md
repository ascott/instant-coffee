We are seeking 10 to 12 creative women of colour to participate in the Winter 2019 
session of The Future. The program gives young women of colour opportunities to 
develop confidence, creative and professional skills and a network of like-minded peers 
and mentors.

The Future Is You and Me is a program designed to support young women of colour to 
take on leadership positions in creative and arts organizations. Our goal is to engage a 
group of young and creative women to build skills through mentorship and workshops 
facilitated by the leaders of today. The program also provides networking opportunities 
for participants to form relationships with established creative professionals.

If you're passionate about exploring the arts through the lenses of gender and race, join 
us and connect with a like-minded community of women.Were gonna have candid and 
challenging conversations; were going to learn from each other; were gonna eat 
snacks; well be friends and mentors to one another. If youre still curious about 
attending, check out our website at the thefutureisyouandme.com. Here you can find 
information on our past cohorts, upcoming workshops, mentors, and application 
information.

If you have any questions about the program or the application, please email us at info 
[at] thefutureisyouandme.com.

Call for Canadian Artists
Spirit Square Public Art in Okanagan Lake Park, City of
Penticton
Penticton, British Columbia, Canada
Art budget: $45,000
Deadline for Submissions: 2 PM Tuesday September 23, 2008
Installation Completed: April 21, 2009

The Penticton Public Art Advisory Committee, on behalf of The
City of Penticton, in its partnership with the Province of British
Columbia's BC150 Spirit Square Program, is pleased to announce an
opportunity for Canadian artists to submit proposals for public art in
Penticton's Spirit Square. Artists or artist teams are invited to submit
proposals for three-dimensional public artwork in this open space park
on the southernmost shore of Okanagan Lake.
The City envisions the creation of artwork that will reflect the
uniqueness of the location and express the BC150th anniversary in
Penticton's Spirit Square. Artists are asked to consider the following
themes in their vision for the work:
a.. Bridging Cultures
b.. Celebration
c.. Environment
d.. Heritage
e.. Penticton's Identity
f.. Vision for the Future
g.. BC 150 Spirit Square Character

This opportunity is open to professional artists, artist teams
and design professionals who are:
* Established professional Canadian artists.
* Able to meet the creative, eligibility and performance criteria.
* Can demonstrate the skills to manage all phases of production
and implementation - from design to installation, and the ability to
meet timelines and work cooperatively with others.
For more information, and to download the application package,
visit the City of Penticton Website: www.penticton.ca and click on the
Public Art icon on the main page. The Public Art Advisory Committee will
manage the artist selection process. Contact Public.Art@penticton.ca for
information on this opportunity.

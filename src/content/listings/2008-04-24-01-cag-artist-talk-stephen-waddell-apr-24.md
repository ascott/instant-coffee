The Contemporary Art Gallery and the Contemporary Art Society of Vancouver co-present:

STEPHEN WADDELL

ARTIST TALK: Thursday, April 24th at 7pm

Stephen Waddell is a photographer based in both Vancouver and Berlin. These two cities provide the subjects and motivations for his work. His artist talk on April 24th will look at aspects of his solo exhibition at the Contemporary Art Gallery, elaborating on the influence of painting and film within his photographic practice. Curated by Roy Arden, the exhibition presents work spanning from 1997 to 2008, illustrating the start of his career as a painter and his shift to photography. His formal and subject interests have been constant throughout his career, regularly using solitary figures captured unknowingly in unidentifiable urban environments to build complex compositions.

The Stephen Waddell exhibition runs until Sunday, June 1, 2008. The exhibition is accompanied by an illustrated catalogue featuring texts by Arden and German writer Katrin Blum.

 This exhibition is supported by Phil Lind, Lucia Lundin, Donna G. Molby and Robin Vousden.

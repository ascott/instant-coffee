In conversation: Paul Kajander and Colleen Brown (moderated by Vanessa Kwan) 
Thursday, October 15th
7pm
Fillip 
305 Cambie Street
www.fillip.ca

Paul and Colleen will be talking of work, discussing ideas and chatting with you. Do come. Drinks.

Paul Kajander's cross-disciplinary works in installation, video, performance and drawing have 
been exhibited in Vancouver, Los Angeles and Tokyo. His activity with the bands The Emanglons 
and Kick Evrything--as well as the Directorship of the Helen Pitt Gallery Artist Run Centre-- each 
represent an important extension of his practice. 

Colleen Brown is an artist and writer living in Vancouver. Prior to her involvement with the art 
community, Colleen received her BA in Psychology and a diploma in Electrophysiology. After 
completing her BFA at Emily Carr Institute, Colleen worked as the Programme Coordinator at 
Artspeak for five years. She is currently studying sculpture at Bard College and will receive her
Master's degree in 2010. Her work has been exhibited in British Columbia and Quebec. 

Vanessa Kwan is a Vancouver-based artist and writer. Her work has been exhibited at a number 
of galleries, including solo exhibitions at the Or Gallery, Access, and the Art Gallery of the South 
Okanagan. She is a founding member of the arts collective Norma, and currently works as Public 
Programs Coordinator at the Vancouver Art Gallery. A public art commission for the City of 
Vancouver is forthcoming in 2010.

In association with the exhibition Black Hole is Also Supernova: Colleen Brown, Paul Kajander 
and Kara Uzelman
Curated by Vanessa Kwan
17 September - 1 November 2009
Richmond Art Gallery

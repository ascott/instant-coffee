Vancouvers Equinox Gallery, one of Canadas pre-eminent private galleries that 
features a dynamic program of exhibitions by artists it represents as well as works from 
the secondary market, is seeking an enthusiastic member for its tight, energetic team.

POSITION SUMMARY
The Gallery Manager reports to the Gallery Director. Working closely with other staff, 
oversees and executes all of the administrative functions of the gallery that allow it to 
function smoothly and efficiently"from communication systems, 
databases/archives/library, insurance, office supplies and book-keeping to contact with 
visitors, artists, collectors, artworld peers and vendors. This full-time position is the 
public face of the gallery and the incumbent will have excellent interpersonal and 
administrative skills with a positive, team-oriented, professional approach. Normal 
workdays are Tuesday through Saturday with some extended hours as required for 
openings, special events and other purposes.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Welcomes clients and gallery visitors

Manages and coordinates office operations including: creating and maintaining 
physical and digital filing systems; managing and updating client database; cataloguing 
works in art inventory; maintaining office supplies and equipment; overseeing all office 
IT and telecommunications support including hardware and software renewal; managing 
and responding to correspondence, emails and telephone calls; regularly updating 
website; maintaining a professional reception area

Financial management including: general ledger accounting; bill payments; producing 
and tracking invoices; timely payments to artists and consignees; negotiating vendor 
rates; producing monthly receivable/payable and sales reports 

Ensures physical and digital security systems are running smoothly and are updated 
as needed; assesses insurance needs and procures coverage

Develops and executes an overall marketing plan including coordinating production of 
print and online marketing materials such as press releases, ads, announcements, 
social media posts

Maintains and updates artist archives and organizes the library

Recruits and manages interns 

Tracks and monitors artworks available at auction; assists in negotiating and 
overseeing purchases through private sale and auction

Coordinates special projects such as commissions and specific client needs

Event coordination with outside vendors for openings and special events

Other needs as they arise. 

QUALIFICATIONS
2+ years of management experience in a visual arts organization preferably a private 
gallery context

Post-secondary education in art history or related field with a working knowledge of 
contemporary art

Excellent administrative skills, including experience in book keeping, running an office, 
record maintenance, developing materials for various communications platforms

Proficient with MAC, Microsoft Office suite, Filemaker

Experience in creating marketing plans especially for social media

Exceptional written and verbal skills and experience with the public, Mandarin 
language an asset

Attention to detail, follow-through and outstanding diplomatic and interpersonal skills

Positive demeanor and keen to grow in a fast-paced, collaborative environment

Knowledge of conducting market valuations and appraisals an asset

YOUR APPLICATION

If you have the required qualifications for this position, please send your application 
including a cover letter and CV to info@equinoxgallery.com by midnight Monday April 
22, 2019 with the subject: Gallery Manager. Only those candidates selected for an 
interview will be contacted. Remuneration is commensurate with experience. Start date 
is May 2019.

Call for Applications
2015 Public Artist Roster Competition
Washington State Arts Commission
Olympia, WA
Eligibility: Open to artists in the U.S. and British Columbia
Deadline: June 23, 2015, 5 pm PST

This competition establishes a roster of professional artists eligible for
commissions of site-responsive works of art at K-12 schools, colleges,
universities, and state agencies throughout Washington State.

For more information and submission guidelines:
http://www.arts.wa.gov/public-art/2015-roster-competition

For questions regarding the submission requirements or process, contact the
Washington State Arts Commission by email at
mike.sweney@ARTS.WA.GOV<mailto:mike.sweney@ARTS.WA.GOV>.

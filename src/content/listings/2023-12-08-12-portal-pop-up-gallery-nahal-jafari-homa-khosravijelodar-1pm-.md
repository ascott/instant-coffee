Nahal Jafari + Homa Khosravijelodar
November 24 - December 15, 2023
Reception: December 9, 1-3pm

To launch our new ongoing window space at 407 W Cordova, we present the work of
two Vancouver-based emerging Iranian women artists, Nahal Jafari and Homa
Khosravijelodar.

Nahal Jafari's evocative sculptures are responsive to the ongoing struggles/conflicts
in the world. Delving into the realms of physical and psychological trauma, Jafari's
art explores the profound and transformative impact of our experiences, leaving
indelible marks on our psyche and body. These unseen imprints, a testament to the
weight of perception, serve as a poignant reminder of the enduring echoes of our
journey.

Homa Khosravijelodars painting Raspberry Bush blurs boundaries between reality
and dreamscape. This surrealistic study inspired by the natural world beckons
viewers to navigate the layers of paint, constructing their own narratives.
In this
artistic journey, one finds a sacred space for reflection, contemplating themes of
mortality and decay.

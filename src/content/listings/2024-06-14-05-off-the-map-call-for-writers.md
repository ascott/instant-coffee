CALL FOR WRITERS

Off the Map: Vancouver Writers with Lived Experience of Mental Health Issues

Deadline for submissions: June 15, 2024

Off the Map is a forthcoming anthology that will showcase Vancouver writers with lived
experience of mental health and/or substance use issues. The collection aims to
highlight the unique voices and diversity of writing among individuals with lived
experience of these issues, and introduce new talent to the literary community.

The anthology, which will be published by Bell Press in 2025, will be edited by Betsy
Warland, Seema Shah, and Kate Bird.

For more information and submission guidelines, visit:
www.bellpressbooks.com/submissions

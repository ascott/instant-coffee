Artist Talk with Tony Robins at Elan Fine Art

Sunday, October 14th, 2 pm

Tony Robins' exhibition Transmediation opened on September 29th at Elan
Fine Art. Transmediation is the process of translating a work into a
different medium, his work explores the new significance, reception, and
approach brought to light by this unexpected narrative process.

Tony Robins, Vancouver's well-known architect and writer, recipient of the
prestigious Prix de Rome and Western Living's "Architect of the Year"
award, Tony's design and writing has gained international recognition.
Tony's body of art can be viewed as an extension of not only his
architecture, but also his passion towards investigation in screenwriting,
fiction and art. His works, often minimalist, portray poignant
psychological and historical juncture, paralleled with monumental
contextual significance.

Please join us for this artist talk with Tony Robins, he will be speaking
about his bold referential approach to everything from art history,
literature, cinema, to psychoanalysis in his artwork.

The Fort Gallery is pleased to present
its annual auction fundraiser to be held at the
gallery and online, November 12-December 6. The annual auction is integral to
sustaining and growing the gallery, with proceeds from artwork sales put towards
programming and operational costs, public outreach and education, and thought
provoking exhibitions that foster critical conversations in the community.

This years fundraiser will look a bit different, with the auction being held in the gallerys
physical space and online over several days, instead of the traditional evening of live
music and dancing at the Fort Langley Community Hall; but the variety and quality of
artwork available at the auction will endure.

Bidding will be available in person at the gallery November 12-December 6 and online
the last week of the exhibition through

Instagram: [https://www.instagram.com/fortgallery/](https://www.instagram.com/fortgallery/)
To bid online, simply add your bid to the posted artwork in the comments.

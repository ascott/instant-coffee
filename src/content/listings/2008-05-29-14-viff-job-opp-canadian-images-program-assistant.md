Canadian Images Program Assistant
$9.50 / hour
35 hours per week - June 16 - August 29

Through the federal government's Canada Summer Jobs program, the Vancouver International Film Festival has received funding for this position.	To be eligible, the applicant must be between 15 and 30 years of age and  have been registered as a full-time student in the previous academic year and intend to return to school on a full-time basis in the next academic year.

Canadian Images is one of the main programming strands of the Vancouver International Film Festival.  It may, in fact, be the largest annual exhibition of new Canadian cinema in the world.   Each year several hundred entries are submitted for programming consideration.  The final line-up will consist of approximately 100 films.

Job Description:
The Canadian Images Program Coordinator will work in the Canadian Images department at the Vancouver International Film Festival assisting the department staff with administration and programming duties. Applicants should have excellent communication skills and be detailed oriented with a film, arts or administrative background.

 Responsibilities:
- Database management of film entries and contacts
- Previewing Submissions
- Coordination of Pre-Screening Committee
- Assist in coordination of film publicity materials and media conference
- Collection and entry of credit and image information for program guide
- Coordination of filmmaker materials
- Film research
- Filing
- General office administrative duties
 
Qualifications:
- Interest in Canadian Cinema
- Interest in Arts Administration
- Excellent communication skills
- Detailed Oriented
- Ability to work independently or as a team
- Ability to work under pressure
- Computer - PC platform, Microsoft Office
- FileMaker Pro -  an asset
- Photoshop - an asset
- French Language Skills - an asset

If you are interested and are eligible under the Canada Summer Jobs criteria detailed above, please send your resumé to viff@viff.org by 5:00pm, Friday, June 6th, putting "Canadian Images Program Assistant" in the subject field of the email.  If you are short-listed, you will be contacted for an interview for the week of June 9.

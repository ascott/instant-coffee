Republic Gallery
 Tel: 604-632-1590
 www.republicgallery.com

 INVITE:

 A Trip to the Moon is a group exhibition featuring photographs, sculpture 
 and mixed media work by Maggie Groat, Jimmy Limit and Andrea 
 Pinheiro. 

 The exhibition will include Maggie Groat’s Moonlight Reflectors or A 
 Proposal for Returning Moonlight Back to the Moon (June 13th, 2014), 
 photographs from Jimmy Limit’s Show Room series, which will be shown 
 alongside his slipcast ceramic works, and two new inkjet works from 
 Andrea Pinheiro, Bridge and Ladder. 

 A Trip to the Moon opens to the public on Thursday, June 26th and runs 
 through Saturday, August 16, 2014. Gallery hours are 10am to 5pm, 
 Wednesday through Saturday, and by appointment.

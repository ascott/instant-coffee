Exhibition: Before Demolition, Emily Neufeld
Opening Reception: Friday, March 23, 2018, 6-8 P.M.
Location: Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art, 103-421 Cawston Avenue, Kelowna 
B.C.

Join the Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art on Friday, March 23 2018, 6-8 P.M. for 
the opening of North Vancouver artist Emily Neufelds Before Demolition. 

Before Demolition examines the link between a persons identity and their location. 
Using homes slated for demolition, Neufeld performs interventions in the space dictated 
by the physical marks and patterns -- wear patterns in carpet, cut marks on the counter 
" that residents inadvertently left behind while living there. The resulting large-scale 
sculptures are documented and the homes surrendered to developers to be demolished, 
only to be remembered in photographs. The exhibition explores our complex 
relationship with the built environment, private property and land ownership with 
particular focus on contemporary issues including the rising cost of accommodation and 
increased homelessness. 

Emily Neufeld received her BFA from Emily Carr University of Art and Design in 2013, 
and has exhibited throughout BC and Alberta.

This event is free and open to the public. Before Demolition will be on show at the 
Alternator from March 23 " April 5 2018. 

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is an artist-run centre located in Kelowna, 
B.C. at the Rotary Centre for the Arts, 421 Cawston Avenue. The Alternator is a 
registered non-profit charitable organization dedicated to the development of the 
creative community. Since 1989 the Alternator has shown the work of emerging 
Canadian artists and focused on innovative and non-traditional mediums engaged in 
social and cultural issues.

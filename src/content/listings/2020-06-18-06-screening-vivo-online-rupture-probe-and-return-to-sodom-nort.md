Rupture Probe and Return to Sodom North
ONLINE SCREENINGS
Rupture Probe and Return to Sodom North
July 18-19, 7-9PM, 2020
FREE

Two evening programs of videos by Vancouver and Canadian artists

Rupture Probe, 53min, Canada
Streaming July 18, 7PM PDT

Return to Sodom North, 65min, Canada
Streaming July 19, 7PM PDT

Rupture Probe and Return to Sodom North explore the continuity of themes
found in Queer Video produced two decades apart. Return to Sodom North
revisits Vancouver Queer Video art from the 1990s; at Video In and elsewhere, a
new generation of queer artists were expanding the west coast response to
ongoing racialized and gendered suppression of
queer bodies, expression, and
desire.

Rupture Probe features recent works that probe transgender and non-binary
experience, new erotic signifiers, and inventive strategies for dissent and
celebration.

Sign up: https://queerartsfestival.com/rupture-probe-media-night-with-vivo/

A Co-Presentation of VIVO Media Arts Centre and Queer Arts Festival 2020

Dear friends of the Or Gallery,

We hope you have all been well while social distancing over the last couple of months.
When Or Gallery closed temporarily in March to comply with the regulations, our
upcoming exhibition schedule was put on hold.

In response to the recent guidelines from the provincial government we are delighted to
announce that we will re-open the gallery on June 2.

The exhibition Pao Houa Her: Emplotment will be on view until Saturday June 27.

In the interim, we have been working closely with Her and guest curator Godfre Leung
to present an on-line program featuring Hers work as a photo essay. Youll also find
documentation of the public art project After the Fall of Hmong Tebchaw, a series of
photographs displayed on ten transit shelters around the city of Vancouver. Featured as
part of the 2020 Capture Photography Festival, After the Fall of Hmong Tebchaw is on
view until June 14 (view map).

Over the next few weeks we will add more content on-line, including documentation of
the new multisensory installation Green Rush, and a video-conversation with the artist.

On June 2, gallery opening hours will return to normal. Our bookstore will remain closed
until further notice. To ensure safe social distancing, a maximum of 4 people will be
allowed in the gallery at one time.

We look forward to seeing you in June!

new landscape paintings

by Ben Stephenson

at Liquidation World 1965 Main St.

Final week of exhibition

Gallery Hours:

Wednesday & Thursday 4-7pm

Saturday & Sunday 2-5pm

At a distance, it appears as if the earth has switched on a spotlight. An internal, fast-
flowing subterranean river. A massive man-made sinkhole burning methane gas for
decades. The player navigates the environment via pre-rendered still images, quickly
learning that something has gone wrong"when we gesture at a landscape or pass
an aestheticizing hand over it, are we talking about the land itself, or its
representation? Its difficult to think of anything more palpable or vivid than the
internment of the Group of Seven in the hallowed ground of the McMichael. Viewed
from below, sakura appear to pour down like a waterfall; each weighs more than
250,000 tons and contains 99 elevators and 21,800 windows. The largest known
cave passage by volume. Well-managed working forests contribute to biodiversity, to
our communities, to our economy, and to climate resiliency, bringing value to our
customers via a continuous, long-term supply of the wood products they want and
need. Efficient floor plans, abundant parking, natural light, dramatic mountain views,
and a vastness typical of the Martian landscape. By 2100, Antarcticas ice loss is
expected to add eleven centimetres to global sea-levels, rarely presented as a
simple backdrop, but rather as a complexly storied thing with a distinct personality.
Its been said that camel spiders, seduced by its warmth, will approach and plummet
to their deaths. It is common to see people transfixed in the street, photographing
the blossoms from many different angles. For a brief period upon their completion in
1973, they are the tallest buildings in the world. Then"just like clouds"they
suddenly disappear.

Ben Stephenson is an artist and a writer. He is the author of the novel A Matter of
Life and Death Or Something"which was nominated for the IMPAC Dublin
International Literary Award and prompted CBC Books to name him one of Ten
Canadian Writers to Watch"and the story collection Good Dog. His work has
appeared in Best Canadian Stories, Joyland, The Ex-Puritan, EVENT, and King
Skateboard Magazine. He earned a BFA from NSCAD and an MFA in Creative
Writing from UBC. He lives on land that settler colonialists stole from the Coast
Salish peoples, in Vancouver, BC, where he teaches kids to write and make books,
and he is the founder and publisher of L.A.S.E.R. Press. new landscape paintings is
his first show of visual art in fifteen years.

All This Time
Online Exhibition/Blog
Launching on July 8th @ number3gallery.com
In response to the various cultural shifts that occurred this spring, Vancouver based
curatorial project Number 3 Gallery initiated a blog space that doubles as an online
exhibition. Based on an open call for submissions, #3 invited participants of any creative
genre to submit evidence of the ways in which they managed to adapt their energies,
expectations and creative practices under new or restructured circumstances. The blog
highlights undersung instances of slow practice such as routines, traditional and craft-
based methods of making, self teaching, exploration and methods of self regulation. All
this time will function as an ongoing project with the intention of contributing to existing
discourses around ability, institutional critique and the ways in which value is ascribed.

Number 3 Gallery is a mobile curatorial project focused on initiating tangential
collaborations with contemporary artists and spaces. As a satirical response to the lack
of feasible real-estate in Vancouvers economic landscape, #3s projects take place in a
variety of contexts and question the ways in which access determines success and
visibility.

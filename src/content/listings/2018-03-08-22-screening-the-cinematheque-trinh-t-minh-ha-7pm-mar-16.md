Forgetting Vietnam Vancouver premiere
Talk and Q&A with Trinh T. Minh-ha

Centre A and the Cinematheque welcome renowned filmmaker, writer, and scholar Trinh 
T. Minh-ha for the Vancouver premiere of her film, Forgetting Vietnam (2015). Drawing 
on ancient stories of Vietnams creation, this lyrical film essay moves between Hi-8 
footage shot in 1995 and digital footage filmed in 2012. Images of contemporary life in 
Vietnam unfold in a dialogue between land and water, the two elements that form the 
Vietnamese term for country, -'t nc. Through the experiences of local inhabitants, 
immigrants, and veterans, Forgetting Vietnam honours the survivors of the Vietnam War 
and commemorates the 40th anniversary of the wars end.
Trinh T. Minh-ha will be in attendance to introduce and discuss Forgetting Vietnam. A 
reception will be held afterwards in The Cinematheque lobby. Copies of Trinh's most 
recent book, Lovecidal: Walking with the Disappeared (2016), will be available for 
purchase.

Post-screening Q&A moderated by Helen Leung, Professor of Gender, Sexuality & 
Women's Studies at SFU.

LONDON RESIDENCY OPPORTUNTY

In response to increasing requests from artists Acme Artists Studios have
launched a new residency initiative: The Associate Artist Programme (AAP).
AAP operates alongside our existing international partner programmes and
allows international artists for the first time to apply directly to Acme
for London residency opportunities.

AAP residencies are tailored to suit the networking needs of international
artists, allowing them to research, develop relationships and focus on
their work in a supported environment using the residency framework as a
base to explore London; one of the most creative and dynamic cultural
capitals in the world.

Residencies are for one, two or three months duration and may be realised
around a particular project or focused primarily on networking.
Applications are accepted throughout the year and further information,
including application forms can be downloaded from Acmes website:
www.acme.org.uk/assoc-artist-prog.php

Moving Matter

Robinson Studio Gallery
Suite 440 " 1000 Parker St. Vancouver BC
Opening: Saturday November 4th, 2-5pm
Gallery hours: November 7th " December 1st, Tuesday to Friday, 11am-4pm and by 
appointment

Please join us for the opening of Moving Matter on Saturday November 4th at Robinson 
Studio Gallery in Vancouver. Were excited to be showing Davids work along with two 
Parker Street Studio peers- Stefany Hemming and Cybele Ironside. 

From enchanting visual fields to complex, entangled objects, Robinson, Hemming and 
Ironside investigate a convergence of matter and movement, producing evocative forms 
in paint and sculpture. Approaching diverse materials and subject matter through 
common modes of dedicated enquiry, these three artists push the boundaries of their 
own practices in unique ways. While Ironside uses multiple layers of paint on panel to 
explore the mysterious sublime of the Canadian landscape, Hemming's paintings reveal 
detailed forms"masses of entangled shapes"in sharp focus, offering a sense of 
complicated refuge. Robinsons sculptures explore a spirited sense of gestural 
movement and materiality through various media, investigating the interior space of the 
figure in dynamic, oftentimes precarious situations. Using intense material processes of 
shaping, layering and uncovering, the artists give form to spaces of inward reflection.

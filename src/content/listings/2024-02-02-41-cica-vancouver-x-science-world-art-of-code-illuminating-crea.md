We are thrilled to announce a second collaborative educational program with
Science World: Art of Code: Illuminating Creativity with Micro:bits.

We invite art and science loving families to embark on a unique learning experience
with Science World and CICA Vancouver, where art and technology will converge to
present an unprecedented immersive educational experience. Art of Code:
Illuminating Creativity with Micro:bits is an interactive workshop designed to
empower the next generation of creative minds and young coders, especially as it
encourages creativity through the reflection of James Turrells monumental
installations
in an intimate and family-friendly environment. No prior coding
experience required.

The itinerary of the program is as follows:

'Introduction and a family-friendly guided tour of the current exhibition,

'Self-guided exploration of the gallery, with creative worksheets provided by us

'Creative Coding Workshop with block coding and Micro:bits, led by Science
World

'Show-and-tell time!

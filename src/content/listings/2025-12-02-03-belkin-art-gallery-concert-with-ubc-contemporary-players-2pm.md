Concert with UBC Contemporary Players

Wednesday, December 3 at 2 pm

Join us in the gallery for a concert by UBC School of Music Contemporary Players inspired
by the current exhibition, Abbas Akhavan: One Hundred Years. Led by Director Paolo
Bortolussi, this graduate and undergraduate student ensemble from the UBC School of
Music will animate the gallery for an afternoon program celebrating themes and responding
to chosen works from this exhibition.

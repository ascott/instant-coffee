The City of Vancouver and the PNE are excited to announce a new call exclusively for
artists or artist teams from xmky"m (Musqueam), swxw7mesh (Squamish), and
slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations to submit Expressions of
Interest for creating three
different Coast Salish artworks at the new PNE amphitheatre. Panelists will select three
artists or artist teams who will each be supported to create a concept and design for the
different opportunities.

Deadline to apply is October 3rd.

To learn more about the call and to apply, please go here: Indigenous artist call for PNE
Amphitheatre | City of Vancouver

Creative Collages: Exploring Abstraction, Design and Painting with Renee Van 
Halm

Saturday January 14, 2017
Age: Kids and Family
Cost: $125 + GST | 10% discount for Members
Registration: Call 604.962.0413 ext. 109, or email 
admissions@audainartmuseum.com

Join contemporary artist Renee Van Halm for our Winter artist-led workshop. 
Through an introduction to collage, Van Halm will illustrate how dcor, fashion 
and architectural space govern contemporary experiences, guiding participants in 
the creation of an abstracted painting inspired by their own unique collaged 
compositions.

Mitch Speed
Styles of Behaviour

Opening Reception Wednesday September 11th, 7-11pm
Sunset Terrace
2028 Clark Drive
Vancouver 

Please join us for the opening of an exhibition of new work from Vancouver 
based artist Mitch Speed. The exhibition will be comprised of a series of wall 
scale charcoal drawings, and smaller oil paintings on paper. In the former, 
glyphs of uncertain origin float un-moored, while also participating in pattern. 
In the latter, colour and shape form idiosyncratic, self-contained pictorial 
structures. 

Here, shadows and silhouettes have been plucked from visual experience -- 
the artist's own work included -- and re-incorporated as characters in a new 
visual lexicon. The resultant works are less interpretive translations of the 
visual world, than of the process of interfacing with it. Their purpose, carried 
within the movements of their operation as artworks, and in the movements of 
their making, is to open an experiential region between blind affect and 
disenchantment.

CO-LAB Catalogue Launch and Closing Potluck
recorders
You are cordially invited
to the CO-LAB catalogue launch and closing potluck this Saturday 
from 3 - 7pm at Centre A. Come check out the exhibition for the last 
time, listen to the sound, and join us for some snacks and finger 
food before we close for the rest of the summer. The catalogue will 
be FREE on Saturday! You are welcome to bring anything that you'd 
like to share with other people, but it's not an obligation.
We hope to see you all!

-- We will be open again for the exciting fall season on Sept 13 with 
the world renowned, Korea based two-person collective YOUNG 
HAE CHANG HEAVY INDUSTRIES (YHCHI). Stay tuned for more 
information soon.

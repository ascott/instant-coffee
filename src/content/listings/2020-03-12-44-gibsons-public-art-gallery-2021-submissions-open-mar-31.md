Are you an artist with a body of work to exhibit? Or do you have a vision for a compelling
community group installation, and have the time and skill set required to put it all
together and turn the idea into reality? Here's an opportunity to make it all happen.
GPAG, on the beautiful Sunshine Coast, is accepting submissions for our 2021
exhibition season. Get your ideas down now and to the Gallery by March 31.

The Gibsons Public Art Gallery presents exhibitions in two gallery spaces, the Main
Gallery and the Eve Smart Gallery. GPAG is committed to providing the public access to
the best art that we can offer, from both on and off the Sunshine Coast. We support and
encourage diversity and have a commitment to represent
aboriginal artists' work. GPAG
continues to provide a professional venue for artists on the Sunshine Coast to exhibit
their work. Our aim is to be an active participant in the organization and presentation of
visual art and to promote a better understanding and appreciation of visual art through
education and public events. Our mission is to present exhibitions for the purpose of
promoting and encouraging public interest in contemporary art.

Deadline: March 31, 2020
Visit http://www.gpag.ca/submissions for further information.

Call to Artists: Pride Public Art Project

Request for Proposals (RFP 2018-29)

The City of Port Coquitlam seeks proposals for an artist-led, community-engaged public 
art project that recognizes and celebrates Port Coquitlams LGBTQ2+ community, and 
its presence and contribution to the social fabric of the city.

The City has a vision to be a happy, vibrant, safe community with healthy and engaged 
residents, and is guided by values and principles of accessibility, diversity and inclusion 
for all members of the community. The Pride Public Art project presents artists with an 
opportunity to develop an artwork that builds on these values, deepening relationships, 
creating a visual marker of presence of the LGBTQ2+ community, while providing a 
space for reflection, opportunities for dialogue and fostering inclusivity and community 
pride. The artist or artist team will lead the development and creation of the artwork and 
will engage members of the community in the artmaking process.

Deadline: January 29, 2019, 2:00pm (Pacific Time)

Budget: $17,500 

How to submit a proposal:

This call is open to professional artists and artist teams. Qualified artists will 
demonstrate an ability to work collaboratively with a community group, organization or 
public. Artists identifying as LGBTQ2+ are strongly encouraged to apply.

Download the call with all the information you need to submit a proposal: 
[https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/purchasing/current-opportunities/](https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/purchasing/current-opportunities/)

Studio Space available for Emerging Ceramic Artist

The James Black Gallery is an artist-run centre operating within the Main Street arts 
district of Vancouver, BC. The JBG presents exhibitions of local and international artists, 
provides studio space to members of our collective, and hosts interdisciplinary events. 
We also run a live-in guest residency program for international artists, who are given the 
opportunity to engage with the public through a solo exhibition of the works produced 
during their stay.

We currently have an opening for an emerging ceramic artist to join our studio collective. 
Ideally you would be someone with a committed art practice, who works creatively and 
experimentally in ceramics. For $210 per month, you would have a personal space in a 
studio shared by two others within our collective of six ceramic artists, with 24-hour 
access, as well as access to some slips and glazes, and a small cone 6 kiln fired by our 
Studio Head. Opportunities for group exhibitions and participation in JBG talks and 
events is available.

Our studio head can be reached at cosmoscoralie@gmail.com for a tour of the space.

Call for Curatorial Proposals

Deadline: November 14th, 2015

Gallery 295 has extended the deadline for our Annual Emerging Curator
Exhibition and is open to curators currently residing Vancouver and
surrounding areas. Now in its third year, the Emerging Curator Exhibition
will highlight the vision of an aspiring curator, and provide the
opportunity to realize an exhibition directed towards photographic
dialogues. Our 2015 emerging curator recipient, Francesca Szuszkiewicz,
featured new works by the artist Michael de Courcy, an exhibition that had
wide appeal within Vancouver's contemporary photographic community. Our
2014 emerging curator, Avalon Mott has since co-founded FIELD Contemporary
and continues to develop her independent curatorial practice.

Proposals Must Include:
- Curatorial Statement & Exhibition Proposal
- List of Works to be exhibited (10-20 images)
- Image List & Supporting Project Statements
- List of Artists including Bio & CV
- Curator Bio & CV

All proposals are juried by the Director & Curators of Gallery 295, as well
as the previous emerging curator recipient. All applicants will be notified
of our decision within two weeks following the submission deadline. Please
be advised, Gallery 295 operates completely by volunteers and we are unable
to offer CARFAC compliance at this time.

The Emerging Curator Exhibition runs from March 4th - April 2nd, 2016.

Please email your proposals to info@gallery295.com with the headline
"Emerging Curator Submission".

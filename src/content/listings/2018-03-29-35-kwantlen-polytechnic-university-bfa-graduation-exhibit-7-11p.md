until now - KPU's BFA Graduate Exhibition

Opening April 13 from 7pm-11pm at 120 E Broadway, 2nd floor, Vancouver

Exhibition runs April 13-28, Tuesday - Sunday from 12pm-6pm 

Kwantlen Polytechnic University's Faculty of Fine Arts annual BFA graduation exhibit 
"until now" showcases emerging artists from Surrey and Vancouver. Featuring 
painting, installation, sculpture, video, and carving in a pop-up gallery space, this 
year's graduates from KPU's intimate and supportive community highlight the cross-
disciplinary nature of the school's intensive four-year program. 

Works on display include Kenneth Yuen's interactive sculptures and photo 
documented onsite performance pieces about intimate gestures, physical objects, 
and the relationship between the two. Gurleen Virk, working in performance, 
explores the sometimes conflicting cultures and aesthetics of South Asia and the 
West in video and installation. Alanna Edwards, an artist of Mi'gmaq and settler 
descent, combines natural and synthetic materials in humorous ways, questioning 
ideas of authenticity through assemblage. Kimberly Ateah's lush, sensuous ceramics 
of botanical forms with open reproductive elements are metaphors for human 
sexuality. 

Painting is well represented in the exhibition. Sara Grieve's large scale oil paintings 
feature the female form in fragmented spaces full of decoration and patterns. Eric 
Berg, working in oil on panel, evocatively depicts figures in layered interior and 
exterior environments, referencing the interaction of thought and the material world. 
Stephane Dufault's colourful acrylic paintings skew the conventions and traditions of 
comics and animation, subverting the expectations of the audience. Cameron 
Palfreyman's black and white digital paintings were created using custom brushes 
sourced from photographs of everyday materials. 

Join us in celebrating the work of our grads with refreshments at this free event open 
to the public.

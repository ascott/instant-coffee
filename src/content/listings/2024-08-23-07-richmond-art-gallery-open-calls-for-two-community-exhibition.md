City Hall Galleria

Located just across the street from the Richmond Art Gallery and on the ground floor
of Richmond City Hall, City Hall Galleria is an offsite exhibition space overseen by
Richmond Art Gallery. As part of its outreach to the local
community, City Hall
Galleria primarily highlights artworks with a close connection to Richmond in terms of
themes and subject matter. Comprising two major walls, City Hall Galleria primarily
showcases framed or ready-to-hang two-dimensional artworks. There is also a 32
flat screen TV where videos can be displayed.

Deadline: August 30, 2024 for 2025 programming. We offer CARFAC fees for
Exhibitions in Other Public Places (2025 schedule): $571 for solo exhibitions; $286
for two-person exhibitions. Selected artists will be contacted by September 27, 2024.
Due to the volume of submissions, only selected artist will be contacted.
Submissions will be kept on file for a period of up to one year for consideration.

Submission Checklist
Brief bio
One-page (maximum) artist statement
One-page statement of proposed work
Five to seven JPEGS (up to 300 dpi and 2 MB each) of proposed work through file-
sharing software Google Drive or Dropbox. Make sure the link is open as multiple
staff may view
it using different emails
An image list with thumbnails of the work, artist name, title, medium, dimensions,
and year
Website and/or any social media accounts
Please email complete submissions to galleria@richmondartgallery.org with the
subject line City Hall Galleria Submission. If you need assistance or any
accommodations, please dont hesitate to reach out via email. For more info, please
visit: https://www.richmondartgallery.org/galleria-submission

The Annex Gallery
Located right across the main entrance of the Richmond Cultural Centre, and on the
ground floor of Richmond Cultural Centre Annex, The Annex Gallery is an offsite
exhibition space overseen by Richmond Art Gallery. As part of its outreach to the
local community, The Annex Gallery is dedicated exclusively to Richmond-based
artists. Comprising three major walls, The Annex Gallery primarily showcases
framed or ready-to-hang two-dimensional artworks. There is also a 32 flat screen
TV where videos can be displayed.

Deadline: August 30, 2024 for 2025 programming. We offer CARFAC fees for
Exhibitions in Other Public Places (2025 schedule): $571 for solo exhibitions; $286
for two-person exhibitions. Selected artists will be contacted by September 27, 2024.
Due to the volume of submissions, only selected artist will be contacted.
Submissions will be kept on file for a period of up to one year for consideration.

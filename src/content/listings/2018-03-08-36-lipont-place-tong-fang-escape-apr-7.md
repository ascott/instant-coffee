Lipont Place
Tong Fang: Escape

Opening: 2PM @ April 7
M - Su: 9 am - 5 pm

Fang Tongs Escape series continues her subjective and movie-narrative style with a 
little more romance and humour. Instead of staging the scenes, this time Fang Tong 
places herself, the traveller, in the real scenery, a real trip, but she intentionally made 
the scenes look surreal and staged. She is creating a temporary escape. Most people 
have flashes from the subconscious where they want to escape from their daily bonds 
and cares"not to run away, but to break free. We can call it dissociating by drifting 
away, creating an instantaneous sense of freedom, but then returning to realism with a 
better sense of harmony.

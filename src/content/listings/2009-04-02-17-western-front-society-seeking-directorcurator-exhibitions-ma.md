The Western Front is one of Canadaâ&#65533;&#65533;s pioneering artist-run centres and
produces and presents works in five programmes: Exhibitions, Performance
Art, New Music, Media Arts, and FRONT Magazine. The Western Front was
founded in 1973 by a small group of interdisciplinary artists, and has
developed into an exemplary multi-disciplinary environment for experimental
art practice and research. With a staff of ten plus interns and volunteers,

The Exhibitions Programme has a mandate to present contemporary visual art
by local, national and international artists. Intentionally open, this
mandate has historically focused on artwork that is anti-object,
conceptual, media-based or otherwise ephemeral in nature. Currently the
program promotes experimentation with conceptual models and contexts for
visual art that have allowed the program to expand beyond gallery
exhibitions to include artist books and posters, cross-disciplinary works,
and site-specific projects and commissions.

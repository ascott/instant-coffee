Transform the ordinary by redefining everyday materials in unexpected setting

Maplewood House, 399 Seymour River Place, North Vancouver BC V7H 1S6
Thursdays, 6.30pm : 8.30pm
February 8, 2024 (6 weeks)

Workshop Description:
Throughout this 6-week workshop series, participants will create a diverse array of
sculptural artworks, challenging traditional norms by deconstructing and reimagining
everyday materials. Participants will delve
into movements such as Ready-Made,
Found Art, and Site-Specific, crafting temporary and permanent pieces, mixed media
compositions, soft sculptures, abstract sculptures, bas-relief works, and collages.

Participants will be invited to document everyday moments through photography,
finding beauty in ordinary things, encouraging them to appreciate the world around
them adding further depth to their creative process.

Workshops led by Kyla Gilbert and Steohanie Blundell through North Van Arts.

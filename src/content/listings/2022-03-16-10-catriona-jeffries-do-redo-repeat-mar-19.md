Valrie Blass, Laurie Kang, Christina Mackie, and Liz Magor each exploit iteration and
duplication"in their processes, in the distinct individual works they produce and in
their overall practices. In the past three years, we have collectively and individually
shared the experience of extended routine in relative isolation,
altering our
understanding of time and making the act of repetition an integral concept in our lives.
In dialogue, each artist here uses the iterative as a generative force.

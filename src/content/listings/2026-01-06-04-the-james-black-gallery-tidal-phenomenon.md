January 22 - February 8, 2026

3 consecutive weeks, Thursday to Sunday

Presented by The James Black Gallery

Tidal Phenomenon is a three-part exhibition series that explores processes of return,
repetition, and regeneration. Occurring over three consecutive weeks at James Black
Gallery, the series presents three new solo works by emerging artists Creations by Night,
meeti dhanoa, and Naomi Watkins.

Engaging with materials, objects, and beings gleaned from personal memory, recollection,
and wandering, the artists reflect on cycles of loss, recovery, and transformation within
their relationships to home, land, memory, and history. Embedded within their processes
are intentions of slowness, repetition, and ambiguity, allowing them to pull back layers of
colonial narratives that have ruptured personal and collective relationships to land, culture,
and history.

The result is an intertidal zone where materials and memories, once made forgotten, now
turn into sites of rebuilding, resistance, and remembering. Pulses and rhythms radiating
from these new works slopes into the past, gathering potential energy, awaiting to break
and transform wherever they land.

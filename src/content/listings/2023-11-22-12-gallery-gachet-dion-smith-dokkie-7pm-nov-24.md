Dion Smith-Dokkie: This Will Be the First of a Thousand Worlds

Opening November 24, 7pm - 9pm

Artist Talk, November 24, 7:30pm

Running November 24-January 20

In This Will Be the First of a Thousand Worlds, we witness a new approach to
cartography " a counter map, an alternate account of things. Grounded in
Haraway's postulations on situated knowledge, Said's notes on synchronic
essentialism, and Indigenous cartographical traditions and counter-mapping, this
exhibition presents a decolonial schema for place-making that disrupts the logic of
exploitative mimetic
representation and upholds epistemological systems conjured
by the dispossessed.

Smith-Dokkie's unorthodox mixed media canvases are discombobulating,
disorientating, and almost insurgent. They are maps that emerge from painted
remediations overtop conventional topographic maps, maps whose flesh-like organic
materiality establishes a relational network of aliveness possessed by the bodies
and landscapes they represent, maps that cannot be unraveled with a legend "
maps that make themselves obscure in an act of cultural preservation that
confounds the colonial imagination and its machinations that aim to stabilize its
worldview at the price of the "other."

Gallery Gachet is located in the Downtown Eastside of Vancouver on the unceded
and occupied territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh
(Squamish) and Sl"lwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations. Gallery Gachet has a mandate
to support artists and offer art programs addressing mental health and socio-political
marginalization, while
promoting art as a means for survival, cultural participation,
and human rights.

City of Vancouver Artist Live/Work Studios: Deadline

REMINDER: The deadline for applications to the Artist Live/Work Studio 
Program is October 7th, 2011 at 4:30 PM. For general information on the 
Program, photos of the studios, and ftp submission instructions, please access: 
http://vancouver.ca/commsvcs/cultural/gasp/awards/artiststudio/index.htm

Incubating Talent - City to Award Four Artist Live/Work Studios

Initiated in 1996, the City of Vancouver Artist Live/Work Studio Program now 
numbers four purpose built artist live/work studios awarded to local artists. 
These studios provide support for Vancouver-based professional artists to 
pursue their practice for a period of 3 years.

Artists who demonstrate artistic excellence and financial need are encouraged to 
submit applications for consideration by October 7, 2011. Occupancy for the 
four studios will begin February 1, 2012.

Located at East 4th Avenue and Scotia Street, the City of Vancouver Residency 
Studio (Studio 1) is provided rent-free while the three Rental Studios are rented 
at the GAIN (Guaranteed Annual Income for Need) maximum shelter allowance 
(currently $375 per month). Rental Studios 2 and 3 are located at West 8th Ave 
and Cambie Street. Rental Studio 4 is located at East Pender and Clark Drive.

The Artist Studio Award recipients are selected by an independent jury of local 
arts/culture professionals. Recipients of the Award Studio and Rental Studios 
have included: Myfanwy MacLeod and Alex Morrison (2002), Rhonda Weppler 
and Kevin Schmidt (2005), and Michelle Allard, Josh Beamish, Kristen Roos and 
Althea Thauberger (2008).

The Artist Studio Awards are available to artists of all disciplines but to ensure 
that the space meets the needs of your practice, please attend an Open House 
or read the studio descriptions online.

Deadline for applications: Friday, October 7, 2011

Reception with Artist + Curator - Requiem for Mirrors and Tigers

grunt gallery is pleased to host the reception and book launch of 'Requiem for Mirrors 
and Tigers,' which focuses on the exhibition of a series of six documented video 
performances by Guatemalan-Canadian artist Naufus Ramrez-Figueroa. 

DATE: 5 April 2018
TIME: 7 " 9 PM 
LOCATION: grunt gallery 
Free event
**The show is curated by Susan Gibb and facilitated by Glenn Alteen.

In celebration of this exhibition of Requiem for Mirrors and Tigers, If I Can't Dance is 
pleased to launch the work's accompanying publication in Vancouver, BC April 5th, 
2018.Requiem for Mirrors and Tigers: Naufus Ramrez-Figueroa, the book, is published 
by If I Cant Dance with Bom Dia Books, the publication documents the six 
performances.The publication will be launched and a reception will be held on 5 April 
2018 at 7pm at grunt gallery, Vancouver in the presence of the artist, Frdrique 
Bergholtz and Susan Gibb, who will conduct a short Q&A as an introduction to the work 
and the book.The publication will be available for purchase for $30 at grunt gallery and 
books are first come, first serve basis as there is a limited amount available.

**About the Artist:
Naufus Ramrez-Figueroa lives and works in Berlin and Guatemala City. He was born in 
Guatemala City in 1978 and immigrated to Vancouver in the early 1980s. In 2006 he 
received a BFA in Media Arts from Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design and, in 2008, 
an MFA from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago. He was also a postgraduate 
researcher at Jan Van Eyck Academie, Maastricht, the Netherlands, in 2013. He has 
had recent solo exhibitions at CAPC muse dart contemporain de Bordeaux; DAAD 
Galerie, Berlin (2017); and Gasworks, London (2015). Group exhibitions include So 
Paulo Biennial, Venice Biennale; LACMA, Los Angeles (2017); Lyon Biennial; and EFA 
Project Space, New York (2015). He has participated in performance series at the 
Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum (2017); KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Berlin 
(2016); and Tate Modern, London (2015). In recognition of his achievement, Ramrez-
Figueroa was awarded the Mies van der Rohe Award (2017); and a DAAD fellowship 
(2015-16). 

This work was commissioned and produced as part of Corpus, a network for 
performance practice. Corpus is: Bulegoa z/b, Bilbao; CAC, Vilnius; KW, Berlin; If I Cant 
Dance, Amsterdam; Playground, STUK and M - Museum Leuven; and Tate Modern, 
London. Corpus is co-funded by the Creative Europe Programme of the European 
Union.

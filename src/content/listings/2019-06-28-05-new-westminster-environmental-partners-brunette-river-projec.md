New Westminster Environmental Partners has a "Call for Artists" along with some 
criteria. The subject matter is the Brunette River and its tributaries which extends into E. 
Vancouver as well as part of Port Moody, Burnaby & Coq. If you would like a map of the 
Brunette Watershed, we can send that as well (it is set up to be printed in colour on 11" 
X 17" paper). As you will note there are a wide variety of mediums that can be done, 
including photography as well as quite a varied subject matter.

Note: Part of the Brunette on the North side from Coq. to Burnaby will be close to the 
proposed pipeline and in some cases will be less than 40 ft from where it will be laid. 
There are a number of streamkeeper groups who have been successful in keeping the 
Brunette viable to salmon for the past 35 years. (Members of the Sapperton Fish & 
Game Club started cleaning up this river in 1969 and it took them 15 years before the 
first of the salmon were able to return through semi-clean waters in 1984. SF&GC toiled 
hard to reduce the pollution and took out 10's of thousands of pounds of garbage which 
infested these waters. There are at least 5 more streamkeeper groups along the 
Brunette & its tributaries.)

If you have any questions, we will try to answer them to the best of our ability. Regards,

General Manager Position
Other Sights for Artists Projects (Other Sights)
othersights.ca

Deadline to apply is Sunday, April 29, 2018. 12:00 AM (midnight)

Organization Description:

Since 2005, Other Sights has created a presence for art in spaces and sites that are 
accessible to a broad public such as the built environment, communications 
technologies, the media and the street. We are dedicated to challenging 
perceptions, encouraging discourse and promoting innovative perspectives about 
shared social spaces. Founded in 2005 in relation to the growing field of art in public 
space, Other Sights focuses on temporary projects that participate in the newest 
developments of public art practice - whether large-scale works by local or 
international artists, or more modest, intimate initiatives.

Our team of Producers consists of a skilled artistic and curatorial collective who 
bring many years of experience in producing temporary projects in public space. The 
Producer team includes Lorna Brown, Barbara Cole, Colin Griffiths, Vanessa Kwan, 
Marko Simcic, and Jen Weih.

Our annual program presents artworks created by the collective and/or invited 
artists, publications, events and activities that consider the aesthetic, economic and 
regulatory conditions of public places and public life. We work to develop 
unexpected exhibition platforms and provide support to artists, writers and curators 
to create temporary, critically rigorous work for highly visible public locations.

Job Description:

Other Sights is seeking a part-time (60%) General Manager. Reporting to the 
Producer team, the General Manager will oversee administration and day-to-day 
operations of the organization. In consultation with the Producers, they will develop 
annual operating budgets, assess funding needs, and work toward yearly 
fundraising goals. The General Manager will liaise with consultant contractors and 
manage student assistants and volunteers.

Other Sights looks forward to welcoming a new individual who has an exceptional 
capacity for administration and enthusiasm for the work that our organization 
performs.

Responsibilities :

- General administration

- Attend Producers regular bi-weekly meetings and other extraordinary meetings as 
needed

- Develop and implement administrative systems for optimally efficient operations

- Develop annual operating budgets

- Manage project budgets

- Identify and assist with meeting fundraising goals

- Assist with grants, communicating with funders and final reporting

- Liaise with bookkeeper and accountant

- Assist with communications, announcements

- Maintain website

- Respond to inquiries and requests

- Expand, maintain membership database, process payments and proper 
acknowledgement, issue tax receipts and membership notifications

- Generate a variety of reports as required

- Recruit, coordinate and provide support to student assistants and volunteers

Qualifications:

- University degree with experience in non-profit arts organization administration

- Interest in and knowledge of the arts and culture community

- Interested in visual and media art and events in public spaces

- Excellent writing skills, especially for external and funding audiences

- Takes initiative, very self-motivated

- Enjoys working in teams

- Understands project management

- Communicates clearly in collaborative environments

- Excellent organizational skills, strong business sense

- Proficient with business software, basic graphic design software, social media, 
WordPress

across

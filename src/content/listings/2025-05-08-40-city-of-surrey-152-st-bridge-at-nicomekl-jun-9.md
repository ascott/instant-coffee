The City of Surrey is inviting Expressions of Interest for a public artwork for the 152 St
Bridge which crosses over the Nicomekl River. The artwork will be a series of 12 steel
panels, powder coated and affixed to the bridge railing which separates the roadway
and a multi-use path. The artwork will consider the rivers movement as an abundant
pathway, that connects communities and creates biodiversity.

Details
Please read Call to Artists, 152 St Bridge at Nicomekl, for more information about the
project and how to apply.

Design Fee: $7,500
Deadline: June 9, 2025

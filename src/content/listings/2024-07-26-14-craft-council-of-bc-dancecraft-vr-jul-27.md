The Craft Council of British Columbia is proud to host an immersive VR viewing of
Dance:Craft, our multi-faceted performance collaboration with Joe Ink about craft
materiality and dance. It will take place on July 27 and 28 at the CCBC on Granville
Island.

Visitors will be able to drop by anytime between 12pm - 4pm to explore artist studios
and experience a dance performance showcasing contemporary craft in virtual
reality. Additionally, they will have the opportunity to attend a ticketed panel
discussion about the VR film.

This project aims to immerse the viewer in the artists studios and explore
interpretations of craft materiality through the communicative and ephemeral
language of dance. The collaborative project recognizes the development of refined
material understandings as a transformative method of building stronger relations
with the natural material world.

Talk by Tam Irving: Meaning - A Journey of Discovery
Thursday, February 25, 7:30 pm
Room 245, North Building
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
1399 Johnston Street, Granville Island

Free admission. Organized by the North-West Ceramics Foundation
www.nwcf.ca

Since retiring from teaching at Emily Carr University of Art + Design in
1996, Tam Irving has maintained an active studio practice, exploring the
expressive sculptural potential of handmade ceramic forms. Please join us
for this lecture during which he will discuss significant events of his
career and explore the changes that transpired in his work along the way.

Tam Irving was a founding member of the North-West Ceramics Foundation and
is an internationally recognized educator, studio potter, and ceramic
artist. He lives and works in West Vancouver. His work was featured in the
exhibition, "Thrown: Influences and Intentions of West Coast Ceramics" at
the Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery at the University of British
Columbia (2004) and was the subject of a solo exhibition, "Transitions of a
Still Life," curated by Carol Mayer at the Burnaby Art Gallery (2007). His
work is in the collections of The Victoria and Albert Museum, the Canadian
Museum of Civilization, the Gardiner Museum, and the Surrey Art Gallery.

Monday, Mar 21, 2016 - 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM

Please join us for a talk with Surrey Art Gallery exhibiting artist Graeme
Patterson

ECU MoCap studio I 285e, North Building

Graeme Patterson creates playful miniature environments based on
personal memories. Stemming from a self-taught approach to stop-
motion animation, his practice also encompasses large video/sculptural
installations using robotics and interactivity. Since completing a BFA at
NSCAD in 2002, his work has shown internationally. Notable accolades
include a 2012 Canada Council for The Arts Victor Martyn Lynch-
Staunton Award (media arts), selection as the Atlantic finalist for the
2014 and 2009 Sobey Art Award, and a 2011 Juno award nomination.
Graeme's "Secret Citadel", a solo exhibition of large-scale sculptural
installation and stop-motion animation, has been touring Canada and is
currently is on view at the Surrey Art Gallery from January 16 to March
20, 2016.

This is a free event, open to everyone.

The Visual Art Forums are presented by the Audain Faculty of Visual
Art and Material Practice at Emily Carr University.

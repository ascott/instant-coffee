Guest curated by
Melissa E. Feldman, this groundbreaking show challenges
traditional perceptions of Canadian contemporary art and its standard associations
with conceptualism and postmodernist critique. The exhibition instead highlights a
rich history of whimsical and satirical work over the past five decades. From the
folksy Victoria school to Regina funk, Vancouvers edgy Intermedia scene to Halifax
conceptualism, and First Nations artists embrace of manga and comics, Seriously?
explores the intersection of humour, politics, and visual narrative to reveal
unexpected connections between different regional styles.

Seriously? features 20 artists from Canada and also includes selected American
artists from feminist and California Funk movements. Sponsored by Christina
Anthony and Odlum Brown, the exhibition extends from January 25 " May 18, 2025
and includes a series of related public programs:

Wednesday, February 26, 5:30"7PM" Seriously? Artist Panel Discussion (in
person)

Presented in collaboration
with Bill Reid Gallery (at Bill Reid Gallery: 639 Hornby
Street, Vancouver)

Featuring Sonny Assu and Michael Nicoll Yahgulanaas, whose work is featured in
the Seriously? exhibition, this panel explores Indigenous approaches to humour and
satire in contemporary art.

Break out the popcorn and join us for a Sunday matinee featuring The Cheech

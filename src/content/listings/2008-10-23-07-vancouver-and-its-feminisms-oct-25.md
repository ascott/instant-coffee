Vancouver and its Feminisms
A Question of Difference

Saturday, October 25, 10:00 AM - 5:00 PM
In 4 East at the Vancouver Art Gallery

This day long participatory event focuses on artworks from the past and
present and the body of thought they create across generations and
aesthetic approaches. A number of Vancouver artists have been asked to
present artwork by another local colleague, a work that intrigued,
perplexed or in some way influenced their thinking. The event will give the
opportunity to reflect upon how art practices form a conversation across
time, and the potential for a work, whether presented by a young artist or
understood through documentation from years past, to generate a
conversation about the themes and strategies of feminist practice.

Attendee will also have the opportunity to view WACK! Art and the Feminist
Revolution

Moderated by Lorna Brown and Marina Roy
Panelists: Margot Butler, Christine D'Onofrio and Isabelle Pauwells
Respondent: Helena Rickett

$30 ($24 Members and seniors, $15 students)
To register call 604.877.0678

Call for applications: Indigenous Artist in Communities Program, 
honouring the Year of Reconciliation
As part of the Year of Reconciliation, the Vancouver Park Board will 
host an Indigenous Artist in Communities Project. The artist or artist 
team will develop and deliver a participatory, community-engaged art 
project in the spirit of Reconciliation.
The artists will work with community members of all ages from diverse 
cultural backgrounds at community centres across the city. An arts 
programmer from the Arts, Culture, and Environment (ACE) team will 
assist in the delivery of the project.
Apply by October 21, 2013, 5:00pm

Date: Wednesday, October 9, 5:00pm – 7:00pm
Location: 2325 Franklin Street, Pandora Park fieldhouse
Project summary
Eligibility
This opportunity would best suit an Indigenous artist or artist team with 
an established practice that includes imaginative social encounters with 
community members.
If you have experience in fostering reconciliation and community arts 
engagement processes across different cultures and communities, we 
encourage you to apply. 
Location
Pandora Park Fieldhouse studio
2325 Franklin Street, at Garden Drive
Grandview area

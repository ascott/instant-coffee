INTRO
"After twenty years at 1965 Main Street, VIVO Media Arts Centre is searching for a new home. The building that houses the organization has recently been sold, and VIVO has been given notice to vacate by May 1st, 2014.  
Founded in 1973, VIVO has been at the forefront in the development of independent media and video art. Incorporated as the Satellite Video Exchange Society, it has for decades remained one of the countryʼs most important facilities for the production, exhibition and dissemination of new media artforms. 
From 1973 to 1986 VIVO (then known as Video In) operated out of a 2800 square foot storefront at 261 Powell Street, from 1997 to 1993 in Yaletown, first at 1160, Hamilton Street and six months after at 1102, Homer Street). In 1993 VIVO moved to its current location 1965, Main Street. Since its beginning the organization has shifted and expanded alongside the development of new digital technologies."
(May 6, 2013)

DESCRIPTION
The VIVO Events and Exhibition department seeks proposals from artists or groups for a commissioned project, to be realized during the relocation of the organization, between November 2013 and May 2014.  This important transitional phase for the centre should be a point of departure in production of a new work.

Submitted proposals should:

- demonstrate an awareness of VIVO's history and leading role supporting video art and experimentation that utilizes both old and new technologies
- consider current discussions regarding the politics of documentation, the archive and the meaning of production within institutions
- reflect upon the roles of both physical and virtual space in the formation of communities and function of organizations  
- consider the changing landscape of media arts and the challenges an organization like VIVO faces in supporting forms of experimentation, both locally and internationally.
- consider local socioeconomic changes, including urban development, redevelopment, and the roles non profit arts organizations like VIVO play (and have played) in relation to these changes.
- demonstrate an interest in engaging with various people and communities that have contributed to the centre over the past four decades

ELIGIBILITY
Proposals can be submitted by any media artist or group. Collaborations between media artists and others with project specific expertise and research experience are encouraged.

The outcome of the project, in whatever form, will be presented at the new VIVO location and will then become part of the Crista Dahl Media Library & Archive.

The selected artist/s 
- will have access to VIVO facilities for the entire duration of the project
- are welcome to access the Crista Dahl's Media Library & Archive for the duration of their residency
- must demonstrate commitment for the entire duration of VIVO transition 

PROPOSAL GUIDELINES
Applicants must submit a proposal, which includes the following:
- artist statement  (300 words)
- a project proposal describing project (1000 words) by specifying the various steps involved and resources required to carry it out - a current CV of the applicant(s) (max 3 pages)
- links to artist portfolio or selected works (max 20 images or max 15 minutes length for audiovisual material)
- estimated project costs  

Proposals are intended to provide overall intention and background for the project.  
Further development of the project will be executed upon selection.

SELECTION PROCESS
A preliminary selection of three projects proposals will be made. Three shortlisted applicants will be notified by January 30, 2014 followed by an interview. Only one of the three shortlisted proposals will be selected. Programming Coordinator to make final decision with input from Events and Exhibitions Committee.

FUNDING
Total artists' fee: $ 4,000

DEADLINE
January 15, 2014 @ 6pm

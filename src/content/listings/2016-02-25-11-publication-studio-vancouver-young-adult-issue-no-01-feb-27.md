Young Adult: Issue No. 01
"Friends are not the same as fans"

With readings by:

Steve Hubert
Carey Mercer
Jacquelyn Ross

Saturday, February 27th at 7:00PM

Publication Studio Vancouver
222 East Georgia Street, Vancouver

Young Adult is a literary journal promoting new writing by emerging and
established voices. Published two or three times a year in a very small
run, each issue includes a few short stories or otherwise prosey texts and
a special edition artist poster*. Edited by Jacquelyn Ross. Printed and
bound at Publication Studio Vancouver. 

Copies of the journal will be available for $15. Cash bar.
Space is extremely limited, so arrive early to guarantee entrance.

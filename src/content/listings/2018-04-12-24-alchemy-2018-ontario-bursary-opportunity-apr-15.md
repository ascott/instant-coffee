BURSARY OPPORTUNITY ALCHEMY 2018, ARTISTS RESIDENCY, PRINCE 
EDWARD COUNTRY, ON. DEADLINE APRIL 15 Through the generosity of textile 
artist and Alchemy 2017s Lynn Hubbs we are proud to offer our first bursary to 
support one artist to join Alchemy 2018 (www.makealchemy.com). This artist led 
residency explores the synergy between artistic practice and the growing, cooking 
and sharing of locally cultivated food in a community setting. Alchemy has grown 
since its founding at Artscape Gibraltar Point in 2015 through the thoughtful 
participation of working artists " the majority of whom form collaborations extending 
well past the actual time they spend in residence.

A community and relationship building residency, Alchemy is based on four pillars: 
Art, Food, Garden and Community. 

Alchemy facilitates collaboration, conversation and connections through the making 
of art and the growing and sharing of food in a community setting. Making art is often 
a solitary practice and Alchemy (particularly for new generation and emerging 
artists) provides the opportunity to share in a safe. nurturing community setting. This 
experience provides a lasting positive effect on participant's work and well being. 

Emerging and established artists who would benefit from participating in Alchemy 
and do not have the financial means to do so are encouraged to apply by April 15th, 
2018 for an early May decision. The bursary covers accommodation, studio/work 
space and shared meals at Alchemy 2018 ($1250 plus HST). Please note travel 
costs are not included at this time. Alchemy is LBTGQ friendly. Visit 
www.makealchemy.com for more information. Submit applications to 
apply@makealchemy.com:

a bio/artist statement and cv
a letter of intent (up to 500 words) outlining why you should be considered for 
Alchemys first subsidized bursary, why you would like to join us, and initial thoughts 
about the project you hope to undertake 
up to 10 images of your artwork and/or a link to a website.
Questions about the bursary or our residency are also welcome at the same email 
address. Please share this post with artists in search of Alchemy.

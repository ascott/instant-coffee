We are pleased to announce the fourth iteration of the Emerging Creators
Incubator from May-August, 2024. We will invite a group of six multidisciplinary
emerging creators between the ages of 18-30 to participate in a mentorship and
residency program that includes hands-on workshops and studio visits with
professional artist mentors Derek Chan
and Krystal Paraboo. Emerging
creators will receive a $500 honourarium, ongoing support from Evergreen staff, and
access to our facilities (including studio space, studio theatre, a kiln, wood-working
shop, etc.) to develop their practice and experiment with new art forms.

Who is an Emerging Creator?
An emerging creator is defined as an early emerging artist who works in any
discipline(s) within the visual and performing arts. They are between the ages of 18-
30, in the early stages of their professional career, and are committed to the
development of their craft and practice.The Emerging Creators Incubator centers a
multidisciplinary approach to the arts and will prioritize local early emerging artists
from the Tri-Cities area and Host Nations who engage in the performing, written and
visual arts. Applications are also open to emerging creators from across the
Vancouver Lower Mainland.

We welcome and will prioritize applications from Black, Indigenous and racialized
artists,
persons with disabilities, persons of diverse sexual orientations, gender
identities or expressions (LGBTQ2S+), and any of these intersections. Please head
to the application where you will find our Commitment to Justice, Equity and
Inclusion to learn more about the concrete action we are taking.

This program centers a multidisciplinary approach to the arts and will prioritize local
emerging artists from the Tri-Cities area who engage in the performing, written and
visual arts. Applications are also open to emerging creators from across the
Vancouver Lower Mainland who are interested in engaging with the ECC and AGE.

Application Deadline:
The written application deadline isMarch 17, 2024, at 11:45PM
The interview deadline is March 15, 2024, at 6PM

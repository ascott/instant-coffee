EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY Competition #SA058-2023

CT Events Technician

Casual On-Call Positions

Pay Grade E ($25.44 to $27.24 per hour)

Emily Carr University of Art + Design is a school of students, faculty, thinkers and
makers unlike any other. Established in 1925, we are the only specialized,
accredited, public, post-secondary university in British Columbia solely devoted to
education and research in the creative sector and its associated knowledge
economy. We merge research, critical theory and studio practice in an
interdisciplinary environment, strengthening our work by the integration of our
personal and professional practices.
Our strategy, facilities, partnerships and
resources are intentionally student-centred to foster dialogue, expression and open
connections in support of the next generation of creative and cultural leaders.

The CTS Events Technician provides the delivery, set-up, operations and strike of
multi-media equipment and systems for conferences, talks and performance events.
The CTS Events Technician sets up and operates audio visual equipment which
includes working with projection systems, IP based AV control systems, audio
mixers, speakers, microphones, computers, lighting and video conferencing,
streaming and recording equipment. Events may be held off-site or within the
University. Events are often during evenings and weekends. The CTS Events
Technician works with various university departments and with outside groups in pre-
production planning of events. The CTS Events Technician works directly with
presenters, talent and performers to maintain the flow during events.

As the
successful applicant, you should possess:

Two years post-secondary training in audio visual or media related field.
One year of field-based event technology experience in the following areas: Hotel
Audio Visual, Ministry, College AV or Media Department, Technical Theatre
Department, AV Integration.
Customer service experience required.
Considerable knowledge and troubleshooting skills required in operating audio,
video, IP based AV control systems and lighting for events.
Technical aptitude for operating computer hardware (Windows, OS) and software
(PowerPoint, Keynote).
Strong skills in sound set ups: wiring, isolating noise, balancing and tuning audio
for optimum sound presentation and recording.
Experience operating video streaming, recording and conferencing systems.
Basic knowledge of electricity.
Effective communication skills with ability to understand client needs and to accept
direction.
Good time management skills, the ability to work effectively under pressure and act
quickly resolving technical issues.
Dependability, punctuality and follow-through.
Excellent attention to detail.
Ability to lift and carry10kg and to work at heights.

Some Typical Duties:

1 Ensures equipment is booked, picked up and set up on time and in a safe manner
with user ease in mind. Operates equipment during events and safely dismantles
and packs equipment for transport.

2 Provides follow-up communication with event organizer on technical requests.
Confirms technical set-up and tear-down times. May provide clarification or
information on university equipment and software to event organizers.

3 Corrects equipment faults prior to or during event to ensure smooth operation of
multi-media equipment and systems. Directs complex equipment problems to CTS
Production Lead and Senior Events Technician.

4 Work in other production capacities including sound, lighting, stage management
and house management as required.

5 Attends meetings with various university departments in
pre-production planning
and works directly with presenters, talent and performers to maintain the flow during
events.

6 Supervises and directs work of student monitors and volunteers for events.

7 Assists and advises internal and external clients to ensure that their needs are met
within the terms of their event contract while ensuring that the theatre, building and
campus policies, rules and safety regulations are adhered to.

8 Performs other related duties as required.

VIVO WORKSHOPS: www.vivomediaarts.com/workshops

ABLETON LIVE - INTRO
2 sessions x 3 hours
Thu/Fri June 3 & 4, 6-9 PM

Cost: $120 or $100 with Producer Membership
Instructor: Carey Dodge
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This is a two-part introductory workshop to the Ableton Live audio software
with special emphasis on its uses for sound art and experimental music.
Ableton Live is a non-linear realtime audio sequencing and manipulation
software (www.ableton.com). In the first part, we will go over all the
main functions of Ableton Live: playing and manipulating clips, midi
functions, effects and Ableton Live's powerful realtime functions. In the
second part, we will explore various ways to interface with Ableton Live as
well as its more experimental uses, such as for installations, connecting
with other software (i.e. PD, Max/MSP, other).

Prerequisites: An interest in sound or art or both. A basic knowledge of
recording and editing digital audio would be beneficial.

What you need to bring: Ideally participants should bring their laptops
with Ableton Live installed. There is a free demo version available for
download on their website: www.ableton.com. For the second part, bring any
interfaces you may have (i.e. Midi keyboard, drum machine with usb or midi
connection, home-made contraption with some form of midi connection,
joystick, other)

VJ: PERFORMANCE VIDEO
1 session x 7 hours
Sun June 6, 11 AM - 6 PM

Cost: $160 or $130 with Producer Membership
Instructor: Julie Gendron
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This workshop is being offered to video artists who would like to learn how
to mix (edit) video live in order to work with musicians and/or sound
artists in a performative setting. The course covers equipment set-up,
editing video to create loops, and using hardware and software to mix
different material sources and formats. A variety of scenarios are
demonstrated and time for practice and experimentation is integrated into
theory and history of video performance and manipulation.

Part 1: Equipment set-up (i.e. multiple display, live feed, creating
feedback, connecting multiple sources), vj theory, hardware based mixing,
sourcing materials and working with long form sources.

Part 2: Software mixing using Modul8, mixing with sound, vj theory
involving digital and generative sources, working with short-form loops and
how to make a gapless loop.

LOGIC PRO - INTERMEDIATE
2 sessions x 3 hours
Mon/Thu June 7 & 10, 6-9 PM

Cost: $120 or $100 with Producer Membershi
Instructor: Jonathan Bierman
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This workshop aims to address a variety of issues particular to the
individual attendees of the class, going beyond the most basic principles
of sequencing and audio recording and editing. These might include, but are
not limited to, simultaneous multitrack recording, mix and effect
automation, destructive and non-destructive audio file editing, audio bus
and submix routing, virtual instruments, project and file management,
keyboard shortcuts and techniques for increasing the speed of your
workflow. The goal of the workshop is to resolve any issues the users may
have with interfacing with Logic in order to ultimately increase their
power, speed and overall ability while using the software..

Participants are asked to prepare a list of issues in advance of the
workshop so that the instructor can adequately prepare for their needs.

Participants must provide their own laptop with a version of Logic
installed (Logic Express, Logic 7, Logic Pro 8 or Logic Pro 9).
Participants are also advised to bring an audio/MIDI interface if they have
one, and headphones.

CIRCUIT BUILDING
2 sessions x 3.5 hours
Wed/Thu June 9 & 10, 6-9:30 PM

Cost: $130 or $100 with Producer Membership
Instructor: David Leith
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

A base knowledge of electronics gives the artist the opportunity to
introduce electronic circuitry into a variety of media including sculpture,
sound, and light-based works. This course will cover some basic theory of
electronic principals that is essential for beginning to work with this
medium. From there we will look at some of the components that make up
simple circuits and that create the building blocks for more complex
circuits. Students will learn some of the basic skills needed for
electronic work such as soldering, breadboarding, and reading a schematic.
These skills will be used to build a simple audio amplifier. We will
experiment with different inputs such as contact microphones and how to
modify the sound output from the speaker.

The cost of materials that you take home with you ($10-15) is included in
the workshop fee.

"The Mining Injustice Solidarity Network has launched a "Call for Imaginings" and invites
you to submit, and help us spread the word!
We're
beginning a project of sharing art that imagines a future beyond extravism. We are
inviting submissions of art that explores the theme: What would a world without extractivism
look like for you? This can take many forms, including drawings, paintings, photographs,
collages, or other pieces of art; murals; poems, stories, and songs; short videos; or any
other response you can think of. New and old work is welcome, and all skill/experience
levels are appreciated. The deadline for submissions is March 31st, and you can find more
details here. Any help spreading the word would be much appreciated!

Can you think of examples of art that already exists from extraction-impacted communities?
We would like to know so we can reach out to the artist directly. Email us at
info@mininginjustice.org.

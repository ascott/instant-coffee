Buy It Or Burn It - Art by David Jardine
July 22nd, 7pm-11pm
Burnout Cafe, 2032 E. Hastings Street

An unconventional art show, featuring abstract paintings by Vancouver artist - David
Jardine.

Showing over thirty paintings, this art show will allow you to bid to buy a new painting, or
bid to witness it destroyed!

Whether you're an art enthusiast, a curious explorer, or simply seeking an unforgettable
experience, join us and ignite your imagination at the "Buy It or Burn It" Art Show!

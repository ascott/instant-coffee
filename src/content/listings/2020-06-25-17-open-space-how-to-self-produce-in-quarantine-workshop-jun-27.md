Saturday, June 27 from 7-9pm, Open Space is hosting an online Zoom workshop,
'How to Self-Produce in Quarantine!' with Dominique Wakeland! as part of the
ongoing Vogue project and series of events.

From Dominique: In these times of uncertainty, internet visibility is key. Not everyone
has a professional camera or high speed webcam, so how do you stay relevant?
And
by relevant... I mean paid. This workshop will be an exercise in filming with what
you have, finding your aesthetic, and how to make a moodboard. Get ready to find
your angles baby! Your art is valid, and your anxieties can go fuck themselves.

Dominique Wakeland (they/them) was born on unceded Kwantlen territory and is a
graduate of Simon Fraser Universitys Theatre Performance program. Dom is a poet/
Drag artist/and theatre creator. Their practice is fundamentally movement and text
based with an emphasis on the politics we bring in our bodies and how that
manifests into knowledge. Their drag persona Mx. Bukuru is "the church being your
mama never wanted you to meet." Subverting colonial ideas of Blackness, Mx.
blends fierce performance with fiercer politics. Every month is Black History Month
baby!

Please note: advance registration is required to attend! Email office@openspace.ca
by end of day Friday, June 26 to reserve your spot.

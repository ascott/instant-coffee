Artists’ Tour

 

Ian Wallace, Kevin Schmidt, Andrew Dadson and others on

The Tree: From the Sublime to the Social

 

Tuesday, February 26 at 7pm

at the Vancouver Art Gallery

 

Curators Daina Augaitis and Emmy Lee join exhibiting artists for a tour of The Tree: From the Sublime to the Social.

 

The Tree: From the Sublime to the Social considers the tree as a subject in art from the late nineteenth century to the present. In art the tree has been used as a symbol for all of nature and its overwhelming, even terrifying beauty. It is a powerful signifier of Canada’s national identity and the individual’s struggle against the wilderness; currently it even serves as a reminder of our precarious ecological situation. The diverse representations of the tree in this exhibition are indicative of its enduring power as an evocative symbol of our complex and changing relationship with the natural environment.

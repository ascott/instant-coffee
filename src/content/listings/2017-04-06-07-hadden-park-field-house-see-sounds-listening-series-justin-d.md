See Sounds Listening Series: Justin Devries
Thursday April 6, 2017, 8pm
Hadden Park Field House, 1015 Maple St, Vancouver

It's time for our fourteenth monthly listening party at the field house! Bring something 
you would like to listen to together. Discussion will follow. We will have most available 
formats (digital, vinyl, CD, tape, radio). 

Meet at the field house at 8pm. Our guest presentation will start at 8:30. It will be a 
phones off environment!

As part of our See Sounds series, we are featuring a guest artist each listening party to 
curate the early portion of the evening. We have invited local drummer Justin Devries to 
be this month's guest. Devries is dedicated to the study of drums, percussion, 
electronics and international music, and his studies strive to explore and celebrate the 
multicultural realities of life in the 21st century. Justin recently returned from a self 
directed study of Balinese music in Indonesia.

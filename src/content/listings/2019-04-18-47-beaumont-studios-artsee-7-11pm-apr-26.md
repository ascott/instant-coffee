ArtSee is an interactive visual and performing arts festival that takes place on the last Friday 
of every month. It always has an Open Studios portion where as many of our resident artists 
open their studios to the public. We curate the event differently every month with different 
community partnerships and this is the 3rd iteration of this series. 

This particular ArtSee has a Voice Arts theme and will feature:

- 8 different voice artists (Singing, Poetry, Comedy, Theatrical performances)
- 4 resident artist interviews
- 16 open artist studios
- a scavenger hunt with awesome prizes
- a delicious food truck 
- and a chance for members to win a Basscoast ticket *or* a brand new bike (you win - you 
choose)

The Beaumont Studios is a massive artist collective sprawling over 2 buildings and 18,000 
square feet including 40 resident artists studios, a massive events space, an art gallery, and 
a thrift store to boot!

animated films Cat Soup and Mind Game

Blim
197 East 17th Avenue (corner of Main Street)
Entry by donation

Powell Street Festival and Blim present a double-bill screening of Japanese
animated films on Saturday October 18, 2008. Yuriko Iga has selected 2
celebrated anime films, Cat Soup (2001) and Mind Game (2004), for Powell
Street Festival's final 2008 event.

Cat Soup, an award-winning anime directed by Tatsuo Sato, is inspired by
the work of manga artist Nekojiru. This surreal black comedy follows the
antics of the kitten Nyatta on his travel to the land of the dead in an
effort to save his sister's soul. Cat Soup won an Excellence Prize
(Animation Division) at the 2001 Japan Media Arts Festival, the "Best Short
Film" award at the 2001 Fantasia Festival and the Silver Award for
Animation 2003 New York Exposition of Short Film and Video.

Mind Game is an anime based on Robin Nishi's comic book. Written and
directed by Masaaki Yuasa, it tells the story of Nishi, a young aspiring
comic book artist who has a crush on his childhood friend Myon. While
visiting Myon, Nishi gets caught up in the midst of Myon's father's shady
business transactions and is killed. Fleeing the afterlife and going back
in time, Nishi tries to change the course of events that lead to his death.
Mind Game is celebrated for its eclectic and disparate use of visual styles.

Films in Japanese with English subtitles

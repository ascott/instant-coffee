Dundarave Print Workshop + Gallery is calling for print submissions to our upcoming
show, Shifting Ground, this spring!

Change is constant. Landscapes erode,
identities evolve, histories are rewritten, and
what once felt solid may suddenly shift beneath our feet. Dundarave Print Workshop
invites printmakers from across Canada to explore themes of transformation,
instability, and adaptation"whether personal, political, environmental, or abstract.

As printmaking itself is a process of layering, erasure, and impression, this exhibition
seeks works that reflect the fluidity of our times. How do we navigate uncertainty?
What emerges in moments of transition? Through relief, intaglio, screenprint,
lithography, and experimental techniques, we welcome interpretations that
challenge, document, or embrace change. We encourage submissions from artists
of all backgrounds and experience levels. Let your work become part of the
conversation as we examine the shifting ground beneath us.

Deadline for online application: 11:59 pm, March 31st, 2025

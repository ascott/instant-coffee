Carrie Walker

To While Away the Time
October 8 - November 7

Please join Carrie Walker on Thursday October 8, 6-9PM. for the opening reception of her fist 
solo exhibition at the gallery, 

To While Away the Time is a collaboration with unwitting partners. Through online auctions Carrie
Walker acquires what she considers impoverished landscape drawings - some dating to the 19th 
century - and rescues them through the addition of her own work. With disregard for plausibility 
of scale or context the end result is often humorous - the collision of the sincerity of hobbyists' 
landscape sketches and her own highly detailed and skilled drawing of wildlife, they become 
fragments of fantastical narratives, of stories half told.

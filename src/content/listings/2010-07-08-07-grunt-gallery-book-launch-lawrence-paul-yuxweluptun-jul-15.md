Book Launch:
Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun
Neo-Native Drawings and Other Works

Thursday July 15, 2010, 6-8 pm at the grunt gallery

The week following the opening of grunt's new exhibition ACTIVATING THE
ARCHIVE, the grunt gallery will be hosting the launch of a publication
featuring the drawings of Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun. Archival materials
from Yuxweluptun's past projects at grunt will be featured in the gallery
space.

[Edit Image]

The publication Neo-Native Drawings and Other Works documents and expands
Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun's exhibition-of the same name-that was held at
the Contemporary Art Gallery, March 19 to May 16, 2010.

This publication, a 40-page softcover book, is illustrated by thirty-nine
drawings, and with texts by artist Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun ("Drawing");
artist, writer and MFA student Peter Morin ("Storytelling: Articulating
Ancient Pathways"); and curator Petra Watson ("An Insurgent Act of Cultural
Translation").

The exhibition included over 60 drawings spanning a time period of three
decades. These drawings, many which serve as preliminary studies for
paintings, are an important body of work through which to examine his
process and gain insight into his oeuvre as a whole.

Neo-Native Drawings and Other Works is published by Curatorial Projects
International.

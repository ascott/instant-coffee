Join us for THESE Poems at THIS Gallery. THAT'S February 26th for our in-person
celebration of all things chapbook. Four Vancouver poets will be reading from their most
recent publications. A selection of volumes will also be available for purchase.

Hosted by the inimitable Kevin Spenst and featuring THESE POETS: Justyna Krol,
Catherine Lewis, and Marc Perez

Please be prepared to show your proof of vaccination. Masks are mandatory for the
duration of the event. Doors at noon, readings at 1 pm. We are limiting this event to 20
people, so arrive early!

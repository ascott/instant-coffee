The Magnetic North Theatre Festival presents
crow's theatre in association with mammalian diving reflex 
[boxhead]
a bedtime story for your brain

&#65532;Written by Darren O’Donnell
Directed by Chris Abraham
Starring Andrew Shaver as Dr. Thoughtless Actions and Adam Lazarus as Dr. Wishful Thinking

A young geneticist wakes up to discover a box secured to his head; he finds all his thoughts 
come from God, all his words come from the devil and his desire for love is a habit acquired from
the movies. Sound familiar? Don’t be so hard on yourself. From the addled consciousness that 
brought you White Mice, A Suicide-Site Guide to the City and Haircuts by Children, 
comes [boxhead] – a bedtime story for your brain.

"But let's be clear. This is theatre on the edge, and as such, is not recommended for those who 
consider Mamma Mia! the zenith of the theatrical experience... This is theatre that is, in its way, a 
sort of carnival ride for the mind, irreverent, often profane but still both clever and intelligent. For 
people who like a mental workout, however, one thing is certain. You certainly won't be bored." -
John Colbourne, Toronto Sun 

June 4-8, 9PM
Waterfront Theatre, Granville Island, Vancouver

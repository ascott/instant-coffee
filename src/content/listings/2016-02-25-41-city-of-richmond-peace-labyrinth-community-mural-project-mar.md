REQUEST FOR PROPOSALS
Peace Labyrinth Community Mural Project
The City of Richmond
Richmond, BC
Budget: $10,000 (plus applicable taxes)
Eligibility: This opportunity is open to professional artists residing in 
British Columbia with experience in executing outdoor public murals
Submission Deadline: Monday, March 18, 2016, 5pm PST

The City of Richmond's Public Art Program and the Richmond Peace 
Labyrinth Committee are seeking an artist to provide services to 
engage the local community to create a collaborative community mural 
on a wall adjacent to an outdoor labyrinth.

Link to Call: [http://www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities.htm](http://www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities.htm)

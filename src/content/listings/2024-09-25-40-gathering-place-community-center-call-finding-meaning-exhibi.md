Call: Finding Meaning Exhibition

Deadline: October 13th, 2024

Place: Connection Salon Gallery at the Gathering Place Community Center Gallery,
609 Helmcken

We are continuing our series of exhibitions on the theme of Recovery. This one is
about: Finding meaning in mental health experience and living a purposeful life.

The Connection Salon Artists Collective invites artists to submit two-dimensional
(2D) art for a Group show of 12 artists (October 25th - December 8th)

Artists are invited to submit 4 pieces of work. There
are no size limitations and each
piece submitted must be ready to hang.

Please complete submission form at [http://connectionsalon.ca/call.html](http://connectionsalon.ca/call.html)

Connection Salon Artists Collectives mission is to provide low barrier opportunities
to artists that are underrepresented and have lived experience with mental health.

The exhibition is supported by the Canada Council for the Arts, the Connection
Salon Collective and the Gathering Place Community Center.

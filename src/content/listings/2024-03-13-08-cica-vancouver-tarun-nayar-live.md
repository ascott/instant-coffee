performance | 730PM | MAR 16
Calling out to all lovers of art, technology. and mushroom music!

We are pleased to host the renowned artist Tarun Nayar, also known as Modern
Biology, for a live performance in response to the upcoming exhibition, Ksawery
Komputery: RRGGBB.

Nayar, widely recognized as a "mushroom musician," crafts captivating soundscapes
using bioelectric fluctuations from plants and mushrooms. He employs modular
synthesis, homemade synthesizers, and other analog gear to spontaneously create
music that resonates with the unique vibrations of a specific location and moment,
tapping into sources like plant bioelectricity, ambient electromagnetic radiation, and
the earth's natural resonance. Join us for this exceptional interdisciplinary gathering,
where we will immerse ourselves in the auditory wonders of Modern Biology and the
natural world, complemented by the extraordinary artwork of Ksawery
Komputery.

Date: March 16, 2024

Door: 7:00 PM

Show: 7:30 - 8:30 PM

Sound of the Sun Vol. III Cassette Launch with Edith Skeard, Anju Singh, Sam
Meadahl, Immix

Hosted by UNIT/PITT
LOCATION: 2954 West 4th Ave
DATE: June 20, 2024
TIME: 6:00 - 10:30pm

Please join us for our June 20th cassette release event at U/Ps Khatsalano gallery
and garden, featuring an outdoor cassette listening room, small meal, and live
performances by SOTS artists Edith Skeard, Anju Singh, Sam Meadahl, and Immix
(Robyn Jacob).

SOTS Vol. III, guest curated by Jefferson Alade, meditates on the cyclical nature of
solar movements, and our relationship with the sun; again in flux as the days
lengthen. Artists were invited to observe, respond, record, and retell their
experiences through music, poetry, field recordings, synthesis, storytelling, or any
other
audio-based method.

The cassette features sound works by Thomas Evdokimoff, Sam Meadahl, Immix
(Robyn Jacob), Matt Nish-Lapidus, Marianne Thodas, Joel Destefano, Jen
Yakamovich & Inky Dew the Companion, Hn Phm, Edith Rattary, and Anju Singh.

A limited release of 50 cassettes will be available for purchase during and after the
event.

Please note that attendance will be limited to ensure adequate accessibility and
seating. A second version of the cassette listening room will be installed in the
gallery through July for future listening, and a free SoundCloud playlist will be linked
after the launch event.

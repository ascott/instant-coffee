Artist: Lyle XOX
Exhibition title: Obscurious
Artist Talk: Saturday, march 29th at 2pm at Gallery Jones, last day of the
exhibition

Exhibition statement.

Eager to learn about the peculiar, offbeat or unusual.
Examining the origin of a fabricated world, where the collected contents amplify the
value and meaning of the whole yet to be defined.

A side-view mirror warning reminds us that objects are closer than they appear.
Similarly, things we think are distant memories still exist in the present and play a role
in our current lives.

This collection of work encapsulates this concept consisting of found objects sourced
from travels around the globe with the man I love, mementos from childhood and
ephemera which invoke a myriad of emotions. Each self portrait captures memories
from my life,
creating a narrative around the people who have impacted my journey in ways that
continue to surprise me and foster personal evolution.

This is my journal, my yearbook and my up close and personal interview wrapped into
one.

The past is very much present in my obscurious life.

Anita Cirillo

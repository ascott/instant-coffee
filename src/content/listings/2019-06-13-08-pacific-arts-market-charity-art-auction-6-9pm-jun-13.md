Place: Pacific Arts Market 

Date: Thursday June 13th 

Time: 6pm - 9pm

Location: 1448 W. Broadway, Vancouver - 2nd floor

Join us for PAMs first art auction and mix and mingle!

Over 50 artists in the NEW South Granville creative space - PACIFIC ARTS MARKET will be 
hosting a soiree happening from 6 to 9 p.m. on Thursday, June 13th. Free to the public, join 
us for an evening featuring live music, wine, appetizers and the opportunity to interact with 
local artists.

Highlighted by a Silent Art Auction, guests will have the opportunity to 
bid on fine arts, jewellery, and gift baskets made locally with a portion of the proceeds going 
to The Kids Up Front Foundation.

The event is a marvellous opportunity for the public to engage in Vancouvers thriving local 
art community, says Crystal Holli Cornthwaite, Owner of PACIFIC ARTS MARKET. I am so 
excited to be hosting this silent auction and seeing some of the proceeds going to such a 
worthy organization like The Kids Up Front Foundation. 

About the Kids Up Front Foundation

Kids Up Front Vancouver, is a charitable organization that provides access to arts, culture, 
sport and recreation for children and families facing financial and/or social barriers.

About PACIFIC ARTS MARKET

Pacific Arts Market is a family owned business dedicated to showcasing local creativity and 
high-quality artisan goods in Vancouver. We provide an economical way for Vancouver 
Artists to showcase their work or launch their business. We support all Artistic 
Entrepreneurs with a network of over 50 artists, no long leases, and zero commission taken.

Learn more: [https://www.pacificartsmarket.ca](https://www.pacificartsmarket.ca)

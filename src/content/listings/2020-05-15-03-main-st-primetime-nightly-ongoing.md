Main St. Primetime
Online Location (Instagram): @mainstprimetime
Physical Location: Alley on the west side of Main Street between 27th and 28th Street
Date: Nightly, ongoing

Main St. Primetime is a simple and safe exhibition platform aimed at giving people
something to do and artists something to work towards while they are unemployed and
kept apart during the COVID-19 pandemic. The idea is to project art or text onto an
outside wall across from a second-floor apartment window. As an alternative to strictly
virtual exhibitions we are interested in providing a safe physical destination for art
viewing. Our projection is visible from an alley on the west side of Main Street between
27th and 28th Street in Vancouver, BC from dusk until late every night. Artists from
across Canada have been
invited to submit artwork; each artists work is projected for a
total of 3 nights. We have made an Instagram account to archive each projection
(@mainstprimetime) where we post information about the art being projected. We do
this so viewers have the option of engaging with the work online or of going to see it in
person depending on their circumstance. For those who are able, Main St. Primetime
can be an excuse to get out of the house and go for a safe walk at a time when few
people are out on the streets.

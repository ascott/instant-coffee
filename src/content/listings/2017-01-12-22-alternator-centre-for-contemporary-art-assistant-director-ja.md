Application Deadline: January 24, 2017 (Midnight)
Interviews to be conducted January 28, 2017

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art invites applications for the position of:
Assistant Director (Permanent, Part-time)

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is a non-profit artist-run centre in Kelowna, 
BC. Our mandate is to support the development of innovative contemporary work by 
local, national and international emerging and mid-career artists. The Alternator 
operates three exhibition spaces, hosts an artist co-op program and facilitates a number 
of weekly and monthly community-based arts events.
Working under the Artistic and Administrative Director, the employee will help manage 
daily operations of the Alternator. The preferred applicant will have both education and 
experience in the visual and media arts and familiarity with artist-run centres. He/she is 
an engaging, highly responsible team player with excellent interpersonal and 
organizational skills and is able to demonstrate personal initiative and leadership within 
a collective environment. For the right candidate, this position offers an excellent 
opportunity to develop skills in arts administration and has potential for internal 
advancement.

Major responsibilities include: assisting with the management of the Alternator's day-to-
day operations including human resources, administrative duties, exhibition logistics, 
installation and maintenance, and offer support to the Artistic and Administrative 
Director in grant applications, fundraising and programming activities.

The successful candidate will possess:
- Undergraduate degree in Fine Arts, Media, Design or a related field (Postgraduate 
degree highly desirable, but not necessary)
- Outgoing personality with an interest in networking and developing new relationships 
for the gallery
- Eagerness and aptitude to work both independently and part of a team
- Physical ability and knowledge to perform preparatory and installation work, including 
basic curatorial knowledge
- Capacity to work within both creative and traditional business domains
- Excellent organizational and communication skills
- Familiarity with the Mac environment, including Word,and Excel (Adobe In-Design, 
Photoshop and Illustrator an asset, but not necessary)
- Experience in basic accounting
- Professional experience in an arts environment, preferrably in the context of an artist-
run centre

Remuneration: $21,216 per annum - approx. 24 hours per week
Anticipated Start Date: February 28, 2017
Please note: this position will require evening / weekend shifts.

To apply, submit (in PDF format) a cover letter, resume and contact information for two 
references to hr@alternatorcentre.com. Please write 'Attn. Hiring Committee' in the 
subject line.

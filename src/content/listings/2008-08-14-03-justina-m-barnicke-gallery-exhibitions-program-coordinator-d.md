The Justina M. Barnicke Gallery at Hart House, University of Toronto seeks 

Exhibitions and Program Coordinator (09N) 
Job Number: 0800771 
Employee Group: United Steelworkers (USW) 
Faculty / Division: Hart House 

Deadline: August 20 

Description: 

Under the direction of the Executive Director/Chief Curator of the Justina M. Barnicke Gallery, the 
Exhibitions and Program Coordinator is responsible for coordinating the overall operations of the
Justina M. Barnicke Gallery with specific emphasis on planning, budgeting, and implementing 
exhibition and outreach programs as set by the Executive Director/Chief Curator. The incumbent 
will be responsible for maintenance of schedules and implementation of the gallery's programs, 
including but not limited to research, fundraising, contractual and budgetary processes, advocacy
and publicity, and administration of technical and practical aspects associated with the production 
of contemporary art exhibitions and events. The latter include and exceed publishing processes, 
installations, development of touring exhibitions, and registration. Engaged in a broad and 
diverse communication network, the Coordinator will be responsible for ongoing (budgetary, 
logistical, administrative and time-table) coordination of guest curators, visiting lecturers, artists 
and artists-in-residence, as well as funding agencies, sponsors, the general public and students 
and staff of the University of Toronto. The incumbent will also provide leadership for a changing
team of contract staff, assistants, work-study students, and volunteers. This includes staff 
development (with fundraising) activities, grant-writing and training, as well as coordinating and 
setting specific achievable, clerical and creative goals for gallery attendants and students 
associated with the gallery's operations in the context of Hart House. The Exhibition and Program
Coordinator is part of a tightly knit team moving the JMB Gallery forward as a center for 
contemporary art and interdisciplinary undertakings at Hart House and at the University of 
Toronto. 

Qualifications: 

(Minimum) 

Education: Graduate degree, or equivalent combination of education and experience, with 
demonstrated, sustained and recognized interest and engagement in the histories, ideas, and 
trajectories of contemporary art in Canada and international contexts (through administration, 
program coordination, communication, curatorial activity). 

Experience: Minimum of three (3) years experience in a leading role within the (non) profit gallery 
or museum context, as well as significant and directly relevant prior organizational and 
administrative position(s) in the context of contemporary art institution(s), with a proven track-
record of project management. 

Skills: Technical expertise and proficiency with digital applications (word processing, database,
webpage and design), as well as equipment common in contemporary art installation such as 
video projectors, playback systems, audio recording, image processing, but also deep knowledge 
of installations of art works (from wall construction, sound proofing, equipment use, and proper 
handling of art works of all media); etc. 

Other: Exceptional (written and oral) communication skills, superior organizational skills (ability to 
multi-task and work to a hectic schedule and demanding deadlines, overlapping schedules and 
timelines), self-directed, ability to navigate intellectually challenging materials (research, 
educational texts, concepts), ability to build and lead a team of assistants, networking and 
fundraising abilities.

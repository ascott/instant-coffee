Published in the 1970s and 80s, Vanguard was a prominent arts and
culture magazine. Vanguard critically explored Vancouver's vibrant art
scene and kept its readers abreast of international developments. A
number of local and national arts critics, gallery professionals, and
professors contributed to each issue.

FRIDAY, JANUARY 25
RECEPTION AT BELKIN SATELLITE 7-10PM

Music by Saelan Twerdy

Introductory remarks by Josh Hite, Jacqueline Mabey, Charlotte
Townsend-Gault, and William Wood at 8pm

Draw for Prize Baskets at 9pm
(Tickets are 2 for $5, 6 for $10)

This reception is a fundraiser for the CCST/ MFA exhibition at the
Organ Artist Run Centre and symposium at the Sichuan Fine Arts
Institute in Chongqing, China.


Contributors to the prize baskets and opening reception are Bard on the
Beach, Blendz Coffee, Caffe Artigiano, Cowboy Coffee Roasting Company,
Fairmont Hotel Vancouver, Festival Cinemas, Goh Ballet, Keg
Restaurants, Kokoro Dance, Les Amis du Fromage, Pyramid
Power Magazine, Rocky Mountain Chocolate Factory, Salute to Vienna,
Terra Breads, T Tea Room, The Fillip Review, the Morris and Helen
Belkin Art Gallery, the Vancouver Art Gallery, the Vancouver Recital
Society, Videomatica, UBC Bookstore, and Village VQA Wines—Dunbar.

Belkin Satellite, 555 Hamilton Street, Vancouver, BC V6B 2R1

Join Fillip and Vancouver"based writer Nathan Crompton on Saturday, November 18, at 
2pm for a seminar-style discussion on the writings of postwar Italian architecture critic 
Manfredo Tafuri. In his Architecture and Utopia (1973), Tafuri developed a neo-Marxist 
critique of twentieth century architectural modernism, raising questions about the 
apparent stability of postwar architecture and its politics of utopian reification. In 
response to the architecture of the welfare state, Tafuri develops a theoretically complex 
critique of the city that places urban development on a background of class conflict and 
political antagonism.

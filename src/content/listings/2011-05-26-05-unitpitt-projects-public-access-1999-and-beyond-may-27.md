Join us at UNIT/PITT Projects, 15 East Pender Street this Friday, May 27
from 8 to 11 pm for the launch of a book, commemorative VHS edition, and
encore screening of Public Access: 1999 And Beyond.

Curated by Weekend Leisure, this project includes work by Angus Ferguson,
Kaitlin Fontana & Nicole Passmore, Emmett Hall, Nicolas Sassoon, and Hank
Bull & Patrick Ready. The book, co-published by Publication Studio,
contains interviews, scripts and colour reproductions of this artists'
television project. The project is also available in a VHS edition, with
bonus extras.

Public Access: 1999 And Beyond will also screen continuously in the front
window of 15 East Pender during this event.

For more information, please see http://publicaccess.helenpittgallery.org/

UNIT/PITT Projects gratefully acknowledges the support of the British
Columbia Arts Council, the Canada Council, the City of Vancouver, and the
support of its members, volunteers and individual donors.

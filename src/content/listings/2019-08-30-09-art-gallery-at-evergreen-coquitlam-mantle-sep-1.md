MANTLE

Diyan Achjadi, Sean Alward, Tsema Igharas, Kevin Michael Murphy and Holly Schmidt

July 13 to September 1, 2019

Set against the picturesque backdrop of Lafarge Lake, a reclaimed sand and gravel 
quarry, the Art Gallery at Evergreen presents work by artists Diyan Achjadi, Sean 
Alward, Tsema Igharas, Kevin Michael Murphy and Holly Schmidt. These artists engage 
with ideas inspired by geology, resource extraction, Indigenous knowledges and deep 
time. The exhibition takes its title, Mantle, from both the inner layer of rock that forms 
the earths planetary body and the responsibility passed down from one person to 
another. As a verb, mantle also describes the covering of something, like a quarry, 
perhaps, that lies beneath the visible surface. Through artistic research, these artists 
explore the transformation of organic materials and draw learning from knowledge 
holders (be they people, land or other forces). The resulting artworks, which take the 
form of sculpture, photography, video, drawing and painting, draw attention to the 
interconnectedness of humankind and geology.

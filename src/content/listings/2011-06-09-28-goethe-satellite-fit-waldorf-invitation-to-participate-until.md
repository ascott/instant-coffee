Invitation to Participate
FIT - freie internationale tankstelle
Waldorf Hotel
June 2 – September 15, 2011

FIT is a “fueling station for the creative spirit”  by Berlin artist Dida
Zende, the first project presented in connection with the Goethe Satellite
Vancouver, a two-year initiative of the Goethe-Institut with partner
Revised Projects <http://www.revisedprojects.com/> .

Founded in 2002, FIT has created cultural platforms within industrial
structures such as gas stations in Berlin, Copenhagen and Miami. The
project is inspired by Joseph Beuys and the idea of social sculpture.

FIT is accepting proposals for projects to participate onsite at the FIT
over the summer. Projects can take any form, including exhibitions, events,
installations, performances, screenings or talks and can happen in any
timeframe, from one hour to two weeks.

The FIT Vancouver is located on the exterior west side of the Waldorf Hotel
in a custom architecture with interior measurements 40 feet by 10 feet. The
structure has a west-facing door that opens to create a stage, as well as
overhead skylights and solar power and can be equipped with speakers and
projectors.

Projects that are accepted to the FIT will become part of the global
project documentation.

To apply, please send a brief description of your project as well as a bio
and relevant links to info@revisedprojects.com and zende@gmx.de.
Applications are administered by Revised Projects and will be accepted on
an ongoing basis throughout the summer.

Box Studio | 7PM | JUN 23
ONLINE WORKSHOP - TRADE SECRETS: ARTIST RESIDENCIES WITH
SPARK
BOX STUDIO | JUNE 23rd 7pm- 8:30pm
EST | $30.00 | PRESENTED BY THE
GATHERED GALLERY, HOSTED ON ZOOM

Are you an artist who has been wanting to get away and create?

Do you love the idea of making work in a new environment and studio?

Do you love to connect with new people and share ideas and resources?

An artist residency might be a perfect experience for you! But maybe you dont
know about artist residencies or arent sure if they are the right fit for you.

In this workshop artist residency owners Chrissy Poitras and Kyle Topping speak
about all topics related to artist residencies. With 10+ years of running their
residency Spark Box Studio, which has hosted over 400 artists from all across
the globe, these two have loads of helpful tips to share.

This workshop will be focused on addressing the most common questions
artists
have, such as:

- Funding

- Where to find residency opportunities

- Understanding the application process

- Residency expectations

- Community engagement

- Best practices

Additionally, this workshop will act as an incubator for comprehensive solutions.
While a vital part of this workshop is Chrissy and Kyles commitment to sharing
their personal story and lessons theyve learned through running Spark Box
Studio, they also want to hear from you! Perhaps you have had a residency
experience and would like to share the knowledge you have. Perhaps their are
questions you want answered and havent found yet. This open dialogue,
moderated by Chrissy and Kyle, will generate a wealth of information, related to
the exact questions the you are seeking answers for.

Are you ready to join a supportive community of artists teaching you how to build
a profitable, sustainable, and authentic creative career?

Trade Secrets is a series of accessible online workshops for working artists to
help connect with industry experts & peers, and break down creative barriers,
and get the information you need to move your career forward.

(We have implemented a subsidized
payment option for those who self-identify as
members of the BIPOC and/or LGBTQ2S+ communities to support artists facing
statistically fewer opportunities in galleries, magazines, and institutions.)

I hear a silent dissonance

Woojae Kim, with Adrian Avendao

Feb 28, 2026

2PM

Please join us for the final day of the exhibition, with a live performance by Woojae Kim and
Adrian Avendano at Afternoon Projects, beginning at 2PM.

Woojae Kim is a Korean artist and writer living on the unceded territories of the
Swxw7mesh, Musqueam, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples (Vancouver, Canada). His works
explore rituals of interdependency and listening to inaudible frequencies of relationships with
non-humans and the land. His work has recently been exhibited in Vancouver (Artspeak,
Dreams Comma Delta), Toronto (The Plumb) and New York (Psychic Readings*). His texts
were published in the Canadian Art and the Capilano Review. Kim received an MFA from
Milton Avery Graduate School of the Arts at Bard College.

Adrian Avendao is a canadian sound artist, improvising percussionist and recordist based
in Vancouver, Canada [stolen First Nation territories of the xmky"m (Musqueam),
Swxw7mesh (Squamish), and slilwta (Tsleil-Waututh) nations] of Peruvian and Chilean
heritage. His work explores the connection between sound, space, and place through linear
and non-linear time. He has a deep connection and interest in ecological sound thinking. His
contributions can be seen in a diverse array of musical and sound art performances with local
and international artists.

MANAGING EDITOR, BLACKFLASH: Lens Site Scene 
 
Buffalo Berry Press, the publisher of the lens-based and new media arts journal BlackFlash: Lens Site Scene (www.blackflash.ca), is looking for a managing editor who has experience with small press publishing as well as knowledge of and interest in contemporary visual art and non-profit organizations, a strong grasp of the editorial process, experience with administrative duties, computer and web literacy and the ability to work collaboratively as well as independently. As a tri-annual publication, the journal requires a managing editor who is a self-motivated, multi-tasker with an eye for details and the ability to execute short and long term plans. The candidate should possess superior writing, communication and interpersonal skills. S/he must be well organized and able to direct and maintain production schedules and adhere to strict grant and publication deadlines. Buffalo Berry Press welcomes a creative problem-solver who has the desire to contribute to both the magazine’s 
mandate
and editorial vision as well as working with the board and Marketing Coordinator to expand our current subscriber base and funding resources. All responsibilities include working with those persons who are involved in fulfilling various tasks (board, editorial committee, designer, contributors, printer, granting agencies, marketing coordinator) with the aim of encouraging efficiency and maintaining of mutual responsibilities. 
 
Responsibilities related to the administrative and editorial management of BlackFlash magazine:

- working with editorial committee to find material for the magazine

- performing structural editing of written material

- managing magazine editorial, production and distribution; maintaining editorial flow

- budgeting, bookkeeping, preparing books for annual audit 

- report generating (annual report, grant reports, other legally mandated reporting)

- management of the marketing coordinator with circulation and promotional efforts

- coordination and execution of grant applications 


The ideal candidate will have demonstrated experience in the following areas:

Day to day office administration and financial management. 
Knowledge of and experience in small magazine management, including print production, editorial, circulation, distribution, marketing and promotion. 
Arts grant writing and knowledge of federal, provincial, and municipal agencies with regards to grant and funding opportunities. 
Non-profit arts management 
Filemaker Pro, Excel, photoshop, Quark and basic website maintenance 
Copy editing 
Timeline

Position begins 1st May 2008

$23,000 per annum + full benefits

Approximately 25 – 30 hrs per week

Guest speaker: Dr. Prabhjot Parmar

Talk: Coexistence

Saturday, February 10, 2-3:30pm

Free Event - pre-registration required.

The ACT Art Gallery

11944 Haney Place, Maple Ridge

Join us on Saturday, February 10 at 2pm for our next Inside/Out Tour and Talk,
Coexistence. The talk will be led by Dr. Prabhjot Parmar, Associate Professor in
Faculty of English at University of the Fraser Valley. Responding to the ACT Art
Gallerys current exhibition, Stories for Futures " Real and Imagined, Dr. Parmars
talk will focus on the art of storytelling within Diyan Achjadis and Sara Khans works.
The free event starts with a 30-minute Curators tour, followed by Dr. Parmars 1-
hour discussion-based talk.

About the
Series: Inside/Out is a series of talks and discussions that takes selected
ACT Art Gallery exhibitions as the catalyst to explore larger social, cultural, and
artistic issues. The series welcomes thinkers and writers from a wide variety of
disciplines whose work gives broader context to exhibitions in the gallery.

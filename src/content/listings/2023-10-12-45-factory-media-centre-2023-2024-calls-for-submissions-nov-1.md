2023-2024 Calls for Submissions

Factory Media Centre is excited to announce that submissions for our 2024
programming year are now open!

Applications are now open to Factory Media Center members for the following
programs: Technical Residency, &NOW Production Residency & Scholarship program,
and Exhibition Proposals for our 2024 programming calendar!

Multimedia and experimental film artists at any stage of their career are encouraged to
apply! Visit www.factorymediacentre.ca to learn more.

If you have any questions about the submission process or would like to become a
member with FMC please contact us at info@factorymediacentre.ca

We look forward to your submissions!

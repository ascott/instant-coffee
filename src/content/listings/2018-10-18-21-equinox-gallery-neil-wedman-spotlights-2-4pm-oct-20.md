Neil Wedman: Spotlights
October 20 - November 17, 2018

Neil Wedman: Spotlights is the culmination of work done over the summer of 2016 
and the summer of 2017. Consistency is key in this series of ten: each piece is 
vertically oriented and measures 78 inches by 60 inches, with each composition 
seemingly a copy of the previous. These paintings - each depicting the image of a 
spotlight on nothing but the rolling, vertical folds of a theatre curtain - are made 
through a process of extraction and dilution of the paint. Acrylic paint is mixed to a 
completely liquid consistency and poured down the surface of the canvas, causing 
the paint to behave in an atypical way where the individual components making up 
each colour separate from one another, creating subtle auras and stains as well as 
accidental blemishes and unanticipated inconsistencies. The paintings are 
composed around the description of recognizable forms with the aim of achieving 
pictures that fall perceptually between representation and abstraction.

Neil Wedman lives and works in Vancouver, BC.

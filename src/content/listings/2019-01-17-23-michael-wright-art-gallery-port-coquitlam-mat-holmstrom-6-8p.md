Mat Holmstrom
Mount Delectable

Opening Reception: January 24th, 2019 | 6-8pm
Show Dates: January 10 - April 29, 2019
2253 Leigh Square, Port Coquitlam, BC

The Michael Wright Art Gallery presents Vancouver artist Mat Holmstrom's third solo 
exhibit. Mount Delectable explores both classic and contemporary subject matter 
presented onon paper, canvas and sculpture.

--
Why climbst thou not the Mount Delectable,
Which is the source and cause of every joy? - Inferno, Canto I, The Divine Comedy
--

The Mount Delectable is referenced as a thematic title, depicting the artists own 
sources of joy residing in nature, love and harmony. A rich color palette and the 
integration of metallic paint stimulates a unique viewing experience in a variety of 
lighting situations. 

Originally from Beaumont, Alberta, Mats practice is influenced by his French, 
Scandinavian and Canadian ancestry, resulting in a distinct style that fuses modern 
design knowledge with painterly intuition. Holmstrom studied at the Alberta College 
of Art and Design and MacEwan University. His work is in private collections across 
Canada.

Evolution of Inspiration
Opening Reception: November 6, 2009, 8 pm
Gallery Atsui, 602 East Hastings (at Princess).

"If history had worked out differently, and Michelangelo had been
commissioned to paint a ceiling for a giant Museum of Science, mightn't he
have produced something at least as inspirational as the Sistine Chapel?"
Richard Dawkins, The God Delusion

With Dawkins' questions in mind and coinciding with the bicentennial of
Charles Darwin's birth, as well as November 2009 being the 150th
anniversary of the publication of Darwin's seminal work On the Origin of
Species, Gallery Atsui is thrilled to present an exhibition of art
influenced by science, featuring new and original works by: Verena
Kaminiarz, Mark Mushet, Sonja Hébert, Jesse Gray, Suzanne Kay, Helen Eady,
Patrick Blaeser.

Exhibition on display until November 28, 2009. Visit www.galleryatsui.com
for gallery hours in November, and for more information.

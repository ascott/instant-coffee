Ceramair Artist in Residence Program
Caetani Cultural Centre, Vernon, BC, Canada

DATES: January 3rd to April 5th 2019 (dates are somewhat flexible)
APPLICATION DEADLINE: November 5th, 2018

WHO IS ELIGIBLE: Open to emerging and established visual artists in ceramic or 
overlapping interdisciplinary practices.

INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: accommodations at the Caetani Cultural Centre (a 
heritage house walking distance of downtown Vernon) and a shared studio at the Vernon 
Community Art Centre. A solo exhibition at the end of the residency.

NOT INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: travel expenses, food, health insurance, art 
materials, shipping costs, exhibition costs and other personal expenses.

LEARN MORE + APPLY HERE: https://www.caetani.org/ceramair-ceramics-residency/

grunt gallery
Archive Project Launch Party!

Come one, come all!
Saturday June 23, 2012, 7-10pm
At grunt gallery

After a year of organizing, digitizing, contracting, web designing, writing, 
and curating grunt is ready to launch Activating the Archive! Through 
this project we have developed six curated websites and an interactive 
database featuring content from grunt's archive. Join us this Saturday 
for a party to celebrate the rich history of grunt gallery and the work 
that's been done on this project.

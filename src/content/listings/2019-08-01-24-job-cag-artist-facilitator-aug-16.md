Artist Facilitator

The Contemporary Art Gallery (CAG) is searching for an Artist Facilitator

Contemporary Art Gallery
The Contemporary Art Gallery is Vancouvers longest standing independent art gallery 
dedicated to the research, exhibition, education and documentation of contemporary 
visual art as it is practiced locally through to internationally. It aspires to generate 
significant audiences for its innovative and diverse programs through free access and a 
profile that is international in scope. CAG aims to be perceived as a producer and 
transmitter of new ideas and activities, as a container for artistic experience. Radiating 
out from our centre in Vancouver, our program seeks to signify an understanding of art 
as something of meaning in our daily lives, asserting a continuum, enmeshed within and 
of significance to all other experience. We deliver around twenty exhibitions, off-site 
projects and residencies each year, have an ongoing publication series and provide an 
extensive range of learning and public programs for adults, families and children. For 
more information about CAG, please visit www.contemporaryartgallery.ca.

Get R.E.A.L

Get R.E.A.L comprises a group of programs at Windermere Secondary School offered 
by the Windermere Community Schools Team (CST) providing support to referred youth 
in Grades 8-9 and Grades 10-12. R.E.A.L stands for Resilient, Engaged, Active 
Leaders, emphasizing the potential of participants to contribute to their school and the 
wider community. CAG has an ongoing partnership with Windermere CST to support 
Get R.E.A.L through the development of media projects based on issues, interests or 
topics brought forth by participants. The group is student-led and supported by CST 
programmers and staff and community partners including the Artist Facilitator. 
Participants meet regularly on Wednesdays and Thursdays from 3pm to 5pm. Additional 
cultural and recreational fieldtrips occasionally happen outside regular program times. 

Job Summary
Working closely with CAG, the Artist Facilitator will collaborate with CST programming 
staff to develop and produce a media arts project led by the program participants. The 
Artist Facilitator will plan and deliver workshops, including organizing workshops 
featuring guest artists, to enable the conceptualization and production of a media arts or 
film project, giving participants the skills and confidence needed for basic video 
production. The Artist Facilitator will be responsible for editing any footage and creative 
content to create a final presentation and/or 5-10 minute video, culminating in a final 
screening/presentation event at CAG, incorporating feedback from Get R.E.A.L 
members and support staff. Some weekend and evening work is required.

The project starts early September 2019 and ends June 2020. It is expected there will 
be flexibility of delivery and time across the three phases of the project including pre-
production, production and post-production with attendance at weekly workshop 
sessions in collaboration with CST staff.

Key Responsibilities

Supported and supervised by the Curator of Learning & Public Engagement, the Artist 
Facilitator will be responsible for:

Facilitation:

Working with youth of diverse backgrounds, knowledge and experiences
Mentorship
Collaborative community-based filmmaking or art practices
Planning and delivering workshops leading toward specific professional quality 
outcomes
Facilitating decision making and consensus building

Technical:

Strong proficiency in all aspects of filmmaking: camera, sound, directing, editing, 
cinematography, etc. across all stages of development
Coordination of a video project from pre-production to post-production
Operation of camera and sound equipment
Experience in working with a range of video formats and sources encompassing 
iPhones, iPads, through to professional standard cameras
Proven experience of direction, composition and providing instruction to participants as 
needed
Ability to work across a range of visual, moving image styles " documentary, music 
video, fiction, etc.
Experience with and access to editing software such as Premiere Pro, Final Cut, Pro 
Tools

While these duties and responsibilities are representative, the incumbent may be 
required to perform other duties as assigned within the context of the project as a whole.

Qualifications

A degree in an arts discipline with particular knowledge of video production plus 2-3 
years experience developing, facilitating, and leading others in the delivery of public 
programs for diverse audiences including work with youth. A criminal record background 
check with a satisfactory result is a pre-contractual condition.

Skills and Abilities
Flexible and able to improvise
Strong problem solving skills
Collaborative and inclusive approach
Comfortable with student-led processes
Punctual and able to meet deadlines

Remuneration

The position is offered at a rate of $25 per hour with a maximum fee not to exceed 
$3,800 for the complete project.

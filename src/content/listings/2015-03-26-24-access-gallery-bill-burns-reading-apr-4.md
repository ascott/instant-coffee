Bill Burns Reading Saturday April 4, 2015 at 2:00 PM at Access Gallery

We are pleased to announce that Bill Burns will read a few passages and

Bill Burns, Jenifer Allen, Dannys Montes de Oca Moreda and Dan Adler and is
published by YYZ BOOKS and BLACK DOG PUBLISHING.

Access Gallery is located at 222 East Georgia Street, Vancouver.

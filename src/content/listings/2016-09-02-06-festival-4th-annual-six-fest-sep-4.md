4th Annual SIX Fest

Sunday September 4th, 2016 
4:00 PM - 9:00 PM 
6th Avenue between Manitoba and Ontario Street, 
Vancouver, BC.

Hosted by SIX FEST & SiteFactory. Join us in the 4th Annual SIX 
Fest, an all ages, free, day-long gathering that enhances the local 
neighbourhood by celebrating community, artistic expression, and 
homegrown food. The interdisciplinary arts event and community 
gathering includes the following: musical performances on house 
porches, visual artists, street installations, storytelling, community 
gardening initiatives and hosting a children's art and zine creation 
station. The event will also feature a shared community meal, seed 
sharing and gardening station. 

Featuring:
Agente Costura & SiteFactory
prOphecy sun
Children's Storytelling
Lantern Ritual & Video Projections
Green thumb gardening collective

Agente Costura & SiteFactory
Berlin-based performance artist Lisa Simpson a.k.a. Agente 
Costura will join suitcase drum legend Ben Brown, choreographer 
Kelly McInness and invited dancers to present an improvised 
Musical Sewing Performance, making music out of making clothes. 
The collaborative performance joins stitching, movement and sound 
to explore notions of consumption as well as social relation to 
garments. The September 4th SIX FEST performance will take 
place on the SiteFactory bus, followed by two days of Agente 
Costura's Itinerant Sewing Station, also on the SiteFactory bus. For 
more info visit sitefactory.ca

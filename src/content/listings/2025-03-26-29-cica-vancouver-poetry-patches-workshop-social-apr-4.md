Join us for Poetry
Patches Workshop + Social, a creative gathering for the 2STGD
community. This is a chance to slow down, make something with your hands, and
connect with peers in an affirming space.

Well explore the connection between poetry and visual art, transforming words and
emotions into custom fabric patches for your clothes, walls, or wherever you want
them. With private access to Clouds for Breakfast by non-binary artist Daisy Parris,
well reflect on their powerful work and draw inspiration from their exploration of
identity and feeling deeply.

All materials are provided, with loose guidance to help spark your creativity. Whether
youre a maker or just curious, come join us for a thoughtful, hands-on evening of
art, community, and connection. Light refreshments included.

Cost: Sliding scale $20 ~ $50

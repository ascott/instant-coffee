We are pleased to announce the opening of SALT SPRING STORIES at ArtSpring, 
100 Jackson Ave, Salt Spring Island.

Opening reception on Aug 11, 5-7 PM.
Exhibit open Aug 12 - Aug 22, 10 AM-4 PM, with daily speaker series (details 
below).

Stories are at the heart of the way we create and communicate meaning.

SALT SPRING STORIES by artists Greg Klassen and Andrea Locke is an exhibit of 
images and text designed to communicate our sense of wonder and awe at this 
amazing place, and to provide a space to come together as a community to share in 
the stories of this place and its people and in the making of new stories.

Anchoring the exhibit are Gregs photographs - composites of past and present. 
Each image is designed to simultaneously engage the reader in a story about the 
historical context associated with specific sites around the island and to contribute to 
the collective story of this island, its people, and its larger context in contemporary 
Canadian society. Accompanying texts, written by Andrea, interpret the images and 
serve as a jumping-off point for further storytelling based on written and oral 
histories, the collections of the Salt Spring Historical Society, and our own 
experiences. The images, text and a daily speaker series highlight the diversity of 
communities that have made Salt Spring what it is today. The speakers will present 
stories and perspectives of a wide range of communities with important roots in the 
past and present of the island " including First Nations, Kanaka settlers, Black 
settlers, and Japanese-Canadian history; the arts, disability, and LGBTQ 
communities; and present-day life on Salt Spring (as experienced by Arthur Black). 
Visitors to the exhibit will have the opportunity to record their own stories.

Greg Klassen is a visual artist who refers to himself as a photo-based Autist. 
Diagnosed with Aspergers Syndrome in his fourth decade of life, many of Gregs 
artistic explorations have focused on questions of identity and he is an active 
advocate for the disability community through his artwork. Andrea Locke is a 
relentless researcher, writer, fiber artist, goat-keeper, gardener and marine biologist. 
Greg and Andrea moved to Salt Spring in 2012 and plan to stay here forever, or 
longer if possible.

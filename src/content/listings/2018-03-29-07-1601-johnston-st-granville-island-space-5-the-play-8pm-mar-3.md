Space 5 - The Play - Saturday, March 31st - 8pm - 1601 Johnston St - Granville 
Island

ONE NIGHT ONLY! Queer Future Sci Fi Love Story - A cluster fuck of hot AF 
gayliens fighting for their existence, travelling the universe for love, and battling 
oppressive Zyborgs with their super powers and lazer spore guns.

Written & Directed by Luey McQuaid (with the help and support of her many brilliant 
and wonderful friends). McQuaid is a community theatre director based in 
Vancouver. Her previous plays include Annie The Musical and Rage! Distraction! 
Witchcraft! She aims to provide inclusive theater to friends and an extended social 
network with varying acting experience and opportunities in the professional theater 
world. This play explores the struggles queers face dating and why often long 
distance dating is a regular occurrence within LGBTQI2S relationships. This show is 
not to be missed!

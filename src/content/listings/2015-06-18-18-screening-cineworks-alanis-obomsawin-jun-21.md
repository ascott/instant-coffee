In celebration of National Aboriginal Day 2015, DOC BC and Cineworks, in
collaboration with the NFB, present a special edition of Doc Talk:

Kanehsatake: 270 Years of Resistance
Director: Alanis Obomsawin
1993, 119 min

On a July day in 1990, a confrontation propelled Native issues in
Kanehsatake and the village of Oka, Quebec, into the international
spotlight. Director Alanis Obomsawin spent 78 nerve-wracking days and
nights filming the armed stand-off between the Mohawks, the Quebec police
and the Canadian army.

This powerful documentary takes you right into the action of an age-old
Aboriginal struggle. The result is a portrait of the people behind the
barricades.

This film contains scenes of violence. Viewer discretion is advised.

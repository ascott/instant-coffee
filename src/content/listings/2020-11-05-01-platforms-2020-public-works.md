For the November edition of "Platforms 2020: Public Works", the City of Vancouver Public
Art Program presents artworks on
transit shelter, billboards and screens throughout the
city by the artists Sunroop Kaur, Dejan Radovanovic, Osvaldo Ramirez Castillo, Manuel
Axel Strain, Gerri York and Ryley OByrne. The projects presented were selected through
an open call inviting Vancouver-based artists to share artworks that they have been
working on, which respond to our current circumstances of living during a pandemic and
critical time of change. For more information about this program visit:

vancouver.ca/platforms

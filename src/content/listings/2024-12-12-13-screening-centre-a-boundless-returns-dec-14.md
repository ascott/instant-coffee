This is your last chance to view Centre A's film screening: boundless returns, bringing
together four films by the artists Rehab Nazzal, Basel Abbas & Ruanne Abou-Rahme,
Razan AlSalah, and Larissa Sansour.

Looking to how Palestinian artists are employing lens-based mediums to disrupt colonial
framings and contribute to a living archive of resistance--the artists in this series turn to
the language and potential of vegetation, archaeology, digital mapping, and memory as
potent spaces to assert Palestinian presence, while questioning the limits and
possibilities conjured by these returns.

The films will be on view in Centre A's New Media Gallery from November 30 to
December 14.

Film Screening Period: November 30-December 14
Gallery Hours: Weds-Sat, 12-6 PM
Location: Centre A (205-268 Keefer Street)

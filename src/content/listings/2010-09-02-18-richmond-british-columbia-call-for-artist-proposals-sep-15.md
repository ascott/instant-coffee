Call for Artist Proposals
No. 4 Road Pump Station "A Working River" Bas-relief
Richmond, British Columbia, Canada
Artist Fee: $5,000
Deadline for Proposals: Wednesday, September 15, 2010, 4:00 pm

The City of Richmond Public Art Program invites professional and
emerging artists/artist teams, 18 years of age or older, and who reside
within the Metro Vancouver region to participate in a one-stage
competition to provide an original concept design for a bas-relief to be
incorporated into the north and east facades of the City of Richmond's
No. 4 Road Pump Station. The selected artist/artist team will develop a
detailed design for a bas-relief to be fabricated and installed by the
City of Richmond. Artists are encouraged to consider creative designs as
they interpret the theme, "A Working River".
Full project information is available at:
http://www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities.htm

Please send any questions to Eric Fiss, Public Art Planner, City
of Richmond mailto:efiss@richmond.ca

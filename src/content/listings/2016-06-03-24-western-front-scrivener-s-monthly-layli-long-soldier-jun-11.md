SCRIVENER'S MONTHLY
LAYLI LONG SOLDIER READING FROM WHEREAS

Saturday, June 11 @ 2:00pm

Scrivener's Monthly welcomes poet Layli Long Soldier for a reading from her
forthcoming book Whereas. Written in response to the Congressional
Resolution of Apology to Native Americans signed by Barack Obama in 2009,
Long Soldier's project addresses the language and delivery of the Apology.
Following the reading, Long Soldier will be joined by curator cheyanne
turions for a conversation about her work.

Layli Long Solider is a graduate of the MFA program at Bard College in New
York and her writing has appeared in The American Poet, American Indian
Journal of Culture and Research, PEN America, The Brooklyn Rail, and Mud
City, among others. She lives in Tsaile, AZ on the Navajo Nation.

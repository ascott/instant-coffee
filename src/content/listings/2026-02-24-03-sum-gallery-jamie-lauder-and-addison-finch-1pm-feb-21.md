Join us on February 21st at 1pm for an Artist Talk with Jamie Lauder and Addison Finch as
they discuss the themes and works of our group exhibition, Gender Mutual.

Gender Mutual brings together works by six artists from across the gender spectrum. Though
the exhibition presents works that vary in medium and scope, all are deeply personal and
speak to the unique, often non-linear personal journeys of those living beyond the binary.
Bringing together painting, textile art, printmaking, photography + projection, sound art, and
costume design, Gender Mutual invites audiences into a space of reflection and possibility.

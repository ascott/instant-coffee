221A
SAD 2: On Spirits and Sadness, talks and tastings by David McIntosh and 
the Everything Co.. Spirits by Jeffrey Allport/ Kara Uzelman and the 
Everything Co. 
APRIL 5, 8pm

Here, spirits are investigated as a companion to artistic practice, not only in 
their consumption, but in their means of production. Kara Uzelman, with 
partner and collaborator Jeffrey Allport, has created a artist edition of 
moonshine-- distilled in Nokomis, SK, in the landscape that inspired her 
recent body of work. This event encourages the act of tasting as a shared 
avenue for interpretation.

David McIntosh continues his Bob’s Salon Series at 221A. This series pairs 
artists and performers with unique, guided tastings. Utilising his training as a 
sommelier to match an alcohol to a work, David will pour Kara and Jeffrey's 
moonshine, alongside a reading of the exhibition. By drawing attention to the 
sensations of our own bodies when tasting a spirit, Bob’s Salon creates a 
shareable conversation that enhances engagement, uniting artists and 
audiences in a common lived experience. This evening's presentation will also 
feature performers Arash Khakpour and Alana Gerecke.

The Everything Co., consisting of local artists Jason Gowans, Michael Love 
and Chris Dahl, will expand on their practice of distillation in the context of 
artistic production. In honour of the exhibition, The Everything Co. will pour 
small tastings of their new production: a 19th-century recipe for absinthe.

SAD is a series of talks accompanying the exhibition Stratiform | Kara 
Uzelman. Curated by Vanessa Kwan and Kimberly Phillips, these 
presentations investigate objects and practices in the context of 
contemporary forms of melancholy. For more information, visit 
[http://221a.ca/stratiform](http://221a.ca/stratiform)

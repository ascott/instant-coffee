Art School High:Influence and Inspiration

A poetry workshop with Sheryda Warrener in response to the summer exhibition at 
the Gordon Smith Gallery of Canadian Art: Art School High

Under each picture there is always another picture, wrote historian and art critic 
Douglas Crimp. Workshop participants will reflect on the currentArt School 
Highexhibit, generating original material through a series of generative writing 
prompts based on mash-up methodologies. At play with memories of lived high 
school experiences - as well as notions of originality, identity, influence and imitation 
- we will write in the space of the gallery in direct response to the images on display. 

Open to both emerging and established writers in the community!

About Sheryda Warrener

Sheryda Warreneris the author of two poetry collections, most recentlyFloating is

Everything(Nightwood, 2015). In 2016, she became the director of the inaugural

Artspeak Studio for Emerging Writers, where she mentors students who use 
language

primarily as a material in visual art.

About Art School High

From May 13th to August 26, 2017, the Smith FoundationpresentsArt School 
Highat the

Gordon Smith Galleryof Canadian Art. The exhibitionexplores socio-cultural issues 
relating to

identity, adolescence, conformity,marginalization, and rebellious behaviours. Invited 
artists

have made significant works reflecting on the experienceof attending high school. 
These works

probe the very nature of learning in diverse environments. Schooling may have 
made us who we

are, but it has also been a source of serious anxieties - and for many, a time of 
traumatic

experiences.

Curated by Patrik Andersson, participating artists includeKarin Buba, John Collins, 
Rodney

Graham, Scott Livingstone, Ken Lum, Kyla Mallet, Jean MacRae, Shannon 
Oksanen, Kathy Slade,

Ron Terada,Neil Wedman and David Wisdom" all artists who have made 
significant work that

reflect on the high school experience.

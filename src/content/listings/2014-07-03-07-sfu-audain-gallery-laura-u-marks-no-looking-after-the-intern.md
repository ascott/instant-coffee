No Looking After the Internet: Affective Analysis
Facilitated by Laura U. Marks
Wednesday, July 9, 6pm
Audain Gallery

In support of Ursula Mayer’s exhibition Not a curse, nor a bargain, but a
hymn, which runs until August 2 at the Audain Gallery, Laura U. Marks will
facilitate an open reading of Mayer’s images using affective analysis, a
method that postpones thinking in order to attend to affective and bodily
responses and perceptions. Laura U. Marks is a scholar, theorist and
programmer of independent and experimental media arts. She teaches in the
School for the Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser University.

No Looking After the Internet prompts the open reading of images and
objects, and encourages visual literacy through sustained private and
public attention in the gallery.

Curated by Amy Kazymerchyk, Not a curse, nor a bargain, but a hymn is
presented with support from the The Austrian Federal Chancellery, Division
II: Arts.

All works are courtesy of the artist; Juliètte Jongma, Amsterdam; Krobath
Wien | Berlin; and Monitor, Rome.

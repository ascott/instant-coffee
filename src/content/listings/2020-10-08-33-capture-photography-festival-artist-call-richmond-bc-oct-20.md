Capture Photography Festival Artist Call, Richmond, BC

The Richmond Public Art Program and Richmond Art Gallery in partnership with the Capture
Photography Festival invite artists and artist collectives to submit proposals for temporary,
photo-based public art installations to be presented at a Richmond Canada Line station. The
artist projects will be part of the Capture Photography Festival in April 2021.

Artist Fee: $1,500.00 CAD
Deadline: October 20, 2020, 5:00pm PDT
Duration: April, 2021 - September, 2021

For more information and to apply for this artist opportunity, please visit:
www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities

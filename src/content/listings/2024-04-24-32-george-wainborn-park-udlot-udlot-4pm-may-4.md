As part of the multi-part project, Jos Maceda: Echoes Beyond the Archipelago,
Western Front and the Roundhouse Community Arts & Recreation Centre are pleased
to present a performance of Jos Macedas composition Udlot Udlot at George
Wainborn Park, Vancouver.

Udlot Udlot is a 40-minute composition for 30 up to 1,000
performers. Composed for
simple traditional bamboo instruments, wooden percussion sticks, and voices, the work
was designed so performers without musical expertise could equally participate.
Modelled on tropical rain forest rituals and ceremonies, Udlot Udlot implies a
fundamental connection between human activities and nature, and explores how
drone, colour, and melody come together as independent elements in performance.
Udlot Udlot was first performed in 1975 by 800 students at the University of the
Philippines, and has since been presented internationally and recognized as a key
work within Macedas career and the canon of avant-garde performance. In Vancouver,
the work will be performed by 100 community members who responded to an open
call.

To learn more, visit our website: westernfront.ca/events/udlot-udlot

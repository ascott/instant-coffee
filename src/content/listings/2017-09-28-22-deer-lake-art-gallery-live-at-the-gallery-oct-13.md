Event Title: Live at the Gallery, October 2017
Event Date: October 13th, 2017, 7 to 9 pm
Featured Artists: Matt Silverman and Joel MacKenzie
Media: Live music
Tickets: FREE General Admission ~ www.eventbrite.com

Live at the Gallery is a monthly concert series being held at the Deer Lake Art Gallery. 
The first concert features local artists Matt Silverman and Joel MacKenzie. The event is 
being hosted and organized by the Burnaby Arts Council. The first Live at the Gallery 
event will be held at 7 pm on Friday, October 13th.

Live at the Gallery is a chance to bring music into the gallery setting, to connect the 
visual and the musical arts, and the fans of both. Live at the Gallery is an exciting mix of 
art and sound! From the sweet sounds of classical music to chilled out beats of local 
folk bands. Expect the unexpected!

The Burnaby Arts Council are passionate about promoting the arts as a vital component 
of our citys culture. We take great pleasure in offering this FREE music concert series 
and welcome you to the Deer Lake Art Gallery! Live at the Gallery is an opportunity for 
Deer Lake to have another regular venue for local musicians to play and local 
enthusiasts to listen. It also provides an opportunity to subvert the often rigid structure of 
an art gallery by having an interactive presentation being held within it.

Silverman is from Burnaby, BC, a Capilano University graduate and holds a Bachelor in 
Performance specializing in the classical guitar from the University of Victoria. He 
started playing the bass at age 13 and gradually switched to electric guitar, eventually 
finding the classical guitar. Matt performs solo or with the Capilano Guitar Trio at public 
and private events around the lower mainland. More recently he has been composing 
original instrumental music for the guitar. Matt is also a full time private music instructor 
at Neil Douglas Guitar Shop in New Westminster where he has over 50 students. 

MacKenzie is a performer and teacher. He received a minor in music composition from 
SFU before studying jazz guitar at VCC, where he currently attends. MacKenzie has 
performed in the groups Audinos, Ecstatic Waves, as a solo singer/songwriter, and as 
an actor in the Burnaby Arts Council Summer Theatre program. His influence from all 
types of music, from jazz to pop, comes through his writing and playing. He is a 
passionate performer with a commanding stage presence that has to be seen. You can 
find more info at www.joelmackenzie.ca.

Please join us at the Deer Lake Gallery for Live at the Gallery on Friday, October 13th 
@ 7 pm.

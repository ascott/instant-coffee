3/3 SFU MFA Graduating Exhibition - 

Artists: Steve Hubert, Ryan Mathieson, and Anna Marie Repstock
Runs: Sept. 20 - 29, 2012
Audain Gallery, Simon Fraser University, Goldcorp Centre for the Arts. 149 
West Hastings

Gallery Hours: Tuesday-Saturday 12-6pm

Exploring the unresolved interplay between subject matter and material form, 
Steve Hubert's large-format oil paintings of rarified settings are populated with 
fountains, nudes, musicians, and other coded objects. 

Ryan Mathieson assemblages, made out of a combination of found and made 
materials, question the way we think about and use objects, including 
photographic objects.

In Anna Marie Repstock's work, the thickness of oil paint and the temporality of 
meaning in language underpin the development of her own philosophy of text 
painting.

3/3 showcases the graduating projects of the MFA Visual Arts students at 
Simon Fraser University's School for the Contemporary Arts. A month long 
exhibition featuring new works in video, sculpture, painting, and photography, 
3/3 is separated into two parts, each showcasing the work of three MFA 
candidates. This exhibition is presented by the School for the Contemporary 
Arts in partial fulfillment of the requirements for the degree of Master of Fine 
Arts. Please join us as we celebrate the completion of our degrees.

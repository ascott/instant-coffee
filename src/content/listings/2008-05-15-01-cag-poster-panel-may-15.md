The Contemporary Art Gallery presents:

POSTER PANEL

ALLISON HRABLUIK
MATT HERN
BYRON CAMERAMAN

Moderated by LORNA BROWN

Thursday May 15th 7pm.

A public forum about urban postering: what are the aesthetic and rhetorical uses of this form? Three speakers will present views across the spectrum from art to activism to open a public forum about postering, its uses and it prevalence in the visual environment. 

 The Contemporary Art Gallery is generously supported by the Canada Council for the Arts, the City of Vancouver and the Province of BC through the BC Arts Council, the BC Gaming Policy and Enforcement Branch and the BC Arts Renaissance Fund which is administered by Vancouver Foundation.  We are grateful for the support of The Andy Warhol Foundation for the Visual Arts, Vancouver Foundation and our members, donors and volunteers.

 Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, Vancouver, BC, V6B 6R5, Canada www.contemporaryartgallery.ca. Gallery Hours: Wed. - Sun. 12 – 6 pm. Admission is free by donation.

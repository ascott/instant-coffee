We will be scheduling opening hours from Wednesday to Friday, July 29 to 31, 2020, for
our current exhibition Pixy Liao: Experimental Relationship (for your eyes only, or maybe
mine, too). This will be the only chance to see the exhibition in person!

Pre-book your visit: info@centrea.org

Guidelines for your visit:

In order to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for our visitors, we encourage you
to book your visit in advance. We have made changes to ensure physical distancing,
including limited occupancy and implementing enhanced cleaning procedures. We ask
that you refrain from touching artworks and displays in the gallery. The Gallery Boutique
and Library
will be closed; however, items will be available by request. Please visit our
website www.centrea.org for written information about exhibitions and to access our
online content.

The Sun Wah Centre is not currently open to the public, please locate the security guard
posted at the front gate to be let into the building. Otherwise, please call Centre A at
(604) 683-8326. We thank you for your cooperation and patience.

We encourage you to wear a mask while in the gallery, and that you sanitize your hands
before and after visiting. Masks and hand sanitizer will be available for you as needed.

Private bookings for individuals who are immunocompromised or otherwise identify as
high risk are available, please email us at info@centrea.org to schedule an appointment.

Our next exhibition, We cast Spells on the Daughters of our Mothers and Mothers of our
Daughters, curated by our Curatorial Skills Development Intern, Hana Amani, will open
on August 14. The exhibition will be available for
viewing between Wednesday to Friday,
from noon to 5 PM, or by appointment, as of August 14.
Centre A gratefully acknowledges the generous support of the Canada Council for the
Arts, the British Columbia Arts Council, the Government of British Columbia, the City of
Vancouver, and all of our funders, donors, and programming partners.

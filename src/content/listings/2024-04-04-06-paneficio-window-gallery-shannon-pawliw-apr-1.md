Paneficio Window Gallery is showing works by Shannon Pawliw from her series
Weavings and Water for the month of April.

Her abstract paintings reflect a profound exploration of vibrant emotions and experiences.
Each canvas captures the interplay of colours, shapes, and textures.
Many of the titles draw inspiration from the verses of E.E. Cummings, which adds an
additional layer of meaning to the compositions. www.paneficiogallery.com800 Keefer

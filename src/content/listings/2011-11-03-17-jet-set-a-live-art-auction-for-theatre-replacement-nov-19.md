JET SET helps to raise much needed funds for Theatre Replacement's touring 
works. This year we are very pleased to have available the work of some truly 
amazing local artists. Our authentic prairie auctioneer, Wally Roth will take you 
through the thrilling paces! A collaboration between the local visual arts 
community and Theatre Replacement, JET SET is a wonderful way to support 
our artists and the arts. In the weeks leading up to the event, we will feature one 
or two of these artists in our ARTIST SPOTLIGHT. Get to know them and their 
work before coming to bid at JET SET by contacting 
ruthie@theatrereplacement.org to receive ARTIST SPOTLIGHT newsletters.

Angela Teng

Up a Lemon Tree

September 14-November 3, 2019

Opening Reception

Saturday, September 14, 2019, 6-9 pm.

A solo exhibition of abstract works by Vancouver-based artist Angela Teng opens the Art 
Gallery at Evergreens 2019/20 season. Known for her experimentation with the medium 
of paint, Teng has gained national recognition for her innovative process of crocheting 
acrylic into geometric paintings"just one of her distinctive methods of painting.

Angela Teng is an artist based in Vancouver. Her work has been presented nationally 
and internationally, including as part of Vancouver Special: Ambivalent Pleasures at the 
Vancouver Art Gallery in 2017. Teng was a finalist for the RBC Canadian Painting 
Competition in 2016 and 2017. She received her BFA from Emily Carr University of Art + 
Design, Vancouver, in 2011.

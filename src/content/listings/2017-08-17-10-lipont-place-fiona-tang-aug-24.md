Artist Talk ft Fiona Tang
Thursday, August 24, 2017 from 6:00 PM - 8:00 PM

Lipont Place
4211 Number 3 Road
Richmond, BC V6X 2C3

The stunning large-scale drawings of animals by local artist Fiona Tang have been 
drawing much attention in recent years. While her work is on display at Vancouver 
Lipont Art Centre's Work on Paper summer group exhibition, she will be hosting a 
demonstration on her creative process, from inspiration to completion. She will also be 
explaining how creatures choose to be painted; showing some of the clay figurines that 
she created to understand the anatomy of the animals and how she translates it onto 
paper. She will also be demonstrating the media she works with in a very expressive 
way.

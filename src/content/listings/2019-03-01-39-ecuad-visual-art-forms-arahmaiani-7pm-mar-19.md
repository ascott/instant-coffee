Please join us for a talk with visiting artist Arahmaiani.
March 19 at 7pm in the Reliance Theatre at Emily Carr University of Art 
+ Design. 

Born in Bandung in 1961, Arahmaiani is a leading figure in the 
contemporary art scene in Indonesia, and a pioneer in the field of 
performance art in Southeast Asia. Her works, ranging from 
performance to painting, installation, video, poetry, dance and music 
have been exhibited at major institutions worldwide. She was one of 
the featured artists in the Indonesia National Pavilion at the 50th 
Venice Biennale in 2003, and participated in international events such 
as the Gwangju Biennale (2002); Bienal de So Paulo (2002), 
Performance Biennale, Israel (2001); Biennale de Lyon (2000), among 
others, and most recently at Kunming Biennale (2016) and Yinchuan 
Biennale (2018). 

Her work has grappled with contemporary politics, violence, critique of 
capitalism, the female body and in recent years, her own identity, which 
mediates between Islamic, Hindu, Buddhist, and animist beliefs. She 
often uses her public presence in order to attract attention to violence 
and discrimination. Since September 11, she has been working at the 
intersection of critical perspectives towards Islam all the while fighting 
against its general stigmatization. She has spent the past several years 
working with Tibetan monks to confront environmental issues in Tibet 
Plateau.

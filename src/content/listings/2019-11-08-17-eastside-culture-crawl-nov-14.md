Eastside Culture Crawl - November 14-17, 2019

The 23rd edition of the Eastside Culture Crawl, Vancouvers beloved four-day
celebration of visual arts,
design, and crafts - runs November 14-17, 2019 in 500 +
artists studios in 80 buildings throughout Vancouvers Eastside. The Crawl is the heart
of Vancouvers cultural district where art happens. Boasting its largest line-up of artists,
venues, and events to date, the Crawl will welcome more than 45,000 visitors to
intimately experience this vibrant and inspiring neighbourhood - the most densely
populated community of artists across Canada. Patrons are invited to attend a series of
talks the week leading up to the Crawl, as well as intimate workshops and
demonstrations in pottery, live chainsaw carving, natural textile dyeing, and
glassblowing to deepen their understanding of the unique art-making process. The
Eastside Culture Crawl runs November 14 & 15 from 5pm-10pm and November 16 & 17
from 11am-6pm.

For more information, visit culturecrawl.ca

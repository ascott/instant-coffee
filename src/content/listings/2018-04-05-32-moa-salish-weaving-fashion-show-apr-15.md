The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC invites visitors to celebrate the closing of 
the Fabric of Our Land exhibition with a Salish Weaving Fashion Show on Sunday, April 
15, 2018 at 1pm. Weavers will walk down the catwalk in MOAs iconic Great Hall, 
modelling their fabulous works. This is the last chance to visit the popular and innovative 
exhibition, which shares a rich legacy of traditional weaving with the public and inspires 
contemporary weavers.

Free with museum admission. For more information visit: 
https://moa.ubc.ca/event/salish-weaving-fashion-show/

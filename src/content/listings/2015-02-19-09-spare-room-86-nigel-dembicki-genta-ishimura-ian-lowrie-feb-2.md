"86"
Nigel Dembicki
Genta Ishimura
Ian Lowrie

Curated by Sung Pil Yoon

Inaugural opening reception: Friday February 20, 2015 19:00
Exhibition date: February 21 - March 20, 2015

SPARE ROOM
2F / 222 E. Georgia - Chinatown
(backdoor entrance behind Access Gallery/Publication Studios)
For viewing arrangements and other inquiries: info@spare-room.ca

"Sometimes there's nothing like a glass of ice cold tap water to really hit
the spot"

SPARE ROOM presents an environment based loosely on the aesthetics of
Japanese Rock Garden design in collaboration with Nigel Dembicki, Genta
Ishimura and Ian Lowrie. Titled 86, the space will be transformed into an
archive of soil samples, a glimpse into narrowed-perspective walls and
deliberate assignments of medium-sized rocks in odd-number based systems.

Dembicki, Ishimura and Lowrie are part of an architectural design
collective studioCAMP (http://studiocamp.ca/)

SPARE ROOM is an exhibition space located in Vancouver that observes the
relationship between art and space through site-specific works.
First conceived in 2014, the exhibition room strives to construct (and
deconstruct) the concept of space as an environment, situation, or place in
order to analyze the experimental stages of spatial design.

For more information please visit:
http://spare-room.ca/

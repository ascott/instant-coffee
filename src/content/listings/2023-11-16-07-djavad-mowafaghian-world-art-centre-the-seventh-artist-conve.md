THE SEVENTH
Artist Conversation

KWKWI: Lauren Brevner and James NexwKalus-Xwalacktun Harry in conversation
with Xwalacktun Rick Harry, Tuyttanat-Cease Wyss, and Tawxsin Yexwulla Aaron
Nelson-Moody, moderated by Jenn Jackson

Saturday, November 25, 2:00pm
Djavad Mowafaghian World Art Centre

Saturday, November 25, 2:00pm, at the Djavad Mowafaghian World Art Centre, located
on the 2nd floor of Goldcorp Centre for the Arts (GCA) at Simon Fraser University. Join
artists KWKWI: Lauren Brevner and James
NexwKalus-Xwalacktun Harry in
conversation with Xwalacktun Rick Harry, Tuyttanat-Cease Wyss, and Tawxsin
Yexwulla Aaron Nelson-Moody. Introduction and moderation by Or Gallery Director
Jenn Jackson.

Together the artists will discuss Coast Salish design principles and intersectional
collaboration as it relates to KWKWI: Lauren Brevner and James NexwKalus-
Xwalacktun Harrys exhibition, The Seventh, on view at Or Gallery from October 13,
2023, to February 10, 2024. The artists will engage the exhibitions principles of
Indigenous representation and discuss land-based gestures in contemporary art.

This conversation is free and open to all, and is co-sponsored by Or Gallery, SFU
Galleries, and the Audain Foundation. Registration is recommended but not required.

Learn more: [https://orgallery.org/events/the-seventh-artist-conversation](https://orgallery.org/events/the-seventh-artist-conversation)

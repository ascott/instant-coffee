Talks at the Vancouver Art Gallery

Tuesday, January 25, 7pm

Annual Emerging Artist Lecture

Germaine Koh

The Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to present the first of the Annual
Emerging Artist Lecture, a series that highlights the work of local artists
whose practice is burgeoning out on to the international scene and
demonstrates critical rigour and commitment to contemporary discourse and
culture. This annual lecture opens with artist Germaine Koh. Malaysian-born
and internationally active, Koh looks at everyday objects and daily
activities that often go unnoticed, yet profoundly shape our experiences.
She has shown at many galleries around the world, including the BALTIC
Centre for Contemporary Art in 2005, the Liverpool Biennial and the Art
Gallery of Alberta in 2004, Frankfurter Kunstverein in 2003, Bloomberg
SPACE in 2003.

Additionally, She has had solo exhibitions across Canada and
internationally at such institutions as Ottawa Art Gallery, Plug in ICA in
Winnipeg, McMaster Museum of Art in Hamilton, Contemporary Art Gallery and
Charles H. Scott Gallery in Vancouver, Kunstlerhaus Bethanien in Berlin,
and the British Museum in London UK, and was awarded the prestigious Jack
and Doris Shadbolt Foundation's VIVA Award in 2010.

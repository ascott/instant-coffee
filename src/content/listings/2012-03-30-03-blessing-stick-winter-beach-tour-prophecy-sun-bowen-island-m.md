WINTER BEACH TOUR: PROPHECY SUN @ BOWEN ISLAND| 
BLESSING STICK | MAR 30

Blessing Stick presents WINTER BEACH TOUR with the Ministry of 
Casual Living (Victoria BC). Blessing Stick is a collaboration between 
Heidi Nagtegaal and Jenny Lee Craig.

For the Winter Months of 2012, Blessing Stick will present one artist 
project per month, on the beaches along the Vancouver - Victoria 
Shorelines, connecting the communities of Blessing Stick and the 
Ministry of Casual Living http://www.mocl.com/

For the final installation of this project, PrOphecy Sun will reign on 
Snug Cove, Bowen Island BC. WINTER BEACH TOUR has featured 
Zain Burgess on Trafalgar Beach, Vancouver BC, the Garden City 
Hardbodies on Cattle Point Beach, Victoria BC. 

BOWEN ISLAND, SNUG COVE (right by the ferry exit)
FRIDAY, MARCH 30
7PM - 9PM

prOphecy sun is a interdisciplinary performance artist who interphases 
with sound, movement and installation to create emotive environments. 
Her works, compositions, videos and collaborations have been 
exhibited in Canada and abroad. She is a master theremin player, an 
improvisational looping maestro and an experimental throat singer. Her 
current musical projects include: prOphecy sun, Tyranahorse, Under 
the Sun, Her Jazz Noise Collective, Spell and The Adulthood. She is 
also a performer in two movement collectives: Dance Troupe Practice 
and So So So. Sun graduated from Emily Carr University with a 
Bachelors Degree in Visual Arts majoring in Sculpture & Ceramics. 
Mostly, she enjoys riding on her road bike Mr. Falcon around East 
Vancouver and being a mom to three lovely cats.
--

All exhibits within the WINTER BEACH TOUR will embody provisional 
methods of displaying art, using site specific materials, clotheslines, 
strings, rocks as paperweights, generators to fuel projectors run onto 
rocks or white sheets. BYOB, hot toddies in thermoses, and smiles to 
warm by. All events are one time only.

See you there!

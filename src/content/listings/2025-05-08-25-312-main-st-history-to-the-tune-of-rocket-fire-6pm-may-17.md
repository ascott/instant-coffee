Storytelling with Bassam: History to the Tune of Rocket Fire
Saturday, May 17, 2025 I 6:00PM - 8:30PM I $15

From the rural Palestinian countryside in the 1930s, to the streets of Beirut four
decades later, resistance music has long been a part of the Palestinian struggle. This
talk explores the ways in which Palestinian resistance factions have utilized music in
combination with other communicative strategies to chronicle the history of their
struggle.

Down on the Corner is pleased to present: 

Johan Bjorck
 
November 20th to December 15th
Reception December 8th 6pm

Johan Bjorck is a Swedish artist based in Vancouver. A master with words, sound and material, Johan presents ceramic works made at The Banff Centre while participating in the residency A Paper A Drawing A Mountain. 

Down on the Corner looks at the pre-existing social and public space as a larger conversation within which artwork can circulate.

Located in the window space of Gene coffee shop, on the corner of Main St and Kingsway in Vancouver. Down on the Corner invites artists to utilize the space considering its physicality and location, creating work or interventions that integrate the coffee shops environment and architecture.

Programmed by Guadalupe Martinez and Kate Moss.

DAA presents: Lord of The Files: Adam Dodd & Charlotte McHarg

Please join us for an opening reception on July 9th / 7pm - 11pm

Adam Dodd and Charlotte McHarg reconnect for this occasion via digital,
sculptural and painterly modes.

Adam contemplates space and digital media, -particularly the display of
Apps on digital interfaces and spaces for excavation- they inform his
paintings and sculptural works in this exhibit.

Charlotte's work focuses on our increasing migration towards an augmented
environment. Deconstructing basic virtual footsteps that have became second
nature to us through projections and interactive sculptures.

Adam Dodd is an artist living in Vancouver. He received his BFA from Emily
Carr University in 2006. This fall Adam will begin his MFA at Goldsmiths'
University in London.

Charlotte McHarg lives and works in Los Angeles as an animator for the Tech
industry. Charlotte is currently focusing on integrating her art practices
with robotics & programming.

Mantle

Artists Talk with Kevin Michael Murphy and Holly Schmidt

Saturday, July 13, 2 pm

From a road trip through Southern Ontario to a boat trip along the coast of British 
Columbia, the artwork of Kevin Michael Murphy and Holly Schmidt both begin with a 
journey. During the opening weekend of the exhibition, Kevin and Holly will join in a 
conversation about their art projects included in Mantle. The artists will discuss their 
travels, from Oil Springs, Ontario to Hardy Island, British Columbia and consider topics 
of transformation, extraction and artistic research in the context of ecology and art 
making.

About the exhibition:

Mantle

Diyan Achjadi, Holly Schmidt, Kevin Michael Murphy, Sean Alward, and Ts-"m- Igharas

July 13" September 1, 2019

Set against the picturesque backdrop of Lafarge Lake"a reclaimed sand and gravel 
quarry" the Art Gallery at Evergreen presents work by artists Diyan Achjadi, Sean 
Alward, Ts-"m- Igharas, Kevin Michael Murphy and Holly Schmidt. These artists engage 
with ideas inspired by geology, resource extraction, Indigenous knowledges and deep 
time. The exhibition takes its title, Mantle, from both the inner layer of rock that forms the 
earths planetary body and the responsibility passed down from one person to another. 
As a verb, mantle also describes the covering of something"like a quarry, perhaps"
that lies beneath the visible surface. Through artistic research, these artists explore the 
transformation of organic materials and draw learning from knowledge holders (be they 
people, land or other forces). The resulting artworks, which take the form of sculpture, 
photography, video, drawing and painting, draw attention to the interconnectedness of 
humankind and geology.

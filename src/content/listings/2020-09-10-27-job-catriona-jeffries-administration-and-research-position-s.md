Administration and Research Position

Catriona Jeffries invites applications for the gallery position of Administration and
Research.

Working closely with the Directors, the Administration and Research positions primary
responsibility is the multi-faceted administration and research of diverse areas of the
gallerys practice. This is a central position within the gallery team. Administration and
Research is responsible for direct, in-person and written communication with the gallerys
local, national and international community. The administration and office management
responsibilities include maintaining a complex contact database,
coordinating
organizational calendars, multiple agendas and generating and processing a variety of
time sensitive forms, documents and applications. Alongside the Directors, the position is
also responsible for generative and responsive research summaries through a detailed
and inquisitive process on an ongoing basis. Administration and Research is an
expansive, ambitious and dynamic position that allows considerable room for growth.

QUALIFICATIONS:

Applicants should have at minimum an undergraduate degree in a relevant discipline.
Organizational skills and attention to detail are the foundation of this role. Interpersonal
and writing skills are imperative. Experience in a Mac OS environment, a deep working
knowledge of Microsoft Office, and experience with and a strong understanding of
database management essentials is key. An interest and ability in learning new digital
organizational systems is also required.

HOW TO APPLY:

Please submit an expression of interest and resume to
gallery@catrionajeffries.com. We
look forward to a diversity of qualified applicants to apply for the Administration and
Research position at Catriona Jeffries. Applications will be reviewed on an ongoing basis
and only successful applicants will be contacted.

DEADLINE: Sep. 25, 2020

Contact Email: gallery@catrionajeffries.com

To mark the closing of ANTI-ICON: APOKALYPSIS, The Polygon is pleased to
present P.L.U.R.O.M.A. (Peace Love Unity Respect Oxygen Music and Autonomy), a
multimedia fashion show produced by Liquidation World under the
creative direction
of artist Katayoon Yousefbigloo.
Bringing together performance, live feed video installation, photography, music, and
front row drama, the P.L.U.R.O.M.A. runway shows are produced by a community of
experimental artists and fashion elite. The spectacle is setting sail across the
harbour for its third season to launch the 2024 Resort collection at The Polygon
Gallery with a complete takeover of the waterfront property.
This seasons artists are: Dana Qaddah, Jake Kimble, Khan Lee, Maya Beaudry,
Second Spring Apparel (Julian Hou & others), Olga Abeleva & Leon, Ximena
Velzquez in addition to Liquidation Worlds in-house line.
Special music performance by Sophia Stel.

About Liquidation World
Liquidation World is an art collective formed in 2021 with the intention of producing
and showcasing experimental artworks and performances. Projects the collective
undertake reach at the unexpected and incalculable phenomena that emerge from
combining different practices, and communities in unconventional presentation
spaces. Past projects include an open-submission art consignment store, a multi-
room audiovisual Rock Opera, visual art exhibitions, a laser show, video
performances and installations, and an ongoing series of screenprinting events and
artist-led workshops. In 2022 the collective curated Wrong Wave: The Liquidation
World Guide to Maximizing Your Autonomy, a multi-venue festival of art rock,
experimental film, visual art and community projects. The collective currently
operates a multi-purpose art space at 1965 Main St, in addition to supporting off-site
projects and collaborations.

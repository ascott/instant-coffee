Harana
May 27 - July 2 | Opening Reception May 26, 7-9pm
Presented in partnership with the Philippine Consulate General in
Vancouver, join us for this colourful and spirited exhibition showcasing
the distinctive visual art of a society with diverse cultural influences and
traditions. Harana features the work of internationally renowned artist
featuring Manuel Baldemor and local Filipino Canadian artists Ed
Araquel, Bert Monterona,Danvic Briones, Chito Maravilla, Donna Osea
Menor, Jo Galang, J.A. Tan, Lenore RS Lim, and Tessie Dichupa.

CityScape Community Arts Space (335 Lonsdale Avenue, North
Vancouver)

Edge of the Knife: Fundraiser film screening for Unist'ot'en
Date: March 1, 6:30 PM - 9:00 PM
Location: The HiVE, 210-128 West Hastings St, Vancouver

Join 350Vancouver and the HiVE for a fundraiser film screening to support Wet'suwet'en 
resistance to Coastal GasLink/Transcanada and the Unist'ot'en Camp's ongoing assertion of 
sovereignty on their unceded territory. Filmed on stunning Haida Gwaii, Gwaai Edenshaw and 
Helen Haig-Browns 19th-century epic is a nod to the grand storytelling traditions that lure us to 
the big screen. The fact that its the first narrative scripted and shot in two dialects of the 
endangered Haida language"which has only 20-odd fluent speakers left"also certifies it as a 
landmark work of cinema. A silent auction featuring art and other items will follow the screening. 
Sliding scale tickets and details available at: [https://bit.ly/2U04kjq](https://bit.ly/2U04kjq)

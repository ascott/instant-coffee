Border Crossings NEW Issue!

Volume 28 No. 4 Issue No. 112

This issue features a cover designed by Canadian artist Micah Lexier,
interviews with conceptual artists Dan Graham and Lawrence Weiner, a
rigorous and playful article on philosophy and photography by AD Coleman
and a look at Zin Taylor's imaginative Bakery of Blok.

Issue 112 also includes an essay by Myrna Kostash on politics, memory, and
art, and reviews of the MOMA's Judith Rothschild Drawing Collection, Sarah
Anne Johnson's AGO exhibition, contemporary Iranian art, Laura Kikauka,
Sandra Meigs and much more.

Hey Loser!
July 17-August 3, 2013
OPENING RECEPTION: Friday July 19 from 7pm
at Hot Art Wet City
2206 Main Street, Vancouver BC
http://hotartwetcity.com/hey-loser/

Hey Loser! is a collaborative mixed media exhibition exploring the work
of artists who are inspired by the lame and unsophisticated; who celebrate
the crafty and un-cool. Featuring art and illustration from local Vancouver
artists Alison Bruce, Brandon Cotter, Hamish Olding, Daniel Tatterton,
Tylor Macmillan, and Victoria Sieczka. Opening reception features musical
guest Spun Suns.

The On Edge readings series presents:
Fabiola Nabil Naguib

Thurs, Jan. 28 - 7 pm

in South Building Room 406
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
1399 Johnston St.
Granville Island, Vancouver/Coast Salish Territories

This reading is free and open to the public. All are welcome.

Fabiola Nabil Naguib is an artist, writer and activist. Naguib cites no
separation between her practice as an artist, poet, or activist, energizing
all that she creates as an integrated process rooted in the continuity of
creativity, re/membering, resilience and self-determination. Naguib's first
book, a collection of poetry, essays and artwork entitled Uninhabiting the
Violence of Silencing: activations of creativity, ethics, and resistance
(Creativity Commons Press, 2007) is receiving international attention and
praise, with Nobel Laureate Archbishop Desmond Tutu describing this
collection as "deeply moving, sensitive and courageous." Naguib's poetry,
critical essays and artwork have been published in a wide range of
multidisciplinary anthologies and journals. She has exhibited and performed
in public and art spaces, in places such as Cairo, New Delhi, Havana,
Paris, Oujda, Dhaka, Florence, Pasadena, Vancouver, Ottawa, and Montreal.

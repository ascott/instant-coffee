JENEEN FREI NJOOTLI

2025 Yukon Prize Finalist Exhibition
Yukon Arts Centre
11 September - 14 November 2025
Exhibition Opening & Performance by Jeneen Frei Njootli: September 11, 2025

We are thrilled to share that Jeneen Frei Njootli has been selected as a finalist for
the 2025 Yukon Prize for Visual Arts. The award aims to acknowledge excellence
among Yukon visual artists and serve as a catalyst for promoting Yukon visual art
nationally and internationally, and awards a financial prize of $20,000. The winner of
the prize will be announced during the Yukon Prize Festival Weekend, October 3-5,
2025.

The Yukon Art Prize's finalist exhibition opens September 11th, 2025 at the Yukon
Arts Centre's main gallery. Jeneen will perform at the opening reception that
evening. The performance is free and open to the public.

Jeneen Frei Njootlis work is also currently on view in Indian Theater: Native
Performance, Art, and Self-Determination since 1969 at Mackenzie Art Gallery in
Regina, Saskatchewan.

Indian Theater: Native Performance, Art, and Self-Determination since 1969 is the
first large-scale exhibition of its kind to centre performance and theatre as an origin
point for the development of contemporary art by Native American, First Nations,
Mtis, Inuit, and Alaska Native artists, beginning with the role that Indigenous artists
have played in the self-determination era, sparked by the Occupation of Alcatraz by
the Indians of All Tribes in 1969.

On September 26, 2025 Jeneen will present an artist talk titled: fighting for the title
not to be pending followed by a performance: Jii jidi tinch'u? Jil jak t'iinch'u. Both
events are free and open to the public.

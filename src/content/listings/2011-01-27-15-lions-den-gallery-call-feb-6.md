The EVENT: Held on Thursday February 17, 2011 at the Lion’s Den 
Gallery, “To All Tomorrow’s Parties” is an epic night of visual arts, 
film screenings, live performances followed by an open floor dance 
party.

SUBMISSIONS: Seeking short films/videos, performances (in 
music, dance and/or the performing arts) and 2D works with 
contents themed around the reminiscence of the past. Up to 2 
submissions per person. Maximum duration for films, dance and 
acts/plays is 10 minutes. Wall works must include labels, their own 
frames if needed. Artists will have to take it upon themselves to 
provide additional information on labels should they choose to sell 
their work(s).

Send submissions to tatpsubmissions@gmail.com 
with “submission” along with the artistic area it most relates to in the 
subject line (e.g. “TATP call for submissions: short film”). Please 
include your full name, a brief description of your finished or in-
progress work(s), the title, year of production, dimension, duration, 
and 2-3 film stills if relevant. For live performances, an audio or 
video link should be included. As well, please state any required 
materials needed for installation. We are also looking for local 
bands and musicians to join the party! Submission deadline is 
Sunday, February 6, 2011.

Please contact tatpsubmissions@gmail.com or visit 
facebook.com/toalltomorrowsparties for more information. “TATP” is 
a fundraiser event for the 4th year visual art student grad show in 
April. All proceeds will go towards publication, building, and event 
expenses.

Radix presents YVR
Every Thursday night til December 1st, Radix Theatre hosts YVR, a workshop in 
preparation for an installation at the Roundhouse Community Centre. 
Participants are asked to recall something about Vancouver that only exists now 
in their memories, then build a paper version of that memory using a single 14" x 
18" piece of paper. The results will be gathered into five neighbourhoods and 
suspended in mid-air during the installation December 6 - 9, with an immersive 
soundscape filling the space. Part of the City of Vancouver's 125th Anniversary 
Celebrations.

YVR Workshop
Thursdays, 7 - 9 pm, til December 1
Roundhouse Community Centre, (corner of Pacific and Davie)
Room B
Free, materials supplied

Also, an all-day YVR workshop will be featured this Thursday November 3rd from 
9am - 5pm during CreativeMix, a creative conference at the Roundhouse.

Field Contemporary invites you to July 4, a summer bbq and closing for 
 Thomas McDonell: Warner Bros. Suite. 

 Warner Bros. Suite is the first exhibition in Canada by the American artist 
 Thomas McDonell. A roll of linen, printed with the famous film studio's 
 logo, was divided by the artist and transported through diverse 
 environments wherein the pieces of fabric collected a variety of marks. 
 These marks range from handmade scrawls to machined embroidery to 
 evidence of weather and the passage of time. The pieces of fabric -- each 
 stretched to a standard widescreen aspect ratio and assembled in the 
 gallery in a long piecewise rectangle -- suggest a dramatic, non-linear 
 narrative. 

 Please join us on July 4 in the gallery, and back alley, from 6pm till late. 
 Food provided with drinks available for purchase.

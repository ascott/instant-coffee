Move the Slow Hand
Andrea Cownden + RJ Svelnis

Neither naked nor clothed
Neither walking nor riding
Neither on the road nor off it

We are dancers in an unhurried life-long collaboration. We work on community,
accessibility, solidarity, grief, god, shame, and calling on the spirit of dance. Our
practices take form when we teach, facilitate, organize, collaborate in the
choreography of others, write descriptions of dance, take class, and witness
performances.
__

Aiden Cass + Sarah Hutton

We are curious to use this time at Boombox to zoom in on one character from a
group work we are planning to premiere next
year. Its overwhelming how many
ideas, concepts, and worlds we often find inside one of our works and Boombox
seemed like a nice place to not have to neatly make sense of what we were finding
for Aidens character. This project was also a chance for Aiden to perform solo and
Sarah to direct from the outside. This is a dynamic we rely on in Generous Mess, but
to have it as the sole structure for this creation was a fun change for us.

Born in North Vancouver BC, Aiden Cass began his training with North Shore
Academy of Dance before joining Modus Operandi (MO) in 2015. Born in Hanna AB,
Sarah Hutton moved to Vancouver in 2013 and trained with the Source Dance
Company and EDAM before joining MO in 2016. MO was the container for Aiden
and Sarahs unfolding artistic practice and forever changed how they thought about
and experienced dance. During their graduate project at MO in 2019, Aiden and
Sarah collaborated for the first time and have been ever since. Research and
innovation in movement
pathways and partnering ground their collective practice,
Generous Mess.

Compass I: That Lonesome Ocean

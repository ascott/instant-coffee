RECEPTION
NANITCH
Presentation House Gallery
April 16, 7:30 pm

NANITCH features rarely seen historical photographs of British Columbia
selected from the Uno Langmann Family Collection of BC Photographs recently
donated to UBC Library. Spanning the 1860s to the 1920s, the exhibition
reveals dramatic changes in the region, as well as in how and why
photographs were made. The title, NANITCH, meaning "to look" in the Chinook
trade language, signals the significant role of the camera in colonization
and the cultural intersections in this region at that time. The exhibition
brings to light the contradictions of colonial settlement through rich
photographic material, including rare albums, albumen prints, stereograph
cards and postcards.

2.
Mandy Boursicot | three-week portrait drawing course
Mandy Boursicot is offering a three-week portrait drawing course. Mandy is 
fresh back from a four-year intensive in Florence, Italy, where she attended the 
Angel Academy of Art, learning and mastering the full range of Classical Realism 
skills. First on scholarship, and then working as a student teacher, Mandy spent 
the last year as the full-time drawing instructor for new entrants to this difficult 
program.

Mandy wants to take your drawing to a whole new level this summer ! Because 
this intensive is designed to teach artists how to combine foundational skills with 
your own unique artistic expression, you'll be drawing easily and effortlessly in 
no time. You'll work in a friendly, supportive environment with a very small 
group, with Mandy as teacher, coach and mentor.
Drawing from master copies of PORTRAITS, students will cover the 
fundamentals of 

- measuring and proportion: a simple method for blocking in and achieving 
likeness

- values: capturing three-dimensional effects on a flat surface using patterns of 
light and dark

- form: understanding the structure of a human head and the facial features in 
different perspectives

- materials and techniques: a refined way to use graphite pencils to achieve 
beautiful results

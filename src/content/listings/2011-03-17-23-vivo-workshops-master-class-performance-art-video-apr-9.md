MASTER CLASS: PERFORMANCE ART / VIDEO

A 4-day performance workshop facilitated by Maya Suess, New York / Vancouver

Presented by VIVO Media Arts Centre

When: April 9, 10, 16 and 17
Where: VIVO Media Arts Centre, 1965 Main Street, Vancouver

Cost: $150

This special session of Performance Art / Video is an intensive forum for
artists working within the mediums of performance art and performance for
video, to bring their ideas and works-in-progress, for development and
completion.

Utilizing the resources of VIVO, participants will have the opportunity to
work out conceptual, aesthetic and formal challenges. Extending from the
need of each project, artists can explore various strategies of lighting,
shooting, editing, durational art and exhibition options. Within the
structure of the workshop, formal methods of performative video will be
investigated through a collective group symposium and an in-depth analysis
of individual work. Participants will have limited access to VIVO recourses
for the duration of the week between sessions. We will conclude with an
informal public event at VIVO, presenting the works produced.

Networking and community building is an important element of this workshop,
providing a space for emerging and established artists to develop working
relationships. Also included is the opportunity to present finished works
to Video Out for possible distribution.

Participants are expected to bring their experience and curiosity, and be
ready to collectively problem-solve.

Maya Suess is a New York based artist currently teaching a senior class in
Performance Art at Emily Carr. She brings her experience, keen performative
eye, and strong facilitation skills to VIVO for an intensive exploration of
the form.

WORKSHOP LAYOUT & SCHEDULE

Day 1: Sat April 9th, 12pm -4pm
Introduction, project descriptions and screening of works by contemporary
performance artists followed by a critical discussion.

Day 2: Sun April 10th, 11am -3pm
Pre-production: participants will present their ideas in depth, and expand
on their production plans and challenges. Experimentation with cameras and
lighting will follow.

Day 3: Sat April 16th, 12pm -4pm
Participants will continue to work on various stages of their production.
Editing bays are available for those ready for post-production.

Day 4: Sun April 17th, 11am -3pm
Participants will continue to work on their production/post-production.
Editing bays are available. A screening of works and discussion will close
the program.

Tue April 19, 7:30-10 pm
Public Presentation at VIVO

WORKSHOP REQUIREMENTS

- Engagement with performance practice

- Some experience in video production

- Commitment to attending all workshop sessions

MAYA SUESS

Maya Suess uses video, performance, installation and practical magic to
take on the challenges of living in the contemporary urban environment. She
has exhibited in galleries, museums, theaters and film festivals in Canada,
the United States, Europe and Japan. She holds a BFA in Media Arts from
Emily Carr Institute, and an MFA in contemporary performance from Simon
Fraser University. Her recent work It It and the Gimme Box received a 2009
Production Grant from The New York State Council on the Arts, and she is a
2011 resident at 3 Legged Dog New Media Center in Manhattan. She lives and
works in both Vancouver and New York City.

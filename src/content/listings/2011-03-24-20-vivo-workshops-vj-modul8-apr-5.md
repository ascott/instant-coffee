VJ: Modul8
1 session: 3 hours
Tue April 5 , 7-10 PM

Cost: $100 or $80 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: Brady Marks
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

Modul8 is a real-time video mixing software used by VJs. Its well-designed
interface and flexibility make it a popular option. Over the three hours we
will cover how it works and how it relates to a live mixing situation. This
workshop is appropriate for people who are curious, just starting to VJ, or
have never used Modul8 in their VJ practice.

FINAL CUT PRO - INTRO
2 sessions: 8 hours
Tue/Thu April 12 & 14, 6-10 PM

Cost: $200 or $160 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: Mark Curry
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

This workshop provides an overview of Final Cut Pro 6 from capturing video
to printing to tape. It covers all the basics including starting and
organizing a project, log and capture (DV), basic editing, trim editing,
introduction to transitions and filters, titling, audio tools, and
exporting to tape.

CIRCUIT BUILDING
2 sessions: 7 hours
Mon/Wed April 18 & 20, 6-9:30 PM

Cost: $160 or $130 with VIVO Producer Membership
Instructor: David Leith
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 604.872.8337/ext.1

A base knowledge of electronics gives the artist the opportunity to
introduce electronic circuitry into a variety of media including sculpture,
sound, and light-based works. This course will cover some basic theory of
electronic principals that is essential for beginning to work with this
medium. From there we will look at some of the components that make up
simple circuits and that create the building blocks for more complex
circuits. Students will learn some of the basic skills needed for
electronic work such as soldering, breadboarding, and reading a schematic.
These skills will be used to build a simple audio amplifier. We will
experiment with different inputs such as contact microphones and how to
modify the sound output from the speaker.

The cost of materials that you take home with you ($10-15) is included in
the workshop fee.

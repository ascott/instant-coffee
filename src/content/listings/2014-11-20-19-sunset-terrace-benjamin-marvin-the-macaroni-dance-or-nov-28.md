SUNSET TERRACE

Benjamin Marvin
The Macaroni Dance! Or: Fryin' up Some Cucumbers.
November 28 - December 15
2028 Clark Dr.

Are you familiar with the intricacies of the macaroni dance?

The macaroni dance exists as a possibility, it is always present. Perhaps
it is a line, or it could be a pattern - but does it exist without us?

No

It is a product of our very own technology, and because of that it is
fragile, and it is not perfect, it has charm and is vulnerable. We are
selfish and that is okay. Although the macaroni dance, or frying up some
cucumbers, may be exercises limited to our inevitable condition of
isolation, it may have the capability to generate something else, something
new.

New works by Benjamin Marvin

Opening reception November 28 2014, 7pm.

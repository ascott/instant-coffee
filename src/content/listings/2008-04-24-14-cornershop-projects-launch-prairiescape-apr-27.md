CORNERSHOP PROJECTS: Prairiescapes Westcoast Launch: Sunday April 27th, 2-5pm

Cornershop Projects is pleased to present the Westcoast launch of Prairiescape, a web-based project commissioned by PLATFORM Centre for Photographic + Digital Arts featuring KC Adams, Richard Dyck, and  Erika Lincoln, three Winnipeg-based artists. 

While the prairies have not yet fallen victim to climate-related natural disasters such as Hurricane Katrina, Environment Canada states that seven out of the ten warmest  years in the Prairies have occurred since 1981, and the average yearly temperature continues  to rise in Manitoba. Heat waves and drought have been predicted; causing crop yields to fall,	soil erosion, and increased energy demands due to cooling and irrigation.  

Artists KC Adams, Richard Dyck, and Erika Lincoln are each creating artworks that record and 
respond to the landscape of the prairies at the height of consumption before the impending 
dramatic consequences of our collective actions.  These artists are looking at the expanse of 
the prairies as a counterbalance to complicated times and a record of the effects of our 
technological lifestyles. In a poignant twist, they will be considering the condition of the prairie
landscape in their new media artworks; re-purposing the very technologically driven culture that 
threatens the landscape.  

Each of the three new web-based works are interactive, navigable, and offer various views (metaphoric
and literal) of life on the prairies.

Cornershop Projects is is an open framework for engaging with economic exchange. Located in a former corner store, Cornershop hosts, initiates, and encourages projects that examine the possibilities of transactions including, but not limited to: buying, selling, begging, borrowing, stealing, renting, owning, bartering, downsizing, unionizing, transnationalizing, securitizing, blackmarketing, etc…

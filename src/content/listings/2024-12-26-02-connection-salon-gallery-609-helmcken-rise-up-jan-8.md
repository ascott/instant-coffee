Opening ; RISE UP Exhibition January 8th
Place: Connection Salon Gallery, 609 Helmcken
We are continuing our series of exhibitions on the theme of Recovery.
This one is about
Empowerment Having control over life, focusing on strengths, and taking personal
responsibility.
Connection Salon Artists Collectives mission is to provide low barrier opportunities to
artists
that are underrepresented and have lived experience with mental health.
12 visual artists will be on show December 19 to February 3. Join us for the Meet the
Artists
event January 8 th , 2025, from 5:30 to 7:00PM with singer /songwriter Matt Hermano.
The exhibition is supported by the Canada Council for the Arts, the Connection Salon
Collective
and the Gathering Place Community Center.

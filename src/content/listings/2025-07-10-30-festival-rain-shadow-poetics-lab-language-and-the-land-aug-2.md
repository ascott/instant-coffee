rain shadow poetics lab, formerly Cascadia Poetics Lab, has been bringing world-class
poetry with a bioregional focus to the Comox Valley since 2017. Pursuing a sense of
deep time and place, we aim to build community by sharing creative language forms
intimately linked to their place of origin"the Cascadia bioregion.

Language and The Land, our 2025 Festival, brings together poets from across
Cascadia with the question, What is a bioregional poetry practice?

From Friday, August 22 to Sunday, August 24, 2025, Cumberland BC will host
readings, workshops and lectures with poets from Quadra Island, Courtenay-Comox,
Vancouver, the Okanagan Valley, and Washington State. Well meet at Artful: The
Gallery, The Abbey Studio, and the Cumberland Village Heritage Museum.

Watershed Press joins the party with two book launches: Lorin Medleys chapbook, On
the Way to Kluusms, and the anthology Cascadian Zen: Volume Two.

Gallery Coordinator

Department of Art History, Visual Art and Theory
Competition # 080617356
Salary Range: $23.41 – 25.46 per hour
One year staff appointment Part Time, Hourly (CUPE 116, Research Technician 4)
Start Date: September 1, 2008
UBC, one of the largest and most distinguished universities in Canada, is located in Vancouver, a
young, vibrant and ethnically diverse city contributing to the unique opportunities associated with 
this position. The visual art program partners with strong art history and curatorial studies 
programs, integrating the visual arts, art history and theory within the curriculum. The department 
is also associated with a leading contemporary art gallery, the Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, 
and the internationally recognized Museum of Anthropology.

This Gallery Coordinator, is a temporary part time appointment, working closely with the 
Department Administrator and Gallery Director to established a new Art Gallery and student 
training program at the Undergraduate and Graduate levels. The long-term vision is for the 
Gallery to be student run under the supervision of a Faculty Gallery Director.

The Gallery Coordinator is responsible for the day-to-day operation of the Gallery. 
Responsibilities include: establishing a volunteer program; coordinating special events including
conferences, lectures, and exhibition openings; coordinating didactic information for exhibition 
labels, promotional information; assist with recruitment of student Gallery Assistants and 
Volunteers and oversee their daily scheduling, duties, supervision, training and mentoring. The 
Coordinator will work with the Director and student advisory committee regarding programming 
and collaborations including planning of exhibitions; development and distribution of submission 
calls and implementation and coordination of a Promotional Plan. The Coordinator will maintain 
records and databases including exhibition schedule, special events, media coverage, exhibition 
and events documentation; and will prepare and track gallery expense reimbursements.

Qualifications

> * A University degree in Fine Arts, Art History or related field, courses or related training in 
Volunteer Management and preventative gallery/museum conservation practices and techniques 
plus a minimum of 4 years experience in planning and overseeing Special Events and/or Public 
Programs in a public art gallery or related organization.
> * Experience in a public relations or marketing function or equivalent in a related field;
> * Good understanding and knowledge of visual information pertaining to contemporary art 
practices and art history;
> * Significant experience in preventative conservation practices and techniques regarding the 
handling, and exhibition of artwork and objects composed of diverse materials in accordance with 
gallery/museum conservation standards;
> * Experience at planning, setting priorities and working under firm deadline pressures;
> * Ability to simultaneously work on several projects;
> * Effective oral and written communications and research skills;
> * Demonstrated ability to effectively communicate issues in contemporary art, gallery ethics 
and practices to large groups of people from diverse backgrounds;
> * Demonstrated ability to instruct, manage and work with students and volunteers;
> * Ability to establish and maintain effective working relationships with diverse internal and 
external contacts such as curators, artists, staff, faculty, professionals from other institutions and 
departments, graphic designers, rental/equipment suppliers;
> * Ability to both make independent decisions exercising initiative and work within a team 
environment;
> * Good understanding of Gallery ethics, practices, policies, issues in contemporary art, issues
of copyright and representation
> * Computer skills on PC and Macintosh
> * Serving It Right License required.

UBC hires on the basis of merit, is committed to employment equity, and encourages all qualified 
individuals to apply. However, Canadians and permanent residents of Canada will be given 
priority.

Preference for internal applicants: Following the collective agreements between UBC and its 
employment groups, internal applicants will be given first consideration for available positions in 
their union.

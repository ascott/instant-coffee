Description of the Exhibition

Join New Leaf
Editions Gallery on April 18th at 6 PM for an exclusive exhibition,
"SixteenEighteen," featuring the collaborative works of artists Kari Kristensen and
Tara Lee Bennett. Explore the world of paper artistry as we celebrate the historic
Arnhem 1618 paper. Discover the intricate craftsmanship and innovative techniques
of Kristensen and Bennett, showcasing the timeless beauty of paper as a medium.
Don't miss this unique opportunity to witness the fusion of tradition and creativity in
printmaking and paper sculpture. Experience the captivating allure of
"SixteenEighteen" at New Leaf Editions Gallery.

Artist Biographies

Kari Kristensen

Born and raised in Dutton, Ontario, Kari Kristensen is a contemporary Canadian
artist. Her art celebrates the divine perfection of the natural environment through a
fusion of traditional printmaking techniques and innovative forms.

Tara Lee Bennett

Hailing from Zimbabwe and having lived in Australia, Tara Lee Bennett brings a
global perspective to her
paper artistry. Her intricate creations reflect personal and
professional journeys, exploring themes of growth and renewal with meticulous
detail.

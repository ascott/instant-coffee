| No Linguistic Content

Exhibition runs until March 21 

Feb 19, Wednesday 8pm 
Artist Talk, Gabriel Mindel-Saloman with Jayne Wilkinson

An artist talk focusing on the transformation of our "ways of seeing" and 
"ways of making" in the emergent surveillance culture of the 21st Century. 
This talk asks how art and artists are being changed by a society in which 
we begin with an assumption of already and always being looked at. 
Considerations of JPEGS, John Berger and the "synopticon" will be explored 
in relation to the work on exhibit in No Linguistic Content.

No Linguistic Content is an exhibition of new work by Antonia Hirsch (Berlin), 
Gabriel Mindel-Saloman (Vancouver) and Luke Munn (Auckland, New 
Zealand) that responds to contemporary forms of surveillance, considering 
how design can work to counteract an excessively coded city.

You're invited to:
Geist's 2nd Decennial Class Photo
Geist Tenth Anniversary Photo

Greetings Reader,

Ten years ago (ish) Geist celebrated its tenth anniversary by hosting a party at 
the W.I.S.E. Hall in Vancouver, where 100 people posed in a group photo taken 
by Goran Basaric.

Now you are invited to be included in the second decennial Geist class photo!

Billeh Nickerson, Jill Mandrake and Brad Cran will be reading their Geist work, 
and once again a class photo will be taken (no fingers in noses or bunny ears 
please).

There will also be adult beverages available for purchase.

Geist's 2nd Decennial Class Photo
Location: W.I.S.E. Hall, 1882 Adanac Street, Vancouver BC
Date: Friday, June 24, 2011
Time: 7:00 – 10:00 pm. The W.I.S.E. Hall will be open later for those who wish to 
continue celebrating the greatness of Geist.
Price: Admission is by donation to the Geist Writers and Artists Fund.

The latest issues of Geist, including the 20th Anniversary Collector's Issue, will 
also be on sale. While you're at it, why not buy a subscription and help support 
Canadian literature!

Geist, 210 - 111 West Hastings Street, Vancouver BC V6B 1H4.

West Vancouver Art Museum, Fred Hollingsworth: Art of Architecture
680 17th St, West Vancouver, BC

Exhibition talk: November 17, 2-4 p.m. 

Join Director/Curator Darrin Morrison in conversation with Greg Bellerby about 
our current exhibition, Fred Hollingsworth: Art of Architecture. Free and open to 
all.

About the exhibition:
Architect Fred Hollingsworth helped pioneer West Coast modernism by designing 
numerous significant homes and buildings in Vancouver and beyond. The 
exhibition explores the masterful colour renderings, furnishings and artworks that 
Hollingsworth created for his clients and celebrates the architect's draftsmanship 
and belief that design should be accessible to everyone.

About Greg Bellerby:
Greg Bellerby was born in Vancouver and studied at the Vancouver School of Art. 
He has been a curator and gallery director for more than thirty years and 
produced many exhibitions and publications on visual art, architecture, and 
design. He was the commissioner and co-curator of the Canadian Pavilion at the 
2006 Venice Biennale of Architecture. From 1988 to 2013, he was the 
director/curator of the Charles H. Scott Gallery at the Emily Carr University of Art 
+ Design.

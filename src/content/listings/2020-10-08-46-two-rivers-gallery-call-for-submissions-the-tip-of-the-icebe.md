Artists from across BC are invited to submit visual art in any medium that considers the
impact of climate change, and/ or proposes solutions to the problems of climate change,
ranging from the political, to the theoretical, to the practical.

Guidelines

Artists can submit up to six completed works.
All artwork must have been produced within the last three years.
Apply online at: https://form.jotform.com/202715878855267

Deadlines

All submissions must be received no later than 12:00am on Monday, November 16th,
2020. Please ensure your images accurately represent your finished artwork. Exhibition
dates are January 21st " April 11th, 2021 (subject to change).

Direct submission enquiries to:

Assistant Curator, Meghan Hunter-Gauthier

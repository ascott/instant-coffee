Chun Hua Catherine Dong is a performance artist living in 
Vancouver. She holds a BFA from Emily Carr University, 
pursuing her MFA at Concordia University. As part of her 
interest in identity politics and postmodern feminism, she 
utilizes her own body as a disturbance in order to explore 
ruptures in society. Her performance mainly deals with 
deterritorialization and disessentialization in the taken-for-
granted world through examining her multifaceted struggles 
associated with identity, gender, and sexuality. Her recent 
research interests focus on wearables that examine how 
technology transforms the materiality of the body to create a 
new language acknowledging that the body can be generated 
and re-invented.

Patrick Cruz is born and raised in Manila, Philippines. He 
recently studied clownology in 2010 and is currently living and 
working in Surrey, BC. Inspired by the subversive nature of 
language and the exoticization of the other, Patrick Cruz uses 
sound to explore notions of absurdity and humour in the work -
"Closing, mp3, 1:33 loop, 2011". Sampled from a clothing 
store closing in Chinatown, the one minute and thirty three 
second audio loop renegotiates the relocated context of 
language within the closed premise of the gallery.

In his wide-ranged practice (usually involving painting, 
drawing, installation & sculpture), Kristofferson San Pablo 
investigates current ideas about piracy, sexuality, and the idea 
of the remix. For the show, San Pablo takes the platform of the 
webcam/internet as a way to take on the subject of the classical 
nude figure drawing. For the show opening, an all night 
performance titled "7 Minutes In Heaven" in which a room with 
a live webcam feed with San Pablo in his Los Angeles studio 
will be open for viewers to participate and sit in as a model for 
his ongoing series of drawings. This will be his first time 
showing outside the U.S. since his solo show earlier this year in 
San Francisco's M_NI_C gallery, titled Mania II. San Pablo 
holds an MFA in Fine Art from Art Center College of Design in 
Pasadena and currently works & lives in Los Angeles.

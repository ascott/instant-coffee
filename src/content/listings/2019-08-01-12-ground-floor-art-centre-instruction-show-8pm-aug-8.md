'Instruction Show' 

Opening Reception: August 8th, 8-11pm 

Gallery hours: August 9th - 13th, 12-4pm 

Location: Ground Floor Art Centre (288 E Georgia)

************************************************************

Instruction Show is an instruction based art show where 5 local and international artists 
were invited to write a set of instructions for the construction of a piece. Once the 
instructions were written they were disseminated to 5 other multidisciplinary emergent 
individuals. This show addresses themes of diverse interpretations, obvious failures 
that come with being an emergent artist, and the precarity of balancing life and practice.

THOUGHT ON FILM XIII

A monthly reading + discussion group, Thought on Film aims to promote
critical thought around film product and practice through community-based
discussion. Rooted in our membership but open to the public, Thought on
Film fosters the close reading of texts confronting issues in contemporary,
cutting-edge cinematic practice and philosophy.

February's reading coincides with DIM's presentation of Cinema +
Disjunction, reading an excerpt from David Harvey's The Condition of
Postmodernity. This Vancouver premiere screening of Adrian Blackwell's
Night Equals Day and Daniel Young and Christian Giroux's Every Building, Or
Site, That a Building Permit Has Been Issued for a New Building in Toronto
in 2006 as DIM's presentation of Cinema + Disjunction brings two recent

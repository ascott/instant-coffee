Come to Sunset Terrace on Friday, Dec 8th for the opening of group show
HIPPOMANCY.

Opening reception 6-9pm

Hippomancy will be on view at Sunset Terrace from Dec 8-10.

Hippomancy presents the work of Rot Collective and Josh Koole in an effort to explore
and
understand the connotations of horses and cars. We see the two as vessels of
multiple meanings, and aim to explore the ways in which they contract and intertwine with
one another.
In this exhibition, these concepts are explored through themes of intention and attention,
progress and nostalgia, utility and luxury, and collaboration. These individually made
sculptures prove the artists with agency to explore their own meaning, as well as their
peers.

2028 Clark Dr. Vancouver, BC

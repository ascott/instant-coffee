OPENING | ECUAD | ART & DESIGN FACULTY SHOW | 4-7PM | JAN 30
EMILY CARR UNIVERSITY OF ART & DESIGN FACULTY SHOW: The Work of the Work

The Faculty at Emily Carr University of Art & Design are excited to announce their first
exhibition in our new campus on Great Northern Way. The show features a wide selection of art,
media, and design by the Universitys distinguished faculty that explores the complex
relationships between teaching, creative practice, and labour.

A unique feature of this years exhibition is The Working Studio where faculty members will
publicly share their working processes. The show will be on exhibit throughout the second floor
on Emily Carr's campus at 520 East 1st Avenue, Vancouver. The exhibition runs from January
31st to February 14th, with an opening reception on Thursday, January 30th from 4pm to 7pm.
All are welcome.

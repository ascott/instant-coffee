Fireside Chat with Krista Belle Stewart
Thursday, March 20, 6:30-8:30pm
Burnaby Art Gallery| 6344 Deer Lake Avenue, Burnaby, BC V5G 2J3

In collaboration with Artspeak and the University of British Columbia, the 
Burnaby Art Gallery is pleased to present Krista Belle Stewart, Lucien Durey 
and Katie Kozak, as well as Randy Lee Cutler through the rotating display of 
Shelved. Shelved is a project which examines various archives and filing 
systems producing discursive and experimental library spaces through 
research, curatorial, and publication projects. Shelved will present rotating 
displays at the BAG, Artspeak, and in the Ridington Room at the Art + 
Architecture Library at The University of British Columbia.

Krista Belle Stewart is an artist who lives in Vancouver, British Columbia. She 
is currently a photography MFA candidate at the Milton Avery Graduate 
School of Arts at Bard College and holds a BFA in Photography from Emily 
Carr University of Art + Design, Vancouver. Her work engages the 
complexities of intention and interpretation made possible by archival material. 
The work approaches mediation and story-telling as a process of 
development to unfold the interplay between personal and institutional history. 
Stewart has exhibited in numerous group exhibitions, most recently as part of 
Fiction/Non-fiction (2013) at the Esker Foundation. She is a member of the 
Upper Nicola Band of the Okanagan Nation.

Join us on Thursday, March 20, 6:30-8:30pm for Fireside Talk with Krista 
Belle Stewart. Stewart will discuss her research-based curatorial project that 
focuses on historical exhibitions at the BAG as well as objects held in the 
permanent collection. Suggested donation to the gallery is $5.

Gallery Atsui presents: 10 x 10
December 3 to December 20

Gallery Atsui
602 East Hastings (at Princess)
www.galleryatsui.com
info@galleryatsui.com

The exhibition is curated by Gallery Atsui's directors, and features
original works by Brian White, Rebecca Chaperon, Erin Green, Sean Coggins,
Emma Lehto, Ryan Walter Wagner, Lisa Cinar, Jessica Bushey, Alex Grewal and
Sascha Yamashita.

Gallery Atsui provided the artists with 10, 10-by-10-inch panels to do with
as they wish. We are very excited with the results, as artists explore
their individual medium through the use - and abuse - of the space
provided. Individual panel will be available for purchase at the cash only
price of $100.

Preview Nights:
Wednesday, December 3rd, and Thursday, December 4th from 5 to 9 p.m.

First-come, first-serve show and sale:
Friday, December 5 beginning at 8 p.m.

The exhibition runs until December 20, when purchased works will be claimed.

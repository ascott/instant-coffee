Museum of Anthropology | Double Exhibition Opening: Inuit Prints: Japanese 
Inspiration and Faces and Voices of the Inuit Art Market | JUN 19

The opening will feature family-friendly film screenings, a drawing activity with 
the Vancouver Draw Down Team and two new exhibits! Inuit Prints, organized 
by the Canadian Museum of Civilization, features prints from the Dorset Fine 
Arts collective and explores the influence of Japanese printmaking on their 
works. Faces and Voices, a UBC Student exhibition includes objects, images, 
text, video, and recorded sounds related to artists, collectors, critics, and 
researchers involved in the Inuit art market.

Check out our website for more details: http://www.moa.ubc.ca/exhibits. 

Admission $15.68 Adults; $13.44 Students & Seniors 65+ / 6393 N.W. Marine 
Drive, Vancouver BC V6T 1Z2

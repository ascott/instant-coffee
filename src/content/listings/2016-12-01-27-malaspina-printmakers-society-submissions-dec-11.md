Malaspina Printmakers Society:

Holiday Salon Print Sale Submissions

From now until Sunday, December 11th

Currently accepting framed, ready to hang prints under the price range of $300

Malaspina will be hosting a holiday salon style print sale in the gallery opening on 
Wednesday, December 14th. The sale will act as a fundraising initiative and 
additionally as a way encourage and facilitate the purchasing of artworks that are 
specific to printmaking and printed matter.

Help support local artists, artist run centres, and the giving of excellent gifts by 
choosing to 1) donate work or 2) consign work. A donation sale will go %100 to 
Malaspina Printmakers Society and a consignment sale will work the same way 
that our standard consignment does, so you get a portion of the sales too.

Submissions and submission inquiries can be directed to 
gabi(@)malaspinaprintmakers.com. Please include an image of your work and 
subject line: holiday print sale.

Accepted prints can be dropped of at Malaspina during our gallery hours: Mon-Fri 
10am-5pm and Sat + Sun 11am-5pm.

Thank you in advance, hope to you around and we look forward to receiving 
your work!

Tristesse Seeliger: Point of Life
August 28 - November 10, 2023

Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to present Point of Life, a
show of new work by AR&S Featured Artist Tristesse Seeliger.

In her latest series of paintings, Point of Life, Tristesse Seeliger employs solid lines of
color alongside expressive and
gestural mark-making to create a sense of visual
depth. Clearly defined and controlled lines are interlaced with spontaneous pours and
splatters of paint to reveal contrasts between static and shifting forms, permanent
shapes, and fleeting fragments. Point of Life builds upon an established body of work
where investigations of mapping inform collages, paintings, and murals.

Tristesse Seeliger's work will be featured in the Showroom
August 28 to November 10, 2023

Stop Motion Animation Workshop
with Deborah Thompson

June 1st and 2nd
9am -5pm

Grand Forks Art Gallery,
Grand Forks, BC
gallery2grandforks.ca

Join exhibition artist Deborah Thompson for an engaging opportunity to make and
animate your very own hinged paper puppet. All materials will be supplied and
participants are encouraged to bring any paper elements that they may wish to include.
NO animation experience is required. Workshop is open for ages 16 and up.

Registration at gallery2grandfolks.ca

CACV Eco-Arts Salon

When: April 23, 2014, from 7:00 to 9:00 PM
Where: UBC Learning Exchange - 612 Main Street, Vancouver, BC
RSVP at eco-arts-2014.eventbrite.ca

Are you interested in or curious about environmentally engaged art?
Are you looking for a place to let your creativity out to play?
Would you enjoy an opportunity to learn from and create with
professional artists?

If your answer to the above questions is YES, come share your creative
talents and curiosity with the Community Arts Council of Vancouver and
the Art Is Land Network. Share your stories, have some refreshments,
meet people, learn things, make art and have fun.

The Eco-Arts Salon will be held at the UBC Learning Exchange in
association with the CONDUIT Project.

Donations are welcomed. Minimum $5 suggested.

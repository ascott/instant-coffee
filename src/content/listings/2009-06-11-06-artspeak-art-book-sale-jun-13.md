June 13 - July 4, 2009

Summer Reading is a bargain book sale of hard-to-find older publications, donated art books by 
local artists, writers, curators, collectors, thinkers, and institutions as well as discounted Artspeak 
publications.

All books will be sold cheap, cheap, cheap (starting at $0.25). 

To complement the book sale, we will have summer reading "picks" from a selection of local artists 
and writers, including Stan Douglas, Geoffrey Farmer, Lee Henderson, Liz Magor, and Gordon 
Smith.

Come get your summer reading!

Botanical Printmaking with Bonnie Devine and Raven Davis: June 8th & 9th, 10am-
3pm

We are thrilled to invite you to our Botanical Printmaking workshop with Deer Lake
Artists in Residence, Raven Davis and Bonnie Devine on June 8 and 9! The Deer
Lake Artist Residencies run year round and provide opportunities for artists from
across Canada and beyond to stay and create in
Burnaby. This spring, we are
pleased to work with Serpent River Ojibwa artist Bonnie Devine and Anishinaabe
two-spirit artist and educator Raven Davis as part of their residency. Their two-day
workshop on Botanical Printmaking uses traditional alum transfer methods and inks
made from locally gathered flowers, barks, and seeds. Davis and Devine will also
share the outcome of this workshop at a public talk about their practices on June 22
at 2pm, at FLEET Studio in Edmonds Park.

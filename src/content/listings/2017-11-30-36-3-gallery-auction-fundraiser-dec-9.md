About #3:
With a satirical nod to the lack of feasible community spaces in Vancouver, this new
curatorial project exists between private and public realms. #3 Gallery was 
conceived as a non-invasive design object to be situated in a rotating variety of 
artists homes. Exhibitions are available to be viewed by appointment and during 
monthly public openings.

#3 Gallery is organized, curated and designed by practicing artists Julie D. Mills, 
Julia Lamare, Pongsakorn Yananissorn, and Logan Mohr. The construction of #3 
was made possible thanks to the generous project funds allocated by the Westside 
Neighbourhood Small Grant Service.

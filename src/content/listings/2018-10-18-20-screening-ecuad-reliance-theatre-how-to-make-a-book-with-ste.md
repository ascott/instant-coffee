2018 Vancouver Art Book Fair
Free Screening
7PM in the Reliance Theatre
520 East 1st Ave, Vancouver

In collaboration with Fillip's ongoing Means of Production series and the seventh 
annual Vancouver Art Book Fair, join us for a free screening of How to Make a Book 
with Steidl.

For 40 years, Gerhard Steidl has combined the roles of printer and publisher, and 
personally supervised the production of some of the most significant books on 
fashion, art, and photography.

Steidls independent empire is founded on several sources of income"a significant 
portion of his publishing efforts are dedicated to Karl Lagerfeld and Chanel (printing 
everything for the designers, from admission tickets to catalogues). The press also 
prints material for the German Metal Workers Union and, for many years, each new 
book by Nobel laureate Gnter Grass.

These commercial projects help fund Steidl Verlags photo books. How to Make a 
Book with Steidl observes Gerhard Steidl as he collaborates on books with the 
photographers Joel Sternfeld, Robert Frank, Ed Ruscha, Jeff Wall, and Robert 
Adams at their studios and in Gttingen, Germany, on press, providing an insightful 
look at a dynamic, entrepreneurial model dedicated to the craft of bookmaking.

Directed by Jrg Adolph and Gereon Wetzel.
Presented with thanks to Steidl Verlag.
2010 (88 Min)

Collage Salon with Elizabeth Zvonar
Sep. 27, 6:30 p.m. - 9:30 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada
This upcoming Collage Salon will be led by Elizabeth Zvonar, a local Vancouver artist 
working in both collage and sculpture. Zvonar will locate collage through art history with 
a brief set of images that are intended to give some context to how collage has been 
used in the past. The workshop will include some quick exercises to get us started. 
Come sift through magazines and materials to find some treasures to begin working. 
Mobil will supply scissors, glue and some books and magazines to look through. If 
youve got some material that you would like to use, please feel free to bring along. We 
will work collectively on a giant long table. You can take away your piece(s) at the end of 
the session
Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our FAQ.
Drinks available for purchase. No minors, no outside drinks
$25

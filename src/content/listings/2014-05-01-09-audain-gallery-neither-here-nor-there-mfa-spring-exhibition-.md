Neither Here Nor There: MFA Spring Exhibition

Audain Gallery, Vancouver
May 8 – 17, 2014

The idea of a place that is "neither here nor there" invokes somewhere in 
limbo, an in-between space where nothing is what it seems, and where 
anything is possible. This space may be one of limits, boundaries, and 
margins, but it is nevertheless full of potentials, perhaps even more so 
because it exists in the margins, in fragments and constellations; it is in the 
unknown that vitalisation and re-vitalisation can occur at a moment's notice, 
that we can become more than human, and that matter and energy pulse 
powerfully with a profound, non-organic life.

Neither Here Nor There is a presentation of works by students in the MFA 
program at Simon Fraser University's School for the Contemporary Arts. It is 
comprised of an exhibition of interdisciplinary works both inside and outside 
of the Audain Gallery, accompanied by a five night series of live 
performances at SFU at the Goldcorp Centre for the Arts.

Audain Gallery and offsite: Luciana D'Anunciação, Lucien Durey, Deborah 
Edmeades, Curtis Grahauer, Jeff Langille, Avery Nabata, Clint Sleeper, Jaime 
Williams and Nathaniel Wong.

Performance Series: Barbara Adler, Yves Candau, Emmalena Fredriksson, 
Isabelle Kirouac, Megan Stewart and The Party (Kyla Gardiner and Layla 
Marcelle Mrozowski).

Neither Here Nor There is presented by the School for the Contemporary Arts 
at SFU.

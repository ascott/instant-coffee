SE SOUVENIR DE VANCOUVER

The first solo show by photographer Peter Holmes
November 6th - November 20th, 2009
Opening: November 6th, 7-11pm

The Toast Collective
648 Kingsway (Kingsway at Fraser)

se souvenir de vancouver
to remember vancouver

A collection of photographs regarding the souvenirization of human experience.

Selection from the artist statement:
"While watching people around the classic tourist attractions in
Vancouver, it became clear that many people spend more time looking at
the backs of their digital cameras than the actual
subject/monument/landscape. The decision was made in the individuals
to sacrifice some of the here and now for a token of the experience to
be possibly used later. "

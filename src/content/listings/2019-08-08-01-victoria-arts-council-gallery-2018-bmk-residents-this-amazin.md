2018 Bonnie McComb Kreye Studio Residents -- Alex Morrison, Caitlyn Murphy, James 
Gardner, Matthis Grunsky, Sylvie Ringer, Danielle Pasko, Sarah Leckie
Curated by Wendy Welch
Victoria Arts Council Gallery 1800 Store St. Victoria
July 25 - Sept 7 Tues - Sat 12-5pm
Closing event Sept 5

The BMK Studio Residency was established in 2016 by the Kreye family in memory of 
Bonnie McComb Kreye, artist and educator. Each year, up to 7 local and international 
artists are selected to work in the garden studio. In May and June, accommodation is 
provided on site. We are self-funded and our aim is to support the artists creative 
process. The residency has partnered with the Vancouver Island School of Art and is 
supported by the Victoria Arts Council Gallery, Fifty-Fifty Arts Collective and the Ministry 
of Casual Living. For more information: bmkresidency.com IG bmkresidency

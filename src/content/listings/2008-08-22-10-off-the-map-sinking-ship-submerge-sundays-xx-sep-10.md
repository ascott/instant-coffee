The Sinking Ship is an off-the-map gallery/studio space in downtown
Vancouver. It was once the famed "Misanthropy" Gallery, but has since
changed hands. We're hosting a weekly "experimental" night on Sundays
called "Submerge". I'm not sure what "experimental" means, exactly,
but I think it might have something to do with not knowing what
"experimental" means, exactly. So far the night has been focused on
live improvised music but there is also plenty of space for other
kinds of art. This is a loosely curated affair, not a "jam night", so
if you'd like to take part please get in touch. The one limitation is
that we can't . We're also looking for people who'd be into curating a
Sunday per month. The night starts at 8pm, usually ends 10-ish.
Low-key Sunday action. Drop us an email at
overmindproductions@gmail.com for the location, password, or to get
involved.

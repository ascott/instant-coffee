Audain Prize and Viva Awards

Thursday, May, 22, 2008, 7pm

In partnership with the Audain Foundation and the Jack and Doris Shadbolt Foundation for the Visual Arts, the Vancouver Art Gallery will present the 2008 VIVA Awards honouring Jeff Wall, the 2008 recipient of the Audain Prize Lifetime Achievement, and VIVA Awards honourees Tim Lee and Kevin Schmidt. The ceremony and reception will be held Thursday, May 22, at 7 pm at the Gallery.

The Audain Prize for Lifetime Achievement, awarded annually by the Audain Foundation to honour the exceptional career of an outstanding British Columbia artist, includes a cash prize of $25,000. Vancouver artists Tim Lee and Kevin Schmidt will each receive cash awards of $12,000 as the 2008 recipients of the VIVA Awards, an annual prize provided by the and Doris Shadbolt Foundation to exemplary British Columbia artists in mid-career.

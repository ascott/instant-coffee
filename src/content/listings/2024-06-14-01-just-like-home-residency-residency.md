DUE JUNE 25th! Just Like Home Residency residency aims to create time, space, and
networks for four Vancouver Based emerging and mid-career interdisciplinary artists
interested in developing and sharing their ideas communally and collaboratively.

Just Like Home Residency is intended for small practices that could have
been done at
home by creating space and emphasising research and risk-taking. Priority will be given
to artists who identify as BIPOC and 2SLGBTQ+.

Starting in early July 2024, artists will have access to a shared 244 sq ft studio space at
1889 Franklin St for 3 months. The residency includes studio visits with the curator and
Duplex board members, a stipend, an opportunity to exhibit or share their work, and
access to Duplex facilities.

Curated by Yasmine Haiboub

This project is supported by the Canada Council for the Arts.

More info at https://linktr.ee/duplexsociety
and

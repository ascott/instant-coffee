Upon Occasion
July 20 - 25
Concourse Gallery
Emily Carr University of Art + Design

Opening Reception: Thursday, July 21, 6-9pm

Emily Carr University of Art + Design is pleased to present "Upon
Occasion", an exhibition of work by the inaugural cohort of the Low
Residency stream of the Master of Applied Arts program. It showcases works
produced in the first year of research.

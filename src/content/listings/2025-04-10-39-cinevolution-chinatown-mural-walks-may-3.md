Chinatown Mural Walks | May 2025

Join a guided Chinatown mural walk this May in celebration of Asian Heritage Month.
Discover the people, stories, and significance behind the murals in Vancouvers
Chinatown through the eyes of community members. Tours are offered in English,
Cantonese, and Mandarin. Tickets are Pay-What-You-Can, with all proceeds going
to host community organizations.

Tour Meeting Point : Dr. Sun Yat-Sen Public Park, 565 Columbia St., inside
the main courtyard at the "Suzhou Alley Womens Mural"

CULTURAL JOB OPPORTUNITIES

Social Planner I (Cultural Planner)
City of Vancouver (BC), Canada - Vancouver Park Board
Deadline to apply: November 15, 2015

The Social Planner I (Cultural Planner) is responsible in assisting the
Arts, Culture and Engagement Coordinator (ACE) in project
management related to public art, monuments and memorials; cross-
cultural and First Nations cultural planning; community public art and
the Neighbourhood Matching Fund. This includes program design and
advising applicants on proposals for community cultural development
funding; and donations of monuments and memorials in Park areas.
The Cultural Planner will be responsible for conducting research into
other City Models and consulting with the Musqueam, Squamish and
Tsleil-Waututh, the City's Reconciliation Committee and other
stakeholders such as the Chinese Community and Not-for-Profit
organizations.

PUBLIC ART COORDINATOR
City of Surrey, BC, Canada
Part time auxiliary starting fall 2015

Join the City of Surrey's Public Art staff team, and contribute to the
specialized cultural development work related to the public art projects.

Working as part of a team, you will be involved in the administration,
coordination, promotion and implementation of public art projects
throughout Surrey. You will conduct research and produce writing
including drafting the terms of reference for projects, recruit artists,
coordinate competitions, provide critical feedback on technical
drawings and engineering specifications, and support the completion of
art projects with a team, from concept development through
installation. Tasks also involve ensuring the maintenance of the art
collection, organizing events, and working with volunteers.

Working out of the Surrey Arts Centre in Bear Creek Park, you will be
surrounded by colleagues working in visual and performing arts, in a
beautiful park setting.

This auxiliary position works approximately 21 hours per week.
Compensation is $29.71/hour plus benefits payout. If you flourish in an
innovative atmosphere, and are looking to make a positive contribution
to the City of Surrey, please apply online at:

* www.surrey.ca/careers

* http://www.surrey.ca/city-government/6541.aspx

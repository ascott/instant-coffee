Wander and Words Workshop at Art Gallery at Evergreen
May 4, 2024, 2 p.m. - 4 p.m.
1205 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam BC

Join us for Wander and Words, an interactive workshop based on the exhibition
"Paths." Take inspiration from the art in the gallery and the local landscape to create
your own poetic response to the question, What path brought me here today?

The workshop will begin with a tour through the exhibition "Paths" at Art Gallery at
Evergreen, which features artwork by Canadian artists exploring paths, both real and
imagined, through the mediums of painting, drawing, sculpture, photography, collage
and printmaking. Paths is organized and
circulated by Artists for Kids & the Gordon
Smith Gallery and curated by Amelia Epp. A walk through the park surrounding
Lafarge Lake will follow. After wandering the scenic park and engaging in self-
directed writing time, participants will have the opportunity to share what theyve
written and observed.

No writing experience required. Please dress for the weather, bring a water bottle
and wear appropriate footwear for walking. The gallery is wheelchair accessible. The
paved loop around the lake takes between 15-30 minutes to complete and is partially
wheelchair accessible. Please contact Anna Luth at
anna@evergreenculturalcentre.ca if you require accommodations.

Free event. Ages 12+. Registration recommended. 30 spots available.
Register here: https://evergreenculturalcentre.my.salesforce-
sites.com/ticket/#/events/a0SOK000000O3mL2AS. Pay parking available on-site,
easy access from Lafarge Lake-Douglas College Skytrain Station.

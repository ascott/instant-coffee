Richmond Art Gallery
Swimming the River, A Panel Discussion with Gu Xiong

Thursday, October 28, 7:30pm

Catalogue Launch at 7:00 pm

In conjunction with the exhibition, Waterscapes, by Vancouver artist Gu
Xiong, Richmond Art Gallery presents, Swimming the River, a panel
discussion. The discussion will examine what happens when large numbers of
people move around the world. What are the implications? What are some of
the local and global transformations that are taking place?

Panellists include; Gu Xiong Artist, Associate Professor of Fine Arts
University of British Columbia, Dr. Glenn Deer Professor, Department of
English University of British Columbia, Parm Grewal Executive Director
Richmond Multicultural Concerns Society and Justin Tse Ph.D student,
Department of Geography University of British Columbia.

For more information, contact Nan Capogna, Education and Public Programs
Coordinator at 604.247.8311

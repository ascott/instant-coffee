From Sunday September 17 - Friday September 29, 2023, Peripheral Review will
host our second annual online auction of artworks from thirty-seven Canadian and
international artists. The auction will take place entirely on our website and includes
a wide-range of works at varying prices for increased accessibility. All proceeds of
the sales will go to the artists and Peripheral Reviews publishing activities.
We need your help to meet our ambitious future programming and
publishing
projects, including a guest-edited digital chapbook series, our annual print edition,
exhibition at international art book fairs, and to be able to continue to pay fair
CARFAC-standard fees to our contributors and staff.

By bidding and purchasing a work in our online auction this year, you are helping PR
reach our goal of paying fair contributor fees and disseminating our publishing
projects from now into the future, allowing us to continue to represent, and give
space for, accessible and experimental critical dialogue on art, artists, and spaces
considered underrepresented, or otherwise in the periphery.

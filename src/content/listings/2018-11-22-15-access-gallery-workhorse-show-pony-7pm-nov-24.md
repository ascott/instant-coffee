AUCTION EVENING 24 November 2018 at 7pm
PREVIEW Wednesday 21 November at 6pm
ON VIEW 21 to 23 November from 12 to 5pm

workhorse, show pony is an energetic celebration of hardworking and dynamic artist-run 
culture! This years fundraising auction features a range of fantastic work generously 
donated by over 50 emerging and established artists including Vanessa Brown, David 
Khang, Guadalupe Martinez, Ramey Newell, and Christian Nicolay. Jonathan Middleton 
and drag queen Anne Xiety take the reins as MC and auctioneer while DJs Ron Tran 
and Rebecca Brewer bring us the tunes. As is tradition, expect a mystery artist whose 
identity will only be revealed through bidding. The generosity of members, donors, and 
friends like you continue to make it possible for the gallery to support compelling and 
critically engaged emergent practices. Show up in your ribbons and tack to Access 
most important fundraiser of the year!

With gratitude as guests, Access is located on the unceded territories of the 
xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish), and Sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh

Access additionally recognizes its location in Vancouvers Chinatown, an area for the

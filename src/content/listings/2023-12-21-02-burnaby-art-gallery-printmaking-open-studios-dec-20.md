Do you have a printmaking project but no press? Maybe youve taken a few classes
with us and want to revisit the techniques you learned at your own pace?

Burnaby Art Gallery is now offering Open Studio sessions for self-directed work. On
the third Wednesday of every month, we open our studio with access to our table top
printing press with 4.5 diameter upper drum, tools, and BAG
inks for self-directed work.

Bring your own paper and matrix, or purchase them at the Shadbolt art store in
advance - well supply the inks and the atmosphere.

Please note - this is a self-directed printmaking opportunity with no instructor. Some
printmaking experience is required.

Visit our website or call 604-297-4422 to register or learn more!

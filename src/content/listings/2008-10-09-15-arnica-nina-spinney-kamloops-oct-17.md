Nina Spinney
Chromatic Environment

Kamloops native & 2008 graduate of Emily Carr, Nina Spinney, works with the
psychology of colour. Using light boxes illuminated by LEDs and controlled
by a microchip, Spinney creates a mood- changing environment.

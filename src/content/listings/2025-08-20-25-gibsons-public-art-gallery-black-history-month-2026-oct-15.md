In 2026 were holding space for Black History Month from February 12th - March 8th.
All visual artists of African descent, working in any style or medium, are invited to
submit a proposal using our online form by the deadline of October 15th. Taking place
in our Eve Smart Gallery, the aim of this exhibition is to provide an opportunity for
conversation and connection. Previous Black History Month exhibitions include T.
Owens Unions Stories That Still Need to Be Told (2025), Ezmina Samaroos Distant
Shores New Horizons (2024), Black History Month with Teri Ejere (2023), and Denise
Browns The Fabric of Freedom " Quilts and the Underground Railroad (2021).

Please read our Submission Guidelines before applying using our online form before
October 15th, 2025. For more information about this exhibition check out our website,
or for any questions contact Lesley Bullard (GPAG Director/Curatorial Committee) at
lesleypacific@gmail.com. Please note that we will not accept applications made by
email.

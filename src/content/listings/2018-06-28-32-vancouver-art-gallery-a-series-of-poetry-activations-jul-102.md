The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Reclusive Cosmopolitans: A Series of Poetry 
Activations

Vancouver Art Gallery
750 Hornby Street Vancouver BC 

Date: Tuesday, July 10 at 6:30pm on the First and Second Floor Galleries " Second 
iteration of the series features readings by Colin Browne, Jaclyn Bruneau, Shazia 
Hafiz Ramji, Kerriann Cardinal, Steffanie Ling and Stacey Ho.

Date: Tuesday, July 24 at 6:30pm on the First and Second Floor Galleries - poetry 
series wraps up with readings by Colin Browne, Danielle LaFrance, Shazia Hafiz 
Ramji and Kerriann Cardinal.

In conjunction with the exhibitions David Milne: Modern Painting and Cabin Fever, 
join the Gallery for a series of poetry interventions taking place on select Tuesday 
evenings through June and July. This series of programs will provide a space for the 
literary and visual arts to convene and find new ways of relating to each other.

Poems will be selected and performed throughout these exhibitions by Danielle 
LaFrance, Colin Browne, Shazia Hafiz Ramji, Kerriann Cardinal, Steffanie Ling and 
Stacey Ho, among others. Using the ideas of retreat and solitude as a starting point, 
texts selected will be read, sung or otherwise offer different kinds of encounters with 
the work on view at the Gallery. The texts recited by the poets represent a dialogue 
with the exhibitions and their concerns"be it an interrogation, an argument, an 
appreciation, a love letter, a question, a complaint, a commentary, a 
contextualization"performing myriad ways of engaging.

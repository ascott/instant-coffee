Vancouver based artist, illustrator and editor Marc Bell will be at Massy Books signing
books and sharing about his practice. Marc Bell is a Canadian cartoonist and artist,
initially known for
creating comic strips (such as ), but Bell has since
created a number of mixed media and watercolour drawings.

This event takes place in conjunction with Bell's exhibition - -
. Using a visual stream of consciousness, Bell overcrowds each ink
drawing, creating dense, chaotic image planes reminiscent of labels and diagrams
intended to be read but not followed. In his mixed media works, Marc draws attention to
seemingly random material (messy drawings and other bits of paper that would normally
be discarded), and builds these up in layers in a meditative process in an attempt to
create an object of interest that is both scrappy and refined.

We are excited to offer this opportunity to exhibit in a public group exhibition coming
March 2024. The exhibition, entitled SIXBYSIX, will consist of 40-60 66 panels
exhibited at the same height around the circumference of the gallery. There is no
other theme, however, the curator will give preference to work that is innovative,
thoughtful and well-executed.

All work in the exhibition will be offered to the public for $300 each. This is a great
opportunity for artists to try something outside of their comfort zone! Its also a great
opportunity for collectors to purchase an original work of art at an affordable price.

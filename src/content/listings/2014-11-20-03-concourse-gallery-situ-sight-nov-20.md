SITU SIGHT
Concourse Gallery
Emily Carr University
1399 Johnston Street
November 17-21

Reception 7pm, Thursday November 20

This exhibition features the work of senior Emily Carr Students considering
the notion of the photographer as social agent. Working in photography,
video, audio, sculpture and performance, their projects deal with histories
and current issues of particular tangible and intangible sites/situations,
as well as the social and political implications of their interventions.
Their works are informed by the production of space and social situations
in the present moment but also engage with past narratives, technologies,
and ideologies.

Organized by Althea Thauberger

Artists:
Ezra Bloom, Peter Ullstrom, RJ McDonald, Alison Westdorp, Aileen Choi,
Raine Gauthier, Ally Czernin-Spencer, Melissa Woodley, Sam Lim, Katy
Morrison, Mengyao Liang, Josiah Westley, Rikke Goldbech.

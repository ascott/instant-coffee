Last Chance! Exhibition closes Saturday, July 26

Errol and George: Two Sad Swashbucklers
An exhibit of paintings, drawings and video by Michael Markowsky

Access Art Gallery 
206 Carrall Street
Vancouver, BC
(604) 689-2907
[http://www.vaarc.ca](http://www.vaarc.ca) 
Tuesday to Saturday from 12pm - 5pm

Did you know that movie star Errol Flynn died in Glenn Gould’s Uncle’s West End apartment, 
nearly 50 years ago? Did you know that in June 1792, Captain George Vancouver became the 
first European to explore and map the area around Vancouver?

Artist Michael Markowsky made dozens of drawings while standing on a powerboat as it sped 
along the original path taken by Captain Vancouver as he surveyed this area. This dangerous 
stunt is documented on video which is also on display, as well as two giant mural sized oil 
paintings based on the Vancouver landscape.

Michael Markowsky is has exhibited his artwork throughout the United States and Canada, 
showing alongside many of the world’s most recognized artists including Robert Rauschenberg 
and Ed Ruscha. He was a student the Royal College of Art in London, England, Cooper Union in 
New York City and Art Center in Los Angeles (MFA). He has won numerous awards, including the 
'Power of Art' award from the Robert Rauschenberg Foundation, in 2007.

An eating performance with Olivia Chow

July 19, 7pm
Centre A
Gallery Hours: Tuesday - Saturday, 12-5pm

Centre A is now located at Unit 205-268 Keefer Street on the 2nd Floor. 

This exhibition and performance takes place on the unceded Coast Salish territories of the 
Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples.

Conceived during a five week residency at the John and Maggie Mitchell Gallery in 
Edmonton, Olivia Chow will perform We Do Chicken Right for I know you are but what am I, 
an exhibition curated by Joni Cheung. Hosted by Centre A, the performance draws 
inspiration from personal stories and realizations based on meals shared and dialogue 
exchanged with families from diasporic communities.

During her residency, Chow was invited to peoples homes to have dinner. At these 
gatherings, the artist conducted research through conversations with participants and the 
food served. On July 19, 7pm, Chow will present the culmination of her notes and texts, 
while serving the audience Kentucky Fried Chicken. Through this eating performance, she 
investigates the violence and politics behind the food that connects us and brings us joy.

Please RSVP to programs@centrea.org as space is limited.

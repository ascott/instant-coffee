The Department of Visual Arts at Western University in London, Ontario is seeking
applications from emerging to established curators for a one-year Curator-in-Residence
in Social and Environmental Justice in the Arts. The Department of Visual Arts supports
numerous projects committed to social and environmental justice and would welcome
the successful candidates input and/or collaboration in these areas.

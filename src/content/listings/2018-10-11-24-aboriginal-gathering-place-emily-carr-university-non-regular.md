EVENT: Launch of Non-Regular, a book about precarious labour at Emily Carr
University

Tuesday Oct 23
Reception at 6pm followed by artist's talk
at the Aboriginal Gathering Place
Emily Carr University of Art & Design, 520 East 1st Avenue.
Wheelchair accessible.

Join us for the launch of "Non-Regular: Precarious academic labour at Emily
Carr University of Art + Design," an artist book by Terra Poirier made in
collaboration with 26 ECU instructors and artists, and published by
UNIT/PITT Projects.

Panelists will address the question:

HOW DOES EMPLOYMENT PRECARITY AFFECT OUR ART & RESEARCH PRACTICES?

With:
Kristina Lee Podesva, artist, writer, editor and former ECUAD sessional
instructor Deneige Nadeau, ECUAD sessional instructor, Critical & Cultural
Studies Terra Poirier, Non-Regular editor and ECUAD grad Rita Wong, poet,
activist & former ECUAD Faculty Association President

Moderated by Magnolia Pauker, ECUAD lecturer, Critical & Cultural Studies

Presented by UNIT/PITT Projects and the Emily Carr University Faculty
Association.
A Vancouver Art Book Week and Fair Employment Week event.

More info: nonregular.ca. Books will also be available at the Vancouver Art
Book Fair Oct 18-21.

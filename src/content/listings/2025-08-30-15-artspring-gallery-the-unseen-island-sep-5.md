The Unseen Island, ArtSpring Gallery, Art Spring Island, BC

Opening: September 5, 6-8 pm
Exhibition dates: September 5-21, 2025, 10 AM-4 PM daily. (Closing 2 PM, Sept. 21)
Artist Panel: September 13, 1:30-3:30 pm
Discussion and mentoring: by appointment

Please join us for the opening of The Unseen Island to be held at ArtSpring Gallery
from September 5-21, 2025.

Exploring the experiential, poetic, unseen and simple, The Unseen Island is an
exhibition about how the poetic can choose its time and place to reveal itself.
Curated by Juan Carlos Rodriguez Valds, the show features three Salt Spring
artists: Eli Horn, James K-M, and Denise Okan.

The exhibition will present new video, photography, painting, and sculpture. A video
by Rodriguez Valds will also be shown along with a collaboration piece created on
Salt Spring Island with James K-M.

The exhibition has a strong international connection through curator Juan Carlos
Rodrguez Valds from Pinar del Rio, Cuba. In advance of The Unseen Island, all
three artists participated in his Complicit Landscapes project as part of the Farmacia
project in Pinar del Rio. Rodrguez Valds is in residence on Salt Spring from Cuba
for the exhibition, and will be present at the opening, in the gallery, and on the artist
panel on September 13 from 1:30-3:30 PM. Salt Spring Island resident Wilson
Gonzalez-Rico will provide translation.

The opening event on September 5th, will feature music by Graham Van Pelt and
will introduce The Indefinite Threeatre.

The exhibiting artists and curator are available for discussion during gallery hours.
Artists and young artists are welcome to arrange an appointment to discuss their
work, as future exhibitions are in development. Email: info@unseenisland.com

The Unseen Island is grateful for the support of Salt Spring Arts, Country Grocer,
and Farmacia (Cuba).

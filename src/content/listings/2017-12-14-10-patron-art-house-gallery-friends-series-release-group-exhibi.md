Gallery Friends Series Release & Group Exhibition
December 16 - 23, 2017
Opening reception Saturday, December 16, 12:00pm - 6:00pm

Patron Art House
923 W 8 Avenue, Vancouver, BC
Canada V5Z1E4, Wed-Sat 12- 6pm
www.patronarthouse.com

Patron Art House is pleased to present the Gallery Friends Series, as part of our
mandate to educate a wider audience about Vancouver artists. This series is comprised
of original works of art commissioned to local artists, presented on specially designed
cards, all 5 by 7 inches. In addition to the series release we are presenting a group
exhibition of available work by Thomas Arden, Adam Brickell, Mark DeLong, Sharona
Franklin, Marisa Kriangwiwat Holmes and Matthew Shields.

For information about works for purchase, please contact info@patronarthouse.com

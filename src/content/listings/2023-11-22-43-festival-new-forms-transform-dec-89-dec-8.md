Featuring acclaimed Almaazz, C. Diab,
Jairus Sharif & Mustafa Rafiq, and PuBu.

New Forms Festival, Acceleration Radio, and Fromlife reimagine the annual precipice
with a two-day network of generative art, raves, and immersive music. Situated at KW
Studios in the Woodwards Building, TRANSFORM will occupy two spaces. In the KW
atrium, visitors can expect a hybrid of unique
visuals, DJ sets, and instrumental
textures. In the production studio below, a series of radical live performances will
unfold in parallel. Sets will begin at 9:00 PM and end at 2:30 AM in both rooms.

The concept of TRANSFORM is rooted in movement. The singular form is static and
material. In its morphed state, it is without boundaries " no longer confined by
conventions of genre or the need to be still. As three organizations working locally,
TRANSFORM is an audiovisual confluence. Over the course of two days, audiences
will celebrate the existing network and immerse themselves in its transformation.

Artist Talk & Tour with Jason Young Relational / Reflexive

Location: PoMoArts, 2425 St Johns Street Port Moody.

Date: April 11, 2024 Time: 7:00 PM

Website: https://pomoarts.ca/exhibitions/relational-reflexive

Relational / Reflexive is Youngs debut solo exhibition charting the development of his
intensive art practice. His work investigates the space between queerness and
abstraction through deconstructed portraiture, honouring members of Vancouver's Queer
community.

Join us at PoMoArts on Thursday, April 11th for an artist talk and exhibition tour of
Relational / Reflexive with Jason Young, as he shares insights on his process and
production, inspiration and influences, as well as obstacles and breakthroughs.

The Deer Lake Gallery is currently accepting exhibition submissions for our 2024
programming.

The DLG is an art house in Burnaby, BC that provides opportunities for emerging
and established artists to create meaningful, thought provoking and experimental
work. Founded by the Burnaby Arts Council, the DLG hosts 10 " 12 exhibitions per
year, and acts as a host location for performances, concerts, and a variety of arts
initiatives from partnering organizations.

We invite visual artists working with all media including painting, photography, mixed
media, sculpture, ceramics, wood, metal work and installation work. Photography or
digital mixed media work may include prints, otherwise all other work must be
original. No giclee prints, photocopies, numbered prints, or commercial
reproductions will be accepted for exhibition.

Exhibitions typically last five weeks, with an opening reception and the possibility for
artist talks or workshops.

Application deadline: October 21, 2023 (Midnight)

For more information on how to apply:
https://deerlakegallery.ca/exhibitions/submissions/

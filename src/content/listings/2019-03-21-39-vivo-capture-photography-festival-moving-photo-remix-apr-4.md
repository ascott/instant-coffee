A fun and creative introduction to animating photos using Photoshop. 

Each session combines visual study of examples, group discussion, and hands-on 
experience. 

Gain technical knowledge of Photoshop. Learn about local and international artists who 
manipulate photos and discuss the meaning and effect of the resulting images. Create photo 
remixes of your own!

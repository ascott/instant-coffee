Dates: September 8th & 9th, 2016

Time: 6 p.m. onward

Web: www.paarc.ca/swarm17

SWARM is an annual 2-night event that marks the launch of 
Vancouver's artist-run centre programming season with exhibition 
openings, performances, screenings, and special events. On the 
evenings of Thursday, September 8th and Friday, September 9th, 
the public is invited to "gallery-hop" from venue to venue to 
experience the vibrancy of Vancouver's artist-run centres and 
independent non-profit galleries.

Most SWARM events are free and open to the public!

Thursday September 8th: Mount Pleasant & Beyond

Participating venues include: Cineworks, grunt gallery, Malaspina 
Printmakers, VIVO Media Arts Centre, Western Front, and other 
non-profit contemporary art spaces.

Friday September 9th: Chinatown/DTES

Participating venues include: 221A, Access Gallery, Artspeak, 
Dynamo Arts Association, Gam Gallery, Or Gallery, VIVO Media 
Arts Centre, and other non-profit contemporary art spaces.

For detailed information about SWARM programming, events, and 
venue locations visit: www.paarc.ca/swarm17

Join the conversation: #ArtistRun

About SWARM
Organized since 1999, SWARM is an annual festival put on by the 
Pacific Association of Artist-Run Centres (PAARC) to launch the 
programming season of Vancouver's artist-run, non-profit, and 
independent galleries. This year, we celebrate the 17th edition of 
SWARM. 
About PAARC
The Pacific Association of Artist-Run Centres (PAARC) represents 
artist-run centres in BC, with the primary mandate of working 
towards the benefit of the practicing artist, within the context of 
artists' self-determination. PAARC counts over 20 member centres 
(located in Vancouver, Victoria, Kelowna, Kamloops, and Nelson) 
and represents them regionally, provincially, and nationally. For 
more information visit: http://www.paarc.ca

West Coast nature painting with Sean Karemaker

Jul. 27, 7:00 p.m. - 9:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Learn how to paint layered, lively, vibrant West coast nature scenes using acrylic paints with 
Sean Karemaker and create a painting (or two) to hang at home. The techniques in this course 
are based on Sean's wonderful mural paintings at Vancouver General Hospital.

All skill levels welcome, including beginners. Materials required: basic acrylic paints, flat 
brushes, canvas, paper or board.

Please contact us at info@mobil-art.ca if you have any questions regarding materials. $45

Call for Submissions – Curators and artists welcome to submit

Deadline is Jan 31, 2009

Proposals will be considered for 2010

PUBLIC GALLERY MANDATE:
The Comox Valley Art Gallery's public gallery provides an 
environment for
the exhibition of Canadian and international conceptually and 
aesthetically
engaging art and related cultural practices. CVAG’s primary 
concern is to
support the professional development of emerging artists 
through critical
activities that include exhibitions, lectures, publications, events 
and
special projects. Local emerging artists are encouraged to 
submit proposals
and are often invited to exhibit alongside established artists.
ARTS & CRAFT GALLERY MANDATE:
This gallery is an innovative approach to exhibit two-
dimensional artworks
complemented by three-dimensional artworks to achieve a 
combination that
reflects craft as fine art. The manipulation of paint, clay or any 
medium
with the intention to create fine art, is a craft. To 
accommodate this aim
some rules of aesthetics have to be set. An emphasis must be 
on good
presentation so that the gallery appears pleasing as a set piece.
WINDOW GALLERY MANDATE:
The Window Gallery is in place to promote contemporary 
artwork and
community projects that reflect excellence in the arts. This 
exhibiting
area is open to view 24 hours a day and is most suited to three 
dimensional
artwork or artwork not affected by intense daylight.
GEORGE SAWCHUK GALLERY MANDATE:
The GS gallery is in place to promote fine art for young people 
and by
young people. Didactic material must accompany all exhibits 
mainly for the
benefit of young people as an introduction to the artwork and to
contemporary fine art.

Applicants are requested to provide the following:

1) Cirriculum Vitae, including the applicants’ exhibiting history 
beginning
with the most recent.

2) 10 - 20 slides, CD (jpegs or tiffs only) of your most recent work 
or of
the work relating to the proposal. Include a detailed image list 
of work
submitted.

3) Artist statement

4) Exhibition proposal, explaining your intentions of how and 
what you
expect to exhibit.

5) SASE for the return of your materials.

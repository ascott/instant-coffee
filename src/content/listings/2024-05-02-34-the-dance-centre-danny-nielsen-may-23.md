The Dance Centre presents the Global Dance Connections series
Danny Nielsen
In Conversation (world premiere)
Thursday & Friday May 23 & 24, 2024 | 8pm
Scotiabank Dance Centre, 677 Davie St, Vancouver
Tickets $35 regular/$26 students, seniors
Info and tickets: https://thedancecentre.ca/event/danny-nielsen/

The Dance Centre presents Canadian tap dance artist Danny Nielsen's In Conversation,
a collaboration with renowned
pianist/percussionist Kristian Alexandrov which delves
into the symbiotic relationship between tap dance and jazz piano. Touching on new
takes of old standards, and original music, they will co-create a performance drawing on
everything from traditional and contemporary jazz to R&B, hip hop and rock, in a
celebration of tap and musical artistry.

Vehicle: Thinking Through the Box

For the inaugural artist residency at the Langara College

Centre for Art in Public Spaces, Kristina Lee Podesva

presents Vehicle. The project re-purposes the symbolic and

architectural space of shipping containers to catalyze

conversations and collaborations among the Langara College

community, neighbourhood and beyond about our experience and

understanding of globalization.

Thought for Food: a Gastronomic Reading Room

with Jeffrey Swartz

Through February 10

In this installation, Jeffrey Swartz addresses the absence

of classic gastronomic texts from local public and

university libraries. Thought for Food presents a citation

reading room featuring key examples of gastronomic thought

and writing. Arranged like a tapas bar, this installation

entices visitors with a rare collection of books dedicated

to the art of cooking and eating while raising

questions about the status of this genre within the history

of thought. Among the texts included in Thought for Food is

How to Cook and Eat in Chinese by Buwei Yang Chao, considered

one of North America's first introductions to Chinese

cooking. Vehicle's inaugural reading room sets the stage for

a series of programs that connects rituals of food to current

economic, political and cultural conditions.

This Week's Program: Tuesday, February 10, 7pm

Maximum Consumption

Vehicle is proud to announce the presentation of Maximum

Consumption, a theatrical production, inspired by the Kinks'

song from 1972. A collaboration between Studio 58's third

term students, Alison Kelly, Kathryn Shaw, and Kristina Lee

Podesva, Maximum Consumption offers an original work

developed through a series of improvisations framed by the

song and experiences of food and consumption.

Cast and crew are Paula Burrows, Jimmy Elston, Dustin

Freeland, Hannah Johnson, Anton Lipovetsky, Graeme McComb,

Pandora Morgan, Gili Roskies, Mark Shelling, Katie Swinwood.

MEDIATED VISIONS : Film, Art + Technology

Tuesday, October 18th, 7:00- 10:00 / Doors 6:15
Anvil Theatre + New Media Gallery
Tickets at the Door / Cash Bar

Please join us for a brilliant evening of film and art + technology talks
in the beautiful Anvil Theatre! We will begin with the newest film from
Werner Herzog. This will be followed by a couple of short-talks by
Canadian artist Adam Basanta and cognitive systems specialist Mazdak
Gharibnavaz from Open Media. This promises to be an eye-opening,
mind-expanding evening you will not want to miss!

New Media Gallery, in partnership with DOXA Documentary Film Festival's
Motion Pictures Film Series, New West Film Fest, Momentum Youth Arts
Movement and the City of New Westminster/Arts Services is proud to present
this unique event. Built around ideas generated by the New Media Gallery
exhibition WITNESS, the Mediated Visions event will address the
interconnected nature of human society, culture and technology.

LO AND BEHOLD: Reveries of the Connected World, is the new film by Director
Werner Herzog. Described as a 'rare filmmaker with a big personality' this
fascinating film describes the promises and pitfalls of the digital age.
It charts a winding course from the earliest days of what was once dubbed
'The world-wide web' to the more frightening implications of today.
Namely, can we exist without the Internet? Would we want to?

WITNESS, an exhibition presented by New Media Gallery, will stay open until
7:00pm to welcome early visitors to the event. Five international works of
art allow us to explore shifting levels of machine vision and recognition.
Through these works we can begin to understand the fragmented and
interconnected nature of the vision process and the possible future of
interconnected, seeing machines.

Two short-talks will follow the screening:
ADAM BASANTA is a young, award-winning, Canadian composer, musician and
installation artist who exhibits worldwide. What happens when a new media
artist is a technological sceptic? Can inspiration be found in unexpected
corners of the media-scape? Basanta will attempt to tease out answers to
these questions through a discussion of his work; A Truly Magical Moment.

MAZDAK GHARIBNAVAZ is the Grassroots Development Officer at OpenMedia. He
is a fervent believer in the power of the Internet to address the upcoming
challenges of the 21st century. He initially joined OpenMedia as a
volunteer, and became a staffer in the fight for a free and open Internet.
He too will describe the experience of dealing with technology and our
interconnected world.

Earlier in the day, from 4:00 - 5:00pm, a workshop is presented by Momentum
Festival. In Phoned Footage, Louisa Phung has worked with youth to create
new media art.

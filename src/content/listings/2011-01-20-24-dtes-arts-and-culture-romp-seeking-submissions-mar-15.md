The DTES arts and culture Romp is seeking submissions for its
first ever show. The deadline for registration is march 15th, 2010.

May 13th, 14 & 15th will see the first ever Downtown East Side Arts
and Culture Romp. The Romp was started in support of the arts and
activism in the Downtown East Side. The Downtown East Side is
one of the oldest arts communities in Vancouver and has long been
a Mecca for emerging artist, unconventional political thought and
community activism. Over the course of the weekend there will be
open studios, live events and entertainment.

For more information or for registration, please e-mail
info@theromp.com

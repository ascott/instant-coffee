Mother Tongue opens February 26
CityScape Community ArtSpace
https://northvanarts.ca/events-exhibitions/mother-tongue/

What is motherhood? What does it mean to be a mother? These questions are explored in
Mother Tongue, the upcoming exhibition at North Van Arts.

Artists Sara Khan, Laura Rosengren, and Katherine Duclos address the complex range of
emotions and practices inherent in the life-changing transformation motherhood brings. This
exhibition draws together multiple mothers narratives by creating a visual language of their
experiences, challenging the idealized preconceived notions of motherhood. We invite
viewers to experience the joyful, chaotic, loud, incredible, and surreal elements of being a
Mother.

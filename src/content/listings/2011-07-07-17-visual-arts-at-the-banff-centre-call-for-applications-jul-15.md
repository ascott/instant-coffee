Banff Artist in Residence (BAIR) Programs

Ongoing opportunities

Banff Artist in Residence programs offer independent periods of study where 
artists, curators, and other arts professionals are free to experiment and explore. 
Participants are provided with an individual studio accessible 24 hours a day, as 
well as use of Visual Arts facilities including printmaking, papermaking,

opportunities to work at a remove from the constraints of everyday life.

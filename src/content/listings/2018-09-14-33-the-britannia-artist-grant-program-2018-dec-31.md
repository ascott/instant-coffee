34. SELLing/WANTing/RENTing/SUBLETing/ANNOUNCINGing
1.
StudioLand | Studios available immediately

Available immediately or Oct 1, at StudioLand, brand new studio
spaces on East Pender Street between Columbia and Main Street.

The original space has been completely renovated and divided into 10
individual studios with exhibition opportunity in the common area.

Located on the second floor of a protected heritage building, the studios
are available in a variety of sizes starting at $400 per month.

Several spaces have windows looking onto Pender Street with great natural
light, and there is also a small kitchen area, bathroom and shower, sorry-
no live-ins.

Bright, clean, safe, private, 24 hour access, free wifi and utilities, slop
sink, bike parking, and an outstanding central location. Shared studios are
welcome.

One of the studios may be rented on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis for
shoots, short-term projects, studio visits, or meetings. Inquiries are
welcome.

Were looking for quiet, serious, long-term studio mates, and the spaces
are ideal for painters, graphic artists, designers, illustrators, writers,
animators, fabric artists, filmmakers, or any other creative pursuits!

A five year lease is in place until June 2023 with an option to renew.

StudioLand is in Chinatown, near Strathcona, and is within the Culture Crawl
boundaries, steps away from galleries, cafes, restaurants, bus lines, and
bike paths.

Please message me at jdolinko@telus.net to set up an appointment to view
them at your earliest convenience!

SCREENING + TALK | VIVO Media Arts Centre | LINE IN THE SAND | APR 22

WEDNESDAY, APRIL 22, 7-10PM

This is the Vancouver premiere of Line In The Sand, also featuring a talk
by Sakej Ward (Mik'maq Warrior Society) discussing the film, and the
potential impact of proposed Bill C-51 on Indigenous people's right to
protest and public assembly. Directors Tomas Borsa and Jean-Phillipe
Marquis will be in attendance and available for questions.

Suggested donation $10, but no one turned away.

SYNOPSIS:
Filmed over the course of two years, Line In The Sand is a feature-length
documentary which examines the political context precipitating the approval
of the Northern Gateway. Highlighting the imminence and uncertainty felt at
the local level, Line In The Sand lays bare the visceral connection felt by
those who call the land home, their struggles to be heard, and the lengths
to which many are willing to go to protect their sense of place and
identity. The film contains interviews with a wide range of enigmatic,
powerful characters from Alberta to Haida Gwaii, including educators,
farmers, and Indigenous knowledge keepers.

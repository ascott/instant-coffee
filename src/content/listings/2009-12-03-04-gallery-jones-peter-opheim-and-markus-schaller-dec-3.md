Peter Opheim and Markus Schaller at Gallery Jones: opening reception
Thursday, Dec. 3, 6-9pm

Peter Opheim, a painter from New York City, will be exhibiting new work on
paper and canvas. Opheim was a recent recipient of the Pollack/Krasner
Foundation grant and has exhibited widely across North America, with
paintings in the Museum of Fine Arts, New Mexico and the Seattle Art Museum.

Markus Schaller is a sculptor and film-maker based in Berlin. He
represented Germany at the Venice Biennale and his recent exhibitions
include the Sylt Outdoor Exhibition, Germany; the International Sculpture
Conference, Chattanooga, TN; Vancouver International Sculpture Project;
Ural Industrial Biennale, Magnitogorsk, Russia.

The exhibition runs until December 23rd.

Gallery Assistant - Paul Kyle Gallery

Submission Deadline: Sunday, December 7
Interviews Begin: Tuesday, December 9

Organization Description
Paul Kyle Gallery is a commercial gallery specializing in fine contemporary and historical
art, presenting a tightly curated program from both the primary and secondary markets.
The gallery is known for rigorous scholarship, thoughtful exhibition-making, and close
relationships with artists, collectors, and institutions.

Website: paulkylegallery.com

Job Description

Paul Kyle Gallery is seeking a highly capable, intellectually curious, and well-organised
Full-Time Gallery Assistant to join our team. This is a long-term role for someone who
thrives in a dynamic environment where precision, initiative, and strong interpersonal
abilities are essential.

The Gallery Assistant plays a key role in daily operations, including visitor engagement,
media outreach, research support, writing, and exhibition assistance. The ideal candidate
is someone who absorbs information quickly, communicates clearly, and naturally makes
connections between ideas, people, and tasks. As you become more familiar with our
artists, clients, and inventory, responsibilities will expand into more complex and rewarding
areas.

Flexibility is required for occasional evening and Sunday events or openings.

Responsibilities

Greet visitors warmly and provide informed introductions to exhibitions and artists

Respond to email inquiries with professionalism and accuracy

Lead media relations tasks: soliciting press coverage, preparing press releases,
and submitting event listings

Regular outreach and communication with writers, curators, collectors; comfortable
initiating professional conversations by phone and email

Support research, writing, and preparation of gallery materials

Assist with print and digital marketing, including scheduling and implementing
posts on social media

Pack, unpack, and handle artworks safely

Assist with installation and deinstallation of exhibitions

Receive and catalogue new artworks; ensure accurate inventory tracking

Financial record keeping of exhibitions, creating invoice and reconciliation

Maintain the cleanliness and presentation of the gallery

Assist during openings, including basic food preparation

Perform daily opening and closing procedures

Qualifications and Skills

Warm, articulate, and naturally comfortable engaging with a wide range of people

Excellent judgment, strong observational skills, and the ability to make connections
quickly

Highly organised and detail-oriented; able to prioritise effectively

Demonstrated experience in media relations, marketing, or communications, or
existing relationships with media is required

Confident making outreach and cold calls. Sales experience is an asset

Strong research ability, with genuine interest in learning about artists, collectors,
and institutions

Post-secondary education or equivalent experience in art administration, museum
or cultural studies, or a related field. Strong interest in contemporary art required

Capable of manual labour including moving, hanging, and packing artworks; ability
to lift 50 lbs

Experience in professional art settings is an asset

Typing speed and accuracy; able to take dictation

Proficient with MS Office (Word, Excel, Outlook)

Knowledge of website building is desirable

Skills in Adobe Photoshop, InDesign, Illustrator, or Premiere are strong assets

Valid Class 5 drivers license and personal vehicle are assets

Nice handwriting is an asset

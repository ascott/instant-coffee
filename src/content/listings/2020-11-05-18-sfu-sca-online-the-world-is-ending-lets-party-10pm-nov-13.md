The World is Ending, Lets Party
Friday, November 13, 2020 | 7:00 PM - 10:00 PM (PST) | Zoom | PWYC

A SCA Theatre Student Union Fundraiser Cabaret

The icecaps are melting, the sky is burning, and a deadly virus has spread across the
planet - it's the end of the world as we know it, and it turns out we're not feeling fine! The
SCA Theatre Student Union (TSU) still
wants to make the best of this crappy year by
showcasing out artists at The World is Ending, Let's Party, a fundraising cabaret.

This is a pay-what-you-can event, hosted on Zoom. Tickets are available on Eventbrite:
https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/the-world-is-ending-lets-party-a-tsu-fundraiser-cabaret-
tickets-122932958789

The Cabaret will be a fundraiser to celebrate our community of artists and to raise
money so that the TSU can support future opportunities, such as grants and workshops
for its members!

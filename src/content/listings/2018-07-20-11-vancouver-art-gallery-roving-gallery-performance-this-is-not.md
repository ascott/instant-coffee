The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Roving Gallery Performance: this is not us

Date: Saturday, July 21 at 2pm in the Third Floor Galleries.

This roving Gallery performance by artist Ayumi Goto and Peter Morin and
Senior Curatorial Fellow, Indigenous Art, Tarah Hogue will activate three
portrait masks carved by Haida artist Corey Bulpitt, commissioned for the
exhibition Ayumi Goto and Peter Morin: how do you carry the land?. The
portrait masks both protect the face from view, while revealing a likeness,
and draw upon histories of production and practice across cultural
contexts. What becomes visible in this encounter?

Free for Members or with Gallery Admission.

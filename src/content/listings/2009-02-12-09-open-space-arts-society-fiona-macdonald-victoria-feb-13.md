Index project (renovated)
Fiona Macdonald
Installation: Monday, February 9 - Friday, February 13
Opening: Friday, February 13
Feedback: Tuesday, February 9, 11am -1pm and 2pm - 4pm
Opening: Friday February 13, 7:30pm

Fiona Macdonald's (Australia) Index project (renovated) investigates
how historical art inhabits contemporary art production and presentation.
Macdonald bases her project on the collaborative conceptual art group Art &
Language, whose Index Project was presented at Documenta 5 in 1972 the
same year Open Space was incorporated.

The Index project (renovated) involves two parts. The first is a
large-scale wall
text an index of conceptual formulations with accompanying catalogues and
ample space for the addition of notations and relational entries. The second
part is performative, and will be videotaped and included in the exhibition
as a
projection. It will spark subsequent annotation to the wall work. Macdonald has
issued a casting call for participants. As the work is modified, it becomes an
experiment in modality: generational, regional and political.

Fiona Macdonald will install the Index project (renovated) from Monday,
February 9 until Friday, February 13. She will be videotaping participants on
Tuesday, afternoon from 11am to 1pm and from 2pm to 4pm. To participate,
please contact Open Space Director Helen Marzolf at director@openspace.ca or
250-383-8833. Participants interested in written contributions can stop by
at any time.

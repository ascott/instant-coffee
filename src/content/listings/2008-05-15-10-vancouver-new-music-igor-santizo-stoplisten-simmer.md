Vancouver New Music
presents:

stop.listen   simmer  
by: igor santizo 


"We have fallen into the place, where everything is music."
_Rumi

"The highest ecstasy is the attention at its fullest."
_Simone Weil.


As part of Vancouver Vibrates festival; 
the Vancouver New Music celebration of the works and influence of R. Murray Schafer, 
Igor Santizo will be launching his latest work, a poster project to be distributed around the city. Stop.

The printed poster still remains one of many quintessential vehicles for the dissemination 
of image, culture & advertising; considering the ubiquity of mass and digital channels. 

Artists have been always interested in using this multiplex avenue, including to address issues that exist often beyond the ethic and functionality of adverts.

Within his multidisciplinary forays, Santizo has inclusively explored sound, via experimental found-percussion music and soundwalks. A soundwalk being a silent group walk led along a planned route to experience a location's ambiance and underlying rhythms. 
 
Furthermore a soundwalk is a fantastic and concise vehicle where: perception and consciousness, become the experiential lens through which the world once heard emerges refreshed. Listen.

This poster gesture is meant to incite listening moments. A simple invitation or reminder to become aware. Wherever it is posted in the city: on bulleting boards, posts, artist studios, quietly in homes, amidst institutional walls, busily in cafés or hidden in unexpected corners; the hope is that its simple message will allow for listening attention to be awakened throughout the city.  Simmer.
...

Igor Santizo is a Vancouver based artist and teacher, his is a practice of experiential discovery and experimental communication. He continues to feel keen about Being, Consciousness and Wholeness. 
 
He recently showed in Lee Henderson's infamous and mobile Attaché gallery. See interview about project at: [http://whitehotmagazine.com/whitehot_articles.cfm?id=1261](http://whitehotmagazine.com/whitehot_articles.cfm?id=1261)

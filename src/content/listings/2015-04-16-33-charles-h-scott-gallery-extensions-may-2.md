EXTENSIONS
Master of Applied Arts Graduate Exhibition

Saturday, May 2 – Sunday, May 17
Opening reception Saturday, May 2, 17:00 - 22:00
Charles H. Scott Gallery
Emily Carr University of Art + Design | 1399 Johnston Street, Vancouver

Emily Carr University presents EXTENSIONS a group exhibition featuring the
works of graduating MAA students. Held in exquisite tension, so as to
consider, in the end, the materiality of which we too are composed, and
through which we live, dream, carry our histories, and make utterances in
the world.

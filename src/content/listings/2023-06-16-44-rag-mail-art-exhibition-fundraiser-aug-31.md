Open Call for Mail Art Exhibition & Fundraiser
Deadline: August 31, 2023

Organized within the framework of the exhibition Sonja Ahlers: Classification Crisis,
this call is open to artists from anywhere in the world (18 years and over) who wish to
contribute one original piece of Mail Art
in response to the theme of collecting and
reassembling.

Mail Art, also known as postal art and correspondence art, is an artistic movement
centered on sending small-scale works through the postal service. This call is inspired
by the work of Sonja Ahlers known for her zines, chapbooks, collages, and mixed
media works. Artists are invited to respond to this theme by collecting and
reassembling their own stories, images, and ephemera into a postcard-sized artwork,
and to mail their postcards to the Richmond Art Gallery.

All artworks received that follow the Guidelines below will be documented and
included in an online exhibition and fundraising auction.
All works received before the postmark deadline of August 31, 2023 will go on display
in the Richmond Art Gallerys Art Lounge during Sonja Ahlers exhibition, from
September 9 to November 5, 2023. (space permitting)
All works will go to online auction after November 5, 2023, and proceeds raised will
be used to support the Gallerys free
public programming.
Any 2-D media accepted, must be original artworks by artists over 18 years of age.
The organizers reserve the right of admission in the event that some of the images
are considered inappropriate (that appeal to intolerance of discrimination or that
contradict the artistic objectives).

Guidelines for Submission to Mail Art Exhibition and Auction Fundraiser:

REQUIRED ARTWORK DIMENSIONS: 13 cm x 17.5 cm (5 in x 7 in)
THEME: Collecting and reassembling.
MATERIALS: Original 2D artwork must be composed of collage and/or mixed media,
including but not limited to painting, drawing, printmaking, fabric, and more.
MAIL TO: Richmond Art Gallery Mail Art Exhibition, 7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond BC,
V6Y 1R9, Canada.
Please write your name, address, e-mail address and Instagram handle or website
(optional) on the back of the postcard so we can use this for gallery labels and to keep
in touch.
CONSENT: By sending in a Mail Art work, the artist consents to having their artworks
sold for the Richmond Art Gallery Association auction.
DEADLINES: Artwork must ARRIVE by August 31, 2023 via the postal service to be
included in our gallery exhibition.
Artwork received after this date, but POSTMARKED by August 31, 2023 will be
included in the online exhibition only.
AUCTION OF ARTWORKS: All works received will start with a minimum bid of $10.

QUESTIONS: For specific submission questions, send an email to the exhibition
coordinator, Kathy Tycholis: ktycholis@richmond.ca

www.richmondartgallery.org

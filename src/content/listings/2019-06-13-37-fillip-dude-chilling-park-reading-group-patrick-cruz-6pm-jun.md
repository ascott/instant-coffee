Reading Group: The Culture Game with Patrick Cruz 
Presented by Fillip

https://www.eventbrite.com/e/reading-group-the-culture-game-w-patrick-cruz-tickets-
63324709912

6PM, June 20, 2019 
Dude Chilling Park (southwest corner)
2315 Brunswick Street Vancouver, Canada

Join Fillip and artist Patrick Cruz on Thursday, June 20, at 6pm at Dude Chilling Park, 
Vancouver, for a a seminar-style discussion on the fraught cultural space non-Western 
artists are often required to inhabit. The conversation will focus on a discussion of Olu 
Oguibes essay Double Dutch and the Culture Game (PDF download at 
https://fillip.ca/events/reading-group-patrick-cruz) which describes a contemporary cultural 
context defined at once by entrenched cultural prejudices and a thin veneer of Western 
liberalism and inclusion. 

Entrance to this event is free but space is limited and an RSVP is required. Beverages 
provided. 

Patrick Cruz is a Filipino-Canadian artist and organizer working between Toronto, Canada 
and Manila, Philippines. Driven by folk sentiment, his multi-disciplinary practice is informed 
by his interest in cultural hybridity, the project of decolonization, and the paradoxical effects 
of globalization. Cruz is the founder of Kamias Special Projects, an artist run space in 
Quezon City, Philippines, that hosts the Kamias Triennial, an educational week-long event 
that serves as a platform for cross-cultural exchange. Solo and group exhibitions include 
those at Forest City Gallery, the 1st Manila Biennale, Oakville Galleries, Plug In ICA, Wil 
Aballe Art Projects, and Centre A, among many others. 

This event will take place at the southwest corner of Dude Chilling Park (a.k.a. Guelph 
Park), Vancouver. In the event of inclement weather, we will alert registered attendees of an 
alternate location the day before the event. Feel free to bring a blanket and a picnic dinner.

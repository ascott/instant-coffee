Summer Professional Development Residency at the NSCCD

The Nova Scotia Centre for Craft and Design is accepting 
applications for Summer Professional Development Residencies in 
the Ceramics, Metal, Wood and Weaving/Textile Studios.

These residencies are open to advanced students and recent 
graduates. They are designed to suit pre-professional and self-
directed artists who need time and space to develop a body of work 
while exploring individual style and technique. Applicants must be 
able to work independently, conduct themselves in a professional 
manner and be comfortable working in a cooperative studio 
environment. Residents will be expected to keep their spaces and 
the studio tidy; as well as treat the facilities and equipment 
respectfully. The studios will be accessible 24 hours a day. There 
will be a group exhibition of Resident’s work held in the Mary E. 
Black Gallery in September.

The cost of the residency is $275 for the summer plus a $50 
damage deposit to be returned upon the completion of the 
residency. The residency will take place from July 1 to August 31. 

For more information please contact the Studio Coordinator at (902) 
492-2524. Application forms are available on our web site www.craft-
design.ns.ca. The deadline for all application forms is Friday April 
29th, 2011 4:00pm.

29. CABARET ZURICH | CALL TO ARTIST/ PERFORMERS/ POETS/ COMEDIANS | MAY 7
CALL TO ARTIST/ PERFORMERS/ POETS/ COMEDIANS
CABARET ZURICH- based on the Dadaist Cabaret Voltaire

How do you express in a time where you are not being given the chance to? 

Dadaist Raoul Hausmann stated " We wanted to change the world but without
any particular idea". The Cabaret Voltaire was initiated by Dada Artist
Hugo Ball. It became a "centre for artist entertainment, where young
artists and poets were invited to bring along their ideas, contributions,
read their poems aloud, hang their pictures, sing and dance, play music".

The Cabaret Zurich invites all creators to collaborate, co-mingle and
create a dialogue with a variety of disciplines around the topic of
censorship ( visual, spoken, written, dance, performance, music). This is
a performance "based" series along with a visual gallery component. The
cabaret and space itself becomes it's own entity and will create it's own
magic with interactive events and good old fashioned madness and expression. 

The Cabaret Zurich is an alternate space for discourse. This playful
evening/ cabaret show will happen May 7th 2011 and will be centred around
the topic of Censorship. 

1. Written word- sound poetry, slam poetry, page poetry, poetic poetry,-
all forms. A total of 6-8 writers- writers will be selected and must be
willing to read their piece and then read a censored version of their
piece. ( Cabaret Zurich has only a few spots left in this category)

2. Performance- all forms of meaningful or absurd performance. A total of
3-6 performers will be selected. Both video and live performance are welcome!

3. Music- Cabaret Zurich has already gotten its 1 main act, but is open to
smaller more performance based acts.

4. Visual Art- a series of artwork will be selected on the topic of
censorship or bending the rules as a concept. Assemblage, collage, text
driven, and pieces that bridge the gap between 2-D and 3-D will be strongly
considered. 

5. Interactive performers staged as Valets and Poppycocks for the evenings
event. These performers need to be able to carry on conversations with
anyone in a playful yet meaningful manner.

For more info contact K.Bourgh at borgiborga@gmail.com and you can read a
more description about the event and requirement for submissions at
www.borgiborga.com/site/news/ 
Deadline for submissions is April 1st.

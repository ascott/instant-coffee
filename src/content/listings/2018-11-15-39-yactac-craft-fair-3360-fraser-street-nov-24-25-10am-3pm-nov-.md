Yactac is proud to partner with John Howard Society again! for their 7th annual craft 
fair this year. It will take place on Saturday Nov. 24th and Sunday Nov. 25th from 
10am-3pm at 3360 Fraser street. A silent auction will also take place with the 
proceeds going to support JHS.

Vendors:
Stitches of Green
Blue Trimble
Jesse Gray's Designs
Jona Sumague's Soup
Purebodycare.ca
Tarot Cards Reading by Britt Ninders
Justine Cheung
Karen & Peggy Ngan
and more

Please contact hello@yactac.com for more info if you are interested in being a 
vendor.

The John Howard Society of the Lower Mainland of BC provides assistance with 
housing, life-skills, and community-based support, with the goal of assisting 
individuals to value their positive contributions to society. Thank you John Howard 
Society for their support.

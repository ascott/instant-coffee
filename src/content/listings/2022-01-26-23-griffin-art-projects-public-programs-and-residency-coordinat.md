Public Programs and Residency Coordinator

Griffin Art Projects is seeking an
innovative and dynamic thinker who can help to build
audiences and excitement through the development and delivery of public programs
and residency programs that capture the publics imagination and expand the
experience of art and art galleries.

Griffin is a non-profit art gallery and residency located in North Vancouver. GRIFFIN is
a non-collecting institution that has quickly become a vibrant contributor to the North
Vancouver cultural landscape.

Griffin is offering a one-year contract that will assist in the coordination of its residency
and public program. The successful applicant will assist in the design of programs that
offer unique opportunities for visitors of all ages and levels of interest to engage with
art and culture at GRIFFIN. This position will assist both with the development of adult
public programming and youth engagement in order to encourage new audiences to
participate and to generate new knowledge and innovative thinking on contemporary
art and culture with
the ambition of creating dynamic learning opportunities for a wide
range of visitors whose familiarity with contemporary art may differ.

This role will engage with best practices in pedagogy and for the impact of learning and
public engagement and offer a unique opportunity to create a meaningful program that
integrates deeper learning and participation, involving high schools, higher education
institutions, the local, national and international arts community, and the general public,
and liaising with artists, thinkers, and innovators from various disciplines to support
GRIFFIN in producing innovative and impactful learning and public engagement.

Reporting to the Director, the Public Programs and Residency Coordinator will produce
a schedule of public programs, events and community outreach activities that animate,
complement, and enhance our exhibitions and residency program, and GRIFFINs
exploration of art collecting and collections practices and methodologies. During the
current
moment, most public programs are delivered digitally via Zoom.

Duties include: assisting with the conceptual development, administration and
implementation of adult and youth focused public programs including: artists talks and
tours, lectures, workshops, film series, symposia and performances. The Public
Programs and Residency Coordinator will also manage and prepare grants, assist with
the development and coordination of special community-based partnership and
education initiatives and related special projects.

It is a one year, part-time two day per week position, with scheduled work from Friday
to Saturday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm. This position includes some evening hours, as
required to fulfill responsibilities and be on hand for gallery programs.

Qualifications

The successful candidate will possess a bachelors degree in art or art history,
museum or curatorial studies, thorough knowledge of Canadian and international art
history and issues in contemporary art; 2 years working
experience in a museum,
public art gallery and/or artist run centre as well as demonstrated experience in
program development and delivery. Exceptional written, presentation and interpersonal
communication skills are required. Experience working successfully with internal and
external partners including artists, educators, curators, producers and academics and
the ability to problem solve and work independently and quickly, and manage the
program on budget, while also keeping statistics, documentation, and monitoring. Must
be able to work with youth as well as adults, with the relevant clearances.

Candidates will have an ability to communicate effectively, both orally and in writing;
strong organizational and administrative skills and the ability to work under general
direction and within a small team environment. Experience developing programs for
and working with youth, families and elementary and post secondary district school
systems highly recommended. Fluency in French is
considered an asset.

About Griffin Art Projects:

Griffin Art Projects is a non-profit art residency and gallery located in North Vancouver
devoted to supporting artists in the production of new work through its residency
program, and in creating new research on contemporary Canadian and international
art, artists and art collections from around the world in its exhibition program. GRIFFIN
is a non-collecting institution that has quickly become a vibrant contributor to the North
Vancouver cultural landscape and visual art practices in the region through its
exhibitions, residency, and public programs. Griffin Art Projects' exhibitions and events
are always free and open to all to attend.

Established in 2015, GRIFFIN was founded by Brigitte and Henning Freybe who began
collecting art in the early 1970s. GRIFFIN considers the methodologies, thematics, and
narratives that shape and direct both visual culture and creative work, and collecting
practices. In addition, GRIFFIN supports and
develops solo and group projects and
thematic exhibitions of works, collaborating with established cultural producers, guest
curators, artists, writers, and art educators in the region, nationally and locally to
produce exhibitions, public programs, and publications on contemporary art in the
region.

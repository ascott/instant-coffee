Burnaby, BC - July 8th , 2017 - Visitors to the Deer Lake Gallery in Burnaby will once
again be dazzled by an event noted for being fun for the entire family! The Summer
Arts Festival is an all-day event on Saturday, July 8th from 10 am to 5 pm. We have
booked the best in local performers and professional artists set against the beautiful
Deer Lake Park. The Festival activities are surrounded by majestic trees and park on
nearby Deer Lake, Burnaby.

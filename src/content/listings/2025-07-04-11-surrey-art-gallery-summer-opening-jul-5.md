Celebrate the Gallerys 50th anniversary series of art parties with our summer
opening of 10 and 10: Story of Stories and Experiments in Solitudes.

10 and 10 pairs permanent collection artworks acquired from the Gallerys first ten
years of collecting (1975"1985) and the last ten years (2014"2024) through themes
of landscape, architecture, portraiture, and movement. Exhibiting artists Lyse
Lemieux and Sara Khan will kick off the event in conversation with Assistant Curator
Jas Lally.

In Experiments in Solitude, longtime friends Brittney Appleby and MV Williams come
together to present works in analogue photography, videography, cyanotypes, and
more. Throughout the evening, visitors can create to a wearable collage inspired by
Williams ideas about home, place, and memory.

In celebration of the recently re-opened saga shop, guests can also join artist Yan
Song for a watercolour technique demonstration and try out watercolour paints.

The evening will also include the ARTS 2025 Peoples Choice award announcement
and short talks with Annie Briard and Sahil Mroke about their UrbanScreen projects.

Afterwards, the celebration continues offsite at UrbanScreen with commissions from
the 5X Festival art program featuring artists Chand Bhangal, Harleen Grewal, Stuti
Gulati, Pavan Sangha, Nishita Malik, Aman Khinda, and Sabid Ali, curated by Sahil
Mroke. Other works that will be shown are Justen LeRoys Lay Me Down in Praise, a
celebration of Black environmentalism, and Annie Briards Refracted Fields, a
shimmering vision of Northwest Coast landscapes.

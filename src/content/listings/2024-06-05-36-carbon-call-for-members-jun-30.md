CARBON Call for Members

Online, across Turtle Island

Deadline: June 30th, 2024 11:59PM

Dates: Meeting once per month, continuous.

Submit your application on google forms, or email carbonartistcollective@gmail.com
to answer any questions.

To all artists, non-artists, and interested parties, we are seeking applications of
interest to CARBON, a Community of Artists Researching the Beinghood of Nature.

Seeking connection? Reach out through a CARBON network of rhizoids, and
mycelium to find this collective. We are seeking you too. Like underground roots,
have you been searching for a familiar entanglement? Maybe you found it, right now!

Our CARBON collective is concerned with and considering, while experimenting with
how to make ethical art in the age of disconnection. Bio-art, land-art,
slime-art, mold-
art, mycelia-art, dirty-art, soiled-art, bugged-art, terraforming-art, interspecies-art,
root-art, and more. This collective is focused on learning, growing, and making
connections with fellow artists, animals, plants, fungi, moss, water, fire, soil, and
other forms of nature.

We want to create a community of artists who come together to learn, facilitate
dialogue, debate, research, and more. The intent of this collective is to explore
ethical practices in art-making while sharing knowledge, opportunities, and inviting
respectful and constructive discussions. We seek to host artist talks, show-and-tells,
roundtables, special interest topics, and research presentations, foster professional
development and mentorships, and encourage collaborative practices between
members. As a new entity, this group will be working together to discover its identity
and purpose as an inter-artistic hub across Turtle Island. We ask that all members
share their ideas, thoughts, artwork,
and foster community through an anti-
oppressive, anti-racist, equity sharing, neurodivergent, social disability, and queer
lens. This communitys organization will stem from decolonial and reciprocal
practices, which will give voice and space to whoever wishes to share, and actively
listen with compassion.

Once a month, we will gather with a goal in mind. However, it is possible we might
end up meeting more, as the collective community will be determining this as well.

***Please note that at this time, CARBON does not have funding and is being solely
organized by founding members Stephanie Florence and Arcana Shanks.***

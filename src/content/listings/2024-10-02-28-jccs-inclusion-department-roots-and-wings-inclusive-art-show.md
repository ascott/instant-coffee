We are excited to announce that the JCC's Inclusion Department is now accepting
applications for our Seventh Annual Inclusion Art Show! This years theme is Roots
and Wings, which invites artists of all abilities and experience levels to showcase
their unique perspectives on how our roots ground us and our wings give us the
courage to move forward and reach new heights.

This is an opportunity for you to express and share your story, your experiences, and
your dreams.

Important details:

Admission is free.
To apply, please complete the Application Form
Application deadline: November 30th, 2024
Exhibition dates: January 30th " March 2nd, 2025
Location: Sidney and Gertrude Zack Gallery @ JCCGV - 950 W 41st Ave
100% of sales will go directly to the artists.
Become part of this inspiring, inclusive exhibition that celebrates the diversity and
creativity of our community. We are excited to see how you interpret Roots and
Wings through your artwork.

Please help us spread the word! If you know artists who may be interested or others
who believe in the power of inclusion and creativity, please share the Call for Artists
attached below. If you have any questions or need assistance, please dont hesitate
to reach out.

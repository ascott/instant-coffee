The On Edge series presents:

Kateri Akiwenzie-Damm

7 pm Thursday, March 3, in the ECU Library

The public is welcome
Kateri Akiwenzie-Damm is an Anishinaabe writer of mixed ancestry
from the Chippewas of Nawash First Nation, Saugeen Ojibway Nation.
She lives and works at in her community at Neyaashiinigmiing on the
Saugeen Peninsula in southwestern Ontario. Her writing has been
published in various anthologies, journals, and magazines in Canada,
the U.S., Aotearoa/New Zealand, Australia, Ireland, and Germany. She
is the author of the book of poetry, my heart is a stray bullet
(Kegedonce Press,1993) and has two CDs of spoken word poetry,
Standing Ground and A Constellation of Bones which was nominated
for a Canadian Aboriginal Music Award. Her new book of short stories,
The Stone Collection was released by Portage and Main Press in
November 2015.

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for the
Arts and the Coast Salish peoples whose traditional unceded territories
we are on.

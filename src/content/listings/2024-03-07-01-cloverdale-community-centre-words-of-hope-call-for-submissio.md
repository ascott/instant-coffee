Unpaid opportunity

Cloverdale Community Centre is looking for visual artists and writers from the local
community to exhibit on our exhibition wall. The theme is What would you say to your
younger self? This is an opportunity to present 2D art including drawings, paintings, short
prose and poetry. This exhibition is for artists at any level of their artistic journey. This call
is open to all ages but we are particularly interested in applicants from older creatives.

Deadline: March 18

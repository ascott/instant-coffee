The Night Swim brings together artists Yoanna Kraleva, Meg Kockx, Ella MacQueen-
Denz, Juliana Sech, and Shae Seqaqkind. Born from discussion about finding a way to

individual studio work of sculpture, painting, drawing, and writing in ongoing dialogue,

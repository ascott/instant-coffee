The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Offsite: Erwin Wurm

Reception: Thursday, September 19, 6 PM to 7:30 PM at 1100 West Georgia Street

This event is free of charge.

The Gallery will host a reception at 1100 West Georgia Street to mark the opening of the
19th installation of its public art series, Offsite: Erwin Wurm, on view September 20, 2019 to
February 23, 2020. The latest Offsite features works by the famed Austrian artist best known
for his One Minute Sculptures. Wurms 2016 works Big Disobedience, Half Big Suit and Flat
Iron demonstrate his manipulation of material and human forms to alter their meaning while
confronting social norms with savvy wit and cynicism. As part of the reception, artist Mike
Bourscheid, who works between Luxembourg and Vancouver, will perform the first of a
series of interventions at Offsite: Erwin Wurm. Bourscheid, who holds a Masters of Fine Arts
from the University of Arts Berlin (UdK) translates his heritage through sculpture,
photography and performance.

For more info, visit: vanartgallery.bc.ca

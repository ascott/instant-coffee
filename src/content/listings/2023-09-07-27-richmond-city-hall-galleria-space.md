DITCH | SEP 19
Final 2 weeks
Ends Sep 19

Artist Julie Pappajohn creates drawings, paintings, mixed media works and narrated
video/slideshow in response to stories she received from those who grew up with
Richmonds exposed drainage ditches. In 2021, Pappajohn put a callout to the Facebook
group, You grew up in Richmond and garnered over 400 replies. Drawing from research
of early Richmond, archival materials and collected stories, DITCH celebrates the
significance of memory and a deeply rooted attachment to time and place. The Tyee staff
reporter, Chris Cheungs July 14th article includes an interview with the artist.
https://thetyee.ca/Culture/2023/07/14/Wild-Childhood-Ditches-Richmond/

28. EDUCATION
| Eastside Atelier | Weaving Workshop | SEP 22
Beginner Rigid Heddle Weaving Workshop

Weave a scarf or two to three towels on a simple rigid heddle loom in this beginner
workshop with teaching artist Amanda Wood. No previous weaving experience needed.

Sept 22 & 23, Friday 6"8pm and Saturday 10am"2
Eastside Atelier, 1310 William Street
More details and register through eventbrite: https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/learn-to-
weave-cloth-for-beginners-tickets-681706140417?aff=oddtdtcreator

ABOUT THE CLASS

Working with a neutral palette of silks, imported cotton and linen in a warm studio
atmosphere you will weave beautiful, textural cloth in a choose your own adventure
style.

The looms will be set up and prepared for you. You can choose to weave a longer scarf
or a collection of 2-3 towels. Amanda will help you choose textures, design ideas and
colour choices to make weaving simple and satisfying.

On Friday evening
we'll begin with an introduction to the loom and materials, and a
conversation about what qualities you enjoy in cloth before getting started with
weaving. You'll be offered design suggestions and material recommendations and be
able to use Amanda's sample library to see how your cloth might turn out.

Saturday will be a dedicated and supportive weaving day. In all you will weave up to 2
yards of cloth which you can divide into 2-3 towels or keep whole as a scarf. Finishing
techniques will be discussed with options for sewn hems and non-sewn hems options.

Amanda's approach to weaving is grounded in technique but invites experimentation
and marks of the maker. This practice based approach is gentle and inclusive of all
levels of experience.

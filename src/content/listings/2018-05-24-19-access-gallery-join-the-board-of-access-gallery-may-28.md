Join the Board of Access Gallery

Application
Interested individuals are asked to submit a CV and letter of intent as a single PDF 
to submissions@accessgallery.ca with subject line BOD 2018 by 9am on Monday 28 
May 2018.

===

Access is currently seeking dedicated and enthusiastic individuals to join the Board 
of Directors. Join this dynamic artist-run centre at an exciting time in its 25+ year 
history!

The Board of Access is comprised of artists and arts workers, who bring a broad 
range of skills and experiences, including design, fundraising, governance, and 
financial literacy, as well as their social and professional networks, and knowledge of 
artistic and curatorial practices.

What you bring

commitment to artist-run values
a keen interest in contemporary art
experience (or willingness to learn) in the following areas:
community fundraising and/or development
non-profit governance
financial management
Commitment
Board members attend monthly meetings and volunteer their time to committees, 
fundraising and attending Access events. Two-year terms are preferred.

Current plans
Presently the Board is working towards the annual fall fundraiser, and supporting the 
new Director/Curator as she develops her first artistic program at Access. The Board 
and staff are committed to improving equity in our organization, and in the next year 
will be undertaking specific learning to support us in this work. We welcome the 
knowledge you contribute, and support you in learning with us.

Application
Interested individuals are asked to submit a CV and letter of intent as a single PDF 
to submissions@accessgallery.ca with subject line BOD 2018 by 9am on Monday 28 
May 2018.

If the application process poses a barrier to you in any way, please reach out to 
Katie directly at k.belcher@accessgallery.ca, by phone 604-689-2907, or in person 
during gallery hours (Tuesday to Friday 12 to 5pm), to express your interest.

Access is committed to an equitable consideration of all prospective board members 
regardless of race, colour, ancestry, place of origin, religion, family status, physical or 
mental disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or age.

*

About Access
Founded in 1991, Access Gallery is an artist-run centre with a mission to create 
conditions of emergence for provocative ideas and work in the visual arts. We 
enable critical conversations and risk taking through new configurations of artists, 
audience, and community. We aim to bring visibility and critical reception to artists 
and cultural practitioners when they need it most, often at moments early in their 
practices, and have offered important exhibition opportunities to many of Canadas 
most recognized contemporary artists.

Access is located on the unceded and ancestral territories of the xmkwy"m 
(Musqueam), Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), and Sl"ilwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) 
Nations.

*

Accessibility notes
There is an 8cm (3.5 in) lip at the entrance to the gallery. We plan to soon have a 
portable ramp for the exterior entrance, and can assist with the 112cm (44 in) sliding 
door. The self-contained gender neutral washroom is not wheelchair accessible. 
There is limited metered street parking and a parking garage on East Georgia.

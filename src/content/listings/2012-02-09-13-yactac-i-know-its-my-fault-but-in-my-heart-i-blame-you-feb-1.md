I Know It's My Fault, But In My Heart I Blame You

Works by Viku Chan

GIRL: are you coming to the opening?
BOY: when is it?
GIRL: Valentine's Day.
BOY: are you inviting me?
GIRL: I'm inviting all my friends.
BOY: am I your friend?
GIRL: I don't know. are you?
... so are you coming?

ARTIST BIOGRAPHY:

Mr. Chan was found at a __________ on Granville early in the morning 
on March 22, 2008, mumbling to himself and grasping his head as 
though he was in pain. He was verbally non-responsive and exhibited a 
decreased level of consciousness, as well as agitation. He did not have 
any neurological deficits on examination. He was not having 
hallucinations or delusions.

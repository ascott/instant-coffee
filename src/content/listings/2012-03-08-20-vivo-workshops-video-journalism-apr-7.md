VIDEO JOURNALISM
2 sessions: 10 hours
Sat April 7 & 14, 12-5 PM

Cost: $200 or $160 with VIVO Producer Membership (Extended)
Instructor: Flick Harrison
To sign up email us at education@vivomediaarts.com or call 
604.872.8337/ext.1

Make docs that ring true and bring the best low-budget production 
values.

Learn on-camera reporting, off-camera interview skills, documentary 
shooting techniques and polished journalistic writing. Participants will 
come up with a documentary concept (or bring one you have in mind) 
and shoot footage that will be analyzed and edited.

Session 1:
Learn how to set up a good interview with lights, camera, and good 
questions, plus documentary shooting and sound skills.

Session 2:
With footage you've shot as homework from session one, learn to cut 
together a short news story with voiceover, on-camera introduction, 
and solid structure.

Prerequisite: Completion of VIVO workshops Camera Lights & Sound 
and Final Cut Pro Intro, or familiarity with the basics of video production 
and post-production.

Workshop leader Flick Harrison was one of Canada's first television 
videographers and has degrees in film production and journalism.

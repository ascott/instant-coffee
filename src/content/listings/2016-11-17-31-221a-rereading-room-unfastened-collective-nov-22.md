Rereading Room & Unfastened Collective presents:
Rhea Tregebov reading from a 1980s copy of "Fireweed" and dialogue
Tuesday, November 22, 2016
7-9pm

Rhea Tregebov is the author of poetry, fiction and children's picture books, and 
has edited a number of anthologies. She is an Associate Professor of Creative 
Writing at the University of British Columbia, Vancouver, Canada, where she 
teaches poetry, children's literature and literary translation. Her work has received 
a number of literary awards, including the J. I. Segal Award for fiction, the Pat 
Lowther Award, the "Prairie Schooner" Readers' Choice Award, and the "Malahat 
Review" Long Poem Award.

Hosted by Unfastened, Tregebov will reflect on her early experiences as a 
feminist and particularly on her work with the Fireweed Collective. "Fireweed: A 
Feminist Quarterly of Writing, Politics, Art & Culture", was founded in Toronto, 
Canada, in 1978 by the Fireweed Collective. Issues of Fireweed have focused 
on issues such as "Fear & Violence" (#14), "Women of Colour" (#16), "Sex & 
Sexuality" (#37 & 38), and "Language" (#44/45).

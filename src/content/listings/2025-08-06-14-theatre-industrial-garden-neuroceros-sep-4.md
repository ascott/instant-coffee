Inspired by Ionescos 1959 absurdist play Rhinocros* and designed to counter raw
nerves rubbed wrongly in our own time, NEUROCEROS! will be a site-specific work
in former brass foundry, warehouse and current music venue Industrial Garden at
236 Clark Avenue.

Sept 4,5,6

Doors and BAR open at 7PM, with performance to start at 8PM.

Cast and Crew:

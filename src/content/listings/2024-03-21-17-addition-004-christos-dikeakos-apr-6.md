CHRISTOS DIKEAKOS

Pictures From Here

Exhibition Opening: Saturday, April 6, 2-4 PM

Location:

ADDITION

Silver Sea, 638 Kinghorne Mews (water side), Vancouver, BC

The work of Christos Dikeakos investigates the archaeological and historical
through
photography. As a first-generation member of the Vancouver Photoconceptualists, he
offers a critique of urban history, topography, cultural aspects of inhabitation and the
city in a state of flux. His sites and Place Names photographs open up discussions of
ideological contexts and track significant cultural shifts. These pictures engage in the
memories and the multi-linguistic narratives of urban typologies in context with
contemporary habitations. Dikeakos produced a touring exhibition for the McMaster
Museum of Art, unpacking a 40-year work of drawings, sculptures and photography on
the French American artist Marcel Duchamp. This was followed by a solo show in 2013
at the Kelowna Art Gallery, Nature Morte, picturing the seasonal paradigms of his
familys apple orchard in the southern Okanagan Valley. His solo show of 2015-16 at
the Vancouver Art Gallery showed a series of large-scale photographs of West Coast
Pacifica engaging in ideas that there exists conductive resonances
in both cultural and
natural spaces. In 2023 he was given an in-depth retrospective exhibition at MOMus-
Museum of Photography of Thessaloniki Greece.

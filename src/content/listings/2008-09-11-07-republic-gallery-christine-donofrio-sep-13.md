Republic Gallery is pleased to present

Christine D'Onofrio | Falling Woman

September 13th through October 18th

opening reception Saturday, September 13th, 2.00pm - 5.00pm

Falling Woman features a new large scale video work, depicting a woman trapped
in a never-ending fall. The fall specifically references a number of art
historical events and works, particularly that of Ana Mendieta whose
site-specific installations and performances attempted to retrieve the nude
body from the masculine eye. In Falling Woman the body continues to be a
battleground of desire and exploitation. The piece plays with the dream of
falling interpreted as a loss of control and the feeling of fear and
humiliation that comes in dreams of being naked. The wind from the fall forces
the protagonist's dress over her head, which simultaneously conceals her
identity and exposes her body, acting as a natural yet exploitative flirtation
with the camera. Autonomy and control clash for the subject has not jumped nor
has she been pushed, and she will never land, instead she is in the midst of a
continuous fall.
Christine D'Onofrio would like to express her gratitude to the Canada Council
for the Arts for their generous support in the research and execution of this
project.

Emily Carr University of Art and Design

"The Subject of Education"

November 7 to November 16, 2008
Opening Reception Thursday November 6 at 6:00pm

Robert Arndt, Kim Kennedy Austin, Jesse Birch, Steven Brekelmans, Aaron
Carpenter, Hadley + Maxwell, Keith Higgins, Kyla Mallett, Sara Mameni,
Gareth Moore, Demian Petryshyn, Kathleen Ritter, Ken Singer, Elizabeth Zvonar

Curated by Jonathan Middleton

To mark the occasion of its new university status, and the appointment of
its founding Chancellor John C. Kerr and President and Vice-Chancellor Dr.
Ron Burnett, Emily Carr University of Art and Design is pleased to present
The Subject of Education, an exhibition that brings together a group of
Emily Carr alumni working along pedagogical themes.

Education in its myriad forms is a subject integral to art practice not
only do artists seek out education to develop their skills and ideas, but
the practice of making art of asking questions, seeking answers,
experimenting, and making decisions can be seen as a practice of both
learning and teaching in itself.

The group of artists in this exhibition brings these concerns to the fore.
While generally sharing an interest in text and performance, elements
arguably intrinsic to education, their works take a broad approach to the
topic, ranging from Hadley + Maxwell's instructions on how to make a video
installation, Sara Mameni's re-staging of hypnotic self-improvement tapes,
Kyla Mallett's photographs of art texts and margin notes, to Kim Kennedy
Austin's embroidered advertisements for how-to booklets on blueprint
reading and electric wiring.

Through their work, the artists in the exhibition complicate the
relationship between institutional and non-institutional forms of education
and of the active participants in, or subjects of, education. Following
with historical examples such as Robert Filliou's Teaching and Learning as
Performing Arts, and Ian Wallace's The Idea of University, in addition to
more recent works such as Kristina Lee Podesvaâ��s Colourschool project, the
works in this exhibition simultaneously critique and extol the educational
process.

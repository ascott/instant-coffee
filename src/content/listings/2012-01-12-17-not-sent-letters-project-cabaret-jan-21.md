Not Sent Letters is an ongoing series of epistolary detours by Jeremy Todd (
an entanglement with the politics of representation, technologies of the self and 
the formation of cultural memory) 
from unceeded Coast Salish territory, Vancouver, BC, Canada. 
Not Sent Letters Project 
began with a blog in 2005. Digital film shorts based on the resulting image/text 
posts (or Not Sent Letters) became another ongoing aspect of the project by 
2008. Not Sent Letter performance works (or Sets) were first realized in 2009, 
integrating image projection, staging, music scores and play with live readings 
of selected Not Sent Letters. Recurring co-operative and non-funded events 
have followed (Not Sent Letters & Guests), presenting the work of others in 
conjunction with Not Sent Letter Sets.

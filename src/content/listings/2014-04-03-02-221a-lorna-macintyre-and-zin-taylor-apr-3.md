Dirt and Not Copper
April 4 - May 10, 2014 
Lorna Macintyre and Zin Taylor 
Curated by Meredith Carr and Jacquelyn Ross 

Saturday, April 5, 2pm 
Artist Talk, Lorna Macintyre and Zin Taylor 

Dirt and Not Copper is an exhibition featuring work by Lorna 
Macintyre 
(Glasgow) and Zin Taylor (Brussels) that explores the 
connections between 
objects, narrative, and methods of storytelling. Reflecting on the 
aestheticised, and often ironic role that objects play in the 
building of narrative 
within an exhibition, the project examines the ways in which 
narrative is 
obscured through the process of making.

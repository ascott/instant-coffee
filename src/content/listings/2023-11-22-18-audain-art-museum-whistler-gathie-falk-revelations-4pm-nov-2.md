Gathie Falk: Revelations
Curatorial Tour
Saturday, November 25
4pm - 5pm | Registration required

Gathie Falk: Revelations, organized and circulated by the McMichael
Canadian Art
Collection, investigates the career of a legendary Canadian artist. With more than 90
works on display, this exhibition underlines the incredible range of Falk's creative
output in ceramic and bronze sculpture, acrylic and oil on canvas painting,
photography and video, as well as multi-media installation.

Curated by Sarah Milroy, Executive Director and Chief Curator of the McMichael
Canadian Art Collection, Revelations is drawn from public, corporate and private
collections across Canada, in addition to being accompanied by a full-colour 200-
page book with contributions by Sarah Milroy, Daina Augaitis, Hank Bull, Liz Magor,
Landon Mackenzie, John Geoghegan, Jocelyn Anderson and Nancy Tousley.

Join Sarah Milroy, Executive Director and Chief Curator of the McMichael Canadian
Art Collection, for a tour of Gathie Falk: Revelations.

This curatorial tour is open to the public, and is included with the cost of admission

Gallery FUKAI is pleased to announce Salutes: New Work by Liam 
Hogan.

Opening this Friday December 2 8pm and closing on the 31st.

Liam Hogan's new exhibition, Salutes, examines relationships between 
physical gesture and youthful disobedience. Like the candid snapshot 
images that they are based on, the artist’s collages feel spontaneous 
yet rich in detail and are meticulously planned. All delicately and 
elegantly constructed of paper, the images show various physical 
gestures intended to offend, enrage and intimidate but are sarcastically 
referred to as salutes in a nod to the F.T.W. spirit and do-it-yourself 
mantras of the outsider cultures often referenced in his art. Presented 
in Hogan’s latest work is an assorted community of characters from 
different subcultures including bikers, flashers, freedom fighters, and 
metal heads in scenes of rebellion and barely repressed violence 
drawn from his own teen years.

Liam Hogan is an artist living and working in Vancouver. His work has 
been exhibited in North America and Europe, most recently in Toronto 
and Paris. He is the owner of State Creative Group design studio and 
co-founder, with Kevin Romaniuk, of The Acid Sweat Lodge, a unique 
Vancouver based think-tank. The work of the Acid Sweat Lodge has 
been critically acclaimed with continuing mentions by several leading 
websites, magazines, and agencies, notably being cited by GQ 
magazine France as one of the internets “20 influential blogs.” Most 
recently, A.S.L. has been cited in publications such as Harpers Bazaar, 
Color Magazine, and The Reference Council among numerous others. 
In 2010, The Acid Sweat Lodge collaborated with Paris based Art 
Direction Agency and Fashion House “Surface To Air” to create an 
exhibition that launched their flagship boutique in Paris.

For additional work by Liam Hogan please refer to his website, 
www.liamhoganwork.com

Gallery FUKAI is located at 602 Hastings St E @ Princess. The artist 
will be in attendance.

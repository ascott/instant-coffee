Change without Notice
Susan Stewart & Dorothy Seaton

November 28, 2008 - January 25 , 2009
Opening: Friday November 28 at 7:00pm
Artist Talk: Saturday, January 17 at 2pm

Film and video technology have been used above all to promote conventional
fantasies of sexual, economic and social achievement. Through a set of
overlapping video projections at the Richmond Art Gallery, Susan Stewart
and Dorothy Seaton will search to propose new modes of thought and to
project alternative futures alternative fantasies - in response to
current social and environmental crisis. Given our participation in
multiple biosystems and levels of interdependence, the artists working
assumption for "Change Without Notice" is that to radically shift the terms
of sets of relations here, the ecology of gender in particular is to
evoke change on many other levels of social and environmental
interconnectedness. The layering and interaction of multiple projections
will transform the Gallery into a kind of lever for change a place where
new fantasies, difficult and joyous alike, challenge current modes of relation.

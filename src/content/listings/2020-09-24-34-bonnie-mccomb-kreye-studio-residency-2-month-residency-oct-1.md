The Bonnie McComb Kreye Studio Residency, which was established in 2016 in
Victoria , BC, by the Kreye family, has partnered with the Vancouver Island
School of Art and is supported by Victoria Arts Council Gallery, Fifty-Fifty Arts Collective, and
the Ministry of Casual Living.
This year artists will have the use of a private 30
sq. m. garden studio
for a residency of 2 months (studio only).The residency is open to artists working in all
media.

Talk | Tai Smith: Dissecting Textiles

Saturday, January 20, 3pm, free

Join us for an informal seminar on the gallery floor by Tai Smith, Associate Professor in 
the Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory at UBC, where she will address a 
history of textiles as objects of material and metaphorical dissection.

Author of Bauhaus Weaving Theory: From Feminine Craft to Mode of Design (2014), 
Smiths work looks at questions of mediation in art, design, and political economy. She 
has published in Art Bulletin, Art Journal, Grey Room, Texte zur Kunst, Zeitschrift fr 
Medien- und Kulturforschung, among other venues. She is currently completing a book 
manuscript titled Fashion After Capital: Frock Coats and Philosophy From Marx to 
Duchamp.

Contemporary Art Gallery
555 Nelson Street, Vancouver

Tim Lilburn and Daphne Marlatt
Reading and Book Launch
Thursday, April 17, 7:30pm 

Please join us for the launch of Orphic Politics and The Given. Tim Lilburn and Daphane Marlatt will be reading from their latest books published by McClelland & Stewart.

Tim Lilburn's Orphic Politics is preoccupied with illness and citizenship in the country of the sick. The poems are incantatory and in a way endless. This is his seventh book of poetry. The last one, Kill-site, won the Governor-General's Award for Poetry.

The Given by Daphane Marlatt is a multi-layered long poem which reads with all the urgency and depth of a novel. Set in a present-day East End neighbourhood of Vancouver and in a North Vancouver neighbourhood of the 1950s, it begins with the news of a mother's death then opens up to become an intricate tapestry of lives as Marlatt interweaves the past with the present, replicating the arc of memory while questioning the meanings of home and identity.

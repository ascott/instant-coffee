THE LOVE LETTER SHOW
OPENING THURSDAY FEBRUARY 12TH, 8PM - MIDNIGHT
The Love Letters Show came to me during my most recent move to a new
apartment. I found a shoe box full of love letters. They were
enthralling.The show has developed into a mix of submitted old found
letters and newly written ones. We've made these into posters, cards and
all sorts of Valentines day goodies.
The Love Letters Show is a joint collaboration with Queen & Shaw project
gallery Toronto.

PREMIERE OF THE NEW CIRCLESQUARE VIDEO: DANCERS

From the new Album: Songs About Dancing and Drugs

Vancouver born, based in Berlin, Circlesquare have returned with their
sophomore full-length Songs About Dancing And Drugs, on Boompa Records via
!K7. Formed in 1997 by Jeremy Shaw, Circlesquare focused on integrating
electronic beats, powerful basslines and rich, sensuous vocals. Shaw has
been featured internationally in The New York Times, Vogue, The Face, Dazed
and Confused, Jockey Slut, Musik and Sleazenation, his music has been heard
on "CSI:Miami", "Queer as Folk", and EA's video game, "The Need For Speed".
Songs About Dancing And Drugs pulsates, reverberates. It is an album for
late nights and long drives and breakups and lovemaking and cold mornings
and matted-velvet comedowns. Best heard from the floor. Come hear the
goodness on Thursday. (yes copies of the album will be available)

The video 'Dancers' features outstanding performances by Jeremy Shaw and
dancers Julie Chapple, Allison Denham, Miles Faber & Vincent Noiseux of So
You Think You Can Dance Canada.Yay!

The Ferry Building Gallery is proud to announce the first exhibition of the year, Staying
Curious, opening on Thursday, January 11, 6"8 p.m. Featuring the creative output of 12
West Vancouver art instructors, Staying Curious reflects on each artists unique approach
to making art. Participating artists: Leila Afshari, Celan Bouillet, Mary Hay, Tracey Hayes,
Sonia Mocnik, Jennifer Pickering, Marina Ross, Nazanin
Sadeghi, Heidi Seeto, Mariana
Sola, Javid Tabatabaei, Helen Weiser.

For the duration of the show, instructors will be running creative taster workshops. For
more information, please go to https://ferrybuildinggallery.ca/exhibitions/staying-curious

If you require any further information, please dont hesitate to reach out. And Happy New
Year!

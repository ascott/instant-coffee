Field Contemporary
17 West Broadway
Vancouver, BC

It is with a heavy heart we bid adieu to our lovely little spot at 17 West Broadway. I would 
like to extend my deepest gratitude to the community that has continued to support the 
gallery over the last nearly 6 years! A special thank you to those who have supported the 
artists exhibited on or between these walls, Brandon Cotter and Avalon Mott for beginning 
this special project with me in 2013, my family, all of you who have attended the openings 
and showed thoughtful interest in the exhibitions, and above all, the 100 some-odd artists 
and collaborators that I have had the pleasure to 
work with over the years. It has been a real honor. 

Due to recent developments into the future of the 92 year old building, I will be closing the 
doors to this rendition of Field Contemporary at the end of July 2019 to focus on new 
projects elsewhere.

Please come by for one last time, have a drink, tell stories, and show Steve Hubert's 
exhibition and 17 West Broadway a good send off.

From the bottom of my heart.

My very best and thank you,

Daniel Jefferies
Director Field Contemporary

On Saturday July 27th the last regular hours will be from 2:00-6:00PM with an interlude until 
the reception at 7:00PM. I look forward to seeing you all!

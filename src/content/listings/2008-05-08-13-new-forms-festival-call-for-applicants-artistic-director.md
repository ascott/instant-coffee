The New Forms Media Society is sending a callout for the position of Artistic Director.  The successful application will have the opportunity to work with an engaging team of curators and staff around the conception and artistic direction of The New Forms Festival.  The New Forms Festival is the largest Media Arts Festival on its kind in Western Canada focusing on a mix of genres around Media Arts, New and Electronic Music, Internet and Mobile Technologies and Film.  The festival is looking for applicants looking to come with an energetic and interesting approach to the festival, continuing the boundary shifting works NF has become known for.
 
About the position
 
 This position is a part-time, year round position.  The role of the Artistic Director includes the following: 
 
-Responsible for working with New Forms Curators on the Artistic Direction of the New Forms Media Society and the New Forms Festival.  Works with Programming Committee to develop programming for the festival and for ongoing programming during the year. 
 
-Works with the Operations Director on Communication between the Programming Director and all Curators involved in New Forms is crucial, and includes correspondence both online and in person with all curators during the course of the year.  
 
-Working on the Annual Theme for the New Forms Festival
 
-Responsible for budget review and programming support needs (in Coordination with the Operations and Executive Director) Contracts with Artists, etc.
 
-Responsible to help assemble the programming content around the festival for grants related to programming assistance funding.  (Done in conjunction with curators)
 
-To research and make partnerships, community collaborations and co-presentations for the purposes of assisting the development of future programming (Exhibitions, Education, Conferences, Residencies, etc).	This may require attending other events, travel, and public presentations and talks about NFF.
 
-Will work with the Outreach and Grants Coordinator to develop a strong profile of organization at public events that require a representative from organization.
 
For this application we are asking that all candidates submit a 1-3  page brief vision concerning different ideas that they would want	bring to New Forms.  All briefs should include its relevance to  NF’s mandate and mission.
 
CV’s and any other materials felt beneficial to the application are also accepted.  All applicants must have prior experience in Festival Event planning, show a developed understanding of Media Arts and Electronic Music, and have experience and commitment in the non-profit sector.
 
Please send all inquiries and submissions to executive@newformsfestival.com.

Call for Artistic Director

The Steveston Salmon Festival is seeking an Artistic Director to work with the
Steveston Salmon Festival Committee to develop

a creative vision and
programming to celebrate the 75th anniversary of the Steveston
Salmon Festival.

Project Budget: $20,000 CAD

Eligibility: Local event professionals/creatives from the Lower Mainland with experience
in leading creative visioning and artistic direction for local events/festivals.

Submission Deadline: March 31, 2022 at 5:00p.m. PST

Completion: July 1, 2022

About the Steveston Salmon Festival

The Steveston Salmon Festival has long been Richmonds marquee Canada Day
celebration. Residents have come together every

year since 1945 to plan a community event to celebrate Canadas birthday and the rich
heritage of the community. Prior to the

COVID-19 pandemic, the festival was famous for its salmon barbecue where over
1,200 pounds of wild salmon filets are grilled

over open fire pits. Attracting 60,000- 80,000 people, the festival featured multiple
stages with over 40 performances, an expansive

community parade, a big fireworks finale and many traditional activity zones
representing the
pioneering spirit of Steveston,

the importance of the fishing industry and the value of community connections to keep
visitors of all ages entertained.

Given ongoing uncertainties around the pandemic, planning for this years re-imagined
Steveston Salmon Festival includes

the following activities taking place over a two-week span leading up to a community-
wide celebration on July 1 that is

spread out geographically across Steveston Village to minimize crowding:

June 19: Bullhead Derby at Garry Point Park

June 25-26: Kite Festival at Garry Point Park

June 30: Drive thru Salmon Sale

July 1:

Programming and site dcor to celebrate the 75th anniversary of the Steveston
Salmon Festival;

The Steveston Salmon Festival Parade, re-imagined as a reverse walk- or bike-
through parade with

approx. 40 parade stations;

A welcome ceremony including including First Nations and local, federal and
provincial government;

Traditional programming such as the Japanese Cultural Show; Sammys Fun
Zone
(childrens programming);

Horticulture Show; Art Show and Car Show with pre-registration and ticketing
contingency

plans in place;

Entertainment featuring local artists on a community stage, as well as pop up
performances (estimated

at 30-40 performance slots) around the Steveston Community Centre and park site
and

through Steveston Village; and

Complementary online and/or City-wide Canada Day engagement opportunities.

The event is produced by a Steveston Salmon Festival Planning Committee comprised
of City staff and volunteers from the

Steveston Community Society/ Richmond Agricultural and Industrial Society.

Artist Opportunity

The Steveston Salmon Festival is seeking a creative Artistic Director with experience
in: festival design and production; community/

stakeholder engagement and visioning; community-engaged artistic programming; site
animation and design; and

development of a festival visual identity. Candidates will be selected based on
experience, skill and
diversity.

Submission Requirements

Email a proposal as one PDF to: llegear@richmond.ca

Proposal should include:

a list of examples of past projects of similar nature, and for each past project include
references

including contact person information;

a list of key individuals and a list of subcontractors, consultants and subconsultants if
any that are

intended to be used to support this project, together with a brief description of the
qualifications,

relevant past experience and anticipated role for each such person or firm;

a proposal that includes a description of the proposed approach to planning and
implementing the

project deliverables.

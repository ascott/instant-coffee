The On Edge Readings Series presents:

Oct 20 Wayde Compton & Rahat Kurd 

at 7 pm on Thursdays
in the Library at Emily Carr University of Art & Design
1399 Johnston St.
Granville Island, Vancouver
unceded Coast Salish territories

The public is welcome.

Author bios:
Wayde Compton's books include The Outer Harbour (Arsenal Pulp 
2014, winner of the City of Vancouver Book Award), After Canaan: 
Essays on Race, Writing, and Region (Arsenal Pulp 2011), 
Performance Bond (Arsenal Pulp 2004), and 49th Parallel Psalm 
(Arsenal Pulp 1999). He edited The Revolving City: 51 Poems and 
the Stories Behind Them (with Rene Sarojini Saklikar) and 
Bluesprint: Black British Columbian Literature and Orature. He co-
founded the Hogan's Alley Memorial Project, dedicated to 
preserving the public memory of Vancouver's black community. He 
is the associate director of Creative Writing in Continuing Studies at 
SFU, where he administrates the Writer's Studio. 

Rahat Kurd is the author of Cosmophilia, a collection of poems 
published by Talonbooks in 2015. Her poetry sequence "Seven 
Stones for Jamarat", published in Exile Literary Quarterly, is 
nominated for a Pushcart Prize, and was a finalist in the 2014 
Gwendolyn MacEwen Poetry Prize. Rahat was selected as 
Emerging Artist in the Literary Arts category of the 2013 Vancouver 
Mayor's Arts Awards. A prose writer whose essays have appeared 
in The Walrus and Maisonneuve magazines, she is at work on a 
memoir about the making of Muslim culture in North America. 

We gratefully acknowledge the support of the Canada Council for 
the Arts.

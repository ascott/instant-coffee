Vancouver International Film Festival (VIFF) announces its 39th edition, on screens
September 24-October 7, 2020. Showcasing more than
100 feature films and events,
including boundary-pushing Canadian work, adventurous East Asian cinema, inspiring
documentaries, elevated genre cinema, and festival favourites, the online-primary festival
is available throughout the province of British Columbia on the new VIFF Connect
streaming platform.

VIFF 2020 will also feature a select number of in-cinema
presentations, including the opening film and world premiere of Loretta Sarah Todds
Monkey Beach. The adaptation of Eden Robinsons beloved novel will be screened at
several independent cinemas throughout the province in compliance with COVID-19
capacity restrictions and the latest provincial health and safety regulations. To explore
VIFFs 2020 programming, and to purchase Single Tickets and Subscriptions, visit: viff.org

Danielle Bobier
Transmissions
June 10 to August 23, 2024
Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery is pleased to present Transmissions,
by Danielle Bobier.

Guided by the horizon line"a threshold between the known and unknown" the
heart of Transmissions resides in the contemplation of the notion of heaven. Created
over a period of five years, the paintings in this exhibition began as an exercise in
refuge from
existential anxieties. Drawing inspiration from my upbringing on the
West Coast, this body of work has become a means of exploration into the climate
and tectonics of my inner world. The recurring house-shaped icon becomes an
otherworldly messenger, embodying the concept of numen, a divine presence that
presides over a place. The artworks honour the cyclical nature of existence, shifting
between creation and erosion, comfort and unease, and invite the question of what
may lie beyond our own horizons.

Danielle Bobier is a Vancouver-based artist who finds influence in the forces of
nature and the ever-changing atmosphere of the internal experience. Through the
actions of observation and transformation, her practice filters both inner and outer
landscapes through an abstracted and symbolic lens. Working primarily as a painter,
she fosters an interest in the perceptual and emotive effects of colour as a vehicle
for meditation.

Bobier's work will be featured in the Showroom March 25 to
June 7, 2024

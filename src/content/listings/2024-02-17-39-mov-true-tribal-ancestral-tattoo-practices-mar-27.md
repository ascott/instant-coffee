True Tribal: Contemporary Expressions of Ancestral Tattoo Practices

Opens at the Museum of Vancouver March 28, 2024

Embodied Mark Making a Reclamation of Indigenous Culture

True Tribal: Contemporary Expressions of Ancestral Tattoo Practices explores 30-
plus years of Indigenous tattooing from around the world and the artists who are
reconnecting with traditional skin marking practices. The revival of ancestral tattoo
designs and motifs, the re-envisioning of meaning and protocols, and the re-
fashioning of ancestral application methods is part of Indigenous peoples efforts to
reclaim their lands, cultures and identities.

Tattoo artists Tristen Jenni Sanderson (Woodland and Plains Cree), Terje
Koloamatangi (Tongan), Nolan Malbeuf (Mtis), Mo-Naga (Uipo Naga), Julie Paama-
Pengelly (M-ori), Gordon Sparks (Mikmaq), Nathalie Standingcloud (Cherokee) and
Dion Kaszas (Nlakapamux) use contemporary technologies to build upon ancestral
design conventions as seen on ancestral belongings. Their work gives power back to
Indigenous people to think through and theorize their own world and life views while
taking away the colonial tools that separated them from the visual language of skin
markings.

Portraits of people with the artists tattoos will be exhibited alongside cultural
belongings that act as a visual dictionary for the markings, as well as projections,
blood impression paintings and augmented reality (AR). The AR app will allow
visitors to view tattoo designs created by the True Tribal artists expanded in the
exhibition space with the use of their own devices.

This exhibition seeks to enliven our communitys relationship to their ancestral
visual languages, which are written on the rocks, in our families homes or on
ancestral belongings in museum
collections. "Dion Kaszas, Co-Curator and Artist

True Tribal is co-curated by artist and podcaster Dion Kaszas and curator Mireille
Bourgeois, in collaboration with the curatorial team at the Museum of Vancouver and
its collection of cultural belongings. The project is co-managed by IOTA Institute, a
Nova Scotia based art collective and creative agency. True Tribal will be on display
at the Museum of Vancouver starting March 28, 2024 with an opening reception on
March 27th.

Friday August 28th, Dusk

A SLIDE SHOW

McLean Park, in Strathcona, corner of Keefer and Hawks

To mark the end of David Wisdom’s Teck Gallery exhibition of photographs of Vancouver 1970-
1975, the

SFU Gallery presents a slide projection event that captures the spirit of the legendary 1970s 
Point Grey

slide festivals, with slide shows by some of our best photographers/artists.

David Wisdom will show his best Vancouver pictures taken over the last 40 years

One of our most inspirational and influential artists, Ian Wallace will show something revealing

Legendary lensman James O’Mara, now based in old Europe will get soulful and sublime

Video/photo artist and musician Carol Sawyer & photographer Michael O’Connell will show

gems from the past, their past and yours

Architect/artist Robert Kleyn will show portraits of weathermen and women from then and now

Writer and all-round arts guy Michael Turner zips up Kingsway

Industrial photographer Stuart McCall will present something big

Painter/raconteur Neil Wedman will show 5 movies in 8 minutes

Our city’s most important photo/documenter, Fred Herzog, will present an epic cityscape from 
1982

Please bring something low to sit on, like a blanket or cushion. Folding chairs may be at the rear.

The event is WEATHER DEPENDENT. If it rains, no show.

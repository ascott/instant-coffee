Burnaby Arts Council & Opus Art Supplies are pleased to present the 5th Annual 
Summer Arts Festival taking place July 5th & 6th at the Deer Lake Gallery

Celebrate the start of summer with a fun family-friendly event including the popular 
Parks Edge Paint Off!

Burnaby, BC " July 5th & 6th, 2019" Visitors to the Deer Lake Gallery in Burnaby will 
once again be dazzled by an event noted for being fun for the entire family! The 
Summer Arts Festival is a 2-day event on Friday, July 5th from 6 pm to 9:30 pm & 
Saturday, July 6th from 10 am to 5 pm. Set against the beautiful Deer Lake Park, its a 
chance to experience the best of Burnabys art scene.

We are stoked about this years 2 day Summer event!, says local artist and Burnaby 
Arts Council Executive Director Teena Martin, This year we are kicking it up a notch on 
Friday with live entertainment at the Night Market plus action packed day Saturday with 
presenting sponsor Opus, Parks Edge, live artist demonstrations & entertainment. A 
chance to really experience the diverse talent of our local arts community.

Festival highlights:

Artisan Market featuring BCs finest artists and designers displaying a variety of 
hand crafted items including: Nasty Free Natural, SaGa Stone, Burnaby Artists Guild, 
and Linda Designs just to name a few.

Exhibition Opening of Redefining featuring Kelly Cannell from the Musqueam 
Nation in British Columbia. Kelly was shown Coast Salish Art and culture from birth; she 
lives it every day among her community and with her family, including her two children. 
In each and every piece, a story is told or a message is passed down; sometimes 
personal, sometimes apparent, though keeping true to Coast Salish traditions

Coast Salish Welcome & Introduction with Alex Dan of the Musqueam & the 
Coastal Wolf Pack. Tsatsu Stalqayu translated into English means, Coastal Wolf Pack is 
a traditional Salish song and dance group. The members of the group representing the 
following communities: Musqueam, Skway, Sto:lo, Lil Wat, Squamish, Tsartlip, 
Cowichan, Punalikit, Nanaimo and many more

Traditional Coast Salish Cedar Weaving with Kwantlen Elder Hazel. Kwantlen 
Elder, Hazel (Fillardeau) Gludo has been weaving cedar for a number of years. Hazel 
will teach and talk about how important it is in our lives and how years ago the people 
used it, and how we have great respect for our cedar tree and we too need to learn 
respect our trees that are around us, for the air, and how beautiful it is that we have 
trees.

Brandon Gabriel will be among one of the artisans selling their work. Brandon is a 
multi-talented contemporary mixed media artist, who was born and raised on the 
Kwantlen First Nation Reserve in Fort Langley BC. Brandon was educated in Cultural 
Anthropology, Visual Art, and Marketing at Kwantlen Polytechnic University, then 
received his Bachelors Degree in Visual Art from the Emily Carr University of Fine Art 
and Design.

Artist Demonstration with Peter Lattey. Peter developed a love for wood and 
natural forms at an early age. Latteys work can best be described as Jazz music carved 
into wood. The forms and shapes flow sensuously. The mystery of that emotional or 
intellectual connection is what Peter strives to achieve.

Creative Station hosted by our presenting sponsor, Opus Art Supplies. Explore 
new things " experiment, get hands-on, try something new, and stock up on summer 
memories of creating in the park! Stop by the Opus tent to experience the magic of 
marbling, where you will create swirls of glorious colour and lift it onto a paper surface. 
This ancient art technique involves floating paints on the surface of a gel-like medium 
(known as the marbling size) and then transferring the floating design onto paper or 
fabric. In addition to marbling, festival participants are invited to collaborate on a large 
scale painting with POSCA Acrylic Paint Markers. From doodles to details, express 
yourself and make your mark on a communal painting! Opus will supply the surface and 
paint " bring your creative ideas to add to the artwork and help make a community 
masterpiece.

Parks Edge Paint Off, the festivals annual live painting competition featuring 20 
local artists. Come and see how paintings are created and vote for your favourite! 
Audience vote determines the winner and all paintings will be available via silent 
auction. 50% of the proceeds go the artist the other 50% towards the BAC community 
engage event & activities.

Craft for kids includes Mandala Dotting Demo's and some free hands on dotting 
hosted by local Burnaby business Muckabout Gift Gallery: Mandala Dotting Tools is for 
those crafty people both beginners, and those with no art skills, to create PERFECT 
circles in different sizes with no brush, paint skills or experience needed.

Live Entertainment including the Burnaby Summer Theatre dont miss their live 
performance of a la commedia, an original play written, directed by performing art 
students from SFU. Music from the amazing Rocket Fuel Band, Eddie Lam, Smile 
Band, and Carolyn Neapole, and Latin American rhythm featuring Breaking Boundaries 
Band Music. Art Exhibition: Redefining featuring Kelly Cannell & Masks from My Mind 
featuring Peter Lattey!

AcroFire will be putting on an epic show. Lukas Knab is a contemporary circus 
performer and object manipulator specializing in a variety of different juggling and flow 
arts. He will be presenting one of his favorite modalities in the form of contact juggling, 
which is a mesmerizing art using a single crystal ball which is sure to trick the viewers 
senses! AcroFire Entertainment here in Vancouver which specializes in epic fire 
performances and many other types of creative circus entertainment.

Beer Garden in the gallerys backyard featuring local Burnaby brewery Dageraad.

Silent Auction of the painting from the Paint Off " a chance to take some of the 
magic home!

The Summer Art Festival is organized by the Burnaby Arts Council and takes place at 
the Deer Lake Gallery, located at 6584 Deer Lake Avenue in Burnaby.

About the Burnaby Arts Council

The Burnaby Arts Council (BAC) was established in 1967 to fund raise for a Burnaby 
based public art gallery. In its 45+ years, the BAC has continued with its mandate of 
supporting, promoting and expanding the arts throughout Burnaby. Burnaby Arts Council 
has evolved to become a dynamic community orientated cultural organization with many 
successful arts programs and projects to its credit. The objectives of the organization 
are to serve our membership and to support, enhance and expand the arts in the City of 
Burnaby.

For more information, visit: www.burnabyartscouncil.org 

About Our Presenting Sponsor Opus:

Opus Art Supplies has been a quality supplier of fine art materials and picture frames for 
the visual arts community in BC for the past 45 years. From humble roots as a 
wholesale framing company in Vancouver, Opus has grown to include 7 retail locations, 
a national mail order service, and an online store. Opus is committed to being a 
resource for anyone with a creative spark through an excellent selection of art supplies, 
artist workshops and free demonstrations, and involvement in community events and 
initiatives.

Nhan Duc Nguyen Residency

Lao Oi, Lao A… (O Ancient One…) 

April 1 - April 30

To most Vietnamese, to eat is to celebrate.  It is no coincidence then that the common greeting of "Hi, how are you?" can be translated to "Have you eaten rice?"  Similarly, the literal translation of 'celebrating the New Year' is 'to eat New Year'. 


In his current residency at Open Space, Vancouver artist Nhan Duc Nguyen takes the ability of food to adapt, transform, and often become hallmarks of culture (much like the import of chilli to Southeast Asia) as well as the ideas of nationhood and citizenship to create the installation Lao Oi… ('O Ancient one…').

Lao Oi… will coincide with Citizenship Week in October 2008 as part of British Columbia's 150 Year Celebration.  Nguyen will create a Vietnamese shrine, dedicated to Lao, as well as develop a petition calling for Harmony.  It is a Vietamese tradition for organizations and individuals to erect these shrines in public spaces during governmental events or civic holidays.  They are a way for citizens to ask for resolution and harmony pertaining to the health and welfare of the state and its citizens.  Nguyen explains; "One's health is beyond Lao's influence but one can ask for better healthcare. Lao is not able to help you in matters of love but if it is a matter of marriage—put it on the petition."

For shrine submission information please see [http://www.openspace.ca/web/item/343](http://www.openspace.ca/web/item/343)

Employment Opportunity: Technician

OPEN SPACE, a public exhibition and performance space for contemporary art, is seeking a part time Technician. The Technician's duties include technical preparation and set up for visual art exhibitions, performances and new music concerts and other cultural activities. The Technician is responsible for maintenance of the facility and equipment; and supervises volunteers and casual staff. The Technician also assists in planning, documents events and exhibitions, and arranges shipping.

Qualifications:
Knowledge: Ability to grasp installation and performance requirements of contemporary art and music practices.	(A BFA is an asset.) The Technician is capable and experienced with safe and effective building practices, power tools, and is able to perform heavy physical work. The Technician understands art gallery and basic theatrical /performance lighting. Familiarity with audio and sound equipment is important. Open Space hires contract sound technicians for complex music performances.

Skill and Aptitude: The Technician must possess excellent communication skills; strong problem-solving capability; sensitivity to the unique and sometimes unexpected needs of contemporary art and music; pragmatic organizational skill and ability to thrive in a deadline-driven environment. Demonstrable skill in the documentation of contemporary art with photography, video and audio equipment and basic knowledge of business software and computer hardware are required.

The Technician must be capable of arranging for ongoing cleaning, maintenance and repair of the building. Occasionally the Technician must obtain estimates for complex repairs and assist the Executive Director in finding capable contractors.

The Technician's hours are variable, averaging approximately 25/hrs/wk @$15.00/hr. For detailed information visit the Open Space website www.openspace.ca.

THE EYES HAVE WALLS: NICOLE ONDRE & MINA TOTINO

Join us for our new exhibition, The Eyes Have Walls: Nicole Ondre & Mina Totino. This
exhibition features paintings and ceramic works by Vancouver-based artists Nicole
Ondre and Mina Totino.

Ondre and Totino challenge the mechanical processes of their materials and, through
their experimentations, cause their artistic media to elude fixed forms.

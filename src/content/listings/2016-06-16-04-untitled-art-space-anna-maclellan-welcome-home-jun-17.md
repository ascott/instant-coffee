Welcome Home
Anna MacLellan
Untitled Art Space 
436 Columbia Street
June 17th, 2016 / 8pm-late

We're excited to announce Welcome Home, a solo show by Anna 
MacLellan where the gallery space will be transformed into a series of 
unexpected domestic environments. Through assemblage, installation 
and mixed media, the artist explores ideas of domesticity and intimacy 
and how these are shaped by the items we surround ourselves with. 
Anna's unusual yet appropriate arrangements aim to create questions 
about home and how the feeling is created. The viewers are 
encouraged to interact with each element of the show in order to be 
immersed in Anna's intriguing and welcoming world.

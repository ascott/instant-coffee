The 12th Annual Oppenheimer Park Community Art Show | The World As We Create It

September 13 " October 26, 2019
Opening Reception: Friday, September 13, 2019 " 6pm-9pm

Opening Day Parade: Friday, September 13, 5pm
Join the parade with exhibiting artists and Carnegie Centres Artists-in-Residence, Amal 
Rana and Erv Newcombe. Meet outside Carnegie Centre, 401 Main St.

From September 13 to October 26, 2019, Oppenheimer Park and Gallery Gachet are 
pleased to present The World As We Create It. Exploring the theme of humans and the 
natural world, the exhibition showcases artwork from the Oppenheimer Park community, 
including paintings, drawings, print, sculpture, mosaic and mixed media works.

Oppenheimer Park has a rich and colourful history as a place of recreation, celebration, 
activism, resistance, culture, healing, community and belonging. It is also one of few 
green spaces in the Downtown Eastside and one of the oldest surviving parks in the city. 
This years exhibition reflects on our interactions with history, nature, place and space 
and how these narratives inform our worlds as we create them today. More than
thirty artists diverse and nuanced interpretations will be featured in the show.

Many artworks will be available for sale. Proceeds from all sales will directly benefit the 
artists.

roundabout features works made between 2022 and 2025, inviting viewers into
Marin Majic's evocative world. Across these paintings, roundabout conjures a realm
suspended between memory and immediacy, dream and waking life-a space that
slowly dissolves into soft veils of light and time.

Maji-s paintings start out as vibrant drawings in coloured pencil on linen canvas.
These rich underlayers are then painted over with a diaphanous mixture of oil paint
and marble dust, creating a milky haze that softens the surface and imbues each
image with an ephemeral quality. Into this opacity, Majic reenters the work, drawing
and brushing the surface to reveal fragments of colour beneath. The act is both
additive and subtractive, as each gesture negotiates the slow logic of the drying
medium.

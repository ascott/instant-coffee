Submissions to The LAB will now be accepted on an ongoing basis. We are
currently accepting proposals for The LAB 11.0 Series which begins April

The LAB

Designed specifically for presenting challenging and dynamic experimental
projects, The LAB is a contemporary space within the Art Gallery. This
exhibition space is a site for new work, as well as a project space in
which proposals are invited to engage with the site itself. The Gallery
welcomes all forms of media, performance or innovative museological
projects. Projects may also involve the Gallery’s permanent collection.

Seeking unconventional space
Hello Vancouverites.

My wife and I are moving to Vancouver July 1st. She will be studying
architecture at UBC, and I am a professional driver. We are both also
artists, and so are looking for the impossible: a large (800+ s.f.)
affordable ($1000/month), unused space to make into a live/work studio.

We're tidy, respectful, non-smokers, n/d, have EXCELLENT REFERENCES
(available upon request), and one beautiful cat. I also have landscaping
and pruning experience, and would be happy to work on the property.

Any space will do- second floor storage space of a business, unfinished
large basement, outbuilding, even industrial space (we're hoping for some
sort of natural light, even if it's only a skylight). We're hoping to find
something fairly close to UBC.

All we need is a sink and bathroom. We can make do with a hotplate and
toaster oven, and we enjoy decorating unconventional space to make it a home.

Questions? Know of anything? Please feel free to contact us!

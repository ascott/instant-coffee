Join us on October 2nd for the opening of a new exhibition featuring work by the artists
of the Phoenix Gallery.

This show brings together a wide range of disciplines as our artists respond to a
passage from Margaret Atwoods Alias Grace:

"When you are in the middle of a story it isn't a story at all, but only a confusion; a dark
roaring, a blindness, a wreckage of shattered glass and splintered wood; like a house
in a whirlwind, or else a boat crushed by the icebergs or swept over the rapids, and all
aboard powerless to stop it. It's only afterwards that it becomes anything like a story at
all. When you are telling it, to yourself or to someone else."

The exhibition reflects on chaos, memory, and transformation"how lived moments
shift into narrative with the passage of time.

Phoenix Gallery - 45 West Hastings, alley entrance
Opening: October 2nd, 6-9pm

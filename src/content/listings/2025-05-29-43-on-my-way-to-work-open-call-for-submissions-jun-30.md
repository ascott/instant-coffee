Open Call for Art: Group Exhibition "On My Way to Work"
When and Where: August to October, 2025 at Behind VA Shadows Gallery, Boston,
US

About: "On My Way to Work" explores the everyday journey of commuting that marks
a physical, psychological, and intellectual transition. On our ways to work, we move
across different spaces and assume different identities. What occupies your mind
when youre physically contained in transit or at work? How do artistic ideas or
thoughts flow through these spaces and what comes out of it? The exhibition seeks to
recount these creative and intellectual activities and bring them into conversation with
each other.
Submission: open for all who have worked at arts and cultural institutions via google
form
Deadline: June 30, 2025

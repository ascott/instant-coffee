Life In Harmony

J.J.H. Lee, Nick O'Connell, Lyndsay Pomerantz

Hours by appointment: November 1st-5th 2018 

Opening: Wednesday, October 31, 6:30pm 

Dynamo Arts Association

Along these lines, it seems as though life is a construct of various placeholders that 
fuel the habitual practice of banality as meaningful. Its like an empty container that 
could change the read of the content within the vessel simply by altering its facade. 
If the content within is equal among many different products, then the labeling of the 
product functions as a heuristic, in order to further enjoy its contents. To further the 
argument around festivities, one must distinguish the function of this placeholder. 

Social practice and culture isnt a singular notion; therefore, the relationship between 
the providing and the receiving end should be defined. The enjoyment and the duty 
of preserving festivities do not necessarily lie on the subject who is receiving the gift. 
Rather it is those who are gifting that continue the desire to pass on the faith of 
festivities. There is the contradiction of continuing this falsehood when the giver also 
acknowledges its folly. Such contradiction of belief is inherited by the next generation 
but perhaps it is precisely how we enjoy these events..

We know exactly what were doing"know what it is not"nevertheless we continue, 
steadily.

Despite such logical discord, the will to maintain festivities is woven throughout our 
immediate surroundings. This is the issue of faith. With a leap, one is either a 
believer or the other (non-believer or the other). This does not mean that neither the 
act nor the subject of festivities is separated by their strength in belief. An individuals 
decision in their belief or non-belief is both true; however, the elements that make up 
festivities are composed of a series of fictitious narratives. These fictitious narratives 
are widely accepted as such. Ironically, truth in festivities is a collection of pre-
acknowledged bogus; its almost perverted to question its legitimacy. But their 
specificities demand the labeling of the containers, which then illustrates the 
particular festivity. And particularity is important because without this labeling, the 
particularity cannot be established for its unique enjoyment. 

But what is this particularity actually doing? The argument serves that all festivities 
are a kind of placeholders in life, like an empty container. Different containers carry 
the same values but their systemic labeling differentiates them. Locating the 
particulars of festivities portrays that such events are grounding points in a day, a 
month, a season, or a year. They start to become hallmarks of time that serves the 
gamut of experience in life. Festivities only serve as a ground to the ordinary and 
reproduce banality for its own consideration.

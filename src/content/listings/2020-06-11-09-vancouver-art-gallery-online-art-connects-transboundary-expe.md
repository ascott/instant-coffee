Art Talk
Tuesday, June 16, 1:30pm, free and open to the public on the Gallery's Zoom channel.

In this edition of Art Connects, Sheng Zhengtian and Keith Wallace will discuss the 
founding of Yishu: Journal of Contemporary Chinese Art, the first English-language 
journal to focus on Chinese contemporary art and culture.

Celebrating its 18th anniversary of publication last month, Yishu has become a diverse 
platform for a wide range of voices who are living and telling the story of contemporary 
Chinese art. The journal provides dialogue and debate around current visual and literary 
forms constituting an expanded understanding of contemporary Chinese art. Featuring 
scholarly essays, interviews with artists and curators, and critical
commentary on 
exhibitions and books, Yishus high standard of critical writing by thinkers from around 
the world allows them to voice ideas that communicate across cultures.

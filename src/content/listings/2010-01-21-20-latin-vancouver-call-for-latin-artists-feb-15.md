CALL FOR LATIN ARTISTS

Submission Deadline | FEBRUARY 15, 2010

Latin Vancouver is calling for Latin Artists living in the Lower
Mainland interested on presenting at 2 Latin Art Shows that will take
place in the spring and summer.
The Art shows will feature Art in different forms.

Submissions shall be addressed to Paola Murillo @ paolavm@latincouver.ca
and cc: Graham.rocio@gmail.com

Please provide with your submission:
-Contact information (including e-mail)
-Medium used (mixed media, painting, sculpture, music
-Address of your arts studio (if any)
-Artist statement
-Education (if applicable)
-Exhibition record (if any)
-One picture sample of your work (visual artists only).For musicians a
link to a recorded event/song shall suffice, samples of written form can
be sent in a document not to exceed 3 pages. Further information will be
provided once we get your contact information.

Latin Vancouver is also working on creating a network for advocacy and
development of the Latin Artists living in the lower mainland. If you
would like to receive further information to networking events please
note it in your e-mail.

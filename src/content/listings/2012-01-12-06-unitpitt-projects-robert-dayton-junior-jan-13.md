Robert Dayton Junior Presents: AN EVENING WITH THE CANADIAN 
ROMANTIC

An evening of performance, fine food, and the launch of a new book by The 
Canadian Romantic, presented by UNIT/PITT and Publication Studio Vancouver 
at the Dunlevy Snackbar, 433 Dunlevy Ave., Vancouver BC on Friday, January 
13 2012 | Tickets ($25, performance plus dinner; $40, performance plus dinner 
plus book) in advance, available from Dunlevy Snackbar, READ Books, 
UNIT/PITT, or for sale online. Seating is limited, so reserve now.

The Canadian Romantic

Dinner, soft lighting, and a captivating stranger who is giving you his full 
attention. You won't stay strangers for long.

The Canadian Romantic is a melodramatic figure of faded glamour who tries to 
bridge and explore the gap between Canada and romance with somewhat 
dubious results. The Canadian Romantic himself will be performing live, taking 
topic suggestions from the crowd for his infamous impromptu candlelight 
Recitations. Expect glittery Bon Mots aplenty from a man of way too much 
experience. You will come alive with laughter feeling like youâ€™ve woken up at an 
undisclosed time with make up smeared across your face.

The evening will also include a tribute to the Ac-tor as singer, and the launch of 
a new, lavishly illustrated Canadian Romantic book. This is a rare chance for 
Vancouver to meet The Canadian Romantic, accompanied by audio-visual and 
musical splendor, and walk away with a warm glow and a new paperback.

Check www.unitpitt.ca for previews of the book and performance, and details of 
the dinner menu!

Robert Dayton is The Canadian Romantic. He is a multidisciplinary entertainer, 
writer, and artist from British Columbia who resides for the moment in Toronto. 
His work has appeared in Hunter and Cook, Carousel, The Toronto Onion, 
Roctober, Cinema Sewer, Paper Rodeo, Bananafish, CBC3 and numerous other 
places. He is known for being in such curious musical acts as Canned Hamm 
and July Fourth Toilet, as well as acting in the films Male Fantasy, Ivory Tower, 
and Leslie My Name Is Evil.

RADICAL VISIONS
Video Works by Leslie Supnet

July 14th
7:00pm Doors
7:30pm Screening
Admission is free

VIVO Media Arts Centre
2625 Kaslo St., Vancouver

On Tuesday July 14th, VIVO Media Arts Centre will hold a free screening of
works both old and new by Toronto-based video artist, Leslie Supnet.

Winnipeg-born Leslie Supnet utilizes a variety formats in her practice
including, hand-drawn animation, Super 8 & 16mm film, and found images. The
ethereal wonder that characterizes many of her works belies a darkness that
acts as their foundation.

EAST FEAST Vancouver

On June 5, 2011 the Britannia Community Services Centre Society presents
the first EAST FEAST Vancouver, a micro-funding event that supports artists
who live or work in the neighbourhoods of Grandview-Woodland & Strathcona
through a community meal that raises funds through ticket sales to the
event which in turn directly funds an artist or artist group. This FEAST is
grassroots micro-funding at its best, relying on community engagement to be
successful. We are looking to support and stimulate experimental,
creative, and critical projects that would benefit the local arts community.

The FEAST will fund an artist or artist group up to $1000. The artist
submission process has been made purposely simple and we invite artists
from all disciplines to apply.

For the price of your ticket participants receive a meal prepared
specifically for the FEAST by Michael Speier of Open Sesame Thematic
Catering, entertainment, and a ballot for choosing one of three presenting
artists at the event who will receive funding. Tickets are $40 each.

If you are an artist (or artist group) interested in submitting to the
FEAST or you wish to support local artists and attend the FEAST please see
our website: www.britanniacentre.org under Quicklinks click on Arts &
Culture then Special Events

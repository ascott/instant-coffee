The Kootenay Gallery of Art, a public art gallery situated in Castlegar, BC is seeking 
applications from artists for our future exhibition schedule. We are looking for artists 
from diverse backgrounds who create critical contemporary art which is relevant to a 
local audience. The Gallery pays artists fair fees in accordance with CARFAC. Deadline 
for submissions is Friday, May 12 at 5:00 pm.

To apply, visit http://www.kootenaygallery.com/submissions/ .

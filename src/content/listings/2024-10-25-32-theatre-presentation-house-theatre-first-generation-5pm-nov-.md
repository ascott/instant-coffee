Location: Presentation House Theatre
Title: First Generation
Organization/event: STAND Festival

Please join us for a community art reception with live music, talk and demo,
refreshments provided by Bean on 5th, and Presentation House bar.

First Generation opening reception:
Novemeber 9th, 5-8pm
Presentation House, North Vancouver

First Generation is a group exhibition featuring the art of immigrants whose voices are
often inaccessible to the public: children and older adults. At the beginning of their
journeys, immigrant children are still in the process of developing their cultural
identities when they find themselves in a new country. This is juxtaposed with older
adults, who have a wealth of lived experience and have had the time to create a body
of work
that speaks to that. By looking at the art of these two demographics, this
curated show works to bridge the generational gap that immigrants often face as a
result of being separated from their cultural origins.

First Generation is curated by artist Annie Hung for the 2024 annual STAND Festival, a
non-profit event supporting immigrant and refugee artists. Annie Hung is a community
art instructor with the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts and SFU doctoral student.

Book Launch + Readings | Aja Moore: hotwheel
READ Books
Thursday, November 15
7-9pm

Please join us at READ Books on Thursday, November 15 at 7pm for the launch of Aja 
Moore's hotwheel (Metatron Press), with additional readings by Amna Elnour, Tin Lorica, 
and Beni Xiao.

Aja Moores debut collection of poetry is awash in sincerity and the ways in which we 
simultaneously crave, doubt, avoid, and totally disparage it. hotwheel, in turn, reveals its 
precious life-affirming conviction, despite its incertitude"or precisely because of it.

---

Aja Moore is a graduate of the University of British Columbia. She has volunteered with 
the B.C. Book Prizes, Summer Literary Seminars, and as a Poetry Editor for PRISM 
International. hotwheel is her first book.

Amna is a writer and DJ. She attributes her difficulty with being on time to either her 
astrological sign or her ethnicity, depending on her mood. 

Tin Lorica is an emerging poet and comedian who resides in East Vancouver. They are 
an Aries sun with a Cancer moon. They are your local grumpy barista. They have 
recently re-discovered the healing powers of shit-talking in Tagalog. 

Beni Xiao is dead, oh my god.

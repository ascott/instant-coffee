Stoppage
Hamed Teymouri

March 21st through April 23rd
Opening reception Friday, March 20th, 6.00-8.00pm

Stoppage is about the necessity of urban war, the implacable urgency to
fire ammunition, set off ordinance from all directions and dominate by
force of arms the population in a city. It is about hunting people through
their streets and across their walls. It is about the transformation of
urban space into battle space.

The images in Stoppage are registers to the process of response to urban
war, an over-saturated outside view of the urgency of something that one
should not so easily pretend to understand. This urgency is driven by the
question of what comes first: the economic, cultural and political
conflicts, or the civilian armed conflicts that go with them. Where does
one find the ultimate stratum of meaning in these rounds of fear, hate and
destruction?

Hamed Teymouri holds an M.A. in studio art and art history from the
University of British Columbia. Stoppage is his second solo show with
Republic Gallery. Most recently he was featured in the exhibition The Most
Violent Thing, curated by Vanessa Sorenson.

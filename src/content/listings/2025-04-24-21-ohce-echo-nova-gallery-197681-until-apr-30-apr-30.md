NOVA Gallery: 1976-81

OHCE-ECHO, 1469 Venables

Open Saturdays in April, 2-6pm, and by appointment until April 30.

-_-

NOVA Gallery"the photography gallery co-founded by Claudia Beck and Andrew
Gruft"has been at the centre and the periphery of many histories of photography in
Vancouver. Acknowledging the stories that have already been told, OHCE-ECHO
presents a little history of the gallery, centred on a poster archive documenting the
fifty-odd exhibitions that were held at 1972 West 4th Avenue between December
1976 and June 1981.

Candle Smoke With a Long Draw
Francisco Berlanga & Tiffany Law

One day only " August 3, 2025 | 2"4 PM
Performances at 2:00 and 3:45 PM

In Candle Smoke With a Long Draw, artists Tiffany Law and Francisco Berlanga
present a one-day collaborative exhibition of sculptural shrines and ritual gestures
that explore memory, grief, and cultural inheritance. Centred around a large altar and
surrounded by corner shrines, mobile sculptures, and a coffin lying in wake, the
installation creates a space of reverence"an ephemeral reliquary for both past and
present.

The artists draw from objects once charged with personal meaning and introduce
new material offerings, crafted in an attempt to echo the emotional gravity of their
predecessors. What emerges is a shrine to a shrine: a quiet, unresolved reaching
toward understanding through ritual, repetition, and care.

Permanent as Permanence & Study for Antoni Wojtyra
Double edition launch
Saturday, November 2nd, 2-4 p.m.

WAAP
528-2050 Scotia St.
Vancouver
Buzzer 189

Wil Aballe Art Projects is pleased to announce the launch of two ediitons by 
two bright and rising Vancouver-based talents, Evan Broens and Jason 
Gowans. The two photography editions are launched as part of the 2013 
Capture Photography Festival.

Permanent As Permanence is a photographic-based edition that operates 
under the conceptual framework of the proximate relationship between 
sculpture and photography, endeavouring to unify these two mediums. The 
impetus for this work occurred by an incidental glance at a detritus of home 
improvement - a large piece of broken styrofoam leaned against the side of 
house, the light catching the upper ridge of the fissure that contrasted the 
darkness that happens between two homes. Evan Broens photographed this 
moment on 35 mm film, printed it digitally, and cut the form out of the page. 
This was mounted on board, cut again, and encased in an epoxy resin. The 
work is positioned on the wall in the precise angle this detritus was initially 
photographed. From styrofoam to photograph to resin, this work entombs 
material and time.

During 2012/2013, Jason Gowans and Antoni Wojtyra created many works for 
the upcoming exhibition Dumb as a Painter, opening November 15th at 
Gallery 295 in Vancouver. Authorship, intent and reasoning become muddled; 
such is the nature of collaboration. How do two artists collaborate when their 
time, studios and practices are divided? This edition, Study for Antoni 
Wojtyra, is a photographic "sketch" sent from Gowans' studio to Wojtyra's 
along with the hundreds of sketches, articles, videos and research sent via 
email. This photo sketch, similarly derived from sculpture as Broens' edition, 
was later finalized for the exhibition.

Evan Broens' (b. 1983) current work and practice consists of researching the 
formal, poetic and philosophical possibilities of sculpture. He completed his 
studies in Sculpture at the Alberta College of Art & Design in 2005 and has 
since exhibited both nationally and internationally. Last year, he was artist in 
residence at the DRAWinternational Residency in southern France. Currently, 
he is artist in residence in the thematic residency Another World in the Studio 
at the Banff Centre. Broens' upcoming exhibitions include a two person show 
at Dynamo Arts Association, Vancouver and a solo exhibition at WAAP.

Jason Gowans (b. 1981) lives and works in Vancouver. He received a BFA in 
photography at Concordia University in photography. He is a photo-based 
artist, the co-founder of Gallery 295 and the founding member of The 
Everything Company art collective. Recent solo projects include Dumb as a 
Painter with Antoni Wojtyra, Gallery 295, Vancouver (2013); Three Wrongs 
Don't Make a Right with The Everything Co, Access Gallery (2013); Five 
Landscape Modes, Gallery Fukai, Vancouver (2013). Recent group exhibitions 
include: Magenta Flash Forward Exhibition (2013-14); Romancing the 
Anthropocene with The Everything Co., Scotiabank Nuit Blanche, Toronto 
(2013); Nouvelles Geographies, Galerie Perception Park, Paris (2013); 
Recreation of The Lure of the Sea for 40th Anniversary, The Western Front, 
Vancouver (2013).

Saturday, November 2nd is also the last day to catch Scott Massey's

CALL FOR ARTISTS EXPLORING WATER

Application deadline April 24th

IN PERSON : July 5th -13th, 2024

ONLINE portion June 1st- June 30th

(bi weekly residency meetings ( 1.5 hour sessions)

Artists are
expected to attend both portions of the residency.

Agua Viva is a 9-day art residency that explores water as a creative force. Artists will
be guided based on non-western traditions to develop a relationship to /
understanding of water that invites curiosity, study, reverence, play, healing and
stewardship.

Agua Viva will take place in Santa Rosa Arts, located in British Columbia, Canada
overlooking Christina Lake at the convergence of the Kettle River and
Christina Lake
Creek with unobstructed views of various bodies of water.

Key aspects of this residency:

Water in ancient civilizations and sacred spaces
Understanding the language of water
First peoples water teachings
Water in contemporary art practices
Portfolio reviews with guest curator
Exhibition opportunities Arts on 3 art space ( Christina Lake)
Artists talks
Anti Colonial community building
Water and healing
Sound water and art
Field trips to watersheds with water specialists
Lots of time spent in bodies of water (
swimming suits will be your best friend).
Water rituals
Building communities of care based on water teachings

Richmond Cultural Centre Mural, Request for Qualifications (RFQ)

The Richmond Public Art Program seeks an artist or artist team to create a mural for the 
exterior fascia and structural columns of the Richmond Cultural Centre, located at 7700 
Minoru Gate. Artists with demonstrated experience and skill sets working with multiple 
project stakeholders and executing outdoor murals are encouraged to apply. A two-
stage artist selection process will be implemented for this commission. Shortlisted artists 
will be invited to outline a public engagement process to develop a design concept 
working with community members, Cultural Centre user groups and project 
stakeholders.

Budget: $30,000

Eligibility: Artists residing in British Columbia

Deadline: March 15, 2019

Completion: Summer 2019

For more information and to download the artist call, please visit: 
https://www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities.htm

Call for Short Films

Videodrunk

Deadline: October 15th

Between November 20-22, The White House Studio Gallery in Toronto will be
hosting Videodrunk, a celebration of independent and experimental short
film. We have 15 slots in our program open and welcoming all to submit.
Submit your short films (between 5-25 minutes) to us by October 15th. All
genres and styles are welcome.

When submitting please include the following info and a link to the film
-Film's name -Director's Name -Country of Origin -Year of Release -Run Time
-Genre -Any previous screenings

Please send all submissions and questions to videodrunkfest@gmail.com

Only the makers of films selected to screen will be notified. Notifications
will be sent out by October 31st.

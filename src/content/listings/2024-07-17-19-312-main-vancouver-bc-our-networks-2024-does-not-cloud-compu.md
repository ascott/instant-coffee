*Our Networks 2024: Does not (cloud) compute*
July 27, 2024
312 Main, Vancouver, BC

What does collaboration and presence look like when a network is
optional? Drawing on practices of local-first, solar-powered servers,
folk software, and permacomputing, Our Networks 2024 explores
transitional technologies that allow us to reframe what computing could
mean when the internet, and the cloud, are optional.

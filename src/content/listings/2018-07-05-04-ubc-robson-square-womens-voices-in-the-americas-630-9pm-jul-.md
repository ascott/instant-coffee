Womens Voices in the Americas is a filmmakers panel that provides a
different, intercultural perspective of the Americas told using women's
voices. This event uses works created by women as a starting point for
dialogue and incorporates unique historical views showcasing cultures
throughout the Americas from the point of view of women. Speakers include
Ana Cruz Navarro (Mexico), Jill Sharpe (Canada), and Marilyn Norry (Canada).

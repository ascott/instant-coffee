Join us for our next Zine Night on Friday, July 12. The evening will include a zine-
making workshop and an artist talk by Ho Tam, followed by a show-and-tell
presented by Jonathan Middleton of Vancouver Art Book Fair.

This series of programs is presented in conjunction with the exhibition Copy Machine
Manifestos: Artists Who Make Zines.

Find out more and register at [https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/events/zine-night-jul-12](https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/events/zine-night-jul-12)

SCHEDULE OF EVENTS | JULY 12

Zine Night Workshop with Ho Tam and Friends
5:30"8:30 PM, In the Annex Workshop

Facilitated by the Gallerys knowledgeable Art Educators and designed
by artist
Sonja Ahlers, this workshop will begin with a short zine demo. Participants are
invited to create a small zine while mingling and listening to music.

Artist Ho Tam, whose work is on view in the exhibition Copy Machine Manifestos:
Artists Who Make Zines, will give a short artist talk. He has also prepared a playlist
for the evening.

All zine-making materials will be provided.

This event is free and offered on a drop-in basis. Advanced registration is
recommended.

Show-and-Tell with Jonathan Middleton
6:15 PM, 2nd Floor

Join Jonathan Middleton of Vancouver Art Book Fair for a spotlight tour of the
exhibition Copy Machine Manifestos. He will discuss the power of self-publishing and
the upcoming book fair, returning to Vancouver from July 26 and 27, 2024.

Free for Gallery Members or with admission.

Lobby Lounge
5"8 PM, Gallery Lobby

On Friday evenings between 5 and 8 PM, the Gallery Lobby transforms into a
relaxing lounge where members and visitors can enjoy a drink
before or after their
visit to see the exhibitions.

Ideas and Experiences Members receive one free drink, PLUS a discount on their
second. Visitors who have purchased an admission ticket that day will also receive
one free drink.

Please note that Access Pass Holders are not eligible to receive a complementary
drink.

Teach-in on the environmental impact of streaming media, for Global Climate Strike week

with Laura Marks and Adel Iskander

September 23, 8:00-8:45 p.m.

Djavad Mowafaghian Cinema, SFU Woodwards, 149 West Hastings St.

Yikes, did you know that streaming video"viral videos on your phone, Netflix binges on your
computer"occupies about 7% of the global energy footprint?! Dont panic! Join students at
SFU Contemporary Arts as we lay out the problem and propose solutions, from low-tech
alternatives to true-cost accounting to activism.

For more information, or to attend the full lecture on media materiality (starts at 6:30) contact
Laura Marks, lmarks@sfu.ca.

15. Cineworks | INTRODUCTION TO PROTOOLS | JUL 25
This introductory workshop is geared towards the independent filmmaker with
a focus on proper set-up of a ProTools project and is intended for those
unfamiliar with the program. Participants will learn the basics of industry
standard sound design, using demonstration and hands-on play to explore the
program. Workflow will be discussed, beginning with importing audio tracks
from the picture edit, editing dialogue, and using sound effects libraries.
A short sample scene, to be supplied by the instructor or of the student's
own in-progress work, will be sound edited and mixed, applying basic
effects. Students will gain an understanding of how to successfully mix
audio for their own work. The workshop includes one-on-one time with the
instructor, one week of practice time between sessions so that participants
can explore some basic hands-on exercises, and concludes with instructor
feedback.

17. The Miss Guides | Walking the Ruins: Fragments of Vancouver | til | AUG 1
The Miss Guides: A Cultural Walking Collective is presents
Walking the Ruins: Fragments of Vancouver, a theatrical, site-specific
urban walk that strives to uncover the city's forgotten past and
overlooked present. Part historical tour, part fictional performance,
and part virtual navigation (themissguides.com), this hour-long walk
is rooted in the idea of Ruins as 'fragmentary remains', both past and
present and attempts to piece together conflicting visions of the city
by making unexpected connections between four diverse and surprising
sites of Ruin. Using the streets as their stage, The Miss Guides
reveal the simple act of walking to be politicized and empowering: a
way of both reclaiming public space in an increasingly privatized
world, and remaining alert to the sights, sounds and smells of an
ever-changing city. Walking The Ruins becomes an unforgettable way of
experiencing our everyday environment, of triggering memories, of
inhabiting and laying bare the narratives of the city, of speculating
on imagined spaces and lost futures.

Audiences are limited to 6 adults, and groups meet on the Northwest

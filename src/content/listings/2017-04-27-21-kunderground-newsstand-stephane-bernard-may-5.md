Announcing the presentation of KUNDERGROUND Newsstand, an immersive 
installation of vintage Kanadian (Quebec + Canada) UNDERGROUND ephemera, 
meant to acknowledge the publication of the first issue of the Georgia Straight 
newspaper in Vancouver, on May 5th 1967.

KUNDERGROUND Newsstand
8 West Cordova Street, Vancouver
May 5-22, 2017
Opening Reception: Friday May 5th, 2017 " 4-7pm

KUNDERGROUND Newsstand is an exhibition of artifacts, furniture and original 
publications culled from a private collection of works, that is meant to transform the 
gallery space into an archival newsstand. In Japan of the 1950's, there was a type of 
store called Kashi-hon ya, which consisted of a rental library for the general public, 
where people would pay a fee to borrow books as they couldn't afford to purchase them 
due to the cost of living at the time. With this in mind, visitors can experience the curated 
selection of underground newspapers, comix and artist publications dating from 1960 to 
the present, either as a throwback to an age when newsprint was the media of choice, 
or for a nominal fee, they are invited to peruse the selection of materials on-site, during 
business hours.

Collected, curated and presented by Stphane Bernard, KUNDERGROUND Newsstand 
is a self-contained artistic experience looking to chart the network of links between what 
is commonly referred to as early artist-run culture, and what is popularly known as the 
underground press movement throughout Kanada.

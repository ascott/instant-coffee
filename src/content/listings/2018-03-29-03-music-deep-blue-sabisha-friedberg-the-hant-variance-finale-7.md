Sabisha Friedberg: The Hant Variance finale

7pm
Deep Blue
255 E 2nd Ave. 
March 29, 2018

Free
Sabisha Friedberg will be performing a live mix of the third and final movement of 
The Hant Variance composition, presented specially for Deep Blue in quadraphonic. 
The finale is the resolution to the first two movements, released by ISSUE Project 
Room as a double LP (2015), and, of the series, is the most symphonic in its 
construction and melancholic in its tonality.

Sabisha Friedbergs composition, performance and installation work draws upon a 
chimeric arcane, exploring perceptual delineation of space through sound, sculpture, 
and low-end experiential thresholds. She has performed and exhibited 
internationally, and received commissions through residencies at institutions 
including ISSUE Project Room, EMPAC (Experimental Media and Performing Arts 
Center, RPI), and the Clocktower Gallery (now Clocktower Productions), as well as 
being included in MoMA PS1's LP release, There Will Never Be Silence (2014). Born 
in Johannesburg, South Africa, and currently residing in New York City and Paris, 
Friedberg received her MFA from Bard College, and her BA in Fine Arts, with an 
emphasis on sound and installation, from the New Genres Department at the San 
Francisco Art Institute where she studied recording and electro-acoustic/tape music 
composition with Don Llloyd, Paul de Marinas and Chris Brown of Mills College of 
Contemporary Music.

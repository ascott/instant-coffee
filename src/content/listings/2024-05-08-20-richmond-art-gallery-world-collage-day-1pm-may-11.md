RICHMOND ART GALLERY
World Collage Day: Collage Party for Joyce Wieland
Saturday, May 11, 2024
1:00 PM - 4:00 PM

Free admission for ages 16+.
All collage materials and tools provided. Open to creatives of all skill levels.

As part of World Collage Day, RAG presents a Collage Party
for adults. It's the
perfect opportunity to get together with friends and make some art!

Using imagery inspired by the work of Canadian art icon Joyce Wieland, participants
are invited to cut and paste while you chat, listen to music, and experiment with
simple art materials.

World Collage Day is an annual, international celebration of collage on the Second
Saturday of May that was initiated by kolaj magazine in 2018. World Collage Day is
about artists connecting across borders against a global context of entrenchment
and separation. And the day is about an art medium that excels at bringing different
things together to create new
forms and new ways of thinking.

Inspired by the Hazel Meyer exhibition, the focus will be on the Canadian artist who
inspired Meyer: Joyce Wieland. Wieland (1930-1998) explored various materials and
media as her art became assertively political, engaging with nationalism, feminism,
and ecology. Using similar themes as a starting point, gallery staff will
share images
of Wieland's works and demonstrate how to use cut and ripped posters,
photographs, magazines, and various papers and fabrics to develop new collage
artworks. Work on solo or collaborative collage pieces, or take a break for a quick
casual tour of The Marble in the Basement.

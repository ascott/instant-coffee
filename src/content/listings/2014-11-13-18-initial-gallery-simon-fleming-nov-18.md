initial gallery | Simon Fleming Vacancy / No Vacancy | Nov 18

Simon Fleming
Vacancy / No Vacancy
November 18 - December 13, 2014

Opening reception
Tuesday, November 18
6-8 pm

Initial Gallery is pleased to present Vacancy/No Vacancy featuring recent
work by Simon Fleming. The recent series Vacancy/No Vacancy presents two
derelict homes as catalysts for numerous narratives of tenants past. The
two repeated structures act as windows revealing histories and
personalities of previous occupiers now long gone. Avoiding literal visual
cues and signs, Fleming relies on the emotive qualities of colour,
structure and material application. The works wander from representational
to abstract and back again as they reflect upon the different tapestries of
previous tenants.

Simon Fleming recently moved to Vancouver with his wife and son after
living in Ireland for 4 years. He has exhibited his work in Ireland, the
UK, Germany, France, Sweden, Canada and Chile.

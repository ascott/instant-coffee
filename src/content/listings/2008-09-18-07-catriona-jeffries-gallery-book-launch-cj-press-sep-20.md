BOOK LAUNCH: 
CJ PRESS Anthology of Exhibition Essays 2006/2007 

Catriona Jeffries Gallery, Vancouver
Saturday September 20th, 2008 3 – 5 pm

We invite you to join us at Catriona Jeffries on Saturday 
September 20th 3–5pm for a book launch of the gallery’s 
inaugural publication CJ Press: Anthology of Exhibition Essays 
2006/2007. 

Since 1996, Catriona Jeffries has commissioned writers to 
contribute and respond to the exhibitions at the gallery through 
critical essays produced as small booklets by CJ Press. Over a 
decade after the project's inception, a collection of essays 
commissioned from September 2006 – December 2007 has 
been gathered together for the first time as a book. 

The publication includes essays by: Jessie Caryl | Kevin 
Schmidt; Monika Szewczyk | Geoffrey Farmer; Clint Burnham | 
Isabelle Pauwels; Aaron Peck | Damian Moppett; Jeff Derksen 
|Sam Durant; Candice Hopkins | Brian Jungen; Christopher 
Brayshaw | Arabella Campbell; Juan A. Gaitán | Judy Radul; 
Ian Wallace | Ian Wallace; Jerry Zaslove | Alex Morrison. 

Hardcover book
135 pages | 8 ½ “ x 5 ¾”
$20 CAD

Lucky’s Comics, Books and Gallery presents // 
Celebrated Summer: Photos by Katie Webster 
//
Opening: Friday November 15th from 8-11pm
Location: Lucky's Comics, Books and Gallery - 3972 Main St. 

In this selection of sun-drenched photographs Webster chronicles her summer 
travels to the remote corners of British Columbia, from the spectral alpine lakes 
of the north to the windswept beaches of the southwest. Shot with a Yashica 
Electro 35mm, these grand landscapes are shown in raw unedited form and 
presented in celebration of the great northern summer.

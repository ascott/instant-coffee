Event Title: ECLECTIC Fest: Digital Revolution
Website Link: [http://eclectic47.com/eclectic-fest-about](http://eclectic47.com/eclectic-fest-about)

We are seeking short films of any genre for our digital film festival!
Entry is free and open to all
films so long as they are available on Vimeo and under 20 minutes long.

May 5 is the deadline for submissions. Entrants will hear back on May 10 with the selected films
being posted on our website from May 15 - May 17.

Selected films will receive write ups, laurels, free promotion, and fan art.

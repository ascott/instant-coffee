Localisms
 Explorations of Vancouver’s regional art history and the nature of the 
 local. 

 Maya Beaudry/Vanessa Brown/John Burgess/Patrick Cruz/Colleen 
 Heslin/Gabrielle Hill/Brennan Kelly/Sydney Koke/Graham Landin/Ben 
 Marvin/Les Ramsay/Simon Redekop/Jeff Stuckel/Alex Stursberg 

 East Van Studios
 870 East Cordova St.
 July 11 & 12, 2014
 Opening Reception July 11th, 7pm - 12am
 Afternoon Viewing and BBQ July 12th, 12pm - 5pm

 Vancouver’s art making methods have long been determined by the 
 region itself rather than by external influence. Our isolation, limited size, 
 and the push/pull between the urban and rural are all factors that have 
 served to make the Vancouver art world unique. Today’s emerging artists 
 are highly shaped by the weight of the city’s rich but isolated art history. 
 Yet they are also involved in new approaches, which seek to rectify the 
 local within a highly globalized and image saturated visual culture.

 We are interested in exploring not only how today’s emerging Vancouver 
 artists are influenced by the region's art history, but how they are also 
 impacted by each other. What does today’s local aesthetic look like? 
 What conversations are being held within the emerging artist community? 
 Where do we find common ground and where do we diverge?

 This exhibition provides one perspective of the current emerging 
 Vancouver artist community. A small snapshot of the scene, we present a 
 group of peers pursuing varying practices who speak a familiar language. 
 These are friends and frequent collaborators who possess an ever 
 growing regard for the regional amidst the dominant global art world. 

 Curated by Double Happiness

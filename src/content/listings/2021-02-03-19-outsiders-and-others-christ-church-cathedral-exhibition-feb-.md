Christ Church Cathedral exhibition

Outsiders and Others is partnering with Christ Church Cathedral, and posAbilities, and
are looking for artists that are self-taught and/or under represented, to participate in a
curated exhibition at Christ Church and on-line, from March 27 " May 2, 2021.

Spirituality, Art, and Community: A group exhibition of small works featuring 24 artists
from Christ Church Cathedral's congregation and neighbouring communities.

The exhibition will be curated by Yuri Arajs, Director at Outsiders and Others, and will
be limited to 24 artists.

Priority will be given to self-taught artists, but we encourage all artists of all abilities and
skill levels and to
apply. We want to see your work!

The space we are using for the exhibition restricts us to showing smaller artworks. Your
artwork cannot be bigger than 20 wide or 24 tall, including the frame. This is for all 2D
works such as painting, drawing, photography, textile, and others works that sit on an
easel.

If you are submitting sculpture, your artwork can be no taller than 24 and the base
cannot be wider than 10 x 10.

Your application should include:

1. Your name, email address, and website if you have one.

2. A written statement expressing what the name of the exhibition means to you.
Spirituality, Art, and Community.

3. A written statement about yourself with things you want us to know about you
and your artwork. Please indicate if you are a self-taught artist.

4. You can submit up to two pieces of artwork for consideration. Please send us
jpeg images only. The name of the image files should be your name and title of the
artwork. Example: 1. Yuri Arajs " Sleeping Cat in the
Field

5. Email everything to us at: outsidersandothers@gmail.com and please put
Christ Church application in the subject line.

There is no cost to apply & all participating artists will receive a $50 honourarium.

Selected artworks do not have to be for sale.

All sales are done by Outsiders and Others, and a 25% commission will be retained.

Important dates:

Application deadline: Feb 8, 2021

Artists notified of selection result: ~ Feb 15, 2021

We are planning a series of events that will include artist talk with all participating
artists. At this time we anticipate them to be online events.

This project is in partnership with Christ Church Cathedral, posAbilities, and Outsiders
and Others.

If you have any questions, comments or concerns, please email Yuri Arajs at
outsidersandothers@gmail.com and please put Christ Church in the subject line.

Open Studio: Between Canvas and Celluloid with Paige Smith
22 JUL 1:00 PM - 3:00 PM

Open Studio is a monthly program for young visitors and families inspired by
contemporary art practices and CAGs current exhibitions. In July, we invite you to
join us for an art-making workshop led by artist Paige Smith.

Inspired by CAGs current exhibition Gabrielle LHirondelle Hill: M*****, participants
will learn to manipulate film celluloid using paint, ink and scratching to create
moving-image paintings. Throughout this workshop, Paige will introduce cameraless
filmmaking techniques exploring how to translate tactile gestures of paint brushes
and pens into movement.
Each participant will create a short segment, and the
group's work will be stitched together and screened at the end of the workshop.

Registration for this event is free, but limited. A number of spots are reserved for
families. RSVP to secure your place here: https://cagvancouver.org/event/open-
studio-paige-smith

Paige Smith
(she/her) is an experimental filmmaker and visual artist whose artwork
collapses distinctions like fiction/reality or nature/culture to fabulate new ontological
understandings of ourselves and the places where we exist. She draws attention
towards the characteristics, histories, and meanings of the places where her artwork
is created and experienced, investigating how viewer perception is informed by its
surrounding context. Smith attended Simon Fraser University to study film and visual
art, and has recently exhibited with Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society,
Massy Arts Society, Audain Gallery, Vines Art Festival and VIVO Media Arts Centre.

"Architecture in a Bag"
Lincoln Heller - fiveleft leather
June 30th-August 1st at Circle Craft on Granville Island
1-1666 Johnston Street, in the Net Loft

The rigid vegetable tanned leather Lincoln uses has unique structural
properties. Its paper like quality can be pinched, folded and creased to
create forms that cannot be achieved with other leathers. For this 
show, he explores these characteristics and creates a series of objects 
that bring architectural aesthetics and the wearable vessel together.

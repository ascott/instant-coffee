2009 - 2012 Artist Studio Award Program
Call for Applications:

Application Deadline: 4:00 p.m. November 21, 2008
Open House: Saturday, November 1, 2008 10:30 a.m. - 2:30 p.m.

The City of Vancouver invites applications from Vancouver-based, low-income
professional artists for the 2009 - 2012 Artist Studio Award Program.

The City of Vancouver is offering two types of studio awards for practicing
Vancouver artists:

1. The Artist Award Studio provides a rent-free artist live/work studio at
Scotia Street and East 4th Avenue for a non-renewable term of three years
less two weeks beginning February 1, 2009.

2. The Artist Rental Studios provides three artist live/work studios
leased at the maximum shelter allowance for Income Assistance (currently
$375 per month). Two studios are located at Cambie Street and West 8th
Avenue and one studio is located at East Pender and Clark Drive. All
rental studios are provided for a non-renewable term of three years less
two weeks beginning February 1, 2009.

Three of the studios - the Award Studio and two of the Rental Studios- are
located within industrial/commercial districts and are zoned for the
production of dance or live music, moving or still photography, painting,
drawing, pottery and sculpture. The third Rental Studio is zoned for the
production of dance, music, creative writing, painting, drawing, pottery,
sculpture, video and moving or still photography. This studio is a one
bedroom unit, suitable for an artist and child.

Vancouver-based professional artists who can demonstrate artistic
excellence and financial need are eligible to apply. Further information
on eligibility and the application process can be accessed at the City's
website at: www.city.vancouver.bc.ca/commsvcs/oca/Awards/artiststudio

All awards will be decided by an independent jury of local arts and
culture professionals, jointly administered by Cultural Services and the
Contemporary Art Gallery.

Studios will be open for viewing Saturday, November 1, 2008 10:30 a.m. -
2:30 p.m. Please see the website for details.

Potential applicants who wish further information should contact Marcia
Belluce at 604-871-6875, well in advance of the application deadline.

Comox Valley Art Gallery
2014 Call for Submissions 

The CVAG artistic program is set annually at least one year in advance by a 
combination of adjudicating submissions and by curatorial invitation. CVAG 
invites proposals and submissions from artists, collectives and curators for 
our three exhibition spaces. Submissions to the Contemporary Gallery are 
juried by a panel of regional arts professionals assembled and led by the 
gallery Curator. Adjudication will take place in late February/early March of 
2013, with decisions communicated by the end of April 2013.
We are now accepting proposals for the 2014 exhibition year, please ensure 
that your submission arrives by January 31, 2013 to be considered.

Submissions to the Community Gallery and George Sawchuk Gallery are 
reviewed on an ongoing basis by the Curator. Although proposals are 
accepted throughout the year, there is a deadline to facilitate planning. 
Submissions are accepted by post mail; please do not send submissions by 
fax or email.

For full details: [http://www.comoxvalleyartgallery.com/submissions.html](http://www.comoxvalleyartgallery.com/submissions.html)

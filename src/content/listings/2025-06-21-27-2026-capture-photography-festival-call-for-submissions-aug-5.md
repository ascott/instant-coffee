Call for Submissions - 2026 Capture Photography Festival in Richmond. The
Richmond Public Art Program and Richmond Art Gallery invite artists and artist
collectives/groups to submit lens-based proposals for temporary public art
installations at one of two locations on the Canada Line: Aberdeen Station and
Brighouse Station, as part of Capture Photography Festival 2026.

Artist Fee: $1,500.00 CAD

Submission Deadline: August 5, 2025

Eligibility: Open to Professional Artists

Exhibition Duration: April 2026 - March 2027

For more information and to apply:
https://www.richmond.ca/culture/howartworks/publicart/artist-calls.htm

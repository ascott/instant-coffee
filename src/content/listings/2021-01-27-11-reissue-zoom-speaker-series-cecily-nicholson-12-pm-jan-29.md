ReIssue is kicking off 2021 with a series of free, virtual talks and presentations by artists,
writers, and artist-writers whose practices, though varied, meet at the intersection of text,
visual art, sound art, and teaching.

The Speaker Series will happen for five consecutive Fridays at 12:00 PST over Zoom, the
second-to-last being Cecily Nicholson on January 29.

The event will be a Zoom Webinar. Attendees will not be visible, but they may submit
questions to the speaker through the Chat function. Live Closed Captioning will be
available. For additional accessibility accommodations, please email
admin@reissue.pub

Bio:
Cecily Nicholson is an award-winning author of three books of poetry. She volunteers with
community impacted by carcerality and food insecurity. Her performances, talks, and
residencies have been hosted by educational spaces such as New York Universitys Tisch
School of the Arts, Centre for Indigenous Research Creation at Queen's University,
Woodland Pattern Book Centre, Milwaukee, and the Surrey School District.

About:
ReIssue is an interdisciplinary art writing platform focused on shaping and sustaining a
contemporary west coast discourse rooted in critical engagement with experimental art
practices. ReIssue is a partnership between UNIT PITT Society for Art and Critical
Awareness and VIVO Media Arts Centre, funded through a Vancouver Foundation Systems
Change Grant. https://reissue.pub/

ReIssue operates on unceded Musqueam, Tsleil-Waututh, and Squamish homelands
known as Vancouver, British Columbia.

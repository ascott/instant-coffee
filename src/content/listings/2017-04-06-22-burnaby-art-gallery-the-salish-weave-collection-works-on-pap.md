April 18-May 31, 2017
Opening Reception: Thursday, April 13, 6-8pm
burnabyartgallery.ca

This exhibition features works from the Salish Weave Collection, a private collection of 
contemporary Coast Salish art that weaves together the distinctive forms and designs of 
established and emerging artists. The Salish Weave Collection includes work by Andy 
Everson, Maynard Johnny Jr., lessLIE, Luke and John Marston, Chris Paul, Susan 
Point, and Dylan Thomas, artists who embrace the practice and revival of Salish art, 
drawing upon traditions and stories of their cultures, while challenging the definition of 
Salish art as they explore contemporary forms and techniques.

Artists' Talk with Cedric Nathan and Jim Bomford
Thursday August 7, 6 pm

Presented by Other Sights
at the Vancouver Maritime Museum
1905 Ogden Avenue, Vancouver BC

Join us for an illustrated talk with the Bomfords who will share the
backstory of the 3+ years leading to the launch of Deadhead, a floating
sculptural installation mounted to a WWII barge and towed by tug to
different locations. The team will discuss their unique collaborative
process and how distinct phases of research, salvaging materials, studio
production, and assembling on the Fraser River influenced Deadhead’s final
form. This informal presentation will provide opportunities for
conversation and discussion about the artwork’s references to British
Columbia’s west coast and its unique ecologies, economies, myths and legends.

Something Beautiful for the Sunset
An ambient improvised duet of horns will be performed at sundown aboard
Deadhead.
Karen Graves on saxophone and Vincent Mai on trumpet.

Deadhead is Presented by Other Sights for Artists’ Projects
Hosted by the Vancouver Maritime Museum
In Heritage Harbour,
1905 Ogden Avenue, Vancouver, BC

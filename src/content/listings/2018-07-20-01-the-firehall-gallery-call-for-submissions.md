The Firehall Gallery is a unique brick faced gallery space located in the
lobby of the Firehall Arts Centre. The Centre is launching its new season
of contemporary dance, theatre and multidisciplinary work. The Firehall has
an active schedule of performances and festivals and the Lobby Gallery
provides exposure to a diverse audience.

The Firehall is currently inviting proposals from emerging and established
artists for exhibitions during the 2018/2019 Season. We are particularly
interested in work that engages with contemporary issues of diversity and
community. We welcome all 2-dimensional work, but unfortunately we cannot
exhibit video works at this time. Exhibitions will run approximately 4-6 weeks.

Firehall will take 25% commission from sales.

SUBMISSION GUIDELINES

Submitted works must be no smaller than 6x 6

ONLINE- Documents and images are to be sent by email to
marketing@firehallartscentre.ca. We will not open your images if sent in an
attachment with an email. Please email a LINK to an online digital folder
(such as dropbox), containing a PDF or WORD documents, and JPEG format
image files.

MAIL- Submit up to 15 numbered images, on CD, 300 dpi, with .jpg
extension. List images as they appear on the CD.

PLEASE INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING

Brief exhibition proposal in WORD or PDF format " please outline
the plan for your exhibition, and why you believe your work is suitable for
the Firehall

Up to 15 images (high res, clear images)

List of images/titles/dimensions of work

CV

Artists Statement " 300 words max

Please email a digital folder link to: marketing@firehallartscentre.ca

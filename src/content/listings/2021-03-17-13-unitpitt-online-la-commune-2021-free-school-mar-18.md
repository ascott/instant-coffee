U/P Presents: LA COMMUNE 2021 Free School
From March 18th through early June 2021, writer and scholar Roxanne
Panchasi will
be hosting a virtual free school focused on the Paris Commune of 1871 in collaboration
with UNIT/PITT Society for Art and Critical Awareness.

Roxanne Panchasi is an Associate Professor of History at Simon Fraser University in
Vancouver, Canada who specializes in twentieth and twenty-first century France, the
French empire, and its legacies. Her first book, Future Tense: The Culture of
Anticipation in France Between the Wars was published by Cornell University Press in
2009. She has written on a range of subjects including: nineteenth-century graphology,
prosthetics, the Paris Commune, experimental film, and the teaching of History. Her
current book project focuses on the cultural politics of French nuclear weapons and
testing in the Algerian Sahara. In it, she examines military, scientific, diplomatic, literary,
audio-visual, testimonial, and memorial sources as a broad archive of the "French"
bomb in empire. Her most recent article, No Hiroshima in Africa: The Algerian
War
and the Question of French Nuclear Tests in the Sahara appeared in the Spring 2019
issue of History of the Present. In 2013, Dr. Panchasi launched New Books in French
Studies, a podcast channel featuring discussions with scholars of France and the
Francophone world about their latest books that she hosts on the New Books Network.

Roxanne Panchasi and UNIT/PITT are situated in Vancouver on the unceded
territories of the xmky"m (Musqueam), Swxw7mesh (Squamish), and
slil"wta" (Tsleil-Waututh) peoples. Given the current global pandemic, the free school
is planned as an entirely online/remote experience.

About LA COMMUNE 2021
Lasting 10-12 weeks, the free school will be open to the wider public. Anyone can
participate by taking in the content shared each week, by attending online events
(including screenings, discussions, and presentations by special guests, etc.).
Participants can follow the whole of the school's run and/or drop in to check out
specific materials or happenings on
offer. How much or how little of the free school
experience participants engage with is entirely up to them. Free in this instance means
without charge, but also without course/attendance requirements, assignments, grades
or participant evaluation of any kind.

The Content of LA COMMUNE 2021 will Consist of:
Weekly open access reading materials, including primary sources from the era of the
1871 Paris Commune, scholarly and other sources engaged with the Commune's
historical and political meanings, contemporary sources that reflect on or may be used
to think about connections between 1871 and 2021. A number of written materials will
be drawn from the following two open access sites:

Documents of the Paris Commune at marxists.org

The Anarchist Library

Audio-visual materials including film, video, and music featuring the Commune and/or
related events and themes.

Panel Discussions featuring scholars, artists, writers, and
historians

A series of weekly broadcasts under the rubric of radio 1871, including episodes with
special guests that discuss various aspects of the Paris Commune and its continued
significance 150 years later

The affiches: responses by artists-in-residence who will take in the content from the
free school simultaneously with participants

The LA COMMUNE 2021 Artists in Residence will be engaging alongside all Free

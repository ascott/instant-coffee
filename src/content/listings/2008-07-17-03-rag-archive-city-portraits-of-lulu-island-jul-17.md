Archive City: Portraits of Lulu Island
Lois Klassen, Cindy Mochizuki, Jaimie Robson 

17 July – 31 August, 2008
Opening Reception: Thursday 17 July, 7:00-9:00pm 

Richmond Art Gallery is pleased to host a project by Archive City, “a memory collection agency” 
composed of three Vancouver based artists, Lois Klassen, Cindy Mochizuki and Jaimie Robson. 
For their project Portraits of Lulu Island, Archive City members have researched and collected 
various facts and myths about Lulu Island, where the City of Richmond is situated. They have 
created a memory archive comprised of stories, images, ephemera, tracings and other related 
research materials, as well as archival boxes which contain the memories of Richmond residents 
they have interviewed in the form of dioramas.

Archive City explores how memory of a place is preserved and archived. By mimicking 
bureaucratic procedures and presentation methods, such as creating official-looking documents 
and an office setting in the gallery, the group members seek to expose the processes involved in 
translating traces of memory into concrete documents preserved in an archive.

Archive City will offers seminars to accompany each memory collection that they produce. For 
Portraits of Lulu Island, Archive City will be offering seminars that have people of all ages 
consider different processes of memory that include drawing, tracing, monument-building, taste 
and smell. They have created five seminars to be done individually, as a group, with family or 
friends. Drop in or to book an appointment contact RAG by phone at 247.8311. 

Archive City Memory Seminars: 
Thursdays in July & August from 11am - 1pm and on Saturdays, July 26 and August 23, from 
11am - 1pm. Archive City website www.archivecity.net will launch 17 July, 2008.

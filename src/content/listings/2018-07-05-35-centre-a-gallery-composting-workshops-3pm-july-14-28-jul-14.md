Composting Workshops by Candice Jee

July 14 and July 28, 3pm
Centre A
Gallery Hours: Tuesday - Saturday, 12-5pm

Centre A is now located at Unit 205-268 Keefer Street on the 2nd Floor.

This exhibition and workshop takes place on the unceded Coast Salish territories of the 
Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples.

As part of artist Candice Xiaobing Jees work for I know you are but what am I, an exhibition 
curated by Joni Cheung, Centre A will host two composting workshops entitled Choy Sum 
Field (How to make mishap into ground) on Saturday July 14 and July 28 at 3pm. The 
workshops are part of the installation piece Choy Sum Field, which consists of a makeshift 
garden used to grow choy sum throughout the exhibition. The choy sum seeds used were 
cultivated by the artist in Taipei, Taiwan by planting Malaysian seeds with compost made out 
of calligraphy students discards, as well as her own drafts and mistakes. Workshop 
participants will create fertile compost, using old calligraphy drafts and mistakes Jee has 
sent to Vancouver, Canada. 

This work is in conversation with two others; Many flowers have foreign names (after Yu 
Jing) " a light box sign mimicking Taipei shop signs, displaying poetry lines from a 
suspected ancestor from the Song Dynasty and Walkie Talkie (After Guan Daosheng) " a 
calligraphy scroll. Through this multi-part installation, Jee uses real and imagined lives and 
memories from her and others family history. Using installations to make place and 
meaning, she creates a space to reflect on cultural identity, migration, hybridization, mobility, 
and authenticity, as well as the recycling and mutability of culture. 

Please RSVP to programs@centrea.org as space is limited. We encourage participants to 
bring their own materials to compost, such as food scraps (but no meats, bones, or oily food 
scraps), dry leaves, tea leaves, coffee grounds, and their own old calligraphy drafts and 
mistakes, or other Chinese language paper material, such as newspaper.

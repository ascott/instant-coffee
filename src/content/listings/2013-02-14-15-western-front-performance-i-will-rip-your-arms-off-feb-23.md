I Will Rip Your Arms Off

A performance by Ieva Miseviciute on Saturday February 23rd at 8pm in the 
Luxe Hall at the Western Front.

Western Front Exhibitions presents a performance by Lithuanian artist Ieva 
Miseviciute, that started as a series of stand-up acts that have been 
abstracted through dance and free-floating characters leaving the original 
routines as occasional outbursts. The show consists of short acts, episodes, 
stage images, and sometimes intersecting narrative threads. In the world 
where the future is sideways, life can never be as sweet as the sponge.

This performance is part of Edible Glasses, an exhibition in which people and 
objects perform alongside, or in lieu of one another. Miseviciute will punctuate 
the exhibition by performing on the last day of the show. Come early to see 
the gallery exhibition which will close ten minutes before the performance.

Action Workshop

With Ieva Miseviciute on Tuesday, February 26th from 7pm to 10pm in the 
Luxe Hall.

In addition to her performance, Ieva Miseviciute will present an Action 
Workshop. This will encompass a number of exercises and techniques that 
come from Action Theater and some from action films. While it is true that 
most of the thinking and creating happens in our minds it is not true that the 
mind is stuck alone in the head and the only way to activate it is by sitting 
down and thinking. There are a number of physical and vocal ways to trick 
oneself into finding inspiration.

This workshop is designed with two purposes in mind: one, to trace and 
discover the repeating, habitual patterns in thinking, movement and speech; 
second, to provide the participants with a toolkit of exercises easily to be 
done on their own. The exercises are based on improvisation thus all physical 
abilities and levels of experience are welcome.

This workshop is free of charge but space is limited. To register please 
contact exhibitions@front.bc.ca with the subject heading Action Workshop.

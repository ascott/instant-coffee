Open Call for Artist-Initiated Projects

The City of Vancouver Public Art Program invites artists to submit their ideas for public 
art projects in any scale, scope, and medium.

We are looking for artists to propose meaningful pieces for public spaces in the city that 
reflect the complexities of place and publics, engage cultural practices, and provoke 
critical dialogue. 

The artist-initiated call is open to all artists, working individually or in collaboration. 
Artists and cultural practitioners of the Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh 
Nations are especially encouraged to apply.

Submission deadline: Thursday, June 6, 3pm PDT

For more information and to apply: https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/open-
call-for-artist-initiated-projects.aspx

The Robson Reading Series presents

Karen Houle and Rob Winger
Tuesday, June 3, 7 pm
UBC Bookstore Robson Square - 800 Robson Street, Plaza Level

Free admission

Karen Houle is an associate professor of philosophy at the University of Guelph. Houle's most recent book of poetry, During (Gaspereau Press), is a study of continuity, of being in process and of seeing through. Her first collection, Ballast, was nominated for the Gerald Lampert Award in 2001. Her essay "Double Arc" was published in Dropped Threads 2.

Rob Winger grew up in a tiny Ontario town, and has since lived in eastern Canada and Asia. His work has been published in literary journals across the country, and selections from his book Muybridge's Horse won first prize for poetry in English in the 2003 CBC Literary Awards judged by P.K. Page, Dionne Brand, and George Bowering. Muybridge's Horse, published by Nightwood Editions, was shortlisted for the 2007 Governor General's Award for Poetry. Winger lives with his family in Ottawa. 


The Robson Reading Series is an ongoing multi-genre series that features some of the finest writers from Canada and abroad. Events are organized in collaboration between the UBC Library and the UBC Bookstore at Robson Square. For a complete list of upcoming events, please visit our website at [http://www.robsonreadingseries.ubc.ca/](http://www.robsonreadingseries.ubc.ca/)

A bad image of good work is just as unhelpful as a good image of bad work.
Christine Morrow, Curator, Museum of Contemporary Art, Sydney

Who Should Attend
This workshop is aimed at artists looking for information on how to document their artwork. Both artists with no experience, and those who are not achieving the results they'd like, will benefit from this workshop.

The Workshop
Through demonstration and discussion, this workshop will introduce participants to the tools and techniques needed to successfully document two- and three-dimensional work to be used in submission packages. The techniques demonstrated are applicable to both film and digital formats.

What You Will Receive
Participants will receive a handout as well as an overview of information on camera types, lighting techniques, equipment and correct exposure to achieve professional looking results.

The Facilitator
Paulo Majano was born in El Salvador, and spent his early years in Mexico, the United States, and eventually Canada. Paulo has lived in Vancouver since 1992. Holding degrees from Concordia University and the University of British Columbia, Paulo  has taught painting and drawing courses at the University of B.C., The Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, and The Emily Carr Institute of Art & Design. His work is represented by Atelier Gallery in Vancouver. He has received the Elizabeth Greenshield Award for figurative art as well as Canada Council Production Grants for photography and media art.

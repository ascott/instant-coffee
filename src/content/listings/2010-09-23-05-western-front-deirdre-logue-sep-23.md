Deirdre Logue in Conversation with Judy Radul

Thursday September 23, 2010 | 6.30 PM
Location: Western Front, Luxe Hall, 303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver.

Please join us for an evening of conversation between Judy Radul and
current Western Front Media Artist-in-Residence Deirdre Logue. The
conversation will take selected works, both new and old, from Logue's
performance and video based practice as a point of departure.

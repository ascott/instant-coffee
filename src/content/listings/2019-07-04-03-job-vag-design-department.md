VANCOUVER ART GALLERY ASSOCIATION

1 TEMPORARY FULL-TIME POSITION AVAILABLE

Temporary Supervisor/Senior Designer - Design Department

Dates of Temporary Contract: July 2019 - October, 2020

Reporting to the Director of Operations and Museum Services (Director OMS), the 
Temporary

Supervisor and Senior Designer coordinates, directs, administers and reviews all 
aspects of the

design, production, preparation and installation of exhibit graphics and in-house signage 
for the

Design Department. Responsibilities include scheduling and delegating tasks to 
permanent and

casual staff; organizing and tracking timelines for design, production and installation in 
collaboration

with Gallery staff; and adhering to the Design Department budget as directed by the 
Director OMS.

The incumbent ensures all departmental projects are completed by the provided 
deadline, follow

Gallery standards and style guides, are completed accurately and are installed safely. 
The incumbent

ensures the feasibility of timelines and workload in collaboration with the design team 
and other

Gallery departments. The incumbent ensures the approval of all costs associated with 
the design,

production and installation of projects and coordinates departmental projects as 
required.

DUTIES

Delegates, prioritizes, assigns, tracks, monitors and organizes the workflow of Design 
department

staff in consultation with the Director of Operations and Museum Services based on 
requests from

Gallery staff and in consideration of the requirements and timelines of the department to 
ensure the

completion of design, production and installation on deadline while maintaining a high 
standard of

quality.

Creates, coordinates, develops and monitors design, production and installation 
timelines and

schedules for Gallery exhibition graphics and Gallery signage in collaboration with the 
design team,

Gallery staff and external contractors.

Assigns, reviews, edits, approves and ensures accuracy and quality of work done by 
Design

department staff and gives direction on priorities and goals for performance. Ensures 
staff

adhererence to the Gallery and departmental procedures, processes, and the design, 
production and

installation standards. Provides constructive feedback and coaching.

Attends all exhibition/event project, departmental and divisional planning meetings as 
related to the

Design department.

In consulation with and with direction from the Curatorial department and the Director of 
Operations

and Museum Services, the incumbent conceptualizes, formats, reviews, assigns and 
approves

exhibition graphic design projects through to installation. This can include design 
presentation

drawings, elevation drawings, floorplans, technical production drawings, 3D modeling 
and design

proofs.

Coordinates, directs and monitors the installation of exhibition graphics in collaboration 
with the

Curatorial department and external contractors and coordinates approvals from the 
Curatorial

department on final exhibit graphics placements and any changes or updates required 
while

ensuring the feasibility of the timelines for production and installation.

Coordinates and schedules the production of both exhibition and signage graphics with 
support from

the design team.

Assists in hiring new staff in consultation with the Director of Operations and Museum 
Services and

Human Resources department. Advises Director on requirements for additional staff 
work time

based on timelines and workload.

Participates in short range planning, purchasing and research for department during 
contract.

These duties and responsibilities outlined above are representative, but not all inclusive.

TRAINING, SKILLS, AND EXPERIENCE

Post secondary degree in design or related field plus 10 years of experience in a senior 
design or

design supervisory role, coordinating a minimum of 15 design projects per year in a 
multi-staffed

environment and supervising a minimum of 5 reporting staff members. Demonstrated 
experience of

design projects from concept through to final production required (portfolio.) Experience 
with

unionized environments an asset.

Excellent knowledge and skills with Adobe InDesign, Illustrator and Photoshop 
applications, Sketchup,

Filemaker, MS Office, the current Mac OS and an understanding of file management on 
a shared server

structure is required. Experience with designing for large format printing and die-cut 
vinyl is required.

Knowledge of graphics print and die-cut materials, production requirements and graphic 
installation

methods is required.

A proactive work ethic, excellent time management, analytical and communication skills, 
high attention

to detail, clear comprehension of a multifaceted workflow, and the ability to work 
effectively with others

in accomplishing projects with high production standards and compressed timelines is 
required.

SALARY: Pay Grade 21 Step 1 $26.03 per hour plus 10% in lieu of benefits

HOURS: Nine-day fortnight schedule

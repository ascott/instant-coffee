Colour and Weave: Introduction to Rigid Heddle Weaving
October 19, 26
9am-11am
$120
Three Bags Full, 4458 Main Street

For new weavers and those that have woven before, this project based class will tackle the
complex looking, but actually very straightforward, colour and weave stripe patterning.

Students will work on a pre-warped Ashford SampleIt loom while Amanda walks you through
basic weaving techniques. These techniques will include weaving vocabulary and finishing
options for your woven scarf. Students who have woven before will learn some
more
intermediate colour and weave techniques.

Over the course of two sessions youll make a beautiful scarf featuring GOTS certified
Venne cottolin in a palette similar to the ones shown in the sample. A small class size
means there will be lots of one on one to make sure everyone completes their work.

No previous weaving experience necessary!

Amanda Wood is a contemporary textile artist. Her practice is defined by processes and she
brings that approach to her weaving workshops.

For more information: [https://threebagsfull.ca/classes/colour-and-weave/](https://threebagsfull.ca/classes/colour-and-weave/)

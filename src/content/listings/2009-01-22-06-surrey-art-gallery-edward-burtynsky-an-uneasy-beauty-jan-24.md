Edward Burtynsky: An Uneasy Beauty - Photographs of Western Canada
Presented by Vancouver 2010 Cultural Olympiad and Surrey Art Gallery
This exhibition presents large format photographs, many never previously
exhibited in British Columbia. These photographs show both the vast
wilderness and impressive landscapes of western Canada, and the monumental
scale of the resource industries that underlie the Canadian economy. The
images are drawn from six series: Alberta Oil Sands, Container Ports,
Homesteads, Mines, Oil Fields and Railcuts, and represent over twenty years
of work. Continues to March 22.

John Wynne: Wireframe
Visitors will experience a three dimensional drawing created by sound with
the new installation by audio artist John Wynne. Wireframe is a
site-specific, 16.1 channel sound art installation. The precise
computer-controlled movement of sound traces the three dimensional form of
the exhibit space to create an immersive architectural sound drawing. With
the gallery darkened, visitors will hear - rather than see - the space in
which they find themselves. Continues to March 22.

Open Sound: Audio Art Projects
Open Sound is a new program developed by the Surrey Art Gallery that
supports the production and presentation of audio artforms as part of
contemporary art practice. The exhibition consists of a series of
commissioned audio art projects by Frederic Brummer, Ian Gregory James, Lee
Hutzulak and Giorgio Magnanensi that visitors will encounter throughout the
Surrey Arts Centre. Continues through 2009.

Glocal
The Glocal artist team has been busy hacking, coding and building systems
to manipulate how we make, view, and think about digital photography. Their
interactive exhibit, now in its second prototype phase, invites visitors to
experience thousands of photographs, made locally and globally, on a giant
touchable screen. The Glocal project is growing, and soon it will become
one of Canada's largest digital artworks. Glocal is one of Surrey's
Cultural Capitals of Canada initiatives. Continues to March 15.

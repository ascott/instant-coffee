Artists
Hotam Press Gallery is now accepting artwork for our upcoming exhibition COVER ME -
- Records & Cassettes by Artists.

From Nov 21, 2020 to Jan 31, 2021, Hotam Press Gallery will be transformed into a
record shop/discotheque/listening space. We would like to bring together artistss work
made with or inspired by music of all genres. Vinyl records, cassette tapes, and
paraphernalia are welcome.

Please send your well packaged work by mail to us, along with description and
biography. All accepted works will be available for sale on consignment (70/30) unless
otherwise arranged. Please note that submissions are accepted ongoingly until the end
of the exhibition (Jan 31, 2021).

For inquiries, our contact email is: hotampress @ gmail.com

Mirrored Hearts : Sophia Boutsakis

Opening: Friday, March 1, 7-9 PM
Exhibit Dates: March 2 - April 14th
Gallery Hours: Weekends, 12 - 4pm

City Center Artist Lodge

2111 Main St, Lobby

Sophia Boutsakis show, Mirrored Hearts, aims to explore the exchange of meaning
within our social tapestry. Through multiple mediums, including stained glass, metal
sculptures, delicate drawings, and intimately scaled paintings, the exhibition
contemplates relationships, intimacy, distance, memory, and childhood.

Boutsakis employs repeated motifs in each work
that reference architectural
barricades like railings, fences, and window coverings, traditionally used to both
protect and decorate spaces. Elements taken from these reference architectural
sources are extracted to become symbols within themselves. Hearts, diamonds,
stars, butterflies, bows and flowers hold their own context and narrative, suggesting
themes of love, nurturement, growth and strength.

Formally, Boutsakis navigates the tension between high art and hobbyist craft,
employing self-taught, amateur fabrication techniques and low brow materials such
as soldering, welding, stained glass, steel rod, galvanized wire, and chain.
Conceptually, the artist offers a nuanced exploration of how symbols and aesthetics
contribute to the construction of identity, particularly how decoration and
beautification are associated with the performance of gender and femininity.

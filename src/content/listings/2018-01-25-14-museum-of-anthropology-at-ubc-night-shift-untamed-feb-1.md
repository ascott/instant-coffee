The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC presents Night Shift: Untamed, an 
Indigenous burlesque night with Virago Nation and DJ Kookum on Thursday, February 
1, 2018 at 7 pm. MOA is bringing out the catwalk and turning up the heat with an 
evening of humour, seduction, and striptease that is guaranteed to reclaim and 
rematriate Indigenous sexuality in a big way! Night Shift is MOAs monthly cabaret party 
that reimagines the future of culture and social change.

Free with museum admission. For more information visit moa.ubc.ca/night-shift-
untamed/

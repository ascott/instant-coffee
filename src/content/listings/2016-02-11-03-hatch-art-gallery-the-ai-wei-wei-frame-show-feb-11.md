The Ai Wei Wei Frame Show
Untitled Art Group

Opening: February 11th at 7pm
Exhibition: February 8 - 26th, Monday-Friday 12 - 5pm
Hatch Art Gallery,
Vancouver, B.C.

The Untitled Art Group is a loose group of individuals that formed
out of UBC's Art History, Visual Art and Theory BA/BFA program. The impetus
for becoming a group came both from an awareness of the economic realities
in Vancouver for emerging artists as well as a goal to build a supportive
base from which to launch group shows and events in order to create a space
for ourselves within a Contemporary Art discourse.

The Ai Wei Wei Frame Show developed after the Morris and Helen Belkin
Gallery began selling frames from their exhibition Ai Wei Wei: New York
Photographs 1983-1993. The Untitled Art Group's Ai Wei Wei Frame Show aims
to engage in a conversation around the aura of celebrity art in general,
and in Vancouver, by physically linking our work with that of Ai Wei Wei as
emerging artists. There is no thematic or discursive association with Ai
Wei Wei's work, or between the works themselves necessarily, other than the
ready-to-hang frames which held snapshots of his time as a young man in New
York.

22. City of Vancouver | competition for the Viaducts and Eastern Core | NOV 4
The City of Vancouver announces the launch of "re:CONNECT"
an open ideas competition for the Viaducts and Eastern Core

The Eastern Core stretches from Main Street in the west to Clark Drive in the 
east, Prior in the North and Great Northern Way in the south, and some of the 
immediately adjacent boundary areas. Housing much of Vancouver's industrial 
community, the area also houses artists, production studios and workshops, 
multi-tenant studio buildings and the industries that support artists and the 
cultural community.

The purpose of the competition is to open the ideas generation process to the 
public, cultural and design community. While no decisions on the future of the 
viaducts or eastern core are being made through re:CONNECT, fresh, new 
ideas from citizens and design professionals will spark dialogue and help inform 
and inspire planning for this part of Vancouver. A planning process for the 
Viaducts and the Eastern Core is now underway with a high level strategy 
expected in late spring 2012.

The competition will run through November 4th. Please refer to the website: 
www.vancouver.ca/reconnect for more details and to register for the competition.

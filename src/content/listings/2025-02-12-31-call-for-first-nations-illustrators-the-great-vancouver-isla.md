Call for First Nations Illustrators - The Great Vancouver Island Land Grab

The Vancouver Island Local History Society and the Nanaimo Museum have
partnered to develop a travelling exhibit about the history and legacy of the
Esquimalt & Nanaimo Railway land grants. The development team seeks one or
more First Nation artists to work with them to develop artworks for the exhibit.

We welcome applications from First Nations artists who are interested in
collaborating with us to tell the Great Land Grab story. Preference will be given to
artists from First Nations whose territory is impacted by the E&N Railway Land Grant
on Vancouver Island.

There are four
opportunities for First Nation artwork to be incorporated into the
exhibit. Artists can apply to produce one artwork or all of them. Please indicate in
your application which artwork(s) you are interested in producing.
1. Intro panel / Title artwork
2. Illustrated interpretive panels
3. Illustrated map of the Land Grant
4. One contemplative illustration

We are looking for professional artists to take part in this opportunity. Selected
artist(s) will be paid fairly, following professional standards.

COMBINE Art Fair returns for its third year, featuring two new Vancouver-based
galleries Afternoon Projects and Pale Fire, and Los Angeles based Royale Projects
alongside founders Griffin Art Projects, Monica Reyes, Unit 17 and WAAP.
COMBINE is unique for its collaborative, lateral leadership model that offers space
for dialogue between local and international galleries, by invitation only. In 2023,
COMBINE convenes aspiring and experienced collectors who are interested in
learning about vibrant artists shaping global art scenes, and offers a platform to
converse with gallerists in a convivial boutique environment.

Combine Art Fair gratefully acknowledges the support of Acera Insurance in the
presentation of this project.

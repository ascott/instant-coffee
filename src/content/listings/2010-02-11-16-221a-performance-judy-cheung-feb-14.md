-ISOMORPHISM -- CONVERGENCE 2010,

Performance: 8:00pm

Isomorphism - Convergence 2010 is a performative installation
engaging two distinct but intersect paths that reflect urban
navigation, spatial experience and state of being while exploring
aspects of connection, disconnection, mobility (staying/leaving) and
decision-making. The introspective walk concurrently functions as a
mind-expanding exercise. While we celebrate the spirit of
sportsmanship during the Winter Olympic games within Vancouver, a
large number of women remain missing. Were it not for the efforts of a
few, their names might have been forgotten.

Also presenting REACT2010
The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is engaging artists and the
global community in a creative cultural conversation about the values
surrounding the Olympics. We invite you to join us for an entertaining
and engaging experience showcasing photography, video, drawing,
painting, poetry and other artistic expressions of the modern day
Olympic Games which we hope will lead to an examination and
appreciation of diverse cultural values.

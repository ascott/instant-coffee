SLaM 2024 Open Call

Boca del Lupo is now accepting applications for our 4th annual SLaM creative
development, mentorship and residency program. Join the roster of innovative projects
that have gone on to further funding, final production as well as national and
international presentation.

SLaM provides support for the early stages of creative development. SLaM can assist
artists in their early stage of artistic exploration, creating projects suitable for project
funding applications to final development. Support is available in the form of a financial
stipend, mentorship, professional development opportunities, and/or residency or use
of space.

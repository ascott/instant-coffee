Artists in the Classroom Mentorship Salon: Presented by ArtStarts in
Schools, Hosted by Elfred Matining

Thursday, May 5, 2016
Doors open at 6pm, Program runs 6:30pm-9pm
ArtStarts in Schools, 808 Richards St, Vancouver, BC
Questions? Contact Emily at emily@artstarts.com

Are you an artist with experience or interest in working with young
people? Are you looking for ways to connect with like-minded artists in
your community? Ever wonder if mentorship might help you advance
your career? We want to hear from you!

Join us for an evening of connection and dialogue with a diverse group
of like-minded artists working in a variety of disciplines. Through a
series of energetic and thought-provoking activities, we will share
experiences, reframe traditional models and ideate possibilities for
building a local community of mentorship for artists in the classroom.
This event is one of five salons being held across the province that is
contributing to ArtStarts' development of a new project to support
artists in the classroom.

About ArtStarts in Schools: The Artists in the Classroom Mentorship
Salon is presented by ArtStarts in Schools, a not-for-profit organization
that provides innovative arts programs for young people, practical
resources for teachers and artists, and leadership in advocacy for arts
in education. Learn more at artstarts.com or
http://artstarts.com/blog/aic-mentorship-salons-invitation

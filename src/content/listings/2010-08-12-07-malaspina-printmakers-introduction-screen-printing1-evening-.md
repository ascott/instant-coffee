This workshop provides a thorough introduction to monotype printmaking.
Often referred to as the "painterly" print, this direct method can involve

Instructor: Andrea Pinheiro

This course provides an introduction to preparing plates and printing
Photolithographs. Originally developed for off-set printing, the sensitized
aluminum plates used for photo litho can hold a considerable range of
detail and tone. Photolithography is an excellent way to print both
drawings and photographic images, and the plates can be printed on both
intaglio and litho presses. This course is intended for those with some
previous printing experience.

Introduction to Collography

November 6 - 27, 1 â€“ 5 pm Saturdays

Course Fee: $190 Material Fee: $15

Instructor: Molly Winston

This course provides a thorough introduction to collograph printmaking. A
collograph is a collaged plate in which the surface is built up using a
variety of mediums. Collographs can achieve dramatic tonal effects and
vibrant colours due to the depth of relief and differential inking that
results from the collograph plate's highly textured surface. This 4-day
workshop will cover building a collograph plate, inking techniques such as
relief, intaglio and viscosity, and creating prints using multiple plates
and colours.

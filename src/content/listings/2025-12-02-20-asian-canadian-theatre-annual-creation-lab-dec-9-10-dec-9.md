Vancouver Asian Canadian Theatre's annual Creation Lab Readings return to the
Roundhouse next week! The Creation Lab is vActs new work development program. Two
brand new works-in-progress are shown over the course of two days.

"... December 9-10
Doors at 6:30PM Show at 7:00PM
" 181 Roundhouse Mews, Vancouver
Free tickets at www.zeffy.com/en-CA/ticketing/vact-creation-lab--2025

Featuring:

On The Tracks
by Tanya Banerjee
Tuesday, December 9 at 7pm
Largely set on the train in present-day Greater Vancouver, On The Tracks follows Meena
and her mom, Radhika, who are on their way for a fun day in Gastown to celebrate Meenas
university graduation. When Radhika receives a call from Mumbai, the trajectory of their
journey takes a sharp turn: beloved Rajen Mama, Meenas uncle and Radhikas older
brother, has died. Meena and Radhika each grapple with this devastating news in their own
ways, and eventually, together. On The Tracks explores loss and grief, memory, time, and
hope, while experimenting with sound and music.

Draw Me a Home
by elika mojtabaei
Wednesday, December 10 at 7pm
Content warning: The presentation this evening contains references and images of war and
violence, as well as references to childhood experiences as shaped by these events.

Draw Me a Home is a poetic retelling of memories of a war, surrealist stories of lives lived
elsewhere, a peeling away of the dust blanketing a shared history. Based on interviews with
people who experienced the Iran-Iraq War (1980-1988) as children, the piece explores
themes of displacement, survival, and the echoes of a fractured homeland. Through
fragmented narratives swaying between English and Farsi, Draw Me a Home evokes the
resilience of a people caught between the violence of history and the tenderness of personal
remembrance with moments of joy buried just beneath the rubble"an invitation to witness,
imagine, and feel the weight of a past that is both distant and ever-present.

Calling all artists! Taking applications now for 2025 shows. Submit your proposal for an
art
exhibition at the Crescent Beach Pop-Up Gallery.

- Find all details here: https://www.surrey.ca/arts-culture/community-art/crescent-
beach-pop-gallery/crescent-beach-pop-gallery-artist-call

- Deadline to Apply: December 1, 2024

- Gallery rental is offered at no charge to selected applicants

- Exhibitions are open to groups of two or more, with at least one of the artists
being a Surrey resident. This opportunity is open to artists from all disciplines, levels,
and backgrounds.

The Crescent Beach Pop-Up Gallery is in the heart of Crescent Beach. The gallerys
purpose is to support Surrey artists and build connections with local community.

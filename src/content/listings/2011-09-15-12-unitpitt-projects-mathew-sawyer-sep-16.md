In 1983, UNIT/PITT presented the Wrong Wave Festival, featuring artists who
made music and bands that made art. Twenty-eight years later UNIT/PITT has
invited The Music Appreciation Society to present Wrong Wave 2: Art rock is
dead/long live art rock. The Music Appreciation Society is an ongoing
project of Kathy Slade and Brady Cranfield.

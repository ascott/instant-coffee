The Richmond Art Gallery is seeking a new Director. Application deadline is
August 23, 2016.

The Richmond Art Gallery (RAG) is a public gallery operated in partnership
between the City of Richmond and the Richmond Art Gallery Association
(RAGA). The vision of the RAG is "to be one of the most important public
galleries in Canada and to increasingly enlarge our audiences and their
understanding and enjoyment of contemporary art".

The Gallery is currently looking for an energetic and innovative individual
who has the keen ability to multi-task and communicate effectively to join
the team as the Director of the RAG. Reporting to the Manager, Arts
Services, the Director works closely and collaboratively with fellow staff
and the RAGA Board to set the direction and overall vision for the RAG, and
to set goals to attain this vision. The Director of the Richmond Art
Gallery is responsible for directing the activities and operation of the RAG.

RICHMOND ART GALLERY
PREPARATOR
Auxiliary Position

The Richmond Art Gallery is a public gallery, owned and operated by the
City of Richmond. We are looking to increase our current pool of qualified
Preparators for periodic employment to assist with the installation and
de-installation of exhibitions.

Preparators performs skilled technical work involving art handling and
movement, and installation of a wide variety of exhibitions. Preparators
perform work to various degrees of specialization in the following areas:
art handling; condition reporting; mount making; packing and crating;
matting and framing; installing two and three dimensional and new media art
works and exhibitions; carpentry; the installation and maintenance of AV
equipment; installation and troubleshooting of information and computer
technology in exhibition and displays; performing other related tasks as
needed.

REQUIREMENTS

· Completion of two years minimum course work in the visual arts and
one or more relevant trades relevant to the work

· A minimum of two to three years experience and knowledge in one or
more of the following areas required: experience working in a museum or
gallery and the techniques of safe handling and movement of works of art
(required of all incumbents); packing and crating of artworks; mount
making; techniques, materials and methods used in the installation of two
and three-dimensional works of art; standard practices, methods and skills,
materials, variety of tools and equipment for carpentry and cabinet making.

· Ability to install and maintain computer hardware and software in
exhibition and perform basic hardware and software troubleshooting.

· Ability to lift and move heavy objects, communication and teamwork
skills required.

· Demonstrated understanding of hazards and necessary safety
precautions involved in the work.

· Valid B.C. Driver’s License; agree to become certified to drive
City vehicle.

· Candidate must pass and maintain a criminal and police record search.

SALARY: $24.11 12% in lieu of benefits
HOURS: Hours may include weekends and evenings. This is an on-call casual
position.
Closing Date: November 15, 2014

Please send your resume to the Curator: ncapogna@richmond.ca

7700 Minoru Gate
Richmond BC V6Y 1R9

Call for Proposals from artists enrolled in MFA, MVS, MA, and PhD programs:

Projects Class 2010

University of Toronto Art Centre

Curated by Ginger Scott and Joanna Sheridan
August 31 - November 6, 2010

Projects Class 2010 is a series of art-making events, workshops, and
performances that re-enact the structure of David Askevold's Projects Class
that began in 1969 at the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design, Halifax.
As well as being a unique moment in Canadian art education, the Class was
at the epicentre of the conceptual art heyday at the small Nova Scotia
university. Askevold's class built an international reputation by
attracting conceptual artists including Sol LeWitt, Joseph Beuys, Robert
Barry, Lawrence Weiner, and Lucy Lippard, who submitted instruction-based
projects for students to perform and create.

Running parallel to UTAC's portion of the multi-site exhibition Traffic:
Conceptualism in Canada c. 1965 to 1980, Projects Class 2010 invites
artists working in MFA, MVS, MA, and PhD programs to submit instructional
artworks for undergraduate and post secondary visual arts students to
realize. We will accept submissions that operate as extensions of the
artworks completed in Askevold's historic class or new works that operate
within the same conceptualist spirit.

The full set of 1969 projects can be viewed at:
http://www.ccca.ca/resources/searches/event_detail.html?languagePref=en&vk=7539.

Instructions for artworks must be considerate of time (occurring in a
duration that is within the two month period of the exhibition) with
participating class sizes being no more than 20 students at any given time.

SUBMISSIONS: DUE JUNE 18, 2010

Please include: Title, instructions, contact information, and the name of
your school and program.

Artists must be current students.

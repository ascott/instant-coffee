Burnaby Mountain Honour Stand 

Friday and Saturday, November 29 and 30, 12-5pm 
SFU Gallery, Burnaby
Academic Quadrangle 3004 

The Tent Shop will host a two-day art sale in collaboration with SFU Gallery on Burnaby Mountain.

An old canvas wall tent will act as a backdrop supporting a collection of framed works. These paintings and drawings by lesser known artists will be available for $20 each in an honour stand format (select artwork, deposit money, take your art work). A few of these works might look nice over your mantel, most will look better over your parent's mantel and all of them will look great over your grandparent's mantel. 

Developed by Vancouver based artist and facilitator Jacob Gleeson, The Tent Shop is a divergent project comprised of an online gallery/store, portable event space, screening room and a workshop. These environments act alternately as accessible outlet, migrating venue, private theatre and production studio. The intent is to provide various informal settings for exploring a range of interests including but not limited to; art and ephemera, unique objects,16mm film, field recordings, readings, local history, nature, discourse, card games, tea and experimental commerce. Previous events have been held at Western Front (40th anniversary), VIVO (Institutions for Artists Event), TOQUE, Hardscrabble Gallery, Solder & Sons and Sight Unseen. 

The Tent Shop will live near the entrance of SFU Gallery, sharing the gallery space with Samuel Roy-Boi's exhibition Not a new world, just an old trick, on display until December 14th, 2013.

Vanessa Brown: Shadow Cast
March 8 – April 5, 2015

Opening reception Sunday, March 8th, 2-5 PM

Hardscrabble Gallery is very happy to open a new set of works by Vanessa Brown.

For her show at Hardscrabble, Brown has continued her engagement with the
surface of steel and oil paints. The four works in the exhibition do not
hide the hand painted qualities of their colour, with brush strokes and
obvious worked gestures on the surface. The steel displays these multiple
washes in a unique way, as layers of paint begin to resemble rough skin or
hide. This is all done around a flurry of figurative and abstracted shapes,
cut directly from the sheets, creating a relief.

The shapes evoke family crests or coats of arms, scribbled into the cover
of youthful notebooks. Brown is working through a relationship with the
illustrative in art making, mixing patterns and naïve shapes. Her aim is
not to deliver through the icon, instead leaning on the quality of the line
to draw the viewer into initially making connections between the individual
figures within each work, then as a whole.

The scale of the works in the exhibition is modest in size, seeking out the
traditional sculptural ethos of balance and form. Conscious of teetering on
the edge of monumental works from the high modernist era from the 1960’s
and 70’s, Brown bends those ideals of industry and display to fulfill her
own autobiographical and representational needs. Sketching by making,
Brown’s process contains no eraser, no undo. Instead of postproduction,
Brown has focused, absolute lines, unable to be mended or repaired. The
finished products have a spark of chance in their creation.

Brown could be seen as a steel butcher, slapping down some small cutlets
from a larger industry, composed from the pull of gravity and pressing
points. The figures and shapes reveal her escaping allusions; forms appear
then recess back into the wall. However she subtracts her figures from the
support, a very sharp knife is used.

Vanessa Brown is a Vancouver based artist. She has recently exhibited at
Field Contemporary (with Deirdre McAdams), Vancouver, and has an upcoming
engagement later this month at Erin Stump Projects (with Collen Heslin),
Toronto.

DEADLINE: March 15th, 2016

Gallery 295 is pleased to announce a call for proposals for INDEX 
2016, our Annual Juried Exhibition for Emerging Artists.

We encourage all emerging artists who work through photography to 
complete a submission. Gallery 295 embodies all aspects of 
photographic engagement within contemporary art practices, and we 
seek submissions that push on the limits of photography as well as 
those that maintain a stead-fast photographic tradition.

INDEX 2016 Invited Jurors: Althea Thauberger (Artist & Film Maker), 
Meredith Preuss (Program Director, Capture Photography Festival), 
and Shaun Dacey (Curator, Contemporary Art Gallery).

Please email the following information to info@gallery295.com with 
"INDEX 2015 - Submission" in the title of the email.

Applications Must Include: 
- Images of current work (10-20 images)
- Image list
- Supporting project statement (300 words max)
- Artist Bio (100 words max)
- CV

Application DEADLINE is Tuesday March 15th, 2016.

Please contact info@gallery295.com or visit www.gallery295.com for 
further information.

INDEX 2016 Emerging Artist Juried Exhibition runs from June 10th to 
July 22nd, 2016.

**This exhibition is open to artists currently residing in Vancouver and 
surrounding areas.

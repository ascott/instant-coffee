The Ground Floor Art Centre is pleased to announce UNDERSTORY, on view 
November 15 to 18, 2018.

Understory is a group show interpreting the tilts and transformations of the natural world. 
Through a variety of material approaches, edges come into focus, apparitions are 
conjured, and luminous hauntings are revealed once more.

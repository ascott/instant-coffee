Scott Kemp
An Upper Middle Class Fantasy, 2017
Feb 07 - Mar 07, 2017

...the red nylon of your jacket crumples, its neck pushing up and obscuring your 
mouth. 
I lean over and dip my still-naked right hand in the water, cupping my fingers to 
lift out some of the liquid. 
You shift your weight and fall against me gently. 
The water drains from my hand...

Scott Kemp's An Upper-Middle Class Fantasy opens on a view of skiers gliding 
through a mountainous snowscape. This scene weaves itself within the text of a 
narrative set in the jacuzzi bathtub of a ski-in ski-out resort, where two unnamed 
individuals share intimate but cumbersome moments together.

Kemp's video/text piece uses static video shots and writing techniques borrowed 
from erotic fantasy to layer two separate but not dissimilar scenes of corporeal 
exchange. In both instances natural elements are encountered in designed 
environments, and the experiences of the subjects are mediated by technology. 

Scott Kemp is a visual artist based in Vancouver, Canada. His art practice 
explores modern human experience and mechanisms that augment or confuse it. 
His work has existed widely as sculpture, drawing, text, painting, performance, 
and digital illustration. Kemp was a member of Avenue, an artist-run exhibition 
space that operated in downtown Vancouver from 2013 to 2015 and in 2016 
helped establish Duplex, an exhibition space focused on supporting emerging 
artists and experimental projects in Vancouver. Solo exhibitions include Master 
and Apprentice, Lobster and Leaf, Duplex, Vancouver, BC, 2016; Major 
Appliance, Index, Vancouver, BC, 2014. Group exhibitions include Soil Gallery, 
Seattle, Washington, 2017; Flux Gallery, Winnipeg, Manitoba, 2015; 221a, 
Vancouver, BC, 2015; Or Gallery, Vancouver, BC, 2014. 

After Hours is a series of projections presented in READ Books bookstore 
window. For this project, Artists, designers, writers, musicians and curators are 
invited to present video work, select films, create YouTube playlists or put 
together slideshows to be screened from sunset until midnight.

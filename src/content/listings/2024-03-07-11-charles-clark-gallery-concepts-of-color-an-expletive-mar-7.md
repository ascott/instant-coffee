OPENING Thursday March 7th - 7pm - 9pm

Concepts of Color; an Expletive

Solo show exhibiting
local Artist - James Welk

Charles Clark Gallery

1345 Clark Drive

Show runs March 7th - April 3

Through my work I seek to mimic abstraction and figuration through a system of dense
compositions of shape and marbled colour. I work to develop this through the use of
abstracted or analogous forms that relate to content which is not directly explicative but
a pretence of reality.

Do you ever stair at the ceiling spackle as day breaks and see images meandering
above your head? Like this, I want you to find what I see.

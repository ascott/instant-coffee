Ciara Phillips
Cold Friends, Warm Cash
An exhibition & print shop for kids

Opening: Thursday, March 17, 2016 @ 7pm
Exhibition: March 18 - April 30, 2016
Gallery Hours: Tuesday-Saturday, 12-5pm
Free Admission

Cold Friends, Warm Cash, an iteration of Phillips' ongoing project
Workshop (2010 - ), takes over the gallery as a site of both production
and exhibition. Using the physical space of the institution and its
supporting infrastructure, Phillips will turn the gallery into a simple
screen printing studio which will serve as a place for discussion
mediated through the process of making things together. Situated
within the context of her own prints, Phillips will be using this exhibition
and residency to develop new artworks, placing the interests of
children at the heart of the project. Local kids aged 6 - 10 years old are
invited to visit the gallery to learn how to screen print collaboratively
with Phillips to create posters, magazines and printed fabrics.

Collaborative Screen Printing Times for Kids:
All printing sessions are FREE including all supplies and materials.
Advance booking is essential as we can only host 8 participants per
session. To register, email exhibitions@front.bc.ca. First come, first
served. Participants are encouraged to come to multiple sessions.
Dates: March 22nd - April 23rd 2016
Printing times:
Tuesdays & Thursdays, 4 - 5:30pm
Saturdays, 10am - 12 noon

Artist Biography:

Ciara Phillips was born in Ottawa, Canada and went to Queen's
University before she completed a Masters degree at the Glasgow
School of Art (GSA) in 2004. She has been nominated for the UK
Turner Prize 2014 and recent solo shows include Warm Friends, Cold
Cash, Konsthall C, Stockholm; Workshop, The Showroom Gallery,
London; and Funktion / Dysfunktion, Neues Museum Nrnberg. Phillips
is the founder of the artist collective Poster Club.

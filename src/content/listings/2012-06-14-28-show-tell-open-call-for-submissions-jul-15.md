Open Call for Submissions

Show & Tell is a new publication and distribution house, which aims to 
help keep the turning page alive. Our goal is to generate further 
interest in the book arts and to foster a more collaborative, hands on 
approach to the process of publication. In early August, Show & Tell 
will launch the first edition of what will become a quarterly publication. 
In addition, Satellite Gallery has granted us the opportunity to launch a 
pop up shop in the gallery's bookstore, as well as a storefront reading 
library and zine-making space this summer. We would like to extend 
the opportunity to artists that may wish to sell their own zines or 
catalogues in the Show and Tell pop up shop. 

What we will need from you:

1. Your name and telephone number
2. A one sheet explaining your zine
3. Photographs of the work you are interested in selling
4. Website or link to online examples of work
5. How you heard about the call for submissions
6. Short bio, including a description of your practice and where your 
work has been presented (prior exhibitions and inclusion in book fairs 
not required)
8. Please include the cost/retail cost of each book you're submitting for 
the pop up shop

Satellite Gallery is not directly affiliated with the open call process, so 
please refrain from contacting the gallery directly. 

Send your submissions digitally to Showtellpress@gmail.com.

Work must be well crafted and clean. We will accept submissions on 
the basis that the digital information provides this, but if the hard copies 
appear otherwise submission acceptance is subject to change. 

Deadline for submissions: July 15th, 2012 at midnight.

We are very excited to hear from everyone!

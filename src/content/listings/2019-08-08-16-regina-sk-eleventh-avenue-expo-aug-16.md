Eleventh Avenue Expo
2417 11th Avenue 
Regina, SK Treaty 4 Territory
August 16 - 18, 2019

Eleventh Avenue Expo is a weekend long exhibition and sale of artist multiples, printed 
matter, ephemera and artists' books located on 11th Avenue in Regina, SK, Treaty 4 
Territory. Presenting over 40 artists from across Canada and abroad, Eleventh Avenue 
Expo brings together a variety of work from emerging to established contemporary 
artists for an exciting 3 day long exhibition in downtown Regina. 

Featuring the work of: Black Flash Editions (Joi T.Arcand, Jayce Salloum, and Lori 
Blondeau), Rebecca Casalino, Ava Cantharis, Jane Cheng, Hilarey Cowan, Patrick 
Cruz, Hannah Doucet, Lucien Durey & Katie Kozak, Blair Fornwald, hannah_g, Fanny 
Gicquel, Daniel Griffin Hunt, Nestor Kruger, Jera Macpherson, Jeff Meldrum, Colin 
Miner, Sheila Nadimi, Julie Oh, Sophia Oppel, Claire Paquet, Amber PB, Zo Schneider, 
Shit Fireplace, Emma Welch, Jon Vaughn, and more!

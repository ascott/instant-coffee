Exhibition Opening
Contemporary Art Practices

Saturday, September 8, 6-9pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5

Join us to celebrate the opening of Contemporary Art Practices, an
exhibition culminating a three-week visual arts intensive hosted by the
Contemporary Art Gallery and Arts Umbrella.

Designed for young artists between the age of 15-19 interested in
developing their visual art practice, this intensive focused on
experimenting and pushing the boundaries of the participants own art making.

Students had the opportunity to work with leading artists, curators, and
educators as they explored a range of contemporary art practices and
approaches to exhibition making. Participants engaged in critiques and
discussions about developing ideas, working with materials, and viewing
works of art.

Generously supported by Lesley Stowe and Geoffrey Scott.

Ground Floor is 100% volunteer-run and relies on fundraising initiatives and donations 
to support our programming and keep our doors open. For the last year, we have held 
art exhibitions, workshops, concerts, festivals, clothing and supply swaps, and craft 
sales, as well as provided space for auditions, rehearsals, tutoring, fundraisers, and 
studio work for over 100 emerging art practitioners in Vancouver. Your support directly 
benefits the upcoming year of more ambitious programming and events!

Education: Collage and Mixed Media, Fridays, May 30 - June 27, from 10:00 am -
Noon, at Roundhouse Community Arts and Recreation Centre
ID Code: 551686
Call: (604) 713-1800
Get together with new friends and make art and have Fun chatting to each other as
you cut, tear, paste, glue various media at this hands-on social activity for all levels of
interest. Collage is a way of art making that embraces combining a wide variety of
everyday materials from photographs, magazines, fabrics, buttons, ribbons, everyday
items, etc. The materials can be anything including what you can find in your recycle to
assemble images on cardboard or paper. We will use glue sticks, glue, and scissors or
will tear paper to adhere to the surface. Individual attention is given every second to
encourage personal growth. Open to all skill levels. Supply list will be provided at
registration. Let go of perfection and trust the creative process with Faye Fayerman

First Thursdays: Traditional Squamish Dance Performance | 6-8PM | August 1

Join us on August 1 for the First Thursdays Art Walk to explore art in Ambleside and 
Dundarave. Enjoy our series focusing on Indigenous culture sharing, take a self-guided tour 
of other local galleries, public art and community spaces, discover and celebrate visual art 
and local artists and create a collaborative community art piece that will be unveiled in 
August!

First Thursdays: Weave a Cedar Bark Friendship Bracelet with Todd Devries (Giihlgiigaa)
August 1, 6"8 p.m.
Create a traditional friendship bracelet using twisted cedar bark. You will learn the basics of 
preparing the bark, custom sizing your bracelet and threading on a bead.

The workshop will take place in a large tent on the lawn just west of the gallery.

Cost: $5 (material fee)

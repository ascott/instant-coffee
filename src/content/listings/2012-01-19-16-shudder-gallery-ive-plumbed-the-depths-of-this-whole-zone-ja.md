I’ve Plumbed the Depths of this Whole Zone is a group exhibition 
featuring Jeff Halladay, Lee Henderson, Michelle Jukic, and Shay 
Semple. We’re done with any sort of lengthy exegesis regarding the 
meaning and purpose of our art. We’ve been down that toilet before 
and it’s boring. This show represents the last remaining vestiges of our 
mutant community that hasn’t packed up and vacated the sewer system 
known as Vancouver for more saxophone oriented cities like New York. 
We have stayed, in the sub-basements and the abandoned tenements. 
Studying, slowly growing in power, becoming victims of our erudition. 
This exhibition represents the last sewer blast and testament to the 
forgotten sub-prole of the outer zones. Non-escapist drawing to conflict 
by or with.
Jeffro Halladay (b. 1973) grew up in Duncan, British Columbia, Canada 
and studied art at Emily Carr Institute of Art & Design in Vancouver, 
where he currently lives and works. A multi-media artist, Halladay works 
from a layered history of drawing, painting and illustration, influenced 
by science-fiction, horror, comic-art, mid-evil times, cartoons, 
printmaking, skateboarding, psychedelia, thrift & gutter street culture 
and the supernatural. Previous projects include: Is it Today Already?, 
Delusional Bros Incorporated, Mental Msias, World War 3 (Nog-A-Dod, 
pre-historic Canadian psychodoodlia), Nine Winds, Flea Studies, The 
Grassy Knoll Association, Zero, #1 Kitchen, Bad Manors, Bogus Tokus, 
Klown Mag, Swamp Donkey (Hillbilly films), Blotto, S.T.R.E.E.T.S., 
Future World, Curse of the Thrift, Blastramp, Pearl, Harvey Spannos, 
Machotaildrop, Fog Bum, Bang Master Generals Warning, Beyond the 
Black Rainbow & "Chains" the movie.

Lee Henderson has written two books, The Broken Record Technique, 
a collection of short stories, and The Man Game, a novel, both 
published by Penguin Canada. Lee also writes regularly on visual art 
for Border Crossings and The Walrus, and has a curated a few art 
exhibits and experimental music events. He recently left Vancouver and 
now lives in Victoria.

Michelle Jukic (b.1983) was born and raised in an East Vancouver 
Special. A self-taught artist, Michelle started by drawing scenes from 
the Bible, most notably illustrating the story of the Ten Lepers. This 
began a lifelong preoccupation with all things weird, fantastic and 
absurd. Her artistic practice reached maturity under the tutelage of the 
Delusional Brothers when asked to participate in the illustrating of Flea 
Studies; an ongoing anthropological exploration of flea market culture 
in Vancouver. A multimedia artist, her projects include script 
development/story boarding and art design for film as well as vocal 
projections in the minimalist electronic music/performance art duo 
Judgement Day. She is currently working on illustrating an as yet 
undisclosed science-fiction/noir graphic novel project with a notable 
author. 

Shay Semple is a Sub-prole basement dweller living in a frontal, 
massive, chiaroscuro world. A Vancouver artist who is currently on the 
lamb from the G-men and trying to stay one step ahead of the law. He 
works primarily in drawing but his diverse practice also includes literary 
experimentation, provocations, satirism in the Swiftian tradition, firearms 
enthusiasm, character assassinations, Beckett-soid depth plumbing, 
and Quixotic flights to the outer zones . He has shown work in 
Vancouver, Toronto, New York, Tokyo, etc. etc. Shay has never asked 
for or received grant money from the Canada council of the arts.

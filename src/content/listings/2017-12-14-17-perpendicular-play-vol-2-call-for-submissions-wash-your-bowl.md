Perpendicular Play is an ongoing collaborative zine series run by the Vancouver-
based This is What arts collective. We are now accepting submissions for our 
second volume, on the theme Wash Your Bowl.

DETAILS:

An old Zen Buddhist fable tells of a monk who travelled a great distance to study at 
the remote mountain monastery of the venerable master Chao-chou. The morning 
after his arrival, the monk got the opportunity to meet Chao-chou face-to-face.

I am new at this monastery, said the monk. Please tell me, what is the key to the 
Buddha way?

Have you been served breakfast? asked Chao-chou.

Yes, I have, replied the monk.

Wash your bowl, said Chao-chou. The monk understood.

FORMAT:

Work on paper: vignettes, photography, waka, creative non-fiction, water color, 
collage, poetry, prose, line drawingsanything capable of achieving tangible reality 
on a two-dimensional printed page. Think outside the bowl; we love to be surprised! 
Work must be submitted in digital format.

DEADLINE: 

All submissions must be received by midnight on December 31st, 2017. For more 
information on the submission process, visit:

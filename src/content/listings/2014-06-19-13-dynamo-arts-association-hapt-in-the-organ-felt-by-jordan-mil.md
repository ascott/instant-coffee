Hapt in the Organ Felt

June 26th @ Dynamo Arts Association 30 E. 6th AVE
Opening reception 7pm

From here you can look around for a while 
Then maybe over there 
You wonder what beyond that 
But slowly
You must take your time 
Pace
Always in the mind
An immanent rhythm is present 
And is about all that it can be 

Recent work by Jordan Milner
Curated by Tommy Chain

Little Mountain Gallery

LILAC LANG: Pictures of Something

April 21st-May 10th 2011

Opening Thursday April 21st 7-10 PM

Pictures of Something features new paintings by Lilac Lang. Started 
during a recent artist residency with Spark Box Studio in Picton, 
Ontario, her latest series seeks to further the investigation of 
abstract painting while loosely exploring its relationship with 
advertisement, design, and photography. Referring to sources 
external to painting, Lang borrows colour combinations from 
domestic knick-knacks and textiles and bases compositions on 
isolated areas of advertisements, sometimes translating a single 
photograph from a home decor magazine into multiple paintings. 
The results range from non-objective work employing the language 
of modernist painting to obscured, figurative portraits of interior 
spaces.

Lang was born in Kelowna, BC where she received her BFA from 
the University of British Columbia Okanagan in 2007. She has also 
lived in Edmonton, Guelph, and currently resides in Vancouver. Her 
work has been exhibited at The Red Head Gallery in Toronto, Diane 
Farris Gallery in Vancouver, and Sopa Fine Arts and The Alternator in 
Kelowna.

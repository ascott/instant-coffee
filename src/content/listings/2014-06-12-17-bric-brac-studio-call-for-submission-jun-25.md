Call for Submissions: Open to international artists in all mediums

Bric-à-brac studio is accepting participants for Keepsakes, a group show of 
small works. Each artist will submit one piece to be shown. Following the 
closing of the exhibition, we will return each work--but to a different artist. 

It follows that each piece submitted will be a kind of gift from one stranger to 
another, a token acting as a delegate on your behalf in an unknown land. 
Keepsakes is an experiment in curation, organization, art-trading and making 
connections. Taking cues from the impulses of mass image collection and 
carefully selected visual aesthetic in micro-blogging, Keepsakes aims to join 
distant artists together to meet in the physical world through their surrogate 
objects.

WHAT:
- Work can be sculpture, painting, drawing, photography, textile, print, multi-
page-accordion-book-of-poetry, etc.
- Size should be no more than 4x4x4inches.

TO SUBMIT: Email bric-a-brac@nadinemaher.com with:
- description of proposed piece, and/or images of finished work
- link to website, blog, or CV

Deadline to email submissions: June 25th, 2014
Following the June 25th Deadline, notified participants will then have until July 
25th for their works to be received by snail-mail. There are no fees to submit, 
aside from your cost to ship your work once selected. Bric-à-brac studio will 
cover the cost of returning to you a new work.

Blog: http://bric-a-brac-studio.tumblr.com

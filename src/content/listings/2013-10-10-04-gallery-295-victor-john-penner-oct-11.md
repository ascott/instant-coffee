Gallery 295 presents Victor John Penner's Not Safe to Occupy opening 
October 11th at Gallery 295. This exhibition is in conjunction with 
Capture Photography Festival.

Opening Reception: Friday October 11th 7:00 - 9:00

Exhibition Runs: October 11 - November 9

Between 2001 and 2002, Victor John Penner traveled alongside drug 
enforcement officers during their marijuana grow-operation busts 
throughout Vancouver. The images in ‘Not Safe to Occupy’ not only 
point to the scale of this illegal cottage industry, with its industrial 
lighting and ventilation, but also expose the desperate living conditions 
that the pawns of the drug trade are subject to.

Far from the glamorous lifestyle portrayed in pop culture, Penner’s 
images show us the stark reality of badly maintained, sparsely 
furnished homes with treacherous electrical wiring and stocks of 
hazardous chemicals. But his lens has also focused on the more 
mundane day-to-day activities of those involved.

With the failings of the ‘War on Drugs’ becoming ever more prevalent, 
and street gangs violence in the lower mainland becoming ever more 
brazen, the debate over marijuana legalization has continued to 
growing louder in recent years. Penner’s images give us an objective, 
unfiltered view into a trade that is unlikely to ever go away.

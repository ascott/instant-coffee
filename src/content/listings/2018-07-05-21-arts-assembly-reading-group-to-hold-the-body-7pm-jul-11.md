to hold the body | vcudu tutmak

ARTS ASSEMBLY 
Reading Group
July 11th, 7pm

During our tenure at PLOT, ARTS ASSEMBLY will facilitate reading groups, workshops and 
artistic research as part of our upcoming collaborative exchange project, tutma | hold. 

This reading group investigates the body as a site of holding. Texts chosen account for 
grappling with direct and inherited memories, pain, and loss. Highlighting rituals and modes of 
understanding the ongoing negotiation of what we and how we hold through embodiment. 

rsvp to info@artsassembly.ca, reading packs are sent as a pdf. Please advise if you require a 
printed pack.

Michael Drebert, Amy Gogarty and Demian Petryshyn
Curated by Allison Hrabliuk
26 October - 17 December 2017
CSA Space. #5-2414 Main Street, Vancouver

Reception for the artists and curator Thursday 26 October 2017, 6-9pm

These three artists spend their time responding to where they find themselves. Through 
daily walks around East Vancouver, Amy Gogarty interjects herself directly into the city 
landscape, painting en plein air to create an archive of her changing neighbourhood on 
clay pots. Demian Petryshyn goes inward, to his apartment, where he expands space 
through the minutia of natural aquarium cultivation. Michael Drebert finds himself at the 
beach, where the sea and the city meet, and has been writing about his daily 
encounters of the two.

This exhibition presents their recorded discoveries in clay, watercolour, and writing, 
humanizing our encounters with the built structures and detritus of the city.

Michael Drebert will read from his writings Cormorant, during the exhibition opening at 
7:00 pm.

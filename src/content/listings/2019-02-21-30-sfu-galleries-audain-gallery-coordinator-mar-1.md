Audain Gallery Coordinator

Application deadline: March 1, 2019

ORGANIZATION DESCRIPTION: 
Simon Fraser University's art galleries are dynamic centres for the presentation and 
interrogation of art practices and ideas. There are three distinct galleries: SFU Gallery on the 
Burnaby campus (established 1970), Audain Gallery at the Goldcorp Centre for the Arts in 
Vancouver (established 2010), and Teck Gallery at Harbour Centre in Vancouver 
(established 1989). SFU Galleries stewards the Simon Fraser University Art Collection that 
includes, in its holdings of over 5,500 works, significant regional and national art works 
spanning the last century.

Simon Fraser University is known for its commitment to academic freedom, interdisciplinary 
research, and innovative pedagogy, and it has created opportunities for important local and 
international artists to exhibit, teach, study, and produce work. Within this context, SFU 
Galleries encourage conceptual and experimental programs that explore ways in which 
contemporary art is socially and politically engaged. Undertaken by SFU Galleries curatorial 
staff, programming includes exhibitions, publications, symposia, conversations, screenings, 
performances, and other events. Collaborating with SFUs School for the Contemporary 
Arts, Audain Gallery hosts exhibitions with Audain Visual Artists in Residence Program as 
well as BFA and MFA student exhibitions. SFU Galleries engage the general public and the 
art community as well as the SFU community. All galleries are free of charge.

JOB DESCRIPTION: 
The Gallery Assistant coordinates openings, exhibitions, and artist talks presented at the 
Audain Gallery of SFU Woodward's. The incumbent of the position schedules events and 
exhibitions; coordinates the shipping, installation, and de-installation of Gallery exhibitions; 
responds to enquiries; provides guided tours of the Gallery; coordinates the activities of 
volunteers; tracks and maintains records of Gallery expenses; word processes 
correspondence; and orders Gallery supplies. The Gallery Assistant provides administrative 
support to the Curator of the Gallery by drafting artist contracts; preparing grant applications; 
researching and drafting Gallery publications and marketing materials; updating website and 
preparing HTML announcements; and coordinating activities for the Audain Artist-in-
Residence program.

RESPONSIBILITIES: 
Daily:
1. Responds to enquiries received in person and in writing.
2. Schedules Gallery events and exhibitions. Coordinates the shipping, installation, and de- 
installation of art works used for Gallery exhibitions. Prepares required customs documents.
3. Drafts artist contracts and prepares correspondence to participating artists and lending 
institutions.
4. Schedules events related to the Audain Artist-in-Residence Program (e.g., Artist talks, 
symposiums, exhibition openings) and coordinates all technical requirements.
5. Tracks expenses and processes invoices relating to exhibition projects and daily Gallery 
operating costs.
6. Word processes correspondence, lists, application forms, critical path documents, etc.
7. Maintains files and databases.
8. Coordinates the ordering of supplies required by the Gallery.
9. Maintains of the Gallery space to ensure that it is clean, attractive, and presentable at all 
times and brings any irregularities or problems to the attention of the Curator or appropriate 
department.
10. Coordinates Gallery volunteers and assigns them tasks.

Weekly:
1. Researches and drafts materials for the preparation of publications for review by the 
Curator.
2. Updates and maintains Gallery databases; generates reports and other documents 
pertaining to the exhibitions and general Gallery activity.
3. Coordinates the procurement of external services required by the Gallery (e.g., 
photographers, Gallery preparators, carpenters, etc.)
4. Attends weekly and bi-weekly production meetings, as well as occasional meetings with 
other arts organizations and other Galleries.
5. Researches information regarding the use of technologies, equipment, and protocols 
required for upcoming exhibitions.

Bi-Monthly:
1. Assists the Audain Artist-in-Residence with their VISA/tax waiver applications and any 
other documents required for travel.
2. Makes accommodation arrangements by confirming travel dates and type of 
accommodation desired for visiting artists.
3. Researches and drafts Gallery publications and marketing materials for review by the 
Curator.
4. Coordinates the mailing out of exhibition invitations.
5. Prepares updates for the website, HTML announcements and obtains/organizes 
exhibition graphic materials.
6. Provides administrative support to the Curator in the preparation of grant applications.

Annually and Periodically:
1. Prepares annual budget reports collaboratively with the Curator.
2. Revises filing system by reordering, rearranging, or re-categorizing files to maintain and 
improve effectiveness of filing system.

QUALIFICATIONS: 
High school graduation and completion of one year of post-secondary education in 
contemporary visual arts, or formal program equivalent.
3 years of related experience.
An equivalent combination of formal education, certificate/program of study and experience 
is acceptable.
Good knowledge of issues in contemporary visual arts.
Good knowledge of gallery administration practices.
Good knowledge of exhibition scheduling, installation and de-installation.
Good knowledge of art protocols and art presentation techniques.
Good knowledge of proper art handling techniques.
Basic knowledge of equipment commonly required in the presentation of contemporary art 
exhibitions (e.g., projectors, monitors, and audio equipment).
Excellent oral, written and interpersonal and communication skills.
Excellent organizational skills.
Excellent editing and proofreading skills.
Excellent analytical reasoning and problem solving skills.
50 words per minute keyboarding with a high degree of accuracy.
Ability to use word processing, database, spreadsheet, graphic design, e-mail and website 
applications at an intermediate level.
Ability to research and draft written materials and publications.
Ability to handle confidential material with discretion.
Ability to exercise mature judgement and initiative.
Ability to work independently.
Ability to lift, move or carry materials weighing up to 15 kg.
Ability to work flexible hours, including occasional evenings and weekends.

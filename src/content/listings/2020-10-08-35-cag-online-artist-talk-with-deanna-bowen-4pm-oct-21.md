Artist Talk with Deanna Bowen
Vancouver Heritage Foundation and Contemporary Art Gallery
Online via Zoom
Wednesday, October 21, 4 - 5pm PDT
$10

Presented by the Vancouver Heritage Foundation, join Deanna Bowen for a discussion of the
artists large-scale public artwork, Night Prowl (2019), which features the extinguished neon
light of the Blue Sky dance club, a hub of Black nightlife in 1950s Vancouver. Night Prowl is
an extension of Bowens recent work mapping the history of a Black entertainment
community in Vancouver, much of which has been erased from the citys physical fabric and
collective memory, but continues to exist as traces in archives. Bowen will be joined by CAG
Acting Associate
Curator Julia Lamare.

Tickets to this virtual event are $10. Register online via the Vancouver Heritage Foundation
at www.tickettailor.com/events/vancouverheritagefoundation/424893

This year Art Waste is proud to announce 16 art shows across Vancouver, 
supporting and promoting over 30 emerging and professional artists and 
curatorial teams for the Music Waste weekend June 6-9.

To kick off the weekends events the Art Waste curatorial team is putting 
together a group show at Interurban (1 E Hastings st) conceived in the spirit of 
Music Waste. An open call to all artists who live, work and create across 
Vancouver.

Vancouver’s cultural habitat is endangered, yet the art and artists not only 
remain but thrive. As in nature, we adapt through resourcefulness and 
ingenuity to survive. Merely by practicing and creating, we are protesting the 
gentrification and displacement that has been so rampant in the Vancouver art 
scene in the past few years. We want to focus on the positive outcomes of all 
of this: as a habitat we may be endangered but as a creative force we are 
flourishing.

Please submit:
- 3 low rez jpegs
- Links to your online portfolio (timblr/flickr or personal)
- A super short bio
- Final art works should be no larger then 24x23"

Aligned with the curve of night

Katie Lyle
Lauren Rice
Agnes Scherer
Sojourner Truth Parsons

Organized by Julian Hou and Tiziana La Melia

Reception: Thursday, August 20
Open Aug 21-September 21
By appointment, please email: modelprojectspace@gmail.com
Model, 147 Main St.

Katie Lyle is a visual artist based in Toronto. Recent exhibitions include
Garden Gallery in collaboration with Shelby Wright, Nanaimo Art
Gallery, Erin Stump Projects, Deluge Contemporary. Upcoming projects
include Erin Stump Projects and HPI Gallery and a publication with
SLOW EDITIONS.

Lauren Rice is an artist based in Vancouver. She received a BA from the
University of British Columbia and in 2013 was a guest student of Lucy
McKenzie at the Dusseldorf Kunstakademie.

Agnes Scherer is a german artist and cultural scientist. Born 1985, she
grew up in a rural Bavarian region where music events for young
people are still called "Beat Nights". She studied art history,
archaeology, cultural anthropology, sociology and fine art in
Tubingen, Vienna and Dusseldorf. Currently she is working on an
operetta project in Scotland.

Sojourner Truth Parsons is getting older and isn't so thrilled about it.
Though many things made her feel happy this year like living with a
dog, driving a car and experiencing American kombucha.

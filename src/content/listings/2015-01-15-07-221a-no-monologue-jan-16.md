No Monologue
Opening Reception: Friday, January 16 at 7pm

221A is pleased to present No Monologue, an exhibition of work by Dustin
Brons, Gabi Dao, Scott Kemp, Kara Hansen, Emma Metcalfe Hurst, Ellis Sam
and Zebulon Zang in response to a 24-foot long table.

Long tables can be located in the politics of congregation by looking at
one occasion when Danish furniture designer Piet Hein tried to intervene in
peace negotiations after the Vietnam War.

For more information please visit

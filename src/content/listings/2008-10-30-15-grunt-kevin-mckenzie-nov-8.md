For the upcoming project SCREEN at grunt gallery, Kevin McKenzie will be
building a wall. This wall will then serve as a screen for video
documentation of its own making. Join us at grunt for the artist's wall
building performance during the opening for the project on Saturday
evening, November 8, at 8pm.

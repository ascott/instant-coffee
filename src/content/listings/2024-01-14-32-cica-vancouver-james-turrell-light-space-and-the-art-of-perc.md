CICA Vancouver is honored to present James Turrell: Light, Space, and the Art of
Perception. Join us as we delve into the profound world of an artist who refined the
interplay of light and human perception.

Turrell once articulated, We eat light, drink it through our skins Seeing is a very
sensuous act - theres a sweet deliciousness to feeling yourself see something. As you
navigate through this exhibition, his words will resonate deeply.

Journey with us through the curated brilliance of the Glass Series installations, prints,
and drawings and take a glimpse of the monumental Roden Crater Initiative.

We invite connoisseurs and enthusiasts alike to partake in this
unparalleled sensory
experience.

The exhibition runs until February 17, 2024. Please book in advance as slots are timed
and limited: https://cicavancouver.com/product/buy-ticket/.

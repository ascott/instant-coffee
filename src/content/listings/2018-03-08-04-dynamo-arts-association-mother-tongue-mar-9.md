Mother Tongue

CLOSING RECEPTION - March 9, 2018
Dynamo Arts Association : 7pm - 10pm

This exhibition brings together new work by Gabriela Godoi and M.E. Sparks. Both 
artists explore painting as a material language that, although tied to dominant patriarchal 
histories, continues to offer potential for empowered feminist discourse surrounding 
concepts of visual pleasure, resistance, and bodily experience. The artists approach 
painting as a shared mother tongue of sorts - not only as a shared material language, 
but as a shared feminist narrative that speaks from and to the body. Through contorted, 
cut and confrontational forms, symbolism and inner monologues, material opacity and 
tension, the artists explore the concept of painting as present body, historical body, and 
misbehaving body; painting as carrier, as woman; painting as an indexical, political, and 
material utterance.

Gabriela Godoi is a Brazilian artist based in Vancouver, BC. She graduated from the 
MFA program at Emily Carr University of Art and Design in 2017 and holds a BFA from 
F.A.A.P. (So Paulo - Brazil). Recent exhibitions include O negativo como primrio at 
Espao Cpsula (So Paulo) and Moist, mucous, membrane at ECUAD Concourse Gallery
(Vancouver).

M.E. Sparks received a MFA (2016) from Emily Carr University of Art + Design and BFA
(2013) from NSCAD University. She was a finalist in the 2017 and 2016 RBC Painting
Competition and the recipient of the 2016 Nancy Petry Award. Recent exhibitions 
include A Beast Salient at Franc Gallery (Vancouver) and A Terrible Signal at Access 
Gallery (Vancouver).

We acknowledge that this event takes place on unceded Indigenous land belonging to 
the Coast Salish peoples, including the territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam),
Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), and Sl"ilwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

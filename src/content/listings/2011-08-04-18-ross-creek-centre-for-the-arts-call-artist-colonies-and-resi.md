Call: Artist Colonies and Residencies at Ross Creek Centre for the 
Arts
Now accepting applications for 2011 Fall and 2012 Winter/Spring 
Artist Colonies and Residencies
Deadlines: Aug 5, Sept 15, Dec 15

Located on 186 acres of farm and forest overlooking the Bay of 
Fundy, The
Ross Creek Centre for the Arts is the ideal artist's retreat. Our
residencies provide tranquil space and time for artists to develop 
their
projects and ideas. Participants receive 24-hr access to large 
brightly lit
studios, accommodations in our farmhouse, and fresh meals 
prepared by our
chef.

We welcome submissions from emerging and professional artists 
of all
disciplines, including but not limited to individual or collaborative 
visual
artists, curators, dancers, writers, actors, musicians and architects. 
In
the submission proposal, please make reference to how your 
project might fit
within the context of the Centre and our rural community. The Centre 
is a
registered not-for-profit organization dedicated to encouraging and
promoting the arts through education and arts development. 
Selection for our
residencies is a juried and competitive process.

Submissions for our fall season (September 1- December 15, 
2011) should be
postmarked on or before Aug 5th, 2011. Applicants will be notified 
by the
third week of August. Residencies last from four weeks to four 
months.

Upcoming Deadlines:
August 5, 2011 for September 1 - December 15, 2011 
colony/residency
September 15, 2011 for January 10 - April 30, 2012 colony/residency
December 15, 2011 for May 1 - June 30, 2012 colony residency

PLEASE NOTE: We encourage gallery artists to also be colony 
artists. If you
are interested in being considered for both, please ensure you fill 
out both
the gallery application form and residency package.

For more information and to apply:
http://www.artscentre.ca/artists/colonyandresidency.html

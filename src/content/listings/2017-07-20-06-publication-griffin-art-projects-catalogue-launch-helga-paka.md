Catalogue Launch

Saturday, July 22, 1-3pm 

Join us in launching two new publications which document the exhibitions Woosh and 
Surrogates, curated by Helga Pakasaar. These first exhibitions hosted by the gallery, 
which were drawn entirely from private collections, display the remarkable, museum 
quality works collected locally. 

It is also an opportunity to see our summer exhibition, A Cool Breeze: LA and 
Vancouver Art from the 1960s and beyond, which continues Seattle-based curator 
Melissa E. Feldmans research in 1960s California Light and Space and its 
reverberations, in this case Vancouver artists with a penchant for optical minimalism. 
The exhibition features work by artists associated with L.A. Glass and Plastic or Finish 
Fetish, with works by Laddie John Dill, Mary Corse, John McCracken, Helen Pashgian 
and De Wain Valentine, Through the process of visiting collections in Vancouver, 
Feldman discovered works by local artists previously unknown to her, such as Joan 
Balzer, Michael Morris, and Gordon Smith. Two current generation artists, Neil Campbell 
and Phillip K. Smith III, from Vancouver and L.A., respectively, bring these investigations 
into the present.

The exhibition will be on view until August 15. The gallery will reopen in the fall with an 
exhibition by Paul P. on September 22. 

Griffin Art Projects is located at 1174 Welch Street, North Vancouver, and open 
Saturdays 12-5pm and by appointment. Please contact us at info@griffinartprojects.ca

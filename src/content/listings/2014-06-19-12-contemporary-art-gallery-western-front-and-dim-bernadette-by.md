Contemporary Art Gallery, Western Front and Dim Cinema present

'Bernadette' (2008) by Duncan Campbell will screen in the Grand Luxe Hall 
at the Western Front, 303 8 Ave E, Vancouver from June 26 to June 29 
during opening hours: noon to 5pm. Please join for a reception and 
screening

Thursday, June 26 from 8-10pm.

This screening is part of The Act of Seeing with One's Own Eyes a group 
exhibition featuring work by

Ed Atkins
Edgardo Aragon
Kim Beom
Rossella Biscotti
Stan Brakhage
Duncan Campbell
Fabiola Carranza
Maryam Jafri
Toril Johannessen
Shahryar Nashat

June 13 to June 29, 2014 at the Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson 
Street, Vancouver. info@contemporaryartgallery.ca

'The Act of Seeing with One's Own Eyes' presents recent film
and video that seeks to interrogate notions of uncertainty within the
documentary format. Work by ten artists engages with the conventions of
source footage, narrative voice and re-enactment, questioning perceptions 
of such devices, while also reclaiming them in order to redefine their intent 
and potential.

A physically interactive sensory exhibition - free entry & free special events!

The Neurodivergent Artist Collective is delighted to invite you to "interplay: visions of
difference and possibility", an exhibition for which we created art that intentionally
invites visitors to interact in multiple ways. Each piece of art is an invitation for
sensory engagement whether through proximity, touch or through contributing to the
art by using the materials provided.

When: Saturday January 25 - Sunday February 9 - Open daily 3-7 pm
Where: James Black Gallery, 144 East 6th Avenue Vancouver, BC V5T 1J5

Special events during our show:

Workshop - Sensory Stuffies: Sewing Light-Up Plush Toys
Saturday February 1, 3 -7
pm
In this free workshop, participants will learn how to sew a soft LED circuit and
incorporate it into a hand sewn plush toy to take home. No prior experience in
sewing or electronics necessary; materials provided.

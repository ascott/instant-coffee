Readings

Anna Marie Repstock and Casey Wei
Opening reception April 24th 7-11pm
Show runs April 25th -May16th 
Gallery hours Wednesday through Saturday 12:30 to 5:30pm
Shudder Gallery, 433 Columbia St, Vancouver V6A 2R9
604 488 5477 shuddergallery.com

Readings is an exhibition of new work by emerging Vancouver artists Anna Marie Repstock and 
Casey Wei. Romantic ideals associated with painting and writing are examined in works that 
highlight the artists' shared interest in the fraught relationship between painting, poetry and 
contemporary art.

Collapsing representation and abstraction, Repstock's paintings draw upon the history of hard 
edged abstraction and conceptual, procedural painting. While the paintings are recognizable as 
replicas of lined papers, and are suggestive of reading and writing, the colored rays breaching 
them disrupt the facsimiles. As abstract and poetic forms, these rays address the space where 
readability yields to poetic abstraction. These paintings guide the viewer to intuit what text will fill 
the space between the lines.

Wei plays with the visual and aural qualities of the typewritten page, investigating the relationship 
between laborious process and the desire to return to an obsolescent technology. Dried Up uses 
this antiquated mode of writing to visually recreate one of Rodney Graham's upside down trees, a 
canonized image from the Vancouver school of Photo-conceptualism. The Past is Better Left a 
Bone, blogs from 2003-2008 is a sculptural transubstantiation of old blog entries, which have 
been printed, shredded and rendered as papier mache. By combining the material with a sound 
recording of the typing process, the viewer is made aware of the labor involved in their 
transformation, but denied access to their written content. 

Readings is an attempt to find the middle ground between the analog and digital; the first look 
and the second reading.

CiTR is hiring a Spoken Word Coordinator

CiTR 101.9 FM is the broadcasting voice of the University of British Columbia,
situated on the unceded, traditional Coast Salish territory of the Hnqminm
speaking Musqueam people. CiTR began as a student club in 1937, gaining not-for-
profit status and a place on the FM dial in 1982. CiTR offers students and community
members a variety of services which include broadcast and multimedia training,
promotional support, live on-location coverage of events and much more. CiTR also
provides the Lower Mainland unique and alternative coverage of municipal current
affairs, providing a platform for underrepresented peoples, opinions and stories not
heard through mainstream media outlets.

CiTR is looking for a Spoken Word Coordinator to support and refine the spoken
word content that CiTR produces for broadcast and/or podcast. Currently, CiTR has
between six and nine collectively produced spoken word programs, provides a
variety of spoken word training sessions and workshops, supports the production of
high-quality radio and podcasts and undergoes a variety of other initiatives related to
spoken word programming.

Tasks and Responsibilities:

Supporting and facilitating the creation of relevant, high quality local current affairs
and spoken word programming

Coordinating, mentoring and supporting volunteers
interested/involved in spoken
word and current affairs programming

Create and update training resources that reflect best practices in current affairs
programming for radio and podcasting, including written and audio resources on
ethical coverage of vulnerable and underrepresented populations.

Training and developing CiTRs spoken word volunteers through hands-on
training. This includes creating and facilitating weekly current affairs, interviewing
and podcasting workshops.

Creating and maintaining a database of UBC departments, community
organizations, municipal political institutions and individuals who will serve as points
of contact for story leads

Supporting our collectively produced spoken word programs, providing feedback,
aiding in recruitment, and editing content

Supporting and participating in additional station-wide projects as needed

Qualifications: The ideal candidate will have:

- Experience in a relevant field.

- Proven skills in radio programming & journalism,
sound editing, sound recording,
recruiting, training, and motivating volunteers.

- Experience with and an interest in working with underrepresented populations.
They will be flexible, motivated, detail-oriented, approachable, have excellent
communication skills and a desire to work in an open and collaborative environment.

Working conditions & compensation: Hours: 28 hours/week. There is some flexibility
in creating your own schedule, and an option to work from home if needed. Wage:
$17.00/hour The Spoken Word Coordinator will be hired on a one year contract, with
an option to renew if both parties consent. There will be a three month probationary
period at the commencement of the position.

EMPLOYMENT EQUITY:

CiTR/Discorder is an equal opportunity employer and employs staff without regard to

religion, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, physical and/or mental ability
or financial
ability. We encourage underrepresented groups to apply.

To apply, forward a resume, cover letter, and two-three examples of radio or podcast
work to Ana Rose Carrico, Station Manager, at stationmanager@citr.ca. Applications
are due Friday, July 10 with interviews being conducted from July 13- 17. Only

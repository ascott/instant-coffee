Bring Your Own Reading Series: Danielle Lafrance and Geoffrey Nilson

February 21, 2025
6:30-9PM

Reading starts at 7PM sharp

UNIT/PITT
2954 West 4th Ave

UNIT/PITT is pleased to host Bring Your Own Reading Series once again on
February 21, 2025, with readings by Danielle Lafrance and Geoffrey Nilson,
organized by Scott Innis. Doors open at 6:30PM, with readings at 7PM sharp.

Danielle LaFrance is the author of the poetry-research-theory books JUST LIKE I
LIKE IT (Talonbooks, 2019), Friendly + Fire (Talonbooks, 2016), species branding
(Capilano University Editions, 2010), and #postdildo (Talonbooks, 2022). Verbal
Violence
is forthcoming in spring 2026, a long-form email correspondence on the
professional managerial class, ideological reproduction, and the trend of
"progressive except Palestine" across liberal democratic institutions. Other poetry
and critical writing have appeared in such magazines and journals as ASAP/Journal,
The Capilano Review, LESTE, Tripwire, and Organism for Poetic Research.
Geoffrey Nilson is a poet, editor, musician, and literary critic born in Duncan, BC. His
poetry, book reviews, and critical writing have appeared widely, and his most recent
book, Light Makes a Ruin, appeared in 2022 with Ottawas above/ground press.
Nilson is a PhD student in the department of English at Simon Fraser University
studying the long poem, where he was the 2024 recipient of the Charles Olson
Award.

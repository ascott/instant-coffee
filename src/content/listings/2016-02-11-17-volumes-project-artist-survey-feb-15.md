You have had studios before, and you are an expert in sleuthing out your
next space. We at the Volumes Project need your invaluable feedback as we
embark on creating purpose-built, affordable studio spaces for Vancouver's
emerging artists.

Volumes Project is a non-profit organization that was created to address
the lack of affordable studio spaces in Vancouver. We are currently in the
final planning stages with architects and building experts to design
sustainable and affordable art studios that will be placed on underutilized
land throughout the city. Volumes Project intends to have these units
available for occupation by emerging artists in 2016. For more information,
visit our website at www.volumesproject.com.

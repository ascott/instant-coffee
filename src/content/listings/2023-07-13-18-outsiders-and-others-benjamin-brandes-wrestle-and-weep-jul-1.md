Benjamin Brandes: Wrestle and Weep

July 1-29, 2023
Outsiders and Others
716 East Hastings St., Vancouver, BC

ARTIST DISCUSSION: Saturday, July 15, 2023 at 1pm
Limited seating. Reserve your seat by emailing outsidersandothers@gmail.com

Outsiders and Others is excited to present a solo exhibition with Vancouver artist Ben
Brandes. This is Ben's first exhibition in a gallery.

About the artist:

Benjamin Brandes is a "self-taught" visual artist/musician/singer-songwriter. Ben was
born and raised in Windsor, Ontario, and is now based in Vancouver, BC.

From Benjamin:

"Lucky to have been a teenager in a band in Windsor during the 90s, my youth was
filled with music, mania, anxiety, depression, weird drawings, extreme experimentation
and sculpture-making. Those early days,
mixed with 8 strangely unforgettable, magical
years in the thick of the Montreal music scene (2006 - 2014)continue to feed my
creative monster to this day.

Working with paint revealed itself to me five years ago, in the form of an abandoned
canvas, stretched and ready, just leaning against a dumpster. I added my 2 cents (and
then some) to the surface and it's been a thing ever since. Rescued canvas and found
surfaces (paper, tabletops, orphaned custom canvases, etc.)have become a safe
haven for my darkness and anxiety; a safe place where the crippling waves can mix
with my weird, wonderful and complicated past."

The exhibition is also viewable online at www.outsidersandothers.com.

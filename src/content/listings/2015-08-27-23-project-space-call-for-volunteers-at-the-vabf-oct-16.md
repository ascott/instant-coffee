CALL FOR VOLUNTEERS at the Vancouver Art / Book Fair this October 16th-18th

Presented by Project Space, the VA/BF is a two-day festival of artists’
publishing featuring nearly one hundred local, national and international
publishers, as well as a diverse line-up of programs, performances and
installations. The VA/BF is the only international art book fair in Canada
and one of only two on the West Coast. In 2015 the event is anticipated to
attract over 1,500 visitors from across the Greater Vancouver Area and beyond.

Having a dedicated group of volunteers is an invaluable asset towards
making our fair a success. Any help between the afternoon of Friday,
October 16th and the evening of Sunday, October 18th, would be greatly
appreciated! To register as a volunteer, please email
sarah@vancouverartbookfair.com as soon as possible.

This is a great opportunity to meet like-minded artists and creatives while
getting involved in arts publishing. The VA/BF relies on dozens of
invaluable volunteers for support!

More information about Project Space can be found here: [http://projectspace.ca/](http://projectspace.ca/)
More information about this year’s fair can be found here:
[http://2015.vancouverartbookfair.com/](http://2015.vancouverartbookfair.com/)

To register or inquire about volunteer opportunities please email
sarah@vancouverartbookfair.com

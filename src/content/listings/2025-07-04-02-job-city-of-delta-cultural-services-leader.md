The City of Delta is looking for enthusiastic individuals to provide visitor reception
and be responsible for delivering tours, programs, and special events at the Douglas
J. Husband Discovery Centre in Delta. Duties may include: greeting and orienting
visitors; promoting self guided tours; leading tours, school and public programs;
assisting with the preparation and development of activities and special events;
assisting with exhibition set up and take down; researching and assisting in the
development and delivery of heritage and cultural programs; monitoring displays;
assisting with collections inventory, documentation, processing, and digitization;
minor janitorial maintenance in exhibition spaces and the archives reading room;
communicating with and supporting volunteers; and performing other related work as
required.

This auxiliary position provides a competitive salary of $24.97 - $29.20 per hour +
12% in lieu of vacation, public holidays, and benefits.

The posting closes on July 21, 2025. View the full position details and submit an
application on our website at http://www.delta.ca/jobs

At the City of Delta, we are committed to recruiting and retaining a diverse workforce
that is representative of our community, providing equal opportunity and fostering an
inclusive workplace where our individual differences are recognized, valued and
celebrated. The City of Delta acknowledges that we are on the shared, traditional,
ancestral, and unceded territories of the scw"an (Tsawwassen), xmky"m
(Musqueam), and other Coast Salish Peoples. We extend our appreciation to these
First Nations for the opportunity to enjoy this land together.

So Far.a Bunch Of Faces: Jesse Blanchard

Red Gate Art Gallery
855 E Hastings
Opening April 23rd 4PM - 9 PM 
Contact: lastdoor@gmail.com

Jesse presents a complete work of seemingly fragmentary bodies, where lips may 
escape the face and pursue new adventures. Phrases of words appear in rooms, far 
from the time or place they are spoken. Individual lines and colours, appear of their own 
volition as memoized structures with larger history. The artist includes torn fragments of 
other works, disposing with the inert finality of the illusory picture frame where nothing is 
explored, lost, or found. 

Join us for the opening Sunday April the 23rd 4pm, or contact for an appointment until 
May 3rd.

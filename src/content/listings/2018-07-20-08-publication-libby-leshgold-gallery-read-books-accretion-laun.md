Accretion: Launch and Reading
Libby Leshgold Gallery + READ Books
Saturday, July 21 at 2pm

Please join us at the Libby Leshgold Gallery on July 21 at 2pm for the
launch of Holly Schmidt's project Accretion.

As a part of 10 Different Things, a collaborative public art initiative
among ECUAD Living Labs, City Studio and The City of Vancouver Public Art
Program, artist Holly Schmidt examines precarity through a particular
granite used in the construction of civic buildings at the turn of the last
century and in recent development in False Creek. In her ongoing project,
Accretion, Schmidt uses photo, video and writing to document the extraction
of a slab of granite from Hardy Island, its transportation to the Mainland,
and its transformation once it enters public space. Accretion acts as an
inquiry into the confluence of geological and historical time on coastal
shores.

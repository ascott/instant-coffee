Conceptions of Whiteness and the Geopolitics of Korean Beauty

January 24, 6:30 PM

Join us for a talk by S. Heijin Lee, Assistant Professor of Women, Gender, and
Sexuality Studies at the University of Hawaii at M-noa, exploring how racialized traits
and plastic surgery
procedures are deployed in the service of capitalist expansion,
even in the absence of white bodies. This talk will be presented on Zoom and
simultaneous interpretation will be provided from English to Mandarin. Learn more:
https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/events/jan24geopoliticsofkoreanbeauty

Celebrating Black Futures

Throughout February

This February, in honour of Black Futures Month, the Vancouver Art Gallery presents a
series of special programming celebrating Black Futures in Vancouver, Canada and
beyond. This years programming will focus on the intersection of art, film and poetry.

The Gallery has partnered with the Vancouver International Film Festival (VIFF) to
screen three feature films and four shorts that speak to the future of Black and African
cinema. These films present a contemporary snapshot of the multilateral nature of
African, African-American, Caribbean and Black Canadian culture and cinema.

Our month-long programming series, Celebrating Black Futures, will
begin on
Thursday, February 8, with an evening of live jazz, performed by the Feven Kidane
Sextet at VIFF, followed by a screening of Sam Pollard and Ben Shapiros
documentary, Max Roach: The Drum Also Waltzes (2023).

The next film screenings will include Babatunde Apalowos All the Colours of the World
Are Between Black and White (2023), which tells the story of a Nigerian man struggling
to come to terms with his sexuality; Kelly Fyffe-Marshalls When Morning Comes
(2022), a familiar tale of Jamaican Canadian immigration; and series of recent shorts
exploring diverse Black experiences. Through colourful visual rhythms and dynamic
storytelling, these stories of love, immigration, pain, ambition and family are
representative of the new wave of Black and African cinema. All films have been
selected by Kika Memeh, Public Programs Assistant at the Vancouver Art Gallery, and
Tom Charity, VIFF Centre Year-Round Programmer.

On Friday, February 16, our inaugural Poet in Residence Jillian
Christmas will activate
the Gallery with A Day of Delight, featuring thoughtful conversations, workshops and
poetic interpretations of exhibitions on display.

Find out more: https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/celebrating-black-futures-2024/

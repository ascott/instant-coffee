The Ferry Building Gallery is excited to announce the upcoming exhibition by Aileen
Vantomme, titled "deposit/ed." This exhibition explores the impact of climate change
through ceramic art and will be on display from October 3 to November 3.

"deposit/ed" reflects on the accumulation of shell remnants found in tidal pools during
extreme heat events. The inspiration for
this exhibition stems from the artists shoreline
walks, where she encountered the debris left behind by a changing climate.

Attached are the press release and the exhibition poster. Please feel free to reach out if
you need further information or would like to interview the artist.

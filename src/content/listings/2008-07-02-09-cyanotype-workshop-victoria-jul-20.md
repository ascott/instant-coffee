Sunday July 20

Arnica Artist Run Centre

232 Victoria Street, Victoria

10:00 am- 4:00 pm

A hands on workshop using one of the first non-silver photographic processes to be invented. 
Images will be printed on a variety of papers and cloth. We will mix our own chemistry, coat the 
materials, dry, and expose to UV light as a contact print and then develop in water. We will 
experiment with images photocopied on transparencies, text and drawings on Mylar, and with 
acrylic lifts. Bring family photographs, diaries and found images from magazines that could be 
photocopied. Also bring pressed botanicals, feathers, lace - anything that is semi transparent that 
could be used to make photograms. We will combine prints with other media such as ink, 
watercolour and acrylic.
Weather permitting, a giant human mural photogram will also be attempted!
To preregister: email emsedgman@gmail.com or phone 250-372-2444.
Limit: 12 –15 participants
Cost: $55 or $45 for Arnica members (includes materials)
Proceeds go towards Arnica Artist Run Centre.
Arnica is a not-for-profit artist-run centre that supports and encourages experimentation, 
presentation and dissemination of contemporary art that engages artists and the community.

Instructor Elaine Sedgman has a degree in Fine Arts from UCC (now TRU) and a BA from the 
University of Victoria in English and Sociology. She has been working with the cyanotype process 
for the past five years. Elaine has participated in solo and group exhibitions in Canada, USA & 
Japan.

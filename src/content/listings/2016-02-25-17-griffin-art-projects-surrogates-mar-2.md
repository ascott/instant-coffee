Surrogates
March 5 - June 4, 2016
Griffin Art Projects
1174 Welch St., North Vancouver

Please join us to celebrate the opening of a new exhibition at Griffin Art
Projects on Wednesday, March 2, at 7 pm.

Surrogates, selected from three local art collections by curator Helga
Pakasaar, brings together some of Vancouver's most prominent artists with
significant international figures. The works in a diversity of mediums have
all been produced in the last decade. The exhibition is pervaded by various
kinds of surrogates, both literal and metaphoric. Many of the artists
question the very act of making unique objects through their ambiguous
relationship to authorship and notions of originality. At times they
inhabit other personas, as with Rodney Graham and Simon Starling's
reinventions of Man Ray and the darkroom performances with chemicals and
light of Walead Beshty and Mariah Robertson. Through such performative
actions with surrogate objects, different realities collapse into one
another. Similarly, Annette Kelm's deadpan photographs of seemingly
identical purse clocks suggest that things are off kilter and not as they
might appear. This sense of the absurd is present throughout the
exhibition, especially in the works of Kim Kennedy Austin, Kevin Beasley,
Myfanwy MacLeod and Marlo Pascual. These diverse artworks, together with
others in the exhibition, suggest that what appear to be stand-ins between
one state and another, point to possible ways to negotiate our place in an
unstable world.

The exhibition was made possible through generous loans from the GreyChurch
Collection, Lara-Prout Collection and Freybe Collection.

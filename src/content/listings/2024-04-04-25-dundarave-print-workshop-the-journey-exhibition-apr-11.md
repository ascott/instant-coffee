The Journey Exhibition Opening Night
April 11, 6-9pm.

Exhibition dates:
April 11 to May 5, 2024
Wednesday to Sunday, 10-6pm

Dundarave Print Workshop invites you to attend its first group show consisting of artists
from across Canada starting this April. We will be displaying work incorporating
printmaking mediums from selected artists across the country in relation to the show
theme The Journey. The exhibition aims to showcase the printmaking talent in the region
and to build relations among the printmaking community.

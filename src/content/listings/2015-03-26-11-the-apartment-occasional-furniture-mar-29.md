Occasional Furniture

March 29 - May 23, 2015
Opening Reception: Sunday March 29, 2-5pm.

Antiques Warehouse, Derya Akay, Rosa Aiello, Alan Belcher, Niels Bendtsen
(Bensen), Eli Bornowsky, Matt Browning, Barb Choit, Aaron Chan, Adam
Brickell, Cobra, Larry Cohen, Bruce Connor, Mark Dion, Charles and Ray

Knauf and Brown, Kelly Lamb, Lee Lozano, Roy McMakin, Tiziana La Melia,
Maynard Monroe, Matt Murphy Design, Elizabeth McIntosh, Otto Muehl, Gailan
Ngan, Wayne Ngan, Josée Aubin Ouellette, Danica Phelps, Althea Thauberger,
Lisa Turner Design, Sylvain Sailly, Steven Shearer, Joanna Staniszkis,
Mungo Thomson, Victor Vasarely and more!

The Apartment is very excited to announce the largest, most diverse
exhibition it has ever organized. Occasional Furniture presents a mise en
scene of art works in a myriad of mediums. This baroque assortment of
objects has been selected and arranged to emphasize their individual
aesthetic accomplishments.

Through this assembled cacophony, comparisons are presented which are
intended to provoke a sense of contrast over cohesion. In this way the show
attempts to pose questions about the relative values of art objects,
especially work that manages both aesthetic excellence and actual
utilitarian purpose; even if that purpose is pure expression, forming a
container for cultural critique, furnishing your home or inciting a
materialist revolution.

In this dream like assemblage of things past and proposed futures a
psychological landscape of cultural specters answer to the needs of our
questioning spirit.

This exhibition brings together some of the most intriguing artists and
incombasses a vast breadth of creative production. It is a celebration of
that spark of expression, the beautiful mistake, the forgotten complexity
of ideal revelry and our shared compulsion for the baroque and the
grotesque. The exhibition will host a series of events, many hosted by
exhibiting artists, and have an extensive on line presence. Stay tuned!

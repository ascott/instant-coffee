The Kootenay Gallery of Art located in Castlegar, BC is accepting
proposals from artists and curators for exhibitions for their 2017/18
calendar. This public gallery promotes the visual arts within the West
Kootenay region of British Columbia is committed to include, through
exhibitions and programming a diversity of artists and art forms that
enrich, challenge and educate the audience. The Gallery pays artists
fees in accordance with CARFAC. For a complete outline of what is to
be included in an exhibition proposal visit the gallery website at
www.kootenaygallery.com. If you have any further questions please
contact the Gallery staff at 250-365-3337 or email to
kootenaygallery@telus.net

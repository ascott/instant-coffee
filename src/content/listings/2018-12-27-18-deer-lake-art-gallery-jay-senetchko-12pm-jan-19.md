Please join us at our opening reception January 19th, 2019

Rich Dark Soil refers to the practice and heritage of artist Jay Senetchko. Senetchko is 
of Canadian (Albertan) and Ukrainian descent; and although he is most easily defined as 
a painter, he situates his practice in a broader material context and process, which 
includes photography, digital media, sculptures, performance and installations.

The exhibition will open Saturday, January 19th, 2019 from 12 to 4 pm and will run until 
February 9, 2019.

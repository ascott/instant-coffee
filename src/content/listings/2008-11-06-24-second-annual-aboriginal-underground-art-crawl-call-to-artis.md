CALL TO ARTISTS:
Victoria, BC: Second Annual Aboriginal Underground Art Crawl is taking
place March 13th, 2009.

Needed:

Aboriginal artists and musicians producing provocative,
contemporary work in all mediums to submit portfolios for selection.
Work will be shown at one of six participating galleries in Victoria BC
and will be included in a downtown gallery walk featuring art
and entertainment the night of the crawl.

Please send the following:

- Up to ten images of past or current work (any format - photos,
jpegs, slides, cd, emailed)
Please do NOT send original work

- A brief bio/artist statement

- A brief proposal if you'd like to submit new work that's still being produced

This year's (loose) theme is "THE OLD GUARD IS DEAD"

Submissions will be accepted at the email and mailing address below:

Aboriginal Underground Art Crawl
c/o Marlaina Buch/Juli Steemson
#5 - 604 Wilson St
Victoria BC, V9A 3G9

Email: marlainabuch@gmail.com
radjuli@gmail.com

Please contact the crawl organizers if you have questions regarding
how to apply. Deadline for submissions: Dec 31st, 2008.

BIOS:
Marlaina Buch is a Victoria curator and artist. The Aboriginal Underground
Art Crawl was started as a way for her to address her own ignorance
surrounding Aboriginal art and culture and in response to difficulties her
peers were experiencing in navigating the gallery system. Her interest
in Aboriginal art stems from her childhood in Thunder Bay and contact
with the Anishnaabe community there. A strong committment to the
do-it-yourself ethos permeates her curatorial and artistic practice
and continues to drive her civic engagement.

Juli Steemson is a musician and multi-disciplinary artist working in
leather, textiles, collage, and beadwork. She is fascinated by, and is
independently studying, Northwest Coast traditions and culture, with
specific focus on the Haida Nation. Accomplishments include
participating in the Simon Fraser Student Society/Cedar Table Series'
Anti-Colonial Art Contest and an ongoing collaboration in leather with
Haida carver Gwaai Edenshaw. Juli also performs internationally as a
percussionist with many musicians, most recently with Juno-Award winner
Leela Gliday.

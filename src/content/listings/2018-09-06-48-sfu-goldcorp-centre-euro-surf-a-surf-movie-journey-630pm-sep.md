Free Public Event!

Euro-Surf: A Surf Movie Journey along the European Coastline

Curated by Diego Slosse

When: Friday, September 21, 2018. 6:30 p.m.
Where: Djavad Mowafaghian World Arts Centre, Goldcorp Centre for the Arts, 149 W. Hastings
St., Vancouver
Cash Bar

Europe is and always has been a travelling surfers dream "Matt Crocker in Endless Winter 2
When a Hollywood film crew came to France to shoot a movie in 1956, the European surf
movement as we know it today took off in Biarritz. Soon, surfers in VW vans were making
pilgrimages along the European coastlines in search of the perfect wave.
Travelling through nine countries from England to Morocco, these movies stories lie within
the search and the friendship while chasing the elusive swell. Euro-Surf will make you feel
the rad spirit of surfing.

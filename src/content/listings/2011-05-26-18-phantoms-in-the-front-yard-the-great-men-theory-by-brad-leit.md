Phantoms in the Front Yard Monthly Free Lecture Series presents:

The Great Men Theory by Brad Leith

Brad Leith will moderate a discussion around theme's advanced by the 19th
century English philosopher Thomas Carlyle's Great Man Theory of History.
The purpose of our discussion will be to interrogate and problematize the
phallocentric logism surrounding the discourse on the simulacra of heroic
identity as it pertains to race, gender and class (a.k.a. The Holy
Trinity). All attendees are encouraged to participate but will not be
require to understand or comprehend a damn thing that is said.

The lecture is free and open to all however please RSVP by phone or email
info@phantomsinthefrontyard.com as seating is limited to the first 40
people to request. Contributions to the wine and refreshments table are
greatly appreciated

Speaker: Brad Leith
Location: 566 Beatty Street (Dunsmuir) Vancouver, BC 778.999.7436
Time: Sunday, June 5th 7pm

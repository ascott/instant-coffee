August 16 - 31, 2024

In creating this exhibition and the formation of The Anti-Bland Brigade, Shannon
Pawliw has had to navigate a complex spectrum of thoughts and feelings towards
her fellow humans. Perhaps
naively, she envisions a world where not only do we
NOT kill each other over land and ideologies, we prioritize ensuring that everyone
has enough. A world where we cherish and protect this remarkable planet.

Despite moments of catastrophic disappointment, she remains steadfast in her belief
in the potential of our species.

Pawliw's hope is for humanity to come together to address our vital challenges
through community connection at a grass-roots level. To advocate for the fostering of
innovation and minimizing of exploitation, focusing on love and wellness over
punishment and self-interest. The Anti-Bland Brigade encourages us to spend time
with loved ones, care for each other, and learn to appreciate and understand those
who are different from us.

Building community and connection should be at the heart of everything.

The world would be very bland if we were all the same. If we all had exactly the
same outlook, beliefs and traits there would be nothing to marvel at. Nothing to
discuss. No innovation or expression.

The paintings in this exhibition reflect Pawliws desire to work through her
disappointment in humanity through exploration and play " two quintessential traits
of the human animal. At its core, the work is an effort to process emotions and find
joy in the world. It is a celebration of individuality and creativity, standing against the
blanding and conformity of modern life.

The Anti-Bland Brigade is also a call to action. Viewers are invited to reflect on their
own lives and the ways in which they can contribute to a more vibrant and
compassionate world.

Anti-Bland Brigade members swear an oath: to be themselves, spark conversations,
to be more mindful, empathetic, loving and playful in their daily lives.

There will be an opening reception on Saturday, August 17 from noon to 4pm. The
artist will be in attendance.

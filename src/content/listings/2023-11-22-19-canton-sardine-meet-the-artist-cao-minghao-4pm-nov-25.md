Meet the UBC Distinguished Visiting Artist Cao Minghao

Saturday, November 25, 2023
4 - 6 PM
Canton-sardine
071-268 Keefer St, Vancouver
*RSVP required

-

We are pleased to host a reception for artist, Cao Minghao, at Canton-sardine in
Chinatown. Cao Minghao (she/her) is an artist and transdisciplinary researcher who
collaborates with her partner, Chen Jianjun, in Chengdu, China. Their projects have
been
widely presented in top-level art events and institutions, including Documenta
15 (Kassel, Germany 2022), the 13th Shanghai Biennale (Shanghai, China 2021-
22), and the Centre Pompidou (Paris, France 2019). They were recipients of the
2022 Jonathan KS Choi Foundation Contemporary Art Award and the Cit
Internationale des Arts de Paris in 2020.

Cao Minghao is currently visiting Vancouver and the University of British Columbia
from November 16th to 30th, invited by the Department of Art History, Visual Art &
Theory (AHVA) as part of their Distinguished Visiting Artist series. Her artist talk will
be held at UBC on Wednesday, November 29th, co-organized by Green College and
AHVA, with support from the Departments of Geography and Asian Studies, and the
UBC Centre for Chinese Research. (Please find the details of the artist talk
in the following text.)

Canton-sardine is honoured to co-organize the "Meet the Artist" reception with AHVA
on Saturday, November 25, in our gallery. We would
like to invite you to join this
event and meet the artist. Please click this link to RSVP:

https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/meet-the-ubc-visiting-artist-cao-minghao-saturday-nov-
25-2023-46pm-tickets-761813644117?aff=oddtdtcreator

Canton-sardine is situated on the unceded territories of the Musqueam, Squamish,
and Tsleil-Waututh peoples. We honour, respect, and give thanks to our hosts.

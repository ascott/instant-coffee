MONTY CLARK

MARK LEWIS
APRIL 1 TO MAY 1, 2010
Opening Reception: Thursday, April 1, 6pm to 8pm
Artist in attendance
The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to announce an exhibition of new works
by Mark Lewis.
Internationally recognized for his silent, single-shot films, Lewis
examines visual-scapes through
panned movement and zoom manipulation. Time is central in his works, all of
which illuminate a single,
uninterrupted study. This exhibition will feature three new works by Lewis:
Hendon F.C. (2009), Pull
Focus: Gasometer (2010), and Willesden Laundrette; Reverse Dolly, Pan
Right, Friday Prayers (2010).
Mark Lewis was Canada's representative at the 53rd Venice Biennale
International Art Exhibition
(2009). Born in Hamilton, Ontario, Lewis now lives and works in London,
England. Lewis's films can be
found in the permanent collections of the Centre Pompidou (Paris), MuHKA
(Antwerp), the Museum of
Modern Art (New York), the National Gallery of Canada (Ottawa), and others.
His films have been
exhibited at such venues as the Provinicia di Nuoro (Italy), PS1
Contemporary Art Centre (New York)
and the Vancouver Art Gallery. Lewis is the co-editor and co-founder of the
international art journal
Afterall(London, Los Angeles), and professor in fine art at Central
Saint Martins College of Art and
Design, London.

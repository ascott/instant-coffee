Germinal
Matthew Suib + Nadia Hironaka (USA), Santiago Lara + Beatriz Coto (Spain),
Suhee Lee + Oliver Smith (UK)

OPENING April 7, 7-10pm
EXHIBITION April 8 - June 5

Germinal pulls together three works that explore ideas around hybridity,
change, transformation and potential. The works are populated by hybrid
creatures, words, languages & processes. NMG is proud to include the work
of Guggenheim Fellows Matthew Suib and Nadia Hironaka ; 5 screen video
installation 'Soft-Epic or; Savages of the Pacific West'. 

Some Adult Content.
Free Admission.

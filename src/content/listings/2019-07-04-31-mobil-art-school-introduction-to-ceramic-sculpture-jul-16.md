Introduction to ceramic sculpture
Jul. 16, 6:30 p.m. - 9:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

This course will introduce you to making ceramic sculptures from clay, and will cover the 
basics of prepping your clay and techniques for making your unique creation. Feel free 
to bring your own prepared ideas or use one of our suggestions. Just be prepared to 
get youre hands dirty! Taught by the talented Emmma Canning (@dirtspindle) Age 10+ 
welcome! Price includes materials and the cost of firing and glazing. Pieces will be fired 
and glazed and can be picked approximately 2 weeks after completion of the class.

Folded Paintbrush
Digital Works & Sculpture

Barry Doup

September 9 to October 7, 2023
Opening Saturday, September 9 at 2pm

Deluge Contemporary Art
636 Yates Street, Victoria BC | deluge.ca
Wednesday to Saturday, noon to 5pm

Over the last decade, Barry Doup has produced an acclaimed body of hand-drawn
and computer-generated animated films. Shaped in part by experimental writing and
drawing exercises, Doups films are often characterized by fragmented and porous
narrative structures, richly textured characters and surreal everyday situations. The
artist has recently produced a series of digital drawings and animations using software
developed for the Commodore
Amiga, a computer that was first introduced in the mid-
eighties and became the industry standard for graphics and animation. The resulting
works revel in the capacities of this early technology to evoke both strange and
nostalgic visual effects.

Born in Victoria in 1982, Barry Doup holds a Bachelor of Media Arts from Emily Carr
University of Art + Design, Vancouver. His films have been screened nationally and
internationally at festivals and venues including the Ann Arbor Film Festival,
International Film Festival Rotterdam, Anthology Film Archives (New York), Muse dart
contemporain de Lyon, Pleasure Dome (Toronto), MOCCA (Toronto), Whitechapel
Gallery (London), Centre Pompidou (Paris) and Tate Modern (London). Recent
exhibitions of his work have taken place at the Western Front (Vancouver) and
Ghebaly Gallery (Los Angeles).

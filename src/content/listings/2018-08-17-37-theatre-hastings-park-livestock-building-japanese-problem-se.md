Japanese Problem
Produced by Universal Limited
September 13-29, 2018
Hastings Park, Livestock Building
(The barn across from the Hastings Race Course)

Japanese Problem is the award-winning, site-responsive experience about the Japanese 
Canadian incarceration at Hastings Park. It invites a small audience into a stall, which 
residents have turned into a temporary home and place of wonder, as they are filled with the 
uncertainty of their next destination. The piece exists in the contemporary moment 
simultaneously, where evidence of Hastings Parks former tenants has been erased, where 
survivors are uncertain if they want their names included in a memorial; and where refugees 
to North America are being treated in a fashion that is terrifyingly familiar. Last year, this 
piece sold out before the show opened and our next presenter, Toronto's Soulpepper 
Theatre, has already sold out its October run. 

Co-creator Yoshi Bancroft says, Our premiere production at the Hastings Park Livestock 
Building " which is the historical site of the 1942 Japanese incarceration "was sold out 
before we opened. We were able to include a handful of walk-ups each performance, but we 
still had hundreds of people who were really disappointed they missed the piece.

We decided to increase the amount of performances we had last year, so that we could get 
everyone who missed the premiere into the show, adds co-creator Joanna Garfinkel. Were 
thrilled that people are really drawn to the show and that its impacting audiences like this. 
And when we found out our October Soulpepper tour was also sold-out before single tickets 
even went on sale, we knew wed have to do as many performances as possible to meet the 
audience demand the shows been receiving.

For audiences who saw the premiere and want to attend again, Bancroft notes, The 
material has grown since touring this past year. Weve expanded the piece to reflect the 
stories we have learned performing around the province, so anyone who saw the first 
production will see how its evolved since the premiere.

With more tour plans across Canada in the works, this may also be the last chance for 
Vancouver audiences to see Japanese Problem at the historical site itself.

ABOUT UNIVERSAL LIMITED
Universal Limited is a collective who works to uncover hidden stories in unusual locations. 
We work collectively to write and design our shows as experiences that are accessible to all. 
Previously they built the pedicab adventure Tour in Vancouver (premiering as part of the 
Cultural Olympiad/HIVE 3), Victoria (TheatreSKAM), and Toronto (SummerWorks), telling 
the hidden histories of previous residents "from First Nations, to waves of immigration, to 
the current stories buried beneath previously-familiar neighbourhoods.

For Japanese Problem Yoshi Bancroft and Joanna Garfinkel set out to tell the 
unacknowledged story of the Japanese Canadian Incarceration at Hastings Park, 
interviewing the incredible, resilient survivors and families who are grappling with the legacy 
of the Incarceration. The creators believe that our country, province, and citys legacies of 
racially-motivated uncompensated labour, exclusion and dispersal must never be forgotten.

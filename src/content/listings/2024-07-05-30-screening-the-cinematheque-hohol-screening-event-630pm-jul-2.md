Pabalik sa Takipsilim (Return to Twilight) - HOHOL Screening Event
Miko Revereza and Kidlat Tahimik

Thursday, July 25 / 6:30 PM / The Cinematheque (1131 Howe Street, Vancouver)

Four Short Films (2014 to 2023) by Miko Revereza and Kidlat Tahimiks Balikbayan
#1 Memories of Overdevelopment Redux III (2015) will be screened in this double
bill that explores personal, fictional and historical stories of Filipino migrants,
expatriates and balikbayans.

These screenings are part of HOHOL, a project presented by artists and curators
Patrick Cruz and Christian Vistan with grunt gallery, bringing together Filipino
contemporary artists from across the diaspora to reflect on their shared and
individual contexts and personal histories and to continue to think through themes
and questions of diasporic and postcolonial Filipino identities and practices.

Presented in partnership with the Cinematheque.

Sascha Yamashita
'Secret Sacred Ritual'
Exhibition Opening Reception: June 2 @ 7:30

Robert Lynds Gallery
1639 West 3rd Avenue
Vancouver, BC V6J 1K1

The Robert Lynds Gallery is pleased to present the work of Sascha
Yamashita. Yamashita’s interdisciplinary solo-exhibition, Secret Sacred
Ritual, explores elements of rite within his esoteric ceremonial
performance in the construction and deconstruction of the art object.
Yamashita transforms the gallery space into a sacrosanct domain to house
his series of process-based panels, and for the witnessing of the artifact
of his ceremonial practice. Examining secret societies, Yamashita’s work
references various religions and sacred cults throughout history through
the appropriation of symbols, and challenges the supremacy of the
commodified art object.

Exhibition date: June 2 - June 27
Established in 2012, Robert Lynds Gallery is an intimate space located in
the armoury district in Kitsilano, Vancouver. Robert Lynds Gallery
represents established contemporary artists who demonstrate analytic art
practices. RLG presents a vision that focuses on setting a precedent in
exhibiting local/national and international works, while aiming to advance
the intellectual and material vanguard; engaging public and industry
discourse on local and international platforms. The gallery publishes the
magazine, MAGTEAR, which concentrates on attracting and inspiring diverse
audiences in its dedication to advocating the advancement of knowledge
pertaining to the arts. RLG also juries Frontlines, which supports and
encourages the involvement of members within the emerging art scene,
fortifying its staying power as a prominent west-side gallery.

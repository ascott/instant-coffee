HERE + THERE
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
Low Residency Master of Applied Arts Graduate Exhibition
July 20 to 28, 2012
Charles H. Scott Gallery

Opening Reception: Thursday, July 19, 6pm to 9pm

Emily Carr University of Art + Design is pleased to present HERE + THERE, an
exhibition of work by the inaugural cohort of the Low Residency stream of the
Master of Applied Arts program. It showcases work reflective of over two years
of research and production.

Ric Beairsto
Jan Beringer
Bruce Emmett
G. Funo O'Kain
Greg Klassen
Galia Kwetny
Nathalie Lavoie
Joyce Lindemulder
David M.C. Miller

We are in the epoch of simultaneity; we are in the epoch of juxtaposition, the
epoch of near and far, side-by-side, of the dispersed.
--Michel Foucault

In this exhibition, the gallery is a meeting point. Nine artists have converged in
Vancouver for three consecutive summers to engage in a period of production,
study and reflection. The works in this room represent a culmination of those
summers in Vancouver, and of exchanges spoken from diverse locations--
international, local, urban, rural and remote during the intermediate months.

The artists are dispersed by geography, as much as their works are separated
by mode of representation, personal history, theory, and language.

The artists are connected by Vancouver, by the shared experience of the MAA
program, and by the influences they have had on one another.

You are invited to consider this process and the resulting works here as the final
products of a momentary, conclusive heterotopia.

Arts Council of Surrey
ARTS 2025
Call for Artists
Deadline to Submit: March 28, 2025

Arts Council of Surrey (ACS), in partnership with the Surrey Art Gallery, presents this
annual juried art
exhibition. Artwork will be on display in-person at the Surrey Art Gallery and
virtually
on the Arts Council of Surreys website (artscouncilofsurrey.ca).

ACS Members in good-standing for 2025 are invited to submit up to a maximum of
three (3) artworks for adjudication. Please note that adjudicators will only select up to
one (1) artwork per category per artist, to a maximum of two (2) artworks total per
artist.

To encourage applications by post-secondary art students, the membership
requirement is waived with a copy of a valid student ID accompanying the entry
form.

A complete submission should include: your filled-in entry form, and hi-res digital
images of up to three (3) art pieces. Your submission must be submitted via e-mail to
info@artscouncilofsurrey.ca by 1:00pm, Friday March 28, 2025. (Please see Pg 3 of
application regarding Criteria for Digital Submissions). Late submissions will NOT be
accepted.

For information, rates, and application forms to become a member of the Arts
Council of Surrey in order to be eligible to enter this competition please
contact ACS
at 604-594-2700 or info@artscouncilofsurrey.ca

Application form: https://artscouncilofsurrey.ca/wp-

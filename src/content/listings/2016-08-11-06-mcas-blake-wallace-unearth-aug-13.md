Opening
Blake Wallace - Un/Earth
MCAS

Saturday August 13th 7pm

The Maillardville Cultural Appreciation Society is pleased to present an
exhibition of new work by Blake Wallace. Growing from an in depth and
interminable dedication to drawing, Wallace's practice has grown through an
interests in the depiction of the fantastic and the absurd, from 19th
century plate illustrations to contemporary animation. For the exhibition
at MCAS he has produced drawings and sculptures serving as a pseudo
sociological overview for a set of fictional beings that exist between
animal and human. Using a recombinative collection of features from across
the zoological and cryptozoologicial spectrum, Wallace creates a new
society that appears at times to be an indecipherable merger of mouths eyes
teeth and proboscis.

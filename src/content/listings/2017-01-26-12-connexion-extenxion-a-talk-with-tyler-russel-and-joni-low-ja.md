Centre A, 229 E Georgia St, Vancouver

Executive Director of Centre A, Tyler Russell, and Connexion curator Joni Low invite

Hank Bull: Connexion Artist Talk

Sunday, January 29, 2pm
Burnaby Art Gallery
Artist Hank Bull gives a talk about the current exhibition, Hank Bull: Connexion. Bull has 
played an important role in Vancouver's telecommunications art scene and is known for 
his involvement with the Western Front and Centre A.

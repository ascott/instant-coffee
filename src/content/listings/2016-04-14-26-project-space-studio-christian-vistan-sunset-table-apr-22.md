Event:
Project Space's Monthly Open Studio | Christian Vistan | Sunset Table

Location: Project Space studio, 2 - 236 E Pender St, Vancouver
When: Friday, April 22, 7-10pm

Join Project Space for our Monthly Open Studio presentation of "Sunset
Table," a one-night exhibition of poem/research and an artist's book by
Christian Vistan, featuring three new collages and a reading by the artist.

"In the North Pacific Union Gleaner, Vol. 58, No. 17, April 29, 1963 (a
newsletter published by the Pacific Northwest Seventh-Day Adventists):
documentation of a 1962 Flood in North Philippines and relief, a Sunset
Table, for Friday, May 3,1963 and other events and announcements in the
congregation at the time.

On the bottom of the Sunset Table: 'Add one minute for each 13 miles west.
Subtract one minute for each 13 miles east.'"

-

CHRISTIAN VISTAN is a Vancouver-based Filipino-Canadian artist originally
from Bataan, a peninsular province in the Philippines. With an interest in
the in-between, his work, in painting, textiles, writing, and curation
occupy liminal moments and spaces. Recent projects and exhibitions include
Labada ng Makata/Poet Laundry (2016) at The Packing Plant in Nashville, TN,
and the No Shoes Reading Room (2015) at the 2015 Vancouver Art/Book Fair.
He is currently working towards a BFA in Visual Art with a minor in
Curatorial Studies at Emily Carr University,.

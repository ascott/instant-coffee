Wanda Koop and Gu Xiong | A moderated dialogue
November 12, 2009
5:30 pm to 7:30 pm

Emily Carr University of Art + Design hosts a moderated dialogue with Wanda
Koop and Gu Xiong.

Koop is one of Canada's most distinguished and inventive artists and has
received numerous awards, including the induction to the Order of Canada in
2006.

Koop's forthcoming solo exhibition at the Richmond Art Gallery, Face to
Face, will present portraits and figures spanning nearly 25 years,
including numerous works that have never been exhibited. The exhibition
will include works developed from her first visit to China as well as
recent works on robotics. The exhibition precedes and compliments Koop's
major exhibition at the National Gallery of Canada in 2011.

Xiong , a multi-media artist from China who now lives in Canada, works with
painting, drawing, printmaking, sculpture, photography, video, digital
imagery, text, performance art and installation. His work is represented in
the collections of the National Gallery of Canada, the China National
Museum of Fine Arts, and the Vancouver Art Gallery, among many other
museums and private collections.

Guest Curator, Deborah Koenker, will moderate the discussion. Admission is
free. Seating is limited.

12 November, 2009 | 5.30pm - 7.30pm
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
Intersections Digital Studios, Room 285
1399 Johnston Street, Granville Island
Vancouver

Artist talk with Ruth Beer, in conversation with Patrick James Bravo and
Justin Muir
Sunday, March 20, at 2pm

Malaspina Printmakers, 1555 Duranleau Street, Vancouver

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to present an artist talk with Ruth Beer,
in conversation with printmaking assistant Patrick James Bravo and curator
Justin Muir, on the occasion of her exhibition Extract, form, and print, at
Malaspina Printmakers until April 3, 2016.

Ruth Beer's work is multidisciplinary, including sculpture, video, woven
structures and interactive projections combining minimalism, craft, and
20th century avant-garde aesthetics. Her latest research and creation
project, "Trading Routes: Grease Trails, Oil Pipelines" is supported by the
Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council of Canada (SSHRC). She is
currently a Professor in the Faculty of Visual Art and Material Practice at
Emily Carr University of Art and Design.

Patrick James Bravo is currently completing his Bachelor of Fine Arts at
the Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design. His work has been included in
exhibitions in Vancouver, Australia, and New Zealand, and published in Woo,
the Emily Carr University student publication. He assisted Ruth Beer in the
production of new monoprints for this exhibition at Malaspina Printmakers.

Justin Muir is the Executive Director and Curator of Malaspina Printmakers.

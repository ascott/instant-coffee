FRONT Magazine is holding our first ever Meet'n'Greet & Brainstorm with a
focus on generating content ideas for our Jan/Feb and Mar/Apr issues
(themed Properties and Disciplines, respectively).

Thursday, November 13th, 7pm
Western Front's Grande Luxe Hall - 303 e. 8th Ave

Space is limited! Please RSVP before noon on Nov. 12th by emailing us at
frontmagazine@front.bc.ca

__Why Hold a Brainstorm?

FRONT Magazine is approaching our 20th anniversary. Published by the
Western Front Society, we have been a submissions-based bi-monthly vehicle
for presenting the work of established and emerging artists, work that
includes drawing, photography, experimental writing, artist projects,
poetry and ephemera.

We will continue to publish these things, but we want to do MORE.

This is an invitation to artists and writers to help us dream up new
projects for the magazine on a one-off and continuing basis. We will not be
accepting content at this meeting -- just indulging in some serious
speculation.

__What Happens at One of These Things?

We hope to...

Meet artists, writers, event organizers, obsessive proof readers and
energetic interns
Find out about awesome Vancouver-area artists, projects and events that
we should be covering through articles, interviews, essays and photo essays
Dream up ideas for small, repeatable content items
Learn about studio collectives we can profile in our regular column
Solicit proposals for guest editors and guest art directors
Educate artists about what works in a magazine context and help them to
develop work for the format

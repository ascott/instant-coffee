The Contemporary Art Gallery is accepting applications for the position of Program Administrator.
This is a full-time, one year maternity leave replacement position.

The ideal candidate will be familiar with contemporary art, non-profit administration and will have 
excellent skills and experience in a full range of administrative procedures. The position reports to 
the Director/Curator, supervises the work of the Gallery Coordinator, Bookkeeper, Education 
Programmer, Preparator and volunteers, and is responsible for ensuring the professional 
standards of routine gallery operations. 

Responsibilities will include:

•	Management of the Society’s finances;
•	Coordination of the gallery programs;
•	Management of computer network and information systems;
•	Supervision of facility maintenance;
•	Management of office systems and equipment;
•	Assisting with grant applications and reporting;
•	Other duties as required. 

Interested candidates will have the following qualifications:

•	Excellent interpersonal, written and verbal skills;
•	Proven organizational abilities;
•	Ability to work in a team environment;
•	Knowledge of budgeting and accounting procedures, familiarity with computer and manual 
accounting systems;
•	Proficiency with MS Office applications and Simply Accounting;
•	Knowledge of computer network systems and maintenance;
•	Some knowledge of contemporary art is necessary;

Salary range: $45,500 – 56,000 plus benefits

Hours are Monday to Friday 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. plus occasional openings and special events. 

Application deadline: August 15, 2008 6 pm.
Start date: September 15, 2008

Please email your resume and cover letter to info@contemporaryartgallery.ca
or send it by mail to 555 Nelson Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada V6B 6R5

We would like to thank all candidates for their applications but only those selected for an 
interview will be contacted.

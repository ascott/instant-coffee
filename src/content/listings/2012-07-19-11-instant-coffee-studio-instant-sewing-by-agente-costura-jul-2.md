Instant Sewing @ Instant Coffee

Sunday July 22
441 Powell Street
4 pm for upcycling
8 pm performance

AGENTE COSTURA (Portuguese double standard: sewing agent/we sew) is a
Brazilian upcycling studio that will be in residence for a day at Instant
Coffee
Studio. We will be selling Brazilian Vintage, upcycling on the spot, and
presenting
a performance piece that brings together fashion, music and dance. The sewing
machine acts as a percussion instrument, and the clothes are transformed
during
the show.

we make it all up on the spot!

Border Zones Webzine Project

Border Zones: New Art Across Cultures is an exhibition of international
contemporary art at the UBC Museum of Anthropology until September 12,
2010. It brings together the work of twelve artists engaged in a dialogue
about cultural boundaries.

Border Zones.ca: The Ideas Behind the Exhibition, is an interactive online
magazine at www.BorderZones.ca. Here you'll find personal and provocative
articles by distinguished contributors such as award-winning journalist Jan
Wong, educator and activist Gerald Taiaiake Alfred, and filmmaker and
artist Loretta Todd, among others. You'll also find aritst interview
videos, a writing contest, regular updates on artist files, artwork
exclusive to the webzine, provocative reviews of the exhibition, and a blog
devoted to
the idea of borders.

Participating artists include Hayati Mokhtar, Dain-Iskandar Said, John
Wynne, Edward Poitras, Thamotharampillai Shanaathanan, Tania Mouraud,
Marianne Nicolson, Gu Xiong, Prabakar Visvanath, Rosanna Raymond, Ron
Yunkaporta, and Laura Wee Láy Láq.

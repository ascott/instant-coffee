The Photography Annual
CALL FOR ENTRIES - JURIED ISSUE
For publication in Pipeline July/August 2014

This year for the first time, our photography issue is a juried issue.
Hong Kong-based M+ curator Tobias Berger, Paris-based art historian and 
theorist André Rouillé and Chinese photographer Maleonn join our Editor-in-
Chief Cristina Sanchez-Kozyreva in the selection of the best submissions.

Eligibility:
This Call for Entries is open to all photographers with a particular interest in 
making works relevant to issues of contemporary art (reportage or fashion 
photography are excluded). Pipeline magazine invites experimental and mixed 
techniques, all mediums and schools of thought, with a particular emphasis 
on singularity and novelty, independently of your age.

We encourage submissions with images from a single project or limited to two 
/ three bodies of works for a cohesive selection for the juried issue. A 
cohesive photography project shows the jury that you’ve carefully edited 
your selection and are committed to your photography.

Please send low-resolution files, possibly in a PDF, along with a statement 
about your body of work to PhotoPipeline2014@mail.com.

Only submissions sent to the magazine will be considered.

Contents of Submission:
Easy to access selection of images + max 600 words statement + personal 
details such as contact, year of birth, location.

Submission Deadline: May 26th, 2014.

About Pipeline:
Pipeline is an independent contemporary art magazine based in Hong Kong.
The magazine’s name comes from its focus in bridging artists from East and 
West, and to connect the different types of curatorial thinking and aesthetic 
appreciation across the globe with an open mind. The magazine editorial is 
international, non-event or hype driven, focused on contemporary art but 
non-academic.
Some issues offer a stage for artists and experimental initiatives while others 
gather the works of artists around the globe through a particular theme 
(previous themes included Death, Hoarding, Bruce Lee, and Art and Fashion). 
In addition to its international editorial the magazine offers reviews of shows 
as well as an exclusively Asian Calendar of contemporary art shows (also 
available at www.hkgalleryguide.com).
Pipeline is published six times a year by independent publishing house Double 
Pipe Publishers Ltd. in Hong Kong. The magazine is available in bookshops 
across Asia and online on Apple Newsstand and Magzter.com
For more information about the magazine, please visit www.pipelinemag.com

Acceptance notification:
Selected artists will be notified personally by email before June 11th, 2014, 
and required to send high-resolution images.

Please do not contact Pipeline or any members of the jury to inquire about 
whether your images were chosen.

Use Rights:
Artists maintain copyright on all of their work. By submission, artists grant 
Pipeline the right to print their images and profiles for the purpose of showing 
their works in the July/August 2014 issue of the magazine, The Photography 
Annual, for marketing the magazine and for reproduction online, all in relation 
and limited to The Photography Annual and the magazine. Artist’s recognition 
is provided with any use. Submitting artists will be added to Pipeline’s 
newsletter subscriber list. They may opt out using a link on each newsletter 
at any time.

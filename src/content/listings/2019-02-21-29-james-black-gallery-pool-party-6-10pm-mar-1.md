James Black Gallery

Teresa Holly (@tina__tuna) and Kelsey Jacobsen (yuckyboistudio.com)
March 1st 2019 Opening 6-10pm, March 1st-15th, 2019 By Appointment
144 E6 ave Vancouver, BC V5T 1J5

In preparation for the warm summer days ahead, The James Black Gallery presents Pool 
Party featuring Vancouver artists Teresa Holly (she/her) and Kelsey Jacobson (they/them) 
working in soft sculpture and illustrations.

Holly and Jacobson explore serene setting of pool parties where frolicking, scantily clad 
bodies are abound. Behind the surface of this scene however, Holly and Jacobson provide a 
revealing lens which distorts and accentuates the awkward feeling that comes from having a 
body not in line with how you see yourself.

Made from bright fabric and other textile mediums, Jacobsens sculptures are displayed the 
way that teenagers curate their bedrooms to create a cultural womb that cultivates comfort 
andexpression. They manipulate the exhibition space to create an environment immersive to 
the viewer which celebrates diversity and queerness.

Hollys work engages different perceptions of the female form by depicting impossible 
contortions and proportions that help skew the lines of femininity. As a response to her own 
dysmorphia, her figures are often imagined in sexualized poses with little to no clothing. 
These figures give a glimpse of only a shiny boot or arm-length glove to accentuate a 
desired strength in stance or confidence in touch. 

Pool Party is curated by Moody Rose Christopher in association with the mentorship 
program at The James Black Gallery.

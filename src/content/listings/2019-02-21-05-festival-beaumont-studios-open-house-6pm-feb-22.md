LOCAL ARTIST HUB TO LAUNCH A MONTHLY ARTS FESTIVAL STARTING THIS 
FRIDAY FEB 22

Finale Friday is a monthly visual and performing arts festival in the heart of Vancouvers 
creative hub
- The Beaumont Studios. Like a mini culture crawl - Finale Friday is produced by members 
of The Beaumont Studios Artist Society.

The first ever Finale Friday will be taking place THIS FRIDAY starting at 6pm!
This particular event is hosted by Regi Anselmi, a longtime resident of the Beaumont 
Studios and owner of Hair Love Studios.
What started out as a small community building idea - has turned into an event of epic 
proportions including live music, several rooms of entertainment, open studios and more:
At this LAUNCH there will be:

40+ open artist studios
A Rumpus room with Disco Bingo & Games
Mini roller disco performance
7 piece funk band (Confiture)
Disco DJ
Fashion show & release of Soul-Sister fashion line
Historical Tours of the Beaumont Studios
The event is all ages and there is something for everyone.
Tickets are $5 for Beaumont Members and $20 for non-members.
Upgrade your ticket to a $20/ yearly membership for a chance to WIN awesome prizes!
The Beaumont Studios has been a staple in Vancouvers arts & culture scene for the last 15 
years.
Founded by Jude Kusnierz, The Beaumont is home to 40 artist studios and 70 resident 
artists, an event
space, a thrift store and a gallery.
Come out to this event and be a part of Vancouvers thriving Arts Tribe at The Beaumont 
Studios
which is - the place to BE.
Rachel Kirkpatrick
Member and Resident Relations
The Beaumont Studios Artist Resource Society

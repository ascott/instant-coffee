WE ARE SEEKING Vancouver-made short films or videos under 5 minutes.
STYLE Animated, abstract, and narrative (must be family friendly).
DEADLINE JUNE 1st, 2014.
TO SUBMIT Email a link to your full film (a screenable hi-res digital file: 
YouTube, Vimeo, other link) to the Iris Film Collective at this email address: 
irisfilmcollective@gmail.com

We are looking for local films and videos for outdoor screenings in late June 
as part of a Small Neighbourhood Grants Project in the Grandview-
Woodlands area of Vancouver. Films will be screened from windows in the 
neighbourhood onto walls, sheets, or other surfaces. The Festival will be lo-
fi, street-level, with locations revealed a day prior to the shows. Shows will 
tentatively fall on June 20/21/22 of 2014. We are interested in local works 
only, under 5 minutes, and family friendly!

Do you have a spot you think would work well for this event? 
A great window with a nice wall opposite in the Grandview-Woodlands 
neighbourhood?
Sidewalks, alleys and yards are preferable, but no public space (ie parks) as 
this requires permits etc. Let us know and maybe we can screen near you!

Please spread the word…

-The Iris Film Collective

Join us for an exciting evening of science-art and discussion as we celebrate the launch 
of RE-COMPOSITION online in the Polyfield Gallery and in Science World at TELUS 
World of Sciences Atrium.

During this engaging event, four local artists " Cara Gibson, Scott Mallory, Jen 
Burgess, and Katrina Vera Wong " will showcase and discuss their work, creative 
processes, and inspirations in relation to the theme RE-COMPOSITION. After enjoying 
some amazing science-art and thought-provoking conversation, we invite you to round 
out the evening with networking and drinks at our cash bar. 

Doors open @ 6:30pm
Artist Showcase @ 7pm
Networking with cash bar @ 8 pm

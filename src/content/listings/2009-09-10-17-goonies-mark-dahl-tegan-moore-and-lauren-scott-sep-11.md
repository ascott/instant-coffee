Goonies
Exhibition
And laid him on the green: Mark Dahl, Tegan Moore, and Lauren Scott
Opening reception Friday, September 11
September 12-18

And laid him on the green presents a selection of work from Vancouver artists Mark Dahl, Tegan 
Moore, and Lauren Scott, examining the manner in which specific objects and scenarios can be 
made uncertain through vaguely scientific methods and purposefully poetic choices.

The exhibition is curated by Francesca Bennett for Goonies, a multi-purpose studio and gallery 
space located at 108 east Hastings. The gallery will have open hours for the duration of the 
exhibition: 12-5 on September 12, 13, 16 and 18, 5-9 on September 15 and 17, and closed on 
September 14.

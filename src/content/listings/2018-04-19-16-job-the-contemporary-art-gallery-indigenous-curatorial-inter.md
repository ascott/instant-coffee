Indigenous Curatorial Intern

The Contemporary Art Gallery (CAG) is searching for a part-time, fixed-term Indigenous
Curatorial Assistant, a position made possible by the generosity of the CMA-RBC

Foundation Museum Mentorship Program

Position: Indigenous Curatorial Assistant

Reports to: Curator and Curator of Learning & Public Engagement
Works With: All Staff, external contacts: artists, museums, galleries, partner 
organizations, suppliers; visitors, members and others

-- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -- -
The Organization
The Contemporary Art Gallery is Vancouvers longest standing independent public art 
gallery dedicated to the research, exhibition, education and documentation of 
contemporary visual art as it is practiced locally through to internationally. It aspires to 
generate significant audiences for its innovative and diverse programs through free 
access and a profile that is international in scope. CAG aims to be perceived as a 
producer and transmitter of new ideas and activities, as a container for artistic 
experience. Radiating out from our centre in Vancouver, our program seeks to signify an 
understanding of art as something of meaning in our daily lives, asserting a continuum, 
enmeshed within and of significance to all other experience.

For more information about the CAG, please visit www.contemporaryartgallery.ca.

Job Summary

Made possible by the generous support of the CAM-RBC Foundation Museum 
Mentorship Program, the Indigenous Curatorial Assistant is a 20 week mentorship 
opportunity providing an Indigenous emerging museum professional with valuable 
curatorial experience. The successful candidate will contribute to all aspects of program 
planning, installation and delivery for the CAGs upcoming solo presentation of the work 
of Jeneen Frei Njootli (July 12 " September 16, 2018), which includes the commission 
of new work and the creation of the first publication dedicated solely to Frei Njootlis 
practice. They will also be involved in early planning toward related forthcoming 
programming as well as more general planning, programming and all work as part of the 
overall staff team. The position is a limited-term; part-time contract (21 hours/week). 
Some evening and weekend work is required.

Key Responsibilities

Reporting to the Curator and Curator of Learning and Public Engagement, the 
Indigenous Curatorial Assistant will contribute to all program planning, installation and 
delivery for Frei Njootlis upcoming exhibition and publication. The Indigenous Curatorial 
Intern will also contribute to the preparation and delivery of all public programming 
initiatives to be developed alongside Frei Njootlis exhibition, and will have a voice in 
various curatorial planning meetings for later 2018-19 exhibitions, as well as 
organizational planning meetings to develop an understanding of thematic planning 
across our broader exhibition, residency and off-site projects programming, and 
understand the critical process, mandate, and work flow of this visual arts organization 
as a whole.

Key responsibilities will include:

Assist programming staff with curatorial administration and coordination
Attending studio visits with exhibiting artist(s)
Assist with the coordination of production support for new commissioned work
Assist with artwork shipping and storage, loan forms, artist contracts, condition 
reports, insurance lists, checklists, labels and documentation
Working closely with Curator to assist with the coordination of all aspects of 
publication development and realization, which includes correspondence with artist and 
designer, content development, correspondence with contributing writers, establishing 
and maintaining timelines and budgets, image permissions, proofing, file delivery
Work with the Curator on early planning toward other forthcoming programming and
exhibitions with First Nations artists
Contributing research and resources for the programming of the CAG Reading Room
Working closely with Curator of Learning to contribute to the development, 
coordination
and realization of all public programming for the exhibition, which may include artist
talks, roundtables, readings, workshops, forums, or symposia
In consultation with the Curator of Learning, producing resource packages, Teachers
Guides, and providing other support for the Schools Program
Preparing for and delivering a public guided tour of the exhibition
Producing blog entries for CAG blogs, which may include interviewing and video-
recording artists and reflecting upon programmed events
Working with artists representatives to obtain images and related information for
marketing, publicity, learning and public programs as needed
Qualifications
Must be a Canadian Indigenous citizen (First Nations, Inuit, Mtis)
Must be 18 to 35 years of age at the start of the mentorship
Must be enrolled within or a recent graduate of (within past 24 months) a museums
studies program and/or arts studies, curatorial, conservation or an arts administrators
program
Must be willing to commit to the full duration of the mentorship
Must not be a current or previous employee of the museum/organization
Skills and Abilities
Excellent time management skills and demonstrated ability to manage multiple 
projects
Good understanding of the arts and cultural sector with knowledge of contemporary 
art
Outstanding verbal and written communication skills
Careful and thorough attention to detail and the ability to research, integrate and 
present
data
Within a team environment, ability to work independently to support the work of others
Scheduling and coordinating activities and resources
Enter and integrate data and present information in documents and spreadsheets
Coordinate and deliver administrative support
Strong computer skills in a PC environment including Microsoft Word, Excel, Outlook
Creative Suite skills are desirable

AUDAIN ART MUSEUM

Visit the Audain Art Museum in Whistler on June 24 for the Public Opening of Manabu
Ikeda: Flowers from the Wreckage from 11am to 6pm. Revel in over 60 meticulously
detailed pen-and-ink drawings that are filled with astonishing images. From 1-2pm,
Manabu Ikeda and Curator Kiriko Watanabe will be signing exhibition catalogues in the
Museum lobby. As well, Manabu Ikeda will be working
on his latest piece in the Upper
Galleries from 3pm to 4:30pm, in his Studio Gallery. This interactive studio will be open
for viewing every Thursday, Friday, and Saturday at the same time.

4350 Blackcomb Way, PO Box 5
Whistler BC

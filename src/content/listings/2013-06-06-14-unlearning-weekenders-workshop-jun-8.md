Unlearning Weekenders are a series of workshops that invite the public to co-
develop a public procession. We are looking for new ways to activate our 
perceptions and find creative forms of criticality. With the use of props, 
costume and our individual + collective bodies we wish to challenge invisible 
social structures, disrupt hierarchies and celebrate new narratives. The 
procession looks to reclaim internal personal space and external public 
space.

* WORKSHOP DETAILS:

WEEKENDER 2
June 8th & 9th 1-6pm @ Public Dreams, #141-2050 Scotia St., back alley 
entrance

“Costumes and Props for Unlearning”

These two days of workshops will combine open craft studio with 
presentations and collaborative construction. The public is invited to prepare 
costumes, accessories and props that can function as triggers and instigators 
of unlearning during the procession: i.e. props that require collaborative 
engagement, costumes to wear with strangers or that challenge our usual 
habits, and objects for ceremonial acts. Small readings and presentations will 
be performed throughout the day to help inspire ideas and critical thinking. 
There will be one ongoing collaborative project over the two days: designing 
and building the props and tool for the shared picnic during the June 15th 
procession. Bring material, ideas, readings and inspiration! We will need lots 
of hands to help out – come on down!!! 

These workshops are open to drop-in; to participate in collaborative prop + 
costume project please arrive at 1pm.

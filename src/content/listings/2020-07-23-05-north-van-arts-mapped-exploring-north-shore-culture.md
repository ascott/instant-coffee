New Exhibition
Mapped: Exploring North Shore Culture
Until Saturday, September 5 at CityScape Community ArtSpace
335 Lonsdale Avenue
North Vancouver

Our sense of place is shaped by our experience of culture; by creative industries,
stories, events, and art. Guiding us through the assets that create our local identity, the
North Shore Culture Compass is an online navigational tool helping us to visualize the
cultural landscape of the North Shore, allowing residents, visitors, and planners to
connect with the community.

Mapped: Exploring North Shore Culture invites visitors to
discover the powerful ability of
cultural mapping to build community. This interactive exhibition offers an opportunity to
learn how to use the map, and to experience mapping as a means of defining space.
The North Shore Culture Compass encourages us to acknowledge our history and to
allow it to guide us as we imagine the future of our shared cultural community.

For further information, please contact info@northvanarts.ca or call us at 604-988-6844.

the Tsleil-Waututh, Squamish, and Musqueam Nations.

About North Van Arts
North Van Arts (North Vancouver Community Arts Council) is a grassroots, not-for-profit,
charitable organization with a mandate to build strong and healthy communities through
the arts. Founded in 1969,
North Van Arts has been dedicated to supporting artists and
the community by maximizing the intrinsic value of the arts in all disciplines for over 50
years

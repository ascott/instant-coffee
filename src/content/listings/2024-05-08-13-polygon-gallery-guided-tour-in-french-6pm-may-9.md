Guided Tour in French | Polygon Gallery

Date: Thursday, May 9th

Time: 6:00pm-7:30pm

THE TOUR: With the Polygon Gallery, Alliance Franaise Vancouver invites its
members, students and community to come together for a guided tour in French of
the exhibition "Velvet Terrorism: Pussy Riot's Russia".The tour will be led by Elaine
Gervais, Polygon's French-speaking guide-interpreter.

THE EXHIBITION:
"Velvet Terrorism: Pussy Riots Russia" is the first exhibition
about the feminist art collective Pussy Riot. Formed in Moscow in 2011, Pussy Riot
came together in opposition to their countrys increasingly authoritarian government.
The exhibition features vivid documentation of the groups performances and street
activism as assembled by member Maria (Masha) Alyokhina.

THE VENUE: Polygon Gallery continues the forty-year legacy of Presentation House
Gallery, introducing the public to the most visionary artists of our time. A new
waterfront landmark on Vancouver's North Shore, Polygon offers a unique space to
discover contemporary visual art, particularly photography.

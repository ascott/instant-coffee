Exercise is pleased to announce: 

Tiziana La Melia
Neck of Thirsty Flower

August 25 - September 23

Please join us for the opening reception Friday August 24, 7-10 pm

Tiziana La Melia (b.1982, Palermo, Italy) is a Vancouver based artist working 
in painting, poetry, drawing, collage, sculpture, and performance. She 
received her BFA at the Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design (2008) and her 
MFA from the University of Guelph (2011). Recent group exhibitions include 
Freedom of Assembly at Oakville Gallery, Oakville (2012),Sediment, G 
Gallery, Toronto (2012), and The Most She Weighed, The Least She Weighed 
at Susan Hobbs Gallery, Toronto (2011). Solo exhibition include Table of 
Contents (Accompanying Poem) at Clint Roenisch, Toronto (2011) and The 
Hands of V&U at G Gallery, Toronto (2011). Her writing has appeared in 
Pyramid Power, W, Pelt, and West Coast Line.

A print edition and a publication by La Melia will be launched at the end of the 
exhibition. The publication includes a collection of poems, short stories, 
anecdotes and an essay by contributors: Julia Feyrer, Kayla Guthrie, Tamara 
Henderson, Oscar Hüber, Jen Hutton, Maryse Lariviere, Michael Loncaric, 
Nicolas Matranga, and Lisa Robertson.

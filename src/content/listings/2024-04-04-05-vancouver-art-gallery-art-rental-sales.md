CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS | Art Rental & Sales operated by the Vancouver Art Gallery

Art Rental & Sales is a not-for-profit program operated by the Vancouver Art Gallery
with a mandate of supporting emerging artists
throughout British Columbia. Through
the rental and sale of original artwork by a juried selection of artists, AR&S generates
income for artists and provides funding for programs at the Vancouver Art Gallery.

Once a year, Art Rental & Sales hosts an open call for artists based in British Columbia
to consign artwork to our program. All submissions are reviewed by a selection
committee, and applicants are notified as to the status of their submission within 2
months of our submission deadline.

This years call is open from April 2nd through April 30th, 2024. To learn more or to
apply, please visit https://artrentalandsales.com/pages/submissions

Due to the volume of applications we receive, we kindly request no phone calls or in-
person visits regarding the submission process.

The Vancouver Art Gallery commits to providing equitable access to everyone. We
actively support accessibility, anti-racism, LGBTQ2S+ inclusion and encourage
diversity in our staff, visitors, volunteers and
programming. We cultivate a safer space
where all voices are heard, valued, and represented.

WOVEN : Setsuko Piroches Wonderland

Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre

6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby BC V5E 4M7

Opening reception March 16, 2024, 1 - 3pm with artist in attendance

We are delighted to present the unique world of Setsuko Piroche (ne Setsuko Hane),
a colourful and diverse cross-cultural artist whose career spans more than 70 years,
co-curated by guest curator Makiko Hara and NNMCC director-curator Sherri
Shinobu Kajiwara.

Setsuko was born in Asakusa, Tokyo in 1932 and studied under Setsu Asakura after
the Second World War. After 10 years of exhibiting Japanese paintings as a member
of the Shinseisaku Bijutsu Kyokai, she left the closed world of
Japanese painting and
departed for adventures to India and Australia, where she discovered a variety of
cultures. She moved to BC in 1967 with Pierre Piroche, a French adventurer and
botanical garden owner whom she met in India. In the 1970s, she was part of an
artist community at Handcraft House in North Vancouver, where she learned
weaving techniques. This inspired numerous creative experimental expressions,
including woven sculptures, dolls, and interactive installations that allowed viewers to
touch and enter into the works. In the 1970s, her artworks were highlighted as a new
feminist art movement that bridged the craft movement and contemporary art. This
exhibition brings together more than 40 pieces of her unique artistic worldview,
including three-dimensional textile works that were central to the Arts & Crafts
Movement in the 1970s, suspensions, nihonga paintings she mastered before
immigrating to Canada, and illustrations for children books published in both
Japanese and
English. Please enjoy Setsuko's curious and mysterious world.

I myself strongly believe that textile or woven material art are adding greatly to our
existing limits by providing yet another frontier in giving soft, airy, or transparent
feeling to the spaces we live in. It is not a passing fad- it is here to stay

A quote from a brochure, Setsuko Piroche Soft Sculpture and Painting from 1965-78,
Surrey Art Gallery, 1978

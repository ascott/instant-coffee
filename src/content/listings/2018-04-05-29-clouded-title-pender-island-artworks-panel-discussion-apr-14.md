CLOUDED TITLE

artworks and discussions about ownership & launch of Citizen Artist News

Clouded Title is a series of artworks and conversations about ownership " its 
ambiguities, histories, and areas of contestation, especially in relation to land. 
Alternative forms of claim-making and landholding models, especially those 
emphasizing social and ecological relationships over private possession, are explored. 
The project is led by Emily Artinian, artist and founder of Street Road Artists Space 
(Pennsylvania), and Fawn Daphne Plessner, artist and academic (Vancouver).

Launch of Citizen Artist News: Clouded Title

CA News: Clouded Title is a special edition newspaper on the topic of the Douglas 
Treaty - a treaty with a history of disputed interpretations and widely differing world 
views of land and ownership. The newspaper presents readers with a thought 
experiment and explores various meanings (both historical and contemporary) of the 
Treaty, contrasting it with discussions of a WSNE- creation story, providing readers 
with an opportunity to puzzle through and reflect upon the implications of the Treaty and 
being present on WSNE- Nation lands today.

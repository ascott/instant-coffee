Opening Reception: Fri. April 26, 7-9PM 
Free and Open to the Public
April 26 - June 4
268 Keefer St suite 205, Vancouver, BC

Intersection-Two Cities is an ongoing collaboration by photographer Thomas Lin (Hong Kong) 
and installation artist George Ho (Canada). Comprised of a series of poetic tte-bche images 
where the urban landscape of Hong Kong and Vancouver merge and blur, the exhibition traces 
a transpacific partnership of thirty years even as it meditates upon questions of memory, history, 
and chance.

This project was inspired by Yichang Liu's influential novel, Intersection (1972).

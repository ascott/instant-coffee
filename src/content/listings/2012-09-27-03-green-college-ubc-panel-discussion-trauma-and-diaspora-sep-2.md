TITLE: Trauma and Diaspora
PANELISTS: Janet MacArthur, Creative and Critical Studies, UBC-O; Judy 
Fong Bates, writer, storyteller; Tamio Wakayama, photographer; 
YaliniDreams, poet, playwright
DATE: September 27, 8:00 pm - 9:00 pm
LOCATION: Coach House, Green College, UBC (6201 Cecil Green Park 
Road, Vancouver)
LECTURE SERIES: Canada Council Writer-in-Residence at Green College 
Series
DESCRIPTION: A varied panel of artists will question how trauma is portrayed 
in literature and performance, particularly in relation to the issue of trauma as 
experienced by diasporic people, first in the mother land and then the trauma 
brought on by migration. This event is open to the UBC community and the 
public to attend at no cost.

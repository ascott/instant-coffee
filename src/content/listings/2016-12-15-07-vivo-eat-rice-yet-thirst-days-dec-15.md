December 15th at 7:30pm
VIVO Media Arts Centre
2625 Kaslo St., Vancouver, BC
(604) 872-8337

The programme includes poetry reading by Fred Wah, videos by Michael Rakowitz 
and Ng Chee Wang, talk by Meeru Dhalwala, and performance by Vanessa Richards 
+ Anna Baignoche. There will be drinks (throughout) and food (at the end); 
admission is free.

It's part of Thirst Days, a monthly series at VIVO Media Arts Centre in Vancouver 
initiated and coordinated by Jayce Salloum. Diyan and I are focusing on the theme 
of eating as a communal experience, as a community-building act, as a gifting, 
sharing and building of mutual understanding, and perhaps more specifically, on rice 
as a staple that much of the world's population consumes, depends on and 
identifies with culturally and corporeally.

Colleen Brown
'Three Sets'

opening reception: 29 July 7 - 9pm 

Unit 17 is thrilled to announce its second offsite event as part of Les Jardins: Three Sets 
by Colleen Brown.Three Sets takes place at Copley Community Orchard and Crows 
Point Community Garden in East Vancouver where the artist has installed three new 
sculptural works. Each sculpture - or set - is positioned amongst a select plant, 
including Dandelions, Buttercups and Tomatoes. The imagined flora theatres are 
inspired by significant Classic and Modernist theatre and rock concerts.

Colleen Brown (b. 1965) explores the relationships of objects and materials as a means 
of thinking through abstraction and social encounters. Brown lives in Vancouver and 
holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts from Emily Carr University of Art + Design (Vancouver) 
and a Master of Fine Arts from the Milton Avery Graduate School of the Arts, Bard 
College, Annandale-on-Hudson (New York). Recent exhibitions and events have been 
held at the Vancouver Art Gallery; Western Gallery, Bellingham; Hedreen Gallery, 
Seattle; Airbnb, Seattle. Brown is the recipient of the Vancouver 2016 Portfolio Prize.

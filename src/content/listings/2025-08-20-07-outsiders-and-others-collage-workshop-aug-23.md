Seema Shah: Missing Pieces / solo exhibition

August 2 - 30, 202 at Outsiders and Others
#100-938 Howe Street, Vancouver, BC

Missing Pieces offers a subtle and nuanced representation of the lived experience of
complex trauma, challenging preconceived ideas about how personal trauma
manifests and is creatively expressed. The work doesnt scream out, but rather calls
the viewer in. The exhibition is centred around a collection of 366 miniature collages
on shipping tags, one created every day of 2024. Collages on vintage envelopes
accompany the tags. www.outsidersandothers.com/seema-shah-missing-pieces

There will be a collage workshop with Seema on Saturday, August 23 from 2-4 pm at
Outsiders and Others.

In this workshop, participants will create mini self-portraits on shipping tags. Space is
limited; registration required. For more information, visit www.eventbrite.com/e/tag-
youre-it-a-self-reflective-collage-workshop-with-seema-shah-tickets-1462289530349

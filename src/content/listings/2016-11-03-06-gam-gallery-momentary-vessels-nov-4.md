Please join us at the Gam Gallery for the opening reception of 'Momentary 
Vessels' with works by Julia Kreutz in the front gallery and 'Flags for New 
Countries' with works by Tristesse Seeliger in the studio gallery.

November 4, 2016 - November 26, 2016
Opening reception November 4, 7 pm - 11 pm

'Momentary Vessels' aims to describe our spirit through what we hold and give 
symbolic reference. Vessels became a fascination for Kreutz during her 
undergraduate degree while majoring in ceramics. As ancient ceramic vessels 
describe life of cultures centuries ago, the works in 'Momentary Vessels' 
illuminate the qualities of life that are ephemeral and exist only for a moment.

This exhibition is a part of the Heart of the City Festival and the Eastside Culture 
Crawl.

Young creators art camp Aug 12-16, 2019
Aug. 12-16 9:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Last chance for summer 2019! 
Our innovative and extremely fun summer camp will expose participants to a variety of 
media and processes, from clay sculpture to printmaking to building projects and more. 
Each day the group will learn something new from an expert teacher in the field and 
make a variety of work throughout the week. The kids will end the week by displaying 
their work in an exhibition in our beautiful space and be able to invite parents and 
friends to come and appreciate the work! We guarantee a safe, fun environment. Ages 
10-14 $280
Sorry, we are unable to accommodate drop-ins. Please pre-register and read our FAQ.

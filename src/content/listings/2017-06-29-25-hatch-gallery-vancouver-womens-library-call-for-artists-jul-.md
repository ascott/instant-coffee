Deadline July 15th - Call for Artists - Hatch Gallery and Vancouver Women's Library

The Hatch Gallery and the Vancouver Women's Library are accepting proposals from
women for an upcoming exhibition taking place on September 5th to 22nd, 2017. The
exhibition will be hosted at the Hatch Gallery in the AMS Student Nest at UBC on
unneeded Coast Salish Territories.

Interested women, of any creed/race/class/gender/sexuality/age/profession, are
encouraged to submit a brief biography, relevant images and/or statement in one Project
Proposal.

Please e-mail your file to hatch@ams.ubc.ca

*All artistic mediums are welcome including textiles, art, poetry, performance, film etc.

Ketty Haolin Zhang
Nocturnal Daydream
Opening Thursday June 27, 7-11pm
1636 Venables St, Vancouver, BC V5L 2H2

Nocturnal Daydream settles on nightlife as a way through which to re-address feelings of
uncertainty that have informed the artist's identity since her teenage years.

Capturing universally recognizable experiences of human exploration - a night drive, a
bar banter, tangled bodies in front of DJ equipment, television glow on a red-eye flight -
Zhang is interested in creating images that embody both closeness and distance,
recognizing the disorientation of
living in a state of constant uprooting and finding
comfort in the unplaceable.

Exhibition period: Thursday June 27 - Sunday June 30, 2024

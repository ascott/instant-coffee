In Tune, Unbound is a new publication created by the undergraduate students of CA 
319W: Critical Writing in the Arts at SFU's School for the Contemporary Arts. It includes 
a collection of interviews with Vancouver-based artists and reviews of local events. 
Published by Decoy Magazine and funded by Simon Fraser University. The publication 
launch is taking place at Artspeak from 7-9pm, Wednesday November 28.

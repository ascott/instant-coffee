at Plaza Projects
Aberdeen Square #2370-4000, Richmond

Xingyue Feng & Yuan Liu
Invite you to a celebration of their Divorce / wedding! 

The Divorce is a collaborative art project by Feng Xingyue & Liu Yuan. The artist 
couple hosted a lavish wedding ceremony as part of their project. This project is for 
their MFA program at Emily Carr University. 

Bio
Feng Xingyue & Liu Yuan are partners in both art and life from China, work with 
video, performance and installation. They are in Emily Carr University of Art and 
Design MFA program now.

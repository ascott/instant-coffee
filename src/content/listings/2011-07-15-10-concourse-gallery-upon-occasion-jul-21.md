11. Roundhouse Community Arts | Eco-Arts Salon with Naomi Steinberg: ELEMENTAL | JUL 27
Eco-Arts Salon with Naomi Steinberg: ELEMENTAL

Presented by the Community Arts Council of Vancouver

Wednesday July 27, 7-9pm

Roundhouse Community Arts and Recreation Centre Room B

Naomi Steinberg will be presenting and leading a dialogue. Naomi's work in
the last 9 years has been closely focused on community engagement and
performance. As Executive Director of the Vancouver Society of Storytelling
(VSOS), Naomi has developed the ELEMENTAL initiative, which uses stories
about the elements (air, earth, water, fire, metal) as the metaphoric
access point for an intergenerational story-share and workshop series.

During ELEMENTAL: earth extracts from community stories will be represented
in a cob storytellers bench to be built in Robson Park (13th and
Kingsway). This site is an important geographic locater as it is part of a
watershed from which St. George Creek flows towards the False Creek Flats,
networking with many other underground streams along the way. The bench is
in support of community groups who have been working towards day-lighting
the creek.

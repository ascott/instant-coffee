12PM | MAR 1
Alex Tedlie-Stursberg
One Thousand Years

March 1st to 29th, 2025

Opening reception:

Saturday, March 1st, noon to 5pm

One Thousand Years presents new sculptures by Alex Tedlie-Stursberg. The exhibition
explores the material alchemy of everyday objects transformed by oceanic currents,
embodying the passage of time and carrying stories that reflect human evolution.
Stursberg contrasts the permanence of bronze with the discarded materials he
repurposes, elevating them into lasting monuments to modern existence.

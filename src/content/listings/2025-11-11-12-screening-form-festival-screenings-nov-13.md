Public Screens: A Body Uncontained " Watch Party + Live Artist Talkback facilitated
by Hn Phm

Presented in Collaboration with Form Festival and Gallery Gachet

Form Festivals Public Screens program, A Body Uncontained, reimagines how
movement-on-screen is viewed and experienced. This collection of films cannot be
contained within the walls of the cinema, they extend beyond the body and the
camera to find a landing place on outdoor screens across Metro Vancouver.

These films were created with deeply personal ponderings in mind, where each artist
questions their relationship to a larger concept beyond themselves. Whether this be
ones relationship to time, an object, our surrounding environment, or social system
in place, A Body Uncontained pushes us to contemplate new ways of existing
beyond the confines of these dynamics.

Thank you to Cinevolution for supporting this program!

Panel Discussion with Emily R. Blyth, Manuel Axel-Strain, (POWER) and Syrus

Join advocates and researchers for a conversation on police-free futures and the
realities of colonial police violence north of the Medicine Line.

With an opening from local artist Manuel Axel-Strain (Musqueam, Secwpemc, and
Sylix Nations), speakers include members of the local community-based research
project Police Oversight with Evidence and Research (POWER); scholar, artist, and
advocate Syrus Marcus Ware; health scholar and Walk in Gratitude; Write in
Empathy curator Emily R. Blyth.

Books and merch will be available for purchase at the event, with proceeds
supporting local prison-abolitionist organizing led by Joint Effort and the Prisoners
Justice Day Committee " Vancouver.

Join us at Gallery Gachet on Sunday, November 16 (4:00"5:30 PM PT) for the
Vancouver launch of How to Abolish Prisons: Lessons from the Movement Against
Imprisonment, published by Haymarket Books.

This gathering brings together organizers, artists, and scholars imagining prison-free
futures and working toward transformative justice. The evening will open with an
Indigenous welcome from 2-Spirit artist Manuel Axel-Strain(Musqueam, Secwpemc,
and Syilx Nations), followed by a conversation featuring Cathee Porter and Jacquie
Nicole from Joint Effort Collective, Mercedes Eng, poet and scholar, and Justin
Pich, co-author of the book. The discussion will be moderated by Magn Payet of
Stark Raven Radio.

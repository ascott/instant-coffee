CinemaShine: Heartworn Highways
Saturday, June 15, 2019, at 8:30 PM - 2 AM
Bestway Studios 21 E. Pender Street Vancouver

Please join us for the next instalment of CinemaShine at Bestway Studios. This time around 
we are happy to have Del Hillier as our guest artist, screening Heartworn Highways, a 
documentary film by James Szalapski whose vision captured some of the founders of the 
Outlaw Country movement in Texas and Tennessee in the mid-1970s.

Alongside the screening, Hillier will be presenting his sculpture A Case Of Me, a musical 
instrument case designed to hold his body. He will also be recycling some old materials 
inside his studio to create decorative faux speaker covers in homage to the film.

Lonesome country folk music, hooch and beer. What's not to like. Come on down and bend 
an elbow with us.

Presented by Bestway Projects and DRIL.
Screening starts at 9 pm!
Cash bar
Admission is free!

Del Hillier is an artist and musician working and living on Coast Salish land. His current 
projects include My Name Is Del, an musical act that incorporates pedal steel guitar, old 
skipping country records, improvised vocals, and handmade props. Hilliers most recent 
performance was at Wrong Wave 6 (UNIT PITT) and this Fall he will be releasing a cassette 
with Agony Klub. Last summer, Hillier was invited by Intersite Arts Festival to create a video 
installation inside a modified semi truck trailer at The Blackfoot Diner Truckstop in Calgary, 
AB. The installation was an experimental intervention that connected members of the art 
world, the long haul trucking community, and the staff and patrons of The Blackfoot Diner.

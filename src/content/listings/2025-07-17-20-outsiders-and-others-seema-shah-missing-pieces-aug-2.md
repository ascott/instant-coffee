Seema Shah: Missing Pieces / solo exhibition

August 2 - 30, 202 at Outsiders and Others
#100-938 Howe Street, Vancouver, BC

Opening Reception: Saturday, August 2 from 6-8 pm - free & open to the public

About the exhibition: Missing Pieces offers a subtle and nuanced representation of
the lived experience of complex trauma, challenging preconceived ideas about how
personal trauma manifests and is creatively expressed. The exhibition is centred
around a collection of 366 miniature collages on shipping tags, one created every
day of 2024. Collages on vintage envelopes accompany the tags.

About the artist: Seema Shah is a self-taught collage artist. She was a recipient of
The Beaumont Studios Artist To Watch Award 2022 and a highly commended artist
in the 2022 & 2023 Contemporary Collage Magazine Awards. Her miniature collages
have been exhibited in two dollhouse galleries in the UK, The House of Smalls and
Small House Gallery, where she had a solo show in 2024.

Additional exhibition events:
Artist Talk & Exhibition Tour: Saturday, August 16 at 2pm - free & open to the public
Collage workshop with Seema Shah: Saturday, August 23 from 2-4 pm. Register for
the workshop on the gallery's website.

XINEMA | NORQUAY PARK FIELDHOUSE
XINEMA Darkroom Drop-In sessions (free / by donation)

Norquay Park Fieldhouse (5050 Wales St, Vancouver, BC V5R 3M6)

Wednesdays, 7-10pm

Over the last handful of months, XINEMA has been working hard on converting an
old, grimy changing room in the Norquay Park Fieldhouse into a darkroom space.
We have finally gotten it to a usable condition (with much work to go), but are antsy
to get people in the door. On July 16th we launched the first of many weekly drop-in
sessions to come in this space, with our intention being to provide low-barrier
materials, and learning and connection opportunities for curious newbies and
seasoned like-minds, through an open and flexible community resource.

Come by on an upcoming Wednesday (e.g. July 23rd, 30th, etc.) to process your still
or moving images, and play around with enlarger or contact printing techniques"
whether its photo negatives from last weeks wanderings, or rolls of 16mm youve
been meaning to soup"all development materials will be provided.

- Bring your own black & white film (photo or motion picture formats)! If you don't
have any, we have some you can play with, and cameras you can shoot with (16mm,
35mm, super8)"we are happy to show you how to use them.

- Bring materials to enlarge or contact print with (ex. 35mm negatives, fabrics, optical
glass). We will also have some materials handy for you to use.

- Bring your garden weeds, compost, leftover lunch, etc. to make a developer. We
will have some prepped plant-based developer, as well as some expired D76
available to play with. We also have supplies to make a caffenol developer for those
who are interested.

If youre new to these processes, well walk through it together. If youve done this a
hundred times, come get some work done and help guide others through their first
go"maybe you will learn something too!

XINEMA is an experimental film space and series run purely by volunteer efforts. To
help us keep putting on these sessions, if you have the means, please also consider
donating! All donations from our Darkroom Drop-In sessions will go directly into
supporting material reimbursement, maintenance and expansion for these events,
and contributions of any amount go a long way.

Come sip some tea, talk some tea, and develop with tea (?) on an upcoming
Wednesday night. Whether you have something to process, want to see how its
done, need a reason to get your hands stained, or just want to hang out, wed love to
see ya!

Accessibility Notes: XINEMAs Fieldhouse and darkroom space have 29 doorways,

We will have some available for those who wish to use them. Well have red lights
going indoors, and if its warm well spill out into the evening air between batches.
Accessible public bathrooms are open until 9pm; afterwards, indoor bathrooms will
be available inside our Fieldhouse via a short ramp.

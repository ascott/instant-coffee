The Mole Gallery 1157 Pendrell Street (Back Alley Entrance), Vancouver, BC., V6E1L3, 
Canada.
A Gift of Vision is an eclectic art exhibit by Vancouver artist, Kathleen Katon Tonnesen, 
who will be in attendance on July 19 and 26, 2019 - Friday afternoons only 1pm - 5pm. 
Kathleen shares her journey thus far, adapting to vision loss and the impact it has on her 
creativity going forward. Kathleen has just launched her new book titled "How to Draw 
Step-by-Step Charcoal Portraits for Beginners" soon to be available in stores and on 
Amazon.

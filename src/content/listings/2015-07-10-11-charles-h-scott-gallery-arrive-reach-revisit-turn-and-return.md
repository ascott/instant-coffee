arrive, reach, revisit, turn and return

Galan Akin, Troy Gronsdahl, Christann Kennedy, Melany Nugent, Dionne Paul,
Valerie Tremblay Blouin, Trevor Van den Eijnden.

July 16–27, 2015
Charles H. Scott Gallery, Vancouver

Opening Reception
Thursday, July 16, 6:30–10:00 pm

emily carr university of art + design
low residency masters of applied arts thesis exhibition

arrive, reach, revisit, turn and return is the graduating exhibition for
the 2015 Emily Carr low-residency master’s candidates. The works presented
feature interactive media, painting, photographic work, text, sculpture,
and installation, and represent the culmination of three years of practice
based research.
Through a variety of overlapping concerns, the exhibition reflects on
contemporary experience and its historical determinants with sensitivity,
curiosity, and humour.

Session 1 - Saturday, September 7, 2:00 - 3:30pm

Richmond Art Gallery

Pre-registration required.

Richmond Art Gallery is partnering with Mobil Art School to present a series of low-cost
classes on the history of Canadian Landscape Art.

What has historic landscape painting got to do with environmental transformation? Join us
for this series of 2 illustrated talks where we examine the collision of history, art and the land
we live in.

Session 1 - September 7
2:00 - 3:30 pm
Picturing the West Coast: Ice Age to Now
Sean Alward
Tickets at: [https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/art-history-series-canadian-landscape-inside-out-](https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/art-history-series-canadian-landscape-inside-out-)
session-1-tickets-64681431904

We will look at how the West Coast of Canada has been pictured in art, photography, and
film with examples ranging from Emily Carr to the X-files. We will also consider what the
coast was like long before it was ever represented in pictures, with insights provided by
archaeological sites dating back to just after the after the last ice age, 12,000 years ago.
This one hour slide show will be a combination of history, art, and archaeology that may
challenge commonly held stereotypes of West Coast landscape.

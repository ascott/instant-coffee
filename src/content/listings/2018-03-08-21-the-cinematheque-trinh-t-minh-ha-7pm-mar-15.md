Surname Viet Given Name Nam

Centre A and the Cinematheque invite you to a special screening of Surname Viet 
Given Name Nam, one of the best known works by celebrated film artist and theorist 
Trinh T. Minh-ha. The film explores identity, popular memory, and culture through 
Vietnamese womens resistance in Vietnam and the United States. It combines dance, 
text, folk poetry, and womens testimony to call into question official histories and the 
politics of documentary and interview.

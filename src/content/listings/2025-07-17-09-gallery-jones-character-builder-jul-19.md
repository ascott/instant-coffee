Character Builder

Featuring:

Jay Dart

Claire Scherzinger

July 19 - August 16, 2025
Opening reception: Jul 19, 2025, 2-4pm.

The artists included in Character Builder converge across generations and mediums
to explore the human figure not as a fixed form, but as an evolving site of fiction,
identity and contradiction. Embracing definitions such as hyperrealism (Jen Mann)
and surrealism (Claire Scherzinger) the exhibition elucidates the human compulsion
to define a character in space and time and the creative impulse to turn that
definition on its head.

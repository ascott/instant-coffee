Cineworks presents:

Fantasy VFX, Geopolitics and Spirituality: A Salon With Director/VFX 
Supervisor David Feuillatre

Cineworks Studio, 1131 Howe Street (lane entrance)
Wednesday August 19, Doors 7.00pm, Talk 7.20pm
FREE

David Feuillatre is an award winning Visual Effects Supervisor, Director, 
and Documentary Photographer. In advance of running a series of VFX 
workshops for indie filmmakers and media artists at Cineworks this 
Fall, David joins us to give a presentation on his professional practice 
and diverse accomplishments. Combining fluent knowledge in 
storytelling, documentary and visual effects techniques, David trained 
at ESRA Film School in Paris and the International Center of 
Photography in NYC and applies his skills to big-budget blockbusters, 
indie innovations and personal documentary adventures. 

As a VFX Supervisor and Senior Cloth Technical Director, David has 
worked for companies such as Sony Pictures Imageworks (Los 
Angeles and Vancouver), Framestore (London), Weta Digital (New 
Zealand) and MPC (Vancouver), with a filmography including three 
Best Visual Effects Academy Award winners and one in selection:

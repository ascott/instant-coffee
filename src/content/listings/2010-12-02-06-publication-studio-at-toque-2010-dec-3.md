PUBLICATION STUDIO at Toque 2010

Friday, December 3, 6 pm – 9 pm

Saturday, December 4, 11 am – 5 pm

At the Western Front Luxe Hall, 303 East 8th Ave, Vancouver

Publication Studio is pleased to announce its participation in Toque, a
craft/book fair at the Western Front. We will be set up in the Luxe Hall
and ready to print and bind your books! We can produce bound books from
your PDF file, or we can re-bind your beloved-but-disintegrating
paperbacks, making them into new, unique editions.

We will also be offering a selection of our publications for sale, which
will make excellent gifts this holiday season! Our entire catalogue can be
viewed at www.publicationstudio.biz and we would be happy to produce any of
these books onsite.

Publication Studio is an experiment in sustainable publication that prints
and binds books on demand, creating original work with artists and writers.
Publication Studio is a laboratory for publication in its fullest sense —
not just the production of books, but the production of a public. This
public, which is more than a market, is created through deliberate acts,
the circulation of texts, discussions, and gatherings in physical space,
and the maintenance of a digital commons. Please visit
www.publicationstudio.biz for more detailed information, or contact
psvancouver@publicationstudio.biz.

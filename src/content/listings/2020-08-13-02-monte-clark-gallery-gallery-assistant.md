Monte Clark Gallery is currently accepting applications for a part
time Gallery Assistant.

The Gallery Assistant will work closely with staff and artists to
ensure the smooth operations of the gallery, providing assistance when
needed and being proactive to improve workflow. The role requires a
professional demeanor, dedication to efficiency, and creative
solutions for the varied and dynamic gallery environment. This role
has the potential for growth for the right candidate.

Requirements:
- Experience with photography and photo editing
- Valid BC Drivers License
- Ability to lift 50 lbs
-
Excellent listening, organizational, and problem solving skills
- Fluent in Mac computers

Assets:
- A background in visual art
- Familiarity with best practices for handling, packing, installing,
and moving artwork

To apply, please email a resume with relevant experience to
info@monteclarkgallery.com

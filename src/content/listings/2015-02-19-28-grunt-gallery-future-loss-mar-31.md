Exhibition Title: Future Loss
Exhibition Dates: January 1st - April 30th, 2015
Location: grunt gallery (116-350 East 2nd Ave, Vancouver, BC) and Main Street
Closing event(s): March 31 - April 2, 2015 (full details to come)

Is there a corner where you can feel the change?
Hold this heavy until the weight grows warm.
Is this how we create an artifact we can preserve?

Vancouver-based artist Zoe Kreye interprets sculptural incarnations of
loss, here on Main Street. Over the course of 12 weeks, the artist will
engage directly with shopowners, organizers and residents in discussions
around what it means to hold space in a shifting landscape. Space, on this
strip and in this city, is currency, and Kreye's practice reaches through
the overarching narratives of real estate, gentrification and speculation
to consider the poetics of an individual's connection to place. What bodies
exist here? What corners? And in between these - what would the loss of one
or the other look like?

Kreye's practice quietly inhabits the street: she will meet with
participants in their own spaces, and work with them to create discrete
objects and impressions in plaster. These works–abstract, raw, wrinkled,
angular– will be combined to form a collective sculptural utterance, a
statement from the community that is both abstract and earnest. Not quite
documentary, but certainly infused with real bodies and things, FutureLoss
is an evolving portrait of this neighbourhood, and this moment in time.

For updates, and to see the work in process, please visit futureloss.ca.

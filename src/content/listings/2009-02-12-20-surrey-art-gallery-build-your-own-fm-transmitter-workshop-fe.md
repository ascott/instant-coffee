Build your own FM Transmitter
workshop lead by audio and media artist Bobbi Kozinuk

2 say workshop February 20-21 2009
Fee: $50. For materials (provided).

Based on a now famous design by electronics pioneer and media activist
Tetsuo Kogawa of Tokyo Keizai University, participants will learn the
fundamentals of radio transmission and build their very own portable Two
Watt FM transmitter. No
previous experience necessary, wireless satisfaction guaranteed!

The fist day participants will build a working transmitter circuit board,
the 2nd day the participants will learn to tune the transmitter, build an
enclosure, an antenna and a power supply.

This workshop is suitable for ages 14 and up, and has a maximum enrollment
of 10.

Bobbi Kozinuk uses radio, electronics, projections and performance to
explore issues of gender, the environment and community involvement. He has
been working with artists across the world and workshop participants across
Canada, empowering communities and individuals to access and work with
electronic media and helped numerous artists create installations and
outdoor performances.

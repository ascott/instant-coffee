Locate: An exploration of Landscape and Place
Printmaking exhibition at Malaspina Printmakers Society on Granville Island,
November 22 to December 23, 2023.
The opening reception is on Thursday, November 23, 2023. 6 to 9pm.

To locate is to identify, but always in reference to something else. Local artists Peter
Bodnarus & Edward Fu Chen Juan explore the local environment through personal
interaction and professional experiences.

Gallery open Thursday to Sunday, 12pm-5pm, November 23 to December 23

THIS Gallery Presents Look What the Clown Dragged In, a Solo Exhibition by Jess
MacCormack

In an era where the self is increasingly fragmented and reconstituted through digital
interfaces, Jess MacCormacks Look What the Clown Dragged In offers a darkly
humorous yet deeply unsettling meditation on contemporary identity. Their
installation"comprising AI-generated images and videos interwoven with cardboard
and text"pushes against the boundaries of selfhood, inviting viewers to witness the
disjointed narratives of interior life under the weight of surveillance capitalism, social
media, and systemic violence. Through a practice that embraces the absurd, the
grotesque, and the melancholic, MacCormack forces a reckoning with the ways
digital aesthetics shape, distort, and even commodify our most intimate experiences
of self and Other.

MacCormacks work is rooted in a history of critical digital art that interrogates the
intersection of technology and identity. The lineage can be traced through Cindy
Shermans self-portraiture, which exposes the performativity of identity, and Lynn
Hershman Leesons explorations of cyborg femininity. However, whereas these
artists work within the confines of traditional photographic and cinematic media,
MacCormack embraces AI as an inherently unstable collaborator"one that
generates imagery at the intersection of algorithmic randomness and human intent.
The result is an aesthetic that feels both eerily familiar and entirely alien, amplifying
the uncanny dissonance that underlies contemporary digital existence.

MacCormacks digital art has been shared through various online platforms, such as
Artforum International, Hyperallergic, Canadian Art, VICE Creators project, Bitch
Magazine, PAPER Magazine and Art F City. Their animations have been screened
internationally at festivals such as the Ottawa International Animation Festival, MIX-
26 the New York Queer Experimental Film Festival, LA Film Fest at UCLA, Inside
Out, Imaginative Film Festival and International Festival of Films on Art (FIFA).
MacCormacks interdisciplinary practice has been supported and exhibited by the
Academie der Knste der Welte (Cologne, Germany), arbyte (London, UK), articule
(Montral, Canada), Western Front (Vancouver, Canada) and many other local and
international galleries. They have an MFA in Public Art and New Artistic Strategies
from the Bauhaus University (2008) and were an Assistant Professor of Studio Arts
at Concordia University (2010-2013). Jess is currently an instructor at Emily Carr
University of Art + Design and is working towards their PhD in Contemporary Art at
Simon Fraser University.

Look What the Clown Dragged In closes Saturday, April 12. THIS Gallery is open
Thursday, Friday and Saturday from noon to 5.

Access Gallery and Father Zosima Presents...
Crys Cole and Sick Buildings 
Saturday, July 26, 2008
8:00pm - 10:15pm 
Access Artist Run Centre.
206 Carrall Street

Straight from Winnipeg but formerly of Vancouver: Crys Cole

Straight from our block: Sick Buildings

Crys Cole is incredible. Reminds me of Sachiko M but with a touch more melancholy. Very good. 
She played Signal & Noise many years ago and I wrote about her great style for The Wire 
magazine.

Sick Buildings is just about the best thing in town. You know Josh, he puts out great noise, 
drone, extremes, has a record label, plays Fake Jazz, and basically: RULES.

We start at 8:30 sharp so that we're done by 10pm and the night is still yours to get lost in.

THIS IS THE LAST FATHER ZOSIMA SHOW! After a year and a half of shows nearly every 
month showcasing the best of creative music in Vancouver and the world, the money has dried 
up.

Vancouver noise. Here to stay anyway.

Always filled to capacity seating.
Beer. Wine. Spirits.

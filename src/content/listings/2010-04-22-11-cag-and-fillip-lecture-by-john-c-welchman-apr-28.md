The Contemporary Art Gallery and Filip magazine invite you to a lecture by
John C. Welchman on Wednesday, April 28 at 7:00, 2010 at the CHURCH

Mr. Welchman is Professor of art history in the Visual Arts department at
the University of California, San Diego and his written widely and
extensively on contemporary art. Some titles include "Modernism Relocated:
Towards a Cultural Studies of Visual Modernity, Invisible," "Colours: A
Visual History of Titles" and "Art After Appropriation: Essays on Art in
the 1990s."

The topic of his lecture in Vancouver is "Actions Against the State: Paul
McCarthy's Pirate Project." The lecture discusses some of the wider social,
political and aesthetic questions, raised by the artist's work in
performance, mega installation, drawing and video.

LOCATION: The Church, 3092 Fraser Street, Vancouver
(@ East 15th Street and Kingsway)

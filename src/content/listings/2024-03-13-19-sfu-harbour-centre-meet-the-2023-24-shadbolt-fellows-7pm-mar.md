NEW DATE! Future Losses and Resisting Disciplines: Meet the 2023-24 Shadbolt
Fellows

Through resistance of traditional norms, our new Shadbolt Fellows are redefining their
respective disciplines especially in todays landscape of conflict, loss, and grief.

Join us on Thursday, March 21st to welcome the 2023-24 Shadbolt Fellows to SFU for
an evening of conversation moderated by SFU English professor and poet Clint
Burnham, followed by a Q&A session and a catered reception. Doors open at 6:30pm,
presentations begin at 7pm.

Learn how the 2023-24 Shadbolt Fellows are each carving new paths for the arts,
humanities, and society at large. Register

Dynamo Arts Association presents:

Whitney Brennan and Ifigenia McGowan Papadatou
Take A Deep Breath
January 18 - February 1, 2019

OPENING RECEPTION
Friday, January 18, 7-11pm 
Dynamo Arts Association, 
Suite 103 - 30 East 6th Ave, Vancouver, British Columbia 

Take a Deep Breath occurred through a dialogue between artists Whitney Brennan 
and Ifigenia McGowan Papadatou focusing on existential questions and patterns we 
use to comfort ourselves. We present if you can build them, I can draw them by 
Ifigenia Papadatou and intimate sound works by Whitney Brennan, to share our 
perception of our body and the collective experience of our everyday rituals to 
overcome stressful circumstances.

WHITNEY BRENNAN
Whitney Brennan is a curator and sound artist from Vancouver, BC. She received 
her Master of Arts in Art History: Critical & Curatorial Studies from the University of 
British Columbia in 2018. She has curated exhibitions at The FALL, Gam Gallery 
and Access Gallery, in Vancouver, and is a member of the media artists collective 
The Work Group. She is currently a curator with Decoy Magazine and has been 
published in Luna Luna Magazine and C Magazine. Following her time working with 
Documenta 14 in Athens, Greece in 2017, she began exploring the uses of sound 
and aural stimuli as a method for processing environmental sensitivity brought on by 
her anxiety disorder. By focusing on aural triggers, shes been exploring how 
emphatic, intentional breathing and sounds of human presence can amplify or 
subdue manifestations of environmental overstimulation.
Website: [https://soundcloud.com/whitney-brennan](https://soundcloud.com/whitney-brennan)

IFIGENIA MCGOWAN PAPADATOU
Papadatou Ifigenia (b. 1990 in Athens, Greece) graduated in 2015 from the 
American College of Greece, (DEREE) with a BA in Visual Arts. Her working 
experience is mostly related to curatorial practices, visuals arts education and event 
management. During her studies, she participated as an artist but also worked in 
various small-scale exhibitions and galleries. In 2014 she became the curator for the 
Installations category of the 10th edition of Athens Digitals Arts Festival. Later that 
year she co-founded an urban non-profit company, Implode Platform, dedicated to 
new forms of sound and visual arts. In 2017 she worked as a Producer in the 
Education Department for Documenta 14 in Athens, where she worked closely on 
the project A- Letheia as well as the educational series of talks, Synantiseis. During 
the summer of 2018, she moved to the Greek Island of Santorini where she 
produced her latest painting series If you can build them, I can draw them.

Take A Deep Breath will be on display at Dynamo Arts Association from January 18 - 
February 1.

PORTAL POP-UP GALLERY

407 W. Cordova St., Vancouver BC

Presents

Ella MacQueen-Denz

Artwork on display in window gallery:
January 7- February 1, 2025

Portal launches its 2025 program with the work of Vancouver-based artist Ella
MacQueen-Denz.

MacQueen-Denzs paintings are subdued, intimate, and layered. Evoking the
complex emotional states of human relationships, the artist's cool blue watercolours
speak to connection, vulnerability, and memory. Through this body of work Ella has
created a surreal world emotive of a chilly January evening.

She uses primarily watercolour and graphite on paper, and has a multi-disciplinary
practice that also includes textiles, graffiti, and murals. Her work is largely figurative,
and explores themes of vulnerability, sexuality, femininity, and how memory and
emotion are held in the body. She takes on illustrative and narrative styles, and
perceives her figures and landscapes
as characters that inform each other, telling
stories in a visual language that thread together in continuous dialogue. She
references real and imagined places, creating dreamlike compositions that seek
what is not always obvious, and attempt to leave space for the unknown.

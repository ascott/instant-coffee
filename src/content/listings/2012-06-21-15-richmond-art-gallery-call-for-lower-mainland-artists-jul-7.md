RICHMOND ART GALLERY
CALL FOR LOWER MAINLAND ARTISTS

Group Exhibition: Temporary Assignment
(a temporary exhibition and performance space)

The Richmond Art Gallery is calling for proposals from artists 
(individuals, artist
collectives, performance artists, musicians etc.) whose practice 
includes some aspect of
“drawing” , to work directly on/with/against gallery walls for the end of 
year exhibition,
Temporary Assignment. All media will be considered.

Following this exhibition the Gallery is replacing its walls after 20 years 
of re‐painting
for each exhibition. “Artworks are temporary”  – they go out with the 
drywall.
From November 15 to 18, 2012 artists may work in the gallery space.

The Gallery is open to the public from November 19 to December 2.
Artists may continue to work, perform etc. during that time period.
Temporary Assignment closes Sunday, December 2.

On Friday, November 30, 2012 the Gallery will host a Closing party for 
all participants
and will be open to the public.

The Gallery is not able to provide artist’s transportation or 
accommodation for this
Exhibition

To APPLY:
A current CV, a brief artist statement, a statement of the proposed 
work, up to 6 images
of similar work or 5 minutes of video. Indicate in your proposal how 
much wall/gallery
space you require – i.e. postage stamp size and upward. (see gallery 
floor plan at
www.richmondartgallery.org). Online submissions will not be accepted.

Deadline: July 7, 2012

Send proposal to:
Temporary Assignment

Richmond, BC V6Y 1R9

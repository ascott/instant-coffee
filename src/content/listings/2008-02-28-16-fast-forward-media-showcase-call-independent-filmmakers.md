Dear all,

I am again putting together an independent filmmakers' booth for the next Fast Forward Media Showcase, April 30th and May 1st at the Capilano College Sportsplex in North Vancouver, BC.  For those of you who aren't familiar with the showcase, Fast Forward brings together media distributors who sell documentary, business, technical, educational and children's programs at all levels, with people who buy media.

Librarians, teachers and faculty from schools, colleges, universities and libraries -- many are keen to view productions created locally, especially those on topical issues or that support particular curriculum or courses.

Therefore, I'd really like to find productions by Canadian filmmakers that are not already carried by a distributor but have public performance rights so that their works may be shown in class, to promote at the show to the net benefit of all.

If you're interested in your production(s) being featured at this showcase, please let me know, and I will send you a package with the details and cost for this service. If you register by February 29th (deadline extended!), you can also take advantage of the early bird rate.  And if you want to know more about Fast Forward, please click on the link below or contact me if you have any questions.

Stale Buns
October 19th - 21st, 2018
Opening Friday October 19th 7:00 - 10:00PM
Saturday 20th " Sunday 21st 2:00 - 8:00PM

Stale Buns brings together a group of publishers, artists and collectives to showcase 
a variety of artists books, multiples, video, animation and editions. The event 
coincides with Vancouver Art Book Fairs Art Book Week. Hot dogs and 
refreshments will be available as partner pieces to the book ephemera. The event 
runs alongside Dylan Townley-Smith | Situ Comoedia. Opening Thurs, Oct, 11th. 
opening on Thursday, October 11th.

Including:

+more

Field Contemporary

Part of 2018 Vancouver Art Book Fair's Art Book Week. Art Book Week is a parallel 
series of events hosted in spaces across Vancouver by a variety of artists, curators, 
collectives and institutions who are actively creating and presenting work in this 
medium.

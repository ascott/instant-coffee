The 7th Annual UBC Art History Undergraduate Symposium.

Date: March 25, 2011
Location: Lillooet Room Irving K. Barber Learning Center, 1961 East Mall, UBC
3 to 6:30 pm, Reception to follow. All are welcome!

The 7th Annual Art History Undergraduate Symposium will feature the
research papers of five undergraduate students who have used innovative
approaches to art and visual culture in a variety of historical periods and
geographical areas. In the company of students, professors and other
members of the community the speakers will present their work with ample
time following to ask questions and make comments. Refreshments will be
provided during the intermission as well as for the following reception.

Friday March 25th

3:00-3:05
Opening Remarks

"A Response to Conceptual Paternity: Theoretical Labour Pains in the
Tautological and Entropic Structures of Robert Smithson, Lawrence Weiner
and Robert Rauschenberg"
Yasmin Nurming-Por

"Negotiating Corporeality, Negotiating Identity: The Artist's Use of the
Cyborg as a Response to a Technological Age 1969-2009"
Carli Vierke

"A Golem for the Twentieth Century: The Filmic Geography of Paul Wegener
and Hans Poelzig"
Casey Preston

5:00-5:20
Intermission Break

"The Spread of Christianity and the Fate of Pagan Monuments in Late Antiquity"
Jocelyn Plant

"Agostino Tassi's Competition on the Capitoline Hill and La Cuccagna:
Access and Resistance to the Gastronomic Land of Plenty"
Kyle Besuschko

6:30
Reception

Local studios in Maple Ridge / Pitt Meadows

Maple Ridge / Pitt Meadows Art Studio Tour

Date/Time: Saturday November 25th, 10am - 4pm

Location: Vicua Art Studio - #1, 11491 Kingston St. Maple Ridge, B.C., V2X 0Y6

Vicua Art Studios Annual Winter Art Show 2023

Celebrating our 15th Anniversary!

The most wonderful time of the year is approaching, and that means it's time for
Vicua Art Studios Annual Winter Art Show at our Kingston Street gallery on
Saturday, November 25th from 10 am until 4 pm. A total of forty-five artists are
participating in the
show this year and guests will have the chance to meet many of
them in person at the show. These amazing artists will be volunteering their time to
provide live art demonstrations and chat with you about their works and answer any
questions you may have throughout the day. With over a hundred items available for
purchase, including original paintings, illustrations, pottery sculptures, art cards,
ornaments, and more we are sure you will find a perfect gift for someone in your life.
As always, guests are welcome to enjoy a complimentary refreshment and enter our
annual free raffle to win a specially curated work of art.

Lobe Artist Residency Series: ZAMEEN-A

ZAMEEN-A presents: Cry [No More]
Friday August 11th | Doors at 6pm
Location: Lobe Studio | 713 East Hastings Street Vancouver

Shows at 6:30pm and 7:45pm

Join us at Lobe Studio for an immersive performance by Vancouver based
artist/violinist ZAMEEN-A.

Cry [No More], ZAMEEN-A's debut album, seeks to challenge prevailing cultural
assumptions by exploring musical possibilities beyond the confines of conventional
western melodic and rhythmic structures, tapping into some of the most universal yet
rarely shared emotional states. Alongside musicians Gregory Coles and Cayne
McKenzie, ZAMEEN-A looks to explore the poetic and deeply emotional state of
ecstatic suffering by combining the sensual and moody
expression of the violin as it is
juxtaposed with the progressive textures of noise, ambient, and industrial production.

Tickets: Sliding Scale, PWYC Donation. Suggested $15-$30.

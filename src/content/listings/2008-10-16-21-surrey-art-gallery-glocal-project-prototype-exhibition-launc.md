Glocal Project: prototype exhibition launch

Saturday, October 18th

1pm - 5pm

Surrey Art Gallery

13750-88th Avenue, Surrey, BC

Artists: M Simon Levin, Jer Thorp and Sylvia Grace Borda

An ongoing artist-in-residency project at the Surrey Art Gallery, the
Glocal Project attempts to intervene in 21st century photographic practice,
and to reinvigorate and re-think image-making in the digital age. The
ubiquity of digital imaging devices (digital cameras, cell-phones, etc.)
and their ease of use have created a unique situation: while there are more
image-makers than ever before, image-making as a practice tends to be done
with less and less conscious thought. Glocal challenges participants to
engage in experimental approaches to image-making, to build, modify, and
re-purpose digital hardware and circuits, and to rethink some basic
questions: What is a camera? What is a photograph? What is a photographer?

Using a variety of custom-built open source software, hardware, and
conceptual practices, the Glocal team has conducted workshops locally,
internationally, and online. Drawing upon image contributions from
thousands of participants, an interactive, evolving new media installation
is being built. Currently a prototype, the installation allows the viewer's
physical movements to configure and navigate a network of images, and to
explore image relationships from both human and machine perspectives.

This early afternoon open house event provides an opportunity for visitors
to generate images using the custom-built software and hardware toolkits,
to experience the interactive prototype, and to learn about this
large-scale contributive digital artwork. Please see www.glocal.ca for more
info. Or email us at info@glocal.ca

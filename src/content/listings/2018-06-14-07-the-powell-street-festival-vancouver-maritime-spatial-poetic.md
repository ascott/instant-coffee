The Powell Street Festival Society in association with the Vancouver
Maritime Museum present:

Spatial Poetics XVII: ONCE LOST

Friday, June 15th 7:30pm

Vancouver Maritime Museum
1905 Ogden Avenue, Vancouver
Tickets: $11 on Eventbrite

Curated by:

LEANNE DUNIC

Created by:

NAOMI HORII & MARK WICKSTEAD, MAYUMI YOSHIDA, HIDE EBINA, and FISH

Spatial Poetics, the Powell Street Festival Societys annual evening of
experimental performance pieces, returns June 15th, 2018 for its
seventeenth year with Spatial Poetics XVII: ONCE LOST. For Spatial Poetics,
artists are invited to create a new, collaborative presentation together
and are encouraged to work beyond their usual disciplines.

In the one-night-only presentation of ONCE LOST artists Naomi Horii & Mark
Wickstead, Mayumi Yoshida, Hide Ebina and Fish explore the multilayered
effects of WWII on Japanese Canadians. ONCE LOST incorporates the Vancouver
Maritime Museums Lost Fleet exhibition by interpreting complementary
narratives. Audiences are invited to experience an immersive journey
through performance, film, sound, and ceramic installations.

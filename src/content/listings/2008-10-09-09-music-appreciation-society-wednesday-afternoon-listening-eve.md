Music Appreciation Society presents Wednesday Afternoon Listening Events

Josh Rose
"Untitled for Balloons and People"
October 15th at 3:30pm
Charles H. Scott Gallery

As Part of the Charles H. Scott Gallery exhibition "The Sound I'm Looking
For" The Music Appreciation Society is pleased to present Vancouver-based
Josh Rose who will present his work "Untitled for Balloons and People".

Josh Rose is an active member of the independent music community,
performing under the name Sick Buildings, and as a member of The Holy Sea,
Leviathans, Blouse, and Robe and Allied. He also operates the independent
noise and experimental music label and distributor Run Down Sun.

Rose is the first in a series of five Afternoon Listening Events

For further information please contact the gallery at 604.844.3809.
Admission is free.

Charles H. Scott Gallery

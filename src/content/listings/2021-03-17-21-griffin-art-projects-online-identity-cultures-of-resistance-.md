Identity & Cultures of Resistance in Printed Media with Gord Hill, Rachel Lau, Jenny
Lin and Cole Pauls, Sunday, March 21, 1pm

Griffin Art Projects

www.griffinartprojects.ca

Zoom link: https://zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_hrqWM0MRRBu8dD5nejVwlA

Join four Canadian comic artists, illustrators and storytellers for a collaborative panel
discussion exploring means of defining identities, retelling histories, creating cultures of
resistance and reclaiming space through creative practices in the world of print and
publishing. Gord Hill, Rachel Lau, Jenny Lin and Cole Pauls will deliver short
presentations, followed by a group discussion and a live audience Q&A.

Offered in conjunction with Griffins current exhibition, Whose Chinatown? Examining
Chinatown Gazes in Art, Archives and Collections, this live virtual event expands upon
key themes of cultural and community preservation, and explores how printed
narratives hold the power to evoke change, resist imposed narratives and inspire hope
for a resilient future.

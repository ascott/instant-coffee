Join us for an evening of jazz and commiserating, featuring the jazz stylings of
Vancouvers Ben Frost. Entrance is $15 suggested donation, RSVP or at the door.
Donations go towards supporting the performers and UNIT/PITT.

Ben Frost is a trumpet player and composer based in Vancouver, BC. Ben has
performed on concert stages and festivals across Canada, his unique, thoughtful
writing and lyrical approach to the instrument making him a mainstay in the Vancouver
jazz scene. He completed his Bachelors of Music Degree at Capilano University and
holds a Masters of Music Degree in Jazz Performance from the University of Toronto,
where he studied with
Brad Turner, Jim Lewis, and Andrew Downing.

New Documents

New Documents @ Offprint Paris
Nov 14 – 16, 2014

New Documents is please to be part of Offprint Paris this weekend, sharing
a table with BFF Fillip (Vancouver).
In connection with Paris Photo, New Documents presents photo based books by
Jennilee Marigomen, David Horvitz, and David Hartt, as well as new titles
by Will Holder and Alex Waterman, Dan Starling, Walter Benjamin, and Maria
Fusco.

On the 22nd, we will be presenting books at the Three Letter Words fair in
London at the Rochelle School & Club Row in Shoreditch.

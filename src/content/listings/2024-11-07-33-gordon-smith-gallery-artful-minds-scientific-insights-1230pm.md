Art Education, For Life speaker series
Artful Minds, Scientific Insights
Dr. Maiya Geddes in conversation with Dr. Randy McIntosh

Saturday, November 23, 2024
Reception: 12:30pm / Talk: 1:15pm

Gordon Smith Gallery of Canadian Art
2121 Lonsdale Ave, North Vancouver, BC V7M 2K6

Dr. Maiya Geddes, assistant professor in the Department of Neurology and
Neurosurgery at McGill University will be in conversation with Dr. Randy McIntosh,
Professor at SFU and Director of
the Institute for Neuroscience and
Neurotechnology, exploring the intricate relationship between art and neuroscience.
Geddes will delve into the neural mechanisms shaping creativity and aesthetic
experiences, unraveling the mysteries of the mind at the crossroads of artistic
expression and cognitive processes.

Please join us at 12:30pm for a welcoming reception before the talk begins at 1:15
PM. All ages are welcome, attendance is by donation. Underground parking is
available.

Art Education, For Life is generously supported by The Audain Foundation, Polygon
Homes and Anthem Properties, with additional support provided by the North
Vancouver School District and the North Vancouver Recreation and Culture
Commission.

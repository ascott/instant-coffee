Matt Sesow: POLITICIZED
Opening Reception: Friday October 21st, 2016 at 7pm
Location: Gallery1515 (1515 West 7th ave. ) 
Show dates: Oct 21-Dec 4
On view from October 21- December 4 at Gallery1515, POLITICIZED 
will feature a selection of paintings by self-taught Washington, D.C.-
based artist Matt Sesow. The exhibition focuses on the US election 
and politics around the world. Sesow's expressive painting style is 
influenced by street art, politics, and current events. His symbolically 
rich compositions use raw, rapid, and expressive techniques, often 
depicting figurative scenes, dense with detail and fraught with emotion. 
His work has been widely exhibited, most notably at the American 
Visionary Art Museum in Baltimore, Maryland, the Chrysler Museum of 
Art in Norfolk, Virginia, and galleries in France and Barcelona. In 
September of 2013, Sesow was honored to have his work featured on 
a postage stamp issued by the United Nations Postal Administration 
(UNPA). Artist will be in attendance at opening reception.

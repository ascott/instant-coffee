Art Show
Opening reception is on Thursday, April 20th at 7pm.
The show runs until Thursday, April 27th.

Come join us in celebrating the works of Langara Fine Arts graduating students!

More information about Langara's Fine Arts program:
[http://langara.ca/programs-and-courses/programs/fine-arts/](http://langara.ca/programs-and-courses/programs/fine-arts/)

Art Sale
The 2017 Langara Fine Arts grad class will be putting on an Art Show from April 20th
to April 27th, and on April 28th you can purchase the works on display or other
works the artists have made!

It's a great place to find local art and support young artists! We have spent a lot of
time, effort, and love in our work and would love to see our pieces go to a good
home!

There will be: paintings, drawings, books, pottery, prints, sculptures, and more!

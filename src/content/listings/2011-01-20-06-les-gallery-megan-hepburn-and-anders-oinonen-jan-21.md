This show of new works by Megan Hepburn and Anders Oinonen
explores contrasting yet complementary painting processes.
Oinonen starts his works using the form of a face as a framework
for building an abstraction. The faces look into the distance,
passively suggesting a similarity with the between the viewer and
the subject of the painting. Megan Hepburn slowly builds her
geometric shapes according to the relationships between works in
process, they inform each other and develop according to a shared
logic which develops as they are created.

Megan Hepburn won the 2010 Joseph Plaskett Award for Excellence
in Painting and was a semi-finalist in the 2010 Royal Bank of
Canada Painting Competition. She has shown at Galerie Werner
Whitman, Montreal and at Art Mur, Montreal. Megan recently
completed her MFA at Concordia University in Montreal.

Last weekend for solo, pop-up show, works by Anne Sargent.
A large body of work show in an amazing light filled venue at 107 E. 3rd. Ave. Vancouver.
Mixed media utilizing image transfers of faces (drawn
photocopied, photographed, altered
and re-photocopied), plexiglass painted and layered, wax, applied and scraped, bronze
powder. Works on board, paper and lead.
The work is figurative, abstracted, non-narrative and subtle.

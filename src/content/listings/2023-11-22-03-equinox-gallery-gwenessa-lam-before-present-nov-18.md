Gwenessa Lam: Before Present
November 18 - December 16, 2023

Throughout her practice, Gwenessa Lam investigates how belief systems come into
being through an exploration of misidentified or yet to be identified historical objects
and the speculative theories they generate. In Before Present, Lam draws upon the
history of bronze magic mirrors to explore relationships between ancient forms of
divination or fortune-telling and current predictive technologies. With these works,
Lam asks: are the algorithms and machine learning
programs we now use our
contemporary versions of ancient divinatory tools, and are they linked through our
ongoing desire to predict and forecast the future?

Lam received her BFA from the University of British Columbia and MFA from New
York University. She has taught at New York University, the University of British
Columbia, the Alberta University of the Arts and Emily Carr University of Art +
Design. She has attended residencies at Skowhegan, MacDowell Colony, Yaddo,
and the Banff Centre. Her work has been exhibited at the Bronx Museum of Art and
the Queens Museum of Art in New York.

Gwenessa Lam lives and works in Vancouver, Canada.

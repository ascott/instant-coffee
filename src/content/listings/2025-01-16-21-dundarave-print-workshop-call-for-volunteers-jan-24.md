Dundarave Print Workshop & Gallery is looking for volunteers!

Do you have printmaking experience and are interested in having access to a
workshop? Dundarave currently has volunteer positions available for gallery
attendants.

As a volunteer, you would oversee the daily duties of the workshop and gallery three
days a month, while having 24/7 access to our print workshop and the opportunity to
sell work in our gallery space.

To apply, send us your resume via email at info@dundaraveprintworkshop.com by
Friday, January 24th! For more information visit our website at
https://www.dundaraveprintworkshop.com/

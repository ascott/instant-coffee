Project Space is pleased to announce the launch of a series of monthly art
book and publishing events, to be held out of our studio at 2- 236 E Pender
Street.

Join us at the studio on Friday, January 16th from 7-10PM for Switchblades
& River Children: Drawn Narratives from Justin Gradin.

Switchblades & River Children: Drawn Narratives from Justin Gradin, is an
extension of Latent Aesthetic, a collaboration with Cape Town artist
Sebastien Borckenhagen that was on view at the 2014 Vancouver Art/Book
Fair. Switchblades & River Children features an expanded catalogue of
Gradin's work, and is curated by Nathan Jones. With an aesthetic that
alludes to West Coast punk roots, the exhibition takes a post-modern jab at
the marginalized pop-culture references made in Gradin's drawings.

The studio bookshop will be open at this event. Project Space members get a
discount on our books with proof of membership.

----

Justin Gradin is a visual artist, writer and musician who has shown across
North America, the Netherlands and Japan, including a 2013 performance at
the Vancouver Art Gallery. Gradin was formerly the music editor for the
print magazine Color, which featured art, music and skateboarding. He has
published seven art books, and has self-published dozens of zines. Gradin
has also been active in the community, running several artist and
performance spaces, from L.A.’s Mime School to Vancouver’s Emergency Room.

Nathan Jones is an artist, curator and one of the founding members of Print
Ready, an ongoing Vancouver-based zine and art book exhibition series. He
has a B.A. from Emily Carr University of Art + Design and a B.Ed. from
Simon Fraser University. Jones’ art practice has its foundation in drawing
and painting. He has worked in a wide breadth of media, including
independent and commercial film, musical performance, illustration and
design for print. His work has been exhibited in Vancouver, Montreal and
Tokyo. He is currently one of the principal coordinators for the Dynamo
Arts Association.

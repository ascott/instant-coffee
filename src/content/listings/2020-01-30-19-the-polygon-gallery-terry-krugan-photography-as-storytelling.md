Eminent South African artist and writer Terry Kurgan will be in conversation with
Vancouver-based urban planner, designer, and photographer Lance Berelowitz about
her recent book Everyone is Present: Essays on Photography, Memory, and Family.
Kurgans book won the Sunday Times Alan Paton Award, and is her first work of
creative nonfiction, and moves between photographs and writing to see how images and
texts may be read for insights about the world and about what it means to be human.

Presented in partnership with the Vancouver JCC Jewish Book Festival and the
Vancouver Holocaust Education Centre.

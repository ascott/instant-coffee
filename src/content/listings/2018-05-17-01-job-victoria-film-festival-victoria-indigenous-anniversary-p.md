Indigenous & Anniversary Programming Assistant

This 6-month, full-time, paid internship is contingent on funding from the
BC Arts Councils Early Career Development Program. This position
collaborates closely with the Festival Director, Programming Director, and
Guest Programmers to program, promote and increase the number of Indigenous
films in the 25th Victoria Film Festival. As well, this position will focus
on the curation of a program celebrating the 25th anniversary of the VFF.

The Indigenous & Anniversary Programming Assistant Internship is aimed at
supporting the development of knowledge, learning and capacity through
professional exposure and practical experience within the world of
independent film for an early career or emerging culture worker. The ideal
candidate will possess authentic knowledge of and be invested in the
success of Indigenous film and wish to work within a not-for-profit arts
institution. Please see the requirements of the BC Arts Council funding at
the bottom of this job description to determine your eligibility for the
position.

The Indigenous & Anniversary Programming Assistant will:

Work directly with the mentor to find and select titles for the
Indigenous Perspective section
Watch incoming entries to our Indigenous Perspective section
Work with the Festival Director, Programming Director, and Guest
Programmers to develop and transform ideas into compelling narratives for
the program guide and website
Community engagement for the Indigenous Perspective section
Lead Q&As and context-building at screenings during the Festival
Scout and research contemporary Indigenous films and filmmakers
Assist Programming Director in curation of the VFFs 25th Anniversary
Program
Work with the Program Director to integrate uncanny film-going
experiences and community engagement opportunities into the festival
Assist in bringing the festival to life with event design and
promotion, including light shows, prop design, and construction
Database maintenance
Film and video archiving and cataloguing

Qualifications:

Demonstrate a command of written language
Have experience drafting engaging narratives in curatorial or
programming statements
Possesses a keen understanding of diverse Indigenous cultures in Canada
A deep interest in the power of film as an expressive medium
Be interested in pursuing a career in the arts

In order to be eligible for support from the BC Arts Council program, you must:

Be a B.C.-based early career professional individual artist, arts
practitioner, or culture worker. For the purposes of this program, early
career and emerging artists and arts practitioners are defined as artists
and practitioners who:
Are committed to developing a practice and career in the arts that
will allow them to work full-time at their practice when financially possible;

AND EITHER

Are under the age of 30;

OR,

Have completed peer-recognized basic training in your discipline
(not necessarily in academic institutions).
If you will complete basic training within 6 months of the
application deadline and before the proposed activity begins, you are
eligible to apply; however, priority may be given to applicants who have
completed their basic training.
Full-time students are not eligible. Please see the BC Arts
Council Scholarship Program or Component I: Co-op Placements for
information on grants available to support full-time students.
Be a Canadian citizen or Permanent Resident.
Be a resident of British Columbia and have lived in British Columbia
for at least twelve months immediately prior to application. Applicants who
have resided outside the province within the last twelve months must be
able to demonstrate that their absence was temporary (for example, for
educational or artistic opportunities) in order to be eligible for support.

Interested in applying? Please contact Kathy Kay, Festival Director before
sending an application: director@victoriafilmfestival.com

Applications must include:

A two-page statement from the intern describing their artistic
practice, why they wish to work with the organization and mentor, and the
importance of the internship to their career development.
A brief bio of the intern (maximum 250 words).
Resum or CV of intern, including relevant education, professional
training, related artistic or employment activities and experience, and
appropriate dates. Include a list of artistic works if applicable.

Please join us for a talk with artist Lyse Lemieux

Monday October 24 | 6pm

MoCap Studio | Rm 285e, North Building

Emily Carr University of Art + Design

During her thirty year career, Lemieux has become known for her 
expanded drawing practice and more recently for her large scale 
wall drawings with collaged elements. Lemieux graduated from 
UBC and has exhibited nationally and internationally including The 
Canadian Cultural Centre in Rome, La Commune di Perugia in Italy, 
Republic Gallery in Vancouver, Papier Art Fair in Montral, Equinox 
Gallery in Vancouver, and Katzman Contemporary in Toronto. Last 
spring the Richmond Art Gallery presented her solo exhibition A 
Girl's Gotta Do What A girl's Gotta Do. Her work will be included in 
the upcoming Vancouver Art Gallery's triennial Vancouver Special: 
Ambivalent Pleasures.

This is a free event, open to everyone. 

The Visual Art Forums are presented by the Audain Faculty of 
Visual Art and Material Practice.

Family Day | Textile Collage

Saturday, January 27, 12-3pm, free

On the last Saturday of each month, the CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short 
exhibition tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Using recycled paper and fabric, create a collaged artwork inspired by the work of Brent 
Wadden and the Gees Bend Quiltmakers.

2 APR 1:00 PM - 3:00 PM

Open Studio is a monthly program for young visitors and families inspired by
contemporary art practices and CAGs current exhibitions. Join us next month for an art-
making workshop designed by artist Svava Tergesen and led by CAG staff.

Inspired by Michelle Buis upcoming exhibition Mutable Materialism, participants will be
invited to create unique sculptures made out of food, learning different techniques and
approaches to building their very own sculptural creations with kitchen staples.

This event will be held in person at CAG. A limited number of spots are available; to
RSVP or for more information:

https://cagvancouver.org/event/open-studio-svava-tergesen

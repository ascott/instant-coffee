Ar(n)t Write is pleased to announce a call for submissions for the online and printed
publications of artists writing about other artists!

Ar(n)t Write is an artist-run public platform for artists to share casual style writings. The
aim of the platform is to reinforce artistic communities and encourage a variety of writing
approaches about art-making.

All selected submissions will be posted on Ar(n)t Write website and instagram page. A
portion of selected submissions will be printed as part of the Ar(n)t Write Zine.

The current deadline is June 1st.

To submit please follow the guidelines on our website:

https://arntwrite.wixsite.com/home/submit

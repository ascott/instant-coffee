#artoffline DOCUMENTARY FILM SCREENING 
+ PANEL DISCUSSION

"I think it's a bit of a fantasy to think that we can have a non-digital 
existence today, or create art that's not of the digital." - Clint 
Burnham

Join us for the North American premiere of the documentary 
#artoffline, followed by a panel discussion. Born of a young artist 
and filmmaker's attempt to understand the art world he wanted 
access to, #artoffline explores the relevancy of physical art in a 
digital world. What happens to art in the Internet age? How has the 
ability to surf endless digital images changed the gallery-going 
experience? In this documentary, philosophers, artists and 
exhibition-makers look at whether infinite reproduction liberates art 
from a volatile art market and an undemocratic exhibition circuit, 
and question whether the desire for physical objects is really just 
nostalgic fetishism.

The screening of #artoffline will be immediately followed by a panel 
discussion, moderated by Presentation House Gallery 
Director/Curator, Reid Shier.

Panel Participants:

Manuel Correa, Director of #artoffline
Sylvia Grace Borda, media artist and instructor
Clint Burnham, art writer and SFU professor
Mohammed Salemy, art critic and independent curator
Andy Sylvester, Director of Equinox Gallery

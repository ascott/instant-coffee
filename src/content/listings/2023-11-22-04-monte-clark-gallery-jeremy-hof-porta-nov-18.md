Jeremy Hof: Porta

November 18-December 23, 2023

Porta is defined in Latin as a gateway or a threshold, a transitionary space that is both
a termination and a beginning. Hofs large scale canvases depict bold geometric motifs
that stand out against vibrant backgrounds. This new work represents patterns of
exploration that borrow shapes from his previous reveals and reference woodblock
printing and twentieth century Op Art.

Monte Clark
53 Dunlevy Ave
Vancouver, British Columbia

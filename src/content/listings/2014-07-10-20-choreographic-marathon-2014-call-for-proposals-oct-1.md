Question 1: how long is a marathon? 26.2 miles •
Question 2: how long is the choreographic marathon? 26.2 hours •
Question 3: why? to learn the Art of the Possible in a shared community
pulling each other along not because it’s easy, but because it is hard, going
deep inside yourself and drawing on inner creativity and strength that you
might have never known even existed.

Bring an idea & Run with it for 26 hours 385 seconds & beyond

flow.

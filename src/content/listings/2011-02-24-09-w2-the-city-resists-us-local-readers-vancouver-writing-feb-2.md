Friday February 25, 2011

W2 Community Media Lounge - KSW Meeting Space
111 W. Hastings St. - Suite 250
(Hastings St. entrance @ Hastings and Abbott - Woodward's Heritage Building)

Join us for the reception at 7:30 pm (snacks and drinks), followed by reading.

THE CITY RESISTS US: LOCAL READERS + VANCOUVER WRITING

The Kootenay School of Writing will launch its new space in the W2
Community Media Arts complex in the Woodward's Heritage building with a
community event featuring Vancouver writers and writing.

The event will celebrate KSW's history as a site that encourages
critical/creative practices within controversial and/or contested urban spaces.

The community reading will feature 30+ writers reading from their own work
or cover poem. KSW writers will also participate in the reading.

Please join us in opening up our new space to the literary community!

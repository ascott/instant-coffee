(dis)location (dis)connect (dis)appearance
June 20 - August 10, 2019

Centre As 6th Annual Recent Graduates Exhibition is curated by Concordia University 
graduate, Diane Hau Yu Wong. Artists who will be participating in this exhibition are 
Florence Cing-Gaai Yee, Reyhan Yazdani, Aaniya Asrani, and Olivia Chan.

This exhibition focuses on intergenerational relationship gaps arising from language loss, 
and the resulting erosion of the transference of culture and tradition between generations. 
Featuring work from artists at different stages in exploring and rediscovering their roots, 
while attempting to facilitate communications between generations, this show also invokes 
themes of domesticity, gender, and migratory experiences.
Please join us for the opening reception on Thursday, June 20th, 6-9pm.

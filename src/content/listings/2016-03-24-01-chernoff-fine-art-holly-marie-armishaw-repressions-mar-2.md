Holly Marie Armishaw - Repressions

Chernoff Fine Art
21 January - 27 February 2016
OPENING RECEPTION: Saturday, April 2, 2-5 pm

Repressions embodies a unique approach to the formal qualities of the 
photographic medium while simultaneously provoking socio-political 
discussion. Each of the photographs in the series depicts a partial 
view of a young woman either dropping various objects, in a play 
between 2D and 3D objects, or interacting directly with frame. The 
frames are not mere formal devices in this series, but active 
participants in the minor performances of the artworks. The subjects 
engagement with the photographic surface connotes a consciousness 
of the confines of the medium. Viewed collectively, one is able to piece 
together a unifying thematic thread beyond the formal qualities of the 
work through symbols of femininity, fragility and anger. The more 
"active" works suggest states of struggle, desperation, isolation, and 
even violence as the woman confronts the invisible boundaries of 
feminine existence. 

Holly Marie Armishaw is a graduate of Emily Carr University. Her work 
has been exhibited and published in Canada, the U.S., France and 
Brazil. Canadian venues include Dazibao (Montreal), YYZ (Toronto), 
and the Presentation House Gallery(North Vancouver)

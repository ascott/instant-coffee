Thursday, February 21, 2008 at 7:30 pm
—
Emily Carr Institute of Art and Design, Theatre, room 301


Artist Talk: ISABELLE PAUWELS

In conjunction with Exponential Future at the Morris and Helen Belkin Gallery, and her solo exhibition Triple Bill at Artspeak, Isabelle Pauwels will discuss her recent video works.

Isabelle Pauwels graduated from Emily Carr Institute in 2001 with her BFA and received her MFA at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago. Her work has been shown in solo exhibitions at Contemporary Art Gallery, (Vancouver); Mercer Union, (Toronto); Or Gallery, (Vancouver); and at Catriona Jeffries Gallery, (Vancouver). Her work has also been included in exhibitions at Signal, (Malmo); and the Alberta Art Gallery, (Edmonton). In 2007 Pauwels was honoured with a VIVA award from the Jack and Dorris Shadbolt Foundation.

This event is free and open to the public.

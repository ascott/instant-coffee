Rebecca Brewer and Veit Laurent Kurz's Locus Amoenus: Extinct Flame is a site-
specific, cultivated pond delineated by an impassive phosphorescent cube. The
vignette emphasizes a modest grotto which rises above the self-oxygenating pool,
where a small flame continually burns. Accompanying this courtyard installation are
two projected films: Veit Laurent Kurz's Rise and Fall of Hummerkreis (2024) and
Benjamin Saurer's Candle to Candle (2025) performance with organ.

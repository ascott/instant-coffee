/ / ALL SYSTEMS SPECIAL

/ / SATURDAY, MAY 24TH


vancouver's dubstep & bass music monthy. li-li-li-lighta! sound.

10pm - late. $15. VIVO (main & 4th ave). non-smoking venue. serious sound.
drinks at the bar.


/ /  MATTY G	argon records / dub police / san fran

Matty G, hailing from the San Francisco, brings his own style to the international dubstep arena. Merging west coast rap flavours and grit with the south london sound. Gaining worldwide recognition and respect after his hit single 50,000 Watts destroyed dance floors across the globe, Matty G has since played at the most prestigious dubstep events in the world, like Dubwar N.Y. and DMZ London. Steady on the uprise, Matty has gone further to join up with Caspa and Rusko's Dub Police imprint.
[http://www.myspace.com/mattygbeatz](http://www.myspace.com/mattygbeatz)  [http://www.argonrecords.com](http://www.argonrecords.com)


/ /  CALAMALKA	  lighta! sound

producer-live instrumentalist-DJ from Vancouver B.C. with releases on labels such as PLUG RESEARCH, STEREO VIDUAL, METATRONIX, LIGHTA! and B.E.A.R. performed live alongside: KODE 9, LOEFAH, BENGA, HATCHA, THE BUG, HOT CHIP, THE JUAN MCLEAN, DEADELUS, CUT COPY, MR LIF, JAH WOBBLE, OUTHUD, WHY?, SUBTLE, CANNIBAL OX, DEADBEAT and shitload more.. [http://www.myspace.com/calamalka](http://www.myspace.com/calamalka)


/ /  GLITCHY & SCRATCHY    integrated grime unit

Young gun Bevvy Swift and 8 year turntable vetran Dj Global come together to form Glitchy and Scratchy. With tunes reflecting influences all over the map, these two never fail to raise eyebrows and drop the heavies. For glitchy upbeat wompers and grimey downbeat stompers, stay tuned for more
from these cats.  [http://www.myspace.com/glitchyscratchy](http://www.myspace.com/glitchyscratchy)


/ /  MICHAEL RED    lighta! sound

vibrations reader, immatator detector and inner space astronaut m r ed will be doing the closing set of night. picking it up where matty g leaves off and eventually winding the night down at the very end. it could stay hype til the end or it could smooth out a bit. whatever seems called for.
[http://www.myspace.com/michael_red](http://www.myspace.com/michael_red)


/ /  FRAME    dubstep.fm

Freshly imported from Victoria to Vancouver, Frame looks to spread the more chill and musical side of dubstep to the people. Coming up in the Vancouver drum and bass scene, Frame has made the transition over to dubstep finding his style within the heavy roots and rhythm aspects of the music. Holding down residencies at The Reef and Mo Fire radio show on
dubstep.fm, Frame is ready to set the right tone for the night.
[http://www.myspace.com/djframebccanada](http://www.myspace.com/djframebccanada)


/ /  TACHICHI	 low pressure / camobear

T will be handling the mic for this night. this might be percieved as a musical risk, but at the same time it's kinda a sure shot (for some good times in the least). T comes from a rap background clearly, and dubstep's pretty new to him. but one thing that's NOT new to him is rocking the mic, hyping the crowd and having a good time, collecting respect from both the
fans and the fellow mc's. so it go.

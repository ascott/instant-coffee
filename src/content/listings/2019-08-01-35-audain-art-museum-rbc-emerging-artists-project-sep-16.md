New AAM Emerging Artist Project Call for Artists

The Audain Art Museum (AAM) is deeply committed to fostering the growth of a thriving 
arts community and the support of Canadas emerging artistic talent. Museums are a 
key element in the advancement of a successful artistic career, yet there is a lack of 
opportunity to directly connect emerging artists with museum professionals. In 
conjunction with Emily Carr: Fresh Seeing - French Modernism and the West Coast, the 
AAM has developed the Emerging Artists Project. This new program will provide 
professional development opportunities for ten emerging artists through a series of 
dialogues with a group of museum professionals and art collectors. Participants will ask 
questions and discuss challenges while gaining insight on museum practices.

Eligible

devotes 24 hours a week or more to the pursuit of their art,

has not yet had a major exhibit of their work,

has not yet emerged/been showcased beyond their primary country of residence,

18-35 years of age upon.

Applications will be accepted by email, and must include an expression of interest 
artists statement and 5 digital images of recent works with short descriptions, and a 
resume.

The program will be held at the Audain Art Museum in Whistler,

Transport will be provided throughout the Lower Mainland of BC.

First Session date- October 26, 2019

Second Session Date " November 23, 2019

Please Submit Application by September 16, 2019 To

at the 2nd Annual Yellow Crane Festival

http://www.yellowcranefestival.com/

When - August 1st, 2nd, 3rd & 4th 
11-4pm Daily / Free Admission
On Granville Island between Emily Carr University & The Granville 
Isl. Hotel

From August 1st to 4th from 11am to 4pm daily, over 20 Emily Carr 
students will be transforming a less travelled area on Granville Island 
into a bustling and inspirational hub of art making and design 
construction Ð with daily performances and refreshing snacks to 
nourish the appetite for culture and food. The towering and historical 
yellow crane located between Emily Carr University and the Granville 
Island Hotel, formerly carried goods from train to boat along a 
platform. Where the past mixes with the present, it is here that 
students will set up gazebos and invite the public to watch the 
creative process unfold. 

ÒWe will be working in a wide variety of media including ceramics,

2012 participant, who will be working on sculptural paintings and 
illustrations. ÒThere will also be some great opportunities to dive in 
and either help an artist or make something yourself.Ó

ÒThe supportive and collaborative energy of the student and teacher

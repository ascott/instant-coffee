MOODYVILLE FILMS

FREE ADMISSION

Empire Esplanade 6 Cinemas
200 West Esplanade, North Vancouver

Thursday June 5, 2008
7 pm

An hour and a half of films about North Vancouver in conjunction with
Presentation House Gallery¹s current exhibition, Moodyville: Karin Bubas,
Jim Breukelman, Babak Golkar, Mike Grill, Kyla Mallett, Jeremy Shaw, Dan
Siney that ends on June 15.

Moodyville Films looks at different lifestyles and legends of North
Vancouver, past and present hippie culture, skateboarding, extreme
mountain biking, and straight edge dancing. The program includes Mudflats
Living, a 1972 half-hour NFB documentary about the ³counter-culture²
community of squatters who lived at the Mudflats near Dollarton in an inside
view of alternative lifestyles; The Seylynn Story (2006) directed by George
Faulkner about North Vancouver¹s world famous skateboard park built in 1978;
and the already legendary film style of North Shore extreme mountain biking.
Excerpts will be screened from the brand new video Seasons by The Collective
and from NSX 9: Trail Tales & Other Lies by Todd Fiander of Digger Know Fear
Enterprises featuring ³ball busting² footage from the highest vertical drop
on the North Shore. Also in the program are two video artworks by Jeremy
Shaw. 7 Minutes (1995/2002) shows two teenage girls in a fistfight at a
house party in North Vancouver. The tension in this mesmerizing footage of
reckless youth is emphasized by Shaw¹s original soundtrack as Circlesquare,
with lyrics about the ever-present anxiety of living in an earthquake prone
city. The slow-motion footage of Shaw¹s Best Minds Part Two (2007),
accompanied by an original soundtrack by Circlesquare, was shot at a
straightedge dance at North Vancouver¹s Seylynn Hall.

The Moodyville project is financially supported by the Spirit of BC
Opportuntities Program, BC Museums Associaiton: BC 150 Grant, and the City
of North Vancouver and District of North Vancouver Office of Cultural
Affairs.

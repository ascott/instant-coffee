In Disharmony n Flow, Claire Sarfeld falls in love with colour all over again. Reflecting on David 
Hockneys quote I dont believe there are off-putting colours, the artist combines her signature 
bold abstract style with a unique palette. In this new series of work, Sarfeld immerses in neons 
and metallics, intertwining wild splashes of paint with marks made with makeshift brushes. The 
artist engages the viewer by offering small windows into her process while simultaneously 
obliterating its sight with solid, flat surfaces. Her process starts intuitively in disharmony, and 
through a flow of brushstrokes, finally comes into a balance. In this exhibit, one is invited to go 
on a visual and auditive journey, fueled by art and rock n roll.

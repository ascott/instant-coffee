Emerging Painters of Vancouver

Open Call:

This is an independent publication project, focused on contemporary painters in and
around the Vancouver area, with the intention of connecting and collaborating with the
local emerging painting community.

The project will be a serialized publication looking into the process, influence, daily
experience and overall practice of local painters. Each issue will blur the line between
conventional feature and artist book, exploring the work by unique means, shaped in
conjunction with the artists themselves.

This is a nascent and independent publication; as such, compensation will be fulfilled
by ongoing royalties (50% of all profits from the issue will go to the artist the remaining
profits will go back into the publication) as well as copies of the issue.

There is no restriction on medium or genre so long as painting is the key focus of your
submission.

Applications are free and accepted on a rolling basis.

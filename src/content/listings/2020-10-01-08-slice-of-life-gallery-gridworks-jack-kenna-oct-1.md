Jack Kenna: Gridworks

October 01 - 04, 2020

Slice of Life Gallery - 1636 Venables St.

Pre-booking preferred at sliceoflifevancouver.com

Opening Day Thursday, October 1st, 11am - 11pm

Featuring bar, food truck, and artist in attendance from 6pm onwards

Viewings Friday to Sunday, 11am - 11pm

Masks required, physical distancing protocols in effect

Gridworks presents new drawings and paintings by artist Jack Kenna. Evolving out of an
ongoing body of work inspired by milk crates, this exhibition is devoted to paring down
and abstracting this subject with an attention to tessellating geometry, colour interaction,
visual perception, and the particularities of the artists hand. Special thanks to visual
artist Kalvin Valko for his contribution to the exhibition as well as ExotiK Custom
Framing and Exhibition Services for framing six new drawings by the artist. This is
Kennas second exhibition with the gallery.

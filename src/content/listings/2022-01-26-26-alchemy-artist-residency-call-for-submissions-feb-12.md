Alchemy Artist Residency: Prince Edward County 2022
Program Dates: June through September 2022 (2-to-4-week time slots available)
Submission Deadline: February 12, 2022
Programming/coordination: $500.00 - $600.00 per artist

Alchemy is an international artist-led residency exploring the common themes between
artistic practice and the cooking and sharing of food in community settings. Since
2017, this residency has taken place in rural Prince Edward County, Ontario, Canada,
during the Harvest Season.

Alchemy Prince Edward County 2022 plans to host and curate 10-12 artists (2-3 per
month) for two to four weeks at a time. Participating artists will:

leave something behind for either a virtual exhibition or a site exhibition, or a self-
directed public art tour during Field
Notes 2022 at the end of the Fall Harvest Season.
be paired to a site (farm/vineyard) and have access to land for site-specific work
pursue studio work in a local community public space
contribute up to one day per week to our Table Settings supper program (working
under the supervision of our kitchen brigade coordinator)

Alchemy 2022 will be facilitated by two long time collaborators for this work:

Claire M Tallarico, a visual artist and trained chef whose social practice explores how
the everyday activities of art making and food sharing, can contribute to the well-being
of a community and its citizens.

Tonia Di Risio, a visual artist whose work has developed through ongoing
investigations of the growing, making, preserving, celebrating, and sharing of food.

TO APPLY

Artists with an appetite for living in a community setting while creating art and shared
meals are encouraged to apply. Applications must include artist CV, artist statement, a
letter of intent, and images/artist
website.

2022 plans will include adherence to all federal, provincial, and local COVID-related
safety precautions to keep both community members and participating artists safe.

Details about how and where to submit your application can be found at
http://www.makealchemy.com

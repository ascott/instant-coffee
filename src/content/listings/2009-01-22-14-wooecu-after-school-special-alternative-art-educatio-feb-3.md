After-School Special: Alternative Art Educations

with guests Laiwan, Randy Lee Cutler and Kit Grauer

February 3, 2008

4:00pm-6:00pm

Emily Carr University - South Building - room 401B

WOO is the Emily Carr student newspaper. As part of our programming,
panel discussions will be held on a monthly basis addressing
issues in contemporary art as a means to create a dialogue both within the
school and between the school and the larger community. A group of
Emily Carr students will join guests Laiwan, Randy Lee Cutler and Kit Grauer
in a discussion about alternative methodologies in contemporary art education.

Refreshments will be served.

Emily Carr University South Building - room 401B

1399 Johnston Street, Granville Island

Vancouver BC V6H 3R9

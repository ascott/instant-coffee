Wild Clay Workshop

Digging deep into ancient pottery techniques using locally hand foraged clay

Three Mondays in October

October 3rd and 10th, 6pm-8pm. October 17th 4-8pm

$390, class limited to 5 participants

Email to register: ilenaleepottery@gmail.com

Join me to learn how to make pottery in the most ancient methods. Connect with this
land with respect and reciprocity. In this workshop you will learn how I find usable clays
in the wild, and how to forage ethically and respectfully. I will show you how I process
the clays into a usable form with simple everyday tools you probably already own. We
will make small vessels and fire (bake) the pots in a pit fire.

Ilenaleepottery.com for more information on the artist and available classes

ERIC DEIS
Shadows Cast on Imagination’s Past

October 1 - 29, 2009

Opening Reception
Thursday October 1, 2009
6 pm - 8 pm

Shadows Cast on Imagination’s Past is an exhibition of photography by Vancouver
artist Eric Deis. Featuring images from Vancouver, Tokyo and Toronto, Deis weaves
narratives of introspective moments by composing regular people within sublime
landscapes.

Deis’ iconic image Hipsters and Drug Dealer captures Vancouver’s complex
social histories by portraying the demise of the historic Woodward’s building through
a chance encounter with gentrifying residents.

Photographed using a virtual-view camera, a custom digital-analog system developed
by Deis, his large-scale images, over a gigapixel in resolution, produce a remarkable
clarity that completely immerses the viewer within the scene.

ELISSA CRISTALL GALLERY
2245 Granville Street, Vancouver, BC

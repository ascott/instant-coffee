Job Description

Position:Marketing and Communications Coordinator
Reports to:Executive Director
Works With:All Staff, Board of Directors, members and potential members.

Job Summary
The Marketing and Communications Coordinator is responsible for advancing 
CAGs presence locally, nationally and internationally through development of 
innovative marketing, publicity and communication strategies and impactful print 
and digital materials. By working collaboratively with staff, board committees and 
external parties, the Marketing and Communications Coordinator develops 
approaches that drive and support achievement of CAGs objectives.
Key Responsibilities Reporting to the Executive Director, the Marketing and 
Communications Coordinator is responsible for the following:

Duties:
MARKETING AND COMMUNICATIONS
Works closely with the Executive Director to articulate and develop CAGs
overall communications strategy Develops communication materials, ensuring 
messages are consistent with the CAGs goals and objectives Writes and edits for 
a variety of communication streams, and assists others by reviewing, editing, 
writing and/or finalizing copy to align with CAGs standards and
style sheets Develops copy as ready-to-print and using content written by other 
staff, integrates into existing CAG templates, or develops design copy, at times 
using and coordinating with external designers and printers
Develops design and layout of some marketing and publicity material using
design software for advertisements, newsletters, signage, bulletins, special 
events, flyers, visitor card, membership brochures, signage, etc.
Prepares, updates and manages communication material for website including
creative management of social media across all platforms and the broader on-line
presence, and updates gallery listings on external websites
Maintains e-lists and mailing lists and coordinates distribution of exhibitions and
event invitations and notices Has responsibility for the communications budget
Coordinates and negotiates costings with external printers, vendors,
advertising, marketing and web services Oversees work of temporary 
communications interns and staff

PRESS
Drafts press releases that deliver core messaging and reflect the CAGs
branding and vision Liaises with press, journalists and magazines in print and 
digital forms to promote CAG program Works with the Executive Director and 
Curators to liaise with artists and their representatives for interview requests, 
images for press and any other materials as relevant
Liaises with external designers in production of marketing materials including
but not exclusive to the bulletin, advertisements, brochures, social media 
templates, website etc. Responds to and/or directs media enquiries to the 
appropriate person and works with development for press launches and other 
special events Monitors and files press clippings
Works with Development to organize and coordinate press events, media
sponsorships and partnership initiatives Qualifications Education and Experience
Degree in communication, journalism or related art discipline with three to five 
years experience including comprehensive knowledge of the arts and culture 
community.

Skills and Abilities
Solid knowledgeable of business operations including communication
strategies, effective processes and branding. Solid leadership, customer-service, 
diplomatic and professional skills that represent CAGs values
Advanced ability to build relationships with key stakeholder groups including
corporate partners, Board of Directors, the Arts community, and donors.
Strong ability to conduct analysis, strategize, plan and initiate projects that
result in achieving targets and completing projects on time and on budget
Solid ability as a flexible and creative thinker, with effective problem-solving
skills Solution-driven with a constant desire to make things more effective and
efficient Advanced skills to write clearly, strategically, and quickly with appropriate 
styles for different audiences Solid media experience; can develop, write, and 
deliver convincing news stories Knowledge and understanding of the media, 
including print, television, radio and internet and is well networked Strong 
computer literacy including InDesign and other software, MS Office, Outlook, 
spreadsheets, Internet and digital interfaces

Deadline for Application: Monday, May 29 at 5:00
Salary range: $41,000 - $45,000. 40 hrs per week, full time. 10 days leave 
annually plus stats.

The Curators Sauna - Closing Party

July 29th Saturday 12-8pm

Canton-sardine, 268 Keefer St, unit-071, Vancouver

Free Admission

1.

Sauna To-Cha

Bryan Mulvihill Trolley Bus

2-3pm

Calling 8 tea-tasting players with handy artwork to join!

To-Cha is a tea-tasting contest, originating as a participatory social discourse in China
and later spreading to Korea and Japan. It involves social rituals to discern the finer
qualities of tea. We invite 8 participants, each of whom must bring their handy artwork
to join this game in the sauna. The winner
will earn all the others' artwork; no gain, no
game!

2.

Beef and Broccoli but No Beef ($234)

KitKit Para

3-6pm

Calling 30 guests cook and eat broccoli in the sauna.

With an interactive installation commenting on food inflation and the new grocery
rebate, KitKit spent $234 on materials for the project, which included 10kg of broccoli.
We invite 30 guests to steam their own portion of broccoli while using the sauna.
Participants who successfully cook and eat the broccoli with Kit's secret beef sauce will
receive a certificate from the artist.

3.

Game of Truth

Kristin Man

I-Ching Divination, Join anytime before 6pm

Exploring terrain marking and augmented reality via a cellphone app, Game of Truth is
a so-called meditational game of I-Ching, enabling participants to interact with physical,
virtual, and mental features.

4.

HeatTek

Timothy Fernandes & Arti Struyanskiy

6-8pm

Live techno music

"HeatTek" embraces the intense energy and unity found within saunas and
sweatboxes,
translating these qualities into a captivating live music performance. The
duo will create a sonic experience that captures the essence of the immersive
environments and the transformative power of electronic and techno music.

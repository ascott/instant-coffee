Or Gallery & Or Bookstore at the 2018 Vancouver Art Book Fair 

Opening Night Members Preview October 18, 6 - 8 PM
October 19 - 20, 11 AM - 7 PM
October 21, 11 AM - 6 PM

Reece and Claxton will present a talk on Saturday, October 20 at 6 PM in the 
ECUAD library. For more details, please visit the Vancouver Art Book Fair website.

VABF @ ECUAD
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Come visit the Or Gallery and Or Bookstore at the 2018 Vancouver Art Book Fair! 
We are pleased to be launching Northwest Coast #1 - Skeena Reece: The Business 
of Native Art Becomes Critical, the first publication in a new series edited by Dana 
Claxton, at the fair this year. Each publication in the series will feature a text by an 
artist which considers their art-making within the context of the Northwest Coast. 

Also launching: the Select//Or Mixtape subscription series! Dig out your Walkmans! 
With a subscription to the series you will receive twelve mixtape audio cassettes 
posted directly to your door. Each mix has been selected by a mystery DJ whose 
identity will only be revealed in an audio outro. Listen to a new mix every couple of 
months and guess whether it was crafted by Alexander Alberro, Sepake Angiama, 
Julia Feyrer, Diedrich Diederichsen, Raven Chacon, Deborah Edmeades, The Music 
Appreciation Society (Kathy Slade and Brady Cranfield), or others. Gift subscriptions 
are a great idea too. A two-year subscription costs $149.99.

The Or Gallery acknowledges its presence on unceded xwmkwy"m 
(Musqueam), Skwxw7mesh (Squamish) and sl"lwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) 
territories.

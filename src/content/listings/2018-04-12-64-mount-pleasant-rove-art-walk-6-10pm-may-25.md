ROVE is a free one-night art event in Mount Pleasant that designates a path for 
people to explore what the local art scene has to offer. ROVE provides guests the 
opportunity to discover a range of artwork, mix and mingle with local artists and take 
home a few unique pieces of their own. Attending the event is as simple as heading 
to Mount Pleasant on Friday May 25th between 6-10 p.m., picking up a map at any 
of the designated locations and following the plotted route to check out artwork at 
the various venues.

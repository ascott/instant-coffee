Workshop w/Tanis Saxby | OCT 23, 24 | OCT 23
Porcelain Clay Workshop with Tanis Saxby
Oct. 23, 6:30 p.m. - Oct. 24, 9:30 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

This workshop is "salon style" meaning Tanis will work with students individually on what
they choose to make, alongside her guidance and demonstrations of porcelain clay
techniques. Porcelain is a beautiful clay to work with and its properties are unlike any
other type of clay.
Designed for all levels, this workshop focus' on the elements of working with hand built
porcelain. Some introduction to historical and contemporary porcelain sculptors and
potters will also be discussed.
Class
times are: October 23 6:30pm-9:30pm, & October 24 6:30pm-9:30pm Beginners
are welcome.

Layout Fluid

Von Coffin

April 18, 2026

6 - 8 PM

Afternoon Projects is pleased to announce Von Coffins solo exhibition Layout Fluid.

Von Coffin (b 1983) lives in Redmond, Washington with their wife and two young children.
Coffin received their MFA in Painting and Printmaking from Yale University (2016) and BFA
from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago (2008). They run Coffin Farm, an experimental
sculpture park on their familys farm. Coffins experience raising animals, working at grocery
stores, and building museum display cases evolved into the objects, paintings, and food
works that constitute their practice. These modes act to render experiences of psychosis and
gender dysmorphia into, or onto, something else. Coffin engages various levels of abstraction
to examine such spectrums as sane-insane, F-M. Color is used as both symbol and signifier,
triggering nostalgia through specific imitations of candy and other material.

Ultimate Traveller: Impressions of Sudan

September 11
7-9 p.m.

Ferry Building Gallery
1414 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver, BC V7T 1C2

Did you know that Sudan actually has more pyramids than Egypt? The Sudanese are among
the friendliest and most hospitable people on earth. This audiovisual presentation will take you
on a kaleidoscopic journey exploring the many cultures and ancient sites of the Sudan, from
Khartoum, where two Niles become one, to the labyrinthine souks of Omdurman to the ecstatic
dances of the Sufi dervishes.

Cost: $15

Register at 604-925-7270 (course # 60200 ) or register online.

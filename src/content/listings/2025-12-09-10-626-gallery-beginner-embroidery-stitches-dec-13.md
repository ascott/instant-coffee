Workshop: Beginner Embroidery Stitches at 626 Gallery
December 13, 5:00-7:00 pm

Ready to dive into the world of embroidery? Join us in person for a fun and relaxing session

creating on your own! Join us for a relaxed embroidery workshop and embrace the slowness.

Poetry Workshop with Michael Turner
Oct. 19, 10:00 a.m. - 1:00 p.m.
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

This workshop is designed to assist those who appreciate poetry, who are
interested in writing
poems but have a hard time getting started, or would like to work in a different way.
Building on Arts ability to inspire, participants will acquire the necessary skills to produce (new)
poems from (old) song lyrics that bear no visible relationship to their source.
Participants are asked to bring to class the lyrics of between three to five songs of their
choosing (preferably popular songs from the 1950s to the present).
$45

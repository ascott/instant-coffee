Ruan
Guang-Min

Comics on the Island : Taiwan

Jan 27 - Feb 22, 2025

As part of this years Lantern City Festival celebrating the Lunar New Year, Pendulum
Gallery is hosting a solo exhibition featuring Taiwanese manhua artist Ruan Guang-Min.
Ruan's graphic novels vividly depict the local Taiwanese culture and way of life in
insightful and humorous ways. The exhibition includes drawings, books, and videos, and
features a series of large hanging lanterns suspended within the exhibition displaying
well-known characters from his graphic books and comics.

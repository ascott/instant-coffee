Shudder Gallery presents CALMATIVE selected works from Logan 
Sturrock
and Trevor Baird.

CALMATIVE employs the visual language of architectural and 
household
debris to explore the ideas of intuition and beauty. Formed directly
from the transient landscape surrounding the studio, the objects and
ideas inhabit a flow of discarded construction materials; clothing and
furniture coming and going, arbitrarily put together, playing. Using
intuitive choices with a notebook or a camera to capture something
curious, a moment, something of beauty in a setting or object, a
quickening, and then scavenging of material to fit the idea. While
recreating these compositions from note or memory a layer of
inaccuracy is added to the original, encouraging the viewer to become
an active participant in the work and aware of their presence within
the compositions.

Logan Sturrock and Trevor Baird are students and artists from
Vancouver, BC. Sturrock is currently completing a BFA in critical
studies and sculpture at ECUAD. Baird is also completing a BFA at
NSCAD in Halifax, NS.

G Visual Art
Reflections on Depression
Carnegie Community Centre 3rd Floor Gallery
401 Main St, Vancouver BC

March 1 - 30th 2019
Opening Reception Wednesday March 6 7-9pm

Carnegie Community Centre is proud to present a solo exhibition by 
2017 DTES Small Arts Grant recipient G Visual Art. Reflections of 
Depression is a series in Acrylic medium and shows us the different 
signs of depression as well some downward spiral it can lead us too.

G Visual Art is a DTES Artist and is currently enrolled at Emily Carr 
University of Art and Design. Also a 2018 and 2019 DTES Small Arts 
Grants recipient.

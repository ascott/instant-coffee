The Parent Show

We are excited to announce The Parent Show showcasing the work of our parents!

8 East Cordova - Parking Spot Projects

Opening: Thursday May 25 5-8pm

These selected photographs, sculptures, drawings and functional objects were created 
by our parents over the years. Initially started as a lighthearted idea to showcase their 
work, the project has encouraged us to reflect on how our art has been influenced by 
those closest to us. 

Parent Artists: Elizabeth Clarkson, Brigitte Gagne, Ben Holland, Margot Holland, Peter 
Lattey, Glen Mills, Rita Mills, Daniel Alan Muir, Linda Neumann

Hosted by: Stephanie Gagne, Denise Holland, Pippa Lattey, Julie Mills, Hayley Dawn 
Muir, Juan Cisneros Neumann

Window Design: Hei Lam Ng and Pippa Lattey

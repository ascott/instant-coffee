2019 Vancouver Arts Carnival 'Splendour of Canada"Painting 
Exhibition 

The 2019 Vancouver Arts Carnival Splendour of Canada Painting 
Exhibition ", the most inclusive, comprehensive and transparent 
painting exhibition and competition of British Columbia will be held in 
coming May. We have already received a good number of applications 
of very high standard. This is friendly- reminder to artists to submit your 
application before the deadline. 

Deadline for Submission: 31st March 2019 (Please visit our website at 
for details. 

BRIEF INTRODUCTIONS: 
Objective: Promotion of crossing boundaries exchange of arts and 
culture amongst ethnic communities of BC Province; Encouraging 
creativity and harmony of community with this open-to-all Arts Carnival. 

Themes: Beauty of Nature; Wonderful and Prosperous Life; 
Multiculturalism and Community Care. 

All the paintings shall be standardized in size for uniformity and 
fairness. Only images of paintings are required for first screening. 120 
paintings will be selected by the screening committee to enter the 
exhibition and competition. 

The exhibition will be hosted at the International Arts Gallery for 28 
days. 
Exhibited paintings will be adjudicated by a panel of 34 community 
leaders in a transparent, just and fair manner to determine the gold, 
silver and bronze awards for each category. 

Awarded winners of each category will receive Trophies/Certificate of 
Awards and Prize Money: and will be invited to attend on 
complimentary basis the dinner banquet at the evening after the 
closing ceremony at the Peninsula Seafood Restaurant of the Oakridge 
Mall in recognition of their achievements. 

All the 120 selected artists will be invited to attend the Closing 
Ceremony as well as the VIP reception. 

Entrance Fee: I. A non-refundable administrative/entrance fee of $20 
for each painting entered, 

**** If selected. An additional $80 will be charged for each painting 
which includes the cost of framing. The painting and frame will be 
returned to the artist after exhibition. ****

Sale of Painting: If the artwork shall be put up for sale during the 
exhibition, the process and terms shall be arranged and agreed 
between the artist and the arts gallery.

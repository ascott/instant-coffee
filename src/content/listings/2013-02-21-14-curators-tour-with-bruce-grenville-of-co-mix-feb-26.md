19. Labspace Studio | Noise Intercepted | CALL | MAR 4
Labspace Studio presents...
Noise Intercepted

Call for Collaborators across Canada
Noise Intercepted
March - July 2013

Noise Intercepted is a series of 10 experience-activated noise challenges, that 
prompt participants to listen, observe and interact with their urban 
soundscape in new and unlikely ways.

Over the course of 4 months you'll be sent 10 noise challenges/ creative 
prompts via text message and email. You'll have exactly 1-week to respond to 
each of these challenges and document your findings in a creative way on the 
Noise Project website.

Some examples of noise challenges we might throw at you:

● Spend at least one hour observing the ebbs and flows of rush-hour traffic in 
your city or town from close (but safe) proximity. Document your experience. 
Share your discoveries visually.

● Pick one song on your mp3 player and play it on repeat. Today, you can 
only listen to this one song alone. Listen to it on your way to work, or on your 
way to school. Listen to it on your lunch break. Take a long walk and listen to 
it again, and again, over and over. Document your experience. Share your 
findings.

Interested in participating? Visit http://thenoiseproject.net/ for more details 
and info on how to register. Deadline to register is March 4, 2013.

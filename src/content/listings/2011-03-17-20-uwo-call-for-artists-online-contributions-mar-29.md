Call for artists’ contributions to an online curatorial project undertaken
jointly by PhD, MA and MFA students at the University of Western Ontario.
The impetus of this project is to investigate the duality of artistic
identity as both a producer and consumer.

Please send us documentation of a souvenir that you own, and complete the
questions below, as soon as possible and not later than Tuesday, March
29th. Documentation could include, but is not limited to, drawings,
photographs or videos.

Images and information will be compiled and exhibited online. By
participating you acknowledge and permit the use of any material received
for the purpose of this exhibition.

If you would like further information please email: uwo.souvenir@gmail.com

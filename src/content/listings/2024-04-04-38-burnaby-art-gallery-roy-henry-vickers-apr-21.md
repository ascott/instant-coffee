Roy Henry Vickers

February 2-April 21, 2024
Burnaby Art Gallery
6344 Deer Lake Ave

Tuesday - Friday: 10:00 am-4:30 pm

Saturday - Sunday: 12:00 pm-5:00 pm

The extensive printmaking practice of Roy Henry Vickers spans more than six
decades. Vickers is a renowned Indigenous artist of Tsimshian, Haida, Heiltsuk, and
English ancestry. Born in the northern village of Laxgaltsap (Greenville), Vickers has
spent a lifetime drawing inspiration from the Gitxsan, Wet'suwet'en, Gitxaala, Tla-o-qui-
aht, and Lekwungen traditional territories he has called home. Vickers studied
traditional First Nations art and
design at the Gitanmaax School of Northwest Coast
Indian Art in Hazelton, and in 1986 opened his own gallery in Tofino, set within a
traditional northwest coast longhouse.

Vickers works can be found in private and public art collections around the world. He
has completed dozens of significant carved works and totem poles, advised on
numerous major projects including YVR Airport, and is the author of more than 10
illustrated books. This exhibition explores the imagery, stories, lands and waters at the
heart of his work.

RICHMOND ART GALLERY
Preparator
Auxiliary/On-call

POSITION OVERVIEW
The Richmond Art Gallery is a public gallery, owned and operated 
by the City of Richmond. We are looking to increase our current 
pool of qualified Preparators for periodic employment to assist with 
the installation and de-installation of exhibitions.

Preparators performs skilled technical work involving art handling 
and movement, and installation of a wide variety of exhibitions. 
Preparators perform work to various degrees of specialization in the 
following areas: art handling; condition reporting; mount making; 
packing and crating; matting and framing; installing two and three 
dimensional and new media art works and exhibitions; carpentry; 
the installation and maintenance of AV equipment; installation and 
troubleshooting of information and computer technology in 
exhibition and displays; performing other related tasks as needed.

EDUCATION AND EXPERIENCE
Completion of two years minimum course work in the visual arts 
and one or more relevant trades relevant to the work. A minimum of 
two to three years' experience and knowledge in one or more of the 
following areas required: experience working in a museum or 
gallery and the techniques of safe handling and movement of works 
of art (required of all incumbents); packing and crating of artworks; 
mount making; techniques, materials and methods used in the 
installation of two and three-dimensional works of art; standard 
practices, methods and skills, materials, variety of tools and 
equipment for carpentry and cabinet making.

KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ABILITIES
Ability to install and maintain computer hardware and 
software in exhibition and perform basic hardware and software 
troubleshooting.
Ability to lift and move heavy objects, communication and 
teamwork skills required.
Demonstrated understanding of hazards and necessary 
safety precautions involved in the work.
Valid B.C. Driver's License; agree to become certified to 
drive City vehicle.
Candidate must pass and maintain a criminal and police 
information check

HOURS OF WORK
Auxiliary/On-call work. Shift work with various hours. Successful 
candidates will be available to work a variety of shifts including 
days, evenings, and weekends.

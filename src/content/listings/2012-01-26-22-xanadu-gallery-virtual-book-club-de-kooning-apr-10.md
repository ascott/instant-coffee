After our last book club meeting in November where we discussed 
Caravaggio | A Life Sacred and Profane, participants voted to read de 
Kooning | An American Master as our next book club selection. Even 
though I read this book when it was first released in 2005, I'm excited to 
revisit the book as I feel it is not only one of the best artist biographies I 
have ever read, I consider it my favorite book of the last decade in any 
genre. I'm not alone my esteem for the book - it won the Pulitzer Prize 
for biography, and the National Book Critics Circle Award , along with 
toping many best-of lists for 2005.

De Kooning's life is fascinating, and this book does an amazing job of 
creating an intimate portrait of the artist, while at the same time 
capturing the spirit of the age of abstract expressionism.

Now an admission - I initially hesitated to read the book because I 
wasn't a big fan of de Kooning's work. If you find de Kooning's work 
difficult (or, dare I say? ugly) I want to encourage you to give this book 
a chance to change your mind. I came away with an understanding of 
where the art came from and I now look at de Kooning's work in a whole 
new light. Whether the book changes your mind or not, de Kooning is 
arguably one of the most important artists of the last century and I can 
promise you will enjoy the book.

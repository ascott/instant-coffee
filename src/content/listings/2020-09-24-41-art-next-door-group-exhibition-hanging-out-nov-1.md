"Art Next Door is looking for new and emerging artists to contribute to a new online
exhibition. The title of the exhibition is 'Hanging Out' deadline November 1st 2020

New Group Exhibition - 'Hanging Out'

In this exhibition we explore the ubiquitous idea of hanging out. This used to be a simple
(or was it ever really?) idea, that has become quite complex, especially with all the
proliferation of noise in the background of our minds. Is it safe? What's going to happen
tomorrow? Can I think of something better to do with my time? What happens to affection
and spontaneity when everyone has a spot marked X?
How are we colouring outside the lines now? Is it ethical to go rogue and blow someone a
kiss? Or is saliva our new poison? Is this temporary? Or have we been social distancing
for decades, driving the wedge between class and race and newcomer/old timer deeper
with every passing generation.
We've taken hanging
out for granted, but it means so much more now that we can take a
look at it from a safe distance and start to pull it apart.
Perhaps the strongest act of defiance is simply a small step in the direction of someone
else? If you can handle the intrusion."

Please send all your submissions with titles in the image itself to
ariaroundtheworld@gmail.com On average we can pick maximum 3-5 images based on
how much response we get but even just one would be great. You may include a bio or
any contact info you want attached. Thanks so much and we're looking forward to seeing
what's out there.

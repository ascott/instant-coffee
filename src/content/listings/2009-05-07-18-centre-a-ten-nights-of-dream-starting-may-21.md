TomoeArts and Centre A present:
Ten Nights of Dream
a movement-music-image performance based on stories by Natsume Sôseki
May 21-23, 8:30pm
Centre A, Vancouver International Centre for Contemporary Asian Art
2 West Hastings Street, Vancouver

The uncanny world of dreams and the unconscious is brought to life through movement, music 
and visuals in TomoeArts new solo dance-theatre work, Ten Nights of Dream. 

A woman buried with a piece of fallen star; a blind child riding on the back of the dreamer; a huge 
ship seemingly going nowhere; a panama hat; a barber; a goldfish seller; a thousand pigs. These 
are a few of the haunting images found in this TomoeArts performance.

Ten Nights of Dream is a full-length performance combining solo choreographies, original music 
composition, and visual projected images, all based on Japanese novelist Natsume Sôseki's 
uncanny and ghostly series of tales, Ten Nights of Dream (written in 1908). The choreographies 
are based on the forms and aesthetics of nihon buyoh (Japanese classical dance). Each piece 
focuses on central metaphors from the tales, expanding them into movement and character 
motifs that embody the core energy and atmosphere of each dream. Original electro-acoustic 
music incorporates live shakuhachi flute and recorded sounds. Projected images weave still and 
moving images into each choreography completing the dream-like atmosphere of the 
performance.

Choreography and performance: Colleen Lanki 
Composition and Performance: Alcvin Ramos
Projections and Lighting design: Craig Alfredson, David Kim, Yulia Shtern, and Ana Maria 
Espinoza Vaca
Direction: Matthew Romantini 

NOTE: Dr. Sharalyn Orbaugh (Asian Studies, UBC) will give a free talk on Natsume Sôseki before 
the Friday performance (7pm Friday May 22).

TomoeArts (pronounced toh-moh-ay) is a new company that promotes, teaches and performs 
nihon buyoh (Japanese classical dance). It creates and presents performances incorporating the 
forms and aesthetics of Japanese traditional performing arts. Ten Nights of Dream is presented 
as part of the explorASIAN Festival celebrating Asian Heritage Month in Metro Vancouver.

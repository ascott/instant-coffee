The Crying Room Mural Projects is pleased to present a public mural 
project by Devon Knowles, May 1-31, 2012.
Devon Knowles, Drifter, medium density overlay panel and latex paint, 
4' X 7', 2012

The Crying Room Projects, 157 East Cordova Street, Vancouver BC

Working with a material based practice Devon Knowles examines 
modern production models using craft techniques, fine art traditions 
and DYI ideologies. Her installation work responds to site-specific 
architectural spaces, creating whimsical distortions of optical patterns 
and reflections using plywood, bronze and stained glass. By privileging 
her role as fabricator, she allows for the individual material sensibilities 
and attentiveness to the optical experience to expand beyond the 
range of material and craft customs.

Knowles lives and works in Vancouver, British Columbia. She received 
her MFA from the University of Victoria in 2008 and attended the 
University of Guelph where she acquired a BA Honours. Devon 
Knowles' work has been shown throughout Canada, the United States 
and Europe.

Curated by Colleen Heslin

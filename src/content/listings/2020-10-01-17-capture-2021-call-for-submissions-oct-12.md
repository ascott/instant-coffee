From September 14 to October 12, 2020 at 11:59 PM, Capture Photography Festival is
accepting Exhibition and Event proposals for inclusion in the juried Selected Exhibitions
Program and non-juried Event program. The 2021 Capture Photography Festival runs
from April 2"30.

Capture strives to support artistic excellence in diverse lens-based practices, with a
focus on critical and challenging work, while nurturing emerging talent. The Festival
cultivates partnerships with local and international artists and curators with the goal of
collectively presenting compelling lens-based art. Capture is committed to
presenting
perspectives from diverse backgrounds and members of underrepresented groups.

Capture is pleased to be launching our inaugural Printing Prize with Studio f6o4. At a
value of $10,000 CAD, the Printing Prize will be awarded to an artist participating in
Capture's 2021 Selected Exhibition Program. To be considered, please review the
details in the submission form below and indicate your interest in being considered by
filling out that section of the form.

All proposals will be processed through our submission platform ArtCall. There is a
submission fee of $25 for Exhibitions, which includes a 1-year Capture Photography
Festival Society membership. There is no Event submission fee. Please note that
ArtCall requires account registrations (its free!).

Learn more at capturephotofest.com/submissions. Before submitting, please visit the
Submissions FAQ. For any other queries including the ArtCall submission platform,
please email info@capturephotofest.com.

We look forward to
reviewing your photography and lens-based submissions for April
2021!

Trench would like to invite you to the launch of the gallery's first in 
depth look into a specific period of Vancouver Painter Ron Stoniers 
artistic legacy.
1964: The Early Abstracts of Ron Stonier will consist of 12-16 small 
works on panel (never before exhibited) and 5 larger canvas’s and 
examines Stonier’s progress as a young artist as he moves away
from abstract expressionism toward his post painterly works of the mid 
to late 1960’s.

We hope that you can join us for this interesting and exciting exhibition.

1964: March 8th till April 7th, 2012.

Opening Reception: Thursday March 8th 6-9pm.

Where: In the Trench. 148 Alexander Street. Gastown.

Also, On until this Saturday March 3rd: PARIS WHITE: The Language 
Paintings of Enn Erisalu.

Look forward to seeing you soon.

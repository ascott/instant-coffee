Lecture and book signing | Together and Apart with Alex Webb and Rebecca
Norris Webb | Saturday May 19, 7pm

Join us for a unique opportunity to hear from two internationally renowned
photographers who will discuss the process of working Together and Apart,
and will profile a selection of their photographs.

Alex Webb is a member of Magnum Photos and has had photographs printed in
TIME and National Geographic. Together, Webb and Norris Webb have published
a total of 18 books and have exhibited internationally.

Together and Apart is organised in collaboration with FotoFilmic, and in
conjunction with the two-day workshop, Finding Your Vision, on Bowen
Island, BC from May 19-20.

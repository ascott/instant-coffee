INTERSECTIONS
Goes Live!

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society in partnership with The Tomorrow 
Collective’s Brief Encounters is pleased to present the premiere of 
INTERSECTIONS, a new moving image project where a local artists and 
filmmakers are matched together for a two month span to create new works 
online.

Inspired by the work of Tomorrow Collective's Brief Encounters series, 
INTERSECTIONS will also feature a live event in the summer of 2013.

Please visit 
[http://cineworks.ca/screeningroom/category/collaborations/intersections/](http://cineworks.ca/screeningroom/category/collaborations/intersections/) to 
watch the first INTERSECTIONS pairing. New works will then be released 
online each month. Stay connected by following us on Twitter @Cineworks, 
@TTC Vancouver and join our group page on Facebook for all the latest news, 
behind-the-scenes photos, and premieres.

Please join us for a lecture by visiting curator and writer Negarra A. Kudumu, who will be 
sharing her research on African art history and African diasporic contemporary art.

Residing at the intersection of contemporary art, curation, and critical theory, Negarra A. 
Kudumu's practice is situated squarely within the domain of cultural production and 
consumption. She is ever investigating the ways in which these processes are visually 
and discursively interpreted, with an expertise in the contemporary art of the African 
continent and its diaspora, and a special interest in the emerging visual culture of Iran, 
South Asia, and their respective diasporas. Negarra received her BA from Dartmouth 
College and her MA from Leiden University. She is Manager of Public Programs at the 
Frye Art Museum in Seattle and Founder and Owner of Perpetuity Healing Arts.

29. Yactac | Rule of Three | MAR 22

Rule of Three

Chanel Bode
Minahil Bukahari
Krystle Coughlin
Frances Hart D'Emilo
Emilio Rojas

Curated by Micaela Mae Kwiatkowski

Opening Night: Thursday, March 22nd 7:00-10:00PM
Location: 855 E. Hastings, Vancouver (Red Gate Arts Society)

Rule of Three creates sensations through artistic acts of exposure, disruption and 
transformation. The exhibition is informed by lived experience as artistic practice and 
Sara Ahmeds willful subject who is willing to get in the way to pose a problem 
(Ahmed, Living a Feminist Life). Rule of Three poses questions through various 
processes of becoming that provoke reactions by recognizing and acknowledging the 
limits and interconnectedness of the subject, while challenging the structures that push 
back on them. The exhibition features artwork from emerging artists Chanel Bode, 
Minahil Bukahari, Krystle Coughlin, Frances Hart DEmilio, and Emilio Rojas and takes 
place on the unceded traditional territory of the Coast Salish Peoples including the 
territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxwu7mesh (Squamish), St:l and 
Sl"ilwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

Curated by Micaela Mae Kwiatkowski

30. Vancouver Heritage Foundation + CBC Radio-Canada | Call for Artists | MAR 23

CALL FOR ARTISTS

Vancouver Heritage Foundation in partnership with CBC Radio-Canada, with
support from JJ Bean Coffee Roasters and produced in partnership with the
City of Vancouver Public Art Program, is once again accepting submissions
for a large scale art installation on Hamilton Street, on the CBC Plaza. The
WALL, a 40x30 vertical concrete expanse, offers opportunity each year for
an artist to respond to the theme of Vancouvers built environment.
Independent curators and artists, including those who have previously
applied but not been selected, are invited to apply by March 23rd, at 5pm.
The successful artist and/or curator will have the opportunity to visit the
CBC Archives as a source for material and inspiration.

We invite you to share this call for artists with your network and encourage
those interested to apply. Attached is the Call for Proposals, and below is
the link to our webpage where more information, as well as details about
previous artists and their work, are available. Any questions or inquiries
can be directed to mail@vancouverheritagefoundation.org.

31. UNIT 17 | Leslie Thornton | SO MUCH MUCH | MAR 25

Leslie Thornton 
SO MUCH MUCH

26 March - 6 May 2018 

opening reception: 25 March 12 - 6pm
DIM Cinema screening: 28 March 7:30pm

Unit 17 is thrilled to present SO MUCH MUCH, a solo exhibition by New York artist 
Leslie Thornton. This exhibition and screening mark the artist's first presentation in 
Canada. Thornton will be in attendance for both the opening reception and screening. 

Leslie Thornton (b. 1951) is a recognized pioneer of contemporary media aesthetics 
who is engaged with film, video, photography and installation. Retrospectives have been 
held at MoMA (New York), FemCine (Santiago), Anthology Film Archives (New York) 
and at the Brooklyn Academy of Music. Her work has been widely exhibited at venues 
such as dOCUMENTA (Kassel); MoMA PS1 (Long Island City); Centre Pompidou 
(Paris); the Whitney Biennial (New York); Raven Row and Rodeo Gallery (London); the 
New York Film Festival, Oberhausen, and Rotterdam International Film Festival, among 
others. Thornton is in the permanent collections of Fundaci Antoni Tpies, Centre 
Pompidou, MoMA, the Pacific Film Archives, the Walker Art Center and Jeu de paume. 
In 2018 she was the first recipient, in company with James Richards, of a new CERN 
Artist Residency, sponsored by Centre d'art Contemporain Genve. Forthcoming 
exhibitions include Kuenstlerhaus Stuttgart, Secession (Vienna) and Malm Kunsthalle.

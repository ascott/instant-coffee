Job Description
Position Title: Communications Director
Location: Arnica Artist Run Centre
Reporting to: Gallery Administrator
Hours: 10 hours per week; some evening and weekend work; flexible schedule
Start date: August 24, 2016 (9 month contract with the possibility of
extension, depending on funding)
Pay schedule: $20-$30 per hour, commensurate with experience.

General Description of the Position:
The Communications Director is responsible for public communications for
Arnica and reports directly to the Gallery Administrator. The
Communications Director is responsible for creating, disseminating and
maintaining current and archived information about exhibitions and other
activities related to Arnica's programming and public image (press
releases, newsletters, advertisements, social media, documenting and
archiving exhibitions). The CD oversees the production of exhibition
monographs, catalogues and other publications for the Centre. The CD is
also responsible for website management and updates.

Required Competencies:
- Graphic Design, photography and videography, photo- and
design-based computer skills;
- Creativity, imaginative problem-solving, resourcefulness;
- Collaboration, commitment, reliability, schedule flexibility;
- Visual, verbal and written communication skills.

General Responsibilities:
- Promoting programming through designing website and social media
content, and crafting newsletters, press releases and mass emails;
- Website management, regularly updating content and periodically
developing new methods of interactive outreach that augment Arnica's
mandate and programming;
- Exhibition and event documentation for archiving and interactivity
on Arnica's website (e.g., interactive virtual gallery, live-streaming,
exhibition content submissions from community);
- Participation in the graphics and communications aspects of group
projects as required;
- Working with the Gallery Administrator towards effective
communications and promotion;
- Other duties as required.

Required Skills and Education:
- Advanced graphic design education, writing competence, and an
ability to creatively showcase exhibition programming online and in print;
- Competent working knowledge of MS Office, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe
InDesign, Adobe Illustrator, Mac System, and WordPress;
- Excellent working knowledge of social media (e.g. Flickr, YouTube,
Twitter, Facebook and Instagram);
- Advanced digital photography and videography skills;
- Strong time management skills and the ability to work without
supervision;
- Strong technical skills;
- Understanding of Canadian artist-run centres an asset;
- Post-secondary education in design, communications and/or promotion;
- Previous paid work and/or volunteer experience with arts
organizations is helpful.

Other Job Requirements:
- Must be highly organized, detail-oriented, committed to quality and
able to work independently, with minimal supervision;
- Must be able to collaborate with others on projects that extend
Arnica's outreach into a virtual presence and a regional/global and diverse
audience;
- Must be willing to learn new technologies as required for
innovative communications;
- Must maintain a professional, cooperative manner while working for
Arnica;
- Must understand and maintain confidentiality on sensitive
Arnica-related business.
- Ability to work flexible hours.

Arnica is an artist-run centre in Kamloops, BC and a registered BC Society
and charity, with exhibition programming that fosters multicultural, First
Nations, and multidisciplinary interests, and the intent of engaging and
educating the public about contemporary art through the presentation of
work by emerging and established artists, and through lectures, workshops,
readings, performances, and other community-based events. Arnica is an
equal opportunity employer. Pay schedule commensurate with experience.

Deadline for Submissions is August 15, 2016. Applications should include:
- Cover letter with statement of design philosophy
- Digital e-portfolio
- Rsum (maximum 3 pages)
- Sample of writing (identify project)
- Self-addressed envelope with sufficient return postage (for
applications sent by surface mail)

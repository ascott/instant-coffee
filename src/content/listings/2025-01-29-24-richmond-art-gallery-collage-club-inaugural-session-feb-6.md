Richmond Art Gallery and the Richmond Media Lab host a monthly art-making social
for adults every first Thursday night of the month, February - December 2025
$5.00 Registration fee includes ALL tools and materials.
Register here: https://www.eventbrite.ca/e/rag-collage-club-february-2025-inaugural-
session-tickets-1143363995489?aff=oddtdtcreator

Get together with friends and make some art! Open studio time led by gallery staff
for adult participants to cut and paste with a variety of materials and tools while you
chat, listen to music, and sip some tea at our Collage Club social.

Inspired by the Gallery's
exhibitions, each month will feature a new theme to inspire
our projects. We will also connect to international collage groups for you to share
your collage works online, and provide printed copies of upcoming challenges from
the Paris Collage Collective, Februllage, World Collage Day, and more.

RAG and Media Lab staff will open their vast collection of cool collage materials,
digital tech, and more to lead a hands-on art-making night for adults. While the focus
is on the tenets of collage and cut-and-paste culture, we welcome participant
suggestions each month to experiment and play with various art materials and
techniques.

All levels of artistic skill are welcome! Limited spaces, registration is required.
Recommended for ages 16+

$5 per person includes all art supplies and use of our equipment.
Please note - no refunds. Funds raised go towards supporting this program.

February Theme: UPCYCLE
This month we celebrate this new program by creating something new out of
something
old. Recycle, reuse, and repurpose old papers, magazines, photos, and
posters to create a new artwork.
This session will be hosted in the Richmond Art Gallery Program Room.

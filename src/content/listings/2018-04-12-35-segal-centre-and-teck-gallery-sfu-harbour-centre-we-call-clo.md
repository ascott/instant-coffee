Cathy Busby: WE CALL Closing Reflections and Reception
THU, APR 19, 2018 / 7 - 10PM

Segal Centre and Teck Gallery, SFU Harbour Centre
515 W Hastings St, Vancouver
In April 2017 Cathy Busby's WE CALL opened at the Teck Gallery. The installation is 
comprised of selections from the Truth and Reconciliation Commission of Canadas 
(TRC) 94 Calls to Action that highlight the ways that governmental, educational and 
cultural institutions are called on by the TRC to cultivate Indigenous leadership, 
stewardship and participation within structural systems. 

Following its opening, Busby and curator Amy Kazymerchyk met with SFU faculty 
across disciplines to query how the Calls to Action were being activated in 
departmental structure, pedagogy and curriculum. In Fall 2017 SFU Galleries hosted 
an advisory committee at SFU Gallery comprised of faculty, alumni and students to 
discuss how SFU Galleries could better support Indigenous artists and audiences.

Through 2017 Busby also collaborated with staff, faculty, students and community 
members of the Gitksan Wet'suwet'en Education Society (GWES) in Hazelton, BC, 
to install a parallel iteration of WE CALL in their school halls.

Please join us at 7PM in the Segal Centre, for a reflection on WE CALL and its place 
within SFU Galleries structural operations and public programming with Cathy 
Busby, advisory committee members June Scudeler, Gabrielle L'Hirondelle Hill and 
Roxanne Charles, and GWES staff Kirsten Barnes, Andy Busby and Bonnie 
McCreery.

WE CALL will be exhibited until June 8, 2018.

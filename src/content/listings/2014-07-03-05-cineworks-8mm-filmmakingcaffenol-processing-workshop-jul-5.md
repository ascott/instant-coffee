Cineworks: 8mm Filmmaking/Caffenol processing Workshop
Handmade filmmaking workshop! Participants learn the basics of analog
cinema techniques and shoot short collaborative films using traditional
motion picture cameras. Hand processing of films using non-toxic
Eco-developers. Screening & discussion of experimental results.

Instructors: Poolo Davanzo & Lisa Marr- (Directors of Echo Park Film
Center, LA California)
Location: 235 Alexander Street (IronWorks building)
Date: Sat. July 5th, noon-5pm
Fee: $50
Limited to 12 participants.

Call for Submissions

The Maynard, an online poetry journal, is published in April and October.

Guidelines:

For Poetry Submissions:

Send 3-5 previously unpublished poems (no more than 10 pages total) to:
submission@themaynard.org, prepared as a .doc or .docx attachment. Please
put your name, address, and email on each page of your submission. Include
a short bio with your cover letter in the body of your email. We accept
simultaneous submissions. The deadline for the October issue: July 31.

For Art Submissions:

We accept submissions of cover art for each issue according to the same
deadlines as poetry submissions. The only constraint/requirement is that
the art involves an orange or oranges in some way. Send a high quality
image in the format of your choice, along with your bio, to:

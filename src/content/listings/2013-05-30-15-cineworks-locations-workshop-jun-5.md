Having the right location is like having the right actor, and finding and 
managing locations for your film productions can be a very challenging 
process for the indie production. How do you go about your search and then 
convince people to work with you and allow your entire cast and crew into 
their home/office/store? What are some best practices around not "burning" 
your locations and making sure that the next crew who comes along is 
welcomed as well? What do you need to know about General Liability 
insurance or shooting on city property?
Join us for this two hour panel discussion that aims to answer all these 
questions and more, and make your next shoot go that much smoother. 
Representatives from Creative BC and the city's film permit office will appear 
alongside one of Vancouver's most experienced Location Managers, to 
discuss how they work together and their end of getting a project from script 
to screen.

Panelists include:

Gordon David Hardwick, 

Manager of Community Affairs, British Columbia Film Commission, 
Creative BC

Gordon Hardwick has worked in British Columbia’s motion picture production 
industry since 1984. As a Locations Manager and Locations Scout he has 
helped produce over 30 projects, including TV movies, series, feature motion 
pictures and commercials. Since 1999 he has been employed as Manager of 
Community Affairs at the British Columbia Film Commission. On April 1, 
2013 the operations of the BC Film Commission were merged with BC Film 
and Media to become Creative BC. Gordon has been working towards better 
management of Motion Picture Location Resources. This has included 
working to standardize Film Policies and Guidelines for municipalities and all 
levels of government; and develop strategies to raise awareness of the motion 
picture industry and its importance to the economy of BC. 

Ann Goobie, Locations Manager

Ann Goobie has been working in the Vancouver Film Industry since 1991. 
She started as a Producers’ Assistant, worked as a Production Assistant, 
Location Scout and as an Assistant Location Manager before becoming a 
Location Manager in 1996.

Ann has worked on TV Series, TV Movies and small to large scale Feature 
Films. Since 2000, she has mainly worked on big budget feature films, such 
as X Men, Mission Impossible: Ghost Protocol and the A Team.

Working on larger features has many challenges for a Location Manager. 
From controlling blocks of downtown streets for car chases or explosions to 
dealing with business owners who are not happy seeing a Film Crew on their 
street. There is never a dull moment.

Cael Hopwood, City of Vancouver

Currently A/Manager of the City of Vancouver Film and Special Events Office. 
Cael has been involved with the Film and Event industry since 2006 working 
as a Liaison and now Manager. Cael is well versed on the City, 
knowledgeable of City bylaws and understands the complexities of ‘getting 
the job done’.

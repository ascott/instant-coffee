Open Studio

Every Sunday, 11 AM - 4 PM

Stop by the Annex Workshop from 11 AM to 4 PM during your Gallery visit! Explore
your artistic side and create your own work inspired by the current exhibitions. Free
for Gallery Members or with admission.

Indigenous Drawing Workshop with Artist Ungwa-Kasu Chris L. Wilson

October 1, 11 AM - 3 PM

In honour of National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, learn about Indigenous
design in this in-person workshop with Ungwa-Kasu Chris L. Wilson. Register here:
https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/events/oct1openstudio

=======

Panel Discussion: Lab Here//Too//For: Responsible Visions and Neglected Materials

October 5, 6 PM

Join us for a keynote address by landscape architect Dr. Alice Lewis, followed by a
discussion with invited guests Tara Klager, Aaron (Splash) Nelson MoodyTawxsin
Yexwulla (Skwxw7mesh Nation) as they discuss clothing/cloth/material as it relates
to embodied actions and reciprocal relations we have with one another, both human
and the more than human. Learn more:
https://www.vanartgallery.bc.ca/events/oct5forum

Open Season by Pongsakorn Yananissorn and Denise Holland 

Saturday, March 10 - Sunday, April 29, 2018
Location : 800 Robson Street

Opening March 10th
6:00PM - 9:00PM

Open Season introduces a series of ten artist projects that will be played out on a mock 
tennis court located on 800 Robson Street between the Law Courts and the Vancouver 
Art Gallery. The work draws from various meanings of the term court - a tribunal 
presided over by judges and magistrates, an area marked for sports, and an 
establishment of sovereignty.

Artists: Kaylee Louie, Marika Vandekraats, Denise Holland, Stephanie Gagne, Katharine 
Meng-Yuan Yi, Temporary Investments, Terry-Dayne Beasley, Sahand Mohajer, 
Chelsea Yuill, Julie Mills.

Projects include:

March 10 - April 29 / March 10 / 7:30PM Open Season , Pongsakorn Yananissorn

March 10 / 1:00 pm and 6:30 pm 42, Kaylee Louie

my Mothers Hands, Marika Vandekraats

Ten Different Things is a series of public art commissions in Vancouver that engages ten 
artists to investigate the role of culture as a critical ingredient in the construct and vitality 
of the contemporary city. 

Ten Different Things is a collaboration between CityStudio Vancouver and Living Labs at 
Emily Carr University of Art + Design, curated by Kate Armstrong and supported by the 
City of Vancouver Public Art Program. Janet Moore and Duane Elverum are co-founders 
of CityStudio Vancouver.

Activating Pigeon Park: Roots & Resilience
Hosted by Gallery Gachet at 399 Carrall St.

Activating Pigeon Park is a community curation project that is rooted in the
composite history of Pigeon Park located at Hastings and Carrall. A month of art
workshops will invite the community to interact with Pigeon Parks history and foster
imaginings of new stories through mixed media pathways.

August 19, 11am-1pm: Community Super 8 Film with Hives4Humanity
Artists Sarah & Cait of Hives4Humanity will lead a workshop that engages all your
senses: sight, sound, touch, taste, and smell. Together,
well create a community
Super 8 film through a series of interactive and sensory activities.

August 26, 11am-1pm: Chalkboard Mural, Word Garden & Bannock
Help create a chalkboard mural with Priscillia Mays Tait, take part in community
poetry creation with Dag Davidge at the word garden, and eat fresh bannock made
by Wayne Belong.

The address of the park is 399 Carrall Street. An accessible washroom is nearby at
Gallery Gachet. Full details at gachet.org!

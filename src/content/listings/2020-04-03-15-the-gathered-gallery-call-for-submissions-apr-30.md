CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS
THE GATHERED GALLERY
FALL 2020 PROGRAMMING
APRIL 30TH

Submissions received by April 30 are considered for our Fall programming (September -
December)

The Gathered Gallery is an
art blog of curated art and writing, which features at least 52 artists
each year through features, reviews, and a digital residency.

This online concentration of visual art facilitates connection between readers and artists, allows
the discovery of unique contemporary practices, and creates opportunities for gaining new
perspectives. The Gathered Gallery aims to contribute to the Canadian and International art
communities and add its voice to the conversation surrounding contemporary art practices.

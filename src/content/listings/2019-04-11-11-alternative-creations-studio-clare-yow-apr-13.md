From April 11 - 17, you are invited to an exhibition of new conceptual artworks by 
Vancouver-based visual artist, Clare Yow. These photographic pieces draw on the body 
as a site of inquiry and resistance, and grapple with its intimate ties to various lands and 
belonging.

Probing maternal loss and desire, migration ruptures, diasporic settlerhood, and the 
intersections between climate and bodily justice, the camera lens orbits home to the 
geography closet in, the artist's body. The exhibition title " common wealth " further 
assumes its name from the old meaning of wealth, which is well-being or welfare, 
as Yow finds herself constantly weighing the individual and specific, alongside the 
collective and universal.

Free and open to the public.

To apply, visit vancouver.ca/viva-refresh-invite. 
Applications are due by 5pm on Friday April 28, 2017.

VIVA is turning six this year, and the program is growing up. Were looking for 
creative ways to make the city more fun, and to get public input on VIVAs future 
direction.

You are invited to apply for up to $5,000 to fund a pop-up project to activate and 
reimagine a public space. We are looking to fund up to 10 projects located 
across the city. It could be something youve done before, or an entirely new 
idea. They should be:

Interesting, fun, and engaging
Easy to set-up and move
Deliverable within the budget and timeframe

We also want to learn what people think about Vancouvers public spaces. So 
were asking successful applicants to include our short survey as part of their 
pop-up project. The survey will help us gather input on the future direction of 
VIVA and support other public space initiatives, such as the upcoming Downtown 
Places and Spaces Plan and the Parks & Recreation Master Plan.

Further details:

Each selected project is eligible to receive up to $5,000.
Projects will activate one or more public spaces of the proponents choosing for 
at least one day, and rotated through two other existing public spaces of the 
Citys choosing.
The spaces could be on a sidewalk, parking space, street, or laneway, or in an 
existing plaza. The City reserves the right to suggest alternate locations where 
the proponents location(s) are not feasible.
Projects will go live between June and October 2017, following a special 
workshop event in May.
All logistics (e.g. insurance, set up and take down, moving and storage) will be 
the responsibility of the applicant. The VIVA team can help with permitting and 
other City processes.
Preference will be given to past VIVA partners and groups that have 
demonstrated experience enlivening public space, however all are welcome to 
apply. Please share with your networks!

To apply, visit vancouver.ca/viva-refresh-invite. Applications are due by 5pm on 
Friday April 28, 2017.

Submissions will be reviewed by the VIVA team, and successful applicants will be 
notified of selection outcomes by 5pm on Friday May 5, 2017.

We look forward to hearing from you!

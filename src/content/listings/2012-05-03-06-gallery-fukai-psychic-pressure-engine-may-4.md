Gallery FUKAI is pleased to announce PSYCHIC PRESSURE ENGINE. 
An installation by Vancouver based artist Christopher Donnelly.

Christopher Donnelly was born in Vancouver, Canada. He holds 
degrees from the University of British Columbia and Emily Carr 
University of Art and Design. He is an alumnus of the Capilano 
University Art Institute for Sculpture. He has exhibited in Vancouver at 
the Helen Pitt Gallery (2007), The Emergency Room (2008), The Grace 
Gallery (2009 & 2010) and The Capilano University Art Gallery (2009 & 
2011).

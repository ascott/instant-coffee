Emma Lake Artists’ Workshop with Kim Dorland is now accepting applications. July 19 - Aug 2, 
2009. 
Emerging and professional artists are invited to apply to this years Workshop with guest artist 
Canadian Painter Kim Dorland.
The Kenderdine Campus is located in Saskatchewan’s Boreal forest. Meals, accommodation and 
studio space are provided for the two weeks. Participating artists share a large studio facility with the 
guest artist– an environment that contributes to a dynamic and creative atmosphere. The sharing of 
ideas, attitudes and work processes takes place on a personal level.
Early Application Deadline has been extended to May 18
For more information http://www.emmalake.usask.ca/1artistsworkshop.html

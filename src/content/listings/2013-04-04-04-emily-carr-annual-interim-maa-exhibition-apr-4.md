EKPHRASIS - EMILY CARR ANNUAL INTERIM MAA EXHIBITION
CONCOURSE ART GALLERY
EMILY CARR UNIVERSITY OF ART AND DESIGN
1399 Johnston St, Vancouver
Phone: 604 844 3800
March 26 - April 6
Closing Reception: April 4, 6pm
ekphrasis.info

Emily Carrs first year MAA cohort presents Ekphrasis. An exhibition 
composed of new work, from design, media arts, and visual arts students 
Madeleine Campbell, Adam Cristobal, Hoda Hamouda, Amiel Logan, Ann 
Milligan, Michelle OByrne, Patryk Stasieczek, Pascale Théorêt-Groulx and 
Melanie Waddell.

Signalling the midpoint of the Master of Applied Arts graduate program, this
exhibition explores the cohesion of diverse practices and conceptual
approaches.

Please join us for the closing reception of Ekphrasis on Thursday, April 4th 
from 5 to 7pm, in the Concourse Gallery located on the Emily Carr campus

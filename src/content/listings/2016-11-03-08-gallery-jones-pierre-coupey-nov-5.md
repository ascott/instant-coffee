ARTIST TALK with PIERRE COUPEY, Saturday, Nov. 5, 2pm.

Please join us for a talk with Pierre Coupey in relation to his solo exhibition of two 
parallel bodies of work, the "Untitled" oil on canvas and linen paintings and the 
"Stanzas", a suite of work on paper. Coupey will discuss his process, his enduring 
commitment to abstraction, and the ramifications of his current work.

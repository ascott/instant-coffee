As part of The Living Room project, Centre A presents two series of curated film
programs in partnership with Vtape and the SFU David Lam Centre. The second
program, presented as part of Capture Photography Festival 2022, running from April
to May 2022, is built upon an examination of Canadas role in the Multicultural
discourses in terms of inequality, intermediacy, and extraction. Featured artists include
Skawennati, Richard Fung, John Greyson and Ali Kazimi, Jennifer Chan, Zacharias
Kunuk, Lisa Steele & Kim Tomczak.

Artists and filmmakers, as well as programmers, are also invited to submit films, video
work, and moving images to be considered for a screening during The Living Room.
We encourage artists, collectives, and other community members or organizations with
diverse backgrounds to submit works for consideration. Centre A will offer CARFAC-
standard fees for selected submissions.

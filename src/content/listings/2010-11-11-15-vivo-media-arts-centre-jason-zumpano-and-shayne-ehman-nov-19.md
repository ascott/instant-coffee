Cyrillic Typewriter
Jason Zumpano and Shayne Ehman
November 19 - December 4 2010
Opening Reception Thursday November 19, 7-11pm
Closing Reception Saturday December 4, 12-6pm Music Bazaar, 4pm Happy Hour
with Zumpano spinning vinyl
Gallery Hours Wednesday - Saturday 12-5pm

Jason Zumpano's work comprises sonically sculptural pieces, multi-layered
units of musical composition. In a departure from his previous work,
Zumpano is creating music whose purpose is neither performance nor pop -
rather, it aims to communicate a singular, impressionistic moment to a
listener.

The title Cyrillic Typewriter is an acknowledgement of the idiosyncrasies
of Zumpano's compositional relationship to the components of his work: the
main instrument, a keyboard, may be familiar, but its interpretation is
decidedly foreign. The music is unwritten, the playing technique is
manipulated through multi-tracked recording to emulate a more virtuosic
performance, and the product is a tumbling, at times cinematic, expression
of tone.

Accompanied by collaborator Shayne Ehman's animations, Zumpano's musical
sculpture juggles anarchic themes and repetitive sonic statements - a
sensorial montage for short attention spans.

Shayne Ehman, currently living in Toronto, is in the habit of sketching
from life and dream; maintaining an art practice which includes drawing,
painting, writing, printmaking, bookmaking, animation, sculpture,
filmsound, and songwriting. He loves working collaboratively and has worked
on projects with many artists including Jeffro Halladay, Jason Mclean,
Terry Peircey, Marc Bell, Tony Romano, Corin Sworn, Seth Scriver, Sandy
Plotnikoff and others. His artwork explores themes of enlightenment,
miracles, the supernatural, paranormal phenomena, extraterrestrial contact,
prayer, transcendental meditiation, visualization, sparks from the
racetrack, mystery, and experience.

Jason Zumpano is a pianist living in Vancouver, BC, Canada. Apart from
piano composition he has been involved in various musical ensembles such as
Zumpano, Destroyer, Sparrow, Loscil and Attics + Cellars.

Blanket Gallery
Laura Piasta
New Works On Paper
February 16 - March 15	2008
Opening Reception Saturday February 16, 6-9pm
Artist in attendance


Blanket Gallery is pleased to present an exhibition of new works by emerging Vancouver artist Laura Piasta. This is the artist’s first solo show at Blanket.

Born in 1981, Piasta received her BFA from Emily Carr College of Art and design in 2006. Apart from Blanket, her work has appeared at galleries and institutions including the Contemporary Art Gallery, the Video In, and the Helen Pitt Back Gallery in Vancouver; Ephemeroptera Gallery in Brooklyn, NY; 
El Levante in Rosario, Argentina; and Sign Gallery in Tokyo, Japan. In 2007, Piasta was the recipient of a Vancouver Artist Development Award.

Piasta’s new works, deals with the volatile, “layered” nature of memory. Executed in the fluid, unstable medium of watercolor, these figurative works take subjects originally held hostage by the laws of controlled posing (such as Greek sculptures, 1960’s fashion photographs and 1970’s porn) and transforms them completely. The overall effect is one where we are liberated 
from the image’s original context and are instead invited to enjoy a meditative calm that seems to slow and stop time altogether. These images, fragile and yet refreshingly presumptuous, alternately linger in the mind and disappear, not unlike the song that one loved in the past, but just can’t quite remember.

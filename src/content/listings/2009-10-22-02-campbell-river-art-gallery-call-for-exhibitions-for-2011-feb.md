Campbell River & District Public Art Gallery

Call for Exhibitions for 2011

Submission Deadline; February 6, 2010

We invite proposals for exhibitions for the Main and/or Discovery Galleries for 2011. We are 
interested in all media and look for submissions from individuals, groups and/or curators. 

Please mail or deliver submissions with:

(No e-mail or fax submissions please) 
A maximum of 20 slides or photos or images on CD (must be in jpeg format) with a written list 
of titles, dimensions and media corresponding to the images.

Your CV (exhibition history of training and experience).

Artist’s statement (tell us about your proposed exhibition)

A self-addressed envelope with return postage (to ensure your submission is returned). 
The Campbell River and District Public Art Gallery Offers:

CARFAC artist fees

Two exhibition areas, both available in this call: the Main Gallery at 1,250 square feet and the 
Discovery Gallery at 220 square feet - a floor plan is available at www.crartgallery.ca

Annual visitation of 23,000 people

Artist’s talks, workshops and classes related to the exhibitions

Excellent media coverage

An exhibition publication produced in-house

Please send your proposal to:

The Exhibition Selection Committee

c/o Exhibitions Committee Chairperson

Campbell River & District Public Art Gallery

1235 Shoppers Row, Campbell River, B.C. V9W 2C7

Phone (250) 287-2261 or curator@crartgallery.ca 
Artists who have previously had a solo exhibition at the Gallery are asked to wait five years 
before applying again. Artists who have participated in group exhibitions (excluding the 
Members’ Show) should wait three years before submitting new proposals. Please do not 
propose a body of work that has been exhibited in the past year within a 100 km radius of 
Campbell River. 
Check out our web site at www.crartgallery.ca for floor plan details, etc.

Womens bubbly and some, Cornelia Wyngaarden: Artist File

Tuesday, April 25, 2017, 7 PM
Luxe Hall at Western Front
303 East 8th Avenue, Vancouver

Join artist Cornelia Wyngaarden and curator Liz Park for an informal evening of 
conversations and bubbly to celebrate the launch of Artspeaks latest publication 
Cornelia Wyngaarden: Artist File. Park will provide a brief background on the 
compilation of various ephemera and print materials that chart Wyngaardens 
decades long practice as a pioneering media artist. Wyngaarden will provide a 
slideshow of images as a backdrop to the gathering.

Printed and bound in an edition of 300 at Benedict Press, Mnsterschwarzach, 
Germany. Edited by Kim Nguyen and Liz Park. Photographed and scanned by Erik 
Hood and Kim Nguyen. Designed by Julie Peeters and Scott Ponik. Published by 
Artspeak.

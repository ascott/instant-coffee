Jonathan Middleton

Confounded by Time

OHCE-ECHO, 1469 Venables

An exhibition in moving parts, open Saturdays in September, 2 to 6pm, starting on
Saturday, September 6, with the first part.

_-_

Jonathan Middleton (b. 1974, Edmonton) is an artist, curator, publisher, and
educator, who has contributed significantly to artist-run culture as an arts worker,
manager, and board member.

OHCE-ECHO is honoured to present Confounded by Time, Jonathan Middletons
first solo solo exhibition, to coincide with his return to Vancouver, where he will begin
his MFA at The School for Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser University this fall.

An unspecified number of Jonathans open edition of chewed pencils, Monument to
the Gallery Worker, will be available for purchase at OHCE-ECHO in September.

All welcome at the closing celebration on Saturday, September 27, evening.

Sundays April 26, May 3, May 10, 1pm

At a time when many of us are striving to build deeper connections with the communities we
live in and the lands on which we live, Open Space is launching a series of land-based
livestreams. Filmed on Instagram and archived on our website, these weekly walks and talks
take us to different sites within Lekwungen and WSNE- territories to spend time with and
learn from local Indigenous artists, educators, and knowledge keepers.

Ongoing since April 5th, we are presenting a livestream every Sunday afternoon at 1pm.
Tune in to learn stories and teachings about these lands we are on, and to join us in
thinking
about the knowledge, cultural practices, and art that come from these places.

Schedule:
April 5 - Cheryl Bryce
April 12 - Sarah Jim
April 19 - Brianna Bear
April 26 - Beangka Elliott
May 3 - PEPAKIYE Ashley Cooper
May 10 - Tyrone Elliott and PEPAKIYE

The livestreams can be accessed at

https://www.instagram.com/openspacevic/

by clicking on the Open Space logo. They will be accessible there for 24 hours following the
event, after which time they will be archived on the Open Space website,

https://www.openspace.ca/programming/on-land

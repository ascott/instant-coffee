Book Launch, Saturday April 30 2011, 2pm - 4pm

Accompanying Michael Turner's "The Rolling Stones Trilogy: An 
Inadvertent Opera In Three Acts, At Once", UNIT/PITT Projects and 
Publication Studio Vancouver are pleased to announce the 
publication of "Free Concert", a new book by Michael Turner re-
presenting words spoken from the stage at the Altamont concert. A 
critical companion volume, "Rock Lore: Acts of Persuasion and 
Perversion", Published by PS Vancouver, will be launched at the 
same event. Please join us at 15 East Pender for this launch of two 
new books, and for the final day of "The Rolling Stones Trilogy".

Designing Death
An exhibition of Contemporary Funerary Architecture and Objects

Curated by Cate Rimmer

January 25-April 21
Opening reception: Thursday, January 24, 7pm

Please join us at Libby Leshgold Gallery for the opening reception of Designing 
Death, an exhibition that looks at design related to mortality, from cemetery to 
columbarium design to urns and other forms that house human remains. This 
exhibition addresses issues such as body internment within the urban environment, 
sustainable/ecological design, and memorialisation. 

Designing Death features work by Waugh Thistleson's award-winning Bushey 
Cemetery project, Avanto Architects, Capsula Mundi, D A Studios, James Michels, 
Northwoods Casket Company, Nurn. Pechet Studio, Studio Nienke Hoogvliet and 
Waugh Thistleton Architects.

The Libby Leshgold Gallery is situated on the unceded territory of the Coast Salish 
Peoples, including the territories of the xmkwy"m (Musqueam), Skwxwu7mesh 
(Squamish), St:l and Sl"ilwta"/Selilwitulh (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.

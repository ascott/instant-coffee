Art Sundays: How the Swans Came to the Lake with John Pickering
Sunday, October 20
2-3 p.m.

Join exhibiting artist John Pickering for an artistic and spiritual journey filled with raku-fired
pottery and molten glass, mentored along the way by the Persian poet Shams i Tabrizi, French
sculptor Georges Jeanclos, and the Vietnamese monk Thich Nhat Hanh.

Free. No registration required.

Ferry Building Gallery

1414 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver

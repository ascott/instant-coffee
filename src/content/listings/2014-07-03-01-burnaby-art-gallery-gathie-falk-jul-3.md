The Burnaby Art Gallery is pleased to present a new exhibition featuring
the works by renowned Canadian

artist, Gathie Falk. Please join us on Thursday, July 3 from 7-9pm for the
opening reception of Gathie Falk: paperworks.

The gallery is also hosting an Afternoon Tea with the Artist on Saturday,
July 5, from 2-4pm. Gathie Falk will be in attendance

and will be signing copies of her catalogue: Gathie Falk: paperworks
(available for purchase). The exhibit opens to the public

on Friday, July 4 and runs until August 24.

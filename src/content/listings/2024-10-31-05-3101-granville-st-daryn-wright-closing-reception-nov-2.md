3101 Granville St, Vancouver BC

Daryn Wright (b. 1989, Vancouver, BC) is a ceramicist, painter, and installation artist
working in
Vancouver. Her work primarily involves using the archive as source material for
interdisciplinary
approaches to installation. Food, folklore, and domesticity play a major role in her
work, subjects which are often explored through the lens of play and irony. Recent
exhibitions include
Aphrodisiac Cookery: Ancient and Modern at 4 by 4 on 5th in Vancouver and a photo
essay
titled Eat Your Cake in Babbles 3: Mealtime. She didnt go to art school but wishes
she had "
instead she worked on a Masters of Publishing at
Simon Fraser University in 2015.
Using ceramics, textiles, and ready-made objects, Wright creates immersive
installations that
take their inspiration from vintage cookbooks and obscure ephemera. The Dinner
Party is an
expression of decadence and debauchery as much as its about the joys of the kind
of dinner
party that stretches late into the evening. The ceramic food appears both ripe and
spent,
enticing and repulsive, strange and familiar, inviting viewers to reminisce on their
own unhinged
dinner parties of yore.

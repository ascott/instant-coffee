In 2025, the Vancouver International Dance Festival (VIDF) celebrates 25 years of
presenting
world-class dance with no aesthetic boundaries!

From March 5-15, with OFFSITE events happening March 2-22, VIDF 2025
transforms our beautiful city into a vibrant hub for live art, where raw, kinetic, and
experimental works take centre stage.

This years festival features 25 performances by 14 artists/companies, with
over 100 artist collaborators. We're also offering BATTLES * BALLS * OFFSITE
PARTNERSHIPS* INSTALLATIONS * WORKSHOPS * LATE NITES *

Promoting exchange between local, national and international artists who challenge
norms, communicate beyond language, and reflect societal issues, VIDF 2025 will
leave audiences feeling transformed.

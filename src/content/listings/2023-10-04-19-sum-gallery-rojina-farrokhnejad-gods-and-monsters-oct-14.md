SUM gallery presents fall exhibition
Rojina Farrokhnejad: Gods and Monsters
Exhibition runs Oct 14 " Dec 1 | 2023
Gallery open hours: Tue - Sat, 12 to 6pm
Opening reception: Oct 14, 5 to 7pm

Gods and Monsters " the SUM gallery debut of Vancouver artist Rojina Farrokhnejad "
uses figurative art to explore themes of queer sensuality, religiosity, and isolation.
Employing elements of animation, painting, and sculpture, Farrokhnejad blurs the line
between the representational and abstract; the religious and the mythological; and the
grotesque and divine.

For more information, please visit sumgallery.ca/gods-and-monsters/

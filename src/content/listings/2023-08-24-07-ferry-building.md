Gallery | Hope, Grief & Beauty: Climate Change Panel | AUG 25
Hope, Grief & Beauty: Climate Change Panel Discussion

Ferry Building Gallery, West Vancouver

August 26, 2"4 p.m.

With the current air quality advisory in effect due the wildfire smoke, it feels more
pressing than ever to address climate change and what groups and individuals can do
to alleviate their carbon footprint. Denial and inactivity due to feeling overwhelmed are
two responses that are most unhelpful at this juncture.

On August 26, we will be hosting a free Panel Discussion on Climate Change titled,
Hope, Grief & Beauty. Desire Patterson, artist and environmental activist will
moderate the discussion, joined by Dr. Jason M.Brown, Ph.D. at UBC in Ecological
Humanities, author and lecturer at Simon Fraser in comparative religious studies and
interdisciplinary courses related to the ecological humanities, and Erzsi Institorisz,
multi-lingual
singer-songwriter, Co-Founder of GreenSeeds Music Society, Climate
Reality Project Leader with the Richmond and Vancouver Climate Hubs, as well as, a
Suzuki Speaks and ButterflyWay Project Ranger with the David Suzuki Foundation.

For more information on the panel and the event, please go to: Special Events | Ferry
Building Gallery

We are also proud to present the opening of Drift on August 30, 2023.

Bettina Harvey's Drift series of graphite drawings encapsulate both the natural world
and her personal experiences, acting as metaphors for her relationship with her father.
Following his dementia diagnosis, the artist's walks along Denman Island's beaches
with her father, who collected driftwood as gifts, became a poignant symbol of his
memory. In these delicate fragments, Harvey saw reflections of her father's fragile
mind, paralleling the impermanence of nature and the human
experience.

This exhibition is generously sponsored by British Pacific Properties.

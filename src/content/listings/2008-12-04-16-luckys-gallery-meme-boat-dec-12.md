Meme Boat features drawing, painting, animation, photography, zine and
sculpture works by International and Canadian artists Matthew Brown, Barry
Doupe, Julia Feyrer, Kayla Guthrie, Lief Hall, Mathieu Malouf, Jason
McLean, Shawna McLellan and James Whitman, curated by Lief Hall. Connected
through the Vancouver art scene these artists share a similar sense of
visual play and experimentation. Often inspired by music, performance and
poetry, these works reflect a dialogue between modes of expression and the
cultures they inhabit.

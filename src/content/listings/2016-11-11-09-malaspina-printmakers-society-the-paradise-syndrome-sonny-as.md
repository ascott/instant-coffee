Opening reception Nov 10, 6-9PM
Nov 11 to December 11, 2016

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to present new archival pigment prints by
Sonny Assu.

Combining Indigenous iconography with appropriated images and
text from marine chart maps, covers from; choose your own adventure' novels, 
science fiction television series;, and paintings by Emily Carr, this exhibition 
continues Assu's exploration of the intersection of Indigenous people and popular 
culture.

The opening reception will take place onThursday November 10, 2016, from6pm to 
9pm.

Sonny Assu is a Liwildaxw (We Wai Kai) of the Kwakwaka'wakw nations. His
work often focuses on Indigenous issues and rights, and how the past informs
contemporary ideas and identities. His work has been accepted into the National
Gallery of Canada, Seattle Art Museum, Vancouver Art Gallery, Museum of
Anthropology at UBC, Burke Museum at the University of Washington, Art
Gallery of Greater Victoria, Hydro Quebec, Lotto Quebec and in various other
public and private collections across Canada, the United States and the UK.

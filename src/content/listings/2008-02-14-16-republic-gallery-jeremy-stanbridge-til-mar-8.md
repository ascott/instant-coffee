Jeremy Stanbridge: Stack and Augment

February 8th to March 8th

The Stack and Augment series are paintings on shaped plastic. These works are rooted in 1950-70's abstract painting and particularly in the light space movement of the 1960's in Los Angeles, while also relating to the Kustom Kulture movement of hot rod cars of the 60's. It is the connection between artists like Craig Kaufmann and "Big Daddy" Ed Roth that holds interest
for me. Although the works have volume and are quite sculptural, I see these works as optical paintings.

Open Studio is a monthly program for young visitors and families inspired by
contemporary art practices and CAGs current exhibitions. For our March Open Studio,
we are delighted to present an in-person workshop with artist Jack Kenna. Inspired by
Alex Morrisons exhibition Nooks and Corners, Jack will lead the group in creating their
own unique paper collage while sharing tips and techniques to create 3D
illusions within
their 2D collage.

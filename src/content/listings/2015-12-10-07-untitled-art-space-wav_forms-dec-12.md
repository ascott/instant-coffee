Some info:

WAV_FORMS
Saturday December 12
Untitled Art Space
436 Columbia St.
Doors at 8:30, performance at 10pm.

WAV_FORMS is an performance based piece that combines works of
ambient, drone and soundscapes with custom projection and
animation.

Sean Evans and Chris Dammeyer are two Vancouver based
multidisciplinary artists as well as longtime friends. A year in the
making, WAV_FORMS is a collaboration between Evans' explorations
in sound design and Dammeyer's ongoing experimentation with light,
color and texture. With the gracious aid of Untitled art space they've
moved their work in to a one-off format involving both live and recorded
visual projections and improvised ambient music to create a unique
experience within an intimate space.

Our good pal Big Tiny Smalls will be soundtracking throughout the
evening.

Come join us for the premiere of an immersive audio visual experience.

$10.00

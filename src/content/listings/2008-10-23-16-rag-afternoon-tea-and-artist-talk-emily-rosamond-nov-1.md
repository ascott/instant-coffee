Richmond Art Gallery | Afternoon tea and artist talk: Emily Rosamond

Saturday, November 1
Afternoon Tea: 2:00 pm
Artist Talk 3:00pm

In her current exhibition at Richmond Art Gallery, The Patron Saint of
Waterskiers and Other Entities, Emily Rosamond examines the ability, and
the desire, to derive perceptions of character from objects and spaces.
Interested in invoking, rather than depicting character, Rosamond presents
furniture-like sculpture and text-based drawings that, together, invite
both real and imagined entities to convene.

Your invited to join us for an Afternoon Tea in the Gallery, November 1 at
2 pm, followed by an Artist Talk at 3 pm. Afternoon tea provides an
informal means to experiment with interaction in the gallery space.
Visitors can drink tea, have a light snack, sit down amidst the sculptures
for a period of time, play the Theremin, or even spot a giraffe! One or two
characters will populate the space; anyone wishing to join in by bringing a
costume is more than welcome to do so. Artist Rafael Tsuchida will be
creating a new video work from footage of the event.

Following the tea, the artist talk will offer both a contextualization of
the exhibition within the trajectory of Rosamond's practice, and a forum
for discussion of some of the ideas, issues and experiences raised in the
exhibition and tea.

Emily Rosamond is an artist, academic and educator currently working in
Vancouver, BC. She received a BFA from York University in 2002 and an MFA
from Simon Fraser University in 2007. Recently, Emily has taught
sessionally at Simon Fraser University, presented videos at the Toronto
Urban Film Festival, and delivered a paper at the New Radical
Subjectivities conference, University of Nottingham. She continues to
exhibit across Canada, including an upcoming solo exhibition at Aceart,
Inc. in Winnipeg.

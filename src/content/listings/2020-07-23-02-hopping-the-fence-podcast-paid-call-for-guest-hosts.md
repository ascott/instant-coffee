Hopping the Fence Podcast

Paid Call for Guest Hosts

Applicants from creative disciplines are encouraged to apply, including but not limited to:
poets, drag performers, visual artists, and tattoo artists. This podcast is dedicated to
emerging, deviant and DIY practices with the borders of, what is now called, Canada.
Applicants from emerging practices are preferred.
Black and Indigenous applicants will be prioritized as well as POC artists, collectives,
and, organizations. Submissions from trans, non-binary, and 2-Spirit applicants are
welcome.

An honorarium of $200 will be paid to the guest host for a commitment of one episode.
The
guest host will interview an artist of their choice. For applicants within Toronto,
access to a professional microphone can be provided, and outside of Toronto, a small
microphone can be mailed to you. Access to a computer or smartphone and solid
internet connection. Editing, production, and assistance will be provided by Rebecca.

Hopping the Fence is a podcast dedicated to the fringes of the Canadian art scene. It
is hosted and produced by Rebecca Casalino in Tkaranto (Toronto). Listen to the first
episode wherever you get your podcasts or at hoppingthefence.ca. For updates follow
the podcast on Instagram @hoppingthefence.

Please email rebeccaecasalino@gmail.com to apply and include a short introduction
and explanation of any experience you have with hosting/public speaking/creative arts.

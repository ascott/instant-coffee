ATLAS - A collection of imaginary maps:

Two years ago I unfolded a map and travelled many miles to the west of the
globe. I was
going from Spain to this country, Canada. I arrived to a land of which I
had no geographical
reference but shortly afterwards, I came to this realization:

I love the feeling of not knowing where I am. I love the sensation of being
in a random coordinate of this world. I like the earth to be round and to
have no beginning or end.

The exhibition ATLAS was born from these thoughts.

ATLAS is a compilation of imaginary maps created under a view of infinity
and randomness.
Through abstract paintings and collages, ATLAS calls for reflection on the
geographic
space and our physical positions within the planet. It brings together
unknown landscapes
that trace, erase, overlap and blur the many borders and boundaries between
mountains,
oceans and land. They serve to remind us that these undefined lines bring
us together
rather than break us apart.

About the Artist

Marta Ribas (b. 1992 Barcelona, Spain) moved to Vancouver in the fall of
2016. She has been working as a graphic designer for more than four years,
and ever since she moved to Canada her creative practice started to drift
towards contemporary art. Although her work is primarily abstract, her main
inspiration comes from tangible experiences with the west coasts ocean and
mountains.
She experiments with collage and painting by composing with organic
geometric shapes, colours and textures. Sometimes implementing photographs,
torn pieces of paper or just applying fields of colour with an experimental
stain painting technique.

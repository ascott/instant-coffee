January 30, 2025 (Thursday), 7-9pm, Morris J. Wosk Centre for Dialogue, Vancouver

Join us for an evening of conversation with the 2024-2025 Shadbolt Fellows, moderated
by SFU English professor and poet Clint Burnham, followed by a Q&A session and a
catered reception. Featuring Fiana Kawane, Justin Neal, Nadine (King) Chambers, and
Tania Willard.

The Shadbolt Fellows are renowned writers, artists and scholars, bridging together the
arts, community, and academia. The 2024-25 Fellows are publicly engaged in
championing the arts and boldly interrogate the relationship between modes of
communication
and its connection to colonialism.

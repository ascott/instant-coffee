Vancouver | Platforms 2020: Public Works

Platforms 2020: Public Works

The City of Vancouver Public Art Program is pleased to announce the September
edition of Platforms 2020: Public Works. The artworks presented this month come from
an open call process inviting Vancouver-based artists to share artworks they have made
that respond to the COVID-19 pandemic and the current circumstances that we find
ourselves in. Each piece highlights the crucial role that art plays in our community at this
critical moment.

This months program features works by Deanne Achong on digital screens throughout
the city, Bagua Artist Association and Randy Lee Cutler on transit shelters throughout
the city, Jackie Dives on a billboard at Broadway and Prince Edward street, Jag Nagra
on a billboard at Helmcken near Granville, and Nicolas Sassoon on the VanLive! screen
on the corner of Granville and Robson streets.

For more information
about this program visit vancouver.ca/platforms.

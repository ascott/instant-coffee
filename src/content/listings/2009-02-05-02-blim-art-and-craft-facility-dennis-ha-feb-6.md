New Photographs And Diorama By Dennis Ha

Opening: 8 PM, Friday February 6 | Runs February 6 - 28

Artist Presentation, 3 x 2 x1 (music performance): 2 PM, Saturday February 7
FLIM films curated by Dennis Ha: 8 PM, Saturday February 28

I feel like something is about to happen and nothing can stop it. My finger
nails grow, and I could only cut them. Last night this was part of a dream
inside another dream, framed within another dream next to a dream. Water
meets water, one shelf above another. He was always like a hungry dog and
it made me feel sick. So this way of arranging the water is a compromise
for the both of us. Red is inside orange, part magenta and close to purple.
Wouldn't it be easier if the whole world was just colored black? Yes, stop it.

Dennis Ha was born in Hong Kong and grew up in Richmond, BC. His recent
interests include sandbags, the color chart, compact discs, fake foods,
product catalogues and so on. This is the artist's first show at Blim in
which he will exhibit photographs and an installation, along with a music
performance.

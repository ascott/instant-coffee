Afternoon Projects is pleased to present the spaces debut
group show baby, featuring
the works of Joeun Kim Aatchim (NYC), Lucas Regazzi (Toronto), Mel Paget (Vancouver),
Hayley Dawn Muir (Vancouver), Arthur Marie (France) and Sarah Wong (NYC). The
exhibition offers a full display of the intense struggle, pain, confusion and celebration from
an imaginative and metaphoric birth of a new entity.

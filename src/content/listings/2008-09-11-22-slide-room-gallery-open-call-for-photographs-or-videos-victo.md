SLIDE ROOM GALLERY
Open Call for Photographs or Videos

We are looking for submissions for photographs (8 x 10?), colour or black &
white, of unusual or interesting aspects of Victoria (scenes of Victoria
that aren't usually included in the typical travel brochures) for an
exhibition to take place October 24 - November 17, 2008 in the Slide Room
Gallery (located in the Vancouver Island School of Art).
Victoria: Site UnScene
Site UnScene is an exhibition that draws attention to aspects of Victoria
that we don't usually see in the tourist brochures. 'Sites UnScene' could
encompass anything from unusual viewpoints of city streets to odd objects
found in an urban environment-the main stipulation is that the location of
the photograph be limited to Victoria and its environs.

This exhibition will include work by amateur and professional
photographers. Selected artists have also been invited to contribute work
(d bradley muir, Mike McLean and Jan Gates) based on their particular focus
on Victoria.

Please submit the following by September 30 , 2008

* CD with 6 -12 photographs (colour or black & white)
* list of titles and/or location of photograph
* your name, address and contact information

Please send your materials to (or drop them off at):
Slide Room Gallery/Vancouver Island School of Art
2549 Quadra Street
Victoria, BC V8T 1E2

You will be notified of inclusion in the exhibition by October 5, 2008.

All selected artists will receive a small stipend of $50 for their
participation.
Photographs submitted for exhibition should be unframed (will be attached
to wall with Rare Earth magnets)

This exhibition is sponsored by the CRD Arts Development.

For more information please do not hesitate to contact me.

Wendy Welch
Executive Director

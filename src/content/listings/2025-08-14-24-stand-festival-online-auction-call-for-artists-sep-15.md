STAND FESTIVAL 2025 is excited to announce a call for submissions for our upcoming
online auction, launching in October 2025. All proceeds from the auction will be shared
between STAND and the artists.

We welcome submissions from immigrant, refugee, and Indigenous artists across all
visual art mediums.

The deadline for all submissions is September 15, 2025. Please send an image of your
artwork(s) and artist statement to outreach@standfestival.com.

Vancouver's Lattimer Gallery is hiring for several part-time sales
positions. There is also the opportunity to work part-time at our new
location at Vancouver International Airport.

Lattimer Gallery has the unique, warm atmosphere of a Northwest Longhouse.
We offer a fascinating display of gold and silver jewellery, argillite
carvings, bentwood boxes, masks, and much more. In our "longhouse" we also
feature a large selection of original paintings and limited edition prints
by many well known Native artists. Please visit our website for additional
information: www.lattimergallery.com

Requirements:
Retail experience
Cash-handling experience
An interest in Northwest Coast Native Art

Assets:
Secondary languages
Post-secondary education
Flexible schedule

Responsibilities:
Informing customers about the art and artists represented, processing
sales, packaging art for transport, cash-handling, inventory processing,
and merchandising.

How to apply:
Please email a cover letter and resume to info@lattimergallery.com

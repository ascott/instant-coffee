The Bill Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art presents Royal Portrait, a solo exhibition of 
carvings, jewellery, and portraits by Morgan Asoyuf (Tsmsyen) on display from October 
1-January 19, 2020. Honouring the Indigenous matriarchs in her life, Asoyuf challenges 
the classical definition of the royal portrait with new photographs of activists wearing 
crowns, frontlets, and headdresses. Presented alongside more than 30 pieces of wood, 
silver, and gold metalwork, the portraits will be contextualized with historic and 
contemporary Tsimshian cultural treasures. Asoyuf questions colonial understandings of 
power and leadership in her artistic practice, highlighting the Indigenous meaning of 
royalty as one closely tied to stewardship of the environment and compassion for 
others.

Admission details and information at: billreidgallery.ca

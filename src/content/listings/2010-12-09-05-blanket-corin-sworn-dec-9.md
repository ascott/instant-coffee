Please join us:
Corin Sworn
December 9-January 21, 2011
Opening Reception Thursday December 9th, 2010, 6-9pm

Blanket Gallery is pleased to present a second solo exhibition with Corin
Sworn.
Sworn’s exhibition for Blanket Gallery continues her interest in the
use of artifacts as indexes to assist particular stories. Her present work
examines the reflexive viewer or reader as they make sense of their
experience through the interpretive acts that these stories encourage.

In the video work The Lens Prism an actor performs various narratives in an
empty theatre. These stories are delivered either as memory or as the
citation of research. Each describes some structure of digressions- Chris
Marker’s La Jette, a poem by Raymond Roussel or the Great Exhibition of
1851. The stories although reflecting one another remain separate and
contemplation is prolonged as a movement though rather than as a conclusion
to be reached.

A companion piece uses formal aspects of the film script to
suggest it as a structure that encompasses both sequences that are attended
to by the camera as well as other latent content that never manifests on
screen.

Sworn has recently presented solo exhibitions at Tramway and Glasgow
International 2010. Group exhibitions include: Lewis Glucksman Gallery, ICA
London, the Witte de With, EASTInternatinal, the Kunsthalle Basel, the
Contemporary Art Gallery, Bogota, Participant Inc. New York as well as The
Belkin Art Gallery in Vancouver. In 2011 Sworn will have a screening at Hors
Pistes Festival 2011 at the Centre Pompidou. She studied psychology and art
history at the University of British Columbia, obtained BFA from the Emily
Carr Institute of the Arts and an MFA from the Glasgow School of Art.

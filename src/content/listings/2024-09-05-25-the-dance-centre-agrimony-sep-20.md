The Dance Centre presents
Sophie Dow + Laura Reznek
Agrimony
Friday September 20, 2024 | 8pm and Saturday September 21, 2024 | 2pm + 8pm
Scotiabank Dance Centre, 677 Davie St, Vancouver
Tickets $37 regular/$28 students, seniors (+ $1 facility fee per ticket)

The Dance Centre presents the West Coast premiere of choreographer Sophie Dow and
composer/multi-instrumentalist Laura Reznek's striking multidisciplinary work, Agrimony.
Co-created by Dow and Reznek, Agrimony walks the edge between evocative live
concert and embodied contemporary dance, and examines our desperate search for
authenticity within a society that forces us to mask our truest selves.

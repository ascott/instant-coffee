Cultural Services' Projects: FREE Grant-writing Workshop

City of Vancouver Cultural Services staff will present two grant-writing 
workshops on October 19th and 24th, 4:00 - 7:00 pm; for non-profit 
organizations who would like to apply to our Project Grant program in advance of 
the upcoming November 18th deadline.
These workshops are free, but space is limited to 18 places per workshop. 
Register through Eventbrite.com<[http://www.eventbrite.com/event/2289185014](http://www.eventbrite.com/event/2289185014)> 
ASAP!
In addition to a review of the eligibility criteria for organizations, eligible projects 
and expenses, we will review the application form itself, explain the financial form 
and financial reporting requirements and what can be submitted as support 
material. Each workshop will also offer insights into how to present your 
organization and project details within the context of the program's goals and 
objectives, assessment criteria and evaluation process.
We recommend that you review the Project Grant program 
guidelines<[http://vancouver.ca/commsvcs/cultural/gasp/grants/documents/Cultu](http://vancouver.ca/commsvcs/cultural/gasp/grants/documents/Cultu)
re-Grants-Projects-2012-InformationGuide.pdf> [PDF] in advance of attending 
this workshop.
For further information you may also contact one of our cultural planners. 
Names and contact details are included in the guidelines.

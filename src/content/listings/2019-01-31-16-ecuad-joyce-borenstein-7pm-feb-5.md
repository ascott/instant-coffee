Screening and Artist Talk with Joyce Borenstein, Animation Filmmaker
TUES, FEB 05 | 7 - 9p
Reliance Theatre, 1st flr
Emily Carr University of Art + Design
520 East 1st Avenue, Vancouver BC V5T 0H2 

Ms. Borenstein's animation career with the NFB and her own studio, Illumination 
Animation, has brought beautiful animated documentaries to life. This event is screening 
two of those: Lida Moser, Photographe, and Colours of My Father, followed by post-
screening discussions of the work with Ms. Borenstein. Hosting the conversation and 
providing historical insight will be Prof. Norman Cornett of the University of Manitoba.

Lida Moser's subject is a New York photographer who worked for Vogue Magazine, and 
travelled throughout Quebec in 1950, documenting the Quebec people and lands along 
the St. Lawrence River. She took over one thousand exquisite photos of a society and 
place that was on the cusp of modernization. Sixty years later, Moser recounts this epic 
journey to the filmmaker Joyce Borenstein. Trailer

Ms. Borenstein is an Academy-nominated animation filmmaker, having directed and 
animated numerous films with the National Film Board as well as under the independent 
umbrella of her own production company Illumination Animation. Her topics range from 
the educational documentary to the poetic and whimsical. Visual warmth arises in these 
lovely 2D hand-crafted animations, as materials vary from paint-on-glass, back-lit clay, 
to collage and hand-drawn. Filmmakers Website

Prof. Norman Cornett (Univ. of Manitoba) is a specialist in theology and culture, 
particularly theology and the arts. His career focus has developed the dialogic method of 
inquiry, with his research and translations featured in reference literary journals such as 
Canadian Literature, Windsor Review, Rampike, Literary Review of Canada, FreeFall & 
ARC. University profile

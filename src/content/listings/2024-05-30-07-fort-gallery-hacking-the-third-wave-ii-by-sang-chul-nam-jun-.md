Artist Talk + Opening: Hacking the Third Wave II by Sang Chul Nam

Artist Talk and Tour: Saturday June 1 at 2:00pm
Opening Reception: Saturday June 1 at 3:00pm (following the talk)

Fort Gallery is pleased to exhibit Sang Chul Nams exhibition Hacking the Third
Wave II which presents kinetic sculpture, projections, and abstract paintings in an
immersive installation at the historic Fort Langley Community
Hall. Investigating our
multifaceted relationship to nature in an increasingly manufactured and technological
age, Sang Chul creates a space to sensitively grapple with the many forms of
change facing our climate and local ecologies. Contrasting artificial and organic
forms, Hacking The Third Wave II envisions possible futures, layering critical and
hopeful perspectives on what may be transformed with time.

Exhibition Runs June 1-9, 2024 at the Fort Langley Community Hall, 9167 Glover
Rd, Langley Twp

Exhibition Opening hours:
June 1-2 12-4 pm
June 3-6 by appointment [email fortgalleryarc[at]gmail.com to book]
June 7-9 12-4 pm

DOG RISING

As the celestial bodies align and launch into orbit, creating a symphony of vibrations
in a lustful, hypnotic
ritual, we invite you to join us for an extraordinary dance
performance: Dog Rising.

Date and Time: January 12, 2024, at 7:00 p.m.
Location: Roundhouse Community Centre
Presented by plastic orchid factory in partnership with Roundhouse Community Arts
and Recreation Centre
TICKETS on a sliding scale: $5-$50

In this breathtaking creation by Clara Furey/BENT HOLLOW COMPAGNIE
[Tiotia:ke], Dog Rising mirrors the life cycle and the dynamic flow of matter. From
primal impulses to gestures that are both sexual and mechanical, the performance
unfolds as a polyphony of pulsating bodies, at times dissonant and in unison.

Building on the exploration of physical phenomena initiated with Cosmic Love, Clara
Furey delves into the intricate relationship between sound vibrations and the human
body. The choreography listens closely to the presence of the body as it fully
manifests itself, paying attention to the way sound penetrates bones and how our
skeletons absorb shocks.

Through tireless
movement work, tensions are released in the endless repetition of
cyclical gestures. Dog Rising beckons you to embark on an extreme journey, a
mesmerizing, haunting, and penetrating spiral. It is more than a performance; it's an
invitation to grow, to regenerate physical strength through shocks and discomfort
sublimated into joy.

Enhancing this captivating journey is a musical score by Clara Furey's loyal
collaborator, Tomas Furey, adding another layer of depth and emotion to the
experience.

Fabricating Space: The Politics of Domesticity
UBC VISA 475 Exhibition

Opening reception: Tues, Jan 16, 6-9 PM 
Artists will be in attendance 

Exhibition: Jan 16 - Jan 17, 2018 
Hours: Tu-Sa, noon - 5 PM 

Wil Aballe Art Projects | WAAP 
688 East Hastings St. (lower level) 
VANCOUVER, BC V6A 1R1 

WAAP is pleased to present this exhibition, curated by graduating students from UBC's 
Art History and Visual Arts department, as a way to provide space for independent and 
emerging curatorial talent. 

*** 
The central purpose of our exhibition is to explore contemporary feminist art practices 
through the use of textiles and found objects, on the level of materiality. We invite 
critique on the concept of exhibition spaces through the conflation of the public gallery 
space with concepts of domesticity and home, ultimately in a negotiation of what 
constitutes public/private space. The artists included in our exhibition all engage with the 
concept of interiority, but also ones interaction with physical space. Each artist engages 
with interiority through a number of different frameworks, including the 
internet/technology, installation practices, and lived experience.

REAR WINDOW CINEMA: LETTERS FROM ISOLATION, WAVE TWO
OUTDOOR SCREENING
An Offsite VIVO Partnership with flavourcel animation collective
Friday November 20-27, 7-7:30pm PST
https://www.instagram.com/rear_windowcinema/
FREE

Wave two of Rear Window Cinema: Letters From Isolation will be running nightly from
November 20"27th between 7-7:30pm, featuring new works by flavourcel animation
collective members Alia Hijaab, Chhaya Naran, Gil Goletski and Harlo Martens.

The screenings will be located at:
Grandview-Woodland (alley west of Commercial Drive between Adanac and Georgia)
Riley Park (St. George + 22nd Ave)
Riley Park (21st Ave between Fraser and Prince Albert)

If you find yourself in those
neighbourhoods, go for an evening walk and experience
the window as a private-public urban display surface. What can a community-focused,
location-specific media exhibition look like during a pandemic? How can social
gatherings that have always been part of artists cinema adapt to the context of
physical distancing?

Between the months of October and December, animation artists will display animated
works to their neighbourhoods, using their windows as the creative point of departure
and eventual surface for projection. Rear Window Cinema is a partnership project
between Emily Carr University, VIVO Media Arts Centre, and flavourcel animation
collective with the support of the Social Sciences and Humanities Research Council.

Follow the project and exhibition updates on Instagram at:
https://www.instagram.com/rear_windowcinema/

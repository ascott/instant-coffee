TELLING STORIES: Tales, Text and Testimonies 
Is the first literary event with leading national, provincial and local talent, offered at the 
newly emerging village of Spirit Bay, Vancouver Island with story tellers: 
ANTANAS SILEIKA, PAUL BUDRA, SHEEBA SAWYER, KRIS OBRIGEWITSCH

Date: Saturday, January 26, 2019, 
Time: 11:00 AM-2:30 PM, doors open at 10:30
Location: Spirit Bay Visitors Centre
Spirit Bay is located between Metchosin and Sooke at 
4901 East Sooke Road, Vancouver Island, BC, V9Z 1N6

ANTANAS SILEIKA Author, humorist, educator, historian, Antanas Sileika reads from 
selections of his much-loved books including Buying on Time and The Barefoot Bingo 
Caller.

PAUL BUDRA How Shakespeare Became Shakespeare. Insights from selected 
readings of William Shakespeares Plays by the Simon Fraser University 
Shakespearean scholar.

SHEEBA SAWYER History, Place and Plans. Stories, information and anecdotes from a 
Scianew First Nation woman and young mother, making a place for her herself, her 
family and community. 

KRIS OBRIGEWITSCH Spirit Bay, All About, Walk About. Where we came from and 
where we are going. The Trust for Sustainable Development and Scianew First Nation 
community development.

More at: CivicStudies.ca/ALL-Spirit-Bay

About Spirit Bay: sustainable, walkable, beautiful and vibrant, Spirit Bay is fast 
becoming a new model for First Nation vitality. Spirit Bay is a contemporary village, 
surrounded by forest, on the ocean, teeming with wildlife, 40 minutes from Victoria, 50 
minutes to an international airport, 60 minutes to ferries that service Vancouver and 
Seattle. This culturally and environmentally rich community is settled on 100-year lease 
land in partnership with Scianew First Nations. It has lightening-fast fiber internet, ocean 
thermal heating, and is a dark-sky compliant community with direct ocean access.

Proposal for Live/Work Homes A.L.L. Spirit Bay proposes to build more affordable 
live/work space to respond to the displacement of urban cultural producers while also 
helping to enhance the vitality and diversity of the evolving Spirit Bay Village. Homes for 
professionals in the creative community are imagined with open floorplans for flexible 
use as is, or self-customized as required. ALL planned to make uniquely healthy 
working and living environments for individuals, families with children, start-ups and co-
sharing.

Resonance Theatre Collective is presenting LION IN THE STREETS by Judith 
Thompson, July 20-22 and the 27-29 at the Pacific Theatre. 

Lion in the Streets is written by one of Canada's most acclaimed playwrights, Judith 
Thompson, and follows Isobel, a lost and frightened nine-year-old Portuguese girl, 
who wanders around her neighborhood observing graphic glimpses into the 
desperate and unsettled lives of the strangers in her community. As Isobel observes 
the distress of those in her Toronto neighborhood, Thompson narrows in on evil and 
violence also concealed within the Vancouver community. 

Resonance Theatre is a newly formed collective of professionals from across 
Canada and The United States. Their mandate is to showcase material that can 
entertain, while also being a catalyst for change. Resonance seeks to challenge the 
audience in meaningful ways and put an end to the age of "good job" theatre.

DETAILS: Lion in the Streets by Judith Thompson 
When: July 20-22, 27-29 at 8:00pm. 2pm matinee on July 29.

Where: Pacific Theatre - 1440 W 12th Ave, Vancouver, BC

Tickets: $17 in advance at www.resonancetheatre.info or $20 at the door.

About the Exhibition
Velvet Terrorism: Pussy Riots Russia is the first survey exhibition of the infamous
feminist art collective Pussy Riot. Formed in Moscow in 2011, Pussy Riot came
together in opposition to their countrys increasingly authoritarian government. The
exhibition features vivid documentation of the groups performances and street
activism as assembled by member Maria (Masha) Alyokhina. Visitors encounter more
than fifty courageous actions, and how Russias repressive state apparatus was turned
into a non-consensual creative partner, boldly playing with fire.

* * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * * *
After a one-year hiatus, Centre A is excited to host another distinctive gala event 
in support of our 2017/2018 exhibitions and programming. Located at Coal 
Harbours Westin Bayshore Hotel, we are hosting a fantastic three-course dinner, 
our classic live and silent auction, and a featured keynote presentation by Asian 
Canadian comedy collective, Assaulted Fish.

At Centre A, we champion deepening diversity in our communities and building 
the Asia-Canada relationship. We do this with 5 to 7 exhibitions per year, various 
mentorship opportunities, international exchange, and countless public 
programmes. All of the proceeds from the gala will be put towards amplifying and 
enriching these aspects of what we do, including enhancements to our facilities, 
a debut summer workshop series for teens, and a very exciting new project to be 
further revealed at the event!

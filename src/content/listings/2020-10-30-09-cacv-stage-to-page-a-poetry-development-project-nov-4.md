Stage to Page - A Poetry Development Project

Date: Wednesday, November 4, 2020
Time: 6 - 8 pm

Stage to Page is a professional development workshop for poets, especially spoken
word artists, looking to expand into publishing. Join us on Wednesday, November 4th,
as an ensemble of selected poets have their poetry performed and workshopped by a
panel of industry professionals.

Poets and Panellists include Taran Kootenhayoo, Amanda Eagleson, Anita Movazzafi,
Soramaru Takayama, Lindi Nolte, Lillian Allen, Kai Cheng Thom, Brandon Wint, Johnny
MacRae, and Shane Sable.

This is a free event. Please register online at http://www.cacv.ca/stage-to-page/

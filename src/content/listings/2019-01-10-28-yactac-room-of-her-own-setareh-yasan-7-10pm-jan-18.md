Room of Her Own - New Works by Setareh Yasan
Opening Date: Friday January 18th 7-10pm
Location: #5 - 115 E Pender St, Vancouver BC V6A 1T6

Yactac is pleased to present "Room of Her Own", new works by Setareh Yasan at 
our new location on Pender St in Chinatown.

The new body of work by Setareh Yasan explores her ongoing interest in the 
fundamental existential quandaries that individuals face during different stages of 
their being. In Room of her Own, Yasan uses her sculptures as props to stage the 
psychological and emotional state of a fictional character who is struggling to adapt 
to her ever changing body. Room of her Own is specifically made for Yactac's new 
location in Chinatown. 

Setareh Yasan works and lives in Vancouver. Yasan directed a residency program 
for artists and writers in Ferndale, Washington; she was one of the selected artists at 
Beyond Spectacle Residency at the Simon Fraser University. Her work has been 
exhibited at Axis Mundi Gallery, Two Rivers Art Gallery, AHVA Gallery, Satellite 
Gallery and Access Gallery in British Columbia, Canada and Siks Art Center in Siks, 
Sweden.

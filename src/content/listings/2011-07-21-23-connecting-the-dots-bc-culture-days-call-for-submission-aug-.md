Call for submission| Connecting the Dots| BC Culture Days| August 21

Call for Submissions

Artists in visual, performance, installation, new media / digital arts, photography, 
video art, design,fashion and music are invited to submit their work for an 
exciting curated exhibition Connecting the Dots, presented during the Culture 
Days in Vancouver and showcased at Five Sixty. Artists are invited to submit a 
work as a reflection on their community, their city, and political, social, 
economical and environmental aspects of everyday life. What defines our 
communities and the art practice in Vancouver?

About the exhibition:

Connecting the Dots celebrates Vancouver`s rich and diverse cultural and 
artistic character. It aims to catalyze the connection among the public, 
communities, and artists with varied cultural background and forms of artistic 
expression.

About the Culture Days:

Culture Days is a collaborative pan-Canadian volunteer movement to raise the 
awareness, accessibility, participation and engagement by all Canadians in the 
arts and cultural life of their communities.

For more information visit : [http://www.culturedays.ca](http://www.culturedays.ca)

About Five Sixty:

Five Sixty is known for its involvement in Vancouver’s art and culture scene. This 
4 floor multi-media complex has over 28,000 square feet of state-of-the-art 
sound, design, lighting and visual technology, and boasts 3 levels of 
nightclubbing, plus the Blanket Contemporary Art Gallery and The Satellite 
Gallery on the top floor.

How To Apply:

Please send us:
- Your resume/CV including your contact information: name, address, telephone 
number and email.
- Artist statement (no more then 200 words).
- An image / visual sample, (a concept for performance arts) of your work that 
you will present for the show. Please include size, medium, year of each artwork 
and any specific instructions for its presentation.

Please send your submission to: connectingthe3dots@gmail.com

Artists selected for the show will be contacted during the first week of 
September.

Deadline for entries : August 21, 2011

Art Talk
Tuesday, June 30, 1:30pm, free and open to the public on the Gallery's Zoom
channel.

In conversation with Pearl C. Hsiung, Maia Ruth Lee, Astria Suparak, Stephanie
Syjuco,
Hong-An Trng and Christine Tien Wang, host stephanie mei huang invites all
to hear from six Asian women artists on reckoning with a racialized and gendered
spectrum of visibility, moving through their practices with and of yellow embodiment,
and the possibility of racial (or transgressively, non-racial) alternatives.

This talk was convened by stephanie mei huang with Pearl C. Hsiung, Maia Ruth
Lee, Astria Suparak, Stephanie Syjuco, Hong-An Trng and Christine Tien Wang as
part of Contemporary Calgarys Collider residency, which ran from May 1 to June 15,
2020.

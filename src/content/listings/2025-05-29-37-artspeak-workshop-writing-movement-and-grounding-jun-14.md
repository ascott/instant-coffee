Please join us for a fun day of workshops here at 233 Carrall St on Saturday June 14,
2025

11 am- 2 pm Writing Workshop with Mercedes Eng
A guided walking tour of the Downtown Eastside, Chinatown, Japantown, Hogan's
Alley, and Strathcona. Participants will explore meaning making through various
practices of map making as a form of resistance to gentrification. Refreshments will be
provided, please bring a journal and your curiosity about your neighborhood.

2. 3:00pm-5:00pm Movement and Grounding Workshop with Jotika Chaudhary
Samant

In conversation with our current exhibition 10,000 Flowers Bloom. How can we move
with intention and care for our communities while also holding space for ourselves?
How can rest be a deep care practice, how can we honour and make space for grief?
All bodies & voices welcome.

To join us for either or both events, please email info@artspeak.ca to RSVP. We hope
to see you there!

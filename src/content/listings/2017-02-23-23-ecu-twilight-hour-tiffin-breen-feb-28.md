Twilight Hour | Tiffin Breen
Tuesday February 28, 2017 | 5:30pm
Rm 410B, SB

Tiffin Breen is a visual artist and a writer. She received her BFA from Emily Carr 
University in 2011 and completed her MFA at the Piet Zwart Institute in Rotterdam, The 
Netherlands in 2015. Her paintings investigate ideas of influence, attraction and the 
capturing of atmosphere taken from her surroundings and inspiration. Her latest works 
are painted on large scale canvases sourced from abandoned semi - trucks in 
Rotterdam. While studying to complete her MFA Tiffin worked as an escort as means to 
support her living and education. Her latest novel titled Hi, My Name is Stephanie, I'm 
Studying in Holland, published by Brick Press in 2016, investigates ideas surrounding 
education, intimacy, communication and takes the form of a social document as well as 
narrative story.

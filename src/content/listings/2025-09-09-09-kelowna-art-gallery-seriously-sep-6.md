Seriously? Comedy and Satire in Canadian Art 1970s-Now

September 6, 2025, to January 11, 2026

Fans of visual art and comedy will have a healthy dose of both through a unique
exhibition that opens at the Kelowna Art Gallery this weekend. Seriously? Comedy
and Satire in Canadian Art 1970s"Now presents artwork that uses humour"
sometimes light, sometimes biting"as a lens to explore Canadian identity, politics,
and cultural shifts across time and geographies.

Seriously? brings together two generations of artists"from Anna Banana to Maud
Lewis to Sonny Assu"who offer powerful reflections on culture and community,
says Nataley Nagy, Executive Director at the Gallery. Visitors will be able to explore
and discover a remarkable depth of artwork that has been borrowed from private
collections across the country and brought to Kelowna to be seen.

Seriously? traces unexpected connections"from the folksy Victoria school,
Vancouvers edgy Western Front scene, Halifax conceptualism, Regina funk, to
Indigenous artists embrace of manga and comics. Senior artists such as Judy
Chartrand, Maxwell Bates, and Anna Banana offer sharp cultural critiques, while
others including Shuvinai Ashoona, Victor Cicansky, and Maud Lewis find humour
and joy in the everyday. A wide range of media will be on display for audiences"
from ceramics and beadwork to cartoons and performances"offering visitors a
chance to engage with works that explore humour and whimsy, qualities rarely
highlighted historically in the Canadian contemporary art landscape.

Seriously? Comedy and Satire in Canadian Art 1970s"Now runs from September 6,
2025, to January 11, 2026. It is organized by Griffin Art Projects and curated by
Melissa E. Feldman. It is presented with support from Acera Insurance, Trove Fine
Art, and Kelowna Paint Supply.

An Opening Reception will be held on Friday, September 5, 2025, from 6:00 to 8:00
pm at the Kelowna Art Gallery. It is free and open for the public to attend.

A Live Performance of Adrians Awetopsy with Montreal-based artist Adrian Norvid is
scheduled for Saturday, September 6, 2025, at 10:00 am.

JUNO-nominated Comedian Charlie Demers offers audiences a night of comedy on
Friday, October 3, 2025, at 7:30 pm. It is organized by and being held at the Rotary
Centre for the Arts.

Bob Rennie: Collectors Talk offers attendees an unforgettable morning with Rennie,
who has been recognized as one of the worlds top 200 art collectors. It is planned
for Sunday, October 5, 2025, at 10:00 am. Guests interested in attending are asked
to kindly R.S.V.P. to the Gallery.

The Kelowna Art Gallery is located at 1315 Water Street in the heart of Kelownas
Culture District. For more information visit kelownaartgallery.com or call 250-762-
2226.

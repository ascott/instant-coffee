The Door Project
by plastic orchid factory

Conceived by Nlaka'pamux/Secwepemc multidisciplinary artist, Chris Bose, and
gifted to plastic orchid factory, The Door Project is a new multidisciplinary work that
offers sound, light, media and dance in service of stories about transformation and
passages into new destinies. Invitations to witness, coinciding with the Summer
Solstice.

The materials used are derived from interviews with over 20 people who have
shared a story of transformation and "passage through a door to a new destiny" with
us. We're deeply grateful to have been in the presence of such vulnerable and
courageous humans as they answered five questions, and now, supported by an
incredible team of artists, we bring the work into space and time. We look forward to
welcoming you!

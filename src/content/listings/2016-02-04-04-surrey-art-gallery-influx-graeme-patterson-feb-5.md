Open Studio Night with Makers, Musicians and Performers

February 5, 8-11pm

The Surrey Art Gallery is transforming into a large open studio, bringing
together local makers, artists, musicians. On February 5 from 8-11pm, the
Gallery presents inFlux, an evening packed of DIY activity and live
performances. Visitors can be inspired by new works, dive straight into the
creative process, or just hang out and connect with Surrey's growing
creative community.

This event features a varied lineup, including performances and interactive
workshops with artist prOphecy Sun and electronic dance music DJ King
Darwin. There will also be demos by Makercube, the SFU Arduino Club, and
students from SFU School of Interactive Art and Technology. For those
wanting to work hone their writing skills, the City of Surrey's newly
minted Poet Laureate Rene Sarojini Saklikar will partnering with up and
coming musician Ava Carich to collaborate with visitors in the writer's
lounge. Elizabeth Carefoot and Makercube's Karen Cancino will offer a
chance to get hands-on with knitting, felting, and making toothbrush
robots. This night also includes making stop-motion animation videos with
Reel to Real Film Festival.

inFlux is presented as part of the Surrey Art Gallery exhibition Secret
Citadel by Graeme Patterson. This exhibition is on now and continues until
March 20, 2015.

Surrey Art Gallery gratefully acknowledges the financial assistance of the
City of Surrey, BC Arts Council and the Province of British Columbia,
Canada Council for the Arts, and the Surrey Art Gallery Association.

Surrey Art Gallery is located at 13750-88 Avenue, Surrey, BC, Canada.

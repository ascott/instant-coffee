July 29 to October 2, 2016
Opening: Thursday, July 28, 7-9pm

Please join us for the opening of The weakened eye of day an 
exhibition that presents a major body of recent work by Irish artist, 
Isabel Nolan. Taking its title from Thomas Hardy's poem The Darkling 
Thrush (1899), painting, sculpture, ceramics and textiles combine to 
examine how the light of the sun, physically and symbolically, 
manifests or resonates in our thoughts, preoccupations and pursuits.

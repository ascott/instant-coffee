More or Less is an installation by the locally based collective studioCAMP.

More or Less examines the relationship between interior architectural systems
and its inhabitant. Focusing on the surface level of interaction in which we
engage in, the viewer is encompassed by structures which challenge our typical
understanding of conventionally constructed physical environments.

The architecturally based design group; Ian Lowrie, Nigel Dembicki, and Genta
Ishimura work together in Vancouver, BC to construct human scale spatial
interventions.

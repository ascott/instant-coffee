The Crying Room Mural Projects presents a public mural by Jenny G, January
1- 31, 2013.

Vancouver based Jenny G derives her current painting from a kind of studio
frenzy only to arrive at red and green again and again. Always wrangling
with contradictions and limitations her work aims to balance the space
between representation and abstraction, between specificity and
non-preciousness. Her practice becomes an ongoing event- inseparable from
everyday life without being about everyday life.

The Crying Room Projects, 157 East Cordova Street, Vancouver BC

Curated by Colleen Heslin

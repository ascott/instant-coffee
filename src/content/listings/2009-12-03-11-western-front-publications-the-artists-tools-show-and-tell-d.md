When you have a nail, everything looks like a hammer: the Artists' Tools
Show and Tell

Friday, December 5th, 2009// 9:30 - 11:00 pm
Western Front // 303 e 8th Ave at Scotia // admission: $5

The Western Front Publications Program animates the Winter issue of Front
Magazine with When you have a nail, everything looks like a hammer: the
Artists Tools Show and Tell, a launch party in three acts.

Act I  Front friends and contributors Diyan Achjadi, Francisco-Fernando
Granados and Natalie Purschwitz will each offer an educational presentation
centered around an important tool in their art-making practice: a toy
printing press, a cigarette lighter and a selection of everyday products
that cleanse and beautify.

Act II	First-time screening of RECITAL: Contemplative and Spontaneous
Destructive Piano Performances, curated by Vancouver-based Amy Lynn
Kazymerchyk and Frederick Brummer.

Act III  Live performance on a dismantled soundboard by Frederick Brummer
and Keenan O'Connor.

And there's more: In the spirit of employing the new issue as a
multi-purpose tool, we invite you to come and sit on copies of Front
Magazine before anyone get their hands on them. For one night only,
pristine copies of Front will become benches for your comfort. How totally
conceptual!

Come down and pick up a fresh copy of Vancouver's favorite free art magazine.

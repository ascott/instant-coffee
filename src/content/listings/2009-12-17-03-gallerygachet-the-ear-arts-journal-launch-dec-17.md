Gachet Publications is pleased to announce its inaugural arts and literary
journal. The Ear is published three times a year by Gachet Publications, an
arm of Gallery Gachet. Its mandate is to provide a space for marginalized,
professional artists to publish their work. Each journal will feature
literary and visual contributions; stories, poems, essays, photography,
drawings and paintings. This issue's theme is atypical perspectives, a
celebration of work that looks at reality through diverse perceptions.
Contributors of the issue include: Bonitto, Sharon Burns, Daniella
Campirano, C. Clarke, Delazzra, Bernadine Fox, Afuwa Granger, Taralee
Guild, matthew heinz, Peter Lojewski, Laurie Marshall, Quin Martins, W. N.
(Bill) Pope, Vicki Rae, Bruce Bryon Ray and Karen Ward. Biographical for
each artist is available on our website, www.gachet.org.

All are invited to a spectacular launch party next Thursday, December 17th,
from 7pm onwards at Gachet (88 E Cordova St.), to celebrate the awesome
power of the printed page with live music, fun, djing and possibly
dancing. Including Vanessa Richards, a special mystery guest and so much more!

Gallery Gachet is a collectively-run arts exhibition, studio, and
presentation space located in Vancouver's Downtown Eastside Community.
Gallery Gachet is unique in Canada. We believe art is a means for survival,
self-expression and health and as such provide professional artists
marginalized by mental health illness, trauma, or abuse experience with
opportunities to exhibit, curate, perform, teach, publish, and develop
leadership skills. Members are supported in their goals for developing
sustainable arts practices and careers, while contributing to the
artist-run centre. Through the display of avant-garde art (outsider,
contemporary and community-engaged) and public discourse events, we aim to
eliminate stigma related to issues around mental health.

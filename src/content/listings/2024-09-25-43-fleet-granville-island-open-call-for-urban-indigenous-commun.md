Open Call for Emerging Artists from Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh
and Urban Indigenous Communities

FLEET: Granville Island Artist Residency

Open Call for Emerging Artists from Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh
and Urban Indigenous Communities

A project of Other Sights for Artists Projects, in partnership with CMHC-Granville
Island

FLEET is a studio space on wheels located on Granville Island until Spring 2025.
Produced by Other Sights for Artists Projects and a team of artists and professionals
as a collective effort to provide adaptable studio spaces for artists, FLEET seeks to
create opportunities for artists to work in a range of cultural and community contexts.

This is a call
for participation at the FLEET studio located on Granville Island.

Project Context

FLEET operates primarily on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territories of the
Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh Nations, with studio placements
throughout the territories of Musqueam and Squamish speaking peoples.
FLEET studio design and architecture was created in conversation with artists,
designers and community members from Musqueam, Squamish, and Tsleil-Waututh
First Nations and Urban Indigenous communities.

FLEET is seeking applications from emerging artists from Musqueam, Squamish,
and Tsleil-Waututh and Urban Indigenous Communities for artist
residencies at FLEET: Granville Island. This is a self-directed residency for artists
and cultural practitioners to develop their practice, create work, or engage with the
community. Residency periods are four weeks long, and will take place in winter
2024-2025.

This project is generously sponsored by CMHC-Granville Island, BC Arts
Council,
and Metro Vancouver's Regional Cultural Project Grants program.

To apply, please submit the following to fleetstudios@othersights.ca by 11:59 PM on
October 25, 2024.

1) A Project Proposal - 1 page outlining your intended use of the studio space

2) A short (50-100 word) biography describing your relevant background

3) Preferred dates and desired period of studio use, and if you are flexible on
timeline

4) Optional support material, which may include:

- Links to your website(s) to show past work or projects

- Images of past work, programs or events made into a PDF portfolio

Financial Support for Artists

In addition to working space, artists will receive a residency honorarium. For the 4-
week residency term, artists will receive $3000 ($750 per week). Artists are
encouraged to hold an artist talk or workshop during their residency, which will
include an additional fee at CARFAC rates.

If you have questions, please email fleetstudios@othersights.ca

Info Sessions

October 3, 2024 at 6 PM, online, email fleetstudios@othersights.ca to RSVP.

October 10, 2024 at 6 PM, in-person at FLEET: Granville Island (1425 Anderson St,
in the Chain & Forge Plaza).

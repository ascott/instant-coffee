The 8th year of The Earring Show fundraiser features contemporary designer-makers
producing wearable art in a whole host of mediums. For the first time, The Earring Show
welcomed submissions by international artists, resulting in a show of over 200 pairs of
earrings by over 90
artists from around the world. All funds raised from the event go
towards CCBC, its programs, and to help support contemporary craft in BC.

Opening virtual event: May 6, 2021, 7pm

On display at CCBC Gallery: May 7-15

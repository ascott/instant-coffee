Evolution

Centre for Digital Media @ Great Northern Way

August 1-10, 2014

Presented at the Centre for Digital Media in Vancouver, Canada, Evolution
is an interactive exhibit exploring the evolution of gaming. Journey back
to revisit the classic games, systems, and characters that came to define a
culture and revolutionize interactive entertainment.

At their very heart, video games are interactive experiences that exist to
be played. Thus, whenever possible, EVOLUTION tells the tale of gaming
through playable installations. Each exhibit is designed to take people
back in time, creating an opportunity to relive these experiences together
and across generations.
Through the various installations, Evolution highlights the unique
challenges facing the young video-game industry, including limited
technology and designing for an audience that had never seen a video game
before. With both an eye to preservation as well as rediscovery, Evolution
is a chance to reflect on some of the amazing developments in gameplay,
technology, graphics, interfaces, music, and even the player.

EVOLUTION brings back the arcade and living-room hits (and misses) of the
‘70s and ‘80s, while exploring some the medium’s earliest influences. The
exhibition also features hints of the future with modern-themed tributes to
the classics. EVOLUTION shows not just how it was, but offers a lens
through which we might see how it could be.
The curatorial team, Isabelle Arvers, Dr. Kimberly Voll, and Malcolm Levy
welcome you to EVOLUTION. Please, join us as we revisit the strange, new
world of yesterday, merging pixels, polygons and gameplay into a nostalgic,
inspiring, and fun-filled exhibition.

Featuring consoles, games and plenty of hands-on interactive exhibits to
enjoy, Evolution of Gaming runs from Friday, August 1st to Sunday, August
10th, 2014.

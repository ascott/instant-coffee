Marketing Coordinator (6 month contract)

Each fall, the Pacific Association of Artist Run Centres (PAARC), organizes
SWARM, a city-wide three-day festival of artist run culture. In 2009, PAARC
will be organizing two SWARM-like culture crawls in addition to the annual
fall event. These two extra events will take place in Vancouver (February)
and Ottawa (April). Both events will include diverse locations across a
city and will require working with a variety of galleries, venues and
programmers. PAARC is seeking a Marketing Consultant to develop a two-year
marketing and communications plan for all PAARC events for 2009 and 2010
(five events in total). This contract will also fund the implementation of
the first two events of the plan, in February and April. Some travel to
Ottawa will be required. This position will report to a sub-committee
consisting of two members of the PAARC board of directors.

Responsibilities:

* Identify strategic opportunities to enhance the SWARM brand and
promote artist-run-culture in British Columbia for five (5) PAARC events to
be held over a two year period
* Develop a strategic Marketing and Communications plan for above
mentioned PAARC events
* Implement the Marketing and Communications plan for the February 2009
and April 2009 events and coordinate and manage these events
* Manage the complete planning and execution of marketing materials and
other identified communication strategies in the two (2) events
* Achieve milestones and communicate deadlines with participants and
PAARC board members and committee members
* Lead material and website development process and
dissemination/implementation
* Coordinate input from national and regional partners
* Manage the collection of program information from event participants
* Manage the internal communication system (invites, maps, website, etc.)
* Conduct or coordinate market research as required
* Develop and maintain materials archive and community
databases/information
* Coordinate the planning and execution of any additional receptions,
lectures, and additional programming as related to the events
* Research and recommend new marketing opportunities for the remaining
three (3) events included in marketing plan
* Prepare a final report at the end of the contract term for the two
(2) complete events with recommendations for the remaining three (3) events
* Act as an ambassador for PAARC, artist run culture, the festival and
participating centres

Vancouver Light Expo 2014
Presented by Vancouver Art and Leisure
in collaboration with BYOB (bring your own beamer)
November 14th, 2014
8pm-3am

Vancouver Light Expo '14 opens it doors for a one night exhibition hosting
artists and their projectors. This multimedia experience showcases dozens
of video installations, participatory media projects, and light sculptures.
Journey through a magical 6000sf visual environment of spaces both real and
imaginary.

Dynamo Arts Association
30 East 6th avenue, unit 103

Opening May 4, 7-10pm

gallery hours by appointment

Alina Senchenko and Bara Hladikova collaborative project sustains a conversation 
around placelessness and longing that maps a shared relationship to the old country. 
'Behind the Curtain' highlights the artists embodied experiences through an amalgam of 
mediums including sound, photography, and writing.

While the Soviet regime fell twenty-five years ago, it is still unclear what is behind the 
Iron Curtain. Senchenko and Hladkova draw on their own diasporic narratives, as a 
first-generation Czech/Roma woman raised in Canada and a born-and-raised Ukrainian 
woman now living in Canada, to map the depths and complexities of an often 
misunderstood society.

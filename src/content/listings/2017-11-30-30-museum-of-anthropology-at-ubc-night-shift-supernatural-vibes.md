Bar + music | 7 pm
Live Show | 8 pm
Admission $10 | Free for UBC Students + Staff, MOA Members, Indigenous Peoples 

The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC invites you to hear an all-female lineup of 
Indigenous spoken word and hip-hop artists, curated by emcee Suzette Amaya of 
SAMAYA entertainment on Thursday, December 7 at 8pm.

This event celebrates Northwest Coast matriarch voices to honor womens stories, 
talents, and histories. Artists include JB The First Lady, Sister Says, Christie Lee 
Charles, DJ O Show, and Candace Curr. While youre at MOA, check out the new 
Gallery of Northwest Coast Masterworks and the inaugural exhibit, In a Different Light.

Tickets $10. For more information, please visit:
moa.ubc.ca/portfolio_page/night-shift-supernatural-vibes/

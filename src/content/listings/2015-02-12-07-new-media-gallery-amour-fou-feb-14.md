AMOUR FOU

Feb 14 - April 12
Opening Reception. February 14, 7:00 pm - 9:00 pm.
Complimentary Morsels + Cash Bar.

New Media Gallery
Anvil Centre - 777 Columbia Street, New Westminster.
Across the street from New West sky train.

Ten international works expose love in all its messy, anti-social glory...
tied up in a big, stained bow of misbehaviour. Deranged and fertile they
chart an obsessive journey to love gone wrong. Tracey Emin,Tracey Moffatt,
Pipilotti Rist, Nicolas Provost, R.Luke DuBois, Jillian McDonald, Matthias
Muller & Christoph Girardet, Brendan Van Hek, Angela Washko.

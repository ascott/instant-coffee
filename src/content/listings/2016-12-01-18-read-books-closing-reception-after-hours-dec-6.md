Please join us for the closing reception of A min. of silence by Jacob Gleeson on
December 6th at READ Books from 6:00 - 8:00 pm.

A min. of silence, 2016
HD video, 1 min loop
Jacob Gleeson

For this iteration of After Hours in the READ Books window, Vancouver based artist 
Jacob Gleeson presents an intimate portrait of a natural hot spring in an undisclosed 
Pacific Northwest location.

Jacob Gleeson is a Vancouver based artist and facilitator whose work has appeared 
in numerous galleries, festivals and publications including: SFU Gallery, Western 
Front, Belkin Satellite, Catriona Jeffries, Charles H. Scott, Helen Pitt, VIVO, Access, 
Open Space, Noorderzon Film Festival (NL), Nuit Blanche, Contemporary Magazine, 
Fillip, Pyramid Power and Vancouver Art & Economies (Artspeak).

Gachet Writers Group

at Gallery Gachet, starting April 19th, 11 to Noon.

Calling all writers and storytellers! The Gachet Writers Group is a democratically run
inclusive writers group that will provide space to share stories, receive feedback from
peers, take part in writing exercises, and hold discussions. Each one hour session
will also include time for automatic writing. The group is facilitated by Bruce Ray, who
has previously held the roles of President, Collective Member, and Board Member at
Gachet.

The first meeting is April 19. The group will meet weekly on Fridays from 11"Noon.

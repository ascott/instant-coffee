husks | 10AM | SEP 23
Gwenyth Chao pop-up residency: reimagining husks

Location: Agassiz Fall Fair on the Agricultural Grounds

Time: Saturday, September 23rd from 10 am-4 pm

Description: Join transdisciplinary artist Gwenyth Chao and be part of her exhibition
fieldnotes ... after capitalist ruin (at the Ranger Station Art Gallery Sept. 2-Oct. 1).
Using corn husks gathered from the Agassiz Fall Fair, the artist will demo how she
made paper for the drawings in her exhibition. Come doodle with Gwenyth and
speculate on the different bodies that may symbiotically emerge in future ecosystems
after capitalist ruin. Bring your imagination, but no experience necessary.

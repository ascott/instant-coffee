Panel Discussion: Stanley Park Environmental Art Project 1pm - 2:30pm, Saturday, Sep 26 
Roundhouse Community Art and Recreation Centre, 181 Roundhouse Mews (corner of Davie and 
Pacific)

Artists from the Stanley Park Environmental Art Project come together to discuss the works recently 
installed in Stanley Park and the inspiration for their environmental art practices: Peter von 
Tiesenhausen, Shirley Wiebe, Tania Willard, John Hemsworth, Davide Pan and Cease Wyss. Q & A 
to follow presentation. For more information on the Stanley Park Environmental Art Project, please 
visit: 
http://vancouver.ca/spea

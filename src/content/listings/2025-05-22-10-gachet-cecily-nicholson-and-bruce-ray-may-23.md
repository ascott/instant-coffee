Cecily Nicholson and Bruce Ray
Writing Workshop and Zine Launch Hosted by the Gachet Writers Group

Friday May 23. Writing workshop at 11am. Zine launch at 12:30pm
Location: Carnegie Theatre in the Community Centre Theatre

The Gachet Writers Group cordially invites you to attend a writing workshop and
open mic session, followed by the launch of a zine, the Auricle, published by Gallery
Gachet with support from WePress. The event will commence with a series of
stream-of consciousness writing exercises led by facilitators Cecily Nicholson and
Bruce Ray, followed by an open mic where participants can read from their written
work. The event will end with Gachet Writers Group members reading from their
contributions to The Auricle.

Gachet Writers Group: Created in April 2024, led by Bruce Ray, the group brings
people together who are concerned with the practice of writing. We are a collective
who gather to participate in a conversation about the process of creativity"in the
spirit of mutual respect and equality. We are a democratically run inclusive group that
provides space to share stories, receive feedback from peers, take part in writing
exercises, and hold discussions. Each session includes time for stream of
consciousness writing. Each member may contribute their views on the subject of
literature and art -and we take great enjoyment from mutual perspectives. Each one
of us has our own style and personal language. We encourage self-direction as well
as a mingling of attitudes -without any fear or shame. We speak to mental illness
and the problem of stigma. Our group is a safety zone where all are welcome to
come and take part in the open-minded atmosphere. How we exchange our thoughts
and all of us grow in response to the energy of this space. Let us celebrate the
differences as well as the community. The group meets on Fridays from 10:30-Noon
at Gallery Gachet, and all are welcome to join. (The May 23rd event will instead be
taking place at the Carnegie Centre.)

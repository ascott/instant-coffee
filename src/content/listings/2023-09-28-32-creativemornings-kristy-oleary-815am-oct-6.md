CreativeMornings/Vancouver is a FREE monthly breakfast speaker lecture series by
and for the creative community. Everyone is welcome!

The global theme for October is endurance and we are thrilled to present Kristy
OLeary, a creative consultant, multidisciplinary systems thinker, and a B Corp
Ambassador who helps businesses embed social, environmental, and economic
justice in their products and services so that every time they turn a dollar, something is
left better than they found it.

The event will kick off with free breakfast and live music by Samuel Romero, a
Salvadoran born songwriter/guitarist, visual artist and Expressive Arts Therapist living
in Vancouver. Drawing inspiration from folk traditions from English to Latin-American,
Samuel blends his passion for the guitar
reflecting on the soul as the pillar of his
music.

WESTERN FRONT
Broadcasts from Here
Lex Brown and Geo Wyeth
Jan 22 - Apr 16, 2022

Lex Brown, still from Communication (2021). Courtesy of the artist and Deli Gallery.
[Lex Brown wearing a white top and cropped red wig stands behind a dark blue cut-out
shape resembling a bush and holds a phone to her ear.]

Western Front is pleased to present an exhibition of work by multidisciplinary artists
Lex Brown and Geo Wyeth, who each respectively engage with broadcast mediums in
their practices. With so many of us having now taken up the task, broadcasting is less
a technical question of sending transmissions than a way of being in the world: a
broadcaster subjectivity, which is always in one space while performing for another.
What forms, poetics, and idioms are available to us that
think through such a multiply-
emplaced subject?

Location: Gallery, Western Front
Admission: Free

Visit westernfront.ca for more information

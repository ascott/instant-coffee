Please join us for our book launch, last weekend of our current exhibition
Triumphant Carrot: The Persistence of Still Life and our not to be missed
book sale.

STILL LIFE

Saturday August 21, 2010, 4 to 6pm

In conjunction with the exhibition Triumphant Carrot: The Persistence of
Still Life, with essays by Evan Lee, Jenifer Papararo and Trevor Mahovsky

Jenifer Papararo looks at the continued relevance of this genre and its
relation to the development of the avant-garde. Also included are texts by
two of the artists in the exhibition. Trevor Mahovsky draws parallels
between recent theoretical interests in the everyday and the value of still
life; and Evan Lee uses the dollar store to discuss the complex relations
between the consumption of objects and what a still life represents.

Still Life is the second publication in a series of three that use the
petit genres of landscape, portrait and still life to reflect the CAG’s
recent programming and to frame current tendencies in contemporary art.

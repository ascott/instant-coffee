I'm doing everything I've always wanted, all the time, everyday.
New works by Jason Gowans

Wednesday November 2 – 13th, Opening reception November 4, 7-9pm.

Little Mountain Gallery presents "I'm doing everything I've always
wanted, all the time, everyday", a solo exhibition of new work by
Jason Gowans.
This show will mark Gowans' first solo exhibition in Vancouver since
moving back to the West Coast. Please join us for the opening on
Friday, November 4 from 7:00 - 9:00 pm.
For this exhibition, Gowans presents a large scale photographic
installation that reflects the artist's interest in idealized imagery
within landscape.
This exhibition is in two parts: The individual within a distinctly
West Coast landscape, and also location as a fabricated idea. Gowans
explores the constructs of his
own romanticism and personal nostalgia by presenting utopic
iconography as a package.

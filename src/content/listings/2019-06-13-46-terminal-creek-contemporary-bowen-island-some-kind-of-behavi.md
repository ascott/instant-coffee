Some Kind of Behaviour
June 22 - July 14
Terminal Creek Contemporary 569 Artisan Lane Bowen Island, BC
www.tccontemporary.com

Opening: Saturday, June 22nd, 12-4 p.m.

Exhibition Hours: 12-4 p.m. Friday, Saturday, Sunday from June 22 to July 14

Some Kind of Behaviour, curated by Paul Jackson, brings together three artists who share 
an overlapping sensibility in their observations of the bodily endeavour. The individual studio 
practices of Robin Arseneault, Allison Hrabluik, and Lyse Lemieux offer an amorphous 
narrative through the collection and manipulation of diverse materials, methods and formal 
considerations. The resulting works hint at a base human experience through ideas of 
failure, fragility, humour and tragedy. Together, their varied works compel us to view the 
world we inhabit through a lens of the theatrical and absurd.

Robin Arseneault is a graduate of the Alberta University of the Arts (formally, Alberta College 
of Art & Design (BFA, 1998)) and the Edinburgh College of Art (MFA, 2005).
A semi-finalist for the Sobey Art Award in 2007, Arseneault received the Lieutenant 
Governor of Alberta Emerging Artist Award in 2008 and has been awarded grants from the 
Canada Council for the Arts and the Alberta Foundation for the Arts. Arseneaults practice 
includes sculpture, drawing, photography and collage. She has exhibited within Canada, the 
USA, Scotland, Germany, Italy and The Netherlands. In 2011, she collaborated with Paul 
Jackson to complete the public sculpture commission, Hunting Blind for the Alberta 
Foundation for the Arts, that was on view at the Art Gallery of Alberta.

In her exploration of narrative construction, Allison Hrabluiks visual practice finds its focus in 
the voices, rhythms and methodologies of storytelling. Her work includes video, animation, 
drawing and installation, often to humorous or absurdist ends. Hrabluik lives and works in 
Vancouver. Her recent short films include War Song & 7 Things About Guinevere, and 
Lover/Hater both music videos for Veda Hille directed by Hrabluik and released online and 
at The Cultch, Vancouver. 

For over 35 years Lemieuxs Vancouver-based practice has focused primarily on drawing 
and installation. Through considerations of process and materiality her work explores the 
space between abstraction and representation, while consistently maintaining an interest in 
the human figure. Originally from Ottawa Lyse Lemieux attended the University of Ottawa
from 1974 to 1976 and graduated with a BFA from the University of British-Columbia in 
1978. She has exhibited nationally and internationally including the Canadian Cultural 
Centre; Rome, the Vancouver Art Gallery; Vancouver, Oakville Galleries; Toronto, Richmond

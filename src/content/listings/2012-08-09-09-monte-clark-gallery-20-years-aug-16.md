MONTE CLARK GALLERY
20 YEARS

Opening reception: August 16th, 6pm

In 1992, Monte Clark opened his first gallery in Gastown, Vancouver
BC. In celebration of 20 years in business this fall, the Monte Clark
Gallery will present a exhibition of specially selected works from
gallery artists. Alongside artists who have been working with Monte
for years, the exhibition will introduce three new artists to the
Monte Clark Gallery roster: Jay Issac (Toronto), Mari Eastman (Los
Angeles) and Paul Housley (London UK).

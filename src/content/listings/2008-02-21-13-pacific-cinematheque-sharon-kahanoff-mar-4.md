The Here To Gathered | Sharon Kahanoff
16mm, 20min
2008

March 4, 2008
Pacific Cinematheque
1131 Howe St. Vancouver, BC
Opening Reception and Screening 7:30 - 10:30

A reception hall, a park, an art gallery, a living room, a street, a beach and a gymnasium– The Here To Gathered plays inside these theatres of the social, performing along with the actors who move through them.  Gestural, visceral, corny and critical, meditative, artificial and often realistic, the film threatens to erupt at the seams from its own difference.  But The Here To Gathered is held in check by a rigid structuralism that, like any law excessively applied, vents itself into the absurd.  Lurking on the borders between documentary, narrative and experimental film, The Here To Gathered is a portrait, in both major and minor keys, of nothing more than passage.


Sharon Kahanoff is a filmmaker, artist and writer. Her artwork has been exhibited in galleries and cinemas locally and in the US.  Interested in critical writing, Sharon publishes in exhibition catalogues and art journals and gives occasional lectures.  This exhibition is in partial fulfillment for the degree of Master of Fine Arts at Simon Fraser University.  http://www.sfu.ca/sca

Art and science collide in QUANTUM, an exciting new work by Swiss
choreographer Gilles Jobin inspired by his residency at the largest
particle physics laboratory in the world - CERN in Geneva. Six outstanding
dancers power through intricate, densely textured choreography to explore
the subtle balances of the forces that shape our world: matter, gravity,
time and space. The work is illuminated by acclaimed German artist Julius
von Bismarck's kinetic installation of suspended industrial lamps which are
activated by the movement of the dancers, and American composer Carla
Scaletti's electronic score incorporates data from the Large Hadron
Collider. QUANTUM epitomizes the adventurous, searching spirit of both
artistic and scientific inquiry.

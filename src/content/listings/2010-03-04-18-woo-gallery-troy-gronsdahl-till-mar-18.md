WOO Gallery - Troy Gronsdahl: The Generosity of Mechanics
___________________________________________________________

WOO Gallery
Emily Carr University
March 3 - March 18.

The Generosity of Mechanics features video and audio works produced by Troy
Gronsdahl between 2002 and 2008. Incorporating trace elements of comedy and
solemnity, these=works expand upon western conventions of memorialization,
and make available the complex subject-object scenarios that underpin the
logging of memories as well as the construction and recounting of personal
and cultural narratives.

__________________________________________________________

Troy Gronsdahl is an interdisciplinary artist and cultural worker based in
Saskatoon Saskatchewan. He produces independent music under the moniker
soso, and is the owner of Clothes Horse Records.

the generosity of mechanics is curated by Mitch Speed.

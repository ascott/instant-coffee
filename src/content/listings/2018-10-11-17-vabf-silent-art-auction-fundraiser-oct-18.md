// WHY SUPPORT VABF?

Operating since 2012, the Vancouver Art Book Fair is an annual
international event that is the largest and longest-running art book fair
in Canada. It has brought hundreds of artists from around the globe to
Vancouver to share their work and has supported increased visibility,
production and community among local art book publishers.

By purchasing art at the silent auction, becoming a member, or making a
donation you help support VABF, a non-profit society, as a critical piece
of Canadian cultural infrastructure that benefits artists locally and
nationally.

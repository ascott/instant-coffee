The District of West Vancouver invites artists residing on the North Shore to submit 
applications to be considered for a series of art wraps on utility boxes located in and around 
West Vancouver. Artists may be selected for one or more utility boxes.

Artist Fee: $300 CAD per image
(Reproduction and installation costs will be paid by the District of West Vancouver)

Eligibility: Artists residing on the North Shore

Deadline: Thursday, August 30, 2018 by 4:00 p.m.

Installation: Fall 2018-2019

BACKGROUND

The District of West Vancouver wishes to wrap several District-owned utility boxes to add to 
the utility boxes that have already received this treatment previously. The artists will be 
expected to work with District staff and the Districts printing contractor to wrap the boxes 
with artwork. The artwork will be digitally reproduced on special film and then adhered to 
utility boxes throughout the District. It is expected that the art wrap will last for approximately 
three or more years.

THEMES

The District is looking for artists who can produce artwork that is mindful of the diverse 
historical, geographical and cultural heritage of West Vancouver. Heritage considerations 
can be particularly relevant for art wrap opportunities.

LOCATIONS

Priority will be given to utility boxes located in highly visible pedestrian areas in West 
Vancouver.

DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS

The utility boxes vary in dimensions and shape, ranging from approximately 
45x75x150cm to 60x250x130cm.

Commissioned artists will be required to provide the District with artworks in a 
digital format for printing and reproduction. The artwork can be a reproduction of an original 
painting, sculpture or installation. Equally acceptable would be an original photographic 
image or new media artwork.

Commissioned artists shall not promote goods and services of any businesses 
and shall not violate any federal, provincial or local laws. Additionally, the artwork shall not 
convey partisan politics, negative imagery, religion and sexual content.

Commissioned artists should keep in mind that busier designs are more 
successful in deterring graffiti and vandalism, while open spaces in the design are often 
targets for tagging by graffiti artists.

ARTIST ROSTER SELECTION PROCESS

Qualified artists will be evaluated based on availability, appropriateness of the artists media 
and experience for specific project requirements. Selected artists will enter into a contract 
with the District of West Vancouver.

ARTIST ROSTER SELECTION CRITERIA

Qualifications and proven capability to produce work of the highest quality.

Capacity and experience to work with communities, diverse groups and other 
design professionals.

Capacity and experience to produce work that will reflect community identity and 
West Vancouvers unique diversity, history and environment.

Demonstrated capacity and experience in producing sustainable and technically 
feasible work.

Demonstrated capacity and experience to complete work within established project 
schedules and timelines.

SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS

(E-mail all documentation as one (1) pdf document, not to exceed a file size of 5MB to: 
arts@westvancouver.ca)

CHECKLIST:

o STATEMENT OF INTEREST - 300 words or less, explaining artistic practice, why the 
artist is interested in this opportunity and how their practice relates to this project and the 
selection criteria.

o CV - One page (maximum) current professional CV. Teams should include one-page 
CVs for all members as one document.

o REFERENCES - Applicants must provide the names, titles, contact telephone and e-mail

o WORK SAMPLES - Five (5) image examples. One image per page for a total of five (5) 
pages.

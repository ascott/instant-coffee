Random

 Opening Reception:

2-4 p.m.

Artist in attendance.

The Atelier Gallery is pleased to announce a solo exhibition for Vancouver artist, Nancy Boyd.
 
In Random, Boyd explores her interest in views unseen by the naked eye.  Inspired by images from MRI’s and microscopes, Boyd dissects this previously ordered evidence, and rearranges the pieces into random patterns.  This act results in an “apparent casual relationship”, with “hints of the kind of order, as well as surprise, which permeates the natural world.”

Nancy has shown extensively around Greater Vancouver in the public galleries of Richmond, Surrey, Burnaby and Coquitlam. In addition, her work has been exhibited in the U.S., Japan and Australia.

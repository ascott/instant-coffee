FADE to WHITE

Opening reception, Thursday Feb 23, 7-10PM

Unity 434 Columbia Street

Unity is pleased to present FADE to WHITE: Stephen Walters (Los Angeles/Joshua 
Tree), Sean Alward (Vancouver), and Scott Bowering (Vancouver). Utilizing a mixture 
of natural and chemical processes relating to sunlight, FADE to WHITE draws from 
the intersection of physics, social history, and geographies specific to the Southwest 
desert and Pacific coastal environments. 

Stephen Walters (Los Angeles and Joshua Tree, CA): Solar Degradation Paintings 
(Dye on Canvas) 48x48" each/ Samuelson's Rock 20 x 22 "each (Screenprint on 
Paper) 

Sean Alward (Vancouver BC): Untitled (Chlorophyll Print) 

Scott Bowering (Vancouver BC): Idyll (Bleachprint on inkjet on paper) 38x50" each.

For Captures 2021 Festival, Mikmaq visual artist Jordan Bennett was commissioned to
create a site-specific work titled altaqiaq: it spirals for the Dal Grauer Substation on Burrard
Street in Vancouver. For altaqiaq: it spirals Bennett took inspiration from a photograph of a
Mikmaq porcupine quill basket created by ancestor Mikmaw artists, an artifact currently
held in the collection of the Museum of Vancouver. The large-scale photograph features a
moose skull brightly painted with patterns from the ancestral designs found on the
porcupine basket, photographed on Mikmaki (the traditional land of the Mikmaq). Through
photography, Bennett reconnects the spirit of the displaced basket back to its origin and
home territory. In this talk, Bennett will situate this work within his practice which utilizes
painting, sculpture, textiles, video, installation, public art and sound to explore land,
language, the act of
visiting, familial histories and challenging colonial perceptions of
Indigenous histories and presence with a focus on exploring Mikmaq and Beothuk visual
culture of Ktaqamkuk.

Presented by Capture Photography Festival in partnership with the Audain Faculty of Art at

Deadline to submit: APRIL 24, 2017

Artist and Curator Aurora Pagano and art students of Cardinal Carter Academy 
for the Arts in Toronto have created a national collaborative art project to help 
build connections with Indigenous communities and all Canadians across the 
country, called ON THIS ROCKwe call home
City TV News recently promoted our project. 
What is #OnThisRock? - CityNews

The art exhibit and installation will be housed in the Catholic Education School 
Board and will feature a center piece consisting of a First Nations Medicine 
Wheel and a 6ft Stone house sculpture. The rocks used to create the house 
sculpture, will be those sent to us from Canadians across the country. We' ALL 
LIVE ON THIS ROCK' we call home.
It will be symbolic in representing a unified nation, built on compassion and 
healing for all Canadians.

How To Participate Options:
1.UPload a video of yourself holding up home rock/pebbles "I am _____ from 
_____ Canada, and I live on this rock". Hashtag it #onthisrock and upload to 
your social media. City TV will collect videos for the final exhibit.

2.Send us some pebbles from your community with a note or photo indicating 
where in Canada they come from
Carve it or decorate it. IT will be featured in the collaborative monument and 
your name will be added to the artists' roster for publicity.
address: CCAA 36 Greenfield AVE, Toronto Ontario M2N3C8. att: A.Pagano

3.Send a story, photo or small work of art to be included in the exhibit 
contact aurorapagano@rogers.com for details

Centre A is excited to kick off 2025 with the announcement of our upcoming exhibition
TechniCowlour! Created by elika mojtabaei + Aryo Khakpour and in collaboration with
Alanna Ho, ellis cheadle, Jaewoo Kang and SF Ho, this exhibition is an installation and
performance exploring the intersections of mythology, memory, and sensory
experience. Inspired by a process that began as a response to the Iranian film The Cow
(1969),
TechniCowlour transforms nostalgia into a portal to presence and asks: What
does it
mean to live between worlds that carry us across times?

Featuring a 20-min performance performed by Aryo at 6:30pm on Jan 18 (opening), Feb
12, Mar 1, and Mar 15 (closing)
EXHIBITION PERIOD: January 18 - March 15, 2025
GALLERY HOURS: Wed - Sat, 12 - 6 PM

Opening night performance is free admission. The other three performances are pay-
what-you-choose tickets at $10/$20/$30.

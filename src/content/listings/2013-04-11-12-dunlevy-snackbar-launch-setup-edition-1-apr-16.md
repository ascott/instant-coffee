Setup Edition 1:

Yunhee Min, Mark Neufeld, Silke Otto-Knapp, Judy Radul and Sean 
Weisgerber

433 Dunlevy Ave

Dunlevy Snackbar is pleased to present a collection of new prints that 
have been published by the Vancouver based publishing project, 
Setup. The collection features five archival inkjet prints, included are 
works from Yunhee Min, Mark Neufeld, Silke Otto-Knapp, Judy Radul 
and Sean Weisgerber, all whom have made contributions to Setup in 
some form. Each work has been specially commissioned. The 
collection has been released as a limited edition archival box set in an 
edition of thirty. In purchasing a set buyers will be offering crucial 
support to an independent art publication, and the community of artists 
and writers of which it is a part.

About Setup:

Setup publishes texts and artist projects. The artworks, essays, stories, 
poems and interviews herein reflect the diversity of forms through 
which art is currently produced and considered. We feature a spectrum 
of contributors, from students to established artists and writers, all the 
while prioritizing a combination of creativity and rigorous inquiry. Setup 
magazine began as a project for The Practice of Artists’ Publishing, a 
course taught by Kathy Slade at Emily Carr University in the fall of 
2010. Published by Publication Studio Vancouver, it is independently 
funded and released on an unfixed schedule. We gratefully 
acknowledge the generosity of our contributors and readers.

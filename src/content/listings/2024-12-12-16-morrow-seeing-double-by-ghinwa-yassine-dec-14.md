SEEING DOUBLE

A storytelling/reading performance/auditory experience by anti-disciplinary artist
Ghinwa Yassine.

Date: Saturday, December 14th

Time: 7:00 pm " 8:40 pm PT

Location:

MORROW

910 Richards St, SUITE 204, Vancouver

Ghinwa Yassines practice is a somatic engagement with story through space. She
creates multi-media performances and installations for her feminist speculative
writings. Incorporating humor and absurdity, she embodies characters or alter-egos
that constitute what she calls feminist freedom futurity, a liminal space between
embodying a militant activist and a Barbie girl"in her case, a belly dancer.

Seeing Double, is an apocalyptic reading/storytelling performance/auditory
experience that blends fact and fiction, and is heartwarming, heartbreaking, and
chaotic. As you sit back in your floor chairs, the artist will take you on a journey
between Lebanon, Vancouver, and outer space, where
your senses get titillated, and
your imagination awakened.

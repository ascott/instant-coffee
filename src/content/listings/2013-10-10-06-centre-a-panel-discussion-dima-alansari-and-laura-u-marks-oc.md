CENTRE A | Minutes from a Second Story Panel Discussion: Dima 
Alansari and Laura U. Marks in conversation with Haema Sivanesan | 
OCT 12

Saturday, October 12, 2013
3pm
at Centre A, 229 East Georgia Street

Hajra Waheed’s art practice responds to strictly imposed restrictions on 
photography during her years growing up in Saudi Arabia. This panel 
discussion examines histories of aniconism in the Arab-Islamic world, 
and its tense relation to visual cultures and photo-practices that are 
ubiquitous in the West. This discussion will consider the role of 
photography and film/video with relation to the rise of modernity in the 
Gulf region, with implications for questions of history, memory and the 
imagination.

Dima Alansari is a film-maker, producer, and community activist based 
in Vancouver.

Laura U. Marks is Dena Wosk University Professor at the School for 
the Contemporary Arts at Simon Fraser University.

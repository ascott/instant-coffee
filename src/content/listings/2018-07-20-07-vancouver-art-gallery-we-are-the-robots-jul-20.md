The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Amateur Quests " Artist Talk for Kevin
Schmidt: We Are the Robots

Date: Friday, July 20 at 7:00pm

Interested in the aesthetics of knowledge production by DIY communities,
Kevin Schmidt replicates the processes and modes of address to manifest
utopian ideas. For this conversational and participatory tour, join the
artist as he invites visitors to be implicated in the processes of
production, listening and learning with the interactive and instructional
components of the works on display.

Free for members or with Gallery Admission

Antimatter Enters Its Second Decade

Antimatter Film Festival
September 19-27, 2008 | Victoria, BC, Canada | 
www.antimatter.ws

The Antimatter Film Festival is set to unleash nine stellar 
nights of films, gallery installations, panel discussions, 
performances and workshops on Victoria from September 19 to 
27, 2008.

Focusing on the international avant garde vs the commercial 
film industry, Antimatter is dedicated to the exhibition and 
nurturing of film and video as art. Since its inception in 1998, 
the quality and creativity of its programming, commitment to 
audience development, international touring programs and 
respect for filmmakers and their work have made Antimatter 
one of the most important media arts events in Canada and an 
irresistible destination for media artists from across the globe.

The 11th annual festival features 160+ films from 25 countries 
in 24 separate programs of work to be screened at Open Space, 
The Ministry of Casual Living and Cinecenta. As in past 
festivals, the Foreign Matter component screens a range of film 
and video from around the world, this year offering specially 
curated programs from the Netherlands (Just because I'm 
standing here doesn't mean I want to), Mexico (100% 
Mexicano) and Quebec (Images in Back of the Head).

Deluge Contemporary Art is the venue for Toronto artist and 
filmmaker Christina Battle's multimedia installation Uncharted 
Histories: Pirates, on view through October 18th. Murmur, a 
single channel installation by Peter Byrne, Carole Woodcock 
and Czech composer Michaela Eremiasova can be seen in the 
Deluge stairwell throughout the festival.

Antimatter will also feature a screening and performance by 
San Francisco's Kerry Laitala from her Muse of Cinema series. 
Live cinema artists Potter-Belmar Labs of San Antonio, Texas 
will present Fortune, bringing the ancient tradition of the magic 
lantern show to the 21st century by inviting the audience to 
participate in a collective fortune-telling experience.

Artists talks, panel discussions and hands-on workshops are 
offered throughout the festival.

Full details can be found in the Antimatter Film Festival guide, 
available at any South Island location of Serious Coffee, Open 
Space, Deluge, Ministry of Casual Living, Cinecenta and CFUV 
in the UVic SUB, or online at www.antimatter.ws

Venues: Open Space Arts Centre, 510 Fort St; Cinecenta, UVic 
SUB; Deluge Contemporary Art, 636 Yates; Ministry of Casual 
Living, 1442 Haultain St; CineVic Society of Independent 
Filmmakers, 2022 Douglas St.

Please Note: If job action at the UVic SUB continues into the

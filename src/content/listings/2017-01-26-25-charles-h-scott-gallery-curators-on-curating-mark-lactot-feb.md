Monday, Feb 6, 11:30am
Charles H. Scott Gallery

Please join us in the Charles H. Scott Gallery for a lunchtime talk by Mark Lanctt, 
curator at the Muse d'art contemporain de Montral (MACM). As part of the Curators 
on Curating series, Lanctt will discuss an exhibition that has influenced him alongside 
an exhibition he has curated.

Born in Montral, Lanctt completed his Master's in art history at the Universit de 
Montral in 2002. He has published articles in Canadian Art and Esse: Art + Opinions, 
among other magazines. Before joining the MACM in October 2006 as a curator, he 
worked as an independent curator and was director of the Contemporary Art Galleries 
Association in Montral. At the MACM, he coordinated the exhibitions Guy Ben-Ner 
(2007), Arnaud Maggs: Nomenclature (2008) and Beat Nation: Art, Hip Hop and 
Aboriginal Culture (October 2013). He curated solo exhibitions of Yannick Pouliot 
(2008), Tacita Dean (2009), Marcel Dzama (2010), Runa Islam (2010), Daniel Young & 
Christian Giroux (2011), Pierre Dorion (2012), Michel de Broin (2013), Ryan Gander 
(2016) and Olafur Eliasson (upcoming 2017). He also co-curated the first two editions of 
The Qubec Triennial (2008 and 2011) and the Claude Tousignant retrospective (2009).

Waldorf Hotel

Multi-Artist Event

Help kick off the 15th annual Eastside Culture Crawl by attending Echo 
Chamber at the Waldorf Hotel.

Saturday, November 5, 7PM-2AM

Echo Chamber is a multi-room event featuring many of the artists, 
musicians, writers and dancers that East Van is so famous for. Everything 
from fusion bellydance, to live music, to poetry will be on display. Artists 
include: Mezamazing, Naomi Steinberg with Michael Alleyne, Blackberry 
Wood, Haisla Collins with Nasty, Brutish and Short,  Luciterra 
Bellydance, 
Chris Murdoch, Mind of a Snail, DJ Michael Fraser with his violin, 
Carnival Band, Four Maladies, Synthcake, Abbla Banji, Daniel Zomparelli, 
Elizabeth Bachinsky, and Jenn Farrell.

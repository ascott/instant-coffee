September MOA Unmasked tours are now on sale! MOA is happy to announce that
due to popular demand we have added an extra date to our monthly behind-the-
scenes tours. We will now be offering tours three times a month. Take note of the
new tour days and times
below.

September's tour, MOA Unmasked: Beading + Textiles in Motion, will feature MOA
Curator Karen Duffek and MOA Library + Archives Assistant Emily Teh, who will lead
visitors on a closer look at Indigenous beadwork and robes in the collection to
explore how object lives and stories extend beyond the walls of the museum.

Due to the nature of these tours during MOA's temporary closure for Great Hall
seismic upgrades, capacity is very limited. Tickets will not be available at the door,
and latecomers cannot be accommodated. Tours fill up quickly " book your spot
today!

Thursday, September 14 at 11 am + 7 pm
Friday, September 15 at 4:30 pm
In-person at MOA
$20 Regular | $12 MOA Members, Indigenous peoples, UBC students/faculty/staff
Very limited capacity; tickets required

MOA Unmasked: Behind-the-Scenes Tours are offered three times a month on
select Thursdays (11 am + 7 pm) and Fridays (4:30 pm). Future tour dates

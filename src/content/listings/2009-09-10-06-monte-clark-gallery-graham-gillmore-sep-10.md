The Monte Clark Gallery is pleased to present an exhibition of new
works by Graham Gillmore entitled "Refusalon".

GRAHAM GILLMORE
Refusalon

September 10 - October 3, 2009
Opening reception: Thursday September 10, 6-8pm

Featuring large-scale panels of glossy phrases, Gillmore’s new body of
work has shifted from candid, autobiographical, confessional texts to
more opaque self disclosures. His new approach is less a reaction to
the psychological character of the artist, and more towards the notion
of language as a suit of armour. "Refusalon", a play on the phrase
“Salon des Refuses” (or “exhibition of rejects”) typifies Gillmore’s
twisting of language to suit his agenda, both ironic and intellectual.

Completed in oil and enamel on panel, the works create scenes where
hope and doubt run in counterpoint to each other. "Refusalon" focuses
on pushing the boundaries of what could be considered plausible source
material while acknowledging the perpetual intervention of the
subject.

Graham Gillmore’s work has recently been shown at the Museum of Modern
Art (USA), the Tate Modern (England), and Gallery Fucares (Spain). His
work is held in numerous permanent collections including the Ghent
Museum (Belgium), Gian Enzo Sperone, (Italy), the Museum of
Contemporary Canadian Art (Toronto), the Museum of Modern Art, RCA
Records, (USA), and the Vancouver Art Gallery.

Open Call for Submissions

The Fort Gallery I June 2020-December 2021

SUBMISSION DEADLINE MARCH 16, 2020

The Fort Gallery was established as an artists co-op in 2005 and is currently in the
process of transition to become the first and only artist run centre in the Fraser Valley
region. The gallery is situated on the banks of the Fraser River, in the historic town of
Fort Langley, on unceded Kwantlen, Katzie, Semiahmoo and Matsqui territory. The Fort
Gallerys vision is to create a space for curiosity and
conversation where visual and
performing art form the heart of a vital and inquisitive community.

We are seeking proposals for solo and group exhibitions and exhibitions organized by
artists, independent curators or collectives for our June 2020-December 2021
programming year. Exhibitions will run 4-6 weeks. Priority will be given to artists who
can suggest community engagement activities or are willing to facilitate workshops.

Proposals are invited from emerging and established artists. We welcome submissions
of ideas/proposals, completed works, and works in progress.

Selected exhibitions will receive a $500 exhibition fee. For group exhibitions the fee will
be split between all participating artists.

Please click here to view the Exhibition Submission Guidelines and gallery floorplan.

Submission Guidelines: June 2020-December 2021 Programming

Please attach all text in PDF format. Images should be JPEG format. Send all materials
to fortgallery@hotmail.com

1. Exhibition
Proposal:

Max 1 page in PDF format. Please include: name, e-mail, address, phone number of
primary contact. Describe the exhibition including a title. What kind of work do you
propose to exhibit? Who will be involved? Why is your project relevant to the
Langley/Fort Langley communities? Do you have any special installation requirements?
Do you have any suggestions for community engagement or workshops related to the
exhibition? Sketches or renderings are welcome and encouraged (additional pages may
be attached), especially for installation-based projects. Please refer to the floor plan.
Please specify any dates June 2020-December 2021 when your work is unavailable for
exhibition.

2. Artist Information:

For each artist participating please include in PDF format

Max 1 page CV
Max 100 word bio
Link to website

3. Images:
10-15 images in JPEG format of the work you intend to exhibit OR work that is relevant
to your proposal. Please ensure that image resolution is adequate (1-2MB
files). Please
label files 01_FirstInitial_LastName eg. 01_J_Smith, 02_J_Smith, 03_J_Smith
15_J_Smith
For time-based work please provide a link to online video (YouTube, Vimeo etc.) Max 5
min

4. Image List:

Image list must be numbered corresponding to image files and include the following
information:
Artists name
Title
Medium
Dimensions (or duration for time-based work)
Year produced
Description or further information (if necessary)

Incomplete applications will not be considered.

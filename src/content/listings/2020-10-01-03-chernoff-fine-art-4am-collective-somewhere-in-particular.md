4AM Collective - Somewhere in Particular

Chernoff Fine Art
September 12 - October 17

It is important to consider how the worlds we represent act upon and are transformed by
the medium in which they are represented, by the maker of that representation, and by
the viewer of the representation. Our interpretations of landscape are relative and reveal
just as much about ourselves as they do about the landscape we examine. Somewhere
in Particular examines the relationship between place, artist, and viewer without the
specific context or condition in which the image
was produced. The photos become
cultural constructs which create and are part of an intricate network of narratives which
are both created and lived by the viewers who help to construct them.

4AM Collective are a group of Vancouver based photographers. They are concerned
with the tradition of formalism within photography as well as various political and social
issues, notions of representation, memory, and experiential learning. Their goal is to
develop a local photographic community through their love of the medium. The
collective is made up of four members: Clarissa Fragoso Pinheiro, Dane Murner,
Matthew Wong and Scott Rossi

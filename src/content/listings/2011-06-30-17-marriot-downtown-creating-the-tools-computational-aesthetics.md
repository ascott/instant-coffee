The artistic theme "Creating the tools" of the Computational 
Aesthetics conference 2011 revolves around the creative 
activity of writing software.
Programmers of computational tools define cultural processes 
as they shape code with their own mental images and 
worldviews. Whether conscious of this influence or not, these 
programmers enter into creative communication with the users 
of their software, and become co-creators of the aesthetic 
output.
The Art Show of the Computational Aesthetics Symposium 
responds to this theme, and presents a stunning variety of local 
and international pictorial and interactive works.

Computational Aesthetics will be jointly run with Sketch-Based 
Interfaces and Modeling (SBIM), and Non-Photorealistic 
Animation and Rendering (NPAR), co-located with SIGGRAPH.

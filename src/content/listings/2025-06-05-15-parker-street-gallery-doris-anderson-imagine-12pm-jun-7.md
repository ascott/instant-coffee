Doris Anderson: IMAGINE! - Selected Abstract and Colour Field Paintings

Opening Reception: June 7, 12-4pm

Exhibit: June 7-21

Parker Street Gallery

1000 Parker St #102, Vancouver, BC V6A 2H2

Gallery Hours: M-F 11am to 3pm or by appointment (www.parker.st/visit)

IMAGINE! by Doris Anderson is a celebration of intuitive creation, inspired by
meditation and the healing power of art. Each piece invites you to lose yourself in a
world of vibrant energy and love. Come experience the transformative beauty of
Doris' work.

Doris is an international abstract painter from the Vancouver lower mainland. She
creates her abstract acrylic paintings in the moment. Her intention is that her works
may bring to each viewer a moment of reflection.

Collectors of Doris artworks have remarked that they find mystical slices of time
within her paintings. Her artworks are held in public and private collections
throughout the world.

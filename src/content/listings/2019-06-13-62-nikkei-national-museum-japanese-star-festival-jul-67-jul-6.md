JAPANESE STAR FESTIVAL, TANABATA, OFFERS A FAMILY FRIENDLY WEEKEND 
WITH ITS BOOK SALE, FOOD, MOVIES AND MORE!

Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre is hosting a Japanese summer event, Tanabata, 
on Saturday, July 6 and Sunday, July 7. The Tanabata Festival kicks off with more activities 
than ever before with Japanese movies, a Japanese book sale, games, Japanese festival 
food and more. Tanabata is known as the Star Festival, a popular festival in Japan. It 
celebrates the meeting of the deities Orihime and Hikoboshi (represented by the stars Vega 
and Altair respectively). In Japan, colourful displays of bamboo with paper streamers are put 
up in various places. The paper steamers, called tanzaku, are rectangular strips of paper on 
which people write wishes and then attach to the bamboo stalks. During the Festival, our 
popular book sale takes place offering a variety of Japanese novels, comics, and some 
English books, DVDs and CDs at discounted prices. It is also with great pride and joy that 
we can announce that we have 3 recognized Japanese films: Okko's Inn, Mirai, and 
Shoplifters.

Okko's Inn: The latest feature from famed anime studio Madhouse and director Kitaro 
Kosaka, who was a key animator on numerous classic films at the venerable Studio Ghibli, 
seamlessly blend immersive, idyllic landscapes with the storybook charm of Okkos beloved 
ghosts. Okkos Inn delivers a rare ghost story that "despite several floating characters " is 
firmly grounded in the trials and joys of humanity.

Mirai: Mirai has achieved a historic first time ever, for a non-Studio Ghibli anime, nomination 
for the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature. An official selection at Cannes Directors 
Fortnight, and the epic capstone of director Mamoru Hosodas career, Mirai is a sumptuous, 
magical, and emotionally soaring adventure about the ties that bring families together and 
make us who we are.

Shoplifters: The film premiered in 2018 at the Cannes Film Festival, where it went on to win 
the Palme d'Or. Shoplifters was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign 
Language Film and the Golden Globe Award for Best Foreign Language Film.

Throughout the 2-day festival, the game zone and food zone feature Japanese festival 
themed activities and festive food such as cotton candy and shaved ice. Explore and enjoy 
this Japanese festival with films, games, and food!

The French, Hispanic, and Italian Studies Research Seminar at the University of British 
Columbia presents

Performing Utopias in the Contemporary Americas | A book launch

An innovative examination of the utopian impulse through performance as a proposition 
of practical engagement in the contemporary Americas.

With the editors: Kim Beauchesne and Alessandra Santos

And contributors: Jon Beasley-Murray, Rita De Grandis, Carmen Miranda Barrios, 
Manuel Pia, Jayce Saloum, Aerlyn Weissman

Wednesday, October 11, 2017, 3-4:30pm
FHIS Departmental Lounge, 7th Floor, Buchanan Tower (1873 East Mall, UBC campus 
in Vancouver)

All welcome

Pop! After Hours: Poetry Night

Thursday, Sept 12th from 5-9pm
Registration cost: Free
Address: Lot 19, 855 W Hastings (Outside)

Whether you want to join a community poetry writing workshop, register
for an open mic spot, or just listen to the local poets on stage, this
event
is for you as we will transform Lot 19 into a haven for poetic
expression and community connection! This entire event is free!

5:00 " 6:30 PM: Community Poetry Writing Workshop
Reflect on your relationship to being witnessed when expressing your
voice or gifts, and then to write a poem that captures your
experience. Engage in meaningful dialogue, explore and acknowledge
your ideas and creativity, and connect with others through shared
writing. No prior experience with writing poetry is necessary to
participate, all are welcome! Pens and paper will be provided, but if
you have a notebook and pen you love to write with please feel free to
bring them.

6:30 " 7:30 PM: Enjoy Food, Drinks, and Activities
While we turn Lot 19 into a stage, enjoy some food, drinks, and
various activities around the lot, including a fun poetry-inspired
activation with New Westminsters fifth and youngest Poet Laureate,
Elliott Slinn.

7:30 " 8:30 PM: Poetry Performances + Open Mic
For our main event of the
night, join us for an intimate evening of
poetry featuring six local poets on stage, wholl be sharing poetry on
a very special theme: their relationship to being witnessed in the
expression of their strengths and gifts. Open mic spots available. We
believe the art of poetry can help connect us and hope you will leave
this event with a rejuvenating sense of belonging!

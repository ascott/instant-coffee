A Conversation on Queer Mentorship (Virtual event) at Queer Arts Festival 2020 - July 25,
2020 at 12pm

Curious about the writer's life and the role of intergenerational relationships in queer &
BIPOC communities? Author Hiromi Goto (@hiromigotowrites) and emerging writer Erica
Isomura (@ericahiroko) explore the nuances of intergenerational mentorship during this
critical socio-political moment
as queer and poc writers. Hiromi and Erica share learnings
and reflections from their own mentorship relationship as part of Queer Arts Festival 2020.

Free event. ASL interpretation is provided. Limited spots - registration is required in advance.

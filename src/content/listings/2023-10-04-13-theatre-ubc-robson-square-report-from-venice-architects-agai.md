Report from Venice! " Architects Against Housing Alienation

Wednesday, October 11

7:00pm

Theatre, UBC Robson Square

800 Robson Street

Vancouver

Open to the public

Architects Against Housing Alienation (AAHA) is a collective of architects, activists, and
advocates fighting for socially, ecologically, and creatively empowering housing for all.
This event will be a report on the latest developments of AAHAs campaign, Not for
Sale!, as seen from its current heartquarters at the Venice Biennale of Architecture.
Come learn about the heartquarters and the architecture, policy, and financial aspects
of the Not for Sale! campaign. All are welcome.

AAHA is Canadas official representation at the 18th International Architecture
Exhibition " La Biennale
di Venezia.

Speakers:

Simoogit Saa Bax, Patrick R. Stewart Architect

Travis Hanks, Haeccity Studio Architecture

Ouri Scott, Urban Arts Architecture

Matthew Soules, UBC School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture

Sara Stevens, UBC School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture

Tijana Vujosevic, UBC School of Architecture and Landscape Architecture

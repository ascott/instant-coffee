SD 45 ONE.five

March 29 to April 5, 2016
Reception: March 30 from 6 to 8 p.m.

Local residents investigate their perceptions of West Vancouver's social,
cultural, economic, and topographical settings. Their personal inquiries of
this distinct community are documented in a collaborative mural.

Students, educators, artists, and a politician and an architect were
invited to document their communities using a camera. Their work is being
shown in the West Vancouver Museum for visitors to discover both the links
between these groups and what defines them as distinct populations.

Artists featured in this exhibition include Jody Broomfield, Cedric
Burgers, Craig Cameron, Chris Kennedy, Ross Penhall, Victor John Penner,
Steve Rauh, and West Vancouver School District students.

SD 45 ONE.five is guest-curated by Jackie Wong, West Vancouver Secondary
School teacher, in collaboration with the Museum's Education Coordinator
Isaac Vanderhorst.

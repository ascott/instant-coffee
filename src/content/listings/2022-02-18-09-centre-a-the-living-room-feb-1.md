Centre A invites you to The Living Room (February " May 2022)
Program I (February " March 2022)
Gallery Hours:
Wednesday to Saturday, 12 PM " 6 PM*
*Subject to change as per COVID-19-related protocols

Centre A is excited to welcome everyone to The Living Room, our first program of
2022. We invite visitors to come experience the furnished space and watch a selection
of films that will continually play during our gallery hours.

As part of The Living Room project, Centre A presents two series of curated film
programs in partnership with Vtape. The first program, running from now to March
2022, addresses reconnection, lineage, and exile, and features works by Thirza
Cuthand, Roy Dib,
Richard Fung, Cherie Valentina Stocken, and Ho Tam.

Artists and filmmakers, as well as programmers, are also invited to submit films, video
work, and moving images to be considered for a screening during The Living Room.
We encourage artists, collectives, and other community members or organizations with
diverse backgrounds to submit works for consideration. Centre A will offer CARFAC-
standard fees for selected submissions.

Contact us at info@centrea.org for submission details.

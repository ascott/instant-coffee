Gwen Allen
Lecture: On Aspen Magazine
Thursday, January 22, 2015, 6pm, ECU Theatre

Please join us for a lecture by San Francisco-based art historian Gwen Allen.

In conjunction with the exhibition Aspen Magazine: 1965-1971, Allen
discusses Aspen within the context of art history, exploring its important
function as an alternative exhibition space for 1960s and 1970s art. She
will also discuss the history of artists' magazines more generally both
during this earlier period and today. Allen is the author of Artists'
Magazines: An Alternative Space for Art (MIT, 2011) and is a Professor of
Art History at San Francisco State University.

Aspen Magazine was a multimedia magazine conceived of, edited and published
by Phyllis Johnson in New York from 1965 to 1971. Aspen broke new ground in
terms of its editorial concept, design approach and distribution strategy,
which continues to be resonant and influential today. The exhibition is on
loan from the Whitechapel Gallery in London and continues at the Charles H.
Scott Gallery to February 8, 2015

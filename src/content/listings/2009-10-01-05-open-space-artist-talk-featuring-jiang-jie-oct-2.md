Date: Friday October 2, 7pm

Admission: Free

Artist: Jiang Jie ((Beijing)

Place: Open Space, 510 Fort St.

Victoria - Join us on Friday October 2, 7pm for an Artist Talk featuring Jiang Jie, one of the 
leading sculptors currently working in China. Jiang Jie is Associate Professor of Sculpture at the 
Central Academy of Fine Arts in Beijing. Her most famous works are larger-than-life sculptures of
babies. Several of Jiang Jie’s recent works have combined babies’ or children’s faces with adults’ 
facial expressions, suggesting a universality that transcends age, but also the things babies 
cannot say and that which adults cannot understand. “I work with images of children and babies 
because people react instinctively to them,” Jiang said in one interview. “I want to use my artwork 
to discuss problems of humanity. I want to reflect deep issues that we deal with in society.”

Jiang Jie’s babies are an alarming yet intriguing study into the emptiness and distortion that 
devours youthful innocence in a capitalist society. Jiang’s work is simultaneously ugly and 
beautiful, sacred and profane.

Xi Chuan, the Orion Visiting Asian Artist, Faculty of Fine Arts, University of Victoria, will act as 
translator. Jiang Jie will also present a lecture on Contemporary Women Artists in China at the 
University of Victoria on October 5, 8pm, Rm A162 Visual Arts Building

La Comunidad
de los Amantes
(The community of lovers)

Opening: November 23rd 2012 7pm until late
http://www.topdownbottomup.ca/shows/
https://www.facebook.com/topdownbottomup

TopDown BottomUp presents work form three emergent Mexican artists:
Paola V. Montoya (currently living and working in Barcelona, Spain),
Miguel Pérez R. (currently living and working in Cholula, México),
and Nelly César (currently living and working in Vancouver, Canada).

In times of hate based societies it becomes relevant to rethink love.
Considering that the normative relation to love is the main
justification for violence based on a profound and visceral
attachments to capitalist emotional systems and values. La Comunidad
de los Amantes consists on video- performance work of three amatory
sessions of radical intercourse that crash upon utilitarian loving and
retrace forms of building attachments with the different and the
unknown. Redirecting violence, daughterhood, zoophilia, incest and
cannibalism to an inoperative transaction of unfruitful yet powerful
loving.

TopDown BottomUp- Unit 212- 2280 East Hastings Street, Vancouver, BC.

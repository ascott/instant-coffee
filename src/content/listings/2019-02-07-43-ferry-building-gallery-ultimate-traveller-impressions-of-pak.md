Ultimate Traveller: Impressions of Pakistan

Wednesday, February 27, 2019 7-9 p.m.

Ferry Building Gallery
1414 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver, BC V7T 1C2

Join us for a series of imaginative travel talks on faraway places by geographer and 
travel photographer Peter Langer. Discover the wealth of human history, the natural 
beauty of our planet and explore a diversity of cultures. Experience the magic of 
these fascinating worlds through a beautiful audio-visual journey.

You will also hear Peter's impressions, curious anecdotes, frustrations and some of 
the little secrets these places have to offer. Peter Langer has travelled to over 175 
countries.

Come explore the wonders of Pakistan in this stunning audio-visual presentation. 
The country offers great mountaineering and trekking in the Hindu Kush, Karakoram 
and Himalayas. Pakistan also features a vast artistic heritage, from the ancient Indus 
Valley civilization to the Moghul Empire and has 6 UNESCO World Heritage sites for 
you to explore.

Discover the Hunza Valley, also known as "Shangri La" from James Hilton's novel 
"Lost Horizons", an area of stunning natural beauty, as well as the ancient Silk Road 
Town of Gilgit linking Western China with this region and the Buddhist impressive 
rock carvings of the Buddha near Kargah. Above all, experience a multitude of 
cultures with a long tradition of hospitality.

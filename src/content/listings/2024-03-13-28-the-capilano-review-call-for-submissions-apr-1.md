The Capilano Review invites submissions for its forthcoming ti-TCR web folio, "On
Collective Care," edited by Emma Jeffrey.

What purpose does
writing serve in times of collective grief? Drawing upon grassroots
liberation movements and models for solidarity-building, this forthcoming web folio
will
explore literary practice as a tool to expand our capacity for empathy and care on a
collective scale.

As we navigate white supremacist systems of capitalism, many of us have become
desensitized from the pressures of living in a constant crisis state. This forthcoming
issue of ti-TCR will explore the ways in which we can foster practices of community
care in our daily lives which disrupt frameworks of individualism and scarcity. What
are
the tangible ways that we uplift and are uplifted by others in times of great despair?
How can the process of artistic creation focus our resolve and unite us collectively
around a shared purpose?

It is most evident in times of economic and social
collapse that we all share the
responsibility of creating positive change. Why write poetry during the apocalypse, if
not for the hope of a kinder
world?

On Collective Care: Call for Submission Guidelines:
- Submission deadline: April 1, 2024 (midnight PST)
- Work must be original and previously unpublished
- Submit up to 6 pages of poetry, prose, or other short experimental forms (PDF or
Word doc formats only)
- Simultaneous submissions are accepted, but please notify us immediately if your
work is accepted for publication elsewhere
- All pieces accepted for publication will be compensated at a flat rate of $100 CAD
- Email your submission, along with a short 50-word bio, to
submissions@thecapilanoreview.com with the subject line On Collective Care

Please send any questions about your submission to
contact@thecapilanoreview.com
before the deadline. View the full call here: https://thecapilanoreview.com/on-

Call for Proposals - THE WALL
In Partnership with the Vancouver Heritage Foundation, CBC Radio-Canada,
and the City of Vancouver
Eligibility: Proposals are welcome from artists and independent curators in
Vancouver
Project Fee: $5,000
Submission Deadline: April 21, 2016 - 5pm

The Vancouver Heritage Foundation is currently accepting proposals from
independent artists and curators for the next The WALL artist installation.
The selected artist will relate their work to the theme of Vancouver's
built environment. The work will be installed for approximately one year at
the CBC Plaza located at 700 Hamilton Street. The artist will also have the
opportunity to explore the CBC Archives to research and inform their
artwork. The intended installation date is August 2016. THE WALL is an
artists' platform made possible through a partnership between the Vancouver
Heritage Foundation and CBC Radio-Canada, and produced in partnership with
the City of Vancouver Public Art Program.

Link to Call:
http://www.vancouverheritagefoundation.org/special-projects/the-wall/

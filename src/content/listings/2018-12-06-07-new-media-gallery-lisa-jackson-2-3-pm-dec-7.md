Artist Talk - Lisa Jackson
Friday, December 7, 2:00 - 3:00pm, free
New Media Gallery, Anvil Centre, 777 Columbia St, New Westminster

Join us in welcoming multi-award-winning filmmaker and artist Lisa Jackson who will 
speak on her practice and on her VR work included in the current Reclaim exhibition; 
Biidaaban: First Light. 

With a background in documentary film, she expanded into fiction, winning a 2010 
Genie award from Best Short Film. Her work has been shown at festivals around the 
world including Berlinale, Hot Docs, SXSW, Tribeca, London BFI and she has presented 
at galleries such as Audain Art Museum and the Belkin. Her work Biidaaban : First Light, 
was made with the National Film Board and Mathew Barrett, and premiered at Tribeca in 
2018.

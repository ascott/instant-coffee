Opening Reception: Thursday, Jan 30th, 5 - 8 pm.
Exhibition Dates: Jan 23 - February 22, 2020

Gallery Jones is pleased to present the abstract paintings of three extraordinary
Vancouver talents: Sara Genn, Mira Song, and Scott Sueme. Intercepting the Nature of
Colour and Form explores the unique voices of three artists who cleverly use abstraction
to build bridges, both to express the inexpressible and to craft their ideas into a
language that surpasses all limitations: a language
of colour and form.

Artists will be in attendance.

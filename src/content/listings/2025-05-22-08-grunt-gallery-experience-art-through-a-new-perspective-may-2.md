Join Jinnie Saran for FREE blind-led tours of grunt gallerys current exhibition,
Falsework this May and June! Jinnie, a blind Indo-Canadian emerging artist and
writer, will guide visitors through Simon Grefiel and kiyoshi's exhibition, Falsework,
curated by Mitch Kenworthy.

"... Wednesdays: May 28, June 4, 11

Tours start at 4 PM and 6 PM

30 minutes + Q&A

" grunt gallery (#116-350 E 2nd Ave)

All are welcome"sighted, low-vision, and blind visitors!

No registration required, just drop-in when you can!

Questions? Email access@grunt.ca

grunt gallery is excited to announce a series of free, drop-in volunteer-led tours this
summer, facilitated by Jinnie Saran, a blind Indo-Canadian emerging artist and
writer. These unique tours allow visitors to experience contemporary art through
visual and non-visual perspectives, challenging traditional ways of engaging with
gallery spaces.

The 30-minute tours will be held on Wednesdays at 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM on May
28, June 4, and June 11. Each tour will be followed by a question-and-answer
session, creating space for dialogue about diverse ways of experiencing and
interpreting art.

Visit Falseworks exhibition page at https://grunt.ca/exhibitions/falsework/

Check out Jinnies website at https://jinniesaran.com/

Please join us for an artist talk with participating artists in attendance from It Is Still
Living, moderated by Mandana Mansouri.

Saturday, October 7, 11am
Participating Artists: Aileen Bahmanipour, Azadeh Hussaini, Sholeh Mahlouji, Marzi
Mosavarzadeh, Yasaman Moussavi, Elmira Sarreshtehdari

Artspeak welcomes guest curators Yasaman Moussavi and Elmira Sarreshtehdari. The
exhibition acts as an interwoven tapestry of narratives from six Iranian artists living in
North America, as they reflect on the recent women-led uprisings, and their
experiences as immigrant bodies in relation to law, nature, life and death, senses of
freedom, and collective identities. It Is Still
Living .. . resides in the unsettling and ever changing in-between spaces of material
and immaterial, knowing and unknowing, embodiment and disembodiment,
construction and deconstruction of the living world.

The exhibition is on view until October
14, 2023

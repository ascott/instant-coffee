Audience Participation Art Exhibition
at Emily Carr University

Drop off is December 6th and 7th, 1-3pm. Show Opening December 7th at 5pm.

Accession: A Cumulative Collaboration, is a student-run exhibition that seeks to 
provide access to our school and the artistic process of group curation. We want to 
empower our donors as artists and creators of a new kind of show. 

Open Call for materials.

Bring us your cast-offs, the unwanted items that have been taking up space in your 
studios, your houses, your garages.
If you can carry it with two hands we want it.

We wish to welcome you to the new campus so come in to stroll and explore.

Our group will be ready and waiting to receive your donated items and add them to 
our exhibition, in the Atrium Gallery the East side of the 2nd (Main) floor. Nothing is 
too strange; all is welcome.

Help to break in this new space. There is lots of area available for showing.

An old toothbrush or grade-school drawings. Doorknobs and doodads, lightweight 
building materials. Scraps. Art.

Drop off is December 6th and 7th, 1-3pm. Show Opening December 7th at 5pm.

Emily Carr University of Art + Design. 520 E 1st Ave. 

We need your help. Bring anything you can part with, or your items maybe also be 
returned if desired.

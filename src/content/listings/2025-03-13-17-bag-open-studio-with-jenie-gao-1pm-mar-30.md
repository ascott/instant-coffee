In the BAG and Open Studio with Jenie Gao

Sun, Mar 30 2025, 1 - 4pm

Come to the Burnaby Art Gallery for a special In the BAG session featuring an Open
Studio by Jenie Gao, the current Deer Lake Artist in Residence. Visit Jenie in the
Fireside Room of the Gallery to get a glimpse into their process, current projects and
learn about their techniques. Then pop over to the BAG Barn Studio to create
artwork inspired by Gaos practice with our In the BAG instructor.

Jenie Gao (they/she) is an artist specializing in
printmaking, public art, social
practice and community storytelling. Gao runs an anti-gentrification arts business
and does consulting for cultural organizations and the public sector on equity and
ethics.

In the BAG is a free, drop-in program for all ages. All children must be accompanied
by an adult.

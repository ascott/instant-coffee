Challenges in Formalizing Cognitive Art-Science Research
Seminar with Shanti Ganesh

March 22, 2018. 3:00 - 5:00 PM
Free, no registration required.
Room 2205, Goldcorp Centre for the Arts, 149 W. Hastings St.

In this talk, Shanti Ganesh will discuss the challenges and opportunities of doing art-
science research related to the cognitive sciences. Since 2012 Ganesh started exploring 
a novel research method in which critical reflection through art practice, e.g dance and 
performance, can inform theoretical and methodological issues in cognitive science and 
neuroscience. These challenges include avoiding the fetishization of the scientific 
discourse (Vass-Rhee, 2011) and associated power differences between artists and 
scientists in terms of status, finances and privilege. As well as the challenge of 
translating results from artistic research that does not necessarily follow the formal rules 
of scientific enquiry, in such a way that these results are applicable in science or, more 
fundamentally, can even alter the practice of scientific enquiry itself.

Shanti Ganesh

Shanti Ganesh is a cognitive neuroscientist and psychologist (PhD, 2013) also trained in 
public administration (MA, 1994), currently working on the crossroads of critical art, 
cognitive science and new technologies, e.g. humanoid robots. Since 2015 Shanti 
started engaging with a dynamic group of artists, scholars and scientists in Vancouver 
that come together in varying configurations to train, produce bold work and critically 
reflect on their art practices and political contexts. See also: www.kladlab.nl.

Co-presented by SFU's Vancity Office of Community Engagement and the School for

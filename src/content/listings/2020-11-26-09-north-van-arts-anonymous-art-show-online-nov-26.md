North Van Arts' Online Holiday Fundraiser,
Anonymous Art Show

Hundreds of local artists have contributed over 500 original artworks for North Van Arts'
annual festive fundraiser, the Anonymous Art Show. CityScape Community ArtSpace will be
transformed with hundreds of 8x8 artworks, creating a poetry of colour, expression and
wonder in the gallery. Under the new Provincial Health Order, all in-person visits for this
exhibition are suspended until December 7, so we encourage everyone to have fun
exploring
all the artwork online.

The proceeds from this festive fundraiser provide significant support to ongoing North Van
Arts' programmes and projects while also showcasing local artists in the community.

Important Dates:

November 21 - 25: All the artwork will be available to view online

November 26, 7pm: Online Sale Begins. Buy your favourite pieces and come by for easy
curbside pick-up.

https://northvanarts.ca/events-exhibitions/anonymous-art-show-2020-online-fundraiser-and-
sale/

November 27-December 19: Sale continues online. Support your local art community with
every $100 artwork you buy!

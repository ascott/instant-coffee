New Design British Columbia Call for Submissions

call for submissions

New Design British Columbia
Cutty Contemporary Art Gallery - Alternate Exhibition Space
564 Beatty St, Vancouver, BC
Sept 30th - Oct 18th
Opening Reception: September 30th

New Design British Columbia is a design exhibition seeking contemporary, multi-
disciplinary work that occupies the field of design through object making, 
furniture design, lighting, installation design, functional art, graphic design, 
materials and application research. Opportunities for floor, wall, ceiling and 
spatial installations are open for proposal. Design work is being accepted in both 
a conceptual/prototype stage as well as finished/production ready pieces. 
Applicants must be from or working in British Columbia, or have been educated, 
are sourcing materials, or have specific reference to British Columbia in their 
work.

This exhibition is strategically set to align with IDS West in an effort to broaden 
the exposure of British Columbia’s regional design.

Submissions will be judged on conceptual merit and/or execution of design.

Applications should consist of:
A brief project proposal with supplementary images.
A link to online portfolio or website.

Please send applications to newdesignbc@gmail.com
visit newdesignbritishcolumbia.tumblr.com

Deadline for Submissions is September 18th

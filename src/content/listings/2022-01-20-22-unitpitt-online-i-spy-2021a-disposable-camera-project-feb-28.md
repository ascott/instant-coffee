I SPY 2021...a disposable camera project
is a mail art program created by UNIT/PITT
Society for Art and Critical Awareness. Six artists were each sent a disposable camera
and a unique crowd-sourced text prompt. They were instructed to photograph whatever
they want, as long as one photograph responds to the prompt. UNIT/PITT chose nine
unedited photos from each roll to showcase online.

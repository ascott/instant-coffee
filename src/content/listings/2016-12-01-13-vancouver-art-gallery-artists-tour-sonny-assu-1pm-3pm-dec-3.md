Artist's Tour: Sonny Assu
We Come to Witness: Sonny Assu in Dialogue with Emily Carr
Saturday December 3, 1pm & 3pm
In the Gallery, 4th floor

Join interdisciplinary artist Sonny Assu for a tour of his exhibition In Dialogue with 
Emily Carr: Sonny Assu. Challenging the colonial gaze, Assu merges Indigenous 
iconography with a pop art sensibility to intervene into the work of Modernist painter 
Emily Car and her representations of the landscape and First Nations people. Assu 
will discuss select sculptural works, his ongoing series Interventions on the 
Imaginary, which includes digital tags based on a selection of Carr paintings from the 
Vancouver Art Gallery's collection and a new collaboration with ceramic artist 
Brendan Tang.

Free for Members or with Gallery Admission.

RED 8 #3: The Final Blow-out is a queer event to be held in conjunction
with World AIDS Day involving Scotiabank Dance Centre, Vancouver New Music
and Cineworks. RED 8 #3 is the final RED 8 and will be an exciting evening
involving a group of innovative queer artists working in film/video, sound,
painting, drawing, installation and performance art. The last two RED 8
functions at Video In (VIVO) were very successful in terms of participation
from artists, attendance and diversity and this next event is shaping up to
be more thought provoking and boundary-pushing than ever. The artists
exhibiting are Bruce La Bruce, Attila Richard Lukacs, Andrew Powers, Clark
Nikolai, Shawn Chappelle, James Diamond, Brian Gotro, Trolley Bus and
Frederick Cummings. The doors open at 9 pm, performance art by Sisterfuck,
Bobbi Kozinuk and friends start at 11p.m. and then partying, dancing and
music until 1 a.m. Curated by Frederick Cummings.

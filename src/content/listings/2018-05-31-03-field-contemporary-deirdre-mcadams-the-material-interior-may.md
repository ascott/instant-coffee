Deirdre McAdams
The Material Interior
May 31st - June 30th, 2018
Opening May 31st, 6:00 - 10:00PM.

Field Contemporary
17 West Broadway
info@field-contemporary.com

McAdams' work is an exploration of how fundamental aspects of painting
(i.e. gesture, colour, line, form, support) are expressed or represented
through the filter of software and print technology, and how this filtering
can offer artists new possibilities for image-making. McAdams' is
interested in examining how the use of software can facilitate inventive
perspectives and therefore progress for the medium, and in how new
image-making modalities can evolve from expansive material perspectives.

These paintings are portraits of interiority, in the sense of representing
an imaginary digital space as well as the intimate internal space of
memory. The works seek to reconcile digital and analog approaches to the
medium of painting through a process of translation from one to the other,
in both directions. The canvas paintings seek to make real what is
conceived of in a digital realm, while the digital prints allude to or echo
the particular physicality of a painted surface.

Deirdre McAdams is a visual artist living and working in Vancouver, BC. She
is a graduate of Emily Carr University of Art and Design (2008), and the
Victoria College of Art (2003), where she studied painting. She was awarded
an Honourable Mention in the 2011 RBC Painting Competition, as well as a
prize in 2010 from Canadian Art Magazine for her writing on contemporary
art, and has received multiple grants from the BC Arts Council. Her recent
exhibitions include solo shows at CSA Space in Vancouver and G Gallery in
Toronto. She is currently a MFA candidate at the University of Guelph and
was recently granted the Joseph-Armand Bombardier Canada Graduate
Scholarship (SSHRC).

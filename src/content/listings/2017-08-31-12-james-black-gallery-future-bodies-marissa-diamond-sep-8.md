Future Bodies is an exhibit by Marissa Diamond, which investigates new ways of 
viewing the body. In this series of paintings and sculptures, Diamond uses crochet and 
collage, techniques labeled as craft and low art as a means to challenge conventional 
definitions of beauty and art.

Opening is on September 8th, 2017 from 6pm-9pm, additional hours on September 9th, 
from 1pm-4pm, Location: James Black Gallery, 144 East 6th Avenue, Vancouver BC

Reception : Saturday September 9, 2-4pm

Tiko Kerr is a local artist who since the 1980s, has been an important figure in the
Vancouver cultural scene through his artmaking and community activism. Kerrs work is
fundamentally informed by how he personally sees the world and the ways human vision
functions to interpret the world. This multi-part installation derives from Kerrs long-time
interest in collage informed by his practice as a painter and includes a number of new
pieces, including collaborations with poet Otoniya J. Okot Bitek, that merge the work of
the two artists and reflect on the nature of collaboration across artistic disciplines.

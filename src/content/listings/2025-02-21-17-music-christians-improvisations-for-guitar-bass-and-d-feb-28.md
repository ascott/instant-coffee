Christians is an improv project that appears in various configurations led by
Vancouver guitarist Jeremy Stewart. This trio performance will also feature bassist
Oli Shakur and percussionist Wayne Adams. The group translates the sonic
vocabulary of metal, blues, and punk into the musical language of free improvisation.

"...This is edge-of-seat music linking many extreme styles and melting them into a
glorious mess"
" Marc Masters, Best Experimental Music on Bandcamp

[Christians S/Z is] splendidly fiery close to the ideal of what fusion can be
" Byron Coley, The Wire

Jeremy Stewart is a guitarist who has
performed various metal, punk, noise, and folk
styles including as lead guitarist for Lheidli T'enneh Elder Marcel Gagnon. WIth his
project Christians, Jeremy has shared stages or collaborated with artists like Oxbow,
Lightning Bolt, Lori Goldston, Stanley Jason Zappa, among many others. He is the
artistic director of Casse-Tte: A Festival of Experimental Music.

Oli Shakur inhabits a mad, queer, Brown settler body that has appeared as an
elusive and mercurial utility player in creative music, noise, punk, jazz, and the
institutional avant-garde over the last thirty years. Oli currently plays in the improv
duo Ears of Lead and jazz quartet Big Shoulders; the noise project Guilty Of Being
White By Minor Threat will debut in 2025.

Wayne Adams' music career has been spent exploring the outer boundaries of punk,
folk and jazz as a longtime member of Zolty Cracker and Geoff Berner's band. Many
other musical ventures have drawn him here and there but his fascination with the
spirit of pure
expression in the moment always brings him back to his love of
improvisation.

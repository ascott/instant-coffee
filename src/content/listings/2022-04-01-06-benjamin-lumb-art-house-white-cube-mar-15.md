Open call to all Artists
Benjamin Lumb Art House|BLAH

"White Cube"
March 25 - May 5

White Cube is a "Free For All" salon that offers an open
platform to all artists at all
stages of their careers. The exhibition will be pieced together based upon the artworks
submitted. Modeled on an egalitarian philosophy, original artworks of all sizes,
mediums and subject matter, created over the past 2 years may be submitted to the
gallery and will hang in a salon-style mosaic.

In principle, White Cube is an exhibition showcasing inclusivity, diversity and cross-
pollination, allowing artists and collectors to find one another. Weekly salons will
encourage and support new connections and conversations. Artworks may be
delivered throughout the specified dates but when the gallery walls are full artworks
may be pulled by the curator to accomodate new work arriving at the gallery. Only sold
and selected works will stay on the walls until closing night May 14th.
Participating artists: Bring up to three recently completed artworks to BLAH between
March 25th & May 5th, Monday to Thursday, 7-830pm. Sculpture and installation must
be
submitted via email at gallery@benjaminlumb.art. Maximum of 3 pieces per artist.
The artist must be present upon dropoff. Works on paper must be framed. Canvases
may be unframed. Work must be ready to hang with appropriate hardware installed.
Artists may submit anonymously. If choosing to sell include fairmarket value. If artworks
are uninstalled artists will be notified and must pick up their work within two days.

Benjamin Lumb Art House,1370 Marine Drive, West Vancouver
gallery@benjaminlumb.art

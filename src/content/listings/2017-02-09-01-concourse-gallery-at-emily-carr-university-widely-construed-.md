26th February- 3rd March
Concourse Gallery (1399 Johnston St, Vancouver, BC V6H 3R9)
2nd of March, 6-9pm

https://www.facebook.com/events/651203871735135/

Widely Construed is the Emily Carr University of Art and Design, Master of Applied Art, 
class of 2018, interim exhibition show. The show will be held at the Concourse Art 
Gallery from 26th of February to the 3rd of March and will be showcasing eleven artists 
who make up the cohort of the program.
Reception will be held on the 2nd of March 6-9pm where food and drinks will be 
available under suggested donation.

Artists:
Anna Firth, Effy Chee, Kyle Scheurmann, Olivia de Fleuriot, Riya Ding, milie Grace 
Lavoie, Karin Kunzo, Pilar Guinea Gil, Laura Krick, Adiba Muzaffar, and Melina Querel.

Follow us on Instagram @maa2018ecuad

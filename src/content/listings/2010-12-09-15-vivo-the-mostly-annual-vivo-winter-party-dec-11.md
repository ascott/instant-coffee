The Mostly-Annual VIVO Winter Party

Members, artists, volunteers, friends: please come out of hibernation and
join us at 7:00 pm on Saturday, December 11th in the VIVO Salon for our
almost-but-not-quite-annual winter holiday event. We've been busy putting
together a selection of the finest entertainment and festive distractions
for your enjoyment. Highlights include:

-Fire (Walk with Me) video installation by Sharon Bradley
-indoor fire pit by Amy Nugent
-fortune telling by Psychic DJ
-music by DJ Trail Mix, dj alexface, DJ StandOuglas and DJ Jazzy Jeff Wall
-mulled wine! spiced rum! snacks!
-dancing! (eventually, probably)

We very much look forward to being your host.

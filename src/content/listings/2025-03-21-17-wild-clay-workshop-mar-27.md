Wild Clay Workshop

Digging deep into ancient pottery techniques using locally hand foraged clay

Three Tuesdays in April, 2025

April 15th and 22nd, 6pm-8pm. April 29th 4-8pm

$390, class limited to 5 participants

Email to register: ilenaleepottery@gmail.com

Join ceramic artist Ilena Lee, learning how to make pottery in the most ancient
method, and connect with this land with respect and reciprocity. In this workshop you
will learn how I find usable clays in the wild, and how to forage ethically and
respectfully. Ilena will show you how to process the clays
into a usable form with
simple everyday tools you probably already own, make small vessels and fire (bake)
the pots in a pit fire. You will come away from this workshop with the ability and
understanding of how to make pottery using nothing but your hands and your
creativity.

Ilena (she/them) lives and creates on the stolen, occupied, ancestral and traditional
lands of the xmky"m, Sl"lwta", and Skwxw7mesh Nations of the Coast
Salish peoples (Vancouver, BC, Canada). A ceramic artist, teacher, writer, and
theatre designer, she has been a lifelong student of ceramics and clay. Her current
practice finds local landscape, environment and materials encountering myth, story
and history.

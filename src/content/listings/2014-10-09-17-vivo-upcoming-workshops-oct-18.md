Upcoming Workshops at VIVO:

Camera, Lights, Sound
October 18th & 19th.
Includes 2 sessions: total 12 hours
$288 or $230 + GST with VIVO Producer Membership (Extended). Register via
Eventbrite

This intensely hands-on workshop introduces students to the Blackmagic
Cinema Camera and the Sony HVR-Z5U high definition camera package (both
available to rent from VIVO following the workshop). Basic technical
aspects of camera operation, sound recording, and lighting principles are
taught through instructor demonstration and practical exercises. Our
knowledgeable instructors guide participants to gain skills best suited to
their own project needs.

Intro to Mathematica with Adam Barlev

November 1st, 4-7pm | $25 Non-Members ; $20 for VIVO Producer Membership
(Extended) Register for this event via Eventbrite.

Mathematica is a computational programming language with an amazing range
of capabilities, and it’s simple to learn for people with little or no
programming experience. This powerful language now comes free on the
Raspberry Pi. It is incredibly simple to make anything you can describe
mathematically interactive. This brief introduction will focus on how to
create interactive graphics and output animations as gifs, but with
Mathematica on the Raspberry Pi, the possibilities are endless.

There will be 6 Raspberry Pi’s available for use. If you have your own,
please bring it!

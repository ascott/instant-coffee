Position Description & Candidate Specifications: EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR

Organizational Profile

CAMDO is an organization whose membership is made up of art museum
directors. CAMDO is a forum for the exchange of information to provide
informed and collegial communications; to advocate for the arts; and to
maintain standards of ethics and practice for art museum directors.

CAMDO was founded in 1964 to promote professional standards for art museum
directors in Canada. Among the founding members were William Withrow of the
Art Gallery of Ontario, Ferdinand Eckhardt of the Winnipeg Art Gallery and
Charles Comfort of the National Gallery of Canada. The organization was
incorporated in 2002.

After securing funding from the Canada Council for the Arts in April 2007,
CAMDO hired its first Executive Director. CAMDO has adopted an
Organizational Plan to articulate the organizationâ&#65533;&#65533;s functions and projects
through 2010. The Executive Director is the sole employee of the association.

Position Summary

Reporting to the Board of Directors and guided by its mandate, vision and
values, the Executive Director will provide leadership to CAMDO and will
accomplish its organizational development goals, manage operations,
administer projects & programs, and facilitate advocacy initiatives. The
scope of responsibility will include strategic direction, programmatic
development, internal member relations, external stakeholder relations and
fund development, as well as operational, financial and administrative
management.

Major Responsibilities

Advocacy

* Implement advocacy priorities identified by the Board and members.
* Engage CAMDO members in advocacy efforts when appropriate.
* Cultivate and maintain professional relations with government
officials and members of governmental agencies.
* Remain well-informed about public affairs affecting the artistic and
cultural sectors.
* Establish and maintain an extensive network of contacts across Canada.
* Work collaboratively with advocacy networks including the Visual Arts
Alliance, the Canadian Museums Association and the Canadian Conference of
the Arts.

Programmatic Development

* Initiate and manage new programs, including a current priority to
establish a centre for directorial training.
* Assist Board committees developing and implementing their goals and
objectives,
* Work with the Professional Development Committee to organize regular
symposia, lectures and other projects.
* Engage in ongoing self-education to remain current on the most
pressing issues facing art museums and galleries and the arts sector as a
whole.

Board Relations

* Develop and maintain a strong working relationship and effective
communication.
* Provide a written report/presentation on operating activities,
progress and performance related to approved budgets.
* Schedule and actively participate in Board meetings (6 per year).
* Implement policies as defined by the Board.
* Work with legal counsel, when needed, in representing and negotiating
contractual matters.

Communications, Internal and External Relations

organizations, advocacy networks, funders, foundations, government and
agencies to establish and maintain beneficial relationships.
* Serve as one of the organizationâ&#65533;&#65533;s chief spokespersons (the President
being the other).
* Communicate the work and mission of the organization to the public
through the media and at public speaking engagements.
* Facilitate communication between the organization and its key
stakeholders including directors, members, funders, governments,
authorizing environments (whatâ&#65533;&#65533;s this?) and peer associations.
* Enable Web-site and list-serve development and maintenance.

Administrative & Financial Management

* Develop and achieve all budgetary and financial planning as approved
by the Board of Directors.
* Oversee management of cash flow and present quarterly financial
statements for Board review.
* Take primary leadership role to achieve both short and long-term
fundraising goals.
* Identify, cultivate and solicit funding from government, agencies,
foundations, corporations and other granting opportunities to ensure the
growth and sustainable operation of the organization.

The Executive Director will work closely with the four official Standing
Committees on all aspects of advocacy, professional development, legal
affairs and programmatic development. The four committees are Advocacy,
Collections, Professional Development and Legal Affairs. Each committee is
chaired by a member of the Board.

Call for Submissions

RPM: The Lost Art of LP Covers

April 10 to April 25, 2009
Opening April 10, 7 to 10pm
Special Artists Preview at 6:30pm
Deluge Contemporary Art
636 Yates Street, Victoria, BC
Exhibition Hours: Wed to Sat, 12 to 5pm

Remember the LP cover? Two square feet of eye-popping, groin-stirring,
world-rocking graphics, titles and liner notes rolled into one precisely
measured object of desire? Well, the infamous RPM fundraising exhibition is
back by popular demand.

Deluge Contemporary Art is asking for your support by creating a
one-of-a-kind record sleeve. Over the years, hundreds of artists, including
music and art-world luminaries and emerging artists alike, have
participated in this unique fundraising exhibition. In the recent past RPM
has been invitational, but this year we're looking for new blood and fresh
takes on our concept. However, we do have space limitations and will
confirm the first 60 artists who respond to this call.

Here's the deal: Deluge wants you to make and donate a record. Not the
music, (unless it's part of your concept and you wish to) just the cover.
Here's your opportunity to honour a disappearing medium and help a worthy
cause. To see your name on the charts. To show the world that fabulous
concept you've been saving for when you finally make it big as a famous
rock star, DJ, lounge crooner or polka king.

Any medium: painting, drawing, photography, collage, sculpture, etc. One
side, two sides, elaborate conceptual creations, whatever. (Note: 3D
sculptural works may have to be freestanding or wall mounted.)

All works submitted will be part of the RPM exhibition, including the gala
opening on Friday, April 10th. (With complimentary thank-you drinks and
exclusive Artists Preview for participants!) All pieces will be for sale at
the ridiculously low flat rate of $45.
Long a local and national bastion of the forward-thinking contemporary art
scene (and home to the internationally acclaimed Antimatter Film Festival).
Deluge Contemporary Art -- formerly Rogue Art -- has produced over 200
exhibitions and shown over 2,000 local, national and international artists
to an audience of more than 300,000 since its inception in 1991, cementing
its role as an inestimable asset and ongoing resource to the arts community
at large.

Please confirm your participation as soon as possible via phone or email.
Photos of completed works for promotional use would be invaluable to us if
received by March 19th (please include photo credit and artist
information). Works can be shipped, to arrive by, or dropped off at the
gallery on Tuesday, April 7th from 4 to 6 pm, or Wednesday, April 8th from
11 am to 1 pm, or by special appointment.

Drawing Game App

Mobil Art School is pleased to announce the release of the mobile drawing game app,
EQ.

EQ is an interactive app inspired by the surrealist drawing game "exquisite corpse".
Users anywhere can connect for fun, creative expression and collaboration through
drawing, painting, images, and writing, allowing users around the world to create
together and produce surprising and unexpected results.

We need creative connections now more than ever. In light of the covid-19 pandemic,
which has resulted in social distancing, self-isolation, school and gallery closures, we
are planning to launch our beta version ahead of schedule. We are hoping it will help
people create and connect during difficult and lonely times.

EQ is created by Mobil Art School Inc. Mobil is based out of Vancouver, Canada, and
aims to broaden access to art through meaningful collaboration and play. It is founded
on the belief that
the world of art and creativity should be available to everyone. We are
developers of what we call creative technology, and provide a comprehensive
organizational team building program in addition to a range of course offerings.

For phase 1 of EQ we have partnered with Living Labs at Emily Carr University of Art
and Design / Shumka Centre for Creative Entrepreneurship.

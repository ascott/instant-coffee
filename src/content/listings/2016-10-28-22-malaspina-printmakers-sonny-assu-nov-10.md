Malaspina Printmakers Society

Upcoming exhibition: Sonny Assu

November 11 to December 11, 2016

Opening reception: November 10, 6PM-9PM

Malaspina Printmakers Society is pleased to announce a solo exhibition by
Sonny Assu.
Sonny Assu (Liwildaxw of the Kwakwaka'wakw nations) and his work often
focus on Indigenous issues and rights, and how the past informs
contemporary ideas and identities. He is developing two new series for his
solo exhibition. One finds Assu's work coming full circle, reinvestigating
his appropriations of consumer pop-culture and nostalgia. The other is
based on the colonial construct of map-making and the imaginary boundaries
that aim to separate.

Artists can now apply to take part in Burnabys Artist in Residence program, an
opportunity to for artists to examine, create and refine their artistic practices in the
tranquil and inspiring natural setting of Deer Lake Park.
Located on the ancestral and unceded territories of the hn"q"min"m" and
Skwxw7mesh speaking peoples, Deer Lake Park provides an environment for
artists to immerse themselves in nature while they focus on their creative endeavors.
Cultural amenities around Deer Lake Park include Shadbolt Centre for the Arts,
Burnaby Art Gallery and Burnaby Village Museum.
Successful applicants will live in accommodations in one of two heritage homes:
Baldwin House, designed in 1963 by Canadian architect Arthur Erickson; and Eagles
Estate, built in 1929 by former owners Drs. Blythe and Violet Eagles. Studios and
community engagement spaces are available at the arts centre, gallery and
museum, and may include
Burnaby libraries, parks and community centres.
Artists across multiple disciplines " visual artists, performers, musicians, land-based
practitioners, poets, writers, potters and more " are encouraged to apply online for
this program. Residencies typically range from two to six weeks and include housing
and/or studio use.
Resident artists may work with the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Art Gallery
and Burnaby Public Library staff to support artist-led community engagement
projects aligned with the requirement of each residency. Alongside housing, resident
artists will have access to rehearsal spaces and studios as well as other resources
to support their creative endeavours.
Application Deadline: March 29, 2025 at 5 pm (PST). Covering residency requests
between March 1, 2026 to December 5, 2026.
Learn more and apply: Burnaby.ca/air or email air@burnaby.ca.

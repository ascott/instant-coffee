CALLING CURIOUS ARTISTS! 
At HCMA Architecture + Design were on a quest to explore whats possible"for 
architecture, for design, for art, for society, and for all points in between. As part of our 
TILT Curiosity Labs initiative, our Artist in Residence (AIR) program is one of our 
experimental forays that arranges a new collaborative relationship between disciplines: 
What happens when artists set up shop in an architecture and design studio?

HOW CAN ARTISTS PROVOKE COLLABORATIVE EXCHANGE WITH OTHER 
CREATIVE DISCIPLINES? 
Were interested in broadening the discourse between disciplines. So, in 2014 we 
launched our Artist in Residence (AIR) program to stimulate discussion and challenge 
preconceptions about architectural and design practice. Since then we have invited 
numerous artists to challenge us, spark our curiosity, help us ask better questions about 
the world around us, and discover new forms of creativity.

FOR MORE INFORMATION VISIT hcma.ca/tilt or email tilt@hcma.ca

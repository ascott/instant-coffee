Program Assistant

The recipient of this position will work both independently and with
a team, and will report to the Director and the Curator. The
recipient will be hired to assist with the ongoing development,
coordination and implementation of Neutral Ground's exhibition
program. The Program Assistant will also be involved with the
development of our 30th year anniversary gallery & exhibition project, 
NG30.

Duration of position: 16 weeks
35 hours a week
Level of Education
University or college students currently enrolled in the field of
fine arts; studio or history,
Additional experience working and volunteering in the arts,
Fund-raising and special events planning will be considered assets 
as
will any new media/multi-media experience.

General Qualifications
Experience with exhibition installation or exhibition management & 
administration
Excellent verbal and written communication skills, computer 
keyboard and Mac environment
Excellent interpersonal and teamwork skills
Ability to perform administrative and coordination tasks
Ability to report on duties and track tasks and statistics

Must be student status, enrolled in a post secondary institution and
returning to school. Must have been registered as a student during
the preceding academic year. Must be a Canadian citizen or 
permanent
resident.

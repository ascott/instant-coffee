You are invited to the: Queer Zone
By Kate Barry
Friday, August 29th, 11-3PM
800 Robson Plaza, Vancouver
(In front of the stairs to the Vancouver Art Gallery - front of the building).

The Queer Zone is an art event for gender non-conforming, 2SLGBTQ+ and trans folks.
Its a public, collaborative, queer performance where we use the aesthetics of play to
create large-scale drawings in marker on canvas. The idea with the Queer Zone is to
decolonialize public space by reclaiming it as safe space for the 2SLGBTQAI+
community. The Queer Zone has different areas where people can contribute to a
drawing about queer joy & queer peace through symbols, writing or through drawing
prompts taken from handmade cue cards.

Thank you City of Vancouver, Placemaking and LIVE! Biennale

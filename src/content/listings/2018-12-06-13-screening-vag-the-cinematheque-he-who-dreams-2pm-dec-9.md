The Vancouver Art Gallery presents The Gifts of Fringe Speaker Series, in celebration 
of Dana Claxton: Fringing the Cube
Film Screening: He Who Dreams
Sunday, December 9 at 2PM at The Cinematheque (1131 Howe Street)
This event is free and open to the public.

Dana Claxtons experimental video He Who Dreams (2013) depicts a surreal journey 
into one mans conflicted psyche while exploring the narrative of identity and politics of 
being a so-called Indian. Rich in meaning and metaphor, her work addresses the trauma 
wrought by the imposition of Western values, customs and systems upon the First 
Nations of North America and the resurgence of an Indigenous cultural presence. 

Several short films complementary to the themes of He Who Dreams will also screen 
the same afternoon. A talk-back with the artists will follow.

See full program below:

Dana Claxton, He Who Dreams (2013 | 52 minutes)

Yasmin Pyarali Karim, Zarra: a film about the particle of Soul (35mm | colour | 4 
minutes)

Karylinn Ming Ho, For the Left Hand Alone (2017 | 9 minutes)

Elizabeth Price, User Group Disco (2009 | 15 minutes)

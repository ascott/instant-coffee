Harald Szeemann Research Project Fellowship

Application deadline: November 1, 2013

A two-year residential post-doctoral grant is being offered as part of 
the Getty Research Institute's Harald Szeemann research project. The 
Harald Szeemann Archive and Library, acquired by the GRI in 2011, 
contains a comprehensive record of Szeemann's correspondence with 
major artists, curators, and scholars from the late 1950s until his death 
in 2005, as well as significant collections of material from the early 20th 
century. We invite scholars interested in any aspect of Szeemann's 
career and archive to apply for this position, which will also contribute 
to the organization of a major conference and publication about 
Szeemann and his impact.

Click here for more information on Harald Szeemann and the 
collection. 

Eligibility

Applications are welcome from scholars of all nationalities. 

Current Getty staff and members of their immediate family are not 
eligible for this fellowship. 

Applicants must not have received their degree earlier than 2008.

Terms
The fellowship provides a workspace at the Getty Research Institute, 
an apartment in the Getty scholar housing complex, and airfare to and 
from Los Angeles. These terms apply as of October 2013 and are 
subject to future changes. 

Application Availability and Deadline for all Fellowships
Application materials are accepted through an online application 
process. The deadline for this fellowship is 6:00 p.m. (PDT) on 
November 1, 2013. 

Notification
Applicants are notified of the Getty Research Institute's decision within 
six months following the deadline. 

How To Apply
Applicants must have access to a Web browser that allows cookies. 
We strongly recommend use of the the following browser versions (or 
later): Internet Explorer 8; Mozilla Firefox 3.6.1; Safari 5; Google 
Chrome 5. 

Part 1:
Applicants are required to complete and submit the online application, 
which includes completing a short information section and uploading a 
Project Proposal, Doctoral Dissertation Plan or Abstract, Curriculum 
Vitae, Writing Sample, Selected Bibliography, and Confirmation Letter 
of Academic Status by 6:00 p.m. (PDT) November 1, 2013.

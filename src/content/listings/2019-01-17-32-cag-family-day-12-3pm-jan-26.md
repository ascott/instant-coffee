Family Day| Flocking

Saturday, January 26, 12-3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson Street, V6B 6R5 

On the last Saturday of each month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short exhibition 
tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions.

Responding to the work of Anne Low and her explorations of historic decorative arts, 
experiment with flocking powder to create your own textured artwork inspired by flocked 
wallpaper designs from the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries.

No reservation required. Simply just turn up!

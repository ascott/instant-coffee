The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is currently accepting proposals for 2020. 

The deadline to apply is Thursday, February 28, 5 pm. 

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is an artist-run centre located in Kelowna, 
BC. Since 1989 the Alternator has shown the work of emerging Canadian and 
international artists with an experimental approach, particularly those engaged in social 
and cultural issues.

For more information or to apply, visit: https://www.alternatorcentre.com/submissions-
maingallery/

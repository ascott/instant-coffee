Upcoming Workshop and Social Event Details!

StudioHour: Meet Up & Make: VOL 3: Custom Mold Making

Join us this Sunday, September 21st to learn how to create your own custom molds
from scratch and connect with other creative minds!

Imagine creating your very own custom silicone mold: for crafting, ceramics, candles
or soaps, and we will provide food safe options for those interested in making
chocolates, candies or even fancy ice for cocktails! There will be a social,
snacks, and refreshment included with your custom mold you make on the day! This
workshop is beginner friendly and well be there to guide you every step of the way!

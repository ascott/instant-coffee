Curated by Haema Sivanesan
28 October 2017 - 1 April 2018
Art Gallery of Greater Victoria

This exhibition takes the site of Nootka, in the traditional territories of the Nuu-chah-nulth 
peoples, on the Pacific coast of Vancouver Island, as a case study. It considers how 
artists have contributed to shaping ideas of place on the West Coast. Nootka was the 
site of first contact between Indigenous and non-Indigenous peoples, and as such, has 
been a subject in art since the 18th century. Juxtaposing works by Indigenous and non-
Indigenous artists, this exhibition looks at place as a cultural imaginary. It features works 
by artists including Patrick Amos, Emily Carr, Stan Douglas, Jock Macdonald, Tim Paul, 
Takao Tanabe, Art Thompson, John Webber and Hjalmer Wenstob.

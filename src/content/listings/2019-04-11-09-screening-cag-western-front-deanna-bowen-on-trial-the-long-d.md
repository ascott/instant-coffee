Artist Talk | Deanna Bowen | Screening and Discussion

Thursday, April 11, 7pm, free

Western Front, 303 East 8th Ave, V5T 1S1

Join us for a special screening and discussion of Bowens edited cut of On Trial The 
Long Doorway (2017), co-commissioned by CAG and Mercer Union, Toronto, and 
produced with support from the Western Front.

The film focuses on a lost 1956 CBC teledrama titled The Long Doorway, in which 
Bowens great uncle Herman Risby played a supporting role. It tells the story of a Black 
legal aid lawyer tasked with representing a white University of Toronto student charged 
with violently assaulting a rising Black basketball player.

The screening will be followed with a talk by Bowen, taking up familial connection points 
and a passion for uncovering repressed histories and traumas of Black and White 
Canadian histories.

Find out more about the exhibition at cag.contemporaryartgallery.ca/deannabowen

Brick Press is pleased to host:

Mountain Moves

Publication Launch + Readings by Ginger Carlson, Catherine de Montreuil, Tao Fei and 
Ryley O'Byrne
Brick Press
247 Main Street
Sunday, November 25th @ 4:00 PM

"too blessed to be stressed!"

Readings:
an excerpt from 'The Wasteland Footnotes' by Ryley O'Byrne
'The Geo Wyeth Welt' by Tao Fei
'you hold me, as I fumble to emulate your care' by Ginger Carlson
an excerpt from 'Return of the Peregrine' by Catherine de Montreuil

Join us Sunday, November 25th for an afternoon of readings, and the launch of 
Mountain Moves; a publication featuring new writings by Jenna Swift, Maeve Hanna, 
Erik Benjamins, Tao Fei, Ryley O'Byrne, Andrew Berardini, Daniella Sanader, Ginger 
Carlson and Catherine de Montreuil. All works were written, edited and/or mused upon 
at the foot of Sleeping Buffalo in Banff, Alberta during the Creative Writing Ensemble III 
residency at Banff Centre for Arts and Creativity.

Publications will be available for sale by cash only.
Refreshments will be served.

**Please note: Space is very limited, and will largely be standing room only. There will 
be a few chairs available for those who need.

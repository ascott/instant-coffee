March 18, 7 PM - 11 PM
221A E. Georgia St.

221A hosts an evening with Robin Selk and Jean Brazeau of Deep Blue.

In anticipation of a collaborative archival project with 221A, Selk and Brazeau will 
speculate on alternative modes of archiving that attempt to capture the 
performative, experiential, and collective practices of Deep Blue.

Named after the IBM chess-playing computer, Deep Blue is a non-profit space 
dedicated to the thinking machine, thinking about machines, and thinking with 
machines. Its output is registered in sequences, frequencies, and oscillations 
purposed to move bodies in space and space in bodies. Within a community in 
step with electronic music, Deep Blue has become a centre for the production 
and distribution of a variety of local and international practitioners of 
contemporary dance music.

This event is presented in collaboration with the Vancouver Art Gallery as part of 
the Vancouver Special: Ambivalent Pleasures Public Program with the support of 
the British Columbia Arts Council.

This event is also programmed as part of Spring Fever: Vancouver Independent 
Archives 2017.

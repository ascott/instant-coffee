When?

Submissions accepted during gallery hours (Mon-Fri 10AM-5PM, Sat + Sun 11AM-
5PM) from NOW until 5PM, Sunday Dec. 11.

Where?

Drop off at Malaspina Printmakers Society, 1555 Duranleau St.

Malaspina is hosting a mini print holiday sale in the gallery! We are looking for small, 
framed, ready to hang prints that are no larger than 10 x 15 cm. Support local 
artists, artist run centres, and the giving of excellent gifts.

The sale will work the same way that our consignment does, so sold prints will 
receive a portion of the sales. Submissions with dimensions, a gift giving price point, 
an image of the work or questions regarding can be directed 
gabi(@)malaspinaprintmakers.com

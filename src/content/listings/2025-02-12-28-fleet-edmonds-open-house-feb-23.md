FLEET: Edmonds

Intersection of Humphries Ave and Rosewood St, Burnaby

February 23rd, 2025 at 1-3 PM and 4-8 PM

Join us for an Open House with Kay Slater at FLEET: Edmonds! This Open House
day will include a Tactile Illustration Workshop with the Temporary Tactile
Arts
Collective from 1-3 PM, and an Open House and artist "talk-back" from 4-8PM. 4-5
PM is mask-on in the FLEET.

The Tactile Illustration Workshop aims to explore and share the intersection of tactile
art, accessibility, and spatial wayfinding. This event, facilitated in collaboration with
Kaymi Nakashima-Ochoa and Johnny Tai as part of the Temporary Tactile Arts
Collective, invites participants to engage with tactile illustration techniques to create
accessible representations of space and place.

The Open House and artist talk-back serves as a culminating moment to share the
work developed during the residency at FLEET: Edmonds while amplifying the
practices of non-verbal artists in the community. This event is designed to provide an
inclusive and engaging space for reflection, dialogue, and visibility for diverse
creative practices. Kay will showcase the results of their month-long exploration into
accessible exhibition design, radical silence, and wayfinding, inviting visitors
to
engage with their findings and contribute feedback.

The Open House will include a special sunset watch party, allowing for a quiet
moment of reflection and connection, reinforcing themes of radical silence and
community presence. This evening creates a space for dialogue, collaboration, and
celebration of the residencys outcomes while promoting the value of non-verbal
interaction and creative access within the arts. By inviting the community to witness
and participate, Kay hopes to spark lasting conversations about inclusivity,
accessibility, and non-verbal communication in public and creative spaces.

This program is free and open to the public.

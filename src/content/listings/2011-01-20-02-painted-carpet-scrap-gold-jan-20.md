PAINTED CARPET

presents

SCRAP GOLD

A collection of profound artistic modalities by artist Logan Sturrock,
implemented by Chris Boni.

These multi-originary 3-D realm-based-and-nominated sculpturally
cognized products for our sensorial pleasure rupture, through the
artistically nominated placement of themselves in the orbit of
culture, our vernacularly composed space/time postost-modern
orbits.

Rescued from the regurgitated past-times of most modern day
users, the formidably and tantalizingly deployed architectures of the
derelict are anthropologically one half found rescued space-time
formulaic transgressive deterritorialized edited collections from a
convoluted phenomenology in our 21st century, and another half
executed supersensible Kantian subjectivity.

In a breath, Logan Sturrock is assembling a feast for all cognitive
receptors, and under the guidance and implementation of Chris
Boni, the exhibition SCRAP GOLD will make you pan the gutters on
your way to the next Canucks match…no doubt. Come buy Art or
juiced beet & ginger shots.
Please. And thank you.

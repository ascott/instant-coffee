Exhibition themes operate around the notion that our perception of the world is
influenced by mechanisms that extend, enhance, or reinterpret subjectivity. Such
themes invite the viewer to consider a number of questions. How do mediating
structures affect our sense of place? What defines experiences across media?
In what way can aesthetic artifacts be transposed into different modes of
experience? What if objects were liberated from their original context to become
an end in themselves?

GRANT AND DEVELOPMENT COORDINATOR

Reporting directly to Capture Photography Festivals Executive Director and Chief
Curator, the Grant and Development Coordinator researches, develops and writes
grants for the Festival and supports all development and philanthropic activities
including researching and preparing sponsor, donor and foundation applications and
collateral plus supporting development events. This role is a 28 hr/week position.

ABOUT CAPTURE:

Capture seeks to connect Vancouver to the world through lens-based art. Our
mission is to:

- Act as a platform to expand visual literacy through lens-based art;

- Connect communities to incite meaningful dialogue between artists, curators,
audiences, organizations and institutions;

- Give voice to traditionally underrepresented communities; and

- Present compelling, urgent lens-based art exhibitions annually.

DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

Reporting to and supervised by Captures Executive Director, the Grant and
Development Coordinator is responsible for:

- Researching and writing potential grant, foundation and sponsor applications;
- Drafting texts for grant applications including statistics;
- Managing Captures grant calendar to ensure deadlines are met;
- Liaising with grant officers, foundations, government agencies and sponsors;
- Tracking and preparing final reports for grants, foundations, sponsors and donors;
- Support the development of budgets and
coordinate the finalization of budgets; and
- Supporting the Executive Director in nurturing donor and sponsor relations;
- Supporting the Executive Director in forecasting, planning donor and sponsor
events;
- Maintaining contact lists for donors and sponsors;
- Preparing collateral for sponsor and donor solicitation and events;
- Attend Festival events in the month of April; and
- Other duties related to grant, foundation and sponsor applications and
engagement.

ESSENTIAL KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS AND ATTRIBUTES

- Degree in fine arts, liberal arts, curatorial studies or related arts
- Knowledge of and strong interest in contemporary art
- Interest and engagement with local arts community
- Experience in grant writing
- Exceptional written and verbal communication skills.
- Exemplary organization skills
- Exceptional attention to detail
- Knowledge of professional practices and standards in the field
- Demonstrated ability to work flexibly
- Proactive thinker
- Excellent planning and time
management, with the ability to multitask, and prioritize
within a busy working environment
- Capacity to work calmly under pressure
- Ability to work collaboratively both with a small internal team of colleagues and a
diverse array of external stakeholders including artists, curators, sponsors, and
donors

DESIRABLE SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE
- Knowledge of local, national and international artists and arts organizations,
particularly as relates to lens-based practices
- Experience working for a not for profit
- Knowledge of marketing and audience development in the arts

Title: Grant and Development Coordinator

Reporting to: Executive Director and Chief Curator

Contract Terms: $30/hr, preferably Monday-Thursday; some flexibility will be required
for occasional evening events, particularly during the month of April.

Work Location: Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. Please note the office is,
unfortunately, not wheelchair accessible but accommodations will be made for those
with
mobility issues.

Interview Dates: August, 2024.

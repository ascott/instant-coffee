As part of the October edition of "Platforms 2020: Public Works", the City of Vancouver
Public Art Program is pleased to present artworks on transit shelter, billboards and screens
throughout the city by the artists Laurie M Landry, Wen
Wen Lu, Rina Lyshaug, Lindsay
McIntyre, Jayce Salloum and Chrystal Sparrow.

The projects presented were selected through an open call inviting Vancouver-based artists
to share artworks that they have been working on that respond to our current circumstances
of living during a pandemic and critical time of change.

For more information about "Platforms 2020: Public Works" visit

www.vancouver.ca/platforms

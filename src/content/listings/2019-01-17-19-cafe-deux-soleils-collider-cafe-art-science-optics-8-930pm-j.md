Collider Cafe: Art. Science. Optics.
When: Wednesday, January 23, 8-9:30pm (doors open at 7:30pm)
Where: Cafe Deux Soleils (2096 Commercial Drive, Vancouver, BC V5N 4B2)
Event info: [http://bit.ly/ArtSciOptics](http://bit.ly/ArtSciOptics)
Website: [https://curiositycollider.org/events/collider-cafe-art-science-optics/](https://curiositycollider.org/events/collider-cafe-art-science-optics/)

From contemporary art to science illustration, is "seeing" really "believing"? Or is 
there more to it than there seems? How can we explore the possibilities through art 
and science?

#ColliderCafe is a space for artists, scientists, makers, and anyone interested in 
art+science. Meet. Discover. Connect. Create. Are you curious? Join us at "Collider 
Cafe: Art. Science. Optics." to explore how art and science intersect in the 
exploration of curiosity.
What our eyes perceive but we do not see | Annie Briard, Contemporary Artist
The Museum as Muse: natural history collections as a resource for artistic 
exploration | Catherine Stewart, Visual Artist
Paleoart: A Mutualism of Endeavors | Julius T. Csotonyi, Paleoart, Natural History 
and Scientific Illustration
Manifest Obscura: Reimagining/reimaging landscape through microbial collaboration 
| Ramey Newell, Photographer/Film Maker/Artist
The Art of Science & Medical Illustration | Vicky Earle, Medical and Scientific 
Illustrator
Proceeds will be used to cover the cost of running this event, and to fund future 
Curiosity Collider events. Curiosity Collider is a registered BC non-profit 
organization.

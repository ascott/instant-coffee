________________________________

Outsiders and Others is a non-profit art society with a focus on bringing non-traditional
artists to the forefront. This includes outsider, folk, self-taught, visionary, intuitive,
and
artists with disabilities.

We opened in March 2020 at the beginning of the Covid-19 pandemic in order to bring
new art and culture to our community on a regular basis when no one else was. We
knew we had to do this to help us all survive the world standing still.

In our first year we have worked hard to produce a new exhibition every month;
generally featuring artists that have never shown their work before; have sold over 75
pieces of original art; and we paid the artists an exhibition honourarium. We need your
help so we can do more.

We are a window art gallery so we have our lights on 24 hours a day, 7 days a week,
that includes a QR code you can scan with your phone and go to our website.

This month we are celebrating our first anniversary with a special exhibition featuring
artists from all of our previous exhibitions, and having this fundraiser.

We are raising money for paying artist honorariums, gallery rent, insurance, and all
expenses related to running our
non-profit art society.

All the auction items have been donated by supportive artists and organizations who
understand the value of giving underserved artists the opportunity to have their artwork
seen. We cant thank them enough for caring and choosing to support our fundraiser.

Outsiders and Others is located at 716 East Hastings Street in Vancouver.

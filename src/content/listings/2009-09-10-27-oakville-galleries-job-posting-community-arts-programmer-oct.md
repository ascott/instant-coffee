Oakville Galleries 
JOB POSTING – COMMUNITY ARTS PROGRAMMER

Terms of position: Contract, full-time (28–35 hours/week)
Duration: 3 years
Start date: Immediately
Language of work: English

Oakville Galleries is a leading public art museum where contemporary art is the impetus for 
advancing imagination and self-reflection. Through two sites, the Galleries serves communities in
Oakville, Halton Region and wider audiences nationally and internationally. We are currently 
looking to add a Community Arts Programmer to our dynamic education team. 

Nature and Scope of Position 

The Community Arts Programmer will assist in the implementation of a three-year plan of art-
based workshops and special projects in various spaces and places in Oakville. Exploring art as a
tool for social and civic engagement, the Community Arts Programmer will support the Director of 
Education and Public Programmes in creating learning opportunities for under-served and/or 
marginalized groups such as youth, seniors, and newcomers, and engages with Oakville’s 
community of artists and artisans. 

General responsibilities and functions

· Coordinating the design, delivery and evaluation of an innovative slate of community-based 
programmes, including off-site projects and in-service workshops
· Researching, establishing and maintaining a network of programme partners and participants
· Overseeing all outreach and promotion for community-based programmes
· Contributing to broader audience development efforts at Oakville Galleries

Qualifications

The ideal candidate has:
· post-secondary training in Art History, Fine Art, Cultural Studies, Art Education or a related 
discipline
· proven expertise in contemporary art and community arts practices
· excellent communication and outreach skills
· strong organizational skills, detail-oriented focus and the ability to manage multiple priorities
· excellent interpersonal and relationship management skills 
· the ability to work both independently and in a team environment
· a valid driver’s license and access to a vehicle

Second language skills are an asset.

Oakville Galleries is an equal opportunity employer. We thank all applicants for their interest; 
however, only those selected for an interview will be contacted.

Please submit applications to Catherine Sicot, Director of Education and Public Programmes at 
catherine@oakvillegalleries.com. 

Application deadline: 2 October 2009

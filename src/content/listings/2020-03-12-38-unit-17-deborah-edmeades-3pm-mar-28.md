"Illegitimate Wisdom: unfounded enthusiasms; fraudulent texts (A Short Introduction)"

A talk by Deborah Edmeades

Saturday 28 March 3pm

As a show and tell, Edmeades will address some of the meaning behind her work
currently in the gallery: the narrative depicted in the transparency; its source as one of
the founding documents of the Enlightenment; and the light through which we perceive
the accompanying objects.

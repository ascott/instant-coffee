Come party with the Queer Arts Festival on February 14 for our winter fundraiser, 
Bleeding Hearts & Artists, graciously hosted by Bruce Munro Wright and Paul Wong at 
Bruce's elegant home in Vancouver. The soire will feature a silent auction and 
performances by Frdrik Robert and Rachel Kiyo Iwaasa, Cease Wyss, and other 
fabulous artists, with MC David C. Jones. All funds raised will go directly to produce our 
2017 festival, UnSettled.

Silent auction items include artwork by Attila Richard Lukacs, Angela Grossmann, Dana 
Claxton, Douglas Coupland, Paul Wongto name a fewincluding a Two-Spirit 
inspired piece created especially for the occasion by Lawrence Paul Yuxweleptun. You 
can also bid on tickets to some of the hottest events in the city, including Vancouver 
Queer Film Festival, Vancouver Opera Festival, Vancouver Recital Society.

Bleeding Hearts & Artists
Tuesday February 14, 8pm
At the home of Bruce Munro Wright (address available upon RSVP)

Tickets $35

Check our event page for more about the silent auction items and Absentee Bidding: 
http://queerartsfestival.com/event/bleeding-hearts-artists/

Recognized as one of the top 5 festivals of its kind worldwide, the Queer Arts Festival is 
an annual artist-run multidisciplinary festival at the Roundhouse in Vancouver, BC. This 
year's festival UnSettled is Two-Spirit curated and developed in collaboration with 
Indigenous arts organizations and artists. UnSettled runs June 17-29, 2017.

PoMoArts Ceramic Artist in Residence is a core part of our commitment to nurturing
artistic excellence and fostering cultural development in the ceramic arts. Designed
for emerging ceramic artists, this 6"7-month residency combines on-site studio
practice with mentorship, research, and community engagement, offering a flexible
yet immersive experience tailored to todays creative professionals.

Throughout the residency, emphasis is placed on hands-on making, artistic
exploration, teaching, public engagement and includes a solo exhibition. PoMoArts
ceramic studios are active, collaborative, and supportive environments that host a
wide range of programs, including adult classes in wheel throwing, hand building,
and sculpture, as well as youth programs and vibrant student studio sessions.

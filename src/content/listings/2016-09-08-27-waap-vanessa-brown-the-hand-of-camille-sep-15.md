VANESSA BROWN
The Hand of Camille

Reception: Thurs, Sept 15, 7-9 p.m.
Exhibition: Sept 16 - Oct 15, 2016

Please note our new location: 688 E. Hastings St. (lower level), 
Vancouver
For a preview of the works, contact wil@waapart.com or +1 778 
229 3458
www.waapart.com

The Hand of Camille is a reflection on the often invisible labour that 
comprises art and exhibition making. Reflecting on Brown's own 
work as an artist, her work history as a preparator and artist 
assistant as well as the historical example of Camille Claudel, this 
exhibition reinserts the body attached to the hand that produces.

VANESSA BROWN works in sculpture, painting and photography 
and is based in Vancouver on unceded Coast Salish Territories 
(CST). She graduated with a BFA from Emily Carr University in 
2013 and was the recipient of the Chancellor's Award. She has 
exhibited in Canada, Germany, and the USA. She is represented by 
Wil Aballe Art Projects.

ONLINE 6-WEEK INTENSIVE PROGRAM - AFTER ART SCHOOL: A SIX WEEK
INTENSIVE DESIGNED TO SUPPORT NEWLY GRADUATED AND EMERGING ARTISTS
AS THEY BUILD PROFESSIONAL ARTS PRACTICES | May 15th- June 27th | $125 |
PRESENTED BY THE GATHERED GALLERY, WEEKLY SESSIONS EACH THURSDAY
7:30- 9PM EST, HOSTED ON ZOOM

This six-week intensive program is built for newly
graduating and emerging artists as they
build professional arts practices. After Art School has been thoughtfully curated with
feedback from artists across careers and experiences to create conversations, spark
inspiration, and demystify the art world. This program creates opportunity for knowledge
sharing and connection for artists at similar places in their careers.

Weekly Topics:
Week 1: Artist Essentials
Week 2: Portfolios & Studio Systems
Week 3: Applications 101 with Daniel Rumbolt
Week 4: Platforms & Galleries with Alissa Sexton
Week 5: Money & Mindset with Nicole Hand
Week 6: Creative Careers Panel (TBD)

Each week will include:

Access to a virtual community
Resources, examples, and information on the weekly topic
Opportunities for questions and answers
Weekly live Zoom Session Thursday evenings 7:30pm -9pm EST (run by Emily & Natalie
and/or invited professionals)
Prompting questions on the topic for discussion and reflection

Peripheral Review is a new website based out of Vancouver dedicated to
documentation and critical reviews of Vancouver-area art exhibitions,
performances (art, music, dance, theatre) and events. We are currently
looking for writers and artists who would be interested in documenting
Vancouver's independent art scene.

We are looking for:
-Critical reviews of Vancouver-area art exhibitions, performances (art,
music, dance, theatre) and events (650-1500 words max.) Must include title
of the show, venue name and full names of artists.
-Artistic responses to art exhibitions and performances
- -poems/creative writing
- -drawings/sketches/collages/creative photography/digital media (GIFF,
collage, etc.)
- -recorded descriptions/soundscapes
- -other: propose it to us!
-Short reviews/descriptions of exhibitions/performances/events (200-300
words max.) Must include title of the show, venue name and full names of
artists.

Any submission MUST include an image of the exhibition/performance/event
for reference for the published piece, with photo credit (Artist Name,
title of work/exhibition title, year. Photo/Image credit: name.)
-JPEG format, if possible submit high and low quality versions
-if no photo attached please insert link for appropriate source/website, etc.

Also include your preferred social media handles, link to website, short
bio (150 words max), and if you have previously submitted article/response
anywhere else

Currently, as we are grassroots and unfounded, we can offer no monetary
compensation for submission. We will work at getting our reviewers tickets
to events in the future, show passes, etc.

All general and submission inquiries please send to: peripheralreview@gmail.com

With all submissions please format according to the submission guidelines.
For general inquires please note your query in the subject line.

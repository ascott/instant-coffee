This course examines 20th-century Jewish artists and their influence on the art world,
shaped by their
diverse lived experiences as Jews from around the globe. We will
explore how these artists convey intense personal narratives and evoke powerful
reactions from viewers. Ultimately these works represent the processes of
transformation and recovery, offering a profound articulation of the Jewish spirit.
Lecturer: Faye Fayerman

Torrie Groening

Sanfrancouver

February 14- March 7, 2009

The Atelier Gallery is pleased to present an exhibition of recent work by
San Francisco artist Torrie Groening which will be on view February 14th to
March 7th, 2009 . There will be an artist's reception Saturday February
14, 2-4 p.m.

Groening is well known locally as a talented artist and accomplished
printmaker. In 1989 she established Prior Editions Studio and until her
departure from Vancouver in 1999 worked with many artists including Gathie
Falk, Toni Onley, Gordon Smith, Jack Shadbolt and Takao Tanabe.

These large format prints explore her interest in both digital media and
the still life genre.

I work on the still life images like stage sets the objects, the actors
and myself, the director. Objects are chosen for their evocative sense and
may take on a new understanding when linked by proximity to another.
Torrie Groening

Staged still lives are recorded then digitally manipulated incorporating
her own work done in other mediums and selected images from other sources.
The completed pieces invoke a response from all the senses and take digital
images into a new, exciting and uncharted territory.

Additional photos and interviews available upon request.

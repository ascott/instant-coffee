Retail and Publications Coordinator

About
The Contemporary Art Gallery (CAG) is an independent, non-profit public art gallery and
one of Canadas leading venues for contemporary art. Offering free access to everyone,
we work with artists from Canada and around the world to produce exhibitions, off-site
projects, residencies, learning, outreach, community engagement programs, events and
publications. Each of these interdependent strands of programming varies in scale,
duration and location to challenge how and where we might engage with contemporary
visual art.

Job Summary

CAG is seeking a skilled and driven Retail and Publications Coordinator to join our
dynamic institution. Reporting to the Operations Administrator and working closely with
the Curatorial and Communications departments, the Retail and Publications
Coordinator will be situated in Front of House and responsible for driving sales and
outreach of CAG publications, artist editions and merchandise.

Key Responsibilities

Reports to the Operations Administrator and responsible for shop sales,
publication distribution strategies, edition production and library coordination

Oversees and coordinates all retail aspects of the CAG Shop, including in-store
and online sales, displays and visual merchandising, discounts and special promotions,
e-commerce, packaging and shipping

Coordinates and facilitates publication exchanges, distribution, consignments and
outreach

Coordinates the
production of CAGs artist editions

Oversees front of house operations on weekends and is the weekend contact and
staff support person for volunteers

Keeps accurate inventory counts and data management and oversees annual
publication inventory procedures

Coordinates the Abraham Rogatnick Library, including management of logbooks,
cataloguing and overseeing the work of the library volunteers

Coordinates and oversees CAGs sales and returns policy

Skills and abilities

Excellent written and verbal communications skills
Outstanding customer service and working with the general public
Understanding of visual merchandising and driving sales
Confidence with Wordpress and e-commerce platforms
Confidence handling artist editions, packing and shipping
Experience working with inventory and auditors
Comprehensive computer and technology skills
Experience with library operations
Confidence leading and supervising front of house operations
Ability to prioritize and problem solve in high
pressure situations

Qualifications and attributes

University degree in the arts, publishing or related discipline. Minimum one to three
years working in retail, front of house operations and customer service. Front of house
experience within a museum or gallery setting, knowledge of contemporary art, and
experience in library science will be a considerable asset. Excellent customer service
and people relations skills are essential to the role.

Deadline to apply

Monday, January 20, 2020 at 5pm

Please send your resume and cover letter in one PDF to Shayla Perreault at
employment@contemporaryartgallery.ca

$17.00 per hour

This is a permanent, part-time position of 24 hours per week that includes extended

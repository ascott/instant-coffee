Art Metropole is seeking an Executive Director

Art Metropole is seeking an Executive Director with vision who will 
lead the organization in an exciting period of renewal. Art Metropole 
is an internationally renowned artist-run centre that promotes the 
appreciation and importance of conceptually based art. Founded by 
General Idea in 1974, Art Metropole fosters a dynamic network of 
artist-initiated publishing in any media, especially those formats pre-
disposed to sharing and circulation. Art Metropole specializes in the 
contextualization and dissemination of artist’s books, multiples, and 
related materials with an emphasis on innovative formats and 
emergent technologies.

The Executive Director will help shape and implement an expanded 
vision of Art Metropole’s role as a prominent advocate and 
distributor of concept driven artistic activity, in collaboration with a 
committed, active Board of Directors and an experienced staff. The 
Executive Director will be capable of executing quality artistic 
programming that builds upon Art Metropole's historical legacy while 
re-envisioning it in the context of the digital age.

The Executive Director will actively research and execute fundraising 
initiatives and be able to create and maintain strong relationships 
with private and public partners. As the leader of a team of four staff, 
the Executive Director will have exceptional governance and 
administrative skills indispensable to overseeing the day-to-day 
operations of a public shop and exhibition space with a strong 
online presence. The Executive Director will engage in community 
outreach, sustaining the long-lasting, positive relationships Art 
Metropole has with artists, collectors, curators and the public.

This challenging and exciting position promises an ambitious, hard-
working individual the opportunity to engage with a sophisticated 
international art community and to make a significant contribution to 
contemporary art by leading Art Metropole into the future.

Essential skills for this position include:
- Excellent management and organizational leadership skills
- Keen engagement with new and old forms of artist-initiated 
publishing and distribution
- Demonstrated ability in grant writing and fundraising, including 
corporate sponsorship and donor relations
- Experience with strategic planning and policy development
- Practical knowledge of Internet and digital technologies
- Substantial experience with non-profit artist-run centres
- Retail management experience an asset

Permanent full-time, 32+ hours/week
Remuneration: $40,000

Please submit a letter of interest, a current curriculum vitae, a short 
sample (1-2 page excerpt) of relevant writing, and 3 references by

OPENING
MNP Tower

Kate Metten
Work House

February 29, 5-8pm
1021 W Hastings

In collaboration with the BC Achievement Foundation and MNP Tower, Kate Metten
Ceramics proudly presents Work House: an immersive wheel throwing performance
unveiling an evolving exhibition of meticulously crafted ceramics by Vancouver artist
Kate
Metten. As the esteemed recipient of the 2023 Judson Beaumont Award for
Applied Art and Design, Kate will grace the venue with live demonstrations infusing
public space with creative energy and education about ceramics. Witness the
transformation of raw clay into functional vessels as the exhibition wall will evolve
dynamically over March with work made on site. Don't miss the opportunity to marvel
at this enchanting process and meet the artist by visiting during office hours March 4-8.
Plus, take home a piece of this extraordinary journey with all ceramics and oil paintings
available for sale.

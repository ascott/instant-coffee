The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Pride at the Gallery on August 2, 5pm " 9pm

Regular admission applies: $24 General | $18 Student + Senior | Free for Members!

In partnership with Vancouver Pride Society, the Vancouver Art Gallery presents a night of 
creativity, fun and connection in celebration of LGTBQ2+. The popular event Me Love 
BINGO! will be hosted in the Rooftop Pavilion as one of two themed, licensed bars. Part 
variety hour, part game night and part immersive theatre, Me Love BINGO! is created by 
Kyle Loven, a Vancouver-based performance and visual artist. Dressed in drag as the 
nights bingo host extraordinaire, Loven will provide endless entertainment with games, 
performance numbers and prizes. At a second themed bar, guests will be invited to indulge 
their creativity with communal mural painting as part of Paint & Drink! in the Annex 
Workshop. Stationed throughout the four floors of the Gallery will be Queer Living Library. 
Guests will hear from Orene Askew aka DJ O Show, the two spirited activist named a 2019 
Pride Parade Grand Marhal, as well as painter Leanne Christie, Shanique Kelly aka DJ 
Softieshan, photography artist Tom Hsu and Gallery owner Wil Aballe of Will Aballe Art 
Projects (WAAP).

Included with admission, visitors will have access to the Gallerys summer exhibitions and 
are invited to discover works aligned with explorations of gender and sexuality as presented 
in Vikky Alexander: Extreme Beauty, Robert Rauschenberg 1965"1980, Moving Still: 
Performative Photography in India and Views of the Collection: The Street.

For tickets and info, visit: vanartgallery.bc.ca

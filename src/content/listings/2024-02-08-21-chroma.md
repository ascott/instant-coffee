Hori7ons
March 7-10
at The Kent 534 Cambie St, Vancouver, BC V6B 2N7

Hori7ons, hosted by the artist-run Chroma Link Collective is a group digital art
exhibition featuring an immersive video installation with interactive elements.

This event, held at The Kent from March 7-10 and open to all ages, showcases the
works of 7 local artists exploring the impact of technology on culture.

Visitors can expect a vivid, engaging digital experience, blending art and interactive
technology in a uniquely curated space.

The program begins with an Opening Reception on March 7th, including a forum
discussion with the artists and a performance by Miki Aurora.

For the following 3
days, the works will be on view for drop-in visits, looping every 30
minutes.

The exhibition concludes with a Closing Reception on Sunday, March 10th
accompanied by a DJ performance and celebrations.

Participating artists:
Carmilla Sumantry
Saida Saetgar
Guile Twardowski
Kevin Holliday

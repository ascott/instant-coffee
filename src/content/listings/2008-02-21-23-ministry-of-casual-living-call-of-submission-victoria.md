The Ministry of Casual Living is accepting submissions for its modest, but accommodating exhibition space. Exhibitions are two weeks in duration. The Ministry of Casual Living is a non-profit artist run space that has been exhibiting new experimental artwork, hosting screenings, fundraisers, and anything fun that fits in a backyard, since 2002. Visit the website below to see the floorplan and gallery images.

The Ministry is also seeking donations to its library. Beginning soon, the gallery will host open library hours on Saturdays from 12-5. But first we need to develop our collection of art related materials (Book, Catalogues, Photocopies, Magazines, etc.) Donations can be picked up in Vancouver or Victoria, and will be greatly appreciated, and of course used well. E-mail to arrange pick-up.

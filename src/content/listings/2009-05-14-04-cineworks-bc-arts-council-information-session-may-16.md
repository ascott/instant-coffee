Cineworks is pleased to host an information session from the BC Arts Council on Saturday, 16 May 
2009 at 1pm at the new Cineworks Analogue Annex space in the historic Ironworks building.

Walter Quan, Coordinator for the Arts Awards Programs, will speak about recent changes to the 
PROJECT ASSISTANCE FOR MEDIA ARTISTS [Deadline: 31 May 2009] and will be available to 
answer questions about the application process and your applications.

Come by to say hello or to pick up an application package.

Please contact Programs Manager + Curator cheyanne turions for more information at 
604.685.3841.

Calling All Artists!
The Langley Arts Council invites you to apply to BC Wide Landscape Competition,
Exhibition, and Live Painting Event!
In this call, artists are welcome to enter three works for consideration in the first round
of judging through the application. All artists will be considered for both the exhibition
and the painting competition. After applications are received, 9 artists will be chosen to
compete in this live landscape painting competition. The rest of the artists will be
showcased in the exhibition alongside the competition.

Application Deadline: July 15th , 2024
Exhibition Dates: August 21, 2024 - September
30, 2024
Location: The Timms Community Center 20399 Douglas Cres, Langley BC V3A 4B3.
Competition Date: Saturday, September 7, 2024
Location: Williams Park (located in the Township of Langley 6596-6726 238th St.
Langley)

For more information or to apply, visit our website at
https://www.langleyarts.ca/submit/landscape-competition

EVANN SIEBENS
EXHIBITION OPENING AND BOOK LAUNCH

Please
join us for a presentation of recent photographic collage work by Evann Siebens,
resulting from her bodies of work Plus TheCo, Minus Helen Goodwin, initially presented
in its original iteration at the Belkin Gallery in the summer of 2018, and her recent
performance work, Gesture by Number, which was first presented at A Performance
Affair in Brussels, September 2019. This ever-evolving piece will be presented at the
Witte de With in Rotterdam in Feburary 2020.

Wil Aballe Art Projects is pleased to announce the publication of The Indexical,
Alphabetized, Mediated, Archival Dance-a-Thon! This volume, realized with publication
design by Courtenay Webber and The Future and authored by Sunshine Frre and
Brynn Catherine McNab, is a companion work to the exhibition at the gallery in February
2016 of the same name. Distributed through Art Metropole, the book will be released in
Vancouver on November 14th 2019 and in Europe at Moveable Types, a curatorial
project by Brussels-based curator, Frances Horn, in February 2020 in Rotterdam at the
Witte de With museum. The book measures and is available in an edition of 300 only.

Sunshine Frres text There is More than One Way to Learn the Alphabet was
originally published as a monograph by WAAP. Brynn McNabs text A Moving Archive
was originally published in The Dance Currents September / October 2016 issue.

The SUM gallery presents, Unsavoury Witness-- Alejandro A. Barbosa and Patryk
Stasieczek. Unsavoury Witness is a photo-based installation that includes laser-
engraved photographs, photographic murals, intermedia, print media, and court
transcripts from the Supreme Court of British Columbia on the 2001 murder of Aaron
Webster in Stanley Park. This immersive exhibition foregrounds homophobia's intimate
connections with public spaces, institutionalised systems, societal responses, queer
bodies, and desire.

Within the safety of SUM gallery, the exhibition is crafted as a diorama where artist and
viewer intersect the public figure of the witness toward a queer ethics of
memorialisation.

Unsavoury Witness is part of the 2025 Capture Photography Festival and is
generously supported by The Parachute Fund and the Deux Mille Foundation.

Join us on April 16, from 6-9 PM, for the opening reception.

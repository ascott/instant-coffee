NISHA PLATZER Work In Progress: BACK HOME
Open House: Thursday March 26, 5-7pm
Window screening: Monday March 30, 6-7
Burrard View Fieldhouse 545 N Slocan Street

For the fifth month of our WORK IN PROGRESS series, Iris Film Collective member Nisha
Platzer will be continuing work on her feature film BACK HOME, a kaleidoscopic piece reflecting
on the life and loss of her older brother Josh, who took his own life in 1999. Parts of the film are
shot on Super 8 and 16mm and then manually altered to create an expressive and disturbing
visual quality. Throughout the month of March Nisha will be trying out different techniques with
the aim of finding the right aesthetic for these more abstract sections of the film.
On Thursday
March 26 from 5-7pm Nisha will host an Open House where the public is welcome
to have a look at the work she has been doing at the Fieldhouse this month.This will be followed
on Monday March 30th from 6-7pm, by a Window Screening of her work in progress.

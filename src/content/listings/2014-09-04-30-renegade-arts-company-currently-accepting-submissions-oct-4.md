ART + MUSIC SHOW ALL DAY EVENT!
**Currently accepting submissions**

event link: https://www.facebook.com/events/1472965246291661/

Renegade Arts Company will be partnering up with The Drift Art Walk on Main
Street this year. Renegade has a warehouse/labyrinth-like atmosphere. We
will be using our hallways to display art, leading up to a large space
where artists will have tables and walls with their work set up. The show
will be accompanied by music throughout the day. Our Shop Theatre (same
building) will open up at 8pm, where bands will be performing to end the event!

If you would like to apply to be a part of The Drift at Renegade, please
send images of your artwork, or a link to an online portfolio to:
renegadearts@outlook.com. Musicians are welcome to apply as well! After
approval, there will be a $15.00 fee to display - tables are an extra
$5.00. The date will be Saturday, October 4, 2pm till late. 

**SUBMISSION DEADLINE: SEPT. 20**

Please feel free to ask any questions that you may have. Looking forward to
seeing your art!

2pm-8pm: FREE

8pm on: $5.00

tickets may not be available at door)

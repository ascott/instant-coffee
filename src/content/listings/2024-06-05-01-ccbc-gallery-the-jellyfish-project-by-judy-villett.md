| JUN 1
Title: The Jellyfish Project by Judy Villett

Dates: June 1 - June 30, 2024

Hours: Monday - Sunday, 10a - 5:30p

Description:

What is the downside of a floating mass of garbage? Experience a fishs view of sea
life under a raft of cleaned and curated plastic. Imagine that the trash is mashed
together by the currents, afloat far from its source, providing unwitting shade and
shelter for plankton proliferation. Join the jellyfish as they gorge on their favourite
food.

Shaped and stiffened antique doilies with graceful ribbon and yarn tentacles sway in
the air as people pass by, simulating the movement of ocean currents. Jellies and
some items of trash are suspended by invisible fish line. The overall impression may
be quite beautiful, until passersby notice the newspaper articles and text on the
ocean floor describing how the jellyfish are decreasing fish stocks for human
consumption. In this
interconnected web of life, not every creature is endangered by
climate change. Some are thriving on and under the pollution.

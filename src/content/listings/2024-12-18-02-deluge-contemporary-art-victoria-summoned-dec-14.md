Summoned
Lana Z Caplan
Gloria Chung
Abinadi Meza
Mike Rollo

December 14, 2024 to January 11, 2025
Nightly in the gallery transom window and online at deluge.ca

Deluge Contemporary Art

636 Yates Street, Victoria BC | deluge.ca

A program of artists films that convoke elemental spirits and the power of nature in a
time of human-made catastrophe"a command for light against impending darkness.
Four filmmakers consider primordial phenomena and current crises, summoning
intangible forces of creation to counter the destruction of the natural world. Ancient
energies are captured and released, bookended by the words of the late Steve Albini:
This is a real goddamn emergency!

The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC invites you to the next edition of its monthly 
Night Shift cabaret art series on Thursday, April 5, 2018 from 7pm to 9 pm.

At Night Shift: Quantum Futures, co-hosted with Curiosity Collider, dive deep into 
quantum physics through hands-on activities, dance, spoken word, projection sculpture 
and virtual reality. Expand your understanding of nano-scale physics and the 
implications of potential future breakthroughs.Free with museum admission. For more 
information visit: moa.ubc.ca/ night-shift-quantum-futures/

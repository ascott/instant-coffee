Tiziana La Melia (1982) was born in Palermo, Sicily and raised on an orchard on
Syilx/Okanagan territories. She works across many media such as painting, poetry,
sculpture, collaboration, collage and drawing. Recent exhibitions and books include
The Eyelash and the Monochrome
(Talonbooks, 2018), and Oral Like Cloaks, Dialect
(Blank Cheque Press, 2019); Global Cows 2020 on Conceptual Fine Arts and hosted
as part of Images Festival, (Toronto, 2021; Ozone Gleaners at Projet Pangee
(Montreal), 2020; Fly Robin Fly at Mecenes du Sud (Montpellier, 2021); 17:4 at Unit 17
(Vancouver, 2021); Comics Trip! at Collection Lambert (Avignion), 2022. Her poetry
and criticism have been published in The Capilano Review, Art 21, C Magazine,
Montez Press, The Organism for Poetic Research among others.

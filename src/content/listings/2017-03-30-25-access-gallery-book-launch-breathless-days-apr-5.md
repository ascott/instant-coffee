Book Launch: Breathless Days
Wednesday, April 5, 2017, 7:00 - 9:00 PM

Access Gallery is delighted to host the Vancouver launch of Breathless Days, a new 
book co-edited by acclaimed Vancouver-based art historians Serge Guilbaut and 
John OBrian. Taking 1959-1960 as a pivotal cultural and political moment, 
Breathless Days replaces prevailing national narratives with a set of heterogeneous 
studies that bring Western Europe and the Americas together. The book reads 
kaleidoscopically, its pages flickering with the myriad events that in two years 
changed entire aesthetic and political horizons. The book, as its reviewers have 
remarked, will leave readers bout de souffle. 

*With live jazz accompaniment by Hank Bull.

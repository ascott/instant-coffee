Applications open for Scholarship Residencies at Malaspina 
Printmakers

Deadline: Monday, March 14, 2016

Malaspina Printmakers invites applications from current students and 
recent graduates for our four-month and eight-month Scholarship 
Residencies.

Our four-month scholarship residency is intended for students returning 
to their studies in the fall, and provides the Scholar with free rent at our 
studio on Granville Island, from May to August, 2016. The Scholar will 
also present an artist talk or demo at the end of their residency, on 
Sunday, August 21, 2016, as part of our regular public programming.

Our eight-month scholarship residency is intended to assist recent 
graduates to become practicing artists, and provides the Scholar with 
free rent at our studio on Granville Island, from September 2016 to 
April 2017. The Scholar will present an artist talk or demo during their 
residency, as part of our regular public programming. To be considered 
a recent graduate your graduation date must fall within one year of our 
application deadline, March 14, 2016.

We welcome applications from individuals from outside Vancouver; 
however, we cannot contribute funding for accommodation or travel. A 
jury will select the successful applicants based on a combination of 
factors, including the significance of their print-based artwork within the 
discourse of contemporary art and cultural theory.

We encourage applications from art students who use print media 
within the scope of other practices or who approach print from an 
inventive and experimental perspective. We do not attempt to define 
print media or printmaking, rather we leave this expanding discipline 
open to interpretation.

Please apply by Monday, March 14, 2016, through our submissions 
page: malaspinaprintmakers.com/submissions

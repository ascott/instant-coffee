BC Culture Days: Free Guided Outdoor Tours

Museum of Anthropology, Saturday, Sept 30 " Sunday, October 1, 2017 at 1pm and 
3:30pm

The Museum of Anthropology (MOA) at UBC presents BC Culture Days: Free Guided 
Outdoor Tours, Saturday, September 30 and Sunday, October 1, 2017. On each day, 
there will be two, free guided outdoor tours at 1pm and 3:30pm, starting outside in front 
of the museum entrance. This 30-minute tour features the museum's outdoor sculpture 
complex, including two Haida Houses and totem poles by some of the finest 
contemporary First Nations artists of the Northwest Coast. Free for All Visitors.

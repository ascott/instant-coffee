'True Colours' - October Exhibition
OPENING RECEPTION
October 13th from 6-9pm

140 W Hastings St, Vancouver, BC

COMPLIMENTARY
TICKETS
Join us for an exciting art event - 'True Colours'! This October, we are showcasing a
diverse range of artworks that will captivate your senses. Explore the vibrant world of
colours and let your imagination run wild. Our in-person exhibition will be held at 140
W Hastings Street, Vancouver BC. Immerse yourself in the artistic atmosphere as
you admire the stunning masterpieces created by talented local artists.
Discover a kaleidoscope of artistic expressions, from abstract paintings to intricate
glass & lightening sculptures. Let the creativity and passion of these artists inspire
and uplift you.
Whether you're an art enthusiast or simply looking for a unique experience, 'True
Colours' is the perfect event to indulge your senses and appreciate the beauty of art.
Don't miss this chance to witness the magic of colours come to life!

Referencing a shift in viewpoints, TILT/ is an annual open call group exhibition
emphasizing the power of art. PoMoArts invite artists to contribute diverse
perspectives and create immersive experiences across all mediums.

Building from Art 4 Life, this call welcomes painting, drawing, sculpture, installation,
video, performance, and a wide range of visual and experimental practices. We are
seeking immersive, participatory, and thought-provoking pieces that spark curiosity.

Submission Deadline: November 30, 2025

Exhibition Dates: January 22 " March 8, 2026

Apply online at pomoarts.ca/artist-calls

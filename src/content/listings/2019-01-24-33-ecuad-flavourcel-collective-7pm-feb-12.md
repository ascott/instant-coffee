Flavourcel Collective - Artist Talk

Tuesday, Feb 12, 2019 - 7:00 PM - 9:00 PM

@ Reliance Theatre, Emily Carr University

Flavourcel is a Vancouver-based animation collective formed with the aim to uplift its 
members and the communities it belongs to. Flavourcel is comprised of local talented 
animation artists brought together by their enthusiasm for the fun, the weird, but most of 
all for the art of animation. The Collective creates and hosts live events and installations, 
animation challenges and recently theyre cranking along on some cool music videos 
theyve been hired to do.

At this artist talk, the Collective will discuss their groups mission and efforts, and share 
media from their live events and installations, and music videos.

This event is part of The Time, Light + Sound Series 2019 spring schedule of guest 
artists. Curator Lorelei Pepi has crafted a special focus on the theme of Collaborative 
Spirit which suffuses much of this semesters line-up. Artists are capable of astounding 
acts of creativity, and their ideas and energies are often amplified and transformed when 
working in crews, ensembles and collectives.

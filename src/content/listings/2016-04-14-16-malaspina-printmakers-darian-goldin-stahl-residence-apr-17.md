Darian Goldin Stahl: Residence
Artist talk on Sunday, April 17, at 2pm

Malaspina Printmakers, 1555 Duranleau Street, Vancouver

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to present an artist talk with Scholarship
Resident Darian Goldin Stahl, on the occasion of her exhibition Residence.

Darian Goldin Stahl's exhibition at Malaspina Printmakers presents new
works produced during her eight month Scholarship Residency. Stahl's
arts-based research is a collaborative cycle of informing and
reconstructing identity with her sister, Devan Stahl, who is a clinical
bioethicist at Michigan State University.

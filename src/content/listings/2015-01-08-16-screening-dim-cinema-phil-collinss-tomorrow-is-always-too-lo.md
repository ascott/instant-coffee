Great Britain 2014. Dir: Phil Collins. 82 min. DCP

giddily mixes genres: documentary musical, silhouette animation and

environments to the accompaniment of the Royal Scottish Orchestra. The
musical sequences are punctuated by public-access broadcasts from a
disused 1960s TV studio, its doors re-opened by Collins to people from

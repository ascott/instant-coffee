Exhibition Reception: Faber Mo Neifer: Its All Chinese to Me, Saturday, April 12, 6 to
8 p.m. Town Centre Park Community Centre (1207 Pinetree Way, Coquitlam)

Join us for the exhibition reception for Faber Mo Neifer: Its All Chinese to Me. In this
exhibition, Faber Mo Neifer explores the ways in which Chinese people are othered,
and asserts belonging for himself and the Chinese diaspora in Canada. The series
depicts the signs of Chinese restaurants from across Canada.

Faber Mo Neifers practice is rooted in the discursive nature of photographic images,
exploring contemporary identity. He completed his Master of Fine Arts in Studio Arts
with a concentration in Photography at Concordia University, and graduated from
Emily Carr University of Art + Design with a major in Photography and a minor in
Social Practices and Community Engagement.

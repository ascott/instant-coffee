North Van Arts invites artists to submit proposals for What We Hold Close, an
exhibition exploring the physical and symbolic spaces of domestic life and what it is to
feel "at home".

This group exhibition will feature 2D and 3D artworks made from, inspired by, or
challenging household and domestic spaces, objects and materials, in a variety of
mediums.

We encourage submission of works that explore the meaning of home in different
cultural contexts, including the experiences of immigrants, newcomers, and people
who have been displaced.

Domestic and Household spaces carry complex and varied meanings shaped by
culture, history, and lived experience. We welcome submissions that consider how
home is remembered, reimagined, or reconstructed through these lenses.

Artists are encouraged to explore and question.

What makes you feel at home?
What parts of home do you carry with you, even when you move?
How do food, furniture, objects, or celebrations shape your idea of home?
How does your idea of home change across time, place, or generations?

What We Hold Close will present a range of perspectives, stimulating conversation
around belonging, memory, and identity.

Deadline to submit artwork is Thursday, October 30,2025 at 5pm PST
Submit here: https://form-can.keela.co/what-we-hold-close-open-call-for-artwork

ARTIST TALK
Surrey Art Gallery - At Home and Elsewhere: Sonny Assu, Heidi McKenzie, Helma
Sawatzky, Jan Wade

Saturday, Nov 14 at 2 pm

On the Surrey Art Gallery Facebook page and YouTube channel

Surrey Art Gallery is pleased to announce At Home and Elsewhere, an online panel
discussion featuring artists who will speak about their work in the Gallerys current
exhibition, the themes of the exhibition, and also how the dynamics of the pandemic
have shaped their practice"at home and in
public. Their works variously address
themes of diaspora, racism, communication, desire, pop culture, and more. The panel
features a diverse roster of practicing contemporary artists: Sonny Assu, Heidi
McKenzie, Helma Sawatzky, and Jan Wade. The conversation will be introduced and
facilitated by Surrey Art Gallery Assistant Curator, Rhys Edwards

Where We Have Been commemorates the Gallerys 45th anniversary with paintings,
photographs, sculptures, and video installations, reflecting on the connection between
place, nature, memory, and identity in the South of the Fraser. It also speaks to the
challenges of the present moment: the difficulties of finding a place to live, thrive, and be
present with one another.

The pandemic is hitting the art world very hard, says Edwards. For example, The
United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) recently
estimated that one in three museums worldwide will close as a result. Now more than
ever, it is vital to bring
artists voices to the table. At Home and Elsewhere is an
opportunity to discuss how art practice has been impacted by our current moment, how
it has suffered and thrived, and to comment on the increasingly blurred lines between
work environments and domestic spaces.

About the Artists

Sonny Assu is an interdisciplinary artist whose practice is informed by Kwakwakawakw
and Western principles of artmaking. His work is often autobiographical and explores his
familys history as a way to shed light on Canadas treatment of the First Peoples. He
currently resides in unceded Ligwidaxw territory (Campbell River, BC).

Heidi McKenzie is a Toronto-based ceramic sculptor and potter, arts journalist, and
curator who is dedicated to the craft of re-invigorating modernism in our times. Inspired
by her mixed Indo-Trinidadian and Irish American ethnicity, her work addresses issues
of race, identity, and belonging. Her work seeks to transcend Eurocentrism through
abstraction.

Helma Sawatzky is an
interdisciplinary academic and artist based in Surrey. Her
background includes undergraduate degrees in music education, visual arts, a masters
degree in communication, and a PhD from Simon Fraser University School of
Communication. Her research focuses on photography, embodiment, and mediation.

Jan Wade is a Vancouver-based artist. Her research focuses on the sociopolitical
symbols and practices of diasporic and African slave cultures. To explore these themes,
she juxtaposes pop culture, new world imagery, and found materials and objects in her
mixed-media sculptures and assemblages. She has lived and worked in Cuba, Haiti,
South Africa, and the United Kingdom.

Oshogatsu: Ring in the New Year with Us!

Join us for a fun-filled day of traditional Japanese New Year celebrations at the
Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre in Burnaby.

On Saturday, January 4, 2025, from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM, we invite you to
experience the beauty of Japanese New Year. Enjoy a variety of free activities,
including:

Kakizome New Year Calligraphy: Write your New Years resolutions in Japanese
characters with brush and ink.

Traditional Board and Card Games: Try your hand at Shogi, Igo, Karuta, and
Hanafuda (locally called Gaji).

Loose Leaf Tea Open House: Savour the delicate flavors of Japanese tea.

Hawaiian Hula Dance Class: Join a free beginners class.

Meet Our Instructors: Learn about our programs and meet our instructors.

This is a great opportunity to learn about Japanese culture while spending time with
friends and family. All ages are welcome!

Event Details:

Title: Oshogatsu: Japanese New Year Celebrations

Date: Saturday, January 4, 2025

Time: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM

Location: 6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby

Access: Free underground and street parking. Walk or bus from Edmonds Skytrain
Station.

Admission: Free

For more
information, please visit our website at centre.nikkeiplace.org or contact us
at 604.777.7000 or info@nikkeiplace.org.

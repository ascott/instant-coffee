Salish Singing and Drumming Workshop

Sept 27 | 6PM | FREE | 149 W HASTINGS ST.

Join us on Wednesday, September 27th for Salish Singing and Drumming! This is a
free workshop with Russell Wallace, a composer, producer, and traditional Lilwat
singer.

All folks, regardless of age, experience, or ability, are invited to participate and try their
hand at Salish singing and drumming. There will be a limited number of drums
available on the day of, but if you happen to have your own drum, you are encouraged
to bring it with you!

Per Diem Part II: The Gerd Metzdorff Collection - Exhibition Tour: July 23, 2:30PM

Join Griffin Art Projects Indigenous Curatorial Assistant, Emmett Hanly, for a series of
guided tours of Griffins current exhibition, Per Diem Part II: The Gerd Metzdorff
Collection, on Sunday afternoons at 2:30PM throughout the season!

Per Diem Part II: The Gerd Metzdorff Collection features Vancouver collector Gerd
Metzdorffs international painting collection. Following up on Per Diem Part I, which
featured primarily photo-based and print work, Per Diem Part II will focus on painting, in
all
its forms, along with selected sculpture and prints.

With the passing of this enigmatic and remarkable collector, Gerd Metzdorffs stunning
collection is presented to the public for the first time at Griffin Art Projects.

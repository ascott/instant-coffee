MAKe and Black & Yellow Gallery present...

Hana Pesut
SWITCHEROO
Exhibition and Book Launch

Opening reception: Friday, July 12, 2013
7 pm-11 pm
257 East 7th Avenue
Show runs July 12 - July 19

Join us at Black & Yellow Gallery for the exhibition and book launch for
Switcheroo, a photography project by Hana Pesut aka Sincerely Hana.

Switcheroo is a dual portrait series where accomplices are photographed
twice, once in their own outfits and again wearing each others’ outfits
against the same background.

Hana Pesut (aka Sincerely Hana) is a self-taught photographer raised in a
small town in the mountains and currently living in Vancouver, Canada. Her
main focus in photography is the ’little moments’ that people sometimes
miss and later wish they had captured. She hopes to inspire others to take
more photos in their daily lives.

Copies of the Switcheroo book will be on sale at the opening reception for
$30 each.
The Switcheroo Experience will be on sale at the opening reception for
$100. Book a session with Hana and a friend and Switcheroo.

Black & Yellow is a year round project space. Its mandate is to research
and exhibit emerging artists producing innovative and challenging work. The
gallery is not limited to any particular media or disciplines and instead
encourages diversity from show to show. Black & Yellow is supported by The
Cheaper Show and The Emerging Arts Foundation.

MAKe is an integrated marketing and new media company. In life, it's
important to know how to have a little fun. At MAKe, we cultivate a culture
that supports the big ideas and the strategic backbone, but also the
smaller moments of art and inspiration. We do this to fuel our company and
community. We are proud to host Hana and the Switcheroo book launch as an
extension of this culture. From strategy to Switcheroo, we MAKe creativity
happen.

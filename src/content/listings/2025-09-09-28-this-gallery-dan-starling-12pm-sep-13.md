In IT DOESNT STOP IT DOESNT STOP, Starling assembles a series of
lithographic prints that combine text panels from the manga Deadman Wonderland
(2007"2013), still images from his film Sans Sans Soleil (2018), and handmade
abstractions derived from screen-tones. What begins as representation gradually
becomes dense with layers of imagery, ultimately dissolving narrative coherence.

Starling explores how printmaking " a medium defined by sequence and repetition
" both aligns with and disrupts storytelling. Each plate or stone carries the potential
for variation: shifts in inking, mis-registrations, or accidental overlaps introduce
disjunctions that fracture the smooth flow of narrative. These interruptions mirror
wider cultural ruptures, where systems of power and continuity falter.

Dan Starling is a Vancouver-based artist whose research-driven practice
investigates how art can unsettle dominant cultural narratives. Drawing on strategies
of intervention, repetition, and recombination, his work addresses how myths
maintain the status quo while also imagining futures that are more equitable and just.

He holds a Meisterschler from the Stdelschule, Frankfurt, and is an Assistant
Professor at the University of British Columbia. His solo exhibitions include the
Burnaby Art Gallery (2022), Klondike Institute of Art & Culture (2021), and Wil Aballe
Art Projects & VIVO Media Arts Centre (2018). His work has been shown
internationally at Kunsthall Oslo, Kunstverein Frankfurt, and M HKA Antwerp.

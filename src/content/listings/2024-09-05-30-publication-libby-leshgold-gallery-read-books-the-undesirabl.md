Join us for the launch of Myfanwy MacLeods new book The Undesirables published
by ECU Press. The Undesirables features work drawn from her public art
endeavours over the past fifteen years. It includes realized and unrealized projects
as well as propositional ideas. The publication includes a newly
commissioned text
by Lisa Robertson. MacLeod and Robertson will both read.

Myfanwy MacLeod (b.1961) is a Vancouver-based artist known for her thought-
provoking public artworks. Her practice engages with themes of urban development,
sustainability, and the human-nature relationship. MacLeods recent work focuses on
creating monuments that challenge traditional heroic narratives typically associated
with public art. By shifting the spotlight to non-human species, MacLeod aims to
address how human history, and monuments often elevate individuals or dominant
cultures while neglecting our role within the natural world. Her approach reflects a
broader commitment to questioning the status quo and advocating for a more
inclusive and thoughtful representation of our place in the environment. The Song of
the Dodo, her most recent commission for the new headquarters of the National
Bank of Canada in Montreal will be inaugurated this fall.

Lisa Robertson is a Canadian poet, essayist and
novelist who lives in France. Born
in Toronto in 1961, she was a long-time resident of Vancouver, where in the early
nineties she began writing, publishing and collaborating in a community of artists
and poets that included Artspeak Gallery, the Western Front, and the Kootenay
School of Writing. She has published nine books of poetry, most recently Boat
(2022), 2 books of essays (Nilling (2012), and Occasional Work and Seven Walks
from the Office for Soft Architecture (2003), and one novel, as well as contributing to
many artists catalogues and monographs internationally.

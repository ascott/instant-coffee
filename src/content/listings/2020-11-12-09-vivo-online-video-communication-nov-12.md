Video Communication: Art and Technology between Canada and Japan, 1967-1985

www.vivomediaarts.com/archive/video-communication

Curator Jesse Cumming explores the rich history of influence and exchange between
Japan and Canada in the early, exploratory years of video. Each program highlights
little-seen work and classics by both Japanese and Canadian practitioners, tracing a
history of mutual influence and emphasizing important collaborations.

Artist interviews and curator essay, Switched On. (now until Dec 15)
Video screening: Intermedia. (Nov 24-Dec 1)
Video screening: Vox Populi. (Dec 8-Dec 15)

This free exhibition is a special edition of VIVOs Sticky Impulse Archive Nights series.

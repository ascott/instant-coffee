Richmond Art Gallery hosts:
ART AT WORK: MARKETING YOUR ARTWORK

Saturday, April 30, 2016
1:00 PM - 4:00 PM
7700 Minoru Gate, Richmond BC

Receive advice and tips from artist and marketing consultant, Bryce 
Evans, on making the most of digital media, social media and other 
promotional tools to get your artwork noticed.

$20 RAGA or CARFAC BC Members / $30 Non-Members
Pre-registration required via the City of Richmond website, Call Centre 
or in person at the Richmond Cultural Centre.

Bryce Evans is an artist, marketing consultant and Founder of The One 
Project. He specializes in using photography and interactive art to 
share insights, create conversation and build community to bring 
positive social change to the world. Bryce has a Business 
Administration diploma majoring in Marketing with over five years' 
experience creating brands and developing communities on social 
media. He has been featured as a suggested user on Instagram twice, 
gathering a community of over 52,000 people, and his work has been 
exhibited across Canada, the United States, as well as internationally - 
winning multiple awards.

Art at Work is a series of professional development events and 
workshops designed for visual artists. Presented by Public Art 
Richmond, Richmond Art Gallery, and CARFAC BC, this series is 
intended to provide artists with the knowledge and skills required for 
pursuing a professional arts practice in the field of public art, visual art 
and community arts.

For more information, contact Kathy Tycholis at the Richmond Art

Please join us for a conversation and opening reception with Wael Shawky on Thursday,
October 17 at 7pm.

This exhibition brings the extraordinary and timely art of Wael Shawky to the west coast for the
first time. Shawkys ambitious, multilayered film productions look at the ways in which history
and
mythologies are recorded, highlighting the fallibility of cultural memory, while offering critical
perspectives on our current narratives of uncertainty and change. Shawky brings into dialogue
real and imagined histories of the Arab world in provocative retellings that pose timely questions
about truth and fabulation.

8pm: Opening Reception

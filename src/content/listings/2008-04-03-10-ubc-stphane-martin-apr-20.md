Green College Lecture Series 2008
Curating in Institutions: International curators talk about their practice

Public lectures by Serge Guilbaut, Kitty Scott, Candice Hopkins,
Régis Michel, and Stéphane Martin

The Morris and Helen Belkin Gallery is pleased to present a dynamic five part lecture series featuring renowned international scholars and curators. The series is intended to illuminate the current challenges and critical issues that define the practice of curating in our present situation.

Wednesday, April 9 at 5pm
Stéphane Martin
President-Director General, Musée du Quai Branly (Paris)

Stéphane Martin has held a variety of posts in the cultural sector - notably, that of General Director of the Centre Georges-Pompidou (1989-1990), Director of Music and Dance (1993-1995), and Director of the Office of Philippe Douste-Blazy, Minister of Culture (1995-1997).  At the same time, he was Vice-President of the association of the Musée de l'Homme, des Arts et des Civilisations. Since 1998, he has presided over the Ensemble Intercontemporain.	Martin was appointed President-Director General of the public establishment of the Musée du quai Branly, by the President of the Republic's decree of 30 December 1998.  His presidential post at the public establishment of the Musée du quai Branly was renewed in November 2003.

THIS LECTURE WILL TAKE PLACE AT THE UBC FREDERIC LASSERRE BUILDING, ROOM 102

This series is organized in conjunction with the Critical and Curatorial Studies Program by the Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, the Department of Art History, Visual Art & Theory, and the Museum of Anthropology at the University of British Columbia, with the support of Green College.

Stéphane Martin’s talk is co-sponsored by The Canadian Museums Association and by the Consulat Général de France à Vancouver.

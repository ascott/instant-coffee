CAG Family Day | Collagraph Monoprints

Saturday, May 26, 12-3pm, free

Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson St

On the last Saturday of each month, CAG invites all ages to drop-in for
short exhibition tours and free art-making activities that respond to our
current exhibitions.

Inspired by the work of Grace Ndiritu and Kara Uzelman, join us to create
an assemblage of found materials to form a unique printing plate.
Experiment with creating multiple prints and 'ghost' prints, revealing new
aspects of the 3D material in 2D.

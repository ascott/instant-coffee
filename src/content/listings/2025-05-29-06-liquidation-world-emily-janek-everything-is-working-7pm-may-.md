"Everything is everything. Using symbolism, synchronicities, the power of love and
religiously repeated mantras, I aim to transcend beyond the physical world and learn
the secrets of being through my practice. Through that weird big feeling of needing
to draw and create something everyday, my inspirations comprise of my daily
experiences that are translated to dreams and fantastical future scenarios. I like to
keep my inner child at the forefront of the crayon with mark making and bright
highlighter colours. Simple and recognizable imagery is layered and overlapped to
create patterns and dialogue between works. As the more physical layers are added
to a painting I believe the more magic it begins to hold. Cartoon-y characters are
repeated to bring a sense of spirituality and religion. These characters personify
different trials and tribulations of being and help me build a frame of how the spaces
and people around me operate. May my practice inspire curiosity and a joyful
whimsy for eternal life." - Emily Janek

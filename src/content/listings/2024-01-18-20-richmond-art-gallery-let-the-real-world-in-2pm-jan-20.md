Shen Xin - but this is the language we met in

January 20 - March 31, 2024

Richmond Art Gallery is proud to present
Chinese artist Shen Xins haunting debut
exhibition in Canada. At its heart is a poetic new work called but this is the language
we met in, the first film in a forthcoming series with the overarching title of Grounds
of Coherence. With this project accompanied by four small paintings, the artist
deepens their ongoing engagement with what they describe as ways of coming to
knowing, and the ecosystems of languages.

Xin sees language as encompassing the entire body including the voice, the gaze,
gestures, facial expressions, laughter, and even dance. The video highlights how
language has evolved over time: while some aspects of it have changed, others
have remained the same, emphasizing the advantages and drawbacks of modern
communication.

Let the real world in

January 20 - March 31, 2024

Let the real world in presents a
selection of contemporary videos by Kirsten
Leenaars, Yoshua Okn, and Wapikoni Mobile that centers on children and youth
who boldly articulate director Jean-Luc Godards credo that film should bear witness
to the period.

Young people are often associated with qualities like simplicity, authenticity, and
ingenuity. Indeed, in North American society, which often enjoys the privileged
position, can attempt to preserve these qualities as long as possible by
endeavouring to protect young people from difficult realities. In Let the real world in,
rather than speak on behalf of children or try to protect them from difficult realities,
the videos take young people's perspectives seriously and their agency is brought to
the forefront. It also features a commissioned series of screenprints by local artist
and graphic designer Yaimel Lpez Zaldvar, created in response to the videos in
this exhibition.

The Sointula Art Shed is now up and running in Sointula, BC! We are a 
small artist-run artist residency/music/project space, with a shop and 
various other projects still in the works, run by artists Tyler Brett and Kerri 
Reid. Our residency is open to visual and performing artists, musicians, 
and writers, and is located in a very cute cottage on our property, with a 
shed next door for a studio. It is located one block up from the ocean, 
and a short walk from the ferry, various walks and trails, the Sointula 
Museum, library, co-op grocery store, hardware store, pub, thrift shop, 
bakery, etc. Sointula is a small fishing village on Malcolm Island, off of 
Northern Vancouver Island, and was founded by Finnish socialist 
utopians led by Matti Kurikka in 1901; the name 'Sointula' means 'Place 
of Harmony' in Finnish. Please visit our website and/or send us an email 
for more information on Sointula, applying to our residency, playing a 
music show in Sointula, working on a project to be shown in our shed, or 
all of the above. We're at: www.sointulaartshed.ca and can be emailed 
at: sointulaartshed@gmail.com. You can also see photos of what we've 
been up to on our facebook page, here: 
https://www.facebook.com/TheSointulaArtShed

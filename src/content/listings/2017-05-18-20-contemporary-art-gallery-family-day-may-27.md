Saturday, May 27, 12-3pm

On the last Saturday of each month, the CAG invites all ages to drop-in for short 
exhibition tours and free art-making activities that respond to our current exhibitions. 
Presented in collaboration with ArtStarts on Saturdays. For more details visit: 
www.artstarts.com/events

Inspired by the work of Niamh OMalleys Glass House, take photographs through a 
variety of windows to create different light, colour and texture effects.
We acknowledge the generous support of the Peter Szeto Investment Group for our 
Family Day program.

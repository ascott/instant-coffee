Event: Everywhere, Forever. Immigrant Group Exhibition. August 2-29

Place: Art Council of Surrey Gallery, 13530 Seventy Second Avenue, Surrey

We all have our roots in a story of emigration. This exhibition is about those stories from
immigrants. They are
diverse, unique, and complex. Gilles Clement, a philosopher
gardener coined the terms brassage plantaire to note that everything- inorganic as
well as organic- on the planet is in continuous motion since its creation. In fact, our
evolution depends on it from the biological to the spiritual levels. The earth has
witnessed numerous episodes of relocations of human beings which are not just short
term and temporary phenomena. Each uprooted individual has their own story to tell: a
story of injustice, daily struggle in alien land, helplessness but also sometimes hope and
joy. Hoda Mir, Alex Lavrov, Linda Haftner, Johnny Tai, Natasha Boskic, Pierre Leichner,
and Barbara Tevarotto in this show uses different medias to explore and share their
experiences.

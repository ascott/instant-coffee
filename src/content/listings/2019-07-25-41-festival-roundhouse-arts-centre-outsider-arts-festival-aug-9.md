The 3rd Annual Vancouver Outsider Arts Festival presented by the Community Arts Council of 
Vancouver is Canadas first and only festival dedicated to outsider art. VOAF offers 150 visual 
and performing artists facing social exclusion and other barriers, opportunities for exhibition and 
sales, performance and participation, connection and learning.

All ages, all events are free. 
100% of sales go to the artists. 
Schedule details and pre-registration for artists talks and workshops @ www.cacv.ca

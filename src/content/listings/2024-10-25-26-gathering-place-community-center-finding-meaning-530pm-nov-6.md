Event; Finding Meaning Exhibition
Place: Connection Salon Gallery, 609 Helmcken
We are continuing our series of exhibitions on the theme of Recovery. This one is about
Meaning: Finding meaning in mental health experience and living a purposeful life.
Connection Salon Artists Collectives mission is to provide low
barrier opportunities to
artists
that are underrepresented and have lived experience with mental health.

12 visual artists will be on show November 5 to December to December 9. Join us for the
Meet the Artists event November 6 from 5:30 to 7:00PM with David Granirer and the
Stand up for Mental health Comedians.
The exhibition is supported by the Canada Council for the Arts, the Connection Salon
Collective and the Gathering Place Community Center.

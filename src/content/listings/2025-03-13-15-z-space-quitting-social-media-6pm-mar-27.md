Why its time to move off big tech social media
A session for artists, craftspeople, makers, designers, and creatives

Using big tech platforms and social media is no longer sustainable for artists,
designers, and other creative industries. There has also been recent interest and
growth in Bluesky and Pixelfed as potential alternatives.

Many people are struggling with the decision of what to do next. What are these
alternatives to Instagram or X? How will
they avoid the issues existing platforms
have? and is it worthwhile to move to new social media?

In this three hour peer learning workshop, we will guide you through the social media
landscape and identify options that are better aligned with your values and goals.
We want to help people get the information they need.

Topics we will cover:

What are the alternatives to X, Instagram, etc?
What is open social media? Decentralized social media?
What is the fediverse? What is bluesky?
Examples of how artists are using these
Strategies to help you leave
Tools to help you migrate your data
Tools for managing accounts
Items you will need to bring: smart phone, laptop, notebook, water bottle

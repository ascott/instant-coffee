Rhys Edwards - Studies for a Holy Vehicle

Chernoff Fine Art
1 November - 30 November
OPENING RECEPTION: Friday, November 1, 6-9pm

The work of Rhys Edwards takes as its premise the strictures of representation itself:
the elements of visual language. Each of his works
reconfigures the elements of this
language into a body of imagery that defies categorization. His ongoing Studies for a
Holy Vehicle series embraces an ecstatic passion for pictorial figuration. Each individual
drawing within the series is a speculative study of a form, real or imagined. Through the
rendering of light, shadow, texture, material and substance, Edwards imbues each form
with apparent weight and objecthood, suggesting a precise realism. Yet, he denies the
viewer any kind of contextual framework within which to situate these forms.

Without any specific signification in and of themselves, the cumulative realization of
these elements reflects a kind of visual logorrhea: a body of distended metaphors which
accrue meaning only in virtue of the attention furnished upon them. An alchemical,
glossolalic enterprise, Edwards' work engenders a loving reduction of all forms to
visuality. Interwoven within the same fabric of fragments, each subject decouples from
preconception,
abiding only in form itself"re-birthed in aesthetic poiesis.

Richmond Community Centre Public Art Project | MAR 29
2021 Richmond Pride Week

West Richmond Community Centre Public Art Project

Expression of Interest (EOI)

In celebration of this years Pride Week, the City of Richmond Public Art Program in
partnership with West Richmond Community Association, East Richmond Community
Association and City Centre Community Association seeks an LGBTQ2S+ artist or artist
team to collaborate and develop a community and socially-engaged public art project with
LGBTQ2S+ youth, ages 13 to 19 in Richmond. The project will be presented as part of
Richmond Pride Week, July 26 to August 1, 2021.

Budget: $6,500

Eligibility: LGBTQ2S+ artists residing in the Greater Vancouver Area

Application Deadline: March 29, 2021

Project Duration: May to July 2021

For further information and to download the Call to Artists, visit:
www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/opportunities

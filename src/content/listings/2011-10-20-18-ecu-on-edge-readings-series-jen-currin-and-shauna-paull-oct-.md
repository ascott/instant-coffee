The On Edge readings series presents:

Jen Currin and Shauna Paull

7 pm, Thursday, Oct 27

South Building 406
Emily Carr University of Art and Design
Granville Island, Vancouver
Coast Salish Territories

Readings are free and open to the public. All are welcome.

Bios for Authors:

Jen Currin has published three books of poetry: The Sleep of Four Cities (Anvil, 
2005), Hagiography (Coach House, 2008), and The Inquisition Yours (Coach 
House, 2010), which won the Audre Lorde Poetry Award. Born and raised in 
Portland, Oregon, Currin lived in many places before settling in Vancouver in 
2002. She teaches writing and literature at Vancouver Community College and 
creative writing at Kwantlen University and SFU's Writer's Studio.

Shauna Paull is the author of roughened in undercurrent (Leaf Press, 2008). A 
well-respected poet, educator and community advocate, she completed her MFA 
in Creative Writing at the UBC. She has led creative writing workshops at the 
Shadbolt Centre for the Arts in Deer Lake Park, Burnaby, and for many different 
organizations in Vancouver. In community, Shauna has worked extensively with 
migrant and refugee women in areas of labour and mobility rights, poverty 
alleviation and legislative reform.

The On Edge series gratefully acknowledges the support of the Canada Council 
and Emily Carr University.

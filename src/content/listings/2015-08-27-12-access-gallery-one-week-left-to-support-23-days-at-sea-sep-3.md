Residency Update:
Twenty-Three Days at Sea
Final week!

Dear Friends,

We are overwhelmed with the support our Twenty-Three Days at Sea
Kickstarter Campaign has received so far; we have raised over $8000 to
sustain this residency in the coming year. We still have $3000 to raise in
the next eight days and we need your help to get there!

Our project was recently selected by Kickstarter as both Staff Pick and
Project of the Day on August 14! We have made great progress thus far and
have received attention from local and international press including the
CBC, The Vancouver Sun, The Vancouver Courier, Art Radar,The Georgia
Straight and VancityBuzz, among others. If you would like to know even more
about the residency and follow us for the journey, please visit our website
Twenty-Three Days at Sea and follow our official residency Facebook page.

Our second residency artist, Nour Bishouty from Beirut, Lebanon, arrived
safely yesterday in the Port of Shanghai on the MV Hanjin Brussels. Our
next artist, Christopher Boyne, based in Montreal, Québec, will depart from
Vancouver on Sept 22. We hope to give many more promising emergent artists
the opportunity to make this courageous journey over the next three years
if our project reaches it's fundraising goal! To acknowledge your support,
we have many more exciting incentives to offer you on our Kickstarter
Campaign!

Innovative and brave projects such as this require funds over and above our
regular operating budget, and by contributing to this fundraiser, you'll
become part of a remarkable initiative, granting exceptional emerging
artists a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity that will make a tremendous impact
on their practices.

Every little bit helps! Our sincerest thanks to all of you who have so
generously contributed already and to those of you yet to do so, please
consider supporting Twenty-Three Days at Sea!

Hugs,

Kimberly Phillips
Director/Curator Access Gallery

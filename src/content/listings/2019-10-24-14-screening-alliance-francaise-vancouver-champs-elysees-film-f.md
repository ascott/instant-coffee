CHAMPS ELYSES FILM FESTIVAL at the Alliance Franaise Vancouver
Friday, November 1, 2019
7:00 PM-
Saturday, November 2, 2019
7:00 PM-
Alliance Franaise Vancouver
6161 Cambie Street, Vancouver, BC V5Z3B2

Visions Ouest Productions and the Alliance Franaise are pleased to present for the first
time in Vancouver the rerun of the famous independent film festival Champs-Elyses
Festival.
Major international film festival in Paris, Champs-lyses Film Festival is the essential
meeting place to discover the best of independent French and American cinema. The
eighth edition took place from 18 to 25 June 2019 in all the rooms of the most
beautiful
avenue in the world, with a selection of French and American films in competition (long
and short feature films), previews and a sharp musical selection. Created by the
producer, distributor and operator Sophie Dulac, the Festival welcome more than 25,000
spectators and professionals each year on the Champs-lyses. The festival gathers
the heart of contemporary filmmaking by presenting audacious, unpublished and
ambitious films.

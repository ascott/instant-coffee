Please join us for an evening reception with the artists of Blue Greens Workers on Friday,
February 2, 2024. The gallery will be open from 6"10PM, with refreshments and snacks
available by donation.

Additionally, we are pleased to extend the exhibition to Friday, February 9, 2024.
Featuring artworks by Alyson Bucharest, Sauha Lee, and Kiyoshi Whitley, Blue Greens
Workers is UNIT/PITT's inaugural exhibition at our new gallery location, curated by
Catherine de Montreuil. Learn more and view exhibition documentation on our website.

Our regular gallery hours are Wednesday-Friday, 12-5PM, and we can accommodate
some Saturdays by request. Please note that we have limited gallery staff, and welcome
you to email info@unitpitt.ca, or call 604-974-8689 ahead of your visit to confirm that we
will be on site.

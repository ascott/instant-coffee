Join us at the Pacific Cinematheque   on May 14th for the launch of DAMP: Contemporary Vancouver Media Art  , a visually exuberant, art-infused, idea-filled new book on Vancouver’s vibrant, on-the-vanguard media arts scene. Published by Anvil Press, and edited by Vancouver media-makers Oliver Hockenhull and Alex MacKenzie, this beautifully designed, full-colour volume is bursting with lively and incisive content from more than 25 writers and media artists intimately and inextricably connected to the Vancouver scene.

We’re celebrating the launch of DAMP with a special evening featuring films and videos, performances and lectures by writers and media artists who contributed to the book, including Jeff Carter, Sarah Butterfield, Pia Massie, Barry Doupé, David Rimmer, John Price, Amanda Dawn Christie, Ileana Pietrobruno, Chris Welsby, Amy Lockhart, Andrew Herfst/David Crompton, Fiona Bowie, Oliver Hockenhull, Alex MacKenzie, Jayce Salloum, Clint Burnham, Cease Wyss, and Warren Arcand. There’ll be other surprises as well.




D A M P W O R L D · 7:30 PM

OPENING SONG BY CEASE WYSS · A short 5-minute piece sung with a hand drum will serve as recognition of the unceded territories of the Coast Salish Peoples and welcome all present.

LECTURE BY JAYCE SALLOUM: "perception & process: art/media making as convergence" • Vancouver media artist and theorist Jayce Salloum will discuss the issues at stake in the global village of our everyday worlds, using his own multidimensional media practice as a reference. (30 mins.)

SCREENINGS:
• Post Colonial Indian Food Diet - "The Bruce" (Cease Wyss/2005. 5 mins.)
• On the Road to Kandahar (David Rimmer/2006. 5 mins.)
• Touched (Sarah Butterfield/2006. 6 mins.)
• Periphery (Part 1) (Alex MacKenzie/2008. 3 mins.)

PERFORMANCE BY WARREN ARCAND: "From Notebooks Found in a Single Room Occupancy"
• "I heard a rumour that when occupants of rooms die or skip on their bill, their belongings are held for a period. With some cajoling and bribery, I was allowed to satisfy my curiosity by looking through certain suitcases and boxes." (25 mins.)

Total running time: approx. 80 mins.


D A M P C I T Y · 9:30 PM

Parallels 02: North Valparaiso

The Polygon is pleased to announce Parallels 02: North Valparaiso. In the latest addition to
our newly launched chapbook series, acclaimed historians Jean and Roderick J. Barman,
together with Jenn Ashton, document the history of Chilean communities that formed on the
North Shore decades before North Vancouvers official incorporation. The book is free to
read digitally, with printed copies forthcoming.

The Library of Outsider Research
Ongoing through the Summer Tues-Sat 12-5 pm

The Library of Outsider Research is a collection of materials from 
artists Kevin Romaniuk & Liam Hogan, to accompany their installation 
"Rock N Roll Pleasure Henge", on display through the summer in the 
UNIT/PITT reading room. The library consists of books and magazines 
on a variety of topics, including but not limited to: Heavy Metal, Knife 
Fighting, Vans & Street Machines, Satanism & Sorcery, and 
Communes. It is open to the public, Tues-Sat from noon until 5 pm, 
with further programming events to be announced.

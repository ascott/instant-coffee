Timeraiser helps nonprofit organizations, large and small, recruit skilled 
volunteers who bid 20-100 hours of their time in exchange for works of art. 
Timeraiser Vancouver is looking to purchase artwork from local, emerging artists 
to help inspire participants to donate their time in support of a great cause. You 
set the price of the artwork submitted, up to $1000 (including framing and taxes).

Deadline for applications: March 3, 2014

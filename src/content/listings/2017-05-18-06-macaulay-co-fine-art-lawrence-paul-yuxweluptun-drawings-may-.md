Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun
Drawings
Opening reception May 20th, 2-4pm

Macaulay & Co. Fine Art is pleased to present an exhibition of new drawings by 
Lawrence Paul Yuxweluptun. This exhibition features further continuations in the 
artists Tree Studies and Neo-Native series, along with new compositions expanding 
on his interest in figurative designs, landscape and conceptual abstraction.

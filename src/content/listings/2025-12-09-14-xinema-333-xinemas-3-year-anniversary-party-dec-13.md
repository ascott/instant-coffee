3!3!3!: XINEMAs 3-year Anniversary Party!
Saturday, December 13, 2025, 7"11pm
UNIT/PITT " 2954 W 4th Ave, Vancouver, BC V6K 1R4

Join us for a special night celebrating the many filmmakers, friends, and supporters who
have been with us throughout this past year. This event also serves as a fundraiser for our
upcoming programming, our Fieldhouse, and our growing darkroom facility, with by-
donation entry at the door.

The evening includes:
A live set by SNOOZR (Pig Freeze Studios Justin Patterson)

Readings by Dana Qaddah and Christian Vistan

A collaborative performance with an ambient DJ set by La Posmobaby and a visual
installation by Eric Tkaczyk

A live video installation by Anna Shkuratoff

A collective 16mm loop installation, featuring film clips submitted by artists, collaborators,
workshop participants, and darkroom users from this past year

A merch drop with new t-shirts, totes, and stickers designed by XINEMAs team and alumni
filmmakers

FREE first-come, first-served 16mm and 8mm cameras

Special celebratory drinks and birthday cake!

This event includes a 40-minute outdoor performance in The Garden, which will take place
rain or shine. The rest of the festivities will be indoors in The Gallery " please dress
accordingly.

3!3!3! is presented in partnership with UNIT/PITT as part of their anniversary exhibition 50.
More information: unitpitt.ca.

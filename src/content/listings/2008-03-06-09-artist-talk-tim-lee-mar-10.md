Artist Talk: TIM LEE

Date: Monday, March 10
Time: 6:00 pm
Location: UBC Lasserre, 6333 Memorial Road, room 102

In conjunction with the exhibition "Exponential Future" at the Morris and Helen Belkin Art Gallery, artist Tim Lee will present a talk about his work and invite questions.


Tim Lee is a Vancouver based artist whose work is represented in museum collections worldwide. Recent exhibitions include New Work/New Acquisitions, Museum of Modern Art (New York), Intertidal, MuHKA Museum van Hedendaagse Kunst (Antwerpen, Belgium), Sliding Doors, Tate Modern (London), and Appearances, Musée d'art contemporain (Montréal). Upcoming exhibitions include solo shows at Presentation House Gallery (North Vancouver), CCA Wattis Institute (San Francisco), and the Contemporary Art Museum, (Houston), as well as the Sydney Biennale. He is represented by Cohan & Leslie (New York), Johnen & Schöttle (Cologne), Tracey Lawrence Gallery (Vancouver), and Lisson Gallery (London).


For more information contact Julie Bevan at julie.bevan@ubc.ca or 604 822 3640.

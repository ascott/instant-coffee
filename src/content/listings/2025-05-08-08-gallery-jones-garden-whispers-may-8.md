Artist: Mira Song
Gallery: Gallery Jones
Exhibition title: Garden Whispers
Exhibition dates: May 8th to May 31st
Exhibition Statement:

As a young child, Mira Song"an artist who splits her time between Vancouver,
Canada and Seoul, South Korea"spent hours among her mothers carefully
manicured bonzai trees. She would imagine herself miniaturized, sitting on a bench
beneath the branches of a tree whose growth had been managed with reverence.
These early imaginings are the foundation of a creative life that challenges aspects
of space, rewards deep-looking and takes a comprehensive approach to the
experience of art.

The unbounded meets the ordered in Songs interpretation of the natural world. The
relationship to nature in a densely populated urban environment in Asia is quite
different from the full-frontal assault that the Canadian wilderness can present,
sometimes just beyond the city limits. Aspects of space are the tool used to reign in
the teeming organic matter within her canvases. Song plays with scale, and subtle
compositional choices allude to a sense of the viewer looking at the paintings as if
looking into a museum diorama or at an architectural model. The imagination is
activated and in that moment the images of fern fronds and potted plants slip from
the real to the surreal.

This is the act of deep-looking that Song is pointing us toward, where absorbing the
art is not so much an intellectual exercise as an emotional and psychological one. In
several Asian languages, the notion of rest is expressed with a combination
stopping, deep breathing, tree, human, leaning and other expressions of
physicality. The mind / body dichotomy is broken down in the experience of nature
as it is in the experience of Songs paintings. Like lying on your back under a tree
and looking up through the foliage, so the paintings layers serve to both provide
exciting details and a place for the mind to rest.

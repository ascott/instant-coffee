PREFIX PHOTO: CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

Prefix Photos call for submissions deadline is quickly approaching!
Submit your work by July 1, 2019 for consideration in the fortieth issue
of Prefix Photo magazine.

Prefix Photo is an engaging magazine that seeks to represent the breadth
of practices and concepts that surround the transformation of light into
image. Each issue presents the work of emerging and established Canadian
and international artists and analyses the ideas, issues and contexts that
inform the reception of their work.

Prefix Photo encourages submissions of new or previously unpublished
photographic, media and digital art for editorial consideration. Proposals
from young and emerging artists and from artists from all cultural and
regional communities of Canada are particularly encouraged.

Submissions should include the following:
up to 20 digital image files numbered and labeled with your name and
title of the work (1024 x 768 pixels, JPEG, max 500K)
an image list 
a written description of the project
a curriculum vitae

Please email submissions to:
Eliot Wright
Production Manager
production@prefix.ca

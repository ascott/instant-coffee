The Vancouver Art Gallery presents Reclusive Cosmopolitans: A Series of
Poetry Activations

Date: Tuesday, July 24 at 6:30pm in the First and Second Floor Galleries -
poetry series wraps up with readings by Colin Browne, Danielle LaFrance,
Shazia Hafiz Ramji and Kerriann Cardinal.

In conjunction with the exhibitions David Milne: Modern Painting and Cabin
Fever, join the Gallery for a series of poetry interventions taking place
on select Tuesday evenings through June and July. This series of programs
will provide a space for the literary and visual arts to convene and find
new ways of relating to each other.

Poems will be selected and performed throughout these exhibitions by
Danielle LaFrance, Colin Browne, Shazia Hafiz Ramji, Kerriann Cardinal,
Steffanie Ling and Stacey Ho, among others. Using the ideas of retreat and
solitude as a starting point, texts selected will be read, sung or
otherwise offer different kinds of encounters with the work on view at the
Gallery. The texts recited by the poets represent a dialogue with the
exhibitions and their concerns"be it an interrogation, an argument, an
appreciation, a love letter, a question, a complaint, a commentary, a
contextualization"performing myriad ways of engaging.

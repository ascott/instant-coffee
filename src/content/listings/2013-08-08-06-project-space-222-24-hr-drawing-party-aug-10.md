24-hr Drawing Party is a free collaborative neighborhood drawing
event where community members are invited to Project Space to
draw, paint, doodle, share ideas and meet other community
members. It is open to all, including Chinatown residents, seniors,
young families and members of the general public.

The event will remain open for 24-hrs from August 10 at noon to
August 11 at noon. There will be various workshops on drawing and
being creative. All participants will be encouraged to draw, paint or
doodle and all materials—such as paper, markers, traditional
Chinese calligraphy pens, crayons, pens and pencils—will be
provided.

The event will create a space to share ideas and meet other
members of the community in a very accessible and no-cost
environment. The 24-hr time is given so participants can attend at
any time of the day or night, before breakfast, at lunch time or after
dinner or work.

Snacks and beverages will be available.

Project Space is a bookshop, publisher and alternative art space
located in Chinatown.
The 24-hr Drawing Party is funded by the Vancouver Foundation.

Project Space, 222 E Georgia St
August 10 at Noon to August 11 at Noon

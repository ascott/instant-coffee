Third Friday reading series
Friday, February 22
1391 Commercial Dr, Vancouver
730pm

Join us at People's Co-op Bookstore for a reading.

Natalie Knight is Yurok from northern California and Navajo from New Mexico. She is an 
organizer with Alliance Against Displacement and and editor with The Volcano newspaper. 

Aaron Vidaver was declared and ordered to exist by the Supreme Court of British Columbia in 
spring 1970. Documentary residue of the acts leading up to and including this declaration and 
order, with related selections from the subsequent twenty years, are transcribed in Counter-
Interpellation, Volume One, which appeared in summer 2018, from CUE.

Contemporary Art Gallery

A Public Conversation with Jan Verwoert and Elizabeth McIntosh

Friday, December 10, 7pm

In conjunction with Violet’s Hair, Elizabeth McIntosh’s solo exhibition at
the Contemporary Art Gallery, we are hosting a public conversation between
art critic Jan Verwoert and the artist. The conversation will be led by a
slide presentation of McIntosh’s work.

Available at the CAG is A Good Play, an artist monograph on McIntosh’s
recent work with an essay by Verwoert published by ECU Press, 2010.

Jan Verwoert is an art historian and critic based in Berlin. He is a
contributing editor to Frieze magazine and also writes for various art
publications including Afterall and Metropolis M. His book Bas Jan Ader -
In Search of the Miraculous was published in 2006 by Afterall Books/MIT
Press. Verwoert recently co-curated Yes, No & Other Options for the
contemporary art festival Art Sheffield 08 and has been a guest professor
of Contemporary Art and Theory at the Academy of Umeå, Sweden, and the
Royal College of Art, London.

Elizabeth McIntosh’s recent exhibitions include A Good Play, Diaz
Contemporary, Toronto (2010), Cut Out, Goodwater, Toronto (2009) and
Parisian Laundry, Montréal. She has participated in group exhibitions at
the Vancouver Art Gallery (2006), Santa Monica Museum of Art (2006), and
Hallwalls, Buffalo, (2003). McIntosh lives and works in Vancouver, where
she is a respected educator at the Emily Carr University of Art + Design.
She is represented by Diaz Contemporary in Toronto.

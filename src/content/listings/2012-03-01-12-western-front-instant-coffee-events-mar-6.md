==Classic Reading Group==
Every Tuesday evening in March (6, 13, 20 & 27) 7pm Instant Coffee 
will host a reading group for TALE OF TWO CITIES by Charles 
Dickens.

2012 is Charles Dickens 200th birthday. This bicentennial seems a
remarkable event. Sure time passes, and Dickens is a quintessential
marker of his era. His prose tightly detailing the minutia of a
propose abundance of characters that navigate a precipitously
industrializing western civilization. He manifests the drudgery of the
everyday expressed through his long drawn out descriptions that are
tumbled with the stress of navigating insurmountable changes in
technology, class values and social mores. Yet we’re searching for
something in his manifold stories that is prescient. We’ve chosen A
Tale of Two Cities for a reading group we’re hosting as part of our
exhibition Feeling So Much and Doing Little at the Western Front. This
of all his novels seems to traverse historical events of upending
economic and social revolution that draw analogous lines to current
extremes in monetary divisions.

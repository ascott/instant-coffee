ABOUT THE ARTISTS

Shizuka Kai is a versatile artist skilled in puppetry, set design, illustration, directing
and jewelry making. With extensive training from Nihon University in Japan and
Studio 58, where she earned the Earl Klein Memorial Scholarship, she has built a
robust career in television, film, and theater. Kai has collaborated with notable
companies such as Arts Club and Electric Company, winning three Jessie
Richardson Awards and receiving both an Ovation Award and nomination.
Additionally, her achievements include the Bill Millerd Artist Fund in 2019 and the
Steven B Jung Award in 2022.

Cindy Mochizukicreates multimedia installations, audio fiction, performances,
animation, and drawings. Her works explore the manifestation of story
and its
relationship to site specificity, invisible histories, archives and memory work. She has
exhibited, performed and screened her work in Canada, the US, Australia and
Japan. Recent exhibitions and screenings include the DOXA Documentary Film
Festival, Vancouver; Kamloops Art Gallery; Nanaimo Art Gallery; Vancouver Art
Gallery; Frye Art Museum, Seattle;and Yonago City Museum, Japan. She was the
recipient of the Vancouver Mayors Arts Award in New Media and Film (2015) and
the Jack and Doris Shadbolt Foundation for the Visual Arts VIVA Award (2020).

ABOUT THE EXHIBITION
Some stories are best told at twilight. As the sun sets below the horizon and
darkness emerges, the veil lifts between the everyday and the spirit realm.
Supernatural creatures inhabit our world: haunting dreams and lullabies, appearing
in the pages of legends and recounted through the memories of elders. This magic
manifests in the whimsical storytelling of artist Cindy Mochizuki. "stories best told at
twilight"
focuses on the artists telling of Japanese myths, legends and folklore. Each
artwork"which might manifest as a short animation, an elaborate installation or a
singular performance"is the result of an abundance of images and objects that
came before. This solo exhibition goes behind the curtain, taking a rare look at the
artists usually hidden creative process and spanning artworks created over more
than a decade.

"stories best told at twilight" is on view until November 17, 2024.

Lauren Crazybulls Reception celebrates works from her 18-month Fellowship with 221A.
Working primarily in oil portraiture and painting, Lauren considers Indigenous presence and
multiplicity by creating worlds where honest portrayals trespass onto romantic
representations of Indigeneity.

Reception showcases three of Laurens oil portraits, accompanied by photographic portraits
of Blackfoot community members. Visitors are invited to take flowers home and engage with
a selection of annotated books that were informative to her fellowship work.

A new poem by Cree poet and author Billy-Ray Belcourt, written for Reception, will also be on
display.

Reception runs from April 16 to May 15 at 825 Pacific Street. It is open for viewing on
Tuesdays to Saturdays at 11AM-4PM (except Friday May 1).

Reception also includes two free public events:

Opening Night: Wednesday, April 15, 6"8PM

Artists Tea: Saturday, April 25, 2"4PM

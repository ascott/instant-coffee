CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

Painting on the Edge

Deadline: June 5th, 2008

Exhibition Dates: August 19-September 7, 2008

$8000.00 in Prizes

 

Federation Gallery
1241 Cartwright Street | Vancouver, BC Canada V6H 4B7
Telephone: Office: 604.681.2744 | Gallery: 604.681.8534

 

 

The Federation of Canadian Artists is proud to announce the 6th annual

Painting on the Edge, Open International Juried Exhibition. 

 

This annual exhibition is open to artists working in all painting, printmaking & drawing media. Abstract, expressive, impressionist or realistic styles are welcome. Successful entries will combine an original subject or concept and feature excellent composition and skillful use of the chosen medium. The theme is to be interpreted by the innovation of the artist and reflected in the work.

 

This years Jurors are Makiko Hara, Alan Wylie and Mark Neufeld.

 

The Federation of Canadian Artists was founded in 1941.  With over 2,000 members across Canada and around the world, we are a national charity whose mandate is to support and promote the visual fine arts in Canada through education, exhibition and communication programs. If you are interested in becoming a member please contact our office at 604.681.2744 or fcaoffice@artists.ca

 

For more information or to download the Painting on the Edge Prospectus please visit our website, www.artists.ca

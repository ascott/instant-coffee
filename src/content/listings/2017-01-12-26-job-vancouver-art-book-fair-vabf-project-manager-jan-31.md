Organization Description:
The Vancouver Art Book Fair is a non-profit organization that seeks to establish 
Vancouver as an international centre for artists' publishing. Its core project, the annual 
international Vancouver Art Book Fair, is a critical piece of cultural infrastructure that has 
served as a platform for local artists and has brought hundreds of international artists' 
work to Vancouver over the past five years. Acting as a hub for Vancouver's art 
publishing communities year round, the organization supports the visibility, creation and 
circulation of art publishing as an artistic medium and social practice, and produces 
curatorial and publishing projects that are inclusive to dialogues in the visual arts, 
literary arts and graphic design. Past and ongoing projects include Monthly Open 
Studios, publication projects and collaborations as VABF Press, participation in book 
fairs such as the Toronto Art Book Fair, LA Art Book Fair and Open Air Art Book Fair 
(Stockholm), and collaborative exhibitions such as Print Ready: Stockholm Edition 
(Minibar Gallery, Stockholm, May 2016) . 

Job Description:
VABF is seeking a new Project Manager for the 2017 Vancouver Art Book Fair. Working 
with guidance and supervision from the organization's Executive and Artistic Director, 
Tracy Stefanucci, this role will oversee all aspects of the international event and will 
require significant project management and leadership experience. For the right 
candidate, this challenging position offers an opportunity for significant professional 
growth and a chance to shape this ambitious national project as it moves into its next 
chapter.

Reporting to the Executive Director, the Project Manager is responsible for supervising 
and leading a team made up of independent contractors and volunteers in order to 
organize and execute a successful Vancouver Art Book Fair event from October 13 to 
15, 2017 at the Vancouver Art Gallery.

The successful candidate will be responsible for: 
- Maintaining an awareness of all of the moving parts of the event's organization and 
production and ensuring that all deliverables are achieved on time and to standard 
(supporting, solving problems, liaising and ensuring communication is clear among all 
team members)
- Leading the team for VABF 2017 (comprised of a Curatorial Assistant, Marketing & 
Publicity Coordinator, Design Team and VABF Leadership Team), with support from the 
Board
- Confirming and liaising with VABF 2017 presenters (Talks and Artists' Projects) and 
handling all Letters of Agreement, payouts, etc.
- Working with the Artistic Director to draft the VABF 2018 presenter line-up in July 2017 
and collecting preliminary documents and interest for fall granting season
- Coordinating Exhibitors and overseeing Exhibitor callout, applications and registrations
- Overseeing the VABF 2017 project budget
- Assisting the Board with fundraising to ensure targets for VABF 2017 are met
- Undertaking grant reporting for VABF 2017 and grant writing for 2018
- Outreach, liaising and making agreements with partners, sponsors, collaborators and 
contracted individuals or organizations (including ensuring VABF's end of agreements 
are met)
- Supporting the development of the organization's institutional archive
- Shaping and maintaining a long-term vision for the event and related projects and 
relationships

Requirements:
- Strong event coordination experience managing multifaceted, multi-day projects
- Strong leadership experience managing teams of staff or volunteers
- Stellar writing, communication and networking skills
- Excellence at multi-tasking, meeting deadlines and maintaining timely correspondence 
and accurate records
- Proven track record of successful fundraising and financial management
- Ability to be self-directed and achieve deliverables on deadline with little supervision
- An awareness of and deep curiosity toward local, national and international art 
publishing activities and communities
- Passion for artists' publishing and an interest in the intersection of art, design and 
literature in publication

Additional Info:

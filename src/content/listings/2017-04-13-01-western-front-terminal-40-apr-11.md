Website: terminal.front.bc.ca

TERMINAL is a four-part project that examines single user interfaces, and the 
influence of technology on the adaptation of artistic forms. The project addresses 
itself to an idea of what different hardware units, operating systems, and user 
environments have offered to artists and to the viewers, readers or users of their 
work. Each iteration considers a touchstone in computer interfacing. 

The final installation and website launch looks to so-called 'social media' and the 
surface of art forms that have emerged alongside a super-saturated online culture of 
image-based exchange. 

Included are two new provocations: a beta version of the State of Exceptional 
Webnation, a new website project-in-development by Desearch Repartment, and a 
newly commissioned text by Toronto-based artist Jean-Paul Kelly, who offers a critical 
analysis of interface surfaces, calling for an ethics of the formal analysis required to 
resist 'zombie formalism' as it occurs in certain Post Internet practices.

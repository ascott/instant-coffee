Vancouver artist Jin-me Yoon has revisited her film negative archive to produce Hey 
You, Ya You! (Jimmie Yoo), a set of fifteen B&W 8 x 10" silver gelatin prints originally 
shot in 1991 on colour 2 " film. Bridging nearly three decades of Yoon's artistic 
practice, the work testifies to the artist's sustained investment in notions of 
performativity involving the intersubjective formation of identities and the apparatus 
of the camera.

Each image is unique. Individual price | $475

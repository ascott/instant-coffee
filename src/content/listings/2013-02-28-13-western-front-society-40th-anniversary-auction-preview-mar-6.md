Western Front / 40th Anniversary Auction Preview / MAR 6

Wednesday, March 6, 7-9pm
Western Front Grand Luxe Hall
303 East 8th Avenue

FREE

Join us for a free public preview of this year's auction artworks in the Western 
Front's newly renovated Grand Luxe Hall. The evening will also open the new 
exhibition by Abbas Akhavan in the Western Front gallery space.

Participating artists in this year's auction include:

Jeremy Bailey, Ahbyah Baker, Neil Beloufa, Hank Bull, Aleksandra 
Domanovic, The Everything Co., Vrendan Fernandes, Julia Feyrer, Fieldwork, 
Luke Fowler, Rodney Graham, Jordy Hamilton, Mona Hatoum, Tamara 
Henderson, Allison Hrabluik, Paul Kajander, Eun Kyung Kim, Robert Kleyn, 
Nick Lakowski, Tiziana La Melia, Oliver Laric, Khan Lee, Arvo Leo, Sara Ludy, 
Kelly Lycan, Liz Magor, Ryan Mathieson, Eric Metcalfe, Isabelle Pauwels, 
Nicolas Sassoon, Corin Sworn, and more.

For more information about the auction and participating artists, please visit 
the Western Front's auction website at 
http://westernfrontauction2013.tumblr.com/

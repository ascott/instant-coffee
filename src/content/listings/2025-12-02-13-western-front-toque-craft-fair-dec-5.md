Celebrate our biggest Toque Craft Fair yet at the Opening Party!

Join us this Friday night and be among the first to shop a curated lineup of over sixty BC-
based artists, craftspeople, and designers.

Sip and snack as you browse: enjoy a Toque Negroni crafted by The Woods Spirit Co., or
choose from drinks by our Mount Pleasant neighbours Please! Beverage Co., 33 Acres
Brewing Company, and Oddity Kombucha. Super Thai Food Truck will also be on site,
serving street"food favourites perfect for nibbling while you shop.

Skip the line by making an advance donation here to get straight to the party. The first 300
donations of $15 or more receive a limited-edition Toque tote bag upon arrival.

Your support matters! Proceeds from entrance fees and 30% of all vendor sales help fund
Western Fronts artistic programs and the conservation of our heritage building.

We cant wait to celebrate our vendors and artist-run culture with you!

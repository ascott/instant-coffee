The Syndicate of Public Speakers: Eight times an unknown quantity
Saturday, February 6, 2010

Six Acres (map)
203 Carrall Street
Vancouver, BC

Invented by Portland novelist/publisher Matthew Stadler and curator
Stephanie Snyder, Public Speakers is a collective of autonomous cells in
five cities that commissions and presents new public lectures for a popular
audience. Past events include Psychedelic Logging and Monkey Wreaks Havoc
in Suburbia.

Eight times an unknown quantity brings together eight or more speakers
presenting lectures for Vancouver's first-ever Public Speakers event.

Shudder Gallery Opening:

 Periphery, February 9th - March 1st

Opening reception, February 8th 7pm - 11pm

A collection of photographic work by Paul Christian.

Paul Christian is a photo based artist who resides in Vancouver, B.C. His work is derived from an academic perspective that is both art historic and urban geographic. These influences contribute to the aesthetics and content of his photographs. A recurrent theme of predominately man made constructs concerning city and it's inevitable decay are evident in Paul's work. There is a sense of unveiling the reality behind the glory of our "Olympic" cityscape. The work examines that which is overlooked, discarded, and valueless in a city that is rapidly transforming.

In this time of great change, rebirth, and reconstruction, it is important to have documentation of the diversity of Vancouver, before it is white washed into a sleek, steal, suburbia. Paul's work documents aspects of the city soon to be lost and will have you looking at your surroundings with a new perspective.

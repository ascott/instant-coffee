Means of Production:
Sound, Silence, and Process
With Guillermo Galindo and Raven Chacon
June 25, 2020, 7pm

Please RSVP here: https://www.eventbrite.com/e/means-of-production-sound-
silence-and-process-tickets-110167152920?aff=ebdssbonlinesearch
Join Fillip and artists Guillermo Galindo and Raven Chacon on Thursday, June 25, at
7pm for a free, online Means of Production workshop.

Reflecting on the current socio-political moment from California and New Mexico
respectively, Galindo and Chacon will discuss sound and silence as a means and
method for engaged artistic production.

Recent projects by both artists"including Chacons Manifest Destiny opera Sweet
Land, produced in collaboration with The Industry this past March, and Galindos
monumental Border Cantos,
produced in collaboration with photographer Richard
Misrach, and Echo Exodus for documenta 14"reveal ways in which sound can be
used to explore memory and lived experience. In this conversation, Galindo and
Chacon will unpack the concept of a score and performance, discussing ways sound
can function as weapon, instruments can become totemic sculptures, and silence
offers a tool for (non)violent engagement.

Means of Production is an occasional series of free, theoretical workshops
investigating the material conditions of contemporary artistic practice. Organized
alongside our Reading Group series, these ongoing workshops address issues of
labour, infrastructure, and production in a small, seminar-style environment.
Entrance is free but an RSVP is required.

About the Artists

Guillermo Galindo is a Post-Mexican composer and artist currently based in San
Francisco. Galindos artistic practice emerges at the crossroads of instrumental
composition, live improvisation, and ritual,
redefining the conventional limits between
between art, politics, spirituality, and social awareness. His work has been shown at
documenta 14, the CTM Festival (Berlin), and the High Line (NY, forthcoming) and
has been featured on BBC Outlook, NHK World, Radio Tlvision Suisse, NPR, and
CBC, and in Art in America and the New York Times.

Raven Chacon is a composer, performer and installation artist currently based in
Albuquerque, NM. Chacon's work, solo or as a member of Postcommodity, has been
shown at The Kennedy Center, the Whitney Biennial, and documenta 14, among
many others. Chacon has taught hundreds of students to write string quartets as
part of the Native American Composer Apprenticeship Project and is the recipient of
the American Academys Berlin Prize for Music Composition.

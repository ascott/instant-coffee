Please join New Star Books and the Or Gallery to celebrate the publication
of Loitersack, a new book of poetry, poetics, theory, theory theatre and
laugh particles by Donato Mancini.

This launch will include a short poetry reading by Mr. Mancini, to be
followed with the Vancouver premiere public reading of Mancini's 1-act
neo-absurdist play "THEQRY." Featuring: Ash Lee as OFFICER, Jesse Malakoe
as DONNY, Lisa McLeod as ISADORA, Dawn McLeod as MOTHER.

Performance will begin promptly at 9:00 pm.

Books for sale. Refreshments provided. Ask around about the afterparty...

"If the loitersacke be gone springing into a taverne, I'll fetch him
reeling out."

On the same night as the Loitersack event, there is also a reading by Sarah
Dowling and Amy De'Ath at Pulpfiction Books at 7:00 pm. If you are going to
either, you should go to both! We've coordinated the times and locations of
the events to make this possible. Things will get underway at Pulpfiction
at 7:00, and we won't start the performance at the Or Gallery until 9:00.
It's very very very very very easy to get from Pulpficiton books to the Or
Gallery. Just grab the #3 bus right out front of the bookstore. It will
take you right to Hamilton/Cambie, barely a block from the Or. Ample time
to amble downtown.

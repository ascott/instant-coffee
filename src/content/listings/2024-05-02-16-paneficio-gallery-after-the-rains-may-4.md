After the Rains
Arleigh Wood

This series After the Rains follows Woods most recent series Shelter from the
Storm.
These highly textured mixed media paintings are filled with silkscreened abandoned
shelters, painted geometric shapes, hand drawn roots and trees.
Both beautiful and unsettling, these landscapes explore themes of climate change,
Japanese Canadian
internment, ghost towns, gentrification and human resilience.
While these houses are located in spectacular places such as Vancouver, Iceland
and Joshua Tree, the actual location is not of primary importance. It is more the
universality and ubiquity of these makeshift shelters and the stories they contain.
These paintings are part of a search for world that offers safety and hope,
sometimes steeped in difficult histories, environmental threats or touched by the
surreal.
After the rains, new shoots grow from the stilted home bases suggesting strength in
roots as well as promising beginnings.

An Evening of Art & Dance with
Lyse Lemieux and Ziyian Kwan
Friday, May 27, 7:00-9:00pm

The Richmond Art Gallery and dumb instrument Dance are pleased to
present An Evening of Art and Dance. The event will feature two
dances that respond to the Gallery's current exhibition by visual artist
Lyse Lemieux. With her company dumb instrument Dance, Ziyian
Kwan choreographs, performs site-specific installations, and
commissions the work of other artists. In response to Lyse Lemieux's
exhibit, Ziyian will perform an improvised score in dialogue with the
specific works in the gallery spaces. She has also choreographed for
dancer Vanessa Goodman, a dance that considers the exhibit in
Gallery Three. Both performances will be accompanied by musician
and award-winning composer Stefan Smulovitz on viola. The
performance will be followed by a panel discussion with Ziyian Kwan
and exhibiting artist Lyse Lemieux, moderated by RAG Curator Nan
Capogna. Kwan and Lemieux will discuss their inspiration for this
collaboration. This free event is sponsored in part by Canada Council
for the Arts.

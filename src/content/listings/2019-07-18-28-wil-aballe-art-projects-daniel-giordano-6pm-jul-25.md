WAAP is pleased to present Even Cowboys Get Caught in the Rain, the gallerys first 
solo exhibition with New York-based artist, Daniel Giordano. We previously presented 
the artists work at our Lower East Side group exhibition last year at VACATION, where 
his sculpture, Toutoune, was included. The materials list for the work includes the 
following strange conglomerate of objects: aluminium, plastic bags, plastic wrap, goat 
skin cock ring, aluminum tape, cork, correctional fluid, dust, Dior lipstick, bald eagle 
excrement, epoxy paste. His materials often incorporate detritus from the Hudson River 
(and so, the detritus of New Yorkers, and America). Based between New York City and 
Newburgh, Giordano goes on long walks to collect many of his materials, picking up 
what was once forgotten, and his studio is his familys former coat factory. His sculptures 
tell multiple stories, so many stories that theyve become one, freaky, fragmented tale. 
His material sensibilities are informed by his upbringing in industrial Newburgh, NY 
(across the river from Beacon in the Hudson Valley); his environs, particularly his 
family's now defunct coat factory and its detritus; and his Italian-American upbringing. 
The remarkable thing with his material choices is that though they are personal, they are 
evocative of broader American culture. Giordano has experimented with new sculptural 
processes; in a recent show at Mother Gallery, he has deep-fried a motorcycle. The 
sculpture feels thoroughly American and contemporary, as deep frying feels like a wholly 
American invention, and the motorcycle is evocative of Route 66, Harley-Davidson and 
The Big Easy.

For the WAAP show, he will be showing his new series of abject Venetian masks (whose 
bases are moisturizing face masks that he has used and then put through his particular 
set of processes - whether electroplating, glazing, or deep-frying, and then applied with 
a varied set of evocative materials like Orange Tang drink mix, bald eagle excrement 
and Dior lipstick, among others. A second series of Pleasure Pipes, shows him at his 
restrained best; each is configured with a base of black locust wood and adorned with 
glass from Murano.

Giordano is the founder of Vicki Projects, located within his familys factory, which was 
founded by his grandfather, Frank; he named it Vicki Clothing Company, after his aunt. 
Since 2016, the project space has hosted a number of exhibitions; most recently in the 
spring, he curated a show with three Montreal-based artists; the exhibition was featured 
on ArtViewer.

DANIEL GIORDANO lives and works in Newburgh, NY. He earned his MFA from the 
University of Delaware, and has shown nationally and internationally. Recent exhibitions 
include Menage/rie' at Super Dutchess; A Salve of Sorts at Vacation; 'The Big Linguini' 
at Sardine in Brooklyn, NY and Living/Breathing at Morgan Lehman Gallery in New 
York, NY, as well as at the Karpeles Manuscript Library and Museum in Newburgh, NY. 
Daniel Giordano also co-founded and runs Vicki, a project space in upstate New York.

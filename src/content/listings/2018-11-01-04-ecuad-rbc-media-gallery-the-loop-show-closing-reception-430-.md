Visit The LOOP Show, an animated and audio loop installation.
Closing Reception: Thurs, Nov 1, 4:30 - 7:30p

RBC Media Gallery
Oct 27 - Nov 2, 2018
Emily Carr University campus
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

The LOOP Show exhibition is an audio-visual expression of the LOOP, a cyclical and 
seamless return of crafted timelines, using a dynamic installation environment that 
creates an emergent and evolving relationship of all the media. All work is from 
Emily Carr University students and alumni.

VIDEO ARTISTS Alex Gibson, An Mai, Anna Firth, Catelyne Ma, Chhaya Naran, 
Chris Strickler, Donna Chen, Jay Castro, Kaho Yoshida, Kate Woodward, Kat Morris, 
Katrina Schellenberg, Lillia Lai, Merve Yelmen, Nicole Sitanski, Sitji Chou, Sunny 
Nestler

AUDIO ARTISTS Mana Saei, Chantal Croze, Donna Chen, Katrina Schellenberg, 
Lily Margison, Marieke Helmke

Curation and presentation technology were established by Lorelei Pepi (animation 
artist, Asst. Prof), Peter Bussigel (audio artist, Asst. Prof) and Simon L. Overstall 
(audio artist, Tech Staff.)

Thanks to Kevin Romaniuk of Galleries and Exhibitions and Stephen Wichuk of 
Animation.

This event is a production of the Time, Light + Sound Series, a project of the Emily 
Carr University Dynamic Media Faculty, bringing public focus and awareness to the 
arts of cinema, animation, new media and sound.

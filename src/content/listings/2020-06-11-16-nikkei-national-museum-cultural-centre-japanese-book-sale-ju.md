The NNMCC's Pre-loved Japanese Book Sale is taking place Wednesdays - Saturdays 
until June 27, from 11am - 4pm. Since Japanese language bookstores closed their 
doors in Vancouver long before the pandemic, this popular event historically took place 
over one weekend. Now, to support physical distancing and a limit of up to 7 people in 
the book sale room at the main lobby level at any given time, we are extending the book 
sale over the entire month. All book titles are clearly visible to discourage handling 
before purchase. Please
check our website for information on what you should know 
before you visit. https://centre.nikkeiplace.org/

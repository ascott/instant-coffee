Emily Carr University of Art + Design is a school of students, faculty, thinkers and
makers unlike any other. Established in 1925, we are the only
specialized, accredited, public, post-secondary university in British Columbia solely
devoted to education and research in the creative sector and
its associated knowledge economy. We merge research, critical theory and studio
practice in an interdisciplinary environment, strengthening our

work by the integration of our personal and professional practices. Our strategy,
facilities, partnerships and resources are intentionally student-
centred to foster dialogue, expression and open connections in support of the next
generation of creative and cultural leaders.

The Internal Exhibitions Coordinator oversees the production and support of space
allocation and presentation opportunities for students at Emily
Carr. This includes the production of the annual Grad Show,
student exhibitions in
the Michael OBrian Exhibitions Commons (MOEC) and
associated spaces, and other student-led presentation venues. The Coordinator
bridges the public facing mandate of Gallery + Exhibitions with
the learning environment of the university, supporting the ongoing professional
development of students via exhibition and presentation
opportunities.
As the successful applicant, you should possess:
Undergraduate degree, preferably in fine art, from a recognized post-secondary
university.
Minimum of three years recent experience working in an art gallery or university in
an administrative capacity.
Or an equivalent combination of education and experience.
Demonstrated experience with the administration and project management of art
projects, exhibition, or festival presentations.
Sound knowledge of exhibition development and implementation.
Demonstrated experience working with students and emerging artists.
Demonstrated experience working with large teams and multiple,
interdisciplinary
collaborators.
Strong systems thinker and problem solver.
Superior organizational and planning skills.
Strong attention to detail.
Strong interpersonal and communication skills, and a desire to collaborate.
Ability to take initiative and problem-solve.
Ability to work independently and as part of a team.
Ability to work well under pressure and complete work within set time constraints.
Ability to build and maintain effective working relationships with individuals and
groups representing diverse interests.
Proven project management skills.
Some Typical Duties:
1 Leads the administrative support for the production of the annual Grad Show,
including coordinating with faculty leads, administrative supports,
technicians, Academic Affairs, Communications and Student Services. Updates
appropriate timelines and budget documents, coordinates production
schedules and adapts/modifies internal procedures to improve processes.
2 Supports the production of Grad Show outreach
materials, signage, and website,
including working closely with Communications and Events staff to
facilitate content transfer and solicitation from students and faculty. Coordinates the
flow of information for work placement, labels, and exhibition
maps/ wayfinding.
3 Works closely with technical and preparator staff and Facilities on the spatial
design and preparation of university spaces for Grad Show and other
student presentations.
4 Coordinates the installation and de-installation timelines for Grad Show and other
year-round presentation opportunities.

5 Leads institutional debriefs, reporting and creates recommendations for
streamlining systems within the university in support of Grad Show and year-
round student exhibitions opportunities.

6 Administers the Grad Show budget, creating and submitting payment vouchers,
generating reports, tracking budgets, and organizing
interdepartmental expenses.
7 Collects and maintains data regarding student participation in Grad Show and
other presentation opportunities.
8 Liaises with the Student Union in support of Student Exhibitions opportunities,
selection processes and installations throughout the school year.
9 Coordinates and/ or supports opportunities for student presentations (e.g.: mural
installation in stairwells, media screens and other opportunities), in
collaboration with the Student Union.
10 Works closely with Gallery staff to maintain exhibitions booking calendar; liaising
with cross-institutional collaborators around bookings process, install
and de-install timelines for student-led and curricular exhibitions.
11 With the Director + Curator, coordinates workshops, talks, and other learning
opportunities around professional development for students year-round.
12 Reviews and maintains information guidelines for students, staff, and faculty
regarding student exhibition opportunities.
13 Working with the Director + Curator and other appropriate departments/ faculties,
supports the creation of strategic
initiatives in support of student
professional development, space allocation and presentation opportunities.
14 Completes internal event booking and risk assessment forms, as necessary.
15 Performs other related duties as required.

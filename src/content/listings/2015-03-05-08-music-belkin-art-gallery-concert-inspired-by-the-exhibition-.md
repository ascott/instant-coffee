Concert at the Belkin
Friday, March 6, 2015, 2-3 pm

Once again, we are pleased to welcome the UBC Contemporary Players to the
Belkin Art Gallery for a concert inspired by the exhibition Tom Burrows.
Led by Directors Corey Hamm and Paolo Bortolussi, this graduate and
undergraduate student ensemble from the UBC School of Music will animate
the Gallery for an afternoon program celebrating themes from the exhibition.

All welcome. Admission is free.

Brian Topp, Insomnia (2013)

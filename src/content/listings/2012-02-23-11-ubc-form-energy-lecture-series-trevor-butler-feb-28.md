SPRING 2012 FORM & ENERGY LECTURE SERIES
University of British Columbia, School of Architecture + Landscape
Architecture

TREVOR BUTLER (Kelowna, Archineers, www.archineers.com) 
LECTURE
WHEN: February 28, 2012, 12.30pm
WHERE: CIRS Decision Theatre, 2260 West Mall, UBC. Lecture is free 
and open
to the public.

ABOUT: Trevor Butler, P.Eng, CEO Archineers, has over 18 years 
experience
designing leading-edge sustainable systems for buildings in Canada 
and
around the world and is a registered zero-carbon consultant in British
Columbia. He established Archineers in 2000 to bridge the gap 
between
architecture and engineering and champion the cause of sustainability 
in the
design of the built environment. The firm collaborates closely with
architects to develop fully integrated, ecologically efficient responses to
complex problems arising in the design of sustainable buildings.

Form and Energy Series sponsored by BC Hydro PowerSmart. For

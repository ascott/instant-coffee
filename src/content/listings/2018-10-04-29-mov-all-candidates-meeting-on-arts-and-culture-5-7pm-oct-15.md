Vancouver All Candidates Meeting for Arts & Culture at the Museum of Vancouver

Event information:
Date: Monday, Oct. 15, 5-7 pm
Location: Museum of Vancouver (1100 Chestnut St.)
Moderated by Dani Fecko
Admission: Free; please register at artsvotebc.ca

Museum of Vancouver (MOV) and BC Alliance for Arts + Culture have partnered to 
host an All Candidates Meeting prior to the Vancouver municipal election. Eight 
mayoral and three city council candidates will reveal their stance on the importance 
of arts, culture and heritage to a thriving, sustainable Vancouver, 

Confirmed attendance:
Yes Vancouver's Hector Bremner
Pro Vancouver's David Chen
Vancouver First's Fred Harding
Independent Kennedy Stewart
Independent Shauna Sylvester
Coalition Vancouver's Wai Young
Independent Golok Buday

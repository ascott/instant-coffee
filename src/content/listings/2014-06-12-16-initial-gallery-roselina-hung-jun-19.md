Initial Gallery is pleased to present Of Myth and Men featuring recent oil 
paintings by Vancouver based artist Roselina Hung. Combining art-historical 
and well-known mythological motifs with self-reflective imagery, Hungâ€™s 
fantastical oil paintings toy with modern myth-making. The artist, herself often 
portrayed in obvious disguise, interacts with characters and symbols pulled 
from mythological story lines and personal memory. By interweaving personal 
reflection with archetypal symbolism, Of Myth and Men considers the 
parallels between modern personal storytelling and the grand mythologies of 
the Ancients. 

Roselina Hung (b. 1980) is a visual artist living and working in Vancouver. 
She received her MA Fine Art from Central Saint Martins College of Art & 
Design, London, UK and her BFA from the University of British Columbia. 
Artist-in-residencies include SIM, Reykjavik, The Banff Centre, Banff, and Ox-
Bow School of Art, Saugatuck.

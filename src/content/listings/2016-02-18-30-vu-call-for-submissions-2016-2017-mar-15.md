Call for submissions 2016-2017 programming
VU, centre de diffusion et de production de la photographie

Working to position photography within the multidisciplinarity that 
characterizes many contemporary practices, VU invites artists who 
wish to reflect on the photographic image to submit a project for its 
upcoming programming.

Exhibitions (deadline: March 15)
The centre wants to promote innovative practices which demonstrate 
enquiry into and reflection upon the artistic potential of photography. 
Artists working in other disciplines and who wish to investigate their 
work's relation to photography are also invited to propose a project. VU 
has two galleriesthe Americas space and the Europe spacein 
which it presents some twelve exhibitions annually.

Artist's residencies (deadline: March 15)
For artists who work in photography or wish to experiment with the 
medium, VU offers residencies. Priority is given to innovative proposals 
that will benefit from the centre's facilities, and experimental and 
research-based projects are encouragedthe residency might be used 
to take images, reflect on the medium, or set out on new avenues of 
artistic enquiry. A residency may eventually lead to public 
dissemination of the work through an exhibition, publication, 
conference, etc.

The centre offers artistic and technical support to artists chosen for a 
residency. According to the specifications of the chosen project, a 
residency provides the artist with a period of access to its equipment 
and includes accommodation as well as a credit towards production 
fees. VU's production facilities have a complete range of equipment, 
including scanners, large-format inkjet printers, and darkrooms. 
Technical support is provided by a team of professionals. Residencies 
usually last around ten days.

For an exhibition or residency your submission should include:

a cover letter
a project description (1 page max.)
an artist's statement (1 page max.)
your CV (3 pages max.)
a maximum of 20 images of the proposed project and recent work
(Mac-compatible RGB JPEG files, max. 1024 x 768 pixels at 72 ppi)
any other relevant documentation
Your submission must be sent as a .zip folder by e-mail via WeTransfer 
to artistique@vuphoto.org. Please indicate in the subject line whether 
you are submitting a proposal for an exhibition or a residency.

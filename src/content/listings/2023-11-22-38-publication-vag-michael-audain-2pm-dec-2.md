MICHAEL AUDAIN | PICTURES ON THE WALL: BUILDING A CANADIAN ART
COLLECTION BOOK LAUNCH

SAT DEC 2, 2023 | 2 PM

Michael Audains passion for art began when he was a teenager, taping
reproductions of Bruegel paintings to his dorm room walls. Over the years, his eye
for art developed and together with wife Yoshiko (Yoshi) Karasawa, he acquired one
of Canadas most notable collections. InPictures on the Wall, Audain tells the story
of his first tentative purchases to ultimately donating much of the collection to
Whistlers Audain Art Museum.

Come
celebrate one of Canadas most dedicated and generous patrons of the arts,
Michael Audain, as he unveils his new memoir, Pictures on the Wall: Building a
Canadian Art Collection.

Michael Audain will be in conversation with Sirish Rao, Director of Public
Engagement & Learning at the Gallery, which will be followed by a reception where
Audain will sign copies of the book.

Books are available for sale at the event.

Submit your music video to Render: The 3rd Annual Vancouver International
Music Video Festival!

Render is an annual event showcasing the art of the music video and the
creativity of the directors/artists who produce them. Submissions from
independent and commercial directors, emerging or established, are
encouraged. Render is currently the only festival of its kind in Canada and
aims to become North America's premier music video festival.

Last year Render screened 26 winning music videos from more than 20
countries. Included in the 2015 Official Selections were videos such as
Bjork's Black Lake (directed by Andrew Thomas Huang), Royskopp's Skulls
(directed by Stian Anderson), and Death From Above 1979's Virgins (directed
by Eva Michon).

Render is looking for the best music videos of 2015 & 2016.

You can submit your music video here: [https://filmfreeway.com/festival/render](https://filmfreeway.com/festival/render).

The final deadline is Monday, August 15th, 2016.

The screening takes place at the Vancity Theatre, the home of the Vancouver
International Film Festival, on Friday, November 4th, 2016.

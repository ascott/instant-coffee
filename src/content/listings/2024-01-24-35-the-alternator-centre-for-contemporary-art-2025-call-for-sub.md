The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art
2025 Call for Submissions

The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is now accepting exhibition proposals from
artists and curators for the 2025 programming of their Main Gallery and Project Gallery.
The Alternator Centre for Contemporary Art is an artist-run centre located in Kelowna,
B.C.
The Alternator presents
year-round exhibitions in all media by emerging and mid-
career
Canadian and international artists, as well as artist talks, exchanges, panel
discussions,
fundraising events, publications, workshops and other events that promote an
awareness
of contemporary art practices.
Works will be selected by the Programming Committee based on the following criteria:
Compatibility with the Alternator's mandate
Quality of the artwork (with an emphasis on experimental approach)
Clarity of the written proposal
Production cost and availability
Further, the Programming Committee seeks diversity of perspective, medium and
theme
when establishing the annual exhibition calendar.
The Alternator welcome submissions from under-represented artists of all backgrounds
including, but not limited to, Indigenous, Black, and racialized persons; refugee,
newcomer
and immigrant persons; two-spirit, LGBTQ+ and gender non-binary persons, persons
with
diverse abilities, and those on low-incomes or living in poverty.
They are committed to
ensuring equitable access to our submissions process. Please contact them if you
require
any accommodations to participate.
The Alternator is committed to fostering artists' professional autonomy and is proud to
pay
successful applicants CAR/FAC fees. The deadline for applications will be March 18,
2024
(Midnight).
To see the full description of submission requirements for both the Main and Project
Galleries please visit [https://www.alternatorcentre.com/submit-main-project-gallery](https://www.alternatorcentre.com/submit-main-project-gallery).

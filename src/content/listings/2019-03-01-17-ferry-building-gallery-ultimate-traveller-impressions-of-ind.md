Ultimate Traveller: Impressions of Indonesia
Wednesday, March 6, 2019 7-9 p.m.
Ferry Building Gallery
1414 Argyle Ave, West Vancouver, BC V7T 1C2
Join us for a series of imaginative travel talks on faraway places by 
geographer and travel photographer Peter Langer. Discover the wealth 
of human history, the natural beauty of our planet and explore a 
diversity of cultures. Experience the magic of these fascinating worlds 
through a beautiful audio-visual journey.
You will also hear Peter's impressions, curious anecdotes, frustrations 
and some of the little secrets these places have to offer. Peter Langer 
has travelled to over 175 countries.
The Indonesian archipelago spreads over 5,200 km between the Asian 
mainland and Australia, all of it within the tropics, and comprises 
17,000 islands. Its ethnic, cultural and linguistic diversity is 
correspondingly great - more than 500 languages and dialects are 
spoken by its 246 million people, whose fascinating customs and 
lifestyles are a major attraction. The largely volcanic nature of the 
islands has created tall cloud-swept mountains swathed in the green of 
rice terraces or rainforests, dropping to blindingly bright beaches and 
vivid blue seas, the backdrop for Southeast Asia's biggest wilderness 
areas and wildlife sanctuaries.
This show and lecture will explore the islands of Sumatra, Java, 
Borneo, Flores, Komodo and Sulawesi. We conclude our journey 
visiting the legendary Baliem Valley in the highlands of Western New 
Guinea.

Walk-In Cinema: The Lost Weekend

Come join us for a neighborhood movie night! Iris Collective presents a 
weekend Walk-In Cinema with the participation of Purple Thistle and 
support of Vancouver Small Neighborhood Grants. Screenings will take at 
the Purple Thistle, #260-975 Vernon Drive. 
Lost in the City: Friday October 24th 7pm join us for "Playtime" by Jacques 
Tati 
Lost in the Wilderness: Saturday October 25th 7pm join us for "Walkabout" 
by Nicolas Roeg 
Feature films will be preceded by short films from Iris Collective and Purple 
Thistle. The event is all ages and free admission. please visit 
[http://purplethistle.ca/](http://purplethistle.ca/) for details on venue accessibility and 
[http://irisfilmcollective.tumblr.com](http://irisfilmcollective.tumblr.com) for event details

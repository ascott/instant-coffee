Help us raise $5,000 for our new project: "peripherie-peripherie"

We're $2,000 away from reaching our goal with less than a week left! We need to raise
$5,000 to pay better contributor
fees to the writers and artists involved in the first of our
special new digital project, called "peripherie-peripherie."

This project has been in the works for literal years now, and we need your help to
make it come to fruition! peripherie-peripherie is an experimental thematic chapbook
project produced by Peripheral Review, to be released bi-annually in Spring/Summer
and Fall/Winter beginning in late June 2023.

Each issue will be guest edited by a different Canadian artist, curator, or writer and will
include an exhibition review, an interview, an experimental feature piece, and a
featured artist"-all encompassed within a unique thematic concept. The issues will be
available for purchase on a sliding scale and includes access to an interactive digital
version of the issue on our website, as well as a downloadable print version that
readers can print out and enjoy irl.

We know we can't work alone, and due to release setbacks due to unsuccessful grant
applications etc. we now need
the extra financial backing of our dedicated readers and
supporters to help us make this project happen. Peripheral Review is an independent
platform that prioritizes emerging and underrepresented artists, writers, and spaces
and our platform strives to critically expand the notion of how art criticism can be read
and presented.

peripherie-peripherie is a new way of reading art writing and engaging with artist
projects that is beyond the physical page and pushes the limits of what can be
engaged with digitally...

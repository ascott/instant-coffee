Battle Your Demons in Dreamspace/ concert with Linda Foxx

Thursday, June 29 at 9:22 PM - 12 AM (Starts at Sunset)

144 E 6th Vancouver BC The James Black Gallery

Guest resident Layla Mrozowski from Philidelphia regales us with her character person 
Linda Foxx in a performative audio visual experience. She engages with instruments 
that function as both sonic instruments and sculptural objects, without one role 
(instrument or sculpture) dominating the other. In other words, the aesthetics of an 
object are equally as important as its sonic function, and the objects will be designed 
and built with equal attention. Her show will happen twice with a 30 minute interim.

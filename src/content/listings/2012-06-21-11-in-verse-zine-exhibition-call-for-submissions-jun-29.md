IN-Verse is a zine exhibition highlighting small run self-publications by 
young creatives of Vancouver. These publications include but are not 
limited to, comic books, illustrated guides, narrated images, short 
stories or small pieces of writing. The event will be hosted by Solder 
and Sons with the opening date scheduled for July and book screening 
to continue throughout the summer months. 

Submissions are open to all members of Vancouver’s emerging 
creative community, but please remember that due to space limitations 
we will only be able to accept a limited number of artists. 

Please submit one of the following:

-a pdf copy of a completed publication that has yet to be publicly 
released

OR

-a short written proposal (max 250 words) for a small publication as well 
as samples of past work

All artists should also include a short statement introducing who they 
are and their current art practice.

Email submissions to inverse.submission@gmail.com. 

Deadline is June 29th, 2012

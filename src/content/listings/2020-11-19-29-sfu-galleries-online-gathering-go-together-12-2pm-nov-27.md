Gathering: Go Together

Friday, November 27, 12 - 2pm PDT
www.sfu.ca/makebelievebathroom

Join the artist for group visit hours in the Make-Believe Bathroom. Bring your friends and
use the chat rooms to vent, ask questions, give advice, gossip, et cetera.

Amy Lam is an artist and writer. She was born in Hong Kong and lives in Toronto. Lam
collaborated with Jon McCurley as the artist duo Life of a Craphead, in conceptual art,
performance, media art, and curating. Their work will be presented at the Seoul
MediaCity Biennale in 2021. Lam is a founding member of Friends of Chinatown
Toronto, a grassroots group working against displacement in Torontos downtown
Chinatown, and she is also currently working on poetry.

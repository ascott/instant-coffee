a breeze, for you - by dani neira

Opening reception on Friday May 24, 7-9 pm
The Vault Gallery - 1st floor 750 Fairfield Road, Victoria, BC
Exhibition runs until June 9, see @thevault_gallery for hours

a breeze, for you explores the architectures of power"including algorithms, private
property, and language"that shape our experiences of the world. Using the breeze
block as a point of departure, the mixed-media installation wields objects that are
ornamental, speculative, porous, and reflective to consider our relationships to built
spaces. By drawing attention to, simulating, and disrupting everyday materials, a
breeze for you creates an offscreen world that blurs the boundaries between digital
and physical. It asks, by becoming a breeze, what structures
might we slip through,
refuse, collapse?

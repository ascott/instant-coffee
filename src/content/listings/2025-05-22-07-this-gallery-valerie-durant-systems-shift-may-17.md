In the exhibition Systems Shift, artist Valerie Durant presents work from several
series that explore the boundaries between photographic image-making and
sculpture. These pieces serve as an inquiry into material culture, consumption, and
the interconnectedness of living systems, addressing themes of regeneration,
ecological precarity, and the impact of human intervention on the natural world.

Systems Shift blurs the line between photography and sculpture through four
interconnected bodies of work that explore material culture, environmental precarity,
and regeneration. Using repurposed materials"from paper shopping bags to forest
floor debris"the artist reflects on climate change, human fragility, and the cycles of
the natural world. Through layered imagery, cast forms, and sensorial installations,
she offers a poignant call for reflection, rooted in a commitment to sustainability and
care for future generations.

The exhibition closes Saturday, May 17th. The gallery is open Fridays and Saturdays
from noon to 5 pm.

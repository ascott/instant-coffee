Burnaby Art Gallery 2009 Lecture Series

The Compulsion of Collecting: From the Personal to the Institutional

Sunday January 18, 2009, 1-4 pm

(panel discussion from 1-3pm, reception/open conversation from 3-4pm)

".... Every passion borders on the chaotic, but the collector's passion
borders on the chaos of memories." - Walter Benjamin, Unpacking My Library

What do you collect? Why is one object more collectable then another?

How do public collections connect or derive from our individual compulsion
to engage in the act of collecting? When do artists respond to shifts in
collecting trends?

These are just some of the questions that will be posed for discussion.

Join us for a conversation that will examine and dissect the human
compulsion to collect.

Panelists:

Burnaby Art Gallery director/curator, Darrin Martens

Artist, Deborah Koenker

First Nations artist, author and independent curator, Peter Morin

Popular culture object collector, Maurice Guibord

Moderator:

Artist and fine art programmer, Sharon Kallis

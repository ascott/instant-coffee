Hey, hey, mama, said the way you move, gonna make you sweat, gonna 
make you groove: The Music Appreciation Society Presents Led Zeppelin

Tuesday, April 8, 2014 
Panel and Performance
7pm, In the Gallery 

The Music Appreciation Society chairs a panel discussion with contributions 
by Myfanwy MacLeod, Kevin Schmidt, Phil Smith, and Jean Smith, followed by 
a musical performance by KID ZEPPELIN, an ad-hoc Led Zeppelin tribute band 
comprised of young teens.

The Music Appreciation Society (TMAS) is a make-believe secret society 
dedicated to the public appreciation of music. The primary activity of TMAS is 
to promote the discussion of music, especially popular music, in an art 
context. TMAS presents panel discussion listening events and music 
performances, and has produced a compilation CD, titled The Music 
Appreciation Society Presents Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. TMAS is 
currently working on a series of books, interviewing artists about their 
relationship with music. The Music Appreciation Society is an ongoing 
collaborative project of Vancouver-based artists Brady Cranfield and Kathy 
Slade. 

Kidzeppelin is Jacqueline (vocals), Myles (guitar), Ayla (bass) and Gina 
(drums).

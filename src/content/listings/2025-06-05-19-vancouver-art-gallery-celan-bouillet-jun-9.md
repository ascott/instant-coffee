Featured Artist | Celan Bouillet|
Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery

In To Meet the Moon, Celan Bouillet creates dreamlike landscapes shaped by
memory, myth, and moonlight. Inspired by Edo prints and botanical illustrations, her
mixed media paintings feature vast florals, animated rocks, and shifting light that
transforms familiar terrain into the surreal. Minimizing the human figure, Bouillet
invites viewers into quiet, layered worlds where nature takes centre stage. Each
work is a meditation on wonder and wandering"a search for connection between
past and present, home and adventure, and something just out of reach.

Celan Bouillet is a mixed media painter and installation artist. Her upbringing in the
American South influenced her love of spinning a good tale, textiles, and sublime
landscapes. A graduate of the Maryland Institute College of Art, Bouillet was a
Vermont Studio Center Fellow, a two time recipient of the Houston Artist Association
Individual Artist Grant, and has work in permanent collections across the US and
Canada, including at the Bush Intercontinental Airport and MD Anderson Cancer
Center. Her work has been featured in numerous publications, such as ArtMaze
Mag, Create Magazine, Houstonia Magazine, and Houston Chronicle.

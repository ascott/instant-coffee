Executive Director
Deadline: Feb. 13, 2017
Part-time with expectation for Full-Time
Hours & Wage: 15 hours/week at $25/hour

The Projectile Publishing Society is inviting applications for the position of 
Executive Director. Reporting to the Board of Directors, the Executive Director will 
manage all day-to-day operations of the Society, including the activities of Fillip 
and associated public programs. The Executive Director will be responsible for 
leading the society towards its mission through the development of public, 
private, and self-generated funding, generating partnerships, supporting board 
development and strategic planning, establishing organizational systems 
including budgeting, financial reporting, ethical and operational policies, and 
working closely with the Creative Director to steer the ongoing programming. 
Strong candidates will have enthusiasm for art publishing, writing, and 
contemporary art. Previous editorial experience is a strong asset.

The ideal candidate will live and work in Vancouver, Canada, and have 
management, administrative, and curatorial experience in order to meet the 
demanding requirements of running an art non-profit. The Executive Director will 
represent the organization to a diverse public, its expanding member/subscriber 
base and existing and new funders. We are seeking a forward thinking 
candidate, with an eye toward developing the financial stability of the 
organization while addressing and resolving the limitations of its historically 
underfunded editorial, administrative, and artistic staff.

The Projectile Publishing Society is an equal opportunity employer and employs 
personnel without regard to race, ancestry, place of origin, colour, ethnic origin, 
language, citizenship, creed, religion, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital 
status, disability. While remaining alert and sensitive to the issue of fair and 
equitable treatment for all, Projectile Publishing Society has a special concern 
with the participation and advancement of members of four designated groups 
that have traditionally been disadvantaged in employment: women, visible 
minorities, aboriginal peoples, and persons with disabilities.

Interested candidates should submit their CV and letter of interest to 
jobs@fillip.ca.

Behind the Lens with John Huet

Friday March 5th, 2010 7pm-10pm

Langara College invites you to the one-night event, Behind the Lens, on
Friday, March 5, 2010. International Olympic Committee's (IOC)
photographer, John Huet, will show a collection of photos and speak about
his unique experience as the official photographer with the Vancouver 2010
Olympic Winter Games from February 12 - 28.

Huet is the International Olympic Committee's (IOC) photographer for the
Olympic Games. A professional sports photographer with a career spanning
over 25 years, his photography has been consistently awarded in numerous
publications such as Communication Arts and various photography, design,
and advertising annuals. His commercial clients include many of the world's
leading advertising agencies, design firms, and companies including BMW
Motorcycles, Adidas, Reebok, Nike, and Nikon.

Whether you are an Olympics fan, sports enthusiast, or photographer this
evening is sure to entertain and engage all attendees. We look forward to
seeing you there.

Welcome to our 2nd Art in the Park!

Spend a day at the beach with Vancouvers creative community! On Sunday, August 27,
Josh Byer will be hosting his fourth complimentary art event in 2023 " an art party and
picnic at Second Beach from noon to 4pm. Mr. Byer believes in the fundamental power of
art to foster community, friendship and goodwill and is
dedicated to providing free-of-
charge creative events throughout the Lower Mainland and beyond.

Join us at the picnic table area under the big trees, where everyone is invited to draw,
paint, chat and chill while meeting other artistically inclined Vancouverites. Bring a
sketchbook, a canvas, or help yourself to the complimentary paints and paper available
for anybody who wishes to sketch or paint. Josh will be available to demonstrate his Faux
Fauvist painting technique and is happy to provide free art instruction to anyone who
requests it.

See you there!

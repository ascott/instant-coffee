Organized by the Aird's director Carla Garnet, curated by emerging curator Erin Storus and
juried by Niki Dracos, the principal director of General Hardware Contemporary, PAINTING
2020 is John B. Aird Gallerys annual showcase of paintings by contemporary Canadian
artists.

This year's show is presented as an online exhibition and art book. It features paintings by
Jackson Abrams, John Abrams, Kelly Rose Adams, Maria-Bida Albulet, Sidia Atabales-
Schnitzler, Phill Atwood, Anne Barkley, Samuel Bernier-Cormier, Anastessia Bettas, Jamie
Bradbury, David Brown, Carlina Chen, Donna Chudnow, Dalla Coletta, Tanya
Cunnington,
Sara Cuthbert, Patrick DeCoste, Julie Donec, Elizabeth Elkin, Deena Errampalli, Marlene
Etherington, Marie Finkelstein, Marianne Fowler, Julie Gladstone, Peter Hastings, Jennifer
Hinrichs, Kee Ip, Avleen Kaur, Gertrude Kearns, June Kenner, Joan Krawczyk, Rosalie
Lam, Ruth Lane, Marie Lannoo, Miklos Legrady, Em Leighton, Arnie Lipsey, Neil
McClelland, Cate McGuire, Andrew McKay, Sarah Merry, Nikki Middlemiss, Alison
Mittertreiner, Molly Moldovan, Nancy Moore, Patricia Morris, Moira Ness, Ruthia Pak Regis,
Dimitri Papatheodorou, Sherry Park, Jangmee Park, Gary Paiva, Maureen Paxton, Jemmie
Ponting, Stephanie Porter, Jill Price, Devon Pryce, Leena Raudvee, Margaret Rodgers,
Elena Roginsky, Victor Romao, Amita Sen Gupta, Dawn Steeves, David Stonhouse, Doug
Stratford, Gwen Tooth, Gerda Wekerle, Emma White, Marian Wihak, Xiao Wu and
Dzombak Yanka.

To view PAINTING 2020 click here.

The John B. Aird Gallery is an independent not-for-profit public art gallery in founded 1985.
The
gallery relocated to Artscape 906 Queen West in 2019 and will remain at this swing-
space until renovations are completed at its original location in the Macdonald Block (at Bay
Wellesley) in 2024-25.

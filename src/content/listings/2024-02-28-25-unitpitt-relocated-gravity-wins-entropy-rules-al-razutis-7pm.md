Gravity Wins, Entropy Rules: Al Razutis

March 2 " April 30, 2024
Opening Reception Saturday March 2, 7-9PM
2954 West 4th Ave, Vancouver, BC
Curated by Felix Rapp

The opening reception
of Gravity Wins, Entropy Rules takes place this Saturday,
March 2, 2024 from 7-9PM. The exhibition features holographic sculptural plates and
assemblages made by Al Razutis in the early 1970's at Visual Alchemy"his studio
space formerly located beneath the Granville Street Bridge in Vancouver. Guest
curated by Felix Rapp, this exhibition presents a rare and critical opportunity for the
public to encounter the distinctly West Coast artistic discipline of holographic arts.

Al Razutis is a multimedia artist, educator and innovator in motion picture film and
video, holographic art and technologies, stereoscopic 3D video art, and web-digital
graphics for websites and interactive 3D virtual reality. He is a writer of critical
essays, a book, and historian-preservationist of West Coast holographic art history.
Educated in physics and chemistry, he has brought a combination of technological
interests to his multimedia arts which have spanned over five decades. His works in
avant-garde films
continue to be exhibited internationally. He continues to write on
historical-critical-biographical media subjects with new publications and interviews
added to previous bodies of work. This is far from over he is fond of saying.

A DIY, limited-edition publication featuring written works by Sidney Gordon,
Catharine MacTavish, Felix Rapp and Al Razutis will launch alongside the opening of
the exhibition.

An experimental film screening in UNIT/PITTs garden space featuring Razutis film
work, organized by XINEMA, will be announced for April.

This exhibition is part of Capture Photography Festivals featured exhibitions
program.

Our regular gallery hours are Wednesday-Friday, 12-5PM, and we can
accommodate some Saturdays by request. Please note that we have limited gallery
staff, and welcome you to email info@unitpitt.ca, or call 604-974-8689 ahead of your
visit to confirm that we will be on site.

26. OPENING |
VisualSpace Gallery | Julia Soderholm - Inherent Light | 2PM | MAR 2

Inherent Light explores themes of perception and presence in nature with a focus on
colour, texture, and light. Julia's practice is primarily concerned with recording her
perceptual experiences through painting, combining loose observation with luminous
colour.

Like the sound of two voices singing, Julia interweaves layers of imagery, with leaf
and stem motifs nodding to the tangled gardens of the artist's imagination. Colour
relationships work to advance and recede elements of the work, inviting long looking
as one sifts through the botanical forms. The artist's approach to each painting is
inquiry based, and rooted in questions of colour, exploration of space, and the
playful making of marks.

The exhibition will run from February 29 - March 16. There will be an opening
reception on Saturday, March 2 from 2 to 4pm. The artist will be in
attendance.

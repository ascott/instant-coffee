Siobhan Angus in Conversation with Hannah Rickards
Sunday April 26, 2pm " 3:30pm
North Gallery at the Gibson Art Museum
Free with registration: eventbrite.ca/e/speaker-series-3-siobhan-angus-in-conversation-with-
hannah-rickards-tickets-1984502535505?aff=oddtdtcreator
In this conversation, media scholar Siobhan Angus discusses her current research on
ecological seeing with artist Hannah Rickards, within the context of Rickards solo exhibition I
am the infant and I am the bird at the Gibson Art Museum. With shared interests in
perception, experience, media, and environmental processes, they will consider how the
ways we look at images, sites, and landscapes shape how we relate to and value the natural
world. This exhibition is part of the 2026 Capture Photography Festival Featured Exhibition
Program.

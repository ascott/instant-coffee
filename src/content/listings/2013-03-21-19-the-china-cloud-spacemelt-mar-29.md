Are you prepared to be "facemelted"? 

SpaceMelt @ The China Cloud
Friday, March 29, 2013
524 Main Street, Vancouver
Doors at 9pm
Donation suggested for admission

SpaceMelt is an experimental music + art group comprised of Terri 
Hron, Michael Markowsky and Sam Davidson, that is based in 
Montreal and Vancouver. SpaceMelt is excited to be collaborating on 
this rare Vancouver performance with four special guest performers (to 
be announced leading up to the event)!

SpaceMelt exists at the nexus of contemporary classical electro-
acoustic music, avant-garde jazz and performance art. The ensemble 
is dedicated to improvisation as a form of creative expression, as well 
as exploring collaborations between musicians and artists from as 
diverse a spectrum possible.

Additionally, SpaceMelt was established to not only foster an ongoing 
creative relationship between Terri, Sam and Michael, but also to use 
the result of that partnership to create an inviting environment for other 
artists (both well-versed and new to improvisation) to join in their 
collective music and art-making. It has been their experience that the 
stronger their voice and facility with instant structures, the less need 
there is for verbal instruction and decisions in advance.

Twilight Hour | Dustin Hodges
Tuesday, March 7th 2017 | 5:30pm
Rm 410B, SB

Dustin Hodges was born in Portland, Oregon in 1984. He received a BA in Visual and 
Environmental Studies from Harvard University in 2006, was a guest student at the 
Stdelschule in Frankfurt from 2006 to 2007, and completed an MFA in painting at Bard 
College in 2011. Hodges' recent solo exhibitions include Miguel Abreu Gallery in New 
York (2014), Off Vendome in Dsseldorf (2015), and Galera Mascota in Mexico City 
(2016).t

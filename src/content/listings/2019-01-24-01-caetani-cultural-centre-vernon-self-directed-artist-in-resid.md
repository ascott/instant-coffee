Self-Directed Artist in Residence Program
Caetani Cultural Centre, Vernon, BC, Canada

DATES: ongoing availability (minimum 2 week stay, maximum 3 months)
RATES: between $10-$30/night depending on room + season
APPLICATION DEADLINE: anytime
NOTIFICATION DEADLINE: within 5 days of application being submitted

WHO IS ELIGIBLE: Open to emerging and established writers, artists and creatives (of 
all disciplines)

INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: for a reasonable fee (between $10-$30/night 
depending on room + season) artists stay in their own room with shared 
accommodations (kitchen, bathroom, living room, entryway) at the Caetani Cultural 
Centre (a heritage house walking distance of downtown Vernon.) If desired, artists are 
offered the opportunity to teach a workshop and/or offer a reading/presentation/pop-up 
exhibition/etc.
There is also a self-contained suite available (Bassiano Suite.)

NOT INCLUDED IN THE RESIDENCY: travel expenses, food, health insurance, art 
materials, shipping costs, exhibition costs and other personal expenses.

CURRENT + UPCOMING AVAILABILITY:
Fogliano Room: from February 2nd onwards.
Caetani Room: From February 4th onwards. 
Sermoneta Room: From April 9th - May 11th. Anytime between November 1st 2019 and 
April 30th 2020.
Bassiano Suite: from now until April 10th. From April 30th-May 29th. June 24th onwards.

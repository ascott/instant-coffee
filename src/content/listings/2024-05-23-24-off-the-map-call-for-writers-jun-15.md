CALL FOR WRITERS
Off the Map: Vancouver Writers with Lived Experience of Mental Health Issues

Off the Map is a forthcoming anthology that will showcase Vancouver writers with
lived experience of mental health and/or substance use issues. The collection aims
to highlight the unique voices and diversity of writing among individuals with lived
experience of these issues, and introduce new talent to the literary community.

Our editors are looking for your best creative nonfiction, fiction, poetry, or mixed-
genre work on any topic. If writers choose to write about their lived experience of
mental health and/or substance use issues, were looking for creative takes that
resonate beyond the personal. We are partial to writing that reflects the complex
realities of these issues.

The anthology, which will be published by Bell Press in 2025, will be edited by Betsy
Warland, Seema Shah, and Kate Bird.

This collection will only feature writers who currently reside in the City of Vancouver.

We strongly encourage submissions from BIPOC and LGBTQ2S+ writers, and
writers with disabilities.

SUBMISSIONS CLOSE JUNE 15, 2024.

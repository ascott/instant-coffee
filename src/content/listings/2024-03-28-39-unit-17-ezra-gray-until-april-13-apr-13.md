Ezra Gray

Sweetie (Relationships Play Out)

until 13 April 2024

Ezra Gray (b. 1986) lives and works in Paris. He completed his MA in painting from
the Royal College of Art (London) & BFA from Concordia University (Montreal).
Select solo exhibitions include Maxium Yearn, Unit 17, Vancouver (2020); Joech
God, Emalin, London (2018); Brown Space, Almanac Inn, Turin & Infinitive, Jeffrey
Stark, New York City (both 2017). Select two person & group exhibitions include
Nothing but murmuring, 2x2x2 by im labor, Tokyo (2021); Folly, Emalin Projects,
Dunmore Pineapple, Scotland; Honolulu Triennale with Sean Nicholas Savage,
Honolulu, Zurich (both 2016) & Rundgang, Kunstakademie Dsseldorf (2014).

Beginner Rigid Heddle Weaving Workshop

Weave on a simple rigid heddle loom in this beginner workshop with teaching artist
Amanda Wood. No previous weaving experience needed. All materials and loom
rental included.

May 2 and 3
Friday 6pm"8pm and Saturday 10am"2pm
Eastside Atelier, 1310 William Street
More details and register through eventbrite
ABOUT THE CLASS

Working with a neutral palette of silks, imported cotton and linen in a warm studio
atmosphere you will weave beautiful, textural cloth in a choose your own adventure
style.

The looms will be set up and prepared for you. Amanda will help you choose
textures, design ideas and colour choices to make weaving simple and satisfying.

On Friday evening we'll begin with an introduction to the loom and materials, and a
conversation about what qualities you enjoy in cloth before getting started with
weaving. You'll be offered design suggestions and material recommendations and be
able to use Amanda's sample library to see how your cloth might turn out.

Saturday will be a dedicated and supportive weaving day. In all you will weave up to
2 yards of cloth. Finishing techniques will be discussed.

Amanda's approach to weaving is grounded in technique but invites experimentation
and marks of the maker. This practice based approach is gentle and inclusive of all
levels of experience.

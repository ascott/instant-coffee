Ongoing call for submissions*

EMERGENCE is a monthly effort to bring our local Vancouver artistic
talent out of hiding and into the public eye. The Yaletown Gallery
offers new artists a chance for exposure and offers the public a
chance to catch a first glimpse at new talent.

The first Emergence, this March, went off without a hitch as we packed
in a couple hundred people, spilling out into the common area where
Land of Deborah impressed us all with her bright and shining attitude
and sweet sounds. Everybody was impressed with the turn out since this
was the busiest night that Yaletown Gallery has seen since it's
original opening two years ago.

Two of our first Emerging artists had their contracts with us extended
as a result of positive feedback during the show. And we have offered
solo shows to two other artists, each with an expansive body of
impressive work. This is an excellent resource to receive
non-discriminate gallery experience and a chance at exposure and
discovery.

*We are now opening our lines to the next line-up of up-and-comings.

We are welcoming submissions for May and onwards, just fill in the
dates that you would like to submit for and we will consider you
accordingly.

Please follow the Emergence link to submit.
[http://www.yaletowngallery.com/emergence/](http://www.yaletowngallery.com/emergence/)

We look forward to extending our connections as we are well on our way
to creating a strong network of new artists and professionals.

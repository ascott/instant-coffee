Are you an Outsider Artist 19 years and older residing in Vancouver? Want to learn more about
being a working artist, and connect with other artists and audiences? Ready to
show/sell/perform your work?

The Vancouver Outsider Arts Festival is looking for Visual and Performing Outsider Artists, now
through March 30, 2020.

To learn more please visit: www.cacv.ca

VOAF is dedicated to supporting self-identified outsider artists who face social exclusion and
other visible/invisible barriers to sharing their work.

Organizations working with socially excluded artists please
contact us about group exhibition
opportunities.

ARTIST TALK: Hua Jin
DATE: Saturday, June 2, 1-2pm
LOCATION: Richmond Art Gallery

Vancouver artist Hua Jin will give a tour of My Big Family and will speak 
about the project and her family stories in relation to the Chinese One 
Child policy and contemporary China. 

The photographic and video works comprising the exhibition, My Big 
Family, are part of a long-term project by Vancouver based artist, Hua 
Jin, that explores a changing family structure in China as a result of its 
population control programs, particularly the One-Child Policy 
established in 1979. Jin, who was three years old at the time, belongs 
to one of the first generations affected by the mandate. The project, 
begun in the summer of 2010, documents over fifty relatives living in 
northern and eastern China as they attend to their daily tasks. My Big 
Family dually functions as a portrait of a specific family and as a 
cultural portrait of an evolving China, undergoing dramatic economic 
and social transformations. The exhibition continues until June 10.

Jin lived in Shanghai for more than 20 years before moving to 
Vancouver in 2007. She was trained as an animator and graphic 
designer and has worked as an artistic creative director for more than 
10 years. Her works have been shown in exhibitions in China, 
Netherlands and Canada since 2005. Her photo-based works are 
included in many publications and private collections, as well as the 
public collection of Museum of Contemporary Art in Shanghai (MOCA). 
Hua Jin is a recent graduate from Emily Carr University and was the 
winner of the 2012 Canon Photography Award.

The event is open to the general public and admission is free. 
Refreshments will be served.

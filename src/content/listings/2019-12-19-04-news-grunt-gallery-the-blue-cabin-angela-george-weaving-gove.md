The Blue Cabin Floating Artist Residency Welcomes Multidisciplinary Tsleil Waututh
Artist Angela George as Artist-in-Residence

Angela George to create foundational woven document of governance for Tsleil Waututh
Nation during residency at The Blue Cabin.

VANCOUVER, BC " The Blue Cabin Floating Artist Residency is thrilled to welcome
Tsleil Waututh artist Angela George as the second artist-in-residence in the yearlong
inaugural program Skeins: Weaving on the
Foreshore, examining Salish weaving
practices. During a two-month residency at The Blue Cabin in False Creek, George will
create a monumental panel-weaving, Weaving Governance, which will reflect a
foundational governance model for the Tsleil Waututh Nation that draws from ancient
knowledge and connection to the lands, waters and ancestors and will continue to guide
sustainability in modern times. Georges intricate woven document will be created in its
entirety during her residency, and will be a lasting legacy for the Tseil Waututh Nation.

With the resurgence of weaving among the Coast Salish peoples, there is a growing
desire to connect to and understand the value of the intricate weaving patterns and
methods their ancestors used. Weaving was an essential practice throughout the region
that reflected the weaver, the respective family origins, and who and what the weaving
was designed for. There have been significant losses suffered through the disruption of
oral traditions and
generational record keeping due to residential schools and
colonization. These losses include languages, history and customs related to traditional
weavings. As a weaver, Angela George has come to understand the significance of the
revitalization of these practices, along with language and cultural revival. The Tsleil
Waututh people have never given up their aboriginal rights and title, nor their ancient
jurisdiction to protect the lands, waters and traditional territory in and around slilwt
(now known as Burrard Inlet and Indian Arm). Tsleil Waututh ways of knowing and doing
are key factors in an overall revitalization of self-governing customs. The weaving
design George is drafting is a compilation of the essential traditional laws, values and
vital references for the modern Tsleil Waututh Nation to consider when moving forward
with constitutional processes and systems of governance.

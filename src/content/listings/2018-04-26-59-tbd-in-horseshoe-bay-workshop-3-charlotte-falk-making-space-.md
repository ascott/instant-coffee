ARTS ASSEMBLY
CHARLOTTE FALK | MAKING SPACE
Free of Charge
Registration Required

Location in TBD in Horseshoe Bay

Making Space is a series of participatory workshops in three distinct neighbourhoods 
in the Greater Vancouver area. 
Charlotte Falk will lead the group in activities that examine how spatial opportunities 
can be found through various site analysis techniques. Over the course of the three 
workshops, we will explore how existing site conditions interface with rudimentary 
materials and individual bodies, resulting in site-specific design interventions. 

These events seek to democratize the ways in which residents interface with built 
environments as well as explore how spaces can be inclusively co-visioned. By 
promoting a dialogue which addresses the larger economic concern surrounding 
development and livable space, participants are encouraged to challenge accepted 
norms surrounding the urban climate.

Charlotte Falk is an interdisciplinary artist, designer, and educator based in 
Vancouver. Her practice focuses on creative processes at the convergence of 
disciplines, analog and digital, and 2D/ 3D/ 4D, with an emphasis on how materiality, 
methodology, and technique impact not only process work, but also final outcomes. 

These workshops are free of charge, registration is required due to limited space. 
Participants can register for more than one session, participation in multiple sessions 
not required. Register online at www.artsassembly.ca/making-space 
Please wear comfortable clothing and sensible running shoes, workshops are 
interactive though welcome to all bodies and abilities.

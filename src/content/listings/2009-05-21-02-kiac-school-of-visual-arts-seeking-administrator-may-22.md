The KIAC School of Visual Arts (SOVA) is an accredited art school located in the historic town of
Dawson City. Through a partnership between the Dawson City Arts Society, Tr’ondëk Hwëch’in 
First Nation and Yukon College, the school offers a unique Foundation Year Arts program 
integrating studies in traditional and contemporary art practices, history and theory. 
Yukon College is seeking a highly motivated professional responsible for the administration, 
marketing and recruitment of the KIAC School Visual Arts program. Duties include: budgeting, 
marketing, coordinating, planning and administrating the program; supporting students, faculty 
and staff; and networking, building and maintaining partnerships. 

The ideal candidate will have a minimum undergraduate degree in Arts (Arts Administration/ Fine 
Arts/ Education, or closely related) combined with experience managing programs and people in 
a cross-cultural context, working with First Nations agencies/organization and in marketing and 
recruitment within educational programs. 

A combination of related education and experience may also be considered.

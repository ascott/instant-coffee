SWARM is an annual 2-night festival that marks the launch of Vancouvers artist-run 
centre programming season with exhibition openings, performances, screenings, and 
special events. On the evenings of Thursday, September 8th and Friday, September 9th, 
the public is invited to gallery-hop from venue to venue to experience the vibrancy of 
Vancouvers artist-run centres and independent non-profit galleries.

All SWARM events are free and open to the public!

Thursday September 7th: Mount Pleasant & Beyond

Participating venues include: grunt gallery, Malaspina Printmakers, and more.

Friday September 8th: Chinatown/DTES

Participating venues include: Access Gallery, Artspeak, Centre A, Cineworks, Gallery 
Gachet, Gam Gallery, Or Gallery, UNIT/PITT Projects, Yactac, and more.

For detailed information about SWARM programming, events, and venue locations visit:

About SWARM

SWARM is an annual festival organized by the Pacific Association of Artist-Run Centres 
(PAARC) to launch the programming season of Vancouvers artist-run, non-profit, and 
independent galleries. Now in its 18th edition, SWARM has celebrated local artist-run 
culture since 1999.

About PAARC

The Pacific Association of Artist-Run Centres (PAARC) represents artist-run centres in 
BC, with the primary mandate of working towards the benefit of the practicing artist 
within the context of artists self-determination. PAARC counts over 20 member centres 
(located in Vancouver, Victoria, Kelowna, Kamloops, and Nelson) and represents them 
regionally, provincially, and nationally. For more information visit: www.paarc.ca

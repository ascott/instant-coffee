JONATHAN MIDDLETON 
Curators on Curating Lecture
Monday January 20, 2014 at 12:00pm
Charles H. Scott Gallery

Jonathan Middleton is the first speaker to present in Curators on Curating, a 
series of talks where leading curators discuss their work and the work of 
others. Middleton is currently the Director/Curator of the Or Gallery and Or 
Gallery Berlin. He graduated from Emily Carr Institute of Art + Design in 1998 
and was Curator at the Western Front from 1999 to 2005.

Please join us in the gallery for the first talk in this series. This lecture is free 
and open to the public.

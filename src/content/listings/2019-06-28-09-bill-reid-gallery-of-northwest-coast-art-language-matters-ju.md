The Bill Reid Gallery of Northwest Coast Art presents Language Matters, an exhibition 
that brings awareness to the importance of Indigenous language revitalization. Guest 
curated by Jordana Luggi (Dakelh and Wetsuweten) in recognition of the United 
Nations International Year of Indigenous Languages, this group exhibition features new 
graffiti and collage works created by urban Indigenous youth and community members. 
These works were created in workshops led by Tsleil-Waututh artist and language 
activist Ocean Hyland and Heiltsuk artist KC Hall. Language Matters highlights language 
as a way for Indigenous youth to reconnect with their culture and support healing from a 
long history of cultural erasure. On view June 19 through September 16, 2019.

For more information visit: billreidgallery.ca

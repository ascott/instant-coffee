Climate Justice Happening 
Saturday February 27, 2-5 p.m.
SFU Woodward's ground floor lobby, 149 W. Hastings St.
and parade Thursday March 3, time TBD

A happening to celebrate climate activism successes so far, send 
powerful messages to leaders, and have fun. 

We invite all to help create spectacular collective letters to Prime 
Minister Trudeau and Premier Clark and a collective video on the 
themes of Celebrate! (our government's promises at Paris, Kinder 
Morgan delayed, Enbridge Northern Gateway almost dead, Great Bear 
Rainforest protected, and more), and More solutions! (keep our 
government's promises at Paris, respect First Nations constitutional 
rights, close Kinder-Morgan tank farm, stop fracking, close the tar 
sands, and more).

Bring your songs, poems, raps, performances, dances, clowning, 
stunts, and spectacles. We'll provide live video capture and synthesis 
and materials for letters. Come as a being of forest or seaanimal, 
plant, element, molecule, spiritand your beautiful selves.

At the First Ministers meeting on March 3 (time TBD) we will parade 
colorfully to the Vancouver Convention Centre to present the letters 
and video to our leaders. 

If you can't come but wish to add a letter, send it signed (and decorated 
if you want) to Laura Marks, SFU Contemporary Arts, 149 W. Hastings 
St., Vancouver, BC V6H1B4.

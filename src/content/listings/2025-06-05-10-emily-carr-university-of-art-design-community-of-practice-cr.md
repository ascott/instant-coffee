Community of Practice: Creative Work by Emily Carr University Staff

Opening reception June 5, 5pm to 9pm

Exhibition on view through June 16 during campus hours

Emily Carr University of Art + Design

520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Community of Practice marks the first all-staff exhibition at Emily Carr University of
Art + Design (ECU) in nearly three decades.

The exhibition features works from across art and design disciplines created by more
than 80 ECU administrative and support staff working in areas such as Technical
Services, the ECU Library + Archives, Human Resources, Media Resources,
Facilities, Academic Administration, Advancement, Information Technology Services,
Communications + Marketing, and Counselling, Wellness + Accessibility Services.

Mounted in ECUs 100th year and in the run-up to the official launch of its centennial
celebrations, the show offers an exciting opportunity to bring the community together
and deepen engagement.

Attendance is free + open to the public. Sponsored by Brassneck Brewery and
Granville Island Brewing.

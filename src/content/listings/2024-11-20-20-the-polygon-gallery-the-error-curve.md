B.Michaael Listening Party | NOV 28
The Error Curve is an immersive live musical performance by Vancouver producer
B.Michaael. The show weaves together intricate sound collages, blending lush ambient
soundscapes with bold synthetic textures.
Doors at 6:30pm
Show at 7pm
RSVPs are appreciated
RSVP here: https://thepolygon.ca/event/the-error-curve-b-michaael-listening-party/

Branton Olfert, known professionally as B.Michaael, is a Vancouver-based composer,
producer, and sound artist whose work explores the intersection of synthetic and
organic worlds through immersive, multi-layered soundscapes. His approach, shaped
by early ambient music, high fantasy, and media themes of conflict and place,
showcases a mastery over expressive MIDI and detailed sound collages that bridge
ambient passages and intense sonic textures. His latest album, Gore, channels a
synaptic new age style, blending verdant synthesis, environmental
textures, and
intricate sound design to create compositions that feel lush, playful, and emotionally
evocative.

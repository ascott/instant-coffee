Oct 30, 2025
Dream Diary and Ocean Breathing
Itziar Okariz
7:30 p.m. (doors at 7:00 p.m.)
Grand Luxe Hall, Western Front
Admission by donation, suggested $5-$15

Western Front is pleased to present an evening of live performance by the Basque
artist Itziar Okariz, offering an introduction to her practice ahead of a workshop
series and presentation with students and recent alumni from Emily Carr University
of Art + Design. The evening will feature two works that explore language, sound,
and the body, built from small linguistic units and signs that generate meaning
through reiteration.

In Dream Diary (2013"), Okariz draws from her ongoing practice of recording
dreams. In the month leading up to the performance, she will keep a diary of
recollections written immediately upon waking, fragments that often manifest as vivid
narratives or fleeting impressions. The performance transforms these texts through
fragmented, repetitive reading: phrases are multiplied, inverted, and reiterated, with
each reading carrying forward the last word into the next. Over time, the dream
gradually accumulates in its entirety.

The program will conclude with Ocean Breathing (2018), a collaboration with Izar
Okariz. Rooted in ujjayi breathing"a yogic practice often compared to the sound of
waves"the work amplifies breath through microphones, as two performers inhale
and exhale in and out of sync. This accumulative chorus of breaths oscillates
between abstraction and figuration, evoking the rhythmic image of the ocean.

About the Artist
Itziar Okariz is an artist based between Bilbao and New York. Her work revolves
within the framework of action and performance, questioning the ways of regulating
language and the production of signs that define us. Her work"vocal performances,
instant acts, videos, installations and text pieces"examines the ties between
architecture, territory, body, ritual, sexuality, and semiotics. Often associated with
feminist practices, punk-rock and the queer critique of normative gender constructs.

Fort Gallery, Fort Langley, BC

Executive Director - Part Time position

Salary and hours - 15 -18 hours a week @ $24.00/hour. Wednesday through Friday,
12:00 - 5:00 in the gallery. Some evening work. Hours have the potential to increase
with funding.

The Fort Gallerys vision is to create a vibrant community space for exhibiting
contemporary art that facilitates dynamic conversations and deep engagement around
pressing and locally relevant social and environmental issues. Originally an artist co-
operative the Fort Gallery became an Artist-Run Centre in 2019. We are in the process
of building the gallery to reflect this new purpose and focus. In 2019 we
received our
charitable status with the CRA. The Fort Gallery is uniquely poised as the first and
only Artist-Run Centre, and one of only a few non-commercial exhibition spaces, in the
Fraser Valley. The Fort Gallery plays a vital role in the cultural ecosystem of the Fraser
Valley by exhibiting contemporary art that is responsive to community concerns and
engages key questions of our time and place.

We are looking for an experienced Executive Director or Arts Administrator who is
passionate about the arts and understands what the arts contribute to the community .
Reporting to the Board of Directors, the Executive Director (ED) will have overall
strategic and operational responsibility for the Fort Gallery staff, exhibitions, programs,
day to day operations, expansion, and execution of its mission. The ED represents the
Organization to the artists, volunteers, community, donors, funders, supporters, and the
general public. This is a new position for the Fort Gallery. The ED will
manage and
lead the organization towards the realization of its new purpose and mission.

General
Acts as the spokesperson for the organization advocates on behalf of the gallery to
local government and community organizations
Executes board-approved policies
Directs short and long term strategic plans
Attends board meetings and provides support to the Board of Directors
Develops and implements operational plans, policies, and goals that further the
mission of the organization
Maintains official records and documents, and ensures compliance with federal,
provincial and local regulations
Sees that the board is kept fully informed regarding the organization and environment
Ideally we are looking for someone who is already embedded in the community and
has connections in order to build our social and financial capital
Management and coordination of staff (currently two part-time positions),committees,
contractors and volunteers, creating a collaborative working environment
Deepens and
refines all aspects of communications"from web presence to external
relations with the goal of creating a stronger brand
Actively engage and energize Fort Gallery volunteers, board members, event
committees, partnering organizations, and funders.
Financial
In cooperation with the Treasurer ensures accurate and complete financial and
accounting records of the organization
Ensure that financial goals are achieved and operating expenses are within budget
Work with the board in the preparation of the annual budget

Fundraising
Promote and cultivate membership
Expand and maintain fundraising activities to support and grow existing programs
Solicit sponsorships and corporate donations
Target and write grant proposals
Oversee the operations of the gift shop
With the Board of Directors manage fundraising campaigns, membership and donor
cultivation
Qualifications
At least 5+ years of experience as an Arts Administrator or Executive Director
Is proficient at writing and editing grants and
managing funding campaigns
Excellent networking skills
Proficient social media skills
Flexible and cooperative approach to problem solving
Respects other peoples viewpoints and skills, good listening and communication skills,
willingness to compromise and collaborate
Values diversity and helps to create an organizational culture that appreciates and
respects differences
Knowledgeable and passionate about the arts
Recognizes arts contribution to the community.

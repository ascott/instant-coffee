Western Front

Book Launch: The Chef's On Vacation
By Mark DeLong

Thursday Sept 25 @ 8pm
Grand Luxe Hall, Western Front

Join us for a reading and launch of a new publication by Vancouver artist
Mark DeLong, The Chef's On Vacation (Publication Studio, 2014). DeLong's
new book is produced in conjunction with the exhibition Life and People:
Mark DeLong, Barry Doupe and Lorna Mills at the Western Front, on until
October 25.

Mark DeLong (b. 1978, New Brunswick) is a self-taught artist. His work has
been displayed at Acme Gallery, Los Angeles; Bee Studios, Tokyo; Edward
Thorp Gallery, New York; Abel Neue Kunst Gallery, Berlin; Perugi Art
Contemporenea, Padova, Italy; Museum Of Contemporary Canadian Art, Toronto;
and Little Cakes, New York. DeLong has collaborated with such artists as
Paul Butler, Jason McLean and Geoffrey Farmer. His work has been seen in
Border Crossings and Canadian Art Magazine and he has published books with
Nieves, Switzerland; Seems Books, and TV Books in New York. DeLong
currently lives and works in Vancouver.

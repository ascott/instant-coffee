CAG Four-Channel Sound Performance with Scott Gailey and Yu Su

Thursday, June 7, 7 - 9pm, free
Contemporary Art Gallery, 555 Nelson St

Composers Scott Gailey and Yu Su will present a quadraphonic installation
as an immersive experience of ambient and evolving sonically sculptural
gestures and tableaux.

The audience is invited to sit in the centre of four speakers resting at
the perimeters of the CAG B.C. Binning Gallery, as gradual exchanges
emitting between them are realized through percussion, field recordings and
electronic instruments.

The collaborators electro-acoustic practice underscores the environmental
disposition of musical experience alongside the contestation of sound as an
intrusion into a space or as supplemental to often visually dominant
aesthetic instances.

Programmed alongside CAGs current exhibition 'The Blue Hour', the
performance considers the movement inherent within sound as a time based
medium and responds to the seeming "stillness" of the works within the
exhibition.

Queer Happenings

More info & sign up:
[https://vivomediaarts.com/queer-happenings](https://vivomediaarts.com/queer-happenings)

Application closes
Sept 27, 2020.

This FREE mentorship is designed to inspire queer youth and their allies, 15 to 18 years
old.

Learn about local LGBTQ2S+ art and activism from the past 50 years and make a creative
project in response to what inspires you, with the help of mentoring artist Alysha Seriani.

Mentorship runs from October 26 to November 28 -- every Monday, Tuesday, and
Wednesday, from 7pm to 9pm; and also on Saturdays, from 2pm to 4pm. That's 20 online
sessions, 2 hours each.

Explore VIVO's newly released Every Queer Thing collection, created by archivist Sophie
Roberge. More info about the collection here:
[http://www.vivomediaarts.com/archive/every-queer-thing](http://www.vivomediaarts.com/archive/every-queer-thing)

With Alysha Seriani's help, you'll work individually or with a partner to create a new work
using media from the collection including video, audio, photo, and text.

Know a friend who might be interested in this project? Tell them to apply too!

Community Arts Council of Vancouver | Stage to Page | Poetry Development Project | OCT 1
Stage to Page
A Poetry Development Project, November 4, 2020

Spoken Word Poets, have you ever considered what it means when someone
else reads your words out loud?

Stage to Page is a program where spoken word poets interested in getting their
work published are offered an opportunity to have their work read and discussed
by a panel of industry professionals. Honorariums will be paid to participating
poets. The goal is to help spoken word poets translate their words from the stage
to the page.

Selected poets will participate in a live stream event, where a panel of
publishers, authors, writers,
and performers can support their work, fine-tune
their editing, and provide insight to all viewers on the editing and publishing
process. The live stream event will take place on November 4, 2020.

To submit please visit:
[http://www.cacv.ca/stage-to-page/](http://www.cacv.ca/stage-to-page/)
Submission deadline: Thursday, October 1, 2020

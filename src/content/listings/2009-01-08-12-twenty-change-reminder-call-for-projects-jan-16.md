TWENTY + CHANGE

REMINDER: CALL FOR PROJECTS

DEADLINE: 16TH JANUARY 2009, 5PM

Twenty + Change is an ongoing exhibition series dedicated to profiling
emerging designers working in architecture, landscape and urban design. The
first edition of Twenty + Change in 2007 showcased the work of Torontoâ&#65533;&#65533;s
emerging design practices. Based on the success of and interest in the
first exhibition, the second edition of Twenty + Change is going national,
with a Canada-wide Call for Projects.

Call for Projects for Exhibition:

Twenty + Change seeks to provide a public forum for young designers who
have yet to receive widespread public and media attention for their
speculative or completed work. The coordination committee of Twenty +
Change would like to extend an invitation to emerging Canadian design
practices to submit projects to be considered for inclusion in the
exhibition. The exhibition will open in Toronto on June 3rd, 2009 and is
scheduled to travel across Canada. All projects selected for exhibition
will also be included in an exhibition catalogue for the opening of the
2009 Twenty + Change exhibition.

Projects will be selected by a curatorial committee of authors, educators,
and practicing professionals including:

Joost Bakker, Principal, Hotson Bakker Boniface Haden architects +
urbanistes in Vancouver

Ian Chodikoff, Editor, Canadian Architect magazine

Anne Cormier, Director, School of Architecture at the University of Montreal

Christine Macy, Dean, Faculty of Architecture and Planning at Dalhousie
University, Halifax

Chris Pommer, Principal, PLANT Architect Inc. in Toronto

Deadline for submissions is 5pm, Friday, January 16th, 2009. Please note
that the submission requirements have been updated - see attached pdf for
updated submission criteria.

For further information visit www.twentyandchange.org

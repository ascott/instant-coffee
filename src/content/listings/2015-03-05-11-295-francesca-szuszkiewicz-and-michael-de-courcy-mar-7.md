Gallery 295 is pleased to announce a curatorial talk with Francesca
Szuszkiewicz and artist Michael de Courcy. Szuszkiewicz was the 2015
recipient of Gallery 295's Annual Emerging Curator Exhibition. Her
exhibition, it's about time, focuses on the works of artist Michael de
Courcy. Join us as we discuss the curatorial approach to the exhibition and
unpack de Courcy's work.

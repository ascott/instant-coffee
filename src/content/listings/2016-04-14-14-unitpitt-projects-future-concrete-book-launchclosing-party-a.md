Book launch/closing party for Future Concrete: Poetics After This

Saturday, April 16, 7pm
UNIT/PITT, 236 East Pender Street, Vancouver

Please join us for the final hours of Future Concrete, an exhibition of
seven North American poets imagining a future through poetry. Exhibiting
artists include Jordan Abel, bill bissett, Danielle LaFrance, Donato
Mancini, sidony o'neal, Anahita Jamali Rad, and the Third Thing.

This evening will also be the launch of a publication cataloging work from
the show, co-edited by curator Gabriel Saloman and the publication's
designer Anahita Jamali Rad. In addition to collecting pieces from the
exhibition, the publication includes an introductory essay by Saloman, a
conversation between Donato Mancini and poet/editor Michael Nardone, an
interview with the legendary Vancouver concrete poet Judith Copithorne by
C. Diab, and many other contributions.

Without Mountains or Water. Installation by Vancouver based artist 
Rebecca Donald.

Opening Reception, Friday April 1, 2011 at 7 pm

Walk-ins Mon and Wed April 4-30, 12–4 pm

Gallery FUKAI is pleased to announce Without Mountains or Water, 
an installation by Rebecca Donald consisting of millions of opium 
poppy seeds in Gallery FUKAI located on the fringes of Chinatown. 

The title is taken from a direct translation of the Japanese word for 
dry garden karesansui (dry-mountain-water) in which sand or gravel 
is raked into wave-like patterns around rock formations that 
symbolize mountains. In this case, opium poppy seeds will stand in 
for both the sand and the rock. The seeds have the potential to be 
tracked out of the gallery space on the viewers’ feet and to 
germinate and bloom into opium poppies in the cracks of the 
pavement outside.

An essay by Zoe Li, a writer and art historian, accompanies this 
exhibition and provides an historical context for the installation. This 
exhibition is curated by Sascha Yamashita, Director of Gallery FUKAI.

Rebecca Donald lives in North Vancouver. She received her MFA 
from the University of British Columbia in 2006 and currently 
teaches painting at Emily Carr University. She has exhibited at 
Balcone, CSA Space and the Morris and Helen Belkin Gallery in 
Vancouver. She combines installation, painting and drawing in her 
active art practice.

Zoe Li holds an MA degree in Art History from the University of British 
Columbia, specializing in Chinese Literati landscape painting. Her 
other areas of expertise include Japanese art from the Edo period, 
and contemporary Chinese Political Pop art. She is a Vancouver 
writer with a particular interest in social media and has a blog 
entitled Woodwardsmile that documents life within a one mile 
radius of Gastown.

About Curated Tastes

Curated Tastes (CT) is Vancouvers first and only art mediation company. Through fun,
engaging and interactive events, classes and tours, we actively make contemporary art
approachable and relatable while familiarizing our audience with the local Vancouver art
scene.

At CT, we understand the feeling of being intimidated, unfamiliar and unconnected to
contemporary art. This disconnection motivates us to demystify contemporary art and make
it approachable to all art enthusiasts. Our educational and entertaining activities, develop
audiences for diverse cultural spaces in Vancouver while nurturing art collectors, patrons
and
supporters.

We create welcoming environments for our audience to feel confident around art,
encourage conversation, art acquisitions, interaction, play and personal connections. In
sum, curating a unique taste for contemporary art.

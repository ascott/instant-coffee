Job Opportunity: Video Out Technician at VIVO Media Arts Centre

This is a part-time staff position paid on a bi-weekly basis, responsible for assisting in 
the technical aspects of Video Out, VIVO's distribution department. This role 
completes tasks that range from file transferring, video digitizing, encoding, data 
organizing, and archive management. 

16 hours/week | Remuneration commensurate to experience

Interested candidates should send their CV and cover letter outlining suitability to 
the position to distribution@vivomediaarts.com, attn: Video Out Technician Hiring 
Committee by 5pm, January 2nd 2017. 

Please compile CV and cover letter into 1 .pdf document.

Pushing Boundaries: Our Home on Native Land Artist Call

Guest Curator: Sho Sho Esquiro

Pushing Boundaries 2023 will be held at CityScape Community ArtSpace from Friday,
September 29 to Saturday, November 4, 2023.

North Van Arts is seeking professional and emerging artists that identify as First
Nations/Metis/Inuit people of the land known as Canada, for the bi-annual Pushing
Boundaries exhibition. Artists are welcome to submit all forms of artwork; 2D, 3D,
video, textile, and multimedia.

Deadline for submissions:

Thursday, August 24 at midnight.

https://northvanarts.ca/events-exhibitions/call-for-artists-pushing-boundaries-2023/

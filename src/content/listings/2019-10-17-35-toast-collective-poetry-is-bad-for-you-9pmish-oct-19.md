A sorta-Halloween edition of a sorta-regular reading series. As always, entrance is by
donation. Doors at 8:00, poets at 9ish.
Chapbooks, featuring the first "season" of PIBFY, are available for $5-10 sliding scale.

Featuring the enchanted words of:

Laura Fukumoto
KC Wei
Emma Lee Toyoda
Dallas Hunt
Maddy Kelly
Eirinn McHattie

At the Toast Collective (648 Kingsway).

Contact peotryisbad4u@gmail.com (that's right, "peotry") for more information.
Submissions to future instalments of PIBFY may be directed to the same address. We
may be tardy in replying, but I swear: we're not going to ghost.

// accessibility info //

The Toast is a store front with the following measurements:

-Front Door 40 inches across
-Bathroom Door 27.5 inches across, very small bathroom area, door opens inwards
-Kitchen Door 36 inches across
-There is a 6.5
inch step at the front entrance, there is a portable ramp to use to
accommodate this step
-Entrance is through the back door for most events, up a narrow flight of stairs.
However, if you need to enter through the front please contact an organizer and we will
accommodate.

We respectfully acknowledge that this event takes place on the traditional, unceded, and
ancestral
territories of the Squamish, Musqueam, and Tsleil-Waututh peoples.

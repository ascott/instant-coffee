More info: [https://www.vivomediaarts.com/work-with-vivo](https://www.vivomediaarts.com/work-with-vivo)

VIVO Media Arts Centre (VIVO) is a settler-founded and operated organization
located on the stolen, sacred and ancestral territories of the xmky"m
(Musqueam), Swxw7mesh (Squamish), and sl"lwta (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.
Incorporated as Satellite Video Exchange Society (SVES), VIVO acts as an agent
for emergent experimental media arts practices. Our programs foster formal and
critical approaches to media arts and reflect the diversity of contemporary
technologies and communities that coalesce around new forms of knowledge and
creativity. VIVO builds an engaged audience through workshops, production support,
distribution, artist residencies, performances and exhibitions, as well as curatorial
and archival research. We also attend to a significant and substantial
repository of
media art history. Through these activities and our extensive archival resources,
VIVO plays a unique role in facilitating and fostering artistic practices in the region.

VIVOs vision is for everyone to have the means and agency to creatively question
and participate in their mediated worlds.

VIVOs mission is to nurture past, present, and future media arts discourses and
communities through equitable and public access to resources for preservation,
production, and dissemination.

Position Description:
The Program Manager will be a key member of the core management team.
Working closely with the Programming Committee (including technical, installation,
archival, digitization, engagement, and administrative support), the Program
Manager is primarily responsible for coordinating artist-focused public programs and
exhibitions, events, screenings, and other special projects within our resources. Will
demonstrate an artist-centered and critical understanding of artistic
histories,
possible futures, and current practices in media arts regionally and internationally, as
well as the commitment to working independently and with a broad range of people
and perspectives in a highly collaborative work environment with care for relational
accountability. This is a hybrid position at 2625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver, BC.

While we thank all candidates for their interest, only those short-listed will be

(admin@vivomediaarts.com).

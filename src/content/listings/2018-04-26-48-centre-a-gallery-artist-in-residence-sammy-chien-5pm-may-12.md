Introducting Sammy Chien,
Centre A New Media Studio Artist-in-Residence
Research Presentation: Saturday, May 12th at 5pm

From May 1st to May 16th, we will be welcoming Sammy Chien as the inaugural artist-
in-residence at Centre As new media studio at 268 Keefer St. During his time at Centre 
A, Chien will be working on research and development for a solo interdisciplinary new 
media performance piece, We(a)ves. This work will investigate migration, race and 
gender politics, transcultural identity, and spiritual philosophies, all articulated through 
Chiens own performance language, which draws from digital technologies, audiovisuals, 
text, contemporary dance, and Qi Gong practices. We(a)ves will tell a story of life in 
Vancouver at the intersections of immigration, queerness, racialization, and mental 
illness.

Visit Centre A for a presentation of Sammy's research in progress on this work on 
Saturday, May 12th at 5 pm.

Centre A Gallery
268 Keefer St, Vancouver, BC

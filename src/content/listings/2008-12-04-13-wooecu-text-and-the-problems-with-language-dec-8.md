Text and the Problems with Language

with guest speakers Micah Lexier, Melanie O'Brian and Kristina Lee Podesva

December, 8, 2008

11:30am - 1:30pm

Emily Carr University - South Building - room 401B

WOO is the Emily Carr student newspaper. As part of WOO's programming,
luncheons/panel discussions will be held on a monthly basis addressing
issues in contemporary art as a means to create a dialogue both within the
school and between the school and the larger arts community. A group of
Emily Carr students will join guests Micah Lexier, Melanie O'Brian and
Kristina Lee Podesva in a panel about the role of text in contemporary
cultural production and the problems with language. Open discussion will
follow.

There will be food provided and we ask that you bring your own plate and
cutlery to make it more environmentally friendly and less of an effort to
clean up.

Emily Carr University South Building - room 401B

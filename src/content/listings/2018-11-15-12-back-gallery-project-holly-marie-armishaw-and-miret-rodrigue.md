Saturday, November 17, 1-2 PM

Back Gallery Project
602 East Hastings Street, Vancouver, BC

A FEMINIST AND A FRANCOPHILE:

Holly Marie Armishaw in Conversation with Miret Rodriguez

Back Gallery Project is pleased to invite you to a dialogue between artist Holly Marie 
Armishaw and curator Miret Rodriguez surrounding the current exhibition. A FEMINIST AND 
A FRANCOPHILE is the result of extensive research and a deep interest in both French 
culture and feminism, both as separate entities and as they intersect. The exhibition includes 
text-based art, photography and installation, each of which inform one another through 
various threads. Armishaws unique manner of working is infused with multi-faceted layers of 
concept, theory and historical references. Together, she and Rodriguez will be discussing 
inherent themes such as the effects of the Enlightenment era on self-awareness and the 
development of photography, as well as notions of democracy, equality, atheism and the art 
of protest. The resultant works challenge their corresponding mediums using unconventional 
feminine painting materials and fresh approaches to the photographic image.

Miret Rodriguez has recently moved to Vancouver from Paris, France where she worked as 
a cultural planner, art consultant and independent curator. In 2017 Rodriguez was selected 
out of 20 finalists to curate a group show of 90 artists in Nuremberg, Germany and has also 
curated the Arthouse Tulum residency in Mexico. She has a BA in Art History from UBC and 
a Masters degree specialized in Cultural Businesses Administration and Management from 
MECIC Paris- Groupe ESC Dijon-Bourgogne.

Holly Marie Armishaw is a Vancouver-based multi-media artist. Her works have been 
exhibited and published extensively throughout Canada, the U.S., France and Brazil. Recent 
acquirers of her work include the Director/Founder of the McEvoy Foundation for the Arts in 
San Francisco and SF MoMA Trustee. In 2013 her work was chosen in an international 
competition to be shown in a solo show concurrent with Art Basel Miami. Armishaw holds a 
BFA in Photography from Emily Carr University and continues her diverse research and 
education as an integral part of her practice. Armishaw's work has been a success in 2019 
art fairs such as Texas Contemporary, Art Market San Francisco and Art on Paper in NYC.

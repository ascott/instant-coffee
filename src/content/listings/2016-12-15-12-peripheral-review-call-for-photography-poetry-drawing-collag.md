Peripheral Review is a website based out of Vancouver and Toronto dedicated to 
publishing documentation and critical reviews of Vancouver & Toronto-area art 
exhibitions, performances (art, music, dance, theatre) and events. 

We are currently looking for artists to submit photos or photo essays, poetry, 
drawings or collage works in response to past or current art exhibitions and 
performances for a printed publication. 

Submission guidelines:
All images must be digital and sent as highest quality possible (TIFF at min 300 
dpi)
Poetry or written text works should be sent as an editable file (keep in mind 
publication will be in full colour)

Also include your preferred social media handles, link to website, short bio (150 
words max), and if you have previously submitted anywhere else.

Currently, as we are grassroots and unfounded, we can offer no monetary 
compensation for accepted submissions. 

Submissions should be send to: peripheralreview@gmail.com by December 20, 
2016. 

Visit: www.peripheralreview.com

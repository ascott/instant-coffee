The Maillardville Cultural Appreciation Society would like to invite you 
to the closing reception of Katrina Niebergal's exhibition Room for all of 
It. 

Niebergal's work, while operating closely to it, slyly upsets the 
everyday-the domestic sphere-embedding it with new narratives, with 
a considered sense of weight and consequence. The works in the 
exhibition-sculpture, print, and video-are component parts of the 
larger installation, a liminal environment. 

There will be a BBQ and refreshments provided Please feel free to 
bring your own items for the BBQ as well. Come celebrate.

Friday June 17 7pm

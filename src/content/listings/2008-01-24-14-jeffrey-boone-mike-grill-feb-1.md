Please join Mike Grill at the opening reception for his exhibition of photographs, People, Places & Things, on Friday February 1st from 5-8PM.

Working with landscape, still-life and staged scenes, all within the broader tradition of Vancouver photography, Mike Grill’s pictures are known for their formalist qualities and erudite references to both historical and contemporary artworks.

His pictures are held in the Vancouver Art Gallery Permanent Collection and significant private collections.

15. Tracing the Lines: Roy Miki: A Symposium on contemporary poetics
We are writing to let you know about an exciting event planned for the
end of May in honour of Roy Miki. Please forward this along to people
who you think would like to attend and participate.

Program and venue details are still in the works, but attached is a
general description.  More detailed information should be available by
mid-February. Mark your calendars now!

Tracing the Lines: A Symposium on contemporary poetics and cultural
politics in honour of Roy Miki

May 28th to 31st 2008, Vancouver, BC

Following Roy Miki’s example as a socially engaged poet, editor,
critical theorist, and teacher, the symposium will address the
challenges of linking intellectual and political work while imagining
spaces of freedom and production. Daytime events will feature creative
and scholarly presentations on topics such as contemporary poetics,
politics of the imagination, the role of the public intellectual,
Asian-Canadian identity, editorial activism, and the history, politics
and art of Redress. Evening events will include a reading by Roy Miki
and an “oral anthology” of readings by Miki’s former students and
contemporaries.

Canadian and international scholars and writers participating include
Jeannette Armstrong, Marie Annharte Baker, Michael Barnholden, Shirley
Bear, Pauline Butling, Louis Cabri, George Bowering, Alessandra
Capperdoni, Susan Crean, Wayde Compton, Jeff Derksen, Phinder Dulai,
David Fujino, Hiromi Goto, Jamelie Hassan, Marwan Hassan, Smaro
Kamboureli, Christine Kim, Larissa Lai, Chris Lee, Tara Lee, Monika Kin
Gagnon, Jacqueline Larson, Walter K. Lew, Glen Lowry, Nicole Markotic,
Daphne Marlatt, Ashok Mathur, Cindy Mochizuki, Mark Nakada, Mark Nowak,
Baco Ohama, Mona Oikawa, Grace Eiko Thomson, Kirsten Emiko McAllister,
Scott Toguri McFarlane, Fred Wah, Rita Wong, and more.

Website and contact information will follow in February.

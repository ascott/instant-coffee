Viewing by appointment, contact gallery@jccgv.bc.ca

The Sidney and Gertrude Zack Art Gallery recently re-opened with the exhibition,
Beyond the Surface. The show runs to September 8th, 2020.

The gallery is pleased to present the work of 5 women artists, Olga Campbell, Jane
McDougall, Janice Beaudoin, Ellen Pelto, and Dorothy Doherty. The work involves
abrading multiple layers of paint and paper on panel.

Roy Arden
What the Jellyfish Wants

October 12-November 9, 2024
Opening reception:
Saturday, October 12, 1pm to 5pm

Internationally known for his previous photographic and video work of the eighties,
nineties and early 2000s. Since around 2006, Arden has produced hundreds of works
in a variety of media including, collage, drawing, painting, sculpture, assemblage,
printmaking and installation. This exhibition continues the
trajectory of his last two
decades, focussing on paper collage made from a vast array of printed materials, and
assemblages made from a wide assortment of largely found objects.

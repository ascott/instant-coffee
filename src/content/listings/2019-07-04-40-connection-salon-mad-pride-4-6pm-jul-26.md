As part of the International Mad Pride movement, we celebrate mental diversity and 
confront stigma. Mad Pride rejects the inference of illness and disorder, reclaiming the 
term Mad. 
What does it mean to live in a Mad Universe? 
Fourteen artists share their creative, stimulating reflections or impressions.
Connection Salon aims to be a low barrier exhibition space that is committed to 
exhibiting underrepresented artists.

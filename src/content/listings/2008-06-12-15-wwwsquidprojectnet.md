Century North American ART HISTORY at the Assistant Professor level, commencing August 15,

measures to achieve diversity among its faculty and staff. We therefore particularly encourage 
application from qualified aboriginal Canadians, persons with disabilities, members of visible 
minorities and women. All qualified applicants are encouraged to apply, however, Canadians and 
permanent residents will be given priority. 

A complete application will include a current curriculum vitae, email address, statement of 
research and teaching interests, and samples of publications. Applicants should arrange to have 
three confidential letters of reference sent to:

17. Upgrade International | CALL for submissions 
Upgrade International is an international network for the art and technology
community. The series is a forum for presentation and is meant to foster
dialogue and create opportunities. Upgrade Vancouver launched as the first
node of Upgrade outside New York in 2003 under the direction of Kate
Armstrong. In 2004 the Upgrade network started to expand beyond Vancouver
and New York: There are currently 27 Upgrade nodes in operation, most recent
members include Poland and Tijuana.

Upgrade Vancouver invites artists to submit short art videos (experimental,
animation, sound-based). Selected works will be included in a series of
international screenings presented by Upgrade Vancouver in 2008-2009.

18. Western Front | Curatorial Internship | 
Curatorial Internship
Media Arts Program
Western Front
Deadline: June 30, 2008
Start date: September 4, 2008

Media Arts Director / Curator Alissa Firth-Eagland seeks an 
artist/designer/writer/curator/troublemaker/troubleshooter interested in assisting with curatorial and 
promotional initiatives for Western Front's Media Arts Residency Program. By liaising with local 
and international participants, developing publicity strategies and facilitating media arts projects, 
the Media Arts Curatorial Intern will have a key role in programming. This 6-month internship is an 
unpaid position requiring 10 hours per week, beginning in early September, 2008. The 
candidate can expect to gain skills in the presentation and packaging of experimental creative 
projects. In particular, he or she will develop methodologies specific to profile-building, 
programming methods and research processes applicable to alternative curatorial practices.

The Media Arts Program at the Western Front is an international hub for media arts. The Media 
Arts Program is expansive and elastic and presents emergent, new, and diverse media art 
practices through several areas of activity and resources. Research, creation, production and 
development activities are seen as converging dialogues that inform one another. As such, the 
Media Arts Program maintains several areas of activity and resources such as the Artist-in-
Residence Program, the Media Archive, collaborative projects, co-productions, international 
exchanges and mentorships. 

The backbone of Western Front Media Arts is the Artist-in-Residence Program which promotes 
experimentation and innovation in media arts. In keeping with the Western Front's historical 
emphasis on creative exchange and the interdisciplinary, this program supports unique 
explorations of new creative areas including, but not limited to: video production, sound art, 
robotics, net.art, site-specific gestures, experimental projects, process-based pieces and unique
initiatives in media art practice today. 

Past visiting artists have included Laurie Anderson, Ant Farm, William Burroughs, Colin Campbell,
Janet Cardiff and George Bures Miller, Tom Dean, Sara Diamond, Stan Douglas, Margaret Dragu, 
Robert Filliou, Vera Frenkel, General Idea, Ken Gregory, Mona Hatoum, Tetsuo Kogawa, Tanya 
Mars, Jeff Mann, David Rokeby, Tom Sherman, Thecla Schiphorst, Lisa Steele, Kim Tomczak, 
Elizabeth Van der Zaag, Paul Wong and Cornelia Wyngaarden. Recent visiting artists include 
Jude Norris, Judy Radul, Darsha Hewitt, David Clark and Emily Vey Duke + Cooper Battersby.

For more detailed information on this and other programs at the Western Front, please visit 
www.front.bc.ca.

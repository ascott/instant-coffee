All U Can Eat Animation from Flavourcel Collective 

7pm Thursday August 9th @ Luckys Comics

Flavourcel is a Vancouver-based animation collective still in its infancy. It is comprised of 12 
talented artists, brought together by their enthusiasm for the fun, the weird, but most of all for 
the art of animation. Flavourcel aims to uplift its members, as well as the communities it belongs 
to.

All U Can Eat is the first event put on by Flavourcel! It will be a night of food, friends, and 
fantastic animation. Feed your belly and feed your eyes at this buffet of delights. Graciously 
hosted by Luckys Comics, the event starts at 7pm. No alcohol please.
$3-5 sliding scale @ door, no one turned away

Peripheral Review is a website and publication based out of Toronto dedicated to 
documentation and critical reviews of Vancouver & Toronto-area art exhibitions, 
performances (art, music, dance, theatre) and events. We are currently looking for 
artists to submit images of work made or exhibited in 2017 in either Vancouver or 
Toronto. 

Accepted images will be awarded a small honorarium and published in our 2017 
print publication, which is exhibited and distributed by stores and at art book fairs 
nationwide.

Please read the submission guidelines carefully as our requirements have changed. 
We strongly believe in supporting artists and writers and even though we are still 
working towards funding we are now offering successful submissions an honorarium 
for published pieces on our website and in print.

Submission Guidelines: 

- All images must be digital and sent as highest quality possible (jpg at min 300 dpi)

- File names should be submitted: (01_Artist Name_Title_year.jpg) in a compressed 
folder if necessary, no links to external downloading sites (Dropbox, etc.)

- Artists must submit their own work, unless it is documentation of a group exhibition 
or collaboration, in which case permission from all artists must be given

- In the body of the email or as an attachment, please include an artist bio, link to 
website, preferred social media handles, along with a brief description of the work 
submitted and image list

Deadline: Monday May 7, 2018 at midnight EST.

All submissions should be sent to peripheralreview@gmail.com

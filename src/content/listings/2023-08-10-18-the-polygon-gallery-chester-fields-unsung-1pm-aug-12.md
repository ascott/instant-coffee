Chester Fields is a professionally juried photography competition for high school students.
Initiated in 2008 and named for The Polygon Gallerys former home at 333 Chesterfield
Avenue, the program proposes a theme for artists to respond to through an original work
of photographic art. Over a hundred submittals were received from eleven schools across
the Lower Mainland and beyond. Forty works were then longlisted for the printed
catalogue, with twenty of those works selected for exhibition. This years theme,
Unsung, asked young photographers to consider important aspects of their own lives
that they havent seen reflected in exhibition spaces. It challenged them to think about
how photography could provide a platform, raising into public awareness of something
that deserves more attention.

Exhibition is on view until September 10

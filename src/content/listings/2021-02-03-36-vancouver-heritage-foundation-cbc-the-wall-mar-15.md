Vancouver
Heritage Foundation is accepting proposals from independent curators and
artists for the next WALL project to be installed this summer.

The WALL is a large format artists platform made possible through a unique partnership
between Vancouver Heritage Foundation and CBC/Radio-Canada. It is produced in
partnership with the City of Vancouver Public Art Program and additional support is
provided by JJ Bean Coffee Roasters. The WALL is a 60x 40 concrete wall with an
aluminum frame that is the site of a changing program of artworks. It is located at the
CBC building on Hamilton Street between Georgia and Robson Streets. The selected
artist or curator will have the opportunity to explore the CBC Archives collection to
research and inform their artwork intended for exhibition on The WALL.

The deadline to apply is March 15, 2021 at 5pm.

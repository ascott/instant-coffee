The Student Publication retrospective Who is Woo? features a
selection of publications that define the student life of Emily Carr
University of Art+Design over the past 90 years. The exhibition will
celebrate the artistic works and publications created by past students.
Come visit the library to interact and explore the rich history of Emily
Carr and creativity of the students.

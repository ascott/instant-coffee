Langara College

Vehicle Retrospective
Tuesday, April 21, 1 - 6pm

Closing Party
Tuesday, April 21, 6pm

The Langara Centre for Art in Public Spaces concludes its inaugural artist-in-residence with a 
retrospective look at Vehicle, a project by Kristina Lee Podesva. Sited in the project’s shipping
containers, the Vehicle Retrospective offers a museum of itself in a day-long exhibition featuring 
documentation of the project’s installations, events, and related activities. 

A programmatic and architectural framework for localizing and understanding our experience of 
globalization, Vehicle is the culmination of a series of playful, poetic, and critical investigations 
that re-signify the symbolic space of globalization to constitute a zone in which new “global” 
knowledge may form and circulate. Over the course of four months, Vehicle housed a series of 
reading rooms, inspired by Aleksandr Rodchenko’s Worker’s Room (1925) and developed by 
Podesva with Jeffrey Swartz, Jeff Khonsary and Vanessa Kwan. The reading rooms loosely 
investigate the broad categories of global phenomena such as consumption (Thought for Food), 
property (Copy Room), and labour (Book Drop) while also providing a space and structure for 
specific and local explorations and collaborations among the Langara College community, its 
neighbourhood and beyond. 

The Vehicle Retrospective offers an introduction and opportunity to the public to review the broad 
range and reach of the residency project. All are invited to attend.

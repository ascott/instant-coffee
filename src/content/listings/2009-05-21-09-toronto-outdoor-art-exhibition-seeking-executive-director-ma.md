The Toronto Outdoor Art Exhibition (TOAE), a charitable not-for-profit organization, is seeking to 
hire a new Executive Director (ED).

TOAE is a juried showcase featuring contemporary fine art and craft that takes place on Nathan 
Phillips Square for three days on the second weekend of July each year. TOAE serves as a 
fresh-air alternative to conventional art shows and galleries. Approximately 500 artists participate 
and over 100,000 visitors attend the exhibition every year. Side by side, established artists, 
undiscovered talents and innovative students sell their work directly to the public and make 
lasting connections with art dealers and collectors. 

Reporting to a volunteer Board of Directors, the ED may be employed first on a part-time basis 
from June 29 until July 24, 2009 to shadow the current ED. The successful candidate will then 
begin a year-to-year contract starting September 1, 2009, including 4 weeks of vacation.

The position requires an energetic and enthusiastic person who can work flexible and sometimes 
lengthy hours, especially throughout the jury process and the duration of the exhibition. The ED 
is responsible for the financial and administrative management of the organization and the co-
ordination of the exhibition. This includes the overall management of the jury process for the 
selection of exhibitors, fundraising for both the event and the Awards Program as well as 
strategic planning. The ED will work closely with the various TOAE Board committees and City of 
Toronto staff.

Contract Fee Range: $37k - $42k

Required Qualifications
At least 3-5 years work experience in non-profit arts administration
A degree in Art, Art History, Arts Administration or equivalent
Experience working with a Board of Directors and committees
Experience managing staff and volunteers
Event planning experience
Computer skills, including Photoshop, word processing, spreadsheets, email, data management 
on a Mac computer
Knowledge of website management
Direct knowledge of contemporary visual art, especially the Toronto arts scene
Communications: written, verbal and interpersonal skills
PR experience
Print design knowledge (for ads in newspapers, magazines, flyers, posters, etc)
Grant writing experience
Developing and managing budgets
Independent worker

For a complete job description, please visit www.torontooutdoorart.org.

Applications must be received by 5pm on May 29, 2009.

Exhibition Opening: Thursday, February 17, 7 PM
Exhibition February 18 - April 16, 2011

Performance: Songs for a Better Future by Matei Betenjaru with DB Boyko and
the VOICE OVER mind Choir, Saturday, February 19, 6-7 PM

Following Vector Associations presence at Frieze Art Fair 2010 (London ,
UK) as a Frieze Projects EU Partner, The Western Front is pleased to
present an exhibition in Vancouver as the next chapter of the institutions
year on the road, while they produce projects outside of their home city of
Iasi, Romania. As a multi-faceted platform, Vector Association is active
in producing the Periferic Biennial, publishing Vector Magazine,
commissioning public realm art works and hosting residents as part of the
Accented Residency Network. At the Western Front, Vector Association will
showcase the range of its activities through the work of Dan Acostioaei
(RO), Matei Bejenaru (RO), Florin Bobu (RO) and their current resident
Ayman Ramadan (EG). Vector Association has become a major catalyst in the
past decade for the contemporary arts scene in Eastern Europe and through
this project, Vector brings the knowledge of many novel institutional
strategies for operating a publicly oriented arts platform in an unstable
cultural economy.

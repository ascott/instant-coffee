Steve Hubert
Dragging the Deleted Block into the Deleted Shape Input: DONT REMIND ME!!!
Artist tour with Steve Hubert, Friday, October 14th, 2016, 6:30 - 7:30pm
Closing reception, 7:30 - 10:00pm
Featuring a special musical performance by Upper Level Bosses

Join us for the closing reception of our inaugural exhibition, Steve
Hubert's Dragging the Deleted Block into the Deleted Shape Input: DONT
REMIND ME!!!

As part of Duplex's unofficial mandate we are asking exhibiting artists to
organize an event (performance, screening, critique, etc.) in addition to,
and separate from, an exhibition's opening reception. We ask this in order
to encourage critical discoursebecause we register its lack, because we
value its presence.

Beginning promptly at 6:30pm on Friday, October 14th, Steve will host an
informal artist tour, discussing his conceptual and material processes in
relationship to his work. Conference and questions will be welcomed; cheese
and wine will be served.

Closing reception and performance to follow.

A limited edition of posters printed by Moniker Press will be available
free of charge.

We appreciate your support.

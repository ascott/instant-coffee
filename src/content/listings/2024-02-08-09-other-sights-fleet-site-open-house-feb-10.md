Join us this Saturday for an Open House event at the FLEET site on Granville island.

FLEET Open House
Saturday February 10, 2-5pm
Location: The site is located across the street from 1494 Old Bridge Street (Liberty
Distillery) on Granville Island. Look for Bright yellow fences with FLEET signs.

Come visit, say hello, and learn more about what is happening with FLEET in the
coming months. Coffee, Tea, and refreshments will be provided.

RSVPs are suggested but not required.
To RSVP please email fleetstudios@othersights.ca.

If you cant make it but want to be informed about future open house events and
FLEET news, sign up for the FLEET newsletter.

Accessibility:
FLEET is accessed from the sidewalk connected to a parking lot. No stairs, inclines, or
elevators are necessary to access the covered outdoor area where the Open House
will take place. The site is an active construction zone, so parts of the site closest
to
the studios may have limited access or dust present.

For questions about access or directions, please email fleetstudios@othersights.ca.

FLEET is produced by Other Sights in collaboration with Germaine Koh, Cedric
Bomford, John Steil, Javier Campos, Annabel Vaughan and Jay Pahre. It is supported
by the City of Vancouver, The British Columbia Arts Council, Canadian Heritage,
Granville Island, the City of Burnaby, and the Canada Council for the Arts.

Seaside Compositions is a painting exhibition by Kai Liu that celebrates the dynamic
interplay between land, sea, plants, and sky through semi-abstract art. This
collection showcases eight paintings that depict the intricate layers of coastal
landscapes, blurring the lines between reality and imagination. Inspired by rugged
coastlines and serene shores, each piece captures the visual
complexity of the
seaside. The bold brushstrokes, textured layers, and vibrant hues reflect the ever-
changing moods of the coastal environment, creating a visual symphony that
resonates with nature.

There will be an opening reception on Saturday, July 20 from noon to 5pm. The artist
will be in attendance.

Kai Liu is a Chinese-Canadian artist based in Vancouver, BC, renowned for his
expressive portrayals of landscapes. Utilizing painting, printmaking, and mixed
media, Liu explores the intricate interplay between observed nature and imaginative
imagery. Born and raised in China, Liu honed his artistic skills through rigorous
training in academic drawing, painting, and traditional Chinese art. His journey led
him to Canada, where he initially worked as a graphic designer in Ontario.
Eventually, he found his artistic home in Vancouver, BC, where he pursued
printmaking at Langara College. Liu furthered his education with a Bachelor of Fine
Arts (BFA) and a Master of Fine Arts (MFA) from
Emily Carr University. Liu's work
has been exhibited across Canada, including a feature in the 2023 Art Rental and
Sales Program at the Vancouver Art Gallery. His unique blend of cultural influences
and artistic disciplines continues to captivate and inspire audiences.

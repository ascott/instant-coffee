Friday, April 6
Audain Art Centre
6398 University Boulevard

Symposium: 2:00 to 5:00 in room 1002 (main floor)

UJAH launch and Networking: 5:00 to 7:00, with speech by editor-in-chief Mary 
Buckland (foyer)

The symposium is a chance for undergraduate students in the Department of Art 
History, Visual Art & Theory to present their research and writing followed by discussion 
and active engagement from the audience. Topics this year will focus on a range of 
sociohistorical and ethical issues surrounding gender, race, and the family. This event is 
an amazing opportunity to connect with active members of the UBC and Vancouver Arts 
community. Refreshments will be provided during both intermission and the networking 
event. If you are interested in seeing emerging research and wish to get to know those 
who are engaged in the arts (for possible future connections, and even just to have a 
great chat about all things art!), then we will love to see you there!

Presentations:

Karina Greenwood " The Tartan Ladies: A Comparative Analysis of Tartan and Gender 
through Queen Victoria and the Scottish Suffragettes

Brenna Goodwin-McCabe " Secure the Shadow Ere the Substance Fade: How Post-
Mortem Photography Captures and Evades

Maxim Greer " Re-Branding and Hybridization: New Approaches to Drag in the Age of 
Neoliberal Queer Affirmation 

Jeffrey Tse " The Offender Behind the Lens: The Enduring Ethical Legacy of Beitlers 
Lynching Photograph

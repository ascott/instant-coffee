2018-2019 TEACHING ARTISTS IS NOW OPEN!!! 

Pass it along to anyone you think might be a great fit!

ABOUT: We are accepting applications from artists of any discipline to work with 
secondary school (high school) students in Eeyou Istchee, the Cree Nation in Northern 
Qubec for 6 weeks at a time. This is a paid position (fee of $7,500 + travel, per diem, 
and housing provided) and artists get to work on their own projects at the same time. We 
prioritize Indigenous artists, especially those from the communities we work with. 
Experience teaching is an asset but not a requirement. The program mostly takes place 
in English.

MORE ABOUT THE PROGRAM AND APPLICATION:
http://mikwchiyam.com/artist-residency/

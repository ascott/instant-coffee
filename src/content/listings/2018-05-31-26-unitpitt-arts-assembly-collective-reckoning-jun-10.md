COLLECTIVE RECKONING

Session 2: June 10, 2018
ACCESS Gallery
222 East Georgia Street
1pm - 4pm
refreshments and snacks provided
Limited space
RSVP EARLY to info@artsassembly.ca

COLLECTIVE RECKONING is a joint project between
UNIT/PITT and ARTS ASSEMBLY in the wake of
socially driven calls to address sexual harassment,
violence and trauma that disproportionately impacts
womxn identified and non-binary folks. Through a
series of open forums, discussions and resource
sharing, we hope to not only tangibly address these
issues within our creative community, but to foster
long-term accountability and change.

In response to the voices of our first session, the
second will be led by community members and CR
facilitators so we may concretely identify the changes
we would like to see and our ability to instigate
that change . With a focus our collective voice,
Session Two will act as requisite to determine the
resources our community desires and to ensure the
changes we require are met.

COLLECTIVE RECKONING is a process initiated by us,
but which belongs to the community.

West Vancouver Art Museum

Fred Hollingsworth: Art of Architecture
October 17 - December 22, 2018

About the Exhibition

Architect Fred Hollingsworth helped pioneer West Coast
modernism by designing numerous significant homes
and buildings in Vancouver and beyond. This exhibition
explores his masterful colour renderings, furnishings
and artworks and celebrates his belief that design
should be accessible to everyone.

Hollingsworth was born in England on January 8, 1917. He
immigrated to Canada with his family in 1929 at the age of
12, and grew up in Vancouver. As a young adult, he was
passionate about model airplanes, becoming the Canadian
National Model Airplane Champion in 1935 and again in
1939. He played saxophone and sang in the Fred
Hollingsworth Orchestra, a jazz band, in the 1930s and 40s.
During World War II, he worked at the Boeing plant in
Vancouver, converting technical aircraft plans into those
more easily read by inexperienced fabricators.

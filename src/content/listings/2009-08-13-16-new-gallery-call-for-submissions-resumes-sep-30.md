Call for Submissions & Resumes: Fine art photographers, Co-Director &
Associate needed for New Gallery

An established Toronto gallery is building a new stable of fine art
photographers.

Artists should be professional working artists of emerging, mid-career, or
late-career status.

Artist Submission Guidelines:

Please send submissions in confidence via email only:
newphotogallery@gmail.com

Please indicate in subject line as : ŒPhotography submission¹ 

You may provide a link
to your website which contains images, contact information, CV, and artist
statement

Alternatively,
please email jpeg images. Please attach 4 to 6 jpeg images, under 100
kilobytes each. Please include image details,
contact information, artist statement and CV. This email should not
exceed 1 MB. Artists of interest will be
contacted by the gallery to discuss the possibility of representation. 

Gallery Co-Director Position:

Open call for resumes

This is a full time position available in new
photography-based commercial gallery in downtown Toronto. This co-director
position begins mid-fall 2009. This position offers 37.5 hours per
week, including weekends. Salary level will be determined according to
experience.

The ideal candidate will have extensive experience in the field of fine art
photography, and experience in one of the following: curating, gallery
management or art sales & service.

The Co-Director will manage staff and be
responsible for gallery and artist promotion, sales and
marketing, strategic development
of gallery artist¹s careers, exhibition scheduling, show installation,
gallery
maintenance, daily operations and client liaison on and
off site.

Requirements:

Extensive knowledge and experience in the field of fine art photography

Superior oral and written communication skills

High level of professionalism

Working weekend hours

Logical thinking and problem solving skills

Heavy lifting ability (25 lbs)

Comfortable working on ladders

Experience with the following is considered an asset:

Commercial gallery sales or service

Adobe Photoshop, and digital photography skills

Sale of luxury consumer goods

Staff management

Curating

Accounting/Business

Fine art framing/handling

Please send a cover letter and CV by September
30th, 2009. Interviews will be conducted in October 2009. Preferred
communication is via email at newphotogallery@gmail.com

Call for First Nations/Artists: Caravans International Indigenous Art Fair

Hello!

We at the Downtown Eastside Centre for the Arts (DECA) are excited to be hosting a 
new art fair in Vancouver: Caravans: International Indigenous Art Fair, which will take 
place November 30 to December 3, 2017 at the Chinese Cultural Centre, 50 E Pender 
street, Vancouver, BC V6A 3V6.

We are inviting First Nations artists and artists from international cultures to participate 
in this fair.The event is to celebrate the art of diverse cultures and genres within the 
lower mainland, to promote each artist and offer an opportunity for them to sell their art 
to the public. 

Please contact me with any questions you may have and to recieve applications. 
Deadline for Applications is Nov 27 at 5 pm.

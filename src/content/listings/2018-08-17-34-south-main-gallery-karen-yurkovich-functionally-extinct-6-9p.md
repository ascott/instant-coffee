South Main Gallery
279 East 6th Avenue | Vancouver BC

Karen Yurkovich - Functionally Extinct
SEPTEMBER 6 - SEPTEMBER 30, 2018 
Opening Reception: Thursday, September 6, from 6-9pm

These works continue an exploration of the natural world and the cultural landscape. They also 
reach into my personal artistic trajectory; reconsidering themes from different moments of my 
practice.

Functionally extinct is a term which means a species whose population is so reduced that it no 
longer plays a significant role in ecosystem function. In projects with the Beaty Biodiversity 
Museum and Archeologia Arborea, Ive investigated plant species and how their relevance has 
shifted and, in some cases, become inconsequential, especially in relation to us. And Ive tried 
to do this through art, and primarily painting; mediums which teeter on the edge of functional 
extinction.

This show constitutes a landscape or the creation of an eco-system, or at least my attempt to 
create one. - Karen Yurkovich

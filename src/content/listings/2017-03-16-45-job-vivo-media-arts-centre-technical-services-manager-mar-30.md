Technical Services Manager
4 days | 28 hours per week | 25,850 per annum

Organization Description
The Satellite Video Exchange Society/VIVO Media Arts Centre 
(www.vivomediaarts.com) is a Vancouver-based, non-profit, artist-run media arts 
centre that encourages and directly supports the creation, production, education, 
exhibition, distribution, and appreciation of media works by independent artists 
and alternative, community-based producers and activists. We provide a dynamic 
artistic program that includes exhibitions, performances, lectures, workshops, 
residencies, historical archives and access to contemporary research materials. 
We are dedicated to exploring the potential of new technologies in art and 
examining current technological evolutions in a critical context.

Position Overview
The Technical Services Manager is responsible for managing, developing and 
maintaining technical aspects of VIVO Media Arts Centre. Areas of responsibility 
include coordinating equipment rentals to members and scheduling equipment 
usage, overseeing maintenance and proper care of technical equipment, 
providing or managing technical support for VIVO programs and renter events 
and presentations, coordinating with VIVO management and staff on technical 
matters, managing the departmental budget and supervising all technical staff 
and volunteers.

Departmental Responsibilities
- Manage and maintain a professional and creative environment, providing 
technical assistance to members and VIVO staff for media production and 
presentations
- Perform and/or supervise the research, purchase and regular maintenance of 
post-production suites and creative labs, A/V and studio equipment rental, and 
general computer systems
- Deliver or manage contract staff to provide full-time technical support for all 
equipment, post-production suites and production and presentation space
- Deliver or manage contract staff to install/de-install shows and work as a live AV 
technician
- Research technical developments and determine members' present and future 
technical needs
- Train and supervise technical support staff, volunteers and interns
- Coordinate with other departments regarding all technical and facilities needs
- Provide an effective interface with VIVO's membership, technical staff, 
volunteers and community groups
- Manage departmental administration, including technical support training 
programs, and equipment/facilities access
- Manage internal and external equipment rental requests and booking 
schedules
- Accept payments for rentals and manage departmental finances according to 
VIVO financial and bookkeeping practices 
Managerial Responsibilities
- Manage the department's operations in accordance with the organization's 
mandate and mission
- Understand and implement VIVO policy and procedure
- Develop and implement departmental policies and procedures 
- Support departmental budget development by providing strategic projections to 
the General Manager
- Manage departmental spending within budget targets, present quarterly budget 
updates to the Management Collective and make budgetary adjustments as 
needed in consultation with the General Manager
- Develop materials for VIVO's operating grants, including forthcoming programs, 
reports on past activities, documentation, and statistics
- Hire, train, and manage departmental volunteers, interns and personnel when 
needed and available to assist in the delivery of your program
- Develop department resources such as partnerships, community relations, and 
project funding 
- Prepare department reports for the annual general meeting and other annual 
reviews
- Maintain the department's section of the VIVO website and social media
- Participate in the VIVO Management Collective through weekly meetings, 
collective duties and committee involvement if pertinent to department/duties.
- Work with the Management Collective on new and ongoing organization-wide 
initiatives and assist with collective tasks as they arise
- Contribute to facility maintenance, including participation in shared cleaning 
responsibilities
Qualifications
- Excellent knowledge of analogue and digital audio and video production and 
post- production formats, systems and set-ups, including format 
transfers/digitization
- Strong knowledge of analogue and digital A/V presentation equipment and 
installation
- Strong knowledge of live sound, lighting, projection, and media art installation 
and performance
- Good understanding of computer networking and maintenance
- Excellent organizational, verbal, written and interpersonal communications skills
- Personal commitment to some form of creative cultural practice
- Experience working with artist-run centres, collectives or community based 
organizations.
- Demonstrated ability to be self-motivated, with an ability to organize day to day 
operations, set and meet deadlines, work well under pressure and exercise 
sound judgment in setting priorities
- Proficient in OSX MacOS computing environment, including Final Cut Pro, MS 
Office, Adobe software, Filemaker Pro etc. (or demonstrate ability to learn 
software quickly)
- Driver's license an asset

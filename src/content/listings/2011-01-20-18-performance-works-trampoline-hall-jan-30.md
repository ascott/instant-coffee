Trampoline Hall is a barroom lecture series usually based in Toronto, but
sometimes in other places. On Jan 30, it will be in Vancouver, as part of
the Push Festival.

Trampoline Hall is this: Three people talk, on subjects outside their
professional field of expertise. The lectures are sometimes ridiculous,
sometimes moving, and always wildly unpredictable. Each talk is followed by
a Q&A with the audience which is usually also a ...lot of fun.

Trampoline Hall was invented by the writer Sheila Heti, and is hosted by
Misha Glouberman. In Toronto, it is something of an institution, playing
sold-out shows every month for the past eight years or so. Itâ€™s also played
to great crowds in around a dozen US cities, including Atlanta, Boston, New
York, Louisville, Chicago, and San Francisco, and at the Push Festival in
Vancouver in 2009.

All lecturers for the Vancouver show selected are selected by Veda Hille.
Here is the lineup she has chosen:

1) Yuriko Iga will talk about Liking Things.
2) Nick Krgovich will discuss Extreme Sports.
3) Althea Thauberger will give a lecture about Bruce Willis.

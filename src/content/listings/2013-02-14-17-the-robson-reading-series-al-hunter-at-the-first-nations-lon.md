The Robson Reading Series
Presents
Al Hunter
at the First Nations Longhouse 

Join us at the UBC First Nations Longhouse (1985 West Mall, Vancouver, BC 
Canada V6T 1Z2) on Wednesday, March 6, at 6:30 pm for a reading by Al 
Hunter from his latest book of poetry, Beautiful Razor: Love Poems and Other 
Lies.

“I find Hunter’s new work a rare melding of Blues, Kabbalah, and personal 
transcendence– a piercing, hard-won angelic love mantra. A blazing tour de 
force!”
- Juan Felipe Herrera, California Poet Laureate

“What lies here are the vagaries of a heart wounded, shattered, and redeemed 
by love. Such generosity of spirit deserves acclaim. A bravura work.” 
- Richard Wagamese, author of Indian Horse

Beautiful Razor: Love Poems & Other Lies, the much awaited third book of 
poems by Al Hunter, explores the span between the sensual and the profane, 
the distance of which can sometimes be vast or on the razor's edge of 
experience.

Al Hunter is an Anishinaabe writer who has published poetry in books and 
journals around the world, taught extensively, and performed internationally, 
including, at the International Poetry Festival of Medellin.

A member of Rainy River First Nations and former chief, Hunter has expertise 
in land claims negotiations, and is a longstanding activist on behalf of 
indigenous rights and wellness, and environmental responsibility. Hunter lives 
in Manitou Rapids, Rainy River First Nations in Ontario.

Al is also the founder and president of Good Life for Young Peoples 
(www.goodlifeforyoungpeoples.com).
This event will take place at the UBC First Nations Longhouse located at 1985 
West Mall, Vancouver, BC Canada V6T 1Z2. Please refer to the campus map 
for directions. 

Admission is free and refreshments are served. Books will be available for 
purchase and signing.

Please visit us online at www.robsonreadingseries.ubc.ca

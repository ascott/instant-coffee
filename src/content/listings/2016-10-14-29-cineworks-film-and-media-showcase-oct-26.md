Cineworks, WIFTV and DOC BC present:

Film and Media Showcase
Wed Oct 26; 7:00pm-9:00pm
Cineworks Studio, 1131 Howe Street (lane entrance)
FREE

The Film and Media Showcase is a public forum for members of Cineworks,
WIFTV and DOC BC. It provides filmmakers with an opportunity to share their
work with other artists and the public, across genres and in a variety of
lengths, in a space dedicated to discovery, discussion and inspiration.
Each screening will be followed by a question and answer session with
creators, with informal conversation and networking afterwards.

Program:

The Tale of Two Nazanins; 26 min 51 sec; HD Video; 2007
Filmmaker: Hossein Martin Fazeli (Cineworks)

This film helped save the life of a minor called Nazanin Fatehi on Iran's
death row by galvanizing public opinion. When the film was broadcast on
major national and international networks in 2007, the campaign to free
Fatehi attracted 350,000 people to sign a petition, which they later
submitted to the UN. A few days later, Iranian authorities dropped the
charges against the young woman and released her. The film is written and
directed by Hossein Martin Fazeli. It was shot, and edited in the course of
five weeks.

Hossein Martin Fazeli has been writing and directing dramatic and
non-fiction films for the past 15 years. His films have been broadcast
around the world, including on BBC, ARTE, SBS, and Canal+. In 2007 Fazeli
was short-listed by the Sundance Institute to take part in their
International Filmmakers Award. His films have won 37 international awards.

Wildhouse; 17 min 7 sec; HD Video (2016)
Filmmaker: Mia Fiona Kut (WIFTV)

Ariel, a lonely girl, is claimed to be the reincarnation of Sabina
Spielrein, the patient-turned-lover of Carl Jung. Along with her brother
Oberon, they run an underground psychiatry business, utilizing Ariel's
medium powers for profit. When the new girl in town, Luna, seeks Ariel out
for advice, they strike up a quick friendship, but with devastating effects.

Mia Fiona Kut ventured into film after completing her degree at Simon
Fraser University. Her first web series, Ivy Tower, landed a spot at the
Los Angeles Web Series Festival, and she has recently directed, written,
produced, and acted in short film Wildhouse. In 2015, she was named Top 30
Under 30 and made the cover for the Richmond Review, and she was awarded as
part of 'Best Ensemble Cast' for her supporting role in the feature film
Floating Away. She has recently appeared on CW's 'The Flash', FOX'S
'Wayward Pines', DirecTV's 'Rogue', and horror film Poignant.

Cineworks Independent Filmmakers Society (est. 1980) is an artist-run
production and exhibition centre that supports independent filmmakers and
media artists working in digital video, celluloid film media and in
relation to cinema in expansive ways. We engage our membership and the
broader community through initiatives and services that foster dialogue and
experimentation with the moving image. Cineworks acknowledges and pays
respect to the Musqueam, Squamish and Tsleil-Waututh First Nations on whose

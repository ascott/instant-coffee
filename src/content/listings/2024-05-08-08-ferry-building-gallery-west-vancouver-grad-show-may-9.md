The Ferry Building is excited to present the West Vancouver Grad Show, featuring the
artwork of graduating students from West Vancouver local schools including
Collingwood, Mulgrave, Rockridge, Sentinel, and West Vancouver Secondary
schools.
The Grad Show has been part of the gallerys annual programming since 2024 and
provides a platform for the youth of the community to share their artistic creations and
unique perspectives.

Exhibition dates are May 9 - June 2. For more information please view the attached
press release or visit the Ferry Building Gallery website.

If you have any questions, or would like more information regarding the exhibition,
please get in touch.

The Polygon Gallery warmly welcomes producer Jill Henderson and
director/producer Aquiles Ascencion of AvidEye Productions as well as artist Samuel
Roy-Bois for the premiere screening of Dtournement | Samuel Roy-Bois, a
documentary encounter with the
internationally acclaimed Canadian contemporary
artist in his home, in his studio, and in the land of the Syilx/Okanagan Valley. At the
time of filming in 2023, the area was directly impacted by the catastrophic McDougall
Creek and Okanagan Wild Fires which left their mark on the land, and on Roy-Bois
work.

About Samuel Roy-Bois
Samuel Roy-Bois artistic practice encompasses installation, sculpture, and
photography, focusing on the built environment, vernacular practices, and art as an
emergent phenomenon. In 2020 his Reward Friends, Punish Enemies was the third
in a 10-year commissioning program of new public artworks installed at The Polygon
Gallery, in collaboration with the Burrard Arts Foundation. Components of the work
remain at the entrance to The Polygon Gallery and
find multiple public use to this
day.
About AvidEye
AvidEye Productions is an award-winning independent production company based in
Calgary. They have produced over thirty productions working with local and national
arts organizations and non-profits creating documentaries on iconic artists Katie Ohe
and Anna Torma; on Calgary artist and art preparator Doug Haslam; and on
influential and award-winning Indigenous contemporary art curator, Lee-Ann Martin.
AvidEye productions has been awarded Best Documentary awards from On Art Film
Festival (Europe's largest outdoor film festival), and Edmonton Short Film Festival,
among other awards and official selections.
AvidEye is comprised of Jill Henderson a Scottish/Canadian producer and
communications professional; and Venezuelan/Canadian director and producer
Aquiles Ascencion.
Aquiles Ascencion is a Calgary-based producer and director originally from
Venezuela. Ascencion graduated from the Conservatory of Dramatic Arts, Geneva,
Switzerland in
2003, with further studies in Writing for Film and Television at the
Vancouver Film School completed in 2013. Prior to co-founding AvidEye, he
collaborated with renown directors in Europe, including Claude Stratz and Alain
Tanner. In 2021, his screenplay for Kaporito el Guardian de la Montaa (2021) was
awarded the best feature screenplay at the Caracas Iberoamerican Festival.
Jill Henderson is a Calgary-based producer, artist and communications and
marketing professional, currently engaged as Head of Communications at Esker
Foundation, a non-profit private contemporary art gallery in Calgary. Previously, she
was the Marketing and Communications Manager at the Contemporary Art Gallery in
Vancouver. Henderson has maintained her artistic practice while also bringing her
expertise to support leading international artists and non-profit art organizations in
Glasgow, New York City and Toronto.

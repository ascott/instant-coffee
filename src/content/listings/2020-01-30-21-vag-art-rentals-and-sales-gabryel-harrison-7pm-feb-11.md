Art Rental & Sales at the Vancouver Art Gallery presents an artist talk with Featured Artist
Gabryel Harrison in the Art Rental & Sales showroom on Tuesday, February 11 at 7PM.

Gabryel Harrison is an oil painter whose wider body of work encompasses media as diverse
as print, video and sculpture. Harrison is inspired by the natural world and in particular its
botanical forms. In her hands, the temporality of our
existence is revealed in the gestural
abstractions of florals, foliage and landforms. Her large scale oils on canvas speak in
washes, layers and heavily impasto passages of our own brief transit through this
mysterious universe. As a visual artist who also writes poetry, Harrisons favorite poets are
also a major source of inspiration for her work.

Every year the Public Art Program commissions artwork to be displayed on approximately
100 street poles near significant cultural destinations in Surrey. These artist-designed
banners beautify and brighten streetscapes and contribute to placemaking.

The citywide banner series for 2026 seeks designs from artists that offer unique perspectives
of place. The 2026 citywide banner guiding principles are to invite colour, vibrancy, and
cultivate connection to community

Artist Fee: $3,000

19. 1612 Gallery | CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS | FEB 21
CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

A FURTIVE CONUNDRUM: PRACTICING FURTIVE

1612 Gallery Vancouver April 7-16 2011
Curatorial Panel Mariane Bourcheix-Laporte & Ryan Mathieson

We are inviting artists to submit works investigating a change in 
social structures of space

through barely perceptible actions. We are looking for works that 
might be qualified as furtive,

that is, they operate on the level of camouflage and demonstrate the 
potential to seep into an

environment. We are conscious of the fact that exhibiting these 
works potentially counteracts

their ‘unframed’ nature. With this call for submissions, we seek 
creative ways of negotiating

this conundrum. *

How might a furtive practice re enforce its original environment from 
the 1612 Gallery space?

Is the trace, the document, the remnant the only means to 
showcase these works? Maybe the

way to have a furtive work enter the gallery is through furtive means.

Please forward submission proposals by Monday February 21st 
2011, 4pm to

furtive.conundrum@gmail.com.

All mediums will be considered.

SUBMISSION GUIDLINES

-A description of work (max. 200 words).

-Specific details relating to installation and dimensions of work.

-An artist statement (max. 150 words) relating the work to furtive 
practice.

-Max. 5 images (jpg format @ 72 dpi, 400-500k) of work,

OR max. 5 images related to ideas about the proposed work,

OR max. 2 minutes time-based media.

DEADLINE FOR SUBMISSIONS: MONDAY FEBRUARY 21st 2011, 
4PM.

Send any questions to furtive.conundrum@gmail.com.


20. Drop Out Video Arts | CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS | FEB 28
CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

Drop Out Video Arts Presents “Drop In/ Drop Out” Video Arts 
Exposition at

W2 Storyeum. 

http://www.facebook.com/event.php?eid=124247927644812

Deadline: February 28, 2011

Drop Out Video Arts (DOVA) is a newly-founded non-profit 
organization motivated toward the promotion of experimental video-
art in Vancouver, Canada. Previously a tight-nit group of 
independent artists residing in the Downtown Eastside, DOVA 
formed a non-profit in late 2010 with a goal of bringing this highly 
collaborative art-form into larger, more publicly accessible venues. 

We are openly seeking submissions from Vancouver artists for a 
large-scale group projection expo at W2 Storyeum. This event is set 
to take place on Friday, March 18, 2011. 

Drop In/ Drop Out is affiliated with Dutch artist Rafaël Rozendaal’s 
(http://www.newrafael.com) BYOB (Bring Your Own Beamer) project 
(http://www.byobworldwide.com/). Simple in concept, but limitless in 
possibilities,BYOB invites artists to showcase their projected video 
works in a given exhibition space by bringing their own equipment. 
The first BYOB took place on July 20, 2010 in Bureau Friedrich 
Projectstudio, Berlin and has since enjoyed successful homes in 
Kunsthalle Athena, Athens (http://www.kunsthalleathena.org/), 
Spencer Brownstone Gallery, New York City 
(http://www.spencerbrownstonegallery.com/), The Gayle and Ed 
Roski MFA Gallery at the University of Southern California, and the 
New American Art Union in Portland, Oregon 
(http://www.newamericanartunion.com/). DOVA is to bring the first 
Canadian leg of this international movement to W2, as exhibitions in 
Montreal and Toronto are planned for later in the year. 

“Drop In/Drop Out” will incorporate no less than 30 artist projections 
throughout the W2 exhibition space. This exhibition will share with 
wider audiences the open communication of methodology, sharing 
of equipment and passion for the possibilities of moving light that 
are integral parts of experimental video production in Vancouver. All 
artists and community members are invited to show work, provided 
that they bring their own projection equipment (with a few 
exceptions, as the collaborative aspect of this show will allow for 
low-income individuals who may not afford equipment to be 
included).

For more information about BYOB visit 
http://www.byobworldwide.com/

If you have any other questions, please contact 
dova.society@gmail.com

Find attached a downloadable application form with further 
information for artists. This form and further information about 
DOVA is also available on our website dropoutvideo.com.

Please look forward to further information about this exhibition once 
the lineup of artists and musicians is confirmed. Thank you for your 
interest!

Mairin Cooley, President
dova.society@gmail.com
dropoutvideo.com

Drop Out Video Arts
201- 79 Pender Street East
Vancouver, B.C.



21. VIVO MEDIA ARTS CENTRE | SIGNAL & NOISE | CALL | MAR 1
VIVO MEDIA ARTS CENTRE (Vancouver BC Canada)
SIGNAL & NOISE 11 OLD FORMS FESTIVAL
CALL FOR SUBMISSIONS

The Signal & Noise Media Art Festival, presented by VIVO Media Arts 
Centre in Vancouver BC Canada, is now accepting submissions for 
its 11th annual festival of single and multi-channel audio & video 
work, audio visual performance and installation.

With reflection upon the proliferation of New Media, New Forms, and 
New Technologies festivals and forums, Signal & Noise 2011 will 
focus on presenting work that is in dialogue with Old Forms, 
Antiquated Ideas, Archives, Outmoded Platforms, Old Habits, Old 
Fashioned Mannerisms and Obsolete Technologies. Signal & 
Noise 2011 encourages applicants to consider the relationship 
between private and professional production, collaboration between 
archivers of personal and public histories and fictions, cultural 
production outside of contemporary art, the revitalization of 
discarded and repurposed materials and equipment, and the 
resuscitation of analogue communication.

Special attention will be paid to work that converses with the 
aural/oral and visual history and identity dating, pre-dating and post-
dating Vancouver’s 125 years. We encourage artists, curators and 
centres to submit works or ideas for programs that highlight 
Vancouver’s media art history.

Signal & Noise Media Arts Festival: June 23-26 2011
Submission Deadline: Must arrive by Tuesday March 1 2011 at 
Midnight
Inquiries: events@vivomediaarts.com
www.signalandnoise.ca / www.signalandnoise.ca/about/2011-
submissions

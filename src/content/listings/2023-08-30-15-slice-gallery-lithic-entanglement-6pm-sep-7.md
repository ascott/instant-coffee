An exhibition of weavings by Ana Diab and dance performance by Ana Diab, Sierra
Megas and Alex Schwarz featuring music by Missy Donaldson

Sept 7
6-10pm
performance starts at 7pm

Slice Gallery 1636 Venables
Street

Admission: by donation

Lithic Entanglement is a series of weavings that depict geological features: small
minerals, large scale mining operations, and cross section diagrams of volcanic
activity. Woven with natural plant and animal fibres and recycled yarn, the weavings
are tactile explorations of the intersection between geological and organic matter.
The Dance Performance, Geochoreography is a journey through the movement of
rocks to bring the geological narrative to life. Drawing inspiration from the fluidity and
resilience of the Earths processes, we move like lava, tectonic plates, rock slides,
intertidal pebbles, mountains, and boulders, and we intervene with the natural state
and position of stones.

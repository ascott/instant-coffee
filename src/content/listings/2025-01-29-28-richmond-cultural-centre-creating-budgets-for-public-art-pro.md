About the Presenter

Sara Graham is an artist based in Port Moody, BC. Graham takes a cross-
disciplinary approach to her practice, often employing the methods and materials of
architecture and city planning. Her work proposes alternative ways of looking at the
city and its parts using the
real to construct the imagined. The results range from
paper, sculpture, photography to large scale public artworks. Graham holds a BFA
from the Nova Scotia College of Art and Design and an MFA from the University of
Guelph.

Solder and Sons Revival

As in days past, when the bookshop hosted music born from temples,
a revival comes your way on Friday March 25. Come down and train
your eyes and ears on Akhil Jopbanputra, Davinder Singh, and Sunny Matharu 
as they belt out some spiritual evening ragas at the Interurban Gallery 
1 E. Hastings(@ Carrall) 
Floor seating courtesy of Tent Shop.
Doors open at 7.30, and the concert will begin at 8. 

Juice bar and tea at intermission. Tickets at door $10

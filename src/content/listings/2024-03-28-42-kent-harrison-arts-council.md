2024-25 Artist-in-Residence | APR 15
Call for our 2024-25 Artist-in-Residence

Our Artist-in-Residence program supports an artist with a live-works space overlooking
Harrison Lake, for a year. If an inspirational natural setting, public art gallery work
experience and a self-directed, independent studio practice is of interest to you, please
email your application to: khacresidency@gmail.com

Deadline for applications is April 15th, 2024 at 11:59 pm PST.
The announcement will be made May 1, 2024 and the new AIR will move in July 1,
2024.

For more information and application requirements, see our site
posting:

BC for Bernie offers lively options for US expat voters and "Primary
Watchers"

For immediate release

Vancouver, BC (February 29, 2016) - BC for Bernie encourages all US
citizens in BC to participate in the Democrats Abroad Global
Presidential Primary. To help Vancouverites they are offering "The
Bernie Bus" to transport eligible voters from the Fox Cabaret's "Super
Tuesday Watch Party" to the official Democrats Abroad polling place at
East Van's Cedar Cottage Neighbourhood Pub, March 1, 2016 from
6:00pm to 8:00pm.

BC for Bernie will have a Voter Information table at the Fox Cabaret's
"Super Tuesday Watch Party" where partygoers will watch live
streaming US Primary election results on the Fox's giant projector
screen. Democrats Abroad will host a Primary Watch Party event at the
Cedar Cottage Neighborhood Pub, which is an Official Voting Center
between 6:00pm and 8:00 pm.

The Democrats Abroad Super Tuesday Official Voting Centers in the
Vancouver region are:
The Wolf and Hound, 3617 W Broadway, Vancouver: 1:00pm - 5:00pm
The Cedar Cottage Pub, 3728 Clark Drive, Vancouver: 6:00pm -
8:00pm
Sammy J Pepper's Restaurant, 15770 Croydon Drive, Surrey: 6:00pm
- 8:00pm
Vancouver-based BC For Bernie hopes a huge worldwide turnout will
help Senator Bernie Sanders become the Presidential nominee. "This
race is much closer than the mainstream media would have people
believe," says BC For Bernie co-founder Julie Boton. "Every vote
counts and nothing can be taken for granted."

The Democrats Abroad Global Presidential Primary runs from March 1-
8, 2016. US citizens can register to vote by email, fax, post, or in-
person at Voting Centers in countries around the globe on March 1,
2016.

Voting with Democrats Abroad means each vote "carries more weight"
because it's one vote cast in a smaller pool as opposed to an entire
state. In the Democratic Party, US citizens living abroad are treated as
a "state" with Presidential candidates awarded delegates in proportion
to the number of votes cast in the Democrats Abroad Global
Presidential primary. 17 delegates are "in play" and will be awarded to
either Senator Bernie Sanders or to Hillary Clinton.

Voters can also register online to vote in their home states at
votefromabroad.org. (Deadlines vary.) Voters must be registered in
their home states to vote in the November General Presidential
Election.

Often compared to Franklin Delano Roosevelt, Senator Sanders is the
longest-serving independent in US Congressional history with an
impressive voting record and a progressive agenda that includes
universal healthcare for all Americans, reducing poverty, raising wages,
environmental protection and more.

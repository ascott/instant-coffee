Access Gallery

OPENING RECEPTION: Friday 1 December from 7 to 9pm

19,000 Scanned Anal Queefs and Counting
Christopher Lacroix

on view 1 December 2023 to 13 January 2024

With the rise of anti-LGBTQIA2+ rhetoric,
demonstrations, and legislation, the
representation of queer bodies requires strategy. Though our visibility must be
insisted on and our stories heard, exposure is always accompanied by targets.
Christopher Lacroixs solo exhibition 19,000 Scanned Anal Queefs and Counting
employs methods of representing queerness and queer sexuality that evoke the
queer body without relying on its renderings. Sculpture and photography illicit and
document a repeated non-gendered, queer performance. Continuing the artists
practice of hybrid fetish furniture and gym equipment, the exhibition consists of a
machine designed to induce and scan anal queefs. Accompanying the machine are
19,000 laser prints of the scans covering the gallery walls. Gallery visitors are
encouraged to take an anal queef home.

The exhibition is in partial fulfilment of the PhD in Contemporary Arts at SFU's
School for the Contemporary Arts.

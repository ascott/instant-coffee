SIGNAL + NOISE MEDIA ART FESTIVAL 09: CALL FOR VOLUNTEERS
Deadline: April 20 2009

Signal + Noise volunteers are artists. Artists who understand what it
takes to build a community and a structure for their own productivity.
Many hours of sweat equity went into preparing the VIVO facilities for
Signal + Noise 2008; from power washing, painting and building walls to
trouble shooting installations, answering the phones and bartending. As
Signal + Noise 2009 nears, we are once again asking for your help. Signal
+ Noise runs from April 23-26 and will need volunteers beginning on April
13th. The board and staff of VIVO are deeply grateful for this 
generosity and good humor. Without you, the festival doesn't exist. Thank
you!

As a volunteer for Signal + Noise you will earn $14 video bucks an hour
which you can use toward equipment and facilities rentals at VIVO (with a
membership). In additi¬on, for each for 4hour shift work you will receive
1 ticket to a program of your choice. Volunteers who contribute over 12
hours will receive a festival pass.

Please let us know when and how you would like to help out by completing
a short questionnaire at http://spreadsheets.google.com/viewform?
hl=en&formkey=cHNyenNUMlp6a2RKMUZrRDJtdnoxTGc6MA.

VIVO Media Arts Centre presents the ninth annual Signal + Noise Media Art
Festival April 23-26 2009. Showcasing a spectrum of audio & video works,
online games, live action performances and immersive installations, Signal
+ Noise is Vancouver’s premier alternative media event.

Signal + Noise is the highlight in VIVO’s programming. The festival
presents the work of local, national and international artists. Created
nine years ago in response to Vancouver’s explosive contemporary arts
underground, Signal + Noise has become an essential forum for creative
exchange.
www.signalandnoise.ca

VIVO Media Arts Centre

VIVO is a not for profit media production, exhibition and distribution
centre. We are an evolving organization operated by and for artists and
media/community activists. Since 1973, with the founding of the Satellite
Video Exchange Society (SVES), VIVO has grown into a vibrant artist-run
centre with state of the art facilities and educational workshops. We have
production equipment, studio space, audio, video and new media
post-production facilities, and during regular hours of operation,
light-technical support is available to producers as they work. Other
exciting facets of the centre include ongoing public exhibitions of video
and new media programs and Video Out's International Distribution network.
www.vivomediaarts.com

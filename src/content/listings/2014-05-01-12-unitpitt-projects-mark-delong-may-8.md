Mark DeLong 
Unit/Pitt Projects 
May 8 8-11pm
236 E Pender Street
Vancouver BC

Please join us for a one-night-only show by Vancouver-based artist Mark 
Delong:

Unit/Pitt Projects is delighted to host a book launch and one-night show with 
Vancouver-based artist Mark Delong. The artist's book, entitled The Bloke With 
One Thousand Fingers, is a self-published illustrated poetic chapbook which 
laments the idle concerns of the life left with an overabundance of choices. His 
slyly glib tones take on matters of judgement and accusation, and the simple 
referential drawings skirt around playful aphorisms and advertisement. 
The show will also feature several paintings, and a video projection of "Powerful 
Bitch". The book itself will be on sale for $15. Refreshments served, 8-11pm.

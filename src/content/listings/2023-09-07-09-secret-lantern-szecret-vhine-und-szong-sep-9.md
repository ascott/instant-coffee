SZECRET VHINE UND SZONG: An Eclectic Evening of Music & Beauty with Veda Hille -
A Fundraiser for the Secret
Lantern Society
SAT SEPT 9 || 7:00-10:00pm
$50 entry (includes snacks + bev ticket)

It's the 30th anniversary of the Secret Lantern Society and its annual Winter Solstice
Lantern Festival! Come hang out with Vancouver treasure Veda Hille for an eclectic

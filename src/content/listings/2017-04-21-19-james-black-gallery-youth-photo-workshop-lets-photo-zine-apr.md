Sun, Apr 23, 10 am - 2 pm
Youth Photo Workshop:
Lets Photo Zine

James Black Gallery, 144 E 6th Ave, Vancouver
Free but registration req. by April 14 (Space limited)
Email: RSVP@CAPTUREPHOTOFEST.COM

This workshop for high school"aged youth introduces participants to the history and
concept of the photo book as an art form and mode of documentation. Led by artist
Julia Dahee Hong, participants will collaborate to design, layout, and produce a
collectively authored photo book. The workshop, run as part of the inaugural
Vancouver Photo Book Fair, will engender passion for photography and book
making and equip young, emerging artists with skills that provide increased agency
in their creative practices. (Venue not wheelchair accessible)

***************************
Consumption is a daily ritual, a rite that is the byproduct of a culture that worships 
capital. A result of such is the loss of ones connection to self and the strengthening of 
ones connection to commodities. It is not the fetishization of objects that gives us a 
cheap understanding of truth, it is our naive belief that we can know without feeling, that 
we can receive without belief. This show of incantatory sculptural studies was made 
through an object-oriented ontological approach to spiritual meaning-making. Infertile 
Ground is a celebration of the divinity in all things, in the meaning given to us by the 
mundane.

***************************
From the Treaty 7 land of Calgary, Alberta, Carlan Savage-Hughes has come to BC to 
expand her studies in visual arts and community engagement. Through interdisciplinary 
methods of making, she is a painter, writer and sculptor. Her artistic practice is 
accompanied by an on-going effort to strengthen independent arts communities as an 
act of resisting the capital-driven values of the art world. Curation has had a key role in 
this process of creating opportunities. In her artistic practice, Carlan explores the 
emotional realm as a place of legitimate embodied knowledge.

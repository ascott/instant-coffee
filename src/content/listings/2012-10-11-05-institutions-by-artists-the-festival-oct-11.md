The Pacific Association of Artist run Centres is pleased to launch 
Institutions by Artists: The Festival, a series of exhibitions, 
presentations, and events between October 8 and 19, 2012.

Institutions by Artists: the Festival presents special free public 
programming at Vancouver artist-run centres and partnering venues. 
From wandering archives, to grassroots cinema collectives, to media 
pirates and institutional revisionists, audiences will be invited to 
experience such tactics as radical distribution of printed matter, the re-
purposing of data and systems, as well as DIY models of education. 
Including the collective projects Red76 (Unit Pitt); Arbour Lake Sghool 
(Western Front), Claire Fontaine (Audain), Cine Falcatrua (W2), The 
Piracy Project (221A), F.A.G.(Access), Art Metropole (Project Space) 
and the Museum of Non Participation(VIVO) as well as Chumpon and 
Chantawipa Apisuk(Gachet); Gabi Ngcobo(Artspeak), Jakob Jakobsen

Convention Sold Out!
On October 12 through 14, Institutions by Artists: The Convention 
brings together artists, curators, critics, and academics to evaluate and 
activate a dialogue about the performance and promise of 
contemporary artist-run centres and initiatives internationally. The 
Convention will use experimental discussion formats, performative 
frameworks, and participatory vehicles to challenge and generate new 
thinking about artist-run culture globally, examining the urban and 
rural, fixed and mobile, the local and the virtual with the aim of 
catalyzing new speculative frameworks to consider for their futures.

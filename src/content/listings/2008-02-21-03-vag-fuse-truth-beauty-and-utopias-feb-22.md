Theatre Conspiracy

Theatre Conspiracy’s Live from the Bush of Ghosts tells a ghost story for the digital world. The multi-floor installation is inspired by Nigerian novelist Amos Tutuola's 1954 book My Life in the Bush of Ghosts and Brian Eno and David Byrne's 1981 record of the same name. It is co-created by Tim Carlson, Richard Wolfe, electronic hip hop band no luck club, choreographer/performer Tara Cheyenne Friedenberg and videographer Candelario Andrade.

ion Zoo

ion Zoo sets up shop at FUSE in the Gallery’s historic courtrooms---places associated with binary thinking and ideas of good versus evil---to explore through sound and image the boundary where concepts of utopia and distopia bleed into one another. The improvising quartet draws inspiration from a wide range of musical ingredients, including bebop, noise, baroque aria and Weimar cabaret, to create an aural feast of haunting melodies, angular chamber music and lush atmospheric textures. ion Zoo members include saxophonist Steve Bagnell, vocalist Carol Sawyer, bassist Clyde Reed and pianist Lisa Miller.

Safa

Three of Vancouver’s most well known musicians bring Persian contemporary improvisations and neo-traditional settings to life through a concert that unfolds in rhythmic play and timbral contrasts. Largely based in Persian idioms, but with an exuberant new-world edge to complement the old world's passion and melancholy, Safa’s music is improvised dialogue for clarinet and kulintang (Philippine gong row) to arrangements of four Sufi poems sung in Farsi.

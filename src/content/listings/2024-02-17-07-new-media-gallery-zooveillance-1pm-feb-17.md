The works in ZOOVEILLANCE offer insights into the paradox of technological
advancement and a digital world progressively restructured and governed by social
media and artificial intelligence.

Extensive research into artificial intelligence or social media inform these three
extraordinary works. The works reference the evolution of two formidable, hyper-
transformative technologies, rooted in the 20th century and now blossoming in the
21st... Artificial Intelligence and Social Media.

Through the use of video & sound, code and robotics, we are presented with the
suggestion of three animal species engaged in uncanny, ritualistic and performative
actions and movements; each rooted in an uneasy, even dysfunctional association
with AI or Social Media. The works allude to the tremendous potential of these
technologiesat the same time presenting us with unsettling observations and
information. None of the animals may be separated from its host; each is bound to
its technologies in some way. There is a beauty, horror and pathos to these
apparitions; the reactive apes, the conditioned pigeons and the critically endangered
northern white rhino. Confined, subjected and diminished by technology, each
animal body is disciplined and defined by a fraught relationship to AI or Social Media.

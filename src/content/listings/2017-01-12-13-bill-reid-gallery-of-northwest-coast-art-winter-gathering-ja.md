January 19, 2017 | 6:30 PM - 9:00 PM

The Bill Reid Gallery welcomes you to their Winter Gathering to enjoy an evening of live 
performances by local artists, delicious food and beverage, and seasonal warmth.

Schedule:

6:30 PM Doors Open
7:15 PM Coast Salish Welcome
7:30 PM Kenthen Thomas (Interactive Storytelling)
7:45 PM Molly Billows (Poetry)
8:00 PM Sol Diana (Poetry)
8:15 PM Coolhand Uke (Music)
8:30 PM Robert and Jamie Thompson (Music)

Entry is by donation. No one turned away for lack of funds. All proceeds will go to 
support this year's Downtown Eastside Women's Memorial March. Click here to learn 
more about this cause: womensmemorialmarch.wordpress.com/

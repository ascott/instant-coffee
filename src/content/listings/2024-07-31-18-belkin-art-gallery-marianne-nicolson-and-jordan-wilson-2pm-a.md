Conversation with Marianne Nicolson and Jordan Wilson
Saturday, August 10 at 2 pm

Join us for a conversation between artist and activist Marianne Nicolson
(Kwakwakawakw, Musgamaw Dzawadaenuxw) and curator and writer Jordan Wilson
(Musqueam) on the current exhibition This Is An Emergency Broadcast and how it
emerges from investigations into the history of political activism. The exhibition
explores significant moments of Indigenous activism over the past 100 years by
examining the role of gathering and resistance. Activated by a timeline of archival
images and recorded audio, the exhibition addresses moments relevant to a history of
collective colonial
resistance, and the conversation will explore the relevance of this
knowledge to continued efforts of resistance today.

Everyone is welcome and admission is free.

Please note, the Belkin will be closed Sunday, August 11 for a private gathering.

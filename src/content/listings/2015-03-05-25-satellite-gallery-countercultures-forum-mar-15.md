Sunday, March 15, 2015, 12 to 5 PM

Satellite Gallery
560 Seymour Street, 2nd floor
Vancouver, BC

Please join us at Countercultures Forum, an afternoon of discussion and
debate hosted by Presentation House Gallery.

This afternoon brings new perspectives and possible reorientations to the
counterculture momentum of the 1960s and ‘70s through presentations by
artists, writers, scholars, and curators. Conceived by Presentation House
Gallery curator Helga Pakasaar and programmed by independent curator Jesse
McKee, this forum is inspired by recent Presentation House Gallery
exhibitions about the period. The afternoon will provide an opportunity to
reflect on this seminal period from a wide perspective.

Counterculture Forum includes contributions from art historians Jonathan D.
Katz (State University New York at Buffalo), Ignacio Adriasola (UBC), Robin
Simpson (UBC), anthropologist Terje Toomistu and sound artist KIWA
(Tallinn, curators of Soviet Hippies: The Psychedelic Underground of 1970s
Estonia), Michael Turner and Allison Collins (Vancouver, curators of
Mainstreeters: Taking Advantage 1972-1982), Andrew Berardini (Los Angeles,
critic and writer), curator Martha Kirszenbaum (Los Angeles, Director of
Fahrenheit) and Vancouver artists Raymond Boisjoly and Isabelle Pauwels.
Archival video recordings from the period will feature cultural luminaries
like Buckminster Fuller and Adrian Piper.

The program maintains the period’s ethos through studied moments,
screenings, music and performance, and offers productive encounters with
subjects across the arts, social justice and alternative lifestyles.
Discussions will touch on the diverse activities of artistic and cultural
movements from South America to Eastern Europe, and will locate Vancouver’s
own cultural histories within a global heritage.

The forum aims to critically consider how to read the cultural production
of a loose network of international actors from contemporary perspectives:
Where does a current generation of cultural thinkers working with this
historic material place their emphases? What is at stake with a retreat
from the “mainstream”? How do contemporary conditions of withdrawl from
dominant culture take advantage of opportunities to learn about past
conditions?

Produced in conjunction with the exhibition Mainstreeters: Taking Advantage
1972 -1982.

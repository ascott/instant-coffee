TALK I Twilight Hour
LuChan

Wednesday, October 24 | 6 pm
Sculpture Studio, D1357
Join us for a talk with Emily Carr University faculty member LuChan.
520 E 1st Ave, Vancouver, BC

Guyana-born artist LuChan holds a BFA from ACAD and a MFA from NSCAD. 
LuChan works with selected participants as the basis for drawings, animations, and 
uses written and recorded text towards layered installations. Her work explores the 
awareness of disadvantaged positions among seemingly disparate human 
experiences. She has participated in group exhibitions at the National Gallery of 
Canada, Carleton Art Gallery, MOCCA, Richmond Art Gallery, TRUCK Gallery, & 
Oboro among others. Her work has also been featured in solo exhibitions at the Art 
Gallery of Nova Scotia, the MAI (Montral Arts Interculturels), and Mount Saint 
Vincent University Art Gallery. She was long-listed for the Sobey Art Award in 2005 
and 2010. She is often found making work in natural environments through 
residencies in Portugal, France, Spain, and Italy. 

Presented by the Audain Faculty of Art, Twilight Hour is a series of talks hosted by 
the Painting and Sculpture + Expanded Practices programs at ECU.

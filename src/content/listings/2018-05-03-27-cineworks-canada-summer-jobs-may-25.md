PROGRAMS ASSISTANT
Temporary Position
Cineworks; Canada Summer Jobs
Proposed Start Date: June / Flexible
Hours: Typically M-F 12-6, with possible evening and weekend events
Terms: Temporary (9 week) position: $14/hour " 30 hours per week

DESCRIPTION
The Programs Assistant will work closely with our Director of Programs,
researching, developing,
promoting, and delivering a series of media arts screenings, workshops,
outreach events, and online
support materials. This will include assistance with developing and
organizing online materials related to
the organization, as well as participation in aspects of planning and
facilitation for Mirrored in Stone: an
experimental documentary commission supported by a Canada Council for the
Arts New Chapter grant,
with artists Marianne Nicolson and Althea Thauberger, in collaboration with
the Dzawadaenuxw (Tsawataineuk) First Nation.

RESPONSIBILITIES
The successful candidate will assist the Program Manager with:
program related research and development
publicity and promotion of programs and events
program delivery
coordinating the recruitment and training of program volunteers
preparing funding proposals and reports
archiving program information and updating Cineworks website
collecting and collating information on Cineworks productions
other administrative duties as required

QUALIFICATIONS
excellent oral and written communication skills in English
competent knowledge of computer applications: MS Word, Excel, Photoshop,
web and social media (preferably on a Mac platform)
excellent organizational and multi-tasking skills
ability to work independently and as part of a team
ability to design and produce promotional materials an asset

TECHNICAL COORDINATOR
Temporary Position
Cineworks; Canada Summer Jobs
Start Date: June / Flexible
Hours: Typically M-F 12-6, with possible evening and weekend events
Terms: Temporary (9 week) position: $14/hour " 30 hours per week

DESCRIPTION:
The Technical Coordinator will work closely with the Facilities and
Equipment Manager, providing
technical and organizational support for Cineworks members, programs and
events. This position will
have a special focus on supporting Cineworks analogue activities,
including managing and maintaining
analogue facilities and coordinating workshops.

RESPONSIBILITIES
The successful candidate will assist with:
maintaining an analogue film facility
coordinating materials for workshops and events
preparing facilities and equipment for rental
providing members and volunteers with access to equipment and facilities
co-hosting a bi-weekly new-member orientation
preparing equipment inventories
coordinating equipment repair and purchase recommendations
other administrative and technical duties as required

QUALIFICATIONS:
excellent oral and written communication skills in English
thorough knowledge of a range of digital and analogue film materials and
equipment
excellent organizational and multi-tasking skills
ability to work independently and as part of a team
ability to design and produce promotional materials an asset

Art at Work Program: How We Make Public Art
Sunday, February 21, 2021
1:00-3:30 pm

This discussion panel will be hosted by the Richmond Public Art Program and feature
presentations by artists Germaine Koh, Sara Graham and Alyssa Schwann who have
recently completed large-scale public artworks for the City of Richmond. The virtual
presentation will focus on how they started their public art practice and how they
produce, administer and manage large-scale public art projects. This session is
ideal for
emerging or mid-career artists who are interested in expanding their art practice into the
public realm. A Q & A session will follow the artist presentations.

To register for the Zoom presentation, visit: www.richmond.ca/culture/publicart/whatsnew

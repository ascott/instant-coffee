Charitable Donations Workshop
Presented in partnership with CARFAC BC and Burnaby Art Gallery

Date: Sunday, January 28, 2018 from 10:00am-1:00pm
Location: Fireside Room, Burnaby Art Gallery, 6344 Deer Lake Avenue, Burnaby, 
BC
Facilitated by CARFAC National Executive Director April Britski and Jessica Somers 
(CPA, CGA)

This presentation will be an opportunity for artists in all stages of their careers to 
learn about the complexities of donating artwork, and will address:

-- the ethics of charitable donations and art-based fundraisers
-- tax issues related to the process of in-kind giving, including a hands-on analysis 
on how to estimate the tax effect of an artwork donation and how to properly report 
the transaction
-- case studies: following CARFACs best practice guidelines, well examine case 
studies and discuss how requests for artwork could be more fair to artists, with tips 
on negotiating better terms within contracts

We are looking forward to an engaging discussion, and we hope you will join us. 
Please let us know if you are coming by registering before Thursday, January 25, 
2018. To register, call the Burnaby Art Gallery at 604.297.4422 or 
burnaby.ca/webreg (Barcode: 458644).
CARFAC BC Members $20 / Non-Members $32.

About the facilitators:

April Britski is the National Executive Director of CARFAC, Canadas national 
association for visual and media artists. She joined the staff of CARFAC National in 
2005, and has worked and volunteered as an art educator, curator, and arts 
administrator since 1998. She has an MA in Canadian Art History from Carleton 
University, and a BA in Art History and Studio Art from the University of 
Saskatchewan. She is a co-founder of Artists Legal Services Ottawa, and the Visual 
Arts Alliance, and has served on the board of directors of CARFAC Saskatchewan, 
Galerie SAW Gallery, the Canadian Senior Artist Resource Network, and the 
Coalition for Cultural Diversity. In 2010, she spent five months working in the UK, 
researching how the Artists Resale Right is applied internationally. During her time 
in Europe, she worked on secondment with various visual arts organizations, 
including Artquest, the Design and Artists Copyright Society, and Artists Interaction 
and Representation (AIR). She is currently based in Burnaby, BC.

Jessica Somers is a chartered professional accountant (CPA, CGA) with over ten 
years of experience advising freelancers, artists, entrepreneurs and local businesses 
in Vancouver on tax, accounting, and business process. As founder of Cordova 
Street Consulting, Jessica is working to change the landscape of tax advising from a 
black box to an open conversation. Jessica approaches clients from a perspective of 
knowledge sharing, outreach, and taking the stress and mystery out of tax! Jessica 
is also a session leader at CPA Western School of Business where she teaches the 
next generation of Vancouver accountants and tax advisors.

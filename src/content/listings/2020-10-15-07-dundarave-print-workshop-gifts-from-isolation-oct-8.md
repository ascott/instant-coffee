October 8 - 25, 2020
New gallery hours: Friday-Sunday 11am-4pm Closed Monday-Thursday
Despite the pandemic and the workshop and gallery closing for a few months the
Dundarave artists continued to make new prints. It has been a challenge juggling studio
time to keep everyone safe but artists are resilient and determined. The
Gallery re-
opened July 1st with new hours. Stop by to see this exhibition of new works created
during a time of isolation and uncertainty.

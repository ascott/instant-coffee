and dissemination. 

WHAT YOU BRING:

teamwork
equity-driven practice
fundraising and facilities experience 
non-profit experience 
strategic planning and governance experience 
a commitment to artist-centred values
WHERE WE FOCUS:

experimental media arts 
equitable and responsible stewardship of media arts resources 
responsive and decolonizing practices

ADMIN@VIVOMEDIAARTS.COM 

BY APRIL 22.

ORGANIZATION AND ENCOURAGES APPLICATIONS FROM SELF-IDENTIFIED

The Pitt, 221A & YOU
A Saturday Afternoon "Meet-&-Greet"
2 - 5pm, Saturday, January 9th, 2010

Please join us for great coffee, tea and donut-like things! Board
Members, volunteers, funders, and supporters of both 221A and the
Helen Pitt Gallery Artist-Run Centre will be in attendance. Show up
and introduce yourself. Find out what exciting things are in store
during this dynamic time of redevelopment for the Pitt.

Ask about the Pitt's new curatorial residencies programming
initiative, student co-op appointments, upcoming part-time staff
position and relocation plans. Learn more about what the Pitt and
221A will be up to in the coming months. See the 221A contemporary
art gallery and studio space. Become a member of both organizations.
Sign up to volunteer. Help us realize exciting fundraising events in
the coming months.

Lots of handy literature and good cheer will be readily available.
Catalogues, posters, invites and other surprises from throughout the
Pitt's 35-year history will be temporarily unearthed from our archives
for your perusal. Don't miss out! Rumour has it that the Helen Pitt
Gallery ARC Time Capsule will also be on display. Come out to touch
and stare at this amazing crate -- not to be opened until 2019 -- and
experience profound feelings of anticipation and hope.

There will most likely be free donuts (or donut-like things) AND a
few welcoming words of introduction from representatives of both
organizations.

We look forward to your company!

Please email info@helenpittgallery.org for more information.

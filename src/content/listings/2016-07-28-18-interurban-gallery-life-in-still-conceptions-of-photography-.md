The exhibition forms a dialogue between visual and conceptual 
practices, using both photography and painting interchangeably as 
the primary mediums.

Highlighted in the exhibition is an array of interdisciplinary art 
practices such as photography, two-dimensional forms, sculpture, 
painting, mixed media, and film. The dialogue created between 
both visual and conceptual forms allows the artists to expand upon 
their own understanding of the art while allowing viewers to 
recognize the complexity of the relationship between subject and 
sign. 

Our mission is to connect strangers through the shared passion of 
Vancouver's contemporary art community. 

Artists featured: Debbie Westergaard Tuepah, Gordon Poppy, Judy 
D Shane, Patricia Polo, Tess Sereda, and Jacob Harris & Sarah 
Bednash

About LIFE IN STILL: 

When: Friday, August 19th 6 - 10 PM

Curated by Stephanie Gagne and Avery Hannig

Under Construction features new works by Bree Castle, Stephanie Gagne, Sylvan
Hamburger, Avery Hannig, and Stephanie Wu. These artists explore notions of
home, using construction materials to reflect on the instability of urban landscapes.

Through photographic and sculptural installations, the exhibition engages with the
aesthetics of provisional architecture. The works highlight the dissonance between
fantasy and the lived reality of housing precarity in British Columbia.

Using familiar materials and imagery, the artists examine the impact of rapid
development and the erasure of neighborhoods. Domestic spaces become theatrical
sets, where comfort is rendered uncanny.

Under Construction casts a critical, sometimes sardonic, gaze on the Canadian
cityscape. It challenges viewers to reconsider the meaning of home in an
environment defined by transience, spectacle, and longing.

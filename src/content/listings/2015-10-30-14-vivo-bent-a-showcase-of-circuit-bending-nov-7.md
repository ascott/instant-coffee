BENT: A Showcase of Circuit Bending at VIVO

Saturday, November 7th . Doors at 7pm
2625 Kaslo Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada, BC V5M 3G9

If you’re remotely interested in electronics, experimental music, hacking,
or just want to get transported to another dimension, this event is worth
visiting.

Featuring over 15 artists/hackers and musicians, this evening is a showcase
of Vancouver artists and makers exploring the art of circuit bending.

Featuring performances by:

Giorgio Magnanensi // Kendrick James // Frederick Brummer // Sara Gold //
Emily Thacker // Vincent van Haaff // Bella McKee // Norah Lorway // Kiran
Bhumber // Chris Hixon

Suggested donation is $10. No one turned away for lack of funds. All
donations go towards making this and other events like it, possible. You
can RSVP in advance via Eventbrite.

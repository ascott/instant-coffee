Assistant Preparator

The Kamloops Art Gallery is seeking a collaborative and enthusiastic individual to assist
with the realization of exhibitions by working closely with the Kamloops Art Gallery
Curator, Preparator and exhibiting artists to plan and implement exhibitions in the Gallery.
The ideal candidate has completed a Degree or Diploma in Fine Arts or Visual Arts. They
must have a willingness to learn, a passion for art, experience with photography and audio
visual equipment and excellent communication skills.

For a detailed job description please see below.

Salary: $32,000 to
$40,000/per annum

Employee status: Permanent, 4 days per week or 80% equivalent

Application deadline: Friday, July 31, 2020

Please direct a cover letter and resume addressed to:

Kait Methot
Administrative Coordinator

Job Purpose

The Assistant Preparator assists the Preparator with the installation of exhibitions to
realize the artists vision.

Primary Duties and Responsibilities

Develops deep knowledge of KAG exhibitions and artists through research and
engagement in Program Team meetings and collaborative planning with Program Team
members.

Participates in pre-exhibition planning and construction.

Conducts research on the equipment and technical requirements for exhibitions,
including staying up-to-date on current innovations, skills and knowledge in the
contemporary art
museum field as it relates to new media and exhibition installation

Prepares video files and equipment requirements for exhibitions.

Provides assistance with all aspects of exhibition de-installation and installation
including, painting, construction, installing audio visual equipment, art work, technical set-
up and other duties as needed.

Takes and edits photos of the collection, public programs and exhibitions.

Takes video of Curator and artist tours and edits work for final presentation.

Maintains inventory of all a/v equipment (audio, projectors, media players, etc.)

Performs maintenance on all a/v equipment (firmware updates, projector lamp
replacements, etc.)

Assists with the maintenance and organization of the packing and collection area.

Oversees KAG facility and maintenance.

Assists in the maintenance of the public art collection off-site.

Relationships

The Assistant Preparator is responsible for assisting with the realization of the artists
vision by working
closely with the Kamloops Art Gallery Curator and exhibiting artists to
plan and implement exhibitions in the Gallery. The Assistant Preparator works
professionally with artists, suppliers, contractors and other organizations. The Assistant
Preparator works closely with all KAG employees, but requires extensive collaboration
with the Preparator, Curator, Assistant Curator, Registrar, Education and Public Programs
Director and the Gallery Attendants.

The Assistant Preparator reports directly to the Preparator.

Requirements

Experience working in a contemporary art gallery.

Knowledge of contemporary art practices, particularly new media.

Ability to contribute to long-term strategic exhibition planning.

Must be detail oriented with a strong desire to ensure any finished product meets
safety standards with particular attention to aesthetic details and artistic requirements.

Excellent communication skills with an ability to take direction as well as delegate
tasks to contractors and
employees.

Experience in an office environment, excellent administrative and communication
skills.

Ability to be flexible and manage competing priorities with tight timelines.

Knowledge of the standard tools, practices and methods used in painting, rough
and finish carpentry, cabinet work and the use of different materials used in construction.

Ability to perform carpentry and electrical tasks.

Experience with photography and videography of objects, spaces and people
considered an asset.

Must be physical capable of climbing ladders and scaffolding, lifting up to 50lbs,
standing for long periods of time and handling delicate objects.

Proficient with MS Office programs, Photoshop, Final Cut Pro or similar video
editing software, Adobe Illustrator, BrightAuthor (media player software).

Completion of a Degree or Diploma in Fine Arts, Visual Arts or other related field of
study considered an asset.

First Aid certification is an asset.

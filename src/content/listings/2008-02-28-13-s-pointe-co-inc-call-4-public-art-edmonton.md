We require your assistance in locating artists who are creating artwork appropriate for the following Art Acquisition Project for the new Lois Hole Hospital for Women. Would you be willing to share this notice with artists you are familiar? If you have other comments, we would like to hear them.

In addition to Commissioning two outdoor public art installations, Capital Health Edmonton is procuring a collection of original interior artwork from western and northern Canadian artists for the new Robbins Pavilion, home of the Lois Hole Hospital for Women and Centre for Cardiac Services, located on the Royal Alexandra Hospital Campus in Edmonton.

The budgets for the first ten locations of indoor artwork range from $8,500 to $60,000. Later this year we will be choosing artists/artwork for additional locations.

We are open to artists working in visual or craft media, 2D or 3D. We are seeking artwork that delights, surprises and moves us, particularly elements of whimsy, surprise and humour. We are seeking imagery and stylesthat would speak to the experiences and diversity of cultural backgrounds of the patients the inner city hospital will serve. And finally, imagery that calms the spirit, that celebrates beauty, joy, wellness and regeneration.

This invitation is directed at artists from western & northern Canada as this is the area from which patients and families are drawn to be served in the Capital Health Edmonton Region.

Our deadline for the submissions is postmarked byMonday March 17, 2008. Inquiries can be directed to us at submissions@pointearts.com.

To maintain an efficient submission process, we are seeking only the following:Artist's Resume, Short Biography, Five JPEG Imagesor Slides and anImage List.Terms of the Invitation are attached for more details.

Thank you so much for your assistance and consideration of our request.

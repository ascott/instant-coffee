Friends Issue 1: Utopian Visions
Call for Submissions
Deadline:
Fri. March 19th, 2021 11:59 PM (ET)

Calling all photographers and art/photography writers! Were excited to announce an
open call to submit images to the first issue of Friends. Friends explores the publication
as an alternative exhibition space. Each issue focuses on contemporary photography,
revolving around a central idea and experiments with forms of dissemination. The
various formats challenge dominant modes of commercial art, client-based design, and
mainstream publishing. The project aims to build community and artistic camaraderie
through conversation, connection, and engagement with the public, through both
analog and digital forms of dissemination.

If selected your image will be displayed within the tabloid publication, public wheat
paste project and on friendsmagazine.ca

For the first issue, we're looking for photo-based work that revolves around the people,
places, and things that inspire you. And specifically what utopia means to you. This
could be your own personal
paradise, either physical, completely imagined, spiritual or
emotional.

WHAT TO SUBMIT:
5 JPG images or a link to a larger portfolio
no minimum or maximum file size (if you are selected, you may be contacted for high
resolution images)
for writing submissions please submit PDF files
format for naming your files: FIRSTNAME_LASTNAME_01.jpg or .pdf
please send submissions to quinn.branch@student.ocadu.ca

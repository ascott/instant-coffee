"Colour Stories"

A photography exhibit at Untitled Art Space - a part of the Capture 
Photography Festival. 

Opening on Friday, April 8
8pm - late

The gallery is open Saturdays and Sundays from 1pm-5pm.
The show runs from April 8-18, 2016

Photographer Tanya Goehring and Creative Director/Stylist Nariman J 
collaborated to create "Colour Stories." This series is a trip through the 
decades, playing on the aesthetic sensibilities of each period. Through 
their use of a limited colour pallet within each photograph they explore 
a unique feeling from one image to the next that play out a moving 
visual poetic. Nariman created the set and Tanya designed the lighting 
to create photographs that transport the viewer into their surreal world.

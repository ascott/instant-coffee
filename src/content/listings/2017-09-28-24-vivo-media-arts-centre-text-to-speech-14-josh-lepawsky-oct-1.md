Join us at VIVO as we read from georgrapher Josh Lepawsky's forthcoming book, 
Reassembling Rubbish: Worlding Electronic Waste. We'll be reading chapter 5 which 
unpacks the interchange that takes place between the object--the geographies 
discarded electronics--and how such a complex subject can possibly be known. 
Lepawsky will be in attendance!

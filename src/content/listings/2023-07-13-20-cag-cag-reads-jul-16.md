CAG Reads: Audie Murray presents adrienne maree brown, George Fulford and Julie
Flett
16 JUL 4:00 PM ' 5:30 PM
Online via Zoom

Were looking forward to the July installment of CAG Reads, led by artist Audie Murray.
Audie has chosen to read a selection of texts including Love As Political Resistance
(2019) by adrienne maree brown, Net Charms in Cree and Ojibwe Language (2012)
by George Fulford and the children's book Owls See Clearly at Night: A Michif
Alphabet/Lii Yiiboo Nayaapiwak lii Swer: L'Alfabet
Di Michif (2010) by Julie Flett.

Access to all of Audie's CAG Reads selections will be provided upon registration. To
RSVP and for more information: https://cagvancouver.org/event/cag-reads-audie-
murray

Audie Murray is a visual artist, dreamer, and skin-stitcher. Her practice is informed by
the process of making and visiting to explore themes of contemporary culture,
embodied experiences and lived dualities. These modes of working assist with the
recentering of our collective connection to bodies, ancestral knowledge systems, and
relationality. She has exhibited widely, including at Remai Modern, Saskatoon; The
Vancouver Art Gallery; Centre for Contemporary Arts, Glasgow; and the Anchorage
Museum. Murray is Cree-Mtis from the Lebret and Meadow Lake communities located
on Treaty 4 & 6 territories and is currently based in Oskana k-asastki (Regina,
Saskatchewan). Murray is represented by Fazakas Gallery, located on Swxw7mesh,
Sl"lwta", and xmky"m territory.

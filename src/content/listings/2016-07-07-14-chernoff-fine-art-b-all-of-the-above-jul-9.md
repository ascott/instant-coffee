Jow - B) All of the above

Chernoff Fine Art
9 July - 20 August 2016
OPENING RECEPTION: Saturday, July 9, 2-5pm

Jow studied fine arts at Capilano College and the Emily Carr Institute 
of Art + Design in Vancouver before relocating to the United States. 
She has previously exhibited her irreverent and witty drawings about 
contemporary life in Vancouver, New York and Los Angeles including 
numerous group shows + solo exhibitions. Her work employs word play 
and text-based codes to form a simple, yet complex link between the 
purely visual and literal aspects of language.

You are cordially invited to join the mail art project, As The Crow Flies.

This project will use mail art as a mechanism
for cross-cultural exchange between
the West Coast and East Coast of so-called Canada.

There are two criteria for participation:

You must consider some part of the land called Canada home.

You must currently reside near or have active ties to a coastal area within Canada.

As The Crow Flies Is a community-based mail-art project connecting the coastal
communities of so-called-Canada. This project will foster dialogue between people
from opposite sides of the country, creating new communities of cross-pollination on
a host of topics from local ecology to Canadian identity. Participants will be free to
send mailings of their own design but are encouraged to consider themes of home,
land, language, cultural identity and Canadian history and mythology. This project
will be documented in an online format and, pending funding, a print publication in
2025.

If interested, please read the project description and add yourself to our address
book. Further instructions will be sent by the post
in early spring:

Vancouver International Film Festival 2016 Volunteer Team Lead 
Positions Call

VIFF and our volunteers are looking for bigger and better things 
each year; this year we are offering the opportunity for Team Lead 
positions. These roles not only provide crucial mentorship, team 
development, and exceptionally skilled experience to the festival - 
they also provide an opportunity for valued professional experience 
and development. These lead roles are for individuals, who have a 
working knowledge in team building, arts organizations, leadership 
and streamlining communication. Each role is different and can 
encompass areas of administration, marketing, photography, 
publicity to supporting diverse teams of people. These roles will 
take place throughout the city at our various venues throughout the 
preparation and duration of the festival. Being accepted into a 
position will aid you in developing professional exposure in an 
exciting industry; it is also a unique opportunity to support the arts 
through your personal contribution of time and expertise. In 
exchange we will provide you the chance to broaden your 
knowledge in volunteer management, human resources, arts 
administration and event planning, and of course the amazing 
volunteer benefits. 

Team Lead Position Descriptions: 
Festival Exhibitions - Team Lead
Info Line - Customer Support Team Lead
Marketing and Distribution - On Screen Personalities
Marketing and Distribution - Photography Team Member Lead
Marketing and Distribution - Volunteer Videographer 
Office - Administrative Team Lead

To find out more information please visit 
[http://www.viff.org/volunteer](http://www.viff.org/volunteer) to find the application and other 
opportunities.

Dina Gonzlez Mascar: Vessels are Never Empty 

March 9th - April 6th, 2019
Opening Reception: Friday March 8th 6-8 pm

From the mountain structures she "entered" to meditate, to the vessels 
she molded to contain the transformative energy, Gonzlez Mascar 
has built a delimited space that acts not only as a container but is also 
a way to put in place the ontological relationship between life death and 
the body, and finally to let go
Franc Gallery I 1654 Franklin Street I Vancouver, BC

[http://francgallery.com](http://francgallery.com) I 604-428-4248

Artist talk: Saturday, March 23rd, 3 pm
A Conversation with Kate Hennessy, Associate Professor, School of 
Interactive Art and Technology, Simon Fraser University and Dina 
Gonzlez Mascar about the liveliness of objects and their place in 
marking the passage of life.

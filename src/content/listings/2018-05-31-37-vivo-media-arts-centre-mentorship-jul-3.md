Still Creek Salmon Sounds youth mentorship project

Call for participation, ages 14-24

Deadline to Apply: July 3, 2018

SIGN UP & MORE INFO: http://vivomediaarts.com/stillcreeksalmonsounds

Still Creek Salmon Sounds is a mentorship project providing technical and
conceptual support to create a sound-based installation artwork that
combines field recording and data sonification to explore the salmon
spawning season in a local creek.

VIVO encourages applications from self-identified members of under-served
communities as well as visible and invisible minority groups. We thank all
who express interest in participating.

STUDIOS AVAILABLE | EAST PENDER STREET starting at $300/mo | JUN 1

We are pleased to announce the opening of StudioLand, nine brand new studio
spaces on East Pender Street between Columbia and Main Street.

The original space has been completely renovated and divided into
individual studio spaces, with exhibition opportunity in the common area.

Located on the second floor of a protected heritage building, the studios are
available in a variety of sizes starting at $300 per month.

Several spaces have windows looking onto Pender Street with great natural
light, and there is also a small kitchen area.

Were looking for quiet, serious, long-term studio mates, and the spaces
are ideal for painters, graphic artists, designers, illustrators, writers,
animators, jewelers, and filmmakers.

A five year lease is in place until June 2023 with an option to renew.

Bright, clean, safe, private, 24 hour access, free wifi and utilities, slop
sink, bike parking, and an outstanding central location. Shared studios are
welcome.

Steps away from galleries, cafes, restaurants, bus lines, bike paths, in
Chinatown, near Strathcona.

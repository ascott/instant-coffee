Please join us the evening of Monday, October 26th from 8-10pm, for a
free event at Interurban Gallery featuring works by alumni from SFU’s
School for the Contemporary Arts Dissonant Disco and New(to)Town
Collective.

In celebration of SFU's 50th Anniversary, the School for the
Contemporary Arts is hosting six evenings of dialogue and
performance over the next academic year. The SCA Studio Series
showcases past, present, and in-progress work by a diverse and
interdisciplinary cross-section of SCA alumni. Taking place at the
Interurban Gallery on the last Monday of every month between
September 2015 and March 2016, the SCA Studio Series provides a
unique opportunity to participate in / view a scope of work by artists
from the SCA community since the program's conception in 1975.

A new work by Dissonant Disco

Dissonant Disco is a Vancouver based composers collective dedicated
to the creation of new chamber music, site/context-specific
performance, and interdisciplinary collaboration. The group first formed
out of various dance and theatre collaborations, employing the use of
live electroacoustic improvisation within a defined structure. It has
since moved on to perform as a music ensemble in various venues
around Canada. The group is currently developing a new concert
series and an interdisciplinary, experimental opera based on the
creation of the first internet meme. Dissonant Disco has collaborated
with Steven Hill, Rob Kitsos, Ray Hsu, MACHiNENOiSY, Hong Kong
Exile, and QUASAR (quatuor de saxophones).

Dissonant Disco is Ashley Aron, Clinton Ackerman, Annie Therrien
Boulos, J.J. Hartmann, Scott Jeffrey, Gabsung Lim, Maren Lisac, Sarah
Lubbe, Alex Mah, and Remy Siu.

dissonantdisco.com

KIND (Child) A Staged Reading:

It began with one South African woman provoked by the death of a
black South African child. From this pain and conflict, she gives birth to
a poem that called for change within an apartheid torn nation. Her
name was Ingrid Jonker. KIND (Child) tells Ingrid’s story of her struggle
to express herself uncompromisingly in the face of oppression. Her
experiences reflect our own common longing to be loved and
understood and the courage to be ourselves. I invite you to witness at
our staged reading, the woman who was and will always be, Ingrid
Jonker.

New(to)Town Collective is an up and coming theatre collective
dedicated to creating innovative interdisciplinary works. With an
interest in collaboration and cross pollinating practices and ideas with
willing collaborators, New(to)Town Collective brings together a unique
blend of practices including: Grotowski, devising, playwriting,
dramaturgy, directing and more.

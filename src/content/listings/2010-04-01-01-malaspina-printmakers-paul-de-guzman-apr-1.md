Museum for the Administration of Aesthetics

Paul de Guzman

April 2 - May 1, 2010

Opening Reception: April 1, 7 - 9 pm

Malaspina Printmakers is pleased to present Museum for the Administration
of Aesthetics by Paul de Guzman. Operating from April 1- May 1, 2010, this
exhibition is the second of four taking place during the 2010 calendar year
that are devoted to presenting an expanded concept of print media.

Museum for the Administration of Aesthetics a participation-based project
devoted to compiling an archive that will reflect attitudes toward
contemporary art and the world in general. For the past six weeks, as the
Director of Museum for the Administration of Aesthetics (MAA), de Guzman
has been gathering information via personal interviews with individuals who
have responded to a request for participants. The results of these
interviews will be presented in the gallery, and interviewees will also be
able to take away a copy of their interview.

De Guzman uses bureaucratic administrative procedures and the questionnaire
as mechanisms to reveal attitudes to contemporary art and the world in
general. The procedural elements of administration serve to highlight the
seemingly arbitrary juncture of intuition, indoctrination and informatics
in aesthetics. Through MAA's passive attitude towards engaging individuals,
people must take the initiative to contact the Museum. Standing in contrast
to the persistent tactics of telemarketers, this serves as a reminder of
potential: one must put forth an effort in any realm in order to possibly
gain access or benefit.

De Guzman will be holding office hours in the gallery during the month of
April on Fridays and Saturdays from 2-5pm. If you would like to arrange an
interview please email m.aesthetics@yahoo.ca.

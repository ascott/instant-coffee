Job Opening: Gallery Assistant with Presentation House Gallery

Under the direction of staff, the Gallery Assistant will help with
operational and administrative activities of Presentation House Gallery.
Requires flexible hours, knowledge and curiosity about visual art, and
excellent research and writing skills with strong attention to detail.

Core Activities:

Curatorial Assistance: Work with curatorial staff to research and develop
exhibitions, publications, public programs and education activities.
Involves participation in all aspects of exhibition coordination from the
research phase to generating label copy. Activities include: research (at
times offsite), coordination of information for promotion, PHG newsletter,
press releases and interpretive material; preparation and coordination of
contracts and loans; research and organization of written and visual
material for publication; proofreading and production assistance for
publications; assistance with grant applications; and coordination of public
programs.

Administrative Assistance: Assist with gallery promotion and marketing such
as mail outs and announcements; management of the gallery¹s membership and
contact database; coordination of information for the strategic plan.
Assist with daily office activities and reception duties.

Gallery and Event Assistance: Opening and closing the gallery and managing
retail sales in the bookshop. Assist in organizing and carrying out
hospitality and education events. Requires flexible hours, working weekends
and other times as requested.

Preparatory Assistance: Assist with exhibition installation and
de-installation.

Salary: based on experience

If you are interested, please submit your resume to
info@presentationhousegall.com <mailto:info@presentationhousegall.com> with
the email title ³Summer Position² by June 9th at 4pm.

Please note: This position is funded in part by HRDC, and as such the
successful candidate must be under the age of 30 with plans to return to
school in the next school year 2008 - 2009.

The City of Vancouver Public Art Program is pleased to present the work of
Swxw7mesh (Squamish) artist Austin (Aanyas) Harry who has made a short video
called Salish Spirit as part of Platforms: Nine Places for Seeing. The artwork was
created through digital compositing and features a 3D animated thunderbird moving
through video landscapes of the city of
Vancouver and adjacent natural spaces from sea
to sky. The thunderbird is an important figure in Salish legends who represents
connection to the spirit world and the Creator. This work asks the viewer to imagine this
powerful, spiritual creature existing in our own reality. In the artists own words: Having
Salish legends and stories brought to life in the center of the downtown core would be a
great opportunity to show residents of and visitors to Vancouver the rich cultural identity
tied to the land that they are a part of.

Austins work is being presented on the VanLive! video screen at the corner of Granville
and Robson at various times throughout the day with a dedicated showing at 12 noon,
daily. The work will be up until February 26th.

You can learn more about this work and the works of other featured artists on the Citys
website at https://vancouver.ca/platforms

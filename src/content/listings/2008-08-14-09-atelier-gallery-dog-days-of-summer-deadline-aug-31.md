DOG DAYS OF SUMMER
Featuring selected work by gallery artists
July 31 – August 31, 2008

Dog Days of Summer returns for its 2nd annual run at the Atelier Gallery. This exhibition will 
feature a broad selection of work by gallery artists, with subjects ranging from ice cream-coloured 
mountains to mythical creatures and celebrity dolls. We hope you will be able to drop by and view
some of the country's most refreshing art.

Featuring Selected Work By:

Stephanie Aitken

David Antonides

Luc Bernard

Camrose Ducote

Michael Dumontier

Neil Farber

Takashi Iwasaki

Hugh Kearney

Erin McSavaney

Julie Morstad

The Royal Art Lodge

Michael Swaney

David Sloan

Suzie Smith 

Leslie Supnet

David Wilson

Dog Days of Summer will be on display from July 31, to August 31, 2008.

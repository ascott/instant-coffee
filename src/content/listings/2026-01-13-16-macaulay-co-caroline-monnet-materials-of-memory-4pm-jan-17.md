Macaulay + Co. is thrilled to present a solo exhibition of work by Mooniyang/Montreal-based
Anishinaabe/French artist Caroline Monnet. Materials of Memory brings together photo and
textile-based works that engages a buoyant feeling for the future. Reframing materials and
images that have been controlled or exploited, Monnet reclaims them as sites of presence,
resistance, and future-making. Anishinaabe geometric patterns override contractor grade
building supplies with sacred symbolism offering a glimpse back and a path forward,
grounded in Indigenous womens knowledge and lived experience.

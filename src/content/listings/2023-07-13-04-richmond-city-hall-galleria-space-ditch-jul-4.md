In 2021, artist, Julie Pappajohn put a call out to the Facebook group, You grew up in
Richmond, asking for memories from those who spent their childhoods with Richmonds
exposed drainage ditches. The over 400 replies is testament to the significance of these
waterways, and the memories they generated. Storytelling through memory plays an
extraordinary part in a communitys history and shows the importance of collecting and
telling those stories. Drawings, paintings, mixed media works and narrated
video/slideshow.

July 4 until Sep 19

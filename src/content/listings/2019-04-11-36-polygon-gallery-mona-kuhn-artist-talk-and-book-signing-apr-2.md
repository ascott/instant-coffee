TALK: Mona Kuhn: Artist Talk and Book Signing | 3 " 5pm | April 28

Internationally known, LA-based artist Mona Kuhn is best known for her large-scale, 
atmospheric photographs of the human form. In this talk, Kuhn discusses her work 
included in The Polygon Gallery's 'a Handful of Dust' exhibition, as well as her latest 
artist book 'She Disappeared into Complete Silence' (Steidl, 2019). 

Kuhns talk will be followed by a book signing with the artist.

This event is organized in partnership with the 2019 Capture Photography Festival.

https://thepolygon.ca/event/mona-kuhn-artist-talk-and-book-signing/

You Are Here 2020 Calendar Exhibition
Jul 10 - Sep 30, 2019
Calendar Launch and Artist Reception: Thu, Aug 1, 6:30-8pm

The 12 paintings in this exhibition tell the story of the North Shore through recognizable 
places and scenes. The images have been brought together into a 2020 calendar as a 
fundraiser for the North Shore Cultural Mapping project, spearheaded by North Van 
Arts. To be launched in 2020, the map will be a free, online hub for North Shore culture.

Participating artists: Jenn Ashton, Sahar Banisoltan, Tim Bennison, Miles Brook, Victoria 
Eftimova, Geoffrey Caofei Feng, Martin Henry, Danyne Johnston, Lucas Kratchowil, 
Shelley MacDonald, Kim Rosin.

Calendars will be for sale at the launch event, $20 each, and at various locations 
throughout the North Shore following the launch. Sales locations will be listed on our 
website.

District Library Gallery is located on the 2nd floor of Lynn Valley Library
1277 Lynn Valley Road, North Vancouver

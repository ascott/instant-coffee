Analogue Basics: Introduction to Bolex 16mm Cine Camera

Sunday, Jul 15; 2 PM - 6 PM
Cineworks Studio, 300-1131 Howe Street (lane entrance) 
Vancouver, British Columbia V6Z 2L7
$40"$60 + taxes

REGISTER: [https://bit.ly/2tTW2i7](https://bit.ly/2tTW2i7)

A 4 hour entry-level workshop introducing participants to the principles and practice of 
exposing film with a Bolex 16mm film camera.

$60 + taxes | General Admission
$50 + taxes | Concession / Community Partner Member*
$40 + taxes | Member Admission

*Community partners include WIFTV, DOC BC and VIVO

Elements include:

The mechanics and chemistry of exposing film
Understanding lenses
Handling film
Loading and unloading film in light and dark conditions
Shooting in natural light

Film shot in this workshop will be processed at the Analogue Basics: Intro to Hand 
Processing workshop on Sunday Jul 22. If interested in both components (optional), please 
register for the Hand Processing workshop via the following link:

[https://bit.ly/2MMZG4p](https://bit.ly/2MMZG4p)

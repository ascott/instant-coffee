Call for Submissions: The Hatch Art Gallery
Deadline: June 26, 2020 at 11:59 PM.

The Hatch Art Gallery is UBCs only student-run exhibition space. Situated on the
unceded, ancestral and traditional lands of the xwm0"kwym (Musqueam),
the Hatch aims to stimulate the exchange of art and ideas between artists,
students and the public. The Hatch seeks to present innovative projects on
critical subjects relevant to larger discourses in contemporary art, visual culture
and theory. In addition to biweekly exhibitions, the Hatch also maintains and
displays the AMS Permanent Collection, an impressive assortment of Canadian
art that provides a cross section of artistic growth and development in Canada
since 1945.

The Hatch Art Gallery is now accepting submissions for the
upcoming 2020-2021
exhibition season.

We happily invite you to submit proposals for artwork, exhibitions and
performances to take place at the Hatch Art Gallery and through online
programming. Proposals for projects containing artwork of all media will be
considered. Priority will be granted to submissions from UBC students and
alumni.

Due to COVID-19, we are currently working to adapt our usual exhibition
schedule, complying with social distancing measures for any in-person
exhibitions this year. Keep in mind that usual programming may be subject to
change but nevertheless, we are excited to explore more opportunities for artists
to showcase on our various online platforms. We encourage you to also submit
proposals for online activations such as digital or video works, live performance
streams, panel discussions, etc. For more information, please consult our
application.

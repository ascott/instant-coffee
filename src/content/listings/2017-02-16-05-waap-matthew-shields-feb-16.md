MATTHEW SHIELDS
OPENING RECEPTION: Thurs, Feb 16, 7-9 PM
Feb 16-Mar 18, 2016

HOURS: Tues - Sat, 11 - 5 PM

Wil Aballe Art Projects | WAAP is pleased to announce its first exhibition with 
Matthew Shields. The exhibition will include works of bricolage built on worn 
pants and a sand sculpture in the form of a throne. The exhibition also features 
a video, Tim, 2015 presented in lifescale on a large flatscreen monitor. Tim is a 
video portrait of an older man standing on an urban rooftop, singing songs and 
handling wooden puppets. The songs are traditional doggerel, full of obscenities 
and grotesque imagery. One original song takes humorous and critical aim at ex-
prime minister, Stephen Harper.

MATTHEW SHIELDS is a Vancouver based artist. His work was recently shown at 
the Vancouver Art Gallery in the exhibition An Agreeable State of Uncertainty.

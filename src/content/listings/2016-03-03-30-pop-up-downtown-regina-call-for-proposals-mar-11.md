Pop Up Downtown - Regina

Call for Proposals: 2016

Application Deadline: Friday March 11th

Successful Applicants Notified: By March 28th
Installation Timeline: Week of September 12th 2016

Theme: Walking - Bodies in Urban Space.

The Regina Downtown Business Improvement District (RDBID), in
partnership with Creative City Center, is seeking visual artists to create
high-impact installation work in underused downtown spaces. This
successful public art installation series, Pop Up Downtown, is
approaching its fourth year with the thematic focus of:

Walking - Bodies in Urban Space: The meditative, and physiological
impacts of the act of walking; the relationship between walking and
thinking within an urban space; references to body within the language
of architecture and the urban landscape; the subversive potential of
bodies in public spaces; the social hierarchy of the urban landscape.

Successful candidates will create an engaging satellite artwork to be
installed for a duration of up to three months. Locations will include;
unused storefronts, hidden and under-used spaces in the downtown
core. Successful candidates will also receive artist fees of $426 plus a
materials budget of up to $750.

For more information, and to apply please visit:
http://reginadowntown.ca/experience/pop-up-downtown

Please direct any questions to Amber: artist@reginadowntown.ca

Space Exploration 1: Acrylic painting with Etienne Zack
Jun. 26, 6:30 p.m. - 9:30 p.m. 
Mobil Art School, 268 Keefer Street, Vancouver, BC, Canada

Learn acrylic painting with renowned Canadian painter Etienne Zack. Space is limited so book 
now! Join us for a series of 4 painting workshops with renowned Canadian painter Etienne Zack 
as we explore creative ways of arranging pictorial space in acrylic painting. Each class will 
begin with a look at historic techniques of creating pictures from early Modernism to today. 
Explore techniques ranging from Cubism to the contemporary use of photography and painting 
with acrylic gel transfer. Click below on more to see additional dates and materiel required. This 
class is open to all skill levels.
June 26/July 3/July 10; each session: $75

https://www.mobil-art.ca/event-info/space-exploration-1-acrylic-painting-with-etienne-zack

mobil art is an independent school dedicated to making quality art education available to people 
of all ages, through offering unique and engaging courses in art making and appreciation/history 
both in the studio and off-site, from workshops for kids to university-quality courses for adults.

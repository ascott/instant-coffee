WESTERN FRONT

Feeling So Much International Prospective Lecture Series

Talk by Bernd Milla
Thursday, March 8, 7pm

Bernd Milla, Director of the Kunststiftung Baden-Wuerttemberg will
reflect on exhibition praxis and possibilities for artists' work and
presentation.

This lecture series is part of *Feeling So Much Yet Doing So Little*
by Instant Coffee, an exhibition and series of events that is a
tangential revisiting of the Canadian Artist Collective’s 12 year
history. Bernd Milla is the first speaker in the series. Sofía
Hernández Chong Cuy and Lise Nellman will follow. Each lecture is
moderated by Caitlin Jones and will address their distinct theoretical
and collaborative approaches.

This series is generously supported by Jane Irwin and Ross Hill.

Bernd Milla, is the co-founder and Director of the halle fuer kunst
Lueneburg e.V. 1995 - 2001 (with Heike Munder). 2001 - 2006 
Managing
Director of the Deutscher Kuenstlerbund with the setting up of a
project space in Berlin-Mitte. Since 2004 responsible for the
programme at the Kunstverein Goettingen. Visiting tutor and lecturer
at the HfG Zurich in the module curating. 2006 - 2009 Artistic
Director of the AIR Kuenstlerhaeuser Worpswede. Since 2010 Director
of the Kunststiftung Baden-Wuerttemberg (art foundation of the State
of Baden-Wuerttemberg). Lives in Stuttgart and Berlin.

Instant Coffee in collaboration Milla will present Bild n Sound a
program a selection of silent videos by German artists Inviting
Canadian musicians to play a live score. Silents videos by Andreas
Lorenschat, Mirko Martin, Pia Maria Martin, Gabriela Oberkofler,
Patrick Fabian Panetta and Sebastian Stumpf. Live music by Jeremy
Schmidt (Black Mountain and Sinoia Caves), Stephen Lyons, Shanto
Bhattacharya, Skye Brooks (Fond of Tigers), Marta McKeever, Tess
Kitchen & Diona Davies (E.S.L.) On March 10th at the Western Front. A
project by the Goethe-Institut in cooperation with Instant Coffee and
Revised Projects

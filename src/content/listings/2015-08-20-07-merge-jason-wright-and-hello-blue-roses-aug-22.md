This past May Jason Wright showed part one of a series of work
entitled To Serve Man is a Cookbook: A Dog's Dinner at Merge. The
wonderful sounds of Parkland and Copilots made for a lovely opening
and evening.

On Saturday, August 22, part two of the series will be shown entitled To
Serve Man is a Cookbook: Cornucopia and will be keeping with the
food-related theme of the previous show. This time around, much to
our delight, the incredible Hello, Blue Roses will be playing a short set.

You may wish to check out www.jasonwright.ca to get an idea as to
what you may be in for. The show will be comprised of photography,
drawing, sculpture, foodstuffs, collage and prints.

The evening will be free! Refreshments will be available. Come and
hang out- lots of comfy seeing and joy to be had. Everyone welcome!

Opening: 8:00- 10:00pm
Hello, Blue Roses 10:00pm

HELLO BLUE ROSES is a Vancouver based duo fronted by Sydney
Hermant and Dan Bejar. Their 2015 full length release WZO on local
imprint JAZ Records, was produced by Carey Mercer of Frog Eyes at
Quadraticus Studios and bears the stamp of the cooked guitars and
echoing beats associated with that room. The record is haunted by
Hermant's etherized and insistent vocals about women and ghosts,
accompanied by her guitar drones and plucks. Holding it down are
Melanie Campbell and Terri Upton (Frog Eyes rhythm section on loan),
Josh Wells (Black Mountain, Lightning Dust, Sur Une Plage, Destroyer)
with yet another star bongo turn, and Mercer himself, whose singular
axework, drum progamming and live knob-twiddling are a cornerstone
of the WZO Sound.

Dan and Sydney will be performing a post art viewing mellow two
person set.

Merge@ the Northeast corner of Powell and Clark

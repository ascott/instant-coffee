JOB POSTING
Family Sunday Facilitator needed at the Richmond Art Gallery

Family Sunday is a free drop in art making session for parents and children held the fourth 
Sunday of the month at the Richmond Art Gallery. The Family Sunday Facilitator works with 
volunteer high school students leading them through the development and delivery of each 
exhibition related project.

The Facilitator will:

keep informed of Gallery exhibitions
present exhibition content to students during after-school prep sessions
develop exhibition related projects with students
oversee Family Sunday day (excluding December)
liaise with the Education and Public Programs Coordinator 
assess the program and the volunteer students
assist with recruitment of students when needed
assist with promotion of the program, gallery and sponsor
submit to Criminal Check
maintain budget and purchase materials
organise timelines and tasks
keep accurate records and documentation of the program

Qualifications:

Hold a BFA
Have experience working with volunteers and/or teens.
Have a sound knowledge of contemporary art and be able to communicate central ideas to high 
school students
Be able to work with a variety of 2D and 3D material
Enjoy working with teens, children and parents

Position requires approximately 15 hours a month. Salary: not specified

Please submit resume to FS Hiring Committee, Richmond Art Gallery, 7700 Minoru Gate, 
Richmond, BC V6Y 1R9 or by email: ncapogna@richmond.ca

We thank you for your interest. No phone calls please. Only short listed applicants will be notified. 
Deadline: Thursday, September 3, 2009.
